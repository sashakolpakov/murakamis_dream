# A conditional signed spectral route to an actual peripheral volume gap

Workspace research draft, 31 October 2026.
The incidence-character statements below are proved finite algebra.
The source-to-group and absolute-regulator assertions in Section 3 are

## 1. What the absolute critical equations actually imply

Consider the original full-V face source, with its original ordinary
targets d=N-1 and its two protected zero faces. Fix a strict real spectral
chamber and a real coefficient-flow datum with finite positive ordinary
Lagrange multipliers. Retain each exact unused-row sign and each
omitted-half Chebyshev mode.

On a strict LEFT straddling interval, the unsigned coefficient action has
the actual outside log|alpha| integrals and the tail action. It is not
the convexified max(log c,Q_b+log c/2). Its unused suffix contributes
the incidence character (-1)^(n_c), with the exact scalar in
[the local phase obstruction](research_sharp_spectral_phase_obstruction.md),
Section 4. The RIGHT case is its recorded reflection.

At a real free-spectral critical point, partial_delta h=0. Each retained
omitted-half mode contributes +/-pi i delta to the principal complex
action, at both parities away from the spectral endpoints. Consequently
its full spectral derivative is

    partial_delta A=+pi i or -pi i,                            (1)

provided the remaining ordinary stationarity has been restored and no
additional spectral phase has been discarded. A fixed discrete incidence
character has no delta derivative inside the chosen chamber.

Thus an actual UNTWISTED primitive adapter, if proved, would give an
auxiliary meridian with trace squared ZERO, not an identity meridian.
Formula (1) uses the original longitude polarization

    U=partial_delta A, V=-4pi i delta, M=exp(U/2).

At U=+/-pi i, the two eigenvalues of M are +/-i and its trace is zero.
For a retained half-twist epsilon the recorded convention is instead

    U_epsilon=U_0-epsilon V/2.

Writing ell=exp(V/2) for the CENTERED longitude eigenvalue gives
M_epsilon² ell^epsilon=-1. The literal mixed source retains the
preferred SL2 longitude lambda=-ell and its explicit closing-pivot
central character; see actual_mixed_crossing_circle_preferred_longitude,
section6. On a regular semisimple collar the matrices commute and
have the same two eigenlines. Thus the ACTUAL preferred word is

    rho(m_epsilon² longitude^epsilon)=(-1)^(1+epsilon) I.     (1a)

This is a fixed nontrivial peripheral word, killed in PSL2. It is
not a claim that the half-twisted meridian itself has trace0.
The epsilon character is restored exactly once. In particular the
right side is -I for epsilon0 and +I for epsilon+1 or-1. This
research correction changes no PSL kernel or volume-gap implication.

## 2. Exact solvability of the shared real-to-complex sign repair

Let epsilon_(e,f) be the local incidence character of the chosen
chamber. The necessary and sufficient finite sign-absorption test is

    epsilon_(e,f)=gamma_f+kappa_e  in F_2,                     (2)

on every allowed ordinary incidence. Equivalently every source-incidence
cycle has even character sum. The proof and exact target scalar are
in [the sign-cocycle lemma](research_spectral_face_sign_cocycle.md).

If (2) holds, set the shared clock of ordinary face f to
(-1)^gamma_f L_f. Each local row gauge changes only its homogeneous
degree-d scalar. On the EXACT marked coefficient, the combined
character is the constant

    (-1)^[d sum_f gamma_f+d sum_e kappa_e].                    (3)

This makes the absorption an equality of finite monomial phases,
not an informal independent replacement of a port's output clock.
The protected incidences have target0 and remain absent.

It also explains the stationary interpretation precisely. On a real
chamber where the remaining principal coefficient action and its flow
derivatives are real, the imaginary flow phase is just pi times the
incidence character. A full phase saddle on this real datum must
annihilate every allowed integral cycle direction modulo2pi. Thus
(2) is necessary when all those directions have relative-interior
slack. Conversely, (2) permits a constant phase representative on
the exact transportation fiber, so the real stationary equations
lift to the exponential complex flow equations after the common
signed-clock change. Integer 2pi characters are recorded as identical
finite monomial phases; no new contribution or contour is inferred.

An arbitrary absolute stationary point does not automatically satisfy
(2). The actual width-three cycle in the linked note has odd character.
A general complex saddle is different again: non-character imaginary
derivatives of its complex tail action may compensate such a cycle.
The simple real sign test neither excludes nor constructs those saddles.

Even when (2) holds, physical half powers require their original
central square-root lifts, and all three head derivatives must be
checked. The constant (3) does not by itself select those lifts.

## 3. The precise conditional remaining lemma

Fix an original certified drilled exterior Y_D. Suppose a compact
stratum of candidates for the SHARP shared spectral rate has the
following properties:

1. Its real row, outer-degree and shared-face critical equations are
   the original coefficient equations. Ordinary multipliers are finite
   and nonzero; protected faces retain their literal boundary adapters.
   All free spectral derivatives vanish at the candidate.
2. Its required real-to-complex phase repair is performed by (2), or
   by a proved more general full complex-clock construction. The actual
   omitted-half modes, the original half-twist/framing phases, and every
   original cap, cup and unrestricted mark remain present.
3. The resulting full physical derivatives preserve the SAME ordered
   product on both legs of every port and sew to an ACTUAL SL2(C)
   representation rho of the original drilled group, with its fixed
   based peripheral words and the primitive normalization above.
4. Its absolute action is identified with that representation's
   canonical relative volume, in the precise longitude form

       2pi Re A = Vol(rho) - (1/2) sum_i Im U_i Re V_i.         (4)

   This identity must come from the original relative fundamental
   chain or a calibrated continuation preserving its decoration.
   The disproved local Gauss-reference reset is not an admissible proof.
5. At at least one regular semisimple collar, the retained local phase
   gives U_0=+/-pi i as in (1), with no missing spectral character.
   Its actual original marking is either the untwisted trace0 case
   or the retained-half-twist case (1a).

Then these candidates have a UNIFORM strict rate gap below Phi_D.
Indeed delta is real, so Re V=0 and (4) gives
2pi Re A=Vol(rho). In the untwisted case the actual selected meridian
has trace squared0, distance4 from its complete trace squared4. The already proved
[general peripheral-gap theorem](general_representation_volume_peripheral_gap.md)
applies to the finite fixed list of original crossing-circle meridians:
there is g_D>0 such that

    |Vol(rho)|<=Vol(Y_D)-g_D,
    Re A<=Phi_D-g_D/(2pi).                                   (5)

That theorem uses the canonical peripheral-regulator volume for actual
SL2 representations, including nonparabolic peripheral images, and
proves its near-maximal compactness by the recorded natural-map
argument. We are not substituting the volume of an unglued collection
of local pyramids or a different boundary normalization.

For (1a), the SAME proved near-maximal compactness gives a uniform
gap directly. If representations killing one of the fixed words
m_i² longitude_i^(epsilon_i) had absolute volumes tending to Vol(Y_D),
pass to one word occurring infinitely often and a fixed volume sign.
The theorem's common-conjugation convergence would take its identity
PSL image to its complete holonomy. But this fixed peripheral word
has nonzero meridian coefficient2, so its complete holonomy is a
noncentral parabolic, not identity. This is a contradiction. The
argument uses the already proved marked compactness theorem, not a
newly imported volume-rigidity result. It also does not need a
uniform choice of cusp coordinates across the representations.

The finite list is fixed with D; no growing peripheral slope or
representation-dependent word is used. The original source here is
still the spectral numerator, not its filled Gaussian critical phase.

## 4. What remains to turn this into a general bound

Items 3 and 4 above have NOT been established for every sharp absolute
spectral critical stratum. The local obstruction shows why they do
not follow from the real absolute equations alone. In particular the
positive-clock pair can have two different Casimir traces at one
port even when the spectral coordinate is at a strict maximum.

One also needs honest treatment of nontrivial sign cycles, vanishing
or escaping ordinary multipliers, row/pole/terminal boundaries,
degenerate forced contacts, and the remaining diagonal/one-sided
strata. Existing endpoint hybrid reduction does not automatically
extend across those spectral sign changes.

Finally a geometric bound on all such representations would need
an original finite-source upper reduction showing that every possible
maximal rate is covered by these constructed strata. No contour,
cycle-intersection or global cancellation theorem is supplied here.
This note isolates a potentially useful fixed-peripheral gap once
the exact adapter and absolute action have been proved.
