# The lower-left corner middle integral with both connecting sides

Private research proof, 8 November 2026. The exact real middle
integral of the first type-four conditional source can be moved through
its original critical point on the entire closed lower-left outgoing
interval. Both unweighted connecting sides remain present. The result
has two separate error bounds: one below the conditional critical
action, and one already paid against the actual following source plane.

The earlier lower-output deformation assumed an outgoing real part at
least \(11/50\). Its root and curvature hypotheses are replaced here by
the newly proved closed-seam guards and correlated-height curvature.
Every postwall estimate used below is checked on the present domain.
No numerical evaluation or frozen-source edit is used.

## 1. Original finite source and statement

Retain the actual ordinary-next references and allocations of
[the corner middle reduction](research_mixed_next_lower_left_corner_middle_reduction.md).
The next own type is four. In particular,
\[
\begin{gathered}
a=x-i\eta,\qquad \frac12\le x\le\frac7{10},\qquad
0\le\eta\le\frac9{125},\\
y=q-i\xi,\qquad 0\le q\le\frac{11}{50},\qquad
\frac1{1000}\le\xi\le\frac{51}{500},\\
z=u+ih_z,\qquad u\in P=[11/50,9/20],\qquad
-.108\le h_z<0 .
\end{gathered}                                                    \tag{1}
\]
The \(y,z\) heights and reference labels keep their original
correlations. The incoming target \(a\) varies in the displayed
rectangle. The first conditional root statements below hold on its
whole containing parameter domain; the following-plane statements
use the stated actual ordinary references.

Set
\[
\begin{gathered}
r=e^{-2\pi i/N},\quad P_j=(r;r)_j,\quad p_j(V)=(rV;r)_j,\\
X=e^{-2\pi ia},\quad Y=e^{-2\pi iy},\quad Z=e^{-2\pi iz},\\
C_{1,N}(X,Y)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}
                 \frac{X^{-j}p_j(X)p_j(Y)}{P_j^3},\\
L_N=\lfloor2N/25\rfloor+1,\quad M_N=\lceil N/2\rceil,\quad
\ell_N=(L_N-\tfrac12)/N,\quad b_N=(M_N-\tfrac12)/N .
\end{gathered}                                                    \tag{2}
\]
The original canonical row and phase are
\[
\begin{split}
T_N(s;a,y)&=e^{2\pi iNas}\Pi_N(a+s)\Pi_N(y+s)
                         E_N(a)E_N(y)E_N(s)^3,\\
Q(s;a,y)&=g(a+s)+g(y+s)-3g(s)-g(a)-g(y)+2\pi ias .
\end{split}                                                       \tag{3}
\]
Here \(E_N=1/\Pi_N\) is the original reciprocal-factorial
interpolant, distinct from the finite Fourier polynomial \(d_N\).
At \(s=j/N\), \(T_N\) is exactly the summand in (2).
No carrier, action constant or level scalar is changed.

Let \(s_*=v_*+ih_*\) be the original simple zero-lift critical point,
and put
\[
\Theta(a,y)=Q(s_*;a,y),\qquad M(a,y)=\Re\Theta(a,y).
                                                               \tag{4}
\]
The symbol \(h_*\) denotes this private critical height, not the
outgoing height \(-\xi\). For the actual attached source write
\[
W_N(a;y,z,w)=U_{0,N}(w,X)d_N(Y)C_{1,N}(Y,Z).
                                                               \tag{5}
\]
The cap, Fourier polynomial and next conditional sum in (5) are
complete. The outgoing allocation remains \(|d_N(Z)|^{1/2}\).

**Theorem.** On fixed local parameter charts there is an actual
holomorphic local integral \(G_N(a,y)\) through the original critical
point, with
\[
G_N(a,y)=N^{-1}\mathcal B(a,y)e^{N\Theta(a,y)}
                       \bigl(1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})\bigr),
\qquad \mathcal B(a,y)\ne0,                                    \tag{6}
\]
where the original coefficient is given in (22) below. There are
uniform constants \(\gamma>0,C,B\), after a finite compact cover,
such that on (1)
\[
\begin{split}
&\left|W_N\{C_{1,N}(X,Y)-G_N(a,y)\}\right|\,|d_N(Z)|^{1/2}\\
&\quad\le C N^B |W_N|\,|d_N(Z)|^{1/2}
                   e^{N(M-\gamma)}
       +C N^B e^{N\{\Lambda_{123}(z)-1/1000\}} .
\end{split}                                                       \tag{7}
\]
This holds for all sufficiently large integer levels of both
parities and all real ordinary colors. The function
\(\Lambda_{123}\) is the unchanged actual following action plane.
The first term retains the literal outside source; it is not
divided by \(W_N\) at its possible zeros.

Equation (7) does not compare \(W_Ne^{N\Theta}\) with that plane.
It is not a relative asymptotic for the complete first kernel or a
bound for the whole incoming corner.

## 2. Exact source inventory and the new critical chart

The corner middle reduction proves the exact identity
\[
C_{1,N}(X,Y)=N\int_{\ell_N}^{b_N}T_N(t;a,y)\,dt+\mathcal E_N .
                                                               \tag{8}
\]
Its error includes every physical row \(j<L_N\) and \(j\ge M_N\),
both horizontal Fourier remainders at heights \(-2,+2\), and all
four weighted vertical pieces. In particular the literal last row
and all revived rows are present. That theorem proves
\[
|W_N\mathcal E_N|\,|d_N(Z)|^{1/2}
 \le C N^B e^{N\{\Lambda_{123}(z)-1/100\}}.
                                                               \tag{9}
\]
No unweighted connector has been absorbed into (9).

The [closed-seam root annulus](research_mixed_conditional_corner_root_annulus.md)
and [real-coordinate guard](research_mixed_conditional_corner_root_angle.md)
prove on all of (1)
\[
Q_s(s_*;a,y)=0,\qquad
\frac2{25}<v_*<1-x,\qquad
\frac12<|e^{2\pi is_*}|<2,\qquad
|h_*|<\frac{\log2}{2\pi}.
                                                               \tag{10}
\]
These are the original root and logarithmic lift, including \(q=0\).
The old proof that assumed \(q\ge11/50\) is not used.
The [prewall curvature theorem](research_mixed_next_lower_left_corner_prewall_curvature.md)
gives
\[
-\Re Q_{ss}(v+i\sigma;a,y)>
\frac{\pi}{540000}
\frac{\sin(2\pi v)}
     {\cosh(2\pi\sigma)-\cos(2\pi v)}>0
                                                               \tag{11}
\]
for \(2/25\le v<1-x\) and
\(|\sigma|\le\log2/(2\pi)\).
Thus the root lies in the proved strict curvature strip.

The root domain is compact. Its simplicity and all strict
separations in (10) consequently have uniform margins.
For all sufficiently large \(N\), \(\ell_N<v_*<b_N\).
At the compact line \(v=2/25\), (11) has a uniform strict margin
and all factorial arguments are regular. Continuity therefore
extends it a fixed small distance to the left. This covers the
possible negative half-grid displacement of \(\ell_N\).

The original upper rate of the corner middle reduction is
\[
\begin{split}
\mathfrak p(\zeta)&=
\begin{cases}
\Re g(\zeta),&0<\Re\zeta\le1,\\
\Re g(\zeta-1)+2\pi(\Im\zeta)_+,&1\le\Re\zeta<2,
\end{cases}\\
\widehat R(v,\sigma)&=\mathfrak p(a+v+i\sigma)
 +\Re\{g(y+v+i\sigma)-3g(v+i\sigma)-g(a)-g(y)
                         +2\pi ia(v+i\sigma)\}.
\end{split}                                                       \tag{12}
\]
It is jointly continuous, including the incoming numerator wall
and its real zeros. The exact identity
\(\Pi_N(\zeta+1)=(1-e^{-2\pi iN\zeta})\Pi_N(\zeta)\)
retains both terms of that period factor. The original midpoint
and reciprocal reflection bounds give
\[
|T_N(v+i\sigma;a,y)|
\le C_\tau N^{B_\tau}
             e^{N\{\widehat R(v,\sigma)+\tau\}}
                                                               \tag{13}
\]
for every prescribed \(\tau>0\) on the retained bounded rectangles.
This is precisely the zero-safe source estimate in the corner
middle reduction, §2. It is used only as an upper bound outside
the regular critical chart.

In particular neither \(\eta=0\) nor \(h_*=\eta\) requires division
by \(1-e^{-2\pi iNa}\). The original holomorphic \(T_N\) is used
throughout, not a separated shifted meromorphic chart.

## 3. The complete critical-height line

Before the wall, (10)–(11) make the original phase strictly
increase to \(v_*\) and strictly decrease after it on height \(h_*\).
We now check the postwall part without any outgoing lower-angle
hypothesis.

Put \(U=e^{2\pi h_*}\in(1/2,2)\), \(T=e^{2\pi\eta}<8/5\),
and \(R=|Y|\le1\). For \(1-x\le v\le1/2\), the exact horizontal
force of either upper-rate branch is
\[
\exp(2\partial_v\widehat R)=
\frac{|T-Ue^{-2\pi i(x+v)}|^2
      |1-RUe^{-2\pi i(q+v)}|^2}
     {|1-Ue^{-2\pi iv}|^6}.
                                                               \tag{14}
\]
The extra term \(2\pi(h_*-\eta)_+\) in (12) is independent of
\(v\), so it does not alter (14).

For \(U\ge1\), use \(x+v\in[1,6/5]\), \(v\in[3/10,1/2]\)
and the elementary bounds
\(\cos2\pi(x+v)>3/10\), \(\cos2\pi v<-3/10\). Then
\[
\exp(2\partial_v\widehat R)
\le
\frac{(U^2-3U/5+64/25)(1+8U/7)^2}
     {(U^2+3U/5+1)^3}<1.
                                                               \tag{15}
\]
The outgoing bound uses only \(R\le1<8/7\), not \(q\ge11/50\).
For completeness, denominator minus numerator in (15) is
\[
U^6+\frac95U^5+\frac{3398}{1225}U^4
+\frac{14173}{6125}U^3+\frac{1357}{1225}U^2
-\frac{604}{175}U-\frac{39}{25}.
\]
For \(U\ge1\), dropping positive excess coefficients and using
\(U^k\ge U\) bounds it below by
\((411U-273)/175\ge138/175>0\).
This is the corrected polynomial from
[the half-cut proof](research_mixed_conditional_half_cut_below_incoming_zero.md),
§3, whose hypotheses have just been checked here.

For \(1/2\le U\le1\), split the postwall interval at \(v=2/5\).
On \(3/10\le v\le2/5\) one has \(1\le x+v\le11/10\),
\(\cos2\pi(x+v)>4/5\), and \(\cos2\pi v<-3/10\). Convexity
in \(T\in[1,8/5]\) gives
\[
\exp(2\partial_v\widehat R)
\le
\frac{(U^2-64U/25+64/25)(1+U)^2}
     {(U^2+3U/5+1)^3}<1.
                                                               \tag{16}
\]
The larger endpoint bound in \(T\) is the one at \(8/5\):
the difference from the bound at one is \(39/25-24U/25>0\).
The exact denominator-minus-numerator polynomial is
\[
U^6+\frac95U^5+\frac{77}{25}U^4
+\frac{547}{125}U^3+\frac{141}{25}U^2
-\frac{19}{25}U-\frac{39}{25}.
\]
It has value \(2251/8000>0\) at \(U=1/2\) and increases for
\(U\ge1/2\): the quadratic and linear terms alone have derivative
\((282U-19)/25>0\), and the higher derivatives are positive.

On \(2/5\le v\le1/2\), the incoming cosine is positive and
the own cosine is less than \(-4/5\). Hence
\[
\exp(2\partial_v\widehat R)
\le
\frac{(U^2+64/25)(1+U)^2}
     {(U^2+8U/5+1)^3}<1.
                                                               \tag{17}
\]
Here the difference is
\[
U^6+\frac{24}{5}U^5+\frac{242}{25}U^4
+\frac{1462}{125}U^3+\frac{178}{25}U^2
-\frac8{25}U-\frac{39}{25}.
\]
Its last three terms equal \(3/50>0\) at \(U=1/2\) and
increase thereafter; every higher term is positive.
These are the two exact inequalities in
[the earlier lower-output deformation](research_mixed_conditional_lower_output_gaussian_deformation.md),
§2. Their outgoing estimates are only \(|1-RUe^{-i\theta}|\le1+U\).
Thus no former outgoing-angle restriction enters (16) or (17).

At a numerator zero, use the continuous primitive in (12) and
integrate its one-sided horizontal bounds. No nonvanishing symbol
is asserted there. It follows from (11), (15)–(17) that the entire
main line from \(\ell_N+ih_*\) to \(b_N+ih_*\) has its unique
maximum at \(s_*\), with value \(M\). Outside a fixed small regular
critical chart it has a uniform strict loss \(\gamma_0>0\).
Compactness and continuity of (12) supply this margin, including
the postwall portion when \(h_*=\eta\).

## 4. Both unweighted connecting sides

The exact interpolant (3) is holomorphic between the real line
and height \(h_*\). The global numerator chart, the regular
denominators and the inward-seam continuation used in the middle
reduction prove this directly. Consequently
\[
\begin{split}
N\int_{\ell_N}^{b_N}T_N(s)\,ds
={}&N\int_{\ell_N}^{\ell_N+ih_*}T_N(s)\,ds\\
&+N\int_{\ell_N+ih_*}^{b_N+ih_*}T_N(s)\,ds
 +N\int_{b_N+ih_*}^{b_N}T_N(s)\,ds .
\end{split}                                                       \tag{18}
\]
All orientations and the original positive density \(N\) are retained.
The deformation of this original chart introduces no extra residue.
Zeros of its numerator do not become poles.

On the lower connecting side, (11) and its small half-grid extension
give vertical convexity throughout the interval between \(0\) and
\(h_*\). Its upper rate is therefore bounded by the larger of its
two endpoint rates. The main endpoint is below \(M-\gamma_0\),
after reducing \(\gamma_0\) if necessary. Its real endpoint has
the original low-cut absolute cap rate at most \(2383/1500\),
with any prescribed tolerance, by the middle reduction, §5.

For the upper side first use the exact limiting half cut.
Let \(r_0=x-1/2\in[0,1/5]\) and
\(A(r,h)=\Arg(1-e^{2\pi h-2\pi ir})\) on the original strip.
The own argument at \(1/2+i\sigma\) is zero. The full rate (12)
has
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\partial_\sigma\widehat R(1/2,\sigma)
 \le-2\pi(x-q)\le-14\pi/25,&\sigma<\eta,\\[2mm]
\partial_\sigma\widehat R(1/2,\sigma)\ge0,&\sigma>\eta .
\end{array}                                                       \tag{19}
\]
Indeed below \(\eta\), the incoming shifted argument
\(A(r_0,\sigma-\eta)\) is nonnegative and the outgoing argument
at \(q+1/2\) is at least \(-2\pi q\). Above \(\eta\), the
incoming argument is at most \(\pi-2\pi r_0\), the outgoing
argument is nonpositive, and the period term contributes \(+2\pi\).
Thus the lower bound there is
\(-(\pi-2\pi r_0)-2\pi x+2\pi=0\).
At \(r_0=0\) use the one-sided limits from (12). At \(q=0\)
the outgoing argument is exactly zero, so both estimates remain
valid. These bounds hold also for negative \(\sigma\).

The half-cut rate decreases to \(\eta\) and increases thereafter.
It is continuous at the separating height. Its maximum on the
interval with endpoints \(0,h_*\) is therefore at one of those
endpoints. This includes \(h_*=\eta\) and \(\eta=0\).
For \(b_N=1/2+O(N^{-1})\), use uniform continuity of the integrated
rate, rather than differentiating a moving kink. The same endpoint
bound holds with any prescribed small tolerance. This keeps the
odd cut exactly at one half and the even cut approaching from below.

The upper main endpoint is below \(M-\gamma_0\). The upper real
endpoint, multiplied by the complete cap, has the original upper-row
rate at most \(32563/21000<2383/1500\). Both real-cut bounds are
the actual positive source bounds in the middle reduction, §5;
they do not require either cut to lie below \(M\).

It follows that the two entire unweighted sides are bounded by
the sum of a term with conditional rate \(M-\gamma\) and their
respective real-cut terms, for some uniform \(\gamma>0\).
The latter, with the literal complete cap, are eligible
index-independent preceding factors for
[the bounded-prefix attachment theorem](research_mixed_next_lower_left_bounded_prefix_four.md).
That theorem attaches the whole \(d_N(Y)C_{1,N}(Y,Z)\), retaining
the outgoing halfweight and the forcing of \(d_N\), with deficit
\(1/100\). Small finite-source and half-grid allowances can be
chosen before \(N\) tends to infinity. The weaker deficit
\(1/1000\) in (7) leaves room for all of them.

## 5. The original local Gaussian and the two errors

Only a fixed prewall neighborhood of \(s_*\) requires a nonzero
factorial symbol. There all individual and shifted index arguments
lie in fixed compact subsets of their regular strips. At \(q=0\)
the unshifted argument \(y\) uses
[the exact inward-seam factorial theorem](research_factorial_symbol_across_inward_seam.md),
which retains the original square root, action constant and \(N\)
power. Thus the actual local row has
\[
\begin{split}
T_N(s;a,y)&=N^{-3/2}b(s;a,y)e^{NQ(s;a,y)}
                          \bigl(1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})\bigr),\\
b(s;a,y)&=
\frac{a_0(a+s)a_0(y+s)}{a_0(s)^3a_0(a)a_0(y)},\qquad
a_0(\zeta)=\sqrt{2\sin\pi\zeta}\,e^{-i\pi\zeta/2}.
\end{split}                                                       \tag{20}
\]
At the seam \(a_0(y)\) is its specified inward continuation.
It is nonzero. The same is true of every other factor in (20).

Strict horizontal concavity fixes the Gaussian square root with
\(\Re\sqrt{-Q_{ss}}>0\) on the original real parameter domain.
At each base parameter choose a small analytic Morse contour
through \(s_*\), with its oriented Morse-coordinate endpoints fixed.
Its actual contribution, including \(+N\), defines \(G_N\).
Taylor expansion on that chart, or the analytic Morse coordinate,
gives
\[
G_N=N^{-1}\mathcal B(a,y)e^{N\Theta(a,y)}
                      \bigl(1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})\bigr),
\qquad
N^{-3/2}\cdot N\cdot N^{-1/2}=N^{-1},                         \tag{21}
\]
where
\[
\boxed{\qquad
\mathcal B(a,y)=
\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}\,b(s_*;a,y)}
                  {\sqrt{-Q_{ss}(s_*;a,y)}}\ne0 .
\qquad}                                                        \tag{22}
\]
The orientation is the increasing real-index orientation of (18).
No unproved cycle coefficient is assigned. Small connections from
this local contour to the critical-height line remain in the
strict-loss region of §3.

The portion of that line outside the local chart, those small
connections, and the main-end alternatives of both full sides in
(18) have conditional rate at most \(M-\gamma\). Multiplying them
by the literal outside factor (5) gives the first term of (7).
The real-end alternatives of the sides are paid by §4. The
previously discarded complete physical and weighted groups are
paid by (9). Adding these exact groups proves the second term
of (7), with its stated conservative deficit.

Replacing \(G_N\) by its leading term in (21) adds the explicit bound
\[
C N^{-2}|W_N|\,|d_N(Z)|^{1/2}e^{NM}.
                                                               \tag{23}
\]
The local Gaussian statement and its normalized fixed derivatives
hold on the fixed local complex charts just described. The actual
following-plane bound (7) is stated on the parameter and reference
set (1); no separate fixed complex neighborhood or normalized
outside-jet assertion for that global bound is needed here.

Every physical row, both horizontal aliases, four weighted sides,
both unweighted sides, and both numerator period branches have
been retained. The cap and Fourier polynomial remain their
complete finite sources. There is no outgoing halfweight removal
away from its already established actual observation domain.
The original outside measures remain \(N\,da\) and \(N\,dy\).

The outstanding action comparison is the actual product
\(W_Ne^{N\Theta}\). Equation (7) does not make its first term
small relative to the following plane without that comparison.
No complete incoming-corner, own-three, terminal-source or
whole-transfer conclusion is asserted in this note.
