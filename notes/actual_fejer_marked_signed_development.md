## 1. Exact hypotheses from the original source

  theta_j=2 atan(l_j/sqrt(l_0 l_1+l_1 l_2+l_2 l_0)).       (1)

A one-line triangle has angle zero at that line and strictly positive
angles summing pi at its finite vertices. A two-line triangle uvf has
angles (0,0,pi). Assign each triangle a sign sigma=+1 or -1. At cross
ports both signs are positive. At a left Green port the input triangle
(x,b,y) is positive and output (x,c,y) is negative; a right port reverses
these roles. The negative triangle's middle is the overloaded face.

Assume the actual shared degree equations hold. Every ordinary vertex
has demand1 and protected vertices have demand0. The [exact signed-angle formula for E](actual_fejer_half_block_entropy.md) gives, at every finite vertex f,

  sum_(triangles at f) sigma theta_(triangle,f)
                  =2pi(1-o_f),                         (2)

where o_f is the number of its overloaded-middle incidences. Original
finite feasibility gives o_f in {0,1}; protected faces have o_f=0.
No independent angle optimization has replaced the source constraints.

The two uv triangles necessarily have POSITIVE sign. Indeed uv is a
MAIN edge, so in either triangle one of u,v is its middle. A negative
triangle would overload that protected middle, which is impossible.
These two triangles have no other degeneracy than their two line faces.
