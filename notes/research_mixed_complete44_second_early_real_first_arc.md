Private written proof, 11 November 2026. The complete first real arc
from one half to one is paid for an actual prefix \(444\), while the
second coordinate is on its early interval at its original negative
height and the third remains on its original plateau. A new prewall
conditional contour retains all physical rows and sampling corrections.
No absolute estimate of the central physical rows is used.

## 3. Prewall curvature

Write
\[
 {\cal C}(u+i\sigma)=\Re\cot\pi(u+i\sigma)
 =\frac{\sin(2\pi u)}{\cosh(2\pi\sigma)-\cos(2\pi u)}.

\[

Otherwise \(u<2/5\), and \(\sin(2\pi u)>9/20\).

\(|\sigma|\). Its larger real angle makes its denominator larger.

\[

\]
which suffices. Suppose \(\sigma\ge0\), and put \(U=e^{2\pi\sigma}\).
For \(1\le U\le2\), the own hyperbolic parameter is at most \(5/4\),

Use \(\cot(7\pi/20)=\tan(3\pi/20)<2/3\) in the last range.

For \(2\le U\le4\), the outgoing radius \(RU\ge U/2\ge1\).
Its cotangent decreases with radius. At radius \(U/2\), it also
decreases with the angle here: the angle cosine is below \(23/50\),
whereas the reciprocal hyperbolic parameter is at least \(4/5\).
Its maximum is at \(R=1/2,q=1/10\).

\[
 \theta=2\pi u,\quad\phi=\pi/5,\quad c=\cos\theta,\ s_\theta=\sin\theta,
 \quad C=\cos(\theta+\phi),\ S=\sin(\theta+\phi).

 K(U)=\frac{S}{s_\theta}
             \frac{2(U^2+1-2Uc)}{U^2+4-4UC}.                       \tag{18}

It increases strictly on \([2,4]\). Its derivative has the sign of

For \(1/4\le u<2/5\), both braces in (19) are positive.

On \(3/40\le u\le2/25\), retain the incoming cotangent.

 9/20<s_\theta<1/2,\quad6/7<c<25/28,\quad C<23/50.                  \tag{21}
