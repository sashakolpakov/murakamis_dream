# The complete selected-left incoming corner

Private research proof candidate, 8 November 2026. The original incoming
corner is paid as an oriented union on the selected left interval for
actual complete words beginning \(343\), of length at least five.
The proof keeps the complete cap, both conditional kernels, the forced
Fourier polynomial, all physical rows, and every canonical current.
The new selected-corner geometry is a separate input, and this note
requires its full review before adoption. No mathematical numerical
evaluation or frozen-source edit is used.

## 1. Actual source and replacement path

Use exactly the actual reference and notation of

In particular
\[
\begin{gathered}

z=u+ih_z,\quad 3/10\le u\le39/100,\\

X=e^{-2\pi ia},\quad Y=e^{-2\pi iy},\quad Z=e^{-2\pi iz},
\qquad R=|Y|=e^{\pi/50}<16/15 .
\end{gathered}
\tag{1}
\]
The reference correlations, original action \(\Lambda_{123}(z)\),
and ordinary real colors \(w\in[-2,2]\) are unchanged. There is no
outgoing halfweight for the following own-three factor.

Let \(D=N-1,\ r=e^{-2\pi i/N},\ P_j=(r;r)_j,\ p_j(V)=(rV;r)_j\),
and retain
\[

             \frac{V^{-j}p_j(V)p_j(W)}{P_j^{2+\beta}} .
\]
The full degree-\(D\) polynomial \(d_N\) has its original values
\(d_N(r^b)=P_b^{-1}\). Put
\[

U_{0,N}(w,X)C_{1,N}(X,Y)d_N(Y)Y^{-N}C_{0,N}(Y,Z).
\tag{2}
\]

to \(7/10\), are

\Gamma_{\rm new}&=[a_L,7/10+ih_a]\cup[7/10+ih_a,7/10].
\end{split}
\tag{3}
\]

**Theorem.** For both parities, all real ordinary colors, and all
sufficiently large integer levels, uniformly on (1),
\[
|\mathcal D_N(a)|\le CN^B e^{N\{\Lambda_{123}(z)-1/4000\}}
\quad(a\in\Gamma_{\rm new}),
\qquad
\left|N\int_{\Gamma_{\rm old}}\mathcal D_N(a)\,da\right|
\le CN^B e^{N\{\Lambda_{123}(z)-1/4000\}}.
\tag{4}
\]
The second assertion concerns the original union, not absolute
estimates on its two old pieces. No outside-parameter derivative,
full shared-cycle, or transfer noncancellation statement is made.

Here is a positive reduction for all first upper physical rows

For \(v\ge1/4\), the logarithms on the omitted complementary

## 4. Six weighted canonical currents

Retain exactly the original interpolation and its continuous rate:
\[
\begin{split}
T_N(s;a,y)&=e^{2\pi iNas}\Pi_N(a+s)\Pi_N(y+s)
                         E_N(a)E_N(y)E_N(s)^3,\\
\widehat R(v,\sigma)&=\mathfrak p(a+s)+
\Re\{g(y+s)-3g(s)-g(a)-g(y)+2\pi ias\},
\quad s=v+i\sigma,\\
\mathfrak p(z)&=
\begin{cases}\Re g(z),&0<\Re z\le1,\\
\Re g(z-1)+2\pi(\Im z)_+,&1\le\Re z<2 .
\end{cases}
\end{split}
\tag{20}
\]
At \(s=j/N\), \(T_N\) is the literal first row. Both terms of
\(\Pi_N(z+1)=(1-e^{-2\pi iNz})\Pi_N(z)\) are retained.
For bounded heights \(-8\le\sigma\le8\), the original zero-safe
numerator estimates and reciprocal midpoint estimates give the
upper rate (20). At \(q=0\), the unshifted \(y=iH\) is in a
fixed upper half-plane compact set. The original
[upper half-plane factorial theorem](research_factorial_uniform_halfplane_continuation.md)
provides its continued symbol and upper bound with the same
chirp and primitive constant. No entireness-only asymptotic or
relative approximation for \(d_N\) is used.

Set \(A(v,h)=\Arg(1-e^{2\pi h-2\pi iv})\) on \(0<v<1\).
Define \(J_{\rm eff}=-\partial_\sigma\widehat R\), using
one-sided values at a wall. We claim
\[
J_{\rm eff}\le19\pi/10\quad(\sigma\le0),\qquad
J_{\rm eff}\ge-46\pi/25\quad(\sigma\ge0),
\quad c\le v\le1/2 .
\tag{21}
\]

Before the incoming wall, for \(\sigma\le-H\) the incoming
argument is nonpositive and the outgoing one is at most
\(\pi(1/2-q-v)_+\). Discarding the nonnegative own argument
gives \(J_{\rm eff}\le2\pi x+\pi(1/2-v)\le19\pi/10\).
For \(-H\le\sigma\le0\), put
\(r_0=e^{2\pi\sigma}\ge1/R>15/16\), \(\theta=2\pi v\),
and \(B=A(v,\sigma)>0\). If \(q+v\ge1/2\), the outgoing
argument is already nonpositive. Otherwise it is at most
\(\pi-\theta\), and
\[
B\ge(\pi-\theta)/3 .
\tag{22}
\]
To verify this, if \(\theta\le\pi/2\), perturb
\(1-e^{-i\theta}\) by at most \(1/16\).
Its modulus is greater than \(\sin(\pi/10)>3/10\), so the
argument change is less than \(\arcsin(5/24)<\pi/12\).
Its original argument is \((\pi-\theta)/2\), proving (22).
If \(\theta\ge\pi/2\), put \(\phi=\pi-\theta\). The derivative
of \(\Arg(1+r_0e^{i\phi})\) in \(\phi\in[0,\pi/2]\) is
at least \(r_0^2/(1+r_0^2)\ge225/481>1/3\).
Integrate from zero. Thus the outgoing argument is at most
three own arguments, giving \(J_{\rm eff}\le2\pi x\).

After the incoming wall, \(v\ge3/10\). For \(\sigma\le0\),
the incoming continued argument is at most \(\pi/2\).
The outgoing argument is nonpositive if \(q+v\ge1/2\).
Otherwise angular monotonicity on \(v\ge3/10\), and
\[
A(v,\log(Rr_0)/(2\pi))\le R A(v,\log r_0/(2\pi)),
\]
bound it by \(16/15\) times the own argument. The last inequality
follows by integrating the radial derivative
\(\sin(2\pi v)/(1+s^2-2s\cos2\pi v)\): its denominator increases
with \(s\ge0\) because \(\cos2\pi v<0\).
There is no period term for \(\sigma\le0\), so again
\(J_{\rm eff}\le\pi/2+2\pi x\le19\pi/10\).

For \(\sigma\ge0\) before the wall, the elementary original
argument bounds give
\[
J_{\rm eff}/\pi\ge-2+4v+\min(0,1-2q-2v)\ge-9/5 .
\]
After the wall and below \(\eta\), its incoming argument is
nonnegative, giving an even stronger lower bound. Above \(\eta\)
the exact period term subtracts \(2\pi\) from \(J_{\rm eff}\).
Using \(x+v\ge1\) gives
\[
J_{\rm eff}/\pi\ge-3+4v+\min(0,1-2q-2v)\ge-46/25 .
\]
For the last comparison, its two cases have lower bounds
\(-9/5\) and \(-61/25+2v\ge-46/25\).
This proves (21), with the actual numerator-period branch retained.

Choose alias heights \(-8,+8\). Each weighted vertical rate
\(\widehat R-2\pi|\sigma|\) decreases away from its real cut,
with reserves \(\pi/10\) below and \(4\pi/25\) above.
The real lower cut is paid by (10), and the real upper cut
by (15). Thus all four weighted half-sides are paid.

For the horizontal aliases use the elementary real-row ceiling
\[
\widehat R(v,0)<(7/4)v-3J(v)\le11/8 .
\tag{23}
\]
The incoming folded logarithm is less than one, the selected
outgoing logarithm less than \(3/4\), and \(-J(v)<1/6\).
The smaller of the two alias losses in (21) is \(4\pi/5>12/5\).
After (6), either horizontal has preceding rate strictly below
\[
1549/1500+11/8-12/5=23/3000<B_f .
\tag{24}
\]
Its complete next attachment is therefore paid as well.

The original cotangent identity now leaves exactly
\[
C_{1,N}(X,Y)=N\int_{\ell_N}^{b_N}T_N(s;a,y)\,ds+\mathcal E_N ,
\tag{25}
\]
where \(\mathcal E_N\) consists of the low and upper physical
blocks, two horizontal aliases, and four weighted vertical pieces.
All these terms, with the literal outside source
\(U_{0,N}d_N(Y)Y^{-N}C_{0,N}\), have a common finite gap
at least \(1/3000\). Half-grid shifts use integrated-log continuity
at the incoming numerator wall; their tolerances are chosen
inside the strict displayed reserves. Every residue and the
positive sampling factor \(N\) are unchanged.

## 7. Attachment of the critical term and exact incoming cancellation

Equations (31)--(32) of the selected-corner geometry prove
\[
B_*+\Re\Theta(a_L,y)+\varphi+W_\nu
 <\Lambda_{123}(z)-1/100
\quad(\varphi\in\mathscr P).
\tag{35}
\]
Their proof uses the actual pointwise profile theorem at \(a_L\),
before any signed sum. The same original branch has
\(\Re\Theta_a<0,\Im\Theta_a>0\). Hence on \(\Gamma_{\rm new}\),
\[
\Re\Theta(a,y)\le\Re\Theta(a_L,y).
\tag{36}
\]
Combining (5), (7), and (35)--(36) pays the local term (34)
and all its conditional strict-loss remainders with the complete
outside factor
\(U_{0,N}d_N(Y)Y^{-N}C_{0,N}\).
The two real-end alternatives of the joins were paid in
(10),(15). All physical and weighted terms were paid in
Sections 3--4. Choose the finitely many rate tolerances within
these fixed reserves. Summing the exact groups proves the
pointwise estimate in (4), with its conservative gap \(1/4000\).
No division by the cap, a Fourier weight, or another source
that may vanish occurs.

For fixed \(N,w,y,z\), the literal density (2) is entire in \(a\):
its two \(a\)-dependent factors are finite Laurent polynomials in
\(X=e^{-2\pi ia}\), which never vanishes at finite \(a\).
Cauchy's theorem on the actual intervening rectangle gives exactly
\[
N\int_{\Gamma_{\rm old}}\mathcal D_N(a)\,da
=N\int_{\Gamma_{\rm new}}\mathcal D_N(a)\,da .
\tag{37}
\]
On the upward right side \(da=i\,d(\Im a)\).
The path lengths and the factor \(N\) cost only a polynomial,
so the pointwise estimate proves the second assertion in (4).

The old incoming corner is paid only in this oriented union.
The full first incoming cycle can be assembled only with the
separately proved initial, taper, central, right-horizontal and
far-real pieces, on the same actual reference. The old filter
correction and both Fourier coefficients remain unchanged.
This theorem does not remove the paired right remainder or
prove noncancellation of the complete transfer.
