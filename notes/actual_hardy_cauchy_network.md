# Exact Hardy replacement of the internal finite-head projectors

28 October 2026. The original scalar Euler/Gaussian network admits an
exact simplification: **every internal finite Dirichlet kernel can be
replaced by an ordinary Hardy Cauchy kernel**, provided its existing
finite factorial and Gaussian kernels are retained. This holds for
every signed braid word and its full original source. With the
proved fused plat source, the boundary is monomial as well.

Thus the Dirichlet numerator branches are unnecessary in this
presentation of the complete word. This does not remove the actual
finite Euler/Gaussian endpoint sectors. Their support is essential
to the proof. Nor does the theorem identify a new geometric edge's
three indices with a charged degree.

The [exact audit](../experiments/audit_actual_hardy_cauchy_network.py)
and [results](../experiments/actual_hardy_cauchy_network_results.json)
check the full original signed crossing matrices and original
whole-word pivotal cuts while allowing nonzero intermediate
indices beyond the head.

## 1. The actual operators, extended without an upper head projection

Let $\mathcal P=\mathbb C[z]$, and let $H_N$ be its subspace of
degree below $N$. On $\mathcal P$ use the unilateral shifts

$$
 J_+z^a=z^{a+1},\qquad
 J_-z^a=\begin{cases}z^{a-1}&a>0,\\0&a=0.\end{cases}
$$

They have no upper cutoff. Define the ORIGINAL finite polynomials

$$
 u_N(t)=\sum_{h=0}^{N-1}\frac{t^h}{F_h},\qquad
 v_N(t)=\sum_{h=0}^{N-1}
       \frac{(-1)^hQ^{h(h-1)/2}t^h}{F_h},
 \qquad F_h=(Q;Q)_h.
$$

On a pair put

$$
 U_-^\infty=u_N(x^{-1}J_-\otimes J_+),\quad
 V_-^\infty=v_N(x^{-1}J_-\otimes J_+),
$$

and define $U_+^\infty,V_+^\infty$ by reversing the two shifts.
Only the lower polynomial wall is imposed.

Extend each existing finite diagonal kernel by zero outside its
ORIGINAL active-head support:

$$
\begin{split}
 \mathsf Fz^a&=F_az^a,\quad
 \mathsf F^{-}z^a=F_a^{-1}z^a &&(0\le a<N),\\
 \mathsf Fz^a&=\mathsf F^{-}z^a=0 &&(a\ge N).
\end{split}                                                   \tag{1}
$$

The superscript minus here is the zero-extended finite inverse
diagonal, not an inverse on $\mathcal P$. Likewise retain
$\mathsf D,\mathsf D^{-}$ with their original nonzero entries
for $0\le a<N$ and zero entries above the head. Spectator
heads are identities on all nonnegative indices.

No new cutoff has been added to (1): these are exactly the
finite scalar kernels already present in the
[literal network](actual_euler_gaussian_cauchy_network.md).

## 2. The support lemma removes the internal upper projections

Let $P$ project onto $H_N\otimes H_N$ and put

$$
 C^\infty=\mathsf F_2U_-^\infty\mathsf F_1\mathsf F_2^{-},
 \qquad
 \overline C^\infty=
       \mathsf F_2\mathsf F_1^{-}V_-^\infty\mathsf F_2^{-}.
                                                            \tag{2}
$$

These are the original strict fusion and inverse-fusion words.
Their support has the following exact properties:

$$
 C^\infty=PC^\infty P,\qquad
 \overline C^\infty=P\overline C^\infty,\qquad
 C^\infty|_{H_N^2}=C,\qquad
 \overline C^\infty|_{H_N^2}=C^{-1}.                  \tag{3}
$$

For the first operator the two right diagonal factors kill
inputs outside the head. Its transfer only lowers the first
index, so that index stays in the head; the left $\mathsf F_2$
kills every second-index overflow. For the second operator
the two left diagonals enforce its output support. On a
head input, an overflow generated by $V_-^\infty$ is precisely
the second-index overflow killed by $\mathsf F_2$. These are
the same coefficient sums as the finite fusion and its
finite inverse.

Keep the original Cartan scalar $\kappa$, and define

$$
\begin{split}
 R_+^\infty&=\kappa C^\infty
                     \mathsf D U_+^\infty\overline C^\infty,\\
 R_-^\infty&=\kappa^{-1}C^\infty
                     V_+^\infty\mathsf D^{-}\overline C^\infty.
\end{split}                                                   \tag{4}
$$

Then for the actual inclusion $i:H_N^2\to\mathcal P^2$,

$$
 \boxed{\qquad R_\pm^\infty i=iR_\pm.\qquad}           \tag{5}
$$

Indeed $\overline C^\infty i=iC^{-1}$. The transfer $U_+^\infty$
or $V_+^\infty$ can overflow only its first index. Before the
next transfer, $C^\infty$ applies its input support $P$, which
kills that overflow. Its surviving part is exactly the
truncated $U_+$ or $V_+$ in the original strict factorization.

The two extensions in (4) need not be inverses on the whole
polynomial space. Their restrictions to the original head are
the prescribed signed inverse crossings. That is sufficient
for an arbitrary signed word.

The proof is unchanged for unequal colors in the actual
[typed strict formula](actual_rogers_bridge_transport.md):
$C_x^{-1}$ on input, $C_y$ on output, the original $D_{x,y}$,
and the stated $x^{-1}$ or $y^{-1}$ Euler argument. Their
coefficients change; the support argument does not. All
unrolled common Cartan and external normalization lines
multiply the same two realizations.

### Every nonempty upper-tail branch vanishes in the complete crossing

One may replace ANY subset of the original internal finite projections
by Hardy projections, not merely all of them at once. To see this,
follow the three transfer stages in (4), in traversal order.

1. The inverse-fusion $V_-^\infty$ can overflow only head two.
   The following $\mathsf F_2$ kills that overflow before the
   central transfer.
2. The central $U_+^\infty$ or $V_+^\infty$ can overflow only
   head one. The input $\mathsf F_1$ of the final fusion kills
   it before the final $U_-^\infty$ transfer.
3. The final $U_-^\infty$ can overflow only head two, which the
   last $\mathsf F_2$ kills.

The remaining factors between these barriers are diagonal; they
cannot return an overflow to the head. Spectator heads remain in
their original finite heads. Earlier killing by any additional
finite projection therefore removes only paths that the barrier
would already kill.

On Laurent inputs the exact decomposition is

$$
 \Pi_N=\Pi_+-\Pi_{\ge N},\qquad
 \Pi_{\ge N}(z,w)=\frac{(z/w)^N}{1-z/w}.               \tag{5a}
$$

In the nested Hardy chamber, expand the original finite projection
factors using (5a). **Every term containing at least one upper-tail
factor has zero full-crossing action on the original head.**
The same assertion holds for boundary fusion factors and for every
original full word by (6).

This is an ordered barrier identity. It is not an assertion that
an arbitrary endpoint coupon or Ward current is zero. Inserting a
new operator between a transfer and its barrier can invalidate it.

## 3. Whole-word and source identity

For $b$ heads write $i_b:H_N^{\otimes b}\to\mathcal P^{\otimes b}$.
Embedding the two-head operators of (4) at every letter gives
for EVERY signed braid word

$$
 \boxed{\quad W^\infty(\beta)i_b=i_bW(\beta).\quad}     \tag{6}
$$

This follows by induction from (5); no intermediate finite-head
projector is used. Every original input/output entry, pivotal
partial trace, marked source, peripheral function and external
framing line is consequently preserved.

The same inclusion intertwines any original finite-head coupon
inserted BETWEEN complete crossings, provided its extension
restricts to that coupon on the head. This includes the original
Hopf currents, so the full Ward identities and their original
source pairings are preserved. It does not assert the same
statement for an arbitrary coupon inserted between the elementary
factors of one crossing.

The original closed-braid boundary still has its prescribed
finite pivotal sum. Alternatively use the already proved full
fused $2k$-plat formula

$$
 P_{\beta,N}=\chi_{\beta,N}N^{1-k}
       \langle e_{\rm out},B_\beta e_{\rm in}\rangle.  \tag{7}
$$

Here every input pair is $(d,0)$; every ordinary output pair
is $(0,d)$; the marked output pair is $(d,0)$.
Expand $B_\beta$ into its actual pair fusions, signed
crossings and retained original scalar lines. Apply (3)--(6).
The result is exactly (7) on the polynomial space, with
monomial input and coefficient-extraction output.

The Ward corner in (7) is the theorem for the FULL assembled
intertwining word. It is not applied separately to a Hardy,
cyclic, or sewing summand.

## 4. The scalar integral has no finite Dirichlet numerators

Let

$$
 K_+(t)=\frac1{1-t},\qquad |t|<1.
$$

This is the Hardy reproducing kernel for nonnegative powers.
For example the transfer kernel on two heads is now

$$
 \prod_j K_+(z_j/w_j)\,
                   u_N(x^{-1}w_2/w_1),              \tag{8}
$$

with $v_N$ and the reversed argument for the other transfers.
The diagonal kernels are exactly the existing finite ones:

$$
 \mathsf F^{-}:u_N(z/w),\qquad
 \mathsf F:N(z/w)^{N-1}v_N(Qw/z),\qquad
 \mathsf D^{\pm}:\Gamma_{\pm,N}(z/w).                 \tag{9}
$$

Their spectators contribute $K_+$ instead of $K_N$.
Thus **all internal factors $(1-t^N)/(1-t)$ are replaced by
$1/(1-t)$**, without approximating or discarding a period.
The proof is the complete ordered support identity above,
not replacement of one split reproducing residue in isolation.

For monomial boundary data $e_a,e_c$ and a word with $L$
elementary factors, its entry is

$$
 \operatorname{CT}_{z^0,\ldots,z^L}
  (z^0)^a(z^L)^{-c}
      \prod_{\ell=1}^{L}K^\infty_\ell(z^\ell,z^{\ell-1}).
                                                            \tag{10}
$$

Choose nested positive radii
$|z^\ell_j|<|z^{\ell-1}_j|$. Every Hardy series converges
there; every other factor is a finite Laurent polynomial.
The CT is consequently a literal rational Cauchy integral.
There are a fixed number of variables for a fixed plat,
independent of $N$.

No cycle made solely of Hardy inequalities is being imposed:
the boundary in (10) is monomial. The initial/final finite
indices and every scalar in (7) remain the original ones.

## 5. Exact gain and the remaining geometric charge

This eliminates a specific exponential branch family from the
original scalar Cauchy presentation: the finite Dirichlet
numerators and their artificial split poles. Nonzero
intermediate overflow routes are allowed and then killed by
the support of the original diagonal kernels.

The earlier source-visible coupon $X_-X_+$ has no intervening
finite diagonal barrier: an upper excursion can return before
it is tested. Its nonzero endpoint correction is therefore
consistent with this theorem. Likewise the cyclic-sewing and
Fermat-cap walls are different relative terms and have not been
deleted here.

It does not eliminate the finite endpoints of $u_N,v_N$ or
$\Gamma_{\pm,N}$. The diagonal support in (1) is essential;
replacing those kernels by unrelated nonfinite symbols is
outside the theorem. The Hardy poles in (8) are also genuine
reproducing poles; contour transport must retain their actual
residues.

All elementary transfers preserve total INTEGER degree.
Hence (10) retains the original total plat degree $k(N-1)$,
and after ordinary caps the remaining pair has the exact
affine charge $D+1=N$ from
[the charged cap theorem](actual_degree_carry_and_cap_winding_obstruction.md).
The theorem does not impose that pair charge at every
intermediate slice: crossings between pairs move degree
between them.

What is still missing is the source-preserving identification
of a geometric Pachner edge's three angle clocks with that
augmented charged class. The Hardy replacement supplies
a simpler exact whole-word integral on which to seek it;
it does not prove $h_1+h_2+h_3=N$ for an unassigned
geometric edge or select the hyperbolic critical cycle.
