# Exact height currents and local recentering of the polarized source

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

Changing a frozen height in the contour-polarized family produces an
explicit original endpoint current. It contains a sine of the charge
offset and vanishes at unit charge. Its exponential majorant is the
same one used for the original vertical current. Consequently nearby
height tables in the certified small-charge region define sources
that are exponentially equivalent, including all asymptotic
coefficients. This is a local source transport statement; it supplies
no new global boundary estimate along an arbitrary charge path.

## 1. Exact one-coordinate current

Use the carrier-removed operators of the pattern-adapted initializer.
Let
\[
 \eta=(-1)^{N-1},\quad N\ge2,\quad
 e^{iNa}=-\eta,\quad 0<a\le\pi,\quad
 k=N(1-\chi),\quad K=-\frac{N^2}{\pi i},
\]
\[
 g(t)=\sin t\,A(t)e^{inNt^2/(4\pi)},\qquad
 W_N(t)=\frac{e^{-iNt}}{(1-\eta e^{-iNt})^2}.
 \tag{1}
\]
The spectator \(A\) is even and holomorphic on the required angular
rectangle. It is held fixed when differentiating height. Put
\[
 T_\sigma^h=\mathcal H_\sigma^h+\mathcal V_{\cos}^h,
 \qquad \sigma\in\{+1,-1\},\qquad h>0,
 \tag{2}
\]
retaining either of the original signed vertical-side conventions.
The exact identity
\(\mathcal L=\mathcal O+\mathcal H_{\cos}^h+
\mathcal V_{\cos}^h\) makes the cosine sum independent of height.
Therefore
\[
 \partial_hT_\sigma^h[A]
 =\partial_h(\mathcal H_\sigma^h-\mathcal H_{\cos}^h)[A].
 \tag{3}
\]
Since \(e^{\sigma ikt}-\cos(kt)=\sigma i\sin(kt)\), differentiation
of the horizontal integral at fixed real cuts gives
\[
 \partial_hT_\sigma^h[A]
 =\sigma K\{g(a-ih)\sin(k(a-ih))W_N(a-ih)
            -g(-a-ih)\sin(k(-a-ih))W_N(-a-ih)\}.
 \tag{4}
\]
Here the moving endpoints contribute the factor \(-i\). The function
\(g(t)\sin(kt)W_N(t)\) is even, and the physical midpoint gives
\[
 W_N(a-ih)=W_N(a+ih)=-\frac{\eta}{4\cosh^2(Nh/2)}.
 \tag{5}
\]
Thus the exact current is
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 J_\sigma^h[A]:=\partial_hT_\sigma^h[A]
 =-\frac{\sigma\eta K}{4\cosh^2(Nh/2)}
   \{g(a-ih)\sin(k(a-ih))-g(a+ih)\sin(k(a+ih))\}.
 \quad}
 \tag{6}
\]
One may replace \(a\) by \(\epsilon a\), \(\epsilon=\pm1\), if
the right side is also multiplied by \(\epsilon\). This is exactly
the original signed-side orientation, and permits the side chosen
from the base crossing power in the initializer proof.

For any two positive heights whose intervening rectangle is
admissible,
\[
                    T_\sigma^{h_1}-T_\sigma^{h_0}
                        =\int_{h_0}^{h_1}J_\sigma^h\,dh.
 \tag{7}
\]
This includes both endpoint values of the original spectator. No
factorial representative is substituted at those endpoints. Equations
(6)--(7) are entire in \(\chi,n\), and are divisible by \(1-\chi\)
as entire functions. In particular changing this height has no effect
at \(\chi=1\), with every physical and outside normal retained.

## 2. Patternwise assembly and a quantitative kernel bound

Let \(\mathbf h=(h_v^S)_{S\subseteq I,\,v\notin S}\) be the height
table in the pattern-adapted source, denoted here by
\(\mathfrak Z_{N,\boldsymbol\sigma}^{\mathbf h}\). With its
prescribed polynomial numerator \(F_N\),
\[
 \partial_{h_v^S}\mathfrak Z_{N,\boldsymbol\sigma}^{\mathbf h}
 =\left[\prod_{u\in S}\mathcal O_u
        \prod_{w\notin S\cup\{v\}}T_{\sigma_w}^{h_w^S}
          J_{\sigma_v}^{h_v^S}\right]F_N.
 \tag{8}
\]
All outside arguments and all parameters of the operators are kept
as in the source definition. In particular \(\mathcal O_u\) still
includes its literal full physical angular derivative. Those
derivatives commute with the finite integrals and endpoint
evaluations in (8). The spectator remains even in the coordinate
where (6) is used.

Integrating (8) along the straight path between two admissible height
tables, or telescoping one entry at a time, gives the exact full-source
difference. Each summand has a factor \(1-\chi_v\) at its changed
vertex. Thus the difference belongs to the entire ideal generated by
those charge offsets; a product of all offsets is not asserted.
Averaging the signs restores zero current, as it must for the
original height-independent cosine source.

The sine factor has a useful uniform bound. For \(t=\epsilon a\pm ih\),
\[
 \frac{|\sin(N(1-\chi)t)|}{4\cosh^2(Nh/2)}
 \le \min\{1,N|1-\chi|\sqrt{a^2+h^2}\}\,
   e^{-N\{(1-|1-\Re\chi|)h-a|\Im\chi|\}}.
 \tag{9}
\]
Indeed \(|\sin z|\le\min\{1,|z|\}e^{|\Im z|}\), and
\(4\cosh^2(Nh/2)\ge e^{Nh}\). For real \(0\le\chi\le1\),
(9) becomes
\[
 \frac{|\sin(N(1-\chi)t)|}{4\cosh^2(Nh/2)}
 \le\min\{1,N|1-\chi|\sqrt{a^2+h^2}\}\,e^{-N\chi h}.
 \tag{10}
\]
The exponential factor is precisely the original vertical-current
majorant. Consequently
\[
 |J_\sigma^h[A]|
 \le\frac{N^2}{\pi}
       \min\{1,N|1-\chi|\sqrt{a^2+h^2}\}
       e^{-N\{(1-|1-\Re\chi|)h-a|\Im\chi|\}}
       \bigl(|g(a-ih)|+|g(a+ih)|\bigr).
 \tag{11}
\]
The actual spectator and crossing growth on both sides remain in
(11). Exact vanishing at \(\chi=1\) does not turn an
\(O(N^{-1})\) approach to that charge into an exponential gain.

## 3. Local exponential equivalence and recentering

Fix a same-index graph, base real powers \(\mathbf b\), complete
outside angles, and a base small charge
\(\boldsymbol\xi\in(1/4000,1/2000)^I\), as in the adopted
pattern-adapted initializer. Let \(\mathbf h^0\) be its table of
reduced-saddle heights, and let
\[
 S(\boldsymbol\chi)
       =\Phi_{\mathbf b,\boldsymbol\chi}(\mathbf t^*(\boldsymbol\chi))
 \tag{12}
\]
be its marked holomorphic action.

There exist \(\rho,\gamma>0\), a bounded complex neighborhood \(U\)
of \(\boldsymbol\xi\), and constants \(C,p\) such that any two real
height tables \(\mathbf h,\widehat{\mathbf h}\) within \(\rho\) of
\(\mathbf h^0\) satisfy
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 |\mathfrak Z_{N,\boldsymbol\sigma}^{\mathbf h}
       (\mathbf b,\boldsymbol\chi)
   -\mathfrak Z_{N,\boldsymbol\sigma}^{\widehat{\mathbf h}}
       (\mathbf b,\boldsymbol\chi)|
 \le C N^p\|\mathbf h-\widehat{\mathbf h}\|_1
                  e^{N(\Re S(\boldsymbol\chi)-\gamma)}
           \quad(\boldsymbol\chi\in U).
 \quad}
 \tag{13}
\]
The bound holds through both parities and for every sign vector.
The outside values in this assertion are the complete ones; no
arbitrary-exterior gap is claimed.

To prove it, first evaluate a derivative (8) at the base parameters
and table. Expanding the remaining \(T\)'s into horizontals and
verticals gives exactly the contour patterns in the small-charge
proof, with at least one actual endpoint face. At the changed
coordinate, (10) has the same pointwise exponential envelope as the
old vertical density. If \(S\ne\varnothing\), the original centered
outer-row kernel bound and its mixed normals apply at the reduced
heights \(h^S\). If \(S=\varnothing\), the current provides a
nonempty vertical subset. The archived strict outer-row and
mixed-current gaps therefore bound every term by
\(C N^p e^{N(\Re S(\boldsymbol\xi)-\gamma_0)}\), with
\(\gamma_0>0\). A minus horizontal sign only improves its real
small-charge envelope.

Choose \(\rho\) so all heights stay positive and below the original
thin-rectangle bound \(1/200\). The retained angular domains are
compact; the actual holomorphic kernel upper phases and the crossing
quadratics change by \(O(\rho)\). The centered outer-row bound has
the same uniformity and retains its first normals. Shrinking
\(\rho\) preserves a fixed part of every strict gap along the full
interpolation between the two height tables. A small complex charge
change costs at most \(e^{C_1N\varepsilon}\) in each of (9) and the
other coupon bounds, with only polynomial normal cost. Shrink
\(U\) to keep this cost and the change of \(\Re S\) below the
remaining gaps. This gives a uniform bound for (8). Its integral
along the height-table segment proves (13).

The normalized difference \(e^{-NS}\Delta\mathfrak Z_N\) is
holomorphic in the charges. Cauchy estimates on smaller neighborhoods
therefore retain the exponential gap for every fixed holomorphic
derivative. In particular all-plus sources from these nearby tables
have the same complete asymptotic expansion and the same nonzero
coefficient \(2^m A_\Gamma\). The suppressed sign families remain
suppressed. Since the reduced-saddle heights depend continuously on
the real base charge, sufficiently close small-charge base points
can recenter their height tables with this exact exponentially small
correction on their common certified neighborhood.

No claim in (13) follows from the sine factor alone. Extending this
recentring along a general charge path requires an actual endpoint
face estimate beyond the presently certified region. Positivity of a
local classical Hessian is not substituted for that estimate.

## 4. Full-midpoint specialization and the off-lattice defect

Set \(a=\pi\) and now require the literal spectral spectator
\(A(t)=A^\sharp(-2\cos t)\), with its ordinary polynomial retained.
Then, exactly,
\[
 A(\pi-ih)=A(\pi+ih)=A^\sharp(2\cosh h)=:A_h.
 \tag{14}
\]
Evenness alone would not imply (14); no equality of raw factorial
representatives is used. Put
\[
 E=e^{inN(\pi^2-h^2)/(4\pi)},\qquad b=nNh/2.
\]
The bracket in (6) is
\[
 2A_h\sinh h\,E
 \{\cos(\pi k)\sinh(kh)\sinh b
          +i\sin(\pi k)\cosh(kh)\cosh b\}.
 \tag{15}
\]
This follows from \(\sin(\pi\mp ih)=\pm i\sinh h\) and the
Gaussian multipliers \(E e^{\pm b}\). Formula (6), with its original
normalization, multiplies (15) by
\(-\sigma\eta K/[4\cosh^2(Nh/2)]\).

At zero power it simplifies to
\[
 J_{\sigma,0,\chi}^h[A]
 =\frac{\sigma\eta N^2}{2\pi}
       A_h\frac{\sinh h\cosh(kh)}{\cosh^2(Nh/2)}\sin(\pi k).
 \tag{16}
\]
Thus every finite height difference vanishes when \(n=0\) and
\(k=N(1-\chi)\in\mathbb Z\). This includes the physical charge
lattice at both parities. The identity tensorizes in changed
zero-power coordinates because the other operations leave an
ordinary spectral polynomial spectator in that coordinate.

There is a precise off-lattice failure. At such an integer \(k\),
with the spectator held fixed in this charge coordinate,
\[
 \left.\partial_\chi J_{\sigma,0,\chi}^h[A]\right|_{k\in\mathbb Z}
 =-\frac{\sigma\eta N^3}{2}(-1)^k A_h
       \frac{\sinh h\cosh(kh)}{\cosh^2(Nh/2)}.
 \tag{17}
\]
For \(h>0\) this is nonzero whenever \(A_h\ne0\). Hence lattice
height independence is not height independence on a complex charge
neighborhood. The spectator in (14)--(17) is still the full original
source evaluated at \(2\cosh h\), not a deleted graph or a controlled
geometric amplitude.
