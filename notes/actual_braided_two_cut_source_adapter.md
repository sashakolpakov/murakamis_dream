# A braided second-cut co-source with its actual pivotal weights

30 October 2026. finite-source theorem. The literal second identity
cut cannot be replaced by another normalized boundary state, or even by
an untransported pivotal marginal. There is an exact repair: retain the
actual braid carrying the selected wire to the exposed position. Its
conjugated matrix-unit insertion gives a second co-source for the SAME
whole marked scalar. The original pivot, all exchange bands, and all
genuine quantum derivatives remain explicit.

This is a global finite-source identity. It does not prove that this
co-source has a nonzero coefficient on the selected geometric framing
fiber, or replace the two-cut tensor by simultaneous point evaluation.

## 1. The genuine generic source and its original first cut

Fix N, d=N-1, q0=exp(pi i/N), and zeta=q0 exp(epsilon). Use the
original ordinary highest-weight-d module and the ACTUAL generic
physical cup. Its pivotal matrix is

    g(epsilon)=(-1)^d K(epsilon),
    g_a(epsilon)=(-1)^d zeta^(d-2a),
    D_N(epsilon)=Tr g=(-1)^d[N]_zeta.                  (1)

At the root this is the original K^(1-N), on every physical index.
Away from the root the latter frozen power is a different germ. In
particular the derivative in (1) is (d-2a)g_a, not
(1-N)(d-2a)g_a. All assertions below use (1).

Take an actual parallel physical level with b ordered heads and write
the cut word as A B, with matrices composing right to left. Cups and
caps elsewhere are their original maps. On the complete source use
the regular Hopf lifts of its root complete-circle sectors, or the
proved exactly equivalent literal polynomial presentation WITH all
its moving scalar adapters. A bare root-value substitution is not a
generic lift. Let

    G=g tensor ... tensor g,
    P(C)=Tr_(heads 2,...,b)[(I tensor g tensor ... tensor g)C].

The exposed endomorphism is H=P(A B)=z(epsilon)I. This follows from
the actual generic Hopf covariance and the distinct K weights and
nonzero interior F arrows. Its root marked value is z(0), while the
ordinary closed source is

    F=Tr(G A B)=D_N z,
    z(0)=F'(0)/nu_N,
    nu_N=2N(-1)^N/(q0-q0^-1).                         (2)

This is the same original exposed mark and trace normal as in the
[full source theorem](general_matching_graph_full_normal_source.md).
The retained exterior normalization and peripheral factors multiply
both sides; they are not changed here.

The formulas also apply at an actual Morse level after transporting
its oriented objects with their literal duality maps. A braid route
between different ordered boundary objects then includes those maps.
The following displayed parallel-head formulas specify the simplest
case; one must not replace a typed route by an unclocked permutation.

## 2. What the literal second identity cut does not satisfy

Insert E_bb on a chosen intermediate wire and keep the original first
cut open. With the second index renamed j to avoid confusion with
the number of heads, set

    J_(a,j)=[P(A E_jj^(slot) B)]_(a,a).                (3)

Summing the actual identity edge gives

    sum_j J_(a,j)=z,  (1/N)sum_(a,j)J_(a,j)=z.        (4)

It follows that the first head can be fixed at ANY actual physical
a0, giving z=sum_j J_(a0,j), with no new scalar. But the column sums
need not be constant, and multiplying the first index by g_a does
not fix that defect. The old exposed marked row is not a Hopf cap
on an arbitrary partially summed tensor.

For a concrete exact example, use the original three-positive-crossing
two-strand word at N=3, cut after its first crossing and select its
second wire. In the audit's cyclotomic field F_1009, with every original
pivotal factor retained and the common exterior scalar omitted on both
sides, its row sums are (227,227,227), while its column sums are
(758,555,377). With g=(374,1,634), its pivotal column sums are
(903,221,894). Thus neither proposed second marginal is a source identity.
These examples only distinguish the formulas; the correct identity is
proved next at every generic parameter and every level.

## 3. The actual braided projector and its two exact marginals

Number the selected wire by j=0,...,b-1. Let R_i be the actual
generic crossing between slots i-1 and i. Choose the recorded route

    U=R_1 R_2 ... R_j,
    U^-1=R_j^-1 ... R_2^-1 R_1^-1.                    (5)

Thus R_j acts first and the selected wire moves left one position
at a time. A different specified signed braid route is allowed with
its literal inverse; the route is data. Every crossing and its full
scalar is retained. Put

    X_l=U^-1(E_ll tensor I)U,
    J^U_(a,l)=[P(A X_l B)]_(a,a).                     (6)

The X_l are actual mutually orthogonal idempotents with sum I. They
are GENERALLY NOT local matrix units on the selected wire. Their full
exchange and prefix bands are indispensable.

The universal identities are

    J^U 1=z 1,                  g^T J^U=z g^T.       (7)

For the first, sum X_l=I and use H=zI. For the second define

    C_new=U B A U^-1.

It is an actual Hopf intertwiner, so P(C_new)=z_new I. Every factor
commutes with the total pivot G. Cyclicity of the FULL ordinary trace
therefore gives

    D_N z_new=Tr(G U B A U^-1)=Tr(G B A)=D_N z.

Cancel the nonzero generic function D_N before root specialization.
Both open endomorphisms are regular, so z_new=z through epsilon=0.
For each l, the same trace calculation now gives

    sum_a g_a J^U_(a,l)
      =Tr(G A X_l B)
      =Tr[G U B A U^-1(E_ll tensor I)]
      =g_l z.                                        (8)

This proves (7) with the actual order of U and its inverse.
It uses the whole closed trace, not movement of a bare root mark.
Since g_l is always a unit, it supplies the exact second co-source

    z=g_l^-1 sum_a g_a J^U_(a,l)                     (9)

at any physical l. The original first-cut source remains
z=(1/N)sum_(a,l)J^U_(a,l). The formula is valid for arbitrary fixed
complete-channel assignments and their finite sum, with their original
signs and scalar weights. Differentiating it retains the actual U,
U^-1, g and coupon derivatives; no unproved generic normal is suppressed.

The two cuts need not have the same Morse width. More generally let
V=V_d^tensor b and W=V_d^tensor c, with b,c>=1, and use the actual
rectangular covariant maps A:W->V and B:V->W. The selected route U
and X_l act on W; use P_V in (6). Covariance gives
G_V A=A G_W and G_W B=B G_V. The entire trace step is then

    Tr_V(G_V A X_l B)=Tr_W(G_W B A X_l).

The ordinary partial closures P_V(A B) and P_W(U B A U^-1)
are scalar on the same exposed simple object. Cancelling the SAME
generic D_N proves (7)-(9) unchanged. This includes actual intervening
births and deaths, with their original cups, caps and typed duality
transport; it introduces no new pivot on the second identity edge.

At the root g^T1=D_N(0)=0. Thus (9) is an essentially canceling pivotal
co-source, not another positive normalized row. Equations (7) do not
determine the individual entries of J^U: perturbations Z satisfying
Z1=0 and g^T Z=0 remain. This kernel has dimension (N-1)^2:
it is precisely Hom(C^N/span(1),ker(g^T)). It cannot be removed by
two independent point evaluations.

## 4. Root characters and the change of cut-density line

There are two distinct continuations to complex magnetic indices.
The finite generic matrix pivot in (1) is defined on physical integer a.
The specified actual CUP continuation instead gives, by its literal
ratio p_d(a,d-a)/p_d(d-a,a),

    ghat(epsilon;a)=(-1)^d exp(2pi i a) zeta^(d-2a).   (10)

It agrees with (1) at every physical integer a and every epsilon.
At the root, for complex a, it is exactly the marked literal power
q0^[(1-N)(d-2a)]. Consequently

    ghat(0;a)/ghat(0;l)
        =exp[2pi i(a-l)] A_l/A_a,
    A_a=exp(2pi i a/N), A_l=exp(2pi i l/N).             (11)

A_l is the clock of the new FIRST head after the recorded braid U;
it is not automatically the clock of the old bare selected wire.
The integer-linear character is one on the physical grid, but not
on a continued complex magnetic chart. Nor may the root power in
(11) be differentiated as if it were the generic cup germ (10).
This distinction is independent of the matrix identity (8).

The actual geometric cut forms have the corresponding algebraic line
transition: writing the conjugation-invariant form on the ordered-flag
surface as Omega=-Tr(Pi dPi wedge dPi), their fixed-cut forms are
Omega_cut,i=-A_i Omega. Their ratio is A_l/A_a. Thus the algebraic
factor in (11) is exactly the expected change of cut-density line,
WITH its source character still present. This does not identify the
whole continued tensor, its integration cycle, or its boundary residues
merely from equality of that ratio.

## 5. Remaining fiber and source problem

The braided correction (6) is a concrete finite source operation; it
neither normalizes away the kernel in (7) nor supplies an asymptotic
bound for that kernel. Its added routes are actual matrices and may
carry substantial magnetic growth before contraction.

A geometric framing reduction must still transport this entire
co-source, including the exchange bands, into the residual centralizer
fiber and its original relative ends. The nonzero unit g_l^-1 in (9)
is an exact normalization of the whole scalar, not a proof that a
particular local geometric block survives this functional. Existing
two-cut current identities retain the relevant band and endpoint data;
no replacement by a trace-one state on the second identity edge is made.
