## The structural change made here

`heisenberg_period_torsor.md` presents the local normalization as an
explicit H_n-torsor over the classical central shape t. The norm relation
is the Steinberg relation, and the third root makes the first two deck
transformations fail to commute by a central phase. This identifies the
same finite Heisenberg structure behind the cyclic operator and its cover.

The coordinate-free finite face contraction is proved for every finite
level, including composite levels and nonfree support groups. Its measure
is exactly |W|^(-1/2), and its support and quadratic form follow from one
ideal-cohomology vanishing. This replaces separate rational nullspace
cases by a single finite-character argument. It is an application of
finite Fourier duality, not a claim to have invented that theory.

The boundary module is obtained by primitive isotropic edge reduction
and is irreducible of dimension n, independent of face projection rank.
Its unpointed intertwiner line is rigid. The selected intrinsic boundary
period remains to be identified; the subsequent local pointed results
below do not already supply that global identification.

The pointed recoupling note goes further at the local algebraic level:
the quantum tetrahedron has the canonical vacuum A/A(X+Y-1).
The audited Faddeev shift equation identifies its root-of-unity
central fibre with this pointed module exactly, including the choice
X=-q^(-1/2)exp(2pi b Z). Thus the algebraic vacuum is a representation
of the existing local difference object, rather than a separate
quantum definition selected for its asymptotic behavior.
Its factorization preserves the vacuum by two exact left-ideal
identities at generic quantum parameter. The associated q-factorial
kernel has determinant [t(1-st)/(1-t)^2]^(n(n-1)/2), so its normalization
unit is explicit before any peripheral specialization. Coassociativity
proves the basis-free pentagon uniformly. This does not yet identify
all global period sections or their growth with the classical regulator.

The exact rational Faddeev formula also exhibits a continuous Heisenberg
dilogarithm period in addition to the finite root cover. Thus the correct
comparison object needs its flat connection and period/cycle data. A
plain algebraic G_m-torsor does not retain all of those data.

`pointed_period_descent.md` explicitly identifies the Racah operator
with the same q-factorial factorization, fixes the permutation sign,
and makes the scalar normalization a shared-face coboundary. This proves
selected-vacuum coherence through every finite recoupling diagram.

`affine_frame_period_transport.md` proves a necessary correction to the
proposed pointing: unit vacua in two endpoint frames are generally
different vectors under the central intertwiner. It constructs their
exact norm-one transport and proves full affine covariance.
`projective_frame_period_torsor.md` then proves that a fractional linear
change rescales triangle generators by D_j/D_i and D_k/D_i. These
rescalings commute with all four ordered Racah operator embeddings.
The remaining comparison is consequently a scalar; determinant roots
make it mu_(n^2)-valued and an identity-frame anchor fixes it on its
irreducible lifted component. Shared-face roots preserve the pentagon.
This supplies a local projective period system with its actual vector
transport, uniformly at all odd levels. The proof uses precisely stated
Hilbert-90 and Zariski-rigidity hypotheses and retains the deck phases.

These results reconstruct and clarify part of the existing cyclic
matrix framework. The new global question for this project is the exact
identification of its selected period with the prescribed literature
quantum-A state, followed by compatibility across levels with the Bloch
regulator. Constructing another algebraic character cover would not
alone establish either comparison.

There is also an exact parameter condition on that last comparison.
Section 5.1 of the rational Faddeev note substitutes the actual state
argument at b^2=1/n and obtains t_n=exp((n+1)y), with a recorded omega
deck shift of its chosen n-th root. Thus a fixed semiclassical shape
does not mean a fixed finite central character. This distinction must
be preserved in any claimed compatibility between the two period models.

`bloch_lift_and_boundary_torsor.md` identifies a further missing datum:
the tetrahedral symbol is a lift of the boundary wedge, with
delta beta=-(1/2)m wedge l in the recorded unhalved conventions.
Its Bloch-Wigner regulator is the volume exactly. Thus the target
includes the Bloch-period lift, rather than only an unpointed split
boundary algebra. The wedge and five-term arguments are uniform.

The [actual peripheral-fusion calculation](actual_peripheral_fusion.md)
now reconstructs the fundamental-color boundary action directly from
the fixed unrolled matrices. Explicit adjacent-color projectors give
the full modified-dimension ratios; heterogeneous double crossing fixes
the meridian sign. The complete fusion wall retains a square-zero
Casimir residue of rank N-1 and pivotal trace identity. Its actual
cabled-knot coefficient and the normal Kashaev functional are specified
derivatives. General unrolled/projective representation coverage is in
[Costantino--Geer--Patureau-Mirand, 1406.0410](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.0410),
and the torus skein/quantum-torus framework is covered by
[Frohman--Gelca, math/9806107](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9806107).
Derivative/radical-center knot invariants also have coverage in
[Murakami, 1406.1287](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.1287).
Their abstracts establish coverage only; no proof is imported. The
normalization-matched formulas give a direct implication from peripheral
word relations to actual ADO annihilators. They do not prove equality
of the ideals, survival of a geometric classical factor, or the
selected period's regulator norm.

The [ramified Weyl-fibre calculation](ramified_weyl_fibre.md)
uses the existing parabolic/ramified-descent framework, with coverage

the marked ramified fibre, not merely an unpointed root-level
isomorphism. Its parity traces also give a necessary dimension test
for sector injectivity. No new general descent theorem or volume
growth result is claimed.

curves. Their periodic congruence counts give a finite certificate
for generic rational rank one, allowing non-toric initial factors.
The rational-coefficient scope and transfer to a minimal actual
right factor are explicit. This is a structural rank test, not a
classical-symbol identification or a knot-family volume theorem.
