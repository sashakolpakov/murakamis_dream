# A complete signed central source at bounded first depth

Private finite-source deduction, 9 November 2026. Swapping the two
legs of the adopted opposite-height edge supplies the negative
first-angle source for a wider real outgoing angle. The positive
source extends through zero because the outgoing leg has a fixed
positive depth. No crossing is conjugated.

The result below is an absolute envelope with an explicit reflected
action debit. It does not compare its positive maximum with the
closing action, or estimate the complete right outer connector.
No mathematical numerical evaluation or frozen-source edit is used.

## 1. Exact source bound on the whole signed interval

Retain
\[
 U_N=\lfloor N/2\rfloor-1,\qquad
 K_N^<(t,u)=\sum_{j=0}^{U_N}e_j(-2\cos t)e_j(-2\cos u).
 \tag{1}
\]
Consider the independent compact family
\[
 t=a-ih,\quad |a|\le\pi/2,\quad0\le h\le7/10,\qquad
 u=\beta-iK,\quad\pi/2\le\beta\le2\pi/3,\quad
                         1/10\le K\le7/10.
 \tag{2}
\]
Use the original zero-charge primitive \(\psi\), with its complete
constant. Its branch on the signed box is checked in Section 2.

**Theorem.** For every fixed \(\tau>0\), uniformly on (2) at both
parities,
\[
 |K_N^<(t,u)|\le C_\tau
       \exp N\left[
           \max\left\{\Re\psi(t,u),\frac{h+K}{2}\right\}+\tau
       \right].
 \tag{3}
\]
The entire literal sum, both physical endpoint groups, all caps and
both full Fourier corrections are retained.

For \(a\ge0\), apply the proof of
[the positive-first-angle wider envelope](research_mixed_symmetric_closing_positive_first_angle_wider_edge.md).
Here its apparent exclusions \(a=0\) and \(h=0\) can be removed.
In detail, \(A=\cos^2(t/2)\) has nonnegative imaginary part,
while \(B=\cos^2(u/2)\) has a uniformly positive imaginary part
on the outgoing set in (2). Thus
\(\Im(1/W)<0\), \(W=AB/(A+B-AB)\), stays uniformly strict.
The outgoing one-leg argument excludes a real-wire bottom
minimum when the incoming leg is real or purely imaginary.
On any nonempty real incoming cut the same strict cut-force
bound is retained. At \(a=0\) that cut has no open interior
segment. These are the only new bottom degeneracies.
The side sublevels remain connected, and the alias reserve is
\[
 \frac{h+K}{2}-\frac{ah+\beta K}{2\pi}
                  \ge h/4+K/6\ge1/60.
 \tag{4}
\]
All cut estimates, factorial ranges and cap orientations in the
cited proof are unchanged. Calibration starts at \(a=\pi/2,h>0\)
and continues to these boundary values with \(B\)'s imaginary
part still strict. This proves (3) on the closed positive half
of (2), uniformly through both newly included faces.

For \(a=-a_0\le0\), use the exact finite identities
\[
 K_N^<(-a_0-ih,\beta-iK)
       =K_N^<(a_0+ih,\beta-iK)
       =K_N^<(\beta-iK,a_0+ih).
 \tag{5}
\]
The first is evenness of \(\cos t\); the second is symmetry of
the literal row product. The last source lies precisely in the
adopted [opposite-height high-edge box](research_mixed_symmetric_closing_opposite_height_high_edge.md):
its first real angle is \(\beta\), its first depth is \(K\),
its second real angle is \(a_0\le\pi/2\), and its upper second
height is \(h\le7/10\). That theorem gives (3), including
\(a_0=0\) and \(h=0\).
Its continued critical value equals the phase in (3): the
original factorial phase is symmetric in its legs and even
in either angular variable, with the same complete constant.
The same unique algebraic critical point and continuous zero
charge implement these identities. This is not a Schwarz
reflection of the cubic trace.

## 2. Original phase and a stronger negative-angle debit

For the phase calculation use the connected box
\[
 |\Re t|\le\pi/2,\quad|\Im t|\le7/10,\qquad
 0\le\Re u\le2\pi/3,\quad-7/10\le\Im u\le0.
 \tag{6}
\]
Write
\[
 c=\cos(t/2),\quad C=\cos(u/2),\quad
 A=c^2,\quad B=C^2,\quad D=A+B-AB.
\]
The same positive-series height bounds used in the source theorems
give
\[
 |1-A|<13/20,\quad |1-B|<9/10,\quad
 |D-1|<117/200,\quad \Re D>83/200.
 \tag{7}
\]
Since \((117/200)^2<1/2\), the last disk has
\(|\arg D|<\pi/4\).
Also
\[
 |\arg c|<7/20<\pi/4,\qquad
 |\arg C|<\pi/4.
 \tag{8}
\]
The second inequality follows from
\(\tan(\Re u/2)\tanh(|\Im u|/2)
 <\sqrt3\,7/20<1\); the first uses \(|\tan(\Re t/2)|\le1\).
The sharper \(7/20\) bound for \(\arg c\) makes
\[
 |\arg(D/c^2)|<\pi/4+7/10<\pi,\qquad
 |\arg(D/C^2)|<3\pi/4<\pi .
 \tag{9}
\]
Thus neither ratio is nonpositive real. The exact identities
\[
 1+\{\tan(t/2)C\}^2=D/c^2,\qquad
 1+\{\tan(u/2)c\}^2=D/C^2
 \tag{10}
\]
exclude both principal inverse-hyperbolic-sine cuts.
The original closed one-form
\[
 d\psi=-\pi^{-1}\{
  \operatorname{arsinh}(\tan(t/2)C)\,dt+
  \operatorname{arsinh}(\tan(u/2)c)\,du\}
 \tag{11}
\]
therefore has its original holomorphic primitive on a neighborhood
of (6). The roots in (10) satisfy
\[
 c\sqrt{1+\{\tan(t/2)C\}^2}
 =C\sqrt{1+\{\tan(u/2)c\}^2}=\sqrt D
 \tag{12}
\]
by their common square, connectedness and calibration at
\((t,u)=(0,0)\). Closedness and the constant
\(\psi(0,0)=-8J(1/4)\) are exactly those of the original
[signed phase proof](research_mixed_next_inner_three_negative_horizontal_phase.md).
Consequently
\[
 \psi(-t,u)=\psi(t,u),\quad
 \psi(\overline t,0)=\overline{\psi(t,0)},\qquad
               \psi_{tt}=-\frac{C}{2\pi c\sqrt D}.
 \tag{13}
\]

Restrict the outgoing parameter back to (2). Since
\(\cos\beta\le0\),
\[
 |C|^2<13/20,\qquad |c|^2\ge1/2.
\]
Equations (7),(13) imply
\[
 \left|\frac{C}{c\sqrt D}\right|^2<\frac{260}{83}<4,
                    \qquad|\psi_{tt}|<1/\pi .
 \tag{14}
\]
For \(0\le a\le\pi/2\) put
\[
 \Delta(a)=\Re\{\psi(a+ih,u)-\psi(a-ih,u)\}.
\]
Evenness gives \(\Delta(0)=0\), and vertical integration of
(14) gives \(|\Delta'(a)|\le2h/\pi\). Hence
\(\Delta(a)\le2ah/\pi\).

Keep the original charge-one cubic phase
\[
 \Phi(t,u)=\psi(t,0)+\psi(t,u)+3it^2/(4\pi)-it .
 \tag{15}
\]
The first-edge real parts agree at \(a-ih\) and \(-a-ih\).
The linear charge has equal real parts, and the quadratic
crossing difference is \(-3ah/\pi\). Therefore
\[
 \Re\Phi(-a-ih,u)
       \le\Re\Phi(a-ih,u)-ah/\pi .
 \tag{16}
\]
This debit includes the actual cubic crossing and trace charge.

## 3. An attached central envelope, without an action comparison

The original first-short slit theorem bounds the first edge
\(K_N^<(t,0)\) by
\(\exp N(\Re\psi(t,0)+\tau)\) on the compact signed first-angle
family in (2). It is eligible because
\(-2\cos t\notin[2,\infty)\) there. For a fixed positive
height \(h\), retain the exact inner trace factors
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \eta_N=(-1)^{N-1},\qquad
 W_N(t)=\frac{e^{-iNt}}{(1-\eta_Ne^{-iNt})^2},\\
 g_N^{<<}(t,u)=\sin t\,K_N^<(t,0)K_N^<(t,u)
                         e^{3iNt^2/(4\pi)},\\
 -\frac{N^2C_{N,3}}{\pi i}
                   \int g_N^{<<}(t,u)W_N(t)\,dt,\qquad
 C_{N,3}=e^{3\pi i(N-1)^2/(4N)} .
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{17}
\]
The crossing and full double-pole sampling factor have rate
\(3ah/(2\pi)-h\) on this lower line; their complete denominator
differs from this rate by a bounded factor, uniformly for
\(N\) large. These are the same factors as in the adopted
[diagonal taper](research_mixed_symmetric_closing_diagonal_taper.md),
not a first-pole replacement of the Hermite trace.

For \(0\le a\le\pi/2\), define
\[
 \begin{split}
 G_h(a,u)&=\Re\Phi(a-ih,u),\\
 E_h(a,u)&=\Re\psi(a-ih,0)+(h+K)/2
                                  +3ah/(2\pi)-h,\\
 B_h(a,u)&=\max\{G_h(a,u),E_h(a,u)\}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{18}
\]
Equations (3),(17), with the original polynomial trace scalar
retained, bound the full signed central integrand at \(a-ih\)
by a polynomial times \(\exp N(B_h(a,u)+\tau)\).
At the reflected point \(-a-ih\), (16) bounds the geometric
sector by \(G_h(a,u)-ah/\pi\). The endpoint sector has the
stronger debit \(3ah/\pi\), since its two first-edge real
parts agree. Thus the reflected full integrand has upper rate
\[
                         B_h(a,u)-ah/\pi+\tau.
 \tag{19}
\]
For every fixed \(\eta>0\), the whole negative subinterval
\([-\pi/2,-\eta]-ih\) consequently has a strict rate debit
relative to \(\sup_{a\in[\eta,\pi/2]}B_h(a,u)\).
The point \(a=0\) is already covered by the exact source proof;
no interpolation from physical outgoing nodes is used.

Equations (18)--(19) are explicit envelopes, not a proof that
their supremum lies below the original closing action. In
particular this note does not move the positive first-angle
line to an outgoing-dependent critical height, estimate the
joining current, or pay the low/far first-angle contours.
The original right outer connector remains separate.
