# A uniform mixed-color source gap for arbitrary robust Conway expressions

29 October 2026. The positive complete-circle Conway source controls its
ENTIRE original auxiliary-color packet. The signed radical contributions
are uniformly exponentially smaller than the complete source, even after
arbitrary finite branching and turns. For every retained cylindrical
factor there is also a strict gap outside any fixed small complete-color
corridor. These are whole-source estimates, not bounds on selected colors.

The complex corridor packet and the marked geometric peripheral
interpretation remain separate statements. No Newton output is declared
to be a meridian.

## 1. Exact source and conclusions

Use the original pure leaf h_j=Rturn(L_j), with actual auxiliary color
1<=j<=N. Put D=N-1, M=floor(D/2). The
[mixed-leaf theorem](actual_pure_conway_auxiliary_color_leaf.md) gives

    h_j=C_j+R_j,
    C_(N-h)=sum_(v=h)^M N^2 s_(2v)/(s_v^2 s_(v-h)s_(v+h)) Bhat_v
                       for 0<=h<=M,
    C_j=0              for j<=floor(N/2).                 (1)

Every C_j has nonnegative Newton coefficients bounded coefficientwise
by h_N. Every R_j lies in the square-zero nilradical

    J_N=span{Bhat_v:v>=ceil(N/2)},  J_N^2=0,              (2)

and each coefficient has absolute value at most N/2. These are identities
in the full repeated-root algebra, including its original normal data.

Let E be any fixed robust expression generated by A=h_N^3, actual turn,
and product. Give every individual circle leaf an independent ordinary
color j_l. Let A_(E,N)(j) be its original marked cylindrical scalar,
C_(E,N)(j) the same circuit with each leaf replaced by C_j, and
Z_(E,N)=A_(E,N)(N,...,N)>0.

**Uniform radical-gap theorem.** There are gamma>0 and N_0 such that
for all N>=N_0 and ALL physical auxiliary-color choices,

    |A_(E,N)(j)-C_(E,N)(j)|<=exp(-gamma N) Z_(E,N),
    0<=C_(E,N)(j)<=Z_(E,N).                              (3)

The analogous coefficientwise estimate holds for the OPEN operator at
every output. It is preserved under further positive Conway composition,
not only by the final closure.

For a retained cylindrical factor C(A), or C(FG) with F,G robust, write
F0=max A_E, where A_E=V_E/pi is the complete source action after removing
only the forced flat final product gate. For every sufficiently small
fixed delta>0 there is gamma_delta>0 such that, for large N,

    max_(some N-j_l>delta N) |A_(E,N)(j)|
                 <=exp[N(F0-gamma_delta)].               (4)

Both parities and composite levels are included. The expression and
delta are fixed before N tends to infinity. The gaps are proved below;
no numerical profile is used to infer their existence.

## 2. The two-leaf profile has its free maximum at output 1/2

Set

    J(x)=integral_0^x log(2sin(pi u))du,
    g(x)=J(2x)-4J(x),  0<=x<=1/2,
    m(x,y,z)=J(x+y)+J(z)-J(x)-J(y)
                      -J(z-x)-J(z-y)-J(x+y-z).

The product domain is max(x,y)<=z<=x+y<=1. The proved ideal-atom
decompositions make g and m concave on their closed domains, with a
negative definite Hessian on their effective interior variables. Define

    F2(t)=max [g(x)+g(y)+m(x,y,t)],  0<=t<=1,             (5)

with x,y<=1/2 and the product constraints retained. It is finite,
continuous and concave on [0,1]. For 0<t<1 a strict feasible point is
x=y in (t/2,min(t,1/2)). The nonvertex-flat inward barrier and negative
Hessian give a unique strict optimizer and a strictly concave analytic
profile on (0,1).

Its free maximum occurs at

    t=1/2,  x=y=a=(1/pi)atan(sqrt(2)).                    (6)

Indeed, in the strict domain,

    exp(m_x)=sin(pi(x+y))sin(pi(z-x))
                  /[sin(pi x)sin(pi(x+y-z))],
    exp(m_z)=sin(pi z)sin(pi(x+y-z))
                  /[sin(pi(z-x))sin(pi(z-y))],
    g'(x)=2log cot(pi x).

At (6), cos^2(pi a)=1/3, exp(m_x)=exp(m_y)=2,
g'(a)=-log 2, and exp(m_z)=1. Thus all three derivatives vanish.
Concavity and strictness prove the unique global maximum and hence

    F2'(t)>0  for 0<t<1/2.                              (7)

## 3. A radical leaf cannot maximize a three-leaf seed

Let F3(b) be the complete h_N^3 coefficient profile, normalized as
log(coefficient)/N. Exact positive associativity and its variational
limit give

    F3(b)=max [F2(t)+g(y)+m(t,y,b)],                     (8)

with 0<=y<=1/2. Every b in (0,1) has a unique strict optimizer. At b=1
the final gate is forced flat, but the three leaves and preceding gate
are strict at the unique maximum. These are purely variational facts
from the [boundary profile proof](actual_conway_boundary_volume_relation.md)
and its cylindrical endpoint argument; no complement identification is
needed here.

An alias leaf has output y>=1/2 and polynomially bounded coefficient.
Its relaxed envelope inside the three-leaf seed is

    G(b)=max [F2(t)+m(t,y,b)],
       y>=1/2, max(t,y)<=b<=t+y<=1,  1/2<=b<=1.         (9)

F3 and G are continuous on this closed interval. For G, convex
combination of feasible states proves concavity; compactness gives upper
semicontinuity and endpoint interpolation gives the opposite inequality.

**Key claim: every maximum in (9) has y=1/2.**

Take 1/2<b<1 and suppose a maximum had y>1/2. There is a strict feasible
point with y>1/2: choose y in (1/2,b) and then
t in (b-y,min(b,1-y)). Joining to it excludes every flat prism boundary
by the nonvertex-flat inward volume barrier. At t=0, y=b and the atom
(b,0,1-b) supplies the same barrier. F2 has no negative infinite inward
derivative. Therefore the supposed maximum has a strict prism,
0<t<1-y<1/2, and stationarity gives

    m_y=0,  F2'(t)+m_t=0.                              (10)

The first equation yields

    exp(m_t)=sin(pi y)sin(pi(b-t))
                    /[sin(pi t)sin(pi(b-y))]>1.         (11)

The numerator minus the denominator is exactly
sin(pi b)sin(pi(y-t))>0. Equations (7),(11) contradict the second
equation in (10). This proves the claim for 1/2<b<1.

At b=1/2 the constraint y<=b forces y=1/2. At b=1 the constraints force
t+y=1, the last product action vanishes, and (7) makes F2(t) maximal at
t=1/2, again giving y=1/2.

Since g(1/2)=0, (9) is thus the restriction of the complete problem (8)
to the boundary leaf y=1/2. The strictness and uniqueness in (8),
including b=1, imply F3(b)>G(b) for every b in [1/2,1]. Consequently

    gamma_*=min_(1/2<=b<=1) [F3(b)-G(b)]>0.              (12)

This uses continuity and compactness, not finitely sampled outputs.

## 4. Uniform exact seed comparison

Expand the original three-leaf seed:

    h_(j1)h_(j2)h_(j3)
       =C_(j1)C_(j2)C_(j3)
        +sum_(i=1)^3 R_(ji) product_(k!=i) C_(jk).      (13)

Terms with two aliases vanish EXACTLY by (2), including their normals.
Commutativity of the actual pair algebra puts the remaining alias last
inside this seed. No operator is moved through a turn or another box.

By positivity the coefficientwise absolute value of each summand in
(13) is bounded by the corresponding coefficient of

    (N/2) [sum_(v=ceil(N/2))^D Bhat_v] h_N^2.           (14)

It is zero at outputs r<ceil(N/2). For any sequence of remaining
outputs r/D->b, endpoint-uniform factorial estimates and polynomial
state counts give

    limsup_N (1/N)log coefficient_r(14)<=G(b).          (15)

To verify (15), select a largest summand and pass to convergent scaled
indices. Their limits satisfy the closed constraints in (9), and the
two complete leaves give (5). The alias has zero exponential cost.
Replacing scale N by D changes the exponent by O(log N), also at sine
zeros. This argument includes b=1/2 and b=1.

For the complete seed coefficient a_(N,r), the exact-output boundary
limit says, along EVERY such output sequence,

    (1/N)log a_(N,r)->F3(b).                            (16)

This is the proved prescribed-output theorem, not an assumption that
rounded real optimizers have the correct output. Compactness and
(12),(15),(16) now imply, uniformly over all outputs and all colors,

    |mixed_seed_r-core_seed_r|
                     <=exp(-gamma_* N/2) a_(N,r)       (17)

for sufficiently large N. Otherwise a sequence of failures would have
a convergent output subsequence contradicting the strict gap (12).
The factor three in (13) and polynomial factors are absorbed by halving
the gap. Outputs below ceil(N/2) have identically zero error.

## 5. Arbitrary turns and branching preserve the gap

Let S be the fixed number of seed occurrences. The actual turn permutes
Newton coefficients; multiplication has nonnegative structure constants;
the marked closure extracts one coefficient. Thus absolute
coefficientwise inequalities propagate through every operation.

Each seed is its nonnegative core plus an error bounded by epsilon_N
times the complete seed, with epsilon_N=exp(-gamma_*N/2). Each core is
itself bounded by the complete seed. Expanding multilinearly in seed
errors, without moving a turn, bounds the error at every open output by

    [(1+epsilon_N)^S-1] times the complete coefficient. (18)

This proves the open version of (3), and then its marked closure.
Turns do not preserve the nilradical; the entire propagated radical
was bounded, not removed by passing to a semisimple quotient.

## 6. The whole physical color complement has a strict gap

For a retained cylindrical factor the reduced complete angle problem
has a unique strict maximum x_*. Every leaf lies in (0,1/2), and every
unforced prism is strict. The complete variational theorem gives

    log Z_(E,N)=NF0+o(N).

If some j<=floor(N/2), the core circuit is zero and (3) proves (4).
Otherwise put chi_l=(N-j_l)/N. For a leaf with output x>=chi, its core
action is

    g_chi(x)=g(x)-Delta(x,chi),
    Delta(x,chi)=J(x-chi)+J(x+chi)-2J(x)>=0.             (19)

For 0<x<1/2 and 0<chi<=x, this loss is strictly positive. Differentiate
in chi<x:

    partial_chi Delta
        =log[sin(pi(x+chi))/sin(pi(x-chi))]>0,

and use continuity at chi=x. The loss may vanish at x=1/2, so the
strict COMPLETE optimizer is essential.

Take the compact joint domain of feasible complete states and parameters
0<=chi_l<=x_l with at least one chi_l>=delta. Its deformed action is at
most F0. Equality would require x=x_* and every loss zero, impossible
at the selected interior leaf with chi_l>=delta. Hence the maximum on
this joint domain is F0-c_delta for some c_delta>0.

Uniform factorial estimates and polynomial state counts bound the
entire positive core outside the corridor by exp[N(F0-c_delta/2)] for
large N. Combining this with (3) proves (4). All physical auxiliary
colors, signed radical terms and original normal directions are covered.
