# Conditional completion of the whole type-four terminal polynomial

Research assembly, 5 November 2026. This note states the full terminal
consequence of the twenty-one comparisons in the new unified
certificate. It remains conditional until a complete author record
and independent saved replay establish all of them. A running or
partial author record does not discharge the condition. No numerical

The conclusion concerns the complete original type-four terminal

open-word contour theorem or the arbitrary short-filling conclusion.

## 1. Literal source and reference scope

Keep the original \(r=e^{-2\pi i/N}\),
\(P_j=(r;r)_j\), \(p_j(X)=(rX;r)_j\), and
\(X=e^{-2\pi ia}\). The entire ordinary-color source is exactly
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal V_{1,N}(w,X)
 &=\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}\frac{X^{-i}p_i(X)}{P_i^2}
                                  \mathcal U_{1,N}(w,r^i)\\
 &=\sum_{i,j=0}^{N-1}
       \frac{X^{-i}p_i(X)}{P_i^2}
       \frac{p_j(r^i)\mathcal H_N(w,r^{-j})}{P_j^2}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{1}
\]
There is no additional literal \(N\) in (1). The exact
identity \(P_jp_i(r^j)=P_ip_j(r^i)\) makes the full-square
terms vanish when \(i+j\ge N\). Each \(\mathcal H_N\)
contains its whole Newton sum and every ordinary derivative.
These are the sources of [reversed-cap assembly](research_mixed_reversed_cap_assembly.md)
and [the coherent private-tail reduction](research_mixed_terminal_four_private_tails.md).

Let \(a_*=A+iH\) be the incoming nonprivate leg at a type-four
terminal vertex of an actual open word of length \(m\ge2\).
Let \(\nu\in\{0,1\}\) be its own type minus three. For
\(m\ge3\) it is an ordinary leg, and for \(m=2\) it is
the opposite endpoint's attached leg. The original source roots give
\[
 \begin{array}{c|cc}
 &\text{ordinary input}&\text{attached input}\\ \hline
 \nu=0&\Im a_*>-.065&\Im a_*>-.072\\
 \nu=1&\Im a_*>-.102&\Im a_*>-.108 .
 \end{array}
 \tag{2}
\]
All these nonprivate legs lie in their corresponding reduced disks
\(D'_3,D'_4\), hence in the full original disks \(D_3,D_4\).
The bounds are the full-map and exact witness statements cited in
[local factor coverage, Section 1](research_mixed_open_local_factor_coverage.md),
not a periodic replacement of an open word.

Consequently every such reference lies in the certificate domain
\[
          \mathcal D_3=D_3,\qquad
          \mathcal K_4=D'_4\cap\{-.108\le\Im a_*\le-.001\}.
 \tag{3}
\]
Here \(D'_4=\{|a-(.30-.052i)|\le.060\}\).
The ordinary type-four upper height is below \(-.001\), and
the attached type-four upper height is below \(-.020\), by the

Thus the disk and both height restrictions in (3) retain every actual

For actual references, also \(.24\le A\le.433\), and

Write \((e_*,t_*,u_*)\) for the original own-four endpoint

## 3. Conditional full polynomial and its normalization

Let \(A_C(e,w)\) be the original nonzero endpoint amplitude,
normalized by
\(\mathcal C_N(e,w)=N^{-3/2}A_C(e,w)e^{NS(e,w)}(1+O(N^{-1}))\).
The complete-color amplitude of the exact reduced double interpolant is
\[
 \widehat A_1(a,e,t)=e^{i\pi(e+t)/2}A_C(e,-2)
 \frac{\sqrt{2\sin\pi(a+t)}\sqrt{2\sin\pi(e+t)}}
 {(2\sin\pi a)^{1/2}(2\sin\pi t)^{3/2}
                  (2\sin\pi e)^{1/2}}.
 \tag{6}
\]
This is exactly the amplitude in the reduced-current theorem; all
roots continue on its original regular sine branches. It is nonzero.
Define
\[
 H_{2,*}=-\widehat{\mathcal F}_{1,(e,t)}''(a_*;e_*,t_*).
 \tag{7}
\]
Its real part is positive definite. The determinant square root is
the accretive Gaussian branch, with the original contour orientation.

**Theorem 1, conditional on C.** As \(N\) tends to infinity
through all integer levels of either parity, the **whole** polynomial
has the nonzero asymptotic
\[
 \mathcal V_{1,N}(-2,e^{-2\pi ia_*})
  =\frac{2\pi}{N^2}
    \frac{\widehat A_1(a_*,e_*,t_*)}{\sqrt{\det H_{2,*}}}
       e^{N\mathcal P_1(a_*)}(1+O(N^{-1})).
 \tag{8}
\]
For every fixed ordinary derivative order \(j\ge0\),
\[
 \left.\partial_w^j\mathcal V_{1,N}(w,e^{-2\pi ia_*})\right|_{w=-2}
 =2\pi N^{j-2}
    \frac{\widehat A_1(a_*,e_*,t_*)}{\sqrt{\det H_{2,*}}}
    \left(-\frac{\tan(\pi u_*)}{4\pi}\right)^j
       e^{N\mathcal P_1(a_*)}(1+O(N^{-1})).
 \tag{9}
\]
The expansion is the original holomorphic local ordinary-color
packet, uniformly on smaller fixed color disks. Every displayed
leading coefficient is nonzero.

*Proof.* The exact reduced interpolation keeps the complete
\(\mathcal C_N\) on every contour. Its symbol has power
\(N^{-3}\). The two original residue operations contribute
\(+N^2\), and the two-dimensional Gaussian contributes
\((2\pi/N)/\sqrt{\det H_{2,*}}\). Thus the power and
scalar are exactly
\[
                       N^2N^{-3}(2\pi/N)=2\pi N^{-2}.
 \tag{10}
\]
The original stationary equations give its phase
\(\mathcal P_1\), not merely the exponential of that phase.
The eight flattened tests and twelve alias tests suppress every
other current of the retained rectangle. Section 2 adds only
exponentially smaller physical sectors. At \(y=A\), the bare
plane equals \(\Re\mathcal P_1(a_*)\), so these errors
cannot cancel the nonzero Gaussian coefficient.

There is also an exact consistency check with the unreduced source.
Put \(H_{3,*}=-\mathcal F_{1,(e,t,u)}''\), and let
\(h_{u,*}=-\mathcal F_{1,uu}\) at the same original root.
Stationary elimination is the complex Schur complement, hence
\[
        \det H_{3,*}=h_{u,*}\det H_{2,*},\qquad
        \widehat A_1=\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\mathcal A_1}
                             {\sqrt{h_{u,*}}}.
 \tag{11}
\]
Here \(\mathcal A_1\) is the literal triple amplitude in
[the joint-source theorem](research_mixed_terminal_four_joint_source.md).
The second identity is precisely its original inner Gaussian
normalization. Fubini for the accretive Gaussian, or continuation
within the accretive matrix domain, fixes the matching square-root
sign in (11). Therefore (8) is equivalently
\((2\pi)^{3/2}N^{-2}\mathcal A_1e^{N\mathcal P_1}/
\sqrt{\det H_{3,*}}\), in agreement with the original triple
residue coefficient \(+N^3\) and symbol \(N^{-7/2}\).

The endpoint coefficient, exact interpolants, critical point and
Gaussian amplitude have their original holomorphic color germs.
Choose the color disk so the phase variation and all discarded-source
variations fit within the single tolerance in Section 2. The strict
bare gap then persists relative to the perturbed critical phase.
Cauchy's formula on a smaller disk transfers the full packet and
its relative error. The original color marginal at stationarity is
\(-\tan(\pi u_*)/(4\pi)\); it is nonzero on the certified
auxiliary rectangle. This proves (9). \(\square\)

## 4. Conditional independent-plateau upper plane

**Theorem 2, conditional on C.** For every \(\tau>0\),
uniformly on actual references and \(y\in P\), a sufficiently
small fixed ordinary-color disk and polynomial prefactors give
\[
 |\mathcal V_{1,N}(w,e^{-2\pi i(y+iH)})|
 |d_N(e^{-2\pi i(y+iH)})|^{\nu/2}
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
              e^{N\{B_{1,\nu}(a_*;y)+\tau\}}.
 \tag{12}
\]
The analogous bounds hold for every fixed ordinary-color derivative
on a smaller fixed disk. The terminal private coordinate and its
original Fourier alternatives have already been summed exactly;
(12) is not a termwise bound for those alternatives.

*Proof.* The actual-height horizontal current has its fixed-reference
upper plane by the reduced Schur Hessian. Every other current and
sector is strictly below that bare plane by Section 2. Their sum
therefore gives the bare form of (12). For an actual unselected
type-four incoming leg, \(H<0\), and the original Fourier
halfweight has upper rate \(-\Re g(y+iH)/2\). Since
\(\Re g\) is horizontally convex on \(P+iH\),
\[
 \begin{split}
 B_{1,0}(a_*;y)-\frac\nu2\Re g(y+iH)
 &=B_{1,\nu}(a_*;y)\\
 &\quad-\frac\nu2\{
      \Re g(y+iH)-\Re g(a_*)-\Re g'(a_*)d\}
 \le B_{1,\nu}(a_*;y).
 \end{split}
 \tag{13}
\]
Multiplying by the actual halfweight, with one total source
tolerance, proves (12). This uses neither a division by that
polynomial nor a selected or positive-height Fourier branch.
\(\square\)

If transverse target derivatives are needed, reserve an additional
