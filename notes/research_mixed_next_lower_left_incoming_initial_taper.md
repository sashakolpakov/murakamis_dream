# Complete initial and left-taper source on the lower-left interval

Private research proof, 8 November 2026. For an ordinary next type
four, the literal complete first cap and both complete conditional
kernels are below the original next plane on the initial incoming
interval and the following left taper. A steeper real index cut
transports the low and main profiles to the established central
source. The physical upper rows use a mixed half-row and positive
prefix estimate; no endpoint-only assertion is made for that entire
block. All original rows, forcing terms, contour currents and level
parities remain. No numerical evaluation is used.
