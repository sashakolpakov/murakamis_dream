# Recent advances and the remaining conjecture

29 October 2026. This records the current internal research status.
The general volume conjecture remains open.

## Exact positivity in the original source

The [Newton multiplication theorem](actual_positive_newton_multiplication.md)
gives an exact nonnegative multiplication table in the full root algebra.
It retains the repeated-root normal terms. The actual turn reverses the
Newton coordinates, and the actual closures extract coordinates. Thus
the covered two-head circuits become positive finite sums within the
original source, without replacing it by an auxiliary positive model.

## The actual source now has a single Racah formula

The [generic identity](actual_generic_trivalent_racah_source_identity.md)
expresses the original trivalent source as one finite Racah sum, with
its normalization, high fold partners and normal tails retained. Its
proof was independently read and its algebraic certificates replayed.
An exceptional endpoint in the uniqueness argument was repaired by a
direct cup projection before the proof was frozen.

Together with the [normal-jet envelope](central_racah_normal_jet_envelope.md),
this establishes polynomial conditioning of the actual correlated
boundary Gram in a fixed central corridor at both level parities.
It does not establish a bound over the entire color domain or for
arbitrary recoupling operators.

## A source period equals an actual non-octahedral core volume

For the odd-level central cube source, the
[positive period theorem](actual_positive_newton_cuboctahedral_period.md)
reduces the growth to a strictly concave finite-dimensional maximization.
The unique maximum satisfies

\[
\tan^2(\pi t)=2.
\]

An explicit decomposition into fourteen ideal tetrahedra identifies the
maximum with the volume of the actual ideal right-angled cuboctahedron
\(Q_{\rm cube}\), approximately \(12.04609204009\).

The [four-rung theorem](actual_cube_four_rung_positive_source.md) then
allows all four untwisted rung colors to vary. The sum lies between the
central term and \(16(m+1)^4\) times that term, where \(N=2m+1\).
Consequently this partial original source has growth

\[
\lim_{\substack{N\to\infty\\N\text{ odd}}}
\frac{2\pi}{N}\log Z_N^{\rm four\text{-}rung}
=16v_8+2\operatorname{Vol}(Q_{\rm cube}),
\]

the volume of the actual augmented cube complement. Here \(v_8\) is
the volume of the regular ideal octahedron. Eight cycle colors remain
fixed to their central channels. This is not yet the full cube invariant.

## General geometric gluing is available

For the certified ideal cores in the
[graph geometry theorem](actual_nc_graph_rectified_core_geometry.md),
the actual complement volume is \(4b v_8+2\operatorname{Vol}(Q)\).
The [three-port sewing theorem](actual_nc_three_port_source_sewing.md)
also gives an exact whole-source gluing identity; the
[six-head pruning theorem](actual_exposed_six_port_complete_circle_pruning.md)
eliminates unsupported seam channels before the exposed mark or exterior
acts. These supply geometric and algebraic gluing, without yet proving
the required asymptotics for arbitrary graphs.

## What remains on the critical path

The remaining obstacle is global control of the original growing-color
sum: the unfixed cycle channels, possible phase cancellation, behavior
outside the central corridor, and the even-level cube source. The
general graph problem also needs the resulting global energy to recover
the certified core volume. These are substantive missing estimates.

The earlier all-level results for the specified sufficiently long
fillings of signed octahedral belt stacks remain in force in the notes.
The [frontier](general_equality_frontier.md) states their quantifiers
and the other established families. Internal proof reviews and finite
audits are recorded separately; finite checks alone do not establish
the asymptotic theorems.
