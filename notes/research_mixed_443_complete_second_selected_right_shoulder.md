# The complete first polynomial on the selected 443 right shoulder

Private written proof, 11 November 2026. Author readback and direct-source
version checks are complete. Independent full proof/direct-source review
is required before adoption.

The third type in this note is three. The smaller actual third-index
radius permits a joint comparison which does not spend the small common
gap of the completed first contour. The first coordinate is integrated
over its whole period, with both of its original Fourier contributions.
The second coordinate is selected and moves from \(9/20+i/100\) to
\(1/2+i/100\). No second height connection or complete second period is
asserted.

## 1. Literal source and statement

with \(443\). All reference points, heights, primitive constants and
ordinary boundary markings are the original coupled ones. Put
\[

 \tag{1}

 -.065\le h_3,c_3\le.068.
 \tag{2}
\]

\(p_j(V)=(\zeta V;\zeta)_j\), retain
\[
\begin{split}
 U_N(w,X)&=\sum_{i<N}H_N(w,\zeta^{-i})p_i(X)/P_i^2,\\
 C_{\epsilon,N}(X,Y)&=\sum_{j<N}
                X^{-j}p_j(X)p_j(Y)/P_j^{2+\epsilon},\\

       [U_N(w,X)d_N(X)(1+X^{-N})C_{1,N}(X,Y)],\\

\end{split}                                                     \tag{3}
\]
There is no outgoing halfweight because the third type is three.
The complete first sum is exactly
\[

              U_N(w,\zeta^k)C_{1,N}(\zeta^k,Y)/P_k .
 \tag{4}
\]
Neither a root average nor a further factor two is present.

Use the original primitive \(g'(u)=\operatorname{Log}(1-e^{-2\pi iu})\),
with continuous value \(g(0)=0\), and the original cap phase. Put
\[
\begin{split}

                         -g(a)-g(b)+2\pi ias,\\
 F_{123}&=\Psi(e,z_1)+Q_1(s_2;z_1,z_2)+Q_0(s_3;z_2,z_3)
                                      -g(z_1)-g(z_2),\\

 \Lambda_{123}(z)&=\Re F_{123,*}+\Re\{p_R(z-r_3)\}.
\end{split}                                                     \tag{5}

**Theorem 1.** On a sufficiently small fixed ordinary-color
disk about \(-2\), for every prescribed \(\tau>0\),
\[
 |{\cal D}_N(w,Y,Z)|
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
       \exp N\{\Lambda_{123}(z)-1/100000+\tau\}
 \tag{6}
\]
uniformly on (1), at both parities and all sufficiently large levels.
Every fixed ordinary-color derivative has the same assertion on a
smaller disk. Integration on the specified second shoulder retains the
original density \(+N\,dy\). This statement does not assert an all-real
ordinary-color estimate.

## 3. A first-index barrier on the enlarged first path

Set \(r=e^{2\pi H}<16/15\), \(\rho=e^{2\pi h_2}\in(1/2,1)\).
For every \(a=x+ih\) with \(.1\le x\le.5\) and \(c_1\le h\le H\),

 \frac{AB}{D^3},\qquad
 D=1+\rho^2-\frac9{25}\rho,\quad
 A=\rho^2+\frac{27}{80}\rho+\frac{225}{256},\quad

 \tag{10}

These are strict row-ratio comparisons on the original finite

 \tag{17}

## 6. The complete first real interval from one half to seven eighths

The positive-output conditional theorem extends to
\[
 .5\le a\le7/8,\qquad y=q+iH,\qquad .45\le q\le.5.
 \tag{23}
\]
We verify its changed hypotheses. For \(q<.5\), the original unit
incoming branch theorem applies unchanged. At \(q=.5\), write
\(Y=-R\), \(1<R<43/40\). If \(\Im X>0\), a real root would force
\(\Im Y=-(v-1)^3\Im X/|v-X|^2=0\), impossible since \(v=1\)
is excluded. At \(X=-1\), the cubic is
\[
 2v^3+(R-2)v^2+(R+3)v-1=0.
 \tag{24}
\]
Its derivative is positive on the real axis, since its discriminant
is \(4(R-2)^2-24(R+3)<0\). Hence it has one real root and one
nonreal conjugate pair; the upper member is simple.
The original upper branch therefore continues to this boundary.
The original incoming-ray exclusion also persists: setting
\(\Im Y=0\) in its two exact ray identities still forces
\(\Re Y>1\), a contradiction. Thus \(0<\Re s_*<1-a\) remains
strict, with the same zero logarithmic lift.

The radius bounds \(1/2<e^{2\pi\Im s_*}<4\) and the lower cut
\(\Re s_*>2/25\) in the positive-output proof are unchanged.
Their outgoing cosine and argument comparisons only improve when
\(q\) is raised from \(.45\) to \(.5\).
On its prewall core \(u\ge2/25\), both shifted cotangents are now
negative, so its real concavity is immediate.
Every low, postwall and high physical-row estimate in that proof
persists. In particular the outgoing low numerator is still greater
than one; the upper radius is unchanged; and the high-row estimate
used only that radius, not the upper angle.

For its aliases the former upper slope \(87\pi/50\) becomes at most
\(46\pi/25<2\pi\). In the lower half-strip the outgoing argument
is now nonpositive, giving the stronger lower slope
\(-2\pi a\ge-7\pi/4\). Sufficiently tall fixed aliases therefore
retain all horizontal and vertical corrections.
The same moving prewall right cut, physical wall collar, exact
factorial interpolant and \(+N\) residue identity prove
\[
 |C_{1,N}(e^{-2\pi ia},Y)|
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
  e^{N\{\max(\Re\Theta(a,y),L(y))+\tau\}},
 \quad L(y)=E_{1/2}(2/25;y).
 \tag{25}
\]
The strict root margins are compact also at \(q=.5\), as just
proved, so no level-dependent strip limit is used.

in \(a\). At \(q=.5\) the weak inequality follows by continuity,

\[
 \Re\Theta(a,y)\le\Re\Theta(.5,y)
 \le M_2(y):=\max_{2/25\le t\le r_0}

 \tag{26}
\]
Choose \(r_0\) inside its permitted arbitrarily small collar so that
