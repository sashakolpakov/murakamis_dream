    R_r -> S_j, f -> f v_j                           (5)

Proof. The exact all-degree circle factorization is L_j=F_j F_j^dagger P.

    epsilon_1 ... epsilon_r v_j !=0.                 (6)

## 3. Exact three-circle rank

On a surviving regular cube the top product has rank ONE. Let

    K=3M-d.

The number of surviving folded triples is the number of nonnegative
h_1,h_2,h_3 with sum h_i<=K, by h_i=M-j_i. Since K<=M, their
individual upper bounds impose no additional restriction. Therefore

    rank[(L_N(A_1)L_N(A_2)L_N(A_3))|S_N]
       =binom(K+3,3) if K>=0, and 0 if K<0.          (7)

Equivalently the rank is binom(m+3,3) at odd N=2m+1, and
binom(m+1,3) at even N=2m (zero at N=2). This closes the former
interior/even nonvanishing gap in the support theorem. It does NOT
make the three-ordinary-circle quotient faithful on the whole marked
source: the previously proved marked radical obstruction is unchanged.
