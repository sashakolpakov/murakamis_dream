# Uniform joined real-face comparisons and the remaining bounds

Research result, 6 November 2026. Exact recombination of the completed
face reserves and joined-plane ceilings settles both complete-weight
alternatives for thirteen of eighteen real-face and entropy families.
The separate private-left theorem supplies a fourteenth family. Seven
individual branch comparisons remain unresolved by these estimates.
No analytic value, root or source has been reevaluated.

## 1. Common reference, contour and normalization

The external input has flag \(\beta=1\), the preceding ordinary block
has flag one, and the terminal flag is \(\epsilon=0,1\). The canonical
reference parameter \(b_*\) ranges over the full actual domain
\(K_4\). The [source-domain lemma](research_mixed_terminal_canonical_source_domain.md)
supplies the inherited terminal source hypotheses throughout this
family and identifies every relevant actual suffix of length at least
three. It does not identify independent points of a rectangular image
enclosure with source references.

All comparisons use the same selected height \(H=1/100\), original
complex reference action \(M_*\), and incoming marginal \(p_*\).
The literal allocated product retains
\(d_N(X_a)X_a^{-N}|d_N(X_b)|^{1/2}\). The two alternatives are
\(\nu=1\) (factorial) and \(\nu=0\) (forced), both with the full
selected real interval \(a_R\in P=[.22,.45]\). No alternatives
are combined across different heights or replaced by their minimum.

Let \(U_{\epsilon,f}\) be the saved bare upper for one of the nine
real-face or entropy families from the
[uniform extraction](research_mixed_terminal_uniform_face_completion.md).
Let \(E_{\epsilon,\nu}\) be the saved ceiling from the
[joined-plane protocol](research_mixed_terminal_uniform_joined_plane.md).
The original halfweight allocation and zero-cost height transport give
\[
 \sup D_{\epsilon,\nu,f}\le
        B_{\epsilon,\nu,f}:=E_{\epsilon,\nu}+U_{\epsilon,f},
 \qquad
 D=\text{allocated profile rate}
            -\Re\{M_*+p_*(b-b_*)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
All independent real target variables and original internal reference
heights remain as in the source theorem.
