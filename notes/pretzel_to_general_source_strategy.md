# Extending the proved pretzel argument to general source networks

29 October 2026. The main route is the proved pretzel mechanism:
estimate the whole original source, prove survival of its geometric
contribution, and identify that contribution with the actual complete
or filled hyperbolic metric. The cube calculations and alternative
positive circuits are retained as tests and possible ingredients.
They do not replace the general objective.

## What is already proved in the pretzel case

The [complete augmented pretzel theorem](actual_pretzel_coherent_circle_and_period_asymptotic.md)
covers every fixed odd width s>=3, both level parities and all original
normal terms. Its explicitly weighted Fourier energy has the sharp
volume rate. The original marked row has polynomial dual cost, and a
separate coherent calculation proves a nonzero period with only a
polynomial overlap loss. Weighted Cauchy gives an upper bound; that
last calculation supplies the matching lower bound.

The [filled-source proof](actual_long_pretzel_full_source_survival.md),
with the [whole-color width improvement](actual_pretzel_all_width_extension.md),
covers fixed odd widths s>=9 with independently sufficiently long
fixed signed fillings. The geometric part is the
[Casson--Rivin equality proof](actual_long_pretzel_casson_rivin_equality.md)
on the actual filled-slope angle slice, including smooth end completion.
It does not maximize the unfilled volume instead.

The Karamata suggestion led to useful positive-energy and transport
questions. The [separate Tauberian criterion](actual_fourier_energy_tauberian_criterion.md)
is not a missing hypothesis silently assumed in the pretzel proof:
the latter establishes its whole-source estimates and nonzero
asymptotic directly. A cumulative Tauberian law alone would still
need cross-level control to give a limit at every N.

## A general part of the weighted-Cauchy construction is now complete

On every supported six-head source cell the prescribed positive
correlated Gram H has actual costs

    ||v_j||_H^2=K_j,
    ||lambda_j||_(H^-1)^2=tau_j^2 K_j.

Here tau_j is the explicitly evaluated positive marked top moment.
The [whole-support theorem](actual_whole_support_boundary_gram_envelope.md)
now proves K_j<=poly(N) uniformly over EVERY supported folded triple,
including saturation boundaries, high partners, simple axes, and both
parities. This removes the former central-corridor restriction.
The Gram is determined by the original local source and marked row,
before choosing an exterior graph.

Its proof compares the entire finite Racah sum, including its
second-root-zero terms, to a positive midpoint term. A global
sine-product inequality replaces the need for a uniform tail gap.
Thus this step transfers beyond the one-dimensional pretzel corridor.

The exact [three-port sewing identity](actual_nc_three_port_source_sewing.md)
also already holds for the whole complete-circle source:

    Z_new=sum_j Z_A(j) Z_B(j)/Z_theta(j).

The sum is over supported triples and Z_theta(j)>0. Defining
F_A(j)=Z_A(j)/sqrt(Z_theta(j)) and similarly F_B gives the actual
bilinear contraction sum_j F_A(j)F_B(j), and therefore

    |Z_new|^2 <= (sum_j |F_A(j)|^2)(sum_j |F_B(j)|^2).

All shared colors remain correlated. This algebraic rewrite neither
computes the two energies nor ensures that their bilinear overlap is
nonzero. After the three complete circles act, each regular cell is
observed through its top monomial; local Gram conditioning must not
be presented as a bound on the exterior's unknown scalar response.

## The remaining general theorem must control the transported energy

For an original graph source written as K_N=b_N v_N, a useful metric
must establish both

    b_N G_N^-1 b_N^* <= exp(o(N)),
    v_N^* G_N v_N <= exp[N Vol(M)/pi+o(N)].

It must also prove geometric survival, equivalently no exponential
loss in the original source pairing at the geometric contribution.
The pretzel proof does all three jobs in its explicit Fourier frame.
For branching, the unresolved operation is transport between different
pair decompositions while retaining the correlated metric, phases,
boundary currents and common physical normal parameter.

Polynomial local costs alone do not make this transport isometric.
Nor can one multiply local theta rates and omit a global contribution:
the [actual graph geometry](actual_nc_graph_rectified_core_geometry.md)
has volume 4b v8+2 Vol(Q). The positive cube period explicitly detects
that extra core term in a specified original sector. It is a useful
test of any proposed sector-wise energy bound, while its full color
sum remains an independent task.

## The geometric conclusion has a general finite certificate

The [source-volume certificate](source_volume_casson_rivin_certificate.md)
applies beyond pretzels once the actual source supplies a positive
angle point on the actual triangulation, an absolute equality of its
action with tetrahedral volume, and stationarity on the FULL edge and
peripheral angle slice. Strict concavity then gives its unique global
maximum; the decoration and end checks give the actual hyperbolic
metric. A stationary point on a smaller source slice is insufficient.

The next decisive estimate is therefore the sharp energy of the
coherently transported source, with a nonzero geometric overlap.
The full-support boundary theorem is a completed input to that work;
it is not yet the general volume equality.
