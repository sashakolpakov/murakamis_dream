## 2. Positivity everywhere and exact elimination of the private force

**Lemma 1.** Throughout the endpoint rectangle in (2), \(d_L>0\).

Indeed \(x+e_0\in(.516,.620)\), while
\(x+E\in(.579,.713)\), and \(E>e_0\). Both arguments lie
in \((.5,.75)\), where \(-\cos(2\pi y)\) strictly decreases
with \(y\). Also \(e^{2\pi h}>e^{2\pi(h+j)}>0\).
The function \(1+r^2-2r\cos(2\pi y)\) strictly increases in
\(r\) when the cosine is negative. Thus both changes from the old
summed coordinate to the new one strictly increase the squared modulus of
the original logarithm's argument. Their logarithmic ratio is \(2d_L>0\).

The original terminal-index critical equation is
\[
 g'(t_*+a_*)+g'(t_*+e_*)-3g'(t_*)+2\pi ia_*=0.
 \tag{5}
\]
Taking its real part in (3), without changing any logarithmic lift, gives
\[
 A_t+d_L=
 \Re\{g'(t_*+A+iH)+g'(t_*+e_0)-3g'(t_*)\}-2\pi H.
 \tag{6}
\]
In particular neither \(e_*\) nor the original selected height \(c\)
occurs on the right. Set \(r=e^{2\pi h}\) and \(R=e^{-2\pi H}\).
Then (6) is equivalent to the exact identity
\[
 e^{2(A_t+d_L)}=
 \frac{\{R^2+r^2-2Rr\cos(2\pi(x+A))\}
       \{1+r^2-2r\cos(2\pi(x+e_0))\}}
      {\{1+r^2-2r\cos(2\pi x)\}^3}.
 \tag{7}
\]
The factor \(R\) records precisely the retained \(-2\pi H\)
chirp contribution. Equation (6), unlike the modulus formula defining
\(d_L\), is only asserted at a stationary original reference. It must
not be imposed on arbitrary points of an independent reference box.

## 4. Uniform transfer and the remaining coverage question

Let \(G_s,G_{a,\nu},G_t\) denote the exact scalar convex gains in
[the original transfer](research_mixed_terminal_private_left_joint_transfer.md),
equation (9), on the full original restricted main intervals. Under the
other main curvature hypotheses and any region of Theorem 2,
\[
 \sup D_\nu^L
 \le T_\nu+U_L+G_s(A_s)+G_{a,\nu}(A_{a,\nu})+G_t(A_t+d_L)
 \le T_\nu+G_s(A_s)+G_{a,\nu}(A_{a,\nu})+G_t(A_t)-1/20000.
 \tag{14}
\]
For the last step, a nonpositive terminal force maximizes its scalar
conjugate to the left of the true center. Increasing that force toward
zero decreases the gain. Theorem 2 gives exactly this ordering. The
actual bare-source conclusion (4) supplies the strict extra debit.
Thus every valid main three-cost upper budget transfers automatically to
this private-left face throughout the union of these four regions.

This also permits incoming heights in \([-.12,.08]\): use the
[full-input barrier and curvature replacement](research_mixed_terminal_full_input_index_barrier.md).
Its incoming pair retains only the \(\beta/2\) own cost instead of
the old \((1+\beta)/2\) cost. That loss is immaterial here, because the
incoming force is exactly zero. The selected, ordinary-index and
terminal-index costs in (14) are unchanged. The terminal-index barrier
\(t_R\ge.18\) includes the real private cut, and all target intervals,
original heights, and both full-\(P\) Fourier alternatives are retained.

For \(H\ne.01\), (14) is a same-height transfer of the corresponding
main scalar budget, with the same unchanged curvature argument; an old
budget at \(H=.01\) cannot be substituted without its separate height
transport. The force theorem itself is uniform for the entire interval
\([.01,.014]\).

It remains to establish that a proposed whole canonical family lies in the
union of the four sufficient regions, or to control its remaining part by
a different force estimate. The broad endpoint rectangles alone do not
supply that inclusion. No failure outside this union is asserted. For the
source implication one must additionally retain the actual oriented and
inverse-feasible subset underlying (4); existence on the full auxiliary
\(D_3,D_4\) disks does not enlarge that completed source scope. This note
therefore proves a uniform transfer on explicit root-image regions, not a
new whole-disk, whole-word, or knot-volume theorem.
