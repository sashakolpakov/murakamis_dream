# Source-cell terminal comparisons: five remaining branches

Research result, 6 November 2026. Matching each joined-plane bound to
its own original source cell proves the forced physical-entropy
alternative for both terminal types. It also strengthens the already
negative type-three forced right-cut alternative. Together with the
previous comparisons and the separate private-left theorem, 31 of the
36 real-face and entropy branch comparisons are proved. Five remain
unresolved. This is not a complete terminal-kernel or whole-source
estimate.

## 1. Same-reference comparison and fixed domains

The external flag is \(\beta=1\), the preceding ordinary factor has
type four, and the terminal type is \(3+\epsilon\), with
\(\epsilon=0,1\). The original canonical input \(b_*\) ranges over
\[
 K_4=\{|b_*-(.30-.052i)|\le .060\}
                  \cap\{-.108\le\Im b_*\le-.001\}.
\tag{1}
\]
The [source-domain theorem](research_mixed_terminal_canonical_source_domain.md)
supplies the original oriented and inverse-leg source hypotheses.
Every comparison uses the same selected height \(H=1/100\), the
original reference action \(M_*\), and its full complex incoming
marginal \(p_*\).

The independent real targets remain \(a_R,b_R\in P=[.22,.45]\),
with \(\Im a=H\) and \(\Im b=\Im b_*\). The original active-index
intervals are unchanged. The complete selected weight
\(d_N(X_a)X_a^{-N}\) and incoming modulus allocation
\(|d_N(X_b)|^{1/2}\) remain present. The alternatives
\(\nu=1\) and \(\nu=0\) mean factorial and forced, respectively;
both retain the full target interval \(P\).

Let \(j\) denote one of the immutable closed source cells meeting the
certified \(a_*\)-image. Write \(U_{\epsilon,f,j}\) for its saved
bare source upper for family \(f\), and \(E_{\epsilon,\nu,j}\)
for the joined-plane upper in the
[source-cell theorem](research_mixed_terminal_source_cell_joined_plane.md).
The cell restricts only the reference \(a_*\). It does not restrict
the target \(a\), \(b\), or any active index. On that reference cell,
\[
 \sup D_{\epsilon,\nu,f}
       \le E_{\epsilon,\nu,j}+U_{\epsilon,f,j},
 \qquad
 D=\text{allocated profile rate}
        -\Re\{M_*+p_*(b-b_*)\}.
\tag{2}
\]
The 45 type-three cells and 25 type-four cells cover the complete
respective image rectangles, including their boundary contacts.
Consequently the uniform bound is
\[
 B^{\rm cell}_{\epsilon,\nu,f}
   =\max_j\{E_{\epsilon,\nu,j}+U_{\epsilon,f,j}\}.
\tag{3}
\]
The same cell index is essential in (3).

The [earlier global bound](research_mixed_terminal_uniform_real_face_outcome.md)
\(B^{\rm old}_{\epsilon,\nu,f}\) remains valid on exactly the same
domain. Thus their minimum is another valid upper:
\[
 B^{\rm best}_{\epsilon,\nu,f}
   =\min\{B^{\rm old}_{\epsilon,\nu,f},
                        B^{\rm cell}_{\epsilon,\nu,f}\}.
\tag{4}
\]
This takes the minimum of two bounds for the same branch. It does not
take the minimum between the two complete-weight alternatives.

## 3. Updated conditional complete-kernel implication

Use the original 27-label terminal envelope and the normalization of
[the conditional kernel assembly](research_mixed_terminal_uniform_kernel_assembly.md).
Assume the five entries in the second table have a common strict
uniform gap \(\gamma_5>0\), on the domains of Section 1. Then the
previously available comparisons give
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c}
 \text{comparison}&\text{available raw gap}\\ \hline
 \text{main, both terminal types and alternatives}&1/400\\
 \text{all sixteen penalized alias endpoints}&1/20\\
 \text{constant profile}&1/100\\
 \text{31 paid real/entropy branches}&>1/400 .
 \end{array}
\tag{5}
\]
The alias and constant dependencies are the
[uniform alias completion](research_mixed_terminal_uniform_alias_completion.md)
and [constant-profile theorem](research_mixed_terminal_uniform_constant_profile.md).
The main dependency is the
[uniform main comparison](research_mixed_terminal_uniform_main_comparison.md).
Their original hypotheses and selected height agree with Section 1.

It follows that the complete local allocated kernel would have the
common raw gap
\[
                       \delta=\min\{\gamma_5,1/400\}.
\tag{6}
\]
Indeed, replace the seven hypotheses in the earlier assembly by the
five remaining hypotheses above and the two new forced signs.
The improved forced right-cut bound removes the earlier smaller
explicit floor. Every nonmain ordinary label is pointwise dominated
by its main label with an additional positive gap; that domination
does not reduce (6).

Under these five hypotheses, real-cut bounds and penalized alias
endpoints control all vertical-side endpoints and joins by the
original height-convexity argument. This use of real cuts is still
conditional; the alias endpoint result alone does not pay an
uncontrolled zero-height endpoint. The physical entropy and constant
profiles retain all remaining finite rows and support-wall sectors.
The resulting literal estimate would be
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\left|C_{1,N}(X_b,X_a)\,
       \mathcal V_{\epsilon,N}(w,X_a)\,
       d_N(X_a)X_a^{-N}\right|\,|d_N(X_b)|^{1/2}\\
 &\qquad\le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
   \exp N\{\Re[M_*+p_*(b-b_*)]-\delta+\tau\}.
 \end{split}
\tag{7}
\]
The finite-source tolerances and polynomial factor can then be
absorbed to retain any gap at most \(\delta/2\), in particular
\[
                   \frac12\min\{\gamma_5,1/400\}.
\tag{8}
\]
The same compact ordinary-color neighborhoods, fixed color jets and
both level parities as in the earlier assembly are retained. Color
derivatives act on the original integer-weighted holomorphic source
before the modulus halfweight is allocated.

For an actual open-word suffix of length \(m\ge3\) with final types
\((4,4,3+\epsilon)\), original uniqueness identifies its reference
with this canonical family. The incoming reference is attached when
\(m=3\) and ordinary when \(m>3\), as in the earlier assembly.

The five hypotheses are not proved here. Thus (7) is conditional.
Even after they are proved, its scope is the selected terminal
plateau with unselected incoming leg, external reference \(K_4\),
and an ordinary preceding type-four factor. It does not control a
selected incoming leg, incoming type-three references, length-two
endpoint compositions, outer real tails, or nonplateau joining
contours. The incoming affine marginal still has to be matched to
the adjacent original source. No whole-word asymptotic, nonzero
global coefficient, or knot-volume theorem follows from this note.
