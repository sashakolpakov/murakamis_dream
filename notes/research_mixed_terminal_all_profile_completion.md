## 3. Complete finite two-factor theorem

For any of the four fixed canonical references define
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal K_{N,\beta,\epsilon}(b,a;w)
 &=\left|C_{1,N}(X_b,X_a)\mathcal V_{\epsilon,N}(w,X_a)
                    d_N(X_a)X_a^{-N}\right|

                                      -\tfrac\beta2g(b_*),\\

 \end{split}
 \tag{5}
\]
Here \(P_\epsilon\) is the original stationary terminal action.
The incoming half-power is an allocation of moduli, not a holomorphic

**Theorem 1.** For every sufficiently small fixed \(\tau>0\), a
sufficiently small fixed ordinary-color disk and constants

\[
 \mathcal K_{N,\beta,\epsilon}(b,a;w)
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}

 \tag{6}
\]
uniformly for \(\Re b,\Re a\in P=[.22,.45]\),
\(\Im b=\Im b_*\), and \(\Im a=.01\), for all sufficiently
