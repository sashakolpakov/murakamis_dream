# A stronger actual half-input outgoing ceiling

Private exact-source deduction, 8 November 2026. For the actual
ordinary \(343\) family, the exact outgoing polynomial has upper
rate \(\Lambda-17/10\) on the whole fixed spectral ellipse of
height \(1/2\). This includes a fixed complex neighborhood of the
complete-color point and every physical outgoing row and current.
The improvement uses the adopted \(343\)-specific shared radius,
the original allocated action floor, and the fixed face \(q=9/20\).

This ceiling is still insufficient by itself to pay the complete-color
inner factor. No mathematical numerical evaluation or frozen-source
edit is used.

## 1. Actual reference and unchanged finite polynomials

Use complete words in the original powers three and four beginning
\(343\), of length at least five, at \(w=-2\) and \(Z=Z_*\).
Keep
\[
y_*=t_2^*=\alpha+ic,\qquad t_*=s_3^*,\qquad z_*=t_3^*,\qquad
\Lambda=\Re\mathcal P_0^+(-2,z_*)>\frac{419}{200}.
\tag{1}
\]
The actual domains give
\[
\frac6{25}<\alpha<\frac9{25},\quad
R_*=e^{2\pi c},\quad \frac7{10}<R_*<\frac{41}{50},\quad
-\frac3{100}<\Im t_*,\Im z_*<\frac1{50},\quad
\frac3{10}\le\Re z_*\le\frac{39}{100}.
\tag{2}
\]
The stronger radius interval is exactly
[the correlated \(343\) height theorem, equations (2)--(4)](research_mixed_cap_343_correlated_height_bounds.md):
its coordinate \(y=t_2\) is this \(y_*\), and its
\(R=|e^{-2\pi i y}|\) is this \(R_*\).
The general type-four containing domain gives only \(R>10/19\);
it is not substituted for the actual refinement.
The third vertex is ordinary. The terminal word \(343\) of length
three is excluded.

Keep the notation of
[weighted outgoing](research_mixed_next_middle_weighted_outgoing_kernel.md)
and [the fixed ellipse](research_mixed_next_middle_fixed_spectral_ellipse.md):
\[
\begin{gathered}
D=N-1,\quad r=e^{-2\pi i/N},\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad
M=\lceil N/2\rceil,\quad U=D-M,\quad Y_N=r^M,\\
L=\lfloor N/6\rfloor,\quad d=U-L<N/3,\quad K=\lfloor N/4096\rfloor,\\
P_k=(r;r)_k,\quad p_k(V)=(rV;r)_k,\qquad
\mathscr A_N(\xi,T)=\sum_{\ell=0}^{d}\widehat P_{\ell,N}(\xi,T).
\end{gathered}
\tag{3}
\]
The \(\widehat P_{\ell,N}\) are the unchanged exact all-Casimir
products. Define
\[
\begin{split}
H_N^{\rm all}(\xi,Z_*)&=\sum_{k=0}^{U}
 \frac{Y_N^{-k}p_k(Y_N)p_k(Z_*)}{P_k^2}
                   \mathscr A_N(\xi,e^{2\pi ik/N}),\\
H_N(\xi,Z_*)&=\sum_{k=K+1}^{U}
 \frac{Y_N^{-k}p_k(Y_N)p_k(Z_*)}{P_k^2}
                   \mathscr A_N(\xi,e^{2\pi ik/N}).
\end{split}
\tag{4}
\]
Both have degree at most \(d\) in \(\xi\).
Let \(\mathcal E\) be the filled ellipse bounded by
\(\xi=2\cos(\vartheta+i/2)\), \(\vartheta\in\mathbb R\).

**Theorem.** For every \(\tau>0\), uniformly for (1), both parities,
and all sufficiently large \(N\),
\[
\boxed{\quad
\sup_{\xi\in\mathcal E}
 \bigl(|H_N^{\rm all}(\xi,Z_*)|+|H_N(\xi,Z_*)|\bigr)
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}e^{N(\Lambda-17/10+\tau)}.
\quad}
\tag{5}
\]
This covers \([-2,2]\) and a fixed complex disk about \(-2\)
contained in the interior of \(\mathcal E\). Every fixed spectral
derivative has the same rate on a smaller such disk. No arbitrary
larger spectral neighborhood is asserted.

## 2. The actual allocated action exceeds \(91/50\)

Use exactly the allocated action
\[
A_*=\Re\mathcal P_{34}(-2,y_*)+\frac12\nu(y_*),
\qquad \nu(y_*)=-\Re g(y_*),
\tag{6}
\]
from [the allocated action floor, equations (1),(5),(10)](research_mixed_next_allocated_action_floor.md).
That source gives
\[
\Re\mathcal P_{34}(-2,y_*)>
\frac{350711}{192000}-\frac6{25}b(R_*)+\frac{b(R_*)^2}{20},
\qquad b(R)=\frac12\log\frac2{1+R^2},
\tag{7}
\]
\[
\frac12\nu(y_*)\ge
       \frac{\sin(2\pi\alpha)}{4\pi}\log(1+R_*).
\tag{8}
\]
These are applied on their same original branch and at their same
reference radius, now with the actual refinement (2).

Since \(R_*>7/10\),
\[
0<b(R_*)<\frac12\log\frac{200}{149}<\frac3{20}.
\tag{9}
\]
For the last inequality,
\[
e^{3/10}>
1+\frac3{10}+\frac9{200}+\frac9{2000}
=\frac{2699}{2000}>\frac{200}{149},
\]
whose final cross-product difference is \(2151>0\).
The quadratic in (7) decreases on \([0,3/20]\), so
\[
\Re\mathcal P_{34}(-2,y_*)>
\frac{350711}{192000}-\frac9{250}+\frac9{8000}
=\frac{344015}{192000}.
\tag{10}
\]
The real interval in (2) gives \(\sin(2\pi\alpha)>3/4\), and
the positive inverse-hyperbolic-tangent series gives
\(\log(17/10)>14/27\). With \(\pi<22/7\), (8) therefore gives
\[
\frac12\nu(y_*)>\frac3{16\pi}\frac{14}{27}>
\frac{49}{1584}.
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently
\[
\boxed{\quad
A_*>\frac{344015}{192000}+\frac{49}{1584}
=\frac{91}{50}+\frac{16975}{6336000}>\frac{91}{50}.
\quad}
\tag{12}
\]

## 3. Fixed-face calibration with both marginal signs

Keep the original marginal
\[
p=\Re\{g'(s_2^*+y_*)-\tfrac32g'(y_*)\}
\]
and third-factor supporting plane \(B_3(q,\Re z_*)\).
The complete source comparison in
[lower-output upper block, equations (17)--(18)](research_mixed_conditional_lower_output_upper_block.md)
gives
\[
A_*+p(q-\alpha)+B_3(q,\Re z_*)\le\Lambda,\qquad
-J(q)/2-p(q-\alpha)\le11/80\quad(p\le0),
\tag{13}
\]
where \(J(x)=\int_0^x\log(2\sin\pi v)\,dv\) on \((0,1)\).
Its ordinary-source and factorial allocations give, for each
controlling next main, real-cut, or last profile \(\phi\),
\[
\phi(q+ic)-\Re g(q+ic)
 \le\Lambda-A_*-J(q)/2-p(q-\alpha).
\tag{14}
\]
This is the profilewise consequence used in
[half-grid bounded prefix, equations (9)--(10)](research_mixed_next_three_half_grid_bounded_prefix.md):
it comes from the original individual row comparisons before finite
summation, not from estimating a coherent sum and then its terms.

Set \(q_b=9/20\). If \(p\ge0\), (2) gives \(q_b-\alpha>0\), hence
\[
\begin{split}
-J(q_b)/2-p(q_b-\alpha)
&\le-J(q_b)/2\\
&=\frac12\int_{9/20}^{1/2}\log(2\sin\pi v)\,dv
 <\frac1{40}\log2<\frac7{400}<\frac{11}{80}.
\end{split}
\tag{15}
\]
For \(p\le0\), retain (13). These exhaust the original marginal.
Thus (12)--(15) give for every controlling profile
\[
\phi(q_b+ic)-\Re g(q_b+ic)
 <\Lambda-\frac{91}{50}+\frac{11}{80}.
\tag{16}
\]

## 4. Transport to the literal half input

The original own-three phase is
\[
Q(t;y,z_*)=g(y+t)+g(z_*+t)-2g(t)-g(y)-g(z_*)+2\pi iyt .
\tag{17}
\]
Its main profiles use \(t=v+i\Im t_*\), \(3/20\le v\le1/2\);
the real cuts are \(t=3/20,1/2\). The last profile is
\(L(h)=2\pi(-h)_++2\pi(\Im z_*)_+\).
All logarithms keep their original factorial-strip lift.

First hold shared height \(c\) and increase \(q\) from \(q_b\)
to \(1/2\). Half-grid bounded prefix, equations (11)--(12), proves
for every main and real-cut profile
\[
\partial_q\{\Re Q(t;q+ic,z_*)-\Re g(q+ic)\}
 <\log\frac{80}{117}.
\tag{18}
\]
For the last profile the derivative is
\(-\log|1-R_*e^{-2\pi iq}|\), and obeys the same upper bound:
\[
|1-R_*e^{-2\pi iq}|^2>
1+\frac{49}{100}+\frac{133}{100}
=\frac{141}{50}>
\left(\frac{117}{80}\right)^2.
\tag{19}
\]
Here the retained exact cosine witness is
\(\cos(2\pi q)<-19/20\).

At \(q=1/2\), increase shared height from \(c\) to zero.
The original \(\Re g(1/2+ih)\) is identically zero.
For a fixed private index \(t=v+i\Im t\),
\[
\partial_h\Re Q(t;1/2+ih,z_*)
=-\Arg(1-e^{2\pi(h+\Im t)-2\pi i(1/2+v)})-2\pi v\le0.
\tag{20}
\]
The original shifted argument is at least \(-2\pi v\).
At \(v=1/2\), take its zero-safe one-sided strip limit.
The last profile is also nonincreasing as \(h\) increases to zero.
Consequently every profile at the limiting half input satisfies
\[
\phi(1/2)<
\Lambda-\left\{\frac{91}{50}-\frac{11}{80}
                   +\frac1{20}\log\frac{117}{80}\right\}
<\Lambda-\frac{17}{10}.
\tag{21}
\]
Indeed
\[
\log\frac{117}{80}>
2\,\frac{37}{197}=\frac{74}{197}>\frac7{20},\qquad
\frac{91}{50}-\frac{11}{80}+\frac7{400}=\frac{17}{10}.
\tag{22}
\]
The comparisons leave a fixed strict reserve.

If \(\Im t_*<0\), raise the private main to height zero as in
weighted outgoing, Section 3. Its derivative and curvature proof
is independent of the preceding ceiling and only decreases the
rate. Thus (21) holds on the same source line
\[
                         \Im t=h_+=\max(\Im t_*,0).
\tag{23}
\]

## 5. Complete currents, both parities, and the spectral ellipse

Apply weighted outgoing, Sections 4--5, with only its calibration
replaced by (21). The exact cuts and physical input remain
\[
a_N=\frac{\lfloor3N/20\rfloor+1/2}{N},\quad
b_N=\frac{U+1/2}{N},\quad y_N=M/N,\quad
                         y_N+b_N=1-\frac1{2N}.
\tag{24}
\]
Thus the incoming factorial stays prewall at both parities;
odd levels do not substitute \(Y_N=-1\). Every physical row
above \(U=D-M\) vanishes exactly.

The proof retains the original \(+N\) middle integral, upper
and lower horizontal aliases at heights \(8,-1/5\), the four
weighted vertical sides, and both full unweighted joins to (23).
Their phase and amplitude comparisons are unchanged. Lower
sides decrease at least \(7\pi/30\) per unit height; the lower
horizontal has reserve \(37\pi/1500\). All upper remainders
retain their original strict losses. The unweighted joins use
vertical convexity and the main/real-cut endpoints now paid in
(21). Moving to the exact cuts (24) costs \(O(\log N)\) in the
finite product exponent, including the half-index numerator wall.
No vanishing period factor is divided out.

For the spectral domain, use fixed spectral ellipse, Theorem 1
and Sections 4--5. For each \(0\le\ell\le d\), its exact product
on the whole \(\mathcal E\) has upper-contour bound
\(CN^Ae^{-\ell/400}\); lower reflection adds only
\(e^{2\pi\ell(-\Im t)_+}\), within the same retained alias debit.
The remaining positive low physical group is covered by that
source's exact comparison
\[
F(v)+B_\ell(v)\le F(3/20)-\ell/(400N),
\quad F(v)=\Re Q(v;1/2,z_*),\quad
B_\ell(v)=\frac{\ell}{N}
 \left(\frac{5103}{47600}+\log\cos\pi v\right).
\tag{25}
\]
It holds for \(0\le v\le3/20\), including zero by its continuous
finite-product upper bound. Its proof uses the original positive
row derivative and does not depend on the old exponent \(E_*\).
Equation (21) now pays its real-cut endpoint as well.

Let \(B_{\ell,N}^{\rm all}\) be (4) with the single insertion
\(\widehat P_{\ell,N}\). This full inventory gives uniformly in
\(\ell\)
\[
\sup_{\mathcal E}|B_{\ell,N}^{\rm all}|
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
        e^{N(\Lambda-17/10+\tau)-\ell/400}.
\tag{26}
\]
All original finite measures and contour prefactors cost only
powers of \(N\).

The subtraction \(0\le k\le K\) must also be paid on this domain.
Before insertion its absolute rate is at most \(1/200\).
The fixed-ellipse product adds at most \(5103\ell/(47600N)\).
Including the degree gain to be retained in (26), its rate is less than
\[
\frac1{200}+
\frac{\ell}{N}\left(\frac{5103}{47600}+\frac1{400}\right)
<\frac1{200}+\frac1{25}+\frac1{1200}
=\frac{11}{240}<\frac1{20}
<\frac{79}{200}<\Lambda-\frac{17}{10}.
\tag{27}
\]
Here \(\ell/N<1/3\), \(5103/47600<3/25\), and (1) were used.
Thus subtraction proves (26) for \(B_{\ell,N}^{>}\), with \(k>K\);
no error is dropped inside a coherent sum.
Summing over the exact range \(0\le\ell\le d\) proves (5).
The real interval and a fixed disk about \(-2\) lie inside the
proved ellipse. Cauchy's formula on a smaller central disk gives
the spectral derivative assertion.

## 6. Quantitative scope

The former common rate was \(E_*=\Lambda-2383/1500-1/50\).
The improvement is
\[
\frac{17}{10}-\frac{2383}{1500}-\frac1{50}=\frac{137}{1500}.
\tag{28}
\]
The full source, its two versions in (4), and every cutoff are
unchanged. But the adopted complete inner action satisfies
\(\mathfrak M_3>9/5>17/10\).
Therefore this ceiling does not certify an absolute estimate for
the central outgoing polynomial times that inner factor below
\(\Lambda\). It does not lower-bound \(H_N\) or the signed outer
observation. The decisive correlated comparison or cancellation
estimate remains open.
