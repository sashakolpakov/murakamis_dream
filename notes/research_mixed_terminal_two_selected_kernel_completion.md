# Completing the terminal window with two retained selections

Research completion, 6 November 2026. Both sufficient upper bounds
\(U_\mu\) for the additional shared-debit budgets are negative on the
complete canonical reference families. This proves
a uniform bound for the original three-factor terminal window with its
last two retained coordinates selected. It also permits those sectors
to be added to the previously constructed plateau polynomial. The full
open source, its remaining contour sectors, and a new knot-family
theorem are not conclusions of this note.

## 2. The complete literal terminal source

Write \(c=z_{m-3}\), \(b=z_{m-2}\), \(a=z_{m-1}\), and
let \(r,s\) be the two ordinary indices. Put
\(P=[.22,.45]\), \(H=1/100\), and \(X_z=e^{-2\pi iz}\).
The external coordinate \(c\) is unselected; both \(b,a\)
have target height \(H\). Define the original allocated modulus
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal L_N={}&
 \left|C_{1,N}(X_c,X_b)C_{1,N}(X_b,X_a)
       \mathcal V_{\epsilon,N}(w_R,X_a)
       d_N(X_b)X_b^{-N}d_N(X_a)X_a^{-N}\right|
                                  |d_N(X_c)|^{\gamma/2}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{3}
\]
This is exactly source (21) of the
[composition theorem](research_mixed_terminal_two_selected_budget_composition.md).
Each selected integer weight occurs once. The incoming halfweight is
a modulus allocation; no holomorphic square root is introduced.

Use the [original terminal phase](research_mixed_terminal_joint_main_budget.md)
\(\Phi_\epsilon(a;t,v)\), where \(v=\xi\) for \(\epsilon=0\)
and \(v=e\) for \(\epsilon=1\). With the original regular-strip
primitive and chirps, put
\[
 \begin{split}
 Q(r;c,b)&=g(r+c)+g(r+b)-3g(r)-g(c)-g(b)+2\pi icr,\\
 \mathcal F&=Q(r;c,b)+Q(s;b,a)+\Phi_\epsilon(a;t,v)
                  -g(b)-g(a)-\frac\gamma2g(c),\\
 \mathcal M_*&=\mathcal F(q_*),\\
 p_c&=\mathcal F_c(q_*)
    =g'(c_*+r_*)-(1+\gamma/2)g'(c_*)+2\pi ir_*.
 \end{split}
 \tag{4}
\]
Here \(q_*\) is the exact original stationary reference, and all six
internal derivatives vanish. The full complex marginal \(p_c\)
is retained. In particular no enclosing midpoint is used as an
exact critical point.

**Theorem 1.** For every fixed positive tolerance \(\eta\), (3)
has a uniform finite-source bound of the form
\[
 \mathcal L_N\le C_\eta N^{A_\eta}
 \exp N\left[
   \Re\{\mathcal M_*+p_c(c-c_*)\}-\frac1{2600}+\eta\right].
 \tag{5}
\]
The real targets \(c_R,b_R,a_R\) vary independently over \(P\).
The original complete ordinary and terminal contours, including
their physical complements and vertical sides, are retained.
The bound holds for every real \(w_R\in[-2,2]\), and on a fixed
sufficiently small complex neighborhood of the complete ordinary
color. It holds through both level parities. Every fixed color jet
has the corresponding bound on a smaller such neighborhood.
In particular, after one aggregate tolerance allowance,
\[
 \mathcal L_N\le C N^A
 \exp N\left[
   \Re\{\mathcal M_*+p_c(c-c_*)\}-\frac1{5200}\right].
 \tag{6}
\]
The uniform family is the actual canonical \(K_{3+\gamma}\)
family identified above, with the inherited original source
eligibility. It is not an independently enlarged product of image
rectangles or an assertion about arbitrary external points in
\(D_3\cup D_4\).

*Proof.* Let \(\mu,\nu\in\{0,1\}\) denote the factorial
value one and forced value zero of the two complete selected-weight
alternatives. Keep \(I_1=P\) and \(I_0=[.4,.45]\). The
[simultaneous branch-domain theorem](research_mixed_selected_weight_branch_domains.md)
justifies these restrictions pointwise for the product of complete
weights. It does not delete an additive source summand. The forced
conjugates retain their true reference centers and their possibly
negative values even when those centers lie outside \(I_0\).

The two-selected window has the simultaneous costs
\(2g_r,(\mu+1)g_H,2g_s\), together with the costs of its
terminal certificate route. The shared \(s\)-force is \(u+v_s\),
where \(u<0<v_s\). Its one available cost is used once. By the
[shared-index cancellation theorem](research_mixed_terminal_shared_index_gain_cancellation.md),
\[
 G_s(u+v_s)-G_s(v_s)
       \le (1/2-\Re s_*)\,(-u-2v_s)_+.
 \tag{7}
\]
The protocol bounds the right side using the same variables
\(\kappa=\pi\Delta_b\) and \(I_{sb}\) that occur in the
ordinary forces. Its \(U_\mu\) therefore bounds the complete
additional trial and the \(r,b\) gains, plus an upper for (7).
The signed uncertain-center cubic formula and successive endpoint
bounds are applied to the exact convex scalar gains before rounding.
For the forced branch the same endpoint value \(q_e\) occurs in
the trial and the force. Thus no correlation asserted in the proof
is removed when passing to (2).

Every completed one-selected terminal route has upper
\(\mathfrak A_{\sigma,\nu}<-1/2600\), by the
[complete terminal theorem](research_mixed_terminal_uniform_kernel_completion.md).
The composition proof gives, pointwise on the original reference
family,
\[
       \mathfrak D_{\mu,\nu,\sigma}
                    \le \mathfrak A_{\sigma,\nu}+U_\mu
                    <-1/2600.
 \tag{8}
\]
This uses only nonpositivity in (2); its additional strict margin
is left unused. The identity applies to main, private-cut, direct
real-cut and entropy routes and to the joined-plane routes for
aliases and constants. Their original source-cell associations
are unchanged. There are 108 terminal-label and Fourier comparisons
for each fixed \((\gamma,\epsilon)\); both ordinary seven-label
alphabets reduce by their proved pointwise comparisons, with added
loss. The terminal compiler includes the real endpoints needed for
vertical alias sides and all physical complementary terms.

The finite source inequalities now give (5), with an arbitrary
aggregate positive tolerance and a polynomial prefactor. The
[real-color lift](research_mixed_terminal_profile_real_color_lift.md)
uses the same full terminal upper envelope over the complete real
color period. The new ordinary factors and weights have no ordinary
color dependence, so this lift passes unchanged through (8).
The original holomorphic finite source yields the fixed-jet statement
by Cauchy's formula on smaller complex neighborhoods. No modulus
halfweight is differentiated. Finally choose the total tolerance
strictly below half the raw gap to obtain (6). \(\square\)

## 3. Adding the last two selected positions to the plateau polynomial

Fix a word satisfying (1), put \(h=m-1\), and let
\(r_j=a_j+ic_j\), \(1\le j\le h\), be its original retained
critical coordinates. Use the ordinary phases \(\phi_j\), first
cap \(L\), terminal phase \(\Pi\), action \(\Phi\) and
\(M=\Re\Phi(\mathbf r)\) of the
[unselected plateau theorem](research_mixed_full_unselected_plateau.md).
Let
\[
 I_m^{(4)}=\{j:6\le j\le m-5,\ \epsilon_j=1\}.
 \tag{9}
\]
For each \(S\subseteq I_m^{(4)}\), add the sector
\(S^{++}=S\cup\{h-1,h\}\). Its real coordinates are all in
\(P\); the two final selected heights are \(H=.01\), the
interior selected heights are \(7/500\), and all other heights
are their original \(c_j\). Its literal integrand is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathscr J_{S^{++},N}={}&
 U_{\epsilon_1,N}(w_L,X_1)
 \prod_{j=2}^{h}C_{\epsilon_j,N}(X_{j-1},X_j)
 \mathcal V_{\epsilon_m,N}(w_R,X_h)\\
 &\qquad\cdot\prod_{j=1}^{h}
 d_N(X_j)^{\epsilon_j}X_j^{-N\mathbf1_{j\in S^{++}}}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{10}
\]
Define \(E_N^{\mathrm{last\,two}}\) by summing its integrals over
\(P^h\) and restoring the original scalar
\(\kappa_N^{m+k_4}N^{m+k_4-1}\), where
\(k_4=\sum_j\epsilon_j\) and \(|\kappa_N|=1\).
There are no newly chosen source normalizations.

Theorem 1 replaces exactly factors \(h-1,h\) and the terminal
factor. At their original reference,
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal M_*&=\phi_{h-1}(r_{h-2},r_{h-1})
                  +\phi_h(r_{h-1},r_h)+\Pi(r_h),\\
 p_c&=\partial_1\phi_{h-1}(r_{h-2},r_{h-1}),\\
 \partial_2\phi_{h-1}+\partial_1\phi_h&=0,
 \qquad \partial_2\phi_h+\Pi'=0 .
 \end{split}
 \tag{11}
\]
Thus its full complex tangent replaces the sum of the old three
tangents, including both changed-height displacements. It consumes
the complete selected weights at \(h-1,h\), and half the
unselected weight at \(h-2\). The other half is supplied by
factor \(h-2\), or by the first cap when \(m=4\). For
\(\gamma=0\) both incoming halfweights are absent.

Every selected interior position \(j\in S\) has incident factors
\(j,j+1\), whose indices are at most \(m-4=h-3\).
They are disjoint from the two ordinary factors in Theorem 1.
The [interior-selection theorem](research_mixed_interior_selected_plateau.md)
supplies their complete bounds, including adjacent interior selections.
All remaining factors use the completed unselected tangents.
The original stationarity equations cancel their total affine
displacement exactly. Choosing the sum of their tolerances below
half the local raw gap proves
\[
 |E_N^{\mathrm{last\,two}}(w_L,w_R)|
                \le C N^A e^{N(M-1/5200)}.
 \tag{12}
\]
This holds on fixed small complex color neighborhoods, on the full
real color square, for both parities, and for fixed color jets on
smaller complex neighborhoods. Finite summation, integration volume
and the original scalar alter only the polynomial prefactor.

## 4. The restricted leading packet and its boundary estimate

Let \(Z_N^{\mathrm{int,last}}\) be the polynomial from the
[one-last-selection assembly](research_mixed_last_selected_plateau_completion.md).
Define
\[
 Z_N^{\mathrm{int,last\,two}}
       =Z_N^{\mathrm{int,last}}+E_N^{\mathrm{last\,two}}.
 \tag{13}
\]
The added selected sets contain \(h-1\); the earlier polynomial
has that position unselected. These contributions are disjoint.
Every ordinary color still has degree at most \(N-1\), since
the original finite endpoint polynomials have that degree and all
integration regions are fixed independently of the colors.

After shrinking the complex color disks if necessary, (12) is
exponentially smaller than the original leading packet. Consequently
\[
 Z_N^{\mathrm{int,last\,two}}
 =\kappa_N^{m+k_4}N^{-3/2}A(w_L,w_R)
       e^{N\mathcal W(w_L,w_R)}(1+O(N^{-1})),
                  \qquad A(-2,-2)\ne0,
 \tag{14}
\]
with exactly the old action, amplitude, branches and scalar.
The added sectors cannot cancel that coefficient.

**Corollary 2 (the stronger recorded margin).** The raw gap in (5)
may be replaced by \(1/100\), and the finite gap in (6) and (12)
by \(1/200\), with the same hypotheses and source allocation.
Indeed the exact saved audit proves \(U_\mu<-1/104\), and
\[
                   \frac1{104}+\frac1{2600}=\frac1{100}.
 \tag{15}
\]
Apply (8) with this additional margin, then choose the aggregate
finite-source tolerance below \(1/200\). This strengthens the new
two-selected contribution only; it does not strengthen the older
sectors in (13) or alter their leading packet.

For real angles put
\(Q_N(\theta_L,\theta_R)=
 Z_N^{\mathrm{int,last\,two}}(-2\cos\theta_L,-2\cos\theta_R)\).
If \(\delta_\rho>0\) is the old real boundary gap away from the
complete-color angle, the triangle inequality and (12) retain gap
\(\min(\delta_\rho,1/5200)\). The unchanged local expansion,
degree bound and real gap satisfy the
[open-source stability lemma](research_mixed_open_source_stability_reduction.md),
so the same type of fixed angular-strip estimate holds for this
larger restricted polynomial. No angular loss is asserted for each
new terminal profile separately.

The sector with \(h-1\) selected but \(h\) unselected, other
boundary selections, and the exterior and joining contour regions
are still absent from (13). The suffix restriction (1) also remains.
Therefore (14) and its boundary stability estimate concern the
explicit restricted polynomial, not the whole original open source
or invariant. They do not change the currently proved knot-family
theorem or settle the finite-exceptions target.
