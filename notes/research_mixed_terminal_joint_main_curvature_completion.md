Research result, 6 November 2026. The factorial and forced joint main
comparisons now have a common gap greater than \(1/800\), uniformly

\[
 |\mathcal M_N(b,a)|\le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
 \exp N\left\{\Re[M_*+p_*(b-b_*)]-\frac1{800}+\tau\right\}.
 \tag{3}
\]
This is uniform on the full retained \(b_R,a_R\in P\), at their

rate gap \(1/1600\). This consequence concerns the indicated main
current only, not the whole \(\mathcal K_N\).
