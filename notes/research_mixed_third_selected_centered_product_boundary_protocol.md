# A bounded product-boundary pass using centered action differences

Fixed evaluation protocol, 6 November 2026. The
[saved-cell comparison](research_mixed_third_selected_recentered_cell_completion.md)
shows that preserving the first-order cancellation resolves the cell
that stopped the earlier pass. This protocol applies that proved
enclosure to the full product boundary. It does not increase the depth
or precision in response to the earlier inconclusive result.

## 1. Mathematical inequality and original parameter domains

Retain exactly the canonical family, eight certified center anchors,
two clipped domains, nine boundary charts, and twelve or six profiles
of the [center-comparison theorem](research_mixed_third_selected_center_comparison.md).
There are 162 ordered product-chart cases. Raw Cartesian chart images
are passed to the original four-leg reference provider without
clipping. Only a strict geometric incompatibility of an entire factor
interval excludes a parameter cell.

For every retained reference enclosure, use the
[centered action-difference lemma](research_mixed_third_selected_recentered_defect.md),
equations (7)--(12). The target support still uses the original labeled
concavity on the full real box. Both boundary marginals and the same
center-anchor correction are retained. The cell acceptance condition is
\[
 \max_{\rho}
 \{U_{\rho,\mathrm{centered\ trial}}
       +U_{\rho,\mathrm{target\ support}}-C_0^-\}<-1/2000.
\]
The hypothetical all-cell conclusion is exactly the same uniform
comparison as in the original center-comparison theorem.

## 2. Reuse completed computations without changing their scope

Bind the entire first product-boundary record at SHA-256
`e63cdc8ceb70046bac13ba855542657623c4b00e792ad367b0d7369c914a461e`
and its saved-data audit at
`9aa582ab0e3ab3914bd89737ed84e5963ee7bf65280ba1e2084bacf7e7b50647`.
When a new cell has exactly the same case, address, type flags and raw
external rectangles as a recorded cell, reuse its original reference
certificate. The old provider's analytic counters belong to that saved
certificate; they are not counted as new evaluations.

Likewise reuse each saved exact target trial when its reference and
profile label agree. Do not use the old optimizer's success flag as a
mathematical assumption. The new action enclosure and target support
are evaluated for all required labels. Saved references and trials
need no new root or optimization call.

For every other reference, the original provider makes one proposal
from its prescribed typed centers, at most twenty Newton steps and
fifty decimal digits. Its full-box original residual, not numerical
convergence, certifies the enclosure. Each new target uses one bounded
proposal with at most two hundred optimizer iterations. There are no
alternative seeds or retries.

## 3. Fixed budgets and stopping rules

The interval precision is 256 bits, combined binary depth is at most
sixteen, and at most 4,000 cells are visited. At most 2,000 retained
reference cells are processed, including reused certificates. Therefore
there are at most 2,000 new provider invocations, and fewer when saved
certificates are available. Keep separate counts for reference cells,
reused certificates, new provider invocations, new reference proposals,
reused target trials, and new target proposals.

Start all 162 chart roots in lexicographic order. Use depth-first
traversal and split the longer normalized parameter interval, choosing
the incoming parameter on ties. Keep closed endpoints in both children.
All labels on an enclosed reference are retained even if one does not
pass. An unresolved cell is subdivided only within the stated budget.

Stop at the first unresolved cell at depth sixteen, an unexpected
error, or a visit/reference-cell cap. Preserve every unexamined frontier
cell, and retain all already evaluated witnesses. Nonfinite arithmetic
or a nonnegative upper bound is inconclusive, never a geometric
exclusion. Do not change any limit or launch another pass automatically.

the ordinary two-factor comparison at position three, with its
