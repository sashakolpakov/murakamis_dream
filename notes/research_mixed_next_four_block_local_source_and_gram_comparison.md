# The actual four-block local source and its Gram comparison

Private source deduction, 9 November 2026. The complete proved
\(343\) transfer supplies the input to a further local attachment
of type three or four. This gives a nonzero local contribution
with its original two-variable normalization. It does not estimate
the complement of that next integral.

The new two-step Gram bound can therefore be compared with a
specified conditional action, rather than with a closed-word
volume. Its comparison on the original following families reduces
to two complete parameter-circle inequalities. No sign for those
inequalities is proved here. The task attachment was reread; no
mathematical numerical evaluation, import, compilation, saved-value
arithmetic or frozen-source edit is used.

## 1. Actual reference and exact next integral

Fix an original positive open word of length at least five whose
first four letters are
\[
                    \mathfrak w_\beta=(3,4,3,3+\beta),
                       \qquad \beta\in\{0,1\}.
                                                               \tag{1}
\]
Use its original geometric reference, with
\[
       y_*=t_3,\qquad s_*=s_4,\qquad z_*=t_4,\qquad c_*=s_5.
                                                               \tag{2}
\]
The fourth vertex is ordinary. At length five \(c_*\) is an
attached endpoint leg; at longer lengths it is ordinary.
In either case, if the fifth type is \(3+\nu\), the
[original role theorem](research_mixed_actual_last_row_reference_domains.md)
gives
\[
 y_*\in O_3\subset P_3,\qquad
 s_*,z_*\in O_{3+\beta}\subset P_{3+\beta},\qquad
 c_*\in P_{3+\nu}.
                                                               \tag{3}
\]
No private terminal coordinate is substituted for \(z_*\).

Write \(D=N-1\), \(r=e^{-2\pi i/N}\), \(P_j=(r;r)_j\), and
retain the whole conditional polynomial
\[
C_{\beta,N}(Y,Z)=
 \sum_{j=0}^D
 \frac{Y^{-j}(rY;r)_j(rZ;r)_j}{P_j^{2+\beta}}.
                                                               \tag{4}
\]
Let \(T_{343,N}(w,Y)\) be the complete polynomial of the
[two-variable transfer theorem](research_mixed_next_transfer_complex_neighborhood.md),
with incoming ordinary color \(w\). Its original packet near
\((-2,y_*)\) is
\[
T_{343,N}(w,e^{-2\pi iy})
 =N^{-2}A_{343}(w,y)e^{NP_{343}(w,y)}
                    (1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})).
                                                               \tag{5}
\]
Here and below the symbols and absolute primitive constants are
the original ones. In particular (5) concerns the complete input,
including its already paid left and right remainders.

The literal next transfer is
\[
\begin{split}
F_{\beta,N}^{(4)}(w,Z)
 &=\sum_{a=0}^D T_{343,N}(w,r^a)C_{\beta,N}(r^a,Z)\\
 &=N\operatorname{CT}_Y
       [T_{343,N}(w,Y)C_{\beta,N}(Y,Z)].
\end{split}                                                     \tag{6}
\]
Its \(Y\)-band is \([-D,D]\). There is no nonzero multiple of
\(N\) in that band, no old-four weight, and no second Fourier
sector. These are the exact conventions of
[Fourier propagation, (2)--(4)](research_mixed_next_attachment_fourier_propagation.md).

Choose a sufficiently small fixed \(\epsilon>0\), and orient
\(\gamma_*=[y_*-\epsilon,y_*+\epsilon]\) in increasing real
coordinate. Define the exact local integral
\[
\boxed{\quad
\mathcal J_{\beta,N}^{(4)}(w,z)
 =N\int_{\gamma_*}
       T_{343,N}(w,e^{-2\pi iy})
       C_{\beta,N}(e^{-2\pi iy},e^{-2\pi iz})\,dy .
\quad}                                                         \tag{7}
\]
It is a portion of the original increasing-coordinate period
representing (6). All endpoints in (7) stay fixed as \(w,z\)
vary. Both factors retain every finite row.

## 2. The original stationary phase and its Hessian

Use the original primitive \(g\) and put
\[
\begin{split}
Q_\beta(s;y,z)
 &=g(s+y)+g(s+z)-(2+\beta)g(s)+2\pi iys,\\
\psi_\beta(y,z)
 &=Q_\beta(s_\beta(y,z);y,z)-g(y)-g(z),\\
\Psi_\beta(y,s;w,z)
 &=P_{343}(w,y)+Q_\beta(s;y,z)-g(y)-g(z),\\
\varphi_\beta(y;w,z)
 &=P_{343}(w,y)+\psi_\beta(y,z).
\end{split}                                                     \tag{8}
\]
The conditional index root is the original zero-lift root.
At the base, the ordinary two-leg equations give
\[
(s_*,z_*)=F_{3+\beta}(y_*,c_*).
                                                               \tag{9}
\]
Consequently the
[complete reduced-disk conditional theorem](research_mixed_reduced_disk_conditional_packets.md)
supplies the full \(C_{\beta,N}\) packet on an ambient
neighborhood of \((y_*,z_*)\). The \(343\) input in (5) is
eligible at the same reference: it is precisely the prefix of
the original word in (1), with following inverse leg \(s_*\).
There is no substitution of an independently chosen old
reference for this correlated input.

Let \(\mathcal P_{\mathfrak w_\beta}\) be the absolute pre-Gram
action from the
[arbitrary-prefix theorem, (17)](research_mixed_arbitrary_prefix_pregram_curve.md).
At \(w=-2\), the stationary value in (8) satisfies
\[
\begin{split}
\Pi_\beta(z)
 &:=\operatorname*{crit}_{y,s}\Psi_\beta(y,s;-2,z)\\
 &=\mathcal P_{\mathfrak w_\beta}(z)-(1-\beta)g(z).
\end{split}                                                     \tag{10}
\]
To check the allocation, the prior source has action
\(P_{343}(-2,y)=\mathcal P_{343}(y)-g(y)\), whereas
\[
\psi_\beta(y,z)
 =V_\beta(s,z)+G(y,s)+g(y)-(1-\beta)g(z)
                                                               \tag{11}
\]
at its index root. Adding these expressions gives exactly
the four-block pre-Gram action and (10).
No terminal ordinary endpoint action has been added.

The stationary pair \((y_*,s_*)\) is nondegenerate.
Indeed the cap-initialized four-block action with its final
incidence \(G(z,c_*)\) has
\[
                     -\Re\mathcal A''\succ(6\pi/49)I
                                                               \tag{12}
\]
on the original convex product, by
[cap-phase entrance, Lemma 1](research_mixed_alternating_cap_phase_entrance.md).
Fixing \(z\) removes only a principal coordinate.
Eliminating the six earlier variables leaves
\(-\Psi_{\beta,(y,s)(y,s)}''\) as the corresponding Schur
complement. The final incidence and the subtraction in (10)
depend only on the fixed \(z\), so do not change this block.

For completeness, accretivity survives that Schur complement:
if \(K\) is the original negative Hessian, \(S\) its Schur
complement, and \(v\) a retained vector, choose the eliminated
vector \(u=-K_{11}^{-1}K_{12}v\). Then
\[
\Re(v^*Sv)=\Re((u,v)^*K(u,v))
             \ge(6\pi/49)(\|u\|^2+\|v\|^2).
                                                               \tag{13}
\]
Thus \(S\) is invertible and has positive Hermitian part.
This argument uses the complex symmetric Hessian, not a
real-matrix replacement.

The implicit function theorem gives the original holomorphic
stationary pair \((y^\#,s^\#)(w,z)\) near the base. Put
\[
\begin{split}
\Pi_\beta(w,z)&=\Psi_\beta(y^\#,s^\#;w,z),\\
k_s&=-Q_{\beta,ss}(s^\#;y^\#,z),\\
k_y&=-\varphi_{\beta,yy}(y^\#;w,z).
\end{split}                                                     \tag{14}
\]
At the base both sequential negative Hessians have positive real
part and \(\det(-\Psi'')=k_s k_y\). They remain nonzero on a
smaller fixed bidisk. This calibrates the Gaussian square roots
by the original increasing contours.

## 3. Full local source theorem and scalar restoration

Set
\[
a_0(v)=\sqrt{2\sin\pi v}\,e^{-i\pi v/2},\qquad
b_\beta(s;y,z)=
\frac{a_0(s+y)a_0(s+z)}
     {a_0(s)^{2+\beta}a_0(y)a_0(z)}.
                                                               \tag{15}
\]
The square roots are continued from the original positive sine
chamber.

**Theorem 1.** For the actual ordinary four-block reference
(1)--(3), the exact integral (7) satisfies on a fixed independent
complex bidisk about \((-2,z_*)\)
\[
\boxed{\begin{split}
\mathcal J_{\beta,N}^{(4)}(w,z)
 &=N^{-2-\beta/2}\mathcal A_\beta^{(4)}(w,z)
       e^{N\Pi_\beta(w,z)}(1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})),\\
\mathcal A_\beta^{(4)}(w,z)
 &=\frac{2\pi A_{343}(w,y^\#)
             b_\beta(s^\#;y^\#,z)}
            {\sqrt{k_s}\sqrt{k_y}}\ne0 .
\end{split}}                                                    \tag{16}
\]
The estimate is uniform on smaller fixed bidisks, at both
parities, with all fixed mixed derivatives of the relative
error \(O(N^{-1})\).

*Proof.* Shrink \(\gamma_*\) so that (5) and the ambient
conditional packet hold on its entire fixed neighborhood.
The latter is the complete finite expansion
\[
C_{\beta,N}(e^{-2\pi iy},e^{-2\pi iz})
 =N^{-(1+\beta)/2}
   \frac{\sqrt{2\pi}\,b_\beta(s_\beta;y,z)}
        {\sqrt{-Q_{\beta,ss}(s_\beta;y,z)}}
   e^{N\psi_\beta(y,z)}(1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})).
                                                               \tag{17}
\]
Every symbol argument stays in a compact regular strip.
Equations (12)--(14) give strict horizontal concavity on a
small neighborhood of \(y_*\). After shrinking the bidisk,
the exact fixed segment can be deformed to a horizontal
segment through \(y^\#\), with two short endpoint connectors.
The full integrand is entire in its finite source variables;
the local expansions hold throughout the deformation.
At the base the connectors have a fixed strict action loss
from concavity. Continuity preserves that loss after the
parameter shrink. The whole relative errors in (5),(17)
are kept inside this integral.

The outer Gaussian has factor
\(\sqrt{2\pi}/\sqrt{Nk_y}\). Its exact power is
\[
       N\cdot N^{-2}\cdot N^{-(1+\beta)/2}\cdot N^{-1/2}
                         =N^{-2-\beta/2}.
                                                               \tag{18}
\]
This proves (16). There is no \(a_0(y)^{-1}\) from an old-four
weight: the preceding vertex is three. All factors in the
coefficient are nonzero, with the branches in (14),(15).
Holomorphy and Cauchy estimates on smaller fixed bidisks give
the derivative assertion. \(\square\)

The original Fourier message normalization is still
\[
\mathcal E_\beta F_{\beta,N}^{(4)}
 =\frac{v(w)^TA_3A_4A_3A_{3+\beta}h}
             {\kappa^{5+\beta}N^{1+\beta}}.
                                                               \tag{19}
\]
In particular, at an output physical index \(b\), the local
pre-Gram coefficient is
\[
\kappa^{5+\beta}N^{1+\beta}P_b^{1-\beta}
                         \mathcal J_{\beta,N}^{(4)}(w,b/N).
                                                               \tag{20}
\]
On the regular complex output neighborhood, applying the original
factorial normalization to (16) gives
\[
\kappa^{5+\beta}N^{-1/2}
 a_0(z)^{1-\beta}\mathcal A_\beta^{(4)}(w,z)
 e^{N\{\Pi_\beta(w,z)+(1-\beta)g(z)\}}
                      (1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})).
                                                               \tag{21}
\]
This is a normalization of the local current. It does not define
a new off-grid interpolation of the full pre-Gram row.
All ordinary color derivatives in (16),(19) act on the original
\(v(w)\).

## 4. A source-matched comparison on two complete circles

Write \(c_{\rm b}=-2J(1/6)\), and retain exactly the profiles
\[
\begin{split}
V(a)&=-2J((1-a)/2)-J(a),\\
B_\beta(W)&=\max_{0\le s\le1}
 \left\{\int_0^s\log|1-We^{-2\pi iu}|\,du
                         -(1+\beta)J(s)\right\}
\end{split}                                                     \tag{22}
\]
of the [complete Gram bound](research_mixed_next_two_step_gram_fourier_bound.md).
Its full propagated right contribution has rate
\[
\mathcal C_\beta(W)=
 \Gamma(1/2)+V(1/2)+2c_{\rm b}+B_\beta(W),
                                                               \tag{23}
\]
in the original normalization \(\kappa^{-2}/N\).

For each following type \(\nu=0,1\), the arbitrary-prefix
theorem supplies the original holomorphic four-block curve
\[
z(c)=q_{\mathfrak w_\beta}(c),\qquad
H_{\mathfrak w_\beta}(c),\qquad c\in P_{3+\nu}.
                                                               \tag{24}
\]
Define on a neighborhood of that closed disk
\[
\begin{split}
\mathscr P_\beta(c)
 &=H_{\mathfrak w_\beta}(c)-G(z(c),c)
                                      -(1-\beta)g(z(c)),\\
\mathscr D_\beta(c)
 &=\Gamma(1/2)+V(1/2)+2c_{\rm b}
       +B_\beta(e^{-2\pi iz(c)})-\Re\mathscr P_\beta(c).
\end{split}                                                     \tag{25}
\]
At the actual reference (2), \(\mathscr P_\beta(c_*)\)
equals precisely \(\Pi_\beta(-2,z_*)\) in (16).
The all-disk phase in (24),(25) is proved independently of
a whole finite-source packet on that disk.

**Proposition 2.** The function \(\mathscr D_\beta\) is
continuous and subharmonic. Therefore, for \(\nu=0,1\),
\[
\boxed{\qquad
\max_{c\in P_{3+\nu}}\mathscr D_\beta(c)
       =\max_{c\in\partial P_{3+\nu}}\mathscr D_\beta(c).
\qquad}                                                        \tag{26}
\]

*Proof.* For each fixed \(s\), the integral of
\(\log|1-We^{-2\pi iu}|\) is subharmonic in \(W\).
Its logarithmic singularities are integrable. The same
uniform-integrability argument as in the Gram theorem
proves joint continuity of this potential in \((s,W)\)
on compact sets. Thus \(B_\beta\), its maximum over the
compact interval in (22), is continuous and subharmonic.
One may verify the submean inequality by selecting a
maximizing \(s\) at the center and applying the inequality
to that fixed potential. Holomorphic composition in
(24) preserves subharmonicity. The remaining term in
(25) is harmonic. The maximum principle proves (26).
\(\square\)

Consequently a uniform strict negative bound on the two
complete circles in (26) would pay the entire propagated
right contribution at every corresponding actual ordinary
four-block reference, against the nonzero local current
(16). No such negative bound is proved here.

There is also an exact way to display what the old physical
anchor does and does not pay. At an actual reference, write
\(Y_*=e^{-2\pi iy_*}\), \(W_*=e^{-2\pi iz_*}\), and let
\(E_0(Y_*)\) be the original half-input outgoing action of
the previous \(343\) transfer. Then (8),(10) give
\[
\begin{split}
\mathcal C_\beta(W_*)-\Re\Pi_\beta(-2,z_*)
={}&\{\Gamma(1/2)+E_0(Y_*)-\Re P_{343}(-2,y_*)\}\\
 &+\{V(1/2)+2c_{\rm b}+B_\beta(W_*)
                    -E_0(Y_*)-\Re\psi_\beta(y_*,z_*)\}.
\end{split}                                                     \tag{27}
\]
The [physical half-input anchor](research_mixed_next_physical_half_input_action_anchor.md)
bounds the first braces by \(-1/(2\cdot10^{13})\).
It supplies no sign for the second braces. In particular the
positive Gram norm cost cannot be treated as the original
stationary conditional increment \(\Re\psi_\beta\).

Even if the comparison in (26) were settled favorably, a
whole next-transfer theorem would still require the actual
\(\mathsf T_{0\beta}E_N^-\) and the complement of the next
local integral to be paid. Equations (16),(23)--(27) supply
the local reference and a specific full-source upper bound
for one of those components; they do not suppress the other
components by a local packet alone. No ordinary closing or
short-partial-filling theorem is inferred.
