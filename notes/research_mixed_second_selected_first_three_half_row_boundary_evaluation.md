# A saved-boundary half-row calculation for a first block of type three

Research evaluation specification, 6 November 2026. This is a guarded
calculation design, not a sign assertion. It applies to the original
first flag zero, second type four and outgoing type three:
\(\alpha=0,\beta=0,H=1/100\). Execution requires a completed and
independently audited first-type-three central boundary certificate.
Its reference bank, audit identities and exact inventory counts remain
unset until that certificate exists. No first-type-four reference,
response bound or numerical sign is transferred.

## 1. The original half-row comparison

Use the canonical prefix and original marginal tangents of the
[first-type-three extension](research_mixed_second_selected_first_three_extension.md).
The allocated reference phase is
\[
 F=Q_1(u;a,z)+Q_0(v;z,b)-g(z),\qquad
 p_L=g'(a+u)-g'(a)+2\pi iu,\qquad
 p_R=g'(v+b)-g'(b).
 \tag{1}
\]
In particular no incoming or outgoing allocated halfweight is present
when \(\alpha=\beta=0\). The original reference and its affine
marginals remain fixed when the target outgoing index is placed on the
real half row \(v=1/2\). The reference value \(g(v_*)\) and the
complex half-row constant remain in the exact trial difference.

Equation (28) of the
[coupled half-row budget](research_mixed_second_selected_half_row_budget.md)
bounds both literal defects by
\[
 T_\nu+\phi_u^*(A)
 +\Gamma_{\mu_\nu}(P_\nu;.22-\Re z_*,.45-\Re z_*)
 +\Gamma_{57/10}(D_b;.22-\Re b_*,.45-\Re b_*),
 \quad \mu_0=32/5,\quad\mu_1=69/10.
 \tag{2}
\]
Here \(T_\nu\) is exactly equation (9) of that proof, with
\(\beta=0\); \(\nu=0\) is forced and \(\nu=1\) is factorial.
Both branches use the entire original selected-coordinate interval
\([.22,.45]\), and the incoming ordinary-index maximum is legitimately
restricted to \([.22,.5]\) by the original index barrier. No main/main
forced-domain restriction is used. The incoming force vanishes after
subtracting the correct \(p_L\). Its remaining nonnegative cost may
be dropped also for first flag zero, so the scalar formulas in (2)
are unchanged in the new reference family.

The [reviewed adapter](research_mixed_second_selected_first_three_half_row_evaluation.md)
calls the frozen original half-row arithmetic once. It returns the
new first-flag metadata together with the frozen calculation's legacy
label and a canonical hash and restoration recipe for its complete
original result. Every branch, status, count and scalar witness is
preserved. It does not supply reference existence or stationarity;
those come from the new certified bank.

## 2. The response cost on a complete external boundary

Sections 3--4 of the
[half-row boundary extension](research_mixed_second_selected_half_row_boundary_extension.md)
apply to (1) with first flag zero. Their formal phase has constant
target fourth coordinate and uses
\[
 \Lambda_R=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0,0,1),\qquad
 \mathcal B(c)=|p_L'(c)a_*'(c)|+|p_R'(c)b_*'(c)|.
 \tag{3}
\]
The four active target variables have the required nonpositive real
Hessian, and the formal fourth Hessian row and column vanish. The
original reference equations \(F_u=F_z=F_v=0\) preserve the literal
reference debit and boundary tangents. Thus the same response already
certified for the new central comparison bounds the half-row Laplacian.
Only the generic proof is reused; the numerical corollaries for first
flag one in that note are not inputs.

For each of \(K_3,K_4\), independently minimize the saved lower
boundary deficit over every reference cell and both branches, and
maximize the saved response upper bound over that whole boundary:
\[
 \gamma_K=\min_{I,\nu}(-U_{I,\nu}),\qquad
 B_K=\max_I B_I,\qquad
 \eta_K=\gamma_K-c_KB_K,
 \quad c_{K_3}=\frac{6889}{2000000},\quad
 c_{K_4}=\frac9{5000}.
 \tag{4}
\]
The two constants are half the squared radii \(.083\) and \(.060\).
They are applied to separate global extrema, not to cellwise
compensated minima. The inherited strict whole-image height and
common-product certificates extend harmonically where required.
Equation (4) is a whole-domain bound only after every nonexcluded
boundary interval has a finite half-row bound. Positivity proves a
uniform comparison. The additional recorded test \(\eta_K>1/3200\)
is merely a fixed candidate gap; it is not an assumed sign or a
transferred central theorem.

## 3. The guarded input and full nine-chart inventory

The proposed input is
`repro/results/research-mixed-second-selected-first-three-boundary-reference-bank.json.gz`,
produced by the
[first-type-three saved-reference extractor](../experiments/extract_mixed_second_selected_first_three_boundary_references.py).

`repro/results/research-mixed-second-selected-first-three-external-boundary-saved-audit.json`.
Before execution the driver must receive their final SHA-256 identities,
the final source, checker and extractor identities, and the exact
beta-zero accepted, typed and excluded inventory counts. Any missing
identity or count is a hard pre-execution guard. The source must be the
completed audited original-reference cover, with both outgoing flags
and all eighteen case trees complete and positive central corrected
bounds. The new driver reads the compact bank and audit; it only hashes
the large compressed source to verify its binding.

The bank must have first flag zero, precision 256, no pending or
unresolved frontier and no first-type-four reference data. The driver
verifies every inherited binding before importing arithmetic providers.
It checks all compact payload hashes for the selected references, their
reference/response family and index associations, the exact saved
coordinate, shape and derivative inventories, and the response's
strict height and common-product flags. Each saved response upper bound
must equal the upper endpoint of its original interval.

All five \(K_3\) and four \(K_4\) boundary charts are retained.
For each chart, the original binary addresses must be distinct,
prefix-free and have total mass one. Every accepted terminal has exactly
one corresponding reference, and every selected reference has exactly
one accepted terminal. Both original exclusion types are preserved:
strict external-chart geometry and certified necessary reference-domain
exclusion. Neither is replaced by a new decision. The independently
audited exact source associations justify the stored exclusion witnesses.
The reference boxes remain conditional on actual external parameters in
their saved chart intervals; finite-suffix realization is not asserted.

Each beta-zero reference is evaluated exactly once, in its saved order.
Coordinates and negative shapes are reconstructed outward from their
exact rational endpoints at 256 bits. They are never replaced by a
midpoint reference or combined across cells. The original derivative
and response certificates are reused; no derivative is recomputed.

## 4. Bounded arithmetic, preservation and completion

The [new standalone driver](../experiments/certify_mixed_second_selected_first_three_half_row_boundary.py)
is adapted from the frozen first-type-four boundary-pass control logic.
There is no root proposal, source evaluation, transport, matrix inverse,
parameter subdivision or new reference call. Old source and reference
callbacks are disabled. The adapter calls the frozen half-row helper
once per visited reference, with at most one node conjugate and eleven
centered primitive evaluations. Both original Fourier alternatives and
all resulting witnesses are saved. The driver's wrapper explicitly
checks first flag zero and verifies the adapter's exact restoration
hash; the legacy first-flag-one helper label is recorded only as
arithmetic provenance.

A routine arithmetic failure is recorded as unresolved and the fixed
pass continues. Finite upper bounds of either sign are retained without
refinement. An unexpected error or explicit stop signal stops the pass,
leaving all remaining bank indices pending. All inherited geometry and
exclusion witnesses remain in the output. A changed dependency, an
unexpected error, any unresolved or pending reference, or a stop signal
prevents a complete-comparison claim. The response maxima are compared
with the independently audited central extrema. A successful whole-domain
claim requires both values in (4) to be strictly positive.

The output path must be fresh and end in `.json.gz`. Exclusive streamed
`gzip.open(..., 'xt', compresslevel=1, encoding='utf-8')` with compact
`json.dump` preserves every field while bounding disk use. The reported
output SHA-256 binds the compressed bytes. Dependencies and the new
script's own hash are saved and checked again after the pass. No old
script, proof, source record or bank is modified.

No sign or complete half-row theorem is asserted by this specification.
Even a successful calculation would supply this local second-selection
comparison for first type three and outgoing type three. It would not
itself establish all finite source currents, simultaneous endpoint
selections, the entire invariant or the cofinite filling-volume theorem.
