# A-polynomials, selected periods and the volume problem

25 November 2026. This page distinguishes the algebraic objects in the
research program. The [papers](../papers/README.md) state the proved
results and their hypotheses.

## A classical branch does not select a quantum solution

An algebraic gluing curve records possible peripheral eigenvalues
\((M,L)\). A recurrence relates consecutive terms of a sequence.
Neither datum alone specifies the scalar obtained by the original knot
evaluation. Initial values, the prescribed closing, normalization and
derivative data at singular steps can all be needed to select it.

The [all-level period note](actual_conway_pointed_quantum_a_volume.md)
explains these additional data in its recurrence formulation. Recovering
the same invariant transfers an already established limit for a covered
knot. It does not prove a volume theorem for every quantization of a
classical curve.

The [meridian-expansion paper](../papers/meridian-expansions/README.md)
proves classical-symbol divisibility under a specified genuine
annihilating operator. It does not construct that operator. The
[preferred-longitude paper](../papers/preferred-longitudes/README.md)
supplies the geometric peripheral comparison under its stated
conditions. A selected irreducible factor and a full recurrence ideal
are different conclusions.

## Why the period and torsor viewpoint remains useful

A change of coordinates can preserve an algebraic equation while changing
a scalar factor, a boundary functional or a chosen section. Those data
must be retained. The
[initialized coideal period](research_coideal_normalized_marked_period.md)
constructs a source and marked observation with explicit commuting
unipotent changes between neighboring-height presentations. It is finite
algebra at the prescribed level; it does not identify a knot peripheral
germ or geometric integration cycle.

The [coideal Fourier compiler](research_coideal_full_fourier_crossing.md)
retains the crossing and cup source in orthogonal coordinates. The
[cyclic Hardy comparison](research_cyclic_atom_hardy_head_comparison.md)
controls actual scalar functions and their finite coefficient
projections. A lower bound for the original marked closure still
requires a further argument.

## The unresolved implication

To cross arbitrary short partial fillings, one must show that the original
selected source has the required geometric exponential rate, with its
prescribed closing and cancellation retained. A local algebraic critical
point, nonzero abstract solution or positive ambient norm is insufficient.
The [research status](research_finite_exceptions_progress.md) states the
remaining theorem and the orthogonal variational reduction.
