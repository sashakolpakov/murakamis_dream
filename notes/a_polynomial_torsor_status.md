# A-polynomial volume limits and torsors: current status

The [pointed quantum A-period theorem](actual_conway_pointed_quantum_a_volume.md)
reconstructs the reduced Kashaev invariant exactly from its genuine
all-level recurrence, finite initial values and the derivative data
needed at singular steps. For the covered knot families its volume
limit follows from the theorem for that same scalar invariant.

The proved families now include the long-net-twist Conway
constructions and the
[quantitative ordered circle trees](actual_all_circle_quantitative_volume.md).
The latter satisfy
\(\sum_v d_v/(d_v^2+n_v^2)\le2^{-25}\), with the original knot and
framing conditions. Their volume is the original knot-complement
volume; the meridian profile and preferred-cusp correction are also
proved.

The [local tree theorem](actual_all_circle_local_message_volume.md)
adds the alternative condition
\(\max_v d_v/(d_v^2+n_v^2)\le2^{-120}\). It gives a twist threshold
independent of the number of vertices and the same exact pointed
operator-period volume limit.

The [full microscopic expansion](actual_main_meridian_filtered_characteristic.md)
now determines an analytic curve \(M=e^u\),
\(L=-e^{\mathcal S'(u)}\) that lies in the classical symbol of every
specified genuine all-level annihilator. Its selected irreducible
factor is reciprocal. The note gives a rational peripheral formula
and, for the circle trees, explicit finite rational critical equations.

For the covered circle-tree presentations, the [geometric longitude theorem](actual_all_circle_geometric_characteristic.md)
now identifies the whole curve with the preferred geometric eigenvalue
germ. The proof constructs nonparabolic representations of the original
pieces and retains the puncture eigenlines under half twists. The
boundary pairing makes the residual longitude factor independent of
the auxiliary parameters. The source field has no additional algebraic
constants over the main meridian, and regular reconstruction at every
nonzero meridian value excludes further zeros or poles. The remaining
monomial is fixed by the preferred framing; a Seifert surface fixes the
negative complete lift.

The subsequent [strict-tree longitude proof](actual_strict_tree_geometric_characteristic.md)
extends this identification to the covered strict long-filling
presentations, including their bare product junctions. The
[directed all-orders theorem](actual_all_circle_directed_all_orders.md)
also supplies the expansion required for every covered directed
circle-tree certificate.

Under these respective hypotheses, the selected geometric irreducible
factor divides the first nonzero classical symbol of every genuine
all-level annihilator. This does not identify the full A-polynomial,
all quantum solutions, or an entire recurrence ideal. In particular,
the decorated planar volume theorem alone supplies no corresponding
moving-meridian or selected-factor statement for that larger class.

geometric component of its peripheral A-curve, using a finite augmented
cover if needed. Write m,l for the marked peripheral functions in the
conventions of the existing boundary calculation. Conversion between
these functions and another eigenvalue/power convention must be retained.

On the logarithmic continuation cover put U=Log(m), V=Log(l).
The marked Bloch lift beta of the fundamental class gives a period R
with

    dR=-(U dV-V dU)/4,
    Im R=D(beta)+(Re U Im V-Re V Im U)/4.             (1.1)

These are the explicitly proved identities in
[the boundary regulator note](boundary_regulator_period.md).
Define P=exp(2R/(pi*i)) and give its line the norm

    log||P||=log|P|-(Re U Im V-Re V Im U)/(2pi).

Then

    log||P||=2D(beta)/pi,
    log||P_hyp||=2 Vol(S^3 minus K)/pi.               (1.2)

The line has deck multiplier exp(bU-aV) under
(U,V)->(U+2pi*i*a,V+2pi*i*b). Its connection is
-(U dV-V dU)/(2pi*i). The construction retains the fundamental-class
lift and logarithmic branches. The A-equation supplies the peripheral
curve; the normalization of this period has not been extracted from
the coefficients of A alone.

The subsequent [formal A-curve period construction](formal_a_curve_period_torsor.md)
now lifts a simple branch of a specified operator's classical symbol
and computes its formal connection. Its leading action is this
regulator action after an explicit peripheral monomial conversion.
At an ordinary double point it retains the colliding pair in a
regular rank-two formal connection lattice. Its hypotheses and the
remaining actual-period comparison are stated there; no quantum-knot
classical-symbol theorem is inferred.

### An algebraic connection behind this period torsor

The logarithmic coefficients can be absorbed into a rank-three
unipotent connection. Set

    H=-2R+UV/2,
    Pi = [ 1  U  H ]
         [ 0  1  V ]
         [ 0  0  1 ].

Equation (1.1) gives dH=U dV. Hence exactly

    Pi^(-1)dPi = [ 0  dm/m   0   ]
                 [ 0    0   dl/l ]
                 [ 0    0    0   ].                 (1.3)

The right side is an algebraic matrix of differentials on C. It
defines a flat connection: d(dm/m)=d(dl/l)=0 and their wedge is
zero on a smooth curve. Equivalently, Pi is a horizontal period
frame for the right equation dPi=Pi*Omega. All normalized unipotent
solutions differ by a constant left Heisenberg matrix. This gives
an explicit Heisenberg period torsor over the A-curve, with the
central coordinate selected by beta. It is standard unipotent
connection algebra, proved here by the displayed multiplication.

The algebraic connection is not a claim that its complex period
values are algebraic numbers. The period frame and its integral
monodromy/branch data are part of the object. In particular replacing
R by R+c is not prevented by the differential equation; the marked
fundamental class fixes the remaining normalization.

## 2. A quantum A-operator gives an explicit algebraic norm torsor

Start with an actual specified annihilating operator, rather than
quantizing a classical polynomial by a chosen ordering:

    Ahat=sum_(j=0)^r a_j(q,x)T^j, Tx=q xT.

At a primitive n-th root q=zeta, assume this specialization and its
leading/trailing coefficients are defined and nonzero in C(x).
Write its companion module as

    T=B(x)sigma, sigma(x)=zeta*x,
    H=T^n=B sigma(B)...sigma^(n-1)(B).

Then sigma(H)=B^(-1)HB, so

    D_n(u,v)=det(v-H) belongs to C(u)[v], u=x^n.      (2.1)

Take a component where v is simple, with function field k. Set

    S=k[x]/(x^n-u), L=ker(H-v).

S is a cyclic finite etale algebra, possibly a product. L has rank
one over S. In a frame e, write T e=a e. The exact equation is

    Norm_(S/k)(a)=v.                                (2.2)

Thus the intrinsic algebraic object is

    T_(n,v)={a in Res_(S/k)G_m : Norm(a)=v},

a torsor under the norm-one torus ker(Norm), of dimension n-1.
Its isomorphism/comparison torsor is a different object: once two
realizations a,b have the same norm, its nonzero intertwining maps
form a G_m-torsor. Keeping these two groups distinct avoids calling
every normalization ambiguity a scalar without proof.

There is no claim that the norm-fiber set is additive. The actual
comparison line is the kernel of the k-linear equation
a*sigma(f)-b*f=0 in S. Hilbert 90 supplies a unit solution; every
solution is then a k-multiple of it. Its nonzero frames form the
scalar torsor. Thus linearity is supplied by a genuine Hom space,
not inferred from nonlinear products of proposed sections.

## 3. Hilbert 90 supplies the comparison map, including its lattice

For r=a/b of norm one, put

    r_0=1, r_j=r sigma(r)...sigma^(j-1)(r),
    f(c)=sum_(j=0)^(n-1) r_j sigma^j(c).

Direct translation of the sum gives r*sigma(f)=f. Some c makes f a
unit: over a separable closure its coordinates are nonzero linear
forms, and an infinite field has a point outside their finitely many
zero hyperplanes. Hence

    a/b=f/sigma(f).                                 (3.1)

Two such f differ by k^*. This is the proved comparison torsor.
It works for composite as well as prime levels and for split etale
algebras. The full proof is in
[the intrinsic norm-torsor note](intrinsic_quantum_a_norm_torsor.md).

On the normalized spectral curve, let Lambda_0 be a fractional line
lattice and assume u,v are units. The finite construction

    Lambda_cyc=sum_(j=0)^(n-1) T^j Lambda_0           (3.2)

is an invertible line lattice, stable under T. Coherence and rank one
give invertibility on the smooth curve, while T^n=v gives stability.
It is the smallest stable lattice containing Lambda_0. Thus an
apparent pole is handled by an actual algebraic lattice, not an
undefined evaluation of separate numerator and denominator.

At repeated spectral roots one must retain the generalized spectral
algebra if the whole module is needed. The same Hilbert-90 construction
extends to characteristic-zero Artin bases by finite nilpotent log/exp,
as proved in the intrinsic note's linked extension. This qualification
does not identify a spectral curve with the classical A-curve.

### The source gives an additional affine period torsor

The subsequent [affine extension note](affine_quantum_a_period.md)
retains a specified inhomogeneous equation A y=b. Its companion

under the stated affine gauges. A finite sigma-stable algebraic
cover cannot kill a nonzero class, by normalized trace. At a root,
cyclic affine descent constructs the rational period; when the
geometric block is nonresonant it is the explicit resolvent
(I-H)^(-1)s. A unique extremal M-coefficient of A proves this
nonresonance uniformly, without a growing determinant computation.

The [adequacy argument](adequacy_and_algebraic_periods.md) now
proves that all nontrivial adequate knots have quadratic color
degree span, excluding any fixed finite algebraic meridian trace.
For a connected reduced alternating diagram the exact q-span is
c*j*(j-1). Under the stated actual-source and nonresonance
hypotheses, this forces the affine extension to be nonsplit.
Alternatively a formal trace criterion proves the same nonsplitting
without any root-rank computation: expand at q=exp(h), M=-exp(Xh),
and check that the leading shift operator has no rational X-solution.
Polynomiality of the actual perturbative color coefficients supplies
the proof, without an MMR or Alexander theorem.
The source factorization and its regulator identification are not
assumed for every knot operator. This does not prove volume growth.

The subsequent [regular-period construction](regularized_kashaev_period.md)
provides a different rootwise source without a rational left-factor
hypothesis. All ordinary-color poles of the full ADO state have
prescribed principal parts; an independently reconstructed positive
trace bounds them uniformly by a polynomial in N. Subtract their
unique proper polar function to obtain a Laurent regular period R_N.
For any specified actual operator, a fixed rational gauge and clearing
factor give A_hat R_N=b_N. The source b_N belongs to a fixed finite-
dimensional Laurent space and its coefficients are polynomially bounded.
It need not be a single generic rational b(q,Y), and its nonzero
geometric coupling is not proved. Under the separate rational rank-one
hypothesis, regularity at the anchor and the complete point makes R_N(1)
the unique complete value of that affine equation.

The [peripheral normal-period analysis](peripheral_normal_period.md)
now proves a necessary boundary condition for positive volume growth.
If any coefficient a_i(q,Y) of a cleared actual polynomial operator
has a_i(q,q^(-2i)) nonzero, the ordinary-color bound forces K_N to
have polynomial growth. A positive rate therefore requires the right
factor P=B*(Y-1). Its delta quotient R/R*(Y-1) is killed by meridian-
field localization, so it must be retained by the pointed lattice.
At U=Y^N=1 the complete value is the normal derivative of the actual
kernel vector (Y-1)F_N. Under the stated special-fiber rank condition,
the first lifting obstruction determines it exactly. A checked
quantum Weyl reciprocity permits a weaker condition: invertibility
on just the relevant symmetry sector. Neither boundary divisibility
nor these rank conditions are asserted for every hyperbolic operator.

The same checked reciprocity gives an explicit quadratic Hilbert-90
frame descent under U->U^(-1): for a reflected kernel ratio gamma
of norm one with gamma(1)=1, the frame g=(1+gamma)/2 is a unit at
the marked fiber and preserves the normal period. On the ambient
orbit the Weyl relation becomes dihedral after the exact ADO twist
kappa=-q^(-1); its N-th power remains (-1)^(N-1). This fixes an
equivariant frame, not the still-unknown exponential norm.

The subsequent [actual Weyl-descent proof](actual_weyl_descent.md)
now supplies the quantum symmetry uniformly for every knot. It proves
F_N(M)=F_N(M^(-1)) by the actual Chevalley turnover, retaining the
shifted color, pivotal and full dimension factors. For the primitive
minimal operator B of v_N=(Y-1)F_N, reflected minimality forces the
unitary monomial intertwiner c(q,Y)=sigma*q^(dm)*Y^m, sigma=+/-1.
This requires no root-rank or AJ hypothesis. An actual F-annihilator
P=B*(Y-1) is therefore available in this canonical normalization;
this does not assert the boundary divisibility of every primitive
reduced-state operator.

The normal source has the required -1 parity without an additional
state-symmetry hypothesis. It suffices that B's restriction to that
sector be injective, since the actual source is already in its image.
The source coefficients of the regular polar subtraction are exact
finite sine moments of the ordinary colors. Primitive generator
units act by unitary row factors and a fixed scalar on the orbit,
preserving the exact normal period and its positive exponential rate.
Sector injectivity, geometric-factor survival and the regulator norm
are still not proved for a broad hyperbolic class.

The [actual peripheral-fusion calculation](actual_peripheral_fusion.md)
now gives an additional uniform quantum comparison. Tensoring the
actual color with the two-dimensional fundamental color splits into
its adjacent colors with explicit Casimir projectors. Their left
pivotal traces are exactly d_N(q^(+/-1)M)/d_N(M), including their signs
and complete-meridian poles. Hence the full state has boundary actions
mu=M+M^(-1) and lambda=T+T^(-1). Any word relation in those boundary
curves annihilating the empty skein evaluates to an actual annihilator,
with coefficient roots retained. This supplies one direction of
peripheral-operator compatibility, not equality of annihilator ideals.

The splitting degenerates at M=1 with a nonzero square-zero Casimir
residue of rank N-1 and pivotal partial trace exactly identity. Its
coefficient in the actual cabled-knot map is the specified derivative
-(q-q^(-1))*(M partial_M F_N)(q). In the regular boundary lattice
w_N=(M-M^(-1))F_N the parallel acts as -(T+T^(-1)), and the complete
Kashaev value is (1/2)partial_(Log M)w_N at M=1. These are concrete
quantum extension and pointing data for comparison with a triangulation.
They do not prove geometric coupling or its volume norm.

The [ramified Weyl-fibre theorem](ramified_weyl_fibre.md) now turns
that normal period into an ordinary algebraic evaluation, with the

z=(U-1)/(U+1), s=z^2. Explicit algebraic units linearize both Weyl
involutions, and the invariant lattice is R_s E_+ plus z R_s E_-.
Its special fibre records (a_0,b_0), where a_0 consists of ordinary
colors and N_N(1)=(eta_N/2)e_0^*b_0. This exact evaluation needs no

is the lifting equation E(0)b_0=-D(0)a_0.

A comparison that is identity on the ordinary root fibre can still
change b_0 by a nonzero equivariant shear. Thus the quantum-to-regulator
comparison must preserve this marked ramified fibre and its functional.
Under generic rank one the saturated kernel supplies its pointed line
even at a rank jump; generic rank one itself remains additional.
Explicit parity traces also detect signatures for which the simpler
negative-sector injectivity is impossible. No geometric contribution
or volume growth follows from descent alone.

The [torsion initial-rank theorem](torsion_initial_rank.md) now supplies
a finite sufficient condition for the generic root line. For a
rational-coefficient actual operator, its extremal polynomial
P(q,Z) has, outside an explicit finite range, root-grid zeroes only
on finitely many contained binomial torsion curves. A cyclotomic
degree proof gives the bound, and exact congruences give periodic
counts retaining q_N versus q_N^2. A single occupied curve gives
generic rank one at every sufficiently large level, allowing an
arbitrary non-toric remainder of P. A primitive minimal actual
right factor inherits that rank, without assuming its coefficients
are rational over the constants.

Conversely an actual all-level rational state forces at least one
nonvertical torsion curve and a nonzero count in every eventual
residue class. These are uniform coherence tests, not a knot census.
Once a second regular Weyl period is explicitly placed in the same
marked operator kernel and has the ordinary anchor one, rank one
forces equality of the descended fibre and its complete period.
The geometric realization meeting those conditions is still missing.
The finite certificate has not been proved for all hyperbolic or
alternating knot operators. It addresses the generic line; geometric
factor survival and the selected regulator norm remain unproved.

The [actual threaded-character theorem](ado_threaded_shadow.md) now
determines additional interior data. For any framed loop gamma,
its 2N Chebyshev threading acts on the actual ADO evaluation by

At U=1 the even character is two on both coordinates of the

Every hyperbolic knot has a null-homologous commutator gamma with

possible: if it carries a compatible observable

eigenvector is ((2-G)^(-1)d_gamma,1) and is automatically horizontal.
This supplies a precise source-selection criterion. The actual
relative gluing extension and d_gamma have not been constructed,
and their regulator norm remains unproved. The degree-2N
observable does not give a fixed generic-q splitting operator.

## 4. The state and its peripheral pointing are already fixed

Use the literature ADO meridian family with its reduced scalar F_N(M),
not an arbitrary solution of Ahat. The
[independent pointing calculation](kashaev_ado_pointing.md) proves

    F_N(1)=<K>_N,
    N_N(M)=d_N(M)F_N(M),
    d_N(M)=eta_N M^(N-1)/(1+M^2+...+M^(2N-2)),
    d_N(1)=eta_N/N, |eta_N|=1.                       (4.1)

Moreover d_N(qM)/d_N(M) is the single rational gauge

    g(q,M)=-q^(-1)(q^2 M^2-1)/(M^2-1).              (4.2)

The parameter q=exp(pi*i/N) has order 2N. The subsequent
[squared-meridian descent](even_meridian_norm_descent.md) proves
F_N(-M)=F_N(M) and descends the actual minimal operator to Y=M^2,
where sigma(Y)=q^2Y has order N. The natural root norm extension
then has degree N. The full ADO period is a section with parity
(-1)^(N-1): its N-cyclic sector is W=(-1)^(N-1), while the
reduced sector is W=1. The former 2N-monodromy squares this W
and loses its sign. The full factor in (4.1), its germ at M=1,
and its explicit constant-character twist are all retained.

The subsequent [root-orbit pointing theorem](root_orbit_pointing.md)

central comparison factor exactly at that fiber. For the full state
the anchor is the nonzero residue
[(M^(2N)-1)N_N]_(M=-q)=eta_N*(-1)^N*(q-q^(-1)).
The same note gives a cofactor formula for the actual period from
a specified operator or ideal with a one-dimensional rational kernel.
It keeps the saturated generic kernel line even when the special
kernel becomes larger, and retains a first meridian jet for the
full-state normalization. The required rank condition is not assumed

The subsequent [all-level A-period theorem](all_level_a_period.md)
now removes that rank condition from exact period recovery. For any

The [finite cut-state proof](finite_sum_annihilator.md) now proves

## 5. The precise unresolved bridge

The two curves in Sections 1 and 2 must not be identified by notation.
The classical geometric A-curve is obtained from peripheral holonomy.
D_n is obtained from a specified root-of-unity q-difference module.
Equality or a suitable family-level comparison is additional work.
The new formal route does not require D_n=A_K. It works on a simple
component of the actual classical symbol at q=1; proving that the
relevant operator has the geometric component is still necessary.

The [triangulation bridge](triangulation_regulator_bridge.md)
identifies the audited positive ideal triangulation theorem's role:
it supplies the marked geometric Bloch lift for all hyperbolic knots.
An exact local root calculation places tetrahedron and edge
coinvariants over the classical central gluing support, with parity
and peripheral roots retained. Its geometric block may enter the
actual affine source extension, whose rational period has a V=1
sector. A literal equality of that whole sector with the geometric
central curve is not inferred. The quantum relative period and its
norm must still be compared with the marked regulator.

The subsequent [relative octahedral presentation](relative_octahedral_period.md)
extracts actual local data for that comparison. The crossing's five
quantum dilogarithm factors have exactly the ideal-octahedron Bloch
symbol. Its finite factorial obeys an explicit inhomogeneous cyclic
equation, with homogeneous multiplier 1-Y^N, source Y^N, and
marked affine multiplier 1. The cyclic system is invertible for
Y!=0, including where its unit completion has poles. The missing
unit transition at Y=M^2 is exactly 1/S_N(M^2), giving the full
ADO dimension after its known phase and monomial. These identities
hold at every parity. Their shift acts on an internal state index;
the global pushforward to the meridian source extension and the
selected quantum period's regulator norm are still necessary. The
classical geometric representative and action norm are identified below.
