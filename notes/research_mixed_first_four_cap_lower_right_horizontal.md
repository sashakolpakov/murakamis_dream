# Complete type-four cap transport on the lower right horizontal

Private written deduction, 10 November 2026. The entire original
type-four first cap is bounded by its original allocated half-face
plane on the horizontal segment from \(1/2+ic\) to \(7/10+ic\).
Here \(c<0\) is the actual first height of an initial four/four
word. The proof transports the complete retained contour and bounds
every omitted physical row, including both true ends.

This is a bound for the first cap. The adjacent complete conditional
factor and its action comparison remain separate.

## 1. Actual reference, source and half-face anchor

Fix an actual positive three/four word of length \(m\ge3\) beginning
with four and four. Retain its original first-cap reference
\[
 e_*=u_*+ih_e,\qquad a_*=a_0+ic,\qquad
 .246<a_0<.350,\quad -.078<c<-.020,\quad
 .333<u_*<.363,\quad -.051<h_e<-.019.
\tag{1}
\]
These are precisely the coupled references in Section 1 of
[the first four/four calibration](research_mixed_first_four_four_true_last_row_calibration.md).
Their private and outgoing coordinates are not independent
substitutions. In particular the actual second coordinate and all
later heights remain fixed.

Write \(r_N=e^{-2\pi i/N}\), \(P_j=(r_N;r_N)_j\), and retain
\[
 U_{1,N}(w,X)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}
       H_N(w,r_N^{-j})\,\frac{(r_NX;r_N)_j}{P_j^2}.
\tag{2}
\]
Every \(H_N\) is the full original endpoint polynomial.
Let \(T(a)\) be the original bare cap action and put
\[
 L_1(a)=T(a)-\frac12g(a),\qquad
 \kappa=\frac1{2500000},\qquad
 B_*=\Re L_1(a_*)+\Re L_1'(a_*)(1/2-a_0)
                               -\kappa(1/2-a_0)^2 .
\tag{3}
\]
This is the allocated cap plane of
[complete upper-target support](research_mixed_cap_upper_target_completion.md)
at the actual-height half face, not a newly calibrated constant.

At \(a_L=1/2+ic\), \(\Re g(a_L)=0\). The complete upper-target
proof therefore pays the bare cap with rate \(B_*\) there.
More precisely, its five retained profiles have the following bounds:
the original main profile is at most \(B_*\), and each real-cut
or horizontal-alias profile is below
\(B_*-1/200000\). The private rectangle is
\[
                  E_c=[.27,.49]+i[-.055,.04].
\tag{4}
\]
The main height is \(h_e\), the real cuts are \(.27,.49\), and
the lower/upper alias penalties are \(.11\pi,.08\pi\).
Private vertical convexity in
[the upper-target domain theorem](research_mixed_cap_upper_target_extension.md)
pays the two zero-mode joins and all sign halves of the vertical
aliases at \(a_L\) by these same endpoint profiles. Thus the whole
fixed retained contour, not just its main horizontal, is available
at the anchor.

We shall also need the source-plane floor
\[
                              B_*>\frac{149}{200}.
\tag{5}
\]
Here is its precise normalization. Section 3 of complete upper-target
support gives the bare tangent plane
\[
 B_T=\Re T(a_*)+\Re T'(a_*)(1/2-a_0)
                               -\kappa(1/2-a_0)^2
                              >149/200 .
\]
On the fixed-height interval from \(a_*\) to \(a_L\),
\(\Re g''=\pi\Re\cot\pi a\ge0\), and \(\Re g(a_L)=0\).
Consequently
\(\Re\{g(a_*)+g'(a_*)(1/2-a_0)\}\le0\), so \(B_*\ge B_T\).
This proves (5) using the original bare and allocated planes.

## 2. Every retained complex current decreases to the right

Use the canonical factorial \(\Pi_N\) and original complete
coefficient continuation \(\mathcal C_N(e,w)\). The exact retained
type-four interpolant is
\[
 \mathscr A_{1,N}(e,a,w)=
 D_N(e)^{-1}\mathcal C_N(e,w)
             \frac{\Pi_N(a+e)}{\Pi_N(a)\Pi_N(e)} .
\tag{6}
\]
At each physical private index this is exactly the summand of (2).
All denominator arguments remain in their original regular strips
for \(e\in E_c\), \(a=x+ic\), \(1/2\le x\le7/10\).
Only the numerator \(a+e\) can cross real part one.

The continuous, zero-safe numerator rate in Section 2 of
[the complete corner transport](research_mixed_next_selected_incoming_corner_union.md)
is
\[
 \mathfrak p(q)=
 \begin{cases}
   \Re g(q),&0<\Re q\le1,\\
   \Re g(q-1)+2\pi(\Im q)_+,&1\le\Re q\le6/5 .
 \end{cases}
\tag{7}
\]
It retains both terms of the exact multiplier
\(\Pi_N(q+1)=(1-e^{-2\pi iNq})\Pi_N(q)\).
The two expressions meet continuously at the wall. On the present
product \( .77\le\Re(a+e)\le1.19<6/5\).

The complete-color upper rate of (6) is
\[
 \widehat\Psi_1(e,a)
   =\Re\{S(e)+J(e)-2g(e)\}
                      +\mathfrak p(a+e)-\Re g(a).
\tag{8}
\]
It is the earlier own-three rate with precisely the additional
\(-\Re g(e)\). That additional term is independent of \(x\).
All constants, chirps and original lifts are retained. At the
anchor before the wall it is the original type-four bare phase.

Put \(X=e^{-2\pi ia}\), \(p=e^{2\pi ie}\),
\(R=|X|\), and \(q=|p|^{-1}\). The actual domains give
\[
 \frac12<R<1,\quad \frac7{10}<q<\frac43,\quad
 \Arg X\in[3\pi/5,\pi],\quad
 \Arg p\in[27\pi/50,49\pi/50].
\tag{9}
\]
The bound \(R>1/2\) is the existing type-four radius bound
already used by complete upper-target support. The same elementary
angle and private-radius witnesses used in corner transport give
\[
 \cos(\Arg X-\Arg p)>\frac18,\qquad
                    \cos\Arg X<-\frac4{13}.
\tag{10}
\]
The difference of squared moduli consequently obeys
\[
\begin{split}
 |1-X|^2-|1-X/p|^2
 &=R^2(1-q^2)-2R\cos\Arg X
                        +2Rq\cos(\Arg X-\Arg p)\\
 &>R\left(\frac8{13}+\frac7{40}-\frac79R\right)
 \ge\frac{59}{4680}R>\frac{59}{9360}.
\end{split}
\tag{11}
\]
Since \(|1-X|^2\le4\), this implies
\[
 \left|\frac{1-X/p}{1-X}\right|^2
                         <1-\frac{59}{37440}<1.
\tag{12}
\]
On each branch of (7),
\[
 \partial_x\widehat\Psi_1(e,x+ic)
                   =\log\left|\frac{1-X/p}{1-X}\right|<0.
\tag{13}
\]
The period term in (7) is constant in \(x\). Integrating across
its continuous wall and any integrable numerator zero therefore
shows that each fixed private-contour point decreases in rate as
\(x\) moves from \(1/2\) to \(7/10\).

This applies to every main point, both horizontal aliases, every
weighted vertical alias and both unweighted joins of the original
retained contour. Their sampling penalties are independent of \(x\).
No new convexity beyond the wall is required: the entire old contour
is transported pointwise from its paid anchor. The strict bound
(12) and continuous integrated upper rates absorb the original
\(O(N^{-1})\) half-grid displacements with an arbitrary prescribed
total tolerance. The full coefficient \(\mathcal C_N\) on (4),
including its all-real-color estimate, is unchanged.

## 3. All omitted compact physical rows remain below their anchor cuts

For a real private index \(e\in(0,1)\), let
\(\lambda(e)=\Re S(e)\), and define
\[
 I_{x,R}(e)=\int_0^e\log|1-Re^{-2\pi i(x+t)}|\,dt,\qquad
              \mathcal R_1(e,x)=\lambda(e)+I_{x,R}(e)-J(e).
\tag{14}
\]
This is the actual type-four compact row rate: the full coefficient
\(H_N\) has rate \(\lambda(e)+J(e)\), the numerator has rate
\(I_{x,R}\), and the two denominator factorials subtract \(2J(e)\).
The complete real-color source in
[complementary real-color bounds](research_mixed_three_four_complementary_real_colors.md),
Section 2, gives the same upper rate with arbitrarily small tolerance,
uniformly on every compact physical-index interval. No Newton
summands inside \(H_N\) are replaced by their moduli.

For \(0<e\le1/2\),
\[
 \partial_x I_{x,R}(e)
   =\log\frac{|1-Re^{-2\pi i(x+e)}|}
                       {|1-Re^{-2\pi ix}|}\le0
                              \qquad(1/2\le x\le7/10),
\tag{15}
\]
because the squared numerator minus squared denominator is
\(4R\sin\pi e\sin\pi(2x+e)\le0\). Here
\(1\le2x+e<2\). Thus each omitted compact lower row
\(e\le.27\) is at most its old value at \(x=1/2\).
The lower-tail monotonicity in the upper-target domain theorem
places that value below the paid real cut at \(e=.27\).

For every \(x\) on the new interval,
\[
       \partial_e\mathcal R_1
       =\lambda'(e)-J'(e)
                           +\log|1-Re^{-2\pi i(x+e)}|.
\tag{16}
\]
The exact endpoint inequalities in Section 1 of
[the complete cap envelope](research_mixed_boundary_window_real_color_envelopes.md)
give
\[
 \lambda'<-3/5,\quad J'>3/5\quad(.49\le e\le.5),
 \qquad
 \lambda'-J'\le-\log4\quad(e\ge.5).
\tag{17}
\]
Since \(R<1\), the last logarithm in (16) is at most \(\log2\).
It follows that (16) is strictly negative on all of \([.49,1)\):
on the bridge use \(\log2<7/10<6/5\), and above one half
use \(-\log4+\log2=-\log2<0\).
Hence every omitted compact upper row is bounded by
\[
 \mathcal R_1(e,x)\le\mathcal R_1(.49,x)
             \le\mathcal R_1(.49,1/2)
                              <B_*-1/200000.
\tag{18}
\]
The second inequality is (15). This retains all rows past the
numerator wall and the physical half index, including those revived
away from an outgoing sampling node. It does not assume an
endpoint maximum for a signed sum.

## 4. The two true private ends on the whole closed disk

For clarity, the endpoint bound is checked for the type-four
denominator, rather than inferred from a compact row asymptotic.
Write \(S_j=|P_j|\) and \(E_l(w)=|e_l(w)|\). The exact finite
coefficient inequalities of complementary real-color bounds give
\[
 \frac{|H_N(w,r_N^{-j})|}{S_j}
          \le\sum_{l=0}^j\frac{E_l(w)}{S_lS_{j-l}},
 \qquad E_l(w)\le\frac{4^l}{S_l^2},
                         \qquad -2\le w\le2.
\tag{19}
\]
For \(|X|\le1\), \(|(r_NX;r_N)_j|\le2^j\). Thus the type-four
row in (2) is at most
\[
        2^j\sum_{l=0}^j\frac{4^l}{S_jS_l^3S_{j-l}}.
\tag{20}
\]
The uniform sine-product bounds, including index zero, give on a
sufficiently small fixed \(s=j/N\le\delta_0\) the upper entropy
\[
                          3s\log2-5J(s).
\tag{21}
\]
Indeed \(J\) decreases near zero, so replacing each of the smaller
arguments \(l/N,(j-l)/N\) by \(s\) only enlarges this bound.
The expression (21) tends to zero as \(s\downarrow0\). Choose
\(\delta_0>0\), fixed before \(N\), so that it is less than
\(1/4\) on this whole interval. No mathematical value is evaluated
to make this existence choice.

At the upper end put \(j=N-1-m\). The same exact finite source
identities give
\[
 \frac{|(r_NX;r_N)_j|}{S_j}\le2^m,\qquad
 |H_N(w,r_N^{-j})|\le
             \frac{2^m}{S_m}\sum_{l=0}^jE_l(w),\qquad
                         S_jS_m=N .
\tag{22}
\]
Therefore the type-four row is at most
\[
                 \frac{4^m}{N}\sum_{l=0}^jE_l(w).
\tag{23}
\]
The original all-real Newton estimate has rate \(b_2<73/125<3/5\).
Reduce \(\delta_0\) if necessary so that
\(b_2+\delta_0\log4<3/5\). Equations (22)--(23) then
bound the entire upper-end group by a polynomial times \(e^{3N/5}\).
The harmless difference between \(m/N\) and \(1-j/N\) is \(1/N\).

Thus both true ends of (2) have rates strictly below \(B_*\) by (5).
These are exact coefficient and product bounds uniform for all
\(|X|\le1\), both parities and all real ordinary colors. No
complete-color coefficient is divided out, and every odd-level
zero remains in its original finite support.

## 5. Complete finite conclusion and the retained first weight

**Theorem.** For every \(\tau>0\), uniformly on the actual references
(1), both parities, \(1/2\le x\le7/10\), and \(w\in[-2,2]\),
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 |U_{1,N}(w,e^{-2\pi i(x+ic)})|
             \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}e^{N(B_*+\tau)}.
 \quad}
\tag{24}
\]
The same conclusion holds on a sufficiently small fixed ordinary-color
disk about \(-2\), with every fixed ordinary derivative on a smaller
disk and at most polynomial cost.

*Proof.* Apply the original marked finite cotangent identity to (6)
on the same half-grid cuts as at \(a_L\). It has the original
\(+N\) constant mode, both horizontal aliases, all four weighted
vertical pieces and the two zero-mode joins. The numerator
continuation (7) retains its complete period multiplier; the
additional type-four denominator \(\Pi_N(e)\) is regular on
(4). Section 2 bounds every retained current by its anchor rate.
Sections 3--4 bound every physical row outside the cuts, including
both true ends. All source constants are uniform on the fixed
compact parameter domains. Allocate one total tolerance below
\(\tau\); finite sums, contour lengths and residue factors cost
only a fixed power of \(N\). This proves (24).

The literal cap is a polynomial of degree less than \(N\) in \(w\).
The all-real bound, followed by the elementary
[Chebyshev continuation](research_mixed_horizontal_complex_extension.md)
in the ordinary variable with any small exponential allowance,
therefore gives a fixed disk about \(-2\). Cauchy's formula on
smaller disks gives its fixed derivatives; Markov's inequality
gives the corresponding all-real fixed derivatives. These steps
do not change the outgoing target or its height. \(\square\)

For the actual unselected first Fourier weight the same rate holds:
\[
 |U_{1,N}(w,X)d_N(X)|
              \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}e^{N(B_*+\tau)},
            \qquad X=e^{-2\pi i(x+ic)},\quad 1/2\le x\le7/10.
\tag{25}
\]
Indeed \(\Re g(1/2+ic)=0\), and
\[
 \partial_x\Re g(x+ic)
       =\log|1-Re^{-2\pi ix}|>0
\]
on \(1/2\le x\le7/10\), since \(\cos(2\pi x)<0\).
Thus \(\Re g(x+ic)\ge0\). The exact absolute theorem for the
[forced Fourier polynomial](research_mixed_fourier_weight_continuation.md)
has rate \(\max\{-\Re g,2\pi c_+\}=0\) here. This proves
(25) without dividing by \(d_N\) or dropping its forcing.

To use (25) in the original first-pair density one must still estimate
the complete \(C_{1,N}(X,Y)\), retain \(|d_N(Y)|^{1/2}\), and
compare their combined rate with the actual \(\Lambda(z)\).
In particular no such comparison is inferred from a conditional
critical point alone. This note does not pay the vertical segment
at \(x=7/10\), the remaining real arc, the selected-height first
path, or the whole first-coordinate integral.
