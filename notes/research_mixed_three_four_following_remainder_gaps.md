# Two complete prefix remainder gaps on the following reduced disks

Research deduction, 7 November 2026. The completed saved-only boundary
certificate proves uniform positive action margins for the complete real
initial interval and the complete fixed complementary contour. The result
holds on both full reduced following disks and hence all four clipped
following domains. It does not complete the remaining first path or the
next Fourier observation.

## 1. The actual sheet and the two fixed finite sources

Keep the original cap-initialized \(34\) half-chain on
\[
 P_3=\{|c-(7/20-i/100)|\le83/1000\},\qquad
 P_4=\{|c-(3/10-13i/250)|\le3/50\}.
 \tag{1}
\]
The [actual critical-curve theorem](research_mixed_three_four_following_critical_curve.md)
provides its holomorphic last leg \(q(c)\) and absolute action
\[
 \mathscr P(c)=H_{34}(c)-G(q(c),c),\qquad
 G(z,c)=g(z+c)-2g(z)-2g(c)+2\pi izc.
 \tag{2}
\]
Here \(H_{34}\) is the stationary value of the original five-variable
cap-initialized action, with its original endpoint primitive and chirp.
Thus (2) removes the final Gram term explicitly; it is not a post-Gram
value or a reset bulk normalization. The root sheet is holomorphic near
both full closed disks, and \(q(c)\in O_4\) there. In particular
\(1/2<|e^{-2\pi iq(c)}|<1\).

For \(r=e^{-2\pi i/N}\), \(P_j=(r;r)_j\), \(p_j(X)=(rX;r)_j\),
retain the complete polynomials
\[
 \begin{split}
 U_{0,N}(w,X)&=\sum_{j<N}H_N(w,r^{-j})p_j(X)/P_j,\\
 C_{1,N}(X,Y)&=\sum_{j<N}X^{-j}p_j(X)p_j(Y)/P_j^3,\\
 f_{0,N}(w,Y)&=N\int_0^{1/10}
                  U_{0,N}(w,e^{-2\pi ia})C_{1,N}(e^{-2\pi ia},Y)\,da,\\
 f_{+,N}(w,Y)&=N\int_{\Gamma_+}
                  U_{0,N}(w,e^{-2\pi ia})C_{1,N}(e^{-2\pi ia},Y)\,da.
 \end{split}                                                     \tag{3}
\]
All coherent coefficients, all \(j<N\), numerator zeros and both level
parities are retained. There is no extra \(\kappa_N\), final Gram,
\(d_N\), or Fourier-selection factor in (3).

The complementary path is the **unchanged fixed path** of the original
ordinary pair:
\[
 \Gamma_+:[1/2+ih_{\rm pair},1/2]\cup[1/2,1],
                 \qquad -7/200<h_{\rm pair}<0.
 \tag{4}
\]
The height in (4) is the saved original pair height. It does not vary
with \(c\), the new critical height, or \(q(c)\).

The [whole real-initial theorem](research_mixed_transformed_real_initial_interval.md)
bounds \(f_0\) by a polynomial times \(e^{7N/4}\). The
[whole radial-complement theorem](research_mixed_transformed_following_four_radial_majorant.md)
bounds \(f_+\), for \(|Y|\le R\), by a polynomial times
\(e^{N(\mathfrak C(R)+\tau)}\), for every fixed \(\tau>0\), where
\[
 \mathfrak C(R)=\frac{17539}{9600}-\frac{23}{64000}
                -\frac6{25}\beta(R)+\frac{\beta(R)^2}{20},
 \qquad \beta(R)=\frac12\log\frac2{1+R^2}.
 \tag{5}
\]
These estimates are uniform on compact radius intervals inside \((0,1]\),
for all real ordinary colors, fixed ordinary/outgoing derivatives, and
on a sufficiently small fixed complex disk about the complete left
color. They apply to the literal sources (3), including their endpoints.

## 3. Uniform gaps and normalized derivative bounds

**Theorem.** On both full disks (1),
\[
 \Re\mathscr P(c)-7/4\ge\mu,\qquad
 \Re\mathscr P(c)-\mathfrak C(e^{2\pi\Im q(c)})\ge\mu.
 \tag{8}
\]
Consequently there is a fixed \(\delta>0\), which may be chosen as
\(\mu/4\), such that, for each pair of fixed nonnegative integers
\(k,\ell\),
\[
 \sup_{\substack{c\in P_3\cup P_4\\w\in[-2,2]}}
 \left|\partial_w^k\partial_c^\ell
   \left[e^{-N\mathscr P(c)}
        f_{j,N}(w,e^{-2\pi iq(c)})\right]\right|
       \le C_{k,\ell}N^{B_{k,\ell}}e^{-\delta N},
                 \qquad j=0,+ .
 \tag{9}
\]
The same conclusion holds for \(w\) in a sufficiently small fixed
complex disk about \(-2\), on a smaller disk for derivatives.
In particular (8)--(9) hold on all four original clipped domains
\(A_3,O_3,A_4,O_4\), without promoting their independent containing
boxes to realized reference families.

*Proof.* The saved bounds and (7) give (8) on the two complete circles.
The deficits
\[
 7/4-\Re\mathscr P(c),\qquad
 \mathfrak C(e^{2\pi\Im q(c)})-\Re\mathscr P(c)
\]
are harmonic and subharmonic, respectively, on neighborhoods of their
full closed disks, by the actual critical-curve theorem. The maximum
principles give (8) throughout both disks. Each clipped following domain
is contained in its corresponding disk, so no clipping chord remains
to be checked.

Compactness and continuity extend both margins to at least \(\mu/2\)
on a fixed neighborhood of \(P_3\cup P_4\) inside the original sheet.
The outgoing radii there lie in one compact subset of \((1/2,1)\).
Apply the two complete-source estimates with \(\tau<\mu/8\).
Their constants and small complete-color disks can be chosen uniformly.
This bounds each normalized source by a polynomial times
\(e^{-3\mu N/8}\) on that neighborhood. These normalized functions are
holomorphic in \(c\); fixed Cauchy estimates on smaller neighborhoods
give the \(c\) derivatives. The existing whole real-color derivative
bounds, or Markov before the source estimate, give the ordinary
\(w\) derivatives; on the small complex disk use Cauchy instead.
Weakening the exponent to \(\mu/4\) proves (9). No finite source is
divided by its possibly zero value. \(\square\)

The normalization in (9) uses the complete-left-color geometric action
(2), also when estimating other real colors. It does not assign a new
color-dependent critical action to those sources.
