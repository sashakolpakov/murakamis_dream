# Real ordinary colors on the following-family left taper

Research corollary, 7 November 2026. The complete following-family
left-taper bound holds for every real ordinary color, normalized by
the complete-color pre-Gram action. The extension uses upper bounds
for the whole original endpoint coefficients and their physical
ends. It does not compare a signed cap at two colors.

The final section records the exact contour partition and its
conditional angular consequence. The connector from the moving
reference height to the old fixed complementary contour remains a
separate required source estimate.

## 1. The complete cap input at all real colors

Use the base references, taper and literal source of
[complete following-family left transport](research_mixed_three_four_following_left_taper_source.md).
Thus \(c_0\in K=O_3,O_4,A_3,A_4\), \(a_*=A+ih_0\),
\(z_*=q(c_0)\), and
\[
 a_0(x)=x+ih_0(x-.1)/.12,\qquad .1\le x\le.22 .
 \tag{1}
\]
The path is fixed at this base reference. Put
\[
 w=-2\cos\theta,\qquad D(\theta)=1-\cos\theta,\qquad
                         -\pi\le\theta\le\pi .
 \tag{2}
\]

Theorem 1 of
[endpoint real-color decay](research_mixed_endpoint_real_color_decay.md)
bounds the whole original endpoint coefficient on its private
rectangle \(E_c=[.27,.49]+i[-.055,.04]\):
\[
 |C_N(e,-2\cos\theta)|
 \le CN^B e^{N\{\Re S(e)-c_ED(\theta)\}},\qquad c_E>0.
 \tag{3}
\]
The theorem includes a fixed neighborhood of \(E_c\), both
parities and every fixed ordinary-color derivative. Its exact
identity \(H_N(w,e^{2\pi ie})=G_N(Ne)C_N(e,w)\) preserves
the full coherent \(H_N\); no physical samples replace that
polynomial off the sampling grid. On every compact physical
private-index interval \(I\Subset(0,1)\), the same theorem gives
the original coherent real rate with a positive angular loss.

Insert these estimates into the unchanged first-cap interpolation.
Theorem 3 of
[the early-taper cap envelope](research_mixed_cap_early_taper_complex_profiles.md)
then gives, on the whole path (1),
\[
 |U_{0,N}(-2\cos\theta,e^{-2\pi ia_0(x)})|
 \le C_\tau N^{B_\tau}
 \left[
 e^{N\{A_0(a_0(x);k_*)-c_UD(\theta)+\tau\}}
 +e^{N\{E_{\rm end}(\Im a_0(x))+\tau\}}
 \right].
 \tag{4}
\]
Here \(k_*\in[-.019,.037]\) is the actual first-cap private
height, \(A_0\) is precisely its five-profile envelope, and
\[
 E_{\rm end}(h)=3/5+2\pi h_++(\log4)/100 .
 \tag{5}
\]
The interval for \(k_*\) is a compact subinterval of the original
private rectangle. The constant \(c_U>0\) can consequently be
chosen uniformly over the actual following families.

For clarity, (4) includes three different finite-source mechanisms:

* Every central private density, join and alias contains the
  whole coefficient in (3). The remaining factors and sampling
  penalties are color independent, so they keep the same five
  complete-color upper profiles.
* On the compact physical tails \([.01,.99]\), the physical
  coefficient theorem supplies their original coherent rate.
  The early-taper tail transport to \(.27,.49\) therefore
  remains unchanged, with its actual positive-height taper.
* The two extreme private layers use their literal finite
  Newton and product estimates. Below the Newton half point
  \(V_\theta\le V_0\); above it only \(V_\theta\le0\) is
  used. Section 4 of
  [the early real-row theorem](research_mixed_cap_early_taper_real_rows.md)
  consequently retains (5), through every numerator zero and
  at both parities. No angular loss is asserted for these layers.

This is not the false inequality
\(|U_{0,N}(w,X)|\le|U_{0,N}(-2,X)|\). Both terms of (4)
are absolute source majorants obtained before any cancellation
in the cap is used.

## 2. Every calibrated left-taper sector remains paid

Let \(\mathcal P(w,z)\) be the original local pre-Gram action
of the following plateau theorem, and put
\[
 \mathcal P_0(z)=\mathcal P(-2,z),\qquad
 \mathcal P_0(z_*)=\mathscr P_{34}(c_0),\qquad
 \gamma_L=1/1000000000 .
 \tag{6}
\]
The conditional polynomial
\[
 C_{1,N}(X,Y)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}
                  X^{-j}p_j(X)p_j(Y)/P_j^3
 \tag{7}
\]
is independent of \(w\). The complete-left proof estimates it
against each of the two cap alternatives in (4), not merely
against a signed complete-color cap value.

In detail, its moving real cut \(s(x)=39/400+x/8\) and
all lower physical rows use the same profilewise derivative and
ordinary \(1/8\)-face calibration. The added main segment is
raised at the same height to \(.15\); the rest of the main,
both tall aliases, every zero-mode join and all vertical aliases
use the same original phase inequalities. None of these
inequalities differentiates or changes the color. The true cap
ends in (5) are explicitly transported and calibrated in that
proof, so they are not absorbed by pretending that (3) applies
at a private-index endpoint.

For the conditional high rows, the endpoint theorem is entirely
color independent. Its half-row branch uses the same profile
envelope. Its true-last branch uses the complete coherent
all-real cap estimate
\[
 |U_{0,N}(w,X)|\le C_\tau N^{B_\tau}
   e^{N\{433/500+1/3+2\pi(\Im a)_++\tau\}},
 \quad X=e^{-2\pi ia},\quad w\in[-2,2].
 \tag{8}
\]
This is the disk-plus-polynomial-growth estimate retained in
[the following-domain row theorem](research_mixed_four_upper_rows_following_domains.md),
Section 5. After adding the exact last-row chirp, its ceiling
is below \(5/3\), independently of the color. The already
proved original-action floor
\(\Re\mathscr P_{34}(c_0)>7/4\) pays it, exactly as in
the complete-left proof.

Dropping the favorable angular loss in (4), every upper profile
in that proof is therefore still valid for all real \(w\).
Its raw minimum gap remains
\[
 \delta_0=2\kappa_*(29/500)^2
       =1682/625000000000>1/400000000,\qquad
                       \kappa_*=1/2500000 .
 \tag{9}
\]
One common choice of finite tolerances and neighborhoods retains
the same conservative finite gap \(\gamma_L\).

**Theorem 1.** For each fixed base \(c_0\in K\), on a sufficiently
small fixed neighborhood of \(z_*\), uniformly in \(x\in[.1,.22]\)
and every real \(w=-2\cos\theta\),
\[
 \left|
 e^{-N\mathcal P_0(z)}
 U_{0,N}(w,e^{-2\pi ia_0(x)})
 C_{1,N}(e^{-2\pi ia_0(x)},e^{-2\pi iz})
 \right|
 \le CN^B e^{-\gamma_LN}.
 \tag{10}
\]
The constants and neighborhood radii can be chosen uniformly
over each actual \(K\). Every pair of fixed nonnegative integers
\(k,\ell\) satisfies, on smaller neighborhoods,
\[
 \left|
 \partial_w^k(N^{-1}\partial_z)^\ell
 \left[
 e^{-N\mathcal P_0(z)}
 U_{0,N}(w,e^{-2\pi ia_0(x)})
 C_{1,N}(e^{-2\pi ia_0(x)},e^{-2\pi iz})
 \right]\right|
 \le C_{k,\ell}N^{B_{k,\ell}}
 e^{-N\{\gamma_L/2+(\gamma_L/4)D(\theta)\}} .
 \tag{11}
\]
The same conclusions hold for the exact taper integral, including
its original factor \(N\) and Jacobian \(a_0'(x)\).

*Proof of the derivative and neighborhood statements.* All
coefficient bounds used in (3)--(4) are uniform in the real
color, and their compact physical-tail bounds preserve the
same rates at the actual taper heights. The strict complete
source inequalities and zero-safe product estimates therefore
extend uniformly to a fixed small neighborhood in \(z\).
The outgoing coordinate alone varies; the incoming contours,
heights and half-grid prescription stay fixed at \(c_0\).
The continuous action \(\mathcal P_0(z)\) uses the same
absolute constant. Choosing the common tolerances below the
unused reserve in (9) proves (10).

For each \(x,z\), the exact source in (10) is a polynomial
of degree at most \(N-1\) in \(w\). Its normalizing factor
is independent of \(w\). Markov's inequality on \([-2,2]\)
therefore gives every fixed \(w\) derivative with a polynomial
cost, before any contour integration or finite summation is
estimated. Cauchy's formula on smaller fixed outgoing
neighborhoods gives the \(z\) derivatives, again at polynomial
cost. These estimates do not differentiate a modulus or divide
a finite source zero. Finally \(0\le D(\theta)\le2\) gives
\[
             \gamma_L/2+(\gamma_L/4)D(\theta)\le\gamma_L,
 \tag{12}
\]
which proves (11). Bounded path length, the fixed Jacobian and
the factor \(N\) preserve the conclusion for the integral.
Both level parities and all physical rows remain in (7).
\(\square\)

The normalization in (10)--(11) is intentionally the complete-color
action \(\mathcal P_0\), for every real \(w\). It does not assign
a distant color-dependent critical point to this source.

## 3. Exact fixed-base contour partition

The literal transformed prefix is
\[
 f_{34,N}(w,Y)=N\operatorname{CT}_X
                   [U_{0,N}(w,X)C_{1,N}(X,Y)].
 \tag{13}
\]
This has no final Gram factor, Fourier-selection factor, or
\(\kappa_N\) multiplier. Its exact normalization is the one
in the transformed-complement and complete-prefix theorems.

For fixed \(c_0\), let \(h_0=\Im a(c_0)\), and let
\(h_{\rm pair}\) be the original, fixed ordinary-pair height
used in the certified complementary source. The following
oriented chain has endpoints zero and one:
\[
 \begin{split}
 0&\longrightarrow .1
 \longrightarrow .22+ih_0
 \longrightarrow .45+ih_0
 \longrightarrow .5+ih_0\\
  &\longrightarrow .5+ih_{\rm pair}
 \longrightarrow .5
 \longrightarrow 1 .
 \end{split}                                                   \tag{14}
\]
The second segment is the affine taper (1); the next two are
horizontal. The fifth segment is the required connector.
The last two together are exactly the already certified fixed
complementary contour \(\Gamma_+\).
The connector is allowed to have either orientation, or zero
length. If geometric portions overlap, their orientations are
retained as an equality of contour chains.

For fixed \(N,w,Y\), the integrand in the incoming variable
\(a\) is an entire one-periodic function: it is a finite Laurent
polynomial in \(e^{-2\pi ia}\). Thus its integral over (14)
equals its integral from zero to one. Fourier orthogonality
gives exactly the constant coefficient in (13). Every segment
carries the same original factor \(N\), and the taper carries
its actual differential \(da=a_0'(x)\,dx\). No sampled-root
sum is substituted for an open segment.

All paths in (14) are fixed when \(w,z\), or a local following
parameter, varies about the base \(c_0\). An exact integral on
one base contour is not identified with an individual component
of a different base decomposition. Their complete sums both
equal the same polynomial (13).

## 4. Angular decay after a paid connector

Here is the precise assembly consequence; its connector premise
is not asserted in this note. Suppose the complete connector
in (14) has a uniform positive normalized source gap on the
actual following families, for every real ordinary color.
For a local packet conclusion also require its corresponding
fixed-base holomorphic gap near the complete color.

All other nonplateau segments already have all-real bounds:
the initial interval and fixed complementary contour use
[following remainder gaps](research_mixed_three_four_following_remainder_gaps.md);
the left taper uses Theorem 1 above; and the right segment uses
[the following right-segment theorem](research_mixed_three_four_following_right_segment.md).
Take a common \(\delta>0\) smaller than these finitely many
gaps and the assumed connector gap, after polynomial costs.
Their exact sum is bounded by
\[
                 CN^B e^{N\{\Re\mathscr P_{34}(c_0)-\delta\}}
                            \qquad(w\in[-2,2]).
 \tag{15}
\]

On the plateau, the whole-cap angular estimate in
[endpoint real-color decay, equation (14)](research_mixed_endpoint_real_color_decay.md)
and the complete ordinary conditional support, with its known
outgoing halfweight removed, give for some uniform \(c_P>0\)
\[
 |I_{P,N}^{c_0}(-2\cos\theta,z_*)|
 \le CN^B e^{N\{\Re\mathscr P_{34}(c_0)-c_PD(\theta)\}} .
 \tag{16}
\]
This is the all-real version of the following plateau source,
with the original stationary marginal cancellation. There is
no fixed positive rate tolerance hidden in (16). To retain
this exact exponent, keep the regular main-current bound,
whose factorial symbols have polynomial errors, at its
supporting plane. Every other ordinary contour or physical
piece has a strict deficit from that plane on the compact
actual family: the left and half rows use their \(1/2000\)
certificates, the last row has its compact strict deficit,
and the joins and aliases have their original gaps.
Choose a single tolerance only inside those deficits.
This proves the complete ordinary upper bound with a
polynomial prefactor at the plane itself. Multiplication
by the cap's exact angular plane preserves \(c_PD\);
one does not obtain (16) by formally setting an unspecified
tolerance to zero.

Adding (15)--(16), with the exact partition (14), gives
\[
 |f_{34,N}(-2\cos\theta,e^{-2\pi iz_*})|
 \le CN^B
 e^{N\{\Re\mathscr P_{34}(c_0)
                     -\min(c_PD(\theta),\delta)\}}.
 \tag{17}
\]
Since \(D\le2\),
\[
 \min(c_PD,\delta)\ge c_*D,\qquad
                       c_*=\min(c_P,\delta/2)>0 .
 \tag{18}
\]
Thus the complete prefix has a strict all-real angular
loss at its actual complete-color action, conditional on
the stated connector estimate. The fixed ordinary
derivatives have the same angular rate: use the existing
plateau derivative estimate and (12) on the gapped pieces,
or the angular Laurent-polynomial/Cauchy argument of
endpoint real-color decay on the resulting degree-\(<N\)
polynomial.

If the connector also has its stated local holomorphic gap,
the already proved nonzero plateau packet and the exact
decomposition (14) give the complete-prefix packet by
adding exponentially smaller remainders, with the original
\(N^{-3/2}\) power and absolute action. This is an assembly
implication, not an assertion that the connector comparison
has been completed. Neither (17) nor that implication
evaluates the two coefficients in the next Fourier transfer
or proves arbitrary short-filling closure.
