# Coverage check for the belt-stack filling route

29 October 2026. This is a check of stated scope in primary sources,
not an audit or use of their proofs. No novelty claim follows from
this limited search.

* Roland van der Veen, [Proof of the volume conjecture for Whitehead
  chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611181v2), states a volume-conjecture
  result for an infinite link family containing the Whitehead link and
  Borromean rings. The complete belt stacks may be covered by this
  family. An exact tangle/peripheral dictionary remains necessary
  before claiming either inclusion or a new topological class.
* Dionne Ibarra, Emma N. McQuire and Jessica S. Purcell,
  [Augmented links, shadow links, and the TV volume conjecture: a
  geometric perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09296v1), describe
  an isometry between octahedral fully augmented link exteriors and
  fundamental shadow-link exteriors. Their abstract states a
  Turaev--Viro volume result for the case without half-twists.
  An isometry of exteriors does not itself identify our marked
  original quantum source, its root convention or its filling kernel.
* Ce Shen, [The Chen--Yang volume conjecture for long integral fillings
  of fundamental shadow links](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11839v3),
  states a theorem for sufficiently long integral fillings of any
  fixed marked fundamental shadow-link exterior, allowing mixed signs
  and unrelated magnitudes. Its stated invariant is SO(3) Turaev--Viro
  on odd levels, with a further signed WRT expansion. This directly
  overlaps the geometric long-filling theme; it does not, by its
  statement alone, establish our original marked Kashaev source on
  the full sequence of levels.
