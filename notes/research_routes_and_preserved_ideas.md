# Research methods and their present roles

25 November 2026. The [current status](research_finite_exceptions_progress.md)
states the open finite-exception problem. The
[papers](../papers/README.md) contain the established restricted volume
theorems. The following methods have distinct roles.

## Exact finite sources and orthogonality

The actual source includes a fixed closing functional and normal
directions at repeated spectral roots. The
[ordinary-color Parseval calculation](ordinary_color_plancherel_bound_audit.md)
separates a remainder by exact orthogonality; it does not evaluate the
resulting whole norm geometrically.

The [two-dimensional Hermite source frame](actual_tame_c0_hermite_source_frame.md)
and [cap-tree energy](actual_cap_tree_angle_energy.md) give positive
forms that retain the marked observations. Their stated compatibility
conditions matter: a direction invisible at one cut can become visible
after another crossing.

The [normalized source energy](research_convex_normalized_conway_source.md),
[orthogonal linear elimination](research_conway_orthogonal_linear_elimination.md)
and [finite-fiber comparison](research_conway_reciprocal_finite_fiber_lower_bound.md)
give the general variational formulation. Its remaining minimum still
needs a geometric exponential evaluation.

## Algebraic periods, recurrences and torsors

The [torsor status](a_polynomial_torsor_status.md) separates finite
operator identities, algebraic branches, recurrence solutions and volume
limits. A classical curve alone does not select the original quantum
solution. The source, closing and normalization must survive every
change of presentation.

The [initialized coideal period](research_coideal_normalized_marked_period.md)
is a finite realization of this viewpoint. The
[cyclic Hardy comparison](research_cyclic_atom_hardy_head_comparison.md)
provides a function-theoretic approach to coefficient norms. Neither
calculation identifies an arbitrary closed source with a contributing
geometric period.

## Geometry and optimization

The [marked-filling paper](../papers/marked-fillings/README.md) constructs
the geometric data used by the volume arguments. The
[strict Conway geometry](../papers/strict-conway-geometry/README.md)
identifies their marked action with the absolute knot-complement volume.
The geometric maximum and its rigidity do not by themselves prove
survival of the original quantum contribution.

The [Rivin presentation comparison](research_rivin_jones_partial_filling_assessment.md)
retains the necessary boundary indices. The
[cover laws](regge_period_covers_and_a_curves.md) and
[filled-slope obstruction](research_filled_cover_slope_length_obstruction.md)
explain why a cover requires a separate filling and quantum descent
argument.

## Obstructions and other comparisons

The illustrated [methods chapter](../book/chapters/methods.md) explains
weighted Cauchy--Schwarz, Fourier analysis, positive coefficient bounds
and stationary phase. Its counterexamples distinguish upper bounds from
noncancellation. The
[analytic transport examples](actual_local_analytic_transport_action_barrier.md)
show why distinct instantaneous eigenvalues do not give a single-branch
asymptotic. The
[relative-homology criterion](research_dominant_saddle_transport.md)
states sufficient additional conditions for persistence of a saddle.

The [source and transport chapter](../book/chapters/mechanism.md) retains
the Racah, magnetic Fourier, reflection-double and positive-sewing
approaches with their local conclusions and unresolved global steps.
Earlier parameter pilots and superseded proof notes are in the
[pre-cleanup snapshot](https://github.com/sashakolpakov/murakamis_dream/tree/19d5b911bc409486f0e72b51a876a6a2bc70ee02/notes),
without duplicate archive directories in the active tree.
