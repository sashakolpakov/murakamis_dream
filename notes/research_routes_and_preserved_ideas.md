# Research routes and preserved ideas

The new exceptional-period route retains the exact generic Casimir polynomial,
its canonical scalar action and general local character relation, the actual
continuant resolvent period, strict-support Green estimates, and separate
endpoint controls. Finite-propagation windows and the universal marked
rectangle bound are retained with their limitations. The coherent Fourier
phase and global ordinary Legendre chart remain complementary tools.
These results do not replace the cube, positive sewing or other earlier
routes, and do not yet prove the general contributing source period.

## 2. Cube, shared cores and projected chains — preserved with their scope

The cube is a concrete primitive-core test, not the asserted general
answer. Its [actual rectified-core geometry](actual_nc_graph_rectified_core_geometry.md)
has volume 16v8+2Vol(Q_cube); the extra shared-core term cannot be
replaced by a product of local theta rates.

The durable source chain of results is:

* [Literal central cube reduction](actual_odd_central_cube_reflection_packet.md):
  exact original marked scalar N^12 S_N^8 phi(h^4), with all twelve
  channels fixed to the odd Steinberg channel and all phases retained.
* [Positive cuboctahedral period](actual_positive_newton_cuboctahedral_period.md):
  the real positive sum has the actual Q_cube volume rate, identified
  by its explicit geometric tetrahedra.
* [Four-rung packet](actual_cube_four_rung_positive_source.md): all four
  rung labels are summed, including paired normals. Eight cycle labels

  augmented-cube volume rate and a strict unfrozen-color gap.
* [Two-boundary transfer and projected chains](actual_three_port_cauchy_transport_and_cube_kernel.md):
  an explicit coupled weighted-Cauchy kernel has the sharp cube-minus-
  theta norm increment. Typed iterations retain every rung sum but
  still fix 2+6k cycle labels and use odd levels.

Full cube color summation, cancellation/dominance after removing those
cycle projections, even-level cube growth and arbitrary primitive-core

## 3. Positive Conway expressions and Karamata-style variational growth

The [actual positive Newton algebra](actual_positive_newton_multiplication.md)
retains every root normal. Its genuine turn, multiplication and closures
give a [whole-source positive Conway class](actual_positive_complete_circle_conway_trees.md),
without cube channel projections. The
[variational limit theorem](actual_positive_conway_variational_limit.md)

fixed robust expression. Exact integer support supplies the lower bound
at every level, including boundary maxima.

Its angle triples still need identification with the actual complement's
face/edge incidence. Some expressions have polynomial growth; positivity
does not imply hyperbolicity or volume equality. This route is preserved
alongside the cube's more explicitly calibrated but projected result.
