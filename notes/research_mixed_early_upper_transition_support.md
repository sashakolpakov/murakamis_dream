## 1. The early supporting plane beyond .45

Use the original phase \(\phi_{\eta\epsilon}\), Fourier branches
\(\psi_f\), reference plane \(\Gamma_r\), and oriented credits
\(q_0=11/2,\ q_1=151/20\) of the
[early-transition theorem](research_mixed_early_transition_support.md).
The reference is an actual cyclic critical pair, with either
orientation and every permitted selection. Choose the early
position \(j\in\{0,1\}\) and let \(k=1-j\). The target domain is
\[
 x_j\in[.1,.12],\qquad x_k\in[.1,.5],
 \qquad
 h_\ell\in
 \begin{cases}
 [-1/200,1/300],&\text{type three},\\
 [-19/2000,0],&\text{unselected type four},\\
 [0,7/500],&\text{selected type four}.
 \end{cases}
 \tag{1}
\]
These independent heights are used for a phase comparison, not for
an assertion that the full source packet exists on the whole
enlarged rectangle.

**Proposition 1.** On (1), every true Fourier branch obeys
\[
 \Re\{\psi_f(x+ih)-\Gamma_r(x+ih)\}
       +q_j(\Im r_k)^2
 < -\frac1{100}-\frac{(x_k-.45)_+}{500}.
 \tag{2}
\]
For the actual periodic hybrid with the early coordinate in
\([.1,.12]\) and the other in \(Q\), this is a complete-source
upper rate, with any fixed positive tolerance. Both forcing
branches, all original rows and both level parities are retained.

To prove the additional range, first fix a height corner of (1).
The old proof partitions the other real coordinate into sixteen
intervals. Its last interval is \([137/320,9/20]\), with tangent
point
\[
             x_j^0=\frac3{25},\qquad x_k^0=\frac{281}{640}.
 \tag{3}
\]
Let \(T_f(x)\) be the real affine tangent to
\(\Re\psi_f(x+ih)\) there. The old 64-product reference
certificate, with its complete 251-cell cover, already proves
\[
 T_f(x)-\Re\Gamma_r(x+ih)+q_j(\Im r_k)^2<-1/100
 \quad\text{for }x_j\in[.1,.12],\ x_k=.45.
 \tag{4}
\]
It checks the two early-coordinate endpoints; affinity gives the
whole segment.

The new focused check reevaluates the old saved point-root witnesses
at (3), with their original logarithmic self-maps. Across all types,
selections, branches, height corners and both orientations there
are 128 derivative tests and 50 distinct point roots. They give
\[
 \partial_{x_k}T_f<-\frac{101}{500},
 \qquad
 \max\partial_{x_k}T_f<-.2025975948.
 \tag{5}
\]
The rational inequality is the acceptance condition; the decimal
only reports the resulting maximum. The original
[geometric slope theorem](research_mixed_geometric_support_slopes.md)
gives \(\Re\phi_k(r)>-1/5\). Thus the slope of the left side
of (4) in \(x_k\) is less than \(-1/500\).

The cotangent estimate from the early proof remains valid as the
upper real endpoint increases from .45 to .5: on this interval
the minimum sine is still \(\sin(\pi/10)\), and
\(\Re\cot\pi(x+ih)\ge0\). The
[closed-cotangent root theorem](research_mixed_closed_cotangent_roots.md)
supplies the original holomorphic branch before taking a tangent.
The two positive triangle forms and nonnegative remaining factorial
coefficients in
[the selected-height curvature deduction](research_mixed_selected_height_reference_extension.md),
Corollary 2, give \(-\Re\psi_f''>0\) throughout this compact
product. Hence \(T_f\) bounds the real phase throughout the
enlarged real rectangle, including .5. Extending (4) by its
negative slope proves (2) at the height corners. Separate
height convexity of every branch then proves (2) for all (1).
For \(x_k\le.45\), the original theorem supplies (2) directly.

The actual early heights lie in the old caps, and every additional
neighbor suppression preserves them. If the other real coordinate
lies in \([.45,.5]\), its physical taper lies in the right
triangle because \(400(.5-x)^2\le20(.5-x)\); its extra

early-cap source. The actual Fourier upper bound supplies the

## 2. A partially attenuated Q coordinate beside R

**Proposition 2.** For both orientations and every permitted

                  B_N\le C N^A

 \tag{6}
\]
On \([.22,.45]\) this is the unrounded \(1/200\) raw ceiling

independent typed height bands. The intervals overlap and cover

## 3. The remaining allocation issue

These propositions do not replace \(P\) by Q in the completed
[low/early/plateau/right theorem](research_mixed_early_plateau_right_clusters.md).
The original one-height reset bound allows both real coordinates
up to .5, but it requires the other target height to remain at
its reference value. In a block joining an early-attenuated plateau
coordinate to an unaffected upper-taper coordinate, both heights
can differ from their references. Its full matrix reset ceiling is
valid, but is not the single oriented cost that the early edge
reserved.

For example the pattern E--P--U--R, \(U=(.45,.5)\), leaves
this issue on P--U. The coherent right-clamping theorem compares
both incident coherent phases at U. Its other incidence U--R is
a raw source bound in this mixed assembly, so that theorem does
not directly clamp this configuration. This is a missing comparison,
not a counterexample to the desired larger region. Selected height
corners require the same care: the selected taper theorem permits
arbitrary taper heights but keeps its plateau leg prescribed.
