## What changes in the comparison

replaces the trial enclosure of \((H-\operatorname{Im}a_*)\operatorname{Im}p_T\).
At an actual canonical reference, the terminal and ordinary formulas for

\[
p_T=g'(t_*+a_*)-\frac32g'(a_*)+2\pi i t_*
   =\frac32g'(a_*)-g'(s_*+a_*)
\tag{2}
\]

agree by the original shared-leg critical equation. The two formulas need not
agree at an approximate center. The new Taylor enclosure retains this
distinction, all posterior errors, and the common complex input parameter.

The [fixed protocol](research_mixed_terminal_terminal_side_plane_protocol.md)
changes only this trial contribution. It reuses the previous force vertices,
scalar-gain certificates, original target intervals, and source-cell reserve
associations. For each complete comparison it takes the better of two valid
routes: a whole-family plane ceiling plus the family reserve, or the maximum
of the sums over all original intersecting source cells. Each route separately
covers the full reference family. No target restriction or gain optimization is
introduced.
