# The actual negative-band boundary moment is a Ward current

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The boundary moment suggested by the
pure-prefix framing chart has an exact whole finite-source model. It
is the original cut paired with a particular K-Ward current, rather
than the original identity source. Literal signed crossing tensors
disprove a universal normalization identifying the two. Separately,
the original Ward factorial has an exact inverse-norm boundary atom
with a nonzero simple polar coefficient. These are explicit actual
operations, but their occurrence in the selected full geometric
contraction is not asserted.

The physical edge clock B and the Fourier output-band variable eta_F
remain INDEPENDENT. The geometric parameter eta_geo=p inverse is
used only in the stated principal Fourier incidence and its algebraic
boundary chart, not as an automatic image of the finite cap.

## 1. An exact all-level finite boundary functional

Retain the literal tensor and all original pivots of
[the two-cut construction](actual_two_cut_framing_port_reconstruction.md).
Let C_X=P(A X B_word) be its original one-cut endomorphism after
inserting the coupon X at the second cut. Here B_word is a word
prefix; it differs from the physical clock B. In the balanced frame
the original source is Phi(C)=(1/N)sum_(c,a) C_ca. Put

\[
 F(\eta_F)=\sum_{a,b,c,d}T_{c,d;a,b}\eta_F^{d-b}.
\]

The identity second-cut source is Phi(C_I)=F_0/N. In the pure-prefix
geometric chart one instead encounters

\[
 J(\eta_{geo})=\frac{\eta_{geo}}{s(1-\eta_{geo})^2},\qquad
 [\eta^0]J(\eta)F(\eta)
           =\frac1s\sum_{m=1}^{N-1}mF_{-m}.                 \tag{1}
\]

The finite sum in (1) is exact on the Fourier side too, but this
does not identify its source with the geometric one. Let U be the
literal raising shift, U e_j=e_(j+1), U^N=0. Define

\[
 X_- =\sum_{m=1}^{N-1}mU^m=U(1-U)^{-2},\qquad
 \mathcal M_N(T)=\frac1N\sum_{m=1}^{N-1}mF_{-m}.
\]

Then for EVERY four-port tensor, including its arbitrary whole
crossing couplings and actual sources,

\[
                    \mathcal M_N(T)=\Phi(C_{X_-}).          \tag{2}
\]

Indeed the coefficient of E_bd in X_- is b-d for b>d and zero
otherwise, exactly the negative output-band weight. All finite
bands, and the same single factor 1/N, are retained. This is a
coupon/source formula, not a replacement of the original identity
coupon by an asymptotically convenient function.

## 2. The coupon is an exact true K-Ward boundary

In the actual head frame K=xQ^(-J_head). For m=1,...,N-1,

\[
 K U^m K^{-1}=Q^{-m}U^m.
\]

Consequently the WHOLE rational coupon

\[
 H_- =\sum_{m=1}^{N-1}\frac{m}{Q^{-m}-1}U^m
\]

is regular at a primitive N-th root and satisfies

\[
                    X_-=(\operatorname{Ad}K-I)H_-.          \tag{3}
\]

Every denominator in (3) is a unit there. The meridian scalar x
cancels in Ad K; its other appearances in the original tensors
and source are still retained. Actual A and B_word intertwine
the true total K. The diagonal pivotal trace, for both the ordinary
and frozen pivotal routes, commutes with this group-like action.
Thus the exact source identity is

\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \mathcal M_N(T)=\Phi\bigl((\operatorname{Ad}K_{cut}-I)C_{H_-}\bigr)
   =\frac1N\sum_{c,a}(Q^{a-c}-1)(C_{H_-})_{ca}.\quad}        \tag{4}
\]

This is a genuine Hopf Ward row. The last source row is generally
NONZERO: the original balanced rank-one source is not invariant on
every cut matrix. Its value on the scalar C_I does not imply its
invariance on the charged C_(H_-). Additional weighted crossings,
prefix coupons or nonintertwining coupons retain their actual K
defects in the same ordered insertion formula; they may not be
commuted away.

At the physical unitary root, in the old flat head metric,
the shift U has norm one. Using
|1-Q^m|=2 sin(pi m/N) at least 4 min(m,N-m)/N gives

\[
 \|X_-\|\leq N(N-1)/2,\qquad
 \|H_-\|\leq\frac N4\sum_{m=1}^{N-1}\frac{m}{\min(m,N-m)}
                  =O(N^2\log N).                           \tag{5}
\]

This is a polynomial root bound for this specific Ward primitive.
It is not a whole-word R norm estimate or a tame comparison of
different source metrics. Whole q-normal derivatives must still be
taken from (3)-(4) with the actual moving tensors and source.

## 3. Actual signed tensor counterexamples to identity-source recovery

For the literal flip tensor (the two-cut unknot),
F_j=N-|j| and sum_(m>0)mF_-m=N(N^2-1)/6. Hence its proposed
unknot-normalized boundary functional would be

\[
 \widehat{\mathcal M}_N(T)
       =\frac{6}{N(N^2-1)}\sum_{m>0}mF_{-m}.                \tag{6}
\]

It cannot be identified with the original source for arbitrary
actual words. Here is an exact characteristic-zero actual crossing
calculation. At N=2 let q=i, eta_half=exp(pi i/4), and retain

\[
 R_+=\begin{pmatrix}
 \eta_{half}&0&0&0\\
 0&0&\eta_{half}^{-1}&0\\
 0&\eta_{half}^{-1}&2i\eta_{half}^{-1}&0\\
 0&0&0&\eta_{half}
 \end{pmatrix},\quad
 g=K^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(-i,i),\quad R_-=R_+^{-1}.
\]

These are the literal actual balanced coefficients, not an unrelated
matrix model. Keep the external line (i/2)eta_half^(-3 writhe)
from the existing actual implementation. Cutting between a positive
crossing and its inverse gives

\[
 T^{pair}_{c,d;a,b}
     =\frac i2\operatorname{ptr}_g
          [R_-(I\otimes E_{bd})R_+]_{ca},\qquad
 F_{pair}(\eta_F)=i(\eta_F^{-1}-\eta_F).                     \tag{7}
\]

Thus its original source is zero whereas its negative-band moment
is nonzero. Cutting outside that cancelling pair instead gives a
constant F equal to zero and moment zero. The complete original
source is unchanged; (1) detects the actual inserted Ward current
and its placement. No discarded pivotal or framing factor explains
the discrepancy.

The failure is not limited to a zero-source link. With the second
cut immediately after the first positive crossing, the actual
one-crossing and three-crossing words have, in the SAME retained
normalization,

\[
 \begin{array}{c|c|c}
 \text{word}&\Phi(C_I)&F_{-1}\\\hline
 \sigma_1&-i/2&1/2\\
 \sigma_1^3&3i/2&1/2.
 \end{array}                                                \tag{8}
\]

Their closed braids are the unknot and trefoil. No common rescaling
calibrated on the former makes the moment agree with the latter's
original source. The common external conventions in (8) matter;
they have not been separately normalized to preferred knot values.
Equations (3)-(4), not equality to the identity source, explain the
nonzero moment.

## 4. An actual Ward factorial has the required inverse-norm pole

There is also a constructive local atom, retaining both the physical
edge clock and its quantum normal. Use the actual unfolding F(Q,z)
of the original factorial block. Ward iteration gives the EXACT
whole negative N-step ratio

\[
 R_N^-(Q,B)
   =\frac{F(Q,BQ^{-N})}{F(Q,B)}
   =\frac1{(BQ^{-N};Q)_N}
   =\frac1{(1-B/Z)\,V_N(Q,B)},\quad Z=Q^N,                  \tag{9}
\]

where V_N=product_(r=1)^(N-1)(1-BQ^r/Z). Near the actual corner
Q=Q_N,B=1 it is a unit with V_N(Q_N,1)=N. In particular the
off-root divisor is B=Z, not B=1 obtained by first forgetting
Q^N. Every scalar and this moving divisor are retained in (9).

At the physical root,

\[
 R_N^-(Q_N,B)=\frac1{1-B^N}.                                \tag{10}
\]

The scaled physical boundary B=exp(w/N) makes (10) exactly
1/(1-exp w), for EVERY N. Its pole at w=0 has coefficient -1.
This is a uniform root-slice boundary model. No uniform growing-N
off-root estimate for V_N is inferred from its fixed-N unit property.
The fixed-N exceptional normal ratio of the same actual atom,
Q=Q_N exp(epsilon), U=exp(tN epsilon), is (1-B^N)^t. Formula (9)
is its t=-1 case as an exact whole finite-product identity, with
U=Q^(-N). Thus the pole is an original negative Ward circuit and
its norm zero, not a pole supplied by a chosen unrelated amplitude.

Its place in an actual factorial crossing coefficient can be specified
without discarding the other factors. For any literal block
C_k(z)=(z;Q)_k, the whole N-carry identity is

\[
 \frac{C_{k+N}(z)}{C_k(z)}=(zQ^k;Q)_N,
 \qquad \frac{C_k(z)}{C_{k+N}(z)}=(zQ^k;Q)_N^{-1}.           \tag{10a}
\]

The actual balanced R coefficients in
[the marked holonomy construction](marked_braid_holonomy.md), Section 3,
are products and quotients of precisely these blocks and Cartan lines.
For a denominator block, put B=zQ^(k+N) in (9); (10a)'s inverse
is exactly the same atom. At the root its full norm is
1-(zQ^k)^N, rather than an unmarked numerical multiplier. This
identifies a specific original denominator-block carry that supplies
the pole. It does not treat that block in isolation as the full
crossing coefficient: its numerator blocks, resonant factorials,
Cartan line and actual source remain necessary and can cancel it.

For the pure-prefix GEOMETRIC chart, take c=U_21 nonzero, s nonzero.
Its principal relation is

\[
 k-1=-\frac{c\eta_{geo}}{s(1-\eta_{geo})^2},\quad B=k^{-1},
 \quad
 \Omega_{cut}=\frac{d\log A\wedge d\eta_{geo}}
                         {s(1-\eta_{geo})^2}.              \tag{11}
\]

If the actual negative Ward circuit (9) is inserted and this
principal geometric chart is used, its amplitude (10) has the

\[
 [\eta_{geo}^{-1}]R_N^-(Q_N,B(\eta_{geo}))=-\frac{s}{Nc},
 \qquad
 \mathop{\mathrm{Res}}_{\eta_{geo}=0}
                  (R_N^-\Omega_{cut})=-\frac1{Nc}d\log A.   \tag{12}
\]

This follows from B-1=(c/s)eta_geo+O(eta_geo^2), so
1-B^N=-(Nc/s)eta_geo+O(eta_geo^2). Only that first polar coefficient
enters (12). It is a nonzero actual-atom boundary coefficient.

It is NOT a theorem that the original full knot co-source contains
this negative circuit, or that its Fourier cap cycle maps to this
