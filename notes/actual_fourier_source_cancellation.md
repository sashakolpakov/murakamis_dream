# An actual zero-source Fourier current retains a geometric formal block

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. The last Fourier-label source has
an explicit, uniformly tame cyclic current complex. A literal Cartan
port current of the original knot tensor lies in its zero-source
subspace, retains the actual highest-input one-sided support, and on
the proved regular geometric chart has a NONZERO formal block with
the SAME regulator action. Thus nonzero geometric partial support,
root scalarity, the pointed boundary line and one-sided support do
not by themselves prove survival in the selected sum.

This is an actual current/source obstruction, not a replacement state
or a proposed positive model. The geometric formal assertion below
uses the regular good-A chart; it does not assert that chart extends
to the highest-input boundary A=1.

## 1. A literal whole Cartan current has zero selected source

Let T_(c,d;a,b) be the original two-cut tensor, with every original
cap, pivot, scalar and peripheral line retained. Put B=diag(Q^b)
on the SECOND physical wire and define

    W(T)=[(I tensor B)T(I tensor B^-1)-T]/(Q-1).   (1)

Its literal band coefficient is

    W(T)_(c,b+q;a,b)=[q]_Q T_(c,b+q;a,b),
    [q]_Q=(Q^q-1)/(Q-1).                         (2)

For every integer q this is a Laurent polynomial in Q, including
negative q; it has a regular Q=1 continuation. Formula (1) is a
specified actual position/Cartan PORT current. It makes no assumption
about an untwisted total Hopf action on a reshaped tensor.

Partial trace on the second identity wire is invariant under this
conjugation. Therefore the ENTIRE reclosed first-wire matrix obeys

    ptr_second W(T)=0                             (3)

as a WHOLE finite-matrix identity, including all q-normal derivatives.
No root scalarity is used in (3). The first input/output source,
highest vector, Whittaker row and external dimension line remain
exactly the originals.

The multiplier (2) changes no index or support. If T is charge zero
and the first input is a, its band interval is [a-(N-1),a]. At the
highest input a=0 it remains the original nonpositive interval.
In particular the actual endpoint defects 1-Q^N and M-M^-1 of
[the highest boundary](actual_highest_whittaker_boundary_and_moving_walls.md)
have not been discarded or replaced by cyclic off-root formulas.

    J_W,j=(J_(j+1)-J_j)/(Q_N-1),                  (4)

## 3. The same actual current has nonzero geometric normal support

Now use the proved good first-position phase, not the unproved
highest-boundary extension. The actual proper root lift and its
nondegenerate Fourier Gaussian give

    Z(v,H)=C(H) exp(S_geo/H)[A_0(v)+H A_1(v)+...],
    eta=exp(v), p_e=eta^-1.

Here S_geo is independent of v; all original base, peripheral,
half-density and Fourier phase marks stay in C and the chosen line.
Multiplication of the actual open coefficient by (2) is EXACTLY
the external shift identity

    Z_W(v,H)=[Z(v+H,H)-Z(v,H)]/(exp(H)-1).         (8)

Equation (8) uses the specified proper root lift of the partial
family; it does not equate that lift's q-normal jets with the
whole-Vandermonde family. It follows coefficientwise, rather than
by a growing-band analytic limit. Hence

    Z_W=C(H) exp(S_geo/H)[partial_v A_0+O(H)].      (9)

The leading derivative is NOT identically zero. Indeed the uniform
actual Gaussian/holonomy density and its Fourier Jacobian show at
fixed good A that

    A_0(v)=c(M,A) partial_v r(eta),                (10)

up to the retained fixed ordered Fourier phase. Here is the needed
frame-independence justification, rather than a free normalization:
the [actual uniform density theorem](quantum_source_bridge_extension.md)
gives Omega_beta^2=(-T_beta/2)Omega_cut^2. Its determinant ratio
T_beta is invariant under simultaneous representation framing;
the frame-change determinants in its two chain maps cancel and
det Ad(g)=1. On the marked half-density cover the square of
Omega_beta/Omega_cut is therefore frame-constant. Differentiating
that square in characteristic zero makes the marked ratio itself
frame-constant. The exact Fourier Jacobian then gives (10), with
a nonzero c depending only on M,A and the retained base mark.
At completeness r(eta) is the nonconstant
rational inverse of the actual birational (A,p_e) chart. If
partial_v A_0 vanished, eta partial_eta r would be a constant d.
Then dr=d deta/eta. A derivative of a rational function has zero
logarithmic residue at eta=0, so d=0, forcing r constant. This
contradicts that birational chart. Thus partial_v A_0 is nonzero
on a dense regular open. The result persists on the marked
meridian deformation germ.
