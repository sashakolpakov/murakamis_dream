# A terminal window with both retained inputs selected

Research deduction, 6 November 2026. Moving the incoming retained leg
to a selected height introduces a real force that was zero in the
completed unselected-input terminal comparison. Adding the preceding
own-four ordinary factor makes this leg internal: its original
stationarity cancels its entire linear height response. This gives
an exact five-cost main criterion and a four-cost real-cut/entropy
criterion. No new comparison sign is asserted or evaluated.

## 1. Original source, weights and the incoming-height issue

Use the original regular-strip primitive \(g\), the original terminal
phase \(\Phi_\epsilon(a;t,v)\), and
\[
 Q(s;b,a)=g(s+b)+g(s+a)-3g(s)-g(b)-g(a)+2\pi ibs,
 \qquad P=[.22,.45],\qquad H=1/100.
 \tag{1}
\]
Here \(\epsilon=0,1\) denotes terminal type three or four, and
\(v=\xi,e\), respectively. All lifts, private actions and chirps
are original. Use the height hypotheses of the
[symmetric-cost theorem](research_mixed_terminal_symmetric_pair_costs.md):
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 -.102\le h_s,h_r\le-.001,\\
 (h_t,h_v)\in
 \begin{cases}
 [-.072,.021]\times[.049,.077],&\epsilon=0,\\
 {}[-.108,-.020]\times[-.051,-.019],&\epsilon=1.
 \end{cases}
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{2}
\]
Only an original exactly stationary reference is used below.
An enclosing midpoint is not declared stationary.

First consider the original two-factor terminal window with both
outer retained coordinates selected. Its allocated modulus is
\[
 |C_{1,N}(X_b,X_a)\mathcal V_{\epsilon,N}(w,X_a)
                d_N(X_a)X_a^{-N}|\,|d_N(X_b)X_b^{-N}|^{1/2}.
 \tag{3}
\]
The half-power is a modulus allocation, restored to the full selected
factor by the adjoining window, not a holomorphic square root.
Put \(\mu,\nu\in\{0,1\}\), where one means factorial and zero
means forced. The four complete-weight rate alternatives contain
\[
 -\frac\mu2\Re g(b)-\nu\Re g(a)-\pi\mu H-2\pi\nu H.
 \tag{4}
\]
Both selected heights are \(H\). Their original heights, hence their
height displacements, need not agree. The branches in (4) are not
four separately continued source polynomials.

Let \(q_*=(b_*,s_*,a_*,t_*,v_*)\) be an original reference, with
\(\Im b_*,\Im a_*\in[-.108,-.001]\), and set
\[
 F=Q+\Phi_\epsilon-g(a)-\tfrac12g(b),\quad
 M_*=F(q_*),\quad p_*=F_b(q_*),\quad
 \bar b=b_*+i\Delta_b,\quad\bar a=a_*+i\Delta_a,
 \tag{5}
\]
where \(\Delta_b=H-\Im b_*>0\), \(\Delta_a=H-\Im a_*>0\).
The original internal equations give \(F_s=F_a=F_t=F_v=0\).
Write
\[
 \mathcal B_g(z;d)=g(z+id)-g(z)-idg'(z),\qquad
 L_d(z)=g'(z+id)-g'(z),\qquad G_z=\Re g(z).
 \tag{6}
\]
After subtracting the unchanged complex affine term
\(M_*+p_*(b-b_*)\), the lifted-reference value is exactly
\[
 \begin{split}
 T^{(2)}_{\mu\nu}={}&\Re\{
  \mathcal B_g(s_*+b_*;\Delta_b)
       -\tfrac32\mathcal B_g(b_*;\Delta_b)\\
 &+\mathcal B_g(s_*+a_*;\Delta_a)
       +\mathcal B_g(t_*+a_*;\Delta_a)
                    -3\mathcal B_g(a_*;\Delta_a)\}\\
 &+\tfrac{1-\mu}{2}G_{\bar b}+(1-\nu)G_{\bar a}
                       -\pi\mu H-2\pi\nu H .
 \end{split}
 \tag{7}
\]
Its incoming real force is now
\[
 B^{(2)}_\mu=
 \Re\{L_{\Delta_b}(s_*+b_*)-\tfrac32L_{\Delta_b}(b_*)\}
                 +\tfrac{1-\mu}{2}\Re g'(\bar b).
 \tag{8}
\]
It is not generally zero. The ordinary-index force also changes by
\(\Re L_{\Delta_b}(s_*+b_*)-2\pi\Delta_b\), retaining the
incoming chirp. These are the missing terms in a direct reuse of the
unselected-input comparison. Matching the boundary affine terms
does not discard them.

## 2. A larger window cancels the internal linear response

Take an original word of length \(m\ge4\) with final four types
\[
 (n_{m-3},n_{m-2},n_{m-1},n_m)
            =(3+\gamma,4,4,3+\epsilon),\qquad\gamma\in\{0,1\}.
 \tag{9}
\]
Write \(c=z_{m-3}, b=z_{m-2}, a=z_{m-1}\). Keep \(c\)
unselected, and select \(b,a\) at height \(H\). Let \(r,s\)
be the indices of the two own-four ordinary factors. The literal
allocated modulus is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal L_N={}&|C_{1,N}(X_c,X_b)C_{1,N}(X_b,X_a)
                   \mathcal V_{\epsilon,N}(w,X_a)\\
 &\hspace{22mm}\cdot d_N(X_b)X_b^{-N}d_N(X_a)X_a^{-N}|
                                         |d_N(X_c)|^{\gamma/2}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{10}
\]
Each internal selected factor occurs once. No additional terminal
diagonal or monomial is inserted.

At the exact reference
\(q_*=(c_*,r_*,b_*,s_*,a_*,t_*,v_*)\), define
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal F&=Q(r;c,b)+Q(s;b,a)+\Phi_\epsilon(a;t,v)
                       -g(b)-g(a)-\tfrac\gamma2g(c),\\
 \mathcal M_*&=\mathcal F(q_*),\qquad
 p_c=\mathcal F_c(q_*)
       =g'(c_*+r_*)-(1+\gamma/2)g'(c_*)+2\pi ir_* .
 \end{split}
 \tag{11}
\]
All six internal derivatives vanish. In particular,
\[
 \begin{split}
 g'(r_*+b_*)+g'(s_*+b_*)-3g'(b_*)+2\pi is_*&=0,\\
 g'(s_*+a_*)+g'(t_*+a_*)-3g'(a_*)+2\pi it_*&=0.
 \end{split}
 \tag{12}
\]
For the main terminal label its four defects are
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal D_{\mu\nu}={}&\Re\{Q(r;c,b)+Q(s;b,a)
                   +\Phi_\epsilon(a;t,v)
                -\mu g(b)-\nu g(a)-\tfrac\gamma2g(c)\}\\
 &-2\pi H(\mu+\nu)-\Re\{\mathcal M_*+p_c(c-c_*)\}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{13}
\]
The original targets have \(c_R,b_R,a_R\in P\),
\(r_R,s_R\in[.125,.5]\), \(t_R\in[.15,.5]\), and the full
private interval \([.1,.4]\) or \([.27,.49]\). Only the heights
of \(b,a\) change. Assume (2), \(c_*\in K_{3+\gamma}\),
\(\Im b_*,\Im a_*\in[-.108,-.001]\), and reference real parts
in the containing localized intervals below. These are the original
strong reference hypotheses for the actual word, not a declaration
of a new canonical family or of finite-word realization.
Here the actual typed domains, as in the
[source-domain theorem](research_mixed_terminal_canonical_source_domain.md), are
\[
 \begin{split}
 K_3={}&\{|z-(.35-.01i)|\le.083\}
       \cap\{-.072\le\Im z\le.068,\ \Re z-3\Im z\le.608\},\\
 K_4={}&\{|z-(.30-.052i)|\le.060\}
       \cap\{-.108\le\Im z\le-.001\}.
 \end{split}
\]

At \(\bar q=(c_*,r_*,\bar b,s_*,\bar a,t_*,v_*)\), put
\[
 \begin{split}
 V_b&=\Re\{\mathcal B_g(r_*+b_*;\Delta_b)
               +\mathcal B_g(s_*+b_*;\Delta_b)
                           -3\mathcal B_g(b_*;\Delta_b)\},\\
 V_a&=\Re\{\mathcal B_g(s_*+a_*;\Delta_a)
               +\mathcal B_g(t_*+a_*;\Delta_a)
                           -3\mathcal B_g(a_*;\Delta_a)\}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{14}
\]
The exact trial value is
\[
 \mathcal T_{\mu\nu}=V_b+V_a
                +(1-\mu)G_{\bar b}+(1-\nu)G_{\bar a}
                                -2\pi H(\mu+\nu).
 \tag{15}
\]
There is no mixed \(b,a\) remainder: at fixed main indices the
original phase separates in those variables. Equations (12) cancel
both linear height responses, including their chirps. This is an
exact-reference identity, not stationarity imposed on an enclosing
rectangle.

The real gradient in the order \((c,r,b,s,a,t,v)\) is
\[
                   (0,A_r,A_{b,\mu},A_s,A_{a,\nu},A_t,0),
 \tag{16}
\]
where
\[
 \begin{split}
 A_r={}&\Re L_{\Delta_b}(r_*+b_*),\\
 A_{b,\mu}={}&\Re\{L_{\Delta_b}(r_*+b_*)
             +L_{\Delta_b}(s_*+b_*)-3L_{\Delta_b}(b_*)\}
                                  +(1-\mu)\Re g'(\bar b),\\
 A_s={}&\Re\{L_{\Delta_b}(s_*+b_*)
                         +L_{\Delta_a}(s_*+a_*)\}-2\pi\Delta_b,\\
 A_{a,\nu}={}&\Re\{L_{\Delta_a}(s_*+a_*)
             +L_{\Delta_a}(t_*+a_*)-3L_{\Delta_a}(a_*)\}
                                  +(1-\nu)\Re g'(\bar a),\\
 A_t={}&\Re L_{\Delta_a}(t_*+a_*)-2\pi\Delta_a .
 \end{split}
 \tag{17}
\]
In particular \(A_s\) contains both height moves with their actual
signs. It must not inherit the one-height positive-force assumption.
The \(c\)-force is zero because the lifted variables do not occur
in \(\mathcal F_c\) at fixed \(r_*\). The private derivative is
also unchanged at fixed \(t_*,v_*\).

## 3. Simultaneous costs and a five-scalar main criterion

The [full-input ordinary barrier](research_mixed_terminal_full_input_index_barrier.md)
applies to both ordinary factors. The left outgoing height is \(H\),
and the right incoming height is \(H\in[-.12,.08]\).
Thus their maxima have \(r_R,s_R\ge.22\). The
[terminal barrier](research_mixed_terminal_main_optimizer_localization.md)
is independent of \(b,c,r,s\) and still gives \(.22<t_R<.4\).
These pointwise barriers are independent of the four weight choices.

On the localized containing product the symmetric pair theorem gives
the single matrix inequality, in the order (16),
\[
 -\mathcal D_{\mu\nu}''\succeq\operatorname{diag}\left(
 \tfrac{1+\gamma}{2}g_{h_c}'',\ 2g_{h_r}'',\
 (\mu+1)g_H'',\ 2g_{h_s}'',\ (\nu+1)g_H'',\
 \tfrac{3+\epsilon}{2}g_{h_t}'',\ 0\right).
 \tag{18}
\]
Indeed decompose each ordinary factor as its two \(V\) pairs,
minus its index \(g\), plus the original chirp. Each pair retains
half of both node curvatures. At \(b\), the two ordinary incidences
retain one full curvature before adding \(\mu\). At \(a\), the
ordinary and terminal incidences do the same. The terminal private
block is handled exactly as in symmetric-cost Proposition 2.
All pair real parts are at least .22, their individual heights
have modulus at most .12, and their summed real parts are strictly
below 1. The type-three private block uses its separately proved
larger domain. All off-diagonal motions are retained before this
comparison.

For \(\phi_h(x;x_*)=g_h(x)-g_h(x_*)-g_h'(x_*)(x-x_*)\), set
\[
 \Gamma_{h,k}(F;x_*,I)
       =\sup_{x\in I}\{F(x-x_*)-k\phi_h(x;x_*)\}.
 \tag{19}
\]
This is a signed supremum, not its maximum with zero. Let \(I_1=P\)
and take either \(I_0=P\) or \(I_0=[.4,.45]\).
The [simultaneous branch-domain theorem](research_mixed_selected_weight_branch_domains.md)
allows the latter choice separately for \(b,a\), with the same
complete choice throughout each allocation. The centers remain
their true reference real parts even if they lie outside a forced
target interval. Concavity and segment integration use the full
containing \(P\) product.

**Proposition 1.** Each complete-weight branch satisfies
\[
 \begin{split}
 \sup\mathcal D_{\mu\nu}\le\mathcal T_{\mu\nu}
 &+\Gamma_{h_r,2}(A_r;\Re r_*,[.22,.5])\\
 &+\Gamma_{H,\mu+1}(A_{b,\mu};\Re b_*,I_\mu)\\
 &+\Gamma_{h_s,2}(A_s;\Re s_*,[.22,.5])\\
 &+\Gamma_{H,\nu+1}(A_{a,\nu};\Re a_*,I_\nu)\\
 &+\Gamma_{h_t,(3+\epsilon)/2}(A_t;\Re t_*,[.22,.4]).
 \end{split}
 \tag{20}
\]
Integrate (18) along each segment from \(\bar q\) to a target.
The \(c,v\) forces vanish, so their nonnegative costs can be
discarded. The remaining scalar suprema give (20). This uses the
usual convex-remainder method; the source-specific content is
the cancellations (14)--(17) and simultaneous allocation (18).

Under (2), the
[cubic-cost theorem](research_mixed_terminal_cubic_cost_and_response_bounds.md)
permits replacing these five terms by its signed cubic upper bounds
with coefficients, respectively,
\[
 \frac{88}{5},\quad\frac{49}{5}(\mu+1),\quad
 \frac{88}{5},\quad\frac{49}{5}(\nu+1),\quad
                         \frac{22}{5}(3+\epsilon).
 \tag{21}
\]
No force sign or optimizer direction is assumed. The scalar tests
retain their common original reference and any useful correlations
among (17).
For the optional forced interval \(I_0=[.4,.45]\), this is an
analytic clipped-supremum statement: the clipping must retain that
actual interval, even when the center is outside it, and must not
replace a negative answer by zero. The existing executable cubic-gain
helper assumes its center is inside the target interval. It cannot
be used unchanged for such a restricted forced interval. Taking
\(I_0=P\) is already valid and retains that helper's hypothesis;
no modified evaluator or restricted-interval calculation is supplied
here. The same distinction applies to (29).

## 4. Real transform cuts and entropy require only four gains

Let \(\tau=.15\) or \(.5\). Suppose an original terminal profile
has the literal form
\[
 T_\tau(a;y)=\Re\Xi_\tau(a)+E(y),\qquad
 \Xi_\tau(a)=g(\tau+a)-g(a)+2\pi i\tau a,
 \tag{22}
\]
and its original bare-source estimate at the same \(a_*\) is
\[
 T_\tau(a_*;y)-\Re P_\epsilon(a_*)\le U
                      \quad\hbox{for every original private target }y.
 \tag{23}
\]
These are the real-transform and entropy profiles of the
[direct-cut theorem](research_mixed_terminal_real_transform_cut_budget.md).
That theorem proves, without moving the source bound,
\[
 T_\tau(a;y)\le\Re P_\epsilon(a_*)+U+
                    \Re\{\Xi_\tau(a)-\Xi_\tau(a_*)\}.
 \tag{24}
\]
The elimination is independent of \(b,c,r,s\), so it remains valid
with both retained heights changed. It eliminates the full private
interval or entropy triangle, including its physical walls.

Keep its literal
\[
 \begin{split}
 D_\tau(z)&=g'(\tau+z)-g'(t_*+z)+2\pi i(\tau-t_*),\\
 C_\tau&=-\int_{\Im a_*}^{H}\Im D_\tau(\Re a_*+iy)\,dy,
          \qquad d_\tau=\Re D_\tau(\bar a).
 \end{split}
 \tag{25}
\]
After removing the additive \(U\), the reduced trial value is
\(\mathcal T_{\mu\nu}+C_\tau\), with real gradient in
\((c,r,b,s,a)\)
\[
                   (0,A_r,A_{b,\mu},A_s,A_{a,\nu}+d_\tau).
 \tag{26}
\]
Equivalently, the old \(t_*+a_*\) remainder cancels exactly:
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal T_{\mu\nu}+C_\tau={}&V_b-\Delta_a\Im D_\tau(a_*)\\
 &+\Re\{\mathcal B_g(s_*+a_*;\Delta_a)
       +\mathcal B_g(\tau+a_*;\Delta_a)
                         -3\mathcal B_g(a_*;\Delta_a)\}\\
 &+(1-\mu)G_{\bar b}+(1-\nu)G_{\bar a}
                                      -2\pi H(\mu+\nu).
 \end{split}
 \tag{27}
\]
The chirp and signed first-order cut correction remain. No
main-height optimizer theorem is applied to a height-zero cut.

The direct-cut scalar curvature is
\(-\Re\partial_a^2V(\tau,a)\ge\sigma_\tau g_H''\), with
\(\sigma_{.15}=3/5,\ \sigma_{.5}=5/3\). Repeating the
simultaneous allocation gives, in the order (26), costs
\[
 \left(\tfrac{1+\gamma}{2}g_{h_c}'',\ 2g_{h_r}'',\
        (\mu+1)g_H'',\ 2g_{h_s}'',\
                      (\nu+\tfrac12+\sigma_\tau)g_H''\right).
 \tag{28}
\]
Thus a sufficient upper bound for this profile is
\[
 \begin{split}
 U+\mathcal T_{\mu\nu}+C_\tau
 &+\Gamma_{h_r,2}(A_r;\Re r_*,[.22,.5])\\
 &+\Gamma_{H,\mu+1}(A_{b,\mu};\Re b_*,I_\mu)\\
 &+\Gamma_{h_s,2}(A_s;\Re s_*,[.22,.5])\\
 &+\Gamma_{H,\nu+1/2+\sigma_\tau}
                  (A_{a,\nu}+d_\tau;\Re a_*,I_\nu).
 \end{split}
 \tag{29}
\]
The four cubic coefficients are \(88/5\),
\((49/5)(\mu+1)\), \(88/5\), and
\((49/5)(\nu+1/2+\sigma_\tau)\). A source-cell \(U\) and
the other terms must always refer to the same reference cell.

## 5. Finite-source implication and remaining quantitative premise

At \(H=.01\), both ordinary factors lie in the full rectangle of
the [ordinary-label completion](research_mixed_terminal_ordinary_label_completion.md).
The left incoming height is the original height of \(c_*\in K_{3+\gamma}\);
the right incoming height is .01. Both outgoing heights are .01,
their index heights satisfy (2), and the outer real parts remain
independent throughout \(P\). The finite seven-label envelope also
applies because their outer height moduli are at most .108.
Every nonmain ordinary label can therefore be replaced by its main
maximum, with extra loss \(1/500\), while keeping the other factor,
terminal profile, both selected weights and full affine subtraction.
No physical row, contour current or zero-height join is dropped.

**Proposition 2 (conditional finite-source consequence).** Suppose
on the required compact original reference family each of the 27
complete terminal labels, with both ordinary indices main and each
of the four choices \((\mu,\nu)\), has defect below
\(-\delta\), for a fixed \(\delta>0\), relative to (11).
Then for each fixed \(\eta>0\), the full original (10) obeys
\[
 \mathcal L_N\le C_\eta N^{A_\eta}
  \exp N\{\Re[\mathcal M_*+p_c(c-c_*)]-\delta+\eta\}
 \tag{30}
\]
on the stated targets, for all sufficiently large levels of both
parities. The source hypotheses are those of the complete terminal
envelope on actual \(b_*\in K_4\), justified for actual suffixes by
the [canonical source-domain theorem](research_mixed_terminal_canonical_source_domain.md).

Indeed the ordinary losses add to the required terminal gap, without
creating a smaller bottleneck. The terminal compiler retains its
27 labels, including both ends of each sign-split vertical side,
all real cuts, entropy and the constant. The complete weights use
one branch choice per node. Sum these finitely many estimates with
one total tolerance. Original compact-source color neighborhoods
and fixed ordinary-color jets are inherited. The unchanged
[real-color envelope](research_mixed_terminal_profile_real_color_lift.md)
also gives (30) at real outside colors if these same 27 comparisons
are paid; it asserts no angular loss for transported cuts.

This reduces the selected-incoming task to 108 terminal/branch tests,
without a new \(7^2\)-fold ordinary-profile calculation. Equation
(20) supplies the main tests; (29) supplies the fixed-transform and
entropy tests without private-action evaluation. Other terminal
labels still require their own quantitative comparisons. Neither
the completed unselected-input gap nor (12) proves these new signs.
No new parameter cover, evaluation or negative rate is included here.

At the left boundary, the adjoining original source must supply
the complementary \(c\)-halfweight and opposite complex marginal.
At \(m=4\) it is the first cap; for \(m>4\) it is ordinary.
The case \(m=3\), where making \(b\) internal requires a first-cap
phase in place of \(Q(r;c,b)\), is not covered by Propositions
1--2. Neither are nonplateau regions, their joining contours, or a
nonzero whole-word coefficient.
