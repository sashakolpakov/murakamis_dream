# The actual cap has affine winding one and a degree-blind unit carry wall

28 October 2026. The ORIGINAL cap has an exact charged winding
equation D+1=N, with the affine +1 forced by its actual dual color.
Its signed carry current is nevertheless identically zero. The original marked
cap detects the canonical cyclic/noncyclic wall with the nonzero
WHOLE meridian value -x/N. The two individual wall charges each
have marked augmentation one; total degree sees only their difference.

This does not identify the last oversized geometric edge with an
actual quantum head or prove its coefficient zero/nonzero. It fixes
the affine charge and augmented wall class that comparison must carry. The
existing common-tree carry lattice and cyclic sewing identities are
not re-proved or called new here. The new point is the actual dual
condition, four-atom degree calculation, and original marked source
evaluation together.

## 1. The source fixes the cyclic wrap ratio

Use the ORIGINAL semicyclic heads V(x,phi), with

    K v_a=xQ^-a v_a,
    F v_a=v_(a+1) (a<d), F v_d=phi v_0,
    d=N-1, k=x^N.

The actual dual is V(y,psi), with

    y=x^-1 Q^-1,        psi=-k phi.                      (1)

These are the proved [actual oriented duals](relative_pivotal_duality.md),
not freely chosen cyclic directions. In particular the total F
center on their cup is phi+k^-1 psi=0. For phi!=0 form the
actual four-atom monomial

    A=F_1^-1 tensor F_2.

Let W_d have basis b_a=v_a tensor v_(d-a), 0<=a<N.
It is stable under A, whose exact restriction is

    A b_a=b_(a-1) for a>0,
    A b_0=-k b_d.                                      (2)

The ratio psi/phi=-k cancels phi. Consequently (2) has a
canonical phi-independent extension to the nilpotent source.
Let T be the nonwrapped part, T b_0=0 and T b_a=b_(a-1).
The source's missing edge is exactly

    A-T=-k B,       B=|b_d><b_0|,
    A^N=-k I,       T^N=0.                             (3)

The central -k is retained, not replaced by a complete numerical
value. This is the canonical carry limit of the genuine cyclic
monomial, rather than an arbitrary added wrap.

## 2. The total degree current is zero on that actual fiber

Let J_i have the literal integer eigenvalues 0,...,d and D=J_1+J_2.
On the full two-head space, let P_1 project to first index 0 and
P_2 project to second index d. The EXACT cyclic degree identity is

    [D,A]=N A(P_1-P_2).                                (4)

Indeed lowering the first index changes J_1 by -1 except at its
wrap, where it changes by N-1; raising the second changes J_2 by
1 except at its wrap, where it changes by 1-N. This proves (4)
with the actual nonzero wrap coefficients as well.

For the four genuine crossing monomials in
[the four-atom factorization](four_cyclic_atoms.md),

    m_i=c_i A S_1^(b_i),
    b_i=(-1,0,0,1),
    c_i=(-Qx,x^-1,Qx,-x^-1),

the same equation holds with A replaced by m_i, since the
integer degree commutes with each clock S_1. Thus the physical
total degree records a DIFFERENCE of the two wrap charges.

On W_d, exactly

    D=d I,       P_1=P_2=P_0=|b_0><b_0|.               (5)

The degree current (4) therefore vanishes identically, although
the common deleted edge (3) is nonzero. Any spectral projector
depending ONLY on D is either zero or identity on this whole
fiber. It cannot remove the cyclic wall while retaining its
noncyclic source. Thus the signed carry CURRENT alone does not
identify the common augmented class.

The degree VALUE, in contrast, carries a precise affine winding.
The actual dual color has xy=Q^-1, so on the entire two-head space

    K_total=Q^(-1-D).

Consequently its eigenvalue-one subspace is exactly W_d:
since 0<=D<=2N-2, the only possible multiple of N for D+1
is N. Every actual invariant cup therefore obeys

    D+1=N,       W_cap=(D+1)/N=1.                      (5a)

This is an exact integer equation, not a choice of log lift or
only an asymptotic statement. The affine +1 is forced by the
original dual K root. It excludes total degree zero on this
channel. The same conclusion holds for the remaining pair
after the other ordinary caps of any actual plat are contracted.

What remains unproved is that a new Pachner edge's three angular
indices give THIS charged D+1. A zero Euler-transfer route in
the actual crossing does not disprove (5a): its cup input can
be (d,0), so that zero transfer still has charged degree N.

Individually the two degree equations retain an affine term:

    [J_1,A]=-A+N A P_0,
    [J_2,A]= A-N A P_0.

Since A is invertible on this fiber, the winding cochain is

    P_0=(I+A^-1[J_1,A])/N,       Tr(P_0)=1.             (6)

Its unit augmentation disappears on summing the two degree
currents. The signed total-degree carry is a coboundary with
zero cyclic sum; the single common wall retains the fundamental
unit cyclic class. An edge-winding comparison needs this
augmented class, not just D.

    V_j=epsilon/[(1-Q)(Q^2 delta;Q)_j

## 8. Its normalized diagonal source is an exact discrete Poisson measure

Let G=sum_j W_jV_j and R=V_d. Finite-product cancellation gives

    W_jV_j=R epsilon(1-delta)/
       [N(1-delta Q^(j+1))(1-delta Q^(-j-1))],
    G=R(2-epsilon)/(1+delta).                          (13)

For the sum, the exact partial-fraction identity gives

    sum_(n=0)^d 1/[(1-delta Q^n)(1-delta Q^-n)]
       =N(1+delta^N)/[(1-delta^N)(1-delta^2)].

This follows by summing
1/(1-delta z)+1/(1-delta/z)-1 over z^N=1 and dividing
by 1-delta^2; the single sums are N/(1-delta^N).
Substitution proves (13), with its actual nonzero scalar.

Normalize w=W/G. The diagonal weights pi_j=w_jV_j are

    pi_j=epsilon(1-delta^2)/
       [N(2-epsilon)(1-delta Q^(j+1))
                         (1-delta Q^(-j-1))],
    sum_j pi_j=1.                                     (14)

For real 0<epsilon<1 and the marked real delta=(1-epsilon)^(1/N),
these are STRICTLY POSITIVE discrete Poisson weights:
the denominator product is |1-delta Q^(j+1)|^2. This is a
positive DIAGONAL boundary functional. It does not make the
full transformed braid kernel positive.

All its clock moments are explicit. For 0<=m<=N,

    sum_j pi_j Q^(m(j+1))
               =(delta^m+delta^(N-m))/(1+delta^N).     (15)

For |delta|<1, expand the two denominator factors in geometric
series and sum the root characters. Only exponent differences
congruent to m survive; the root-summed denominator is
N/(1-delta^2) times
(delta^m+delta^(N-m))/(1-delta^N). Multiplying by the
prefactor in (14) proves (15), with
the same formula at m=0,N. Algebraic continuation gives the
general marked identity. Moments of A itself have the additional
retained factor Q^-m.

Thus the paired source has a local cyclic operator, its true

### The real paired source is a positive rank-one density

Put A_scalar=epsilon/[N(1-delta)]>0 and R=V_d. Exact root

    W=(A_scalar/conjugate R) V^dagger,
    ||V||_2^2=|R|^2(2-epsilon)/[(1+delta)A_scalar],
    w=W/G=V^dagger/||V||_2^2.                          (16)

conjugate A_(j+1)=epsilon/[(1-delta)A_(d-j-1)] and
