# Established volume results and the remaining research question

25 November 2026.

The [paper collection](../papers/README.md) is the current source for
the proved statements. The [dependency graph](../docs/papers/dependencies.html)
records which statements their proofs use. The
[reference manuscript](../manuscript/README.md) gives a unified account;
it does not add hypotheses or conclusions to the individual papers.

The strict Conway theorem proves the reduced Kashaev volume identity
for each eligible fixed construction with independently sufficiently
long auxiliary fillings. Ordered circle trees have separate summed and
directed degree-and-twist criteria. The planar results require their
specified balance or certificate hypotheses. A nonzero central
contribution for a more general diagram is distinct from a bound on its
entire complement. These distinctions are stated in the
[illustrated results guide](../book/chapters/results.md).

For each fixed strict template, long-filling assignments have density
one in its integer lattice. The theorem leaves infinitely many
short-coordinate assignments untreated. It does not establish finitely
many exceptions, density one among distinct knots, or a theorem for
every arborescent knot.

The extension problem is to permit an arbitrary fixed short partial
filling and prove the limit for all sufficiently long remaining fillings
that give hyperbolic knots. The
[research status](research_finite_exceptions_progress.md) explains the
quantifiers, infinite-fiber difficulty, exact orthogonal elimination of
linear packets and current finite-source reductions. The
[route guide](research_routes_and_preserved_ideas.md) keeps the distinct
methods and their limitations accessible.
