# The right incoming horizontal segment with a deeper type-three output

Private research proof, 8 November 2026. The complete original
right horizontal incoming segment is bounded for next type three
when the shared radius is at most \(7/10\). The next private height
may have either sign. Both complete conditional sums, the complete
cap and the forced shared Fourier polynomial are retained.

The incoming corner is not part of this theorem. No mathematical
numerical evaluation or modification of a frozen source is used.

\]

\]

The numerator wall in (25), all zeros, the even half row,
