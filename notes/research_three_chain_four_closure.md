# Four blocks of three crossings, with the original outside boundaries

Research proof, 4 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

The two original prepared caps can be closed to give the four-block
source. Its critical Fourier point lies outside the previously proved
radius-.005 packet. We therefore prove a directed source extension
before changing the closing contour. All original outside variables,
first normals and both level parities are retained.

## 2. The actual four-vertex critical point

## 3. The asymmetric closing contour and its coefficient

Choose \(e_-=-.006\) and \(e_+=.007\). The complete V formed by
\([e_-,z_c]\cup[z_c,e_+]\) lies in (2). Put
\(d_\pm=e_\pm-z_c\). By (3), the total Hessian defect of
\(U\) is less than \(K\). The fresh correlated checks give
\[
 Q_\pm:=\Re(k_0D d_\pm^2)-K|d_\pm|^2>.00006,
\]
\[
 \pm\Re(k_0D d_\pm)-K|d_\pm|>.09,\qquad
 \frac12\Re(k_0D z_c^2)-\frac12K|z_c|^2>-.0003.
 \tag{14}
\]
The same conservative defect at the old endpoint \(+.005\) is
insufficient, which is why Lemma 1 is necessary.

Along either V segment, (12)--(14) imply
\[
 \Re U(z_c+s d_\pm)\le\Re U(z_c)-\tfrac12s^2Q_\pm,
                 \qquad0\le s\le1.
 \tag{15}
\]
The two rays lie in opposite Gaussian descent sectors. In fact the
real part is strictly decreasing from the saddle along each ray:
its second derivative is bounded above by \(-Q_\pm\).
Therefore each individual \(S\) attains its minimum on the V at
one of the two real endpoints. From (13),
\[
 \Re S(x_h+e_\pm)
 \ge M-.0113|e_\pm|-8e_\pm^2
 >1.9109189>1.9109.
 \tag{16}
\]
This establishes the source hypothesis of Lemma 1 on the exact V.
It is an absolute-to-relative deduction from the original finite
source complements, not differentiation of a pointwise expansion.
Compactness gives a genuine holomorphic neighborhood of the V and
of its outside parameters on which both packets hold.

The exact original cardinal identity and \(A^T=A\) give
\[
 N\int_{x_h+[-1/2,1/2]} F_N(x,w_L)F_N(x,w_R)\,dx
                  =v(w_L)^TA^4v(w_R).
 \tag{17}
\]
Only the subinterval between \(e_-\) and \(e_+\) is deformed
to the V. Its endpoints are fixed, so no extra connector is removed.
The identity (17) is the all-level bilinear cardinal identity used in
[the original larger closing proof](research_three_larger_source_closure.md);
it is not inferred from a finite matrix sample.

**Theorem 1.** The original four-block source has the nonzero asymptotic

Here \(\Xi_4(-2,-2)=U(z_c)\), and the packet is holomorphic near

## 4. Exhaust the original period and restore the outside boundaries

For clarity the complete-color complementary estimates are written
before their outside-color strengthening. The actual action drift is
\[
                    \Re U(z_c)>2M-.0003.
 \tag{19}
\]
At both V endpoints (14) gives the outward derivative signs. The
existing real prepared phase has \(-\Re S''>9.3\) on \([-.02,.02]\).
Thus the principal real annuli from \(e_-\) to \(-.009\), and from
\(e_+\) to \(.009\), decrease away from the saddle contour.
Their endpoint gap exceeds \(.00003\) by (15).

On \(.009\le|t|\le.02\), the two real principal source terms
have combined rate at most
\(2M+.0226|t|-9.3t^2\). Relative to (19) their loss is at least
\[
 9.3(.009)^2-.0226(.009)-.0003=.0002499>.00024.
 \tag{20}
\]
The real expansion has its separate absolute plateau \(1.9107\).
A term containing one such plateau and one principal term has loss
at least
\[
 M-1.9107-.0003-\frac{.0113^2}{18.6}>.00038.
 \tag{21}
\]
The term with two plateaus is still smaller. Finally, outside
\(|t|<.02\), both original caps have whole-period rate at most
\(1.91115\), so the loss is
\[
          2(M-1.91115)-.0003>.00018>.00017.
 \tag{22}
\]
This exhausts the original period and keeps the principal algebraic
errors separate from the absolute plateaus. The marked original
phase calibration gives
\[
        2\pi\Re\Xi_4(-2,-2)=\operatorname{Vol}(M_4),
 \tag{23}
\]
where \(M_4\) is the original marked partial filling with its two
outside ends complete.

**Theorem 2 (full outside-boundary source).** Formula (18) is uniform

                                    +B_4(h_L^2+h_R^2)]},

 \tag{24}

Both level parities and the original full ordinary polynomial are retained.

*Proof.* On all critical neighborhoods in (5), the actual free cap
edge satisfies the established whole-period outside angular loss.
Its hypotheses hold uniformly: \(1.5<\Re\tau<1.55\) and
\(.97<-\Im\tau<1\). On every noncritical cap piece the previous

retained real-index subsets, already bounded in (7). Thus the same
proof as Lemmas 1--2 of
[the outside-boundary theorem](research_three_chain_outside_boundary_decay.md)
provides the complete phase profile minus a fixed outside angular

For small complex ordinary outside parameters, the critical equations
