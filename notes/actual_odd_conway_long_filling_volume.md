# Signed half twists and long fillings of arbitrarily branching Conway factors

29 October 2026. The original marked quantum invariant has its full
hyperbolic-volume limit for every fixed signed half-twist pattern on
C(FG), where both children are robust, after independently sufficiently
long fixed signed integral fillings of any auxiliary subset. Every
such expression has a constructible pattern giving a KNOT when all
auxiliary circles are filled. No fixed diagram width, branching depth,
turn depth, level parity, or prime-level condition is imposed.

This research proof assembles the independently checked full-source
gap, finite complex packet, actual marked representation, and primitive
geometric completion. It is not a theorem for arbitrary hyperbolic
knots. The exact grammar and the excluded minimal case are below.

## 1. The class and theorem

Start with the original retained crossing-circle leaf h and seed A=h^3.
Let F and G be any fixed finite expressions generated by A, literal
quarter-turns, and stacking. Use the actual cylindrical closure C(FG).
At each individual leaf l choose epsilon_l in {0,+1,-1}, replacing
h_j by the actual T(R^epsilon_l L_j). Keep all original auxiliary
circles, marks, and normalization. This includes arbitrary finite
branching, arbitrary turn depth, and independent half-twist signs.

Let Y^epsilon be this augmented-link exterior. Choose any subset I
of auxiliary circles and independently sufficiently large fixed signed
integers p_i. Fill by the original disk-framed full twists R^(-2p_i).
The resulting literal crossing power at a filled leaf is epsilon_i-2p_i.
Unfilled auxiliary colors remain complete; p_i=0 is not used to
represent an unfilled cusp.

Then Y^epsilon(p) is the actual complete hyperbolic filled exterior and

    lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N) log|K_(E,N)^epsilon(p)|
                          =Vol(Y^epsilon(p)).                (1)

Both level parities and composite levels are included. The diagram,
half twists, filling subset, and slopes are fixed before N tends to
infinity. The threshold may depend on the fixed diagram and signs;
it does not require the slopes to have the same sign or comparable
magnitudes. The invariant is the original marked one-cut scalar,
with its explicit nonzero enhancement factor of subexponential modulus.
For the reduced enhanced Kashaev invariant this outer factor has unit
modulus. For the full one-cut modified normalization it has modulus
1/N, once rather than once per auxiliary circle. These facts follow
from the [original normalization comparison](actual_complete_augmented_source_normalization.md)
and the independently derived [Kashaev crossing comparison](kashaev_crossing_and_period_lattice.md).

The minimal C(A) is excluded from this mixed-color filling statement.
Its complete half-twist volume equality is a separate proved result.
No equality for all alternating, arborescent, or hyperbolic knots is
claimed simply from the name of this expression grammar.

## 3. The global peripheral shear

Let Phi_0(t) be the already proved pure critical action, with all
auxiliary colors allowed to vary near completeness. Write

    a_i(t)=partial_i Phi_0(t),
    U_i^0=-2pi i t_i, V_i^0=-2a_i(t),
    G_0(t)=2pi i Phi_0(t).                                  (2)

The exact changed-source/geometry correspondence is

    chi_i=t_i-epsilon_i a_i(t)/(2pi i),
    Phi_epsilon(chi)=Phi_0(t)
                         -sum_i epsilon_i a_i(t)^2/(4pi i). (3)

At zero, Phi_0''=-2pi diag(c_i), c_i=cot(pi x_(i,*))>0.
The Jacobian of t->chi is diag(1-epsilon_i i c_i), hence invertible.
Equation (3) defines a unique holomorphic branch t(chi) near zero.

The local selected-source identity is proved in the complex packet
by its exact Ward quadratic and the zero-action endpoint x=1/2.
Its global extension is elementary. At fixed wire coordinate x the
implicit local relation is chi=t-epsilon*g_t(x,t)/(2pi i), and

    f_epsilon(x,chi)=g(x,t)-epsilon*g_t(x,t)^2/(4pi i).

Differentiation at fixed chi gives f_epsilon,x=g_x; differentiation
at fixed x gives f_epsilon,chi=g_t. Thus the complete network wire
equations are exactly the old pure equations at the hidden colors t.
At their critical point g_t=partial_i Phi_0. This proves (3), including
all interactions between distinct cusps and all turns and products.

The same computation gives

    partial_i Phi_epsilon=a_i(t),
    U_i^epsilon=U_i^0-epsilon_i V_i^0/2=-2pi i chi_i,
    V_i^epsilon=V_i^0=-2 partial_i Phi_epsilon,
    G_epsilon=G_0-sum_i epsilon_i (V_i^0)^2/8,
    dG_epsilon=(1/2)sum_i V_i^epsilon dU_i^epsilon.          (4)

Its packet cusp ratios are

    tau_i^epsilon=i c_i/(1-epsilon_i i c_i),
    Im tau_i^epsilon=c_i/(1+epsilon_i^2 c_i^2)>0.           (5)

For epsilon=-1 this is (1+exp(2pi i x_(i,*)))/2; for
epsilon=+1 it is -(1+exp(-2pi i x_(i,*)))/2. The geometric
primitive cusp ratio is twice the packet ratio, as required by
the original full-twist convention.

Equations (3)--(5) are used together with the actual selected finite
packet and the original odd-link primitive paths. Neither the algebraic
shear alone nor a match of Hessians is used to infer the asymptotic.

## 4. Filling stationarity and an exact action simplification

The general original-source filling theorem uses the unique small
critical point chi_*(p) of

    S_epsilon,p(chi)=Phi_epsilon(chi)
                      +(pi i/2)sum_(i in I)(chi_i-1)^2/p_i. (6)

In the original normalization it proves the stronger asymptotic

    K_(E,N)^epsilon(p)=Xi_N eta_N kappa^(-sum_(i in I)p_i)
        N^(3/2) D_(E,epsilon)(p) exp[N S_epsilon,p(chi_*)]
                                      *[1+O_p(N^-1)],
    eta_N=exp[(i pi (N-1)/4)sum_l epsilon_l],
    D_(E,epsilon)(p)!=0.                                  (6a)

Here Xi_N is the already recorded enhancement/framing factor, not
another copy of a factor inside the raw source. The nonzero D is
the explicit Gaussian-and-sinh prefactor of the general transfer.
Its minimal signed charge sectors add. Every original color, alias,
normal term, endpoint current, and nonminimal charge is controlled
before taking this limit. In particular (6a) is a full-sequence
statement and is not obtained by taking the modulus term by term.

Its saddle equation gives the primitive filling condition

    U_i^epsilon+p_i V_i^epsilon=-2pi i.                   (7)

The regulator anchor is the actual complete volume, and the marked
volume identity is

    Vol(rho_chi)=Im G_epsilon(chi)
                       -(1/2)sum_i Re U_i^epsilon Im V_i^epsilon.

At (7) this equals 2pi Re S_epsilon,p(chi_*). The
[general geometric completion proof](general_source_regulator_hyperbolic_equality.md)
applies to the actual nearby bounded framed representation, with all
unfilled cusps parabolic and the primitive small logs in (7). It
constructs the smooth complete hyperbolic metric on the intended
filled topology. Combining its volume with (6a) proves (1).

There is also a useful exact simplification. At the saddle put
P_i=p_i-epsilon_i/2 and substitute (3). Then

    a_i(t)+(pi i/P_i)(t_i-1)=0,
    S_epsilon,p(chi_*)=Phi_0(t_*)
                          +(pi i/2)sum_(i in I)(t_i-1)^2/P_i. (8)

For an unfilled cusp, chi_i=0 implies t_i=0 on the local branch:
separate evenness factors a_i=t_i Q_i(t), and its coefficient remains
nonzero near completeness. Thus it contributes no extra term in (8).

Equation (8) is an identity of selected actions, not an assertion
that the odd link is the pure link with a rational filling. At an
odd leaf multiplying U_i^0+(p_i-epsilon_i/2)V_i^0=-2pi i by two
produces the primitive pure-cusp slope (2,2p_i-epsilon_i) with total
logarithmic angle -4pi i. Ordinary smooth pure-link Dehn filling
therefore cannot replace the marked odd-link completion in (7).

## 5. The constructive knot subfamily

The [literal connectivity theorem](actual_conway_half_twist_partner_geometry.md#5-literal-main-connectivity)
gives a linear-time parity assignment for EVERY robust expression.
The allowed endpoint matchings are I,U,X; a seed realizes U or X
without internal circles, a turn swaps I and U, and stacking has
only one circle-creating pair, U times U. Every subtree retains at
least two feasible matching types, so the root can be chosen U or X.
Its cylindrical closure has one main component.

Either sign can be used at every odd leaf. Full-twist fillings preserve
the resulting endpoint permutation. Filling all auxiliary circles
therefore gives knots for arbitrary fixed branching and turn depth.
Equation (1) applies to this entire constructible knot
subfamily. No fixed diagram width or turn depth is imposed; a statement
about the knots' intrinsic minimal bridge numbers is not needed here.

## 6. A-structure scope

The relevant marked branch is algebraic. With E=exp(2pi i x),
T=exp(2pi i t), H=exp(2pi i chi), and Q=(E-T)/(ET-1), the local
equations following from (3)--(4) are

    H=T Q^(-epsilon), M=H^(-1), L=Q^(-2),
    kappa=-(1-E/T)(1-ET)/(1+E)^2.                        (9)

Together with the actual product and turn equations these describe
the selected auxiliary peripheral branch. All multipliers here are
projective similarity multipliers (squared SL eigenvalues), with the
original centered logarithms retained. The pole divisors are excluded
and the complete oriented branch is specified.

The calibrated primitive G_epsilon and the source-selected nonzero
period carry information beyond the elimination ideal. No whole
classical knot A-polynomial, generic-main-meridian quantum operator,
or automatic dominant-period selection is inferred from elimination.
Those general extensions remain part of the larger active objective.

The half-integral notation has a simple algebraic explanation. Over
the peripheral torus with coordinates M,L, retain ell with ell^2=L.
This finite etale double cover is a mu_2 torsor in characteristic zero.
On it the change is the INTEGRAL monomial map

    (M^0,ell) -> (M^0 ell^(-epsilon),ell).              (10)

The source branch supplies ell=Q^(-1)=exp(V/2), continued from ell=1.
Thus the internal algebraic data trivialize the pulled-back torsor
locally. With several cusps the cover has group (mu_2)^b. Forgetting
this marked square root loses the information needed to distinguish
the physical odd meridians. It does not justify a half-integral change
of primitive basis on the same cusp torus.

On the logarithmic cover, the primitive changes by
G_epsilon=G_0-sum epsilon_i V_i^2/8, as in (4). This is an explicit
algebraic correspondence together with its calibrated action change.
Neither the elementary torsor nor its trivialization alone proves
the asymptotic: selection and survival of the period are supplied by
the finite-source packet and the whole-color bound.
