# The complete ordinary three/four source on its left taper

Research theorem, 7 November 2026. The entire original product
\(U_{0,N}V_{1,N}\) is exponentially smaller than its ordinary
geometric action on the left taper \(.1\le x\le.22\).
The proof retains the original transform cuts, all two-index
currents, every physical complement and the whole Newton source.
It uses only the proved analytic reductions and the previously
completed source comparisons. No numerical evaluation is used.

## 1. Original observation, reference and quantified conclusion

Use the exact ordinary source in
[the pair target](research_mixed_three_four_ordinary_pair_target.md):
\[
 Z_{34,N}(w_L,w_R)=\kappa_N^3N^2
 \oint U_{0,N}(w_L,X)V_{1,N}(w_R,X)\frac{dX}{2\pi iX},
 \qquad |\kappa_N|=1.                                    \tag{1}
\]
No retained diagonal weight or external Fourier alternative is
present. The complete terminal factor is exactly
\[
 V_{1,N}(w,X)=\sum_{i,j=0}^{N-1}
       \frac{X^{-i}p_i(X)}{P_i^2}
       \frac{p_j(r^i)H_N(w,r^{-j})}{P_j^2}.              \tag{2}
\]
Every Newton term stays inside \(H_N\). The product
\(p_j(r^i)\) retains the zero wall \(i+j\ge N\).

Let \(a_*=A+ic\) be the actual ordinary \((3,4)\) shared
critical leg, with
\[
 .278<A<.391,\qquad -.072<c<.021,
 \qquad M=\Re\{T_0(a_*)+P_1(a_*)\}.                   \tag{3}
\]
These are the original action, marginal and additive normalization.
Their shared equation is \(T_0'(a_*)+P_1'(a_*)=0\).
On the left taper set
\[
 a(x)=x+i c\frac{x-.1}{.12},\quad X(x)=e^{-2\pi ia(x)},
 \quad .1\le x\le.22,\qquad
 \kappa_*=\frac1{2500000},\qquad
 \gamma_*:=\frac1{160000}.                              \tag{4}
\]

**Theorem 1.** On sufficiently small fixed ordinary-color disks
about \((-2,-2)\), every fixed pair of ordinary derivative orders
has constants \(C,B\) such that
\[
 \left|\partial_{w_L}^{k}\partial_{w_R}^{\ell}
 \{U_{0,N}(w_L,X(x))V_{1,N}(w_R,X(x))\}\right|
 \le C N^B e^{N(M-\gamma_*)},\qquad .1\le x\le.22.      \tag{5}
\]
Both level parities are included. Consequently this exact segment
of the original contour (1), with its original scalar and Jacobian,
has the same exponentially smaller rate and fixed ordinary-jet
bounds. The theorem concerns this complete contour sector; it does
not identify a partial root sum with an arc.

## 3. The main current retains an explicit geometric gap

Keep the original actual terminal main heights
\(\Im t=h_t\in(-.108,-.020)\) and \(\Im b=h_b\in(-.051,-.019)\).
The [original fixed-cut theorem](research_mixed_three_four_left_original_transform_cut.md)

\(1/100\) for every regular first-cap profile and every
\(.15\le\Re t\le.5\) at this nonpositive transform height.
The same coupled expression with the cap true-end alternative
is nondecreasing. The complete right endpoint action and its
private allocations are independent of the incoming motion.
Integrating each fixed profile before maximizing therefore bounds
the entire actual-height main current at any \(x\le.22\) by
its corresponding value at \(x=.22\).

At that endpoint \(a=.22+ic\), the individual first-cap profiles
have the original supporting plane \(B_U(.22)\). The true-end
ceiling lies below that plane by the explicit comparison (9) of
[the moving-boundary theorem](research_mixed_three_four_left_moving_boundary.md).
The terminal main retains the stronger original horizontal loss
\(\pi/200\) in all three displacements, by Lemma 1 and
equations (9)--(11) of the reduced-current theorem. The segment
from its actual reference to any main-current point at \(x=.22\)
stays in that old convex product: incoming real parts are between
\(.22\) and \(A<.391\), and both internal real coordinates
stay in their original intervals, at their actual fixed heights.
The endpoint Schur domain is precisely the one already proved
there. No new incoming Hessian bound is used.
The actual-reference eligibility is the completed one in
[the full plateau theorem](research_mixed_full_unselected_plateau.md).

There is no retained halfweight in this word. Adding this stronger
main bound to \(B_U(.22)\), original stationarity cancels the

\[
 \text{coupled main rate}
 \le M-(\pi/200+\kappa_*)(A-.22)^2
 <M-\frac{2523}{50000000}<M-\frac1{20000}.               \tag{9}
\]
Here \(A-.22>.058=29/500\), \(\pi>3\), and
\((3/200)(29/500)^2=2523/50000000>1/20000=8\gamma_*\).
This is the main-current gap throughout the left taper. It uses
the original coherent geometric action, not the sum of independent
absolute maxima.

## 6. Finite bound, ordinary jets and scope

For the central currents use their exact interpolants for contour
deformation, followed by the uniform source upper bounds.
Choose the sum of all phase and color tolerances smaller than
\(\gamma_*/2\). The central raw gaps are at least
\(8\gamma_*\), while every already finite physical-sector gap
in Section 5 is at least \(2\gamma_*\). Thus all pieces retain
more than \(\gamma_*\). The finite number of currents, physical row counts,
residue factors and finite symbols contribute only polynomial
prefactors. This proves (5) at complete colors and on sufficiently
small fixed ordinary-color disks. Cauchy's formula on smaller
disks proves every fixed mixed ordinary derivative with the same
rate and a possibly larger polynomial factor.

Finally \(a'(x)=1+i c/.12\) is uniformly bounded on this
segment. Its bounded length and the exact external
\(\kappa_N^3N^2\) in (1) only change the polynomial prefactor.
The complete original left-taper contour sector is therefore
exponentially smaller by the stated quantified gap.

No real-color-period extension is asserted in Theorem 1: that
requires the corresponding whole-coefficient bounds and the
support-preserving column deletion explicitly. The far incoming
half-circle, the remaining contour transitions and an arbitrary
ordinary closing are outside this theorem. It establishes a
complete discarded sector of the literal ordinary \((3,4)\)
source, not a new knot-family conclusion.
