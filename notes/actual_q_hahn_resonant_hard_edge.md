# The original q-Hahn source at a resonant framing boundary

30 October 2026. The actual prefix words
give an explicit degeneration of the original white arguments when the
residual framing parameter tends to infinity. One of its large-clock
regimes has a uniform, source-normalized Bessel limit. The numerator is
the actual LARGE generic-channel numerator specialized at the root;
neither a scalar symbol nor an off-lattice channel is substituted.

This is a local root-value boundary model. Genuine quantum normals are
separate. It neither puts every box in this regime nor computes the
coefficient of the geometric component in the original global period.

## 1. Actual prefix words determine the escaping heads

Normalize the reference positive meridian to J=[[1,1],[0,1]], and keep
the original common framing F(r,s)=[[r,1-r],[s,-s]], with s nonzero.
For every actual based prefix Q=[[a,b],[c,d]] put

    L_Q(r)=Tr(Q F E11 F^-1)
          =-c r^2/s+(a-d+c/s)r+d+b s.                 (1)

These are the actual prefix diagonals. On a fixed horizontal cut the
physical clock of its i-th wire is X_i=-L_(Q_(i-1))/L_(Q_i).
No independent local frame is chosen.

For the complete discrete faithful holonomy, each prefix is either
genuinely quadratic in (1), or constant. Indeed c=0 implies a=d=+/-1.
To see this directly, conjugating J by Q supplies translations a^(2n).
If |a^2| differs from one these accumulate at the identity. If |a^2|=1,
the discrete additive group generated by these translations has rank
at most two; rotations preserving it form a finite group. Thus a^2
is a root of unity. If a^2 is not one, the upper triangular Q has
finite projective order, contrary to the free deck action. Consequently
Q=+/-[[1,b0],[0,1]], whose L_Q is constant. This argument uses actual
holonomy words, not arbitrary matrices assigned to ports.

Fix s so all the finitely many constant L_Q are nonzero. Let e(Q)=0
in this constant case and e(Q)=1 in the quadratic case. At r=N rho,
with rho in a fixed compact subset of C*,

    X_i=C_i(s) r^k_i (1+O(1/r)),
    k_i=2(e(Q_(i-1))-e(Q_i)) in {-2,0,2}.              (2)

For two quadratic prefixes the limiting ratio is the ratio of their
lower-left entries, with the displayed minus sign. These are actual
geometric limiting data. They are not independently optimized.
The k_i obey the homogeneous original cup/cap/coupon incidence
equations, since the exact products of the corresponding clocks do.
Thus the logarithmic escape is one coupled integral flow. On a cut,
each run of quadratic prefixes has paired -2/+2 boundary transitions.

The exact finite affine offsets have a uniform convenient realization.
If X_i=exp(2pi i x_i) with the recorded logarithmic lift, set

    i_i=N x_i-1/2,       Q_N^(i_i)=q_N^-1 X_i.         (3)

This prescription applies to the PHYSICAL V_d heads. It gives every
physical cup/cap i_1+i_2=N-1 and q_N^(d-2i_i)=-1/X_i. At a white
vertex define the channel row by its original equation
r0=i_1+i_2-(d-a), with the actual discrete a retained. An auxiliary
V_(2a) head is not assigned the same universal -1/2 offset. Its finite
color is equivalently (a+1/2)/N. Thus degree constraints remain exact,
but this is not evaluation of every finite channel at the complete
parabolic stationary point. It samples the principal geometric germ
without asserting finite-N stationary equations. For k_i!=0,
the imaginary index has size N log N. For X_i=1+zeta/r+O(r^-2),
instead,

    i_i=zeta/(2pi i rho)-1/2+O(N^-1),                 (4)

up to its retained integer logarithmic lift. The -1/2 is essential.
A point near a negative half-integer is not an original integer support
zero. An O(1/N) perturbation of a clock near one must not be discarded.

The same caution applies to characters:

    exp(2pi i i_i)=-X_i^N.                           (5)

Its magnitude need not be one, and can be N^(2N) in (2).

## 2. The literal white, dual and exceptional arguments

At a circle write a0=L_W, a1=L_(AW), a1'=L_(Aout W), a2=L_(PW),
where P=BA=Bout Aout and W is its original prefix. Its incoming
geometric clocks are

    U=-a0/a1, V=-a1/a2, T=UV=a0/a2,                  (6)

and the output replaces a1 by a1'. By the actual generic white formula,
the numerator is H_m^B(q_N^-1 U,q_N^-1 V), with m=d-a, B=Q^-N.
The channel a remains the actual discrete integer.

For prefix degrees (deg a0,deg a1,deg a2), the root arguments have
the following orders; the factors q_N^-1 in (3) remain in each entry.

| Degrees | U | V | T |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 000 | finite | finite | finite |
| 002 | finite | r^-2 | r^-2 |
| 020 | r^-2 | r^2 | finite |
| 022 | r^-2 | finite | r^-2 |
| 200 | r^2 | finite | r^2 |
| 202 | r^2 | r^-2 | finite |
| 220 | finite | r^2 | r^2 |
| 222 | finite | finite | finite |

Here "finite" means a nonzero limit, which can still meet a further
chart divisor, including one. For the original complete-circle boxes,
000 and 020 are impossible: a0 and a2 constant would make P preserve
the reference main cusp, although P is a nontrivial longitude of a
different, auxiliary cusp. Distinct cusp lifts have disjoint small
horoballs and cannot have the same center. For routed crossing bands
one must retain all eight rows unless that extra hypothesis is proved.

There is no implicit Hermitian adjoint. The literal pi dual uses the
reversed complementary indices (d-j,d-i). The exact Chevalley identity

    W_a(d-j,d-i;2a-r0)
                 =zeta^[(d-2a)(a-r0)] W_a(i,j;r0)    (7)

reduces it to the incoming white with its actual scalar retained.
Alternatively its two raw H arguments are the reversed reciprocals
Q^d/Q^j,Q^d/Q^i. All routing spectra and their paired partners remain.

For the exceptional inverse turn retain its actual coordinates

    h=v-u, ell=t-v, sigma=d-h, r0=a-h.

Its two numerator pairs are exactly

    (Q^(sigma-u),Q^u), (Q^ell,Q^(sigma-ell)).          (8)

With physical half-index sampling these are the original outgoing
and incoming pairs in (6), respectively. The moving factorial arguments
are r0,2a-r0 in the channel cup and v,d-v,ell,d-ell in the inverse
physical cups; ordinary pi has the analogous original complemented
indices. This specifies their whole marked logarithmic lifts.

The specified generic exceptional lift has root character
exp(-2pi i v). Restoring the original physical turn contributes the
inverse exp(2pi i v), exactly as in the
[source lift](actual_matching_edge_generic_deficient_operator_lift.md),
equation (13). They cancel BEFORE an estimate. Bounding them separately
at (2) could introduce artificial N log N growth. None of this proves
uniform asymptotics for the listed moving factorials or numerators.

## 3. A uniform original-root packet when the first clock is large

Put Q=exp(2pi i/N), and keep an actual integer m with

    delta <= mu=m/N <= 1-delta, delta>0.

At the root the exact polynomial is

    H_m^1(U,V)=sum_(k=0)^m c_k V^k
          product_(j<k)(U-Q^j) product_(j<m-k)(V-Q^j),
    c_k=(-1)^k Q^[k(k+1)/2] [m choose k]_Q^2.         (9)

This includes the actual large m=d-a. Suppose

    U=N^2 kappa_N, V=V_N,

where kappa_N and V_N range in fixed compact subsets of C*. Keep the
EXACT last Newton term

    E_N=c_m (UV)^m product_(j<m)(1-Q^j/U),
    c_m=(-1)^m Q^[m(m+1)/2].                         (10)

It is nonzero for large N. Define the entire function

    Bessel(K)=sum_(l>=0) (-K)^l/(l!)^2
              =J_0(2sqrt(K)),
    K_N=sin^2(pi mu)(V_N-1)/(pi^2 kappa_N V_N).       (11)

The series, not a square-root branch, defines the notation. Uniformly
on those compacts,

    H_m^1(U,V)/E_N=Bessel(K_N)+O(N^-1).              (12)

In particular U=N^2 kappa(1+O(N^-1)), V=V0+O(N^-1) gives the same
formula with K=sin^2(pi mu)(V0-1)/(pi^2 kappa V0). The finite m/N,
the phase c_m, and the product in (10) have not been discarded.
Equation (12) is an absolute normalized estimate. A relative packet
is asserted only on a compact set where Bessel(K) is nonzero.

Here is a proof retaining the WHOLE finite polynomial. Set k=m-l.
The exact ratio of that summand to (10) is

    (-1)^l Q^[-ml+l(l-1)/2] [m choose l]_Q^2
      * (UV)^(-l) product_(j<l)(V-Q^j)
      /product_(j=m-l)^(m-1)(1-Q^j/U).                (13)

For j<=m<=(1-delta)N, |1-Q^j|>=c_delta j/N. Consequently
|[m choose l]_Q|<=(C_delta N)^l/l! for EVERY l<=m.
The denominator in (13) has inverse bounded independently of N,l,
since |U|>=cN^2. Thus every term is bounded by C^l/(l!)^2.
This proves uniform summable tail domination, not just convergence
of the fixed-l terms.

For fixed l, expand the exact Gaussian products. The identity

    exp(-2pi i mu)(1-exp(2pi i mu))^2/(-2pi i)^2
                                      =sin^2(pi mu)/pi^2

gives precisely (-K_N)^l/(l!)^2. Uniformly for l<=N^(1/4), the
product expansion has absolute error at most
C^l(1+l)^C/[N(l!)^2]. This follows by expanding each of the l
numerator and denominator factors; their relative errors are O(l/N),
while product_(j<l)(V-Q^j) is compared in absolute value if V=1.
The factorial bound makes the remaining tail smaller than every
power of N. Summing proves (12).

## 4. A geometric transition with an explicit nonzero model

Consider a 200 box whose second raw puncture is the reference B=-J.
Then Q1=AW=epsilon[[1,b],[0,1]], Q2=BQ1, epsilon=+/-1. Write
A21=c_A. The actual complete disk relation Tr(BA)=-2 and Tr A=-2
give c_A=4. Since W=A^-1Q1, its lower-left entry is -epsilon c_A.
Substituting (1),(6), at r=N rho gives

    kappa=Q0_21 rho^2/[s L_Q1],
    V0=-L_Q1/L_Q2,
    K=sin^2(pi mu) s^2/(4pi^2 rho^2).                (14)

The q_N^-1 argument corrections are included in the O(N^-1) allowed
in (12). They were not dropped in (4), where their size is decisive.
The inverse orientation B=-J^-1 gives the same squared expression.

In the actual first-slot Fourier convention eta_j=Q^j and p=eta_j^-1,
the resonant scale for each fixed nonzero integer j is

    r=1/(1-exp(-2pi i j/N))-beta s,
    rho -> 1/(2pi i j).

Hence this local boundary factor tends

    Bessel(-j^2 s^2 sin^2(pi mu))
                       =I_0(2j s sin(pi mu)).          (15)

For Re s!=0 it is nonzero. An elementary proof avoids a zero theorem:
if I_0(z)=0, f(t)=I_0(zt) obeys (t f')'=z^2 t f, f(0)=1,
f'(0)=0,f(1)=0. Integration against conjugate f gives
z^2 integral_0^1 t|f|^2=-integral_0^1 t|f'|^2<0.
Thus z is purely imaginary. The ordinary regular framing exclusions
remove finitely many s values; Re s!=0 can also be imposed wherever
that first-cut frame is legitimately varied. In particular on the
legitimate real interior first-cut slice A_cut=exp(2pi i alpha),
0<alpha<1, one has s=exp(-2pi i alpha)-1 and
Re s=cos(2pi alpha)-1<0. The local nonvanishing condition is therefore
automatic on that slice, after its other regular exclusions.
This is not a proof that
an arbitrary selected global frame path reaches every required box
in the 200 regime.

The j=0 node is the exact resonance r=infinity, not an additional
finite rho value. No uniform result in growing j or across all eight
regimes is claimed. In particular 202 leaves a nontrivial whole
boundary polynomial in T; it does not reduce to the last term in (10).

## 5. The paired small-second-clock endpoint

The 002 regime has an equally direct original-source proof; it does
not use an unrecorded interchange of the two physical legs. Let

    V=kappa_N/N^2, U=U_N,
    E_N^0=product_(j<m)(V-Q^j),
    K_N^0=kappa_N sin^2(pi mu)(1-U_N)/pi^2,             (16)

where kappa_N,U_N lie in fixed compact subsets of C*. Then

    H_m^1(U,V)/E_N^0=Bessel(K_N^0)+O(N^-1).            (17)

The EXACT ratio of its kth Newton term to E_N^0 is

    c_k V^k product_(j<k)(U-Q^j)
                 /product_(j=m-k)^(m-1)(V-Q^j).        (18)

The denominator product differs from
(-1)^k Q^[mk-k(k+1)/2] by a relative O(k/N^2), uniformly for
k<=m. Together with the same Gaussian bound as before, this gives
C^k/(k!)^2 domination for EVERY k. Its fixed-k limit is
[kappa_N sin^2(pi mu)(U_N-1)/pi^2]^k/(k!)^2. The product-error
argument in section 3, including absolute comparison when U_N=1,
proves the uniform O(N^-1) remainder. Both the exact phase and
the full endpoint product are retained in (16),(18).

For a genuine 002 disk with first puncture A=-J write
Q0=epsilon[[1,b],[0,1]], Q1=AQ0 and Q2=BQ1.
The complete trace relation gives B21=4. Substitution into (1),(6)
now gives

    U0=(1+b s)/(1+(b+1)s),
    kappa=s(1+(b+1)s)/(4rho^2),
    K^0=sin^2(pi mu)s^2/(4pi^2rho^2).                 (19)

Thus the same fixed-Fourier I0 factor and its legitimate real
first-cut nonvanishing apply. The exact physical argument shifts
q_N^-1 change U0,kappa by the permitted O(N^-1); the actual
endpoint product is still kept. This proves paired 200/002 models,
not the unproved reduction of 022/220 or 202 to either one.

## 6. The mixed 202 boundary retains a whole positive polynomial

Put V=T/U and define the exact boundary polynomial

    A_b(T)=(-1)^b Q^[b(b-1)/2] P_b(QT),
    P_b(w)=sum_(k=0)^b beta(b,k)^2 w^k,
    beta(b,k)=s_b/[s_k s_(b-k)].                        (20)

Termwise in (9), A_b is the limit as U tends to infinity with T fixed.
For the actual small channel a<=floor((N-1)/2), P_a is precisely
the two-binomial polynomial Q_a^(a,a) in the
[proved negative-root theorem](actual_q_hahn_negative_root_source_polynomial.md).
Its roots are negative real and simple. Retain this whole polynomial;
it is not a replacement by its largest coefficient.

If U=N^2 kappa with kappa in a fixed compact subset of C*, and T
in a fixed compact subset of C minus (-infinity,0], then

    H_a^1(U,T/U)=A_a(T)(1+O(N^-1)),                     (21)

uniformly in the actual discrete a. There is no lower bound on the
distance of a/N from zero needed for this statement. For the ACTUAL
large channel m=d-a, the exact root partner identity gives

    H_m^1(U,T/U)=A_a(T)
          product_(r=a)^(m-1)(T-Q^r)(1+O(N^-1)).         (22)

The forced unit-arc factors in (22) are displayed, including their
zeros. At such a zero both sides are zero exactly. No generic normal
is inferred by differentiating this root collision identity.

Here is a uniform relative proof, including complex cancellation.
After taking out its exact phase, H_a is

    sum_(k=0)^a beta(a,k)^2(QT)^k
       product_(r<k)(1-Q^r/U)
       product_(r<a-k)(1-(T/U)Q^-r).                    (23)

Expand BOTH correction products by the finite quantum binomial
identity, proved by multiplying their linear factors. The term of
degree i in the first is a polynomial in Q^k proportional to

    U^-i product_(r<i)(1-Q^(k-r))/(Q;Q)_i;

the degree-j term in the second is proportional to

    (T/U)^j product_(r<j)(1-Q^(-a+k+r))
                                      /(Q^-1;Q^-1)_j.  (24)

The omitted factors in (24) have modulus one. These identities hold
also when i>k or j>a-k: the corresponding numerator contains zero.
Therefore expanding (24) gives an EXACT finite linear combination
of A_a(Q^hT), with 0<=h<=i+j. Since i,j<=a<N/2, the sum of absolute
coefficients of this combination is at most

    (C/N)^(i+j)/(i!j!).                               (25)

Indeed |(Q;Q)_i|>=(c/N)^i i!, and each numerator polynomial in
Q^k has coefficient sum at most 2^i; the second factor is identical.
The i=j=0 term is exactly A_a(T).

Write P_a(w)=C_a product_l(w+rho_l), rho_l>0. On the indicated
compact T domain, w=QT remains a fixed distance in angle from the
negative ray for large N. For 0<=h<=cN, choose c small enough so
the rotation from QT to Q^(h+1)T remains in this domain. The bound

    |w P_a'(w)/P_a(w)|<=C a

then gives |A_a(Q^hT)/A_a(T)|<=exp(C h). For EVERY other h one
still has the crude bound exp(CN), by applying
(|w|+rho)/|w+rho|<=C to each root. Sum (25) first over
1<=i+j<=cN; the exp(C h) factor leaves O(N^-1). For i+j>cN,
the factorial tail in (25), even multiplied by exp(CN), is smaller
than every inverse power of N. This proves (21) without replacing
a complex sum by its positive envelope. The original root identity
H_m=H_a product_(r=a)^(m-1)(T-Q^r) proves (22).

    A=-[[1,0],[2,1]], B=-[[3,-2],[2,-1]],
