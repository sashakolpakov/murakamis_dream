# A complete lower-source bound on the following-image corridor

Research deduction, 8 November 2026. The complete prefix already has
a source theorem on its actual following image. On every connected
horizontal part of that image, its allocated action has a uniform
strict concavity bound. Combining this with the complete ordinary
conditional supporting plane pays the portion outside the local
geometric segment. The conditional packet is not continued to
independent target arguments.

The interval below is specified by the proved geometric image.
No numerical width is asserted, and it is not the full independent
plateau. All sources and the old filter remain unchanged.

## 1. A source domain, rather than an unspecified packet neighborhood

Let \(q=q_{34}\) be the original univalent following map in
[the following-curve theorem](research_mixed_three_four_following_critical_curve.md),
and put
\[
 K=O_3\cup O_4,\qquad
 \mathcal V=q(\operatorname{int}K).
 \tag{1}
\]
The interior here is the complex-plane interior. The compact set
\(K\) lies strictly inside the original following domain \(\Omega\).
The map \(q\) is holomorphic and injective on a neighborhood of \(K\).
In particular \(\mathcal V\) is open and
\(\overline{\mathcal V}\subset q(K)\).

Fix an ordinary-next reference of type \(3+\beta\), as in
[the local next-source theorem, Section 2](research_mixed_three_four_next_local_source.md):
\[
 y_*=q(s_*),\qquad (s_*,z_*)=F_{3+\beta}(y_*,b),\qquad
 \beta\in\{0,1\}.
 \tag{2}
\]
The external \(b\) retains its actual attached or ordinary role:
\(b\in A_{3+\nu}\) or \(O_{3+\nu}\), with the sufficient larger
typed charts of the three-row source theorem also allowed.
These domains are contained in the corresponding reduced disk;
no independent full reduced-disk input is promoted to that
supporting-plane scope. The whole-map image bounds place
\(s_*\) strictly inside \(O_{3+\beta}\),
also when \(b\) is on its permitted boundary. Hence \(y_*\in\mathcal V\).
Write \(x_*=\Re y_*\) and \(h_*=\Im y_*\).

Let \((\ell_*,r_*)\) be the connected component containing \(x_*\) of
\[
                 \{x\in\mathbb R:x+ih_*\in\mathcal V\},
\]
and define the closed source segment
\[
                   I_*=[\ell_*,r_*]+ih_* .
 \tag{3}
\]
Both endpoints belong to \(q(K)\). The full-image guards imply
\[
              I_*\subset q(K)\subset
                   \{.24<\Re y<.36,\ -.102<\Im y<-.001\}.
 \tag{4}
\]
In particular \(I_*\) lies inside the original lower plateau
\([.22,.45]+ih_*\), but it is not asserted to exhaust it.

There is a uniform positive distance from \(x_*\) to both endpoints
of (3) over the compact ordinary-next families. Indeed the actual
\(s_*\)-images are compact subsets of \(\operatorname{int}K\),
by the strict whole-map bounds and continuity of the original
half-chain. Their \(q\)-images are compact subsets of the open set
\(\mathcal V\). Their distance from its complement is positive.
This gives an existence statement for a common interval radius,
not a numerical enclosure of \(\ell_*,r_*\).

The complete following-prefix theorem holds on a fixed neighborhood
of \(\{(-2,y):y\in q(K)\}\). It therefore applies on all of (3),
including its endpoints. It is this already proved source domain,
not an arbitrary enlargement of a local germ at \(y_*\), that is
used below. When outside parameters vary, (3) remains fixed at its
base reference.

## 2. Uniform concavity of the allocated prefix action

Use the original four internal coordinates \((u,e,a,p)\) of the
\(34\) prefix. With \(y\) retained, its unreduced action is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal A(u,e,a,p;y)
  ={}&E(e,u)+g(e+a)+g(a+p)+g(p+y)\\
     &-2g(a)-3g(p)-g(y)+2\pi iap,\\
 E(e,u)
  ={}&-2J(1/2-u)-3J(u)-J(e-u)
                +i\pi eu-\frac{i\pi}{2}(e-e^2).
 \end{split}
 \tag{5}
\]
Its internal stationary value is exactly \(\mathcal P_{34}(-2,y)\)
on \(q(K)\), with the absolute constant of the following theorem.
There is no final \(G(y,c)\) in (5).

The Hermitian real Hessian of (5) equals that of
\[
 B(e,u)+W(e,a)+V(a,p)+V(p,y)-J(p),
 \tag{6}
\]
where
\[
 \begin{split}
 B(e,u)&=-2J(1/2-u)-3J(u)-J(e-u),\\
 W(e,a)&=J(e+a)-J(a),\\
 V(v,w)&=J(v+w)-J(v)-J(w).
 \end{split}
\]
The difference between (5) and (6) is a polynomial with purely
imaginary coefficients and real quadratic coefficients after
division by \(i\). Thus it contributes zero to this Hermitian
real Hessian.

On the original product \(U\times E\times Q^3\), the estimates in
[the open-word Hessian theorem, Sections 2--3](research_mixed_open_word_hessian.md)
give
\[
 -\Re B''\succ\mu I_2,\quad
 -\Re W''\succeq(\pi/8)\,da^2,\quad
 -\Re V''\succ(\pi/8)I_2,\qquad \mu=\frac{6\pi}{49}.
 \tag{7}
\]
All coordinates of the prefix sheet over \(q(K)\) lie in this
product. The term \(-J(p)\) adds a nonnegative negative real
Hessian. Consequently
\[
                    -\Re\mathcal A''\succ\mu I_5,
 \tag{8}
\]
since \(a\) and \(p\) each receive two pair contributions, and
even the final \(y\) receives \(\pi/8>\mu\).
This verifies the boundary multiplicity after removing the last
Gram incidence; it is not a principal-block claim about a
different closed action.

Set
\[
                     L(y)=\mathcal P_{34}(-2,y)-\frac12g(y).
 \tag{9}
\]
The additional term \(-g(y)/2\) has positive negative real
Hessian because \(\Re\cot\pi y>0\) on \(Q\). It does not change
the four internal critical equations. Eliminating those four
variables in (8) gives
\[
                              -\Re L''(y)>\mu
                              \qquad(y\in q(K)).
 \tag{10}
\]
For precision, this is the complex Schur complement: if \(H\)
is the full negative Hessian, test its Hermitian form on
\((-H_{II}^{-1}H_{Iy},1)\). The resulting value is the scalar
Schur form, with real part greater than \(\mu\).
No Schur complement of entrywise real parts is substituted.

Integrate (10) along the segment (3), whose entire interior stays
in the stated source domain. With \(d=x-x_*\),
\[
 \Re L(x+ih_*)\le
       \Re L(y_*)+\Re L'(y_*)d-\frac{\mu}{2}d^2
                 \qquad(\ell_*\le x\le r_*).
 \tag{11}
\]
The same assertion at its endpoints follows by continuity.

## 3. The full conditional source supplies the other tangent

Let
\[
 \begin{split}
 Q_\beta(s;y,z)&=g(s+y)+g(s+z)-(2+\beta)g(s)
                         -g(y)-g(z)+2\pi iys,\\
 \psi_\beta(y,z)&=\operatorname{stat}_s Q_\beta(s;y,z),\\
 \phi_\beta(y,z)&=\psi_\beta(y,z)-\frac12g(y)-\frac{\beta}{2}g(z).
 \end{split}
 \tag{12}
\]
Only the value and derivatives at the actual reference \((y_*,z_*)\)
are used in its supporting plane. The complete ordinary source
theorem, [three-row assembly, Theorem 1](research_mixed_three_row_source_assembly.md),
gives, for every \(\tau>0\) and every \(x\in[.22,.45]\),
\[
 \begin{split}
 &|C_{\beta,N}(e^{-2\pi i(x+ih_*)},e^{-2\pi iz_*})|\,
 |d_N(e^{-2\pi i(x+ih_*)})|^{1/2}
 |d_N(e^{-2\pi iz_*})|^{\beta/2}\\
 &\quad\le C_\tau N^B
 \exp N\{\Re\phi_\beta(y_*,z_*)
             +\Re\phi_{\beta,y}(y_*,z_*)d-\kappa d^2+\tau\},
                    \qquad \kappa=\frac1{2500000}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{13}
\]
It includes every conditional row, both physical ends and all
alias and joining currents. In particular (13) does not require
a moving conditional source packet at \((x+ih_*,z_*)\).
Its incoming type is four, its own type is \(3+\beta\), its
other external input is the typed \(b\) just specified, and the
outgoing target in (13) is the same actual \(z_*\), at its
unchanged reference height. All weights in (13) are unselected.

The complete prefix and actual Fourier-weight packets on \(q(K)\)
bound
\[
 |F_{34,N}(-2,e^{-2\pi iy})|\,
           |d_N(e^{-2\pi iy})|^{1/2}
                 \le C N^B e^{N\Re L(y)} .
 \tag{14}
\]
For every real ordinary color, the all-real part of the prefix
theorem gives the same complete-color upper rate in (14).
The stronger angular loss may be retained before filtering, but
is not needed here.

The older filtered prefix satisfies (14) as well. Indeed its
complete correction has unit-disk rate \(77/50\), by
[the filtered selected-plateau theorem, equation (12)](research_mixed_next_filtered_selected_plateau.md).
On \(q(K)\), the [following remainder theorem, equation (8)](research_mixed_three_four_following_remainder_gaps.md)
gives \(\Re\mathcal P_{34}(-2,y)>7/4\), and \(|e^{-2\pi iy}|<1\).
The correction is therefore uniformly exponentially below the
right side of the prefix bound, since \(7/4-77/50=21/100\).
This argument holds for all real colors and on smaller fixed
complex complete-color neighborhoods. It retains the exact old
filter, including its overlap correction.

Stationarity of the actual next action gives exactly
\[
 \begin{split}
 L'(y_*)+\phi_{\beta,y}(y_*,z_*)&=0,\\
 L(y_*)+\phi_\beta(y_*,z_*)&=
             \mathcal P_\beta^+(-2,z_*)-\frac{\beta}{2}g(z_*).
 \end{split}
 \tag{15}
\]
The output \(d_N(e^{-2\pi iz_*})\), when \(\beta=1\), has its
proved nonzero factorial packet on the actual \(O_4\) output.
Remove only that output halfweight from (13). Multiplying
(11)--(14) and using (15) proves the following whole-source bound.

**Theorem 1.** Put \(c_0=3\pi/49+\kappa\).
For either \(F=F_{34,N}\) or the older \(F=F_N^{\rm mid}\),
for both next types and both parities,
\[
 \begin{split}
 &|F(w,e^{-2\pi iy})d_N(e^{-2\pi iy})
                     C_{\beta,N}(e^{-2\pi iy},e^{-2\pi iz_*})|\\
 &\qquad\le C_\tau N^B
       \exp N\{\Re\mathcal P_\beta^+(-2,z_*)-c_0(x-x_*)^2+\tau\},
 \qquad y=x+ih_*\in I_*,\quad -2\le w\le2 .
 \end{split}
 \tag{16}
\]
All complete finite factors are retained. There is no division by
a cap or a conditional sum, and no assertion of a conditional
asymptotic at independent off-reference targets.

On any closed portion of \(I_*\) at positive real distance from
\(y_*\), the strict exponential bound persists on fixed small
complex complete-color and output neighborhoods, with normalized
fixed mixed derivatives. To see this, use the whole prefix packet
on its fixed neighborhood of \(q(K)\), the original complex
extension of the integer weighted polynomial
\(C_\beta^2d_N(Y)d_N(Z)^\beta\) behind (13), and the nonzero output
factorial packet. Choose \(\tau\) inside the quadratic loss,
then shrink the outside neighborhoods and apply fixed-radius
Cauchy estimates. All paths remain fixed at their base references.

## 4. Remove the paid part of the lower cyclic complement

Keep \(J_*\), \(K_N\), and the two remaining upper intervals from
[the top seam arc reduction](research_mixed_next_top_seam_arc_removal.md).
Suppose \(J_*=[y_*-\varepsilon,y_*+\varepsilon]\), with its
already chosen fixed positive half-length. Define
\[
 \mathscr W_N=\int_{I_*\setminus J_*}^{\rightarrow}K_N(y)\,dy .
 \tag{17}
\]
This definition requires no change to \(J_*\). If it was initially
chosen inside (3), the domain in (17) consists of two full
off-saddle subintervals of that source corridor.

On the unchanged lower height,
\[
 |1-Y^N|\ge1-e^{2\pi Nh_*}\ge\frac12
\]
for every sufficiently large \(N\), uniformly in the actual
reference family. Theorem 1, the original numerator factor \(N\),
and fixed path lengths therefore give
\[
 \partial_w^j\partial_z^k
       [e^{-N\mathcal P_\beta^+(w,z)}\mathscr W_N]
            =O_{j,k}(e^{-Nc_0\varepsilon^2/8})
 \tag{18}
\]
on sufficiently small fixed outside neighborhoods. The displayed
allowance leaves room for the single source tolerance, parameter
variation, polynomial costs and Cauchy estimates. An empty set in
(17) contributes zero.

The exact shorter remainder is now
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathscr R_N^{\rm img}
  ={}&\int_{\Gamma_0\setminus(J_*\cup I_*)}^{\rightarrow}K_N\,dy\\
    &-\int_{[s,\,11/50]+iH}^{\rightarrow}K_N\,dy
     -\int_{[9/20,\,1-s]+iH}^{\rightarrow}K_N\,dy .
 \end{split}
 \tag{19}
\]
The previous remainder equals
\(\mathscr R_N^{\rm short}=\mathscr R_N^{\rm img}+\mathscr W_N\)
exactly. Consequently, for
\[
          \eta=\min\{\Delta/128,c_0\varepsilon^2/8\}>0,
                   \qquad\Delta=59/440000,
\]
\[
 \partial_w^j\partial_z^k
 \left[e^{-N\mathcal P_\beta^+(w,z)}
  \{\mathcal T_\beta[F_{34,N}]-I_{\rm geom,N}
                               -\mathscr R_N^{\rm img}\}\right]
                       =O_{j,k}(e^{-N\eta}) .
 \tag{20}
\]
The original local geometric coefficient, power
\(N^{-2-\beta/2}\), extraction \(N\), and outside row scalar
are unchanged. Equation (20) has only the ordinary-next source
scope of its inputs.

The remaining lower pieces lie outside the proved horizontal
following-image component, apart from any overlap already removed
by \(J_*\). The upper middle intervals remain. The present theorem
gives no numerical endpoints for (3), no continuation across a gap
in that image, and no full-period suppression or knot-family
conclusion.
