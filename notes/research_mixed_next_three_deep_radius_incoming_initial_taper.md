# The initial incoming interval and left taper for next type three at deeper shared heights

Private research proof, 8 November 2026. The complete first source on
the initial real interval and its left taper can be attached to the
complete next type-three kernel when the shared radius is at most
\(7/10\). The first-factor contour transport is unchanged. Its endpoint
calibration and its bounded-prefix attachment are supplied by the new
type-three theorems, with either sign of the next private height.

All physical rows, the forced shared weight, both parities and the
original incoming measure are retained. No numerical evaluation or
modification of a frozen input is used.

private height \(h_t\) is allowed to be positive.

Thus the taper ends at \(11/50+ih_a\). Put

The finite factors are precisely

 &=\sum_{k=0}^{D}H_N(w,r^{-k})p_k(X)/P_k,\\

&\hspace{5mm}d_N(r^b)=P_b^{-1},\qquad \deg d_N\le D .
\end{split}                                                       \tag{3}
\]
The next own flag is zero. There is no outgoing halfweight and no
selected factor \(Y^{-N}\).

Let \(\Lambda_{123}(z)\) be the unchanged actual following affine
plane from the central theorem. At the actual output it satisfies
\[
\Lambda_{123}(z_*)=\Re\mathcal P_0^+(-2,z_*),                       \tag{4}

\boxed{\quad
|U_{0,N}(w,X)C_{1,N}(X,Y)d_N(Y)C_{0,N}(Y,Z)|
 \le C N^B e^{N\{\Lambda_{123}(z)-10^{-12}\}} .
\quad}                                                           \tag{5}
\]
The same bound holds for either oriented incoming integral with its
original measure \(+N\,da\). At the actual output, the integrals
normalized by \(e^{-N\mathcal P_0^+(w,z)}\) have all prescribed fixed
mixed derivatives on sufficiently small fixed complete-color and
output neighborhoods, with gap \(10^{-13}\). Paths, reference labels
and cuts are fixed at each base point before outside differentiation.

This theorem covers just the first two incoming pieces. The first
right chain, the remaining shared-coordinate contours and the full
next transfer are not conclusions of (5).
