    pi=W^dagger P,    pi W=I,

    L_endpoint=N E_St,
    R^epsilon E_St=omega_m^epsilon E_St,                    (1)

where

    omega_m=q^(d^2/2) (-1)^m q^(m(m+1)).

P in (1) is the physical two-head reversal. It is part of the actual
pivotal dual, not a replacement of a bilinear contraction by a norm.
All formulas apply to the full physical pair, including every degree.

Split each E_St into its ordered W and pi factors. Its intermediate
V_N edge is the crossing-disk edge d_i. The other legs follow precisely
the original outside main segments. Thus the resulting trivalent

[the actual matching construction](general_augmented_plat_matching_geometry.md):
its marked perfect matching D consists of these intermediate edges,

All edges now carry the SAME object V_N. At a vertex one retains the

put them in two horizontal rows. General Delta need not be bipartite.

Consequently the marked all-endpoint contribution is

    Z_endpoint=N^r omega_m^(sum_i epsilon_i) Z_Delta,       (2)

where Z_Delta is the original marked contraction of these typed W/pi

## 3. Why this is the geometric graph, and what the global polytope is

Suppose the actual matching graph Delta has the labeled positive
right-angled ideal polyhedron Q_Delta. Its triangular shaded faces
are exactly the just-identified source vertices, and its ideal vertices
are exactly the source graph edges. Thus the geometric and quantum
constructions share the actual labeled incidence, not merely a cubic
vertex count. The complete augmented exterior has volume 2Vol(Q_Delta).

Cone an ideal vertex of Q_Delta to compatible triangulations of its
nonincident faces, and use the identical subdivisions in the second
copy. Keep the actual white and shaded face identifications, including
all retained half twists. This gives an actual positive ideal
triangulation T of the augmented exterior. Its global angle polytope is

    alpha_(t,0)+alpha_(t,1)+alpha_(t,2)=pi for every t,
    sum_(tetrahedral incidences at e) alpha_(t,e)=2pi for every e,
    alpha>=0.                                           (8)

The incidence matrix is calculated from these FULL face-pairing words;
it is not one independent simplex per source vertex. The proved
[general positive gluing theorem](general_marked_positive_gluing_certificate.md)
gives its unique positive geometric maximum

    max_(8) sum_t V_ideal(alpha_t)=2Vol(Q_Delta).          (9)

It also supplies an algebraic optimizer and its cusp-decoration gauge.
A separately certified marked positive triangulation can replace Q_Delta.

Equations (7)--(9) identify the precise source and geometric endpoints
of a proposed coherent resummation. They do not yet identify the
variables or weights BETWEEN them. To do that, an actual recoupling
or face-state expansion must retain its finite physical coefficient,
all pole/normal terms, and a proved map to the angle incidences (8).
Its factorial action must equal the SINGLE constrained functional
in (9), with the source's phases/cycle and absolute normalization.
Triangle inequalities for ordinary spin colors, by themselves, are
not the global edge-sum equations (8). Matching the counts of labels
or tetrahedra is not an incidence proof.

This also explains why locally maximizing each trivalent scalar is
insufficient. The source tensors remain contracted through every
physical magnetic edge. The exact six-entry phase obstruction already
rules out making them all positive by independent leg gauges. A
positive resummation has to use those contractions coherently.

## 4. What is actually established beyond local vertices

The primitive cube supplies one nontrivial calibration. Its ORIGINAL
odd central sector was reduced by a generic, clocked, all-input rung
identity to a positive Newton period. The exact first normal survives
that reduction, and its exponential rate contains the shared geometric
core term, not just the product of the local source scalars. This is
a concrete example of the kind of global resummation required by (7).
It is not a generic identity for arbitrary Delta.

For an NC source graph Gamma, the present graph is its Fisher expansion
Delta=Fisher(Gamma). The original trivalent-source contractions reduce
its facial triangles to the known local coefficients and leave the
ordinary Gamma-network first normal. This is consistent with (7),
but does not extend that special facial-triangle reduction to an
arbitrary matching graph. General Delta can have nonmatching cycles
of other lengths and need not satisfy the original NC ordering.

At even N the endpoint node is double; the circle is nilpotent on
that sector. W pi with pi W=I is NOT its all-input replacement.
A full even normal or paired-channel source theorem is still required.
Even at odd levels, (7) alone neither proves endpoint dominance in
the whole color sum nor a nonzero endpoint value. Independently long
fillings additionally require the actual mixed auxiliary-color packet,
whole-color gap, marked peripheral branch and unit logarithmic charge.

## 5. Literature coverage only, with the actual scope retained

[Van der Veen, arXiv:0805.0094v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.0094),
Definitions 1--2 and Theorem 2, uses a specified sequence of KTG moves.
Its main theorem gives a sufficiently large augmentation multiplicity
for that sequence. Its invariant has the stated formula at ODD N and
is zero at EVEN N; its conclusion is explicitly the so(3) version.
The singly augmented outside is built from regular ideal octahedra.
Thus its theorem is not an all-level result for every singly augmented
matching graph, nor a theorem for all independently long fillings.
Only those definitions and statements are used here for coverage.

[Ibarra--McQuire--Purcell, arXiv:2506.09296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09296)
addresses OCTAHEDRAL fully augmented links. Its stated TV result is
for links without half twists and concerns a different invariant/root;
its introduction explicitly sets Dehn fillings aside. It does not
supply the original marked all-level mixed packet needed here.
[Belletti, arXiv:2002.01904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.01904)
formulates a Yokota upper-bound conjecture, proves it for a stated
large family, and derives TV consequences. That also does not identify
the original first normal (7) with a positive state sum for every Delta.
No proof technique or unreviewed asymptotic theorem from these papers
is an input to sections 1--4.
