# The complete 44 message at the second half face for an actual 443 word

Private written proof, 11 November 2026. Author full proof/source
readback is complete. Independent full proof/direct-source review is
required before adoption.

This note first transports the unselected second coordinate from its
old right plateau edge to one half at its actual negative height. It
then pays its connection to the real half point, and the selected
connection from height \(1/100\) to that same point. The first
polynomial remains complete. The two second Fourier sectors are used
on their respective height intervals; no estimate for the other
sector at either remote endpoint is inferred.

## 6. The shared half-height envelope and its two Fourier sectors

\[

\]

\end{split}                                                     \tag{25}

 0\le\partial_h\Re Q_1(s;a,y_h)\le\pi,\qquad

\]

\(-2\pi\Re s<\Arg(1-e^{-2\pi i(y_h+s)})<0\);

 \partial_h E_2(u)\le0\quad(h<0),\qquad

\(|1-e^{2\pi h}e^{-2\pi iw}|=
