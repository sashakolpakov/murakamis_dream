# Transport of the complete upper terminal triangle on the left taper

Research deduction, 7 November 2026. The entire upper physical
transform triangle of the ordinary terminal-four source admits a
strict coupled transport with the whole first type-three cap along
its early affine height path. Every original private and Newton row
in this triangle is retained. The comparison at the plateau end of
the path remains separate. No numerical evaluation is used.

## 1. The original positive majorant and its half face

Use the ordinary \((3,4)\) source and notation of
[the pair target](research_mixed_three_four_ordinary_pair_target.md).
Let
\[
 a(x)=x+ih(x),\quad h(x)=c\frac{x-.1}{.12},\quad
 .1\le x\le.22,\quad -.072\le c\le.021,\quad
 \lambda=-c/.12,\quad r=e^{2\pi h}.
 \tag{1}
\]
Thus \(a'=1-i\lambda\), \(-7/40\le\lambda\le3/5\),
\(3/5<r<23/20\); the signs of \(\lambda\) and \(r-1\)
are opposite.

The literal triple source is equation (3) of
[the terminal-four right-triangle theorem](research_mixed_terminal_four_right_triangle.md).
Write \(\mathfrak V^{\rm hi}_{1,N}(w,X)\) for the sum of
absolute values of all of its summands with transform index
\(i/N\ge1/2\), on their exact support \(i+j<N\), \(l\le j\).
No coherent coefficient is replaced after restricting an inner sum.

Let \(M_4(1/2)\) be the entropy maximum in that theorem, and put
\[
 Q_a(t)=\int_0^t\log|e^{-2\pi h}-e^{-2\pi i(x+s)}|\,ds,
 \qquad G(a)=M_4(1/2)+Q_a(1/2).
 \tag{2}
\]
The chirp \(X^{-i}\) is included in \(Q_a\).
The original physical rate satisfies
\[
 R_1(a;t,e,u)\le G(a)-\log(80/63)(t-1/2),
                                      \qquad t\ge1/2.
 \tag{3}
\]
This holds on (1). Indeed the entropy argument is independent of the
incoming angle, and its sole external bound is
\(\partial_tQ_a\le\log(1+e^{-2\pi h})<\log(63/20)\).
The radius lies in the old range \(|h|<.12\).
This angle-independent source extension, including all zeros, is
also explicitly recorded in Section 3 of
[the full-radius comparison](research_mixed_incoming_half_circle_radius_comparison.md).
Consequently the original uniform product and Newton bounds give
\[
 \mathfrak V^{\rm hi}_{1,N}(w,e^{-2\pi ia})
       \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}e^{N(G(a)+\tau)}
 \tag{4}
\]
on sufficiently small fixed ordinary-color disks, with fixed jets
and both parities. The true upper triangle, its wall zeros, and the
even half row all remain included.

## 2. Exact incoming derivative of the half face

On (1), all arguments in the half-face identity are regular, and
\[
 Q_a(1/2)=\Re\{g(a+1/2)-g(a)+\pi ia\}.
 \tag{5}
\]
Write
\[
 L(a)=g'(a+1/2)-g'(a),\qquad \ell=\Re L,\quad\omega=\Im L.
\]
The original rotated-ratio argument, with real shift \(1/2\), gives
\[
 r\le1:\quad \omega\ge-\pi/2,
 \qquad
 r\ge1:\quad \omega\le-\pi/2.
 \tag{6}
\]
Its angle hypothesis is
\(0<\pi/2<2\pi x+\pi/2<\pi\), which holds throughout
the present interval. The same original logarithmic lift is used
on both sides of \(r=1\).

In particular \(\ell>0\). More quantitatively,
\[
 e^{2\ell}
 =\frac{r+r^{-1}+2\cos(2\pi x)}
        {r+r^{-1}-2\cos(2\pi x)},
 \qquad \cos(2\pi x)>9/50.
 \tag{7}
\]
For \(3/5\le r\le1\), convexity gives
\(r+r^{-1}\le34/15\), so
\[
           \ell>\tfrac12\log(197/143)
             =\operatorname{atanh}(27/170)>27/170.
 \tag{8}
\]
For \(1\le r\le23/20\), the convenient weaker bound
\(r+r^{-1}\le43/20\) gives
\[
           \ell>\tfrac12\log(251/179)
             =\operatorname{atanh}(36/215)>36/215.
 \tag{9}
\]
These are exact rational comparisons and the positive atanh series.

## 3. Both complete first-cap alternatives increase

Let \(\mathcal C_0(a,\vartheta)\) be the complete early first-three
cap envelope of
[early-cap transport](research_mixed_cap_early_taper_envelope_transport.md).
Its regular part is the maximum over real \(e\in[.27,.49]\) of
the original \(\Re\Psi_0(e,a)\), with its fixed angular loss.
Its other part is the true-index ceiling
\[
 E_{\rm end}(h)=\frac35+2\pi h_++\frac1{100}\log4.
 \tag{10}
\]
Every part of the finite first cap is bounded by this envelope;
no true-end branch is removed.

For a fixed regular row, the already proved cap half-stencil is
\[
 H^{\rm cap}_0(e)
 =\Re[(1-i\lambda)\{g'(e+a)-g'(a)+3i/2\}]>1/20.
 \tag{11}
\]
Its proof retains the actual radius/height relation (1), and applies
on the whole stated real private interval. Adding (5) cancels this
gauge and gives the exact derivative
\[
 \frac{d}{dx}\{\Re\Psi_0(e,a(x))+G(a(x))\}
       =H^{\rm cap}_0(e)+\ell
                         +\lambda(\omega+\pi-3/2).
 \tag{12}
\]
For \(\lambda\ge0\), (6) makes the last term nonnegative,
since \(\pi/2>3/2\). For \(\lambda\le0\),
\(\omega+\pi-3/2\le\pi/2-3/2<1/14\), and hence that
term is bounded below by \(-1/80\). Equation (9) pays this
loss with room. Thus (12) is strictly greater than \(1/20\)
in both cases, including \(c=0\).

For the true-end alternative, when \(c\le0\) the endpoint
ceiling is constant along the path, so the derivative of its sum
with (5) is
\[
                         \ell+\lambda(\omega+\pi)
                                      >27/170>1/20.
 \tag{13}
\]
When \(c\ge0\), retain the derivative of \(2\pi h_+\) in
(10). The sum instead has derivative
\[
                         \ell+\lambda(\omega-\pi)
                                      >36/215>1/20.
 \tag{14}
\]
These conclusions use (6), (8)--(9) with their original sign
correlations. At \(c=0\) the formulas agree. At the left endpoint
of the path, the one-sided derivatives suffice.

Integrate each regular row or endpoint branch before taking its
maximum. The angular loss is constant on the path. This proves:

**Theorem 1.** For the complete finite-source envelope on (1),
\[
 \mathcal C_0(a(x_0),\vartheta)+G(a(x_0))
 \le\mathcal C_0(a(x_1),\vartheta)+G(a(x_1))
                            -\frac{x_1-x_0}{20},
 \qquad .1\le x_0<x_1\le.22.
 \tag{15}
\]
No differentiability of its maximizing private row is required.

In particular the exact product of the whole first cap and the
entire upper physical terminal triangle obeys
\[
\begin{split}
 |U_{0,N}(w_L,e^{-2\pi ia(x)})|
     \mathfrak V^{\rm hi}_{1,N}(w_R,e^{-2\pi ia(x)})
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
 \exp N\left\{
   \mathcal C_0(a(.22),\vartheta)+G(a(.22))
                      -\frac{.22-x}{20}+\tau\right\}.
\end{split}
 \tag{16}
\]
Both whole ordinary boundary polynomials, fixed jets and both parity
lattices are retained, with the original cap envelope's color scope.
Finite zeros are handled by upper bounds throughout.

The right side of (16) is an actual half-face envelope value, not
the geometric action \(M\). The existing calibrated whole-cap bound
at \(a(.22)\) cannot replace \(\mathcal C_0(a(.22),\vartheta)\)
inside this inequality without a separate comparison: the latter
uses a real-private majorant. Thus the theorem pays transport of
this complete source branch while leaving its original-action
calibration explicit. It does not estimate the lower transform
rows, the other private contour pieces, or the whole ordinary pair.
