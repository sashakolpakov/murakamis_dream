## 3. Explicit half-input branch and literal actions

For either box, put \(Q=\cot(\pi z)\), and calculate only
\[
 D_Q=Q^2+4-4iQ,\qquad
 S=\frac{-Q+\sqrt{D_Q}}2,\qquad
 T=\frac{S+i}{S-i},\qquad s_{\rm h}=\frac{\Log T}{2\pi i}.
                                                               \tag{6}
\]
The square root and logarithm are principal.
The complete-strip proof in 623ebfda gives one right cotangent
root. Its discriminant avoids the nonpositive real axis: if
\(Q=q+i\beta\), \(q>0\), the imaginary part of \(D_Q\) is
\(2q(\beta-2)\); when it is zero, its real part is \(q^2+8>0\).
The principal square root continues from \(Q=0\), so the plus
sign in (6) is the right root. No alternate sign is tried.

the discriminant interval avoids the nonpositive real axis;

\(0<\Re s_{\rm h}<1/2\); and all original arguments
\(s_{\rm h},1/2+s_{\rm h},z,z+s_{\rm h}\) remain in
\(0<\Re v<1\). A failed guard is retained as a failure;
there is no branch fallback.
