**Theorem.** Every reference in the six typed products, both orientations and all

\[

 \tag{4}

No fixed numerical margin is asserted here.

*Proof.* The 72 exact charts cover the ordinary

weighted-target envelopes are tested on every accepted leaf.

This is exactly the implication proved in
