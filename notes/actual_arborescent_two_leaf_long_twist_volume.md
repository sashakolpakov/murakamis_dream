# Knot complement volume for arbitrary trees of two-or-more-leaf Conway seeds

31 October 2026. This research theorem removes the geometric leaf test
from the previous two-leaf extension. The argument contracts an entire
two-leaf block before estimating its aliases. The resulting uniform
bound applies automatically to every proper seed in the stated grammar.
This is an theorem for an explicit arborescent
class, not a theorem for every arborescent or hyperbolic knot.

## 1. The class and theorem

Start with the original complete crossing-circle coupon
\(h=\mathcal R(L_N)\), where \(\mathcal R\) is the actual ordered
quarter-turn. Use distinct leaves and fixed finite expressions

\[
 A_s=h^s,\quad s\ge2,\qquad
 E::=A_s\mid\mathcal R(E)\mid E_1E_2.                         \tag{1}
\]

Use the original cylindrical closure \(C(FG)\), with both children
generated by (1), or an isolated closure \(C(A_s)\) with \(s\ge3\).
Choose a fixed leaf half-twist pattern
\(\epsilon_\ell\in\{0,1,-1\}\) whose literal endpoint pairings
give a knot after all auxiliary circles are filled. Such a loop-free
pattern can be constructed for every expression. The isolated
\(C(A_2)\) is excluded.

Insert independently chosen fixed disk-framed full twists
\(R^{-2p_\ell}\) at the auxiliary crossing circles. Thus the actual
all-filled leaf is the rational tangle

\[
                 \mathcal R(R^{\epsilon_\ell-2p_\ell}).           \tag{2}
\]

**Theorem.** For each fixed expression and knot half-twist pattern
there is a finite threshold \(T(E,\epsilon)\) such that, whenever
all fixed signed integers satisfy \(|p_\ell|>T(E,\epsilon)\), the
resulting knot \(K_{E,\epsilon}(p)\) is hyperbolic and

\[
 \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}
        \log\left|K_N\bigl(K_{E,\epsilon}(p)\bigr)\right|
   =\operatorname{Vol}\bigl(S^3\setminus K_{E,\epsilon}(p)\bigr).
                                                                 \tag{3}
\]

Both level parities and composite levels are included. The number
of leaves, branching and turn depth are arbitrary finite numbers.
The expression and twists are fixed before \(N\) tends to infinity;
the theorem imposes neither common slope signs nor comparable slope
magnitudes. It does not assert a threshold uniform over all trees.

The invariant is the original marked one-cut invariant with its
retained enhancement. The reduced enhanced Kashaev normalization has
an outer factor of unit modulus, and the full modified one-cut
normalization has the recorded single factor of modulus \(1/N\).
These are the existing [normalization comparison](actual_complete_augmented_source_normalization.md)
and [crossing calculation](kashaev_crossing_and_period_lattice.md).
Neither normalization changes (3).

Only auxiliary crossing-circle cusps are filled. The knot cusp remains
unfilled, and the right side of (3) is the knot complement volume.
Literal rational leaves, actual stacking, turns and the prescribed
closure establish arborescent membership; no arithmetic identity of
tangle fractions is substituted for those operations.

## 2. The estimate that removes the leaf condition

The [complete two-leaf geometry](actual_conway_two_leaf_seed_geometry.md)
already proves for every proper \(A_2\) seed

\[
 \frac12<X=\cot(\pi a)<1,\qquad
 \cot(\pi b)=X-\frac1{2X},\qquad
 F_2'(b)=2\log(2X^2).                                      \tag{4}
\]

Previously a supporting plane at each leaf separately required
\(X\ge2/3\). The [uniform block theorem](actual_conway_uniform_two_leaf_block_alias_gap.md)
instead includes the original multiplication gate and proves

\[
 G_{AC}(z),\ G_{AA}(z)
       \le F_2(b)+F_2'(b)(z-b)-\frac1{100},
                 \qquad 0\le z\le1,\quad \frac12\le X\le1.
                                                                 \tag{5}
\]

The proof uses joint concavity and a finite exact rational certificate
for the whole slope interval. Each supporting plane bounds the entire
allowed polytope, and affine endpoint checks cover each slope interval.
It keeps the true \(x+y\le1\) constraint. It does not sample unknown
maximizers or extrapolate from selected knots.

The [exact finite block transport](actual_conway_two_leaf_block_source_gap.md)
groups two original leaves and their one gate without changing the
source. Every Hermite term, physical color, phase and normalization
remains present. The internal first derivatives vanish at completeness,
so the block tangent in (5) has exactly the same derivative on its
surrounding wire as the original action.

In any sector containing an alias, use (5) on a contaminated two-leaf
seed. Larger proper seeds retain their already automatic positive
leaf bounds. Sum with the other complete seed and macro-product
tangents. The original closed-tree stationarity cancels all free
linear terms. The result has a strict gap below the complete source
action. The actual \(d=N-1\) shifts cost only \(O(1)\) in the
exponent; a fixed tree has polynomially many wire states and finitely
many alias subsets. Thus the bound holds for the entire original
alias contribution, uniformly in all physical colors.

The all-core majorant agrees near the complete point with the strictly
concave pure action and has that same unique global maximum. Outside
a fixed strict neighborhood it has a compact gap. Inside, the original
far-color core estimate supplies the strict loss when any physical
color leaves the complete corridor. These facts give both whole-source
hypotheses of the filling transfer.

## 3. Why the rate is the actual knot complement volume

The [two-leaf source proof](actual_conway_two_leaf_seed_source.md)
retains the exact primitive lattice and all offsets, including the
missing even-level top coefficient of \(h^2\). Strict interior
rounding supplies every sufficiently large level. Its complex packet
has power \(N^{3/2}\) and nonzero leading amplitude, with the actual
changed local action \(\Phi_\epsilon\) and explicitly retained unit
phase. No new packet is chosen to fit the block estimate.

Consequently the [original-source filling transfer](general_original_source_long_filling_transfer.md)
gives a nonzero asymptotic for the full invariant, with exponential
action at the selected small saddle of

\[
 S_{\epsilon,p}(\chi)=\Phi_\epsilon(\chi)
       +\frac{\pi i}{2}\sum_\ell
                      \frac{(\chi_\ell-1)^2}{p_\ell}.             \tag{6}
\]

The geometric extension retains the actual signed augmented-link
group, ordered face maps and primitive peripheral coordinates

\[
 U_\ell=-2\pi i\chi_\ell,\qquad
 V_\ell=-2\partial_\ell\Phi_\epsilon,\qquad
 G=2\pi i\Phi_\epsilon.                                         \tag{7}
\]

Stationarity of (6) is exactly
\(U_\ell+p_\ell V_\ell=-2\pi i\). The absolutely calibrated
regulator and marked geometric completion on this same branch give

\[
 2\pi\Re S_{\epsilon,p}(\chi_*)
      =\operatorname{Vol}\bigl(S^3\setminus K_{E,\epsilon}(p)\bigr).
                                                                 \tag{8}
\]

In particular the right side is neither merely the augmented-link
volume nor the volume of an unrelated critical solution. Equations
(5)--(8) and the nonzero full-source asymptotic prove (3).

## 4. The previously excluded three-leaf closure

The [isolated three-leaf theorem](actual_conway_isolated_three_leaf_long_twist_volume.md)
also removes the old exclusion of \(C(A_3)\). Its complete state is
\(a=\arctan(2)/\pi\), with pair output \(b=1-a\),
\(X=1/2\), and \(F_0=2v_8/\pi\). The block lemma includes this
endpoint. The exact full Newton algebra is commutative, so a sector
with one or two aliases may pair those aliases first, leaving a core.
The block slope equals that core's complete slope, \(-\log4\), and
the original closing relation cancels their linear terms.

For three aliases, each leaf has upper rate \(v_3/\pi\), and the
one retained product has rate at most \(3v_3/\pi\). The elementary
telescoping sine-series bound proves

\[
 F_0-\frac{6v_3}{\pi}
   =\frac2\pi(v_8-3v_3)
           >\frac{5747}{25344}>\frac1{100}.                     \tag{9}
\]

This supplies the whole-source gap for all seven alias sectors. The
all-core maximum and far-color loss give the physical-color complement
bound. Its existing primitive three-dimensional packet retains the
essential \(N-1\) shift, nonzero amplitude and changed half-twist
phases. The existing geometric closure has one completed child and
one leaf, so its CAP seam has angle \(\pi/2\); that separate case
is retained explicitly. Its actual complete volume, primitive paths
and absolute regulator then give the same filling conclusion (3).
The already proved isolated closures \(C(A_s)\), \(s\ge4\),
continue to apply. Hence every isolated seed closure with \(s\ge3\)
is included, while \(C(A_2)\) remains excluded.

## 5. Scope of the enlargement

The former local-tangent failure \(C(A_2\mathcal R(A_4))\) is now
covered, as are all other fixed expressions in (1) with the prescribed
closure. There is no remaining algebraic leaf test to pass. The earlier
[leaf-certified theorem](actual_arborescent_algebraic_leaf_long_twist_volume.md)
remains valid as a sufficient special case and records the original
limitation; the present theorem supersedes that limitation for this
grammar.

A coverage theorem for every arborescent presentation,
or for arbitrary rational-tangle additions outside (1), has not been
proved. The exact quantum boundary contraction and the signed estimate
must survive those additional operations before claiming that scope.
Likewise no generic main-meridian quantum A-operator is produced here.

The theorem applies the stated packet and marked-completion results to this recursive class. Extension beyond that class remains a separate question.
