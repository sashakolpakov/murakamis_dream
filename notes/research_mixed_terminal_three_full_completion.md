## 1. The exact source and the completed raw face

Keep the original conventions
\[
 r=e^{-2\pi i/N},\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad
 P_i=(r;r)_i,\quad p_i(X)=(rX;r)_i,\quad X=e^{-2\pi ia}.
 \tag{1}
\]
The exact terminal transform is
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathcal V_{0,N}(w,X)
 &=\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}\frac{X^{-i}p_i(X)}{P_i}
                         \mathcal U_{0,N}(w,r^i)\\
 &=N\sum_{i+l<N}(-q)^{-l}e_l(w)
          \frac{X^{-i}p_i(X)P_{i+l}}{P_i^3P_l^2}.
\end{split}
 \tag{2}
\]
These are the literal identities of
[the terminal Newton reduction](research_mixed_terminal_three_newton_reduction.md).
Every outer physical row in the first line includes its complete
Newton sum. In the second line the triangular support is exact for
every ordinary color; no term with \(i+l\ge N\) has been revived.

Let \(a_*=A+iH\) be an actual type-three terminal reference,
\((t_*,u_*)\) its original critical pair, and \(\mathcal P_0\)
its calibrated action from
[the original critical curve](research_mixed_terminal_three_critical_curve.md).
For \(y\in P=[.22,.45]\), set \(d=y-A\) and
\[
\begin{split}
 B_0(a_*;y)&=\Re\mathcal P_0(a_*)+
                \Re\mathcal P_0'(a_*)d-\kappa d^2,\\
 \Pi_{0,\nu}&=\mathcal P_0-\nu g/2,\\

                \Re\Pi_{0,\nu}'(a_*)d-\kappa d^2,
 \qquad \kappa=1/2500000.
\end{split}
 \tag{3}
\]
Here \(\nu\in\{0,1\}\) is the type minus three of the
neighboring retained coordinate. Its target is \(a=y+iH\), at

The completed [raw-face certificate](research_mixed_terminal_three_right_face_certificate.md)

                  <B_0(a_*;y)-\frac1{2000},

Both index heights in (4) are zero; there is no alias penalty.
Section 5 records the exhaustive author and saved replay establishing

The [right-triangle theorem](research_mixed_terminal_three_right_triangle.md)

\(\log(80/63)(t-1/2)\). It keeps the external product literal

 \sum_{\substack{i/N>1/2\\ i+l<N}}

       \frac{X^{-i}p_i(X)P_{i+l}}{P_i^3P_l^2}\right|

for all sufficiently large \(N\). The gap pays the common

The same conclusion holds for each fixed ordinary-color derivative

The strictly upper condition \(i/N>1/2\) in (5) is legitimate

## 2. A bare gap and the entire nonzero packet

Write the exact disjoint decomposition
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathcal V_{0,N}&=\mathcal L_N+\mathcal Q_N,\\
 \mathcal L_N&=\sum_{0\le i\le\lfloor N/2\rfloor}
          \frac{X^{-i}p_i(X)}{P_i}\mathcal U_{0,N}(w,r^i),\\
 \mathcal Q_N&=\sum_{i>\lfloor N/2\rfloor}^{N-1}
          \frac{X^{-i}p_i(X)}{P_i}\mathcal U_{0,N}(w,r^i).
\end{split}
 \tag{6}
\]
The [lower-half completion](research_mixed_terminal_three_rectangle_completion.md)
proves the original packet for \(\mathcal L_N\). Its exact
partition first removes the full coherent lower end-index sector,
then uses positive low/high Newton bounds and the original strip
contours on the remaining rows. We do not repeat or alter that
partition, nor restrict the inner Newton sum of an end-index cap.

For clarity, its discarded-sector estimates are already **bare**
estimates against \(B_0\), before adding any neighboring Fourier
weight. This is necessary to transfer the nonzero coefficient when
\(\nu=1\). The mixed-face and alias comparisons are bare by
definition. The left, Newton-exterior and upper-cut-column estimates
inherit those bare comparisons. The low/high/near-half Newton
majorants have raw rate below \(.879\), whereas the bare supporting
plane exceeds \(.89\). The coherent end-index majorant has raw
rate below \(.868\), against that same bare plane, by
[the terminal-tail theorem](research_mixed_terminal_transform_tails.md).
Thus the lower-half discarded sum has a uniform strict bare gap.
Together with (5), this bounds every noncentral contribution by
\(\exp N(B_0-1/8000)\), after shrinking the common color disk
and taking \(N\) sufficiently large. No Fourier factor is divided
out to obtain this conclusion.

**Theorem 1.** At each actual terminal reference and complete color,
the entire transform has the original nonzero asymptotic as \(N\)
tends to infinity through all positive integer levels, of either parity:
\[
 \mathcal V_{0,N}(-2,e^{-2\pi ia_*})
 =\frac{2\pi}{N}\,
   \frac{\mathcal A(a_*,t_*,u_*)}{\sqrt{\det H_*}}
   e^{N\mathcal P_0(a_*)}(1+O(N^{-1})),
 \qquad H_*=-\mathcal T''_{(t,u)}(a_*;t_*,u_*).
 \tag{7}
\]
The determinant square root is the original accretive Gaussian
branch, and its coefficient is nonzero. For every fixed \(j\ge0\),
\[
 \left.\partial_w^j\mathcal V_{0,N}(w,e^{-2\pi ia_*})\right|_{w=-2}
 =2\pi N^{j-1}\,
   \frac{\mathcal A(a_*,t_*,u_*)}{\sqrt{\det H_*}}
   \left(-\frac{\tan\pi u_*}{4\pi}\right)^j
   e^{N\mathcal P_0(a_*)}(1+O(N^{-1})).
 \tag{8}
\]
Every fixed ordinary jet therefore has a nonzero leading coefficient.

*Proof.* The completed central rectangle has the holomorphic ordinary-
color Gaussian packet of
[zero-mode completion](research_mixed_terminal_three_zero_mode_completion.md),
with its aliases exponentially smaller. The normalization is exactly
\(N^2\cdot N^{-2}\cdot(2\pi/N)\): the two residue factors,
the literal double-source symbol and the Gaussian integral.
The lower-half proof adds only its suppressed sectors. Equation (5)
adds the entire remaining right triangle as a suppressed positive
majorant. At \(y=A\), the common bare plane is
\(B_0(a_*;A)=\Re\mathcal P_0(a_*)\), so all those errors are
exponentially smaller than the nonzero Gaussian.

The central phase and coefficient extend holomorphically on a fixed
ordinary-color disk. Shrink that disk so its phase variation spends
only part of the strict bare gap; the uniform error remains
exponentially smaller there. Cauchy's formula on a smaller disk then
transfers the packet and its relative error to the full transform.
The original color marginal is
\(-\tan(\pi u_*)/(4\pi)\), which is nonzero on the certified
endpoint range. This proves (7)--(8) with all original scalars and
both level parities. \(\square\)

## 3. The full independent-plateau upper bound

**Theorem 2.** For every \(\tau>0\), uniformly on the actual
reference families and all \(y\in P\), there is a fixed small
ordinary-color disk such that
\[
 \left|\mathcal V_{0,N}(w,e^{-2\pi i(y+iH)})\right|
 \left|d_N(e^{-2\pi i(y+iH)})\right|^{\nu/2}
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
                     e^{N\{B_\nu(a_*;y)+\tau\}}.
 \tag{9}
\]
Here \(d_N\) is the original Fourier polynomial. The terminal
coordinate has been summed completely into \(\mathcal V_{0,N}\);
(9) is not a bound for individual terminal Fourier alternatives.

*Proof.* The lower-half completion gives the bare upper plane
\(B_0+\tau\), and (5) bounds its disjoint complement strictly
below that plane. This proves the bare case directly. For the actual
unselected type-four neighbor, the height is negative, and its
Fourier halfweight has upper rate \(-\Re g(y+iH)/2\).
Horizontal convexity gives
\[
\begin{split}
 B_0(a_*;y)-\tfrac\nu2\Re g(y+iH)
 =B_\nu(a_*;y)-\tfrac\nu2\bigl\{
   \Re g(y+iH)-\Re g(a_*)-\Re g'(a_*)d\bigr\}
 \le B_\nu(a_*;y).
\end{split}
 \tag{10}
\]
Multiplication, with one total source tolerance, therefore proves
(9), including Fourier zeros. No positive-height or selected-neighbor
branch has been inferred from the auxiliary-disk face certificate.
\(\square\)

The ordinary-color estimates and their fixed derivatives are uniform

## 4. A whole-terminal first-collar corollary

Consider a fixed mixed word of length \(m\ge4\), with terminal
type three, and put \(h=m-1\). Let its first two types be
\((3+\alpha,3+\beta)\). Retain exactly the first-collar and
height hypotheses of
[three-row source assembly, Section 3](research_mixed_three_row_source_assembly.md):
\[
 x_1\in[0,\rho],\qquad
 y_2\in I_{3+\beta},\quad
 I_3=[.267,.433],\ I_4=[.24,.363],\qquad
 y_3,\ldots,y_h\in P.
 \tag{11}
\]
Both allowed first selections and the proved second type-four
selection are retained. The second height follows its original
physical attenuation/selection path. Coordinates \(3,\ldots,h\)
are unselected and keep their reference heights. All real plateau
coordinates in (11) are independent; they need not lie near their
reference real parts or in a moving critical image.

Let \(\mathcal L_{h,N}\) be the literal prefix magnitude from
that assembly, with its one cut halfweight, and put
\(d_j=y_j-\Re r_j\). The completed ordinary row bounds and
height transport give
\[
\begin{split}
 N^{-1}\log\mathcal L_{h,N}
 \le M_h+\Re P_{h,b}d_h-\delta_{\alpha\beta}+\tau_L
 -\kappa\left(d_2^2+2\sum_{j=3}^{h-1}d_j^2+d_h^2\right)
 +O_m(\log N/N),\\
 \delta_{00}=3/100,\quad\delta_{10}=3/125,\quad
 \delta_{01}=\delta_{11}=4/125.
\end{split}
 \tag{12}
\]
The terminal bound (9) carries the other cut halfweight with
\(a_*=r_h\), \(\nu=\epsilon_h\). The original allocations satisfy
\[
 M_h+\Re\Pi_{0,\nu}(r_h)=M_{\rm open},\qquad
                  P_{h,b}+\Pi_{0,\nu}'(r_h)=0.
 \tag{13}
\]
These are the exact action and marginal identities from
[the same source assembly, equation (14)](research_mixed_three_row_source_assembly.md),
now used with the full terminal transform instead of an end tail.

**Corollary 3.** On the sector (11), the exact contracted source
with its **whole** type-three terminal transform has upper rate
\[
 M_{\rm open}-\delta_{\alpha\beta}+\tau
 -\kappa\left(d_2^2+2\sum_{j=3}^{m-1}d_j^2\right)
 +O_m(\log N/N),\qquad \tau=\tau_L+\tau_R.
 \tag{14}
\]
In particular \(\tau<1/250\) leaves a common loss \(1/50\),
with polynomial prefactors retained.

Indeed multiplication of (9) and (12) cancels the cut tangent by
(13) and counts its two halfweights once. The two cut quadratic
terms combine to give the endpoint multiplicity in (14).
The terminal upper plane has no extra strict loss; the whole
\(1/50\) conclusion is paid by
\(\min\delta_{\alpha\beta}=3/125\) and the single total
tolerance. For a fixed word, choose the individual ordinary source
tolerances to sum to that total, so no fixed loss is paid at every
edge. The original scalar
\(\kappa_N^{m+k_4}N^{m+k_4-1}\), summation or integration over
this fixed contour sector, and each fixed ordinary boundary
derivative change only the polynomial prefactor. A fixed pair of
ordinary-color disks is retained. Integer-allocated complex extension
uses an explicitly reserved part of the same tolerance.

This is a strict discarded-sector theorem, not a noncancellation
theorem for the whole open-word source. It does not cover later
selected heights, nonplateau coordinates, lengths two or three in
this collar assembly, a type-four terminal transform, or arbitrary
short partial fillings. Theorem 1 does prove noncancellation of the
entire specified terminal factor at its actual reference; those
remaining global and filling obligations are separate.
