# The complete three/four plateau at actual following references

Research deduction, 7 November 2026. The complete transformed
three/four prefix has its calibrated plateau bound and a nonzero
plateau packet throughout the actual following-reference families.
The second vertex is now an ordinary block, so the already completed
ordinary source planes apply with their actual correlated inputs.
The proof removes only a known nonzero outgoing Fourier halfweight.
It does not divide a signed conditional sum or an ordinary terminal
packet. All physical conditional rows remain present.

The incoming contours in the holomorphic packet statement are fixed
at each base reference. No maximum principle is applied to a
reference-dependent contour. The incoming taper, right extension,
and complete prefix remain separate obligations. No numerical
evaluation is used.

## 1. The actual following family and its original pre-Gram action

Let \(K\) be any of \(O_3,O_4,A_3,A_4\) in
[the actual-reference domains](research_mixed_actual_last_row_reference_domains.md).
These are compact subsets of the reduced typed disks \(P_3,P_4\),
strictly inside
\[
 \Omega=D_3^\circ\cup D_4^\circ,\qquad
 D_3=\{|c-(.35-.01i)|\le.09\},\quad
 D_4=\{|c-(.30-.052i)|\le.064\}.                         \tag{1}
\]
Use the unique original-log root of the cap-initialized \(34\)
action from
[the global following curve](research_mixed_three_four_following_critical_curve.md):
\[
 (u(c),e(c),a(c),p(c),q(c)).
\]
Here \(e,a\) are the first vertex's private and attached legs,
and \(p,q\) are the second vertex's two ordinary legs. Their
external closing coordinate is \(c\). In particular
\[
 a(c)\in A_3,\qquad p(c),q(c)\in O_4,\qquad
 (p,q)=F_4(a,c),                                       \tag{2}
\]
where \(F_4\) is the original zero-lift ordinary map.
The first endpoint map has external input \(p(c)\).
The closed source enclosures give
\[
 \begin{split}
 .278\le\Re a\le.391,\quad& -.072\le\Im a\le.021,\\
 .24\le\Re p,\Re q\le.36,\quad&
               -.102\le\Im p,\Im q\le-.001 .
 \end{split}                                                   \tag{3}
\]
Their actual images have the strict margins from the cited source.

Write \(a_0=a(c_0)=A_0+ih_0\), \(p_0=p(c_0)\),
\(z_0=q(c_0)\) for a base \(c_0\in K\). Keep the original
bare conditional phase
\[
 Q(t;a,z)=g(a+t)+g(z+t)-3g(t)-g(a)-g(z)+2\pi iat.
                                                               \tag{4}
\]
Near \((a_0,z_0)\), let \(t(a,z)\) be its original conditional
critical branch and set
\[
 \psi(a,z)=Q(t(a,z);a,z),\qquad
 \mathcal P(w,z)=\operatorname{stat}_{a}
                 [\mathcal T_0(a,w)+\psi(a,z)] .         \tag{5}
\]
At \(w=-2,z=z_0\), these roots are \(a_0,p_0\).
The action in (5) has the original additive constant:
\[
 \mathcal P(-2,q(c))=\mathscr P_{34}(c)
                         =H_{34}(c)-G(q(c),c).          \tag{6}
\]
Indeed eliminating the first private and auxiliary variables
in the original action gives precisely
\(\mathcal T_0(a,-2)+Q(p;a,q)+G(q,c)\).
The [all-word pre-Gram theorem](research_mixed_arbitrary_prefix_pregram_curve.md)
therefore identifies (5) with the unique absolute action on the
actual image \(q(\Omega)\). There is no final Gram factor,
outgoing ordinary cap, or newly chosen phase constant in (5).

## 2. Why the completed ordinary estimates apply

The external input pair of the second vertex is
\((a(c),c)\in A_3\times K\), with the left external type three
and the right external type determined by \(K\). This is one
of the typed input products in
[three-row source assembly](research_mixed_three_row_source_assembly.md),
Section 1. Its conditional orientation is exactly
\((p;a,q)\), where \(q\) is the output of the same map.
No private endpoint leg is substituted for an ordinary input.

The conditional packet of
[the reduced-disk theorem](research_mixed_reduced_disk_conditional_packets.md)
applies as well, since \(a(c)\in P_3\) and \(c\in P_\nu\).
That theorem supplies an ambient neighborhood of the conditional
image \((a,q)\), not just its values on the parameterized image.
All rows revived at a complex \(q\) are included.

For the first cap, the reference is its actual attached output
\(a(c)\), with external input \(p(c)\in O_4\). The own-three,
input-four branch of
[complete first-cap support](research_mixed_first_cap_plateau_completion.md)
is valid on this family: its original reduced input disk and
negative-height restriction hold, and the extra adjacent-block
exclusion is only needed in the own-four, input-four family.
The complete cap packet also applies on the compact attached image.
Thus the argument uses the proved sufficient source domains even
if a particular \(c\in K\) is not realized by a closed word.

Set \(P=[11/50,9/20]\) and \(\kappa_*=1/2500000\).
For \(x\in P\), put \(d=x-A_0\). The whole-cap plane is
\[
 B_U(x)=\Re\mathcal T_0(a_0,-2)
                +\Re\mathcal T_0'(a_0,-2)d-\kappa_*d^2.
                                                               \tag{7}
\]
The allocated ordinary phase and its plane, with the outgoing
target fixed at its reference \(z_0\), are
\[
 \phi(a,z)=\psi(a,z)-\tfrac12 g(z),\qquad
 B_\phi(x)=\Re\phi(a_0,z_0)
                  +\Re\phi_a(a_0,z_0)d-\kappa_*d^2.     \tag{8}
\]
The left halfweight is absent because its type is three. The
outgoing halfweight is exactly the own-four halfweight.

## 3. Removing the known Fourier halfweight

Retain the literal finite functions
\[
 \begin{split}
 U_{0,N}(w,X)&=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}
                     H_N(w,r^{-k})p_k(X)/P_k,\\
 C_{1,N}(X,Y)&=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}
                     X^{-j}p_j(X)p_j(Y)/P_j^3,\\
 r&=e^{-2\pi i/N},\quad
 P_j=(r;r)_j,\quad p_j(V)=(rV;r)_j .
 \end{split}                                                   \tag{9}
\]
No triangular cutoff is imposed on the second sum.
The ordinary source plane, at \(X=e^{-2\pi i(x+ih_0)}\)
and \(Y_0=e^{-2\pi iz_0}\), is the complete bound
\[
 |C_{1,N}(X,Y_0)|\,|d_N(Y_0)|^{1/2}
           \le C_\tau N^{B_\tau}e^{N(B_\phi(x)+\tau)} .
                                                               \tag{10}
\]
All low rows, high rows, zero-mode joins, and horizontal and
vertical aliases are already included in this inequality.

The set \(q(K)\subset O_4\) lies strictly inside the regular
lower half-strip. On a fixed neighborhood of that compact set,
[Fourier weight continuation](research_mixed_fourier_weight_continuation.md),
Theorem 1 and Corollary 2, prove
\[
 d_N(e^{-2\pi iz})
 =N^{-1/2}\frac{e^{i\pi z/2}}{\sqrt{2\sin\pi z}}\,
                  e^{-Ng(z)}(1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})).     \tag{11}
\]
The symbol is nonzero; the exact polynomial \(d_N\) is therefore
nonzero there for every sufficiently large \(N\), uniformly at
both parities. Dividing (10) by its positive modulus halfweight
is consequently legitimate and gives
\[
 |C_{1,N}(X,Y_0)|
 \le C_\tau N^{B_\tau}
 e^{N\{\Re\psi(a_0,z_0)+\Re\psi_a(a_0,z_0)d
                                  -\kappa_*d^2+\tau\}}.
                                                               \tag{12}
\]
No analytic square root of a possibly vanishing finite polynomial
has been used, and no lower bound for \(C_1\) was assumed.
The outgoing factor removed here is the known Fourier polynomial,
not a complete signed terminal source.

Original stationarity in the incoming leg says
\[
             \mathcal T_0'(a_0,-2)+\psi_a(a_0,z_0)=0.
                                                               \tag{13}
\]
Multiplying (12) by the whole-cap plane proves the following
complete finite-source statement.

**Theorem 1.** For every \(\tau>0\), uniformly for \(c_0\in K\),
\(x\in P\), and both level parities,
\[
 \begin{split}
 &|U_{0,N}(-2,e^{-2\pi i(x+ih_0)})
           C_{1,N}(e^{-2\pi i(x+ih_0)},e^{-2\pi iz_0})|\\
 &\qquad\le C_\tau N^{B_\tau}
   \exp N\{\Re\mathscr P_{34}(c_0)
                          -2\kappa_*(x-A_0)^2+\tau\}.
 \end{split}                                                   \tag{14}
\]
The same bound on a fixed sufficiently small ordinary-color disk
uses its prescribed arbitrarily small extra rate tolerance.
For real color \(w=-2\cos\theta\), the stronger complete cap
estimate retains a uniform additional loss
\(-c_*(1-\cos\theta)\), \(c_*>0\), by
[endpoint real-color decay, Section 3, equation (14)](research_mixed_endpoint_real_color_decay.md).

All source rows and both parities are inherited literally from
(9)--(12). Fixed derivatives follow on smaller complex
neighborhoods: the ordinary theorem first extends the integer
weighted polynomial \(C_1^2d_N(Y)\), and (11) then removes
its known nonzero factor. The cap uses its original color
neighborhood. This proves a bound for a holomorphic finite
function before Cauchy's formula is applied.

For any fixed \(\delta>0\), (14) gives a uniform exponential
gap on \(|x-A_0|\ge\delta\), after choosing
\(\tau<\kappa_*\delta^2\) and absorbing the polynomial costs.
In particular both ends of \(P\) are separated from \(A_0\)
by at least \(29/500\). This is a paid noncentral incoming
plateau region on the entire following family, not just at the
original ordinary-pair private coordinate.

## 4. Nonzero local packets with fixed incoming contours

For each base \(c_0\in K\), fix the contour
\[
 \mathcal I_N^{c_0}(w,z)
   =N\int_{11/50+ih_0}^{9/20+ih_0}
       U_{0,N}(w,e^{-2\pi ia})
       C_{1,N}(e^{-2\pi ia},e^{-2\pi iz})\,da .
                                                               \tag{15}
\]
It is an exact holomorphic function of \(w,z\); its endpoints
are held fixed when these parameters vary.

**Theorem 2.** On sufficiently small fixed neighborhoods of
\((-2,q(c_0))\), the exact integral (15) has
\[
 \mathcal I_N^{c_0}(w,z)
    =N^{-3/2}\mathcal A(w,z)e^{N\mathcal P(w,z)}
                              (1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})),
                     \qquad\mathcal A\ne0.              \tag{16}
\]
The constants, neighborhood radii, and fixed derivative bounds
can be chosen uniformly for \(c_0\in K\).

*Proof.* Near \((a_0,z_0)\), the two complete finite packets are
\[
 \begin{split}
 U_{0,N}(w,e^{-2\pi ia})
    &=N^{-1}\mathcal B_0(a,w)e^{N\mathcal T_0(a,w)}
                                    (1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})),\\
 C_{1,N}(e^{-2\pi ia},e^{-2\pi iz})
    &=N^{-1}\mathcal B_C(a,z)e^{N\psi(a,z)}
                                    (1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})),
 \end{split}                                                   \tag{17}
\]
with nonzero symbols and the original Gaussian branches.
These are the complete-cap and reduced-disk conditional source
theorems; no main-current approximation is substituted into an
uncontrolled conditional sum.

The negative Hessian of the full original pre-Gram action in
\((u,e,a,p)\) is the principal block of the accretive Hessian
in the all-word cap theorem. The final \(G(q,c)\) is constant
in these four variables. Eliminating \(u,e,p\) gives the scalar
Schur complement
\[
 -\partial_a^2[\mathcal T_0(a,w)+\psi(a,z)] ,
                                                               \tag{18}
\]
whose real part is positive at the center parameters. To check
this directly for a complex accretive matrix, test its quadratic
form on the vector whose eliminated coordinates solve their
linear equations and whose retained coordinate is one. Its
value is the Schur complement, so its real part retains a
positive lower bound. Thus the incoming saddle and its nonzero
Gaussian determinant extend holomorphically.

Theorem 1 removes the rest of the incoming plateau outside a
small fixed interval about \(A_0\). Uniform holomorphic
extension of its strict exterior bound keeps that region paid
when \(w,z\) vary slightly. On the small interval, multiply
the two whole symbols (17). Translate the finite local contour
through the moved saddle, retaining both short endpoint
connectors. At the base parameters those endpoints have a
strict horizontal phase loss by (18); compact continuity keeps
the two connectors exponentially below the moved action.
The one-dimensional Gaussian consequently gives
\[
 \mathcal A(w,z)=
   \frac{\sqrt{2\pi}\,
      \mathcal B_0(a^\#,w)\mathcal B_C(a^\#,z)}
   {\sqrt{-\partial_a^2[
       \mathcal T_0(a,w)+\psi(a,z)]_{a=a^\#}}}\ne0 .
                                                               \tag{19}
\]
The exact power is
\(N\cdot N^{-1}\cdot N^{-1}\cdot N^{-1/2}=N^{-3/2}\).
The incoming orientation is increasing real coordinate.
The original two source packets retain both level parities.
The usual symmetric local Gaussian expansion and nested Cauchy
estimates give (16) with all fixed normalized mixed jets.

All reference images are compact and lie inside the original
source neighborhoods with their strict margins. The negative
Hessian and the symbols have uniform bounds there. A finite
subcover therefore supplies uniform choices for the local
parameter disks and constants in (16). On overlaps, the
original critical equations and their absolute calibration
identify the phase and leading coefficient. The contour
integrals for two different bases are still distinguished as
the exact functions (15); they need not be equal.
\(\square\)

## 5. Remaining first-path issue

Theorems 1--2 use the actual correlated following family and the
complete ordinary conditional source. They are not a promotion
of the old terminal-private theorem to an independent ordinary
box. They also do not apply on the entire larger boundary
circles of \(D_3,D_4\): the typed source input in Section 2
is \(c\in K\subset P_3\) or \(P_4\).

The remaining incoming intervals need their own estimates on
the same pre-Gram action. There is a precise cut mismatch on
the left. The completed ordinary own-four supporting-plane
proof calibrates its lower physical cut at \(t=1/8\), whereas
the already proved coupled left-taper transport starts at
\(t=3/20\). A bound for the whole \(C_1\) does not imply a
bound for an individual real \(3/20\) row or vertical face.
Nor may a reference-dependent horizontal contour be inserted
into a maximum-modulus argument as if it were one fixed
holomorphic remainder. No such substitution is made here.
