# Direct joint control of the terminal-four private left face

Research deduction, 6 November 2026. A saved bare terminal-face reserve
can be used at the original joint trial, without replacing the terminal
phase by its horizontal plane. Flattening the private coordinate then
changes only one of the three nonzero horizontal forces. At the two
saved terminal-four canonical references that force remains negative
and decreases in modulus. The existing factorial and full-interval
forced budgets therefore remain valid. This requires no new private
action, primitive, reference or target evaluation.

## 3. The same three convex costs on the flattened face

Retain the height and coordinate hypotheses of
[the three-cost theorem](research_mixed_terminal_joint_main_curvature_budget.md),
equations (1) and (13). The ordinary index can again be restricted
to \(s_R\ge.22\), since the terminal factor is independent of \(s\).
The terminal index can be restricted to \(t_R\ge.18\):
[selected terminal-four transport](research_mixed_terminal_four_selected_height_completion.md),
equations (7)--(8), includes the private height zero and the real
private cut \(e_0=.27\). This is a pointwise real-target comparison,
not a contour replacement or a claim about a moving saddle.

Write \(g_h(x)=\Re g(x+ih)\) and
\(\phi_h(x;x_*)=g_h(x)-g_h(x_*)-g_h'(x_*)(x-x_*)\).
On the resulting four-dimensional target product,
\[
 -\Re(D_\nu^L)''
 \succeq\operatorname{diag}\left(
       \tfrac{1+\beta}{2}g_{h_b}'',\
       g_{h_s}'',\
       (\nu+\tfrac12)g_H'',\
       g_{h_t}''\right).
 \tag{8}
\]
For clarity, this does not invoke the old main statement's negative
private-height hypothesis at the new real cut. Decompose the ordinary
factor into \(V(s,b)+V(s,a)-g(s)\), and the terminal factor into
\(V(t,a)+V(t,e_0)-g(t)+R_1(e_0)\), with the original imaginary
quadratics, \(V(x,y)=g(x+y)-g(x)-g(y)\), and
\(R_1(e)=S(e)-i\pi(e-e^2)/2\).
The ordinary pair estimates retain their two outer half-reserves.
The pair \(V(t,a)\) is positive semidefinite in negative real
Hessian. So is \(V(t,e_0)\), because \(t_R\ge.18\),
\(|h_t|\le.108\), \(e_0=.27\), and the original cotangent-pair
criterion applies. Restricting the latter matrix to fixed \(e_0\)
keeps its nonnegative \(tt\) entry. The remaining \(R_1(e_0)\)
is constant. Adding the selected and incoming weights proves (8)
without any \(S''(e_0)\) estimate.

Define the exact scalar gains
\[
 \begin{split}
 G_s(q)&=\sup_{x\in[.22,.5]}
                   \{q(x-s_R^*)-\phi_{h_s}(x;s_R^*)\},\\
 G_{a,\nu}(q)&=\sup_{x\in P}
                   \{q(x-A)-(\nu+\tfrac12)\phi_H(x;A)\},\\

                   \{q(x-t_R^*)-\phi_{h_t}(x;t_R^*)\}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{9}
\]
Integrating (8) from the trial in (5), then discarding only the
nonnegative incoming cost with zero force, gives
\[
 \sup D_\nu^L\le T_\nu+U_L+
             G_s(A_s)+G_{a,\nu}(A_{a,\nu})+G_t(A_t+d_L).
 \tag{10}
\]

**Proposition 1.** If
\[
                         A_t<0,\qquad 0\le d_L\le-A_t,
 \tag{11}
\]
any of the existing joint-main three-cost upper budgets transfers
to the private-left face with the additional debit \(U_L\).

For \(q\le0\), convexity permits the last supremum in (9) to be
restricted to \(x\le t_R^*\). On that interval increasing \(q\)
toward zero only decreases its affine term. Hence
\(G_t(A_t+d_L)\le G_t(A_t)\). The \(s,a\) gains and the trial
value \(T_\nu\) are unchanged. This proves the proposition for
both full-\(P\) alternatives simultaneously.

## 4. An elementary logarithmic increment bound

The following rational guards suffice for (11):
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 .304<t_R^*<.308,\qquad .345<e_R^*<.347,\\
 -.066<h_t<-.065,\qquad -.038<h_e<-.037,\qquad A_t<-23/100 .
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{12}
\]
They imply
\[
                              0<d_L<1/5<-A_t.
 \tag{13}
\]

To prove this, put \(r=e^{2\pi h_t}\) and
\(\rho=e^{2\pi(h_t+h_e)}\). Then
\[
 r<2/3,\qquad \rho>1/2,\qquad
 t_R^*+.27\in(.574,.578),\quad t_R^*+e_R^*\in(.649,.655).
 \tag{14}
\]
The new summed real part is closer to \(1/2\), and the new radius
\(r\) is larger than \(\rho\). Both doubled-angle cosines are
negative. Each change strictly increases the squared modulus
\(1+r^2-2r\cos(2\pi x)\), proving \(d_L>0\).

More quantitatively,
\[
 \cos(2\pi(t_R^*+.27))>-9/10,\qquad
 \cos(2\pi(t_R^*+e_R^*))<-11/20.
 \tag{15}
\]
Therefore the original logarithms satisfy
\[
 e^{2d_L}
 =\frac{|1-e^{-2\pi i(t_*+.27)}|^2}
        {|1-e^{-2\pi i(t_*+e_*)}|^2}
 <\frac{1+4/9+(9/5)(2/3)}
         {1+1/4+11/20}
 =\frac{119}{81}<e^{2/5},
 \tag{16}
\]
which proves the upper bound in (13). These are moduli of the
original \(g'\) arguments; no logarithmic lift is replaced.

Here are exact rational witnesses, using \(157/50<\pi<22/7\).
For \(x=2041/5000<.13\pi\),
\[
 1+x+x^2/2+x^3/6-\tfrac32
                    =\frac{2137369921}{750000000000}>0.
 \tag{17}
\]
This gives \(r<2/3\). The bound \(\rho>1/2\) follows from

exact \(e^{17/25}<2\) witness. For
\(y=5809/12500<.148\pi\) and \(z=341/350>.31\pi\),

 \begin{split}
 \tfrac9{10}-(1-y^2/2+y^4/24)
   &=\frac{3538461877040639}{585937500000000000}>0,\\

Finally
\[

## 6. Residual safeguards for any later interval formulation

The proof above uses stationarity only at the already proved reference.
For clarity, on an arbitrary regular Cartesian point let
\[
 F=Q_1+\Phi_1-g(a)-\tfrac\beta2g(b),\qquad \Delta=H-\Im a.
 \tag{21}
\]
The literal lifted-and-flattened value relative to that point is
\[
 T_\nu-\Delta\Im F_a+\Re\{R(e_0;t)-R(e;t)\},
 \tag{22}
\]
and the three nonzero candidate horizontal forces are
\[
 \Re F_s+A_s,\qquad \Re F_a+A_{a,\nu},\qquad
 \Re F_t+A_t+\Re\{g'(t+e_0)-g'(t+e)\}.
 \tag{23}
\]
None of these residual terms can be dropped at a box center.
If a direct centered private-action enclosure is ever used, it must
retain
\[
 R(e_0;t)-R(e;t)
  =(e_0-e)F_e+(e_0-e)^2
       \int_0^1(1-\tau)R_{ee}(e+\tau(e_0-e);t)\,d\tau .
 \tag{24}
\]
The no-private-primitive route (6)--(7) avoids this calculation
entirely. It applies the saved face theorem at the actual reference
and uses full-box endpoint guards only for the elementary increment
bound (13); it imposes no false stationarity on the enclosing box.
