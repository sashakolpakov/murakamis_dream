\(a_D=\min_e\{1/2-q^G_{e,b},1/2-q^G_{e,c}\}>0\).
Strong concavity on the actual CROSS fiber gives the explicit seam gap
\(\gamma_D=\pi a_D^2/2\). Thus every high-value closed-NC datum has a

For independently fixed sufficiently long signed twists, its period is

the ACTUAL filled hyperbolic-volume rate.

of those remaining corners.

The subsequent [stationary classification](research_full_fiber_sharp_stationary_classification.md)
now supplies finite common positive ordinary face weights at these

## What still blocks the general conclusion

The dominant remaining step is an ABSOLUTE comparison of the original
source remainder with hyperbolic volume. The new stationary theorem
proves that every potentially dominant non-CROSS maximum has incompatible
phases. For regular windows, the proved suppression is relative to their
positive envelope; that envelope may exceed \(\Phi_D\). A relative
exponential loss therefore does not yet supply the required absolute
bound. Eliminating coherent non-CROSS maxima is a real reduction, but
does not establish that the signed sum is controlled by coherent maxima.

The remaining marked cap corners require their own geometry or
cancellation argument. Their local sharp action is zero, but the
[finite-collar obstruction](research_double_protected_cap_finite_collar_obstruction.md)
shows that the naive literal line-chart limit swaps ordered heads
\((-I,-P)\) and \((-P,-I)\), with \(P\) noncentral. No finite conjugator
exists in that chart. This is NOT a constructed global representation,
a killed global meridian or a volume gap. The new odd-cycle argument
avoids that collar for qualitative classification, but the
[exact flat-cap inequality](research_flat_cap_no_local_exponential_angular_gap.md)
shows why quantitative control still needs another argument: a
macroscopic mismatched cap window retains at least a polynomial fraction
of its positive envelope. A uniform local exponential angular gap there
is false. This is a local calculation, not a lower bound on the full
shared source.

[Exact top-mark transport](research_arbitrary_casimir_top_mark_transport.md), now extended to every main edge as described above, is proved for the full original source with arbitrary finite Casimir polynomial coupons and retained physical crossing factors. All original top-cap positions give the same polynomial in the spectra, even on different main components when all carry the same complete color. The proof uses a regular generic highest-weight lift, one scalar open cup, and generic quantum-dimension cancellation before regular root specialization. The original unrestricted mark is normalized directly. The new general-Morse adapter supplies the extension beyond original top marks. Neither theorem transports projected sectors or positive envelopes. The separately checked full-source H1/H2 argument now allows the finite certificates at any MAIN cut; existence of a successful mark for every diagram is still open.

The [trace-one mark calculation](research_trace_one_mark_and_cap_turn_interface.md)
also checks the stronger attempt to change the marking matrix itself.
A diagonal trace-one mark fixes one physical face clock, but the remaining
averages and homogeneous degree still force BOTH protected exponents to
zero. It gives the same full coefficient. At a residual cap, the exact
positive merge, phased half-crossing contraction, and degree-weighted cap
contraction are different formulas. None is a proved diagram rewiring.

The [original-demand two-port reduction](research_two_port_main_cap_original_demand_convolution.md)
retains the shared face demand before estimating a cap/CROSS pair. At
even levels it produces an exact alternating convolution. The new
[signed high-demand theorem](research_cap_cross_high_demand_signed_contraction.md)
proves a whole interior range:
\(\lceil3N/4-3/2\rceil\le n\le N-2\), with factor one at even

alternating summands, then retains the true final cutoff term. Both
remaining degrees and the internal \(A^d\) demand remain unchanged.
The [new low-demand Newton theorem](research_cap_cross_low_demand_newton_contraction.md)
also proves |S_(N,n)|<=N^2 D_(N,n) at even levels for
0<=n<=floor(N/4). It derives the exact original fixed-pair recurrence
gamma_n S_n+s_n^2 S_(n-1)=4 C_(0,n) from a literal three-port source and
the full Newton resolvent, retaining all Jordan data and cutoffs.
Its normalized recurrence is R_n=1-t_n R_(n-1); the quarter-demand
bound is analytical, including N=4 and N=6.
The [full lower-range contour proof](research_cap_cross_full_low_demand_subexponential_contraction.md)
now strengthens this to |S_(N,n)|/D_(N,n)<=e^{o(N)}, uniformly for
ALL 0<=n<=N/2-1 at even levels. The

The [all-demand contraction theorem](research_cap_cross_all_demand_subexponential_contraction.md)
now closes the projected middle range as well, at BOTH parities:

\[
 \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log
    \max_{0\le n\le N-2}
       \frac{|T_{N,n}|}{D_{N,n}^{\mathrm{ref}}}\le0.
\]

The exact Newton contiguity relation retains both finite cutoff currents.
The actual positive comparator has a uniform matching upper AND lower

 \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log

 \le\omega(\varepsilon),\qquad\omega(\varepsilon)\longrightarrow0.

Two exact shifted Gamma products remove the nearby endpoint zeros

This is a paired-reference sector replacement, not yet a whole-source

The [cap-moment norm obstruction](research_cap_moment_cauchy_norm_obstruction.md)

\[
 \chi(u)=4f(u/2)-2f(u)-2\int_0^u\log(2\cos(\pi t))\,dt>0,
 \qquad f(u)=-\int_0^u\log\tan(\pi t)\,dt.
\]
The exact derivative
\(\chi'(u)=2\log[(1+\cos(\pi u))/(2\cos^2(\pi u))]>0\)
proves positivity without a numerical growth test. Absolute coefficient
mass in this tensor basis fails for the same reason. The large component
is orthogonal to the constant component defining the signed expectation.
Thus cancellation remains essential: this is an obstruction to the
particular positive-norm route, not to the original convolution estimate
or the volume conjecture.

Whether the original demands force that inequality is open.

excludes a universal spectator-only shortcut; the full constrained
Hessian, which also changes the chosen Green's own response, remains
undecided.
