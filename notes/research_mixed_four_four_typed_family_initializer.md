# The complete 44 initializer on the four typed following families

Private written source-domain deduction, 10 November 2026. The complete
two-letter polynomial \(f_{44,N}\) has its original nonzero packet near
the compact last-leg image of each of the four closed typed following
families. The external following leg need not be realized as part of a
longer open word.

The new step is to check the coupled-reference premises of the complete
first-period proof on those families. An enclosing coordinate rectangle,
or continuity from some realized references, would not suffice. In
particular the sharper attached height and the selected first-four
certificate require the original endpoint/ordinary compatibility.
No new root, action, certificate, or finite sum is evaluated here.

 \tag{3}

\[

Put \(q_N=e^{-2\pi i/N}\), \(P_j=(q_N;q_N)_j\), and
\(p_j(X)=(q_NX;q_N)_j\). The fixed polynomial is
\[
\begin{split}
 U_{1,N}(w,X)&=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}H_N(w,q_N^{-j})p_j(X)/P_j^2,\\
 C_{1,N}(X,Y)&=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}X^{-j}p_j(X)p_j(Y)/P_j^3,\\
 f_{44,N}(w,Y)
 &=N\operatorname{CT}_X
   [U_{1,N}(w,X)d_N(X)(1+X^{-N})C_{1,N}(X,Y)]\\
 &=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}
                  U_{1,N}(w,q_N^j)C_{1,N}(q_N^j,Y)/P_j .
\end{split} \tag{5}
\]
This is the literal normalization of
[pointwise prefix replacement](research_mixed_four_four_pointwise_prefix_replacement.md),
equations (2)--(3). There is one positive density \(N\,da\), no
additional factor two, no outgoing halfweight in \(f_{44,N}\),
and no external \(\kappa_N\) scalar. Both level parities and all
rows through \(N-1\) are retained. The polynomial itself is
independent of \(c\).

For each reference \(c\), the plateau approximant is
\[
 I_N^c(w,\zeta)=
 N\int_{P+i\Im a(c)}
 U_{1,N}(w,e^{-2\pi i t})d_N(e^{-2\pi i t})
 C_{1,N}(e^{-2\pi i t},e^{-2\pi i\zeta})\,dt,
 \qquad P=[11/50,9/20].
 \tag{6}
\]
Its endpoints and height are fixed while \(w,\zeta\) vary.
Only this approximant, not (5), depends on the reference label.

## 4. Uniform remainder and the nonzero initializer

**Proposition 1.** For each \(\tau>0\), there are constants
\(C_\tau,A_\tau\), independent of \(c\in\mathfrak K\), such that,
for both parities and \(y\in P\),
\[
\begin{split}
 R_N^c(w,\zeta)&=
 f_{44,N}(w,e^{-2\pi i\zeta})-I_N^c(w,\zeta),\\
 |R_N^c(w,y+ik(c))|\,|d_N(e^{-2\pi i(y+ik(c))})|^{1/2}
 &\le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
           e^{N\{\Lambda_c(y)-\gamma_0+\tau\}} .
\end{split} \tag{22}
\]
It holds for all real ordinary colors \(w\in[-2,2]\), and on a
common sufficiently small fixed ordinary disk about \(-2\), with
an independently reserved allowance.

*Proof.* The source checks in Section 3 pay the fourteen currents.
Their lengths are uniformly bounded because \(h(c)\) is in (10).
Their smallest gap is bounded below by (16). The ordinary source
domains, zero-safe endpoint products and fixed finite contours in
the inputs are compact, with their stated strict margins.
Their asymptotic tolerances are uniform on these domains.

More explicitly, the map (2) is holomorphic near the compact
following sets, and its strict coordinate, height and source-separation
bounds above have common slack on the closed image. Membership of
the external leg in a closed typed set need not be strict; the
uniform estimates there are taken relative to that set. The selected finite covers
already have uniform strict margins on their retained sets.
The written supporting-plane and subsequent elementary estimates
use the same closed parameter bounds. Where a proof chooses a
small cut collar or an ordinary disk after a strict comparison,
one first chooses it on a neighborhood of that parameter and
then takes a finite cover of the compact image. The finitely many
choices may be decreased together, with allowances distributed
before summing currents. Their maximum thresholds and polynomial
powers are finite. This supplies common constants in (22).
It does not assume that every nearby independent reference is
inverse-feasible or that realized words are dense.

The all-real color estimates in the complete inputs and their
finite-degree ordinary continuation give a common disk. No
factor depending on a later block is divided out. \(\square\)

The normalized phase is
\[
 \mathcal P_{44}(w,\zeta)=
 \operatorname{stat}_{t}
 [\mathcal T_1(t,w)+\psi_1(t,\zeta)-g(t)].
 \tag{23}
\]
At complete color its absolute normalization is
\[
 \mathcal P_{44}(-2,q_{44}(c))
                     =H_{44}(c)-G(q_{44}(c),c).
 \tag{24}
\]
There is no last-three correction, since the last flag is one.
Its outgoing allocated action is
\(L_{44}=\mathcal P_{44}(-2,\cdot)-g/2\). Equations (17)--(18)
give
\[
 \Re L_{44}(z(c))=M_c,\qquad L_{44}'(z(c))=p_c.
 \tag{25}
\]
This identifies the comparison in (22) with the original action,
including its constant, rather than with a separately chosen
primitive of a logarithmic derivative.

**Theorem 2.** There are a complex disk \(B\) about \(-2\), an
open neighborhood \(V\) of the compact set \(q_{44}(\mathfrak K)\),
and single holomorphic functions \(\mathcal P_{44},\mathcal A_{44}\)
on \(B\times V\), with \(\mathcal A_{44}\ne0\), such that
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 f_{44,N}(w,e^{-2\pi i\zeta})
 =N^{-5/2}\mathcal A_{44}(w,\zeta)
       e^{N\mathcal P_{44}(w,\zeta)}
                    \{1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})\}.
 \quad} \tag{26}
\]
The estimate is uniform on a fixed smaller neighborhood of
\(\{-2\}\times q_{44}(\mathfrak K)\), at both parities.
Every fixed derivative of the normalized relative error is
\(O(N^{-1})\) there after shrinking once more.
The action and coefficient are exactly the retained-prefix ones.

*Proof of replacement.* The union of horizontal segments
\[
             \{y+ik(c):y\in P,\ c\in\mathfrak K\}
 \tag{27}
\]
is compactly inside \(0<\Re\zeta<1/2,\ \Im\zeta<0\).
[Fourier-weight continuation](research_mixed_fourier_weight_continuation.md),
Section 5 and Theorem 1, supplies on a common neighborhood of
(27) its zero-free relative expansion
\[
 d_N(e^{-2\pi i\zeta})
  =N^{-1/2}\mathcal D(\zeta)e^{-Ng(\zeta)}
               (1+O_{\rm hol}(N^{-1})),\qquad \mathcal D\ne0.
 \tag{28}
\]
Its positivity criterion for \(-\Re g\) applies to the whole
compact lower strip in (27), not only to the smaller explicitly
displayed Fourier rectangle. Thus the sole weight removed from
(22) is proved nonzero, uniformly. This gives the unweighted
rate \(\Lambda_c(y)+\Re g(y+ik(c))/2-\gamma_0+\tau\).

For fixed \(c\), \(R_N^c\) is a polynomial of degree at most
\(N-1\) in \(Y=e^{-2\pi i\zeta}\), since the integration limits
in (6) are fixed. Apply
[horizontal complex extension](research_mixed_horizontal_complex_extension.md)
to this polynomial family. The reference \(c\) and incoming
color \(w\) are compact labels; that theorem requires no
holomorphic dependence on a label. The reference points
\(z_0(c)\in(.24,.36)\) stay a fixed distance inside \(P\).
Equation (25), uniform continuity of the analytic rates on a
compact neighborhood, and an allowance smaller than a fixed
part of \(\gamma_0\) give common radii \(r_w,r_z>0\) and
\(\delta>0\) such that
\[
 |e^{-N\mathcal P_{44}(w,\zeta)}R_N^c(w,\zeta)|
          \le C e^{-\delta N},\qquad
 |w+2|<r_w,\quad |\zeta-z(c)|<r_z .
 \tag{29}
\]
For example the budgets may be chosen to leave
\(\delta=\gamma_0/8\) after absorbing fixed polynomial factors.
This is a uniform family of local exponential replacements; it
does not give a global remainder with one reference-independent
plateau height.

The retained-prefix theorem already proves the local packet for
(6), uniformly over each compact following family. Its power is
\[
       N^{-3/2}\,N^{-1}\,N^{-1/2}\,N\,N^{-1/2}=N^{-5/2}.
 \tag{30}
\]
The factors are the complete cap, conditional kernel, internal
diagonal weight, exact outer density and final Gaussian. Adding
(29) preserves this packet and its coefficient.

*Proof that the local packets form one holomorphic packet.*
The pre-Gram theorem makes \(q_{44}\) injective and unramified on
\(\Omega=D_3^\circ\cup D_4^\circ\), with agreement of the original
branches on the disk overlap. Hence (24) is a single holomorphic
function on \(q_{44}(\Omega)\). The compact image in Theorem 2
lies inside this open set.

With \(\zeta\) fixed, the original internal stationary system
has invertible, strictly accretive negative Hessian. The final
Gram term in (3) is constant in these internal variables.
The implicit-function branches for \(w\) near \(-2\) therefore
continue the same critical coordinates. On overlaps they agree
by uniqueness in the original convex critical chamber. Compactness
permits a common \(w\)-disk and a neighborhood \(V\) of the
compact image. Thus (23) extends (24) to one
\(\mathcal P_{44}(w,\zeta)\), without introducing patchwise
action constants.

Retained-prefix equation (25), in the two-letter case, gives
the coefficient
\[
 \mathcal A_{44}
 =\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\mathcal B^{\rm cap}_1
              \mathcal B^C_1\,\mathcal D(t)}
              {\sqrt{-\Phi_{tt}}}
 \quad\hbox{at the same stationary coordinates},\qquad
 \Phi=\mathcal T_1+\psi_1-g .
 \tag{31}
\]
The complete cap and conditional symbols and \(\mathcal D\)
are the original nonzero ones. The Gaussian square root is the
original real-oriented branch on the accretive Hessian domain;
it cannot acquire an independent sign on a new chart. Therefore
(31) agrees on all overlaps and is a single nonzero holomorphic
coefficient. The different horizontal approximants \(I_N^c\)
have this same local symbol because their saddles, orientation
and source symbols agree; their reference-dependent remote
pieces are exponentially small. A finite subcover and (29)
give the stated uniform normalized error for the fixed polynomial
(5). Cauchy's formula proves the derivative assertions. \(\square\)

## 5. Exact scope

The uniformity in Theorem 2 is over neighborhoods of the compact
image of the four complete typed following families for the
single fixed prefix \(44\). It is stronger than a packet at each
realized longer-word reference, but it is not a statement about
constants uniform in arbitrary word length. No such length
uniformity is needed or asserted: the polynomial in (5) has
exactly two letters.

The deduction neither covers an arbitrary independent outgoing
rectangle nor asserts the finite packet on all of \(q_{44}(\Omega)\).
The global pre-Gram sheet on that larger set identifies branches;
