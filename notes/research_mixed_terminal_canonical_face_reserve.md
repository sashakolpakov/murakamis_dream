# Saved real-face reserves at four canonical terminal references

Research extraction protocol, 6 November 2026. This uses only exact
fractions, hashes and previously certified records. It does not evaluate
an action, root, logarithm, optimizer or source. The proposed extraction
has not yet been run. A failed threshold remains a failed comparison;
a missing numerical maximum remains unresolved.

## 1. Reference association and complete cell coverage

Use the four genuine canonical references in the unchanged
[joint-main diagnostic](../repro/results/research-mixed-terminal-joint-main-budget-diagnostic.json),
SHA256 `2b6e9b17d1a16071c545c0ba9f9bb2502e1f18c5009fafc1212fd326d2a621f9`.
Their original-coordinate associations were checked in the
[saved-data audit](../repro/results/research-mixed-terminal-joint-main-budget-saved-audit.json).
The case labels are \((\beta,\epsilon)\in\{0,1\}^2\):
\(\epsilon\) is the terminal own flag; \(\beta\) is the outside
ordinary input flag. In every case the selected terminal input
\(a_*=A+ic\) has type four. Its original endpoint-map input flag is
therefore **one**, independently of \(\beta\).

Let \(R_a\) be the entire saved Cartesian enclosure of \(a_*\),
with its exact directed rational endpoints. Reconstruct the original
four endpoint trees from the immutable
[critical-localization record](../repro/results/research-mixed-open-word-critical-localization.json)
and verify their equality with its
[original replay](../repro/results/research-mixed-open-word-critical-localization-replay.json).
Their normalized boxes start at \([-1,1]^2\); a split at depth \(d\)
uses axis \(d\bmod2\), with the original right-child-first stack order.
The physical parameter is
\[
 a=(3/10-13i/250)+(8/125)(X+iY).
 \tag{1}
\]
Use exactly the own-\(\epsilon\), neighbor-one leaves. Select **every**
closed leaf rectangle meeting \(R_a\), including boundary contacts.
No choice is made by midpoint or by a preferred leaf. The original
partition has 1,702 such own-three leaves and 909 such own-four leaves.
The own-type source list also includes neighbor-zero leaves first;
these preceding indices must be retained in the source-job numbering.

The checker verifies the exact original partition, geometric excluded
leaves, and that the full \(R_a\) lies strictly inside the input disk.
It requires both enclosure widths to be positive, then verifies that
intersections with the selected leaves cover the
whole enclosure, by exact area and inclusion. Original exclusion leaves
cannot meet this strictly interior enclosure. Shared boundaries are
retained, and the complete closed partition supplies their coverage.

The canonical terminal coordinates satisfy the same original zero-lift
endpoint equations. Uniqueness in the original open product identifies
them with the endpoint graph used by these source records. For own four,
the source domain is the actual oriented, inverse-feasible subset from
[the coupled continuation](research_mixed_terminal_four_coupled_source_continuation.md),
not every point of the full type-four disk. The canonical suffix is
admitted because its original ordinary equations reconstruct its
preceding \(s,b\) as the inverse legs, with the saved reduced-disk and
oriented guards of the
[horizontal-envelope reference diagnostic](research_mixed_terminal_horizontal_envelope_diagnostic.md).
This is canonical-reference existence, not finite-word realization.
No independent points in the surrounding Cartesian box are asserted to
satisfy those coupled restrictions.

For own three, use the completed mixed-face and raw-half-face

- The [raw-half replay](../repro/results/research-mixed-terminal-three-right-face-replay.json)
  has one family per original cell: \(t=.5\) real and the whole real
  private interval. It controls the positive right-triangle entropy

For own four, use the completed
[source replay](../repro/results/research-mixed-terminal-four-source-replay.json):
1,911 original cells, 21 families per cell. The eight real faces are
0--7, with private cuts \(.27,.49\); twelve alias families are 8--19;
family 20 is the raw half-transform face, retaining both real private
variables \(e\in[.27,.49]\), \(\xi\in[.1,.269]\). All 21 family labels are
checked, although only 0--7 and 20 are selected for this extraction.

A job is indexed by \(n i+f\), where \(i\) is the original own-type
cell index, \(n=8,1,21\), and \(f\) is the family index. The checker
verifies equality of the complete author/replay trees, summary order,
source/exclusion counts and all current input bindings. Every saved
`maximum_upper` encloses the largest certified source-leaf upper in
that job. As in the existing
[type-three](research_mixed_terminal_three_alias_reserve.md) and
[type-four](research_mixed_terminal_four_alias_reserve.md) extractions,
a finite displayed Arb ball \([m\mathbin{+/-}r]\) supplies the exact
rational upper \(m+r\). Only this outward ball display is accepted; a
bare rounded decimal is not assigned an unproved zero radius. No
shortened decimal summary is substituted.

For each canonical case and each of its nine required families, take
the maximum of these outward bounds over **all** intersecting original
cells. A null maximum denotes a wholly excluded source job, not a
numerical bound. This extraction conservatively marks that family
unresolved if any intersecting job has a null maximum; it does not use
a coupled exclusion to discard part of \(R_a\). A non-null maximum
bounds the required actual reference even when other descendants in
its original cell are geometrically excluded, by the completed replay
and the canonical-reference association above.

The proposed strict test is
\[
                  U_f<-\frac{11}{200}.
 \tag{2}
\]
All rational maxima, attaining jobs, individual contributing bounds,
null jobs and signed excesses \(U_f+11/200\) are saved regardless of
outcome. There is no subdivision, replacement, replay or automatic
follow-up.

## 3. A stronger bare reserve survives the height change

Write \(B_0\) for the original bare horizontal terminal plane, and
\(B_T\) for its allocated version using \(P_\epsilon-g/2\).
Horizontal convexity of \(\Re g\) at the negative reference height
implies
\[
 B_0(a_*;y)-\tfrac12\Re g(y+ic)\le B_T(a_*;y).
 \tag{4}
\]
Thus any stronger bare source gap survives the original half-allocation.
The following transfers preserve that same gap for both complete
selected halfweight alternatives and every \(0\le H\le.014\):

1. At real transform cuts, the original real-row selected-height
   inequality is pointwise and costs zero after allocation. It is
   independent of the private coordinate, so applies also to corners.
2. At a real private cut with transform on its original main height,
   the complex selected-height inequality costs zero when \(t_R\ge.18\).
   If \(.15\le t_R<.18\), first increase \(t_R\) to .18 at the selected
   incoming height, leaving both index heights fixed. The original
   positive transform derivative proves this increases the phase for
   either private cut. The resulting target is still in the same
   private-cut source family. Apply zero-cost transport there, then
   the stronger bound for that family.
3. The physical right-triangle majorant transports to its real
   half-transform anchor with no added allocated height cost. Its
   internal entropy/private variables remain in the original source
   comparison, and no coherent row is split without its positive
   majorant justification.

These are precisely the arguments in
[selected terminal-three completion](research_mixed_terminal_three_selected_height_completion.md),
Section 3 and its physical complement, and
[selected terminal-four completion](research_mixed_terminal_four_selected_height_completion.md),
Sections 2--3. They use the original internal reference heights.
In particular there is **no additional .041 debit** for these nine
families. That debit belongs to unrestricted complex transform alias
endpoints, which are not being extracted here.

Consequently, if (2) passes for all nine families of one canonical
case, each of its eight real-cut profiles and its positive entropy
majorant is below \(B_T-11/200\) at the selected height. At \(H=.01\),
the [reserved-plane transfer](research_mixed_terminal_reserved_plane_transfer.md)
then yields a strict negative joined comparison, provided its original
saved guards hold. More generally (3) proves the joined upper
\(U_f+\max(E_{0,r},E_{1,r})\), retaining both complete Fourier
alternatives. The record retains this exact excess to quantify any
resulting gap. This does not by itself change the main-profile
height or combine branch bounds at different heights; it makes no
uniform-reference-domain or whole-finite-source conclusion.

## 4. Fixed implementation protocol

The standalone standard-library checker is
[check_mixed_terminal_canonical_face_reserve.py](../experiments/check_mixed_terminal_canonical_face_reserve.py).
It binds this proof, all source author/replay pairs, the original root
partition/replay, the four-reference diagnostic/audit, and the source
and transport proofs. It verifies all inherited hash maps before using
any numerical endpoint and repeats the checks before exclusive output.
The only arithmetic is rational partition, intersection and bound
comparison. It imports no old module. Four cases and nine required
families per case are fixed; the input partition and source families
are immutable. A completed extraction may report insufficient reserve
or unresolved jobs and still preserves every comparison in its output.

Normal invocation after review uses a fresh output path:

```sh
python3 experiments/check_mixed_terminal_canonical_face_reserve.py --output repro/results/research-mixed-terminal-canonical-face-reserve.json
```
