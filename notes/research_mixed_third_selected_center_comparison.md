## 3. The two parameter responses cancel in one centered value

Fix one of the certified center references \(r^0\) from Section 1
for each triple. Set \(p_L^0=F_a(r^0)\), \(p_R^0=F_b(r^0)\).
The exact centered compensation is
\[
 C_0(a,c)=\tfrac12\Re\{(p_L-p_L^0)\overline{a-a_0}
                         +(p_R-p_R^0)\overline{b_*-b_*^0}\}.
 \tag{5}
\]
It is the same for every target, index label and Fourier alternative.
The anchor in (5) is the actual canonical root enclosed by the saved
certificate, not the rounded Newton proposal.

**Lemma 2.** Throughout (4), \(C_0\le0\).

*Proof.* The internal derivatives \(F_u,F_z,F_v\) vanish at both
\(r\) and \(r^0\), while the two boundary derivatives are
\(p_L,p_R\). Put \(d=r-r^0\). Symmetry of the holomorphic Hessian
gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 2C_0
 &=\Re\{(F'(r)-F'(r^0))\cdot\bar d\}\\
 &=\int_0^1\Re\{\bar d^{\,T}F''(r^0+td)d\}\,dt\le0.
 \end{split}
 \tag{6}
\]
The straight segment stays inside the common convex product. The
real part of its quadratic form equals the sum of the quadratic
forms of \(\Re F''\) on \(\Re d\) and \(\Im d\); the imaginary
symmetric cross terms cancel. This proves the sign. \(\square\)

For an incoming main index put \(\lambda_u=1\), otherwise zero;
for an outgoing main index put \(\lambda_v=1\), otherwise zero.
In the order \((a,u,z,v,b)\), define
\[
 \Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda_u,0,\lambda_v,1),\qquad
 h=(0,0,H,0,0),\qquad
 w=x+ih+\tfrac12\Lambda(r-\bar r).
 \tag{7}
\]
At a cut, its real index is fixed to the original cut value. Use
formal constant-coordinate substitutions \(\iota\) in the target phase
\(\widehat\Phi(w)=F(\iota(w))+(1-\nu)g(w_z)+2\pi i\nu w_z\),
so its inactive rows and columns vanish. Then the original defect is
\[
 D=\Re\{\widehat\Phi(w)-F(r)-p_L(w_a-a)-p_R(w_b-b_*)\}.
 \tag{8}
\]
Since \(F_u=F_z=F_v=0\), this is precisely the stationary-reference
defect, including at the real cuts. Both boundary coefficients in
(7) equal one; only the selected coordinate and inactive indices
have zero coefficients.

For either complex parameter \(\zeta=a\) or \(c\), the
[stationary signed-compensation identity](research_stationary_reference_signed_boundary_compensation.md)
gives
\[
 \Delta_\zeta(D-C_0)
 =-\Re\{(\Lambda\partial_\zeta r)^T
             \widehat\Phi''(w)\Lambda\overline{\partial_\zeta r}\}
 \ge0.
 \tag{9}
\]
The reference is holomorphic in both parameters. Its original
boundary responses are exactly the Laplacian of (5), and the
remaining sign is the full labeled target concavity. No reference
second derivative or derivative of a chosen numerical target occurs.

**Proposition 3.** If, for every remaining label and every fixed real
target, \(D-C_0\le-\delta\) on
\(\partial K_{3+\alpha}\times\partial K_{3+\sigma}\), then
\[
                      D\le-\delta
              \quad\hbox{throughout (4)}.
 \tag{10}
\]

*Proof.* Apply the maximum principle successively in the two
complex parameters to the separately subharmonic function in (9).
Both boundaries include all their clipping segments, as well as their
circle arcs. Then use \(C_0\le0\). The target and label are held
fixed during these maximum-principle arguments. \(\square\)

 \tag{11}
