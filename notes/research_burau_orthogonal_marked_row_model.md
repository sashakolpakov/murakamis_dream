# An orthogonal two-register model for the marked Burau source

Private written derivation, 22 November 2026. Each three-variable cyclic
crossing space in the exact marked-source formula has an explicit
\(N^2\)-dimensional observable row module. This is an actual orthogonal
subspace of the dual grid space. Its local generators have norms at most
\(2,1,1\), its initial row has norm one, and its final source column has
norm exactly \(N\). Tensoring the local models reduces the source
presentation from \(N^{3k}\) to \(N^{2k}\) coordinates, with all ordered
operators, phases and the original closing retained.

No lower bound for the resulting selected inverse element is proved.

## 1. The source that will be compressed

Keep the definitions of the [exact marked source bridge](research_burau_cyclic_marked_source_bridge.md):
\[
 N\ge2,\quad d=N-1,\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad Q=q^2,\quad z=Q^{-1}.
 \tag{1}
\]
For a physical column-operator braid word \(W\) on \(b\) heads, with
\(k\) crossing occurrences and writhe \(w\), use the reversed list with
every crossing sign changed to form its auxiliary Burau product.
The signs below are the signs in that auxiliary list. Its specified
nilpotent operator on the \(3k\)-variable grid is \(C_N\), with
\[
 L=k(N-1)+1,\qquad C_N^L=0,\qquad
 K_N(W)=\zeta_N\ell_N(I-C_N)^{-1}\mathbf1,\qquad
             \zeta_N=Q^{(b-1+w)/2}.
 \tag{2}
\]
The closure is assumed to be a knot, so the exponent is integral.
The row \(\ell_N\) evaluates \(x_j=y_j=z,u_j=1\) at every occurrence.
The source comparison (2) includes its original framing and tail pivot.

For one crossing, the grid function space is
\(\mathcal F=\mathbb C[(\mu_N)^3]\), with its unweighted Euclidean norm.
Point evaluations are orthonormal rows in its dual. Put
\(\ell f=f(z,z,1)\). The local operators are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 a_+&=(u-y\tau_x^{-1})\tau_y^{-1},&
 b_+&=u^2,&c_+&=x\tau_y^{-2}\tau_u^{-1},\\
 a_-&=(\tau_y-x^{-1})\tau_x^{-1}\tau_u,&
 b_-&=u^2,&c_-&=y^{-1}\tau_x^{-1}\tau_u ,
\end{aligned}
 \tag{3}
\]
where \(\tau_x f(x,y,u)=f(Qx,y,u)\). In this metric \(b_\pm,c_\pm\)
are unitary and \(\|a_\pm\|\le2\), since each \(a_\pm\) is a difference
of two unitary phase-permutation operators.

Let \(\mathcal S_\pm\) be the unital algebra generated by a local
triple, and consider its actual observable row space
\[
                       \mathcal R_\pm=\ell\mathcal S_\pm.
 \tag{4}
\]
The construction below proves \(\dim\mathcal R_\pm=N^2\) at every
level, including even and composite \(N\). Right multiplication is
the relevant action on these rows.

## 2. The positive crossing: orthogonal PBW rows

The local relations are
\[
 ab=ba,\qquad ac=Qca,\qquad bc=Q^2cb,\qquad
                 a^N=0,\quad c^N=I,\quad \ell b=\ell .
 \tag{5}
\]
Here and in this section the sign is positive. Normal ordering shows
that (4) is spanned by
\[
                  e_{r,l}=\ell c^r a^l,
                  \qquad 0\le r,l<N.
 \tag{6}
\]
Indeed a monomial can be ordered with all \(b\)'s at the left; their
powers then disappear against \(\ell\), with their commutation phases
retained. The bounds on \(a,c\) give the displayed ranges.

These spanning rows are orthogonal in the actual grid dual. To see this,
expand
\[
 a^l=\left[\prod_{j=0}^{l-1}
                (u-Q^{-j}y\tau_x^{-1})\right]\tau_y^{-l}.
 \tag{7}
\]
The two factors \(u\) and \(y\tau_x^{-1}\) in the product commute.
Its coefficient at the latter's power \(h\) is
\[
          (-1)^h Q^{-h(h-1)/2}{l\brack h}_{Q^{-1}}.
 \tag{8}
\]
All Gaussians here mean their standard-base polynomial values.
Since \(c^r=x^r\tau_y^{-2r}\tau_u^{-r}\), the support of \(e_{r,l}\)
consists of points
\[
        (x,y,u)=(zQ^{-h},\,zQ^{-2r-l},\,Q^{-r}),
                       \qquad 0\le h\le l .
 \tag{9}
\]
Every coefficient in (8) is multiplied only by a unit phase. Distinct
\((r,l)\) have disjoint supports: \(u\) determines \(r\), and then
\(y\) determines \(l\). The coefficient with \(h=0\) is nonzero.
Consequently the \(N^2\) rows in (6) are independent and orthogonal.
Their norms are
\[
 \|e_{r,l}\|^2=w_l^2,\qquad
 w_l^2=\sum_{h=0}^l\left|{l\brack h}_{Q}\right|^2>0.
 \tag{10}
\]
The norm is independent of \(r\), since \(Q^{-1}=\overline Q\).

Define the orthonormal rows \(f_{r,l}=e_{r,l}/w_l\).
Their right actions are exactly
\[
\begin{aligned}
 f_{r,l}a_+
   &=\frac{w_{l+1}}{w_l}f_{r,l+1}\quad(l<d),&
 f_{r,d}a_+&=0,\\
 f_{r,l}b_+&=Q^{-2r}f_{r,l},&
 f_{r,l}c_+&=Q^l f_{r+1,l}.
\end{aligned}
 \tag{11}
\]
The \(r\) index is cyclic modulo \(N\). For example,
\(c^ra^lb=Q^{-2r}bc^ra^l\), whereas
\(c^ra^lc=Q^lc^{r+1}a^l\); these prove the phases in (11).
The initial marked row is \(f_{0,0}=\ell\).
The two latter actions are unitary and
\[
                         0<\frac{w_{l+1}}{w_l}\le2.
 \tag{12}
\]
The upper bound follows at once from the actual norm bound for \(a_+\)
and (11), without estimating an inverse basis change.

### The exact terminal column and its norm

Evaluating these orthonormal rows on the constant function gives
the known column
\[
 H^+_{r,l}
   =f_{r,l}\mathbf1
   =\frac{Q^{-r(l+1)}}{w_l}
                 \prod_{j=0}^{l-1}(1-Q^{-1-r-j}).
 \tag{13}
\]
In particular this entry is zero if \(r+l\ge N\).
It retains its displayed phase rather than only its modulus.

There is no exponential cost hidden in the norm of this column.
Put
\[
 P_l(T)=\prod_{j=1}^{l}(1-Q^jT)
       =\sum_{h=0}^l(-1)^hQ^{h(h+1)/2}{l\brack h}_Q T^h.
 \tag{14}
\]
The degree \(l\) is below \(N\). The finite identity
\(\sum_{r=0}^{N-1}Q^{r(h-h')}=N\delta_{h,h'}\) therefore gives
\[
 \sum_{r=0}^{N-1}|H^+_{r,l}|^2
   =\frac1{w_l^2}\sum_{r=0}^{N-1}|P_l(Q^r)|^2=N.
 \tag{15}
\]
The equality of moduli in (15) uses
\(|1-Q^{-r-j}|=|1-Q^{r+j}|\). Summing over \(l\) proves
\[
                              \|H^+\|=N.
 \tag{16}
\]
Thus the exact factorial products in (13) are normalized by their
actual orthogonal row norms, not by a knot-dependent scalar.

## 3. The negative crossing: an invariant coordinate surface

For the negative operators, the ideal \((xu-z)\) of the grid function
algebra is preserved by every operator in (3). The shifts in
\(\tau_x^{-1}\tau_u\) preserve \(xu\), and \(\tau_y\) does not change it;
the multiplication factors also preserve this ideal.
The marked point belongs to the surface \(xu=z\).

Its \(N^2\) point rows are an orthonormal family:
\[
 \xi_{s,h}f=f(zQ^{-s},zQ^h,Q^s),\qquad
                           s,h\in\mathbb Z/N.
 \tag{17}
\]
Direct substitution in (3) gives the full right actions
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \xi_{s,h}a_-
    &=\xi_{s+1,h+1}-z^{-1}Q^s\xi_{s+1,h},\\
 \xi_{s,h}b_-&=Q^{2s}\xi_{s,h},\\
 \xi_{s,h}c_-&=z^{-1}Q^{-h}\xi_{s+1,h}.
\end{aligned}
 \tag{18}
\]
No variable has been set to \(u=1\) during transport: its actual value
is \(Q^s\). In particular the phase from the \(u\)-shift remains in
the explicit dynamics.

The span in (17) is exactly \(\mathcal R_-\). It contains \(\ell=\xi_{0,0}\)
and is right invariant by (18), which gives one inclusion.
For the other, examine \(e^-_{r,l}=\ell c_-^r a_-^l\), \(0\le r,l<N\).
Its support is
\[
                   \{\xi_{r+l,h}:0\le h\le l\}.
 \tag{19}
\]
The coefficient of \(\xi_{r+l,l}\) is \(z^{-r}\ne0\): it comes from
choosing the \(\tau_y\) term at every step. Fixing \(s=r+l\) modulo \(N\)
and increasing \(l=0,\ldots,d\) gives a triangular system on the
\(N\) distinct \(h\)-values. Hence these rows span every \(\xi_{s,h}\),
proving equality and dimension \(N^2\).

The initial row is \(\xi_{0,0}\) and the terminal column is particularly
simple:
\[
                  H^-_{s,h}=\xi_{s,h}\mathbf1=1,\qquad
                                      \|H^-\|=N.
 \tag{20}
\]
The last two actions in (18) are unitary; the first is a difference
of two unitary operators and has norm at most two.

## 4. Local singular values

Both local \(a\)-operators have rank \(N(N-1)\), and their nonzero
singular values have polynomial lower bounds. These bounds concern
the individual local generators, not a polynomial in them.

For the positive model, its nonzero singular values are the weights
in (11), each repeated \(N\) times. They satisfy
\[
                 \frac1N\le\frac{w_{l+1}}{w_l}\le2.
 \tag{21}
\]
To prove the lower bound, write \(P_{l+1}=(1-\alpha T)P_l\),
\(|\alpha|=1\), in (14). Recovering the first \(l+1\) coefficients
of \(P_l\) from those of \(P_{l+1}\) uses the triangular matrix
with entries \(\alpha^{i-j}\), \(0\le j\le i\le l\).
Its operator norm is at most \(l+1\), by its row and column sums.
Thus \(w_l\le(l+1)w_{l+1}\), proving (21).

For the negative model, the block \(s\longmapsto s+1\) of \(a_-\)
is a cyclic shift in \(h\) minus the scalar \(z^{-1}Q^s\), which is
an \(N\)-th root of unity. Its singular values are
\[
                   2\sin(\pi j/N),\qquad 0\le j<N .
 \tag{22}
\]
These blocks have orthogonal input and output subspaces. Hence the
nonzero singular values of the full \(a_-\) are at least
\(2\sin(\pi/N)\ge4/N\), and at most two.
This proves the rank assertion and all the stated local bounds,
including \(N=2\).

## 5. Source-preserving compression and operator order

For either sign, let \(V_\sigma\) be the \(N^2\)-by-\(N^3\) matrix
whose rows are the orthonormal rows just constructed:
\[
 V_\sigma V_\sigma^*=I,\qquad
 \ell=e_0^{\mathsf T}V_\sigma,\qquad
                         H^\sigma=V_\sigma\mathbf1.
 \tag{23}
\]
The coordinate row \(e_0^{\mathsf T}\) selects \((0,0)\).
Right invariance gives, for every generated operator \(P\),
\[
 V_\sigma P=\widehat P\,V_\sigma,\qquad
       \widehat P=V_\sigma P V_\sigma^*,\qquad
       \ell P\mathbf1=e_0^{\mathsf T}\widehat P H^\sigma .
 \tag{24}
\]
These are actual isometries of row spaces with their grid inner
product. They are not orthogonality statements about redundant
polynomial coefficients.

Matrices in (11) and (18) act on coordinate rows from the right.
Thus \(\widehat{PQ}=\widehat P\,\widehat Q\): evolving a row by \(P\)
and then by \(Q\) represents its literal product \(PQ\).
This retains the ordered products in the quantum principal minors.
There is no new transpose, braid reversal or sign change in (24).
The reversal already required to relate the auxiliary product to
the physical braid is exactly the one recorded in (2).

For \(k\) crossings take
\[
 V=\bigotimes_{j=1}^k V_{\sigma_j},\quad
 \mathfrak e^{\mathsf T}=\bigotimes_j e_0^{\mathsf T},\quad
 H=\bigotimes_j H^{\sigma_j}.
 \tag{25}
\]
Then \(V\) has \(N^{2k}\) orthonormal rows, and
\[
 \|\mathfrak e\|=1,\qquad \|H\|=N^k,\qquad
 \widehat C_N=VC_NV^*,\qquad
            VC_N=\widehat C_NV,\qquad \widehat C_N^L=0.
 \tag{26}
\]
Equivalently, construct \(\widehat C_N\) by substituting the
explicit local right-action matrices into exactly the same ordered
principal-minor formula. All source data are supplied independently
of the value of the invariant.

The original source (2) is now
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 K_N(W)=\zeta_N\mathfrak e^{\mathsf T}
       \left(\sum_{r=0}^{L-1}\widehat C_N^r\right)H,
 \qquad
 \zeta_N=Q^{(b-1+w)/2},\quad L=k(N-1)+1 .
 \quad}
 \tag{27}
\]
The ordinary determinant of \(I-\widehat C_N\) is one, so the same
original scalar is also
\[
 K_N(W)=\det
 \begin{pmatrix}
       I-\widehat C_N&H\\
       -\zeta_N\mathfrak e^{\mathsf T}&0
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{28}
\]
This bordered matrix has size \(N^{2k}+1\).

## 6. Scope, coverage, and proof source

This eliminates one cyclic register at each crossing through a
prescribed source-observable row module. It retains the exact clock
feedback, nilpotent directions, both crossing signs, original word
order and marked closing at every level. The source column has only
polynomial norm for fixed \(k\). The local \(a\)-operators have the
polynomial range bounds in Section 4.

These facts do not bound the coherent sum of powers of
\(\widehat C_N\), its selected inverse element, or the angle between
that element and the original closing. In particular they do not
produce a volume lower bound or a geometric trial field. The exact
dimension \(N^2\) of the whole local row orbit does not rule out a
smaller reduction tailored to a particular full operator.

already performs source-preserving Fourier and charge elimination with
the feedback retained. The [integral incidence transform](research_incidence_burau_gauss_reduction.md)

Fourier lattice. The all-root Verma

[Gevrey](research_all_root_verma_gevrey_bound.md), and
[reduced-cap](research_reduced_frobenius_trace_and_cap_transport.md)
notes retain different graded, carry and source data. All these
coverage files were read in their entirety for this comparison.
