# The complete selected first four/four pair through one half

Private written proof, 10 November 2026. The selected first-four/next-four
finite pair remains below its original geometric affine rate when its
first real target extends from \(9/20\) to \(1/2\), at the same fixed
selected height \(1/100\). Both Fourier upper alternatives are retained.
The adjacent target remains on its whole plateau at its actual negative
height. Every cap current, ordinary row and residue correction is
included. This does not change the first height or complete its period.

## 1. Original source and statement

Fix an actual positive three/four open word of length \(m\ge3\) whose
first two types are four. Keep the coupled reference
\((e_*,s_*;a_*,b_*)\), marking, primitives and additive constants of
the [selected finite reduction](research_mixed_selected_four_four_finite_reduction.md).
Its actual unchanged heights satisfy
\[
 -.051<h_e<-.019,\qquad -.102<h_s,d<-.001.
\tag{1}
\]
The same actual family includes the attached next external input at
length three. Set
\[
 H=\frac1{100},\quad a=x+iH,\quad b=z+id,\qquad
 \frac9{20}\le x\le\frac12,\quad z\in P=[11/50,9/20].
\tag{2}
\]
Write \(q_N=e^{-2\pi i/N}\), \(P_j=(q_N;q_N)_j\),
\(p_j(X)=(q_NX;q_N)_j\), \(X=e^{-2\pi ia}\), \(Y=e^{-2\pi ib}\).
The literal source is
\[
\begin{split}
 U_{1,N}(w,X)&=\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}
                  H_N(w,q_N^{-i})\frac{p_i(X)}{P_i^2},\\
 C_{1,N}(X,Y)&=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}
                  \frac{X^{-j}p_j(X)p_j(Y)}{P_j^3},\\
 \mathcal K_N(w;X,Y)&=U_{1,N}(w,X)C_{1,N}(X,Y)d_N(X)X^{-N}.
\end{split}
\tag{3}
\]
The outgoing allocation is \(|d_N(Y)|^{1/2}\). There is no division
by a Fourier polynomial or choice of its holomorphic square root.
The whole original endpoint coefficient \(H_N\) is retained.

On the original regular logarithmic strips put
\[
\begin{split}
 \Psi(e,a)&=S(e)+g(e+a)-g(a)-g(e)-\frac{i\pi}{2}(e-e^2),\\
 Q(s;a,b)&=g(s+a)+g(s+b)-3g(s)-g(a)-g(b)+2\pi ias,\\
 \mathcal F&=\Psi+Q-g(a)-\tfrac12g(b),\\
 M&=\Re\mathcal F(e_*,s_*;a_*,b_*),\quad
 p=\partial_b\mathcal F(e_*,s_*;a_*,b_*),\\
 \Lambda(z)&=M+\Re p\,(z-\Re b_*).
\end{split}
\tag{4}
\]
Here \(g'(v)=\operatorname{Log}(1-e^{-2\pi iv})\), with its
original continuation. The two upper rates for the one selected
polynomial in (3) are
\[
 W_1(x)=-\Re g(a)-2\pi H,\qquad W_0(x)=0.
\tag{5}
\]
Their maximum bounds the complete polynomial, including its forcing;
neither is omitted. The outgoing rate is \(-\Re g(b)/2\), since \(d<0\).

**Theorem 1.** For every \(\tau>0\), both parities satisfy
\[
 |\mathcal K_N(w;X,Y)|\,|d_N(Y)|^{1/2}
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}e^{N\{\Lambda(z)-1/4000+\tau\}}
\tag{6}
\]
uniformly on (2), for every real ordinary color \(w\in[-2,2]\).
At any prescribed added rate allowance it also holds on a sufficiently
small fixed ordinary-color disk about \(-2\), with every fixed
derivative on a smaller disk. The original horizontal integral and
outside scalar have the same exponential gap. No selected right
height connection or remaining outer period is asserted.

## 2. Complete cap profiles and the old right face

Use the five bare cap profiles
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
 A_0=\max_{.27\le u\le.49}\Re\Psi(u+ih_e,a),&
 A_L=\Re\Psi(.27,a),\quad A_R=\Re\Psi(.49,a),\\
 A_-=\max_u\Re\Psi(u-.055i,a)-.11\pi,&
 A_+=\max_u\Re\Psi(u+.04i,a)-.08\pi .
\end{array}
\tag{7}
\]
The [upper-target cap envelope](research_mixed_cap_upper_target_extension.md),
Theorem 2 and its physical-tail proof, applies on (2), including
the positive height \(H=.01\). It retains every cap current and
all compact physical tails. Its true ends admit the separate ceiling
\[
 E_*=\frac35+2\pi H+\frac{\log4}{100}<\frac{69}{100}.
\tag{8}
\]
Its exact upper-end denominator angles remain in \([.21,.5]\);
the original ceiling with \(\log(40/19)\) is smaller than (8).
The lower true end is smaller still. Thus the maximum of (7) and
(8) is a complete all-real-color cap envelope on the enlarged interval.
No main packet is substituted for an endpoint row.

At the old face \(x_0=9/20\), the individual profile comparisons in
the [complete selected plateau theorem](research_mixed_selected_four_four_full_product.md)
give gap \(1/2000\) for every cap profile paired with the ordinary
main, left cut, half row or last row, for both (5). These are
phase-profile bounds proved before finite summation. The complete
right-row reduction in its finite reduction therefore gives the
same gap for every physical row \(t\in[1/2,1]\).
The original left cut is \(1/8\).

The finite reduction also proves, throughout the old plateau,
\[
                         A_0>199/250.
\tag{9}
\]
Consequently (8) lies below \(A_0\) by more than \(53/500\).
It can replace any regular cap profile at the old face with this
additional reserve. The outgoing affine term and halfweight are
exactly those in (4), for all these comparisons.

Choose a fixed \(0<\varepsilon<1/100\), sufficiently small that
the old-face joint physical-row comparisons also hold on
\[
                         [1/2-2\varepsilon,1/2]
\tag{10}
\]
with gap \(1/4000\), for all six cap alternatives and both (5).
This follows from uniform continuity of the integrated zero-safe
row rates on the compact actual-reference family, with the old
strict gap \(1/2000\). The five private profile sets are compact,
and the true-end rate is constant. No continuity of a logarithmic
summand through its zero is required. Put \(r_0=1/2-\varepsilon\).
All subsequent contours and constants are fixed before \(N\).

## 3. Central main comparisons on the added interval

Use the private interval \([1/8,r_0]\), leaving (10) inside the
physical tail. Fix \(b\). For each of the cap main or real-cut
choices, maximize its joint rate with the ordinary main line at the
old face, retaining either alternative (5). Every such ordinary
private maximizer has real part greater than \(11/50\).

Indeed Lemma 1 of the
[next-four endpoint budget](research_mixed_first_four_next_four_endpoint_budget.md)
proves \(\Re Q_s(.22+ih_s)>0\) at every old plateau pair.
Its proof, equations (7)--(10), uses only the actual negative
heights in (1), the fixed \(H\), and the two real outer targets.
It is pointwise in \(b\) and does not use the cap coordinate.
On the present face this derivative decreases as the private real
coordinate varies: with
\[
 U=\Re\cot\pi s,\qquad C_b=\Re\cot\pi(s+b)
\]
one has \(U-C_b>0\) throughout \([1/8,r_0]\), and
\(\Re\cot\pi(s+a)<0\).
The proof of equation (12) of the
[early central theorem](research_mixed_selected_four_four_early_central.md)
gives \(U>u_0/2\), \(u_0=\cot\pi\Re s\), and, when \(C_b>0\),
\[
 C_b\le\frac{u_0p_b-1}{u_0+p_b}<u_0/2,\qquad
                         p_b=\cot\pi z<4/3.
\tag{11}
\]
When \(C_b\le0\), the assertion is immediate. Hence
\(\Re Q_{ss}<0\). The positive derivative at \(.22\) excludes
every smaller private coordinate by increasing it. The argument
also applies when the cap is fixed at either real cut and when the
private upper endpoint is \(r_0\), since \(r_0>.49\).

Write
\[
 r=e^{2\pi H},\quad q=e^{-2\pi h_s},\quad
 D=|1-re^{-2\pi ix}|,\quad
 N_e=|1-rR e^{-2\pi i(u+x)}|,\quad
 N_s=|q-re^{-2\pi i(v+x)}|.
\tag{12}
\]
For cap main or real cuts \(R=e^{2\pi\Im e}\le1\).
The original elementary witnesses in the same endpoint proof give
\[
                  \frac{53}{50}<r<\frac{43}{40},\qquad q<\frac{19}{10}.
\tag{13}
\]
The lower bound follows already from
\(e^{\pi/50}>1+\pi/50>53/50\).
At an old-face private maximizer \(v>.22\), so
\[
 N_e^2<1+r^2+\frac{19}{50}r,\quad
 N_s^2<\frac{361}{100}+r^2+\frac{19}{10}r,\quad
 D^2>1+r^2+\frac{19}{10}r.
\tag{14}
\]
Here \(u+.45\in[.72,.94]\) has cosine greater than
\(-19/100\), \(v+.45\in(.67,.95)\) has cosine greater than
\(-1/2\), and \(\cos(.9\pi)<-19/20\).

The squared denominator of the forced real derivative minus
these squared numerator bounds is
\[
\begin{split}
 P(r)={}&(1+r^2+19r/10)^2\\
 &-(1+r^2+19r/50)(361/100+r^2+19r/10)\\
 ={}&\frac{38}{25}r^3+\frac{139}{500}r^2
                       +\frac{2641}{5000}r-\frac{261}{100}>0.
\end{split}
\tag{15}
\]
All nonconstant coefficients are positive. From \(r>53/50\),
one has \(r^3>119/100\) and \(r^2>28/25\), so (15) exceeds
\[
 \frac{38}{25}\frac{119}{100}
 +\frac{139}{500}\frac{28}{25}
 +\frac{2641}{5000}\frac{53}{50}
 -\frac{261}{100}=\frac{17513}{250000}>0.
\tag{16}
\]
Exact differentiation therefore gives, at every such maximizer,
\[
 \partial_x(\Re\Psi+\Re Q-\tfrac12\Re g(b)+W_0)
                       =\log(N_eN_s/D^2)<0.
\tag{17}
\]
For \(W_1\) the denominator is \(D^3\); its derivative is
also negative, since \(D>1\).

These signs extend the old-face maximum by joint concavity,
rather than by fixing a moving optimizer. On the added strip put
\[
 A=\Re\cot\pi a\ge0,\quad
 d_e=-\Re\cot\pi(e+a)>0,\quad
 d_s=-\Re\cot\pi(s+a)>0.
\]
Both shifted real parts are between one half and one; \(r_0\)
keeps the latter uniformly below one. The canonical cap proof in
the upper-target envelope gives \(-\Re S''(e)/\pi>1/43\).
For the forced joint phase, with \(b\) fixed, the negative real
Hessian divided by \(\pi\) in \((u,v,x)\) is
\[
 \operatorname{diag}\!\left(
 -\Re S''/\pi+\Re\cot\pi e,\ 3U-C_b,\ 2A\right)
 +d_e(1,0,1)^T(1,0,1)
 +d_s(0,1,1)^T(0,1,1).
\tag{18}
\]
Its first two diagonal entries are positive by (11); the rank-one
terms make the full matrix positive even at \(x=1/2\).
For the factorial alternative add \(A\) to the last diagonal.
For either real cap cut delete its private row and column.
These are the original Hessians; the chirp has zero real Hessian.

At an old-face maximizer, private first-order optimality makes
its private tangent nonpositive in every allowed private direction,
including boundary maxima. Equations (17)--(18) then give a
nonincreasing supporting tangent for every \(x\ge x_0\).
Thus cap main or either real cap cut, paired with the truncated
ordinary main, retains its old gap \(1/2000\), for both (5).
No condition on a new critical root is used.

## 4. Cap aliases and true ends paired with the main line

The canonical lifted identity in the
[cap-alias proof](research_mixed_selected_cap_alias_transport.md),
equations (9)--(12), continues on the same private rectangle.
Write \(e=u+i\eta\), \(q_c=\Re u_c(e)>.17\), and
\[
 \Im\Psi_e=\mathcal A(u+x,\eta+H)+T(e),\qquad
 \mathcal A(t,y)=\arg(1-e^{2\pi y-2\pi it}).
\]
The retained canonical bounds are
\[
 T<.56\pi,\quad T>\pi(2u-1),\qquad
 T>\pi(2u+q_c-1)\quad(\eta\le0).
\tag{19}
\]
Now \(u+x\ge.72\), so its argument is always nonpositive and
\(\Im\Psi_e<.56\pi\).
When \(\eta\le-H\), the unit-radius bound
\(\mathcal A(t,y)\ge\pi(1/2-t)\) for \(t>1/2,y\le0\)
gives \(\Im\Psi_e>-.56\pi\).
For \(-H\le\eta\le0\), the general radius bound gives
\(\Im\Psi_e>\pi(q_c-2x)>-.83\pi\).
For \(\eta\ge0\), it gives \(\Im\Psi_e>-2\pi x\ge-\pi\).
These bounds keep the original logarithmic lift.

Integrating from the actual \(h_e\), pointwise at the same private
real coordinate, gives
\[
\begin{split}
 \Re\Psi(u-.055i,a)-.11\pi
 &<\Re\Psi(u+ih_e,a)-\frac{1123\pi}{12500}
 <\Re\Psi(u+ih_e,a)-\frac14,\\
 \Re\Psi(u+.04i,a)-.08\pi
 &<\Re\Psi(u+ih_e,a)-\frac{437\pi}{50000}
 <\Re\Psi(u+ih_e,a)-\frac1{50}.
\end{split}
\tag{20}
\]
The second line's total upper-height cost is at most
\[
 \pi\left\{\frac{14}{25}\frac{41}{1000}
       +\frac{83}{100}\frac1{100}+\frac1{25}\right\}
                         =\frac{3563\pi}{50000},
\]
against penalty \(2\pi/25\).
The first uses height distance less than \(.036\) and marginal
upper bound \(14\pi/25\). Both aliases thus reduce to the main cap.

For the true-end constant (8), paired with any ordinary main point,
the forced derivative is \(\log(N_s/D)\). On the added interval,
\[
 N_s<19/10+43/40<3,\qquad D^2>39/10,\qquad D>3/2,
\]
so this derivative is less than \(\log2<1\). Its integrated
increase over length \(1/20\) is less than \(1/20\).
The factorial derivative \(\log(N_s/D^2)\) is negative.
The old true-end reserve \(53/500\) exceeds the possible cost by
\(7/125\). Hence the true ends paired with the ordinary main also
retain gap \(1/2000\), for both alternatives. This transports
the literal constant ceiling, not a regular endpoint packet.

All six cap alternatives times the truncated ordinary main are
therefore paid with the original outgoing allocation and gap \(1/2000\).

## 5. Every physical row and both real cuts

Use the original bare absolute row rate
\[
 E(t;a,b)=
 \int_0^t\log|1-e^{-2\pi i(a+v)}|\,dv+
 \int_0^t\log|1-e^{-2\pi i(b+v)}|\,dv
                         -3J(t)-2\pi Ht.
\tag{21}
\]
No incoming chirp is discarded. For every regular cap point,
including both aliases, its radius \(R=e^{2\pi\Im e}<4/3\).
On the added outer strip,
\[
\begin{split}
 N_e^2&<1+\frac{16}{9}r^2+\frac8{15}r<D^2,\\
 D^2-N_e^2&>
 r\left(\frac{41}{30}-\frac79r\right)>\frac{191}{360}r>0.
\end{split}
\tag{22}
\]
Here \(\cos2\pi(u+x)>-1/5\),
\(\cos2\pi x<-19/20\), and \(r<43/40\).
For every physical index \(t\in[1/8,1]\),
\[
                         N_t=|1-re^{-2\pi i(x+t)}|\le D,
\tag{23}
\]
because \(t\ge1-2x\), and the cosine on \([1-x,1+x]\)
is at least its equal endpoint values.

The exact joint derivatives, retaining the first full weight
and outgoing halfweight, are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 &W_0&W_1\\ \hline
 \text{regular cap point}
   &\log(N_eN_t/D^2)&\log(N_eN_t/D^3)\\
 \text{constant true end}
   &\log(N_t/D)&\log(N_t/D^2).
\end{array}
\tag{24}
\]
Every entry is nonpositive by (22)--(23) and \(D>1\).
Alias penalties are constant in \(x\). Thus all old-face high
comparisons and the collar (10) transport pointwise before a
supremum or finite summation. They retain gap \(1/4000\).
The left real cut \(1/8\) retains gap \(1/2000\).

For \(0<t\le1/8\), the exact row-ratio rate obeys
\[
 e^{E_t}=
 \frac{|1-re^{-2\pi i(x+t)}|
       |1-e^{2\pi d-2\pi i(z+t)}|}
      {r(2\sin\pi t)^3}>1.
\tag{25}
\]
The first numerator divided by \(r\) is greater than one because
\(\cos2\pi(x+t)<0\).
For the second, \(z+t\in[.22,.575]\) has cosine less than \(1/5\);
minimizing over its positive radius gives squared modulus greater
than \(24/25\). Finally \(2\sin(\pi/8)<4/5\).
These give a uniform strict reserve, so all low rates are bounded
by the left cut, including \(t=0\) by continuity.
The finite zero-safe numerator estimates and denominator endpoint
error are exactly the argument in Section 2 of the
[finite contour allowance proof](research_mixed_conditional_contour_allowance.md).
That smoothed-logarithm proof remains valid on this compact target
family and does not need the old central-root assumption to bound
physical rows.

## 6. Exact central contour and all corrections

Use half-grid cuts tending to \(\ell=1/8,r_0\), with lower height
\(-1/5\) and upper height \(5/4\). Explicitly,
\[
 L_N=\lfloor N/8\rfloor+1,\quad K_N=\lceil Nr_0\rceil,\qquad
 \ell_N=(L_N-1/2)/N,\quad u_N=(K_N-1/2)/N.
\]
With the original negative-root factorial interpolation put
\[
 T_N(s)=e^{2\pi iNas}
  \frac{\widehat P_N(N(s+a))\widehat P_N(N(s+b))}
       {\widehat P_N(Ns)^3\widehat P_N(Na)\widehat P_N(Nb)}.
\]
The exact central identity is
\[
 \sum_{j=L_N}^{K_N-1}\frac{X^{-j}p_j(X)p_j(Y)}{P_j^3}
 =\frac{N}{2i}\oint_{\partial\mathcal R_N}^{\rm CCW}
                             \cot(\pi Ns)T_N(s)\,ds .
\tag{26}
\]
All factorial arguments remain in fixed compact original regular
strips; in particular \(\Re(s+a)\le1-\varepsilon+o(1)\).
The [exact factorial theorem](exact_factorial_interpolation_and_complex_contours.md)
permits the fixed imaginary heights. No interpolation pole is crossed.
The original \(+N\) zero-mode coefficient, both horizontal aliases
and all four vertical alias halves remain.

The all-height argument calculation in Section 4 of the
[unselected right-half proof](research_mixed_initial44_right_half_interval.md)
depends only on the real coordinates and original logarithmic lift,
not on the signs of the outer heights. Thus
\[
                     -\frac{33\pi}{20}<\Im Q_s<\frac{131\pi}{100}.
\tag{27}
\]
Indeed the first shifted real argument \(v+x\) is above one half.
If \(v+z\) is also above it, the lower and upper bounds divided
by \(\pi\) are \(-1+2v-2z\) and \(2x\). If it is below, they
are \(-2+4v\) and \(1-2v-2z+2x\). These give (27), with the
unchanged carrier \(2\pi x\).

Comparison to the actual \(h_s\in[-.108,0]\) gives
\[
\begin{split}
 \Re Q(v-i/5)-2\pi/5
   &<\Re Q(v+ih_s)-69\pi/500,\\
 \Re Q(v+5i/4)-5\pi/2
   &<\Re Q(v+ih_s)-2593\pi/10000.
\end{split}
\tag{28}
\]
The cap and both Fourier alternatives are independent of this
private height, so Section 4 pays both horizontal aliases.
At a real cut the upper weighted derivative is less than
\(-7\pi/20\), and the lower one is greater than \(69\pi/100\).
Every vertical alias half is bounded by its real-cut endpoint,
already paid in Section 5.

On the zero-mode joins from \(0\) to \(h_s\), (11) holds with
the intermediate private height, since only its absolute-height
bound was used. Also \(\Re\cot\pi(s+a)<0\).
Thus \(\Re Q_{ss}<0\), and its real phase is convex in private
height. Each join is bounded by its main and real-cut endpoints,
including the new right cut \(r_0\).

The half-grid displacements have a fixed regular neighborhood.
Continuity, uniform factorial symbols and zero-safe physical rates
absorb them into one arbitrary total tolerance. The complete cap
envelope already includes its own joins and physical tails; its
maximum can be multiplied into every ordinary comparison above.
No simultaneous correction is omitted.

## 7. Finite conclusion and scope

The ordinary rows split exactly into \(j<L_N\),
\(L_N\le j<K_N\), and \(j\ge K_N\).
Sections 5--6 bound all three blocks, with gap at least \(1/4000\)
after the collar choice. Every cap profile and true end is retained.
The absolute Fourier continuation bound supplies exactly
\(\max(W_0,W_1)\) for the whole selected polynomial and
\(-\Re g(b)/2\) for its outgoing allocation.
Polynomial row counts, symbols, lengths, residue densities and
rounding costs use one prescribed \(\tau\). This proves (6),
including the original last index and both level parities.

The cap envelope holds on every real ordinary color. The exact
source has degree less than \(N\) in that color; the other factors
are independent of it. Interval polynomial continuation, as in the
[complex-neighborhood proof](research_mixed_horizontal_complex_extension.md),
gives fixed disks with arbitrarily small added rate allowance.
Cauchy's formula supplies fixed derivatives on smaller disks.
At a zero of a color-independent factor the bound is immediate;
no division is used.

The original affine calibration, outgoing halfweight, increasing
horizontal measure and outside scalar remain unchanged.
Only the selected horizontal interval (2) is paid. The right
height connection, subsequent targets, other coordinate departures
and the whole original period remain separate. No whole-word
noncancellation or knot statement is made.
