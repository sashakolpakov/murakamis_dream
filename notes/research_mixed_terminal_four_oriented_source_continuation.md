# The adjacent-four input restriction for the terminal source

Research continuation design, 5 November 2026. This stage retains
both previous terminal-source tree prefixes and uses an already
proved oriented image bound for the actual incoming leg. It changes
neither the twenty-one target families nor their strict gap. No
new author attempt or saved replay of this stage has been executed.

The new driver is
[continue_mixed_terminal_four_source_oriented.py](../experiments/continue_mixed_terminal_four_source_oriented.py).
The [original source design](research_mixed_terminal_four_source_certificate.md),
the [first domain continuation](research_mixed_terminal_four_source_continuation.md),
and all their proof, driver and partial-record files remain unchanged.

## 1. The actual incoming leg adjacent to a type-four terminal block

Let the last two vertex types both be four. If \(m\ge3\),
the incoming coordinate of the terminal transform is
\(a=t_{m-1}\), the second output of the original ordinary
own-four block
\[
        (s_{m-1},t_{m-1})=B_1(t_{m-2},s_m).
 \tag{1}
\]
Its second external leg \(s_m\) is the attached leg of the
type-four terminal endpoint. On the full original input products,
the exact affine image extraction gives
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c|c}
 \text{own/external types}&\text{output}&\text{real lower bound}\\\hline
 4/(3,4)&t&259/1000\\
 4/(4,4)&s,t&263/1000.
 \end{array}
 \tag{2}
\]
The full-map interpretation and reflection identity are proved in
[the adjacent-four domain lemma, Section 1](research_mixed_first_cap_oriented_plateau_certificate.md).
Thus (1) has \(\Re a>.259\) regardless of the preceding type.
No cyclic replacement of the open reference is made.

At \(m=2\), the word in this type family is \((4,4)\),
and \(a=t_1\) is the left endpoint's attached leg. It is
specifically the own-four/neighbor-four endpoint map. Its 909 saved
original root disks give the exact lower bound
\[
 \Re t_1\ge
 \frac{13834284001670119}{50000000000000000}
            >\frac{69}{250}>\frac{259}{1000}.
 \tag{3}
\]
This is stronger than the older bound obtained by pooling both
neighbor types. It is not a claim about the private endpoint leg.

The stage therefore keeps the full original \(D_3\) for a
type-three incoming leg and uses, for a type-four incoming leg,
\[
 K_4^{\rm adj}=
 \{|a-(.30-.052i)|\le.060\}
 \cap\{-.108\le\Im a\le-.001\}
 \cap\{\Re a\ge.259\}.
 \tag{4}
\]
Every required actual terminal reference at every \(m\ge2\)
remains strictly inside the added real bound. The disk and height
inclusions are the same ones proved in the preceding continuation.
Neither a one-vertex terminal source nor an independent general
type-four leg is included in this argument.

### Exact extraction and its provenance

The driver calls only the two pure rational extraction helpers of
the cited domain lemma. `oriented_real_floor` verifies 16,421
inequalities from the saved affine enclosures, with all six original
accepted-address lists matched exactly to the complete original
four-real-parameter localization record. It bounds every required
real output by the rational center minus the error radius and the
absolute linear-response contributions over its full parameter box.
`attached_four_four_floor` checks the rational attached center minus
its radius in all 909 original endpoint witnesses and verifies (3).

The saved affine image file, its extractor, source proof and driver,
the original localization author and independent replay, and the
complete endpoint author/replay are hash-bound. The ordinary
extraction covers full input products. The endpoint extraction uses
the full original root disks. Neither helper calls a root proposal,
a source test or the first-cap author routine. In particular the
truth or completion status of a first-cap **source** certificate is
not an assumption here: only the established geometry deduction is
used.

## 3. The only new exclusion and interval restriction

The original type-four disk coordinates are
\(a=.30-.052i+.064(x+iy)\). The new real bound is exactly
\[
                     x\ge-41/64.
 \tag{5}
\]
For an appended node, clip its rational rectangle to
\[
          [-41/64,15/16]\times[-7/8,51/64].
 \tag{6}
\]
Exclude an empty intersection, or a rectangle whose exact minimum
of \(x^2+y^2\) is strictly greater than \((15/16)^2\).
Equality and all boundary contacts remain. For type-three input,
use the unchanged original disk rule.

Every nonexcluded rectangle is passed to the original source helper
with that same clipped midpoint and those same clipped halfwidths.
The helper's weaker original lower mask makes no further change.
All target gains, original complex root enclosures, implicit
responses, phase primitives and alias penalties are unchanged.
In particular
\[
       \mu=3/100,\qquad \kappa=1/2500000,
       \qquad D<-1/20000
 \tag{7}
\]
are exactly the previous acceptance conditions. The outgoing real
targets retain their entire original intervals; no target domain is
trimmed.

## 4. Coverage, resume and complete saved replay

The driver reconstructs every tree from its inherited root rectangle
using its recorded arbitrary split axes. A split retains both exact
children, an exclusion is checked with its fixed stage rule, and a
leaf has its saved valid target trial. Complete trees exhaust their
right-first stacks. The active tree reconstructs exactly the recorded
pending boxes and depths. This validates the union of the admitted
reference set without a new parameter proposal.

Every subsequent resume uses a new output path, verifies the immutable
input hash, retains all completed trees unchanged, and extends its
active tree as an exact prefix. Both original pinned snapshots and
their proofs, drivers and dependencies remain bound. An author
attempt is capped at 600,000 additional committed nodes and 600
seconds of numerical continuation, with a recorded depth limit
(default 30, at most 45). Attempted and committed counts remain
separate. An active box stays pending through fallible arithmetic;
signal-masked commits and atomic publication preserve the complete
frontier at deadline, depth or arithmetic stops.

Complete saved replay has no author time alarm. Both complex-root
and target proposals are forbidden. All first-stage leaves use the
first-stage helper, all second-stage leaves use the second-stage
helper, and every appended leaf uses (6) with the original source
helper. Every original witness is revalidated before its source
tests. No leaf refinement or search is permitted. The two exact
geometry extractions are repeated from their saved rational data
and recorded separately from source acceptance.

Only a complete author plus that saved replay proves all twenty-one
comparisons on the new reference domain. Since (4) contains every
required actual reference, the proof of
[conditional whole-terminal completion](research_mixed_terminal_four_full_completion.md)
then applies with its Condition C restricted from the older auxiliary
\(K_4\) to \(K_4^{\rm adj}\). Every formula, normalization,
bare discarded-current margin, ordinary-color germ and plateau
allocation in that assembly is unchanged. This implication does not
assert the stronger unused comparison on all of \(K_4\), or
the global open-word source theorem.
