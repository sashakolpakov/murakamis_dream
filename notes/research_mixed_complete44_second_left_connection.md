# A complete second left connection after the first \(44\) period

Private written deduction, 11 November 2026. The literal completed
first polynomial can be carried from the real second seam down its
inward radial segment and across to the early second plateau.
We freeze the already calibrated first-coordinate contours at the
second face \(q=1/10\), and transport their individual allocated
profiles. Both first Fourier coefficients and every conditional
physical row are retained. The second coordinate is unselected.

\]

\end{split}                                                       \tag{2}

                          -g(z_1)-g(z_2)-\tfrac12g(z_3),\\

asymptotic or nonzero leading coefficient is asserted.

## 3. The shared-coordinate inequality at all private heights

Assume
\[
 0\le q\le1/10,\quad 1/2\le R=e^{2\pi h}\le1,\quad
 3/40\le s_R\le1/2,\quad1/8\le t_R\le1/2,\qquad y=q+ih.
                                                                    \tag{8}
\]
The private imaginary parts may be any finite real numbers.
The actual horizontal height in (1) has \(R>1/2\):
\(2\pi(51/500)<2/3<\log2\). The radial raise only increases \(R\).
Set
\[
 D=|1-Re^{-2\pi iq}|,\quad
 N_s=|1-R e^{2\pi\Im s}e^{-2\pi i(q+s_R)}|,\quad
 N_t=|e^{-2\pi\Im t}-Re^{-2\pi i(q+t_R)}|.
\]
The original chirp gives exactly
\[
 \partial_q\Re\{Q(s;a,y)+Q(t;y,z)-g(y)\}
                         =\log(N_sN_t/D^3).                     \tag{9}
\]
Neither the cap nor \(a\) occurs in this derivative.
Minimizing distance to a ray, and using \(q+s_R,q+t_R\le3/5\),
gives, at every private height,
\[
 N_s\ge S_1(q):=\sin(3\pi/20+2\pi q),\qquad
 N_t\ge R S_2(q):=R\sin(\pi/4+2\pi q).                            \tag{10}
\]
For shifted angles above \(1/4\) the minimum is the full fixed
radius, which also implies (10).

Here is a written proof that
\[
                         D^3<R S_1S_2.                          \tag{11}
\]
Put \(\theta=2\pi q\), \(c=\cos\theta\). The function
\[
 D^2/R^{2/3}=R^{4/3}+R^{-2/3}-2cR^{1/3}
\]
has second derivative
\[
 \tfrac49R^{-2/3}+\tfrac{10}{9}R^{-8/3}
                                   +\tfrac{4c}{9}R^{-5/3}>0.
\]
It suffices to check \(R=1/2,1\). At \(R=1\),
\(D^2<2/5\), \(S_1S_2>63/200\), and
\((2/5)^3<(63/200)^2\). At \(R=1/2\), use
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
q\text{ interval}&D^2\text{ upper bound}&S_1S_2\text{ lower bound}\\ \hline
[0,1/40]&21/80&63/200\\
[1/40,1/20]&3/10&7/15\\
[1/20,3/40]&3/8&14/25\\
[3/40,1/10]&9/20&18/25 .
\end{array}                                                       \tag{12}
\]
The four required rational comparisons are
\[
 (21/80)^3<(63/400)^2,\quad (3/10)^3<(7/30)^2,\quad
 (3/8)^3<(7/25)^2,\quad (9/20)^3<(9/25)^2.
\]
The table follows from
\[
\begin{gathered}
\cos(\pi/20)>79/80,\ \cos(\pi/10)>19/20,\
\cos(3\pi/20)>7/8,\ \cos(\pi/5)>4/5,\\
\sin(3\pi/20)>9/20,\ \sin(\pi/4)>7/10,\
\sin(\pi/5)>7/12,\ \sin(3\pi/10)>4/5,\\
\sin(7\pi/20)>4/5,\qquad\sin(2\pi/5)>9/10 .
\end{gathered}                                                       \tag{13}
\]
Use \(\cos t>1-t^2/2\), \(\sin t>t-t^3/6\),
\(25/8<\pi<22/7\), and the positive elementary quarter/pentagon
values. Thus (11) and strict positivity in (9) hold without
evaluating a phase or critical reference. The point \(D=0\)
is not used in differentiation.

Last alternatives are also covered. The first literal last
is independent of \(q\); the next last is \(-2\pi h\).
With only the next alternative last the derivative is
\[
 \log(N_s/D^2)>0,                                                \tag{14}
\]
because \(N_s>9/20\) and \(D^2<9/20\).
With the first alternative last, or any \(q\)-independent
first ceiling, it is
\[
 \log(N_t/D^2)>0.                                                \tag{15}
\]
Indeed \(D^2/R\le5/2-2\cos\theta<S_2(q)\).
The function \(S_2(q)+2\cos\theta-5/2\) is concave on
\(0\le\theta\le\pi/5\); it is positive at zero and at the
other endpoint exceeds \(83/(60\sqrt2)-9/10>0\), by (13).
With both alternatives last the derivative is \(-\log D>0\).

Therefore every fixed allocated profile, including its unchanged
sampling penalties, is no larger at \(q+ic_2\), \(0\le q\le1/10\),
than at \(y_0\). This holds before maximizing or summing currents.

## 4. Every physical block after enlarging the second interval

The derivative alone does not bound omitted physical rows.
Write \(S(t)=2\sin\pi t\). For \(q\in[0,1/10]\), \(h\le0\),
and \(0\le t\le1/8\), radial minimization gives
\[
 |1-e^{2\pi h}e^{-2\pi i(q+t)}|
               \ge\sin(2\pi t)=S(t)\cos\pi t.                   \tag{16}
\]
Here \(q+t\le9/40<1/4\).

For first-path targets \(0\le\Re a\le1/2,\Im a\le0\), the
folded incoming modulus is at least \(S(t)\): its radius is
at least one, and \(\Re a+2t<1\). The low own-four force is
therefore at least \(\frac12\cot\pi t>6/5\).
For the nonnegative first heights, \(\Re a\in[1/10,1/2]\).
The folded incoming modulus exceeds \(1/2\) for \(t\le1/10\)
and \(9/10\) for \(1/10\le t\le1/8\), again by radial
minimization. In these two intervals respectively,
\(S(t)^2<2/5,3/5\), while \(\cos\pi t>9/10\).
With (16) the forces exceed \(9/8,27/20\).
Every first low row thus reduces to the old cut \(1/8\),
including its zero-row endpoint.

For first negative heights use the
[all-angle bounded-drift high-row theorem](research_mixed_complete44_two_early_angles_high_rows.md):

\(-.078\le\Im a\le0\).

[all-angle zero-drift theorem](research_mixed_all_angle_high_row_sign_class.md):
half alone suffices. Their integrated inequalities extend to
\(q=0,\Re a=1/2\) by continuity. Their zero-safe uniform
numerator proof extends to these closed angular boundaries;
no physical zero is divided out.

For the next factor, Section 2 of the
[lower-left low-block proof](research_mixed_next_lower_left_low_physical_blocks.md)
already has \(0\le q\le.22\). Its folded incoming modulus is
at least \(S(t)\), the third numerator exceeds \(19/20\),
and its low force exceeds \(19/12\). These inequalities
require only \(h\le0\). Section 3 of the
[next own-four high-row proof](research_mixed_next_lower_left_own_four_upper_rows.md)
uses \(e^{-2\pi h}<2\), the third negative height and \(u\in P\).
It reduces the full high block to
\[
                  E_1(1/2;y,z),\qquad E_1(1;y,z)=-2\pi h.        \tag{17}
\]
It remains valid while \(h\) increases from \(c_2\) to zero.
The different first high-row cutoff \(-.078\) is not imposed
on this next factor.

For real \(a\in[1/2,7/8]\), use the early real-first source's
notation \(E_a(t;y),I_a(t),\delta=1-a,\ell=3/40\).
The entropy comparison \(I_a(t)\le I_{1/2}(t)\) for
\(t\le1/2\) is independent of \(y\). For \(t\le\ell\),
the half-incoming force, using (16), is at least
\(\frac12\cot^2\pi t>1\). Hence the low block is bounded
by \(L(y)=E_{1/2}(\ell;y)\).
For every postwall middle row,
\[
 E_a(t;y)\le E_1(t;y),\qquad
 E_1(t;y)-L(y)<-7/1200,\quad\ell\le t\le1/2.                     \tag{18}
\]
This is equation (25) of that source. Its proof uses only
\(|Y|\le1\) and \(I_y(t)-I_y(\ell)\le(t-\ell)\log2\),
not a positive lower bound on \(q\).
Its separate rounded assertion \(L>1/3\) is not used.
Uniform continuity of the integrated rates permits a smaller
fixed \(\eta>0\) for which half the reserve in (18) pays
\(\delta-\eta\le t\le\delta\), uniformly over the new \(q,h\)
set. At \(y_0\), this smaller cut remains to the right of
the original critical point. All cuts are chosen before \(N\).

For \(t\ge1/2\), retain instead
\[
                         E_a(t;y)\le H(y):=E_{1/2}(1/2;y).       \tag{19}
\]
Equations (19)--(20) of the
[intermediate real source](research_mixed_four_conditional_intermediate_real_source.md)

The rate \(H(y_0)\) is separately calibrated by the

On \(7/8\le a<1\), retain the complete conditional constant

or the double-last comparison. No inequality
\(7/15\le E_{1/2}(1/8;y)\) at \(q=0\) is required.

All these continuous rate comparisons give bounds for every
literal physical index with an arbitrarily small common
tolerance. Use the original uniform factorial \(O(\log N)\)

Its integrated error tends uniformly to zero, including real

Here every physical half or last is a continuous rate, not

## 7. Endpoints, colors, and the exact integral

intervals \([\rho,1/10]\) and \([7/8,1-\rho]\).

lemma in

\[
 [-(1+\sigma)N+1,(2-\sigma)N-2]\subset[-2N,2N].                   \tag{26}
\]

continuation, including the outgoing halfweight.

then sufficiently smaller \(\rho\), and only then \(N\).

\([1/10,11/50]+ic_2\). This proves (5).

The additional second integral \(N\int_{{\cal L}_2}{\cal D}_N\,dy\)
