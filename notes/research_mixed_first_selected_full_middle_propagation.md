# A selected first plateau boundary with an unrestricted middle

Private written deduction, 10 November 2026. The completed first-only
selected prefix is compatible with the exact protected-cut reduction.
It therefore permits every original free-middle state, rather than only
the earlier safe interior plateau selections. Its strict finite-source
gap also pays a fixed small extension to the left of the first plateau
endpoint. The full early selected interval and the intervening outer
height changes remain separate.

## 1. The complete selected prefix and its original tangent

Fix an actual positive three/four open word of length \(m\ge3\)
whose first type is four. Write \(n_j=3+\epsilon_j\),
\(\epsilon_1=1\), \(\eta=\epsilon_2\), and retain the original
complete-color reference \(r_j=a_j+ic_j\), markings and lifts.
Put \(k_4=\sum_{j=1}^m\epsilon_j\) and retain the original
calibrated action \(M_{\rm open}=\Re\mathcal F_m(z_*)\).
Put \(P=[11/50,9/20]\), and use the original selected contour
\[
 z_1=x_1+iH_\eta,\qquad z_2=x_2+ic_2,\qquad
 H_0=7/625,\quad H_1=1/100,\qquad (x_1,x_2)\in P^2.
\tag{1}
\]
The first position is selected; the second is unselected. With
\(X_j=e^{-2\pi iz_j}\), define the literal holomorphic product
\[
 K_{\eta,N}(w;X_1,X_2)=
 U_{1,N}(w,X_1)C_{\eta,N}(X_1,X_2)
                         d_N(X_1)X_1^{-N}.
\tag{2}
\]
The outgoing allocation is \(|d_N(X_2)|^{\eta/2}\), without
choosing a holomorphic square root. Both original alternatives of the
complete selected weight in (2) are retained.

Let \(L_1,\phi_2\) be the original allocated phases, including
the first full and second half diagonal allocations in their sum.
Set
\[
 M_{12}=\Re\{L_1(r_1)+\phi_2(r_1,r_2)\},\qquad
 p_R=\partial_2\phi_2(r_1,r_2),\qquad d_*=1/2000.
\tag{3}
\]
The complete [next-three](research_mixed_selected_four_three_full_product.md)
and [next-four](research_mixed_selected_four_four_full_product.md)
theorems give, for every \(\tau>0\),
\[
 |K_{\eta,N}|\,|d_N(X_2)|^{\eta/2}
 \le C_\tau N^{A_\tau}
    \exp N\{M_{12}+\Re[p_R(z_2-r_2)]-d_*+\tau\}.
\tag{4}
\]
This holds through both parities on fixed small ordinary-color disks.
It is the complete source, including cap endpoints, all ordinary rows,
aliases and joins. Its original phase decomposition and marginal are
also recorded in the [first-selected assembly](research_mixed_first_selected_plateau.md),
equations (7)--(9). No estimate at the original negative first height
is substituted for (4).

## 2. Exact propagation behind protected triples

Choose either exact source chart of
[completed boundary propagation](research_mixed_completed_boundary_state_propagation.md).
Keep its safe ordinary cuts \(p,\ldots,q\), its eight-vertex halo,
and its contracted-graph inheritance condition when applicable. Require
\[
 T_L=\{p-2,p-1,p\},\qquad T_R=\{q-1,q,q+1\},\qquad
 F=[p+1,q-2]
\tag{5}
\]
to have unselected targets in \(P\) on the protected triples.
The complete prefix window (2) lies wholly before those triples.
Keep \(x_1,\ldots,x_{p-1}\in P\), position one at (1),
and positions \(2,\ldots,p-1\) unselected at their reference
heights. Every free symbol in \(F\) may be any original alphabet
state, with its original Fourier choice, height rule and priority.

The literal left cut modulus is
\[
 L_N=|U_{1,N}|\prod_{j=2}^{p-1}|C_{\epsilon_j,N}|
      |d_N(X_1)X_1^{-N}|
      \prod_{j=2}^{p-2}|d_N(X_j)|^{\epsilon_j}
      |d_N(X_{p-1})|^{\epsilon_{p-1}/2}.
\tag{6}
\]
Define the original baseline \(M_L\), cut derivative \(P_{p,a}\)
and calibrated rate by
\[
\begin{split}
 M_L&=\Re\left\{L_1(r_1)+\sum_{j=2}^{p-1}
                          \phi_j(r_{j-1},r_j)\right\},\qquad
 P_{p,a}=\partial_1\phi_p(r_{p-1},r_p),\\
 G_{L,N}&=N^{-1}\log L_N-M_L+
                   \Re[P_{p,a}(z_{p-1}-r_{p-1})].
\end{split}
\tag{7}
\]
At zeros use \(\log0=-\infty\). Multiply (4) by the complete
ordinary supporting planes from the
[whole plateau theorem](research_mixed_full_unselected_plateau.md)
through factor \(p-1\). The outgoing
halfweight at coordinate two receives its other half from factor three;
all later internal weights occur twice in half allocations, and the
cut weight once. Stationarity cancels \(p_R\) with the next incoming
marginal, every internal marginal thereafter, and the last marginal
with \(P_{p,a}\). The remaining quadratic losses are nonpositive.
Consequently
\[
                    G_{L,N}\le-d_*+\eta_0+O_m(\log N/N).
\tag{8}
\]
The first positive height is internal to (2); no height cost is
charged again at the cut. The protected triple insulates that cut
from every free middle departure.

For clarity the two literal full sources remain
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathscr J^c_{\sigma,N}
  &=U_{1,N}\prod_{j=2}^{h}C_{\epsilon_j,N}\,
       \mathcal V_{\epsilon_m,N}(w_R,X_h)\prod_{j=1}^{h}D_j,
           &&h=m-1,\\
 \mathscr J^u_{\sigma,N}
  &=U_{1,N}\prod_{j=2}^{m}C_{\epsilon_j,N}\,
       \mathcal H_N(w_R,X_m^{-1})\prod_{j=1}^{m}D_j,
           &&D_j=d_N(X_j)^{\epsilon_j}X_j^{-N\sigma_j}.
\end{split}
\tag{9}
\]
Here \(\sigma_1=1\), \(\sigma_2=0\), and the other prefix
selections are zero. The scalars are respectively
\(\kappa_N^{m+k_4}N^{m+k_4-1}\) and
\(\kappa_N^{m+k_4}N^{m+k_4}\). There is no selection inside
the already contracted terminal transform. The inverse terminal
argument in the uncontracted chart is unchanged.

**Theorem 1.** Choose any proved right state for the same chart, with
its original height, type, selection, window and neighborhood conditions.
If its calibrated bound is
\(G_{R,N}\le-\Delta_R-K_R+\eta_0+O_m(\log N/N)\), then
for every \(\tau>0\),
\[
 |\mathscr J_{\sigma,N}|\le C_{m,\tau}N^{A_{m,\tau}}
 \exp N\left\{M_{\rm open}-d_*-\Delta_R-K_R
          -\frac1{20000}\sum_{i\in F}p_i+\tau\right\},
 \quad p_i=\min\{1,\sigma_i+400(\operatorname{clamp}_P x_i-a_i)^2\}.
\tag{10}
\]
Both parities, fixed small ordinary-color disks and fixed color jets
on smaller disks are retained.

*Proof.* The exact cut inequality, equation (10) of completed boundary
propagation, accepts the literal boundary rates (7) for arbitrary
boundary products; its subsequent boundary menus are applications, not
hypotheses of that algebraic identity. Insert (8) and the chosen right
bound. The four clean closing losses and the free occupation charges
are unchanged. Spend one total source tolerance within \(\tau\).
The finite integer-weighted products are holomorphic, so Cauchy's
formula proves the color statement without differentiating modulus
halfweights. No middle argument is reapplied to the selected prefix.
This proves (10). \(\square\)

The available right menus include the contracted unselected plateau
suffix, every listed contracted selected-terminal state, and the
uncontracted coherent or active-private-tail state on their respective
domains. The two charts are not mixed. Empty free intervals or shorter
words retain exactly the old safety and window-placement hypotheses.
This is additional source coverage beyond safe interior plateau
selections, not permission to put an unproved state in the right boundary.

## 3. A genuine but small selected overlap into the early interval

For each fixed reference there is \(\rho_*>0\), fixed before \(N\),
such that (4) retains a gap \(d_*/2\) on
\[
 x_1\in[11/50-\rho_*,9/20],\qquad x_2\in P,
 \qquad \Im z_1=H_\eta,\quad\Im z_2=c_2.
\tag{11}
\]
One may require \(\rho_*<1/100\). This is a complete finite-source
upper bound on an additional real interval, rather than a phase-only
continuation.

To prove it, square the allocated modulus and use the Laurent polynomial
\[
             B_N(X,Y)=K_{\eta,N}(w;X,Y)^2d_N(Y)^\eta.
\tag{12}
\]
The exact cap has degree at most \(N-1\) in \(X\), the conditional
kernel has \(X\)-band \([-(N-1),0]\) and \(Y\)-degree at most
\(N-1\), and \(\deg d_N\le N-1\). Thus the bands of (12) lie in
\[
 X:\ [-4N+2,2N-4],\qquad
 Y:\ [0,(2+\eta)(N-1)].
\tag{13}
\]
These are exact coefficient-support statements, independent of zeros
and cancellations. Apply the
[horizontal complex-extension theorem](research_mixed_horizontal_complex_extension.md)
to (12), with rate
\(2M_{12}+2\Re p_R(x_2-a_2)-2d_*\), and added rate allowance
\(d_*\). The compact label includes a closed smaller original
ordinary-color disk. Take square roots of moduli, never holomorphic
square roots. The resulting neighborhood contains (11), comparing
\(x_1<11/50\) to the original endpoint \(11/50\); the rate is
independent of \(x_1\). This proves the stated gap, with any desired
further finite-source tolerance. The same argument gives a small
complex tube around that left endpoint, so short vertical pieces
contained in that tube are also bounded.

Substituting this new bound for (4) gives (10) on (11), with \(d_*/2\)
in place of \(d_*\). The second coordinate and every subsequent
prefix target remain in their old domains; no later supporting plane
is evaluated at an enlarged target. Cauchy gives the same ordinary-color
jets on smaller disks. This proves an actual selected early overlap,
but supplies no quantitative width reaching \(1/10\).

## 4. The remaining early selected gate

The unselected early pair at the original first-four height \(c_1<0\)
cannot be used for the first selected source without its exact factor:
\[
                         |X_1^{-N}|=e^{-2\pi Nc_1}.
\tag{14}
\]
That is a positive exponential debit. The selected estimates above use
their own positive-height contours and the complete selected polynomial.

For the rest of \([1/10,11/50]\), the unpaid product is precisely (2)
with its outgoing halfweight, original calibrated tangent (3), and
positive first height (1). The small overlap (11) does not pay that
entire interval or the outer graph joining it to the zero-height first
collar. In particular the negative-height one-wall proof uses
\(\theta(e^{2\pi c}R,V-A)\le\theta(R,V-A)\), which requires
\(c\le0\); it is not a source bound at the positive heights (1).
An independent argument is needed there.

The preceding statements do not identify different-height plateau
integrals, bound every first-coordinate interval, or establish a whole
open-source, knot or short-filling theorem.
