# The current general frontier

The goal is the broad original-source equality with actual hyperbolic volume. The specified families and local identities establish parts of that argument; they do not complete the general conjecture.

## The target: arbitrary bridge width and long twist regions

Fix any $2k$-strand plat, $k\geq2$, whose designated crossing circles give a hyperbolic augmented exterior. The [general target](../../notes/general_augmented_plat_long_twist_target.md) asks for the original Kashaev volume equality whenever every independent signed twist parameter exceeds a diagram-dependent threshold in absolute value. The twists are fixed before the quantum level $N$ tends to infinity. A matching test specifies the knot parity patterns. Hyperbolicity of a knot alone does not certify every chosen augmentation.

There are now exact [complete-color](../../notes/actual_augmented_plat_phase_compiler.md) and [all-color](../../notes/actual_mixed_augmented_plat_compiler.md) formulas for this source at every fixed plat width. They retain the normal terms at repeated roots, all phases, and the factorial rates. The [entire core function](../../notes/actual_augmented_plat_entire_core_color_packet.md) makes the Fourier proposal concrete: its coefficients and Parseval identity are exact at both level parities.

The [matching-graph certificate](../../notes/general_augmented_plat_matching_geometry.md) supplies actual complete geometry, primitive markings, and a positive ideal triangulation when its positive realization is provided. The [geometric endpoint theorem](../../notes/actual_augmented_plat_geometric_channel_endpoint.md) locates the complete disk trace at $c=-2$ and explains why its marked longitude contains information lost by the trace coordinate.

More generally, the [positive gluing and optimization criterion](../../notes/general_marked_positive_gluing_certificate.md) proves directly that an interior volume critical point on an actual ideal triangulation gives complete geometry and the unique maximum. Its algebraic certificate does not require a right-angled polyhedron. It does require the actual topology and a strict critical point.

**The main gap remains:** prove a uniform complex asymptotic for the full source, suppress its physical aliases and other colors, and show that its selected period is the geometric one with nonzero coefficient. Exact Fourier reconstruction and a positive geometric triangulation do not establish those estimates. The endpoint is an internal channel coordinate; the color deficit is its Fourier-dual parameter.

One exact shortcut is excluded: an [endpoint tensor calculation](../../notes/actual_central_endpoint_magnetic_phase_obstruction.md) rules out making all local magnetic entries positive by separate diagonal leg gauges at every level. It does not rule out a positive coherent resummation or a suitable global energy estimate.

The [source-divisor theorem](../../notes/general_long_filling_source_divisor.md) weakens one transfer hypothesis: the leading density may vanish at completeness provided its analytic germ is nonzero. With the other hypotheses supplied, it gives equality outside an analytic exceptional divisor, including density one of long slopes in each signed box. A monomial times a unit gives all independently long slopes. This is a conditional general theorem, not a proof for all plats.

## 1. Full source transport

**Available:** a general exact four-port Racah formula; a uniform local scalar volume theorem; polynomial local Gram costs throughout the supported folded domain; exact typed sewing identities.

The [all-partner theorem](../../notes/actual_all_partner_first_normal_bound.md) now bounds the first normal on all 64 reflected branches, including every genuine nonempty tail. It uses a positive presentation of the full generic source to supply the information missing from the earlier two-current reduction. Fixed higher jets are controlled on face-supported and tail-free branches.

The preceding results include equality of all 64 partner root values, exact weighted cluster-current identities, and a polynomial spectral gap for the folded Racah polynomial. The all-partner first-normal proof supplies additional information: root values and spectral derivatives alone do not control a nonempty generic normal tail.

**Missing:** arbitrary higher partner normals and a sharp estimate for the completed physical changes of pairing. The first-order scalar estimate does not bound the whole change of physical frame. An [exact cluster-projector obstruction](../../notes/actual_cluster_projector_positive_metric_obstruction.md) rules out making both physical cluster decompositions orthogonal in one positive Hermitian metric; weighted nonorthogonal estimates remain possible.

**What would close this step:** a source-preserving metric or equivalent global energy estimate whose source and dual costs are subexponential, and whose action retains the true shared color constraints. The corresponding lower bound must establish a nonzero geometric overlap whenever cancellation is possible.

## 2. The actual global geometric problem

**Available:** actual full-source hyperbolic volume equality for every retained cylindrical robust Conway factor and every independently sufficiently long fixed signed integral filling of any subset of its auxiliary circles, with arbitrary finite branching and turn depth. The proof gives explicit cells, ordered face matching, the actual crossing-disk cut, four-step edge cycles, and complete cusp horospheres.

The [signed extension](../../notes/actual_odd_conway_long_filling_volume.md) now includes arbitrary fixed pure or signed odd coupons on $C(FG)$, with robust children. Every expression has a constructive pattern giving one main component and hence knots after all auxiliary fillings. Its full quantum rate is proved equal to the actual filled volume, with the changed primitive meridians retained.

The [arbitrary-width seed extension](../../notes/actual_conway_arbitrary_width_seeds.md) permits every seed $h^s$, $s\geq3$, mixed within that grammar, and also covers isolated $C(h^s)$ for $s\geq4$. It is a broader established application of the method; arbitrary plats remain outside it.

The positive source maximum is algebraic and has a rational Bloch lift. For these cylindrical factors, the source atoms now have degree-one geometric cells in the actual complement. For open robust expressions, every interior output has a unique strict optimizer, and its algebraic boundary conjugate is the modulus of a literal ideal quadrilateral port.

**Missing:** the corresponding theorem for arbitrary knot diagrams and a general comparison with the knot's quantum A-operator. The new auxiliary-color parameters do have an [actual marked peripheral branch](../../notes/actual_conway_marked_peripheral_deformation.md), with a calibrated action primitive. This is distinct from the Newton output, which remains a quadrilateral modulus; elimination does not by itself identify an entire classical or quantum A-polynomial.

**New quantum progress:** the [whole mixed-color gap](../../notes/actual_conway_uniform_mixed_source_gap.md) and [uniform complex packet](../../notes/actual_conway_complex_window_laplace.md) now supply the source hypotheses for every retained cylindrical factor. The [long-filling source theorem](../../notes/actual_conway_long_filling_source_limit.md) gives a nonzero full-sequence asymptotic for independently sufficiently long signed integral full twists. The new exact face-map construction supplies its actual marked peripheral representation and absolute regulator, proving that the limit is the filled hyperbolic volume.

**The changed source is now controlled:** the [supporting-plane bound](../../notes/actual_odd_conway_uniform_mixed_source_gap.md) handles every alias subset and the entire physical color complement after half twists. The [finite complex packet](../../notes/actual_odd_conway_complex_color_packet.md) has a nonzero amplitude with every phase and lattice offset retained. Its action matches the [actual odd peripheral deformation](../../notes/actual_odd_conway_marked_peripheral_deformation.md) through an exact half-longitude shear on a marked double cover. The primitive filling equation then gives the smooth filled volume.

The [pure component obstruction](../../notes/actual_conway_full_filling_component_obstruction.md) remains valid for unchanged pure diagrams. The odd parity construction resolves it for the new class. Still excluded are the minimal $C(h^3)$ mixed-color filling case, arbitrary short fillings, arbitrary knot diagrams, and a general theorem identifying the entire knot quantum A-operator.

Cap-closed products split as componentwise connected sums, their original quantum sources multiply, and their entire angle polytopes split as Cartesian products. This preserves the actual sum of the exhibited geometric factor volumes without asserting a hyperbolic metric on a connected sum.

## Ideas kept in play

The cube and projected-chain constructions test sharp sewing with a shared core. The Fourier and Plancherel approach supplies explicit positive energies. The Karamata-inspired direction seeks cross-level control; its Tauberian formulation is not silently substituted for the direct asymptotic proofs. The pointed A-structure and regulator torsor retain the selected period and peripheral normalization.

These routes can inform each other. Their exact claims, counterexamples, and historical checkpoints remain available in the archive. The [detailed frontier](../../notes/general_equality_frontier.md) and [preservation record](../../notes/research_routes_and_preserved_ideas.md) retain the longer technical history.

## Reading status labels

“Full equality” means the exact family and hypotheses in its statement. “Local theorem” stops at the stated local object. “Conditional” means the listed hypotheses still have to be supplied in a new application. “Diagnostic” means a computation that guides research without establishing a general theorem.

An audit marked `PASS` verifies the checks implemented by that audit. It does not upgrade a conjecture, diagnostic, or restricted theorem to a general proof.
