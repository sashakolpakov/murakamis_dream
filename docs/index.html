Murakami’s Dream

Quantum invariants, geometric volume, and the missing global argument

The question driving this project is whether the original quantum invariant of a hyperbolic knot has exponential growth determined by the volume of its complement:

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(K)|=Vol⁡(S3\K).\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log|K_N(K)|=\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K).

We pursue this through the pointed quantum A-structure, exact finite source formulas, positive energies, and hyperbolic angle optimization. The original marked source and peripheral factors stay part of the problem.

The current target allows every fixed bridge width k≥2k\geq2: start with an essential hyperbolic augmented plat, then put independently sufficiently many signed twists into each designated region. The diagram and twists stay fixed as NN grows. The frontier separates the exact formulas and geometric certificates already available from the general quantum asymptotic still needed.

The general conjecture remains open in this repository. The notes contain internally reviewed arguments for specified families, general local identities, and obstructions to proposed extensions. Internal proof review, finite checks, and exploratory calculations are distinguished throughout this guide.

Read the argument in order

The research library organizes the work by question: equality results, quantum sources, geometry, torsors, Fourier methods, the cube, and the general frontier. Each route gives a main argument, its scope, and supporting notes. The figure gallery introduces the main objects visually.

Start with the equality results for the exact knot and link families and their quantifiers. Then read the mechanism: what the source formulas do, where weighted Cauchy enters, and why a local scalar estimate does not finish the problem. The geometry chapter explains what certifies the volume of the actual complement.

The current frontier identifies the remaining general steps. Notation and reproducing audits are reference chapters. The searchable archive contains the full technical notes, scripts, and recorded results; it is not a second reading list to complete before understanding the project.

Where the evidence stands

Part of the argument Current scope
Volume limit of the original invariant Signed Conway families with mixed seeds hsh^s, s≥3s\geq3, arbitrary fixed trees and constructible knot patterns, after sufficiently long signed full fillings; isolated C(hs)C(h^s) for s≥4s\geq4; specified pretzel and belt-stack families
Exact finite source identities All auxiliary colors at arbitrary fixed plat width; full matching-graph trace normal; Fourier core and Parseval; full-color three-port reflection doubles
Local quantum action Joint complex ordinary amplitude and polynomial fixed-derivative bounds for small channels; polynomial paired first-normal bounds on the physical grid; additional poles obstruct a whole-domain complex normal theorem; the channel stays discrete
General geometric branch Under a positive gluing certificate, marked rigidity and the actual critical scheme give a stationary branch with the true volume anchor; contributing source sheet still to be established
Common geometric framing An all-central selected frame is impossible for the certified right-angled cut; generic geometric strip flags localize strict physical-support failure to exactly two factors, at any bridge width
Whole positive source limit Every robust Conway expression; cylindrical factors have actual hyperbolic volume, while cap products split into geometric factors
General knot or graph equality Uniform complex source asymptotics, physical alias bounds and selection of the actual geometric period remain unresolved

The cube, Fourier, torsor, and alternative approaches are preserved. A failed intermediate estimate is useful evidence about the argument; it is retained beside the corrected scope.
