Which results use which?

An arrow points from a prerequisite to the component that uses it. The overview groups related statements and omits transitive arrows. Select a component, then a statement, to see its direct inputs, the reason each is needed, its downstream uses and the exact source locations.

This is an explicit, manually traced proof inventory. The checks detect missing source anchors, invalid article-import witnesses and cycles in the statement, component and article graphs. They do not establish the truth of a statement or prove that every dependency has been found. Article ownership is declared explicitly; absence from a bibliography does not establish proof independence. Component boundaries organize the results and are not additional theorems.
