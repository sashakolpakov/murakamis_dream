The geometric volume of the literal augmented pretzel source

29 October 2026. The link underlying the original ss-box source Bi=R−1LNB_i=R^{-1}L_N, for odd s≥3s\geq3, is a half-twist partner of the untwisted fully augmented pretzel link. It has s+1s+1 components, not one. Both complements have the volume

Vs=8s[Λ(π4+π2s)+Λ(π4−π2s)]. \boxed{\displaystyle V_s=8s\left[ \Lambda\left(\frac\pi4+\frac\pi{2s}\right) +\Lambda\left(\frac\pi4-\frac\pi{2s}\right)\right].} \tag{1}

Here is an explicit face-labeled geometric construction and a direct volume calculation. No quantum invariant is changed. In particular, volume invariance of the half-twist partners does NOT permit deleting the retained half twists from their original quantum source.

1. A right-angled ideal antiprism constructed explicitly

Put α=π/s\alpha=\pi/s, h=tan⁡(α/2)h=\tan(\alpha/2), and r=1−h2r=\sqrt{1-h^2}. In the ideal boundary of the Klein ball take

Ak=(rcos⁡(2kα),rsin⁡(2kα),h),Bk=(rcos⁡((2k+1)α),rsin⁡((2k+1)α),−h). A_k=(r\cos(2k\alpha),r\sin(2k\alpha),h), \quad B_k=(r\cos((2k+1)\alpha),r\sin((2k+1)\alpha),-h).

Their convex hull is the ss-antiprism 𝒜s\mathcal A_s: two ss-gonal faces and the 2s2s staggered side triangles. For example the side face (A0,A1,B0)(A_0,A_1,B_0), in coordinates rotated by α\alpha, has supporting plane

X+bZ=c,b=r(1−cos⁡α)2h,c=r(1+cos⁡α)2. X+bZ=c,\qquad b=\frac{r(1-\cos\alpha)}{2h},\quad c=\frac{r(1+\cos\alpha)}2.

Its spacelike Lorentz normal is (c,cos⁡α,sin⁡α,b)(c,\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha,b). The top face normal is (h,0,0,1)(h,0,0,1). Their Lorentz product is b−hc=0b-hc=0, exactly because h2=(1−cos⁡α)/(1+cos⁡α)h^2=(1-\cos\alpha)/(1+\cos\alpha). The adjacent lower side face has normal (c,1,0,−b)(c,1,0,-b); its product with the upper normal is cos⁡α−b2−c2=0\cos\alpha-b^2-c^2=0. Rotations and the top/bottom symmetry give every other edge. Thus ALL dihedral angles are π/2\pi/2. All vertices are distinct and ideal, all the face planes are supporting, and their normals are spacelike. This constructs the metric directly.

2. The actual projection polyhedra are DOUBLES of antiprisms

Double 𝒜s\mathcal A_s across one of its ss-gonal faces. The adjacent triangle pairs become coplanar and merge to quadrilaterals. The resulting ideal right-angled polyhedron 𝒫s\mathcal P_s has vertices

Zk,Uk+,Uk−,k∈ℤ/s, Z_k,\ U_k^+,\ U_k^-,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z/s,

and the following face cycles:

W+=(Us−1+,…,U0+),W−=(U0−,…,Us−1−), W_+=(U_{s-1}^+,\ldots,U_0^+),\quad W_-=(U_0^-,\ldots,U_{s-1}^-), Qk=(Zk,Uk+,Zk+1,Uk−),Tk+=(Zk,Uk−1+,Uk+),Tk−=(Zk,Uk−,Uk−1−). Q_k=(Z_k,U_k^+,Z_{k+1},U_k^-), \quad T_k^+=(Z_k,U_{k-1}^+,U_k^+),\quad T_k^-=(Z_k,U_k^-,U_{k-1}^-). \tag{2}

The white faces are W±,QkW_\pm,Q_k, the shaded faces are Tk±T_k^\pm. There are 3s3s ideal vertices, 6s6s edges, ss white quads, two white ss-gons and 2s2s shaded triangles. Every edge separates a white and a shaded face.

This is exactly the topological projection-plane cut of the untwisted pretzel port graph in the original source theorem. The kk-th crossing disk meets the adjacent planar arcs labeled Uk−1,UkU_{k-1},U_k; flattening its two sides gives the bowtie Tk+,Tk−T_k^+,T_k^- with common ideal crossing-circle vertex ZkZ_k. Between consecutive disks, the planar arc gives the quad QkQ_k. The two remaining planar regions follow the cycles W+,W−W_+,W_-. Thus cutting the two complementary balls along the crossing disks gives TWO copies 𝒫s(0),𝒫s(1)\mathcal P_s^{(0)},\mathcal P_s^{(1)}, with the displayed face identifications. This is a labeled cellulation of the literal link complement, not just a match of its cusp count.

In the untwisted graph, white faces glue across the two copies by their labels, while each Tk+T_k^+ glues to Tk−T_k^- WITHIN its own copy, fixing ZkZ_k and matching each outer index to itself. Restoring a half twist changes that shaded gluing to

Tk+⊂𝒫s(ϵ)↔Tk−⊂𝒫s(1−ϵ),Zk↦Zk,Uk−1+↦Uk−,Uk+↦Uk−1−. T_k^+\subset\mathcal P_s^{(\epsilon)} \longleftrightarrow T_k^-\subset\mathcal P_s^{(1-\epsilon)}, \quad Z_k\mapsto Z_k,\quad U_{k-1}^+\mapsto U_k^-,\quad U_k^+\mapsto U_{k-1}^- . \tag{3}

The CROSS-COPY change in (3) is necessary: swapping the two outer labels inside the same oriented copy would give the wrong boundary orientation. Equation (3) is the actual exchange of the two punctures of the crossing disk. It realizes the retained odd half twist of R−1LNR^{-1}L_N; its sign affects the peripheral marking, not this metric volume. The ordinary circle is not filled or removed.

Every edge cycle has FOUR right angles under either choice, including mixed choices at different disks. Symmetric small horospheres can be chosen at the two vertex orbits ZZ and UU. The white isometries preserve them. The two outer vertices of each shaded triangle have equal decorations by its reflection symmetry, so BOTH the untwisted and half-twisted shaded identifications preserve them. Thus the cusp rectangles glue without dilation to compact flat tori. This verifies completeness as well as the 2π2\pi edge equations.

The untwisted link has ss planar main components and ss crossing circles. With every half twist restored, following the main strands advances their labels by two modulo ss; for odd ss these form one main component. The literal quantum base therefore has s+1s+1 cusps. It is an AUGMENTED LINK base, not the filled pretzel knot.

We have constructed the same complete finite-volume metric from two copies of 𝒫s\mathcal P_s, hence FOUR copies of 𝒜s\mathcal A_s, for all these half-twist partners. No hyperbolic Dehn filling is used in this equality.

3. Direct volume of one antiprism

Send the north ideal point to ∞\infty in upper half-space by stereographic coordinates. For t=tan⁡(π/4−α/2)t=\tan(\pi/4-\alpha/2) and ρ=t−1/2\rho=t^{-1/2}, the vertices are

ak=ρe2ikα,bk=ρ−1e(2k+1)iα. a_k=\rho e^{2ik\alpha},\qquad b_k=\rho^{-1}e^{(2k+1)i\alpha}.

Cone the oriented boundary faces to ∞\infty, triangulating the two base polygons in matching fans. This is the ordinary signed pyramid volume identity, obtainable first with a finite cone point and then by taking its ideal limit. The two base-cone volumes cancel: the similarity z↦ρ−2eiαzz\mapsto\rho^{-2}e^{i\alpha}z takes one fan to the other, fixes ∞\infty, and their boundary orientations are opposite. No assertion that all these outside cones form a disjoint triangulation is needed.

Each side pair gives the two oriented ideal tetrahedron shapes

z+=12+i2t,z−=12−i2t, z_+=\tfrac12+\tfrac i2 t,\qquad z_-=\tfrac12-\frac i{2t},

with net volume D(z+)−D(z−)D(z_+)-D(z_-). Put β±=π/4±α/2\beta_\pm=\pi/4\pm\alpha/2. Their Euclidean triangle angles give

D(z+)=2Λ(β−)−Λ(2β−)=2Λ(β+), D(z_+)=2\Lambda(\beta_-)-\Lambda(2\beta_-) =2\Lambda(\beta_+), −D(z−)=2Λ(β+)−Λ(2β+)=2Λ(β−). -D(z_-)=2\Lambda(\beta_+)-\Lambda(2\beta_+) =2\Lambda(\beta_-).

The last equalities follow by differentiating the elementary Lobachevsky duplication identity and fixing its value at zero. Summing over the ss side pairs proves

Vol⁡(𝒜s)=2s[Λ(β+)+Λ(β−)], \operatorname{Vol}(\mathcal A_s) =2s[\Lambda(\beta_+)+\Lambda(\beta_-)],

and multiplying by four proves (1). At s=3s=3, the explicitly constructed antiprism is a regular ideal octahedron, so V3=4v8V_3=4v_8, not 2v82v_8.

4. Coverage and exact quantum scope

For coverage only, Kellerhals’s abstract, introduction and Theorem C identify the untwisted pretzel FAL with the 2s2s-chain-link family, state the four-antiprism decomposition, and state the same volume formula. The derivation above is independent of that paper’s proof. The Meyer–Millichap–Trapp abstract also records the study of fully augmented pretzel link complements. No literature volume-conjecture proof is being used.

Equation (1) is the genuine geometric right side for the unfilled source Bi=R−1LNB_i=R^{-1}L_N. It does not prove its quantum asymptotics, nonzero geometric coefficient, or the analogous assertion for its fixed-slope fillings. All retained half-twist phases, the original marked cut and the full peripheral line remain in the source theorem; they have not been replaced by the untwisted invariant.
