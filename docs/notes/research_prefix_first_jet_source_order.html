The actual prefix order and its first normal layer

Private written proof, 19 November 2026. Author-final for independent review.

The original cut pairing bounds the pole of the inverse prefix coordinate by one. Consequently the entire root intertwiner, not only its closing trace, is determined by the value and first meridian derivative of its actual reduced matrix. The part lost by the root reduction is a square-zero ideal. The admissible root reduced matrices form a self-annihilating subalgebra for the polar part of the original source pairing.

These are statements about the actual finite source and its common-meridian germ. They do not give a positive pairing, an elliptic kernel, or a nonzero prescribed closing. The complete directs are L, the physical endpoint duality, and R, the actual prefix algebra and pointed source. All order and first-normal arguments are proved below.

1. The two coordinates and the original source

Fix N≥2,d=N−1,q=eπi/N,Q=q2,m=r+1≥2,M=eμ,x=−M/q.(1) N\ge2,\quad d=N-1,\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad Q=q^2, \quad m=r+1\ge2,\quad M=e^\mu,\quad x=-M/q. \qquad\text{(1)} The quantum root is fixed. Let UU be the remaining rr-head occupation space, with its actual ordered totals E,F,KE,F,K. Put K(M)=MrK0,K0N=εI,ε=(−1)(N+1)r,𝒜={H∈End⁡(U):[K0,H]=0}.(2) K(M)=M^rK_0,\qquad K_0^N=\varepsilon I, \quad\varepsilon=(-1)^{(N+1)r},\qquad \mathcal A=\{H\in\operatorname{End}(U):[K_0,H]=0\}. \qquad\text{(2)} This is a fixed direct sum of full matrix algebras. No occupation index or remaining matrix block is replaced by a scalar.

L identifies every H∈𝒜H\in\mathcal A with the unique actual total intertwiner WH(M)W_H(M) having physical highest block HH. The identification and its inverse are holomorphic wherever the first head coefficients ea=MQ−a−M−1,1≤a≤d,(3) e_a=MQ^{-a}-M^{-1},\qquad 1\le a\le d, \qquad\text{(3)} are nonzero. This includes M=1M=1. Write ⋆μ\star_\mu for the transported product, so WH⋆μJ=WHWJW_{H\star_\mu J}=W_HW_J. L’s opposite physical endpoint is the holomorphic linear unit ℒμH=(WH)dd.(4) \mathscr L_\mu H=(W_H)_{dd}. \qquad\text{(4)} With gμ=K(M)1−Ng_\mu=K(M)^{1-N}, its exact source identity is Tr⁡(gμ(H⋆μJ))=Tr⁡(gμHℒμJ).(5) \operatorname{Tr}(g_\mu(H\star_\mu J)) =\operatorname{Tr}(g_\mu H\mathscr L_\mu J). \qquad\text{(5)} Both ℒμ\mathscr L_\mu and its inverse are regular at zero.

Keep R’s literal finite prefix and quotient: S(M)=∑a=0d(x−1L1F)afa,fa=∏j=1a(1−Qj),ℬμH=p0S(M)−1WH(M)i0.(6) S(M)=\sum_{a=0}^d\frac{(x^{-1}L_1F)^a}{f_a},\qquad f_a=\prod_{j=1}^a(1-Q^j),\qquad \mathscr B_\mu H=p_0S(M)^{-1}W_H(M)i_0. \qquad\text{(6)} Thus ℬμ\mathscr B_\mu is a holomorphic linear map on 𝒜\mathcal A, with every original prefix coefficient and cap. It satisfies ℬμ(H⋆μJ)=(ℬμH)(ℬμJ),ℬμI=I.(7) \mathscr B_\mu(H\star_\mu J) =(\mathscr B_\mu H)(\mathscr B_\mu J), \qquad\mathscr B_\mu I=I. \qquad\text{(7)} It is not presumed invertible at μ=0\mu=0.

For clarity its finite construction can also be read directly from L. If Ca(H)=(WH)a0C_a(H)=(W_H)_{a0}, then C0=H,Ca+1=−ea+1−1K−1[E,Ca],ℬμH=∑a=0d(−1)aQa(a−1)/2x−afaFaCa(H).(8) C_0=H,\quad C_{a+1}=-e_{a+1}^{-1}K^{-1}[E,C_a],\qquad \mathscr B_\mu H= \sum_{a=0}^d\frac{(-1)^aQ^{a(a-1)/2}x^{-a}}{f_a}F^aC_a(H). \qquad\text{(8)} Only the nonzero first-head arrows and f1,…,fdf_1,\ldots,f_d are divided by in this formula.

Set Dμ=DN(M)DN(MK(M)),DN(z)=zN−z−Nz−z−1.(9) D_\mu=\frac{D_N(M)}{D_N(MK(M))},\qquad D_N(z)=\frac{z^N-z^{-N}}{z-z^{-1}}. \qquad\text{(9)} The Laurent-polynomial convention for DND_N is retained. R proves the exact original highest-cut and pivotal source Tr⁡(gμH)=Tr⁡(DμℬμH).(10) \operatorname{Tr}(g_\mu H) =\operatorname{Tr}(D_\mu\mathscr B_\mu H). \qquad\text{(10)} The right side is first interpreted generically and then by its prescribed continuation. Since DμD_\mu is a function of K0K_0, it is central in 𝒜\mathcal A.

2. A simple pole for the entire inverse coordinate

Use the ordinary bilinear trace pairing on 𝒜\mathcal A. For a linear map TT, define its transpose by Tr⁡(ATH)=Tr⁡((T*A)H).(11) \operatorname{Tr}(A\,T H) =\operatorname{Tr}((T^*A)H). \qquad\text{(11)} There is no complex conjugation. This trace pairing is perfect on each Cartan matrix block, so T*T^* is uniquely defined. Put 𝒢μX=Xgμ\mathcal G_\mu X=Xg_\mu and 𝖣μX=DμX\mathsf D_\mu X=D_\mu X. Equations (5), (7), and (10), applied to the actual product, give 𝒢μℒμ=ℬμ*𝖣μℬμ.(12) \boxed{\qquad \mathcal G_\mu\mathscr L_\mu =\mathscr B_\mu^*\mathsf D_\mu\mathscr B_\mu. \qquad}\qquad\text{(12)} Indeed the source of WHWJW_HW_J is both Tr⁡(gμHℒμJ)\operatorname{Tr}(g_\mu H\mathscr L_\mu J) and Tr⁡(Dμ(ℬμH)(ℬμJ))\operatorname{Tr}(D_\mu(\mathscr B_\mu H)(\mathscr B_\mu J)). Move the latter’s central DμD_\mu and take the transpose in (11). Equality for every HH proves (12), including its order.

On a punctured sufficiently small meridian disk the scalar on each Cartan block in (9) is a unit. The left side of (12) is invertible. Thus ℬμ\mathscr B_\mu is injective, and hence bijective in the same finite dimension. In particular generic surjectivity does not require a separate recursive Cartan inversion. Equation (12) then gives the explicit inverse ℬμ−1=ℒμ−1𝒢μ−1ℬμ*𝖣μ.(13) \boxed{\qquad \mathscr B_\mu^{-1} =\mathscr L_\mu^{-1}\mathcal G_\mu^{-1} \mathscr B_\mu^*\mathsf D_\mu. \qquad}\qquad\text{(13)}

The source matrix has the exact expansion Dμ=d−1μ+d0+O(μ),d−1=K0−K0−12εm,d0=K0+K0−12ε.(14) \begin{split} D_\mu&=\frac{d_{-1}}\mu+d_0+O(\mu),\\ d_{-1}&=\frac{K_0-K_0^{-1}}{2\varepsilon m},\qquad d_0=\frac{K_0+K_0^{-1}}{2\varepsilon}. \end{split}\qquad\text{(14)} To check it, replace its denominator by ε(emNμ−e−mNμ)\varepsilon(e^{mN\mu}-e^{-mN\mu}) and its numerator by DN(eμ)(emμK0−e−mμK0−1)D_N(e^\mu)(e^{m\mu}K_0-e^{-m\mu}K_0^{-1}), using (2). All other factors in (13) are holomorphic at zero. Consequently 𝒞μ:=μℬμ−1 is holomorphic at μ=0.(15) \boxed{\quad\mathscr C_\mu:=\mu\mathscr B_\mu^{-1} \text{ is holomorphic at }\mu=0.\quad} \qquad\text{(15)} This is an operator statement for the full inverse coordinate, not just a cancellation after one closing trace.

3. Exact admissibility and whole-intertwiner first jets

Let 𝒪\mathcal O be the ring of holomorphic germs in the fixed parameter μ=Log⁡M\mu=\operatorname{Log} M, and set 𝒜𝒪=𝒜⊗𝒪,ℐ=ℬ(𝒜𝒪),V=im⁡ℬ0⊂𝒜.(16) \mathcal A_{\mathcal O}=\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal O, \qquad\mathcal I=\mathscr B(\mathcal A_{\mathcal O}), \qquad V=\operatorname{im}\mathscr B_0\subset\mathcal A. \qquad\text{(16)} It follows from (15) that μ𝒜𝒪⊂ℐ⊂𝒜𝒪.(17) \mu\mathcal A_{\mathcal O}\subset\mathcal I \subset\mathcal A_{\mathcal O}. \qquad\text{(17)} For the left inclusion, μZ=ℬ(𝒞Z)\mu Z=\mathscr B(\mathscr C Z). Write ℬμ−1=ℛμ+𝒬+O(μ),ℬμ=ℬ0+μℬ1+O(μ2).(18) \mathscr B_\mu^{-1}=\frac{\mathscr R}{\mu}+\mathscr Q+O(\mu), \qquad\mathscr B_\mu=\mathscr B_0+\mu\mathscr B_1+O(\mu^2). \qquad\text{(18)} Multiplying in both orders gives ℛℬ0=ℬ0ℛ=0,ℬ0𝒬+ℬ1ℛ=I,𝒬ℬ0+ℛℬ1=I.(19) \mathscr R\mathscr B_0=\mathscr B_0\mathscr R=0, \quad\mathscr B_0\mathscr Q+\mathscr B_1\mathscr R=I, \quad\mathscr Q\mathscr B_0+\mathscr R\mathscr B_1=I. \qquad\text{(19)} In particular ker⁡ℛ=V.(20) \ker\mathscr R=V. \qquad\text{(20)} One inclusion follows from the first identity; if ℛZ=0\mathscr R Z=0, the second gives Z=ℬ0𝒬ZZ=\mathscr B_0\mathscr Q Z.

Hence the exact image order is ℐ={Z∈𝒜𝒪:Z(0)∈V}=V⊗𝒪+μ𝒜𝒪.(21) \boxed{\qquad \mathcal I=\{Z\in\mathcal A_{\mathcal O}:Z(0)\in V\} =V\otimes\mathcal O+\mu\mathcal A_{\mathcal O}. \qquad}\qquad\text{(21)} Equivalently, an analytic proposed reduced matrix is the prefix of an analytic actual intertwiner if and only if ℛZ(0)=0\mathscr RZ(0)=0. Indeed (18) has only that possible pole. There are no successive higher-order divisibility conditions. The condition remains a full matrix condition on the extensive remaining-head seed.

There is a corresponding physical co-source for an arbitrary analytic Cartan-preserving reduced mark Z(μ)Z(\mu): ΞZ=ℒμℬμ−1Z=(ℬμ*(DμZ))gμ−1,Tr⁡(Dμ(ℬμH)Z)=Tr⁡(gμHΞZ).(21a) \Xi_Z=\mathscr L_\mu\mathscr B_\mu^{-1}Z =\bigl(\mathscr B_\mu^*(D_\mu Z)\bigr)g_\mu^{-1}, \qquad \operatorname{Tr}(D_\mu(\mathscr B_\mu H)Z) =\operatorname{Tr}(g_\mu H\Xi_Z). \qquad\text{(21a)} Its residue is exactly ℒ0ℛZ(0)\mathscr L_0\mathscr RZ(0). It is holomorphic if and only if Z(0)∈VZ(0)\in V. Thus (21a) transports an actual reduced marked closing to the opposite physical endpoint, but does not declare an arbitrary local window admissible. If Z=Z0+μZ1+⋯Z=Z_0+\mu Z_1+\cdots, its unrestricted finite part is ℒ0(ℛZ1+𝒬Z0)+ℒ1ℛZ0,ℒ1=∂μℒ|0.(21b) \mathscr L_0(\mathscr RZ_1+\mathscr QZ_0) +\mathscr L_1\mathscr RZ_0, \qquad\mathscr L_1=\partial_\mu\mathscr L|_0. \qquad\text{(21b)} Both moving-mark and moving-endpoint normal terms remain.

For such an actual reduced family write Z(μ)=Z0+μZ1+O(μ2)Z(\mu)=Z_0+\mu Z_1+O(\mu^2). Its entire physical root seed is H(0)=ℛZ1+𝒬Z0.(22) \boxed{\qquad H(0)=\mathscr RZ_1+\mathscr QZ_0.\qquad}\qquad\text{(22)} Applying L’s finite highest-block reconstruction then recovers every block of WH(0)W_H(0). Thus the first meridian jet of the actual reduced matrix determines the whole root intertwiner. This does not mean its first normal needs no further data: H′(0)H'(0) generally uses Z″(0)Z''(0) as well. Higher fixed jets follow by the ordinary product rule in H=𝒞Z/μH=\mathscr C Z/\mu. No quantum parameter or forced outward arrow is differentiated.

4. The lost root ideal is square-zero

The map ℬ\mathscr B is an algebra isomorphism from the physical intertwiner order, expressed with ⋆μ\star_\mu, to ℐ\mathcal I. After specializing it identifies the whole root intertwiner algebra with ℐ/μℐ\mathcal I/\mu\mathcal I. Reduction to the ordinary root matrix has kernel 𝒦=μ𝒜𝒪/μℐ,0→𝒦→ℐ/μℐ→V→0.(23) \mathcal K=\mu\mathcal A_{\mathcal O}/\mu\mathcal I, \qquad 0\longrightarrow\mathcal K\longrightarrow \mathcal I/\mu\mathcal I\longrightarrow V\longrightarrow0. \qquad\text{(23)} Every factor in the numerator of 𝒦2\mathcal K^2 has one μ\mu; (17) gives μ2𝒜𝒪⊂μℐ\mu^2\mathcal A_{\mathcal O}\subset\mu\mathcal I. Therefore 𝒦2=0.(24) \boxed{\quad\mathcal K^2=0.\quad}\qquad\text{(24)} In physical coordinates this is the kernel of ℬ0\mathscr B_0 with the actual product ⋆0\star_0, not ordinary multiplication of the highest blocks. Its unit kernel is consequently additive: (1+k)(1+l)=1+k+l(1+k)(1+l)=1+k+l, (1+k)−1=1−k(1+k)^{-1}=1-k for k,l∈𝒦k,l\in\mathcal K.

There is also an explicit splitting in the fixed prefix coordinates. By (21), constant matrices in VV belong to ℐ\mathcal I. The data of a class modulo μℐ\mu\mathcal I are exactly (Z0,[Z1])∈V⊕(𝒜/V).(25) (Z_0,[Z_1])\in V\oplus(\mathcal A/V). \qquad\text{(25)} The multiplication is (b,[c])(b′,[c′])=(bb′,[bc′+cb′]).(26) (b,[c])(b',[c'])=(bb',[bc'+cb']). \qquad\text{(26)} It is well-defined because VV is a subalgebra, by (7). This is a split square-zero extension with its literal bimodule; it does not erase that bimodule or its possible source value.

5. The polar pairing and reciprocal blocks

Define the symmetric bilinear, possibly degenerate form β(A,B)=Tr⁡(d−1AB).(27) \beta(A,B)=\operatorname{Tr}(d_{-1}AB). \qquad\text{(27)} Its central coefficient is exactly the residue of the original source. Taking the residue of (13) gives ℛ=ℒ0−1𝒢0−1ℬ0*𝖽−1,𝖽−1X=d−1X.(28) \mathscr R=\mathscr L_0^{-1}\mathcal G_0^{-1} \mathscr B_0^*\mathsf d_{-1}, \qquad\mathsf d_{-1}X=d_{-1}X. \qquad\text{(28)} The first two factors are units. Thus ker⁡ℛ\ker\mathscr R is the annihilator of im⁡ℬ0\operatorname{im}\mathscr B_0 for (27). Combining this with (20) proves the source-specific identity V=V⟂β.(29) \boxed{\quad V=V^{\perp_\beta}.\quad}\qquad\text{(29)} In particular VV contains the radical of β\beta.

Here is the exact pair decomposition, without a new spectral projector premise. The actual total Casimir ΩΣ=FΣEΣ+qKΣ+q−1KΣ−1\Omega_\Sigma=F_\Sigma E_\Sigma+qK_\Sigma+q^{-1}K_\Sigma^{-1} commutes with all total generators. R’s relations S−1FΣS=F1S^{-1}F_\Sigma S=F_1, [S,KΣ]=0[S,K_\Sigma]=0, Si0=i0Si_0=i_0, and p0F1=0p_0F_1=0 give ℬm(ΩΣ)=−MK−M−1K−1.(30) \mathcal B_m(\Omega_\Sigma) =-MK-M^{-1}K^{-1}. \qquad\text{(30)} At the root two actual weights χ,ψ\chi,\psi have the same number −χ−χ−1-\chi-\chi^{-1} exactly when ψ=χ\psi=\chi or ψ=χ−1\psi=\chi^{-1}. Polynomial interpolation on these distinct numbers puts every inversion-pair projector P𝔬P_{\mathfrak o} in VV, using the actual polynomial intertwiner p𝔬(ΩΣ)p_{\mathfrak o}(\Omega_\Sigma). It is unnecessary to assert that this full polynomial coupon is itself a projector. The matrices P𝔬P_{\mathfrak o} are central in 𝒜\mathcal A, so VV decomposes over these pairs.

Each physical Cartan weight space has dimension n=Nr−1n=N^{r-1}: choose r−1r-1 occupations freely and the last is uniquely fixed modulo NN. On a nonself pair, 𝒜𝔬=End⁡(Uχ)⊕End⁡(Uχ−1),β((A,B),(C,D))=χ−χ−12εmTr⁡(AC−BD).(31) \mathcal A_{\mathfrak o} =\operatorname{End}(U_\chi)\oplus\operatorname{End}(U_{\chi^{-1}}), \quad \beta((A,B),(C,D)) =\frac{\chi-\chi^{-1}}{2\varepsilon m} \operatorname{Tr}(AC-BD). \qquad\text{(31)} This form is nondegenerate. Equation (29) therefore gives dim⁡V𝔬=n2\dim V_{\mathfrak o}=n^2, half the displayed space. It also identifies 𝒜𝔬/V𝔬\mathcal A_{\mathfrak o}/V_{\mathfrak o} with V𝔬*V_{\mathfrak o}^* by (27). For a self weight χ=±1\chi=\pm1, the entire block is in the radical and hence in VV: no root matrix is lost there. These are bilinear statements, not Hermitian orthogonality or a positive resolution. A half-dimensional subalgebra need not be the graph of an isomorphism between the two matrix blocks.

The source in the root coordinates (25) is exactly τ0(b,[c])=Tr⁡(d0b)+Tr⁡(d−1c).(32) \boxed{\qquad \tau_0(b,[c])=\operatorname{Tr}(d_0b)+\operatorname{Tr}(d_{-1}c). \qquad}\qquad\text{(32)} The residue vanishes because I∈VI\in V and (29) gives β(b,I)=0\beta(b,I)=0. Changing cc by an element of VV leaves (32) unchanged for the same reason. Thus the normal layer is retained as a genuine part of the original cut, even when the ordinary reduced root matrix misses it. Formula (32) has exactly the original factors 2ε2\varepsilon and mm, not a freely chosen normalization of the paired sheets.

6. Finite conditioning, true normals, and scope

The construction is entirely finite and keeps every head cap. It also gives an exponential, rather than superexponential, bound for the regularized inverse. Fix the width and 0<η<π/m0<\eta<\pi/m, and set μ=u/N\mu=u/N, |u|≤η|u|\le\eta. L bounds highest reconstruction, ℒ±1\mathscr L^{\pm1}, and the finite prefix coefficients by eCNe^{CN}. In particular (6),(8) give ∥ℬ∥≤eCN\|\mathscr B\|\le e^{CN}. One may use the Hilbert–Schmidt norm on each Cartan matrix block to take the transpose in (11); passage to occupation operator norm costs only a polynomial in NN at fixed width. Also g±1g^{\pm1} are bounded on this collar.

The explicit expression used in (14) gives ∥μDμ∥≤C\|\mu D_\mu\|\le C there, with its removable value at zero: DN(eu/N)=O(N)D_N(e^{u/N})=O(N) and sinh⁡(mu)/u\sinh(m u)/u is bounded away from zero on the smaller disk. Equation (13) consequently proves ∥𝒞μ∥≤eCN.(33) \|\mathscr C_\mu\|\le e^{CN}. \qquad\text{(33)} On nested collars, every fixed jj-th μ\mu derivative of 𝒞\mathscr C costs at most CjNjeCNC_jN^je^{CN}, by Cauchy’s formula. These bounds include the actual meridian dependence of the prefix, totals, pivot, and endpoint maps. They are not zero-exponential-rate estimates for a geometric kernel.

All crossing and zero-framing scalars included in an actual word remain in its physical seed and reduced matrix. A separately prescribed scalar remains outside (10), with its own product-rule derivatives. Nothing here differentiates the quantum root or resets a missing endpoint arrow.

The result gives a single residue condition for analytic source admissibility, a first-jet reconstruction of the whole root operator, and the exact square-zero normal layer of its reduction. It does not estimate the extensive remaining seed of a particular braid. The reciprocal blocks in (31) are whole physical Cartan blocks, not selected geometric eigenflags. No local phase, geometric window transport, positive pairing, or nonzero prescribed closing is inferred from (29) or from perfect cut duality.
