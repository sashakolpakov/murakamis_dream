A hybrid spin-coherent lower chart for the actual Euler star

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

The actual finite Euler star has a nonzero ordinary-binomial coherent kernel near its lower donor row. Physical recipients may range over any fixed compact set off incidence. Recipients in a factorial gauge are allowed near their lower coherent row. The latter caps are restored by exponentially small, relative, finite Cauchy tails. All donor slots and all physical recipient caps are exact throughout.

This supplies a mixed chart for every resolved lower-donor configuration whose physical K,fK,f are units, using a physical recipient when p≠0p\ne0 and a factorial recipient at p=0,A=1p=0,A=1. It does not put the rest of a closed period in this chart. All gauge interfaces, physical crossings, closing rows, and meridian derivatives remain.

Complete directs G and C are pinned below. G supplies the literal star and its resolved rational correspondence. C supplies the finite-degree padding method; the different recurrence, its estimates and graph identification are proved here.
