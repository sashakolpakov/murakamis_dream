The actual augmented plat as a cubic graph with a marked perfect matching

30 October 2026. This gives a geometry/topology interface for an arbitrary fixed 2k-plat, k>=2, rather than only a two-port Conway tree. Its input is the ACTUAL diagram, its designated crossing boxes and their retained half twists. The reversible cut produces a plane cubic graph Delta with a specified perfect matching D. A positive labeled right-angled ideal rectification certificate for Delta then gives the actual complete augmented complement, with volume 2 Vol(Q_Delta).

The certificate is an explicit geometric hypothesis. This note does not prove its existence for every reduced plat, the growing-color source limit for that plat, or a generic auxiliary deformation from an unmarked polyhedron. It does identify the correct graph and exactly why the existing trivalent NC source theorem is only a subclass. Here k is the supplied plat width; no claim that this presentation realizes the knot’s minimal bridge number is required.

1. Start with a literal diagram and its crossing disks

Fix a 2k-plat word together with a disjoint collection of two-strand crossing boxes covering ALL crossings. The outside strands, original caps and cups have no remaining crossing. One may first take maximal twist regions, but the chosen boxes and their order are recorded data. Put a crossing circle around each box and retain its original twice- punctured spanning disk. Remove full twists, retaining epsilon_i in {0,+1,-1} as its half-twist sign. Full twist integers remain recorded in the peripheral collar marking when they are to be restored.

For the cut first remove these half twists as well; their effects are restored by the explicit shaded pairings in section 4. The resulting flat diagram has main arcs in the projection sphere and perpendicular crossing circles. Let r be the number of crossing disks. Assume every main component meets at least one of them, so that the pieces outside the punctures are exactly 2r intervals pairing 4r labeled terminals. For a nontrivial knot with all its crossings in the designated boxes this requirement is automatic. An extra circle with no disk incidence must not be silently discarded.

At disk i choose the four CYCLIC local labels C_i0,…,C_i3 so that its untwisted strands restore the pairs 0–1 and 2–3. This is a local crossing-disk chart; it is not an unrecorded permutation of the global plat endpoints. Its upper half disk has the two sides

S_i0=(O_i,C_i1,C_i2),
S_i1=(O_i,C_i3,C_i0).                              (1)

The diagram’s actual outside intervals, including all plat cups and caps, give a perfect matching of the C terminals. Two matched ends are the two ends of ONE actual main segment. The embedding and cyclic orders are retained, not just its abstract endpoint permutation.

2. Construct Delta and its marked matching without a geometry guess

Give Delta one vertex v_i0 and v_i1 for each pair (1). Add the edge d_i=v_i0 v_i1 and mark it as a crossing-disk edge. For each actual outside main segment join the two vertices whose shaded triangles contain its two terminal labels. The incident edge order at each vertex is exactly the cyclic order in its triangle (1).

Each vertex has one disk edge and two main-segment edges. Hence Delta is a plane CUBIC ribbon graph, and

D={d_i} is a specified perfect matching,
|V(Delta)|=2r, |E(Delta)|=3r.                     (2)

The embedding follows by contracting the small shaded sectors to their centers and following the actual planar identity strips. This operation does not move an endpoint through another endpoint. Loops, parallel edges or repeated facial walks are recorded if the supplied diagram produces them. They are not repaired by an arbitrary graph isomorphism or by changing the knot diagram.

The original bowtie boundary graph is the MEDIAL graph of Delta:

  • An ideal vertex x_e corresponds to an edge e of Delta. For e=d_i it is O_i, the crossing-circle semicircle. Otherwise it is the actual main segment represented by e.
  • The three edges incident to v give the shaded triangle T_v.
  • The other facial walks are the original white projection regions; their edge labels are the face walks of Delta.

This identification follows at a main segment by concatenating its two oriented shaded sectors, and at O_i by its two half-disk sectors. These local rotations determine EVERY medial face cycle. If the graph is a cellular connected sphere embedding, Euler gives r+2 white faces; the cut then has 3r vertices, 6r edges and 3r+2 faces. These counts are checks of the labeled ribbon construction, not its topological proof.

3. A finite sufficient geometric certificate

The required input Q_Delta is a convex three-dimensional ideal polyhedron with the following EXACT labeled incidence:

  1. Its ideal vertices are distinct points x_e for e in E(Delta).
  2. Its shaded faces are triangles T_v, with the three incident edge labels in the original cyclic order at v.
  3. Its white faces F_f follow the original face walks of Delta.
  4. Every edge separates one shaded and one white face, with dihedral angle pi/2. All indicated faces and edges are nondegenerate.

A concrete certificate consists of future null vertex lifts, outward spacelike face normals, their incidence equalities, strict nonincident supporting inequalities, and the labeled convex-hull face cycles. Adjacent face normals have zero Lorentz product. Finite incidence and strict inequalities certify the asserted convex three-dimensional polyhedron. An unlabeled polyhedron, matching volume or matching cusp count is not a certificate for this diagram.

This hypothesis automatically excludes degenerate graph data that cannot be the stated polyhedral boundary. It is uniform in k and r; there is no restriction to a trivalent source partition or to a tree of four-port metric cells.

4. The actual face quotient, including every half-twist sign

Cut the actual flat augmented complement along its projection sphere. In each projection ball, each crossing-circle semicircle is boundary parallel by its original half disk. Drilling it and opening THAT half disk cancels the introduced handle in the explicit semicircle collar. The half disks are disjoint, so all cancellations give one cut ball in each half. Main segments on its boundary give ideal ends and do not add an interior handle. Its boundary is precisely the labeled medial sphere of section 2.

By the certificate, this is also the oriented labeled boundary of Q_Delta. The boundary cell identification extends over the ball by radial extension after choosing interior points. Take two copies Q+,Q- with opposite abstract orientations. White faces glue across copies by their original projection labels. At an even disk glue within each copy, fixing O_i and matching

C_i1 -> C_i0,       C_i2 -> C_i3.                  (3)

At an odd disk glue across the two copies:

S_i0+ -> S_i1-: C_i1+ -> C_i3-, C_i2+ -> C_i0-,
S_i1+ -> S_i0-: C_i3+ -> C_i1-, C_i0+ -> C_i2-.    (4)

In both lines the crossing-circle vertex maps to the same disk’s vertex in the indicated copy. The reverse maps are inverses.

For (4), rotating one side of the twice-punctured disk by ±pi sends (y,z) to (-y,-z), with the projection line z=0. It exchanges its two punctures AND the two half disks. This gives exactly (4) for either sign. The opposite signs retain different collar paths, differing by one full twist; they are not the same peripheral marking.

Reversing these literal cuts recovers the original augmented plat exterior. Thus the quotient is the ACTUAL diagram with its original disk labels, main components and half-twist pattern. No assertion about all abstract cubic graphs being realized by a chosen plat is needed: Delta was constructed from that given plat.

5. Edge holonomy and complete cusps

Every ideal vertex link of Q_Delta is a rectangle: four right-angled faces alternate shaded and white there. Its two opposite shaded sides have equal length. Scale its horosphere so that both have length one, independently at every ideal vertex. Consequently every shaded ideal triangle has its canonical length-one horocycle at each corner. Use the identical decoration in the second copy.

Every prescribed shaded triangle map, including the corner exchange in (4), preserves these canonical horocycles. White maps preserve the copied decorations. At each boundary edge the disk partner is distinct from itself: the two triangles paired along a matching edge share only their marked vertex O_i. An alternating disk/white cycle has four pieces, for both (3) and (4), and has total angle 2pi.

Here is also the pointwise holonomy check. The edge cycle fixes its two ideal endpoints and preserves the chosen horosphere at each. Its translation/dilation along the edge is therefore zero. Its rotation angle is the sum of the four right angles, namely 2pi. An orientation-preserving hyperbolic isometry fixing both endpoints is determined by these dilation and rotation parameters; the word is the identity. Thus there is no leftover edge translation hidden by an angle calculation alone.

After a uniform horosphere shrink, the ideal links glue to finite Euclidean surfaces with no cone or dilation defect. The actual link-exterior cut identifies them as cusp tori. Their ends are complete standard hyperbolic cusps. The remaining part is compact, so the entire quotient is a complete finite-volume hyperbolic manifold. Its volume is

Vol(S^3 minus L_Delta,D^epsilon)=2 Vol(Q_Delta).   (5)

This proves a conditional geometric theorem at EVERY fixed plat width. The hypothesis is the explicit labeled positive certificate, not an assertion that a polynomial stationary point must be geometric.

The certificate also gives an actual POSITIVE ideal triangulation. Choose an ideal vertex a of Q_Delta. Triangulate every face not containing a by noncrossing diagonals, and subdivide each face that does contain a by its fan from a. Cone a to every triangle in the nonincident faces. Convexity makes these tetrahedra tile Q_Delta with disjoint interiors. Strict supporting inequalities put a strictly off every nonincident face plane, so each tetrahedron is nondegenerate with positive orientation. Use the identical face subdivisions in the second copy. All white subdivisions then match; shaded faces were already triangles. The actual quotient is therefore triangulated by positive ideal tetrahedra, with its complete edge and cusp equations inherited from the metric just proved. This is a constructive geometric triangulation, not an identification with a particular factorial source or an independently proposed potential.

6. Primitive crossing-circle paths are still local and explicit

At any crossing-circle apex O_i put it at infinity. Its four adjacent white/shaded planes are vertical; their intersection on a horosphere is a rectangle. After a Euclidean similarity its other ideal vertices can be written

(C0,C1,C2,C3)=(1,E,-1,-E), E=exp(2pi i x),
0<x<1/2.

This x is a measured local geometric rectangle parameter, NOT yet a Newton source variable for a general plat. Put c=cot(pi x)>0. The actual two-white-face longitude and the collar meridians are

lambda=-2(E+1),
mu_0=E-1,
mu_+=-2, mu_-=2E, mu_+-mu_-=lambda.               (6)

The elementary white and disk maps giving these translations are exactly the local maps (5) in the half-partner cusp proof. Their use requires only this rectangle and the original disk pairing, not a Conway prism decomposition. The parallelogram area of each (mu,lambda) pair equals the area of the TWO actual cusp rectangles. Thus these are primitive disk-framed bases, with shapes

tau_0=2ic, tau_+=2ic/(1+ic), tau_-=2ic/(1-ic).     (7)

The choice of + or - is the recorded geometric collar convention. Any proposed quantum variable must be calibrated against the actual braid/loop convention; a cusp Hessian does not select that convention. Further full twists retain their actual integer meridian shear.

Equations (6)–(7) give the COMPLETE marking. They do not construct the general plat’s mixed-source deformation or identify its action.

The main-component test is also literal. Let sigma be the outside terminal matching. At disk i let kappa_i be (01)(23) for even parity and (02)(13) for odd parity, in the cyclic chart of section 1. The main components are exactly the connected components of the graph on the 4r terminals with the sigma edges and the kappa edges. Every vertex has degree two, counting parallel edges, so these are cycles. After all crossing circles are filled, the result is a KNOT precisely when this graph is connected. Adding full twists changes neither kappa nor this criterion. This is an exact test on the supplied diagram, not a claim that every parity pattern produces a knot.

7. The exact relation to the older NC rectified-core theorem

The existing NC theorem starts with a different cubic graph Gamma: its 2b trivalent cup/cap vertices and its 3b physical EDGE coupons. Its crossing-circle diagram has the present matching graph

Delta=Fisher(Gamma).

Namely replace each vertex of Gamma by the triangle of its three ordered incident slots. The original edges of Gamma become the matching D; all other edges form those facial triangles. Thus |D|=3b and Deltais a union of 2b facial 3-cycles. The earlier construction of Q_Gamma plus 2b octahedra produces precisely this medial Delta boundary. Its formula

4b v8+2Vol(Q_Gamma)

is consistent with (5), since its half-polyhedron has volume 2b v8+Vol(Q_Gamma).

An arbitrary augmented plat does NOT automatically have this Fisher structure. Its matching complement can have other cycle lengths or nonfacial cycles. Even the crossing-circle count r need not be a multiple of three. For example the actual isolated h^4 compiler has r=4 and two nonmatching 4-cycles, so it cannot literally be a source with 3b edge circles and trivalent NC vertices. Its geometry was proved separately; it is not an instance of that NC theorem by relabeling.

Even facial triangles alone would not certify the existing original NC source: its black/white ordering and its two noncrossing triple partitions must also match the actual cups and caps. The graph, matching, ribbon order and original marked source all matter.

8. What replaces a four-port tree in the general plat

The full original quantum source is a planar Morse network of actual two-strand coupons, cups and caps. A sweep may have 2k active strands; coupons at different levels can share a physical strand. The finite operator product is therefore not a product of independently chosen commuting edge scalars or of unrelated theta evaluations.

On the representation side a cutting sphere disjoint from the crossing circles and meeting 2m main strands carries the ORDERED puncture meridians H_1,…,H_(2m), their flags, and H_(2m)…H_1=I, modulo the actual common frame. A pants decomposition of that punctured sphere introduces internal curve holonomies and their gluing frames. Matching their traces alone loses the twist conjugators. These frames form a centralizer torsor: with ordered eigenflags the regular semisimple gluing group in PSL(2,C) is multiplicative, and the noncentral parabolic gluing group is additive. An SL(2) formulation also retains its central signs. The original braid word supplies the literal transport, as in the all-bridge Hurwitz presentation already proved in the repo.

Thus a multiport replacement must preserve those marked sewing data and the full finite quantum source, including its first normals and recoupling coefficients. The present graph theorem supplies the actual complete geometric target. It does not provide the source’s selected period, its nonzero amplitude, its whole-color gap, or the identification of a general mixed-source action with this marked geometric branch.

9. Coverage and the precise existence gap

For coverage only, Purcell’s exposition already describes fully augmented links by cubic graphs with dimers, and states hyperbolicity for a nonsplittable prime twist-reduced diagram with at least two twist regions. The broad geometric-triangulation statement for sufficiently long fillings also appears in Ham–Purcell. No geometric existence or filling proof from those sources is used in sections 1–8, and no novelty of the cubic/dimer viewpoint is claimed.

Hyperbolicity of the original knot alone does not certify a CHOSEN plat’s augmented skeleton. A nugatory kink can be inserted in the diagram of the same hyperbolic knot; its designated crossing circle bounds a disk disjoint from the knot and gives a split added component. The chosen diagram and crossing regions require their own check.

For the desired all-k-bridge, all-long-twist-regions theorem, the remaining geometry existence step is therefore explicit: construct or independently prove the positive labeled Q_Delta certificate for the actual reduced augmented skeleton. The quantum source/period and marked mixed-deformation steps are separate. None follows merely from calling the diagram hyperbolic, prime, or a plat.

10. Independent finite checks and their scope

The executable audit constructs the original terminal intervals, their cubic marked graph, and the full ORIENTED medial face cycles in two independent ways. It checks 87 original Conway/compiler dictionaries, including the isolated h^s examples for 3<=s<=20, and 21 literal plat diagrams with 2<=k<=8. In every plat case all outside intervals and all upper/lower caps are retained; no free component is discarded. The 2k-plat test family has one odd layer joining the k original cap components in a chain, so the direct terminal test also gives a knot parity witness at each tested width. These finite checks audit the actual labels and construction. They do not infer a positive geometric certificate, twist reduction, or a quantum asymptotic from a graph.
