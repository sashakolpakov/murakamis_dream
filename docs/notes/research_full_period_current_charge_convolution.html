Full-period vertical currents as charge convolutions of reduced sources

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The full angular trace rectangle admits an exact inclusion–exclusion expansion in which every surviving vertex keeps its full original trace. Pairing the two ends of each vertical side then gives an integral of reduced graph sources with explicit endpoint kernel coupons. Multiplying all coupons at a shared surviving vertex before expanding them turns those insertions into finite symmetric charge convolutions. The resulting bound uses actual reduced sources, with their signed powers and full normals, instead of an absolute unfilled spectator norm.

This is the full-π\pi vertical current of the original trace rectangle. It is not the older one-step Ward current. The decorated Ward identities and the classical saturated-face factorization remain distinct. The formula below does not assume their face gap is a bound for this current, and proves no source noncancellation.

5. A quantitative estimate in terms of actual reduced sources

The classical saturated-face energy identity retains the surviving charges and imposes a compatibility condition involving the removed powers and charges.
