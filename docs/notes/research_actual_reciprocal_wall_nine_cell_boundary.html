The actual reciprocal wall on the nine-cell boundary

Private written proof, 17 November 2026.

The finite wall in the original middle five-point chart has an explicit local transport to the paired boundary faces of the proved nine-cell source. Its factorial gauge sees the exact endpoint factor N2N^2. After the remaining crossing factor and the actual Fourier adapter, the original wall has rank NN and norm NN. Its lowering-current defect is retained, with norm N|Q−1−1|N|Q^{-1}-1|, and changes the total boundary character by one. This is not an identification with the entire negative-ray current of a full-function period.

6. Current and analytic scope

Writing HL=LHL−1H_L=LHL^{-1}, the actual current equation is [HL,LWL−1]=LJL−1.(30) [H_L,LWL^{-1}]=LJL^{-1}. \qquad\text{(30)} The right side has been evaluated explicitly in (17),(23),(27), not discarded. All dual rows use the literal inverse adapters. The partial-factor generator HLH_L is not asserted to be the unchanged external coproduct. The weight commutator remains zero, since LL commutes with K1K2K_1K_2.

This is a local realization of the specified finite reciprocal wall and lowering defect on the actual nine-cell boundary. It neither makes the wall Hopf-natural nor relocates it to the marked cut. It does not identify it with the full negative-ray atomic current, its intersections, or its continuous cycle: those functions and chains still require their own source-preserving transport. All formulas are at the complete fixed odd root. Decoration derivatives do not supply a generic-meridian or off-root quantum identity. No selected geometric coefficient or whole scalar lower is inferred.
