The actual source lattice, companion monodromy, and its normal cut

Research construction, 27 October 2026. This gives explicit maps from an actual scalar quantum equation and its pointed source to the root monodromy kernel, cokernel and first normal equation. It also identifies the distinguished cut as a normal lattice functional, rather than assuming it is a cyclic invariant line. No geometric-component or regulator comparison is asserted.

1. The scalar source complex maps exactly to companion monodromy

Fix a primitive N-th root zeta, N>=2. Write E=k(Y), F=k(U), U=Y^N, sigma f(Y)=f(zeta Y). Start with a specified actual scalar operator

P(Q)=sum_(j=0)^d a_j(Q,Y) sigma_Q^j,
sigma_Q f(Y)=f(QY),

regular at Q=zeta, with d>=1 and a_0 and a_d nonzero there. Laurent shift operators can first be put in this form by a fixed shift. These choices retain the actual source; they are not minimal-operator assumptions. All maps below are initially over E and F, so no regularity at U=1 is being silently imposed.

Let A be the solution companion matrix, so its first d-1 rows shift coordinates and its last row is -(a_0,…,a_(d-1))/a_d. Put

J(Q)f=(f,sigma_Q f,...,sigma_Q^(d-1)f)^t,
T(Q)=A(Q,Y)^(-1) sigma_Q,
r(Q)=A(Q,Y)^(-1)e_d/a_d(Q,Y),
S_N(Q)=I+T(Q)+...+T(Q)^(N-1),
M(Q)=T(Q)^N-I.

The following are exact identities of operators before specializing Q:

(T-I)J=rP,
M J=S_N r P.                                      (1.1)

Indeed sigma_Q J-AJ has only its last entry nonzero, equal to Pf/a_d. The second identity is the geometric-series factorization. Thus the actual scalar two-term complex maps to the monodromy complex by J in degree zero and S_N r in degree one. The actual source is transported by the displayed operator, including every coefficient and shift.

At the root write T_0,M_0,J_0,r_0,S_0 for these operators. They are F-linear on E or E^d. Set

K=ker_E M_0, C=coker_E M_0, pi:E^d -> C.

The operator T_0 acts on K and C, with N-th power identity. There are canonical F-linear isomorphisms

J_0:ker_F P_0 -> K^(T_0),                       (1.2)
kappa:coker_F P_0 -> C^(T_0),
kappa([h])=pi(S_0 r_0 h)/N.                    (1.3)

Here scalar kernels and cokernels are taken for P_0:E->E as an F-linear operator. Equation (1.2) is the usual exact companion reconstruction, with no loss of the scalar section.

For (1.3), first (T_0-I)J_0=r_0P_0 shows that r_0 induces an isomorphism coker P_0 -> coker_F(T_0-I). To prove surjectivity, use the first d-1 rows of sigma-A to eliminate the first d-1 coordinates of any inhomogeneous equation; the remaining coordinate is e_d h/a_d. If e_d h/a_d=(sigma-A)v, those same rows force v=J_0 f, hence h=P_0 f. Multiplication by A^(-1) gives precisely r_0.

For an arbitrary F-linear T_0 the remaining map is

E^d/(T_0-I)E^d -> C^(T_0), [v] |-> pi(S_0v)/N.

It is onto by averaging a lift of an invariant class. It is injective because S_0=N+(T_0-I)R(T_0) for a polynomial R: if S_0v=M_0w, then

v=(T_0-I)(S_0w-R(T_0)v)/N.

This proves the claimed source-cokernel identification, including its normalizing factor N. It requires no semisimplicity of M_0.

2. The first normal map commutes with this source identification

Put Q=zeta(1+epsilon). For every Q-dependent operator write subscripts 0,1 for its constant and first epsilon coefficients. In particular

P_1=sum_j (a_(j,1) sigma^j+j a_(j,0) sigma^j D),
D=Y partial_Y.

In ambient A[Y] notation the same physical derivation is

D=Y partial_Y+N U partial_U;                    (2.1)

it preserves Y^N-U. A derivative at fixed U cannot replace it.

Differentiate (1.1), restrict to f in ker P_0, and project to C. The terms M_0 J_1 f and (S_Nr)_1P_0f disappear. Hence

pi(M_1 J_0 f)=pi(S_0r_0P_1f).                  (2.2)

Consequently the canonical first quantum-normal operator of the monodromy construction obeys

D_N(J_0 f)=kappa([P_1 f])/N.                  (2.3)

This is an exact scalar-to-companion normal-source map. In particular, if the supplied whole Q-germ has

P(Q)f(Q)=epsilon h_1+O(epsilon^2),

then [P_1f_0]=[h_1] in coker P_0 and

D_N(J_0f_0)=kappa([h_1])/N,
s=S_0r_0h_1.                                 (2.4)

The actual off-root section is not assumed P-horizontal. Its first coefficient f_1 is retained in P_0f_1+P_1f_0=h_1. The source class is independent of changing f_1; the selected section and cut are not replaced by this class alone.

For example the central derivative of the actual finite-sum lift constructs h_1 in the cited first-normal note. Equations (2.3)–(2.4) transport that actual source, rather than positing an abstract one.

There is also a useful scalar form of the connection symbol. Put W_P=sum_j j a_(j,0)sigma^j. For f in ker P_0,

kappa([W_P f])=iota(J_0 f),                    (2.5)

where iota:K->C is inclusion followed by quotient. To see this, apply (2.3) to g(U)f and subtract g times the equation for f. Since [P_1,g]=N U g’(U) W_P, comparison with the monodromy Leibniz rule gives (2.5), for instance by taking g=U. Thus the correct scalar symbol is the shift derivative W_P and not an assumed invertible scalar. Jordan degeneracies remain visible in iota.

3. Keeping the actual source lattice and the companion cut

At the root put A_0=k[U,U^(-1)], B=k[Y,Y^(-1)] and use the lattice

L=(1-Y)A_0[X], X=Y+Y^(-1),
(U^2-1)B subset L subset B,

from whole-source Newton transport. Actual reciprocity gives f_N=(1-Y)F_N in L, and an actual invariant annihilator acts on L by the banded matrices proved there.

The map J_0 embeds L into the companion space by its literal shifted coordinates. It need not embed it into a preselected regular power lattice if a companion denominator vanishes at the cut. Keep the fractional lattice J_0(L) and the source image S_0r_0(L), or retain the undivided scalar complex. In particular, localization by a_d followed by evaluation at U=1 is not an integral comparison theorem.

The original cut transfers without ambiguity to J_0(L):

cut_comp(J_0f)=-[D(pr_0 J_0f)]_(Y=1)
             =cut(f),                         (3.1)

because the first component of J_0f is exactly f. The derivative is the physical one (2.1). This is a normal co-source, not evaluation of the first component in the ambient reduced fiber.

More intrinsically, localize A_0 at w=U-1. Then wB subset L, and the cut is a well-defined functional on the thickened module B/wL,

ell_B([b])=-[Db]_(Y=1).                       (3.2)

It kills wL because every element of L vanishes at Y=1. The source fiber L/wL injects into B/wL and (3.2) restricts to its actual cut. This retains precisely the extra normal classes lost by B/wB. The same construction in the first companion coordinate transports this normal co-source through (1.1); no inverse of a vanishing peripheral factor is used to define it.

There is a canonical Bockstein description. If f in L has zero ambient fiber, write f=wb. The map

ker(L/wL -> B/wB) -> (B/wB)/im(L/wL),
[f] |-> [b]                                  (3.3)

is an isomorphism. Changing f by wL changes b by L. Surjectivity uses wB subset L. On this kernel the marked cut is

cut(f)=-N b(1).                               (3.4)

This is a normal-lattice source map, not an unproved invariant line in the actual monodromy kernel.

4. The distinguished X=2 normal sector

On L/wL the characteristic and minimal polynomials of X are C_N(x)-2, where C_N(Y+Y(-1))=YN+Y^(-N). The root x=2 is simple since C_N’(2)=N^2. Thus

e_2(X)=(C_N(X)-2)/(N^2(X-2))                 (4.1)

is a canonical rank-one projector on this abstract fiber. The right side is a polynomial. The cut satisfies ell X=2ell and ell(v_0)=1, so ell e_2=ell and identifies this line with k.

Its literal generator before specialization is

e_2(X)v_0
  =-Y(U-1)sum_(j=0)^(N-1)Y^j/(N^2 U).      (4.2)

It has zero ambient fiber. Its divided-normal value at Y=1 is -1/N, so (3.4) gives cut=1. The selected sector therefore sits in the normal defect, and disappears if L/wL is replaced by its ambient image.

Over the completed central ring k[[w]], the marked branch Y=1 determines a canonical etale idempotent e_0 in Bhat. It satisfies

e_0 Lhat=w e_0 Bhat.                          (4.3)

Indeed every source vanishes on this branch at w=0, and wBhat is contained in Lhat. Thus this is a rank-one direct summand of the completed source lattice; its fiber is the line (4.1). The physical connection D/N preserves etale idempotents. On this summand its logarithmic residue is +1, and dividing by w identifies it with the regular trivial branch line. The cut is -N times the constant coefficient in this divided-normal trivialization.

This supplies an exact local normal sector. It does not imply that an actual annihilator, companion connection, or crossing transport preserves it. Nor does it automatically extend to a rational line over the whole central U-curve: the generic algebra B tensor F is the field F(Y), Y^N=U, and has no nontrivial idempotents. The branch projector becomes algebraic after the marked Kummer splitting cover and exists canonically in the completion at U=1; cyclic deck shifts permute those generic branches. Descent of one chosen branch is additional data, rather than a consequence of its simple special eigenvalue.

Already at N=3, writing zeta^2+zeta+1=0,

ell(v_0)=1, ell(v_1)=3,
S v_0=-zeta^2 v_0, S v_1=2zeta^2 v_1.

Therefore ell S is not a scalar multiple of ell. The cut is not even a common quotient character of the full banded algebra. Transport over the actual A-curve still requires a proved map of the actual scalar/source complexes, not just this boundary projector.

5. The exact cyclic envelope has a parity defect

Let L_cyc=sum_(j=0)^(N-1) sigma^j L inside B. This is stable under sigma. The exact result is

N even: L_cyc=B,
N odd:  L_cyc=sum_(j != j_0) A_0 Y^j
                direct_sum (U-1)A_0Y^(j_0),
        j_0=(N+1)/2.                         (5.1)

Thus cyclic saturation is not always the ordinary power lattice. For the proof use the Fourier projectors E_j=(1/N)sum_l zeta(-jl)sigmal. Each character coefficient ideal is generated by the coefficients of the v_n.

For 1<=j<=floor(N/2), the highest term -Y^j of v_(j-1) has no other exponent congruent to j modulo N. It gives the unit generator. For j>(N+1)/2 the lowest term Y^(j-N) of v_(N-j) does the same. The constant character is present in v_0. These exhaust the even case and all but j_0 in the odd case.

For N=2m+1 the highest and lowest terms of v_m are the only terms with residue j_0=m+1; their coefficient is U^(-1)-1. Conversely the congruence Jf=f modulo U^2-1 implies that the j_0 coefficient of every f in L vanishes at U=1: the weighted reflection acts by -1 on this character there. All its coefficients are therefore divisible by U-1. This proves (5.1).

Neither enlargement makes the cut descend to an ordinary ambient fiber: the row (3.2) still retains the normal direction. Bare sigma does not preserve L, and the parity defect is an additional reason to avoid assuming a cyclic invariant cut line.

6. What the construction reduces, and what remains

The actual scalar equation, its first-normal source and its cut now have explicit maps to companion monodromy, with the exact cyclic normalization (1.3), (2.3), and the normal co-source (3.1)–(3.4). Thus an actual-to-geometric comparison may be checked directly on these pointed two-term complexes. It is not necessary to guess a global quantum connection or discard the source in order to make that comparison.

The remaining issue is substantive: construct a geometric complex and a map matching the actual scalar source class and this marked normal row, including its integral lattice at U=1. No general invariance of the X=2 sector, regulator identity, or selected norm formula follows from boundary algebra alone.
