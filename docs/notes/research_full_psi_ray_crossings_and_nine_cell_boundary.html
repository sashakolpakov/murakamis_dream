Full-Psi ray patterns on the actual crossing boundary

Private written proof, 17 November 2026. The entire negative-ray functional of the original scalar cycle has a finite, explicit replacement at each original crossing. We compute both signed replacement matrices and their literal nine-cell boundary arrays. This transports a supplied original exterior observation; it does not replace that observation by a fixed-zero cut or by scalarity. The single finite reciprocal wall of [W] is not substituted for a ray.
