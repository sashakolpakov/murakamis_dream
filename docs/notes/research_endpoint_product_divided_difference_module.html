A divided-difference module for original endpoint products

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

An exact six-channel divided-difference module for one original endpoint kernel gives a fixed-dimensional module for every fixed ordinary product. All source aliases are retained. A polynomial change of channels gives a controlled-degree matrix equation whose bounded-variable principal determinant has only the terminal and microscopic factors. Its original-turn transport is literal.

The result is algebraic. It supplies neither a whole-source rate nor a nonzero characteristic coefficient. In particular it does not identify the rate of a turned product with the terminal rate.
