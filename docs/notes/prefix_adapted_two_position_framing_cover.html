Actual prefix clocks give a quadratic framing cover with a twisted density

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. The physical Gauss meridian on an internal edge is not its developed Wirtinger meridian. Retaining the actual prefix adapter shows that ANY two physical position clocks have degree at most two on the framing torsor; braid length does not increase that degree. The density is generally NOT odd under the quadratic deck transformation. Its exact field trace is computed below, including a nonzero actual figure-eight example.

The local wave momentum still has a reciprocal deck law at the complete point. Together with an ACTUAL prefix clock insertion, the nearest two-cut bands realize a nonzero local orientation observable. None of these calculations selects the global framing cycle or identifies its field trace with the original quantum source.

1. Actual Gauss reconstruction retains both prefix products

At any level of an arbitrary mixed braid word, write the developed meridians as H_1,…,H_b in the convention of the actual holonomy proof. The physical meridians are mu_i=-H_i; their trace is M+M^(-1). Set

P_j=H_j...H_1,
V=mu_(j-1)...mu_1=(-1)^(j-1)P_(j-1),
U=mu_j...mu_1=(-1)^j P_j.                       (1.1)

For a fixed representation these are fixed determinant-one matrices; their entries lie in its marked algebraic representation field. Changing the framing by F conjugates EVERY prefix simultaneously. At a new braid level the Hurwitz words defining U,V change, but the following formulas are unchanged.

For a determinant-one matrix P write

B_-(P)=[[1,0],[-P_21,P_11]],
B_+(P)=[[P_11,P_12],[0,1]].

The exact inverse of the developed holonomy map gives

b_(+,j)=B_+(P_j)B_+(P_(j-1))^(-1),
b_(-,j)=B_-(P_j)B_-(P_(j-1))^(-1),
K_j=(P_j)_11/(P_(j-1))_11.                    (1.2)

In particular, if a_l=(P_l)_11, b_l=(P_l)_12, c_l=(P_l)_21, then the local physical entries are

e_j=b_j-K_j b_(j-1),
K_j f_j=c_j-K_j c_(j-1).                      (1.3)

These identities follow by multiplying the displayed triangular matrices. They are identities of the actual classical crossing graph; no developed matrix is substituted for G_j.

Classically x=-M and K_j=x/A_j. Consequently the SIGNED physical inverse position is

k=-K_j=M/A_j=(F^(-1)UF)_11/(F^(-1)VF)_11.      (1.4)

At completeness M=1, write B=A_j=1/k. At a literal finite quantum level x=-M/q; that extra q factor is retained below.

2. Degree at most two is independent of every prefix word length

At the complete point choose the reference cut meridian J=[[1,1],[0,1]] and the actual cut-framing chart

F(r,s)=[[r,1-r],[s,-s]], s!=0,
tau=r/s, A=1/(1+s).

The selected inverse flag is 1. Its rank-one projector is

P_fr=F E_11 F^(-1)
    =[[r,r(1-r)/s],[s,1-r]].

For ANY fixed matrix W=[[a,b],[c,d]], define

ell_W=Tr(W P_fr)
     =d+(a-d)r+bs+c r(1-r)/s
     =d+c tau+s[b+(a-d)tau-c tau^2].            (2.1)

Thus the ACTUAL second position is the ratio ell_U/ell_V, not ell_(mu_j). Put C=U-kV. Its fiber equation is

ell_C=0.                                     (2.2)

This is a quadratic in tau, with coefficients depending on s,k. Therefore a generically finite two-position chart has degree one or two, NEVER a degree growing with the braid or prefix length. Common factors or a linear fiber can reduce the degree. Etaleness means the actual local derivative below is nonzero; it does not force the degree to be exactly two.

There is a coordinate-free version at varying M. The projector coordinates r,s,t obey r(1-r)=st, and every ell_W is affine linear in them. Fixing the first physical clock is one affine hyperplane condition; fixing ell_U/ell_V is a second. Their intersection with this quadric has length at most two on a generically finite chart. All M-dependent eigenvalues and flags remain marked. No positivity or geometric action estimate is needed for this degree statement.

Let h_C=a_C-d_C, t_C=Tr C, c_C=C_21, and set

D=partial_tau ell_C=c_C+s h_C-2s c_C tau.

On the fiber,

D^2=Delta=c_C^2+2c_C s t_C
                          +s^2(t_C^2-4 det C).              (2.3)

Since UV^(-1)=mu_j has trace M+M^(-1),

det(U-kV)=(k-M)(k-M^(-1)).                    (2.4)

At M=1 this is (k-1)^2. Formula (2.3) follows by taking the discriminant of (2.2), not by treating C as a unipotent matrix. When c_C!=0, the deck map is

tau -> (c_C+s h_C)/(s c_C)-tau, D -> -D.       (2.5)

3. The actual density has an essential prefix factor

The complete framing density is Omega_cut=d tau wedge dlog A. Differentiate ell_U-k ell_V=0, fixing the representation. At fixed s this gives

dk=(D/ell_V)d tau.

Consequently, on the actual regular etale chart,

Omega_cut=ell_V/(B D) dlog A wedge dlog B.       (3.1)

The numerator ell_V is part of the physical prefix adapter. It generally differs on the two sheets. Thus D changing sign does NOT imply that Omega_cut is anti-invariant.

Write c_W=W_21 and h_W=W_11-W_22, and put

W_0=c_V h_U-h_V c_U.

For a genuine quadratic fiber, direct root summation gives

Tr_(two sheets)(ell_V/D)=W_0/(c_U-kc_V)^2.

Indeed ell_V has coefficients -s c_V, c_V+s h_V, d_V+s b_V. Reduce it modulo the quadratic ell_C; its remaining linear coefficient is s(h_V c_C-c_V h_C)/c_C. Divide the difference of its two values by the corresponding opposite derivatives D; the result is exactly the displayed expression. Multiplying (3.1) gives

Tr Omega_cut
  = W_0 B/(B c_U-c_V)^2 dlog A wedge dlog B.    (3.2)

This is GENERALLY NONZERO. The earlier odd-density special case V=I has W_0=0, as do some other special prefixes.

Nonzero field trace does not prove a nonzero period. On this rational chart (3.2) is exact. If c_U!=0, its primitive is

beta=(W_0/c_U)/(B c_U-c_V) dlog A,
d beta=Tr Omega_cut.                           (3.3)

If c_U=0,c_V!=0, take beta=-W_0 B/c_V^2 dlog A instead. The double pole has no ordinary logarithmic residue. An actual relative endpoint/cycle can still see it; it cannot be assigned or erased by replacing the source with an unmarked field trace.

These density coefficients are intrinsic MARKED character data, rather than adjustable framing parameters. For J=[[1,1],[0,1]], direct multiplication gives

c_W=Tr(JW)-Tr(W),
W_0=Tr[J(VU-UV)]=-Tr([J,U]V).                 (3.4)

Conjugation by the residual centralizer Z_t=[[1,t],[0,1]] of J fixes c_W and sends h_W to h_W-2t c_W. It therefore fixes W_0, lambda=c_V/c_U and gamma=W_0/c_U^2. Common conjugation of the marked triple also leaves the trace expressions unchanged. These are rational functions of its marked character. On a geometric representation cover finite over the marked peripheral curve they are algebraic over its A-curve field; they need not be rational functions of M,L alone.

The whole cyclic density calculation keeps the resulting exact source character 1-lambda^N. Its finite root averaging is a separate algebraic functional, not an automatic interchange with the selected Gaussian/source map.

4. The actual local wave momentum still detects the deck sign

Let primes mean conjugation by the SAME F. On a fixed k fiber,

C'=F^(-1)(U-kV)F=[[0,beta],[gamma,t_C]].

Equations (1.2)–(1.3), including their signed prefixes, give

beta=(-1)^j(k-M^(-1))p_j,
gamma=(-1)^(j+1)(k-M)/p_j,                    (4.1)

where p_j is the ACTUAL balanced/factorial local wave momentum. Equivalently e_j=(k-M^(-1))p_j and f_j=(k-M)/(k p_j). The product in (4.1) is compatible with det C=(k-M)(k-M^(-1)); no peripheral factor is discarded.

At M=1 the cut matrix in the current physical frame is [[1+s,-s],[s,1-s]]. The invariant Tr(J C), together with fixed s,k,t_C, fixes beta-gamma. Moreover direct differentiation of F gives

F^(-1)partial_tau F=s[[1,-1],[1,-1]],
D=s(beta+gamma).                              (4.2)

Thus the deck map sends (beta,gamma) to (-gamma,-beta), and

p_j -> p_j^(-1),
D=(-1)^j s(k-1)(p_j-p_j^(-1)).                (4.3)

This proof uses the ACTUAL physical Gauss reconstruction. It does not assume that G_j equals a developed H_j. All prefix effects have already entered U,V and (4.1).

For reference a genuine regular quadratic generic-meridian fiber has the corresponding twisted reciprocity. With the actual first cut inverse clock k_0 and p_cut=1, its physical meridian is

[[k_0,M^(-1)-k_0],[k_0-M,M+M^(-1)-k_0]].

The invariant pairing with C fixes a linear combination of p_j and p_j^(-1), so the other sheet is

p_j -> [(k_0-M^(-1))/(k_0-M)]
               [(k-M)/(k-M^(-1))] p_j^(-1).   (4.4)

This is stated on the regular chart where the displayed factors are nonzero. The complete specialization of (4.4) is (4.3). No complete-point sign formula is extended across its singular Gauss boundary by separately evaluating a zero and denominator.

5. A prefix-adapted odd observable comes from actual clocks and bands

The physical two-cut theorem proves for the nearest actual exchange bands, with their original input/output and factorial marks,

R_(+1,-1),0=p_j^(-1),
R_(-1,+1),0=p_j.

Put z=s(k-1). By (4.3),

R_(+1,-1),0-R_(-1,+1),0=(-1)^(j+1)D/z.        (5.1)

Its product with (3.1) still contains ell_V, so it need not be deck-even. There is an ACTUAL finite source/current insertion that supplies the missing inverse prefix. Telescoping (1.2) gives

ell_V=M^(j-1) product_(i<j) A_i^(-1).

At a literal finite level K_i=x Q^(-J_i), x=-M/q. Therefore the inverse signed quantum prefix is exactly

[(-1)^(j-1) product_(i<j)K_i]^(-1)
      =(q/M)^(j-1) product_(i<j)Q^(J_i).       (5.2)

It is a product of original diagonal edge clocks, with its full q/M meridian factor. It is not an invented density or a scalar chosen after source evaluation. Its principal symbol is ell_V^(-1). The ordered crossing/cap currents of this insertion remain those of the actual word; it is not declared central through them.

At M=1 the prefix-adapted principal band observable

ell_V^(-1)(R_(+1,-1),0-R_(-1,+1),0)
                           =(-1)^(j+1)D/(z ell_V)

is odd under the TWISTED density law, in the precise sense that its product with the density has the nonzero DECK-EVEN value

(-1)^(j+1)/(B z) dlog A wedge dlog B.           (5.3)

It realizes the normalized orientation section D/ell_V by an actual bounded clock/band insertion. The prefix length j-1 is fixed by the diagram, not by N. Formula (5.2) also specifies its whole meridian/lift adapter before any quantum jets are taken.

The uninserted quantum period has not been replaced by (5.3). Whether its original co-source/relative cycle provides this particular inserted section remains a separate actual-current comparison. In particular a nonzero local even density is not already a nonzero globally selected framing period.

6. A genuine figure-eight prefix has nonzero field trace

Consider the ACTUAL three-strand braid (1,-2,1,-2). In its Hurwitz convention put

A=[[1,1],[0,1]], B=[[1,0],[-u,1]],
u^2+u+1=0,
C=B A^(-1) B A B^(-1).

The positive Hurwitz move is (X,Y)->(X^(-1)YX,X), and its inverse is (X,Y)->(Y,YXY^(-1)). Direct multiplication fixes the triple (A,B,C) under the displayed braid: its equations are C A C^(-1)=B and C=B A^(-1)B A B^(-1). Every matrix has trace two and determinant one. A and B have distinct fixed flags, so this parabolic representation is irreducible. This is an explicit actual knot-representation example, rather than an arbitrary unrelated pair of matrices.

For the physical third strand the SIGNED prefixes are

V=BA=[[1,1],[-u,1-u]],
U=CV=[[u+1,2u+1],[-u(u+2),1-3u-2u^2]].

They give

W_0=-u^2(u+2)=2u+1 != 0.                      (6.1)

For instance at s=1,k=2, c_C=1+u and the quadratic discriminant is -3(u+4), both nonzero. Formula (3.2) has coefficient 2-2u, which is nonzero. Thus the blanket anti-invariance assertion fails on an irreducible parabolic representation of the actual figure-eight braid. The degree-two cover, the corrected momentum deck law, and the ACTUAL prefix normalization (5.2) remain valid.
