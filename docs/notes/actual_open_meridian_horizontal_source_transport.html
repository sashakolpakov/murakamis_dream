Actual open meridian transport has a source-preserving horizontal polarization

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. The actual open R-word has an explicit endpoint half-character under M->qM. An M-dependent SECOND- wire gauge removes that half-step and preserves the whole source on arbitrary current tensors. A first-wire alternative has its separate explicit Cartan Ward correction. This constructs a real word-induced meridian/current connection, retaining the original finite source, power characters, cyclic wrap and peripheral factor.

It does not yet turn the selected word class into the marked geometric period. The remaining insertion/current equation is stated explicitly in Section 7; no principal rational-density filter is substituted for the actual period.

1. The exact endpoint character of an arbitrary open braid word

Use the original zero-framed knot braid on b strands, with writhe w. The first cut is at strand zero. The second is an INTERNAL IDENTITY wire at a specified braid level and strand slot ell, counted from zero. Use the literal tensor convention

T_(c,d;a,b0)=ptr_other[A E_(b0,d)^(ell) B].

Thus d is the position immediately BEFORE the removed identity and b0 immediately AFTER it. Every ORIGINAL pivotal matrix remains in ptr_other; opening this wire neither removes its old cap nor adds a new pivot. Put nu=d-b0. Conservation gives c=a-nu for the uninserted tensor. All four physical head bounds and internal finite supports remain the original ones.

For one actual crossing with first input i and first output c_1, the already proved finite coefficient identity is

r_sign(qM)=x^(-sign) q^(c_1-i) r_sign(M)/tau_sign,
x=-M/q, Y=M^2,
tau_+=BC/x *(1-Y/A)/(1-YC/A),
tau_-=x/A *(1-Y/B)/(1-YC/B).                  (1)

The ratios in (1) mean their CANCELLED WHOLE finite products, as in the actual longitude-insertion theorem. No zero Pochhammer or peripheral denominator is divided separately.

To telescope the half-character use the INTEGER position potential J(a_vec)=-sum_i i a_i. At every checked crossing,

J_out-J_in=c_1-i.

The final/initial closed strands have identical positions and the first position has coefficient zero in J. The matrix-unit insertion at the second cut changes J by -ell(b0-d)=ell nu. Therefore exactly

sum_crossings(c_1-i)=-ell nu.                 (2)

No root square root or cyclic interpretation is used in this equality.

Write n=N-1, kappa_D=w-b+1, and retain the whole scalar

gamma_N=q^(n kappa_D),
L=gamma_N x^(-w) product_crossings tau_sign^-1. (3)

The original closed pivotal/framing lines give gamma_N, just as in the closed calculation: each old closed pivot contributes q^(1-N), and the residual zero-framing line contributes q^(w(N-1)). The opening has not changed them. All common full Cartan scalars are kept with their original twist cancellation. At a knot root only, kappa_D is even and gamma_N=q^(-kappa_D). Off root keep gamma_N=q(-kappa_D)(qN)^(kappa_D), with its mark.

For every raw finite open weight I the result is

I(qM)=q^(-ell nu) L I(M).                    (4)

Let T1 mean the SAME finite tensor with that cancelled whole insertion on every actual path. Put rho=q^(-ell) and D_rho=diag(rho^j) on the SECOND cut. Then (4) is the literal matrix identity

T(qM)=(I tensor D_rho)T^[L](M)(I tensor D_rho^-1). (5)

For the full ADO period multiply L by the ACTUAL whole ratio

g_N=d_N(qM)/d_N(M)
   =q^(N-1)(q^2M^2-1)/(M^2-1)
                   *(M^(2N)-1)/(q^(2N)M^(2N)-1). (6)

Its original phase cancels only by using the SAME phase lift on both sides. The root reduction of (6), its complete norm-zero lattice and all q-normal source terms are not suppressed.

2. The raw Fourier source and independent alias powers

Keep the full band kernel F(M,eta)=sum_nu f_nu(M)eta^nu, with the first input fixed and original output row still summed. Equation (4) reads

F(qM,eta)=F^[L](M,rho eta).                  (7)

The actual co-source is its constant coefficient, so CT_eta is unchanged by rho dilation, for WHOLE functions and every finite tail. No physical-position sum is replaced by a Fourier evaluation. For one uninserted first-input head a, the band interval [a-(N-1),a] has length N; root averaging computes CT exactly. For unwrapped currents keep an independent V_eta=eta^N:

Av_(N,V_eta)F=sum_k f_(kN)V_eta^k,
V_eta ->rho^N V_eta=(q^N)^(-ell)V_eta.        (8)

Thus an odd ell changes the physical root grid V_eta=1 to the antiperiodic grid -1. The original head CT agrees on these grids, but alias powers of a current do not. They retain the sign and the whole (qN)(-ell) character before normal differentiation.

On the complete root head-band window, dilation by rho is a genuine finite matrix on the Fourier-label array: conjugate diag(rho^nu) by its finite Fourier evaluation matrix, using the ACTUAL unwrapped representatives in that interval. It fixes the constant-mode source, commutes with cyclic Fourier-label shifts, and is unitary when |rho|=1. It is not obtained by silently identifying every tail with its residue modulo N. In a whole generic-Q Vandermonde frame, use the original full tensor and its last-row carry, not a root permutation extended without corrections.

3. The second-wire horizontal gauge preserves the entire source functor

On the SECOND identity wire put

D_2(M)=diag(M^(-ell b)),
T_h=(I tensor D_2^-1)T(I tensor D_2).

Its coefficient factor is M(ell(d-b))=M(ell nu) for EVERY tensor entry, including charged current tensors. It therefore cancels (4) for the uninserted open word:

I_h(qM)=L I_h(M),
F_h(M,xi)=F(M,M^ell xi).                    (9a)

The second partial trace is invariant under this conjugation on ALL matrices, not only diagonal or scalar reclosures:

ptr_second T_h=ptr_second T.                (9b)

The first source, original caps, peripheral line and every source current are unchanged. On other internal pieces transform their ordered operators by the same basis map. This is a strict whole source/current functor before normal jets. No Schur argument or source replacement is used in (9b).

There is an equally explicit GLOBAL braid-frame construction. On a level of b strands use G(M)=tensor_j M^(-j J_j), with first strand j=0. For one crossing its coefficient in G^-1 R G is M^(-(c_1-i))r. Thus (1) immediately loses its q^(c_1-i) factor under M->qM. All adjacent level gauges cancel in a word; the original closed diagonal pivots commute with them, and the first source is untouched. Cutting this global gauge at the second wire gives exactly D_2 above.

In the actual generic-Q Fourier frame this is a SCALED Vandermonde isomorphism, not a root-only kernel substitution:

W(M,Q)=V(Q)D_2(M)^-1,
W_(j,b)=(M^ell Q^j)^b,
W^-1=D_2 V^-1.

The node gaps are only Mell(Qi-Q^j), so no additional source norm-zero divisor has been inverted. Equations (5) and (9a) give the exact whole matrix meridian identity

(I tensor W(qM))T(qM)(I tensor W(qM)^-1)
   =(I tensor W(M))T^[L](M)(I tensor W(M)^-1). (9c)

Indeed W(qM)D_rho=W(M). The raw first-output sum of the diagonal j entries therefore has fixed exterior xi=Q^j under the meridian step. Its remaining action is the ACTUAL insertion L, not a scalar chosen from the geometric branch.

The forced normal connection on this whole frame is

A_M=(D_M W)W^-1=ell V J V^-1,
A_Q=(D_Q W)W^-1=(D_Q V)V^-1  (M fixed).

Thus D_M T^W=W(D_M T)W-1+[A_M,TW] on that wire; the commutator has zero second partial trace entrywise. At a complex root, ||A_M||<=ell(N-1) and the old bound for A_Q is unchanged. On |M|=1, W/sqrt(N) is unitary. These are source-frame bounds, not full crossing-word bounds. Along M(q), retain the chain-rule connection and the marked M^N and q^N powers.

There is a precise ROOT-only cyclic current corollary. For a charge-zero fixed-input packet put

F_a[j,c]=Q_N^(j(a-c)), 0<=c,j<N,
H_a(M)=F_a diag(M^(ell(a-c)))F_a^-1.

Then J_h=H_a J, Sigma H_a=Sigma, H_a 1=1. Every factor is diagonal in the same finite Fourier basis, so H_a commutes with the cyclic shift, its normalized difference and its centered primitive. Its M-connection is

A_a=ell F_a diag(a-c)F_a^-1,
Sigma A_a=0, A_a1=0, ||A_a||<=ell(N-1).

For |M|=1 this is an ISOMETRY of the actual cyclic source/current complex. In particular its distance to the zero-source current space is unchanged. This finite-packet statement is not extended to charged/unwrapped tails by forgetting their alias powers; those use the strict whole tensor map and (8).

The packet connection A_a is NOT the full matrix A_M and is not independent of the unwrapped input window. For 0<=a<N-1, let Pi_(a+1) be the root Fourier projector on residue a+1. Then

A_(a+1)-A_a=ell N Pi_(a+1),
H_(a+1)H_a^-1=I+(M^(ell N)-1)Pi_(a+1).

This is the exact branch carry as that head window moves by one. It is trivial in value at M=1 but not in its normal jet. The full tensor functor retains it, rather than treating A_a as a window-independent current connection.

4. Optional first-wire gauge and its exact Ward correction

On the FIRST wire define

D(M)=diag(M^(ell a)),
T_hor=(D(M)^-1 tensor I)T(D(M) tensor I).      (9)

For a charge-zero path its coefficient is M(ell(a-c))=M(ell nu). Substituting (4) at qM cancels the factor q^(-ell nu). Hence

I_hor(qM)=L I_hor(M),
F_hor(M,xi)=F(M,M^ell xi),
F_hor(qM,xi)=F_hor^[L](M,xi).               (10)

This is an alternative meridian-horizontal polarization, with no guessed quiver transport. Its endpoint half-character has been absorbed by a specified whole diagonal gauge, while L itself remains the actual word insertion rather than its critical value.

The first-wire reclosure C=ptr_second T is diagonal for every whole uninserted auxiliary word by total position conservation. Therefore

ptr_second T_hor=D^-1 C D=C                  (11)

even OFF root. The selected uninserted state and full d_N line are exactly unchanged. For a current tensor of total charge d, c+nu=a+d; its factor is M^(ell nu)M^(-ell d). This extra source character is retained, rather than extending (11) to nonscalar charged matrices without their row correction.

There are two equivalent complete current presentations. Transport all source data by the actual gauge: psi_hor=D^-1 psi and ell_hor=ell D, with the corresponding transformed coupon. Then the pairing is identical on EVERY matrix. If one keeps the convenient original fixed-input/all-ones row instead, its exact whole defect is the Cartan Ward row

lambda_a(D^-1 C D)-lambda_a(C)
  =sum_(c!=a) [M^(ell(a-c))-1]/[Q^(a-c)-1]
                                  (D_K C)_(c,a),
D_K C=K C K^-1-C.                           (12)

Only genuine head units are inverted. No color edge or 1-Q^N is removed. Equation (12) handles all ordered/Green current tensors. On homogeneous charge d its selected line factor is M^(-ell d).

The forced M-connection in the horizontal matrix frame is

(D_M+ad_(ell J_head)) C_hor
             =D^-1(D_M C)D, D_M=M partial_M. (13)

Its sign follows by differentiating D^-1 C D. The q-normal connection is zero with M held independent; on an actual path M(q) retain its chain-rule multiple of ell J_head. This does not freeze the fixed-level power graph or any normal source character.

On the physical unit-meridian arc D(M) is unitary. The row in (12), in the original balanced flat metric, satisfies

||beta_(a,ell)(M)||^2 <=(N^2-1)/3, |M|=1,
beta_(a,ell)(1)=0,
||D_M^k beta_(a,ell)(1)||
                 <=ell^k N^(k+1)/sqrt(12), k>=1. (14)

Proof: numerator modulus is at most two and the denominator runs once through all nontrivial N-th root gaps; their inverse-square sum is (N^2-1)/12. For k derivatives use |a-c|<N. These are polynomial bounds for the SOURCE GAUGE and its fixed normal jets, not for the whole R-word, the external d_N pole or a complex neighborhood of the physical arc.

5. The pointed cyclic polynomial and wrap cannot be dropped

The MAIN second-wire construction leaves the original first source and its supplied chi polynomial untouched. On any wire carrying a pointed cyclic source, an actual basis change transports its source vector, co-source and spectral polynomial simultaneously. For the optional first-wire gauge, the original first source is highest input and the orbit output row; in a cyclic frame its repair P=U+W has P^N=I. Equation (9) gives EXACTLY

P_hor=D^-1 P D
   =M^(-ell)[P+(M^(ell N)-1)W],
P_hor^N=I, ell_hor P_hor=ell_hor.            (15)

The marked source vector and row transform as just specified. Thus its characteristic polynomial and FORCED pointed factor are retained: chi(z)=(z-1)chi_red(z). In the actual cyclic repair chi=z^N-1. For an unfolded sourced companion the full conjugation also preserves its supplied chi and transports its chi_red row. Replacing (15) by bare M^(-ell)P would instead change the characteristic polynomial and its source eigenvalue. Its wrap character M^(ell N) is essential, including at a normal root. None of this identifies chi_red with the knot A-polynomial.

6. Compatibility with the geometric pole, with the exact scope

The horizontal open phase adds ell Log M times the boundary displacement t=Hnu. Its principal incidence is therefore

xi=M^(-ell)p_e^-1, p_hor=M^ell p_e.          (16)

The critical displacement is still zero, so its marked geometric action is unchanged. The nondegenerate Fourier block persists on the same regular meridian germ. At COMPLETENESS the proved Möbius framing coordinate and its pole lambda_e apply. Away from it, only a supplied marked polar branch, if it persists, can be said to rescale to lambda_hor=M^ell lambda_e(M). No global generic-M Möbius or single-pole theorem is assumed.

For the rational principal coefficient T(p)=G p/(p-lambda)^2, sampling p^N=S gives EXACTLY

Av_(N,S)T=G N S lambda^(N-1)/(S-lambda^N)^2. (17)

Thus the antiperiodic source grid changes 1-lambda^N to 1+lambda^N with the corresponding numerator sign. In horizontal coordinates the retained candidate norm is

1-M^(ell N)lambda_e(M)^N.                   (18)

The differential Jacobian is essential: at fixed M, dr expressed in p_hor=M^ell p gives G_hor=M^ell G as well as the rescaled pole. Where both expressions are regular, the RATIO of the two principal filters under this coordinate rescaling alone is

M^(ell N)[(1-lambda_e^N)/
                (1-M^(ell N)lambda_e^N)]^2. (19)

At M=1 its extra logarithmic derivative is ell N(1+lambda_eN)/(1-lambda_eN), retaining the pole. Equation (19) is a geometric filter comparison, NOT a proved adapter of the actual selected quantum period. The exact main tensor gauge (9a)–(9c) preserves that period while its raw principal filter may be singular. Its missing whole word numerator/current correction must cancel or account for such poles; (17) alone cannot do it.

7. The remaining source-current equation is now explicit

On any regular proper-array chart let R_i=S_i I_hor/I_hor be the actual internal ratios. Since the main identity (9a)–(9c) is exact,

sigma_M(R_i)/R_i=S_i(L)/L.                  (20)

For the finite Green insertion operator d_R,i=R_i S_i-I, this gives the coefficientwise chain identity

L d_(sigma R),i(f)=d_R,i(Lf).              (21)

The global meaning is the zero-extended array identity, retaining all original endpoints and cancellation lattices. Together with the semilinear shift of f, it is the actual word-induced meridian connection on the insertion/current complex. After finite source pushforward it is the exact period equation

T_M P_N=source(L I_hor),                    (22)

with (6) in full normalization. Finite coefficient elimination already supplies a nonzero actual scalar meridian operator.

What (20)–(22) do NOT prove is a geometric quotient of this SELECTED class. On ordinary finite-array currents multiplication by L leaves the exact residual

sum_s L(s)(S_i-I)H(s)
   =sum_s [L(s-e_i)-L(s)]H(s).             (23)

Discarding it, or replacing L by its critical longitude inside the sum, would change the current/source comparison. A solution of gate C must transport (21), its MAIN whole source functor (9b)–(9c), its supplied pointed spectral/co-source data and the norm-pole corrections (18), then prove that the resulting original class is nonzero on the marked geometric period. This note removes a concrete endpoint twist and supplies tame actual source transport, but does not assert that last global map. The first-wire rows (12),(15) describe only the optional alternative.
