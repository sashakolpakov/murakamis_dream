The actual marked framing density from Casimir-source Ward currents

30 October 2026. local source theorem. This concerns the leading LOCAL density of the actual complete-color augmented-plat source after transverse Gaussian elimination. It retains the original mark, all finite offsets and the actual magnetic top-form. The invariant ordered-flag form alone is not its density: the marked cut supplies a meromorphic factor. No coefficient of this local density in the original global period is asserted.

1. Exact white Ward identities on the specified complex-head source

Write Q=zeta^2, d=N-1, m=d-a, B=Q^(-N), and use the actual LARGE polynomial H=H_m^B(U,V) of the generic white-source theorem. With A0=Q^d=1/(BQ), it satisfies the two exact identities

(1-U)H(U/Q,V)+U(1-V)H(U,V/Q)
                            =(1-UV Q^(-m))H(U,V),       (1)

A0(U-A0)H(QU,V)+U(V-A0)H(U,QV)
                            =(Q^m UV-A0^2)H(U,V).       (2)

They are polynomial identities in independent complex U,V and generic Q,B, before root specialization. Thus the actual raising and lowering intertwiner equations continue to the selected complex head chart; they are not inferred from agreement on a finite root grid.

Here is an elementary finite proof. Put

P_j(X)=product_(r=0)^(j-1)(X-Q^r),
N_b=V^b P_b(U)P_(m-b)(V), H=sum_(b=0)^m c_b N_b.

Each N_b individually satisfies (1), by canceling the endpoint factors in P_j(X/Q). For (2), subtract its right side and apply the resulting operator L to one N_b. With M_b=V^b P_b(U)P_(m-1-b)(V), direct factor cancellation gives

L N_b=-UV { Q^b(A0-Q^(m-b-1))(Q^(m-b)-1) M_b
              +A0(A0-Q^(b-1))(Q^b-1) M_(b-1) }.     (3)

The out-of-range endpoint terms have their displayed zero coefficient. The ACTUAL finite coefficients obey

c_(b+1)/c_b
   =-B Q^(b+1)(1-Q^(m-b))(1-BQ^(m-b))
                   /[(1-Q^(b+1))(1-BQ^(b+1))].     (4)

Substituting A0=1/(BQ) into (3) makes the coefficient of each M_b zero by (4). This proves (2) and its endpoints, with every generic coefficient retained.

To check the source convention, the actual white is

W_a(i,j;r)=(-1)^m zeta^[-dr+di-m(m-1)]
                     H(Q^i,Q^j)/F_m, i+j=m+r.

The original coproduct F equation is

(1-Q^i)W(i-1,j;r-1)
   +zeta^(-d)Q^i(1-Q^j)W(i,j-1;r-1)
                              =(1-Q^r)W(i,j;r),

which is (1). The original Ecal equation gives (2) after multiplying by UV and its fixed monomial. No small/large root collision is used. The K equation is the literal affine degree constraint.

The specified cups and inverse caps obey their exact unit-step Ward equations by the moving-factorial recurrence, with the highest weight fixed. For example p_L(x)/p_L(x-1) is

-zeta^(L-2x)(1-Q^x)/(1-Q^(L-x+1)).                   (5)

This makes the two terms in Delta(F)p_L cancel identically. Reversing the literal cap and using the same recurrence proves its dual equation. These identities are meromorphic; neither an endpoint pole nor its normal is deleted. The actual signed circle factors, fixed label weights and generic scalar adapters are independent of magnetic heads.

2. The actual half-index symbols, and their first transport

This section uses the PHYSICAL semiclassical parameter

h=Log Q=2pi i/N,

not the distinct generic epsilon derivative at one fixed root. Set A=Q^(u+1/2), q=Log A, and for a fixed highest-weight-L head put M_L=zeta^(L+1). The row action of its original balanced generators is

K=M_L/A,
F=(1-A/zeta)T_-,
Ecal=(M_L^(-1)-M_L zeta^(-1)/A)T_+,
T_+ f(q)=f(q+h), T_- f(q)=f(q-h).                    (6)

In the Weyl convention Op_W[a(q) exp(sp)]f(q)=a(q+sh/2)f(q+sh), their EXACT symbols are

K=M_L/A, F=(1-A)exp(-p),
Ecal=(M_L^(-1)-M_L/A)exp(p).                        (7)

Thus there is no order-h subprincipal term in these symbols. At a complete physical head M_L=-1. Different-head products in the original coproduct have the same property. A right matrix action uses the transposed shift; direct substitution gives the same symbol with p reversed. This retains the input/output operator ordering.

All half-index shifts and the exact parameters M_L=exp[h(L+1)/2] are kept in the action and amplitude. In particular L/(2N) and (L+1)/(2N) are not identified near the fold. At each finite N, change the already proved selected packet to the half-index variables exactly. Reexpanding it retains the order-one exponential from the shift of its action, as well as the amplitude and coordinate Jacobian. The fixed-derivative estimates for that packet justify this operation; one does not replace it by its limiting phase before making the change. The finite affine offsets and their characters are exactly those already retained in the complete local marked-density theorem.

The total K equation is an affine head constraint, so a trivalent tensor is initially supported on that constraint rather than an ordinary function of three unconstrained positions. Before the next calculation, take a primitive linear Fourier transform in one constrained head. Equivalently eliminate the same primitive affine equation with its full coarea half-density. The retained mixed position/momentum coordinates then give an ordinary local generating family. The Fourier normalization is kept as a base scalar and its Jacobian is kept in the density. This step does not apply an ordinary WKB formula to a delta-supported tensor or discard the K constraint.

The first transport implication can be proved directly. Suppose P_h=sum_v a_v(q,h)T^v has Weyl symbol H(q,p)+O(h^2), and P_h[exp(S/h)(b+O(h))]=0 on a regular local Lagrangian chart. Expanding the actual shifted amplitude to first order gives, on H(q,S_q)=0,

V dot partial_q b+(1/2)div_q(V)b=0,
V=partial_p H(q,S_q).                               (8)

Indeed each term contributes a_v exp(v dot S_q) v dot partial b; its remaining first correction is half the derivative of that same coefficient along v, including v^t S_qq v. Their sum is precisely half the displayed divergence. This is transport of the genuine Lagrangian half-density, rather than invariance of S alone. Primitive linear Fourier/constraint coordinates carry the same formula, by the ordinary Gaussian change of variables with its Jacobian retained.

There is a useful normalization check. On the Hom correspondence P_out=P_in=P=[k,e;kf,*], the three principal constraint fields are

X_K=(k/2)A_h, X_E=k A_f,
X_F=A_e-(f/2)A_h,                                  (9)

where A_xi is actual simultaneous conjugation. In invariant group volume their divergences are 0,-e,f. Since X_E Log k=-e and X_F Log k=f, (8) gives k^(-1/2) times invariant half-density, not bare invariant half-density. Turning the input into an outgoing leg by the ACTUAL channel cup removes this adapter. For completeness, in the half-index coordinate u=(r+1/2)/N and lambda=(L+1)/N, the root cup is

exp(pi i r) q0^(-r) G_N(r)G_N(L-r)/G_N(L).

The uniform interior sine-factorial expansion makes each numerator G_N(Nu-1/2)=sqrt(N)exp[N J(u)](1+O(N%5E-1)). Therefore its variable amplitude is exp(-pi i u), namely sqrt(K) times a fixed-color scalar. The exact denominator G_N(L), also near the folded endpoint, stays in that scalar. This checks the required modular half-density factor without dropping an endpoint normalization.

Equivalently the all-output vertex (W tensor I)p_(2a) is annihilated by its original total K-1,Ecal,F. On P_total=I, their fields are A_h/2,A_f,A_e, so its leading half-density is invariant. Ordinary caps have the dual property. This supplies a local check of the assembly below; it is not an assumption that every scalar kernel is a graph with nonzero mixed input/output Hessian. The latter would be false for the rank-one complete-circle kernel.

3. Actual scalar currents for an arbitrary fixed plat

Keep the original Morse word, all complete-circle sectors, its cups, caps and the original exposed physical cut. Expand each sector as the literal W/pi network, or use its proved scalar-one exceptional packet with its restoring character. Equations (1)–(5) give its actual intertwiner identities in the independent complex heads. The retained cyclic turns and categorical duals use their original order. The exceptional numerator and its restored integer characters obey the same unit-step identities: an integer-affine exp(2pi i head) character is unchanged by every such shift.

Compose the original Hopf-adjoint insertions through this network. There is an explicit finite scalar-current construction. Move each inserted K,Ecal,F through the next vertex using the displayed local identity; pair the two insertions on each contracted wire with its actual cup or cap. Keep every resulting multiplication coefficient. For a shifted factor c and an insertion j the reindexing is exactly

j T^v c-(T^(-v)j)c
                     =(T^v-I)[(T^(-v)j)c].          (10)

Thus each unmatched internal term is a specified integral difference current, with its insertion shifted in the OPPOSITE direction. Use the original saturated affine head lattice to express these translations in a primitive free basis. The local degree equations ensure that the matched current has zero remaining constraint defect. No independent edge maximization or cyclic wrapping is involved.

Ordinary pivotal contractions cancel the matched Hopf insertions. The only cut terms are the literal dual rows of the original mark, computed in the next section. This is the same finite Green-product construction as in the ordered Gaussian-current calculation, but here its local inputs are the independent-head Casimir identities (1),(2), not a braid mixed-Hessian model. The construction is an algorithm on the actual word and works at every fixed width and both level parities. It retains finite endpoint currents on an original finite domain. In the local regular meromorphic chart it is the corresponding interior current identity; no claim identifying that completion with the whole cyclotomic source is needed below.

The principal vector of a local insertion is obtained by differentiating its ACTUAL symbol with respect to p: expansion of a shifted amplitude gives sum_v h_v exp(v dot p)v. Equations (7)–(9) therefore identify the local variations as the actual conjugation fields on its trivalent correspondence. Their propagation through the original shared heads agrees with the explicit incidence/momentum inverse of the critical-scheme theorem. On the regular complete geometric germ they are tangent to that exact critical scheme. The two cut-compensated combinations below are its two actual framing fields. This uses the source critical-scheme inverse, not merely constancy of the critical action.

4. The actual marked co-source, including its sign

Use the original balanced cut e_ab=1/N. The one-cut array is diagonal term by term after the other actual caps, by total-degree conservation. For Phi(C)=Tr(eC), the exact dual Hopf rows are

e_K=K^(-1)eK-e,
e_E=K^(-1)e Ecal-Ecal K^(-1)e,
e_F=eF-KF e K^(-1).                                (11)

At complete color, with A=Q^a in the original integer-index convention, their diagonals are

(e_K)_aa=0,
(e_E)_aa=q0(1-Q)A/N,
(e_F)_aa=(1-Q)A/N.                                (12)

These follow by two neighboring matrix entries, with the actual zero end arrows retained. The original scalar summand already contains 1/N. Consequently the boundary coefficients for

W1=2(ad_K-I)+2ad_Ecal, W2=ad_F+ad_Ecal

are b1=2q0(1-Q)A and b2=(1+q0)(1-Q)A. Both satisfy

b_i/h -> -2 A.                                     (13)

The half-index replacement changes A only by 1+O(h), so not this leading co-source. This is a ROOT physical semiclassical statement; it does not identify any generic epsilon-normal of a bare marked row.

Normalize the cut momentum to one as in the actual critical-scheme slice, and use

F(r,s)=[[r,1-r],[s,-s]],
mu=F^(-1)[[1,1],[0,1]]F,
A_cut=(1+s)^(-1).

The two actual fields, with the same conjugation convention, are

xi1=-2(1-r)partial_r+2s partial_s,
xi2=(2r-1)partial_r+2s partial_s.                   (14)

Their determinant is -2s, a unit on this chart. Their difference is partial_r. The exact residual difference current has boundary row (1-q0)(1-Q)A/N, so its divided leading co-source is zero.

The ordinary-cap comparison also exposes the marked insertion directly. At the exposed pair, with SECOND physical index x, its exact mark/cap ratio is N(-1)q0(-d2)Q(d x). Since d=N-1,

Q^(d x)=exp(2pi i x) exp(-2pi i x/N).                (15)

The first factor is the retained integer character. On complementary integer indices a+x=d, the second is Q times the first integer clock Q^a (or its corresponding fixed half-index scalar). Up to that retained fixed-color scalar its leading factor is A_cut. Thus the marked source has a genuine A_cut insertion, not a constant Haar normalization. Equation (12) computes its differential covariance from the actual source.

5. Gaussian elimination transports the divergence and its Jacobian

Use the selected ordinary packets and the joint deficient packet at the proved regular geometric frame. The complete marked-density theorem gives their nonzero relative product amplitude with its original magnetic top-form. The positive-certificate critical scheme gives exactly two magnetic null directions; all transverse magnetic Hessians are units. Keep two actual free magnetic coordinates chi which are etale on this framing orbit, and call the remaining coordinates u. One chi can be chosen to include the actual cut head. Keep every original finite offset, Poisson character and constant phase in this same branch.

Contract only u by its local geometric Gaussian. This defines the leading two-form Omega_src: its coefficient is the actual product amplitude divided by the marked square root of det(-F_uu), with the full chi/u Jacobian and original magnetic top-form retained. The new local packet theorem makes this form NONZERO. This construction does not choose a component of the original global integration cycle.

For clarity, the effect on a current can be checked without an invariance guess. A term j(chi,u,h)T_chi^gamma T_u^beta in the exact current has Gaussian image obtained by replacing its insertion by

E_(chi+h gamma)[T_u^(-beta)j]

and then shifting the external Gaussian block by h gamma. The insertion is translated oppositely, exactly as in (10). This is the substitution u_new=u+h beta with the local critical point recentered. Equivalently one proves the required leading identity by Gaussian integration by parts. Normal total currents have zero formal Gaussian integral; an external current has leading coefficient xi_i^chi times the Gaussian density. Its external divergence differentiates that WHOLE density, including the inverse Hessian determinant and coordinate Jacobian. No separately averaged product of insertions is substituted.

The source packets are holomorphic on a common smaller chart with relative O(h) errors and fixed-derivative bounds. These justify the first transport expansion of the actual continued factors; unknown higher amplitude coefficients cancel from the leading current because its principal characteristic vanishes. A local descending normal Gaussian cycle gives the same leading computation. Its small outer boundary is exponentially below the critical value. None of these local operations moves the original finite-head cycle across a source divisor.

Combining this calculation, the actual principal fields (14) and the explicit boundary rows (13) gives

Lie_(xi_i) Omega_src-2 A_cut Omega_src=0, i=1,2.      (16)

This is the missing subprincipal transport statement. It was obtained from (1)–(13) and the original product amplitude, not inferred from invariance of the stationary action or from the old braid determinant.

6. The resulting orbit form and its scope

Put

Omega_cut=-dr wedge ds/[s(1+s)].                     (17)

Direct differentiation gives Lie_(xi_i)Omega_cut=2 A_cut Omega_cut. Divide (16) by this nonzero form. Both independent vector fields kill the ratio, so on a connected geometric framing chart

Omega_src=c_D Omega_cut, c_D!=0,                    (18)

where c_D is independent of the framing point. It retains the original source’s base-only normalization, phases, parity convention and chosen Gaussian square roots. The theorem does not choose or erase these data. The local channel Gaussian multiplies c_D by its nonzero cusp-shape factor, since the cusp shapes and its marked Hessian are frame-invariant. Thus the actual local folded sum at either parity has the same orbit form, with its own retained nonzero constant.

In the ordered-flag convention Pi=g^(-1)E11 g, with image v and kernel w, let Omega=dv wedge dw/(v-w)^2. For g=F^(-1),

Pi=[[r,r(1-r)/s],[s,1-r]],
w=(r-1)/s, 1/(v-w)=s,
Omega=dr wedge ds/s.

Consequently the ACTUAL marked answer is

Omega_src=-c_D A_cut Omega.                         (19)

The insertion at 1+s=0, the omitted s=0 chart, and all source-chart divisors remain. No nonconstant additional local factor survives the two proved Ward equations, but this says nothing about residues at the excluded boundaries or contributions from other WKB sectors.

In particular, formal Gaussian current transport is not an invertible map on all fixed-root carry sectors: at h=2pi i/N the Bernoulli adapter for exp(hD)-1 vanishes on a literal A^N character. These source sectors, normal directions and original cotangent weights are not removed by (18). The selected global framing period, its boundary/current map, and its coefficient in the original finite cycle remain separate.

There is a concrete residual-fiber consequence. Since A_cut=(1+s)^(-1), the same proved density is

Omega_src=(c_D/s) dr wedge dLog A_cut.              (20)

Relative to the displayed base form dLog A_cut, its differential on a fixed affine framing fiber is c_D dr/s=c_D d(r/s). It is exact. Thus a specified first-cut localization in A_cut, with its actual base coefficient retained, does not by itself produce a nonzero closed period of this residual leading form. Its contribution requires the original relative boundary/current coefficient or other retained source weight. Equation (20) neither chooses a centralizer section nor discards the original cotangent, character or divisor terms.

7. Finite source checks

The focused exact audit and results check 14 termwise F identities, 14 E telescopes and 10 adjacent coefficient cancellations; 300 original H four-jet identities with 1,200 retained coefficients; 570 actual cup four-jet ratios; and 308 truncated complete cut-row diagonals. Symbolic checks also retain the exact Weyl half-steps, root cup phase, three modular divergences, two cut-field covariances, flag Jacobian and marked finite clock offset. A variable Gaussian and a coordinate change separately check the transverse determinant derivative and full top-form Jacobian. The general finite-current construction and its Gaussian transport are proved analytically in §§3–5, not inferred from these checks.

The conclusion is the local framing form (18)–(19). Its global cycle coefficient and boundary currents remain unproved; no global period statement follows.
