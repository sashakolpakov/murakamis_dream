The actual root head: a smooth matrix block and its retained cut

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The literal highest-weight NN-head lies at a smooth Azumaya point of the unrestricted algebra in its actual even-order root convention. An explicit local matrix presentation proves this; simplicity of the matrix image alone would not. The whole Frobenius fiber is larger, and the original marked cut is not a cyclic trace on this block.

The complete proof directs are C and B, pinned below. C identifies the root head and its existing regular matrix-image family. B fixes the balanced basis, full crossing and original selected closure. All central presentation and trace-descent calculations are given here. No geometric framing measure or nonzero closure is inferred.

1. Conventions and the actual point

Fix N≥2N\ge2, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, Q=q2Q=q^2, and ρ=q−q−1≠0\rho=q-q^{-1}\ne0. Let UU have generators E,F,K,K−1E,F,K,K^{-1} and relations KE=QEK,KF=Q−1FK,[E,F]=(K−K−1)/ρ.(1) KE=QEK,\qquad KF=Q^{-1}FK,\qquad [E,F]=(K-K^{-1})/\rho . \qquad\text{(1)} This is the unrestricted specialization with these generators, not the divided-power form or a quotient imposing central powers. In B’s factorial basis E=ℰ/ρ2E=\mathcal E/\rho^2. At the physical complete color M=1M=1, its head has Kwj=−q−1Q−jwj,ℰwj=(Q−j−1)wj−1,Fwj=(1−Qj+1)wj+1,0≤j<N,(2) K w_j=-q^{-1}Q^{-j}w_j,\quad \mathcal E w_j=(Q^{-j}-1)w_{j-1},\quad Fw_j=(1-Q^{j+1})w_{j+1},\quad 0\le j<N, \qquad\text{(2)} with the original zero extreme arrows. All internal arrows in (2) are nonzero.

Put C=ρ2FE+qK+q−1K−1,e=EN,f=FN,k=KN.(3) C=\rho^2FE+qK+q^{-1}K^{-1},\qquad e=E^N,\quad f=F^N,\quad k=K^N. \qquad\text{(3)} These elements are central. For the powers, expand [EN,F][E^N,F] as a sum of NN commutators and move K±1K^{\pm1} past the powers of EE; both coefficients are geometric sums of a nontrivial primitive NN-th root and vanish. The other commutators are the same calculation. Substitution of (1) checks centrality of CC. In particular FE=C−qK−q−1K−1ρ2,EF=C−q−1K−qK−1ρ2.(4) FE=\frac{C-qK-q^{-1}K^{-1}}{\rho^2},\qquad EF=\frac{C-q^{-1}K-qK^{-1}}{\rho^2}. \qquad\text{(4)}

On the actual head, e=f=0,k=(−1)N−1,C=−2.(5) e=f=0,\qquad k=(-1)^{N-1},\qquad C=-2. \qquad\text{(5)} For example FEwj=[j]q2wjFEw_j=[j]_q^2w_j, and qK+q−1K−1=−(Q−j+Qj)qK+q^{-1}K^{-1}=-(Q^{-j}+Q^j). Equations (2)–(5) agree with C’s head after its explicit diagonal basis change. No tensor coproduct, pivot or framing has been changed.

2. An explicit local presentation

Adjoin a central root λN=k\lambda^N=k, and choose its value λ0=qN−1=−q−1\lambda_0=q^{N-1}=-q^{-1} over (5). This is an étale extension: NλN−1N\lambda^{N-1} is a unit. The KK spectral projectors are pj=1N∑r=0N−1(K/(λQ−j))r,0≤j<N.(6) p_j=\frac1N\sum_{r=0}^{N-1}(K/(\lambda Q^{-j}))^r,\qquad 0\le j<N. \qquad\text{(6)} They are mutually orthogonal, sum to one, and Kpj=λQ−jpjKp_j=\lambda Q^{-j}p_j. Indices on the following djd_j are taken modulo NN: dj=C−qλQ−j−q−1λ−1Qjρ2,Dj=∏a=1jda,D0=1,D=DN−1.(7) d_j=\frac{C-q\lambda Q^{-j}-q^{-1}\lambda^{-1}Q^j}{\rho^2}, \qquad D_j=\prod_{a=1}^j d_a,\quad D_0=1,\quad D=D_{N-1}. \qquad\text{(7)} Thus FEpj=djpjFEp_j=d_jp_j and EFpj=dj+1pjEFp_j=d_{j+1}p_j. At (5), d0=0,dj=sin⁡2(πj/N)sin⁡2(π/N)≠0(1≤j<N).(8) d_0=0,\qquad d_j=\frac{\sin^2(\pi j/N)}{\sin^2(\pi/N)}\ne0 \quad(1\le j<N). \qquad\text{(8)} This includes both parities of NN.

Localize at the central element DD. Define uj=Fjp0,vj=Dj−1p0Ej,mij=uivj.(9) u_j=F^jp_0,\qquad v_j=D_j^{-1}p_0E^j,\qquad m_{ij}=u_iv_j . \qquad\text{(9)} The weight projectors and (4) give viuj=δijp0,ujvj=pj,mijmab=δjamib,∑jmjj=1.(10) v_iu_j=\delta_{ij}p_0,\qquad u_jv_j=p_j,\qquad m_{ij}m_{ab}=\delta_{ja}m_{ib},\qquad \sum_jm_{jj}=1. \qquad\text{(10)} For example EjFjp0=Djp0E^jF^jp_0=D_jp_0, while FjEjpj=DjpjF^jE^jp_j=D_jp_j. The unit factors in (7) are essential.

In these matrix units the generators are exactly K=∑j=0N−1λQ−jmjj,F=∑j=0N−2mj+1,j+fm0,N−1,(11) K=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\lambda Q^{-j}m_{jj}, \quad F=\sum_{j=0}^{N-2}m_{j+1,j}+f\,m_{0,N-1}, \qquad\text{(11)} E=∑j=1N−1djmj−1,j+eDmN−1,0.(12) E=\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}d_jm_{j-1,j}+\frac eD m_{N-1,0}. \qquad\text{(12)} The last arrows follow from the actual central powers FN=fF^N=f and EN=eE^N=e, not from deleting a wrap by hand. The remaining corner relation FEp0=d0p0FEp_0=d_0p_0 is precisely ef=∏j=0N−1dj=Dd0.(13) ef=\prod_{j=0}^{N-1}d_j=Dd_0. \qquad\text{(13)} An equality first obtained on p0p_0 holds as a scalar equality on all corners by (10).

Consequently the exact local algebra is U[λ]/(λN−k)[D−1]≅Mat⁡N(R),R=ℂ[λ±1,C,e,f,D−1](ef−∏j=0N−1dj).(14) U[\lambda]/(\lambda^N-k)[D^{-1}] \ \cong\ \operatorname{Mat}_N(R), \quad R=\frac{\mathbb C[\lambda^{\pm1},C,e,f,D^{-1}]} {(ef-\prod_{j=0}^{N-1}d_j)} . \qquad\text{(14)} Here is also an inverse-presentation check, so no hidden relation in the corner is assumed. Over the displayed commutative ring RR, take the matrices (11)–(12) in the standard matrix units. Their products are the two diagonal matrices in (4); their difference is (1), and their weight relations hold also on the wrap entries. Their NN-th powers are exactly e,f,k=λNe,f,k=\lambda^N; their Casimir is exactly CC. These give a homomorphism from the left side of (14) to the right. It sends (9) to the standard matrix units. Conversely (9)–(13) give the homomorphism back; (11)–(12) show that both composites fix all generators and the central coefficients. Thus (14) is an isomorphism.

At the point (5) the derivative of the relation (13) with respect to CC is −ρ−2D≠0.(15) -\rho^{-2}D\ne0. \qquad\text{(15)} Therefore RR is smooth of dimension three there; analytically, e,f,λe,f,\lambda are local coordinates and CC is determined. This is an actual smooth matrix-algebra neighborhood, not only a dimension count for one representation.

For precision this is over the full center locally. Center formation commutes here with the above flat root extension: it is the kernel of the three commutator maps with E,F,KE,F,K, and a flat extension preserves that kernel. Central localization has the same property. Since the center of the right side of (14) is RR, (14) gives an étale splitting over the actual center of UU. Smoothness and the Azumaya property descend from this étale neighborhood. There is no assertion of an NN-uniform size or condition number for this neighborhood or its chosen matrix units.

3. Full central character, Frobenius fiber and tensor blocks

Specializing (14) at (5) gives U/(EN,FN,KN−(−1)N−1,C+2)≅Mat⁡N(ℂ).(16) U/(E^N,F^N,K^N-(-1)^{N-1},C+2) \cong\operatorname{Mat}_N(\mathbb C). \qquad\text{(16)} The calculation is also valid directly in that quotient: (8) makes DD a nonzero scalar and one may set λ=λ0\lambda=\lambda_0. All remaining central elements therefore act as scalars. The ideal in (16) is the ideal generated by the full central character of the head: it is contained in that ideal, and its matrix quotient has no further proper nonzero ideal. The actual nonzero head representation rules out the whole quotient.

This is not the entire fiber obtained by imposing only EN=FN=0E^N=F^N=0 and KN=(−1)N−1K^N=(-1)^{N-1}. To see its size without a semisimplicity assertion, the usual ordered monomials FaKbEcF^aK^bE^c, a,c≥0,b∈ℤa,c\ge0,\ b\in\mathbb Z, are a basis of (1). Spanning follows by its rewrite rules. Independence follows by mapping to the skew Laurent algebra over ℂ[C,K±1]\mathbb C[C,K^{\pm1}] with E=T,F=d(K)T−1,TK=Q−1KT,d(K)=(C−qK−q−1K−1)/ρ2.(17) E=T,\qquad F=d(K)T^{-1},\qquad TK=Q^{-1}KT,\qquad d(K)=(C-qK-q^{-1}K^{-1})/\rho^2 . \qquad\text{(17)} These elements satisfy (1); at a fixed Laurent TT-degree, the largest FF-degree has the largest CC-degree, with nonzero leading coefficient. Its distinct KK-monomials cannot cancel. Descending in that degree proves independence.

Thus UU is free of rank N3N^3 over ℂ[EN,FN,K±N]\mathbb C[E^N,F^N,K^{\pm N}], with the three ordered exponents restricted to 0,…,N−10,\ldots,N-1. The whole Frobenius fiber has dimension N3N^3, whereas (16) has dimension N2N^2. Iterating (4), without any localization, gives ENFN=∏j=0N−1d(QjK)E^NF^N=\prod_{j=0}^{N-1}d(Q^jK). Thus the following relation holds on the whole Frobenius fiber, including its other blocks. Its Casimir relation at the chosen root of kk is ∏j=0N−1(C+Qj+Q−j)=0.(18) \prod_{j=0}^{N-1}\bigl(C+Q^j+Q^{-j}\bigr)=0. \qquad\text{(18)} The root C=−2C=-2 is simple. Any repeated values in (18) are not removed by merely specifying the Frobenius powers.

Nor does (16) turn a tensor product into a single simple block. The coproduct has its original Casimir and multiplicity data; in particular the separate Hermite circle algebra is not the one-head central-character quotient in (16). C already cautions that its Borel pointing has a tensor mark relative to the actual quantum coproduct. All those distinctions survive (14).

4. What Morita trace descent does to the original source

On a split patch write the ordinary unnormalized matrix trace as tr\operatorname{tr}; it is the reduced trace of the split Azumaya algebra. The original highest cut on an arbitrary marked one-cut operator AA is ϕT0(A)=tr⁡(T0A).(19) \phi_{T_0}(A)=\operatorname{tr}(T_0A). \qquad\text{(19)} The tail pivots and all word factors are already part of AA; none is replaced by an ordinary full trace. For a general probe PP, its exact cyclic defect is ϕP([X,A])=tr⁡([P,X]A).(20) \phi_P([X,A])=\operatorname{tr}([P,X]A). \qquad\text{(20)} The trace pairing on matrices is nondegenerate, so (20) vanishes for every X,AX,A if and only if PP is scalar. Explicitly, in the head matrix units, ϕT0([m01,m10])=1.(21) \phi_{T_0}([m_{01},m_{10}])=1 . \qquad\text{(21)} Thus the actual marked highest cut does not factor through the cyclic quotient U/[U,U]U/[U,U], even on the smooth matrix block. Replacing it by the Morita cyclic trace changes the marked source. Diagonal softened probes have the same test: the value on this commutator with indices i,ji,j is Pii−PjjP_{ii}-P_{jj}.

For completeness, the cyclic quotient of Mat⁡N(R)\operatorname{Mat}_N(R) is RR, via the ordinary trace. Off-diagonal matrix units are commutators with diagonal ones, and diagonal differences are [mij,mji][m_{ij},m_{ji}]; these generate exactly the trace-zero matrices. This proves both the descent and its limitation directly.

The actual unmarked knot operator is scalar, A=cIA=c\,I, as fixed by B’s original selected closure. Hence every trace-one probe gives ϕP(A)=c=1Ntr⁡(A).(22) \phi_P(A)=c=\frac1N\operatorname{tr}(A). \qquad\text{(22)} This is the legitimate positive use of trace descent. It recovers the existing whole-function scalarity, retaining all external normalization factors. It gives no nonvanishing assertion about cc. The unmarked equality does not extend to arbitrary marked operators.

A change of splitting by gg preserves the full marked pairing only with the simultaneous transports A↦gAg−1,P↦gPg−1.(23) A\longmapsto gAg^{-1},\qquad P\longmapsto gPg^{-1}. \qquad\text{(23)} Keeping PP fixed produces exactly (20) infinitesimally. For an ordered marked word BOBO the same identity is tr⁡(P[X,B]O)=tr⁡([P,X]BO)+tr⁡(PB[O,X]).(24) \operatorname{tr}(P[X,B]O) =\operatorname{tr}([P,X]BO) +\operatorname{tr}(PB[O,X]). \qquad\text{(24)} It retains the cut/pivot and marked-word defects. All identities are finite-dimensional and hold with the original parameter germs.

Accordingly the source-compatible datum to descend is the matrix bundle together with its probe and marked word, using (23), or its linear current description with (20), (24) retained. It is not the cyclic trace class of the word alone. A proposed quantum lift of the residual geometric framing flow must still transport this probe/current and its original relative chain. The local central coordinates in (14) do not identify that geometric framing coordinate or supply a measure on its fiber. In particular they give no bound, nonzero integral, or cycle coefficient for that fiber. No new relative-cycle descent is claimed.

Coverage and complete proof sources

The broad central-block/Azumaya theory is classical coverage, not a novelty claim. Brown–Gordon’s ramification-of-centers paper distinguishes the full center from its central Hopf subalgebra; its quantum discussion has odd-order hypotheses. Baseilhac’s root Uq𝔰𝔩2U_q\mathfrak{sl}_2 review is further coverage. Neither is a proof premise here. The explicit presentation above uses the actual qq of order 2N2N, at every NN.
