The complete crossing circle is positive after the literal face reflection

29 October 2026. The original complete-color crossing-circle operator has a positive reflected tensor at every level and in every physical integer-degree block. Its original ordinary cap has an exact polynomial dual cost in this semidefinite form. Same-pair signed twists are exact isometries. None of these statements identifies an arbitrary augmented link contraction with a positive norm, or proves an open cusp-color geometric-survival corridor.

1. Actual operator, reflection, and complete channels

Put (d=N-1), (q=e^{i/N}), (Q=q^2), (x=q^d), and

sa=|(Q;Q)a|,s0=1,0≤a≤d. s_a=|(Q;Q)_a|,\qquad s_0=1,\qquad 0\leq a\leq d.

On the two original complete physical heads let Ω\Omega be the coproduct Casimir, including the original Cartan normalization. The literal mixed-circle calculation proved, with the actual auxiliary pivot and no new color average,

LN=(−1)dUd(Ω/2),𝒫N(Ω)=0,𝒫N(z)=2TN(z/2)+2. L_N=(-1)^d U_d(\Omega/2),\qquad \mathcal P_N(\Omega)=0,\qquad \mathcal P_N(z)=2T_N(z/2)+2. \tag{1}

Here the scalar polynomial UdU_d has U0=1,U1(z/2)=zU_0=1,U_1(z/2)=z. Let P|a,b⟩=|b,a⟩P|a,b\rangle=|b,a\rangle be the LITERAL tensor flip. It is a face reflection, not an added crossing or a braid half-twist.

For total degree 0≤T≤d0\leq T\leq d, use |u,T−u⟩|u,T-u\rangle, 0≤u≤T0\leq u\leq T. The original block JTJ_T is complex symmetric, and direct conjugation of its displayed entries in the complete channel theorem gives

PJT¯=JTP. P\overline{J_T}=J_TP. \tag{2}

Its nodes, with multiplicities retained, are

cj=q2j+1+q−2j−1,0≤j≤T,νj=(−1)jqj(j+1). c_j=q^{2j+1}+q^{-2j-1},\quad 0\leq j\leq T, \qquad \nu_j=(-1)^j q^{j(j+1)}.

Let vj=f(cj)v_j=f(c_j) be its canonical eigenvector with first component one, as defined by the original continuant recurrence. Its last component is (vj)T=xTνj(v_j)_T=x^T\nu_j. Thus uniqueness of its eigenline, (2), and its first component give

Pvj¯=(vj)T¯vj. P\overline{v_j}=\overline{(v_j)_T}\,v_j. \tag{3}

All formulas below use this specified physical normalization, not a rescaled abstract Jordan basis.

2. Positive rank-one resolution in EVERY physical block

The exact theorem is

HN=LNP≽0,CN=PLN=PHNP≽0. \boxed{\quad H_N=L_NP\succeq0,\qquad C_N=PL_N=PH_NP\succeq0.\quad} \tag{4}

In a low block T≤dT\leq d, its full nonzero rank-one resolution is

HN|T=∑j<d−j≤T2sT2sT−jsT+j−dvjvj*+𝟏Nodd,d/2≤TsT2sT−d/22vd/2vd/2*. H_N\big|_T=\sum_{\substack{j<d-j\leq T}} \frac{2s_T^2}{s_{T-j}s_{T+j-d}}\,v_jv_j^* +\mathbf1_{N\ \mathrm{odd},\ d/2\leq T} \frac{s_T^2}{s_{T-d/2}^2}\,v_{d/2}v_{d/2}^*. \tag{5}

For T>dT>d, set K=2d−TK=2d-T; translation of the first physical index by T−dT-d identifies the block with the low block KK, including its literal reversal PP. Thus (5) covers all N2N^2 physical pair states. In particular

rank⁡HN=rank⁡CN=⌊(N+1)/2⌋2. \operatorname{rank}H_N =\operatorname{rank}C_N=\lfloor(N+1)/2\rfloor^2. \tag{6}

Proof, including the signs. At a double node c=cj=cd−jc=c_j=c_{d-j}, write Qj(z)=∏k≤T,k≠j,d−j(z−ck)Q_j(z)=\prod_{k\leq T,k\ne j,d-j}(z-c_k) and BT=(−1)TsT2B_T=(-1)^T s_T^2. The exact bilinear CD measure is

σj=vj𝖳f′(c)=(vj)TQj(c)/BT. \sigma_j=v_j^{\mathsf T}f'(c) =(v_j)_T Q_j(c)/B_T.

Since LN(c)=0L_N(c)=0, its entire Jordan contribution, including its normal part, is

Equation (3) converts the reflected contribution to

(LNP)|c=LN′(c)BTQj(c)vjvj*. (L_NP)\big|_c =\frac{L_N'(c)B_T}{Q_j(c)}v_jv_j^*. \tag{8}

There are exactly d−Td-T unpaired nodes; all lie above every paired node on the real line. Other paired factors in Qj(c)Q_j(c) are squares, and a possible self-paired node −2-2 lies below it. Consequently sign⁡Qj(c)=(−1)d−T\operatorname{sign}Q_j(c)=(-1)^{d-T}. Together with sign⁡BT=(−1)T\operatorname{sign}B_T=(-1)^T, this proves strict positivity in (8), including even levels. The exact magnitude from the sine-factorial CD formula is the first coefficient in (5).

At odd NN, the self-paired simple node −2-2 has LN(−2)=NL_N(-2)=N. The same argument uses 𝒫T′(−2)\mathcal P_T'(-2) in place of Qj(c)Q_j(c); its sign is (−1)d−T=(−1)T(-1)^{d-T}=(-1)^T, since dd is even. Its exact coefficient is the second in (5). At any other simple node LN(cj)=0L_N(c_j)=0. This proves (4)–(5). Counting the paired nodes, with both physical high/low blocks, gives (6).

At the selected total degree T=dT=d, (5) simplifies particularly:

3. Exact ordinary-cap cost and same-pair isometries

Keep the original cup pp, ordinary cap ℓ\ell, and normalized Hermite normal tt from the source frame theorem. They satisfy, with their literal scalar normalizations,

ℓ=p*P/N2,ℓt=1,LNp=0,LNt=2Np. \ell=p^*P/N^2,\quad \ell t=1,\quad L_Np=0, \quad L_Nt=2Np.

Therefore

CNt=2N3ℓ*,ℓCN+ℓ*=12N3,ℓ*ℓ≼CN2N3. C_Nt=2N^3\ell^*,\qquad \boxed{\quad \ell C_N^+\ell^*=\frac1{2N^3},\qquad \ell^*\ell\preceq\frac{C_N}{2N^3}.\quad} \tag{10}

Here ++ is the positive Moore–Penrose inverse. The first display explicitly puts ℓ*\ell^* in the range of CNC_N; any inverse solution differs from t/(2N3)t/(2N^3) by its kernel, which ℓ\ell annihilates. Positive Cauchy then proves the second formula. This is an ALL-INPUT inequality on the physical pair, extending by zero outside degree dd on its left and by the positive blocks on its right. No inverse of LNL_N is used.

Tensoring (10), and tensoring with arbitrary spectator identities, gives for every physical 2r+12r+1-head vector ψ\psi

|(ℓ⊗r⊗⟨d|)ψ|2≤(2N3)−r⟨ψ,(CN⊗r⊗|d⟩⟨d|)ψ⟩. |(\ell^{\otimes r}\otimes\langle d|)\psi|^2 \leq(2N^3)^{-r}\, \langle\psi,(C_N^{\otimes r}\otimes|d\rangle\langle d|) \psi\rangle. \tag{11}

It applies to the original marked scalar when the already proved whole-intertwiner theorem converts that scalar to this specified ordinary-cap matrix element. Arbitrary inserted marked exteriors retain their actual marked row. In particular pp lies in the radical of CNC_N, while the marked row has mp=1mp=1; the marked row is NOT bounded in this seminorm by an illicit local replacement.

The literal original same-pair crossing RR satisfies R*PR=PR^*PR=P and commutes with LNL_N. This holds on every physical block: (2) gives Ω*P=PΩ\Omega^*P=P\Omega, and the actual polynomial rNr_N has unit values and purely imaginary logarithmic derivatives at the real double nodes. Thus rN¯rN−1\overline{r_N}r_N-1 vanishes with the full multiplicity of 𝒫N\mathcal P_N. Its functional calculus proves the assertion, with the original unit Cartan factor retained. Hence

(Ra)*CNRa=CN,a∈ℤ. (R^a)^* C_N R^a=C_N,\qquad a\in\mathbb Z. \tag{12}

Thus every signed same-pair twist is an exact isometry on this positive quotient. No invariance under an overlapping pair crossing is asserted.

There is also a quantitative ordinary-color transmission bound on this SAME inserted-circle quotient. Since Ω*CN=CNΩ\Omega^*C_N=C_N\Omega, its induced operator on (Vd⊗Vd)/ker⁡CN(V_d\otimes V_d)/\ker C_N is selfadjoint. Every quotient eigenvalue is therefore one of the real cjc_j. For c=2cos⁡θc=2\cos\theta, |Uj−1(c/2)|≤j|U_{j-1}(c/2)|\leq j, hence

Lj*CNLj≼j2CN,1≤j≤N. L_j^*C_NL_j\preceq j^2C_N,\qquad 1\leq j\leq N. \tag{12a}

If gcd⁡(j,N)=1\gcd(j,N)=1, its retained nodal values are all nonzero and have modulus at least sin⁡(π/N)≥2/N\sin(\pi/N)\geq2/N. The possible odd simple node is interpreted by its removable value Lj(−2)=jL_j(-2)=j. Thus the inverse on this quotient has norm at most N/2N/2, and

4N2CN≼Lj*CNLj≼j2CN(gcd⁡(j,N)=1). \frac4{N^2}C_N\preceq L_j^*C_NL_j\preceq j^2C_N \quad (\gcd(j,N)=1). \tag{12b}

These operator inequalities tensor with arbitrary spectators. They bound a transmission inserted on an existing complete-circle form; they do NOT express replacement of its defining LNL_N insertion by LjL_j. That distinction is essential at the original cup below.

4. A once-power positive angle coefficient and a nonzero local channel

For a paired node put

A=T−j,B=T+j−d,D=d−T. A=T-j,\quad B=T+j-d,\quad D=d-T.

These are nonnegative ACTUAL integer indices with A+B+2D=dA+B+2D=d. The coefficient in (5) is exactly

hT,j=2N2sAsBsD2. h_{T,j}=\frac{2N^2}{s_As_Bs_D^2}. \tag{13}

The odd simple coefficient has the same formula without the factor 2 and with A=BA=B. This uses sTsd−T=Ns_Ts_{d-T}=N; it is a positive once-power four-angle factorial weight. It is not asserted to give the full edge incidence of an augmented-link triangulation.

One original physical corner can be calculated without a Clebsch envelope, since every (vj)0=1(v_j)_0=1:

(HN|T)0,0=sT2∑a=02T−d1sas2T−d−a,2T≥d, (H_N|_T)_{0,0} =s_T^2\sum_{a=0}^{2T-d}\frac1{s_as_{2T-d-a}}, \quad 2T\geq d, \tag{14}

and it is zero for 2T<d2T<d. The full sum over both members of a double pair supplies its factor2; the odd simple midpoint is counted once. Consequently the corner is strictly nonzero on every admitted physical block.

The uniform all-index factorial estimate and midpoint maximization give, with τ=T/N\tau=T/N,

log⁡(HN|T)0,0=Nπ[−2Λ(πτ)+2Λ(π(τ−12))]+O(log⁡N),2T≥d. \log (H_N|_T)_{0,0} =\frac N\pi\left[-2\Lambda(\pi\tau) +2\Lambda\bigl(\pi(\tau-\tfrac12)\bigr)\right] +O(\log N),\qquad 2T\geq d. \tag{15}

The formula is uniform through the endpoints, with the continuous odd extension of Λ\Lambda if τ=1/2−1/(2N)\tau=1/2-1/(2N). To verify midpoint maximization, at fixed sum s≤1s\leq1 the function Λ(πa)+Λ(π(s−a))\Lambda(\pi a)+\Lambda(\pi(s-a)) increases up to s/2s/2 and decreases after it: its derivative has the sign of sin⁡(π(s−a))−sin⁡(πa)\sin(\pi(s-a))-\sin(\pi a). Selecting a nearest integer midpoint gives the lower bound; summing at most NN terms gives the upper bound. Integer shifts by d=N−1d=N-1 cost only O(log⁡N)O(\log N).

The bracket in (15) has its unique maximum at τ=3/4\tau=3/4, equal to 4Λ(π/4)=v8/24\Lambda(\pi/4)=v_8/2. Thus

This is a quantitative, positive, nonzero original local circle channel at all levels. It is not a nonzero geometric coefficient of an arbitrary assembled augmented-link source.

5. The open color corridor still requires an actual source argument

The positivity is special to the COMPLETE circle. For any 1≤j<N1\leq j<N, the reflected ordinary circle PLjPL_j is Hermitian but INDEFINITE already on the original source plane. Indeed

Ljp=λjp,λj=(−1)j−1sin⁡(πj/N)sin⁡(π/N)≠0. L_jp=\lambda_jp,\qquad \lambda_j=(-1)^{j-1}\frac{\sin(\pi j/N)}{\sin(\pi/N)}\ne0.

In the basis p,tp,t, its Hermitian quadratic form has top-left entry zero and off-diagonal entry N2λj≠0N^2\lambda_j\ne0, so its two-by-two determinant is negative. Thus a nonzero geometric coefficient on an OPEN cusp-color corridor cannot be inferred by continuing local positive-semidefiniteness. It may instead follow from a source-preserving contour and analytic continuation, but that requires its own original-cycle and Stokes/boundary estimates.

Likewise local positivity does not identify a whole planar augmented network with a squared norm: all reflected leg identifications, ordinary pivots, the sole modified cut and any extra complete components have to match in the global tensor contraction.
