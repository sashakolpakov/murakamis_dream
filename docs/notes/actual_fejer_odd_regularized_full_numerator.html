Odd spectral regularization has the same full shared entropy

31 October 2026. All derivatives here are SPECTRAL derivatives at the fixed original root. No genuine-q normal is obtained by differentiating a root-only identity. The actual positive coupon below includes the semisimple derivative data of the full V.

1. Exact odd normalization and one comparison block

Let N=2m+1>=3, d=2m, theta=pi/N. On the literal polynomial source block z^j, 0<=j<=d, write A=2+Omega, with diagonal and lowering coefficients

a_j=2+c_j=4cos^2((j+1/2)theta),
s_j=2sin(j theta),
A z^j=a_j z^j-s_j^2(x+y)z^(j-1)
                     -s_j s_(j-1)xy z^(j-2).

The sole zero diagonal is a_m=0. All other diagonals and all allowed lowering weights are positive. The original endpoint zeros are retained. Let

P_N(u)=H_(V_N(-2-u)), P0=P_N(0), Q=P_N'(0),
F_N=V_N(-2)-N^(-2) V_N'(-2),
H_(F_N)=P0+N^(-2)Q.                              (1)

V_N is the full original polynomial numerator chi_N(sigma)/(sigma-Omega), chi_N=2T_N(sigma/2)+2. The second prime in (1) differentiates sigma; the Q prime differentiates u. Thus the minus sign in F_N is literal.

The unused-row product expansion gives nonnegative coefficients of P_N(u) in u,x,b,c,y. At odd N its full diagonal product is

product_j(a_j+u)= -chi_N(-2-u)
                =2cosh(N theta_u)-2,
theta_u=arccosh(1+u/2).

Its first two Taylor coefficients give exactly

product_(j!=m)a_j=N^2,
sum_(j!=m) a_j^(-1)=(N^2-1)/12.                  (2)

Replace ONLY the zero central diagonal of A by a_m*=N(-2), keeping all lowering coefficients and other diagonals unchanged. Call this positive triangular block Atilde, and let Ktilde be the homogeneous polynomial of Atilde^(-1). It is a comparison kernel, NOT asserted to be a covariant physical coupon. Its full diagonal product equals1.

Split the coefficient Q according to whether its underlying path visits m. Write Q_skip for the paths not visiting m. The unused-row formula and (2) give EXACTLY

Ktilde=P0+N^(-2)Q_skip,
0<=Q-Q_skip<=((N^2-1)/12)P0.

The second inequality is coefficientwise: on each path visiting m, the derivative adds the sum of reciprocal UNUSED noncentral diagonals. Consequently the ACTUAL polynomial coupon has the finite comparison

Ktilde <= H_(F_N) <= (13/12)Ktilde.              (3)

In particular both have every degree-d monomial, with positive coefficient. One may also see this by the path consisting entirely of one-steps. No spectral projector has been substituted for V’.

2. Exact half and cross pieces of Ktilde

As usual an input j and output i contribute b^i c^(d-j), with total outer degree j-i. Partition its full monomial support into

left: c>=m+1; right: b>=m+1; cross: b,c<=m.      (4)

For a left coefficient i<=j<=m-1 no path visits m. Define Q_ij by the ACTUAL lower-block recurrence

Q_ii=1,
Q_ij=(s_j^2/a_j)(x+y)Q_(i,j-1)
              +(s_j s_(j-1)/a_j)xyQ_(i,j-2),

with the missing second term zero. Then the coefficient at x^k b^i c(d-j)y(j-i-k) is EXACTLY

a_i^(-1)[x^k y^(j-i-k)]Q_ij.                    (5)

The right piece is its literal row-reflection b<->c; the steps and diagonals have this exact symmetry.

Every cross path either visits m or skips it by the unique two-step m+1->m-1. Replace that skipped step by the two one-steps through m. Since xy <= (x+y)^2/2 coefficientwise, its weight is at most

[1/(2N^2 s_m s_(m+1))] times the repaired path.  (6)

The repair is injective and its endpoints are unchanged. The paths visiting m have EXACTLY the P0 weights because the new reciprocal central diagonal is N^2. Therefore

P0 <= Ktilde_cross
          <=[1+1/(2N^2 s_m s_(m+1))] P0.        (7)

The same visited-central path split gives an exact factorization

P0=N^2 D_m(x,b,y)D_m(x,c,y),                    (8)

with D_m monic of degree m in its middle variable. This is also the actual odd simple-fold factorization (the sine beta_m equals1). It can be proved without a value-only interpolation: split each original path at m and reflect its upper part. If D_m(x,b,y)=sum_i b^i G_i(x,y), then G_m=1 and, for i<m,

G_i=[s_m^2(x+y)Q_(i,m-1)
            +s_m s_(m-1)xyQ_(i,m-2)]/a_i,      (9)

where a nonexistent Q term is zero. Equations (5)–(9) retain every physical endpoint and every finite outer unit.

3. Uniform CLOSED coefficient entropy on the shifted odd mesh

The closed half-block proof applies to (5), with the following explicit checks rather than an inference from a weighted limit. For j<=m-1,

a_j=4sin^2((m-j)theta),
s_j=2sin(j theta).

On any fixed interior interval j/N in [eta,1/2-eta], the recurrence coefficients converge uniformly to the SAME positive monomer/dimer coefficients, with Perron root

L^2-(x+y)tan^2(pi t)L-xy tan^2(pi t)=0.

The corresponding normalized Jacobi recurrence has positive, uniformly bounded interior coefficients and hence the same bounded negative-root and Bernoulli local coefficient estimate. The boundary estimates also remain unchanged in order: for j near0, s_j is comparable to j/N; for r=m-j near0, a_j is comparable to (r/N)^2, with r>=1. Thus removing eta N endpoint indices costs at most

O(eta N log(1/eta)+log N)

in the monomer products and upper bounds. There is no zero coefficient inside either lower half. The all-monomer x/y choices realize every outer degree, including the extreme coefficients. These are precisely the ingredients of the closed proof: finite-tilt compact-cover upper bounds, trim/fill lower bounds at endpoints, the interior coefficient estimate on the trimmed interval, and the collapsing-interval bound. They prove uniformly for ALL 0<=i<=j<=m-1 and 0<=k<=j-i

N^(-1)log([x^k y^(j-i-k)]Q_ij)
                 =E(i/N,j/N,k/N)+o(1).          (10)

The factor a_i^(-1) has logarithm O(log N), uniformly. Formula (9) then gives the same uniform TRIANGLE coefficient entropy for D_m. For i<m its first term supplies every permitted k by choosing x or y; one of k or k-1 lies in [0,m-1-i]. All arguments in (10) differ by O(1/N) from the desired endpoint E(i/N,1/2,k/N). The second term only adds another argument with the same O(1/N) displacement. Their finite positive weights have polynomial upper/lower bounds. Closed continuity therefore proves the upper and lower limits uniformly. The corner i=m is the exact coefficient1 and entropy zero.

Normalize all local exponents by d=2m. For D_m each triple divided by d has sum1/2; changing i/N,k/N to i/d,k/d changes them by O(1/N). Thus its absolute limit is the ALREADY normalized

C(alpha)=E(alpha_b,1/2,alpha_x)=V(2pi alpha)/(2pi).

The positive convolution (8), with O(N) summands and integer split endpoints, has the same uniform cross entropy max(C+C). Equations (5) and (7), closed continuity at both seams and the constant comparison (3) now prove, for every degree-d local exponent n,

N^(-1)log [L^n]H_(F_N)=h(n/d)+o(1)              (11)

UNIFORMLY on the full closed simplex. This is the same h as the even full Fejer theorem, not only its positive-radius weighted maximum.

4. Actual shared coefficient and full complex spectral disks

For a fixed original certified flat plat retain its original ordinary targets d and protected targets0. The actual polynomial coupon F_N in (1) is admissible in the original homogeneous compiler. By positivity, (11), and unrestricted integral flow rounding, its whole shared source satisfies

N^(-1)log Z_(D,N;F_N at every port) -> W_D.       (12)

Here W_D is EXACTLY the same finite-dimensional full h maximum as at even levels. The separate even endpoint volume theorem therefore identifies W_D=Vol(Y_D)/(2pi). No odd identity-deletion or geometric reconstruction has been inferred by differentiating an even statement.

For u>=0 define C_N(0)=1 and

C_N(u)=product_(j!=m)(1+u/a_j)
  =[sinh(N theta_u/2)/(N sinh(theta_u/2))]^2
  <=exp((N-1)theta_u).                          (13)

The product of UNUSED noncentral ratios on any path is at most C_N(u). Visiting-central paths start from P0; skipping paths have their one central factor u. Consequently the exact positive path comparison is

P_N(u)<=C_N(u)[P0+u Q_skip]
       <=C_N(u)max(1,N^2u) H_(F_N).             (14)

For arbitrary complex sigma the source-proved factorwise majorant is |H_(V_N(sigma))|_coef<=P_N(|sigma+2|). Multiplying (14) at all original ports and taking the UNCHANGED shared marked coefficient bounds the absolute full numerator contraction by

Z_(D,N;F_N) product_e
   {exp((N-1)theta_(u_e)) max(1,N^2u_e)},
u_e=|sigma_e+2|.                                (15)

In particular it has upper rate at most W_D+sum_e arccosh(1+u_e/2) on fixed bounded spectral disks, and upper rate at most W_D uniformly on ANY shrinking polydisks |sigma_e+2|<=r_N->0. This includes all actual semisimple numerator data. Cauchy estimates may be applied to spectral derivatives with their stated disk-radius cost; they are not genuine-q normal estimates. No Gaussian contour selection, complement estimate, or nonzero filling-cycle contribution is concluded here.

5. Exact whole-source spectral jets, both parities

Let S_N(sigma_1,…,sigma_R) be the ENTIRE shared contraction of the original V_N numerators, with the original homogeneous face targets and protected zero specialization. Use F_N from (1) at odd levels and F_N=-V_N(-2)=P_N at even levels. For each variable choose the actual Cauchy radius r=N^(-2). The elementary inequality cosh t>=1+t^2/2 gives

arccosh(1+1/(2N^2))<=1/N,
max(1,N^2r)=1.

At odd levels (14)–(15) therefore bound |S_N| on the whole polydisk by e^R Z_(D,N;F_N). At even levels the exact unused-factor comparison instead has factor

product_j(1+r/a_j)=cosh^2(N theta_r/2)<=exp(N theta_r)<=e,

at every port, and gives the SAME bound. Cauchy’s formula yields for every multiindex alpha the finite all-parity estimate

|partial_sigma^alpha S_N(-2,...,-2)|
   <= e^R alpha! N^(2|alpha|) Z_(D,N;F_N).       (16)

There is no unspecified exponential constant in (16): its right side is the actual positive shared source whose rate is W_D. This includes the full spectral semisimple derivatives at the parabolic endpoint. It does not identify these spectral jets with genuine-q normal jets, and it does not select any Gaussian or filling cycle.

Validation and scope

The focused audit and its results independently construct V and its SPECTRAL derivative on the original coproduct matrices. They compare 3,672 physical-grid entries, including 1,200 wrong-unwrapped-total zeros, and six whole original marked F_N sources. Four literal simple-fold factorizations retain the root scalar. The 75-digit positive checks cover 2,510 derivative paths, 1,035 full- support coefficient comparisons, 783 central-cross repairs and 3,105 spectral-radius coefficient bounds. The maximum identity error is below 3.90e-73. These are exact-source/formula checks, not numerical proofs of the uniform entropy limit or of the volume theorem.
