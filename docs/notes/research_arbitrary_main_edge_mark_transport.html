Every original MAIN edge gives the same full marked coefficient

31 October 2026. , unpublished finite source theorem. This extends the existing top-cap statement by an actual spherical rerouting and a general Morse compiler. It does not insert a newly reset mark into an internal head of the old presentation. No source sector or absolute envelope is transported.

1. Statement and exact scope

Fix a connected complete-colored flat-plat source with R recorded boxes, N>=3, d=N-1, q_N=exp(pi i/N). Each original box carries its full matrix

f_e(Omega) R_e^(epsilon_e), epsilon_e in {0,+1,-1},

in the original order, with the full Cartan/crossing scalar. The f_e are arbitrary finite polynomials; their coefficients may depend on N and on additional spectral parameters. Let H_e be their original homogeneous face polynomials, of degree d. Treat the crossing as its actual root polynomial only when forming H_e; its auxiliary generic lift below uses the actual finite universal crossing.

Keep the actual labeled plane matching graph and its ordered quartet (x_e,b_e,c_e,y_e) at each box. Set

P(L)=product_e H_e(L_xe,L_be,L_ce,L_ye), deg P=dR.

For an original MAIN edge separating distinct faces a,b, put

Z_(a,b)=[product_(f!=a,b)L_f^d] P |_(L_a=L_b=0). (1)

Then Z_(a,b) equals the original top-cap-marked scalar, with the same full finite normalization. In particular it is independent of the MAIN edge, including edges on different complete-colored components. A fixed recorded exterior framing or component character multiplies both sides equally. No component-dependent normalization is introduced.

The graph connectedness here is the projection connectedness already needed by the face compiler. MAIN edges have distinct adjacent faces in the intended certified simple-white class. More generally that property also follows by contracting each matching edge: the resulting connected 4-valent graph has no bridge, since every vertex degree is even.

The theorem includes the full cleared numerator V_N(sigma), all its root Hermite data, both level parities and composite levels. It is not an equality for individual LEFT/CROSS/RIGHT projections, which are not the intertwiners used in the proof. Equation (1) does not claim that every choice of edge passes a geometric or absolute-source certificate.

2. A regular generic pivotal lift which matches the root exactly

Hold N fixed and use a variable q on a neighborhood of q_N, with the same local square-root choice for Cartan factors. Work in the category generated by finite-dimensional integral highest-weight U_q(sl2) modules, tensor products, duals and direct sums. Write H for the integral weight operator and K=q^H. Define

z=(-1)^H,             g_tilde=zK.                 (2)

E and F shift H by two, so z is central. It is group-like because the tensor-product weight is the sum, and z^2=1. Since the actual antipodes give S^2=Ad K, g_tilde implements the same S^2 and is a valid pivotal structure. On V_L and its dual, z=(-1)^L.

At q=q_N, on EVERY integral weight, including tensor-product weights,

q_N^(-NH)=(-1)^H,
g_tilde=q_N^((1-N)H)=K^(1-N)=g.                  (3)

Thus this auxiliary pivot specializes literally to the original pivot. It is not the generic ADO expression K^(1-N), which would not implement S^2 at generic q. No identity of their transverse derivatives is used.

The regular complete-color lift is the ordinary highest-weight module V_d with

K v_i=q^(d-2i)v_i, Fv_i=v_(i+1),
Ev_i=[i]_q[N-i]_q v_(i-1),

and endpoint arrows zero. Its self-duality is

J(q)v_i=(-1)^i q^(i(i-N)) v_(d-i)^*.             (4)

Directly, the F equation is c_(i+1)=-q^(-d+2i)c_i, and the E,K equations agree. At the root c_i=q_N(i2), the specified actual duality. The balanced basis v_i/(q2;q2)i is regular at q_N, since i<N. So are J, its inverse and all four g_tilde-pivotal turns. In the unbalanced self-dual frame one also checks directly J(q)(-t)J(q)=(-1)d K=g_tilde on V_d: its i-th diagonal entry is c_i/c(d-i)=(-1)^d q^(d-2i). Thus the chosen generic double-dual frame already has the displayed pivot, not a missing sign.

Extend each f_e by holding its coefficients fixed and using Omega(q). The actual R and R^(-1) have finite sums with factorial denominators [h]_q!, h<N, all units at q_N. Hence the original full box matrices are regular Hopf intertwiners in these exact local frames. For V_N one may use the polynomial

[chi_N(sigma)-chi_N(z)]/(sigma-z),
chi_N(z)=2T_N(z/2)+2,

and only at the root use chi_N(Omega)=0. No generic resolvent or simple-node interpolation replaces the numerator.

3. Sphericality for every generic endomorphism used here

The two pivotal traces for any endomorphism A in this tensor-generated category are

Tr(g_tilde A),             Tr(g_tilde^(-1) A).   (5)

They agree, not only on one complete simple module. Here is an elementary proof that also specifies what is not specialized at the root.

For real q>0, q!=1, use the involution

K*=K, E*=KF, F*=EK^(-1).

It is compatible with the actual coproducts Delta E=E tensor K+1 tensor E and Delta F=F tensor 1+K^(-1) tensor F. On V_L give consecutive basis vectors positive squared norms

n_0=1,
n_i=[i]_q[L-i+1]_q q^(-L+2i)n_(i-1).

These make precisely E*=KF; the remaining adjoint identities follow. Tensor-product norms give finite-dimensional unitary representations. Duals are unitarizable as well: the regular generic J identifies the dual of V_L with the corresponding V_L, and the transported positive norm gives the same adjoint relations. Reversed tensor products of these duals therefore remain in the same finite unitary category. Every invariant subspace has invariant orthogonal complement, since the adjoints of the generators remain in the represented algebra.

For completeness, every irreducible summand is V_L with L a nonnegative integer. Choose a highest-weight vector of weight L; the commutation relations give

E F^i v=[i]_q[L-i+1]_q F^(i-1)v.

If r is the first zero F^r v, then [r]_q[L-r+1]_q=0. For real q>0 this forces r=L+1. The preceding ladder has distinct weights and spans the irreducible summand. Thus an arbitrary intertwiner on a tensor-generated object has blocks

A=direct_sum_L (I_(V_L) tensor A_L).

On such a block, both traces in (5) equal

(-1)^L [L+1]_q Tr(A_L),

because the weights L,L-2,…,-L are symmetric. Summing proves (5) for every endomorphism, including non-scalar multiplicity blocks. The original matrix entries and any specified generic endomorphism are rational in q^(1/2), with fixed complex coefficients. Identity on an interval of positive real q therefore proves the generic rational identity. This is sphericality for the category and maps actually used.

Only the original regular finite matrices are later specialized at q_N. Neither the irreducible decomposition nor its projections are specialized; they may be singular there. In particular this proof discards no root nilpotent or Jordan datum.

4. Rerouting with all coupon disks and frames fixed

Regard the original source as a ribbon graph on the oriented sphere, with each actual box contained in a small parametrized closed disk. These disks include every retained crossing, its local ordered inputs and outputs and its blackboard ribbon framing. They are kept fixed pointwise throughout the following construction.

Choose a point p in face a outside the disks. A path from p to the old point at infinity can be chosen outside all coupon disks and transverse to finitely many MAIN arcs, missing their endpoints. A smooth vector field in a thin neighborhood of this path moves p to the old point at infinity, while fixing every coupon disk. Its time maps are orientation-preserving sphere diffeomorphisms. The image graph misses the new infinity because p was not on the graph. Thus it gives a plane presentation with a as its exterior face and the SAME ordered local coupon frames. No crossing is created; the ribbon framing is transported inside the sphere, with no normal twist. Only MAIN arcs are crossed: the disjoint coupon disks contain the matching edges, and their complement in the sphere is connected.

The selected edge is incident to this unbounded face. Take a short subarc of it and a simple path through that face to a region above all coupon disks and the rest of the compact drawing. A thin rectangular neighborhood of the path is disjoint from the graph except at the chosen subarc. Push that subarc along the rectangle, leaving two parallel sides joined by a semicircular tip. This is an ordinary planar finger isotopy of an arc, not a new cup/cap component or a crossing. Its tip is an exposed top cap. Outside it is a; inside it is the same face b, extended along the thin strip. The coupon disks stay fixed. A small perturbation of the remaining arcs makes the presentation Morse. It need not be a flat plat.

These operations have a finite tensor description. Away from passages of the outside point across a MAIN arc, ordinary planar isotopy is generated by tensor interchange and the four actual snake identities; their J and g_tilde factors cancel exactly. When the outside point passes a MAIN arc, the two plane readings close the same correctly bent endomorphism by its two pivotal traces. Equation (5) makes them equal. The cut boundary is transported by these turns, not assigned a new matrix in its old internal basis. This proves that the rerouted generic CLOSED scalar is the original one. All R/Cartan factors remain inside the fixed coupon disks.

Leave the exposed cap open. The remaining network is a Hopf map to V_d tensor V_d^* or its reversed order. Under the actual cup/duality it is an endomorphism of V_d (or its dual). Generically it is a scalar a_(a,b)(q)I: distinct K weights force a commuting endomorphism to be diagonal, and the nonzero F arrows force equal diagonal entries. This scalar is regular at q_N, being one entry of a regular open network after the regular cup identification.

Closing it with the corresponding ordinary cap gives

Z_closed(q)=(-1)^d[N]_q a_(a,b)(q).              (6)

The same generic closed scalar is obtained with any original top cap left open, and its dimension factor is the same. Since [N]_q!=0 generically, their regular open scalars agree. They agree at q_N by specialization. No root quantum dimension is divided by zero.

For a right cap use the original reduced defect g_tilde^(-1)e; for a left cap use g_tilde e, where Tr(e)=1 in the actual boundary basis. Their marked evaluations of the scalar open map are both a_(a,b)(q). At q_N these are the actual root turns/marks by (3). Therefore the rerouted marked scalar equals the old top-cap-marked scalar exactly. There is no further root sign to insert: the auxiliary pivot already matches g. In the original flat presentation it reproduces the old standard-K conversion factor (-1)^(d(k-1)) automatically.

5. Exact ordered root turns in the new Morse presentation

The whole face compiler can now be repeated; it is not inferred from the old flat-gap sweep. Put x0=q_N^d and Q=q_N^2. The original balanced left/right matrices are

ev_L(a,b)=x0^a/N delta_(a+b,d),
coev_L(b,a)=N x0^(-a) delta_(a+b,d),
ev_R(b,a)=x0^a g_b/N delta_(a+b,d),
coev_R(a,b)=N x0^(-a)g_b^(-1) delta_(a+b,d),
g_b=q_N^(-d^2-2b).

In their literal left-to-right ordered physical pair (i,j), ALL four are exactly

cup(i,j)=N q_N^(-d^2+di) delta_(i+j,d),
cap(i,j)=N^(-1) q_N^(di) delta_(i+j,d).           (7)

For the right cap the ratio to (7) is q_N^(-2Ni)=1; for the right cup it is q_N^(2N(d-i))=1. No local logarithmic lift is reset. The two marked arrays are genuinely different off support:

mark_R(i,j)=x0^j/N^2,
mark_L(i,j)=x0^i g_(d-i)/N^2.                    (8)

Each contracts to one with its corresponding original cup. With one cap exposed, global unwrapped degree conservation forces i+j=d: all boxes preserve total head degree, each cup supplies d and every other cap consumes d. On this forced support the two arrays (8) both equal N(-2)q_N(dj). This is not an early restriction of the mark.

6. Winding gives the unchanged P and exactly the two missing targets

At each fixed box the existing full homogeneous matrix adapter remains

matrix_e=N^(-1) H_e(x,b,c,y)/(bc)^(d/2).         (9)

Its local ordered frame was never changed. In the centered face lift, a physical head along a vertical segment is

i=d/2+N(Log L_left-Log L_right)/(2pi i).

At a cup or cap the first head sees the outside face o and inside z, so q_N(di)=q_N(d2/2)(L_o/L_z)(d/2). Let M_f count the original input/output middle incidences in face f. Let T_f count +1 for the outside of every ordinary physical turn and -1 for its inside, including the cap to be marked at this stage.

In this GENERAL Morse presentation one has

M_f-T_f=2 for f!=a,     M_a-T_a=-2.             (10)

Here is the turning proof. Traverse each face boundary with the face on its left, rounding repeated boundary passages by a thin regular neighborhood when necessary. Inside a fixed box, take the same local planar resolution as the original input/output matching pair. Its net boundary turn is +pi at each middle face and zero at each outer face: the two middle passages reverse the vertical tangent, while each outer passage continues it. Along a Morse arc, an inside U-turn contributes +pi and its outside passage -pi. All remaining arc segments have zero net turn after a vertical-tangent deformation; this can equally be read as the signed crossing count of the horizontal tangent direction. Thus total boundary turn is pi(M_f-T_f). The bounded-face boundary has winding +1, and the exterior boundary winding -1. This proves (10), including repeated face labels by counting each occurrence. It does not require every turn to lie above or below every coupon.

Multiplying (7),(9), the ordinary face character is therefore

product_f L_f^((d/2)(T_f-M_f))
  =L_a^d product_(f!=a)L_f^(-d).                (11)

At the exposed marked cap the original second head gives

mark/cap=N^(-1)(L_b/L_a)^d.                    (12)

Both a,b characters cancel; every other face has exponent -d. If the new Morse presentation has k’ cups, it also has k’ caps. The turn constants are N(k’)N(-(k’-1))N(-2)=N(-1). Their q_N(d2/2) head constants cancel the k’ cup constants q_N(-d2), exactly. The whole literal marked summand is consequently

N^(-R-1) P(L)/product_(f!=a,b)L_f^d.            (13)

All half powers have assembled into integer face exponents. This establishes the coefficient CHARACTER with the same polynomial P, not a rotated, smoothed or independently renormalized local coupon.

7. Primitive grids, actual support and the unique alias

Adding degree-two bends changes neither the closed graph’s cycle lattice nor its dimension. Centering a head by d/2 turns the support i+j=d at a bend into a sign reversal. At a fixed box, unwrapped total conservation gives the actual divergence equation. Hence the integral homogeneous source lattice is still the divergence-free lattice of the same connected graph. On a sphere it is integrally isomorphic to face-potential differences modulo one common constant: integrate an integer flow along dual paths; its sum around a dual face is precisely the original vertex divergence, and every closed dual walk is generated by these face boundaries. No finite-index Jacobian is introduced.

There are R+2 white faces, so fixing L_a=1 leaves R+1 independent N-point grids. At odd N the centered heads have integral offsets and the grids are L_f=Q^(p_f). At even N use the original cocycle whose value is one on MAIN edges and zero on MATCHING edges. Each shaded triangle has two MAIN edges, so it integrates to face parities t_f; set t_a=0 and take

L_f=q_N^(t_f)Q^(p_f), p_f in Z/N.              (14)

In particular t_b=1. These are the same actual half-index offsets in the rerouted frame, not new independent choices of local carries.

Every grid point gives canonical physical heads in {0,…,d}. First apply the established canonical physical support of the full-polynomial adapter (9), including its original endpoint zeros; it kills any failure of unwrapped conservation. At a turn the congruence i+j=d modulo N already implies i+j=d in this physical range. Conversely a supported physical state determines one grid point by the primitive face-potential construction. Thus summing (13) over the N^(R+1) grid points is exactly the original finite source. For supported states, changing integrated face-log lifts coherently by 2pi i leaves the entire summand unchanged, since its final face exponents are integral. These coherent face changes induce the actual closed integral head-flow changes. Arbitrary independent single-head resets are not justified: recorded local carries cancel globally only after physical support and coherent face integration are established.

Let a monomial of P have nonnegative degrees n_f and total dR. Its grid average can survive only if

n_b=0 modulo N,
n_f=d modulo N for f!=a,b.

The latter degrees are at least d. There are R of them, so their minimum already exhausts dR. Consequently the only alias is

n_a=n_b=0,             n_f=d for f!=a,b.         (15)

The even coset phase is one at (15). The normalized grid average in (13) is therefore exactly (1), with no additional N factor. Together with section 4’s equality of actual reduced scalars, this proves the statement.

8. What this changes, and what it does not

The permitted marking is an actual cut exposed after the described rerouting. It carries the original cup/dual boundary transport and the auxiliary pivot specializes exactly to the original root pivot. It is not an identity obtained by moving the old nonintertwining D through the interior coupons or by replacing it with the same matrix on another old head. That incorrect operation already fails in a direct finite internal-head test; that separate operation is not used anywhere in this proof.

The equality is for the full polynomial source and hence for any common linear spectral/Fourier functional applied to it, with the same retained twists and exterior framing scalar. A successful full-source certificate at any such MAIN edge would therefore suffice for the original marked scalar, provided all hypotheses of that certificate have independently been checked in that marked geometry. No universally successful mark, sectorwise transport, equality of envelope capacities, or general strictly-overloaded source bound is inferred.

In particular the existing flat-plat certificate theorems may use their original top-cap position, gap forest, or specific Morse sweep. Those hypotheses have not been rechecked for every rerouted presentation here. The finite equality does not itself extend those certificate theorems to arbitrary MAIN marks.

No generic-q ADO derivative, root semisimplicity, scalar orthogonality, or low-demand cap/CROSS inequality is part of this proof. Only the auxiliary generic finite tensor identities are continued to the root.

9. Source dependencies and internal verification

The exact inputs are the original top-cap transport proof, literal duality and snakes, all four balanced turns and the original mark, full homogeneous polynomial adapter, original face-character and grid theorem, and primitive integral face-potential lattice. The full fixed-coupon sphere rerouting is written in section 4; it does not require an unrecorded planar-frame transformation.

The focused audit and its results construct the original physical coproduct matrices, full divided-polynomial numerators and actual positive/negative crossings, including their Cartan scalars. They contract the old physical top mark independently of the homogeneous calculation, then compare every actual MAIN-pair two-zero coefficient. The exact fixtures comprise 20 sources, 160 MAIN-pair coefficients, 19 nonzero sources, and 10 cases at each level parity. They use widths two and three, with both full numerators and arbitrary polynomial coupons. Field agreement and conservative integer-overflow bounds are explicit. No optimization, projected-sector identity, numerical asymptotic, or geometric certificate is inferred from these finite checks. The general identity is proved in sections 2–7.
