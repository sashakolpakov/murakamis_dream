Regular signed spectral circle development and original group sewing

31 October 2026. This is a conditional group lemma on the regular stratum. It does not identify the globally sewn regulator with the sharp absolute rate and does not cover every compact maximum.

1. Exact hypotheses and the proposed conclusion

Keep the actual labeled simple-white sphere of an original certified flat plat, its port matching, and its original adjacent protected MAIN faces u,v. At every ordinary face give a finite magnitude L_f>0. At the two protected faces keep the exact zero targets.

Suppose the following data have genuinely been obtained from the selected local source phases:

  1. The local incidence characters have a common solution epsilon_(e,f)=gamma_f+kappa_e. The signed ordinary clocks are (-1)^gamma_f L_f; no independent clock is used at another occurrence of f. Exact row scalars and the original framing remain recorded.

  2. Every port has one of the calibrated two-triangle action branches: CROSS Q_b+Q_c, LEFT Q_b-Q_c+Log c, or the reflected RIGHT branch, with its actual spectral scalar modes retained. For a strict post-pole endpoint the signed triangle is the one proved in research_signed_straddling_green_relation.md. Each finite triangle lies on a REAL, nondegenerate circle branch described below.

  3. All original ordinary face stationary equations hold. If sigma_t is the sign of a triangle in its local action and o_f is its overloaded-middle count, those equations are exactly

    sum_(t incident f) sigma_t theta_(t,f)
                  =2pi(1-o_f).                         (1)

    Here theta_(t,f) is the ACTUAL center angle, with the prescribed oriented triangle branch. Protected incidences have zero load. No alternative target or quotient mark is used.

  4. The two forced marked corners use the literal line adapters of research_signed_spectral_line_flags.md. No other ordinary circle collapses, no adjacent finite centers coincide, and no free row, pole, diagonal or spectral endpoint is being passed through.

Then the proposed conclusion is that these data develop to ONE set of white circle/line reflections and their literal primitive collars, and hence to a representation of the original drilled group. The following proof explains why no convex embedding is needed and where the marked flags enter. It does not assert that hypotheses1–4 hold at an arbitrary maximum of an absolute envelope.

2. The real local metrics and their force angles

An ordinary face has signed radius r_f=(-1)^gamma_f/L_f and geometric radius |r_f|. At a MAIN edge the center distance is

|r_f+r_g|;

equal signs give external tangency and opposite signs give internal tangency. On a matching edge with spectral parameter delta, the center distance squared is

r_f²+r_g²+2r_f r_g cos(2pi delta).                         (2)

The incidence character has zero outer entries. Hence the two finite outers have equal gamma, so (2) is a real circle intersection metric on the prescribed branch.

To check the actual angle, normalize one port by its common row sign so x,y>0. Its middle is either q>0 with T_q>0 or a post-pole q=-c<-L_end with T_q<0. The latter satisfies c>max(x,y); its internal tangent distances are positive and nonzero. With D=(x+y)²-4xy sin²(pi delta), the law of cosines gives

cos theta_y=
  [y(x-y)+q(x+y cos(2pi delta))]/[(y+q)sqrt D],
sin theta_y=
  2y cos(pi delta) T_q/[(y+q)sqrt D],                     (3)

and

exp(-i theta_q)=(T_q-iq cos(pi delta))/
                              (T_q+iq cos(pi delta)).   (4)

These are exactly the exponentiated source forces from the local generating relation. The signed q<0 does not invalidate the law of cosines: (q+y)/(qy)>0 is its actual internal-tangent distance. In the selected branch all three angles lie in (0,pi) and sum to pi. The input/output signs sigma_t in (1) orient their placement relative to the original white triangle order.

At a line corner the same assertions use the explicit finite line matrices, not an inference from a vanished scalar root. The two finite angles use the directed normal of the cooriented line as the infinite center direction. Their sum is pi. At a two-line corner the finite angle is pi and both line angles are zero.

Thus each local triangle is a rigid configuration up to an orientation-preserving Euclidean motion, including its coorientations and selected matching intersection flag. Adjacent triangles have the same actual edge data because every ordinary signed clock is shared. They need not place their disks disjointly.

3. Edge gluing, including the protected-line signs

Along a finite/finite edge its two distinct ordered centers determine the Euclidean gluing. Along a MAIN line/finite edge the tangent line and the finite center determine it. Along a MATCHING line/finite edge the selected J intersection point is retained as well. This matters at cos(2pi delta)=0, when the finite center lies on the line: the selected intersection fixes the remaining possible half-turn.

There is also a lift check. Normalize the reflection of each finite circle by its global signed curvature, the real lower-left entry of its determinant-minus-one matrix. An orientation-preserving Euclidean coordinate change preserves this normalization. Across a MAIN line/finite edge both local raw products have trace-2. Once the finite reflection is matched, the opposite central sign for the line would give trace+2, so it is excluded. Across a MATCHING edge the two triangles are the halves of the SAME original port; their common ordered product and selected J flag already fix the same line reflection, also at trace0.

Thus the edge gluings propagate actual line reflections, not an unrecorded independent sign at each occurrence of a protected face. No positive sign is arbitrarily assigned to a zero clock.

The marked line/line edge uv is unique in the original simple sphere. It will be a boundary edge in the developing disk and is never used as an underdetermined interior gluing.

4. The disk holonomy and the same two marked lines

Remove one of the two triangles incident to uv. The remaining sphere is a triangulated disk, with u,v on its boundary. The other uv triangle supplies the initial two distinct parallel lines and its finite circle, using the exact two-line adapter. A marked triangle cannot be a negative Green triangle: one of its middle faces is protected, whereas a negative triangle would overload that face.

Develop every local triangle by the unique edge gluing above. Around an interior finite vertex, the holonomy fixes its center. Its rotation is the signed center-angle sum (1), hence an integral multiple of2pi. The Euclidean holonomy is therefore identity, including its translation part. This remains true for a vertex incident to a marked line: the directed line normals are part of the same local angle data.

Cycles of the disk dual are generated by these finite-vertex stars. Consequently development is independent of path. Both marked vertices are boundary vertices; no translation loop around an infinite vertex has been silently assumed trivial.

Each boundary line fan is connected, so it propagates the SAME line reflection. The last finite vertex belongs to one developed circle and has its two prescribed incidences to those same lines. The selected line flags and coorientation, not an arbitrary choice of a parallel translate, determine its local two-line configuration. It therefore closes the omitted uv triangle. In particular the last corner must satisfy the exact height relation H=2cos²(pi delta)/L in its local normalized two-line chart; it is not an extra free strip parameter.

Here is the raw-lift calculation that makes this last assertion explicit. Normalize the propagated lines by J_u=-iI and J_v=[[i,-2H],[0,i]], H>0, and write the last finite circle’s signed curvature as q and center as X+iY. Its actual MATCHING product with u has trace -2qY=-2cos(2pi delta), while its MAIN product with v has trace 2q(Y-H)=-2. Subtraction gives qH=1+cos(2pi delta)=2cos²(pi delta). Thus q>0 and H=2cos²(pi delta)/q exactly. The retained selected J flag fixes the remaining ordered branch. Merely two unoriented incidences would not have supplied this conclusion.

The result may overlap, contain internally tangent circles or have zero winding at an overloaded vertex. None of those properties invalidates the original reflection assignment.

5. Original Morse and peripheral relations

Assign one resulting reflection to each original white face. At each matching port use its original ordered main heads A,B and product P=BA. The signed local action and line adapters prove that its primitive C transports BOTH heads and the chosen third spectral flag. It is therefore the actual three-point shaded pairing, not only a matrix with an appropriate trace.

The original main prefix products telescope. The original cups and caps supply their based inverse pairs, with the same unrestricted mark. At each auxiliary circle the literal threading/Hurwitz word uses the recorded physical lift of C^(-1) and returns both heads. These are the actual Morse presentation relations used by the previous complete and signed-endpoint assembly. They are algebraic relations of the one reflection family; no embeddedness of its developed image is required.

Thus the proposed regular data give an actual drilled-group representation. Its primitive peripheral ratio is the local one, including R0=-E_b/H_c on a straddling chart. The source scalar mode fixes the additive log branch; the retained half-twist uses the recorded change U_epsilon=U_0-epsilon V/2 exactly once.

6. The still-separate regulator and source questions

This argument is deliberately GROUP-only. Matching white circles now intersect and the selected ideal flag is one of two intersection points; the endpoint tangent-face volume formula cannot simply be reused. The original two cut balls still provide a candidate abstract chain with their ordered contact polygons and shaded pairings, but its absolute Bloch-Wigner value must be compared to the actual signed spectral action, including the peripheral correction.

Neither an arbitrary local Gauss-reference change nor agreement of action differentials proves that comparison. Likewise this regular lemma does not supply a sign-cocycle solution at every absolute maximum, a relative asymptotic for every remote coefficient, or a boundary reduction for escaping ordinary multipliers and free KKT strata. Those are necessary before it could yield a general quantum upper bound.

The conditional group argument and exact two-line closure do not supply the source hypotheses or the separate regulator comparison.
