A selected small-activity saddle of the actual factorial coupon

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The residual last-head coupon in U has an actual nonzero complex asymptotic on a small open sector. Its finite polynomial is represented by its original constant-term circle. A direct Taylor-cap estimate and a single saddle on that circle give the coefficient, including the binomial half-density. No complex occupation interpolation or assumed saddle occurrence is used.

This evaluates the coupon factor only. Its convolution with the quotient, turnover, resolvent, and original source remains as in U. In particular it does not select a geometric contribution to that larger period.

The sole complete direct U is pinned below. It identifies the actual coupon and its source interfaces. All finite recurrences, contour identities, estimates, and saddle calculations are proved here.

The factors outside this coupon in U’s opposite-source interface remain outside it.

Let Ds=s∂sD_s=s\partial_s and 𝒬sH(s)=H(Qs)\mathcal Q_sH(s)=H(Qs). Consecutive coefficients in (2) give [Ds(1−𝒬s)+s(d−Ds)]HN=0,HN(0)=1.(3) \boxed{\quad [D_s(1-\mathcal Q_s)+s(d-D_s)]H_N=0,\qquad H_N(0)=1 . \quad}\qquad\text{(3)} Indeed, for 1≤a≤d1\le a\le d, a(1−Qa)ha=−(N−a)ha−1a(1-Q^a)h_a=-(N-a)h_{a-1}. 5. Source and normal scope

Formula (2) is exactly the kernel of the retained ZG−1ZG^{-1} coupon in U. Pulling (24) back by s=wzs=wz gives an actual local coherent-kernel asymptotic wherever wzwz lies in this sector. The original binomial dual, the sign ZZ, and the degree d=N−1d=N-1 are all responsible for factors in (14),(22),(24); none was absorbed into an unspecified amplitude.

There is a source-incidence restriction as well. In U’s full turnover formula, the residual last-head coupon corresponds to the original donor, not automatically to a recipient coherent identity kernel. Its contraction with the upper column must therefore be retained. Formula (24) is the bare coupon kernel; it is not substituted for that different composed matrix row.

At fixed quantum root this coupon is independent of the meridian MM. Its meridian-normal burden in the full source therefore lies in the other interfaces and in any prescribed MM-dependent arguments. Fixed derivatives of (24) may be used for such arguments while they remain in compact subsets of the proved domain. This is not a computation of the whole opposite-source first jet.

Nor is the root reflection used in U differentiated in the quantum parameter. Off the root, the reflected donor diagonal contains its full finite products and is not automatically the simplified coupon (2). No ordinary quantum-normal identification is inferred from the present fixed-root saddle proof.

The upper-cap component, the finite Euler forcing, and the source complements have not been discarded. The first two are accounted for by the exact polynomial identities and the actual circle estimate. Complements belonging to other factors or to a larger paired-source contour remain as prescribed in U. The result does not show that a loxodromic geometric source frame has wzwz in this sector, or that the product of this coupon with the upper resolvent and quotient has a nonzero geometric period.
