Actual memory-charge clocks and first-normal transfer carries

Research theorem and exact audit, 27 October 2026. A transfer clock has an exact head/Frobenius memory-charge expression on the actual high integral color. At the root its carry vanishes. In the first quantum normal only one carry can occur on a tracked strand, and its entire correction is a bounded whole scaled-clock unit. Together with the actual quadratic first-normal R theorem, this gives a finite differential source realization of these inserted transfer clocks and their bounded moments.

The root clock identity alone is a rewriting of the old position clock factorization. The additional content is its ORIGINAL first-normal block carry, bounded Frobenius differential degree, and canonical actual source germ. This is not yet a Gaussian two-framing current map.

1. The integer charges and exact operator formulas

Use the actual high integral color with basis wa,sw_{a,s}, 0≤a≤r0\le a\le r, 0≤s<N0\le s<N. Define diagonal operators Rwa,s=awa,s,Swa,s=swa,s,J=NR+S,C=R−S=(N+1)R−J. Rw_{a,s}=aw_{a,s},\qquad Sw_{a,s}=sw_{a,s},\qquad J=NR+S,\qquad C=R-S=(N+1)R-J . Here C is a MEMORY CHARGE, not a critical-field observable. All three operators are actual finite matrices with their constant integral grading before root specialization.

Track input strand 1 to output strand 2. The literal checked crossing routes are R+:(i,j)↦(j+k,i−k),R−:(i,j)↦(j−k,i+k). R_+:(i,j)\mapsto(j+k,i-k),\qquad R_-:(i,j)\mapsto(j-k,i+k). Writing $\Delta R=R_{\rm out,2}-R_{\rm in,1}$ and likewise ΔC\Delta C, exact integer arithmetic gives k=ΔC−(N+1)ΔR(+),k=−ΔC+(N+1)ΔR(−). k=\Delta C-(N+1)\Delta R\quad(+),\qquad k=-\Delta C+(N+1)\Delta R\quad(-). \tag{1} These are identities of UNWRAPPED positions, not congruences.

Let R±(w)R_\pm(w) multiply each literal transfer-k coefficient by wkw^k, with w≠0w\ne0. Define the charge-twisted crossings R+C(w)=(I⊗wC)R+(w−C⊗I),R−C(w)=(I⊗w−C)R−(wC⊗I). R_+^C(w)=(I\otimes w^C)R_+(w^{-C}\otimes I),\qquad R_-^C(w)=(I\otimes w^{-C})R_-(w^C\otimes I). Then at every generic q, R+(w)=(I⊗w−(N+1)R)R+C(w)(w(N+1)R⊗I), R_+(w)=(I\otimes w^{-(N+1)R})R_+^C(w) (w^{(N+1)R}\otimes I), R−(w)=(I⊗w(N+1)R)R−C(w)(w−(N+1)R⊗I). R_-(w)=(I\otimes w^{(N+1)R})R_-^C(w) (w^{-(N+1)R}\otimes I). \tag{2} The retained Cartan and mixed half-Cartan signs are unchanged: (2) multiplies the ACTUAL coefficients in their original order.

Thus no initial diagonal projector is needed for the clock identity. A projector correlating R and S has a separate source/current role; it is not an R-invariant subspace.

2. Root and first-normal support sharply bound the carry

Put δ=1−QN\delta=1-Q^N. By the actual first-normal crossing theorem, all transfers k≥2Nk\ge2N vanish modulo δ2\delta^2. For k<Nk<N, the tracked head can cross at most one boundary. For a resonant k=N+bk=N+b, its first normal factors through E(N)⊗F(N)E^{(N)}\otimes F^{(N)} and a root b-transfer. The latter cannot cross another head boundary: its ordinary root E or F edge would be zero. Hence ΔR=0 at order zero, \Delta R=0\text{ at order zero}, $$ \Delta R\in\{0,-1\}\quad(+),\qquad \Delta R\in\{0,+1\}\quad(-) \quad\hbox{through first order}. \tag{3} $$ A putative two-boundary carry has zero first-normal coefficient. The negative assertion also follows by differentiating the actual inverse crossing and its root block permutation.

Denote a first-normal charge-twisted component of block shift d by R±,1C,[d](w)R_{\pm,1}^{C,[d]}(w). Equations (1)–(3) give R±(w)=R±C(w)+δ(wN+1−1)R±,1C,[∓1](w)+O(δ2). R_{\pm}(w)=R_\pm^C(w) +\delta\,(w^{N+1}-1) R_{\pm,1}^{C,[\mp1]}(w)+O(\delta^2). \tag{4} The same positive carry unit appears for both recorded signs because their nonzero tracked shifts have opposite signs. For arbitrary components the exact unit is w∓(N+1)ΔRw^{\mp(N+1)\Delta R}, as in (2).

The interaction first normal has Frobenius PBW degree at most two. Charge twists rescale raising/lowering operators and act on head matrices; selecting a fixed block shift is its ordinary weight grading and does not raise PBW degree. Consequently the memory/carry form (4) has the SAME degree bound. The exact one-sided source dictionary of the two-clock source turns it into an operator of differential order at most two, with the original normalized source terms retained.

These q-derivatives belong to the original generic integral matrix family. Replacing its ordinary pivot by a frozen root ADO pivot before differentiating would require the previously retained route adapter.

3. Whole scaled clocks and finite transfer moments

For the needed source r=N, the charge eigenvalues lie in [−(N−1),N][-(N-1),N]. Put w=ez/Nw=e^{z/N}. Then wCw^C and its inverse have operator norm at most e|Re⁡z|e^{|\operatorname{Re}z|} in EVERY common diagonal metric. On the first-normal carry, wN+1=ez(1+1/N)→ez, w^{N+1}=e^{z(1+1/N)}\longrightarrow e^z, \tag{5} with all fixed z-derivatives uniformly bounded on compact sets. No exponential normalization cost in N occurs in this carry. This is a statement about clock units, not an estimate of the whole crossing norm or the source’s quantum-normal derivatives.

Equation (1) also transports bounded normalized transfer moments: (k/N)h=[ΔC/N−(1+1/N)ΔR]h(+), (k/N)^h= [\Delta C/N-(1+1/N)\Delta R]^h\quad(+), (k/N)h=[−ΔC/N+(1+1/N)ΔR]h(−). (k/N)^h= [-\Delta C/N+(1+1/N)\Delta R]^h\quad(-). \tag{6} Expand these finite binomials on the carry components (3). Equivalently differentiate the WHOLE identity (2) in z. Every fixed h has finitely many charge-moment/carry terms; no endless WKB expansion is needed. Unnormalized khk^h retains its explicit NhN^h.

The endpoint R=N is distinct from R=0 as an integer memory. A root Q-clock cannot separate them, but the actual block clock ZRZ^R does so in its first normal: DQZR|QN=NRD_Q Z^R|_{Q_N}=NR. Folding these endpoints together would lose precisely the additional normal current. Zero-extended current tails and their literal cut endpoint coupons must likewise remain; no cyclic identification is made by (1)–(6).

4. The memory clock has an actual whole source

For the canonical high-color two-flag source, write x=tux=tu and retain its actual normalization Us(q;t,u)U_s(q;t,u). The charge character is exactly Φh;t,u(wC)=∑s=0N−1Hs(h)w−sUs(q;tw,u)Us(q;t,u). \Phi_{h;t,u}(w^C)= \sum_{s=0}^{N-1}H_s(h)\,w^{-s} \frac{U_s(q;tw,u)}{U_s(q;t,u)}. \tag{7} At the root this becomes (1+h/w1+h)N−1(1+xw1+x)r. \left(\frac{1+h/w}{1+h}\right)^{N-1} \left(\frac{1+xw}{1+x}\right)^r . \tag{8} Away from the root the source-unit derivatives do NOT cancel for this insertion, since wRw^R depends on the block. They are the actual derivatives of U_s, not an arbitrary continuation of (8).

Alternatively, for the normalized ordinary TRACE co-source, wC=wR⊗w−Sw^C=w^R\otimes w^{-S} has the product of the exact dual clock lifts, in source dimensions r+1r+1 and N respectively. Tensor products, actual source permutations and their duals are transported as matrix operators. They are not pointwise products of coherent symbols. A source permutation need not have bounded norm in every coherent metric; no such assertion is used.

The trace extension agrees with the original average cut on the actual conserving diagonal arrays. It is not a replacement for that average on arbitrary off-diagonal Ward matrices.

5. What advances beyond the old factorization

At root order, (2) is algebraically equivalent to splitting a transfer into input/output POSITION clocks. By itself it introduces no independent geometric co-source. In a closed uninserted root knot, Frobenius labels are transparently transported around the component; their normalized source dependence cancels.

The new source content is the first-normal carry in (3)–(4): it is an original high-color block arrow with a concrete quadratic Frobenius operator, actual source-unit derivatives, and a whole bounded scaled-clock factor. It can be compared to the second current without guessing an abstract Borel isomorphism or discarding an unwrapped carry.

A complete current map must still transport the inserted source/coupon and its endpoints through the whole word, including its contravariant star action. This theorem does not identify the resulting insertion with the independent geometric framing flow, nor equate the low/high whole color jets or prove Gaussian naturality. It specifies the exact finite operator data that such a map must use.

6. Exact audit

The script uses independently assembled ORIGINAL positive and negative integral R entries over a finite field with dual numbers. At (N,r)=(2,3),(3,3),(4,4),(5,3)(N,r)=(2,3),(3,3),(4,4),(5,3), it checks 13,188 coefficient routes, their exact integer clock factors, all root/first-normal carry restrictions and the correction (4). It also multiplies the full positive and negative total-position blocks and checks their inverse relation to first normal order. Results record the signs, carries and block counts.
