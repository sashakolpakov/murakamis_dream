Actual braid transport has a flat Borel-relative algebra and a divided cap period

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The actual diagonal quantum Borel action has an explicit free fusion basis. Actual crossing words become matrices over a finite flat algebra, including at the original nilpotent source. The pivotal highest-input cap is an explicit Frobenius functional with its marked density. It is a divided central-F trace on a cyclic chart; bare semisimple reduction loses a nonzero actual period.

This supplies an actual word AND source comparison, rather than only an auxiliary source-frame classification. Its target is not identified with a geometric gluing algebra or the A-polynomial. The central-F parameter below is not identified with the meridian or Q-normal.

1. The actual Borel algebra and its flat central extension

Fix the actual root Q=exp(2 pi i/N), N at least two. On each regular holonomy splitting module retain K_i=x_i diag(Q^(-j)) and the actual F_i with F_i^N=phi_i. We can use the original balanced head frame: its nonwrapping arrow j to j+1 is 1-Q^(j+1), and its wrapping arrow is phi_i/N. This is exactly the factorial gauge of the regular module, not an altered representation. Put

H=(K1⋯Kb)/(x1⋯xb),f=Δb−1F=∑i=1bK1−1⋯Ki−1−1Fi. H=(K_1\cdots K_b)/(x_1\cdots x_b),\qquad f=\Delta^{b-1}F=\sum_{i=1}^bK_1^{-1}\cdots K_{i-1}^{-1}F_i.

Actual quantum-group multiplication and the root binomial identity give

HN=1,Hf=Q−1fH,fN=Φ:=∑i=1b(x1⋯xi−1)−Nϕi. H^N=1,\qquad Hf=Q^{-1}fH,\qquad f^N=\Phi:=\sum_{i=1}^b(x_1\cdots x_{i-1})^{-N}\phi_i. \tag{1}

For example for two terms A=F_1,B=K_1 inverse F_2 one has BA=QAB, so all intermediate coefficients of (A+B)^N vanish. Iteration proves (1). Every complete center and meridian factor is retained in Phi.

Define the algebra A_Phi by these three relations, with idempotents

ej=1N∑a=0N−1QjaHa,za,j=faej,0≤a,j<N. e_j=\frac1N\sum_{a=0}^{N-1}Q^{ja}H^a,\quad z_{a,j}=f^a e_j,\quad 0\leq a,j<N.

It has the EXPLICIT free basis z_(a,j). Multiplication is

za,jzc,k={Φ⌊(a+c)/N⌋za+cmod⁡N,k,k=j−c(mod⁡N),0,otherwise. z_{a,j}z_{c,k} =\begin{cases} \Phi^{\lfloor(a+c)/N\rfloor} z_{a+c\bmod N,k},& k=j-c\pmod N,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \tag{2}

This formula is associative (it records the shift of the idempotent and total integer f exponent) and realizes all defining relations, so it also proves the basis assertion without a dimension guess. Thus this is a flat rank-N-squared algebra over the WHOLE Phi base. At Phi=0 its ideal (f) is nonzero, nilpotent and two-sided, with quotient the N diagonal characters. It is the truncated quantum Borel algebra, also known as a Taft algebra. It is not Mat_N there.

For Phi nonzero the cyclic module f e_j=e_(j+1) for j<N-1 and f e_(N-1)=Phi e_0, with H e_j=Q^(-j)e_j, gives Mat_N. The idempotents and nonzero cyclic arrows exhibit every matrix unit. After retaining a root rho^N=Phi, the normalized shift f/rho has Nth power one and gives a cyclic companion frame. This is a generic cyclic chart, not an extension of its bare matrix algebra through the nilpotent source.

2. Explicit free fusion for any number of actual strands

For b=2 let u=e_0 tensor ones, and define

ℱ:AΦ→V1⊗V2,za,j↦fa(e0⊗ej). \mathcal F:A_\Phi\longrightarrow V_1\otimes V_2, \quad z_{a,j}\longmapsto f^a(e_0\otimes e_j). \tag{3}

This is A_Phi-linear, because e_j u=e_0 tensor e_j. It is an isomorphism, including on Phi=0. Indeed the unique term with first output index a is F_1^a(e_0) tensor e_j, with coefficient (Q;Q)_a. Every other term has first index strictly smaller than a. No first-leg wrap occurs in fewer than N applications. In order of first index, (3) is block triangular with diagonal (Q;Q)_a times I_N. These are original nonzero factorial units. The determinant is their product, independent of phi_1,phi_2.

For b greater than two, let t=(t_3,…,t_b) index the remaining physical heads and set u_t=e_0 tensor ones tensor e_t. Then

AΦ⊗RNb−2→V1⊗⋯⊗Vb,a⊗t↦aut A_\Phi\otimes R^{N^{b-2}}\longrightarrow V_1\otimes\cdots\otimes V_b, \quad a\otimes t\longmapsto a u_{t} \tag{4}

is again an isomorphism. The projector e_j selects the unique second index j-sum(t_i) modulo N; all-first-leg f terms give the same triangular diagonal (Q;Q)_a. Hence the actual tensor module is a FREE left A_Phi module of rank N^(b-2). This is a non-naive fusion basis built from the actual coproduct; no proposed product of two repaired head shifts has been substituted for it.

The inverse is regular in all phi_i because that determinant is a fixed unit. Every actual matrix, source vector, cap and current has an exact conjugate under (4). There is no discarded endpoint, choice of replacement co-source or asymptotic remainder here.

3. Actual local R and arbitrary words act on this relative algebra

The actual checked R intertwines the diagonal coproduct. On the same-color nilpotent section it commutes with H and f. Therefore (4) takes each actual local crossing and inverse into

End⁡AΦ(AΦNb−2)≅Mat⁡Nb−2(AΦop). \operatorname{End}_{A_\Phi}(A_\Phi^{N^{b-2}}) \cong \operatorname{Mat}_{N^{b-2}}(A_\Phi^{op}). \tag{5}

For two strands it is right multiplication by a unique unit a_R, computed from R u using (3). It is NOT an unspecified scalar: the literal actual R matrix, including its half-Cartan and scalar line, fixes a_R. Composition and every signed braid relation follow by conjugating the actual word through the same basis change.

The same construction applies to the full actual holonomy Hom line on its admissible splitting covers in the regular holonomy theorem. The checked map preserves the diagonal coproduct, the product of the marked K roots and Phi in (1). Source and target versions of (4) give A_Phi-linear maps. Retain the actual Hom line and all closing root phases; no new global scalar frame is supplied here. The semicyclic case is the explicit polynomial intertwiner in the carry-braiding theorem, including its transported centers and corrected wrap factor.

Thus (5) is actual root-parameter word transport across central Phi=0, not an assertion of topological invariance of a new period. The original selected word and its original source are transported together. On the nilpotent section their value is exactly the original invariant with every external normalization retained.

4. A regular Frobenius functional is a divided cyclic trace

Define on the WHOLE flat algebra

τtop(za,j)=δa,N−1. \tau_{top}(z_{a,j})=\delta_{a,N-1}.

Its left-dual basis is explicitly

da,j=fN−1−aej+amod⁡N,τtop(da,jzc,k)=δa,cδj,k. d_{a,j}=f^{N-1-a}e_{j+a\bmod N},\qquad \tau_{top}(d_{a,j}z_{c,k})=\delta_{a,c}\delta_{j,k}. \tag{6}

No Phi enters this Gram matrix: a matching product has exponent N-1 and never crosses a full f cycle. Thus this is a nondegenerate Frobenius functional on every fiber, including the nilpotent one. For any actual co-source Lambda on this relative algebra the marked density is the explicit finite element

CΛ=∑a,jΛ(za,j)da,j,Λ(a)=τtop(CΛa). C_\Lambda=\sum_{a,j}\Lambda(z_{a,j})d_{a,j},\qquad \Lambda(a)=\tau_{top}(C_\Lambda a). \tag{7}

For several strands apply (7) to each relative matrix entry. Their coefficients are the literal pulled-back pivotal/cut rows from (4). This gives a concrete relative period for an arbitrary actual braid, not a replacement regular trace.

For clarity the arbitrary-braid source formula is explicit. Let s,t index the tail labels of (4), and let a_(t,s) in A_Phi be the actual relative word matrix: its action is a tensor s to sum_t a a_(t,s) tensor t. For a closed input tuple (c,s), with c the second physical input, put k=c+sum_i s_i modulo N. Define the actual finite rows

L(c,s),t(z)=(onest⊗ect⊗est)ℱ(z⊗t), L_{(c,s),t}(z) =(\mathrm{ones}^t\otimes e_c^t\otimes e_s^t) \mathcal F(z\otimes t), Λt,s(z)=∑c=0N−1(g2(c)∏i=3bgi(si))L(c,s),t(ekz). \Lambda_{t,s}(z) =\sum_{c=0}^{N-1}\left(g_2(c)\prod_{i=3}^b g_i(s_i)\right) L_{(c,s),t}(e_k z).

The g_i are the original pivotal entries with their actual colors. The original period, with its assigned external line gamma_beta, is EXACTLY

Pβ=γβ∑t,sΛt,s(at,s)=γβ∑t,sτtop(Ct,sat,s), P_\beta=\gamma_\beta\sum_{t,s}\Lambda_{t,s}(a_{t,s}) =\gamma_\beta\sum_{t,s}\tau_{top}(C_{t,s}a_{t,s}), \tag{7a}

where C_(t,s) is given by (7). This uses the original INPUT e_0 on the first strand, the SUM over its output, and every other original pivotal closure. Indeed e_k u_s is the literal input e_0 tensor e_c tensor e_s. Applying the A_Phi-linear word then gives this formula entry by entry. All closing identifications and the root of total K remain on their original cover. On the cyclic chart (7a) is the sum of Tr_N(f C_(t,s) a_(t,s))/Phi by (8), with its regular divided extension at Phi=0. No unspecified cap functional is introduced.

In the N-dimensional cyclic splitting of Section 1, a stronger WHOLE polynomial identity is

Tr⁡N(fa)=Φτtop(a). \operatorname{Tr}_N(fa)=\Phi\,\tau_{top}(a). \tag{8}

It suffices to check the basis: only a=f^(N-1)e_j contributes, when f a=Phi e_j. Consequently tau_top is exactly the retained DIVIDED central-F normal trace Tr(fa)/Phi, regular at Phi=0. Bare ordinary trace kills the nilpotent ideal and loses this value. The Frobenius functional is twisted rather than cyclic: its Nakayama transport is H to QH, f to f. It becomes conjugation by f only on Phi nonzero. No categorical modified-trace axioms are claimed solely from (6)-(8).

5. The actual two-leg cap density is explicit

On the actual nilpotent same-color section put F_a=(Q;Q)_a, x=K on e_0 and g_j=(xQ(-j))(1-N). For a right multiplication operator R_a on A_0, the original highest-input pivotal cut is

Λcap(a)=∑j=0N−1gjLj(eja),Lj(z)=(onest⊗ejt)ℱ(z). \Lambda_{cap}(a)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}g_j L_j(e_j a),\quad L_j(z)=(\mathrm{ones}^t\otimes e_j^t)\mathcal F(z). \tag{9}

All zero-framing, full d_N(M) and common crossing lines remain their original external factors. There is no extra 1/N here. For the basis z_(a,k), the finite binomial expansion of the ACTUAL coproduct gives

Λcap(faek)={x1−N−aQ−(k+a)Fk+a/Fk,k+a<N,0,k+a≥N. \Lambda_{cap}(f^a e_k)= \begin{cases} x^{1-N-a}Q^{-(k+a)}F_{k+a}/F_k,& k+a<N,\\ 0,&k+a\geq N. \end{cases} \tag{10}

For the first case e_j f^a e_k requires j=k+a, and closing the second output at j forces all a lowering steps onto that leg. In the second case the required wrap is zero. This proves (10) with the original pivotal weights rather than an abstract trace.

The density in (7) factors explicitly as C_cap=L R with

L=∑j=0N−1xjFjfjeN−1,R=∑k=0N−1x1−N−kQ−kFkfN−1−kek. L=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\frac{x^j}{F_j}f^j e_{N-1},\qquad R=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}x^{1-N-k}Q^{-k}F_k f^{N-1-k}e_k.

The multiplication rule (2) gives

RL=γeN−1,Ccap2=γCcap,γ=Nx2−2NQ≠0. R L=\gamma e_{N-1},\quad C_{cap}^2=\gamma C_{cap},\qquad \gamma=N x^{2-2N}Q\ne0. \tag{11}

Thus the cap is a marked primitive projective density, with its full source character gamma retained. Formula (11) is asserted on Phi=0 here: modulo the nilpotent ideal C_cap/gamma is the primitive idempotent e_(N-1), so it is primitive. A generic cyclic cap must keep its actual wrap coefficients. Neither cyclicity of tau_top nor erasure of gamma is used to convert it into a preferred modified trace.

6. An actual period detects the nilpotent relative source

At N=2, q=i, eta=exp(pi i/4), x=i, direct use of the actual positive balanced R gives

aR=ηI+η−12fe0. a_R=\eta I+\frac{\eta^{-1}}2 f e_0.

Here (f e_0)^2=0 and the second term lies in the nilpotent radical. The original cap (9)-(10) satisfies

Λcap(I)=0,Λcap(fe0)=2. \Lambda_{cap}(I)=0,\qquad \Lambda_{cap}(f e_0)=2. \tag{12}

Thus Lambda_cap(a_R)=eta inverse, the actual nonzero one-crossing cut before its retained external lines. Its entire value comes from the radical term; semisimple reduction gives zero. More generally Lambda_cap(a_R^m)=m eta^(m-2) in this example. The full framing and modified-dimension lines multiply these exact values and have not been reset.

This is an actual source obstruction to factoring the word period through the semisimple quotient, not the previously noted failure of a naive tensor product of repaired shift pairs. The regular module’s ordinary trace also kills the radical, whereas the actual divided cap functional does not.

7. Quantum normal and geometric comparison remain distinct

The flat A_Phi transport is at a fixed original root Q. It does not by itself extend through a moving Q^N normal. For example, at N=2 the original finite matrices have

ftot2=(1+Q)F1K1−1F2,[ftot21−Q2]Q=−1=12F1K1−1F2≠0. f_{tot}^2=(1+Q)F_1K_1^{-1}F_2,\qquad \left[\frac{f_{tot}^2}{1-Q^2}\right]_{Q=-1} =\tfrac12F_1K_1^{-1}F_2\ne0. \tag{13}

On the actual highest product source at x=i its all-ones output value is -2i. A claimed whole Q-normal Taft model setting f^N=0 would erase this real actual current. Its divided-power/endpoint extension must be kept. The central Phi normal, the meridian norm and the Q-normal are not equated by their notation.

The construction provides an actual non-naive word/source transport and an explicit divided-trace period candidate before jets in the central parameters. It still does not identify that period with the marked geometric Bloch regulator, prove survival of its geometric block under the final finite source, or control its hyperbolic-volume norm. The smooth companion frame alone does not supply those additional identifications.

There is a further essential distinction. Phi in this note is the Frobenius-center value of a coproduct power at a FIXED root Q_N. It is not a coordinate on the q-to-one geometric critical variety. At the actual M=1 augmentation all root central meridians are I. The direct Wirtinger induction in the closed-braid center calculation, Section 4, proves that its completed knot-representation germ is entirely abelian. Consequently deforming this center/source family locally at that closed augmentation cannot produce the hyperbolic flat connection. The generic semicyclic comparison remains on its reducible character locus.

The full holonomy line and this fusion basis can be considered at other admissible center points, but that does not continue the specified augmentation frame or identify its selected period on another component. Any regulator comparison must leave this local germ by an independently proved global center or q-to-one critical source transform. The exact socle/divided-trace formula by itself does neither. In particular its nonzero nilpotent source class must not be renamed the geometric period or its coefficient.
