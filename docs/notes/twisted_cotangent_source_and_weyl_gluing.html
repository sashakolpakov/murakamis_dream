The compact source torsor and its actual Weyl gluing

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The fixed compact source is a nontrivial cotangent torsor with an explicit Chern transition. The actual finite root module has a meridian-dependent Weyl adapter whose square is exactly the full modified-dimension function times its recorded scalar. The resulting order-two cocycle has a marked rational splitting at every level, including even levels.

Quantum/dynamical Weyl operators have existing coverage; see the scope of Etingof–Varchenko. We use that reference for coverage, not a proof or a claim that its precise normalization equals the one below. The cotangent torsor is identified directly by its transition equations (1)–(2). The already proved actual Weyl descent gives state reciprocity. The new calculations identify the compact source transition and the explicit balanced-basis scalar cocycle with our full peripheral factor.

These are source and gluing theorems. They do not identify the actual global geometric period or prove its volume norm.

1. An explicit twisted cotangent torsor

Use the compact source

X=(P1 times P1) minus {s_0t_0+s_1t_1=0},
omega_0=dw wedge dz/(1+wz)^2.

Project to the first P1. On its affine chart w define the fiber coordinate b=z/(1+wz). Each fiber is an affine line, including the value z=infinity when allowed. On the second chart put

w'=1/w, b'=1/(w'+z).

The exact overlap formulas are

b'=w-w^2 b, dw' wedge db'=dw wedge db=omega_0. (1)

In particular X is a torsor under the cotangent bundle O(-2), with the affine term w retained. For the local primitives

lambda=-b dw, lambda'=-b' dw'

one has

d lambda=d lambda'=omega_0,
lambda'-lambda=dlog w.                         (2)

This is the connection torsor of O(1), in this explicit transition convention. It is not the split total cotangent bundle. On the marked real sphere z=bar(w), lambda is the Chern connection form -bar(w)dw/(1+|w|^2), and

integral_Gamma omega_0=-2pi*i,
integral_Gamma[-omega_0/(2pi*i)]=1.             (3)

Thus the sign agrees with c1(O(1))=1. Equation (2), rather than a globally defined primitive, records the nonzero source class.

There is a useful obstruction to an incorrect global comparison. On a split framing chart with global tau and invertible A,

Omega_cut=dtau wedge dlog A=d(tau dlog A)

is exact. No globally regular map from X to this split chart can pull Omega_cut back to a nonzero multiple of omega_0 in cohomology: pullback preserves exactness and (3) forbids it. A nonzero whole comparison must retain a twisted transition, a divisor residue or its equivalent marked relative class. Equality of local two-forms does not supply that data.

2. The actual complete head patches globally

Let N>=2, n=N-1, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2. At complete color M=1 identify the actual balanced head with H^0(P1,O(n)), by e_j=w^j. Its exact generators on this space are

K=-(1/q)D_(Q^-1),
Ecal=w^(-1)(D_(Q^-1)-1),
F=w(1-QD_Q), D_Q f(w)=f(Qw).                 (4)

The E numerator cancels at w=0. The F coefficient on w^n is 1-Q(n+1)=1-QN=0, so all three preserve this finite section space. They are exactly the finite matrices, not an independent quantization.

The line transition is f_infinity(u)=u^n f_zero(1/u), u=1/w. It transforms the shift to

D_Q^(w) -> Q^n D_(Q^-1)^(u)=Q^(-1)D_(Q^-1)^(u).

Consequently the generators in the second chart are

(K,Ecal,F) -> (K_u^(-1),-F_u,-Ecal_u).         (5)

Equivalently let C e_j=e_(n-j). Direct matrix multiplication gives C K C=K^(-1), C Ecal C=-F, C F C=-Ecal. The original constant source vector, average co-source, and coupon e_ij=1/N are fixed by C. Thus the actual complete source has this exact Weyl gluing, not just a matching semiclassical two-form.

The source level also has a precise half-form description:

O(N-1)=O(N) tensor K_(P1)^(1/2),
K_(P1)^(1/2)=O(-1), dim H^0(O(N-1))=N.       (6)

This is a bundle identity with the unique half-canonical line on P1. In the physical convention Omega_phys=2pii omega_0, h_N=2pii/N, its prequantum curvature is Omega_phys/h_N=N omega_0, with the Chern sign of (3). Thus the root level N and the actual section degree N-1 differ by this specified half-form factor. No limit is required for (4)–(6).

3. The balanced Weyl matrix at generic meridian

Keep the actual root finite head with meridian M, and write

K_M e_j=-(M/q)Q^(-j)e_j,
E_M e_j=(M Q^(-j)-M^(-1))e_(j-1), j>0,
E_M e_0=0,
F e_j=(1-Q^(j+1))e_(j+1), j<n, F e_n=0.

Here E_M is Ecal, not the unscaled Chevalley E. Put

P_j=(Q;Q)_j, P_j(M^2)=(M^2 Q;Q)_j,
D_M e_j=M^j P_j/P_j(M^2) e_j,
W_M=D_M C.                                  (7)

Work on the marked open where the displayed denominators and numerators are units; in particular they are units near M=1. Then the actual generator comparison is

W_M K_M W_M^(-1)=K_(M^-1)^(-1),
W_M E_M W_M^(-1)=-F,
W_M F W_M^(-1)=-E_(M^-1).                    (8)

For E after reversing the basis, its raising coefficient is M Q(j+1)-M(-1). The adjacent ratio of D is

D_(j+1)/D_j=M(1-Q^(j+1))/(1-M^2Q^(j+1)),

giving -(1-Q^(j+1)). The F equation follows from the inverse ratio and 1-Q(-j)=-Q(-j)(1-Q^j). The K equation is immediate from Qn=Q(-1). This proves (8) including both endpoints.

On full unrolled colors the same matrix sends H_M to -H_(M^-1), with the meridian lift alpha’=2n-alpha retained. Termwise substitution in the actual universal R therefore gives the Chevalley turnover with reversed tensor order. The original Cartan half-character, cups, caps and twist transform as in the generator proof of actual Weyl descent. This does not discard the stripped-Cartan multiplier recorded there.

4. The square is the full peripheral function

Following each reversed basis vector twice gives

W_(M^-1) W_M=c_N(M) I,
c_N(M)=N M^(N-1)/S_N(M^2),
S_N(Y)=1+Y+...+Y^(N-1).                     (9)

For a direct proof the j-th diagonal product is D_(M^-1,j) D_(M,n-j). Using Q(N-r)=Q(-r) pairs its suffix factors and proves independence of j. At j=0 it is

D_(M,n)=M^n (Q;Q)_n/(M^2Q;Q)_n
       =N M^n/S_N(M^2),

since (Q;Q)_(N-1)=N and (M2Q;Q)_(N-1)=S_N(M2).

This is exactly the already fixed full modified-dimension adapter:

c_N(M)=(N/eta_N)d_N(M), eta_N=i^(N+3).       (10)

It is invariant under M inversion and has value one at M=1. Its whole meridian germ is not constant. On M=exp(u),

c_N(exp u)=N sinh(u)/sinh(Nu)
         =1-(N^2-1)u^2/6+O(u^4).           (11)

Thus even where the ideal-point multiplier equals one, its peripheral normal data must not be reset. Formula (10) ties the Weyl cocycle to the specified period normalization, rather than an arbitrary projective scalar.

5. A marked rational splitting at odd and even levels

The cocycle in (9) can be solved explicitly, with its divisors kept. For N=2m+1 put

beta_N(M)=(M^2Q;Q)_m/(Q;Q)_m.

For N=2m put

beta_N(M)=[(1+iM)/(1+i)]
                  (M^2Q;Q)_(m-1)/(Q;Q)_(m-1).            (12)

The specified i is in the root coefficient field at even N; its phase is retained. In both cases beta_N(1)=1 and

beta_N(M) beta_N(M^(-1))=c_N(M)^(-1).         (13)

To prove this, pair Q^r with Q^(-r). For odd N all nonzero residues pair, and

(Q;Q)_m^2=N(-1)^m Q^(m(m+1)/2).

For even N the unpaired residue is Q^m=-1, so

(Q;Q)_(m-1)^2=(N/2)(-1)^(m-1)Q^(m(m-1)/2).

The remaining scalar norm is (1+iM)(1+i/M)/(1+i)2=(1+M2)/(2M), precisely the missing factor. These identities prove (13). Therefore

Wtilde_M=beta_N(M) W_M,
Wtilde_(M^-1) Wtilde_M=I.                   (14)

This is a marked rational norm splitting, not a permission to omit c_N or beta_N from an exact comparison. Their zero/pole divisors are part of the integral source lattice. On the M=1 chart the splitting is regular and fixes the original complete source.

6. Relation to the remaining geometric boundary theorem

The actual source now has an explicit cotangent-torsor cocycle, complete quantum chart gluing, and generic-meridian Weyl scalar whose norm and full modified-dimension role are known exactly. This solves the order-two source gluing problem on its stated charts, uniformly in the knot and level.

Equations (7)–(14) are identities of whole meridian functions at the fixed quantum root. They do not assert the same finite-matrix relations off the root Q^N=1. A q-normal extension must retain the actual two-wall source and its corrections before differentiating.

It does not assert that the standard polynomial SL2 action on H^0(O(N-1)) equals the actual quantum dressing of every geometric framing. The actual finite Weyl adapter (8) is proved; a general unipotent dressing and its source-normal comparison require their own derivation. Nor does (14) select which internal geometric stationary component contributes to the original period.

The global regulator/selected-norm comparison remains to be proved.

Exact source and cocycle checks and results verify all three generator comparisons, the full scalar square and marked norm splitting in 78 actual root-module cases at N=2,…,14. They also check the cotangent transition and full peripheral germ. The independent audit confirms the proof, normalizations, phases and endpoints.

The geometric framing compactification now produces this same twisted cotangent torsor by rescaling its centralizer coordinate. Its nonzero principal Gaussian residue is computed in the explicit gauge calculation. The physical selected-cycle comparison remains separate from that exceptional-divisor residue.
