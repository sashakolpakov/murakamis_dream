Two physical cuts reconstruct both framing positions and their port currents

Research construction, 27 October 2026. Two actual edge positions can be used as integral framing coordinates. Opening those two physical edges gives a literal four-port tensor whose double diagonal is the partially summed two-position array. The original source is its sum divided by N, with no second pivot inserted. The full port bands retain the actual Hopf currents and their cut co-sources.

For bounded port shifts, proper coefficient ratios and the same transverse Gaussian give a canonical nonzero LOCAL band-kernel map. It is linear on the interior current identities; the exact finite endpoint rows remain separate relative data. It is not a product-preserving finite-matrix map, nor a selection of the global framing cycle or its orientation.

1. An etale pair of physical edge positions exists integrally

Use the original actual coefficient model, its full marked peripheral factors, and the regular geometric critical chart of the partial support theorem. At fixed meridian its critical framing torsor has dimension two. The original cut position a_0 has nonzero differential there. Every transfer is an integer difference of physical edge positions, in the actual oriented boundary convention. Here differentials on the critical chart mean the continuous logarithmic physical clocks u_e=H a_e; the integer labels specify their lattice and are not independent continuous functions there.

If every edge differential were a multiple of da_0 on this two-dimensional tangent space, all transfer differentials would be multiples of da_0. All independent flow differentials would then have rank at most one, a contradiction. Thus some other physical edge e has

da_0 wedge da_e != 0                            (1.1)

on a dense open subchart. This selects a FIXED edge of the diagram, not a level-dependent change of state. Shrink to its regular etale chart and put a=a_0, b=a_e.

There is no hidden lattice index. Let G be the connected closed four-valent diagram graph, with the actual oriented integer flow lattice L=ker(incidence). Bivalent bends/cups/caps may be collapsed after transporting their dual basis labels; their actual frame matrices and scalar coefficients remain in the tensor network. The affine relabeling a->N-1-a in a self-dual chart, if used, is recorded rather than discarded. It does not change L or its index.

For any set S of removed edges, incidence ranks give

rank(ev_S:L->Z^S)=|S|+1-c(G\S).                 (1.2)

Indeed rank L=E-V+1, and its kernel under evaluation on S is the cycle lattice of G, of rank E-|S|-V+c(G). For S={e_0,e}, (1.1) forces rank two, hence Gis connected. Choose a spanning tree disjoint from both edges. Fundamental cycles of the complementary chords form an integral basis of L, and their evaluations on these two edges are the two coordinate vectors, up to the fixed orientation signs. Thus

(a,b,j_1,...,j_(m-1))                          (1.3)

are unimodular actual flow coordinates. No finite cover or congruence class is introduced. The actual finite support and all zero-extension boundary inequalities are transported by this integer change of variables. The physical clocks are A=Q^a and B=Q^b. Actual qa,qb and the original half-Cartan root marks are retained in the coefficient field, as in the partial support theorem. The unimodular argument introduces no NEW lattice cover and does not erase those existing lift characters.

2. A literal cut tensor, rather than an invented second state

In a braid circuit write the word as A B at a level containing the selected edge e, on tensor slot t. Let E_bd^(t) be the literal matrix unit inserted into that wire. Keep the original closure caps, pivots and framing/peripheral lines in the SAME places. Let P denote their contraction, leaving the original cut open. Define the four-port tensor by

T_(a,b)^(c,d)=[P(A E_bd^(t) B)]_(c,a).          (2.1)

This is an explicit reconstruction using N^2 insertions in the ORIGINAL network. The inserted matrix unit replaces the identity on that one edge; no extra color, trace, state, cap or pivot has been introduced. The same definition works in any cup/cap/plat diagram: replace that literal wire identity by E_bd and contract all other original tensors. It is independent of how the finite contraction is parenthesized.

Since sum_b E_bb=I, re-closing just this identity edge gives

C_(c,a)=sum_b T_(a,b)^(c,b).                    (2.2)

If the original diagram happens to put a diagonal transport on this edge, it is retained on its ORIGINAL side in (2.1), or its known entries are included in T. An independently added g_b would change (2.2). In particular cutting an ordinary internal identity edge does NOT authorize a second pivotal trace.

Set

J_(a,b)=T_(a,b)^(a,b).                         (2.3)

Actual total-index conservation makes the reclosed C diagonal termwise for the original kernel and all scalar Green moments. The original cut coupon has diagonal entries 1/N, hence

original reduced period=(1/N)sum_(a,b) J_(a,b).  (2.4)

This is 1/N, not 1/N^2. Every original pivot, zero-framing line, meridian lift character, base compact factor and external d_N(M) remains where it was in (2.1); multiplying (2.4) by the retained external line gives the original full state.

Equivalently the exact weighted family is

F(x,y)=(1/N)Tr_cut[
            x^J P(A y^(J_t) B)].              (2.5)

Its coefficient of x^a y^b is J_(a,b)/N. Thus the two-position array is realized by two physical wire couplings before any formal completion. It is not only an arbitrary array relabeled as a matrix.

3. Actual Hopf transport through the cut coupon

In a genuine integral q-family use the ordinary cap P_o. At the original root use the original pivotal cap with its retained scalar adapter. For any actual h, pivotal Hopf naturality gives the exact insertion identity

D_h P_o(A X B)=P_o[A D_h,tot(X) B],             (3.1)

where X is ANY inserted operator at the chosen intermediate level, and D_h=ad_h-epsilon(h). The words A,B intertwine the respective actual coproduct representations; their boundary object orders are not silently identified. For a plat, the same proof uses its actual intertwiners and duality maps before and after the chosen wire. Thus (3.1) is valid with all actual cups/caps present.

For X=E_bd^(t), (3.1) is a specified current map from the physical second cut to the original cut. It contains the TRUE total adjoint, including its mixed terms; the local coupon is not presumed to be a tensor-product submodule. For example, in a parallel ordered tensor object

E_tot=sum_i E_i K_(i+1)...K_l,
F_tot=sum_i K_1^(-1)...K_(i-1)^(-1) F_i.

For a local coupon X in slot t, put k_X X=K_t X K_t^(-1) when X is a weight matrix unit. Then

D_E,tot(X)=[E_tot,X] K_tot^(-1),
D_F,tot(X)=F_tot X-k_X^(-1) X F_tot.             (3.2)

The E commutator has its local term [E_t,X]K_(t+1)…K_l and, for EVERY i<t, the term

E_i K_(i+1)...K_(t-1) [K_t,X] K_(t+1)...K_l.

The F expression has its local term K_1(-1)…K_(t-1)(-1)(F_tX-k_X^(-1)XF_t), and the prefix terms (1-k_X^(-1))X K_1(-1)…K_(i-1)(-1)F_i for i<t. Terms i>t cancel exactly. These are the actual induced off-diagonal/prefix rows; omitting them amounts to replacing the true coproduct by a different one. In a dual tensor slot use its actual contragredient matrices, with the same ordered formula.

If the weighted word or scalar Green insertions occur, (3.1) has the explicit additional local crossing/weight currents from the whole Ward construction. For the frozen off-root ADO cap add its recorded defect

R_h=chi^(-1)ptr_K[M_(Z^J),ad_h].                (3.3)

Thus this construction does not assume auxiliary truncated generic-q/M matrices are genuine Hopf intertwiners. It applies directly on the original root family or on the genuine integral q-family, and keeps the actual defect rows on another lift.

4. The off-diagonal port bands and the ORIGINAL source

Retain the complete bands

J^(p,q)_(a,b)=T_(a,b)^(a+p,b+q),               (4.1)

zero outside all four actual port ranges. This is a lossless finite encoding of T. In a parallel oriented charge convention the original scalar tensor has p+q=0. A contragredient cut uses its recorded sign or index reversal, not a new conservation law.

The source on these bands is essential. With the original cut row e_0, (2.2) gives on EVERY current tensor

Phi^(2)(T)=sum_(a,b,p) (e_0)_(a,a+p)
                                         J^(p,0)_(a,b).    (4.2)

In the original balanced source e_0 has every entry 1/N. Thus a homogeneous current of cut degree d uses its p=d band with the same factor 1/N. Only a charge-zero scalar kernel reduces to (2.4). Applying the J^(0,0) source to all Hopf currents would delete precisely the original off-diagonal cut co-sources.

For illustration the first output/input port actions are exactly

(L_A T)^(p,q)_(a,b)
   =sum_r A_(a+p,a+r) J^(r,q)_(a,b),
(R_B T)^(p,q)_(a,b)
   =sum_s B_(a+s,a) J^(p-s,q)_(a+s,b).          (4.3)

The second-port formulas interchange a,p with b,q. Substituting the actual E,F,K, antipodes or dual matrices gives every shifted band and its endpoint coefficient. The independent band-current audit records these rows explicitly. All divided rows are obtained with the full actual coproduct, not by raising a projected scalar row.

The grouped array matrix has ROW=a and COLUMN=b. It is not the ordinary matrix of a one-cut tangle. Even the actual two-cut unknot has T_(a,b)^(c,d)=delta_(c,b)delta_(d,a), so J=I. Then the double diagonal of (E tensor I)T is zero, whereas EJ=E is nonzero. Thus a single physical Hopf port action does NOT descend to ordinary left multiplication on J. Actual boundary reordering may also require braidings or dualities. Those matrices, and their transported coproduct order, must be kept rather than assigning the grouped T a naive Delta action.

There is nevertheless an explicit scalar-coordinate lift. For matrix units define the balanced port channel

L_ij(T)=(E_ij tensor I)T(E_ji tensor I).

Its double diagonal is E_ij J. On the second port the channel (I tensor E_ji)T(I tensor E_ij) has double diagonal J E_ij. These formulas produce exact row and column position shifts and clocks. Their matrix-algebra unit on the full tensor space is a diagonal projection, not its identity. A physical crossing can leave that projection and return through an exchange band, so these channels are NOT a product-preserving replacement of the whole R network.

The two-coordinate Green/coherent construction supplies the full left/right Toeplitz shifts with both row/column tails and their corner. Apply its relative endpoint construction bandwise to the actual allowed windows in (4.1). No tail created by a Green translation is folded back into a physical port.

5. A canonical nonzero local Gaussian map for bounded bands

This physical opening supplies more than scalar coordinate names. Remove both cut edges. The remaining internal graph is connected. Prescribing port shifts p,q with the balanced total charge gives an integer boundary-divergence vector of total zero. The incidence matrix of the chosen spanning tree solves it integrally: the flow on an oriented tree edge is the signed sum of divergences in its child subtree. All internal chord flows remain free.

Consequently each fixed band has actual coefficients

I^(p,q)(a,b,j), j in Z^(m-1),

on an affine integral flow coset, with the same free chords as I^(0,0). Relative to the latter parametrization, every local edge position and crossing transfer changes by an integer constant, bounded in terms of the diagram and |p|+|q|. Actual proper factorial/Cartan ratios therefore give, on the strict interior,

I^(p,q)=R_(p,q)(q,M,clocks) I^(0,0).            (5.1)

This is an identity derived from the ORIGINAL coefficients. R_(p,q) is a finite product of the recorded rational shift ratios and meridian/lift monomials. Every common base-only Cartan, compact-factor and peripheral line remains common; it is not reset to a numerical ideal value. On the regular geometric chart, the variable shape arguments avoid 0 and 1, so for FIXED bounded port shifts its leading coefficient is a unit. The strict-support cone remains nonempty for all large N after such a fixed shift. Equation (5.1) is an INTERIOR identity; its finite zero-extension endpoint rows remain their separate relative data.

Because (a,b) is etale on the critical torsor, the remaining m-1 Hessian is nondegenerate. Let G_(a,b) be the original transverse Gaussian and define

Z^(p,q)=G_(a,b)[R_(p,q) I^(0,0)].              (5.2)

This has the same marked action/base line as Z^(0,0), and its leading amplitude is R_(p,q)|_crit times the nonzero amplitude of Z^(0,0). In particular it is nonzero on the stated chart.

The construction is independent of the integral particular-flow choice. Two choices differ by a cycle supported entirely in the internal graph, hence by an integer translation of the transverse chords. The exact formal Gaussian translation identity, with its correspondingly shifted insertion and critical point, identifies the two formulas when these choices are fixed independently of N (or differ by uniformly bounded shifts). Adding an N-dependent full cycle is a finite logarithmic-deck move and also requires transport of its separate flattening/period mark. A different fixed unimodular chord basis changes only the actual recorded orientation/half-density frame. This is a canonical LOCAL band-kernel after those original marks are fixed.

Every bounded ordinary port Ward row in Sections 3–4 is a finite linear combination of shifts and insertions in these same actual affine arrays. Formula (5.2), with the exact opposite shift on its insertion, is consequently a linear COCHAIN pushforward of their INTERIOR current identities. The exact finite support and framing endpoints stay in the explicitly recorded relative summand; this assertion does not evaluate their geometric images. This is the translation-covariant Wick formula of the residual-current theorem, now with BOTH external coordinates represented by physical cuts. The principal B1 fields project independently to (a,b) by (1.1). Their scalar position directions therefore have the left/right array realization above; their complete Hopf rows retain the off-diagonal and prefix data of (3.2)–(4.3).

This is not an identification of a finite fixed-N matrix algebra with a scalar function algebra. It is a linear map on an actual local coefficient/current completion. It also makes no claim for port shifts growing like N, for summing all such bands, or for the behavior at the two external finite-window boundaries.

6. The nearest actual bands measure the physical wave momentum

Their principal ratio can be computed without guessing an extra source. Use the ACTUAL balanced/factorial boundary frame. The crossing generating functions satisfy

dS_vertex=sum_out Log(p_new) du
                            -sum_in Log(p_new) du.         (6.1)

This follows from the exact generating relations already derived from the actual crossing ratios in the marked holonomy calculation, with the canonical boundary form in the determinant-line proof. The actual diagonal factorial frame change adds its output-minus- input boundary primitive; accordingly its momentum is p_new=(1-A)p_old. Using p_old in (6.1) while keeping the new constant source would change the band marks.

Sum (6.1) over the open network at its original geometric critical point. All internal edge terms cancel by momentum continuity. The only boundary terms are

Log p_(0,out) du_(0,out)+Log p_(e,out) du_(e,out)
  -Log p_(0,in) du_(0,in)-Log p_(e,in) du_(e,in). (6.2)

The original cut has p_(0,in)=p_(0,out)=1. At the second identity edge p_(e,in)=p_(e,out)=p_e. The original closed pivotal phases can add 2pi i integer multiples to the logarithmic momenta. Their exponentiated multipliers are one; their finite coefficients and flattening/period marks remain retained in the exact ratios.

In the band J^(p,q), the inputs a,b are held fixed and the outputs are a+p,b+q. Hence the first action change is

(S_(p,q)-S_0)/H=p Log p_(0,out)+q Log p_(e,out)+O(H).

The integral tree particular flow makes no additional term: its internal contributions cancel in (6.2). The shifted regular amplitude tends to the original amplitude. Thus the ratio in (5.1) has the EXACT marked principal symbol

R_(p,q),0=p_cut^p p_e^q.

For the nearest charge-zero exchange bands this gives

R_(+1,-1),0=p_e^(-1),
R_(-1,+1),0=p_e.                              (6.3)

These are the ACTUAL local edge wave momenta in the physical Gauss matrix. If a developed Wirtinger meridian is written in a common global frame, its prefix conjugation must be retained: that matrix is not silently identified with the local Gauss matrix solely because both are meridians.

There is a useful precise parity consequence on a physical two-parabolic chart. Suppose the matrices in the same ACTUAL physical cut frame are as follows, with Tr(g_cut g_e) a FIXED representation invariant on that framing chart:

g_cut=[[1+s,-s],[s,1-s]],
g_e=[[k,(1-k)p_e],[(k-1)/p_e,2-k]].

Put z=s(k-1), c=Tr(g_cut g_e)-2 and

c=z(2-p_e-p_e^(-1)),
D=-z(p_e-p_e^(-1)).

The two branches at fixed position clocks exchange p_e and its inverse. On the regular chart z!=0,p_e!=0, (6.3) therefore proves

R_(+1,-1),0-R_(-1,+1),0=D/z.                 (6.4)

The principal ODD orientation section is realized by two actual nearby port bands. It was not imposed by a field trace or by a postulated Weyl action. If the two-position density is Omega_cut=dlog A wedge dlog B/(B D), its product with this actual odd-band symbol is the nonzero DECK-EVEN density

dlog A wedge dlog B/(B z).                   (6.5)

Equations (6.4)–(6.5) require the displayed physical Gauss chart (including any prefix adapter); they are not a replacement of that dictionary for an arbitrary pair of developed word matrices.

7. The remaining orientation and global-source datum

An etale two-position chart need not have degree one. On a two-sheeted framing chart its leading density can be odd under the deck involution. The local choice of geometric critical point and marked square-root/orientation in (5.2) then selects a local sheet; ordinary field trace of that odd density would be zero. The literal source (4.2) and full relative finite-window tails do not authorize replacing a selected cycle by that field trace.

Section 6 now computes an actual local odd-band observable that distinguishes those sheets, when the physical chart is fixed. However the original second-edge closing operation is the q=0 identity-cap source (4.2). It has not been proved that its global relative current/cycle selects the odd combination in (6.4). Substituting that inserted observable for the uninserted scalar state would be a different problem. The construction supplies a specified physical current/source problem, a nonzero local band map and two independent position directions. It does not yet determine the global marked cycle or prove that its geometric coefficient survives the final contraction.
