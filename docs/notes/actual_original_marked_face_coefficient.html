The original marked source is one positive face coefficient

30 October 2026. Independently reviewed and frozen. For every fixed folded channel assignment, the literal complete-circle plat source equals one coefficient of an explicit homogeneous polynomial with nonnegative real coefficients, times its known scalar and retained half-twist phases. The proof retains the original marked cap, finite support, primitive measure and both level parities. It does not substitute a newly chosen period for the physical source.

The identity below is finite and exact. It does not yet estimate that coefficient, prove it nonzero for every channel assignment, or establish a hyperbolic-volume asymptotic.

1. Literal source and polynomial

Fix a connected 2k-plat projection, k>=2, with all crossings in R recorded two-strand boxes. The exterior contains the actual k bottom cups and k top caps, one of the latter carrying the ORIGINAL unrestricted mark. Retain each box’s half twist epsilon_i in {0,+1,-1}. The source is the original complete-circle contraction, not a routed replacement. Any separately recorded exterior framing line multiplies both sides of the identity below and is not erased.

Remove half twists only in the planar graph used to name its regions; their exact scalars omega_(a_i)^epsilon_i remain in the source. The plane matching graph Delta has R+2 white faces. Let u be the exterior face and v the bounded face inside the actual marked top cap. These are the two faces adjacent to the marked physical edge. Write F_* for the other R faces. The geometric certificate is not needed for the finite identity; when it is present these are exactly the two constant-prefix faces of the resonant chart.

Connectedness here concerns the planar source graph, not the number of main link components. Multiple main components are allowed. In particular the all-untwisted standard 2k-plat generally retains k main components; the positive untwisted specialization is a statement about that augmented LINK source, not automatically a family of k-bridge knots.

Put N>=3, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2. For each actual integer label 0<=a_i<=floor(d/2), set s_i=d-2a_i and

beta_a=S_(2a)/S_a^2, S_j=product_(r=1)^j 2sin(pi r/N),
k_a=1 if s=0, and k_a=2 otherwise.

Use the exact local factors proved in the positive-circle theorem:

D_a(x,z,y)=det[z I+(x/2)H_-+(y/2)H_+],
Ptilde_s(x,y)=sum_(j=0)^s S_s/[S_j S_(s-j)] x^(s-j)y^j.

At box i let (f0,fin,fout,f2) be its ORIGINAL outer, input-middle, output-middle and final white regions. Define

P_(N,a)(L)=product_i
   D_(a_i)(L_f0,L_fin,L_f2)
   D_(a_i)(L_f0,L_fout,L_f2)
   Ptilde_(s_i)(L_f0,L_f2).                       (1)

Repeated region labels, if the actual plane diagram has them, are substituted literally. Every coefficient of (1) is nonnegative real, and its total degree is sum_i(2a_i+s_i)=dR. Define

C_(N,a)=[product_(f in F_*) L_f^d]
              P_(N,a)(L) evaluated at L_u=L_v=0. (2)

The fixed-channel contribution A_(N,a)^epsilon to the original marked root source is EXACTLY

A_(N,a)^epsilon
   =N^R product_i[k_(a_i) beta_(a_i)^2
                      omega_(a_i)^epsilon_i] C_(N,a). (3)

Thus C_(N,a)>=0, with zero allowed. The full original source is the finite sum of (3) over its original folded labels. In particular the untwisted terms have a positive coefficient description; signed half twists retain their actual channel phases. No cancellation between different half-twisted channels is ruled out by (3).

2. A signed face index in the original flat-plat sweep

Use the actual bottom-to-top order of physical heads and adjacent boxes. Number the gaps between strands by g=1,…,2k-1. Odd gaps start inside a bottom cup and end inside a top cap. Even gaps start and end in the exterior. A box on zero-based slot a replaces ONLY the region in gap g=a+1: its old middle region ends at the input pi vertex, and its new middle region starts at the output W vertex. All other gap regions continue through the box unchanged.

Let M_f count these middle corners in face f. Let T_f be the signed ordinary physical turn incidence: each original cup or cap contributes +1 to its outside face and -1 to its inside face. These signs are the literal source signs: both cup and ordered ordinary cap have the same positive first-head monomial q^(d i) on i+j=d. In particular a top cap does not acquire an independently reversed sign.

Every bounded segment in an odd gap has two ends, each either a middle corner or an inside cup/cap. Every bounded segment in an even gap has two middle ends. Therefore

M_f-T_f=2 for every bounded face.                 (4)

Connectedness forces at least one box in each internal even gap: an unused such gap would separate the source projection into two disjoint plat blocks. Each of the k-1 even gaps therefore contributes two middle ends to the exterior, whereas all 2k cups/caps contribute their outside incidence there. Hence

M_u=2(k-1), T_u=2k, M_u-T_u=-2.                   (5)

This is the signed Euler turning identity in the literal Morse convention. Equivalently, middle corners and inside turns contribute +1 to twice the tangent winding of a face boundary; outside turns contribute -1. The sweep proof specifies all signs without selecting an arbitrary face orientation.

The literal pi dualization does not invalidate (4). It reverses all three ports, and each adjacent face sees two of these local reversals. More directly the exact complete-circle factor already combines its auxiliary cup and two inverse physical cups, leaving precisely the two middle denominators with exponent d/2. There are no unrecorded turns to add after applying that exact factorization.

3. The original mark gives exactly the two missing face exponents

Use centered face logarithms. For an ordered physical pair with outside face x, middle face z and final face y,

i=d/2+N(Log L_x-Log L_z)/(2pi i),
j=d/2+N(Log L_z-Log L_y)/(2pi i).                  (6)

On each actual cup/cap y=x. The exact complete-circle expression is its scalar k_a beta_a^2 times its numerator in (1), divided by the product of its two middle variables to power d/2. Thus the ordinary whole-source face monomial has exponent (d/2)(T_f-M_f). Equations (4),(5) give

L_u^d product_(f != u) L_f^(-d).                  (7)

This holds also at EVEN levels: d/2 may be half-integral locally, but the assembled exponents in (7) are integers. Every local logarithmic lift is retained until this assembly.

The mark sign is fixed by the original ordered matrices. At its cap with first head i and SECOND head j=d-i,

ordinary cap(i,j)=N^-1 q^(di),
mark(i,j)=N^-2 q^(dj).

The original mark is unrestricted before contraction. The k bottom cups fix total physical degree kd, every actual box preserves that integer degree, and the other k-1 top caps use degree (k-1)d. Thus the two surviving marked endpoints necessarily have i+j=d. This is a consequence of whole-source conservation, not an early diagonal replacement of the marked tensor.

Their ratio is N^-1 q(-d2)Q^(dj). The second head is the one whose raw face clock is -L_v/L_u. On the centered lift (6), the ratio is

N^-1 (L_v/L_u)^d.                                (8)

Using the first head in (8) would reverse the marked character and give an incorrect target coefficient. Equation (8) changes exponent +d to zero at u, and exponent -d to zero at v. All other exponents remain -d. No integral character has been selected anew or discarded.

For completeness, the source constants also agree exactly. There are k cups N q(-d2)q^(di), k-1 ordinary caps N^-1 q^(di), and one marked cap N^-2 q^(dj). Each centered physical head contributes the constant q(d2/2); these 2k constants cancel the k cup factors q(-d2). Their total scalar is therefore precisely N^-1. Consequently the assembled meromorphic root product is

N^-1 product_i[k_(a_i)beta_(a_i)^2omega_(a_i)^epsilon_i]
       P_(N,a)(L) product_(f in F_*) L_f^(-d).     (9)

The local root cup poles have been canceled by the proved complete-circle identity before (9) is used. No generic-q cancellation is inferred.

4. Primitive face grids at both parities and exact physical support

The centered homogeneous source lattice is the cycle lattice of Delta. Its integral identification with face-potential differences modulo a constant is proved in the original rank theorem, section 1. Fix the exterior face potential to zero. There are R+1 independent integral face potentials, with no finite-index Jacobian.

At ODD N, d/2 is integral. Set L_u=1 and let every other L_f range independently through mu_N={Q^p:0<=p<N}. Formula (6) gives integral heads. At EVEN N there is an exact specified coset. Give each main edge the Z2 value1 and each matching edge the value0. Every shaded triangle has two main edges and one matching edge, so this cochain sums to zero on each triangle of the dual sphere. It integrates to face values t_f in Z2, unique after setting t_u=0. In the literal plat sweep this is simply the parity of the gap in which a region is born; the exterior has parity0. In particular t_v=1.

Use the grid

L_f=q^(t_f) Q^(p_f), p_f in Z/N, L_u=1.           (10)

On a main edge, d+t_left-t_right is even, so (6) is integral. On a matching edge, its auxiliary row is a plus a face-potential difference; the matching parity0 makes that integral too. The physical cup complements and all channel equations hold on the chosen affine lifts. The number of grid points is exactly N^(R+1) at either parity.

There are no extra physical states hidden in this modular grid. Reduce physical heads to 0,…,d. The local support theorem proves that a surviving circle has BOTH physical totals in the actual interval [d-a,d+a], which contains at most N integers. Their congruence modulo N therefore forces equality as UNWRAPPED totals, with the original auxiliary row in [0,2a]. Unsupported pairs are killed by the small white or by the ONE forced factor retained after the cup cancellation. This is a zero of the entire root circle, not an undefined zero times a pole. Cups/caps similarly have unique physical total d in their congruence class. Thus every nonzero grid point gives an actual allowed state.

Conversely every allowed state defines one grid point by the primitive face-potential isomorphism. Distinct allowed states cannot differ by N times a nonzero flow, since every head lies in an interval of length less than N. The actual allowed states are therefore exactly the possibly nonzero grid points, with zero points harmlessly included.

Finally retain the even-level carry signs rather than canceling them locally by fiat. A surviving canonical state and its chosen affine lift differ by N times an integral CLOSED source flow. By the integral face-potential theorem this is N times integral face shifts. The full expression (9) has integer face exponents and is unchanged by such shifts. Hence the product of the local recorded carry multipliers is one. This proves the global carry cancellation in the actual source; it does not assert independent periodicity of each physical tensor.

5. A unique alias and the exact measure

Write the nonnegative-coefficient polynomial as P_(N,a)=sum_n c_n product_f L_f^(n_f), with n_f>=0 and sum_f n_f=dR. In (9), the target exponents are0 at u,v and d at the R other faces. Averaging independently over (10) annihilates a monomial unless

n_v=0 modulo N,
n_f=d modulo N for f in F_*.

Because 0<=d<N, nonnegativity forces n_v>=0 and n_f>=d. Their minimum total already equals dR. Homogeneity therefore forces

n_u=n_v=0, n_f=d for every f in F_*.

There is exactly ONE possible alias, namely the actual coefficient (2). At that exponent the even coset multiplier is1. The normalized face average equals C_(N,a), and the unnormalized source sum multiplies it by N^(R+1). Together with the single N^-1 in (9), this proves (3). No second boundary average or matching-edge trace factor is inserted.

The same calculation also checks the original unmarked ordinary root closure. Its monomial is (7), so every one of the R+1 bounded faces would require polynomial degree at least d. This exceeds the total degree dR. Its whole root sum is consequently zero, as required by the original trace-normal identity. The mark is essential to the nonzero admissible target, not an optional renormalization.

6. Scope and review

Formula (3) is the original fixed-channel ROOT contraction, not merely an equality of off-grid interpolants or local density values. It uses the proved canceled root circle and its exact modular-support zeros. It does not replace genuine generic-q lifts or their normal derivatives. The source weights h0 already include the actual complete-circle normal.

The finite formula is valid at both parities and composite levels. All folded labels occur. A positive geometric certificate supplies additional candidate angle data but is not inferred from positivity of the polynomial. Estimating (2), proving a sharp full-channel bound with retained signed phases, and identifying the filled mixed-color limit remain separate tasks. No volume conjecture is asserted here.

The focused audit, with recorded results, checks 735 signed flat-plat sweeps and 15,386 exact marked face exponents. It compares 17 original fixed-sector W/pi matrix contractions, including the unrestricted original mark, with the independently built determinant coefficient. Its full face-grid checks cover 19,737 assignments: 2,179 supported states, 17,558 forbidden states, and 117 nontrivial even-level carry cancellations. Both level parities and composite levels occur; ordinary root closure zero is checked separately. These are finite identities over exact cyclotomic finite fields, not numerical evidence for an asymptotic or a nonzero coefficient in every sector.
