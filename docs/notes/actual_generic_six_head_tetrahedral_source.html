A generic all-partner factorial formula for the actual six-head source

29 October 2026. This derives the local nested-cup coefficient and its ordinary closed trace at GENERIC zeta, for every physical pair channel 0<=j<=d=N-1, including the high partner d-j. The construction uses the actual Clebsch maps, genuine ordered ribbon duals, and physical cups. No root factorial reflection, conjugation, or quoted quantum 6j formula is used. Combining the factors gives a finite double-sum expression with an explicit root pole on each paired axis. Every actual marked joint-cluster moment is consequently an explicitly bounded-order Taylor coefficient, of order at most four.

This supplies the previously missing generic input for the actual boundary moments. The finite double source sum below is now reduced to an exactly normalized SINGLE generic Racah sum in the actual source-column identity, including all high partners and normal tails. It does not prove that their ratios, the boundary Gram, or recoupling maps have polynomial or subexponential conditioning.

1. Generic modules and literal duality

Fix N>=2, d=N-1. In this note Q=zeta^2 is GENERIC, and

F_k=(Q;Q)_k, F_0=1.

The highest-weight L module, in its divided-F basis |u>, has

F|u>=(1-Q^(u+1))|u+1>,
Ecal|u>=zeta^(-L-1)(1-Q^(L+1-u))|u-1>,
K|u>=zeta^L Q^(-u)|u>.

Use Delta(F)=F tensor I+K^(-1) tensor F and Delta(Ecal)=Ecal tensor K+I tensor Ecal. The canonical cup is

P_L(u)=(-1)^u zeta^[u(L-u-1)] F_u F_(L-u)/F_L,
p_L(u,L-u)=P_L(u), ell_L=p_L^(-1).             (1)

Its first coefficient is one. The physical cup is pV=cV p_d, cV=N zeta(-d2), exactly the regular lift of the original cup. All generic formulas below are rational identities, so their stated continuations do not assume denominators remain nonzero at the root.

For every j=0,…,d set l=d-j and define

h_j(u)=(-1)^u zeta^(-du)
   Q^[u(l-1)-u(u-1)/2] F_(j+u)F_(d-u)/(F_j F_d),
      0<=u<=l,
W_j|r>=Delta(F)^r h_j/F_r, 0<=r<=2j.          (2)

The highest E equation gives (2) directly. The E,F commutator gives every interior covariance equation. The last F equation follows either from the endpoint below or from the full-column-rank physical E matrix above degree d. Thus W_j is the ACTUAL generic intertwiner from the ordinary highest-weight 2j module, without restricting j to the root low fold.

Here is a useful formula with ONLY physical factorial denominators. For 0<=A,B<=d and A+B=l+r,

W_j[r](A,B)=F_A F_B sum_u
   h_j(u) zeta^(-dt) Q^(ut)
                /[F_u F_(l-u) F_t F_(r-t)],
t=B-l+u,
max(0,l-B)<=u<=min(l,A), 0<=t<=r.             (3)

Outside these ranges the entry is zero. Indeed, write Delta(F)=A0+B0 with A0 B0=Q^(-1)B0 A0, expand with the finite Gaussian binomial, and cancel F_r. The two physical moves have lengths t and r-t; therefore ALL four denominator indices in (3) are <=d, even when r>=N. Formula (3) proves the entire W_j matrix is holomorphic at zeta=q=exp(pi i/N), including the high partner channels.

Let P exchange the two physical legs. The actual right ribbon dual is

DW_j=p_(2j)^T W_j^T(ellV^T tensor ellV^T)P,
DW_j[r;u,v]=P_(2j)(2j-r)
   W_j[2j-r](d-v,d-u)/[pV(d-v)pV(d-u)].        (4)

There is no Hermitian conjugation in (4). The swaps and inverse cup coefficients are fixed by the actual nested arcs.

2. Own endpoint and projector normalization

The generic lowest endpoint is EXACTLY

W_j[2j](j+u,d-u)=zeta^[j(d-2j)]h_j(u).        (5)

Proof. The lowest F equation and the highest E equation have the same coefficient ratio, so it suffices to take u=0 in (3). The remaining finite sum is

sum_s (-1)^s Q^[s(s-1)/2]
              F_(d-s)/[F_s F_(l-s)F_(j-s)]=Q^(lj).

For an own finite verification, rewrite it as F_d/(F_l F_j) times 2phi1(Q-j,Q-l;Q^-d;Q,1). The finite identity

sum_(s=0)^n (Q^-n;Q)_s(b;Q)_s
     (cQ^n/b)^s/[F_s(c;Q)_s]=(c/b;Q)_n/(c;Q)_n

follows from the already finite-binomial proof, section2, by replacing Q,b,c with Q(-1),b(-1),c^(-1) and clearing the displayed finite powers. Substitution n=j,b=Q-l,c=Q-d gives the endpoint constant in (5). This is a finite summation proof, not an assumed recoupling formula.

The exact generic norm is

DW_j W_j=n_j I,
n_j=(-1)^(d+j)cV^(-2)
   zeta^[j(j+1)-d(2j+1)]
                  F_(d+j+1)/[F_(d-j)F_(2j+1)]. (6)

To prove its scalarity directly, DW_j W_j commutes with the generic ordinary K,F: its distinct K weights force diagonality, and nonzero interior F arrows force equal entries. Take its first entry, apply (5), and insert the cups (1). The only sum remaining is

sum_(u=0)^l Q^[-(j+1)u]
         [j+u choose j]_Q[d-u choose j]_Q
   =Q^[-(j+1)l][d+j+1 choose l]_Q.

After multiplication by Q^((j+1)l), this is the coefficient of z^l in 1/[(z;Q)(j+1)(zQ^(j+1);Q)(j+1)]. Expanding the reciprocal finite products by their inductive coefficient formula proves it. All remaining factors give (6).

Consequently

e_j=W_j DW_j/n_j, j=0,...,d                   (7)

are the genuine generic pair projectors and sum to the physical identity. Indeed they are intertwiners with distinct ordinary highest weights; (6) gives their own nonzero retractions, and their dimensions sum_j(2j+1)=N^2. Their pair Casimir eigenvalues are

c_j(zeta)=zeta^(2j+1)+zeta^(-2j-1).

3. Finite factorial source corner and full closed trace

Take ANY ordinary triangle a,b,c in 0,…,d. Put

J=a+b+c, r=b+c-a, r1=a+b-c, r2=a+c-b.

The original six-head source is pV_16 pV_23 pV_45. Apply DW_a,DW_b, DW_c on consecutive physical pairs and extract ordinary output (2a,r,0). Define the following completely explicit analytic sum:

B_abc=sum_(k=0)^(d-max(a,c))
   h_a(d-a-k) h_c(d-c-k)
   *W_b[r1](d-c-k,a+k)
   /[P_d(d-a-k)P_d(k)P_d(d-c-k)].              (8)

Each W entry in (8) is the physical finite sum (3). Hence (8) is a finite DOUBLE quantum-factorial sum, with at most N^2 terms; every denominator factorial index is <N. In particular B_abc is holomorphic on a fixed neighborhood of each chosen root, including when a,b,c are high partners.

The literal source corner is

sDW_abc=cV^(-3) zeta^[c(d-2c)-2c]
                            P_(2b)(r1) B_abc. (9)

To check (9), the three source cup constraints reduce the contributing physical indices to

(u1,u2,u3,u4,u5,u6)
   =(d-k,a+k,d-a-k,c+k,d-c-k,k).

In (4), the first W is its highest column, the third is its lowest column (5), and the middle is W_b[r1]. Three of the six physical inverse cups cancel the three actual source cups. The remaining three give precisely (8). This derivation retains every original port and source coefficient.

Let Theta_abc be the genuine ordinary invariant with corner Theta_abc(2a,r,0)=1. The generic ordered pairing already derived by finite Vandermonde in the marked two-vertex note, section2, is valid WITHOUT a low-fold restriction:

Xi_abc=D(Theta_cba)Theta_abc
   =(-1)^r1 zeta^[2c-r1(r-1)]
   F_(2b)F_r2 F_(J+1)
     /[(1-Q)F_r F_(2a)F_r1 F_(2c)].             (10)

The original ordinary closure with projectors e_a,e_b,e_c is therefore

T_abc=sDW_abc sDW_cba Xi_abc/(n_a n_b n_c).

The reversed labels in its second factor are necessary: white is the genuine ordered dual, not a same-order adjoint. Outside ordinary triangle support T_abc=0 identically.

Combining ALL cups and norms yields the simpler exact formula

T_abc=(-1)^d zeta^E/(1-Q)
   *F_r F_r1 F_r2 F_(J+1)
   *product_(j=a,b,c) [F_(d-j)(1-Q^(2j+1))/F_(N+j)]
   *B_abc B_cba,
E=d(3a+2b+3c+3)-4a^2-4c^2+2ac-2J.            (11)

This is an actual generic source trace, not a tetrahedron assigned by name. For a direct normalization check, multiply the two ordinary cup factors in (9) by (10): their surviving factorials are F_r F_r1 F_r2 F_(J+1)/[(1-Q)F_(2a)F_(2b)F_(2c)]. Division by (6) replaces F_(2j+1)/F_(2j) by 1-Q^(2j+1). All physical scales cV cancel. The total sign is (-1)^d, since r1+J is even, and the remaining zeta exponent is E. Formula (11) keeps the ordinary quantum dimension and every high-partner term.

4. A nonsingular coefficient formula for every marked cluster moment

Now specialize near the principal root q=exp(pi i/N), using the NORMAL COORDINATE

Z=1-zeta^(2N), zeta=q(1-Z)^(1/(2N)), zeta(0)=q. (12)

This specifies the analytic germ; it is not a free physical-color parameter. For each folded label l_i<=floor(d/2), take branches

J_i={l_i,d-l_i} if 2l_i<d,
J_i={l_i}       if 2l_i=d.

Let r0 be the number of paired axes. It is fixed for all <=8 branch triples. Each denominator F_(N+j) in (11) contains exactly one factor Z. On an odd simple axis, 1-Q^(2j+1)=Z cancels it. Therefore

G_abc(Z):=Z^r0 T_abc(zeta(Z))                  (13)

is EXPLICITLY holomorphic, by replacing each F_(N+j) by F_(N+j)/Z and each odd simple numerator 1-Q^(2j+1) by 1. No cancellation of large singular matrices is needed to evaluate this formula.

Use the literal root polynomial L_N(z)=(-1)^d U_d(z/2). A legitimate generic lift of the actual root eta on an axis is L_N(Omega(zeta)) times its analytic full cluster projector. At a paired branch j,

L_N(c_j(zeta(Z)))=Z ell_j(Z),
ell_j(Z)=(-1)^d zeta^[-d(2j+1)]
   *[sum_(v=0)^(2j) (1-Z)^v]
                  /[1-zeta^(4j+2)].           (14)

The denominator in (14) is a root unit on every paired axis. This is the finite character identity, not an omission of a Hermite jet.

For I a subset of the paired axes, define

H_I(Z)=sum_(a,b,c)in(J_1,J_2,J_3)
              G_abc(Z) product_(i in I) ell_(j_i)(Z),
k_I=r0+1-|I|, 1<=k_I<=4.                      (15)

Then the ACTUAL original marked boundary moment is EXACTLY

m_I=(-1)^d(q-q^(-1)) [Z^k_I] H_I(Z).          (16)

In particular the top-circle moment needs one normal; the empty moment can require four. All lower moments, high fold partners and their cancellations are retained. This formula applies outside the regular saturation region too; no unsupported cell has been deleted.

Proof of the coherent cancellations. At the root, distinct Casimir nodes coalesce only in the pairs j,d-j. The finite-dimensional Riesz projector for such a whole cluster is analytic and equals e_j+e_(d-j) generically; at a simple node it is e_j. This can equally be written by finite polynomial Chinese remainders. Thus the original closed physical lift with selected circles is the REGULAR function

F_I(Z)=Z^(|I|-r0)H_I(Z).

Keep the original globally exposed marked outer cap (16); the other caps are ordinary. Cutting it and bending with the SAME physical cup gives a generic Hopf endomorphism of V_N. Its distinct generic K weights and nonzero F arrows force a scalar z_I(zeta)I. The open maps are regular because the FULL cluster projectors and all actual physical maps are regular. Its ordinary trace is

F_I(Z)=D_N(zeta) z_I(zeta),
D_N=(-1)^d[N]_zeta
   =(-1)^(d+1) zeta^(-N) Z/(zeta-zeta^(-1)).

Hence H_I is divisible by Z^k_I, exactly. The original unrestricted marked row applied to the original cup is one, so m_I=z_I(q). Taking the coefficient gives (16), since q^(-N)=-1. It agrees with the t-normal formula F_I’(0)/nu_N for zeta=q exp(t), but removes all apparent negative Laurent orders before computing that normal.

This is a regular Hopf lift of the prescribed ROOT moment. It does not identify a formal continuation of a root scalar with the actual moving-q circle or colored-source family. Lift independence follows from the exposed physical scalar cut and its identical root maps. A bare marked row at an arbitrary nested white cap is not substituted for this cut; see the corrected hypothesis in the first-normal source theorem.

5. Bounded jets and the remaining analytic gate

Equations (3),(8),(11)–(16) give a finite algorithm with no matrix minor inversion. There are <=8 branches, each B has <=N^2 terms, and every needed jet order is <=4, independently of N. The generic qdim has not been canceled before the actual marked quotient, nor have the first normals of paired circles been replaced by values.

The formulas also admit explicit factorial ENVELOPES for their fixed jets. Divide every root-zero factor out first. On |Z|<=c/N^2, each remaining quantum factor with index<=3N changes by relative O(1/N), uniformly, because its nonzero root magnitude is >=2sin(pi/N), while the change of its exponent is O(|Z|). The product of O(N) factors is therefore bounded by a constant times its root product. Powers zeta^E, |E|=O(N^2), have the same property. Cauchy’s coefficient bound multiplies this envelope by at most C_k N^(2k) at fixed order k. The finite character in (14) costs an additional polynomial factor. Applying this termwise to the explicit finite sums gives genuine bounded-normal factorial upper bounds, with only polynomial jet cost.

This does NOT prove that m_I/m_top or the boundary square-root unit has polynomial growth. The absolute factorial envelope can exceed the coherent top moment, and (16) retains cancellations between different high/low branches and different summands. A quantitative comparison for that EXACT coefficient remains necessary for a tame source-aware Gram or general graph sewing theorem.

Audit: generic full physical and root-normal comparisons. The audit checks 11 generic finite-field families, all j=0,…,d, 784 literal source corners, and 134 full physical ordinary closures. It also checks 14 actual root joint-cluster families through N=7, all marked moments on each cluster, including unsupported triples, and exact cancellation of every pole and ordinary trace constant. These finite checks supplement the derivations and do not establish conditioning or a new graph volume limit.
