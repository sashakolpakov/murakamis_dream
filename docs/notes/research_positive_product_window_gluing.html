A common positive-product baseline and exact two-endpoint window repair

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The regular row constructions can be assembled on a fixed positive product chart. A finite boundary Cauchy integral gives one holomorphic baseline with the original action bound. The endpoint-role comparison controls this projection, including its actual normal data on the chosen real windows. Two one-sided row repairs, with their common mixed-jet correction subtracted once, then preserve both row families exactly.

The windows remain fixed bulk windows. The conclusion is an angular holomorphic representative with a designated-quadrant bound, not a whole-grid or full-lens replacement. All original exterior and interface currents retain their original spectator.

1. Fixed product domains and designated paths

Fix a positive pair (t*,u*)(t_*,u_*) in the original strips 𝒮={|ℜt|<π}\mathcal S=\{|\Re t|<\pi\}. The pinned product-domain theorem provides symmetric simply connected domains Vt,Vu⊂𝒮V_t,V_u\subset\mathcal S, containing the full real intervals and the target, on whose product a=tan⁡(t/2),b=tan⁡(u/2),q*2=1+a2+b2∉(−∞,0],ℜq*>0.(1) a=\tan(t/2),\quad b=\tan(u/2),\quad q_*^2=1+a^2+b^2\notin(-\infty,0],\quad \Re q_*>0. \qquad\text{(1)} In tangent coordinates each domain is the union of a thin horizontal strip and a thin neighborhood of the segment joining the target tangent to its negative. Both pieces contain zero.

Choose bounded smooth Jordan domains Ωv0⋐Ωv1⋐Ωv2⋐Ωv3⋐Vv,v=t,u,(2) \Omega_v^0\Subset\Omega_v^1\Subset\Omega_v^2 \Subset\Omega_v^3\Subset V_v,\qquad v=t,u, \qquad\text{(2)} containing zero, the target coordinate and fixed small positive real seed intervals. They may be chosen symmetric, with star-shaped tangent images. To see this, take a compact radial set consisting of the required segments and small real intervals in the open star-shaped tangent domain. A smooth positive radial boundary lying between that compact set and the domain boundary gives a bounded star-shaped Jordan neighborhood. Repeat with buffers and apply 2arctan2\arctan. All choices precede NN.

The original phase ψ(t,u)\psi(t,u) is single valued on this product. For precision its derivatives are rt=12Log⁡q*+aq*−a,ru=12Log⁡q*+bq*−b,ψt=−rt/π,ψu=−ru/π.(3) r_t=\tfrac12\operatorname{Log}\frac{q_*+a}{q_*-a},\quad r_u=\tfrac12\operatorname{Log}\frac{q_*+b}{q_*-b},\qquad \psi_t=-r_t/\pi,\quad\psi_u=-r_u/\pi . \qquad\text{(3)} The logarithms have their original value at completeness. Moreover ∂urt=∂tru=−ab/(2q*)\partial_u r_t=\partial_t r_u=-ab/(2q_*), so (3), with the original complete constant, defines the continued original phase. It agrees with every row phase at its real seed.

Lemma 1. A nonzero point of either Ωv2\Omega_v^2 can be joined to its positive real seed interval by a compact path avoiding zero which stays in the sign of its imaginary part, apart from its real seed. If the target is real, either side may be chosen. Fixed buffered tubes supply the regular continuation domains, uniformly for the other endpoint throughout a fixed buffered parameter compact.

Proof. Work in the star-shaped tangent domain. Follow its radial target segment from a sufficiently small nonzero point to the target. Join that small point to a positive real seed through a small arc about zero on the same side, followed by the real spine. This arc lies in a disk contained in the domain. For a=A+iβa=A+i\beta, ℑ(2arctan⁡a)=12log⁡A2+(1+β)2A2+(1−β)2,(4) \Im(2\arctan a) =\tfrac12\log\frac{A^2+(1+\beta)^2}{A^2+(1-\beta)^2}, \qquad\text{(4)} which has the sign of β\beta. Thus the angular path has the claimed side. A thin tube about a simple version of this path is simply connected and avoids zero and the angular endpoints.

On this tube the selected degree root is λt=q*+aq*−a,1−λt−2=4q*a(q*+a)2.(5) \lambda_t=\frac{q_*+a}{q_*-a},\qquad 1-\lambda_t^{-2}=\frac{4q_*a}{(q_*+a)^2}. \qquad\text{(5)} Every factor is a unit on the compact buffered product, because the first angle avoids zero. This proves the required uniform separation without imposing off-real modulus ordering. The same proof applies with the endpoints interchanged. ▫\square

First choose the buffered tube and construct its mode. Then shrink only the opposite-side collar to the width supplied by its positive repair exponent. This order is important: no fixed preselected collar is declared smaller than an exponent not yet constructed. The path itself stays on the designated side, and its real seed has a nonempty permitted collar. Compactness leaves an open neighborhood of each target in the resulting bounded domain.

In particular, if both target angles are nonzero, the two endpoint roles have a common designated product domain with two real physical seed intervals. Use the same domains (1), choose the two paths of Lemma 1 independently, and include each path in the other construction’s parameter domain. Their product is connected to the joint seed and has both normalized bounds. This verifies the hypotheses of the endpoint-role overlap theorem on compact subsets of that product. Merely having two separate continuation paths would not have supplied this common domain.

4. Original source and current scope

For a selected first window and any actual joint spectator PP, the closed window contour of (21) is exactly the finite residue expression ΦN,IX♯(w)=η∑Xj∈IX{(4−cj2)[Ĝj(w)P(cj)+Fj(w)PZ(cj)]−cjFj(w)P(cj)}.(26) \Phi^\sharp_{N,I_X}(w) =\eta\sum_{X_j\in I_X} \{(4-c_j^2)[\widehat G_j(w)P(c_j) +F_j(w)P_Z(c_j)]-c_jF_j(w)P(c_j)\}. \qquad\text{(26)} This follows from (22), with every other variable held fixed. The original window trace uses GjG_j instead. Their difference is PN(w)P_N(w) times a holomorphic slit function, and equality with the original follows after the required subsequent complete second trace, including all spectators and normal products. The second-window statement is identical with the roles reversed.

At a joint selected physical pair, (22) retains the value, both first normals and their mixed normal of the original kernel. Thus the double selected-window trace is exact. A local parameter domain need not contain the remaining second grid: a later full trace is taken on the global finite residue expression (26), or on a covering retaining all other rows. No arbitrary free-boundary polynomial has been replaced.

The four representatives have one common baseline. Their normalized differences are exponentially small where the relevant designated bounds hold; the original trace kernels and the entire spectator must still multiply those differences. The usual removable one-sided real interface and every oriented exterior half-side remain in each closed-window formula. An exponential local saving does not by itself pay a larger spectator or parent action.

The Cauchy baseline extends through both angular folds, but (22) matches only the chosen bulk rows. No new fold value or fold normal is asserted. Neither the baseline nor the exact repairs are asserted even in both angular variables, so no spectral descent at an unmatched fold or parity-based full-lens substitution is inferred. The original endpoint rows near ±π\pm\pi, all unmatched rows, and the full geometric cycle and coefficient remain separate.
