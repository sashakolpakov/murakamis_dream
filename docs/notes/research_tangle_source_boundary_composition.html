Composition of original matched-disk boundary data

31 October 2026. exact-source and analytical research theorem. Unpublished. This is an exact composition law for admissible disk modules built from WHOLE original matched ports. It concerns the actual positive CROSS source and its classical boundary response. It does not assert that every Conway/arborescent tangle admits this geometry, that arbitrary diagonal changes preserve a filled knot, or that signed quantum volume gaps follow from composing classical curves.

1. The compositional object retains weights, loads and the primitive

A module is a finite union of original matched triangle pairs, with all its boundary edges MAIN. Its coupons retain their ordered frames. Protected face weights are set to zero before every extraction. Let I be its ordinary vertices whose original targets are internal to the module, and B its remaining ordinary boundary vertices. For every ordinary graph component require a vertex in B. Suppose the module has one finite positive weight assignment with all supported ordinary incidences positive and every I load equal to one.

At the actual CROSS endpoint define, at either parity,

Pℳ,N(LB)=[∏i∈ILid]∏e∈ℳPe,CROSS(−2;L),d=N−1. P_{\mathcal M,N}(L_B) =\left[\prod_{i\in I}L_i^d\right] \prod_{e\in\mathcal M}P_{e,\mathrm{CROSS}}(-2;L), \qquad d=N-1.\tag{1}

Every actual coupon scalar and fixed raw sign is retained, or a single declared product of its fixed raw unit signs is removed consistently. The degree in all boundary weights is

deg⁡Pℳ,N=dκℳ,κℳ=|ports⁡(ℳ)|−|I|. \deg P_{\mathcal M,N}=d\kappa_{\mathcal M},\qquad \kappa_{\mathcal M}=|\operatorname{ports}(\mathcal M)|-|I|.\tag{2}

The object is this original coefficient module, not a separately closed tangle invariant with a guessed pivotal trace. No new cup/cap normalization is introduced along the cut.

Let z_b=log L_b and put

Fℳ(zB)=infwI{∑t∈ℳQt(L)−∑i∈Iwi}. F_{\mathcal M}(z_B) =\inf_{w_I}\left\{\sum_{t\in\mathcal M}Q_t(L)-\sum_{i\in I}w_i\right\}. \tag{3}

The recession proof of the algebraic boundary-pressure theorem applies verbatim: the known strict allocation makes every recession summand nonnegative; equality forces a constant on every supported triangle and hence zero after the boundary anchors. The grounded Hessian is positive definite at every finite positive weight. Thus the minimizer is unique and finite for every finite real z_B, and F_M is real analytic there. Define the actual lifted boundary loads and phases

qℳ,b=∂zbFℳ,Bℳ,b=exp⁡(2πiqℳ,b). q_{\mathcal M,b}=\partial_{z_b}F_{\mathcal M},\qquad B_{\mathcal M,b}=\exp(2\pi i q_{\mathcal M,b}).\tag{4}

They obey sum_B q_(M,b)=kappa_M by homogeneity. The phase tuple does not determine these integer lifts or the absolute value of F_M.

As before, introduce positive square roots T_t^2=ab+ac+bc for every nonmonomial triangle, retain pure-corner factors -1, and impose the interior equations prod_t (T_t+iL_i)/(T_t-iL_i)=1 with their original load-one lifts. The invertible interior Jacobian makes the selected boundary phases algebraic functions of the boundary weights. Their graph defines an algebraic boundary correspondence on the selected regular component, with differential

dFℳ=∑b∈Bqℳ,bdlog⁡Lb,∑bdlog⁡Bℳ,b∧dlog⁡Lb=0. dF_{\mathcal M}=\sum_{b\in B}q_{\mathcal M,b}\,d\log L_b, \qquad \sum_b d\log B_{\mathcal M,b}\wedge d\log L_b=0.\tag{5}

The latter follows from Hessian symmetry. The graph has dimension |B| in the 2|B|-dimensional boundary weight/phase torus; locally it is Lagrangian for that displayed logarithmic symplectic form. Common scaling is retained, with its known linear primitive kappa_M log(scale). This is source boundary data, not an asserted knot character variety.

At odd levels the actual stable positive determinants and the anchored ordinary phase graph give the same finite positive saddle as in the one-cap pressure theorem, now with several external z coordinates: the interior torus has one angular maximum, a positive grounded Hessian and an analytic critical point on a fixed small complex boundary neighborhood. Its normalized logarithm tends to F_M there. The determinant and original coefficient measures retain their polynomial N factors. This statement uses the actual positive source; it is not an assertion about a negative-axis or truncated signed sum.

2. Admissible disk gluing and exact finite coefficients

Take two such modules with disjoint port interiors. Identify a specified MAIN boundary arc of the first with a specified MAIN boundary arc of the second, reversing boundary orientation and respecting the recorded vertex labels and bipartite colors. Alternatively glue their entire boundary circles to form a sphere. Within each old module the map on vertices is injective. All old matching edges and ordered port disks remain internal and unchanged.

Identified vertices must agree in their protected/ordinary status. If a proposed new mark changes that status, first specialize the newly protected labels to zero in BOTH module definitions, then recheck their stated positivity and anchoring hypotheses. One cannot glue a positive ordinary boundary variable to a protected zero and silently keep the old positive pressure branch.

This constructs the usual disk or sphere cellulation. For a geometric simple-white application it must additionally have no loops, repeated edges or repeated vertices in a port. The last condition follows from the injectivity just stated; repeated edges from two old diagonals are an actual possible obstruction and must be checked. These are explicit combinatorial admissibility conditions, not automatic facts about arbitrary Conway compositions.

For an OPEN glued module impose an additional anchoring condition: EVERY resulting ordinary graph component must meet a retained ordinary boundary vertex. This does not follow merely because both child modules were anchored; gluing may consume their last anchors. If an unanchored component is instead a balanced closed component, treat its scalar coefficient factor separately and fix one common scale in that component for the classical minimization. A strict load-one solution requires its total triangle supply to equal its total ordinary demand; an unbalanced closed component has no such branch. For an eligible full sphere with connected ordinary graph, in particular with the original protected adjacent MAIN pair, one global scale is enough.

Let S consist of the identified ORDINARY boundary vertices that become internal and acquire their original demand one. Shared vertices that remain on the external boundary keep a single common boundary weight. Protected vertices retain zero weight and are never assigned a d extraction. Under the literal source identification,

Pℳ1∪ℳ2,N(LB)=[∏s∈SLsd]Pℳ1,N(LB1)Pℳ2,N(LB2). P_{\mathcal M_1\cup\mathcal M_2,N}(L_B) =\left[\prod_{s\in S}L_s^d\right] P_{\mathcal M_1,N}(L_{B_1})P_{\mathcal M_2,N}(L_{B_2}).\tag{6}

This is simply the coefficient of the product of the same original coupons: the two sets of old internal variables are disjoint, and they are absent from the other module. Coefficient extractions therefore commute. Shared unextracted variables are identified before the last extraction. There is no independent variable for a repeated face occurrence, no shifted residual target, and no missing normalization. The degree check is kappa_glued=kappa_1+kappa_2-|S|.

The right side of (6) is not claimed to preserve joint real stability under an arbitrary total-degree projection. It uses the precise individual original face extractions. If a different tangle operation requires a total degree or a linear combination of cut states, (6) does not silently replace that operation.

3. Classical pressures and algebraic boundary correspondences compose

The exact classical law corresponding to (6) is

Fglued(zB)=infzS{F1(zB1)+F2(zB2)−∑s∈Szs}. F_{\mathrm{glued}}(z_B) =\inf_{z_S}\left\{F_1(z_{B_1})+F_2(z_{B_2})-\sum_{s\in S}z_s\right\}. \tag{7}

Indeed expanding (3) on the right gives the joint infimum over ALL old interior weights and the new shared interior weights of the sum of the unchanged triangle potentials with each interior target log subtracted exactly once. This is exactly the definition of the glued pressure. Infimum associativity proves (7) even at a boundary dual limit; no exchange with a shared entropy maximum is used.

For a finite strict positive glued branch satisfying the resulting anchoring condition, the same coercivity and Hessian argument gives unique finite minimizers. Each balanced closed component is instead handled with its separate scalar factor and one scale fixed, as just specified. In an eligible full sphere with connected ordinary graph, in particular with the original protected adjacent MAIN pair, this is the single common scale. At each shared interior face the actual gluing condition is

q1,s+q2,s=1,B1,sB2,s=1. q_{1,s}+q_{2,s}=1,\qquad B_{1,s}B_{2,s}=1.\tag{8}

The SECOND equation alone loses the required integer. At a retained boundary face b the loads add over its actual old occurrences and the phases multiply:

qglued,b=∑i:b∈Biqi,b,Bglued,b=∏i:b∈BiBi,b. q_{\mathrm{glued},b}=\sum_{i:\,b\in B_i}q_{i,b},\qquad B_{\mathrm{glued},b}=\prod_{i:\,b\in B_i}B_{i,b}.\tag{9}

Thus the algebraic boundary correspondence composes by matching common weights, imposing the phase equations in (8), and eliminating the shared variables, while retaining the original load-one lift and the selected positive branch. Elimination without those choices may keep unrelated components or wrong integer loads.

The pressure primitive composes with the explicit target correction:

Fglued=F1+F2−∑s∈Slog⁡Ls. F_{\mathrm{glued}}=F_1+F_2-\sum_{s\in S}\log L_s.\tag{10}

evaluated at the shared critical weights. Differentiating (10), the shared-weight terms vanish by the FIRST equation in (8), leaving exactly the external differential in (5). Therefore the corresponding relative logarithmic periods add, with their original absolute base constants and the displayed target-log term. The eliminated curve alone does not retain those constants.

Finite coefficient associativity, joint infimum associativity and the lifted equations prove associativity of these laws for any finite admissible assembly of modules. This is a closure theorem for actual source boundary data, not just a relabeling of a list of knot families.

4. What this supplies for a tangle formulation

When a tangle decomposition is represented by such cuts of actual ordered matched-port disks, the original source coefficient and its positive boundary curve/period can be computed module by module using (6)-(10). If the resulting simple matched sphere carries the eligible original protected adjacent MAIN pair and the hypotheses of the original complete-packing theorem, that theorem identifies the full complete critical value with its original drilled hyperbolic volume. The inherited zero set and mark are part of this hypothesis, not consequences of simplicity alone. Nothing in the composition requires calling the whole diagram a bridge presentation.

To apply this to a proposed Conway/algebraic tangle operation one must still verify its literal MAIN-arc boundary identification, original coupon frames and face targets, and the simplicity/packing hypotheses of the resulting source. This note does not identify every algebraic knot with such an assembly or transport integer long fillings through a rotated diagonal. The signed non-CROSS/cap cancellation inequality, even-parity negative-axis adapter, and filled-period estimates remain their separate original-source questions.

Inputs

The original homogeneous source and matched-sphere Morse construction retain the literal ordered port disks, face variables and target extractions used in (1) and (6). The triangle entropy theorem fixes the local half-supply potentials in (3). The algebraic boundary-pressure theorem supplies the coercivity, regular algebraic branch and retained-load arguments generalized to several boundary variables here. The actual CROSS determinant factorization and positive face-coefficient saddle supply the odd-level positive pressure identification. The complete packing capacity supplies the drilled-volume identification only under the original marked packing hypotheses stated in section 4.

Every resulting ordinary component must satisfy the stated anchoring condition. Gluing also preserves protected versus ordinary status and the inherited marked pair.

No numerical audit is used or claimed. This theorem composes actual admissible source boundary data. It does not prove that every Conway tangle operation preserves the required source geometry, identify a knot A-polynomial, transport arbitrary fillings, or supply an unsigned or signed non-CROSS volume bound.
