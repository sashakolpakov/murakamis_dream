A source-exact full half-clock dilation of the physical head

28 October 2026. At complete color the original N-dimensional head is an invariant orthogonal window in an explicit 2N-clock representation. The weighted cyclic shifts have zeros at both window boundaries. The unrolled H, full half-Cartan, genuine dual, N-normalized turns, original pivot and selected cut all lift exactly. For a one-component knot, the selected cut forces the physical window around the entire component, so the full extended contraction is the original scalar.

This supplies a legitimate unitary 2N Fourier ambient space. It does not supply the global triangulation edge character required by the positive-angle majorant. The new ambient clock dilates the old window; its conserved physical charge is already present in the original unrolled degree equations.

1. Exact complete-color generators

Put q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, d=N-1, x=qd=-q-1, and c=q-q^-1. Use the original balanced basis w_a=v_a/(Q;Q)_a. The original head is

H w_a=(d-2a)w_a,       K w_a=x Q^-a w_a,
F w_a=(1-Q^(a+1))w_(a+1),
E w_a=(Q^-a-1)/c^2 w_(a-1),    0<=a<N,                      (1)

with the nonexistent endpoints set to zero. Indeed the original unbalanced E coefficient is [a]_q[N-a]_q=[a]_q^2 and [a]_q2/(1-Qa)=(Q-a-1)/c2. No factorial metric change is being made in this note: (1) is the already used balanced presentation.

On W=C^(2N), with orthonormal basis u_J, define

T u_J=q^J u_J,     X u_J=u_(J+1 mod2N),
F_W=X(1-Q T^2),   E_W=X^-1(T^-2-1)/c^2,
K_W=x T^-2,       H_W u_J=(d-2J)u_J.                          (2)

Here TX=qXT; Q is still q^2 of order N. The zeros of F_W occur at J=d,2N-1 and those of E_W at J=0,N. Hence

W=W_0 direct_sum W_1,
W_0=span(u_0,...,u_d),  W_1=span(u_N,...,u_(2N-1))            (3)

is an orthogonal decomposition into invariant unrolled modules. Under u_(N epsilon+a) <-> w_a, E,F,K are the original matrices, and H is shifted by -2N epsilon. In particular

W=V_d direct_sum V_(d-2N),
E_W^N=F_W^N=0.                                               (4)

The first-window inclusion i and p=i^* are isometric and satisfy p i=I. Any prescribed original head metric can instead be copied orthogonally to both summands; the inclusion remains isometric. Unitary ordinary DFT statements below use the balanced flat metric.

The unrolled quantum relations follow on each summand from (1). For example [H,E]=2E and [H,F]=-2F hold at the cyclic wraps because the corresponding weights are ZERO, not because H was made cyclic. Replacing H by a residue class would lose this justification.

2. The full Cartan and the genuine dual

Use the original finite universal crossing, in its original order,

Rcheck=Flip q^(H tensor H/2)
      sum_(n=0)^d q^[n(n-1)/2] c^n/[n]_q!
                                  E^n tensor F^n.            (5)

Formula (5), and its actual inverse, are well defined on W. Every denominator has n<N and is nonzero. Since each summand in (3) is invariant, a crossing transports its two summand labels with the physical strands. On the selected W_0 tensor W_0 its restriction is EXACTLY the original Rcheck, including the common Cartan scalar kappa=q(d2/2). No kappa was stripped or replaced by a 2N scalar.

The full Cartan is not the bare cyclic phase Q^(ab). On the original window it is

q^[(d-2a)(d-2b)/2]
    =kappa q^[-d(a+b)] Q^(ab).                               (6)

On W_epsilon tensor W_eta its ratio to the original Cartan is (-1)1. This is a unit phase; it follows by expanding the two unrolled H shifts before taking q powers. The restriction statement itself does not require dropping this phase elsewhere.

Use the GENUINE dual W^, with the original antipode and pivotal duality. It is V_d^ direct_sum V_(d-2N)^*. In the copied original dual basis u_(N epsilon+a)^vee its H weights are

H_Wvee=d+2N epsilon-2a.                                     (7)

In particular, its second dual summand is not assigned the naive weight d-2(N+a). Those two expressions differ by 4N, which is invisible to q^H but not to unrolled H as an operator. The true dual is what justifies the cups, caps and half-Cartan identities.

Write the original pivotal matrix as

g_a=x^(1-N) Q^-a.

The root parameter is still N: the pivot on W is K_W^(1-N), not K_W^(1-2N). Copy this exact matrix to both summands. In the original unrolled convention the twist at highest weight alpha is q2; its values at d and d-2N agree, since their ratio is q(2N2)=1. Thus the original zero-framing correction also lifts with its exact scalar. The original left turns, in the original factorial boundary frame, lift as

coev_L^W=sum_(epsilon=0,1) sum_(a=0)^d
          N x^-a u_(N epsilon+d-a) tensor u_(N epsilon+a)^vee,
ev_L^W(u_(N epsilon+a)^vee tensor u_(N eta+b))
          =delta_(epsilon,eta) x^a/N delta_(a+b,d).           (8)

The right turns have the SAME original pivot factors; for example

coev_R^W=sum_(epsilon,a) N x^-a g_(d-a)^-1
           u_(N epsilon+a)^vee tensor u_(N epsilon+d-a).    (9)

These are the direct-sum genuine turns, not new normalization choices. They use N and 1/N, not 2N and 1/(2N). Restricting every leg to the first summand gives the literal original turns. The ordinary cap is still not declared to be the Hermitian adjoint of the cup; this dilation does not change the pivotal trace’s positivity.

3. Exact original one-component source

Let P_0=i p be the first-window projector. If the original closed-braid source is the normalized averaged cut, retain it literally as

(1/N) Tr_(W cut)(P_0 C_W),                                  (10)

with every original closed pivot and zero-framing factor retained. If the original source is a specified cut matrix entry, retain that entry in W_0 instead. These choices agree for the uninserted scalar knot endomorphism; they are NOT interchanged for arbitrary inserted exteriors. In a plat retain its actual marked coupon g E_00 and its prescribed pivotal side, extended in W_0. No replacement by I_W/(2N) is made.

Source theorem. Replace every physical head by W, every dual by W^*, and use (5),(8),(9) and their actual inverse/right versions. For ANY one-component knot presentation, its original selected scalar is unchanged.

Proof. Expand the extended contraction by summand labels epsilon. Each crossing transports the two labels with their physical strands; each cup or cap matches the label on its two incident physical legs. Thus the label is constant along each physical link component. The original selected cut forces label0 on its component. Since there is only one component, every nonzero term lies in W_0 on every physical leg. Each local restriction is exactly the original coupon, including Cartan, pivot, boundary and framing constants. Therefore the entire sum equals the original scalar, not merely its modulus or its leading exponent. This proof also retains literal original insertions which preserve or are lifted inside the selected summand, provided their original cut functional is kept unchanged. It does not apply a scalar-knot trace conversion after inserting new coupons.

For a multi-component link, each component must carry its specified selected projector if this same statement is wanted. Leaving another component unselected permits its other summand and is a different source. Likewise arbitrary new offdiagonal summand-changing coupons are outside the theorem.

The construction is an exact COMPLETE-COLOR lift. At generic color the direct sum V_alpha direct_sum V_(alpha-2N) still gives an exact isometric module lift, but the simple E polynomial in (2) no longer gives its boundary values: endpoint projectors are then required. No unproved whole-meridian polynomial-weight extension is inferred from the complete-color q-number zeros. The original full modified dimension remains an external normalization line throughout.

4. What full-clock information actually follows

On W and its genuine dual the copied half-clock is T u_J=q^J u_J. Although their unrolled H lifts differ, both satisfy

T=q^(d/2) q^(-H/2).                                         (11)

The dual second-summand difference4N contributes q^(2N epsilon)=1. Unrolled total-H conservation therefore gives the EXACT identity

[Rcheck,T tensor T]=0.                                      (12)

On every full turn, J+Jvee=2N epsilon+d, so

(T tensor Tvee)coev=q^d coev.                               (13)

The corresponding cap has the same affine charge. Consequently a full q-character average is legitimate at these PHYSICAL vertices. The charge in (12) is not only modulo N: on the original window, the conserved unwrapped total lies in[0,2d], whose width is <2N, so its modulo2N equation is equivalent to exact integer equality.

However, (12)–(13) already hold on the original N-window. They are its existing integer flow and inverse-meridian pairing laws, now realized in a full-clock ambient space. The quantum factorial still has base Q=q^2 and length N; the nilpotency order in (4) is still N. Nothing has changed it to a 2N-dimensional cyclic dilogarithm.

There is an explicit limitation for the OLD unweighted N-cyclic shift used in cyclic-atom factorizations. Its exact blockwise lift is not X. Put

P_wall=|d><d|+|2N-1><2N-1|,
S_hat=X(I-P_wall)+X^(1-N)P_wall.                             (14a)

Then S_hat is the direct sum of the two original N-cyclic shifts, so S_hat^N=I. Directly on every basis vector,

T^2 S_hat=Q S_hat T^2,
T S_hat-q S_hat T=-2q X^(1-N)P_wall T.                        (14b)

The right side is a nonzero rank-two wall current, of norm2 in the flat metric. The two weights in (2) kill their respective endpoint terms, which is why the complete original R lifts cleanly. An old cyclic-atom factorization, however, must use S_hat and retain (14b) if its old Q-Weyl relation is refined to a q relation. Replacing its unweighted S_N by X without this correction changes its physical restriction. Thus the dilation does not automatically upgrade the old cyclic shape algebra to a free full-q Weyl algebra. The small rank alone supplies no bound after arbitrary factorial conjugations.

Let F_(2N) be the unitary 2N discrete Fourier transform. The physical window becomes the rank-N projector

(F_(2N) P_0 F_(2N)^*)_(rs)
       =(1/(2N)) sum_(a=0)^d q^[a(r-s)].                     (14)

Its diagonal is1/2. For r!=s its entry is zero if r-s is even and 1/[N(1-q^(r-s))] if r-s is odd. Thus the selected Fourier source is not an independent uniform full-clock source: it retains this exact projector, or its propagated equivalent under the full contraction. Plancherel preserves it rather than deleting it.

To apply the positive affine-angle theorem one must STILL construct the actual source map to tetrahedral nonnegative angles with sum d, identify the geometric edge-incidence rows, and prove the full edge character Ek=2d modulo2N with subexponential comparison norm. Physical head conservation (12) is not that global geometric edge equation. Joint noncommuting quantum shapes are not automatically positive angle labels either. This dilation resolves the cyclic-wrap issue for the physical head; it does not close those geometric/source gates.
