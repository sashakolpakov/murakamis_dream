Residual Gaussian Ward currents and the cyclotomic source resonance

Research proof and audit, 27 October 2026. At COMPLETE color, the literal local currents and explicitly computed cut boundary rows give an all-order transverse Gaussian Ward ideal on the geometric framing torsor. It is a flat rank-one ideal, with the actual Gaussian amplitude as its nonzero horizontal generator. The original cut co-source has precisely the leading covariance required by the uniform Gaussian density. These formal statements do not identify the global finite framing contraction. An explicit cyclotomic resonance obstructs a naive extension to that source; keeping the whole power normal gives a separate exact divided-current/source comparison.

1. An explicit operator on the actual transverse block

Use the actual independent flow coordinates of the partial support theorem. Keep two coordinates chi=(chi_1,chi_2) external, and contract the other r=m-1 logarithmic coordinates u by the nondegenerate geometric Gaussian. Write H=Log Q and

I=C_beta(H) exp(S/H) a(chi,u,H),
Z(chi,H)=G_chi(I).

All original characters, half-steps, pivots and base-only factors are kept. The normalized moment of a regular insertion is

E_chi[h]=G_chi(h I)/Z(chi,H).                  (1)

The denominator is a nonzero formal Gaussian block, not the selected finite knot scalar. After its recorded common exponential and base line are factored out, it is a formal unit. Thus every coefficient of (1) is recursively computable by Wick contraction and finite formal division, and is algebraic on the stated geometric/half-density cover.

Compose the literal local currents in the crossing-current note, keeping their multiplication insertions. Use the same unimodular integral closure solver as for the actual flow coordinates. Every resulting interior term has the explicit form

h_(gamma,beta)(chi,u,H) T_chi^gamma S_u^beta I,

where the shifts translate logarithmic coordinates by H times their displayed integer vectors. Transverse translation gives its exact Gaussian image

E_(chi+H gamma)[S_u^(-beta)h_(gamma,beta)(chi,u,H)]
                                  T_chi^gamma Z.           (2)

The proof is the literal substitution y=u+H beta, with the critical point recentered at the unique corresponding branch. The insertion is shifted in the opposite direction; it may not be replaced by its unshifted value. External base substitution commutes with the finite Wick recursion. Summing (2) defines an explicit all-order residual difference operator for each composed current. It annihilates Z whenever the corresponding interior current identity is zero. Finite endpoint indicators remain their separate source terms; they have not been given a fictitious value in this interior completion.

For the two full-tensor Hopf-adjoint combinations

2(ad_K-I)+2 ad_Ecal,       ad_F+ad_Ecal,

the already proved moment-map calculation gives the two cut-Borel principal fields A(h_sl+2f_sl) and A(e_sl+f_sl). Formula (2) transports each term of their interior current equations. The marked e,D coupon is not Hopf invariant, so its cut boundary insertions must also be written and transported. The next section does this explicitly at complete color. The generic-M endpoint terms are retained and are not covered by the homogeneous complete-color assertion.

2. Gaussian covariance supplies genuine quantum corrections

The map (1) is linear, not multiplicative. This matters even in the coherent all-level interior model. If H_perp=S_uu is the critical Hessian, direct quadratic Wick contraction gives

E[fg]-E[f]E[g]
  =-H (partial_u f)^t H_perp^(-1)(partial_u g)+O(H^2),     (3)

for H-independent regular insertions, evaluated at the critical point. The sign comes from covariance -H H_perp^(-1) for the phase convention exp(S/H). Cubic-phase and amplitude-drift terms cancel from this covariance at first order.

Consequently replacing each coefficient of a product or bracket of Ward currents separately by its scalar expectation is incorrect. Formula (2), applied to the whole ordered term with all insertions, retains these corrections. This is different from the impossible product-preserving fixed-root matrix-to-scalar map.

3. The actual complete cut co-source matches the principal density

The missing cut terms can be calculated directly, without postulating a Haar form. For Phi(C)=Tr(e C), cyclicity gives the exact dual Hopf rows

e_K=K^(-1)eK-e,
e_E=K^(-1)eEcal-Ecal K^(-1)e,
e_F=eF-KFeK^(-1).

Thus Phi((ad_K-I)C)=Tr(e_K C) and similarly for the other two generators. The actual one-cut operator has diagonal support term by term, before the remaining internal sums, by total-index conservation. Only the diagonals of these rows contribute.

Use A=Q^a, k_a=xQ^(-a), epsilon_a=M Q^(-a)-M^(-1) for 1<=a<N, and actual endpoints epsilon_0=epsilon_N=0. Direct multiplication gives

(e_K)_aa=0,
(e_F)_aa=(1-Q)A/N,
(e_E)_aa=(Q-1)A/(N M x)
      +kappa(1_(a=N-1)-1_(a=0))/(N x).        (3a)

For example the E diagonal is [epsilon_a/k_a-epsilon_(a+1)/k_(a+1)]/N; the displayed two endpoint corrections are exactly what replacing the actual truncated edges by their interior formula would miss.

At complete color M=1,x=-q^(-1), these simplify exactly to

(e_E)_aa=q(1-Q)A/N,
(e_F)_aa=(1-Q)A/N.                           (3b)

If the original scalar summand already includes its 1/N, the boundary multiplication coefficients of the two displayed cut-Borel combinations are therefore

b_1=2q(1-Q)A,
b_2=(1+q)(1-Q)A.

Both have b_i/H -> -2A as H=Log Q tends to zero. These are literal co-source contributions, not guessed scalar connection terms. Choosing the external pair to contain the actual cut index is always possible: its differential is nonzero on the framing torsor, and one more actual coordinate completes it to an etale pair.

The matching geometric divergence is equally explicit. At M=1 right multiplication of F(r,s)=[r,1-r;s,-s] by a row-stochastic frame change has fields

X_a=r partial_r+s partial_s,
X_b=(1-r)partial_r-s partial_s.

The conjugation convention delta G=[xi,G] gives

xi_1=A(h_sl+2f_sl)=-2X_b,
xi_2=A(e_sl+f_sl)=X_a-X_b.

Explicitly both traceless matrices have eigenvalue one on (1,1). Subtracting I gives the row-sum-zero matrices with parameters (a,b)=(0,2) and (-1,1), respectively, and normalized frame variation is delta F=-F(xi-I). This checks the projective normalization and the signs in the two fields.

For the actual cut density

Omega_cut=-dr wedge ds/[s(1+s)], A_cut=1/(1+s),

direct differentiation gives

div_(Omega_cut)(xi_1)=div_(Omega_cut)(xi_2)=2 A_cut.

Consequently the actual co-source terms satisfy the precise leading covariance identity

Lie_(xi_i) Omega_cut+(b_i/H)|_(H=0) Omega_cut=0.           (3c)

The uniform Gaussian density is a nonzero base-only multiple of this form, so it obeys the same identity. This proves the principal source covariance with the original cut. It does not yet prove that all finite normal/endpoint insertions have the required all-order image.

4. The complete residual current ideal is a flat line

The actual graph current and its first-order field

Use the local current identities with the input/output operator order fixed in the crossing-current note. Pair adjacent edge insertions along the braid. Every reindexing is retained as its finite difference current. The elementary product rule is

j T^v c-(T^(-v)j)c=(T^v-I)[(T^(-v)j)c].

It fixes the sign and placement of each resulting boundary multiplier. The actual pivotal caps cancel the matched Hopf-adjoint insertions; their remaining boundary is exactly the dual cut row computed in Section 3. The determinant-one flow solver expresses all translations in the independent actual flow coordinates. This gives scalar divergence identities with the boundary coefficients b_i included. A translation by an integer vector can be written as a sum of coordinate differences by telescoping its steps, so no nonintegral current is introduced.

At COMPLETE color these are homogeneous equations on the original scalar array after the explicit boundary term is brought to the same side. Each multiplier is N diag(e_h), since the scalar summand already contains 1/N. At generic M, equation (3a) instead leaves the two specified kappa-endpoint currents. They must not be omitted by continuity from the complete value.

Here is a direct principal-field proof, beyond just identifying the full End-space Hopf symbols. For a local observable

h(q,T)=sum_v h_v(q,H) T^v,

acting on a generating kernel exp(S/H)a, its first amplitude-derivative coefficient is

sum_v h_v(q,0) exp(v dot p) v=partial_p h_cl.

This follows by first-order Taylor expansion of each shifted amplitude. The right kernel action uses the opposite input translation; therefore its commutator gives the position velocities of the corresponding Hamiltonian field on the canonical graph. The zero-order first correction is carried by the current’s amplitude and boundary terms; it is not used to determine these vector coefficients.

At a crossing with actual update q_out=P q_in+R k, the transfer velocity is uniquely obtained from that crossing’s two output velocities. For example, in the positive update (a,b)->(b+k,a-k) it is simultaneously k_dot=c_dot-b_dot=a_dot-d_dot; equality follows from conservation of the local total position. The negative update gives the same statement with the prescribed signs. Determine this velocity crossing by crossing, then compose. This avoids trying to solve an underdetermined whole-word position equation.

Restrict to the complete critical germ. The exact critical-scheme correspondence identifies this propagated Hamiltonian variation with infinitesimal conjugation of the framed representation. For the two compensated cut-stabilizer combinations it is exactly xi_1,xi_2 above. The ad_K part alone can have zero position projection; no independent field is inferred from it alone. The compensated fields are tangent to the full critical framing surface. Since the retained actual coordinates are etale there, their two projected velocities are independent.

Transverse Gaussian pushforward evaluates the leading velocity coefficients at the critical point and kills the transverse total currents. Formula (2), together with the explicit complete boundary weights, therefore constructs two homogeneous residual operators with these independent first-order vector fields. Their scalar leading term agrees with (3c). All higher coefficients are given by the same actual normalized moments, with the boundary terms still included. No separate scalar Ward character was assumed.

Exact operator-ideal reduction

Here is the exact operator-algebra reduction, without assuming multiplicativity of (1). Factor the framing-independent action and base line from Z, leaving an algebraic formal amplitude

z(chi,H) in K_geo[[H]]^times.

The full critical action has zero derivatives along the framing torsor, so it cancels from external framing shifts. Let Wbar_1,2 be the complete-color residual operators just constructed, normalized by their common H. Expand their external shifts as formal differential operators; each H coefficient has finite differential order. Conjugate by the computed actual amplitude:

P_i=z^(-1) Wbar_i z.

Then P_i(1)=0. Right-normal ordering in commuting etale framing derivations writes, exactly,

P_i=sum_(a=1)^2 A_ia(H,partial_chi) partial_(chi_a),
A_ia(0)=xi_i^a.

The leading 2 by 2 matrix is invertible on the framing chart. Its inverse in the H-adically completed matrix algebra of differential operators is the convergent Neumann series. Hence

partial_(chi_a)=sum_i (A^(-1))_ai P_i.

Conversely every P_i is a left differential-operator combination of these derivatives. Thus the residual current left ideal is precisely the annihilator ideal of the constant line after this gauge. Ungauged it is the flat connection

nabla_a=partial_(chi_a)-partial_(chi_a)Log z.                (4)

Its flatness follows by direct commutation of logarithmic derivatives. This identifies the resulting complete-color formal Ward ideal and its cyclic rank-one module. Every other residual current annihilates z and hence belongs to this same annihilator ideal. It does not assert that separately expectation-substituted generators obey an undeformed Borel bracket, or that their full relation complexes have already been identified with the finite-source Ward complex.

The uniform principal-density theorem gives Omega_perp^2=-(T_beta/2)Omega_cut^2. With the recorded square-root mark, the leading framing density is a base-only nonzero multiple of Omega_cut. Consequently (4) has that canonical leading trivialization. Its higher gauge coefficients are the actual Gaussian coefficients, rather than freely chosen transport data.

5. Why this interior comparison does not extend coefficientwise to the source

Even one frame direction detects the missing completion. For the Euler derivation D=A partial_A, ordinary formal shift-to-current comparison uses

exp(H D)-1=H D B(H D),
B(v)=(exp(v)-1)/v, B(0)=1.                    (5)

On the local stationary formal completion, the corresponding operator has positive stationary degree, so the Bernoulli adapter B is a unit. On a cyclotomic carry sector A^N, with H=2pi*i/N, it instead has

B(H D) A^N=B(2pi*i) A^N=0.                  (6)

The ordinary Euler current there is nonzero. These sectors are not regular coefficients in the same WKB line: A^N is an extra exponential character when N=2pi*i/H. Thus the local formal current isomorphism cannot be extended by the same invertible adapter while preserving the literal finite carry source.

Deleting A=1 by the proved relative-Ward homotopy does not remove this resonance. The relation A^N=U and its whole normal remain on the retained source. Nor does the normalized moment (1) control spectral degree near N, endpoint currents, or exponentially small local functions with a different global cut pairing.

6. The divided Frobenius current gives the exact normal repair

The necessary extra direction can be computed, rather than postulated. Retain

C_U=k[U^+/-1,A]/(A^N-U).

Unfold the shift coherently by

sigma_Q(A)=Q A, sigma_Q(U)=Q^N U.

It preserves the relation. At Q=zeta its N-th power is identity. Before specializing put delta=1-Q^N. Then

sigma_Q^N(A)=(1-delta)A,
sigma_Q^N(U)=(1-delta)^N U.

Therefore on every Laurent polynomial, and hence every regular whole normal germ, the exact divided normal is

[(sigma_Q^N-I)/(1-Q^N)]_(Q=zeta)=-D_phys,
D_phys=A partial_A+N U partial_U.             (7)

The derivation preserves A^N-U. It does not descend to the fiber U=1: D_phys(U-1)=N U is nonzero there. This proves precisely which normal datum a fixed-fiber framing derivative would discard.

Let tau_N=Tr_(C_U/k[U^+/-1])/N, the original averaged cut on this whole finite algebra. In the canonical basis 1,A,...,A^(N-1), tau_N(f)=f_0(U), so direct coefficient differentiation gives

tau_N D_phys=N U partial_U tau_N.             (8)

This is an actual source-current chain map with all carries retained. It also extends coefficientwise through the polynomial relative source resolution and its endpoint transgression.

More explicitly mark alpha(U)^N=U, alpha(1)=1, and use the evaluation branches A=alpha(U)zeta^a. In this basis D_phys is N U partial_U. On matrix families it gives the induced connection on endomorphisms. The coefficientwise extension of the original head projector e is constant. The whole-normal pivot has two distinct marked lifts:

K_U=x A^(-1),
g_red=x^(1-N)A^(-1), g_lit=K_U^(1-N)=U g_red,
D_red=g_red^(-1)e, D_lit=U^(-1)D_red.

Only g_lit is the literal clock power away from U=1. Their physical normal rates are

D_phys(g_red)=-g_red, D_phys(D_red)=D_red,
D_phys(g_lit)=(N-1)g_lit,
D_phys(D_lit)=-(N-1)D_lit.

Both products are exactly e, so their paired cut has the same normal covariance. Their separate pivot/source jets differ by the retained U adapter and must not be identified from the common root value.

Thus for the original marked matrix cut,

Phi_U(D_phys C)=N U partial_U Phi_U(C),
Phi_U(C)=Tr(g_red D_red C)
         =Tr(g_lit D_lit C)=Tr(e C).         (9)

No Haar division or normalization replacement is used. This e is the coefficientwise whole-normal projector, not the finite-rank Verma coupon Gamma=i e pi_0. Under the explicit Mellin comparison, those act respectively as tau_N(f)1 and [tau_N(f)](U=1)1. The latter has the additional normal row [nabla,Gamma_U]f=-[tau_N(nabla f)](U=1)1, nabla=D_phys/N. Equation (9) does not discard that finite-vacuum cut derivative.

The boundary split Ward complex can be carried along this normal connection as well. On degree zero use the displayed derivative; on its image degree one define nabla^1(delta_W C)=delta_W(D_phys C). It is well defined because ker delta_W is the scalar line, which the derivative preserves. The explicit splitting makes this a strict normal current map on the relative-Ward boundary complex. Its degree-one action is not an uncorrected entrywise derivative of the varying K,F Ward rows.

7. Exact scope of the new comparison step

Equations (1)–(2), the explicit complete dual rows, and the principal-field proof construct the complete-color all-order residual Ward ideal. Equation (3c) identifies the original cut’s leading covariance with the Gaussian framing density. Equations (7)–(9) independently identify the divided Frobenius normal current with the literal whole cut source, including g,D and endpoint transgression. These are proved pieces of the needed comparison.

They are not yet a map between those two completed modules. The interior connection is over a single geometric WKB sector; the whole source carries the resonant exponential characters and normal modes exposed by (6)–(7). A source-compatible extension must transport these sectors, not replace the adapter in (5) by a unit outside its domain. The explicit marked transgression therefore does not yet prove that this residual geometric class has nonzero image under the selected finite cut. No nonzero selected global cycle, Stokes coefficient, or volume limit follows.

Exact source audit and results check the actual complete/generic-M boundary rows, both geometric divergences and field independence, and whole-normal Euler/carry identities. The all-order current/ideal argument above is a proof, not inferred from these finite diagnostics.
