A coherent two-twist/turn source packet with a sharp norm rate

29 October 2026. Set A=T_2 P_rev T_2 in the COMPLETE original Newton metric. The whole source vector A1 has an exact second-order Ward recurrence, an exact reflected boundary equation with an inhomogeneous current, and a sharp all-level norm exponent. The proof uses both actual source endpoints and never assumes that distinct spectral roots alone prevent conversion. It does not identify the reflected circuit with a hyperbolic link or prove the rate for arbitrary longer reduced words.

1. Exact original coefficient and Ward equation

Use the original N,d,q,Q,kappa,F_i,s_i of the Newton metric. Define

W_i=F_i^2 sum_(j=0)^i
       (-1)^j Q^[(3j^2+j)/2]/(F_j^3 F_(i-j)),
H_i=F_i W_i,                  0<=i<=d.

The all-entry T_2 formula and F_j F_(d-j)=N nu_j give the exact SOURCE vector, with no phase or endpoint discarded,

(A1)_i=kappa^4 N(-q)^(i+d) W_i.                       (1)

Indeed T_2 1 has entries kappa2(-q)j F_j. Apply the genuine reversal and then T_2; its inner j coefficient is nu_j^3 Q^(-j), exactly (-1)^j Q1.

The whole-index recurrence is

(1-Q^i)W_i=(1+Q-3Q^i)W_(i-1)
                      -Q(1-Q^(i-1))^2 W_(i-2),
H_i=(1+Q-3Q^i)H_(i-1)
                      -Q(1-Q^(i-1))^3 H_(i-2),       (2)
H_0=1, H_1=1-2Q.

At i=1 the second coefficient is ZERO; no H_(-1) is needed. These equations hold for every physical index i<=d and all N>=2.

For an own derivation, put a_j=(-1)jQ[(3j^2+j)/2]/F_j^3, G_i=sum_j a_j/F_(i-j), and use formal generating series modulo z^N. The shift T:f(z)->f(Qz) gives

(1-T)^3 a(z)=-Q^2 z T^3 a(z),
E(Qz)=(1-z)E(z), E(z)=sum z^k/F_k, G=aE.

Consequently

(1-z)(1-Qz)G(z)-3(1-Qz)G(Qz)
                                  +3G(Q^2z)-G(Q^3z)=0.

Coefficient extraction gives (1-Qi)3G_i=(1+Q-3Q^i)G_(i-1)-QG_(i-2), hence (2). This argument can first be made at generic Q and then specialized coefficientwise below degree N. It never divides a zero root factorial. The physical source identification (1) uses the root complementation identity. A generic-Q continuation of this displayed scalar recurrence is not thereby identified with the actual generic-q tangle/source germ. All estimates here use its exact physical root values.

2. The second source endpoint is exactly the known Gaussian

Let P_d be the ORIGINAL normalization endpoint, with its already proved identities and all-parity Gaussian asymptotic:

P_d=N^2 sum_(j=0)^d Q^j/F_j^2
   =sum_(j=0)^d F_j
   =N^(3/2) exp[i pi(1/4-N/12+1/(4N))]+O(sqrt N).

See the endpoint proof. In particular cN(3/2)<=|P_d|<=CN(3/2) for all large N. Root factorial complementation in the actual sum gives

W_d=N sum_j Q^(j^2)/F_j^2
   =(Q/N)sum_j Q^j F_j^2=conjugate(P_d)/N,
H_d=conjugate(P_d).                                  (3)

The penultimate coefficient is also fixed, not a free backward datum:

(conjugate(F_1))^3 H_(d-1)
                     =(1-2/Q)H_d+Q^(-1).             (4)

For completeness set C(t)=sum_j F_j2tj. Its exact finite Ward is

(1-t)C(t)+2Qt C(Qt)-Q^2t C(Q^2t)=1.

At t=1 this evaluates C(Q^2) from C(Q); substituting the complemented finite expression for H_(d-1) gives (4).

Define J_0=0,J_1=1 and evolve J by (2) only for i>=2. Direct reversal of (2), with g_k=conjugate(F_k)^3 H_(d-k), now proves

g_k=H_d conjugate(H_k)+Q^(-1)conjugate(J_k), 0<=k<=d.  (5)

The corresponding PHASED source coefficient, useful before any future color-contour deformation, is exactly

(A1)_(d-k)=kappa^4 Q^(-1) q^(k^2)/s_k^2
             [H_d conjugate(H_k)+Q^(-1)conjugate(J_k)]. (5a)

Indeed W_(d-k)=nu_k g_k/(N s_k^2); the actual factor (-q)(2d-k)nu_k=Q(-1)q(k2) gives (5a).

The constant term Q^(-1) in (4)–(5) is a retained original boundary current. It is not set to zero. The independent J solution does not satisfy the resetting i=1 source equation, which is why that current cannot be absorbed into a second homogeneous source at the endpoint.

3. A regular gapped half-circle and a uniform envelope

Put u=pi i/N and Y=exp(2iu). The classical monic transfer has roots

mu_±(u)=[2-3Y ± Y sqrt(4Y-3)]/2.                     (6)

Choose the square root continuously from +1 at u=0. On 0<=u<=pi/2, the minus root mu is nonzero and dominant until the terminal equality of moduli; write rho(u)=|mu(u)|. The eigenvalue difference has modulus |sqrt(4Y-3)|>=1, so its eigenframe and first derivatives are uniformly bounded on this entire closed half-circle.

Here is a direct modulus check. For a=2-3Y, b=(1-Y)^3 and 0<u<=pi/2,

a^2/b=(9+16sin^2u)/(8sin^2u)
         -i cos(u)(1-16sin^2u)/(8sin^3u).

Two roots of lambda^2-a lambda+b have equal modulus exactly when a^2/b belongs to [0,4]. The imaginary part vanishes only at sin u=1/4 or cos u=0. The first gives 20, outside that interval; the second gives25/8. Thus the only modulus equality is u=pi/2. At u=0 the roots are -1,0 and the source selects -1.

Let I(u)=pi(-1)integral_0u log rho(v)dv. Uniformly for all physical i with 0<=i<=d/2, the exact transfer matrix differs by O(1/N) from its classical matrix. In the bounded moving eigenframe, each normalized step has norm at most rho(u_i)(1+C/N). Hence

|H_i|+|J_i| <= C exp[N I(pi i/N)].                    (7)

The constants do not deteriorate at the terminal modulus equality: the eigenVALUES remain separated. The sum-to-integral error is O(1).

On every smaller interval 0<=u<=u_0<pi/2 the source is sharper:

log|H_i|=N I(pi i/N)+O_(u0)(1),
|J_i/H_i|<=C_(u0).                                  (8)

To justify the nonzero statement, write h_i=H_i/H_(i-1). The exact Riccati map is h_i=a_i-b_i/h_(i-1). Its derivative at the dominant classical root is mu_other/mu, of modulus uniformly less than one. An O(1/N) disk around the smooth nonzero dominant root is therefore invariant under every step, by induction. The literal reset H_1=1-2Q starts inside that disk. Thus every h_i is nonzero and h_i=mu(u_i)+O(1/N). Summing logarithms proves (8)’s first assertion. For the second, the difference of successive J_i/H_i has consecutive ratio b_i/(h_i h_(i-1)), uniformly less than one; its first difference is1/H_1. This proves the uniform bound. No claim of adiabatic survival is made across the modulus crossing.

4. Sharp whole-source norm from the two actual endpoint solutions

For the first half, (7) and the uniform sine-factorial estimate give

log|W_i| <= N[I(u)+Lambda(u)/pi]+O(log N).

For the second half put k=d-i, u=pi k/N. Equations (3),(5), and s_i s_k=N give

|W_i|=|g_k|/(N s_k^2),
log|W_i| <= N[I(u)+2Lambda(u)/pi]+O(log N).            (9)

These bounds cover EVERY index, including the middle and endpoints. Since Lambda(u)>=0 on [0,pi/2], the backward profile dominates the forward one. Write B(u)=I(u)+2Lambda(u)/pi. For each fixed u in(0,pi/2), (3),(5),(8) also give the matching lower rate in(9): the J term is relatively O(N^(-3/2)) and cannot cancel the main term.

There is a unique maximizer u_* of B. Indeed

B'(u)=pi^(-1)log[rho(u)/(2sin u)^2].

For 2sin u<1, rho>=|b|^(1/2)=(2sin u)^(3/2)>(2sin u)^2. For u>=pi/6, an equality rho=(2sin u)^2 can occur only when

z^3-4z^2+3z-1=0, z=4sin^2u.                        (10)

One can verify this without choosing a root phase: impose |mu|2=z2 in mu^2-a mu+b=0 and its conjugate. The resulting equality is z(1-z)2=1-2z+2z2, exactly (10); here |conjugate(b)a-conjugate(a)z2|2=z3(1-2z+2z2)>0. The polynomial has a unique root z_* in[1,4], in fact in(3,4). At pi/6 the ratio rho/(2sin u)^2 is greater than one, whereas at pi/2 it is1/sqrt2<1. Continuity and uniqueness therefore prove the claimed strict maximum, with u_=arcsin(sqrt(z_)/2).

Using (1), the polynomial number of positive norm terms and a rounded index near the backward maximizer now prove the ALL-LEVEL estimate

log||T_2 P_rev T_2 1||_Newt^2 = N L+O(log N),
L=(2/pi)[integral_0^(u_*)log rho(v)dv+2Lambda(u_*)].   (11)

Numerically L is approximately .9060724478038353. This number is only a calibration; formula (11) is the proved constant. It comes from the fully coherent source vector before taking its positive norm.

5. Scope and reuse

The actual adjoint-word closure of A^dagger A is the original numerator source ||A1||^2, with the unrestricted marked cap and kappa retained. Thus (11) is a new coherent estimate for an ENTIRE specified original source circuit, not an expanded positive path. Its link topology, hyperbolicity and regulator identification are separate questions; no volume equality is asserted from its numerical rate.

This proof offers a checkable recurrence mechanism beyond distinct eigenvalues: an exact resetting source endpoint, a regular half-domain modulus gap, an independently evaluated nonzero opposite endpoint, and a reflection identity retaining the inhomogeneous current. Those inputs allow the two gapped solutions to be estimated separately. There is no unsupported propagation through a general modulus crossing. The modular reduction removes arbitrarily many redundant turns; genuinely longer reduced mixed words still require their own coherent packet/current estimate.

The finite profiles supplement that proof; they are not its justification.
