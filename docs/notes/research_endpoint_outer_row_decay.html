Decay of the actual omitted endpoint rows

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The omitted high Newton band has an additional physical-row penalty, beyond its previously proved polynomial norm. This estimate applies before any remaining coupled trace is bounded. It concentrates the uncontrolled weights near the angular endpoint on the square-root level scale. A final proposition explains why the local low/high cancellation exponent cannot generally be improved to half the imaginary height for the same truncated kernel.

2. A uniform physical-row penalty

Theorem 1. For every fixed A<πA<\pi, there is CAC_A such that, at both parities, for |α|≤A|\alpha|\le A, every 0≤L≤m−10\le L\le m-1, and every row in (4), |ωa,N,L(α)|≤CAmin⁡{N3/2,N4[sin⁡2(τa/2)]L+1}.(9) |\omega_{a,N,L}(\alpha)| \le C_A\min\left\{N^{3/2}, N^4\bigl[\sin^2(\tau_a/2)\bigr]^{L+1}\right\}. \qquad\text{(9)} The second bound in fact holds on every bounded complex α\alpha-set, without A<πA<\pi. The stricter parameter condition will be used for shared full-kernel values in Section 3.

Proof. Fix a physical row and write z=sin⁡2(τa/2)z=\sin^2(\tau_a/2). For L=⌊δN⌋L=\lfloor\delta N\rfloor, 0<δ<1/20<\delta<1/2, the pointwise exponential penalty in (9), after polynomial factors, is −δlog⁡(sin⁡2(τ/2)).(14) -\delta\log\bigl(\sin^2(\tau/2)\bigr). \qquad\text{(14)} It is strictly positive on every fixed physical angular compact away from π\pi. 3. Shared coupons and all surviving traces

The product of two OiO_i’s has zero values and first normals. Thus the exact full-normal product rule is ϕN[(∏i=1rBi−∏i=1rPi)f]=∑a≤r*Ωaf(ca),Ωa=∑i=1rωa,N,L(αi)∏j≠iBj(ca).(17) \phi_N\!\left[\left(\prod_{i=1}^r B_i-\prod_{i=1}^r P_i\right)f\right] =\sum_{a\le r_*}\Omega_a f(c_a),\qquad \Omega_a=\sum_{i=1}^r\omega_{a,N,L}(\alpha_i) \prod_{j\ne i}B_j(c_a). \qquad\text{(17)} No Bj′B_j' or f′f' term is missing: in every surviving product-rule term the single normal has already fallen on the zero-valued factor OiO_i. 4. A sharp restriction on the partial-kernel cancellation rate

The exact forcing of the adopted low/high cancellation equation also gives a limit on a proposed stronger bound. This concerns the same partial kernel (3), not a synthetic solution of its equation.

Fix δ∈(0,1/2)\delta\in(0,1/2), set aδ=sin⁡2(πδ)a_\delta=\sin^2(\pi\delta), and suppose H>0H>0 satisfies b:=sinh⁡2(H/2),b+2aδ<1.(21) b:=\sinh^2(H/2),\qquad b+2a_\delta<1. \qquad\text{(21)} This is exactly the condition that −iH-iH belong to the general-domain contraction set. At α=0\alpha=0, let EN(t)=PN,⌊δN⌋(0,−2cos⁡t)QN(−2cos⁡t), E_N(t)=\frac{P_{N,\lfloor\delta N\rfloor}(0,-2\cos t)} {Q_N(-2\cos t)}, where QNQ_N is the monic distinct-node polynomial, and put Iδ(H)=−∫0δlog⁡b+sin⁡2(πx)cos⁡2(πx)dx>0.(22) I_\delta(H)= -\int_0^\delta \log\frac{b+\sin^2(\pi x)}{\cos^2(\pi x)}\,dx>0. \qquad\text{(22)}

Proposition 2. There is a constant c>0c>0 such that, for all sufficiently large NN at either parity, maxj=−1,0,1|EN(−iH+2jπ/N)|≥cN−2e−NIδ(H).(23) \max_{j=-1,0,1}|E_N(-iH+2j\pi/N)| \ge cN^{-2}e^{-NI_\delta(H)}. \qquad\text{(23)} If also H≥log⁡3H\ge\log3, then Iδ(H)≤δH<H/2.(24) I_\delta(H)\le\delta H<H/2. \qquad\text{(24)} Consequently no neighborhood-uniform estimate |EN(t)|≤CNCe−N|ℑt|/2|E_N(t)|\le C N^C e^{-N|\Im t|/2} can hold near this point. The proposition does not give that lower bound at the center −iH-iH alone.

Proof. The exact gauged equation in the cancellation source is MNEN+ÃN(EN(t−2θ)−EN(t))+C̃N(EN(t+2θ)−EN(t))=ℱN, M_NE_N+\widetilde A_N(E_N(t-2\theta)-E_N(t)) +\widetilde C_N(E_N(t+2\theta)-E_N(t))=\mathcal F_N, ℱN=(ck*−2)βN(0)∏l=0L−1ρl,N(0,t),L=⌊δN⌋,k*=k0+L.(25) \mathcal F_N=(c_{k_*}-2)\beta_N(0) \prod_{l=0}^{L-1}\rho_{l,N}(0,t), \quad L=\lfloor\delta N\rfloor,\quad k_*=k_0+L. \qquad\text{(25)} The three left-hand coefficients are uniformly bounded near −iH-iH: the source proves MN→4cot⁡2(t/2)M_N\to4\cot^2(t/2) and ÃN,C̃N→cot⁡2(t/2)\widetilde A_N,\widetilde C_N\to\cot^2(t/2) there. At this point every ratio in the product is negative, and its absolute value is cos⁡2Al[b+sin⁡2Al]cos⁡4Bl.(26) \frac{\cos^2A_l\,[b+\sin^2A_l]}{\cos^4B_l}. \qquad\text{(26)} All factors are bounded away from zero and infinity over the fixed interval 0≤l/N≤δ0\le l/N\le\delta. Their logarithms differ uniformly by O(N−1)O(N^{-1}) from log⁡[(b+sin⁡2(πl/N))/cos⁡2(πl/N)]\log[(b+\sin^2(\pi l/N))/\cos^2(\pi l/N)]. A Riemann-sum estimate therefore gives ∑l<Llog⁡|ρl,N(0,−iH)|=−NIδ(H)+O(1).(27) \sum_{l<L}\log|\rho_{l,N}(0,-iH)| =-NI_\delta(H)+O(1). \qquad\text{(27)} Moreover βN(0)\beta_N(0) is bounded above and below by positive constants times N−2N^{-2}, and ck*−2→−2(1+cos⁡(2πδ))≠0c_{k_*}-2\longrightarrow-2(1+\cos(2\pi\delta))\ne0. Thus |ℱN|≥c1N−2e−NIδ(H)|\mathcal F_N|\ge c_1N^{-2}e^{-NI_\delta(H)}. Taking absolute values in (25) proves (23).

Finally b≥e−Hb\ge e^{-H} is equivalent to H≥log⁡3H\ge\log3: b−e−H=(eH−3)(eH+1)4eH. b-e^{-H}=\frac{(e^H-3)(e^H+1)}{4e^H}. The ratio inside the logarithm in (22) is at least bb, so −log⁡[(b+sin⁡2(πx))/cos⁡2(πx)]≤H-\log[(b+\sin^2(\pi x))/\cos^2(\pi x)]\le H. This proves (24). All three points in (23) have imaginary height −H-H and approach the same center. The asserted uniform half-height estimate would contradict (23)–(24). ▫\square

The forcing obstruction does not contradict the positive, generally smaller exponential constant in the adopted cancellation theorem. Nor does it make the restored full kernel homogeneous: the original full kernel has its own nonzero highest-index forcing. Equations (19)–(20) and the retained vertical currents remain part of any comparison with the filled action. No whole-source upper bound or geometric coefficient follows from this note.
