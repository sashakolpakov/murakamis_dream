Actual auxiliary-color packets through the complete-circle quarter transition

29 October 2026. This extends the complete-circle boundary packet to the ACTUAL inner auxiliary color j=N-h, rather than declaring its pair-Casimir argument to be a peripheral parameter. It proves an exact finite analytic inner packet on a fixed complex color disk, with a nonzero joint spectral/color asymptotic, and a full physical inner-color complement gap. Every original first Casimir normal and Cartan phase remains present.

The final outer-circle color functional is a separate operation. The estimates below supply its inner inputs; they do not by themselves assert the hypotheses of the general long-filling theorem for the whole stack or identify a deformed representation.

1. The actual mixed circle, including its nonzero second band

Use N,d,q,Q,kappa,M,F_r,s_r and the original continuants f_v from the exact mixed two-band source. For every physical 0<=h<=M, the FULL all-port identity is

z_(N,h)=mathscrR(R^(-1)L_(N-h))=c_(N,h)+R P_(N,h),
c_(N,h)=kappa^(-1) sum_(v=h)^M
              N^2 s_(2v) f_v/[s_v^2 s_(v-h)s_(v+h)].       (1)

Here L_j is the literal ordinary mixed-color auxiliary trace and mathscrR is the genuine unscaled physical turn. For j<=N/2 the whole box is the pure alias (-1)^N R P_(N,j), including the even middle color. Thus all physical colors are accounted for.

The reflected box is z_(N,h)^sharp. The sharp operation means coefficient conjugation, including kappa and the fixed root. Its complex-color continuation will be defined by Schwarz reflection of the specified analytic packet, not by conjugating its parameter inside an allegedly holomorphic expression.

For the physical low core, interchanging its two finite sums gives

c_(N,h)(c)=N^2 sum_(r=0)^M b_(N,r)(h) B_r(c),
b_(N,r)=kappa^(-1) q^(h^2)(-1)^r q^(2r^2+r)
                                                A_(h,r)/F_r^3,
A_(h,r)=sum_(v=max(h,r))^M
  Q^(-vr)F_(2v)/[F_v F_(v-h)F_(v+h)F_(v-r)],
B_r(c)=product_(a<r)(c_a-c).                           (2)

These are exact root identities with their original marked phases. No upper band, source normal or normalization is suppressed.

2. A source-selected complex-color channel THROUGH the quarter point

Put tau=r/N, chi=pi h/N, H=exp(2i chi), Y=exp(2pi i tau), epsilon=1/N. On a fixed compact r-window about N/4, define A_(h,r) for COMPLEX h=N chi/pi by replacing F_(v-h),F_(v+h) in its finite sum v=r,…,M by the canonical marked Fhat_N. All these arguments remain in regular nonzero factorial strips after choosing a fixed small complex chi disk. The continuation is even in chi and equals the physical coefficient whenever h is an integer below the window.

Normalize U_(h,r)=A_(h,r)/D_(h,r), where

D_(h,r)=Q^(-r^2)F_(2r)/[F_r Fhat_N(r-h)Fhat_N(r+h)].

The literal mixed Ward and high source prove, for THIS finite factorial continuation,

U_r=p_N(tau,chi) U_(r+1)+q_N(tau,chi) U_(r+2),        (3)
p_N=-Q^(-2)Y^(-1)(2cos(2chi)Q^3Y^2+Q+1)
                                  /[(1-QY/H)(1-QYH)],
q_N=-Q^(-4)Y^(-2)(1-Q^2Y)(1-Q^3Y^2)(1+Q^2Y)
   /[(1-QY/H)(1-QYH)(1-Q^2Y/H)(1-Q^2YH)].

The original source is U_M=1; its q_(M-1)=0 wall is exact at both parities and remains independent of complex chi. In particular the actual source, rather than an arbitrary solution of (3), is holomorphic and nonzero at a strict high-index anchor.

Let mu_s,mu_f be the roots of the principal companion, continued from mu_s=-Y^(-1), mu_f=Y^(-1)cot(pi tau)^2 at chi=0. They are distinct and nonzero near tau=1/4 on a fixed complex neighborhood and small chi disk. Their relative multiplier in HIGH-to-LOW transport at chi=0 is -cot(pi tau)^2.

Apply the supplied-eigenframe analytic diagonalizer from the boundary-packet proof, now holomorphically in chi. First fix its neighborhood and positive exponential remainder barrier sigma. Then choose a small fixed delta, anchor u_b=pi/4+2delta+O(1/N), and target |u-pi/4|<=delta/4. At chi=0 the relative fast action from anchor to ANY target is at most -gamma_0<0, while its entire intermediate action width is O(delta^2)<sigma/4. Continuity on the fixed complex chi disk preserves a strictly negative target action and width<sigma/2. Shrink chi AFTER delta; merely distinct roots do not imply this.

The established high-index continued fraction supplies the exact anchor vector. Relative to its nonzero selected scalar, its slow coefficient is1+O(1/N), its fast coefficient O(1/N), uniformly for complex chi. Multiplying the exponentially diagonalized recurrence in its diagonal interaction frame bounds EVERY conversion error by exp[-N(sigma-width)]. The initially present fast source component is exponentially damped on the target. No source component or compensating current is deleted. This proves selection through the modulus crossing, uniformly on that fixed complex parameter disk.

Optimal analytic Euler–Maclaurin of the slow diagonal product, anchored to the SAME exact high source coefficient, now gives a holomorphic zero-free Uhat_N(tau,chi) on a tube containing the whole anchor-to-target interval. On the target physical r-grid,

U_(h,r)=Uhat_N(r/N,chi)[1+O(exp(-gamma N))].           (4)

All statements are uniform in complex chi. The first transport coefficients are exactly the old rational expressions

S(tau,chi)=integral_tau^(1/2) Log mu_s(t,chi)dt,
C(tau,chi)=sqrt(mu_s(tau,chi))
                exp[integral_tau^(1/2)nu/mu_s dt],
nu=[p_1+q_1/mu_s-q_0(partial_tau mu_s)/mu_s^2]
                                     /[1+q_0/mu_s^2].    (5)

The roots are continued from the original high interval to the target; the denominator is1-mu_f/mu_s and does NOT vanish at the quarter point. The normal-form diagonal and its first correction are holomorphic. Their explicit first-order scalar transport gives

Uhat_N=C(tau,chi) exp[N S(tau,chi)] [1+O(1/N)].        (6)

The constant is fixed by the old high source on the overlapping anchor tube, not by an unknown closed circle value. Formula (5) agrees there with the proved selected transport; the same analytic nonresonant transport equation continues it uniquely across the quarter point. This separates the algebraic leading error in (6) from the exponential original-grid error in (4).

3. Exact finite analytic inner packet and its joint asymptotic

Choose a fixed index window I_N about the quarter point whose closure lies STRICTLY INSIDE the controlled target neighborhood in section2; for example use |u-pi/4|<=delta/8. Its endpoints may move by O(1/N) to the physical grid. It is nonempty for all sufficiently large N. Take the finite polynomial

Z_N^win(c,chi)=N^2 sum_(r in I_N)
    kappa^(-1) q^(h^2)(-1)^r q^(2r^2+r)
                                        Ahat_(h,r) B_r(c)/F_r^3, (7)

where Ahat is the EXACT finite factorial completion in section2. It is even holomorphic in chi on a fixed complex disk, with all Casimir derivatives literal. The reflected packet is Z_N(win,sharp)(c,chi)=conjugate(Z_Nwin(conjugate(c),conjugate(chi))). Both are holomorphic, with their separate typed source phases.

Let J_sin(tau)=integral_0^tau Log(2sin(pi t))dt and define

B(tau,chi)=S(tau,chi)-S(tau,0)+2J_sin(tau)
             -J_sin(tau-chi/pi)-J_sin(tau+chi/pi),
D(tau,chi)=[C(tau,chi)/C(tau,0)] sin(pi tau)
             /sqrt(sin(pi tau-chi)sin(pi tau+chi)).        (8)

The marked factorial reflection shows these are precisely the exponential correction and amplitude ratio of q(h2)D_(h,r)/D_(0,r). In particular B(tau,0)=0,D(tau,0)=1, and D is nonzero. Put

Psi(u,eta,chi)=[-Lambda(2u)+6Lambda(u)]/pi
   +(1/(2pi))integral_0^(2u)Log(2cosh eta+2cos v)dv
   +B(u/pi,chi).                                         (9)

The whole phase is even in eta and chi. At (eta,chi)=(0,0), its selected index is u=pi/4 and Psi_uu=-8/pi. The implicit function theorem gives a unique holomorphic u_(eta,chi) nearby. Let j(eta,chi)=Psi(u_,eta,chi), and

a(eta,chi)=[sqrt(sin(2u_*))/sin(u_*)^(5/2)]
   exp(iu_*/2)D(u_*/pi,chi)
                     /[4sqrt(pi)sqrt(-Psi_uu(u_*,eta,chi))]. (10)

These are the SAME source-calibrated functions as the old mixed high-index theorem on its overlap eta>0; now both parameters range on fixed complex disks about zero. The exact finite packet satisfies

Z_N^win(-2cosh eta,chi)
   =exp(-i pi d/4) a(eta,chi) exp[Nj(eta,chi)]
                                                [1+O(1/N)], (11)

uniformly on smaller fixed complex disks. Its amplitude is nonzero.

Proof: (4) supplies an exponentially accurate original-grid interpolant. Canonical factorials and the half-shift Newton factor are regular on this window and have the same optimal EM residual bound. The exact half-grid cotangent rectangle retains every vertical endpoint and horizontal nonzero-mode current. Its complete real phase has a fixed endpoint gap; the top/bottom cotangent errors have exp(-2Nd) while the phase increases O(d^2). Choose contour height first, then shrink the fixed eta,chi disks. All residuals are below the selected saddle by a fixed exponential rate. The two constant cotangent pieces give the same saddle integral with density N/pi. Analytic Gaussian expansion gives (10)–(11), including both original parity phases. This is the earlier exact cotangent proof with the newly justified source across the quarter point, not continuation of a merely pointwise leading formula.

The rational principal quadratic gives, by differentiating in chi^2,

j(eta,chi)=j(eta,0)
                 -(1+exp(2iu_*(eta,0)))chi^2/(2pi i)+O(chi^4).

Consequently at the complete spectral point,

j(eta,chi)=j_0+eta^2/(4pi)
                      +(-1+i)chi^2/(2pi)+O((|eta|+|chi|)^4), (12)

with j_0=v8/pi. Reflection reverses the imaginary color coefficient, with its own explicit opposite complete-source phase. The real physical-color curvature is strictly negative. All fixed Casimir normals follow from the uniform holomorphic c-disk in (11); this does not assert moving-q normal derivatives of an unspecified off-root turn family.

These estimates apply to the EXACT packet, not only its leading expression. Writing theta=s+it, chi=x+iy, and using eta=i theta, one may shrink the fixed disks so that

|Z_N^win(-2cos theta,chi)|
  <=poly(N) exp N[j_0-a(s^2+x^2)+C(t^2+y^2)]         (12a)

for some a,C>0. The same holds for the typed reflected packet. The cross terms from the imaginary quadratic coefficient are absorbed by a fixed completion of squares; the quartic remainder is absorbed after shrinking. The uniform relative error in (11) changes only the prefactor.

There is also a fixed angular gap away from theta=0. On the whole physical r-window the exact coefficient bound is

|b_(N,r)(chi)|<=poly(N) [s_(2r)/s_r^6]
                                               exp(CN|chi|^2).

It follows from (4)–(8) and their even analytic action perturbation. The old Newton arc-convolution has a strict angular gap uniformly on this compact r-window about pi/4. Therefore, on the remainder of the physical theta circle outside any fixed small central arc, the packet is bounded by poly(N)exp[N(j_0-g+C|chi|^2)]. The gap persists on a small fixed complex theta strip and chi disk. Cauchy on radius1/N then controls every fixed derivative with only an additional polynomial factor. These are bounds for the explicit finite polynomial (7), suitable before the outer-color transform.

4. Actual-source replacement on a full fixed cusp corridor

Use the faithful original Hermite norm

||f||_H=max_double(|f(c)|+N^(-2)|f'(c)|),

also including the odd simple value. It is submultiplicative; ||R||_H,||R^(-1)||_H<=9/8, and |phi(f)|<=3N^3||f||_H. For some fixed delta_0,gamma>0, uniformly for the ACTUAL integers 0<=h<=delta_0 N,

||z_(N,h)-Z_N^win(.,pi h/N)||_H
                            <=poly(N)exp[N(j_0-gamma)].  (13)

The proof includes two different retained source errors. First, the removed r-indices have the original all-index envelope J_1(u)+E(u)+C chi^2. Its unique maximum at chi=0 is pi/4, so the complement of I_N has a fixed gap. To obtain a uniform segment bound without a root singularity, enclose[-2,2] by a fixed small Bernstein ellipse: its uniform log-integral profile approaches the unit-radius profile, hence preserves that gap. The factorial and mixed-Ward bounds are polynomial times this exponential. A degree<=M complex Markov bound preserves it for the first normals.

Second, the entire original upper band obeys ||RP_(N,h)||_H<=56^h. Shrink delta_0 until delta_0 log56<j_0-gamma. The finite errors in (13) are not zero; they are bounded before any exterior closure. This is why the actual even packet can replace the non-even full mixed source only with its explicit residual.

For any fixed number n of stack pieces and fixed crossing power m, telescoping products in this faithful norm proves the same type of exponential replacement bound for the entire original stack, after an additional fixed shrink of delta_0. It is uniform for every physical outer color because

max_(1<=j<=N)||L_j||_H=poly(N),
||R^m||_H<= (9/8)^|m|.                              (14)

Indeed |U_(j-1)(c/2)|<=j on[-2,2] and Markov costs at most N^2/2 per derivative. No positive quotient of the radical or source-operator commutation beyond the actual full pair algebra is used.

5. A width-free gap for the ENTIRE physical inner-color complement

For every fixed delta>0 with a nonempty physical complement there is gamma_delta>0 such that

limsup_N N^(-1)log max_(0<=h<N)||z_(N,h)||_H<=j_0,
limsup_N N^(-1)log max_(delta N<=h<N)||z_(N,h)||_H
                                               <=j_0-gamma_delta. (15)

Here h=N-j labels EVERY actual auxiliary color, not just the low half. For j<=N/2, the entire box is a pure alias, bounded by the proved uniform rate B_alias=(1/2)log(4+sqrt15)<j_0. For j>N/2, keep both the low core and that alias. The uniform core upper rate j_0 follows from the global mixed-source spectral envelope.

Its STRICT complement gap can be made uniform as the enclosing ellipse shrinks to the unit circle. Specifically choose ONCE 1/4<beta<1/2 and a closed contracting interval I subset(pi beta,pi/2). Every actual source propagating from the original terminal wall to r<=beta N crosses I. The exact spectral ellipse gives a uniform strict factor there for ALL delta N<=h<=M, including the repeated-root endpoint color. Frozen finite blocks turn it into a fixed exponential contraction, without choosing a root or assuming an eigenbasis. This factor is independent of the spectral radius R of the outer Newton estimate.

For r>beta N, the complete profile J_R+E has a fixed gap from j_0 uniformly for R sufficiently close to1, since its maximizer converges to pi/4. Choose that R so small that j(log R)-j_0 is less than one quarter of the fixed contraction/gap budget. The maximum principle on the segment and complex Markov give the stated Hermite gap. Thus a vanishing gap in the limit R->1 has NOT been assumed away. The old uniform subexponential frozen- block cost is absorbed by half the fixed gap. This proves (15), including every color endpoint and its first normal.

It follows immediately that for every fixed stack width n>=1, any fixed signed R power, and ANY physical outer color, the full original mixed scalar has a fixed exponential gap below n j_0 whenever at least one INNER deficit is at least delta N. The source cost in (14) is polynomial and one factor in (15) loses a fixed rate; the other fixed n-1 factors cost at most j_0+o(1). No minimum-width restriction is required: unlike the pretzel marked-period problem, the stack reference height is n j_0 with no outer Legendre loss. The separate complement in the OUTER auxiliary color remains the task of its exact Fourier-Hermite source transform.

6. The complete local input for the original outer-color source

Fix n>=1 and m in Z. For physical inner deficits in the corridor, let

F_phys(theta)=R(-2cos theta)^m
                product_i z_(N,h_i)^(typed i)(-2cos theta),

where each type is z or its literal sharp. Its definition here means the original full-Hermite polynomial. On a fixed small central complex theta window take the EXACT finite analytic function

F_win(theta,chi)=kappa^(m/2) exp[iNm theta^2/(4pi)]
                product_i Z_N^(win,typed i)(-2cos theta,chi_i), (16)

with kappa^(m/2)=exp[i pi m d^2/(4N)], without a principal-root reset. This is separately even in theta and every chi_i.

The replacement of R by its displayed Gaussian retains BOTH the value and first normal at every physical node in this window. Indeed, for theta_j=(2j-d)pi/N, the exact nodal phase is R(c_j)=kappa(1/2)exp[iNtheta_j2/(4pi)]. At every double node, the full original source derivative is

R'(c_j)/R(c_j)=-(d-2j)/[2(q^(2j+1)-q^(-2j-1))],
partial_theta log R(c_j)=iNtheta_j/(2pi).              (17)

The second identity follows using q(2j+1)-q(-2j-1)=-2i sin theta_j and dc/dtheta=2sin theta. At the odd simple node only its value is prescribed, as required. Thus (16) is not an arbitrary spectral extension of the physical crossing; it matches exactly the jets used in the original closure.

Equations (12a),(13) and submultiplicativity now give, after a fixed shrink of the inner corridor, a gamma>0 with the following actual source estimates, uniformly on its physical grid:

  • On the central nodes, F_phys-F_win and their first theta derivatives are bounded by poly(N)exp[N(nj_0-gamma)].
  • Outside any fixed smaller central theta window, F_phys and its first theta derivative obey the same exponential-gap bound.

For the second assertion use the uniform angular gap of the finite packet on the compact r-window, the H-residual(13), and the fact that the original R^m has unit nodal values and at most a polynomial first-normal cost. Derivatives of the fixed n-factor product cost only another polynomial. Thus the original physical spectral complement, not just the interpolant, is estimated.

On smaller fixed complex theta/color disks, the exact function (16) has a nonzero analytic asymptotic with phase

n j_0 -[n-im]theta^2/(4pi)
        +sum_i[-1+i sigma_i]chi_i^2/(2pi)+O_4,
sigma_i=+1 for z, -1 for z^sharp.                      (18)

The unit kappa and complete source phases remain in its specified external factor. In particular, for theta=s+it and chi=x+iy,

|F_win(s+it,x+iy)|
  <=poly(N)exp N[nj_0-a(s^2+||x||^2)
                                  +C(t^2+||y||^2)]   (19)

for some a,C>0 depending on n,m. The real quadratic coefficients at t=y=0 are n/(4pi) and1/(2pi); imaginary cross terms are absorbed by completing squares. Shrinking the fixed disks absorbs the quartic remainder. All fixed derivatives have polynomial additional cost on still smaller disks. These give the exact local holomorphic input, original-grid residual and spectral complement required BEFORE the outer-color Fourier-Hermite transform; no contour endpoint or alias is set to zero here.

Audit: actual mixed-circle operators, canonical complex quarter source and original crossing normals. the finite checks cover seven full physical mixed-circle finite-field families and seven entire low-color alias families; two canonical complex-Fhat source families across the quarter point; nine high-precision literal crossing/first-normal families; and six all-parity selected complex-source calibrations at N=32,33,64,65,128,129. The last checks compare the ACTUAL terminating finite U sum with its endpoint-normalized C exp(NS), not just a formal root. These finite calibrations supplement the analytic proof of its uniform domains, exponential interpolation residuals and source gaps.

The physical outer-color transform and geometric holonomy remain separate from the inner packet theorem.
