A global color gap for the actual coherent low core

29 October 2026. The literal mixed-source recurrence has a global two-branch upper envelope no larger than the complete-color envelope. Every macroscopic nonzero color deficit gives a STRICT contraction on a fixed high-index interval, uniformly even at the repeated-root color. Together with the proved coherent circle profile, this yields a strict circle and original-source gap for the low cores at all colors 0≤h≤⌊(N−1)/2⌋0\leq h\leq\lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor outside a fixed complete-color corridor.

This is a theorem about the actual low core. The original nonzero crossing-times-short-polynomial band RPN,hRP_{N,h} remains separate. Its global macroscopic-color bound is not supplied here. Thus the theorem does not yet bound the full mixed-color filling sum.

1. Exact recurrence and a bounded source at its literal wall

Use the notation of the selected mixed-inner source theorem. Put M=⌊(N−1)/2⌋M=\lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor, H=QhH=Q^h, χ=πh/N\chi=\pi h/N, and normalize by the COMPLETE diagonal:

Vh,r=qh2Ah,r/D0,r,0≤r≤M. V_{h,r}=q^{h^2}A_{h,r}/D_{0,r},\qquad0\leq r\leq M.

The exact selected Ward identity gives, with Z=Qr+1Z=Q^{r+1},

Vh,r=ph,N(r)Vh,r+1+qN(r)Vh,r+2, V_{h,r}=p_{h,N}(r)V_{h,r+1}+q_N(r)V_{h,r+2}, ph,N=−Z−1[(H+H−1)Z2+1+Q−1](1−Z)2,qN=−Q−2Z−2(1−QZ2)(1+QZ)(1−Z)2(1−QZ). p_{h,N}=-\frac{Z^{-1}[(H+H^{-1})Z^2+1+Q^{-1}]}{(1-Z)^2}, \quad q_N=-\frac{Q^{-2}Z^{-2}(1-QZ^2)(1+QZ)} {(1-Z)^2(1-QZ)}. \tag{1}

This holds at EVERY physical rr, including r<hr<h. At r=M−1r=M-1, qN=0q_N=0 at both parities. Thus one may start the recurrence at the actual Vh,MV_{h,M}, assigning the auxiliary Vh,M+1=0V_{h,M+1}=0; no nonexistent factorial is divided out.

The terminal source is especially well controlled:

|Vh,M|=sM2sM−hsM+h={1,N odd,cos⁡χ,N even. |V_{h,M}|=\frac{s_M^2}{s_{M-h}s_{M+h}} =\begin{cases}1,&N\text{ odd},\\ \cos\chi,&N\text{ even}. \end{cases} \tag{2}

For odd N=2M+1N=2M+1, complement the two denominator factorials. For even N=2mN=2m, M=m−1M=m-1, one has sM2=N/2s_M^2=N/2 and sM−hsM+h=N/(2cos⁡χ)s_{M-h}s_{M+h}=N/(2\cos\chi). This proves (2) uniformly for all physical colors 0≤h≤M0\leq h\leq M. The endpoint is the original finite source, not a chosen solution of an annihilator.

2. An elementary spectral ellipse controls all colors

Freeze u=(r+1)π/Nu=(r+1)\pi/N and let Q→1Q\to1 in (1), keeping Z=e2iuZ=e^{2iu} and H=e2iχH=e^{2i\chi}. Write T0(u,χ)T_0(u,\chi) for its 2×22\times2 companion. Its eigenvalues are Z−1vZ^{-1}v, where

v2−bv−c=0,c=cot⁡2u,b=(c−1)cos⁡2χ−2icsin⁡2χ. v^2-bv-c=0,\qquad c=\cot^2u, \quad b=(c-1)\cos^2\chi-2i\sqrt c\sin^2\chi. \tag{3}

For every 0<u≤π/20<u\leq\pi/2, 0≤χ≤π/20\leq\chi\leq\pi/2,

ρ(T0(u,χ))≤ρ0(u):=max⁡(1,cot⁡2u). \boxed{\qquad \rho(T_0(u,\chi)) \leq\rho_0(u):=\max(1,\cot^2u).\qquad} \tag{4}

Moreover the inequality is strict when χ>0\chi>0 and u≠π/4u\ne\pi/4; it also holds strictly at u=π/2u=\pi/2.

Here is a direct proof, including collisions. For 0<c<10<c<1, a root on the unit circle gives b=v−c/vb=v-c/v, the ellipse

(ℜb1−c)2+(ℑb1+c)2=1. \left(\frac{\Re b}{1-c}\right)^2+ \left(\frac{\Im b}{1+c}\right)^2=1.

The displayed bb has the left side

cos⁡4χ+4c(1+c)2sin⁡4χ≤1, \cos^4\chi+\frac{4c}{(1+c)^2}\sin^4\chi\leq1, \tag{5}

strict for χ>0\chi>0. Both roots at b=0b=0 have modulus c<1\sqrt c<1. The segment from zero to an interior point of this convex ellipse cannot cross the unit-root locus. Continuity therefore puts both roots inside the disk; limiting boundary points give the non-strict assertion. For c>1c>1, apply exactly this argument to v/cv/c, with parameter 1/c1/c. At c=1c=1, the roots of v2+2isin⁡2χv−1v^2+2i\sin^2\chi\,v-1 lie on the unit circle. At c=0c=0, the roots are 0,−cos⁡2χ0,-\cos^2\chi. These computations prove (4), with no root-label selection.

In particular, for fixed χ0>0\chi_0>0 and any fixed interval I⊂(π/4,π/2)I\subset(\pi/4,\pi/2), compactness gives

supu∈I,χ0≤χ≤π/2ρ(T0(u,χ))≤θ<1. \sup_{u\in I,\ \chi_0\leq\chi\leq\pi/2} \rho(T_0(u,\chi))\leq\theta<1. \tag{6}

At χ=π/2\chi=\pi/2 the two roots coalesce throughout this interval. Their common modulus is c<1\sqrt c<1. No eigenbasis or gap between the two roots will be used.

3. Frozen blocks turn the spectral bound into a uniform source bound

Define

E(u)={2π∫uπ/4log⁡cot⁡vdv,0≤u<π/4,0,π/4≤u≤π/2. E(u)=\begin{cases} \displaystyle\frac2\pi\int_u^{\pi/4}\log\cot v\,dv, &0\leq u<\pi/4,\\ 0,&\pi/4\leq u\leq\pi/2. \end{cases}

The following are actual uniform estimates, not an adiabatic assumption:

limsupN→∞max0≤h,r≤M{1Nlog|Vh,r|−E(πr/N)}≤0, \limsup_{N\to\infty}\ \max_{0\leq h,r\leq M} \left\{\frac1N\log|V_{h,r}|-E(\pi r/N)\right\}\leq0, \tag{7}

and for every fixed δ>0\delta>0, 0<β<1/20<\beta<1/2, there is γ=γ(δ,β)>0\gamma=\gamma(\delta,\beta)>0 such that

limsupN→∞maxδN≤h≤M,0≤r≤βN{1Nlog|Vh,r|−E(πr/N)}≤−γ. \limsup_{N\to\infty}\ \max_{\delta N\leq h\leq M,\ 0\leq r\leq\beta N} \left\{\frac1N\log|V_{h,r}|-E(\pi r/N)\right\} \leq-\gamma. \tag{8}

We take log⁡0=−∞\log0=-\infty. If δ≥1/2\delta\geq1/2, the range is eventually empty, so only 0<δ<1/20<\delta<1/2 matters.

Proof. First fix a small lower cutoff a>0a>0. On a≤u≤π/2a\leq u\leq\pi/2, all coefficients of (1), and their first uu derivatives, are uniformly bounded, independently of h,Nh,N. The normalized exact matrix BN(r)=TN(r)/ρ0(ur)B_N(r)=T_N(r)/\rho_0(u_r), ur=(r+1)π/Nu_r=(r+1)\pi/N, differs from the Lipschitz family B(ur,χ)=T0(ur,χ)/ρ0(ur)B(u_r,\chi)=T_0(u_r,\chi)/\rho_0(u_r) by Oa(1/N)O_a(1/N).

For a bounded 2×22\times2 matrix BB with both eigenvalues in the closed unit disk, Cayley–Hamilton gives directly

∥BL∥≤L∥B∥+L−1≤CaL. \|B^L\|\leq L\|B\|+L-1\leq C_a L. \tag{9}

Indeed its divided-power coefficient is ∑k=0L−1λ1L−1−kλ2k\sum_{k=0}^{L-1}\lambda_1^{L-1-k}\lambda_2^k, whose modulus is at most LL; this formula also covers the repeated root. If both roots instead have modulus at most θ<1\theta<1, the same proof gives CaLθL−1C_aL\theta^{L-1}.

Choose a fixed block length LL. A telescoping expansion of a block of adjacent exact normalized matrices differs from the frozen B(ur,χ)LB(u_r,\chi)^L by Oa,L(1/N)O_{a,L}(1/N), uniformly in χ\chi. For any prescribed ε>0\varepsilon>0, choose LL so large that CaL<eεLC_aL<e^{\varepsilon L}, and then choose NN large. Partition the product into such blocks. Two incomplete end blocks cost a constant. This bounds the normalized product by Ca,εe2εNC_{a,\varepsilon}e^{2\varepsilon N}.

For (8), choose a fixed closed interval I⊂(max⁡(πβ,π/4),π/2)I\subset(\max(\pi\beta,\pi/4),\pi/2). Every source propagation from the terminal point to r≤βNr\leq\beta N crosses all of II. By (6), choose the same LL sufficiently large that each full block inside II has norm at most e−γ0Le^{-\gamma_0 L}, with γ0>0\gamma_0>0. There are N|I|/(πL)+O(1)N|I|/(\pi L)+O(1) such blocks. Make the ordinary-block ε\varepsilon smaller than, for example, γ0|I|/(8π)\gamma_0|I|/(8\pi). The negative block budget therefore remains a fixed negative multiple of NN.

Near the only singular coefficient endpoint, direct use of (1) gives a universal constant CC with

∥TN(r)∥≤CN2(r+1)2,0≤ur≤a<π/8. \|T_N(r)\|\leq C\frac{N^2}{(r+1)^2}, \quad0\leq u_r\leq a<\pi/8. \tag{10}

For the second coefficient use |1−QZ2|/|1−QZ|≤C|1-QZ^2|/|1-QZ|\leq C, |1+QZ|≤2|1+QZ|\leq2, and |1−Z|≥4(r+1)/N|1-Z|\geq4(r+1)/N. The first coefficient has the same bound because |H+H−1|≤2|H+H^{-1}|\leq2. Thus all steps with r+1≤aN/πr+1\leq aN/\pi cost at most exp⁡{O(Nalog⁡(1/a))+O(log⁡N)}\exp\{O(Na\log(1/a))+O(\log N)\}, by the elementary factorial bound. This tends to rate zero as a↓0a\downarrow0. It does not grow like NO(N)N^{O(N)}.

Finally the scalar envelope product is bounded by the exact right-endpoint Riemann sum

∑j=rM−1log⁡ρ0((j+1)π/N)≤NE(πr/N). \sum_{j=r}^{M-1}\log\rho_0((j+1)\pi/N) \leq N E(\pi r/N). \tag{11}

Together with the source bound (2), these estimates prove (7). For (8), first fix the strictly contracting interval and its budget, then make alog⁡(1/a)a\log(1/a) small enough, and finally make ε\varepsilon small enough. The loss from (10) and all ordinary blocks is then strictly smaller than that budget. This proves (8) uniformly in every indicated physical color and index.

The proof supplies exponential-rate bounds. It does not assert a polynomial prefactor uniform through every spectral collision.

4. Strict circle and original-source core gaps

Interchange the two original finite sums as in the mixed source theorem. In the complete Newton basis Br(w)=∏a<r(ca−w−w−1)B_r(w)=\prod_{a<r}(c_a-w-w^{-1}), the exact mixed low-core coefficient has magnitude

|bh,r|=s2rsr6|Vh,r|. |b_{h,r}|=\frac{s_{2r}}{s_r^6}|V_{h,r}|. \tag{12}

All marked phases remain in the equality defining that coefficient; (12) is used only for its upper bound. The uniform factorial and Newton-factor estimates in the coherent complete-base theorem therefore imply, for every fixed R>1R>1,

limsupN1Nlog⁡max0≤h≤M∥cN,h∥R≤j(log⁡R). \limsup_N\frac1N\log \max_{0\leq h\leq M}\|c_{N,h}\|_R\leq j(\log R). \tag{13}

For every fixed 0<δ<1/20<\delta<1/2, there is εR(δ)>0\varepsilon_R(\delta)>0 such that

limsupN1Nlog⁡maxδN≤h≤M∥cN,h∥R≤j(log⁡R)−εR(δ). \boxed{\quad\limsup_N\frac1N\log \max_{\delta N\leq h\leq M}\|c_{N,h}\|_R \leq j(\log R)-\varepsilon_R(\delta).\quad} \tag{14}

To prove strictness, let u*∈(π/4,π/2)u_*\in(\pi/4,\pi/2) be the unique maximum of the complete profile JRJ_R. Choose u*/π<β<1/2u_*/\pi<\beta<1/2. For r≤βNr\leq\beta N, use (8). For r>βNr>\beta N, use (7): that interval lies strictly above the complete maximum, so its continuous profile JR+E=JRJ_R+E=J_R has a fixed gap below jj. Both assertions are uniform around the entire |w|=R|w|=R circle; the already proved arc inequality bounds each Newton factor by its negative-axis value up to a constant. The finite rr sum costs only a factor NN.

There is also a direct original-source consequence. For each fixed odd s≥3s\geq3, choose its complete geometric radius Rs=exp⁡[2arsinh⁡(tan⁡(π/s))]R_s=\exp[2\operatorname{arsinh}(\tan(\pi/s))]. If every color deficit is 0≤hi≤M0\leq h_i\leq M and at least one has hi≥δNh_i\geq\delta N, then

limsupN1Nlog⁡|Φ2(∏i=1scN,hi)|≤Vs2π−εRs(δ). \boxed{\quad\limsup_N\frac1N\log \left|\Phi_2\left(\prod_{i=1}^s c_{N,h_i}\right)\right| \leq \frac{V_s}{2\pi}-\varepsilon_{R_s}(\delta). \quad} \tag{15}

Indeed the exact full-Hermite source residue has the circle bound

|Φ2(F)|≤N2(R+R−1)(1−R−N)2R−Nmax|w|=R|F(w+w−1)|. |\Phi_2(F)|\leq \frac{N^2(R+R^{-1})}{(1-R^{-N})^2}R^{-N} \max_{|w|=R}|F(w+w^{-1})|.

Apply (13)–(14) to its factors, and use sj(log⁡Rs)−log⁡Rs=Vs/(2π)s j(\log R_s)-\log R_s=V_s/(2\pi). This step retains the actual derivative source and every finite winding. It does not replace it by a semisimple trace.

The global alias terms RPN,hiRP_{N,h_i} are not covered by (13)–(15). In the small cusp corridor they have the separate proved source gap for odd s≥5s\geq5. At macroscopic deficits, their old 56hi56^{h_i} bound may be too large. No claim about the full colored cube or fixed long fillings is made without resolving those terms.
