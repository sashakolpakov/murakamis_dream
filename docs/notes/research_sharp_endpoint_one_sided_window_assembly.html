Sharp one-sided endpoint representatives on a physical window

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The reflected sharp normal connection can be inserted into the exact one-sided Hermite repair. This gives local representatives of the original full physical jets whose real interface has any prescribed rate below J*J_* at the target point. The common baseline is bounded at every fixed target height. The original crossing, shared spectators, and all exterior contour sides remain in the exact trace. No bound for their closing is asserted.

5. Scope and direct sources

The previous one-sided window theorem already supplied the repair, interface identity, and full contour normalization. The additional consequence is that their error exponent can now be chosen arbitrarily close to J*(π−2πx0)J_*(\pi-2\pi x_0) on a sufficiently small fixed window, while the common scalar remains moderate at the prescribed heights. It uses the new reflected full-normal baseline, not the old modified-flow baseline.

In particular no estimate of the old baseline’s fractional-grid Green boundary packet is needed or asserted. The exterior-current problem, spectator action comparison, and nonzero saddle coefficient are unchanged by the exact construction above.
