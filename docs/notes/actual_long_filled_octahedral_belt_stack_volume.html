Independently long fillings of signed octahedral belt stacks

29 October 2026. This note assembles the quantum, peripheral, regulator and geometric inputs for the stated theorem.

1. The actual family and statement

Fix n>=1, a word epsilon of length n in {+,-}, and an integer a. Use the two literal mirror/reversed pieces D_+,D_- of the octahedral stack construction, with ONE retained outer complete circle and the actual outer R^a. Denote this ACTUAL augmented link by B_(epsilon,a). It has n inner crossing circles and one outer belt. Its complement is complete hyperbolic of volume2n v8, by its exact regular-cell/collar proof.

Choose ANY subset I of those n+1 auxiliary circles. For each i in I use its preferred meridian and crossing-disk longitude to fill the primitive slope mu_i+p_i lambda_i, for fixed nonzero integers p_i. Equivalently perform the literal1/p_i surgery/full-twist substitution in the original oriented diagram. Circles outside I remain components with their literal color N/L_N; this is NOT p_j=0, which would meridionally fill and remove them. Denote the resulting link in S^3 by J_(epsilon,a,I)(p).

There is a source-dependent finite P such that, whenever

min_(i in I) |p_i|>P,

the actual filled complement is hyperbolic and its original single-cut reduced Kashaev evaluation satisfies

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N)log|K_N(J_(epsilon,a,I)(p))|
                =Vol(S^3 minus J_(epsilon,a,I)(p)). (1)

For I empty this is the already proved COMPLETE stack theorem, with no filling threshold. For nonempty I the threshold may depend on I; for a fixed stack take the maximum over its finitely many subsets if desired.

The limits include every parity and composite level. The slopes may have independent signs and arbitrarily unequal lengths. They are fixed before N tends to infinity. No numerical value of P, uniformity as n or a grows with N, or arbitrary branched-graph theorem is claimed. Full twists preserve the main permutation. When ALL n+1 circles are filled, J is a KNOT for odd a and has TWO components for even a. For a proper subset its unfilled auxiliary circles remain: the total is n+2-|I| for odd a and n+3-|I| for even a.

2. Whole original quantum source, not a formal critical value

The exact color expansion inserts every R^(-2p_i) using the original ordinary mixed circles L_j and the complete Hermite filling coefficients. It retains the marked cut, original pivot, full half-Cartan and all dimension endpoints. Coefficient mass is polynomial for each fixed slope. No closed auxiliary component is assigned an extra modified dimension.

The inner auxiliary-color theorem proves source-fixed nonzero analytic packets through the quarter- index transition, for the ACTUAL colors j_i=N-h_i. Its exact finite factorial completion agrees with the actual physical low core on the grid, with a quantitatively retained exponential replacement error in a faithful norm containing every first Casimir normal. The nonzero RP band and the ENTIRE physical inner-color complement have strict gaps, for every fixed n>=1.

The outer squared-kernel theorem retains the actual full-Hermite outer ordinary color. Its exact double-pole kernel kills the dangerous false h≈N Fourier alias; it proves the remaining outer-color complement gap, together with a fixed-complex-neighborhood, separately even packet for ALL n+1 circle colors. This is not a spectral eta=meridian guess.

Write p and q for the numbers of plus and minus pieces and m=q-p+a. In x_i=h_i/N its local source action has

phi(x)=n v8/pi - pi x_0^2/[n-im]
              +(pi/2)sum_(i=1)^n(-1+i sigma_i)x_i^2+O_4,
sigma_i=+1 for z, -1 for z^sharp.

The real quadratic part is strictly negative in every color. The ENTIRE source cube complement is gapped, not just the individual box coefficients. Thus the hypotheses of the general original-source long-filling transfer hold. Restrict every unfilled color literally to h_j=0/L_N and apply only the original filling functionals indexed by I. The exact complex packet, nonzero amplitude, negative real Hessian and entire-cube complement gap restrict to these coordinates. It proves a NONZERO full-source asymptotic, and the selected point tends to the complete source point as min_(i in I)|p_i|->infinity. The empty subset instead uses the already proved nonzero complete-stack asymptotic directly. Fixed normal jets, endpoint currents and all nonleading charge sectors are controlled by that proof before its conclusion.

3. The selected point is an actual marked representation

The typed character composition supplies the group/flag lift which is essential after the source estimate. Its input is the source-selected internal critical root, not an arbitrary branch of a trace polynomial. The local pair matrices a,b,A=ba remain bounded and irreducible through A’s parabolic trace -2. The selected inner box, its sharp type and every actual bare R interface satisfy their literal threaded Hurwitz equations and ordinary-pivot flag conditions.

At the outer seam, TWO original nested main-head snakes reverse dual tensor order and invert the main meridian paths. Every incident mixed crossing is transported through the SAME original Hopf duality, including its full Cartan and nilpotent factors. The auxiliary cut/pivot and unrestricted marked co-source stay on their original side. This proves the actual outer disk word P_0=A^(-1), not a word selected from a matching cusp derivative. Irreducibility uniquely gives C_0=K_tot at closure.

All complete white and BOTH inner shaded face maps match the regular octahedron certificate, as full three-vertex maps. The actual signed pants collars and outer R^a match too. The outer generator is then uniquely forced by closure to be the geometric one. Consequently the ENTIRE complete representation is the positively oriented holonomy of the ACTUAL B_(epsilon,a), not only a representation with matching flags, trace coordinates or Hessian.

Let z_i=Log H_i and G be the actual logarithmic source primitive, with its real Cartan/spin constant retained. The preferred flagged peripheral data at ALL n+1 auxiliary circles are exactly

M_i=exp(-z_i/2),
lambda_i=-exp(-ell_i), ell_i=partial_(z_i)G.        (2)

For the outer circle ell_0=eta=Log(-w). The preferred minus sign is fixed by the original ordinary pivot and the disk framing. Simultaneously inverting both generators and swapping their common flag is equivalent; inverting only one is not. All surviving main meridians are noncentral parabolic, so BOTH small logs at each unfilled main cusp are zero, including the merged odd-a cusp.

4. The source action is the absolute regulator

Keep the spin term in Gbar=G-pi i sum z_i. The actual source and preferred peripheral data give

u_i=-z_i/2, v_i=pi i-ell_i,
dGbar=2 sum_i v_i du_i,
D_src=Im G-sum_i Re z_i Im ell_i.                  (3)

The developed fundamental chain for the SAME marked representation has Bloch boundary -2 sum M_i wedge lambda_i. Its elementary Bloch–Wigner differential equals dD_src, including its factor2. Both sides have complete value2n v8 by the full geometric anchor. They therefore agree on the connected selected germ. No equality of an unproved transformed dilogarithm list is assumed.

The exact source filling equations are

z_i+2p_i ell_i=2pi i m_i, m_i=+1 or -1, i in I,
z_j=0, j not in I.                                (4)

Separate exact evenness gives ell_j=0 for every unfilled auxiliary coordinate, even as all the other colors vary. Its actual peripheral group remains noncentral parabolic near completeness.

Its kept quadratic chirp is sum_(i in I)(z_i-2pi i m_i)^2/(4p_i). On(4) its imaginary part equals -sum_i Re z_i Im ell_i. Hence the imaginary FULL filled source action is exactly the marked representation volume D_src. This establishes the volume normalization, not just a numerical quadratic/cusp comparison.

5. Primitive completion supplies equality with hyperbolic volume

The small geometric squared-eigenvalue logs are

U_i=-z_i, V_i=-2ell_i,
U_i+p_i V_i=-2pi i m_i, i in I,
U_j=V_j=0, j not in I.                             (5)

Thus the filled slope has its actual primitive2pi angular period. The preferred SL longitude sign in(2) is kept before passing to these small geometric logs. A mere exponentiated PSL identity would not establish this unit winding.

The actual selected representations and peripheral flags tend to the now identified complete geometric ones as the chosen slopes become independently long. For each filled coordinate separate evenness implies ell_i divisible by z_i, so z_i=0 would contradict (4). The full geometric completion theorem therefore applies to these source-selected representations. Its explicit logarithmic torus/tube construction adds a smooth core at each primitive slope, with no missing axis sheet. The compact core and every unfilled main AND auxiliary cusp remain positive and complete.

This constructs the COMPLETE hyperbolic metric on the actual filled topology; it is not merely a nearby incomplete metric. The oriented fundamental class and the two-way Gromov-volume comparison identify its regulator with the actual hyperbolic volume. Combining this with the NONZERO whole-source asymptotic and (3)–(4) proves(1).

6. Scope ledger

The theorem expands a cyclic one-belt source family. It does not assert an arbitrary six-port tensor estimate, arbitrary branched augmentation, or the volume conjecture for every fixed bridge number. The possible overlap of these actual belt-stack diagrams with known Whitehead-chain families remains a coverage caveat; no novelty claim about a literature family is used in the proof.

For partial filling, every unfilled color remains the literal L_N. Its two geometric logarithms vanish by exact separate evenness, and primitive completion leaves that cusp complete. The linked composition note gives the detailed marked proof.
