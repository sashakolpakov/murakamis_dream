The global signed cut-ball regulator, with the original marked apex

31 October 2026. conditional stationary-stratum lemma. Hypotheses are exactly the finite-positive-ordinary-radius stationary stratum and signed/cooriented circle development of the marked signed-development lemma. This proof does NOT use invariance of an open crossing regulator under resetting a Gauss frame: that assertion failed the recorded direct test. The original graph, marking and untwisted shaded pairings stay fixed.

1. An elementary cyclic polygon chain identity

Let z_0,…,z_(n-1) lie on one Euclidean circle, in their RECORDED polygon order, not necessarily its circular order. Choose real arguments phi_j for the points. Consecutive increments d_j=phi_(j+1)-phi_j may be signed and may differ by multiples of2pi; close with phi_n=phi_0+2pi w. Cone an oriented fan triangulation of this polygon to the common ideal point infinity in upper half-space. Its oriented volume is

sum_(j=0)^(n-1) Lambda(d_j/2). (1)

For an oriented triangle of distinct points on a circle, the usual ideal-tetrahedron angle formula gives (1): its three oriented half-arcs are its three angles modulo pi, with the common orientation sign. Lambda(-a)=-Lambda(a), Lambda(a+pi)=Lambda(a), and Lambda(pi-a)=-Lambda(a), so the formula is independent of the chosen argument lifts and holds for either triangle orientation. Sum it over the fan. Each internal diagonal appears with opposite orientations; its two Lambda terms cancel EXACTLY. Only the original boundary edges remain. No convexity or winding-one assumption has been used.

The identity also includes repeated nonadjacent polygon vertices. First apply the finite fan identity with distinct generic points on that SAME circle. An edge with coincident endpoints contributes Lambda(0)=0. Any degenerate fan tetrahedron has zero limiting volume: its coalescing base points stay on this one circle, so its cross-ratio with infinity tends to the real axis. Equivalently use the normalized alternating ideal chain with repeated vertices set to zero. This is not an arbitrary four-ideal-point collision assertion. For a polygon on a line, every cone tetrahedron is coplanar with infinity and (1) has value zero in the limiting line interpretation.

2. Apply it to the ONE signed developed circle family

At a finite white vertex f, its actual face polygon consists of the contact points with its neighboring white circles, in the original cyclic source order. At a triangle t its two successive contact rays turn by sigma_t theta_(t,f), modulo2pi. Hence (1) gives its oriented white-pyramid volume as

sum_(t at f) sigma_t Lambda(theta_(t,f)/2). (2)

This works at an overloaded vertex with winding zero just as at a winding-one vertex. If its generalized middle corner is written 2pi-theta rather than -theta, its Lambda contribution is identical by pi-periodicity. Thus no integer winding/branch constant is lost. Both marked white faces are lines through the cone apex and have zero contribution, matching their zero center angles.

The shaded face of t is the ideal triangle of the three pairwise contacts of its tangent white circles. For a finite or one-line triangle its Euclidean angles are (pi-theta_(t,f))/2. Its orientation in the common plane is sigma_t times the original positive convention. Consequently coning this original ordered shaded triangle to infinity gives

sigma_t sum_(f in t) Lambda((pi-theta_(t,f))/2). (3)

This elementary contact-angle relation is unchanged by reflection of a center triangle. A two-line uv triangle contains the apex itself; its cone has zero volume, and (3) is zero because theta=(0,0,pi). These are precisely the forced degeneracies allowed here.

Summing (2),(3) for one abstract cut ball gives

V_ball = (1/2) sum_t sigma_t V(theta_t). (4)

The developed boundary can overlap or fold. Equation (4) is the volume of its oriented chain, not the volume of an embedded region.

3. Why this is the original representation fundamental chain

Use the TWO literal topological cut balls of the original augmented projection, with their original white/shaded cellulation and face permutations. On the first ball map every ideal contact vertex to its contact point in the ONE developed family. Map white polygon edges and shaded edges to their geodesics, and triangulate each white face by a fixed original combinatorial fan. These finite oriented face chains form a closed boundary cycle: every original edge occurs twice with opposite orientations, even if developed contacts coincide. Use the EXISTING labeled convex right-angled certificate to select the actual abstract triangulation once and for all: fan each incident white face from the original marked vertex p; triangulate every nonincident white face compatibly; then cone p to the nonincident triangles. The certified convex ball proves that this is a degree-one triangulation of the original ball. Keep that exact labeled abstract chain and merely change its developed ideal vertices to the signed circle contacts. Its volume is the cone of the boundary cycle to p=infinity; triangles already incident to p contribute zero cone terms. No new interior ideal vertex or new cusp is introduced. Degree one concerns the abstract original domain only, not its possibly folded developed image.

Use ONE global reflection r_u to map the second ball. Reverse its abstract orientation together with this orientation-reversing target reflection, so its volume contribution is the same as (4). Do not choose independent block, port or face reflections for that ball.

The face pairings agree with the original representation as follows. On a white face f, the prescribed orientation-preserving map is r_f r_u; reflection r_f fixes the entire white plane. It therefore identifies the complete second-ball white polygon chain with the first-ball chain, including every vertex and the original edge order. Here are the shaded maps with the actual copy labels and permutation. All ports in this endpoint source are UNTWISTED. In each original ball write its two shaded triangles as

S_0=(O,C_1,C_2), S_1=(O,C_3,C_0).

They pair WITHIN that same ball by O->O, C_1->C_0, C_2->C_3; this is exactly (3) of the original matching geometry, not the across-copy half-twist permutation (4). In the first ball, input A_b has its fixed flag C_1=x/b contact, input B_b its fixed flag C_2=b/y contact, and P=B_b A_b its flag O=x/y contact. Equation (5) of the line adapter gives C A_b C^-1=A_c, C B_b C^-1=B_c, and CP=PC. Therefore C sends these THREE flags respectively to C_0=x/c contact, C_3=c/y contact, and O. They are distinct at every permitted finite, one-line and two-line triangle. The ordered ideal-triangle isometry is thus exactly C, including its original pointwise permutation.

On the reflected second ball the map is r_u C r_u (composition of anti-Mobius maps on either side), from r_u S_0 to r_u S_1 with the SAME three labels. The reverse pairing is its inverse. Thus no shaded face is silently moved across copies and no independent reflection is selected for the second map. Its whole ideal face is paired, not only the common auxiliary fixed point. These are precisely the untwisted Morse/threading pairings already used to construct the representation.

Hence the two abstract ball chains descend, with THEIR original multiplicities and orientations, to an equivariant relative fundamental chain of the original exterior. In particular an immersed white polygon is not replaced by its image counted once, and an overloaded winding- zero face is not deleted from the abstract source cellulation.

For completeness this ideal-chain construction has the usual genuine cusp realization. Each main meridian is NONCENTRAL parabolic: its two adjacent white circles are distinct and tangent. Its actual longitude commutes with it, hence belongs in PSL2 to its translation centralizer and preserves every horosphere at the same contact. At an auxiliary cusp the ACTUAL two generators are C^-1 and its preferred longitude P=B_b A_b (with the already recorded harmless central lift). Both are in the same parabolic translation group at the outer contact. This remains true if C=I: P is still noncentral because the matching outer pair is distinct and tangent. Commuting with identity ALONE is not used to infer any peripheral property. Thus the WHOLE peripheral subgroup preserves the chosen contact horosphere in every case.

Decorate each contact by the horosphere of its ordered tangent white pair (normalize the pair to opposite parallel planes at width1, then take height1). The actual shaded map carries this decoration: it carries both adjacent white planes, because each is the unique plane orthogonal to the shaded plane along the corresponding edge. White reflections carry it as well. Use finite INCIDENCE corner points on these paired horospheres, truncate, and attach the original conical cusp tails. The established O(T^-2) horospherical boundary flux compares this with the ideal chain. No parabolic cusp vertex is falsely assigned a single fixed finite point. Only finitely many paired incidence points are used.

Thus representation volume, defined by the original equivariant relative fundamental class, is

Vol(rho)=2 V_ball=sum_t sigma_t V(theta_t). (5)

All boundary/pairing data have been checked at the global chain level. An individual re-framed open crossing word need not have its former regulator. Its boundary correction is not discarded or assumed zero locally; instead (5) computes the sewn fundamental class directly.

4. The conditional actual-entropy consequence

At the stationary stratum under discussion, the proved exact coefficient identity is C(alpha)=V(2pi alpha)/(2pi). A cross port contributes the sum of its two C’s; a strict left/right Green port contributes their signed difference, including the forced marked zero limits. These are the actual local E values, with their absolute normalization. Therefore

sum_e E_e = (1/(2pi)) sum_t sigma_t V(theta_t) = Vol(rho)/(2pi) <= Vol(Y_D)/(2pi). (6)

The last inequality is the proved representation-volume bound for the actual original hyperbolic exterior. No action constant, reference-flag normalization or separately chosen regulator has been introduced.

This handles a FULL stationary configuration with finite positive ordinary radius multipliers and only the forced marked-line nonregularities. It is not yet the inequality for every maximum W_D. Additional ordinary zero/infinite multipliers, flat triangles, diagonal mu=nu, terminal nu=1/2 and other free KKT strata still require their own limiting or block construction. In particular one cannot silently change an eventually feasible forest type into a cross type: the exact finite support can prevent that even when their limiting local entropy and degree vector coincide.

The cut-ball comparison is on the stated stationary stratum. It does not cover every boundary maximum or select a quantum cycle.
