The full numerator after localization: exact cross cofactors and the packet interface

The whole-source local packet now assembles these results for the actual full numerator. The original global spectral filling period remains open.

31 October 2026. . Sections 1–3 are finite identities of the original polynomial source. Sections 4–6 distinguish a concrete remaining asymptotic theorem from those identities. No frozen file is modified and no filling-cycle or relative packet theorem is asserted.

The source normalization is that of actual_homogeneous_casimir_face_source.md; the full resolvent numerator, not a complete-image interpolation, is used throughout.

1. Actual polynomial block and cleared lower cofactors

Put d=N-1, m=floor(N/2), theta=pi/N, sigma=-2-u, and

P_N(u)=(-1)^d H_(V_N(-2-u)),
a_j=2+2cos((2j+1)theta), s_j=2sin(j theta),
A=x+y, P=xy.

The original triangular matrix 2+Omega+u has diagonal a_j+u and negative lowering entries -s_j^2 A and -s_j s_(j-1)P. All physical endpoint zeros remain in these entries. A numerator path j to i has middle monomial b^i c^(d-j), its positive step product, and the product of a_r+u over exactly its UNUSED rows. This is a polynomial identity also at every zero of a_r+u.

Take the lower block with rows 0,…,m-1. Initially off its poles let

A_-(u)=product_(r=0)^(m-1)(a_r+u), K_-=(2+Omega+u)_-^(-1),
D(b)=A_- sum_(i=0)^(m-1) b^i (K_-)_(i,m-1),
E(b)=A_- sum_(i=0)^(m-2) b^i (K_-)_(i,m-2).       (1)

These are CLEARED cofactors, hence polynomials, with degrees m-1 and m-2 in (x,b,y). Set E=0 for m=1. Define polynomials R_j(b) without any denominators by R_0=1, absent negative indices zero, and

R_j(b)=b^j product_(r<j)(a_r+u)
         +s_j^2 A R_(j-1)(b)
         +s_j s_(j-1)(a_(j-1)+u)P R_(j-2)(b).   (2)

The source path recursion, splitting at its first step from j, gives

D(b)=R_(m-1)(b),
E(b)=(a_(m-1)+u)R_(m-2)(b).                     (3)

For odd N also use R_m, with the same original s_m and a_(m-1). Neither (2) nor (3) is a lower-level quantum source; the level stays N. There is also an exact determinant realization. In the block on rows 0,…,j, replace the LAST row of 2+Omega+u by (1,b,…,b^j). Its determinant is R_j(b), by the row-replacement formula det(A)(w A^-1)_j. This remains polynomial at singular A and does not assert that the row-replaced matrix is positive or Hermitian.

2. Exact central-cross factorization at both parities

For EVEN N=2m the CROSS support is i<=m-1,j>=m. Its polynomial is

P_cross(u)=s_m^2 A D(b)D(c)
   +s_m s_(m-1)P [D(b)E(c)+E(b)D(c)].           (4)

Proof: every decreasing path crosses the central cut exactly once, by m->m-1, m+1->m-1, or m->m-2. These are precisely the three terms. Row reflection r->d-r identifies the upper and lower diagonal products and exchanges the corresponding one- and two-step weights. The full diagonal product is A_-^2, which cancels the two cleared cofactor denominators. This proves (4) off a finite set; polynomial continuation proves it everywhere, including u=-a_(m-1).

For ODD N=2m+1 the CROSS support is i<=m,j>=m. Put

G(b)=A_- b^m+s_m^2 A D(b)+s_m s_(m-1)P E(b)=R_m(b).

Then exactly

P_cross(u)=G(b)G(c)+u s_m s_(m+1)P D(b)D(c).    (5)

Visited-central paths split at row m, giving the first product. A cross path skipping m has the unique two-step jump m+1->m-1; its UNUSED central row supplies u, giving the second term. Here s_(m+1) =s_m. The original full diagonal product is u A_-^2, but (5) has no division by u and no discarded derivative term. In particular its u derivative retains both differentiated visited-central paths and the central-skipping term. Thus (4)–(5) include the actual semisimple information; they are not identities only at the complete nodes.

Products of (4) or (5), followed by the ONE original shared target coefficient and protected targets zero, give the exact all-CROSS shared numerator. Distinct ports keep their distinct spectral u_e. There is no new N factor, local mark, or independent face summation.

3. Exact complete-node anchor and what it does not imply

At a paired physical node c_a, the full-Hermite identity gives

L_(N,a)=(-1)^d (2/N)V_N(c_a).

At the odd simple endpoint c_*=-2 it instead gives

L_(N,*)=V_N(-2)/N.

The original homogeneous complete-circle theorem writes

H_(L_(N,a))=N k_a B_(N,a),
B_(N,a)=beta_a^2 D_a(x,b,y)D_a(x,c,y)P_(d-2a)(x,y),
k_a=2 paired, k_*=1 simple.

Consequently, UNIFORMLY over both multiplicity types,

(-1)^d H_(V_N(c_a))=N^2 B_(N,a).                (6)

The right side has b,c degrees at most a<=floor(d/2), hence lies entirely in the CROSS support. Thus (4)–(5) agree with the FULL numerator at EVERY folded spectral node. On the fold cover

sigma(delta)=-2cos(2pi delta),
u(delta)=-4sin^2(pi delta),
delta_a=(d-2a)/(2N),                            (7)

equation (6) supplies the exact original anchor for comparison with actual_positive_face_exponential_fold_interpolation.md.

It does NOT justify differentiating that interpolation to get V’. There are fewer distinct spectral nodes than the degree of V, and the semisimple interpolation ambiguity is real. Nor does (4) imply that its three summands have a common nonvanishing complex phase. The new identity makes the remaining object explicit; it does not settle its Stokes structure or a relative amplitude.

4. A concrete continuum candidate, not a claimed packet

For a fixed starting row i, its cleared interval continuant obeys

K_(i,j)=s_j^2 A K_(i,j-1)
         +s_j s_(j-1)(a_(j-1)+u)P K_(i,j-2).

With s(t)=2sin(pi t), a(t)=4cos^2(pi t), its positive constant-block root at real u>=0 is

Lambda(t;u)=[s(t)^2 A
      +sqrt(s(t)^4 A^2+4s(t)^2(a(t)+u)P)]/2.     (8)

At the upper fold t=1/2 its discriminant is nonzero whenever A>0. Thus clearing the half cofactor removes the artificial a_j^-1 singularity there. It does not remove the lower endpoint t=0.

The corresponding candidate action for D(b), normalized at u=0, is

F_u(x,b,y)=max_(0<=mu<=1/2)
   [mu log b+int_0^mu log(a(t)+u)dt
                +int_mu^(1/2)log Lambda(t;u)dt]. (9)

For b>0, its interior saddle solves b(a(mu)+u)=Lambda(mu;u), hence

sin^2(pi mu)=b^2(1+u/4)/[(b+x)(b+y)].            (10)

Under (7), this is the already recorded deformed middle-angle equation. At u=0, the proved half-block weighted theorem normalizes (9) to the actual triangle F(x,b,y), with no arbitrary constant. For b=0 the initial row is forced to i=0. If also xy=0 the recurrence is an exact monomer product; otherwise the lower-endpoint transfer eigenvalues approach equal modulus and must be treated explicitly.

Equations (8)–(10) are an explicit candidate and algebraic saddle calculation. This handoff does NOT claim uniform complex asymptotics or a nonzero relative prefactor from this calculation. In particular, the proved positive logarithmic rate is weaker than such a packet.

5. The precise relative theorem needed by the original period

The spectral-polydisk-complement theorem gives, for the original shared numerator S_N and its exact all-CROSS part C_N,

S_N-C_N=O(exp[N(Phi-gamma/2)])                   (11)

on a sufficiently small FIXED spectral polydisk, at both parities. It also gives exact spectral-jet bounds on radius N^-2. These are absolute estimates, not a nonzero approximation for C_N.

A useful next theorem must give C_N on the ACTUAL delta cover near the saddles used by the original spectral cycle, after the original shared face coefficient extraction. It may be a single branch only where that is proved, or a finite branch sum with its actual sector coefficients. Schematically one needs

C_N(delta)=sum_tau N^(beta_tau) A_(tau,N)(delta)
                  exp[N S_(tau,N)(delta)]
                 + controlled relative/absolute remainder, (12)

uniformly on specified complex sectors or saddle neighborhoods, with nonzero amplitudes for the selected contributions and enough holomorphic margins to differentiate. Finite-N actions must retain the actual parity anchor and all original scalar characters.

The theorem must include the original face torus: a local pointwise cofactor formula alone does not license Gaussian reduction of the shared coefficient. The old complete-source face Hessian and torus gap provide a plausible starting point at delta=0, but their transfer to (4)–(5) still requires relative control, including every protected zero pattern. A fixed complex disk free of zeros or Stokes curves is NOT imposed here; a sectorial finite-branch theorem sufficient on the contributing saddle neighborhoods would suffice.

Once such a packet has real rate at least Phi-gamma/4 on those neighborhoods, (11) is exponentially smaller there. Uniform holomorphic errors then legitimately control the V’ semisimple term by Cauchy. This would use an estimate of the ACTUAL cofactors, not differentiation of complete node samples.

6. Why shrinking endpoint disks do not by themselves suffice

For a fixed integer crossing power n the exact full-Hermite Gaussian is

R^n=q^(n d^2/4) exp[n N K(Omega)/(4pi i)],
K(sigma(delta))=-(2pi delta)^2.

The retained scalar q^(n d^2/4) remains outside the local phase. In the exact midpoint Fourier formula of actual_spectral_total_derivative_source.md, put phi=pi-2pi delta. The charge ell contributes, apart from its recorded constant sign,

exp[N(pi i n delta^2+2pi i ell delta)].          (13)

More explicitly, let I_(D,n) denote the original marked contraction with the actual coupon R^(n_e) at each port. Any separately recorded exterior framing scalar stays outside this formula. The existing single-coupon total-derivative identity, followed by the exact shared coefficient extraction, gives the following WHOLE-source formula:

I_(D,n)=q^[(d^2/4)sum_e n_e] (-i)^R
  sum_(ell in (Z\{0})^R)
    [product_e ell_e] (-1)^[(N+1)sum_e ell_e]
  int_[-1/2,1/2]^R [product_e sin(2pi delta_e)]
    S_N(-2cos(2pi delta_1),...,-2cos(2pi delta_R))
    exp{N sum_e[pi i n_e delta_e^2
                          +2pi i ell_e delta_e]} ddelta.  (13a)

Indeed the one-coupon prefactor -iN^2/2 times the Fourier measure 1/(2pi) times B_f=2sin(phi)fV/N^2 is -i/(2pi). Substitution phi=pi-2pi delta supplies 2pi, while the midpoint sign (-1)^ell and exp(-pi i N ell) combine to the displayed sign. Thus all N^2 factors cancel in the period prefactor, not in S_N itself. For each fixed N the sum is absolutely convergent by the proved C^1 periodic endpoint regularity and Fourier O(|j|^-3) bound. Its constants need not be uniform in N.

Because S_N and the Gaussian are even in EACH delta_e, reversing a single ell_e reverses its integral and also the prefactor ell_e. Thus the terms are equal under independent charge sign reversal; one may equivalently sum over positive ell_e with prefactor (-2i)^R. This is an exact parity identity, not proof that only ell_e=1 survives. Endpoint currents remain encoded by the actual finite interval and its periodic regularity; integration by parts cannot silently erase them. Formula (13a) uses the original FULL V at every port.

The ell=0 integral of the total derivative is EXACTLY zero. For a bounded surviving nonzero charge, the local critical equation is

partial_delta S(delta)+2pi i n delta+2pi i ell=0. (14)

Its candidate near-parabolic solution is delta=O(1/n), with the diagram’s coupled derivatives included. For independently large FIXED twists, this displacement is fixed as N grows. The required packet is therefore near a nonzero fixed delta*, not just on the N^-2 spectral disk (delta=O(1/N)) controlled by endpoint jets.

Even a proved (12) would leave the source-preserving cycle comparison, the nonzero charges and their possible cancellations, and the global complement to check. The +2 Gaussian closing cap is already proved in actual_gaussian_resolvent_numerator_and_cap.md and is not a new missing endpoint estimate. Its bulk sign warning still applies: a whole spectral ellipse has a Gaussian-growing side. Largeness of n does not by itself justify dropping it. The existing strict physical prefix and cross-central Green actions are selected integrand rates; they neither identify (4)–(5) on complex face sectors nor prove (12). Likewise actual_q_hahn_full_physical_complex_packet.md has a different strict physical/complex-magnetic chart and an integer channel, so an explicit adapter would be needed before importing its relative bound.

7. A proved central-strip relative bound and the endpoint amplitude anchor

This section is stronger than a bare endpoint jet majorant but still has a SHRINKING spectral domain. It does not prove (12) at fixed delta*.

Fix 0<eta<1/4 and N>=1/eta. Restrict a local numerator to endpoints

i<=N(1/2-eta), j>=N(1/2+eta),

and denote it P_eta(u). A fixed integer rounding of these inequalities does not matter. The following is COEFFICIENTWISE in all four faces:

[u^k]P_eta(u) <= binom(N,k) C_eta^k P_eta(0),    (15)
C_eta=max{1/[4sin^2(pi eta/2)],
                1/[4cos(pi eta)cos(2pi eta)]}.

Here and below a zero coefficient on the right also forces zero on the left. In particular this is not an estimate only after choosing positive face radii.

Proof. Expand each one-step factor x+y into its two labeled terms. A coefficient of u^k consists of an actual path and a k-element set S of unused rows, at which the factor a_r+u contributes u rather than a_r. For a selected row outside the open central strip N(1/2-eta)<r<N(1/2+eta), leave the path unchanged and restore a_r. Its weight ratio is 1/a_r<=1/[4sin^2(pi eta/2)].

A selected central row lies strictly between both endpoints. If unused it MUST be skipped by the step r+1->r-1. Replace that dimer by r+1->r->r-1, assigning x to the first step and y to the second. This preserves the exact outer exponents and both middle exponents. The old dimer with its selected unused factor removed has weight s_(r+1)s_r xy; the repaired two steps have weight s_(r+1)2s_r2 xy. Their ratio is 1/[s_r s_(r+1)], bounded by the second quantity in C_eta. Selected skipped rows cannot be adjacent, so all these repairs are compatible. Given S and the repaired labeled path, collapsing exactly those two-step runs recovers the original path. Thus the map is injective for each S, with weight ratio at most C_eta^k. There are at most binom(N,k) choices of S.

At odd N a surviving term from a path skipping m necessarily selects m in S: otherwise its unselected a_m factor is zero. Repair then visits m, exactly as required for P_eta(0). All other repaired paths also have nonzero zero-spectral weights. This proves (15) at BOTH parities without dividing by the zero row.

Summing (15), for complex u with |u|<=r,

|P_eta(u)-P_eta(0)|_coef
      <=[(1+C_eta r)^N-1] P_eta(0).             (16)

Now choose eta from the unique geometric maximizer, so every actual geometric middle load is <=1/2-4eta. Let

Z_N(u)=(-1)^(dR) S_N(-2-u_1,...,-2-u_R)

be the actual shared numerator, and Z_eta its restriction to these central-strip endpoints at every port. The complement lies in a closed incidence set separated from q_geom: for all sufficiently large N some middle load is >=1/2-2eta. The same uniform full-h entropy and support-preserving spectral comparison used for the one-sided gap therefore give a number gamma_eta>0 and one fixed spectral polydisk such that

|Z_N(u)-Z_eta(u)|<=exp[N(Phi-gamma_eta)].        (17)

Shrinking the fixed polydisk absorbs its spectral majorant cost. At u=0, Z_N(0)>0 and log Z_N(0)/N -> Phi: for even N this is the proved full endpoint theorem; for odd N it is the exact simple-fold complete coefficient. Positivity gives Z_eta(0)<=Z_N(0). Applying (16) before the original shared coefficient extraction consequently proves, uniformly in this polydisk,

|Z_N(u)/Z_N(0)-1|
   <= product_e(1+C_eta |u_e|)^N-1+O(e^(-cN)).  (18)

Choose fixed c0>0 so exp(R C_eta c0)-1<1/2. The full numerator is then nonzero on |u_e|<=c0/N for all sufficiently large N, and is uniformly 1+O(N max|u_e|)+O(e^(-cN)) relative to its actual endpoint value when max|u_e|=o(1/N). Cauchy on c0/N also gives, for every multiindex alpha and sufficiently large N,

|partial_sigma^alpha S_N(-2,...,-2)|
    <=2 alpha! (N/c0)^|alpha| |S_N(-2,...,-2)|. (19)

These are spectral derivatives only. The constant c0 is attached to the fixed original diagram; no growing-width uniformity is claimed.

There is a useful EXACT-SOURCE amplitude anchor. For even N the maximal complete node has delta_min=1/(2N) and u_min=-4sin2(pi/(2N))=O(N-2). Apply (18) at that node in every port and use the exact node identity (6). If

B_top=[original ordinary targets] product_e B_(N,M),

with the original two protected targets zero, then

(-1)^(dR) S_N(-2,...,-2)
         =N^(2R) B_top [1+O(N^-1)]   (N even), (20)
S_N(-2,...,-2)=N^(2R) B_top          (N odd).  (21)

The odd formula is the exact simple-node identity, not an asymptotic consequence of (18). Formula (20) controls the actual even semisimple endpoint amplitude against the known complete-fold anchor. It still does not extend a relative packet to the fixed O(1/n) delta saddle: the zero-free disk just proved has delta size O(N^-1/2).

8. Exact cofactor interpolation and a global all-CROSS spectral bound

Write J=floor((N-1)/2) at even levels and J=m at odd levels. Thus J=m-1 for N=2m and J=m for N=2m+1. Every R_j, 0<=j<=J, has spectral degree at most j. The J+1 distinct physical nodes are

u_a=-a_a=-2-2cos((2a+1)pi/N), 0<=a<=J.

The following coefficientwise fact supplies the needed nodal control:

0<=R_j(b;u_a)<=R_j(b;0), for all j,a<=J.         (22)

If j<=a, every diagonal a_r-a_a actually used by (2) is nonnegative and no larger than a_r; (22) follows directly by induction. If j>a, the inhomogeneous term of (2) vanishes after row a, giving exactly

R_j(u_a)=K_(a,j)(u_a) R_a(u_a).                 (23)

Normalize q_r=K_(a,a+r)/product_(v=a+1)(a+r)s_v2. The exact trigonometric identity

a_(j-1)-a_a=-s_(j+a)s_(j-a-1),
s_j s_(j-1)-s_(j+a)s_(j-a-1)=s_a s_(a+1)

gives q_0=1,q_1=x+y and

q_r=(x+y)q_(r-1)-gamma_r xy q_(r-2),
gamma_r=1-s_a s_(a+1)/[s_j s_(j-1)] in [0,1],
j=a+r<=J.                                     (24)

In particular q_r>=0 COEFFICIENTWISE: induct on

q_r-xq_(r-1)
  =y[q_(r-1)-xq_(r-2)]+(1-gamma_r)xyq_(r-2)>=0.

Also q_r<=(x+y)^r, by (24) and induction. At u=0 the same interval continuant contains its all-monomer term plus only nonnegative terms. Hence K_(a,j)(u_a)<=K_(a,j)(0). Together with the j=a case of (22), the product in (23) is bounded by K_(a,j)(0)R_a(0), a literal subset of the zero-spectral paths contributing R_j(0) (those whose start is at most a and which visit a). This proves (22). The nodal K factors were not assumed positive without proof.

Even N has the m shifted Chebyshev-root nodes. Odd N has m+1 reflected Chebyshev-Radau nodes, including u=0. Since deg_u R_j<=J, ordinary Lagrange interpolation is EXACT. Its Lebesgue function on [-4,0] is bounded by C log(N+1). One elementary proof uses the full N-point odd-angle cosine grid, pairing reflected nodes. For even N the cosine degree is at most m-1 and the Dirichlet kernel has degree m-1; for odd N it has degree m. Discrete cosine inversion is exact on these spaces. The sum of absolute normalized kernel values is O(log N), by |D_k(t)|<=min(2k+1,1/|sin(t/2)|) and the harmonic sum over equally spaced points. Pairing cannot increase that bound. No interpolation is being applied directly to full V.

Apply interpolation separately to each FACE coefficient in (22):

|R_j(u)|_coef<=C log(N+1) R_j(0), -4<=u<=0.    (25)

On the delta cover, the ordinary polynomial Bernstein-Walsh bound in z=-1-u/2=-cos(2pi delta) then gives

|R_j(u(delta))|_coef
   <=C log(N+1) exp[2pi j |Im delta|] R_j(0).   (26)

For completeness, this bound follows by writing z=(w+w^-1)/2: the exterior maximum principle for w(-j)p((w+w-1)/2) bounds it by sup_[-1,1]|p|, with |w|=exp(2pi|Im delta|). Apply this scalar argument to each coefficient polynomial of actual degree at most j.

Return to the exact central formulas (4)–(5). On any fixed strip |Im delta|<=T, the factors |u| and |a_(m-1)+u| are bounded by a constant depending only on T. The other cofactor products compare COEFFICIENTWISE to the zero-spectral central kernel. Indeed at even N,

D_b(0)>=s_(m-1)^2 (x+y) R_(m-2)(b;0),
(x+y)^2>=2xy,

so either xy D_b(0)R_(m-2)(c;0) term is bounded by a constant times (x+y)D_b(0)D_c(0). The constants are uniform for N>=4. The latter is itself a summand of P_cross(0). At odd N,

G_b(0)>=s_m^2(x+y)D_b(0),
G_b(0)G_c(0)>=2s_m^4 xy D_b(0)D_c(0).

Thus (26) yields the all-level coefficientwise bound

|P_cross(u(delta))|_coef
   <=C_T log^2(N+1) exp[2pi d |Im delta|]
                                        P_cross(0).       (27)

It respects each actual original face exponent, so taking products and the ONE original marked shared coefficient gives

|S_(N,cross)(sigma(delta))|
   <=[C_T log^2(N+1)]^R
      exp[2pi d sum_e |Im delta_e|] Z_(N,cross)(0). (28)

In particular the all-CROSS numerator has global REAL-spectral upper rate Phi throughout the complete real interval [-2,2]. This is stronger than a small endpoint-disk bound, but is not an angular gap on the face torus and not yet a FULL-numerator bound on that interval. It retains the actual shared targets and no independent choice of face radii.

For clarity, the remaining one-sided issue has an exact formulation. At even N, P_left(u) is A_-(u) times the lower-block adjugate generating polynomial (with its actual c^m factor), and A_-(u)=2(-1)^m T_m((-2-u)/2) is bounded by 2 on the real interval. The adjugate entries have degree at most m-1 and thus the same exact interpolation. At a node a an entry i,j is zero unless i<=a<=j; its surviving expression has factors K_(i,a)K_(a,j), nonnegative, but also product_(r>j)(a_r-a_a), with sign (-1)^(m-1-j). Separate absolute prefix/suffix ratios against u=0 can be exponentially large. A uniform comparison of their COMBINED nodal coefficients has not been proved here. The global bound (28) must not be extended to the full one-sided remainder by silently dropping these factors.

In fact such a POLYNOMIAL COEFFICIENTWISE extension to the full real interval is false. Take N=4k, m=N/2=2k, and j=k-1. The LEFT monomial x^j c^(d-j), with b=y=0, has exactly one path, using j one-steps. Its full-numerator coefficient is

A_-(u) [product_(r=1)^j s_r^2]
                   product_(r=j+1)^(m-1)(a_r+u).           (29)

At u=-4, equivalently sigma=+2, |A_-(-4)|=A_-(0)=2. Its absolute ratio to the positive endpoint coefficient at u=0 is therefore

product_(r=k)^(2k-1) tan^2((r+1/2)pi/N).         (30)

The logarithmic rate of (30) is

2 integral_(1/4)^(1/2) log tan(pi t)dt
    =2 Catalan/pi>0.                             (31)

The endpoint logarithm is integrable; deleting a fixed small terminal interval and using the elementary sine-product bound there proves the midpoint Riemann limit, with no numerical extrapolation. Thus the combined one-sided adjugate coefficients really can exceed their u=0 values exponentially, even after restoring A_-(u). The issue is not merely a weakness of separate prefix/suffix estimates.

It also occurs at a FIXED STRICT INTERIOR spectral point, not just at the already controlled closing cap. Take sigma=sqrt(3), u=-2-sqrt(3), and N divisible by24. Then m=N/2 is divisible by12, so A_-(u)=2T_m(cos(pi/6))=2. The same coefficient ratio is

product_(r=N/4)^(N/2-1) [sqrt(3)-c_r]/[2+c_r]
    >=(sqrt(3)/2)^(N/12) (1+sqrt(3))^(N/6)
    =(3+2sqrt(3))^(N/12).                       (32)

Indeed c_r<=0 throughout the product and c_r<=-1 on its last N/6 indices. This exact positive exponential lower bound requires no asymptotic extrapolation or endpoint limit.

This is a LOCAL COEFFICIENT obstruction. It is not a counterexample to a shared-source spectral bound or to the original +2 physical resolvent cap. The marked coefficient may enforce additional global cancellations and incidence constraints. It shows precisely why the all-CROSS coefficientwise theorem cannot be substituted for a full real-spectral numerator theorem on the original period.
