A strict Fock-to-cyclic-normal Mellin map and its logarithmic obstruction

Research construction, 27 October 2026. This gives an explicit linear comparison from the actual completed Fock source to the whole cyclic normal source, including its Weyl operators, cut, pivot adapter and relative endpoint carry. The target must retain a marked logarithmic torsor. A global algebraic comparison with the same nonzero vacuum and normal derivative is impossible on every finite cover.

The comparison below is between specified source/normal complexes. It is not an algebra map of fixed-N matrix algebras and does not identify their full quantum-group actions. It also does not fix the physical 2pi*i adapter required for a geometric Fourier integral.

1. The two actual source modules and the explicit map

Fix a primitive N-th root Q in characteristic zero. In the common-Verma source, write the completed Fock module as

Fhat=H[[t]], h_j t^r/r!=v_(rN+j),
B=partial_t, T=t, [B,T]=I, 0<=j<N.

Here H is the original balanced finite head, with source psi=1 and co-source phi=(1/N)sum_j. Its block vacuum projection is pi_0.

Retain the whole cyclic normal algebra and physical derivative:

Chat=k[[U-1]][A]/(A^N-U),
D_phys=A partial_A+N U partial_U,
nabla=D_phys/N.

Mark alpha(U)^N=U, alpha(1)=1. Put u=Log U, u(1)=0, and

epsilon_j=(1/N)sum_(l=0)^(N-1)Q^(-jl)(A/alpha)^l.

These are mutually orthogonal idempotents summing to one. They evaluate to delta_(j,a) on A=alpha Q^a, and nabla epsilon_j=0. The literal topological linear isomorphism is

M(v_(rN+j))=epsilon_j u^r/r!.                (1.1)

It is invertible because the epsilon_j form a basis over k[[U-1]] and u is a formal parameter with first coefficient U-1. Directly,

M B=nabla M, M T=(Log U)M,
M theta_s(h_j)=epsilon_j U^s.                (1.2)

Thus all integer resonant sectors U^k are present as the images of coherent kernels theta_k. They are functions of the whole normal U, rather than being set to one before transport.

This is an exact normal polarization comparison. It does not say that the actual clock q-normal, block-number operator T B, and nabla are the same operator. In particular M(T B)=u nabla M; other current directions must be transported with their own marked operator adapters, not inferred from (1.2).

2. The original cut and its normal pivot line are preserved

Let tau=Tr_(Chat/k[[U-1]])/N. The canonical A-power basis gives tau(f)=f_0(U), and tau(epsilon_j)=1/N. Hence

[tau M(f)]_(U=1)=phi pi_0(f),
M(psi)=1,
tau nabla=U partial_U tau.                  (2.1)

There are two different source projectors. The coefficientwise extension e_H tensor I is carried to f |->tau(f)1, retaining every normal coefficient. The actual finite-rank ambient coupon Gamma=i e_H pi_0 is instead carried to

f |-> [tau(f)]_(U=1) 1.

They agree on the vacuum but not on its first normal. In particular the first projector commutes with nabla, whereas

[nabla,Gamma_U]f=-[tau(nabla f)]_(U=1)1.     (2.2)

This is the additional finite normal cut row. It cannot be discarded by identifying the whole-normal and finite-vacuum projectors. These are strict linear cut comparisons, without identifying matrix composition with scalar coefficient multiplication.

The root pivot also has two distinct whole-normal lifts. With K_U=xA^(-1), define

g_red=x^(1-N)A^(-1), g_lit=K_U^(1-N)=U g_red,
D_red=g_red^(-1)e, D_lit=U^(-1)D_red.        (2.3)

The reduced representative agrees with the original pivot on U=1. Only g_lit is its literal clock power off that fiber. In the horizontal epsilon frame the exact adapters are

g_red=alpha^(-1) M g_H M^(-1),
D_red=alpha M D_H M^(-1),
g_lit=alpha^(N-1) M g_H M^(-1),
D_lit=alpha^(1-N) M D_H M^(-1).             (2.4)

Thus their physical normal derivatives differ:

D_phys(g_red)=-g_red, D_phys(D_red)=D_red,
D_phys(g_lit)=(N-1)g_lit,
D_phys(D_lit)=-(N-1)D_lit.

Both products are exactly e and horizontal. The adapter U and its first derivative remain part of a comparison of the two lifts; the common root value does not identify their normal phases. The full external d_N(M) line is unchanged and remains distinct from this internal U normal.

The underlying full quantum modules are not identified: for example the uncorrected cyclic operator (1-A)S^(-1) has N-th power 1-U, whereas the root balanced head F has N-th power zero. The map (1.1) does not suppress that endpoint source or claim to intertwine these two different F actions.

3. An explicit relative source-chain map with endpoint U

Use an independent source-centralizer coordinate sigma with marked endpoints zero and one. Keep it distinct from any physical geometric framing parameter. On Fhat-valued series define the twisted relative complex

P=H[sigma][[t]],
R_T^1=P,
R_T^2=P direct_sum Fhat direct_sum Fhat,
delta_T h=((partial_sigma-T)h,h(0),h(1)).

Every t coefficient in P is polynomial in sigma; endpoint evaluation and antiderivation are therefore defined coefficientwise. The t-adic completion permits exp(sigma T) while retaining those endpoint maps. Conjugation by exp(sigma T), with exp(T) at the second endpoint, identifies this complex with the ordinary relative source complex. Its transgression is

L_T(a,b_0,b_1)
   =integral_0^1 exp(-sigma T)a(sigma)dsigma
                            -exp(-T)b_1+b_0. (3.1)

The inclusion is f |->(exp(sigma T)f,0,0). Both identities L_T delta_T=0 and L_T i_T=I follow by differentiating exp(-sigma T)h(sigma). They hold before applying any cut.

Applying M coefficientwise gives a strict isomorphism to the whole-normal complex

delta_U h=((partial_sigma-Log U)h,h(0),h(1)),
L_U(a,b_0,b_1)
   =integral_0^1 U^(-sigma)a(sigma)dsigma
                                -U^(-1)b_1+b_0.           (3.2)

The coherent inclusion becomes f |->(U^sigma f,0,0). The endpoint carry is literally U. It has not disappeared through specialization of A^N=1. The whole averaged co-source and its residue realization from (2.1) commute with this chain map. The original finite cut is obtained after evaluation U=1; its normal derivative requires the extra row (2.2), rather than a horizontal vacuum assumption.

There is also an exact compatible normal connection on the entire relative complex. On degree one it is B-sigma. On degree two it is (B-sigma,B,B-1). These commute with delta_T because

[B-sigma,partial_sigma-T]=0.

Under M they are (nabla-sigma) and (nabla-sigma,nabla,nabla-1), respectively. Transgression carries them to B and nabla. Using bare B without the sigma term would give curvature -I and would be incorrect. This is an explicit Heisenberg normal/source complex, not a conditional lifting argument.

4. The logarithmic torsor is necessary, not a finite-cover choice

The completed map (1.1) has a global version only after adjoining the marked logarithmic torsor of U. On any simply connected marked sheet, nabla(Log U)=1 and all formulas above are actual functions. Changing the logarithm shifts its additive torsor coordinate.

There is a sharp obstruction to replacing it by algebraic functions. Suppose a linear comparison into the algebraic whole-normal field, or any finite algebraic cover, intertwines B with nabla and carries some vacuum h to a nonzero horizontal value c. Since B(Th)=h, its image f would satisfy

U partial_U f=c.

After applying a constant component functional if necessary, c is a nonzero constant. Thus df=c dU/U. At a zero or pole of U on the normalized compact curve, its right side has nonzero residue. An exact differential of an algebraic function has zero residues. Finite covers multiply that residue by a positive ramification index and cannot remove it. This contradiction proves that no such nonzero vacuum/cut-preserving algebraic linear map exists.

The obstruction already appears on the first excited Fock vector; it is not an asymptotic or high-jet problem. It also does not contradict (1.1): Log U is a valid formal function at U=1. The required common object is a marked logarithmic differential torsor, rather than only a finite algebraic source cover. It can be presented as the algebraic bundle with coordinate t and connection dt=dU/U; the obstruction is to an algebraic horizontal section, not to the existence of that bundle.

5. Relation to the actual two-dimensional divided-adjoint orbit

The explicit divided-adjoint source orbit and its marked geometric framing chart are proved in the framing-orbit note. With its coordinates v=a Ubar+bW, z=a+b, the map is

r=a/z, s_geo=z^(-2),
A_cut=z^2/(1+z^2), tau_geo=r/s_geo=a z,
Omega_cut=d tau_geo wedge dlog A_cut.         (5.1)

This establishes an explicit bare group/form comparison. It does not identify the source-relative sigma in Section 3 with tau_geo at the physical quantum scale. If u=Log U=N Log A_cut, then

Omega_cut=(1/N)d tau_geo wedge du.

At H_N=2pii/N, a geometric Fourier phase tau_geo Log A_cut/H_N is tau_geo u/(2pii). Matching it with the source kernel exp(s_Fock u) would therefore require s_Fock=tau_geo/(2pii), changing the unit source endpoint U to U^(1/(2pii)). A marked residue/phase adapter is required before claiming such a physical polarization identity.

There is also a direct source-collapse limitation. On a diagonal cut matrix C the normalized raw orbit insertion is

Phi(Cv)/Phi(v)
   =(a/z)/(N-1) sum_(j<N-1) C_jj+(b/z)C_(N-1,N-1).

It depends only on a/z. Thus the two orbit parameters do not by themselves become two independently measured internal framing coordinates of the scalar partial knot state. The whole-normal and relative current data above are necessary to retain the additional direction before collapse.

6. What is new here and what remains

The earlier first-quantum-normal and source/monodromy notes give the actual module/cokernel connection, its source, and the normal lattice cut. The relative-framing note realizes the finite boundary cut as a residue complex. They do not contain the explicit Fock Mellin map (1.1), its endpoint-U relative chain map (3.2), or the global algebraic obstruction in Section 4.

Those maps now compare the actual Fock and whole-normal boundary objects directly, retaining both the reduced pivot lift and literal clock-power lift with their exact U adapter, the distinct finite- vacuum cut row, and every endpoint factor. They do not prove that a computed geometric Gaussian class has the original selected image under this map. Its extension through the actual source boundary, the physical 2pi*i adapter and global period coefficient remain separate requirements. No regulator volume identity is inferred.
