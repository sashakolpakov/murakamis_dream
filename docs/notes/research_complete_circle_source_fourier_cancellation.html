Fourier cancellation and a strict upper bound for the complete source

Private written proof, 23 November 2026.

The full vertex closing, including its first spectral normals, is a sampled total derivative. In its Gaussian Fourier representation the zero alias therefore cancels exactly. Away from explicitly retained angular cuts and coefficient-band boundaries, its positive Fourier energy is exponentially concentrated near nonzero integer aliases.

Combining this dual estimate with the exact edge-source estimate gives a joint real localization of the original marked contraction. This is an upper bound for its complementary contribution, relative to the original incidence norm. No geometric lower bound or complex hyperbolic-cycle coefficient is inferred.
