The joint one-sided score as three elementary concavity gaps

31 October 2026. Workspace research lemma, independently checked algebraically. This supplies an exact identity for the new joint score. It does not establish a global transport, a representation volume, or the universal inequality.

Use the actual closed half-block entropy. For a LEFT degree vector write μ=qb\mu=q_b, ν=1−qc\nu=1-q_c, w=ν−μw=\nu-\mu, ξ=qx\xi=q_x, qy=w−ξq_y=w-\xi. The original interval coefficient entropy is

h=C(α)−C(β),h=C(\alpha)-C(\beta),

where the input/output triangle allocations share their actual outer tilt and

α=(a,μ,12−μ−a),β=(b,ν,12−ν−b),a−b=ξ. \alpha=(a,\mu,\tfrac12-\mu-a),\qquad \beta=(b,\nu,\tfrac12-\nu-b),\qquad a-b=\xi.

Here all triangle entries are nonnegative, 0≤ξ≤w0\leq\xi\leq w, and the limiting endpoint allocations are those of the original coefficient formula. The new local relative-energy bound has entropy

g=C(γ),γ=(ξ,12−w,w−ξ).g=C(\gamma),\qquad \gamma=(\xi,\tfrac12-w,w-\xi).

Let f(s)=[Λ(πs)+Λ(π/2−πs)]/πf(s)=[\Lambda(\pi s)+\Lambda(\pi/2-\pi s)]/\pi on [0,1/2][0,1/2] and define

Df(u,v)=f(u)+f(v)−f(u+v),u,v≥0,u+v≤1/2.D_f(u,v)=f(u)+f(v)-f(u+v),\qquad u,v\geq0,\quad u+v\leq1/2.

Concavity and f(0)=0f(0)=0 imply Df≥0D_f\geq0. The reflection identity is f(1/2−s)=f(s)f(1/2-s)=f(s). Direct substitution now proves

h+g=Df(12−a,ξ)+Df(μ,w)+Df(μ+a,w−ξ). \boxed{\displaystyle h+g=D_f(\tfrac12-a,\xi)+D_f(\mu,w) +D_f(\mu+a,w-\xi).}

Indeed, the three sums of arguments are 1/2−b1/2-b, ν\nu, and ν+b\nu+b; all lie in [0,1/2][0,1/2]. Expanding their three differences gives

f(a)−f(b)+f(ξ)+f(μ)−f(ν)+f(w)+f(μ+a)−f(ν+b)+f(w−ξ), f(a)-f(b)+f(\xi) +f(\mu)-f(\nu)+f(w) +f(\mu+a)-f(\nu+b)+f(w-\xi),

which is C(α)−C(β)+C(γ)C(\alpha)-C(\beta)+C(\gamma) by reflection. Thus the joint upper score (h+g)/2(h+g)/2 is a half-sum of three nonnegative concavity gaps. Each DfD_f is also the sharp protected-outer spectral action from the boundary theorem.

The elementary reassociation identity

Df(u,v)+Df(u+v,w)=Df(v,w)+Df(u,v+w) D_f(u,v)+D_f(u+v,w)=D_f(v,w)+D_f(u,v+w)

holds whenever the arguments and total stay in the domain. More generally, any binary parenthesization of nonnegative leaves with total at most 1/21/2 has total gap ∑if(ui)−f(∑iui)\sum_i f(u_i)-f(\sum_i u_i). This is a telescoping identity, not a claimed move of the original knot source.

A possible use is to reorganize interval pieces along the actual middle forest while retaining all face targets. That application is still missing: the identity does not itself prescribe the required reassignment of original corner loads, preserve the original marked line flags, or compare the total with the complete Casson–Rivin optimum. The prior obstruction to lossless cover atomization of a fixed projected flow remains valid.

The local joint score is not concave in general. On a protected-outer row it is Df(μ,w)D_f(\mu,w), with ww fixed and ν=μ+w\nu=\mu+w. Its second derivative in μ\mu is

f″(μ)−f″(ν)=−2πsin⁡(2πμ)+2πsin⁡(2πν). f''(\mu)-f''(\nu) =-\frac{2\pi}{\sin(2\pi\mu)} +\frac{2\pi}{\sin(2\pi\nu)}.

This is strictly positive when 0<μ<ν<1/20<\mu<\nu<1/2 and μ+ν>1/2\mu+\nu>1/2, for example at (μ,ν)=(3/10,2/5)(\mu,\nu)=(3/10,2/5). Thus local concavity of (h+g)/2(h+g)/2 cannot be used to infer the universal shared maximum. This is a local curvature obstruction, not a feasible global-flow excess. Its first μ\mu derivative is always positive by strict concavity of ff, which still records a useful direction for a possible global transport.
