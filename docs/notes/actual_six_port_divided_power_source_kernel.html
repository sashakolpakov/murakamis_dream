The actual six-port source is an explicit divided-power kernel

29 October 2026. The saturated specialization of GENERIC invariant tensors inside the full root-level six-head invariant space has an explicit single divided-power equation. The equation is surjective onto a specified physical degree space. Its right inverse and an algebraic source projection are given below at every NN, including even and composite levels. This does not assume a semisimple root tensor category or a tame recoupling frame.

1. Regular actual divided operators on any fixed number of heads

Put d=N−1,q=eπi/N,Q=q2,ρ=q−q−1d=N-1,\ q=e^{\pi i/N},\ Q=q^2,\ \rho=q-q^{-1}. The actual generic highest-weight NN-head, in the balanced analytic basis, is

F|a⟩=(1−ζ2a+2)|a+1⟩,ℰ|a⟩=(ζ−N−ζN−2a)|a−1⟩,K|a⟩=ζd−2a|a⟩. F|a\rangle=(1-\zeta^{2a+2})|a+1\rangle,\quad \mathcal E|a\rangle=(\zeta^{-N}-\zeta^{N-2a})|a-1\rangle,\quad K|a\rangle=\zeta^{d-2a}|a\rangle. \tag{1}

Outside-basis arrows vanish. Set E=ℰ/(ζ−ζ−1)2E=\mathcal E/(\zeta-\zeta^{-1})^2. Direct adjacent entries give [E,F]=(K−K−1)/(ζ−ζ−1)[E,F]=(K-K^{-1})/(\zeta-\zeta^{-1}). For an ordered rr-head block use the original coproducts

ℰr=∑iℰi∏j>iKj,Fr=∑i∏j<iKj−1Fi. \mathcal E_r=\sum_i\mathcal E_i\prod_{j>i}K_j,\qquad F_r=\sum_i\prod_{j<i}K_j^{-1}F_i. \tag{2}

For n≤Nn\leq N, their divided powers are REGULAR at ζ=q\zeta=q:

ℰr[n]=ℰrn/[n]ζ!,Fr[n]=Frn/[n]ζ!. \mathcal E_r^{[n]}=\mathcal E_r^n/[n]_\zeta!,\qquad F_r^{[n]}=F_r^n/[n]_\zeta!.

Here division is made in the specified generic family before specialization. To prove regularity without dividing a zero root matrix, normal order the QQ-commuting summands of (2). With si=∑j<inj,ti=∑j>inj,C=∑i<jninjs_i=\sum_{j<i}n_j,\ t_i=\sum_{j>i}n_j,\ C=\sum_{i<j}n_in_j, one obtains

ℰr[n]=∑∑ni=nζC⨂i(ℰi[ni]Kisi),Fr[n]=∑∑ni=nζ−C⨂i(Ki−tiFi[ni]). \mathcal E_r^{[n]} =\sum_{\sum n_i=n}\zeta^C\bigotimes_i \left(\mathcal E_i^{[n_i]}K_i^{s_i}\right), \quad F_r^{[n]} =\sum_{\sum n_i=n}\zeta^{-C}\bigotimes_i \left(K_i^{-t_i}F_i^{[n_i]}\right). \tag{3}

This follows directly from the finite Gaussian binomial expansion, and then induction in the number of tensor factors. Terms with a local ni=Nn_i=N are identically zero because the single head has dimension NN. Every other local denominator in (3) is a unit at the root. Thus (3) is also an explicit all-entry formula, with all original Cartan phases and no choice of a root divided by zero.

Define the equivalent first-normal operators

Jℰ,r=[ℰrN1−ζ2N]q,JF,r=[FrN1−ζ2N]q. J_{\mathcal E,r}=\left[\frac{\mathcal E_r^N}{1-\zeta^{2N}}\right]_q, \qquad J_{F,r}=\left[\frac{F_r^N}{1-\zeta^{2N}}\right]_q. \tag{4}

They equal their divided operators times the same nonzero scalar

αN=[[N]ζ!1−ζ2N]q=Nq−N(N−1)/2(1−Q)N. \alpha_N= \left[\frac{[N]_\zeta!}{1-\zeta^{2N}}\right]_q =\frac{Nq^{-N(N-1)/2}}{(1-Q)^N}. \tag{5}

They lower or raise integer degree by exactly NN. Every actual adjacent R±1R^{\pm1} commutes with these total operators: the literal finite generic RR intertwines (2), hence their powers, and scalar division followed by regular specialization proves the claim. This keeps the full original crossing, not its eigenvalue-only quotient.

2. An elementary divided commutation calculation

For the standard E=ℰ/(ζ−ζ−1)2E=\mathcal E/(\zeta-\zeta^{-1})^2, a vector of weight HH satisfies the generic finite identity

E[a]F[b]vH=∑t=0min⁡(a,b)[H+a−bt]ζF[b−t]E[a−t]vH. E^{[a]}F^{[b]}v_H =\sum_{t=0}^{\min(a,b)} {H+a-b\brack t}_\zeta F^{[b-t]}E^{[a-t]}v_H. \tag{6}

Negative upper Gaussian indices, when needed, are interpreted by their finite product. A direct proof starts with

EF[b]=F[b]E+F[b−1]ζ1−bK−ζb−1K−1ζ−ζ−1. EF^{[b]}=F^{[b]}E+ F^{[b-1]}\frac{\zeta^{1-b}K-\zeta^{b-1}K^{-1}} {\zeta-\zeta^{-1}} .

This is the sum of bb elementary commutators; the geometric sum is [b]ζ[b]_\zeta. Repeating it aa times, dividing by [a]ζ![a]_\zeta!, and grouping equal Fb−tEa−tF^{b-t}E^{a-t} terms gives (6) by the Gaussian Pascal recursion. Equivalently its Cartan coefficient is [K;2t−a−bt]{K;2t-a-b\brack t}. Acting AFTER E[a−t]E^{[a-t]} turns its upper integer into H+a−bH+a-b, which explains the weight in (6). This is a polynomial/rational identity derived from (1), not an assumption about root-module decomposition.

Now consider FOUR physical heads and the two specified degree spaces

W=(VN⊗4)2N−2,L=(VN⊗4)N−2. W=(V_N^{\otimes4})_{2N-2},\qquad L=(V_N^{\otimes4})_{N-2}. \tag{7}

Vectors of LL have weight H=2NH=2N. For a=b=Na=b=N, all matrices in (6) have the regular specialization (3). The root Gaussians obey

[2Nt]q=0(0<t<N),[2NN]q=2(−1)N. {2N\brack t}_{q}=0\quad(0<t<N),\qquad {2N\brack N}_{q}=2(-1)^N. \tag{8}

For a direct proof use ∏j=02N−1(1+Qjz)=(1−(−z)N)2\prod_{j=0}^{2N-1}(1+Q^jz)=(1-(-z)^N)^2 and compare coefficients; the symmetric Gaussian has the additional phase q−t(2N−t)q^{-t(2N-t)}. Also E4[N]L=0E_4^{[N]}L=0, simply because its output degree would be negative. Thus (6)–(8) give the exact RIGHT INVERSE

ℰ4[N]F4[N]|L=2(−1)Nρ2NIL,Jℰ,4JF,4|L=−2N2IL. \boxed{\quad \mathcal E_4^{[N]}F_4^{[N]}|_L =2(-1)^N\rho^{2N}I_L,\qquad J_{\mathcal E,4}J_{F,4}|_L=-2N^2I_L . \quad} \tag{9}

For the second equality use (5), 1−Q=−qρ,q−N(N−1)=(−1)N−11-Q=-q\rho,\ q^{-N(N-1)}=(-1)^{N-1}. Both signs in (9) include even levels. In particular Jℰ,4:W→LJ_{\mathcal E,4}:W\to L is surjective, with explicit right inverse −JF,4/(2N2)-J_{F,4}/(2N^2).

3. Original six-head invariants and extraction of this constraint

The complete original source construction, section 5, gives an isomorphism between the FULL root invariant space in six heads WITH unrolled H=0H=0 and WW. Throughout this note Inv⁡root\operatorname{Inv}_{\mathrm{root}} includes this H=0H=0 condition, not only the periodic equation K=1K=1. Write its map as Φ\Phi. Explicitly,

H2(w)=∑c=0d(−xQ)c(Q;Q)cℰ4cw⊗|c⟩,Φw=N∑a=0dxa−d(Q;Q)aF5aH2(w)⊗|d−a⟩. H_2(w)=\sum_{c=0}^d\frac{(-xQ)^c}{(Q;Q)_c} \mathcal E_4^cw\otimes|c\rangle,\qquad \Phi_w=N\sum_{a=0}^d \frac{x^{a-d}}{(Q;Q)_a}F_5^aH_2(w)\otimes|d-a\rangle . \tag{10}

𝖾𝗑𝗍(Jℰ,6Φw)=Jℰ,4w. \boxed{\qquad \mathsf{ext}\bigl(J_{\mathcal E,6}\Phi_w\bigr) =J_{\mathcal E,4}w .\qquad} \tag{11}

Here the extraction is also defined on the shifted output degree. 4. Equality with saturated generic source, at all levels

Let 𝒪=ℂ{ζ−q}\mathcal O=\mathbb C\{\zeta-q\} and 𝒦=Frac⁡𝒪\mathcal K=\operatorname{Frac}\mathcal O. Inside the free balanced six-head lattice take

Λ=Inv⁡𝒦(Vd(ζ)⊗6)∩𝒪N6,SN=Λ/(ζ−q)Λ. \Lambda=\operatorname{Inv}_{\mathcal K}(V_d(\zeta)^{\otimes6}) \cap\mathcal O^{N^6},\qquad S_N=\Lambda/(\zeta-q)\Lambda.

The generic-source saturation proof shows that SNS_N injects into the full root invariants, with dimension (N3+N)/2(N^3+N)/2. Saturation matters: Λ\Lambda is a free direct summand over this DVR, so reduction is injective. It is not the kernel obtained by substituting the root into a generic rank formula.

Every vector in Λ\Lambda is annihilated by the generic ordinary E6,F6E_6,F_6, hence by the regular divided operators (3). Therefore SN⊂ker⁡Jℰ,6∩ker⁡JF,6S_N\subset\ker J_{\mathcal E,6}\cap\ker J_{F,6}. By (11), 𝖾𝗑𝗍(SN)⊂ker⁡Jℰ,4\mathsf{ext}(S_N)\subset\ker J_{\mathcal E,4}. The actual dimensions and (9) are

dim⁡W=2N3+N3,dim⁡L=(N+13),dim⁡ker⁡Jℰ,4=N3+N2. \dim W=\frac{2N^3+N}{3},\qquad \dim L={N+1\choose3},\qquad \dim\ker J_{\mathcal E,4}=\frac{N^3+N}{2}.

The first is the central coefficient of (1+z+⋯+zd)4(1+z+\cdots+z^d)^4; the second counts the unconstrained compositions of N−2N-2 into four parts. The last now follows from PROVED surjectivity, not assumed flatness. Equal dimensions give the all-level identification

𝖾𝗑𝗍(SN)=ker⁡(Jℰ,4:W→L),SN=Inv⁡root∩ker⁡Jℰ,6=Inv⁡root∩ker⁡Jℰ,6∩ker⁡JF,6. \boxed{\quad \mathsf{ext}(S_N)=\ker(J_{\mathcal E,4}:W\to L),\qquad S_N=\operatorname{Inv}_{\mathrm{root}}\cap\ker J_{\mathcal E,6} =\operatorname{Inv}_{\mathrm{root}}\cap \ker J_{\mathcal E,6}\cap\ker J_{F,6}. \quad} \tag{12}

Thus the extra (N+13){N+1\choose3} root invariants are precisely the divided-current obstruction. No tilting decomposition, classical semisimplicity, or numerical braid-cyclic rank was used for equality.

5. Explicit source projection and its exact scope

On WW, define

𝖯=IW+JF,4Jℰ,42N2. \boxed{\qquad \mathsf P=I_W+\frac{J_{F,4}J_{\mathcal E,4}}{2N^2}. \qquad} \tag{13}

By (9), 𝖯2=𝖯\mathsf P^2=\mathsf P, Jℰ,4𝖯=0J_{\mathcal E,4}\mathsf P=0, and its range is exactly the source space (12). The complement is im⁡JF,4\operatorname{im}J_{F,4}, and its rank is (N+13){N+1\choose3}. This is an explicit SOURCE-INDEPENDENT algebraic projection defined by known tensor generators; it is not evaluation at an unknown invariant. It is generally oblique, and no polynomial bound on its norm is claimed. The orthogonal projection onto the same known kernel has norm one.

The original three-cup source lies in SNS_N. This follows either from its regular generic lift or directly: every nontrivial local divided power on a two-head cup is zero (ordinary powers by its Hopf equations, and the NN-th powers by physical degree). The block coproduct (3) then kills the three-cup tensor. Every literal signed braid preserves SNS_N by its regular generic intertwining. Any polynomial interval Casimir coupon has the same property. A complete circle has such a regular polynomial lift; no actual generic-qq pivot germ is replaced when using this statement AT THE FIXED ROOT.

Consequently 𝖯\mathsf P can be inserted between reduced gates on an original source orbit without changing any original exterior co-source. It need not commute with every reduced boundary generator on the UNRESTRICTED root space. An arbitrary root-only intertwiner without a regular generic lift is not asserted to preserve SNS_N.

All genuine original Casimir Hermite derivatives remain in the restricted braid/coupon operators. Restriction (12) removes root invariants absent from the generic source; it is not a semisimplification or an instruction to set their normal currents to zero. A derivative of the whole source in qq satisfies an INHOMOGENEOUS differentiated Ward equation; (12) is a root-value theorem and does not replace that equation by a homogeneous one.

For the six-head original plat, w0=p⊗pw_0=p\otimes p and λ=ℓ⊗ℓ\lambda=\ell\otimes\ell in WW. The literal marked cap is evaluated only after the whole invariant identification (10); it gives this λ\lambda, as already proved in the complete-source note. Physical orthogonal compression to (12) therefore preserves the word scalar and keeps the boundary Cauchy cost at most ∥w0∥∥λ∥=N2\|w_0\|\|\lambda\|=N^2. No new boundary normalization is inserted.

This supplies a concrete smaller six-port source for subsequent recoupling and energy estimates. It does not yet prove a positive geometric norm bound or a new noncyclic volume-equality class.
