A uniform all-index bound for the actual coherent pretzel inner packet

29 October 2026. The exact original-source Ward recurrence, together with its proved high-index source anchor, bounds EVERY remaining inner index. The estimate retains both classical characteristic branches, every endpoint factorial and both parities. Its prefactor is polynomial in N, uniformly even at r=0. No rational first-order factorization or choice of a single growing branch is assumed.

1. Literal selected packet and theorem

Use N>=2, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, M=floor((N-1)/2), and F_a=(Q;Q)_a. In the EXACT original rotated box, reversing the two finite sums gives the inner packet

Ar=∑v=rMQ−vrF2vFv3Fv−r,Dr=Q−r2F2rFr3,Ur=Ar/Dr,0≤r≤M. A_r=\sum_{v=r}^M Q^{-vr}\frac{F_{2v}}{F_v^3F_{v-r}}, \qquad D_r=Q^{-r^2}\frac{F_{2r}}{F_r^3}, \qquad U_r=A_r/D_r,\quad 0\leq r\leq M. \tag{1}

All D_r are nonzero, including r=0 and r=M. These are the same packets in the actual coherent inner elimination theorem, not an arbitrary solution of their recurrence. That finite source transformation proves, at BOTH parities,

|Ur|≤2N,⌈N/4⌉≤r≤M. |U_r|\leq2N,\qquad \lceil N/4\rceil\leq r\leq M. \tag{2}

Only this upper bound is needed here; its nonzero amplitude and phase are not substituted into a different index range. The same theorem proves the full selected Ward equation, with Y=Q^r,

QY2(1−Q2Y)Ar+2+Y(2Q3Y2+Q+1)Ar+1−Q(QY+1)(QY2−1)Ar=0, QY^2(1-Q^2Y)A_{r+2} +Y(2Q^3Y^2+Q+1)A_{r+1} -Q(QY+1)(QY^2-1)A_r=0, \tag{3}

and actual endpoints A_M=D_M, A_(M+1)=0.

Define the continuous nonnegative envelope

E(u)={2π∫uπ/4log⁡cot⁡vdv,0≤u≤π/4,0,π/4≤u≤π/2. E(u)=\begin{cases} \displaystyle\frac2\pi\int_u^{\pi/4}\log\cot v\,dv, &0\leq u\leq\pi/4,\\ 0, &\pi/4\leq u\leq\pi/2. \end{cases} \tag{4}

The integral is finite at zero. The explicit uniform bound is

|Ar|≤8e128N129|Dr|exp⁡{NE(πr/N)},N≥12,0≤r≤M. \boxed{|A_r|\leq8e^{128}N^{129}|D_r| \exp\{N E(\pi r/N)\}, \quad N\geq12,\quad0\leq r\leq M.} \tag{5}

The constants are deliberately coarse and independent of N,r. In particular the exponential error after this full two-branch envelope is uniformly o(N). This is a bound for the literal finite packet BEFORE the original outer Newton sum and Hermite source; no boundary packet or original current has been dropped.

2. Exact factorial cancellation in the normalized recurrence

The adjacent diagonal ratio is

Dr+1Dr=Q−1Y−2(1−QY2)(1+QY)(1−QY)2. \frac{D_{r+1}}{D_r} =Q^{-1}Y^{-2}\frac{(1-QY^2)(1+QY)}{(1-QY)^2}. \tag{6}

The factors 1-QY^2 and 1+QY are EXACTLY those in the backward coefficient of (3). Cancelling them algebraically, rather than estimating inverse factorials separately, gives

Ur=pN(Y)Ur+1+qN(Y)Ur+2, U_r=p_N(Y)U_{r+1}+q_N(Y)U_{r+2}, \tag{7} pN(Y)=−Q−2Y−1(2Q3Y2+Q+1)(1−QY)2,qN(Y)=−Q−4Y−2(1−Q3Y2)(1+Q2Y)(1−QY)2(1−Q2Y). p_N(Y)=-\frac{Q^{-2}Y^{-1}(2Q^3Y^2+Q+1)}{(1-QY)^2}, \quad q_N(Y)=-\frac{Q^{-4}Y^{-2}(1-Q^3Y^2)(1+Q^2Y)} {(1-QY)^2(1-Q^2Y)}. \tag{8}

For r=M-1 the numerator of q_N vanishes at BOTH parities: 1-Q^(2M+1)=0 for odd N, or 1+Q^(M+1)=0 for even N. Thus (7) extends to that last step with any assigned U_(M+1), for example zero. This is an exact cancellation of the terminal factorial zero, not division by D_(M+1). The proof below initializes instead at two genuine high-index U values in (2), so it needs no choice of a value beyond M.

3. Shifted classical matrix and a globally conditioned frame

Put R=ceil(N/4), delta=pi/N, and for 0<=r<R use

Z=Q^(r+1)=exp(2iu), u=(r+1)delta.

The shifted classical backward matrix is

T0(u)=(p0q010),p0=−Z−1(2Z2+2)(1−Z)2,q0=−Z−2(1+Z)2(1−Z)2. T_0(u)=\begin{pmatrix}p_0&q_0\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad p_0=-\frac{Z^{-1}(2Z^2+2)}{(1-Z)^2},\quad q_0=-\frac{Z^{-2}(1+Z)^2}{(1-Z)^2}. \tag{9}

On the unit circle its eigenvalues are

mu_1=-Z^(-1), mu_2=Z^(-1)c, c=cot(u)^2,
rho(u)=max(1,c).

They remain DISTINCT even where their moduli meet. Use the normalized right eigenvector matrix

V(u)=((−Z−1,1)𝖳2,(Z−1c,1)𝖳1+c2). V(u)=\left( \frac{(-Z^{-1},1)^{\mathsf T}}{\sqrt2}, \frac{(Z^{-1}c,1)^{\mathsf T}}{\sqrt{1+c^2}} \right). \tag{10}

Its column correlation is

(1-c)/sqrt(2(1+c^2)), whose modulus is at most1/sqrt2.

Therefore, UNIFORMLY for 0<u<pi/2,

∥V(u)∥2<2,∥V(u)−1∥2<2,∥V′(u)∥2≤4. \|V(u)\|_2<\sqrt2,\qquad \|V(u)^{-1}\|_2<2, \qquad \|V'(u)\|_2\leq4. \tag{11}

For the derivative, write the second column as (e^(-2iu)cos theta,sin theta), theta=atan(tan(u)^2). If t=tan u, then theta’=2t(1+t2)/(1+t4)<=2, since 1+t4-t-t3=(t-1)2(t2+t+1)>=0. Its squared derivative norm is at most8; the first column’s is2. Thus the Frobenius bound is sqrt10<4. This controls the frame all the way toward u=0; there is no hidden exponentially ill-conditioned change of basis.

4. The exact matrix has only a harmonic RELATIVE error

Let T_N(r) be the companion matrix from (7). In the shifted Z coordinate, its coefficients are

pN=−Z−1(2Z2+1+Q−1)(1−Z)2,qN=−Q−2Z−2(1−QZ2)(1+QZ)(1−Z)2(1−QZ). p_N=-\frac{Z^{-1}(2Z^2+1+Q^{-1})}{(1-Z)^2},\qquad q_N=-\frac{Q^{-2}Z^{-2}(1-QZ^2)(1+QZ)} {(1-Z)^2(1-QZ)}. \tag{12}

For N>=12 and r<R, u<=pi/3 and u+delta<pi/2. In particular |1+Z|^2>=1 and

|1-QZ|=2sin(u+delta)>=2sin u>=4(r+1)/N,
|Q-1|<=2pi/N.

The difference in p is exactly -Z(-1)(Q(-1)-1)/(1-Z)^2. For q use the elementary numerator identity

(1−QZ2)(1+QZ)−(1+Z)2(1−QZ)=(Q−1)Z(2+Z−QZ2). (1-QZ^2)(1+QZ)-(1+Z)^2(1-QZ) =(Q-1)Z(2+Z-QZ^2). \tag{13}

Dividing by rho>=c=|q_0| gives the uniform estimates

|p_N-p_0|/rho<=2pi/(r+1),
|q_N-q_0|/rho<=6pi/(r+1).

Hence

∥TN(r)−T0(u)∥2≤8πr+1ρ(u). \|T_N(r)-T_0(u)\|_2\leq\frac{8\pi}{r+1}\rho(u). \tag{14}

Large absolute entries near r=0 have NOT been called small. They are controlled relative to the full eigenvalue envelope, whose large second branch is kept. Combining (11), (14) and the step u->u+delta proves

∥V(u)−1TN(r)V(u+δ)∥2≤ρ(u)(1+128r+1). \|V(u)^{-1}T_N(r)V(u+\delta)\|_2 \leq\rho(u)\left(1+\frac{128}{r+1}\right). \tag{15}

Indeed the frame change contributes at most8pi/N; the error term contributes at most16sqrt2*pi/(r+1). Their sum is less than 128/(r+1). This argument uses a full operator-norm bound and therefore allows arbitrary mixing of the two characteristic branches; it does not posit adiabatic survival of either one.

5. Selected high-index boundary to all-index estimate

Write X_r=(U_r,U_(r+1))^T and X_r=T_N(r)X_(r+1). For N>=12 both R and R+1 are within the high range in (2). Thus ||X_R||<=2sqrt2 N. Iterating (15), using (11) at both ends, gives

|Ur|≤8N∏j=rR−1ρ((j+1)π/N)(1+128j+1). |U_r|\leq8N \prod_{j=r}^{R-1}\rho((j+1)\pi/N) \left(1+\frac{128}{j+1}\right). \tag{16}

The second product is at most exp(128 H_N)<=e^128 N^128. The logarithm of the first is the RIGHT Riemann sum of the nonnegative decreasing function max(0,2log cot u). Even for r=0 it is bounded ABOVE by its finite improper integral:

sum_(j=r)^(R-1) log rho((j+1)pi/N)
   <=(N/pi) integral_(pi*r/N)^(pi/4) 2log cot u du
   =N E(pi*r/N).

This proves (5) for r<R. Equation (2) proves it for r>=R. The argument is uniform at all indices, with no epsilon-dependent unstated endpoint constants.

In particular, after the existing uniform sine-factorial estimate for D_r is applied, the whole coherent inner packet has precisely the two-branch upper envelope. Its lower-index excess is E(u); it is NOT assumed to be zero. Whether the positive outer Newton profile absorbs that excess is a separate variational inequality. This theorem supplies the source-faithful estimate needed for that comparison; it does not alone prove augmented-base survival, a volume LOWER bound, or a filling theorem.

6. Validation

The audit checks the original finite sum, selected Ward boundaries, exact normalized recurrence and endpoint cancellation, both high-range anchors, shifted coefficient differences, uniformly conditioned moving frame and its one-step bound, and the full coherent all-index envelope at both parities. Tests never replace the selected solution by an arbitrary recurrence branch.
