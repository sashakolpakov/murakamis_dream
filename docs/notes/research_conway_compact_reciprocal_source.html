A compact unweighted reciprocal integral for the original Conway source

24 November 2026. Research proof.

A known polynomial change of the normalization budgets makes the positive source integral compact and unweighted after all linearly entering packets have been integrated out. One packet per binary product remains. Its polynomial residual vector retains the original scalar closing, including its phases and all first-normal directions. For a fixed tree the compact integral approximates the reciprocal squared normalized source with relative error O(N−1)O(N^{-1}).

The proof also works in any prescribed positive packet metrics after consistent normalization. It does not evaluate the compact integral from hyperbolic geometry or prove a new volume lower bound.

1. The source and the polynomial normalization

Fix a literal Conway tree with s≥1s\ge1 proper vertices, L≥1L\ge1 prescribed leaf occurrences and p=L−1p=L-1 binary products. Every packet is the complete NN-dimensional physical algebra 𝒜N\mathcal A_N, N≥2N\ge2, including the independent first normals at double Casimir roots. Use the original products, genuine turns and original scalar closing of [P]. The leaf choices may be arbitrary complex physical packets, including prescribed short fillings.

Initially choose known positive budgets bvb_v, including the scalar root budget, so that the normalized leaves, turns, bilinear products and closing row have their respective norms at most 1/161/16. The product norm here is the supremum over two separately unit inputs. No small coefficient-tensor Hilbert–Schmidt norm is assumed. The physical budgets of [P] give one such choice. Write BN=∏vbv,b̂v=Nbv,B̂N=Ns+1BN,σN=116N.(1) B_N=\prod_v b_v,\qquad \widehat b_v=Nb_v,\qquad \widehat B_N=N^{s+1}B_N,\qquad \sigma_N={\frac{1}{16N}}. \qquad\text{(1)} There are ss proper budgets and one scalar root budget. In the rest of the proof use these new budgets, and abbreviate σ=σN\sigma=\sigma_N. Their normalized maps are Φv\Phi_v; their normalized original closing row is cc, extended by zero off the top packet. All their respective norms are at most σ\sigma. The original framing, mark and external scalars remain in these maps.

Choose orthonormal coordinates on every packet and product real Lebesgue measure. Put D=NsD=Ns, and set Gv(x)=Φv((xj)j child of v)−xv,EN(x)=∥G(x)∥2+|cx|2,𝒫N=D!πD∫ℂDEN(x)−D−1dx.(2) G_v(x)=\Phi_v((x_j)_{j\text{ child of }v})-x_v,\qquad E_N(x)=\|G(x)\|^2+|cx|^2,\qquad \mathcal P_N={\frac{D!}{\pi^D}}\int_{\mathbb C^D}E_N(x)^{-D-1}dx. \qquad\text{(2)} This is the reciprocal construction with the newly recorded budgets; its numerical value need not equal an earlier normalized integral. The exact forward field aa satisfies G(a)=0G(a)=0. 2. The compact polynomial integral

At every binary product retain exactly one child occurrence, as in [L,F]. These are pp distinct packets because each proper packet has a unique parent. Write their coordinates as u∈ℂnu\in\mathbb C^n, n=Npn=Np, and the remaining coordinates as z∈ℂhz\in\mathbb C^h, h=D−nh=D-n. The top packet is among the eliminated variables zz.

At fixed uu, every product is linear in its remaining variable. 5. Metric choice, normalization and the remaining lower bound

One may prescribe any positive Hermitian metric on each complete packet space, independently of the unknown closed value. In its orthonormal coordinates choose known base budgets making the actual leaf, linear, bilinear and closing norms at most 1/161/16, then use (1). The unique-parent arguments, triangular real Jacobian one, finite-fiber degree bound and affine proof remain unchanged. The measure and every norm in (2)–(7) must be rebuilt consistently. No comparison with the old packet metrics is required.

The added budget factor is exactly Ns+1N^{s+1}. For a fixed tree its logarithm is o(N)o(N); every actual budget and external phase still has to be restored. Neither the numerical integral nor its polynomial residual vector is claimed to be metric independent. The denominator exponent n+1n+1 grows with the level.

The improvement is removal of the unbounded retained integration domain and its Gram determinant after a specified polynomial normalization, together with a short two-sided comparison in those same metrics. The fixed-ball affine argument is already in [R]; [U] proves an undamped physical comparison with unchanged budgets. Here no physical shrinking-row estimate or Wick coefficient estimate is needed. The present proof does not improve their original normalization constants.

To prove a geometric lower bound one still needs a geometric upper estimate on 𝒥N/B̂N2\mathcal J_N/\widehat B_N^2. The original signed closing remains a component of FF; a zero source gives an infinite integral. Compactness and positivity alone supply no such upper estimate. Both its dimension n=Npn=Np and denominator exponent n+1n+1 still grow with the level; the prefactor n!/πnn!/\pi^n remains. This is not a fixed-dimensional Laplace reduction or an identification with a classical angle integral. There is no new arbitrary-short-filling or finite-exception volume theorem.
