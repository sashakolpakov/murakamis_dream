Closed products and Gaussian weights for the full Conway tensor

Written research proof, 22 November 2026.

The generating polynomial of the entire centered Conway tensor has a single finite sum over the physical Casimir nodes. Every summand uses explicit finite products and a quadratic Gaussian crossing weight. The first-normal contribution is explicit as well. In particular this does not replace the repeated-root algebra by its ordinary values.

The result generates all three-index coefficients algebraically and gives a uniform complex upper bound for their generating polynomial on fixed discs. It does not estimate their coherent contraction with a short component or prove an action-relative geometric bound.

The remaining coefficient vanishes in the full quotient and has degree at most NN in zz, hence is a scalar multiple of PNP_N. We will repeatedly use coefficientwise uniqueness in tt. For a polynomial X(t)X(t) of degree at most dd, its constant term and the right side of (4) determine every other coefficient: in degree tat^a, 1≤a<N1\le a<N, the coefficient of the new unknown is 1−ha≠01-h^a\ne0. 3. The full first normal

6. An explicit cyclic rank-one equation

The finite forcing also determines the exact cyclic compatibility condition. Put a=q−1a=q^{-1} and g(t,z)=1+zt+t2(1+at)2. g(t,z)=\frac{1+zt+t^2}{(1+at)^2}. 7. A uniform complex bound for the full generating tensor

Fix 0<r<10<r<1. Define the dilogarithm only on its convergent disc, Li⁡2(w)=∑k≥1wk/k2\operatorname{Li}_2(w)=\sum_{k\ge1}w^k/k^2, and set 𝒮(v,t)=Li⁡2(−vt)+Li⁡2(−v−1t)−2Li⁡2(−t)2πi,|v|=1,|t|≤r.(26) \mathcal S(v,t)= \frac{\operatorname{Li}_2(-vt)+\operatorname{Li}_2(-v^{-1}t) -2\operatorname{Li}_2(-t)}{2\pi i}, \qquad |v|=1,\quad |t|\le r. \qquad\text{(26)} Every factor in (9) is zero-free on this disc. Here the complex logarithm, not just its real part, is controlled. Write ft(u)=log⁡(1+teiu)f_t(u)=\log(1+te^{iu}). Its size and first derivative are uniformly bounded for |t|≤r|t|\le r. 8. Scope and complete sources

The three-index Fourier calculation has been replaced by one physical node sum of closed products and their specified polynomial derivatives. There is no unknown source coefficient in (22). This is not a low-rank replacement of the NN-dimensional boundary algebra; the generating polynomial still contains all N3N^3 tensor coefficients. The original turns, bilinear gluing and marked closure remain those of the centered compiler.

No positive measure is assigned to the Gaussian-weighted node sum. No individual-entry asymptotic, geometric norm, source-cycle estimate, or new volume-conjecture class is inferred. The valid complex bound (29) takes absolute values of the node terms and is not claimed to retain their cancellations or be sharp. All derivatives here are the original spectral Hermite normals; no moving-qq or peripheral-normal extension is asserted.
