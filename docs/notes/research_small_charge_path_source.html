Finite quantum sums at small Fourier charges

Research proof, 3 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

This note proves a nonzero leading asymptotic for a family of finite quantum sums. Start with the original edge kernels and traces, and insert the Chebyshev factor TN−jv(−wv/2)T_{N-j_v}(-w_v/2) at each traced vertex. We call this inserted factor a coupon. Its Fourier charge is χv=jv/N\chi_v=j_v/N; at jv=Nj_v=N, the factor is T0=1T_0=1, so the insertion disappears.

For every fixed finite graph, with no isolated traced vertex, and arbitrary fixed integer crossing powers, the sum has a unique leading critical point when all charges belong to [1/4000,1/2000][1/4000,1/2000]. Its coefficient is explicit and nonzero for both parities of NN. Loops and repeated edges are allowed. For paths whose powers are positive and at least three, the same conclusion holds on the larger interval [1/640,1/320][1/640,1/320].

Sections 1–6 prove the positive-path result. Section 7 allows negative and zero powers, and Section 8 proves the graph extension. These are statements about the specified finite sums: an arbitrary graph is not asserted to represent a knot invariant. The proof initializes charge continuation near the complete point; it does not continue the nonzero coefficient to charge one.
