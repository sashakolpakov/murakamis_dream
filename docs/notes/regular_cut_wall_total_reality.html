Regular simple cut-wall stationarity is full stationarity

28 October 2026. A regular simple wall of the ACTUAL global radius homotopy has a free radial coordinate. Together with its remaining head angles, Gaussian paths and interior cap paths, its tangent vectors span the full complex tangent space. Therefore complex phase stationarity on this wall is automatically full holomorphic stationarity. On a regular all-pole branch this removes the extra “full critical” hypothesis from the regular simple-wall value bound.

This does not identify the original geometric-thimble coefficient. It does not cover simultaneous corners of the one-parameter homotopy, dependent walls, endpoints, nonsmooth flow cells or escaping variables. Nor does a maximum of only the real exponential rate imply the complex stationarity used here.

The audit and results verify the implicit radial identity and the exact complex Jacobians, including arbitrary radial dependence of the head radii and the distinction between a free multiwall chart and a one-parameter corner.

1. The actual simple-wall radial coordinate is free

Use the global head-radius homotopy (4) in the exact cut-wall pushforward. For a simple ray pole, write

T_j=Log t_j=tau+i epsilon*pi, tau=log r,
U=Log A_j=a.Z+b.E+i*pi*nu,
Z=rho+i theta.                                  (1)

Here a is the primitive nonzero integer head-incidence row; E denotes the Gaussian log variables. The original atomic ratio connects two different retained head segments. All other moment and Gaussian parameters are denoted y for the moment.

The radii are independent of HEAD ANGLES. On a smooth flow cell set

rho_s=(1-s)lambda_0(tau,y)+s lambda_*(y).

The pole’s real equation is

F(s,tau,y)=a.rho_s+Re(b.E)-tau=0.                (2)

A REGULAR SIMPLE crossing means F_s is nonzero and 0<s<1; all other pole factors are regular on this local stratum. The real implicit function theorem solves s=s(tau,y). Thus tau is a genuinely free local parameter, not a constrained boundary value. In particular

a.rho_tau=1.                                   (3)

The imaginary pole equation is a.theta+Im(b.E)+pinu=epsilonpi. It leaves n-1 independent head angles. The dependence of lambda_0 on tau and on the other moment radii causes no difficulty: it enters the implicit function and off-diagonal derivatives, while (3) remains exact.

2. The unreduced residue chain is maximal totally real

Complete a to complex linear head coordinates (U,Y), keeping E as an additional independent coordinate. Let V be a real basis of ker a. On the wall choose

theta=theta_0(E)+V phi, phi in R^(n-1),
U=tau+i epsilon*pi.                             (4)

Every other moment T_k follows its original circle or ray, with a nonzero tangent derivative; each Gaussian E follows a regular complex path, and each interior cap variable p=2pii*s follows its regular complex path. Order the complex coordinates as

(U, T_other, E, p, Y)

and the real parameters as

(tau, moment parameters, Gaussian parameters, cap parameters, phi).

Their complex Jacobian is block triangular. Its diagonal consists of 1, the nonzero moment/Gaussian/cap path derivatives, and i C V, where C is the chosen complementary head-coordinate matrix. The matrix C V is invertible. Thus the Jacobian is NONZERO. All head-radius derivatives in tau or the other base variables occur only in lower off-diagonal blocks. The residue chain is therefore maximal totally real in its complex residue manifold.

For a holomorphic phase S on that manifold, dS vanishing on all real tangent directions consequently implies dS=0 on the full complex tangent space. This follows by multiplying its complex derivative row by the invertible Jacobian. It requires stationarity of the COMPLEX phase, not just a stationary real-part envelope.

This unreduced statement concerns the phase with independent other moment variables and their Cauchy poles. It does NOT yet identify it with the nine-atom W(A), or infer actual knot holonomy before those other atomic variables are pushed forward.

3. The regular all-pole branch gives the actual nine-atom conclusion

Now restrict to a regular local branch on which the OTHER atomic moments are pushed to their Cauchy poles, with no additional local wall crossing. In a regular simple cut stratum these other pole arguments are off their negative-ray cuts. Their circle Cauchy reductions can retain the head variables; locally freeze the radius labels at the circle radii while taking these normal residues. Their normal degree is one, but no global thimble-degree claim is made.

The remaining cycle has head profile rho(tau,E) independent of the free head angles, with a.rho+Re(b.E)=tau. Its coordinates are now (U,E,p,Y) and its parameters (tau, Gaussian paths, cap paths,phi). The same block-triangular proof gives a nonzero complex Jacobian. Thus an INTERIOR stationary point of the actual wall action W_m on this regular simple-wall chain automatically satisfies

gradient W_m=0.                                (5)

Here W_m is the genuine nine-atom action with its integer norm charge, Gaussian and original coupled cap phases. This is precisely the case covered by the calibrated critical regulator and the sharp representation-volume bound. Consequently every such regular simple-wall interior stationary value satisfies

|Im W_m|<=Vol(M).                              (6)

No extra independent “full critical” assumption is required for these regular simple interiors. Additional local pole crossings, rank-deficient Cauchy reductions and endpoints are excluded from this inference.

4. Several genuinely free independent walls

The linear-algebra statement extends if k wall rows form a real rank-k matrix A and the stratum ACTUALLY has k free radial variables tau=(tau_1,…,tau_k), with

A.rho+ell(E)=tau,  A.theta+arg(E)=constant.

Choose n-k remaining angles in ker A. In coordinates consisting of the k logarithmic wall monomials, the Gaussian/cap variables and a complementary head basis, the Jacobian has diagonal blocks I_k, the nonzero path derivatives and i I_(n-k). It is invertible, so restricted complex stationarity is full stationarity there too.

Rank A=k ALONE does not establish these free radial parameters in the actual common tube current. A simultaneous k-fold intersection of our SINGLE-parameter radius homotopy imposes k real modulus equations on k radii and the one homotopy parameter. In general this leaves only one free radial combination and k-1 further relations. It is a corner stratum, not the free multiwall chart just proved. Dependent walls, tangential crossings F_s=0, intersections on a flow cell edge, terminal homotopy faces, cap/ray endpoints and escapes still need their own source-preserving resolution. Nothing here deletes any of their original currents.

5. Transverse intersections: no extra exact corner residue, but a growth caveat

There is a useful exact-current refinement. Suppose k distinct atomic pole divisors meet the homotopy TRANSVERSELY as 2k real equations, at a finite smooth point. Use their complex normal values q_1,…,q_k as real normal coordinates on that homotopy. On a compact patch the form has a smooth numerator divided by q_1…q_k.

In a region where all |q_j| are comparable to epsilon, a rounded simultaneous tube boundary has 2k-1 normal real tangent directions. Its pulled-back form scales at most epsilon(2k-1-k)=epsilon(k-1). For k>=2 its integral tends to zero at fixed N. On each simple wall, the other transverse factors 1/q_j are locally L1 because their normal directions have two real dimensions. Hence the exact total current can be described by simple-wall residues with these L1 kernels; there is no additional independent transverse corner residue. This argument requires actual real transversality of the restricted pole equations, not just distinct divisors or a claimed independence of the moment labels.

This DOES NOT remove transverse intersections from asymptotic growth. Local L1 meromorphic singularities can carry an exponential rate without a full holomorphic critical point. For real a>0 and c,

I_N=integral_R^2 exp(N(c-x^2-y^2+i*a*x))/(x+i*y) dxdy
   =2*pi*i/(N*a) exp(N*c)(1-exp(-N*a^2/4)).       (7)

The pole is locally L1, and its real normal map (x,y)->x+iy is transverse. The only regular holomorphic critical value of S(Z)=c-Z_12-Z_22+ia*Z_1 is c-a^2/4. On the complex pole divisor Z_1+i Z_2=0 its restriction is c+a Z_2, with NO critical point. Nevertheless (7) has exponential rate c, arising at the real chain’s pole intersection. Thus L1 integrability and absence of an independent exact corner term do not give a sharp exponential bound from the regular critical values alone.

For a direct proof of (7), use polar coordinates. The angular integral is 2pii J_1(Nar), obtained by differentiating the angular definition of J_0. Integration by parts reduces the radial integral to the two-dimensional Gaussian Fourier identity and gives integral_0^infinity exp(-Nr^2)J_1(Nar)dr =(1-exp(-Na^2/4))/(N*a). Everything is absolutely integrable, including the pole. The audit checks the resulting identity independently.

This is a LOCAL ANALYTIC countermodel, not a claimed knot-source counterexample. It shows precisely why a further source identity or phase-specific bound is required before eliminating transverse meromorphic intersections from the remaining growth analysis.
