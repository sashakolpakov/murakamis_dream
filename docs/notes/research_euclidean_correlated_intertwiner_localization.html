Actual Euclidean off-matching windows and their Ward current

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

An actual total intertwiner can be restricted by a correlated coherent window which keeps the entire real lowering-observable matching locus and every occupation endpoint. The discarded operator and the resulting Ward current have exponentially small trace norm relative to the norm of the original whole operator. The estimate is in the physical coefficient Euclidean metric, with the actual meridian family and caps.

This is a positive norm estimate for an explicit marked operator. Its budget is the whole-operator norm, not a selected geometric contribution. It neither isolates a geometric component nor estimates the contribution of the original relative cycle.

The complete directs W and B, pinned below, supply the actual source comparison and its Euclidean boundary-kernel bridge. The localization argument is proved here from the finite lowering matrices; no estimate in the binomial Hilbert metric is transferred to this metric.
