Volume equality for the original untwisted augmented source

30 October 2026. This theorem concerns the original complete-circle source of every fixed connected augmented plat carrying the stated labeled right-angled geometric certificate, with NO retained half twists. Its bridge width and matching graph are arbitrary. Both quantum parities and composite levels occur. The source is exactly the original marked root invariant with its recorded normalization; its value is not chosen using the geometric answer.

This is a theorem about complete augmented LINK exteriors. It does not assert the volume conjecture for general long-filled k-bridge knots. In a standard adjacent-cap2k-plat the zero-half-twist skeleton has k main components. A full twist cannot change that component count. Thus the untwisted result must not be advertised as that knot theorem.

1. Precise statement

Fix the literal connected2k-plat box diagram D, k>=2, with R designated crossing circles and its original physical mark. Require a labeled convex ideal right-angled polyhedron Q_D whose face identifications are the actual projection/crossing-disk cut of this diagram. The actual quotient is its complete hyperbolic augmented exterior Y_D and

Vol(Y_D)=2Vol(Q_D).

This is the marked geometric certificate, not merely the existence of an unlabeled polyhedron or a positive triangulation of unspecified topology. There is no restriction on R, bridge width, a Conway grammar, or an octahedral decomposition.

Let A_(D,N) be the original marked complete-circle root source of the full matching-graph identity, with epsilon_i=0 at every box. Write chi_(D,N) for its separately specified exterior framing factor (one when absent); it has modulus1 in the recorded normalization and is retained below. Then

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N) log|A_(D,N)|
                            =Vol(Y_D).             (1)

In particular A_(D,N) is nonzero for all sufficiently large N. The limit runs over all positive integer levels, including even and composite levels. It is not an iterated limit in which the diagram grows with N.

2. The exact positive finite source

Set d=N-1 and M=floor(d/2). The marked face-coefficient theorem gives EXACTLY

A_(D,N)=chi_(D,N) N^R sum_(a in {0,...,M}^R)
             product_i[k_ai beta_ai^2] C_(N,a),    (2)
C_(N,a)=[product_(f!=u,v)L_f^d]
             product_i[D_ai,in D_ai,out P_si] at L_u=L_v=0.

All C_(N,a) are nonnegative, so positivity concerns the scalar after the displayed common unit phase is removed. The original mark removes exactly the two face exponents u,v, and total polynomial degree dR excludes every additional Fourier alias. The primitive face grid supplies precisely N^R after its original single1/N marked factor. The complete-circle normal is already in k_ai beta_ai^2. No root-only replacement for an uncomputed generic normal is made here.

The finite identity also holds for several main components. It requires connected projection and the literal single marked cap. It does not replace that mark by independent reduced normalizations on each link component.

3. Lower bound from an actual positive coefficient

Choose the single original label vector a_i=M at every circle. The global coefficient saddle theorem applies through both folds. It proves a positive coefficient with an exact compact-torus integral, a unique positive critical radius near the actual circle packing, and a nonzero relative Gaussian asymptotic. Its absolute action is computed from the source determinant and the actual facet coning:

lim (1/N)log C_(N,(M,...,M))=Vol(Y_D)/(2pi).         (3)

At the fold beta_M has zero normalized logarithmic rate, k_M is1 or2, and the factor N^R is polynomial. After removing the same unit phase, every other term of (2) is nonnegative. Keeping this ONE actual term therefore proves the required lower bound. No local-contour contribution or cancellation coefficient is left to assume in this step.

4. Upper bound for the whole original sum

Evaluate all face polynomials at the SAME finite positive circle-packing radii l_f, with L_u=L_v=0. Positivity gives for each exact coefficient

C_(N,a)<= product_i[D_ai,in(l)D_ai,out(l)P_si(l)]
                                  /product_f l_f^d. (4)

The uniform channel-rate theorem keeps the original beta factors and proves a uniform logarithmic rate for every integer a_i, including0 and both folds. At these shared radii the local circle rate is strictly increasing in mu_i=a_i/N up to1/2. Its derivative is the explicit positive quantity

log[E_b(mu_i)E_c(mu_i)/D_xy(mu_i)].

Thus (4), WITH the beta factors in (2), has normalized rate at most the all-fold value plus o(1), uniformly over all label vectors. The actual coning identity fixes that value absolutely as Vol(Y_D)/(2pi). There are only (M+1)^R channel vectors, the multiplicities are at most 2^R, and the outside N^R is polynomial. Summing the upper bounds does not change their exponential rate. This proves the matching upper bound and hence (1).

The same argument gives a strict macroscopic channel gap: for each fixed eta>0, terms having some a_i/N<=1/2-eta have a smaller common-radius upper rate by a positive diagram-dependent amount. It does not by itself give a relative asymptotic in the sqrt(N) folded window.

5. What remains for the requested knot theorem

Retained half twists preserve the exact positive coefficients but attach the original phases product_i omega_ai^epsilon_i to different channels. The upper bound above survives. Positivity no longer supplies the lower bound for that signed sum. A uniform relative folded-window estimate and a nonzero signed Gaussian coefficient are still required generally.

Long fillings require a further source theorem in the EXTERNAL crossing-circle colors. These are different from the internal a_i in (2). The existing exact long-filling transform and marked geometric filling threshold do not supply that mixed-color packet or its alias gap. The target remains: every fixed essential hyperbolic2k-plat skeleton, with independently sufficiently long FIXED signed full twists, has the original knot volume limit. This note establishes the complete untwisted augmented-source theorem and a general signed-source upper bound; it does not mark that larger target as solved.

6. Dependencies and verification scope

The proof combines exact source normalization/support/measure, a global positive coefficient saddle, a uniform all-channel upper rate, and an absolute geometric coning identity. Each dependency has a separate written proof and focused original-source audit. No finite set of knot examples, estimated limiting graph values, or unverified literature proof replaces any of these steps. No novelty or literature-coverage claim is made by this note.

The result is the stated complete untwisted augmented-link theorem. It does not settle the general signed long-filled knot conjecture.

The primary-statement coverage comparison records prior overlapping families and root conventions. In particular, the parallel-belt examples with even-level zeros fail the required nondegenerate cut certificate; the exact coefficient formula reproduces their zeros. No external proof or priority claim is used.
