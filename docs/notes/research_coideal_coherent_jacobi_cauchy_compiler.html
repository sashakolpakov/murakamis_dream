A common orthogonal basis for coherent crossing amplitudes

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof.

For fixed actual neighboring heights, one real symmetric Jacobi matrix handles every input label in the cooriented crossing block. Its orthogonal basis is independent of that input. Consequently the actual sum over inputs is an exact finite Cauchy transform in each output mode, and its full cross-input Gram is a divided difference of a matrix resolvent. Here coherent means that the input sum is taken before an absolute square.

These are exact finite source identities. The complementary input can contribute to the same output modes and remains inside those squares. Neither positivity of the Gram nor polynomial conditioning of the Jacobi matrix gives injectivity or a polynomial lower bound for that input map.

This is not asserted to be a peripheral parameter or an off-grid continuation of the entire original quantum source. The uniform condition of A_p does not imply injectivity or polynomial conditioning of K, its input Gram, or a coherent scalar observation.

The boundary output rows and the complementary output rows are retained literally as well. There is no orthogonal separation by input support, and no discarded leakage block. Nor is symmetry of any complementary physical block inferred by numerically ordering a,b.

5. Exact sewing and its limits

These full conditional unitaries may be used as coordinate changes at arbitrary stages of an actual finite composition. Interfaces are products of the neighboring full basis changes and hence are unitary, even for overlapping faces. They are retained as operators; they need not be the identity or preserve the diagonal Cauchy variables of the next crossing. For a full basis change V, the input becomes V^dagger u, the literal closing row becomes ell V, and any source constraint or projector P becomes V^dagger P V. No commutation or invariance of P is asserted. Thus the original scalar, marked position and external factor chi_N are unchanged. The source and cap norms already proved in H are unchanged in G_ht. All Casimir-normal data remain in these invertible full changes; no value-only quotient or source projection is inserted.

This is not a claim that the braid operators themselves are unitary, or that G_ht is the physical Hilbert metric. It gives no additive rank bound for a whole word: consecutive Jacobi generators and their conditional bases depend on different neighboring heights. The exact cross-input Gram and the transformed marked row may still have a small or zero coherent overlap. No phase alignment, geometric cycle, global closed-scalar lower bound or arbitrary-short-filling theorem follows.

J already treats a different original Newton-value Jacobi problem, its Fourier image and the distinct normal-weighted final observation. The abstract spectral theorem, resolvent identity and positive Gram identity are standard finite linear algebra. The additional content here is their exact common-output realization for all inputs of this coideal crossing rectangle, with the rank-at-most-two source forcing, full cross-input terms and literal source/closing transport. It does not replace the original global minimization by independent column energies.
