Adversarial audit of the source-volume Casson–Rivin argument

29 October 2026. The filled-volume identification uses several geometric and source inputs before strict concavity. This note explains those dependencies and distinguishes the complete augmented volume from the volume after filling.

1. The two volumes must remain distinct

The notation VsV_s is the volume of the COMPLETE AUGMENTED base, with its crossing-circle cusps UNFILLED. The source limit for the actual filled link is

Vsrc,s(𝒑)=2πRe⁡𝒜s(𝒑). V_{\mathrm{src,s}}(\boldsymbol p)=2\pi\operatorname{Re}\mathcal A_s(\boldsymbol p).

The whole-source theorem establishes this number as the original quantum limit before asserting geometric equality. It is not set equal to VsV_s. Indeed local charge selection, sections 2–3, gives a strict source action drop for every sufficiently long finite tuple:

Vsrc,s(𝒑)<Vs,Vsrc,s(𝒑)→Vsas maxi|pi|−1→0. V_{\mathrm{src,s}}(\boldsymbol p)<V_s,\qquad V_{\mathrm{src,s}}(\boldsymbol p)\longrightarrow V_s \quad\text{as }\max_i|p_i|^{-1}\longrightarrow0 . \tag{1}

The derivative estimate there, equation (8), proves the strict inequality; it is stronger than an expansion with an unquantified remainder. For orientation, its quadratic formula (11) reads

Vsrc,s(𝒑)=Vs−2π2Im⁡τs∑i|1+2piτs|−2+Os(∥𝒑−1∥4). V_{\mathrm{src,s}}(\boldsymbol p)=V_s- 2\pi^2\operatorname{Im}\tau_s \sum_i|1+2p_i\tau_s|^{-2} +O_s(\|\boldsymbol p^{-1}\|^4). \tag{2}

This is only a local expansion. Equality with the ACTUAL filled hyperbolic volume requires the further geometric argument below.

2. What proves the source has a positive interior angle point

The dependencies in the Casson–Rivin note are:

  1. Original source selection, not formal gluing. Its section 1 invokes whole-source survival, sections 1–2. This establishes the exact original invariant’s nonzero full-sequence asymptotic and hence its rate VsrcV_{\mathrm{src}}. The color complement, all aliases, original normal terms, endpoint currents and noncancellation are analytic premises. Concavity does not prove them.
  2. An actual marked representation near a known geometric point. The literal port theorem, sections 1–6, supplies the source-selected ρz\rho_z, its cap order, preferred peripheral words, and the COMPLETE geometric anchor ρ0\rho_0. This uses the explicit augmented geometry. It does not assume that a filled ρ𝒑\rho_{\boldsymbol p} is geometric.
  3. A positive chart at the COMPLETE base. Casson–Rivin section 2 chooses a fixed positive geometric ideal triangulation of that complete manifold. The CURRENT class-specific input is the direct positive ideal-angle certificate, sections 1–3: exact homogeneous lifts of the actual polyhedra, all original white/shaded half-twist face maps, and finite compatible affine subdivision give this triangulation directly. Its section 4 also checks the full edge/peripheral tangent rank. The former Ge audit, sections 1–3, conditional on Epstein–Penner, remains an alternative route; it is no longer a dependency for this actual class. The direct proof asserts no universal all-ideal existence theorem for an arbitrary augmented base, and is not itself a quantum source-selection theorem.
  4. Continuity, not an assumed maximizer. Equivariantly move those finitely many ideal vertices using the selected peripheral flags. They vary continuously from the complete noncentral parabolic flags. Cross-ratios stay in the upper half-plane in one fixed neighborhood. Exact representation relations give the complex edge equations; their angular lifts remain 2π2\pi. The source’s inverse-slope implicit-function argument puts every sufficiently long independently signed tuple in that neighborhood. It follows that the actual source point is in the RELATIVE INTERIOR of the prescribed affine angle slice.

Thus the interior critical point is not obtained from the statement “a concave functional has a maximum.” It is supplied by an actual source-selected representation which is a small deformation of an independently identified positive complete geometry. None of these steps assumes the desired filled-volume equality.

The alternative finite-center core construction, sections 2–5, and the general equivariant averaging construction, sections 2–5, prove the geometric completion without requiring the all-ideal triangulation input. They are sufficient for final equality even when one does not use the Casson–Rivin angle presentation.

3. The constrained functional is concave and uses FILLED slopes

The actual domain in Casson–Rivin section 3 is the positive angle polytope with tetrahedral sums π\pi, edge sums 2π2\pi, and

Im⁡(Ui+piVi)=−2π,Ui=−zi,Vi=−2ℓi, \operatorname{Im}(U_i+p_iV_i)=-2\pi,\qquad U_i=-z_i,\quad V_i=-2\ell_i , \tag{3}

on each filled auxiliary cusp. The logarithms are the SMALL centered PROJECTIVE logs. An old SL longitude sign is not added as a further geometric rotation. A remaining main cusp has its chosen meridian angle zero; the source representation in fact has both its peripheral logs zero. With two main components, the corresponding conditions are imposed on BOTH unfilled cusps.

Equation (3) is the all-positive unit charge convention. The other minimal charge signs use their corresponding eigenflag marks. The existence/noncancellation of the selected contribution was already proved in the quantum source theorem.

For tetrahedral angles α+β+γ=π\alpha+\beta+\gamma=\pi, let

𝒱(θ)=∑t[Λ(αt)+Λ(βt)+Λ(γt)]. \mathcal V(\theta)=\sum_t [\Lambda(\alpha_t)+\Lambda(\beta_t)+\Lambda(\gamma_t)].

In the two free angles its negative Hessian is

(cot⁡α+cot⁡βcot⁡βcot⁡βcot⁡γ+cot⁡β). \begin{pmatrix} \cot\alpha+\cot\beta&\cot\beta\\ \cot\beta&\cot\gamma+\cot\beta \end{pmatrix}. \tag{4}

Its determinant is 11, and its first diagonal entry is sin⁡γ/(sin⁡αsin⁡β)>0\sin\gamma/(\sin\alpha\sin\beta)>0. Therefore the functional is STRICTLY CONCAVE, not convex, on every affine positive-angle slice.

Maximizing on the old UNFILLED completeness slice would concern VsV_s and would not prove the filled claim. The large signed integers pip_i belong in the actual affine constraints (3).

4. Why stationarity holds for all angle tangents

Casson–Rivin section 4 correctly uses the coisotropic edge reduction, not a derivative only along the much smaller representation variety. For x=Log⁡z,y=Log⁡z″x=\operatorname{Log} z,\ y=\operatorname{Log} z'', direct sine-rule differentiation gives

d𝒱(δθ)=∑t(Re⁡xtδγt−Re⁡ytδαt). d\mathcal V(\delta\theta) =\sum_t(\operatorname{Re}x_t\,\delta\gamma_t -\operatorname{Re}y_t\,\delta\alpha_t).

The REAL log vector of a complex gluing solution and EVERY tangent obeying the homogeneous edge-angle constraints lie in the edge coisotropic space. The audited boundary pairing, independently on each boundary torus, consequently gives

d𝒱(δθ)=12∑i[Re⁡ViδIm⁡Ui−Re⁡UiδIm⁡Vi]. d\mathcal V(\delta\theta)=\frac12\sum_i [\operatorname{Re}V_i\,\delta\operatorname{Im}U_i -\operatorname{Re}U_i\,\delta\operatorname{Im}V_i]. \tag{5}

There are no cross-torus intersection terms. At the source point the REAL part of its actual filling equation is Re⁡Ui+piRe⁡Vi=0\operatorname{Re}U_i+p_i\operatorname{Re}V_i=0. Each term of (5) is therefore

12Re⁡ViδIm⁡(Ui+piVi)=0. \tfrac12\operatorname{Re}V_i\, \delta\operatorname{Im}(U_i+p_iV_i)=0 .

Unfilled cusp terms vanish because both actual logs are zero. This proves the full KKT criticality on the affine slice, including variations not arising from nearby complete gluing solutions. Strict concavity then makes the source point the unique global maximizer, including comparison with the boundary by continuity.

5. Absolute calibration, boundary correction, and smooth completion

The source-to-volume equality is not fixed by a cusp Hessian alone. The marked regulator proof, sections 1–4, uses the SAME group representation, eigenflags, preferred longitude and COMPLETE anchor to prove

Dsrc=Im⁡G−∑iRe⁡ziIm⁡ℓi=D(β(ρz)). D_{\mathrm{src}} =\operatorname{Im}G-\sum_i\operatorname{Re}z_i \operatorname{Im}\ell_i =D(\beta(\rho_z)). \tag{6}

Its section 3 defines β\beta by an actual decorated fundamental chain, not an unproved extension of a unipotent coefficient identity. The literal spin λ=−LH−1\lambda=-L_H^{-1} is retained before the paired norm. Its exact filling term is

W=G+∑i(zi−2πimi)2/(4pi). W=G+\sum_i(z_i-2\pi i m_i)^2/(4p_i).

At stationarity Re⁡zi=−2piRe⁡ℓi\operatorname{Re}z_i=-2p_i\operatorname{Re}\ell_i, so Im⁡W=Dsrc\operatorname{Im}W=D_{\mathrm{src}}. Thus no unestimated complex endpoint term is suppressed when passing from the source action to the volume functional.

Finally Casson–Rivin section 6 invokes independently proved smooth completion. The primitive log theorem, sections 2–5, identifies the actual log-link periods BEFORE multiplying by a large slope. The selected slope period is exactly 2π2\pi in absolute angular value, not merely scalar PSL holonomy and not a 4π4\pi branched fill. The compact log-link and tube proof, sections 2–5, gives the full tube and degree-one complete filling; the unfilled cusp or cusps remain complete.

Therefore the regulator value is the volume of a genuine positive complete hyperbolic metric on the ACTUAL filled topology. Comparing it to a specified hyperbolic metric via the independently proved representation-volume inequality in both directions identifies their volumes without assuming a separate equality-rigidity theorem.

The dependency chain is

whole source rate→actual marked ρ𝒑→positive interior angle point and filled-slope KKT→max⁡𝒱=Vsrc→smooth complete filled volume. \text{whole source rate}\ \longrightarrow\ \text{actual marked }\rho_{\boldsymbol p}\ \longrightarrow\ \text{positive interior angle point and filled-slope KKT} \ \longrightarrow\ \max\mathcal V=V_{\mathrm{src}} \ \longrightarrow\ \text{smooth complete filled volume}. \tag{7}

No arrow is justified solely by the previous maximum’s numerical value. In particular neither a KKT equation without the positive chart/primitive logs nor strict concavity without a verified source point proves the assertion.

6. Current exact quantum scope

The old Casson–Rivin and survival bodies state odd s≥25s\geq25. The subsequent width extension, sections 5–6, supplies the missing whole-color gap for every fixed odd s≥9s\geq9. Section 8 of that note separately verifies the parity-independent source and normalization and gives the original single-cut reduced two-component link theorem at every fixed even s≥10s\geq10. For even width both unfilled main cusps remain parabolic and complete; the boundary-pairing and completion arguments are their direct sum. The even-link statement is not obtained by calling a link a knot or modifying each component separately.

In both cases all |pi||p_i| are independently sufficiently large FIXED integers. Their signs and ratios are unrestricted. The common threshold may depend on the fixed width, and can be enlarged once to enter the fixed positive geometric neighborhood. Neither width nor slopes grow with the quantum level NN; both level parities and composite levels remain included. Widths 3,5,73,5,7 and even 4,6,84,6,8 are not supplied by this current global source estimate.

The general geometric certificate applies far beyond pretzels, but the actual quantum source survival is not thereby established for arbitrary graphs or all hyperbolic knots.

Verdict: the proved-family equality is noncircular with these dependencies. Casson–Rivin is a valid concave variational characterization of the already source-selected point; it is not a substitute for the source-selection and geometric-existence inputs. No new computation was used in this audit.
