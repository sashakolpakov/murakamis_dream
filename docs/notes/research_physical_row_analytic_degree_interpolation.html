Exact moderate analytic interpolation of physical row degree

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The original physical rows admit an exact analytic interpolation in their degree on any fixed interval strictly between the endpoint and the fold. Its size has the original row action, with polynomial loss. The construction uses the attracting original Riccati equation, an exponentially accurate discrete primitive, and a localized cardinal correction. It does not substitute the off-grid full kernel.

The complex height is fixed independently of the level and is bounded explicitly in terms of the contraction and analytic radii below. There is one interpolant on the whole degree interval, so subdivision does not introduce mismatched internal connector functions. The two outer connectors retain their original action cost. No estimate at the filled action, or permission to discard those connectors, follows.

The tail-weight extension below also interpolates the literal terminating consumed-normal coefficient, retaining its action −Jδ-J_\delta and its checkerboard character. It uses a finite prefix and an explicitly bounded error up to the actual physical stop; no infinite off-grid tail is substituted.
