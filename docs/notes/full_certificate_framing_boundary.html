The full scalar certificate leaves two explicit framing boundary currents

Research proof and obstruction, 27 October 2026. The full actual finite-array annihilator can be transported through the transverse Gaussian contraction, including the least-moment repair. Its image is not yet an annihilation equation for the sourced geometric period: it has two explicitly computable framing boundary terms. This note derives those terms and states precisely why the residual flat Ward ideal and the current coherent source constructions do not remove them.

No full geometric-support theorem, selected-source survival, or volume limit is claimed here.

Subsequent explicit source maps now include polynomial centralizer residues and endpoints, the whole actual cut clock, and the degree-N wall row on an actual high color. They retain the raw/matrix normalization and first-normal block arrows. The independent transfer-current map and cancellation in equation (7) below have not been established by these constructions.

1. The least-moment repair has a literal Green certificate

Use the original normalized-average off-root array, whose full flow support has a strict interior. Construct the global certificate as in the finite-array proof, strip the common central root factor from the whole certificate, and specialize. Write the resulting nonzero certificate as

P=sum_beta A_beta(M,T_M) D^beta,
D_i=S_i-I.

All actual flow indices, including the two framing indices, occur among the S_i. The coefficients contain no multiplication by an internal index character. Let h be the least total degree with A_alpha!=0, and choose such an alpha. The actual full scalar moment operator is A_alpha. Put

p_alpha(k)=product_i binom(k_i,alpha_i),
nabla_i p=S_i^(-1)p-p.

The exact discrete product rule is

p D_i f=D_i[(S_i^(-1)p)f]+(nabla_i p)f.       (1)

For a fixed ordered word i_1,...,i_n representing D^beta, set p_0=p_alpha and p_m=nabla_(i_m)p_(m-1). Iterating (1) gives

p_alpha D^beta f
  =sum_(m=1)^n D_(i_m)[(S_(i_m)^(-1)p_(m-1))
                             D_(i_(m+1))...D_(i_n) f]
    +(nabla^beta p_alpha)f.                  (2)

This identity is pointwise on zero-extended integer arrays, with no summation or boundary omission. Multiplication by an external Ore coefficient commutes with these index polynomials and shifts.

If |beta|>h, the final polynomial in (2) is zero. If |beta|=h, it is zero unless beta=alpha, in which case it is (-1)^h. There are no lower-degree coefficients in P. Thus P I=0 and (2) produce the global finite Green certificate

(-1)^h A_alpha I=sum_i D_i J_i,              (3)

where the actual currents are explicitly

J_i=-sum_beta A_beta sum_(m:i_m=i)
       (S_i^(-1)p_(m-1))
                        D_(i_(m+1))...D_(i_n) I.          (4)

Every current is still a finite zero-extended array. Summing (3) over every index kills its right side exactly, proving the actual full scalar all-level annihilation. Equations (3)–(4) give more information than the summed moment argument: they retain its specific boundary currents.

2. Transverse Gaussian pushforward leaves exactly two currents

Choose the two external actual flow coordinates of the partial support theorem, with one chosen to be the cut index. Contract only their nondegenerate transverse complement by the actual formal Gaussian G. The interior rational identity underlying the whole certificate is valid independently of the multiplication characters, by the strict support cone argument. Therefore (3) has the same formal realization.

The polynomial moment insertions cause no local difficulty. With the chosen logarithmic lifts, k_i=u_i/H, so they are finite Laurent powers in H with polynomial logarithmic insertions. Their Wick coefficients are well defined. Transverse total differences have zero formal contraction by the exact translation identity; external shifts commute with the Gaussian base substitution. Hence

(-1)^h A_alpha Z
   =(T_(chi_1)-I) Jhat_1+(T_(chi_2)-I) Jhat_2,             (5)

where Z=G(I) is the actual nonzero transverse block and each Jhat_a=G(J_(chi_a)) is computed by the same actual normalized moment formula used in the residual-current theorem. External meridian shifts in (4) are retained. This is an exact formal identity, with the original base and peripheral lines.

Thus the actual FULL scalar operator has been transferred to the geometric block with a specified two-current defect. It has not been proved to annihilate a final nonzero sourced period.

3. The precise remaining relative boundary functional

Let Gamma be a specified marked relative framing chain and let F_(a,H) be the frame-coordinate translation underlying T_(chi_a). For a top-degree current form J, its final defect is

B_(Gamma,a)(J)=integral_(F_(a,H)Gamma-Gamma) J.              (6)

This notation includes the marked endpoint co-sources and the logarithmic-cover endpoints, rather than silently identifying them. Equivalently the formal Cartan homotopy is

K_(a,H)=integral_0^H F_(a,eta)^* iota_(X_a) d eta,
F_(a,H)^*-I=d K_(a,H)+K_(a,H)d.

Top forms are closed; consequently (6) is the integral of K_(a,H)J over the marked boundary. The formula is coefficientwise formal and needs no convergence of the Gaussian expansion.

Applying a source-compatible final functional to (5) would give

(-1)^h A_alpha V_geo
   =B_(Gamma,1)(Jhat_1)+B_(Gamma,2)(Jhat_2).                (7)

The needed theorem is that the actual finite normal/endpoints map to the co-sources making the right side of (7) zero, with V_geo!=0. Neither equality has yet been proved.

At completeness, in centralizer coordinates t=r/s and cut coordinate A=1/(1+s), the leading form is dt wedge dlog A. The vertical endpoint part is already explicit:

Res_(A=0)[j(1,A)-j(0,A)]dlog A              (8)

for an infinitesimal current partial_t j. In a flat frame-line gauge with horizontal unit g, it becomes

Res_(A=0)[g(1,A)^(-1)j(1,A)
                 -g(0,A)^(-1)j(0,A)]dlog A.               (9)

The complete residual Ward ideal fixes this flat connection. It does not assign the two endpoint co-sources of every Jhat_a.

There is a sharp algebraic reason. Since

nabla_t(t g)=g,

any absolute linear functional annihilating every covariant current would also annihilate the horizontal generator itself. Nonzero source survival therefore requires the relative endpoint terms; it cannot be obtained by quotienting all frame divergences away. Polynomial moments in (4) produce precisely the kind of coordinate insertions to which this issue applies.

The logarithmic direction has a second recorded boundary: continuation around A=0 changes the actual index lift by k->k+N. Thus the moment polynomial has jump p_alpha(k+N)-p_alpha(k). This is the source carry that is absent from a single regular-coefficient WKB sector. It is not removed by the residual flat ideal.

4. Every ordinary one-cut source collapses to one index weight

There is a uniform constraint on attempts to realize the last framing period using a family of finite cut coupons. Every actual crossing conserves the integer sum of positions. Closing all but one strand forces the cut output index to equal its input index, for each individual internal state. Thus the uninserted cut array is diagonal before its remaining internal sums.

For any linear functional lambda_B on that matrix space, including one formed using divided adjoints and additional finite parameters, one has

lambda_B(C)=sum_(a=0)^(N-1) w_B(a) C_aa.                  (10)

The weights may have many parameters, but as scalar flow insertions they depend on the one actual cut index. They do not create a second independent internal framing coordinate.

In particular the exact divided-normal source

Phi_div(C)=-Phi(J_E^ad(CW))/(N-1), W=E_(N-1,0),

satisfies, for every diagonal matrix,

Phi_div(C)=C_(N-1,N-1).                                  (11)

Indeed CW=C_(N-1,N-1)W, and the proved normal pairing is Phi(J_E^ad W)=-(N-1). Thus this source is exactly the endpoint cut on the diagonal state. Its all-matrices Ward homotopy remains valuable, but after ordinary partial trace it does not independently specify the vertical centralizer endpoints in (8).

If a boundary weight has leading phase G(A_cut)/H, then its differential and Hessian along the framing torsor have no component in the vertical direction: V_J A_cut=0. At an unchanged geometric critical point, criticality forces G'(A_cut)=0, and the added Hessian has rank at most one on the two-dimensional framing radical. Thus no family of ordinary one-cut coupons removes both framing modes at that point. This extends the earlier coherent-cut obstruction to all such finite linear source families.

Divided-adjoint pivotal naturality does not evade (10): it moves the full inserted action, with its mixed terms, to a cut functional. A two-dimensional abstract source orbit may still provide a useful relative normal complex, but its group/torsor isomorphism alone is not a map from the two internal framing coordinates to that orbit.

5. What the balanced coherent Verma source adds, and what it does not

The common Verma source supplies literal head inclusion/projection, the two boundary arrows, and their mixed adapter delta w H_beta. The balanced coherent crossing supplies an actual source kernel and changing-source braid matrices. Its selected cut is exactly source independent:

C_beta(s_1,...,s_b)=F_N(M) I.

Consequently any normalized scalar period formed solely from those coherent parameters tensors this same unknown knot scalar by its universal source period. Its meridian/centralizer normalization is specified, but it does not determine the coefficient of the geometric block. In particular the ordinary source independence does not identify the logarithmic polynomial-current jumps or relative endpoints in (7)–(9).

The common module is a promising place to retain those terms: degree-N block carries and the original marked coupon exist there before either wall quotient. The required next map must send the explicit currents (4), including their moment polynomials, through that relative source and identify their geometric endpoint/carry functional. The currently proved coherent-kernel identities and the complete residual formal Ward ideal do not supply this map.

Equation (7), with currents (4), is therefore the precise remaining defect for upgrading the partial support theorem to the actual full scalar certificate. No geometric factor of the selected full scalar operator has been established by this note.

The subsequent whole relative-word construction now supplies the actual coefficient/current map through Fock, Mellin and Rees source complexes for every moment in (4), retaining all projectors and endpoint terms. The compact coherent period also supplies its global algebraic cycle and exact product calculus. The unresolved map is from that specified actual class to the geometric Gaussian class: the compactified critical framing family and its possible polar/escaping terms must be controlled. Neither construction yet proves the nonzero geometric pairing or the cancellation in (7).

Universal Green audit and results verify (2) as an identity in the free shift module, with independent symbols for all shifted array values. They check its least-degree residual in one, two and three dimensions. No knot example or unproved framing-boundary cancellation is used.

Independent Green and source-map audit.

The actual Gauss-wall cut-current theorem now computes one part of this missing boundary map at the finite source level. Its two hemisphere rows have a shared binomial wall co-source and both shifted endpoints. Matrix-normalized currents are N times the original raw Green arrays, so no cut normalization is absorbed. The exact two-current identity retains the second framing divergence. Commuting this finite source map with the internal Gaussian, and transporting its nonzero Cech source class, remain necessary for (7).
