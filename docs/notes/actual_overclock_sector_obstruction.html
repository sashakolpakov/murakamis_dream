A macroscopic clock current surviving exact source fusion

28 October 2026. Fixed overclock sectors cannot separately be bounded by the regulator of the closed knot, even after exact closure/full-block fusion. This is an all-level calculation in the original enhanced source. It identifies a remaining macroscopic current whose coherent clock sum is essential. It does not establish a new hyperbolic case or obstruct a source-specific upper bound after that sum.

1. Exact original source and its clock sectors

Use N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, and exactly the enhanced crossing/pivot conventions of the original crossing audit: mu=-q S and alpha=-q. Take the positive two-strand word [1,1,1], with its original fixed first cut. The exact cyclic-source peeling proof gives

K_N=Q^(-1) (T_+^3)_(0,1).                       (1)

Here K_N is the original reduced enhanced period with unknot value one. In the full modified-trace convention multiply EVERY sector and both sides of the subsequent identities by its original known external line. That common line is retained and has no exponential modulus at completeness; it is never replaced by an unknown scalar.

The factor Q(-1)=(-q)(-2) includes the original closing coefficient and full writhe correction. No new average, source or normalization is introduced. The entries of this actual boundary operator are

T_(0,j)=Q^(1-j), T_(k,0)=0 for k>0,
T_(k,j)=(-1)^(j-1) Q^[k+(j-1)(j-2)/2]
                          [k-1 choose j-1]_Q,
             1<=j<=k<=d.                       (2)

All other entries vanish. These are the directly proved q-Pascal entries of the original crossing, including T_(0,0)=Q.

Every supported cut path has first head zero throughout. At a positive crossing its actual overclock is h=[b-a-1]+[a-c]=[b-1]. Thus its three clocks are (0,H,J), with H,J in 0,…,d, and its intermediate second-head values are

i(H)=H+1 if H<d, i(d)=0;
i(J)=J+1 if J<d, i(d)=0.                       (3)

There is exactly ONE such peeled path for each supported clock pair. The positive-index triangular block and the wrap row in (2) give

K_(N;H,J)=0, H>J,
K_(N;H,J)=(-1)^H Q^[1+H(H+1)/2]
                               [J choose H]_Q, H<=J. (4)

This is an identity of actual original clock contributions, not an arbitrary inserted coefficient. For J<d it follows by multiplying T_(i(H),1), T_(i(J),i(H)) and T_(0,i(J)) and the factor Q^(-1). For J=d it follows from

[d choose H]_Q=(-1)^H Q^[-H(H+1)/2],

which is exact factorial reflection. This also includes H=J=d and the absorbing wrap state zero. There is no excluded parity or prime level and no division by F_N.

2. The same current after all charged full-block reductions

The source of (4) is also visible in the whole charged bridge coefficient. After its three full contractions and one loop extraction, put X,Y for the two surviving anchors. Up to the explicitly retained unit root phases its clock sector is

(Q^(-J);Q)_J [X^d Y^(d-J)]
   B_(d-H)(Y,X;Q)
   B_H(X,Y;Q^(J+1))
   B_(d-J)(X,Y;Q^(H+J+1)),                    (5)

where B_L(U,V;a)=product_(r<L)(U-Q^r a V). The two parallel X-to-Y intervals concatenate to length d+H-J. If H>J this contains a FULL N-orbit, hence vanishes in the actual nilpotent source XN=YN=0. This recovers the exact support condition in (4).

If H<=J, the degree-complement identity at charge d fuses the two remaining edges. Its binomial is [d-H choose J-H]_Q. Its last loop has scalar

(Q^(J+1);Q)_(d-J)=N/F_J,

while the first loop in (5) has modulus |F_J|. Therefore the COMPLETE charged coefficient has modulus

N |[J choose H]_Q|.                            (6)

The N is canceled by the original single exterior 1/N. The other original W_h, kappa, pivot, framing and normalization factors are retained; in this alternating singleton-bridge diagram they have unit modulus, up to the common tame source line. Thus the large sector survives the existing full-block/zero-charge/loop reduction. Equation (4) independently supplies its exact normalized phase.

3. Two successive macroscopic cancellations

Choose H=floor(N/3), J=floor(2N/3). The uniform actual factorial estimate gives

log |[J choose H]_Q|
      =N[v_3/pi]+O(log N)>0,                  (7)

where v_3 is the regular ideal tetrahedron volume. Indeed, with I(s)=integral_0^s log|2sin(pi*t)| dt, the rate is I(2/3)-2I(1/3)=v_3/pi. This is the figure-eight volume rate, despite the present closed word being the trefoil, whose volume is zero. No figure-eight asymptotic theorem is a premise of (7).

The FIRST coherent sum is itself an exact general q-binomial identity inside this actual source:

sum_(H=0)^J K_(N;H,J)=Q F_J,
F_J=(Q;Q)_J.                                  (8)

Use the finite q-binomial theorem with argument Q. This collapses the entire H current before estimation. It lowers its maximal rate, but does not make it polynomial: at J=floor(5N/6),

log |F_J|=N[v_3/(2pi)]+O(log N)>0.             (9)

Thus even the partially summed current in (8) cannot be assigned the closed knot’s zero regulator or dropped as a small boundary. The complete original scalar is exactly

K_N=Q sum_(J=0)^d F_J.                         (10)

The last sum has polynomial size. This follows independently from the already derived ACTUAL spectral-jet closing functional, not a named torus-knot asymptotic. Write its normalized crossing as r_N. At double nodes c=qa+q(-a), a odd, 0<a<N, it obeys

r_N(c)=nu, |nu|=1,
r_N'(c)/nu=-(N-a)/[2(q^a-q^(-a))].

The original pivotal functional is

Phi(f)=-sum_double [c f(c)+(c^2-4)f'(c)]
                 + one simple-node value if N is odd.

For f=r_N^3, each first term has modulus at most 2 and each derivative term at most 3N, because c2-4=(qa-q(-a))2. The possible simple-node term has modulus one. The full writhe factor is (-q)^3, of modulus one. Hence, at EVERY level,

|K_N|<=3N^2.                                  (11)

All node derivatives and the original pivotal source are retained in this bound. These formulas were independently proved from the finite R and its generic tensor specialization in the spectral source note. They do not rely on semisimplicity at the root.

4. Consequence for the next source-specific operation

The first fusion (8) is constructive and exact, but a second macroscopic cancellation is still needed. A bound on individual fixed-h charged coefficients, or even on the first fused current at each J, cannot equal the closed knot regulator. Those currents are not separately honest closed-knot representation periods.

The new physical-face outward estimates can control their actual endpoint continuations; they do not remove the internal coherent sum exhibited by (8)–(11). A proposed broad source fusion must retain and use that final clock connection, rather than fixing the clocks and then applying a knot-volume bound. This is an explicit failure of that sectorwise route, not a no-go for phase-aware fusion of the entire original source or the volume conjecture.

No arbitrary consecutive-run or arbitrary-width positive-kernel extension is claimed here. The theorem is restricted to the actual source and exact current proved above.

Audit checks every sector against the literal original crossing, both coherent sums, the original spectral normalization, the fully reduced charged coefficients and the displayed exponential rates.
