A whole actual high-color Ward contraction by grid flow

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. Four ACTUAL integral quantum-group words give a pole-free contraction of the full high-color matrix Ward complex onto its scalar line. The construction uses two diagonal spectra and a connected grid of nonvanishing ordinary and divided arrows. Every denominator is a unit at the marked root; no power of 1-Q^N is inverted. The original cut source has an explicit whole cochain homotopy to normalized trace, including the formerly resonant Frobenius corner.

The result is a finite actual source/current contraction. It does not identify a partial geometric Gaussian module with this matrix complex, and does not identify low-color and high-color normal jets. Whole source units and the frozen pivotal cap correction remain part of any such comparison.

1. Four actual words and the whole coefficient ring

Fix N>=2, r>=1, D=N(r+1), m=D-1. Use the ACTUAL integral Weyl color D_m(q) in the constant sign basis w_(R,S), 0<=R<=r, 0<=S<N, of the high-color construction. The integer position is j=NR+S. Write

K=q^m Q^(-J), Q=q^2,
F=the original ordinary lowering generator,
L=F^(N)=F^N/[N]_q!,
H=tau [E^(N),F^(N)], tau=(-1)^(Nr-1).            (1.1)

The divided matrices in (1.1) are their regular integral Gaussian matrices, not singular quotients specialized separately. All four are actual U_q words. In particular their coproducts retain the mixed ordinary/divided terms; neither H nor L is declared to have a primitive tensor coproduct.

K is diagonal with entries k_(R,S)=q^(m-2NR-2S). Both divided matrices preserve S and shift R by one, so H is diagonal for ALL q; denote its entries by h_(R,S)(q). At the marked root q_N=exp(pi i/N), Q_N=q_N^2,

K_0=q_N^m I_Fr tensor diag(Q_N^(-S)),
H_0=diag(r-2R) tensor I_head,
F_0=I_Fr tensor F_St,
L_0=f_r tensor I_head,                           (1.2)

where F_St has entries [S+1](q_N), and (f_r)(R+1,R)=R+1. The sign basis is the actual one proving (1.2); no additional q-factorial head gauge is needed.

Work in the rational local coefficient ring at q_N, enlarged only by the original source units and the following finitely many units:

  • k_(R,S)-k_(T,U) for S!=U;
  • h_(R,S)-h_(T,S) for R!=T;
  • the selected nonwrap F arrows and L arrows below.

Their root values are respectively q_Nm(Q_N(-S)-Q_N^(-U)), 2(T-R), [S+1]_(q_N), R+1, all nonzero. Thus this is a whole rational localization before normal completion. The wall delta=1-Q^N is never inverted.

2. Off-diagonal recovery separates the two actual spectra

For a four-tuple of matrices X=(X_K,X_H,X_F,X_L), define O(X) to have zero diagonal and

O(X)_(R,S;T,U)=
  (X_K)_(R,S;T,U)/(k_(R,S)-k_(T,U)),        S!=U,
  (X_H)_(R,S;T,S)/(h_(R,S)-h_(T,S)),        R!=T.  (2.1)

The two cases are disjoint. Consequently, for every matrix C,

O([K,C],[H,C],0,0)=C_off.                       (2.2)

The K row resolves every different head residue. The H row resolves the repeated K eigenvalues between different Fr blocks, including the extreme corner (r,0). Its root gap there is -2r. This is the whole full-grid extension of the actual wall unit.

3. Diagonal recovery is an explicit finite tree flow

Choose the comb tree rooted at (0,0). It consists of every horizontal nonwrap edge

(R,S)->(R,S+1), 0<=R<=r, 0<=S<N-1,

and the vertical trunk

(R,0)->(R+1,0), 0<=R<r.

There are D-1 edges and every vertex has a unique root path. For a horizontal edge e:u->v put a_e(q)=F_(v,u); for a vertical edge put a_e(q)=L_(v,u). These are exactly the actual units of Section 1. Define the current gradient

d_e(X)=(X_F)_(v,u)/a_e      on horizontal edges,
d_e(X)=(X_L)_(v,u)/a_e      on vertical edges.    (3.1)

Both F and L have one possible incoming and outgoing arrow at each vertex in their respective direction. Hence, even if C has off-diagonal entries,

d_e([K,C],[H,C],[F,C],[L,C])=C_(u,u)-C_(v,v).   (3.2)

No off-diagonal subtraction is needed in this four-row version.

For an edge e let T_e be its child subtree and n_e its number of vertices. Let Q_e be the diagonal matrix

(Q_e)_(v,v)=n_e/D-1_(v in T_e).

It has trace zero. The explicit tree-flow primitive is

G(X)=sum_e d_e(X) Q_e.                           (3.3)

Indeed, if b_v=-sum_(e on root->v) d_e, then G_v=b_v-D^(-1)sum_w b_w. Applying (3.2) yields

G(delta C)=C_diag-(Tr C/D)I.                    (3.4)

For this comb, a horizontal edge (R,S)->(R,S+1) has subtree {(R,U):U>S}, so n_e=N-1-S. A vertical trunk edge (R,0)->(R+1,0) has every row T>R as its subtree, so n_e=N(r-R). Thus (3.3) is a fully specified finite formula.

4. The actual Ward complex has a strong whole contraction

Define

delta C=([K,C],[H,C],[F,C],[L,C]),
p(C)=Tr C/D, i(c)=cI, h(X)=O(X)+G(X).            (4.1)

Equations (2.2) and (3.4) prove the exact WHOLE identity

h delta=I-i p, p h=0, delta i=0, p i=1.         (4.2)

In particular the common Ward kernel is precisely the scalar line, without invoking Schur irreducibility. Define the degree-one term of the Ward complex to be the ACTUAL image

W^0=End(D_m), W^1=im(delta).

Then delta h=I on W^1, so (i,p,h) is a strong contraction onto the scalar line in degree zero. The image is a direct summand of the unrestricted four-row space, since delta h is its explicit idempotent projector. No claim is made that the unrestricted codomain End(D_m)^4 has zero cokernel.

Every map is rational and regular at q_N. The same identities hold after arbitrary finite normal truncation or formal completion, with all Leibniz convolutions retained. Unlike inversion of a root memory-Cartan gap, (4.2) is not obtained by inverting delta.

5. The original cut is attached by its exact source row

Let Phi_A(C)=Tr(A(q)C) be ANY specified whole actual cut coupon. Put rho(q)=Phi_A(I)=Tr A. Applying it to (4.2) gives

Phi_A-rho p=b_A delta, b_A(X)=Phi_A(h(X)).        (5.1)

This retains the original source, dual and every normalization unit exactly. For an actually normalized coupon, rho=1; if its raw source uses a normalization unit, that unit is retained in A, not inferred from its root value. In entries the degree-one row is

b_A(X)=sum_(S!=U,R,T) A_(T,U;R,S) (X_K)_(R,S;T,U)
                               /(k_(R,S)-k_(T,U))
      +sum_(R!=T,S) A_(T,S;R,S) (X_H)_(R,S;T,S)
                               /(h_(R,S)-h_(T,S))
      +sum_e Tr(A Q_e) d_e(X).                  (5.2)

In particular this applies to the original correlated coupon e_D, whose root source is the literal selected sum over (R,S)=(R,R mod N) and whose row has coefficients 2^(-N) binom(N,R), for r=N. The diagonal alias at R=0,N and its old endpoint normalization remain part of A. The root wall has original value 2^(-N), not the uniform interval value 1/(N+1). Equation (5.1) makes their difference an ACTUAL Ward boundary using H, rather than incorrectly declaring it a boundary in the smaller resonant memory-Borel complex.

The original root row can be made still more explicit. Write p_R=2^(-N)binom(N,R), S_R=R mod N. Its only two distinct selected vertices having the SAME head residue are (0,0) and (N,0). Therefore its H-row part in (5.2) is exactly

2^(-N)/(2N) * [(X_H)_(0,0;N,0)
                                  -(X_H)_(N,0;0,0)].      (5.3)

This is the original wall weight, with its original sign, rather than an interval-average substitute. The diagonal-flow coefficient Tr(e_D Q_e) is also explicit. On the horizontal edge in row R after S it is

(N-1-S)/D-p_R 1_(S_R>S),

and on the vertical trunk edge after R it is

(N-R)/(N+1)-sum_(T>R)p_T.                     (5.4)

The whole formulas remain (5.1)–(5.2) with the actual supplied source entries and their normalization units; (5.3)–(5.4) only display their root values, not a chosen off-root extension.

If the network is expressed in the controlled Fourier/source frame, conjugate all four actual words, all rows, h, and A by that same whole matrix. Equation (5.1) is preserved. Conjugating the coupon alone and keeping the old current operators would be a different source problem.

The ordinary pivotal cap transports the actual Hopf-adjoint rows with their full divided coproducts. A commutator row is converted by [rho(h),C]=sum_(h) D_(h_1)(C)rho(h_2). Thus the all-word current/cap formulas give an explicit global endpoint for each actual word in (4.1). For the frozen ADO cap retain its whole adapter chi^(-1)ptr_K M_(Z^J) and its recorded defect rows. This grid contraction does not make those Green or cap endpoints zero.

6. A polynomial root bound in the actual hybrid metric

Use the root positive metric

||w_(R,S)||^2=binom(r,R)

with the ordinary integral head basis orthonormal. Give End the induced Hilbert–Schmidt norm, and the four-row domain the orthogonal product norm. This is an explicitly stated root metric, not a bound on an unretained q-factorial change of basis.

K is unitary at the root. Its inverse-gap norm in (2.1) is at most 1/[2 sin(pi/N)] <= N/4. The H inverse-gap norm is at most 1/2. The two off-diagonal pieces occupy disjoint supports.

For a horizontal tree edge the metric-normalized actual arrow has size [S+1]_(q_N)>=1. For a vertical edge its size is sqrt((R+1)(r-R))>=sqrt(r)>=1. Hence the Euclidean norm of the selected gradient vector is bounded by the combined Hilbert–Schmidt norms of X_F and X_L.

Before centering, the root-path integration matrix has one entry -1 for each edge on a vertex path. Its squared Frobenius norm is

sum_(R=0)^r sum_(S=0)^(N-1)(R+S)
                     =D(r+N-1)/2.

Centering is an orthogonal projection. Since the off-diagonal and diagonal outputs are orthogonal, this gives, for example,

||h_0|| <= max(1/[2 sin(pi/N)], 1/2,
                                  sqrt(D(r+N-1)/2)).        (6.1)

For r=N the bound is O(N^(3/2)). It is uniform in the word length because h is a cut contraction, not a product of crossing norms. There is no claim of an N-independent complex neighborhood on which every whole rational unit has this root bound.

The original root correlated coupon e_D is an orthogonal rank-one projector in this hybrid metric: its selected source has squared norm sum_R binom(N,R)=2^N, and its original dual is that metric dual divided by 2^N. Consequently its functional norm is one. Equation (5.1) therefore gives

||b_(e_D),0|| <= ||h_0||,                       (6.2)

a polynomial source-homotopy bound, INCLUDING the old memory wall. The full external modified-dimension line multiplies both sides and is never suppressed. A source comparison through some other nonunitary whole gauge requires its own norm adapter; the present bound concerns the specified actual hybrid frame. The original controlled root head-shift/Fourier matrix with its literal 1/N normalization is a scalar N^(-1/2) times a unitary map. Its conjugation is therefore isometric, so simultaneous transport of operators, current maps and coupon preserves this bound.

7. Two actual words suffice, with an explicit row correction

There is also a shorter whole presentation. Put

A=H+K/2, B=F+L.

At the root A has simple full-grid spectrum. If R!=T then |Delta H+Delta K/2|>=2|R-T|-1>=1. If R=T and S!=U the gap is |Q_N(-S)-Q_N(-U)|/2>=sin(pi/N). Hence every off-diagonal A gap is a whole unit. Recover C_off entrywise from [A,C]. Next subtract

Z=[B,C]-[B,C_off]=[B,C_diag].

The N-step and one-step arrows in B occupy distinct positions for N>=2. Read the same comb-edge gradients from Z and apply (3.3). This gives an explicit two-row contraction with the SAME scalar trace projection. Its off-diagonal subtraction is essential; simply reading a tree edge of [B,C] can include other entries of C. The four-row form has the cleaner bound and source formulas above.

Both presentations use actual integral U_q words. They supply a whole finite Ward target and its original co-source homotopy; a nonzero map from the sourced geometric current complex into this target is still the necessary geometric comparison.
