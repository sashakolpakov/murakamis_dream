The original crossing in the coupled flag Euler exchange

Research note, 22 November 2026. This identifies the nilpotent factor of the original complete crossing in the physical flag coordinates of [D]. The factor moves the multiplicity clock, and participates in both the existing cyclic and marked full-dilogarithm exchanges. All identities hold on the complete two-head space and preserve arbitrary exteriors.

Pascal/Gaussian factorizations of the original crossing already appear in [C, Section 6.1]. The additional identification here is the explicit operator XX in the actual coupled flags, and its exchanges with the same cyclic and full functions used in [A,P]. This does not match every remaining nine-cell factor or give a geometric scalar lower bound.

1. The actual flags and the new nilpotent argument

Use the complete balanced heads and conventions of [D]: N≥2,d=N−1,q=eπi/N,Q=q2,x=−q−1,λ=x−1=−q,Fh=(Q;Q)h.(1) N\geq2,\quad d=N-1,\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad Q=q^2,\quad x=-q^{-1},\quad\lambda=x^{-1}=-q,\quad F_h=(Q;Q)_h . \qquad\text{(1)} The auxiliary flag wraps are S1N=1S_1^N=1, S2N=(−1)dS_2^N=(-1)^d. Thus WW is exactly the stripped matrix of [A, (11)], with W|0,0⟩=|0,0⟩W|0,0\rangle=|0,0\rangle. Its full negative inverse is κ−1W−1\kappa^{-1}W^{-1}. The actual cup and averaged marked row remain p=N∑a=0dxa−d|a,d−a⟩,ℓ|a,b⟩=xb/N2,ℓp=1.(2) p=N\sum_{a=0}^d x^{a-d}|a,d-a\rangle,\qquad \ell|a,b\rangle=x^b/N^2,\qquad \ell p=1. \qquad\text{(2)}

Retain the original flag operators Δ=Z1(λS2−S1),γ=−e2K1,v=Z2−1+S1e2,M=Z1Z2v,S=S1v−1.(3) \Delta=Z_1(\lambda S_2-S_1),\quad \gamma=-e_2K_1,\quad v=Z_2^{-1}+S_1e_2,\quad M=Z_1Z_2v,\quad S=S_1v^{-1}. \qquad\text{(3)} Here MM is the multiplicity clock, not a peripheral meridian eigenvalue. The original S1S_1 commutes with vv, and ΔS1=QS1Δ\Delta S_1=QS_1\Delta, so [S,Δ]=0[S,\Delta]=0. Define the physical nilpotent operator X=(1−Q−1M−1)ΔS−1.(4) X=(1-Q^{-1}M^{-1})\Delta S^{-1}. \qquad\text{(4)}

In the rescaled frame ℬ̃\widetilde{\mathcal B} of [D, (27)], v=Zs,M=Zm,S=Sm,S1=Smv,X|m,s⟩=(1−Q−m)(1−Q1−s)|m−1,s−1⟩.(5) \begin{gathered} v=Z_s,\quad M=Z_m,\quad S=S_m,\quad S_1=S_mv,\\ X|m,s\rangle=(1-Q^{-m})(1-Q^{1-s}) |m-1,s-1\rangle . \end{gathered} \qquad\text{(5)} Indices in (5) are cyclic, and the displayed zero coefficients are retained. Let Ej(v)E_j(v) denote the v=Qjv=Q^j projectors, with EN=E0E_N=E_0, and set 𝒢=∑j=1NgjEj(v),gj=(−1)j−1Qj(j−1)/2,gN=1.(6) \mathcal G=\sum_{j=1}^N g_jE_j(v),\qquad g_j=(-1)^{j-1}Q^{j(j-1)/2},\qquad g_N=1. \qquad\text{(6)} It is unitary in the fixed positive Gram of [D], and commutes with M,S1,vM,S_1,v. 2. Exact crossing factorization on the full space

For a nilpotent argument YN=0Y^N=0, write PN(Y)=∑h=0d(−1)hQh(h−1)/2FhYh,ℰN(Y)=∑h=0dYh/Fh=PN(Y)−1.(8) P_N(Y)=\sum_{h=0}^d\frac{(-1)^hQ^{h(h-1)/2}}{F_h}Y^h, \qquad \mathcal E_N(Y)=\sum_{h=0}^dY^h/F_h=P_N(Y)^{-1}. \qquad\text{(8)} The inverse follows from the finite QQ-binomial theorem in every degree below NN; higher degrees vanish. No FNF_N is divided out. The original crossing has the factorization W=𝒢ℰN(X),W−1=PN(X)𝒢−1.(9) \boxed{\qquad W=\mathcal G\mathcal E_N(X),\qquad W^{-1}=P_N(X)\mathcal G^{-1}.\qquad} \qquad\text{(9)}

Here is a full-space proof from the actual Hurwitz identities. Directly from (5), [X,S1]=(1−Q−1)Δv,X(Δv)=Q(Δv)X.(10) [X,S_1]=(1-Q^{-1})\Delta v,\qquad X(\Delta v)=Q(\Delta v)X . \qquad\text{(10)} Thus [Xh,S1]=−Q−1(1−Qh)ΔvXh−1,1≤h≤d.(11) [X^h,S_1]=-Q^{-1}(1-Q^h)\Delta vX^{h-1}, \qquad 1\leq h\leq d. \qquad\text{(11)} Summing (11) with the coefficients of (8), the only truncated top correction is ΔvXd/Fd=0\Delta vX^d/F_d=0, because it contains ΔN\Delta^N. Therefore ℰN(X)S1ℰN(X)−1=S1−Q−1Δv,[ℰN(X),Δ]=0.(12) \mathcal E_N(X)S_1\mathcal E_N(X)^{-1} =S_1-Q^{-1}\Delta v,\qquad [\mathcal E_N(X),\Delta]=0. \qquad\text{(12)} For A*=𝒢ℰN(X)A_*=\mathcal G\mathcal E_N(X), equations (7),(12) give A*S1A*−1=S1+Δ,A*ΔA*−1=−QΔv−1.(13) A_*S_1A_*^{-1}=S_1+\Delta,\qquad A_*\Delta A_*^{-1}=-Q\Delta v^{-1}. \qquad\text{(13)} These are precisely the original identities for WW: the first is [A, (10)], and the second is obtained by subtracting t2t_2 from t3t_3 in [A, (15)]. Both A*A_* and WW fix |0,0⟩|0,0\rangle: this is also the frame’s |0,0⟩|0,0\rangle column, so XX kills it, and its clock value is v=1v=1, where gN=1g_N=1.

Their identical first conjugation implies equality on every S1m|0,0⟩=|m,0⟩S_1^m|0,0\rangle=|m,0\rangle. Their identical second conjugation then implies equality on every Δa|m,0⟩/Fa\Delta^a|m,0\rangle/F_a. These N2N^2 vectors form the complete invertible frame of [D, (27)]. This proves (9), including every endpoint and both parities, without an unspecified intertwiner scalar or a semisimple quotient.

The resulting clock updates are also explicit: WvW−1=v+X,WXW−1=−QXv−1,WMW−1=M+(Z1Z2)X.(14) WvW^{-1}=v+X,\qquad WXW^{-1}=-QXv^{-1},\qquad WMW^{-1}=M+(Z_1Z_2)X . \qquad\text{(14)} Indeed ℰN(X)vℰN(X)−1=v(1−X)\mathcal E_N(X)v\mathcal E_N(X)^{-1}=v(1-X) by the finite Euler recurrence, and 𝒢X𝒢−1=−QXv−1\mathcal GX\mathcal G^{-1}=-QXv^{-1} by (4),(7). The actual crossing preserves Z1Z2Z_1Z_2. In particular the new factor is not confined to the commutant of MM.

The Gaussian ratios give 𝒢γ𝒢−1=−γv\mathcal G\gamma\mathcal G^{-1}=-\gamma v; the exceptional v=1v=1 arrow of γ\gamma is physically zero. 3. The cyclic and marked full exchanges with the actual crossing

On the marked Fermat cover tβ≠0t\beta\ne0, βN=1−tN\beta^N=1-t^N, let 𝒞t=𝔡N(tv)\mathcal C_t=\mathfrak d_N(tv) be exactly the cyclic matrix of [A, (22)] and [D, (14)]. Its functional equation and Xv=QvXXv=QvX give 𝒞tX𝒞t−1=β−1X(1−Q−1tv).(15) \mathcal C_tX\mathcal C_t^{-1} =\beta^{-1}X(1-Q^{-1}tv). \qquad\text{(15)} The two summands on the right QQ-commute. Their finite Euler factorization is the mixed identity of [A], now on this explicit argument from the original crossing. Inverting it reverses the order. Since 𝒞t\mathcal C_t commutes with 𝒢\mathcal G, (9) gives 𝒞tW=𝒢ℰN(−β−1Q−1tXv)ℰN(β−1X)𝒞t.(16) \boxed{\quad \mathcal C_tW= \mathcal G\,\mathcal E_N(-\beta^{-1}Q^{-1}tXv) \mathcal E_N(\beta^{-1}X)\,\mathcal C_t . \quad} \qquad\text{(16)} Every argument has nilpotent degree NN; apparent higher degrees in the product vanish, rather than requiring a cyclic replacement. The same 𝒞t\mathcal C_t, with its full scalar and root marks, occurs once on both sides. The full crossing is obtained by retaining the original multiplier κ\kappa.

There is also a nonsingular physical-root version. Use the marked matrix FvF_v of [P, (10)–(12)], not a separate specialization of the singular β−1\beta^{-1} factors: Fv=ϕN∑j=1NEj(v)Fj−1,ϕN=Nexp⁡{iπ[14−N+N−112]}.(17) F_v=\phi_N\sum_{j=1}^N\frac{E_j(v)}{F_{j-1}},\qquad \phi_N=\sqrt N\exp\!\left\{i\pi\left[ \frac14-\frac{N+N^{-1}}{12}\right]\right\}. \qquad\text{(17)} The exact relation FvΔFv−1=Δ(1−Q−1v)F_v\Delta F_v^{-1}=\Delta(1-Q^{-1}v), and the commuting factors of (4), imply FvXFv−1=X(1−Q−1v)F_vXF_v^{-1}=X(1-Q^{-1}v). Hence FvW=𝒢ℰN(−Q−1Xv)ℰN(X)Fv.(18) \boxed{\qquad F_vW=\mathcal G\,\mathcal E_N(-Q^{-1}Xv) \mathcal E_N(X)\,F_v . \qquad} \qquad\text{(18)} This equality retains the marked j=Nj=N value of the full function. It is not obtained by identifying its top and bottom logarithmic marks.

4. Original source and exterior scope

The source identities Δp=0\Delta p=0, vp=Qpvp=Qp, and Mp=pMp=p imply Xp=0Xp=0, 𝒢p=p\mathcal Gp=p. Consequently the literal original pairings are ℓ𝒞tWp=𝔡N(tQ),ℓFvWp=ϕN.(19) \ell\mathcal C_tWp=\mathfrak d_N(tQ),\qquad \ell F_vWp=\phi_N . \qquad\text{(19)} For the full crossing they are multiplied by the unchanged κ\kappa. No scalar on either side of (16) or (18) has been reset to one.

More generally, (9),(16),(18) preserve every actual two-head exterior row and input tensor, before any further sums or relative integrals. The exterior remains attached; (19) does not replace it by a special cap. Original turns, pivots, framing and peripheral factors remain unchanged outside these operator equalities. All NN coordinates of each physical head are retained.

This gives an actual crossing factor, not merely a pure-Δ\Delta oscillator identity. It does not assert that distinct overlapping crossings use one common flag frame or one positive Gram with tame transition norms. Nor does it identify the remaining complete nine-cell tensor with the right side of (16), or authorize cancelling its exteriors. No global geometric period, sharp whole-source bound, or nonvanishing of the original general closing is proved here.
