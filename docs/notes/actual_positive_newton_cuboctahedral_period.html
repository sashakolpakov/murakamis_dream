An exact positive Newton period with cuboctahedral hyperbolic volume

29 October 2026. For every odd N=2m+1 define the ACTUAL root polynomial h=Rturn(E_St) in H_N and the original Frobenius period W_N=phi(h^4). The positive multiplication theorem retains the full source and proves W_N>0. This note proves

lim_(N odd ->infty) (pi/N)log W_N=Vol(Q_cube),   (1)

where Q_cube is the explicit ideal right-angled cuboctahedron in the actual core geometry. The proof is a real, strictly concave volume maximization. No stationary-phase noncancellation or contour-selection assumption occurs.

The literal cube source reduction then gives the corresponding actual hyperbolic-volume rate for its ODD CENTRAL JOINT SECTOR. The full color sum and even levels are not asserted here. A sector is not substituted for the whole invariant.

1. Exact positive partition with every root normal

Use d=2m, s_r=product_(u=1)^r 2sin(pi u/N), and Bhat_r from the Newton theorem. That theorem gives

h=sum_(i=0)^m b_i Bhat_i, b_i=s_m^2/(s_i^2s_(m-i)^2)>0,
h^2=sum_(k=0)^d a_k Bhat_k,
a_k=sum_(0<=i,j<=m, max(i,j)<=k<=i+j)
                                b_i b_j mu_(i,j,k),
W_N=sum_(k=0)^d a_k a_(d-k).                    (2)

The last equation is the original full Frobenius pairing, not a value-only trace. Every a_k is strictly positive. Its summation labels can be written (i,j,u,v,k), with the first pair producing k and the second producing d-k. There are only polynomially many such labels.

Put L(x)=integral_0^x log(2sin(pi t))dt, continuously on [0,1]. The uniform sine-factorial estimate is log s_r=N L(r/N)+O(log N), for every 0<=r<=d, including endpoints. Define

g(x)=-2L(x)-2L(1/2-x), 0<=x<=1/2,
mu(x,y,z)=L(x+y)+L(z)-L(x)-L(y)
                 -L(z-x)-L(z-y)-L(x+y-z).       (3)

The mu-domain is max(x,y)<=z<=x+y<=1. The exact summands in (2) have logarithm N times

A(i,j,u,v,k)=g(i)+g(j)+g(u)+g(v)
                    +mu(i,j,k)+mu(u,v,1-k)     (4)

up to O(log N), when the letters now denote their scaled real labels. Replacing d/N by 1 or m/N by 1/2 changes these estimates by O(log N), uniformly. This follows from the modulus of continuity of L at 0,1, not from an invalid bounded derivative at those endpoints.

Let P be the compact convex polytope defined by 0<=i,j,u,v<=1/2 and the two mu-domain constraints in (4). Positivity and polynomial state count give the upper bound by max_P A. Any strictly interior feasible point can be rounded to actual odd-level integers with O(1/N) changes; a single positive term supplies the matching lower bound at an interior maximizer. Thus it suffices to find that maximum.

2. Each multiplication action is a positive tetrahedral volume

For a mu-triple put

p=z-x, q=z-y, r=x+y-z, w=1-x-y.

They are nonnegative and p+q+2r+w=1. Write V(alpha,beta,gamma) for the volume of the ideal tetrahedron with dihedral angles pi alpha, pi beta, pi gamma, where their sum is 1. Thus V=-pi[L(alpha)+L(beta)+L(gamma)]. The identity L(1-t)=-L(t) gives EXACTLY

pi mu = V(p+r,q+r,w)
          +V(p,q,2r+w)+V(p+q,r,r+w).            (5)

All three are positive ideal angle triples when p,q,r,w>0. Also

pi g(x)=2V(x,1/2-x,1/2).                       (6)

The ideal tetrahedron volume is strictly concave on its angle simplex. For an elementary Hessian check, eliminate its third angle; the diagonal coefficients involve cot(alpha)+cot(gamma)>0 and the determinant uses cot(alpha)cot(beta)+cot(beta)cot(gamma) +cot(gamma)cot(alpha)=1. Hence the negative Hessian is positive definite. The linear angle map in (5) is injective: its second triangle supplies p,q and its third supplies r. Therefore mu is strictly concave on its open domain. Continuity extends concavity to the closed domain. The same conclusion holds for g.

This is a positive volume functional for the FULL period partition. Off its maximum the tetrahedra in (5) are not yet asserted to be the metric tetrahedra of a glued cuboctahedron. The exact value at the maximum is compared with the actual polyhedron in section 4.

3. Unique global maximum and its algebraic equation

The polytope and A are invariant under i<->j, u<->v, and exchange of the two pairs with k->1-k. Averaging over these symmetries cannot decrease a concave function. The strict concavity of the four g terms forces i=j=u=v=t at a maximizing point unless those values already agree. With these fixed, strict concavity of the two mu terms forces k=1/2. It is therefore enough to maximize on 1/4<=t<=1/2 the function

A_sym(t)=6g(t)+2[L(2t)-L(2t-1/2)].             (7)

In its interior,

A_sym'(t)=-12log tan(pi t)+4log[-tan(2pi t)].    (8)

The derivative tends to +infinity at 1/4 and to -infinity at 1/2. The original strict concavity, or direct differentiation of (8), gives a unique maximum. Setting y=tan(pi t)>1 in (8) yields

2y/(y^2-1)=y^3, hence y^2=2.

Thus t=atan(sqrt(2))/pi. All inequalities in P that can be strict are strict at (t,t,t,t,1/2). In particular rounding i,j,u,v to the nearest tN and taking k=m gives actual summands at every large odd level. This completes the positive Laplace proof of the limit N^(-1)log W_N=A_sym(t*), without choosing a subsequence inside odd N.

4. Direct volume of the ACTUAL ideal cuboctahedron

Let theta=atan(sqrt(2)) and delta=pi/2-theta. The Klein vertices of Q_cube are the permutations of (±1,±1,0)/sqrt(2). Its supporting planes and all right angles were certified in the core geometry. For a completely explicit volume calculation order its twelve vertices by choosing the zero coordinate in order 0,1,2, then the other two signs in order (–),(-+),(+-),(++). Cone its faces to vertex 0, which is (0,-1/sqrt(2),-1/sqrt(2)). The nonincident face fans give the following fourteen geometric tetrahedra, specified by the unordered sets of their three other vertices; each tetrahedron is positively reoriented when computing its shape:

(4,8,5), (4,5,9), (6,10,7), (6,7,11),
(2,9,3), (2,3,11), (1,5,3), (1,3,7),
(1,5,8), (2,4,9), (3,5,9),
(1,7,10), (2,6,11), (3,7,11).                 (9)

These are disjoint face cones in a convex polyhedron. The first eight triangulate its four nonincident square faces; the last six are its nonincident triangular faces. There is no tetrahedron recognition based only on a numerical volume.

Stereographic coordinates z=(X+iY)/(1-Z) and the cross-ratio formula give ten tetrahedra of angle type A, two of B, and two of E:

A=(theta,delta,pi/2),
B=(theta,theta,pi-2theta),
E=(2theta-pi/2,delta,pi-theta).                 (10)

Representative positive cross ratios are respectively 2/3+i sqrt(2)/3, 1/3+2i sqrt(2)/3, and 2/3+i sqrt(2)/6. Their three cyclic shape arguments give (10) directly. For example the E angles follow from the shapes 2+i sqrt(2) and -1/3+i sqrt(2)/3, together with tan(2theta-pi/2)=1/(2sqrt(2)). The audit verifies every cross ratio in (9) algebraically in Q(sqrt(2),i), using its positive shape representative rather than treating the displayed vertex order as an orientation.

With Lambda(x)=-integral_0^x log|2sin t|dt, (9),(10) give

Vol(Q_cube)=12Lambda(theta)+12Lambda(delta)
                -2Lambda(2theta)+2Lambda(2theta-pi/2). (11)

On the other hand L(t)=-Lambda(pi t)/pi, so substituting t* into (7) gives precisely pi A_sym(t*)=(11). This proves the claimed equality with the ACTUAL complete core volume in (1).

5. The original cube sector and the remaining whole-source problem

The separately proved literal exposed-source identity is

Z_(cube,central)=N^12 S_N^8 W_N omega_m^(sum e),

where S_N is the original positive central trivalent scalar after its recorded unit phase, lim N^(-1)log S_N=v8/pi, and the retained twists are unit characters in this fixed sector. Therefore

lim_(N odd) (2pi/N)log|Z_(cube,central)|
         =16v8+2Vol(Q_cube)
         =Vol(S^3 minus the ACTUAL augmented cube link). (12)

The last equality is the independent complete-core construction with b=4. Thus the geometric growth is present in an actual source sector and its complete positive partition, including every root normal. This does NOT establish that other color sectors cannot cancel or exceed it, nor the corresponding even-level assertion. Those are still required before claiming the full cube volume conjecture, and general graphs need their own full-source control.
