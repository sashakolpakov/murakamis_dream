The full interior phase of an actual weighted crossing

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof. Earlier finals and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The whole physical crossing Gaussian extends beyond small real boundary weights. Its entire strict interior phase is determined by one quadratic root and one explicit action comparison. The amplitude is not globally concave; eliminating two occupation variables reveals the required one-dimensional shape instead.

An unbounded ordering cone of physical head weights lies in this phase. On that cone the complete original Green source, including its carry, has a strict exponential norm gap from the crossing. All statements use the weighted physical metric directly and retain the exact phase. They do not estimate an original closed knot scalar.

4. A whole weighted Gaussian on every compact interior phase

5. An unbounded cone with a complete original source gap

There is a simple unbounded sufficient region for (15): λt≥Bλ.(23) \lambda_t\ge B_\lambda . \qquad\text{(23)} For every finite set of side weights, (9) gives an interior value strictly greater than Fλ(1)=λtF_\lambda(1)=\lambda_t. If (23) holds, the global maximum is consequently strictly greater than both boundary values, so it belongs to ℐ\mathcal I.

In physical head weights the positive sufficient cone is exactly 𝒞+={ρ:max⁡(ρa,ρe)≤min⁡(ρb,ρc)},(24) \mathcal C_+=\{\rho:\max(\rho_a,\rho_e) \le\min(\rho_b,\rho_c)\}, \qquad\text{(24)} since λt−Bλ=min⁡(ρb,ρc)−max⁡(ρa,ρe)\lambda_t-B_\lambda=\min(\rho_b,\rho_c)-\max(\rho_a,\rho_e). It follows from (21), [W]’s operator/HS rate equality, and (25). The carry has not been deleted. No uniform positive gap on the whole unbounded cone is claimed.

Scope and complete sources

[W] already proves the exact global three-variable rate and its one-dimensional reduction, but does not assert arbitrary-weight uniqueness. [G] already proves every fixed Gaussian correction order in a small real tilt chamber. The addition here is the global critical-point classification, its full strict-interior weighted Gaussian phase, and the explicit unbounded sufficient cones for the complete source gap. No new general Laplace method or sine-factorial expansion is claimed.

The estimates do not identify a geometric metric for an arbitrary short component, match full boundary marginals, or supply a relative error for a prescribed closed scalar. The original turns, caps, source normalization and exterior still enter any such composition. There is no new volume lower bound or finite-exception theorem.
