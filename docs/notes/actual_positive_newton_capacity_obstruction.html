The positive Newton capacity can exceed the complete rate

30 October 2026. Independently reviewed. This concerns the proposed absolute Newton majorant, not the original alternating low-color source. The exact local kernel and one-port source interface below apply to the proved flat-plat compiler. The obstruction family is an actual certified matched graph with specified positive determinant polynomials; its full original-source identification is CONDITIONAL on a compiler adapter described in section3. All remaining half-twist signs are explicitly ZERO, so these polynomial Newton coefficients are nonnegative and maximal-label restriction is a lower bound. No signed-source conclusion is made.

0. The exact original Newton coefficient

Put q=exp(pi i/N), d=N-1, s_j=2sin(pi j/N), S_j=product_(k=1)^j s_k, and B_r(z)=product_(k=0)(r-1)(q(2k+1)+q^(-2k-1)-z), Bhat_r=B_r/S_r^2. For the ORIGINAL untwisted face operator T of the exact one-port correspondence, including its q^(dp) character, the finite identity is

Bhat_r(-T)c^m = sum_(0<=i,j<=r; i+j<=m)
   [m r]_s [m+r-i-j r]_s [r i]_s [r j]_s
                    x^i y^j c^(m-i-j),          (0)

for 0<=m,r<N. Here [n r]s=product(k=1)^r s_(n-r+k)/s_k; it is zero for n<r. Terms with second upper index>=N have the exact numerator zero s_N. There is no vanishing denominator. Thus every coefficient is nonnegative. Moreover T and Bhat_r(-T) commute with multiplication by c^N; the higher block is preserved, not wrapped. A proof is given in the appendix.

Let P_rem be the original product of the remaining positive circle polynomials before merging b,c. The actual positive one-port coefficient is Z_r=[L^(d F_sm)] [Bhat_r(-T)P_rem]_(c=b,u=v=0)>=0. Its source scalar is N^(R-1) product_remaining(k_a beta_a^2), since every remaining epsilon is0. The source theorem retains its ordinary caps, mark, physical support and finite-grid measure. Input terms divisible by c^N cannot reach the ordinary merged target d<N. This one-port original-source identity assumes connected identity smoothing and distinct unmarked middle faces, exactly as in its linked source theorem. It does not extend the graph family through an absent flat-plat adapter.

For 1<=h<=floor(N/2), the exact original Chebyshev–Newton identity is

T_h=sum_(r=0)^(h-1) (-1)^(h-1+r) b_(h,r) Bhat_r(-T),
b_(h,r)=S_r^2 S_(h+r)/[S_(2r+1)S_(h-r-1)]>0,
b_(h,r)<=N/2.

The last uniform bound follows from the actual sine product: its log-concavity in h puts the maximum at h=N/2; double-angle cancellation gives 2 product_(k=1)^r s_(2k)/product_(k=0)^r s_(2k+1). Its successive ratio changes once from <=1 to >=1, so the two endpoints suffice. They are csc(pi/N) and either N/2 or csc(pi/N), all <=N/2. This is also proved in the pure-leaf calculation. The majorant studied here is the TRUE sum sum_r b_(h,r)Z_r, retaining these magnitudes rather than replacing all of them by N/2.

1. The complete shared-face angle problem

Use one actual untwisted port with distinct unmarked middle faces b,c and distinct unmarked outer faces x,y. Its two removed triangles are (x,b,y),(x,c,y). Retain ALL other original triangle incidences and the original marked faces u,v. Let R be the number of original circles. For every remaining triangle t and incident face f introduce a_tf>=0, with sum_f a_tf=1/2. Its center angle is theta_tf=2pi a_tf. Corners at u,v are fixed zero. The exact fold local entropy is

S_t(a_t)=V(2pi a_t)/(2pi),
V(theta)=2 sum_j[Lambda(theta_j/2)+Lambda((pi-theta_j)/2)].

These are the source-normalized triangle entropies; no local constant or exterior phase is reset. Put rho=r/N, mu=m/N, xi=i/N, eta=j/N for the exact positive Newton coefficient (0). Its limiting domain is

0<=rho<=1/2, rho<=mu<=1,
0<=xi,eta<=rho, xi+eta<=mu,
mu+rho-xi-eta<=1.                               (1)

The true physical cap is rho<h/N; it approaches1/2 when h=floor(N/2). The actual root-zero wall at the last boundary is interpreted by approach from its nonzero side, not as replacing a vanished coefficient. Let K(mu,rho,xi,eta) be

J(mu)+J(mu+rho-xi-eta)-J(mu-rho)-J(mu-xi-eta)
  -J(xi)-J(rho-xi)-J(eta)-J(rho-eta),           (2)

where J(t)=integral_0^t log(2sin(pi z))dz. This is the sum of four ideal-tetrahedron volumes/pi, with normalized angle triples

(rho,mu-rho,1-mu),
(rho,mu-xi-eta,1-mu-rho+xi+eta),
(xi,rho-xi,1-rho), (eta,rho-eta,1-rho).

The FULL shared-face constraints are

sum_(t incident f) a_tf =1                 for f outside{x,y,b,c,u,v},
sum_t a_tx=1-xi,       sum_t a_ty=1-eta,
sum_t a_tb=1-mu+xi+eta,
sum_t a_tc=mu.                                  (3)

They follow directly from the exact finite monomial: c^m -> x^i y^j c^(m-i-j), followed by c=b and target exponent d. The input b exponent is d-m+i+j. Thus these are not factorwise maxima or independently chosen local degrees. Their sum is R-1, exactly the total remaining triangle degree. Define

C_Newt=max_(1),(3) [sum_remaining S_t+K].        (4)

The polytope is compact and the objective continuous and concave. It is the natural shared capacity for the positive one-port Newton coefficient with every other channel at maximal fold. The ordinary source polynomial and its normalized Newton kernel are nonnegative. The source measure N^(R-1), k factors and odd maximal-fold beta=1 are subexponential; no exponential normalization is omitted.

2. Euler–Lagrange equations and the lost phase

At a strict constrained maximum let l_f>0 be the shared face multipliers. Use the Lagrangian sum S_t+K+sum_f log(l_f)(sum_t a_tf-demand_f). The triangle equations are exactly the original source circle-angle equations, tan(theta_tf/2) proportional to l_f. Write s(t)=sin(pi t). The remaining interior equations are

s(mu)s(mu+rho-xi-eta) /
   [s(mu-rho)s(mu-xi-eta)] = l_c/l_b,

s(mu-xi-eta)s(rho-xi) /
   [s(mu+rho-xi-eta)s(xi)] = l_b/l_x,

s(mu-xi-eta)s(rho-eta) /
   [s(mu+rho-xi-eta)s(eta)] = l_b/l_y,

s(mu+rho-xi-eta)s(mu-rho) /
   [s(rho-xi)s(rho-eta)] =1.                    (5)

At an active rho boundary the last equality is replaced by its KKT inequality. These equations do not retain the original factor (-1)^(h-1+r) in the low-color Newton sum. In the actual complex stationarity equation that factor contributes the linear character pi i rho (with its fixed lattice mark); its exponential rho equation would have the opposite sign. Dropping it is therefore a change of critical scheme, not a harmless choice of a logarithm.

3. A certified two-sided Apollonian matched-graph family

Start from the actual octahedral-white source used in the frozen smoothing-fixed-radius note: white faces0,1,2,3 form the equator, 4,5 are the two poles, matching edges are01,12,34,35, and marked MAIN23 is unchanged. In the marked chart, faces2,3 are the parallel lines y=0,2; circles0,1 have curvatures2 and centers(0,1/2),(0,3/2), and circles4,5 have curvature1 and centers(-sqrt2,1),(sqrt2,1).

Make n literal Apollonian insertions into triangle(0,1,4), and n into triangle(0,1,5), each successive insertion inside the triangle adjacent to the retained matched edge01. On each side the new matching edge is the previous-middle/new-middle edge, exactly as in the frozen one-sided construction. Both constructions have disjoint interiors. The old matching01 remains; marked MAIN23 is untouched. The explicit small disks have curvatures

B_n=4n^2+4sqrt2 n+1

and centers (+/- (2n+sqrt2)/B_n,1). The already proved tangent-disk and supporting-halfspace induction applies separately on the two sides. Thus this is an actual connected certified augmented matched graph, not an arbitrary angle graph. Identity smoothing at01 merges its two opposite final middle faces b_n,c_n; its two chains keep the smoothed projection connected and the marked faces distinct.

There is an essential presentation qualification. The embedded dual and chosen matching give a literal general Morse augmented diagram, with its original cyclic ports. The currently proved marked coefficient compiler, however, assumes a fixed FLAT PLAT with k bottom cups, k top caps and its specified gap sweep. No adapter has yet been proved that carries the successive local subdivisions into that compiler while preserving this positive coefficient formula. Nor is a common bridge width over all n asserted. Choosing a fixed depth gives a finite graph, but a general link-to-plat isotopy by itself is not the missing adapter. Consequently the unconditional obstruction below concerns its specified positive determinant polynomial and capacity. Its conclusion for the original flat-plat quantum source requires that actual adapter; the exact one-port kernel formula alone does not supply it.

The two original triangles at01 now have the SAME curvatures

(l_x,l_b,l_y)=(2,B_n,2),
(l_x,l_c,l_y)=(2,B_n,2).

Put

a_n=(2/pi)atan(1/sqrt(B_n+1)).

Their normalized angles (theta_x,theta_b,theta_y)/pi are (a_n,1-2a_n,a_n), and a_n->0. KEEP EVERY remaining original packing angle exactly as it was. Its demands in (3) are met by

rho=1/4, mu=1/2+a_n, xi=eta=a_n.                (6)

For sufficiently large fixed n these lie strictly in (1), and the four kernel tetrahedron triples are

(1/4,1/4+a_n,1/2-a_n),
(1/4,1/2-a_n,1/4+a_n),
(a_n,1/4-a_n,3/4), (a_n,1/4-a_n,3/4).

Let F0=Vol(Y_n)/(2pi) be the geometric complete capacity of this certified matched graph. Its identification with the original flat-plat complete rate requires the stated compiler adapter. The value of the FULL shared objective at this feasible state exceeds F0 by

E(a_n)=K(1/2+a_n,1/4,a_n,a_n)
               -V(pi a_n,pi(1-2a_n),pi a_n)/pi. (7)

The subtraction is the TWO deleted source triangle entropies, each V/(2pi), not one. By continuity of the actual Lobachevsky functions,

E(a_n) -> 2 Catalan/pi = v_oct/(2pi)>0.          (8)

Indeed the last two kernel tetrahedra flatten, the first two tend to the right ideal tetrahedron with angles(pi/4,pi/4,pi/2), of volume Catalan, while the old two source triangle entropies tend to0. Hence for every sufficiently large FIXED n,

C_Newt >= F0+E(a_n)>F0.                        (9)

This refutes the proposed universal strict comparison even AFTER allowing all shared radii/angles to readjust: (6) is already a feasible full angle state. It does not refute the original volume conjecture.

4. A lower bound for the specified positive coefficient

The following lower-bound step uses only the supplied positive determinant saddle estimates. At odd N, M=d/2, each normalized remaining white polynomial D_M has degree M and nonnegative coefficients. For a fixed strict source-angle triple, its prescribed coefficient with exponents M theta_j/pi+O(1) has exponential rate V(theta)/(2pi). This follows from the SAME PSD contraction proof as the frozen face-coefficient saddle, applied to that single two/three-variable pencil and its prescribed interior logarithmic-gradient target: pair traces give the global torus gap, the Hessian is cN on its homogeneity quotient, and the finite saddle moves o(1). One-variable forced faces are literal monomials. Fixed zero marked corners are deleted before this argument; all other corners in this fixed certified packing are strictly positive.

There are compatible integer exponents with all these triple fractions and all original ordinary face targets. Scale the remaining packing flows by d rather than N: every row then has the integer sum M=d/2, and all face columns outside x,y,b,c already have target d. Round within the bipartite row/face incidence network, allowing the four displayed face columns to vary by O(1). Impose the integer floor and ceiling of each given entry as lower and upper bounds. The row equations and fixed face-column equations form a totally unimodular network matrix. Its bounded feasible polytope has an integral vertex, preserving every fixed integer margin and changing each entry by at most1. Marked zeros stay zero. Strict positive entries stay positive for large N.

The resulting actual remaining input monomial has c exponent m=d(1/2+a_n)+O(1)<N, and its deficits determine i=d a_n+O(1), j=d a_n+O(1), with the merged b target exactly d. Choose r=N/4+O(1) integer. Every finite kernel sine product is then in its strict nonzero domain. The exact Newton coefficient formula and the uniform sine-product estimates show that this ONE product of positive coefficients contributes rate F0+E(a_n). Thus for the specified positive Newton polynomial coefficient at maximal remaining labels,

liminf_(N odd) N^-1 log Z_(r_N) >= F0+E(a_n).   (10)

The specified original-compiler scalar, if its adapter is established, is polynomial and does not change this inequality. For h_N=floor(N/2), r_N=N/4+O(1), the exact coefficient b_(h_N,r_N) has exponential rate zero directly from the exact finite sine products:

lim N^-1 log b_(h_N,r_N)
  =2J(1/4)+J(3/4)-J(1/2)-J(1/4)=0,

using J(3/4)=-J(1/4), J(1/2)=0 and the uniform O(log N) factorial errors. No off-grid color interpolation is needed for this step. Consequently the TRUE absolute Newton expansion

sum_(r<h_N) b_(h_N,r) Z_r

also has lower rate at least F0+E(a_n). This is not an artifact of replacing b by its upper bound N/2.

To obtain a volume-rate upper bound for the alternating polynomial, one would therefore need exponential cancellation in the retained signs (-1)^(h_N-1+r), possibly together with remaining source sums. No such upper bound or cancellation theorem has been proved here. The positive-polynomial majorant cannot supply a capacity comparison on all certified matched graphs. Without the presentation adapter this is not a claimed flat-plat source counterexample.

Finally, (9) prevents an interpretation of the maximizing positive capacity as Vol(rho)/(2pi) for a representation of the SAME original group with the actual source normalization. Such an interpretation would contradict the independently proved universal representation- volume bound. The missing Newton phase in (5) identifies a concrete reason that the positive critical scheme is not the original one.

Appendix: proof of the finite positive Newton identity

One may first work at generic q, writing s_j=(qj-q-j)/i and c_j=q(2j+1)+q(-2j-1). The original operator acts by

T c^m=-c_m c^m+s_m^2(x+y)c^(m-1)
                       +s_m s_(m-1)xy c^(m-2). (A1)

The literal coefficients at the bottom two rows are zero, so there are no negative powers. Define

D(c^m)=s_m c^(m-1),
R_j(c^k)=s_(k+2j+2)c^(k+1)
         +s_(k+j+1)(x+y)c^k+s_k xy c^(k-1).

Direct computation gives

R_0 D=T+c_0,
D R_j=R_(j+1)D+s_1 s_(2j+2)I,
sum_(j=0)^(r-1) s_1 s_(2j+2)=c_0-c_r.

It follows inductively that

B_r(-T)=R_0 R_1...R_(r-1)D^r.                   (A4)

This already proves the exact zero for r>m. For r<=m, the coefficient of x^i y^j c^(m-i-j) asserted by (0), after multiplication by S_r^2, is

C_r(m;i,j)=
 S_m S_(m+r-i-j) S_r^2 /
 [S_(m-r)S_(m-i-j)S_i S_(r-i)S_j S_(r-j)].       (A5)

The recurrence B_(r+1)(-T)=(T+c_r)B_r(-T), using (A1), proves (A5). For completeness, set k=m-i-j. After dividing on the generic open set and clearing the factors, the only identity needed is

s_(k-r)s_(r+1-i)s_(r+1-j) +s_(k+1)[s_i s_(r+1-j)+s_j s_(r+1-i)] +s_(k+r+2)s_i s_j =s_(k+i+j-r)s_(r+1)^2. (A6)

This follows by substituting s_a=(qa-q-a)/i. The zero-index boundary terms of the recurrence are recovered after clearing denominators, or directly from (A4). Thus the generic polynomial identity holds. Specialize it through the finite products in (0): all denominators S_r,S_i,S_j,S_(r-i),S_(r-j) are nonzero for r<N. If m+r-i-j<N, all surviving sine factors are positive; otherwise its interval of r consecutive numerator factors contains s_N, since m-i-j<N. This proves the claimed root positivity.

At the top endpoint r=d, only m=d and i+j=d survive, and every binomial [d choose i]_s equals1. Hence a useful exact endpoint is

Bhat_d(-T)c^d=sum_(i=0)^d x^i y^(d-i),
Bhat_d(-T)c^m=0 for m<d.                       (A7)
