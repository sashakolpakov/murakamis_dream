Fejér entropy signs and the remaining homogeneous-tail comparison

31 October 2026. This checks the actual closed local coefficient entropy and full-kernel entropy. It disproves nonnegativity of the full local entropy, proves strict positivity at every port of the original simple-white geometric flow, and gives a scoped shared-capacity reduction. No estimate for a complementary source with arbitrary signed crossing powers follows.

1. An exact source coefficient disproves nonnegativity

Use the original even level N=2mN=2m, d=N−1d=N-1, M=m−1M=m-1, with

sr=2sin⁡(πr/N),ar=4cos⁡2(π(r+1/2)N). s_r=2\sin(\pi r/N),\qquad a_r=4\cos^2\!\left(\frac{\pi(r+1/2)}N\right).

For a left path from jj to i=0i=0, the coefficient of xjcd−jx^j c^{d-j} has no yy and therefore has no dimer. There is exactly one path, using all single-xx steps. The original half-block identity gives exactly

[xjcd−j]PN=4a0∏r=1jsr2ar,0≤j≤M. [x^j c^{d-j}]P_N=\frac4{a_0}\prod_{r=1}^j\frac{s_r^2}{a_r}, \qquad 0\leq j\leq M.\tag{1}

No other half or cross part has these degrees when j<Mj<M. The source scalar 4/a04/a_0 is retained and has zero exponential rate. For j/N→ν∈(0,1/2)j/N\to\nu\in(0,1/2), the elementary log-sine/cosine Riemann limit, with its integrable lower endpoint, gives

h(ν,0,1−ν,0)=E(0,ν,ν)=2∫0νlog⁡tan⁡(πt)dt<0. h(\nu,0,1-\nu,0)=E(0,\nu,\nu) =2\int_0^\nu\log\tan(\pi t)\,dt<0.\tag{2}

The integral is strictly negative for every 0<ν<1/20<\nu<1/2: its derivative changes sign only at 1/41/4 and its total value at 1/21/2 is zero by t↦1/2−tt\mapsto1/2-t. In particular, for N=4kN=4k and j=kj=k,

h(1/4,0,3/4,0)=−2Gπ,G=−∫0π/4log⁡tan⁡udu>0. h(1/4,0,3/4,0)=-\frac{2G}{\pi},\qquad G=-\int_0^{\pi/4}\log\tan u\,du>0.\tag{3}

Here GG is Catalan’s constant. This is the entropy of an actual coefficient of the full positive numerator, not a factorwise estimate or a missing exponential normalization. Continuity of hh also supplies strictly negative points in the interior of the four-face simplex. Thus restricting away from zero faces cannot repair the assertion h≥0h\geq0.

There is an exact global floor. The closed coefficient formula gives

E(μ,ν,ξ)≥2∫μνlog⁡tan⁡(πt)dt≥−2Gπ. E(\mu,\nu,\xi)\geq2\int_\mu^\nu\log\tan(\pi t)\,dt \geq-\frac{2G}{\pi}.

The last minimum takes the whole negative interval [0,1/4][0,1/4] and no positive part. The cross entropy is nonnegative by Section 2. Therefore

minsimplexh=−2Gπ. \min_{\text{simplex}}h=-\frac{2G}{\pi}.\tag{4}

2. The geometric port entropy is strictly positive

For a triangle of loads αx+αb+αy=1/2\alpha_x+\alpha_b+\alpha_y=1/2, write

Ctri(𝜶)=∑jf(αj),f(t)=Λ(πt)+Λ(π/2−πt)π. C_{\mathrm{tri}}(\boldsymbol\alpha)=\sum_j f(\alpha_j),\qquad f(t)=\frac{\Lambda(\pi t)+\Lambda(\pi/2-\pi t)}{\pi}.

Direct differentiation gives f′(t)=log⁡cot⁡(πt)f'(t)=\log\cot(\pi t), while f(0)=f(1/2)=0f(0)=f(1/2)=0 and f(1/2−t)=f(t)f(1/2-t)=f(t). Hence f(t)>0f(t)>0 for 0<t<1/20<t<1/2. Thus Ctri≥0C_{\mathrm{tri}}\geq0, with equality only at a triangle vertex. In particular every cross port has h≥0h\geq0, including all seams.

Now use the actual simple white triangulation, with the two adjacent protected faces u,vu,v joined by the marked MAIN edge. A matching port has triangles (x,b,y)(x,b,y) and (x,c,y)(x,c,y), with distinct middle vertices b,cb,c and common MATCHING edge xyxy. The geometric ordinary curvatures are strictly positive; the two protected curvatures are zero. In the actual angle formula a corner load is positive exactly when its curvature is positive, except that the one remaining positive corner in a two-line triangle carries the whole 1/21/2.

The two triangles cannot both have zero entropy. If x,yx,y are ordinary, both triangles have at least two positive corners. If exactly one of x,yx,y is protected, at most one of the distinct b,cb,c can be the other protected vertex, so the other triangle has at least two positive corners. Both x,yx,y cannot be protected, because their edge is MATCHING while uvuv is the marked MAIN edge. These exhaust the cases.

Therefore the geometric split has a strictly positive sum of its two triangle entropies at every port ee. The definition of hh as the maximum over cross splits yields

h(𝒒geom,e)>0for every e. h(\mathbf q_{\mathrm{geom},e})>0\qquad\text{for every }e.\tag{5}

This is pointwise for each fixed certified diagram. No diagram-uniform positive lower bound is asserted. Simplicity and the actual MAIN/MATCHING label are used; the conclusion is not silently extended to a repeated-incidence multigraph.

3. Exact mixed rate and a strict partial comparison

Let JJ be any nonempty set of ports. Replace their positive full kernels by

hd(x,b,c,y)=∑a+i+t+s=da,i,t,s≥0xabictys, h_d(x,b,c,y)=\sum_{\substack{a+i+t+s=d\\a,i,t,s\geq0}}x^a b^i c^t y^s,

whose every coefficient is one. This polynomial has the full degree-dd simplex support and local coefficient entropy zero. It is only the absolute homogeneous bound supplied by the far-tail Fourier adapter; it is not identified with the identity coupon H1H_1 or any original covariant coupon.

Keep FNF_N at all other ports, with its original parity-appropriate positive definition, all original ordinary targets dd, and both protected targets zero. The same unrestricted degree-dd bipartite-flow rounding and uniform local entropy theorem prove that this positive mixed coefficient has rate

ΨJ=max𝒒∈𝒫DHJ(𝒒),HJ(𝒒)=∑e∉Jh(𝒒e). \Psi_J=\max_{\mathbf q\in\mathcal P_D}H_J(\mathbf q),\qquad H_J(\mathbf q)=\sum_{e\notin J}h(\mathbf q_e).\tag{6}

Here 𝒫D\mathcal P_D is the original marked flow polytope. The number of arrays is polynomial in NN; floor/ceiling cycle rounding respects the actual balances and incurs only O(1/N)O(1/N) in local loads. Full supports eliminate a fixed-pattern feasibility issue. This argument is valid at both parities using the established common local hh.

The endpoint theorem and unique geometric maximum give

H(𝒒)=∑eh(𝒒e)≤ΦD,H(𝒒)=ΦD⇔𝒒=𝒒geom. H(\mathbf q)=\sum_e h(\mathbf q_e)\leq\Phi_D,\qquad H(\mathbf q)=\Phi_D\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \mathbf q=\mathbf q_{\mathrm{geom}}.

Define the compact restricted set

𝒦J+={𝒒∈𝒫D:h(𝒒e)≥0 for every e∈J}. \mathcal K_J^+=\{\mathbf q\in\mathcal P_D:h(\mathbf q_e)\geq0\text{ for every }e\in J\}.

There is a strict gap on this set:

max𝒒∈𝒦J+HJ(𝒒)<ΦD. \max_{\mathbf q\in\mathcal K_J^+}H_J(\mathbf q)<\Phi_D.\tag{7}

Indeed HJ≤H≤ΦDH_J\leq H\leq\Phi_D there. Equality at a maximizer would force H=ΦDH=\Phi_D and every removed entropy zero. Uniqueness would then give 𝒒=𝒒geom\mathbf q=\mathbf q_{\mathrm{geom}}, contradicting (5) and J≠⌀J\ne\varnothing. Compactness and continuity turn this pointwise exclusion into a positive fixed-diagram gap.

Consequently, if the full comparison ΨJ<ΦD\Psi_J<\Phi_D fails, every maximizing flow reaching or exceeding ΦD\Phi_D has at least one inserted port with h(𝒒e)<0h(\mathbf q_e)<0. Such a port is strictly one-sided: one middle exceeds 1/21/2, its interval has positive length, and it is neither a pure-middle diagonal nor a cross seam. These overloaded edges obey the same original rooted-forest constraint, because each ordinary face has total demand 11, each protected face demand 00, and every original middle-gap cycle contains the protected exterior. Subsets of those edges remain forests.

This is a general reduction of the remaining hdh_d majorant problem to negative-entropy one-sided forest branches. It is not a bound for those branches. The tempting deduction from unique geometric maximality alone is invalid, since replacing a negative hh by zero increases the local objective. Formula (4) gives only the crude unconditional estimate

ΨJ≤ΦD+|J|2Gπ, \Psi_J\leq\Phi_D+|J|\frac{2G}{\pi},

which is not the desired strict gap.

The exact far Fourier tails retain covariance and alternating spectral data that the hdh_d majorant discards. Neither (6) nor (7) is a bound for a complementary source with arbitrary remaining signed RR powers; that stronger source problem still requires its own estimate.

Validation and scope

The local negative-entropy obstruction does not disprove a general shared-source bound, and the positive mixed comparison remains restricted to the explicitly stated subset of its original flow polytope.
