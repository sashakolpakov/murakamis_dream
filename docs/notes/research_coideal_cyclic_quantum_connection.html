A cyclic quantum connection for the actual marked overlap

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The opposed marked overlap satisfies an exact first-order equation on its finite coideal clock. The equation has determinant four and a polynomially bounded inverse. Its homogeneous second-order equation has one normalized cyclotomic source line, without inverting the level. Thus this particular local scalar can be computed by a conditioned quadratic residual problem.

The same displayed coefficients select a unique rational solution of the unreduced cyclic equation. It is invariant under the actual two-sheeted height map. On each compact set outside the limiting height cut, this specified representative converges holomorphically to an explicit nonzero rational function. The result does not identify it with a moving-color period or authorize crossing its extra poles.

5. A rigid rational lift of the displayed equation

8. What is and is not selected

The lift (23) is fixed by an explicit source-derived coefficient equation and cyclic consistency. It agrees with the whole finite scalar operator and supplies a nonzero complex-height representative on every fixed off-cut compact. It is not the assertion that the degree-<N<N polynomial interpolant has the same off-grid bound. Those are different representatives of the same finite clock class.

The possible poles (30) accumulate at the limiting cut. A contour deformation crossing them must keep its residue terms. Nor does the grid congruence identify this rational lift with an independently defined color-deformed source, its meridian derivatives, or a moving quantum-parameter family. Such an identification requires its actual intertwiners and source comparison. No arbitrary complex continuation has been inferred from the real asymptotic.

[W] already proves whole-source clock recovery, cleared equations and the distinction between abstract normal fibers and value fibers. [C] already gives explicit cyclic scalar connections, source lattices and resonant flags with their actual caps. No novelty for quantum connections, finite geometric inverses or cyclic period methods is claimed. The additions are this overlap’s normalized rank-one cyclotomic source, exact determinant and inverse bounds, and the specified rational height representative with its quantitative off-cut asymptotic.

The missing global quantity remains the original coherent scalar after an arbitrary fixed short partial filling. This note does not construct its geometric trial field, select its contour, identify the auxiliary symbol with a knot peripheral curve, or prove the finite-exception volume theorem.
