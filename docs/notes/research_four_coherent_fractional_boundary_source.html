Fractional boundary data for the original four-input scalar

22 November 2026. Written research proof. Earlier author-final notes and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

We retain the full finite pole packet that replaces the three-input Cauchy pole when one boundary angle is of order N−1N^{-1}. A second finite rectangle evaluates this packet. Its right connector has a smaller exponential rate; it is not discarded as a polynomial error. The resulting estimate concerns the original scalar.
