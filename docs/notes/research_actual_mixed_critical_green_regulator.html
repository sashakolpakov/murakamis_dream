Correct mixed-color symbols at a critical signed Green collar

31 October 2026. This local source identity uses the actual transported auxiliary colors. It corrects the color error in the quarantined 20/25-symbol notes: their universal Gauss maps were correct, but their symbols assigned color 1 to an auxiliary strand whose color is not 1.

The result below concerns the ACTUAL mixed crossing Bloch sum on the stated regular critical collar. It does not identify that sum with the original globally sewn relative fundamental class. The latter has a nonunipotent peripheral-decoration correction still to check.

1. Literal mixed source and its monomial terms

Use actual_mixed_crossing_circle_preferred_longitude.md, sections1–3. For input colors chi1,chi2, physical A=chi1/K1,B=chi2/K2 and C=1/(1-E1 F2), the literal positive mixed coefficient gives

beta_mix=[B]+[C]+[chi1²/A]-[BC]-[chi1² C/A].              (1)

Thus, in the universal Gauss coordinates, the five shapes are

chi2/K2, 1/d, chi1 K1, chi2/(K2 d), chi1 K1/d,
with coefficients +,+,+,-,- and d=1-E1 F2.              (2)

The colors exchange places under the actual crossing. The same universal Gauss update applies to arbitrary Casimirs; this is why the previously verified matrix maps survive the correction.

There is also an explicit non-dilog part. Let l1=Log chi1, l2=Log chi2, a=Log A,b=Log B,t=Log C, with the original unwrapped Cartan and parity branches. Writing the leading coefficient as exp[N W/(2pi i)], the literal mixed formula gives

W=sum beta_mix Li2 - Li2(1) + W_mono,
W_mono=l1 l2 +t²/2-l1(b+2t)-l2 a+l2 t
                     +(a-t)(b+t)+pi i t.               (3)

Indeed these terms come respectively from q^(alpha beta/2), q^(k²-k), chi1^(-b_index-2k), chi2^(-a_index+k), Q^((a_index-k)(b_index+k)), and (-1)^k. The -k in q^(k²-k) is a finite prefactor, not an order-N action term. The coefficient Li2(1) is real. Equation (3) is not permission to remove the closing pivot, source cups/caps or integer marks.

All non-dilog terms in (3) have the real-quadratic/imaginary-linear form of the recorded wall-regulator identity. Therefore the exact matched-sheet relation is

Im W-D(beta_mix)=rho dot Im(gradient W),                (4)

including ALL logarithmic variables and monomial terms. At an open crossing the right side need not vanish. A unit-modulus color makes its own rho_color zero, not every boundary contribution. The original auxiliary pivot and source closure phases must still be sewn before invoking any global full-stationarity cancellation.

2. Actual auxiliary colors from the selected raw flag

Keep the critical physical chart of the quarantined algebraic lemma:

s=sin(pi delta), k=cos(pi delta), 0<delta<1/2,
c=b+(x+y)/s², R0=-1, sigma=tr P=-2+4s².

The main colors are exactly-1. In the source flag convention a raw head G has selected eigenvalue chi^-1, while the physical meridian is -G. This fixes the auxiliary color, rather than inferring it from the symmetric trace alone.

For the displayed untwisted raw auxiliary

M0=(2P-sigma I)/(4sk),

its selected J-line eigenvalue is +i, so chi_aux=-i. Reversing its central lift reverses chi_aux. For the displayed retained-half matrices

M_+=((sigma+1)I-P)/(2s), M_-=-(P+I)/(2s),

the selected raw eigenvalue is -i exp(pi i epsilon delta); therefore

chi_aux=i exp(-pi i epsilon delta)=epsilon s+i k.       (5)

Again a central lift multiplies both the raw eigenvalue and chi by the same sign. Independently, choosing the RECIPROCAL raw P eigenflag replaces the displayed eigenvalue and chi by their inverses. This is a separate choice, especially for retained halves. All twelve sign/lift/flag combinations have been checked. The verifier proves chi times its chosen raw eigenvalue is1, chi+chi^-1 is the actual raw trace, and chi conjugate(chi)=1. This covers either actual scalar mode once its original flag has been selected; it does not identify the modes by trace alone or conflate the raw chi^-1 flag with the physical positive-M flag.

The true preferred SL2 longitude remains lambda=P. Its centered version ell=-P carries the explicit mixed-pivot character. For epsilon=+/-1, M_epsilon² lambda^epsilon=I, whereas M_epsilon² ell^epsilon=-I. For epsilon0, M0²=-I. These give the same killed peripheral word in PSL but different recorded central characters; they are not conflated here.

The flag conversion can be stated without a reciprocal ambiguity. Let f=+1 denote the raw chi^-1 flag on the P=J line and f=-1 the reciprocal line. The literal positive physical eigenvalue is M=-chi, so its preferred longitude eigenvalue is lambda=J^(-f), whereas its centered eigenvalue is ell=-J^(-f)=exp(2pi i f delta). Thus V=4pi i f delta; the earlier V=-4pi i delta convention is f=-1. For a half-twisted displayed raw matrix,

chi=lift*f*i*exp(-pi i f epsilon delta),
U=2Log(-lift*f*i)-2pi i f epsilon delta=U0-epsilon V/2.

For the untwisted displayed matrix chi=-liftfi. The difference of the displayed central origins is retained: H/(2k) tends to-I, so matching one external-color lift across the half-twist convention may flip the auxiliary central sign. No unrecorded logarithm reset is made. Covering both f and both lifts avoids assigning the original scalar mode from trace alone.
