The original edge kernel on complex angular rectangles

Research proof, 3 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

Theorem 3 in Section 6 proves the full original kernel expansion on |ℜt|,|ℜu|≤π/2|\Re t|,|\Re u|\le\pi/2, |ℑt|,|ℑu|≤3/10|\Im t|,|\Im u|\le3/10, including both level parities and ordinary boundary derivatives. Section 5 proves the intermediate width-one rectangle; the earlier thin-rectangle proof is retained as well.

The wider proofs separate the constant and nonconstant parts of the cotangent contour, retain every original Newton index, and control all endpoint integrals. The final extension uses a rigorous fixed-patch endpoint certificate together with analytic bounds for the other regions. It proves the coefficient of the finite sum; the classical Hessian alone would not establish that coefficient.
