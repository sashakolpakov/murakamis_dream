The complete original circle has a complex packet through the quarter transition

29 October 2026. The actual complete-circle polynomial z_N has a uniform nonzero asymptotic on a FIXED complex neighborhood of c=-2. The source is continued coherently through the quarter-index modulus crossing, using an exponentially accurate analytic diagonalization, a genuine high-index source anchor, and an explicit negative cumulative action for the unwanted channel. No phase-sector assumption is made.

This is a fixed-root spectral/color-coordinate theorem. Its parameter below is NOT declared to be a peripheral meridian or an independently changed auxiliary-circle color. Such a source identification requires its own exact transform. All physical Casimir normal derivatives remain in the same original polynomial.

1. Statement and original finite source

Keep N,d,q,Q,kappa,M,F_r,s_r and the exact A_r,D_r,U_r of the coherent inner source. Set u=pi r/N, nu_r=(-1)^r q1 and define the EXACT chirp-normalized source on its physical grid by

W_(N,r)=kappa^(-1)e^(i pi d/4)U_r/nu_r.

The original Newton coefficient is EXACTLY

b_(N,r)=e^(-i pi d/4) [s_(2r)/s_r^6] W_(N,r),
z_N(c)=N^2 sum_(r=0)^M b_(N,r) product_(a<r)(c_a-c).  (1)

This follows by inserting D_r and the original factorial phases; no absolute value replaces the inner sum. The established source formula on strict high-index compact sets is

W_(N,r)=w_0(u)[1+O(1/N)],
w_0(u)=e^(iu/2)sqrt(sin u), u>pi/4.                 (2)

There is eta_0>0 such that UNIFORMLY for |eta|<=eta_0,

z_N(-2cosh eta)=e^(-i pi d/4)a(eta)exp[N j(eta)]
                                               [1+O(1/N)], (3)
u_*(eta)=atan(cosh(eta/2)), T=2cosh eta,
a(eta)=e^[i u_*(eta)/2]/[2((T+2)(T+6))^(1/4)],
j(eta)=Psi(u_*(eta),eta),
Psi(u,eta)=[-Lambda(2u)+6Lambda(u)]/pi
         +(1/(2pi))integral_0^(2u)Log(T+2cos v)dv.

All branches are continued from real u=pi/4, eta=0; Lambda denotes its holomorphic local continuation in the displayed regular arguments. The amplitude is nonzero. Both a and j are even holomorphic functions of eta, so (3) is equivalently a holomorphic statement on a fixed disk in c=-2cosh eta about -2. It includes all fixed-order normal derivatives on a smaller disk by Cauchy’s estimate.

2. The exact direction of transport and a source barrier

The original backward Ward for U gives

W_r=a_N(u)W_(r+1)+b_N(u)W_(r+2),                    (4)
a_N=Q^(-1)(2Q^3Y^2+Q+1)/(1-QY)^2,
b_N=-Q^(-1)(1-Q^3Y^2)(1+Q^2Y)
                         /[(1-QY)^2(1-Q^2Y)], Y=e^(2iu).

Thus X_r=(W_r,W_(r+1))^T propagates from HIGH r to LOW r: X_r=T_N(u_r)X_(r+1), T_N=[[a_N,b_N],[1,0]]. Its principal eigenvalues in this direction are

lambda_slow=1, lambda_fast=-cot(u)^2.                 (5)

They are distinct and nonzero near pi/4, including at the modulus crossing. That fact alone is insufficient for a selected-source claim.

Use the proved analytic diagonalization lemma on a fixed complex neighborhood of pi/4, with the shift direction in (4). The same proof with the reversed shift supplies bounded V_N=V_0+O(1/N), nonzero diagonal d_s,d_f and R_N=O(exp(-sigma N)) such that EXACTLY

V_N(u)^(-1)T_N(u)V_N(u+pi/N)=diag(d_s,d_f)+R_N.       (6)

The hypotheses here are explicit rational functions, not an unknown solution bound. On |Re u-pi/4|,|Im u|<=pi/32 the sine and cosine are bounded away from zero. The supplied eigenframe has columns (1,1)T,(-cot2u,1)^T; it and its inverse are bounded. One may use conservative bounds K=20, nonzero-root/gap bounds1/4 and eventual diagonal bound M=20, after rescaling x=u/pi and taking stadium radius 1/256. The rational coefficients have T_N-T_0=O(1/N) there, directly from Q-1=O(1/N) and their nonzero denominators. Hence the lemma gives a COMPUTABLE fixed sigma>0. Constants may be coarse; no optimal conversion exponent is needed.

Choose a fixed sufficiently small delta>0, h=delta/4, and a high anchor grid point u_b=pi/4+2delta+O(1/N). Require delta<pi/4096 and 16delta^2/pi<sigma/4. Propagate(6) over [pi/4-h,u_b]. The cumulative REAL logarithmic action of the fast channel relative to the slow one is

A_f(u)=pi^(-1)integral_u^(u_b)2log cot v dv.           (7)

This is the sign appropriate to HIGH-to-LOW transport, not its inverse. On this interval the total diagonal action width is at most 16delta^2/pi+O(1/N). For every target |u-pi/4|<=h the action in(7) is at most -gamma_0 for a fixed gamma_0>0. For example -8<=d(2log cot v)/dv<=-4 on the small window; the negative contribution from pi/4 to pi/4+2delta exceeds the possible positive contribution from pi/4-h to pi/4 by a fixed multiple of delta^2.

At the high anchor, the ACTUAL source vector from(2) is w_0(u_b)(1,1)^T+O(1/N). Its slow component in V_N is bounded away from zero, and its fast component is O(1/N). Both entries are literal finite original U sums. In the interaction picture, all off-diagonal terms in(6) are bounded by exp[-N(sigma-width)]. Their product is I+O(N exp[-N(sigma-width)]). Equations(2),(7) therefore give, on the whole target grid, the selected slow solution with error O(exp(-gamma N)), for some fixed gamma>0. The other actual source component and every conversion term have been estimated, not deleted. There is no assumed adiabatic survival across a long crossing interval.

3. A holomorphic interpolant of the SAME selected grid packet

The slow diagonal has d_s=1+O(1/N), holomorphically. Apply optimally truncated Euler–Maclaurin to the sum of Log d_s from u to u_b and multiply by the first component of V_N and its EXACT source coefficient at u_b. Construct it first on a fixed smaller complex tube containing the ENTIRE interval [pi/4-h,u_b], and then restrict to a still smaller tube around |u-pi/4|<=h. This defines a zero-free holomorphic function W_hat_N(u); its high-anchor value is part of the same interpolant, not an independent continuation constant. The usual analytic remainder is explicit: Cauchy bounds on a tube of radius a give a remainder bounded by C N[K/(pi aN)]^(2K); choose K=floor(cN). Thus, after shrinking the tube once,

W_(N,r)=W_hat_N(pi r/N)+O(exp(-gamma' N))             (8)

on its physical grid. This construction uses known coefficient functions and the finite source anchor; its normalization is not defined using the unknown closed circle value.

The same EM formula at u and u+pi/N gives its diagonal shift relation with an exponential holomorphic residual. Together with(6) this makes W_hat_N satisfy(4) with an exponential residual throughout the tube. It and its derivatives are uniformly bounded: Log d_s=O(1/N), its sum is O(1), and the slow eigenvector component stays near1.

Its leading amplitude is explicit, not a new connection constant. Expanding(4), using a_0+b_0=1, gives

W_hat_N'/W_hat_N
   =-2i [partial_Q(a_N+b_N)]_(Q=1)/(a_0+2b_0)+O(1/N)
   =iY/(Y-1)+O(1/N).

The denominator is -4Y/(1-Y)^2, so is nonzero. The high source anchor(2) fixes the solution of this first-order transport on the whole anchor-to-target tube. Integration on the fixed complex tube proves

W_hat_N(u)=e^(iu/2)sqrt(sin u)[1+O(1/N)]              (9)

UNIFORMLY and holomorphically on the smaller tube, now through BOTH sides of pi/4. The exponential grid residual in(8) is separate from the inverse-N leading-amplitude error in(9).

4. Coherent outer sum, retained residuals and the full complex saddle

At eta=0 the old ALL-index envelope of the exact source is J_1(u)+E(u), where J_1’=4log cot u/pi and E’=-2log cot u/pi below pi/4, E=0 above it. Hence its unique maximum is j_0=v_8/pi at pi/4. Every index outside the fixed window in(8) has a strict exponential gap. This includes all lower indices; they have not been set to zero.

The same gap holds uniformly for sufficiently small complex eta. One can bound the Newton factor on its fixed enclosing Bernstein ellipse. Its continuum logarithm converges uniformly to the eta=0 logarithm as the ellipse shrinks: the only endpoint singularity is the integrable log(2+2cos v). Thus first choose the r-window/gap, then the fixed eta disk. The polynomial all-index prefactors do not affect the gap.

On the retained window use(8), with the actual half-shift Newton product and sine factorials. These have zero-free holomorphic interpolants with exponentially small RELATIVE grid residuals. For the sine factorial, separate log(sin v/v), keep Gamma(k+1) exactly, and apply the same optimal EM bound to the regular factor. For the Newton product apply midpoint EM to Log(T+2cos v); its leading prefactor is ONE. All arguments here lie in regular compact strips.

The discarded indices and ALL grid interpolation residuals are bounded absolutely by poly(N)exp[N(j_0-g)] for some fixed g>0, uniformly on a sufficiently small complex eta disk. This is not a claim inferred from pointwise O(1/N) continuation. The exact interpolant is used before its leading approximation, just as in the previous mixed-color source.

For the retained finite r-sum choose half-grid vertical endpoints. Its exact cotangent rectangle has vertical |cot(Nu)|<=1. Their source phases have a fixed endpoint gap. At fixed height d_0 the horizontal cotangent errors are O(exp(-2Nd_0)), while the complete phase increases by at most C d_0^2 at eta=0. Choose d_0 small, then shrink the complex eta disk again. All four residual currents are strictly below the nearby saddle. The two constant cotangent parts give the same analytic integral with density N/pi; no integer residue or endpoint term has been discarded.

The exponent is Psi in(3). At eta=0 its unique saddle is pi/4, with Psi_uu=-8/pi. Its analytic continuation is u_*(eta), and stays in the fixed interpolation tube. Ordinary analytic Gaussian expansion, with its fixed nonzero source density from(9), gives(3). Inserting the explicit sine-factorial prefactor gives exactly the stated a(eta). The external exp(-i pi d/4) and the unchanged Cartan normalization are already in(1), at both parities. Alternatively, this analytic leading coefficient is fixed uniquely by its agreement with the already proved real eta>0 formula on the nonempty overlap where the saddle lies strictly above pi/4. This identifies its continuation through eta=0 without an extra endpoint or parity factor.

5. Nodal concentration, original normals, and a retained-belt corollary

The new packet has

j(0)=j_0, j''(0)=1/(2pi),
a(0)=2^(-9/4)e^(i pi/8).

For real theta near0 this yields the whole original nodal packet

z_N(-2cos theta)=e^(-i pi d/4)a(i theta)
                           exp[Nj(i theta)][1+O(1/N)],
j(i theta)=j_0-theta^2/(4pi)+O(theta^4).              (10)

There is also an exponential nodal gap outside every fixed theta neighborhood. For a root c_a in[-2,2], the factor at a unit w obeys |c_a-w-w(-1)|<=|c_a-Rw-(Rw)(-1)| for R>1. Apply the established same-angle circle envelope and let fixed R decrease toward1. Its continuum arc-convolution profile is continuous, has a unique maximum at(theta,u)=(0,pi/4), and has strictly smaller endpoint values. Compactness proves the gap. This uses the coherent U envelope, not a sector claim.

For precision, the convolution is over the symmetric arc[-2u,2u] of g_1(t)=log|1-e^(it)|, centered at pi+theta. For0<u<pi/2, g_1 is strictly increasing with circular distance from0, so this arc integral has its unique maximum at theta=0. At u=0 or pi/2 it is constant in theta, but the resulting all-index actions are strictly less than j_0. Moreover ||g_R-g_1||_(L1(S1))->0, which bounds the difference of ALL these arc integrals uniformly in both u and theta. Thus the limiting argument uses a uniform integral bound, not pointwise logarithmic convergence at a root.

Equation(3) is holomorphic in c, so the original first-normal source is retained by differentiating it on a smaller disk. For example z_N’(-2)/z_N(-2)=-N/(4pi)+O(1). Nothing is replaced by a value-only semisimple trace.

The exact complete-belt source therefore has, for every fixed integer k>=1, the nonzero ALL-parity asymptotic

phi(L_N(z_N^sharp z_N)^k)
   =N^(3/2) 2^[-(9k+1)/2]/sqrt(k)
                                 exp(2kNj_0)[1+O(1/N)]. (11)

Indeed its exact source is N sum_(a=0)(N-1)|z_N(c_a)|(2k). The nodal angles around -2 have step2pi/N, with shift0 for oddN and1/2 for evenN. The Gaussian in(10) gives the same leading sum at both shifts; all other nodes have the proved exponential gap. The derivative source producing this exact belt trace remains literal.

This establishes a genuine complete-circle boundary packet and a whole original retained-belt amplitude. An independently changed ordinary auxiliary color, a filling transform, or a branched six-port sewing must still be identified with this packet by an exact source map. They are not inferred merely from the spectral parameter eta.

Audit: literal finite circle coefficients, quantum recurrence/chirp and boundary Gaussian, the finite checks cover eleven literal all-parity full sources and first normals, exact symbolic chirped recurrence/transport, and six growing-N complex-packet/normal/retained-belt calibrations. The analytic proof, not those finite calibrations, establishes the fixed neighborhood and the exponential current bounds.

The theorem concerns the stated spectral packet and complete retained-belt source. The physical color and filling identification is separate.
