The balanced source normalizer is fixed by its compact Fermat divisor

28 October 2026. The explicit balanced finite source fixes the entire central periodic factor in the trivalent quantum-beta section. For the actual root and the original full-function marks one has EXACTLY

A_N(u)=(N/phi_N) u^N Psi_+(h-Log u),
h=2pi*i/N,
phi_N=sqrt(N)exp{i*pi[1/4-(N+N^(-1))/12]}.               (1)

The factor u^N is indispensable. This is an identity of the actual local source, not a choice of normalization or a comparison after dividing by an unknown knot invariant. The proof uses a compact algebraic curve and its divisor; no asserted published Fourier or cyclic-dilogarithm evaluation is a premise.

1. The exact statement on a balanced source

Let Q=exp h and uN-wN=1. The actual polynomial is

B_N(u,w)=sum_(a=0)^(N-1) product_(j<a)(u-Q^j w).

Its positive Fourier norm proves B_N!=0 whenever w!=0, including u=0. Take z=Log u and c=Log(w/u), with compatible marked continuations. The identity equivalent to (1) is

B_N(u,w)=(N/phi_N)u^N Psi_+(h-z)Psi_+(c)
                                  exp(-cz/h).         (2)

The left side is the original finite polynomial. All full-Psi marks and the scalar on the right are the ones already fixed by its defining integral and original coefficient moments.

Initially take real u>1 and real positive w=(uN-1)(1/N). Set t=u^(-1), s=w/u, so sN+tN=1 with 0<s,t<1. Let

D_N(y)=product_(j=1)^(N-1)(1-Q^j y)^(j/N)

with every factor anchored at y=0. The independently proved rational full-function identity reads

Psi_+(L)=exp[i Li_2(exp(NL))/(2pi*N)]
           (1-exp(NL))^(1+iL/(2pi))/D_N(exp L).       (3)

Use Psi_+(h-z)=Psi_+(-z)/(1-t). Euler’s ordinary dilogarithm identity for sN+tN=1 then cancels ALL logarithmic cross terms in the proposed right side of (2). It becomes

B_cand=K_N s^N/[(1-t)D_N(s)D_N(t)],
K_N=(N/phi_N)exp[i*pi/(12N)].                          (4)

For example the dilogarithm sum is pi2/6+N2cz; the two binomial powers in (3) and exp(-cz/h) cancel that cz term exactly. No transcendental central factor is left in the Nth power of (4):

B_cand^N=K_N^N s^(N^2)/[(1-t)^N
         product_(j=1)^(N-1)(1-Q^j s)^j
         product_(j=1)^(N-1)(1-Q^j t)^j].              (5)

Thus (5) is a rational function on the compact Fermat curve.

2. The divisor of the ACTUAL finite polynomial

Use the smooth projective curve C_N: sN+tN=1. It is connected: its affine polynomial is Eisenstein over C[t] at t-1. In these coordinates the actual finite function is

B_N=sum_(a=0)^(N-1)t^(-a)(s;Q)_a.                     (6)

Its only possible zeros or poles are at s=0, t=0 or infinity. Away from those loci u,w are finite nonzero, and the exact Fourier recurrence proves B_N!=0. At infinity u=0 and w^N=-1; the same recurrence still has every coefficient nonzero, so B_N is finite and nonzero there as well.

At s=0,t=Q^j, one has u=Q^(-j), w=0. The order of B_N is exactly j, for j=0,…,N-1. To prove this, write B_N(u,w)=sum_k b_k(u)w^k. Its finite source recurrence gives

(uQ^k-1)b_k=Q^(k-1)b_(k-1), 1<=k<N.

At u=Q^(-j), the coefficients before k=j vanish; the top coefficient is nonzero, so b_j!=0 by backward recurrence. For j=0 the constant is N. Along the Fermat curve the perturbation of u has order N in w, which does not change this first nonzero order j<N.

At t=0,s=Q^(-k), the order is a POLE of order k, k=0,…,N-1. Indeed s-Q^(-k) has order N in t. In (6), terms a>k therefore have order at least N-a>0, while among a<=k the unique highest pole is a=k with nonzero coefficient. For k=0, B_N tends to one. These calculations also cover every composite and even level.

The divisor of B_N^N is consequently:

zero order Nj at s=0,t=Q^j,
pole order Nk at t=0,s=Q^(-k),

with no other zeros or poles. Orders zero mean ordinary nonzero values, not deleted points.

3. The candidate has the same divisor, including infinity

Read the orders directly from (5). At s=0,t=Q^j, j!=0, t-Q^j has order N in s. The only vanishing t-factor is j’=N-j, with exponent N-j. The numerator has order N^2. The net zero order is N^2-N(N-j)=Nj. At j=0 the factor (1-t)^N instead has order N^2, cancelling the numerator.

At t=0,s=Q^(-k), k!=0, the only vanishing s-factor is j’=k, with order Nk; it gives the required pole. At k=0 no such factor occurs. At infinity both s,t have simple poles. The numerator has degree N^2 and the denominator has degree N+2 sum_(j=1)(N-1)j=N2, with nonzero leading coefficients. Thus (5) has neither a zero nor a pole there.

Therefore B_NN/B_candN is a rational function on the compact connected C_N with no zero or pole. It is constant.

Its constant and its Nth root are fixed by a marked source value, not a free scalar. Take t->0,s->1 on the initial positive branch. Equation (6) gives B_N->1. Also

c=(1/N)Log(1-u^(-N))=-u^(-N)/N+O(u^(-2N)),
Psi_+'(0)=-Psi_+(h)=-phi_N.

The latter follows directly from Psi_+(c+h)=Psi_+(c)/(1-exp c) and its nonzero marked value. Thus the right side of (2) tends to one as well. The constant in the Nth powers is one; their continuous Nth-root ratio is one on this marked branch. Analytic continuation proves (2), and hence (1).

Every deck factor is consistent. Increasing c by h changes w to Qw and multiplies BOTH sides of (2) by 1/(u-w), by the balanced finite source equation and the full-Psi shift. Increasing c or z by 2pi*i has its complete central multiplier canceled by exp(-cz/h). Thus the asserted continuation keeps the original source rather than selecting an independent norm-one trivialization.

4. The actual trivalent source now has no unknown periodic factor

For the original chamber Im u<0, Im(v/u)>0, put c=Log(v/u). The exact beta source is therefore explicitly

B_N(u,v)=S_N(u,v)+(1-u^N+v^N)I_N(u,v),

S_N=(N/phi_N)u^N Psi_+(h-Log u)Psi_+(c)
                                       exp(-c Log u/h). (7)

There is an exact version in the original reflected field variables. Write z=Log U, c_0=Log(V/U), and retain the actual tags u=Q^2U,v=QV with Log u=z+2h and c=c_0-h. The backward full-Psi shift gives the balanced part of its constant coefficient as

C_N^(bal)(U,V)=Q^(2-d)/phi_N
   *U^(N+1)(U-Q^(-1))(U-Q^(-1)V)/V^2
   *Psi_+(-z)Psi_+(c_0)exp(-c_0 z/h), d=N-1.           (7a)

Its remaining original current is exactly

C_N^(current)=Q^(-d)/N*(1-U^N+V^N)I_N(Q^2U,QV).        (7b)

Indeed Psi_+(-z-h)=(1-Q^(-1)/U)Psi_+(-z) and Psi_+(c_0-h)=(1-Q^(-1)V/U)Psi_+(c_0); expanding the Gaussian accounts for Q^2 U3/V2. Thus the full physical right shapes are literally 1/U and V/U. The boundary polynomial, central U^N, Gaussian and Q^(2-d) remain. The bare fiber has the expected nonzero N^(-1/2) factor phi_N^(-1). These equations are at the original physical root; no off-root replacement of Q^N or of its derivatives is being asserted.

Here I_N is its specified upward Barnes contour and is uniformly O(1) on compact positive boundary families. Equation (7) retains the original current; it does not assert that it is negligible for every positive bipyramid.

The full-Psi strip estimate now gives the residue rate on the ENTIRE chamber, including |u|=1 and geometric clock rotations. Uniformly on compact subsets,

log|S_N|=N Lambda(u,v)+O(log N),
Lambda=[ImLi_2(v/u)-ImLi_2(u)
      +(pi-Arg(v/u))log|u|-Arg(u)log|v|]/(2pi).       (8)

Both full-function arguments in (7) stay in regular angular strips. The central source u^N in (7) supplies log|u| to the rate; omitting it changes (8). There is no Diophantine small-factor estimate hidden at |u|=1.

In fact the ENTIRE leading block is explicit. The exact shift Psi_+(h-Log u)=Psi_+(-Log u)/(1-u^(-1)) and the uniform full-function symbol give

S_N=(N/phi_N)
   (1-v/u)^(1/2)/(1-u^(-1))^(1/2)
   exp{[Li_2(v/u)+Li_2(1/u)-Log(v/u)Log u
                                  +2pi*i Log u]/h}
   *[1+O(1/N)].                                     (8a)

All square roots and dilogarithms inherit their original upper-strip marks. The factor N/phi_N retains its exact phase and has modulus sqrt(N). Both shapes v/u and 1/u are upper. The half-density in (8a) is consequently nonzero and bounded above and below, after dividing by sqrt(N), on every compact positive boundary family. The affine 2pi*i Log u term is the retained central source, not an arbitrarily added choice of classical action.

Consequently every compact family with

Lambda>max(0,log|u|,log|v|)

by a fixed positive margin has original local source B_N=S_N(1+O(N^C exp(-eta N))). This extends the local positive-norm growth theorem beyond its elementary |u|<1 product estimate. The exact actual Q shifts u=Q^2U,v=QV and its NQ^d normalization are carried by that theorem unchanged.

Thus in the gap chamber the actual local source has exactly the growing two-face block (8a), with relative error O(1/N)+O(N^C exp(-eta N)). This supplies its nonzero leading coefficient rather than postulating the survival of a saddle.

This is an exact local torsor/source normalization followed by a local noncancellation theorem. A broad knot proof still requires a simultaneous positive-chart and exterior-cycle comparison putting the actual relevant fibers in this gap chamber, or retaining their other currents by another method.
