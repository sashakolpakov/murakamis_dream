A connected holomorphic chamber of the original filling source

Written proof, 24 November 2026.

The entire original filling source has a nonzero asymptotic on a connected complex chamber containing all sufficiently long real signed tuples. The chamber also contains arbitrarily large positive imaginary powers. Uniformity is local on its compact subsets, before parameter differentiation. This gives an actual source logarithm and a calibrated leading logarithmic differential, rather than only formal saddle data. It does not continue the source to an arbitrary short real filling.

6. Coverage and scope

[R] gives the entire Hermite family, [E] the integer endpoint and Fourier analysis, and [C] long integer powers with a complex boundary phase. [A] continues formal geometric coefficients but leaves its actual normalized-source upper estimate open. Here the actual complex-power source is estimated on one connected chamber, including all finite-source errors, endpoints, aliases and fixed jets.

At allowed integers restore the original writhe and peripheral conventions of [M]; no factor has been reset. There is no claimed topological interpretation for noninteger points. Although (4) connects the long real signs through large positive imaginary powers, it does not reach arbitrary fixed short real powers. The chamber does not identify a geometric cycle after such a short partial filling, and gives no new finite-exception or knot-volume theorem.
