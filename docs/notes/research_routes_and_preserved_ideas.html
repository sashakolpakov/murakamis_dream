Research routes and preserved ideas

The new exceptional-period route retains the exact generic Casimir polynomial, its canonical scalar action and general local character relation, the actual continuant resolvent period, strict-support Green estimates, and separate endpoint controls. Finite-propagation windows and the universal marked rectangle bound are retained with their limitations. The coherent Fourier phase and global ordinary Legendre chart remain complementary tools. These results do not replace the cube, positive sewing or other earlier routes, and do not yet prove the general contributing source period.

29 October 2026. This is a navigation and scope record, not a new proof or a replacement for the underlying notes. No route below is abandoned because another has advanced. “Proved” refers to the repository’s internally reviewed research arguments; broader statements remain open.

Start with the current frontier and pretzel-to-general strategy. The objective remains the broad ORIGINAL-source equality with the ACTUAL hyperbolic volume, retaining the mark, normal terms and peripheral normalizations. A projected sector, local volume formula or upper bound is not a substitute for that objective.

1. Pretzels, belt stacks and the general filling mechanism

The complete augmented pretzel proof has an explicit sharp Fourier energy and a separate nonzero original period. The width improvement and geometric equality proof give the stated independently long fixed signed fillings: odd pretzel widths >=9, and the specified two-component even-width links >=10. Widths 3,5,7 are not covered by that alias estimate.

The signed belt-stack assembly is another full-source equality family, with any subset of auxiliary circles independently sufficiently long-filled and no minimum stack width. Its actual diagrams, peripheral words and component counts are part of the theorem.

The reusable general source filling transfer is CONDITIONAL on an actual nonzero even complex packet and an entire physical-color complement gap. The general geometric theorem identifies a genuinely marked nearby representation with the actual filled geometry. Neither theorem supplies missing quantum hypotheses for an arbitrary new graph.

2. Cube, shared cores and projected chains — preserved with their scope

The cube is a concrete primitive-core test, not the asserted general answer. Its actual rectified-core geometry has volume 16v8+2Vol(Q_cube); the extra shared-core term cannot be replaced by a product of local theta rates.

The durable source chain of results is:

  • Literal central cube reduction: exact original marked scalar N^12 S_N^8 phi(h^4), with all twelve channels fixed to the odd Steinberg channel and all phases retained.
  • Positive cuboctahedral period: the real positive sum has the actual Q_cube volume rate, identified by its explicit geometric tetrahedra.
  • Four-rung packet: all four rung labels are summed, including paired normals. Eight cycle labels remain fixed. Its odd-level partial source has the same actual augmented-cube volume rate and a strict unfrozen-color gap.
  • Two-boundary transfer and projected chains: an explicit coupled weighted-Cauchy kernel has the sharp cube-minus- theta norm increment. Typed iterations retain every rung sum but still fix 2+6k cycle labels and use odd levels.

Full cube color summation, cancellation/dominance after removing those cycle projections, even-level cube growth and arbitrary primitive-core graphs remain OPEN. The full-cube diagnostic and its data are preserved as complex128 finite diagnostics, not proofs of positivity, error control or a limit. The scope cautions already appear in the cube proof notes, README and frontier. The projected-chain note is linked explicitly here so it is not lost behind the shorter cube summaries.

3. Positive Conway expressions and Karamata-style variational growth

The actual positive Newton algebra retains every root normal. Its genuine turn, multiplication and closures give a whole-source positive Conway class, without cube channel projections. The variational limit theorem proves an all-level limit as a concave ideal-angle maximum for each fixed robust expression. Exact integer support supplies the lower bound at every level, including boundary maxima.

Its angle triples still need identification with the actual complement’s face/edge incidence. Some expressions have polynomial growth; positivity does not imply hyperbolicity or volume equality. This route is preserved alongside the cube’s more explicitly calibrated but projected result.

4. Fourier, weighted Cauchy and the distinct Tauberian idea

The Cauchy checkpoint and source-preserving weighted Fourier energy record actual metrics and their boundary costs. The whole-support Gram theorem now gives polynomial local source cost on every supported triple, both parities. Exact three-port source sewing and its weighted packet retain correlated labels and the original mark.

Sharp GLOBAL transported energy and nonzero geometric overlap are still separate requirements. The turned-circle inertia obstruction shows why a positive face form does not automatically stay positive through a genuine change of pairing.

The Fourier-energy Tauberian criterion preserves a different use of the Karamata idea: a sharp positive Laplace singularity gives cumulative information, but a full every-level limit also requires cross-level control. That criterion is not silently assumed in the already proved direct pretzel asymptotic.

5. Pointed quantum A-structure and regulator torsors

Keep the A-polynomial/torsor status, intrinsic quantum norm torsor and actual regulator-weighted transfer. They retain the original affine source, normal period, lattice and marked peripheral data. A classical A-curve or an unpointed module alone does not select the original period or its absolute regulator. The explicit transfer calculation also shows that small off-diagonal mixing can become exponentially large after branch-dependent weighting.

The general selected-period/regulator norm comparison and geometric survival remain open. These constructions and obstructions remain available; the explicit-family source proofs do not replace them.

6. Generic four-port kernels and actual local volume

The all-port single Racah kernel is an exact generic source identity with its row/column normalization. The positive scalar/normal theorem and hyperideal geometry theorem give a local quantum-volume law, uniformly over supported low-fold sextuples, including moving boundary colors. The positive face metric is essential; the bare sum has a different action.

Full partner germs carry nonzero [2N] currents and the actual channel gauges may have poles before confluence. A useful full marked transport metric and the actual GLOBAL edge constraints remain to be established. The four local face supports alone maximize at an octahedral boundary, not at an interior manifold saddle. Relevant prior coverage is recorded separately in the literature scope note.

The full source-volume certificate explains the final geometric requirement shared by these routes: stationarity on the entire actual edge/peripheral angle slice, followed by the decoration, winding and end-completion checks. Local formulas, restricted stationarity and matching volume constants do not replace it.

7. Common frames, two exceptional factors, and original generic normals

The joint ordinary-column theorem and finite selected-local certificate remain available with their domains and hypotheses. The right-angled framing obstruction rules out making every original factor central and selected in one frame. Its generic two-facet refinement localizes the weaker physical-support failure to exactly two factors at any bridge width. Continuation of those factors, control outside the central domain and coherent recoupling remain possible routes; none is asserted to be completed.

The small-channel derivative bound uses the original generic source. The partner collision identity retains its actual first normal and surviving tail, whose additional poles exclude an unrestricted individual-normal continuation. The accompanying complex-growth exploration is preserved as a diagnostic, not as a proved exponential lower bound.

The subsequent complex near-fold estimate controls the full paired normal on fixed complex compacts for (l-j) log N=o(N). The matching-edge scalar and marked four-port block preserve another route: regroup the two exceptional factors at a crossing-circle edge, retain every target and the trace normal, and use the exact cancellation with the original complete-circle measure. Target/complement boundary estimates and the mixed-color/global cycle problem remain open. No existing route or diagnostic is removed.

The full physical ordinary packet now removes the central-domain restriction on fixed compacts, with an explicit finite-level boundary correction. The selected-factor corollary puts every ordinary factor except one chosen matching-edge pair on that actual selected packet. The two failures and the exact block remain; target/complement estimates and the global period do not follow from local selection. The all-physical generic-jet theorem retains another complementary domain, with every fixed q derivative controlled on integer physical columns and divided pairs.

The completed target-projector identity removes the remaining target scalar factor and pairs the colliding channels generically. It retains the nilpotent term and the genuine first derivatives of the regular operators. Their native norms and the joint contraction remain separate estimates; the exact cancellation does not make those costs polynomial.

The native-energy theorem makes the operator cost explicit and proves that every fixed derivative adds only a polynomial factor. Its simple midpoint example has positive exponential growth, so the energy must remain in any global estimate. The shared-variable variational route is preserved; separate local maxima cannot replace the actual network constraints.

The Plancherel profile and real shrinking-fold theorem now compute that full local cost. Its local geometric interpretations include an octahedral degree block and a two-cube full cost, with the signed terms and phase conditions retained. The shared-cycle source makes the flow/Fourier route exact at arbitrary fixed width, and coefficient duality supplies positive local variational data on its actual shared indices. The one exceptional geometric block and the global coherent route remain necessary subjects of study; a positive coefficient envelope alone has not resolved them.

The shared positive Fourier partition now gives that route an exact original-source Cauchy bound. The radial reconstruction obstruction records why one direct geometric interpretation fails under the right-angled certificate, without claiming a rate gap or discarding nonradial, boundary or exceptional-block approaches.
