Long-filling volume for reduced-layer plats of unbounded bridge number

theorem, 31 October 2026. Fix k≥4k\geq4 and signs ϵj∈{1,−1}\epsilon_j\in\{1,-1\} for 1≤j≤k−11\leq j\leq k-1. In conventional one-based braid-generator notation, form the standard 2k2k-plat of

βk(𝒑)=(∏j=1k−1σ2jϵj−2p1,j)(∏j=1k−2σ2j+1−2p2,j)(∏j=1k−1σ2j−2p3,j). \beta_k(\mathbf p)= \left(\prod_{j=1}^{k-1}\sigma_{2j}^{\epsilon_j-2p_{1,j}}\right) \left(\prod_{j=1}^{k-2}\sigma_{2j+1}^{-2p_{2,j}}\right) \left(\prod_{j=1}^{k-1}\sigma_{2j}^{-2p_{3,j}}\right).

All products follow the original bottom-to-top order, with jj increasing within a layer. There are 3k−43k-4 independent integer full-twist parameters. Denote the resulting knot by Kk(𝒑)K_k(\mathbf p) and its original normalized root invariant by 𝒦N(Kk(𝒑))\mathcal K_N(K_k(\mathbf p)), retaining the framing and component conventions of the original spectral period.

Theorem. For every such fixed kk and sign pattern there is a threshold T(k,𝝐)T(k,\boldsymbol\epsilon) such that, for every fixed integer vector with all |pr,j|≥T(k,𝝐)|p_{r,j}|\geq T(k,\boldsymbol\epsilon),

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|𝒦N(Kk(𝒑))|=Vol⁡(S3\Kk(𝒑)). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log\left|\mathcal K_N(K_k(\mathbf p))\right| =\operatorname{Vol}\bigl(S^3\setminus K_k(\mathbf p)\bigr).

The twists can have independent signs and magnitudes; they are fixed before N→∞N\to\infty. The conclusion concerns the full original invariant. The proof below verifies the complete-radius balance hypothesis of the general full-source filling theorem on one actual marked packing for every member of this source family. The comparison includes both level parities and the entire charge sum through that theorem.

The supplied knots have bridge number at most kk. The congruence subfamily in which every displayed braid exponent is divisible by 33 has bridge number exactly kk. This additional congruence is used only for the bridge lower bound, not for the volume theorem. Thus the family has unbounded minimal bridge complexity, rather than merely unbounded presentation width.

This explicit construction does not assert balance for every simple-white plat or cover all kk-bridge knots. Its uniform proof covers k≥4k\geq4; no balance claim is made here for the analogous reduced width-three word.

6. General-theorem coverage, knot parity, and genuine width

The general balanced-diagram long-filling theorem therefore applies to DkD_k for every k≥4k\geq4 and every fixed half-twist pattern. For each fixed kk and source pattern there is a diagram-dependent threshold; all fixed filling powers beyond it may have independent signs and magnitudes. The family construction places no comparison constraint between these powers and no joint growing-twist/level limit.

For knot parity put ϵ=+1\epsilon=+1 or −1-1 on each box of the first OO layer, and zero on the other boxes. The first-layer zero-based transpositions (12),(34),…,(2k−32k−2)(1\ 2),(3\ 4),\ldots,(2k-3\ 2k-2) merge the kk initially separate cap cycles successively; each joins two previously different components. Every other box has even exponent and trivial endpoint permutation. Thus the filled main diagram is a knot for every integer full-twist vector. This is a direct computation on the original cups and slots.

There are genuinely unbounded minimal bridge numbers inside this family, not merely arbitrarily wide presentations of two-bridge knots. Require additionally that every actual exponent ni=ϵi−2pin_i=\epsilon_i-2p_i be divisible by 33. This is one residue class of pip_i modulo 33, compatible with all retained parities and arbitrary independent large signs. The Fox-coloring crossing operator on ordered strand colors is T=(2−110)T=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix} over 𝔽3\mathbb F_3; (T−I)2=0(T-I)^2=0 and T3=IT^3=I, for either signed crossing convention. Every whole box therefore acts identically on slot colors, though odd boxes still permute strand endpoints. The kk independent adjacent bottom-cup colors survive every top cap, so the knot has exactly 3k3^k Fox 3-colorings. Any bb-bridge diagram has at most 3b3^b such colorings, because colors on its bb overpassing bridges determine every underpassing segment by the crossing rule. Reidemeister moves preserve colorings, so b(K)≥kb(K)\geq k; the supplied plat gives b(K)≤kb(K)\leq k. Hence b(K)=kb(K)=k for this congruence subfamily. The independent full proof is the Fox-coloring bridge certificate.

The congruence condition is used only for this bridge lower bound. The analytic volume theorem still covers all independently long powers in the prescribed knot parity. Conversely, this explicit family is not claimed to exhaust balanced diagrams, all simple-white diagrams, or all kk-bridge knots. The present uniform construction covers k≥4k\geq4; it makes no balance claim for the reduced k=3k=3 word.
