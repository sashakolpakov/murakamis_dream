The actual crossing comparison retains a nontrivial normal insertion

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The full actual balanced crossing can be compared coefficientwise between the q-normal integral color and the aligned fixed-root meridian color. Away from nonzero sine walls every aligned zero factor differs by an explicit bounded holomorphic unit. The resulting crossing ratio is nonetheless not a diagonal source/cut coboundary. It has a nonconstant finite normal insertion, and the periodic boundary zeros and Frobenius centers give further exact obstructions to a global fixed-fiber comparison.

This follows the actual finite formulas in the common-Verma construction, not a replacement quantum dilogarithm. The scalar Gamma comparison in the whole frame profile remains valid; it does not by itself intertwine the complete crossing.

1. The two actual colors and their full scalar conventions

Put c=2pi*i, n=N-1, and choose b in a fixed complex compact set. Set

H_0=c/N, H=H_0/(1-b/N),
q=exp(H/2), q_0=exp(H_0/2),
M=exp(Hb/2)=-q^N,
M_0=exp(H_0b/2),
x=-M/q, x_0=-M_0/q_0.                               (1.1)

The first head is the genuine integral-color quotient, with highest weight alpha=n. The second head is the actual fixed-root nilpotent color, with marked highest weight alpha_0=n+b. Both have the same shifted interior E zero j=b. Their other boundary conditions differ.

The logarithms are fixed by

Log x=c/2+H(b-1)/2=Hn/2,
Log x_0=c/2+H_0(b-1)/2=H_0(n+b)/2.                  (1.2)

Hence x/x_0=exp[(H-H_0)(b-1)/2], without an unrecorded phase. The common full positive Cartan factor and its negative inverse are

C(H,alpha)=exp(H alpha^2/4).

For the raw full positive crossing their ratio is exactly

C(H,n)/C(H_0,n+b)
     =exp[(Hn^2-H_0(n+b)^2)/4]
     ->exp(-cb/4).                                  (1.3)

That finite factor is retained. The specified auxiliary twist is

theta(H,alpha)=exp[H(alpha^2/4-n alpha/2)],

because its stripped version is x^(-n). Thus the positive zero-framing-normalized crossing is x^n R_+^0; the negative one is x(-n)R_-0. The ratio of the full Cartan factors divided by their matching twist ratio is precisely (x/x_0)^n=1+O(1/N). No scalar in (1.3) is discarded; its cancellation uses that twist.

If the first side is closed with the ordinary integral-color twist and pivot rather than the literal auxiliary ones, the exact route adapter in the divided-color/source notes must be retained as well. The two conventions are not identified by a basis change.

2. Literal positive and negative coefficients

Write P_H(s)=1-exp(Hs), and let i,j be the incoming indices. For the positive checked crossing the output is (j+k,i-k), with 0<=k<=min(i,n-j). Its actual stripped coefficient is

r_+^0(H,b;i,j,k)
  =(-1)^k x^(-i-j-k)
     exp{H[ij+(i-j)k-k(k+1)/2]}
     product_(r=1)^k P_H(j+r)/P_H(r)
     product_(r=0)^(k-1)P_H(b-i+r).                  (2.1)

For the negative crossing the output is (j-k,i+k), with 0<=k<=min(j,n-i), and

r_-^0(H,b;i,j,k)
  =x^(i+j-k)exp(-Hij)
     product_(r=1)^k P_H(i+r)/P_H(r)
     product_(r=0)^(k-1)P_H(b-j+r).                  (2.2)

The Gaussian coefficients in the common-Verma formula are written as finite products here. Their apparent denominators are units at every displayed r<N, or cancel in the original polynomial Gaussian. Full crossings multiply (2.1) by C(H,n), and (2.2) by its inverse, with alpha_0 used on the root side. The original Cartan half-character remains marked.

3. An exact holomorphic unit through every aligned zero

Let J(z)=2sinh(z/2)/z, J(0)=1, and DeltaH=H-H_0. The elementary ratio has its exact removable form

U_N(s)=P_H(s)/P_(H_0)(s)
      =(H/H_0)exp(DeltaH*s/2)J(Hs)/J(H_0s),
U_N(0)=H/H_0.                                       (3.1)

It is an explicitly defined unit through the common zero s=0; no division of two zero coefficients is needed. On any fixed strip |Re(s/N)|<=1-delta, with bounded Im s and bounded b, all J arguments avoid their nonzero zeros for sufficiently large N. Both U_N and its inverse are holomorphic and uniformly bounded.

Define

A_+=ij+(i-j)k-k(k+1)/2-(b-1)(i+j+k)/2,
A_-=-ij+(b-1)(i+j-k)/2.

The exact continued coefficient ratios are

r_+^0(H)=Z_+ r_+^0(H_0),
Z_+=exp(DeltaH*A_+)
    product_(r=1)^k U_N(j+r)/U_N(r)
    product_(r=0)^(k-1)U_N(b-i+r),

r_-^0(H)=Z_- r_-^0(H_0),
Z_-=exp(DeltaH*A_-)
    product_(r=1)^k U_N(i+r)/U_N(r)
    product_(r=0)^(k-1)U_N(b-j+r).                   (3.2)

These are equalities of holomorphic coefficients even where the shared P_H(b-i+r) or P_H(b-j+r) factor is zero. Under the stated sine-safe strip condition, Z_+ and Z_- and their inverses are bounded uniformly in N. There are O(N) factors, but the logarithm of each U_N factor is O(1/N), not O(1).

There is a finite one-index factorial form which makes the potential diagonal pieces visible. Put

G_l=product_(r=1)^l U_N(r),
L_l=product_(r=1)^l U_N(b-r).

Then

Z_+=exp(DeltaH*A_+) L_i/L_(i-k)
                           *G_(j+k)/(G_j G_k),
Z_-=exp(DeltaH*A_-) L_j/L_(j-k)
                           *G_(i+k)/(G_i G_k).       (3.3)

The remaining transfer factorial G_k and Gaussian factor belong to the crossing. They are not removed merely by identifying a scalar half-adapter on one color.

4. The geometric bulk limit is a finite normal insertion

Let u=i/N, v=j/N, t=k/N, and assume a compact sine-safe flow region. For example on the positive crossing take i/N<=1-delta and (j+k)/N<=1-delta; on the negative take j/N<=1-delta and (i+k)/N<=1-delta. Aligned zeros at the origin remain allowed by (3.1).

Define the holomorphic removable function

h(y)=cy exp(cy)/(exp(cy)-1), h(0)=1.                  (4.1)

Uniformly on such regions and compact b-sets,

Log Z_+=b I_+(u,v,t)+O(1/N),
I_+=c[uv+(u-v)t-t^2/2]
   +integral_0^t[h(v+x)-h(x)+h(-u+x)]dx,

Log Z_-=b I_-(u,v,t)+O(1/N),
I_-=-cuv
   +integral_0^t[h(u+x)-h(x)+h(-v+x)]dx.              (4.2)

The logarithms are the ones furnished by (3.1)–(3.2), rather than arbitrary logarithms of the final coefficient. They remain defined through the aligned coefficient zeros. The auxiliary zero-framing scalar ratio is 1+O(1/N), so has the same bulk limit. The raw full Cartan factor adds the explicitly specified term -cb/4 positively, +cb/4 negatively.

Proof: DeltaH=cb/N2+O(N-3). On the relevant strips, Taylor expansion of (3.1) gives

Log U_N(Ny+O(1))=(b/N)h(y)+O(N^-2).                 (4.3)

The expression is uniform at y=0 by the removable formula. Riemann summation in (3.2), followed by the elementary quadratic limits A_+/N^2 and A_-/N^2, proves (4.2). Thus the ratio is an O(1) normal transport insertion. It does not alter the common N-leading local action.

There is an action interpretation with all branches retained. If the actual leading local coefficient is exp(S(x,y,z)/H), with x=Hi,y=Hj,z=Hk, differentiation at fixed integer indices gives precisely

b I=(b/c)[-S+xS_x+yS_y+zS_z].                       (4.4)

Equivalently I=c partial_s[S(su,sv,st)/s]_(s=c). The constant/linear lift terms are the ones of the actual coefficient, not freely chosen phases. Passing from this local identity to a closed stationary network requires its original cut, pivots, twists and source: their Euler terms must be included. It does not by itself select a finite geometric cycle.

5. A diagonal Gamma or cut coboundary cannot remove the insertion

There is an exact test independent of asymptotics. At k=0,i=j the positive checked crossing has the diagonal entry (i,i)->(i,i). Its continued auxiliary-normalized ratio is

exp{DeltaH[i^2-(b-1)i+n(b-1)/2]}.                    (5.1)

Every diagonal similarity leaves every such diagonal entry unchanged. Multiplying by an external scalar cannot fix the problem: for two different i values their ratio is

exp{DeltaH(i_1-i_2)(i_1+i_2-b+1)},                   (5.2)

which is generically nontrivial. At distinct bulk values u_1,u_2, it tends exp[cb(u_12-u_22)]. This applies to arbitrary diagonal leg or pair gauges, including the retained Gamma/frame factors.

Therefore a comparison of this same-fiber type is not a pure diagonal/cut coboundary. A non-diagonal or base/normal/Fock transport is not ruled out by this test; it must account for the actual surviving insertion. Separate spectral multiplicity tests can obstruct even a non-diagonal fixed-fiber similarity.

6. Periodic boundary zeros and the Frobenius center also differ

The bounded-unit statement cannot be extended over nonzero sine walls. Take b=-1, H=c/(N+1), and the positive entry i=n,j=0,k=1. At the fixed root its Pochhammer factor is

P_(H_0)(b-i)=P_(H_0)(-N)=0.

On the integral-color side the corresponding factor is 1-exp[-cN/(N+1)]!=0; all its other factors are units. Hence the root coefficient vanishes while the moving-q coefficient does not. These are periodic root aliases, not the aligned s=0 zero treated by (3.1). On this same example Ecal has different ranks on the two heads, so no regular color-module intertwiner can identify them. This does not say that their selected knot scalars disagree; ordinary-color subquotients can kill the extra directions.

There is also a generic root-central-power residual. Let gamma=(-1)^(N-1). On the moving-q head,

(K_H^N)_i=gamma exp[Hb(n/2-i)],

whereas on the fixed-root meridian head,

K_0^N=gamma exp(cb/2) I.                              (6.1)

Their eigenvalue ratio is exactly

exp[Hb((b-1)/2-i)] -> exp(-cbu).                      (6.2)

Thus the moving-q central power retains the bulk clock and is not a scalar Frobenius character on that normal family. An extension must transport this center/base or a genuine normal source sector; the scalar Gamma half-line is not the whole module. The moving-q power K_H^N is not asserted central away from the root; it is the continuation of the root-central generator whose non-scalar operator is being compared.

7. Meaning of the result

Equations (2.1)–(3.3) are finite whole-source formulas with aligned zero divisors and full scalar conventions. Equation (4.2) gives their uniform bulk insertion. Equations (5.1)–(6.2) show concretely what remains after scalar/frame normalization. The result holds for every allowed crossing, independent of which knot braid later composes it.

The whole-knot Euler cancellation and its geometric normal action are proved with the actual closure and source adapter in the closed bulk-response theorem. Its marked geometric transverse block has multiplier exp(-a S_geo/c), with the preferred regulator action and all original base factors retained. This is a formal calculation on the marked geometric branch, not a proof that the original finite cycle has that branch coefficient.

The full-head Gamma source transport also repairs the naive upper-half scalar source by its actual Weyl complement. It does not remove the crossing periodic-wall example or the Frobenius clock residual in Section 6. Thus the entry comparison still does not control those sectors or the selected trace norm. Those are precisely the sectors lost by treating an interior bounded unit as a global quantum-source isomorphism.

The independent whole checked-crossing spectrum calculation strengthens the fixed-fiber obstruction: its generic channel multiplicities differ for every N, excluding even a nondiagonal similarity up to a scalar. This leaves relative source and base transport open rather than identifying the two full crossings.

The exact audit checks both finite ratios, aligned zeros, full Cartan/twist cancellation, diagonal obstruction, periodic-wall rank example, central powers and the uniform bulk normal insertion.
