Closing phases and the limits of the charged variational bound

Private written deduction, 14 November 2026. Only written identities are used. No numerical calculation, test, or frozen-file change is part of this argument.

The phase-sensitive charged upper bound retains substantial cancellation inside proper-length blocks. It does not retain all closing or clock cancellation. This can be seen in the exact Markov operation, which is a scalar operator before any exterior is attached. The calculation below identifies the missing information and the corresponding stationary equations. It is not a counterexample to a volume conjecture or to a bound restricted to a separately specified reduced hyperbolic presentation.

1. Full-length closing blocks are invisible to the limiting arc loss

Use the functions from the charged block theorem: Iλ,ρ(θ)=12π∫−πλπλlog⁡|1+ρei(θ+s)|ds. I_{\lambda,\rho}(\theta)=\frac1{2\pi} \int_{-\pi\lambda}^{\pi\lambda} \log|1+\rho e^{i(\theta+s)}|\,ds. For every ρ>0\rho>0, I1,ρ(θ)=log⁡max⁡(1,ρ),I0,ρ(θ)=0.(1) I_{1,\rho}(\theta)=\log\max(1,\rho),\qquad I_{0,\rho}(\theta)=0. \qquad\text{(1)} The first identity follows by integrating the logarithmic series for ρ<1\rho<1, applying inversion for ρ>1\rho>1, and taking the integrable limit at one. In particular both endpoint lengths have zero angular loss at every phase.

Call a block proper when 0<λe<10<\lambda_e<1. If the graph of proper blocks is a forest, vertex phases can align every proper block simultaneously: choose a phase at one vertex in each tree and propagate along its edges. All other arc terms are independent of phase by (1). Therefore maxϑ∑eIλe,rv/ru(ωe+ϑv−ϑu)=∑eIλe,rv/ru(0)(2) \max_{\vartheta}\sum_e I_{\lambda_e,r_v/r_u}(\omega_e+\vartheta_v-\vartheta_u) =\sum_e I_{\lambda_e,r_v/r_u}(0) \qquad\text{(2)} at every radius. More generally the same statement holds if the proper-block connection is flat. A cycle using a full-length closing block can have nontrivial holonomy without changing (2).

This is a structural limitation of the limiting functional, not an omitted term in its proof. Every finite closing block and its pivot are still present in the exact source. Its length is N−1N-1, whose normalized limit is one. That limit can lose an exact root zero of a Fourier coefficient while correctly retaining the logarithmic upper bound on the polynomial’s modulus.

2. The Markov crossing is scalar before exterior contraction

Put d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, and AN=q−1/2A_N=q^{-1/2}, with the specified exponential square root. In the external basis let Kextei=q2i−deiK_{\mathrm{ ext}}e_i=q^{2i-d}e_i. The original crossings satisfy Tr⁡2[(I⊗Kext)Rext,+]=q−d−d2/2I,Tr⁡2[(I⊗Kext)Rext,−]=qd+d2/2I.(3) \begin{split} \operatorname{Tr}_2[(I\otimes K_{\mathrm{ ext}})R_{{\mathrm{ ext}},+}] &=q^{-d-d^2/2}I,\\ \operatorname{Tr}_2[(I\otimes K_{\mathrm{ ext}})R_{{\mathrm{ ext}},-}] &=q^{d+d^2/2}I. \qquad\text{(3)} \end{split}

Here is a finite operator proof, with the boundary indices retained. The regular crossing adapter identifies the external crossings with the physical braided intertwiners of opposite sign and transports KextK_{\mathrm{ ext}} to the physical KK. The partial-trace computation in the prescribed-cut theorem, Section 2, proves directly that the KK-weighted partial trace of such an intertwiner commutes with E,F,KE,F,K. Its proof uses the displayed coproducts and cyclicity only in the traced factor. The physical KK has distinct eigenvalues and the adjacent lowering arrows are nonzero. Hence this partial trace is scalar.

The scalar can be evaluated in a single boundary coordinate. The external positive crossing sends (i,j)(i,j) to (j+t,i−t)(j+t,i-t). When the retained input is i=0i=0, the trace forces t=j=0t=j=0. The crossing entry is ANd2A_N^{d^2}, and the pivot is q−dq^{-d}, giving the first line of (3). The negative crossing sends (i,j)(i,j) to (j−t,i+t)(j-t,i+t). At retained input i=di=d, the trace forces j=d,t=0j=d,t=0. Its crossing entry is AN−d2A_N^{-d^2}, and its pivot is qdq^d, giving the second line.

Consequently an arbitrary exterior operator acting on the retained heads can be left in place: tracing the added head replaces its Markov crossing by the corresponding scalar in (3). No assumption that the exterior is scalar, or that its marked row may be averaged, is needed for this local replacement. In the original physical convention g=K/sg=K/s, x=qd=−q−1x=q^d=-q^{-1}, s=xNs=x^N, and κ=qd2/2\kappa=q^{d^2/2}, a positive physical crossing has scalar κx/s\kappa x/s. Its prescribed framing cancels this unit factor.

3. Exact clock cancellation in the closed Markov operation

The same operation can be inspected in the original charged representation, without changing its clocks. Consider the positive physical Markov crossing with its original two closing adapters. Write XX for its over-input, ZZ for its over-output, and YY for its under-input. The under-output is xQ−hYxQ^{-h}Y, where Q=q2Q=q^2, 0≤h≤d0\le h\le d. The two crossing blocks have lengths d−h,hd-h,h; their directed anchor pairs are X→YX\to Y and Y→ZY\to Z. The two closures are Y→XY\to X and Z→YZ\to Y, each of length dd.

The exact gains, obtained from equations (7),(17),(18) of the charged source, are w0=qd−h,w1=qh−d,wA=q2h−d,wB=q−2.(4) w_0=q^{d-h},\qquad w_1=q^{h-d},\qquad w_A=q^{2h-d},\qquad w_B=q^{-2}. \qquad\text{(4)} The last gain is the actual pivot. Let a,ba,b be the head charges of the two crossing blocks. Conservation forces the closing head charges to be h+a,bh+a,b, respectively. All 0≤a≤d−h0\le a\le d-h and 0≤b≤h0\le b\le h are admissible. Closing block coefficients have magnitude one. Apart from the specified unit factors independent of a,ba,b, the entire fixed-clock coefficient is therefore wAhBd−h,N(w0wA)Bh,N(w1wB)=wAhBd−h,N(qh)Bh,N(−qh−1).(5) w_A^h B_{d-h,N}(w_0w_A)B_{h,N}(w_1w_B) =w_A^h B_{d-h,N}(q^h)B_{h,N}(-q^{h-1}). \qquad\text{(5)} This is a sum over the complete source support, not a selected subset. The finite product formula gives Bh,N(−qh−1)=∏r=0h−1(1−Qr),Bd−h,N(qh)=∏r=h+1d(1−Qr).(6) \begin{split} B_{h,N}(-q^{h-1})&=\prod_{r=0}^{h-1}(1-Q^r),\\ B_{d-h,N}(q^h)&=\prod_{r=h+1}^{d}(1-Q^r). \end{split} \qquad\text{(6)} Thus every h≥1h\ge1 coefficient vanishes exactly. At h=0h=0 the second product in (6) is (Q;Q)d=N(Q;Q)_d=N, and the first is one. Restoring χN/N\chi_N/N and the original unit factors gives the scalar χNκx/s\chi_N\kappa x/s, in agreement with (3). All levels and both parities are included. The cancellation occurs already inside a fixed nonzero clock sector; it need not wait for the final sum over clocks.

Nevertheless the limiting upper functional in the angle-optimization theorem has strictly positive value for this same presentation. Write t=h/Nt=h/N in the limit and bλ(y)=Iλ,ey(0). b_\lambda(y)=I_{\lambda,e^y}(0). The proper blocks form a path, so (2) applies. Set the XX radius to one, the YY radius to eue^u, and the ZZ radius to eu+ve^{u+v}. First take 0<t<10<t<1. Equation (16) of that theorem shows that bλ′b_\lambda' lies in [0,λ][0,\lambda], with bλ′(0)=λ/2b_\lambda'(0)=\lambda/2, for 0<λ<10<\lambda<1. Each term in (7) is convex; its one-sided derivatives at zero enclose zero. Hence infu,v𝒜t(u,v)=bt(0)+b1−t(0)=−2J(t/2)−2J((1−t)/2).(8) \inf_{u,v}\mathcal A_t(u,v) =b_t(0)+b_{1-t}(0) =-2J(t/2)-2J((1-t)/2). \qquad\text{(8)} The endpoint lengths follow directly from (1), or by continuity. Thus the variational functional is not Markov invariant, even though its finite source is. No positive loss from the full-length closing phases survives in (7). Equation (9) does not prove that an arbitrary attached hyperbolic exterior suffers this same additive excess; its coupled optimization would require a separate argument.

4. The missing half of the joint stationary equations

The same loss can be located before any topological interpretation. On a regular complex block chart let zz denote logarithmic anchor coordinates, so za=log⁡ra+iϑaz_a=\log r_a+i\vartheta_a, and let H(λ,z)H(\lambda,z) be the holomorphic leading action of the anchor integrand, including the real clock-factor contribution −∑eJ(λe)-\sum_eJ(\lambda_e) and the original coefficient loads. The extracted clock and block phases contribute another term iϕ(λ)i\phi(\lambda). Both are fixed by the original source; ϕ\phi is not adjustable.

Where the anchor phase maximum and radius minimum are smooth and interior, their first-order equations are Re⁡∂zH=0,Im⁡∂zH=0.(10) \operatorname{Re}\partial_z H=0,\qquad \operatorname{Im}\partial_z H=0. \qquad\text{(10)} The second equation follows by differentiating in the anchor arguments, since ∂ϑRe⁡H=−Im⁡∂zH\partial_\vartheta\operatorname{Re}H =-\operatorname{Im}\partial_zH. Thus the anchor optimization retains complex anchor stationarity. At an interior smooth maximum over lengths, envelope differentiation in an allowed real length direction vv gives only Re⁡dλH[v]=0.(11) \operatorname{Re}\,d_\lambda H[v]=0. \qquad\text{(11)} The full original clock saddle additionally requires, with its actual lattice character retained, Im⁡dλH[v]+dϕ[v]to equal the corresponding Fourier lattice character.(12) \operatorname{Im}\,d_\lambda H[v]+d\phi[v] \quad\text{to equal the corresponding Fourier lattice character}. \qquad\text{(12)} In primitive integer clock coordinates that character is an integer multiple of 2π2\pi. In dependent length coordinates one must use the induced lattice, rather than assigning unrelated integers to all block lengths.

The variational upper bound is unchanged if the extracted unit clock factors are changed, while (12) changes. This statement describes exactly which factors its proof takes in absolute value; it does not propose changing the invariant. The original feedback equations (15)–(16) in the clock-feedback normal form retain these terms. A geometric identification based only on (10)–(11) would omit source equations. The Markov calculation also shows that even the anchor coefficient can have exact root cancellation not measured by a limiting maximum-modulus bound.

The constructive conclusion is that exact local contractions such as (3) and (6) should be performed before this optimization when available. A genuinely joint geometric contour must retain (12), all closing factors, and their original relative cycle. The current upper bound remains valid and useful, but its value cannot be declared a topological volume merely from its concave angle dual.
