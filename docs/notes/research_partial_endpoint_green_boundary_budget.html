The partial endpoint Green estimate and its retained boundary packet

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The exact truncation defect has the real product rate, uniformly even at its zeros. Backward propagation on a fixed physical bulk interval preserves that rate for the forced part of the error, including first normals. The estimate also retains an off-grid boundary packet and the defect of the chosen baseline. Neither of those terms is identified with the last Newton product.

An exact ideal-multiple example shows why matching physical jets and thin-strip moderateness do not determine the error. This is not an obstruction to a sharper estimate for the specifically normalized common baseline, and does not provide a filled-leaf scalar seed.

4. What changing the cutoff controls

Fix 0<δ1<δ2<1/20<\delta_1<\delta_2<1/2 and a real buffered arc with 0<t<2πδ10<t<2\pi\delta_1. The two partial polynomials have the same physical full jets on that arc. Their exact difference is the finite Newton band between the two cutoffs. The product proof in section 2 is uniform for every intermediate cutoff. For δ>t/(2π)\delta>t/(2\pi), the ratio sin⁡2(πδ)−sin⁡2(t/2)cos⁡2(πδ) \frac{\sin^2(\pi\delta)-\sin^2(t/2)} {\cos^2(\pi\delta)} increases from zero to infinity. Hence Jδ(t)J_\delta(t), as a function of δ\delta, increases to its unique maximum and then decreases. Its minimum on [δ1,δ2][\delta_1,\delta_2] is at an endpoint. Summing the O(N)O(N) exact terms and using the same small disks proves, for every fixed derivative order, |∂αa1∂xb1(uNδ2−uNδ1)(x)|≤CNpexp⁡{−Nmin⁡(Jδ1(tN(x)),Jδ2(tN(x)))}.(22) |\partial_\alpha^{a_1}\partial_x^{b_1} (u_N^{\delta_2}-u_N^{\delta_1})(x)| \le CN^p\exp\{-N\min(J_{\delta_1}(t_N(x)), J_{\delta_2}(t_N(x)))\}. \qquad\text{(22)} The maximizing cutoff satisfies sin⁡2(πδ*(t))=(1+sin⁡2(t/2))/2\sin^2(\pi\delta_*(t))=(1+\sin^2(t/2))/2, giving the previously proved J*(t)J_*(t). In particular it does not identify the error in (21) with e−NJ*(t)e^{-NJ_*(t)}.

5. An exact alias ambiguity and its limited implication

The full physical relation is PN(C)=2TN(C/2)+2,PN(cN(x))=4sin⁡2(πNx).(23) P_N(C)=2T_N(C/2)+2,\qquad P_N(c_N(x))=4\sin^2(\pi Nx). \qquad\text{(23)} Its composition in (23) is hh-periodic, so it commutes with (3). Consequently 𝒥N(PNF)=PN𝒥NF\mathscr J_N(P_NF)=P_N\mathscr J_NF as an ordinary identity, with the removable polynomial interpretation.

At α=iπ\alpha=i\pi, one has w=c0w=c_0, and the full kernel and every partial polynomial in (2) are exactly 11. For any fixed c>0c>0, however, H̃N(x)=1+e−cNsin⁡2(πNx)(24) \widetilde H_N(x) =1+e^{-cN}\sin^2(\pi Nx) \qquad\text{(24)} is another exact homogeneous solution of (𝒥N−c0)H̃N=0(\mathscr J_N-c_0)\widetilde H_N=0. It has exactly the same full physical jets. In ordinary spectral terms the difference is e−cNPN(C)/4e^{-cN}P_N(C)/4. It has zero first normal at every double root and zero value at the odd simple root.

On every fixed bulk strip of height H<c/(2π)H<c/(2\pi), (24) is uniformly bounded. At real midpoint rows its difference from the partial polynomial is exactly e−cNe^{-cN}, while the truncation defect (4) is identically zero. Choosing c<min⁡x∈IJδ(tN(x))c<\min_{x\in I}J_\delta(t_N(x)) for large NN also rules out a universal Npe−NJδN^p e^{-NJ_\delta} comparison with every such moderate homogeneous representative.

This example proves only that physical jets, thin-strip moderateness, and the truncation forcing do not fix the off-grid normalization. It does not contradict the specifically normalized common baseline, nor exclude an improved boundary estimate for that baseline on a larger domain. In fact its modified-flow construction preserves the constant solution at α=iπ\alpha=i\pi: the initial coefficient matrix kills (1,0)t(1,0)^t, and every flow iteration retains that property. Thus (24) is not that constructed baseline. In the original closed trace the ideal multiple is invisible, with any shared polynomial spectator and its full normals retained. Splitting that trace into exterior and interface currents must keep their cancellation; one separate current is not invariant under this ambiguity.

The unresolved estimate is therefore a source-specific bound on the boundary packet and baseline defect in (21), at the required action. Neither the optimized real product rate nor this Green identity supplies that estimate or a nonzero filled-leaf term.

6. Coverage and direct sources
