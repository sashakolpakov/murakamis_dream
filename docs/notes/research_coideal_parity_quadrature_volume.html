Exact finite quadrature extends the signed coideal volume chamber

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof.

The actual mixed coideal coefficient has an exact positive quadrature on a specified index parity. Its finite zero strings annihilate every sample outside the common negative gap. This replaces the global absolute-integral comparison in C by exact finite orthogonality. Consequently the signed coefficient has the sewn octahedral volume rate throughout the cooriented domain on that parity, including points outside C’s discriminant chamber.

The assertion concerns the full actual local coefficient, with its quadratic height phase and both neighboring metric weights. It does not place an arbitrary geometric character or original marked closing in the stated channels.

The other index parity is not settled by this argument. For the parity proved here no comparison with the whole absolute-circle integral is asserted outside C’s original chamber. The theorem instead evaluates the signed coefficient by an exact positive sampled sum, even when additional absolute-value peaks occur away from its supporting samples.

The original input, marked closing and external scalar χN\chi_N must still be retained in any assembled word. The argument does not show that all intermediate channels lie in this domain, that the resulting face phases align with those boundaries, or that an arbitrary coherent closed sum has a lower bound. No global cycle, new finite-exception theorem or knot-volume identity follows from the local result.

Finite Fourier orthogonality and log-cell estimates are elementary or already available in T and C. The additional statement is the source-specific root-support identity (11), its exact positive quadrature, and the resulting removal of the discriminant restriction on the specified parity.
