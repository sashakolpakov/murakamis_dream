A signed coideal seam has a positive volume chamber

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The actual overlap of two coideal channel frames is a mixed finite Parseval kernel. On an explicit semialgebraic chamber, its whole signed integral agrees, up to its known phase and an exponentially small relative error, with its positive absolute integral. Its exponential rate is the volume of one convex ideal octahedron obtained by sewing two tetrahedral halves. The stationary equation closes their common edge moduli; the halves are not maximized independently.

This gives nonzero coefficients of both actual signed crossings in a single nested channel frame, with its full neighboring metric weights. It does not show that an arbitrary component stays in this chamber or make its prescribed marked closing positive.
