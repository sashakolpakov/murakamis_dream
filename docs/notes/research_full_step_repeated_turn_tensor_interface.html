A full-step tensor interface for repeated endpoint turns

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The full angular step preserves the original value-and-first-normal quotient. It therefore permits exact multiplication of already turned source channels, followed by a further original turn. This note gives explicit polynomial conversion formulas, a finite tensor construction, and the actual initial-data budget. It does not establish a regular highest-order coefficient after the final turn, or a growth bound for that final output.

6. What the interface does not yet estimate

On a regular compact chart the principal full-step matrices of the inputs can be tensorized: their eigenvalues multiply, with the source and recovery branches retained. Formula (23) converts the step symbols to their sum and difference, and (26) performs the actual turn automorphism. It does not replace the ordinary product by a transported product, or identify this correspondence with a positive angle sector.

After (26), a coefficient such as 𝔞(Dτ)\mathfrak a(D_\tau) is an operator. The preceding clearing and descent do not prove that the highest physical shift coefficient of the combined system is invertible, uniformly or otherwise. Eliminating components can also expose terms of smaller asymptotic coefficient size but greater shift order. Such terms cannot be discarded from a transfer proof. The regular leading-order argument for one original endpoint product does not automatically persist under (20)–(26).

The new result is therefore an exact finite interface with full normals and a proved source initialization budget, not a new general annihilator existence statement or an integrated growth theorem. A regular physical companion and its endpoint control are still needed before a maximal characteristic-growth upper bound can be asserted for (2). No selected coefficient, filled-action inequality, or general forest closure follows here.
