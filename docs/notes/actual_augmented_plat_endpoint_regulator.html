The actual augmented-plat endpoint action has its diagram regulator

30 October 2026. At the complete channel endpoint, the fully normalized q-Hahn coupon phase has the SAME Bloch–Wigner regulator as its original crossing-circle diagram, for every retained half twist. This is an absolute comparison: an exact rational wedge calculation gives a constant regulator difference, and a connected real chart proves that constant is zero. It is not an inference from matching peripheral derivatives.

Consequently any regular, correctly marked geometric critical point of the assembled endpoint phase has critical rate equal to the volume of the actual augmented link. Existence of such a point on the source’s required logarithmic sheet, a contributing cycle, and a nonzero controlled amplitude are separate questions. This note does not prove a source asymptotic.

1. The complete normalized endpoint symbol

Use the original phase Theta_epsilon from the local character calculation, equation (1), with its inverse-measure factor H, both q-Hahn columns, and the retained half twist epsilon in {0,+1,-1}. Write

T=exp(2pi i tau), U=exp(2pi i u), V=exp(2pi i v),
Z=exp(2pi i alpha), W=exp(2pi i beta).

At xi=1/2, J=exp(2pi i xi)=-1 and J0(1/2)=0. Adding H to the two column factorial actions cancels the terms J0(tau-1/2). The remaining factorial part is

J0(u)+J0(tau-u)+J0(v)+J0(tau-v)
  -2[J0(alpha)+J0(1/2-alpha)+J0(beta)+J0(1/2-beta)]
  -J0(u-alpha)-J0(tau-u-1/2+alpha)
  -J0(v-beta)-J0(tau-v-1/2+beta).                 (1)

The retained polynomial phase is

pi i [(u+v)/2+(tau-1/2)(alpha+beta)
                            +tau+3(epsilon-1)/4]. (2)

On the original factorial branch,

2pi i J0(s)=-Li2(exp(2pi i s))
                   +pi^2(s^2-s)+Li2(1).          (3)

One verifies (3) by differentiation from 0<s<1 and the common value at s=0, then analytically continues with the recorded logarithmic marks. Thus the Bloch symbol of 2pi i Theta_epsilon is

B_H=B(U,Z)+B(V,W),
B(X,z)=-[X]-[T/X]+2[z]+2[-1/z]+[X/z]+[-Tz/X].     (4)

The half-twist term in (2) contributes a REAL constant to 2pi i Theta, so it contributes no regulator. It does still change the actual auxiliary meridian needed to realize the same main-input/main-output relation. Neither that change nor the coefficient 2 in (4) may be discarded.

2. A rational chart for the endpoint critical correspondence

Both actual internal q-Hahn equations at J=-1 are rationally parameterized by

Z=(a-1)/(a+1), U=T(a-1)^2/(Ta^2+1),
W=(b-1)/(b+1), V=T(b-1)^2/(Tb^2+1).              (5)

Indeed a=(1+Z)/(1-Z), and direct substitution gives the quadratic P(U,Z)=0 of the local character calculation; the same holds for V,W. Every regular endpoint root is represented by this chart away from the listed denominators. The chart has coordinates (T,a,b) in a nonempty Zariski-open subset of C^3. A common nonzero magnetic scale can be fixed at one: it simultaneously conjugates the head matrices and does not change any crossing symbol below.

For clarity all reconstructions use the actual physical head matrices

K=-1/X, F=S(1-X), E=(X^-1-1)/S,
Lminus=[[1,0],[-KF,K]],
G=[[K,E],[FK,FE+K^-1]].                           (6)

Put

Y(X,z)=-(1-X)(Tz+X)/[(X-z)(T-X)],
D=ZW(T-U)(T-V)/[(TZ+U)(TW+V)].                   (7)

In the low physical degree chart the input heads (X,S) are

(V,Y(V,W)), (T/V,1),

and the output heads are

(U,1/[D Y(U,Z)]), (T/U,1/D).                     (8)

Let A=G1, B=Lminus1^-1 G2 Lminus1, P=BA, H=P+I. Then H is nonzero nilpotent and the endpoint transport is

C=I+cH, c=(2Tab-Ta-Tb+a+b-2)
                    /[2(T+1)(a-1)(b-1)].       (9)

This follows either by multiplying the explicit matrices or by c=Tr([H,A]A_out)/32. In particular C A C^-1=A_out. The full ordered product P, including its Borel frame, is preserved.

For the HIGH physical degree chart one must use the actual affine index adapter, rather than substitute the physical pair product into (4). Its input and output heads are respectively

(V/T,Y(V,W)), (1/V,1),
(U/T,D Y(V,W)), (1/U,D Y(U,Z)Y(V,W)).             (10)

The low variables in (4),(5),(7) remain unchanged. The exact finite high-block identification has no extra phase or scalar. Formula (6) with (10), followed by c=Tr([H,A]A_out)/32, gives exactly the same coefficient (9) in the high physical chart. The executable certificate checks it independently with the same original crossing rule below.

3. Reconstruct the actual auxiliary meridian and every crossing

The positive Hurwitz half twist on a trace -2 pair is simultaneous conjugation by P^-1-I. At P=-I+H its projective normalization is I+H/2. Therefore the raw, trace -2 auxiliary meridian for the original coupon R^epsilon L_N is

C_aux,raw=-[I+(epsilon/2-c)H].                    (11)

This sign is its actual complete raw lift; the corresponding physical meridian has trace +2. The literal threading word [2,1,1,2] transports the main pair by C_aux^-1 and closes the auxiliary because it commutes with P. Composing its main half twist gives total conjugator I+cH. This uses the proved original peripheral word, not an assigned auxiliary longitude.

To recover the actual THIRD Gauss head from (11), let L=Lminus2 Lminus1 and set G3=L C_aux,raw L^-1. Its coordinates are

K3=(G3)11, E3=(G3)12, F3=(G3)21/K3.              (12)

Now execute chronological adjacent crossings [2,1,1,2], and then the signed main crossing R^epsilon if epsilon is nonzero. All maps here are rational. For two input heads (k,e,f),(l,g,h), the original positive crossing, with d=1-eh, is

(ld, [dg+e(dl-l^-1)]/k, kh/d),
(k/d, e/(ld), l[df+h(d/k-k)]).                    (13)

With physical clocks A0=-1/k, B0=-1/l its five-dilog symbol is

[B0]+[1/d]+[1/A0]-[B0/d]-[1/(dA0)].             (14)

The original negative crossing has c0=1-(B0/A0)gf and outputs

(lc0, kg, f+h/k-f/(l^2c0)),
(k/c0, le+g-k^2g/c0, f/l),                       (15)

with symbol

[A0]+[c0]+[1/B0]-[A0c0]-[c0/B0].                (16)

Call the sum of these FOUR or FIVE actual symbols B_disk,epsilon. Direct rational substitution checks all nine final Gauss coordinates: the first two heads are exactly (8) or (10), and the auxiliary head is exactly (12) again. Thus this is the actual original closed circle, not an abstract centralizer with a missing closing/pivot convention.

The closing pivot is a monomial; its complete leading contribution and the main marked cup/cap phases are included in (2) and the global source characters. They contribute no extra dilogarithm symbol. A monomial can still change an action value off stationarity; section 5 treats that point explicitly.

4. Exact wedge identity and absolute real normalization

With delta[z]=z wedge (1-z), the following identity holds over the rational function field Q(T,a,b), for BOTH physical degree charts and all epsilon in {0,+1,-1}:

delta(B_H-B_disk,epsilon)=0.                      (17)

Here is a finite algebraic certificate for (17), including its procedure so no fitted regulator or floating point identity is involved. Use (5) in (4); construct B_disk by (6)–(16); factor every argument and its complement into irreducible polynomials over Q. For an argument z write v_z for its integer exponent vector, retaining positive rational prime factors as well as polynomial factors. Then expand

sum_(coefficient n, argument z) n
                              (v_z v_(1-z)^t-v_(1-z) v_z^t). (18)

The matrices from (4) and (14),(16) are identical. Only the torsion sign -1 is removed in this rational exterior square; factors such as 2 are kept. The accompanying audit performs precisely (18), symbolically, and records every rational argument and the empty difference matrix. It also independently checks (9)–(13) and the complete auxiliary closure. Thus (17) is an identity of rational functions, not evidence from a finite sample of shapes. One compact form of the COMMON wedge, useful for checking the expansion without comparing two large symbol lists, is as follows. Put

A_r=2Tr-T+1, B_r=Tr-r+2, F_r=Tr^2+1.

Then BOTH sides expand to

2 T wedge (T+1)
  +sum_(r=a,b) [ A_r wedge T - A_r wedge F_r
      +2 A_r wedge (r-1) + B_r wedge F_r
      -2 B_r wedge (r-1) + F_r wedge r
      +2 r wedge (r-1) ].                        (18a)

There are exactly fifteen nonzero coefficients in this polynomial-factor basis. In particular the mixed factors involving both a and b from the actual four-crossing diagram cancel; this is a global identity for the whole coupon, not equality of independent crossing maxima.

For any Bloch symbol B the differential of its Bloch–Wigner regulator factors through delta B:

dD(z)=log|z| dArg(1-z)-log|1-z| dArg z.

Therefore (17) makes D(B_H-B_disk,epsilon) constant on its regular complex parameter chart. This chart is connected: it is obtained from C^3 by deleting finitely many proper polynomial hypersurfaces, and two generic points can be joined by complex lines avoiding their finitely many excluded intersection points. Every rational expression has real coefficients, so the chart contains a generic real point. At that point every argument is real and every Bloch–Wigner value is zero. Consequently

D(B_H)=D(B_disk,epsilon).                         (19)

This is the required ABSOLUTE regulator identity. Wedge equality alone would not prove it. Nor does (19) claim equality of integral Bloch classes, flattenings, or complex periods: rational wedge zero can retain information not measured by D. Changes of logarithmic sheet must continue to carry the original integer marks in the action.

5. Assemble the actual diagram and the critical action

Apply (19) at every original twist-region coupon. The actual disk words and retained half crossings, with the actual outside wires and caps, form the original augmented-link diagram. The crossing-block proof in the marked fundamental-cycle calculation, sections 2–4, is componentwise: its local octahedral balls, arc-strip face identifications, and degree-one cusp truncation apply to this multi-component diagram as well. No step joins different components or replaces their preferred peripheral paths. The knot-only shortcut H2(M)=0 is NOT used: for a compact connected oriented link exterior H3(M)=0, so the exact sequence makes H3(M,partial M) -> H2(partial M) injective. The displayed degree-one cycle on EACH oriented cusp is precisely the boundary of the relative fundamental class, and hence fixes the relative class uniquely. Accidental developed crossing degeneracies can be avoided by adding their finitely many divisors to the generic frame exclusions, or handled by the continuous extension D(0)=D(1)=D(infinity)=0. Thus at the complete geometric representation

sum_regions D(B_H)=Vol(actual augmented link).    (20)

One can use an actual positive gluing certificate to supply that geometric representation; (20) does not assume convexity of a particular source polytope or independently maximize individual regions.

To pass from a Bloch symbol to the original action value, keep the full stationarity condition. In logarithmic variables z=rho+i theta the assembled action G=2pi i phi has the form

G=sum n_j Li2(exp(a_j dot z+i pi nu_j))
                     +(1/2)z^t C z+i k dot z+c0,

with real a_j,nu_j,C,k and Im c0=0, and C symmetric. This follows directly from (1)–(3), the original monomial cup/cap phases, and the retained integer logarithmic characters. For matching branches the exact identity is

Im G-sum n_j D(exp(a_j dot z+i pi nu_j))
                              =rho dot Im(gradient G). (21)

It follows by expanding Im Li2, the real quadratic, and the imaginary linear term; the full proof is in the repo’s wall regulator calculation, section 3. At a FULL stationary point on the recorded marked sheet the right side vanishes. If the channel is held at its endpoint rather than varied, its logarithm is log J=i pi modulo 2pi i and has rho_J=0, so its unsolved normal derivative contributes nothing to (21). All remaining free magnetic and internal variables still require their actual stationary equations. A constrained endpoint extremum in some other complex variable is not enough.

Therefore, whenever the complete geometric character is realized by a regular correctly marked full critical point of this actual endpoint phase,

2pi Re phi(0)=Im G(0)=Vol(actual augmented link).  (22)

The original exterior framing factors of unit modulus add real constants to G and preserve (22). Equation (22) fixes the complete-volume ANCHOR needed alongside the proved primitive differential

dG=(1/2) sum_i V_i dU_i.

It does not prove a global lift to the required source sheet, a quantum cycle coefficient, a nonzero leading amplitude, a bound on competing or singular regions, or a mixed-color complex corridor. In particular no source limit or general long-filling theorem follows from (22) alone.
