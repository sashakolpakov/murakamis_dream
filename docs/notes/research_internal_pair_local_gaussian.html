Actual local Gaussian preparation for every positive internal pair

Research proof, 3 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

For every fixed pair of integer powers n,p≥3n,p\ge3, the original mixed wire and trace density with its low-index complete outside edges has a nonzero Gaussian component at its geometric critical point, through both level parities. At odd levels those outside edges equal the full original kernels. Section 6 upgrades the local conclusion to the full original complete-end factors at both parities on the actual pair domain. Sections 1–6 obtain its norm and relative annihilation residual from the exact physical-row normalization. Section 7 uses the sharper edge packet to compute a fixed, nonzero leading amplitude and the precise power N−3/2N^{-3/2}. It also allows both outside colors to vary in fixed complex neighborhoods. Neither argument assumes an asymptotic for the filled pair.

This is a local source theorem. It neither identifies the coefficient of this local contour in the whole original cycle nor bounds that cycle’s complement. The free-boundary statement in Section 7 includes all fixed ordinary boundary derivatives, but still concerns only the localized contour.

5. Exact scope of the advance

This theorem establishes local norm growth, Gaussian preparation, and a nonzero local observed coefficient from the actual finite low-outer-edge sector at every fixed positive integer internal pair, at both level parities and complete outside ends. At odd levels this is the full source before contour localization. It is stronger than a formal quadratic expansion of a proposed phase: the actual outer factors in (5) are controlled by their original pinned recurrence and, at even levels, the exact alternating interpolation identity, and their possible NN-dependent phase and magnitude are retained. All traced normal directions and both full trace denominators remain present.

The remaining step is global. Formula (23) defines a local horizontal piece; it does not assert that this piece occurs with coefficient one in a deformation of (3), or that the other currents and regions have lower action. The exact high-index, alias, and boundary terms of that deformation still need to be controlled. The statements of Sections 1–6 fix the outside variables complete, as in (2). Their extension to ordinary derivatives in both outside variables is proved in Section 7. At even levels the last physical row has not been mistaken for the complete endpoint: the low factor is obtained by the separate exact interpolation comparison. Proposition 2 controls the omitted radical outside-edge sectors after their original traces. It does not control the rest of the low-outer-edge cycle, so it cannot by itself turn the local term into a full-source lower bound.

No new universal lower bound for the original closing coefficient, volume theorem, or finite-exception conclusion is asserted.
