The actual framing, its compact-source logarithmic section, and its boundary residue

Research proof, 27 October 2026. This computes the missing common momentum gauge between the actual critical framing and the compact coherent source. The physical meridian map resolves holomorphically at both compact poles after retaining its eigenflag. The full irreducible knot family nevertheless cannot extend there as regular matrices. An explicit associated-frame Jacobian gives a nonzero principal Gaussian boundary residue on the actual compact source.

These are complete-parabolic statements, with the original base and half-density lines retained. No extension of higher Gaussian loops, selected-cycle equality, or volume limit is inferred.

1. Common momentum is an exact diagonal gauge

In the actual finite head let

S_c e_a=c^(-a)e_a.

Then exactly S_c Ecal S_c^(-1)=c Ecal, S_c F S_c^(-1)=c^(-1)F, and K is unchanged. A common scaling on all strands is a Hopf automorphism. Every weighted crossing preserves the integer total index, hence commutes with S_c^(tensor b). Its closed diagonal cut has the same property.

For the actual compact coherent kernel, direct substitution gives

sigma_(S_c C S_c^(-1))(w,z)=sigma_C(w/c,c z).

Thus the exact diagonal gauge preserves

t_c=wz/(1+wz),

and scales the physical momentum p=1/w by c. For unit c it preserves the compact real source cycle; its angle is the exact common diagonal gauge angle. In particular the compact angular coordinate does not automatically replace the internal geometric centralizer coordinate.

The actual critical model has inverse cut flag p_1=1. Write its framing, at M=1, as

F(r,s)=[r,1-r;s,-s], s!=0,
mu_s=F^(-1)[1,1;0,1]F=[1+s,-s;s,1-s].

Adding the common momentum gives the genuine third framing coordinate

D_p=diag(p,1), Ftilde=F D_p^(-1),
mu(A,p)=D_p mu_s D_p^(-1)
   =[1+s,-sp;s/p,1-s], A=(1+s)^(-1).          (1)

The SL2 lift uses the marked square root of p; the displayed conjugation is projective and changes no gauge-invariant shape. For generic M the identical construction uses the already proved F_M(r,s) and A=M/(M+s). The complete-pole conclusions below are restricted to M=1, where the actual kappa vacuum term vanishes.

The physical compact-source coordinates of (1) are explicitly

t_c=Log A/(2pi*i), w=1/p,
z=p t_c/(1-t_c).                            (2)

They forget r. On the original p_1=1 framing, their image has w=1 and rank one. Therefore there is no meridian- and momentum-preserving identification of the two original internal framing coordinates with the whole compact source. The common momentum gauge must first be restored, and the remaining centralizer direction must be treated separately.

2. An explicit Springer map at both compact poles

Use the compact-source chart

v=w, a=z/(1+wz), t_c=v a,

and put c_0=2pi*i. The physical meridian minus identity is

mu-I=eta_S[-v,1;-v^2,v],
eta_S=(1-exp(-c_0 v a))/v.                  (3)

Its eigenflag is (1,v). Division by v is removable: the right side is an entire function of v,a. Moreover

partial_a eta_S=c_0 exp(-c_0 v a)!=0.

Thus this is an explicit holomorphic local isomorphism to the resolved parabolic Gauss chart, rather than a claim obtained by equating symplectic forms.

On the second compact chart set

v'=1/v, a'=v-v^2 a, t_c'=v'a'=1-t_c.

Then

eta_S'=-v^2 eta_S
       =(exp(c_0 v'a')-1)/v'.              (4)

This extends at v'=0 with derivative c_0 in a'. Equations (3)–(4) are precisely the two Springer/eigenflag transition formulas. At the south compact pole (w,z)=(0,0), one has eta_S=c_0 z+...; at the north pole in W=1/w,Z=1/z, the corresponding normal is c_0 Z+.... Both meridians become identity, but their limiting eigenflags are retained.

The marked inverse is rational/logarithmic:

A=(1-eta_S v)^(-1),
a=Log A/(c_0 v).                            (5)

It extends at v=0 by a=eta_S/c_0. Other logarithm sheets are not identified. This local isomorphism need not be a proper global covering; other logarithmic sheets can escape a chart.

The resolved Gauss form can now be checked from the map itself:

dLog A wedge dLog p
  =-d eta_S wedge dv/(1-eta_S v)
  =c_0 dv wedge da.                        (6)

This recovers the physical compact-source form with its sign and normalization, including at the two pole charts.

3. The actual logarithmic centralizer section

The PGL2 conjugator from the reference meridian to (1) is G=Ftilde^(-1). In the Springer chart write

g_N=[1,0;v,eta_S^(-1)],
n(b)=[1,b;0,1].

Direct matrix multiplication shows that G is projectively g_N n(b) with

v=1/p, eta_S=-s p, b=(1-r)/s.               (7)

The renormalized associated-frame coordinate is

a_B=-eta_S b=p(1-r).

Its opposite-chart transition is a_B'=v-v^2 a_B, exactly the compact-source transition. Its associated compact radial coordinate is v a_B=1-r. This is initially different from the physical coordinate Log A/c_0 in (2).

They agree on one explicitly determined logarithmic centralizer section:

r_*(s)=1-Log A/c_0=1+Log(1+s)/c_0.           (8)

Any actual frame reaches this section by the original centralizer action r->r+b s, with

b=(r_*(s)-r)/s.

This is the explicit gauge/eigenflag transport; all developed cross-ratios and knot character functions stay constant. The common diagonal momentum p is a separate gauge from this centralizer normalization.

On the physical compact path A=exp(c_0 t_c), the section is r_*=1-t_c. It tends to r=1 at the south pole and r=0 at the north pole. After one marked A loop,

Log A -> Log A+c_0, r_* -> r_*-1.

The centralizer transition is consequently b=-1/s. Its primitive co-source changes by

-(1/s)dLog A=ds/[s(1+s)].                   (9)

Its residue at s=0 is exactly +1, and at s=-1 it is -1. This is a nonzero boundary source forced by the actual logarithmic section, with its deck orientation specified. A finite algebraic cover cannot make the logarithm single valued: it multiplies, rather than removes, its nonzero residue.

4. The full irreducible knot family must escape the collapsed meridian

Resolving one meridian does not regularize the entire knot representation. Normalize its meridian to mu_0=[1,1;0,1]. Irreducibility supplies a group word with

rho(g)=[A_0,B_0;C_0,D_0], C_0!=0.

Otherwise every group matrix would preserve the meridian’s unique eigenline. Direct multiplication gives the conjugacy invariant

Tr[mu_0,rho(g)]=2+C_0^2!=2.                (10)

If every conjugated group matrix extended regularly while the physical compact meridian tends to identity, this commutator would tend to identity and its trace to two, contradicting (10). Therefore some other holonomy necessarily has a pole. A single Springer flag cannot eliminate this full-family escape.

The pole can be seen explicitly. On the r=1 section, the conjugate of the displayed group matrix is

[A_0+B_0s, -B_0sp;
 (A_0-D_0+B_0s-C_0/s)/p, D_0-B_0s].

It has a south-pole term -C_0/(s p). On the r=0 section the conjugate is

[D_0+B_0s, p(A_0-D_0-B_0s+C_0/s);
 B_0s/p, A_0-B_0s],

with north-pole term C_0p/s. Along the compact real cycle these are respectively of order t_c^(-1/2) and (1-t_c)^(-1/2), with nonzero coefficients. These sections are the limits of the canonical logarithmic section (8). Projective developed flags may still have limits, but their coincidences and normal directions require more data than an ordinary regular matrix representation at the pole.

5. The principal Gaussian has an exact compact boundary residue

Retain the actual marked cut form and covector

Omega_cut=-dr wedge ds/[s(1+s)], lambda_flag=dLog p.

The normalization of the covector is fixed by the exact diagonal gauge in Section 1: it takes value one on its common logarithmic momentum generator. It has not been selected by guessing a Haar volume. In the coordinates (7), direct differentiation gives

d eta_S wedge dv wedge db
                 =-dr wedge ds wedge dp/(s p).

Consequently the exact full-form identity is

Omega_cut wedge dLog p
  =A_cut d eta_S wedge dv wedge db
  =-(d eta_S/eta_S) wedge dv wedge da_B
                                        /(1-eta_S v).   (11)

The interior Haar adapter is A_cut=(1+s)^(-1); it cannot be omitted away from the boundary. It is one at eta_S=0, so (11) has the canonical logarithmic residue

Res_(eta_S=0)(Omega_cut wedge dLog p)=-dv wedge da_B.      (12)

With the actual compact trace form

omega_I=-dv wedge da_B/(2pi*i), integral_Gamma omega_I=1,

equation (12) is a nonzero global compact-source class. The algebraic associated-frame transition makes this statement independent of the two charts.

The uniform Gaussian comparison gives, on its recorded square-root and base-line cover,

Omega_(beta,0)=c_beta Omega_cut,
c_beta^2=-T_beta/2, c_beta!=0.

Here the common original base-only line is factored but retained; it tensors both sides back into their original normalization. Since c_beta is conjugation invariant, (11)–(12) imply

(1/(2pi*i))Res_(eta_S=0)
       [Omega_(beta,0) wedge lambda_flag]=c_beta omega_I. (13)

Thus the normalized principal boundary form has actual compact period c_beta, with the original half-density phase retained. This is a computed residue, not an assumption that the critical family has a proper interior limit. Real gauge-circle orientation and normal-residue orientation must be transported as specified; they are not reset by taking an absolute norm.

6. Exact scope

The physical compact meridian map, the actual common-momentum gauge, the logarithmic centralizer section, and the principal boundary residue have now been computed from the actual frames. The original fixed-cut-flag two-dimensional torsor is not simply the compact coadjoint leaf: restoring momentum adds a third dimension, and the internal centralizer remains a separate fiber.

The associated boundary and the physical section must be kept distinct. In the associated-frame threefold, eta_S=0 is an independent divisor over the entire compact algebraic source X. On the physical logarithmic section, however, (3) imposes eta_S=(1-exp(-2pi*i*v*a_B))/v. At finite nonzero v, that section meets the divisor precisely where v*a_B is integral; at v=0 the removable formula instead requires a_B=0. On the real compact source with 0<=t_c<=1 these are its two endpoint poles. In particular the compact cycle in the residue divisor has not been proved to be the geometric pushforward of the original physical compact cycle.

Equation (13) proves nonzero leading boundary cohomology. It does not show that every higher Gaussian coefficient has only the same logarithmic pole, that all developed-flag degenerations are resolved, or that the actual full Green currents map to this boundary class with no additional contribution. Equation (10) shows that full critical-family escape is a real issue, not a hypothetical warning. The global geometric pushforward preserving the compact current period, and the selected volume growth, remain unproved.

7. Audit and dependencies

The exact symbolic checks in audit_compact_framing_gauge_boundary.py and its results verify the finite coherent gauge at levels 2 through 5, the full frame conjugation and Jacobian, both physical Springer charts, the logarithmic residue, both unavoidable escape poles, and the principal Gaussian boundary-form coefficient. The displayed calculations are universal rational or entire-function identities; the finite levels only audit the coherent-coordinate convention.

The principal determinant normalization is the one proved in the uniform Gaussian comparison, and the finite trace form and physical whole-clock limit are those in the actual compact source. The global associated-bundle construction and its normalization lines are developed separately in the Springer boundary-source note. The independent joint audit also checks the logarithmic deck signs, pole escape and principal residue normalization.
