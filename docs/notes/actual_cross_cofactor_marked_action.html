The full-V cofactor branch and the original marked peripheral action

The whole-source local packet now assembles these results for the actual full numerator. The original global spectral filling period remains open.

31 October 2026. Internally reviewed. The exact central-cofactor formulas are in actual cross cofactor factorization. This note identifies their indicated principal branch, including its absolute normalization and the actual primitive logs. It does NOT prove that a particular global quantum cycle selects it or that every complex cofactor has a single-branch relative asymptotic.

Use spectral w=-sigma-2 (the cofactor note calls it u); the ORIGINAL protected white faces are still named u,v and remain zero. At every port put A=x+y>0 and P=xy. The principal diagonal and cleared tail root are

a(t)=4cos^2(pi t), s(t)=2sin(pi t), Lambda(t;w)=[s(t)^2 A+sqrt(s(t)^4 A^2 +4s(t)^2(a(t)+w)P)]/2. (1)

For positive x,b,y select the root positive at w=0. Set

w=-4sin^2(pi delta), sigma=-2cos(2pi delta), s=sin(pi delta), c=cos(pi delta), T_b=sqrt[bA+xy+b^2 s^2], D=A^2-4xy s^2, E_b=A+2b s^2+2s T_b. (2)

All square roots/logs are the ones at delta=0. The finite physical node is delta_a=(N-1-2a)/(2N), not an unrecorded unshifted row a/N. The finite cofactors keep that half-index and all parity scalars.

1. Exact equality of the two normalized analytic actions

Define F_w by the stationary value

F_w=mu Log b+integral_0^mu Log(a(t)+w)dt +integral_mu^(1/2)Log Lambda(t;w)dt, (3)

where mu is the continuation of the complete interior solution. It obeys

b(a(mu)+w)=Lambda(mu;w), sin^2(pi mu)=c^2 b^2/[(b+x)(b+y)], mu=(1/pi)atan(b c/T_b). (4)

These identities follow by substitution into the quadratic for Lambda; no asymptotic differentiation is involved. For small positive real delta, mu<1/2-delta, so the first logarithm in (3) is positive along its path. For positive x,y, Lambda(t)/t is nonzero and analytic at t=0. Separating its log t singularity therefore defines a holomorphic germ of (3) near delta=0, including b=0 by its limiting stationary value. Ordinary positive b may vary down to zero on a fixed bounded interval after shrinking this germ. Formula (3) is a stationary analytic value for complex parameters, not a complex maximum operation.

For precision at b=0, write Lambda=t L with L(0) a unit and mu=b m with m(0) a unit. In mu Log b minus integral_0^mu Log t, the only potential logarithmic singularity is mu Log(b/mu); it is holomorphic. Thus this is an analytic extension, not just a continuous rate limit.

Let Q_b(delta) be the original determinant-plus-beta-plus-HALF-forced- product triangle action of the coupled complete source. Its proved literal definition, putting nu=1/2-delta, is

Q_b=I(2nu)-2I(nu)+integral_0^nu Log lambda_b(t)dt +(1/4)integral_nu^(1-nu) Log[x2+y2-2xy cos(2pi t)]dt, I(v)=integral_0^v Log(2sin(pi t))dt, lambda_b(t)=[A+2b cos^2(pi t) +2cos(pi t)sqrt(b2cos2(pi t)+bA+xy)]/[4cos^2(pi t)].

This is the actual beta, determinant and forced-product rate from actual positive face channel rate, not a chosen antiderivative. Cancellation of their spectral derivatives gives its normalization and differential

Q_b(0)=F(x,b,y), partial_delta Q_b=-Log(E_b/sqrt D), partial_Log(b) Q_b=(1/pi)atan(b c/T_b). (5)

Then the exact action-germ identity is

                     F_w=Q_b(delta).               (6)

Here is a complete constant check. The envelope rule in (3) gives partial_Log(b) F_w=mu, so F_w-Q_b is independent of b. Also

partial_delta mu=-b s/T_b, partial_Log(b) Log(E_b/sqrt D)=b s/T_b. (7)

The second identity follows directly from partial_b E_b=s E_b/T_b. It remains to fix the b-independent term, not assume it zero.

At b=0, put lambda=2sqrt(xy)/A in(0,1]. With theta=pi t, differentiating the integral in (3) gives

partial_delta F_w|_(b=0) =-lambda^2 s c integral_0^(pi/2) dtheta/[W(theta)(sin theta+W(theta))], W(theta)=sqrt[(1-lambda2)sin2 theta+lambda^2 c^2].

Rationalize the denominator and put z=cos theta. The result is

-s c integral_0^1 {1/sqrt(1-z^2) -1/sqrt(1-lambda^2 s2-(1-lambda2)z^2)} dz/(z2-s2). (8)

The apparent pole at z=s is removable: the two square roots there are both c. For an elementary evaluation, each integral of 1/[(z2-s2)sqrt(B-Cz^2)] has antiderivative

[1/(2s c)] log |(c z/sqrt(B-Cz^2)-s) /(c z/sqrt(B-Cz^2)+s)|,

because B-C s2=c2 in BOTH terms of (8). Subtract these antiderivatives before evaluating: their logarithmic singularities at z=s cancel, with no jump. They both vanish at z=0. At z=1 the first tends to zero and the second is -artanh(lambda s)/(s c). Hence

partial_delta F_w|_(b=0)=-artanh(lambda s) =-Log[(A+2s sqrt(xy))/sqrt(A^2-4xy s^2)]. (9)

This calculation uses small real delta>0, where every real branch is specified; holomorphic continuation then gives the identity of germs. It agrees EXACTLY with (5) at b=0. Equations (7),(9) give the full partial_delta identity for every b>=0. Finally (3) at delta=0 is the already calibrated complete triangle action: integral_0^(1/2)log a=0, and the actual lower-block product contributes no exponential constant. Thus F_w-Q_b vanishes at delta=0 and has zero delta derivative, proving (6) with no additive face-dependent or spectral constant.

2. All allowed marked zeros, including the lost local strip flag

U_i=-Log R0_i-epsilon_i Log(-J_i), V_i=2Log(-J_i)=-4pi i delta_i, partial_delta_i A_epsilon=U_i. (17)

Thus these are actual local coordinates on the same marked geometric germ as the complete Fourier core. This is a LONGITUDE-polarized primitive. Put G_lon=2pi i A_epsilon. Then

dG_lon=-(1/2)sum_i U_i dV_i. (19)

The core instead has chi_i=-U_i/(2pi i) and S_core(chi)=A_epsilon(delta)+2pi i sum_i chi_i delta_i. Consequently

G_mer=2pi i S_core=G_lon+(1/2)sum_i U_i V_i, dG_mer=(1/2)sum_i V_i dU_i. (20)

5. Original Gaussian charges and primitive filling logs

For an actual fixed crossing exponent n_i=epsilon_i-2p_i, the full Hermite Gaussian contributes pi i n_i delta_i^2 and a surviving Fourier charge ell_i contributes 2pi i ell_i delta_i, with their original constant scalar signs retained. Its principal phase is therefore

                 U_i+p_i V_i=-2pi i ell_i.        (22)

             2pi Re S_fill=Vol(rho_delta)           (24)
