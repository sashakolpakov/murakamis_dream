Exact finite-window conditioning near rational phase colors

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

For the complete finite-level repeated-turn equations, a fixed window near a rational phase has finitely many analytic coefficient germs in h=1/Nh=1/N. A nonzero full-column minor gives polynomial conditioning on its leading-coefficient unit chart. This conclusion includes all physical first normals. It does not prove that such a minor exists for every active product, and it does not replace the boundary data required by a window with lower-rate data that have not been supplied.

1. A fixed actual window and its supplied data

Use the original repeated-turn source 𝒴N=UτZN,τ∈{1,−1},q=eπi/N,CN(u)=2cos⁡(2πu+π/N).(1) \mathcal Y_N=U_\tau Z_N,\qquad \tau\in\{1,-1\}, \quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad C_N(u)=2\cos(2\pi u+\pi/N). \qquad\text{(1)} The column ZNZ_N, every cleared full-step row, and all retained source and recovery equations are those of the pinned full-step interface. All counts and signed powers are fixed. Microscopic parameters 𝜶\boldsymbol\alpha remain in a fixed bounded complex neighborhood; they are not independent physical colors being silently quotiented.

Fix a reduced rational phase 0<a/b<1/2,gcd⁡(a,b)=1,v*=exp⁡(2πia/b).(2) 0<a/b<1/2,\qquad \gcd(a,b)=1,\qquad v_*=\exp(2\pi i a/b). \qquad\text{(2)} Let jN=⌊Na/b⌋.(3) j_N=\lfloor Na/b\rfloor . \qquad\text{(3)} Choose a fixed finite set of translated exact rows near jNj_N. Include their value and first-uu-normal equations. Specify a fixed finite list of unknown shifted components and their normals, forming a packet XNX_N of dimension dd. Every other shifted component appearing in the chosen rows must either be included in XNX_N or put in a separately specified supplied packet BNB_N. Any genuine inhomogeneous source packet is denoted FNF_N. Thus the actual finite equations have the form 𝖠NXN=FN−𝖫NBN.(4) \mathsf A_N X_N=F_N-\mathsf L_N B_N . \qquad\text{(4)} Rows containing additional unknowns cannot be used in (4) until those unknowns have been accounted for. Repeated occurrences of the same physical component are identified. The window is interior for sufficiently large NN, so all its spectral nodes are double and the uu-normal and spectral normal differ by uniformly invertible factors.

The choice of supplied packet is part of the assertion. For example, if a two-sided recovery uses a right boundary packet known only to have size eCNe^{C N}, (4) retains that size. It is not an initial-value transport bound at a smaller left boundary rate.

2. Exact residue classes and meromorphic coefficient germs

Lemma 1. Split the levels into residue classes modulo a fixed multiple of bb. On each such class there is a fixed rational δ∈(−1,1]\delta\in(-1,1] such that every shifted physical angular color in the window is exactly vjN+k=q2(jN+k)+1=v*qδ+2k,δ=2⌊Na/b⌋+1−2Na/b.(5) v_{j_N+k}=q^{2(j_N+k)+1} =v_*q^{\delta+2k},\qquad \delta=2\lfloor Na/b\rfloor+1-2Na/b . \qquad\text{(5)} After splitting further for any retained parity or rational carrier phases, every coefficient of 𝖠N,𝖫N\mathsf A_N,\mathsf L_N is the restriction of a meromorphic germ 𝖠(h,𝜶),𝖫(h,𝜶)(6) \mathsf A(h,\boldsymbol\alpha),\qquad \mathsf L(h,\boldsymbol\alpha) \qquad\text{(6)} at h=0h=0, with at most a finite-order pole. Proof. If NN increases by bb, the floor in (3) increases by aa, so δ\delta is unchanged. Formula (5) follows without approximation.

The raw interface coefficients are obtained by finitely many rational operations, shifts and polynomial coefficient clearings from q±1,h,v±1,wi=2cosh⁡(αih),PN(wi)=4cosh⁡2(αi/2).(7) q^{\pm1},\quad h,\quad v^{\pm1},\quad w_i=2\cosh(\alpha_i h),\quad P_N(w_i)=4\cosh^2(\alpha_i/2). \qquad\text{(7)} Removable scalar powers of hh in their definitions are retained with their exact limits. Any remaining coefficient denominator gives a meromorphic germ; on the stated coefficient chart its nonremovable part is a unit after a finite power of hh is extracted. The numbers of operations and shifts are fixed.

For the normal rows, ordinary differentiation at fixed NN acts on the coefficient angular variable by 2πiv∂v2\pi i v\,\partial_v. It preserves this meromorphic class. The ordinary residual is a multiple of PN(CN(u))P_N(C_N(u)), so its value and first derivative do vanish at the grid; no off-grid homogeneous equation is used.

The interface operator coefficients do not require a varying Gaussian carrier to be discarded. If a chosen row normalization retains η=(−1)N−1\eta=(-1)^{N-1} or κ=exp⁡{πi(N−1)2/(2N)}=−iNeπih/2,(8) \kappa=\exp\{\pi i(N-1)^2/(2N)\} =-i^N e^{\pi i h/2}, \qquad\text{(8)} enlarge the modulus to include 44. More generally choose a common multiple for every fixed rational phase actually retained. This finite refinement does not change the source or its modulus on physical levels.

No meromorphic-germ assertion is made about XN,BN,FNX_N,B_N,F_N themselves. They remain the actual possibly exponentially large source packets. ▫\square

The coefficient-chart qualification is substantive. At a parameter where a proposed denominator loses its leading unit, re-clear the original rows or choose another valid coefficient chart before applying the lemma. Nonunit physical clearing factors are not canceled in the original algebra.

3. Nonzero actual minors give polynomial recovery

Theorem 2. Fix one residue class and one coefficient chart from Lemma 1. Suppose hs𝖠(h,𝜶)h^s\mathsf A(h,\boldsymbol\alpha) is holomorphic and bounded there, and some selection of dd actual rows has determinant det⁡𝖠̂0(h,𝜶)=hν{c(𝜶)+hr(h,𝜶)},ν≥0,𝖠̂=hs𝖠.(9) \det \widehat{\mathsf A}_0(h,\boldsymbol\alpha) =h^\nu\{c(\boldsymbol\alpha)+h r(h,\boldsymbol\alpha)\}, \qquad \nu\ge0, \quad \widehat{\mathsf A}=h^s\mathsf A . \qquad\text{(9)} On a compact parameter set where |c(𝜶)|≥c*>0|c(\boldsymbol\alpha)|\ge c_*>0, there is a left inverse of the full matrix 𝖠N\mathsf A_N with norm at most CNKC N^K, for a fixed KK. Consequently the original packet satisfies ∥XN∥≤CNK′(∥FN∥+∥BN∥).(10) \|X_N\|\le C N^{K'} \bigl(\|F_N\|+\|B_N\|\bigr). \qquad\text{(10)} All quantities on the right are the supplied packets of (4). The constants and exponents are uniform on that fixed compact unit chart.

Proof. After decreasing the hh-disk, the expression in braces in (9) has modulus at least c*/2c_*/2. The entries of the adjugate of 𝖠̂0\widehat{\mathsf A}_0 are uniformly bounded, since its size is fixed. Its inverse is therefore O(h−ν)O(h^{-\nu}). Select those rows of hs𝖠Xh^s\mathsf A X, invert, and retain the factor hsh^s on the right. This defines a left inverse of the full matrix; one may take K=max⁡(0,ν−s)K=\max(0,\nu-s). The remaining coefficient matrix 𝖫N\mathsf L_N is bounded by a fixed power of NN by Lemma 1. Applying the left inverse to (4) proves (10). No equation or source channel has been dropped: the selected rows recover the packet, while the other actual rows remain its compatibility equations. ▫\square

For fixed parameters, this gives an exact eventual dichotomy. Either at least one full-column minor is a nonzero meromorphic germ, in which case the rank is full and the conditioning is polynomial for all sufficiently large levels in that residue class, or every such minor is the zero germ. In the latter case the chosen matrix is rank deficient for all sufficiently small hh in its coefficient chart. This is rank of the specified finite window; it is not a statement that the entire global source problem has a free parameter.

For varying parameters, a nonzero minor as a germ in (h,𝜶)(h,\boldsymbol\alpha) does not give uniform conditioning on an arbitrary compact set. The leading coefficient in (9) can vanish there, and parameters may approach its zero set very rapidly with NN. The unit-chart hypothesis must be verified or replaced by a separate stratified estimate.

If the supplied data obey uniform bounds ∥FN∥≤eNAF+o(N),∥BN∥≤eNAB+o(N),(11) \|F_N\|\le e^{N A_F+o(N)},\qquad \|B_N\|\le e^{N A_B+o(N)}, \qquad\text{(11)} then (10) gives only limsupN→∞N−1log⁡∥XN∥≤max⁡(AF,AB)(12) \limsup_{N\to\infty}N^{-1}\log\|X_N\| \le\max(A_F,A_B) \qquad\text{(12)} along the residue class. Thus a right endpoint ceiling larger than the incoming rate remains larger in this result. All fixed parameter derivatives satisfy the same statement if the unit bound holds on a larger parameter neighborhood and the supplied source bounds hold there, by Cauchy’s formula. The actual first physical normals are already entries of (4).

4. What must still be proved for the complete repeated-turn source

The matrices in this note are formed from the full finite-NN rows and their normal equations, with any original recovery and source constraints used in the window explicitly included. They are not formed from a principal scalar certificate. The rank-loss theorem gives the same warning at a single row: the first-normal block can determine a missing value without determining its normal, whereas further aligned rows can restore the full rank.

For fixed active products, finitely many rational exceptional phases and finitely many residue classes therefore reduce local polynomial conditioning to exact nonvanishing assertions of the form (9), together with honestly supplied boundary packets. This note does not establish those assertions for every product or every minimal resonance core. Nor does it prescribe a fixed window that must work universally.

An identity of all candidate minors may reflect a genuine remaining local channel, an unnecessarily large unknown packet, or extra solutions introduced by a nonunit clearing. It must be examined using the original source constraints. Conversely a nonzero minor may arise from small quantum terms even when the classical phase is exceptional. The arithmetic observation (5) does not decide either outcome.

No rate for a general repeated-turn output, no disappearance of a clearing component, and no geometric gap is inferred. The conditioning statement is useful only after its exact rank and boundary-data premises are met.
