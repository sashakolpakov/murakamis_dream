An interior five-point wall cannot be relocated by bare cut cyclicity

Research note, 28 October 2026. This tests a possible simplification of the retained wall in the literal middle five-point calculation. At the marked endpoint that wall contributes a known polynomial scalar. The same assertion is false for an untransported interior wall. An original one-crossing cup and an original figure-eight network give explicit incompatible relocation factors.

The result concerns the original actual source, not a hypothetical state model. It does not exclude a Ward-completed relocation with additional currents and its full relative co-source. It proves which additional data cannot be discarded.

1. Fix the original wall and source conventions

Take N>=2, Q=exp(2pi*i/N), complete color x=-q^(-1), lambda=x^(-1)=Qx, d=N-1, k=xN=(-1)(N-1). Use actual balanced heads K=xZ^(-1), e=q rho^2 E, f=F, and cyclic marks

S_1^N=mu, S_2^N=k mu,
X=x S_2S_1^(-1), W=E_d(Z_1)X.

Equivalently,

W=x mu^(-1)|d><0| tensor S_2.                (1)

The original cup and full averaged marked cap are

p=N sum_(a=0)^d x^(a-d)|a,d-a>,
m|a,b>=x^b/N^2, mp=1.                       (2)

Let B be the original positive checked crossing, with its unchanged common scalar Cartan line kappa external. Thus the full crossing is kappa B and Bp=p. All framing and peripheral factors below multiply the same expressions; none is reset to a new numerical value.

The endpoint identities, already proved in the preceding note, are

Xp=Q^(-1)p, mWp=Q^(-1)/N.

For any actual knot cut C_N=K_N I, a defect on that marked endpoint therefore gives

mW(C_N tensor I)p=Q^(-1)K_N/N,
m(1-W)(C_N tensor I)p=(1-Q^(-1)/N)K_N.        (3)

The second multiplier has modulus between 1/2 and 3/2, so this endpoint correction preserves every normalized volume exponent, without a volume conjecture premise. Equations (3) alone do not relocate an interior two-head coupon.

2. The exact naturality/current condition fails

The coupon (1) acts on two physical heads. Cutting its two ends does not leave a one-strand knot endomorphism. Moreover it is not a Hopf intertwiner. For the actual total lowering current F_tot=f_1+K_1^(-1)f_2, direct multiplication gives

[K_1K_2,W]=0,
[F_tot,W]=(Q^(-1)-1)mu^(-1)
                          |d><0| tensor S_2^2 !=0.      (4)

Indeed f_1W=Wf_1=0. The two remaining products are mu^(-1)|d><0| tensor Q^(-1)f_2S_2 and mu^(-1)|d><0| tensor S_2f_2; their difference is (4). The defect is nonzero at every N>=2 and every nonzero mu. Thus weight zero is insufficient for naturality.

If a single-head coupon D is an actual Hopf intertwiner, then commuting with the distinct K eigenvalues and all nonzero consecutive F arrows forces D=cI. Such a coupon can be relocated along that strand with the original normalizations. For a multi-head coupon, block naturality requires its actual tensor-object intertwining equations, and its closing functional is not automatically a scalar one-strand cut. Coupon (1) does not even pass (4).

More generally, moving an inserted coupon through a typed crossing R must retain the exact discrepancy

Delta_R=R D_in-D_out R.                      (5)

An admissible relative Ward comparison may remove (5) only after exhibiting its actual typed current homotopy and checking its pairing with the full marked co-source. Vanishing of an unmarked scalar or a cyclic matrix trace does not provide that check. The existing Ward co-source homotopy makes the issue explicit: if mu-ell=nu F on the relevant fiber, then with an inserted W the omitted term is nu F(AWBp). For original intertwining exteriors and Fp=0, this contains the term nu A[F,W]Bp. Equation (4) supplies the literal current that must be transported here.

3. Two ordinary partial traces lose the actual source

Retain the original pivot g=x(1-N)Z(-1). Both ordinary pivotal partial traces vanish:

Tr_2[(1 tensor g)W]=0,
Tr_1[(g tensor 1)W]=0.                       (6)

This follows directly from (1): both |d><0| and the cyclic shift S_2 have zero diagonal. Nevertheless mWp=Q^(-1)/N is nonzero. Consequently an ordinary partial trace or an argument dividing by the ordinary quantum dimension cannot give the original marked evaluation. Indeed Tr(g)=0 at the same color. The original modified/relative source is essential, rather than an optional normalization of (6).

Modified-trace cyclicity is a statement about its specified categorical morphisms. Extending it to all arbitrary linear coupons while retaining this marked functional would be false, as the next calculation shows.

4. An all-level one-crossing identity

Define the actual finite Euler value

U_Euler,N(1)=sum_(j=0)^d 1/F_j,
F_j=(Q;Q)_j.

This is the Euler polynomial of the original crossing, not the differently named meridian norm adapter u_N in other notes. Then exactly

m W Bp=Q^(-1)/N,
m B Wp=Q^(-1)U_Euler,N(1).                  (7)

To prove the second identity note Wp=NQ^(-1)|d,0>. The original crossing coefficient from (d,0) to (h,d-h) is

r(d,0,h)=N x^(-d-h)Q^(-h)/F_(d-h).

Multiplication by m contributes x(d-h)/N2. Since x^(-2)=Q, the product with NQ^(-1) simplifies to Q^(-1)/F_(d-h). Summing h proves (7). The first identity uses the original Bp=p. Thus no published modified-trace proof or unknown scalar is involved.

At N=2, Q=-1, x=i, these marked values are

mWBp=-1/2, mBWp=-3/2.                       (8)

The full original crossing changes these to -kappa/2 and -3kappa/2, with identical exterior factors. They are still unequal. This is an actual one-crossing source counterexample to bare cyclic relocation.

5. A scalar multiplier cannot repair all exteriors

Use the actual three-braid figure-eight word

T=B_1 B_2^(-1)B_1 B_2^(-1).

Keep the global typed wrap marks (1,k,1), rather than resetting them independently at each insertion. Close heads 2 and 3 with the original pivots. The marked first cut uses the full functional N^(-1)sum_(a,b)C_(a,b) in the balanced basis, exactly as in the original Gauss source. For the uninserted scalar cut this is the original invariant. After inserting a nonintertwining coupon it is kept fixed and is not replaced by a highest entry.

At N=2 the uninserted cut is 5I. Insert W on heads 1,2 at each of the five displayed word gaps, from left to right. Their original marked values are

(-5/2,-3/2,-5/2,5/2,5/2).                   (9)

These are exact complex-root values. The word has writhe zero, so the common scalar crossing factors cancel in the same way in all five expressions. Its original full peripheral normalization is also the same throughout.

The gap-0 to gap-1 relocation is through the same local positive crossing as in (8). In (8) its ratio is 3; in (9) its ratio is 3/5. Consequently there is no scalar c_N(M), depending only on that local crossing, the root and peripheral color, that repairs this untransported wall move in every original exterior. A phase or a factor such as 1+M^n cannot fix this environment dependence. A larger current/source state must be retained.

6. Consequence for the remaining proof

The marked endpoint wall in (3) is controlled by a known nonzero scalar and costs no exponential uncertainty. That result survives. What fails is moving an arbitrary interior wall to that endpoint while keeping its bare formula and then applying (3).

A possible valid route is to transport W together with its current (4) and the original co-source homotopy, including all residues and normals. The discrepancy (5) then has a precisely stated relative pairing to check. Neither ordinary trace cyclicity nor a local scalar adjustment supplies that transport. The computations here do not prove that the required completed currents cannot cancel across a full five-point move. They show that such cancellation is a genuine missing theorem.

No sharp upper or lower bound relative to a selected geometric period follows from these relocation tests. In particular the exact endpoint polynomial factor does not select a geometric saddle of the interior period.

7. Independent exact audit

Run

python3 experiments/audit_actual_interior_wall_cut_relocation.py

The audit uses original E/Cartan crossing coefficients and the original pivots. It checks twelve complete/wrap families for (4), (6), (7), and twelve original figure-eight gap families through N=7 over exact prime fields. A separate exact SymPy calculation at Q=-1,x=i proves (8)–(9), including the uninserted cut 5I. Highest-entry diagnostics are recorded separately and are never used to replace the full marked source after insertion.
