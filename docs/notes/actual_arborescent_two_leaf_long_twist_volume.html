Knot complement volume for arbitrary trees of two-or-more-leaf Conway seeds

31 October 2026. This research theorem removes the geometric leaf test from the previous two-leaf extension. The argument contracts an entire two-leaf block before estimating its aliases. The resulting uniform bound applies automatically to every proper seed in the stated grammar. This is an theorem for an explicit arborescent class, not a theorem for every arborescent or hyperbolic knot.

1. The class and theorem

Start with the original complete crossing-circle coupon h=ℛ(LN)h=\mathcal R(L_N), where ℛ\mathcal R is the actual ordered quarter-turn. Use distinct leaves and fixed finite expressions

As=hs,s≥2,E::=As∣ℛ(E)∣E1E2. A_s=h^s,\quad s\ge2,\qquad E::=A_s\mid\mathcal R(E)\mid E_1E_2. \tag{1}

Use the original cylindrical closure C(FG)C(FG), with both children generated by (1), or an isolated closure C(As)C(A_s) with s≥3s\ge3. Choose a fixed leaf half-twist pattern ϵℓ∈{0,1,−1}\epsilon_\ell\in\{0,1,-1\} whose literal endpoint pairings give a knot after all auxiliary circles are filled. Such a loop-free pattern can be constructed for every expression. The isolated C(A2)C(A_2) is excluded.

Insert independently chosen fixed disk-framed full twists R−2pℓR^{-2p_\ell} at the auxiliary crossing circles. Thus the actual all-filled leaf is the rational tangle

ℛ(Rϵℓ−2pℓ). \mathcal R(R^{\epsilon_\ell-2p_\ell}). \tag{2}

Theorem. For each fixed expression and knot half-twist pattern there is a finite threshold T(E,ϵ)T(E,\epsilon) such that, whenever all fixed signed integers satisfy |pℓ|>T(E,ϵ)|p_\ell|>T(E,\epsilon), the resulting knot KE,ϵ(p)K_{E,\epsilon}(p) is hyperbolic and

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(KE,ϵ(p))|=Vol⁡(S3\KE,ϵ(p)). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log\left|K_N\bigl(K_{E,\epsilon}(p)\bigr)\right| =\operatorname{Vol}\bigl(S^3\setminus K_{E,\epsilon}(p)\bigr). \tag{3}

Both level parities and composite levels are included. The number of leaves, branching and turn depth are arbitrary finite numbers. The expression and twists are fixed before NN tends to infinity; the theorem imposes neither common slope signs nor comparable slope magnitudes. It does not assert a threshold uniform over all trees.

The invariant is the original marked one-cut invariant with its retained enhancement. The reduced enhanced Kashaev normalization has an outer factor of unit modulus, and the full modified one-cut normalization has the recorded single factor of modulus 1/N1/N. These are the existing normalization comparison and crossing calculation. Neither normalization changes (3).

Only auxiliary crossing-circle cusps are filled. The knot cusp remains unfilled, and the right side of (3) is the knot complement volume. Literal rational leaves, actual stacking, turns and the prescribed closure establish arborescent membership; no arithmetic identity of tangle fractions is substituted for those operations.

2. The estimate that removes the leaf condition

The complete two-leaf geometry already proves for every proper A2A_2 seed

12<X=cot⁡(πa)<1,cot⁡(πb)=X−12X,F2′(b)=2log⁡(2X2). \frac12<X=\cot(\pi a)<1,\qquad \cot(\pi b)=X-\frac1{2X},\qquad F_2'(b)=2\log(2X^2). \tag{4}

Previously a supporting plane at each leaf separately required X≥2/3X\ge2/3. The uniform block theorem instead includes the original multiplication gate and proves

GAC(z),GAA(z)≤F2(b)+F2′(b)(z−b)−1100,0≤z≤1,12≤X≤1. G_{AC}(z),\ G_{AA}(z) \le F_2(b)+F_2'(b)(z-b)-\frac1{100}, \qquad 0\le z\le1,\quad \frac12\le X\le1. \tag{5}

The proof uses joint concavity and a finite exact rational certificate for the whole slope interval. Each supporting plane bounds the entire allowed polytope, and affine endpoint checks cover each slope interval. It keeps the true x+y≤1x+y\le1 constraint. It does not sample unknown maximizers or extrapolate from selected knots.

The exact finite block transport groups two original leaves and their one gate without changing the source. Every Hermite term, physical color, phase and normalization remains present. The internal first derivatives vanish at completeness, so the block tangent in (5) has exactly the same derivative on its surrounding wire as the original action.

In any sector containing an alias, use (5) on a contaminated two-leaf seed. Larger proper seeds retain their already automatic positive leaf bounds. Sum with the other complete seed and macro-product tangents. The original closed-tree stationarity cancels all free linear terms. The result has a strict gap below the complete source action. The actual d=N−1d=N-1 shifts cost only O(1)O(1) in the exponent; a fixed tree has polynomially many wire states and finitely many alias subsets. Thus the bound holds for the entire original alias contribution, uniformly in all physical colors.

The all-core majorant agrees near the complete point with the strictly concave pure action and has that same unique global maximum. Outside a fixed strict neighborhood it has a compact gap. Inside, the original far-color core estimate supplies the strict loss when any physical color leaves the complete corridor. These facts give both whole-source hypotheses of the filling transfer.

3. Why the rate is the actual knot complement volume

The two-leaf source proof retains the exact primitive lattice and all offsets, including the missing even-level top coefficient of h2h^2. Strict interior rounding supplies every sufficiently large level. Its complex packet has power N3/2N^{3/2} and nonzero leading amplitude, with the actual changed local action Φϵ\Phi_\epsilon and explicitly retained unit phase. No new packet is chosen to fit the block estimate.

Consequently the original-source filling transfer gives a nonzero asymptotic for the full invariant, with exponential action at the selected small saddle of

Sϵ,p(χ)=Φϵ(χ)+πi2∑ℓ(χℓ−1)2pℓ. S_{\epsilon,p}(\chi)=\Phi_\epsilon(\chi) +\frac{\pi i}{2}\sum_\ell \frac{(\chi_\ell-1)^2}{p_\ell}. \tag{6}

The geometric extension retains the actual signed augmented-link group, ordered face maps and primitive peripheral coordinates

Uℓ=−2πiχℓ,Vℓ=−2∂ℓΦϵ,G=2πiΦϵ. U_\ell=-2\pi i\chi_\ell,\qquad V_\ell=-2\partial_\ell\Phi_\epsilon,\qquad G=2\pi i\Phi_\epsilon. \tag{7}

Stationarity of (6) is exactly Uℓ+pℓVℓ=−2πiU_\ell+p_\ell V_\ell=-2\pi i. The absolutely calibrated regulator and marked geometric completion on this same branch give

2πℜSϵ,p(χ*)=Vol⁡(S3\KE,ϵ(p)). 2\pi\Re S_{\epsilon,p}(\chi_*) =\operatorname{Vol}\bigl(S^3\setminus K_{E,\epsilon}(p)\bigr). \tag{8}

In particular the right side is neither merely the augmented-link volume nor the volume of an unrelated critical solution. Equations (5)–(8) and the nonzero full-source asymptotic prove (3).

4. The previously excluded three-leaf closure

The isolated three-leaf theorem also removes the old exclusion of C(A3)C(A_3). Its complete state is a=arctan⁡(2)/πa=\arctan(2)/\pi, with pair output b=1−ab=1-a, X=1/2X=1/2, and F0=2v8/πF_0=2v_8/\pi. The block lemma includes this endpoint. The exact full Newton algebra is commutative, so a sector with one or two aliases may pair those aliases first, leaving a core. The block slope equals that core’s complete slope, −log⁡4-\log4, and the original closing relation cancels their linear terms.

For three aliases, each leaf has upper rate v3/πv_3/\pi, and the one retained product has rate at most 3v3/π3v_3/\pi. The elementary telescoping sine-series bound proves

F0−6v3π=2π(v8−3v3)>574725344>1100. F_0-\frac{6v_3}{\pi} =\frac2\pi(v_8-3v_3) >\frac{5747}{25344}>\frac1{100}. \tag{9}

This supplies the whole-source gap for all seven alias sectors. The all-core maximum and far-color loss give the physical-color complement bound. Its existing primitive three-dimensional packet retains the essential N−1N-1 shift, nonzero amplitude and changed half-twist phases. The existing geometric closure has one completed child and one leaf, so its CAP seam has angle π/2\pi/2; that separate case is retained explicitly. Its actual complete volume, primitive paths and absolute regulator then give the same filling conclusion (3). The already proved isolated closures C(As)C(A_s), s≥4s\ge4, continue to apply. Hence every isolated seed closure with s≥3s\ge3 is included, while C(A2)C(A_2) remains excluded.

5. Scope of the enlargement

The former local-tangent failure C(A2ℛ(A4))C(A_2\mathcal R(A_4)) is now covered, as are all other fixed expressions in (1) with the prescribed closure. There is no remaining algebraic leaf test to pass. The earlier leaf-certified theorem remains valid as a sufficient special case and records the original limitation; the present theorem supersedes that limitation for this grammar.

A coverage theorem for every arborescent presentation, or for arbitrary rational-tangle additions outside (1), has not been proved. The exact quantum boundary contraction and the signed estimate must survive those additional operations before claiming that scope. Likewise no generic main-meridian quantum A-operator is produced here.

The theorem applies the stated packet and marked-completion results to this recursive class. Extension beyond that class remains a separate question.
