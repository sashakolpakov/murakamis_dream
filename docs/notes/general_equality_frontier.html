Established volume results and the remaining research question

25 November 2026.

The paper collection is the current source for the proved statements. The dependency graph records which statements their proofs use. The reference manuscript gives a unified account; it does not add hypotheses or conclusions to the individual papers.

The strict Conway theorem proves the reduced Kashaev volume identity for each eligible fixed construction with independently sufficiently long auxiliary fillings. Ordered circle trees have separate summed and directed degree-and-twist criteria. The planar results require their specified balance or certificate hypotheses. A nonzero central contribution for a more general diagram is distinct from a bound on its entire complement. These distinctions are stated in the illustrated results guide.

For each fixed strict template, long-filling assignments have density one in its integer lattice. The theorem leaves infinitely many short-coordinate assignments untreated. It does not establish finitely many exceptions, density one among distinct knots, or a theorem for every arborescent knot.

The extension problem is to permit an arbitrary fixed short partial filling and prove the limit for all sufficiently long remaining fillings that give hyperbolic knots. The research status explains the quantifiers, infinite-fiber difficulty, exact orthogonal elimination of linear packets and current finite-source reductions. The route guide keeps the distinct methods and their limitations accessible.

This page replaces an accumulated list of intermediate local results. The earlier text is available in Git history. Its historical assertions are not a separate current proof inventory.
