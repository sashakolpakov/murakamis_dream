An exact local adapter from the division-free braid to the Newton algebra

Written derivation, 14 November 2026. The external formulas are those of rivin-jones, commit e956fb919ac7d1bbc7cb3d3873fbac993feb94b7, in docs/MATHEMATICS.md and python/rivin_jones/r_matrix.py. Only their written formulas are used. No external code was imported, executed or used for a numerical calculation.

There is an explicit regular change of strand coordinates. With the external choice AN=e−πi/(2N)A_N=e^{-\pi i/(2N)}, it reverses the crossing sign: the external positive crossing becomes our literal negative crossing. The change is unitary at this root. Restriction to a specified cyclic middle-weight space then identifies the full Newton regular module, including every first normal at a repeated Casimir root. This is not a compression of an arbitrary N2N^2-dimensional operator to NN coordinates.

2. Root specialization retains the entire crossing polynomial

Specialize A=ANA=A_N, ζ=q=eπi/N\zeta=q=e^{\pi i/N}, and write TN=TANT_N=T_{A_N}. Every entry on the anti-diagonal of TNT_N has modulus one. Hence TNT_N is unitary for the coordinate Euclidean norms, and (3) introduces no norm amplification in those strand coordinates.

Keep the actual physical pair Casimir Ω\Omega and define Ωext=(TN⊗TN)−1Ω(TN⊗TN). \Omega_{\mathrm{ ext}}=(T_N\otimes T_N)^{-1}\Omega(T_N\otimes T_N). The physical pair calculation gives PN(Ω)=0,PN(z)=2TNCheb(z/2)+2,R+bal(q)=κrN(Ω),κ=qd2/2, P_N(\Omega)=0,\qquad P_N(z)=2T_N^{\mathrm{ Cheb}}(z/2)+2, \qquad R^{\mathrm{ bal}}_+(q)=\kappa r_N(\Omega), \quad \kappa=q^{d^2/2}, where the superscript distinguishes the Chebyshev polynomial from the coordinate adapter. Therefore (3) gives exact full-pair identities R+ext(AN)=κ−1rN(Ωext)−1,R−ext(AN)=κrN(Ωext).(8) R^{\mathrm{ ext}}_+(A_N)=\kappa^{-1}r_N(\Omega_{\mathrm{ ext}})^{-1}, \qquad R^{\mathrm{ ext}}_-(A_N)=\kappa r_N(\Omega_{\mathrm{ ext}}). \qquad\text{(8)} The inverse in (8) is the inverse in the full quotient 𝒜N=ℂ[z]/(PN)\mathcal A_N=\mathbb C[z]/(P_N).

For specificity, at a double node cj=q2j+1+q−2j−1c_j=q^{2j+1}+q^{-2j-1}, choose 2j+1<N2j+1<N. Put νj=(−1)jqj(j+1),αj=rN′(cj)νj=i(d−2j)4sin⁡((2j+1)π/N). \nu_j=(-1)^j q^{j(j+1)},\qquad \alpha_j=\frac{r_N'(c_j)}{\nu_j} =\frac{i(d-2j)}{4\sin((2j+1)\pi/N)}. Neither normal has been dropped. At the possible odd simple node only the value is present. These statements follow from the full matrix identity and the physical confluent polynomial, not an inference from coincident eigenvalues. Although |αj|≤N2/8|\alpha_j|\le N^2/8, this alone does not bound a complete word in an arbitrary recoupling frame.

3. An explicit faithful cyclic space for the Newton comparison

Let Wd=span⁡{|j,d−j⟩:0≤j≤d},A=Ω|Wd,b0=|0,d⟩. W_d=\operatorname{span}\{|j,d-j\rangle:0\le j\le d\}, \qquad A=\Omega|_{W_d},\qquad b_0=|0,d\rangle. This is the physical middle-degree space. Its dimension is NN. The Casimir is the actual tridiagonal matrix, with adjacent entries −(1−Qj)2-(1-Q^j)^2, 1≤j≤d1\le j\le d, where Q=q2Q=q^2. The pair-algebra proof shows that its minimal polynomial is the full PNP_N. In particular b0b_0 is cyclic.

Put Sr=∏a=1r2sin⁡(πa/N)S_r=\prod_{a=1}^r2\sin(\pi a/N), with S0=1S_0=1. Let er(z)=Sr−2∏a=0r−1(ca−z)e_r(z)=S_r^{-2}\prod_{a=0}^{r-1}(c_a-z) be the normalized Newton basis. Define UN:𝒜N→Wd,UNf=f(A)b0.(10) U_N:\mathcal A_N\longrightarrow W_d,\qquad U_N f=f(A)b_0. \qquad\text{(10)} This is an explicitly specified matrix: its column rr is er(A)b0e_r(A)b_0. That column has no coordinates beyond rr; its coordinate at rr is Fr2Sr2=(−1)rqr(r+1)≠0,Fr=(Q;Q)r.(11) \frac{F_r^2}{S_r^2}=(-1)^r q^{r(r+1)}\ne0, \qquad F_r=(Q;Q)_r. \qquad\text{(11)} Indeed only the degree-rr term can reach that coordinate. Its leading coefficient is (−1)r/Sr2(-1)^r/S_r^2, and the unique outermost tridiagonal path contributes (−1)rFr2(-1)^rF_r^2. This proves (11) and invertibility of (10), without diagonalizing the Casimir.

The external conserved degree-dd space is Wdext=span⁡{ej⊗ed−j:0≤j≤d}. W_d^{\mathrm{ ext}}=\operatorname{span}\{e_j\otimes e_{d-j}:0\le j\le d\}. It maps to WdW_d under TN⊗TNT_N\otimes T_N. Set LN=(TN⊗TN)−1UN:𝒜N→Wdext.(12) L_N=(T_N\otimes T_N)^{-1}U_N: \mathcal A_N\longrightarrow W_d^{\mathrm{ ext}}. \qquad\text{(12)} For every polynomial ff, LN−1f(Ωext)|WdextLN=mf,LN−1R±ext|WdextLN=M∓1,(13) L_N^{-1} f(\Omega_{\mathrm{ ext}})|_{W_d^{\mathrm{ ext}}} L_N=m_f, \qquad L_N^{-1} R^{\mathrm{ ext}}_\pm|_{W_d^{\mathrm{ ext}}} L_N=M^{\mp1}, \qquad\text{(13)} where mfm_f is multiplication by ff on the full quotient and MM is our specified positive Newton crossing matrix. The first identity follows immediately from UN(fg)=f(A)UNgU_N(fg)=f(A)U_Ng; the second uses (8). All integer crossing powers follow.

The restriction in (13) is faithful on the pair commutant, because its elements are f(Ωext)f(\Omega_{\mathrm{ ext}}), and the minimal polynomial on this slice is PNP_N. No arbitrary physical matrix is being reconstructed from its middle-degree restriction. Likewise (10) represents the quotient polynomial; it does not reconstruct an unreduced higher-degree free-boundary polynomial from residue data. The diagonal moduli in (11) do not establish a bound on the condition number of UNU_N.

4. Both adjacent generators on the complete multiplicity module

For overlapping crossings the correct space is the complete NN-dimensional Hom space from the complete source multiplicity construction, not an independently projected pair at each step. Its explicit map w↦Iww\mapsto I_w identifies WdW_d with Hom⁡Hopf,H(V,V⊗3)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{ Hopf,H}}(V,V^{\otimes3}). Transport that whole construction by defining Iwext=TN−⊗3IwTN.(14) I^{\mathrm{ ext}}_w=T_N^{-\otimes3} I_w T_N. \qquad\text{(14)} This is an isomorphism onto the complete Hom space for the transported Hopf action. The embedded physical space has dimension N2N^2, exactly as in the cited construction; (14) does not reduce it to one copy of VV. All intermediate pair Jordan channels remain.

In the original multiplicity coordinates the two physical positive generators are κrN(A)\kappa r_N(A) and κrN(B)\kappa r_N(B), with B=J−1AJB=J^{-1}AJ and the explicit complete recoupling matrix JJ from that construction. Equation (3) consequently makes the external positive generators on (14) their literal inverses, simultaneously. The same argument applies to the complete higher-width invariant spaces in that note. It does not assert that adjacent Casimirs commute.

Transporting the specified physical cups, caps and marked observation is necessary to complete a tangle comparison. The local adapter (2), the cyclic map (12), and the complete multiplicity transport (14) provide exact coordinates for that task. They supply no noncancellation or geometric coefficient estimate for arbitrary short fillings.
