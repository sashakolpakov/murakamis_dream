Volume equality through Conway tangle composition

31 October 2026. Current research formulation, following the user’s explicit choice of Conway algebraic/arborescent knots. Bridge number is no longer the organizing parameter. The active objective remains a general equality for the original invariant with the ACTUAL hyperbolic volume; the full arborescent target below is open.

Every strict Conway composition tree

The strict-tree theorem now covers every fixed literal C(FG)C(FG) that passes the finite strict type test: leaves have type L, turns exchange L/H and preserve A; the nonempty products are LL/LA/AA→A and HL/HA→H, and the root pair is AA, AL, AH or LH. Equivalently, each reduced product hub has at most one flagged original leaf. Mixtures of flagged and unflagged hubs are included.

For each such presentation there is a finite B(τ)B(\tau). Every fixed tuple of independently signed integer weights with |ni|>B(τ)|n_i|>B(\tau) at every leaf, and loop-free one-component pairing, gives the original invariant’s hyperbolic knot complement volume limit through all levels. The presentation and twists are fixed before the quantum limit. The auxiliary crossing circles are filled; the main knot cusp stays unfilled.

The new positive probe bound controls each maximal unflagged component at its actual intrinsic boundary action. The spectral propagation theorem transports every physical-color loss through that component and restores angular localization. The geometric adapter proves that these boundary actions sum to the original complete action. The exact complementary-color identity restores the original packet; its primitive marked filling action is the actual knot complement volume.

The pointed quantum A-period corollary also writes this limit through a genuine all-level ADO operator, its finite initial data and bounded quantum-parameter jets. Existence of the operator was already proved from the finite cut-state source.

The remaining general tasks are presentation coverage beyond this strict literal class and identification of the selected classical main-meridian geometric factor with the marked regulator module. No threshold uniform over growing trees or conclusion for arbitrary short weights is asserted. The earlier completed-root TeX manuscript retains its stated theorem. Older scope discussions below record the steps that preceded this extension; their mixed-source obstruction is now resolved for the strict class.

1. The target is a composition theorem

An algebraic tangle is obtained from rational tangles by the literal Conway sum/product operations and rotations. Close the resulting four-port tangle by a specified numerator or denominator closure, retaining its endpoint order. When the closure has one component it is a Conway algebraic, or arborescent, knot. This is the sense used here, consistent with Wu’s definition. It is not the plane-curve-singularity meaning of algebraic knot.

Fix a reduced, marked tangle expression tree tau, with a specified continued-fraction presentation of each rational leaf, explicit twist boxes, and a closure. Every twist box retains its disk framing and a half-twist parity epsilon_e; its final exponent is epsilon_e-2p_e. Require the resulting diagram to be a knot and its marked augmentation to satisfy the actual hyperbolic/source hypotheses being used. Those requirements must be verified, not inferred from the word arborescent.

The desired theorem is: for each such fixed admissible template tau, there is T(tau)<infinity such that every independently chosen fixed signed filling vector with |p_e|>T(tau) has

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(Kτ(p))|=Vol⁡(S3\Kτ(p)). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log|K_N(K_\tau(p))| =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K_\tau(p)). \tag{1}

The template, parity pattern and twists are fixed BEFORE the quantum level tends to infinity. Both level parities and composite levels belong to (1). The right side is the knot complement volume after the auxiliary fillings; the knot cusp itself remains unfilled. No threshold depending only on tree depth, valence or bridge number is asserted.

The useful structural task is to prove that the required source, boundary-period and signed-estimate properties survive the actual tangle operations. Arbitrarily large coefficients in a fixed rational tangle do not by themselves prove such closure.

2. The baseline grammar and its subsequent enlargements

The existing internally reviewed Conway results already give a constructive subclass with unbounded branching and turn depth. After ALL auxiliary circles are filled, write

h(n)=ℛ(Rn),n=ϵ−2p,As=h(n1)⋯h(ns),s≥3. h(n)=\mathcal R(R^n),\qquad n=\epsilon-2p,\qquad A_s=h(n_1)\cdots h(n_s),\quad s\ge3. \tag{2}

Here R^n means the literal integral two-strand twist tangle, and mathcal R is the actual quarter-turn with the recorded oriented cups and caps. Each h(n) is rational. Generate expressions by

E::=As(s≥3)|ℛ(E)|E1E2. E ::= A_s\ (s\ge3)\quad|\quad\mathcal R(E) \quad|\quad E_1E_2. \tag{3}

The retained closure class is C(FG), with both F and G in (3), plus the isolated C(A_s) for s>=4. The isolated C(A_3) is outside the mixed-color long-filling theorem. Each expression and every s are fixed but otherwise unbounded.

The signed long-filling theorem and its arbitrary-width extension state (1) for this literal class, at independently sufficiently long signed fillings, whenever its parity pattern gives a knot. Their connectivity construction supplies such a pattern for EVERY allowed expression. It is a choice of endpoint pairings, not an assertion that every sign/parity pattern has one component.

Here is the exact membership argument. Filling an original auxiliary circle replaces its box by R^(epsilon-2p), with its original disk framing. All auxiliary components disappear when all are filled. Its turned leaf is the rational tangle h(n). Actual stacking and quarter-turns are Conway operations, so structural induction on (3) gives an algebraic tangle. Its literal cylindrical closure is a Conway closure; a loop-free one-component endpoint pattern makes it an arborescent knot. No addition of numerical fractions is used to claim that a sum of rational tangles is rational. With partial fillings, auxiliary components remain and this all-filled membership statement is not the asserted description of the whole link.

This membership audit does not reprove every analytic dependency of the existing long-filling theorem. It locates its exact topological scope. In particular there is NO coverage theorem identifying every algebraic tangle with grammar (3). The seed grouping, closure choices, parity test and long-filling conditions remain substantive. A continued- fraction chain cannot be replaced by a three-leaf seed merely because some numerical tangle fractions coincide.

The new two-leaf theorem extends the retained C(FG) class to seeds of every width s>=2, provided the actual selected complete port solution satisfies

The earlier algebraic leaf test remains sufficient but is no longer required for this grammar.

3. What the algebraic boundary object must retain

For composition, a polynomial relation alone is incomplete boundary data. Retain the original boundary quantum state, an algebraic boundary correspondence, the selected logarithmic lifts, and an absolutely normalized pressure/action primitive. For a geometric CROSS disk module with boundary weights L_b, the currently proved version is

qb=∂F∂log⁡Lb,Bb=e2πiqb,dF=∑bqbdlog⁡Lb. q_b=\frac{\partial F}{\partial\log L_b},\qquad B_b=e^{2\pi i q_b},\qquad dF=\sum_b q_b\,d\log L_b. \tag{4}

The selected phases B_b are algebraic in the boundary weights. The integer lifts q_b and the additive constant of F are extra data. The algebraic boundary-curve theorem constructs these explicitly for the original one-cap exterior and reconstructs its quantum root density from the same branch. Restoring the original complete cap gives a unique algebraic point and an absolutely normalized period equal to the ORIGINAL drilled hyperbolic volume divided by 2pi. This is an actual geometric calibration, not a claim that an arbitrary eliminated curve carries that volume.

For admissible gluing of whole original port disks, internal shared faces s impose the lifted condition

q1,s+q2,s=1,B1,sB2,s=1, q_{1,s}+q_{2,s}=1,\qquad B_{1,s}B_{2,s}=1, \tag{5}

and the positive primitive composes by

F12=inflog⁡LS(F1+F2−∑s∈SlogLs). F_{12}=\inf_{\log L_S} \left(F_1+F_2-\sum_{s\in S}\log L_s\right). \tag{6}

At finite level the same operation is coefficient extraction at the original face demands:

P12,N=[∏s∈SLsN−1]P1,NP2,N. P_{12,N}=\left[\prod_{s\in S}L_s^{N-1}\right]P_{1,N}P_{2,N}. \tag{7}

Shared external variables are identified, and every old internal extraction and original coupon factor is retained. The boundary composition theorem proves these laws and their associativity under its explicit whole-port, anchoring and simplicity conditions. It does not identify arbitrary Conway operations with those cuts automatically. The actual signed four-port tangle tensor carries additional information beyond this positive CROSS coefficient module.

A four-punctured Conway seam has boundary shape data. Its geometric volume is obtained after matching that data and solving the shared critical equations. One cannot replace (6) by a sum of independently maximized piece volumes. Neither this source-boundary correspondence nor its one-variable eliminant is called the ordinary knot A-polynomial without the missing meridian/longitude dictionary.

4. The remaining closure theorem

The general extension beyond these certified grammars must establish, for each allowed rational-tangle building block and composition:

  • The literal original quantum boundary contraction, including turns, full repeated-root information, framing and the final knot closure.
  • The selected algebraic boundary branch and primitive with correct integer lifts and all shared shape variables.
  • A signed estimate that survives that contraction, at both level parities, followed by the original full filling-period argument.

The current two-cap calculation demonstrates why the last requirement is substantive: pair-total extraction can destroy joint stability, and completing two cap sums creates mixed tails as well as a double tail. The joint response theorem retains those terms and gives a finite sufficient comparison. The even-level obstruction shows that the actual local CROSS polynomial already fails real stability at N=6; a positive comparison does not transfer its signed negative-axis rate automatically. These findings concern the general source extension and do not revoke the existing robust Conway proof, which uses a different exact full-Newton estimate.

Thus the immediate target is a tangle-gluing theorem on the original boundary data, capable of enlarging the admissible seed and rational- edge class. The older general prescribed-plat question remains an open presentation of the broader problem; bridge number is not used to organize the present argument. The research goal remains ACTIVE.
