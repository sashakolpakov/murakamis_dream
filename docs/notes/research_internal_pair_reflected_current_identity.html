Reflected middle currents and their original moving-wall residues

Research proof, updated 4 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

Reflecting both negative factorial arguments reorganizes the exact middle integrand into terms with fewer wire or trace poles. The reorganization is legitimate, but its pieces acquire moving poles when extended out of that chamber. Their residues are nonzero and recover the original value-support rows. Thus the pieces without a wire denominator cannot simply be discarded by a wire Cauchy argument.

The later sections estimate the original integrand without discarding those terms. Sections 13–14 give coupled angular contours and the right vertical wire side for every fixed positive pair of powers at least seven. Together with the left contour and endpoint estimates, these supply the full two-boundary source theorem.
