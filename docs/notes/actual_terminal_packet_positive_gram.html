A terminal coherent packet gives a positive Gram on the complete original module

29 October 2026. A sharp packet norm need not be diagonal in complete Casimir channels, and its defining packet map need not be invertible. This note constructs a positive Gram on the SAME complete source module used by the original braid representation. It comes from a specified two-crossing terminal tangle, independently of the preceding word. For the original figure-eight prefix its energy has the sharp volume exponent. The construction extends as a source-exact Cauchy construction to arbitrary plat width; a sharp bound on its energy for general prefixes is NOT proved.

1. A positive completion of a coherent packet seminorm

Let H be a finite-dimensional state space with fixed Euclidean norm. Suppose a prescribed boundary row factors as a=1^T T, where T:H->C^s is an EXPLICIT linear packet map. For any epsilon>0 set

G_epsilon=T^*T+epsilon I.                              (1)

This is positive definite even if T has a kernel. Its boundary cost is

a G_epsilon^(-1) a^*<=s.                              (2)

Indeed T(T^T+epsilon I)(-1)T has eigenvalues sigma2/(sigma2+epsilon)<=1, by singular-value decomposition. Apply it to the vector of s ones. Thus for every input w,

|a w|^2<=s [sum_n |T_n w|^2+epsilon ||w||^2].          (3)

The seminorm inequality without epsilon is also exact, by ordinary Cauchy in C^s. Equation (1) makes it a genuine positive inner product on the full original state space; it neither discards a channel nor asserts that the kernel of T is invariant under preceding braids.

Use epsilon_N=exp(-N^2). If ||w_N||^2<=exp(C N log N) for a fixed word, its added energy is superexponentially small. No unknown knot value is used to choose epsilon. This completion is useful only when T is specified from a limited terminal packet and its coherent energy is independently estimable. Taking T to be the unknown entire closed period would give no new bound.

2. The complete original module and a specified terminal packet

Use N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d, F_a=(Q;Q)a. All original Ecal,F,K, coproducts, full Cartan factors and sources are those in the complete multiplicity theorem. Let W=(V tensor V)(degree d), and write I_w:V->V^tensor3 for its complete intertwiner. Its lowest input column is the explicit map

D w=I_w|d>=F_123^d H_L(w)/F_d,
H_L(w)=sum_c (-xQ)^c E_12^c w/F_c tensor |c>.          (4)

This retains all N multiplicities and their full intermediate Jordan channels. The ordinary cap is the original row ell and the cup is p. Define the physical final row and terminal operator by

c_top=ell_12 tensor <d|,
U_phys=I tensor R_+^2,
U=R_23^2 on W.                                      (5)

R_+ is the literal original checked crossing INCLUDING its common Cartan factor. Let Pi_n project the middle physical head to color n, with the other two heads untouched. Define the N-row packet

T_n(w)=c_top U_phys Pi_n D w, 0<=n<N.                 (6)

Every object in (4)–(6) is fixed before a preceding braid is supplied. In particular this does not use a closed knot value to choose a basis. Summing the physical resolution of identity gives exactly

sum_n T_n=ell U.                                    (7)

To verify the last equality, the complete intertwiner theorem gives U_phys I_w=I_(Uw); closing its first two heads gives (ell tensor I)I_(Uw)=ell(Uw) I_V. Its input/output d entry is (7). This uses the actual whole source and its Hopf closure, not a replacement of an arbitrary marked row by an ordinary cap.

For ANY original prefix B the unchanged closed scalar is therefore

K_N=chi_N ell U Bp=chi_N sum_n T_n(Bp).                (8)

All original external factors remain in chi_N. The specified full marked closure is justified by the complete invariant-tensor theorem; arbitrary nonintertwining inserted exteriors retain their own source.

Equations (1)–(3), with s=N, give a positive metric on the prefix cut. One can put it on the final complete module as well:

G_final=U^(-*) (T^*T+epsilon_N I) U^(-1).             (9)

It obeys ell G_final(-1)ell*<=N. For the actual final state v=chi_N U Bp its energy is exactly

v^*G_final v=|chi_N|^2
               [sum_n |T_n(Bp)|^2+epsilon_N||Bp||^2]. (10)

Thus this is a full-rank positive Gram on the SAME complete module as the Casimir metric. It does not identify two differently contracted scalar formulas without an actual linear map. No claim of invertibility of T is needed, at prime, even, or composite levels.

3. The actual figure-eight prefix has the sharp packet energy

Take the literal prefix B=R_12^(-1)R_23. With the terminal U of (5), the full action order is [2,-1,2,2], the original zero-framed four-plat in the whole-source proof. Its finite forced-transfer calculation can be kept open at the middle physical color n AFTER the second crossing. The initial lowest column I_p|d> is p tensor |d>. After the first two crossings the physical state is (d-k,a+k,d-a), so that n=a+k is precisely the projector index in (6), not a new auxiliary label. The final two crossings and the actual caps force their transfers to a and 0, respectively.

Consequently the proof there, retaining its full Cartan and boundary factors, gives the ROW-BY-ROW identity

T_n(Bp)=sum_(k=0)^n [n choose k]_Q
                        (Q;Q)_k (Q^(-n);Q)_(n-k)
        =(-1)^n Q^[-n(n+1)/2] F_n^2
        =|F_n|^2.                                 (11)

For the middle equality, reverse the last Pochhammer product and use the finite q-binomial identity; all factorial indices are below N. This is an exact statement about the uncontracted terminal packet on the complete module, not just equality after summing n. The original zero-framing normalization has chi_N=1 for this benchmark.

Therefore (10) is

E_final=sum_(n<N)|F_n|^4+exp(-N^2)||R_12^(-1)R_23p||^2.

The uniform sine-factorial estimate proved in the packet note gives

log(sum_(n<N)|F_n|^4)=N Vol(4_1)/pi+O(log N).          (12)

The last term is superexponentially small by Section 4 below. Thus this ACTUAL complete-module positive metric has the sharp energy rate. It mixes distinct Casimir channels; it is not a positive diagonal reweighting of their separate squared amplitudes.

The estimate is reusable for every prefix with this fixed terminal tangle, but equation (11) is special to the displayed prefix. For another knot the full coherent quantities T_n(Bp) must be bounded; replacing them by sums of absolute values is not authorized.

4. The regularizer is negligible for fixed original braid words

At the principal root, for 1<=j<N,

4/N<=|1-Q^j|<=2.

Every original balanced signed crossing coefficient is a monomial of modulus one times a bounded number of factorial/Pochhammer ratios, each of length at most N-1. The explicit signed formulas therefore bound its logarithmic magnitude above by C N log N. A physical crossing has at most polynomially many entries at fixed braid width, so the same order bound holds for its operator norm.

For a fixed prefix acting on the odd heads, the highest vector of the initial source is p tensor |0> (and p^tensor r tensor |0> at larger width), of polynomial norm. Apply its fixed number of physical signed crossings and extract the last color0 coefficient. This extraction IS the complete multiplicity inverse and has norm one. For an arbitrary EVEN-plat prefix, including crossings of the last marked partner, use the original invariant bottom p^tensor(r+1) instead. Its complete multiplicity inverse is (x^d/N) times extraction of the last TWO colors (0,d), as proved in the invariant-tensor source theorem. Both the initial norm and extraction cost are polynomial. These arguments prove

||B p||^2<=exp(C_B N log N),                         (13)

and the same statement for the full all-width initial source. No ill-conditioned eigenframe or unestimated reduced inverse enters this proof. Equation (13) is a coarse original-source bound used only to control the regularizer; it is not offered as a volume estimate.

5. All-width extension and the remaining sharp theorem

For 2k heads, r=k-1, let W_r and I_w:V->V^tensor(2r+1) be the complete original multiplicities. Put D_r w=I_w|d>, c_top=ell^tensor r tensor <d| and lambda=ell^tensor r. For a specified terminal braid U_phys on these odd heads and ANY specified mutually orthogonal physical-color projectors Pi_a summing to identity, set

T_a=c_top U_phys Pi_a D_r.

Exactly the same complete intertwiner calculation proves sum_a T_a=lambda U_red. Choosing a fixed number s of physical heads to record gives N^s packets and a dual norm at most N^s in (1). The literal last marked cap and all original normalizations are retained by the all-width invariant-tensor source theorem. This construction is valid for every preceding signed braid and at all N.

The unresolved geometric problem is now the coherent estimate

|chi_N|^2 sum_a |T_a(B w_0)|^2
                     <=exp(o(N)) Z_N(geometric angles). (14)

This note proves the sharp energy rate (12) for the original figure-eight benchmark and provides a genuine complete-module realization of its sharp Gram. Comparison (14) then follows for any of the already proved positive geometric partitions with that same volume rate. It does not prove (14) for a broad knot class, or prove broad geometric survival. The new structural point is that a controlled physical terminal contraction can define the metric; an invertible global spectral frame is not required to do so.

Audit constructs the entire highest/lowest intertwiner, original signed crossings and the terminal physical projector rows independently of (11). It checks all rows, every input column of (7), and the positive-completion inequality on unrelated state vectors.

The finite tests compare complete terminal modules, figure-eight packet families and positive full-module Grams. The proof keeps the full common Cartan scalar and uses the full even-plat inverse in its regularizer.
