An exact full-grid lens for the original charged source

Private written proof, 15 November 2026.

The full original Hermite trace can be represented by a lower angular arc whose endpoints are the real grid midpoints −π,π-\pi,\pi. There is then no separate vertical term and no discarded physical row. This gives an exact family of joint source contours through any supplied path in the lower canonical strip. It does not show that the geometric contribution of those contours is nonzero, or bound their remaining parts at the geometric rate.

3. Spectral branches, half crossings and all aliases

The endpoints map to z=2z=2, where the canonical function arccosh⁡(−z/2)2\operatorname{arccosh}(-z/2)^2 has a branch. This causes no endpoint pole in the proof: (5)–(11) are angular identities of entire functions and the explicit grid kernel. Moreover PN(2)=4P_N(2)=4, so z=2z=2 is not an original spectral pole. No contour is moved across the spectral branch cut in this argument.

The connection with the adopted even split is exact. Write n=m+ϵn=m+\epsilon, with mm even and ϵ∈{0,±1}\epsilon\in\{0,\pm1\}, and retain the physical factor κn\kappa^n, where κ=qD2/2\kappa=q^{D^2/2}. Here ℏ=2πi/N,Fj(z)=q−jD2/4exp⁡(j𝔎(z)2ℏ),𝔎(z)=arccosh⁡(−z/2)2 \hbar=2\pi i/N,\qquad F_j(z)=q^{-jD^2/4} \exp\!\left(\frac{j\,\mathfrak K(z)}{2\hbar}\right), \qquad \mathfrak K(z)=\operatorname{arccosh}(-z/2)^2 uses the canonical branch on ℂ\[2,∞)\mathbb C\setminus[2,\infty), with its removable value at −2-2. For continuous χ\chi, the coupon representative cos⁡(Lt)\cos(Lt) may have different values on lifts differing by 2π2\pi. 4. Joint original sources and an explicit continuation candidate

For a fixed original circle tree, let SS be the traced vertices. Keep the original full turn kernels 𝒦N(tv,tw)=∑j=0Dej(−2cos⁡tv)ej(−2cos⁡tw)\mathcal K_N(t_v,t_w)=\sum_{j=0}^D e_j(-2\cos t_v)e_j(-2\cos t_w), all ordinary outside polynomials, and all original integer powers. Applying (8) at each selected vertex gives exactly Z𝝌,N=∏v∈S(−N2CN,nvπi)∫∏v∈SΓv∏e𝒦N(tv,tw)⋅∏v∈S[sintveinvNtv2/(4π)WN(tv)cos(N(1−χv)tv)dtv].(14) \begin{split} Z_{\boldsymbol\chi,N} ={}&\prod_{v\in S}\left(-\frac{N^2C_{N,n_v}}{\pi i}\right) \int_{\prod_{v\in S}\Gamma_v} \prod_e\mathcal K_N(t_v,t_w)\\ &\qquad\cdot \prod_{v\in S}\left[ \sin t_v\, e^{in_vNt_v^2/(4\pi)} W_N(t_v)\cos(N(1-\chi_v)t_v)\,dt_v \right]. \end{split} \qquad\text{(14)} The identical statement applies to compiled identity or turn kernels and further polynomial spectators, with those actual factors left in place. Fubini is legitimate for these finite compact integrals. The identity is coefficientwise in the untraced ordinary variables, so subsequent boundary derivatives retain the original normals.

Compact joint deformations are also exact when their interiors stay in the product lower domain and each boundary face stays in its original hyperplane tv=±πt_v=\pm\pi. The holomorphic top form is closed. On the side of such a deformation belonging to that boundary face, the pullback of dtvdt_v is zero. Stokes’ theorem therefore adds no face integral. This statement uses the full integrand in (14), not a selected exponential summand.

Equation (14) holds on them for every parameter and every NN. For a piecewise smooth supplied path they form a piecewise smooth fixed-end isotopy. No existence of the path (15) is proved here.

The small-charge theorem supplies a nonzero initial asymptotic for the same original source with complete outside angles, arbitrary fixed signed powers, and the stated same-index graph kernels. Its proof also applies to the real continuous coupons (3) on its small charge box: on physical real angles the coupon has modulus at most one and first angular derivative at most NN, and its exact reflected contour formula requires evenness, not periodicity. All its strict estimates are unchanged. Formula (14) represents that initialized source and its exact continuation to unit charge.

This removes neither the analytic continuation problem for the geometric projection nor the need for a whole-source estimate. The full edge kernels have not been replaced by their geometric packets. A local descending classical collar can be inserted only as an exact chain deformation with its remaining connecting chain retained. This note gives no bound on that chain, no source-compatible projection onto the local geometric block, and no noncancellation conclusion. The split-current action gap applies when the actual remaining operation is the specified Ward deletion; a general part of the lens integrand has not been identified with such a deletion.
