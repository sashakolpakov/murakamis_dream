The complete value from a fixed number of nearby ordinary-color jets

Private written proof, 23 November 2026.

A single final row of an actual color recurrence reconstructs the original complete value from quantum derivatives of only dd colors strictly below NN. The number of derivatives is fixed by the operator, and the observation row has polynomial norm. No full color orbit, cyclotomic candidate or arithmetic coefficient hypothesis is needed for this local observation.

The input jets can have unknown exponential size. This is a reduction of the actual data and their observation cost, not a geometric lower bound or a new short-filling volume theorem.

1. Original source and the terminal divisor

Use the actual QQ-only reduced color functions 𝒞r(Q)\mathcal C_r(Q) and a specified all-level recurrence of [M]: P=∑j=0daj(Q,Y)Tj,TY=QYT,d≥1, P=\sum_{j=0}^{d}a_j(Q,Y)T^j,\qquad TY=QYT,\qquad d\ge1, ∑j=0daj(Q,Qm)𝒞m+j(Q)=0,m≥1,ad≠0.(1) \sum_{j=0}^{d}a_j(Q,Q^m)\mathcal C_{m+j}(Q)=0, \qquad m\ge1,\qquad a_d\ne0. \qquad\text{(1)} The fixed coefficients are Laurent polynomials after one recorded denominator clearing. Complex coefficients are allowed. The actual reduced, zero-framed closing is QN=e2πi/N,KN=𝒞N(QN).(2) Q_N=e^{2\pi i/N},\qquad K_N=\mathcal C_N(Q_N). \qquad\text{(2)} In [O]’s half-root convention qN=eπi/Nq_N=e^{\pi i/N}, the same ordinary root value is 𝒞r(QN)=Cr(qN)\mathcal C_r(Q_N)=C_r(q_N). Every original crossing phase, pivot and framing stays in these color functions; no closed scalar is used to normalize the row.

In the Laurent ring factor the leading coefficient maximally as ad(Q,Y)=(1−QdY)μbd(Q,Y),B(Q)=bd(Q,Q−d).(3) a_d(Q,Y)=(1-Q^dY)^\mu b_d(Q,Y),\qquad B(Q)=b_d(Q,Q^{-d}). \qquad\text{(3)} Here μ≥0\mu\ge0 is finite and B≠0B\ne0. Indeed the substitution Z=QdYZ=Q^dY identifies this Laurent ring with ℂ[Q±1,Z±1]\mathbb C[Q^{\pm1},Z^{\pm1}]; evaluation at Z=1Z=1 has kernel generated by 1−Z1-Z. Maximality proves B≠0B\ne0. In particular μ\mu is at most the YY-exponent span of ada_d. Put v=ord⁡Q=1B(Q)≥0.(4) v=\operatorname{ord}_{Q=1}B(Q)\ge0. \qquad\text{(4)} All these quantities are fixed by the recorded operator.

2. Exact observation and polynomial stability

At fixed N>dN>d vary the quantum parameter by Q=QN(1+ζ/N).(5) Q=Q_N(1+\zeta/N). \qquad\text{(5)} The color dimensions are held fixed during differentiation. They are not fixed-QNQ_N meridian derivatives of the root ADO state.

3. Proof of the exact row and its cost

Use row m=N−dm=N-d in the generic identity (1), before root evaluation. At its leading entry, ad(Q,QN−d)=(1−QN)μbd(Q,QN−d).(10) a_d(Q,Q^{N-d})=(1-Q^N)^\mu b_d(Q,Q^{N-d}). \qquad\text{(10)} Under (5), QNN=1Q_N^N=1 gives 1−QN=1−(1+ζ/N)N=−ζ+O(ζ2).(11) 1-Q^N=1-(1+\zeta/N)^N=-\zeta+O(\zeta^2). \qquad\text{(11)} The coefficient of ζ\zeta is exactly −1-1 at every level. Also QNN−d=QN−dQ_N^{N-d}=Q_N^{-d}, so the last factor in (10) has value exactly B(QN)B(Q_N). For all sufficiently large NN that value is nonzero. The coefficient of ζμ\zeta^\mu in the last term of (1) is therefore (−1)μB(QN)KN.(12) (-1)^\mu B(Q_N)K_N. \qquad\text{(12)} No positive-order jet of the complete color occurs in (12). The other terms give the convolution in (7), including all lower ordinary-color jets. This proves (7) with its displayed sign.

For the quantitative claim, a monomial QaYbQ^aY^b in any fixed aja_j becomes QENQ^{E_N}, where EN=a+b(N−d)=O(N)E_N=a+b(N-d)=O(N). Finite sums prove |Aj,r,N|≤C|A_{j,r,N}|\le C.

Write B(Q)=(Q−1)vB0(Q)B(Q)=(Q-1)^vB_0(Q), B0(1)≠0B_0(1)\ne0. Laurent polynomials are holomorphic at one, so |B(QN)|≥c|QN−1|v≥c′N−v(14) |B(Q_N)|\ge c|Q_N-1|^v\ge c'N^{-v} \qquad\text{(14)} for all sufficiently large NN. Here |QN−1|=2sin⁡(π/N)≥4/N|Q_N-1|=2\sin(\pi/N)\ge4/N. Equations (13)–(14) prove (8), and the triangle inequality proves (9). No companion-product inversion or algebraic-number estimate is used.

The earlier root-normal coordinate is retained if desired: δ=1−QN=1−(1+ζ/N)N,ζ=N[(1−δ)1/N−1].(15) \delta=1-Q^N=1-(1+\zeta/N)^N,\qquad \zeta=N[(1-\delta)^{1/N}-1]. \qquad\text{(15)} Both substitutions have uniformly bounded fixed Taylor coefficients, with linear coefficient −1-1. Their triangular fixed-depth jet changes and inverses are uniformly bounded. The gain in (7) is the selection of a fixed nearby color block, not a coordinate relabeling.

4. What is now isolated, and prior coverage

The ordinary root estimate [O] is uniform throughout 1≤r<N1\le r<N. For a fixed bb-strand knot presentation it gives |𝒞r(QN)|≤sin⁡(π/N)1−b≤(N/2)b−1.(16) |\mathcal C_r(Q_N)|\le\sin(\pi/N)^{1-b} \le(N/2)^{b-1}. \qquad\text{(16)} If μ=0\mu=0, (7)–(8) consequently imply |KN|≤CNv+b−1|K_N|\le C N^{v+b-1}. More generally the ℓ=0\ell=0 part of (7) is polynomially bounded. Any positive exponential size in this row must therefore enter through the finitely many ℓ≥1\ell\ge1 quantum jets. Equation (16) alone does not bound those derivatives.

The stronger necessary boundary-column condition is already proved in [P, Section 2]: positive complete growth forces aj(Q,Q−j)≡0a_j(Q,Q^{-j})\equiv0 for every jj, equivalently the actual Laurent operator has a retained right factor 1−Y1-Y, up to sign. It is prior coverage, not an addition here.

The full ordinary-orbit reconstruction [J] already gives a polynomial row from quantum jets in all noncomplete residue classes, using genuine high colors up to HN−1HN-1. Formula (7) instead uses one recurrence row and only dd colors below NN; its derivative depth depends on the terminal factor of that specified recurrence.

This is different input from the earlier quantitative Mellin theorem. That theorem controls error from an approximate whole source equation, its prefix and pole lattice. Here those hypotheses are replaced by approximations to the actual near-NN jets themselves. Their construction and geometric asymptotics are not supplied. The row is complex and signed: large packet norm does not give a lower bound for its observed sum. No nonzero geometric coefficient or volume theorem follows.
