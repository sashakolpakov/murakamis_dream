Backward source transport and one forward pass solve the Conway minimum

23 November 2026. Written research proof.

The original normalized quartic admits a contracting iteration consisting of one backward source pass and one forced forward pass. Its contraction factor is at most 15/274415/2744, independently of the quantum level and the size of the literal tree. After exact elimination of the closing turn corridor, the factor is at most 5ζ2/C5\zeta^2/C, with the original, explicit endpoint coefficient ζ\zeta and corridor factor CC. Thus that second factor decreases exponentially with the level.

This is an iteration for the same minimum, not an evaluation of its geometric rate. Starting at zero makes its first forward pass the actual open-source evaluation. Full boundary packets, all their Hermite directions, the original closing, and every normalization budget remain.

1. The exact normalized source

Fix N≥2N\ge2. Use the literal normalized Conway tree of [C], with one packet in the complete Newton space HN≅ℂNH_N\cong\mathbb C^N at each proper vertex. Put σ=1/16,𝒳=⨁vHN,𝒟=∏v{xv:∥xv∥≤1}.(1) \sigma=1/16,\qquad \mathcal X=\bigoplus_v H_N,\qquad \mathcal D=\prod_v\{x_v:\|x_v\|\le1\}. \qquad\text{(1)} Each normalized leaf, turn, and bilinear product Φv\Phi_v has norm at most σ\sigma. The original closing row is cc, supported at the top packet, with ∥c∥≤σ\|c\|\le\sigma. Let G(x)=Φ(x)−x,Ax=DΦ(x),Sx=I−Ax,E(x)=∥G(x)∥2+|cx|2.(2) G(x)=\Phi(x)-x,\quad A_x=D\Phi(x),\quad S_x=I-A_x, \qquad E(x)=\|G(x)\|^2+|cx|^2. \qquad\text{(2)} The positive budget BtotB_{\mathrm{ tot}}, all original phases, and the marking are exactly those in [C]. If e*=min⁡𝒟Ee_* =\min_{\mathcal D}E, then Btot2e*≤|KN|2≤197196Btot2e*.(3) B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2e_*\le |K_N|^2 \le\frac{197}{196}B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2e_*. \qquad\text{(3)} The unique minimizer is interior, and I≤D2E≤3II\le D^2E\le3I. All gradients are real gradients for Re⁡(u*v)\operatorname{Re}(u^*v); the local maps themselves are complex multilinear.

We also use the following direct consequences of the literal tree: ∥Ax∥≤1/8,∥Sx−1∥≤ρ:=8/7,∥Ax−Ay∥≤σ∥x−y∥.(4) \|A_x\|\le1/8,\quad \|S_x^{-1}\|\le\rho:=8/7, \qquad \|A_x-A_y\|\le\sigma\|x-y\|. \qquad\text{(4)} The norm is the Hilbert direct-sum norm. For the last assertion, only products have a second derivative. At one product it is Φv(hL,kR)+Φv(kL,hR)\Phi_v(h_L,k_R)+\Phi_v(k_L,h_R). The two child slots of distinct products are disjoint. Cauchy–Schwarz first on a sibling pair and then over those pairs gives ∥D2Φ(x)[h,k]∥≤σ∥h∥∥k∥\|D^2\Phi(x)[h,k]\|\le\sigma\|h\|\|k\|. Integration along the feasible segment proves (4). Its first two bounds are also proved in [C].

2. Exact inversion of the residual by a forward pass

Given a forcing r=(rv)vr=(r_v)_v, define Y(r)Y(r) from leaves to root by Y(r)v=Φv((Y(r)j)j child of v)−rv.(5) Y(r)_v=\Phi_v((Y(r)_j)_{j\text{ child of }v})-r_v. \qquad\text{(5)} This finite recursion is the unique solution of G(Y(r))=rG(Y(r))=r. 4. The exactly eliminated closing corridor

Suppose the literal tree has a highest binary product. Eliminate only its output and the turns above it exactly as in [F]. The remaining variables are all packets strictly below that product, including its two input packets. Their residual still has the form G=Φ−xG=\Phi-x, on a forest, with the same bounds (4). The reduced objective is exactly F(x)=∥G(x)∥2+|g(x)|2,g(x)=xL𝗍BxR/C,∥B∥=ζ,t:=ζ/C.(16) F(x)=\|G(x)\|^2+|g(x)|^2, \qquad g(x)=x_L^{\mathsf t}B x_R/\sqrt C, \qquad \|B\|=\zeta,\quad t:=\zeta/\sqrt C. \qquad\text{(16)} Here C=1+νcorrC=1+\nu_{\mathrm{ corr}} is the explicit inclusive corridor factor of [F]. In particular C≥1C\ge1, ζ≤1/256\zeta\le1/256, and [F] proves that the minimum in (16) is the original full minimum e*e_*.

For a fixed literal tree with a product, [F] also gives ζ≤exp⁡{−2GCatN/π+O(log⁡N)}.(22) \zeta\le\exp\{-2G_{\mathrm{ Cat}}N/\pi+O(\log N)\}. \qquad\text{(22)} Thus the contraction bound in (21) is at most exp⁡{−4GCatN/π+O(log⁡N)}\exp\{-4G_{\mathrm{ Cat}}N/\pi+O(\log N)\}. 5. What the passes do and do not remove

Each iteration uses the actual local derivatives in one backward pass and the actual local gates in one forward pass. Their number is linear in the number of literal vertices. The packet dimension remains NN; no bound independent of NN is asserted for tensor arithmetic, storage, bit complexity, or the precision needed to resolve a small scalar. The causal compositions can have higher degree than four. The objective being minimized, rather than this iteration map, is the original quartic.

For any of these contractions with factor q<1q<1, ∥xj−x*∥≤qj∥x0−x*∥,∥xj−x*∥≤∥xj+1−xj∥1−q.(23) \|x_j-x_*\|\le q^j\|x_0-x_*\|, \qquad \|x_j-x_*\|\le\frac{\|x_{j+1}-x_j\|}{1-q}. \qquad\text{(23)} These are absolute field bounds. In particular they do not supply relative precision for an exponentially small minimum or a nonzero geometric coefficient. At x=0x=0, the frozen scalar forcing is zero for both full objectives. Thus 𝒯(0)=a\mathcal T(0)=a, and 𝒯F(0)=abelow\mathcal T_F(0)=a_{\mathrm{ below}}: the first forward pass is precisely the true open recursion. No difficulty in the coherent closing is removed by naming it a field iteration.

There are nevertheless direct value certificates. The eliminated objective has D2F≤3ID^2F\le3I: in the Schur formula of [F], test the eliminated variation by zero and use [C]’s upper Hessian bound. Consequently, for q=5t2q=5t^2, F(u)−3∥𝒯F(u)−u∥22(1−q)2≤e*≤F(u).(24) F(u)-\frac{3\|\mathcal T_F(u)-u\|^2}{2(1-q)^2} \le e_*\le F(u). \qquad\text{(24)} This follows from (23) and Taylor’s theorem at the minimizer.
