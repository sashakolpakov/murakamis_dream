An ordinary pivotal cap coherently collapses reassociation to one angle weight

29 October 2026. Contracting the original ordinary pivotal cap with the actual three-head reassociation packet annihilates every middle input except the top index d=N-1. The surviving map is a phase times weighted truncated addition. In the source-fixing quarter-factorial metric its squared coefficient is exactly ONE positive affine-angle weight. All intermediate terms have been summed coherently first.

The inverse-packet/cup move has an equally explicit bottom-index selection and the same one-weight energy. These are exact local operator/source identities for arbitrary exteriors, not merely scalar normalizations. The marked cap is different: its complete retained packet is given below and is not replaced by the ordinary cap.

1. Actual packet, source and metric conventions

Put q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, d=N-1, x=-q-1=qd, N>=2, F_a=(Q;Q)_a and s_a=|F_a|. Keep the original finite transfers

C'_12|a,b>=sum_r x^-r [b+r choose r]_Q |a-r,b+r>,
(C'_12)^-1|a,b>
   =sum_r (-1)^r Q^[r(r-1)/2]x^-r
                     [b+r choose r]_Q |a-r,b+r>,        (1)
0<=r<=min(a,d-b).

These are the original nilpotent Euler matrices and their full inverse, including strict head endpoints. The actual packet is

A=(C'_23)^-1 C'_12.                                    (2)

Its full entry, derived by its UNIQUE intermediate state, is

|a,b,c> -> |a-r,b+r-s,c+s>,
A=(-1)^s Q^[s(s-1)/2]x^[-(r+s)]
                 [b+r choose r]_Q[c+s choose s]_Q,      (3)
r<=min(a,d-b), s<=min(b+r,d-c), r,s>=0.

The normalized original ordinary cap and cup are

ellhat= sqrt(N) ell,
ellhat|u,v>=(xQ)^-u/sqrt(N) delta_(u+v,d),
phat=p/N^(3/2),
phat=sum_(u=0)^d x^(u-d)/sqrt(N) |u,d-u>.              (4)

Indeed the literal original turns are ell|u,v>=x^v g_u/N delta_(u+v,d), p_(u,v)=N x^-v delta, g_u=x^-d Q^-u. Thus(4) retains the original pivot, not a cap with a favorable substituted phase. The normalized cap and cup both have norm1. Literal ell and p are restored by their stated N powers; all external Cartan/framing/peripheral lines are unchanged.

Use the PHYSICAL gauge and squared metric

B_a=L_a^-1/4, h_a=B_a^2=sqrt(N)/s_a,
L_a=s_a^2/N.                                          (5)

Root reflection gives B_a B_(d-a)=1. These original turns are fixed by B on paired legs. Hats on maps below mean conjugation by B on their physical input/output legs. This is not the length normalization b_l=B_l/D_l and is not the different tame-current metric L^(1/2).

2. Exact cap selection, including the coherent root sum

Define K=(ellhat_12 tensor I_3) A. For every physical input,

K|a,b,c>=0 unless b=d and a+c<=d.                     (6)

In the surviving case n=a+c,

K|a,d,c>=N^-1/2 (-1)^a Q^[a(a-1)/2-a]x^(-2a)
                                  [n choose a]_Q |n>. (7)

Proof. The cap condition in(3) fixes s=a+b-d. If this is negative, or s>d-c, the contraction is zero. Otherwise its allowed r range is exactly0,…,d-b. The ordinary pivotal cap phase supplies Q^r, and all the remaining factors are independent of r. Its complete coherent sum is

sum_(r=0)^(d-b) Q^r[b+r choose r]_Q
                              =[N choose b+1]_Q.       (8)

For completeness, q-Pascal gives [j+1 choose b+1]-[j choose b+1]=Q^(j-b)[j choose b]. Sum from j=b to d. At the primitive root the resulting Gaussian polynomial is zero for b<d and one for b=d. Its intermediate factorials have no zero denominator; [N choose N]=1 is its literal polynomial value. This works at every even and composite level as well.

Thus b=d, r=0, s=a, n=c+a, with the strict condition n<=d. Substitution of the original cap coefficient gives precisely(7). This is cancellation of the full finite packet, not one selected term or an assumed homogeneous-source quotient.

3. The doubled packet has exactly one positive angle weight

Define w_d(i,j,k)=1/(s_i s_j s_k), i+j+k=d. Applying(5) to(7) gives the exact entry

Khat_(n;(a,b,c))
   =delta_(b,d)delta_(n,a+c<=d)
       q^[a(n-N-1)] sqrt(w_d(a,c,d-n)).                 (9)

Indeed the squared gauge ratio is h_n/(h_a h_d h_c)=s_a s_c/s_n. Consequently

|Khat_(n;(a,d,c))|^2
  =(1/N)|[a+c choose a]_Q|=w_d(a,c,d-a-c).              (10)

For the LITERAL original cap ell the right side is w_d/N, because ell=ellhat/sqrt(N). No original scalar line was discarded.

The complete doubled input Gram retains the mixed copies:

(Khat^*Khat)_(a,d,c ; a',d,c')
  =delta_(a+c,a'+c')
     q^[a'(a'+c'-N-1)-a(a+c-N-1)]
     sqrt(w_d(a,c,d-a-c)w_d(a',c',d-a'-c')).            (11)

All other middle-input rows/columns are zero. The positive trace on the one remaining output has supplied the UNWRAPPED constraint n=n’. It admits either exact N or2N Fourier character expression, since n,n’ lie in0,…,d. This does not identify n=n’ with a global triangulation edge sum2d. The non-diagonal terms of(11) have not been dephased.

Rows of Khat have disjoint total-input-degree support, so

Khat Khat^*=diag(R_n),
R_n=sum_(a=0)^n w_d(a,n-a,d-n),
||Khat||_op^2=max_n R_n,
||Khat||_HS^2=sum_n R_n=Z_(3,N).                        (12)

Here Z_(3,N) is the positive affine angle-simplex partition, with total d and zero angles retained. Thus the EXACT map has the one-tetrahedron energy exponent

log||Khat||_op^2=log||Khat||_HS^2+O(logN)
                            =N v_3/pi+O(logN).          (13)

The uniform factorial estimate and the unique simplex maximum at thirds prove(13); no isolated numerical saddle is a premise. Equation(12), not a separate entrywise Cauchy estimate, gives its quantitative all-exterior boundary norm.

4. The inverse-packet cup move

Let J=A^-1(phat_12 tensor I_3). Its exact output selection is

J|n> supported on |a,0,c>,
a+c=n+d, n<=a<=d,
J_(a,0,c ; n)=N^-1/2 x^[2(a-d)] [c choose d-a]_Q.      (14)

Proof. Write the cup index as p and apply C’_23 followed by (C’_12)^-1. The desired output fixes s=d-a-b and p=a+r. The remaining finite r sum is

sum_(r=0)^b (-1)^r Q^[r(r-1)/2][b choose r]_Q
                                                =(1;Q)_b.

It is zero unless b=0; for b=0 one has r=0,p=a,s=d-a and c=n+s. Substitution gives(14), with every inverse phase retained. Thus this is an exact cup/current cancellation, not an adjoint claimed from a nonunitary packet.

In the SAME physical metric(5),

|Jhat_(a,0,n+d-a ; n)|^2
                          =w_d(n,d-a,a-n).             (15)

To check it directly, the squared gauge ratio is h_a h_0 h_c/h_n=N s_n/(s_a s_c). Multiplying the squared coefficient in(14) and using s_a s_(d-a)=N gives(15). It follows that Jhat^*Jhat=diag(R_(d-n)), so its operator/HS norms have the same exact one-weight partition as(12). The literal original cup p restores the factor N^(3/2) in amplitude, or N^3 in energy. These polynomial factors remain explicit.

5. What is retained at the marked cap

The actual full marked row is m_(u,v)=xv/N2, WITHOUT a degree delta. It cannot use(8). Its complete packet on arbitrary input has output n=c+s, 0<=s<=d-c, and the exact coefficient

(m_12 A)_(n;(a,b,c))
  =N^-2 (-1)^s Q^[s(s-1)/2]x^[b-2s]
     [c+s choose s]_Q
       *sum_(r=max(0,s-b))^(min(a,d-b)) [b+r choose r]_Q. (16)

An empty interval means zero. This follows directly from(3) and the unrestricted original row; no source component is omitted. It is the remaining finite current packet when a marked boundary is present. Only if the FULL exterior has already forced pair degree d can the corresponding restricted marked row be used. Even then its r sum lacks Q^r and equals (Q^(-(d-b));Q)_(d-b), not the delta in(8). It is generally nonzero away from b=d.

Thus the ordinary cap elimination is source-exact and useful, but does not delete the last marked source or its normal currents. If a beta or divided-Ward factorization is used to express(2), all its terms must add back to(6)–(16). Keeping only a bare balanced residue would not establish these identities.

6. Positive multiplication form and iteration scope

Write sigma_a=q1 and g_j=q2. Since q^(ac)=g_(a+c)/(g_a g_c), the unitary port phases in(9) can be displayed explicitly. After transporting those phases, the positive two-input part is

T^+_(n;a,c)=N^-1/2 delta_(n=a+c<=d)
                          sqrt(s_n/(s_a s_c)).         (17)

For W_j=1/sqrt(s_j) it is exactly

T^+=N^-1/2 W^-1 M(W tensor W),

where M is multiplication in C[z]/(z^N), in its canonical monomial basis. Consequently these positive kernels associate exactly, and their internal factorials telescope on a rooted tree. Actual port phases and the removed middle |d> registers must still be retained in the original diagram. They are not declared globally canceled by(17).

The complete cap-tree calculation gives the arbitrary-width boundary-tree energy with these original node types. This does not assert that every signed braid reduces to such a tree: overlapping recoupling cycles, Gaussian feedback and especially the full marked packet(16) remain. No broad knot survival or sharp whole-source volume estimate follows merely from this local contraction.
