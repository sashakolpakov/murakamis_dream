Exact removal of a physical crossing from the Gauss factor

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

On every upper-chamber two-head degree block, the actual finite Gauss factor times the physical checked crossing is a scalar times EHFHE^H F^H, where HH is the complementary degree. The equality includes every Casimir Jordan derivative. It replaces a specific anti-triangular crossing interface by a polynomial of degree HH; it does not replace that polynomial by its classical symbol.

This is a fixed-root, complete-meridian identity. Its insertion into a meridian-dependent source keeps an explicit normal defect. No large-power coherent estimate or geometric-cycle contribution is claimed.

4. What the factorization changes in the actual kernel

The block Ω\Omega is tridiagonal in |a,T−a⟩|a,T-a\rangle. Thus PH(Ω)P_H(\Omega) has bandwidth at most HH: (ATRT)ba=0if |b−a|>H.(23) (A_TR_T)_{ba}=0\qquad\text{if }|b-a|>H. \qquad\text{(23)} This is an exact finite support reduction, not a decay estimate.

For any ordered actual outer matrices or source rows L0,B0L_0,B_0, L0ATRTB0=κcHL0EHFHB0.(24) L_0A_TR_TB_0=\kappa c_H L_0E^HF^HB_0. \qquad\text{(24)} One may use the equality also in a finite coherent convolution, because that convolution is the exact resolution of the same matrix product. All original binomial factors, finite identity caps, and external source scalars remain on their respective legs. No inverse Casimir measure or diagonalizability assumption is needed to insert (24).

The complementary highest-row relation is likewise exact. It does not identify it with the triangular reduced R1R_1 generator.

There is a source-exact coefficient contour for the remaining power. Define the actual finite Euler polynomials EN(tX)=∑n=0dtnXn/fn. E_N(tX)=\sum_{n=0}^d t^nX^n/f_n . Then, with every finite cap retained, EHFH=fH2[λHμH]EN(λE)EN(μF).(26) E^HF^H=f_H^2[\lambda^H\mu^H]\, E_N(\lambda E)E_N(\mu F). \qquad\text{(26)} The coefficient means two small positively oriented circles about zero, with factors dλ/λH+1d\lambda/\lambda^{H+1} and dμ/μH+1d\mu/\mu^{H+1}. Equation (26) is exact for all HH in (3). It does not estimate that extensive coefficient from a small-activity asymptotic, nor select a later deformed contour.

5. Routed opposite source and the normal terms still required

G’s actual two-tail routing is Top=ℬ3(Wτ−1)ANDtop𝒞2,Jop=DN(MK)−1Top.(27) T_{\mathrm{ op}} =\mathcal B_3(W_\tau^{-1})A_ND_{\mathrm{ top}}\mathscr C_2, \qquad J_{\mathrm{ op}}=D_N(MK)^{-1}T_{\mathrm{ op}}. \qquad\text{(27)} The factor DtopD_{\mathrm{ top}}, the balanced turnover, the routed word, and the source normalization keep exactly G’s order. At the root, on the degree block (3), (6) permits the substitution AT=κcHEHFHRT−1(28) A_T=\kappa c_HE^HF^HR_T^{-1} \qquad\text{(28)} inside (27). A physical positive crossing immediately next to ATA_T is thereby absorbed by (24). No unrelated crossing is commuted through an exterior to create such an adjacency.

In particular (6) must not be differentiated as though it were a proved meridian-family identity. To state the retained normal precisely, keep the original analytic fixed-root matrices and define 𝒟T(M)=AT(M)RT(M)−κ(M)cHE(M)HF(M)H.(29) \mathcal D_T(M)= A_T(M)R_T(M)-\kappa(M)c_HE(M)^HF(M)^H. \qquad\text{(29)} The number cHc_H in this definition is the fixed-root scalar (6). Its root zero alone does not remove it beside a meridian pole. Similarly, (6) is not a quantum-parameter normal identity.

The result pays the literal physical-crossing interface, its complete Jordan data, and a shorter exact coefficient contour. It does not yet pay an estimate for EHFHE^HF^H at HH proportional to NN, an entire signed word on a common coherent chart, or a nonzero geometric contribution to the original closing cycle.
