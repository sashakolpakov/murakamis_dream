Endpoint-current matchings are units in the full boundary algebra

Private research note, 15 November 2026.

The exact simultaneous endpoint-current polynomial has no zero at any physical assignment of its remaining boundary variables, for every level N≥3N\ge3 and every choice of signed current powers, except when one of its even powers is zero. More strongly, its inverse retains all mixed first Hermite normals with a polynomial bound uniform in those powers. This follows from an accretive determinant for the actual current coefficients.

This is a finite boundary-algebra statement. It does not give a lower bound after the prescribed exterior trace, nor identify a selected geometric coefficient of the original scalar. The earlier matching theorem already proves a nonzero leading current coefficient when a complete exterior contour theorem is supplied. The result here requires no such contour theorem.

1. The actual polynomial and its determinant

Let O⊂SO\subset S be its odd-power vertices and E=S\OE=S\setminus O. The remaining boundary variables are wjw_j, indexed by the outside vertices adjacent to SS; write their set as BB, with b=|B|b=|B|.

Put UN=TΠ0U_N=T\Pi_0, the original turned full endpoint idempotent. Let mvjm_{vj} be the number of actual incidences from vv to outside vertex jj, and dv=∑jmvjd_v=\sum_jm_{vj}. For a simple tree these numbers are zero or one, but their incidence notation keeps shared outside variables explicit. The exact monomers are Av(𝒘)={σnvNρ,v∈E,2∑jmvjUN(wj)+η{nvρ+2(dv−1)c0},v∈O,dN=s12(N2−1)3>0.(2) A_v(\mathbf w)= \begin{cases} \sigma n_vN\rho,&v\in E,\\[1mm] 2\sum_jm_{vj}U_N(w_j) +\eta\{n_v\rho+2(d_v-1)c_0\},&v\in O, \end{cases} \qquad d_N=\frac{s_1^2(N^2-1)}3>0. \qquad\text{(2)} The full boundary polynomial of these inserted currents is exactly ℰS,N(𝒘)=∑M⊂E(G[O])MmatchingdN|M|∏v∈S\V(M)Av(𝒘).(3) \mathcal E_{S,N}(\mathbf w) =\sum_{\substack{M\subset E(G[O])\\M\ {\mathrm{ matching}}}} d_N^{|M|}\prod_{v\in S\setminus V(M)}A_v(\mathbf w). \qquad\text{(3)} This is Theorem 1 of the grouped endpoint-current note. It is an ordinary polynomial identity before any boundary reduction; every internal mixed first normal and every actual outside incidence is retained.

If any v∈Ev\in E has nv=0n_v=0, (3) is identically zero. Henceforth assume all even powers are nonzero. Define BE=∏v∈EσnvNρ,m=|O|,xv=σAv(v∈O),𝖣(𝒘)=diag⁡(xv)+idNAdj⁡(G[O]).(4) B_E=\prod_{v\in E}\sigma n_vN\rho,\qquad m=|O|,\qquad x_v=\sigma A_v\quad(v\in O), \qquad \mathsf D(\mathbf w)=\operatorname{diag}(x_v) +i\sqrt{d_N}\,\operatorname{Adj}(G[O]). \qquad\text{(4)} Empty determinants and products equal one. Then ℰS,N=BEσmdet⁡𝖣.(5) \boxed{\ \mathcal E_{S,N} =B_E\,\sigma^m\det\mathsf D.\ } \qquad\text{(5)} Indeed a permutation supported by a forest has only fixed points and edge transpositions. A transposition contributes −[idN]2=dN-[i\sqrt{d_N}]^2=d_N. Factoring σ\sigma from each unmatched odd monomer contributes σm−2|M|=σm\sigma^{m-2|M|}=\sigma^m, proving (5). No sum over physical colors or contraction of the exterior has been taken in this determinant.

For any complex boundary assignment satisfying ℜxv>0\Re x_v>0 at every odd vertex, write 𝖣=𝖱+i𝖧,𝖱=diag⁡(ℜxv)>0,𝖧=diag⁡(ℑxv)+dNAdj⁡(G[O]).(6) \mathsf D=\mathsf R+i\mathsf H,\qquad \mathsf R=\operatorname{diag}(\Re x_v)>0,\qquad \mathsf H=\operatorname{diag}(\Im x_v) +\sqrt{d_N}\operatorname{Adj}(G[O]). \qquad\text{(6)} The matrix 𝖧\mathsf H is Hermitian. 2. Every physical boundary assignment is in this sector

Let ca=2cos⁡((2a+1)θ),0≤a≤⌊D/2⌋(8) c_a=2\cos((2a+1)\theta),\qquad 0\le a\le\lfloor D/2\rfloor \qquad\text{(8)} be the distinct physical nodes. All are double except the last node −2-2 when NN is odd. The exact full Ward-coupon formulas are σUN(ca)=c0+2sin⁡θ∑r=1acot⁡(rθ),UN′(ca)=−σsin⁡θ4sin⁡((2a+1)θ)∑r=a+1N−1−acsc⁡2(rθ)at double nodes.(9) \begin{split} \sigma U_N(c_a) &=c_0+2\sin\theta\sum_{r=1}^a\cot(r\theta),\\ U_N'(c_a) &=-\sigma\frac{\sin\theta}{4\sin((2a+1)\theta)} \sum_{r=a+1}^{N-1-a}\csc^2(r\theta) \quad\text{at double nodes}. \end{split} \qquad\text{(9)} These are equations (21)–(22) of the signed Ward note. The first formula includes the odd simple node. Every cotangent in it is positive, unless the sum is empty.

At an arbitrary physical outside assignment 𝒄=(caj)j∈B\mathbf c=(c_{a_j})_{j\in B}, (2) and (9) give, for v∈Ov\in O, xv(𝒄)=rv(𝒄)−invs1,rv(𝒄)=2c0+4sin⁡θ∑jmvj∑r=1ajcot⁡(rθ)≥2c0.(10) \begin{split} x_v(\mathbf c)&=r_v(\mathbf c)-i n_vs_1,\\ r_v(\mathbf c) &=2c_0+ 4\sin\theta\sum_jm_{vj}\sum_{r=1}^{a_j}\cot(r\theta) \ \ge\ 2c_0. \end{split} \qquad\text{(10)} In particular all powers affect only the imaginary parts of these odd monomers. For N≥3N\ge3, put δN=2c0=4cos⁡(π/N)≥2\delta_N=2c_0=4\cos(\pi/N)\ge2. Equations (5), (7), and (10) prove the exact bound |ℰS,N(𝒄)|≥(Ns1)|E|(∏v∈E|nv|)∏v∈Orv(𝒄)≥(Ns1)|E|(∏v∈E|nv|)δNm>0.(11) \boxed{\quad |\mathcal E_{S,N}(\mathbf c)| \ge (Ns_1)^{|E|}\!\left(\prod_{v\in E}|n_v|\right) \prod_{v\in O}r_v(\mathbf c) \ge (Ns_1)^{|E|}\!\left(\prod_{v\in E}|n_v|\right) \delta_N^m>0 . \quad} \qquad\text{(11)} This holds simultaneously for all assignments, all signed integer powers, and both level parities. Powers may even depend on NN. Restoring the original common crossing units κNnv\kappa_N^{n_v} does not change (11), since those units have modulus one.

The restriction N≥3N\ge3 is necessary. At N=2N=2, two adjacent odd current vertices with no exterior and powers n1=n2=1n_1=n_2=1 have A1=A2=−2iA_1=A_2=-2i and dN=4d_N=4, so (3) is exactly zero. This is a degeneracy of the actual current polynomial, not an asymptotic example.

3. Inversion retains every mixed first normal

Let 𝒜N=ℂ[w]/(2TN(w/2)+2),ℬN=⨂j∈B𝒜N.(12) \mathcal A_N=\mathbb C[w]/(2T_N(w/2)+2),\qquad \mathcal B_N=\bigotimes_{j\in B}\mathcal A_N. \qquad\text{(12)} Use its full Hermite seminorm ∥f∥𝒥,B=max𝒄,J|∂Jf(𝒄)|,∂J=∏j∈J∂wj,(13) \|f\|_{\mathcal J,B} =\max_{\mathbf c,J} |\partial_J f(\mathbf c)|,\qquad \partial_J=\prod_{j\in J}\partial_{w_j}, \qquad\text{(13)} where J⊂BJ\subset B contains only variables assigned to double nodes; each variable is differentiated at most once. These are all local tensor-algebra coordinates, so (13) is a norm on ℬN\mathcal B_N.

Proof. The Chinese remainder decomposition of (12) is a product over physical assignments of rings ℂ[ϵj:jdouble]/(ϵj2:jdouble).(16) \mathbb C[\epsilon_j:j\ {\mathrm{ double}}] /(\epsilon_j^2:j\ {\mathrm{ double}}). \qquad\text{(16)} An element in any such ring is a unit precisely when its constant coefficient is nonzero. Equation (11) proves that condition at every assignment. Its inverse data are those of the analytic function 1/ℰS,N1/\mathcal E_{S,N} near each assignment.

For the quantitative assertion, the normal formula (9) implies |UN′(ca)|≤N2−112.(17) |U_N'(c_a)|\le\frac{N^2-1}{12}. \qquad\text{(17)} Indeed sin⁡((2a+1)θ)≥sin⁡θ\sin((2a+1)\theta)\ge\sin\theta at the double nodes, and ∑r=1N−1csc⁡2(rθ)=(N2−1)/3\sum_{r=1}^{N-1}\csc^2(r\theta)=(N^2-1)/3. Adding the next element either starts a colored singleton in mm ways or inserts it after one of the previous elements; this gives the displayed rising product. The empty-set case has value one.

Now |det⁡𝖣|−1≤δN−m|\det\mathsf D|^{-1}\le\delta_N^{-m} by (7). Multiply (21) by the constant (BEσm)−1(B_E\sigma^m)^{-1} and use (18)–(22). This gives (14). Thus multiplication by the current is injective on the full boundary algebra, not merely on its semisimple quotient. For any boundary state ff, the ordinary product rule and (15) give ∥f∥𝒥,B≤2bCG,SN2b∥ℰS,Nf∥𝒥,B.(23) \|f\|_{\mathcal J,B} \le 2^b C_{G,S}N^{2b} \|\mathcal E_{S,N}f\|_{\mathcal J,B}. \qquad\text{(23)} For fixed powers the forward multiplier also has polynomial Hermite norm: the Ward coupon in (9) has values O(1+log⁡N)O(1+\log N) and first normals O(N2)O(N^2), and (3) has a fixed number of factors. Consequently current multiplication preserves the exponential rate of this boundary norm in both directions for fixed powers.

4. What this does and does not control

The theorem applies to the specified simultaneous one-step endpoint currents in their original slots. It does not identify an arbitrary relative-contour remainder with those currents. Shared outside variables, all unchosen edges, the retained boundary polynomial, and the original exterior contraction remain exactly where (3) places them.

In particular, for the prescribed exterior functional ΛN\Lambda_N, ΛN(ℰS,Nf)(24) \Lambda_N(\mathcal E_{S,N}f) \qquad\text{(24)} may vanish even though ℰS,N\mathcal E_{S,N} is a unit and f≠0f\ne0. Equations (11), (15), and (23) do not supply a scalar lower bound for (24), a nonzero original contour intersection, or a certificate for the ideal of all geometric scalar coefficients.

The pointwise complex criterion is exactly the sufficient condition ℜxv>0\Re x_v>0 used in (6). No positive-period hypothesis was needed to prove it on the physical grid. It has not been deduced for the canonical boundary polynomial at an arbitrary complex geometric evaluation. Agreement of a different representative’s physical Hermite data does not establish its equality as an ordinary polynomial at that evaluation, or a zero-free inverse throughout an entire complex physical window.

Section 3 of the earlier grouped-current note treats a different question: when the complete exterior contour and all its currents are already controlled, the logarithmic Ward representative gives a nonzero leading matching coefficient relative to that exterior packet. The present exact finite unit and uniform inverse-normal bound do not assume that result’s contour hypotheses, and do not remove them from its scalar conclusion.
