Balanced marked observations at every coideal height

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

A balanced physical clock charge moves the last marked observation to any coideal height. On the actual closed source its change is a known unit, because the other ordinary caps fix the degree of the final pair. This gives a two-label auxiliary family for every original signed word, with all shared-height sums retained. Its whole-word coefficient is the original scalar, which is allowed to vanish.

At a matched bottom height the selected two-head vector and graph row are explicit geometric sequences. Their counting norms and overlap are known before the word is evaluated. The resulting clock moments retain the finite endpoint exactly. The literal scaled lowering operator also has a locally uniform analytic initializer limit, the transform of an explicit positive exponential density. These statements do not evaluate the global quartic minimum or identify a contributing geometric cycle.

6. Consequence for fixed-degree formulations

The charges (6) and arrays in (11) are specified before RR is evaluated. They give exact linear observations of its true source, with the full coherent contraction inside each observation. The closed-family equations (13) have fixed Laurent degree and can be retained as linear relations among auxiliary observations. They neither impose new equations on independently summed internal heights nor change the original bilinear Conway gluing maps.

Degree four for a variational energy is not a bound on the degree of the original crossing in the physical EE: its nilpotent Euler sum can have degree N−1N-1. The analytic initializer estimate (22) therefore cannot be inserted through an arbitrary word without the required uniform source bounds and its retained original observation.

In particular the known polynomial initializer angle (18) is not an angle bound after an arbitrary short tangle. No result here identifies the auxiliary labels with meridians, supplies the relative cycle of a geometric saddle, or proves that ZN(R)Z_N(R) is nonzero. The original quartic source problem and its selected geometric lower estimate remain. All formulas hold when its scalar vanishes.
