The actual reduced word as a closed finite Bargmann period

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The Cartan first-meridian-jet formula has an exact closed Gaussian period built from the known reduced generators. Both sides of every interface are specified. The identity kernel is a finite exponential polynomial; its cutoff and all upper and lower projectors remain. A paired kernel estimate gives exponential control of the Gaussian ends using elementary bounds on the local matrices.

This also places a lower, resolved row endpoint in finite complex Bargmann coordinates after the actual factorial gauge. It does not place that point on the original real cycle or prove that its contribution survives the closed pairing. The common meridian varies at fixed qNq_N; ordinary-color quantum normals are not inferred.

The complete proof directs are R, H, V, D and G, pinned below. The earlier compact coherent source and its two-mode realization were read for coverage. The one-mode normalization and estimates used here are proved directly.

7. What the closed pairing still requires

Equation (15) inserts D’s actual donor kernel into (10), but a relative estimate for that one factored kernel is not a relative estimate for the whole period. The neighboring dual, the integer recipient sum and its projectors remain. For any finite kernel error EE, the safe global contraction estimate is the corresponding bound (18) with ∥E∥H\|E\|_H and the other local norms. Pointwise coherent column control does not automatically give that norm.

The superexponential donor tails of D can outweigh exponential factors on a supplied bounded paired domain with the required recipient margin; this does not establish such a domain for the original whole cycle. The fixed global Gaussian tails (21) and their translated versions control only the ends of the exact representation. The compact part, its factorial caps, and its geometric intersection remain to be analyzed. In particular no positivity, geometric coefficient, or scalar lower bound follows.
