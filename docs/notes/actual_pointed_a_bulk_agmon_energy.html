A source-exact bulk Agmon energy for the actual pointed quantum-A system

28 October 2026. The actual bounded-jet pointed system admits a positive weighted energy with its original boundary source. On any compact arc whose weighted classical symbol has a fixed gap from zero, a direct localized Plancherel argument bounds the interior energy by that source and explicit boundary packets. No eigenvector diagonalization, global inverse bound or cancellation of source terms is assumed.

This is a quantitative general theorem for the actual fixed-band system of EVERY knot. It is local in the color coordinate: turning-point, resonance and end packets remain. It does not yet give their sharp geometric connection estimate or a new volume-conjecture class.

1. Actual system and exact positive weighted energy

Use the uniformly scaled bounded-jet matrix and source of the pointed Gram theorem,

M_N y=b_N, R_N y=(C_(j_*),...,C_N)^t.             (1)

Its block size and bandwidth are fixed independently of N, all entries are uniformly bounded, and b_N is supported on the prescribed fixed number of INITIAL IDENTITY rows. Those initial jet values are fixed small-color Laurent polynomials and their bounded-order jets; their scaled norms are uniformly bounded. Every solution has the same constant-color vector, even when its higher jets are not unique.

Interior recurrence rows, indexed by their initial color j, have form

(M_N y)_j=sum_(i=0)^d A_(i,N)(j) y_(j+i).

Uniformly on compact color intervals, their fixed-size matrix coefficients satisfy

A_(i,N)(j)=A_i(j/N)+O(1/N),
A_i are continuously differentiable.             (2)

These are not assumptions about an unknown asymptotic solution. For the actual system they follow directly from its finite monomial and binomial formula (2.1) and the N^-r jet scaling. Its symbol is exactly the proved block polynomial A_B(x,L) of equation (5.2) there. Possible root-level nullspaces and the entire jet budget remain in (1).

Let phi be a fixed real C2 function of x=j/N. Give each COLUMN color block weight exp(N phi(j/N)), calling the resulting diagonal matrix W_c. Give a recurrence ROW the same weight at its row label j; each initial identity row receives the weight of its prescribed color. Call this W_r. Define

u=W_c y,
L_(phi,N)=W_r M_N W_c^-1,
P_(phi,N)=L_(phi,N)^dagger L_(phi,N).              (3)

Then, EXACTLY,

L_(phi,N)u=W_r b_N,
<u,P_(phi,N)u>=||W_r b_N||^2.                   (4)

This is the full sourced Hermitian energy. No affine term or normal component has been discarded. Choose phi(0)=0. Since the source colors are fixed, their N phi(j/N) are bounded; the right side of (4) is uniformly bounded. The original complete color is still the selected constant jet in (1), not an arbitrary solution of the principal symbol.

Despite the potentially exponential values of W_c and W_r themselves, the nonzero entries of L_(phi,N) involve only bounded local ratios. In an interior row their leading coefficients are

A_i(x) exp(-i phi'(x)),                          (5)

where i in (5) is the INTEGER shift index, not sqrt(-1). The uniform error is O(1/N). Thus L_phi and P_phi still have bounded entries and fixed bandwidth. The Hermitian bulk symbol is the POSITIVE matrix

A_B(x,exp(-phi'(x)) L)^dagger
                    A_B(x,exp(-phi'(x)) L), |L|=1. (6)

It is not obtained by silently treating a moving eigenframe as unitary.

2. A proved local coercivity theorem

Let I be a compact color interval contained in (0,1). Suppose the explicit weighted block symbol satisfies

sigma_min A_B(x,exp(-phi'(x)) exp(i theta))>=eta>0
                   for x in I, theta real.      (7)

This is independently checkable from the specified finite operator. For the actual lower-triangular jet symbol, invertibility is equivalent to the scalar A_cl not vanishing on that circle. On a compact arc fixed dimension and continuity then give the positive eta; no semisimplicity or separation of different eigenvalues is needed.

For vectors u supported in I and a fixed small interior margin,

||L_(phi,N)u|| >=(eta-C N^-1/2)||u||.             (8)

Here the operator includes EVERY row meeting that support. There is no artificial Dirichlet deletion of its boundary output rows.

Proof. On the color lattice choose a bounded-overlap partition sum_l chi_l^2=1 with windows of length ell=floor(sqrt(N)) and |chi_l(j+i)-chi_l(j)|<=C/ell for bounded i. Freeze (5) at each window center. The full-line constant-coefficient operator has Fourier multiplier A_B(x_l,exp(-phi’(x_l))exp(i theta)); ordinary Plancherel and (7) give norm at least eta on every localized vector.

The difference from the actual operator is at most C ell/N on each window by (2) and phi in C2. Bounded overlap gives the same bound after summing the squared localized norms. Its commutator with chi_l has summed norm at most C/ell times ||u||, by fixed bandwidth and the displayed difference bound. Since sum_l ||chi_l L_phi u||^2 =||L_phi u||^2, the direct-sum triangle inequality yields

eta||u|| <=||L_phi u||+C(ell/N+1/ell)||u||.

This is (8). The proof handles block coefficients, repeated roots away from the weighted circle, and nonnormal actual transfers.

3. Apply it to the original response, keeping boundary currents

Let chi be a smooth cutoff supported in I and equal to one on a smaller interval I’. Let B_chi contain all its transition colors and their fixed bandwidth neighbors. Applying (8) to chi u and using the EXACT equation (4) gives, for large N,

||chi u|| <=(2/eta)[ ||chi W_r b_N||
                  +(C/N)||u restricted to B_chi|| ]. (9)

The cutoff on recurrence rows uses their original label j. On initial identity rows it uses their prescribed color, so the source convention is unchanged. Formula (9) follows from

L_phi(chi u)=chi W_r b_N+[L_phi,chi]u.

The commutator is EXPLICITLY supported in B_chi and has norm at most C/N for a fixed scaled cutoff. A cutoff over ell lattice steps instead has C/ell and its corresponding retained boundary packet. These are the original normal/endpoint flux terms of the estimate; they are not asserted small without their actual source connection.

Since R_N selects the constant jet and commutes with the color weight, (9) also bounds the weighted interior ordinary-color energy. It holds for every actual truncated-jet solution; higher-jet nonuniqueness is not removed by choosing a favorable solution. A global kernel can still be supported by the boundary packets, and nothing here divides by an unproved finite Evans or resonance determinant.

4. A regulator barrier is a possible weight, not a free boundary estimate

On a genuinely identified peripheral branch set

phi(x)=-R(x)/(2pi).

In the recorded color convention dR/dx=2pi log|L_branch|, so exp(-phi’)=|L_branch|. Exactly at that eikonal radius the symbol gap (7) is zero. On a compact regular arc with distinct root moduli one may tilt phi’ by a fixed small amount so its circle lies strictly between adjacent root moduli. Then (7)–(9) hold. The tilt can tend to zero AFTER the large-N bound, while every boundary packet remains.

This gives genuine controlled propagation THROUGH a forbidden bulk arc, rather than assuming contraction of instantaneous eigenvectors. It works at eigenvalue collisions away from the chosen weighted circle as well. At a root on that circle the estimate stops; the corresponding turning or interface packet must be connected by an additional source estimate. The original fixed initial packet and complete-color resonance also remain.

The geometric bound |R|<=Vol(X) bounds this ABSOLUTE weight whenever the spectral component has actually been identified as a peripheral representation branch. It does not bound its omitted boundary packets, choose among several branches, or prove such an identification for an arbitrary annihilator. A general sharp upper theorem still needs a source-compatible connection estimate across the finite set of turning/interface/end packets. Equations (4) and (9) show precisely where their cumulative amplification enters; it cannot be replaced by uniqueness or by a largest-root integral.

The SOURCE ANCHOR must also remain. For the polynomial initial-source bound following (4), normalize instead

phi(x)=-[R(x)-R(0)]/(2pi).

Recovering an unweighted value then costs the regulator DIFFERENCE R(x)-R(0), whose geometric bound is in general 2Vol(X), not Vol(X). Keeping phi=-R/(2pi) simply puts the compensating exponential into the weighted initial source. Fixed small-color pointing does not by itself prove that every spectral branch has regulator zero there. A sharp bound needs its actual branch/source normalization as well as the boundary connections. No constant action shift has been used to conceal that factor.

Audit: the actual figure-eight affine coefficients with fixed original source, weighted positive-energy identities, compact off-root-circle bulk coercivity, and the retained cutoff commutator equation. Numerical checks supplement the all-fixed-band proof (2)–(9), not a new knot-class assertion.
