The original marked two-vertex contraction in every channel

29 October 2026. The complete magnetic contraction of one actual black vertex with its ordinary white vertex and the original physical marked arc has a positive formula at every supported folded triple. This is a coherent contraction, not a replacement of white by a Hermitian adjoint. The paired circle is retained as a nilpotent coupon. Its first normal and the normal required by the physical mark are included separately.

Write N>=2, d=N-1, M=floor(d/2), q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, F_u=(Q;Q)_u, s_u=|F_u| and x=q^d. For 0<=a,b,c<=M put

J=a+b+c, delta=J-d,
r=b+c-a, r1=a+b-c, r2=a+c-b.

Let L_j be the FULL c_j sector of the actual complete circle L_N, where c_j=q(2j+1)+q(-2j-1). On the original nested six-head source p_16 p_23 p_45, insert L_a,L_b,L_c on the three consecutive physical pairs. Close with the two original ordinary caps and the original unrestricted marked row specified in the actual vertex definition. Denote this raw scalar by Z_N(a,b,c). No extra component normalization is inserted.

Put k_j=2 for a paired node 2j<d and k_j=1 for the odd simple node 2j=d. Then Z_N(a,b,c)=0 for J<d. For J>=d it is EXACTLY

Z_N(a,b,c)
  =N s_delta s_r s_r1 s_r2
     *product_j h0_j/(s_(2a)s_(2b)s_(2c)) *|A_N(a,b,c)|^2
  =N^2 k_a k_b k_c s_r s_r1 s_r2/s_delta
                                        *R_N(a,b,c)^2 >0. (1)

Here A_N and R_N are the proved actual scalar and positive factorial partition in the all-channel source theorem, and

h0_j=k_j N s_(2j)/s_j^2.                         (2)

All factorial indices in (1) lie in 0,…,d. At N=2 there are no supported triples. Formula (1) is a local two-vertex source evaluation; it is not an arbitrary-graph norm or a hyperbolic-volume identification.

1. Genuine duality, including the reversed label order

Use the original ordinary image Q_j, its full Clebsch columns W_j and its positive single-object metric from the all-degree CD normalization:

G_(j,u)=s_(2j)/(s_u s_(2j-u)),
pi_j=G_j^(-1) W_j^dagger P,
L_j=h0_j W_j pi_j.                              (3)

On a paired axis pi_j W_j=0. On the odd simple axis it is I. Nothing below uses a paired retraction or substitutes a value-only projector for L_j.

For Q_j choose the canonical ordinary cup with first entry one:

p^(j)_(u,2j-u)=(-1)^u q^[u(2j-u-1)]
                                  F_u F_(2j-u)/F_(2j),
ell^(j)=(p^(j))^(-1).                           (4)

Its defining E,F equations follow directly from the displayed ratio. The physical maps remain p_(u,v)=N x^(-v)delta_(u+v,d) and ell_(u,v)=x^u/N delta_(u+v,d). The right dual of W_j is

D(W_j)=(p^(j))^T W_j^T(ell^T tensor ell^T)P,
D(W_j)=gamma_j pi_j,
gamma_j=q^[2d^2-2dj-j^2-2j]/N^2
       =q^(2-j^2)/N^2.                         (5)

These are bilinear pivotal duals. To fix the scalar, take row0 and physical input (d-j,0). The original lowest endpoint is W_(j,2j)(d,j)=q1, and p(j)_(2j,0)=q(-2j), giving the first exponent in (5). Its difference from 2-j^2 is a multiple of 2N. Both maps are actual integer-H intertwiners. Their homogeneous Hom space is one-dimensional: after duality its highest vector lies in the degree d-j E-kernel, whose first-order recurrence has all interior arrows nonzero. This proves the all-input equality.

Also D2(W_j)=(K_V(-1) tensor K_V^(-1))W_j K_j=W_j, directly by the cup matrices and K covariance. Thus D(pi_j)=gamma_j^(-1)W_j, even when pi_j W_j=0.

Define the ordered black column and ordinary white row by

U_abc=(pi_a tensor pi_b tensor pi_c)J_source,
Lambda_abc=ell^S(W_a tensor W_b tensor W_c).

Right duality REVERSES the three object labels. The actual nested matching is invariant under the six-head reversal, so

Lambda_abc=gamma_a gamma_b gamma_c D(U_cba).     (6)

In particular a same-order adjoint cannot be inferred when labels are unequal. Let Theta_abc be the genuine ordinary invariant normalized by Theta_abc(2a,r,0)=1. The complete source theorem gives

U_abc=s_abc Theta_abc,
s_abc=N^3 A_N(a,b,c)/G_(b,r),
s_cba=N^3 A_N(c,b,a)/G_(b,r1),
G_(b,r1)=G_(b,r).                               (7)

All source phases in (7) are kept.

2. An own finite ordered theta evaluation

Temporarily use a generic parameter zeta, Q=zeta^2. Let the same ordinary modules have highest weights 2a,2b,2c, and use the cups (4) with q replaced by zeta. The canonical invariants are regular finite Clebsch recursions, with denominators of index at most 2a,2b,2c.

The third-color-zero slice of Theta_abc is, for 0<=k<=r1,

A_k=Theta_abc(2a-k,r+k,0)
   =(-1)^k zeta^[k(2a+1-k)]
                       F_(r+k)F_(2a-k)/(F_r F_(2a)). (8)

This follows by solving its first-order lowest F equation backward from its stated corner. For the reversed tensor, the first color 2c is held fixed. Its E equation has just two terms, giving

B_k=Theta_cba(2c,r1-k,k)
   =zeta^[2k(a-b)] A_k.                         (9)

There is no boundary term: the first E input would be color 2c+1.

Let T be the partial magnetic pairing of Theta_abc and D(Theta_cba) over the first two objects. The invariant E equations imply K_c T is scalar, as cleared equalities between adjacent diagonal entries. At third color zero its scalar is zeta^(2c)T_00. Substituting (8),(9) and the exact cups yields

zeta^(2c)T_00
   =(-1)^r1 zeta^[4c-r1(r-1)] F_(2b)F_r2/(F_r F_(2a))
                                         *[J+1 choose r1]_Q. (10)

For completeness, the sole finite summation in (10) is

sum_(k=0)^r1 Q^[k(r2+1)]
         [r+k choose k]_Q[2a-k choose r2]_Q
   =[J+1 choose r1]_Q.                          (11)

It is the coefficient of u^r1 in

1/[(uQ^(r2+1);Q)_(r+1)(u;Q)_(r2+1)]
   =1/(u;Q)_(2c+2).

The reciprocal finite-product coefficient formula follows by induction on its length; no theta or 6j evaluation is a premise. Taking the pivotal trace of (10), and canceling its factor 1-Q^(2c+1) BEFORE specializing, proves the finite identity

Xi_abc:=D(Theta_cba)Theta_abc
  =(-1)^r1 zeta^[2c-r1(r-1)]
      F_(2b)F_r2 F_(J+1)
             /[(1-Q)F_r F_(2a)F_r1 F_(2c)].     (12)

This keeps both the ordered normalization and its root zero.

3. Retain the physical marked normal by a regular trace quotient

A regular generic lift of the physical W_j is explicit. Put t=d-j. Its highest coefficients and actual divided descendants are

h_u(zeta)=(-1)^u zeta^(-du)
  Q^[u(t-1)-u(u-1)/2](Q^(j+1);Q)_u F_(d-u)/F_d,
W_(j,v)(zeta)=F_pair^v h(zeta)/F_v, 0<=v<=2j.   (13)

Its highest recurrence and the directly inductive E,F commutator give its E,F,K covariance at every interior descendant. The final vector F_pair^(2j+1)h is killed by E_pair by the same commutator. Its degree is d+j+1>d, where E_pair is a rectangular bidiagonal matrix of full column rank: starting at its boundary row eliminates each input in turn, using only nonzero physical interior E arrows. Hence that final vector is zero. This proves the last F endpoint as well, without a generic decomposition theorem. All denominators in (13) have index <=d<N and are units near the chosen root. At that root factorial reflection gives exactly the original W_j. For the physical cup choose its generic canonical formula with highest weight d and scale N zeta(-d2); its root values are exactly the original p and ell.

Keep the ROOT gamma_j and h0_j fixed, and define

pi_j(zeta)=gamma_j^(-1)D(W_j(zeta)).

Thus h0_j W_j(zeta)pi_j(zeta) is a regular Hopf coupon specializing to the full actual circle sector (3), including its nilpotent value. This is a regular lift for a FIXED-ROOT identity. It does not identify the original moving-q germ with a projector or a new scalar germ.

By the original dual index contraction (6), the ordinary closed generic physical source without the h0 factors is

F(zeta)=gamma_a gamma_b gamma_c
                 s_abc(zeta)s_cba(zeta) Xi_abc(zeta). (14)

The source coefficients s(zeta) are holomorphic and their root values are (7). Cut the original physical marked arc. The resulting map is a regular scalar t(zeta)I on the generic physical highest-weight-d module. Indeed its K eigenvalues are distinct and its interior F arrows are nonzero, so commuting with K,F forces a scalar. Direct cup/cap multiplication gives

ell p^T=(-1)^d K_V,
F(zeta)=(-1)^d[N]_zeta t(zeta).                 (15)

At the root this is exactly the original pivot K^(1-N), since KN=(-1)d I. The original unrestricted marked row has m p=1, hence the marked closure is t(q). The mark has stayed on the physical V_N component; no paired-image projection is used to move it.

For J>=d, J+1=N+delta<2N. The numerator F_(J+1) in (12) has EXACTLY one root zero, and all displayed denominator factorials are units. Since

F_(J+1)/(1-Q^N) -> N F_delta,

equations (12),(15) give the finite, nonzero normal quotient

eta_abc=lim_(zeta->q) Xi_abc/[(-1)^d[N]_zeta]
  =(-1)^(r1-d)q^[2c+d-r1(r-1)]
      N F_delta F_(2b)F_r2/[F_r F_(2a)F_r1 F_(2c)]. (16)

This quotient retains the physical marked normal. Because Xi has a simple zero, derivatives of the holomorphic source coefficients in (14) make no contribution to the limit. The full paired-circle CD normal was already included in h0 and is not substituted for this different normal operation.

4. Full phases and the positive answer

Combining (7),(14)–(16), the marked scalar including all three circle sectors is

Z_N=product_j h0_j
   *q^[6-a^2-b^2-c^2] A_N(a,b,c)A_N(c,b,a)
                                  *eta_abc/G_(b,r)^2. (17)

The two actual corner magnitudes are equal. Their ordered source phases satisfy

phase(A_abc A_cba)
   =q^[2a^2+2c^2-2ac-2J-6].

Substitute F_u=(-i)^u q2s_u in (16). The net F-index difference is -d. Combining it with (17) leaves the phase

(-1)^(r1-d) i^d q^[N(N-2J-1)/2]=q^(-2Nc)=1.

Its remaining magnitude is

N s_delta s_(2b)s_r2/[s_r s_(2a)s_r1 s_(2c)].

Use G_(b,r)=s_(2b)/(s_r s_r1). This proves the first equality in (1). The independently proved scalar formula |A_N|=s_a s_b s_c R_N/(N s_delta) and (2) give its second equality. No phase, paired multiplicity or factor of N has been dropped.

As normalization checks, the odd simple triple a=b=c=(N-1)/2 gives Z=N3|A|2. For even N=2m>=4 and a=b=c=m-1, it gives Z=8N3|A|2/cos(pi/N), exactly the separately proved even central normal contraction.

There is also an explicit marked co-source identity. With the original unscaled W-coordinate packet Vm and the independently normalized Theta_abc used above, contracting all magnetic indices gives

sum_magnetic Theta_abc overline(Vm_abc)
  =N^5 s_delta s_r2/[s_(2a)s_(2c)] overline(A_N(a,b,c)). (18)

Indeed the literal vertex normalization is Z=(product h0_j/N^7)sum U_raw overline(Vm)/product G, while U_bal=N^3 A Theta/G_(b,r). Substitution of (1) proves (18). This is a specified linear functional of the entire magnetic tensor, not equality of the marked co-source and an adjoint on other inputs.

For literal signed edge twists R^(e_i)L_(j_i), multiply (1) by product_i omega_(j_i)^(e_i), where

omega_j=kappa(-1)^j q^[j(j+1)], kappa=q^(d^2/2).

This follows on the full root block since its nilpotent circle kills the additional normal term of R. It is not a stripped-Cartan phase. The coherent sum over different triples can then have cancellations. For three UNTWiSTED full complete circles the source is the sum of the positive terms (1); that statement concerns this specified two-vertex wiring only. Any external original line factor stays outside the raw scalar.

5. What this theorem does and does not estimate

The extra growth of the black magnetic tensor is contracted BEFORE an estimate. Its compensation here is supplied by the genuine white duality and the physical marked normal, not by bounding white in a Euclidean norm. Formula (1) supplies an exact positive partition for this local marked two-vertex source. It does not make the marked white tensor an adjoint on arbitrary exteriors, prove a tensor-product metric compatible with general recoupling, or identify its factorial action with the volume of a graph complement.

The focused exact audit checks all folded triples at ten physical levels N=2,…,11, every original Clebsch dual, reversed-label ordinary white and unrestricted marked contraction. A separate generic audit checks (10),(12) on all 65 admissible spin triples <=4 and all covariances of eleven regular physical lifts. Direct ungrouped original physical sources include both parities, unequal labels, genuine full-normal circle summands and signed Cartan crossings, in eighteen closures. These tests supplement the finite Vandermonde, duality and regular-trace proof above.
