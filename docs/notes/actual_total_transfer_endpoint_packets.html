Coherent original-transfer endpoint packets and exact Markov corners

28 October 2026. Collapsing the two Euler factors BEFORE separating transfer endpoints gives a quantitative, source-preserving boundary filtration of every original signed braid word. Both sublinear total-transfer endpoint collars have subexponential operator norm. Consequently the entire all-endpoint corner of the full original source is subexponential, without an assumption about its stationary positions.

This does not bound a mixed corner containing macroscopic transfers by the knot volume. It also does not identify a bridge overclock endpoint with a total physical transfer endpoint. An exact Markov calculation below shows why an isolated endpoint attached to a nontrivial exterior can still carry that exterior’s full rate and must be grouped as a normalization cell.

1. Use the original coherently summed crossing

Put d=N-1, Q=exp(2pii/N), x=-exp(-pii/N), F_r=(Q;Q)_r. The ORIGINAL positive crossing, with its common unit scalar kappa retained, sends input (a,b) to (b+k,a-k), where 0<=k<=min(a,d-b), with exact coefficient

kappa x^(-a-b+k)Q^(b(a-k))
             F_(b+k)F_a/[F_k F_b F_(a-k)].            (1)

This is the independently checked physical coefficient of the weighted-crossing theorem, with j=a-k. Both Euler factors have already been summed coherently; one of their separate orders is not called k here. The negative crossing is the exact balanced antiunitary mirror P conjugate(R_plus)P. It has the same k-indexed magnitude and bounds.

For an integer 0<=R<=d/2 let E_low be the sum of the original matrix entries with k<=R, and E_top those with d-k<=R. These are specified entry submatrices, with every original phase and zero retained. They are not substituted invariants or new quantum atoms.

2. Uniform quantitative normal-scale bounds

In the variables j=a-k,b, the magnitude of (1) is

A(k,b,j)=|F_(k+b)F_(k+j)|/[|F_k F_b F_j|].          (2)

For k<=R each numerator ratio adds k factors of magnitude at most two, while |F_k|>=(4/N)^k k! for k<=N/2. Hence

A(k,b,j)<=N^k/k!.                                  (3)

For k=d-r, support forces 0<=b,j<=r. Exact reflection |F_(d-s)|=N/|F_s| gives instead

A(d-r,b,j)=N/|F_r|
                *|[r choose b]_Q|*|[r choose j]_Q|
             <=N^(r+1)/r!.                        (4)

Indeed the sine formula and decrease of sin(t)/t on (0,pi) give |[r choose s]_Q|<=binom(r,s); their product is at most 4^r, which cancels the factor 4^r in the lower bound for |F_r|.

The polynomial matrix dimension and disjoint k-support now imply the conservative explicit bounds, for BOTH signs,

||E_low||,||E_top|| <=H_N(R),
H_N(R)=N^3 (e N/max(1,R))^R.                         (5)

The exponent at R=0 is interpreted as zero. For every R_N=o(N), log H_N(R_N)=o(N). This includes all bounded normal quantum jets, but also collars growing arbitrarily sublinearly. For R=floor(epsilon N), the rate is at most epsilon log(e/epsilon)+o(1), tending to zero with epsilon. The physical endpoint normal scale has therefore been bounded, rather than evaluated using an invalid bulk Stirling expansion.

The top collar forces both physical inputs and outputs into endpoint collars: a=d-r+j, b<=r, output(d-r+b,j). This is stronger support than a single separate Euler endpoint. The low collar includes its noncentral zero-transfer Cartan branch, so centrality must not be inferred for that branch.

3. A full-source all-corner estimate at arbitrary braid width

Use the original scalar source, already justified before decomposition,

K_N=chi_N/N Tr[(I tensor g tensor ... tensor g) W_N],
g_a=x^(1-N)Q^(-a).

Fix the original word and replace EACH of its m crossings by either E_low or E_top, keeping all signs, order, pivots and chi_N. The entire sum of these 2^m corner words has magnitude at most

|chi_N| N^(b-1) 2^m H_N(R)^m.                        (6)

This follows from the operator trace inequality on the actual N^b-head space; each pivot is unitary. In particular the WHOLE all-endpoint corner is exp(o(N)) times its retained external source for R_N=o(N). No position compactness, separate-sector positivity or removal of clock feedback is a hypothesis of (6).

For a mixed corner one must retain and estimate every remaining bulk operator. Its endpoint factors still cost (5), but this alone gives only the norm bound for the remaining coherent blocks. Neither (6) nor the zero local endpoint rate assigns a volume bound to that mixed source. In particular one may not fix bridge clocks and ignore W_h.

Exact thin-source reduction and the regular top-collar criterion

The top packet has an additional ORIGINAL-source description:

E_top,+(R)=sum_(a-j>=d-R)
              (I tensor E_j)R_plus(E_a tensor I).       (6a)

There are (R+1)(R+2)/2 terms: a=d-s, s,j>=0, s+j<=R. Each term keeps the COMPLETE original R between two PHYSICAL head projectors. Put k=a-j=d-(s+j). Its sole remaining input index b lies in [0,s+j]. The term is exactly

J_out diag_b(r_plus(a,b,k)) J_in^dagger,
J_in|b>=|a,b>, J_out|b>=|b+k,j>.                       (6b)

Both J maps are isometries from a space of dimension at most R+1; the diagonal has the subexponential bound (4). Thus this is a literal lower-dimensional boundary source, with the exterior word and its actual coupons unchanged. The negative version is the exact mirror. It is not a replacement by an ordinary low-color representation.

This grouping also supplies a quantitative version of the REGULAR physical-projector value theorem in the actual cap note, Section 9. Suppose the remaining full phase is stationary in every direction except the fixed projector-clock normals; every crossing belongs to its genuine regular actual canonical graph, or a controlled joint limit of that graph. Bound all physical positions, their inverses and the finitely many based conjugating frames by H>=1. Then for a fixed diagram there is a fixed exponent C such that the top-collar clocks give, throughout their stationary support,

delta_N=H^C(R+1)/N,
|Im W|<=C delta_N(1+|log delta_N|)  if delta_N is small. (6c)

Here W is the complete calibrated leading phase with its original unit-clock and integer marks, not a phase obtained by dropping a factorial face. To verify the estimate, the projector clocks satisfy |B-1|<=2pi(R+1)/N. In the actual finite position chart, e and f vanish to first order at B=1, K=-B^(-1), so ||G+I||<=C H^C(R+1)/N. The actual Hurwitz and inverse-turn relations propagate this estimate through the one-component knot. This does not assume that a projector preserves the meridian. Cut the oriented knot at EVERY projector. Each resulting interval has projector endpoints, and each side of each such endpoint is independently near -I: its clock is near one while either position is bounded by H. Follow an interval from its endpoint. Each actual crossing retains its physical strand meridian or conjugates it by a matrix whose norm and inverse are bounded by a fixed power of H. Each turn inverts the same nearcentral matrix. There are only a fixed number of these operations, so its distance from -I grows by at most a fixed power of H along the entire interval. At the next projector restart with its independently small endpoint value; no equality across its allowed position jump is assumed. All physical arcs, and hence their clocks, are within C delta_N of the central value. This also covers multiple retained top packets, since their number is bounded by the fixed original word.

The projector constraints are pure-imaginary logarithmic constraints. At restricted stationarity their missing normal gradients consequently have zero contraction with Re Z, exactly as in that cap theorem. The phase-defect identity leaves the original direct five-symbol sum. Its arguments are products and ratios of these near-unit physical clocks, and D(z)=O(|z-1|(1+|log|z-1||)) near z=1. This proves (6c). In particular H<=N^epsilon and R<=N^alpha give a vanishing regular action whenever alpha+C epsilon<1.

The estimate does not concern arbitrary auxiliary cleared branches, uncontrolled chart denominators, or a restriction to a REAL cutoff face without stationarity in its normal. At |z|=N^epsilon the missing conormal can contract nontrivially with Re Log z. Thus (6c) alone does not bound gradient-flow exit currents, their mass, or escaping mixed endpoint periods. The low-transfer Cartan branch is not covered by its central-meridian argument. Formula (8) below demonstrates that a top endpoint can still carry an exterior’s full rate through exact normalization; it is not legitimate to discard it from (6c).

4. The maximal-transfer Markov corner is exactly normalization

Let theta=x^(1-N), the ORIGINAL complete twist line. At k=d, (1) has only a=d,b=0, and its coefficient is kappa N. Therefore

E_top,+ at R=0 =kappa N |d,0><d,0|,
E_top,- at R=0 =kappa^(-1) N |0,d><0,d|.              (7)

Open an arbitrary original knot at the strand where a Markov cell is to be attached, BEFORE decomposing that cell. Its complete cut is C=K I. Keep the SAME original averaged cut on that strand and close the new head with its original g. Direct evaluation gives

(1/N)Tr[(C tensor I)(I tensor g)E_top,+]
                                      =kappa theta K,
(1/N)Tr[(C tensor I)(I tensor g)E_top,-]
                             =kappa^(-1)theta^(-1)K. (8)

Here g_0=theta and g_d=theta^(-1). These are exactly the full original positive/negative Markov-cell values, before the known framing line cancels their twist. Thus ALL other transfer slices of that attached cell sum to zero with this prescribed exterior and source. This is a source-level normalization reduction, not an operator equality or a permission to relocate a cut after insertion.

For comparison, its k=0 corner alone has value

kappa theta K/N *sum_(a=0)^d Q^(a^2)                  (9)

in the positive case. This can have the full exterior rate, despite its own unit operator norm. Such endpoint pieces are paired in the exact normalization (8), rather than assigned a strict gap one by one. This is consistent with regular finite-position endpoint action zero: that action statement does not control an escaping source or its complete exterior normalization.

The SAME phenomenon holds for an actually summed low endpoint packet, not only a single k=0 term. For every fixed R and odd N>2R+1 its normalized multiplier relative to kappa theta K is exactly

L_(N,R)=1/N sum_(k=0)^R F_k^(-1)
              sum_(b=0)^d Q^(b^2)(Q^(b+1);Q)_k^2.       (9a)

Indeed tracing (1) forces a=b+k. The factors x^(-2b), Q(b2) and g_b leave theta Q(b2). Terms b>d-k can be included because their displayed Pochhammer is exactly zero. No endpoint source has been changed in (9a).

Let G_N=sum_b Q(b2). At every odd N, |G_N|=sqrt(N): in its squared modulus the invertible change (a,b)->(a-b,a+b) gives sum_(u,v)Q^(uv)=N. For P_k(t)=(Qt;Q)_k^2=sum_s c_s(Q)t^s, completion of the same finite square gives exactly

sum_b Q^(b^2)P_k(Q^b)/G_N
                =sum_(s=0)^(2k)c_s(Q)Q^(-s^2/4),       (9b)

where division by four is modulo N. Along N=1 mod4, Q^(-1/4) tends to i; along N=3 mod4 it tends to -i. Since c_s(Q) tends to (-1)^s binom(2k,s), the right side for k>=1 tends respectively to 2^(2k-1)(1-i) or 2^(2k-1)(1+i). It is a fixed finite expression, so its error is O_R(1/N). Finally F_k=(-2pi*i/N)^k k!(1+O_R(1/N)). Thus the largest k=R term dominates the ACTUAL sum in (9a), and for every fixed R>=1,

|L_(N,R)|= [2^(2R-1)sqrt(2)/((2pi)^R R!)]
                   N^(R-1/2)(1+O_R(1/N)).              (9c)

For R=0 its magnitude is exactly N^(-1/2). These are polynomial, nonzero multipliers of the unchanged exterior knot period. A single Markov cell has no adjacent inverse pair to contract first. Hence a mixed low endpoint packet does NOT in general restore the missing normal gluing equation by itself. Exact complete-cell normalization is necessary even for this simplest source test. This does not assign the same behavior to an endpoint after that whole cell has been pruned.

5. A closed half-integer contour retaining physical support zeros

There is an actual finite-height quadrature starting from (1), without moving upper transfer limits or a free separate Euler endpoint. Use the normalized factorial Fhat of the exact interpolation and replace the factorial ratio in (1) by

Fhat(b+z)F_a/[Fhat(z)F_b Fhat(a-z)].                  (10)

It is holomorphic on a neighborhood of -1/2<=Re z<=d+1/2. To check this, Fhat is nonzero on -1<Re z<N. Its shift equation extends Fhat holomorphically to Re z>-1, with zeros at all integers N,N+1,…; the reciprocal extends holomorphically to Re z<N, with zeros at -1,-2,…. These continuations follow by shifting to the original nonzero strip and multiplying finite factors. At every integer z in [0,d], (10) is the actual coefficient and is zero if z>a or b+z>=N. Thus the forbidden physical tails vanish EXACTLY; they are not restored by declaring a new state support.

After multiplying by the specified port monomials and choosing their logarithms, the finite sum is its integral against pi cot(pi z)/(2pi*i) around a positively oriented rectangle with vertical edges -1/2,d+1/2 and finite nonzero heights. This contour contains precisely the original integer transfer poles 0,…,d. It preserves their endpoints and all corners, for any fixed bridge width and every fixed choice of the other original source variables. Fubini on these compact contours gives the simultaneous word identity.

No infinite-height type estimate or sharp full-source bound is inferred from this representation. Rounding an artificial meeting of contour pieces does not remove their enclosed integer poles. The quantitative normal packets which can presently be grouped without such a claim are (3)–(8); mixed intermediate/endpoint strata remain additional work.

The projected cotangent completion now provides a further OPERATOR-level normal extension: retaining the original physical output Fourier projections makes every added negative integer pole removable, even at simultaneous scalar factorial collisions. Its normal collar cost is exp[O(s log N)], uniformly over all physical inputs, hence subexponential for s<=N^alpha, alpha<1. It keeps the original nonnegative residues and supplies no sharp estimate for a bulk exterior.

The low normal packet has an explicit nonzero source current

The normal-scale equation can be calculated before a contour or saddle argument. Fix the literal a,b and write r_k for (1), with zero forbidden coefficients. Its exact successive ratio gives, at every index,

(1-Q^(k+1))r_(k+1)
   =c(1-Q^(b+k+1))(1-Q^(a-k))r_k,
c=xQ^(-b).                                          (11)

For F_R(t)=sum_(k=0)^R r_k t^k and sigma F(t)=F(Qt), define

L=(1-sigma)-ct(1-Q^(b+1)sigma)(1-Q^a sigma^(-1)).

Then the ENTIRE finite low packet has the exact sourced equation

L F_R=-c t^(R+1)(1-Q^(b+R+1))(1-Q^(a-R))r_R.       (12)

The full physical sum F_d has zero right side: either its last coefficient is already zero, or the actual support endpoint kills one displayed factor. Thus adjacent complementary transfer packets cancel exactly the SAME boundary current, with no arbitrary source normalization. Formula (12) holds inside the original full word with all other head variables and source coupons retained.

This current cannot be dropped on the bounded or sublinear normal scale. Suppose a/N,b/N stay in compact subsets of (0,1), R=o(N), and t stays in a compact nonzero annulus. For every k<=R, (11) gives

|r_(k-1)/(t r_k)|<=C R/N.

The two shifted denominator factors stay bounded away from zero; the numerator is at most 2pi k/N. Summing the resulting geometric tail proves uniformly for R>=1,

F_R(t)=r_R t^R(1+O(R/N)).                         (13)

For R=0 this equality is exact. Dividing the SOURCE in (12) by (13) therefore has nonzero limiting symbol

-ct(1-B)(1-A), A=lim Q^a, B=lim Q^b.              (14)

In particular it does not tend to zero. Omitting this source would incorrectly force the extra normal equation (1-A)(1-B)=0 and a central meridian. Closed half-integer contours do retain its original integer residues; rounding a contour corner alone does not authorize that omission. The operator size of the source in (12) is subexponential by (3), multiplied by a polynomial dimensional factor and the bounded displayed unit-clock factors. Its contraction with a bulk exterior can nevertheless have that exterior’s full rate, as the exact packet calculation (9a)–(9c) demonstrates.

Audit: independent original coefficients at both endpoint collars and signs, their normal-scale inequalities, whole-source Markov normalization corners, summed low packets, their sourced normal equation, and exact holomorphic support zeros in (10).
