Physical highest-block coordinates for the whole intertwiner

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

The physical highest block on one head is a pole-free coordinate on the entire actual intertwiner space near the complete meridian. Both endpoint columns and rows, and then every finite matrix block, are recovered from that one remaining-head operator. Unlike the constant coefficient after the prefix change of frame, this coordinate does not require inverting a resonant remaining Cartan eigenvalue.

The seed is an arbitrary Cartan-preserving operator, not a scalar vacuum value. Its freedom is essential. The theorem supplies an exact finite reconstruction, a coupled low-degree norm envelope, whole-cap bounds, and true meridian derivatives. It does not supply an analytic phase or a nonzero closing coefficient for an unspecified seed.

The sole complete direct is C, pinned below. We use its finite prefix operator and actual generator relations. All endpoint reconstruction, nilpotence, terminal compatibility and estimates are proved here.

5. True meridian jets and the original closing

6. Exact composition and the remaining seed

The coordinate (5) has an explicit transported product. Define Ua(H)=WH0aU_a(H)=W_H{}_{0a} and Va(H)=WHa0V_a(H)=W_H{}_{a0} by (12). Then H1⋆H2=∑a=0dUa(H1)Va(H2),WH1⋆H2=WH1WH2.(34) \boxed{\qquad H_1\star H_2=\sum_{a=0}^d U_a(H_1)V_a(H_2),\qquad W_{H_1\star H_2}=W_{H_1}W_{H_2}. \qquad}\qquad\text{(34)} This is the literal first-head matrix multiplication, with no reset of intermediate rows. It is associative by (5), has identity IUI_U, and retains all finite transfer indices. The original closing of a product is therefore exactly Φ0(WHL⋯WH1)=τ(HL⋆⋯⋆H1),τ(H)=Tr⁡U(K1−NH).(35) \Phi_0(W_{H_L}\cdots W_{H_1}) =\tau(H_L\star\cdots\star H_1),\qquad \tau(H)=\operatorname{Tr}_U(K^{1-N}H). \qquad\text{(35)} Differentiating this finite product differentiates Ua,VaU_a,V_a, the seeds, and the pivot, as in (30)–(33). Formula (35) supplies no positivity or nonvanishing for an invertible product.

For comparison only, C’s transformed constant term is B0=p0S−1Wi0B_0=p_0S^{-1}Wi_0. The exact relation to the present seed is B0=∑a=0d(−1)aQa(a−1)/2x−afaFaCa.(36) B_0=\sum_{a=0}^d \frac{(-1)^aQ^{a(a-1)/2}x^{-a}}{f_a}\,F^aC_a. \qquad\text{(36)} It is holomorphic at the complete point. Generically, where all bn=MKQ−n−M−1K−1b_n=MKQ^{-n}-M^{-1}K^{-1} are invertible, C reconstructs the whole operator from B0B_0. Thus (36) is then an invertible linear change between two copies of 𝒜U\mathcal A_U: injectivity follows from C’s recursion and surjectivity from finite equal dimensions and (5). The B0B_0 chart is also multiplicative there, because it is the constant coefficient of the F1F_1-Toeplitz normal form. The physical HH chart need not be multiplicative. At M=1M=1, (36) can be singular, as C’s explicit source coupon shows; the physical chart (5) stays nonsingular.

This distinction addresses the actual endpoint reconstruction problem but does not eliminate its seed. Every operator in 𝒜U\mathcal A_U really is allowed by the homogeneous total intertwiner equations. In particular those equations alone cannot specify the remaining-head phase, its zeros, or its prescribed pivotal trace. The physical HH generally does not commute with the remaining E,FE,F, so (5) cannot simply be iterated on HH to erase another head.

The initialized Gauss-type analytic propagation would additionally need actual estimates for this coupled remaining-head seed, or a separate source-specific identity fixing it. Equations (23)–(30) are genuine finite transport bounds, but do not provide a uniform analytic logarithm or a nonzero amplitude for an arbitrary seed. No geometric relative-cycle coefficient, full angular-period lower bound, or finite-exception conclusion is inferred.
