The exact degree-elevation residual and its selected endpoint source

Independent proof and source audit, 27 October 2026. Degree elevation maps the original head cut-Ward complex into the Fr source and preserves its original cut. Its missing operator channel has an explicit incidence polynomial. After the two Borel rows are contracted, one resonant endpoint remains, and its ACTUAL selected co-source differs exponentially from the uniform interval row. One-sided clock moments also have an explicit auxiliary correction.

These formulas identify concrete data to compute in the relative orbit co-source test. They do not construct the internal geometric Gaussian map or a selected-volume estimate.

1. An explicit polynomial detects exactly the missing channel

Use the actual divided Fr basis v_i of Sym^N and put bD(u,t)=ℓN(u)DψN(t),𝔬N(D)(u)=uNbD(u,−1/u)=∑i,j=0N(−1)j(Ni)DijuN+i−j. b_D(u,t)=\ell_N(u)D\psi_N(t),\qquad \mathfrak o_N(D)(u)=u^N b_D(u,-1/u) =\sum_{i,j=0}^N(-1)^j\binom NiD_{ij}u^{N+i-j}. \tag{1} This is a polynomial of degree at most 2N. The degree-elevation map satisfies ker⁡𝔬N=ℰNEnd⁡(SymN−1). \ker\mathfrak o_N=\mathcal E_N\operatorname{End}(\mathrm{Sym}^{N-1}). \tag{2} Indeed the symbol numerator of E_N(C) is (1+tu)b_C. Conversely, vanishing in (1) says that the polynomial b_D is divisible by the irreducible polynomial 1+tu. The quotient has degree at most N-1 in each variable, hence is the unique symbol numerator of an old head matrix. Formula (2) is an EXACT image criterion, stronger than vanishing of the two extreme corners alone.

Every degree 0,…,2N occurs from a matrix unit, so (1) is surjective. It realizes the full highest-spin quotient directly: 𝔬N([e,D])=∂u𝔬N(D),𝔬N([f,D])=(2Nu−u2∂u)𝔬N(D),𝔬N([h,D])=(2N−2u∂u)𝔬N(D),𝔬N(Ad⁡Q−JD)=QN𝔬N(D)(u/Q). \begin{split} \mathfrak o_N([e,D])&=\partial_u\mathfrak o_N(D),\\ \mathfrak o_N([f,D])&=(2Nu-u^2\partial_u)\mathfrak o_N(D),\\ \mathfrak o_N([h,D])&=(2N-2u\partial_u)\mathfrak o_N(D),\\ \mathfrak o_N(\operatorname{Ad}_{Q^{-J}}D) &=Q^N\mathfrak o_N(D)(u/Q). \tag{3} \end{split} For example the commutator f acts on the normalized symbol by (-partial_t-u^2 partial_u); it differentiates 1+tu by -u(1+tu). Restriction to t=-1/u gives exactly the second line of (3). The other formulas follow the same way or by matrix units.

For the original whole divided string gauge B_s of the head-Ward map, use instead 𝔬N,s(D)=𝔬N(Bs−1DBs). \mathfrak o_{N,s}(D)=\mathfrak o_N(B_s^{-1}DB_s). \tag{4} All B_s units and their normal derivatives remain present. A Fr Green/current matrix can use the old head co-source homotopy precisely when this polynomial vanishes, or after its nonzero remainder has been accounted for separately.

2. Independent contraction of the quotient current complex

In the quotient write weight m=-N,…,N, let B=ad_f raise weight, and let A=Ad_K-I, K=Q^-J. The actual two-row complex is d0=(A,B),d1(X,Y)=(Ad⁡K−Q−1)Y−Q−1BX. d_0=(A,B),\qquad d_1(X,Y)=(\operatorname{Ad}_K-Q^{-1})Y-Q^{-1}BX. \tag{5} It is a complex because Ad_K B=Q^-1 B Ad_K. Put a_m=Q^-m-1 and Bv_m=b_m v_(m+1). Every b_m for m<N is a nonzero integer in characteristic zero, hence a unit. For each m<N the three-term summand is R→(am,bm)R2→(−Q−1bm,Q−1am)R, R\xrightarrow{(a_m,b_m)}R^2 \xrightarrow{(-Q^{-1}b_m,Q^{-1}a_m)}R, which contracts using b_m. This works even when a_m=0, so the other root resonances at m=0 or -N leave no hidden classes. The remaining bottom degree-one arrow has coefficient QN-Q-1, a unit near a primitive N-th root for N>=2, and contracts as well. What remains is EXACTLY R→Q−N−1Rin degrees zero and one. R\xrightarrow{Q^{-N}-1}R \quad\text{in degrees zero and one}. \tag{6} The top generator may be taken to be f^N=N!E_(N,0). With Z=Q^N and delta=1-Z, its differential is Q^-N-1=delta/Z. Thus the first-normal Bockstein has coefficient PLUS ONE in the delta convention, or -N in the Log Q normal. There is no degree-two quotient homology.

This is the quotient theorem for the specified Fr MEMORY Cartan. It does not identify it with the original high-color quantum K. For the old physical clock C_Q=Q^S Z^R, the same endpoint changes Fr block R by N, so its conjugation character is Z^N instead. Those whole normal characters must not be interchanged because their root numerical values happen to coincide.

There is an equally explicit root-cycle classification. A matrix commuting with the regular nilpotent f is a polynomial in f. Cartan closure at Q_N removes every power f^j except j=0,N. Thus every scalar-coefficient root cycle is D=aI+cEN,0,c=DN,0. D=aI+cE_{N,0},\qquad c=D_{N,0}. \tag{6a} The old degree-elevation image contributes only the scalar part. With head coefficients the same statement holds entrywise; the actual selected head co-source must then be applied as well.

The full quotient construction is recorded in the complement Bockstein wall. The unit-fork calculation above is an independent proof audit.

3. The original selected endpoint co-source is not the uniform row

The uniform rank-one Fr cut and the selected coherent Fr cut give Φuni(EN,0)=1N+1,Φ+(EN,0)=2−N,Φuni(fN)=N!N+1. \Phi_{\mathrm{uni}}(E_{N,0})=\frac1{N+1},\qquad \Phi_+(E_{N,0})=2^{-N},\qquad \Phi_{\mathrm{uni}}(f^N)=\frac{N!}{N+1}. \tag{7} These are literal matrix pairings in the actual divided basis. Under B_s the frozen uniform row acquires the retained whole factor B_(N,s)/B_(0,s); transporting the row itself compensates it exactly. The root factor is one, but its normal coefficients cannot be erased.

E_(N,0) is Ward-closed at the root: it commutes with f, and its K conjugation character is Q^-N=1. Therefore the difference of the two co-sources in (7) is NOT Ward-exact on the full Fr target. Their ratio 2^N/(N+1) cannot be inferred to be a harmless algebraic normalization. The head-map homotopy applies on the image (2), where both extreme corners vanish; it does not extend to this additional endpoint class without its retained source. An exact source gauge can still compare the two descriptions by conjugating ALL currents and the endpoint generator too. It does not justify freezing that generator while replacing its co-source. In the coordinates (6a), the two root periods are respectively a+c/(N+1) and a+2^-N c.

This source character is visible as a whole function, not just as a list of matrix values. For the actual paired Borel coupon e_(alpha,m)=psi_alpha lambda_m, 𝔬N(eα,m)(u)=[(1+u)(1+m)u−(1−m)2]N,Φm(EN,0)=((1−m)/2)N. \mathfrak o_N(e_{\alpha,m})(u) =\left[(1+u)\frac{(1+m)u-(1-m)}2\right]^N, \qquad\Phi_m(E_{N,0})=((1-m)/2)^N. \tag{8} The normalized scale alpha cancels; its UNNORMALIZED power line alpha^N must still be retained in the original source object. At the selected m=0 the first polynomial is 2-N(u2-1)^N. The actual interval average over m=-1,…,1 instead integrates the second character to 1/(N+1). This explains both agreement on closed scalar cuts and disagreement on the new endpoint. The whole retained-weight interval comparison in the global Cech source keeps its density; it does not assert that these two endpoint co-sources are equal or Ward-homotopic on the entire target.

There is a head-matrix version for the actual coupled coupon e_D. Write s(i)=i for i<N and s(N)=0. Its Fr blocks are (eD)ij=2−N(Nj)Es(i),s(j). (e_D)_{ij}=2^{-N}\binom Nj E_{s(i),s(j)}. Consequently 𝔬N(eD)(u)=2−NuNa(u)b(u)t, \mathfrak o_N(e_D)(u)=2^{-N}u^N a(u)b(u)^t, \tag{9} where a_0=1+u^N, b_0=1+(-1)^N u^-N, and for 1<=S<N, a_S=binom(N,S)u^S, b_S=(-1)^S binom(N,S)u^-S. This expression is a polynomial despite its factored Laurent notation. Its bottom and top coefficients are respectively (-1)N2-N E_00 and 2^-N E_00. In particular the ACTUAL selected functional satisfies ΦeD(EN,0⊗H)=2−NH00. \Phi_{e_D}(E_{N,0}\otimes H) =2^{-N}H_{00}. \tag{10} The surviving source cannot be replaced by the uniform interval row merely because both return the same closed knot scalar.

4. Exact one-sided clock and Green moment corrections

Split E=E_0+E_1 using its two terms, so (E_1C)ij=(i/N)C(i-1,j-1). For every retained scalar function g on the indicated finite spectra, E(g(Jhead)C)=g(JN)E0(C)+g(JN−1)E1(C),E(Cg(Jhead))=E0(C)g(JN)+E1(C)g(JN−1). \begin{split} E(g(J_{\mathrm{head}})C) &=g(J_N)E_0(C)+g(J_N-1)E_1(C),\\ E(Cg(J_{\mathrm{head}})) &=E_0(C)g(J_N)+E_1(C)g(J_N-1). \tag{11} \end{split} The functions at the extra argument -1 may be chosen arbitrarily where their multiplier is zero; they must be regular wherever that multiplier is nonzero. These identities follow directly from the row and column indices. In particular E(wJC)=wJN{E(C)+(w−1−1)E1(C)}, E(w^J C)=w^{J_N}\{E(C)+(w^{-1}-1)E_1(C)\}, \tag{12} and the polynomial moment correction is exactly the backward difference [p(J_N-1)-p(J_N)]E_1(C). Ad-clock covariance is strict because the same auxiliary offset cancels between row and column. One-sided Green moments do not have that cancellation. For normalized degree-h moments the backward difference contains the explicit powers N^-s, s=1,…,h; they are retained before any period/character estimate.

5. Whole word strictification retains a genuine auxiliary source

There is a direct positive strictification of (11). On Sym^(N-1) tensor C^2 define i+vk=vk⊗u0+vk−1⊗u1,p+(vk⊗u0)=(N−k)vk/N,p+(vk⊗u1)=(k+1)vk+1/N. \begin{split} i_+v_k&=v_k\otimes u_0+v_{k-1}\otimes u_1,\\ p_+(v_k\otimes u_0)&=(N-k)v_k/N,\\ p_+(v_k\otimes u_1)&=(k+1)v_{k+1}/N. \end{split} Then p_+i_+=I and E(C)=p_+(C tensor I)i_+. The complementary projector is I-i_+p_+; it is the Sym^(N-2) channel. EXACTLY, E(CD)−E(C)E(D)=p+(C⊗I)(I−i+p+)(D⊗I)i+. E(CD)-E(C)E(D) =p_+(C\otimes I)(I-i_+p_+)(D\otimes I)i_+. \tag{13} Thus the full auxiliary operator algebra is multiplicative and preserves an ordered actual word, while a product of upper compressions omits the displayed lower-channel excursions. The original head number is J_total minus the auxiliary bit; its two blocks have total degrees J_N and J_(N-2)+1. This is the operator explanation of (11)–(12).

An exact source-preserving FINAL CUT choice even has both external ends in the upper channel. Set Ψ=i+𝟏N+1=𝟏N⊗(u0+u1),Λ=𝟏N+1tN+1p+. \Psi=i_+\mathbf1_{N+1}=\mathbf1_N\otimes(u_0+u_1), \qquad\Lambda=\frac{\mathbf1_{N+1}^t}{N+1}p_+. It pairs with Psi to one, annihilates the lower channel, and gives Lambda(C tensor I)Psi=sum(C_ij)/N for EVERY head C. The same holds for a full ordered word with all internal excursions kept. A product auxiliary dual is a different source. Closed/pivotal strands require their actual transported caps and pivot operators; the unweighted final-cut identity does not substitute for those. This is an auxiliary matrix-word extension of the old head R, not an equality with the existing actual high-integral-color R.

6. The precise remaining relative source calculation

For the norm-orbit test Lambda_rel omega, the new head map removes the cut K/F rows on its image by its actual co-source homotopy. An arbitrary Fr-valued current first has the explicit remainder (1)/(4). The quotient contraction (6) reduces its cohomological source obstruction to the one resonant endpoint; its selected weight is (7)–(10), NOT automatically the uniform row.

This does not prove that the two INTERNAL framing Green currents land in that head image, or that their remaining relative endpoint and logarithmic carry classes cancel. A map of those actual currents must retain (11), all lower excursions in (13), the physical versus memory-clock distinction, charged normal head neighbors, the original chirp and full peripheral line. The explicit residual polynomials and endpoint coefficient are now finite tests for that map, rather than a general group-theoretic identification or an assumed scalar normalization.
