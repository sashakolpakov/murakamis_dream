Exact Hardy replacement of the internal finite-head projectors

28 October 2026. The original scalar Euler/Gaussian network admits an exact simplification: every internal finite Dirichlet kernel can be replaced by an ordinary Hardy Cauchy kernel, provided its existing finite factorial and Gaussian kernels are retained. This holds for every signed braid word and its full original source. With the proved fused plat source, the boundary is monomial as well.

Thus the Dirichlet numerator branches are unnecessary in this presentation of the complete word. This does not remove the actual finite Euler/Gaussian endpoint sectors. Their support is essential to the proof. Nor does the theorem identify a new geometric edge’s three indices with a charged degree.

The exact audit and results check the full original signed crossing matrices and original whole-word pivotal cuts while allowing nonzero intermediate indices beyond the head.

1. The actual operators, extended without an upper head projection

Let 𝒫=ℂ[z]\mathcal P=\mathbb C[z], and let HNH_N be its subspace of degree below NN. On 𝒫\mathcal P use the unilateral shifts

J+za=za+1,J−za={za−1a>0,0a=0. J_+z^a=z^{a+1},\qquad J_-z^a=\begin{cases}z^{a-1}&a>0,\\0&a=0.\end{cases}

They have no upper cutoff. Define the ORIGINAL finite polynomials

uN(t)=∑h=0N−1thFh,vN(t)=∑h=0N−1(−1)hQh(h−1)/2thFh,Fh=(Q;Q)h. u_N(t)=\sum_{h=0}^{N-1}\frac{t^h}{F_h},\qquad v_N(t)=\sum_{h=0}^{N-1} \frac{(-1)^hQ^{h(h-1)/2}t^h}{F_h}, \qquad F_h=(Q;Q)_h.

On a pair put

U−∞=uN(x−1J−⊗J+),V−∞=vN(x−1J−⊗J+), U_-^\infty=u_N(x^{-1}J_-\otimes J_+),\quad V_-^\infty=v_N(x^{-1}J_-\otimes J_+),

and define U+∞,V+∞U_+^\infty,V_+^\infty by reversing the two shifts. Only the lower polynomial wall is imposed.

Extend each existing finite diagonal kernel by zero outside its ORIGINAL active-head support:

𝖥za=Faza,𝖥−za=Fa−1za(0≤a<N),𝖥za=𝖥−za=0(a≥N). \begin{split} \mathsf Fz^a&=F_az^a,\quad \mathsf F^{-}z^a=F_a^{-1}z^a &&(0\le a<N),\\ \mathsf Fz^a&=\mathsf F^{-}z^a=0 &&(a\ge N). \end{split} \tag{1}

The superscript minus here is the zero-extended finite inverse diagonal, not an inverse on 𝒫\mathcal P. Likewise retain 𝖣,𝖣−\mathsf D,\mathsf D^{-} with their original nonzero entries for 0≤a<N0\le a<N and zero entries above the head. Spectator heads are identities on all nonnegative indices.

No new cutoff has been added to (1): these are exactly the finite scalar kernels already present in the literal network.

2. The support lemma removes the internal upper projections

Let PP project onto HN⊗HNH_N\otimes H_N and put

C∞=𝖥2U−∞𝖥1𝖥2−,C¯∞=𝖥2𝖥1−V−∞𝖥2−. C^\infty=\mathsf F_2U_-^\infty\mathsf F_1\mathsf F_2^{-}, \qquad \overline C^\infty= \mathsf F_2\mathsf F_1^{-}V_-^\infty\mathsf F_2^{-}. \tag{2}

These are the original strict fusion and inverse-fusion words. Their support has the following exact properties:

C∞=PC∞P,C¯∞=PC¯∞,C∞|HN2=C,C¯∞|HN2=C−1. C^\infty=PC^\infty P,\qquad \overline C^\infty=P\overline C^\infty,\qquad C^\infty|_{H_N^2}=C,\qquad \overline C^\infty|_{H_N^2}=C^{-1}. \tag{3}

For the first operator the two right diagonal factors kill inputs outside the head. Its transfer only lowers the first index, so that index stays in the head; the left 𝖥2\mathsf F_2 kills every second-index overflow. For the second operator the two left diagonals enforce its output support. On a head input, an overflow generated by V−∞V_-^\infty is precisely the second-index overflow killed by 𝖥2\mathsf F_2. These are the same coefficient sums as the finite fusion and its finite inverse.

Keep the original Cartan scalar κ\kappa, and define

R+∞=κC∞𝖣U+∞C¯∞,R−∞=κ−1C∞V+∞𝖣−C¯∞. \begin{split} R_+^\infty&=\kappa C^\infty \mathsf D U_+^\infty\overline C^\infty,\\ R_-^\infty&=\kappa^{-1}C^\infty V_+^\infty\mathsf D^{-}\overline C^\infty. \end{split} \tag{4}

Then for the actual inclusion i:HN2→𝒫2i:H_N^2\to\mathcal P^2,

R±∞i=iR±. \boxed{\qquad R_\pm^\infty i=iR_\pm.\qquad} \tag{5}

Indeed C¯∞i=iC−1\overline C^\infty i=iC^{-1}. The transfer U+∞U_+^\infty or V+∞V_+^\infty can overflow only its first index. Before the next transfer, C∞C^\infty applies its input support PP, which kills that overflow. Its surviving part is exactly the truncated U+U_+ or V+V_+ in the original strict factorization.

The two extensions in (4) need not be inverses on the whole polynomial space. Their restrictions to the original head are the prescribed signed inverse crossings. That is sufficient for an arbitrary signed word.

The proof is unchanged for unequal colors in the actual typed strict formula: Cx−1C_x^{-1} on input, CyC_y on output, the original Dx,yD_{x,y}, and the stated x−1x^{-1} or y−1y^{-1} Euler argument. Their coefficients change; the support argument does not. All unrolled common Cartan and external normalization lines multiply the same two realizations.

Every nonempty upper-tail branch vanishes in the complete crossing

One may replace ANY subset of the original internal finite projections by Hardy projections, not merely all of them at once. To see this, follow the three transfer stages in (4), in traversal order.

  1. The inverse-fusion V−∞V_-^\infty can overflow only head two. The following 𝖥2\mathsf F_2 kills that overflow before the central transfer.
  2. The central U+∞U_+^\infty or V+∞V_+^\infty can overflow only head one. The input 𝖥1\mathsf F_1 of the final fusion kills it before the final U−∞U_-^\infty transfer.
  3. The final U−∞U_-^\infty can overflow only head two, which the last 𝖥2\mathsf F_2 kills.

The remaining factors between these barriers are diagonal; they cannot return an overflow to the head. Spectator heads remain in their original finite heads. Earlier killing by any additional finite projection therefore removes only paths that the barrier would already kill.

On Laurent inputs the exact decomposition is

ΠN=Π+−Π≥N,Π≥N(z,w)=(z/w)N1−z/w. \Pi_N=\Pi_+-\Pi_{\ge N},\qquad \Pi_{\ge N}(z,w)=\frac{(z/w)^N}{1-z/w}. \tag{5a}

In the nested Hardy chamber, expand the original finite projection factors using (5a). Every term containing at least one upper-tail factor has zero full-crossing action on the original head. The same assertion holds for boundary fusion factors and for every original full word by (6).

This is an ordered barrier identity. It is not an assertion that an arbitrary endpoint coupon or Ward current is zero. Inserting a new operator between a transfer and its barrier can invalidate it.

3. Whole-word and source identity

For bb heads write ib:HN⊗b→𝒫⊗bi_b:H_N^{\otimes b}\to\mathcal P^{\otimes b}. Embedding the two-head operators of (4) at every letter gives for EVERY signed braid word

W∞(β)ib=ibW(β). \boxed{\quad W^\infty(\beta)i_b=i_bW(\beta).\quad} \tag{6}

This follows by induction from (5); no intermediate finite-head projector is used. Every original input/output entry, pivotal partial trace, marked source, peripheral function and external framing line is consequently preserved.

The same inclusion intertwines any original finite-head coupon inserted BETWEEN complete crossings, provided its extension restricts to that coupon on the head. This includes the original Hopf currents, so the full Ward identities and their original source pairings are preserved. It does not assert the same statement for an arbitrary coupon inserted between the elementary factors of one crossing.

The original closed-braid boundary still has its prescribed finite pivotal sum. Alternatively use the already proved full fused 2k2k-plat formula

$$ P_{\beta,N}=\chi_{\beta,N}N^{1-k} \langle e_{\rm out},B_\beta e_{\rm in}\rangle. \tag{7} $$

Here every input pair is (d,0)(d,0); every ordinary output pair is (0,d)(0,d); the marked output pair is (d,0)(d,0). Expand BβB_\beta into its actual pair fusions, signed crossings and retained original scalar lines. Apply (3)–(6). The result is exactly (7) on the polynomial space, with monomial input and coefficient-extraction output.

The Ward corner in (7) is the theorem for the FULL assembled intertwining word. It is not applied separately to a Hardy, cyclic, or sewing summand.

4. The scalar integral has no finite Dirichlet numerators

Let

K+(t)=11−t,|t|<1. K_+(t)=\frac1{1-t},\qquad |t|<1.

This is the Hardy reproducing kernel for nonnegative powers. For example the transfer kernel on two heads is now

∏jK+(zj/wj)uN(x−1w2/w1), \prod_j K_+(z_j/w_j)\, u_N(x^{-1}w_2/w_1), \tag{8}

with vNv_N and the reversed argument for the other transfers. The diagonal kernels are exactly the existing finite ones:

𝖥−:uN(z/w),𝖥:N(z/w)N−1vN(Qw/z),𝖣±:Γ±,N(z/w). \mathsf F^{-}:u_N(z/w),\qquad \mathsf F:N(z/w)^{N-1}v_N(Qw/z),\qquad \mathsf D^{\pm}:\Gamma_{\pm,N}(z/w). \tag{9}

Their spectators contribute K+K_+ instead of KNK_N. Thus all internal factors (1−tN)/(1−t)(1-t^N)/(1-t) are replaced by 1/(1−t)1/(1-t), without approximating or discarding a period. The proof is the complete ordered support identity above, not replacement of one split reproducing residue in isolation.

For monomial boundary data ea,ece_a,e_c and a word with LL elementary factors, its entry is

CT⁡z0,…,zL(z0)a(zL)−c∏ℓ=1LKℓ∞(zℓ,zℓ−1). \operatorname{CT}_{z^0,\ldots,z^L} (z^0)^a(z^L)^{-c} \prod_{\ell=1}^{L}K^\infty_\ell(z^\ell,z^{\ell-1}). \tag{10}

Choose nested positive radii |zjℓ|<|zjℓ−1||z^\ell_j|<|z^{\ell-1}_j|. Every Hardy series converges there; every other factor is a finite Laurent polynomial. The CT is consequently a literal rational Cauchy integral. There are a fixed number of variables for a fixed plat, independent of NN.

No cycle made solely of Hardy inequalities is being imposed: the boundary in (10) is monomial. The initial/final finite indices and every scalar in (7) remain the original ones.

5. Exact gain and the remaining geometric charge

This eliminates a specific exponential branch family from the original scalar Cauchy presentation: the finite Dirichlet numerators and their artificial split poles. Nonzero intermediate overflow routes are allowed and then killed by the support of the original diagonal kernels.

The earlier source-visible coupon X−X+X_-X_+ has no intervening finite diagonal barrier: an upper excursion can return before it is tested. Its nonzero endpoint correction is therefore consistent with this theorem. Likewise the cyclic-sewing and Fermat-cap walls are different relative terms and have not been deleted here.

It does not eliminate the finite endpoints of uN,vNu_N,v_N or Γ±,N\Gamma_{\pm,N}. The diagonal support in (1) is essential; replacing those kernels by unrelated nonfinite symbols is outside the theorem. The Hardy poles in (8) are also genuine reproducing poles; contour transport must retain their actual residues.

All elementary transfers preserve total INTEGER degree. Hence (10) retains the original total plat degree k(N−1)k(N-1), and after ordinary caps the remaining pair has the exact affine charge D+1=ND+1=N from the charged cap theorem. The theorem does not impose that pair charge at every intermediate slice: crossings between pairs move degree between them.

What is still missing is the source-preserving identification of a geometric Pachner edge’s three angle clocks with that augmented charged class. The Hardy replacement supplies a simpler exact whole-word integral on which to seek it; it does not prove h1+h2+h3=Nh_1+h_2+h_3=N for an unassigned geometric edge or select the hyperbolic critical cycle.
