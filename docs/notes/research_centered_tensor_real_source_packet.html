The original three-boundary source on the real unit cube

21 November 2026. Research note. Earlier finals and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The centered multiplication tensor has a nonzero leading asymptotic on every compact subset of the real cube (−1,1)3(-1,1)^3. Both its phase and amplitude are explicit. This is an estimate of the entire original three-boundary source, with its first Casimir normals and original closing retained.

The source selects two unit Fourier charges. Their contributions add. The zero-charge integral vanishes exactly, and all remaining charges, including their endpoint terms, have a summable uniform bound. No geometric cycle coefficient is assigned by hand.

The result concerns real generating parameters. It does not assert a fixed complex neighborhood, a connection across Stokes regions, or a volume identity after arbitrary short fillings.

7. Scope and complete proof sources

The theorem estimates one universal joint source, not its absolute-value envelope. Its nonzero coefficient comes from the prescribed finite source after its zero-charge cancellation, unit-charge addition and endpoint control.

The proof uses real phase and positivity of (21). For complex generating parameters, endpoint terms can have a different exponential scale. No fixed complex neighborhood, global zero-free domain, or continuation of the asymptotic through such a connection is proved. The estimate does not place the generating variables of an arbitrary short-filled geometric component in this real cube.

All repeated-root data, both original parities, the odd simple node and the explicit scalar factors in (4) remain present. No generic-qq or peripheral family is inferred from this fixed-root statement.
