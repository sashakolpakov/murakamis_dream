The actual degree cycle across the finite-head wall

30 October 2026. The specified generic exceptional lift can be used on a larger original physical-grid residue cycle by adding explicitly proved source-null local tori. This is a statement about PERIODS, not multivariable removability of its poles. Moving that enlarged cycle still meets genuine channel-row divisors. Their pole coefficients, their cotangent finite parts, and their first source-normal densities are calculated below with the actual lattice step and original complement retained.

The theorem does not deform the whole cycle to the geometric saddle or prove a nonzero global coefficient. It supplies the exact boundary currents that such a deformation must retain.

1. The original meromorphic source at a chosen matching cut

Use the specified generic lift, with N>=3, d=N-1, L=2a, m=d-a, s=d-2a, and

zeta=q0 exp(epsilon), q0=exp(pi i/N),
P=pi i/Log zeta, lambda=2 Log zeta.

In the upper ordered physical sector u<=v<=t_target<=d put

h=v-u, ell=t_target-v, sigma=d-h, r=a-h.

The actual generic continued coefficient, including its actual outside contraction when desired, has the form

F(epsilon,h,z)=p_L(epsilon,a-h) E(epsilon,h,z),          (1)

where

p_L(epsilon,r)=exp(pi i r) zeta^[r(L-r-1)]
            Fhat_P(r) Fhat_P(L-r)/F_L(zeta).

Here E is NOT an assigned amplitude. It is exactly the two specified large white numerators H_m, their original scalar and high adapter, the actual physical inverse cups, the face factor if present, and the unchanged complement and source mark. All are the factors in the cited lift. Statements at a single channel divisor below are on charts where these other factors are jointly holomorphic in the index variables AND epsilon on a common germ. Additional source divisors must be kept as additional residues, not discarded by (1).

The original scalar source has the product-cotangent realization in the exact factorial interpolation, section 5, now with the moving-period factorial. Use the ACTUAL free integral lattice coordinates x after its closure constraints. Its initial cycle is the sum of small ordered product tori around the allowed lattice states. The original finite sum, all outside phases, and exposed mark are retained. No degree is assumed independent merely because it has been named h. For n_free lattice variables every period below uses the normalization (2pi i)^(-n_free); each original product cotangent residue therefore equals its literal summand.

The opposite ordered sector is left unchanged unless its own literal adapter is applied. Enlarging only the sector above already preserves the complete source when it is added to that unchanged remainder.

2. Forbidden physical grid tori are source-null at generic q

Consider a physical grid point with h>a, all four ports legal, and all other source factors regular. Then 0<=sigma=d-h<m. The two large numerators in (1) have magnetic rows

i_1=sigma-u=d-v, i_2=ell,
0<=i_1,i_2<=sigma.

Both H_m(Qi,Q(sigma-i)) vanish IDENTICALLY for generic zeta. Indeed each kth Newton summand contains the factors

product_(j<k)(Q^i-Q^j)
   *product_(j<m-k)(Q^(sigma-i)-Q^j).

Either k>i or m-k>sigma-i, because m>sigma. This proves the zero before root specialization. All remaining denominator factors are regular in the specified generic germ.

Let y=x-x0 be the primitive free lattice coordinates near this grid point. If h is nonconstant on that lattice chart, its channel pole has equation l(y)=h-h0, a nonzero integral linear form. Each numerator is in the maximal ideal (y), so their product is in (y)^2. The channel cup has at most a simple pole on l=0 in this range. Hence the local source has the form

F=A(epsilon,y)/l(y)+B(epsilon,y),
A in (y)^2, B in (y)^2.                              (2)

Multiplication by a holomorphic original complement preserves this property. It holds identically in epsilon, including every genuine normal coefficient.

Choose an index with nonzero coefficient of l, and order the local torus radii so its term dominates the sum of the others. The torus then avoids l=0. Expand 1/l by the corresponding convergent geometric series. Every resulting Laurent monomial in F has TOTAL degree at least one. Each factor pi cot(pi y_j) is y_j^-1 times a power series whose nonconstant terms have positive even degree. Thus the coefficient of product_j y_j^-1 is zero. The original local period is exactly

(2pi i)^(-n_free) integral_(that ordered torus)
                     F product_j pi cot(pi x_j) dx_j=0.
                                                           (3)

This proof does not assert that F is holomorphic at the crossing of the divisors. It computes the actual ordered residue. It applies to every epsilon in the generic germ, so all required q normals of (3) vanish as well.

Consequently one may add all such forbidden physical-grid tori to the original supported cycle: their periods are zero term by term. The resulting finite sum of periods still equals the WHOLE original source, after the unchanged other sector and every original matching state are included. This supplies a generic counterpart of the root entire kernel’s enlarged grid, without falsely identifying their off-grid functions.

If h is constant on a free-lattice chamber and equals a forbidden value, the original contribution is identically zero. Its meromorphic lift may not be restricted to that polar chamber by substituting 0/0. Such a chamber is retained as zero; (2),(3) do not manufacture an independent degree direction or a deformation out of it.

3. Exact pole and finite-part coefficients of the original cup

Write h0=a+k, 1<=k<=m, so r=-k. The moving-period recurrence gives

R_k:=Res_(r=-k) Fhat_P(r)
   =-1/[lambda product_(j=1)^(k-1)(1-zeta^(-2j))].       (4)

To compute its finite part, let g0=partial_z Log Fhat_P(0). Then

Fhat_P(-k+z)=R_k/z+R_k c_k+O(z),
c_k=g0-lambda/2
         +lambda sum_(j=1)^(k-1)zeta^(-2j)/(1-zeta^(-2j)).
                                                           (5)

Equations (4),(5) follow by writing Fhat_P(z)/product_(j=0)(k-1)(1-zeta(2(z-j))) and expanding its single zero factor. No reflection with moving endpoint P-1 is substituted for the fixed original L.

Put

M_k=exp(-pi i k) zeta^[-k(L+k-1)],
B_k=Fhat_P(L+k), B'_k=partial_z Fhat_P(L+k).

The CUP coefficients in the r coordinate are

A_k=R_k M_k B_k/F_L,
Bcup_k=R_k M_k/F_L
   *{[c_k+pi i+(Log zeta)(L+2k-1)]B_k-B'_k}.          (6)

Thus p_L(r)=A_k/(r+k)+Bcup_k+O(r+k). Formula (6) keeps B_k and B’_k separately; it does not divide by the complementary factorial when that factorial has a root zero.

In h coordinates the FULL actual lift (1) has

F=A_h/(h-h0)+B_h+O(h-h0),
A_h=-A_k E(h0),
B_h=Bcup_k E(h0)-A_k partial_h E(h0).                  (7)

These are explicit original-source densities, including derivatives of the actual complement. Replacing E by one, or by a selected local eigenvalue, changes the boundary term.

4. Primitive lattice step and the exact contour correction

Fix the other primitive coordinates and write a varying degree as h=A0 x+B0, with A0 a nonzero integer. The original one-form is

pi cot(pi x) dx
  =(pi/A0) cot(pi(h-B0)/A0) dh.                       (8)

If x0=(h0-B0)/A0 is an integer, its Laurent residue in h is ONE and its constant term is zero. The crossed residue of the original weighted lift is therefore B_h, not A_h. If x0 is not an integer, the cotangent is regular and the crossed residue is

(pi/A0) cot(pi x0) A_h.                               (9)

More generally a retained analytic weight with expansion w=c_(-1)/(h-h0)+c0+… gives c_(-1)B_h+c0 A_h. This records the Jacobian, possible nonprimitive step, and the coalescence of the lattice and channel poles.

For two oriented contours bounding a finite annular chain on this chart, Cauchy’s theorem gives the EXACT difference of their source periods as the sum of (7)–(9), with the actual crossing orientations, plus the residues of EVERY other crossed source divisor. On a family of charts the same identity is integrated against all retained remaining source cycles. Coincident additional divisors require their actual iterated or multivariate residues; they are not covered by a single-wall formula. The local ordered tori in section 2 specify the initial lattice intersections, rather than silently changing their residue convention.

This is a concrete correction to a proposed degree transport. It does not assert that any chosen geometric contour is homologous to the original one, or that a noncompact cap has negligible integral.

5. The late-wall first normal is the actual root density

For k>=s+1, n=L+k lies in [N,2N-1]. The complementary finite factorial has exactly one root zero. From the original integer product,

partial_epsilon F_n|0=-2N^2 F_(n-N)(q0).

The root functional equation with a full N-step gives

partial_z Fhat_N(n)=-2pi i F_(n-N)(q0).

Consequently (6) yields

A_k(0)=0,
partial_epsilon A_k|0=P'(0) Bcup_k(0),
P'(0)=-N^2/(pi i).                                  (10)

On these walls the root cup is regular. If the actual other factors in (1) are holomorphic, (10) proves the full source identity

Res_(r=-k) partial_epsilon F|0=P'(0) F(0,-k),
Res_(h=a+k) partial_epsilon F|0=-P'(0) F(0,a+k).       (11)

This includes the actual outside coefficient, rather than only the scalar cup. It is a POLE coefficient. At a resonant cotangent wall the complete boundary period still uses the finite part in (7),(8).

For the early walls 1<=k<=s, sigma=m-k belongs to [a,m-1]. Each actual large numerator has the root factor P_s(T)=product_(j=a)(m-1)(T-Qj), with T=Q^sigma. At this fixed channel wall each is O(epsilon). Their product is O(epsilon^2), whereas the cup pole coefficient is a root unit. Thus the full lift has ZERO first-normal pole residue there. This does not say its cotangent finite part vanishes at an arbitrary off-grid point. At original physical grid points the stronger generic source-null theorem (3) applies.

Finally the original exposed trace normal is nu_N=2N(-1)N/(q0-q0-1). Hence

P'(0)/nu_N=(-1)^(N+1) N sin(pi/N)/pi.                 (12)

Its modulus is bounded away from zero and tends to one, at both parities. Thus an isolated late-wall source-normal residue has an explicit order-one coefficient times its actual root density (and the opposite sign in h). This coefficient is not the global geometric intersection number, the complete cotangent finite part, or a proof that the surrounding coherent contraction is nonzero.

Focused verification: actual numerator and boundary audit and saved results. The audit checks the literal large-numerator generic zeros, both wall ranges, primitive cotangent weights, ordered diagonal-wall residues, the original moving-period cup Laurent coefficients, and the actual exposed-normal normalization. Numerical checks of local densities do not substitute for the ordered-cycle proof in section 2.

Additional divisors, constant-degree chambers, noncompact caps, and the global geometric intersection remain outside the conclusion.

The residue argument does not establish removability of omitted divisors or identify a globally contributing cycle.
