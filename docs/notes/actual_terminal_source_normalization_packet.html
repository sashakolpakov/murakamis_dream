An original cup slide exposes an amplified normalization packet

29 October 2026. The problematic vector in the terminal-Gram transport test is reached by a FIXED original braid prefix applied to the original cup. The complete recorded packet after one further crossing has positive exponential energy, although its original closed scalar is a unit phase. There is also an explicit coherent factorial divergence which removes this amplification at the normalization closure. This is a finite Ward packet with its endpoint retained, rather than a deletion of individual channels or a new positive trace.

The result concerns the present full-circle/recorded-color metric. It does not rule out a nondiagonal coherent metric, a shrinking arc, or a sharp estimate on a source after coherent normalization pruning. The backward Ward disk estimate now proves subexponential shrinking-arc energy for this SAME original normalization packet, including its whole germ and specified sublinear clock-frequency exteriors.

1. A literal source identity on every input color

Use the original complete multiplicity conventions:

N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d=-q^-1,
F_j=(Q;Q)_j, kappa=q^(d^2/2), kappa^2=x^d,
W=(V tensor V)_(integer degree d), e_j=|j,d-j>,
p_j=N x^(j-d), ell_j=x^j/N.

Let R=R_12 be the FULL original positive pair crossing on W and R_23 its adjacent complete multiplicity operator. The original highest chart, applied to the literal physical crossing, gives

(R_23 p)_j=kappa N x^(j-d) F_(d-j),
(R^(-1)e_0)_j=kappa^(-1)x^j F_(d-j).

Consequently the exact source-orbit identities are

R_23 p=N R^(-1)e_0,
R R_23 p=N e_0.                                      (1)

There is no arbitrary inserted state in (1). The Cartan identity kappa2=xd fixes the coefficient N at every even or composite level. The negative coefficient is the literal original inverse crossing, not a unitary adjoint.

For completeness the second identity has a direct finite-binomial proof. Multiplying the original positive coefficients yields

(R R_23 p)_i
  =N x^(-i) Q^[i(d-i)] F_i
       sum_(k=0)^i (Q^(-i);Q)_k Q^(ik)/F_k
  =N x^(-i) Q^[i(d-i)] F_i (1;Q)_i
  =N delta_(i,0).                                    (2)

Every factorial denominator in this calculation has index below N. The equality is the highest column of the all-input physical cup slide

(R_12 R_23)(p_12 tensor I_V)=I_V tensor p_23.           (3)

Indeed both sides intertwine the ACTUAL coproduct. Applying its F^a/F_a to the verified highest column proves (3) on every original |a>, 0<=a<N. Thus (3) is an operator identity and can be tensored with arbitrary spectator ports. No scalar-knot Schur argument or replacement of the marked row is used.

2. Complete terminal packets and their original closed scalars

Keep the full terminal operator

T=N^(-1) X R P_rev R, X=diag(x^j),
P_0=1, P_j=(1-Q^j)^2 P_(j-1)+Q^j,
P_rev=diag(P_(d-j)), P_d=sum_(j=0)^d F_j.              (4)

Its physical-projector rows sum to the original ordinary/marked whole co-source after the two terminal crossings. This is the SAME complete source as in the positive terminal Gram. Retain the original external factor chi_N on every side below.

The two actual prefixes [2] and [2,1] give the exact vector formulas

T R_23 p=kappa P_d e_d,
b:=T R R_23 p
  =x^(-d) (Q^(-j) conjugate(F_j))_(j=0)^d.             (5)

To check the first formula, write T R(-1)=N(-1)X R P_rev, use (1) and R e_0=kappa x^(-d)e_d. For the second use

(R e_d)_j=kappa N x^(j-d)/F_(d-j),
F_(d-j) conjugate(F_j)=N,
x^2=Q^(-1), kappa^2=x^d.

These retain all coherent Green summation; P_j is never replaced by its absolute envelope. In particular

||T R_23p||^2=|P_d|^2<=9N^4,
||T R R_23p||^2=Z_N:=sum_(j=0)^d |F_j|^2.             (6)

The first bound is the original endpoint theorem in the Green-capacity proof. It is not a bound on the sum of absolute factorials.

Both original closed scalars remain explicit:

1^T T R_23p=kappa P_d,
1^T b=x^(-d) Q.                                     (7)

For the last equality, Q^(j+1)F_j=F_j-F_(j+1), with F_N=0, implies sum_j Q^(-j)conjugate(F_j)=Q. Thus the second full word [2,1,2,2] is a normalization source with raw scalar of modulus one. No phase is fitted from its scalar value. The original chi_N multiplies both this scalar and the corresponding packet; any specified framing normalization remains literal.

The usual uniform factorial estimate, with J(t)=integral_0^t log(2sin(pi s))ds, gives

log Z_N=N v_3/pi+O(log N),                           (8)

where v_3 is the volume of the regular ideal tetrahedron. Its proof is elementary: log|F_j|=N J(j/N)+O(log N) uniformly to both endpoints, and J has maximum v_3/(2pi) at5/6. A nearest lattice point supplies the lower bound; N terms supply the upper bound.

Thus a fixed original source trajectory, rather than only an arbitrary all-port vector, realizes the exponential metric-motion obstruction in the terminal-Gram test. With epsilon=exp(-N^2), the same exact regularized quotient there is obtained after multiplying both test vectors by N. A source-dependent changing metric or a further coherent packet is not excluded.

3. Exact coherent pruning and the retained exterior current

The amplified vector (5) is a particularly simple finite divergence. Put G_j=conjugate(F_j) for0<=j<N and G_N=0. Then

b_j=x^(-d) Q(G_j-G_(j+1)).                           (9)

For ANY row f_j of scalar, vector or operator-valued spectator coefficients, distributivity alone proves

sum_j f_j b_j
  =x^(-d)Q [f_0+sum_(j=1)^d(f_j-f_(j-1))G_j].        (10)

No commutativity of the f_j is needed. The factorial factors G_j are fixed scalars. The original constant closing row kills the interior differences and retains the unit endpoint f_0. A general exterior instead keeps the explicit finite-difference current in (10).

The difference matrix in (9) has operator norm at most2, and its finite cumulative inverse has norm at most N. These are coefficient bounds; G itself has exponential norm. Accordingly (10) supplies a source-preserving coherent normalization pruning operation. It does NOT give a bound for a general exterior by dropping its difference. This is the exact point at which an iterated bridge estimate needs the neighboring original Ward equations or another coherent identity.

4. Arbitrary diagonal recorded-color metrics cannot repair this source

For any positive diagonal packet metric D=diag(w_j), including weights with arbitrary N dependence, the complete Cauchy budget is

(1^T D^(-1)1)(b^*Db)
  =(sum_j w_j^(-1))(sum_j w_j |F_j|^2)
  >=(sum_j |F_j|)^2.                                (11)

The minimum is exactly the right side: choose w_j=1/|F_j|, since every F_j with j<N is nonzero. Uniform factorial asymptotics show its logarithmic rate is again N v_3/pi+O(log N), while the squared raw scalar in (7) is one. This is a complete coherent packet counterexample to a diagonal recorded-color metric, not a sum of isolated shear paths.

A genuinely nondiagonal metric can retain the telescoping in (9). Neither (11) nor the full-circle energy proves failure of the shrinking-arc metric. Indeed the first jet of this particular packet is still polynomial: summation by parts gives

sum_j j b_j=x^(-d)Q conjugate(P_d-1).

In fact the whole normalization germ is polynomial on disks of radius C/N, and subexponential on EVERY shrinking disk. Its shrinking-arc energy therefore has rate zero. This uses the complete backward Ward recurrence, beyond the first-jet identity above. The distinct source in the jet diagnostic still has no proved positive all-N jet exponent.

5. Audit and scope

The audit checks (3) on EVERY physical input color directly from the original signed matrices, not from a chosen reduced R_23. It also extracts the complete highest chart, verifies (1),(5)–(7) on every retained packet, and checks (10) with noncommuting spectator matrices. Complex tests verify the complete energies and the optimized diagonal budget.

The algebraic and asymptotic proofs above are all-level. The resulting normalization move is source-exact and usable with arbitrary spectators; the remaining finite-difference exterior in (10) is retained. No broad knot-volume upper bound or selected geometric noncancellation follows.

The finite terminal checks concern the displayed normalization identities only.
