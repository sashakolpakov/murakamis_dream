Exponential loss for auxiliary colors below the complete color

Consider a fixed ordered Conway construction with its original auxiliary crossing circles and half-twist choices. Its reduced multiplication tree is obtained by combining consecutive products with no intervening quarter-turn. A junction is flagged when it carries one original crossing-circle operator used without the outer quarter-turn; a junction with no such operator is bare. We assume the construction has a strict complete state and that no two bare junctions are adjacent. The source-bound theorem defines this class and constructs its actual typed source.

Write YϵY^\epsilon for the complete augmented complement, with all auxiliary circles still unfilled. The exponential rate F0F_0 from the original complete calculation is F0=Vol⁡(Yϵ)2π. F_0=\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(Y^\epsilon)}{2\pi}. \tag{G} This is the volume of that original augmented complement. The intrinsic pressures used to evaluate its source do not insert additional crossing circles.

At level NN, put q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, move the main meridian to M=qsM=q^s, and retain every ordinary auxiliary color 1≤ji≤N1\le j_i\le N. The complete auxiliary color is NN. The theorem below says that a fixed proportional deficit in any one of the original colors gives a strict exponential loss, uniformly in all the other colors.

Theorem. Fix the construction and its half-twist choices ϵi∈{0,±1}\epsilon_i\in\{0,\pm1\}. For every 0≤R<1/20\le R<1/2 and 0<δ<10<\delta<1, there is γ>0\gamma>0 such that its actual reduced cut scalar satisfies sup|s|≤R,1≤ji≤Nsome ji≤(1−δ)N|ℱN,𝒋ϵ(qs)|≤exp⁡N(F0−γ) \sup_{\substack{|s|\le R,\ 1\le j_i\le N\\ \text{some }j_i\le(1-\delta)N}} \left|\mathcal F_{N,\mathbf j}^{\epsilon}(q^s)\right| \le \exp N(F_0-\gamma) \tag{F} for all sufficiently large NN, through both parities. The original framing, independent colors, carrier phases and primitive cut normalization are retained. The strict two-leaf case keeps its original component conditions.

The uniform source upper bound holds on the larger disks R<1R<1. The present strict color gap is proved only for R<1/2R<1/2: the contour below uses one unwrapped interval of the physical crossing roots. Neither estimate identifies the smaller volume of a filled knot. The result also does not supply a lower bound or convergence of a normalized main-meridian profile.

The proof has two parts. First, an exact root-grid formula expresses the actual half-twist as a quadratic phase. Pairing the two grids removes the apparent singularity at the complete meridian and gives a contour estimate against a localized raw source polynomial. Second, the typed pairing exposes any selected original circle, and the existing source induction provides exactly that localization at the original action F0F_0. No complex-strip estimate for the canonical crossing interpolation polynomial is assumed.

The finite algebra comes from the typed compiler and six physical quarter-turns. The needed coefficient bounds and fresh Gaussian estimates are proved in the source-bound theorem. The original geometric normalization is the marked strict-tree geometry, with its rigid-profile and intrinsic-pressure identities.

5.5. Scope

The estimate is for the auxiliary-colored source before the filling transform, and its reference rate is the augmented complete action. It does not prove the filled knot’s lower volume rate or the convergence of the normalized regular main-meridian profile.
