Dimension independent covariance and stable fixed-degree extraction

Private written proof, 23 November 2026.

The existing positive Conway recursion has a stronger covariance estimate when the full tensor normalization is used. Its covariance lies between εI\varepsilon I and 2εI2\varepsilon I for every 0<ε≤10<\varepsilon\le1, independently of the physical dimension and tree size. Fixed-degree extraction can consequently use evaluation points independent of NN, with uniformly conditioned full messages. Chebyshev–Lobatto points also reduce its absolute extrapolation amplification to less than four, independently of the number of leaves.

The original complex source, closing row and budget remain unchanged. These are bounds for the auxiliary evaluation and extraction, not relative-precision bounds for an exponentially small selected scalar.

5. What the improved conditioning does and does not control

If each evaluated scalar FN(εj)F_N(\varepsilon_j) has absolute error at most δ\delta, (13)–(14) give reconstruction error below 4δ4\delta. If instead the normalized observations LStopL†LS_{\mathrm{top}}L^\dagger have absolute errors at most δ\delta, the guaranteed error is below 4BN2δ4B_N^2\delta. The original budget factor must remain in this statement.

The signed scalar sum may vanish or be exponentially smaller than its positive summands. Uniformly conditioned messages and bounded absolute extrapolation therefore do not imply fixed relative precision, a nonzero constant Fourier mode, or a geometric lower bound. They also do not make the full correlated phase Gram uniformly positive in every auxiliary mode.

The quantitative improvement is (6), its calibration (10), and the constant-point conditioning in (13), combined with the absolute amplification (14). Fourier filtering, fixed-degree extraction and elimination of the auxiliary minimization were already proved. Every original cap, complex phase and Hermite direction is retained. The full source value and its volume rate still require their own estimate after arbitrary fixed short partial fillings.
