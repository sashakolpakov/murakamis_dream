Long fillings of the isolated three-leaf Conway source

31 October 2026. original-source theorem. The isolated closure C(A3)=C(hhh)C(A_3)=C(hhh) has a whole-source alias gap and a physical-color complement gap for every fixed pure or signed half-twist pattern. Its existing finite complex packet and marked geometry therefore give the original long-filling volume limit at both level parities. The new input is the uniform two-leaf block lemma, including its endpoint X=1/2X=1/2, together with the elementary three-alias bound below. All original colors, Hermite normal directions, phases, marks and circle factors are retained.

More precisely, let ϵl∈{0,+1,−1}\epsilon_l\in\{0,+1,-1\} at the three original leaves, and let YϵY^\epsilon be the actual augmented exterior. Fill any chosen auxiliary subset II by independently sufficiently long fixed signed integral full twists R−2piR^{-2p_i}. The literal power at a filled leaf is ϵi−2pi\epsilon_i-2p_i; an unfilled cusp is kept complete, not represented by pi=0p_i=0. Then

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KC(A3),Nϵ(𝒑)|=Vol⁡(Yϵ(𝒑)). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log\left|K_{C(A_3),N}^{\epsilon}(\mathbf p)\right| =\operatorname{Vol}\!\left(Y^\epsilon(\mathbf p)\right). \tag{1}

The signs, filling subset and slopes are fixed before NN tends to infinity. The threshold depends on the fixed source, sign pattern and filling subset; all independently chosen |pi||p_i| beyond it are allowed. The slopes need not have the same sign or comparable magnitudes. Both parity subsequences and composite levels are included. After all auxiliary fillings, a loop-free one-main-component parity pattern gives a knot, and the right side of (1) is its hyperbolic knot complement volume. Partial fillings retain the actual remaining cusps. The normalization is the original marked one-cut scalar: its external enhancement has unit modulus for the reduced enhanced Kashaev invariant, and modulus 1/N1/N, once, for the full modified one-cut normalization. These are the unchanged original normalization conventions and crossing comparison.

1. Exact regrouping in the original source

It is an algebraic identity for the original scalar, not an assertion that arbitrary permutations of physical tangles are relative-boundary isotopies.

4. Whole physical-color complement and the finite complex packet

On the all-core support the limiting majorant is

F¯(x1,x2,x3)=∑l=13g̃(xl)+m(x1,x2,1−x3),0≤xl≤12,x1+x2+x3≥1. \overline F(x_1,x_2,x_3) =\sum_{l=1}^3\widetilde g(x_l)+m(x_1,x_2,1-x_3), \qquad 0\le x_l\le\frac12,\quad x_1+x_2+x_3\ge1. \tag{11}

It is concave and agrees with the pure action near the strict complete optimum (a,a,a)(a,a,a), since a>1/4a>1/4. Its Hessian there is negative definite. If another point also maximized it, the intervening segment would contradict this local strictness. Thus this is its unique global maximum, and the complement of any small fixed complete-state window has a compact gap. Continuity carries the gap to the exact finite supports.

Inside that window, every leaf lies below a fixed β<1/2\beta<1/2. 5. Marked geometry and the filling conclusion

Use the existing complete geometry of isolated C(A3)C(A_3), §§2, 6–7.
