Logarithmic degree after exact Conway source compression

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof. Earlier final notes and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The retained residual vector in the compact reciprocal construction need not have degree as large as the number of leaves. First compose the known linear parts of the actual source. Then retain the larger child at each remaining product. An eliminated continuation halves its descendant leaf count at every product. This gives a logarithmic degree bound for the reduced residual, with the original scalar closing still present.

The construction changes the auxiliary energy and integral, but keeps the original closed source and the product of its recorded budgets. It does not estimate that scalar at the hyperbolic volume rate.

1. Compress the actual normalized source

Use the full physical packet space 𝒜N\mathcal A_N and the literal source of [P], for every N≥2N\ge2. Its dimension is NN; all first-normal directions at repeated Casimir roots are included. Write the original normalized recursion and closing as av=Φv((ac)c),KN=BNcNatop,σ=116.(1) a_v=\Phi_v((a_c)_c),\qquad K_N=B_N\,c_Na_{\mathrm{ top}},\qquad \sigma=\frac1{16}. \qquad\text{(1)} Here BNB_N is the product of all recorded original budgets, including the original root budget and external scalar convention. Each leaf constant, unary map, bilinear map and closing row has norm at most σ\sigma. Bilinear norms use two separately unit inputs, as in [P]. True normalized packets have norm at most σ\sigma.

Suppress the unary turns only for counting the product tree. A product then has zero, one or two inputs coming from another product; an input not coming from a product is the known transport of an original leaf. Let n0,n1,qn_0,n_1,q count these three classes. If there is at least one product, counting the edges of this product tree gives n0+n1+q=p,n1+2q=p−1,n0=q+1.(2) n_0+n_1+q=p,\qquad n_1+2q=p-1,\qquad n_0=q+1. \qquad\text{(2)}

Evaluate each zero-product-child vertex at its actual two known inputs. At a one-product-child vertex, insert its actual fixed other input. It is now a known complex-linear map on its remaining input. Compose these maps and the genuine turns along their paths. The vertices with two product children remain bilinear. The resulting literal source has ℓ=q+1constant leaves,qbilinear gates,sc=2q+1proper vertices.(3) \ell=q+1\ \text{constant leaves},\qquad q\ \text{bilinear gates}, \qquad s_c=2q+1\ \text{proper vertices}. \qquad\text{(3)} Its top linear corridor is composed with the original closing row. If the original source has no product, take q=0q=0, compose its unary corridor, and use the same convention of one constant leaf.

This is a composition of the recursion (1), not the Schur-complement objective of [B]. In particular no minimizer of an earlier auxiliary energy is substituted for a source packet.

The original budgets are grouped, rather than replaced by estimates for newly fused unscaled maps. Partition the old vertices into the bottom constant regions with their outgoing linear corridors, the bilinear vertices with their outgoing linear corridors, and the top closing region. Every proper region stops at the next bilinear input; the corridor above the highest product belongs only to the closing region. Fixed side-leaf subtrees belong to the region in which they are inserted. Every old vertex belongs to exactly one region. The new region budget is the product of its old budgets; bilinearity makes its normalized map exactly the corresponding composition of the old normalized maps. Consequently ∏regionsbregion=BN,KN=BNc̃Nãtop.(4) \prod_{\mathrm{ regions}}b_{\mathrm{ region}}=B_N,\qquad K_N=B_N\,\widetilde c_N\widetilde a_{\mathrm{ top}}. \qquad\text{(4)} Every proper region contains an old proper gate or leaf, and the root region contains the old closing row. All remaining normalized factors have norm at most one. Thus each new constant, bilinear map and closing row has norm at most σ\sigma. This argument keeps every actual fixed packet, phase, original mark and first normal in its coefficient; it uses no inverse or cancellation of a fixed packet.

For q=0q=0, the whole proper source has simply become one known packet. Computing that packet is exact source evaluation, not an asymptotic estimate for its closing.

2. Choose the retained child by leaf count

The following argument applies to any binary packet source with ℓ\ell constant leaves and no unary vertices, including (3). For a proper vertex vv, let ℓ(v)\ell(v) be its number of descendant leaves. At every product retain its child with larger ℓ(v)\ell(v); break ties arbitrarily. The other child is eliminated. There is one retained packet per product, so the retained vector is u∈ℂNq,n=Nq.(5) u\in\mathbb C^{Nq},\qquad n=Nq . \qquad\text{(5)} Occurrences are distinct because every proper vertex has one parent. The top proper packet is always eliminated.

Fix uu and solve the eliminated gate equations exactly, as in [L]. Denote their solution by z0(u)z_0(u). A solved leaf is constant. At a solved product, the larger child is its retained independent packet and the smaller child is solved recursively. Therefore the degree d(v)d(v) of its solved value satisfies d(v)=0(v a leaf),d(v)≤1+d(vsmall)(v a product).(6) d(v)=0\quad(v\text{ a leaf}),\qquad d(v)\le1+d(v_{\mathrm{ small}}) \quad(v\text{ a product}). \qquad\text{(6)} After hh such steps, the remaining subtree has at most ℓ(v)/2h\ell(v)/2^h leaves. Equivalently, induction in (6) gives d(v)≤⌊log⁡2ℓ(v)⌋.(7) d(v)\le\lfloor\log_2\ell(v)\rfloor . \qquad\text{(7)} The estimate is valid for a product whose own output is retained as well: only its right-hand side is evaluated by this rule.

Let F(u)F(u) be the vector consisting of the retained gate residuals and the original scalar closing evaluated at z0(u)z_0(u). A retained leaf contributes its prescribed constant minus uvu_v. A retained product contributes its right-hand side minus uvu_v. The closing is linear in the solved top packet. Hence F:ℂNq→ℂNq+1,deg⁡F≤dℓ:=max⁡{1,⌊log⁡2ℓ⌋}.(8) \boxed{\quad F:\mathbb C^{Nq}\longrightarrow\mathbb C^{Nq+1},\qquad \deg F\le d_\ell:=\max\{1,\lfloor\log_2\ell\rfloor\}. \quad} \qquad\text{(8)} Every component is affine separately in every retained packet slot. Indeed the retained child at a product is independent of all variables in the other child’s subtree. The tree has no repeated descendant, so no retained slot is multiplied by itself. Induction in (6), followed by the affine term −uv-u_v, proves the assertion. Thus the real polynomial ∥F(u)∥2\|F(u)\|^2 has degree at most 2dℓ2d_\ell. For q=1,2q=1,2, in particular, FF is affine.

For q=0q=0 no retained variable remains: FF is the scalar c̃Nãtop\widetilde c_N\widetilde a_{\mathrm{ top}}, of degree zero. The convention dℓ=1d_\ell=1 in (8) is only a harmless uniform bound. The full lifted energy before elimination still has degree at most four, independently of this improvement.

3. The exact source and compact reciprocal normalization

Apply [C] to this compressed source, multiplying each of its scs_c proper region budgets and its root budget by NN. The new normalized local maps have norm at most σN=1/(16N)\sigma_N=1/(16N), and B̂N=Nsc+1BN=N2q+2BN,kN=KN/B̂N.(9) \widehat B_N=N^{s_c+1}B_N=N^{2q+2}B_N,\qquad k_N=K_N/\widehat B_N . \qquad\text{(9)} The rescaling only changes known coefficients, so the degree and slotwise multiaffinity in (8) are unchanged. Write cNnew=c̃N/Nc_N^{\mathrm{ new}}=\widetilde c_N/N for the rescaled closing. Write aleafnewa_{\mathrm{ leaf}}^{\mathrm{ new}} for the rescaled constant leaf packets. 4. What the degree bound does and does not simplify

The earlier branching result [B] eliminates constant-coefficient linear response variables from a clamped quartic minimum, retaining 2q2q packets and preserving that auxiliary minimum. Here we instead compose the actual normalized recursion first, then apply the one-child reduction. This gives qq retained packets and a new auxiliary energy and integral. Their numerical values are not identified with those in [B], [L], or the uncompressed [C]. The closed source and the original budget product in (4) are exact.

The logarithmic count in [S] concerns termination of triangular Newton repairs, via Strahler rank. It does not state (8). The argument above bounds the degree of the explicitly eliminated residual vector by choosing which child to retain. No novelty is claimed for the combinatorial principle that repeated halving gives a logarithmic bound.

This degree bound must not replace the scalar degree in the finite-fiber argument. Let GG be the full compressed residual automorphism and f(r)=cNnew(G−1(r))topf(r)=c_N^{\mathrm{ new}}(G^{-1}(r))_{\mathrm{ top}} for the rescaled compiler. Its degree is still at most ℓ\ell. In fact put every internal residual equal to zero and set rleaf=taleafnewr_{\mathrm{ leaf}}=t\,a_{\mathrm{ leaf}}^{\mathrm{ new}}, using the true rescaled leaf constants. Homogeneity gives the exact restriction f(r(t))=kN(1−t)ℓ.(14) f(r(t))=k_N(1-t)^\ell . \qquad\text{(14)} If KN≠0K_N\ne0, this proves deg⁡f=ℓ\deg f=\ell. Thus (8) concerns the vector after integrating linear packet variables, not a lower generic degree for the original scalar fibers.

Even when FF is affine, its coefficients contain the complete actual short components and the prescribed closing row. They can carry cancellation. The remaining geometric task is an upper estimate on 𝒥N/B̂N2\mathcal J_N/\widehat B_N^2 with the original cap retained. The reduced degree, slotwise multiaffinity and compact domain do not supply that estimate or a finite-exception theorem. Overlapping contraction networks with shared descendants are outside the tree argument.
