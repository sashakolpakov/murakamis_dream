Sublinear polynomial observations through fixed Conway words

Written research proof, 22 November 2026.

For any fixed word of original signed crossings and turns, polynomial observations of sublinear Casimir degree retain the exponential norm rate of the transported coherent packet. Both the packet and the observation are transported. The input bidisc has a positive radius depending on the word, and the comparison is in the original Newton coefficient norm.

The proof combines an exact generating-unit gauge identity with explicit finite-head extraction operators. No bound for the norm or condition number of the word is used. The transported packet can have a different rate from the untransported one. Its new rate and its original scalar closing are not evaluated here.

6. Original closing and complete proof sources

Every operation in the argument is an identity on the full original algebra. The low polynomial head is used only to construct the extraction operator; its conjugate acts on the full transported packets. No physical normal or source direction has been declared invisible.

The original marked row ℓN\ell_N may be applied unchanged. For example extraction gives the exact equality ℓN(WwN)=ℓN[𝒜P,N(Z𝒰,L𝒰)WP(Z)wN].(33) \ell_N(Ww_N)= \ell_N\!\left[ \mathscr A_{P,N}(Z_{\mathcal U},L_{\mathcal U}) WP(Z)w_N\right]. \qquad\text{(33)} It does not replace this row by a positive norm, or prove a lower bound for ℓN(WP(Z)wN)\ell_N(WP(Z)w_N). The action of the new packet and its specified scalar closing remain separate questions.

The result concerns fixed linear words of crossings and genuine turns and a word-dependent small coherent input bidisc. It does not give a closed bounded lift through arbitrary branching subtrees. Nor does sublinear Casimir degree mean a bounded number of crossings or degree four of a variational objective. No new knot-volume identity or finite-exception theorem is asserted.
