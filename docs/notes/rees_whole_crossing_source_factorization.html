The whole Verma crossing on the cyclic Rees source

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The actual Verma R-factor has an exact operator factorization after the irregular Rees transformation: a regular norm-one cyclic matrix factor and a specified resonant quantum period. Together with the actual Cartan factor, the latter carries the nontrivial classical holonomy transport. The original head source and every internal head projector are transported explicitly and remain irregular. No regular same-fiber full cyclic R matrix or selected geometric period is inferred.

1. The exact transformed crossing and its ramified factors

Use the genuine balanced common Verma crossing with its full marked Cartan factor retained:

R=D Theta, Theta=P_Q(q Ecal_1 F_2),
log P_Q(z)=-sum_(m>=1) z^m/[m(1-Q^m)].

The nilpotent series terminates on every original fixed total-index sector before transformation. For its transformed functional calculus, introduce a central bookkeeping parameter t and work coefficientwise in t; no convergence assertion is required.

Set d=N(Q^N-1), choose one common marked root eta^N=d, and take the two actual Rees transformations

C_i=D_eta exp(-p_i u_i/d), D_eta h_j=eta^j h_j,
Ehat_i=eta C_i Ecal_i C_i^(-1),
Fhat_i=eta^(-1) C_i F_i C_i^(-1),
Khat_i=C_i K_i C_i^(-1).

The nonzero momenta p_i are not required to coincide. The module theorem proves regularity of these operator coefficients and gives their full cyclic root values. Since the same Hopf scale is used on both factors, its eta factors cancel in the R argument. Thus the exact transformed operator is

Rhat=Dhat P_Q(Zhat),
Zhat=q Ehat_1 Fhat_2,
Dhat=(C_1 tensor C_2)D(C_1 tensor C_2)^(-1).   (1)

Independent choices eta_i instead insert the explicit ratio eta_2/eta_1 in Zhat. Its N-th power is one, but its cyclic phase is retained; it is not reset after specialization.

2. Exact resonance separation before taking any root value

Let W=Zhat^N. Split the logarithmic series into indices divisible and not divisible by N. This gives the exact operator identity

P_Q(t Zhat)=C_Q(t Zhat) R_res,Q(t^N W),
log C_Q(z)=-sum_(N not dividing m) z^m/[m(1-Q^m)],
R_res,Q(w)=P_(Q^N)(w)^(1/N).                (2)

Both factors are functions of the same operator, hence commute. Their logarithms and fractional roots are marked by constant one at t=0. Equation (2) follows by comparing each coefficient; its resonant coefficient is

-(1/N) w^k/[k(1-(Q^N)^k)].

No value of a divergent infinite factor at a root has been assigned. The first factor is regular in the root normal coefficientwise in t, because each denominator with N not dividing m is a unit and Zhat is a regular Rees operator.

The exact functional equation also separates:

C_Q(Qz)/C_Q(z)=(1-z)^(-1)(1-z^N)^(1/N),
R_res,Q((Qz)^N)/R_res,Q(z^N)=(1-z^N)^(-1/N). (3)

Their product is precisely the original Pochhammer transport. This retains the whole central divisor, rather than replacing it by a numerical factor at an ideal source.

3. The regular root factor is the actual marked cyclic dilogarithm

At the root write Z_0 for the cyclic matrix symbol of Zhat, and put

k_i=Khat_i^N, e_i=Ehat_i^N, f_i=Fhat_i^N,
X=Z_0^N=-e_1 f_2.

Here q_N^N=-1 fixes the sign. Let

nu=(1-t^N X)^(1/N),
D_N(t Z_0)=product_(j=1)^(N-1)(1-Q_N^j t Z_0)^(j/N),
g_D(t Z_0)=D_N(t Z_0)/nu^((N-1)/2).

All powers use the common mark at t=0. Weighted root summation in the logarithm gives exactly

C_(Q_N)(t Z_0)=g_D(t Z_0)^(-1).               (4)

For nonresonant degrees the logarithms agree coefficient by coefficient. For resonant degrees the nu normalization cancels the resonant part of log D_N. This proves (4) for even and composite N as well as odd N.

On the cyclic algebra with generator z^N=t^N X, the norm of g_D is one: the deck sum of its logarithm vanishes. On the generic N-squared-dimensional pair matrix space each cyclic eigenvalue occurs N times, so its matrix determinant is also one. These are marked equalities, not permissions to choose a new root of unity phase. Algebraic continuation away from 1-t^N X=0 keeps this root cover and its marking.

When X=0, equation (3) and constant term one determine the nilpotent factor coefficient by coefficient through degree N-1. It is exactly the original finite factorial in the actual R. Thus the finite cyclic factor is anchored to the literature state at the nilpotent source; it has not been independently normalized.

4. The resonant factor is a quantum period, not a regular scalar

For a scalar commuting argument w independent of the normal, equation (2) gives, with d=N(Q^N-1),

log R_res,Q(w)
  =Li_2(w)/d+[Li_2(w)+Log(1-w)]/(2N)+O(d).    (5)

Indeed for Q^N=1+epsilon, 1/(1-(1+epsilon)^k)=-1/(k epsilon)+(k-1)/(2k)+O(epsilon). For the logarithmic normal d_log=N Log(Q^N) the same formula is

log R_res,Q(w)=Li_2(w)/d_log+Log(1-w)/(2N)+O(d_log).

The finite Li_2/(2N) adapter is therefore necessary when using the actual linear Rees normal. For the operator W(d) the whole factor in (2) is retained: its lift and noncommutative normal terms can alter its finite part. Only its leading symbol is Li_2(t^N X)/d.

This fixes the local dilogarithm phase from the actual whole factorial and its zero mark. It does not select a knot fundamental class, a global branch, or a dominant exponential. The normal d is not the physical large-level parameter 2pi*i/N.

5. Leading cyclic-center transport is the characteristic braiding

The generic conjugation formulas of the whole universal R are rational after the Pochhammer functional equation is used. They therefore apply to (1). The Rees operators entering them are regular. On the chart where the full divisor is a unit, their root values give the exact center transport below.

With the fixed multiplication order define

v=q_N Ehat_1 Khat_1^(-1)Khat_2 Fhat_2,
v^N=-e_1 f_2 k_2/k_1,
D_c=1-v^N=1+e_1 f_2 k_2/k_1.

The powers follow by ordered multiplication, including the two factors Q_N^(N(N-1)/2)=(-1)^(N-1) and q_N^N=-1. The uncheck center pullback is

k'_1=k_1 D_c, k'_2=k_2/D_c,
e'_1=e_1 k_2, f'_2=f_2/k_1,
e'_2=k_1 e_2+e_1-e_1 k_2^2/D_c,
f'_1=f_2+f_1/k_2-f_2/(k_1^2 D_c).            (6)

For the checked point map apply the literal flip and the contravariant inverse convention already established in the holonomy calculation. Equation (6) is precisely the characteristic classical braiding on the Gauss leaf. In balanced variables e_G=e/rho_N^N, f_G=-rho_N^N f, the divisor is 1-e_(G,1)f_(G,2)k_2/k_1; the physical meridian uses the separately marked gamma=(-1)^(N+1) factor. All of these scalings are retained.

Equation (4) alone is an inner cyclic matrix factor and preserves the root central scalars. The center change (6) is carried by the resonant/Cartan canonical part. In particular one may write the exact ordered factorization

Rhat=[Dhat R_res,Q(W)] C_Q(Zhat).             (7)

The regular root finite factor in (7) has been identified explicitly. The bracketed factor is the specified whole canonical period; it has not been replaced by an unproved regular matrix.

There is a direct reason such a replacement is generally impossible. A regular invertible matrix on a fixed pair of full cyclic fibers intertwining Delta Ecal with Delta^op Ecal would require

e_1 k_2+e_2=k_1 e_2+e_1.                    (8)

For the dense two-dimensional Rees leaves with independently varying p_i this is false generically. A regular same-fiber matrix limit cannot implement the whole R. It must transport the center/base (or retain a quantum canonical operator). Special pointed subloci where (8) holds do not remove this generic obstruction.

6. The original head and every internal projector are retained exactly

Let i_2,p_2 be the actual two-head inclusion and projection, and put C=C_1 tensor C_2. Define

i_new=C i_2, p_new=p_2 C^(-1).

Then the exact source identity is

p_2 R i_2=p_new Rhat i_new, p_new i_new=I.     (9)

In the head basis their factors are the ramified flags eta^j and eta^(-j) and the source exponential exp[-(p_1u_1+p_2u_2)/d]. They have not been canceled by calling the source normalized.

For an intermediate head projector P_2=i_2p_2, one has

C P_2 C^(-1)=exp[-(p_1u_1+p_2u_2)/d] P_2.   (10)

The finite cut coupon additionally retains its head entry ratios eta^(i-j)/N. These formulas must be inserted at every compression in a word. Dropping them would compare an uncompressed Verma period with the actual finite-head period and lose the specified two-wall excursion adapter delta w H_beta.

The original literal pivot transforms as the literal clock power, with g_lit=U g_red and the inverse defect adapter. The common Cartan half-character, zero-framing scalar and full external modified-dimension function remain exactly the original ones.

The left side of (9) is regular at the root as the actual finite crossing matrix. Thus its right side has precisely the necessary source/period cancellations in this head pairing. This local regularity does not show that the resonant pole survives as a selected geometric action; nor does it make i_new or (10) regular.

7. Exact gain and remaining whole-source step

The actual whole Verma R now supplies, in the same Rees source, the marked cyclic factor, the resonant dilogarithm period, and the characteristic full-cyclic center braiding. The pointing is retained as the explicit irregular and ramified source (9)–(10), rather than reset to a cyclic vacuum.

This identifies the local whole-clock coefficient transport behind the Green carry module more concretely than an unspecified root matrix. It does not compute the continuation of a complete word/current through every retained projector or the pairing of the two final geometric framing currents. In particular it gives no license to set their coefficient carry C-I to zero, erase the finite source jets, or infer a nonzero selected scalar period. Those remain the actual full scalar comparison.

The quantum matrix/base theorem now constructs the local matrix intertwiner on a whole quantum-torus cover. The remaining base transport is explicit and its resonant Hamiltonian yields all six center formulas. The source has its own specified residual; a regular matrix lift alone does not cancel it.

Universal crossing audit and results check the norm-one cyclic coefficients and functional transport, the resonant linear/logarithmic normal adapter, and all six center powers on twelve dense full-cyclic Rees pairs at even and composite levels as well as odd levels. No knot-specific check or selected phase assertion enters these diagnostics.

Independent normalization and source audit confirms the proof and its coefficientwise/finite-head scope.
