The original pivotal source in the canonical coproduct Gram

24 November 2026. Research proof. Earlier finals and manuscripts are unchanged.

The complete cyclic-source coproduct has an explicit spectral graph frame. In that frame the original marked pivotal source is a specified resolvent of the remaining coproduct, followed by its original pivot. Its inherited graph Gram makes this density polynomially bounded, together with its inverse. Consequently the unchanged original co-source has polynomial dual norm in the sharp canonical coproduct Gram of [C]. No comparison between two unestimated metrics is used.

The density is generally complex and has trace zero when there is a tail. These are source and norm identities, not positivity of the original scalar trace or a geometric noncancellation theorem.

7. Quantitative scope and closest coverage

The sharp theorem [C] computes the exponential physical conditioning of JavJ_{\mathrm{ av}}. The present proof adds polynomial original co-source control in that same Gram. Its compensation is the exact identity (20), including its final term, rather than permission to ignore the graph frame. Indeed GλG_\lambda need not be polynomially equivalent to the physical tail metric.

[S] already factors genuine braided source charts into total and multiplicity operators, and [M] already gives complete multiplicity reductions with original caps and Jordan data. [P] constructs positive source Grams from specified terminal packets. The new assertion here is the explicit density (10) and the two-sided bounds (11)–(14) in the inherited canonical coproduct Gram, at arbitrary fixed width.

Every actual fixed short-filled braid component can retain its genuine transported crossings in these identities; no sign or word-length condition has been introduced. They do not bound the resulting word energy by a geometric partition or compare it with hyperbolic volume. A trace may cancel even for an invertible, well-conditioned density. The norm bounds are at the complete roots; no uniform off-root derivative estimate is asserted. No positive scalar trace, nonzero geometric cycle coefficient, source relative precision, or finite-exception volume theorem follows here.
