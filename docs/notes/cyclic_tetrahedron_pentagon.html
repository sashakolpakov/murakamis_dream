Exact root-cyclic tetrahedral pentagons with their source frames

Research note, 26 October 2026. The four-atom crossing construction has a strict local three-to-two operator identity on a compatible anchored root cover. The proof below retains the transported central frames, determinant roots, monomial shears and scalar phase. It does not identify arbitrary independent lifts of the roots, assign all face and boundary tensors to a geometric triangulation, or prove the selected regulator norm of the knot period.

The local function is the literature cyclic dilogarithm already specified in the four-atom note. No different knot invariant is defined. The generic formal identity is proved directly in the Verma transport note.

1. The normalized finite atom

Fix N>=2, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2. For a primitive monomial m with mN=muN, choose nuN=1-muN, with both factors nonzero. On the eigenvalue mu Q^j put

f_0=1, f_j=nu^j/(mu;Q)_j, 0<=j<N,
C(m)=f(m), F=product_j f_j,
D(m)=gamma C(m), gamma^N=F^(-1).                 (1.1)

The root of gamma is retained. Near a zero coefficient tag choose nu=gamma=1; the matrix D then extends with value I. Formula (1.1) is exactly the alternate literature cyclic dilogarithm, with its compatible branches:

mathfrak_d_N(z;Q)=(1-z^N)^((N-1)/(2N))
                  product_(ell=1)^(N-1)(1-Q^ell z)^(-ell/N).

On a module of dimension rN, every eigenvalue has multiplicity r, so det C=F^r and det D=1. All half roots required at even N are included; no division by two modulo N is used.

If m A=Q^k A m, the finite conjugation r_m obeys

r_m(A) D(m)=D(m) A', A'=nu^(-k) A.             (1.2)

Here r_m is the rational conjugation by the generic compact dilogarithm, expressed by finite products of 1-Q^ell m. Its denominators are invertible when 1-m^N is. Equation (1.2), proved by the recurrence in (1.1), specifies the changing target frame.

2. The primitive cyclic pentagon

Take XY=QYX, X^N=U, Y^N=V. Choose three norm roots

a^N=1-U, b^N=1-V, h^N=H=1-U+UV.               (2.1)

Write D(m;nu) to display the root used in (1.1). Then on the compatible root component anchored at X=Y=0,

D(Y;b) D(bX;h)
  =D(X;a) D(-YX/a;h/a) D(Y/h;ab/h).           (2.2)

Zero here refers to the coefficient tags of fixed clock/shift matrices, so the anchor is a regular formal matrix specialization. Away from it the monomial centers are nonzero. The five atom centers in their displayed order are

left:  V, (1-V)U;
right: U, -UV/(1-U), V/H.                    (2.3)

In particular (-YX)^N=-UV at every parity. A compatible spectral root of the middle monomial is qxy/a if x^N=U, y^N=V. The right norm roots h/a and ab/h are forced ratios, rather than new independent choices. The final X,Y splitting roots on both paths are bx,y/h.

Here is a uniform proof of the scalar in (2.2). The directly proved compact pentagon has rational conjugations with no poles other than the indicated norm divisors. Clearing those factors and specializing Q therefore gives the same rational quantum automorphism on both sides. Equation (1.2) implements it between the same initial and final splitting representations. Those representations generate the full matrix algebra. The ratio of the two invertible products is consequently a scalar lambda. Their determinants are one, so lambda^N=1. All the matrices and normalization roots are algebraic on the specified lifted cover. Thus lambda is constant on each irreducible component. At the regular formal anchor every atom is I, so lambda=1 on its component. This proves (2.2).

Independent choices of the five gamma roots can lie on other components; their residual phase is not erased by the theorem. Using raw C instead gives L_raw=lambda_raw R_raw, with

lambda_raw^N=F_L1 F_L2/(F_R1 F_R2 F_R3).        (2.4)

The anchor fixes its compatible lift. Determinants alone fix only the power in (2.4).

3. Include the tetrahedral monomial shears

For three leaves use uniform clock/shift pairs

Z_i e_a=z_i e_(a+1), S_i e_a=s_i Q^a e_a,
S_i Z_i=Q Z_i S_i.

The shear C_ij is the literal permutation

(a_i,a_j) -> (a_i,a_j+a_i mod N).

Its target splitting roots are

z_i'=z_i z_j, z_j'=z_j, s_i'=s_i, s_j'=s_j/s_i.   (3.1)

Indeed the source algebra images of Z_i and S_j are Z_i Z_j and S_i^(-1)S_j. Direct action on the normalized basis proves the two intertwining equations; the other generators are fixed. The shears obey exactly

C_12 C_13 C_23=C_23 C_12.                     (3.2)

In an unnormalized polynomial basis there are also carry factors from reducing exponents modulo N. Formula (3.1) uses normalized monomial frames and does not authorize dropping those factors when comparing with the actual Verma or cut basis.

Evaluate m=-Q t Z_i Z_j^(-1) S_j^(-1) after the shear (3.1). In the roots before that shear, a spectral root is

mu=q t z_i s_i/s_j,
nu^N=1+t^N z_i^N s_i^N/s_j^N.                (3.3)

The commutation integers of m with (Z_i,Z_j,S_i,S_j) are (0,-1,-1,+1). Define the typed tetrahedral operator by

T_ij(t;nu)=C_ij D(m;nu)^(-1).                 (3.4)

Combining the shear with the inverse atom gives its final roots

z_i''=z_i z_j, z_j''=z_j/nu,
s_i''=s_i/nu, s_j''=s_j nu/s_i.              (3.5)

Every subsequent operator is evaluated in these new roots. Products in the formulas below are typed intertwiners from the final module to the initial one, written in traversal order.

4. The strict tetrahedral three-to-two identity

Start with nonzero z_i,s_i and coefficient tags u,v. Put

A=u^N z_1^N s_1^N/s_2^N,
B=v^N z_2^N s_2^N/s_3^N,
a^N=1+A, b^N=1+B, h^N=1+A+AB.               (4.1)

Use the root choices (a,h/a,ab/h) for the left three steps and (b,h) for the right two steps. Then, on the component anchored at u=v=0 and a=b=h=gamma_i=1,

T_12(u;a) T_13(uv;h/a) T_23(v;ab/h)
   =T_23(v;b) T_12(u;h).                    (4.2)

Substitution in (3.5) gives the same final roots on both paths:

z_final=(z_1 z_2 z_3, z_2 z_3/h, z_3/b),
s_final=(s_1/h, s_2 h/(s_1 b), s_3 b/s_2).   (4.3)

The rational conjugations agree by the generic tagged tetrahedral pentagon, whose proof uses only the compact pentagon and (3.2). After clearing the norm divisors, it specializes exactly as in Section 2. Both products in (4.2) therefore intertwine the same full three-pair matrix algebra, and their ratio is scalar. Each normalized atom has determinant one on the N^3-dimensional module. The determinant ratio of the shear words is one by (3.2). Thus this scalar has N^3-th power one. At u=v=0 all atoms are I and the bare shear words agree by (3.2). Algebraic root-of-unity rigidity on the anchored component fixes the scalar to one. This proves (4.2) for even and composite N as well.

For raw inverse atoms retain the scalar lambda_raw. If F_Li,F_Ri are their cycle determinants from (1.1), the exact equation is

lambda_raw^(N^3)
  =[F_R1 F_R2/(F_L1 F_L2 F_L3)]^(N^2).       (4.4)

The diagnostic also finds the stronger Nth-power version of (4.4) in its samples. That strengthening is not needed or asserted as a uniform theorem here.

5. What an external selected evaluation inherits

Let two specified finite tensor presentations differ by (4.2), with the same exterior maps. After identifying their boundary frames as in (4.3), the resulting full external operators are equal. Every transported vector/covector contraction is equal, including the zero contraction. This is an exact local period transport statement, not just equality of unpointed modules.

The four-atom formula uses these same finite literature atoms. Its already specified braid contraction can therefore use this move wherever the typed internal and boundary identifications are supplied. The Cartan, determinant, carry, peripheral and source factors are retained by those identifications. Arbitrary new source vectors are not allowed.

An uncolored ideal triangulation does not supply all these tensor leg, face, turn, source and cut identifications. Nor does (4.2) move a fixed reducible root character to the hyperbolic one. The next triangulation comparison criterion spells out the additional relative comparison and its obstruction.

The subsequent controlled cyclic Euler-source calculation extends the anchored primitive identity to a free formal central order through its nilpotent source. It computes the complete first mixed jet, including the required UV corner term. The completion agrees there with the actual R Euler polynomial. An exact reflected factorial-vector comparison transports its source and co-source, and its scalar is polynomially bounded on marked compact charts. This does not equate arbitrary root lifts, construct an uncolored geometric tensor assignment, or identify the global knot period.

6. The classical five-term check and its limitation

Let D_B denote the Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm. The arguments (2.3) satisfy exactly

D_B(V)+D_B((1-V)U)
  =D_B(U)+D_B(-UV/(1-U))+D_B(V/H).            (6.1)

To verify it, use the ordinary five-term identity at x=U, y=V/H. Its three resulting arguments are UV/H, U(1-V), V. Finally D_B(z/(z-1))=-D_B(z), with z=UV/H and z/(z-1)=-UV/(1-U), gives (6.1). This also explicitly checks the changing central arguments; using frozen arguments would lose the relation.

Every nondegenerate ordinary five-term move preserves the total regulator. In particular it cannot carry a zero-regulator central cycle to a positive hyperbolic fundamental cycle. This is why a strict root pentagon is local input for the selected-period comparison, rather than that comparison itself.

7. Coverage and exact checks

Root-cyclic dilogarithm pentagons and Heisenberg-double tetrahedral operators are existing structures. The literature definitions and coverage are recorded in the linked four-atom and Verma notes; no novelty or published coherence proof is assumed here. The argument above reconstructs the root transport, scalar and frame in the actual conventions of these notes.

The exact audit checks nine primitive cyclic pentagons at N=2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,12 and five full N^3-dimensional tetrahedral identities at N=2,…,6. It checks all five shear maps, the final clock/shift roots, the bare permutation identity, projective matrix equality and the recorded raw determinant equations. All pass. The normalized phase is fixed by the uniform anchored proof, rather than by unrelated finite-field choices of gamma roots. Results.

python3 experiments/audit_cyclic_tetrahedron_pentagon.py

The four-atom audit was also rerun after generalizing the cycle helper to arbitrary spectator multiplicity: all 18 crossings and 72 atom intertwiners pass. These checks do not establish the global selected-source or regulator-norm theorem.
