Irreducible critical sources force nonzero conjugate coefficients

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. A fixed whole algebraic critical source can provide more than uniqueness of a proposed comparison: Galois descent can force its geometric coefficient to be nonzero. The required source density is recovered from finitely many exact moments. A fixed algebraic presentation also gives polynomial bounds on every coefficient along the actual cyclotomic source arc, even though the degrees of the specialized cyclotomic fields grow.

These results supply a finite target for an action-normalized selected period comparison. They do not construct that comparison from the actual quantum state. In particular, algebraicity of Rivin’s critical shapes alone does not prove the hypotheses below or the volume norm.

1. A descended source cannot kill one embedding of a field

Let F be a characteristic-zero field and let A/F be a finite field extension of degree d. Let P be a rank-one A-line, with a source ψ∈P\psi\in P and an F-linear co-source λ:P→F\lambda:P\to F. There is a unique A-linear map η:P→A\eta:P\to A such that

λ=Tr⁡A/F∘η. \lambda=\operatorname{Tr}_{A/F}\circ\eta. \tag{1.1}

Indeed choose a generator p of P. The trace pairing on A is nondegenerate, so x↦λ(px)x\mapsto\lambda(px) is uniquely x↦Tr⁡(bx)x\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}(bx) for some b. Put η(px)=bx\eta(px)=bx. To prove nondegeneracy directly, choose a primitive element theta with distinct conjugates θi\theta_i. The trace Gram matrix in 1,θ,…,θd−11,\theta,\ldots,\theta^{d-1} is VtVV^tV, where Vij=θijV_{ij}=\theta_i^j. Its determinant is the nonzero squared Vandermonde product.

Put a=η(ψ)a=\eta(\psi). After splitting A over an algebraic closure, the source coefficient at its i-th primitive idempotent is exactly

λ(eiψ)=σi(a). \lambda(e_i\psi)=\sigma_i(a). \tag{1.2}

Thus if ψ≠0\psi\ne0 and λ≠0\lambda\ne0, every coefficient in (1.2) is nonzero. The field assumption matters: multiplication by two nonzero elements cannot give zero. A particularly simple exact certificate is

λ(ψ)=1, \lambda(\psi)=1, \tag{1.3}

since Tr⁡(a)=1\operatorname{Tr}(a)=1 forces a≠0a\ne0. Changing the generator of P changes b and the coordinate of psi inversely, and leaves a unchanged. A Gaussian determinant line or its marked algebraic density can therefore be retained as P; no arbitrary scalar trivialization is required. Multiplication by a fixed nonzero algebraic Jacobian or density preserves nonvanishing.

For an A-module P of higher finite rank, (1.1) still holds by an A-basis. The certificate (1.3) still implies every coefficient of the paired element a=η(ψ)a=\eta(\psi) is nonzero. Merely assuming that psi and lambda are individually nonzero is sufficient only in rank one, where their product cannot vanish.

This proves a useful replacement for an independently assumed nonzero geometric weight. It requires the ACTUAL source and co-source to descend to this irreducible algebra. A nonzero pairing elsewhere in a larger critical algebra does not prove (1.3) on its geometric component.

2. The source density is an exact finite moment calculation

Write A=F[θ]A=F[\theta], with a specified irreducible separable polynomial p of degree d. Suppose the actual proposed source descent supplies the finite moments

mj=λ(θjψ),0≤j<d,Gij=Tr⁡A/F(θi+j). m_j=\lambda(\theta^j\psi),\quad 0\le j<d,\qquad G_{ij}=\operatorname{Tr}_{A/F}(\theta^{i+j}).

Then the density a=∑i=0d−1aiθia=\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}a_i\theta^i is determined by

G(ai)i=(mj)j. G(a_i)_i=(m_j)_j. \tag{2.1}

The same nonzero Vandermonde determinant makes G invertible. Thus a whole period comparison need not guess its co-source density: these d exact moments construct its only possible density. In particular a≠0a\ne0 if and only if the moment vector is nonzero, and m0=1m_0=1 is a direct certificate.

If a proposed critical observable Theta acts on a finite F-space V, has irreducible characteristic polynomial of degree dim⁡FV=d\dim_FV=d, and the source psi is nonzero, then V=AψV=A\psi. Indeed A is a field of dimension d and the map A→VA\to V, x↦xψx\mapsto x\psi, is injective. It is therefore an isomorphism. In this situation (2.1) uses actual moments λ(Θjψ)\lambda(\Theta^j\psi). Any operator commuting with Theta is multiplication by an element of A, and the selected scalar is its trace against a. The same statement holds after a specified period-ring extension, for an action operator in that commutant.

To apply this to the knot, one must construct Theta or the critical line on an ACTUAL source quotient and prove the moment descent. The gluing critical field by itself is not such an operator. Neither an abstract isomorphism of Borel groups nor the existence of a local Gaussian block supplies these finite moments.

3. Exact normalized traces give another nonvanishing certificate

Irreducibility can be replaced by a stronger trace realization. Let A be any finite etale F-algebra, faithfully represented on V, and let λ(x)=Tr⁡V(x)/dim⁡FV\lambda(x)=\operatorname{Tr}_V(x)/\dim_FV, with source the identity. After splitting A, each primitive idempotent e_i is a nonzero projection. Thus

λ(ei)=rank⁡eidim⁡FV≠0. \lambda(e_i)=\frac{\operatorname{rank}e_i}{\dim_FV} \ne0. \tag{3.1}

No positivity, self-adjointness, or irreducibility is needed: an idempotent has eigenvalues zero and one, and its trace is its rank. Equivalently, for A=∏KjA=\prod K_j, a faithful A-module has positive integer multiplicities rjr_j, and its trace density on K_j is rj/dim⁡FVr_j/\dim_FV.

Even if dim⁡VN≤CNb\dim V_N\le C N^b grows, the weights in (3.1) lie between C−1N−bC^{-1}N^{-b} and one. They cannot exponentially suppress a geometric sector. Multiplying by a fixed algebraic Gaussian density which is a UNIT on the selected etale algebra preserves this conclusion by Section 4. Nondegeneracy must hold on each selected component; a nonzero density on only one factor of a product would not suffice.

The actual compact cut is a normalized matrix trace, but this does not by itself establish (3.1) for a geometric critical sector. One needs an exact trace-compatible critical summand of the actual transfer or relative period object. Embedding an unrelated critical algebra into a color matrix algebra is insufficient. In particular, the already closed one-cut knot matrix is scalar; projecting that matrix repeats the SAME scalar and does not decompose its phases.

4. A fixed whole presentation gives polynomial bounds on the root arc

Use the root SOURCE slice, after fixing its actual finite phases and power coordinates QN=1Q^N=1, M=1M=1, MN=1M^N=1. On this slice put

F=k(t,Q),k⊂ℂ,(t,Q)=γ(1/N)=(1/N,exp⁡(c/N)),c=2πi. F=k(t,Q),\quad k\subset\mathbb C,\qquad (t,Q)=\gamma(1/N) =(1/N,\exp(c/N)),\quad c=2\pi i. \tag{4.1}

Fix the displayed embedding of k in the complex numbers. The following statement concerns this slice, not the larger space in which QNQ^N is an independent quantum-normal coordinate. Any fixed residue class of levels needed for a marked finite phase can be used. All constants below are uniform along that class.

Polynomial bound theorem. Let a be a nonzero element of a fixed finite field extension A/F. For every conjugate value a_(i,N) of its specialization on (4.1), all sufficiently large N satisfy

C−1N−B≤|a(i,N)|≤CNB. C^{-1}N^{-B}\le |a_{(i,N)}| \le C N^B. \tag{4.2}

The constants depend on the fixed whole algebraic presentation, not on N or the chosen conjugate. No bounded degree of the specialized absolute cyclotomic fields, height assumption, or coherent choice of one analytic branch is needed.

Here is an elementary proof. For any nonzero polynomial

R(t,Q)=∑jpj(t)Qnj,nj distinct, R(t,Q)=\sum_j p_j(t)Q^{n_j},\qquad n_j\text{ distinct},

the pullback R(t,ect)=∑jpj(t)ecnjtR(t,e^{ct})=\sum_jp_j(t)e^{cn_jt} is nonzero. To see linear independence, isolate one exponential by applying ∏k≠j(∂t−cnk)deg⁡pk+1\prod_{k\ne j}(\partial_t-cn_k)^{\deg p_k+1}. On its polynomial factor the remaining operators are ∂t+c(nj−nk)\partial_t+c(n_j-n_k), each invertible on a bounded polynomial space, so a nonzero p_j cannot be killed. If m=∑j(deg⁡pj+1)m=\sum_j(\deg p_j+1), the entire sum solves the constant coefficient equation ∏j(∂t−cnj)deg⁡pj+1f=0\prod_j(\partial_t-cn_j)^{\deg p_j+1}f=0 of order m. Its coefficient recursion shows that a zero of order m would force all Taylor coefficients to vanish. Therefore

ord⁡t=0R(t,ect)≤m−1. \operatorname{ord}_{t=0}R(t,e^{ct})\le m-1. \tag{4.3}

This also proves Zariski density of the arc (4.1) on this two-variable source slice. After clearing Laurent powers it applies to every nonzero rational numerator or denominator on that slice.

Choose a polynomial relation for a over F, clear denominators and write it as ∑j=0dRj(t,Q)aj=0\sum_{j=0}^dR_j(t,Q)a^j=0, with both R0R_0 and RdR_d nonzero. Such a relation exists with nonzero constant term because a is nonzero in a field. On the arc every nonzero coefficient has a finite order at zero by (4.3). Hence for small positive t, |Rd|≥c1tνd|R_d|\ge c_1t^{\nu_d}, |R0|≥c1tν0|R_0|\ge c_1t^{\nu_0}, while all coefficients are bounded above. The elementary Cauchy root bound applied to this polynomial gives |ai|≤Ct−νd|a_i|\le C t^{-\nu_d}. Applied to its reciprocal polynomial it gives |ai|≥C−1tν0|a_i|\ge C^{-1}t^{\nu_0}. This proves (4.2), uniformly over all its roots. Formula (4.3) gives explicit exponents from the fixed coefficient supports.

Apply this to the source density a in Section 1. Irreducible descent and the retained nonzero normalization force ALL geometric conjugate coefficients to be eventually nonzero and subexponential. This does not assume their nonvanishing separately.

5. Height hypotheses: what suffices and what does not

There is also a simple arithmetic criterion, independent of degree. If a nonzero algebraic number alpha satisfies some integer polynomial whose coefficient maximum is H, then

(1+H)−1≤|α|≤1+H. (1+H)^{-1}\le|\alpha|\le1+H. \tag{5.1}

Remove zero constant powers first. The leading and constant integer coefficients then have absolute value at least one. For |z|>1+H|z|>1+H, the sum of the lower terms is smaller than the leading term by the geometric-series estimate H/(|z|−1)<1H/(|z|-1)<1; this proves the upper bound. Reverse the polynomial for the lower bound. Consequently log⁡HN=o(N)\log H_N=o(N) forces log⁡|αN|=o(N)\log|\alpha_N|=o(N), with NO condition on the polynomial degrees. This strengthens the sufficient degree-and-height estimate in the earlier global function audit.

H here is the naive coefficient height of an absolute integer annihilating polynomial, not the absolute logarithmic Weil height. Bounded Weil height without a degree bound is insufficient. For N>=2, the polynomial

zN−2NzN−1−2 z^N-2^Nz^{N-1}-2 \tag{5.2}

is irreducible by the elementary Eisenstein argument at two: reducing a hypothetical monic integer factorization modulo two gives powers of z, so both constant terms would be even and their product would be divisible by four. It has one real root alpha_N in (2N,2N+1)(2^N,2^N+1). Every root with modulus at least one lies in |z−2N|≤2|z-2^N|\le2, since zN−1(z−2N)=2z^{N-1}(z-2^N)=2. On that disk, z↦2N+2z−(N−1)z\mapsto2^N+2z^{-(N-1)} is a strict contraction, so it has at most one root there. Thus every other conjugate has modulus less than one. The Weil height is log⁡αN/N\log\alpha_N/N, bounded and tending to log two, while N−1log⁡|αN|→log⁡2≠0N^{-1}\log|\alpha_N|\to\log2\ne0.

Bounded defining height of the TORSOR alone says even less: a fixed 𝔾m\mathbb G_m torsor contains the points 2N2^N. The whole section or its selected-point height must be controlled. The fixed algebraic section theorem in Section 4 is one way to obtain control which does not require estimating absolute cyclotomic polynomial heights.

6. Two sharp limitations of irreducibility and normalization

First, a split algebra can kill the geometric point. For A=F×FA=F\times F, source (1,1)(1,1), and co-source λ(x,y)=y\lambda(x,y)=y, the normalization is one but the first primitive coefficient is zero. The same failure can occur if a co-source is allowed coefficients in a splitting field rather than descending to F. Thus levelwise algebraicity over ℚ(ζN)\mathbb Q(\zeta_N) is not the whole descent assumption in Section 1. A fixed shape field can intersect these cyclotomic fields and split after such a base change. The theorem requires one whole descended co-source before these specializations, or the faithful trace realization of Section 3.

Second, even field descent and normalization do not bound a merely levelwise source. In A=ℚ(2)A=\mathbb Q(\sqrt2), take

aN=(1+2N2)/2,Tr⁡(aN)=1. a_N=(1+2^N\sqrt2)/2, \operatorname{Tr}(a_N)=1. \tag{6.1}

Both coefficients are nonzero but have exponential size. Multiplying a geometric exponential by either changes its rate by log two. The fixed quadratic field, its discriminant, and the normalization remain unchanged. The selected point has exponentially growing naive height; (6.1) is not a fixed algebraic function of (t,Q)(t,Q) on (4.1). This separates finite-type geometry from bounded complexity of its selected section.

7. The resulting action-normalized period criterion

Suppose an ACTUAL scalar period has been represented, with its source/cut preserved, in a finite global period space as

PN=∑i=1da(i,N)G(i,N). P_N=\sum_{i=1}^{d} a_{(i,N)}G_{(i,N)}. \tag{7.1}

Assume its source density is the fixed whole element of Sections 1–4, or its coefficients have the faithful trace weights of Section 3 times a fixed algebraic density which is a unit on every selected component. Suppose a specified geometric index g has

1Nlog⁡|G(g,N)|→sg,|G(i,N)|≤exp⁡(N(sg−δ))(i≠g) \frac1N\log|G_{(g,N)}|\longrightarrow s_g,\qquad |G_{(i,N)}|\le\exp(N(s_g-\delta))\quad(i\ne g) \tag{7.2}

for some positive delta and all sufficiently large N. The polynomial bounds and nonvanishing already proved imply

1Nlog⁡|PN|→sg. \frac1N\log|P_N|\longrightarrow s_g. \tag{7.3}

Indeed the total of the other finitely many terms divided by the geometric term tends to zero exponentially, up to polynomial factors. This proves both the upper and lower rate, including cancellation control. It does not assume nonvanishing of the geometric coefficient as an extra hypothesis.

If (7.1) is instead a proposed leading-block formula with a remainder, the TOTAL remainder requires a smaller-rate bound as well. Strict Rivin concavity on the positive real angle domain does not bound all other complex critical sectors or supply that global estimate.

For the knot, the marked regulator gives sg=Vol⁡/(2π)s_g=\operatorname{Vol}/(2\pi) on the geometric period. The unresolved input is the source-preserving global period-line realization (7.1), together with (7.2), not another Taylor coefficient at the unique algebraic optimizer. The moment formula (2.1) and trace criterion (3.1) are exact, finite algebraic certificates for the missing source part of such a realization.

This is separate from actual Mellin root reconstruction: that theorem fixes the whole root function once the actual bounded quantum source jets and pole lattice are supplied. It does not yet construct the action-normalized critical trace (7.1). Nor does the present result replace the ACTUAL generally transcendental selected Mellin section by an algebraic function; the adequate-knot transcendence theorem already excludes that replacement.

The complementary whole-unit weight theorem shows why finite type of the torsor CONNECTION is weaker than the bounded algebraic coefficient hypothesis in Section 4: a growing character of one fixed regular algebraic unit can carry a nonzero normal action. Its marked connection and character must be retained, even when the unit has classical value one.

Independent proof audit.

The subsequent actual pointed-commutant theorem rules out obtaining this irreducible critical observable as a whole horizontal endomorphism of the original pointed Mellin lattice. The dual-clock theorem also shows that raw framing moments have infinite Hilbert-matrix rank; they are not the isolated transverse critical algebra. A finite list of proposed moments constructs a density only AFTER the critical polynomial action or quotient has been supplied and verified. These tests do not infer critical descent from a finite moment fit.
