Exact full-circle sewing beyond the cyclic pretzel source

29 October 2026. There is an all-level, source-preserving circle resolution for every original two-port Hopf box. Its dual is a difference of TWO ordinary circle colors, with polynomial norm. This gives an exact alphabet for arbitrary finite arborescent circuits of such boxes, and a quantitative whole-source upper bound. It does not yet give a sharp geometric bound or nonzero geometric period for a general augmented graph.

All identities below retain the first derivatives at repeated Casimir nodes. In particular none takes a semisimple quotient at a root of unity.

1. The original algebra and the actual turn

Put d=N−1,q=eπi/N,Q=q2,x=qdd=N-1, q=e^{\pi i/N}, Q=q^2, x=q^d, and

ℋN=ℂ[z]/PN(z),PN(z)=2TN(z/2)+2,La(z)=(−1)a−1Ua−1(z/2),1≤a≤N. \mathcal H_N=\mathbb C[z]/P_N(z),\qquad P_N(z)=2T_N(z/2)+2, \qquad L_a(z)=(-1)^{a-1}U_{a-1}(z/2),\quad1\leq a\leq N. \tag{1}

The full physical commutant theorem identifies this algebra faithfully with every E,F,KE,F,K-intertwiner on the original VN⊗VNV_N\otimes V_N, by z↦Ωz\mapsto\Omega. It includes all degrees, even and composite levels, and the odd simple node. The operators LaL_a are the literal auxiliary ordinary-circle traces, with their original pivotal trace and no division by aa.

Use the unscaled genuine turn ℛ\mathscr R, with the original cup and cap

pab=Nx−bδa+b,d,ℓab=xaδa+b,d/N. p_{ab}=Nx^{-b}\delta_{a+b,d},\qquad \ell_{ab}=x^a\delta_{a+b,d}/N.

For a Hopf box BB, its symmetry and degree support give

(ℛB)(a,b);(c,e)=xa−eB(d−a,c);(b,d−e),ℛ2=I,ℛ(I)=pℓ,ℛ(R−1)=R. (\mathscr R B)_{(a,b);(c,e)} =x^{a-e}B_{(d-a,c);(b,d-e)},\qquad \mathscr R^2=I,\quad \mathscr R(I)=p\ell, \quad \mathscr R(R^{-1})=R. \tag{2}

The scaled ZB=x−dℛBZ_B=x^{-d}\mathscr R B used in the older charge-block conjugation is different notation. Here every turn is (2), so no xdx^d is lost. Rotation is a linear automorphism, not a product map.

The original marked functional is

ϕ(f)=1NTr⁡[(I⊗K1−N)f(Ω)]=(−1)dN2[zd](fmod⁡PN). \phi(f)=\frac1N\operatorname{Tr}[(I\otimes K^{1-N})f(\Omega)] =(-1)^dN^2[z^d](f\bmod P_N). \tag{3}

The last equality, proved in the commutant note, is a full Hermite identity. In particular ϕ(La)=N2δaN\phi(L_a)=N^2\delta_{aN}.

2. A sparse all-normal Frobenius inverse

The ordinary polynomial product identity and its exact root reduction are

LaLb=∑r=0min⁡(a,b)−1La+b−1−2r,LN+h=LN−h−2(−1)NLh(mod⁡PN),1≤h<N. L_aL_b=\sum_{r=0}^{\min(a,b)-1}L_{a+b-1-2r},\qquad L_{N+h}=L_{N-h}-2(-1)^N L_h\pmod {P_N}, \quad1\leq h<N. \tag{4}

The first follows by multiplying the two finite geometric expressions for Ua−1((w+w−1)/2)U_{a-1}( (w+w^{-1})/2). For the second use

2TN(z/2)Uh−1(z/2)=UN+h−1(z/2)−UN−h−1(z/2) 2T_N(z/2)U_{h-1}(z/2)=U_{N+h-1}(z/2)-U_{N-h-1}(z/2)

and 2TN=−22T_N=-2 in the QUOTIENT POLYNOMIAL RING. Thus (4) retains normal derivatives, rather than merely agreeing on distinct nodes. The coefficient mass in any product of two basis elements is at most 3N3N. At even NN, the cancellations include the exact identity LN2=0L_N^2=0; omitting the high aliases would destroy it.

Only a term LNL_N contributes to (3). The high terms in (4) cannot reduce to LNL_N. Consequently

Mab:=N−2ϕ(LaLb)=𝟏a+b≥N+1𝟏a+b≡N+1(2). M_{ab}:=N^{-2}\phi(L_aL_b) =\mathbf1_{a+b\geq N+1}\mathbf1_{a+b\equiv N+1\ (2)}. \tag{5}

Let Jab=δa+b,N+1J_{ab}=\delta_{a+b,N+1} and let SS be the upper shift. Then

M=J(I−S2)−1,M−1=(I−S2)J,(M−1)ab=δa+b,N+1−δa+b,N−1. M=J(I-S^2)^{-1},\qquad M^{-1}=(I-S^2)J, \qquad(M^{-1})_{ab}=\delta_{a+b,N+1}-\delta_{a+b,N-1}. \tag{6}

Here (I−S2)−1(I-S^2)^{-1} is the finite geometric sum. In particular

La∨=LN+1−a−LN−1−aN2,ϕ(La∨Lb)=δab, L_a^\vee=\frac{L_{N+1-a}-L_{N-1-a}}{N^2},\qquad \phi(L_a^\vee L_b)=\delta_{ab}, \tag{7}

with Lj=0L_j=0 for j≤0j\leq0. This proves nondegeneracy at EVERY level. It also gives ∥M∥2≤⌈N/2⌉\|M\|_2\leq\lceil N/2\rceil and ∥M−1∥2≤2\|M^{-1}\|_2\leq2, hence condition number at most N+1N+1. The Frobenius pairing is bilinear and generally indefinite; (6) is not a claim of a positive pivotal trace. In a positive coefficient norm its Riesz vector must be conjugated in the usual way.

3. A literal all-exterior turn kernel

Write

zN,a=ℛ(R−1La),1≤a≤N. z_{N,a}=\mathscr R(R^{-1}L_a),\quad1\leq a\leq N.

These are the full mixed-circle packets, including both the low core and the nonzero RPRP band. Since RR is invertible in the FULL algebra, the bases R−1LaR^{-1}L_a and RLa∨R L_a^\vee are dual. For EVERY f∈ℋNf\in\mathcal H_N,

ℛf=∑a=1NzN,aϕ(RLa∨f). \boxed{\quad \mathscr R f=\sum_{a=1}^{N}z_{N,a}\, \phi(R L_a^\vee f). \quad} \tag{8}

This is a full physical operator identity before any exterior is attached. It therefore allows arbitrary adjacent physical tensors and spectator couplings; it is not an identity only after evaluating a knot scalar. All original Cartan factors and κ\kappa remain in the literal RR. Products are given by (4), twists by their exact signed powers, and turns by (8). These operations provide a finite source-independent sewing algorithm on any fixed two-port circuit generated by them. Every internal resolution uses only the sparse dual (7).

Both closures also keep their original source. The denominator closure is (3). The original marked row is mab=xb/N2m_{ab}=x^b/N^2, with NO delta. Directly contracting its entries with (2) gives

ϕ(ℛf)=mf(Ω)p=f(c0),c0=q+q−1. \boxed{\qquad \phi(\mathscr R f)=m f(\Omega)p=f(c_0),\qquad c_0=q+q^{-1}. \qquad} \tag{9}

For clarity, in the diagonal trace the coefficient of f(d−a,a);(b,d−b)f_{(d-a,a);(b,d-b)} is gbxa−b/N=xa+b−d/Ng_bx^{a-b}/N=x^{a+b-d}/N. This equals m(d−a,a)p(b,d−b)m_{(d-a,a)}p_{(b,d-b)}; hence the first equality is the genuine unrestricted marked contraction. Finally f(Ω)p=f(c0)pf(\Omega)p=f(c_0)p, and mp=1mp=1. No local replacement of the marked row by an ordinary cap was used.

4. A quantitative faithful norm on all normal channels

For the distinct roots of PNP_N, put

∥f∥H=maxc|f(c)|+N−2maxcdouble|f′(c)|. \|f\|_{\mathrm{H}}=\max_c|f(c)|+ N^{-2}\max_{c\ {\mathrm{double}}}|f'(c)|. \tag{10}

At an odd simple node only its value occurs. This is a faithful submultiplicative norm. Let f=∑a=1NβaLaf=\sum_{a=1}^{N}\beta_aL_a. Then

∥f∥H≤32N3/2∥β∥2,∥β∥2≤6N3/2∥f∥H. \|f\|_{\mathrm{H}}\leq\tfrac32N^{3/2}\|\beta\|_2, \qquad \|\beta\|_2\leq6N^{3/2}\|f\|_{\mathrm{H}}. \tag{11}

For the first estimate use max⁡[−2,2]|La|≤a\max_{[-2,2]}|L_a|\leq a, and the elementary polynomial derivative bound max⁡|La′|≤(a−1)2a/2\max|L_a'|\leq(a-1)^2a/2. For completeness the reverse estimate does not invert a confluent Vandermonde: set wk=q2k+1w_k=q^{2k+1}, hk=f(wk+wk−1)h_k=f(w_k+w_k^{-1}), and dk=(wk−wk−1)f′(wk+wk−1)d_k=(w_k-w_k^{-1})f'(w_k+w_k^{-1}), with dk=0d_k=0 at the simple node. Write f=a0+∑n=1dan2Tn(z/2)f=a_0+\sum_{n=1}^d a_n2T_n(z/2). The finite Fourier transforms Hn,DnH_n,D_n obey

a0=H0,an={Dn+(N−n)Hn}/N,βj+1=(−1)j(aj−aj+2). a_0=H_0,\quad a_n=\{D_n+(N-n)H_n\}/N, \quad\beta_{j+1}=(-1)^j(a_j-a_{j+2}). \tag{12}

Indeed the two aliases are Hn=an−aN−nH_n=a_n-a_{N-n} and Dn=nan+(N−n)aN−nD_n=na_n+(N-n)a_{N-n}. Since |hk|≤∥f∥H|h_k|\leq\|f\|_{\mathrm{H}} and |dk|≤2N2∥f∥H|d_k|\leq2N^2\|f\|_{\mathrm{H}}, (12) proves (11), including the even middle Fourier index and the odd simple node.

The original full crossing satisfies, for EVERY signed integer ee,

∥Re∥H≤1+|e|/8. \|R^e\|_{\mathrm{H}}\leq1+|e|/8. \tag{13}

Its nodal values have modulus one. The exact full-Cartan derivative is (Re)′/Re=−e(d−2k)/[2(wk−wk−1)](R^e)'/R^e=-e(d-2k)/[2(w_k-w_k^{-1})]. At a double node |sin⁡((2k+1)π/N)|≥sin⁡(π/N)≥2/N|\sin((2k+1)\pi/N)|\geq\sin(\pi/N)\geq2/N; division by N2N^2 gives (13). Negative powers are literal inverses, and their derivatives are included. Also ∥La∥H≤3N/2\|L_a\|_{\mathrm{H}}\leq3N/2.

The global full-colored packet bound, sections 3–4, gives a sequence ϵN→0\epsilon_N\to0, independent of aa, such that

max1≤a≤N∥zN,a∥H≤exp⁡[N(j0+ϵN)],j0=v8/π. \max_{1\leq a\leq N}\|z_{N,a}\|_{\mathrm{H}} \leq\exp[N(j_0+\epsilon_N)],\qquad j_0=v_8/\pi. \tag{14}

This LOCAL statement needs no box-count threshold. Its proof keeps the full high band; that band’s upper rate 12log⁡(4+15)<j0\tfrac12\log(4+\sqrt{15})<j_0 is uniform over all colors. Apply (11) to RfRf, expand in LaL_a, and apply (8). It follows that

∥ℛ∥H→H≤7N2exp⁡[N(j0+ϵN)]. \|\mathscr R\|_{{\mathrm{H}}\to{\mathrm{H}}} \leq7N^2\exp[N(j_0+\epsilon_N)]. \tag{15}

The exponential rate in (15) is exact, not an artifact of a physical basis condition number:

limN→∞N−1log⁡∥ℛ∥H→H=j0. \boxed{\quad\lim_{N\to\infty}N^{-1} \log\|\mathscr R\|_{{\mathrm{H}}\to{\mathrm{H}}}=j_0.\quad} \tag{16}

To prove the lower bound, use zN=zN,Nz_N=z_{N,N}. Its input R−1LNR^{-1}L_N has polynomial norm. For every fixed odd s≥3s\geq3, the proved original complete period has N−1log⁡|ϕ(zNs)|→As=Vs/(2π)N^{-1}\log|\phi(z_N^s)|\to A_s=V_s/(2\pi), whereas |ϕ(f)|≤3N3∥f∥H|\phi(f)|\leq3N^3\|f\|_{\mathrm{H}} and the norm is submultiplicative. Hence the lower rate is at least As/sA_s/s. Its explicit volume formula gives As/s→8Λ(π/4)/π=v8/πA_s/s\to8\Lambda(\pi/4)/\pi=v_8/\pi. First take the limit in NN for EACH fixed ss, then the supremum in ss; no exchange of two growing parameters is needed.

5. A reusable original-source upper bound, and its limitation

Consider any fixed finite typed two-port syntax circuit with leaves ReiR^{e_i} and ordinary circle operators LaiL_{a_i}, product vertices, and tt genuine turns (2). Close by either original closure (3) or (9). All colors 1≤ai≤N1\leq a_i\leq N are allowed. For fixed signed powers,

limsupN→∞2πNlog⁡|AN|≤2tv8. \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log|A_N| \leq 2t\,v_8. \tag{17}

This follows inductively from submultiplicativity, (13)–(15), |ϕ|≤3N3∥⋅∥H|\phi|\leq3N^3\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{H}}, and |f(c0)|≤∥f∥H|f(c_0)|\leq\|f\|_{\mathrm{H}}. The only exponential cost is an ACTUAL turn. No additional turn is charged for the numerator closure. The same proof allows growing exponents if ∑ilog⁡(1+|ei|)=o(N)\sum_i\log(1+|e_i|)=o(N).

The object in (17) is the literal original marked scalar. For a diagram using the original reduced zero-framed convention, the explicit turn and whole normalization comparison give only the recorded unit framing character. The one-cut modified convention adds iN+3/Ni^{N+3}/N. These preserve (17); no unspecified meridian normalization is absorbed in the estimate.

Thus (17) applies to any arborescent/conway presentation ACTUALLY built from this typed alphabet, not just a cyclic power of an identical box. It is a broad source upper, but generally not the sharp filled volume: already Vs<2sv8V_s<2s v_8 for the known complete cyclic pretzel examples. An exact regular-octahedron decomposition whose incidence and turn count match (17) would make a stronger base comparison possible. Such an identification has not been proved here for all octahedral FALs.

6. What a prime six-port graph still requires

The circle resolution is valid on a TWO-port Hopf separator. A prime six-port cell is not supplied by assigning an independent commuting circle variable to each incident pair. The actual complete multiplicity space has adjacent operators A=Ω12A=\Omega_{12}, B=Ω23B=\Omega_{23}; they generate the full matrix algebra, as proved in the original observable filtration. This rules out an EXACT quotient preserving both operators by commuting circle coordinates. It does not rule out a geometric microlocal metric.

The concrete missing kernel is the full source-selected recoupling C=Vd−1JVdC=V_d^{-1}JV_d, with every Hermite channel retained, or equivalently the complete-circle split-pair map (I⊗π)(ι⊗I)(I\otimes\pi)(\iota\otimes I). A source-exact six-port octahedral identity, its mixed-color analytic bound, and its original marked dual cost would extend the present sewing beyond two-port trees. None follows from a positive Turaev–Viro partition function alone.

For coverage only, the abstract of Ibarra–McQuire–Purcell identifies octahedral FAL complements with fundamental-shadow-link complements and reports a colored skein formula and a Turaev–Viro result. That abstract supplies no identity with this original marked Kashaev source. No proof or technique from it is used above.

7. Complete-circle connectors: a coherent channel sum, not independent blocks

There is a useful exact positive statement about a complete connector. Since PN′=NUN−1P_N'=NU_{N-1}, one has LN=(−1)dPN′/NL_N=(-1)^dP_N'/N in the FULL algebra. At a double node, LN(c)=0L_N(c)=0 and (4−c2)LN′(c)=(−1)d2N(4-c^2)L_N'(c)=(-1)^d2N. At the odd simple node LN(−2)=NL_N(-2)=N. Substitution in the original trace formula gives

ϕ(LNfg)=N∑cdistinctmcf(c)g(c),mc=2(double),m−2=1(simple). \boxed{\quad \phi(L_Nfg)=N\sum_{c\ {\mathrm{distinct}}}m_c f(c)g(c), \qquad m_c=2\ {\mathrm{(double)}},\quad m_{-2}=1\ {\mathrm{(simple)}} . \quad} \tag{18}

Thus for coefficient reflection f♯(z)=f(z‾)¯f^\sharp(z)=\overline{f(\bar z)}, ϕ(LNf♯f)=N∑mc|f(c)|2≥0\phi(L_N f^\sharp f)=N\sum m_c|f(c)|^2\geq0. All nodes are real. The positive form has rank ⌊(N+1)/2⌋\lfloor(N+1)/2\rfloor, and its kernel is EXACTLY the ideal of polynomials whose values vanish at all distinct nodes. The derivatives of LNL_N, rather than a discarded projective sector, supply the positive weights. Reflection of an actual tensor diagram must still use its genuine dual turns; (18) does not license bending an already formed positive matrix in a different bipartition.

In particular the normalized connector is the JOINT channel average N−1∑mcf(c)g(c)N^{-1}\sum m_c f(c)g(c), not the product of the separate averages. Its rank is greater than one for every N≥3N\geq3. Independent block factorization for arbitrary exterior boxes is therefore false even though the connector is positive on reflected pairs.

Here is an exact test of the proposed purely Frobenius tree sewing. Let a fixed finite tree have vertex tensor ϕ(fv∏Lave)\phi(f_v\prod L_{a_{ve}}). At each edge use the COMPLETE connector

Cab=ϕ(La∨LNLb∨). C_{ab}=\phi(L_a^\vee L_N L_b^\vee). \tag{19}

These are specified actual algebra/trace tensors with the original source normalizations, not a metric chosen from the final scalar. Repeatedly applying the all-exterior resolution (7) at a leaf proves

Ztree=ϕ(LN|E|∏vfv). \boxed{\qquad Z_{\mathrm{tree}}=\phi\!\left(L_N^{|E|}\prod_v f_v\right). \qquad} \tag{20}

This identity includes vertices of arbitrary valency and arbitrary external fvf_v. Every leaf elimination inserts one literal LNL_N; there is no inverse of a nilpotent circle operator. For |E|≥2|E|\geq2, it specializes further to

Ztree={0,Neven,N|E|∏vfv(−2),Nodd. Z_{\mathrm{tree}}= \begin{cases} 0,&N\ {\mathrm{even}},\\ N^{|E|}\prod_v f_v(-2),&N\ {\mathrm{odd}}. \end{cases} \tag{21}

Indeed at a double node the value and first derivative of LNeL_N^e vanish for e≥2e\geq2; only the odd simple node survives. This is an ACTUAL full-normal obstruction to replacing every internal Conway connector by an independent Frobenius complete-circle propagator and then multiplying known local geometric periods. Such a sewing is computable and may describe a particular diagram, but it cannot be identified with a new nonzero all-level augmented tree source without an additional physical port theorem. The true branched graph can have six open physical legs at a vertex; its intertwiner need not be this commutative multiplication tensor. Turns at intermediate vertices also change (20) into the nonmultiplicative kernel (8).

Equations (18)–(21) locate the next constructive task: retain the shared channel and its actual six-port vertex tensor before estimating or factorizing it. They do not rule out a coherent geometric positive metric, or an exact larger class after that tensor has been supplied.

There is a source-exact nonbranched extension that avoids (20): the balanced octahedral belt stack cuts every piece at the SAME outer belt and retains ONE complete circle after cyclic stacking, so its source is ϕ(LN(zN♯zN)k)\phi(L_N(z_N^\sharp z_N)^k), not a power of LNL_N. The actual bandlimited sampling theorem gives its sharp energy and the literal pants/collar certificate gives volume 4kv84k v_8. This does not replace a genuinely branched physical vertex by the Frobenius tensors in (19).
