The actual signed color packet through the quarter-index boundary

31 October 2026. local source theorem. This extends the LOCAL original mixed-color coefficient theorem across x=1/4x=1/4. The selected branch has a source-fixed nonzero amplitude on a fixed joint complex neighborhood, with an exponentially small relative error on the physical Newton grid. Both transport channels and every conversion error are retained in the proof. No global alias estimate, full filled-volume theorem, or arbitrary-syntax geometric theorem is inferred here.

The exact inputs are the selected mixed-inner source, §§1–6; the analytic diagonalization lemma, §§1–3; the complete-color crossing proof, §§2–3; and the signed Conway packet, §§1–5. The last complete-color crossing proof is used as a template, not as a mixed-color identification: the literal mixed finite sum and its recurrence are printed below.
