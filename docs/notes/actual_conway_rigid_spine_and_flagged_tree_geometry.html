Rigid spines and the geometry of fully flagged Conway trees

1 November 2026. structural and complete-geometry theorem. This note uses the strict-output compiler, the general complete-mask geometry and the original marked filling calibration. Its source normal form retains every independent original color and normal coordinate. A rigid classical profile is not a scalar quantum coupon, a source-neutral operation or a tangle isotopy.

The reduced-hub source theorem gives the exact finite algebraic interface. The separate decorated-character estimate supplies its signed analytic closure; no such estimate is deduced here from the geometry alone.

1. The exact rigid-spine decomposition

The strict types are L=(0,1/2),H=(1/2,1),A=(0,1), L=(0,1/2),\qquad H=(1/2,1),\qquad A=(0,1), with LL,LA,AA→ALL,LA,AA\to A, HL,HA→HHL,HA\to H, and HHHH empty; orders can be reversed. Turns exchange L,HL,H and preserve AA. Consequently:

  • A product with output HH has exactly one HH child. Its other child has type LL or AA.
  • No product has output LL.
  • Starting at any L/HL/H root, follow its turn child, or the unique HH child at a product. This reaches exactly one original leaf h*h_*.

Call this path the rigid spine and its off-path subtrees Q1,…,QmQ_1,\ldots,Q_m its satellites. Every satellite has type LL or AA. Each product on the spine has type HH, so turns between successive product vertices have even parity. The original full pair-algebra turn satisfies T2=IT^2=I. Thus the actual full coupons obey EH=(Th*)∏j=1mQj,EL=T[(Th*)∏j=1mQj]. \begin{aligned} E_H&=(Th_*)\prod_{j=1}^m Q_j,\\ E_L&=T\!\left[(Th_*)\prod_{j=1}^m Q_j\right]. \end{aligned} \tag{1} Here EH,ELE_H,E_L name expressions of the indicated types; the equality retains their values and first normals at all repeated source roots.

Every physical leaf can independently be hi=T(RϵiLji)h_i=T(R^{\epsilon_i}L_{j_i}). The product order can be kept as inherited; associativity and commutativity are identities in the original pair algebra. The unreduced framed expression remains the topology. In particular, (1) does not remove a pair of physical turns from that topology.

Nor does it make EHE_H a scalar multiple of Th*Th_*, or ELE_L a scalar multiple of h*h_*. An exact N=3N=3 check gives h=10−z,Th=z2−1,hTh=10z2−2z−8. h=10-z,\qquad Th=z^2-1,\qquad h\,Th=10z^2-2z-8. 7. Coverage and the separate signed estimate

The older belt construction restricts its gluing graph to a cycle along one retained belt and explicitly requires a separate tensor argument for branched multi-belt graphs. The root-star coverage audit establishes exact source overlap of one-hub examples with nonzero inner half twists. Neither result alone proves a branched-hub estimate.

Formula (14) calibrates the complete action of the present source tree. It makes no claim of a new unmarked knot family or a literature-wide coverage classification. In particular, arbitrary Conway diagrams need not have a fully flagged reduced hub tree.

The quantum induction retains the actual unturned RϵLjR^\epsilon L_j immediately before each turn, all independent colors and every normal direction. The separate decorated-character closure proves estimates for this operator. They do not follow from the harmonic response (7), the constant identity (10), or the complete value (14). An unflagged hub requires a different operator, with no original character at that vertex; adding a new circle would change the source.
