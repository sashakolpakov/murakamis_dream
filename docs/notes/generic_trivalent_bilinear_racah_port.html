A generic bilinear Euler reduction toward the actual Racah source sum

29 October 2026. This records two OWN finite identities: a bilinear Euler linearization and a generic Euler-moment formula for the actual source B_abc. They hold before root specialization and include high fold partners. The final source-to-single-Racah identity targeted in section4 is now proved independently by the actual source-column/Jacobi argument. The finite comparisons in this note remain supporting checks, not the proof of that identity.

Use the notation and ACTUAL source normalization of the generic six-head theorem: N=d+1, Q=zeta^2, F_n=(Q;Q)_n, and ordinary triangles a,b,c in 0,…,d. Put r=b+c-a, r1=a+b-c, r2=a+c-b. All formulas in this note are generic rational identities. Only the source formula already proved there, not a named quantum 6j evaluation, is a premise.

1. Own bilinear finite Euler linearization

For nonnegative m,n and a generic parameter V,

(U;Q)_m(UV;Q)_n
   =sum_(k=0)^n C_(n,k) U^k(U;Q)_(m+n-k),
C_(n,k)=Q^[k(k-1)/2] [n choose k]_Q
                     product_(s=0)^(k-1)(Q^(m-s)-V). (1)

Proof by induction on n. For the old basis vector with L=m+n,

U^k(U)_(L-k)(1-UVQ^n)
   =U^k(U)_(L+1-k)
    +Q^n(Q^(m-k)-V)U^(k+1)(U)_(L-k).

Thus C_(n+1,k)=C_(n,k)+Qn(Q(m-k+1)-V)C_(n,k-1). The displayed formula satisfies this by the elementary Gaussian Pascal identity. At n=0 the assertion is exactly (U)_m. No infinite product or summation formula is needed.

The relevant specialization is m=n=b,V=Q^N:

(U)_b(UQ^N)_b
   =sum_(k=0)^b Q^(bk)[b choose k]_Q
                 (Q^(N-b);Q)_k U^k(U)_(2b-k). (2)

At a root Q^N=1, (2) reduces to the previously proved finite square linearization. GENERICALLY its factor (Q^(N-b);Q)_k must remain; it cannot be replaced by its root value before taking a normal.

2. A source-exact generic Euler-moment formula

Write

c_e=[U^e](U;Q)_b(UQ^N;Q)_b,
M_(a,n)=sum_(k=0)^(d-a) Q^[(a+1+n)k]
                      F_(d-k)F_(a+k)/(F_(d-a-k)F_k),
P=2a^2-b^2+3c^2-2ac+2bc
                        -d(a+b+2c+1)+b+c.

Then the ACTUAL analytic source sum from the generic six-head note is

B_abc=(-1)^(d+b-c) zeta^P/(F_a F_b F_c F_r1)
   *sum_(e=0)^r c_e Q^(-ce)
                     F_(2b-e)/F_(r-e) M_(a,r-e). (3)

There is a second elementary expression for its moments:

M_(a,n)=F_a^2 [z^(d-a)]
                   (zQ^(a+1);Q)_n/(z;Q)_(2a+2+n). (4)

Proof of (4). Divide the summand by F_a^2 and use the two Gaussian coefficients [a+k choose a]_Q[d-k choose a]_Q. Their convolution is the coefficient of z^(d-a) in

1/[(z;Q)_(a+1)(zQ^(a+1+n);Q)_(a+1)]
   =(zQ^(a+1);Q)_n/(z;Q)_(2a+2+n).

The reciprocal finite-product coefficient formula is proved by induction on its length, so this is a finite generating-function calculation.

Here are the source steps giving (3), including its phase. The generic cup/highest-vector ratio simplifies exactly to

h_j(u)/P_d(u)=Q^(-ju)[j+u choose j]_Q.

Expand the middle W entry in the actual B using its physical coproduct formula. With k,z its two summation variables, the result is

B=zeta^C/(F_a F_b F_c) sum_(k,z)
   (-1)^(k+z) zeta^[(k+z)^2
                      +(2a+2c-2d+1)k+(2a-2d-1)z]
   *F_(d-k)F_(a+k)F_(b+z)F_(d-z)
   /[F_(d-a-k)F_k F_z F_(d-b-z)
                   F_(a+b-d+k+z)F_(d-c-k-z)],
C=2a^2+2c^2-d(3a+b+2c)+d^2.                  (5)

All ranges here are the true physical ranges: every factorial index is nonnegative. Put k+z=d-a-b+p. The last two factorials become F_p F_(r1-p), so 0<=p<=r1. The two ratios involving z are

(Q^(d-a-b+p-k+1);Q)_b
              (Q^(a+k-p+1);Q)_b.

The k range extends to 0,…,d-a: each added nonphysical state is killed by a factor 1-Q^0 in one of these two finite products, before any root specialization. Reverse the second product. Up to the displayed overall phase it gives

f_k(T)=(TQ^(d-a-b-k+1);Q)_b(TQ^(-a-b-k);Q)_b,
sum_(p=0)^r1 (-1)^p Q^[p(p-1)/2-2bp]
                                  [r1 choose p]_Q f_k(Q^p).

On its monomial T^e this operator multiplies by (Q^(e-2b);Q)_r1. It is EXACTLY zero for r<e<=2b; for e<=r it is

(-1)^r1 Q^[(e-2b)r1+r1(r1-1)/2]
                                  F_(2b-e)/F_(r-e).

Since f_k(T)=f_0(TQ^-k) and the two starts of f_0 differ by Q^N, its coefficient is Q1c_e. Its combination with Q^(er1) is Q^(-ce). The remaining k sum is M_(a,r-e). The outside signs and zeta powers simplify to those in (3). This proves (3) by finite Gaussian algebra, at both parities and including all high partners.

3. The finite gap in this reduction, now closed by the source recurrence

Equations (2)–(4) retain the GENERIC normal, unlike the old root Fourier/square reduction. But their remaining Euler coefficient and finite quantum-Hahn moment are not collapsed directly in this note. The separate generic source-column proof now establishes the desired single-sum identity, preserving precisely these normal terms. The root identity Q^N=1 turns the two Euler products into a square; doing that first loses the normal of their relative shift.

For example, the proposed single-sum representation in the next section has, when a=max(a,b,c) and b+c<=d, the balanced series

4phi3(Q^-r,Q^(N+a+1),Q^-b,Q^-c;
           Q^(N-b-c),Q^(a-b+1),Q^(a-c+1);Q,Q).

The independently proved finite Sears identity transforms it to a series with numerator parameters

Q^-r, Q^-b, Q^(-J-1), Q^(N-b),

and denominators Q(N-b-c),Q(-b-c),Q^(-2b). At the root the apparent extra termination length is delta=J-d. GENERICALLY its length is r, and terms k>delta carry a root zero whose derivative need not vanish. They cannot be removed by differentiating the root-short formula. This is a precise remaining normal-source issue, not evidence that the desired transform fails.

3a. Exact normalization to a reciprocal polynomial of bounded color degree

This subsection is a further PROVED reduction of the generic source. Assume a=max(a,b,c), put X=Q^N and A=Q^(a+1), and define

H_n(X)=sum_(t=0)^n [n choose t]_Q
                              (A;Q)_t(A;Q)_(n-t) X^t,
P_a(X)=(XQ^-a;Q)_(2a+1).

The moment from (4) factors as

M_(a,n)=F_a^2 P_a(X)/F_(2a+n+1) *H_n(X).      (5a)

For a finite proof, expand the numerator in (4). Its t-th term has the factor (XQ^(-a-t);Q)_(2a+n+1), whose common subproduct is P_a(X). The remaining two tails give

sum_(t=0)^n [n choose t]_Q
                       X^t(A/X;Q)_t(AX;Q)_(n-t)=H_n(X).

Here is an own formal-coefficient verification of the last identity, without infinite-product evaluation. The series with coefficients (B;Q)k/F_k is the unique initial-value-one formal series G_B(z) with G_B(Qz)=(1-z)G_B(z)/(1-Bz), as checked coefficient by coefficient. Both products G(A/X)(Xz)G_(AX)(z) and G_A(z)G_A(Xz) have the SAME functional equation and initial value, so their finite degree-n coefficients agree. Only coefficients through n are used.

Define the explicit SOURCE polynomial

R_SRC(X)=sum_(e=0)^r
   c_e(X) Q^(-ce) F_(2b-e) H_(r-e)(X)
                        /[F_(r-e)F_(J+1-e)],
c_e(X)=[U^e](U;Q)_b(UX;Q)_b.                 (5b)

Equations (3),(5a) give the precise actual normalization

B_abc=(-1)^(d+b-c) zeta^P F_a/(F_b F_c F_r1)
                                  *P_a(X) R_SRC(X). (5c)

Every c_e is reciprocal of degree at most e in the sense X^e c_e(1/X)=c_e(X). The same assertion holds for H_n of degree n. Consequently

deg R_SRC<=r,
X^r R_SRC(1/X)=R_SRC(X).                      (5d)

All dependence on the physical d in the residual polynomial has disappeared: it enters only through X and the known outside factor. This is not a polynomial fit to physical data.

The exact TARGET polynomial corresponding to the proposed Racah sum in (6) is

R_TGT(X)=Q^(br) F_b^2 F_c^2 F_r1/F_(J+1)
   *sum_(k=0)^r (-1)^k
     Q^[3k(k-1)/2+(a-2b-2c+1)k]
     (XQ^(-a-r+k);Q)_(r-k)(XQ^(a+1);Q)_k
   /[F_k F_(a-b+k)F_(a-c+k)F_(b-k)F_(c-k)F_(r-k)]. (5e)

To derive the conversion, first take d>=b+c and write z=d+a+k in the proposed sum. Replacing each symmetric factorial by zeta(-n(n-1)/2)F_n/(1-Q)n cancels every 1-Q factor. The ratio F_(N+a+k)/F_(d-b-c+k) is (XQ^(-b-c+k);Q)_(J+1). Its common factor P_a leaves exactly the two tails displayed in (5e). The remaining zeta exponent, relative to P in (5c), is 2br+3k(k-1)+2(a-2b-2c+1)k. The signs agree. The needed finite polynomial identity is

R_SRC(X)=R_TGT(X)                             (5f)

and is now established by the separate source recurrence, own finite Sears transformation and infinite-d polynomial argument. It covers d<b+c and high partners: in the fixed k range, a formally negative bottom index d-b-c+k is represented by the product (XQ^(-b-c+k);Q)_(J+1), which then contains the LITERAL factor 1-Q^0 and vanishes. For every physical d>=a, P_a(Q^N) is a generic unit. Thus precisely the inadmissible extra k terms vanish, restoring the correct lower limit z>=J without assigning a nonzero factorial to a negative index. Neither matching degrees nor exact finite-field tests alone prove (5f).

There is at least one exact triangular interpolation value. Substituting (2) in the coefficient form of (5b) gives

R_SRC(X)=sum_(k=0)^r C_k F_(2b-k)/F_(J+1-k) Q^(-ck)
   *[U^(r-k)] (UQ^-c;Q)_c(UQ^(a+1);Q)_(b-k)
                                  /(UX;Q)_(a+1),
C_k=Q^(bk)[b choose k]_Q(XQ^-b;Q)_k.          (5g)

All manipulations here need only coefficients through r; the weighted Euler linearization agrees through that order even when its formal tail extends farther. At X=Q^b only k=0 survives. Canceling the common positive Euler interval and taking the coefficient gives

R_SRC(Q^b)=F_(2b)/F_(J+1)
               *Q^(br)[c choose r]_Q(Q^(-b-c);Q)_r. (5h)

Explicitly the remaining coefficient is [U^r](UQ%5E-c)c divided by (UQ^b)(c-r+1). Expanding its numerator, the two Gaussian factors combine to [c choose r][r choose p]; the finite binomial theorem then gives (5h). At X=Q^(b-s), 0<=s<=r, the k sum in (5g) truncates at k=s. The exact higher interpolation values, or an equivalent direct coefficient certificate for (5f), are not evaluated here. The complete identity is supplied by the independently proved actual channel recurrence, rather than these interpolation values.

4. The normalized single-Racah identity, proved by the source recurrence

For symmetric quantum factorials

[n]!_zeta=zeta^[-n(n-1)/2]F_n/(1-Q)^n,

define the finite sum

S_R=sum_z (-1)^z [z+1]!
   /([z-J]! product_j[z-d-j]!
      [d+a+b-z]![d+a+c-z]![d+b+c-z]!),
max(J,d+a,d+b,d+c)<=z
                  <=min(d+a+b,d+a+c,d+b+c).

The precisely normalized identity, now proved in the linked generic source-column theorem, is

B_abc = (-1)^J zeta^[a^2-b^2+c^2-ab-ac+bc-cd]
              *[a]![b]![c]!/[J+1]! *S_R.       (6)

The phase and factors agree with exact generic physical calculations, including high partner channels. Those comparisons are NOT the proof of (6): the linked proof uses the actual source coefficient COLUMN, uniform Fricke/boundary certificates and our own finite AW/Sears calculation. Formula (3) remains an independent Euler source reduction.

The low-fold source zero and the one [N] factor in (6) can be compared term by term with the existing positive root Racah partition. High-partner ranges that cross N still require their actual zero orders and coherent cancellations. Neither a general boundary-conditioning theorem nor arbitrary-graph growth is asserted here.
