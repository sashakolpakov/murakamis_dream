Independent audit of the partial geometric-support construction

Audit, 27 October 2026, of the partial geometric-support theorem. The new partial-sum argument is valid given the repository’s actual critical-chart/Hessian theorem and the global zero-extended finite-array dimension lemma. In particular its transverse moment argument avoids assuming the missing covariance of a full framing-period functional. This audit does not assert the selected global period is nonzero on the geometric branch.

1. Why a pair of actual external coordinates suffices

Let V be the tangent space of actual independent flow coordinates, with symmetric Hessian B and two-dimensional radical R. A coordinate projection p:V->k^2 whose restriction to R is invertible exists by selecting a nonzero two-by-two minor of a basis of R. Put K=ker p. Then V=R direct_sum K and B restricted to K is nondegenerate: any vector orthogonal to K is also orthogonal to R, and hence to V; if it belongs to K it is zero.

Fixing those two original coordinates therefore gives precisely a nondegenerate transverse stationary problem. Its internal critical equations have a unique local solution over the external coordinates and meridian. The full two-dimensional framed critical germ maps etale to those same external coordinates, so its internal solution is that unique solution. Thus the full critical gradient vanishes on the partially contracted branch, including its external components. The critical action consequently has zero frame derivatives and retains the marked unframed meridian/longitude derivative.

This is stronger than choosing an arbitrary framing section after performing the finite sum: the external labels remain genuine independent variables in both the certificate and the formal block.

2. Exact contiguity survives the specialization used here

The global certificate annihilates the entire zero-extended array, including the finite boundary. Dividing by a nonzero common central power of z+1 is valid for every fixed generic integer N, since q^N+1 is a nonzero rational function of q. The full-array identity therefore specializes regularly at z=-1, q=q_N, after fixed scalar denominators are cleared. A nonzero fixed rational operator can have only finitely many exceptional q_N levels.

Separately, the interior cone-density argument makes the divided certificate a rational contiguity identity in independent variables q,x,z,Ahat,Bhat,t. That identity can also be specialized at z=-1. This separate independent-variable identity is essential: a relation only on a finite frame grid at one root would not suffice for the formal Gaussian block.

The compact-product formal solution has exactly the same interior shift ratios, since its Bernoulli series obeys the functional equation P_Q(Qz)=P_Q(z)/(1-z) coefficientwise. Fixed singular arguments such as P_Q(Q) occur only in the retained common base line; all variables on the chosen regular geometric chart avoid the polar divisors. Thus the formal identity uses rational factor cancellation, never evaluation of an individual infinite compact product at a root.

3. The finite and formal moment repairs agree exactly

The physical logarithmic internal coordinate is u_i=Hj_i, H=Log Q. Hence exp(v dot j) is implemented in the formal period by the phase S+v dot u, divided by H. Its critical point deforms uniquely because the transverse Hessian is invertible. The weighted formal period belongs to the original WKB line times k((H))[[v]]; its negative H orders may grow with the v degree. No uniform lower H bound over all v degrees is required.

Formal Gaussian contraction satisfies integration by parts. For the quadratic kernel exp(x^t B x/(2H)), Wick covariance is -H B^(-1); contraction of a derivative of a monomial cancels the contraction of (Bx)_i/H times that monomial. Expanding cubic and higher vertices gives the same identity coefficientwise, with finitely many contractions in each allowed order. Thus a formal total derivative has zero contraction.

Translate one internal coordinate by H. The weighted integrand then changes by exp(-v_i) after the corresponding formal change of coordinate. Equivalently the infinitesimal translation equation is G_v(H partial_i f)=-v_i G_v(f); integrating the translation parameter gives G_v(S_i f)=exp(-v_i)G_v(f). The H-dependent shift is a valid formal translation around the unique critical germ. The determinant half-line is continued with that same germ, rather than rechoosing its square root after shifting.

External multiplication and the meridian step commute with this contraction. The latter shifts log M by H/2, while Ahat,Bhat are held fixed, and carries the critical point to its unique nearby one. It leaves H and the retained common base line fixed.

There are no internal multiplication variables in the certificate’s operator coefficients. Therefore its expansion in (S-1) has exactly the same least nonzero multi-degree after either finite weighted summation or formal transverse contraction. At that degree every positive moment is multiplied by a lower, identically zero operator coefficient. The same nonzero D_alpha annihilates the actual partial finite family and its nonzero transverse formal block.

This would not justify an unproved integration functional in either of the two remaining framing directions. They remain external.

4. The leading-symbol conclusion and its scope

After dividing the common scalar q-1 valuation, the operator has a nonzero leading classical symbol. The transverse formal block has unit leading amplitude and the actual marked longitude as its leading meridian ratio. Its annihilation therefore forces that symbol to vanish on the marked geometric longitude branch.

The external coordinates range over a dense open frame chart by the etaleness in Section 1. They are algebraically independent over the geometric branch field; a purely transcendental extension does not split its irreducible rational defining polynomial. Thus the stated A_geo factor divisibility over Q(Ahat,Bhat) is valid.

The conclusion concerns the specifically constructed partial-state annihilator. It neither identifies its minimal operator nor says that the full finite scalar contraction retains that component. In particular a nonzero local formal transverse block is not yet a nonzero selected global geometric contribution. No global cycle, Stokes, cancellation, or volume conclusion has been added here.
