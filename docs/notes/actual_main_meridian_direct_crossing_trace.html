A filled crossing as an exact polynomial trace

The operator obtained by filling one crossing circle is an actual fixed power of the crossing. It can therefore be studied without first expanding that power in the moving auxiliary-color basis. The result below is local: it applies to a fixed product of regular low Newton factors at one crossing-circle junction. It supplies a nonzero leading term, an exponentially accurate analytic interpolation in the incident wire variables, and its exact dependence on the microscopic main meridian. Section 6 identifies its action with the marked long-filling action. The last section explains how the local estimate enters the grouped wire packet and why the joint filling transform is needed when different twist powers are independently long.

The polynomial coordinates and the typed crossing are fixed by the Newton compiler and the turn comparison. The canonical sine-factorial interpolation used below is also derived in the regular circle trace, Section 10. All crossing powers are fixed before the level tends to infinity.
