Hardy norms of the cyclic dilogarithm and its finite coefficients

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The original norm-one cyclic dilogarithm and its inverse belong to Hardy space. Projecting either symbol to its actual first NN Taylor coefficients loses at most a power of the level in norm. The same comparison holds on every concentric disk, uniformly down to radius zero. This controls scalar symbol norms, with their original phases and normalizations; it does not estimate a marked word from below.

2. Square integrability at every root

Put ζj=Q−j\zeta_j=Q^{-j}, βj=(N−1−2j)/(2N)\beta_j=(N-1-2j)/(2N). P’s exact factorization is VN(z)=∏j=0N−1(1−Qjz)βj,−12<βj<12.(5) V_N(z)=\prod_{j=0}^{N-1}(1-Q^jz)^{\beta_j},\qquad -\tfrac12<\beta_j<\tfrac12. \qquad\text{(5)} For each fixed NN, near ζj\zeta_j the other factors form a holomorphic unit. The two boundary square moduli therefore have local powers 2βj2\beta_j and −2βj-2\beta_j, both greater than −1-1. They are integrable. The same powers dominate radial approaches: for r≥1/2r\ge1/2, |1−reiφ|≥c|φ||1-re^{i\varphi}|\ge c|\varphi| near zero, while positive powers stay bounded. With the other factors bounded away from their roots, this gives an integrable dominating function for each fixed NN. Away from the roots the radial limits are uniform. Dominated convergence and Parseval for r<1r<1 prove that both functions belong to H2H^2, and their norms equal their boundary L2L^2 norms. No bound uniform in NN is claimed in this preliminary argument; the next step supplies it.

5. Source meaning and scope

H already identifies (2) as the physical jets and proves the exact ordered support identities for the completed cyclic/Hardy network. C retains the full original source, reflected factorials, Gaussian weights, caps, Cartan factors and external scalar. This note changes none of those operators or projectors. The new conclusion is the polynomial norm comparison with the full scalar symbols, including all radii and the reflected normalization in (11).

These are estimates for the two symbol vectors and their dilates. They do not assert a bounded multiplication operator on arbitrary Hardy inputs, a lower bound for a coherent word contraction, or nonvanishing of its last marked observation. Norm comparison does not select a geometric cycle or prove the finite-exception theorem.
