The actual cap has affine winding one and a degree-blind unit carry wall

28 October 2026. The original cap has an exact charged winding equation D+1=N, with the affine +1 forced by its actual dual color. Its signed carry current is nevertheless identically zero. The original marked cap detects the canonical cyclic/noncyclic wall with the nonzero WHOLE meridian value -x/N. The two individual wall charges each have marked augmentation one; total degree sees only their difference.

This does not identify the last oversized geometric edge with an actual quantum head or prove its coefficient zero/nonzero. It fixes the affine charge and augmented wall class that comparison must carry. The existing common-tree carry lattice and cyclic sewing identities are not re-proved or called new here. The new point is the actual dual condition, four-atom degree calculation, and original marked source evaluation together.

1. The source fixes the cyclic wrap ratio

Use the original semicyclic heads V(x,phi), with

K v_a=xQ^-a v_a,
F v_a=v_(a+1) (a<d), F v_d=phi v_0,
d=N-1, k=x^N.

The actual dual is V(y,psi), with

y=x^-1 Q^-1,        psi=-k phi.                      (1)

These are the proved actual oriented duals, not freely chosen cyclic directions. In particular the total F center on their cup is phi+k^-1 psi=0. For phi!=0 form the actual four-atom monomial

A=F_1^-1 tensor F_2.

Let W_d have basis b_a=v_a tensor v_(d-a), 0<=a<N. It is stable under A, whose exact restriction is

A b_a=b_(a-1) for a>0,
A b_0=-k b_d.                                      (2)

The ratio psi/phi=-k cancels phi. Consequently (2) has a canonical phi-independent extension to the nilpotent source. Let T be the nonwrapped part, T b_0=0 and T b_a=b_(a-1). The source’s missing edge is exactly

A-T=-k B,       B=|b_d><b_0|,
A^N=-k I,       T^N=0.                             (3)

The central -k is retained, not replaced by a complete numerical value. This is the canonical carry limit of the genuine cyclic monomial, rather than an arbitrary added wrap.

2. The total degree current is zero on that actual fiber

Let J_i have the literal integer eigenvalues 0,…,d and D=J_1+J_2. On the full two-head space, let P_1 project to first index 0 and P_2 project to second index d. The EXACT cyclic degree identity is

[D,A]=N A(P_1-P_2).                                (4)

Indeed lowering the first index changes J_1 by -1 except at its wrap, where it changes by N-1; raising the second changes J_2 by 1 except at its wrap, where it changes by 1-N. This proves (4) with the actual nonzero wrap coefficients as well.

For the four genuine crossing monomials in the four-atom factorization,

m_i=c_i A S_1^(b_i),
b_i=(-1,0,0,1),
c_i=(-Qx,x^-1,Qx,-x^-1),

the same equation holds with A replaced by m_i, since the integer degree commutes with each clock S_1. Thus the physical total degree records a DIFFERENCE of the two wrap charges.

On W_d, exactly

D=d I,       P_1=P_2=P_0=|b_0><b_0|.               (5)

The degree current (4) therefore vanishes identically, although the common deleted edge (3) is nonzero. Any spectral projector depending ONLY on D is either zero or identity on this whole fiber. It cannot remove the cyclic wall while retaining its noncyclic source. Thus the signed carry CURRENT alone does not identify the common augmented class.

The degree VALUE, in contrast, carries a precise affine winding. The actual dual color has xy=Q^-1, so on the entire two-head space

K_total=Q^(-1-D).

Consequently its eigenvalue-one subspace is exactly W_d: since 0<=D<=2N-2, the only possible multiple of N for D+1 is N. Every actual invariant cup therefore obeys

D+1=N,       W_cap=(D+1)/N=1.                      (5a)

This is an exact integer equation, not a choice of log lift or only an asymptotic statement. The affine +1 is forced by the original dual K root. It excludes total degree zero on this channel. The same conclusion holds for the remaining pair after the other ordinary caps of any actual plat are contracted.

What remains unproved is that a new Pachner edge’s three angular indices give THIS charged D+1. A zero Euler-transfer route in the actual crossing does not disprove (5a): its cup input can be (d,0), so that zero transfer still has charged degree N.

Individually the two degree equations retain an affine term:

[J_1,A]=-A+N A P_0,
[J_2,A]= A-N A P_0.

Since A is invertible on this fiber, the winding cochain is

P_0=(I+A^-1[J_1,A])/N,       Tr(P_0)=1.             (6)

Its unit augmentation disappears on summing the two degree currents. The signed total-degree carry is a coboundary with zero cyclic sum; the single common wall retains the fundamental unit cyclic class. An edge-winding comparison needs this augmented class, not just D.

3. The original marked source detects the missing class

Use the balanced basis w_a=v_a/F_a only to display the actual source simply. From the exact bridge cap, write b_a^bal=w_a tensor w_(d-a), distinct from b_a in Section 1.

cup coefficient at b_a^bal =N x^(a-d),
marked_cap(b_a^bal)=x^(d-a)/N^2.                    (7)

These include the original averaged cut, pivot and marked defect. Their product at EACH a is 1/N, so the full marked bubble is one. The change from v to w has equal factorial products at b_0 and b_d, hence it leaves the edge coefficient -k in (3) unchanged. For the original functional Phi(O)=marked_cap O cup, exactly

Phi(I)=1,
Phi(P_1)=Phi(P_2)=Phi(P_0)=1/N,
Phi(P_1-P_2)=0,
Phi(A-T)=(-k) x^-d/N=-x/N !=0.                    (8)

Thus each individual wall has N Phi(P_0)=1, while total degree sees zero. The canonical carry coupon has a nonzero original source value on the ENTIRE meridian family. At completeness it is q^-1/N, but (8) has been obtained before that specialization. No peripheral factor has been reset.

At the complete self-dual color, the bare four-atom monomial wall values in this common source frame are respectively (1,-1,-1,1)/N. This follows by multiplying (8) by the c_i and using S_1^(b_i)b_0=b_0 and Qx^2=1. Their sum is zero, but none is zero. This is a common-frame COUPON calculation; it does not replace the differently transported exterior source at each separate factor of the full crossing.

The Ward augmentation theorem and cap-residue theorem give complementary forms of the same obstruction: the marked unit wall and its actual residue survive, while some signed Ward currents can be contracted. Equation (8) supplies the literal whole-meridian source value for the dual-cup carry.

4. The actual cap forces a four-atom norm wall

The precise bare ratio in the four-atom crossing is r=phi_2/phi_1. At a true dual cap it is fixed by (1): r=-k. At completeness k^2=1 and the source color is self-dual. To inspect the local normal direction perturb r=-k+epsilon, keeping that complete color fixed. The four displayed transported atom centers are exactly

1-k epsilon, 1-k/epsilon,
1-k/epsilon, 1-k epsilon,

with norm-root powers

k epsilon, (k epsilon)^-1,
(k epsilon)^-1, k epsilon.                         (9)

Thus their compatible root valuations are (1/N,-1/N,-1/N,1/N). The actual cap is already on this mandatory wall; setting a zero norm factor to zero cannot delete it. Full common-frame products and the original relative source are required. This links the old abstract fusion-wall condition to the literal four-atom factors.

There is also a FULL, uninserted original-source test at this wall, rather than only the coupon test (8). The strict actual crossing core from the bridge theorem satisfies

R_+ C_x|d,0>=kappa C_y|d,0>,

because its Euler shift kills |d,0> and its Gaussian there is one. At completeness y=x, so the original marked one-bridge plat evaluates the whole crossing to exactly its retained nonzero common Cartan scalar kappa. The original zero-framing adapter gives the unknot value one. Equivalently this follows directly from the physical output (0,d): only original input (d,0) and zero transfer contribute, fixing the scalar. Thus the mandatory four-atom norm wall has nonzero coupling to the FULL original source; no auxiliary source vector has been chosen. This remains a test of the actual cap wall (9), not an assertion that it is the last geometric Pachner wall in the oversized example.

A tempting but invalid shortcut is to match two displayed centers whose sum is one. The first two actual bare monomials satisfy m_1 m_2=Q m_2 m_1. The two-factor side of the retained primitive pentagon is D(Y)D(bX), with XY=QYX, so its first/second bare commutation is Q^-1. The actual first pair also DIVIDES the second center by its first norm, whereas that pentagon side MULTIPLIES it. Center matching alone therefore does not put that Pachner move inside this actual word; an additional typed quad/shear/source map is necessary. The two commutation phases differ for N>2; at N=2 they coincide, but the transported norm factors still require the same explicit typed check.

5. Consequence for the oversized winding-zero face

The last Pachner-wall calculation shows that the geometric positive angular branch needs internal edge winding one, and the weak oversized face has winding zero. The present theorem neither admits nor removes that face from the original cycle. It supplies the exact cap-side mechanism: the charged degree is N, while the signed carry current has lost the common augmentation (8). Plain exponentiated edge gluing or the current alone would omit different parts of this data.

What is now required is a literal chain/source map from the new Pachner edge to this AUGMENTED cyclic class, with the compatible log/angle lift and all three new face sources. A claim that the new integer angles sum to N must prove that map, including any affine charge. It cannot be inferred from unwrapped total-degree conservation or from the exponentiated edge equation alone.

For clarity, the elementary angular criterion such a map could use is uniform: if nonnegative integer angles n_j have a positive fixed affine charge c, satisfy sum n_j+c=0 modulo N, and 0<sum n_j+c<2N, then sum n_j+c=N. A zero-charge equation admits the collapsed all-zero branch. Equation (5a) proves c=1 on the actual cap channel. It has not proved c=1 on the new Pachner edge; that is the precise remaining local chain-map target.

The generic primitive pentagon, carry-tree lattice and local atom order do not yet supply this missing identification. No new source-preserving geometric 2↔︎3 theorem or volume claim is asserted by (1)–(9).

6. Exact original-matrix checks

The audit and saved results pass 44 original dual-cap families at N=2,…,12, with complete and generic meridians and two nonzero semicyclic parameters. They construct caps, cups, marked defect and pivot from the original duality matrices, then check (4)–(8), the exact charged K_total eigenspace, all four bare monomial currents/centers, and the FULL mixed crossing source using independent original E and Cartan coefficients. The retained complete Cartan and twist give zero-framed value one. Thirty-three exact perturbed four-atom norm charts check (9) and both level parities. The proofs establish all N; these checks verify the original source conventions and transported scalar factors.

7. The paired Fermat completion has local cyclic source equations

This section checks the projected paired source proposed in the tame paired-source construction. It is a source FRAME family; its parameter is not identified with the semicyclic phi or the meridian normal. Literal conjugation of the whole currents and word is required when using it away from zero. Its endpoint is the original relative pair |d,0>.

Put epsilon=1-delta^N, with delta=1 at epsilon=0, and define

V_j=epsilon/[(1-Q)(Q^2 delta;Q)_j
                              (Q delta;Q)_(d-j)],
W_j=(Q delta;Q)_j(delta;Q)_(d-j)/N, 0<=j<N.        (10)

These are regular at the marked epsilon=0 after whole cancellation, with V(0)=e_d and W(0)=e_d^t. For generic epsilon the consecutive ratios are EXACTLY

V_(j+1)/V_j=(1-delta Q^(-j-1))/(1-delta Q^(j+2)),
W_(j+1)/W_j=(1-delta Q^(j+1))/(1-delta Q^(-j-2)),  (11)

where indices are CYCLIC. For j<d these follow by cancelling one factor. The wrap uses the whole endpoint identities

V_0=(1-delta)/(1-Q), V_d=(1-delta Q)/(1-Q),
W_0=epsilon/[N(1-delta Q^-1)],
W_d=epsilon/[N(1-delta)].

Thus (11) holds also at j=d; no endpoint source has been omitted. Let A have eigenvalues Q^j and let T act on COMPONENTS by (Tf)j=f(j+1 mod N), so TA=QAT. The bounded-complexity source and CO-SOURCE equations are

[(1-delta Q^2 A)T-(1-delta Q^-1 A^-1)]V=0,
W[(1-delta Q^-1 A^-1)T^-1-(1-delta QA)]=0.          (12)

The second is a right row action: the coefficient of its jth component is W_(j+1)(1-delta Q^(-j-2)) -W_j(1-delta Q^(j+1)). In particular W is not a left kernel of the FIRST operator. The original marked co-source must not be changed into an ordinary adjoint cap by that substitution.

On a regular classical chart with Q,delta tending to one, both symbols describe p=-A^-1, respecting the source/co-source orientations. Their Gaussian phase is the cancellation of the two individual dilogarithmic head actions. The exact endpoint A=1 still requires the original pointed normalization in (10).

8. Its normalized diagonal source is an exact discrete Poisson measure

Let G=sum_j W_jV_j and R=V_d. Finite-product cancellation gives

W_jV_j=R epsilon(1-delta)/
   [N(1-delta Q^(j+1))(1-delta Q^(-j-1))],
G=R(2-epsilon)/(1+delta).                          (13)

For the sum, the exact partial-fraction identity gives

sum_(n=0)^d 1/[(1-delta Q^n)(1-delta Q^-n)]
   =N(1+delta^N)/[(1-delta^N)(1-delta^2)].

This follows by summing 1/(1-delta z)+1/(1-delta/z)-1 over z^N=1 and dividing by 1-delta^2; the single sums are N/(1-delta^N). Substitution proves (13), with its actual nonzero scalar.

Normalize w=W/G. The diagonal weights pi_j=w_jV_j are

pi_j=epsilon(1-delta^2)/
   [N(2-epsilon)(1-delta Q^(j+1))
                     (1-delta Q^(-j-1))],
sum_j pi_j=1.                                     (14)

For real 0<epsilon<1 and the marked real delta=(1-epsilon)^(1/N), these are STRICTLY POSITIVE discrete Poisson weights: the denominator product is |1-delta Q(j+1)|2. This is a positive DIAGONAL boundary functional. It does not make the full transformed braid kernel positive.

All its clock moments are explicit. For 0<=m<=N,

sum_j pi_j Q^(m(j+1))
           =(delta^m+delta^(N-m))/(1+delta^N).     (15)

For |delta|<1, expand the two denominator factors in geometric series and sum the root characters. Only exponent differences congruent to m survive; the root-summed denominator is N/(1-delta^2) times (deltam+delta(N-m))/(1-delta^N). Multiplying by the prefactor in (14) proves (15), with the same formula at m=0,N. Algebraic continuation gives the general marked identity. Moments of A itself have the additional retained factor Q^-m.

Thus the paired source has a local cyclic operator, its true co-source operator, a normalized positive real slice, and exact finite moments. It retains the charged origin j+1 and tends to the actual d-corner at epsilon=0. It does not identify the Pachner edge’s angular variables or prove geometric global noncancellation. The current/frame transport and relative source must still accompany the whole word.

The real paired source is a positive rank-one density

In fact the real-slice positivity is stronger than its diagonal. Put A_scalar=epsilon/[N(1-delta)]>0 and R=V_d. Exact root complement reflection gives

W=(A_scalar/conjugate R) V^dagger,
||V||_2^2=|R|^2(2-epsilon)/[(1+delta)A_scalar],
w=W/G=V^dagger/||V||_2^2.                          (16)

For j<d, write A_m=(Q delta;Q)m. Then conjugate A(j+1)=epsilon/[(1-delta)A_(d-j-1)] and conjugate A_(d-j)=epsilon/[(1-delta)A_j]. Substituting in V_j gives conjugate V_j=conjugate R (1-delta)^2 A_j A_(d-j-1)/epsilon, while W_j=(1-delta)A_j A_(d-j-1)/N. At j=d, W_d=A_scalar and V_d=R directly. This proves the first equation; (13) proves the others.

Thus E_pair=Vw is an ORTHOGONAL rank-one projector for real 0<epsilon<1, and its diagonal is exactly (14). Its norms stay uniformly controlled on every real epsilon<=rho<1: use

1-epsilon<=A_scalar<=1,
|R|^2=delta+(1-delta)^2/[4sin^2(pi/N)],
N(1-delta)<=-log(1-epsilon).

This gives positive upper and lower bounds in (16), independent of N. It does not make a general oriented braid operator positive or establish a lower bound for its complex matrix coefficient.

The same exact audit checks 33 paired cyclic parameters, both wraps and operator orders, the normalization (13), and every moment m=0,…,N. Eighteen positive real-slice diagnostics check the orthogonal-density and norm identities (16). Equations (10)–(15) retain the Q^-m factor for the unshifted clock.
