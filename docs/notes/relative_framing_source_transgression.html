Relative framing transgression and the literal finite cut projector

Research note, 27 October 2026. This constructs an explicit relative boundary complex whose source and co-source are the original finite cut projector e and marked defect D. The marked meridian supplies the relative fiber endpoints; no Haar-volume division is introduced. There are two exact presentations: an intrinsic finite-root residue model and a smaller logarithmic residue model using the original canonical representatives. Their chain projection is literally e.

This realizes the finite boundary source. It does not identify the internal quantum pushforward with the geometric Gaussian framing pushforward. In particular it does not prove survival of the geometric block in the selected global period or the volume conjecture.

1. The classical form needs a relative source

At the complete meridian M=1, use the original framing chart

Omega_cut=-dr wedge ds/[s(1+s)],
V=s partial_r, A=1/(1+s), t=r/s.

Then V=partial_t and

beta=dlog A=-ds/(1+s),
Omega_cut=dt wedge beta,
iota_V Omega_cut=beta.                       (1.1)

The ordinary two-form is exact: Omega_cut=d(t beta). This primitive is not harmless for a relative source, because its endpoint values are different.

The relative endpoints have a marked geometric meaning. The regular meridian centralizer in the existing note is C_b=[1+kappa*b b;0 1], with kappa=M-M^(-1), and its action at M=1 is r->r+b s. The meridian itself is the centralizer section b=M, so at M=1 it translates t to t+1. A choice of initial framing section therefore gives the relative pair t=0,t=1. Replacing that section by t_0(A) translates both endpoints together. Since (dt-dt_0) wedge beta=dt wedge beta, the transgressed source below is independent of this choice on its invariant forms whenever the section change is regular on the retained source chart. A section with a pole at an added root requires its own boundary lattice data; no extension through that pole is inferred here.

The geometric invertible-framing chart has s!=0, so A!=1. For the finite cut source we use the explicit extension

F_tilde=A^1_t times G_m,A.

It agrees with the framing chart at A!=1. Its added A=1 divisor is boundary source data, not an assertion that the original geometric frame remains invertible there.

The generic meridian has a further marked period which must not be silently replaced by one. At fixed M, t=r/s gives

Omega_cut=[dt/(1+kappa*t)] wedge dlog A,
A=M/(M+s), V=(1+kappa*t)partial_t.

The meridian sends t=0 to t=M. On its specified local logarithmic branch its relative centralizer period is

L(M)=integral_0^M dt/(1+kappa*t)
     =Log(M^2)/kappa, L(1)=1.              (1.2)

This is a marked regular period, not a rational function. The centralizer power adapter already proved in the existing note gives

L(M^N)=[N/u_N(M)]L(M),
u_N(M)=(M^N-M^(-N))/(M-M^(-1)).              (1.3)

Indeed the adapted power map is t’=[(1+kappa*t)^N-1]/(kappau_N), and it pulls back the invariant differential to (N/u_N)dt/(1+kappat). Formula (1.3) retains exactly the actual modified-dimension factor 1/u_N. The generic centralizer also has an absolute multiplicative cycle, absent in the additive fiber. We do not identify its entire generic relative de Rham group with the polynomial additive resolution below. The finite source coefficients themselves retain their full M dependence throughout.

2. A relative complex with an explicit contraction

For any characteristic-zero vector space E define a two-term complex

R(E)^1=E[t],
R(E)^2=E[t] direct_sum E direct_sum E,
delta h=(partial_t h,h(0),h(1)).             (2.1)

All other degrees are zero. Define

L(a,b_0,b_1)=integral_0^1 a(t)dt-b_1+b_0,
i(f)=(f,0,0).                               (2.2)

The integral is polynomial antiderivation over a characteristic-zero field; no analytic convergence or contour choice is used. We have L delta=0 and L i=I. Moreover the explicit degree-minus-one map

H(a,b_0,b_1)(t)
   =b_0+integral_0^t [a(u)-L(a,b_0,b_1)]du  (2.3)

satisfies delta H+H delta=I-iL. Therefore

H^1 R(E)=0, H^2 R(E)=E, R(E) equivalent to E[-2]. (2.4)

Every linear map E->E’ acts coefficientwise on this complex and commutes with delta,L,i,H. This naturality will retain every actual finite quantum matrix, source and cut.

Geometrically, (2.1) is the relative de Rham complex along t=0,1 after taking a residue quotient in the A direction. A pair of the form (a(t)dt wedge omega,b_0 omega,b_1 omega) represents its degree two element, where omega is the chosen logarithmic normal form. The relative differential is (d omega,restriction omega-d eta). It gives precisely (2.1). The transgression is (2.2).

In particular (dt wedge beta,0,0) gives a nonzero relative class: its image is beta. The ordinary primitive t beta has endpoint pair (0,beta), so its relative coboundary is (dt wedge beta,0,beta), whose transgression vanishes. Ordinary exactness does not kill the sourced relative class.

3. The intrinsic finite-root residue realization

Fix the actual N>=2 and Q primitive of order N, over a field k containing its roots. Let

C_N=k[A,A^(-1)]/(A^N-1),
D_N={A^N=1},
omega_N=A^(N-1)dA/(A^N-1)
                   =(1/N)dlog(A^N-1).       (3.1)

On F_tilde take logarithmic forms along A^1_t times D_N modulo forms regular along that divisor, and take their relative cone for t=0,1. The quotient has terms C_N[t] omega_N in degree one and C_N[t] dt wedge omega_N in degree two; differentiation is partial_t. Its relative complex is exactly R(C_N).

The actual state f in C_N is the class i(f). Its cut functional is

Phi_N(a,b_0,b_1)=(1/N)Tr_(C_N/k) L(a,b_0,b_1). (3.2)

To see its literal residue meaning, first transgress in t and then sum residues at the N points of D_N. At A=Q^j,

Res_(A=Q^j) f omega_N=f(Q^j)/N.

Thus for an actual boundary vector f,

Phi_N i(f)=(1/N)sum_(j=0)^(N-1) f(Q^j)
                   =phi_N(f),              (3.3)

the original balanced co-source. This is intrinsic to the finite algebra: replacing a representative by (A^N-1)g changes its form by one regular at D_N, hence changes no residue class.

For a whole normal family retain U and use C_U=k[U,U^(-1),A]/(A^N-U). If f is regular at U=1, then

Tr_dR(f dlog A)=(Tr f/N)dlog U,
Tr_dR(f omega_N)=(Tr f/N)dlog(U-1),
Res_(U=1)Tr_dR(f omega_N)=phi_N(f|_(U=1)). (3.4)

Here omega_N is pulled back as (1/N)dlog(U-1). The factor U-1 is retained before specialization. Equation (3.4) is also a direct trace/residue realization of the source’s normal direction.

4. The logarithmic zero-residue source and the exact projector

Let R(k) be the same relative complex with logarithmic normal form beta=dA/A along the added A=0 source boundary of the A-line. This added boundary is not a new geometric critical point. For a finite state use its original canonical representative

f_tilde(A)=sum_(j=0)^(N-1) f_j A^j.

Transgression followed by Res_(A=0) sends (dt wedge f_tilde beta,0,0) to f_0. Root orthogonality gives

f_0=phi_N(f).                                (4.1)

Thus the original canonical representative defines a strict cochain map R(C_N)->R(k), applying phi_N to every coefficient. Its section R(k)->R(C_N) applies the constant vector psi=1. Their composition on R(C_N) is the coefficientwise chain map

e=psi phi_N=(1/N)sum_(j=0)^(N-1) S^j,
S f(A)=f(QA).                               (4.2)

This is the literal original finite cut projector, not just a map with the same scalar norm. In the evaluation basis A=Q^a, its matrix has every entry 1/N. It has rank one and e^2=e on H^2. The averaging identity and idempotence also hold strictly on the two-term resolution; rank one here refers to its finite H^2.

The two residue presentations agree by (3.3)–(4.1). They are not an identification of their unweighted ambient differential forms. In the D_N residue model the appropriate normal source is

omega_N=[A^N/(A^N-1)] beta.                  (4.3)

The unweighted beta is regular along D_N and has zero image in that particular quotient. In the A=0 model beta already is its logarithmic source. The chain map in (4.2), with the prescribed canonical representatives, is the exact comparison between them. Neither formula permits evaluating the source’s pole first and then discarding its normal class.

5. The original pivot and marked defect lift unchanged

In the same balanced evaluation basis, the original pivot is

g(A)=x^(1-N)A^(-1), x=-M/q.

This section first works in C_N, where A^N=1, so the displayed formula equals the literal power K^(1-N) for K=xA^(-1).

Multiplication by g and g^(-1) gives coefficientwise chain maps on R(C_N). Define

D=g^(-1)e.                                  (5.1)

Then on H^2, identified with the original color space, and also coefficientwise on its resolution,

gD=e, D^2=0, DgD=D, Tr_(H^2)(gD)=1.         (5.2)

Indeed phi_N(g(-1))=x(N-1)phi_N(A)=0 for N>=2. In the evaluation basis (5.1) has the original matrix D_(a,b)=x(N-1)Qa/N. For every finite cut matrix C,

Tr_(H^2)(g D C)=Tr_(C_N)(e C)
                        =phi_N(C psi).      (5.3)

Every such C lifts coefficientwise and commutes with the explicit relative contraction (2.3). Hence arbitrary finite internal matrix composition and contraction commute with this boundary realization. The same applies when tensoring the cut leg with the remaining finite color spaces. This is exact finite linear algebra at every level, not a semiclassical or knot-by-knot assertion.

The marked defect is still not an ordinary Hopf-intertwining coupon. This lift does not authorize moving D unchanged through a crossing. The whole full-ADO factor d_N(M), with its phase, pole, residue and normal jet, remains a separate marked line multiplying (5.3). In particular the 1/N from the cut source and the value eta_N/N of the modified dimension at completeness are distinct normalizations.

For a whole C_U normal extension, one must distinguish the reduced representative g_red=x(1-N)A(-1) from the literal clock power g_lit=(xA(-1))(1-N)=U g_red. Their defects are respectively D_red=g_red^(-1)e and D_lit=U^(-1)D_red. Both products equal e, so the cut comparisons above remain exact. Their physical normal derivatives do differ: D_phys g_red=-g_red, while D_phys g_lit=(N-1)g_lit, with opposite rates for the two defects. Thus the U adapter is retained whenever a normal pivot jet is compared; a coefficientwise root representative is not relabeled as the literal clock power away from U=1. See the exact Mellin lift comparison, Section 2.

6. The representative carry and deleted-root obstruction remain

The map (4.1) is linear and is not an algebra map. Canonical representatives satisfy the actual carry identity

A f_tilde-(Af)_tilde=f_(N-1)(A^N-1).         (6.1)

In particular phi_N(Af)=f_(N-1), although Res_(A=0) A f_tilde beta is zero. Taking f=A^(N-1) gives phi_N(Af)=1 while phi_N(A)phi_N(f)=0. Thus the zero-residue source is compatible with multiplication only through the retained canonical carry. With U retained, its term is U f_(N-1); no normal source is removed.

The open geometric framing chart A!=1 also cannot by itself carry the whole finite cut. The idempotent

epsilon_1=(1/N)sum_(j=0)^(N-1) A^j

is killed by localization at A-1 but satisfies phi_N(epsilon_1)=1/N. Thus phi_N does not descend through C_N->C_N[(A-1)^(-1)]. This is a precise source obstruction to deleting the excluded framing point. It is distinct from the projector e in (4.2), although both involve a finite average. Epsilon_1 is multiplication by one root’s idempotent; e is averaging the shift and has constant image.

There is now a precise repair after adjoining the actual matrix Ward differential. The source localization theorem replaces the boundary representative by

P=1-epsilon_1, e_S=[N/(N-1)]P e P.

Its matrix cut row has the same Ward class as the original cut, while its color co-source factors through A-1 localization. An explicit chain retraction and homotopy extend this comparison to the combined relative-Ward complex. With U retained, the deleted branch is marked by alpha(U)^N=U, alpha(1)=1, and removed as a whole regular idempotent summand; every normal carry is retained. The original color co-source’s obstruction above remains valid on R(C_N) alone. The repair uses Ward currents and changes its representative with the exact factor N/(N-1).

This is a boundary comparison. Compressing two internal operators separately omits the excursion L C(I-P)D J, so the theorem does not authorize deletion inside crossings or an internal geometric Gaussian comparison without those additional currents.

7. What is still needed for the internal geometric pushforward

This gives a concrete relative boundary object, explicit chain projection and contraction, and literal realizations of psi,phi,e,D. The marked meridian rather than an arbitrary group volume supplies its relative centralizer interval. Absolute exactness of the classical framing form is therefore not by itself a vanishing argument for a source-compatible relative period.

The finite boundary resolution is natural for every actual matrix, so ordinary finite internal elimination respects it. Its auxiliary relative coordinate t is not thereby identified with the zero mode r/s produced by the internal semiclassical Gaussian pushforward. Constructing that comparison, including (6.1), the missing root idempotent, and the normal divisor (4.3), remains necessary.

In particular a constant geometric principal density would have nonzero image under this relative functional if the internal pushforward were proved to give this class. The boundary model does not prove that premise or select a global thimble. It supplies the explicit cochain target and the exact source defects that the missing map must transport.

Finite contraction and formal Gaussian reduction are different maps

The exact naturality statement is the commuting finite diagram

R(C_N) tensor V_rest  --lift(C)--> R(C_N) tensor V_rest
        | L                              | L
        v                                v
   C_N tensor V_rest    --C-->       C_N tensor V_rest,

with the analogous inclusion and explicit homotopy. Thus actual whole finite internal contraction really does induce a chain map on this boundary resolution. What has not been constructed is a comparison from this diagram to the formal Gaussian normal module at a geometric critical representation, sending the auxiliary relative t to its internal framing zero mode r/s and intertwining the canonical carry.

There is a concrete failure of the naive source map. The functions A^N and one are equal in C_N, whereas

Res_(A=0) A^N dlog A=0,
Res_(A=0) dlog A=1.                         (7.1)

Consequently unreduced rational framing functions followed by the ordinary zero residue do not define a map out of the finite cut algebra. Canonical reduction, or its full normal-U source, must precede that operation. On the actual q trajectory h=Log Q, N=2pi*i/h, this lost character is

A^N=exp((2pi*i/h)Log A).

It is not a coefficientwise formal unit in h at a fixed classical shape. A local formal Gaussian contraction does not automatically transport its finite-root relation to one. The meromorphic extension of the principal framing two-form alone does not repair (7.1).

Hence the present result establishes a compatible nonzero relative principal class and an exact finite source resolution. Calling it an actual nonzero selected geometric formal summand would require the additional commuting microlocal/source map just described. No such summand or global selected thimble is inferred here.
