Exact Gaussian stabilization preserves the unresolved geometric source

28 October 2026. The finite Fourier kernel in the actual clock-feedback theorem has a nonzero coefficient with normalization exactly one. Its Jacobian and transverse determinant can be computed without assumptions about a geometric saddle. But it is an exact stabilization of the SAME coupled action and the SAME original source. It does not supply the missing geometric intersection number.

This answers a specific possible shortcut: Fourier-Gaussian ellipticity does not turn the already known geometric normal block into a selected global geometric contribution. No claim about the impossibility of a different global contour construction is made.

1. The finite feedback extension splits exactly

Use the primitive variables and actual diagram matrix T of the feedback theorem, not independent physical spectator parameters. There are m crossings. On a coefficient R^nu Y^h the normalized finite Fourier realization is

N^(-m) sum_(eta,lambda in (Z/N)^m)
       Q^[-lambda dot eta+lambda dot h+nu^T T eta]. (1)

The clock h is the actual over-monotone clock; nu is the integer primitive Laurent exponent. Every original closure, pivot, dimension, framing and Cartan factor remains in its coefficient and its specified charged source. Equation (1) is only the exact feedback kernel.

Make the finite change of variables

eta=h+u, lambda=T^T nu+v,                         (2)

with all entries interpreted modulo N in this finite sum. For EVERY integer nu,h and EVERY diagram T, this is a bijection, and its exponent is exactly

-lambda eta+lambda h+nu^T T eta
              =nu^T T h-v dot u mod N.            (3)

Thus (1) is

Q^(nu^T T h) * N^(-m) sum_(u,v)Q^(-v dot u)
              =Q^(nu^T T h).                     (4)

For each fixed u the v sum is N^m if u=0 and zero otherwise. The extra Gaussian therefore has coefficient ONE, at every N, with no determinant character, unknown periodic unit or knot normalization.

This statement survives the ORIGINAL source pairing, including the contracted closure jets. Indeed the change (2) does not change nu or h, and (4) holds coefficientwise before any anchor substitution, charged coefficient, clock sum or external scalar. Hence those operations commute with eliminating this normalized Gaussian. No source division is used.

It is important that the shifts in (2) depend on the coefficient momenta nu,h. They describe a canonical Fourier stabilization, not a single substitution of the physical spectator coordinates. They cannot be used to replace the coupled action by the zero-feedback action.

2. Exact Jacobian and Hessian comparison

The same fact has a precise local stationary interpretation. Let x be any regular local phase coordinates, with scaled momenta nu(x),h(x). Write alpha=2pii and let S_0(x) be the independent coefficient action. The extended action is

S_ext(x,eta,lambda)=S_0(x)
           +alpha[lambda(h(x)-eta)+nu(x)^T T eta]. (5)

Use the local change

eta=h(x)+u, lambda=T^T nu(x)+v.                    (6)

Its full Jacobian, including x, is block triangular with identity on the diagonal, so its determinant is EXACTLY one. There is the exact identity of functions, not just of quadratic terms,

S_ext=S_act(x)-alpha v dot u,
S_act=S_0+alpha nu(x)^T T h(x).                    (7)

Consequently the critical scheme is the product of that of S_act with u=v=0, including multiplicities and local Artin rings. At a critical point the Hessian is congruent to

Hess(S_act) direct_sum [[0,-alpha I],[-alpha I,0]]. (8)

Thus

nullity Hess(S_ext)=nullity Hess(S_act),
det Hess(S_ext)=(-1)^m alpha^(2m)det Hess(S_act)     (9)

when the determinants are defined in the full coordinates. The same formula applies to nondegenerate transverse slices in a Morse–Bott germ, with the original radical retained. The normalized Fourier half-density has the exact coefficient (4), so this auxiliary block changes no selected transverse amplitude or residual source density.

Equations (5)-(9) are used only when a regular coefficient phase is available. They do not assert uniform asymptotics across a finite window, an endpoint, or a singular Fourier chart. Equation (4) itself has none of those restrictions.

3. The complete geometric fixed set is not isolated by this operation

There is a concrete obstruction to applying an isolated-fixed-point Bethe/Lefschetz formula after merely adding this feedback kernel. For the actual marked knot critical scheme, simultaneous conjugation by B_1=Stab_(PGL_2)(1) preserves the chosen inverse cut flag and all closed braid equations. This group has dimension two. At the complete discrete-faithful representation its projective centralizer is trivial, so its orbit is a smooth two-dimensional family of distinct marked critical points.

This is exactly the framing torsor in the actual geometric normal theorem, whose Hessian has that two-dimensional radical. In particular, for the regular marked Hurwitz return map Phi, its infinitesimal conjugation vectors obey D Phi(v)=v. Therefore det(I-D Phi)=0 before a stated framing reduction. Adding (1) does not repair that determinant: (7) preserves the critical germ and (8) preserves its radical.

One may choose a transverse framing section and obtain the already proved nonzero normal Gaussian block. Its two-form must still be paired with the specified ORIGINAL relative source. Taking an arbitrary section or assigning its relative period the value one would add a source selection, rather than derive it from (4).

The new closure contractions in general flow fusion do retain the original source exactly. Their full length-d block is the reproducing kernel of the finite source algebra: contraction through an arbitrary exterior implements substitution modulo the original nilpotent walls. It is an identity on the transmitted finite state space, with pivots retained. It supplies no additional framing section or condition on the geometric relative period. At a regular phase realization it removes identity-wire coordinates, not the conjugation orbit above.

4. Exact consequence for the critical path

There is no unknown Gaussian normalization left in this part of the comparison. If a source-preserving regular phase comparison with the geometric chart is constructed, its feedback stabilization contributes one and leaves its geometric coefficient unchanged. This is useful normalization control.

But the full source coefficient which would have to be shown nonzero is still the original one. Neither invertibility of G_T, its unit coefficient norm, the perfect socle pairing, nor the nonzero transverse determinant computes that coefficient. The precise unproved step is the image of the ORIGINAL relative class in the action-normalized geometric summand after the remaining framing/charge contraction. The Gaussian (1) does not weaken that step, since (4) eliminates it without changing that class.

This result therefore rules out the proposed inference from a nonzero Fourier-Gaussian fiber to whole-source geometric survival. It is not a volume-conjecture proof, a new sharp scalar bound, or an assertion that a global positive contour cannot exist.

The finite identities and general proofs above are not inferred from the tests.
