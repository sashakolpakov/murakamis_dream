The retained terminal phase is an extra Cartan coupon, with exact Ward sources

29 October 2026. The positive terminal packet has an exact phase integral, but its phase is NOT the knot’s existing meridian-color parameter. It inserts a specified Cartan coupon on one physical wire. Original interval Ward equations acquire explicit sources. A terminal ordinary cap projects back to the complete Hopf multiplicity module, leaving a nontrivial, explicit operator-valued coupon there. Neither that projection nor Parseval makes the coupon an original Hurwitz crossing or a transparent frame change.

Thus the sharp terminal-energy theorem remains a coherent estimate of this extra coupling. The original scalar at phase one and its Cauchy bound are unchanged. No topological identification with a drilled link or with an existing peripheral variable is made by analogy.

1. The exact phase family of the original terminal packet

Use the original notation of the terminal positive Gram: N>=2, d=N-1, Q=exp(2pi i/N), x=-exp(-pi i/N), and

D w=I_w|d>, c=ell_12 tensor <d|,
U_phys=I tensor R_+^2, U_red=R_23^2,
T_n=c U_phys Pi_(2,n) D.

All common Cartan, ordinary-cap, framing and peripheral factors are literal original ones. Put C_t=t^(J_2), J_2|a,b,c>=b|a,b,c>, t!=0. Then, as a ROW IDENTITY on every complete multiplicity input,

F_t=c U_phys C_t D=sum_(n=0)^d t^n T_n,
F_1=ell U_red.                                        (1)

For every original prefix B,

sum_n |T_n(Bp)|^2
   =(1/(2pi)) integral_0^(2pi)|F_(exp(i theta))(Bp)|^2 dtheta. (2)

The unchanged original scalar is chi_N F_1(Bp). Its given factor chi_N remains outside (2). The family off t=1 uses the fixed input and output d cut in the definition above. Scalarity of the original uninserted knot does not identify other cut prescriptions off t=1. In particular (1) is the specified packet deformation of a valid original-scalar presentation. It is NOT asserted equal to inserting C_t into an unreduced even plat and then replacing its unrestricted marked row by the same lowest cut. That different off-phase observable must retain its literal marked matrix functional. The exact terminal Cauchy estimate needs only the prescribed (1) and the original t=1 sum, so it makes no such replacement.

2. Literal quantum Ward identities with t retained

On the physical middle head, including both nilpotent endpoints,

C_t F_2 C_t^(-1)=t F_2,
C_t Ecal_2 C_t^(-1)=t^(-1)Ecal_2,
[C_t,K_i]=[C_t,H_i]=0, all i.                         (3)

In particular every single-head Casimir and its central K^N stay fixed. The actual meridian family x=-M/q instead changes K by M and its Casimir to -(M+M^(-1)). Thus (3) is an independent relative Cartan conjugation on a FIXED-color module, not that meridian family.

Write the literal three-head coproducts as

Ftot=F_1+K_1^(-1)F_2+K_1^(-1)K_2^(-1)F_3,
Etot=Ecal_1 K_2K_3+Ecal_2 K_3+Ecal_3,
At=K_1^(-1)F_2, Bt=Ecal_2 K_3.

They obey the ALL-EXTERIOR matrix identities

[Ftot,C_t]=(1-t)At C_t,
[Etot,C_t]=(1-t^(-1))Bt C_t,
[Ktot,C_t]=0,
[Omega_tot,C_t]
   =(1-t^(-1))Ftot Bt C_t+(1-t)At C_t Etot.            (4)

There is no omitted boundary arrow or small-error term. At larger width the same formulas replace At by K_1(-1)…K_(r-1)(-1)F_r and Bt by Ecal_r K_(r+1)…K_b.

The defect is macroscopic on an ACTUAL original source. For w=p, D p=p_12 tensor |d>, Ftot Dp=0. For |t|=1 equation (4) gives

||Ftot C_t Dp||/||Dp||=sqrt(2)|1-t|, EVERY N>=2.        (5)

Indeed F_2 sends the u-th cup term to a distinct state with coefficient 1-Q^(-u). Original cup terms have modulus N, the K-prefix and C_t are unitary, and sum_(u=0)^d |1-Qu|2=2N. Both squared source norms in (5) retain their exact N^3 factor. Thus an order-one unit phase does not preserve the old lowest-source Ward by an O(1/N) error.

The exact interval-cap identities DO persist after conjugating ALL their operators and their cap/cup by C_t. If iota_p is an original cup insertion, then iota_(p,t)=C_t iota_p satisfies the conjugated interval identities with Omega^(t)=C_t Omega C_t^(-1). Without that simultaneous change the source terms (4) remain. Keeping the rest of the original word unchanged is precisely the relative seam.

3. Projection back to the complete module does not delete the coupon

One must not conclude from (4) that the final amplitude has left the total character forever. The terminal ordinary cap is a Hopf map; the following exact projection accounts for this.

Set

F_d=(Q;Q)_d=N,
xi_d=product_(a=1)^d(Q^(-a)-1)=(-1)^d N.

Let R_high extract the components |u,d-u,0> of a highest degree-d vector. On physical degree 2d define

P_low=Ftot^d Etot^d/(F_d xi_d),
M_t=xi_d^(-1) R_high Etot^d C_t D.                     (6)

Then P_low is an idempotent onto the LOWEST degree-2d slice of the COMPLETE total-Casimir-minus-two isotypic component. It has not been declared orthogonal in the physical norm. To prove the statement, Etot^d of any degree-2d vector is highest of degree d because Etot^N=0. The complete highest-vector theorem identifies it with H_L(w). Also Etot^d D w=xi_d H_L(w) and Ftot^d H_L(w)=F_d D w. Therefore P_low D=D and

P_low C_t D=D M_t, M_1=I.                             (7)

The original terminal row has c U_phys P_low=c U_phys on that degree slice. This can be checked without a spectral assumption: ordinary-cap naturality replaces Ftot and Etot by the remaining single-head F and E, while <d|F^d=F_d<0| and <0|E^d=xi_d<d|. Consequently the precise ALL-INPUT identity is

F_t=ell U_red M_t.                                   (8)

Expanding M_t=sum_n t^n M_n gives sum_n M_n=I and T_n=ell U_red M_n. All N original multiplicities remain; no period or unknown amplitude was used in this completion.

The resulting M_t is generally neither scalar nor unitary and its coefficients M_n need not be positive or projectors. Already at N=2, in the original degree-one physical multiplicity basis,

M_t=[[t,0],[i(t-1),2-t]].                             (9)

This follows from the original E/F/H_L/D matrices in (6), not a chosen interpolation. M_0^2!=M_0, and at t=-1 the operator has eigenvalue three. Thus physical unitarity of C_t does not give a contractive effective coupon after the nonorthogonal Hopf projection. The complete total character has been restored in (8), but the original operator factorization has acquired the additional M_t. No specific semiclassical canonical graph for M_t is inferred from the finite-level example (9) alone.

4. What the doubled phase average matches

On the recorded head, Parseval inserts the exact doubled projector

D_clock=sum_n Pi_n tensor bar(Pi_n)
  =(1/(2pi)) integral C_(exp(i theta))
                     tensor bar(C_(exp(i theta))) dtheta. (10)

It matches the integer recorded colors in the two copies. It commutes with their Cartan clocks. It does NOT commute with the independent raising/lowering Ward operators. For example, (F_2 tensor I)D_clock|0,0>=(1-Q)|1,0>, whereas D_clock(F_2 tensor I)|0,0>=0. The same nonzero example works at every N>=2, with physical endpoints retained.

Thus (10) is a Cartan matching condition, not a full inverse-meridian matrix equation. It does not by itself reestablish the faithful cap interval certificate for a transparent middle wire. Effective projection (8) instead retains its entire nontrivial operator M_t on both copies.

Classically, in an unbased Gauss frame the seam sends

(k,e,f)->(k,t^(-1)e,t f),
G->h_t G h_t^(-1), h_t=diag(t^(-1/2),t^(1/2)).         (11)

The square-root sign has no effect on this conjugation. Based meridians to its right also change through their actual L_- prefixes; they may not be held fixed while ignoring that change. This is a relative Cartan-frame transition. It is not generally a peripheral longitude, either: h_t need not commute with the physical meridian G.

5. Precise geometric implication

The original scalar at t=1 and the positive terminal Cauchy Gram are fully source-exact. At a retained nontrivial phase the equations are the TWISTED equations (4), or equivalently the completed coupon (8). The phase has unchanged color/central powers and so cannot simply be renamed the existing meridian parameter. No theorem here identifies it with a drilled-component holonomy; that would need its own explicit topological/source construction.

To apply the original-knot representation-volume bound to all critical contributions of (2), one still needs to prove that this additional seam’s complete stationary equations make it transparent, or bound the full M_t coupling with the correct calibrated action. Color equality in (10), or total-Casimir recovery in (8), does not prove that statement. The exact figure-eight energy identity in the terminal-Gram note is unchanged; it is a coherent special calculation of this same family.

Audit: literal coproduct Ward operators, full lowest/Hermite multiplicity maps, every input column of (8), all packet coefficients, the exact N=2 coupon, and the macroscopic original-source norm in (5).

The terminal Ward checks do not establish closed-knot geometry.
