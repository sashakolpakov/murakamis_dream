A faithful middle-weight model for Conway face operators

21 November 2026. Research note. Earlier final notes and the public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The second-head wrap correction between the first and middle local five-point descriptions vanishes on a particular invariant physical subspace. That subspace still represents the entire Conway boundary algebra faithfully, including its repeated-root derivatives. Thus the correction can be removed there before taking any asymptotic estimate.

The original closing and turn must be reconstructed from the complete boundary packet. The all-array physical trace is not simply restricted to this subspace. Other endpoint projectors and the full relative coefficient cycles remain.

1. The physical subspace

Fix N≥2N\ge2, d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, and Q=q2Q=q^2. The original two physical heads have basis |a,b⟩|a,b\rangle, 0≤a,b≤d0\le a,b\le d. Their clock operators are Z|a,b⟩=Qa|a,b⟩,T|a,b⟩=Qb|a,b⟩.(1) Z|a,b\rangle=Q^a|a,b\rangle,\qquad T|a,b\rangle=Q^b|a,b\rangle . \qquad\text{(1)} Use the actual cyclic face unit X=xS2S1−1X=xS_2S_1^{-1}, with x=−q−1x=-q^{-1} and the original nonzero wrap marks. It lowers the first index and raises the second, cyclically, and satisfies XZ=QZX,XT=Q−1TX.(2) XZ=QZX,\qquad XT=Q^{-1}TX. \qquad\text{(2)} Here SiS_i raises head ii, with coefficient one before its last index and wrap coefficient μi≠0\mu_i\ne0; the original marks obey μ2=xNμ1\mu_2=x^N\mu_1. Define ℳN=span⁡{va=|a,d−a⟩:0≤a≤d}.(3) \mathcal M_N=\operatorname{span}\{v_a=|a,d-a\rangle:0\le a\le d\}. \qquad\text{(3)} The clock ZTZT has eigenvalue Qa+bQ^{a+b} at |a,b⟩|a,b\rangle. Because 0≤a+b≤2N−20\le a+b\le2N-2, the congruence a+b≡d(mod⁡N)a+b\equiv d\pmod N forces a+b=da+b=d. Consequently ℳN\mathcal M_N is the entire ZT=QdZT=Q^d eigenspace. Equations (1)–(2) show that X,Z,TX,Z,T, their spectral projectors, and every expression in these operators preserve ℳN\mathcal M_N. An inverse, when defined on the full space, preserves it as well. On this subspace, T=Q−1Z−1.(4) T=Q^{-1}Z^{-1}. \qquad\text{(4)}

2. The first/middle wrap correction disappears exactly

In the same physical frame, the first and middle face descriptions use n=e1S2=(Q−Z−1)X,c=n(I−Ed(T)),(5) n=e_1S_2=(Q-Z^{-1})X,\qquad c=n(I-E_d(T)), \qquad\text{(5)} where Ed(T)E_d(T) projects onto second-head index dd. Similarly Ej(Z)E_j(Z) projects onto first-head index jj. The right-hand expression in (5) defines nn directly; e1e_1 is the original first-head Gauss lowering operator. The original finite Euler polynomial is PQ(A)=∑h=0d(−1)hQh(h−1)/2(Q;Q)hAh.(6) P_Q(A)=\sum_{h=0}^{d} \frac{(-1)^hQ^{h(h-1)/2}}{(Q;Q)_h}A^h. \qquad\text{(6)} Here (Q;Q)h=∏j=1h(1−Qj)(Q;Q)_h=\prod_{j=1}^{h}(1-Q^j), with empty product one. Both nn and cc are nilpotent of order at most NN, and their Euler matrices are invertible.

Proposition 1. On all of ℳN\mathcal M_N, n=c,PQ(n)=PQ(c).(7) n=c,\qquad P_Q(n)=P_Q(c). \qquad\text{(7)}

Proof. The only vector of ℳN\mathcal M_N in the range of Ed(T)E_d(T) is v0=|0,d⟩v_0=|0,d\rangle. The first lowering operator e1e_1 kills first-head index zero. Therefore nEd(T)|ℳN=0nE_d(T)|_{\mathcal M_N}=0, which proves the first identity. Both operators preserve the subspace, so every power agrees there, giving the second identity. ▫\square

Equivalently the full-space transition PQ(n)PQ(c)−1−IP_Q(n)P_Q(c)^{-1}-I restricts to zero. Every local face factor in the two displayed five-point descriptions preserves this subspace, so subsequent such factors cannot make that restricted zero visible. No estimate of the correction’s norm or geometric volume is needed for this representation.

This removes precisely the second-head wrap correction in (5). It does not remove the strict first-head endpoint already present in nn, or the middle factor QX(I−E0(Z))QX(I-E_0(Z)). The projector E0(Z)|ℳNE_0(Z)|_{\mathcal M_N} is nonzero.

3. Why the restriction retains the full boundary algebra

Let Ωd\Omega_d be the original pair Casimir on ℳN\mathcal M_N. In the ordered basis v0,…,vdv_0,\ldots,v_d, it is irreducible tridiagonal. The complete source theorem gives its characteristic and minimal polynomial PN(z)=∏j=0d(z−cj)=2TN(z/2)+2,cj=2cos⁡(2j+1)πN.(8) P_N(z)=\prod_{j=0}^{d}(z-c_j)=2T_N(z/2)+2,\qquad c_j=2\cos\frac{(2j+1)\pi}{N}. \qquad\text{(8)} Here TNT_N is the Chebyshev polynomial of the first kind. Thus the representation ρN:HN=ℂ[z]/(PN)→End⁡(ℳN),ρN(f)=f(Ωd)(9) \rho_N:H_N=\mathbb C[z]/(P_N)\longrightarrow \operatorname{End}(\mathcal M_N),\qquad \rho_N(f)=f(\Omega_d) \qquad\text{(9)} is injective. In particular it retains the first derivatives at every double root. It is not the representation by values at distinct roots. Here a whole Hopf box means an actual two-head operator f(Ω)f(\Omega) from the complete Conway compiler. An individual face factor need not belong to this algebra.

There is an explicit packet isomorphism as well. Put sr=∏j=1r2sin⁡(πj/N),br(z)=sr−2∏j=0r−1(cj−z),s0=1, s_r=\prod_{j=1}^{r}2\sin(\pi j/N),\qquad b_r(z)=s_r^{-2}\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}(c_j-z),\qquad s_0=1, CN=[b0(Ωd)v0⋯bd(Ωd)v0].(10) C_N=\big[b_0(\Omega_d)v_0\ \cdots\ b_d(\Omega_d)v_0\big]. \qquad\text{(10)} A polynomial of degree rr in an irreducible tridiagonal matrix, applied to v0v_0, has support only in indices 0,…,r0,\ldots,r. Its index-rr coefficient is its leading coefficient times the nonzero unique outer path. Hence CNC_N is triangular with nonzero diagonal and is invertible. The map ΞN(f)=f(Ωd)v0=CNα,f=∑r=0dαrbr(11) \Xi_N(f)=f(\Omega_d)v_0=C_N\alpha,\qquad f=\sum_{r=0}^{d}\alpha_rb_r \qquad\text{(11)} is an isomorphism from the full packet space onto ℳN\mathcal M_N.

The original marked cap and Frobenius closing, and the genuine quarter-turn, are therefore exactly mN(y)=𝒆0𝖳CN−1y,ϕNpkt(y)=𝒆d𝖳CN−1y,JNpkt=CNPrevCN−1.(12) m_N(y)=\mathbf e_0^{\mathsf T}C_N^{-1}y,\qquad \phi_N^{\mathrm{ pkt}}(y)=\mathbf e_d^{\mathsf T}C_N^{-1}y,\qquad J_N^{\mathrm{ pkt}}=C_NP_{\mathrm{ rev}}C_N^{-1}. \qquad\text{(12)} The 𝒆i\mathbf e_i are standard coordinate vectors, and PrevαP_{\mathrm{ rev}}\alpha reverses the NN Newton coefficients; ϕNpkt\phi_N^{\mathrm{ pkt}} is a functional, not the scalar used below. These are the actual original closures and turn, since in Newton coordinates they are α0,αd\alpha_0,\alpha_d, and reversal. The turn is a linear packet operation; it is not asserted to be multiplicative on matrices.

The Frobenius closing has a particularly simple extension to all middle-block matrices. Let ΛN=(−1)dN2∏j=0d−1(Ωd)j+1,j,ΦNend(A)=ΛNAd,0.(12a) \Lambda_N=\frac{(-1)^dN^2} {\prod_{j=0}^{d-1}(\Omega_d)_{j+1,j}},\qquad \Phi_N^{\mathrm{ end}}(A)=\Lambda_N A_{d,0}. \qquad\text{(12a)} Every factor in the denominator is nonzero. For a polynomial of degree at most dd, only its leading term can reach the outer entry (d,0)(d,0). Since the original Frobenius functional is (−1)dN2[zd]f(-1)^dN^2[z^d]f, this proves ΦNend(f(Ωd))=ϕN(f)\Phi_N^{\mathrm{ end}}(f(\Omega_d))=\phi_N(f). It is the same closing as (12), with its full scalar retained.

Multiplication of whole Hopf boxes is also exact: ρN(f)ΞN(g)=ΞN(fg).(13) \rho_N(f)\Xi_N(g)=\Xi_N(fg). \qquad\text{(13)} One may use (13) or reconstruct the original bilinear Newton product through CN−1C_N^{-1}. Together with (12), induction through any fixed literal Conway expression gives its original full packet and scalar. All recorded Cartan, framing and peripheral factors remain attached.

For a local crossing factorization, restrict each displayed physical face factor to ℳN\mathcal M_N and multiply these full NN-by-NN matrices in the original order. The complete crossing is then the same whole Hopf box as before. One must not replace an individual non-Hopf face factor AA by the polynomial packet reconstructed from Av0Av_0: the identity (AB)v0=ρN(ΞN−1(Av0))Bv0(AB)v_0=\rho_N(\Xi_N^{-1}(Av_0))Bv_0 is not true for arbitrary face matrices. Formula (13) applies to whole Hopf boxes.

4. The factorial interface is an explicit unitary diagonal

Write Fa=(Q;Q)aF_a=(Q;Q)_a, 0≤a<N0\le a<N, and retain the original marked scalar ϖN=Nexp⁡{iπ[14−N+N−112]}.(14) \varpi_N=\sqrt N\exp\left\{i\pi \left[\frac14-\frac{N+N^{-1}}{12}\right]\right\}. \qquad\text{(14)} The middle marked clock and the inverse clock used in the first description are ℱZ=ϖNdiag⁡(Fa−1),ℱT−1=ϖN−1diag⁡(Fb).(15) \mathcal F_Z=\varpi_N\operatorname{diag}(F_a^{-1}),\qquad \mathcal F_T^{-1}=\varpi_N^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(F_b). \qquad\text{(15)} Their clock marks and endpoint logarithmic lifts are the original ones. Since b=d−ab=d-a on ℳN\mathcal M_N, FaFd−a=Nνa,νa=(−1)aQa(a+1)/2(16) F_aF_{d-a}=N\nu_a,\qquad \nu_a=(-1)^aQ^{a(a+1)/2} \qquad\text{(16)} gives the exact interface ℱT−1|ℳN=NϖN2DνℱZ|ℳN.(17) \mathcal F_T^{-1}|_{\mathcal M_N} =\frac{N}{\varpi_N^2}D_\nu\, \mathcal F_Z|_{\mathcal M_N}. \qquad\text{(17)} To prove (16), use Fd=NF_d=N and ∏j=N−aN−1(1−Qj)=(−1)aQ−a(a+1)/2Fa\prod_{j=N-a}^{N-1}(1-Q^j) =(-1)^aQ^{-a(a+1)/2}F_a. All diagonal entries of DνD_\nu and the scalar N/ϖN2N/\varpi_N^2 have modulus one.

The diagonal is retained as an actual finite matrix. Its continuation satisfies νa+N=−νa\nu_{a+N}=-\nu_a, so its cyclic conjugation has an endpoint correction. In the physical middle basis, DνXDν−1=−XZ−1(I−2E0(Z)).(18) D_\nu X D_\nu^{-1} =-XZ^{-1}(I-2E_0(Z)). \qquad\text{(18)} At input a≥1a\ge1 the diagonal ratio is νa−1/νa=−Q−a\nu_{a-1}/\nu_a=-Q^{-a}; at input zero it is νd/ν0=1\nu_d/\nu_0=1. This proves (18), including the wrap. Multiplying by the actual nilpotent coefficient removes that one reflection: DνnDν−1=−nZ−1,(18a) D_\nu nD_\nu^{-1}=-nZ^{-1}, \qquad\text{(18a)} because nE0(Z)=0nE_0(Z)=0 on this subspace. This is an exact identity for nn, not permission to erase the wall in the cyclic unit XX.

Also ν0=νd=1\nu_0=\nu_d=1. Thus the reconstructed Frobenius extension has, for every middle-block matrix AA, ΦNend(DνA)=ΦNend(ADν)=ΦNend(DνADν−1)=ΦNend(A).(18b) \Phi_N^{\mathrm{ end}}(D_\nu A) =\Phi_N^{\mathrm{ end}}(A D_\nu) =\Phi_N^{\mathrm{ end}}(D_\nu A D_\nu^{-1}) =\Phi_N^{\mathrm{ end}}(A). \qquad\text{(18b)} This can be used at those exterior interfaces, or with a fully conjugated word. It does not delete an interior DνD_\nu from an otherwise unchanged product, and it makes no such assertion for the other marked cap in (12).

Unitarity in this paragraph concerns the literal physical basis vav_a. The Newton-coordinate matrix CN−1DνCNC_N^{-1}D_\nu C_N need not be unitary or uniformly conditioned. No energy estimate is inferred from the unit-modulus diagonal alone.

5. Source and geometric scope

The reconstructed source (12) is used on whole Hopf boxes. It is not the restriction of the all-array physical source Φ(W)=1N∑a,c,bgbW(c,b),(a,b)(19) \Phi(W)=\frac1N\sum_{a,c,b}g_bW_{(c,b),(a,b)} \qquad\text{(19)} to arbitrary face matrices, where gb=(xQ−b)1−Ng_b=(xQ^{-b})^{1-N}. Indeed the projector onto |1,d⟩|1,d\rangle has zero middle block but (19) has value gd/N≠0g_d/N\ne0. Thus (19) cannot be recovered from a middle block on all arrays. The Hopf reconstruction is indispensable.

Every local full-function coefficient realization remains its original circle-minus-radial functional. Restricting a finite matrix identity commutes with these exact finite-dimensional coefficient integrals. It does not select one component of their cycles or replace a full-function moment by a scalar evaluation.

The result gives an exact, smaller local representation in which the specified first/middle wrap correction disappears, without losing any repeated-root Casimir direction. This is a fixed-root statement, not an off-root quantum-family identity. It neither places every geometric point in a common positive face polarization nor proves that the turn kernel transports a Rivin contour. The surviving first-head walls, marked relative cycles, source coefficient, and coordinate-change costs still require their own geometric estimates. No new volume identity or finite-exception theorem follows here.
