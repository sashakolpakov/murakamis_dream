A quantitative volume gap for actual long-filled groups

Research note, 18 November 2026.

The full group relation of a Dehn filling forces every nearby centered primitive charge to be a nonzero integer. Consequently the geometric charge comparison applies to every other admitted representation of the filled group, without matching a Fourier label to that centered charge. This is a statement about classical representations, not source cycles.

1. Proof sources, normalization, and theorem

The complete proof sources are:

  • G, A uniform representation-volume gap along centered long cone paths.
  • F, A finite positive certificate supplies every sufficiently long filling branch.

Fix the marked complete hyperbolic core MM, its positive complete certificate, and its positively oriented primitive cusp bases (μi,λi)(\mu_i,\lambda_i), as in these sources. A fixed earlier short partial filling may already be part of MM. Fill a nonempty subset II along primitive slopes αi=μiaiλibi,gcd⁡(ai,bi)=1,ℓi=(ai2+bi2)1/2.(1) \alpha_i=\mu_i^{a_i}\lambda_i^{b_i},\qquad \gcd(a_i,b_i)=1,\qquad \ell_i=(a_i^2+b_i^2)^{1/2}. \qquad\text{(1)} Write M(σ)M(\sigma) for the actual marked filling and π:π1M↠π1M(σ)\pi:\pi_1M\twoheadrightarrow\pi_1M(\sigma) for its quotient map. All remaining cusps, including every unfilled physical main cusp, are retained.

An admitted representation in this note is ρ:π1M(σ)→PSL2(ℂ)(2) \rho:\pi_1M(\sigma)\longrightarrow\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb C) \qquad\text{(2)} whose image on every remaining cusp is boundary-parabolic, with the trivial image allowed. Use the signed representation volume normalized by the same oriented fundamental class as G and F. Section 2 checks its equality with the normalized volume of the core pullback ρ̂=ρ∘π\widehat\rho=\rho\circ\pi.

The parabolic condition on the remaining cusps is substantive. It is what fixes their centered peripheral coordinates to zero near complete holonomy. No uniform gap over varying nonparabolic boundary conditions is asserted. If all cusps are filled, this condition is empty.

Theorem. There are constants L*,δ,c1>0L_*,\delta,c_1>0, depending only on the fixed marked core and certificate, such that for every primitive slope tuple with ℓmin>L*\ell_{\min}>L_*, the filling M(σ)M(\sigma) is the actual oriented hyperbolic filling supplied by F. Let ρhyp,σ\rho_{\mathrm{hyp},\sigma} be its oriented projective holonomy. Every admitted representation not conjugate to it satisfies Vol⁡M(σ)(ρ)≤Vol⁡(M(σ))−min⁡{δ2,3c1ℓmax⁡2},ℓmax=maxi∈Iℓi.(3) \operatorname{Vol}_{M(\sigma)}(\rho) \le \operatorname{Vol}(M(\sigma)) - \min\left\{\frac{\delta}{2}, \frac{3c_1}{\ell_{\max}^2}\right\}, \qquad \ell_{\max}=\max_{i\in I}\ell_i. \qquad\text{(3)} One choice works for all subsets, signs and independently unequal long slope lengths. The comparison is between underlying projective conjugacy classes. Central lifts and different source decorations of the same projective representation are not separated by volume.

2. Pullback volume and cyclic filled peripheral images

Primitivity in (1) implies ℤ2/⟨(ai,bi)⟩≅ℤ\mathbb Z^2/\langle(a_i,b_i)\rangle\cong\mathbb Z. Therefore the image of the ii-th filled peripheral group under ρ̂\widehat\rho is cyclic. In particular it is trivial, parabolic, or fixes both endpoints of an axis individually. There is no peripheral endpoint-interchanging group hidden in this assertion. Together with the admitted unfilled cusps, these are precisely the peripheral types used by the core volume comparison in G.

Here is a map-level verification of the normalization under filling. On a filled solid torus, use coordinates (d,t)∈D2×ℝ(d,t)\in D^2\times\mathbb R on its universal cover, with t↦t+1t\mapsto t+1 representing the core generator gg. Choose a gg-equivariant curve γ:ℝ→ℍ3\gamma:\mathbb R\to\mathbb H^3 and put 𝒟(d,t)=γ(t).(4) \mathcal D(d,t)=\gamma(t). \qquad\text{(4)} For a loxodromic gg, take its axis with the appropriate translation speed. For an elliptic gg, take a constant point on its axis. For a parabolic gg, conjugate it to w↦w+bw\mapsto w+b and use γ(t)=(tb,1)\gamma(t)=(tb,1) in upper-half-space coordinates. For the identity take a constant curve. These maps are equivariant for the actual cyclic peripheral quotient and have differential rank at most one. Their volume integrals are zero.

Prescribe the same maps on the bounding tori of a compact truncation of MM, independent of the meridional coordinate. An equivariant map on the remaining compact core with these boundary values exists by extending over a lifted finite cell decomposition: the target ℍ3\mathbb H^3 is contractible, and each cell extension is propagated equivariantly. Smooth it relative to the fixed collars. Keep identical admissible maps on all unfilled cusps in the two constructions.

For the core volume, replace each solid torus by a cusp tail. In the axis case the tail can be kept in that same axis. In the parabolic case use (tb,es)(tb,e^s), with the cusp-depth variable s≥0s\ge0; its image lies in the vertical hyperbolic plane over the line ℝb\mathbb R b. The trivial case uses a ray. Each such tail has rank at most two and zero three-volume, and matches (4) at its initial torus. These are the cyclic instances of the axis/parabolic cusp models defining the volume in G. Changing the axis-depth parametrization, or comparing the displayed parabolic tail with its conical ideal end, remains in the same axis or plane; the comparison homotopy has zero three-volume as well.

The compact-core integrals and all unfilled ends are thus identical, and the added pieces contribute zero. With the retained orientation, Vol⁡M(σ)(ρ)=Vol⁡M(ρ̂).(5) \operatorname{Vol}_{M(\sigma)}(\rho) =\operatorname{Vol}_M(\widehat\rho). \qquad\text{(5)} This uses the full cyclic quotient at the filled tori. An equation for peripheral eigenvalues alone would not supply the solid-torus map.

3. The full slope relation forces a nonzero centered charge

Use the fixed conjugacy-invariant complete-holonomy neighborhood 𝒰\mathcal U from G, shrinking it if necessary as in its Section 4. Every admitted pullback in 𝒰\mathcal U, with any retained peripheral fixed flags, lies in the actual positive decorated gluing chart. Its centered similarity logarithms satisfy Vi=Uiτi(U),ℑτi(U)≥m>0.(6) V_i=U_i\tau_i(U),\qquad \Im\tau_i(U)\ge m>0. \qquad\text{(6)} For unfilled cusps, boundary-parabolicity gives their centered Ui=Vi=0U_i=V_i=0.

At a filled cusp the full projective matrix relation is ρ̂(αi)=I.(7) \widehat\rho(\alpha_i)=I. \qquad\text{(7)} Taking its similarity multiplier on the retained fixed flag gives aiUi+biVi=2πiqi,qi∈ℤ.(8) a_iU_i+b_iV_i=2\pi i q_i,\qquad q_i\in\mathbb Z. \qquad\text{(8)} Here Ui,ViU_i,V_i themselves remain centered; (8) is not obtained by choosing their deck translates.

Suppose qi=0q_i=0. The denominator estimate in F gives |ai+biτi(U)|≥c0ℓi>0.(9) |a_i+b_i\tau_i(U)|\ge c_0\ell_i>0. \qquad\text{(9)} Equations (6), (8) imply Ui=Vi=0U_i=V_i=0. In the moving selected-flag affine frame, F supplies the actual commuting maps μi(w)=eUiw+Bi(U),λi(w)=eViw+Di(U),Bi(U)≠0.(10) \mu_i(w)=e^{U_i}w+B_i(U),\qquad \lambda_i(w)=e^{V_i}w+D_i(U),\qquad B_i(U)\ne0. \qquad\text{(10)} Moreover, on Ui=0U_i=0, Di/Bi=τi(U).(11) D_i/B_i=\tau_i(U). \qquad\text{(11)} For completeness, differentiate the exact commuting identity (eUi−1)Di=(eVi−1)Bi(e^{U_i}-1)D_i=(e^{V_i}-1)B_i in the coordinate UiU_i, holding all other coordinates fixed, and set Ui=0U_i=0. Since Vi=Uiτi(U)V_i=U_i\tau_i(U), this gives (11).

The slope map when Ui=Vi=0U_i=V_i=0 is therefore the translation w↦w+aiBi+biDi=w+Bi(ai+biτi(U)).(12) w\longmapsto w+a_iB_i+b_iD_i =w+B_i(a_i+b_i\tau_i(U)). \qquad\text{(12)} Its translation is nonzero by (9)–(10), so it is not the identity. This contradicts the full relation (7). We have proved qi∈ℤ\{0}(i∈I).(13) q_i\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\} \qquad(i\in I). \qquad\text{(13)}

If one starts with SL2\mathrm{SL}_2 data, projectivize while retaining its actual central signs. A slope matrix +I+I or −I-I both give (7); the nonidentity parabolic (12) gives neither. Thus the proof does not presume an odd-only or an even-only charge congruence. It also does not identify the integer qiq_i with a quantum Fourier label. The full group relation is what excludes zero.

4. The gap for all admitted filled-group representations

Source G gives a fixed δ>0\delta>0 such that every admitted core representation outside 𝒰\mathcal U has volume at most Vol⁡(M)−δ\operatorname{Vol}(M)-\delta. Its long-slope threshold can be chosen so that Vol⁡(M(σ))>Vol⁡(M)−δ/2.(14) \operatorname{Vol}(M(\sigma)) >\operatorname{Vol}(M)-\delta/2. \qquad\text{(14)} By (5), a filled-group representation whose pullback is outside 𝒰\mathcal U therefore has deficit greater than δ/2\delta/2.

For a pullback in 𝒰\mathcal U, (13) places all its centered charges in the nonzero integer set. G’s neighborhood was chosen so that all these charges lie in its larger box |qi|≤ρ*ℓi|q_i|\le\rho_*\ell_i. Its exact monotonicity estimate gives Vol⁡(M(σ))−Vol⁡M(ρ̂)≥c1∑i∈Iqi2−1ℓi2,c1=π2m1+T2>0.(15) \operatorname{Vol}(M(\sigma)) -\operatorname{Vol}_M(\widehat\rho) \ge c_1\sum_{i\in I}\frac{q_i^2-1}{\ell_i^2}, \qquad c_1=\frac{\pi^2m}{1+T^2}>0. \qquad\text{(15)} If any |qi|≥2|q_i|\ge2, the right side is at least 3c1/ℓmax⁡23c_1/\ell_{\max}^2. If all |qi|=1|q_i|=1, the independent flag-reversal and contraction argument in G identifies the underlying pullback representation with the unit-charge geometric one. Since π\pi is surjective, a conjugacy of these pullbacks is also a conjugacy of the representations of π1M(σ)\pi_1M(\sigma). Such a representation is the excluded ρhyp,σ\rho_{\mathrm{hyp},\sigma}. Together with the outside-neighborhood case and (5), this proves (3).

5. Source scope

The theorem removes a classical charge-matching premise for representations that already satisfy the actual filled-group relations. It does not prove that every source critical configuration satisfies those relations as matrices, or that its action has the required representation-volume normalization. A peripheral exponential equation alone still permits the nonidentity parabolic excluded in (12).

Nor does the theorem compare distinct source lifts of geometric holonomy or determine their combined coefficient. Source boundary currents, escaping decorations, the original highest-cut relative cycle and its physical observation remain separate. No quantum lower bound or noncancellation statement is inferred.
