Whole-boundary decay for the proved path and graph sources

Research proof, 4 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

The full wide-kernel estimate upgrades the recently proved local outside packets to whole-period bounds and quadratic complex strips. The argument retains the original finite source and all its normals. It supplies boundary control for later composition; it does not by itself solve an arbitrary adjacent short component.

1. The original source and the covered classes

Use the exact source, crossing powers, and marking conventions of one of the following proved theorems:

  • The path theorem, Theorem 4: positive powers at least seven with the stipulated complete outside endpoint, or signed powers of absolute value at least eight.
  • The branching theorem: a fixed loopless same-index graph, allowing repeated edges, with |nv|≥max⁡(8,4dv)|n_v|\ge\max(8,4d_v) at each selected vertex.
  • The degree-power theorem: a fixed same-index graph, allowing loops and repeated edges, with nv2+dv2≥4096n_v^2+d_v^2\ge4096 at each selected vertex.
  • The hybrid theorem, Theorem 3: a fixed same-index graph, allowing loops and repeated edges, in which each selected vertex independently satisfies either |nv|≥max⁡(8,4dv)|n_v|\ge\max(8,4d_v) or nv2+dv2≥512\sqrt{n_v^2+d_v^2}\ge512.

The degrees are the actual incidence degrees, with loops counted twice. Each bullet is a separately proved source class. Only the last permits the displayed vertexwise combination. In particular the homogeneous degree-power threshold 6464 is not substituted for the hybrid threshold 512512.

Let II be the nonempty selected set and let B⊆V\IB\subseteq V\setminus I be any set of unselected vertices whose angles will be left free. Other unselected angles stay zero. Delete isolated outside vertices from BB, since the source is independent of their angles. With zb=−2cos⁡ubz_b=-2\cos u_b, define the literal polynomial FN(𝒖)=(∏v∈IϕN(zv)R(zv)nv)∏{v,w}∈EKN(tv,tw),tb=ub(b∈B),tb=0(b∉I∪B).(1) F_N(\mathbf u)= \left(\prod_{v\in I}\phi_N^{(z_v)}R(z_v)^{n_v}\right) \prod_{\{v,w\}\in E}K_N(t_v,t_w), \quad t_b=u_b\ (b\in B),\quad t_b=0\ (b\notin I\cup B). \qquad\text{(1)} Products precede the selected traces. A loop occurs once as KN(tv,tv)K_N(t_v,t_v). Unselected variables are not reduced in the Casimir quotient. Their actual degrees obey deg⁡zbFN≤db(N−1).(2) \deg_{z_b}F_N\le d_b(N-1). \qquad\text{(2)} Let MM be the proved selected stationary action with every outside angle complete. 2. A loss for every free outside incidence

Write 𝒲={t:|ℜt|≤π/2,|ℑt|≤3/10},δ=21200.(6) \mathcal W=\{t:|\Re t|\le\pi/2,\ |\Im t|\le3/10\}, \qquad \delta=\frac{21}{200}. \qquad\text{(6)} The wide edge theorem and wide phase Hessian give, including every fixed ordinary derivative, |KN(t,u)|≤CNAeNℜψ(t,u),−ℜψ″(t,u)≽120πI,t,u∈𝒲.(7) |K_N(t,u)|\le CN^A e^{N\Re\psi(t,u)},\qquad -\Re\psi''(t,u)\succeq\frac1{20\pi}I, \qquad t,u\in\mathcal W . \qquad\text{(7)} All derivatives here and below act on the corresponding ordinary colors, not on an angle divided by a possibly vanishing sine.

For fixed complex t∈𝒲t\in\mathcal W and real |u|≤π/2|u|\le\pi/2, separate evenness gives ψu(t,0)=0\psi_u(t,0)=0. Twice integrating the uuuu entry of (7) yields ℜψ(t,u)≤ℜψ(t,0)−u240π≤ℜψ(t,0)−γ(1−cos⁡u).(8) \Re\psi(t,u)\le\Re\psi(t,0)-\frac{u^2}{40\pi} \le\Re\psi(t,0)-\gamma(1-\cos u). \qquad\text{(8)} Indeed 1−cos⁡u≤u2/21-\cos u\le u^2/2 and π<5\pi<5. For real π/2≤|u|≤π\pi/2\le|u|\le\pi, the centered incidence theorem gives exponent ℜψ(t,0)−δ\Re\psi(t,0)-\delta. Since 2γ<δ2\gamma<\delta, it implies the same outside loss. Thus a retained selected angle anywhere on its actual horizontal or vertical contour satisfies |KN(t,u)|≤CNAeN[ℜψ(t,0)−γ(1−cos⁡u)],t∈𝒲,u∈[−π,π].(9) |K_N(t,u)|\le CN^A e^{N[\Re\psi(t,0)-\gamma(1-\cos u)]}, \quad t\in\mathcal W,\quad u\in[-\pi,\pi]. \qquad\text{(9)} Both branches of this proof already include all fixed ordinary derivatives in either color.

For an edge with two unselected endpoints, both angles can be free. If both have magnitude at most π/2\pi/2, integrate the full real Hessian in (7) from (0,0)(0,0): ψ(u,v)≤M∞−(u2+v2)/(40π)\psi(u,v)\le M_\infty-(u^2+v^2)/(40\pi). The same arguments apply to all ordinary derivatives. A loop’s diagonal derivatives are finite sums of those mixed derivatives.

3. The original traces retain these losses

Use exactly the complete-outside contours and removed-row subsets in the underlying source theorem. No new stationary point at a distant real outside color is assumed. For a retained boundary edge use (9), and for a removed boundary edge use (10). Edges with no selected endpoint use (11). Edges entirely inside the selected set keep their original estimates.

After these replacements, the remaining exponent is precisely the complete-outside exponent of the proved source calculation, minus γ𝒰(𝒙)\gamma\mathcal U(\mathbf x). Every horizontal, mixed vertical, higher-charge, and removed-row estimate therefore retains that same loss. In particular each removed incidence still has its full δ\delta cost before the original partial-charge action budget is applied.

All coefficients and orientations of the finite Hermite identities are unchanged. For negative powers their reflected vertical-side signs are retained, and the zero-power case uses its already proved mixed-current estimate. First normals differentiate a finite incident product; (9)–(11) and the interior bounds retain every such derivative at polynomial cost. Odd simple-node terms remain among the original physical rows. The number of terms and the orders of derivatives are fixed independently of NN. This proves (4), including its ordinary outside derivatives.

4. Why the complex strip has quadratic height growth

Here polynomial continuation alone would give a linear height cost. Use both (4) and the proved local packet, as in the two-boundary Laurent argument. We give the multivariable version with the literal degrees (2).

For the undifferentiated source put Db=db(N−1)D_b=d_b(N-1), and express it as a Laurent polynomial in qb=eiubq_b=e^{iu_b}. The function (∏b∈BqbDb)FN(𝒒)exp⁡[−Nγ∑b∈Bdbqb](12) \left(\prod_{b\in B}q_b^{D_b}\right) F_N(\mathbf q)\, \exp\left[-N\gamma\sum_{b\in B}d_bq_b\right] \qquad\text{(12)} is holomorphic on the closed unit polydisk. Its distinguished boundary norm is at most CNAexp⁡N(M−γ∑bdb)CN^A\exp N(M-\gamma\sum_bd_b), by (4). Apply the maximum principle one variable at a time. Separate evenness permits either sign of each height, so evaluation at |qb|=e−|yb||q_b|=e^{-|y_b|} gives the upper exponential rate M−γ∑bdb+γ∑bdbe−|yb|cos⁡xb+∑bdb|yb|.(13) M-\gamma\sum_bd_b+ \gamma\sum_bd_be^{-|y_b|}\cos x_b+ \sum_bd_b|y_b|. \qquad\text{(13)} The same conclusion holds for every ordinary derivative, since it is again a separately even polynomial of degree at most (2) and has the real bound (4).

Outside any fixed neighborhood of 𝒙=0\mathbf x=0, the quantity 𝒰(𝒙)\mathcal U(\mathbf x) has a positive minimum. Choose h>0h>0 so small that the linear height terms in (13), including the change 1−e−|yb|1-e^{-|y_b|}, are absorbed into half that real loss. This gives (5) on this part of the real torus.

On a fixed small polydisk about zero, use the proved source expansion FN(𝒖)=εNNνa(𝒖)eNW(𝒖)(1+O(N−1)),a(𝒖)≠0,ℜW(0)=M.(14) F_N(\mathbf u)=\varepsilon_N N^\nu a(\mathbf u)e^{NW(\mathbf u)}(1+O(N^{-1})), \qquad a(\mathbf u)\ne0,\quad \Re W(0)=M. \qquad\text{(14)} The phase, amplitude and normalized remainder are holomorphic. Taking pointwise exponential rates in (4) gives ℜW(𝒙)≤M−γ𝒰(𝒙)\Re W(\mathbf x)\le M-\gamma\mathcal U(\mathbf x). The branch is separately even, so ∇W(0)=0\nabla W(0)=0 and −ℜW″(0)≽γdiag⁡(db)>0.(15) -\Re W''(0)\succeq \gamma\,\operatorname{diag}(d_b)>0. \qquad\text{(15)} Holomorphic Taylor expansion on a smaller neighborhood and Young’s inequality for the mixed real-imaginary quadratic terms give ℜW(𝒙+i𝒚)≤M−c𝒰(𝒙)+B0|𝒚|2\Re W(\mathbf x+i\mathbf y) \le M-c\mathcal U(\mathbf x)+B_0|\mathbf y|^2. The bounded local amplitude proves (5) there. All fixed ordinary derivatives have the same exponential upper bound with polynomial cost: apply Cauchy’s formula on ordinary-color circles of radius O(N−2)O(N^{-2}) inside the packet domain. The phase is holomorphic in those colors, including the ramified complete angles, so the change of exponent is O(N−1)O(N^{-1}). No nonvanishing derivative of the leading amplitude is assumed. This proves the theorem.

5. Consequence for marked corridor composition

For a connected path whose only unselected vertex is one free endpoint, e=me=m and ν=0\nu=0. For a selected run between two unselected endpoints, e=m+1e=m+1 and ν=−3/2\nu=-3/2, whether both ends are free or one is kept complete. Equations (4)–(5) and the unchanged nonzero local packet give exactly the boundary source and kernel hypotheses of the existing finite-library and long-turn composition theorems. A fixed finite collection has common constants after taking smaller neighborhoods and strips. This now applies at the positive-seven and signed-eight thresholds, with their original geometric and complete-end qualifications.

The exact corridor threshold criterion can therefore be used at K=7K=7 for its positive complementary presentation with complete retained ends. For example, binary negative corridors with endpoint runs of twos of length at least five and internal runs of length at least four have an admissible marked two-boundary source. Their fixed end twists and proper rotations remain those of the exact tangle identity. The canonical boundary-crossing theorem retains their chirp factors and full ordinary normals; it is applied after the whole-period estimate has been established, not to an arbitrary unproved short source.

The graph assertions concern the literal source (1). At an outside vertex of degree greater than one, they do not assert that canonical reduction to degree less than NN preserves the same global angular loss. Path endpoints have degree one and are already canonical, which is the scope of the immediate admissibility conclusion above. Knot and volume conclusions still use the underlying marked circle-tree realization and component tests. This boundary upgrade neither changes original assignment fibers nor resolves every fixed adjacent short word.

The minimal exact checker check_network_whole_boundary_decay.py verifies the two scalar loss comparisons, incidence degree accounting with loops, and the original powers of NN. Its saved record records the proof dependencies. It does not replace the contour, source, or Laurent arguments above.
