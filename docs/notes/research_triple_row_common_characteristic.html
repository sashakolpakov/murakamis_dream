The common original Jacobi characteristic of the triple-row pressure

Private written proof, 17 November 2026.

The critical equations of the positive triple-row pressure reduce to one strictly monotone scalar equation. Its selected solution is the common characteristic of the original Jacobi operators, and it computes the leading coefficient of every fixed-degree polynomial inserted in the original closing trace.

The source equations hold exactly, with their original forcing canceled by the full trace ideal. The characteristic and fixed-degree observations are asymptotic statements on strict physical support. They do not supply a stable continuation of external first normals or a lower bound after a crossing whose canonical polynomial degree grows with the level.

3. The action gradient and the actual common source characteristic

4. Fixed-degree inserted coupons have a calibrated coefficient

Theorem 2. Fix an integer d≥0d\ge0 and a compact K⋐𝒳∘K\Subset\mathcal X^\circ. For any polynomial q(C)=∑r=0dqrCrq(C)=\sum_{r=0}^dq_rC^r, uniformly over physical triples with 𝒋/N∈K\mathbf j/N\in K, ϕ(q(C)Fj1Fj2Fj3)Γ𝒋=q(ζ(𝒋/N))+OK,d(N−1∑r=0d|qr|).(25) \frac{\phi(q(C)F_{j_1}F_{j_2}F_{j_3})}{\Gamma_{\mathbf j}} =q(\zeta(\mathbf j/N)) +O_{K,d}\!\left(N^{-1}\sum_{r=0}^d|q_r|\right). \qquad\text{(25)} The coefficients may depend on NN; the displayed coefficient norm records that dependence. Proof. By (6) the numerator is exactly q(𝒥N,w1)ΓNq(\mathscr J_{N,w_1})\Gamma_N at the physical triple. For a fixed rr, expand rr successive applications of the three-term physical recurrence. The number of steps must remain fixed here. A literal crossing RnR^n, even for fixed nonzero nn, generally has canonical degree growing with NN; it is not covered by (25). The theorem also does not estimate external GjG_j insertions. Their activated Newton bands require a separate coherent comparison beyond the established fold window.
