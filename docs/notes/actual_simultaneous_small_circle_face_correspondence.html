Exact simultaneous small-circle face correspondences

30 October 2026. For ANY subset of untwisted small-circle ports whose literal identity smoothing is connected, the full original source is one coefficient of a simultaneous polynomial face correspondence. It includes protected outgoing faces and genuine cross-shift dependency cycles. The fixed-positive-radius cost is only exponential in the sum of the small colors. No global ordering of the individual face operators is asserted. If identity smoothing is disconnected, the full original source instead vanishes exactly in the small-degree window of section 6. Signed small ports remain separate.

1. The exact simultaneous object

Fix any subset S of untwisted small ports of the literal flat plat, with actual external colors 1<=h_i<=N. Put N>=3, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2. The remaining circles retain their folded labels and all original half-twist phases. Assume the identity smoothing is connected. Let G have the original white faces as vertices and a deleted edge b_i–c_i for each i in S. The ordinary Morse gap parity t_f is available at EVERY N: middle faces have the same parity, outer faces the opposite parity. In particular every component of G is monochrome and contains at most one of the protected faces u,v.

G is a forest. One proof uses the actual gap segments. In an odd gap its middle regions form a path. In an even gap they form a path with its two ends identified to exterior u. Connected smoothing retains a box in each internal even gap, so no entire such cycle is deleted. Distinct gap paths meet only at u; their remaining pieces do not form a new cycle. Thus every deleted component is a tree. The same fact follows from the literal face count: smoothing |S| boxes in a connected source loses exactly |S| faces.

Root each component at its protected face if there is one, otherwise at any face in that component. Retain variables Z_C for the roots. Every other face is unmarked. Orient each deleted edge from parent to child. If this reverses its original physical input/output order, use the exact ROOT transpose L_h^T=L_h. No marked functional or half twist is moved.

Expand each L_h into its original finite Chebyshev sum of ALL FORMAL CYCLIC STEP WORDS, before selecting any physical state. This is a fixed finite algebraic sum, not a state-dependent set of admissible paths. For a path at oriented edge e let p_e be first-head output-minus-input. Its middle correspondence is

L_child=Q^(-p_e) L_parent.

Thus L_f=Q^(k_f(p)) Z_C, k_root=0, uniquely on the tree. Keep all path coefficients BEFORE expanding away endpoint factors. They are Laurent polynomials with denominators ONLY in their input middle face, and nonnegative powers of their two outer faces. This holds for every word: the L2 local weights are

l0=2(x+y)/b+(q+q^-1)xy/b², l+=(1+qx/b)(1+qy/b), l-=(1+q-1x/b)(1+q-1y/b).

Subsequent steps replace b by Q^(-p)b, a unit phase, and Chebyshev only forms finite signed sums. All forbidden physical wraps have an exact zero at their first illegal step ON THE ACTUAL PHYSICAL GRID. Those endpoint factors remain in the formal words used off grid.

Let P_rem be the positive polynomial product of remaining circles. The simultaneous source correspondence is the finite sum over all oriented small-port formal words of their coefficients times P_rem, after these face substitutions, and with the ACTUAL background phase.

2. Exact phase and target

Before imposing constraints, the scalar face factor is

N^-1 scalar_rem prod_f L_f^(-d 1_(f unmarked))
                prod_(e in S)(L_be L_ce)^(d/2).

On a tree component, its total root exponent is -d if the root is unmarked and zero if it is protected. Its phase exponent divided by d is

sum_f (deg_G f-2 1_(f unmarked)) k_f
  =sum_(parent->child)(k_parent-k_child)=sum_e p_e.

The root term has k_root=0, so the formula holds for either root type. Hence the retained phase is exactly prod_e q^(d p_e). This is the phase of the known polynomial operator T_h, in either literal orientation. The original matrix transpose and its protected-face adapter were proved in the ordered-source theorem, section 4. This uses no generic-q transpose identity.

3. Componentwise polynomiality, including dependency cycles

The simultaneous expression, denoted Q_S, is a finite LAURENT polynomial in the roots Z_C. Fix one component C and hold every formal word in all other components fixed. Their denominators involve only their own middle roots, never a variable in C: outer and middle parity are opposite, so an outer face of a port cannot lie in its own merge component. Their numerators are polynomial in all C-face variables. P_rem is polynomial too.

Now sum the paths in C. Eliminate its tree leaves toward the chosen root. At a leaf f, no other remaining edge of C has f as a middle face, and no edge of C has f as an outer face (parity). All held external path factors are polynomial in f. Thus the exact one-port calculation applies over the Laurent coefficient ring in OUTSIDE component roots: T_h acts on the ENTIRE current polynomial, including the held other-component coefficients, then merges f into its parent. It preserves polynomiality in all C-face variables. Repeat to the root. Consequently after summing C the expression has no negative power of Z_C, for each fixed selection of all outside paths. Summing those paths cannot change this conclusion.

This argument can be performed separately for every component because all sums are finite. The FINAL simultaneous Laurent polynomial therefore has no negative exponent of ANY root, hence is an ordinary polynomial. No single global elimination order is claimed. The two-cycle example ports0,2 of [1,0,2,1] has no valid naive order, but is covered because the two mutually shifted roots have opposite parity and each partial sum is polynomial with the other paths fixed.

4. Exact original coefficient, degree and unique alias

Every local path weight is homogeneous of degree zero. Hence the final polynomial has degree d(R-|S|). There are R-|S| unmarked merged faces and the original two distinct protected components. The primitive final grid has N^(R-|S|+1) points. Exact physical support follows from the original complete-circle zeros and the small-path endpoint zeros. Global carries are retained by the phase computation above. Nonnegative exponents and the degree force the unique Fourier target d on each unmarked component, zero on the two protected ones. Writing r=R-|S| and F_sm for the unmarked merged components, the actual fixed-sector contribution is therefore EXACTLY

A_(N,a;h_S)=N^r product_(i not in S)
   [k_(a_i) beta_(a_i)^2 omega_(a_i)^epsilon_i]
   [product_(C in F_sm)Z_C^d] Q_S(Z)|_(Z_u=Z_v=0). (1)

The original unrestricted mark, ordinary caps, cups and all exterior phases are unchanged. Any separately retained exterior framing unit multiplies both sides. The scalar and measure are precisely those of identity smoothing; no extra auxiliary trace or new boundary average has been inserted. Q_S need not have positive coefficients.

This proof does not assert that two different ordered products of one-port T operators coincide. In the dependency-cycle fixture described above no such naive order gives the simultaneous correspondence in general. Its polynomiality is proved by separate component partial sums.

5. Fixed-positive-radius bound and capacity interface

At fixed positive root values assign every old face the value of its component. All Q phases have modulus one. The sum of weighted absolute coefficients of the three L2 paths is

A_i=2+4(x_i+y_i)/b_i+4x_i y_i/b_i².

Chebyshev bounds the weighted path sum by (A_i+1)^(h_i-1). This estimate can be applied BEFORE polynomial cancellation, directly to the finite Laurent correspondence. For a Laurent polynomial use the weighted coefficient norm sum_n |c_n|l^n, with all l_C>0. Unit clock phases do not change it and identifying equally weighted variables cannot increase it. Thus

||Q_S||_l <= P_sm(l) prod_i (A_i(l)+1)^(h_i-1),
|[Z^(d F_sm)]Q_S|
  <= prod_i (A_i(l)+1)^(h_i-1)
             P_sm(l)/product_(C in F_sm)l_C^d.     (2)

After division by the actual target radius monomial this gives C^sum(h_i) times the original smoothed positive coefficient bound, without any whole-N shifted target. All marked radii are fixed positive for this bound. A proved strict compatible smoothing gap therefore transfers to the arbitrary connected untwisted small subset, including protected outgoing ports. Bound (2) concerns connected identity smoothing; section 6 treats disconnected smoothing in a small-degree window. No signed deleted crossing is covered. The smoothed graph need not have a positive geometric certificate; P_sm is its literal nonnegative-coefficient source polynomial, and the required gap remains a separate capacity theorem. Taking absolute values of the remaining folded-label phases is permitted in the upper bound, but those phases remain in the exact identity (1).

6. Disconnected smoothing gives exact small-degree vanishing

Let S now be any untwisted small subset whose literal identity smoothing is disconnected, and put H=sum_(i in S)(h_i-1). Then the FULL original fixed-sector source is zero whenever

2H<d=N-1.                                        (3)

Every remaining complete coupon and its retained half twist are still present. This is an exact root identity, not a bound obtained by discarding an unmarked physical component. It applies to every fixed number of mesoscopic h_i=O(sqrt N), for all sufficiently large levels of both parities.

To prove it, use the actual gap decomposition again. A fully deleted internal even gap gives a cycle u->…->u. If a gap retains a box, its deleted segments give trees. Distinct gaps meet only at u. Thus G has beta>=1 independent cycles, ALL in the exterior component C_u, and every other component is a tree. There are beta+1 connected physical blocks after identity smoothing. With r=R-|S| remaining complete ports, G has r+beta+2 components: r+beta ordinary roots, the original marked root v, and the exterior root u. Parity keeps u and v distinct.

Orient each fully deleted even-gap cycle in its actual Morse direction u->…->u; orient attached trees away from u, and root all other trees as in section 1. Every nonroot face then has indegree one, and all extra indegrees occur at u. Keep ALL formal cyclic words. Their cycle charge constraints are initially congruences modulo N. The absolute value of each cycle sum is at most H<N under (3), so every such congruence is actual integer zero. This restriction depends only on the formal words in C_u; it is not a state-dependent restriction and does not constrain the words in any other component.

Consistent integer lifts k_f with k_root=0 therefore exist. The same phase telescoping remains exact:

sum_f(deg_G f-2 1_unmarked)k_f
     =sum_(oriented edges)(k_parent-k_child)=sum_e p_e.

For a nonroot vertex, deg-2 equals outdegree-indegree. The exceptional root terms vanish because k_u=0, including all extra cycle indegrees. Hence the actual phase is still q^(d sum p_e); no newly chosen scalar or marked character appears.

The original background monomial now has exponent +d beta at Z_u, exponent -d at each ordinary root, and zero at the marked root. Indeed C_u has e=v-1+beta edges and one protected vertex, so its exponent is -d(v-1)+de=d beta. The primitive compatible grid has N^(r+beta+1) points, retaining the unchanged single N^-1 marked factor. No trace normalization is assigned independently to the disconnected blocks.

The componentwise polynomial proof applies to EVERY component other than C_u. When its words are summed, the cycle constraints only concern the held C_u words and impose no new restriction on this tree sum. Consequently its root has no negative exponent. The resulting Q is homogeneous of degree dr and polynomial in every root except possibly u. A formal L_h word has at most h-1 Casimir steps, so its denominator degree in its own middle root is at most 2(h-1). No word outside C_u has a Z_u denominator. Therefore every surviving Laurent monomial obeys

n_u>=-2H_u>=-2H,

where H_u sums only ports in C_u. Restriction to compatible cycle tuples and cancellation cannot lower this bound.

The original face average requires n_C=d modulo N at each of the r+beta ordinary roots, and n_v=0 modulo N at the mark. These exponents are all nonnegative, hence their sum is at least d(r+beta). Homogeneity forces n_u<=-d beta<=-d, contradicting n_u>=-2H>-d. No monomial survives. This proves (3) with its actual finite measure and mark. Summing the remaining folded labels and phases preserves the exact zero.

The bound is sufficient, not claimed sharp. Outside (3) a disconnected small subset can be nonzero. For example the original four-box source [1,0,2,1], with ports 0 and 3 replaced by L_3 at N=5 and the other ports in maximal folded channels, is nonzero. A fixed number of small ports with h_i<=delta N satisfies (3) when delta is sufficiently small; no disconnected capacity theorem is needed in that window. Retained R^epsilon at a small port and genuine generic-q normals remain excluded.

7. Original-source verification and scope

The focused audit, with saved results, checks 61,743 original full-color operator entries, 4,278 formal-word endpoint zeros and 544 high-total columns. The actual connected-subset charts supply 457 subsets, 2,659 component phase/parity checks, 2,776 opposite-outer incidences and 218 genuine operator-dependency cycles. Twenty-three whole original simultaneous contractions agree with the coefficient correspondence, including both parities, protected faces, several small colors and width three. Their formal sums contain 865,463 negative-exponent terms before the full cancellations.

For section 6 the audit checks 208 disconnected original subsets, 232 exterior cycles, 1,164 background exponents and 956 tree-component phases. Four full cycle correspondences retain the exact compatibility constraints, even coset phase and N^(r+beta) source measure. Ten further original whole matrix contractions check the zero window at both parities, both mark positions, width two and three, with the remaining half twists retained. One original source outside the sufficient window is nonzero. The scalar is computed from the complete background exponent sum, independently of the simplified product q^(d sum p).

The correspondence does not imply a signed-small-port bound, a generic-q normal estimate, or a strict capacity gap.
