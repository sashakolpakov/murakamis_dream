Full long-filling volume from finite shared-curvature covers on a matched sphere

1. The diagram, its source, and the statement

Let 𝒯\mathcal T be a fixed finite simple triangulation of the oriented sphere with at least four vertices. Choose a perfect matching of its cubic dual. The RR matched pairs consist of 2R2R triangles and have R+2R+2 white vertices. A matching pair has an ordered quadrilateral (xe,be,ye,ce),e=1,…,R, (x_e,b_e,y_e,c_e),\qquad e=1,\ldots,R, \tag{1} whose common diagonal is xeyex_ey_e. The actual four-face polynomial is written in the order He(xe,be,ce,ye)H_e(x_e,b_e,c_e,y_e): its input and output middle variables are be,ceb_e,c_e. This distinction between the cyclic order and the polynomial argument order is retained throughout.

Use either an actual augmented diagram with this labeled cut, or the inverse-cut construction of the general Morse source theorem, §§1–4. It produces a link in S3S^3, with the recorded crossing disks and their primitive peripheral bases. Choose a nonmatching white edge uvuv as the original main-strand mark. Its two protected face variables remain exactly zero. There are RR ordinary face variables. The matching diagonal cannot join the protected pair: their unique edge in the simple sphere is the marked nonmatching edge. In particular every actual quadrilateral satisfies xe+ye>0x_e+y_e>0 when all ordinary variables are positive.

Fix the retained signed half twists εe∈{−1,0,1}\varepsilon_e\in\{-1,0,1\}, and require the literal outside connections and local parity pairings to give one main knot component. Filling the original auxiliary circles with disk-framed integers pep_e inserts precisely ne=εe−2pe. n_e=\varepsilon_e-2p_e. \tag{2} signed half twists. No crossing circle or terminal tangle is added by this criterion. All data, including the eventual integer powers, are fixed before the quantum level varies.

Write YDY_D for this actual augmented exterior. The constructive simple-sphere packing and original cut give ΦD=Vol⁡(YD)2π. \Phi_D=\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(Y_D)}{2\pi}. \tag{3} The geometry used here is the one proved in the Morse geometric-contribution theorem, §§1–2. The simple-sphere packing is constructed before any terminal decoration. A supplied positive labeled cut certificate would suffice instead. Neither the source nor the geometry is obtained by asserting that a Morse diagram is a flat plat.

We define below three explicit local weighted rates Ae,CA_{e,\mathrm C}, Ae,LA_{e,\mathrm L}, Ae,RA_{e,\mathrm R}, and a finite set Σ\Sigma of genuine noncentral label strings. Let ℬ\mathscr B be any finite collection of nonempty subsets of Σ\Sigma whose union is Σ\Sigma. For each B∈ℬB\in\mathscr B, choose one fixed positive vector LBL^B on all ordinary faces, with the original protected zeros. A face shared by several quadrilaterals has one common coordinate in this vector. Define GB(L)=max𝒔∈B∑e=1RAe,se(L)−∑f≠u,vlog⁡Lf. Finite-cover theorem. Suppose g:=minB∈ℬ{ΦD−GB(LB)}>0. g:=\min_{B\in\mathscr B}\{\Phi_D-G_B(L^B)\}>0. \tag{5} Then there is a finite threshold TT, depending on the fixed diagram, parity pattern and witnesses, such that every fixed signed filling vector with min⁡e|ne|>T\min_e|n_e|>T gives a hyperbolic knot KD(𝒑)K_D(\boldsymbol p) and limN→∞2πNlog⁡|⟨KD(𝒑)⟩N|=Vol⁡(S3\KD(𝒑)). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log|\langle K_D(\boldsymbol p)\rangle_N| =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K_D(\boldsymbol p)). \tag{6} Here the invariant is the original reduced, zero-framed enhanced Kashaev invariant with unknot value one. 8. Dependency boundary

The transfer is now closed at the stated criterion. Its inputs are the exact general Morse coefficient and normalizations; the phase-uniform real-spectral local CROSS/LEFT/RIGHT bounds; and the proved Morse all-CROSS analytic and geometric theorems. The finite norm theorem supplies the remainder transfer directly. No flat-plat isotopy, full shared positive endpoint theorem with an additional forest hypothesis, or new Fourier-tail theorem is used.

What remains a separate mathematical problem is whether a given original marked sphere admits the strict finite cover witnesses. This theorem neither asserts universal feasibility nor treats the fractional comparison atoms of a LEFT envelope as exact source pieces. It also makes no moving-main-meridian, shifted-chart, operator, or short-filling claim for these new planar applications.
