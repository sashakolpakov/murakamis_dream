The actual balanced Gauss source as a cyclic divisor contraction

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The finite balanced Gauss polynomial in the actual opposite source is an exact Fourier interpolation of a full square of shifted factorial ratios. Those ratios are the multiplication coefficients of one finite cyclic algebra. Its closed residue contour retains all poles, becomes nilpotent at completeness, and has an explicit first-normal carry with coefficient 2N2N. The construction descends polynomially to the actual commuting Cartan and Casimir, including their Jordan data.

This is an exact source adapter. It does not assert an asymptotic for the reconstructed coherent kernel, remove its exterior coupons, or select a geometric contribution. Complete direct G supplies the actual Gauss factor and F supplies the proved meridian frame bounds. All Fourier, algebra, residue, and normal calculations for the adapter are proved here.

Finite-flat Fermat/socle residue constructions already occur in the earlier charged-source note. That is coverage, not a proof premise here. The present identification is for the literal balanced Gauss factor, its calibrated functional, and its quantitative upper-carry sector.
