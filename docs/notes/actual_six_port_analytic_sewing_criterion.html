Source-exact analytic sewing: the physical interface and the remaining six-port estimate

29 October 2026. This separates an exact original-source fact from an analytic criterion. Uncrossed sewing of literal physical ports has only polynomial norm cost. A coherent scalar packet can then be sewn on any fixed graph, including graphs with cycles, if its JOINT marked phase has a controlled nondegenerate saddle and a global gap. Local packet gaps alone do not establish those hypotheses. The genuine six-port source is known exactly, but its required physical coefficient estimate is still missing; no broader knot equality is claimed here.

The distinction matters because the generic six-port channel frame is source exact only after saturation, and is not known to be tame. The theorem below does not assume a tame 6j/Smith frame.

1. The literal physical interface is polynomially conditioned

Retain N,d=N-1,q,Q,x and the ORIGINAL turns, with |x|=1:

p_ab=N x^(-b) delta_(a+b,d),
ell_ab=x^a delta_(a+b,d)/N,
m_ab=x^b/N^2.

Let U_ab=x^(-b)delta_(a+b,d). Then U is unitary, the cup matrix is NU, and the ordinary cap matrix is U^(-1)/N. Consequently a single-leg bend has matrix norm N or N^(-1), with no factorial conditioning. As flattened tensors their exact Euclidean norms are

||p||=N^(3/2), ||ell||=N^(-1/2), ||m||=N^(-1).       (1)

The last equality uses the FULL unrestricted marked row. Its eventual integer-degree restriction may be made only after the assembled source forces it. Pivots, literal leg permutations, and genuine dual-object isometries have norm one. Closing a fixed number of bare wires costs at most a fixed power of N by finite-dimensional Cauchy.

Thus for any fixed planar port graph G whose crossings and nontrivial coupons are kept INSIDE its specified blocks, its remaining original sewing functional satisfies

|Gamma_N(A_1,...,A_v)| <= N^(C_G) product_i ||A_i||_2.  (2)

This follows by composing the displayed turns and partial contractions; C_G is independent of N. Formula (2) retains the actual marked row, all unit characters, and every original scalar normalization. It does NOT make R or a changed recoupling frame polynomially bounded. A braid at an interface must be included in a block, or estimated separately.

In particular the exact six-head source can be sewn in its FULL physical tensor basis without an inverse generic Clebsch minor. Its known orthogonal projection onto the saturated generic source has norm one. But neither the reduced invariant lift Phi nor the unspecified generic channel inverse is thereby proved tame; those are different maps. Nor does a flat orthogonal projection prove preservation of an exponentially weighted geometric corridor.

2. A quantitative error budget that survives the actual contraction

Suppose an exact physical block is A_i=A_i^0+E_i, where, for fixed G,

||A_i^0|| <= N^C exp(N a_i),
||E_i|| <= N^C exp[N(a_i-gamma_i)], gamma_i>0.          (3)

Expansion of the multilinear contraction and (2) give the PROVED bound

|Gamma_N(A)-Gamma_N(A^0)|
  <= N^(C') exp[N(sum_i a_i-min_i gamma_i)].           (4)

If the proposed coherent main signal has rate F, preservation of its nonzero leading coefficient is therefore guaranteed by

min_i gamma_i > sum_i a_i-F.                          (5)

One can improve (4) using boundary-dependent profiles and joint maxima, but one cannot omit the loss sum a_i-F. Relative O(1/N) block errors do not in general preserve an exponentially smaller closed signal.

For an elementary sharp warning, contract vectors

A=(exp(Na),exp(Na),1), B=(exp(Nb),-exp(Nb),1).

Their exact pairing is 1. Perturbing the second coordinate of A by exp[N(a-gamma)] changes the pairing by exp[N(a+b-gamma)]. The contraction itself has norm one. Thus exponential amplification here comes from coherent cancellation, not an ill-conditioned measure.

The actual source has its own stronger warning: at even N the full circle satisfies L_N^2=0 although L_N is nonzero. Hence multiplying nonzero local circle images cannot supply a nonzero sewn density by itself. This is precisely why the genuine six-port junction cannot be replaced by Frobenius multiplication or by dephased positive routes.

3. A nonzero marked lattice-saddle theorem

Here is a sufficient analytic statement with an explicit noncancellation proof. After solving the EXACT wire/complement constraints, suppose a piece of the original scalar is

S_N=c_N sum_(k in NP intersect (Z^r+sigma_N))
                a_N(k/N) exp[N Phi(k/N)],              (6)

where P is a fixed compact convex polytope, r is fixed, and c_N is its original nonzero unit-character/power-of-N line. Affine O(1/N) shifts from d=N-1 must be retained in a_N or Phi_N, not silently replaced by their limit. For simplicity (6) displays one exact lattice coset. For example Phi_N=Phi+Psi/N+O(N^(-2)) changes the leading density to a_0 exp(Psi); that nonzero normalization factor is part of the answer.

Assume the following on a fixed neighborhood of an interior x_*:

  • Phi is C^3 there, a_0 is C^1, and a_N=a_0+O(1/N) uniformly, with a_0(x_*)!=0.
  • For a RETAINED integer Poisson mark m, grad Phi(x_*)=2pi i m.
  • H=-Phi’’(x_*) is complex symmetric with Re H positive definite.
  • Re Phi has its unique maximum F=Re Phi(x_) on P; it has a fixed gap outside every fixed neighborhood of x_. The local drop is quadratic. Globally |a_N| is polynomially bounded.

Then the whole sum (6), not just its absolute envelope, has

S_N=c_N exp(2pi i m.sigma_N) exp[N Phi_m(x_*)]
    N^(r/2) (2pi)^(r/2) a_0(x_*)/sqrt(det H)
    (1+O(N^(-1/2))),                                  (7)

where Phi_m=Phi-2pi i m.x. The square root is the continuous Gaussian branch on Re H>0. In particular its leading coefficient is nonzero.

Proof. On the exact coset the removed mark is the constant phase in (7). All terms outside a small fixed ball have a uniform exponential gap. Inside, put t=sqrt(N)(x-x_*). Taylor expansion gives the Gaussian exp(-t^T H t/2), with an integrable quadratic envelope. Restrict first to |t|<=N^epsilon, epsilon<1/6; the Taylor error is controlled by the Gaussian moments and is O(N^(-1/2)) after summation. The complementary local tail is exponentially small in N^(2epsilon). Gaussian Riemann sums with mesh N^(-1/2) converge uniformly in their bounded coset shift to the Gaussian integral, with O(N^(-1/2)) error by integrating the summable first derivatives over grid cells. That integral equals (2pi)^(r/2)/sqrt(det H): prove it first for real positive H and continue holomorphically on the convex domain Re H>0, using the same integrable bound. This proves (7) without deleting any endpoint residue.

For finitely many lattice cosets or marked sectors, their ACTUAL leading coefficients must be summed. Individual nonzero coefficients need not have nonzero sum. The single-coset theorem does not supply that extra arithmetic/source pairing. The original pretzel proof checks its paired charges explicitly; the six-port problem must do the corresponding work.

If the saddle is off the original real grid, (7) cannot be applied by declaring a new contour. One must first prove an EXACT source contour identity with all side currents retained. On a specified middle- dimensional contour having a real parametrization, the analogous continuous Gaussian proof applies to its pulled-back phase and density. Its Jacobian, boundary gaps and residual source currents are hypotheses, not consequences of a formal complex Hessian.

Fixed original normal jets: a uniform packet is the needed input

The bounded-confluence theorem now bounds the actual six-port source clusters by eight and their required normal coefficient order by a fixed constant (ten coefficients suffice). This removes an unbounded-order jet problem, but not a conditioning or nonvanishing problem.

Here is the exact analytic consequence. Let t=zeta-q be the ACTUAL normal parameter. If a coherent error E_N(t) is holomorphic on |t|<=r_N, r_N>=N^(-B), and

sup_|t|<=r_N ||E_N(t)|| <= N^C exp[N(F-gamma)],

then for every fixed j<=J,

||[t^j] E_N|| <= N^(C+Bj) exp[N(F-gamma)].             (7a)

This is Cauchy’s coefficient formula in any fixed physical tensor norm; finite dimension does not add an exponential loss. A fixed-pole Smith regularization uses only finitely many such coefficients, so (7a) preserves its exponential gap IF its explicit multiplying matrices have polynomial bounds, or their exponential rates are included in the budget (5). The bound on cluster size alone supplies neither fact.

The selected leading normal density must also be nonzero. For a scalar packet exp[N Phi(t)]a(t), any fixed derivative is exp[N Phi(0)] times an explicit finite Bell polynomial in N and the derivatives of Phi,a. The original source’s specified linear combination of those polynomials must be shown to have at least a polynomial lower bound. A fixed derivative order does not prevent that combination from vanishing. If only a finite inverse-N approximation to a(t) is used, it must be taken to sufficient order to survive the N^(BJ) Cauchy cost; a pointwise O(1/N) approximation is not automatically differentiable at that cost.

The bounded factorial envelope controls explicit termwise normal coefficients. It does not imply the UNIFORM COHERENT disk estimate in (7a). The physical original finite tangles have their own regular normal family, which must remain the one used in (7a); a root-only identity or another interpolating germ is not interchangeable with it. This is a precise analytic target for the new bounded six-port source, rather than a claim of automatic survival.

4. Closure on a fixed graph and the geometric test

Suppose every specified scalar block packet is expressed in its actual physical boundary labels. After literal sewing, these labels are affine functions L_i x+b_i of the independent wire coordinates. The total phase and density are the SUM of block phases and the PRODUCT of their densities, with the original wire characters included. The integration domain is the intersection of their actual support domains. In the real-concave case below this sewn domain is required to be compact and convex. If its actual support is not convex, a global uniqueness and gap statement must instead be proved on all its components.

For real concave phases, uniform strict concavity on all free directions follows from the checkable matrix inequality

sum_i L_i^T[-Phi_i''] L_i >= h I, h>0.                (8)

This allows local gauge zero modes if their joint kernel has been removed by the exact lattice constraints. An interior solution of the FULL sewn equations has a unique maximum and the required boundary gap; the finite lattice theorem proves its nonzero coefficient. Nothing in this argument restricts the fixed graph to a tree.

For complex phases the corresponding LOCAL statement is also stable under elimination. If Re H>0 and internal coordinates are eliminated at their genuine stationary point, the effective Hessian is

H_eff=H_ee-H_ei H_ii^(-1)H_ie, Re H_eff>0.             (9)

Indeed for w=(-H_ii^(-1)H_ie v,v), H w=(0,H_eff v), so Re(v^H_eff v)=w^(Re H)w>0. The effective density is multiplied by the NONZERO Gaussian determinant of H_ii. A global gap and the source contour family still need a separate proof; (9) alone is local.

If, in addition, the actual block canonical graphs identify the sewn stationarity equations with full shape matching, true INTEGER edge angles and the marked peripheral periods, the full-slice criterion identifies the positive critical point with the Casson–Rivin maximum. Absolute regulator calibration and primitive smooth end completion then identify its value with hyperbolic volume. These geometric tests cannot be inferred from (8), ordinary channel triangle inequalities, or the fact that a physical source polytope is combinatorially an octahedron.

5. Actual applicability and the next local estimate

The measure hypothesis is ALREADY true for literal original physical sewing by Section 1. Exact cap-tree kernels also give a real, all-width test case for positive squared kernels: their complete contraction has once-power positive simplex energy and strict concavity, with all internal factorials and phases accounted for in the cap-tree theorem. That test case does not give a complete interior six-port block.

For the genuine six-port source, the divided-power theorem provides the exact saturated source kernel, all original marked normalizations, and a finite formula for every physical coefficient. It does NOT yet provide:

  • a coherent physical coefficient packet with the nonzero density and uniform complex/corridor bounds needed in (6), or an exact permitted contour putting its saddle on that real parameter domain;
  • an error exponent satisfying the JOINT budget (5), and a global complementary-source gap;
  • the actual six-port canonical/angle incidence required after (9).

Thus a generic 6j formula followed by an unspecified inverse minor is not the missing estimate. A sufficient concrete target is a uniform packet for the literal six-head tensor of a SPECIFIED prime tangle in its physical ports, retaining its divided Ward relation. Such a result can be sewn without first proving a tame generic channel frame. This changes where the conditioning problem must be solved, but does not pretend the currently known six-port kernel already meets the analytic hypotheses or establish a new general volume-equality family.
