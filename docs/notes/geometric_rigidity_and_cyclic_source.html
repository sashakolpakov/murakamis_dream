Geometric local rigidity and the source lost by scalarizing quantum A

Research note, 26 October 2026. This supplies a local geometric period for the proposed comparison and identifies a precise two-parameter source defect when its two geometric branches are scalarized.

The uniform arguments are elementary polarized linear algebra, formal Gaussian contraction of the existing tetrahedron module, and exact Ore identities. The local formal period is not identified with the selected finite ADO evaluation. No quantum volume limit is proved.

1. Positive shapes make marked gluing unramified

Take a positive complete geometric ideal triangulation with t tetrahedra, as supplied by the audited geometric input. Choose positive local orders and compatible logarithms

u_j=Log z_j, v_j=Log z_j'', z_j''=1-z_j^(-1),
tau_j=dv_j/du_j=1/(z_j-1), Im tau_j<0.          (1.1)

No compatible global branching is required for this calculation. The already proved integral edge/peripheral identities give t-1 independent real edge rows, together with the marked meridian row. Write their full-rank matrix as (A B). Its rows are isotropic:

A B^T=B A^T.                                  (1.2)

The logarithmic gluing Jacobian at fixed meridian is

J=A+B diag(tau_j).                            (1.3)

It is invertible. Indeed ker(A B) is a real Lagrangian subspace. If J a=0, the shape tangent w=(a,diag(tau)a) belongs to its complexification, so Omega(w,bar w)=0. On the other hand

(i/2)Omega(w,bar w)=sum_j Im(tau_j)|a_j|^2<0

for every nonzero a. Thus a=0. This proves nonsingularity uniformly, without a census or an imported rigidity proof.

The exponentiated gluing equations are algebraic on the open set z_j(1-z_j)!=0. Multiplying (1.3) by their nonzero monomial derivatives changes its determinant only by units. Hence the marked-meridian projection of the geometric gluing germ is etale. After passing to its residue field, its completed local ring is K[[U]], U=Log m. Its successive shape coefficients are uniquely recovered by the invertible Jacobian recursion. The complete shapes are algebraic: at m=1 they are an isolated point of equations over Q. This is a local algebraic rigidity statement for this gluing branch, not a quantum-state selection theorem.

2. The two geometric peripheral slopes are distinct

Keep the full marked boundary convention already derived in the regulator note:

Omega_red=-(1/2)dU wedge dV,
U=Log m, V=Log l,
m=M^2, l=L_intr^2.

Along the geometric branch normalize dU=1 and write a_j=du_j/dU. The same polarized identity, now on the edge coisotropic space, gives

Im(dV/dU)=-2 sum_j Im(tau_j)|a_j|^2>0.         (2.1)

The vector a cannot vanish since dU=1. Thus the marked cusp derivative tau_cusp=dV/dU is nonreal and nonzero. Complex conjugation supplies a second nondegenerate gluing germ with derivative bar(tau_cusp). Both map to (m,l)=(1,1).

Use the actual meridian coordinate u=Log M=U/2 and a specified longitude lift L_c=+1 or -1. Its sign is retained. The two longitude branches L_+,L_- satisfy

L_+(0)=L_-(0)=L_c,
L_+'(0)=L_c tau_cusp,
L_-'(0)=L_c bar(tau_cusp).                     (2.2)

Their difference has a simple zero. These two local images form a transverse two-branch subgerm of the peripheral curve. There may be other components; no assertion of their absence or of global irreducibility is made.

3. A nonzero formal period from the existing tetrahedron module

Let Q=exp(epsilon). Use the compact tetrahedron solution already appearing in the pointed gluing construction:

psi_Q(Qz)=(1-z)psi_Q(z), psi_Q=P_Q^(-1).

Here multiplication by z and forward Q-shift have quantum-torus parameter R=Q^(-1). Keeping this inversion is essential. Its formal logarithm on a compatible dilogarithm sheet is

Log psi_Q(z)=-Li_2(z)/epsilon-(1/2)Log(1-z)
   -sum_(g>=1) B_(2g) epsilon^(2g-1)
                     Li_(2-2g)(z)/(2g)!.       (3.1)

It follows either by expanding the defining product formally or by substitution in the displayed difference equation. Its leading amplitude (1-z)^(-1/2) is nonzero on the nondegenerate shape cover. Higher coefficients are rational functions of z after adjoining the recorded square-root frame.

Write w=Log(1-z)=u+v+piik with k odd. The exact Gaussian shear exp(-u^2/(2epsilon)-piik*u/epsilon) changes its leading action to

F_j(u)=-Li_2(e^u)-u^2/2-pi*i*k*u,
F_j'=v, F_j''=tau_j.

Its full shifted equation includes the half-step character

phi(u+epsilon)=exp(-epsilon/2)(1-exp(-u))phi(u). (3.2)

This factor is retained, rather than set to one. Finite-root Weyl characters and SL2 lifts still have to be matched in the actual state comparison; a formal square-root convention alone does not identify those finite-level data.

For completeness, a nondegenerate formal Gaussian presentation can always be obtained. Since det(A-iB)!=0 by Section 1, its multilinear column expansion has a nonzero term choosing one column from each A_j,B_j pair. Swap (u_j,v_j)->(v_j,-u_j) for the corresponding subset. The new B is invertible. These partial formal Fourier transforms keep Im tau_j<0, because tau_j becomes -1/tau_j, and keep the local amplitude a unit. They act on the existing quantum torus; they are not a claimed global tetrahedral face-tensor comparison.

Complete the real gluing rows to a symplectic matrix (A B;C D), using the marked half-longitude lift so that the last momentum is p_U=-V/2. All affine reference characters are retained as in the regulator centering calculation. For invertible B, the full generating action in constraint values q is

W(u;q)=sum_j F_j(u_j)+(1/2)u^T B^(-1)A u
             -u^T B^(-1)q+(1/2)q^T D B^(-1)q. (3.3)

Both quadratic matrices are symmetric. The last term is necessary for the marked momentum: at a critical point,

partial_q W=-B^(-T)u+D B^(-1)q=p.

The internal Hessian is

H=diag(tau_j)+B^(-1)A, Im H<0.                (3.4)

It is invertible. In particular there is a nonzero local formal period, with a Gaussian leading coefficient equal to the product of the local units times det(-H)^(-1/2), and the specified Fourier, measure and character factors. The half-density cover retains all these roots; their absolute scalar is not equated to the ADO cut.

Here the formal Gaussian is well-defined for an elementary reason. Subtract W_* and the full quadratic t^T Ht/2, leaving terms of degree at least three. Wick contraction has covariance -epsilon H^(-1). At each epsilon order only finitely many cubic and higher vertices can contribute. Its leading coefficient is the nonzero amplitude at t=0. No leftover quadratic divided by epsilon is admitted. Formal integration by parts follows directly from the same Wick rule, so this is the local formal gluing period of the supplied tetrahedron and edge equations.

All coefficients are regular at the complete point: the shape denominators and Hessian determinant there are units. This does not show that a finite root-level projected vector is nonzero, or that the formal series converges on the desired sequence.

4. The dual line has the actual marked regulator action

Distinguish the gluing vector from its period functional. At fixed edge values (3.3) gives dW_=-V dU/2. Thus its scalar state has inverse-longitude leading shift in the actual M convention. The dual formal line has action -W_.

The exact Bloch identity used in the actual action proof applied to (3.3) gives, at the complete point,

Im W_*=-D(beta_T)=-Vol(M).                     (4.1)

Indeed the quadratic coefficients are real, the complete constraint values and reference linear coefficients are purely imaginary, the internal derivatives vanish, and the boundary quadratic term at those values is real. Its imaginary part is therefore precisely the negative tetrahedral Bloch sum. No imaginary integration constant is selected to force this value.

Put h=Log q, epsilon=Log Q=2h and

S_geo=-W_*/2, R_geo=-W_*-UV/4.                 (4.2)

Then the dual line exp(S_geo/h) satisfies

dS_geo=(V/2)d(U/2),
dR_geo=-(U dV-V dU)/4,
Im S_geo|_complete=Vol(M)/2.                  (4.3)

The retained SL2 longitude sign is its explicit solution character. Its meridian ratio has leading value L_intr, rather than its inverse. Consequently this dual formal line has the already marked regulator connection and complete regulator norm. This identifies which formal line is the candidate for the actual classical action; it does not identify a finite state with the dual of another state.

On either geometric embedding, its normalized ratio has the form

a(h,u)=L_intr(u)+h a_1(u)+h^2 a_2(u)+...,

with every coefficient regular at u=0. The exact action differential, algebraic unit leading amplitude and rational Gaussian vertices show that these coefficients are algebraic on the normalized gluing germ. Thus the two embeddings supply two regular formal ratios with the distinct slopes (2.2).

If an operator of this dual gluing module annihilates the local section, its first nonzero classical symbol vanishes on this geometric longitude branch: substitute the ratio and take the constant formal coefficient. This gives the geometric factor for that gluing object. It does not identify the annihilator ideal of the selected ADO sum with the dual gluing ideal.

5. A scalar quantum-A equation must retain the collision data

Let sigma(u)=u+h, S=C[[u,h]], and a,b in S^times be any two such regular ratios, with a(0,0)=b(0,0)=L_c and

kappa=partial_u(a-b)(0,0)!=0.

For a scalar operator P=sum p_j(h,u)T^j, put P_0(u,L)=sum p_j(0,u)L^j and P_1=sum [h]p_j L^j. The first-order right-remainder calculation gives on a branch

(P_0)_L a_1+P_1+(L L'/2)(P_0)_(LL)=0.          (5.1)

If P annihilates both regular formal lines, evaluate at the collision. Subtract their two conditions to obtain

(L_c/2)(L_+'-L_-')(P_0)_(LL)(0,L_c)=0.        (5.2)

Thus P_0 cannot be just the reduced two-branch equation times a factor nonzero there: that would have (P_0)_(LL)!=0. It must retain additional vanishing data, such as a meridian factor or an additional characteristic multiplicity. This is a necessary condition on a proposed comparison operator. It does not assert that the unknown actual minimal knot operator already acts on both formal geometric lines.

There is an exact model with no extra characteristic branch. Write Delta=a-b. The primitive regular second-order operator

P=ab sigma(Delta)-(a sigma(a)-b sigma(b))T+Delta T^2 (5.3)

annihilates both lines. Substitution gives zero on each without division. Its classical symbol is

P_0=(L_+-L_-)(L-L_+)(L-L_-).                   (5.4)

The additional factor L_+-L_- is u times a unit. Nevertheless the quantum coefficients have no common local divisor. Indeed Delta and sigma(Delta) have independent linear parts:

Delta=kappa*u+beta*h+O((u,h)^2),
sigma(Delta)=kappa*u+(kappa+beta)h+O((u,h)^2).

Their Jacobian determinant is kappa^2, so they generate (u,h). The constant and leading coefficients in (5.3) are units times sigma(Delta) and Delta. The coefficient ideal is exactly (u,h), which has no common nonunit divisor. Cancelling the meridian factor after h=0 therefore changes the integral quantum data.

6. The complete cyclic source is a length-one point defect

On the split semilinear module V=S e_a direct_sum S e_b let T e_a=a e_a, T e_b=b e_b and choose e=e_a+e_b. The two-vector cyclic frame (e,Te) has determinant b-a and cokernel S/(Delta). It is not in general T-stable. Keep every shift and define

Lambda=sum_(j in Z) S T^j e subset V.

Use f=(1,0), e=(1,1), so V=S e direct_sum S f. The differences of the two components of Te and T^2e are

Delta, a sigma(a)-b sigma(b)
  =a sigma(Delta)+Delta sigma(b).

They generate (Delta,sigma(Delta))=(u,h). Every shifted component difference vanishes at (0,0), including negative shifts since a,b are units. Hence exactly

Lambda=S e direct_sum (u,h)S f,
V/Lambda=S/(u,h), T acts on this quotient by L_c. (6.1)

This is a length-one source supported at the simultaneous quantum and meridian point. Lambda has rank two but three generators in its complete fiber. A free presentation has generators e,u f,h f and the single relation h(u f)-u(h f)=0.

Its ordinary dual loses that point quotient:

Lambda^vee=V^vee, Lambda^(vee vee)=V.           (6.2)

For a direct proof, a functional on the ideal (u,h) sends u,h to A,B with hA=uB. Coprimality forces A=u c,B=h c, so it extends uniquely to S. This proves (6.2) without a duality theorem. The normalization or generic period line alone has therefore forgotten a specified source even though all its dual vectors lift.

The same free presentation also computes the part retained by duality with its relation included:

Ext^1_S(Lambda,S)=S/(u,h).                    (6.3)

Indeed applying Hom(-,S) to S -> S^3 -> Lambda gives the row (0,h,-u), whose image is (u,h). Thus keeping that single relation retains the source that the ordinary dual loses.

The ordered two-variable residue of this source has nonzero value

Res_0 du dh/(Delta sigma(Delta))=1/kappa^2.     (6.4)

This follows directly from their invertible Jacobian. It is a local algebraic source residue, not the hyperbolic volume or the value of the selected knot invariant.

7. Relation to the actual source and what remains

A fixed-level nodal order and its cut can be compared explicitly

Fix N first and retain the two geometric branches marked in (2.2). Write d(u)=L_+(u)-L_-(u), so d’(0)=kappa. Their spectral order is

A_geo=R[xi]/(xi(xi-d)), R=C[[u]],
xi=L-L_-.

The actual two-color Casimir pair c_0,c_last has local order

A_src=R[t]/(t(t-delta_s)),
delta_s=c_last-c_0=rho(M^2-M^(-2)), t=z-c_0.

The unit alpha=delta_s/d has alpha(0)=4rho/kappa. Choosing c_last -> L_+ and c_0 -> L_- fixes the explicit isomorphism

psi:A_src -> A_geo, psi(t)=alpha xi.           (7.1)

It transports the partial normalization and conductor (u,t) to their geometric counterparts. On the fraction fields it also sends the actual source projector E_last=t/delta_s to the geometric branch projector xi/d. Their polar residues therefore agree under the same map. This is a marked local-order comparison; choosing these branch marks is not a proof of the global quantum period comparison.

Let Phi_src be the actual scalar pivotal cut on the local pair. Put C=M+M^(-1), D_N=MN+M(-N), c=q+q^(-1). The already proved two-color projector weights give the exact whole-germ formulas

A(u)=Phi_src(1)=(-1)^N c C/D_N,
B(u)=Phi_src(t)=(-1)^N rho C(qM^2-q^(-1)M^(-2))/D_N. (7.2)

For example the two projector numerators add to (q+q(-1))(M2-M^(-2)); their common denominator is M(2N)-M(-2N). Dividing by the original color dimension ratio cancels MN-M(-N) and leaves precisely D_N. This derives (7.2), including its full 1+U factor:

D_N=M^(-N)(1+U), U=M^(2N).

No specialization of that peripheral factor was made in deriving the comparison.

Use the geometric algebraic residue Phi_geo(a+b xi)=b. It is perfect, but its value on 1 is zero. A scalar rescaling cannot generally match (7.2). The unique matching unit density is

theta=theta_0+theta_1 xi,
theta_1=A,
theta_0=B/alpha-A d
   =(-1)^(N+1) [d/(M-M^(-1))]
               (q^(-1)M^2-qM^(-2))/D_N,
Phi_src(a)=Phi_geo(theta psi(a)).              (7.3)

This equality is checked on 1,t and therefore holds identically. At completeness it reads

theta_0(0)=(-1)^N kappa*rho/4,
theta_1(0)=(-1)^N(q+q^(-1)).                  (7.4)

The first coefficient is nonzero, so theta is a unit. For N>2 the second is nonzero as well. It is fixed by the actual pivotal cut and must be retained. Equivalently the complete actual Casimir projector and its nilpotent satisfy

Phi_src(e_0)=(-1)^N(q+q^(-1)),
Phi_src(J_N)=(-1)^N rho^2.

These are precisely (7.4) under t -> (4rho/kappa)xi. The full ADO cut multiplies theta by the original d_N(M), keeping eta_N.

In fact that full density has a fixed rational central presentation. Put Y=M^2, U=M^(2N) and zeta_N=q(N(1-N)/2)=i(1-N). Then

Theta=d_N(M)theta
  =zeta_N U/((U-1)(U+1))
      [d(u)(q^(-1)Y-qY^(-1))
              -(q+q^(-1))(Y-Y^(-1))xi].        (7.5)

This follows by writing d_N(M)=eta_N(M-M(-1))MN/(U-1) and D_N=M^(-N)(1+U) in (7.3). Its only N dependence is the specified root data and evaluation U=Y^N. Both peripheral factors and the full phase remain. At M=1 the numerator and U-1 must be combined on that actual root curve before evaluation. The expression is not a replacement of the root constraint by an independent geometric central character.

Every fixed meridian jet of (7.1), (7.3), and their inverses is polynomially bounded in N in these marked bases. Indeed kappa and the geometric Taylor coefficients are fixed, rho has size comparable to 1/N, and D_N=2cosh(Nu) has fixed-order Taylor coefficients polynomial in N and constant term two. The other vanishing ratios have nonzero normalized constant terms; their inverse coefficients follow by finite recursive division. At the complete fiber the full density’s coefficients have sizes O(N^(-2)), O(N^(-1)), and its inverse has coefficients O(N^2), O(N^3), respectively. These bounds keep all phases.

This controls a local, non-scalar source/cut normalization. It does not identify the difference transport or chosen global section, control jets whose order grows with N, or exclude cancellation in the final scalar contraction.

The fixed-level wall and the two-parameter point remain distinct

At each fixed N the actual meridian source order has an elementary partial normalization, quotient C[[u]]/(u), and a conductor dual. The formal geometric scalarization here has the different two-parameter defect S/(u,h). Both are explicit data erased by generic line comparison. They must not be identified merely because each has length one. The relation q_N^N=-1 in the actual root family is additional data beyond the formal completion at h=0. The local comparison must retain the actual root germ and pointing as well.

The full integral cut theorem provides the actual self-dual centralizer and its unit-group torsor. Section 6 explains why order or dual-lattice comparison alone can still miss the cyclic source pointing. A full comparison must carry that source as well as its operator, selected section and cut functional.

The positive triangulation now supplies an unramified marked gluing germ, a nonzero local formal period, the required dual regulator action, and a concrete scalarization constraint at the peripheral collision. The actual finite ADO source has not been transported to that formal geometric line. Nor has its coefficient or possible cancellation in the selected period been established. No asymptotic specialization of the formal series is assumed. The original target remains

(4/N)log|F_N(1)|-log||P_hyp|| ->0.

8. Coverage and exact diagnostics

Geometric deformation and peripheral-potential theory are existing subjects of Neumann–Zagier, Volumes of hyperbolic three-manifolds. Its introduction and scope were consulted for coverage, not its proof techniques. No novelty claim is made for polarized transversality, formal stationary phase or cyclic-vector methods. The arguments above reconstruct the exact statements needed for the current marked ADO comparison. The old analytic AK volume calculation remains a separate state-comparison problem.

The audit checks 25 exact rational-complex symplectic gluing charts with one to five tetrahedra, including singular B blocks requiring partial Fourier changes. It checks invertibility, the marked cusp energy identity, all Hessian/Jacobian coordinate factors and the full boundary Gaussian derivative. The local tetrahedron difference equation is checked through epsilon^5, retaining its half-step character. Six exact Ore examples test both remainders, the classical meridian factor, the absence of a common local quantum divisor and the nonzero point-defect residue. These are abstract uniform diagnostics, not six claimed knot operators. Eight additional cases at N=2,3,4,5,6,9,12,15 check the actual nodal cut formulas and the matching unit density, both at the complete fiber and at a generic color. The geometric slope in these finite-field diagnostics is an arbitrary nonzero parameter; the cusp nonvanishing itself is proved in Section 2. Results.

Reproduce with:

python3 experiments/audit_geometric_rigidity.py

The subsequent cut-wall source theorem now relates the two-parameter cyclic point module above to the actual cut. Its normalized branch density values are -p,sigma(p), where p=q(-1)M2-qM^(-2). The resulting point ideal, regularized cut presentation and calibrated residue agree with the geometric cyclic model after an explicit regular formal unit gauge. The physical ADO common frame remains fixed. This identifies a local source presentation, while the global difference action and selected quantum period still have to be compared.
