An explicit general domain for the endpoint half-character amplitude

Private research note, 16 November 2026. Written proof only.

The analytic interpolation of the original endpoint’s entire high coupon extends to an explicit complex domain. Its exact physical-row identity and its domain of uniform asymptotics remain different assertions. No original low coupon, spectator, or contour side term is discarded.

The sole direct proof source is the endpoint half-character amplitude theorem, SHA256 85c866a031d1293a1cc896a172ab465a52f469a19a4326219856d52ed3970ac8. Its entire proof was read at this version. The adopted file is unchanged.

2. The exact limiting contraction domain

Theorem 1.
