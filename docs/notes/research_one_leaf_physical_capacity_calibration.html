A polynomial reference for one prescribed matrix-product leaf

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof. Earlier finals and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

A known leaf of the actual physical matrix-product compiler can be matched to its parent’s full input density with an explicit polynomial change of reference. The other input and output have specified row/column-energy densities. These give a full-matrix logarithmic inequality on every finite metric window, without selecting a largest singular mode or using the unknown closed invariant.

The construction is one-sided. It does not match the second input to an arbitrary subtree, or prove a geometric lower bound.

1. The actual matrix-product compiler

Let 𝒜=⨁ν=1sMat⁡D(ℂ),∥A∥2=∑νTr⁡(Aν*Aν),n=dim⁡𝒜=sD2.(1) {\mathcal{ A}}=\bigoplus_{\nu=1}^{s}\operatorname{Mat}_D(\mathbb C), \qquad \|A\|^2=\sum_\nu\operatorname{Tr}(A_\nu^*A_\nu), \qquad n=\dim{\mathcal{ A}}=sD^2 . \qquad\text{(1)} Vector norms on 𝒜{\mathcal{ A}} use this unnormalized physical Hilbert–Schmidt structure. Map norms labelled op{\mathrm{ op}} are induced operator norms; tensor coefficient norms are labelled HS{\mathrm{ HS}}. Componentwise multiplication is the linearized tensor μ:𝒜⊗2𝒜→𝒜,μ(A⊗B)ν=AνBν.(2) \mu:{\mathcal{ A}}\otimes_2{\mathcal{ A}}\longrightarrow{\mathcal{ A}}, \qquad \mu(A\otimes B)_\nu=A_\nu B_\nu . \qquad\text{(2)} For the actual all-main-color source of [C, Section 5], s=Ns=N and D=NbD=N^b, where bb is the fixed braid width. Its leaves are the original signed physical crossings, or specified nonzero blocked words. Its root takes the original partial pivotal trace and marked entry, including framing and external factors. The output and its positive norm remain YN=(c1,…,cN),∥YN∥2=|KN|2+RN,0≤RN≤(N−1)(N/2)2b−2.(3) Y_N=(c_1,\ldots,c_N),\qquad \|Y_N\|^2=|K_N|^2+R_N,\qquad 0\le R_N\le(N-1)(N/2)^{2b-2}. \qquad\text{(3)} We do not replace this root by an identity trace. The full matrix spaces retain all physical channels and Jordan data.

Fix a known nonzero leaf A=αaA=\alpha a, where α=∥A∥>0\alpha=\|A\|>0 and ∥a∥=1\|a\|=1. The number α\alpha is computed from this prescribed leaf; it can be exponentially large. It is not the closed source and will not be discarded. Write La(B)=aBL_a(B)=aB. Matrix units in each color block give C:=μμ*=DI𝒜,Tr⁡1(μ*μ)=Tr⁡2(μ*μ)=DI𝒜,X:=LaLa*≼C,Tr⁡C=sD3,Tr⁡X=D.(4) C:=\mu\mu^*=D I_{\mathcal{ A}},\qquad \operatorname{Tr}_1(\mu^*\mu) =\operatorname{Tr}_2(\mu^*\mu)=D I_{\mathcal{ A}},\qquad X:=L_aL_a^*\preceq C,\qquad \operatorname{Tr}C=sD^3,\quad \operatorname{Tr}X=D . \qquad\text{(4)} For example μ*Eij=∑kEik⊗Ekj\mu^*E_{ij}= \sum_k E_{ik}\otimes E_{kj}; different color blocks do not interact. Also ∥La∥op=max⁡ν∥aν∥op≤1\|L_a\|_{\mathrm{ op}}=\max_\nu\|a_\nu\|_{\mathrm{ op}}\le1.

We first treat a leaf on the first input. For a leaf on the second input, use Ra(B)=BaR_a(B)=Ba instead of LaL_a, apply the reference on that second input, and exchange the logical input labels below. The same matrix-unit proof applies. No matrix factors are commuted.

2. An input reference which fixes the entire original leaf

Put P=|a⟩⟨a|,Q=I−P,Gt=P+tQ,t≥1,Bt=μ(Gt−1⊗I).(5) P=|a\rangle\langle a|,\quad Q=I-P,\quad G_t=P+tQ,\qquad t\ge1,\qquad B_t=\mu(G_t^{-1}\otimes I). \qquad\text{(5)} Thus Gta=aG_t a=a, GtA=AG_t A=A, and ∥Gt∥op≤t,∥Gt−1∥op=1\|G_t\|_{\mathrm{ op}}\le t,\ \|G_t^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{ op}}=1, with equality in the first bound when n>1n>1. Use GtG_t on the selected leaf’s output and its inverse on the first input of its parent. In coefficient tensor notation the latter is the inverse transpose on the dual leg. The complex bilinear contraction is exactly unchanged.

This is a full bundled compiler-edge gauge. In general PP mixes colors and matrix coordinates. It is not a physical-head gauge or an algebra automorphism. No output reference change is needed. Every original source row, phase and incident inverse remains.

Since μ(P⊗I)=La(⟨a|⊗I)\mu(P\otimes I)=L_a(\langle a|\otimes I), (4) gives Yt:=BtBt*=X+t−2(C−X),Zt:=Tr⁡Yt=D{1+t−2(sD2−1)}.(6) Y_t:=B_tB_t^*=X+t^{-2}(C-X),\qquad Z_t:=\operatorname{Tr}Y_t =D\{1+t^{-2}(sD^2-1)\}. \qquad\text{(6)} In particular Yt≻0Y_t\succ0; no normal channel is removed. Define densities on the output and on the two logical inputs by ρt=YtZt,ωt=Bt*BtZt,ω1,t=Tr⁡2ωt,ω2,t=Tr⁡1ωt.(7) \rho_t=\frac{Y_t}{Z_t},\qquad \omega_t=\frac{B_t^*B_t}{Z_t},\qquad \omega_{1,t}=\operatorname{Tr}_2\omega_t,\quad \omega_{2,t}=\operatorname{Tr}_1\omega_t . \qquad\text{(7)} These are the actual normalized Hilbert–Schmidt marginals of BtB_t, in the logical-input convention. Their transposes belong on the physical dual input half-edges, as in [O]. No independent ordered-density witness is being identified with an actual tensor marginal.

3. Three exact marginal formulas

The canonical comparison states are ρ0=XD,σa=La*LaD,ηt=sD2−1t2+sD2−1.(8) \rho_0=\frac{X}{D},\qquad \sigma_a=\frac{L_a^*L_a}{D},\qquad \eta_t=\frac{sD^2-1}{t^2+sD^2-1}. \qquad\text{(8)} Both comparison states have trace one. Write ϵ=t−2\epsilon=t^{-2} and Z=Zt/D=1+(n−1)ϵZ=Z_t/D=1+(n-1)\epsilon. All three actual marginals have the same explicit form: ω1,t=P+ϵ(I−P)Z,ω2,t=σa+ϵ(I−σa)Z,ρt=ρ0+ϵ(I−ρ0)Z.(9) \boxed{\qquad \omega_{1,t}=\frac{P+\epsilon(I-P)}{Z},\quad \omega_{2,t}=\frac{\sigma_a+\epsilon(I-\sigma_a)}{Z},\quad \rho_t=\frac{\rho_0+\epsilon(I-\rho_0)}{Z}. \qquad} \qquad\text{(9)} For the first identity, trace Bt*BtB_t^*B_t over the second input and use (4), obtaining DGt−2DG_t^{-2}. For the second, cyclicity in the traced first factor moves the two copies of Gt−1G_t^{-1} together. The PP term is Tr⁡1((P⊗I)μ*μ)=La*La\operatorname{Tr}_1((P\otimes I)\mu^*\mu)=L_a^*L_a; the identity term is DIDI. This gives the middle formula. The last formula is (6). These partial-trace identities do not assume that GtG_t commutes with μ*μ\mu^*\mu.

For n>1n>1, every density τ\tau obeys 0≼τ≼I0\preceq\tau\preceq I, so the expression in (9) equals (1−ηt)τ+ηt(I−τ)/(n−1)(1-\eta_t)\tau+\eta_t(I-\tau)/(n-1). The two densities in this convex combination are normalized positive states. Therefore, with ∥T∥1=Tr⁡T*T\|T\|_1=\operatorname{Tr}\sqrt{T^*T}, ∥ω1,t−P∥1=2ηt,∥ω2,t−σa∥1≤2ηt,∥ρt−ρ0∥1≤2ηt.(10) \boxed{\quad \|\omega_{1,t}-P\|_1=2\eta_t,\qquad \|\omega_{2,t}-\sigma_a\|_1\le2\eta_t,\qquad \|\rho_t-\rho_0\|_1\le2\eta_t . \quad} \qquad\text{(10)} The first equality uses the orthogonal supports of PP and I−PI-P. For n=1n=1 all three states agree and ηt=0\eta_t=0.

4. A full-matrix logarithmic calibration

For arbitrary positive metrics Ho,H1,H2H_o,H_1,H_2 on 𝒜{\mathcal{ A}}, write Xi=log⁡HiX_i=\log H_i. Scalar spectral Jensen on the coefficient tensor of BtB_t gives log⁡∥Ho1/2Bt(H1−1/2⊗H2−1/2)∥HS≥12log⁡Zt+12Tr⁡(ρtXo)−12Tr⁡(ω1,tX1)−12Tr⁡(ω2,tX2).(11) \begin{split} \log\|H_o^{1/2}B_t(H_1^{-1/2}\otimes H_2^{-1/2})\|_{\mathrm{ HS}} \ \ge\ &\frac12\log Z_t+\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_tX_o)\\ &-\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(\omega_{1,t}X_1) -\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(\omega_{2,t}X_2). \end{split} \qquad\text{(11)} For clarity, after vectorization the three signed logarithms act on distinct tensor legs. Diagonalize them independently. The squared norm divided by ZtZ_t is a positive average of exponentials of their summed eigenvalues. Jensen gives its mean exponent, which is exactly the marginal expression in (11). This is the identity-reference case of the tensor convexity proof in [C]; it requires no ordering or mutual commutativity of the metrics.

Suppose now that ∥Xo∥op,∥X1∥op,∥X2∥op≤R\|X_o\|_{\mathrm{ op}},\|X_1\|_{\mathrm{ op}},\|X_2\|_{\mathrm{ op}}\le R. Equation (10), applied before bounding any matrix entries, gives log⁡∥Ho1/2Bt(H1−1/2⊗H2−1/2)∥HS≥12log⁡Zt+12Tr⁡(ρ0Xo)−12Tr⁡(PX1)−12Tr⁡(σaX2)−3Rηt.(12) \boxed{\begin{split} \log\|H_o^{1/2}B_t(H_1^{-1/2}\otimes H_2^{-1/2})\|_{\mathrm{ HS}} \ \ge\ &\frac12\log Z_t+\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_0X_o)\\ &-\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(PX_1) -\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_aX_2)\\ &-3R\eta_t . \end{split}} \qquad\text{(12)} Each of the three marginal replacements costs at most RηtR\eta_t. All comparison densities are specified from the actual local leaf.

The unchanged leaf itself has, again by scalar spectral Jensen, log⁡∥H11/2A∥≥log⁡α+12Tr⁡(PX1).(13) \log\|H_1^{1/2}A\| \ge\log\alpha+\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}(PX_1). \qquad\text{(13)} Adding (12) and (13) cancels this incidence’s metric term exactly. The known leaf normalization contributes log⁡α\log\alpha, while the parent contributes 12log⁡Zt=12log⁡D−12log⁡(1−ηt)\tfrac12\log Z_t=\tfrac12\log D-\tfrac12\log(1-\eta_t). No possibly exponential physical leaf norm has been deleted.

5. Polynomial accuracy in the actual color dimensions

In the actual source put s=N,D=Nbs=N,\ D=N^b. For any fixed integer J≥1J\ge1, choose t=sDNJ.(14) t=\sqrt{s}\,D\,N^J . \qquad\text{(14)} Then ηt≤N−2J,2ηt=O(N−2J),log⁡∥Gt∥op=(b+J+12)log⁡N.(15) \eta_t\le N^{-2J},\qquad 2\eta_t=O(N^{-2J}),\qquad \log\|G_t\|_{\mathrm{ op}}=(b+J+\tfrac12)\log N . \qquad\text{(15)} Thus (12) has loss O(RN−2J)O(RN^{-2J}), and O(N1−2J)O(N^{1-2J}) on any supplied window R=O(N)R=O(N). The theorem holds for every RR; it does not identify the anchored Newton window in [O] with a window on this larger matrix algebra. No new window theorem is asserted. A polynomial reference changes spectral bounds for positive metrics by only O(log⁡N)O(\log N) in their logarithmic radius, on the same compiler.

One may perform this construction simultaneously on any set of prescribed-leaf edges with at most one selected input per parent. Each affected parent is exactly its own BtB_t; no product output is gauged and the selected leaves remain fixed. For MM selections, the sum of actual defects on those incidences is at most 2Mηt2M\eta_t, and the reference costs O(Mlog⁡N)O(M\log N) for fixed b,Jb,J. The original output and root remain exact. Other edge defects are not controlled by this statement.

6. Scope and closest coverage

The states ρ0,σa\rho_0,\sigma_a are the complete left/right multiplication energy states of the known leaf. They are not an arbitrarily chosen largest mode or an unknown internal output. The construction works for any specified nonzero crossing or blocked word leaf; it makes no claim that every tangle contraction is nonzero.

It is a one-sided calibration. In fact ∥σa∥op≤1/D\|\sigma_a\|_{\mathrm{ op}}\le1/D, so every unit vector hh obeys ∥σa−|h⟩⟨h|∥1≥2(1−1/D).(16) \|\sigma_a-|h\rangle\langle h|\|_1\ge2(1-1/D). \qquad\text{(16)} Test the trace-zero difference against |h⟩⟨h||h\rangle\langle h|. Thus a second actual pure leaf is not automatically matched. Compatibility of these mixed states through the remaining tree and the original closing is still a separate problem. No Gaussian description of the whole all-color tuple, metric undoing, geometric volume rate or finite-exception result is inferred.

[C] already proves qualitative leaf elimination with singular limiting gauges and full tensor capacity. [O] already supplies full-matrix calibration families and a different anchored Newton leaf shear. [R] gives the actual positive main-color capacity with its original source. The addition here is the explicit finite matrix-product reference, exact defect, polynomial source-dimensional cost and the local logarithmic inequality (12). No general novelty for tensor Jensen or rank-one suppression is claimed.
