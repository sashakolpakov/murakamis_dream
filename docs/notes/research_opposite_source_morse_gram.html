Opposite cyclic sources and the canonical Gram of an original Morse cut

24 November 2026. Research proof. Earlier finals and manuscripts are unchanged.

A fixed original one-cut knot diagram admits a cyclic-source presentation through every odd-width Morse slice. Dual turns use opposite sources; the total cyclic power consequently stays constant when a pair is born or dies. In the canonical averaged Grams, normalized literal cups are isometries, normalized ordinary caps are contractions, and actual crossings transport between the differently labeled Grams without a metric-conversion factor. The whole cut matrix remains the original one.

These assertions retain the complete physical spaces. An ordinary cap is not declared the adjoint of its cup: their ordinary bubble is zero. The result does not lower-bound the knot scalar or its geometric part.

1. Fixed diagram, actual head, and signed sources

Fix N≥2N\ge2, and write d=N−1,q=eπi/N,Q=q2,x=−q−1,γ=xN=(−1)N+1.(1) d=N-1,\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad Q=q^2,\quad x=-q^{-1}, \quad\gamma=x^N=(-1)^{N+1}. \qquad\text{(1)} The physical basis w0,…,wdw_0,\ldots,w_d is orthonormal. At complete color use the regular E-semicyclic chart of [B]: Kwa=xQ−awa,Eμwa=(Q−a−1)wa−1(a>0),Eμw0=μwd,Fwa=(1−Qa+1)wa+1(a<d),Fwd=0.(2) \begin{split} K w_a&=xQ^{-a}w_a,\\ E_\mu w_a&=(Q^{-a}-1)w_{a-1}\quad(a>0),& E_\mu w_0&=\mu w_d,\\ Fw_a&=(1-Q^{a+1})w_{a+1}\quad(a<d),& Fw_d&=0 . \end{split} \qquad\text{(2)} Here EμE_\mu is [B]’s scaled generator ℰ\mathcal E. The coproducts are ΔE=E⊗K+I⊗E,ΔF=F⊗I+K−1⊗F,ΔK=K⊗K.(3) \Delta E=E\otimes K+I\otimes E,\quad \Delta F=F\otimes I+K^{-1}\otimes F,\quad \Delta K=K\otimes K. \qquad\text{(3)} The corresponding antipodes are S(E)=−EK−1S(E)=-EK^{-1}, S(F)=−KFS(F)=-KF, S(K)=K−1S(K)=K^{-1}.

Fix an original oriented 1→11\to1 Morse presentation 𝒟\mathscr D of a knot, using the standard actual turns of [P]. Keep its original signed crossing matrices, common Cartan scalars, pivotal convention, framing and assigned external normalization lines. All internal deaths below are ordinary caps. The distinguished original marking is applied only to the completed one-cut operator.

Choose |μ|=1/N|\mu|=1/N. On a slice with bb ordered heads set νi=γi−1μi,νi∈{μ,−μ},∑i=1bνi=μ.(4) \nu_i=\gamma^{i-1}\mu_i,\qquad \nu_i\in\{\mu,-\mu\}, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^b\nu_i=\mu . \qquad\text{(4)} The signs are the orientations of the sliced strands, with the surviving cut strand assigned +μ+\mu. Thus every width bb is odd. At a birth insert two opposite ν\nu’s; at a death remove them. At a crossing the two ν\nu labels exchange positions. This gives consistent labels along the fixed oriented diagram.

Indeed [B, (8)] at xN=γx^N=\gamma gives, for either crossing sign, (μi,μi+1)↦(γμi+1,γμi);(νi,νi+1)↦(νi+1,νi).(5) (\mu_i,\mu_{i+1})\longmapsto (\gamma\mu_{i+1},\gamma\mu_i); \qquad(\nu_i,\nu_{i+1})\longmapsto(\nu_{i+1},\nu_i). \qquad\text{(5)} Use the genuinely changing-source finite crossings of [B, (7)] and their inverses, rather than the unchanged nilpotent crossing matrix. Their coefficients contain the actual EμiE_{\mu_i}, and they intertwine the total generators between the stated source fibers. All sources have modulus 1/N1/N, at both parities.

3. One common cyclic normalization through every width

For a labeled odd slice let EΣ=∑i=1bEμiKi+1⋯Kb,KΣ=∏i=1bKi.(13) E_\Sigma=\sum_{i=1}^b E_{\mu_i}K_{i+1}\cdots K_b,\qquad K_\Sigma=\prod_{i=1}^b K_i . \qquad\text{(13)} The root product gives EμiN=γNμiIE_{\mu_i}^N=\gamma N\mu_i I. The summands in (13) QQ-commute, so their mixed NN-th multinomial coefficients vanish. Consequently EΣN=γbN∑iνiI=γNμI=:cI,|c|=1.(14) E_\Sigma^N =\gamma^bN\sum_i\nu_i I=\gamma N\mu I=:cI, \qquad |c|=1. \qquad\text{(14)} Choose once and for all eN=ce^N=c, and on every such slice define T𝝂=EΣ/e,T𝝂N=I.(15) T_{\boldsymbol\nu}=E_\Sigma/e,\qquad T_{\boldsymbol\nu}^N=I . \qquad\text{(15)} Births, deaths and both signed crossings intertwine these TT’s. For a turn inserted inside other heads this follows from (3): the pair acts on its cup by Kpair=1,Epair=0K_{\mathrm{ pair}}=1,E_{\mathrm{ pair}}=0, and the cap obeys the corresponding row equations. The earlier and later head terms therefore pass through unchanged. Crossing intertwining is the actual changing-source statement of [B].

No intermediate tail is required to have nonzero central power. If the first normalized label is +μ+\mu, its remaining even tail has EtailN=0E_{\mathrm{ tail}}^N=0. If it is −μ-\mu, that power is 2cI2cI. Crossings can exchange these situations. Formula (15) remains valid in either case; no inverse of the tail coproduct or fusion-wall specialization is used.

4. Source preservation for the completed original cut

Let 𝒜μ(𝒟)\mathcal A_\mu(\mathscr D) be this literal fixed-diagram operator, with all original external factors restored. Its entire one-head matrix satisfies 𝒜μ(𝒟)=𝒜0(𝒟)=KN(𝒟)I.(16) \mathcal A_\mu(\mathscr D) =\mathcal A_0(\mathscr D) =K_N(\mathscr D)\,I . \qquad\text{(16)} The first equality has a direct degree proof extending [B, Section 5]. Give waw_a integer degree aa, without reducing totals modulo NN. A birth (8) adds dd; an ordinary death removes dd. In a positive crossing term Eμ1kF2kE_{\mu_1}^kF_2^k, every ordinary lowering cancels one raising. Each cyclic EE-wrap instead changes the total degree by an additional +N+N. The diagonal Cartan and checked flip preserve the total.

The actual negative crossing uses the finite inverse Euler series in [B, Section 4], evaluated in its recovered source fiber. It also has only EkFkE^kF^k terms with 0≤k<N0\le k<N; FF has no wrap. Hence every source-dependent wrap in either sign contributes +N+N, never −N-N. Products of source labels may have signs, but do not alter this integer-degree accounting.

A 1→11\to1 presentation has equally many births and deaths. If a matrix-entry path has rr wraps, its output minus input degree is therefore rNrN. Both degrees lie in {0,…,d}\{0,\ldots,d\}, forcing r=0r=0. All surviving entries are exactly the original nilpotent entries. This proves the first equality in (16), without changing an intermediate cap or testing an arbitrary marked sector.

For the second equality, the completed original unmarked one-cut operator intertwines E0,KE_0,K. The KK-weights are distinct, so it is diagonal; every ordinary E0E_0-arrow is nonzero, so the diagonal entries agree. Its scalar, with the prescribed framing and external normalization, is the original selected value KNK_N. The original final marking is applied to this completed matrix.

This argument concerns a fixed Morse presentation. It does not infer a local crossing-through-cup identity from Hopf intertwining, define a new semicyclic ribbon invariant, or move a cap through an arbitrary exterior. No such move is needed for (16).

6. Scope and closest coverage

The construction supplies a single source-compatible positive-metric system for a fixed original cut through variable odd widths. It retains every physical tensor coordinate. No multiplicity or normal direction is removed by a quotient or an eigenvalue-only Casimir specialization, and no cap-tree projection is inserted in a neighboring crossing.

[R] already constructs oriented source duality and explains its degenerate fusion character. [S] already controls the original marked density in its complete fixed-width graph Gram. [A] already gives coisometric ordinary-cap tree packets after their specific factorial gauges. The additional statement here is the opposite-center E-source assignment through the fixed original Morse diagram, its full one-cut preservation, and the exact cross-width identities (18)–(20) in the canonical averaged Grams.

The norm of the completed matrix in (16) is of course |KN||K_N| in its one-head Gram. This is the existing scalarity identity, not a separately growing positive energy. The zero bubble in (12) explicitly prevents treating ordinary closure as a positive norm. No geometric boundary packet, nonzero marked overlap, lower bound for KNK_N, or finite-exception volume theorem follows from these facts. Nothing here supplies off-root or quantum-normal estimates.
