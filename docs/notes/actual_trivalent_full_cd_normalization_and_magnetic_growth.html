Full trivalent CD normalization and an actual magnetic-growth obstruction

29 October 2026. The complete-circle image on EVERY physical pair has an exact ordinary-channel normalization. This lifts a black scalar to its entire magnetic tensor, but does not preserve the scalar’s growth rate. An explicit coefficient of the already proved odd central tensor has rate 5j0/4, where its scalar has rate j0=v8/pi. Thus even the central scalar cannot be used as an upper bound for the full CD vertex norm. This is a source-exact obstruction, not a conjecture about a candidate Racah formula or a counterexample to a volume conjecture.

1. Original modules and the general positive single-object metric

Use the full physical conventions of the positive-circle quotient: N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d, F_a=(Q;Q)_a, s_a=|F_a|, and the complete physical module V_N. On V_N tensor V_N write E_t,F_t,K_t for the original ordered coproduct, Omega for the actual Casimir, P for physical leg exchange, and L=L_N. In particular

E_t^dagger P=-P F_t, F_t^dagger P=-P E_t,
H=L P>=0, C=P L>=0.                               (1)

Put M=floor(d/2). At folded node c_j=q(2j+1)+q(-2j-1), 0<=j<=M, set t_j=d-j and define the first-entry-one highest vector

h^(j)=sum_(a=0)^t_j q^(a^2)[t_j choose a]_Q |a,t_j-a>,
W_(j,r)=F_t^r h^(j)/F_r, 0<=r<=2j.               (2)

Its literal highest ratio is -x(-1)Qa(1-Q(t_j-a))/(1-Q(a+1)). Its highest K is q^(2j), and its Casimir is c_j. The established full paired-image theorem gives one image vector in each degree t_j,…,t_j+2j; their nonzero ladder coefficients show that (2) is an injective ACTUAL Hopf map W_j:Q_j->V_N tensor V_N, where Q_j is the ordinary (2j+1)-dimensional module. No root semisimplification is used. At odd j=M it is the simple Steinberg module V_N.

In this divided-F basis its matrices are

K_j|r>=q^(2j)Q^(-r)|r>,
F_j|r>=(1-Q^(r+1))|r+1>,
E_j|r>=q^(-2j-1)(1-Q^(2j+1-r))|r-1>.

Every interior coefficient is a unit at the root. Define

G_(j,r)=beta(2j,r)=s_(2j)/[s_r s_(2j-r)],
G_j=diag(G_(j,r)).                                 (3)

This is positive. Its ratio is sin((2j+1-r)pi/N)/sin(r pi/N), so DIRECTLY

E_j^dagger G_j=-G_j F_j, K_j^dagger G_j K_j=G_j.    (4)

It is a metric of one ordinary object. A tensor coproduct is not therefore unitary in its product metric: its adjoint reverses order.

2. Exact full-degree CD normalization

Let H_j be the ACTUAL c_j summand of L P, after L has acted with its first normal on a paired block. Its full column factor is

H_j=h0_j W_j G_j^(-1) W_j^dagger,
F_j[:,r]=sqrt(h0_j/G_(j,r)) W_(j,r),                (5)

where

h0_j=2 s_(d-j)^2/s_(d-2j)=2N beta(2j,j), 2j<d,
h0_(d/2)=N,                              2j=d.    (6)

The first equality in(6) is the exact canonical CD coefficient in the first degree t_j, whose eigenvector has first entry one. Root reflection s_a s_(d-a)=N gives its second form. The simple odd node has HALF the paired prefactor. It is not continued as two simple copies of a double node.

Here is a proof that the same constant fixes EVERY magnetic row. The original covariance and centrality of L imply

F_t H_j=-H_j E_t^dagger.

The range of H_j is W_j(Q_j). Since H_j preserves physical integer degree and W_(j,r) have distinct degrees, write H_j=sum_r a_r W_(j,r)W_(j,r)^dagger. The covariance gives a_(r+1)/a_r=G_(j,r)/G_(j,r+1). Its first coefficient is h0_j, so (5) follows. This uses actual physical arrows and the original positive circle, not a presumed unitary Clebsch frame.

The correctly typed channel map is

pi_j=G_j^(-1) W_j^dagger P.                       (7)

It is a Hopf map by(1),(4). On paired nodes pi_j W_j=0; on the odd simple node it is I. Indeed L W_j is zero or N W_j respectively, and (5) gives exactly these compositions. In particular the paired map is not a retraction. Equations(5)–(7) retain the normal source before passing to the ordinary image.

These factors need not be polynomial away from the center. Uniformly on physical indices, with Lcal(u)=integral_0^u log(2sin pi v)dv,

log beta(2j,r)
  =N[Lcal(2j/N)-Lcal(r/N)-Lcal((2j-r)/N)]+O(log N). (8)

For example j/N near1/4,r near j has strictly positive exponential rate j0/4. Separate bounds on the factors in(5) can lose the cancellations of a specific magnetic coefficient.

3. The whole black source, its true support and its 3j tensor

Let J_source=p_16 p_23 p_45 be the literal nested six-head cup, with p_(a,b)=N x^(-b)delta_(a+b,d). For any fixed triple j_1,j_2,j_3 put

U_bal=(pi_j1 tensor pi_j2 tensor pi_j3) J_source,
J=j_1+j_2+j_3.

It is an ACTUAL invariant of Q_j1 tensor Q_j2 tensor Q_j3. Its physical support gives the TRUE integer condition

r_1+r_2+r_3=J, 0<=r_i<=2j_i.                     (9)

It vanishes unless the ordinary triangle inequalities hold. The invariant space in this H=0 sector is at most one-dimensional. To see this, let v_r be its third magnetic slice. The first slice satisfies F_12 v_0=0 at degree J. The rectangular bidiagonal F_12 has a one-dimensional kernel exactly in the triangle range and is injective, or has empty domain, outside it. Every later slice is forced by

v_r=-K_j3(r-1)/E_j3(r-1,r) E_12 v_(r-1).          (10)

Its denominators are units. An explicit ordinary trivalent tensor Theta_j is obtained from that lowest-vector kernel and (10), with

Theta_j(2j_1,j_2+j_3-j_1,0)=1.                   (11)

For completeness its initial F-kernel ratios are

v_0(a+1,J-a-1)/v_0(a,J-a)
   =-K_j1(a+1)(1-Q^(a+1))/(1-Q^(J-a)).            (12)

All required indices lie in the physical ranges below N. These are also the regular finite ordinary Clebsch recursions before root specialization; their initial nonzero column and nonzero interior arrows give the stated canonical tensor. The top E endpoint is the same finite intertwiner identity, with no division by F_N.

There is a stronger original-source restriction:

U_bal=0 whenever J<d.                            (13)

This is the cleared missing-corner identity of the actual saturated three-circle source, applied to the genuine six-head cup. Its complete-circle observation is exactly ||(F_j1^dagger tensor F_j2^dagger tensor F_j3^dagger) P_ports J_source||^2. The source Ward identity annihilates it outside J>=d. Positivity then annihilates the black tensor itself. Odd simple axes are included by that proof. This is not inferred from an ordinary semisimple fusion rule. For J>=d the triangle inequalities already follow from j_i<=M, but(13) is not by itself a nonvanishing assertion.

Let the unweighted source coefficient at the magnetic point(11) be N^3 A_j, namely take rows W_j^dagger P before dividing by G_j. Then the ENTIRE tensor is exactly

U_bal=[N^3 A_j/G_(j2,j2+j3-j1)] Theta_j,
U_CD(r)=sqrt(h0_j1 h0_j2 h0_j3)
                     product_i sqrt(G_(ji,ri)) U_bal(r). (14)

This supplies the precise interface for any independently proved scalar formula. It does not assign that scalar’s rate to the whole tensor, nor bound the marked white packet by an ordinary covector.

4. An unconditional central magnetic-growth counterexample

Now take odd N=2m+1 and all three j_i=m. Then G_j=I,h0_j=N exactly. The already proved central actual source gives

U_CD=N^(7/2)q^(-m^2) A_N Theta,
Theta_(a,b,c)=N x^(-c) W_(d-c)(a,b),
A_N=q^[m(2-m)] sum_k beta(m,k)^4.                 (15)

Here Theta uses its physical normalization, not the normalized (11). Its exact lowest-column formula is W_d(m+k,d-k)=h_k, with |h_k|=beta(m,k). Therefore an ACTUAL magnetic coefficient is

U_CD(m+k,d-k,0)=N^(9/2)q^(-m^2) A_N h_k.          (16)

At k nearest m/2, the same affine-shift Laplace expansion already proved for the scalar gives

beta(m,k)=2^(-1/4)N^(-1/2)exp(Nj0/4)[1+O(1/N)],
|A_N|=(1/4)N^(-3/2)exp(Nj0)[1+O(1/N)].

Consequently, at odd levels with that chosen nearest magnetic index,

|U_CD(m+k,d-k,0)|
  =2^(-9/4)N^(5/2)exp[(5/4)Nj0][1+O(1/N)],       (17)
liminf_(N odd) N^(-1)log ||U_CD||_2^2 >=(5/2)j0.  (18)

This is the original positive three-circle black energy J_source^dagger C_St^tensor3 J_source, in a valid CD factor of the actual circles. Changing that factor by a unitary frame leaves the energy unchanged. It is not a freely selected abstract inner product.

Thus an upper bound exp[Nj0+o(N)] for the FULL vertex amplitude, or exp[2Nj0+o(N)] for its Euclidean CD energy, is FALSE. A metric subexponentially equivalent to that CD metric cannot repair it. A rescaling must retain its corresponding co-source dual cost.

5. Consequences for coherent graph sewing

The scalar C R partition, when identified with A_j by its exact source proof, can control a specific normalized magnetic coefficient. Equations(14)–(18) show why it does not by itself control the remaining magnetic sums. Ordinary white Hopf covectors, the sole marked row, genuine bends and their pivotal factors may contract that growth coherently. They must be sewn BEFORE a sharp energy estimate is asserted. Nothing here contradicts a knot-volume bound or rules out a different coherent positive metric on the complete pointed source. It rules out precisely the scalar-to-full-CD-norm shortcut, already at the actual odd central anchor.

The focused exact source audit passes eight all-parity physical levels N=2,…,9: every CD magnetic degree, both typed channel compositions, whole balanced black tensors against independently constructed ordinary 3j recursions, exact forbidden source blocks, and the central magnetic identity(16). Four separately labeled 85-digit growing-level checks calibrate(17). The covariance, support and growth proofs above establish the general claims; these checks are supplementary. No source-to-graph scalar bound is inferred from the finite range.
