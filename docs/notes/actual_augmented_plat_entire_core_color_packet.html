An exact entire Fourier color packet for every augmented plat core

30 October 2026. The original high-color core of ANY fixed augmented plat has an explicit finite, separately-even ENTIRE interpolation in its auxiliary deficit colors. Its frequencies are the centered actual complete-circle channels. It is regular at the odd simple endpoint and has exact tensor Fourier reconstruction and Parseval identities.

This supplies the finite function itself, not its required exponential asymptotic. The full original source also contains explicit alias subsets. Their coherent gap, the core’s selected complex saddle and its geometric period remain unproved for an arbitrary plat.

1. The full-algebra split, including normals

Use the original circle polynomials L_n=(-1)^(n-1)U_(n-1)(Omega/2), L_0=0, and P_N(Omega)=2T_N(Omega/2)+2=0. Here T is the standard first Chebyshev polynomial, T_0=1 and T_1(z/2)=z/2. For each integer 0<=h<N, the already proved exact identity is

L_(N-h)=(-1)^h T_h(Omega/2)L_N+(-1)^N L_h.             (1)

It follows from U_(N-h-1)=T_h U_(N-1)-T_N U_(h-1) and the FULL polynomial quotient T_N=-1. Thus (1) keeps the Casimir first normals and the odd simple endpoint. It is not a nodal-value identity inserted before the complete circle acts.

Let L_(N,j) denote the actual component of L_N at its distinct node c_j=2cos((2j+1)pi/N), j=0,…,M=floor((N-1)/2), in every physical degree. These are the rank-one physical matrices of the complete compiler, with their exact normal-derived coefficients. Multiplication by T_h acts on each such IMAGE by its value at c_j: the derivative term times the complete-circle square-zero normal vanishes. Consequently

(-1)^h T_h(Omega/2)L_N
      =sum_j cos(pi h m_j/N) L_(N,j),
m_j=N-2j-1.                                          (2)

The centering is exact because h is an integer. All m_j are nonnegative, have parity N-1, and are distinct. The odd self-node has m_M=0, so its coefficient in (2) equals one.

2. The actual whole-core function

Fix the original augmented 2k-plat skeleton, with b retained auxiliary regions and fixed signed coupons R_i^(epsilon_i)L_(N-h_i). Let A_N(j_1,…,j_b) be its literal marked contraction after replacing each coupon by R_i^(epsilon_i)L_(N,j_i). This includes its ORIGINAL cups, ordinary caps, unrestricted marked cap, pivots and external scalar. The exact source-degree argument is the one in the compiler. No favorable boundary vector or unknown source normalization enters the definition of A_N.

Define, for complex chi in C^b,

C_N(chi)=sum_(0<=j_i<=M) A_N(j)
                                product_i cos(pi m_(j_i) chi_i).
                                                           (3)

This is a finite separately-even entire function. On every physical high-color tuple h_i=N-n_i with 0<=h_i<=M, its value at chi=h/N is EXACTLY the original all-core source obtained from (1). In particular C_N(0) is the full original complete-color scalar: every alias L_0 vanishes there. Both parity classes are included literally.

The full physical high-color source has the exact expansion

Z_N(h)=C_N(h/N)
      +sum_(nonempty alias subsets S)(-1)^(N|S|) Z_(N,S)(h),
                                                           (4)

where the coupons in S are R_i^(epsilon_i)L_(h_i), and all others are the complete-image cosine factors of (2). Each term in (4) is explicit by the all-color Hermite compiler. Equation (4) is an identity, not an assertion that those alias terms are smaller. No alias is deleted by a change of norm.

For low ordinary colors n<=floor(N/2), use the usual physical split with core ZERO and the full ordinary coupon as alias. The mathematical function (3) still has values at the other grid points; these are its Fourier extension, not a new definition of physical low-color core.

3. Exact two-parity Fourier reconstruction

Put w_j=1/2 when m_j>0 and w_j=1 when m_j=0. Direct finite geometric summation gives

(1/N) sum_(h=0)^(N-1)
  cos(pi h m_j/N) cos(pi h m_l/N)=w_j delta_jl.         (5)

To check it, expand the product into exponentials. The differences m_j-m_l are even integers with absolute value below 2N. The sum m_j+m_l is also even; its only zero or 2N multiple is zero at the odd self-node j=l=M. This gives exactly the weights above. No prime-level assumption is made.

By tensoring (5), the original channel coefficients can be recovered from this finite function:

A_N(j)=1/[N^b product_i w_(j_i)]
   sum_(h_i=0)^(N-1) C_N(h/N)
                       product_i cos(pi h_i m_(j_i)/N). (6)

Its exact positive Parseval identity is

(1/N^b) sum_h |C_N(h/N)|^2
                        =sum_j product_i w_(j_i)|A_N(j)|^2.
                                                           (7)

The transform therefore has only a fixed polynomial cost in N at fixed b. All exponential behavior is still in the ACTUAL coherent coefficients A_N; (7) does not manufacture positivity of their sum C_N(0), nor a nonzero geometric coefficient.

The same function satisfies exactly

C_N(chi+e_i)=(-1)^(N-1)C_N(chi),
C_N(-chi_i,chi_others)=C_N(chi_i,chi_others).            (8)

It is periodic at odd levels and antiperiodic at even levels. In particular values near h=N mirror those near h=0. Thus this symmetry carries every proved near-zero value to the opposite end. IF a fixed-neighborhood analytic corridor asymptotic with complete rate F0 and nonzero amplitude GERM is established, choose arbitrarily small fixed positive real colors where that amplitude is nonzero. Their nearby grid values have rates arbitrarily close to F0 and reflect outside the corridor. This precludes a fixed whole-cube gap for the extension. If the amplitude is a UNIT at zero, h=1 already gives the complete rate and reflects to h=N-1. The unit assumption is needed for that particular h=1 argument with an absolute remainder.

Periodicity ALONE is not an obstruction. In one variable the exact reproducing kernel is

sum_j w_j^-1 cos(pi m_j chi)
  =sum_(r=0)^(N-1) exp[pi i(N-1-2r)chi]
  =sin(N pi chi)/sin(pi chi).                         (8a)

Its values at chi=h/N are N delta_(h,0), for 0<=h<N, at BOTH parities. Multiplying it by exp(N F0)/N exhibits a complete rate F0 and zero at every other physical sample. It has no fixed-scale analytic corridor of the required form. The distinction after (4) is essential when combining a corridor theorem with a physical color-complement bound.

An elementary entire growth bound is

|C_N(chi)|<=exp(pi(N-1) sum_i |Im chi_i|) sum_j |A_N(j)|.
                                                           (9)

It is an exact upper bound, much weaker than the quadratic complex corridor estimate required for long-filling stationary phase.

4. The remaining analytic issue is now concrete

Unlike a direct nonintegral-color substitution into sin(n theta)/sin theta at theta=pi, (3) is regular at the odd self-node and specifies every finite-N continuation exactly. Its extension is the unique one in the stated finite cosine span; no generic-q germ of the whole pointed invariant is replaced.

A general filling theorem would still require a uniform asymptotic of (3) on a FIXED complex neighborhood, a source-preserving selected saddle/period, the entire physical alias and color-complement bounds, and the actual marked hyperbolic regulator. The exact complete and mixed compilers expose those quantities at arbitrary fixed bridge width, but do not prove their exponential estimates. The relevant large twisting threshold T(D) concerns a fixed diagram D; the Fourier frequency scale N here remains the quantum level.
