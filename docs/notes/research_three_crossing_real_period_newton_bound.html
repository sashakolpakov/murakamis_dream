Newton evaluation on the real boundary period

Research proof, 3 November 2026.

The Newton evaluation vector has its largest exponential growth at the complete boundary value. This comparison holds for every original color, including the upper half of the color range and both level parities. It gives an upper bound for the adjacent three-crossing source on the far part of its real boundary period. It also supplies a smaller color cutoff for the remaining intermediate angles.

These are upper bounds. Their use does not require a lower bound for the signed source, a contributing saddle, or positivity of its coefficients.

7. The local phase can force cancellation in the Newton coefficients

The sufficient conditions in Section 6 require information about the actual phases. They are not automatic consequences of the geometric saddle. In fact, the phase of the adjacent three-crossing source prevents those conditions from holding in its original Newton expansion.

The obstruction has an elementary formulation in the ordinary boundary coordinate ww, rather than the angular coordinate uu. Keep fN(w)=∑j=0N−1vN,jej(w)f_N(w)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}v_{N,j}e_j(w) and the positive lower-half sum LNL_N from (29).

8. Scope for the adjacent three-crossing source

The local boundary theorem now proves its nonzero geometric contribution. The whole-period theorem combines the estimates here with the separate current certificates for the two retained domains. It proves the whole real Gaussian bound and the thin complex strip needed by the existing subsequent-turn theorem.

These conclusions concern the specified adjacent pair and its conjugate. They do not establish the corresponding source estimates for arbitrary connected short regions, which remain part of the finite-exception objective.
