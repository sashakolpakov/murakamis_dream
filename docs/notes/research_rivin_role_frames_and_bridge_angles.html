Rivin crossing frames, exact seams, and fused bridge angle volumes

Written derivation, 14 November 2026. The external matrices are those of rivin-jones at e956fb919ac7d1bbc7cb3d3873fbac993feb94b7, docs/MATHEMATICS.md and python/rivin_jones/r_matrix.py. Only their displayed formulas were read; no code was executed or imported.

Each literal signed crossing has a direct two-cell positive angle half-density after an explicit change of its four boundary frames. The frames depend on whether the strand passes over or under. Their seams are part of the exact arbitrary-word identity. The existing coherent overbridge contraction then gives a useful fused form: its complete factorial magnitude is one angle-simplex weight, including the clock factor that would otherwise be mistaken for a harmless phase. On an explicit positive chamber its action is the volume of an ideal bipyramid. None of these statements removes the actual phase or proves survival of the original geometric scalar.

3. The exact seams and the complete source

On an internal strand segment of color aa, let ϵ\epsilon be its role at the preceding output and ϵ′\epsilon' its role at the next input. The required diagonal connector is Hϵ′,ϵ(a)=gϵ′(a)gϵ(a)−1=(SaN)(ϵ′−ϵ)/2.(7) H_{\epsilon',\epsilon}(a) =g_{\epsilon'}(a)g_\epsilon(a)^{-1} =\left(\frac{S_a}{\sqrt N}\right)^{(\epsilon'-\epsilon)/2}. \qquad\text{(7)} It is one for equal roles and is Sa/NS_a/\sqrt N or its reciprocal for a role switch. These are generally exponential weights.

For example, for consecutive complete operators, Rm⋯R1=Gout,m−1R̂m(Gin,mGout,m−1−1)R̂m−1⋯R̂1Gin,1.(8) R_m\cdots R_1 =G_{{\mathrm{ out}},m}^{-1}\widehat R_m (G_{{\mathrm{ in}},m}G_{{\mathrm{ out}},m-1}^{-1})\widehat R_{m-1} \cdots\widehat R_1G_{{\mathrm{ in}},1}. \qquad\text{(8)} Spectator frames are simply transported along their unchanged wires. Equation (8) is an equality of full matrices and therefore holds in an arbitrary exterior. Every original source vector is multiplied by its input frame and every original output row by its inverse output frame. An ordinary turn is transported by that same tensor rule. In particular gϵ(a)gϵ(d−a)=1g_\epsilon(a)g_\epsilon(d-a)=1; two equally framed complementary legs fix their ordinary cup or cap pointwise. Unequal frames have their explicit remaining factor and are not declared source-fixing.

For the external closed-braid prescription the quantum trace is diagonal, so its weight commutes with (7); the closing connector stays in the traced matrix element. The original highest-weight pin remains the index zero. Its possible endpoint-frame ratio is a power of NN, because gϵ(0)=N−ϵ/4g_\epsilon(0)=N^{-\epsilon/4}. The writhe correction is unchanged. Thus (8), including closing seams, computes the prescribed original scalar for every fixed signed braid word. The full boundary adapter and split-incidence theorem transport the same equality to the original tangle source; this does not replace a full pair module by its semisimple spectrum or discard its Hermite normals.

The seam action is also explicit. With x=a/Nx=a/N, uniformly in aa, 2πNlog⁡Hϵ′,ϵ(a)=−(ϵ′−ϵ)Λ(πx)+O(log⁡N/N).(9) \frac{2\pi}{N}\log H_{\epsilon',\epsilon}(a) =-(\epsilon'-\epsilon)\Lambda(\pi x)+O(\log N/N). \qquad\text{(9)} Consequently the whole physical-state expansion has the sum of the two-cell actions (6), plus all terms (9) and the transported source action. The phase is exactly the sum of (3), the original pivot phases and writhe phase. Positive frame changes have not altered it. Strict concavity of an isolated two-cell expression alone says nothing about the Hessian after the seam terms are restored.

4. Coherent bridge fusion absorbs the factorial bookkeeping

There is a source-exact way to combine these weights, rather than discard the seams. First undo the displayed port frames algebraically in (8), then apply the finite Fourier transformations and exact signed overbridge contraction of the bridge theorem and the whole charged-source theorem. These are equalities of the original source. Their Fourier clocks are new summation coordinates; no termwise identification with the physical colors of (2) is asserted.

5. Genuine local geometry and its precise limit

The function (14) is strictly concave on its open simplex. An explicit tetrahedral decomposition proves this without an assumed global angle incidence. For n=2rn=2r, sj=x1+⋯+xjs_j=x_1+\cdots+x_j, ∑a=1nΛ(πxa)=∑j=2n−1V(πsj−1,πxj,π(1−sj)).(15) \sum_{a=1}^n\Lambda(\pi x_a) =\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}V(\pi s_{j-1},\pi x_j,\pi(1-s_j)). \qquad\text{(15)} The equality telescopes using Λ(π−y)=−Λ(y)\Lambda(\pi-y)=-\Lambda(y). Every triple is positive. The map of simplex tangent spaces into all these triangle tangent spaces is injective: the first triangle fixes the variations of x1,x2x_1,x_2, then each next triangle fixes the next variation. Strict concavity of tetrahedral volume proves the assertion.

On the smaller chamber xa<1/2x_a<1/2 for every aa, (14) is literally the volume of a convex ideal cyclic nn-gonal bipyramid. In the sphere at infinity put its two apices at 0,∞0,\infty, and its equatorial vertices successively on the unit circle with angle gaps 2πxa2\pi x_a. Every gap is less than π\pi, so the center belongs to the interior of the equatorial polygon. In the Klein ball the convex hull is the two pyramids on this polygon; the central axis and its successive equatorial edges subdivide it into nonoverlapping ideal tetrahedra. The tetrahedron at gap 2πxa2\pi x_a has dihedral angle triple (2πxa,π/2−πxa,π/2−πxa). (2\pi x_a,\ \pi/2-\pi x_a,\ \pi/2-\pi x_a). Its volume is 2Λ(πxa)2\Lambda(\pi x_a), by the Lobachevsky duplication identity, which also follows by differentiating both sides and fixing the value at zero. Summing proves (14)’s geometric assertion. Outside that chamber, (14)–(15) remain strictly concave positive-tetrahedron identities, but the central-axis bipyramid subdivision just described is not a positive subdivision and is not claimed as one.

The actual anchor equations linearly couple the halfedge charges of different bridges. They have not here been identified with the edge and peripheral angle equations of the desired hyperbolic complement. Nor does a maximum of (14) remove the phase (13), select a complex critical point, or prove its nonzero coefficient under the prescribed closing. What is established is a direct literal-crossing angle half-density, its exact arbitrary-source seams, and their source-exact coherent fusion into an explicit geometric local volume functional. The earlier reciprocal raw-Psi chart obstruction does not apply to these coefficient identities; no replacement of that off-grid density or its relative cycle has been made.
