The actual raw Mellin limit sees the source sector before the residue

Independent actual-state diagnostic, 27 October 2026. The exact cyclotomic Mellin formula shows where an action-normalized geometric moment cannot be read off. Under the already used domination of the ordinary-color tail, the whole selected root function becomes constant after division by its Kashaev residue, on an open X-region. Its leading shift sector there is the complete/source branch. The geometric exponential can still occur in that constant residue.

For the figure-eight, the required domination follows from the already independently proved selected positive-sum asymptotic. Thus this is an actual prototype conclusion, not a competing state.

1. Exact root functions and the open-region theorem

Use the actual cyclotomic Mellin fiber: ZN(X)=AN(X)+KNXN1−XN−pN(X),AN(X)=∑r=1N−1Cr(QN)Xr, Z_N(X)=\frac{A_N(X)+K_NX^N}{1-X^N}-p_N(X),\qquad A_N(X)=\sum_{r=1}^{N-1}C_r(Q_N)X^r, where pNp_N is the fixed starting-color prefix and |Cr(QN)|≤DN|C_r(Q_N)|\le D_N. Its degree and coefficient bounds are polynomial in NN; its length is fixed for pNp_N. Suppose along the sequence in question that NDN/|KN|→0. ND_N/|K_N|\longrightarrow0. \tag{1} Then locally uniformly on {|X|>1}\{|X|>1\}, ZN(X)KN→−1,ZN(QNX)ZN(X)→1. \frac{Z_N(X)}{K_N}\longrightarrow-1,\qquad \frac{Z_N(Q_NX)}{Z_N(X)}\longrightarrow1. \tag{2}

The Q-rotated compact set remains in the same exterior region. Apply the same uniform bound to get the second limit; its denominator stays nonzero for large NN.

Cauchy’s formula gives every fixed X-derivative of the first limit. In particular DXjZN(X)/KN→0,j≥1. D_X^jZ_N(X)/K_N\longrightarrow0,\qquad j\ge1. The errors are exponentially small from the complete term and polynomially bounded ordinary tails divided by KNK_N. If KNK_N has a positive exponential lower bound, multiplying these errors by any fixed power of NN still sends them to zero.

Thus every fixed-order X-differential moment with only a bounded Laurent pole in Q−1Q-1 has the same leading source-sector collapse. It does not expose the geometric residue action. This is consistent with the actual homogeneous Mellin operator: its canonical source quotient supplies the S=1S=1 branch.

3. Consequence for the next source moment

The residue identity remains exactly KN=Res⁡X=∞ZN(X)dX/X. K_N=\operatorname{Res}_{X=\infty}Z_N(X)\,dX/X . The coefficient can have positive geometric exponential growth while the raw normalized function in (2) is constant and its root shift ratio is one. A geometric critical algebra cannot therefore be inferred from a nontrivial leading fixed-X branch of this raw selected function.

This matches the exact centralizer obstruction: a whole commuting endomorphism preserving the actual starting lattice acts on the selected generic Mellin section by a scalar in k(Q)k(Q). Its observable quotient is shift-invariant, hence constant in X; affine source uniqueness then fixes its action. At roots, monodromy moments of the selected section are exactly one and have rank-one Hankel matrix.

The productive next moment must be defined after the residue, action normalization, or an actual relative quantum-normal source construction. The field/trace nonvanishing theorem can then be tested on that new object. Neither the whole compact source trace nor its Gauss-wall row has yet supplied that map.

This diagnostic does not rule out such a relative construction. It fixes the order of operations and prevents interpreting the already scalar closed cut as a decomposition of geometric phases.

4. Independent check of the formal critical-observable adapter

The construction in the actual commutant/critical-observable note passes independently. Normalized actual Gaussian insertion moments form a formal matrix M with a nonzero Vandermonde leading determinant. Thus M−1diag⁡(σθ)MM^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(\sigma\theta)M is defined, Galois-descended, and satisfies the exact critical polynomial. Its differential and difference covariance rules follow by direct conjugation; they are not commuting endomorphism rules on the raw pointed Mellin lattice.

The observable is formally corrected. In general its action on the unit source is not the raw first-insertion basis vector: that raw moment already has higher Gaussian corrections. Its powers therefore require the corresponding formal combinations of the actual insertion columns. The construction gives no final selected co-source on this corrected module. That is precisely the remaining moment transport, rather than an additional monodromy eigenvalue of the raw selected state.
