An actual coherent chart for each capped Euler coproduct factor

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The finite operator EN(λEiK>i)E_N(\lambda E_iK_{>i}) has a regular, nonzero coherent generating kernel for small complex λ\lambda on every fixed compact set away from incidence. Its exact recurrence retains the tail Cartan shifts and their finite coherent caps. A coefficient majorant controls low activity degrees; the actual matrix norm controls every remaining degree. Together these prove a relative O(N−1)O(N^{-1}) asymptotic, including the Cartan half-step and the N−1N-1 endpoint correction.

This is a theorem about one actual coproduct factor. Composition into a full source still requires its original resolutions, interfaces, normals, and cycle. No such source contribution is inferred here.

The complete directs A and C are pinned below. A supplies the actual one-head convention; C supplies the root coproduct and its original- metric estimate. The coupled recurrence and analytic proof are below.
