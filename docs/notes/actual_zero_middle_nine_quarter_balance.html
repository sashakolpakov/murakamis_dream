An elementary balance certificate for one zero middle

source-normalized lemma, 31 October 2026. This is a local analytic test on actual complete curvatures. It does not by itself construct a family of diagrams or assert that the test holds for one.

Let a port have middle curvatures 00 and e>0e>0, outer curvatures x,y≥0x,y\geq0, and A=x+y>0A=x+y>0. Retain the original complete triangle potential QQ from the calibrated source. If

eA≤94, \frac eA\leq\frac94,\tag{1}

then its strict complete-radius balance gap satisfies

Qe(0)+Q0(0)−log⁡e>12500. Q_e(0)+Q_0(0)-\log e>\frac1{2500}.\tag{2}

Here Qe(0)=Q(x,e,y)Q_e(0)=Q(x,e,y) and Q0(0)=Q(x,0,y)Q_0(0)=Q(x,0,y). Either outer curvature may vanish. If e=0e=0 as well, both one-sided sectors vanish exactly and no positive-middle balance inequality is required.

1. The worst outer split has one zero outer curvature

For fixed AA and P=xyP=xy, the zero-spectral lower-tail Perron factor is

ΛP(t)=2sin⁡(πt)[Asin(πt)+A2sin⁡2(πt)+4Pcos⁡2(πt)]. \Lambda_P(t)=2\sin(\pi t) \left[A\sin(\pi t)+\sqrt{A^2\sin^2(\pi t)+4P\cos^2(\pi t)}\right].

It increases with PP for every t∈(0,1/2)t\in(0,1/2). The original weighted start action is

Fb(μ;P)=μlog⁡b+∫0μlog⁡[4cos⁡2(πt)]dt+∫μ1/2log⁡ΛP(t)dt. F_b(\mu;P)=\mu\log b+\int_0^\mu\log[4\cos^2(\pi t)]\,dt +\int_\mu^{1/2}\log\Lambda_P(t)\,dt.

Its maximum over μ\mu is exactly Q(x,b,y)Q(x,b,y); for b=0b=0 the sole start is μ=0\mu=0. Each QQ is therefore nondecreasing in PP at fixed A,bA,b, by taking the maximum after the pointwise comparison of these actual actions. This is a local comparison, not a shared minimax exchange. Consequently

Q(x,e,y)+Q(x,0,y)≥Q(A,e,0)+Q(A,0,0). Q(x,e,y)+Q(x,0,y)\geq Q(A,e,0)+Q(A,0,0).

The exact half-degree homogeneity and monomial normalization give

Q(A,e,0)+Q(A,0,0)−log⁡e=F(r)−log⁡r,r=e/A,F(r)=Q(1,r,0),Q(A,0,0)=12log⁡A. \begin{aligned} Q(A,e,0)+Q(A,0,0)-\log e&=F(r)-\log r,\\ r&=e/A,\qquad F(r)=Q(1,r,0),\qquad Q(A,0,0)=\tfrac12\log A. \end{aligned}\tag{3}

No additive source constant has been removed.

2. Exact one-variable formula and monotonicity

At outer weights 1,01,0, the actual saddle is

μ(r)=1πarctan⁡r,dFdlog⁡r=μ(r),F(0)=Q(1,0,0)=0. \mu(r)=\frac1\pi\arctan\sqrt r,\qquad \frac{dF}{d\log r}=\mu(r),\qquad F(0)=Q(1,0,0)=0.

Define T(z)=∫0zarctan⁡(t)dt/tT(z)=\int_0^z\arctan(t)\,dt/t. Then

F(r)=2πT(r). F(r)=\frac2\pi T(\sqrt r).\tag{4}

The derivative of F(r)−log⁡rF(r)-\log r with respect to log⁡r\log r is μ(r)−1<0\mu(r)-1<0, so it suffices to bound its value at r=9/4r=9/4. Direct differentiation using arctan⁡z+arctan⁡(1/z)=π/2\arctan z+\arctan(1/z)=\pi/2 for z>0z>0 proves

T(z)−T(1/z)=π2log⁡z. T(z)-T(1/z)=\frac\pi2\log z.

At z=3/2z=3/2, (4) therefore yields

F(9/4)−log⁡(9/4)=2πT(2/3)−log⁡(3/2). F(9/4)-\log(9/4)=\frac2\pi T(2/3)-\log(3/2).\tag{5}

3. A rational lower bound without numerical quadrature

Integrating the alternating power series of arctan\arctan gives

T(t)=∑j≥0(−1)jt2j+1(2j+1)2,0<t<1. T(t)=\sum_{j\geq0}\frac{(-1)^jt^{2j+1}}{(2j+1)^2},\qquad0<t<1.

Equations (1), (3), (5), and (8) prove (2), including the exact one-zero-outer limit. The estimate does not depend on normalization scale, because the balance gap is scale invariant.

4. Interface with an actual symmetric plat construction

Suppose an actual certified source has only the following port types in its one complete marked packing:

  • Equal middle curvatures b=cb=c, with A>0A>0.
  • One zero middle and the other ee satisfying e/(x+y)≤9/4e/(x+y)\leq9/4.

Every such port is balanced. For equal positive middles, this follows from Qb(0)>12log⁡bQ_b(0)>\tfrac12\log b; if both are zero, the one-sided sectors vanish. For the second type, it follows from (2). The conditional full-invariant balance theorem therefore applies to any literal source for which these actual geometric statements have been proved.

The reduced-layer family theorem establishes the required packing, reflection, original mark and uniform ratio bound for its literal words at every k≥4k\geq4. Those are separate geometric conclusions, not consequences of the local inequality alone.

Validation and scope

The bound follows from scalar comparison, monotonicity, exact rational inequalities and protected-zero limits. Numerical quadrature or a sample of diagrams cannot establish its uniformity.
