Whole-meridian transport of the capped Gauss row through a physical crossing

Private written proof, 23 November 2026.

The actual terminal Gauss row times one positive physical crossing is exactly a reflected copy of the whole meridian row with an explicit clock multiplier. This identity retains every coordinate and normal tail. It gives a scalar-relative, nonzero transport theorem for the original capped initializer at M=exp⁡(v/N)M=\exp(v/\sqrt N), without applying a large crossing matrix to an asymptotic approximation.

The result concerns one physical generator. No closure under arbitrary signed words, original turns, or an exterior marked closing is asserted.

The advance is an exact whole-family physical crossing step and its scalar-relative capped estimate. An inverse crossing from the resulting family can of course undo the same exact step; this does not assert closure for arbitrary successive signs or native turns. Such compositions must still transport the full graded kernel, the complement, and the actual exterior covector. No filled-knot or arbitrary-short-component lower bound follows here.
