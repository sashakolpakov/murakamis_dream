Whole original mixed-source volume in the untwisted corridor

30 October 2026. Independently reviewed and frozen. The whole original mixed source has the actual geometric volume limit throughout a fixed near-complete corridor at its untwisted external circle ports. This follows from the frozen source gap, complex core and marked geometric assembly; no additional numerical theorem is needed.

Fix a certified connected augmented flat plat D and a set E of its UNTWISTED external circle ports. The other ports stay at external color N and may keep their original signed half twists. Use the original cups, ordinary caps, unrestricted mark and scalar normalization throughout. Write A_N(N-h_E,N_else) for this actual mixed source and F_N^E(h) for its exact empty-subset cosine core, with deficits zero off E.

The block-representation/corridor theorem, section5, supplies delta0,gamma>0 and K such that, for all large N of either parity and every integer 0<=h_i<=delta0*N,

|A_N(N-h_E,N_else)-F_N^E(h)| <=N^K exp[N(Phi_D-gamma)], Phi_D=Vol(Y_D)/(2pi).

The global complex-core theorem, sections1 and6, supplies a zero-free relative unit asymptotic on one fixed complex neighborhood of completeness, with exact finite-N phase S_N tending uniformly to the selected S. In particular uniformly on a smaller real neighborhood,

(1/N)log|F_N^E(N chi)|=Re S(chi)+o(1), S(0)=Phi_D.

Choose 0<delta<=delta0 so the real cube [0,delta]^E lies in that neighborhood and Re S(chi)>=Phi_D-gamma/2 there. Its uniform lower bound gives, after decreasing a constant c>0 and increasing N0,

A_N(N-h_E,N_else)/F_N^E(h)=1+O(exp(-cN)) (1)

uniformly on the entire PHYSICAL integer corridor 0<=h_i<=delta*N. Polynomial factors and the uniform o(N) in the core lower bound are absorbed by, for example, any c<gamma/4 after increasing N0. No equality of off-grid continuations or of genuine-q normal families is used. The ratio is legitimate because the actual core is zero-free there for all sufficiently large N, of both parities.

By the marked geometric assembly, sections2–4, the selected representation rho_chi has main meridians complete and crossing-circle logs U_i=-2piichi_i. For real chi, Re U_i=0, so its regulator boundary correction vanishes. Thus for ANY integer sequence h_N with 0<=h_N<=delta*N and h_N/N tending to chi,

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N)log|A_N(N-h_(N,E),N_else)| =Vol(rho_chi). (2)

This is the WHOLE original mixed source, not merely its cosine core. The class and fixed width are arbitrary within the actual labeled certificate, and constants may depend on D. The representation is the actual near-complete marked geometric family; no cone-manifold completion, distant-color estimate, signed varied-port corridor, filled-knot invariant equality or uniformity over growing diagrams is asserted. Equations (1)–(2) use only already frozen uniform theorems and need no new numerical audit.

Root full analytical review of the corollary and its quantifiers: PASS. The finite-N phase remains in the relative core asymptotic; only its uniform limiting action is used for the exponential comparison and the volume limit. No off-grid or genuine-q identity is inferred. Independent coherent full analytical review: PASS, including uniform core nonvanishing, both level parities, physical integer deficits, untwisted varied ports and the complete signed complement.
