Full volume from finite covers of the actual source sectors

31 October 2026. A sufficient theorem for a fixed marked plat. Universal applicability to every eligible plat remains open.

1. The theorem and its geometric scope

Fix an original certified simple-white augmented plat DD, with RR ports, its prescribed MAIN mark, and a fixed choice of half twists giving one main knot component. Retain the original two protected face variables at zero and every ordinary coefficient target d=N−1d=N-1. Let

ΦD=Vol⁡(YD)2π \Phi_D=\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(Y_D)}{2\pi}

be its complete augmented volume rate. The certification and geometric normalization are those of the complete source theorem and the whole-CROSS filling theorem.

At each port use the actual local labels CROSS, LEFT and RIGHT. A one-sided label whose overloaded middle is protected has zero source and is omitted. A branch BB assigns a nonempty subset SB,eS_{B,e} of the remaining labels to each port. Thus it denotes a Cartesian set of actual label strings.

For positive shared ordinary radii LL, put

Xe(L)=Q(xe,be,ye)+Q(xe,ce,ye),Ae,L(L)=max⁡{log⁡ce,Q(xe,be,ye)+12log⁡ce},Ae,R(L)=max⁡{log⁡be,Q(xe,ce,ye)+12log⁡be},GB(L)=∑emaxs∈SB,eAe,s(L)−∑f ordinarylog⁡Lf,Ae,C=Xe. \begin{aligned} X_e(L)&=Q(x_e,b_e,y_e)+Q(x_e,c_e,y_e),\\ A_{e,L}(L)&=\max\{\log c_e,\ Q(x_e,b_e,y_e)+\tfrac12\log c_e\},\\ A_{e,R}(L)&=\max\{\log b_e,\ Q(x_e,c_e,y_e)+\tfrac12\log b_e\},\\ G_B(L)&=\sum_e\max_{s\in S_{B,e}} A_{e,s}(L) -\sum_{f\text{ ordinary}}\log L_f, \qquad A_{e,C}=X_e. \end{aligned}\tag{1}

Here xe,be,ce,yex_e,b_e,c_e,y_e are the original face coordinates, so a face appearing at several ports has one common radius. The function QQ is the original half-degree triangle potential, with its absolute normalization.

Theorem. Suppose a finite collection of branches covers every allowed non-CROSS string, and each branch has a positive shared vector LBL^B with

GB(LB)<ΦD. G_B(L^B)<\Phi_D.\tag{2}

Then there is a diagram-dependent TT such that, for every independently chosen fixed signed full-twist vector satisfying |pe|>T|p_e|>T, the original invariant obeys

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(KD(𝒑))|=Vol⁡(S3\KD(𝒑)). \boxed{\displaystyle \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log\left|K_N\bigl(K_D(\mathbf p)\bigr)\right| =\operatorname{Vol}\bigl(S^3\setminus K_D(\mathbf p)\bigr).} \tag{3}

The limit includes both level parities. The twist vector and diagram are fixed before NN tends to infinity. The right side is the smooth filled hyperbolic volume, rather than the complete augmented volume 2πΦD2\pi\Phi_D. All original framing and peripheral factors remain in the invariant.

2. Why a cover suffices

The global weighted local estimates used in the anchored theorem hold at every real spectral value and every face phase. Their rates are precisely the three functions in (1), with the recorded odd central convention. They are weighted value bounds; no coefficientwise one-sided majorant is asserted.

Assign each non-CROSS string to one branch containing it. For a string in branch BB, perform the ordinary coefficient estimate on the single shared torus with radii LBL^B. The local rate at port ee is at most the maximum in (1). Subtracting the original face targets gives GB(LB)+o(1)G_B(L^B)+o(1). The replacement of (N−1)/N(N-1)/N by one costs o(1)o(1) because the witnesses are fixed.

There are finitely many strings and witnesses for fixed DD. Hence (2) gives a common strict exponential gap on the whole real spectral cube. Overlapping branches cause no multiplicity problem: each string is assigned once, and even a finite overcount would not change its exponential rate. The same conclusion holds at both level parities.

The remainder has spectral degree at most N−1N-1 in each coordinate. The cosine Bernstein estimate extends its gap to a fixed sufficiently thin complex spectral strip. Apply the existing source Cauchy estimate and three integrations by parts in each Fourier coordinate. These retain the original endpoint identities and second-derivative boundary currents and give a summable product of inverse-square charge weights on entire original domains. Thus the whole non-CROSS filling period remains strictly below ΦD\Phi_D in exponential rate.

The whole-CROSS theorem gives a nonzero full period of rate ΦD−O(∑epe−2)\Phi_D-O(\sum_e p_e^{-2}), equal to the actual filled volume divided by 2π2\pi. Taking every fixed |pe||p_e| sufficiently large makes this rate larger than the remainder bound. The full invariant has the same rate, proving (3). This uses the entire charge sum and the existing full-domain transfer, not a sharply cut infinite Fourier sum.

3. Relation to the simpler criteria

The triangle inequality Q(x,t,y)≥12log⁡tQ(x,t,y)\geq\tfrac12\log t gives the useful identities

max⁡(AC,AL,AR)=max⁡(X,log⁡b,log⁡c),max⁡(AC,AL)=max⁡(X,log⁡c),max⁡(AC,AR)=max⁡(X,log⁡b). \begin{aligned} \max(A_C,A_L,A_R)&=\max(X,\log b,\log c),\\ \max(A_C,A_L)&=\max(X,\log c),\\ \max(A_C,A_R)&=\max(X,\log b). \end{aligned}\tag{4}

Terms with protected middle coordinates are omitted. For example, Qb+12log⁡c≤Qb+Qc=XQ_b+\frac12\log c\leq Q_b+Q_c=X, which proves the second identity. These identities simplify a union of actual labels. They do not turn the comparison atoms inside ALA_L or ARA_R into independent source polynomials.

The anchored criterion uses one LEFT or RIGHT anchor and all labels at every other port. It has at most 2R2R branches. Splitting those branches into smaller actual label sets can lower their costs; each new branch may use its own shared face vector. The certificate separation theorem proves this is a strict improvement for one fixed original marked source: an anchored certificate is impossible there, but forty finer branches pass.

The strongest finite version uses one branch for every individual non-CROSS string σ\sigma. Its sufficient condition is

infL>0[∑eAe,σe(L)−∑flogLf]<ΦDfor every σ≠CR. \inf_{L>0}\left[\sum_e A_{e,\sigma_e}(L) -\sum_f\log L_f\right]<\Phi_D \qquad\text{for every }\sigma\ne C^R.\tag{5}

If the infimum is strictly smaller, some finite positive witness exists; attainment is unnecessary. Since there are finitely many strings, (5) would establish (3) for that diagram. Whether (5) holds for every eligible original plat is the remaining universal question. Acyclicity of overloaded middle edges and the existing endpoint volume theorem do not by themselves prove it: the one-sided comparison allows fractional perspective flows.

4. Checkable input and scope

The theorem admits finite certificates: the original source incidence and mark, a combinatorial branch cover, one positive rational vector per branch, and strict interval bounds compared with a feasible complete triangle flow. Numerical optimization can locate the witnesses, but is not needed to verify them. The portable forty-branch verifier implements this distinction.

This criterion includes the balanced all-width families and the independently deep bridge-gap extensions. It does not assert that every knot of bridge number kk, or every presentation with long twist regions, has the required original geometric certificate or passes (5). Those are coverage questions to prove, not assumptions hidden in (3).
