Original three-channel corner and the positive factorial identity

29 October 2026. The original coproduct fixes a general trivalent corner, including its endpoint phases, at both level parities. The positive factorial evaluation is now proved by the short finite transformation and the independent Fourier/Newton transformation. This historical filename retains the original formulation and exact source normalization. The general theorem and regular-cube corollary record the completed scope. No graph-volume equality or full vertex norm is inferred from this scalar theorem.

1. General physical columns and endpoint phases

Put d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, x=qd=-q-1, F_r=(Q;Q)_r and s_r=|F_r|. For 0<=j<=floor(d/2), let h=d-j and

H^(j)_a=q^(a^2)[h choose a]_Q at |a,h-a>,
W^(j)_r=F_pair^r H^(j)/F_r, 0<=r<=2j.             (1)

These are the original balanced physical pair and its original ordered coproduct. The highest equation follows directly from

H_(a+1)/H_a=-x^-1 Q^a(1-Q^(h-a))/(1-Q^(a+1)).

For a+b=h+r, their finite binomial expansion is

W_r(a,b)=F_a F_b F_h
  *sum_s q^(s^2)x^(-r+a-s)Q^[s(r-a+s)]
   /[F_s^2 F_(h-s)^2 F_(a-s) F_(r-a+s)],          (2)

where max(0,a-r)<=s<=min(h,a). All denominators have index<N. This follows by expanding F_pair=A+B with AB=Q^-1 BA, just as in the central proof.

The exact endpoints at EVERY retained j are

W_(2j)(j+t,d-t)=ell_j H_t, 0<=t<=h,
ell_j=q^[j(d-2j)],
W_j(0,d)=q^(-j^2).                                (3)

For the first, its lowest-vector recurrence fixes the successive ratios; the first coefficient in (2) is

q^(-j^2) sum_s Q^(s^2)[h choose s]_Q[j choose s]_Q
   =q^(-j^2) F_d/(F_h F_j)=q^[j(d-2j)].

The equality in the middle is finite Vandermonde, proved by coefficient extraction in the finite binomial product. Root reflection gives F_h F_j=N(-1)^j q1 and F_d=N. The last expression in (3) comes from the sole s=0 term of (2). The displayed lowest column also has zero next F arrow by its literal ratio; no generic semisimple decomposition is assumed.

2. The corner of the actual black tensor

Choose j_1=a, j_2=b, j_3=c with the triangle inequalities and let r=b+c-a. The magnetic indices (2a,r,0) have physical pair degrees

(d+a, d-a+c, d-c),

whose sum is exactly 3d. Apply the three rows W^dagger P to the original nested cup source p_16 p_23 p_45. Its corner is N^3 A_N, where the exact original-source kernel gives

A_N=conjugate(ell_a) sum_(k=0)^min(d-a,d-c)
   x^(k+a+c-2d)
   conjugate[H^(a)_k H^(c)_k
              W^(b)_r(c+k,d-a-k)].               (4)

Indeed set the first physical cup color to d-k in the literal black vertex kernel. Its other two colors are a+k and c+k. Formula (3) then gives (4), including the conjugate lowest-column scalar. The positive evaluation below is proved by the separate finite transformations linked above.

3. Precisely normalized positive evaluation

Set J=a+b+c and delta=J-d. For delta>=0 define

R_N=N sum_(max(a,b,c)<=t<=min(a+b,a+c,b+c))
  s_t/[s_(t-a)s_(t-b)s_(t-c)s_(d+t-J)
                   s_(a+b-t)s_(a+c-t)s_(b+c-t)],
C_N=s_a s_b s_c/(N s_delta),
e=2a^2-ab-ac+bc-J-3.                              (5)

The proved original-source identity is

A_N=(-1)^(N+J) q^e C_N R_N,   J>=d;
A_N=0,                       J<d.                (6)

The zero branch is now proved independently by the root-Fourier coefficient theorem: the actual scalar is a specified coefficient of degree d in a polynomial of degree J. That proof keeps the complete Fourier mode and its exact low-mode cancellation. The two finite transformations linked above prove the positive branch, its exact phase, and every root denominator.

The asymmetry of e belongs to the specified ordered corner (2a,r,0). The positive modulus C_N R_N is symmetric. Neither C_N nor the support cutoff may be suppressed. For example the unnormalized candidate has R_N(0,0,0)=1 whereas the original source is zero, and at even N=4, a=b=c=1 one has R_N=2sqrt(2), C_N=1/sqrt(2), |A_N|=2.

At odd N=2m+1, (a,b,c)=(m,j,m), C_N=1 and (6) reduces to the proved one-channel formula. At the even nearest center a=b=c=N/2-1, C_N=cos(pi/N), consistent with the separately proved weighted quartic and retained normal.

The joint scalar saddle theorem proves a unique joint saddle, a strict global color gap, and a complex packet for the positive expression in (5). Through the proved (6), these are now original-source SCALAR estimates. The complete CD normalization, the magnetic tensor, ordinary/marked white rows and their ordered sewing are additional data.

4. Exact checks and their logical limit

The independent finite-source audit builds W from the original coproduct, instead of (2) or (5), and evaluates the literal source corner. It checks every retained triangle triple at N=2,…,16: 539 supported triples and 1,212 vanishing triples, including all root phases and both parities. It also checks (3) at every retained j in those levels. A separate high-precision calculation checked the full complex phase and modulus on all 636 triangle triples at N=2,…,13; an additional 305 supported triples at N=11,13,17 were used to determine and check the candidate phase modulo N.

These computations supply reproducible counterexample searches and normalization evidence. They do not by themselves prove the positive branch of (6) for arbitrary N. The finite algebra linked above proves the identity directly.
