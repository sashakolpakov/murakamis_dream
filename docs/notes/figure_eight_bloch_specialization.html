Exact actual Bloch calibration of the figure-eight source

Research check, 27 October 2026. This evaluates the original crossing Bloch class at an exact parabolic representation. It identifies the marked regulator appearing in the newly proved whole-source formula without importing a separate two-tetrahedron triangulation of 4_1.

Put

z=(1+sqrt(-3))/2,   t_Riley=1-z.

The upper-half-plane z is the positive regular ideal shape. The geometric Riley embedding used by the actual braid is instead the lower root t_Riley; this distinction fixes the orientation sign. The marked noncentral parabolic normal form has equation t^2-t+1=0, as the exact Hurwitz fixed-point calculation verifies. The complete hyperbolic holonomy is irreducible and parabolic and therefore occurs among these two conjugate embeddings. The positive fundamental-class regulator below selects the geometric embedding; no separate triangulation recognition is needed for that selection. Use the exact meridians

A=[1 1;0 1], B=[1 0;t_Riley 1],
C=A^(-1) B A B^(-1) A,

and the actual framing F=[2 -1;-2 2], corresponding to (r,s)=(2,-2). Conjugate by F, apply the original prefix/Gauss reconstruction, and follow the actual four Hurwitz crossings in the order (sigma_1,sigma_2(-1),sigma_1,sigma_2(-1)).

The five-factor shape lists are exactly

Crossing Shape list, with signs (+,+,+,-,-)
Positive 1, 1, -1, 1, -1
Negative -1, -1/2, 2z, 1/2, -z
Positive z-1, z, 1, -1, z
Negative 1-z, 1/2, 2, (1-z)/2, 1

These lists are calculated in Q(sqrt(-3)) directly from the original holonomy reconstruction and crossing map. They are not guessed from an expected volume. The exact audit reproduces them and verifies closure of the physical states.

Some factors in this frame are degenerate. This is a specialization of the whole normalized idealized configuration chain: repeated-point tetrahedra are zero, and D(0)=D(1)=D(infinity)=0. Real nondegenerate arguments also have D=0. The physical Gauss reconstructions are finite. No Gaussian determinant or individual quantum normalization is evaluated at this degenerate shape frame.

Consequently the actual total regulator is

D_actual=D(2z)-D(-z)+D(z-1)
                  +D(1-z)-D((1-z)/2)
        =2D(z/2)-2D(-z)-D(z).               (1)

For the second equality, use z-1=-conjugate(z), 1-z=conjugate(z), 2z=1/(conjugate(z)/2), conjugation reverses D, and inversion also reverses D.

This last expression can be evaluated by elementary Lobachevsky identities. Let Lambda(theta)=-integral_0^theta log(2sin u)du. For an upper-half-plane cross-ratio w, the triangle with vertices 0,1,w has angles alpha,beta,gamma, and

D(w)=Lambda(alpha)+Lambda(beta)+Lambda(gamma). (2)

One can verify (2) directly: the sine rule gives log|w|=log(sin beta/sin gamma) and log|1-w|=log(sin alpha/sin gamma). Differentiating both sides using dD=log|w| dArg(1-w)-log|1-w| dArg w gives equality; their real degenerate boundary values fix the constant.

The relevant angle triples are

z:            (pi/3,pi/3,pi/3),
z/2:          (pi/3,pi/6,pi/2),
-conjugate(z):(2pi/3,pi/6,pi/6).

The elementary symmetry Lambda(pi-theta)=-Lambda(theta), the value Lambda(pi/2)=0, and duplication give

2Lambda(pi/6)=3Lambda(pi/3).

For example duplication follows by differentiating Lambda(2theta)=2Lambda(theta)+2Lambda(theta+pi/2), and setting theta=pi/6. Equation (2) now yields

D(z)=3Lambda(pi/3),
D(z/2)=5D(z)/6,   D(-z)=-2D(z)/3.

Substitution in (1) proves the exact marked evaluation

D_actual=2D(z)=6Lambda(pi/3)=4Lambda(pi/6).  (3)

The already proved actual-crossing fundamental-class theorem identifies D_actual with the oriented hyperbolic volume. Thus (3) is the marked volume normalization of the selected source formula, with no separate triangulation-identification input in this case.

There is also an exact check of the algebraic selected coefficient. The earlier principal residue is

rho_4=-1/(2t_Riley-1)=1/sqrt(-3),
sqrt(|rho_4|)=3^(-1/4).

The discrete Laplace coefficient from the actual positive source has this same modulus. Its first correction is

11pi/(36sqrt(3)N)=(11/36) rho_4 h_N,
h_N=pi*i/N.

In H=Log Q=2h the corresponding coefficient is (11/72)rho_4. These are consistency checks with the marked algebraic density; the selection and asymptotic in this prototype are proved from the whole finite source identity, not inferred from this numerical match.
