An exact auxiliary-height identity for the original marked closing

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

For every actual signed word, the original cup and last marked closing give a one-dimensional family over the bottom auxiliary coideal heights. Its known height dependence has degree determined only by the width. The proof keeps the full nested frame and computes the marked seam at every auxiliary height, including its phase. The resulting positive Fourier energy is exactly the original squared scalar times a known factor. It does not estimate that scalar or identify an auxiliary height with a knot meridian.

3. The original source and closing in these frames

The bottom auxiliary σ\sigma here replaces the constant cc in the nested coideal construction, with external height also σ\sigma. When N>2pN>2p and ZN(R)≠0Z_N(R)\ne0, the angle between the vector (𝒮l)(\mathcal S_l) and the highest Fourier character (Qpl)(Q^{pl}) has absolute cosine Mp−1/2M_p^{-1/2}. 6. Coverage and remaining scope

H already gives the nested frame at bottom cc, general ordinary boundary tensors, and the marked tensor (6), with its diagonal evaluation at cc. T gives the all-root finite product (8), the full graph construction and the actual commuting word action. The new steps are the diagonal marked seam at every bottom root, the explicit full-width boundary arrays, and the resulting initialized whole-word auxiliary-height line and Fourier energy.

The separate initialized-period and bidiagonal-transport notes concern a fixed local source and neighboring-frame comparisons. Their norm estimates are not substituted for (14)’s full word. The simple coideal frame is not identified as an algebra with the confluent pair-Casimir compiler; its full Hermite and source constraints are retained by the original physical maps.

The Fourier energy is an exact representation, not a geometric evaluation. It neither proves this energy strictly positive for every knot nor compares its growth with hyperbolic volume. No original phase, last marked cap, shared height, external scalar, or normal direction has been removed. The arbitrary-short-filling finite-exception target remains open.
