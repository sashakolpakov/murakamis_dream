Independent proof audit of the divided Ward / marked-edge bridge

Audit, 27 October 2026. I checked the current proofs in the marked divided-adjoint note, factorial fusion, the general complete-color construction, and section 5.1 of the unfolded Ward theorem. No defect was found in the stated operator, source, pivot, or moving-representation identities. This is a proof audit; I did not rerun the authors’ matrix checks.

1. The divided adjoint and its exact marked pairing

For ℰ=ρ2E\mathcal E=\rho^2E, the genuine generic Hopf adjoint is A(T)=[ℰ,T]K−1=X(T)+Y(T),X=LℰRK−1,Y=−RℰK−1. A(T)=[\mathcal E,T]K^{-1}=X(T)+Y(T),\qquad X=L_{\mathcal E}R_{K^{-1}},\quad Y=-R_{\mathcal E K^{-1}}. The relation Kℰ=QℰKK\mathcal E=Q\mathcal E K gives XY=QYXXY=QYX. On the individual integral Weyl color, XN=YN=0X^N=Y^N=0 identically before specialization. The mixed QQ-binomial coefficients have the single canceled factor 1−QN1-Q^N, giving the stated regular divided Hopf adjoint. Ordinary commutation by a one-factor divided matrix would lose these mixed terms.

For W=EN−1,0W=E_{N-1,0}, the term YlXjY^lX^j, j+l=Nj+l=N, has only the entry (l−1,l)(l-1,l). Its phase is (−1)lQ−l(l−1)/2−jlPN−1ϵlK−N. (-1)^lQ^{-l(l-1)/2-jl} P_{N-1}\epsilon_lK^{-N}. Using cl=(−1)l−1Q−l(l−1)/21−Ql,ϵl=(1−Ql)Q−l,PN−1K−N=N, c_l=\frac{(-1)^{l-1}Q^{-l(l-1)/2}}{1-Q^l},\quad \epsilon_l=(1-Q^l)Q^{-l},\quad P_{N-1}K^{-N}=N, the sign is −1-1 and the exponent is −lN-lN. Thus every superedge coefficient is exactly −N-N, including even and composite NN: $$ J_{\rm ad}(W)=-N\sum_{l=1}^{N-1}E_{l-1,l}. $$ For the literal marked row Φ(T)=Tr⁡(eT)\Phi(T)=\operatorname{Tr}(eT), eab=1/Ne_{ab}=1/N, this gives $\Phi(J_{\rm ad}W)=-(N-1)$.

Consequently $$ \Phi_{\rm div}(T)=-\frac{\Phi(J_{\rm ad}(TW))}{N-1} $$ has $\Phi_{\rm div}(I)=1$. Its equality with the original row on scalar cut operators is exact. The all-matrices homotopy $$ \Phi_{\rm div}-\Phi =(\Phi_{\rm div}-\Phi)h_W\delta_W $$ follows directly from hWδW=I−iph_W\delta_W=I-ip and the fact that the row difference kills II. It neither assumes nor supplies a geometric representative for that scalar cohomology class.

2. Partial trace: the generic pivot and the insertion order

For the genuine generic integral color, the compatible pivot is KK. The all-matrices pivotal partial trace intertwines the Hopf adjoint. This can be checked on generators: the closed EE-terms cancel because K−1K=IK^{-1}K=I, and the closed FF-terms cancel by the same cyclic trace identity. Thus it intertwines every power, and hence the quotient by 1−QN1-Q^N before specialization.

The total tensor-and-dual divided adjoint is regular: its multinomial expansion has individual nilpotent powers and the same canceled root factors. Therefore the divided partial-trace identity specializes legitimately.

Only after this specialization does one use g=K1−N=(KN)−1K g=K^{1-N}=(K^N)^{-1}K with KNK^N scalar. This proves the identity for the actual root pivot gg. It does not replace the generic gg-pivot by KK while taking a derivative. The nonconstant derivative of K−NK^{-N} is correctly retained in the separate normal adapter calculation.

For an arbitrary full matrix TT, ptr⁡g(T(W⊗I))=(ptr⁡gT)W. \operatorname{ptr}_g(T(W\otimes I)) =(\operatorname{ptr}_gT)W. Combining this with divided-adjoint naturality gives exactly the stated ordered formula for $\Phi_{\rm div}\operatorname{ptr}_g$. It does not give a formula with the edge moved outside $J_{\rm ad,total}$. The note retains the correct order.

3. Fusion and the even-level character

The generic ordered factorial product equals the finite quantum exponential of the total raising matrix. At degree kN+skN+s, cancellation gives $$ \left[ \frac{(Q;Q)_{kN+s}}{(1-Q^N)^k} \right]_{\rm root} =N^k k!(Q;Q)_s. $$ Thus the full root defect is exp⁡((−t)NJb/N)\exp((-t)^NJ_b/N), not merely its first term. All powers terminate by total-position degree.

The inherited block term is JX⊗χYJ_X\otimes\chi_Y, where χY=KYN=(−1)(N−1)length⁡(Y). \chi_Y=K_Y^N=(-1)^{(N-1)\operatorname{length}(Y)}. Expanding the two sides of the additive three-block identity cancels every inherited JJ term. Within each side the remaining operators commute because the inner mixed term commutes with its block’s total raising operator and KK. Exponentiation therefore proves the displayed twisted associativity. The factor χ\chi is essential at even NN; the signs in the notes are correct.

The extension to both divided EE and FF directions is explicitly not asserted unipotent. The invariant two-dimensional block with opposite nilpotents generates an SL2\mathrm{SL}_2 action, consistently with that qualification.

4. The moving representation retains exactly one resonant log term

In the integral lift, C(Q)C(Q) is nilpotent generically and C(Q)N=(1−QN)Jb(Q) C(Q)^N=(1-Q^N)J_b(Q) with Jb(Q)J_b(Q) regular. Thus evaluating the unfolded logarithm at −tC(Q)-tC(Q) is a finite rational matrix calculation.

For m≥Nm\ge N not divisible by NN, its denominator 1−Qm1-Q^m is a unit at the root, while C(Q)mC(Q)^m has at least one 1−QN1-Q^N factor. For m=kNm=kN, k≥2k\ge2, its numerator has kk such factors and its denominator only one. These terms vanish at the root. The only extra surviving logarithm term is (−t)NJbN. \frac{(-t)^N J_b}{N}. Since JbJ_b commutes with the root total raising operator, exponentiation gives the exact finite ghost times the literal nilpotent factorial. Higher ghost powers arise from this exponential, even though higher dilogarithm powers vanish before exponentiation.

With Q=QNehQ=Q_Ne^h, C(Q)N=−NhJb+O(h2)C(Q)^N=-NhJ_b+O(h^2). Substitution into the coefficientwise pole −Li⁡2((−tC(Q))N)/(N2h)-\operatorname{Li}_2((-tC(Q))^N)/(N^2h) gives the same surviving term, with its sign. Setting CN=0C^N=0 first would incorrectly discard it.

5. The two normals and the remaining global issue

The absent generic edge w(Q,M)=MQ−N−M−1 w(Q,M)=MQ^{-N}-M^{-1} vanishes on the integral Weyl curve M=−qNM=-q^N, but restricts to κ\kappa on the root wall. The exact auxiliary defect [ℰ,F]−ρ(K−K−1)=−(1−QN)w(Q,M)EN−1,N−1 [\mathcal E,F]-\rho(K-K^{-1}) =-(1-Q^N)w(Q,M)E_{N-1,N-1} is correct by direct adjacent-entry subtraction. The routewise pivot/twist adapter is likewise the product of the b−1b-1 closed factors K−NK^{-N} and the writhe correction. Its root value is one by knot parity; its derivative retains the closed-position moment. The notes distinguish these normals.

The marked divided row is a concrete new realization of the original cut class and is transported through exact finite partial trace. It does not yet map the partial geometric Gaussian block into that class. Ordinary full closure still kills an uncompensated positive divided ghost by position degree, and the nonzero marked-edge tests are cochain tests. No passage to a selected global geometric coefficient follows from these identities alone; the notes’ scope statements correctly preserve that distinction.
