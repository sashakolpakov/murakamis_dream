The actual generic six-head source as a typed recoupling column

29 October 2026. This identifies the full generic source coefficient B_abc with a precisely normalized Clebsch recoupling coefficient. The proof uses the original nested physical cups and ordered ribbon duals, including the pivotal clock at the bend. It applies to every ordinary triangle 0<=a,b,c<=d=N-1, including the high root partners. It is a rational generic identity; root zeros and poles are not discarded.

The finite source sum is defined in the generic six-head note, §3. The unequal Clebsch conventions and complete four-port identities are those of the four-port kernel, §§1–4. No quantum 6j formula, orthogonality assertion, or asymptotic estimate is an input here.

1. The two genuinely typed Hom bases

Put Q=zeta^2, F_r=(Q;Q)_r, and V=V_d. The ordinary object Q_j has highest weight 2j. Every Clebsch map W_AB^C below has its first highest-vector component equal to one. Set

cV=N zeta^(-d^2), pV=cV p_d,
P_L(u)=(-1)^u zeta^[u(L-u-1)] F_u F_(L-u)/F_L,
p_L(u,L-u)=P_L(u), ell_L=p_L^(-1).

For fixed a,b, a basis of Hom(Q_b,V tensor V tensor Q_a) is

L_c=(W_c tensor I_Qa) W_(Qc,Qa)^Qb,
|a-b|<=c<=min(a+b,d).                         (1)

Here W_c:Q_c -> V tensor V is the physical equal-pair map. A right basis instead first couples V tensor Q_a to an ordinary channel of highest weight D. We use its particular channel D=d:

T=(I_V tensor W_(V,Qa)^V) W_b,
T=sum_c H_c L_c.                              (2)

All expressions have the displayed physical order V,V,Q_a. In particular H_c is a RIGHT-to-LEFT coefficient, rather than the inverse four-port coefficient. Generic highest-weight decomposition proves the bases complete; equivalently the explicit unequal Clebsch norms in the four-port note give their retractions before specialization.

Write

r=b+c-a, r1=a+b-c, r2=a+c-b.

The exact source-to-recoupling identity is

H_c=(-1)^(d+c) zeta^E
   F_r1 F_r2 F_(d-a) F_(d-c) F_(2c+1)
   /[F_a F_d F_(d+c+1)] * B_abc,
E=-2a^2-5c^2+2ac+2bc+ad+3dc-c+d.              (3)

This is an equality of generic rational functions, not an equality deduced from root nodal values. The following index proof fixes every clock and scalar in (3).

2. Bend the original three-cup source

Let

J=pV_16 pV_23 pV_45,
U=(DW_a tensor DW_b tensor DW_c)J.

The actual generic source formula gives U=sDW_abc Theta_abc, where Theta_abc(2a,r,0)=1 and

sDW_abc=cV^-3 zeta^[c(d-2c)-2c]
                                 P_(2b)(r1) B_abc.       (4)

Move the first ordinary leg to the last by the ACTUAL inverse cyclic turn, and bend the now first Q_b leg into an input. The resulting map M:Q_b -> Q_c tensor Q_a has literal entries

M_(gamma,alpha;t)=sum_beta ell_(2b)(t,beta)
                K_(2a)(alpha)^-1 U_(alpha,beta,gamma).
                                                           (5)

The K_(2a)^-1 is essential: for an even highest weight 2a, the inverse cyclic cup/cap turn is K_(2a)^-1, not a permutation. Taking the input highest color t=0 and output (gamma,alpha)=(0,r2) gives

M=sDW_abc kTheta W_(Qc,Qa)^Qb,
kTheta=(-1)^r1 zeta^[2c+r1(2a+1-r1)]
                     F_(2b)F_r2/(F_r F_(2a)).               (6)

Indeed ell_(2b)(0,2b)=zeta^(2b) and K_(2a)(r2)^-1=zeta^(2c-2b). The remaining canonical invariant coefficient is the direct highest equation

Theta_abc(r2,2b,0)=(-1)^r1 zeta^[r1(2a+1-r1)]
                          F_(2b)F_r2/(F_r F_(2a)).

There is no division by a root-zero invariant norm in this step.

3. The same bend, keeping all physical ports open

Define an actual map from one physical object into V tensor Q_a by

Wtilde_a(i6,alpha;i2)
   =(-1)^d K_d(i2) K_(2a)(alpha)^-1
              sum_i1 DW_a(alpha;i1,i2) pV(i1,i6).          (7)

This is a rigid bend of DW_a, so it is a generic Hopf map. Directly from the highest entry (i6,alpha;i2)=(0,a;0) one obtains

Wtilde_a=theta_a W_(V,Qa)^V,
theta_a=cV^-1 zeta^[a(2a-d)]
                     F_a F_d/(F_(2a)F_(d-a)).               (8)

For completeness the entry before the remaining physical cup cancels is

DW_a(a;d,0)=cV^-2 zeta^[a(2a-d)]
                     F_a F_d/(F_(2a)F_(d-a)).

It follows by taking the unique term of the physical divided-coproduct formula for W_a[a](d,0). In (7), the signs and the highest physical clock cancel against pV(d,0), leaving exactly cV times that entry.

Now substitute the THREE literal cups of J and the ordered formula for DW_b into (5). The cups pV_23,pV_45 force i3=d-i2, i5=d-i4. Their only surviving ratio is

pV(i4)/pV(d-i4)=(-1)^d K_d(i4).

The ordinary input bend contributes P_(2b)(t)/P_(2b)(2b-t)=K_(2b)(t). Physical weight conservation in W_b[t](i5,i2) gives

K_(2b)(t)=K_d(i5)K_d(i2),
K_d(i4)=K_d(i5)^-1.

Thus the two physical clocks at i4,i5 cancel, with precisely the remaining factors in (7). This proves the all-input map identity

M=(DW_c tensor I_Qa)(I_V tensor Wtilde_a) W_b
  =theta_a (DW_c tensor I_Qa) T.                         (9)

Equation(9) preserves arbitrary allowed exterior tensors and all input colors of Q_b. It is stronger than comparing a single source corner.

Apply DW_c to (2). The generic equal-pair norm is

DW_c W_c=n_c I,
n_c=(-1)^(d+c)cV^-2
   zeta^[c(c+1)-d(2c+1)]
                  F_(d+c+1)/(F_(d-c)F_(2c+1)).            (10)

Different generic channels are killed. Comparing (6),(9) gives

H_c=sDW_abc kTheta/(theta_a n_c).                       (11)

Insert (4),(6),(8),(10) and use

P_(2b)(r1) kTheta
               =zeta^[2c(r1+1)] F_r1 F_r2/F_(2a).

The powers of cV, all three ordinary cups, and F_(2a) cancel. The remaining exponent and factorials are exactly (3).

4. Actual Casimir consequence and limits

The RIGHT map T factors through the ordinary physical object V_d on positions 2,3. Hence

Omega_23 T=(zeta^N+zeta^-N) T.                         (12)

Consequently the vector of actual coefficients H_c from (3) solves the genuine left-channel Casimir eigenproblem, including its endpoint conditions. This supplies a literal source normalization for comparison with a finite Askey–Wilson/Sears polynomial. Identifying that polynomial requires matching the actual channel recurrence and gauge; its identity is not assumed in this note.

All equations above are generic identities. They may be continued as meromorphic identities to the root, with paired columns and their normal derivatives retained together. Individual factors in (3) or (11) can vanish or have poles; the regular original physical source B_abc is still the finite analytic sum in the six-head note. Nothing here bounds recoupling conditioning, turns the ordered dual into a Hermitian adjoint, or proves a primitive-graph positive asymptotic.

5. Verification

The matching audit constructs the physical W_j,DW_j independently, checks (7),(8) on every input color, and verifies (3) by projecting the full map (2) for every admissible triangle at generic finite-field levels. A separate literal six-head contraction verifies (5),(9), including the pivotal clock, before reducing to a scalar. These finite checks supplement the displayed generic index proof.
