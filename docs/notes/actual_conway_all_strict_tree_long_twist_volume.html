Long-twist volume equality for every strict Conway composition tree

31 October 2026. Internally reviewed research theorem. The statement concerns a specified literal presentation and its original marked invariant. It includes arbitrary mixtures of flagged and unflagged source junctions. The new ingredient is an estimate for an unflagged component at its actual geometric boundary action. No classification of all arborescent knot presentations is asserted.

1. The presentation and the theorem

Fix a finite ordered expression τ=C(FG) \tau=C(FG) \tag{1} in distinct leaves h1,…,hHh_1,\ldots,h_H, ordered products and the original quarter-turn TT. Retain the original cylindrical closure, terminal order, one-cut mark and disk framings. There are H≥2H\ge2 leaves. The filled leaf is the integral rational tangle hi(ni)=T(Rni). h_i(n_i)=T(R^{n_i}). \tag{2} Write Kτ(𝒏)K_\tau(\mathbf n) for the literal filled diagram.

The following finite type test is the hypothesis on τ\tau. A leaf has type LL; a turn exchanges L,HL,H and preserves AA. The nonempty product rules, including reversed orders, are LL,LA,AA↦A,HL,HA↦H,HH↦⌀. LL,LA,AA\longmapsto A,\qquad HL,HA\longmapsto H,\qquad HH\longmapsto\varnothing. \tag{3} The root pair in (1) must be one of AA,AL,AH,LHAA,AL,AH,LH, in either order. Call such a presentation strict. The source compiler proves that this test is equivalent to the existence of a strict state in the original reduced wire polytope. In particular, it is a test on the specified finite syntax, not a conjectural property of an optimizer.

Theorem. For every fixed strict presentation τ\tau, there is a finite number B(τ)B(\tau) such that, for every fixed tuple of integers 𝒏\mathbf n with |ni|>B(τ)|n_i|>B(\tau), if its literal endpoint pairing has one component and no internal closed component, Kτ(𝒏)K_\tau(\mathbf n) is a hyperbolic knot and limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(Kτ(𝒏))|=Vol⁡(S3\Kτ(𝒏)). \boxed{\quad \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log|K_N(K_\tau(\mathbf n))| =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K_\tau(\mathbf n)). \quad} \tag{4} Here KNK_N is the original reduced enhanced Kashaev evaluation, with the normalization used throughout the source construction. The limit runs through all positive integer levels, including both parities and composite levels. The integer weights have independent signs and magnitudes. The expression and all its weights are fixed before NN tends to infinity. The threshold may depend on the entire expression.

Every strict presentation has knot-producing pairing choices, by the constructive pairing theorem, §7. The theorem does not say that every parity choice produces a knot. Equivalently, fix a knot-producing choice ϵi∈{0,±1}\epsilon_i\in\{0,\pm1\}, write ni=ϵi−2pin_i=\epsilon_i-2p_i, and take all independent fixed signed integers |pi||p_i| sufficiently large. A maximum over the finitely many relevant parity choices gives B(τ)B(\tau).

The knots in (4) are Conway algebraic, or arborescent: (2), the ordered products and turns are the actual Conway operations on rational pieces. Strictness and the pairing test are substantive hypotheses. This theorem does not identify every arborescent knot with a strict long-weight presentation.

3. Exact source decomposition and its boundary actions

Use the reduced-hub normal form. It is an identity of full colored source tensors. Internal edges between reduced product hubs carry TT; an ordinary external leaf is hih_i, and a flagged external leaf is Thi=PiTh_i=P_i. Strictness is equivalent to at most one flagged leaf at each hub. No relative-boundary tangle isotopy is inferred from this source identity.

For H≥3H\ge3, if no hub is flagged, the scalar reduces to the already proved completed/completed-root grammar, or to the isolated three-leaf closure. If H=2H=2, the strict two-leaf knot case is already included in the weighted-tree theorem. Thus consider H≥3H\ge3 and at least one flagged hub. Root the source at one flagged leaf P0P_0.

A flagged descendant sends a type-LL module Di[E]=T(PiE),E=∏jQj, D_i[E]=T(P_iE),\qquad E=\prod_j Q_j, \tag{9} where the satellite coupons QjQ_j have type LL or AA. An ordinary physical leaf is the case E=1E=1. Group each maximal connected set of unflagged hubs into one component. Its outgoing coupon has the form Q=T(A(F1,…,Fs)),s≥2, Q=T(A(F_1,\ldots,F_s)),\qquad s\ge2, \tag{10} where the output of AA is an unturned product and all boundary modules FiF_i have type LL. Inside this component there are only completed products and turns. The source tree is finite, so (9) and (10) give a well-founded induction.

For any satellite QQ, let fQf_Q be its original optimized positive output profile, and define ΩQ=max0≤b≤1/2{g(b)+fQ(b)},G0=g(1/4)=2Catalanπ. \Omega_Q=\max_{0\le b\le1/2}\{g(b)+f_Q(b)\},\qquad G_0=g(1/4)=\frac{2\,\mathrm{Catalan}}{\pi}. \tag{11} The rigid-profile theorem fixes the absolute constant of (9): fDi[E](b)=g(b)+ci,ci=∑jΩQj,ΩDi[E]=ci+2G0. f_{D_i[E]}(b)=g(b)+c_i,\qquad c_i=\sum_j\Omega_{Q_j},\qquad \Omega_{D_i[E]}=c_i+2G_0. \tag{12} For (10), if fFi=g+cif_{F_i}=g+c_i, then ΩQ=∑ici+Fprobe(C(hA(h,…,h))). \Omega_Q=\sum_i c_i+ F_{\mathrm{probe}}\bigl(C(hA(h,\ldots,h))\bigr). \tag{13} Here FprobeF_{\mathrm{probe}} denotes the complete positive action of the displayed reference expression. Formula (13) is a boundary variational identity, not a physical insertion into the original diagram. The probe geometry proves it with the original unsquared port equations.

At the flagged root the exact source is ZNϵ=ϕ(P0∏jQj). Z_N^\epsilon=\phi(P_0\prod_jQ_j). \tag{14} The original complete action satisfies F0=∑jΩQj. Equivalently, the original complementary root relation gives the same maximum. Thus every boundary constant used below is charged to the actual complete action in (6).

4. The estimate for an unflagged component

Put d=N−1d=N-1, M=⌊d/2⌋M=\lfloor d/2\rfloor, and write er=B̂re_r=\widehat B_r for the normalized Newton basis of the original repeated-root algebra. Its exact turn and full Frobenius pairing are Ter=ed−r,ϕ(eres)=𝟏r+s=d. Te_r=e_{d-r},\qquad \phi(e_re_s)=\mathbf1_{r+s=d}. \tag{16} The faithful norm ∥⋅∥𝒥\|\cdot\|_{\mathcal J} records values and first derivatives at every double node, and the value at the odd simple node. Define the positive comparison weights wN,r={er(−2),r≤M,1,r>M,WN=∑r=0dwN,rer. w_{N,r}= \begin{cases}e_r(-2),&r\le M,\\1,&r>M,\end{cases} \qquad W_N=\sum_{r=0}^d w_{N,r}e_r. \tag{17} They obey ∥er∥𝒥≤NCwN,r\|e_r\|_{\mathcal J}\le N^Cw_{N,r} and log⁡wN,r=Ng(r/N)+O(log⁡N)\log w_{N,r}=Ng(r/N)+O(\log N) on the low range.

The positive probe theorem has an arbitrary-array hypothesis: |[er]Fi|≤exp⁡N(ci+o(1)){wN,r,r≤M,1,r>M. |[e_r]F_i|\le \exp N(c_i+o(1)) \begin{cases}w_{N,r},&r\le M,\\1,&r>M.\end{cases} \tag{18} Let AabsA^{\mathrm{abs}} be the same circuit with the absolute values of these boundary coefficients. Positive Newton multiplication and exact index reversal give ∥T(A)∥𝒥≤NC∑rwN,r|[ed−r]A|≤NCϕ(WNAabs). \|T(A)\|_{\mathcal J} \le N^C\sum_r w_{N,r}|[e_{d-r}]A| \le N^C\phi(W_NA^{\mathrm{abs}}). \tag{19} This includes every high Newton normal direction.

The right side is estimated by the existing scalar block inequalities. Its low input and probe bands have profile gg; their high bands have profile 00. These are bounded by the certified profiles g̃(x)=g(max⁡(x,1/4)),k(x)=−2J(x/2), \widetilde g(x)=g(\max(x,1/4)),\qquad k(x)=-2J(x/2), \tag{20} respectively. The closed comparison has no flags. It is either the isolated effective three-leaf closure or an exact source regrouping of the completed-root grammar. The two-leaf block and single-attachment inequalities apply to arbitrary arrays satisfying these upper profiles: their proofs use positive multiplication, true support, concave tangents and stationary cancellation, rather than the provenance of a color.

Consequently (19) has upper rate ΩQ\Omega_Q from (13). Every sector with a high boundary index or high probe index has a strict exponential gap. The all-low action has the original unique strict maximum; outside any fixed neighborhood of that maximum it also has a strict gap. The finite N−1N-1 offsets, both parities and polynomial numbers of states are retained in this assertion. These comparison bands are not declared to be the original physical core and alias bands.

The spectral propagation theorem now proves two further consequences: ∥QN∥𝒥≤exp⁡N(ΩQ+o(1)),|QN(2cos⁡(θ+iy))|≤exp⁡N{ΩQ−c(θ−π)2+Cy2+o(1)}. \begin{aligned} \|Q_N\|_{\mathcal J}&\le \exp N(\Omega_Q+o(1)),\\ |Q_N(2\cos(\theta+iy))| &\le \exp N\{\Omega_Q-c(\theta-\pi)^2+Cy^2+o(1)\}. \end{aligned} \tag{21} The second estimate uses the canonical representative and a fixed small strip; c>0c>0. Near the clean probe index, the low Newton polynomial has a strict angular Gaussian at −2-2. The remaining output is a canonical polynomial with a fixed norm gap, so the Hermite interpolation cost exp⁡(N|y|)\exp(N|y|) is absorbed by taking a smaller strip.

Finally, a fixed coefficient loss in any boundary module on a compact window around its actual clean index gives a strict loss in ∥QN∥𝒥\|Q_N\|_{\mathcal J}. Outside that window the clean action already has a gap, and high-band sectors are already gapped. This is the mechanism that transports physical color deficits through an unflagged component. No uniform bound on an arbitrary turn’s norm at this smaller action is asserted.

5. Mutual induction and the whole physical color cube

The decorated-character estimate gives, for an actual input EE with norm upper rate cc, |[er]Djϵ[E]|≤NC∥E∥𝒥{wN,r,r≤M,1,r>M. |[e_r]D_j^\epsilon[E]|\le N^C\|E\|_{\mathcal J} \begin{cases}w_{N,r},&r\le M,\\1,&r>M.\end{cases} \tag{22} Its norm rate is at most c+2G0c+2G_0, and its canonical output has an angular Gaussian at that rate. An existing input norm loss is preserved at every output coefficient.

For the newly inserted physical character, the exact root-character Fourier theorem gives, on every fixed compact I⊂(0,1/2)I\subset(0,1/2), N−j≥δN,r/N∈I⇒|[er]Djϵ[E]|≤exp⁡N{c+g(r/N)−ηδ,I},ηδ,I>0, N-j\ge\delta N,\quad r/N\in I \quad\Longrightarrow\quad |[e_r]D_j^\epsilon[E]| \le \exp N\{c+g(r/N)-\eta_{\delta,I}\}, \qquad \eta_{\delta,I}>0, \tag{23} provided EE has the inherited strip envelope. The empty input is allowed. The extension to every such compact interval is proved in the spectral propagation theorem; it is essential because an effective leaf of an unflagged component need not be at the quarter index. The Fourier estimate retains the original normal terms and folded frequency cancellation.

Proceed from the leaves toward the flagged root. Products add norm rates and multiply actual strip envelopes. At (9), equations (22)–(23) give the coefficient bounds needed at the next component, with the absolute constant cic_i in (12). At (10), equations (19)–(21) give a fresh norm and Gaussian estimate at the intrinsic action (13). A deficit in a descendant passes through the norm estimate; a deficit in the distinguished character of a boundary module passes through (23) and clean-state localization. Every original physical color is therefore accounted for.

At (14), the root character has polynomial norm in the marked functional. A far descendant is controlled by its norm loss. If the root character is far, apply the exact Fourier theorem to the product of the satellite representatives, which has the inherited Gaussian envelope. Formula (15) identifies the resulting reference exponent with F0F_0. This proves (8). There are finitely many original colors, so a common positive γδ\gamma_\delta is obtained by taking the minimum of their margins.

The no-flag cases cited in §3 already satisfy (8). The two-leaf knot case does also. Hence (8) holds for every presentation in the theorem.

6. Recovering the original packet and filling the crossing circles

The exact complementary-color identity for every original leaf is hjϵ=Cjϵ+(−1)NhN−jϵ(j>N/2),h0ϵ=0. h_j^\epsilon=C_j^\epsilon+(-1)^Nh_{N-j}^\epsilon \quad(j>N/2),\qquad h_0^\epsilon=0. \tag{24} For j≤N/2j\le N/2, its core is zero. Multilinearity expands the all-core scalar into the whole source plus finitely many terms having at least one color at most N/2N/2. Equation (8), with δ=1/2\delta=1/2, gives a uniform exponential bound for their total. This is the complementary-color principle, §4, applied to the original physical leaves, rather than the comparison bands in §4 above.

7. The selected value is the knot complement volume

Apply the marked geometric theorem to the original literal presentation, not to a rearranged comparison source. It provides the actual nearby representation of its augmented-link group with primitive logarithms and an absolutely calibrated regulator: Ui=−2πiχi,Vi=−2∂iΦϵ,G=2πiΦϵ,Vol⁡(ρχ)=ℑG−12∑iℜUiℑVi. U_i=-2\pi i\chi_i,\qquad V_i=-2\partial_i\Phi_\epsilon,\qquad G=2\pi i\Phi_\epsilon,\qquad \operatorname{Vol}(\rho_\chi)= \Im G-\frac12\sum_i\Re U_i\Im V_i. \tag{28} The stationary equations (27) are precisely Ui+piVi=−2πi. All auxiliary crossing-circle cusps are filled to insert the twists. The main knot cusp stays unfilled. At this solution, 2πℜSϵ,𝒑(χ*)=Vol⁡(S3\Kτ(𝒏)). 2\pi\Re S_{\epsilon,\mathbf p}(\chi_*) =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K_\tau(\mathbf n)). \tag{30} A common reciprocal-slope neighborhood exists for the finitely many ports. Combining its threshold with the analytic transfer threshold proves (4). The original one-cut normalization contributes only its recorded polynomial factor and unit framing character, which do not alter the logarithmic limit.

8. Scope and proof dependencies

The new conclusion includes the previous completed-root and weighted-tree classes and all their strict mixed compositions. The finite admissibility test is (3), or equivalently at most one flagged original leaf per reduced hub. Branching, valence and depth are unrestricted finite quantities. The new estimate does not require every hub to carry a character.

The earlier manuscript retains its stated completed-root theorem. This companion establishes the larger class without silently changing that manuscript or its proof scope. The new proof uses the existing exact two-leaf and single-attachment certificates unchanged, together with the new probe and spectral arguments linked above. No numerical asymptotic or external proof technique is an input to this extension.

The pointed quantum AA-period corollary combines (4) with the existing all-level ADO annihilator and finite-pointing theorems. It expresses the same limit directly through a genuine quantum AA-operator’s companion product and bounded-order quantum-parameter jets. Operator existence and that selected period’s volume limit are therefore available for the covered knots.

There is still no classification of every arborescent presentation by this strict grammar, no threshold uniform over growing trees, and no short-twist theorem. Identifying the selected classical main-meridian geometric factor and its quantum module with the original marked regulator germ is a separate stronger task. The source identities used for estimates do not assert tangle isotopies; the original expression supplies its own packet and marked geometry in §§6–7.

The checks included the exact literal-source reduction, noncircular mutual induction, regular-index color losses, full normal data, absolute boundary constants, two-leaf knot case, original packet and phase, independently signed thresholds, and the original primitive marked completion. All passed after notation and leaf-frame clarifications. The three new proof companions received independent full reviews as well.
