Sourced telescoping: a geometric-support criterion and the endpoint obstruction

Research note, 27 October 2026. This audits the connection between the finite-array annihilator proof and the nonzero geometric normal block. It proves a conditional geometric-support criterion, an interior certificate lemma, and a precise obstruction to the naive integration map. It does not prove that the actual selected finite-state operator annihilates the geometric normal block.

1. Use the same auxiliary lift as the normal coefficient model

The auxiliary lift in the finite-array proof takes the (0,0) entry of the off-root cut partial trace. The normal coefficient model instead uses the balanced constant vector and normalized-average covector. At the root these give the same reduced scalar by scalarity of the actual cut endomorphism. Off the root they need not agree, and their summands have different supports.

For the present comparison use the off-root normalized-average lift

Fbar_N(q,x)=(1/N) sum_(a=0)^(N-1) C_N(q,x)_(a,a),

in the same factorial boundary frame as the normal model. The gauge is diagonal and invertible over generic (q,x), so diagonal cut entries are unchanged by it. At the actual root Fbar_N=F_N. Every crossing, pivot and residual framing factor is retained. The common factor 1/N is independent of all shifts used in the finite-span argument, so it does not affect that argument or any summand shift ratio.

This lift has a full-dimensional interior in the d=m+1 independent flow coordinates. Indeed, close strands 1,...,b-1 using the integral cofactor of the knot permutation, with determinant +1 or -1. The initial positions are integral linear functions of the free cut position a_0 and the m transfers. Put a_0=N/2+O(1) and every transfer near epsilon*N, with epsilon>0 fixed and sufficiently small. At zero transfer all positions equal a_0. Hence all positions on the fixed word are N/2+O(epsilon*N). Every transfer is positive, and all upper position and transfer inequalities are strict for sufficiently small epsilon. Small independent variations of all m+1 coordinates preserve these strict inequalities. Thus the support contains the integer points in a nonempty open rational cone for large N.

The old cut-zero lift need not have this property. For example, one positive two-strand crossing with cut position a_0=0 has update (a_0,a_1)->(a_1+k,a_0-k). Closing the second strand forces a_1=-k; nonnegativity then forces a_1=k=0. Consequently its summand array cannot silently replace the full normal flow array.

2. A global array certificate restricts to an interior rational identity

Let I_N(q,x;k) be one of these proper, aligned summands and let

P(x,z,T,S) I=0,       z=q^N,

be a global zero-extended certificate from the finite-span proof. For each fixed shift occurring in P, choose a smaller open cone in the interior from Section 1. For all sufficiently large integer points of this cone, every shifted term in P remains supported. Divide there by the nonzero reference summand. Proper alignment makes the result a rational function

R(q,x,z,t_1,...,t_d),       t_i=q^(k_i).

Then R=0 as a rational function in the independent variables.

Here is the needed density argument. Clear the fixed rational denominator, then expand the numerator as a finite Laurent polynomial in (q,x,z,t). If it is nonzero, precombine equal monomials and choose an integer point (N,k) in the open cone avoiding every affine hyperplane

e-e' +(c-c')N +(alpha-alpha') dot k=0

arising from two distinct (q,z,t) exponent vectors. Such an integer point exists: an open rational cone contains arbitrarily large lattice boxes, while finitely many proper affine hyperplanes cannot cover them. Distinct exponent vectors then give distinct powers of q after z=q^N,t_i=q^(k_i). The resulting Laurent polynomial in (q,x) cannot vanish, a contradiction. Thus the numerator was zero.

This result imports no summation or cycle. It says that the same global certificate is an interior contiguity identity. Any formal realization of the exact actual shift ratios therefore satisfies that identity before its internal variables are pushed forward. In particular the local exact ratios in the normal-period note may be used; their half-step, pivot, residual framing and base factors may not be omitted.

3. The safe order of central specialization and moment extraction

Clear denominators of the whole certificate, then divide its largest common central factor (z+1)^r, obtaining P0. The generic fixed-N array identity survives this division: q^N+1 is a nonzero element of the rational function field in generic q. Thus

P0(x,q^N,T,S) I_N=0.

The finite summands are regular at q_N=exp(pi*i/N); the crossing denominators have indices below N. Specialization gives

P1(x,T,S)=P0(x,-1,T,S),

which is nonzero as a fixed Ore polynomial and annihilates the root array for all but finitely many levels. If several direct-sum summand components are retained, use their common certificate throughout.

Now expand

P1=sum_alpha A_alpha(x,T)(S-1)^alpha.

For a least nonzero total degree h, every nonzero A_alpha of degree h annihilates the actual summed root period. This is the finite moment argument: for w_i=exp(v_i), translation of the entire finite zero-extended array is exact and

P1(x,T,exp(-v)) F_N(x,v)=0.

At degree v^alpha, all terms containing a positive moment of F_N have a lower-degree coefficient of P1, hence are zero. The surviving term is (-1)^h A_alpha F_N(x,0).

This order is useful for a formal comparison: remove the central factor of the whole certificate, specialize, then extract moments. Dividing a central factor from an individual moment coefficient only after specialization is not justified by a formal source defined solely at z=-1.

4. Conditional geometric-support theorem

Suppose the actual root-specialized contiguity realization admits a linear weighted formal sourced pushforward I_v with all of the following properties:

  1. I_v(S_i f)=exp(-v_i) I_v(f) for every internal shift appearing in P1 and its iterates.

  2. I_v(Tf)=T I_v(f), and external scalar coefficients commute with I_v.

  3. Z(v)=I_v(I) is regular as a formal power series in the independent v_i at zero, and Z(0) is nonzero.

  4. The unweighted period has its marked geometric WKB form

    Z(0)=C(h) exp(S/h) a_0 (1+O(h)),  a_0!=0,

    with h=Log q, the retained base-only line C(h), and the actual meridian-step ratio having leading value equal to the marked geometric longitude L. All character and full-peripheral gauges are included in that statement.

For regularity in item 3 one may use exp(S/h)K((h))[[v]], allowing a different finite negative h order in each coefficient of v; no uniform lower bound in the v degree is needed.

Then one of the actual all-level operators constructed in Section 3 has a nonzero classical symbol vanishing on the geometric A-curve.

Proof: apply I_v to the interior certificate. Items 1 and 2 give

P1(x,T,exp(-v)) Z(v)=0.

The same least-degree coefficient argument as for the finite sum gives A_alpha Z(0)=0. Choose one nonzero such coefficient and divide only its common scalar (q-1) valuation, obtaining Ahat. This division preserves the actual all-level root annihilation and the formal annihilation. Let a_cl(x,L) be the first nonzero classical symbol of Ahat; clear its rational denominators if necessary. Leading WKB order of Ahat Z(0)=0, with a_0!=0, gives

a_cl(x,L_geom(x))=0.

On an irreducible geometric branch this says that its defining geometric A-factor divides the cleared classical polynomial, in the stated peripheral gauge. The operator came entirely from the actual global certificate and finite moment extraction. No known A-factor has been appended to it. This statement concerns that constructed annihilator; it does not assert that every annihilator, or the minimal operator, has that factor. A larger annihilator’s symbol can contain factors absent from the minimal one.

5. Why the current relative source does not supply the hypotheses

The existing relative source realization constructs a literal finite boundary projector, natural for all finite linear matrices. It does not identify its auxiliary relative coordinate with the two internal Gaussian framing modes. Consequently its coefficientwise naturality is not item 1 of Section 4.

The failure of the naive replacement can be written exactly. At completeness write

Omega_cut=dt wedge beta,       beta=dlog A,

with marked endpoints t=0,1. On a relative top form the functional is

L(a,b_0,b_1)=integral_0^1 a(t)dt-b_1+b_0.

For any polynomial f(t,A),

L(partial_t f,0,0)=[f(1,A)-f(0,A)] beta.

It is generally nonzero. The relative coboundary is instead

delta f=(partial_t f,f(0),f(1)),

and only this complete triple has zero transgression. Thus replacing an internal summand divergence by an ordinary derivative on the geometric framing chart loses precisely the endpoint co-source.

The finite-difference statement is equally explicit. For S_eta f(t)=f(t+eta) and

J_v(f)=integral_0^1 exp(v*t/eta) f(t)dt,

one has

J_v(S_eta f)=exp(-v) J_v(f)
  +exp(-v) [integral_1^(1+eta) exp(v*t/eta)f(t)dt
              -integral_0^eta exp(v*t/eta)f(t)dt].

These endpoint series are well-defined coefficientwise for polynomial f, expanding in v with Laurent coefficients in eta. Already f=1 contradicts the desired weighted covariance for the fixed relative interval: S_eta f=f and J_0(f)=1, whereas covariance would require (1-exp(-v))J_v(f)=0. The source endpoints must move or be compensated. Ordinary formal Gaussian integration in the transverse directions does not remove this defect.

There is a second exact obstruction to replacing finite source data by ordinary functions before pushforward. In

C_N=k[A,A^(-1)]/(A^N-1),

the prescribed canonical representative satisfies

A f_tilde - (Af)_tilde = f_(N-1)(A^N-1).

For f=A^(N-1), the actual cut gives phi_N(Af)=phi_N(1)=1, while the naive geometric operation gives Res_(A=0) A^N dA/A=0. The existing source comparison is linear, with this carry retained; it is not a ring map. Local interior contiguity identities do not encode that carry by themselves.

6. Exact remaining statement

A sufficient next result is an explicit chain map from the actual whole internal finite contraction, with all endpoint/carry strata, to the relative geometric Gaussian pushforward, which sends every internal shift to its weighted source action and gives a nonzero unweighted geometric period. Section 4 would then force a geometric factor in a specifically constructed actual all-level annihilator.

At present the normal block is nonzero before the remaining source pairing, and the literal finite relative source is nonzero in its own resolution. The map between these two pushforwards has not been constructed. The endpoint identity above is an explicit reason why their separate nonvanishing and ordinary integration by parts cannot substitute for it. No conclusion about the selected global geometric cycle or its volume growth follows from this note.
