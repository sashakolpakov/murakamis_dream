Coherent endpoint transport through original crossings

Written proof, 24 November 2026.

A fixed positive sweep of overlapping original crossings preserves the endpoint-to-bulk norm rate for every coherent input in a sublinear endpoint box. The inputs may be entangled across all heads, with arbitrary level-dependent coefficients. The proof uses bounded conjugates of prefix Cartans rather than a possibly unbounded conjugate of an individual second-head Cartan.

Every matrix entry of this sweep has one transfer path. Its column norms can therefore be estimated exactly. Multivariate selected-root filters then control cancellation between the columns. The full outgoing packet is retained; no endpoint projection is inserted between crossings. A separate exact returned block has a controlled inverse and two explicitly specified positive incident metrics. No original scalar closing is estimated below.

7. Coverage, phases, and the original closing
