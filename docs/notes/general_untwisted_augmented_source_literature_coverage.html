Coverage of the untwisted augmented-source volume theorem

Checked 30 October 2026 against the primary versions linked below. This compares definitions and theorem statements with the untwisted source theorem. It imports no external proof or proof technique and makes no priority claim.

The present statement fixes one connected augmented plat, its actual labeled convex ideal right-angled cut polyhedron, and its complete link exterior. It concerns the original single-mark root invariant, no retained half twists, and the full integer sequence of levels. It does not concern arbitrary filled knots, all retained half-twist sums, or an invariant chosen only after fixing a geometric triangulation.

1. The invariant name is not a distinction

Murakami–Murakami, The colored Jones polynomials and the simplicial volume of a knot, arXiv:math/9905075v2, 10 June 1999, Theorem 4.9, identifies their Kashaev invariant with the normalized colored Jones evaluation for every link and every integer N>=2. The introduction specifies N-dimensional colors, a primitive N-th Jones root, and unknot value one. Thus an overlapping colored-Jones theorem cannot be excluded merely by calling our source “original Kashaev.” Primary version, pp.1 and15.

Our parameter dictionary is q=exp(pi i/N), Jones t=q^2, and skein A=exp(pi i/(2N)). The literal source identity, section4 divides exactly one ordinary physical trace factor D_N=(-1)^(N-1)[N] before root specialization, equivalently retains its specified first normal. It is not division by one quantum dimension for every component. Mark, framing and dual-order phases must still be matched in any concrete comparison; they cannot convert an exact zero into a nonzero value when they are units.

2. Direct overlapping link results and the parity test

Van der Veen, Proof of the volume conjecture for Whitehead chains, arXiv:math/0611181v2, 30 July 2007, Main Theorem, covers W_(a,b,c,d). Its b=1 conclusion runs over all N; this includes the named Whitehead-link and Borromean-ring examples. For b>=2 the statement is odd-only and the even evaluations vanish. Definition1 makes b the number of Belt tangles. The generic normalization on printed p.4 is sum_n [2n+1]/[N] times the composed tangle eigenvalues, so the even zeros are not explained by a different number of component reductions. The text also records a Seifert piece when b>=2. Primary version, pp.2–4.

This possible contradiction was checked against the actual diagram, not dismissed by terminology. In Figure1 a Belt is one circle around two straight strands. The stated commuting composition permits two Belt copies to be adjacent. Their circles cobound an annulus disjoint from those strands; the literal cut graph has two parallel MAIN edges with an intervening white bigon. A nondegenerate face of the required convex ideal polyhedron cannot be that bigon. Independently, our exact face polynomial reproduces the even zero: the intervening middle-face degree is at most a_1+a_2<=N-2, whereas the marked coefficient requires N-1. This is a finite source compatibility check, not an asymptotic argument. The complete labeled geometric certificate is essential; connectedness or the word “augmented” alone is insufficient.

Van der Veen, The volume conjecture for augmented knotted trivalent graphs, Algebraic & Geometric Topology9 (2009), 691–722, Definition2 and Theorem2, uses a KTG move sequence and a sequence-dependent minimum number of augmentation rings at every unzip. It states an odd-N SO(3) volume law and zero even-N evaluations. The singly augmented outside consists of regular ideal octahedra; additional rings introduce the stated Seifert pieces. The normalization divides by an unlink according to SPLIT components, not by every link component. This is substantial prior augmented-family coverage, but the theorem’s multiplicity and parity hypotheses must be retained. Published primary paper, pp.692,695–696; arXiv:0805.0094v2, 5 February 2009.

3. Octahedral results and nearby quantum regimes

Ibarra–McQuire–Purcell, Augmented links, shadow links, and the TV volume conjecture: A geometric perspective, New York J. Math.32 (2026), 1–34, Definition2.8, restricts “octahedral” to the indicated regular-ideal-octahedron decomposition. Theorem2.12 identifies these complements with fundamental shadow-link complements. Theorem4.15 gives the untwisted Turaev–Viro volume limit at exp(2pi i/r), odd r. Theorem4.16 also gives a colored-Jones limit for equal colors m at t=exp(4pi i/(2m+1)), with m even; Remark4.17 presents the extension to all m as an expectation. This is not the ordinary N-dimensional/Jones-root exp(2pi i/N) specialization merely by renaming m. The introduction explicitly attributes earlier Kashaev coverage of octahedral augmented examples to van der Veen. Published primary version, pp.4,8,30–31. The earlier arXiv:2506.09296v1 has different theorem numbering; the numbers here are those of the journal version.

Wong–Yang, Relative Reshetikhin–Turaev invariants, hyperbolic cone metrics and discrete Fourier transforms I, arXiv:2008.05045v4, 11 December 2022, Theorem6.3, states generalized colored-Jones asymptotics for twisted octahedral fully augmented links at exp(4pi i/r), odd r, with colors giving sufficiently small cone angles. Theorem6.1 includes the corresponding odd-r Turaev–Viro statement, including twists. These are important overlapping geometric families and quantum conclusions, not a theorem restricted to the untwisted case. Their root/color and relative-RT conventions still need to be retained when comparing the exact invariant above. Primary version, sections1.1 and6.

4. General polyhedra and triangulations are separate coverage

Belletti, An upper bound conjecture for the Yokota invariant, arXiv:2002.01904v2, 20 June 2024; published AGT25 (2025), 645–675, Theorem4.9, proves its upper-bound conjecture for graphs obtained from the tetrahedron by the two specified vertex/face moves. The trivalent Yokota invariant is a unitary bracket absolute square, with odd root order and its own admissible colors. The right-angled rectification and TV consequences are relevant prior coverage, but do not identify the original single-mark all-level link source. Primary version, Definition2.7 and Theorem4.9.

Ge’s Geometric ideal triangulations of hyperbolic 3-manifolds, arXiv:2609.27635v1, submitted 23 September 2026, is an existing primary preprint. Theorem1.1 states geometric ideal triangulation existence for every complete finite-volume cusped hyperbolic 3-manifold. This is a geometric statement, not a Kashaev/colored-Jones asymptotic, an invariant-normalization identity, or a theorem selecting an original quantum period. No inference about the correctness of its proof is made in this coverage check. Primary statement.

Jun Murakami’s 13 October 2026 IPAM talk abstract explicitly describes fully augmented right-angled polyhedra and their complexified filling deformations. This is a closely related existing program, but that abstract does not state the present full-level untwisted theorem. Primary event abstract.

The search also found Casandra Monroe’s 2018 Princeton senior thesis, Knot Too Big: The Volume Conjecture for Augmented Links. Its repository abstract concerns fully augmented links. The repository’s PDF endpoint required authentication during this check, so its theorem statements were NOT verified; its title alone supports neither inclusion nor exclusion. Primary repository record.

5. Publication wording supported by this check

The result should acknowledge existing Whitehead-chain, augmented-KTG and octahedral-link volume results. Its precise statement concerns the recorded original source for any fixed untwisted augmented plat with the specified complete labeled right-angled certificate, without assuming a regular octahedral decomposition. The consulted primary statements do not by themselves establish inclusion of that entire class with that exact all-level normalization. This bounded check is not an exhaustive novelty conclusion.

For any claimed overlapping diagram, first match the literal topology, augmentation multiplicities, quantum root and color dimension, one-cut normalization, framing, and level subsequence. Existing covered cases remain covered; a different presentation is not a new case. Conversely, a TV or generalized-Jones theorem cannot silently be substituted for the ordinary root specialization. The known parallel-belt even-zero family shows why these checks are mathematical requirements rather than bibliographical qualifications.

No external proof techniques were used. The theorem’s own proof remains the exact source/coefficient identity, its global coefficient asymptotic, the uniform channel upper rate, and its absolute geometric normalization.

The comparison in Section 2 concerns the literal parallel-belt annulus/bigon and the even-level middle-face degree obstruction. It is not a verification of the external volume proofs.
