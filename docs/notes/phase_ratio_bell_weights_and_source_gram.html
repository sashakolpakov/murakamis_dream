Exact Bell weights and a source-balanced positive Gram

29 October 2026. The phase-ratio coefficient of the original conjugate source has one positive angle-energy profile, rather than its square. Its radius factors and normalization are explicit. A second result constructs a canonical positive Gram for the ACTUAL right/left Fermat frames: both source costs reduce to a rational chord sum, polynomial away from the normalized central divisor. With the original cap normalizers retained, the cost is O(1+|epsilon|logN) uniformly at every cap index. This supplies a quantitative special-inner-product input, not a bound on the interior original word.

1. Exact magnitude on the Fermat fiber

Use sigma=-1 and the actual normalized P,L and Bell basis of the source-exact reflection theorem. Let uN+vN=1 and assume the factors below are nonzero. Define

t=v/barv, a=baru/u,
A_j=|1-t Q^(-j)|,
B_j=|1-a Q^j|,
C_j=|1-at Q^j|.

The two original source equations give BOTH recurrences

|c_(s,r+1)|/|c_(s,r)|=A_r/(|u| C_(s-r)),
|c_(s,r)|/|c_(s-1,r)|=|v| C_(s-r)/B_s.        (1)

For n in Z define Cprod(0)=1 and Cprod(n)/Cprod(n-1)=C_n, using reciprocal products for n<0. Put

f_(s,r)=|u|^(-r)|v|^s
      [product_(j=0)^(r-1) A_j] Cprod(s-r)
                         /[product_(j=1)^s B_j],
              0<=s,r<N.                     (2)

Empty products equal one. Starting at (0,0), (1) proves

|c_(s,r)|=kappa_N f_(s,r),
kappa_N=||P||_2^2/[sum_(s,r) f_(s,r)^2]^(1/2).              (3)

This determines the normalization by a specified positive finite sum, with no unknown knot period. Indeed the Bell transform is unitary and its input is P tensor barP, so sum|c|2=||P||4. The all-nonzero theorem on the open positive-central fiber justifies normalizing at (0,0). Other nonsingular root marks have the same formula with their actual chord factors, not principal arguments silently substituted.

On the PRINCIPAL positive-central component, kappa_N=c_(0,0)>0 also fixes the scalar phase. The defining sum for c_(0,0) is real by pairing a with -a. It never vanishes on this connected component by (1), and as U=u^N tends to zero in the upper half-plane the principal roots give P_a->1 and c_(0,0)->sqrtN. Hence its sign is positive everywhere there. A different root mark must keep its own phase; this argument does not silently identify those marks.

If the actual column is Gamma P, multiply c and kappa_N by |Gamma|^2. No Gamma, inverse-frame normalization or cap residue factor has been removed. The radii |u|,|v| in (2) may depend arbitrarily on N. Formula (3) remains exact and does not declare kappa_N or those radius factors subexponential.

2. Three shifted positive factorials, and both chambers

For a Frobenius center U=u^N in the upper half-plane, take the principal marked roots and write

alpha=Arg(U)/pi, beta=-Arg(1-U)/pi,
gamma=alpha+beta,
0<alpha,beta,gamma<1.

Define the positive shifted factorial

T_N(L;delta)=product_(j=1)^L 2sin[pi(j+delta)/N],
-1<delta<0, 0<=L<=N.

Every displayed sine is positive. Set

A_full=|1-t^N|, C_full=|1-(at)^N|.

Reflection of the numerator arcs in (2) gives the exact formulas

r>s:
|c_(s,r)|=kappa_N A_full |u|^(-r)|v|^s
   /[T_N(N-r;-beta) T_N(s;-alpha)
                            T_N(r-s;gamma-1)],              (4)

r<=s:
|c_(s,r)|=kappa_N A_full C_full |u|^(-r)|v|^s
   /[T_N(N-r;-beta) T_N(s;-alpha)
                            T_N(N-s+r;gamma-1)].            (5)

Proof. A_j=2sin[pi(j+beta)/N], B_j=2sin[pi(j-alpha)/N]. For r>s the reciprocal negative-index Cprod gives the factors 2sin[pi(j+gamma-1)/N], j=1,…,r-s. Replace the A numerator by its complete product divided by the remaining arc, and reflect that arc. This proves (4). For r<=s also complement the positive C numerator by its complete product; the same reflection proves (5).

The three lengths sum to N in (4), and their shifts sum to -1. On the STRICT chamber 0<s<r<N all three shifted endpoints are positive and sum to d=N-1. Thus (4) has ONE angle-energy weight, not a square-root amplitude weight and not its square. In (5) the lengths sum to 2N and the shifted endpoints sum to 2N-1. Boundary rows, including s=0, retain their literal empty products; a negative formal shifted endpoint is not declared a geometric angle. The full half-phase/sign accounting is in the reflection theorem and is not replaced by choosing the favorable chamber.

For shifts in a fixed compact subset of (-1,0), lengths at most N-1, these T_N factors differ from the unshifted s_N factors by polynomial factors: separate the first factor and integrate the logarithmic derivative, bounded by the harmonic sums at the two root endpoints. If a shift approaches a divisor, retain its explicit factor. If L=N, T_N(N;delta)=|1-exp(2pi i delta)|, not the forbidden zero s_N(N). No uniform polynomial comparison through arbitrary exponentially close divisors is inferred.

In particular, U fixed and u=U^(1/N) is only one parameter regime. A fixed semiclassical radius |u|!=1 has an exponentially varying Frobenius center. Equations (2)–(5) retain that regime rather than identifying it with the fixed-center estimate.

3. An explicit non-diagonal Bell Cauchy metric

For the full positive array f in (2), give an exterior array b the norm squared sum f_(s,r)|b_(s,r)|^2. Its original source functional sum c_(s,r)b_(s,r) has exact Riesz norm squared

sum |c_(s,r)|^2/f_(s,r)=kappa_N^2 sum f_(s,r).                (6)

Thus an explicit positive Gram exists in the Bell space; in the original doubled head coordinates it is a conjugate of diag(f) and is generally nondiagonal. It retains ALL sectors. The phase choice b=barc/|c| attains the corresponding weighted Cauchy bound, so (6) is a sharp local all-exterior statement.

The coefficient c already belongs to a DOUBLED amplitude. Squaring its contraction again proves a fourth-power statement, not the desired |K|^2 estimate. Direct triangle comparison instead reads

|sum c b|<=kappa_N sum f |b|.                 (7)

If the actual remaining contraction has |b| bounded polynomially and the actual edge projector is diagonal in this SAME joint basis, (4)–(7) supply a one-power positive partition comparison. Those conditions on the remaining original contraction are not proved here. Introducing a dephasing or discarding (5) does not establish them. Normalization (6) is also explicit rather than assumed tame.

4. A stronger Gram balances the actual source AND co-source

There is a useful quantitative alternative which does not require the Fermat equation. For the literal P,L in either orientation, when u^N!=1 and v!=0, finite cancellation gives

L_a P_a=(1-u)/(1-u Q_s^a).

Define

H_a=|L_a|/|P_a|>0,
U_a=(L_aP_a)/|L_aP_a|.

Then EXACTLY

L=P^* H U, U unitary diagonal,
P^*H P=L H^(-1)L^*
   =S_N(u):=|1-u|sum_(a=0)^d 1/|1-u Q_s^a|.                (8)

This is a prescribed positive inner product, constructed from the known cyclic source frames, not from a knot value. For every specified exterior A,

|L A P|<=S_N(u)||A||_H.                      (9)

The phase U in (8) is compulsory; no positive pivotal trace is claimed. If the actual rows/columns are Gamma_L L,Gamma_R P, their balanced source norm squared is |Gamma_L Gamma_R|S_N. The Gamma product is part of (9), even when exponential.

There is an elementary radius-uniform estimate

S_N(u)<=2N^2 max(1,|u|^N)/|1-u^N|.            (10)

Indeed (1-uN)/(1-uQ_sa)=sum_(k=0)d(uQ_sa)^k. Its absolute value is at most N max(1,|u|^d). Sum over a and use |1-u|<=1+|u|. This proves (10) for arbitrary N-dependent radii. A polynomial lower bound on the NORMALIZED central separation |1-uN|/max(1,|u|N) makes this source cost polynomial. It is not a lower bound on that separation when a source pole approaches the contour.

On the doubled head, use H tensor H. The original doubled source and co-source both have norm squared S_N^2. In the unitary Bell basis its Gram is explicitly Bell^*(H tensor H)Bell, generally NONDIAGONAL. Formula (4) of the reflection theorem preserves the magnitudes of P,L, so this H is invariant under the source antiinvolution, and its compulsory co-source clock is unitary for H as well. No small-root or fixed-radius argument is needed.

The phase-ratio observables are unitary in the flat cyclic metric; they need not be unitary for H. Nor is a diagonal H at one quad chart automatically the diagonal metric at the next chart. Transporting this Gram and retaining its off-diagonal terms is essential. A flat diagonal edge projector need not be contractive in the transported source metric.

The selected corner-sector theorem proves a precise boundary exception: the doubled metric is scalar on the actual corner’s clock-sum sector for real cap parameters, and has polynomial condition number there throughout the complex cap disk. Orthogonal projectors restricted to that sector therefore have a tame norm. This does not discard the other regularized-cap sectors or prove that an interior crossing preserves the selected sector.

5. Polynomial cost at EVERY actual normalized cap

In the literal cap orientation Q_s=Q, put epsilon=v^N,

u=Q^(-j)(1-epsilon)^(1/N), |epsilon|<=rho=1/16.

For j>=1 use the original normalized frames

Phat_j=v^(N-j)P/alpha_j,
Lhat_j=v^j L/beta_j,
alpha_j beta_j=N(1-Q^(-j)).

For j=0 use P,L without those factors. Let Hhat_j be their balanced positive metric as in (8). Their source norm squared is

Shat_j=|epsilon| |1-u|/[N|1-Q^(-j)|]
                   sum_a1/|1-uQ^a|, j>=1,
Shat_0=S_N(u).                               (11)

Uniformly in N,j, and the marked punctured epsilon disk,

Shat_j<=C[1+|epsilon|(1+log N)],
lim_(epsilon->0)Shat_j=1.                    (12)

Proof. Write delta=(1-epsilon)^(1/N). The marked logarithm gives |delta-1|<=2|epsilon|/N. The resonant denominator is bounded below by |epsilon|/[N(1+rho)] from epsilon=(1-delta)sum_(k=0)^d delta^k. Every other denominator |1-delta Q^k| is at least 3min(k,N-k)/N. Their reciprocal sum is O(N(1+logN)). For j>=1, |1-u|/|1-Q^(-j)|<=2, giving (12). For j=0 the resonant term in S_N is exactly one and the others have the same bound. The resonant term tends to one in every case and the other terms vanish. This proof keeps the actual alpha_j,beta_j, whose separate sizes need not be polynomial.

Independent input/output caps may use different Hhat metrics. Their original matrix element is bounded by the geometric mean of these source costs times the operator norm from Hhat_in to Hhat_out. Tensor products preserve this statement. The actual corner source is obtained by Cauchy evaluation of Phat,Lhat at zero; on |v|=rho^(1/N), each normalized dv/v circle has mass one. Thus these cap source factors cost only polynomially in the original whole residue presentation. The INTERIOR word norm between those metrics, and all additional retained scalar lines, remain to be bounded. This does not turn (12) into a knot-volume bound by itself.

6. Off-Fermat/current packets are not erased

Let Delta=1-uN-vN and F_d=product_(a=1)d(1-uQ(-a)). The normalized column satisfies B P=Delta e_0/F_d even off the Fermat curve. Its exact Bell equations acquire

c_sr-u c_(s,r+1)-vQ^(-r)c_(s-1,r)=h_s,
c_sr-baruQ^s c_(s,r+1)-barv c_(s-1,r)=k_sr,
h_s=(Delta/F_d)barP_s/sqrtN,
k_sr=(barDelta/barF_d)Q^(-rs)P_s/sqrtN.

Consequently the recurrence behind (1) is replaced by

[u-t baru Q^(s-r)]c_(s,r+1)
   =[1-tQ^(-r)]c_sr-h_s+tQ^(-r)k_sr.          (13)

Variation of constants retains the finite sum of both displayed sources, with the actual cyclic closure determinant. Resonant closure does not select a free homogeneous coefficient. Thus (2)–(5) are exact Fermat-source formulas; extending them to a complete inhomogeneous beta packet requires the currents (13), not setting their central Delta to zero. The positive metric (8)–(10) itself remains exact off the Fermat locus.
