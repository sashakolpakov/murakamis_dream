An initialized coideal period has a flat abelian framing

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The actual coideal crossing has a canonical polynomial column away from its physical roots. Its endpoint defect selects one source, and the original marked row selects one unit residue of that source. Changing the neighboring height gives an explicit commuting unipotent comparison, preserving both the source and the marked row. All statements are finite algebra at the specified level. No knot peripheral variable or geometric selected cycle is identified.

3. Primitive cyclicity over the cyclotomic lattice

Put 𝒪N=ℤ[q,1/(2N)]⊂ℂ\mathcal O_N=\mathbb Z[\sqrt q,1/(2N)]\subset\mathbb C. All the displayed roots, phases and matrix entries lie in this ring. 5. Exact commuting comparison under a neighboring-height change

6. Scope and prior coverage

K already supplies polynomial coideal kernels and their endpoint normalization. N supplies the marked cap-slide row and its exact scalar; T supplies the actual crossing and inverse frame. C supplies a different scalar quantum connection on the finite height clock, its initialized line and a specified off-cut rational representative. None of its moving-variable or contour conclusions is being extended here.

F’s cyclic comparison principle is realized explicitly by (11),(21) for this finite neighboring-height family. Its fixed-rank whole quantum-lifting hypotheses are not inferred: the present rank is NN. Nor is the neighboring-height coordinate a preferred knot meridian. For example Nσ(z)=det⁡(I+q3/2zMσ)N_\sigma(z)=\det(I+q^{3/2}zM_\sigma), where Mσ=(σ/q−110)M_\sigma=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\sigma/\sqrt q&-1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right); this identifies the explicit local Fricke matrix, not a knot A-component.

Every original root column and the marked functional are retained. The off-root pNp_N forcing, the other polynomial zeros in (15), and any literal boundary and exterior scalars remain. No Bloch-regulator identification, geometric contour coefficient, or arbitrary-short-filling scalar lower bound follows from this source-algebraic period and its flat abelian framing.
