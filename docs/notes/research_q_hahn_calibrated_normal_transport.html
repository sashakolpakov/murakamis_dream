Calibrated transport and the actual q-Hahn normal amplitude

Written proof, 24 November 2026.

The actual paired q-Hahn normal has a nonzero leading amplitude on each fixed strict physical compact when its scaled channel separation is sufficiently small. The allowed separation is a fixed positive number depending on that compact. This improves the shrinking-window upper bound to an amplitude theorem for the original generic derivative.

The proof uses its two exact source equations, an analytic inverse with a controlled remainder, and one small original physical prefix. A convergent closed-walk expansion computes the prefix trace and fixes the normalization constant. No boundary uniformity is inferred from an interior compact estimate. The conclusion is a local packet theorem, not a lower bound for the original closed knot source.
