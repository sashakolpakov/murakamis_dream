The nilpotent Mobius action is not the actual framing Ward action

28 October 2026. A common fractional-linear change of the charged nilpotent anchors is an exact algebra automorphism. Its effect on the ORIGINAL source is computable. However, it does not provide a second geometric framing integral: invariance of a homogeneous top-degree state is automatic, while the ordinary common action fails covariance with an ORIGINAL crossing. Its source-preserving weighted lift also has the wrong nilpotent Jordan type to be the actual Hopf current on a head.

This rules out that specific identification. It does not rule out a coupled quantum dressing which transforms clocks, crossings and source currents together, or the independent positive selection theorem for a network already satisfying its stated hypotheses.

1. Exact automorphism and source Jacobian

Put d=N-1 and A_N=K[X]/(X^N), in characteristic zero. The source is the ORIGINAL functional lambda(P)=[X^d]P. For a!=0 define

phi_(a,c)(X)=aX/(1+cX) mod X^N.                    (1)

The inverse is X/(a-cX), so this is an algebra automorphism. Its SOURCE formula, valid for EVERY polynomial exterior, is

lambda(P(phi_(a,c)(X)))
      =lambda(P(X) J_(a,c)(X)),
J_(a,c)(X)=a(a-cX)^(N-2).                        (2)

Proof. In the specified residue lambda=Res P(X)dX/X^N, make the formal invertible change Y=aX/(1+cX). Then X=Y/(a-cY) and

dX/X^N=a(a-cY)^(N-2)dY/Y^N.

This proves (2) with its exact scalar and exponent. For k anchors the source factor is the product of the k displayed J factors. At a=1, its infinitesimal identity is

lambda(-X^2 partial_X P)=-(N-2)lambda(XP).         (3)

Thus ordinary Mobius substitution does NOT preserve the original source on arbitrary exteriors. The coefficient norm of its source multiplier at a=1 is exactly

||(1-cX)^(N-2)||_1=(1+|c|)^(N-2).                (4)

At fixed nonzero c this is exponential in N. This is a norm statement about the full normal source, not a claim that the original scalar acquires that factor.

2. Why homogeneous-source invariance is automatic

For k original charged anchors let Omega=product_i X_i^d. The top homogeneous piece of A_N^(tensor k) has degree kd and is the ONE line K Omega. Every actual whole-source P_h is homogeneous of that degree before projection, including after the proved closure contractions.

Under common (1), each variable starts with aX_i and all remaining terms have strictly higher degree. Since no degree above kd survives,

phi_(a,c)(P_h)=a^(kd) P_h in A_N^(tensor k).       (5)

In particular c-invariance at a=1 holds for EVERY top-degree state, whether its selected coefficient is nonzero or zero. The induced c-action on this socle line is trivial. It therefore contains no information identifying a geometric relative period or selecting a nonzero second framing fiber. This is the same original homogeneous socle, not a substituted favorable source.

It remains possible to retain lower-degree normal classes and the full Jacobian (2). Their covariance must actually be proved; (5) cannot replace it.

3. The exact source-preserving lift has the wrong Ward generator

There is a specified weighted action preserving lambda on ALL states:

U_(a,c)P(X)=a^(-d)(1+cX)^(N-2)P(phi_(a,c)(X)).    (6)

Indeed (2), or direct substitution in it, gives lambda(U P)=lambda(P). At a=1 its infinitesimal generator is

L=(N-2)X-X^2 partial_X,
L X^j=(N-2-j)X^(j+1).                           (7)

For N>=3 its Jordan blocks are N-1 and 1: it acts in one full chain on 1,X,…,X^(N-2), and kills the separate socle X^d. At N=2 it is zero. The UNWEIGHTED substitution generator -X^2 partial_X likewise has blocks N-1 and 1, with the separate constant line instead.

The ORIGINAL complete quantum E and F on one physical head have a single Jordan block of size N. For F its consecutive coefficients in the actual balanced basis are 1-Q^(j+1), 0<=j<=N-2, all nonzero. The corresponding E coefficients are also nonzero along their whole chain. Consequently no invertible head gauge conjugates (7), or its unweighted version, to the actual E or F. Nilpotent Jordan type is unchanged by any such gauge.

One can instead use the ordinary coherent weight (1+cX)^d, obtaining the regular classical generator dX-X^2 partial_X. But then the source is NOT fixed; exactly

lambda((1+cX)^d P(X/(1+cX)))
       =lambda(P(X)/(1-cX)).                    (8)

Moreover the primitive two-head coherent generator has nilpotency index 2N-1, whereas the actual root Hopf coproduct has its N-th power zero. Thus even this alternative is not a factorwise identification with the actual quantum tensor action. Its source and tensor adapter would have to be constructed and retained separately.

4. A literal original crossing fails common Mobius covariance

This is also visible without a representation classification. Let B be the ORIGINAL positive checked crossing in the actual balanced factorial basis and retain its common Cartan scalar kappa. Complete color means x=-q^(-1), Q=q^2, hence x^(-2)=Q. Its k=0 entries give

[|0,1>] B |1,0>=kappa x^(-1),
[|0,2>] B |2,0>=kappa x^(-2).                   (9)

For N>=3 put D=-X^2 partial_X and D_tot=D_1+D_2. Then

[|0,2>] [B,D_tot] |1,0>
                   =kappa(x^(-1)-x^(-2)) !=0.   (10)

To see the two terms, D_tot|1,0>=-|2,0>; after B only its k=0 term can yield |0,2>. Conversely B|1,0> has |0,1> coefficient in (9), and D_tot sends it to -|0,2>. This proves (10), with no lost factorial, source normalization or Cartan scalar. At the actual half-root x^(-1)=-q and x(-2)=q2, so the difference is -q(1+q), nonzero for every N>=3.

Therefore the common ordinary Mobius action cannot be slid through the unchanged actual crossing. Full E/F Ward identities do not supply that missing covariance, since they concern the different Hopf action in Section 3. At N=2 the unweighted Mobius substitution is already the identity on K[X]/X^2 and supplies no framing direction at all.

5. Consequence for the proposed second framing fiber

The nilpotent source and closure contractions are exact. The ordinary common Mobius changes do not add a selected nonzero fiber to them. On the original top-degree state they act trivially by (5). On general exteriors they require the source multiplier (2), and on the fixed actual network they have the explicit covariance defect (10).

This leaves a precise constructive possibility: a COUPLED quantum framing transformation, including the underclock feedback, Hopf tensor factors and boundary co-source, might have a controlled nonzero relative fiber. It cannot be identified with the common automorphism (1) merely from nilpotent-source invariance. The existing warning about ordinary coherent Mobius action versus actual quantum dressing in the Springer boundary note is consistent with, and now has the explicit selected-source and crossing calculations (2)-(10).

No broad knot-volume lower bound or nonzero global geometric coefficient is proved by this test.

Audit: exact source Jacobians and inverse maps, both weighted source actions, their Jordan indices, original quantum E/F chains and the full original crossing witness (10), with kappa retained.
