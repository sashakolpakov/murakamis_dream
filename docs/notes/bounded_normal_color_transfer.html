The actual complete period is a bounded-source first-normal response

Research proof and independent source test, 27 October 2026. The ordinary-color bound and the normal boundary factor give an exact first-normal descriptor system for every knot. On a regular companion chart this becomes a fixed-rank affine transfer, with the same characteristic curve as the actual normal operator. The forcing is polynomially bounded; the complete value is its specified normal cut.

This goes beyond regarding the complete value as an unspecified large entry of a Gram inverse. It does not prove that the response excites the geometric branch. Section 3 constructs a coherent source-wall change which preserves any prescribed finite prefix of actual colors, Weyl symmetry and the ordinary-color bounds, but kills complete growth and moves the original contribution to arbitrarily high normal order.

2. Fixed-rank transfer and its precise singular-step qualification

Where pj(qN)≠0p_j(q_N)\ne0, put Aj=𝒩j/pj. A_j=\mathcal N_j/p_j. Then (7) becomes the exact rank-dd affine equation xj+1=Ajxj,yj+1=Ajyj+Ȧjxj. x_{j+1}=A_jx_j,\qquad y_{j+1}=A_jy_j+\dot A_jx_j. \tag{11} Here Ȧjxj=edγj/pj\dot A_jx_j=e_d\gamma_j/p_j; the divided normal source retains the same single channel. Equivalently the doubled column has rank-2d2d transport (xj+1yj+1)=(Aj0ȦjAj)(xjyj). \binom{x_{j+1}}{y_{j+1}} =\begin{pmatrix}A_j&0\\ \dot A_j&A_j\end{pmatrix} \binom{x_j}{y_j}. \tag{12} All its input is fixed small-color pointing.

For J=N−d+1J=N-d+1, define Φ(k,j)=Ak−1⋯Aj\Phi(k,j)=A_{k-1}\cdots A_j. The exact selected response is 2KN=edt[Φ(J,j0)yj0+∑j=j0J−1Φ(J,j+1)Ȧjxj]. 2K_N=e_d^t\left[ \Phi(J,j_0)y_{j_0} +\sum_{j=j_0}^{J-1}\Phi(J,j+1)\dot A_jx_j \right]. \tag{13} This is an explicit source coupling, rather than a choice of a largest characteristic root. An adjoint row λj=λj+1Aj,λJ=edt\lambda_j=\lambda_{j+1}A_j,\ \lambda_J=e_d^t gives the equivalent formula 2KN=λj0yj0+∑jλj+1Ȧjxj. 2K_N=\lambda_{j_0}y_{j_0} +\sum_j\lambda_{j+1}\dot A_jx_j. \tag{14}

If the nonzero pj(qN)p_j(q_N) have a uniform polynomial lower bound, then the divided coefficients and the forcing in (11) have polynomial bounds too. A simple sufficient condition is that bd(1,Y)b_d(1,Y) have no zeros on the unit circle. In that case they are uniformly bounded apart from the already bounded xjx_j. This is a checkable operator condition, not asserted for every primitive normal operator.

The transfer has no new classical characteristic actions: det⁡(LI−(Aj0ȦjAj))=det⁡(LI−Aj)2. \det\!\left(LI- \begin{pmatrix}A_j&0\\\dot A_j&A_j\end{pmatrix}\right) =\det(LI-A_j)^2. \tag{15} On a regular limiting chart j/N→u,Y=e2πiuj/N\to u,\ Y=e^{2\pi iu}, the off-diagonal block tends to 2uY∂YAcl(Y)2uY\partial_YA_{\mathrm{cl}}(Y). Thus the first-normal extension doubles the characteristic of the actual operator; it does not introduce a new regulator action. Identification of a geometric factor and excitation of its marked branch are further assertions.

At a zero of pj(qN)p_j(q_N), (13) is understood only through its whole meromorphic germ and cancellation. The descriptor equation (7) stays valid, but its first-normal solutions need not have a unique cut. Higher lifts can select yjy_j. The existing bounded jet budget, applied to BB, supplies a uniform finite procedure: to recover this first derivative, retain the total pole budget plus one input jet. It does not justify deleting singular steps or replacing their actual lattices by (11) at a zero.

Consequently the unconditional theorem is (7)–(10). A regular fixed-rank affine transfer is its stated chartwise strengthening. Bounded forcing alone gives no exponential lower bound for (13); the source-weighted Green response is the quantity to estimate.

3. A coherent source-wall change can hide growth to any normal order

Here is an exact obstruction to deducing the selected growth from ordinary-color bounds, a geometric localized factor and a finite prefix of color pointing. It is not a new knot invariant.

Start with the actual family of any knot. Fix positive integers J,mJ,m, and put X=Y+Y−1,ar(q)=q2r+q−2r,tJ(q,X)=∏r=1J(X−ar(q)), X=Y+Y^{-1},\quad a_r(q)=q^{2r}+q^{-2r},\quad t_J(q,X)=\prod_{r=1}^J(X-a_r(q)), RJ(q,Y)=tJ(q,X)tJ(q,2),hJ,m(q,Y)=(1−RJ(q,Y))m. R_J(q,Y)=\frac{t_J(q,X)}{t_J(q,2)},\qquad h_{J,m}(q,Y)=(1-R_J(q,Y))^m . \tag{16} For N>JN>J, define the whole root function and generic colors by F̃N(Y)=1+hJ,m(qN,Y)(FN(Y)−1), \widetilde F_N(Y) =1+h_{J,m}(q_N,Y)(F_N(Y)-1), C̃j(q)=1+hJ,m(q,q2j)(Cj(q)−1). \widetilde C_j(q) =1+h_{J,m}(q,q^{2j})(C_j(q)-1). \tag{17} The denominator in (16) is nonzero at every such qNq_N.

These families have the following exact properties.

  1. Their first JJ generic colors are literally unchanged, since RJ(q,q2j)=0R_J(q,q^{2j})=0 for 1≤j≤J1\le j\le J.
  2. Every C̃j(q)\widetilde C_j(q) is Laurent polynomial and C̃j(1)=1\widetilde C_j(1)=1.
  3. They satisfy all ordinary-color identities and also complete integral-color compatibility: F̃N(QNj)=C̃j(qN),C̃N(qN)=F̃N(1)=1. \widetilde F_N(Q_N^j)=\widetilde C_j(q_N),\qquad \widetilde C_N(q_N)=\widetilde F_N(1)=1.
  4. Reciprocity is preserved. All ordinary values remain polynomially bounded uniformly in j<Nj<N.
  5. At each level, F̃N(Y)−1\widetilde F_N(Y)-1 vanishes to order at least 2m2m at Y=1Y=1, regardless of how large FN(1)F_N(1) is.

For the only nonobvious algebraic claim in item 2, write Q=q2Q=q^2. For j>Jj>J, direct factorization gives RJ(q,q2j)=(−1)JQ−jJ+J(J+1)/2[j−1J]Q[j+JJ]Q. R_J(q,q^{2j}) =(-1)^JQ^{-jJ+J(J+1)/2} {j-1\brack J}_Q\,{j+J\brack J}_Q. \tag{18} Both Gaussian coefficients are polynomials. Formula (18) follows from aj−ar=Q−j(1−Qj−r)(1−Qj+r),2−ar=−Q−r(1−Qr)2. a_j-a_r=Q^{-j}(1-Q^{j-r})(1-Q^{j+r}),\quad 2-a_r=-Q^{-r}(1-Q^r)^2 . For j≤Jj\le J the numerator is zero. The apparent pole at q=1q=1 therefore cancels in every fixed color, proving item 2.

For item 4, the numerator of RJ(qN,Y)R_J(q_N,Y) is bounded by 4J4^J on the unit circle, whereas its denominator has modulus ∏r=1J4sin⁡2(πr/N)≫JN−2J\prod_{r=1}^J4\sin^2(\pi r/N)\gg_JN^{-2J}. Thus |C̃j(qN)|=OJ,m(Nb−1+2Jm). |\widetilde C_j(q_N)| =O_{J,m}(N^{b-1+2Jm}). In fact the same reasoning bounds the multiplier by a fixed power of the quantum dimension [j]qN[j]_{q_N}: use sin⁡2(πj/N)/sin⁡2(πr/N)=[j]2/[r]2\sin^2(\pi j/N)/\sin^2(\pi r/N)=[j]^2/[r]^2. Thus it is not merely a loss of all semisimple-type polynomial control.

For item 5, 1−RJ(q,Y)1-R_J(q,Y) vanishes at X=2X=2, and X−2=(Y−1)2/Y. X-2=(Y-1)^2/Y. The original complete growth has therefore been moved beyond any prescribed finite number of normal jets, while the new complete period is identically one. Choosing the actual figure-eight family, whose positive exponential growth was proved separately, makes this a concrete growth-changing example. More precisely, set cN,J=∑r=1J12−ar(qN)≍JN2. c_{N,J}=\sum_{r=1}^J\frac1{2-a_r(q_N)} \asymp_J N^2 . All summands are positive at these roots. The first possibly nonzero coefficient is exactly [(Y−1)2m](F̃N(Y)−1)=(−cN,J)m(KN−1). [(Y-1)^{2m}](\widetilde F_N(Y)-1) =(-c_{N,J})^m(K_N-1). \tag{19} Thus its exponential rate is the original one, with a specified polynomial multiplier; the contribution has not merely been discarded from the entire normal tower.

The construction is still uniformly q-holonomic: multiplication by the fixed rational function hJ,m(q,Y)h_{J,m}(q,Y), and addition of the constant section, give a fixed rational all-level operator. To see this directly, if PF=0P F=0, then P(F−1)=−P1P(F-1)=-P1; a first-order operator annihilates the rational right side. Conjugate the resulting homogeneous operator by hJ,mh_{J,m}, and apply the same argument once more to add one. Clear its fixed denominators afterward.

Away from hJ,m=0h_{J,m}=0, its nonconstant component is rationally gauge equivalent to the original one. Hence its original geometric localized factor and characteristic action survive; an added constant component contributes at most an abelian factor. The comparison fails to be a unit exactly on the complete source wall. This is the source-lattice defect, not a failure of color coherence.

The example does not preserve the entire actual knot pointing or its full operator. In particular it does not contradict the exact all-level reconstruction theorem, which fixes those data together. It proves that the geometric localized A-factor, ordinary-color bounds, reciprocity, all-level color compatibility and any preassigned finite prefix alone do not force complete growth or first-normal geometric coupling.

4. What the simplification supplies

The actual period has the exact bounded-source descriptor (7) and cut (8). On a regular normal chart, (13) is a fixed-rank scalar response whose characteristic is the actual A-symbol, doubled by the normal extension. This isolates the desired norm problem in an actual source coupling without positive entrywise crossing estimates.

The next substantive assertion would be a geometric, source-preserving estimate for that coupling, through its singular lattice charts. Classical etaleness does not establish it: (16)–(18) preserve the localized characteristic and hide its contribution at the wall. The required regular pointed comparison must distinguish that change.
