A supplementary normal-fiber calculation for the exact boundary bridge

Private written calculation, 14 November 2026. This records one concrete consequence of the prescribed-cut recovery. It is not a new general lower-bound theorem. The closing stability-radius theorem already gives the abstract obstruction under arbitrary linear constraints; the calculation here identifies the relevant fiber inside the unit group of the actual full Hermite algebra.

Use 𝒜N=ℂ[z]/(PN)\mathcal A_N=\mathbb C[z]/(P_N), PN(z)=2TN(z/2)+2P_N(z)=2T_N(z/2)+2, and its distinct double nodes cc. Put ac=4−c2≠0a_c=4-c^2\ne0. This includes h=Rnh=R^n for every fixed integer nn.

These changed coupons are not asserted to satisfy the Ward equations, endpoint pointing, or fixed-expression constraints of an actual short tangle. The calculation thus blocks an inference from unit spectral data alone; it makes no assertion about the coefficient of a fixed hyperbolic knot source.

The existing positive criteria remain the source-adapted composition criterion, the joint analytic sewing criterion, and transport of the original top relative cycle. The exact external/Newton bridge transports their maps and prescribed observations, but does not establish their source overlap, transverse joint saddle, nonzero original contour intersection, or total complementary-current gap for arbitrary fixed short components. No stronger lower-bound implication is claimed here.
