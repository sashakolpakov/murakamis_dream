The original trace on a path with large signed powers

Research note, 3 November 2026.

For every fixed finite positive path with crossing powers at least twenty, all the mixed vertical terms of a central trace rectangle have a strict gap below the selected geometric action. The gap comes from one inequality at each vertex where a vertical term occurs. Its constants do not depend on the length of the path.

Sections 4–6 compare every complementary original row and every mixed current with the selected action when the signed powers have absolute value at least sixty-four. The full thin-box kernel theorem supplies the required uniform bound and nonzero expansion. Section 7 retains one outside angle and proves its whole-period and complex-strip estimates. Sections 8–9 give the stronger local comparisons used for powers seven and eight in the full path-source theorem. The proved wide-kernel bound now supplies their finite-source input.
