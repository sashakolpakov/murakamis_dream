The actual soft-binomial cut: cycle, action and midpoint critical scheme

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The binomial cut has an exact finite-source cycle and a definite principal boundary action. The latter changes one cut-gluing equation. Conservation of the full classical coproduct then identifies its regular principal critical scheme with the braid fixed-point scheme at the midpoint. This scheme contains the complete geometric representation for every fixed finite braid of a hyperbolic knot. The residual geometric centralizer remains.

The complete proof sources are S (the binomial cut), C (the compact finite coherent calculus), M (the literal braid/holonomy correspondence), and G (the regular midpoint frame). Their pins are below. The stationary statement concerns the principal logarithmic equations of the actual weighted occupation-state array on its regular charts. Its critical scheme uses the exponentiated rows, retaining their logarithmic sheets; it does not select an additive Fourier charge. It does not assert a uniform phase expansion for a growing full coherent kernel, a sum-to-integral deformation, or a thimble coefficient.

6. Scope and complete proof-source pins

The finite cycle, literal principal boundary action and regular principal critical scheme are now fixed. The geometric midpoint is present without an alternating or long-twist hypothesis. All source walls, pivots, framing and marked currents remain. Analytic phase/amplitude control of the full coherent network, the geometric centralizer reduction, its relative cycle and a nonzero physical geometric coefficient remain unproved.
