Polynomial Ward transport to the literal top source

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

The original highest-cut pivotal source differs from a specified top matrix coefficient by an explicit two-head EE-current. Its primitive has norm O(N)O(N) at the root and polynomial norm, including all fixed true meridian jets, on a scaled meridian disk. The operator exponent does not grow with the number of spectator heads. No inverse of the full commutator operator is used.

The sole complete direct F supplies the actual finite head matrices, ordered coproduct, original pointed pivot, and meridian diagonal frame. The primitive, top coupon, source identity, and bounds below are proved inline. All norms below use the physical coefficient Euclidean metric: the displayed vectors waw_a are orthonormal, with tensor-product metrics. No estimate in a different coherent or binomial metric is inferred. F’s diagonal conditioning bounds are unchanged in this metric.

4. Polynomial collar bounds with explicit dimension costs

Fix 0<η<η′<π0<\eta<\eta'<\pi, with mm fixed. F’s complete whole-cap estimate gives, uniformly for 0≤b<N0\le b<N and |Nμ|≤η′|N\mu|\le\eta', (N+1)−Cη′≤|hb(eμ)|≤(N+1)Cη′.(23) (N+1)^{-C_{\eta'}}\le|h_b(e^\mu)|\le(N+1)^{C_{\eta'}}. \qquad\text{(23)} The matrices and their inverses are holomorphic on this disk. The Cartan factors M−J1M^{-J_1} and M−dM^{-d} in (19) have bounded modulus and inverse there. Thus one may take Pη′=1+4Cη′P_{\eta'}=1+4C_{\eta'}, increasing constants when necessary, such that sup|Nμ|≤η′∥𝒜12(eμ)∥≤Cη′(N+1)Pη′.(24) \sup_{|N\mu|\le\eta'}\|\mathcal A_{12}(e^\mu)\| \le C_{\eta'}(N+1)^{P_{\eta'}}. \qquad\text{(24)} The exponent is independent of mm. The spectator scalar has modulus at most e(m−2)η′e^{(m-2)\eta'}; it affects only the constant. Cauchy estimates on the buffered scaled disk consequently imply sup|Nμ|≤η∥∂μk𝒜(eμ)∥≤Cm,η,η′,kNk(N+1)Pη′(k≥0 fixed).(25) \sup_{|N\mu|\le\eta} \|\partial_\mu^k\mathcal A(e^\mu)\| \le C_{m,\eta,\eta',k}N^k(N+1)^{P_{\eta'}} \qquad(k\ge0\text{ fixed}). \qquad\text{(25)} Every derivative has range in the same subspace |0⟩⊗span⁡{w1,…,wd}⊗Z|0\rangle\otimes\operatorname{span}\{w_1,\ldots,w_d\}\otimes Z. Its rank is at most dNm−2dN^{m-2}. Therefore (25) gives ∥∂μk𝒜∥Sp≤(dNm−2)1/pCm,η,η′,kNk(N+1)Pη′.(26) \|\partial_\mu^k\mathcal A\|_{S_p} \le (dN^{m-2})^{1/p} C_{m,\eta,\eta',k}N^k(N+1)^{P_{\eta'}}. \qquad\text{(26)} At μ=0\mu=0, the spectator scalar has modulus one, and the sharper root estimates are ∥𝒜0∥≤N/2,∥𝒜0∥Sp≤(dNm−2)1/pN/2,∥𝒜0∥1≤Nm/2.(27) \|\mathcal A_0\|\le N/2,\qquad \|\mathcal A_0\|_{S_p}\le(dN^{m-2})^{1/p}N/2, \qquad \|\mathcal A_0\|_1\le N^m/2. \qquad\text{(27)} Also QtopQ_{\mathrm{ top}} has rank Nm−2N^{m-2} and norm |M|−(m−2)d|M|^{-(m-2)d}. For an analytic marked matrix C(M)C(M), write Y=[EΣ,C]Y=[E_\Sigma,C] and Δ=ΦM(C)−Tr⁡(QtopC)\Delta=\Phi_M(C)-\operatorname{Tr}(Q_{\mathrm{ top}}C). Trace duality gives the actual current bounds |Δ|≤Cm,η′(N+1)Pη′∥Y∥1,|Δ|≤Cm,η′Nm−1(N+1)Pη′∥Y∥,|Δ|≤Cm,η′N(m−1)/2(N+1)Pη′∥Y∥2.(28) \begin{split} |\Delta|&\le C_{m,\eta'}(N+1)^{P_{\eta'}}\|Y\|_1,\\ |\Delta|&\le C_{m,\eta'}N^{m-1}(N+1)^{P_{\eta'}}\|Y\|,\\ |\Delta|&\le C_{m,\eta'}N^{(m-1)/2}(N+1)^{P_{\eta'}}\|Y\|_2. \end{split}\qquad\text{(28)} At the root the respective factors may instead be taken as N/2N/2, Nm/2N^m/2, and (N/2)(dNm−2)1/2(N/2)(dN^{m-2})^{1/2}. For fixed kk, the product rule and (25) give, on the smaller disk, N−k|∂μkΔ|≤Cm,η,η′,k(N+1)Pη′∑ℓ=0kN−ℓ∥∂μℓY∥1.(29) N^{-k}|\partial_\mu^k\Delta| \le C_{m,\eta,\eta',k}(N+1)^{P_{\eta'}} \sum_{\ell=0}^kN^{-\ell}\|\partial_\mu^\ell Y\|_1. \qquad\text{(29)} The operator-norm version has the extra factor Nm−1N^{m-1}, and the Hilbert–Schmidt version the extra factor N(m−1)/2N^{(m-1)/2}. All quantities are full finite matrices in the stated metric. An O(N−k)O(N^{-k}) current can therefore suffice for an absolute error after paying the displayed powers; its order, scalar normalization, and the desired error scale must be checked. A small normalized coherent kernel is not by itself a bound for any norm in (28).

For example, suppose at the root that LN≠0L_N\ne0, Tr⁡(Qtop,0CN)=LN(1+o(1))\operatorname{Tr}(Q_{{\mathrm{ top}},0}C_N)=L_N(1+o(1)), and the actual trace-norm current satisfies ∥[EΣ,0,CN]∥1≤εN|LN|/N.(29a) \|[E_{\Sigma,0},C_N]\|_1\le\varepsilon_N|L_N|/N. \qquad\text{(29a)} Then (27) proves ΦM=1(CN)=LN(1+o(1)+O(εN))\Phi_{M=1}(C_N)=L_N(1+o(1)+O(\varepsilon_N)). In particular a bound |LN|N−2|L_N|N^{-2} for this current contributes relative error O(N−1)O(N^{-1}). The operator-norm version requires ∥[EΣ,0,CN]∥≤εN|LN|N−m\|[E_{\Sigma,0},C_N]\|\le\varepsilon_N|L_N|N^{-m}. Thus this comparison has polynomial, rather than exponential, norm loss. The assumed norm control is a substantial input; the statement does not assert it for a geometric localization or after a coherent contraction. An outside scalar multiplies both compared terms and retains its derivatives.

5. The true first normal and exact scope

For explicit first jets, set Σa,N=∑j=1aQj1−Qj,ℋ12=−2J1−J2−2ΣJ1,N−2ΣJ2,N.(30) \Sigma_{a,N}=\sum_{j=1}^a\frac{Q^j}{1-Q^j},\qquad \mathcal H_{12}=-2J_1-J_2-2\Sigma_{J_1,N}-2\Sigma_{J_2,N}. \qquad\text{(30)} F’s exact derivative of (16) gives ∂μ𝒟12(eμ)|0=ℋ12\left.\partial_\mu\mathcal D_{12}(e^\mu)\right|_0=\mathcal H_{12}. Differentiating (19),(21) in the actual fixed-qq family proves 𝒜1=([ℋ12,𝒜12,0]−(d+N(m−2))𝒜12,0)⊗KZ,0−N.(31) \mathcal A_1= \bigl([\mathcal H_{12},\mathcal A_{12,0}] -(d+N(m-2))\mathcal A_{12,0}\bigr)\otimes K_{Z,0}^{-N}. \qquad\text{(31)} Subscripts 0 and 1 denote value and ∂μ\partial_\mu at zero. The two pivot derivatives are Qpt,1=−d(m−1)Qpt,0Q_{{\mathrm{ pt}},1}=-d(m-1)Q_{{\mathrm{ pt}},0} and Qtop,1=−d(m−2)Qtop,0Q_{{\mathrm{ top}},1}=-d(m-2)Q_{{\mathrm{ top}},0}. Put Y0=[EΣ,0,C0],Y1=[EΣ,1,C0]+[EΣ,0,C1].(32) Y_0=[E_{\Sigma,0},C_0],\qquad Y_1=[E_{\Sigma,1},C_0]+[E_{\Sigma,0},C_1]. \qquad\text{(32)} The complete first-normal source identity is Tr⁡Qpt,0(C1−d(m−1)C0)=Tr⁡Qtop,0(C1−d(m−2)C0)+Tr⁡(𝒜1Y0+𝒜0Y1).(33) \begin{split} \operatorname{Tr}Q_{{\mathrm{ pt}},0}(C_1-d(m-1)C_0) &=\operatorname{Tr}Q_{{\mathrm{ top}},0}(C_1-d(m-2)C_0)\\ &\quad+\operatorname{Tr}(\mathcal A_1Y_0+\mathcal A_0Y_1). \end{split}\qquad\text{(33)} Higher fixed jets use (6) directly. They require only the corresponding actual derivatives of C,EΣ,𝒜C,E_\Sigma,\mathcal A, with no extra Laurent coefficient. Any retained outside scalar is differentiated as well. If C(M)C(M) commutes with EΣ(M)E_\Sigma(M) throughout the meridian family, all current terms vanish. Root-only commutation eliminates Y0Y_0 but does not eliminate Y1Y_1.

Thus the top coefficient agrees with the original source on actual total intertwiners, and for arbitrary marked matrices its discrepancy is one explicit polynomially bounded Ward functional. This is a source comparison, not an assertion that a chosen geometric window has a small current. It neither constructs such a window nor supplies a geometric relative-cycle coefficient, a relative phase comparison, or a nonzero closed-source lower bound. The separate opposite- polarization return is not identified with this two-head primitive. No new volume-conjecture case follows from these identities alone.
