The original localized spectral period has a nonzero unit-charge filling contribution

analytical theorem, 31 October 2026. This proves a relative asymptotic on an actual fixed real spectral cube, with its original Fourier charges, sine factors, parity and marked coefficient. It does not estimate the integral outside that cube or the remote charge sum.

The inputs are the full spectral numerator packet, the local charge comparison, and the exact period (13a) in the cross-cofactor source interface. The finite-root spectral identity is also recorded in the spectral total-derivative theorem.

1. Exact original normalization and the local quantity

Fix the original certified marked flat plat with RR ports. Put d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, and let nin_i be its actual nonzero integer crossing powers. In the long-twist convention ni=ϵi−2pin_i=\epsilon_i-2p_i. Define the ENTIRE original shared spectral numerator

SN(𝝈)=[∏fordinaryLfd]∏iHVN(σi)|Lu=Lv=0. S_N(\boldsymbol\sigma)=\left[\prod_{f\ \mathrm{ordinary}}L_f^d\right] \left.\prod_i H_{V_N(\sigma_i)}\right|_{L_u=L_v=0}.

The original fixed-root Fourier/Hermite period is exactly

ID,𝒏=qd24∑ini(−i)R∑ℓ∈(ℤ\{0})R(∏iℓi)(−1)(N+1)∑iℓi×∫[−1/2,1/2]R[∏isin(2πδi)]SN(−2cos(2π𝜹))exp⁡{N∑i[πiniδi2+2πiℓiδi]}d𝜹. \begin{aligned} I_{D,\mathbf n} ={}&q^{\frac{d^2}{4}\sum_i n_i}(-i)^R \sum_{\boldsymbol\ell\in(\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\})^R} \left(\prod_i\ell_i\right)(-1)^{(N+1)\sum_i\ell_i}\\ &\quad\times\int_{[-1/2,1/2]^R} \left[\prod_i\sin(2\pi\delta_i)\right] S_N\!\left(-2\cos(2\pi\boldsymbol\delta)\right) \exp\!\left\{N\sum_i\left[\pi i n_i\delta_i^2+2\pi i\ell_i\delta_i\right]\right\} \,d\boldsymbol\delta . \end{aligned}\tag{1}

Here ID,𝒏I_{D,\mathbf n} is the RAW original marked contraction. If the final KNK_N convention is KN=χD,NID,𝒏K_N=\chi_{D,N}I_{D,\mathbf n}, its already recorded external character multiplies every localized formula below as well. The complete-pointing normalization, especially sections 1 and 3, retains the reduced framing character (modulus one) and the enhanced component sign; a full one-cut modified ADO convention has the additional iN+3/Ni^{N+3}/N factor. None is silently absorbed into our raw amplitude. The actual final-diagram framing/component character must be used, rather than the augmented diagram’s component count. Any such separately recorded scalar is still outside (1). In particular (1) has no additional NRN^R, mark, spectral square-root Jacobian or division by a quantum dimension. The displayed qq power is the literal unwrapped scalar exp⁡[πid2(∑ini)/(4N)]\exp[\pi i d^2(\sum_i n_i)/(4N)]. Charge zero is absent by the exact total-derivative identity, not discarded by an asymptotic argument. The full finite-NN charge series in (1) is absolutely convergent; no uniform-in-NN summability is used below.

Choose b>0b>0 within the fixed complex neighborhood of the full packet. For a charge ℓ\boldsymbol\ell let JN,ℓJ_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} be the integral in (1) restricted to the CLOSED real cube Kb=[−b,b]RK_b=[-b,b]^R. All local charge contributions below include the factors preceding that integral in (1). This is a sharp restriction of the original real contour, with its actual boundary. There is no analytic compactly supported cutoff and no assigned Lefschetz-cycle coefficient. Only FINITELY many restricted charge terms are ever summed here. A sharp cube has boundary jumps and its individual JN,ℓJ_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} do not inherit the absolute charge summability of the original full-domain integral.

The proved full packet is

SN(−2cos(2π𝜹))=(−1)dRN2ReNAN(𝜹)(2πN)(R−1)/2det⁡HN(𝜹)[1+O(N−1)]. S_N\!\left(-2\cos(2\pi\boldsymbol\delta)\right) =\frac{(-1)^{dR}N^{2R}e^{N A_N(\boldsymbol\delta)}} {(2\pi N)^{(R-1)/2}\sqrt{\det H_N(\boldsymbol\delta)}} \left[1+O(N^{-1})\right].\tag{2}

It is uniform and holomorphic on a fixed complex polydisc. Every finite scalar and logarithmic normalization stays in ANA_N; the explicit N2RN^{2R} alone is taken out. HNH_N is the ordinary-face Hessian after fixing ONE common face scale. Its determinant root is continued from its positive value at 𝜹=0\boldsymbol\delta=0. The dimension of that face quotient is R−1R-1. The packet restores the full VV numerator, including its semisimple spectral data, by the proved noncross exponential gap.

The finite interpolant used to define ANA_N is real on the real local chart and even in each δi\delta_i. Uniqueness of the face IFT preserves both properties. On smaller fixed polydiscs, with all fixed derivatives,

AN→A0,A0(0)=ΦD,A0″(0)=−2diag⁡(C1,…,CR),Ci>0. A_N\longrightarrow A_0,\qquad A_0(0)=\Phi_D,\qquad A_0''(0)=-2\operatorname{diag}(C_1,\ldots,C_R),\qquad C_i>0.\tag{3}

These are finite-source properties, not a choice of a phase fitted to the answer. In particular

∂iAN(𝜹)=−2δiBi,N(δ12,…,δR2),Bi,N→Bi,Bi(0)=Ci. \partial_i A_N(\boldsymbol\delta) =-2\delta_i B_{i,N}(\delta_1^2,\ldots,\delta_R^2),\qquad B_{i,N}\longrightarrow B_i,\qquad B_i(0)=C_i.\tag{4}

The quotients at δi=0\delta_i=0 are removable. After shrinking the chart, |Bi,N−Ci|≤Ci/8|B_{i,N}-C_i|\leq C_i/8 for all large N. In particular Re⁡Bi,N>Ci/2\operatorname{Re}B_{i,N}>C_i/2, and the real symmetric matrix Re⁡AN″(z)\operatorname{Re}A_N''(z) is negative definite uniformly for ALL complex z in that chart and all large N.

2. Finite-N filling saddles for independent signed twists

There are fixed b,a,n0>0b,a,n_0>0, with a much smaller than b, such that the following holds for every fixed integer vector 𝒏\mathbf n with |ni|≥n0|n_i|\geq n_0. There is no condition on ratios |ni|/|nj||n_i|/|n_j|. For positive real charges satisfying ℓi/|ni|≤a\ell_i/|n_i|\leq a define

ΦN,ℓ(𝜹)=AN(𝜹)+πi∑iniδi2+2πi∑iℓiδi. \Phi_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}(\boldsymbol\delta) =A_N(\boldsymbol\delta)+\pi i\sum_i n_i\delta_i^2 +2\pi i\sum_i\ell_i\delta_i.\tag{5}

It has a unique critical point 𝜹N,ℓ\boldsymbol\delta_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} in the chosen small polydisc. Indeed, writing ti=ℓi/nit_i=\ell_i/n_i, its equations are precisely

δi=−ti1+iBi,N(𝜹2)/(πni). \delta_i=-\frac{t_i}{1+i B_{i,N}(\boldsymbol\delta^2)/(\pi n_i)}.\tag{6}

Uniform contraction proves existence, uniqueness and holomorphic parameter dependence, exactly as in the limiting charge lemma. Bounds on Bi,NB_{i,N} and their derivatives are uniform in N. Consequently

|δi|≤2|ti|,Im⁡δi=ℓiπni2Re⁡Bi,N(𝜹2)|1+iBi,N(𝜹2)/(πni)|2>0. |\delta_i|\leq 2|t_i|,\qquad \operatorname{Im}\delta_i =\frac{\ell_i}{\pi n_i^2} \frac{\operatorname{Re}B_{i,N}(\boldsymbol\delta^2)} {\left|1+i B_{i,N}(\boldsymbol\delta^2)/(\pi n_i)\right|^2}>0.\tag{7}

In particular Im⁡δi≤Cℓi/ni2\operatorname{Im}\delta_i\leq C\ell_i/n_i^2. This sign holds for BOTH signs of nin_i. All coordinates of the saddle are nonzero when all ℓi>0\ell_i>0. Write 𝜹*=x*+iy*\boldsymbol\delta_*=x_*+iy_*, with y*>0y_*>0 componentwise, and put

SN,ℓ=ΦN,ℓ(𝜹*),QN,ℓ=−ΦN,ℓ″(𝜹*). S_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}=\Phi_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}(\boldsymbol\delta_*),\qquad Q_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}=-\Phi_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}''(\boldsymbol\delta_*).

Since the Gaussian contributes a purely imaginary diagonal Hessian, Re⁡QN,ℓ\operatorname{Re}Q_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} is positive definite. No real or complex eigenvalue of Q vanishes. The spectral determinant root used below is the unique Gaussian branch on the convex cone of complex symmetric matrices with positive real part, positive at a positive real matrix. Thus

∫ℝRe−12tTQtdt=(2π)R/2det⁡Q. \int_{\mathbb R^R}e^{-\frac12 t^TQt}\,dt =\frac{(2\pi)^{R/2}}{\sqrt{\det Q}}.\tag{8}

This fixes its phase even when the different nin_i have different signs.

For fixed 𝒏\mathbf n and ℓ\boldsymbol\ell, 𝜹N,ℓ\boldsymbol\delta_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} and SN,ℓS_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} converge to the corresponding limiting critical point and value. No convergence rate is needed and ANA_N is NOT replaced by A0A_0 in a relative formula.

3. Deformation of the actual restricted real contour

We prove the requisite contour coefficient, rather than postulate it. Translate KbK_b coordinate by coordinate to Kb+iy*K_b+i y_*. Cauchy’s theorem on each rectangle gives equality with the translated integral plus its oriented side integrals. The exact integrand is entire in 𝜹\boldsymbol\delta; the packet is only used to estimate it inside the common local chart. The side integrals are retained in this equality.

Here is a uniform exponential bound for every side. Its points have z=x+ivz=x+iv with x in KbK_b, at least one |xj|=b|x_j|=b, and 0≤vi≤y*,i0\leq v_i\leq y_{*,i}. Separate reality and the real Hessian bound give

Re⁡AN(x+iv)≤AN(0)−c|x|2+C|v|2. \operatorname{Re}A_N(x+iv)\leq A_N(0)-c|x|^2+C|v|^2.\tag{9}

To see the quadratic, rather than linear, error in v, Taylor-expand at the REAL point x: the first term i∇AN(x)⋅vi\nabla A_N(x)\cdot v is imaginary. The remaining derivatives are uniformly bounded. The real Gaussian and charge contribution is

−2π∑inixivi−2π∑iℓivi≤Cb∑iℓi|ni|. -2\pi\sum_i n_i x_i v_i-2\pi\sum_i\ell_i v_i \leq Cb\sum_i\frac{\ell_i}{|n_i|}.\tag{10}

The second term is nonpositive. Equation (7) bounds the first term, without using a common sign or a bound on slope ratios. Thus every side has real phase at most

AN(0)−cb2+C|y*|2+Cb∑i|ti|. A_N(0)-cb^2+C|y_*|^2+Cb\sum_i|t_i|.\tag{11}

The critical value satisfies the complementary estimate

Re⁡SN,ℓ≥AN(0)−C∑i|ti|2. \operatorname{Re}S_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} \geq A_N(0)-C\sum_i|t_i|^2.\tag{12}

For completeness, AN(𝜹*)−AN(0)=O(∑i|ti|2)A_N(\boldsymbol\delta_*)-A_N(0)=O(\sum_i|t_i|^2). The critical equations imply |nix*,i+ℓi|≤C|δ*,i||n_i x_{*,i}+\ell_i|\leq C|\delta_{*,i}|; hence the real quadratic-plus-linear phase −2π∑i(nix*,i+ℓi)y*,i-2\pi\sum_i(n_i x_{*,i}+\ell_i)y_{*,i} has the same bound (and in fact a smaller additional factor nmin⁡−1n_{\min}^{-1}). This proves (12) without bounding the large purely imaginary quantity niδi2n_i\delta_i^2 by its absolute value.

First fix b within the source chart, then choose a/b sufficiently small and n0n_0 sufficiently large. Equations (11)-(12) give a fixed κb>0\kappa_b>0 such that ALL sides satisfy

Re⁡ΦN,ℓ(z)≤Re⁡SN,ℓ−κb. \operatorname{Re}\Phi_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}(z) \leq\operatorname{Re}S_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}-\kappa_b.\tag{13}

All polynomial prefactors in (2), bounded sine factors and finite side volumes preserve this exponential loss. Thus the actual local real contour has coefficient +1 on its translated horizontal saddle plane, with only exponentially small side currents.

On that plane Φ′\Phi' vanishes at x*+iy*x_*+iy_*, and for every real x in KbK_b

Re⁡ΦN,ℓ(x+iy*)≤Re⁡SN,ℓ−c|x−x*|2. \operatorname{Re}\Phi_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}(x+iy_*) \leq\operatorname{Re}S_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}-c|x-x_*|^2.\tag{14}

Indeed the real part of its real-coordinate Hessian is Re⁡AN″\operatorname{Re}A_N'', which is uniformly negative definite along the entire segment. The critical point stays a fixed distance from the cube boundary because |x*|≤2a|x_*|\leq2a much less than b. This proves the requisite local and nonlocal estimates on the translated restricted contour. It does not say anything about the original contour outside KbK_b.

4. Relative asymptotic and its nonzero coefficient

Define

aN(𝜹)=∏isin⁡(2πδi)det⁡HN(𝜹). a_N(\boldsymbol\delta) =\frac{\prod_i\sin(2\pi\delta_i)}{\sqrt{\det H_N(\boldsymbol\delta)}}.

The holomorphic Gaussian estimate on (14), using (8), yields

JN,ℓ=(−1)dR2πNR+1/2aN(𝜹N,ℓ)det⁡QN,ℓeNSN,ℓ[1+O𝒏,ℓ(N−1)]. J_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} =(-1)^{dR}\sqrt{2\pi}\,N^{R+1/2} \frac{a_N(\boldsymbol\delta_{N,\boldsymbol\ell})}{\sqrt{\det Q_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}}} e^{N S_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}} \left[1+O_{\mathbf n,\boldsymbol\ell}(N^{-1})\right].\tag{15}

The error in the full packet is holomorphic and uniformly O(N−1)O(N^{-1}); Cauchy bounds, or direct Gaussian integration of that error, preserve (15). The ordinary Taylor Gaussian error is O(N−1)O(N^{-1}), since its odd first correction integrates to zero; replacing the cube by the full real Gaussian changes the answer exponentially. The exact side integrals in (13) are likewise exponentially small relative to (15). Constants may depend on the FIXED slopes and charges. No joint large-twist/large-level error estimate is asserted.

The power and constant follow directly from the TWO actual integrals:

N2R(2πN)−(R−1)/2(2π/N)R/2=2πNR+1/2. N^{2R}(2\pi N)^{-(R-1)/2}(2\pi/N)^{R/2} =\sqrt{2\pi}\,N^{R+1/2}.\tag{16}

There is no undetermined stationary multiplier. Every sine in (15) is nonzero: δ*,i\delta_{*,i} is nonzero by (6) and lies in a small disk with no other zero of sin⁡(2πδi)\sin(2\pi\delta_i). Both determinant roots are units. For each fixed 𝒏\mathbf n and ℓ\boldsymbol\ell the coefficient in (15) converges to a nonzero number. Therefore the local integral is nonzero for all sufficiently large N.

Let UN,𝒏;bU_{N,\mathbf n;b} denote the sum of the original contributions in (1) with ℓi∈{+1,−1}\ell_i\in\{+1,-1\}, each restricted to KbK_b. Reflection of one coordinate δi↦−δi\delta_i\mapsto-\delta_i changes JN,ℓJ_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} by a minus sign and also changes ∏iℓi\prod_i\ell_i by a minus sign. SNS_N and the quadratic phase are separately even, and the parity character is unchanged. Thus all 2R2^R sign choices contribute EXACTLY equally. With SN,𝟏S_{N,\mathbf1}, 𝜹N,𝟏\boldsymbol\delta_{N,\mathbf1}, QN,𝟏Q_{N,\mathbf1} defined above,

UN,𝒏;b=qd24∑ini(−2i)R2πNR+1/2×∏isin⁡(2πδN,𝟏,i)det⁡HN(𝜹N,𝟏)det⁡QN,𝟏eNSN,𝟏[1+O𝒏(N−1)]. \begin{aligned} U_{N,\mathbf n;b} ={}&q^{\frac{d^2}{4}\sum_i n_i}(-2i)^R\sqrt{2\pi}\,N^{R+1/2}\\ &\quad\times\frac{\prod_i\sin(2\pi\delta_{N,\mathbf1,i})} {\sqrt{\det H_N(\boldsymbol\delta_{N,\mathbf1})}\sqrt{\det Q_{N,\mathbf1}}} e^{N S_{N,\mathbf1}}\left[1+O_{\mathbf n}(N^{-1})\right]. \end{aligned}\tag{17}

The parity cancellation is literal:

(−1)(N+1)R(−1)dR=(−1)2NR=1. (-1)^{(N+1)R}(-1)^{dR}=(-1)^{2NR}=1.

Equation (17) holds through BOTH level parities and arbitrary independent signs of the sufficiently long fixed nin_i. The remaining qq scalar has modulus one and is kept exactly. This proves a nonzero contributing localized original unit-charge period, not merely the existence of the corresponding critical point.

5. Optional enlargement to the finite LOCAL charge sector

For fixed 𝒏\mathbf n let Li=⌊a|ni|⌋L_i=\lfloor a|n_i|\rfloor, with n0n_0 enlarged so Li≥1L_i\geq1. Restrict (1) to KbK_b and to 0<|ℓi|≤Li0<|\ell_i|\leq L_i. This is a finite set after the twists are fixed. The exact finite version of the monotonicity calculation applies to ANA_N, because (4) and Re⁡Bi,N>0\operatorname{Re}B_{i,N}>0 hold:

∂ℓiRe⁡SN,ℓ=−2ℓini2Re⁡Bi,N|1+iBi,N/(πni)|2. \partial_{\ell_i}\operatorname{Re}S_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} =-\frac{2\ell_i}{n_i^2} \frac{\operatorname{Re}B_{i,N}} {\left|1+iB_{i,N}/(\pi n_i)\right|^2}.

After the same fixed shrink, for every positive charge in this box,

Re⁡SN,𝟏−Re⁡SN,ℓ≥∑iCi2ni2(ℓi2−1). \operatorname{Re}S_{N,\mathbf1}-\operatorname{Re}S_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} \geq\sum_i\frac{C_i}{2n_i^2}(\ell_i^2-1).\tag{18}

The harmless constants can be decreased if needed to make this inequality uniform in all large N. If any nonunit positive charge exists, the right side has a strictly positive minimum η𝒏\eta_{\mathbf n} over that FINITE set. Formula (15), the exact charge factors ∏iℓi\prod_i\ell_i, and the independent sign symmetry imply that the entire restricted local charge sector equals (17) with an additional relative O𝒏(e−η𝒏N)O_{\mathbf n}(e^{-\eta_{\mathbf n}N}) error (or after a smaller η𝒏\eta_{\mathbf n}). If all Li=1L_i=1, there are no extra local charges. There is no comparison of an infinite charge sum and no assertion about charges near an alias.

6. Geometric value and exact remaining limitation

The limiting action in (5) is

A0+πi∑i(ϵi−2pi)δi2+2πi∑iℓiδi=A𝝐−2πi∑ipiδi2+2πi∑iℓiδi,A𝝐=A0+πi∑iϵiδi2. \begin{aligned} A_0+\pi i\sum_i(\epsilon_i-2p_i)\delta_i^2 +2\pi i\sum_i\ell_i\delta_i &=A_{\boldsymbol\epsilon}-2\pi i\sum_i p_i\delta_i^2 +2\pi i\sum_i\ell_i\delta_i,\\ A_{\boldsymbol\epsilon}&=A_0+\pi i\sum_i\epsilon_i\delta_i^2. \end{aligned}

Thus the half twists occur ONCE. The marked action and collar identity gives the actual primitive saddle equation

U𝝐,i+piVi=−2πiℓi. U_{\boldsymbol\epsilon,i}+p_iV_i=-2\pi i\ell_i.

Here S0,𝟏S_{0,\mathbf1} denotes the limiting critical value. At the all-unit long-filling branch, its Gaussian peripheral correction cancels exactly and 2πRe⁡S0,𝟏2\pi\operatorname{Re}S_{0,\mathbf1} is the actual smooth filling volume. Consequently the localized quantity (17), and the finite local charge sector in Section 5, have that volume as their normalized logarithmic modulus limit. The original exterior unit framing phase does not change this conclusion.

This is NOT yet the original invariant’s volume limit. For the FINITE selected charge set ℒ\mathcal L (the sign-unit set or the finite box of section 5), define the exact remainder as the original full period (1) MINUS that finite localized sector. It is the sum of:

  • the finitely many ℓ∈ℒ\boldsymbol\ell\in\mathcal L, each integrated over the original full cube minus KbK_b, with its original charge factor; and
  • ALL ℓ∉ℒ\boldsymbol\ell\notin\mathcal L, each kept on its WHOLE original domain [−1/2,1/2]R[-1/2,1/2]^R, with the original absolutely convergent summation.

Do not separately sum the remote inside-KbK_b and outside-KbK_b pieces. The sharp boundaries would generally destroy their separate charge summability; such a split would require a specified shared regularization and its boundary cancellation. The endpoint currents are already encoded in the exact identity (1), not additional terms to append to that identity. No endpoint cancellation was discarded in deriving the finite localized formula.

This exact remainder still requires a phase-preserving global estimate excluding cancellation of the nonzero contribution (17). Generic-qq normal derivatives are not an additional requirement for this fixed-root route; the exact marked source and all σ\sigma-Hermite data are already retained in (1)-(2).

Validation and scope

The result proves a localized contribution and its finite local charge sector; it does not prove a bound for the remaining original period or the general filled-knot volume conjecture.
