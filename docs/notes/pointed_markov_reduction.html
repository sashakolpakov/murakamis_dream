Markov reduction of the actual source and its geometric determinant line

Research note, 26 October 2026. This continues the invariant’s own quantum A/torsor program. The source is the original reduced Kashaev/ADO cut. No comparison with Jones, a custom state, or a published regulator theorem is used. We prove an exact finite source contraction, a uniform geometric Gaussian reduction, and explicit Markov chain equivalences for the linearized holonomy complex. The selected global period’s volume norm remains unproved.

Uniform compatibility theorem. Let beta be any braid whose closure is a knot, at any N>=2 with the original quantum cut normalization. For either Markov stabilization:

  1. The actual finite cut contracts exactly to the old cut as a whole meridian germ, with its full modified dimension.
  2. At every nondegenerate geometric critical chart, the added quadratic period complex is the old complex plus one explicit acyclic rank-one pair. Its variable principal amplitude cancels the determinant of that pair.
  3. At every irreducible fixed-trace representation with ker(dF-I) equal to the infinitesimal conjugation orbit, the linearized holonomy complex is the old one plus an explicit acyclic rank-two pair, and its torsion is unchanged. It is also invariant under braid conjugation and braid relations.

The proof below has arbitrary N, braid and representation. The finite computations in the audit validate the formulas; they are not the proof or an assumption on knot examples. This is a uniform structural lemma for the intended volume theorem, not the volume theorem itself.

1. The full finite source has an exact contraction

Use q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, x=-M/q, the original checked crossings B_+,B_-=B_+^(-1), pivot g=K^(1-N), and theta=x^(1-N). For every N>=2 and every whole color germ,

ptr_g(B_+)=theta I,
ptr_g(B_-)=theta^(-1)I.                       (1.1)

Thus the zero-framed Markov contraction is identity. The full modified dimension d_N(M) on the remaining cut is unchanged; no peripheral factor is evaluated separately.

Here is a coefficient proof, in the original finite basis. It uses the independently derived coefficients in the actual crossing note. Integer-index conservation makes either partial trace diagonal. For the positive crossing, write k=a-b. Its diagonal entry divided by theta is

x^(-2a) Q^(a(a-1))
   sum_(k=0)^a (-1)^k Q^(k(k-1)/2+(1-a)k)
                  {a choose k}_Q (t;Q)_k,
t=Q^(1-a)x^2.                                (1.2)

The sum equals t^a. To prove this, expand t^a in the Newton basis P_k=(t;Q)k and use t P_k=Q^(-k)(P_k-P(k+1)). Induction and the Gaussian binomial recursion give precisely the coefficients in (1.2). Its prefactor cancels t^a, proving the first identity.

For the negative crossing put n=N-1-a. Root reflection gives

{a+k choose k}_Q
   =(-1)^k Q^(ak+k(k+1)/2){n choose k}_Q.

Invert the k Pochhammer factors. With w=Q^a x^(-2), the remaining diagonal sum becomes

x^(2a+1-N) Q^(-a(a+1))
           sum_(k=0)^n {n choose k}_Q w^(-k)(w;Q)_k.

The last sum is w^(-n). Equivalently, sum_k {n choose k}_Q w^(n-k)(w;Q)_k=1; induction follows from {n+1 choose k}_Q=Q^k{n choose k}_Q+{n choose k-1}Q and P(k+1)=(1-wQ^k)P_k. The x exponent is then N-1 and the remaining Q exponent is -aN. This proves the second identity in (1.1), for the whole meridian function.

2. The regular geometric contraction has one critical variable

Use the actual balanced coefficients, with position variables A,B, transfer C and Y=M^2, as in the principal density calculation. Closing the added strand imposes

B=A/C for B_+,
B=AC  for B_-.

The original internal shift ratios give the respective stationary multipliers

R_+=-(1-YC/A)(1-B)/[B(1-C)],
R_-=(1-AC)(1-Y/B)/[A(1-C)].

On the nondegenerate chart R_sign=1 has the unique solution

D=1+Y-A,
C_+=A D/Y, C_-=Y/(A D),
B_+=B_-=Y/D.                                (2.1)

The excluded factors are actual chart divisors, including A=1,Y and D=0; they are not deleted from the quantum source.

Let c=Log C and hold a=Log A fixed. The trace-phase Hessian in its single remaining internal variable is

h_+=D^2/[(A-1)(A-Y)], h_-=-h_+.              (2.2)

These follow by taking (0,-1,1) and (0,1,1) directional derivatives in (Log A,Log B,Log C) of the actual phase Hessian. They are units on the stated regular chart.

The original principal amplitudes include their closing pivot and zero-framing factors. Their squares are

b_+^2=a_+^2/B^2,
b_-^2=Y^2 a_-^2/B^2.

Substitution of (2.1) gives exactly

b_+^2=h_+, b_-^2=h_-,
b_sign^2/(-h_sign)=-1.                       (2.3)

Thus all input- and meridian-dependent factors cancel from the one-variable Gaussian contraction. The remaining minus sign belongs to the fixed Gaussian/quantum normalization line; it is not a new adjustable peripheral multiplier. The full finite normalization is fixed by (1.1). We retain that line rather than evaluating an infinite compact product at a root of unity.

The cut-momentum and meridian character multipliers both equal one at (2.1). This follows by multiplying the two input shift ratios for the former and substituting in the actual meridian half-step ratio for the latter. Locally the critical phase therefore differs by a base-only term and integer stationary windings. Those windings are retained characters equal to one on the actual integer lattice. The base-only term has its specified original normalization.

3. An explicit map on the quadratic period complexes

Let X be the old phase coordinates and let v be the column derivative of c_sign(A(X),Y). For a fixed meridian,

partial_(Log A)c_sign
           =sign*(1+Y-2A)/D.

After the stated integer flattening, the critical added phase has no X-dependent value. Its Hessian adjoining the old quadratic form H is consequently

H_new=[H+h v v^t  -h v; -h v^t  h].           (3.1)

The unit-determinant change c_tilde=c-c_sign(X) gives

U=[I 0;v^t 1],
U^t H_new U=diag(H,h).                       (3.2)

This is a chain equivalence of the two-term quadratic complexes. It identifies the old kernel with the graph (xi,v^t xi) and adds the acyclic pair [k –h–> k]. The determinant factor is h. Equation (2.3) supplies its actual amplitude correction, including the universal sign.

This proves stabilization compatibility of the variable geometric normal determinant line for every regular irreducible critical representation. It is not a claim that the selected global relative cycle has already been pushed through an arbitrary pole divisor.

4. Markov chain equivalences for the holonomy complex

We can now prove that the finite-dimensional scalar in the density note, equation (6.4) is invariant under Markov moves on its regular locus.

Let X be the product of the noncentral fixed-trace meridian classes. At an irreducible fixed point H of the Hurwitz map F, use

0 -> sl_2 --a--> T_H X --(dF-I)--> T_H X
                                  --m--> sl_2 -> 0,
a(xi)=([xi,H_j])_j,
m(V)=dP(V)P^(-1), P=H_b...H_1.               (4.1)

It is acyclic when ker(dF-I)=im a. The earlier note proves injectivity of a, surjectivity of m and the well-defined torsion T_beta=det[a,V]/det[(dF-I)V,W], mW=I.

For stabilization put A=H_b and append a new meridian Z. Writing G=F(H), the two extended maps are

F_+(H,Z)=(G_<b, G_b^(-1) Z G_b, G_b),
F_-(H,Z)=(G_<b, Z, Z G_b Z^(-1)).

Their fixed-point schemes are exactly F(H)=H,Z=H_b. This follows by eliminating Z directly and holds over Artin rings, not just on reduced points.

At such a point write w=(dF(V))_b and use the domain coordinate u=z-w. In the output use

V'_b=V_b+Ad_(A^(-1)) z, V'_<b=V_<b,

keeping the new last coordinate z. Both are triangular changes of determinant one. The middle differential becomes

diag(dF-I,-I)                   for F_+,
diag(dF-I,-Ad_A|_(T_A orbit))   for F_-.       (4.2)

The infinitesimal conjugation maps to (a,0). The extended product is P_new=Z P, and its transformed last map is

m_new(V',z)=Ad_A m(V').                      (4.3)

Indeed the z term in d(ZP)(ZP)^(-1) cancels precisely against the correction in V’_b. Equations (4.2)–(4.3) are an explicit chain equivalence with the old complex and one acyclic two-dimensional pair. Both Ad_A on sl_2 and Ad_A on its tangent conjugacy orbit have determinant one; the latter also follows from invariance of dK wedge de/e. The signs in -I have even dimension. Therefore

T_(beta stabilized)(H,H_b)=T_beta(H)          (4.4)

for either sign, and regularity is preserved.

Braid conjugation is also a chain equivalence: a Hurwitz coordinate change is equivariant and preserves P. Its derivative changes both middle bases equally, so the torsion ratio is unchanged. Braid relations are identities of the Hurwitz maps. Thus this scalar is invariant under the braid presentation moves, with its meridian trace and irreducible representation marked. No equality with a separately defined Reidemeister torsion is used or asserted.

5. Consequence for the actual A/torsor program

The actual finite source contraction, its geometric Gaussian pair, and the holonomy deformation complex now all have explicit stabilization maps. This is a compatibility for structures attached to the same invariant. It avoids assuming that two arbitrary periods with the same A-symbol coincide.

The subsequent uniform principal-density proof now compares the variable Gaussian determinant line with (4.1), including its kernel, cut co-source and explicit presentation-parity character. The base-only quantum normalization line and transport of the selected global relative period remain separate. These local contractions do not prove that its geometric block survives with subexponential comparison factor across levels. That norm statement is still the required volume theorem.
