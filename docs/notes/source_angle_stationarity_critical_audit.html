What a source saddle must prove for the Casson–Rivin maximum

29 October 2026. This is an independent implication audit. A saddle of a reduced source does not, by itself, maximize volume on the full angle polytope. There are two valid ways to bridge that gap: control every missing tangent direction, or independently identify a full complex gluing solution with the correct real peripheral constraints. The latter is the mechanism used in the actual long-pretzel argument, not an assumption that its source variables parametrize every angle.

1. The full tangent test, with a direct proof

For n positively oriented ideal tetrahedra use independent angle coordinates (alpha_t,gamma_t), with beta_t=pi-alpha_t-gamma_t>0. The corresponding shapes are uniquely fixed by the sine rule:

z_t=exp(i alpha_t) sin(beta_t)/sin(gamma_t),
z_t''=1-1/z_t.

Write r=(Re Log z_t,Re Log z_t’’) and let Omega=sum_t dx_t wedge dy_t on W=R^(2n). Direct differentiation gives, for EVERY angle variation delta, whether or not it preserves complex gluing,

dV(delta)=Omega(r,delta),
V=sum_t [Lambda(alpha_t)+Lambda(beta_t)+Lambda(gamma_t)].  (1)

Indeed Re Log z=log(sin beta/sin gamma) and Re Log z’’=log(sin alpha/sin beta); inserting delta beta=-delta alpha-delta gamma proves (1) term by term.

The following are explicit linear-algebra hypotheses to check on the ACTUAL marked triangulation. They are not consequences of a source saddle or a dimension count:

  • The edge Hamiltonian space E is isotropic, dim E=n-c.

  • The edge-angle tangent space is E^perp.

  • The peripheral map b:E^perp -> R^(2c), written (U,V), is onto, has kernel E, and satisfies

    Omega|_(E^perp)=b^*[-(1/2) sum_i dU_i wedge dV_i].     (2)

These identities can be checked from the marked edge and peripheral matrices. The quotient rank and its kernel matter as much as the pairing constant. Edge/peripheral logarithmic constants here have zero real part; hence b(r) is exactly (Re U,Re V), with no omitted real affine offset.

Choose a real rank-c matrix C=(A B) with ABT=BAT. The intended angle slice fixes C(Im U,Im V)=kappa, in addition to tetrahedron and edge angles. Its full tangent is

T={delta in E^perp : C b(delta)=0}.                       (3)

Full-slice criterion. Suppose the positive shape point satisfies the REAL edge equations, so r lies in E^perp. Then

dV|_T=0  if and only if  A Re U+B Re V=0.                 (4)

Proof. L=ker C is Lagrangian in the 2c-dimensional peripheral space: ABT=BAT makes the Hamiltonian row span isotropic, and its rank c makes its symplectic orthogonal equal to itself. Surjectivity in (2) gives b(T)=L. Equations (1)–(2) therefore give

dV(delta)=(1/2) sum_i
   [Re V_i delta Im U_i-Re U_i delta Im V_i].             (5)

This vanishes for every delta in T precisely when b(r) lies in L^perp=L, which is (4). This proves the claim for ALL angle tangents, including directions absent from the source parameter space.

For a filled cusp the row is (1,p_i); its imaginary constraint fixes the actual marked value Im(U_i+p_i V_i)=+/-2pi, while (4) requires Re(U_i+p_i V_i)=0. For a retained main cusp one can use the meridian row (1,0); genuine parabolicity gives U_i=V_i=0 and is stronger than the real condition needed for stationarity. All primitive markings and the original centered SL signs still have to be proved separately.

2. Why this gives the unique maximum, and what it does not identify

On one positive angle simplex the negative Hessian in (alpha,gamma) is

[[cot alpha+cot beta, cot beta],
 [cot beta, cot gamma+cot beta]].

Its determinant is 1 and its first diagonal entry is sin(gamma)/(sin(alpha)sin(beta))>0. Thus V is strictly concave on every affine angle slice. An interior point satisfying (4) is its unique global maximum, also over the closed slice: along any segment from that point the derivative starts at zero, then strictly decreases; continuity of Lambda handles its final boundary endpoint.

Three further identifications remain logically distinct:

  1. Source value. Vsrc=V(theta_*) requires the absolute action / marked regulator comparison on an actual oriented fundamental chain, including the 2pi/N scale and all centered peripheral terms. Equality of stationary equations cannot fix an additive action constant. Nor does a peripheral curve alone fix its Bloch-period lift.
  2. Actual topology and ends. The constraints must be those of the desired manifold. A complete-cusp maximum is not the filled-slope problem. For a filling, peripheral holonomy equal to identity only modulo 2pi does not establish a smooth primitive tube: winding twice produces angle 4pi. Positivity, actual face/shape matching, the primitive log period, and smooth end completion must be supplied.
  3. Geometric value. To infer V(theta_*)=Vol(M), either a geometric angle point for the SAME slice must be independently identified (then strict concavity identifies the two), or the selected positive developing structure must be completed to the actual hyperbolic manifold. A representation-volume upper bound alone supplies only Vsrc<=Vol(M), not equality.

The long-pretzel proof supplies these via its literal port lift, absolute marked regulator, positive core and primitive tube completion. Its direct completion argument remains independent of the optional positive all-ideal triangulation and quotient-matrix inputs in (2).

3. A restricted source saddle is insufficient: an exact angle example

In a single ideal tetrahedron restrict to

alpha=pi/2, beta=pi/4+t, gamma=pi/4-t.

At t=0 the restricted volume derivative is zero, and the second derivative is -2: this is the unique restricted maximum. Nevertheless the legitimate full angle tangent

(delta alpha,delta beta,delta gamma)=(-2,1,1)

has dV=log 2>0 there. The point is not the full maximum; the regular tetrahedron has strictly larger volume. This counterexample already uses the TRUE tetrahedral volume and a positive angle point. It is not an issue about choosing the wrong dilogarithm branch.

Generally, for a source map Theta:X -> A with image tangent B, source stationarity gives only dV|B=0. It implies dV|(T A)=0 if B spans T A, or if B plus independently proved zero-derivative directions spans T A. Alternatively, genuine stationary elimination can supply all discarded equations. Without one of these mechanisms, strict concavity of V proves only a maximum on the restricted source family.

No rank condition on Theta is necessary when the full complex gluing and peripheral criterion (4) has already supplied the missing equations. That is the useful stronger implication in Section 1.

There is also a separate optimization shortcut: if a source limit is proved to be max_(theta in Psrc) V(theta), Psrc is contained in the correct angle polytope, and its image is proved to contain the geometric maximizer, the two maxima agree. Merely knowing that Psrc is nonempty, positive, or has its own unique stationary point does not prove that containment.

4. Audit conclusion

The full-angle stationarity step in the existing Casson–Rivin note is valid UNDER its stated positive-shape, real-edge, full-rank quotient and actual peripheral hypotheses: it uses (5), not restricted source stationarity. For a new general source, those hypotheses are substantive gates. Local tetrahedral volume factors, a source Hessian, a restricted maximum, or numerical agreement with cusp derivatives cannot replace them. This note establishes the implication and its precise failure mode; it makes no new unrestricted knot theorem or quantum survival claim.
