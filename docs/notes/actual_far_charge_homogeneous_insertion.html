Exact homogeneous insertion for a far-charge coupon, and why it is not an identity insertion

Independently reviewed, 31 October 2026. This accompanies the whole-domain large-charge theorem. It supplies an exact source coefficient bound, not a shared volume or capacity theorem.

1. The full face-grid transform gives a coefficient bound

For ANY actual fixed-root Casimir coupon F=f(Ω)F=f(\Omega), with the complete Hermite data retained, its homogeneous kernel has degree d=N−1d=N-1:

HF(x,b,c,y)=∑a+i+t+s=dha,i,t,sxabictys. H_F(x,b,c,y)=\sum_{a+i+t+s=d}h_{a,i,t,s}\,x^ab^ic^ty^s.

Set c=1c=1 and sample the THREE independent original clock cosets

b=Qp,x=−qQr,y=−q−1Qs,0≤p,r,s<N. b=Q^p,\qquad x=-qQ^r,\qquad y=-q^{-1}Q^s,\qquad0\leq p,r,s<N.

The actual input clocks U=−q−1x/bU=-q^{-1}x/b and V=−q−1b/yV=-q^{-1}b/y are respectively Qr−p,Qp−sQ^{r-p},Q^{p-s}, and the output relation c=Q−pbc=Q^{-p}b is literal. Write canonical physical heads u=[r−p]Nu=[r-p]_N, v=[p−s]Nv=[p-s]_N, R=rR=r, S=[−s]NS=[-s]_N, and retain the TRUE integer displacement Δ=R−u\Delta=R-u. The original local matrix/grid identity therefore says

HF(−qQr,Qp,1,−q−1Qs)=NqdΔFR,S;u,v. H_F(-qQ^r,Q^p,1,-q^{-1}Q^s)=Nq^{d\Delta}F_{R,S;u,v}.\tag{1}

Although Δ\Delta is congruent to pp modulo NN, their phase ratio is (−1)d(Δ−p)/N(-1)^{d(\Delta-p)/N}, which cannot be dropped at even levels. For example N=4N=4, p=3p=3, r=0r=0, s=1s=1 has u=1u=1, v=2v=2, R=0R=0, S=3S=3 and Δ=−1\Delta=-1, a valid nonzero transition with the opposite carry sign.

If canonical representatives violate unwrapped total conservation, the right side is zero, exactly as is the left side by the original physical endpoint factors. No nonphysical wrap is inserted. All coset and qq factors in (1) have modulus one, at both parities.

Every exponent of HF(x,b,1,y)H_F(x,b,1,y) is between 00 and d<Nd<N. Its coefficients are thus recovered WITHOUT aliases by the three-dimensional inverse DFT on this grid, with the fixed coset phases removed. Consequently

$$ |h_{a,i,t,s}|\leq N\max_{\rm physical\ entries}|F_{\rm entry}| \leq N\|F\|_{\rm op}.\tag{2} $$

The remaining exponent t=d−a−i−st=d-a-i-s is fixed by homogeneity. In particular this is not an estimate that loses an exponential basis conversion. It uses the SAME original balanced physical tensor basis as the endpoint and Fourier-tail theorems.

Let hd(x,b,c,y)=∑a+i+t+s=dxabictysh_d(x,b,c,y)=\sum_{a+i+t+s=d}x^ab^ic^ty^s denote the complete homogeneous polynomial with all coefficients 11. Then

$$ |H_F|_{\rm coef}\preceq N\|F\|h_d.\tag{3} $$

An entire spectral function of Ω\Omega still reduces to its unique finite Hermite polynomial, so (1)-(3) apply to the absolutely summed far-charge coupon TN,n;MT_{N,n;M} as well. Combined with the tail theorem, for M=⌈|n|/2+1+D⌉M=\lceil|n|/2+1+D\rceil,

$$ |H_T|_{\rm coef}\preceq e^{N\varepsilon(D)+o(N)}h_d.\tag{4} $$

with the horizontal tail already made decreasing. The o(N)o(N) is uniform in NN for each fixed n,Dn,D. No arbitrary face radius has replaced an original marked coefficient.

2. The precise partial-source coefficient family

Insert TiT_i on a nonempty subset SS and keep the exact original RjnjR_j^{n_j} on its complement. Multilinearity gives the finite identity

Z=∑κi∈ℤ≥04,|κi|=di∈S[∏i∈ShTi(κi)][L𝒅−∑i∈Sκi]∏j∉SHRjnj. Z=\sum_{\substack{\kappa_i\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}^4,\ |\kappa_i|=d\\i\in S}} \left[\prod_{i\in S}h_{T_i}(\kappa_i)\right] \left[L^{\,\mathbf d-\sum_{i\in S}\kappa_i}\right] \prod_{j\notin S}H_{R_j^{n_j}}.\tag{5}

Here 𝒅\mathbf d is the original target vector, and each κi\kappa_i is extended by zero to the other original faces. Terms with any negative target, including a positive allocation to either protected zero face, are zero. There are at most O(N3|S|)O(N^{3|S|}) terms. Thus (4) bounds this source by a polynomial number of the ACTUAL shifted-target coefficients in (5), with an arbitrarily small extra rate ∑iε(Di)\sum_i\varepsilon(D_i). Those shifted coefficients, with their original signed phases, are the remaining source-specific problem.

The fixed-radius growth of hdh_d is log⁡max⁡(x,b,c,y)\log\max(x,b,c,y), because its number of monomials is polynomial in NN. It is not log⁡max⁡(b,c)\log\max(b,c), the rate of H1H_1. In particular (4) is NOT an identity-insertion or smoothing theorem.

3. Full Fejer entropy is not everywhere nonnegative

A proposed shortcut would replace a port by hdh_d, note its entropy 00, and appeal to nonnegativity of the removed full Fejer entropy hh. That nonnegativity is false for an ACTUAL coefficient.

At even N=2mN=2m let ar=4cos⁡2((r+1/2)π/N)a_r=4\cos^2((r+1/2)\pi/N), sj=2sin⁡(jπ/N)s_j=2\sin(j\pi/N), and P=(−1)dHVN(−2)P=(-1)^d H_{V_N(-2)}. The monomial xjcd−jx^jc^{d-j}, with b=y=0b=y=0 and 0≤j<m0\leq j<m, comes from the unique all-one-step path from jj to 00. Since ∏r=0dar=4\prod_{r=0}^d a_r=4, its exact coefficient is

[xjcd−j]P=4∏r=1jsr2∏r=0jar. [x^jc^{d-j}]P=\frac{4\prod_{r=1}^j s_r^2}{\prod_{r=0}^j a_r}.\tag{6}

Take j/N→ν∈(0,1/2)j/N\to\nu\in(0,1/2). The integrable endpoint Riemann sums give

limN→∞1Nlog⁡([xjcd−j]P)=2∫0νlog⁡tan⁡(πt)dt=E(0,ν,ν)<0. \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log\bigl([x^jc^{d-j}]P\bigr) =2\int_0^\nu\log\tan(\pi t)\,dt=E(0,\nu,\nu)<0.\tag{7}

The integral decreases until ν=1/4\nu=1/4 and returns to 00 only at 1/21/2. At ν=1/4\nu=1/4 its value is −2G/π-2G/\pi, where GG is Catalan’s constant. This is the established CLOSED half-block coefficient entropy and the actual one-sided branch of hh; there is no omitted source scalar. Replacing (6) by coefficient 11 of hdh_d increases this local entropy. The endpoint maximum’s uniqueness therefore does not by itself imply a strict gap after an hdh_d insertion. This is a local obstruction to THAT comparison, not a global source counterexample.

4. The far-charge endpoint functional is not scalar identity

There is also a direct phase-preserving fixed-NN check. Keep N,nN,n fixed, n≠0n\ne0, and let ℓ\ell tend to positive infinity. For the exact whole- interval BN,nB_{N,n} in the tail note,

B(1/2)=B(−1/2)=0,B′(1/2)=B′(−1/2),B″(1/2)−B″(−1/2)=−8π2inNeπinN/4VN(2). \begin{aligned} B(1/2)&=B(-1/2)=0,\qquad B'(1/2)=B'(-1/2),\\ B''(1/2)-B''(-1/2)&=-8\pi^2inN e^{\pi inN/4}V_N(2). \end{aligned}\tag{8}

The source is odd in δ\delta, so its third derivatives also agree at the two endpoints. Five integrations by parts, keeping all endpoint terms, yield

JN,n,ℓ=(−1)NℓeπinN/4nVN(2)πN2ℓ3+ON,n(ℓ−5),FN,n,ℓ=qnd2/4eπinN/4[−in(−1)ℓVN(2)πN2ℓ2]+ON,n(ℓ−4). \begin{aligned} J_{N,n,\ell}&=(-1)^{N\ell}e^{\pi inN/4}\frac{nV_N(2)}{\pi N^2\ell^3} +O_{N,n}(\ell^{-5}),\\ F_{N,n,\ell}&=q^{nd^2/4}e^{\pi inN/4} \left[-\frac{in(-1)^\ell V_N(2)}{\pi N^2\ell^2}\right]+O_{N,n}(\ell^{-4}). \end{aligned}\tag{9}

Using the exact sign-charge equality and the elementary alternating tail sum, this also gives

TN,n;M=qnd2/4eπinN/4[−in(−1)M+1VN(2)πN2M2]+ON,n(M−3). T_{N,n;M}=q^{nd^2/4}e^{\pi inN/4} \left[-\frac{in(-1)^{M+1}V_N(2)}{\pi N^2M^2}\right]+O_{N,n}(M^{-3}).\tag{10}

These limits are ONLY ℓ\ell or M→∞M\to\infty at fixed NN; they are not interchanges with the level limit in (4). They identify the actual endpoint coupon: VN(2)=4(2−Ω)−1V_N(2)=4(2-\Omega)^{-1}, rather than II. For example the one-step xcd−1xc^{d-1} coefficient is a nonzero negative step weight times a product of positive factors 2−cr2-c_r. Thus HVN(2)H_{V_N(2)} has nonzero outer powers, and (10) gives the same nonzero outer coefficient for every sufficiently large MM at fixed N,nN,n. Therefore literal coefficientwise domination by a multiple of H1H_1, whose outer powers vanish, cannot hold for every far coupon. The correct possible next step is a source-specific bound for these near-+2 coupons and shifted targets, not replacement by identity.

Validation and scope

The root and source reviewers independently checked the exact three-clock transform, the true even-level carry, the negative original coefficient entropy, and both endpoint asymptotic constants. The finite carry check uses the actual original Casimir entry; it is not a new large-level estimate. The fixed-level large-charge limits in (9)–(10) are never interchanged with the level limit.
