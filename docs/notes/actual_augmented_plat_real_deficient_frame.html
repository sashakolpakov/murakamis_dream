A global selected frame with one real deficient degree

30 October 2026. For every chosen matching edge of an actual certified right-angled augmented plat, there is a common algebraic frame whose two support failures occur precisely at that edge. Their normalized total is the REAL number sigma=1/6, while their magnetic rows are genuinely complex. Every other ordinary factor remains strictly physical and source-selected.

This is a classical framing and branch-calibration theorem. A fixed integer deficient degree corresponds to an identically zero physical slice of the finite polynomial operator. The construction does not turn that slice into a nonzero quantum period.

1. Use the actual cusp and prefix normalization

Fix the labeled matching-polyhedron certificate and one crossing-circle edge. In its actual disk coordinate eta, the two outward shaded disks are the half-planes below y=0 and above y=H, where

H=1/c>=1.

The inequality is the proved actual rectangle bound, including retained half twists. The two incident white planes are the vertical lines x=0 and x=1, with reflections -conjugate(eta) and 2-conjugate(eta). The actual incoming pair and its transport are

A=[[-1,0],[-2,-1]], P=[[-1,-2],[0,-1]],
C: eta -> eta+iH, A_o=C A C^-1.                (1)

These are precisely the conjugated original matrices and primitive collar, not an independent choice of a rectangle or meridian.

All other shaded outward disks are bounded Euclidean disks in this chart. Their supporting planes do not contain the chosen ideal cusp; their outward sides cannot contain that cusp, which lies in the closed polyhedron. There are only finitely many such disks.

Choose any 0<d<H and a midpoint r with

d/2<r<H-d/2.

For a large positive real X choose the TWO GLOBAL shaded-exterior flags

w=X+i(r-d/2), w0=1-X+i(r+d/2).                 (2)

They are between the two horizontal lines and, for sufficiently large X, outside every other bounded shaded disk. Thus w,w0 are in K. Recover the global image flag v=r_(F0)(w0) using the ACTUAL exterior white reflection F0. Together with kernel w this determines the common rank-one frame, provided v!=w.

At the chosen left prefix white face F, reflection is -conjugation. The literal prefix identity gives local image

z=r_F(w0)=X-1+i(r+d/2), z-w=-1+id.             (3)

This retains W_prefix v; it does not replace it by v. The equality v=w is avoidable: if F0 is one of the two incident vertical faces, its reflected image differs from w by +/-1+id; otherwise its bounded circle reflection sends w0 near infinity to a finite point as X grows, while w tends to infinity.

2. The deficient degree is exactly real and prescribed

Normalize the local projector by its prefix trace. Direct traces in (1) give

a0=1,
a2=Tr(P Pi)=-1-2/(z-w)=(1+id)/(1-id),
a1=Tr(A Pi)=-1+2zw/(z-w),
a1_out=-1+2(z-iH)(w-iH)/(z-w).                (4)

Put sigma=atan(d)/pi in (0,1/2). Then a2=exp(2pi i sigma). Both middle diagonals in (4) have the same leading term 2X^2/(-1+id). Its argument is pi+pi sigma, strictly between pi and pi+2pi sigma. For all sufficiently large X, zero is therefore strictly inside BOTH actual input/output prefix triangles. These are exactly the strict failed-triangle tests, with no boundary limit taken in the final frame.

Their canonical physical wire sums are 2-sigma, so the original high adapter gives the EXACT normalized complex degree sigma, with zero imaginary part. Write its two normalized magnetic rows as r_in, r_out. The actual high coordinates are

U_in=-a2/a1, U_out=-a2/a1_out.

Equations (4) imply

Re r_in, Re r_out -> sigma/2,
Im r_in, Im r_out =log(X)/pi+O(1),
Re(r_out-r_in)=H/(pi X)+O(X^-2)>0.             (5)

The errors are uniform when the midpoint r stays in a compact subinterval of (d/2,H-d/2). Hence both rows are strictly inside the real degree interval and are nonreal; their difference avoids the target-total seam for sufficiently large X.

Because H>=1, the UNIVERSAL choice d=1/sqrt(3) is allowed. It gives

sigma=1/6,  Re r_in,Re r_out ->1/12.           (6)

The construction can instead use any fixed d in (0,H). In particular it gives an open range of real deficient degrees when needed.

3. Exactly these two factors fail; all source seams can be avoided

The global polarization kernel line for w,w0 lies through the interior of the white-normal polyhedron. The two strict relations in (4) put its exits in the interiors of the two designated triangular facets. A line has only two exits from a convex polyhedron. Thus no other facet has zero in its prefix triangle. Once the finite real seams are excluded, every other ordinary factor is strictly physical; its K flags then select its actual source sheet by the full-domain selected-factor theorem.

Here is explicit seam control at FIXED d. It avoids an unjustified genericity argument on this restricted framing family. Up to one common scalar, the actual white prefix diagonals are the outward unit-circle polarizations. For a bounded white circle of center a+ib and radius R>0, remove only its positive factor 1/R and write

L=-X^2+X+a^2-a+(r-b)^2-d^2/4-R^2
                 +i[(r-b)(1-2X)+(a-1/2)d].     (7)

The two oriented vertical white forms are 1-id and 1+id. Formula (7) follows directly from (w-a-ib)(conjugate(w0)-a+ib)-R^2.

For distinct bounded circles, Im(L1 conjugate(L2)) is a nonzero polynomial in X,r. Its X^3 coefficient detects distinct heights b. If the heights agree, its X^2 coefficient detects distinct horizontal centers a because d!=0. If both centers agree, the radius difference multiplies the nonzero imaginary part of L. A bounded-circle/vertical pair has a nonzero X^2 coefficient proportional to d, and the two vertical forms have imaginary product +/-2d. Thus every ratio of distinct white prefix diagonals can simultaneously be made nonreal by choosing (X,r) off finitely many proper real polynomial zero sets. This also supplies the ordinary cup/cap seam tests.

The remaining specific regular-chart exclusions can be checked without claiming that an arbitrary divisor misses this family. Every bounded-circle form has the SAME-sign leading coefficient -1/R times X^2. Thus all required positive linear combinations, including a0+a2+4a1 and adjacent sums, have nonzero leading term. If only the two vertical forms occur in a sum, their sum is 2. For the discriminant a0a1+a1a2+a0a2, at least two bounded faces give a nonzero positive X^4 coefficient. The only case with one bounded face is the chosen pair of vertical faces; its leading coefficient is that bounded form times their sum 2, again nonzero. All prefix diagonals themselves are nonzero for large X.

Finally each head or disk product is a noncentral trace-minus-two parabolic in its actual Borel frame. Its diagonal is nonreal by the ratio tests, and so is not -1. Determinant one then forces both offdiagonal entries to be nonzero. This checks the required main K+1 and total E/F charts. Together with the preceding leading coefficient and discriminant tests, these are the finite exclusions listed in the regular geometric phase-chart theorem. The actual simple internal roots and their rational inverses are therefore regular as well.

All constraints hold on a nonempty open set of large X and allowed r. For an algebraic geometric certificate, take d=1/sqrt(3) and choose algebraic X,r outside the finite excluded polynomial sets. This gives an algebraic common frame. No rationality of the logarithms is claimed.

4. Exact magnetic branch data in this frame

The following identities specify the geometric branch against which an actual second-chamber source continuation must be compared. They do not assume that such a continuation has already been proved.

Put C0=cos(pi sigma)=1/sqrt(1+d^2) and L=2X-1. The two normalized determinant coordinates z_j=exp(pi i sigma)/U_j are exactly

z_in =C0[(L^2+1+d^2-4r^2)/2+2irL],
z_out=C0[(L^2+1+d^2-4(r-H)^2)/2+2i(r-H)L].     (8)

Thus z_in lies in the UPPER half-plane, and z_out in the LOWER half-plane, for every X>1/2 and the stated midpoint interval. The actual original HIGH internal-root formulas give

Z_in=-1/[L+2i(r+d/2)],
Z_out=1/[L+2i(r-H-d/2)].                      (9)

The output uses its ACTUAL disk normalization C^-1, so its kernel coordinate is w-iH. Omitting that shift would give an incorrect output root even though the leading real sign agrees.

For W(z)=1-2C0 z, equations (8) give perfect squares

W(z_in)=-C0^2(L+2ir)^2,
W(z_out)=-C0^2(L+2i(r-H))^2.                  (10)

Take sqrt W positive at negative real z and continue through the upper or lower half-plane, respectively. Its values are -iC0(L+2ir) at the input and +iC0(L+2i(r-H)) at the output. Substitution of (9) in the original magnetic momentum formula Y=(1-U)(TZ+U)/[-(U-Z)(T-U)], T=exp(2pi i sigma), yields exactly

Y_geo(z)=(z-i sqrt W(z))^2/[1-2C0 z+z^2].       (11)

For example, at the input put A=L+2ir. Both sides reduce to [(A-1)^2+d^2]/[(A+1)^2+d^2]. At the output the same formula with A=L+2i(r-H) is inverted. In particular Y_in=1-2/X+O(X^-2) and Y_out=1+2/X+O(X^-2).

Equation (11) is the branch obtained by REFLECTING the high-degree positive-center characteristic to negative z: z_high=-z changes its plus-square-root formula to the minus sign in (11). If an actual finite source continuation is anchored by that reflection, (8)–(11) identify its upper/lower continued characteristics with the actual geometric ones. They do not select a finite source by an arbitrary algebraic root or infer its relative amplitude.

5. The source-zero wall and global period remain

This common frame supplies real sigma and complex rows for a local source analysis. It does not authorize replacing a generally complex degree by discrete samples in a theorem that has not controlled that continuation. In particular, at a fixed INTEGER deficient t the retargeted separation h=d-t exceeds the folded polynomial degree a. The entire physical fixed-h slice of p_a(J) is identically zero, including every genuine generic-q jet. Deforming magnetic rows alone does not create a nonzero original source in that slice.

The geometry is therefore a useful actual branch and normalization test for the required continuation, not a contributing-cycle theorem. The entire finite root continuation is established separately and retains the physical support zeros. Its selected asymptotic through this wall, its genuine generic normals, and the global marked period still require separate proofs.

Focused audit: circle regularity and actual marked branch identities. It checks fourteen exact local identities, eighteen actual disk frames and thirty-six geometric characteristic comparisons. The global convex cut and avoidance arguments are the proofs in sections 1–3, not claims inferred from sampled polyhedra.

The real deficient-frame construction does not supply a finite second-chamber packet or a nonzero global period.
