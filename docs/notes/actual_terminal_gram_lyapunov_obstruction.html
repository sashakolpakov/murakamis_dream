1. A complete exact energy comparison

Put

ZN=∑j=0dsj2,vN=R−1e0. Z_N=\sum_{j=0}^d s_j^2,\qquad v_N=R^{-1}e_0.

Three literal finite crossing identities give everything needed:

Re0=κx−ded,(R−1e0)j=κ−1xjFd−j,|(Red)j|=sj. R e_0=\kappa x^{-d}e_d,\qquad (R^{-1}e_0)_j=\kappa^{-1}x^jF_{d-j},\qquad |(R e_d)_j|=s_j. \tag{3}

The second uses the original NEGATIVE crossing formula, rather than assuming a unitary inverse. The third uses root factorial reflection. In particular ||v_N||^2=Z_N, while ||R v_N||=1. Applying (1),

∥TvN∥2=|Pd|2/N2,∥TRvN∥2=∥Te0∥2=ZN/N2. \boxed{\displaystyle \|T v_N\|^2=|P_d|^2/N^2,\qquad \|T Rv_N\|^2=\|T e_0\|^2=Z_N/N^2.} \tag{4}

Both equalities are of COMPLETE coherent packet energies, not of one expanded shear path. The denominator is an original polynomial source endpoint, and the numerator is the entire positive factorial sum.

Let epsilon_N=exp(-N^2), and let the current positive terminal Gram be

GN=T†T+ϵNI. G_N=T^\dagger T+\epsilon_N I.

Every uniform Lyapunov inequality

R†GNR≼CNGN R^\dagger G_N R\preceq C_N G_N

must therefore satisfy the exact finite lower bound

This includes levels at which T is singular; nothing is inverted except the genuine original crossing. It also holds after tensoring the physical ports with any spectator coefficient Hilbert space.

The sine-factorial estimate, including its endpoint uniformity, gives

log⁡ZN=v3πN+O(log⁡N), \log Z_N=\frac{v_3}{\pi}N+O(\log N), \tag{6}

where v3 is the regular ideal tetrahedron volume. Indeed log s_j=N J(j/N)+O(log N), and J has its unique maximum v3/(2pi) at 5/6. Polynomially many terms and a nearest lattice point give (6). No volume-conjecture asymptotic is a premise.

So the conclusion is not caused only by the Euclidean size of v_N.

2. Any uniformly comparable moving metric must pay the same cost

The statement also quantifies what an adaptive metric has to change. Suppose positive metrics G_before,G_after obey

Gbefore≼ANGN,Gafter≽BN−1GN, G_{\mathrm{before}}\preceq A_N G_N,\qquad G_{\mathrm{after}}\succeq B_N^{-1}G_N,

and a propagation inequality is asserted:

R†GafterR≼CNGbefore. R^\dagger G_{\mathrm{after}}R\preceq C_N G_{\mathrm{before}}.

Testing v_N proves, without additional assumptions,

CNANBN≥ZN+N2ϵN|Pd|2+N2ϵNZN. C_N A_N B_N\geq \frac{Z_N+N^2\epsilon_N}{|P_d|^2+N^2\epsilon_N Z_N}. \tag{9}

If A_N and B_N are exp(o(N)), C_N must carry the exponent in (7). Conversely a subexponential step cost requires exponential metric motion relative to this terminal metric, of at least the displayed amount. Exact pullback of a metric can of course make an individual step an isometry; (9) says that its change of scale cannot be ignored when comparing it with this fixed boundary-conditioned metric.

This does not forbid an angle/gluing dependent metric with a genuine global certificate for its exponential variation. It specifies one necessary quantitative condition such a construction must meet.
