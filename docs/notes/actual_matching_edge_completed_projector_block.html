The matching-edge target gauge and its completed projector pairs

Follow-up: the native-energy theorem now gives the column measures and polynomial relative derivative costs for these completed operators. Their explicit magnetic energy can be exponential; its sharp use in the joint contraction remains open.

30 October 2026. The remaining scalar target normalization in the exact four-port block is the actual Casimir projector normalization times a regular face factor. Its universal physical scale cancels the cup bend. Pairing the two colliding target channels BEFORE specialization gives a regular Hermite block with explicitly controlled scalar jets.

The native norms of the completed projectors and their derivatives remain to be estimated. This theorem neither makes the root channels orthogonal in a positive metric nor bounds a surrounding contraction.

1. Exact objects and the generic norm

Use the original conventions of the marked four-port block. Put d=N-1, zeta=q exp(t), q=exp(pi i/N), Q=zeta^2, and

0<=g<=d, E=2g, n=d-g,
L=N zeta^(-d^2), pV=L p_d, ellV=pV^-1.

The canonical ordinary channel cup p_(2g) has first coefficient one. Let W_g=W_dd^(2g), and retain its genuine categorical dual

D(W_g)=p_(2g)^T W_g^T(ellV^T tensor ellV^T)P,
N_g=W_g D(W_g),
D(W_g)W_g=theta_g I_(2g+1).                      (1)

These are generic rational identities, including high g. The last scalar follows from covariance and the generic highest-weight basis. No root value of theta_g is inverted.

Let Omega be the ORIGINAL generic two-physical-head Casimir. Its distinct generic eigenvalues are

lambda_g=zeta^(2g+1)+zeta^(-2g-1), 0<=g<=d,
Pi_g=product_(h!=g)(Omega-lambda_h I)/(lambda_g-lambda_h).
                                                           (2)

The Clebsch images are independent because their eigenvalues differ, and sum_g(2g+1)=N^2. Thus they exhaust the generic tensor product. Consequently N_g=theta_g Pi_g as an identity of ACTUAL operators.

The exact scalar is

theta_g=(-1)^(d-g) zeta^(2d^2-g^2)/N^2
                   *[d+g+1]!/([d-g]![2g+1]!).   (3)

Here is a direct proof from the original matrix, without a quoted theta or quantum-group norm formula. In physical degree d the entry of the bent tensor S_(2g) at (d,0,0,d) is the previously derived chi X. Undoing the physical bend gives

(N_g)_(d,0)=zeta^[2g(g-d)] F_d^2 /
                        (L^2 F_(d-g)^2 F_(2g)),  (4)

where the two indices in (4) are the first physical magnetic index in that degree-d block. In particular its row d means (d,0), and its column zero means (0,d). The product of the original lower offdiagonals of that tridiagonal block is

product_(r=0)^(d-1) zeta^-N(1-Q^(r+1))^2
                                  =zeta^(-Nd) F_d^2.

The extreme adjugate entry of zI-Omega therefore gives

(Pi_g)_(d,0)=zeta^(-Nd)F_d^2/chi'(lambda_g),
chi(z)=product_(h=0)^d(z-lambda_h).              (5)

This adjugate identity is the unique lower path through a tridiagonal matrix; it also follows by induction on its size. The elementary factorization of eigenvalue differences yields

chi'(lambda_g)=(zeta-zeta^-1)^(2d)(-1)^(d-g)
                          [d-g]![d+g+1]!/[2g+1]. (6)

Divide (4) by (5). Since F_k=zeta[k(k-1)/2](1-Q)k[k]!, the residual exponent before inserting L is -g^2. Substituting L=N zeta-d2 proves (3).

2. The actual target scalar becomes a face factor

The exact target product of the preceding block theorem is

h_g=1/(b_(2g)J_(d-g)V_(d-g))
   =(-1)^d zeta^(g^2+d)
       [g]!^2[d-g]!^2[2g+1]![2d+1]! /
                             ([d]!^2[d+g+1]!^2).

Define the already proved regular face factor and a universal scalar

D_g=(-1)^g[N][g]!^2[d-g]!/[d+g+1]!,
beta_N=zeta^(2d^2+d)/N^2
                      *[2d+1]!/([N][d]!^2).     (7)

Multiplication of the ACTUAL products gives exactly

h_g theta_g=beta_N D_g.                          (8)

At the root D_g(q)=1 for every g, while

beta_N(q)=(-1)^d q^(d+2)/N^2.                    (9)

All these root-zero cancellations are performed generically. Both D_g and beta_N/beta_N(q) are holomorphic units on a uniform disk of radius c/[N^2(1+log N)], and every fixed relative jet has polynomial cost, by their explicit root-unit factorial products.

For clarity, let R be the ACTUAL last-to-first cyclic tensor turn

(R Psi)(u,v,w,z)=(-1)^d zeta^(d-2u) Psi(v,w,z,u),
B(K)=K(pV tensor pV)P.

Equations (1)–(8) give the exact typed target identity

h_g T_(2g)=beta_N R B(D_g Pi_g).                 (10)

It holds at generic zeta through the entire range 0<=g<=d. The physical reversal and odd-d turn remain in (10). At the root the norm of B on Hilbert–Schmidt matrices is N^2, and the norm of R is one. Equation (9) cancels that physical scale. Fixed generic derivatives of the combined map beta_N R B have only polynomial cost. This removes its scalar factorial cost; it says nothing by itself about the operator norms of the Pi_g.

3. Pair before taking the normal

Fix the original folded row a, and put

F_ag=G_ag D_g,
G_ag=(-1)^d S_ag/[N],
F_ag(q)=R_N(a,g)>0.

For a paired target choose 0<=g<g’=d-g and set

Q_g=Pi_g+Pi_g',
M_g=(Omega-lambda_g' I)Q_g,
H_ag=(F_ag-F_ag')/(lambda_g-lambda_g').           (11)

Each of the three quantities in (11) is holomorphic at the root. For Q_g, the quadratic cluster factor (z-lambda_g)(z-lambda_g’) is coprime there to the product of the other cluster factors. Bezout gives its spectral projector as a polynomial in Omega with coefficients regular at q. This is the generic sum in (11), so it remains regular through the collision. The polynomial identity product_h(Omega-lambda_h I)=0 holds generically and hence at q; in particular

Q_g(q)^2=Q_g(q),
M_g(q)^2=0.                                     (12)

The odd simple target g=d/2 is kept separately and its Pi_g is already regular. No positivity or Hermitian self-adjointness is asserted for any of these projectors.

For H_ag, the numerator vanishes because the positive scalar partners have the same root value and D_g(q)=D_g’(q)=1. The denominator has a SIMPLE zero, with its actual TARGET convention

lambda_g-lambda_g'
   =-(zeta^s-zeta^-s)(zeta^N-zeta^-N), s=d-2g,
partial_t(lambda_g-lambda_g')(0)=2N Delta_g,
Delta_g=q^(2g+1)-q^(-2g-1).                      (13)

This sign is opposite to the compiler’s small-index minus large-index gap: its channel index is m=d-g. It must not be inferred from a root-only eigenvalue formula.

The exact regular paired identity is

F_ag Pi_g+F_ag' Pi_g'
                   =F_ag' Q_g+H_ag M_g.          (14)

Thus at the root it is R_N(a,g)Q_g(q)+H_ag(q)M_g(q). The nilpotent Hermite term is retained; equality of the two scalar root values does not remove it.

There are uniform scalar jet estimates, for every fixed r,

|[t^r]F_ag|<=C_r P_N^r R_N(a,g),
|[t^r]H_ag|<=C_r P_N^(r+1) R_N(a,g),
P_N=N^2(1+log N).                               (15)

Indeed the first bound is the existing scalar/face theorem. On a slightly smaller common disk, (F_ag-F_ag’)/t has size at most C P_N R_N(a,g) by Cauchy’s formula and its zero at the center. The function (lambda_g-lambda_g’)/t is a unit on that disk, uniformly bounded below: its root magnitude is 4N sin(pi s/N)>=8, and its two elementary factors have controlled relative changes there. Dividing and applying Cauchy again proves the second bound. Both numerator and gap derivatives are included.

4. The resulting source interface and its remaining estimate

The full weighted recoupling inside the block is now

sum_(g=0)^d G_ag h_g T_(2g)
   =beta_N R B[ sum_(g<d-g)(F_ag' Q_g+H_ag M_g)
              + the odd simple term F_(a,d/2) Pi_(d/2) ].   (16)

The last term is included only when d is even. Its a is still the original folded row label.

Every term on the right is regular BEFORE the whole-network first normal is taken. Its first derivative uses only Q_g,M_g and their FIRST generic-q derivatives. The scalar H_ag’ already retains the necessary second derivatives of the original F weights. Equation (16) is equivalent to the prior Taylor-two Laurent extraction, with the partner cancellation performed explicitly rather than left between separate singular target terms.

There is also a complete cancellation of the original universal scalar. Retain u_a=rho_a/rho_a(q) from the marked block theorem, put v_N=beta_N/beta_N(q), and define the ACTUAL physical reshuffle

A(t)=B(t)^-1 R(t) B(t).

The original measure identity and (9) give

h0_a gamma_a^-1 rho_a(q) beta_N(q)=k_a N,
h0_a K_a(t)=k_a N u_a(t)v_N(t) A(t) E_a(t),      (17)

where E_a denotes the regular completed sum in square brackets in (16). Every factor in (17) is regular. At the root A is an isometry for the Hilbert–Schmidt norm: B is N^2 times a unitary map, R is unitary, and B^-1 is N^-2 times a unitary map. Its fixed jets have polynomial cost. The equalities use the original physical cups, not a new metric on the internal channels.

Let C(t) be the ordinary complementary closure and nu_N its original trace-normal constant as in the preceding marked block theorem. Then the exact signed weighted sector is

h0_a omega_a^epsilon Z_Delta
   =k_a N omega_a^epsilon/nu_N
             *[t^1]{u_a v_N C(t)(A(t)E_a(t))}.  (18)

The retained phase omega_a^epsilon remains unchanged. For a concrete upper bound define, in the ORIGINAL physical basis,

J_C^(1)=max_(r=0,1)||[t^r]C||,
J_g^cl=max_(r=0,1)(||[t^r]Q_g||_HS+||[t^r]M_g||_HS),
J_mid=max_(r=0,1)||[t^r]Pi_(d/2)||_HS
                                  (only when d is even).

Using (15),(17) and |nu_N|=N/sin(pi/N) gives

|h0_a omega_a^epsilon Z_Delta|
   <=C k_a sin(pi/N) P_N^2 J_C^(1)
       *[sum_(g<d-g) R_N(a,g)J_g^cl
                     + R_N(a,d/2)J_mid].        (19)

The last summand is again omitted when d is odd. A weighted Cauchy inequality can be applied to this positive scalar sum, with these specified native cluster costs. Formula (19) improves the earlier unpaired target bound: only regular completed cluster first jets remain, and no target scalar factorial gauge is left unidentified. It does not assert that the J_g^cl are polynomial or subexponential.

The physical generic-jet theorem controls all fixed q jets of original normalized physical columns and their genuine divided pair. It is relevant to the next step, but does not automatically bound the Q_g,M_g here. Their exact column and dual measures, their operator norms, and their sewing against the complementary network must still be controlled. In particular a root spectral derivative cannot be substituted for Q_g’ or M_g’. The latter contain the variation of the actual Casimir matrix and its generic spectral data.

The progress in (16) is an exact regular target block with no unidentified exponential scalar normalization, uniformly at both parities. A sharp bound for its native completed projector costs and the nonzero global selected contribution remain separate tasks.
