Adaptive resonant sampling of the actual source and the raw-quotient obstruction

28 October 2026. There is a constructive EXACT adaptive Fermat sampling which puts the complete original finite source on the resonant divisor; Section 5 proves it entrywise for either crossing sign. This is a genuine base change before the original finite windows. It evades, rather than contradicts, the fixed-scalar raw-quotient obstruction proved first below. No arbitrary exterior coupon, renormalized invariant, or generic oscillatory countermodel is involved. Other original Newton/Rogers currents remain compulsory after this sampling.

1. The closure character is invisible to the complete finite crossing

Keep the ports and complete signed Laurent kernels of the actual trivalent crossing. Inputs are z0,z1 and outputs are z6,z7. Put d=N-1. For fixed t0!=0, independent of the head variables, the character in the exact resonant Fourier source is

A=(z7/z1)^N-(z7/t0)^N,
D=1-A.                                           (1)

For the positive crossing it is exactly exp(N lambda)(1-exp(N z)), with the retained marks lambda=Logz7-Logz1+2pi*i and z=h+Logz1-Logt0. Equation (1) is a Laurent polynomial, so its monodromy notation does not introduce an additional choice here.

Let K_sigma(t) be the COMPLETE raw signed kernel, including all finite Newton/Rogers factors and the original shared negative Gaussian when sigma=-. Let Pi_phys extract the original finite input/output block. Then, identically before taking the t constant term,

Pi_phys[A K_sigma(t)]=0,
Pi_phys[D K_sigma(t)]=Pi_phys[K_sigma(t)].         (2)

This also holds after the exact finite root-orbit average, for every N, every original color, and either crossing sign.

The proof is the actual finite output support. In the positive layer the last Newton factor is

B_N(x^(-1)z7/z6,x^(-2)z7/t),

and in the negative layer the last Rogers factor is

R_N(g_- z7/t,x^(-1)z7/z6).

Every monomial in either has second-output exponent e between 0 and d; none of the other factors contains z7. Both monomials of A contain z7^N. They therefore force second-output exponent at least N, which the original finite output window excludes. The first monomial also raises the required second input by N, but that additional exclusion is not needed. Gaussian integration changes coefficients and cannot change these exponents. The same proof works term by term on any fixed scalar root orbit. This proves (2) without assuming that the meromorphic homogeneous/current pieces separately have finite support.

More generally Pi_phys[A^k K_sigma]=0 for every k>=1. If a polynomial f(A) is inserted directly on this complete local kernel, its finite physical projection is f(0) times that of the uninserted kernel.

2. This is an exact statement for the whole original knot source

Apply (2) at any physical crossing of an actual signed word, retaining all original input/output barriers, pivots, markings, and framing. The physical local operator is unchanged if it is multiplied by D. Consequently its contraction with the original source is unchanged. This applies to nonzero hyperbolic-knot periods as well as to general words; for example the independently proved original figure-eight source is

K_N(4_1)=sum_(n=0)^d |(Q;Q)_n|^2>0.              (3)

Thus there is no exact raw restriction of this original source to A=1 which retains the COMPLETE inserted crossing and its D-inserted source pairing: restriction would send D K_sigma to zero, whereas its original pairing is the nonzero quantity (3).

This does NOT authorize the opposite algebra quotient A=0. Equation (2) is a statement about a specified finite projection of a specified complete kernel. The finite projection is not multiplicative on the Laurent algebra: A has a factor z7^N, while multiplication first by z7^(-N) can return those exponents to the physical output window. An arbitrary later operator/coupon need not retain (2). Accordingly the precise assertion is the actual insertion-functional identity (2), not an algebraic support statement for the whole coupon algebra.

Nor can (2) be applied separately to the meromorphic S/I sectors. Those sectors are not finite Laurent kernels; their continued high Fourier modes are exactly what restore the original finite source in their sum.

3. What the orbit and deck equations do determine

The root-orbit map proves equality of the COMPLETE original sourced functional with its finite-orbit version. The only unwanted orbit alias has positive integer degree N, and the original closure kills every nonempty alias selection. Invariance under changing the fixed scalar t0 is therefore exact. These are consequences of the original finite degree/source data; they are not new constraints forcing A=1.

Likewise the local exact formula

T_N=H_N F_ref+Psi_+(z)(1-A) I_-

has a completely normalized, nonzero reference coefficient H_N on the regular part of A=1. Its compulsory current restores the source away from that divisor. Equation (2) shows why one cannot obtain the original global geometric coefficient by setting that current to zero through a raw multiplicative restriction of the complete source.

Beyond the adaptive map in Section 5, the wanted additional structure is a SOURCE map to an action-normalized geometric vanishing-cycle/formal block, including the finite window and its currents. One must calculate the image of the actual marked class there and show that its coefficient is nonzero with subexponential normalization. This would be a genuinely derived or microlocal source comparison; its local resonant residue cannot simply be substituted for the complete finite pointing.

4. Exactly what compact divisor rigidity would and would not prove

If two meromorphic sections of the same line on a connected compact normal variety have the SAME complete divisor, their ratio is a nowhere zero global holomorphic function, hence constant. One exact nonzero value fixes that constant. This is a valid rigidity principle.

Its present application is missing two pieces of actual data: a globally defined geometric component with its full divisor, and the map of the original source/cosource to that component. Known local homogeneous divisors and total deck invariance do not supply either piece. The complete section includes currents with different divisors and different continued homogeneous components. Even uniqueness of the COMPLETE exact section would not by itself compute its decomposition into action-normalized geometric periods.

The obstruction (2) is stronger than an abstract caution: the original finite pointing already pairs nontrivially with the closure defect which raw resonant restriction would erase. Therefore a successful global compact-torsor rigidity argument must explicitly transport that pointing, rather than infer its balanced support from the fixed-scalar orbit map. The following adaptive construction does transport it to a balanced sampling of the COMPLETE finite kernel.

5. A constructive adaptive Fermat sampling evades the obstruction

Define a head-dependent orbit radius by the algebraic equation

t0^(-N)=c(z1,z7):=z1^(-N)-z7^(-N).               (4)

On c!=0 choose any of its N roots and sample the COMPLETE raw kernel on the orbit t=t0 Q^j. The finite sum is independent of that root choice. Since K_sigma(t) has t exponents in [-2d,d], its average is exactly

CT_t K_sigma+c(z1,z7) C_sigma,
C_sigma=[t^(-N)]K_sigma.                         (5)

Now apply the original physical windows. Every monomial of C_sigma has incoming second exponent -b with b>=0 and outgoing second exponent e with 0<=e<=d. The first term z1^(-N)C_sigma in (5) requires incoming second index b+N>=N, and the second term z7^(-N)C_sigma has outgoing second exponent e-N<0. Both are excluded. Therefore, ENTRYWISE,

Pi_phys [(1/N)sum_(j=0)^d K_sigma(t0 Q^j)]
                       =Pi_phys CT_t K_sigma.    (6)

This is true at either sign and every original color, with the same continuous Gaussian retained for a negative crossing. It consequently preserves every original whole-word source, without needing a global degree argument to cancel residual local aliases. Its scalar and all finite barriers are unchanged.

By (4), the actual character (1) is identically A=1 on this algebraic sampling. Thus the local resonant Fourier comparison has its exact reference coefficient H_N and no additional closure current. This closes a source-balancing step which a fixed scalar orbit did not close. The construction does not erase the OTHER original B/R current sectors created before that one-function comparison. An additional balanced external Fourier-frame completion can remove those local currents too. Its degree proof must be performed before the original whole source is applied; it is not a consequence of (6) alone. That construction is now proved in the local balanced Fourier theorem and the whole-word source theorem. It uses independent regular local grids and retains the exact rational Fourier adapters. The remaining geometric source problem concerns that explicit sewn network, rather than an unidentified local normalizer.

At c=0 an individual sampled radius tends to infinity. The COMPLETE finite average nevertheless has the explicit Laurent-polynomial extension (5), and its physical projection has the extension (6). Separated homogeneous/current expressions require their matched joint continuation there; one cannot infer separate finite limits from (5).

There is no contradiction with (2): substituting (4) before projection changes the head dependence of the moment variables. It is not an A-linear restriction of the fixed-scalar inserted source. Algebraic base change and the physical projection do not commute. This is the precise mechanism by which the adaptive source map evades the no-go.

6. The remaining adaptive local norms form a two-generator boundary ideal

There is a concrete algebraic target for that additional transport at complete color. Write

a=z0^N, b=z1^N, c=z6^N, d=z7^N,
s=x^N, s^2=1, T=t0^N=bd/(d-b).

On the regular torus, the first Newton, final Newton, and middle Rogers norms in the original positive layer are, respectively,

D_first=T[1/(s a)-1/d],
D_B=d[1/b-1/(s c)],
D_R=1-c/a-c/(s T)
    =-(s c/T)D_first-(c/(s d))D_B.               (7)

The last equality uses only 1/T=1/b-1/d and s^2=1. Thus the THREE norms generate exactly the two boundary-exchange relations d-s a and b-s c after localization at the displayed nonzero coordinates. They vanish jointly precisely when

c=s b, d=s a.                                   (8)

Keep the source-selected decompositions B0=S0+D_first I0, R=T_R+D_R J, Bf=Sf+D_B If, with all logarithmic marks. Their complete scalar kernel obeys the exact aggregate identity

B0 R Bf-S0 T_R Sf
  =D_first[I0 R Bf-(s c/T)S0 J Bf]
   +D_B[S0 T_R If-(c/(s d))S0 J Bf].             (9)

It follows by expanding in the stated order and then substituting (7). This reduces the remaining local current transport to TWO specified boundary relations, rather than three unrelated local normalizers. It does not assert that the raw finite projection annihilates each meromorphic term of (9). Regular balanced external sampling can impose (8) before the finite projection, and must retain its Fourier changes of frame when the local blocks are sewn.

The audit checks the full raw signed finite layers, the actual physical R matrices, nonzero discarded terms, finite-orbit aliases, and original pivotal whole-word source identities. The grading/output-support proof above is uniform in N.
