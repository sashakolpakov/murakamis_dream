Exact deck characters of the original four-function crossing

28 October 2026. The exact balanced Fourier crossing has a specified continuation away from its sampling grids. Its four full-turn characters are explicit rational functions of the central coordinates. They compute the logarithmic seams in a contour deformation of the original period. A function that is periodic at the original grid need not remain periodic between the grid points.

This calculation uses the full function, its Gaussian and every affine logarithmic mark. It does not infer a geometric cycle from a rational character or identify the central variety below with the knot A-curve.

1. The fixed continuation and its four characters

Use the EXACT kernel (14) of the balanced crossing theorem. Put h=2pii/N, t=2pii, X=pi*i-h/2, x=exp(X), s=x^N, and retain the port logarithms a,b,c,d. In particular s=(-1)^(N+1). Let

A=exp(Na), B=exp(Nb), C=exp(Nc), D=exp(Nd),
beta=b-a-X, f=d-c-X, rho=c-a,
lambda=d-b+t, G=d-a-X, H=b-c+X,
E=N f+(N+1)lambda/2+(G H+lambda^2/2)/h.

The full continuation is

M=constant * (C/A)(1-C/A)^2 exp(E)
   *Psi(beta)Psi(h-f)Psi(lambda+t+h)/Psi(rho).       (1)

The constant includes the original kappa*zeta_0^2/N. The old central parameters are used as the values of these exponentials, rather than replacing exp(Nrho) by a new independent constant off the grid.

For a full positive turn of the indicated port, the exact ratios are

M(a+t,b,c,d)/M = mu_a = s C(A-sB)/[B(A-C)],
M(a,b+t,c,d)/M = mu_b = s(D-B)/(A-sB),
M(a,b,c+t,d)/M = mu_c = s(A-C)/(D-sC),
M(a,b,c,d+t)/M = mu_d = s B(D-sC)/[C(D-B)].         (2)

These are meromorphic identities on the retained logarithmic cover; the displayed quotients are first interpreted where all factors are regular. No imaginary part is reset to a principal argument.

Proof: the full function obeys

Psi(z+t)/Psi(z)=1/(1-exp(Nz)),
Psi(z-t)/Psi(z)=1-exp(Nz).

The four exponential arguments in (1) have central values

U=sB/A, V=sC/D, W=D/B, R=C/A.

The Gaussian factors exp(Delta E), for turns in a,b,c,d respectively, are

sC/B, -U, sA/D, -sD/C.                         (3)

For example a turn in b has Delta E=-(N+1)t/2+N G-N lambda+Nt/2 =-t/2+N(beta-t), so its factor is -U, including the half-turn sign. Combining (3) with the four full-function multipliers gives (2). Thus neither a Gaussian phase nor a complete-color sign was dropped.

2. Balanced grids and their off-grid seams

On the local sampling multisection C=sB, D=sA all four characters equal one. More precisely,

mu_a-1 = A(sC-B)/[B(A-C)],
mu_b-1 = (sD-A)/(A-sB),
mu_c-1 = (sA-D)/(D-sC),
mu_d-1 = D(sB-C)/[C(D-B)].                     (4)

Hence, on this regular chart, the simultaneous unit-character locus is EXACTLY C=sB, D=sA. The converse follows from the first two numerators, since A,B are nonzero. The other two equations are redundant. Also

mu_a mu_b mu_c mu_d=1.                         (5)

This is the common-scale symmetry, not four independent gluing equations. All four shifts commute because the characters depend only on A,B,C,D, which are invariant under full logarithmic turns.

For example, start with a balanced grid (A0,B,C,D), C=sB and D=sA0, and vary only its a-port away from A=A0. Then mu_a=1 still, but

mu_b-1=(A0-A)/(A-sB),
mu_c-1=s(A-A0)/(D-sC).

The other ports acquire seams. Periodicity in the moved port alone therefore does not justify a simultaneous closed-torus deformation. These formulas locate the exact central differences that recur in the wire-current calculation.

3. Sewn characters and the limitation of grid periodicity

For an internal variable shared by two original crossing ports, the full-turn ratio of the product is the product of its two characters from (2), with the orientations and negative-crossing conjugate transport retained. The rational boundary co-source is single-valued in that variable; it adds no logarithmic multiplier. Thus the sewn scalar form has a directly computable rational deck character nu_e. On the propagated balanced central multisection, nu_e=1 because each incident local character is one.

Off that multisection, imposing nu_e=1 is a NONTRIVIAL equation, not a consequence of the Fourier frame change. A logarithmic branch lip contributes a multiple of (nu_e-1) times the complete kernel. Such a lip cannot be omitted on the grounds that its value at the root grid is zero. In particular, zeros on the grid may cancel the grid denominator and leave a regular but nonzero relative source term. The exact Newton/Rogers examples in the linked wire note exhibit that mechanism.

The open-braid frame theorem now removes all interior frame adapters, but it does not remove the logarithmic seams of this off-grid full continuation. The characters (2) specify those seams without an unspecified torsor multiplier. Geometric selection still requires an actual relative-period transformation, with these terms retained.

4. Verification

The independent finite-product audit computes Psi from its rational product and dilogarithm definition, then checks every full-turn ratio at both parities, without applying the shift law in the evaluator. Symbolic checks verify (4), (5), and the unit-character locus. These checks supplement the uniform calculation (2)–(3); they are not a knot volume test.
