A complete positive Fourier–Hermite filling Gram and an actual source discriminant

29 October 2026. The full crossing-circle kernel gives an explicit positive, full-rank coherent Gram, independent of the unknown period. Its filling-source dual norm is exactly 1+ell2(N2-1)/(12N^2). It retains every confluent Casimir jet. The same construction has a precise limitation: its energy is polynomially equivalent to the whole mixed-color packet energy, so it does not automatically see the smaller filled volume.

1. Complete data and the positive metric

Use the ORIGINAL kernel and conventions of the Fourier–Hermite theorem. For an integer crossing power ell let h_k=nu_k^ell, d_k=-ell(d-2k)h_k/2, w_k=q^(2k+1), d=N-1. Put

Hn=N−1∑khkwk−n,Dn=N−1∑kdkwk−n. H_n=N^{-1}\sum_kh_kw_k^{-n},\qquad D_n=N^{-1}\sum_kd_kw_k^{-n}.

Their exact marked aliases are H_(N-n)=-H_n, D_(N-n)=D_n, D0=0. For n<N/2 write m=N-n and r=n/N. Define N orthonormal compatible-data coordinates

y0=H0,(yn,H,yn,D)=(2Hn,2Dn/N), y_0=H_0,\qquad (y_{n,H},y_{n,D})=(\sqrt2H_n,\sqrt2D_n/N),

with the additional y_mid=D_(N/2)/N when N is even. At that index H_(N/2)=0. Odd N has no middle coordinate; its simple Casimir node has only a value, with logarithmic derivative zero automatically.

Let a be the first-kind Chebyshev coefficient vector. The exact alias inverse a=K y is

$$ a_0=y_0,\qquad \begin{pmatrix}a_n\\a_m\end{pmatrix} =\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1-r&1\\-r&1\end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix}y_{n,H}\\y_{n,D}\end{pmatrix}, \qquad a_{N/2}=y_{\rm mid}. \tag{1} $$

Define the real difference matrix S by βj=(−1)j(aj−aj+2)\beta_j=(-1)^j(a_j-a_{j+2}), with a_N=a_(N+1)=0, and set B=S K. The original all-input filling identity is

Rℓ=κℓ∑j=0dβjLj+1,β=By. R^{\ell}=\kappa^{\ell}\sum_{j=0}^d\beta_jL_{j+1}, \qquad\beta=B y. \tag{2}

Every L_(j+1) is the literal ordinary auxiliary-circle operator, with its full Cartan and pivot and no added color average, by the original circle-source proof.

For a complete mixed-color exterior packet A=(A0,…,Ad)^T put z=B^T A and

EN(A)=∥z∥2=A†GNA,GN=B¯B𝖳>0. \boxed{\quad E_N(A)=\|z\|^2=A^\dagger G_N A, \qquad G_N=\overline B B^{\mathsf T}>0.\quad} \tag{3}

B is real in these coordinates, but the conjugation in (3) makes the complex Riesz convention explicit. There is a particularly simple direct formula. Set eta_n=(-1)^n(A_n-A_(n-2)), A_negative=0. Then z0=eta0, and for n<m,

zn,H=((1−r)ηn−rηm)/2,zn,D=(ηn+ηm)/2, z_{n,H}=((1-r)\eta_n-r\eta_m)/\sqrt2,\qquad z_{n,D}=(\eta_n+\eta_m)/\sqrt2,

with z_mid=eta_mid when present. Thus the energy keeps explicit cross-color interference, not just separate squared color values.

Marked Fourier Parseval gives the EXACT source cost

∥yℓ∥2=1N∑k(|hk|2+|dk/N|2)=1+ℓ2(N2−1)12N2=:cN,ℓ. \boxed{\displaystyle \|y_\ell\|^2=\frac1N\sum_k(|h_k|^2+|d_k/N|^2) =1+\frac{\ell^2(N^2-1)}{12N^2}=:c_{N,\ell}.} \tag{4}

Here sum_k(d-2k)2=N(N2-1)/3. Since B is invertible, this is also the exact dual norm beta^T G_N^(-1) conjugate(beta), not just an upper bound. For the original scalar, with its actual external line chi retained,

|χκℓβ𝖳A|2≤|χ|2cN,ℓEN(A). |\chi\kappa^{\ell}\beta^{\mathsf T}A|^2 \leq|\chi|^2c_{N,\ell}E_N(A). \tag{5}

The pairing is y^T z; its Hilbert Riesz vector is conjugate(y). No unknown closed invariant defines the metric.

Equations (3)–(5) also apply to Hilbert-valued packets. For arbitrary operator-valued exteriors A_j, preserving their original order, they give the all-input positive inequality

(∑jβjAj)†(∑jβjAj)≼cN,ℓ∑rZr†Zr. (\sum_j\beta_jA_j)^\dagger(\sum_j\beta_jA_j) \preceq c_{N,\ell}\sum_r Z_r^\dagger Z_r. \tag{6}

All original Casimir jets are retained. This is a genuine positive filling port, not an inverse of the possibly nilpotent complete-color circle alone.

2. Polynomial conditioning and simultaneous fillings

Each two-by-two block in (1) has norm at most sqrt(3/2) and inverse norm at most sqrt6. Also ||S||<=2 and ||S^(-1)||<=N, by finite summation along each parity chain. Therefore

(6N2)−1∥A∥2≤EN(A)≤6∥A∥2,cond⁡(GN)≤36N2. \boxed{\displaystyle (6N^2)^{-1}\|A\|^2\leq E_N(A)\leq6\|A\|^2, \qquad\operatorname{cond}(G_N)\leq36N^2.} \tag{7}

For s specified twist boxes use the tensor product transform B^T on the s color registers, AFTER retaining their entire original mixed-color exterior. The dual cost is exactly product_i c_(N,ell_i), and the Gram is positive full rank with polynomial conditioning for fixed s. Physical boxes may overlap in time; the original operator order in each packet entry is not changed. This does not assume independent physical contractions.

For full twists ell_i=2p_i, the cost is at most product_i(1+p_i^2/3), independent of N. It is subexponential whenever sum_i log(1+|p_i|)=o(N). All source, framing, and peripheral factors remain the exact ones outside the filling identity (2).

3. A complete actual source discriminant

Use the original four-plat multiplicities, cup p, cap ell and full crossings R1,R2 from the source theorems. Define ONE fixed base packet

Aj=ℓR22Lj+1(Ω12)R1R2p,0≤j<N. A_j=\ell R_2^2L_{j+1}(\Omega_{12})R_1R_2p,\quad0\leq j<N.

The raw filled period with slope p is F_p=ell R2^2 R1^(2p+1) R2 p=kappa^(2p) beta_(2p)^T A. The original normalization/cup slide and whole figure-eight source give the EXACT values

F0=A0=xd=κ2,F−1=ZN:=∑a=0d|(Q;Q)a|2. F_0=A_0=x^d=\kappa^2,\qquad F_{-1}=Z_N:=\sum_{a=0}^d|(Q;Q)_a|^2. \tag{8}

No augmented geometric saddle is assumed. The second value is the literal original figure-eight whole-source identity; the first is the actual all-input normalization tangle. Apply (5) with ell=-2. Since c_(N,-2)<=4/3,

EN(A)≥34ZN2. \boxed{\displaystyle E_N(A)\geq\frac34 Z_N^2.} \tag{9}

At p=0 the exact dual cost is 1 and the filled raw scalar has modulus one, yet the SAME coherent positive energy has positive exponential rate. This is a fully contracted original packet, not an expanded absolute-value path or a guessed augmented invariant.

It also quantifies the requirement on a different color metric H. The bound ||B||^2<=6 and (4) give ||beta_(-2)||^2<=8. Hence ||A||2>=Z_N2/8. The p=0 source is beta0=e0, so its Cauchy budget under ANY positive H satisfies

(A†HA)(e0†H−1e0)≥ZN2/[8cond⁡(H)]. (A^\dagger H A)(e_0^\dagger H^{-1}e_0) \geq Z_N^2/[8\operatorname{cond}(H)]. \tag{10}

A zero-exponent sharp budget for this normalization therefore requires exponential metric anisotropy, or actual coherent pruning before the energy is formed. Polynomial-conditioned changes of the complete Fourier–Hermite norm cannot supply it. Exponential anisotropy is allowed; (10) is a necessary quantitative condition, not a no-go theorem for all coherent metrics.

4. The same defect persists in a hyperbolic final knot

There is a source-exact all-width insertion, not merely a scalar coincidence. Let M_j be the physical one-input/one-output tangle whose complete source entry is A_j. Each crossing, original turn, and auxiliary pivotal loop is a Hopf map. Thus M_j commutes with K and F on the original complete V_N. Its K eigenvalues are distinct, so it is diagonal; every interior F coefficient is nonzero, so all diagonal entries agree. Consequently M_j=A_j I at EVERY N.

The p=0 tangle is topologically the straight unframed strand. Here is the local move proof, separately from its scalar evaluation: slide the free strand past the neighboring cup (the oriented cup-slide Reidemeister-II isotopy), leaving the two crossings directly above that cup. Each is a removable Reidemeister-I kink on the same arc. The remaining cup/cap cancels by the snake isotopy. No closed component remains. In the actual framed tensor calculation the two kinks have the retained unit factor kappa^2, as (8) verifies on every input; the prescribed zero-framing line is kept when this tangle is inserted into a diagram.

Insert it into a strand of any original knot J. The raw mixed-color packet is multiplied by its original cut scalar, and after retaining the original external line its energy is |K_N(J)|^2 E_N(A), up to the displayed unit framing phase. All such phases remain in the exact scalar identity and do not change this norm. The unframed p=0 filled knot is still J. By (9),

$$ E_N^{\rm inserted}(J)\geq\frac34|K_N(J)|^2 Z_N^2. \tag{11} $$

For the independently proved benchmark J=4_1, K_N(J)=Z_N, so this Gram has energy at least 3Z_N^4/4 while the final knot has squared scalar Z_N^2. Its energy exponent is at least DOUBLE the correct figure-eight squared-volume exponent.

This counterexample uses a redundant normalization augmentation and the p=0 filling. It does NOT exclude a properly reduced fully augmented diagram or a long-filling subclass. It proves that exact filling, complete jets, and a polynomial dual source cost do not by themselves imply the filled-volume energy bound. Normalization pruning or a source/slope-dependent geometric norm remains substantive.
