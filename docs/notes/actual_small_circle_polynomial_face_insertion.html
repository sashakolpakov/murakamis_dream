A small untwisted circle is an exact polynomial face operator

30 October 2026. Independently reviewed and frozen. At one actual untwisted port, the complete sum of the original small-color paths preserves the polynomial face ring. Its physical endpoint zeros cancel the negative powers that occur in individual path or clock terms. Consequently the whole source still selects one coefficient, with no level-shift aliases. A fixed-positive-radius bound costs only C^(h-1).

The port has distinct unmarked input/output middle faces, and its literal identity smoothing is connected with the two original marked faces still distinct. Multiple small ports, a marked middle face, and a retained crossing at the replaced port are not included in this theorem.

1. The original operator and its endpoint factors

Put N>=3, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2. Keep the original complete physical module and balanced basis. The external circle operator is

L_h=(-1)^(h-1) P_(h-1)(Omega), 1<=h<=N,
P_0=1, P_1=X, P_(r+1)=X P_r-P_(r-1).             (1)

These are the actual operators of the external reflection theorem, not their eigenvalues on a selected channel. Write U=Qu,V=Qv for the two INPUT physical indices. Directly from the original coproduct, Omega has diagonal and upward/downward transition coefficients

D(U,V)=2q U+2q^-1 V^-1-(q+q^-1)U/V,
A_+(U,V)=(V^-1-1)(1-Q U),
A_-(U,V)=(Q^-1 V^-1-1)(1-U).                    (2)

Here A_+ sends (u,v) to (u+1,v-1), and A_- sends it to (u-1,v+1). Formula (2) is valid for every physical total, including the high blocks. For example expanding F_12 Ecal_12+qK_12+q-1K_12-1 gives (2) directly, with no low-block reflection.

One may use cyclic shifts modulo N in (2) EXACTLY: A_+ is zero at u=d or v=0, and A_- is zero at u=0 or v=d. Hence any cyclic path that attempts to leave the physical square has zero weight at its first illegal step. Every power and the polynomial (1) therefore retain the original unwrapped endpoints. This is a root statement; it does not differentiate a root-periodic operator in generic q.

At the chosen original port let x,b,c,y be its ordered outer, incoming-middle, outgoing-middle and final face variables. In the literal convention of the complete-circle factorization,

S=d/2+N(Log x-Log b)/(2pi i),
R=d/2+N(Log x-Log c)/(2pi i).

Thus p=R-S (OUTPUT minus INPUT FIRST head) gives c=Q^(-p)b. The alternative formula c=Q^p b uses input-minus-output, or the opposite second-head displacement. These conventions must not be mixed. In particular U=-q^-1 x/b and V=-q^-1 b/y.

2. The exact polynomial operator

Temporarily retain b and c as independent variables. Let T act on polynomials in c, with every other original face variable fixed. The unmarked bounded middle face c is distinct from either outer face x,y in the literal Morse region chart, so this prescription is well-defined. The outer labels x=y are allowed and substituted literally:

T f(c)= -q^-1[1+q(x+y)/c+q^2 xy/c^2]f(Q^-1 c)
        -q[1+q^-1(x+y)/c+q^-2 xy/c^2]f(Q c)
        +[2(x+y)/c+(q+q^-1)xy/c^2]f(c).          (3)

Despite the displayed denominators, T preserves the POLYNOMIAL ring. Indeed for each integer j>=0,

T c^j = a_j c^j+b_j(x+y)c^(j-1)+e_j xy c^(j-2),
a_j=-(q Q^j+q^-1 Q^-j),
b_j=2-Q^j-Q^-j,
e_j=q+q^-1-q Q^-j-q^-1 Q^j.                    (4)

The term with b_0 vanishes, and e_0=e_1=0. Thus no negative power occurs. T also preserves total homogeneous degree in ALL face variables. Define

T_1=I, T_2=T, T_(h+1)=T T_h-T_(h-1).            (5)

Every T_h preserves polynomials and their homogeneous degree.

Here the phases in (3) are essential. For L_2=-Omega, substitution of the actual clocks into (2) gives the three path weights

l_0=2(x+y)/b+(q+q^-1)xy/b^2,
l_+=(1+q x/b)(1+q y/b),
l_-=(1+q^-1 x/b)(1+q^-1 y/b).                   (6)

The WHOLE remaining source supplies the factor q^(pd) for a path with displacement p. Since qd=-q-1, the weighted shifts in (6) are exactly (3), evaluated at c=b. For several successive steps, the current middle clock is Q^(-p)b, so the ordered path weights are exactly the successive applications of T. The physical Chebyshev recurrence (1) gives (5). This argument uses the original path product before expanding its endpoint factors; it does not discard forbidden paths one Laurent monomial at a time.

3. Whole-source coefficient and the retained measure

Fix a connected original plat with R complete-circle ports and its literal unrestricted main mark. Replace just the chosen untwisted port by L_h. All other ports retain fixed folded labels a_i and their original half-twist phases omega_(a_i)^epsilon_i. Assume b,c are distinct unmarked faces, and that deleting this port leaves a connected flat projection. The literal identity smoothing merges b,c; its two original marked faces u,v remain distinct.

Let P_rem be the product of the R-1 remaining positive circle polynomials D_a D_a Ptilde_s, retaining their ORIGINAL face incidences and keeping b,c independent. Define the polynomial on the smoothed face graph

Q_h(L)=[T_h P_rem(L)]_(c=b).                     (7)

It is homogeneous of degree d(R-1). It need not have positive coefficients. Let F_sm be the R-1 faces other than u,v after merging. The actual fixed-sector small-circle contribution is EXACTLY

A_(N,a;h)=N^(R-1) product_(i!=port)
   [k_(a_i) beta_(a_i)^2 omega_(a_i)^epsilon_i]
   [product_(f in F_sm)L_f^d] Q_h(L)|_(L_u=L_v=0). (8)

Every cup, ordinary cap, exterior phase and the original mark are as in the marked coefficient theorem. Any separately recorded exterior framing unit multiplies both sides.

To prove the phase used in section 2, first use the exact full-source monomial of that theorem BEFORE merging. Removing the chosen complete coupon removes its two middle denominators, so the remaining scalar face factor is

N^-1 product_(i!=port)[k_a beta_a^2 omega_a^epsilon]
    (bc)^(d/2) product_(f!=u,v)L_f^(-d).          (9)

Since b,c are unmarked, c=Q^(-p)b converts their part to q(pd)b(-d), exactly. No root clock or half-integer power has been reset. This proves (3) with its true scalar and chosen direction.

For fixed paths at the small port, sum over the remaining primitive face grid. All surviving complete coupons enforce their original unwrapped support, and (2) enforces the small port’s physical endpoints. The chosen path specifies c=Q^(-p)b; the input and output middle faces have the same even-level parity, so this relation respects the original coset. Summing paths gives (7) on the smoothed grid. The globally assembled exponents are integers, and the remaining carry signs cancel by the same integral face-flow argument, with the displacement p fixed.

There are N^R smoothed grid points. A monomial of Q_h survives that average only if its exponent is 0 modulo N at the marked bounded face and d modulo N at every face in F_sm. POLYNOMIALITY and total degree d(R-1) force the unique possibility: exponent zero at both marked faces and exactly d at every face in F_sm. No coefficient positivity is needed for this argument. The even coset multiplier at that exponent is one. Combining N^R with N^-1 in (9) proves (8).

Individual clock or diagonal-path pieces can have genuine whole-N aliases. It is the exact sum (3)–(5), including both off-diagonal bands, that cancels their negative powers and restores this argument.

4. A fixed-positive-radius cost of C^(h-1)

Give EVERY smoothed face a fixed positive value l_f, including u,v. Give the two unmerged variables b,c that same value l_b. For a polynomial P write ||P||_l=sum_n |P_n|l^n. From (4),

||T P||_l <= A(l)||P||_l,
A(l)=2+4(l_x+l_y)/l_b+4l_x l_y/l_b^2.            (10)

Thus (5) implies

||T_h P||_l <= [A(l)+1]^(h-1)||P||_l.            (11)

The elementary induction retains the subtraction in (5); it bounds its norm rather than asserting positivity. Merging two variables of equal positive weight cannot increase this weighted coefficient norm. Since P_rem has nonnegative coefficients, ||P_rem||_l=P_sm(l), where P_sm is the original identity-smoothed positive polynomial. Coefficient extraction in (8) therefore gives

|[L^(d F_sm)]Q_h|
   <= [A(l)+1]^(h-1) P_sm(l)/product_(f in F_sm)l_f^d. (12)

This avoids separate estimates of shifted targets or their modular aliases. Equivalently, the finite lowering expansion changes each ordinary exponent by at most 2(h-1), with total displacement bounded by 4(h-1), only AFTER its exact polynomial cancellations.

At fixed l, all remaining label weights are the original ones. Sum their absolute values and use the existing uniform all-label circle rate bound. If the identity-smoothed coefficient bound at these SAME positive variables has a strict gap g below the original volume rate, then the whole one-small-port source has that gap up to (h-1)log[A(l)+1]/N and o(1). In particular h=O(sqrt N) has no rate cost, and sufficiently small h<=delta N preserves a strict gap. The marked variables may be chosen fixed positive as in the fixed-radius smoothing bound; their targets remain zero. They are not set to zero before (12).

This implication requires an established compatible smoothing gap. It does not assert that every smoothing has one. Nor does it cover several deleted ports, a marked middle face, disconnected smoothing, or an R^epsilon crossing retained at the small port. Those cases need their own exact scalar, incidence and endpoint analysis.

5. Focused source verification

The focused audit, with saved results, checks seven symbolic operator/endpoint identities, 15,316 actual coproduct entries and 154 physical endpoint zeros. It compares 1,512 literal L_h path pairings with polynomial monomials, including 504 high-total cases. The whole-source check compares 139 actual marked fixed-sector contractions with (8), for every h=1,…,N in its fixtures, both level parities and both original mark positions. It includes width two and three, maximal/mixed/zero folded channels, and the other ports’ retained half twists. The 121 surviving modular monomials all have the unique asserted target. These checks concern the original finite tensors; the all-diagram identity and norm bound are the preceding analytical arguments.

An independent full analytical source/interface review checked (2)–(9), including the original high-total coproduct, q^(pd), ordered shifts, Chebyshev composition, endpoint cancellations and original marked measure. It also checked (10)–(12). That review makes no claim about multiple, marked-middle or signed small ports.

The same independent reviewer freshly replayed the entire audit with redirected output, reproducing all stated counts and both original mark/parity cases. No source or runtime changes were made during that replay.

A second full analytical review and audit-code read independently checked the original whole-source interface, the q^(pd) factor, physical support, polynomial endpoint cancellations, degree/alias argument and weighted norm estimate. Both reviews preserve the stated one-port scope. The theorem, audit and saved results are frozen.
