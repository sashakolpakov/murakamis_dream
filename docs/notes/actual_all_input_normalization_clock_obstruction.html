The normalization clock disk is not uniformly tame on the physical head

29 October 2026. The selected original normalization scalar has a tame clock disk, but its ALL-INPUT physical operator does not. The distinction can be proved at the first normal jet, with the actual sources and full Cartan factor retained. This gives an explicit physical Ward current which a gluing theorem has to cancel coherently.

The result does not assert exponential growth of a closed knot scalar, or failure of the source-specific shrinking-arc construction. It rules out promoting its selected scalar estimate to a uniform physical-head operator estimate.

1. The literal open-head normalization operator

Let N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d, F_j=(Q;Q)_j, s_j=|F_j|, and kappa=q(d2/2). Let R be the FULL original positive crossing on the physical pair degree-d block W, and set L=kappa^(-1)R. Keep the original cup and ordinary cap.

The all-input cup slide exposes the operator

𝒞N(t)=(ℓ12⊗I3)(I1⊗R2)tJ2(I1⊗p23),J|a⟩=a|a⟩. \mathcal C_N(t)=(\ell_{12}\otimes I_3) (I_1\otimes R^2)t^{J_2}(I_1\otimes p_{23}), \qquad J|a\rangle=a|a\rangle.

Here R^2 acts on the degree-d subspace of the last physical pair; the full original physical crossings restrict to this block and the first head is its untouched spectator. This is the same actual local normalization slot as in the mesoscopic-current theorem. The remaining physical input is arbitrary. Integer degree makes the operator diagonal, with EXACT entries

Ca(t)=x−aQ−a∑n=0d(L2)d−a,n(xt)n. C_a(t)=x^{-a}Q^{-a}\sum_{n=0}^d(L^2)_{d-a,n}(xt)^n. \tag{1}

Indeed the three factors are the cup coefficient N x^(n-d), the original crossing square kappa2(L2)_(d-a,n), and the cap x(-a)Q(-a)/N. The identity kappa2=xd gives (1). The apparent all-input source freedom is therefore fixed, not chosen by a favorable scalar period. At t=1 every entry is xd=kappa2.

Write u(t)_j=(xt)^j and v(t)=L^2u(t). Because x2=Q(-1),

Ca(t)=xavd−a(t),maxa|Ca′(1)|=∥v′(1)∥∞. C_a(t)=x^a v_{d-a}(t),\qquad \max_a|C_a'(1)|=\|v'(1)\|_\infty. \tag{2}

2. The exact finite Ward register before the second crossing

For 0<=i<=d define

Di+(s)=Fi2∑b=0iQb2sbFi−bFb2. D_i^+(s)=F_i^2\sum_{b=0}^i \frac{Q^{b^2}s^b}{F_{i-b}F_b^2}.

Let D_i^-(s) denote the same polynomial with Q replaced by Q^(-1). All factorial indices are below N. Finite Durfee convolution gives D_i+(1)=D_i-(1)=1. Directly from the original crossing entries,

(Lu(t))i=xitdDi−(1/t). \boxed{\displaystyle (Lu(t))_i=x^i t^d D_i^-(1/t).} \tag{3}

For clarity, the intermediate finite coefficient is

(Lu(t))i=x−iQi(d−i)td−iFi2∑k=0i(−1)kQk(k−1)/2tkFkFi−k2. (Lu(t))_i=x^{-i}Q^{i(d-i)}t^{d-i}F_i^2 \sum_{k=0}^i\frac{(-1)^kQ^{k(k-1)/2}t^k}{F_kF_{i-k}^2}.

Replace k by i-b and use (Q(-1);Q(-1))_b=(-1)^b Q^(-b(b+1)/2)F_b to obtain (3). This is an all-port factorial identity, not an adjoint/unitarity claim.

The finite polynomials obey

Di+(s)=(1+Q−2Qi+Q2i−1s)Di−1+(s)−Q(1−Qi−1)2Di−2+(s). D_i^+(s)=(1+Q-2Q^i+Q^{2i-1}s)D_{i-1}^+(s) -Q(1-Q^{i-1})^2D_{i-2}^+(s). \tag{4}

One elementary derivation uses formal series through degree d: U(z)=sum z^k/F_k has U(Qz)=(1-z)U(z), and W(z)=sum Q(b2)s^b zb/F_b2 satisfies W(z)-2W(Qz)+(1-sQz)W(Q^2z)=0. Compare coefficients of U(z)W(z). This proves (4) at the root without using any denominator F_N.

Let P_i be the ORIGINAL Green register,

P0=1,Pi=(1−Qi)2Pi−1+Qi. P_0=1,\qquad P_i=(1-Q^i)^2P_{i-1}+Q^i.

Differentiating (4) at s=1 and factoring the resulting first difference gives the exact normal current

(Di+)′(1)−(Di−1+)′(1)=QiPi−1. (D_i^+)'(1)-(D_{i-1}^+)'(1)=Q^iP_{i-1}. \tag{5}

The base case is (D1^+)‘(1)=Q. Equivalently, set w=Lu’(1); (3) gives w_i=xi[d-(D_i-)’(1)]. Therefore

x−iwi−x−(i−1)wi−1=−Q−iPi−1¯,1≤i≤d. \boxed{\displaystyle x^{-i}w_i-x^{-(i-1)}w_{i-1} =-Q^{-i}\overline{P_{i-1}},\quad1\leq i\leq d.} \tag{6}

This is the retained physical normal current. It is not zero at a normalization leaf merely because its value at t=1 is a scalar.

3. The source endpoint forces a large interior current

The all-parity ORIGINAL source endpoint has the independently derived quadratic-Gaussian asymptotic

Pd=N3/2exp⁡[iπ(14−N12+14N)]+O(N). P_d=N^{3/2}\exp\!\left[i\pi\left(\frac14-\frac N{12} +\frac1{4N}\right)\right]+O(\sqrt N). \tag{7}

Its proof retains the complete pivotal derivative source, including the odd simple node: the endpoint Gaussian theorem. In particular |P_d|>=c N^(3/2) for all sufficiently large N. No volume asymptotic or fitted saddle branch is assumed.

The exact backward Green identity from the terminal capacity proof is

Pt=Ft2(PdN2−∑b=t+1dQbFb2). P_t=F_t^2\left(\frac{P_d}{N^2} -\sum_{b=t+1}^d\frac{Q^b}{F_b^2}\right). \tag{8}

Choose i nearest 5N/6, with 1<=i<=d, and t=i-1. For large N, s_t>N. The sine-factorial sequence increases up to its high peak and then decreases to s_d=N. Consequently s_b>=N for every b>t, so the tail in (8) has absolute value at most1/N. It follows from (7) that

|Pi−1|≥c1N−1/2si−12. |P_{i-1}|\geq c_1 N^{-1/2}s_{i-1}^2. \tag{9}

Put J(s)=integral_0^s log(2sin(pi*u))du. Uniform sine-factorial asymptotics give

log⁡|Pi−1|≥v3πN−O(log⁡N), \log|P_{i-1}|\geq\frac{v_3}{\pi}N-O(\log N), \tag{10}

since J has maximum v3/(2pi) at 5/6, where v3 is the regular ideal tetrahedron volume. The lower bound is a coherent source result: the complex tail in (8) is estimated only AFTER the actual nonzero endpoint has been evaluated.

4. A quantitative all-input obstruction after the second crossing

The literal original NEGATIVE crossing supplies

|(L−1)r,j|={N2sr2sj2sd−r−j,r+j≤d,0,r+j>d. |(L^{-1})_{r,j}|= \begin{cases} \displaystyle\frac{N^2}{s_r^2s_j^2s_{d-r-j}},&r+j\leq d,\\ 0,&r+j>d. \end{cases} \tag{11}

No conjugate-transpose inverse is used. Define the exact row mass B_r=sum_j |(L^(-1))_(r,j)|. Since w=L^(-1)v’(1), (6) implies the FINITE inequality

maxa≥i−1|Ca′(1)|≥|Pi−1|Bi+Bi−1. \boxed{\displaystyle \max_{a\geq i-1}|C_a'(1)| \geq\frac{|P_{i-1}|}{B_i+B_{i-1}}.} \tag{12}

The color restriction follows from the two strict supports in (11), not from an imposed geometric color. It is enough to control the operator on its upper sixth of physical inputs.

The global factorial minimum is log s_j>=-N v3/(2pi)-O(log N), and for r=i or i-1, log s_r^2=N v3/pi+O(log N). Hence (11) gives

log⁡(Bi+Bi−1)≤N(−v3π+3v32π)+O(log⁡N)=v32πN+O(log⁡N). \log(B_i+B_{i-1})\leq N\left(-\frac{v_3}{\pi}+\frac{3v_3}{2\pi}\right)+O(\log N) =\frac{v_3}{2\pi}N+O(\log N).

Combining this with (10) proves a positive explicit exponent:

liminfN→∞1Nlog⁡maxa|Ca′(1)|≥γ:=v32π>0. \boxed{\displaystyle \liminf_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log\max_a|C_a'(1)| \geq\gamma:=\frac{v_3}{2\pi}>0.} \tag{13}

The numerical value gamma is about0.16153. It is a rigorous LOWER bound, not the claimed exact jet exponent.

Every C_a is a polynomial. Cauchy’s derivative inequality now gives, for EVERY fixed c>0,

$$ \boxed{\displaystyle \liminf_N\frac1N\log \sup_{|t-1|\leq c/N}\|\mathcal C_N(t)\|_{\rm op} \geq\gamma>0.} \tag{14} $$

The same assertion holds for any disk radius r_N<=1 with log(1/r_N)=o(N). It is therefore not repaired by choosing a smaller polynomial clock disk. This is a statement about the ALL-INPUT physical operator, not about the selected scalar row.

This particular obstruction is independent of a choice of positive inner product on the SAME physical input/output head. The C_a(t) are the actual eigenvalues of the diagonal operator, so every such operator norm is at least max_a|C_a(t)|, even for N- and t-dependent metrics. A change between DIFFERENT input and output metrics has additional interfaces and is not covered by that observation.

5. The missing moment constraint is an actual normal Ward defect

For a=d the original source packet is still C_d(t)=x^(-d)P_N(t), in the notation of the mesoscopic-current theorem. Its first derivative is x^(-d)Q conjugate(P_d-1), polynomial in N; the whole selected c/N disk is polynomially bounded by the original clock-disk proof. There is no contradiction with (13): the large normal response occupies other physical inputs.

It can also be stated directly as a failure of the untwisted Hopf moment condition. The original single-head F acts by F|a>=(1-Q^(a+1))|a+1>. Therefore

[F,𝒞N′(1)]|a⟩=(1−Qa+1)(Ca′(1)−Ca+1′(1))|a+1⟩. [F,\mathcal C_N'(1)]|a\rangle =(1-Q^{a+1})(C_a'(1)-C_{a+1}'(1))|a+1\rangle. \tag{15}

The terminal C_d’ is polynomial, while (13) is exponential. Telescoping the physical differences and using |1-Q^(a+1)|>=4/N proves

$$ \liminf_N N^{-1}\log\|[F,\mathcal C_N'(1)]\|_{\rm op} \geq\gamma. \tag{16} $$

The same holds for Ecal, whose relevant coefficient magnitudes agree. Thus a polynomial-norm same-head correction cannot make this jet an intertwiner: its commutator would have to cancel (16). The required normal current is explicitly (5)–(6), not an unproved abstract error.

The exact all-input tensor Ward identity identifies the omitted two-color insertion more directly. Put S=I tensor p and A_cap=ell tensor I, and let U=I tensor R_phys^2 act on the FULL physical pair space, not only its degree-d block. Define

$$ \mathcal J_F(t)=A_{\rm cap}U K_1^{-1}F_2 t^{J_2}S,\qquad \mathcal J_E(t)=A_{\rm cap}U E_{{\rm cal},2}t^{J_2}K_3S. $$

The actual coproduct and cup/cap intertwinings give

$$ [F,\mathcal C_N(t)]=(1-t)\mathcal J_F(t),\qquad [E_{\rm cal},\mathcal C_N(t)]=(1-t^{-1})\mathcal J_E(t). \tag{17} $$

Indeed only the middle-head term in the total coproduct fails to commute with t^(J2). In particular [F,C_N’(1)]=-J_F(1) and [Ecal,C_N’(1)]=J_E(1). The F insertion goes through pair degree d+1 and the E insertion through degree d-1. Replacing either by a shift of the recording register inside the degree-d scalar packet would omit that sector. Equations (16)–(17) prove that these ACTUAL physical normal-current operators have exponential norm; no polynomial operator correction may simply erase them.

A full exterior may cancel it with neighboring clock/crossing currents or may retain a source projection excluding these inputs. Such a cancellation must be shown in its actual tensor slot, with the marked source unchanged. Replacing (1) by the selected value C_d(t), imposing a lowest-color projection on an internal head, or declaring the clock insertion a Hopf map omits the defect above.

This proves the requested uniform physical operator extension FALSE. It leaves a source-compatible correlated/mesoscopic gluing theorem open. Tensoring a spectator identity does not change the lower bound; no claim about a general correlated whole-knot scalar or a one-dimensional unit-circle arc follows solely from the disk inequality (14).

Audit checks the original full R/cup/cap operator, the finite Ward recurrence, the exact derivative current, literal inverse row masses, and finite inequality (12). The endpoint Gaussian input has its separate all-level proof and original-source audit.
