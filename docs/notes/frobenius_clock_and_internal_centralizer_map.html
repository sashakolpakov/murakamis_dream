The Frobenius clock needs a logarithmic internal-centralizer adapter

Independent current-map theorem and audit, 27 October 2026. The actual Frobenius source gives the correct finite cut clock and a regular finite approximation to the old physical Springer graph. It does not identify the internal centralizer coordinate by an algebraic symplectic map. That coordinate necessarily contains a logarithm of the source angular flag. The same source Cartan current has two distinct geometric interpretations, which must not be conflated.

There is also an exact metric qualification for the controlled head-shift gauge: its standard-coordinate unitarity does not give a polynomial bound in the compact coherent metric.

2. An algebraic map to the original two-frame torsor is impossible

The original internal two-frame form is Ωcut=dτ∧dlog⁡A,A=(1+s)−1,τ=r/s. \Omega_{\mathrm{cut}}=d\tau\wedge d\log A,\qquad A=(1+s)^{-1},\quad\tau=r/s . This identifies the additional torsor needed for the current map: a logarithmic centralizer with its marked translation and pole clutches, rather than a single-valued coordinate on a split B1 chart.

4. The root gauge cost depends on the metric

The controlled source gauge G=∑RPR⊗VRG=\sum_RP_R\otimes V^R is a unitary permutation in its constant coordinate basis. This correctly gives the root same-color source comparison and does not change any exact period when all operators, sources, duals and projectors are transported.

In the compact head’s binomial coherent metric the conclusion is different. Put n=N−1n=N-1 and use gs=1/(ns)g_s=1/\binom ns; the dual binomial convention gives the same condition-number conclusion. For any diagonal metric on the Frobenius factor, its weight cancels because G fixes R. Choose a central head s and a block R sending it to head zero. Then ∥G∥≥(n⌊n/2⌋). \|G\|\ge\sqrt{\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}} . The inverse has the same lower bound, by reversing that cycle. Consequently cond⁡coherent(G)≥(n⌊n/2⌋)≥2nn+1. \operatorname{cond}_{\mathrm{coherent}}(G) \ge\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor} \ge\frac{2^n}{n+1}. \tag{4} Thus its standard-metric polynomial cost cannot be imported into a coherent-metric leading-norm estimate.

This does NOT affect exact gauge invariance or the exact root equality with K_N. A whole normalized source unit and near-identity matrix germ are likewise sufficient for exact same-color identities, but do not give a uniform estimate on a growing normal neighborhood. Any leading geometric norm argument must retain its actual metric/source adapter. The current-map problem has therefore acquired explicit logarithmic, star, and metric data; none is supplied by an abstract identification of the two SL2 actions.
