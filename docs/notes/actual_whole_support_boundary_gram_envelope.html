Polynomial actual boundary Gram costs on the entire supported color domain

29 October 2026. The correlated six-head marked boundary Gram has POLYNOMIAL source cost on EVERY supported folded color cell, at both level parities. No fixed central corridor is required. The second-root- zero Racah terms do not need a uniform strict exponential gap: a global elementary sine-product inequality bounds every such term by the same action as a positive main term. All low/high partner branches, the original marked normal and every lower moment are retained.

This controls the pointed algebraic Gram from the actual six-pants boundary. It does not identify its constructed reflection with a physical mirror, bound the physical change of frame or general recouplings, or prove a graph’s sharp geometric energy/noncancellation.

1. The whole-support theorem

Let N>=2, d=N-1, M=floor(d/2), q=exp(pi i/N), and take ANY

0<=l_1,l_2,l_3<=M, l_1+l_2+l_3>=d.            (1)

There is one paired axis for each 2l_i<d; let their number be r<=3. The actual regular source cell, complete-circle coordinates eta_i, original marked co-source lambda and nested-cup vector v_l are those of the proved boundary theorem. In particular

m_I=lambda(eta_I v_l),
tau_l=m_all=Z_N(l)/N^(3-r)>0,
q_l(eta)=sum_I (m_(I^c)/tau_l)eta_I, q_l(0)=1,
K_l=sum_I |coeff_I(q_l^(1/2))|^2.              (2)

There are absolute constants C,C0, independent of N and of ALL labels satisfying (1), such that

max_I |m_I/tau_l|<=C0 N^C,
1<=K_l<=C0 N^C.                               (3)

The exponent may be increased between the two estimates. Thus the actual source and dual costs of this prescribed Gram are exactly K_l and tau_l^2 K_l, with log K_l=O(log N)=o(N) uniformly on the whole support. The estimates include saturation faces, edges, corners, nearest paired neighbors and odd simple axes.

Unsupported open source cells are not deleted. They lack the positive top moment in (2), so (3) is asserted precisely on (1). The theorem does not assert a polynomial norm for the embedding of this finite coordinate cell into the physical tensor product.

2. The original generic trace and all its normal factors

The proved generic source identity and the genuine ordered physical trace give, for each unfolded ordinary triangle j=(a,b,c), the EXACT rational function

T_j=(-1)^d product_i[2j_i+1]
     *[r_1]![r_2]![r_3]!/[J+1]!
     *product_i([j_i]!^2[d-j_i]!/[N+j_i]!) S_j^2,
J=a+b+c, r_i=J-2j_i,

S_j=sum_t (-1)^(d+t)[N+t]!
      /{[d+t-J]! product_i[t-j_i]![J-j_i-t]!},
max(j_i,J-d)<=t<=J-max(j_i).                  (4)

Here [n] and [n]! are symmetric quantum integers and factorials at zeta=q(1-Z)^(1/(2N)), with the branch through q. These are the actual source functions, not a separately normalized 6j model.

The unfolded choices are j_i=l_i or d-l_i on a paired axis, and the single l_i on a simple axis. With the original paired-circle unit ell_j(Z)=L_N(c_j(zeta))/Z, the physical moment formula is

m_I=(-1)^d(q-q^(-1)) [Z^(r+1-|I|)]
     sum_unfolded Z^r T_j product_(i in I)ell_(j_i)(Z). (5)

There are at most eight summands and the coefficient order is <=4. The original marked physical trace is exactly the one in (5). No ordinary quantum dimension is specialized to zero before this coefficient is extracted.

Two elementary integer facts now hold on the ENTIRE supported domain. For every unfolded branch,

d<=J<=2d, 0<=r_i<=d.                           (6)

To verify them, increase a coordinate from l_i to d-l_i. The maximum J is 3d-sum l_i<=2d and the minimum is sum l_i>=d. For r_i, its maximum over the flips is 2d-sum l_i<=d and its minimum is sum l_i-2l_i>=d-2l_i>=0. In particular every unfolded branch is an ordinary triangle.

Moreover EVERY one of the seven denominator factorial indices in S_j is less than N, for every true term. Indeed

0<=d+t-J<=d-max j_i<=d,
0<=t-j_i<=some other label<=d,
0<=J-j_i-t<=some other label<=d.               (7)

For example t<=j_i+j_k bounds t-j_i, and t>=j_k bounds J-j_i-t. No root denominator appears in any Racah summand. Its numerator contains [N] once for t<N and both [N],[2N] for t>=N; t<=2d<2N excludes a third zero. This is the only termwise change at the second band.

3. A uniform global profile, including the second-zero band

Let L(t)=integral_0^t log|2sin(pi u)|du, extended periodically to R. Its period is 1 and L(-t)=-L(t). Define the EXACT half-step scaled colors

a_i=(j_i+1/2)/N, S=sum a_i,
x_i=S/2-a_i (i=1,2,3), x_4=1-S/2.

Equations (6) imply

0<x_i<1/2 for all four i, sum_i x_i=1.         (8)

The half-step is useful: a high-partner replacement j_i->d-j_i is exactly a_i->1-a_i. It permutes the four x coordinates while replacing each by its complement 1/2-x. There is no O(1/N) error in this assertion. For instance flipping the first color sends

(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
   ->(1/2-x_4,1/2-x_3,1/2-x_2,1/2-x_1).     (9)

For a summand put s=(t+3/4)/N and v=s-S/2=(t-J/2)/N. Its three child pairs obey

x_i+v=(t-j_i+1/4)/N>0,
x_i-v=(J-j_i-t+1/4)/N>0, i<=3.                (10)

Also x_4+v=1-(J-t+3/4)/N is in (0,1). The fourth minus argument is x_4-v=1-s=(N-t-3/4)/N: it is positive for t<N and negative for t>=N, precisely the second-zero band.

After removing its one or two root zeros, the absolute factorial summand has, up to polynomial factors, the profile

H_x(v)=-sum_(i=1)^4 [L(x_i+v)+L(x_i-v)].       (11)

For t<N the numerator is s_t; for t>=N it is s_(t-N), apart from the complete root-orbit polynomial factors. Both have rate N L(s) up to O(log N), because L has period 1. The denominator d+t-J has rate L(1+s-S)=-L(S-s). Substitution of (10) then gives (11). This same argument covers endpoints: changing an argument by O(1/N) changes N L by O(log N), uniformly even at a zero of sine. One may prove this modulus of continuity by integrating |log u| over an interval of length O(1/N) near the nearest integer.

The decisive inequality is

H_x(v)<=H_x(0)=Phi(x):=-2sum_i L(x_i)          (12)

for EVERY actual term, including the second-zero band. Here is a direct elementary proof. The function is even in v, and (10) gives |v|<min(x_1,x_2,x_3). For v>0 away from its logarithmic corner, put alpha_i=pi x_i and theta=pi v. Then

H_x'(v)=log |P_minus/P_plus|,
P_plus=product_i sin(alpha_i+theta)>0,
P_minus=product_i sin(alpha_i-theta).          (13)

If theta<alpha_4, every factor is positive and sin(alpha_i+theta)-sin(alpha_i-theta) =2cos(alpha_i)sin(theta)>0. Thus |P_minus|<P_plus. If theta>alpha_4, exactly the fourth minus factor is negative. Expand the sum of the two products:

P_plus+P_minus
  =2 sum_(A subset {1,2,3,4}, |A| even)
     cos(theta)^(4-|A|)sin(theta)^|A|
     product_(i in A)cos(alpha_i)
     product_(i notin A)sin(alpha_i)>0.        (14)

Every factor is nonnegative, and an interior term is strictly positive by (8). Since P_minus<0, (14) again says |P_minus|<P_plus. Continuity across theta=alpha_4, where (13) tends to minus infinity, proves (12). Evenness treats negative v. No local concavity or uniform strict exponential tail gap was used.

The integer nearest t=J/2 is a true term of the FIRST band: (6) gives max(j_i,J-d)<=floor(J/2) and ceil(J/2)<=min(J-max j_i,d). At that term |v|<=1/(2N), so the same uniform modulus of continuity gives H_x(v)=Phi(x)+O(log N/N).

4. Positive main value and polynomial normal envelopes

For t<N, the exact root identity is

([N+t]!/[N])|_q=(-1)^t[d]![t]!.

All denominators in (4) are positive symmetric factorials at q. Thus S_main/[N] has ONE common phase (-1)^d. Its positive absolute sum is

Rcore_N(j)=N sum_(true t<N)
   s_t/{s_(d+t-J) product_i s_(t-j_i)s_(J-j_i-t)}.

It has at most N terms and contains the midpoint term identified above. The uniform two-sided sine-factorial estimate and (12) therefore give, throughout the whole support and every branch,

N^(-C)e^(N Phi(x))<=Rcore_N(j)
                          <=N^C e^(N Phi(x)). (15)

The estimate uses only ONE midpoint summand for its lower bound; no bulk Hessian, saddle width or distance from a saturation face is assumed. Second-band terms, after removing [N][2N], obey the same upper bound by (12). They may approach the leading action at boundary scales, which is why a uniform strict gap is unnecessary.

Use the common disk |Z|<=c/N^2. A quantum-factorial ratio with a fixed number of factors and indices <=3N has, after explicitly removing its zeros, a polynomial envelope relative to its nonzero root product on this disk. This follows either from the proved bounded normal factorial estimate or directly: a nonzero 1-q^(2k) has magnitude >=c0/N; its change is O(|Z|), and O(N) such factors accumulate only bounded relative growth here. Removed [N] and [2N] factors and the symmetric-int normalization cost fixed powers of N. Their count is uniformly bounded. Equations (7),(12),(15) hence bound the ENTIRE holomorphic S_j/Z by poly(N)e^(N Phi(x)). Cauchy estimates for four fixed normal orders add only another fixed power of N. No alternating-sum cancellation is needed for the second-zero terms.

To include the complete prefactor of (4), note first that

([N] [j]!^2[d-j]!/[N+j]!)|_q=(-1)^j.          (16)

Its zero-removed envelope is polynomial, with no factorial action. All three r_i are <N, while N<=J+1<2N. The zero-removed triangle factor therefore has rate

P(x)=sum_(i=1)^3 L(2x_i)-L(S-1),

up to O(log N). Each simple axis contributes one dimension zero [2j+1]=[N]; the other dimensions and all ell_j in (5) have polynomial fixed jets, uniformly to the nearest paired neighbor. If s0=3-r is the number of simple axes, the prefactor without S^2 has zero order s0-4. Multiplication by Z^r and S^2, whose order is at least two, leaves order at least ONE. Consequently

Z^r T_j product ell

is holomorphic on the common disk; it is never estimated as a bare singular expression. The preceding bounds give for its fixed jets

|[Z^k]{Z^r T_j product ell}|<=N^C exp(N E(x)), k<=4,
E(x)=P(x)+2Phi(x)
    =sum_(i=1)^4[L(2x_i)-4L(x_i)]
    =-2sum_(i=1)^4[L(x_i)+L(1/2-x_i)].         (17)

The second equality uses L(S-1)=-L(2x_4), and the third is the elementary duplication identity for L. This expression is unchanged by every permutation or individual complement x_i->1/2-x_i. Equation (9) therefore makes E EXACTLY the same function for all unfolded branches of a fixed folded triple, with these half-step coordinates.

Finally the PROVED actual positive top moment has the exact weight

Z_N(l)=N^2 product_i k_(l_i)
          s_(J_l-2l_1)s_(J_l-2l_2)s_(J_l-2l_3)
                /s_(J_l-d) *R_N(l)^2.

The same midpoint argument (15), uniform factorial bounds and its positive prefactor give

N^(-C)exp(N E_fold)<=tau_l<=N^C exp(N E_fold)  (18)

on the ENTIRE supported domain. Endpoint factors s_0=1 cause no problem, and replacing their scaled arguments by the half-step ones changes logarithms by only O(log N). Combine (5),(17),(18), sum at most eight branches, and absorb the polynomial |q-q^-1|: this proves the first estimate in (3).

The finite square root of q_l=1+n_l is sum_(k=0)^r binom(1/2,k)n_l^k, with n_l^(r+1)=0. It is a polynomial of degree <=3 in at most seven normalized moments. Thus the first estimate implies the second, with constants independent of the labels and N. Its constant coefficient is one, so K_l>=1. This completes the whole-support actual Gram theorem.

5. Scope and verification

The new ingredient beyond the central-corridor bound is the global sine-product inequality (12)–(14), together with the half-step folding coordinates. A second-zero action gap may vanish at boundary scales; uniform positive factorial comparison replaces it. The result controls the correlated marked Gram on its actual regular source cells. It supplies neither a physical mirror interpretation of that Gram nor a bound for genuine transitions between different pair decompositions. Those remain separate global source/geometry tasks; polynomial K alone has no hidden cuboctahedral volume term.

The multiprecision source diagnostic uses the proved generic trace (4), every partner and the original physical circle normal. It compares two precisions and two normal steps on saturation faces/corners and interior examples. These are diagnostics; the uniform theorem follows from the finite generic source identity, (12), common-disk factorial envelopes and the positive midpoint bound, not sampling.

The focused audit and recorded results verify the universal even-product polynomial with an identically zero cleared numerator; 46,495 unfolded integer branches and 257,107 true Racah terms at N=2,…,32; 215 independent generic physical-double-sum versus Racah traces; and five all-moment original marked crosschecks. Floating-point action comparisons in that audit are labeled diagnostics; the proof of (12) is the exact sign argument (13),(14).

The separate paired-precision data include ten boundary/interior cases through N=256, both parities, simple axes and saturation corners. The largest displayed K is below 2.78; this finite observation is not used to choose the polynomial exponent or prove its uniformity.
