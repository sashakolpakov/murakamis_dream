Source-normalized transport of the actual q-Hahn partner near the fold

Written proof, 24 November 2026.

The original generic q-Hahn columns have scalar normalizers which vanish near the channel fold. Keeping those normalizers inside the degree recurrence cancels the large coefficients at every nonresonant row. Only finitely many resonant rows retain a polynomial cost. This gives a relative upper bound with a linear scaled-channel exponent, on every fixed compact of the full strict physical tube.

The normal is the original difference of two generic highest-weight N−1N-1 derivatives. No root-only derivative, interpolated normal, or complex continuation of the integer channel is substituted.

6. Consequences, scope, and complete inputs

Taking logarithms of (6) gives log⁡|wj,l/Cj|≤100log⁡N+(s+1)log⁡CK.(27) \log|w_{j,l}/C_j| \le100\log N+(s+1)\log C_K. \qquad\text{(27)} Thus all s=o(N)s=o(N), rather than only slog⁡N=o(N)s\log N=o(N), have subexponential relative upper cost on the same fixed magnetic compact. Uniformly for s≤δNs\le\delta N, its upper exponential cost is at most δlog⁡CK+o(1)\delta\log C_K+o(1). At a fixed positive δ\delta, this still permits exponential cost; subexponential cost there is not asserted. The original scalar ratios remove the nonresonant fold-distance cost.

The old near-fold estimate [N] and its enlargement in [P] paid N16N^{16} at every intervening row. The improvement here comes from the exact actual-source recurrence (13). The normalizer zeros are used before taking absolute values, with finitely many resonances kept explicit. This does not replace the generic derivative in (4) by a derivative of a root collision identity.

The result concerns actual integer channels and fixed complex magnetic compacts. It does not continue the channel to a complex parameter, prove a nonzero leading normal amplitude, or control the complete q-Hahn coupon after its remaining factors and contractions. It does not place a short-filled geometric saddle in this corridor or establish its coefficient in the original closed source. No volume or finite-exception conclusion follows here.
