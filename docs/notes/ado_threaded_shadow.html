The actual ADO threaded character and a separating source projector

Research note, 26 October 2026. This proves an exact, uniform formula for Chebyshev-threaded interior loops in the specified ADO evaluation. It retains the odd-thread signs and framing phases and then isolates a parity-independent even character. A null-homologous loop separates this character from the complete hyperbolic character for every hyperbolic knot. Thus identifying fixed central eigenlines is the wrong proposed comparison in that setting. A source extension can still encode the period as a distinguished splitting; a precise projector lemma states what an actual construction would have to provide.

The proof uses the fixed unrolled matrices and elementary exact sequences, not a published Chebyshev–Frobenius or quantum-trace theorem. No quantum invariant or state is replaced. The volume conjecture and the construction of the required geometric source extension remain unproved.

1. Fixed evaluation and actual annular threading

Let K be an oriented zero-framed knot, q=exp(pi*i/N), N>=2. Use the literature color V_alpha and parameters

x=q^alpha, M=-qx, Y=M^2, U=Y^N=M^(2N).

The pivot is K^(1-N). The core cut evaluation is the one already fixed in the ADO note. Write F_N^L(M) for its reduced scalar with an additional framed fundamental-color link L in the complement; N_N^L=d_N F_N^L is the full period. The fundamental color W and its exact framed skein relation are those of the peripheral-fusion calculation.

For a further framed oriented loop gamma disjoint from L and K, let C_n(gamma) be its annular Chebyshev threading, with

C_0(X)=2, C_1(X)=X, C_(n+1)(X)=X C_n(X)-C_(n-1)(X).

Products mean framed parallels, and the definition is linear on the resulting link combinations. It has integer coefficients and uses no singular Jones–Wenzl projector. Choose the Seifert zero framing in S^3 as reference, and write f for gamma’s framing, ell=lk(gamma,K), b=lk(gamma,L), the sum over the components of L.

2. Integral Weyl modules without divided-factorial denominators

For m>=0 define D_m on u_0,…,u_m by

H u_j=(m-2j)u_j, K u_j=q^(m-2j)u_j,
E u_j=[m-j+1]u_(j-1), F u_j=[j+1]u_(j+1).

These formulas have no factorial denominators. The identity [j+1][m-j]-[j][m-j+1]=[m-2j] checks the commutator, and the H and K relations follow directly from the weights. At the specified root EN=FN=0: every product of N consecutive quantum integers contains a multiple of N. They are therefore actual weight modules for the same restricted unrolled quantum group, even when m>=N. Only the auxiliary loop’s color is changed here; the core color and its reduced or full normalization stay fixed.

For every m>=1 there is the exact sequence

0 -> D_(m+1) -> W tensor D_m -> D_(m-1) -> 0. (2.1)

Here are explicit maps, valid before and after root specialization:

i(v_j)=q^(-j)w_0 tensor u_j+w_1 tensor u_(j-1),
pi(w_0 tensor u_j)=t_(j-1),
pi(w_1 tensor u_j)=-q^(-j-1)t_j,

where missing endpoint vectors are zero. They preserve H and K. Substitution in Delta E=E tensor K+1 tensor E and Delta F=F tensor 1+K^(-1) tensor F verifies E and F using [j+1]=q[j]+q^(-j) and q[j+1]-[j]=q^(j+1). The map i is injective, pi is surjective, and pi*i=0. Their dimensions give exactness. All these assertions use unit matrix entries and do not divide by [N]. Also W tensor D_0=D_1.

Consequently in the Grothendieck group of these actual modules,

[D_m]=U_m([W]/2),
C_n([W])=[D_n]-[D_(n-2)], n>=2.              (2.2)

This follows recursively from (2.1); it is not a generic-q semisimplicity argument specialized through a pole.

3. Root subquotients, including their pivots and twists

At m=N there is the additional exact sequence

0 -> D_(N-2) -> D_N -> C_(+N) direct-sum C_(-N) ->0, (3.1)

where C_h is the one-dimensional module with H=h, K=q^h, E=F=0. The submodule map is u_a -> [a+1]v_(a+1), 0<=a<N-1. These coefficients are nonzero. The quotient consists of the two endpoint weights +N and -N. The endpoint transitions to the quotient vanish because [N]=0. This checks every generator and proves (3.1).

Combining (2.2) and (3.1),

C_N([W])=[C_(+N)]+[C_(-N)].                 (3.2)

For delta_N=(-1)^(N-1), both one-dimensional colors have

dim_q(C_(+/-N))=delta_N,
theta(C_(+/-N))=delta_N*i^N.                 (3.3)

Indeed their pivot is q^((1-N)h), their crossing is q(h2/2), and closing that crossing gives the twist q(h2/2+(1-N)h). These are exactly (3.3) at h=+/-N. No framing phase is omitted.

The polynomial identity C_(2N)(X)=C_N(X)^2-2 and tensoring one-dimensional colors then give

C_(2N)([W])=[C_(+2N)]+[C_(-2N)].             (3.4)

These latter colors have pivot one, twist one, and trivial double braiding with W. Their double braiding with V_alpha is the scalar x(+/-2N)=M(+/-2N). This is the even character used below. It does not silently replace the odd-thread data (3.3).

4. Why these Grothendieck identities compute the actual knot period

The closed auxiliary-component evaluation is additive in a short exact sequence of colors. A direct proof uses a braid with the core cut and a basis adapted to the auxiliary submodule. The finite R, its inverse and the pivot preserve the filtration on each colored strand, with the strand positions exchanged at a crossing. A state can move only down the filtration along the underlying auxiliary component. Closing its trace forces it to return to its initial filtration piece; therefore every contributing state stays in one graded piece around that entire component. Summing those pieces gives exactly the two subquotient-colored evaluations. Separate closed components are treated independently. This is filtered linear algebra on the literal cut contraction, not an assumption about additivity of a modified trace on the core.

Naturality of R and the tensor-product crossing identities proved in the peripheral note identify tensor colors with framed parallels. Thus (3.2)–(3.4) apply to the stated annular threadings for arbitrary gamma, including knotted gamma and diagrams with auxiliary self-crossings.

For C_h, h=+/-N, the ratio between the two possible crossings with a core strand is the scalar monodromy q(hH)=x(+/-N). With a W strand it is -1. Unlinking the auxiliary component by crossing changes therefore records only its linking numbers. Its remaining one-dimensional knot evaluation is its pivotal dimension times its framing twist. This last statement can also be checked directly: on a one-component braid with B strands and writhe w, the zero-framing value is g^(B-w)=g, since g=+/-1 and B-w is odd. Its self-crossing scalar cancels against the twist.

We obtain the exact whole-germ formulas

F_N^(L union C_N(gamma))
   =delta_N*(delta_N*i^N)^f*(-1)^b
                     *(x^(N ell)+x^(-N ell))*F_N^L,

F_N^(L union C_(2N)(gamma))
   =(U^ell+U^(-ell))*F_N^L.                 (4.1)

In the first line xN=delta_N*MN, so its multiplier can also be written delta_N(ell+1)(delta_NiN)f*(-1)b times M^(N ell)+M^(-N ell). In the second line every auxiliary framing and W-linking phase cancels exactly. The same equations hold for the full period N_N^L, retaining the entire modified dimension.

For the boundary meridian ell=1 and f=b=0, the first equation is C_N(M+M(-1))=MN+M^(-N). For the preferred parallel ell=0, it is 2delta_N, agreeing with the full ADO character TN=(-1)(N-1). These provide independent checks of both signs.

No universal Chebyshev homomorphism at degree 2N, or an unproved identification of its source skein parameter, is asserted. Equation (4.1) is the specific evaluation identity proved here, including the actual fourth-root phases at degree N.

5. The marked ramified fibre has the trivial even character

The even eigenvalues in (4.1) are the squared-trace coordinates of the abelian character with meridian

rho_U^ab(mu)=diag(U^(1/2),U^(-1/2)):
(tr rho_U^ab(gamma))^2-2=U^ell+U^(-ell).      (5.1)

The square-root sign is irrelevant to this coordinate. At U=1 the value is two for every gamma. This includes the whole marked fibre of ramified Weyl descent: in its coordinate z=(U-1)/(U+1), Uell+U(-ell) is even with constant term two. It multiplies both a_0 and b_0 by two. No extra odd coefficient changes the complete normal functional.

In particular a null-homologous gamma has ell=0, and its even threading acts by exactly two on the entire meromorphic family, not just at the complete point. This also survives the canonical polar subtraction and its affine source equation: each part is multiplied by the same constant two.

This does not require K_N to be nonzero. The ordinary anchor (a_0)_1/(Q-1)=1 makes the actual marked fibre nonzero at every level. Nor does (5.1) contradict a hyperbolic semiclassical critical point: the threading degree 2N grows with the level. Fixed small observables and fixed root-central characters are different data.

6. A null-homologous geometric separator for every hyperbolic knot

Let rho_hyp be the complete holonomy. Two conjugates of a cusp meridian have distinct fixed points: otherwise the meridian conjugates, which generate the knot group, would all preserve one point, contradicting the non-elementary complete holonomy. After conjugation and harmless SL_2 signs, write them as

p=[1 1;0 1], q=[1 0;s 1], s!=0.

A direct multiplication gives

tr[p^a,q]=2+a^2*s^2,
(tr[p^a,q])^2-4=a^2*s^2*(a^2*s^2+4).        (6.1)

The choices a=1 and a=2 cannot both make the second expression zero when s!=0. Thus one of the two commutator words gamma has

lk(gamma,K)=0,
g_gamma(rho_hyp):=(tr rho_hyp(gamma))^2-2 !=2. (6.2)

Represent the word by an embedded loop and choose its framing. The even threading formula is independent of that framing. The squared trace is independent of the lift to SL_2. The witness is uniform; no alternatingness or knot table is used.

The positive ideal triangulation represents this same complete holonomy, so g_gamma-2 is a unit in its local geometric character ring. This is a precise interior-character distinction not detected by the common parabolic peripheral values alone.

7. What a fixed-central-character comparison can and cannot identify

Suppose a proposed geometric root realization identifies the even threaded observable C_(2N)(gamma) with the scalar g_gamma(rho)=(tr rho(gamma))^2-2. This is explicit comparison data; no published quantum-trace Frobenius theorem is being assumed to establish it here. At the hyperbolic point, (6.2) and the actual evaluation (5.1) give distinct eigenvalues.

Any intertwiner preserving this observable between the actual evaluation fibre and that fixed geometric central-character fibre must be zero: its matrix Phi satisfies

(g_gamma(rho_hyp)-2)*Phi=0.

The same holds locally because the difference is a unit. A scalar Hilbert-90 frame change or finite faithfully flat cover cannot change these scalar eigenvalues. In particular a direct equality of these two fixed central eigenlines cannot be the missing state comparison.

This is not an obstruction to a source extension, a comparison only after the correct relative pushforward, or a varying-centre realization with a hyperbolic semiclassical saddle. Such a second realization need not have the fixed hyperbolic root character. It also does not show that the entire cyclic module of an actual operator lacks geometric factors: its selected evaluation is a particular quotient, and the distinction matters.

8. A separating observable can select the source period exactly

There is a useful positive consequence for the extension approach. Over a difference field (F,sigma), take a specified source system

sigma(w)=A_aug*w, A_aug=[ C  b;0 1 ].

Suppose it carries an actual difference endomorphism

X_gamma=[ G  d_gamma;0 2 ],
sigma(X_gamma)*A_aug=A_aug*X_gamma,            (8.1)

and 2I-G is invertible. Its unique eigenvector of eigenvalue two whose source coordinate is one is

w=(v,1), v=(2I-G)^(-1)*d_gamma.              (8.2)

This vector is automatically horizontal. Applying sigma to its eigenvalue equation and using (8.1) shows that both sigma(w) and A_augw are eigenvectors of sigma(X_gamma) with eigenvalue two and last coordinate one. Invertibility gives uniqueness, hence sigma(w)=A_augw. This proves the source equation, without a monodromy computation or a root-rank hypothesis.

Equivalently the eigenline projector is

P_2=[0  (2I-G)^(-1)d_gamma;0 1], P_2^2=P_2.

It preserves the difference structure by (8.1). If G=g_gamma I is a fixed geometric central character, the separator (6.2) makes the inverse a regular scalar near the complete point. Its denominator supplies no growing-level volume exponent. Any such exponent must remain in the actual source coefficient d_gamma and its selected period pairing.

The even threading theorem supplies the eigenvalue two for the actual null-homologous observable. The geometric triangulation supplies a classical separator with g_gamma!=2. What is still missing is construction of an actual relative gluing/source system with the endomorphism (8.1), its off-diagonal coefficient, and the marked normal functional. Neither block can be chosen to force the desired value. The lemma is a criterion for that construction, not a proof that it has already been achieved.

Specializing a generic nonsplit source extension to a split root-level extension causes no contradiction: its distinguished splitting (8.2) can still carry large periods. Splitting and the norm of the specified splitting are separate statements.

If a specified generic source class is nonsplit, a separating endomorphism of the form (8.1) cannot exist over that generic difference field: (8.2) would split it. It cannot arise after a finite sigma-stable algebraic cover either, by the normalized-trace descent proved in the affine note. The actual threaded observables have degree 2N and depend on the level. Their root-level existence supplies no such fixed generic endomorphism. This retains the distinction between an algebraic evaluation at each level and a fixed-degree presentation across all levels.

9. Literature coverage and what has not been imported

The common skein/gluing framework is already covered by Garoufalidis–Yu, A quantum trace map for 3-manifolds and Panitch–Park, 3d Quantum Trace Map. The later Chen–Kricker, On 3d Quantum Trace Maps advertises partial comparison of these constructions. Their abstracts and the scope of the quantum-gluing definition were consulted for coverage. No global trace-map, equivalence, Frobenius-compatibility, or knot-state-comparison proof from these works is a premise here. In particular no phase identification between their quantum parameters and the actual ADO parameters is inferred by citation.

Chebyshev threading and root-of-unity representation theory have existing coverage; no novelty is claimed for that framework or for the elementary spectral projector. The results here are the independent, normalization-matched evaluation identities, the uniform geometric separator, and the resulting precise source comparison criterion for this fixed knot period.

10. Independent diagnostics

The audit constructs the actual modules and R matrices. It checks 52 integral Weyl sequences and 11 root subquotient sequences through level 18. At levels 2,3,4,5 it checks 192 heterogeneous R-trace cases, including positive/negative linking, an extra fundamental strand, both threading degrees, and all three framing signs. Sixteen actual knotted auxiliary traces test the closed-component additivity through nontrivial positive and negative knot braids. The commutator witness and the separating source projector are also checked symbolically. The results support the uniform algebraic proofs and establish no volume asymptotic.
