The original central high band is an exact half-charge source

Private research note, 16 November 2026. Written proof only.

The controlled endpoint amplitude is the factor of a literal band of the original Newton kernel. Thus it can be inserted into a coupled graph before any remaining trace is estimated. At even levels the band is a half-charge coupon times one polynomial amplitude. At odd levels it is the difference of the two neighboring lattice half-charge coupons times that same amplitude. The omitted high band has explicit macroscopic physical support and polynomial coefficient cost. The low endpoint coupon remains separate.

4. Analytic use and its precise remaining scope

For L=⌊δN⌋L=\lfloor\delta N\rfloor, the adopted general-domain theorem gives, uniformly with all fixed derivatives on compact subsets of |cos⁡t−cos⁡(2πδ)|<1+cos⁡(2πδ),HN,L(α,t)=sec⁡2(t/2)+Ohol(N−2).(20) |\cos t-\cos(2\pi\delta)|<1+\cos(2\pi\delta), \qquad H_{N,L}(\alpha,t)=\sec^2(t/2)+O_{\mathrm{ hol}}(N^{-2}). \qquad\text{(20)} The normalized polynomial amplitude is therefore a unit there for large NN. The scalar (5) also is a unit after its N−2N^{-2} factor is removed on compact α\alpha-sets avoiding cosh⁡(α/2)=0\cosh(\alpha/2)=0. For δ=1/8\delta=1/8, the entire closed rectangle |ℜt|≤π/4,|ℑt|≤log⁡3|\Re t|\le\pi/4,\ |\Im t|\le\log3 lies in this domain.

Thus (15)–(17) put the band into actual original near-half-charge sources with a common controlled local multiplier. This is stronger than identifying a formal off-grid phase or merely matching central physical values. At odd levels the difference in (17) must remain coherent. Its cosine factor is exactly 2sin⁡(Nt/2)sin⁡(t/2)2\sin(Nt/2)\sin(t/2); at even levels the factor is cos⁡(Nt/2)\cos(Nt/2). Their insertion in the original double-pole horizontal trace cancels one denominator factor, giving the same simple-pole half-charge kernel as in the amplitude theorem. All original vertical terms are still present.

The theorem does not bound the full near-half-charge sources, prove a gap for the physical sources in (19), or compare their combined rate with the filled action. It also does not factor the low part of KN(wN(α),z)K_N(w_N(\alpha),z) through a radical charge coupon. Combining that low part with (18), and controlling the remaining full graph source, are separate tasks.
