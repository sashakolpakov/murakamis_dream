A formal five-shape identity on the critical signed Gauss word

Source-identification correction, 31 October 2026. The exact algebra below uses the complete-color five-shape expression at every crossing, although the auxiliary raw meridian has trace zero and color χaux=±i\chi_{aux}=\pm i, rather than −1-1. The universal Gauss/Hurwitz MAP remains valid, but the actual mixed crossing symbol has color factors omitted here. Therefore the asserted identification with the original mixed source or its diagram regulator is withdrawn. The computed rational word closure, formal-symbol differential and calibrated formal-symbol identity remain valid algebraic results. The true mixed-color symbol, its monomial boundary term, and any actual relative-volume comparison are UNPROVED in this note. All occurrences of “actual” below referring to the twenty SYMBOLS must be read subject to this correction; the actual matrix maps are unaffected. This note is unpublished and quarantined from geometric or quantum conclusions.

This section is a formal signed-Green identity. Its source and geometric interpretation require the additional hypotheses stated below.

This is a FORMAL-symbol identity on the universal untwisted four-crossing word on its free-spectral critical straddling branch. Both auxiliary central lifts are included. It does not assert an off-critical identity, a global relative fundamental-cycle identity, or a contribution of any original finite-sum cycle. In fact the corresponding off-critical local identity is false.

1. The critical domain and the retained source primitive

Use the calibrated action and physical conventions of the signed straddling relation. Let x,y,b>0x,y,b>0, 0<δ<1/20<\delta<1/2, and put

The output root is the negative one, T−c=−TT_{-c}=-T. Equation (1) is exactly the free real-spectral stationarity equation for the retained absolute interval action GG:

Write the actual face derivatives as

∂log⁡bG=μ,∂log⁡cG=1−ν,∂log⁡xG=ξ,∂log⁡yG=η,ξ+η=ν−μ. \partial_{\log b}G=\mu,\quad \partial_{\log c}G=1-\nu,\quad \partial_{\log x}G=\xi,\quad \partial_{\log y}G=\eta,\qquad \xi+\eta=\nu-\mu.

This is the fixed-load value of the calibrated continuum source primitive. The present lemma does not prove a new coefficient asymptotic or a selected saddle contribution.

The source reference flag and physical head conventions are unchanged throughout the proof.

5. What this identity does and does not settle

The local primitive includes all source logarithms in (4); its Legendre subtraction is essential. The actual scalar modes have purely imaginary differences on this real critical chart. The auxiliary lift and its primitive logarithm are retained in (8), so the result does not silently replace U0=±πiU_0=\pm\pi i by zero.

This calculation does not discard a global peripheral boundary term. To turn the sum of local identities into representation volume, one must still identify the original sewn relative fundamental chain, including cups, caps, the unrestricted mark and any retained half twist. The conditional group construction is stated separately in the signed group-sewing note. It does not itself imply the needed chain identity.

The present proof also does not extend to a free noncritical spectral parameter, arbitrary complex clocks, or an arbitrary absolute shared maximum. The sign-cocycle and regularity hypotheses remain independent issues. No global period bound or volume-conjecture conclusion is claimed.
