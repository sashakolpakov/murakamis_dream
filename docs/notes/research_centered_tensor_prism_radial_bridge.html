The positive prism boundary and its growing radial path

21 November 2026. Research note. Earlier finals and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The dilogarithmic action of the initialized centered tensor has an exact boundary interpretation in the already constructed ideal prism. Its imaginary part is the negative Newton prism action; its real part is a specified linear phase. Moreover the entire radial segment from the origin to every strict positive prism boundary point lies on the growing side of this action.

There is also an exact finite-source interpretation of the physical boundary grid. A centered generating packet on that grid selects one Newton basis vector. Three such packets therefore recover the positive Newton tensor, including its full repeated-root algebra and original Frobenius closing. These statements connect the geometric boundary symbol to the initialized source near the origin. They do not prove transport of its asymptotic through the whole disk or uniformly onto the unit boundary.

1. The strict prism domain and its original face labels

Let 𝒫={𝒙∈ℝ3:xi>0,xi+xj<1(i≠j),S:=x1+x2+x3>1}.(1) \mathcal P=\{\mathbf x\in\mathbb R^3: x_i>0,\quad x_i+x_j<1\ (i\ne j),\quad S:=x_1+x_2+x_3>1\}. \qquad\text{(1)} Then xi<1x_i<1 and 1<S<3/21<S<3/2. Define L(u)=∫0ulog⁡(2sin⁡πv)dv(0<u<1),τ(𝒙)=∑i<jL(xi+xj)−∑iL(xi)−L(S−1).(2) L(u)=\int_0^u\log(2\sin\pi v)\,dv\quad(0<u<1), \qquad \tau(\mathbf x)=\sum_{i<j}L(x_i+x_j) -\sum_iL(x_i)-L(S-1). \qquad\text{(2)} All arguments in this formula lie strictly between zero and one.

The original directed Newton multiplication has action m(a,b,c)=L(a+b)+L(c)−L(a)−L(b)−L(c−a)−L(c−b)−L(a+b−c).(3) m(a,b,c)=L(a+b)+L(c)-L(a)-L(b) -L(c-a)-L(c-b)-L(a+b-c). \qquad\text{(3)} Set its two input labels to a=x1,b=x2a=x_1,b=x_2 and its output label to c=1−x3c=1-x_3. Conditions (1) are exactly its strict support conditions. The reflection identity L(1−u)=−L(u)L(1-u)=-L(u) gives τ(𝒙)=m(x1,x2,1−x3).(4) \tau(\mathbf x)=m(x_1,x_2,1-x_3). \qquad\text{(4)} The ideal-prism theorem constructs a convex ideal triangular prism with these directed, ordered quadrilateral faces and proves Vol⁡(Prism⁡𝒙)=πτ(𝒙).(5) \operatorname{Vol}(\operatorname{Prism}_{\mathbf x}) =\pi\tau(\mathbf x). \qquad\text{(5)} The complement 1−x31-x_3 is required by the original Frobenius pairing of an output with the third leg. It is not an unrecorded permutation or mutation of the ordered prism faces.

3. The entire strict prism has a growing radial path

Theorem 2. For every 𝒙∈𝒫\mathbf x\in\mathcal P, Im⁡Ψ(𝒕(r))<0(0<r≤1).(14) \operatorname{Im}\Psi(\mathbf t(r))<0 \qquad(0<r\le1). \qquad\text{(14)} The inequality is uniform away from zero on compact subsets of 𝒫×(0,1]\mathcal P\times(0,1].

5. What has been connected

Near the origin, the proved complex source connection supplies its initialized nonzero growing packet wherever Im⁡Ψ≤0\operatorname{Im}\Psi\le0. Every compact family of the radial paths (7) begins in that neighborhood. Theorem 2 shows that its classical saddle height stays strictly positive for every r>0r>0; Theorem 1 identifies its boundary height with the already constructed ordered prism volume. Independently, (27) initializes the actual positive source on the physical boundary grid.

These facts do not prove that the source asymptotic is transported through the entire intervening disk, or uniformly into a level-dependent layer approaching r=1r=1. They also do not move the assembled Conway coefficient cycle to these local evaluations. Estimates for that source transport and the global marked geometric gluing are still required for arbitrary short fillings. No new volume-identity knot family or finite-exception theorem is asserted.
