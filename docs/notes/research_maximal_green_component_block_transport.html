Exact cap-tree and lower-block transport at a maximal Green child

31 October 2026. analytical research theorem. This preserves the original face targets and every finite LEFT/cap cutoff. It gives an exact mixed-block reduction, not an absolute bound by Phi_D. No numerical test or publication claim is used.

1. The actual maximal-child component

Use the classified common-weight datum. Choose a strict LEFT Green port E with maximal child weight among all Green ports, and call its child c. RIGHT is handled by the literal row reflection. Let S be the connected component of actual residual cap edges containing c.

The response/network theorem proves that S is a tree, c has cap indegree zero, every other vertex has cap indegree one, and every other noncap port incident to S is CROSS. The chosen Green has no input in S, since all its supported input weights are strictly smaller than its child weight. Orient every cap from parent to its overloaded child. These orientations are therefore precisely the rooted-tree orientation away from c.

Let P_N((z_v)_(v in S),Y) be the product of ALL the other noncap coupons incident to S, retaining their genuine CROSS projections. They may share arbitrarily many face variables. At endpoint -2, after fixed raw signs, its coefficients are nonnegative. The finite identities below also hold for arbitrary spectra in these CROSS coupons: positivity is not used in their derivation. The unchanged outside source has no incidence to S.

Put N>=3, d=N-1, M=floor(N/2)-1. Each residual cap is held at its actual stationary spectrum +2 and has exact polynomial

F_v(z_v,z_parent)=sum_(j=0)^M (-1)^j U_j
                              z_v^(d-j)z_parent^j,  v!=c,   (1)

in the original cap conventions, or their literal reflections. Here

U_j=csc^2(pi/(2N))
       product_(r=1)^j sin^2(pi r/N)/sin^2(pi(r+1/2)/N).

No regularized reference replaces this raw cap. Write the raw chosen Green LEFT polynomial, removing only its fixed sign (-1)^d, as

P_(E,LEFT)(c,Y;sigma)=sum_(s=0)^M c^(d-s)G_s(Y;sigma).      (2)

Its genuine strict-child support is s<=M at BOTH parities.

2. Original target extraction solves every cap index

Expand the CROSS product in its original component-face variables:

P_N(z,Y)=sum_(k in Z_+^S) p_k(Y) product_v z_v^(k_v).

For v!=c let S_v be its descendant subtree, including v, and put

K_v(k)=sum_(w in S_v) k_w,    |k|=sum_(w in S) k_w.

Extract the original demand z_v^d at EVERY vertex of S. At a nonroot vertex the exponent equality is exactly

(d-j_v)+sum_(w child of v) j_w+k_v=d.

At the root it is

(d-s)+sum_(w child of c)j_w+k_c=d.

Starting from the leaves therefore gives, with no relaxed constraint,

j_v=K_v(k),    s=|k|.                                      (3)

Because k_w>=0 and s<=M, EVERY cap restriction j_v<=M is automatic. Conversely every k>=0 with |k|<=M gives exactly one set of all cap indices satisfying the original face targets. Thus, up to the one retained fixed Green/CROSS raw sign, the exact component contraction is

B_N(Y;sigma)
  =sum_(|k|<=M) G_|k|(Y;sigma) p_k(Y)
               product_(v!=c) [(-1)^K_v(k) U_K_v(k)].       (4)

All exterior face degrees remain explicit in G_s and p_k. This is not a new source on a quotient graph: (3) is the solution of the original unit demands, before any analytic estimate.

The same count explains the classical quotient. At a stationary flow, each cap split is the total CROSS supply in its descendant subtree, and the total CROSS supply in S is 1-q_(E,c)<1/2. In (3) the exact integer version is s<=M, not a freely chosen residual demand.

3. A genuine positive-moment transport, with the total truncation first

The proved cap moment law gives independent positive variables X_v, one per cap edge, with

E X_v^j=U_j/U_0, 0<=j<=d.

Define the signed path dilation

T_c=1,    T_w=product_(edges v on path c to w) (-X_v).

Then

product_w T_w^(k_w)
      =product_(v!=c)(-X_v)^K_v(k).

Let P_(N,s)(z,Y) denote the part of P_N of TOTAL S-degree s. Equation (4) is equivalent to the exact finite formula

B_N(Y;sigma)
  =U_0^(|S|-1) E sum_(s=0)^M
                       G_s(Y;sigma) P_(N,s)(T,Y).          (5)

Equivalently, with one formal root variable z,

B_N=U_0^(|S|-1) E [z^d]
      P_(E,LEFT)(z,Y;sigma)
      (Pi_(total S degree<=M) P_N)((zT_v)_v,Y).            (6)

The TOTAL-degree truncation in (6) is essential. For every monomial retained by it, the exponent of each independent X_v is K_v(k)<=M, so all absolute moments in (5)-(6) are finite. Coefficient extraction and expectation may be interchanged on this finite truncated space.

One must NOT put the untruncated full P_N inside the expectation before extracting the coefficient. Its degree in a descendant subtree may exceed d; the cap law has no such general finite moment. This is the same exact finite-moment restriction as in the shared-star theorem, now applied to a real cap tree.

The formula retains nontrivial quantum information beyond the classical component-sum quotient. Already the monomials z_c^s and z_v^s for a leaf v have the SAME total component degree but receive relative factors 1 and (-1)^(s distance(c,v)) product_(edges on path) U_s/U_0. Therefore replacing all component faces by one equal variable is not the actual finite cap elimination. This is a statement about the exact boundary polynomial operator, not a counterexample to a source estimate on a particular diagram.

4. Exact Green lower-block factorization, both parities

The remaining single Green operation in (5) has a concrete finite spectral reduction. Set theta=pi/N, m=floor(N/2), M=m-1, and

a_r=2+2cos((2r+1)theta), s_r=2sin(r theta),
u=-(sigma+2), alpha_r(u)=a_r+u,
A_-(u)=product_(r=0)^M alpha_r(u).

Let R_j(x,b,y;u) be the original lower cleared cofactor:

R_0=1,
R_j=b^j product_(r<j)alpha_r
    +s_j^2(x+y)R_(j-1)
    +s_j s_(j-1)alpha_(j-1)xy R_(j-2),                   (7)

where absent negative indices and the last term at j=1 are zero. It is homogeneous of FACE degree j. The original prefix/active/suffix path expansion gives exactly

G_j(Y;sigma)=R_j(x,b,y;u) product_(r=j+1)^d alpha_r(u).      (8)

No unused row or initial-state sum has been omitted. Reflection alpha_(d-r)=alpha_r shows

product_(r=j+1)^d alpha_r(u)
  =zeta_N(u) product_(r=j+1)^M alpha_r(u), j<=M,
zeta_N(u)=A_-(u)              if N=2m,
          u A_-(u)          if N=2m+1.                    (9)

Define the actual lower-block numerator

L_M(x,b,c,y;u)
  =sum_(j=0)^M c^(M-j)R_j(x,b,y;u)
                           product_(r=j+1)^M alpha_r(u).

It has FACE degree M and SPECTRAL degree at most M. The exact original Green source is therefore

P_(E,LEFT)(c,Y;sigma)
              =zeta_N(u)c^(d-M)L_M(x,b,c,y;u).             (10)

The raw source has the fixed sign (-1)^d restored afterward. The odd factor u is the UNUSED central row, not a cancelled zero. This is the original level-N lower block, not a quantum source at a different level.

5. A positive nodal tail, with the child character retained

The m lower nodes u_a=-a_a, 0<=a<=M, are distinct. At u=u_a, all terms of L_M with j<a vanish because their retained suffix contains alpha_a=0. For j>=a, the exact cofactor identity is

R_j(u_a)=K_(a,j)(u_a) R_a(u_a).

Here K_(a,a)=1, and K is the original interval continuant. The coefficientwise nodal theorem proves

R_a(x,b,y;u_a)>=0,
K_(a,j)(x,y;u_a)>=0, j<=M.                                (11)

For clarity, positivity of K is nontrivial: normalized by product s_r^2 it obeys q_r=(x+y)q_(r-1)-gamma_r xy q_(r-2), with 0<=gamma_r<=1. Induction on q_r-xq_(r-1) proves positivity and q_r<=(x+y)^r. No termwise positivity of the signed dimer weights has been assumed.

For r>a one has a_a-a_r>0. Put

T_(a,M)(x,c,y)
  =sum_(j=a)^M c^(M-j) K_(a,j)(x,y;u_a)
                      product_(r=j+1)^M (a_a-a_r).        (12)

This is a nonnegative-coefficient polynomial of FACE degree M-a. The entire nodal source factors EXACTLY as

L_M(x,b,c,y;u_a)
                =R_a(x,b,y;u_a) T_(a,M)(x,-c,y).           (13)

The sign on c in (13) is exactly the sign of the unused lower suffix: there are M-j negative alpha_r factors. It cannot be deleted. The first factor is the actual complete-node input cofactor R_a=S_(2a)D_a(x,b,y), where S_k=product_(r=1)^k s_r and D_a is the original complete-circle determinant. This exact normalization is the source adapter in original complete-node cofactor adapter section 1, equations (3)-(4). The second factor is the explicit positive tail (12); no covariance or different-level quantum interpretation of it is asserted.

At the two extremes,

B_M=product_(r=1)^M s_r^2,
T_(M,M)=1,
T_(0,M)=B_M s_(M+1)
     sum_(j=0)^M h_j(x,y)c^(M-j)/s_(j+1),
h_j(x,y)=sum_(r=0)^j x^r y^(j-r).                         (14)

Indeed at a=0 the normalized interval continuant is h_j, and a_0-a_r=s_(r+1)s_r makes the suffix product telescope. These formulas are exact finite source identities, not estimates.

6. Polynomial-cost interpolation reduces the remaining spectral variable

Let ell_a(u) be the cardinal polynomials on the LOWER m nodes u_a. Since deg_u L_M<=M, (13) gives the exact identity

L_M(u)=sum_(a=0)^M ell_a(u)
                     R_a(x,b,y;u_a) T_(a,M)(x,-c,y).       (15)

At even levels the nodes are the shifted Chebyshev roots, with Lebesgue norm O(log N) on [-4,0]. At odd levels use the full physical Radau family including the central u=0 node. For any scalar polynomial p of degree <=M, interpolate u p(u), of degree <=m, on that full grid. Its value at zero is zero. The full-grid O(log N) bound and division by the ONE known endpoint factor cost at most a fixed polynomial in N: for instance |p(u)|<=sup|(u p(u))’| and the elementary degree-m derivative bound C m^3 sup|u p(u)| give

sup_[-4,0]|p|
   <=C N^3 log(N+1) max_(0<=a<=M)|p(u_a)|.                (16)

This proof works for complex scalar values. It does not infer an angular nodal theorem at an unavailable index. A polynomial cost is all that is claimed.

The original prefactor in (9) has a uniform REAL-spectral bound. With sigma=2cos phi, the exact Chebyshev products give

|zeta_N(u)|<=2                         (N even),
|zeta_N(u)|=|4cos(phi/2)cos(N phi/2)|<=4 (N odd).           (17)

The limiting endpoint values are polynomial continuations; no zero is divided. In particular the odd unused central factor is included.

For the ISOLATED component S={c}, put

Q_(a,N)(Y)=R_a(x,b,y;u_a)
                [c^M] T_(a,M)(x,-c,y) P_N(c,Y).

The original isolated Green-star coefficient is exactly

B_N(Y;sigma)=zeta_N(u)
                      sum_(a=0)^M ell_a(u) Q_(a,N)(Y),    (18)

and (16)-(17) yield, for all real sigma in [-2,2],

|B_N(Y;sigma)|<=poly(N) max_a |Q_(a,N)(Y)|.                (19)

The unchanged outside face coefficient can be taken after this identity; it is linear and all original targets remain. One may also apply the scalar cosine-Bernstein bound to B_N as a degree-d polynomial in sigma to extend (19) to a fold strip with the explicit factor exp(2pi d |Im delta|).

For a nontrivial cap component, replace the s-th root coefficient of P_N in (18) by the exact averaged coefficient from (5). Equivalently, apply (18) to the finite polynomial sum_(s=0)^M E P_(N,s)(T,Y)c^s and retain the outside factor U_0^(|S|-1). This is legitimate finite linear algebra by the moment cutoff check in section 3.

7. What is resolved, and the exact remaining signed pairing

Equations (3)-(6) are a finite quantum counterpart of the actual cap quotient, retaining every descendant demand and its character. Equations (10)-(19) reduce the continuous Green spectrum at polynomial cost to explicit finite child-character pairings against the actual CROSS spectator product. No arbitrary matrix, new face target or coefficientwise comparison of an alternating sum has been introduced.

The lower block has distinct nodes, but this does NOT discard the original Casimir Jordan data. The original LEFT numerator vanishes at physical nodes because of zeta_N. Its quotient values L_M(u_a) retain precisely the information lost by that vanishing factor. They are not substituted for full-numerator values at those nodes.

What remains is a bound on the signed quantities Q_(a,N), or their exact cap-tree moment averages, relative to an endpoint/volume quantity on the SAME original fiber. The factors in (13) have nonnegative coefficients before c is changed to -c, but taking their absolute coefficients would remove the cancellation being sought. Their positive capacity need not equal the original Phi_D. The a=M term has no tail and lies at the retained half boundary; the smaller a terms retain the genuine signed tail.

Thus (19) is a concrete finite spectral reduction, NOT an absolute Phi_D estimate, an instability theorem, or a proof of the prescribed long-twist volume statement. No globally feasible maximum or counterexample is manufactured by these identities.
