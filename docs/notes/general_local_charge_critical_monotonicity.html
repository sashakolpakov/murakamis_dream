Independent long twists: monotonicity of the local charge critical value

The whole-source local packet now assembles these results for the actual full numerator. The original global spectral filling period remains open.

The localized original spectral period now supplies the separate contour and nonvanishing proof. Combining it with the comparison below proves dominance within a finite local charge sector. The complete global remainder remains open.

31 October 2026. . This is a local analytic statement about the actual normalized source action. It compares critical VALUES; it does not prove intersection with an original integration cycle, a nonzero amplitude, or control of remote charges.

1. The actual even action and a general analytic lemma

Let A0(𝜹)A_0(\boldsymbol\delta) be the original face-stationary complete CROSS action without the retained quadratic half-twist phase. The two original marked faces remain zero. The cofactor action identity gives each triangle as a holomorphic function of wi=−4sin⁡2(πδi)w_i=-4\sin^2(\pi\delta_i). The unique ordinary-face IFT branch is therefore unchanged under reversal of ANY individual δi\delta_i. Consequently A0A_0 is even in EACH coordinate. Its coefficients are real, because the selected face/action germs are real on the real locus. The actual complete normalization and local curvature give

A0(0)=ΦD,Hess⁡A0(0)=−2diag⁡(C1,…,CR),Ci>0. A_0(0)=\Phi_D,\qquad \operatorname{Hess}A_0(0)=-2\operatorname{diag}(C_1,\ldots,C_R),\qquad C_i>0.

Spectral-independent source phases remain outside this normalized action. They do not change any derivative below.

More generally assume exactly these analytic hypotheses. There are holomorphic functions BiB_i of 𝒛=(δ12,…,δR2)\mathbf z=(\delta_1^2,\ldots,\delta_R^2), with real coefficients, such that

∂iA0(𝜹)=−2δiBi(𝜹2),Bi(0)=Ci.(1) \partial_i A_0(\boldsymbol\delta)=-2\delta_i B_i(\boldsymbol\delta^2),\qquad B_i(0)=C_i.\qquad (1)

This follows coefficient by coefficient from separate evenness; the quotient at δi=0\delta_i=0 is removable. In particular BiB_i is NOT inferred from only the diagonal Hessian without that evenness hypothesis.

For independent nonzero REAL slopes nin_i and real charges ℓi\ell_i define

Φ(𝜹;𝒏,ℓ)=A0(𝜹)+πi∑iniδi2+2πi∑iℓiδi. \Phi(\boldsymbol\delta;\mathbf n,\boldsymbol\ell)=A_0(\boldsymbol\delta)+\pi i\sum_i n_i\delta_i^2+2\pi i\sum_i\ell_i\delta_i.

In the actual source ni=ϵi−2pin_i=\epsilon_i-2p_i are the fixed signed integer crossing powers, and the surviving ℓi\ell_i are integer charges. The analytic statement first allows real charges in order to differentiate.

3. Exact derivative and relative control for disparate slopes

Let S(𝒏,ℓ)=Φ(𝜹(𝒏,ℓ);𝒏,ℓ)S(\mathbf n,\boldsymbol\ell)=\Phi(\boldsymbol\delta(\mathbf n,\boldsymbol\ell);\mathbf n,\boldsymbol\ell) be the selected critical value. For real nin_i,ℓi\ell_i, envelope differentiation gives

∂ℓiRe⁡S=−2πIm⁡δi. \partial_{\ell_i}\operatorname{Re}S=-2\pi\operatorname{Im}\delta_i.

4. Unit charge is the unique largest LOCAL positive-integer value

Increase n0n_0 if necessary so a|ni|≥1a|n_i|\geq1. In the local positive integer box

1≤ℓi≤a|ni|, 1\leq\ell_i\leq a|n_i|,

the all-unit vector is the unique maximizer of Re⁡S\operatorname{Re}S. More precisely, integrate (4) along successive coordinate segments from 11 to ℓi\ell_i; every intermediate vector remains in the same scaled box. Then

∑iCi2ni2(ℓi2−1)≤Re⁡S(𝒏,𝟏)−Re⁡S(𝒏,ℓ)≤∑i2Cini2(ℓi2−1).(6) \sum_i\frac{C_i}{2n_i^2}(\ell_i^2-1)\leq\operatorname{Re}S(\mathbf n,\mathbf1)-\operatorname{Re}S(\mathbf n,\boldsymbol\ell)\leq\sum_i\frac{2C_i}{n_i^2}(\ell_i^2-1).\qquad (6)

For positive REAL charges there is strict coordinate monotonicity, not a claim that charge 11 maximizes the interval (0,1)(0,1). Separate evenness and uniqueness also imply that reversing any individual ℓi\ell_i reverses that δi\delta_i alone and leaves SS unchanged. This is a symmetry of critical values; signs of amplitudes and cycle multiplicities require their actual source identities.

For the original half-twist convention, define explicitly

The already proved Gaussian boundary-term cancellation identifies 2πRe⁡S2\pi\operatorname{Re}S with its representation volume. The monotonicity proved here compares LOCAL critical values within the specified box only.
