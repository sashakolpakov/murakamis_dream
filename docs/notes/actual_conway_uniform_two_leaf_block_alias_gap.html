A uniform alias gap for an entire two-leaf Conway block

31 October 2026. This proof treats two leaves and their original multiplication gate together. It holds on the entire proper-seed range and removes the earlier individual-leaf condition X≥2/3X\ge2/3. Its finite certificate uses exact rational inequalities with proved transcendental remainder bounds, not numerical sampling.

1. The original functions and the uniform statement

Retain the original complete, core and alias profiles

J(t)=∫0tlog⁡(2sin⁡πu)du,g(x)=J(2x)−4J(x),g̃(x)=g(max⁡(x,1/4)),0≤x≤1/2,k(x)=−2J(x/2),0≤x≤1. \begin{gathered} J(t)=\int_0^t\log(2\sin\pi u)\,du,\qquad g(x)=J(2x)-4J(x),\\ \widetilde g(x)=g(\max(x,1/4)),\quad 0\le x\le1/2, \qquad k(x)=-2J(x/2),\quad 0\le x\le1. \end{gathered} \tag{1}

The actual Newton product action and its whole domain are

m(x,y,z)=J(x+y)+J(z)−J(x)−J(y)−J(z−x)−J(z−y)−J(x+y−z),D={x,y≥0:max⁡(x,y)≤z≤x+y≤1}. \begin{split} m(x,y,z)&=J(x+y)+J(z)-J(x)-J(y)\\ &\quad-J(z-x)-J(z-y)-J(x+y-z),\\ D&=\{x,y\ge0:\ \max(x,y)\le z\le x+y\le1\}. \end{split} \tag{2}

All these actions are continuous and concave on their stated domains. The tetrahedral decomposition of mm proves this even when one input exceeds 1/21/2. An alias is not assigned the support of a pure leaf.

At a complete optimized seed A2A_2, set

X=cot⁡(πa),cot⁡(πb)=X−12X,F2(b)=2g(a)+m(a,a,b),λ=F2′(b)=2log⁡(2X2). \begin{gathered} X=\cot(\pi a),\qquad \cot(\pi b)=X-\frac1{2X},\\ F_2(b)=2g(a)+m(a,a,b),\qquad \lambda=F_2'(b)=2\log(2X^2). \end{gathered} \tag{3}

For 1/2≤X≤11/2\le X\le1 this state is strict, a≥1/4a\ge1/4, and −2log⁡2≤λ≤2log⁡2-2\log2\le\lambda\le2\log2. Define the dual upper envelopes

QAC(λ)=maxD,y≤1/2[k(x)+g̃(y)+m(x,y,z)−λz],QAA(λ)=maxD[k(x)+k(y)+m(x,y,z)−λz]. \begin{split} Q_{AC}(\lambda) &=\max_{D,\ y\le1/2} [k(x)+\widetilde g(y)+m(x,y,z)-\lambda z],\\ Q_{AA}(\lambda) &=\max_D[k(x)+k(y)+m(x,y,z)-\lambda z]. \end{split} \tag{4}

Uniform block lemma. For every X∈[1/2,1]X\in[1/2,1],

QAC(λ),QAA(λ)≤F2(b)−λb−1100. Q_{AC}(\lambda),\ Q_{AA}(\lambda) \le F_2(b)-\lambda b-\frac1{100}. \tag{5}

Equivalently, the corresponding output envelopes satisfy

GAC(z),GAA(z)≤F2(b)+λ(z−b)−1100,0≤z≤1. G_{AC}(z),\ G_{AA}(z) \le F_2(b)+\lambda(z-b)-\frac1{100},\qquad 0\le z\le1. \tag{6}

These are upper envelopes for the actual coefficients. No assertion is made that a physical color or a signed coefficient attains them. The coefficient bounds include the original normal terms, both level parities and every retained half-twist sign, as checked in the exact block source proof.

2. Reduction to finitely many global supporting inequalities

Let P(λ)P(\lambda) be the maximum of g(x)+g(y)+m(x,y,z)−λzg(x)+g(y)+m(x,y,z)-\lambda z over DD with both inputs at most 1/21/2. At (3), both input derivatives vanish and the output derivative is λ\lambda. Concavity proves

P(λ)=F2(b)−λb. P(\lambda)=F_2(b)-\lambda b. \tag{7}

Explicitly, the original product derivatives at (3) are mx=my=−2log⁡Xm_x=m_y=-2\log X and mz=2log⁡(2X2)m_z=2\log(2X^2), while g′(a)=2log⁡Xg'(a)=2\log X. Thus the three derivatives of the pure dual action vanish, including at the endpoints of the stated range.

Any strict rational pure state p=(ap,ap,bp)p=(a_p,a_p,b_p) gives the lower bound

P(λ)≥A(p)−λbp,A(p)=2g(ap)+m(p). P(\lambda)\ge A(p)-\lambda b_p, \qquad A(p)=2g(a_p)+m(p). \tag{8}

For either alias action H=k+g̃+mH=k+\widetilde g+m or H=k+k+mH=k+k+m, choose a rational strict state vv; for ACAC, choose its core coordinate above 1/41/4. The global tangent bound is

H(w)≤H(v)+∇H(v)⋅(w−v). H(w)\le H(v)+\nabla H(v)\cdot(w-v). \tag{9}

The full polytopes have exactly these vertices:

To check completeness, use nonnegative coordinates p=z−x,q=z−y,r=x+y−z,w=1−x−yp=z-x,q=z-y,r=x+y-z,w=1-x-y, which obey p+q+2r+w=1p+q+2r+w=1. Thus DD is a tetrahedron. Cutting it by y≤1/2y\le1/2 removes one vertex and adds the two displayed edge intersections. A linear function attains its global maximum at a listed vertex.

Partition λ/log⁡2\lambda/\log2 into sixteen intervals

[−2+j/4,−2+(j+1)/4],j=0,…,15. [-2+j/4,-2+(j+1)/4],\qquad j=0,\ldots,15. \tag{11}

The rational certificate table specifies one pure state and one state for each alias action on each interval, all with denominator 10610^6. For every vertex ww and both endpoints tt of its interval, the exact verifier checks

A(p)−tlog⁡2bp−H(v)−∇H(v)⋅(w−v)+tlog⁡2wz>1100. A(p)-t\log2\,b_p-H(v)-\nabla H(v)\cdot(w-v) +t\log2\,w_z>\frac1{100}. \tag{12}

For each fixed vertex this expression is affine in tt, so its endpoint checks cover the entire interval. Its dependence on ww is affine, so the vertex checks cover the entire polytope. Equations (8)–(12) prove (5) for every real slope in the range. No assumption about the location or uniqueness of an alias maximizer is used; core plateau and boundary maximizers are included automatically.

3. Rational enclosures for every transcendental expression

The verifier performs outward-rounded interval arithmetic with integer endpoints at denominator 104010^{40}. It uses no floating-point trigonometric functions or special-function library. The gradients are

g′(y)=2log⁡cot⁡(πy),k′(x)=−log⁡(2sin⁡(πx/2)),mx=log⁡sin⁡π(x+y)sin⁡π(z−x)sin⁡πxsin⁡π(x+y−z),my=log⁡sin⁡π(x+y)sin⁡π(z−y)sin⁡πysin⁡π(x+y−z),mz=log⁡sin⁡πzsin⁡π(x+y−z)sin⁡π(z−x)sin⁡π(z−y). \begin{split} g'(y)&=2\log\cot(\pi y),\qquad k'(x)=-\log(2\sin(\pi x/2)),\\ m_x&=\log\frac{\sin\pi(x+y)\sin\pi(z-x)} {\sin\pi x\sin\pi(x+y-z)},\\ m_y&=\log\frac{\sin\pi(x+y)\sin\pi(z-y)} {\sin\pi y\sin\pi(x+y-z)},\\ m_z&=\log\frac{\sin\pi z\sin\pi(x+y-z)} {\sin\pi(z-x)\sin\pi(z-y)}. \end{split} \tag{14}

4. Consequence for the whole original source

The exact block source proof contracts only the two original leaves and their one positive Newton multiplication gate. It retains i+j≤N−1i+j\le N-1, all phases and the full Hermite data. Internal first derivatives vanish at the complete seed; its output derivative is exactly (3). Using (6) therefore preserves every surrounding wire derivative when the original stationary supporting planes are summed.

Every proper two-leaf seed in the retained C(FG)C(FG), s≥2s\ge2 grammar has 1/2<X<11/2<X<1. Thus (5) applies automatically. Larger proper seeds retain their old positive leaf margins. Every nonempty alias sector loses a positive amount after summing the actual seed and macro-product bounds. The d=N−1d=N-1 shifts cost only O(1)O(1) in the exponent, and fixed trees have polynomially many wire states.

The all-core concave majorant and original far-color core estimate then give the complete physical-color complement gap. The previously primitive lattice, complex packet and marked geometry for this entire grammar are unchanged. Consequently the original filling transfer gives the hyperbolic knot complement volume after all auxiliary fillings and a loop-free knot parity choice, without the individual-leaf cutoff. Isolated C(A2)C(A_2) is still excluded.

The old local-tangent example C(A2ℛ(A4))C(A_2\mathcal R(A_4)) is now covered: its negative individual deficit is compensated by the original multiplication gate. The grammar still is not asserted to represent every arborescent template.
