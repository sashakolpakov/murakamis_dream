The classical crossing relation in the external Rivin coordinates

Private written deduction, 14 November 2026. The external formulas are those of rivin-jones at commit e956fb919ac7d1bbc7cb3d3873fbac993feb94b7, in docs/MATHEMATICS.md and python/rivin_jones/r_matrix.py. Their text was read; no code, numerical calculation, import, or test was executed.

This gives the holomorphic generating function directly from those coefficients, an explicit rational crossing map, and its triangular matrix and Wirtinger interpretation. It keeps the actual highest-weight cut, quantum pivots, writhe factor and ordinary bends in view. The general critical-correspondence-to-representation theorem is already present in the marked braid calculation, Sections 4–6. The contribution here is its direct derivation and coordinate dictionary for the external matrices, not a new proof that a geometric critical component contributes to the original scalar.

7. What has and has not been transported

The exact external-to-Newton adapter reverses crossing signs at ANA_N. All signs in this note are external signs. The adapter and its scalar lines must be applied when comparing the corresponding physical mirror word; the statement (20) does not silently change that convention. The full finite adapter and split-incidence recovery retain all Newton Hermite data. A principal classical symbol by itself does not estimate those normal coefficients.

The earlier positive-chart obstruction concerns simultaneous strict upper-half-plane choices for particular raw Fourier shapes. The present maps live on complex rational charts; they assert neither positivity of those shapes nor an equality of a raw full-function density with the finite polynomial away from its sampling grid. There is no conflict with its two-crossing reciprocal shape relation or its retained seam character.

This explicit character map does not by itself identify 2πRe⁡𝒮total2\pi\operatorname{Re}\mathcal S_{\mathrm{total}} with hyperbolic volume in the chosen orientation. Here the action is normalized by log⁡R/N\log R/N, so its real part governs exponential magnitude. The earlier marked braid and fundamental-cycle results address regulator comparisons in their recorded coordinates. Equation (6c), together with the joint clock theorem’s calibrated primitive, now supplies the local marked-action comparison among the external, physical and incidence coordinates, with the exact adapter and all boundary/framing conventions retained. Transporting a chosen global branch must retain those same lifts and the already specified orientation; equality of group relations alone would not suffice. More decisively, neither a representation nor a regular critical point proves that the prescribed quantum cycle has a nonzero coefficient there. Global cycle transport, endpoint contributions, cancellation and the lower bound needed for arbitrary fixed short partial filling remain separate requirements.
