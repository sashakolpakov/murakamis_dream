Independent Gauss-wall cycle and peripheral-flux calculation

Proof and audit, 27 October 2026. The fixed-meridian physical and exceptional spheres differ by a nonzero integral wall-torus class. This gives an explicit Gysin obstruction, stronger than an obstruction only to section homotopies. The meridian detour transfers the charge to a peripheral logarithm and changes the retained SL2SL_2 lift. The actual quantum wall residues and the inverse-flux character have the stated normalizations below.

This independently checks the meridian wall theorem and the flux/peripheral character. It does not identify a quantum source pushforward or a volume norm.

1. The cut divisor and its closed detecting form are global

On YY, the two charts have V=v−1,η′=−v2η,a′=v−v2a. V=v^{-1},\qquad \eta'=-v^2\eta,\qquad a'=v-v^2a. The fixed-ambient matrix entry F=μ11=1−ηv=1+η′V F=\mu_{11}=1-\eta v=1+\eta'V is one global function. In particular the south expression has a plus sign. Using 1−η′V1-\eta'V would select the other diagonal entry, not another expression for this same divisor. Its zero divisor is D≃𝔾m,v×𝔸a1,η=1/v. D\simeq\mathbb G_{m,v}\times\mathbb A^1_a,\qquad\eta=1/v.

The closed form Ξ=dv∧dηF=dlog⁡F∧dlog⁡v=dV∧dη′F \Xi=\frac{dv\wedge d\eta}{F} =d\log F\wedge d\log v =\frac{dV\wedge d\eta'}{F} \tag{1} is regular everywhere on Y\DY\setminus D; the displayed affine fractions remove its apparent flag-coordinate poles. Its residue is dlog⁡vd\log v.

Put c=2πic=2\pi i, T=vaT=va, and orient the compact source by v=T/(1−T)eiϕ,a=T(1−T)e−iϕ,Ω=dv∧da=−idT∧dϕ. v=\sqrt{T/(1-T)}e^{i\phi},\quad a=\sqrt{T(1-T)}e^{-i\phi},\quad \Omega=dv\wedge da=-i\,dT\wedge d\phi. For the exceptional sphere η=0\eta=0, the pullback of Ξ\Xi is zero. For the physical sphere η=(1−e−cT)/v\eta=(1-e^{-cT})/v, it is cΩc\Omega. Hence ∫ΓphysΞ=−c2=4π2,∫ΓEΞ=0. \int_{\Gamma_{\mathrm{phys}}}\Xi=-c^2=4\pi^2,\qquad \int_{\Gamma_E}\Xi=0. \tag{2} This already excludes any homology between them in Y\DY\setminus D, without requiring a proposed homotopy to preserve its flag coordinates.

2. Integral Gysin class and its orientation

The radial three-chain η=ληphys\eta=\lambda\eta_{\mathrm{phys}} has F=1−λ+λe−cT. F=1-\lambda+\lambda e^{-cT}. It meets DD once in the transverse (λ,T)(\lambda,T) plane, at λ=T=1/2\lambda=T=1/2, along the circle γv:v=eiϕ,a=1/(2v),η=1/v. \gamma_v:\quad v=e^{i\phi},\quad a=1/(2v),\quad\eta=1/v. Near it, F=−2(λ−1/2)+πi(T−1/2)+higher terms. F=-2(\lambda-1/2)+\pi i(T-1/2)+\text{higher terms}. Thus the complex-normal orientation is opposite the (λ,T)(\lambda,T) orientation, and the oriented intersection is −γv-\gamma_v. Define the tube by a positive complex-normal circle followed by the positive vv-circle. Excision gives [Γphys]−[ΓE]=−Tube⁡[γv]. [\Gamma_{\mathrm{phys}}]-[\Gamma_E]=-\operatorname{Tube}[\gamma_v]. \tag{3} Its period checks the sign: ∫Tube⁡γvΞ=(2πi)∫γvdlog⁡v=c2. \int_{\operatorname{Tube}\gamma_v}\Xi =(2\pi i)\int_{\gamma_v}d\log v=c^2.

This tube is a generator, rather than a possible torsion correction. The affine-bundle projection Y→ℙ1Y\to\mathbb P^1 has contractible two-dimensional fibres. A continuous section and affine fibre contraction give a deformation to ΓE\Gamma_E. Hence H3(Y,ℤ)=0H_3(Y,\mathbb Z)=0, H2(Y,ℤ)=ℤH_2(Y,\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z. A normal neighborhood of DD has the explicit normal coordinate FF, and the relative homology shifts that of D≃ℂ*×ℂD\simeq\mathbb C^*\times\mathbb C by two. Consequently H2(Y\D,ℤ)≃ℤ2H_2(Y\setminus D,\mathbb Z)\simeq\mathbb Z^2, split by ΓE\Gamma_E, and (3) is its nonzero wall component.

3. Meridian variation transfers the charge

For a retained eigenvalue mm, the resolved meridian is μ=(m−ηvη(m−m−1)v−ηv2m−1+ηv),η′=(m−m−1)v−v2η. \mu=\begin{pmatrix} m-\eta v&\eta\\ (m-m^{-1})v-\eta v^2&m^{-1}+\eta v \end{pmatrix}, \quad \eta'=(m-m^{-1})v-v^2\eta. \tag{4} It has determinant one and the stated eigenflag. The global cut function is F=m−ηv=m−1+η′VF=m-\eta v=m^{-1}+\eta'V. The explicit path mλ=ecλ/2,ηλ=mλ(1−e−cλT)/v m_\lambda=e^{c\lambda/2},\quad \eta_\lambda= m_\lambda(1-e^{-c\lambda T})/v has nonzero F=mλe−cλTF=m_\lambda e^{-c\lambda T} and a removable south expression mλ−1(ecλT′−1)/Vm_\lambda^{-1}(e^{c\lambda T'}-1)/V. The sign is necessary for this once-winding clock if the pole clutches remain specified. The local regular varying-meridian forms differ by ΞS−ΞN=2dlog⁡m∧dlog⁡v=d(2log⁡mdlog⁡v). \Xi_S-\Xi_N=2d\log m\wedge d\log v =d(2\log m\,d\log v). Their compact period is I=−2clog⁡m=2clog⁡M,M=m−1.
