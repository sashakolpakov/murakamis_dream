An exact dual clock lift to the centralizer normal co-source

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. Every actual finite cut clock has an explicit polynomial centralizer-normal co-source, valid on ALL matrices. On the physical scaled clock this polynomial converges locally uniformly to a whole exponential, with uniform factorial coefficient bounds. Its derivatives transport every bounded normalized cut-position moment exactly.

The lift is contravariant: it represents a trace FUNCTIONAL. It is not a multiplicative symbol map, a crossing intertwiner, or an internal Gaussian source comparison.

1. The dual Toeplitz map of the actual compact source

Put n=N−1n=N-1. In the source coordinates T=vaT=va, the actual coherent matrix symbol is fC(v,T)=∑i,j=0nCij(nj)vi−jTj(1−T)n−j. f_C(v,T)=\sum_{i,j=0}^n C_{ij}\binom nj v^{i-j}T^j(1-T)^{n-j}. Let ωI=−Ω0/(2πi)\omega_I=-\Omega_0/(2\pi i), whose marked compact period is one. Angular integration and the beta integral give, for every polynomial PP, ∫ΓP(−T)fCωI=1NTr⁡(𝒯N(P)C), \int_\Gamma P(-T)f_C\,\omega_I =\frac1N\operatorname{Tr}(\mathcal T_N(P)C), \tag{1} where the diagonal Toeplitz row is [𝒯N(bh)]j=(−1)h(j+1)h¯(N+1)h¯,h≥0,0≤j<N. [\mathcal T_N(b^h)]_j =(-1)^h\frac{(j+1)^{\overline h}} {(N+1)^{\overline h}}, \qquad h\ge0,\quad 0\le j<N. \tag{2} A bar denotes the rising factorial. Formula (2) holds at ALL polynomial degrees, not only below NN.

On polynomials of degree at most nn, 𝒯N\mathcal T_N is an isomorphism onto the diagonal matrices: its h-th basis column is a polynomial in j of degree h with nonzero leading coefficient, evaluated at the N distinct integer points. This is different from the covariant symbol quantization bh↦(−1)h(J)h/(n)hb^h\mapsto(-1)^h(J)_h/(n)_h.

The all-matrix pairing here is the normalized ORDINARY TRACE. On the actual position-conserving weighted-word and Green cut arrays it agrees with the original average cut because those arrays are diagonal. It is not the original average functional Φ(C)=N−1∑i,jCij\Phi(C)=N^{-1}\sum_{i,j}C_{ij} on arbitrary off-diagonal Ward matrices. Any such Hopf-current comparison must retain and transport that original coupon separately. The trace extension is stated explicitly rather than identified with all possible actual co-sources.

2. Exact inversion for the WHOLE clock

For any complex ww, define the polynomial of degree at most n PN,w∨(b)=∑h=0naN,h(w)bh, P_{N,w}^{\vee}(b)=\sum_{h=0}^n a_{N,h}(w)b^h, aN,h(w)=(N+1)h¯h!∑r=hn(−1)r(rh)(w−1)r. a_{N,h}(w)= \frac{(N+1)^{\overline h}}{h!} \sum_{r=h}^n(-1)^r\binom rh(w-1)^r . \tag{3} Then exactly 𝒯N(PN,w∨)=wJ,Jej=jej. \mathcal T_N(P_{N,w}^{\vee})=w^J , \qquad Je_j=je_j. \tag{4} Thus for EVERY matrix C, 1NTr⁡(wJC)=∫ΓPN,w∨(−T)fCωI. \frac1N\operatorname{Tr}(w^JC) =\int_\Gamma P_{N,w}^{\vee}(-T)f_C\,\omega_I . \tag{5}

Here is a direct proof. The polynomial identity (j)r=∑h=0r(−1)r−h(rh)r!h!(j+1)h¯ (j)_r=\sum_{h=0}^r(-1)^{r-h}\binom rh \frac{r!}{h!}(j+1)^{\overline h} \tag{6} follows, for example, by comparing coefficients in (1+δ)j(1+\delta)^j: sum the right side over r with weight δr/r!\delta^r/r!, use ∑r≥h(−1)r−h(rh)δr=δh/(1+δ)h+1\sum_{r\ge h}(-1)^{r-h}\binom rh\delta^r =\delta^h/(1+\delta)^{h+1}, and then the rising-binomial series. Useful exact endpoint formulas are PN,w∨(0)=∑r=0N−1(1−w)r,PN,w∨(−1)=wN−(w−1)N. P_{N,w}^{\vee}(0)=\sum_{r=0}^{N-1}(1-w)^r,\qquad P_{N,w}^{\vee}(-1)=w^N-(w-1)^N. \tag{7} These are not the actual clock eigenvalues at j=0,n. Confusing dual polynomial evaluation with a matrix endpoint would lose finite endpoint corrections.

3. Scaled clocks, moments, and uniform whole limits

Put w=ez/Nw=e^{z/N}. On every compact complex z-set, |w−1|≤C/N|w-1|\le C/N. The bounded moment has a stronger finite normal-order statement. Put GN,r(b)=∂zrPN,ez/N∨(b)|z=0. G_{N,r}(b)=\left.\partial_z^r P_{N,e^{z/N}}^\vee(b)\right|_{z=0}. 4. Normal residue and global affine clutch

Use the normal centralizer coordinate b=−a/ηb=-a/\eta and $$ \nu_{\rm cut}=-\frac{d\eta}{\eta}\wedge \frac{\Omega_0}{1-\eta v}. $$ For every h, expansion of the geometric denominator gives $$ \operatorname{Res}_{E}(b^h\nu_{\rm cut}) =-(-T)^h\Omega_0 . $$ Linearity, (3), and (5) yield the exact source pairing $$ \frac1N\operatorname{Tr}(w^JC) =\frac1{2\pi i}\int_\Gamma \operatorname{Res}_{E} \left(P_{N,w}^\vee(b)f_C\nu_{\rm cut}\right). \tag{13} $$ This is a SOURCE-KERNEL lift; it is not asserted to be the internal Gaussian image of that kernel.

The north and south torsor coordinates obey bS=bN+1vη,bS−1−bN=1−ηvvη. b_S=b_N+\frac1{v\eta},\qquad b_S-1-b_N=\frac{1-\eta v}{v\eta}. Use P(bN)P(b_N) north and P(bS−1)P(b_S-1) south. Their difference is divisible by the Gauss-wall factor. After multiplication by $\nu_{\rm cut}$ the Gauss-wall pole cancels, leaving only η−rdη∧Ω0\eta^{-r}d\eta\wedge\Omega_0 with r≥2r\ge2. Each has an explicit normal primitive and zero E-residue. Thus (13) retains a compatible affine Cech moment marking. Bare P(bS)P(b_S) would have the wrong residue polynomial.

At finite N the normal pole degree can reach N, since deg⁡P∨=N−1\deg P^\vee=N-1 and $\nu_{\rm cut}$ already has a simple normal pole. It must not be replaced by a bounded normal jet. After taking the explicit residue formula, (8)–(9) do allow locally uniform convergence of the whole residue to its exponential source form. No algebraicity of that limiting horizontal exponential is claimed.

5. Exact finite cut-current endpoints

For an arbitrary zero-extended finite current JjJ_j, set $\lambda_{N,w}^{\rm head}(J)=N^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}w^jJ_j$. For w≠0w\ne0, direct summation by parts gives $$ \lambda_{N,w}^{\rm head}((S-1)J) =(w^{-1}-1)\lambda_{N,w}^{\rm head}(J) +\frac{w^{N-1}J_N-w^{-1}J_0}{N}. \tag{14} $$ Including both actual tails gives the full finite row N−1∑j∈ℤwjJjN^{-1}\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}w^jJ_j, for which the endpoint terms telescope and the factor is exactly w−1−1w^{-1}-1. Its z-derivatives retain every normalized position-moment endpoint.

The original raw Green arrays already contain 1/N1/N. Equations (5), (13), and (14) use matrix-normalized arrays; $I_{\rm mat}=NI_{\rm raw}$, $J_{\rm mat}=NJ_{\rm raw}$. Equivalently use N times these rows on raw arrays. No factor N is absorbed into an endpoint.

6. Whole word composition is exact; Gaussian naturality is additional

An actual transfer clock can be split into literal input/output position clocks. For the recorded checked R conventions, R+:(i,j)↦(j+k,i−k),R−:(i,j)↦(j−k,i+k), R_+:(i,j)\mapsto(j+k,i-k),\qquad R_-:(i,j)\mapsto(j-k,i+k), so wkR+=(I⊗w−J)R+(wJ⊗I),wkR−=(I⊗wJ)R−(w−J⊗I). w^kR_+=(I\otimes w^{-J})R_+(w^J\otimes I),\qquad w^kR_-=(I\otimes w^J)R_-(w^{-J}\otimes I). \tag{15} These are exact coefficient identities with the same Cartan and pivot lines. Products and tensor products use the actual operator composition, or equivalently the exact finite coherent STAR product. Thus all weighted words and their coupling derivatives retain their source meaning.

Pointwise multiplication is not that star product. Already at N=2 the actual inverse clocks obey Tr⁡(wJw−J)/2=1\operatorname{Tr}(w^Jw^{-J})/2=1, whereas ∫01[1+(w−1)T][1+(w−1−1)T]dT=1+w+w−1−26. \int_0^1[1+(w-1)T][1+(w^{-1}-1)T]\,dT =1+\frac{w+w^{-1}-2}{6}. At the actual root clock w=-1 this is 1/3, not one. The DUAL pairing (5) gives one exactly.

Moving a dual clock through an actual crossing therefore requires the transpose of its finite star action. A scalar polynomial in b represents diagonal co-sources only; after an off-diagonal crossing one generally needs the full angular/coherent dual source as well. The normal residue is not a multiplicative operator-algebra map.

Consequently this theorem supplies the actual whole CUT clock and all its normalized moments, including tails and affine clutch. The independent second framing coordinate still needs its network map. Nor is the source-dependent transverse Gaussian pushforward proved natural for this contravariant star action. These are the specific remaining couplings, rather than a defect in the finite clock formula.

7. Why the raw framing moments are not the finite critical field

For the actual scalar closed cut C=FNIC=F_NI, the polynomial normal-centralizer moments from (1) normalize to μ(bh)=(−1)h/(h+1). \mu(b^h)=(-1)^h/(h+1). Their Hankel matrices are signed Hilbert matrices, positive definite at EVERY size: μ(pp¯)=∫01|p(−T)|2dT>0\mu(p\overline p)=\int_0^1|p(-T)|^2dT>0 for nonzero p. Hence no nonzero polynomial ideal is annihilated. This raw framing co-source cannot factor through a fixed finite algebra in b, even for the actual figure-eight.

This does not contradict a finite algebra of ISOLATED TRANSVERSE geometric critical points. The framing variable is meant to be integrated first, or retained in a relative/twisted complex. It does rule out defining that critical observable as the raw Toeplitz image of b: 𝒯N\mathcal T_N is not multiplicative. A finite list of moments reconstructs a density only AFTER its critical polynomial relation has actually been supplied.
