Joint saddle and global gap of the actual trivalent scalar

29 October 2026. The historical filename is retained, but the actual scalar identification is now PROVED by the independent finite Euler-to-Racah transform. This note proves its joint asymptotic and extremal geometry, including the required nonconstant source normalizer and explicit source phase. These are actual black-SCALAR estimates; full magnetic tensors, white/marked sewing and graph geometry remain separate.

1. Exact finite partition and symmetric reflection

Let N>=2, d=N-1, s_a=product_(r=1)^a 2sin(pi r/N). For integer 0<=j_i<=floor(d/2), put J=j_1+j_2+j_3. The positive partition is

R_N(j)=N sum_(max j_i<=t<=min pair sums)
  s_t/[prod_i s_(t-j_i) s_(d+t-J)
                            prod_i s_(J-j_i-t)]. (1)

An empty range means zero. All indices in a nonempty range lie in 0,…,d: in particular J-t<=min pair sums<=d. The exact original sine-factorial reflection s_a s_(d-a)=N rewrites (1) as

R_N(j)=sum_t s_t s_(J-t)
              /[prod_i s_(t-j_i)s_(J-j_i-t)].     (2)

Every term is positive and (2) is EXACTLY invariant under t->J-t. It works at both level parities, including all endpoint indices.

2. Unique joint saddle

Write L(x)=integral_0^x log(2sin pi u)du, a_i=j_i/N, S=sum a_i, and use z=t/N as the summation variable. The uniform physical sine-factorial error O(log N) gives the limiting summand phase

E(z,a)=L(z)+L(S-z)
          -sum_i[L(z-a_i)+L(S-a_i-z)].           (3)

The support is max a_i<=z<=S-max a_i. In its nondegenerate interior p_i=z-a_i and q_i=S-a_i-z lie in (0,1/2); each p_i<=z and each q_i<=S-z. Consequently

E_zz=pi[(cot(pi z)-sum cot(pi p_i))
                +(cot(pi(S-z))-sum cot(pi q_i))]<0. (4)

For either parent angle, a nonnegative parent cotangent is smaller than each of its three child cotangents; a negative parent is already smaller than their positive sum. This proves (4), not just its value at a stationary point. Symmetry in (2) therefore gives the UNIQUE saddle z_*=S/2. The one-sided phase derivatives are respectively +infinity and -infinity at nondegenerate support endpoints.

Put

r_i=S/2-a_i, i=1,2,3; r_4=1-S/2.

For admissible triangles r_i>=0, r_4>=1/4 and sum r_i=1. The exact candidate rate is

Phi(a)=2[L(S/2)-sum_(i=1)^3 L(r_i)]
       =(2/pi)sum_(i=1)^4 Lambda(pi r_i).         (5)

The uniform O(log N) factorial estimate and at most N summands show log R_N=N Phi(a)+O(log N) uniformly on ADMISSIBLE physical triples with nonempty summation range; nonadmissible triples have R_N=0. For a lower bound choose a nearest physical t to J/2. This includes degenerate triangles by the same uniform factorial estimate and continuity.

3. Strict global maximum and all-color gap

The function sum_(i=1)^4 Lambda(theta_i) is STRICTLY concave on theta_i>0, sum theta_i=pi. Its negative Hessian on that tangent space is sum cot(theta_i)v_i^2. If all angles<=pi/2, positivity is immediate. Otherwise let theta_4>pi/2, so A=sum_(i<=3)theta_i<pi/2. Weighted Cauchy and sum_(i<=3)tan theta_i<tan A give

sum_(i<=3)cot(theta_i)v_i^2
   >=v_4^2/[sum_(i<=3)tan theta_i]>cot(A)v_4^2
                                          =-cot(theta_4)v_4^2.

The case v_4=0 is strictly positive unless all v_i=0. This proves strict concavity throughout the simplex. Symmetry then fixes its unique maximum at all theta_i=pi/4. Boundary continuity and the strict line inequality exclude a second boundary maximum.

It follows that (5) has unique maximum

a_1=a_2=a_3=1/2,
Phi_max=8 Lambda(pi/4)/pi=v8/pi=:j0.              (6)

Thus R_N<=poly(N)exp(Nj0). For EVERY delta>0 there is gamma_delta>0 such that a physical admissible triple with max_i(1/2-a_i)>=delta has R_N<=poly(N)exp[N(j0-gamma_delta)]. This is an ENTIRE triple color gap, rather than three separately maximized envelopes. No angle-edge gluing of a full graph has been inferred from (5).

4. Exact central complex packet and its amplitude

Allow both parities by writing m=d/2, which can be a half-integer, and j_i=m-N sigma_i. Physical j_i are still integers. Around sigma=0 fix a summation window I_N near t/N=3/4. With S_hat_N the canonical sine-factorial completion, define the EXACT function

Rwin_N(sigma)=sum_(t in I_N)
   S_hat_N(t)S_hat_N(sum j_i-t)
   /[prod_i S_hat_N(t-j_i)S_hat_N(sum j_i-j_i-t)]. (7)

All scaled arguments lie in compact zero-free bulk strips. On a fixed small complex sigma polydisk it is holomorphic. At physical sigma it is literally the retained part of (2); all other terms have a strict exponential gap uniform in a smaller physical corridor. Equation (7) does not choose a moving-q normal germ.

For the analytic limiting phase use a_i^0=1/2-sigma_i, R_0=sum a_i^0/2 and r_i=R_0-a_i^0, r_4=1-R_0 in (5). The saddle is still z=R_0, up to its actual half-step shift. Put D=sum_(i<=3)cot(pi r_i)-cot(pi R_0)>0. The negative Hessian is 2pi D. The compact sine-factorial expansion in (7), before its half-step shift is included, has saddle prefactor

sin(pi R_0)/[4N^2 prod sin(pi r_i)].

The actual j_i=N a_i^0-1/2 changes each r_i by -1/(4N) and R_0 by -3/(4N). Its exponential constant is therefore exp[-3G(R_0)/2+sum_i G(r_i)/2], G=L’. Combining these and the Gaussian integral gives

Rwin_N(sigma)=N^(-3/2)exp[N Phi(sigma)]
    /[4sqrt(prod_i cos(pi sigma_i))] *[1+O(1/N)]. (8)

The useful elementary identity is

sin(pi R_0)prod_i sin(pi r_i) D
   =prod_(i<k)sin(pi(r_i+r_k))
   =prod_i sin(pi a_i^0).

It proves the amplitude in (8), including its factor 1/4. The square root is +1 at zero. This also proves the amplitude for even physical nearest-center labels, whose sigma_i are half-integral multiples of 1/N; there is no guessed parity factor.

For complex uniformity apply the exact pi N cot(pi N z) rectangle to the fixed physical t window, with half-grid endpoints. On its vertical sides |cot|<=1 and the endpoint phase has a strict gap. On horizontal sides the error is exp(-2pi N b), while phase growth is <=Cb^2+C||sigma||b. Choose b first small, then shrink the complex polydisk. Keep the endpoint connectors, which remain gapped. Deform only the main integral to its analytic saddle. The canonical factorial remainders are holomorphic O(1/N) on a larger tube, so ordinary analytic Gaussian integration proves (8) for the EXACT packet, with every fixed derivative controlled by Cauchy estimates.

Its local action is explicitly

Phi(sigma)=j0-pi sum sigma_i^2
                     +2pi^2 sigma_1 sigma_2 sigma_3+O(||sigma||^4). (9)

Indeed the four r_i are 1/4 plus the four tetrahedral sign rows (+–),(-+-),(–+),(+++) applied to sigma/2. Their squared changes sum to sum sigma_i^2, and their cubed changes sum to 3sigma_1sigma_2sigma_3. Thus the exact complex window obeys a real-coercive bound poly(N)exp[N(j0-c||Re sigma||^2+C||Im sigma||^2)]. In particular the JOINT action is not separately even, although each one-leg restriction is even. Internal pair-Casimir labels are not thereby identified with ordinary auxiliary colors or peripheral meridians.

5. The actual source normalizer and its nonconstant action

and zero when J<d. The root-Fourier source theorem proves the zero branch; the finite Euler/Sears bridge proves the positive branch, including its displayed phase and all label orders. Whenever a permitted j_i is incremented,

C_N(j+e_i)/C_N(j)
   =sin[pi(j_i+1)/N]/sin[pi(delta+1)/N]>=1,

since delta<=j_i and both arguments are on the increasing half of the sine curve. Thus its maximum is 1 at the odd central triple, and cos(pi/N) at the even nearest-central triple (when this domain is nonempty). Consequently the ACTUAL scalar has the same upper envelope and strict macroscopic color gap as (6), unconditionally on the scalar level.

The exact near-central factorial completion prod_i S_hat_N(m-Nsigma_i)/[N S_hat_N(m-Nsum sigma_i)] has the uniform bulk asymptotic exp[N Gamma(sigma)](1+O(1/N)), with prefactor ONE: each half-shifted sine factorial contributes sqrt(N), and their powers cancel.
