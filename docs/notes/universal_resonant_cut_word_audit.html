Independent word/source audit of the universal first-slot cut

27 October 2026. Proof pass of the universal resonant-cut theorem. The inverse-pair insertion is an actual source-preserving presentation change. On the established regular geometric critical branch its added crossings have a nonempty regular chart and preserve a nonzero normal block. This is not a claim about the contribution of the resonant node to the final selected finite sum.

1. The chosen meridian really moves left unchanged

At the complete faithful discrete representation all positively oriented meridians are conjugate to J. If such a conjugate fixes infinity, its conjugating group element also fixes infinity, since J has only that fixed point. An upper triangular group word has diagonal ratio one: a ratio of absolute value different from one contracts conjugates of J toward the identity; an infinite-order unit ratio gives nontrivial such commutators approaching identity; a nontrivial finite-order ratio gives projective torsion. These contradict discreteness/torsion-freeness. Hence that conjugator commutes with J and the meridian is exactly J, not a scaled translation.

The first nonupper initial meridian U therefore has only J’s before it. Negative Hurwitz moves (J,U) to (U,UJU inverse), so U passes left UNCHANGED at every step. The already crossed strands behind it do not change the prefixes before its next move. This proves the stated finite action word and V=I at the FIRST physical slot. The sign sigma=-1 is the first PREFIX index, while the physical slot for the endpoint half-character is ell=0. These are distinct index conventions with the correctly stated different values.

These meridians describe the q-to-one geometric critical representation. They are not the identity Frobenius-central meridians at the actual fixed-root augmentation. No center-deformation identification is needed or implied by this presentation change.

2. The exact finite tensor and source are unchanged

In action order insert the chosen braid P followed by P inverse before the old word. In matrix order the closed operator is R_old R_P inverse R_P=R_old. Opening the new edge gives the literal coupon tensor ptr[R_old R_P inverse E_bd^(0) R_P]. Summing E_bb restores that exact identity. This leaves the first input/output source and all original pivotal caps in their original places. There is no new pivot, color, trace normalization or source vector.

The writhe and strand number are unchanged. The full twist, half-Cartan scalar lines and d_N(M) must be kept in both R_P and its inverse; their common factors cancel as a matrix identity, not as separately evaluated numerical norm values. The specified whole finite inverse exists on the auxiliary q/M lift as well, so the same presentation identity preserves its normal jets. Topological invariance of an unrelated off-root lift is unnecessary.

The internal cut is at ell=0. The exact half-character q^(-ell nu) is therefore one, while the actual longitude insertion, gamma_N and complete peripheral ratio still remain. In particular opening BETWEEN inverse matrices prevents canceling their inserted path weights before the second edge is reclosed. The new partial array is a real changed partial family with the same final state.

3. Admissibility is verified on the actual coefficient atoms

Write U=I+c[-beta;1] * (1,beta), c nonzero, and t=r+beta s. At the step with h left J’s put

ah=1+hs,bh=ah+(c/s)(1−t)(t+hs). a_h=1+hs,\qquad b_h=a_h+(c/s)(1-t)(t+hs).

The four physical positions and negative transfer follow by the actual triangular prefix reconstruction, yielding exactly the five atoms in the main theorem. Their differences from one are controlled by the displayed finite list of factors. In particular

C−1=−c(1−t)2/(bh+1ah),bh+1−bh=s+c(1−t), C-1=-c(1-t)^2/(b_{h+1}a_h),\quad b_{h+1}-b_h=s+c(1-t), bh−ah=(c/s)(1−t)(t+hs). b_h-a_h=(c/s)(1-t)(t+hs).

All are nonzero rational functions on the two-dimensional framing torsor; for example b_h has nonzero quadratic r coefficient -c/s. Their finite product is nonzero in its function field. The common regular open therefore is nonempty and intersects the old regular geometric framing open. Inverse positive atoms are permutations/ inversions of the same regular atoms, so they introduce no new identical-zero condition. This is stronger than just assuming that an arbitrary Reidemeister-II insertion has a regular shape chart.

The first/second physical position pair can fail at its ordinary projection caustic. This does not invalidate the Fourier block: on this regular chart p=1-1/t has p_r=1/t squared, so the pair (A_cut,p) is etale. The bordered-Hessian theorem needs that pair, not an everywhere invertible old two-position projection.

4. The inverse insertion is a genuine Morse stabilization

The actual crossing generating phase has a UNIT mixed Hessian on the regular atom chart, by the previously proved generating-family calculation. Fix its conserved total position and denote that phase by F(y,q), with y the intermediate first output and q the input. The inverse phase is -F(y,q+delta), with its actual inverse marks; any recorded base phase is retained. The surrounding old network has its ordinary open extension S_open(z,delta), with S_open(z,0)=S_old(z). Thus the enlarged local phase is

F(y,q)−F(y,q+δ)+Sopen(z,δ). F(y,q)-F(y,q+\delta)+S_{open}(z,\delta). \tag{1}

At delta=0 the y equation is automatic. The delta equation fixes y uniquely by the old momentum because F_yq is a unit. Its (y,delta) Hessian is

(0−Fyq−Fyq*),det⁡=−Fyq2≠0. \begin{pmatrix}0&-F_{yq}\\-F_{yq}&*\end{pmatrix}, \qquad\det=-F_{yq}^2\ne0. \tag{2}

The reduced critical phase is precisely S_old. Schur elimination therefore makes the enlarged Hessian congruent to its old Hessian plus (2). The original two-dimensional framing kernel is unchanged; it is not a newly assumed Gaussian complement. The intermediate position and net displacement are integer coordinates with determinant plus or minus one in the two added transfers, so this stabilization does not introduce a lattice factor or fractional cover.

For a longer P the inverse pairs cancel successively from the middle. The common regular chart from Section 3 allows this induction. Equivalently the exact canonical graph followed by its inverse eliminates uniquely over local Artin rings. The critical scheme remains the original framed representation germ. This proves the Morse–Bott and nonzero normal-block assertion in the main theorem. It does not assert equality of every formal coefficient in arbitrary Gaussian gauges. All inverse flattenings, density/base lines and fixed phases must still be transported.

The positive statement is on the existing REGULAR geometric branch. It does not manufacture a regular branch for an arbitrary originally degenerate diagram or for unrelated representation components.

5. Universal pole and its precise source limitation

The raw actual first-slot conjugation gives p=1-1/(r+beta s), r=1/(1-p)-beta s, and

Ωcut=−ps(1−p)2dlog⁡Acut∧dlog⁡p. \Omega_{cut}=-\frac p{s(1-p)^2} d\log A_{cut}\wedge d\log p.

The orientation agrees directly with Omega_cut=-dr wedge ds/ [s(1+s)]. The pole is exactly p=1, not a rescaling chosen after evaluating the state. The finite regular-node sum equals (N squared minus one)/(12s), with the main theorem’s sign.

This normalization places the escaping point at EVERY level’s pointed Fourier node. It does not remove the resonance. The actual finite tensor is regular there, whereas the principal Gaussian density is not. Its full source, the nonuniform boundary layer and all higher shape-chart exclusions are still required. The exact presentation change and persistent nonzero partial block do not prove that this block survives the final selected source.

Appendix: a checked first-slot meridian germ, with a split density pole

This appendix does not extend the main complete Gaussian/source theorem to generic M. It computes the raw physical momentum and the explicitly defined invariant form in the regular-centralizer chart, Section 2. Its normalization is A_cut=M/(M+s), Delta=s+(M-M inverse)r and Omega=-dr wedge ds/[Delta(M+s)].

The actual balanced symbol in the marked braid note has x=-M, e=(k-M inverse)p and physical mu=-G. Hence at the first slot p=-(F_M inverse U F_M)_12/(k-M inverse); its minus sign is fixed, not inferred by continuity. Let kappa=M-M inverse and

U=(M−cβκβ−cβ2cM−1+cβ),FM=(r1−rs−κ−s). U=\begin{pmatrix}M-c\beta&\kappa\beta-c\beta^2\\ c&M^{-1}+c\beta\end{pmatrix},\quad F_M=\begin{pmatrix}r&1-r\\s&-\kappa-s\end{pmatrix}.

Then D=U-M inverse I has rank one. Direct matrix calculation gives

p=1−1−κβr+βs,r=1−κβ1−p−βs, p=1-\frac{1-\kappa\beta}{r+\beta s},\qquad r=\frac{1-\kappa\beta}{1-p}-\beta s, \tag{3}

on the regular chart, and for THAT invariant form

Ω=−p(1−p)[s(1−p)+κ]dlog⁡Acut∧dlog⁡p. \Omega=-\frac p{(1-p)[s(1-p)+\kappa]} d\log A_{cut}\wedge d\log p. \tag{4}

The momentum Möbius pole stays p=1, but the density has two simple poles, p=1 and p=1+kappa/s. They coalesce to the main theorem’s double pole at M=1. Thus even this special first-slot calculation does not justify retaining a generic single-double- pole filter. An additional Gaussian-density/source comparison, if needed away from completeness, has not been supplied by (3)-(4).

The exact symbolic check and its output verify the raw conjugated momentum, complete bubble atoms and the density’s full meridian dependence. The root main theorem’s actual R/source and Hessian tests are linked from that theorem.
