From the selected source volume to hyperbolic volume

29 October 2026. The task here starts AFTER an original-source theorem has proved

lim_N (2pi/N) log|K_N| = V_src.

It is to prove that this particular V_src is the hyperbolic volume of the actual complement. The argument below isolates a finite classical certificate for that equality. It does not assume that the displayed quantum limit has been proved for arbitrary knots.

The existing filled-source proof already supplies this certificate for its selected long-filling branch. The independent tangent-space audit checks the distinction between a source saddle and a critical point on the FULL angle domain. Sections 2–3 below give an elementary proof of the complete-cusp Casson–Rivin implication, including completeness.

1. The exact certificate

Let T be an actual oriented ideal triangulation of a manifold with torus ends. Write theta for its three opposite-edge-pair angles in each tetrahedron. Let A impose the tetrahedral sums and E count the local edge occurrences at each global edge, with multiplicities. The positive angle domain is

P={theta>0: A theta=pi*1, E theta=2pi*1}.

Put

V_T(theta)=sum_i Lambda(theta_i),
Lambda(theta)=-integral_0^theta log(2sin t)dt.

For a complete cusped complement the certificate consists of:

  1. A specified theta_src in P, on the actual triangulation and with every tetrahedron positive.

  2. The absolutely normalized equality V_src=V_T(theta_src).

  3. Full constrained stationarity:

    grad V_T(theta_src) in rowspace(A,E).          (1)

Equivalently, one exhibits real vectors mu,eta such that for each tetrahedral opposite edge pair i=(e,e’),

-log(2sin theta_src,i)=mu_t+eta_e+eta_e'.          (2)

The conclusion is

V_src=max_(closure P) V_T=Vol(M).                (3)

Row dependencies cause no problem: (1) is precisely annihilation of ker(A,E). A constrained source saddle proves (1) only if all omitted angle directions have also been accounted for. Section 4 explains two ways to do this without requiring the source variables themselves to parametrize every angle variation.

For a filled manifold replace P by the actual filled-slope angle slice, and retain its primitive angular lifts and its end-completion certificate; see section 5. Imposing the unfilled boundary conditions would compute a different volume.

2. Strict concavity proves the maximum, including the boundary

For one tetrahedron put beta=pi-alpha-gamma. Its negative volume Hessian, in independent coordinates alpha,gamma, is

H=[[cot alpha+cot beta, cot beta],
   [cot beta, cot gamma+cot beta]].              (4)

The first diagonal entry is sin gamma/(sin alpha sin beta)>0 and det H=1. Thus H is positive definite throughout the positive simplex. The sum over tetrahedra is strictly concave on every affine slice.

If theta_src is an interior stationary point, follow the straight segment to any distinct point theta of the closure. This segment is positive except possibly at its final endpoint. Its volume derivative starts at zero and its second derivative is strictly negative. The continuous extension of Lambda to [0,pi] therefore gives

V_T(theta)<V_T(theta_src)  for theta!=theta_src.  (5)

The feasible domain is convex; V_T is concave. The convex function is -V_T. Neither nonemptiness of P nor strict concavity alone guarantees that its maximum lies in its interior. Positivity of theta_src in the certificate supplies the needed interior point directly.

3. Why the complete-cusp critical point is a complete metric

This proves the geometric implication rather than merely naming the maximum a volume. It uses (2), not a presumed complete representation.

Assign one real number to each global edge:

l_e=-2 eta_e.                                    (6)

An ideal tetrahedron admits horospherical decorations whose six signed truncated edge lengths are exactly these numbers. Here is the explicit compatibility check. For any decorated ideal tetrahedron, the three opposite-pair length sums have differences

(l_e+l_e')-(l_f+l_f')
              =2log(sin theta_i/sin theta_j).   (7)

For example normalize its vertices to infinity,0,1,z. If the three finite horospheres have Euclidean diameters d_0,d_1,d_z and the horosphere at infinity has height H, the six lengths are

l_(infinity,a)=log(H/d_a),
l_(a,b)=log(|a-b|^2/(d_a d_b)).

The opposite sums are a common constant plus respectively 2log|1-z|, 2log|z|, 0. The sine rule proves (7) in the corresponding angle order. Equation (2) shows that the target lengths (6) have exactly these differences.

Conversely those two independent differences are the ONLY restrictions. Changing the four horospheres adds r_v+r_w to the length of edge vw. A six-vector delta is of this form precisely when

delta_01+delta_23=delta_02+delta_13
                              =delta_03+delta_12.

For instance r_0=(delta_01+delta_02-delta_12)/2 determines the other three, and the displayed conditions check the remaining edges. Thus each tetrahedron has the required decoration.

Glue the ideal faces according to T. The unique face isometry with the prescribed vertex matching preserves its three horocycles: the signed lengths on the three sides agree. Indeed a change of the three horocycles preserving all three side lengths would have r_0+r_1=r_1+r_2=r_2+r_0=0, hence all r_i=0.

These decorations therefore match across EVERY face. Around an edge there is no residual dilation, since the horospheres at its ends return to themselves. Its total positive dihedral angle is exactly 2pi by E theta=2pi. Thus the edge has an ordinary hyperbolic neighborhood, with neither a cone angle nor a translation defect. There are no finite vertices to check.

First shrink the compatible horospheres, by one common factor for each cusp, until they cut actual triangular corners and the finitely many corner neighborhoods are disjoint. Their matched triangles then form a nonsingular Euclidean metric on each compact link torus. It is complete; its universal development is the whole Euclidean plane. These corner neighborhoods give full complete horoball ends. The remaining core is compact. Hence the glued metric is complete, of curvature -1 and finite volume, on the ACTUAL M. Its volume is the sum of its tetrahedral volumes, V_T(theta_src). This proves (3). To compare with a separately specified complete metric, one can also apply the two marked representation-volume inequalities in opposite directions, as in the existing geometric equality proof.

No universal existence theorem for positive ideal triangulations is used in this implication. An actual positive theta_src is an input.

4. Eliminating internal variables is legitimate when they are optimized

There are two sufficient ways to verify the full stationarity in (1).

Reduced optimization. Choose affine coordinates (y,x) on the allowed angle domain, with y internal and x the retained source variables. Suppose the internal solution y(x) is an interior critical point in EVERY y direction, and define

W(x)=V_T(y(x),x).

Strict concavity makes each such internal point the unique fiber maximum. Its internal Hessian is invertible, so the implicit function theorem gives a smooth local y(x). The chain rule gives

dW=partial_x V_T,

because partial_y V_T=0. A critical point of W is therefore a critical point of the full V_T. This justifies coherent elimination if the eliminated action has been proved to be THIS fiber maximum. Naming an arbitrary reduced source action W is insufficient.

Full gluing and the boundary pairing. The route already used by the actual source is more intrinsic. With x_t=Log z_t, y_t=Log(1-z_t^(-1)), a direct sine-rule calculation gives

dV_T(delta theta)
   =sum_t[Re x_t delta Im y_t-Re y_t delta Im x_t].

At a full complex edge-gluing solution, the edge/peripheral symplectic reduction yields, for EVERY edge-angle tangent,

dV_T=(1/2)sum_i[Re V_i dIm U_i-Re U_i dIm V_i].  (8)

Here U_i,V_i are the actual small logarithms of the squared peripheral eigenvalues, in the fixed oriented marking of our notes. Full edge gluing kills the internal directions; the remaining quotient consists of these boundary directions. At a complete point U_i=V_i=0, (8) proves stationarity on all of P.

The tangent-space audit states the necessary full-rank edge/peripheral hypotheses and proves the analogous equivalence for arbitrary Lagrangian boundary slices. In particular this is stronger than differentiating volume merely along a family of already-glued representations.

5. The actual long-filling source passes the filled certificate

For a filled cusp, take a primitive slope a_i mu_i+b_i lambda_i and its actual log equation

a_i U_i+b_i V_i=2pi i epsilon_i, epsilon_i=+/-1. (9)

The affine angle constraints fix a_i Im U_i+b_i Im V_i=2pi epsilon_i. The real part of (9) annihilates (8) on every tangent to that constraint. Retained cusps are complete. Thus a positive source point satisfying full complex edge gluing and (9) is stationary on the ENTIRE filled angle slice. Strict concavity again makes it the unique global maximum.

For our selected source these inputs are established as follows:

  • The literal port lift is a representation of the actual augmented-link group, based at its explicitly constructed complete oriented geometry. The direct positive ideal subdivision supplies a compatible ideal chart for these actual cells, with no general triangulation existence assumption. Transporting this fixed positive triangulation by its marked flags gives the positive source point for sufficiently long fixed fillings.
  • Its source equation is U_i+p_i V_i=-2pi i on the selected unit-charge branch. The primitive-period proof fixes this as an actual period, not a congruence modulo 2pi i.
  • The marked action calculation proves V_src,s(p)=V_T(theta_p), including the actual filling quadratic and the absolute complete-volume anchor. There is no unidentified boundary constant left in this equality.
  • The geometric completion proves that these primitive periods close to smooth solid tori, with complete retained ends. This rules out a cone metric or a multiple covering at the core.

Consequently

V_src,s(p)=max_(closure P_p) V_T
                       =Vol(S^3 minus K_p).      (10)

The complete augmented value V_s instead belongs to the unfilled boundary problem. It cannot simply be substituted for V_src,s(p).

The improved whole-source bound gives the original quantum limit needed above for every fixed odd width s>=9 and independently sufficiently long fixed signed p_i. The same note records even widths s>=10 for the specified single-cut two-component link invariant. These are the current quantum scopes; the classical certificate itself has no pretzel-width restriction.

For a new source, once its limit is proved, the next work is precisely to supply its positive angle point, full stationarity and absolutely normalized volume equality on the actual topology. No further asymptotic theorem is needed AFTER those certificates hold. The existence of an abstract source rate alone supplies none of them.
