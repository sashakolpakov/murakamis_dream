A convex source minimum without the linear corridor variables

21 November 2026. Research note. Earlier finals and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

One packet at each binary product is enough for a uniformly convex minimum representing the original Conway source. The other packets can be evaluated by forward substitution. The inverse Gram matrix from exact least-squares elimination can then be omitted: the resulting unweighted objective is itself strongly convex, has a unique interior minimum, and admits a projection-free iteration with contraction factor 1/41/4.

The source comparison keeps the original normalization budget. This removes linear solves from the reduced optimization; it does not evaluate its geometric exponential rate.

1. The original normalized source and the retained packets

Use the complete normalized source of the convex theorem. At level N≥2N\ge2, every packet belongs to the full Newton space HN≅ℂNH_N\cong\mathbb C^N, including all repeated-Casimir directions. The local normalized leaf, turn, product and original scalar closing have norm at most σ=116. \sigma=\frac1{16}. For a map, the recorded Hilbert–Schmidt budget implies this operator bound. All phases and source markings are retained. Write av=Φv((ac)c),k=KNBtot=Latop.(1) a_v=\Phi_v((a_c)_c),\qquad k=\frac{K_N}{B_{\mathrm{ tot}}}=La_{\mathrm{ top}}. \qquad\text{(1)} Here BtotB_{\mathrm{ tot}} is exactly the product of the original local budgets, not a new geometric normalization. The true packets satisfy ∥av∥≤σ\|a_v\|\le\sigma, and ∥L∥≤σ\|L\|\le\sigma.

Let pp be the number of binary product vertices. Choose one child occurrence at each such vertex and retain that packet as a variable. Each occurrence has one parent, so this selects pp distinct packets. Call them u=(u1,…,up)u=(u_1,\ldots,u_p), and call all other proper packets zz. The top packet is eliminated, since its parent is the scalar closing root.

For each feasible uu, meaning ∥ui∥≤1\|u_i\|\le1, define z0(u)z_0(u) by solving every eliminated-output gate equation exactly from the leaves toward the root. A retained packet is a prescribed input when its parent is evaluated. Every eliminated packet has norm at most σ\sigma: the inputs are in their unit balls, and each normalized gate has norm at most σ\sigma.

Let Ψ(u)\Psi(u) denote the gate outputs at the retained vertices, evaluated at (u,z0(u))(u,z_0(u)), and retain the original closing c(u)=L(z0(u))top. c(u)=L(z_0(u))_{\mathrm{ top}}. Thus Go(u)=Ψ(u)−u,F(u)=(Go(u),c(u)),H(u)=∥F(u)∥2.(2) G_o(u)=\Psi(u)-u,\qquad F(u)=(G_o(u),c(u)),\qquad H(u)=\|F(u)\|^2. \qquad\text{(2)} In particular ∥Ψi(u)∥≤σ\|\Psi_i(u)\|\le\sigma and |c(u)|≤σ2|c(u)|\le\sigma^2. The original scalar is still the last component of FF; it has not been replaced by a norm.

The earlier orthogonal elimination theorem proved |k|2γret≤min∥ui∥≤1H(u)≤|k|2,γret=1+224−2.(3) \frac{|k|^2}{\gamma_{\mathrm{ ret}}} \le \min_{\|u_i\|\le1}H(u)\le |k|^2,\qquad \gamma_{\mathrm{ ret}}=1+224^{-2}. \qquad\text{(3)} It did not assert convexity of HH. That is the additional statement proved here.

If p=0p=0, there is no optimization: forward substitution gives H=|k|2H=|k|^2. Below assume p≥1p\ge1.

2. First derivatives of the forward substitution

At a feasible field, let AijA_{ij} be the block derivative of the normalized forward maps from variable class jj to output class ii, where i,j∈{u,z}i,j\in\{u,z\}. These are derivatives of the forward maps, before subtracting the output variables.

Every original product has exactly one retained child and one eliminated child. Consequently each of these four block matrices has at most one input block in each row and at most one parent block in each column. Its operator norm is at most σ\sigma. In particular 1415∥h∥≤∥DGo(u)h∥≤1615∥h∥.(5) \frac{14}{15}\|h\|\le\|DG_o(u)h\| \le\frac{16}{15}\|h\|. \qquad\text{(5)} The product norm and all differential statements use the real inner product Re⁡∑ihi*ki\operatorname{Re}\sum_i h_i^*k_i.

3. A dimension-independent second-variation estimate

The important point is that second variations do not accumulate with the number or length of corridors.

Fix a retained variation hh, put δz=Dz0(u)h\delta z=Dz_0(u)h, and differentiate the exact eliminated gate equations twice along this variation. At a binary gate vv, the direct second-derivative forcing is qv=2Φv(hretainedchild,δzeliminatedchild).(6) q_v=2\Phi_v(h_{\mathrm{ retained\ child}}, \delta z_{\mathrm{ eliminated\ child}}). \qquad\text{(6)} At leaves and linear gates it is zero. The child pairs at distinct binary gates are disjoint. Cauchy–Schwarz and (4) therefore give ∑all binary v∥qv∥≤2σ∥h∥∥δz∥≤1120∥h∥2.(7) \sum_{\text{all binary }v}\|q_v\| \le2\sigma\|h\|\,\|\delta z\| \le\frac1{120}\|h\|^2. \qquad\text{(7)}

Separate this forcing into eliminated-output and remaining-output rows, with sums of packet norms Qz,QoQ_z,Q_o, respectively. The scalar closing has no direct second derivative. Write ηz=D2z0(u)[h,h]\eta_z=D^2z_0(u)[h,h]. Differentiating its forward recursion gives ηz=Azzηz+qz.(8) \eta_z=A_{zz}\eta_z+q_z. \qquad\text{(8)} Use the block ℓ1\ell^1 norm, the sum of the packet norms, just for this estimate. 4. Strong convexity and an interior contracting iteration

Theorem. On the product of retained unit balls, 32I≤D2H≤52I.(12) \frac32 I\le D^2H\le\frac52 I. \qquad\text{(12)} The minimum is unique and interior. The iteration u(j+1)=u(j)−12∇H(u(j))(13) u^{(j+1)} =u^{(j)}-\frac12\nabla H(u^{(j)}) \qquad\text{(13)} stays feasible and contracts distances to that minimum by at most 1/41/4 at every step.

Proof. The half-Hessian is 12D2H[h,h]=∥DFh∥2+Re⁡⟨F,D2F[h,h]⟩. \tfrac12D^2H[h,h] =\|DFh\|^2+\operatorname{Re}\langle F,D^2F[h,h]\rangle. Equations (4), (5), and (11) bound the full Hessian between 2[(1415)2−171920]Iand2[(1615)2+12402+171920]I.(14) 2\left[\left(\frac{14}{15}\right)^2-\frac{17}{1920}\right]I \quad\text{and}\quad 2\left[\left(\frac{16}{15}\right)^2+ \frac1{240^2}+\frac{17}{1920}\right]I. \qquad\text{(14)} These imply (12).

It remains to check feasibility without projection. A retained input can affect exactly one remaining output equation: follow its unique parent path through eliminated vertices until reaching the first retained output, or the scalar closing. A retained output is prescribed, so its equation stops that forward influence. There is no cycle and Ψi\Psi_i is independent of its own uiu_i.

The derivative into that one downstream row is a product of local derivative blocks. Its norm is at most σ\sigma, since the path has at least one gate. There is no sum over competing paths. From (2), the ii-th component of the half-gradient step is (u−12∇H(u))i=Ψi(u)−(Di(Ψ,c))*F(u).(15) \left(u-\tfrac12\nabla H(u)\right)_i =\Psi_i(u)-(D_i(\Psi,c))^*F(u). \qquad\text{(15)} Only the one downstream row occurs in the adjoint term. Thus ∥(u−12∇H(u))i∥≤σ+σ(1+σ)=33256<1.(16) \left\|\left(u-\tfrac12\nabla H(u)\right)_i\right\| \le\sigma+\sigma(1+\sigma)=\frac{33}{256}<1. \qquad\text{(16)} The scalar-root case has the smaller bound |c|≤σ2|c|\le\sigma^2.

For two feasible fields, average the Hessian along their segment. By (12), the operator I−12D2H¯I-\tfrac12\overline{D^2H} has norm at most 1/41/4. Hence (13) is a contraction on the complete product of closed balls. Its iterates converge to a unique fixed point u*u_*, with ∥u(j)−u*∥≤4−j∥u(0)−u*∥.(17) \|u^{(j)}-u_*\|\le4^{-j}\|u^{(0)}-u_*\|. \qquad\text{(17)} At the fixed point the gradient vanishes. Strong convexity makes it the unique minimum, and (16) places it in the interior. ▫\square

Forward substitution and its reverse adjoint pass evaluate HH and its gradient. The iteration uses neither the inner normal equations nor an inverse boundary Gram matrix. This does not assert a cheap implementation of the original tensor contractions.

6. Degree, scope, and complete proof sources

Every forward expression is multiaffine in the retained packet groups, meaning affine in each packet separately. A literal tree uses each occurrence once, and eliminating a corridor does not duplicate it inside one output expression. Thus HH is separately quadratic in each retained packet. Its total real degree is at most 2max⁡(1,p)2\max(1,p). This can exceed four. The original lifted formulation retains degree four; the reduction trades that degree bound for fewer variables and no inner linear solve.

The correct Gram of exact least-squares elimination has not been declared equal to the identity. The unweighted objective is a different minimum with its own proved source comparison and the additional convexity theorem above. All original normal directions remain in each retained full packet.

The minimum remains a representation of a coherent quantum scalar. Uniform convexity, a faster iteration, and fewer packet variables do not determine its hyperbolic volume rate or the coefficient of a geometric contribution after arbitrary fixed short partial fillings. Absolute and relative accuracy for an exponentially small minimum are different requirements.
