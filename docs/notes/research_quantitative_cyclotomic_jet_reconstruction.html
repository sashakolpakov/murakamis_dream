Quantitative reconstruction from the actual cyclotomic source jets

Private written proof, 23 November 2026.

The exact bounded-jet Mellin theorem has a quantitative version for every specified actual arithmetic operator in that theorem. Its loss is at most eCN2e^{CN^2}. When the leading coefficient is a product of nonconstant clock binomials, the loss improves to NAeCNN^Ae^{CN}. Both bounds include singular steps and preserve the original prefix, affine source, pole lattice and residue.

The extra factor hypothesis is not proved for every Conway operator. No geometric approximate section is constructed, and no volume identity or arbitrary-short-filling theorem follows.

4. Regular and singular jet inverses

Every coefficient aj(Q,Qm)a_j(Q,Q^m), n0−d≤m≤N−dn_0-d\le m\le N-d, has uniformly bounded RkR_k norm. To see this termwise, its substituted monomials have exponent E=O(N)E=O(N), and [ζℓ](1+ζ/N)E=(Eℓ)N−ℓ(12) [\zeta^\ell](1+\zeta/N)^E=\binom E\ell N^{-\ell} \qquad\text{(12)} is uniformly bounded for every fixed ℓ<k\ell<k, also for negative integer EE. 5. Propagation with exactly the available precision

Subtract the actual truncated jets and write their errors as ere_r. The exact Fourier ordering in (4) cancels the shift offset, giving ∑j=0daj(Q,Qr−d)er−d+j=ρr−d,∥ρr−d∥k≤δN.(15) \sum_{j=0}^{d}a_j(Q,Q^{r-d})e_{r-d+j}=\rho_{r-d}, \qquad \|\rho_{r-d}\|_k\le\delta_N. \qquad\text{(15)} All initial prefix errors vanish. Put Mr=∑t=n0rμtM_r=\sum_{t=n_0}^{r}\mu_t, h(r)=k−Mr≥1h(r)=k-M_r\ge1. At step rr, retain earlier errors only through precision k−Mr−1k-M_{r-1}. These coefficients are already known: earlier steps retained at least that precision.

At a regular step use (13). At a singular step the right side, after subtracting earlier terms, is divisible by ζμr\zeta^{\mu_r} modulo the available precision, since it equals hrerh_re_r. Drop those first μr\mu_r zero coefficients and use (14). This determines and bounds ere_r in Rh(r)R_{h(r)}. No unavailable higher error jet is used or divided at zero.

The coefficient bounds (12), fixed memory dd, and triangle inequality now give a discrete forced estimate: its amplification is bounded by the product of fixed regular-step constants, the regular inverse factors (13), and the singular factors (14). There are at most NN forcing terms. 6. The general arithmetic bound

Drop (2), and use B=D(s−1)B=D(s-1), k≥B+1k\ge B+1. Multiply the whole equation and its source by one fixed nonzero integer so all Laurent coefficients are algebraic integers in a fixed number field KK. Constants absorb this recorded rescaling. This is possible by clearing the finitely many algebraic denominators.

Put h=[K:ℚ]h=[K:\mathbb Q]. At a regular step the pivot λ=ad(QN,QNr−d)≠0\lambda=a_d(Q_N,Q_N^{r-d})\ne0 is an algebraic integer in K(QN)K(Q_N), whose degree is at most hφ(N)≤hNh\varphi(N)\le hN. All of its conjugates have modulus at most one fixed H≥1H\ge1: an embedding sends roots of unity to roots of unity, and the finitely many coefficient conjugates have a fixed sum-of-moduli bound. The field norm of a nonzero algebraic integer is a nonzero integer. Its modulus is at least one. Bounding all factors except the specified embedding by HH therefore proves |λ|≥H−hN.(17) |\lambda|\ge H^{-hN}. \qquad\text{(17)} The integer-norm assertion can also be seen from the monic integral minimal polynomial: its nonzero constant term is the signed product of its conjugates. Passing to the larger field only raises that integer product to a positive integral power.

The zero-step budget is the exact one in [M]. Because r−d>AQr-d>A_Q, the trajectory polynomial ad(Q,Qr−d)a_d(Q,Q^{r-d}) is nonzero identically. Its order μr\mu_r at QNQ_N is at most s−1s-1, by the logarithmic-derivative Vandermonde argument. For all large NN, ad(QN,Y)a_d(Q_N,Y) is not identically zero. Its YY-span is DD and the visited QNr−dQ_N^{r-d} are distinct. Hence at most DD indices vanish, and ∑rμr≤B\sum_r\mu_r\le B.

At such an index write the first nonzero scaled coefficient as βμr=N−μr∑a,bca,bQNa+b(r−d)(a+b(r−d)μr).(18) \beta_{\mu_r}=N^{-\mu_r} \sum_{a,b}c_{a,b}Q_N^{a+b(r-d)} \binom{a+b(r-d)}{\mu_r}. \qquad\text{(18)} The sum NμrβμrN^{\mu_r}\beta_{\mu_r} is a nonzero algebraic integer. Each of its conjugates is bounded by CNμrCN^{\mu_r}, since generalized binomial coefficients of integral exponents are integers and the exponents are O(N)O(N). The same norm argument yields |βμr|≥N−μr(CNμr)−hN≥exp⁡[−C′Nlog⁡(N+1)].(19) |\beta_{\mu_r}|\ge N^{-\mu_r}(CN^{\mu_r})^{-hN} \ge \exp[-C'N\log(N+1)]. \qquad\text{(19)} The uniformly bounded scaled coefficients (12) and the inverse proof (13) now bound each regular inverse by eCNe^{CN}, and each singular unit inverse by eCNlog⁡(N+1)e^{CN\log(N+1)}. The precision argument (15) is unchanged, with BB replacing B0B_0. There are at most NN regular steps and at most DD singular steps. Their product and the NN forcing terms are bounded by C1eC2N2C_1e^{C_2N^2}. The exact residue argument after (16) proves (8a).

7. Necessity of the refinement hypothesis and scope

Factors independent of YY cannot be admitted silently. For the actual unknot colors Cr=1C_r=1, the all-level annihilator P=[((1−Q)T−1)(T−1)]=hT2−(1+h)T+1,h=1−Q, P=[((1-Q)T-1)(T-1)]=hT^2-(1+h)T+1,\qquad h=1-Q, has jet budget one in [M]. Put hN=1−QNh_N=1-Q_N. Fix r0≥n0+2r_0\ge n_0+2 and set er=0e_r=0 below r0r_0, er=hNN−re_r=h_N^{N-r} through NN. Its only finite-window defects are ±hNN−r0+1\pm h_N^{N-r_0+1}; its last coefficient is one. Adding (∑r=r0NerXr)/(1−XN)(\sum_{r=r_0}^Ne_rX^r)/(1-X^N) preserves the exact prefix and pole lattice but changes the residue by one. The defect is exp⁡(−Nlog⁡N+O(N))\exp(-N\log N+O(N)). Thus (8b) is false for general factorizations allowing b=0b=0. This is a test of a nonminimal actual annihilator, not a knot counterexample.

The general bound uses no binomial or classical-end hypothesis. It applies to the specified arithmetic actual recurrence and source of [M]. The finite-array elimination [F] does not prove (2) for every knot, and primitive/Weyl symmetry does not eliminate the classical end losses in [E]. No broad Conway factorization is asserted. A geometric comparison must still produce (6)–(7) with its actual source and sufficiently small defect. No such comparison is supplied.
