The actual one-head finite Euler kernel through a small coherent activity

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The finite Euler polynomial of the actual root lowering operator has a nonzero coherent-kernel asymptotic on every compact chart away from incidence, for sufficiently small complex Euler parameter. This follows from its exact finite recurrence. A coefficient majorant proves that the actual logarithm converges uniformly; a second majorant proves the relative error. Thus the analytic characteristic calculation below is not being used as a substitute for an asymptotic theorem.

The donor and recipient coordinates remain the original binomial coherent coordinates. All finite endpoint terms remain. This result estimates one actual matrix factor; it does not evaluate its contraction with the opposite-source quotient or identify a geometric contribution to a closed period.

The sole complete direct S is pinned below. It supplies the literal root matrices and their coherent convention. All finite identities and analytic estimates needed here are proved inline.

5. The actual coherent chart and its limits

For any compact 𝒦⋐X\mathcal K\Subset X, the two functions θ\theta and w0z1/Iw_0z_1/I in (4) are bounded. Thus a fixed small disk |λ|<ϵ𝒦|\lambda|<\epsilon_{\mathcal K} puts the actual kernel in (30). In homogeneous notation the theorem is KUN(w,z)=IdeNV(1,θ,t)e−cp/2(1+b)Δ(1+O(N−1)).(33) \boxed{\quad K_{U_N}(w,z)=I^d e^{N V(1,\theta,t)} \frac{e^{-cp/2}}{(1+b)\sqrt{\Delta}} (1+O(N^{-1})). \quad}\qquad\text{(33)} It is valid through projective coordinate infinities: all normalized parameters used in the proof are regular on XX. The actual normalized kernel is zero-free on this neighborhood for every NN by (15), and its displayed asymptotic coefficient is a unit. At λ=0\lambda=0 it is exactly the original identity kernel.

In a local trivialization its phase is log⁡I+V\log I+V, with the additional amplitude factor I−1I^{-1} from d=N−1d=N-1. At λ=0\lambda=0, the mixed phase derivative in affine coordinates is ∂w∂zlog⁡(1+wz)=(1+wz)−2\partial_w\partial_z\log(1+wz)=(1+wz)^{-2}, nonzero. Continuity and the proved derivative bounds keep this mixed derivative nonzero on smaller compact charts for sufficiently small λ\lambda. Thus (33) is a regular local generating kernel near the identity, including its computed nonzero amplitude.

The finite canonical rows are also explicit. With the original KK, one has KE=QEKKE=QEK, so EUN=UNE,KUN=(1−λE)UNK.(34) EU_N=U_NE,\qquad KU_N=(1-\lambda E)U_NK. \qquad\text{(34)} For the second identity use the exact polynomial recurrence EN(QX)=(1−X)EN(X)+XN/NE_N(QX)=(1-X)E_N(X)+X^N/N at X=λEX=\lambda E; the last term is zero as a matrix. These rows check the graph of an already proved analytic kernel. They were not used to infer its amplitude or its zero-freeness.

The theorem is at fixed quantum root and complete meridian. It does not differentiate the meridian-dependent lowering matrix, its vacuum row, or a shifted denominator (M−2Q;Q)n(M^{-2}Q;Q)_n. Those are additional actual normal insertions. Likewise no quantum-parameter derivative is obtained by differentiating EN=0E^N=0 or the root products.

All occupations, including the original finite upper and lower arrows, have been retained in (2)–(6). The theorem controls one actual Euler factor on a small coherent neighborhood; it does not replace a coproduct factorization, an inverse-factorial donor contraction, the quotient qFq_F, or the original pivotal first-jet closing row. No assertion is made that a prescribed geometric source frame lies in this neighborhood, or that a closed source period is nonzero.
