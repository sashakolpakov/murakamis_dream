Knot complement volume under repeated single-tangle attachments

31 October 2026. This research theorem enlarges the previously proved two-or-more-leaf composition class by permitting a single original leaf to be attached to either side of any completed child. The source estimate, ordered geometry and marked peripheral deformation are retained together. The conclusion is the hyperbolic volume of the resulting knot complement. Only the auxiliary crossing-circle cusps are filled; the knot cusp stays unfilled.

1. The class and quantifiers

Let h=T(LN)h=T(L_N) be the original turned complete crossing-circle coupon, TT the literal ordered quarter-turn, and multiplication the original stack of four-port boxes. Distinct occurrences have independent leaves and auxiliary colors, with their original local disk framings and the original global one-cut mark retained. Define completed expressions by

E::=hh∣T(E)∣E1E2∣hE∣Eh. E::=hh\mid T(E)\mid E_1E_2\mid hE\mid Eh. \tag{1}

The closed class consists of the original cylindrical closures C(FG)C(FG) with both F,GF,G completed expressions in (1). Equivalently, any hsh^s, s≥2s\ge2, may be included among the initial blocks. Choose a fixed half-twist pattern ϵℓ∈{0,1,−1}\epsilon_\ell\in\{0,1,-1\} whose literal endpoint pairings give a knot without closed components inside the tangle tree. Such a pattern exists for every expression in this class by the constructive endpoint-pairing induction.

At every auxiliary crossing circle insert an independently chosen, fixed disk-framed full twist R−2pℓR^{-2p_\ell}. The resulting rational leaf is the actual tangle T(Rϵℓ−2pℓ)T(R^{\epsilon_\ell-2p_\ell}). Write KE,ϵ(𝒑)K_{E,\epsilon}(\mathbf p) for the resulting knot.

Theorem. For each fixed finite expression and knot half-twist pattern there is a finite threshold P(E,ϵ)P(E,\epsilon) such that, for all fixed signed integers pℓp_\ell with |pℓ|>P(E,ϵ)|p_\ell|>P(E,\epsilon), the knot complement is hyperbolic and

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(KE,ϵ(𝒑))|=Vol⁡(S3\KE,ϵ(𝒑)). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log\left|K_N\bigl(K_{E,\epsilon}(\mathbf p)\bigr)\right| =\operatorname{Vol}\!\left(S^3\setminus K_{E,\epsilon}(\mathbf p)\right). \tag{2}

The number of tangles, nesting depth, and number of single-leaf attachments are arbitrary finite numbers. The signs and magnitudes of the pℓp_\ell need not agree or be comparable. The expression and all twists are fixed before NN tends to infinity, through both parities and composite levels. No threshold uniform over all trees is claimed.

Here KNK_N is the original marked invariant with the retained enhancement. The normalization comparison and original crossing calculation retain an outer unit-modulus factor in the reduced enhanced Kashaev normalization, or the recorded single factor of modulus 1/N1/N in the full modified one-cut normalization. Both give (2).

The previously proved isolated closures C(hs)C(h^s), s≥3s\ge3, are also available from the two-or-more-leaf theorem. They are inherited cases, not an assertion that every root-leaf closure C(hE)C(hE) belongs to (1). The isolated C(hh)C(hh) remains excluded.

2. Strict geometry and the selected finite source

The source composition proof establishes a strict reduced state at every interior output of every completed expression. At closure only the final forced gate is removed; its exact coefficient is one. Its inputs satisfy i+j=N−1i+j=N-1. The original entropy barrier and injective angle maps then give a unique strict complete optimizer with negative definite reduced Hessian.

The geometric composition proof retains the stationary port variables

B=cot⁡(πb),X2+Y2−B2=1,−Y<B<X. B=\cot(\pi b),\qquad X^2+Y^2-B^2=1,\qquad -Y<B<X.

Both completed root children have 0<X,Y<10<X,Y<1, and these bounds propagate to all completed descendants. At a new leaf attachment,

Bp=X−1X+B,Yp=YX+B,X+B>0. B_p=X-\frac1{X+B},\qquad Y_p=\frac{Y}{X+B},\qquad X+B>0. \tag{3}

The proper parent and child bounds imply 1<2X(X+B)<4X21<2X(X+B)<4X^2. The hhhh base gives the same lower bound from Yp=1/(2X)<1Y_p=1/(2X)<1. Every physical leaf consequently satisfies

This is automatic for (1), not an extra test of individual knots. It supplies the strict high-index window required by the original signed complex packet.

with negative real part. These facts concern the original finite source. The global alias bounds needed to pass to the full invariant are supplied next.

3. The estimate that makes a single attachment composable

At an actual attachment, let μ=fE′(b)\mu=f_E'(b) be the child slope and λ=fhE′(c)\lambda=f_{hE}'(c) the parent slope. Use the original leaf actions g,kg,k, gate action mm, and true support tetrahedron

D={(x,y,z):x,y≥0,max⁡(x,y)≤z≤x+y≤1}. D=\{(x,y,z):x,y\ge0,\ \max(x,y)\le z\le x+y\le1\}.

The single-leaf alias theorem proves the two dual bounds

P(μ,λ)=maxD,x≤1/2[g(x)+m(x,y,z)+μy−λz],Q(μ,λ)=maxD[k(x)+m(x,y,z)+μy−λz],P(μ,λ)−Q(μ,λ)≥12500at every proper attachment. \begin{aligned} P(\mu,\lambda)&=\max_{D,\ x\le1/2} [g(x)+m(x,y,z)+\mu y-\lambda z],\\ Q(\mu,\lambda)&=\max_D [k(x)+m(x,y,z)+\mu y-\lambda z],\\ P(\mu,\lambda)-Q(\mu,\lambda)&\ge\frac1{2500} \quad\text{at every proper attachment.} \end{aligned} \tag{6}

The child enters through its output tangent μy\mu y; it is not replaced by a bare leaf. For 1/2<X≤2/31/2<X\le2/3, the actual slopes lie in [−2log⁡2,0]2[-2\log2,0]^2. A finite rational certificate proves the stronger gap 1/10001/1000 on that entire square: 55 dyadic rectangles, four slope corners, and the four exact vertices of DD give 880 outward-rounded rational inequalities. A feasible pure state bounds PP below; a global concave tangent bounds QQ above. The portable verifier uses no numerical optimizer. For X≥2/3X\ge2/3, the retained individual leaf certificate supplies the margin 1/25001/2500. A separate monotone response argument and four-vertex endpoint certificate strengthen the bound on the actual small-XX port set; they are not needed for (6).

For every completed subtree the inductive estimate is its selected output tangent, with zero loss for its core and positive loss for any nonempty alias sector. The hhhh block supplies the base. Turns reverse the slope. Completed/completed products add the child tangents and the gate tangent; stationary equations cancel the internal linear terms.

At hEhE the exact alias split is

Ah⋆CE+Ch⋆AE+Ah⋆AE. A_h\star C_E+C_h\star A_E+A_h\star A_E. \tag{7}

The three terms lose, respectively, the new margin (6), the inherited child margin, and their sum. The kernel ⋆\star retains all original colors, normal directions, and the constraint i+j≤N−1i+j\le N-1. The same argument applies to EhEh. A turn is never replaced by a multiplicative rule.

At the completed/completed root the two output tangents cancel on the original closing relation. The N−1N-1 shifts cost O(1)O(1) in the exponent. A fixed tree has only polynomially many wire states and finitely many alias sectors, so a strict whole-source alias gap follows. The all-core concave majorant has the same unique strict optimizer; compactness away from it and the original local far-color estimate give the physical-color complement gap. This supplies both global hypotheses of the original-source filling transfer.

4. Why the right-hand side is the knot complement volume

The geometry companion constructs the actual augmented-link complement YEϵY_E^\epsilon from the original ideal pyramids, ordered multiplication prisms, bowtie cuts and signed shaded face pairings. A single leaf uses its original π/2\pi/2 CAP case; a completed input uses its completed CAP case. The completed/completed root retains the original closing maps. The cusp decorations and pointwise edge words give the complete metric, with absolute anchor

Vol⁡(YEϵ)=2πF0. \operatorname{Vol}(Y_E^\epsilon)=2\pi F_0. \tag{8}

The original marked deformation retains the primitive auxiliary logs and absolute regulator

Uℓ=−2πiχℓ,Vℓ=−2∂ℓΦϵ,G=2πiΦϵ. U_\ell=-2\pi i\chi_\ell,\qquad V_\ell=-2\partial_\ell\Phi_\epsilon,\qquad G=2\pi i\Phi_\epsilon. \tag{9}

The changed half-twist correction is included. The full-source transfer gives a nonzero asymptotic with selected action

Sϵ,𝒑(χ)=Φϵ(χ)+πi2∑ℓ(χℓ−1)2pℓ. S_{\epsilon,\mathbf p}(\chi) =\Phi_\epsilon(\chi)+\frac{\pi i}{2} \sum_\ell\frac{(\chi_\ell-1)^2}{p_\ell}. \tag{10}

Its critical equations are exactly the primitive filling equations Uℓ+pℓVℓ=−2πiU_\ell+p_\ell V_\ell=-2\pi i. The marked geometric completion and absolute regulator identity on this same branch give

2πRe⁡Sϵ,𝒑(χ*)=Vol⁡(S3\KE,ϵ(𝒑)). 2\pi\operatorname{Re}S_{\epsilon,\mathbf p}(\chi_*) =\operatorname{Vol}\!\left(S^3\setminus K_{E,\epsilon}(\mathbf p)\right). \tag{11}

All auxiliary crossing circles have been filled to insert the chosen twists. The main component becomes the knot, and its cusp is unfilled. Thus (11) concerns its knot complement, whereas (8) concerns the starting augmented-link complement. The nonzero full-source asymptotic and (11) prove (2).

5. Scope and remaining work

The new grammar permits repeated chains En+1=hT(En)E_{n+1}=hT(E_n), starting from E0=hhE_0=hh, as well as arbitrary completed products and turns. This is a wider class of literal presentations than the previous seed grammar. Algebraic equalities between closed source scalars do not by themselves prove tangle isotopy or novelty of a knot class.

No coverage theorem for every arborescent knot is asserted. Independently turned bare leaves and arbitrary root-leaf closures require further work. For example, C(hT(hs))C(hT(h^s)) has a strict complete state but inner leaf coordinates exactly 1/41/4. The two transport eigenvalues there are distinct with equal modulus. The subsequent quarter-index packet theorem extends the local selected packet through that boundary. It does not supply the whole-source alias and physical-color estimates for those closures. The completed-root restriction in (1) remains in force.

The generic main-meridian quantum A-operator and coverage of all desired arborescent presentations remain open. The present theorem uses the original quantum source and its marked algebraic deformation, with the specified analytic and geometric dependencies.
