Independent audit of the whole cyclic Rees crossing

Audit, 27 October 2026. The proof in rees_whole_crossing_source_factorization.md passes this independent normalization, order and source check. No test rerun was needed.

The common head root and Hopf scales cancel in the crossing argument: ℰhat,1Fhat,2\mathcal E_{\mathrm{hat,1}}F_{\mathrm{hat,2}} is precisely the conjugate of ℰ1F2\mathcal E_1F_2. Separate roots instead retain their stated ratio. Consequently the universal series can be conjugated coefficientwise with a central bookkeeping variable; local nilpotence after this irregular change is not assumed.

Splitting log⁡PQ(z)\log P_Q(z) at indices divisible by NN gives exactly PQ(z)=CQ(z)PQN(zN)1/N. P_Q(z)=C_Q(z)\,P_{Q^N}(z^N)^{1/N}. Both factors are functions of the same operator and commute. The two functional ratios have logarithms −log⁡(1−z)+1Nlog⁡(1−zN),−1Nlog⁡(1−zN). -\log(1-z)+\frac1N\log(1-z^N),\qquad -\frac1N\log(1-z^N). Their sum has the required original Pochhammer transport.

At a root, for N∤mN\nmid m, ∑j=1N−1jQNjm=−N/(1−QNm)\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}jQ_N^{jm}=-N/(1-Q_N^m). Thus the inverse weighted product has exactly the nonresonant coefficients of CQNC_{Q_N}. At m=Nrm=Nr, its weighted coefficient is canceled by ν−(N−1)/2\nu^{-(N-1)/2}, with ν=(1−zN)1/N\nu=(1-z^N)^{1/N}. This verifies CQN=gD−1C_{Q_N}=g_D^{-1}, including even and composite levels. Deck summation annihilates every remaining logarithmic coefficient, so the cyclic norm and the marked generic matrix determinant are one.

For d=N(QN−1)d=N(Q^N-1), direct expansion gives log⁡PQN(w)1/N=Li⁡2(w)d+Li⁡2(w)+log⁡(1−w)2N+O(d). \log P_{Q^N}(w)^{1/N} =\frac{\operatorname{Li}_2(w)}d+ \frac{\operatorname{Li}_2(w)+\log(1-w)}{2N}+O(d). The finite Li⁡2/(2N)\operatorname{Li}_2/(2N) term is required for this linear normal. The note correctly does not apply the displayed finite scalar part to a moving noncommutative operator lift.

The rational universal conjugation identities, rather than the inner root matrix gDg_D, supply the center transport. The ordered power vN=−e1f2k2/k1v^N=-e_1f_2k_2/k_1 gives Dc=1+e1f2k2/k1D_c=1+e_1f_2k_2/k_1; the printed center formulas preserve each individual fixed-Casimir equation ef−k−k−1=constantef-k-k^{-1}=\text{constant}. The coproduct NN-th powers also give the stated obstruction e1k2+e2=k1e2+e1 e_1k_2+e_2=k_1e_2+e_1 to any generic regular same-fiber matrix replacement. This is consistent with a canonical operator transporting the center.

Finally inew=Cii_{\mathrm{new}}=Ci, pnew=pC−1p_{\mathrm{new}}=pC^{-1} prove the exact finite-head pairing. Their normalized product does not remove the source exponential e−∑piui/de^{-\sum p_i u_i/d}, the head ratios, or the exponential at each internal projector. The literal pivot, peripheral functions and two-wall correction remain selected.

The proof’s scope is appropriate: a coefficientwise formal whole factorization and its rational canonical transport, with an explicitly irregular source. It does not assert convergence of the transformed series at bookkeeping parameter one, a regular full-cyclic matrix limit, survival of its polar phase in a finite-head pairing, or the physical large-level volume norm.
