Uniform confluence depth for the original generic fusion source

Written research proof, 22 November 2026.

The generic fusion coordinates of the actual invariant source can be regularized using a number of quantum-parameter jets bounded by tensor width, independently of the level. An explicit Clebsch inclusion and Casimir-projector dual give the pole bound. Finite coefficient equations then recover the saturated root source without an unspecified large minor inverse.

The saturated source and its divided-current characterization are already known in [S,D]. The new assertion is an effective uniform confluence budget for its actual generic fusion frames. This is not an analytic norm bound or an identification with a formal cyclic tetrahedron kernel.

1. The actual family and the statement

Fix N≥2N\ge2, d=N−1d=N-1, q0=eπi/Nq_0=e^{\pi i/N}, and an even number ℓ≥2\ell\ge2 of physical heads. Let u=ζ−q0,𝒪=ℂ{u},𝒦=Frac⁡(𝒪),ρ=ζ−ζ−1,[j]=ζj−ζ−jρ,[j]!=∏r=1j[r].(1) u=\zeta-q_0,\qquad \mathcal O=\mathbb C\{u\},\qquad \mathcal K=\operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O),\qquad \rho=\zeta-\zeta^{-1},\quad [j]=\frac{\zeta^j-\zeta^{-j}}{\rho},\quad [j]!=\prod_{r=1}^j[r]. \qquad\text{(1)} All half-integral Cartan powers use the fixed analytic logarithm of ζ\zeta near q0q_0. A pole bound JJ means that multiplying every matrix entry by uJu^J makes it regular. The neighborhood in uu may depend on NN; no uniform analytic radius is asserted.

Use the actual generic balanced leaf family of [S], and put ℳ𝒪=Vd(ζ)⊗ℓ,ℐ𝒦=Inv⁡𝒦(ℳ𝒪⊗𝒦),Λ=ℐ𝒦∩ℳ𝒪.(2) \mathcal M_{\mathcal O}=V_d(\zeta)^{\otimes\ell},\qquad \mathcal I_{\mathcal K} =\operatorname{Inv}_{\mathcal K} (\mathcal M_{\mathcal O}\otimes\mathcal K), \quad \Lambda=\mathcal I_{\mathcal K}\cap\mathcal M_{\mathcal O}. \qquad\text{(2)} The lattice is saturated, free of rank g=dim⁡𝒦ℐ𝒦g=\dim_{\mathcal K}\mathcal I_{\mathcal K}, and its root fiber 𝒱=Λ/uΛ⊂ℳ𝒪/uℳ𝒪(3) \mathcal V=\Lambda/u\Lambda \ \subset\ \mathcal M_{\mathcal O}/u\mathcal M_{\mathcal O} \qquad\text{(3)} is embedded in the original physical root tensor. These statements, and the fact that every original regular braid/cup source lies in this fiber, are established in [S,D]. The entire raw root invariant space may be larger than (3).

Theorem. For every binary fusion tree TT with the ℓ\ell leaves labelled dd and root label zero, there are explicitly defined matrices BT:𝒦g→ℳ𝒪⊗𝒦,LT:ℳ𝒪⊗𝒦→𝒦g B_T:\mathcal K^g\longrightarrow \mathcal M_{\mathcal O}\otimes\mathcal K,\qquad L_T:\mathcal M_{\mathcal O}\otimes\mathcal K \longrightarrow\mathcal K^g such that im⁡BT=ℐ𝒦,LTBT=I,uJℓBT,uJℓLT have regular entries,Jℓ=(5ℓ+4)(ℓ−1).(4) \operatorname{im}B_T=\mathcal I_{\mathcal K},\qquad L_TB_T=I,\qquad u^{J_\ell}B_T,\ u^{J_\ell}L_T \text{ have regular entries},\qquad J_\ell=(5\ell+4)(\ell-1). \qquad\text{(4)} Consequently the intrinsic fusion-coordinate lattice ΛT=BT−1Λ\Lambda_T=B_T^{-1}\Lambda satisfies uJℓ𝒪g⊂ΛT⊂u−Jℓ𝒪g.(5) u^{J_\ell}\mathcal O^g \subset\Lambda_T \subset u^{-J_\ell}\mathcal O^g . \qquad\text{(5)} All confluence depths are therefore bounded in terms of width, independently of NN, including even and composite levels.

More explicitly, set J=JℓJ=J_\ell, CT=uJBTC_T=u^JB_T. The full physical source fiber is computed by the finite formula 𝒱={[u2J](CTz):z∈(𝒪/u2J+1)g,(CTz)=0(0≤k<2J)}.(6) \boxed{\quad \mathcal V= \left\{[u^{2J}](C_Tz): \begin{array}{l} z\in(\mathcal O/u^{2J+1})^g,\\ [u^k](C_Tz)=0\quad(0\le k<2J) \end{array}\right\}. \quad} \qquad\text{(6)} Thus the Laurent coefficients of BTB_T at orders −J,…,J-J,\ldots,J suffice. No jet of an unknown knot value enters.

If WW is any whole actual regular physical braid word, then LT′WBT has pole order at most 2J.(7) L_{T'}WB_T \text{ has pole order at most }2J . \qquad\text{(7)} The bound does not grow with the number of letters in the word. At the root its action on (3) is simply the restriction of the original W(q0)W(q_0); no positive-order jet of WW is needed for that root-value operation.

2. Generic modules and the actual balanced leaf gauge

For a nonnegative integer color AA, use the divided-FF basis vj(A)=Fjv0(A)/[j]!v_j^{(A)}=F^jv_0^{(A)}/[j]!, 0≤j≤A0\le j\le A. The matrices are Fvj=[j+1]vj+1,Evj=[A−j+1]vj−1,Kvj=ζA−2jvj,(8) Fv_j=[j+1]v_{j+1},\qquad Ev_j=[A-j+1]v_{j-1},\qquad Kv_j=\zeta^{A-2j}v_j , \qquad\text{(8)} with the missing endpoint arrows zero. These matrices have Laurent-polynomial entries for every color. Use ΔE=E⊗K+I⊗E,ΔF=F⊗I+K−1⊗F.(9) \Delta E=E\otimes K+I\otimes E,\qquad \Delta F=F\otimes I+K^{-1}\otimes F . \qquad\text{(9)} The normalized Casimir is Ω=ρ2FE+ζK+ζ−1K−1.(10) \Omega=\rho^2FE+\zeta K+\zeta^{-1}K^{-1}. \qquad\text{(10)} It has eigenvalue λA=ζA+1+ζ−A−1\lambda_A=\zeta^{A+1}+\zeta^{-A-1} on a highest weight AA module.

Every sas_a and its inverse is a DVR unit at q0q_0, because ρ(q0)≠0\rho(q_0)\ne0. The gauge and all its phases are retained on the physical source, operators and rows. Its possible large complex norm is not discarded or claimed bounded.

3. A literal Clebsch inclusion and an elementary generic decomposition

Take colors A,BA,B and C=A+B−2k,0≤k≤min⁡(A,B). C=A+B-2k,\qquad 0\le k\le\min(A,B). There is a highest vector hC=∑r=0kbrvr(A)⊗vk−r(B),b0=1.(13) h_C=\sum_{r=0}^k b_r\, v_r^{(A)}\otimes v_{k-r}^{(B)},\qquad b_0=1 . \qquad\text{(13)} The equation ΔEhC=0\Delta E\,h_C=0 gives exactly br+1br=−ζ−B+2k−2r−2[B−k+r+1][A−r],br=(−1)rζr(2k−B−r−1)[B−k+r]![A−r]![B−k]![A]!.(14) \frac{b_{r+1}}{b_r} =-\zeta^{-B+2k-2r-2} \frac{[B-k+r+1]}{[A-r]}, \quad b_r=(-1)^r\zeta^{r(2k-B-r-1)} \frac{[B-k+r]![A-r]!}{[B-k]![A]!}. \qquad\text{(14)} This follows by comparing the coefficient of vr(A)⊗vk−r−1(B)v_r^{(A)}\otimes v_{k-r-1}^{(B)}. Define ιA,BC(vj(C))=(ΔF)j[j]!hC.(15) \iota_{A,B}^{C}(v_j^{(C)}) =\frac{(\Delta F)^j}{[j]!}h_C . \qquad\text{(15)} This is the prescribed inclusion, with all normalization phases fixed by (13)–(14).

For completeness these inclusions give the entire generic pair tensor. The elementary commutator calculation gives (ΔE)(ΔF)jhC=[j][C−j+1](ΔF)j−1hC.(16) (\Delta E)(\Delta F)^j h_C =[j][C-j+1](\Delta F)^{j-1}h_C . \qquad\text{(16)} It proves nonvanishing through j=Cj=C. If FC+1hCF^{C+1}h_C were nonzero, it would be a highest vector of negative weight −C−2-C-2. Its subsequent FF-string could not terminate: all the coefficients in (16), with that negative highest weight, are nonzero over 𝒦\mathcal K. This contradicts nilpotence of FF on the finite tensor. Hence (15) is an intertwiner from the full VCV_C. Its Casimir eigenvalue is λC\lambda_C.

The eigenvalues for the distinct allowed colors CC are distinct over 𝒦\mathcal K. The images are therefore independent, and ∑k=0min⁡(A,B)(A+B−2k+1)=(A+1)(B+1)(17) \sum_{k=0}^{\min(A,B)}(A+B-2k+1)=(A+1)(B+1) \qquad\text{(17)} shows that they exhaust the tensor product. No semisimplicity theorem at the root has been used.

4. An exact projector dual with a bounded pole order

Let 𝒞(A,B)\mathcal C(A,B) be the allowed color list in (13). On the generic pair put PC(Ω)=∏D∈𝒞(A,B)D≠CΩ−λDIλC−λD.(18) P_C(\Omega)= \prod_{\substack{D\in\mathcal C(A,B)\\D\ne C}} \frac{\Omega-\lambda_DI}{\lambda_C-\lambda_D}. \qquad\text{(18)} This is a generic projector, not a root specialization of distinct Casimir values. Let ε0,k\varepsilon_{0,k} extract the coefficient of v0(A)⊗vk(B)v_0^{(A)}\otimes v_k^{(B)}. On the pair degree k+jk+j, define πA,BC(y)=[C−j]![C]!ε0,k(ΔE)jPC(Ω)yvj(C)(0≤j≤C);(19) \pi_{A,B}^{C}(y)= \frac{[C-j]!}{[C]!}\, \varepsilon_{0,k}(\Delta E)^jP_C(\Omega)y\, v_j^{(C)} \quad(0\le j\le C); \qquad\text{(19)} on the other degrees define it to be zero. Since Ejvj(C)=[C]![C−j]!v0(C),ε0,khC=1, E^jv_j^{(C)}=\frac{[C]!}{[C-j]!}v_0^{(C)}, \qquad \varepsilon_{0,k}h_C=1 , the complete decomposition just proved gives πA,BCιA,BD=δCDI,∑CιA,BCπA,BC=I.(20) \pi_{A,B}^{C}\iota_{A,B}^{D}=\delta_{CD}I,\qquad \sum_C\iota_{A,B}^{C}\pi_{A,B}^{C}=I . \qquad\text{(20)} The dual is therefore literal and explicit. No unknown minor or favorable Hermitian form defines its normalization.

Every positive integer quantum factorial satisfies ord⁡u=0[m]!=⌊m/N⌋.(21) \operatorname{ord}_{u=0}[m]!=\lfloor m/N\rfloor . \qquad\text{(21)} Indeed [j][j] has a simple zero precisely when N∣jN\mid j; its denominator ρ\rho is a unit. All colors in a tree with ℓ\ell leaves are at most ℓd<ℓN\ell d<\ell N. Equation (14) is a regular numerator divided by the product [A][A−1]⋯[A−r+1][A][A-1]\cdots[A-r+1]. Its pole order is at most ⌊A/N⌋≤ℓ\lfloor A/N\rfloor\le\ell. The descendant denominator [j]![j]! in (15) contributes at most ℓ\ell. Thus every inclusion has pole bound 2ℓ2\ell.

For the projector dual, the exact factorization λC−λD=ζ−C−1(1−ζC−D)(1−ζC+D+2)(22) \lambda_C-\lambda_D =\zeta^{-C-1}(1-\zeta^{C-D})(1-\zeta^{C+D+2}) \qquad\text{(22)} counts all possible vanishing denominators. The allowed DD’s progress in steps of two over a list of length at most ℓN+1\ell N+1. Each of the two congruences in (22) occurs at most ℓ+2\ell+2 times. Each vanishing factor is simple; C−D≠0C-D\ne0 and C+D+2>0C+D+2>0. When both congruences hold they are counted twice. The whole denominator of (18) consequently has valuation at most 2ℓ+42\ell+4, in particular at most 4ℓ+44\ell+4. Its numerator is a product of regular matrices. The factorial ratio in (19) has pole order at most ⌊C/N⌋≤ℓ\lfloor C/N\rfloor\le\ell, and (ΔE)j(\Delta E)^j is regular. Every dual therefore has pole bound 5ℓ+45\ell+4.

All estimates hold for each N≥2N\ge2, with no prime-level or parity assumption. They are valuations, not estimates of the size of the nonzero factors.

5. Complete tree frames and whole physical words

Compose (15) at the vertices of a binary tree, with root color zero and all admissible internal colors retained. Each root vector 1∈V01\in V_0 gives one invariant column. These columns form BTB_T. Compose the duals (19) in the reverse order to obtain the rows of LTL_T. Iterating (20) proves exactly that their columns form a basis of the generic invariant space and LTBT=IL_TB_T=I. There are ℓ−1\ell-1 internal vertices. A finite sum does not worsen a pole bound, while product bounds add. The local estimates therefore prove (4) with the stated Jℓ=(5ℓ+4)(ℓ−1)J_\ell=(5\ell+4)(\ell-1). Conjugating the external leaves by the unit gauge (11) gives the same bounds in the actual physical coordinates.

The lattice Λ\Lambda is intrinsic, not chosen separately by a tree or word. If z∈𝒪gz\in\mathcal O^g, then uJBTzu^JB_Tz is regular and invariant, proving the first inclusion in (5). Conversely, if BTz=ψ∈ΛB_Tz=\psi\in\Lambda, then z=LTψ∈u−J𝒪gz=L_T\psi\in u^{-J}\mathcal O^g, proving the second. This bounds the elementary lattice exponents without inverting a determinant of growing size.

Every whole original physical braid matrix WW and its inverse are regular at q0q_0: each individual physical crossing has finite summation indices below NN, hence only nonvanishing leaf factorial denominators. The actual cups and ordinary dualities are regular as in [S,M]. Regular generic polynomial circle coupons are included as well. Such maps preserve the generic invariant source and hence the saturated lattices. For an endomorphism word, ord⁡u=0(LT′WBT)≥−2J(23) \operatorname{ord}_{u=0}(L_{T'}WB_T)\ge-2J \qquad\text{(23)} entrywise, no matter how many regular letters occur in WW. This is a bound on the whole product, not a sum of the apparent pole orders of sequential recoupling moves.

Between the intrinsic lattices ΛT,ΛT′\Lambda_T,\Lambda_{T'}, the generic change of tree is an isomorphism, with inverse the reverse change. In any actual lattice bases its matrix is unimodular over 𝒪\mathcal O. The generic coordinate matrices before this regularization need not be regular. Their root values must not be substituted for their complete confluence data.

6. The finite jet construction and the literal root closing

Let CT=uJBTC_T=u^JB_T, a regular rectangular matrix. For any ψ∈Λ\psi\in\Lambda, set x=LTψ∈u−J𝒪gx=L_T\psi\in u^{-J}\mathcal O^g and z=uJx∈𝒪gz=u^Jx\in\mathcal O^g. Then CTz=u2Jψ.(24) C_Tz=u^{2J}\psi. \qquad\text{(24)} Truncating zz modulo u2J+1u^{2J+1} does not change any coefficient in (6). Thus every root source vector occurs in its right side. Conversely, a polynomial representative zz satisfying the equations in (6) gives ψ=u−2JCTz∈ℳ𝒪. \psi=u^{-2J}C_Tz\in\mathcal M_{\mathcal O}. It is generically invariant, hence belongs to Λ\Lambda, and its root is the displayed coefficient in (6). This proves that finite formula in both directions. In particular the jet budget is 2J2J for the regular matrix CTC_T, or orders −J,…,J-J,\ldots,J for BTB_T.

Choose gg independent root vectors from (6). Their integral lifts just constructed form a basis of Λ\Lambda: their reductions form a basis of the embedded fiber, so the assertion follows by the elementary local basis criterion over a DVR. This also gives regularized tree frames without choosing an unspecified generic minor. The number of matrix entries and source coordinates still grows with NN; only the required jet order is bounded independently of NN.

For the original cup source bNb_N, and any specified original root co-source row λN\lambda_N, including the literal marked cap, the original scalar remains λNW(q0)bN.(25) \lambda_N W(q_0)b_N . \qquad\text{(25)} By [S,D], bN∈𝒱b_N\in\mathcal V and each regular original gate preserves that source. Formula (6) computes the same space in the actual physical tensor coordinates. One may therefore restrict each root gate to 𝒱\mathcal V and restrict the very same row λN\lambda_N, without changing (25). All cup, pivot, framing and external normalization factors remain in those specified data. No trace average or unmarked cap replaces them.

For a root-value contraction this restriction needs only the actual W(q0)W(q_0) and the frame jets in (6). There is no demand for positive-order jets of a long word merely because its fusion coordinates have poles. An independently prescribed quantum-normal source still requires its own differentiated word and cap data; these are not determined by W(q0)W(q_0).
