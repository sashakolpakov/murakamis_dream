Actual meridian jets on fixed cyclic-dilogarithm Hankel banks

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

The exact moving diagonal frames of F extend the root estimates of W and H to a complex scaled meridian collar. The result concerns the literal highest-cut pivotal source, with its external scalar displayed. The new conclusion is uniform radial-tail control for that family and every fixed true meridian jet. The root banks do not move. W already supplies an exact meridian period; no whole-meridian Euler/Gaussian factorization is used below.

1. Source, scalar, and exact moving frames

Fix N≥2N\ge2, d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, Q=q2Q=q^2, and a signed braid word β\beta on mm heads with cc crossings and writhe ww. Put r=m−1r=m-1, M=eμM=e^\mu, x=−M/qx=-M/q, and

𝒫N(M)=ΞN(M)Tr⁡[(P0⊗Kr(M)1−N)Wβ(M)].(1) \mathcal P_N(M)=\Xi_N(M)\operatorname{Tr} [(P_0\otimes K_{\mathrm{ r}}(M)^{1-N})W_\beta(M)]. \qquad\text{(1)}

Here P0=|0⟩⟨0|P_0=|0\rangle\langle0|, both the first input and output are zero, and ΞN\Xi_N is the prescribed external dimension, framing, and peripheral line. Every crossing in WβW_\beta includes its actual scalar κ(M)±1\kappa(M)^{\pm1}. We remove precisely that scalar and write ℰN(M)=ΞN(M)κ(M)w\mathcal E_N(M)=\Xi_N(M)\kappa(M)^w, 𝒫N=ℰN𝒫̂N\mathcal P_N=\mathcal E_N\widehat{\mathcal P}_N. This defines the hatted source directly from stripped matrices, even at a zero or pole of the external line.

Let Jiwa=awaJ_iw_a=aw_a, fa=(Q;Q)af_a=(Q;Q)_a, and ha(M)=M−afa(M−2Q;Q)a,Dh(M)=diag⁡(ha(M)).(2) h_a(M)=M^{-a}\frac{f_a}{(M^{-2}Q;Q)_a},\qquad D_h(M)=\operatorname{diag}(h_a(M)). \qquad\text{(2)} Put R̂i,0±=κ(1)∓1Ri±(1)\widehat R_{i,0}^{\pm}=\kappa(1)^{\mp1}R_i^{\pm}(1). F(32), with the actual checked flip and inverse order, gives κ(M)−1Ri+(M)=M−Ji−Ji+1Dh,i+1(M)R̂i,0+Dh,i(M)−1,κ(M)Ri−(M)=MJi+Ji+1Dh,i(M)R̂i,0−Dh,i+1(M)−1.(3) \begin{split} \kappa(M)^{-1}R_i^+(M) &=M^{-J_i-J_{i+1}}D_{h,i+1}(M) \widehat R_{i,0}^+D_{h,i}(M)^{-1},\\ \kappa(M)R_i^-(M) &=M^{J_i+J_{i+1}}D_{h,i}(M) \widehat R_{i,0}^-D_{h,i+1}(M)^{-1}. \end{split} \qquad\text{(3)} We insert these factors at their original stages, without cancelling or commuting adjacent frames. All crossing scalars are counted once in ℰN\mathcal E_N; in particular no extra κ(1)w\kappa(1)^w remains.

For 0<η′<π0<\eta'<\pi, M=eu/NM=e^{u/N}, |u|≤η′|u|\le\eta', F(24)–(25) prove, uniformly over the complete occupation range, ∥Dh±1∥≤(N+1)Cη′,∥M±(Ji+Ji+1)∥≤e2η′.(4) \|D_h^{\pm1}\|\le(N+1)^{C_{\eta'}},\qquad \|M^{\pm(J_i+J_{i+1})}\|\le e^{2\eta'}. \qquad\text{(4)} These diagonal bounds hold in the ordinary Euclidean head norm as well as F’s binomial norm: both norms are the maximum of the diagonal entries. The actual coupon has trace norm exactly ∥P0⊗Kr(M)1−N∥1=Nr|M|−rd≤erη′Nr.(5) \|P_0\otimes K_{\mathrm{ r}}(M)^{1-N}\|_1 =N^r|M|^{-rd}\le e^{r\eta'}N^r. \qquad\text{(5)} This is not W’s summed-output first-input-zero coupon. No equality between those coupons after resolvent insertion is asserted.

For each slot orientation ϵ\epsilon and 1≤j<N1\le j<N, set Hϵ,j(ρ)=(ρ−1)−j/N∏k≠j(1−ρQϵ(k−j))−k/N,1<ρ<∞.(9) H_{\epsilon,j}(\rho)=(\rho-1)^{-j/N} \prod_{k\ne j}(1-\rho Q^{\epsilon(k-j)})^{-k/N}, \qquad 1<\rho<\infty. \qquad\text{(9)} The nonendpoint logarithms continue radially from zero; the first power is positive real. 3. Uniform absolute tails and true meridian derivatives

On every bank, a transfer resolvent has norm at most N/ρN/\rho, a direct factorial kernel at most N/ρN/\rho, and an inverse factorial kernel at most 1/ρ1/\rho. Equations (4)–(5) give constants C,BC,B, depending only on the fixed word and η′\eta', such that |ℛN(𝝆,𝒋;u)|≤C(N+1)B∏ℓρℓ−1.(11) |\mathcal R_N(\boldsymbol\rho,\boldsymbol j;u)| \le C(N+1)^B\prod_\ell\rho_\ell^{-1}. \qquad\text{(11)} For instance B=r+6c+2cCη′B=r+6c+2cC_{\eta'} suffices after changing CC. No exponentially conditioned factorial remains in this rational source. No norm of a prefix quotient or a reduced axis trace is used.

Put Gj=|fj|/Nj/N+1/2G_j=|f_j|/N^{j/N+1/2} and cN=(N−1)(N−2)/(4N)c_N=(N-1)(N-2)/(4N). 4. Restoring the original line and the scope of the estimate

For the standard zero-framed raw source, R(23) supplies the exact compensation ΞN=α−w\Xi_N=\alpha^{-w}, α=κx−d\alpha=\kappa x^{-d}. Consequently its line is not an extra hypothesis: ℰN=α−wκw=xdw,sup|u|≤η′|ℰN(eu/N)|≤e|w|η′.(16) \mathcal E_N=\alpha^{-w}\kappa^w=x^{dw},\qquad \sup_{|u|\le\eta'}|\mathcal E_N(e^{u/N})| \le e^{|w|\eta'}. \qquad\text{(16)} Its μ\mu-derivative of order aa is (dw)axdw(dw)^a x^{dw}. Thus (15) holds unconditionally for every fixed meridian jet of this actual zero-framed pointed source. Explicit additional fixed powers of MdM^d and DN(M)/ND_N(M)/N, or their inverses, preserve the conclusion, including any retained polynomial NN-prefactor: F(26) gives DN(eu/N)/N=sinh⁡u/[Nsinh⁡(u/N)]D_N(e^{u/N})/N=\sinh u/[N\sinh(u/N)], a uniformly bounded holomorphic unit with bounded inverse on the buffered collar.

The full original tail is exactly ℰN(eu/N)𝒫̂N,tail\mathcal E_N(e^{u/N})\widehat{\mathcal P}_{N,\mathrm{tail}}. Every true derivative retains the product rule ∂μk𝒫N,tail=∑a=0k(ka)(∂μaℰN)(∂μk−a𝒫̂N,tail).(17) \partial_\mu^k\mathcal P_{N,\mathrm{tail}} =\sum_{a=0}^{k}\binom ka(\partial_\mu^a\mathcal E_N) (\partial_\mu^{k-a}\widehat{\mathcal P}_{N,\mathrm{tail}}). \qquad\text{(17)} If the prescribed line is holomorphic on the buffered collar and sup⁡|u|≤η′|ℰN(eu/N)|≤eo(N)\sup_{|u|\le\eta'}|\mathcal E_N(e^{u/N})|\le e^{o(N)}, the same rate (15) holds for every full source jet by Cauchy. Otherwise (13)–(14),(17) retain its exact size and derivatives; no subexponential bound for an unspecified external line is assumed. A meromorphic line is retained as a Laurent germ, with its prescribed finite part taken only after multiplication; its additional pole and jet orders are not suppressed.

The radial tail becomes exponentially small in absolute size for R>exp⁡(4⋅9cv3/(2π))R>\exp(4\cdot9cv_3/(2\pi)), after any retained subexponential line. This gives neither relative angular localization nor a nonzero closing contraction. The reduced axis trace has an additional prefix coupon and is not covered by (11) merely by renaming the source; an equality after the full axis contraction does not give separate sample bounds or identify geometrically localized densities. Arbitrary inserted marks likewise require their own bounds. The meridian normal is transported by the actual fixed banks; geometric cycle selection and an arbitrary-short-component lower bound remain open.
