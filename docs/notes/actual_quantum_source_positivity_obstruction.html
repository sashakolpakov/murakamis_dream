The original source is a tame nilpotent extension, not a positive trace

28 October 2026. There is a universal, source-specific obstruction to obtaining geometric nonvanishing from an ordinary faithful positive trace on the actual root quantum-group category. Its tensor-closing map sends the identity to zero but a canonical nilpotent cup/cap to the identity. This holds at every level and can be inserted into ANY original knot cut without changing its value.

The source extension is polynomially tame in the actual flat metric. Consequently this is not an obstruction to a later geometric norm comparison or emergent positive integral. It rules out deriving that comparison just from positivity/unitarity of the original categorical trace, or from a positive semisimplification which discards its jets.

1. Keep the actual turns and boundary projector

Put N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2 and x=-q^(-1). Use the original balanced factorial basis w_a. The actual pivot is

g_a=x^(1-N)Q^(-a), sum_a g_a=0, |g_a|=1.          (1)

At this color the dual color x(-1)Q(-1) equals x. The exact actual turns in the bridge-source proof give the cup column p and right-cap row l on V_N tensor V_N:

p_(i,j)=N x^(-j) delta_(i+j,d),
l_(i,j)=x^j g_i/N delta_(i+j,d).                  (2)

These retain the actual N, 1/N and pivot, rather than rescaling the turns to obtain a desired state. Define Pi_N=p l. Then

l p=sum_i g_i=0, Pi_N^2=0, rank Pi_N=1.          (3)

The original pivotal partial closing is

Phi_N(T)=Tr_2[T(I tensor g)].                     (4)

By direct evaluation of (2),

Phi_N(I_(N^2))=0,
Phi_N(Pi_N)=I_N.                                (5)

For the second identity the only retained diagonal term has j=d-i; its coefficient is g_i g_(d-i)=1, using x2=Q(-1). The actual snake normalization is therefore fixed. No zero quantum dimension is divided out and no full peripheral multiplier is replaced by its root value.

On the open cut retain its original e=psi phi, psi_i=1, phi_i=1/N. Thus e is an orthogonal rank-one projector in this flat metric. Define the actual two-head scalar functional

Lambda_N(T)=Tr[e Phi_N(T)].

It has the exact values

Lambda_N(I)=0, Lambda_N(Pi_N)=1.                 (6)

The positive open-cut functional Tr(e C) has not been replaced. The failure of positivity is at the compulsory pivotal tensor-closing map Phi_N, before that boundary functional is applied.

2. No positive realization of that closing map or full source

Suppose Phi_N were positive as a linear map between operator algebras with ANY positive definite Hermitian metrics. Every positive A obeys 0<=A<=r I for some r. Positivity and Phi_N(I)=0 then force 0<=Phi_N(A)<=0. Positive matrices linearly span the whole algebra, so Phi_N would be zero, contradicting Phi_N(Pi_N)=I.

This proves that no change of positive head metric makes the SAME original pivotal closing a positive map. The argument does not assume the flat metric, a particular knot or an asymptotic expansion.

Similarly no positive functional on a unital star algebra can have both values in (6). Cauchy–Schwarz gives

|tau(a)|^2<=tau(I) tau(a^*a)=0

if tau(I)=0. It would vanish on every a. Therefore the restriction of Lambda_N to the unital actual intertwiner algebra cannot be a positive state or trace for any involution respecting that algebra. A global nonzero scalar phase does not repair a zero value on I.

One cannot evade this by first taking a positive spectral corner while keeping its actual nilpotent source. The earlier direct Casimir proof gives, on the actual tensor module,

Pi_N=(-1)^N (q-q^(-1))^(-2)(Omega-c_0)e_0,
c_0=q+q^(-1),                                    (7)

where e_0 is the actual double-node projector. This makes Pi_N a CENTRAL nilpotent in the quantum-group intertwiner algebra. Every positive star realization of a finite-dimensional algebra kills a central nilpotent: its image commutes with its adjoint, hence is a normal nilpotent and is zero. Equivalently Cauchy–Schwarz iterated on the nilpotent selfadjoint Pi_N^* Pi_N proves its positive functional vanishes. This applies even if the corner’s identity has nonzero trace.

Equation (7) is the retained actual confluence, proved in the meridian Casimir-source theorem, not an assumed property of an abstract quantum topology construction. Thus a positive reduced spectral quotient cannot preserve (6). It must retain a source/normal extension outside that ordinary quotient.

3. This obstruction is functorial for EVERY original knot

For ANY finite cut matrix C, not only a scalar intertwiner, linearity of the partial closing and (5) give

Phi_N[(C tensor I)Pi_N]=C,
Lambda_N[(C tensor I)Pi_N]=Tr(e C).               (8)

In particular let C_K be the actual cut operator of a knot with its specified crossings, pivots and framing convention. Its original external line chi_N is retained, so its scalar is

P_(K,N)=chi_N Tr(e C_K)
       =chi_N Lambda_N[(C_K tensor I)Pi_N].      (9)

This is a canonical cup/cap realization of the SAME period for all knots and levels, not an arbitrary inserted coupon chosen to defeat a positivity claim. The actual complete cut is scalar by the already proved original quantum-group intertwining and irreducibility. If C_K=f_(K,N)I, the state in (9) is f_(K,N)Pi_N, a nilpotent multiple. Dropping the nilpotent source would erase its whole original value, not just a dispensable local correction.

This explains why ordinary-color positive traces do not extend by formal faithfulness to the Kashaev color. The exact ordinary-color positive realization has zero quantum dimension at this endpoint; its positive semisimplification kills precisely the object/source which (5)-(9) retain. The separate regular-period theorem correctly uses that positivity only below color N, not for the volume target.

4. Original braiding is also not positively unitarizable

The all-N actual Casimir interpolation retains the nonzero derivative at c_0. If B=R_checked/kappa removes only the recorded common Cartan scalar, its double-node block obeys

e_0(B^2-I)=(-1)^(N+1)(N-1)(q-q^(-1))Pi_N !=0,    (10)

and the right side has square zero. Therefore B^2, hence B, has a nontrivial Jordan block. A unitary matrix in a positive definite metric is diagonalizable. No positive metric and no common phase normalization makes the original checked crossing unitary.

This is an exact all-N consequence of the original R coefficient and the actual cap jet. The derivative interpolation and its direct matrix derivation are already in the Casimir braiding proof and the meridian source proof. We use them with their scalar and parity, rather than assuming a literature unitarity theorem.

5. The necessary extension is tame, but does not give a lower bound

In the actual flat complex root metric, (2) gives exactly

||p||_2=N^(3/2), ||l||_2=N^(-1/2), ||Pi_N||_op=N.

Also ||Phi_N||_(op->op)=N and ||Lambda_N||=N. The upper bounds follow from summing N unitary-pivot diagonal blocks. Equality for Phi uses T=I tensor g^(-1); equality for Lambda uses T=e tensor g^(-1).

Thus the universal source extension costs only polynomial factors, not an unknown exponential normalization. For the assembled scalar cut there is the exact positive norm identity

|P_(K,N)|=|chi_N| ||(C_K tensor I)Pi_N||_op/N.    (11)

Equation (11) is useful normalization information but NOT a new noncancellation mechanism: C_K already contains the original internal pivotal sums. The right side is the same unknown scalar in a canonical nilpotent line. A local geometric amplitude or ambient operator norm does not supply a lower bound for it.

No source-specific broad positivity/nonvanishing theorem has been obtained here. What is decided is that the actual cyclic/Hopf source does not force it through ordinary positive trace, unitarity or a positive reduced torsor. A successful positive geometric comparison must explicitly transport the retained nilpotent/relative source and prove its nonzero geometric image. Emergent positivity of a suitably reduced global integral is not excluded by this result.

Audit: direct actual cups/caps, pivot, Hopf invariance, original full-twist nilpotent, and functorial reconstruction of actual whole-knot cut operators.
