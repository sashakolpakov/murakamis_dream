Diagonal Fejer ports reduce to a true identity smoothing

31 October 2026. diagonal and identity-forest reduction. This keeps the original protected targets and all remaining cross/left/right data. It does not factor arbitrary Green ports across matching/matching digon cuts.

1. Polynomial cost and the fixed-label distinction

At N=2m,d=N-1 the FULL diagonal part of the positive endpoint numerator is exactly

P_diag(b,c)=sum_(i=0)^d (4/a_i)b^i c^(d-i), a_i=4cos^2(pi(i+1/2)/N).

Thus coefficientwise

H_1<=P_diag<=C N^2 H_1, H_1=sum_(i=0)^d b^i c^(d-i). (1)

Here C is absolute. The root at the half-integer sample closest to N/2 gives min a_i=4sin^2(pi/(2N)), so (1) is immediate. A fixed LEFT or RIGHT diagonal label includes only its corresponding half of the i range. For such a label retain ONLY the upper bound by C N^2 H_1. Equality of exponential rates with the full identity kernel is asserted only after summing BOTH actual diagonal types. At fixed diagram size the product cost remains polynomial.

At the limiting entropy level a diagonal port has exactly

q=(0,mu,1-mu,0), E=0,

or its actual reflection. Enlarging its two possible half ranges to all identity loads sigma_b,sigma_c>=0, sigma_b+sigma_c=1 is therefore valid for an upper-bound reduction, without any phase or entropy cost. No remaining one-sided port is changed.

2. Feasibility forces the diagonal middle graph to be a forest

Take any selected set D of diagonal ports in a feasible limiting source flow. Its middle b-c edges lie in the original gap graph. The only possible cycles are the entire internal even-gap cycles, each containing the protected exterior vertex u. If a component has n vertices and at least n edges, its identity edge loads already sum to at least n. Its vertices have total target at most n-1, since it contains u; all remaining local loads are nonnegative. This is impossible. Hence the diagonal middle graph is a forest, and its literal identity smoothing is connected. A tree cannot contain two protected vertices either: its n-1 edge loads would exceed the at most n-2 available targets. Thus each tree has at most ONE protected vertex directly from the load count.

For a tree with n vertices the sum of its internal edge loads is n-1. The total remaining load is therefore1 when all its vertices are ordinary, and0 when it contains a protected vertex. Collapse that actual tree to one face of the same ordinary/protected type. Push all remaining local incidences to their resulting face label. Every remaining port retains its own local exponent vector and E.

Conversely, for any nonnegative remaining loads with the collapsed face target, the lift of the identity loads is UNIQUE. Root a tree at its protected vertex if present, otherwise anywhere. At an edge parent->child let L be the total remaining load in the child subtree. Then assign

sigma_child=1-L, sigma_parent=L. (2)

Since a subtree load lies in[0,1] (or is zero in a protected tree), these are valid identity loads. Summing the subtree balances proves (2), and subtracting them proves every original face equation. No local E or entropy changes in this transport.

The same is an EXACT finite coefficient identity. For an arbitrary remaining polynomial with nonnegative exponents, a surviving monomial has total degree d on each ordinary merged tree and0 on a protected tree. At an internal edge its endpoint identity degrees are

j_child=d-L_degree(subtree), j_parent=L_degree(subtree).

Both lie in[0,d]. Each H_1 coefficient is one, so there is no counting multiplicity. Consequently extracting the original target after inserting all H_1 factors equals extracting the ordinary d targets of the polynomial obtained by IDENTIFYING the face variables in each tree. This is the actual source-preserving identity smoothing, not a formal zero-radius substitution. Combined with (1), it bounds any fixed diagonal sector by the true smoothed remaining positive source with polynomial cost.

3. Two useful exclusions after deleting ALL diagonal ports

Suppose D contains every diagonal port of the particular limiting flow. The resulting white triangular cellulation has NO loop edge. To prove this, choose the mark-free side of a proposed loop at a. If it contains v interior white vertices, Euler counting gives 2v-1 shaded triangles inside. The dual bridge must be MATCHING: the odd number of dual vertices and one matching incidence per vertex force its single crossing edge to be matching. There are v-1 whole retained ports inside and one inside triangle of that bridge port. Its middle is inside; both outer incidences are at a. The v ordinary interior targets sum to v. The whole ports supply at most v-1 there, so the bridge middle must carry the FULL unit, and every other incidence of that port must be zero. It is therefore a diagonal endpoint, contrary to its retention.

Likewise no remaining MATCHING edge joins the two protected merged faces. Both outer loads of that port would be zero. For a one-sided port this forces nu-mu=0, hence diagonal; for a cross port the two middle loads would have to be1/2 each, but its actual finite cross support needs one outer unit. At the limiting functional this is the same full-diagonal load (1/2,1/2), with zero entropy. It can be included in D ONLY for the LIMITING entropy/load reduction: its zero entropy and identity load are the same. This does NOT apply the finite comparison (1) to that cross sector, which still needs its one outer unit. The separate full-kernel entropy theorem supplies the source RATE comparison for these limiting endpoints. The finite inequality in section1 is still not applied to a cross sector. The combinatorial no-protected-matching conclusion therefore concerns the enlarged LIMITING identity reduction when those cross endpoints are included.

The remaining cellulation may have parallel edges and degree-two faces. Those are not excluded by this count and must not be replaced by a simple graph without retaining the actual topology.

4. What decomposes and what still does not

A MAIN/MAIN two-edge cut leaves complete ports on each side. On its mark-free side, the number of internal ordinary targets equals the number of contained ports. Total-degree conservation then forces ALL incidences on its two boundary faces to have zero load. Thus this cut has the same zero-boundary separation as the complete-source angle proof; absolute local entropy constants are unchanged.

A MATCHING/MATCHING cut is different. Two retained Green ports can cross it. Let ell>=0 be the total load that the v-1 whole inside ports send to the two boundary faces. The exact balance is

q_inside,1+q_inside,2=1+ell.

A cross middle is at most1/2, so in the old complete proof this forced ell=0 and both inside middles to equal1/2. General Green ports may have asymmetric middle loads and may also have ell>0. Therefore the old independent block capacity factorization does NOT extend by the same argument. This is an explicit remaining coupling, not a scalar normalization issue or a reason to alter the original support.

A possible way around it is a DIRECT signed developing disk on the loopless triangular cellulation with parallel edges. Its vertex links are still topological circles/intervals, so the signed center holonomy argument should not require simpliciality. Repeated edges then develop to the same circle contact, which the global oriented chain permits. This still requires proving the correct treatment of structurally forced zero degrees and any escaping ordinary multipliers. The original representation must be restored by actual identity meridians at the deleted ports. If their outer product becomes nonparabolic, its recorded flag and the proved zero-volume filling cap must be used; an artificial x/y tangency must NOT be inserted.

The exact reductions above do not yet show that the reduced mixed-E maximum is attained on the regular signed-development stratum, nor bound the remaining coupled matching cuts. No general diagonal-boundary volume inequality is asserted.

The full-kernel companion gives the rate-level CROSS-seam reduction. No independent Green-block factorization across matching cuts is asserted.
