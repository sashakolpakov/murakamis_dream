A faithful boundary packet for the first actual beta move

Written proof, 23 November 2026. The retained cyclic factorial in the first actual nine-cell beta move can be removed with zero error on a faithful boundary packet. For a positive crossing this packet is one complete middle-degree row; for a negative crossing it is one complete middle-degree column. Explicit continuants reconstruct the whole original Hopf box, including every repeated-root normal, from that packet.

Reconstruction is part of the assertion. The ratio-free array alone is not asserted to be the original crossing. After reconstruction the boxes can be substituted in any fixed literal Conway construction, with its original turns, marked closing, pivots and external scalar lines. This does not require a nonzero knot invariant or a semisimple quotient.

The algebraic reconstruction works at every level N≥2N\ge2. Its literal charged nine-cell application has the existing odd-level scope N>2N>2. No positive gluing formula, tame reconstruction norm, geometric source coefficient, or volume lower bound is proved.

5. Exact reconstructed boxes and arbitrary Conway assembly

Theorem. On the regular marked decoration domain, at every odd N>2N>2, the literal ratio-free beta packets satisfy 𝒫+(Y+♭)=M+0,𝒫−(Y−♭)=M−0.(35) \boxed{\qquad \mathcal P_+(Y_+^\flat)=M_+^0,\qquad \mathcal P_-(Y_-^\flat)=M_-^0. \qquad} \qquad\text{(35)} Consequently the full original signed crossings are R+=κ𝒫+(Y+♭),R−=κ−1𝒫−(Y−♭).(36) \boxed{\qquad R_+=\kappa\mathcal P_+(Y_+^\flat),\qquad R_-=\kappa^{-1}\mathcal P_-(Y_-^\flat). \qquad} \qquad\text{(36)} Proof. For clarity, (35) is not an equality Yσ♭=Mσ0|WdY_\sigma^\flat=M_\sigma^0|_{W_d}. Unobserved rows or columns can change upon deletion. Reconstruction is the explicit kernel (17), and is retained once at every replaced crossing.

Now take any fixed literal Conway expression with signed crossing leaves, products in HNH_N, genuine turns JJ, and its original closing row. Replace each signed leaf by the corresponding right side of (36). Induction over the expression shows that every open subtree packet is unchanged: this is true at the leaves by (36), preserved by the same product, and preserved by the same linear turn. No equality for a modified non-Hopf array is inserted in place of (6).

Integer twist leaves can be reconstructed internally and multiplied before substitution into the original expression. Thus arbitrary fixed short powers and all other prescribed powers are included as finite identities at each level. There is no large-twist hypothesis here. This is an exact source adapter, not a statement about their asymptotics.

If reconstruction is moved to an exterior covector, its full bilinear dual must be moved there. It is not removed by a metric change or by replacing that covector with an endpoint row. The same rule applies to the inverse port factors (24). Individual ray-modified crossing patterns need their actual transported co-source from [R]; (36) concerns the original complete crossings, not a new endpoint closing for those individual patterns.
