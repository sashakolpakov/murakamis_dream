A finite certificate for the selected geometric principal phase

Subsequent scope result (30 October): the right-angled obstruction and localization proves that no common all-central selected frame exists for the actual certified right-angled plat cut. Generic geometric strip flags have exactly two strict physical-support failures, at arbitrary bridge width. The local conditional statements below remain valid; their simultaneous realization in that right-angled class cannot supply the general proof.

30 October 2026. For any fixed augmented plat, of arbitrary width, this note gives explicit tests which identify the geometric stationary branch with the source-selected LOCAL q-Hahn principal action. It also expresses the endpoint sheet test as a condition on the two common framing flags. No intersection with the original finite summation cycle is inferred.

The certificate has not been shown to exist for every positive geometric augmented plat. Its point is to replace an unspecified local branch-identification assumption by finite tests on the actual marked representation, with all input/output signs retained.

1. Data and the selected-critical certificate

Fix the actual augmented-plat Morse word, retained half twists, and primitive peripheral marks. Supply:

  1. An actual marked positive complete gluing certificate, as in the geometric certificate theorem. It gives the geometric representation, not an unrelated component of the character equations.
  2. ONE rank-one projector Pi satisfying all the regular-chart, central-support and cup/cap tests of the physical framing criterion. These include the original affine degree equations with the canonical wire logarithms and the actual low/high adapters.
  3. The actual magnetic multiplier at each ordinary column factor. At an input it is Y_geo=S_1/S_2; at an output it is Y_geo=S_2/S_1. The momenta S are reconstructed from the original prefix matrices, not chosen separately to make a test pass.
  4. The selected-root test in section 2 at every input and output, and the original logarithmic/Poisson characters of the full marked phase. The latter are retained as part of the certificate, rather than erased after exponentiation.

These conditions imply the following LOCAL conclusion. The actual geometric critical germ is a germ of the source-selected principal phase. Modulo its two framing directions its magnetic Hessian is nondegenerate. Its fully marked critical action phi has

d(2pi i phi)=1/2 sum V_i dU_i,
2pi Re phi(0)=Vol(Y_D),
phi_(chi_i chi_j)(0)=pi i tau_i delta_(ij),          (1)

with U_i=-2pi i chi_i and the original primitive cusp shapes tau_i. Its ordinary q-Hahn column amplitude factors are nonzero.

The conclusion concerns the actual marked phase and its ordinary column factors. It is NOT a full-source amplitude theorem, a bound for generic normal currents, a continuation of the finite discrete channel, or a claim that the original cycle contributes at this germ.

2. The local sheet test is algebraic on the marked cover

Use the actual low coordinates tau,nu of each column, after applying the high adapter when required. Put

xi=1/2, zeta=exp[pi i(tau-2nu)], c=-cos(pi tau),
W=1+2c zeta, V=1+2c zeta+zeta^2.                   (2)

Here W is a scalar, not a prefix matrix. In the central domain Re c>0, Re zeta>0. Both W/zeta and V/zeta have positive real part; hence W and V avoid the nonpositive real axis. In particular the principal square root of W has strictly positive real part.

The joint original-kernel theorem, section 7, gives the source-selected multiplier

Y_sel=(zeta+i sqrt(W))^2/V.                         (3)

For the actual geometric multiplier define

r_geo=[V Y_geo-zeta^2+W]/(2i zeta).                 (4)

The actual Casimir characteristic equation says r_geo^2=W. Therefore

Y_geo=Y_sel  if and only if  Re r_geo>0.             (5)

This is an equality test for the selected root, not just a test that both values solve the characteristic polynomial. The source theorem fixes the square root by its actual center phase and finite-source continuation.

All expressions in (2)–(5) are algebraic after retaining the marked square root of T=exp(2pi i tau). Its argument is pi Re tau in (pi/2,pi), which fixes the square-root sign; zeta=T^(1/2)/U with U=exp(2pi i nu). Thus for an algebraic marked representation and projector the endpoint root test is a finite real-algebraic test. The logarithmic characters of the action remain additional marked data, not a consequence of squaring (4).

3. Exact flag formulas, including the output sign and high adapter

Normalize one actual incoming pair to

A=-[[1,1],[0,1]], B=-[[1,0],[-4,1]],
P=B A, H=P+I.

Write Pi=v ell, ell(v)=1. In these disk coordinates let w be the kernel flag of ell and let z be the image flag W_prefix v. This use of the actual prefix is essential. Normalize the local rank-one matrix W_prefix Pi by its trace; it has image z and kernel w.

In affine coordinates a representative of the local common frame is

g=[[1,-w],[1,-z]].

Direct substitution into the original head and prefix formulas gives

U_phys=(z-w)/(z-w+1),
T_phys=(z-w)/(4wz+3w+z+1),
S_1/S_2=-w/(z+1).                                  (6)

The source internal root in a low column is obtained from the actual inverse, at J=-1,

Z=-U[y(T-U)+(1-U)]/[-y(T-U)+T(1-U)].                (7)

For the input use y=S_1/S_2; for the output use its RECIPROCAL. Equations (6),(7) give

Z_in,low=(2w+1)/(2w-1),
Z_out,low=(2z+1)/(2z+3).                            (8)

For the high adapter one must first replace

T_low=T_phys^(-1), U_low=U_phys/T_phys.

Keeping the same actual input/output momentum convention in (7) then gives

Z_in,high=-(2z+1)/(2z+3),
Z_out,high=-(2w+1)/(2w-1).                          (9)

These are rational identities, not deductions from a root-only symmetry. They retain the output sign of a kernel and the actual high-degree translation.

4. The endpoint selected sheets are oriented disk sides

For any strict central triple, its two local framing flags z,w are on the SAME open side of the real disk circle, by section 5 of the physical framing criterion. The two roots of the local characteristic give the two opposite choices of that side.

One can see the latter directly from (6). Fix U_phys,T_phys and put d=U_phys/(1-U_phys)=z-w. The equation for w is quadratic; its roots have sum -(d+1). Consequently its two flag pairs are related by

(z,w) -> (-w-1,-z-1).                              (10)

They therefore lie on opposite sides of the real circle. There is no collision or boundary crossing in the central domain. For example T+1-U cannot vanish there: arg T is in (pi,2pi), arg(1+T) is strictly between arg T and 2pi, while the strict physical U argument lies between 0 and arg T. The other regular root denominators are excluded by the regular-chart assumptions. The simple characteristic and the connected central domain preserve the two sheet labels.

At a strict physical real point, the actual selected internal root Z is in the upper half of the unit disk, as proved by the original q-Hahn selection theorem. Equation (8) then gives w below the real circle for an input, and z above it for an output. Equation (9) gives the SAME respective side choices in the high adapter. Together with the same-side necessity, continuation through the central domain therefore proves the equivalent tests

selected input  <=> Im z<0 and Im w<0,
selected output <=> Im z>0 and Im w>0,              (11)

where each line uses that factor’s OWN actual disk normalization. The inequalities in (11), rather than an upper-half-disk assertion for Z at arbitrary complex parameters, are what continue globally. They agree with (5); either supplies the same endpoint sheet test.

5. A global strip-intersection question remains

For one actual complete coupon its transport is C=I+bH. The primitive mark fixes b-epsilon/2=1/tau_cusp, so Im b<0 for the positive geometric orientation. In the incoming disk coordinate set

eta(s)=1/(2s+1).

Direct substitution gives eta(C s)=eta(s)-2b. The incoming condition in (11) becomes Im eta>0; the outgoing condition, in the disk frame C^(-1), becomes Im(eta+2b)<0. Thus, CONDITIONAL ON the strict central inequalities, both input and output are selected exactly when

0<Im eta(w)<-2 Im b,
0<Im eta(W_prefix v)<-2 Im b.                       (12)

This is the actual oriented region between the two developed disk circles. Positivity of the primitive cusp orientation gives its positive height. It does not prove that all such regions meet.

Let Omega_f denote this region in the one global flag sphere, using the actual input disk normalization at coupon f. Formula (12) gives the necessary separated global conditions

w in intersection_f Omega_f,
v in intersection_f W_f^(-1) Omega_f.               (13)

Once the coupled central tests also hold, (13) is sufficient for ALL endpoint local sheet tests. This is a finite geometric version of the certificate. It identifies the precise role of the actual prefix pullbacks; independent local flags do not establish (13).

No general identification of these intersections with exteriors of polyhedral face disks is asserted here. Such an identification would need the actual based words and both disk copies, as well as the coupled central inequalities. The common-product feasibility lemma in the preceding framing note concerns support only; it does not by itself supply these selected input/output sheets.

6. Proof of the certificate and precise persistence

The sheet test identifies each actual geometric magnetic multiplier with the selected local multiplier. The regular inverse (7) then identifies its internal root with the source-selected root, including the high adapter. The original full coupon phase, its factorial measure, external phases and marked cap characters are retained. Fixing the stipulated logarithmic/Poisson characters gives the actual marked principal phase, rather than merely its exponentiated equations.

The critical-scheme inverse now identifies this germ with the actual marked geometric representation germ modulo the diagonal scale. The positive multi-cusp gluing rigidity theorem makes its only magnetic null directions the two framing directions. A transverse framing slice therefore has nondegenerate magnetic Hessian. The same theorem’s classical action corollary, using the primitive collar differential and the absolute endpoint regulator, gives exactly (1). No new period normalization is introduced by the test.

The joint column theorem gives, for actual integer channels with real xi_N=j/N near 1/2, the nonzero ordinary amplitude

fhat=N^(-1/2) B_(xi_N)(tau,nu)
                exp[N Phi_(xi_N)(tau,nu)](1+O(1/N)).

A fixed finite product of such ordinary factors has the product estimate on a compact common domain. This supplies nonzero ordinary column amplitude germs at the certified branch; it supplies neither generic normal-factor estimates nor a full-source saddle coefficient.

All strict central inequalities and (5) persist under small marked peripheral deformation. The unramified geometric germ and transverse critical slice then continue the certified CLASSICAL selected branch. The selected coefficient functions also continue holomorphically in xi near 1/2. This applies to sufficiently long geometric fillings, with their original primitive marks. It does not continue the finite discrete index j to a complex value or justify a channel contour.

The remaining global tasks are substantive: produce the certificate for a broad geometric class, control all required normal currents, and prove that the original finite cycle has the required contribution and global exponential bound. This note resolves none of those tasks merely by having a stationary point with the correct volume.

Audit: exact original-head, adapter and strip identities.

The certificate distinguishes classical continuation from a finite-channel or whole-source asymptotic. Its general existence and the existence of a contributing cycle are not established here.
