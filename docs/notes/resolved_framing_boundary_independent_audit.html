Independent audit of the resolved framing and Gaussian boundary residue

Audit, 27 October 2026. Sections 2–4.1 of springer_centralizer_boundary_source.md and Sections 3–6 of compact_framing_gauge_and_boundary_residue.md pass this independent global-transition, normalization and scope check. No tests were rerun.

1. The actual framing torsor and its nonextendible centralizer

The printed sections conjugate E12E_{12} to the two Springer matrices. Their ratio is gN−1gS=1v(1−1/(vη)01). g_N^{-1}g_S=\frac1v \begin{pmatrix}1&-1/(v\eta)\\0&1\end{pmatrix}. Thus equality of the right-centralizer framings gives bS=bN+1/(vη)b_S=b_N+1/(v\eta), with the printed sign.

The fibre-degree argument proves a genuine obstruction. At degree −1-1, a coboundary has powers v≥0v^{\ge0} and v≤−2v^{\le-2}; it cannot supply v−1v^{-1}. At every nonnegative fibre degree, the north and south powers cover all Laurent powers, so the whole line bundle has no corresponding additive extension class. The elementary affine additive-descent calculation supplies the stated chart trivializations. This proves nonextension as an ordinary algebraic additive torsor; no asymptotic argument is involved.

Renormalization a=−ηba=-\eta b gives exactly aS=v−v2aNa_S=v-v^2a_N. The invertible matrix g=(η−aηv1−va),det⁡g=η, g=\begin{pmatrix}\eta&-a\\\eta v&1-va\end{pmatrix}, \qquad\det g=\eta, recovers every projective frame on η≠0\eta\ne0; its south representative is −v-v times its north representative. Hence Y\EexcY\setminus E_{\mathrm{exc}} is all PGL2PGL_2, not only a birational open chart. At the boundary the matrix has rank one, the second flag is transverse to the retained first-column direction, and the original constant centralizer action vanishes. The line-bundle-valued translated action remains free, as stated.

The elementary-modification lattice keeps an abstract noncentral meridian. It does not give a regular raw irreducible representation with central meridian. The separate escape argument is correct: irreducibility gives C0≠0C_0\ne0, while Tr⁡[μ0,ρ(g)]=2+C02\operatorname{Tr}[\mu_0,\rho(g)]=2+C_0^2. A regular raw limit with μ→I\mu\to I would contradict this constant commutator trace. The two displayed escape matrices have the stated tc−1/2t_c^{-1/2} and (1−tc)−1/2(1-t_c)^{-1/2} terms.

The logarithmic section r*=1−log⁡A/(2πi)r_*=1-\log A/(2\pi i) has the stated deck change −1-1. The opposite sign of the absolute frame coordinate (1−r)/s(1-r)/s is explicitly retained.

The exceptional divisor over all of XX and the physical exponential section are different objects. On the latter, η=(1−e−2πiva)/v\eta=(1-e^{-2\pi iva})/v; its intersections with the divisor are the integer vava fibres, with the removable special rule at v=0v=0. The real compact physical cycle meets it at its two endpoint poles. This does not identify that physical cycle with the compact cycle used to pair the divisor residue.

The notes correctly make no such identification. They also do not extend all higher Gaussian coefficients or unrestricted logarithmic Green currents. The polynomial coherent clutch and its line weights are intrinsic source data; they are not asserted to be the actual quantum dressing of every geometric frame.
