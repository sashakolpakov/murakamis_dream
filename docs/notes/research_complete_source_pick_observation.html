The complete source has an exponentially small fixed-time observation

Research proof, 3 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

The positive crossing form can be tested on the specified complete-end source, rather than on an arbitrarily chosen vector. For every fixed positive crossing horizon, its observation energy is exponentially smaller than the squared norm of that source. The loss comes from an alternating physical-value observation. The complete source is concentrated near the complete color, but that concentration does not give a lower bound for this observation.

Sections 1–4 give the upper bound for every fixed positive real crossing horizon. Sections 5–6 determine the nonzero leading term of the observation and its positive energy for every fixed real horizon at least three. These are statements about the specified complete source. The original signed internal-pair closure is a different bilinear observation.

7. Complex colors and the exact normal defect
