Literal port gluing and the complete geometric holonomy anchor

At completeness, every white AND shaded face map agrees with the explicit four-antiprism geometry. Thus its base representation is the oriented geometric holonomy, not merely a character with the same cusp Hessian or a representation with similar trace equations.

This note uses the actual stationary identities in the local mixed-character lift. It does not infer a source cycle or a quantum asymptotic from those identities; those were proved separately from the finite source. The lift below is a DIRECT algebraic construction: its group relations, framed peripheral eigenvalues and cap equalities are verified explicitly. It does not require a further assertion identifying the canonical state graph of an arbitrary scalar operator F(Ω)F(\Omega) from its unwrapped eigenphase. In particular a state-versus-observable Hurwitz convention is not silently used to choose between UU and U−1U^{-1}.
