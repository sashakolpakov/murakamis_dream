A source-selected conserved channel and physical Gaussian compression

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof.

The actual cups of an even plat select one conserved channel exactly. At its source-selected parameter the complete conserved spectrum remains simple at every level, with controlled projectors and a known nonzero normalization of zero volume rate. Inside the selected channel its orthogonalizing metric is exactly the original physical metric.

There is also a quantitative estimate on the previously constructed physical source spaces: a short operator block supported on at most half the heads retains its entire Hilbert–Schmidt norm up to an explicit power of the level. Thus a proved whole-block Hilbert–Schmidt model gives a relative operator model on the actual source, without an inverse fusion Gram. The original closing remains. These results do not give a relative scalar error or the hyperbolic value of the fixed-degree minimum.

1. The original source and the spaces being restricted

Put N≥2N\ge2, d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, Q=q2Q=q^2, x=−q−1x=-q^{-1}, and take b=2k≥2b=2k\ge2 physical heads. Use the orthonormal balanced basis of H and G. Its actual complete generators are Kea=xQ−aea,Eea=(Q−a−1)ea−1,Fea=(1−Qa+1)ea+1,(1) Ke_a=xQ^{-a}e_a,\quad Ee_a=(Q^{-a}-1)e_{a-1},\quad Fe_a=(1-Q^{a+1})e_{a+1}, \qquad\text{(1)} with the two missing arrows zero. Here EE is the scaled generator. Totals use ΔE=E⊗K+1⊗E\Delta E=E\otimes K+1\otimes E, ΔF=F⊗1+K−1⊗F\Delta F=F\otimes1+K^{-1}\otimes F, and 𝖪=K1⋯Kb\mathsf K=K_1\cdots K_b.

The literal ordinary cup, ordinary cap and marked cap are, by H and P, puv=Nx−vδu+v,d,ℓuv=xuδu+v,d/N,muv=xv/N2.(2) p_{uv}=Nx^{-v}\delta_{u+v,d},\qquad \ell_{uv}=x^u\delta_{u+v,d}/N,\qquad m_{uv}=x^v/N^2. \qquad\text{(2)} The last has no degree delta. Keep the actual order of the paired heads, and put u0=p⊗ku_0=p^{\otimes k}. Let ℓ0\ell_0 be the tensor of the original k−1k-1 ordinary top caps and the one full marked cap. For the actual unmarked signed braid operator RR, the scalar is 𝒦N=χNℓ0Ru0.(3) \mathcal K_N=\chi_N\ell_0Ru_0. \qquad\text{(3)} All original turns, dual identifications, pivots and normalizations are in these data; χN\chi_N is the unchanged external scalar. No bound for it is inferred in an unreduced convention. The standard complete dual identification makes these the common total operators (1), not an unauthorized permutation of physical wires.

Let ℋ=VN⊗b\mathcal H=V_N^{\otimes b}, with its physical norm, and define T=kd,ℋT=span⁡{|a⟩:∑ai=T},ℐ=ℋT∩ker⁡𝖤∩ker⁡𝖥.(4) T=kd,\quad \mathcal H_T=\operatorname{span}\{|a\rangle:\sum a_i=T\}, \qquad \mathcal I=\mathcal H_T\cap\ker\mathsf E\cap\ker\mathsf F. \qquad\text{(4)} This is H’s full root invariant space. Let 𝒮⊂ℐ\mathcal S\subset\mathcal I be the saturated specialization of the generic invariant lattice in U. It can be smaller than ℐ\mathcal I. U and D already prove that the original cup source and its regular braid orbit lie in 𝒮\mathcal S, with the literal closing retained. They also give stronger invariant-space dimension reductions than the conserved channel used next.

2. The parameter selected by the cups is spectrally regular

Use C’s conserved operators Bc=𝖥−(𝖤−cI)𝖪−1,Hc=Bc−2I,pN(z)=2iTN(z2q),q=eπi/(2N).(5) B_c=\mathsf F-(\mathsf E-cI)\mathsf K^{-1},\qquad H_c=B_c-2I, \quad p_N(z)=2iT_N\!\left(\frac{z}{2\sqrt q}\right), \quad \sqrt q=e^{\pi i/(2N)}. \qquad\text{(5)} Here TNT_N is Chebyshev of the first kind. The auxiliary parameter is now c=2c=2. It is distinct from the eigenvalue 22 being selected. C proves on the full head space pN(Bc)=γbpN(c)Ip_N(B_c)=\gamma^bp_N(c)I, γ=(−1)N+1\gamma=(-1)^{N+1}, with exact companion form and minimal polynomial of degree NN. Since bb is even, the minimal polynomial at this parameter is pN(z)−pN(2)p_N(z)-p_N(2).

Here are estimates at c=2c=2, outside C’s exterior-parameter range. Set θ=π2N,s=sin⁡θ,α=arsinh⁡s,β=arcsin⁡s,w=eα−iβ.(6) \theta=\frac{\pi}{2N},\quad s=\sin\theta,\quad \alpha=\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt s,\quad \beta=\arcsin\sqrt s,\quad w=e^{\alpha-i\beta}. \qquad\text{(6)} Then w+w−1=2/qw+w^{-1}=2/\sqrt q exactly: its half-sum has real part 1+s1−s=cos⁡θ\sqrt{1+s}\sqrt{1-s}=\cos\theta and imaginary part −s-s. Also (2N)−1/2≤α≤π/(2N),Nα≥1,|wN−w−N|≥(1−e−2)eN/2.(7) (2N)^{-1/2}\le\alpha\le\sqrt{\pi/(2N)},\quad N\alpha\ge1, \quad |w^N-w^{-N}|\ge(1-e^{-2})e^{\sqrt{N/2}}. \qquad\text{(7)} Indeed N−1≤sin⁡θ≤π/(2N)N^{-1}\le\sin\theta\le\pi/(2N) and z/2≤arsinh⁡z≤zz/\sqrt2\le\operatorname{arsinh}z\le z for 0≤z≤10\le z\le1.

The roots are λj=q(wQj+w−1Q−j)\lambda_j=\sqrt q(wQ^j+w^{-1}Q^{-j}), 0≤j<N0\le j<N, with λ0=2\lambda_0=2. For vj=wQjv_j=wQ^j, Chebyshev differentiation gives |pN′(λj)|=N|wN−w−N||vj−vj−1|≥1−e−222NeN/2.(8) |p_N'(\lambda_j)| =N\frac{|w^N-w^{-N}|}{|v_j-v_j^{-1}|} \ge\frac{1-e^{-2}}{2\sqrt2}N e^{\sqrt{N/2}}. \qquad\text{(8)} The denominator is at most 2cosh⁡α≤222\cosh\alpha\le2\sqrt2. Thus every root is simple, at every NN, without a parity or prime-level exception. C gives its full eigenspace rank Nb−1N^{b-1}. The Lagrange spectral projectors satisfy ∥Πj∥≤(4b+2+22)N−1[(1−e−2)/(22)]NeN/2,G2=∑jΠj*Πj,N−1I≤G2≤eCbNI.(9) \|\Pi_j\|\le \frac{(4b+2+2\sqrt2)^{N-1}} {[(1-e^{-2})/(2\sqrt2)]N e^{\sqrt{N/2}}},\qquad G_2=\sum_j\Pi_j^*\Pi_j,\quad N^{-1}I\le G_2\le e^{C_bN}I. \qquad\text{(9)} Here ∥B2∥≤4b+2\|B_2\|\le4b+2, and each |λj|≤22|\lambda_j|\le2\sqrt2. The denominator in the projector product is |pN′(λj)||p_N'(\lambda_j)|. There is no product-of-nearest-gaps Nlog⁡NN\log N loss.

Write ℰ2=ker⁡(B2−2I)\mathcal E_2=\ker(B_2-2I). Its exact projector is Π2=rN(B2)pN′(2),rN(z)=pN(z)−pN(2)z−2.(10) \Pi_2=\frac{r_N(B_2)}{p_N'(2)},\qquad r_N(z)=\frac{p_N(z)-p_N(2)}{z-2}. \qquad\text{(10)} The subscript denotes the eigenvalue, and rNr_N is a polynomial; no inverse of B2−2IB_2-2I occurs. All complex phases of the derivative are retained. Its modulus has the explicit calibration |pN′(2)|=N|wN−w−N|22sin⁡θ,log⁡|pN′(2)|=πN/2+32log⁡N+O(1).(11) |p_N'(2)|=\frac{N|w^N-w^{-N}|}{2\sqrt{2\sin\theta}},\qquad \log|p_N'(2)|=\sqrt{\pi N/2}+\tfrac32\log N+O(1). \qquad\text{(11)} For the first formula use |w−w−1|2=8sin⁡θ|w-w^{-1}|^2=8\sin\theta. For the second use α=θ+O(θ3/2)\alpha=\sqrt\theta+O(\theta^{3/2}) and |wN−w−N|=eNα(1+O(e−2Nα))|w^N-w^{-N}|=e^{N\alpha}(1+O(e^{-2N\alpha})). In particular this known nonzero projector normalization has zero exponential rate after division of its logarithm by NN.

3. Exact source selection and the physical norm

The ordinary cup obeys E12p=F12p=0E_{12}p=F_{12}p=0, K12p=pK_{12}p=p. These follow termwise from (1)–(2), including the zero arrows; H and P already establish them for the original turns. Tensoring gives 𝖤u0=𝖥u0=0,𝖪u0=u0,B2u0=2u0,𝒦N=χNℓ0Π2RΠ2u0.(12) \mathsf E u_0=\mathsf F u_0=0,\quad\mathsf K u_0=u_0, \quad B_2u_0=2u_0,\quad \mathcal K_N=\chi_N\ell_0\Pi_2R\Pi_2u_0. \qquad\text{(12)} Every actual unmarked signed crossing intertwines the totals. Thus no other coideal channel contributes to this source. This selection is not inferred from a preferred geometric eigenvalue. It is an exact consequence of the original incoming cups. In particular erH2/hu0=u0e^{rH_2/h}u_0=u_0 and erH2/hRu0=Ru0e^{rH_2/h}Ru_0=Ru_0 for every finite rr, where h=2πi/Nh=2\pi i/N. This removes a redundant finite gauge direction; it constructs no nontrivial relative geometric transport. The eigenvalue alone does not select the discrete-faithful critical branch.

For v∈ℰ2v\in\mathcal E_2, Π2v=v\Pi_2v=v and the other projectors kill it. Hence ⟨G2v,v⟩=∥v∥2\langle G_2v,v\rangle=\|v\|^2: the channel metric is exactly the original physical norm on that whole space. Moreover 𝒮⊂ℐ=ℰ2∩ℋT.(13) \mathcal S\subset\mathcal I =\mathcal E_2\cap\mathcal H_T. \qquad\text{(13)} Indeed 𝖪=1\mathsf K=1 on ℋT\mathcal H_T, so (B2−2)v=𝖥v−𝖤v(B_2-2)v=\mathsf Fv-\mathsf Ev there. Its two terms have different integer degrees T+1,T−1T+1,T-1, even at N=2N=2, and must vanish separately. This does not identify 𝒮\mathcal S with the larger root invariant space, or identify Π2\Pi_2 with an orthogonal projection onto their intersection.

4. A physical norm test for an entire short block

Consider a contiguous block of 1≤r≤k1\le r\le k heads. Let ANA_N be a regular original integer-degree-preserving Hopf-intertwining endomorphism supported on this block, such as a fixed unmarked signed braid block. Its generic lift is required when using 𝒮\mathcal S. Let ANglobA_N^{\mathrm{ glob}} include the spectator identities. For each 𝒯∈{𝒮,ℐ,ℰ2}\mathcal T\in\{\mathcal S,\mathcal I,\mathcal E_2\}, N−r/2∥AN∥HS≤∥ANglob|𝒯∥op≤∥AN∥op.(14) \boxed{\quad N^{-r/2}\|A_N\|_{\mathrm{ HS}} \le\|A_N^{\mathrm{ glob}}|_{\mathcal T}\|_{\mathrm{ op}} \le\|A_N\|_{\mathrm{ op}}. \quad} \qquad\text{(14)} These are physical operator norms. Every indicated space is preserved.

Here is an original-source witness for the lower bound. Let L,RL,R be the outside head counts, L+R=2k−rL+R=2k-r. Choose an integer max⁡(0,r−R)≤a≤min⁡(r,L),a≡L(mod⁡2).(15) \max(0,r-R)\le a\le\min(r,L),\qquad a\equiv L\pmod2. \qquad\text{(15)} Such an integer exists: if the interval contains consecutive integers both parities occur; if it is a singleton, 2k−2r2k-2r even forces the stated parity. Pair the first aa block heads, nested in order, to the closest left outside heads. Pair the remaining block heads nested to the closest right outside heads. Fill the even leftover outside intervals by adjacent ordinary cups. This is a noncrossing matching made from actual cups, not a free physical permutation.

Normalize this tensor to a unit vector vv. It has a regular generic cup lift, so v∈𝒮v\in\mathcal S. Every block head has its own outside partner. Since every cup coefficient has constant modulus, the block reduced density of vv is exactly I/NrI/N^r. Thus ∥ANglobv∥2=∥AN∥HS2/Nr\|A_N^{\mathrm{ glob}}v\|^2=\|A_N\|_{\mathrm{ HS}}^2/N^r, proving (14). All cup phases and normalizations were used before normalization. This applies to a whole supported short block, not merely one entry.

Let Q𝒯Q_{\mathcal T} denote the physical orthogonal projector onto one of these spaces. A supplied whole-block model DND_N gives ∥ANglob|𝒯−Q𝒯DNglob|𝒯∥op≤∥AN−DN∥HS.(16) \|A_N^{\mathrm{ glob}}|_{\mathcal T} -Q_{\mathcal T}D_N^{\mathrm{ glob}}|_{\mathcal T}\|_{\mathrm{ op}} \le\|A_N-D_N\|_{\mathrm{ HS}}. \qquad\text{(16)} Only the pair/block error is used; spectator identities have operator norm one. If AN≠0A_N\ne0, a relative Hilbert–Schmidt error εN\varepsilon_N therefore becomes a relative operator error at most Nr/2εNN^{r/2}\varepsilon_N. The model itself need not preserve the source before this orthogonal compression. A whole-block approximation must actually be proved; it is not supplied by (14).

5. The full physical crossing Gaussian on the source

For b≥4b\ge4, use (14) with r=2r=2 and either signed crossing. For every fixed integer J≥0J\ge0, G’s zero-tilt full physical model 𝒢N,J±\mathcal G_{N,J}^{\pm}, with all exact entry phases and support retained, obeys ∥R±−𝒢N,J±∥HS≤CJN−(J+1)/2∥R±∥HS.(17) \|R^{\pm}-\mathcal G_{N,J}^{\pm}\|_{\mathrm{ HS}} \le C_JN^{-(J+1)/2}\|R^{\pm}\|_{\mathrm{ HS}}. \qquad\text{(17)} Consequently its source orthogonal compression has relative operator error at most CJN−(J−1)/2C_JN^{-(J-1)/2}, independently of the spectator width. One may instead first project the pair model orthogonally onto the full physical algebra ℂ[Ω]\mathbb C[\Omega], as G proves. This contracts the error. The resulting polynomial fN(Ω)f_N(\Omega) has a regular generic lift using the same coefficients and the generic Casimir. It therefore preserves all three source spaces, including 𝒮\mathcal S, without a subsequent global projection.

No Casimir value quotient is used: its first-normal channels remain. There is no fusion-coordinate conditioning or spectator Hilbert–Schmidt factor in this relative operator estimate. In particular (14) and G give log⁡∥Ri±|𝒯∥op=2GCatπN+Ob(log⁡N).(18) \log\|R_i^{\pm}|_{\mathcal T}\|_{\mathrm{ op}} =\frac{2G_{\mathrm{ Cat}}}\pi N+O_b(\log N). \qquad\text{(18)} Here GCat=∑a≥0(−1)a/(2a+1)2G_{\mathrm{ Cat}}=\sum_{a\ge0}(-1)^a/(2a+1)^2 is Catalan’s constant. This Gaussian assertion is at sufficiently large levels for each fixed order. At two heads the source remains exact but this two-head norm comparison is not asserted. The models are not declared exact inverses or a new braid representation.

6. The literal closing still has to observe the propagated vector

There are simple polynomial bounds in the same physical metric: ∥u0∥=N3k/2,N−3k/2≤∥ℓ0|𝒯∥≤N−(k+2)/2,𝒯=𝒮,ℐ.(19) \|u_0\|=N^{3k/2},\qquad N^{-3k/2}\le\|\ell_0|_{\mathcal T}\| \le N^{-(k+2)/2},\qquad \mathcal T=\mathcal S,\mathcal I. \qquad\text{(19)} For the upper bound ordinary caps force degree dd on their pairs. On the central degree kdkd, the last marked pair then also has degree dd. Its restricted row has norm N−3/2N^{-3/2}, and the ordinary rows contribute N−(k−1)/2N^{-(k-1)/2}. This is P’s permitted whole-degree restriction, not a replacement of an arbitrary marked cap.

For the lower bound use the actual cyclic noncrossing cup matching (1,2k),(2,3),(4,5),…,(2k−2,2k−1)(1,2k),(2,3),(4,5),\ldots,(2k-2,2k-1). It has a regular generic lift, norm N3k/2N^{3k/2}, and its literal pairing with ℓ0\ell_0 is exactly one. Indeed all odd indices become one index aa, all even indices d−ad-a; the monomial powers in (2) cancel, and each of the NN remaining summands is 1/N1/N. This proves (19) on both source spaces. Thus the bare linear root row A↦ℓ0Au0A\mapsto\ell_0Au_0 on End⁡(𝒯)\operatorname{End}(\mathcal T) has Hilbert–Schmidt dual norm between 11 and Nk−1N^{k-1}. If χN\chi_N is included in that row, multiply both bounds by |χN||\chi_N|.

H and D already supply stronger source restrictions and polynomial boundary estimates in their stated frames. The additions here are the source-selected spectrally regular coideal channel and the quantitative physical block/Gaussian norm test. They can supply known budgets to an existing fixed-degree compiler; no new capacity identity is needed.

The remaining scalar is still exactly χNℓ0Ru0\chi_N\ell_0Ru_0. A lower bound for a row norm gives no lower bound for its value on this particular propagated vector. Neither (16) nor an all-order local relative operator model gives a relative scalar error after an arbitrary word and closing. The geometric value and noncancellation after fixed short partial fillings remain unproved. Fixed polynomial degree bounds neither the growing source dimension nor this missing precision. All results here are complete-root values, not off-root normal jets.
