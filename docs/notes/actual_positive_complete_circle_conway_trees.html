Positive whole sources for actual complete-circle Conway trees

29 October 2026. This gives an explicit family of WHOLE original sources closed under arbitrary finite binary Conway composition and genuine quarter-turns. Every circle color and every root normal is retained. For the robust seed below every such source is strictly positive at every level N>=3, at either parity and at composite levels.

This is a source theorem, not a hyperbolic-volume theorem. A Conway seam is a four-punctured sphere, which has moduli; its hyperbolic energy cannot be added by the rigid thrice-punctured-sphere argument used for belt sums. No geometric realization, bridge-number lower bound, or noncancellation result for an arbitrary primitive graph is inferred.

1. Compile a literal four-port ribbon diagram

Use the original physical V_N, its cup p, ordinary cap ell and unrestricted marked cap m. Put d=N-1. A box has two incoming lower and two outgoing upper main ribbon ports. Its auxiliary complete-circle coupon is the ACTUAL threaded loop L_N: the original four mixed crossings [2,1,1,2], the original auxiliary pivot, and its ordinary auxiliary closure. No additional factor 1/N is attached to that loop. Its literal normalization was proved in the crossing-circle source theorem.

Define operations on actual boxes as follows.

  • Product: stack two boxes, connecting their two matching intermediate main ribbons by identity wires. The operator is ordinary composition.

  • Turn: rotate a box through a quarter-turn, retaining its four oriented boundary ribbons by bending with the SAME p and ell. On its matrix A, in the all-primal recorded chart, this is

    (Rturn A)_((u,b);(v,e))
    =p(d-e)/p(d-u) A_((d-u,v);(b,d-e)).        (1)

    At the root p(d-r)=N x^(-r), x=q^d, so the factor is x^(u-e). This is not a free leg permutation or an added physical crossing.

These two constructions are precisely the vertical and turned four-port Conway operations on a planar ribbon tangle. Boundary dual objects are transported by the displayed original snakes; one does not silently replace a bend by an identity. A parenthesized expression therefore specifies an actual tangle recursively, including its port order, orientation and all auxiliary circles. No scalar three-port Frobenius vertex is introduced at a branching node.

Close a resulting box by the original one-cut closure phi: close one main object by its original ordinary pivotal trace, and point the remaining physical component with m. In matrices this is

phi(A)=(1/N)Tr[(I tensor g)A],
g=K^(1-N)=(-1)^d K.                            (2)

The partial pivotal trace of a Hopf box is scalar on the simple physical V_N; evaluating its remaining cup with m p=1 gives (2). Thus (2) retains the actual one unrestricted mark. For an expression in the pair Casimir algebra its exact scalar is the established original Frobenius functional. Any external line/framing conversion factors remain those of the original diagram; they are not altered or suppressed here.

2. A robust all-channel seed

Use the FULL Newton algebra and basis of the positive multiplication theorem:

H_N=C[z]/(2T_N(z/2)+2),
Bhat_r=product_(j<r)(c_j-z)/s_r^2, 0<=r<=d,
c_j=2cos((2j+1)pi/N),
s_r=product_(u=1)^r 2sin(pi u/N), M=floor(d/2).

The physical operations and closure are

Rturn Bhat_r=Bhat_(d-r),
phi(sum_r a_r Bhat_r)=a_d.                    (3)

The full complete circle, including its paired nilpotents, has

h_N=Rturn L_N=sum_(v=0)^M a_v Bhat_v,
a_v=N sum_(j=v)^M k_j s_(j+v)/[s_(j-v)s_v^2]>0,
k_j=2 at a paired node, 1 at the odd simple node. (4)

Set the ACTUAL seed box

A_N=h_N h_N h_N.                              (5)

It contains THREE separate complete-circle coupons, each turned by the literal operation (1); it is not a cube of L_N on the same unturned pair. This distinction is essential: L_N^2=0 at even N.

For every N>=3, A_N has STRICTLY positive coefficients at every Newton coordinate 0,…,d. Indeed 3M>=d. For each k<=d choose 0<=i_1,i_2,i_3<=M with sum k. Multiply the corresponding positive terms in (4), always choosing the upper output index in the exact rule

Bhat_i Bhat_j=sum_(t=max(i,j))^(i+j)
   [s_(i+j)s_t/(s_i s_j s_(t-i)s_(t-j)s_(i+j-t))] Bhat_t,
i+j<N.                                       (6)

Both partial sums are <=k<=d<N. Hence the selected product is strictly positive. All other surviving terms are nonnegative. No sign is deduced from a finite-field residue, and no root normal is omitted.

3. Arbitrary trees give exact strictly positive whole sources

Let expressions be generated by

E ::= A | Rturn(E) | E E.                     (7)

An expression is fixed before taking the level limit. It can have arbitrary finite binary branching and turn depth. Compile it to the literal ribbon tangle of section1, and denote its physical operator E_N. Then, for every N>=3,

E_N=sum_(r=0)^d e_r Bhat_r, e_r>0,
original_raw_closed_source(E_N)=phi(E_N)=e_d>0. (8)

Proof. The seed has full strict support by section2. The actual turn reverses all coordinates, preserving strict support. If f and g have full strict support, their product does too: its r-th coefficient contains f_r g_0 times the exact unit coefficient of Bhat_r Bhat_0. Every other term has nonnegative coefficient by (6), or is zero by FULL repeated-root divisibility when i+j>=N. Structural induction proves (8). The coefficient operations are faithful actual physical turn/product identities, so induction also proves equality with the compiled original source, including its mark and all circle colors.

The N=2 exception is real. Then M=0 and h_N is a scalar; a turn can move its support to d=1, and a subsequent product of two such turned terms can vanish. The theorem makes no N=2 strictness assertion. This finite exception has no bearing on a large-level limit but is not erased from the exact source statement.

More generally a tangle built from L_N by turn and product, without the robust seed restriction, has a nonnegative exact source. Its zero/nonzero test is the finite flow problem (6), turn r->d-r, and closure r=d. It need not be nonzero: redundant serial complete circles retain the genuine even-level zero. The robust seed supplies an explicit class in which the global flow problem is always feasible.

4. Exact positive partition, remaining geometry and graph scope

Expand (7) as a tensor circuit. At a seed use (4) three times; at a product use (6); at a turn identify output d-r; at the final closure fix output d. This is an exact finite POSITIVE partition function for the WHOLE original scalar in (8), with at most N to the number of internal index slots terms. It supplies a genuine global conservation problem, not independent maximization of unglued local capacities. The repeated-root algebra, rather than only its nodal quotient, determines every multiplication and the final coefficient.

This coordinate cone is not a claim that each physical reflected box is a positive matrix. In particular Rturn L_N belongs to the cone, whereas its literal swap-reflected form has mixed inertia even on the actual two-vector source plane; see the typed reflection-inertia theorem. The positive scalar statement (8) uses the exact Newton multiplication and original functional, rather than a product of physical Hilbert norms.

Each expression is an explicit operational class of augmented ribbon diagrams. Its constituent boxes have four ports and can be sewn along four-punctured Conway spheres. The source algebra does not certify that these surfaces are essential or that the complement is hyperbolic. It also does not establish that different trees are topologically distinct or that their main-component bridge numbers grow. Those are separate topology questions. In particular no equality of this source partition with a Casson–Rivin angle functional is being asserted here.

The full primitive cube is not replaced by a Conway tree. It still requires a multiport identity or coherent estimate after removing its central cycle projections. The separately computed full-cube finite values are diagnostics, not a consequence of (8).

For reproducibility the full-cube diagnostic script contracts the literal complete physical CD factorization against the original J, ordinary caps and exposed unrestricted mark. Its recorded complex128 values include approximately 7.601732064e9 at N=3 and 3.314692220e20 at N=5; all twelve circle-color sums are retained. The recorded imaginary residual grows with level. No certified error bound, positivity, central dominance, or full-cube volume limit is inferred from them.

Audit: literal physical turns, full Casimir matrices and tree closures. Finite exact-field identities supplement the ribbon compilation and positive-support proof. Principal-root sign conclusions use (4),(6), not signs of residues. No volume theorem is inferred from the audit.

The theorem is restricted to the stated source and four-punctured seam, including the N=2 exception. Its finite audit checks physical levels and whole tree operators with all-input pivotal closures.
