Actual cyclic crossings: a sharp wrap gap and a Gaussian Morse adapter

24 November 2026. Research proof. Earlier finals and manuscripts are unchanged.

The actual complete-color E-cyclic crossing differs from the original nilpotent crossing by a one-wrap matrix. Its sharp physical norm exponent is the regular ideal tetrahedron volume divided by π\pi, strictly smaller than the full crossing’s Gaussian exponent. Consequently the existing whole physical Gaussian model approximates the actual cyclic crossing to every fixed algebraic order.

Projecting this model onto the complete actual pair intertwiner space makes its error an exact intertwiner. The opposite-source Morse Gram then bounds that error, after any spectator embedding, by its pair physical Hilbert–Schmidt norm. No Gram condition number or spectator dimension factor occurs. This is an absolute approximation at the full crossing norm scale, not a geometric scalar lower bound.

5. Spectator-free error in the source-compatible Morse Gram

Embed the error (17) on its actual neighboring pair in any opposite-source odd-width slice of [M], using identities on spectators. Use its correctly updated output labels. Pair E,F,KE,F,K intertwining implies intertwining of the full iterated coproduct: the earlier terms contain the pair KK, the local terms its EE, and the later terms pass unchanged. In particular (19) is valid at an opposite-center pair whose total pair ENE^N is zero: the averaging is on the odd whole slice, not on a nonexistent cyclic pair normalization.

The corrected models and the literal cups/caps of [M] can be composed as intertwiners in the fixed original Morse presentation. Their errors remain absolute estimates at the scale in (19). The two corrected signs are not asserted mutual inverses or a new braiding, and their products are not asserted equal to the original word. Every original cap, marked closing, framing and external scalar remains. The error may exceed the actual geometric packet or closing overlap; no nonzero cap projection or scalar geometric lower follows.

Complete inputs and closest coverage

[G, Section 4] already projects its nilpotent Gaussian onto the full physical algebra. [P, Section 4] already uses physical E,F,KE,F,K projection and warns that it changes a phase representation. [C] already controls a different positive-degree Euler completion and can damp its carry exponentially without changing the source. The addition is (6)–(12) for the actual semicyclic wraps, followed by the updated-fiber Gaussian estimate and the no-condition-number, spectator-free Morse estimate (19).
