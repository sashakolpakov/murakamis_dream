Global two-chart localization of the actual Frobenius source and its endpoints

Research construction, 27 October 2026. The actual high-color source has an explicit global polynomial/Čech realization on P1. Its matrix co-source is a Laurent cocycle, and the original matrix pairing is exactly a residue. All actual whole matrices have global operator lifts, unique up to an explicit cochain homotopy; their finite normal jets admit the bounded-order lifts already proved. The two finite source endpoints survive as named north and south cochains.

There is also a WHOLE rational comparison with the uniform co-source: integrate the actual homogeneous dual WITH its retained pairing weight, then normalize its actual pairing unit. A rational unipotent gauge fixes the actual source and compares the two duals exactly. No quantum-jet inference is needed for that identity.

This localizes the actual source/dual and operators globally. It does not identify the network Gaussian coefficients with geometric ones, bound the norm of endpoint transport, or prove the volume limit.

1. Actual polynomial source and an explicit dual Čech class

Fix N>=2 and use the actual Fr multiplicity basis v_k, 0<=k<=N, of the high integral color. Put

vk↦(Nk)zk. v_k\longmapsto {N\choose k}z^k. \tag{1.1}

The actual Fr module is thereby the global polynomial space of O(N). On the two charts z and w=1/z, a section is a pair satisfying f_infty(w)=w^N f_0(1/w). Thus e_infty=z^N e_0, and its global sections are exactly the polynomials f_0 of degree at most N. This verifies the global-section description directly from the transition, with no cohomology theorem as a premise.

For the dual use e_0^* dz in the north and e_infty^* (-dw) in the south. Its transition is +z^(-N-2). The Čech complex is

C0=k[z]⊕k[w]→dC1=k[z,z−1],d(a0,a∞)=z−N−2a∞(1/z)−a0(z). C^0=k[z]\oplus k[w]\ \xrightarrow{\ d\ }\ C^1=k[z,z^{-1}],\qquad d(a_0,a_\infty)=z^{-N-2}a_\infty(1/z)-a_0(z). \tag{1.2}

The sign in -dw is deliberate. It retains both the canonical-bundle degree -2 and the orientation of the residue below. Its cokernel has the literal basis z(-1),…,z(-N-1); its kernel is zero. The actual row co-source lambda is represented by

cλ(z)=∑k=0Nλ(vk)(Nk)z−k−1. c_\lambda(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{N} \frac{\lambda(v_k)}{\binom Nk}z^{-k-1}. \tag{1.3}

The pairing is EXACTLY the original matrix pairing:

λ(ψ)=Res⁡z=0cλ(z)fψ(z)dz. \lambda(\psi)= \operatorname{Res}_{z=0}c_\lambda(z)f_\psi(z)\,dz. \tag{1.4}

Both kinds of coboundary in (1.2) pair to zero with a degree-N global section: the north term is polynomial, while the south term has powers at most -2 after multiplication. Conversely the N+1 coefficients in (1.3) detect every polynomial coefficient. This proves the perfect pairing directly, including its two-chart quotient, rather than citing Serre duality as a proof.

The literal normalized matrix TRACE is preserved too, without replacing it by a rank-one row:

Tr⁡DN+1=1N+1∑k=0NRes⁡z=0z−k−1(Nk)D((Nk)zk)dz. \frac{\operatorname{Tr}D}{N+1} =\frac1{N+1}\sum_{k=0}^N\operatorname{Res}_{z=0} \frac{z^{-k-1}}{\binom Nk} D\left(\binom Nk z^k\right)dz. \tag{1.5}

This works for arbitrary inserted matrices, not only diagonal closed knot matrices. Source/co-source rows and the trace are distinct retained pairings in this model.

2. The cochain projection and its explicit endpoint homotopy

Let pi retain exactly powers -1 through -N-1 and interpret them by (1.3). Let iota take the canonical representative (1.3). For a Laurent polynomial c write c=c_north+c_mid+c_south, with powers respectively >=0, -N-1 through -1, and <=-N-2. Define

$$ h(c)=\left(-c_{\rm north}(z),\ [z^{N+2}c_{\rm south}(z)]_{z=1/w}\right). \tag{2.1} $$

Then, coefficient by coefficient,

dh=I−ιπon C1,hd=Ion C0. d h=I-\iota\pi\quad\text{on }C^1,\qquad h d=I\quad\text{on }C^0. \tag{2.2}

These formulas explicitly contract the redundant Čech terms; no endpoint is declared zero without its cochain.

For comparison the source complex of O(N) has differential d_S(f_0,f_infty)=z^N f_infty(1/z)-f_0. Its global projection is pi_S(f_0,f_infty)=[f_0]_(0..N), and a right inverse of d_S is

hS(b)=(−b>N(z),[z−Nb≤N(z)]z=1/w). h_S(b)=\left(-b_{>N}(z),\ [z^{-N}b_{\le N}(z)]_{z=1/w}\right). \tag{2.3}

Direct substitution gives d_S h_S=I and h_S d_S=I-iota_S pi_S. These are global chain maps and homotopies over the chosen coefficient algebra.

There is even a cochain-level residue Stokes identity. Pair a source 0-cochain f with a dual 1-cochain c by Res(f_0 c dz), and a source 1-cochain b with a dual 0-cochain a by Res(b z^(-N-2) a_infty(1/z) dz). Then

⟨dSf,a⟩+⟨f,da⟩=0. \langle d_Sf,a\rangle +\langle f,da\rangle=0. \tag{2.4}

The sum is the residue of z^(-2) f_infty(1/z)a_infty(1/z)-f_0(z)a_0(z), which is zero. Thus the source and dual endpoints are paired globally, not only at one formal Taylor chart.

3. All actual whole operators lift, and finite jets have bounded order

In the polynomial model the ORIGINAL root divided generators are

e=∂z,f=Nz−z2∂z,h=N−2z∂z. e=\partial_z,\qquad f=Nz-z^2\partial_z, \qquad h=N-2z\partial_z. \tag{3.1}

Reciprocal transport by f_infty(w)=w^N f_0(1/w) exchanges e and f and changes h to -h. Hence they are global differential operators on O(N), with their actual zero-order N terms retained.

Every actual WHOLE Fr matrix, not just a jet, has an explicit such lift. Put A=z partial_z and Pj(A)=∏l≠j(A−l)/(j−l)P_j(A)=\prod_{l\ne j}(A-l)/(j-l), 0<=j<=N. Matrix units are

Eij={(j!/i!)fi−jPj(A),i≥j,((N−j)!/(N−i)!)ej−iPj(A),i<j. E_{ij}=\begin{cases} (j!/i!) f^{i-j}P_j(A),&i\ge j,\\ ((N-j)!/(N-i)!) e^{j-i}P_j(A),&i<j. \end{cases} \tag{3.2}

They act literally as E_ij on the actual basis (1.1). Thus an actual whole high-color crossing or Green matrix, with arbitrary head coefficients, gives global operators on the product of the Fr projective lines. Formula (3.2) may have order growing with N; it is a whole-function lift, not a uniform-order assertion. At quantum-normal depth B the actual all-order Fr theorem gives the much smaller differential-order bound 2B. Heads are ordinary matrix coefficients throughout.

For D=sum a_l(z)partial_z^l, the dual lift is the formal adjoint

D†c=∑l(−∂z)l(cal). D^\dagger c= \sum_l(-\partial_z)^l(ca_l). \tag{3.3}

Residues of Laurent derivatives are zero, so (3.3) is the EXACT row action lambda -> lambda D under (1.4). With head-matrix coefficients this ordering is essential: the co-source row is multiplied by a_l before differentiating, or equivalently its dual column uses the transpose matrices. In particular

e†=−∂z,f†=z2∂z+(N+2)z,h†=2z∂z+N+2. e^\dagger=-\partial_z,\quad f^\dagger=z^2\partial_z+(N+2)z,\quad h^\dagger=2z\partial_z+N+2. \tag{3.4}

The +2 is the canonical-bundle contribution, not an adjustable normalization. These maps preserve north and south coboundaries. At infinity they exchange e^dagger and f^dagger and change h^dagger to its negative, exactly as their source counterparts do. Thus they are strict chain maps on (1.2).

If two global operator expressions induce the SAME actual matrix, their dual lifts differ by an explicit null homotopy. More generally for any lift D^dagger with induced actual dual matrix D^*,

D†−ιD*π=d(hD†)+(hD†)d. D^\dagger-\iota D^*\pi =d(hD^\dagger)+(hD^\dagger)d. \tag{3.5}

On C^1 this is (2.2), because pi Ddagger=D* pi; on C^0 use commutation with d and h d=I. Thus the construction is a canonical global action UP TO THESE SPECIFIED HOMOTOPIES. It is not a falsely strict embedding of the full finite matrix algebra into a domain of scalar differential functions. Composition and tensor products carry the corresponding homotopies. On source global sections and dual cohomology, all original matrix identities are literal. For the source complex the analogous explicit homotopy is H=h_S D+(iota_S pi_S)D h_S: it identifies D with iota_S D_matrix pi_S. Its extra term is needed because the chosen north coefficient projection need not commute with D on noncycles.

All formulas are linear over the actual whole coefficient ring. The original q^N, M^N, half-root phases and source normalization units stay as coefficients before restriction. A base shift or derivation acts on those coefficients with its original Leibniz law. One may restrict to finite quantum jets AFTER these whole identities; there is no replacement of actual dual coefficients by their root values before differentiation. Explicitly, for a supplied base automorphism sigma and transport T=C sigma, the dual row transport is sigma^(-1)(lambda C), with pairing sigma^(-1)<lambda,Tv>. A supplied connection D_x+Omega has dual D_x lambda-lambda Omega. These original semilinear and connection rules commute with the coefficientwise Čech contractions; the construction does not replace them by a root-only scalar action.

4. Whole clock automorphisms retain their line factors

For the actual clock B_xi=xi^A the north source action is f_0(z)->f_0(xi z). Its southern action is

f∞(w)↦ξNf∞(w/ξ). f_\infty(w)\longmapsto \xi^N f_\infty(w/\xi).\tag{4.1}

The dual north and south actions are respectively

c(z)↦ξ−1c(z/ξ),a∞(w)↦ξN+1a∞(ξw). c(z)\longmapsto\xi^{-1}c(z/\xi),\qquad a_\infty(w)\longmapsto\xi^{N+1}a_\infty(\xi w). \tag{4.2}

They commute with the Čech differential and are ADJOINT for the residue pairing: <B_xi^dagger c,f>=<c,B_xi f>. Simultaneously transporting a source and its dual instead uses the contragredient inverse: c(z)->xi c(xi z), and a_infty(w)->xi^(-N-1)a_infty(w/xi). On z^(-k-1), (4.2) multiplies by xi^k, exactly the actual row clock. Its infinitesimal adjoint is -z partial_z-1; the -1 must not be dropped. These are the global line factors behind the finite coherent dilations, including controlled clock gauges.

5. Literal north and south finite-source endpoints

The actual partial inverses are e^+v_j=v_(j+1)/(N-j) for j<N and zero at N, and f^+v_j=v_(j-1)/j for j>0 and zero at zero. In polynomials, e^+ is the primitive anchored at z=0, with its degree-(N+1) tail removed. The operator f^+ is the reciprocal south primitive. Directly,

e+e=I−P0,ee+=I−PN,f+f=I−PN,ff+=I−P0. e^+e=I-P_0,\quad ee^+=I-P_N,\qquad f^+f=I-P_N,\quad ff^+=I-P_0. \tag{5.1}

They also have explicit global differential lifts. Let q(A) be the degree-at-most-N interpolation polynomial with q(j)=1/[(j+1)(N-j)] for j<N and q(N)=0. Then

e+=fq(A),f+=eq(N−A). e^+=f q(A),\qquad f^+=e q(N-A). \tag{5.2}

Their order can grow with N. Do NOT apply the bound 2B to these arbitrary anchored primitives without their actual degree analysis. Identities (5.1) hold strictly on H0; for the global Čech lifts their differences are the explicit homotopies (3.5), with the endpoint projectors retained.

The dual endpoint cochains are especially transparent:

f†z−1=N+1=d(−(N+1),0),e†z−N−1=(N+1)z−N−2=d(0,N+1). \begin{split} f^\dagger z^{-1}&=N+1=d(-(N+1),0),\\ e^\dagger z^{-N-1}&=(N+1)z^{-N-2}=d(0,N+1). \end{split} \tag{5.3}

They are NORTH and SOUTH boundaries separately. Removing both before pairing a relative source would erase its endpoint data. The actual endpoint-completed clock current uses precisely these complementary finite primitives to restore its missing endpoint. No Hopf-natural tensor inverse is asserted.

6. The actual paired Borel source is a global residue class

For the marked unprojectivized source, use U=alpha^N and m=(1-u)/(1+u). Its ROOT representatives are

fα(z)=U(1+z)N,cU,m(z)=12NU∑k=0N(1−m)k(1+m)N−kz−k−1. \begin{split} f_\alpha(z)&=U(1+z)^N,\\ c_{U,m}(z)&=\frac1{2^NU}\sum_{k=0}^N (1-m)^k(1+m)^{N-k}z^{-k-1}. \end{split} \tag{6.1}

They are globally polynomial in m, including the missing u=infinity chart, and Res(c f dz)=1. The scale and descendant are residues of ACTUAL operators:

U=Res⁡c+fαdz,m=1NRes⁡cU,m(N−2z∂z)fαdz,c+=2−N∑k=0Nz−k−1. U=\operatorname{Res}c_+f_\alpha dz,\quad m=\frac1N\operatorname{Res}c_{U,m}(N-2z\partial_z)f_\alpha dz, \qquad c_+=2^{-N}\sum_{k=0}^Nz^{-k-1}. \tag{6.2}

Consequently the already proved framing identity Omega_cut=(A/N)dlogU wedge dm is an explicit expression in global source/descendant residue pairings, on its marked Kummer cover. The Berry form is still N dlogalpha and flat on this Borel; it has not been substituted for Omega_cut.

There is an explicit relative framing coordinate too. On the same marked cover put s=C alpha^2, r_frame=(1+m)/2+D alpha^2, and

$$ \tau=r_{\rm frame}/s =\frac{1+m}{2C\alpha^2}+\frac DC, \quad A=1/(1+s),\qquad d\tau\wedge d\log A=\Omega_{\rm cut}.\tag{6.3} $$

At C=alpha=1,D=0, one has s=1,A=1/2 and tau=(1+m)/2. Thus the literal framing interval 0<=tau<=1 is m in [-1,1], with d tau=dm/2. Averaging its ROOT global class c_(1,m) gives the uniform row class

$$ c_{\rm uni}(z)=\frac1{N+1}\sum_{k=0}^N \frac{z^{-k-1}}{\binom Nk}.\tag{6.4} $$

The whole extension of this relative identity is Section 7, with its original pairing density retained.

The actual first-normal old cut clock also has a global class here. Its previously proved value on every pure-power source is ((a+b)/2)^N/[N(1-Q_N)]. Since those source vectors span the actual Fr module, the unique linear extension of this SELECTED coherent-family character is represented by c_+(z)/[N(1-Q_N)]. Pairing it with f_alpha returns U/[N(1-Q_N)]. This localizes the actual SCALE normal class globally; it neither equates the framing A with an old clock nor supplies a second internal geometric-current map. The original normal construction has source-dependent pairing-unit ratios. No equality of its full operator action on arbitrary Fr matrices follows merely from this coherent-family character.

For the ORIGINAL whole q-source, use (1.3) on its actual coefficients psi_s(q),lambda_s(q), including the actual units U_s(q,m). Formula (6.1) is their root restriction, NOT their replacement before quantum differentiation. Their whole source and dual remain global Čech data.

7. A whole retained-weight interval gives the uniform co-source

Here is a function identity before any normal-jet restriction. Work over the actual rational q-coefficients near q_N on one residue string. Put E_N(q)=tau E^(N)(q), F_N(q)=F^(N)(q), and

ψq=exp⁡(FN(q))v0,ck(q)vk*=v0*EN(q)k/k!,λ̃q(m)=∑k=0Nck(q)(1+m2)N−k(1−m2)kvk*,Uq(m)=λ̃q(m)ψq,λq(m)=λ̃q(m)/Uq(m). \begin{split} \psi_q&=\exp(F_N(q))v_0,\\ c_k(q)v_k^*&=v_0^*E_N(q)^k/k!,\\ \widetilde\lambda_q(m)&=\sum_{k=0}^N c_k(q)\left(\frac{1+m}{2}\right)^{N-k} \left(\frac{1-m}{2}\right)^k v_k^*,\\ U_q(m)&=\widetilde\lambda_q(m)\psi_q,\qquad \lambda_q(m)=\widetilde\lambda_q(m)/U_q(m). \end{split} \tag{7.1}

Every c_k and psi coefficient comes from the ORIGINAL integral divided matrices. At the root c_k=binom(N,k), psi_q=sum v_k and U_q(m)=1. Thus U is an actual pairing unit formally, and analytically on the compact interval for q sufficiently close to q_N at fixed N.

The literal beta integral, proved by repeated elementary integration of ta(1-t)b, is

12∫−11(1+m2)N−k(1−m2)kdm=1(N+1)(Nk). \frac12\int_{-1}^1 \left(\frac{1+m}{2}\right)^{N-k} \left(\frac{1-m}{2}\right)^kdm =\frac1{(N+1)\binom Nk}. \tag{7.2}

Therefore the WHOLE weighted interval co-source is rational:

Lq=12∫−11Uq(m)λq(m)dm=∑kck(q)(N+1)(Nk)vk*. L_q=\frac12\int_{-1}^1 U_q(m)\lambda_q(m)dm =\sum_k\frac{c_k(q)}{(N+1)\binom Nk}v_k^*. \tag{7.3}

Cancellation of U in (7.3) is an identity of the WHOLE rational integrand, before integration or normal specialization. It retains that pairing line as the integration density. At q_N the row is the uniform row lambda_uni=(sum v_k^*)/(N+1).

This uniform ROW is distinct from the full trace (1.5). With its uniform source it agrees with the trace on diagonal arrays, and both give the actual closed scalar period. No equality on arbitrary off-diagonal Green insertions is inferred from that fact.

Nor is the uniform Fr row the ORIGINAL head-uniform/coherent-Fr coupon after the cyclotomic diagonal projector. Its Fr indices 0 and N both map to head index S=0, so raw Fr uniform averaging counts that head endpoint twice. A literal match to the old head co-source requires the degree-elevation/endpoint map or its retained adapter. The root-original coherent Fr row has binomial weights; it is a different co-source from lambda_uni. The root-identity gauge below compares the WEIGHTED-INTERVAL Fr uniform dual with the Fr uniform dual, not automatically with that binomial coupon.

Put V_q=L_q psi_q and nu_q=lambda_uni psi_q. Both are actual rational units with root value ONE. The normalized retained-weight interval is L_q/V_q. The normalized uniform dual on the SAME actual source is lambda_uni/nu_q. Define

$$ \Delta_q=L_q/V_q-\lambda_{\rm uni}/\nu_q,\qquad K_q=I+\psi_q\Delta_q. \tag{7.4} $$

Since Delta_q psi_q=0, these are exact whole identities:

$$ \begin{split} K_q^{-1}&=I-\psi_q\Delta_q,\quad K_{q_N}=I,\\ K_q\psi_q&=\psi_q,\qquad (\lambda_{\rm uni}/\nu_q)K_q=L_q/V_q . \end{split} \tag{7.5} $$

Thus (7.4) is a concrete regular RATIONAL unipotent source-preserving gauge between the two whole co-sources. It is not an inference from equality of their root jets. The marked scale alpha^N is retained by multiplying psi and the dual inversely; K itself is unchanged.

To compare network operators one must conjugate them too. In the convention v_old=K_q v_new, transport becomes K_q^(-1)T_old sigma(K_q), co-source becomes ell_old K_q, and a connection becomes K_q^(-1)Omega_old K_q+K_q^(-1)dK_q. Its last term is part of the actual comparison. Formula (7.5) alone is not a proof of geometric current intertwining. The chosen source gauge is not declared PBW-degree-preserving, nor is its growing-N presentation declared uniformly bounded.

The UNWEIGHTED normalized integral int lambda_q(m)dm/2 is a different relative rational-function period, potentially logarithmic. No rationality or equality to (7.3) is asserted. The source density U_q(m)/V_q is essential off the root; setting it to one would lose exact actual quantum-normal data.

8. Precise category and remaining geometric identification

At fixed N this construction works on whole algebraic source and dual bundles over the actual rational coefficient ring, retaining its marked units, phase and Kummer charts. Operators are global differential operators and bundle automorphisms, with explicit Čech lifts. Different lifts of the same actual matrix are identified by the concrete cochain homotopies (2.2)–(3.5). Relative periods retain their density and endpoints; unweighted periods need not be rational sections. Quantum jets are restrictions of these already specified whole identities.

Tensoring the displayed two-chart complexes handles all Fr strands of an actual braid: source sections are multihomogeneous polynomials, dual classes are products of the Laurent basis elements, and the matrix pairing is their ordered multiple residue. The local strict operator lifts compose; identities in finite matrices lift up the explicit tensor contractions. Thus actual whole R/Green operators, their source units and named finite endpoints have a GLOBAL source model, not only a formal coordinate patch at the optimizer.

The further map to the INTERNAL geometric framing interval still requires its actual Ward/current comparison, including the charged head neighbors and normal source corrections. The actual relative endpoint analysis also shows that exact endpoint transport can have exponentially large metric norm. Global algebraic localization and equality of source pairings do not supply a tame norm or a nonzero selected geometric contribution.
