Direct signed circle fans and the untwisted relative fundamental chain

The source-to-group sewing is conditional on the stated hypotheses. Contact polygons do not supply the incorrectly inferred complete-color symbol, and the construction is not established at every sharp maximum.

1. Critical local chart and actual contact coordinates

Let x,y,b>0, 0<delta<1/2, s=sin(pi delta), k=cos(pi delta), and

c=b+(x+y)/s^2,
T^2=b(x+y)+xy+b^2 s^2=c^2 s^2-c(x+y)+xy.

These are the free-spectral critical equations on the strict LEFT straddling chart. The input triangle has (q,T_q)=(b,T); the output triangle has (q,T_q)=(-c,-T). In particular c>max(x,y). Put D=(x+y)^2-4xy s^2 and use the following common Euclidean similarity of the actual circle configuration:

L=D/(xy), a=(y+x cos(2pi delta))/x,
O=a-2isk,
Z_q=a+(y-x)/q+2ik T_q/q,
p_x=q Z_q/(q+x),
p_y=L+q(Z_q-L)/(q+y).                         (1)

The outer circle centers are 0,L. Their radii in this chart are sqrt(D)/x and sqrt(D)/y. Z_q is the middle-circle center and its geometric radius is sqrt(D)/|q|. O is the selected lower intersection of the outer circles, the actual P:J flag, where J=-exp(-2pi i delta). The two p’s are the MAIN tangent contacts. The normalization is a Euclidean similarity fixing the original cone apex infinity. There is no resetting of an independent Gauss reference flag.

Define the four literal fan shapes

w_x=p_x/O,
w_q=(p_y-Z_q)/(p_x-Z_q),
w_y=(O-L)/(p_y-L),
z_q=(O-p_x)/(p_y-p_x),

and let D_BW denote the Bloch–Wigner function. Set

V(q,T_q)=D_BW(w_x)+D_BW(w_q)+D_BW(w_y)-2D_BW(z_q).
                                                           (2)

The source force angles and their Legendre normalization are retained:

mu=atan(bk/T)/pi, nu=atan(ck/T)/pi,
G=Q_b+log c-J_c,
h=G-mu log b-(1-nu)log c-xi log x-eta log y,
xi+eta=nu-mu.                                  (3)

Here G is the actual sharp real action, including the outside-row products; the retained scalar modes are pure imaginary on this real chart. The definition and absolute normalization of Q,J,G are those of the signed-straddling generating relation.

Local theorem. On this connected physical critical chart,

V(b,T)-V(-c,-T)=2pi h.                         (4)

The reflected RIGHT identity follows by exchanging input/output and the original orientation. No mixed-color word identification is used in (4).

2. Orientation and the factor two

For a white circle, an original directed polygon edge from contact p to p’ contributes Lambda((arg(p’-Z)-arg(p-Z))/2) to the cone volume with apex infinity. This holds for the recorded polygon order, even when it folds or has winding zero: sum the oriented triangle fan, cancel its internal edges, and use the oddness and pi-periodicity of Lambda. Since D_BW(exp(i theta))=2 Lambda(theta/2), its contribution to TWICE the cut-ball volume is the D_BW of the contact-ray ratio.

For the input triangle the recorded white boundary edges are O->p_x, p_x->p_y, p_y->O. Their three ray ratios are exactly the first three shapes in (2). The shaded boundary has the opposite edge orientation, hence is (O,p_y,p_x). A triangle (p_x,p_y,O) coned to infinity has volume D_BW((O-p_x)/(p_y-p_x)); thus the shaded face contributes -2D_BW(z_q) to twice the ball volume. This proves (2) directly from the actual boundary orientation.

The output triangle has the opposite original white-triangle order. Its displayed contact coordinates may lie on the same side as the input after folding, but its combinatorial order is not changed. Its contribution is therefore -V(-c,-T). The two abstract cut balls have opposite orientations and are developed using one target reflection, so their equal volume contributions give exactly (4), with no additional factor two.

Repeated nonadjacent contacts in a white face cause no ambiguity: the cyclic fan identity is applied in the original edge order on ONE circle. The normalized repeated-vertex chains have zero terms; one does not invoke continuity for arbitrary coalescing tetrahedra.

3. Exact differential and the absolute constant

The identity is scale invariant, so set y=1. Write

s=2v/(1+v^2), k=(1-v^2)/(1+v^2),
x=(T^2-b-b^2 s^2)/(b+1), 0<v<1.

For q=b,T_q=T or q=-c,T_q=-T, put co=1-2s^2 and

N_x=x(1-x)+q(1+x co)+2ixkT_q,
N_y=(x-1)+q(x+co)-2ikT_q,
O_x=1+x co-2ixsk, O_y=x+co+2isk.

The four shapes (1) simplify, without changing any flag, to

w_x=N_x/((q+x)O_x),
w_q=-(q+x)N_y/((q+1)N_x),
w_y=(q+1)O_y/N_y,
z_q=(q+1)[xk-i(T_q+s(q+x))]
      /[k(q(x+1)+2x)+iT_q(x-1)].              (5)

Let p_in=(T-ibk)/(T+ibk), p_out=(T-ick)/(T+ick), and let N_xb,N_xc mean the preceding N_x evaluated at (b,T),(-c,-T). Direct differentiation of the source Legendre expression gives

2pi dh=log b dArg p_in-log c dArg p_out
                     -log x dArg(N_x^b/N_x^c). (6)

Substituting (5) into dD_BW(z)=log|z|dArg(1-z)-log|1-z|dArg z gives EXACTLY (6) for the eight-shape difference. The accompanying verifier factors the rational functions over Q(i)[b,T,v], retains Gaussian-rational unit log norms (in particular log 2), and expands every log|factor| dArg(factor) coefficient. All 49 tensor positions cancel against (6). Conjugate factors have the same norm and opposite argument derivative; only constant arguments have zero derivative. This is a finite rational identity, with no numerical derivative or unrecorded logarithm branch.

There are no shape or 1-minus-shape divisors in the physical interior. Indeed Im O<0, while Im Z_q=2kT_q/q>0 and q/(q+x),q/(q+y)>0 imply Im p_x,Im p_y>0. Also Z_q,L-Z_q are nonzero and

p_x-Z_q=-x Z_q/(q+x),
p_y-Z_q=y(L-Z_q)/(q+y),
p_y-L=q(Z_q-L)/(q+y).

The numerator of p_y-p_x, apart from its nonzero real denominator, is Ly(q+x)+qZ_q(x-y). Its imaginary part is 2kT_q(x-y); if x=y its remaining real term is nonzero. Thus all three contacts are distinct and all eight shapes and their complements are finite and nonzero.

The domain (b,x,delta)>0 with delta<1/2 is connected. Calibrate at b=1,x=2,y=1 and delta->1/2. Then c->4,T->sqrt(6); all six white ratios tend to 1, and the two shaded ratios tend respectively to

-2(3+sqrt(6))/sqrt(6),
 3(2+sqrt(6))/sqrt(6),                         (7)

which are finite real numbers. Their Bloch–Wigner values tend to zero. The verifier checks the leading numerator and denominator separately at this anchor before taking their ratio.

For clarity, the source side tends to zero as well, rather than being calibrated only by a derivative: put t=k tau in its retained row integral. With r_k=sin(pi k tau)/k,

Lambda(k tau)=2k^2 r_k[(x+1)r_k+
                          sqrt((x-1)^2 r_k^2+4x)],
alpha(k tau)=4k^2(1-r_k^2).

The two endpoints tend to b/(pi T_1),c_1/(pi T_1), strictly on opposite sides of 1/pi. The scaled interval is compact; the numerator is uniformly positive and bounded, and the denominator has one uniformly simple zero. Thus the logarithmic integral is O(k), while mu,nu,xi,eta are O(k). Consequently h->0. The differential identity, connectedness, and (7) prove the absolute identity (4).

4. Literal marked-line limits

The local assertion includes the nondegenerate single-line limits by the same contact formulas, in the original apex frame. Here are their explicit limits, with y=1 by the same harmless similarity.

When b->0 with x>0, put T_0=sqrt(x) and F=1-x+2ik sqrt(x). The input contacts become

p_x=F/x, p_y=L+F,
w_x=F/(1+x co-2ixsk), w_q=1,
w_y=-(x+co+2isk)/F,
z_q=[xk-i(sqrt(x)+sx)]/[2kx+i sqrt(x)(x-1)].  (7a)

The actual middle line is Re((z-a) conjugate(F))=2k^2(x+1). Both contacts have positive imaginary part and O has negative imaginary part; p_y-p_x=4k^2+2ik(x-1)/sqrt(x) is nonzero. Thus the limiting shaded triangle remains distinct, and only the middle white fan becomes flat. In the source, mu=bk/(pi sqrt(x))+O(b^2), so mu log b->0. Its interval endpoint approaches 0, where Lambda(t) has a simple zero and alpha(0)>0. The discarded small integral is O(mu |log mu|); all remaining integrals and loads converge. Hence h has the same literal limit.

When x->0 with b>0, first translate every contact and center by -1/x (or -y/x before normalizing y). This is affine and fixes infinity. For q=b,T_q=T or q=-c,T_q=-T the limits are

O*=co-2isk,
Z_q*=co+1/q+2ikT_q/q,
p_x*=co+2ikT_q/q,
p_y*=[(q+2)co+1+2ikT_q]/(q+1),
w_x=1,
w_q=-(-1+q co-2ikT_q)/(q+1),
w_y=(q+1)(co+2isk)/(-1+q co-2ikT_q),
z_q=-i(q+1)(T_q+sq)/(kq-iT_q).                (7b)

Here T2=b+b2s^2 and c=b+1/s^2. The outer x-circle becomes the actual line Re z=co; its two contacts are distinct on that line. The affine map z->i(z-co) identifies it literally with the existing outer-line adapter: O maps to +2sk and the middle center maps to -2kT_q/q+i/q. Thus the same selected raw J flag is retained. Both p’s have positive imaginary part, and p_y-p_x=2k(kq-iT_q)/(q(q+1)) is nonzero. Thus the shaded triangle remains distinct and only the x-white fan becomes flat. The source interval endpoints stay strictly inside (0,1/2), on opposite sides of its simple alpha zero. On this compact interval Lambda is smooth and nonzero even at x=0, and its x-logarithmic derivative is bounded; the alpha zero is independent of x. Therefore xi=x partial_x G=O(x), so xi log x->0, and all other terms of h converge.

For completeness y->0 can also be taken without ANY outer-label reversal: in (1), with x fixed and no translation, the x-center stays 0 with limiting radius1, the y-circle tends to Re z=co, O->co-2isk, Z_q->co-x/q+2ikT_q/q, and

p_x->(q co-x+2ikT_q)/(q+x),
p_y->co+2ikT_q/q, w_y->1.

This is the same selected lower-intersection family. The identical compact-interval estimate gives eta=O(y) and eta log y->0.

These calculations prove continuity for this ONE explicit family and its literal line triangles, not for arbitrary colliding ideal vertices. The only flat white contribution is D_BW(1)=0.

A strict straddling triangle with both x=b=0 (or y=b=0) is NOT added by this statement: T=0 and nu=1/2 there, a terminal/free boundary stratum. The global theorem below permits a forced two-line corner only when its actual local branch is the already proved complete CROSS line triangle. Any other double-zero or free boundary needs a separate adapter.

5. Conditional original untwisted group and chain

Fix the original certified simple-white marked plat. Assume the actual signed source clocks, selected spectral flags and full face stationary equations satisfy the hypotheses of the regular signed group-sewing lemma and yield one global developed reflection family. Restrict the local branches to complete CROSS triangles and strict free-spectral LEFT/RIGHT triangles covered by (4), including only the line limits of section4. All original cups, caps, prefixes and the unrestricted original mark are retained. In particular this is not a construction from an absolute maximum for which the sign cocycle has not been solved.

Use the UNTWISTED drilled exterior Y_D^0. Its literal first-ball shaded pair is S_0=(O,C_1,C_2), S_1=(O,C_3,C_0), paired by O->O,C_1->C_0,C_2->C_3 within that ball. These displayed tuples are the FIXED PAIRING-LABEL listings. They do not override the oriented boundary-chain orders of section2: the input shaded chain has order (O,p_y,p_x). In particular no orientation sign is inferred just from the listing S_0. At a strict critical port, the primitive collar C has projective eigenratio -1 on P’s selected flag. Hence C is the half-turn about the axis joining the two outer-circle intersections. Each outer white plane contains that axis, so C preserves BOTH outer planes. Its proved simultaneous main-head conjugations then show that C carries the input middle plane to the output middle plane. It therefore carries precisely all three ordered contact flags in this original permutation, not only O. At complete CROSS ports use their established three-point collar, including the literal line cases.

Keep ONE global white reflection r_u for the second ball. Its shaded map is r_u C r_u with the same original labels; white face f pairs second to first by r_f r_u. The latter fixes the whole white plane after removing r_u and identifies its complete recorded polygon chain. These are exactly the original UNTWISTED face permutations. No retained-half across-copy permutation is asserted here.

Use the labeled convex complete certificate to fix a degree-one abstract triangulation of the original two balls: fan the incident white faces from the original marked vertex p and cone p to the nonincident boundary triangles. Change only its developed vertex images to the present contacts. The original combinatorial boundary identifications still hold, so this is the original equivariant relative fundamental chain, even when its image folds. No new interior vertex, replacement mark or image-degree assertion occurs. Section2 computes its signed volume by its original face order.

6. Whole peripheral groups, truncation and the uniform gap

Every main peripheral subgroup contains its noncentral parabolic meridian and hence preserves all horospheres at its contact. At a strict critical auxiliary cusp, its actual two generators are the primitive C^{-1} and P, up to the recorded central lifts. They commute, fix the same two ideal flags, and have unit-modulus eigenvalues. Therefore the WHOLE peripheral subgroup preserves every horosphere at the selected flag. At a complete CROSS cusp the corresponding whole subgroup is parabolic and has the same property. Merely commuting with identity is not used.

Choose a horosphere at one lift of each original cusp and propagate it equivariantly. Peripheral preservation makes this well defined. No orthogonality of the now-intersecting white/shaded planes is required. Truncate the finitely many incidence corners compatibly; the ideal-chain limit agrees with the horospherically adapted equivariant volume. For a compact critical peripheral group this is also the canonical normalization used in the published general volume bound: put its chosen flag at infinity and its other fixed flag at 0. The cusp group acts by rotations z->exp(i theta)z on a horosphere y=H. The equivariant homotopy (z,H)->((1-t)z,H) remains on this horosphere and has rank at most two, hence zero three-volume. Its endpoint is the finite axis point (0,H); the remaining axis tail has rank at most one. This is a proof for the CONSISTENT original horospherical boundary cycle, not a claim that an arbitrary rotating torus or open mixed-flag bridge has zero cone volume.

Consequently the canonical representation volume is the computed original two-ball chain volume, and

2pi sum_e h_e=Vol(rho),
|sum_e h_e| <= Vol(Y_D^0)/(2pi).               (8)

If at least one port is strict Green, its ACTUAL auxiliary meridian has trace zero: the raw matrix M_0 satisfies M_0^2=-I. The original complete auxiliary meridians have trace squared 4. Apply the proved fixed-peripheral-word gap to the finite list of original auxiliary meridians, with deviation 4. It gives a diagram-dependent g_D>0, uniform on this entire conditional stratum, such that

sum_e h_e <= [Vol(Y_D^0)-g_D]/(2pi).           (9)

This does not need a lower bound on delta, nor compactness of the ordinary radius parameters beyond the existence of the stated representation and chain. The published gap theorem uses exactly the canonical peripheral volume just verified.

The same bound holds for any finite action limit of a sequence of these coherent regular stationary configurations, each containing a Green port. The finite meridian list and g_D are fixed, so take the limsup in (9). Clocks, row endpoints and spectral parameters may degenerate along that sequence. This is closure of ACTUAL solutions, not a density assertion for an arbitrary KKT boundary datum.

The common original resolvent numerator uses H_V and is independent of retained half-twist signs; those signs enter the outside Gaussian through n_e=epsilon_e-2p_e. Thus (9), if its source hypotheses are established for a relevant sharp remainder stratum, uses Y_D^0 with the same capacity Phi=2Vol(Q_D)/(2pi)=Vol(Y_D^epsilon)/(2pi). It is NOT an identification of the differently marked exteriors or their filling phases. The existing all-CROSS filling theorem retains the actual epsilon and framing separately.

7. Verification and remaining scope

The portable exact verifier and its saved result check four generic contact-coordinate identities, the eight actual fan shapes and 49-position differential tensor, eight real endpoint values and sixteen separately nonzero uncancelled leading anchor factors. The verifier imports only rational factor and differential utilities from the formal-word audit; no complete-color mixed-symbol claim is imported.

The global chain uses the original combinatorics of matching geometry, the abstract-chain construction in the endpoint signed cut-ball proof, and the canonical normalization and uniform conclusion of the fixed peripheral-word volume gap. Its new intersecting-circle fan calculation replaces the endpoint orthogonal-shaded-face formula; that old formula is not imported outside its tangent case.

The global proof still assumes actual signed-clock compatibility, the full original face stationary equations, the stated regular branch list and flags, and the permitted marked-line limits. It does not prove that every sharp absolute coefficient maximum has these properties, handle free spectral/row/ordinary boundary strata, or give a contributing Fourier cycle. Those are the remaining source and compact-boundary questions. The retained-half global face adapter is separate and unnecessary for this common untwisted numerator upper-bound statement.

An inconsistent phase assignment may have an exponential cancellation relative to its own positive envelope. That envelope can exceed Phi; such a relative loss alone does not bound it by Phi. No combination of that local phase exclusion with this conditional geometric theorem is being asserted to cover the missing global remainder.

8. Mixed complete Green ports in the same original chain

The shared-stationarity reduction also checks the following extension for general stationary data. Some one-sided ports may have delta=0 while another is strict positive-spectral Green. Retain their original endpoint signed triangle fans from the tangent cut-ball theorem; do not replace their parabolic collars by identity.

The exact old adapter is C=I+i r(P+I), where r=(eta_c T_c-eta_b T_b)/(2(x+y)) is real, including its literal one-line and two-line versions. Normalize the tangent outer planes to Re w=0,1. Their raw product is P=-[[1,2],[0,1]], so C acts by w->w-2ir. It therefore preserves BOTH outer planes. Its already proved simultaneous head conjugations carry the middle plane and all three original ordered contacts. This proves the same shaded pairing used in section5, without an inference from eigenratio1. Its whole peripheral subgroup is parabolic and preserves the chosen contact horosphere.

Consequently these endpoint Green fans and the new intersecting critical fans sum in ONE developed family and the SAME original two-ball relative chain. The permitted branch list in section5 can be extended by these old endpoint branches, including their proved forced two-line corners. At least one strict positive- spectral Green port still supplies the uniform trace-zero gap. If all spectral parameters are0, the existing endpoint noncross entropy gap applies separately; no trace-zero conclusion is made.

For MAXIMA of the sharp interval functional, the new reduction gives more: a positive- length nonterminal one-sided delta=0 port can be improved at fixed face loads, so such mixed endpoint ports cannot occur on that regular maximizing stratum. Diagonal and terminal strata are not removed by that argument.
