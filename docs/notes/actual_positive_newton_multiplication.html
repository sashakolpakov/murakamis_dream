Positive multiplication in the actual full Newton source algebra

29 October 2026. The actual two-head source algebra has an explicit NONNEGATIVE multiplication table in the same full Newton basis in which the genuine quarter-turn reverses coordinates. This includes every paired first normal, both level parities and composite levels. It is not a positive model substituted for the original source.

Let N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, and

c_j=q^(2j+1)+q^(-2j-1),
s_r=product_(u=1)^r 2sin(pi u/N), s_0=1,
B_r(z)=product_(j=0)^(r-1)(c_j-z),
Bhat_r=B_r/s_r^2, 0<=r<=d.

The physical algebra is H_N=C[z]/P_N with P_N=2T_N(z/2)+2. The actual Newton turn theorem identifies this with the full original Hopf commutant and proves

Rturn Bhat_r=Bhat_(d-r),
phi(Bhat_i Bhat_j)=delta_(i+j,d),
original cup-mark closure(f)=f(c_0)=alpha_0,
phi(f)=alpha_d, if f=sum_r alpha_r Bhat_r.        (1)

The turn in (1) is the actual root operator with its original cups and caps. Its simple root formula is NOT an arbitrary generic-q continuation.

1. Uniform multiplication theorem

If i+j<N, then in the ACTUAL algebra

Bhat_i Bhat_j=sum_(k=max(i,j))^(i+j) mu_(i,j,k) Bhat_k,

mu_(i,j,k)=s_(i+j)s_k
   /[s_i s_j s_(k-i)s_(k-j)s_(i+j-k)] > 0.       (2)

If i+j>=N, their product is ZERO in H_N, including all root normals. Every denominator in (2) has index less than N. The formula is symmetric in i,j and its unit cases are exact.

Here is an own generic finite proof. For generic zeta define [u]=(zetau-zeta(-u))/(zeta-zeta^(-1)), [u]!=product_[1,u], t=-(zeta-zeta(-1))2, and use the same generic nodes and B polynomials. Then

B_i B_j=sum_(k=max(i,j))^(i+j) A_(i,j,k) B_k,
A_(i,j,k)=t^(i+j-k) [i]![j]![i+j]!
   /([k]![k-i]![k-j]![i+j-k]!).                 (3)

By symmetry take i<=j. Starting at i=0, multiply by c_i-z and use

(c_i-z)B_k=B_(k+1)+(c_i-c_k)B_k,
c_i-c_k=t[k+i+1][k-i].                          (4)

The coefficient recurrence is A_(i+1,j,k)=A_(i,j,k-1)+(c_i-c_k)A_(i,j,k). After division by the nonzero interior factorial factors in (3), this is exactly the elementary identity

[i+1][i+j+1]=[k][k-j]+[k+i+1][i+j+1-k].         (5)

Expand the four powers of zeta on each side to verify (5). At the lower and upper endpoints the same recurrence follows by its zero factors or its leading coefficient 1. This proves (3) as a finite rational identity, without a summation formula or a fit to samples.

At the principal root and i+j<N, [u]!=s_u/(2sin(pi/N))^u. The powers of 2sin cancel t, and multiplication by s_k2/(s_i2s_j^2) converts (3) precisely to (2).

For i+j>=N, c_(d-u)=c_u implies the polynomial identity

B_N/B_j=B_(N-j).

Since i>=N-j, B_i B_j is divisible by B_N=(-1)^N P_N. This is divisibility WITH MULTIPLICITIES, not equality only at distinct nodes. Hence the zero assertion holds on the full physical source.

2. A positive cone preserved by genuine operations

Let C_N consist of all sum alpha_r Bhat_r with alpha_r real and >=0. Equations (1),(2) prove that C_N is preserved by:

  • actual serial multiplication of two-head boxes;
  • the genuine quarter-turn;
  • sums and multiplication by nonnegative real scalars.

Both actual closures in (1) are nonnegative on this cone. Thus any specified physical two-head circuit built from cone-valued coupons by these operations has an exact positive finite contraction. There is no cancellation of positive terms and no lost normal coefficient. Associativity is inherited from the actual polynomial/operator algebra. No separate move normalization or conjectural topological invariance has been imposed on a new state sum.

This does NOT say that a crossing R^e is cone-valued, that an arbitrary multiport graph reduces to this alphabet, or that every such circuit is hyperbolic. A proposed application must supply its actual ordered two-head decomposition and preserve its original marking.

The nonzero test is also finite. A multiplication gate permits precisely

i+j<N, max(i,j)<=k<=i+j.                        (6)

A turn replaces r by d-r. A closure fixes r=0 or r=d. Therefore a whole typed circuit with strictly positive allowed input coordinates is nonzero if and only if at least one internal label assignment satisfies (6) and its turn/closure constraints. This is an exact finite support certificate, not a claim that topology alone ensures it.

3. The original complete circle belongs to this cone at every level

For the actual complete circle use

L_N(z)=(-1)^d U_d(z/2)=(-1)^d P_N'(z)/N.

The original Frobenius functional is phi(f)=(-1)^d N2[zd](f mod P_N). Consequently

phi(L_N f)=N Tr(m_f)=N sum_(j=0)^d f(c_j),       (7)

where m_f is multiplication by f in H_N. To prove the trace identity, first take a monic polynomial with distinct roots and apply its exact Lagrange interpolation to P’ f mod P. Its leading coefficient is the sum of f at those roots. Both sides are polynomial expressions in the coefficients of P and f, so equality extends to repeated roots by clearing the interpolation denominators. The trace counts algebraic multiplicity and does not omit the paired blocks.

By (1), the r-th Newton coefficient of L_N is phi(L_N Bhat_(d-r)). Put M=floor(d/2), v=d-r, and let k_l be 2 on a paired node and 1 on the odd simple node. Formula (7) gives the explicit coefficients

lambda_r=0, r<d-M,
lambda_r=N sum_(l=v)^M k_l
            s_(l+v)/[s_(l-v)s_v^2]>0, r>=d-M. (8)

Indeed Bhat_v(c_l)=0 for l<v, while for v<=l<=M the factorization

c_t-c_l=4sin((l+t+1)pi/N)sin((l-t)pi/N)

gives Bhat_v(c_l)=s_(l+v)/(s_(l-v)s_v^2). For v>M the polynomial already vanishes at every root, so its trace is zero. This proves (8) with all multiplicities, including N=2 and both parities.

Thus complete-circle/turn/multiplication circuits give a genuine all-level positive family inside the original source theory. Their exact zeros remain important: for even N the support in (8) starts at N/2, so L_N^2=0 by (2). Positivity does not hide that zero.

The same statement holds for EACH complete-circle sector. Let E_j be the full Hermite cluster projector and L_j=L_N E_j. Formula (7) with f replaced by E_j f gives phi(L_j f)=N k_j f(c_j). Thus, with the original paired multiplicity k_j retained,

Rturn(L_j)/(N k_j)=h_j
   =sum_(v=0)^j s_(j+v)/[s_(j-v)s_v^2] Bhat_v. (8a)

Every coefficient is strictly positive and every other coefficient is zero. This applies to both parities, including paired nilpotent sectors; it does not replace L_j by a projector. No derivative of f is missing: the derivative in L_j is precisely the full Frobenius/trace identity that produced (8a).

4. Odd simple projector and the primitive cube period

At N=2m+1, the simple Steinberg node is c_m=-2. Its genuine spectral projector is

E_St=B_m^2/N^2=Bhat_m^2,                        (9)

because B_m(c_m)=N and s_m^2=N; it has the required DOUBLE zero at every paired node. Apply (2) and the actual turn to obtain

h=Rturn(E_St)=sum_(v=0)^m beta(m,v)^2 Bhat_v,
beta(m,v)=s_m/[s_v s_(m-v)]>0.                 (10)

All coefficients in (10) are positive; this proof uses the complete polynomial projector rather than interpolation at simple values. Write h^2=sum a_k Bhat_k. Formula (2) gives

a_k=sum_(0<=i,j<=m, max(i,j)<=k<=i+j)
                 beta(m,i)^2 beta(m,j)^2 mu_(i,j,k)>0
                                          for EVERY 0<=k<=d. (11)

For nonemptiness choose i+j=k with i,j<=m. Therefore

phi(h^4)=sum_(k=0)^d a_k a_(d-k)>0.             (12)

No identity Rturn(h2)=h2 is needed for (12). In particular a root observation of that identity is not being continued generically. The separate primitive-cube calculation must still prove its actual normalization and the asymptotic/geometric interpretation of this period. Equation (12) supplies its cancellation-free positive form.

More generally every h^p, p>=2, has positive coefficients at ALL indices 0,…,d: h has a positive constant term and (11) starts the induction. In particular phi(h^p)>0 for every such p. This is an exact period statement, not an automatic topological or volume identification for each power.

Audit: generic finite products and full root-algebra verification. The uniform proofs above establish positivity; finite tests independently check the formulas, the actual repeated-root quotient and complete-circle coefficients. They are not a general graph volume theorem.
