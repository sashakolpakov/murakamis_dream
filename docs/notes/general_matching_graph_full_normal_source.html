The full original augmented-plat source on its matching graph

30 October 2026. EVERY fixed original augmented plat has an exact state sum on the SAME marked cubic graph Delta as its geometric projection/disk cut. The nonmatching edges carry the physical V_N; a matching edge carries an ordinary Q_j=V_(2j), summed over ALL folded labels. Every term is the original first normal of its genuine generic pivotal closure. The statement holds at both parities and composite levels, with the actual paired nilpotent circle sectors retained.

This is a full complete-circle source identity, not merely a selected central sector. It supplies the source-compatible graph to be resummed. It proves no positivity, dominant branch, mixed auxiliary-color packet, or volume asymptotic.

1. The actual complete-circle factorization

Fix an original 2k-plat with r crossing boxes, all outside wires and cups/caps recorded, signed retained exponents epsilon_i, and ONE exposed original marked physical arc. Put

d=N-1, M=floor(d/2), q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2,
s_u=product_(v=1)^u 2sin(pi v/N), s_0=1.

The all-input complete circle is the sum of its full folded sectors. The proved physical factorization in the original all-channel source is

L_N=sum_(j=0)^M L_j,
R^epsilon L_j=omega_j^epsilon h0_j W_j pi_j,
pi_j=G_j^-1 W_j^dagger P,
G_(j,a)=s_(2j)/(s_a s_(2j-a)),
h0_j=k_j N s_(2j)/s_j^2,
k_j=2 if 2j<d, k_j=1 if 2j=d,
omega_j=q^(d^2/2) (-1)^j q^[j(j+1)].                  (1)

Here W_j:Q_j->V_N tensor V_N is the actual first-entry-one Clebsch map, and P is the original two-head reversal. Crucially,

pi_j W_j=0 on a paired node,
pi_j W_j=I on the odd simple node 2j=d.                 (2)

Thus W_j pi_j is not generally a projector. Formula (1) includes the circle’s original first-normal coefficient before introducing Q_j; no semisimple root quotient is made. All input physical degrees are included in (1).

2. Split the actual graph, preserving its typed vertices

For a fixed label vector j=(j_1,…,j_r), split each coupon as W_j pi_j. Its intermediate edge Q_j is exactly the crossing-disk edge d_i in Delta. The remaining legs follow the actual outside main intervals and carry V_N. The vertex cyclic orders are the disk-side sectors of the matching-graph construction. Consequently the state graph has

matching edges D: Q_(j_i),
nonmatching edges: V_N,
vertices: the actual ordered W_(j_i) or pi_(j_i).       (3)

Every vertex has two physical legs and one matching-channel leg. This requires no bipartite coloring, no Fisher structure, and no ordering of all black vertices below all white ones. Cups/caps and actual head reversals are retained at every bend. One does not replace a cyclic turn by an unclocked index permutation.

Let Z_Delta(j) denote the contraction in (3) with the original exposed physical mark, after removing only the displayed scalar factors in (1). Multilinearity of the actual ordered coupon word gives

A_(D,N)=sum_(j in {0,...,M}^r)
    product_i[h0_(j_i) omega_(j_i)^epsilon_i] Z_Delta(j). (4)

This is the raw source before its independently recorded exterior framing line. Physical coupons in overlapping strand positions have not been commuted. Only the independent finite color sums distribute over their original order.

3. An explicit regular generic lift at EVERY folded label

Keep N and the root constants h0_j,omega_j fixed. Let zeta=q exp(t) and use the generic balanced highest-weight-d object V_d(zeta), with Q=zeta^2 and F_u=(Q;Q)_u. Its actual physical cup is

pV_(a,d-a)=N zeta^(-d^2) (-1)^a
            zeta^[a(d-a-1)] F_a F_(d-a)/F_d,
ellV=pV^-1.                                          (5)

On Q_j choose the ordinary highest-weight-2j cup pQ_j with first coefficient one, given by the same formula with d replaced by 2j and the initial scale omitted. All its factorial indices are <N.

The regular W_j(t) has highest degree l=d-j and coefficients

h_u(t)=(-1)^u zeta^(-du)
    Q^[u(l-1)-u(u-1)/2] (Q^(j+1);Q)_u F_(d-u)/F_d,
W_(j,a)(t)=F_pair(t)^a h(t)/F_a,
0<=a<=2j.                                            (6)

All denominator indices in (5),(6) are below N. The original highest recurrence and divided coproduct prove its generic Hopf covariance, including its last endpoint; this is the already checked finite construction in the cited all-channel note.

Put gamma_j=q(2-j2)/N^2, a FIXED nonzero root constant, and define

pi_j(t)=gamma_j^-1 D_t(W_j(t)),
D_t(W_j)=pQ_j(t)^T W_j(t)^T
                    [ellV(t)^T tensor ellV(t)^T] P.    (7)

At t=0 this is exactly pi_j of (1). Equation (7) retains the ordinary cup normalization, both physical inverse cups, reversed tensor order and gamma_j. It divides by NO vanishing paired norm pi_j W_j.

Thus W_j(t)pi_j(t) is a regular generic Hopf coupon whose root is the actual normalized circle sector, at both parities. This is a chosen lift of a fixed-root map. It is not asserted to be the original circle’s entire moving-q germ, nor a new off-root link invariant. That distinction causes no ambiguity in the marked root scalar below.

4. Every marked sector is ONE retained trace normal

Let F_Delta(j;t) be the ordinary closed original diagram obtained by using the regular W_j(t)pi_j(t) coupons, the original outside cups/caps, and an ordinary cap also at the chosen marked arc. Splitting its coupons gives exactly the typed generic network (3), with (5)–(7).

Leave that exposed physical arc open. Generic Hopf covariance makes the resulting endomorphism of V_d a scalar z_Delta(j;t) I: generic K has distinct weights and every interior F arrow is nonzero, so its commutant is scalar. All entries are regular, so z_Delta is holomorphic. The literal physical closure therefore gives

F_Delta(j;t)=D_N(t) z_Delta(j;t),
D_N(t)=(-1)^d [N]_(q exp(t)),
D_N(0)=0,
nu_N=D_N'(0)=2N(-1)^N/(q-q^-1) !=0.                    (8)

The original unrestricted root marked row has m pV(0)=1. After the other actual caps act, the remaining column is z_Delta(j;0)pV(0), so that mark extracts precisely z_Delta(j;0). Hence

Z_Delta(j)=F_Delta'(j;0)/nu_N.                         (9)

Only the physical trace factor is divided out. The mark is not put on a channel Q_j, whose dimension or norm could vanish, and no bare mark is moved through an enclosing cap. The exposed one-head cut is part of the specified original diagram.

If another regular generic Hopf lift has the same root coupons, it has the same root open endomorphism and marked scalar. Its ordinary closure differs by D_N(t) times a holomorphic scalar vanishing at zero, so its normalized first derivative is the same. This proves the required lift independence without identifying moving-q germs.

Combining (4),(9) gives the full source theorem:

A_(D,N)=(1/nu_N) sum_(j in {0,...,M}^r)
   product_i[h0_(j_i) omega_(j_i)^epsilon_i]
                              F_Delta'(j;0).         (10)

Equivalently the first derivative is taken after summing the regular finite physical networks. For fixed N the sum is finite. Its regular physical presentation needs only first jets; a singular recoupling frame may require higher Laurent coefficients before its pole cancellations recover this same first normal.

5. The special odd endpoint and the exact geometric target

For N=2m+1 and all j_i=m, Q_j=V_N, h0_m=N and pi_m W_m=I. Then all edges of Delta carry the same ordinary object, and (10)’s central contribution is

N^r omega_m^(sum epsilon_i) F_Delta'(m,...,m;0)/nu_N.

This recovers the earlier endpoint interface, without extending its projector argument to even levels. Equation (10) includes the paired sectors at every parity instead of discarding them.

If Delta has the actual positive right-angled ideal polyhedron Q_Delta, its shaded triangles are the source vertices in (3), and its ideal vertices are the source edges. The literal quotient gives the actual complete augmented volume 2Vol(Q_Delta). Coning Q_Delta and using the identical subdivisions in its second copy gives a marked positive ideal triangulation T. Its GLOBAL angle polytope is

sum_(three opposite pairs in t) alpha_(t,a)=pi,
sum_(all tetrahedral incidences at global e) alpha_(t,e)=2pi,
alpha>=0.                                             (11)

All incidence coefficients come from the actual complete face-pairing words, including the shaded half twists. The proved positive gluing theorem gives its unique positive maximum and absolute value

max_(11) sum_t V_ideal(alpha_t)=2Vol(Q_Delta).          (12)

A separately certified marked positive triangulation may replace Q_Delta.

Thus (10) and (11) are now on the same actual topology. An exact coherent resummation must STILL show how its state variables map to these edge incidences and how its retained source action equals the single constrained functional (12). Ordinary spin triangle inequalities or independently maximized vertex factorials do not supply (11). The original phase obstruction excludes independent magnetic-leg positive gauges; it does not exclude a coherent positive resummation.

The NC class is recovered when Delta is the Fisher expansion of its source graph Gamma. Its facial triangle contractions give the known local coefficients and the Gamma-network first normal. The odd cube’s clocked rung resummation gives a positive global Newton period and its shared core action. Those are actual special reductions of (10), not a proof that every Delta has the same positive form.

6. What this settles and what remains

Equation (10) is a full-source matching-graph state sum for every fixed plat width. It retains all internal folded labels, both level parities, all paired nilpotent circles, actual cup/dual constants and one original mark. No vanishing root norm is inverted. The external line is not renormalized using a hoped-for answer.

It permits zero sectors and zero whole source values. It does not prove endpoint dominance or even complete-color nonvanishing. It is not yet the mixed auxiliary-circle-color packet required for long fillings. Those fillings still require the whole-color gap, selected geometric cycle, actual peripheral branch and unit logarithmic charge. A formal first-normal formula alone provides none of those estimates.

7. Coverage only and exact verification

Van der Veen, arXiv:0805.0094v2, Definitions 1–2 and Theorem 2, starts with a specified KTG-move sequence and gives a sufficient augmentation multiplicity. Its stated formula holds at ODD N and is zero at EVEN N, with an explicitly so(3) conclusion. Its singly augmented outside is built from regular ideal octahedra. That is not an all-level theorem for every single- circle matching graph or for all independently long fillings. Only its definitions and theorem statements are used here for coverage.

Ibarra–McQuire–Purcell addresses OCTAHEDRAL fully augmented links, states its TV result without half twists, and sets Dehn fillings aside. The invariant and root differ from the original marked source here. Belletti states a Yokota upper-bound conjecture, results for a specified large family and TV consequences. Neither supplies the original coherent first-normal estimate for arbitrary Delta. No proof technique from those papers is used here.

The exact audit checks eight original plats at N=2,…,6 and widths k=2,3,4. It checks 204 fixed-label generic trace normals and five EXHAUSTIVE color sums, using independently constructed original Casimir/circle/R matrices. It explicitly verifies the paired zero pi_j W_j, the complete circle sum, actual signed twists, and the unrestricted marked contraction. The outside-interval compiler also checks every oriented matching-graph face. The regular generic jets retain (5)–(7) before specialization.

Recorded results. These are exact source-identity checks, not finite-level evidence for an unproved nonvanishing or asymptotic claim.

The four-box width-two fixture has positions (1,0,2,1), zero based. Its two middle circles surround complementary pairs in a transverse four-punctured sphere and cobound the unpunctured annulus between those pairs. They are parallel augmentations, so this fixture is NOT an essential hyperbolic skeleton. The source also explains its even zeros: on the regular four-head invariant space the two complementary pair Casimirs agree, by the actual generic cup duality. Their circle product is therefore L_N^2, which is zero at even levels. The retained disjoint half twists commute with this product. These exact zeros are not being presented as hyperbolic counterexamples. The audit tests the finite source identity even on such degenerate diagrams.

The even-zero fixture is the stated parallel-circle obstruction. Formula (10) does not imply nonvanishing, select a geometric branch, or give a general asymptotic.
