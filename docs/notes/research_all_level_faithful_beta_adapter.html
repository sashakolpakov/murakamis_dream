An all-level local lift of the faithful beta packet

Written proof, 23 November 2026. This note defines an explicit marked local lift of the nine-cell kernels to every integer level and evaluates its two faithful boundary packets. After the same continuant completion, they are exactly the original positive and negative physical crossings. Every Casimir normal and every displayed scalar is retained.

At odd levels the local kernels are precisely the earlier charged kernels. At even levels this is a specified local extension, with integer charge lifts and the physical square root of the quantum parameter. It is not attributed to an existing even-level charged invariant. No global charged Pachner theory, tetrahedral symmetry, positive geometric period or volume lower bound is asserted. The earlier odd-level final [V] and all frozen files remain unchanged.
