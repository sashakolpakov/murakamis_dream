Three complete belts: the saturated source and the marked-pair obstruction

29 October 2026. On the genuine six-head saturated source, three disjoint positive complete-circle forms define an explicit positive Gram. They do NOT have a finite dual norm for the original marked co-source. There is an all-level source vector whose three-belt energy is zero and whose original marked evaluation is exactly ONE.

The correctly typed original cap inequality uses C tensor C tensor H, not C tensor C tensor C. It detects the same vector with a polynomial lower bound. Thus a simple tensor-cube DCT or a scalar Frobenius branch cannot be used to claim a marked trivalent source theorem. This result retains the full divided-current kernel and root normals.

1. The actual six-port space and the three circle operators

Use d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d, N>=2. The balanced physical generators are Ecal=-F^dagger, Kdagger=K-1. Let

L=(-1)^d U_d(Omega/2), C=P L>=0, H=L P=P C P>=0,

where P exchanges the two physical legs, not a braid crossing. All the literal ordinary-circle pivots and Cartan phases are retained. Their positivity and kernels are the complete-circle theorem.

Let S be the SATURATED generic-q specialization inside the full six-head H=0 invariants. The divided-power source theorem identifies it, with every original cup/braid word included, as

S = Inv_root,H=0 intersect ker J_E,6,
ext(S)=ker(J_E,4:W->Llow),
W=(V_N^tensor4)_(degree2d), dim S=(N^3+N)/2.         (1)

The maps Phi:W->Inv and ext are the specified actual source maps, with ext extracting final physical colors(0,d) times x^d/N. No semisimple projection is substituted for (1).

On W the three disjoint physical pair Casimirs become

A1=Omega12, A2=Omega34,
A3=F4 Ecal4+(q+q^-1)I.                              (2)

For completeness A3 is EXACTLY ext Omega56 Phi. Split the six heads into A=1234 and B=56. On an invariant vector, E_B=-K_B E_A, F_B=-K_A F_A, K_B=K_A^-1. Using F_B K_B=Q K_B F_B and E_A K_A=Q^-1 K_A E_A gives F_B E_B=E_A F_A on that vector. The actual commutator [Ecal_A,F_A]=(q-q-1)(K_A-K_A-1) therefore turns Omega_B into Omega_A. On W, K_A=1, proving (2). In particular the reduced operators in (2) are not guessed trace variables. They commute on the actual invariant space and preserve S.

The positive unmarked-belt form is

G_C(v,v)=v^dagger(C12 tensor C34 tensor C56)v.         (3)

Its kernel equals that of L12 L34 L56, since the product of the three physical P maps is invertible. This kernel assertion does not identify (3) with an original marked link closure.

2. Full cluster projectors, including their first normals

Let c0=q+q^-1. Its actual root cluster is {0,d}: c0=cd, and it is DOUBLE for every N>=2. The distinct other nodes are c_j=q(2j+1)+q(-2j-1), 1<=j<=floor(d/2). Let e0(z) be the FULL Hermite cluster polynomial: value one and derivative zero at c0, and value/derivative zero at the other double nodes (value zero at the possible odd simple node). It may be given by

l0(z)=product_(c!=c0)(z-c)/(c0-c),
e0(z)=l0(z)^2[1-2l0'(c0)(z-c0)] mod P_N(z).         (4)

All denominators are nonzero at the fixed level. Thus e0(Ai) is an actual root cluster projector, not an eigenvalue-only substitution. Write E000=e0(A1)e0(A2)e0(A3). It preserves S and every root normal within the chosen joint cluster. Its rank on S is five, as also follows from the five admissible generic branches of {0,d}^3.

The regular generic pair projectors give an elementary annihilation identity. The branch (d,0,0) has no generic invariant: a positive spin d cannot couple two spin-zero channels to an invariant. Its joint projector is therefore zero on the saturated generic source lattice. Multiply this projector by the THREE within-cluster gap denominators before specializing q. This gives on E000 S

(A1-c0)(A2-c0)(A3-c0)=0.                            (5)

There is no division by a root-zero gap in (5); it is the root value of an exact cleared generic identity on the actual lattice. The complete circle on this cluster is

L_N(Ai)E000=L_N'(c0)(Ai-c0)E000,
(4-c0^2)L_N'(c0)=(-1)^d 2N !=0.

Consequently, with ALL first normal data retained,

L_N(A1)L_N(A2)L_N(A3)E000 S=0.                      (6)

This argument does not assume that a rank-eight generic cube would specialize to a regular three-variable dual-number module.

3. An original planar source whose marked value survives

Take the actual nested planar cups joining physical ends (1,6),(2,3),(4,5). The original cup is

p_(a,b)=N x^(-b) delta_(a+b,d).

Let v_triangle be their tensor insertion in the physical order. It has the regular generic cup lift, so belongs to S. Its exact W coordinate w_triangle=ext(v_triangle) is

w_triangle_(0,b,d-b,d)=N^2 x^(-(d-b)), 0<=b<=d,
all other coefficients zero.                        (7)

The ORIGINAL whole marked co-source on W is

lambda=ell12 tensor ell34,
ell_(a,b)=x^a/N delta_(a+b,d).                       (8)

This is the result of the full six-head invariant identity, not a local replacement of an unrestricted marked row. The only surviving term in lambda w_triangle is b=d, and hence

lambda w_triangle=1.                               (9)

On the whole invariant source lambda is a left c0 eigenvector of ALL three Ai. For A1,A2 this is the original ordinary-cap intertwining equation. After those two caps the remaining pair is an actual invariant cup, with Casimir c0; applying the full marked row afterward proves the A3 assertion too. Thus

lambda E000=lambda on S.

Set u=Phi E000 w_triangle. It is an explicit nonzero original saturated-source vector, with regular cluster coupons and no unknown knot invariant in its definition. Equations (6),(9) prove

G_C(u,u)=0,     original_marked_row(u)=1.             (10)

Therefore there is NO finite constant B_N, even one allowed to grow exponentially, such that the original marked functional satisfies

|original_marked_row(v)|^2 <= B_N G_C(v,v)

for all v in S. This is an actual source Riesz obstruction, not a generic indefinite-metric example or an absolute-value estimate. It does not assert a counterexample to a closed link’s volume limit: u uses known Casimir cluster coupons, and the failed statement is the proposed all-exterior marked positive sewing theorem.

4. The genuine marked metric evades the obstruction

The correctly typed original three-pair form is

The original cap/source inequality gives, after the WHOLE degree restriction of the marked pair,

In particular the same explicit source vector (10) satisfies

The distinction is the physical last-pair reversal, not a harmless choice of positive inner-product coordinates. Thus the joint simplex support of the three unreflected pair-Casimir product cannot simply be transferred to (11) by keeping S unchanged.

5. What remains possible at a genuine trivalent junction

For the three-C observation, joint positivity gives a sum of positive channel weights, but its original source dual already fails (10). The joint branch geometry also removes a full tensor-cube DCT: missing admissible joint nodes cannot reconstruct arbitrary products of three independent half-bandwidth polynomials. A correct simplex interpolation/angle weight would describe that observed quotient, not restore the missing marked functional.

One may instead keep (11), change the entire reflected source by the actual duality/permutation braid, or point an auxiliary belt using its FULL open-loop normal source. Each is a concrete different physical problem. The present result neither deletes that current nor identifies a physical leg flip with an added crossing. A geometric trivalent-source survival theorem would need such a source-compatible kernel before its angle or DCT estimate.

There is an explicit correctly marked all-input kernel, useful for stating that next problem without an unknown recoupling frame. Use the orthonormal single-pair quotient rows of the positive circle quotient,

0<=j<=floor(d/2), 0<=r<=2j.

Here E_j is the stated value Lagrange polynomial; use of values is valid only AFTER its actual complete-circle map, and its g_j, factorials and angle normalizations are exactly those proved there. Define on every W input by the FULL original Phi

  =(pi_(j1,r1) tensor pi_(j2,r2) tensor pi_(j3,r3) P56)Phi_w.

Then G_m(Phi_w,Phi_w) is EXACTLY the sum of |K|^2 over all these indices. Integer degree imposes r1+r2+r3=j1+j2+j3, including every endpoint. The original scalar, not an auxiliary unknown invariant, is precisely

This explains the polynomial dual constant in (12). It also locates the missing moment-map constraint. With Pi=pi tensor pi tensor pi, the vector K=Pi P56 v for v in S satisfies

Bounding the coherent kernel (14), or constructing a differently pointed reflected closure with its full source, remains the next analytic step; the tensor-cube sampling proof does not supply it.
