Polynomially conditioned signed Green transport with the original source

28 October 2026. The physical crossing Green equation has a useful whole-word consequence: its complete clock-family transport and inverse have polynomial norms for every fixed signed diagram and every bridge count. The original contraction is transported as a functional, rather than replaced by a positive contraction or a geometric quotient.

This is an ambient source-transport theorem. It does NOT give a lower bound for the restriction of that functional to geometric data. In fact the complete ground data already contain the original positive crossing entries by an isometric reindexing. That fact sharply limits what can be deduced from the norm theorem alone.

1. The actual signed clock families

Fix N>=2, Q=exp(2pii/N), q=exp(pi*i/N), x=-q^(-1), and F_a=(Q;Q)_a. Use the SAME balanced finite heads V=C^N as in the literal crossing source. Keep the original unit-modulus Cartan scalar kappa external. Give End(V tensor V) its flat Hilbert–Schmidt norm, and put

E_N = direct_sum_(m in Z/N) End(V tensor V),
||X||^2 = sum_m ||X_m||_HS^2.

The physical Green theorem constructs ACTUAL internal-clock insertions K_m^+, and their full retained ground sources H_m^+, with

K_m^+ - A_+ K_(m+1)^+ = H_m^+,
(A_+ X)_(c,d;a,b) = x^(-1) X_(c,d-1;a-1,b).       (1)

An out-of-range index on the right means zero. In particular A_+ retains both physical barriers. It is a partial coordinate shift times a unit scalar, so ||A_+||<=1 and A_+^N=0.

The signed transport proof shows that at this COMPLETE color the balanced negative crossing is obtained by flip and complex conjugation:

R_- = P overline(R_+) P.                          (2)

This includes kappa, since its conjugate is its inverse. For clarity, the proof starts from the original unbalanced crossing conjugation relation with its Cartan diagonal D. The balanced basis change has g_j/overline(g_j)=(-1)^j q^(j(j+1)). Its residual diagonal on a pair (a,b) is q^((a+b)(a+b+2)), depending ONLY on total integer degree. It therefore commutes with both the crossing and the flip, and cancels from the conjugation relation. No exponentially sized factorial gauge is used as an isometry.

Consequently K_m^-=P overline(K_m^+)P and H_m^-=P overline(H_m^+)P are actual negative-crossing clock families, and their equation is (1) with

(A_- X)_(c,d;a,b) = x X_(c-1,d;a,b-1).             (3)

The label m names the conjugated original insertion; it does not reassign its physical clock orientation. Again ||A_-||<=1 and A_-^N=0 in the SAME flat metric. These assertions concern the complete color; a fixed nonunit color cannot be included by silently treating |x| as one.

2. A bounded inverse on the entire clock space

Define T_s on E_N by (T_s X)m=A_s X(m+1), s=+,-. The clock shift is a cyclic isometry and commutes with A_s. Hence

T_s^N=0,  ||T_s||<=1,
G_s=(1-T_s)^(-1)=sum_(j=0)^(N-1) T_s^j,
||G_s||<=N,  ||G_s^(-1)||<=2.                     (4)

Thus the full actual family satisfies K^s=G_s H^s. This is an isomorphism on ALL of E_N, not only on vectors meeting a guessed geometric support condition. It preserves the currents already combined into the explicit H_m; none is set to zero.

Nilpotence here has index N. What makes (4) useful is the contraction bound on EACH power, not nilpotence by itself. A nilpotent matrix with exponentially large entries would not give (4).

For a fixed word with r signed crossings put

E_W = tensor_(v=1)^r E_N,
G_W = tensor_(v=1)^r G_(s_v),
k_W = tensor_v K^(s_v),  h_W = tensor_v H^(s_v).

The tensor Hilbert norm gives the general, all-bridge theorem

k_W=G_W h_W,
||G_W||<=N^r,  ||G_W^(-1)||<=2^r.                 (5)

In particular this change of the COMPLETE data has zero exponential conditioning cost as N tends to infinity for any fixed word. Neither the word nor its bridge count is allowed to grow with N in this claim.

3. Carry the specified original co-source

Let L_W be the EXACT original linear tensor contraction: evaluate each external clock label at zero, connect the original head indices in their original order, and apply the specified cups, caps, pivotal weights and external normalization line. In a braid presentation the same statement uses its original selected cut and pivotal closure. No new trace or averaging is substituted for that selected functional. The original scalar is therefore

K_N(W)=L_W(k_W)=(L_W composed with G_W)(h_W).       (6)

Define L_W^G=L_W composed with G_W. Equations (4)–(5) imply, for the dual norm on the FULL ambient tensor space,

2^(-r)||L_W|| <= ||L_W^G|| <= N^r||L_W||.          (7)

The lower inequality follows from L_W=L_W^G composed with G_W^(-1), so it requires no positivity assertion. The fixed original complete source has polynomial coefficients and polynomially many tensor entries: balanced pivotal entries have unit modulus, and the original cup/marked-cap normalizations are fixed powers of N times unit color phases. Thus its norm is polynomially bounded for a fixed diagram. The exact external factors are retained in L_W on BOTH sides of (7).

For any vector h and nonzero L_W, the elementary Hilbert-space identity dist(v,ker L)=|L(v)|/||L|| now gives

2^(-r) dist(h,ker L_W^G)
   <= dist(G_W h,ker L_W)
   <= N^r dist(h,ker L_W^G).                     (8)

This quantifies preservation of the actual cancellation problem. It does not replace distance by ||h||. A tensor can be large and belong to the exact kernel. Nor does (7) give a lower bound for L_W^G RESTRICTED to a geometric submodule or to the actual ground-support subspace; that restriction can vanish.

4. The complete ground data have an exact visible-support metric

The positive sources are, for alpha+beta<N,

H_m^+|alpha,beta> =
   Q^(m beta) x^(-beta) {alpha+beta choose alpha}_Q
      (Q^(m+1);Q)_alpha |alpha+beta,0>.            (9)

Choose 0<=m<N. The coefficient vanishes if alpha+m>=N, because the displayed Pochhammer then contains 1-Q^N. Every remaining entry maps, under the mth Green term, to the original crossing entry with

input  (alpha+m,beta),
output (alpha+beta,m),

and changes only by the unit scalar x^(-m). Distinct entries in (9) give distinct crossing entries, and every nonzero original crossing entry is obtained this way. Therefore, exactly,

sum_m ||H_m^+||_HS^2 = ||R_+/kappa||_HS^2.        (10)

Flip-conjugation gives the identical negative statement. In particular

(sum_m ||K_m^s||_HS^2)^(1/2) <= N ||R_s||_HS.     (11)

Here |kappa|=1. Equation (11) controls ALL these internal clock responses despite the factorial frames in their definition. For a fixed signed word, (5) and (10) give the analogous tensor bound

||k_W|| <= N^r product_v ||R_(s_v)||_HS.

This does not bound the clock-inserted SCALAR by the uninserted knot scalar: contraction can cancel, and (8) is the exact distinction.

The endpoint channel is not of lower exponential order. Already H_0 has the norm computed in the local source note,

||H_0||^2=max_(t<N)|F_t|^2 sum_(b=0)^t |F_b|^(-2),

with a strictly positive exponential rate. The ground presentation therefore transports the original bulk difficulty; it does not prove that all nonground or current contributions are negligible.

5. What remains on the critical path

We now have an exact polynomially conditioned signed transport with the ORIGINAL functional, without dropping peripheral or endpoint/current terms. This answers a specified transport-norm question for this presentation. It does not identify a geometric quotient, prove that L_W^G is nonzero on the marked Bloch-regulator sector, or bound the total remainder by a smaller exponential. Those are still necessary for the requested volume limit. In particular (5) is not completion of the general source-coefficient gate: that gate concerns a geometric projection, whereas (5) concerns the complete ambient data.

The audit and results verify signed finite transport, nilpotence, the visible-support isometry, and the unchanged original contraction on a nonfactorable ambient test as well as actual crossing tensors. The all-N statements follow from the index and operator proofs above.

6. A global bound with several actual gluing-source insertions

The complete crossing normal source is a different operator from the unweighted H_m in (9). At complete color it obeys the sharp flat bound ||L||=N-1 for N>=3 and sqrt(2) for N=2. Flip-conjugation defines the opposite signed insertion in the same metric. Since its rank is at most N^2, its Hilbert–Schmidt norm is O(N^2); every original scalar factor is still attached.

The uniform weighted crossing estimate proves at zero weights

log ||R_+||_HS = log ||R_-||_HS
              = N v_8/(2*pi)+O(log N),

where v_8=4 Catalan is the regular ideal octahedron volume. This constant is an exact local norm rate, not the volume of the knot.

For a fixed original word with r crossings, replace any s distinct crossing slots by these actual L operators (or their signed versions), and retain its SAME original source. The polynomial source norm and tensor norm inequality give unconditionally

|L_W(tensor with s such insertions)|
   <= N^C exp[N (r-s)v_8/(2*pi)].                 (12)

Here C depends on the fixed diagram, not on N. This is valid for every bridge count and every placement of the specified insertions. It does not assert they form a nilpotent ideal: in particular their degree-N carry contains a nonzero idempotent.

Consequently these terms are exponentially smaller than a POSITIVE target of rate V/(2*pi) whenever (r-s)v_8<V. This is a precise sufficient condition, not a claim that the current decomposition of the original invariant already has that many insertions. A single local source replacement generally leaves a bound much larger than Vol(K). Equation (12) controls some whole-source terms, but neither bounds the remaining invariant sharply nor proves its geometric lower bound.
