A-polynomials, selected periods and the volume problem

25 November 2026. This page distinguishes the algebraic objects in the research program. The papers state the proved results and their hypotheses.

A classical branch does not select a quantum solution

An algebraic gluing curve records possible peripheral eigenvalues (M,L)(M,L). A recurrence relates consecutive terms of a sequence. Neither datum alone specifies the scalar obtained by the original knot evaluation. Initial values, the prescribed closing, normalization and derivative data at singular steps can all be needed to select it.

The all-level period note explains these additional data in its recurrence formulation. Recovering the same invariant transfers an already established limit for a covered knot. It does not prove a volume theorem for every quantization of a classical curve.

The meridian-expansion paper proves classical-symbol divisibility under a specified genuine annihilating operator. It does not construct that operator. The preferred-longitude paper supplies the geometric peripheral comparison under its stated conditions. A selected irreducible factor and a full recurrence ideal are different conclusions.

Why the period and torsor viewpoint remains useful

A change of coordinates can preserve an algebraic equation while changing a scalar factor, a boundary functional or a chosen section. Those data must be retained. The initialized coideal period constructs a source and marked observation with explicit commuting unipotent changes between neighboring-height presentations. It is finite algebra at the prescribed level; it does not identify a knot peripheral germ or geometric integration cycle.

The coideal Fourier compiler retains the crossing and cup source in orthogonal coordinates. The cyclic Hardy comparison controls actual scalar functions and their finite coefficient projections. A lower bound for the original marked closure still requires a further argument.

The unresolved implication

To cross arbitrary short partial fillings, one must show that the original selected source has the required geometric exponential rate, with its prescribed closing and cancellation retained. A local algebraic critical point, nonzero abstract solution or positive ambient norm is insufficient. The research status states the remaining theorem and the orthogonal variational reduction.
