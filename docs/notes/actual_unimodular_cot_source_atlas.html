Exact cotangent source atlases and their universal-transport limitation

28 October 2026. The TU pulling atlas of the original physical source has a finite compatible residue implementation, with explicit fixed integer coefficients and no exponentially conditioned chart adapter. Elementary cotangent changes retain calculable side currents. Artificial chart divisors cancel in their complete aggregate.

There is an essential limit: these are UNIVERSAL identities on every holomorphic test function. Such identities cannot move the whole source to a cycle on which the completed original state is smaller than its largest physical lattice value. This follows from a direct holomorphic hull argument below, and does not require an unproved knot example. Thus the atlas solves an algebraic source-coordinate problem, not the state-specific cancellation needed for the sharp volume bound.

1. The exact original source functional

Fix a nonempty one-component signed braid with m crossings, put r=m+1 and d=N-1, and keep its actual integral coordinates u=(t,k_1,…,k_m). Let

Lambda_N=dP intersect Z^r,
T_N(f)=sum_(n in Lambda_N) f(n).                 (1)

Here P is the physical polytope in the physical-face proof. The cardinal-completed original scalar F_N is holomorphic on the specified O(N) complex box D, equals the EXACT original state at each valid n, and vanishes at every invalid integer state in the box. The complete original invariant is chi_N/N times T_N(F_N), with kappa, pivots, half-Cartan and every conversion phase inside F_N. No scalar line is altered below.

Let kappa_0(s)=1/(1-exp(2piis)); this symbol denotes a cotangent kernel, NOT the original crossing scalar kappa. At an integer n its residue is -1/(2pi*i). Thus, on a sufficiently small product torus Gamma_n around n,

integral_(Gamma_n) f(u) product_i[-2*pi*i*kappa_0(u_i) du_i]
                 /(2*pi*i)^r=f(n).             (2)

The same is true with product_i picot(piu_i) du_i. Exactly,

pi*cot(pi*s)=-2*pi*i*kappa_0(s)+pi*i.           (3)

Every term in the difference of these PRODUCT kernels lacks at least one coordinate pole and integrates to zero on the FULL small torus. These terms are not zero on arbitrary partial chart chains. If (3) is used on a partial domain, its explicit 2^r-1 lower-pole terms are retained as source currents.

The original finite source is therefore the coherent middle-dimensional cycle sum Gamma_Lambda=sum_n Gamma_n with either COMPLETE kernel. It has at most N^r local components. No norm estimate on F_N is used.

2. Finite compatible affine-unimodular charts

The proved pulling triangulation gives finitely many cells

u=d v_j+B_j y, y in d*Delta,
B_j in GL_r(Z),                                 (4)

with their number and matrices depending only on the fixed diagram. Assign shared lattice faces once using a fixed half-open convention. Let Lambda_j be the resulting disjoint partition of Lambda_N. This is a partition of the original finite states, not a new source.

Put A_j=B_j^(-1). Since d v_j is integer and A_j is unimodular, the joint poles of the new chart kernel are the SAME integer lattice. For each n in Lambda_j take

Gamma_(j,n)=n+A_j^(-1) T_epsilon,
omega_j=(-2*pi*i)^r det(A_j)
                product_i kappa_0((A_j u)_i) du_1...du_r. (5)

The affine translation in (4) is harmless by INTEGER periodicity; no exponential monomial multiplier has been canceled. The induced orientation in (5) retains its determinant +/-1. The ordinary multivariate Cauchy residue calculation gives exactly

integral_(Gamma_(j,n)) f omega_j/(2*pi*i)^r=f(n).

Consequently

T_N(f)=sum_j integral_(sum_(n in Lambda_j) Gamma_(j,n))
                                  f omega_j/(2*pi*i)^r. (6)

This identity is valid for EVERY f holomorphic near these cycles and their enclosed local polydiscs. All coefficients are one, all counting Jacobians are one, and it is the exact original functional (1).

There is a simultaneous quantitative initial-cycle construction. Collect the finite integer row forms appearing in all chart matrices and in the elementary side currents of Section 3. Choose small radii epsilon_1 >> epsilon_2 >> … >> epsilon_r with ratios less than 1/(4rC), where C bounds their integer coefficients in any chosen chart. Choose epsilon_1 so every linear form has modulus below 1/4. Its FIRST nonzero term then dominates the remaining terms, so every such form has modulus at least a fixed positive multiple of epsilon_r on the corresponding hierarchical torus. It cannot hit an integer. These radii depend on the diagram, not on N or n.

All source kernels and chart Jacobians on this initial atlas are bounded by diagram constants. Its total logarithmic/Euclidean mass is O_word(N^r). This is a polynomial INITIAL mass bound, not a bound for an unconstructed global steepest-descent deformation. The union may still pass near individual large state values.

3. Explicit elementary side-current algebra

Write x,y for two affine integer row forms. Their integer constants do not affect the periodic kernels. The exact identities are

kappa_0(x)kappa_0(y)
   =kappa_0(x+y)kappa_0(y)
                -kappa_0(x+y)kappa_0(-x),

kappa_0(x)kappa_0(y)
   =kappa_0(x-y)kappa_0(y)
                +kappa_0(x)kappa_0(y-x),

kappa_0(-x)=1-kappa_0(x).                     (7)

For example they follow immediately, with all terms retained, from

kappa_0(x)kappa_0(y)
   =kappa_0(x+y)[kappa_0(x)+kappa_0(y)-1].

The first line changes the first row from x to x+y and leaves y; the second changes it from x to x-y. The extra product is an EXPLICIT side-current kernel in another unimodular basis. A sign change has the displayed lower-pole current. A row swap only changes the recorded coordinate orientation. Multiplying by all spectator kernels gives the r-dimensional identities without hidden factors.

Factor each fixed GL_r(Z) chart matrix into unit row shears, row swaps and sign changes. This is constructive integer elimination. Follow just the intended main basis through this fixed sequence; at each step retain its one extra kernel from (7). Telescoping gives

K_reference=c_main K_chart+sum_n c_n K_side,n,  (8)

where every c is +1 or -1, and every side kernel is specified by that step. The number of terms is at most the number of steps plus one. It is independent of N. The ordinary powers of 2pii and all determinants in converting (8) to normalized forms are fixed diagram constants. Products of lower-pole terms from (3) are also explicit and finite. None is silently declared source-null on a partial cycle.

The side currents can carry the ENTIRE selected residue. Already in two local variables, the leading Cauchy identity is

1/(xy)=1/[(x+y)y]+1/[x(x+y)].                  (9)

On |x|<<|y|, the first right-hand term has integral zero, the second integral one, while the left integral is one. Thus retaining only the main new chart fails even for f=1. This is not an exponentially large adapter; it is a compulsory unit source current.

4. Aggregate cancellation and its exact chain qualification

Equations (7)–(8) are pointwise meromorphic identities. In any common compact domain remove the SAME small tubes about the union of all appearing chart divisors. Apply Stokes on the remaining chains, retaining every tube boundary and every partial-chart side prism. Sum the kernels BEFORE taking the tube limit.

At a divisor artificial to the reference kernel, every principal part cancels exactly in that aggregate. The same is true at dependent intersections: on each common regulator boundary the sum IS the reference meromorphic form, so it is not necessary to pretend that the individual singular currents have independent limits. Original coordinate divisors remain genuine source divisors and retain their original residues. No physical point is deleted by (8).

This proves tame gluing on any PROVIDED compatible common chain homotopy. If neighboring chart homotopies have the same embedded interface with opposite induced orientations and the same regulator, their full side-current sums agree with the reference form and cancel. No exponential chart-change coefficient is introduced. On regions whose distances to all appearing integer hyperplanes are at least N^(-A), every kernel is bounded by N^C: globally

|kappa_0(z)|<=C[1+dist(z,Z)^(-1)].             (10)

In a fixed O(N) real box there are O_word(N) parallel translates per row form, hence polynomially many arrangement cells in fixed dimension. Piecewise-linear common chains with polynomial complexity therefore have polynomial source-kernel overhead.

What is NOT proved is that separately chosen descending chart cycles have compatible interfaces or that their remaining side currents have small phase. The local identity does not construct that homotopy. Replacing each chart by an independently convenient contour and then omitting the difference currents is invalid. In fact there is a general obstruction to closing that gap by UNIVERSAL cot algebra alone.

5. Universal source rewrites cannot lower the state maximum

Let D be a complex domain, Lambda a finite nonempty set in D, and w_p nonzero fixed complex weights. Suppose a compact union C of contours and finite complex source measure mu satisfy

sum_(p in Lambda) w_p f(p)=integral_C f dmu       (11)

for EVERY holomorphic f on D. Each p belongs to the O(D)-holomorphic hull of C. Here is a direct proof, independent of any source model.

If p is outside that hull, choose a holomorphic g with |g(p)|>sup_C|g|. Choose an ordinary polynomial L_p equal to one at p and zero at every other point of Lambda; for instance interpolate a generic linear coordinate separating the finite set. Apply (11) to f=L_p g^k. The result is

|w_p| |g(p)|^k
   <=||mu|| sup_C|L_p| (sup_C|g|)^k,

which is impossible as k tends to infinity. Therefore, for EVERY holomorphic F on D,

max_(p in Lambda)|F(p)|<=sup_C|F|.             (12)

The conclusion holds for any finite number of chart cycles and kernels: combine their measures on their union. It still holds if the cycle was chosen after inspecting F, provided the resulting source identity remains valid for all holomorphic test functions. No polynomial-versus-exponential estimate is needed for this obstruction; finite total variation is enough.

All constructions in Sections 1–4 are universal in this sense. Thus they cannot, by themselves, produce a low-|F_N| source cycle below a larger original lattice summand. An ordinary homotopy in the complement of every genuine integer source divisor is an even more restrictive instance: it separately preserves each Cauchy evaluation. Rewriting the cotangent form does not defeat that issue while its full test-function functional is preserved.

The issue occurs in the actual model: the independently derived signed Markov source family keeps the SAME hyperbolic knot and normalized period while each additional alternating-sign stabilization adds v_8/(2pi) to one valid original state’s exponential rate. Its complete original pivotal traces are checked directly. Exact normalization pruning evades this family by composing the ACTUAL source cell first; it is not an identity on arbitrary multipliers of each intermediate state.

This does NOT forbid a sharp upper bound for the original scalar. That bound concerns cancellations among the values of the PARTICULAR original F_N. Exact q-binomial fusion, Hopf-Ward equations and matched normalization cells can relate those values in ways invalid for an arbitrary holomorphic multiplier. Such an operation can change the individual-evaluation source homology while preserving its actual scalar. Pure coordinate changes and the universal algebra (7) do not.

6. Result and the precise outstanding step

The original finite residue functional has a finite affine-unimodular atlas, polynomial initial mass, explicit fixed-coefficient side currents, and exact aggregate cancellation of ARTIFICIAL cotangent divisors. These statements include its complete original source.

A global phase-small cycle does not follow. Beyond choosing compatible interfaces, one must use an original-STATE-specific Ward/fusion identity to recombine genuine evaluation residues. The transport must then control the resulting full currents. A construction that stays universal on all holomorphic test functions cannot accomplish that step, by (12). This is a mathematical limitation of that proposed route, not a claim that the volume conjecture is false.
