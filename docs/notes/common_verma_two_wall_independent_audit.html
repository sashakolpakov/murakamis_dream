Independent audit of the common-Verma two-wall source

Audit, 27 October 2026. The proof in common_verma_two_wall_source.md is sound as stated. I checked the module, boundary maps, excursion factor, extension class, coherent action, and marked peripheral period directly. No new test run was needed.

1. The actual module and the two rank-one edges

With δ=1−QN\delta=1-Q^N and w=MQ−N−M−1w=MQ^{-N}-M^{-1}, the Jacobian in (log⁡Q,log⁡M)(\log Q,\log M) at the marked point is (−N0−N2), \begin{pmatrix}-N&0\\-N&2\end{pmatrix}, of determinant −2N-2N. The walls are independent. Adjacent products of the infinite coefficients give [ℰ,F]=ρ(K−K−1)[\mathcal E,F]=\rho(K-K^{-1}), including at index zero. Thus the infinite object is a genuine module before either wall is imposed.

The only head-to-tail FF-edge is δU\delta U; the only tail-to-head ℰ\mathcal E-edge is wLwL. Their block multiplication gives [F,P]=δU,[ℰ,P]=−wL, [F,P]=\delta U,\qquad [\mathcal E,P]=-wL, and the head commutator has defect −δwLU=−δwPN−1-\delta w\,LU=-\delta wP_{N-1}. The odd two-corner matrix squares to δwI\delta w I. The source-coupon formulas follow by the same block multiplication, with the stated signs.

At δ=0\delta=0 the head is a submodule. At w=0w=0 the tail is a submodule and the head is the quotient. Near M=1M=1, the latter wall is the genuine integral color M=−qNM=-q^N, rather than the fixed-root meridian direction. These orientations are not conflated.

2. The braid excursion adapter and the Bockstein

The displayed balanced crossing coefficients agree with the actual canceled factorial coefficients. On head inputs, exiting through FF has a Gaussian numerator 1−QN1-Q^N and no zero denominator, hence a factor δ\delta. Returning from a tail input through ℰ\mathcal E crosses index NN and has the factor 1−M2Q−N=−Mw. 1-M^2Q^{-N}=-Mw. The inverse has the same property with its transfer directions exchanged.

Every nontrivial ambient head-to-head path has a first exit and return, hence a factor δw\delta w. All calculations take place in a finite invariant total-position sector, so the path expansion has no infinite convergence issue. At the node, division by δw\delta w retains exactly the single-excursion terms. The resulting adapter need not have rank one, as the note correctly states.

At the fixed root, ℰN=γκNB,γ=(−1)N+1. \mathcal E^N=\gamma\kappa_N B,\qquad \gamma=(-1)^{N+1}. On the first two blocks, its derivative at M=1M=1 is γ2N(0I00)\gamma2N\begin{pmatrix}0&I\\0&0\end{pmatrix}. The zeroth central operator is zero, so a regular first-normal change of splitting contributes a commutator with zero and cannot remove this term. A direct sum of the two head families has the central operator identically zero. This proves non-splitting of the stated first-normal extension; it is stronger than a nonzero unclosed current.

3. Weyl orientation and the literal centralizer action

The divided FF-matrix coefficient is (a+NN)Q\binom{a+N}{N}_Q, whose root value is r+1r+1 on block a=rN+ja=rN+j. Hence B=∂t,T=tB=\partial_t,\ T=t on tr/r!t^r/r!, and [B,T]=I[B,T]=I. The coherent-kernel identities have the stated orientation; treating B↦s,T↦∂sB\mapsto s,T\mapsto\partial_s as a direct algebra homomorphism would be incorrect.

For λb=1+κNb\lambda_b=1+\kappa_Nb, Ubf(t)=ebtf(λbt). U_bf(t)=e^{bt}f(\lambda_bt). Direct composition gives UbUcf(t)=e(b+c+κNbc)tf((1+κN(b+c+κNbc))t), U_bU_cf(t)= e^{(b+c+\kappa_Nbc)t} f((1+\kappa_N(b+c+\kappa_Nbc))t), which is the exact regular-centralizer group law. Its coherent action is s↦λbs+bs\mapsto\lambda_bs+b, with anchor s=0s=0; the locus 1+κNs≠01+\kappa_Ns\ne0 is a genuine torsor. The infinitesimal operator is T(1+κNB)T(1+\kappa_NB), in that order. It changes the central source character and is not claimed to be a fixed-fiber quantum-group intertwiner.

4. The marked relative period and its scope

The head projection satisfies π0Ubθ0=IH\pi_0U_b\theta_0=I_H exactly. Applying the original normalized head cut therefore gives the scalar matrix coefficient one, before integration. With the retained endpoint b=MNb=M^N, the invariant form integrates to $$ \frac{\Log(M^{2N})}{\kappa_N} =\frac N{u_N(M)} \frac{\Log(M^2)}{M-M^{-1}}. $$ The complete value is one. The normalization and the direction of the adapter uN/Nu_N/N are correct; the logarithm sheet and whole peripheral divisor are not discarded.

The earlier unbalanced factorial basis change is singular on tail root blocks. The proof correctly does not identify the two infinite root lattices or remove the earlier Alexander denominator.

The source remains an E-semicyclic/reducible centralizer object. No map to the irreducible hyperbolic framing class, selected Bloch regulator, or volume norm is asserted. The explicit source and Bockstein data are valid inputs for such a future comparison, not a substitute for it.
