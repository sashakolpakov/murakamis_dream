The selected mixed pretzel packet has an explicit regular character lift

29 October 2026. This identifies the regular algebraic branch selected by the original mixed inner source with an explicit auxiliary meridian. It does not identify a representation by matching cusp Hessians. The source Riccati ratio fixes the internal critical root, the literal factorial ratios fix its two remaining conjugate variables, and a two-by-two matrix calculation realizes the resulting disk-product character. The literal quarter-turn port identification and global concatenation are supplied separately; neither a trace equation alone nor this local calculation is being substituted for that gluing proof.

1. Recover the internal critical point from the selected finite source

Use the exact original mixed sums, normalizations, and source endpoint in the mixed inner theorem:

A_(h,r)=sum_(v=r)^M Q^(-vr) F_(2v)/
              [F_v F_(v-h) F_(v+h) F_(v-r)],
D_(h,r)=Q^(-r^2) F_(2r)/[F_r F_(r-h) F_(r+h)],
U_(h,r)=A_(h,r)/D_(h,r).

Here r is in its fixed high window, h is in the small cusp corridor, H=Q^h, Y=Q^r, and the prescribed factorial completion is used for complex h. In particular no arbitrary solution of the Ward equation is chosen. The exact source has U_M=1 and its last backward coefficient vanishes. Its proved selected ratio tends to the simple dominant root mu of

mu^2-p mu-q=0,
den=(1-Y/H)(1-YH),
p=-Y^(-1)[(H+H^(-1))Y^2+2]/den,
q=-Y^(-2)(1-Y^2)^2/den^2.                         (1)

The adjacent D ratio has the limit

d(Y,H)=Y^(-2)(1-Y^2)(1+Y)/den.

There is also a literal, exact moment identity. The ratio of the v-th summand of A_(h,r+1) to that of A_(h,r) is

Q^(-v)-Q^(-r).

The v=r endpoint on the second sum is zero, so this holds after summing, with no missing boundary term. Consequently the effective internal root selected by that source is

X^(-1)=Y^(-1)+d(Y,H)/mu,
X=Y mu/[mu+Yd(Y,H)].                             (2)

Substitution of (2) in (1), followed by clearing its regular denominators, gives precisely

(Y+T+1)X^2-(TY+2)X+Y-1=0, T=H+H^(-1).          (3)

This equivalence is algebraic on the stated regular chart. At H=1 the SOURCE-selected root mu=-Y^(-1) gives

X=(Y-1)/(Y+3),

rather than the other normalized channel. Thus (2) also fixes the branch on a small complex neighborhood by holomorphic continuation. All denominator exclusions in (1)–(3) are retained.

2. Literal phase and its conjugate coordinates

The complete phase S_0 is the finite-factorial phase in the independent symbol audit. The exact mixed summand changes it by

S=S_0-2Li2(X)+Li2(X/H)+Li2(XH)+(Log H)^2/2.       (4)

The last term is the original q(h2) factor, not an adjustable flattening. The original common kappa and all quarter-turn phases remain as in the source theorem. Differentiating the actual product-log chart gives

exp(S_x)=(1+X)^2(X-1)/[(X-Y)(1-X/H)(1-HX)],
exp(S_y)=(1-Y/X)(1-wY)(1-Y/w)/(1-Y)^3,
U=exp(S_t)=(wY-1)/(w-Y),
L=exp(S_LogH)=(H-X)/(1-HX),                       (5)

where x=Log X,y=Log Y,t=Log w. Equation exp(S_x)=1 is exactly (3). The second equation exp(S_y)=1 is

(1-Y/X)[1-(w+w^(-1))Y+Y^2]=(1-Y)^3.             (6)

On the regular branch the same integer inner mark as in the complete source is retained by continuation. Equations (5)–(6) do not erase that mark. They identify the exponentiated critical coordinates; the marked primitive remains the one fixed by the finite source.

The displayed L is also fixed directly by the exact physical h-shift: the original q(h2), F_(v-h), F_(v+h) factors have limiting ratio

H(1-X/H)/(1-HX)=L.

Thus no undetermined square root of an auxiliary longitude is inserted. The auxiliary physical meridian is M=H^(-1/2), with branch M=1 at h=0. A positive physical-color/meridian shift reverses the h-shift. Its preferred longitude marking also retains the original ordinary-circle pivot sign. The typed longitude certificate proves that the preferred M-eigenflag longitude is precisely -L^(-1).

3. The dual Casimir is an actual four-punctured trace

The literal quarter-turn dual-Casimir operator in the original alias theorem has principal symbol

D(w,U)=((w-1)/(w+1))^2(U+U^(-1))+8w/(w+1)^2.     (7)

This symbol has an explicit trace realization. Put t=(w-1)/(w+1), P_0=diag(w,w^(-1)), and define

mu_1=[[2w/(w+1),1],[-t^2,2/(w+1)]],
mu_2=P_0 mu_1^(-1),
mu_3=[[2/(w+1),wU],[-t^2/(wU),2w/(w+1)]],
mu_4=(mu_3 mu_2 mu_1)^(-1).                     (8)

Each matrix has determinant one and trace two. Their ordered product is I, and direct multiplication gives

Tr(mu_2 mu_1)=w+w^(-1),
Tr(mu_3 mu_2)=D(w,U).

For w not in {0,1,-1} and U!=0 this tuple is irreducible: the first pair alone has distinct fixed flags. This is an explicit character lift, rather than an inference from the dimension of a trace variety.

Substituting (5) and using (3),(6) gives the exact identity

D(w,U)=-(L+L^(-1)).                              (9)

For a reproducible rational verification, solve (3) as

Y=[1+2X-(T+1)X^2]/[1-TX+X^2],

then solve (6) for w+w^(-1); substitute these in (7). No equation at a collision divisor is canceled and then reintroduced as a regular solution. At completeness D=-2 although L=1; the central minus sign in (9) must therefore be retained.

4. A framed auxiliary meridian, including its parabolic limit

Let P be the actual unrotated disk-product matrix, with determinant one and trace D in (9). Define

beta=-(H-X)(1-HX)/[sqrt(H)(H+1)(1-X^2)],
C=beta(P-YI).                                    (10)

All square roots are the branch through H=1. Equations (3),(9) imply

det C=1, Tr C=sqrt(H)+1/sqrt(H), [C,P]=0.          (11)

More precisely, on the P-eigenflag with eigenvalue lambda=-L^(-1), C has eigenvalue M=H^(-1/2); on the reciprocal flag it has M^(-1). These assertions follow by the scalar identities

beta(lambda-Y)=H^(-1/2),
beta(lambda^(-1)-Y)=H^(1/2).

The formula has a removable complete-color degeneration. At H=1, equation (3) on the selected branch gives Y=(1+3X)/(1-X), so

C=I-(P+I)/(1+Y), Tr P=-2.                        (12)

For noncentral P this is a NONCENTRAL trace-two unipotent. Thus the auxiliary meridian does not collapse to I merely because its eigenvalue becomes one. Formula (10) is finite in a bounded port frame at this limit, although a frame diagonalizing P becomes singular there.

Here is a direct tangle test. For actual meridians a,b,C, put P=ba. The positive Hurwitz convention is (a,b)->(a^(-1)ba,a). The literal auxiliary threading word [2,1,1,2] sends the auxiliary meridian to P^(-1)CP. Closing that physical auxiliary component is therefore EXACTLY [C,P]=1; under this condition the main outputs are

(C^(-1)aC,C^(-1)bC).

Following it by the retained negative half twist gives

r=C^(-1)bC, s=C^(-1)bab^(-1)C.                  (13)

For generic H!=1 diagonalize P as diag(lambda,lambda^(-1)), with lambda=-L^(-1), and set t_lambda=(lambda-1)/(lambda+1). One may take

a=[[2lambda/(lambda+1),1],
                     [-t_lambda^2,2/(lambda+1)]],
b=P a^(-1), C=diag(M,M^(-1)).

Then (13) gives

Tr(r^(-1)a)=2-t_lambda^2
                  [2+lambda H+lambda^(-1)H^(-1)]. (14)

Substitution of L=(H-X)/(1-HX) and the two actual stationary equations (3),(6) makes (14) exactly w+w^(-1). Thus the selected factorial branch satisfies the mixed threaded-half-twist character relation, with the original auxiliary meridian and a specified flag. The regular formula (10), not this singular diagonal frame, is used at H=1.

5. Scope of the local certificate

This proves a source-selected regular local character lift and an explicit auxiliary-meridian matrix. The additional literal quarter-turn ordering, conjugating frames, and global source/cap equations are proved in the global port theorem. The preferred-longitude certificate retains its sign and framing. The marked action comparison then identifies the source action with the regulator of the extended filled representation. These separately proved steps are not inferred from matching a cusp Hessian. They give a representation-volume upper bound; identifying the deformed representation as the filled geometric holonomy is an additional requirement for equality.

In particular the global propagation is RIGHT TO LEFT in the original left-to-right box label b. If its rotated input is t_b and output t’_b=U_b t_b, the actual original bottom ports are (h(t’_b),h(t_b)^(-1)) and top ports are (g(t’b)^(-1),g(t_b)). Both original neighboring caps therefore impose t_b=t’(b+1); the outer turns impose t_s=t’_1. The condition product U_b=1 closes this propagation in the order s,…,1. One must not replace these literal relations by a forward box-label traversal.

The audit checks the cleared rational identities, full two-by-two character and threading formulas, complete parabolic limit, and the exact finite source moment on both level parities. Finite computations supplement the algebraic proof above.

6. Absolute complete crossing-circle holonomies match the geometric cusp

The literal port and geometric-anchor proof identifies the main meridians with the white-face holonomies of the explicit four-antiprism construction. Here is an absolute matrix check for the AUXILIARY generators; it is stronger than comparing cusp Hessians or only a ratio of translations.

Set alpha=pi/s, c=cos(alpha), t=csc(alpha),

rho^2=sec(alpha)+tan(alpha), R=rho^4,
w=-R, U=exp(2i alpha),
z_i=t rho^(-1) exp((2i+1)i alpha),

and use the actual raw port matrices

g(z)=[[-2w/(w+1),z],[-t^2/z,-2/(w+1)]].

Their fixed flags are z/t. The unrotated disk product is

P_i=g(z_i)g(Uz_i)^(-1),

with trace -2 and fixed flag Z_(i+1)=rho U^(i+1). Direct multiplication gives

(P_i)_21=t^2(1-t)(1-U^(-1))/z_i.

Rotate the boundary coordinate by zeta’=U^(-i-1)zeta and then use m_i(zeta)=1/(zeta’-rho). The new lower-left entry before this last Mobius map is

c'=U^(i+1)(P_i)_21=2i rho(1-t).

For any trace-minus-two matrix fixing rho, write P=-I+N. The kernel of N is (rho,1), so N=c’[[rho,-rho^2],[1,-rho]]. Conjugating by the matrix [[0,1],[1,-rho]] of m_i gives

m_i P_i m_i^(-1)=-I+[[0,c'],[0,0]].

Consequently its PROJECTIVE translation is -c’. Since rho^2(t-1)=ct and the actual cusp rectangle has width W=1/(rho sin(alpha))=t/rho, this translation is exactly

-c'=2ic W.                                      (15)

The source-selected complete Y equals exp(2i atan(sec(alpha))), hence

1+Y=2ic/(1+ic).

Using (12), the same coordinate m_i sends the actual auxiliary meridian to the translation

-c'/(1+Y)=(1+ic)W.                              (16)

Equations (15)–(16) are precisely the primitive disk longitude and the POSITIVE diagonal half-twist meridian in the face-labeled cusp calculation. Thus the complete auxiliary matrices, not just their flags or their first deformation derivative, match those of the actual half-twisted augmented geometry. The central minus sign of the SL2 disk product is retained throughout. Together with the independently checked white and shaded face pairings in the port proof, this anchors the actual complete representation, not merely its numerical volume.

Reversing the cyclic TRAVERSAL label to respect that operator order does not change the oriented complete geometry. There is an explicit holomorphic Mobius isometry F(zeta)=rho^2/zeta. It sends

Z_k -> Z_(-k),
U_k^+ -> U_(-k-1)^-, U_k^- -> U_(-k-1)^+,
Q_k -> Q_(-k-1), T_k^+ <-> T_(-k)^-, W_+ <-> W_-.

Under the literal cross-copy shaded gluing, applying F to both sides gives the SAME gluing at index -k read in its inverse direction: the original U_(k-1)^+ -> U_k^- becomes U_(-k)^- -> U_(-k-1)^+, and the other outer vertex behaves likewise. Thus the reverse traversal is matched by an orientation-preserving isometry, not by an assumed mirror identification. The original boxes and their source labels are not permuted in the quantum formula.
