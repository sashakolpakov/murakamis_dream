The full output of two centered coherent inputs

Written research proof, 22 November 2026.

The product of two specified centered geometric-series packets has an exact positive norm sum. On every compact complex input bidisk this sum has one positive nondegenerate Laplace maximum. It gives a uniform norm asymptotic, concentration of the entire output, and a complex Gaussian approximation in the full Newton coefficient norm.

The proof uses the actual finite wall products, including both endpoint rows. It is a forward estimate for this family of two inputs. It is not a theorem for arbitrary packets or an arbitrary Conway component.

5. Exact endpoints, scope, and coverage

Both original closing functionals are explicit. The compiler gives m(ej)=δj0m(e_j)=\delta_{j0} and ϕ(ej)=δjd\phi(e_j)=\delta_{jd}. Using the two exact wall endpoints in (3) yields m(wN)=κ2DN(y)DN(z)NZN(y)ZN(z)(1+qy)(1+qz),ϕ(wN)=∑a=0d(yz)aZN(y)ZN(z).(26) \boxed{\begin{aligned} m(w_N)&=\frac{\kappa^2D_N(y)D_N(z)} {N\sqrt{Z_N(y)Z_N(z)}(1+qy)(1+qz)},\\ \phi(w_N)&=\frac{\sum_{a=0}^d(yz)^a} {\sqrt{Z_N(y)Z_N(z)}}. \end{aligned}} \qquad\text{(26)} The second identity also follows directly from ϕ(fafb)=δab\phi(f_af_b)=\delta_{ab}. These closings remain bounded on compact bidisks while the internal output norm has the positive exponential rate in (15). Consequently the norm and Gaussian approximation do not supply a scalar closing lower bound.

The result estimates the actual forward product of the two packets (2) in the original centered/Newton coefficient metric. It does not put an arbitrary incoming packet, a geometric short component, or every physical binomial coherent input into this family. No uniform positive conditioning of the full multiplication map or its MM*MM^* factor is proved. Composition through arbitrary further turns and branching, or a filled-knot geometric action, remains outside the theorem.
