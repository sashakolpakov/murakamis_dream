An endpoint-completed whole Jackson current from actual divided matrices

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The actual high-color divided raising/lowering currents, with two literal anchored primitives, recover the WHOLE Jackson differential on the physical unwrapped clock. Its root value is the clock Euler derivative. The missing bottom state is repaired explicitly, so the identity holds on every state, not only in a bulk chart. A rational function and its shift are retained before matrix evaluation.

This is an actual current/source map on the commutative physical character subalgebra. The exact off-diagonal correction below shows why it is not yet a map of all head Hopf currents or a full geometric Gaussian/source comparison.

1. Whole actual matrices and regular anchored inverses

Use the original high integral color with r=N from the actual source theorem. In its fixed sign frame write w_(R,S), 0<=R<=N, 0<=S<N, and set

Z=QN,δ=1−Z,J=NR+S,CQ=QJ=QSZR. Z=Q^N,\quad\delta=1-Z,\quad J=NR+S,\quad C_Q=Q^J=Q^S Z^R.

Keep the original generic integral divided matrices

e(q)=τE(N)(q),f(q)=F(N)(q),τ=(−1)N2−1. e(q)=\tau E^{(N)}(q),\qquad f(q)=F^{(N)}(q),\quad \tau=(-1)^{N^2-1}.

They preserve S and change R by -1 and +1. Their nonzero arrow coefficients alpha_(R,S)(q), R>=1, and beta_(R,S)(q), R<N, are regular units near the marked root: their root values are N-R+1 and R+1. Define actual inverse arrows

e†wR,S=αR+1,S−1wR+1,S(R<N),e†wN,S=0, e^\dagger w_{R,S}=\alpha_{R+1,S}^{-1}w_{R+1,S}\ (R<N), \quad e^\dagger w_{N,S}=0, f†wR,S=βR−1,S−1wR−1,S(R>0),f†w0,S=0. f^\dagger w_{R,S}=\beta_{R-1,S}^{-1}w_{R-1,S}\ (R>0), \quad f^\dagger w_{0,S}=0.

These are whole rational matrix germs of the actual divided coefficients, not inverses of nilpotent matrices. With P_0,P_N the two block endpoint projectors, they satisfy EXACTLY

e†e=I−P0,ee†=I−PN,f†f=I−PN,ff†=I−P0. e^\dagger e=I-P_0,\quad ee^\dagger=I-P_N,\qquad f^\dagger f=I-P_N,\quad ff^\dagger=I-P_0. \tag{1}

Their root operator norms are at most one in the fixed unit basis. No uniform estimate on a growing q-neighborhood is assumed. In the actual positive Fr metric ||v_R||^2=binom(N,R), the sharper bound is ||edagger||=||fdagger||=1/sqrt(N); the two endpoint projectors still have norm one. This follows from the normalized inverse arrow 1/sqrt((R+1)(N-R)).

2. The whole Jackson identity, with its shifted argument

Let g(Q,t) be a retained Laurent polynomial, or a rational function whose denominators at BOTH C_Q and ZC_Q are invertible in the chosen whole matrix chart. Q remains fixed when its t argument is shifted. Define the actual current

𝒥Z(g)=−e†[e,g(Q,ZCQ)]+P0f†[f,g(Q,CQ)]1−Z. \mathcal J_Z(g)=\frac{-e^\dagger[e,g(Q,ZC_Q)] +P_0f^\dagger[f,g(Q,C_Q)]}{1-Z}. \tag{2}

Then, as an exact whole rational matrix identity,

𝒥Z(g)=g(Q,CQ)−g(Q,ZCQ)1−Z. \boxed{\ \mathcal J_Z(g) =\frac{g(Q,C_Q)-g(Q,ZC_Q)}{1-Z}.\ } \tag{3}

Proof. The actual block grading gives eC_Q=ZC_Qe and fC_Q=Z^(-1)C_Qf. The inverse arrows have the opposite degrees. Keeping operator order and the SHIFTED argument in (2) therefore gives

e†[e,g(ZCQ)]=[g(ZCQ)−g(CQ)](I−P0), e^\dagger[e,g(ZC_Q)] =[g(ZC_Q)-g(C_Q)](I-P_0), f†[f,g(CQ)]=[g(CQ)−g(ZCQ)](I−PN). f^\dagger[f,g(C_Q)] =[g(C_Q)-g(ZC_Q)](I-P_N).

Since P_0P_N=0, their combination is the full difference, with no missing endpoint. This proves (3). In particular replacing g(ZC_Q) by g(C_Q) in the first commutator would give a different operator and would not prove this identity.

The right side has a regular value at Z=1 whenever the stated function chart is regular. For every integer k,

𝒥Z(tk)=1−Zk1−ZCQk, \mathcal J_Z(t^k)=\frac{1-Z^k}{1-Z}C_Q^k,

and it obeys the exact twisted product rule

𝒥Z(gh)=𝒥Z(g)h(CQ)+g(ZCQ)𝒥Z(h). \mathcal J_Z(gh)=\mathcal J_Z(g)h(C_Q) +g(ZC_Q)\mathcal J_Z(h). \tag{4}

Thus the construction is a whole function calculus before its normal jets are taken. An explicit q-dependent coefficient is carried unchanged in the difference; its separate q-derivative is not silently included in a clock derivative.

At the root A=Q_N^S, (3) gives

ℰ(g):=[𝒥Z(g)]0=A∂Ag(QN,A)IFr. \mathcal E(g):=[\mathcal J_Z(g)]_0 =A\partial_A g(Q_N,A)\,I_{\mathrm{Fr}}. \tag{5}

The shifted argument in (2) has the same leading divided commutator as its unshifted value, so equivalently E(g)=[-e^dagger[e,g(C_Q)]/delta+P_0 f^dagger[f,g(C_Q)]/delta]_0. For every remaining head matrix entry the output of (5) is the literal multiplication by the indicated head character.

3. The normal jet and the literal finite cut

The derivative in (5) is not a derivation of the reduced finite clock algebra k[A^+/-1]/(A^N-1): it sends A^N-1 to N. The whole normal datum is essential. More precisely, for frozen coefficients k containing Q_N, the actual map g(t)->g(C_Q) modulo delta^2 has kernel

(tN−1)2. (t^N-1)^2. \tag{6}

Indeed root vanishing implies g=(t^N-1)h. On w_(R,S), C_Q^N-1=-J delta+O(delta^2). Vanishing at first normal order then implies J h(Q_N^S)=0 for every R. For each S at least one of J=NR+S is nonzero, so h vanishes at all N roots. This proves (6), and the converse follows by squaring the simple normal zero. Thus (5) is the actual clock conormal-jet derivative

k[t+/−1]/(tN−1)2→k[A+/−1]/(AN−1),g↦Ag′(A). k[t^{+/-1}]/(t^N-1)^2\longrightarrow k[A^{+/-1}]/(A^N-1),\quad g\mapsto Ag'(A).

It is a derivation relative to the root evaluation, not a discarded normal direction on the fixed fiber. Write uniquely g=g_0+(t^N-1)g_1 modulo its square, with both g_i of degree<N. For the original averaged cut phi_N, the exact source row is

ϕN(ℰ(g))=NϕN(g1). \phi_N(\mathcal E(g))=N\phi_N(g_1). \tag{7}

The canonical polynomial g_0 contributes no constant coefficient after Ag_0’. The factor N in (7) is explicit. An unnormalized Fr source line multiplies both sides by its retained pairing unit; normalizing it does not change the head factor.

Apply this linear map coefficientwise to the sourced relative interval complex R(E) of the literal framing transgression. It commutes strictly with the interval differential, its two endpoint rows and its polynomial contraction. Thus (2)–(7) give an ACTUAL Fr-current realization of the clock-normal piece of that relative cut map, with the original averaged co-source. They do not assign an independent transfer coordinate to the cut.

In particular both geometric compensated fields obey xi_i(A)=-2A(1-A). Hence the actual current -2(1-A) E has precisely their common clock component on every retained character g. The difference of the two geometric fields, which moves the other framing coordinate, is not determined by this common component.

4. The two primitives have a global P1 realization

On the actual Fr source identify v_j with binom(N,j) z^j in H^0(P1,O(N)). Then e=partial_z, and the root inverse e^dagger is exactly the anchored integral integral_0^z, with its degree-(N+1) tail projected away. This gives (1) with the constant and top coefficient projectors. The opposite f^dagger is the corresponding south-chart primitive.

These operators also have explicit global differential lifts. Writing D=z partial_z, take q_N(D) with q_N(j)=1/[(j+1)(N-j)] for j<N and q_N(N)=0. Then e^dagger=F q_N(D), f^dagger=E q_N(N-D) on H^0. Their differential order generally grows with N; it is NOT the bounded PBW normal-depth filtration of a crossing. Their identities on the full Cech resolution hold up the explicit contraction homotopy constructed in the global source/co-source complex. The induced H^0 identities (1)–(3) are literal finite matrices. Dual Serre residue carries the same endpoint projectors, rather than deleting the two anchored boundary terms.

All q-dependent arrow inverses and source units in (2) are retained coefficientwise in this lift. This does not make the chosen cut pseudoinverses into Hopf intertwiners on arbitrary tensor products. For example a trivial classical tensor summand is annihilated by both e and f; no repair using their currents alone can produce the identity on that summand. A whole-word map must transport the actual cut projector and mixed coproduct, not blindly tensor (2).

5. Exact off-diagonal correction

Let H(q) be a retained head-matrix insertion, and keep its order to the RIGHT of the clock function. The same current formula with g(ZC_Q)H and g(C_Q)H has the exact result

𝒥Z(gH)=g(CQ)−g(ZCQ)1−ZH+−g(CQ)e†[e,H]+g(ZCQ)P0f†[f,H]1−Z. \mathcal J_Z(gH) =\frac{g(C_Q)-g(ZC_Q)}{1-Z}\,H +\frac{-g(C_Q)e^\dagger[e,H] +g(ZC_Q)P_0f^\dagger[f,H]}{1-Z}. \tag{8}

This follows by the ordered product commutator, using e^dagger g(ZC_Q)=g(C_Q)e^dagger and f^dagger g(C_Q)=g(ZC_Q)f^dagger. If H is an actual head operator whose root commutes with the Fr e,f, the remainder is regular, but it generally does not vanish. If H contains noncommuting Fr insertions, regularity itself requires its additional normal source correction.

Thus (3) does not supply the TRUE head Hopf currents merely by relabeling a scalar derivative. Their missing terms are the two explicit inserted moments in (8). The old physical clock and its whole Fourier-resolvent lift are those in the controlled current dictionary, not the distinct new position clock on the diagonal source. Conjugating (2) and every inverse/projector by the whole source gauge preserves the identity exactly, including that dictionary.

The constant-head correction is an explicit inner connection

For a head matrix H kept CONSTANT in q and R, the root remainder in (8) has a closed form. Define

ℓ0=0,ℓS=−12∑k=1SqNk+qN−kqNk−qN−k,Lh=diag⁡(ℓS),Σ=2P0−I. \ell_0=0,\qquad \ell_S=-\frac12\sum_{k=1}^{S} \frac{q_N^k+q_N^{-k}}{q_N^k-q_N^{-k}},\quad L_h=\operatorname{diag}(\ell_S),\quad\Sigma=2P_0-I.

Then the actual divided-arrow matrices give

ℰ(H)=[Σ⊗Lh,H]. \mathcal E(H)=[\Sigma\otimes L_h,H]. \tag{8a}

To prove this, write alpha_(R,S)=a_R+delta a_(1,R,S)+… and beta_(R,S)=b_R+delta b_(1,R,S)+…, with a_R=N-R+1,b_R=R+1. Exact commutation of E^(N) with the ordinary E, and F^(N) with ordinary F, along adjacent nonzero head arrows gives BOTH recurrences

a1,R,SaR−a1,R,0aR=ℓS,b1,R,SbR−b1,R,0bR=ℓS. \frac{a_{1,R,S}}{a_R}-\frac{a_{1,R,0}}{a_R}=\ell_S, \qquad \frac{b_{1,R,S}}{b_R}-\frac{b_{1,R,0}}{b_R}=\ell_S. \tag{8b}

For example the adjacent F ratio has normal increment -1/2 (qS+q(-S))/(qS-q(-S)); differentiating the actual q-integer gives it directly. The E ratio gives the same increment. On R>=1, -e^dagger[e,H]/delta therefore gives -[L_h,H]; the literal R=0 repair contributes +[L_h,H]. All R-only constants commute with H. This proves (8a), without setting any individual arrow derivative to zero. Pairing k and N-k in the sum proves ell_(N-1)=0. Its root size is O(N log N), and no head endpoint coefficient has been inverted at a zero.

For the actual normalized paired Fr source, the selected coefficient is c_N(m)=<Sigma>=2((1+m)/2)^N-1. Thus the original cut evaluates this correction to c_N(m) Phi_h([L_h,H]). It is an EXPLICIT original head Ward boundary. Let K=xA^(-1) and let B_cut be the actual matrix of the Ward contraction, [K,B_cut]=e_h-I/N. Since [K,L_h]=0,

Φh([Lh,H])=−Tr⁡([Bcut,Lh][K,H]). \Phi_h([L_h,H])=-\operatorname{Tr}([B_{\mathrm{cut}},L_h][K,H]). \tag{8c}

This follows by applying the original cut homotopy and Jacobi; the ordinary head trace of a commutator is zero. Hence this coefficient part vanishes IDENTICALLY on the actual diagonal Green arrays and on Ward-closed head partial traces. The endpoint-dependent polynomial c_N(m), including its values -1 and +1 at m=-1,+1, remains in the cochain homotopy. The unnormalized scale line multiplies this identity by its retained pairing rather than being canceled by hand.

Actual q-dependent insertions have an additional specified lift term. If H=H_0+delta H_1+… with H_0 a constant-head matrix, then

ℰ(H)=[ΣLh,H0]−e0†[e0,H1]+P0f0†[f0,H1]. \mathcal E(H)=[\Sigma L_h,H_0] -e_0^\dagger[e_0,H_1]+P_0f_0^\dagger[f_0,H_1]. \tag{8d}

This includes the true block-wrap and mixed Hopf-normal coefficients. They cannot be removed using only (8c). If the fully closed actual matrix and its first normal are scalar, every term in (8d) vanishes; an unsummed current or a Fr-valued moment need not have that property.

6. A finite spectrum does not determine the whole Jackson current

Fix a head eigenvalue c=Q^S and write C_s=c Z^R on its N+1 block states. Retain the actual polynomial FUNCTION

pN(t)=∏R=0N(t−cZR). p_N(t)=\prod_{R=0}^{N}(t-cZ^R).

Although p_N(C_s)=0 identically, its shifted value is

pN(ZCs)=cN+1ZN(N+1)/2∏k=1N+1(Zk−1)PN, p_N(ZC_s)=c^{N+1}Z^{N(N+1)/2} \prod_{k=1}^{N+1}(Z^k-1)\,P_N, \tag{9}

where P_N is the TOP STATE PROJECTOR, distinct from the polynomial p_N. All lower shifted points remain in the finite spectrum, but the top point cZ^(N+1) does not. Therefore

𝒥Z(pN)=−cN+1ZN(N+1)/2∏k=1N+1(Zk−1)1−ZPN=(−1)NcN+1(N+1)!δNPN+O(δN+1). \mathcal J_Z(p_N) =-\frac{c^{N+1}Z^{N(N+1)/2}\prod_{k=1}^{N+1}(Z^k-1)}{1-Z}P_N =(-1)^N c^{N+1}(N+1)!\delta^N P_N+O(\delta^{N+1}). \tag{10}

Consequently the WHOLE current depends on the retained function and its shifted observable, not only on the single finite matrix g(C_s). It is invisible through every normal order B<N in this example. The degree of p_N is N+1 and grows with N, so this does not contradict a bounded-presentation cyclotomic rigidity theorem. It gives a concrete actual-source reason to retain functions before their finite evaluations and jets.

7. The marked Green logarithm has a carry pole, not the regular Euler value

The character theorem must not silently replace the marked logarithmic torsor. Put h=Log Q near h_N=2pi i/N and retain

Log⁡CQ=hJ,Log⁡Z=Nhnear 2πi. \operatorname{Log}C_Q=hJ,\qquad \operatorname{Log}Z=Nh\quad\text{near }2\pi i.

This is not the branch of Log Z near zero. A retained polynomial moment P(J)=P(Log C_Q/Log Q) shifts to P(J+N). The SAME actual whole current therefore gives the exact identity

𝒥Z(P(J))=P(J)−P(J+N)δ. \mathcal J_Z(P(J))=\frac{P(J)-P(J+N)}{\delta}. \tag{11}

For a coefficient function F, retain F(Q,ZC_Q,J+N) as well as P(J+N). In particular a Cartan chirp q(J2) has root shift factor (-1)^N; freezing that coefficient while shifting P would change the actual carry. More generally let T carry every retained coefficient and the marked index simultaneously. Then the pole residue is exactly

Res⁡δ=0𝒥Z=(I−T0). \operatorname{Res}_{\delta=0}\mathcal J_Z =(I-T_0). \tag{12}

On a coefficient fixed by this transport, T_0=e^(N partial_J), and (T_0-I)^(h+1)=0 on moments of degree<=h. On the normalized index J/N it is translation by one. With a nontrivial coefficient transport the associated grade retains that transport minus I; polynomial degree alone does not make it nilpotent.

Equation (11) is the actual divided-current realization of the bounded Green carry in the whole carry source. The pole is absent for the single-valued regular characters in Section 2, and present for these marked moments. Subtracting it without its relative endpoint/period class would remove the very moment repair used in the full scalar certificate. No such subtraction or global cancellation is inferred here.
