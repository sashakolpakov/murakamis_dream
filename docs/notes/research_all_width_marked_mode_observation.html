Marked-mode observations retain every actual plat source

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The endpoint observations underlying the calibrated marked modes are faithful on the entire actual invariant source, at every fixed even width. They admit an explicit reconstruction with norm eCbNe^{C_bN}. The proof uses a finite qq-binomial inverse for the original E,KE,K action. It needs neither a projective decomposition nor successive inversions of ill-conditioned weight maps.

At four heads these observations are exactly the previously calibrated marked-mode coordinates, after one specified original crossing. Thus they retain the complete NN-dimensional source, including all pair Casimir normal directions. At greater width their image has explicit source constraints; it is not a free array of coideal labels. The original closing functional is transported through the full inverse. Faithfulness does not give a lower bound for its scalar after an arbitrary fixed short partial filling.
