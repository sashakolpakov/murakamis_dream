A whole physical Gaussian packet for one original crossing

24 November 2026. Research proof. Earlier finals and manuscripts are unchanged.

The original signed crossing, in the uniform physical head metric, has a Gaussian approximation in the Hilbert–Schmidt norm of its entire matrix. Every fixed number of correction terms is available. The estimate is uniform for small real exponential boundary tilts and retains the exact physical phase. It strengthens the weighted norm rate of [W]; it does not identify a geometric tilt or control a closed knot scalar.

4. Full packets, interfaces, and scope

At zero tilt, [P] identifies the complete physical algebra 𝒜N=ℂ[Ω]\mathcal A_N=\mathbb C[\Omega] as an NN-dimensional subspace of these pair matrices. Let ΠN\Pi_N be its physical orthogonal projection. Since R±∈𝒜NR^\pm\in\mathcal A_N, projection cannot increase the error in (11). Thus ΠN𝒢N,J,0±\Pi_N\mathcal G^\pm_{N,J,0} is an actual full-boundary approximation at the same rate. No value quotient or deletion of double-root normal directions occurs.

For nonzero tilts the correct space is the exact linear image Wout𝒜NWin−1W_{\mathrm{ out}}\mathcal A_NW_{\mathrm{ in}}^{-1}; its orthogonal projection has the same contraction property in the Hilbert–Schmidt norm of the gauged arrays. It need not be the original algebra or preserve multiplication. Undoing the gauges can cost exponentially in NN. Both incident interface factors, original caps and physical budgets must therefore be retained in a proposed network use. No free transfer of (11) to an untilted whole-component error or unchanged new budgets is claimed. The two Gaussian models are not asserted to be exact mutual inverses.

[W] already proves the compact weighted norm rate and the unique flat maximum. [G, Section 4] already proves a full Hilbert–Schmidt Gaussian operator limit for the N×NN\times N Newton-coordinate crossing. That coefficient-space metric differs from the uniform physical N2×N2N^2\times N^2 array here. The Newton row covariance in [N] and the coherent lower-donor kernels in [H] are further closest coverage. The present addition is the entire physical-matrix Gaussian expansion with arbitrary fixed-order Hilbert–Schmidt errors and its prefactor. No general novelty for lattice Laplace expansions is claimed. Small boundary tilts have not been matched to arbitrary geometric Conway data. Even a full-matrix relative error can exceed the original closing overlap. Neither a whole short-component propagation theorem nor a scalar geometric lower or finite-exception theorem follows.
