Exact strip reconstruction on every compact positive angle triangle

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The actual balanced Gauss polynomial has a single cardinal projection of an exact factorial ratio. Pairing the two sides of a width-one strip gives an explicit boundary current with both Fermat numerators. The current is polynomially bounded on the real scalar torus, uniformly on a scaled meridian disk. Consequently the exact ratio is the selected, nonzero asymptotic throughout every compact positive angle triangle, including a fixed complex neighborhood and fixed normal derivatives.

This is a scalar reconstruction theorem for the polynomial in complete direct C. Its Laurent-polynomial evaluation on the actual Cartan and Casimir, its Jordan data, exterior coupons, and source functional are still the ones in C. No operator norm, coherent-kernel estimate, or geometric source coefficient is inferred from this scalar theorem.

4. The paired current has polynomial size

5. The positive triangle and the selected coefficient

On a compact complex subdomain with x,y,x+yx,y,x+y in the strip, F’s uniform expansion and (19) give FN(Nx+β)=iN(1−ec0x)(1−ec0x)βeNI(x)(1+O(N−1)).(30) F_N(Nx+\beta)= \sqrt{iN(1-e^{c_0x})}\, (1-e^{c_0x})^\beta e^{NI(x)}(1+O(N^{-1})). \qquad\text{(30)} Insert (30) and (19) into (3). 6. Actual normals and the remaining source question

The entire proof keeps c=M−2Qc=M^{-2}Q before specialization. On the root, at fixed x,yx,y, the true μ=Log⁡M\mu=\operatorname{Log} M derivative is ∂μLog𝒮N|μ=0=2Nc0{log⁡N+γE−Log⁡Λ(x,y)}+O(1).(33) \left.\partial_\mu\operatorname{Log}\mathcal S_N\right|_{\mu=0} =\frac{2N}{c_0} \{\log N+\gamma_E-\operatorname{Log}\Lambda(x,y)\}+O(1). \qquad\text{(33)} Indeed ∂μ=−(2N/c0)∂β\partial_\mu=-(2N/c_0)\partial_\beta, and Γ′(1)=−γE\Gamma'(1)=-\gamma_E. Fixed higher scaled normal derivatives of the relative reconstruction error are exponentially small; converting to fixed μ\mu-derivatives introduces the corresponding powers of NN. No moving-root normal is asserted.

C’s upper-carry array and its first normal have not been omitted: they are present in (9)–(11), and therefore in the exact strip current. Likewise (15) explicitly retains the infinity complement through its paired sides. A fixed-height estimate of just one side could have the positive rate −ℜI(x)-\Re I(x); it is (14) and the orbit weight (29) that prove the cancellation needed here.

For the actual operator one still has C’s ordered evaluation 𝒜N=HdKd𝒮N(u,v;c)\mathcal A_N=H_dK^d\mathcal S_N(u,v;c), symmetric in u,vu,v, followed by its original routed factors and source closure. This note does not replace those commuting matrix/Jordan arguments by arbitrary scalar x,yx,y, bound their conditioned evaluation, or put a geometric opposite-polarization kernel into UU. Moving arguments, the outer HdKdH_dK^d, quotient, turnover, central normalization, pivots, and marks retain all their own normal terms. A nonzero scalar coefficient on this fixed chart is not a nonzero original closed source.
