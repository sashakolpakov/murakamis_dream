Complete source multiplicities with exact original braid and cap data

29 October 2026. The original three-head cup source has a COMPLETE N-dimensional Hopf multiplicity module, including all intermediate Casimir Jordan channels. Both original adjacent braids act on this same module. Its recoupling is an explicit reversed q-Pascal matrix, not a projection onto the two-dimensional source channel. The actual cup and ordinary cap become opposite endpoint coordinates after that recoupling. The original Hermite source packet also has a polynomially conditioned recoupled frame, although the full recoupling matrix can have exponential norm.

The same construction reduces the COMPLETE original even plat at arbitrary fixed width to a central-degree multiplicity space. The full marked cap is evaluated only after this whole invariant-tensor identification; its resulting source row and vector have explicitly polynomial norms. Every reduced signed generator is given below. No sharp whole-word norm, geometric coefficient, or complete-channel contractivity is inferred from this exact reduction.

1. Original finite modules and conventions

Put N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, x=qd=-q(-1), rho=q-q^(-1), F_a=(Q;Q)_a. On the original balanced V=V_d use

F|a>=(1-Q^(a+1))|a+1>,
Ecal|a>=(Q^(-a)-1)|a-1>, K|a>=xQ^(-a)|a>,
H|a>=(d-2a)|a>, 0<=a<=d.                            (1)

Missing endpoint arrows are zero. The ORIGINAL coproduct is

F_12=F_1+K_1^(-1)F_2,
E_12=Ecal_1 K_2+Ecal_2, K_12=K_1K_2.                (2)

Use these iterated coproducts on every physical prefix. All their N-th E and F powers vanish: the individual terms Q-commute, and the root q-binomial makes their N-th power the sum of the individual N-th powers. We retain the unrolled H, not only K=q^H.

The original turns, including all N powers and the pivot, are

p_(u,v)=N x^(-v) delta_(u+v,d),
ell_(u,v)=x^(-u)Q^(-u)/N delta_(u+v,d),
m_(u,v)=x^v/N^2, WITHOUT a degree delta.              (3)

The cup p and ordinary cap ell are literal Hopf maps. They satisfy the two snake equations, ell p=0 and m p=1. The marked row m is not an invariant cap and is never replaced by ell. The common Cartan kappa=q(d2/2), original framing, modified dimension and external scalar chi_N remain on the original crossing/source line.

Write

Omega=F_tot E_tot+qK_tot+q^(-1)K_tot^(-1).

The original crossing is kappa r_N(Omega_pair), including its confluent value AND derivative data. Its inverse is the literal inverse polynomial. No semisimple root evaluation is substituted for a double channel.

2. A complete three-head Hom space from an explicit highest lift

Let W=(V tensor V)_(integer degree d), with basis e_j=|j,d-j>, j=0,…,d. For EVERY w in W set

Then I_w is an actual unrolled Hopf intertwiner V -> V^tensor3. Moreover EVERY such intertwiner occurs exactly once. In particular

Hom_Hopf,H(V,V^tensor3) is isomorphic to W,
dim Hom=N, dim sum_w image(I_w)=N^2.                (5)

Here is a direct proof including endpoints. A highest vector of the required weight has integer degree d. If its last-head-c component is z_c, its E equation gives

z_(c+1)=-xQ E_12 z_c/(1-Q^(c+1)),

with the action interpreted as in (4). Thus the c=0 component w determines it and gives exactly H_L. The final c=d equation is E_12^N w=0. Conversely this proves the E equation for every w. Its total K and H are precisely the highest K=x,H=d of V. The quantum-group commutator then gives, along its F string,

E_tot I_w|a>=(Q^(-a)-1)I_w|a-1>.

The last F arrow is zero by F_tot^N=0. This proves the full intertwiner, not only a highest-weight kernel comparison.

For each a<N, applying E_tot^a to I_w|a> returns a NONZERO scalar times H_L(w). Distinct a have different unrolled degrees. Consequently the N copies of V in (5) are independent. The proof also gives an inverse: restrict the highest vector to last color0. No complementary Clebsch channel or internal excursion was dropped.

Both original adjacent signed braids commute with the entire Hopf action and H. They therefore act exactly on this complete module. The source cup intertwiner is w=p: E_12 p=0 gives

H_L(p)=p tensor |0>, I_p(v)=p tensor v.             (6)

The generalized source vector t from the tame Hermite theorem must be lifted as H_L(t). The bare vector t tensor |0> is generally not highest and must not replace it.

The total-character slice has no missing generalized extension

The center identity follows directly by multiplying the N shifted factors FE=Omega-qK-q(-1)K(-1):

Indeed product_(r=0)(N-1)[z-(uQr+u(-1)Q(-r))] =T_N(z)-uN-u(-N), with u=qK and q^N=-1; FNEN=0. On three heads K_totN=(-1)d. The root Omega=-2 of T_N(z)+2(-1)^d is SIMPLE, since T_N’(-2)=(-1)(N-1)N2. Thus total-character extensions at -2 are not being silently removed.

In fact ker(Omega_123+2) is exactly the N^2-dimensional image (5). Any highest vector in it has K=x, hence degree d modulo N. Degree d is covered by (4). The intermediate pair Casimirs DO have their size-two Jordan channels, and all of those remain below.

4a. A COMPLETE positive Hermite metric with both original sources polynomial

4b. A local cap-Ward quotient does not propagate through an adjacent crossing

There is a minimal ORIGINAL-source test for quotienting currents before taking a positive norm. Set D=A-c_*I on the complete W above. Since ell D=0, the ordinary-cap row descends to the LINEAR quotient

The primitive has polynomial norm O(N^(7/2)), by the proved tame source frame and |rho|>=4/N. Thus no large-current asymptotic is needed for this test. On the other hand the literal original crossing gives

The sum telescopes by Q^(r+1)F_r=F_r-F_(r+1), F_N=0. Therefore R_23(im D) is not contained in im D, so there is no induced map R_23:Q_cap->Q_cap. All kappas and boundary normalizations are the original ones, and (13) identifies this pairing with the original whole marked source. This argument uses one actual source and crossing; it does not rely on the separate full-observable-algebra no-quotient theorem.

A GLOBAL equation/source quotient can still retain the transported current and its boundary term. The local calculation above supplies no geometric bound on that global quotient energy. In particular, a polynomial boundary Riesz norm or a polynomial primitive for one Ward current does not prove a sharp energy estimate across arbitrary overlapping crossings.

6. Every induced generator has an explicit actual Casimir

Write the even prefix in (19) as A_prefix tensor B_head, with 2k-3 and one physical head respectively. On its degree D slice put

Here is an extraction proof, rather than an assumed spectral matching. For the penultimate pair only last colors0,1 in (19) contribute to its output color0. Physical generators inside the prefix commute with its total E/F and give the first formula.

These formulas implement the entire original scalar in a coherent source-generated module of polynomial dimension at fixed width.
