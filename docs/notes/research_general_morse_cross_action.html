The actual CROSS geometric action on a general Morse source

31 October 2026. Independent analytical/source extension. The conclusion concerns the existing CROSS principal action, actual geometric marking, local packet, and global spectral gap. It does not restore the full numerator by the older Fejér/flat-forest argument.

Hypotheses retained

Use the actual augmented link and fixed coupon frames constructed in general Morse source realization. Assume its chosen white triangulation is simplicial and has the same labeled nondegenerate positive right-angled certificate required by the existing marked-circle and complete-source theorems. Choose adjacent MAIN faces u,v as the two protected faces. The original local cofactor estimates and complete coefficient anchor are the analytic inputs; section 4 checks their shared-source assembly independently of full-numerator restoration. An arbitrary multigraph diagonal choice without such a certificate is not included in this geometric audit.

Verdict

The extension retains the original positive certificate and local analytic hypotheses. It supplies the general Morse action and the two stated spectral estimates. Restoring the full source requires the new non-CROSS gap, rather than the earlier flat-forest argument.

The unchanged local collar and global regulator inputs are the primitive collar, complete-core group and regulator assembly, marked gluing rigidity, and peripheral action boundary correction. This dependency extension does not assert full-numerator restoration or a global period bound for the full source.
