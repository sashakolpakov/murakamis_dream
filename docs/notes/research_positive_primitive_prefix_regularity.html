Proper Newton prefixes at a positive primitive filling

Private written proof, 15 November 2026.

At a positive solution of the original primitive filling equations, every proper group of incident edges has a nonzero period component in a fixed real direction. This excludes all algebraic divisors of the intermediate Newton labels, independently of the signed powers assigned to the group. A binary resolution that leaves one incident factor for the final complementary pairing therefore has no algebraically singular multiplication or single-crossing gate.

This does not place the labels in the strips required by the original factorial asymptotics. It does not determine a coefficient in the original quantum integration cycle.

1. Comparing one endpoint with its complete value

For an original positive edge use the canonical angles t,ut,u, returns rt,rur_t,r_u, and coordinates a=tan⁡(t/2),b=tan⁡(u/2),q2=1+a2+b2.(1) a=\tan(t/2),\qquad b=\tan(u/2),\qquad q^2=1+a^2+b^2. \qquad\text{(1)} The pinned positive-edge theorem gives the exact criterion ℜq>(ℜa)2+(ℜb)2,(2) \Re q>\sqrt{(\Re a)^2+(\Re b)^2}, \qquad\text{(2)} and the global comparison ℜ{δrtδt¯+δruδu¯}>0(3) \Re\{\delta r_t\,\overline{\delta t} +\delta r_u\,\overline{\delta u}\}>0 \qquad\text{(3)} for any two distinct positive configurations. Their joining segment need not remain positive. Its canonical branches satisfy |ℜt|,|ℜu|<π,|ℑrt|,|ℑru|<π/2.(4) |\Re t|,|\Re u|<\pi,\qquad |\Im r_t|,|\Im r_u|<\pi/2. \qquad\text{(4)}

Lemma 1. If (t,u)(t,u) is positive, so is (0,u)(0,u). If t≠0t\ne0, ℜ(rtt¯)>0.(5) \Re(r_t\overline t)>0. \qquad\text{(5)}

2. A strip containing every proper prefix

Fix a selected vertex of the original circle tree, and count its incident factors individually. Write its angle as tt, its returns as rer_e, and its real signed power as nn. 4. Signed crossings and a regular algebraic resolution

A single crossing of sign σ=±1\sigma=\pm1 changes a proper-prefix return by R↦R−iσt/2.(22) R\longmapsto R-i\sigma t/2. \qquad\text{(22)} Both returns remain in 𝒮t\mathcal S_t, so their input and output label sines are nonzero. The sine of their difference is also nonzero: equality of their clocks would, by (21), force e−iσt=1e^{-i\sigma t}=1, contrary to ℑt<0\Im t<0.

For an integer physical crossing power, any selected vertex of degree at least two has a direct algebraically regular resolution. Leave one incident factor for the final complementary Frobenius pairing. Multiply the other factors by any binary tree, apply the required signed crossing layers, and perform that pairing. Every gate prefix has a nonempty proper edge set, so all the preceding exclusions apply. The degree-two zero-power expression has no gate to check. Distributing crossing layers among other proper subproducts gives the same divisor exclusions.

These are exact operations in the full original Newton algebra. Multiplication is associative and commutative; crossing powers are literal multiplications by RnR^n; signed powers fuse exactly. The final pairing remains ϕN(eiej)=𝟏i+j=N−1\phi_N(e_i e_j)=\mathbf1_{i+j=N-1}, including all repeated-node normal data. No factors or normals are discarded.

The rational fiber Hessians displayed in the spectral-adapter note are consequently finite and nonzero at these labels. Every sine in their formulas is among those just checked, their exponential factors are units, and (2−c)/4=1/(1+a2)≠0(2-c)/4=1/(1+a^2)\ne0. This is an algebraic statement about those formulas, not a factorial asymptotic outside its proved domain.

5. Remaining analytic and endpoint questions

The complete edge set, with its full power, has return −πi-\pi i on the boundary of (11). It is handled by the complementary pairing, not as a strict multiplication gate. A degree-one vertex has no nonempty proper subset, so this theorem supplies no degree-one endpoint chart.

The theorem removes genuine algebraic prefix resonance as an additional obstruction at an existing positive primitive point. It does not prove that every hyperbolic partial filling has such a positive point.

There is a separate analytic question. When ℜt≠0\Re t\ne0, the strip (11) is oblique and its boundary generally has a non-real image under (14). A holomorphic logarithm on (11) does not by itself place the real parts of all arguments (18), or the crossing arguments, between zero and one. The continued primitive and its integer Fourier characters must still be matched to a domain where the original factorial interpolation has the required asymptotics. No label is reset here.

Even a valid factorial comparison and local Morse equivalence do not determine the geometric block’s coefficient in the original finite cycle. Endpoints, other Fourier modes and the full coherent contraction remain. No new volume limit or knot family is asserted.
