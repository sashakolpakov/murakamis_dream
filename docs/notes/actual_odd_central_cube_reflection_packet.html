An actual positive central-sector period for the primitive cube

29 October 2026. For every odd level the all-Steinberg sector of the original ordered cube source is an explicit strictly positive Newton period, times its recorded edge-twist phase. The physical mark, all circle factors and all root normals are retained. The reduction is a generic pivotal contraction followed by a regular specialization; it does not replace the marked source by a Euclidean spin-network norm.

This evaluates ONE specified joint channel of the cube. It does not identify the whole color sum with this sector, prove its dominance, handle even levels, or establish a full cube volume conjecture.

1. The original cube, its pointing and the statement

Put N=2m+1>=3, d=2m, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d, and

s_r=product_(u=1)^r 2sin(pi u/N), s_0=1,
beta(m,r)=s_m/[s_r s_(m-r)],
S_N=sum_(r=0)^m beta(m,r)^4,
A_N=q^[m(2-m)] S_N,
omega_m=kappa(-1)^m q^[m(m+1)], kappa=q^(d^2/2).

The exact A_N is the original odd black source. Use this actual ordered, noncrossing cube, with increasing port orders:

black 0:(0,1,2), 1:(3,4,11), 2:(5,9,10), 3:(6,7,8),
white 4:(0,10,11), 5:(1,8,9), 6:(2,3,7), 7:(4,5,6).

The original unrestricted mark is on the EXPOSED outer cap of white vertex 4. A nested bare mark is not substituted for it. On every edge retain the actual complete-circle sector with j=m, and a fixed signed crossing exponent e_l. This sector is

R^(e_l)L_St=omega_m^(e_l) N E_St.

Let H_N=C[z]/P_N, P_N=2T_N(z/2)+2, be the complete physical pair commutant, including its double-node normals. Its full original functional and genuine turn are denoted phi and Rturn. Define

E_St=the isolated c_m=-2 pair projector,
h_N=Rturn(E_St).

Then the original RAW marked central-sector cube contribution is

Z_cube,St=N^12 S_N^8 phi(h_N^4)
               omega_m^(sum_(l=0)^11 e_l),          (1)

and

phi(h_N^4)>0                                   (2)

at every odd N>=3. External line/framing normalizations, if present in the original invariant, remain external to (1). No factor depending on an ordinary root quantum dimension is divided out after it has been specialized to zero.

2. The generic cups, cyclic clocks and the literal rung reduction

All generic statements in this section use zeta before root specialization, with Q=zeta^2 and F_r=(Q;Q)_r. On highest weight L use the canonical cup with FIRST coefficient one:

P_L(u)=(-1)^u zeta^[u(L-u-1)] F_u F_(L-u)/F_L,
p_L(u,L-u)=P_L(u), ell_L=p_L^(-1).

The actual physical highest-d cup is instead

pV(u,d-u)=N zeta^(-d^2)P_d(u).                  (3)

It specializes exactly to the recorded root physical cup. Both its factorials and its overall scalar are retained. In particular the genuine generic turn of a pair map A is

(Rturn_zeta A)_((u,b);(v,e))
   =pV(d-e)/pV(d-u) A_((d-u,v);(b,d-e)).        (4)

Its root simplification x^(u-e) is NOT continued generically. The global scalar in (3) cancels in (4), but its factorial ratios do not.

Let W:V_d->V_d tensor V_d be the generic ordinary Clebsch map of spin (m,m;m), whose first highest entry is one. Put eta=D(W)W, where D is the genuine bilinear pivotal dual, with reversed abstract dual order. The finite unequal-Clebsch norm calculation in the four-port kernel, equations (4)–(6), gives

eta_m=(-1)^m zeta^[-m(3m+1)] F_(3m+1)/(F_m F_(2m+1)).

That calculation is the coefficient of a single contiguous product of reciprocal finite Pochhammers, not a fitted norm. Write

a_m=(-1)^m zeta^[-m(m+3)],
Theta=(W tensor I)p_d/a_m,
e_m=W D(W)/eta_m,
c_m=eta_m/a_m^2.                               (5)

Thus Theta(2m,m,0)=1, exactly the canonical ordered invariant used in the first-normal source theorem. Equations (3)–(5) involve two uses of the same highest-d object: its ordinary canonical cup in Theta, and its scaled physical cup in the original source. These are not conflated. The scale cancels in the turn and every closed cup/cap snake, while the physical source factors are kept below.

Moving the last magnetic leg to the front by the ACTUAL snakes gives

(C Theta)(a,b,c)=K(a)Theta(b,c,a),
K(a)=zeta^(d-2a),  C Theta=Theta.               (6)

The clock follows directly from ell_d^T p_d=K^(-1), since d is even. For the scalar in (6), C^3 is K_total and hence is identity on integer H=0 invariants. The generic invariant line has dimension one. At a real positive generic zeta the scalar is real, so its cube being one forces it to be one. All entries are rational functions of zeta; therefore this is a rational identity. Dualizing gives the matching white rotation, with inverse clock. No plain permutation of the three magnetic legs is substituted for (6).

Here is the explicit prism cut in the ordered cube:

top cycle    (0,4,1,6), edges (0,11,3,2),
bottom cycle (5,2,7,3), edges (9,5,6,8),
rung pairs   (0,5),(4,2),(1,7),(6,3),
rung edges   1,10,4,7, respectively.            (7)

Apply (6) only to put each indicated rung into the cup/evaluation slot. On a rung, substitute Theta=(W tensor I)p_d/a_m and its actual dual. The two intermediate cup/cap matrices cancel, and the surviving map on its four side legs is

W D(W)/a_m^2=c_m e_m.                          (8)

This is an all-side-legs-open identity: D(W)W=eta_m I is used only to identify the normalized projector e_m. For example the elementary sum in (8) is W_(ab,r)D(W)_(r,ce)/a_m^2; when a side leg is brought around a bend its p_d and ell_d are the same inverses in (6). The opposite cyclic move contributes K^(-1), so no residual independent clock is removed. This proves the rung replacement before doing the four-side contraction.

After the four replacements, (7) is precisely the cyclic four-box pretzel closure with e_m in each box. Its two strand orientations are the ones supplied by the preceding snakes. Writing its edge indices as a_u,b_u (u modulo 4), the remaining contraction is

sum_(a_0,b_0,...,a_3,b_3)
  K(a_0)K(b_0) product_(u=0)^3
    (Rturn_zeta e_m)_((a_u,b_u);(a_(u+1),b_(u+1))).

This is Tr[(K tensor K)(Rturn_zeta e_m)^4]. Equivalently its entry in terms of the unturned box is exactly the ratio pV(d-b_(u+1)) / pV(d-a_u) in (4); it is the generic physical ratio, not a root monomial. Denoting the unmarked ordinary cube spin network by Xi, the result is the generic rational identity

Xi_cube=c_m^4 F_4(e_m),
F_4(e_m)=Tr[(K tensor K)(Rturn_zeta e_m)^4].      (9)

For an explicit index check of this last closure, let t_i be the four top edges in (7), and b_i its four bottom edges, indices modulo 4. The four rung contractions, in that order and after removing c_m, are exactly

K(t_3)^(-1) e_((t_3,t_0);(b_3,b_0)),
K(t_0)      e_((b_1,b_0);(t_1,t_0)),
K(t_1)^(-1) e_((t_1,t_2);(b_1,b_2)),
K(b_2)      e_((b_3,b_2);(t_3,t_2)).             (9a)

For the last row, the initial clock is K(b_3)(-1)K(r)(-1); the black vertex’s H=0 condition makes it K(b_2). The other three rows follow directly from (6), putting the black rung in its third slot and the white rung in its first slot, then applying (8).

The full pivotal self-duality of the channel projector reads

e_((a,b);(c,e))
   =P_d(d-b)P_d(d-a)/[P_d(d-e)P_d(d-c)]
      *e_((d-e,d-c);(d-b,d-a)).                (9b)

It follows by dualizing W D(W)/eta (or directly from the nested pair cup); it includes both reversed tensor order and both cup ratios. Apply (9b) to rows 2 and 4 of (9a). In the four turn factors of (9) make the bijective index substitution

(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3)=(d-t_3,t_0,d-t_1,t_2),
(b'_0,b'_1,b'_2,b'_3)=(b_3,d-b_0,b_1,d-b_2).

Their four e entries are now exactly those in (9a). Their scalar ratios agree by P_d(d-u)=K(u)^(-1)P_d(u) and the e entry’s cleared weight equality K(t_3)K(t_0)=K(b_3)K(b_0). The remaining clocks are precisely K(a_0)K(b’_0), the pivotal trace in (9). This proves (9) by finite substitution, without a picture-dependent cyclic trace.

The vertex orders, rungs and clocks in (6)–(9b) are the source certificate. An unlabeled cube or a product of closed theta values would not establish (9).

3. Regular specialization preserves the original physical mark

At the odd root c_m=-2 is isolated from EVERY other pair node. Consequently the generic e_m has a regular spectral-projector lift, and specializes to the full E_St. This assertion includes its zero first normals on the OTHER double nodes; it is stronger than matching values at distinct nodes. Formula (4) is regular there as well.

The generic ordinary trace of the four-box closure is [N]_zeta times its open scalar, because d is even. The original exposed physical mark extracts exactly the root of this scalar: m pV=1. Equivalently, with t=Log(zeta/q),

nu_N=(d/dt)[N]_(q exp t)|_(t=0)
     =2N(-1)^N/(q-q^(-1)),
F_4(e_m)'(0)/nu_N=phi(h_N^4).                  (10)

This is the genuine marked closure of the full pair source algebra. It is legitimate to choose the regular projector lift here: the root open scalar depends only on the root coupon. A change of lift alters the unmarked closure by [N] times a scalar vanishing at the root, and hence does not alter (10). It does NOT say that E_St is the original moving-q complete-circle germ.

Since F_4(e_m)(q)=0, differentiating (9) retains no c_m’ term. The finite ratio in (5) gives at the root

c_m(q)=(-1)^m q^[-m^2+5m]=q^[-3m^2+4m].        (11)

Indeed F_(3m+1)/(F_m F_(2m+1)) reduces, after removing its COMMON root zero, to product_(u=1)m(1-Q(N+u))/F_m=1. No vanishing F_N is canceled by a value-only substitution.

In the full original source reduction at this central label,

h0_m=N, gamma_m=q^(2-m^2)/N^2,
s_mmm=N^3 A_N, G_(m,r)=1.

There are twelve edge factors h0 gamma and eight source coefficients. The exposed first-normal theorem and (9)–(11) therefore give

Z_cube,St=(N gamma_m)^12 (N^3 A_N)^8
                    c_m(q)^4 phi(h_N^4) product_l omega_m^(e_l).

Its magnitude prefactor is N^12 S_N^8. The entire residual phase is q1=q2=1. This proves (1), including its absolute normalization and original marked cut.

4. A full-root positive polynomial, with every nodal value explicit

Use the real Casimir Newton basis of the genuine turn theorem:

c_j=q^(2j+1)+q^(-2j-1),
Bhat_r(z)=product_(j<r)(c_j-z)/s_r^2,
Rturn Bhat_r=Bhat_(d-r),
phi(Bhat_i Bhat_j)=delta_(i+j,d).               (12)

The full isolated projector is

E_St=Bhat_m^2.

To see this without losing normals, its polynomial has degree d, a DOUBLE zero at every paired node, value one at -2, and product_(j<m)(c_j+2)=N=s_m^2. Thus it is the full Hermite projector. The proved positive multiplication formula followed by (12) yields

h_N(z)=sum_(r=0)^m beta(m,r)^2 Bhat_r(z).        (13)

Its half degree and all its first normal coefficients are fixed by this genuine polynomial identity. For every distinct node j<=m, direct sine factorization gives

Bhat_r(c_j)=0, r>j,
Bhat_r(c_j)=s_(j+r)/[s_(j-r)s_r^2], r<=j.

Consequently

h_N(c_j)=sum_(r=0)^j
   s_(j+r)/[s_(j-r)s_r^2] beta(m,r)^2
   =P_(N,j)>0.                                (14)

This is EXACTLY the positive all-node factorial sum in the actual one-leg source, with its own source phase kept there. Equation (14) identifies the polynomial values with that sum; the stronger full-normal statement is (13). In particular h_N(-2)=S_N and h_N(c_0)=1.

For clarity the positive multiplication coefficients are, when i+j<N,

mu_(i,j,k)=s_(i+j)s_k /
    [s_i s_j s_(k-i)s_(k-j)s_(i+j-k)]>0,
max(i,j)<=k<=i+j;

the product vanishes in the FULL algebra if i+j>=N. Write h_N^2= sum a_k Bhat_k. Then

a_k=sum_(i,j<=m, max(i,j)<=k<=i+j)
          beta(m,i)^2 beta(m,j)^2 mu_(i,j,k)>0,
phi(h_N^4)=sum_(k=0)^d a_k a_(d-k)>0.           (15)

The last strictness holds at EVERY k, since one can choose i+j=k with i,j<=m. This uses the original functional, so the first normals that define phi have not been discarded. No assertion that Rturn(h_N2)=h_N2 is needed. In fact the analogous square-turn identity is false generically with the full cups (3); the audit explicitly records this failed stronger premise.

5. What the positive period does and does not settle

At N=3, h_N=(4-z)/3 and phi(h_N^4)=11. Formula (1) then gives 108^4 times 11 in the zero-twist central sector, agreeing with the independently derived exact spin-one cube source.

The geometric target for the full retained-circle cube source is

Vol(actual augmented cube)=16v8+2Vol(Q_cube),

where Q_cube is the explicitly labeled ideal right-angled cuboctahedron of the rectified-core geometric theorem. The period in (15) is the remaining correlated-core contribution; it is not a product of four independent theta-block norms. The positive cuboctahedral period theorem now proves lim_(N odd) pi log(phi(h_N^4))/N=Vol(Q_cube). Combining it with

S_N=(1/4)N^(-3/2)exp(Nv8/pi)(1+O(1/N))

and (1) proves that THIS central sector has rate 16v8+2Vol(Q_cube), the actual cube-link volume. It does not control the other joint sectors, their interference or even levels.

The accompanying profile calculation tests the more precise candidate

phi(h_N^4) ~ C_4 N^(-9/2) exp(N J_4),
J_4=(16/pi)[Lambda(3pi/8)+Lambda(pi/8)],
C_4=4^(-3) sin(pi/4) cos(pi/4)^(-1/2)/2.

These growing-level values are labeled as prefactor calibrations, not used as a proof of this asymptotic in the present note. The exact source reduction (1) and strict positivity (15) are unconditional.

Audit: original generic cups, full-root kernels and literal marked cubes, with recorded results. It checks six generic cyclic/rung families at zeta=2,3; six odd full physical root kernels including all polynomial normals; eight literal marked cube sources with signed edge powers; and five multiprecision positive-period prefactor calibrations. Finite checks supplement the pivotal and full-polynomial proofs above; they do not establish a whole-color or all-parity source limit.

The result concerns the stated reflected source, not equality with the whole cube invariant.
