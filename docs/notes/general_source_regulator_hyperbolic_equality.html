A general source-regulator equality theorem and its exact quantum scope

29 October 2026. The geometric equality step extends to EVERY oriented complete finite-volume cusped hyperbolic three-manifold. No pretzel, alternating, bridge-width, polyhedral, or positive ideal-triangulation hypothesis is required. The proof below constructs the nearby local geometry explicitly by equivariant averages, rather than assuming a general deformation or Dehn filling theorem.

This does NOT extend the proved original-source asymptotic to every knot. Section8 identifies the additional quantum hypotheses, their proved pretzel instance, and the currently missing broader source estimates. Neither a formal saddle nor a correct cusp Hessian supplies those inputs.

1. General geometric completion theorem

Let M be an oriented complete finite-volume hyperbolic three-manifold with h torus cusps and geometric holonomy rho0. Fix primitive oriented bases (mu_i,lambda_i) of its actual peripheral groups. On each torus fix the complete Euclidean cusp coordinate, so its two holonomies are translations by a real-linearly independent pair of complex numbers.

There is a neighborhood U of the complete FRAMED representation with the following property. A point of U consists of an actual representation

rho: pi1(M) -> PSL(2,C)

and, for each cusp, a common peripheral fixed flag close to its complete flag. Use a frame depending continuously on that flag to send it to infinity. The peripheral transformations are then complex similarities. Let U_i,V_i be the uniquely small logarithms of the complex similarity multipliers of mu_i,lambda_i, continued from zero at completeness.

Select any subset F of cusps to fill. Suppose:

  1. Every unfilled peripheral group is parabolic, so BOTH similarity multipliers are one. It remains nontrivial near rho0.

  2. For each i in F, choose an arbitrary primitive integer pair (a_i,b_i) and a sign epsilon_i in {+1,-1}, and impose the ACTUAL log equation

    a_i U_i+b_i V_i=2pi i epsilon_i.                 (1)

Then rho descends to the Dehn filling of M along the actual slopes sigma_i=a_i mu_i+b_i lambda_i. The descended representation is the holonomy of a positively oriented COMPLETE finite-volume hyperbolic metric on that filled topology. In particular it is discrete and faithful there. Any number of cusps may remain, including zero.

The neighborhood U is fixed before the slopes: the conclusion applies to every slope tuple and framed representation satisfying these conditions inside U. It does NOT assert existence of such a representation for all slopes. Existence is supplied by the actual source branch in the proved application, and must be supplied in another application. For nearby representations, (1) itself forces the relevant slopes to be long.

The proof is independent of a graph presentation and of any geometric triangulation existence theorem. Sections2–5 establish it directly.

2. A nearby equivariant developing map on every compact core

Identify the universal cover of the complete M with H3, and write Gamma for pi1(M), acting by rho0. Choose a compact cusp-truncated core and a slightly larger compact collar. Cover that compact set by finitely many open balls which lift injectively to H3, and choose a smooth partition of unity subordinate to them. Write eta_(j,gamma) for the lifts using one chosen lift of the j-th ball. Thus

sum_(j,gamma) eta_(j,gamma)(x)=1,
eta_(j,gamma)(rho0(delta)x)=eta_(j,delta^-1 gamma)(x).

The sum is locally finite. On a fixed compact fundamental part only finitely many gamma occur. No freeness of the deformed action is assumed.

Use the future unit hyperboloid in Minkowski space for H3. Form

Y_rho(x)=sum_(j,gamma) eta_(j,gamma)(x)
                     rho(gamma) rho0(gamma)^-1 x,
E_rho(x)=Y_rho(x)/sqrt(-<Y_rho(x),Y_rho(x)>).         (2)

Every summand is future timelike unit. A convex combination with nonnegative weights summing to one is future timelike, so normalization is defined. The Lorentz action of PSL(2,C) makes (2) unambiguous without choosing SL signs. Reindexing gamma gives exactly

E_rho(rho0(delta)x)=rho(delta)E_rho(x).              (3)

At rho=rho0 every summand in (2) equals x, so E_rho0(x)=x. On the compact fundamental part, only finitely many matrices and derivatives of fixed smooth weights occur. Consequently E_rho tends to the identity in C1 as rho tends to rho0. For rho in one sufficiently small neighborhood, it is an orientation-preserving local diffeomorphism. Equivariance then gives this property over the whole lifted compact core and collar.

This is the needed compact-core deformation proof. It uses neither an unproved compatible geodesic mesh nor a subdivision that could invert thin tetrahedra. It also does not assume local character-variety rigidity.

3. An explicit positive similarity link and an entire cusp tail

In one complete cusp write its cover as

(w,t) in C x [t0,infinity),
(w,t) -> (w+lambda,t), lambda in Lambda,
dev0(w,t)=(w,exp(t)),

where Lambda is the complete translation lattice. In the moving selected flag frame, denote the deformed affine action by

A_rho(lambda)(z)=a_lambda z+b_lambda.

It is a homomorphism from Lambda to Aff(C), tends to the original translation action, and all a_lambda are nonzero.

Choose a smooth compactly supported nonnegative function eta on C with

sum_(lambda in Lambda) eta(w-lambda)=1.

Such eta is obtained from any bump whose translates cover a fundamental parallelogram by dividing by its positive periodic translate sum. Define

d_rho(w)=sum_lambda eta(w-lambda)
                            A_rho(lambda)(w-lambda). (4)

Affine maps commute with convex combinations of total weight one, so

d_rho(w+nu)=A_rho(nu)d_rho(w).                       (5)

At completeness every term is w. On a fixed compact fundamental domain, d_rho is C1-close to w; its real Jacobian is therefore positive and nonzero. Equivariance propagates this local-diffeomorphism property everywhere. Thus (4) gives a genuine nonsingular similarity structure on the COMPACT cusp torus, not just a formal list of peripheral matrices. The developed images and Euclidean lengths of fixed generator paths are uniformly bounded near completeness.

Let chi_rho:C->R be the real-linear extension of the character lambda -> log|a_lambda|. The map

F_rho(w,t)=(d_rho(w),exp(t+chi_rho(w)))              (6)

is exactly rho-equivariant in upper half-space: its horizontal and vertical coordinates transform by a_lambda z+b_lambda and |a_lambda|y. Its differential is triangular with positive determinant exp(t+chi_rho(w)) det_R Dd_rho(w). Hence it is an orientation-preserving local diffeomorphism for EVERY t>=t0. At completeness it is dev0.

Restore the continuously varying flag frame when viewing (6) in the global target H3. Define it on one chosen lift of each disjoint cusp neighborhood and propagate by rho-equivariance to the other lifts. The exact peripheral equivariance makes the coset representative irrelevant, so this gives a well-defined map on the entire lifted end. On a fixed compact cusp collar it is C1-close to the original developing map. Interpolate E_rho and F_rho by the unique hyperbolic geodesic between their values, with a smooth Gamma-invariant cutoff in t. The interpolation is rho-equivariant and is C1-close to the identity on that compact collar. Thus it too is a positive local diffeomorphism after one further uniform shrink of U. Set it equal to E_rho on the compact part and F_rho on each deep tail.

We have now constructed a global rho-equivariant developing LOCAL DIFFEOMORPHISM on the ideal-end-deleted original manifold. Pulling back the H3 metric yields an actual oriented curvature-minus-one metric. There are no polyhedral edge or vertex singularities to check: the map is a smooth local diffeomorphism everywhere at finite points.

The construction is simultaneous at all finitely many cusps. It works on a single neighborhood of the complete framed representation, with no comparison between different deformation sizes or slopes.

4. The small multiplier logs are the actual log-link periods

At a filled cusp (1) ensures at least one multiplier is nontrivial. Commutativity of affine peripheral maps implies that they have a common finite fixed point q in addition to infinity. For a generator with a!=1, this is q=b/(1-a). The two original generator translations are nonzero. As the representation approaches completeness, at least one such formula therefore gives |q|->infinity, uniformly over the small framed neighborhood with its parabolic locus removed.

The image of an ENTIRE closed fundamental parallelogram under d_rho is uniformly bounded by the compact C1 estimate in section3. Fixed generator paths also have uniformly bounded developed images and Euclidean lengths. For U sufficiently small the whole parallelogram image misses q, including its interior, not only its boundary paths. Equivariance and the fact that every peripheral map fixes q then show that the ENTIRE development misses q. On the simply connected link cover choose

f=Log(d_rho-q).

The differential df is invariant under deck transformations and is invertible as a REAL two-dimensional differential: d_rho is a positive local diffeomorphism and the logarithm has nonzero complex derivative. Thus |df|^2 descends to a nonsingular Euclidean metric on the compact link torus. It is complete. Its lifted local isometry f is globally one-to-one onto C: lift every Euclidean line segment by completeness to get a covering of C, which is simply connected. Its periods therefore form an injective rank-two translation lattice.

For each fixed generator path of length at most L and image in |d|<=R,

|Delta f|<=L/(|q|-R) ->0.                          (7)

The endpoint affine action gives exp(Delta_mu f)=exp(U) and similarly for V. Their differences are integer multiples of 2pi i. Both the actual increments in (7) and the prescribed small multiplier logs tend to zero, so these integers are EXACTLY zero in U:

Delta_mu f=U, Delta_lambda f=V.                    (8)

The neighborhood needed for (8) is independent of the slopes, because one first proves (8) for the FIXED basis before multiplying by a_i,b_i. No diagonalizing-frame singularity or hidden large winding is allowed. SL eigenvalue signs must first be centered to these projective geometric logs; an old longitude spin is not an extra geometric rotation.

For completeness, q need not be bounded by the inverse of one chosen coordinate if that coordinate vanishes. Choose either generator with nonunit multiplier. Each numerator stays bounded away from zero, and max(|a_mu-1|,|a_lambda-1|) tends uniformly to zero. This proves the uniform |q|->infinity statement even when one generator is parabolic.

5. Primitive filling gives a complete smooth hyperbolic manifold

Translate q to zero. Then d_rho=e^f. On the compact link quotient the positive function

exp(t0+chi_rho(w))/|d_rho(w)-q|

is bounded above: numerator and denominator transform by the same |a_lambda|. Thus the explicit end (6) contains the ENTIRE equivariant epigraph y>A|d-q| for some finite A. There is no unproved assumption that an abstract cusp link is realized by an actual full geometric end.

About the axis 0–infinity put r=y/|d-q| and use

R=asinh(1/r), theta=Im f,
X=Re f+(1/2)log(1+r^2).

Direct substitution into the upper-half-space metric gives

ds^2=dR^2+cosh(R)^2 dX^2+sinh(R)^2 dtheta^2.         (9)

The epigraph contains the whole punctured tube 0<R<R0. A log period L acts by (X,theta)->(X+Re L,theta+Im L). By (1),(8), the actual primitive slope has period 2pi i epsilon. Choose integers c,d with ad-bc=1. Its complementary primitive period cU+dV has nonzero real part, since the log-link period lattice has real rank two.

Adding R=0 in (9) is therefore an ordinary smooth hyperbolic solid torus, with meridian EXACTLY a mu+b lambda. The angular period is 2pi, not a multiple or a merely exponentiated relation. The affine holonomy of that meridian is the identity, since it has multiplier one and fixes both q and infinity. Thus rho descends to the actual filled fundamental group. Either epsilon sign changes the orientation of the meridian parameter, not the positive orientation of the metric.

At an unfilled cusp all a_lambda=1. Formula (4) is an ordinary translation-equivariant local diffeomorphism. Its compact Euclidean link is complete, so its lifted development is the Euclidean plane. Here chi=0 and (6) contains a full horoball quotient: the cusp is complete and has finite volume.

The remaining middle region is compact. The finitely many attached bounded-radius solid tori and the ordinary unfilled cusps prove that the whole filled metric is complete and finite-volume. Its complete universal developing cover is H3: lift geodesic segments under its complete local isometry to obtain a covering of simply connected H3. Consequently the descended holonomy is discrete and faithful. This proves the theorem in section1 without importing a deformation, Dehn filling, discrete-faithfulness, or character-uniqueness theorem.

6. Absolute regulator calibration for any such source branch

Let rho_z now be an actual connected marked representation family near rho0, and use the small squared peripheral logs U_i,V_i above. The elementary marked Bloch boundary/fundamental-chain calculation used in the general peripheral volume proof and the source regulator proof is independent of the diagram and number of cusps. In these coordinates it says

d Vol(rho_z)=(1/2)sum_i [Re V_i dIm U_i
                                  -Re U_i dIm V_i]. (10)

Here Vol(rho) is the absolute representation volume of the SAME oriented fundamental class and cusp eigenflags. One way to check the factor is to use eigenlogs u=U/2,v=V/2 (with their fixed central signs): the boundary is -2 sum M_i wedge lambda_i, so the Bloch–Wigner differential is 2 sum(Re v dIm u-Re u dIm v), exactly (10). Distinct boundary tori have zero mutual intersection pairing. No equal-color assumption enters.

Suppose a holomorphic source potential F on this marked branch has

dF=(1/2)sum_i V_i dU_i,
Im F(0)=Vol(M).                                    (11)

These are hypotheses about the actual peripheral connection and its absolute COMPLETE anchor, not just about its Hessian. Define

D_F=Im F-(1/2)sum_i Re U_i Im V_i.                  (12)

Direct differentiation of (11) gives the right side of (10). Both functions equal Vol(M) at the complete point. Therefore throughout the connected marked branch

D_F=Vol(rho_z).                                    (13)

This is an absolute identity. It does not reset logarithms on loops, splice different character lifts at a projected node, or infer a constant from a matching stationary polynomial. Nonzero spin terms must be retained when converting the literal source to the centered form (11); they cancel only in the paired expression (12).

At an actual filled point from section1, the representation volume in (13) is the volume of its constructed complete geometric metric. If comparing with any other complete hyperbolic metric on the same marked oriented filled topology, apply the independently proved representation-volume inequality in BOTH directions. Their volumes are equal. No unproved equality case or imported rigidity theorem is needed to identify (13) with its usual hyperbolic volume.

The finite filling-chain normalization is also diagram-independent: replace the old cusp map by its axis-valued comparison, whose shared conical boundary homotopy has volume O(Y^-2); then extend the SAME finite boundary triangulation through the solid torus with every vertex on that axis. All added tetrahedra have zero volume. This is the compatible construction in the cited peripheral/source proofs; putting only unrelated new vertices on the axis would not suffice.

7. Arbitrary primitive filling action, with the exact boundary term

When a source branch has local U coordinates and (11), a primitive slope a_i mu_i+b_i lambda_i with b_i!=0 has filling potential

F_sigma(U)=F(U)+sum_(i in F)
         [a_i U_i^2/(4b_i)-pi i epsilon_i U_i/b_i]. (14)

Its U_i derivative is

(a_i U_i+b_i V_i-2pi i epsilon_i)/(2b_i).

At its stationary filling point and with unfilled cusps complete,

Im F_sigma
  =Im F-(1/2)sum_(i in F) Re U_i Im V_i
  =Vol(rho)=Vol(M(sigma)).                         (15)

Indeed Im V_i=(2pi epsilon_i-a_i Im U_i)/b_i, which proves (15) without deleting a complex endpoint term. Constants already known to be real do not affect (15), but remain in any exact period formula. If b=0, primitivity forces |a|=1 and (1) cannot occur with small U; there is therefore no omitted near-complete filling in this chart. Equivalently one may change the primitive basis and its potential.

For the existing convention U=-z,V=-2ell,a=1,b=p, epsilon=-m, (14) is

G(z)+(z-2pi i m)^2/(4p)

up to a REAL constant. This recovers the exact chirp/source formula, including its factor two and its retained longitude spin.

8. The conditional QUANTUM bridge, and what is actually proved

The geometric theorem is unconditional for every actual framed representation satisfying section1. To prove the volume conjecture for a new original knot/source family one must additionally prove:

  • An exact original-source construction or selected-cycle argument supplies the ACTUAL near-complete representation of the base group, with the literal caps, all port equations, chosen eigenflags and true primitive preferred peripheral markings.

  • The selected parameter point exists in the fixed neighborhood, with all unfilled cusps complete and the actual small log equations

    1. with unit charges. An exponentiated equation alone is insufficient.

  • The source action has the calibrated derivative and complete constant (11), and its filling kernel has the boundary correction

    1. in the same convention.

  • The COMPLETE original finite source has a nonzero asymptotic contribution with exponential action F_sigma/(2pi i), with all other terms controlled. In particular its scalar has the form

    K_N=exp[N F_sigma/(2pi i)] A_N,
(1/N)log|A_N| ->0,                               (16)

    after the recorded original framing/normalization factors. This must be established from the source, not assumed from a formal saddle. It includes overflow and alias bands, all colors, normal derivative channels, boundary currents, Stokes accessibility and noncancellation, at the desired full sequence of levels.

Then (15),(16) prove

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N)log|K_N|=Vol(M(sigma)).      (17)

This bridge is transparent about which work remains quantum. It does not assert that a positive norm, formal gluing solution, local normal block or representation-volume upper bound implies (16). Nor does it assume every augmented diagram has a known positive original-source base period. Those conclusions need independent proofs.

The existing full original-source pretzel theorem, its literal global lift, and its marked regulator certificate do supply all four inputs for fixed odd s>=25 and independently sufficiently long fixed signed slopes, subject to the unchanged quantifiers of those source theorems. The subsequently independently audited varying-radius alias extension lowers that actual quantum width threshold to every fixed odd s>=9: its entire-color bound is B_*<.81, whereas the exact width-nine source margin is greater than .812633708. All other local analytic and geometric ingredients were already valid there. The remaining widths3,5,7 are not proved by this envelope. The specific positive-core proof was already sufficient for that class. The present result removes ALL graph/polyhedron/width restrictions from the GEOMETRIC implication, but does not claim a new unproved original-invariant family.

For other fully augmented bases, crossing-circle source/filling identities are exact, but a whole-source open-corridor survival estimate and complete global color/alias gap have not been proved just by those identities. For arbitrary knots, a classical A-curve or the general representation-volume inequality still does not provide the missing selected-source asymptotic. These are specific unproved quantum inputs, not residual uncertainty about which hyperbolic volume (15) denotes.

There is now a stronger, independently checked way to supply the last quantum bullet for integral crossing-circle fillings: the general original-source long-filling transfer proves the filled nonzero asymptotic from an EXACT even holomorphic BASE color packet, its uniform complex Gaussian expansion, and a strict gap over the entire complementary physical color cube. Its exact multilinear filling identity retains every confluent normal coefficient, and its Fourier proof keeps the endpoint currents and all charges. Thus one need not assume the desired filled asymptotic in each new application. What remains to prove for another augmented base is that specified original packet and global gap, together with its actual bounded geometric lift and calibration. The theorem does not infer those base certificates from the underlying hyperbolic geometry.

9. Adversarial scope audit of the current equality claim

The existing long-pretzel equality proof passes the following geometric checks: its actual base holonomy is complete and positively oriented; its local port construction is a genuine representation, not trace data alone; its bounded cusp frames persist; the unfilled main cusp is genuinely parabolic; its geometric multiplier logs are centered before forming the slope; and the surviving source charges give primitive 2pi, rather than an accidentally killed PSL word. These are precisely the hypotheses used here.

The following stronger statements are NOT justified by that proof:

  • Equality for every knot, every augmented diagram, short fillings, growing width, or slopes growing with N.
  • Inferring hyperbolicity from killing a slope without the local geometry and actual winding. A 4pi cone completion has the same killed PSL meridian and is excluded only by the unit period.
  • Replacing framed representation convergence by convergence of a few traces or cusp ratios. The finite C1 construction needs the actual finite generator matrices and selected flags in a bounded frame near the complete holonomy. A singular diagonalizing frame is not that input; the literal bounded port lift supplies it in the established application.
  • Calling the complete augmented volume Vol(M) the volume of a nontrivial finite filling. The latter is (15), evaluated at its actual selected parameter point.
  • Obtaining existence of a nearby representation for arbitrary long slopes from the conditional wording of section1. A source analytic family or an independently proved deformation theorem must supply that existence.
  • Treating Casson–Rivin strict concavity on the OLD complete-cusp angle slice as the filled equality proof. Its constraints differ. The direct construction above does not need an all-ideal angle chart or a universal positive triangulation existence claim.

Thus the genuine extension here is a universal geometric completion and absolute calibration theorem for any ACTUAL selected near-complete source branch. It leaves the full-source estimates exactly where they belong, and does not replace the active broader equality goal by a generic conditional assertion.

10. Focused construction checks

The new focused audit passes exact arbitrary-slope action algebra, eight Lorentz normalization/reindexing-equivariance families, and sixteen explicit affine-cusp/primitive-slope families with both winding signs. Its compactly supported cubic partition checks the positive real link Jacobian, exact affine and height equivariance, small log increments, and nonzero complementary core period. This tests the NEW construction, not only its inherited tube-coordinate formula. The universal argument is the analytical C1-continuity/compactness proof above; these finite checks make no additional knot-source or general existence claim.

Two independent analytical readers checked the equivariant geometric construction; the second also checked the arbitrary-slope action and spin conversion through section7. Both reported PASS. The review clarified that the ENTIRE fundamental parallelogram image must avoid q before equivariance is used; this follows from section3’s compact C1 estimate and is now explicit in section4. The concrete width-nine source extension and the general long-filling transfer have separately passed independent proof reads and fresh focused audit replays.
