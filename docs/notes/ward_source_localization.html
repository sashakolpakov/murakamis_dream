Deleting cut roots preserves the pointed relative Ward class

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The original cut covector does not descend after deleting the root A=1. A different literal finite cut does descend, and its matrix functional is explicitly cochain homotopic to the original on the actual Ward complex. The equivalence extends to the relative boundary resolution and to a marked whole normal family. It is a boundary/source comparison, not a rule for compressing crossings or a geometric Gaussian source-survival theorem.

1. The localized source and its exact normalization

Fix N>=2, Q primitive of order N, and the original evaluation color V=C_N=k[A^+/-1]/(A^N-1). In its evaluation basis the source is psi=(1,...,1)^t, the co-source is phi=(1/N)(1,...,1), and e=psi phi. Let S be any nonempty set of retained roots, m=|S|=N-s>0, and let P be multiplication by its characteristic idempotent. Define

psi_S=P psi, phi_S=(N/m)phi P,
e_S=psi_S phi_S=(N/m)P e P.                   (1)

One has phi_S psi_S=1, e_S^2=e_S, and Tr e_S=1. The literal new matrix cut is

Phi_S(C)=Tr(e_S C)=(1/m)sum_(a,b in S) C_(a,b). (2)

The factor N/(N-s) is retained exactly, for every s<N. The original pivot and marked defect remain available via Phi(C)=Tr(g D C); equation (2) changes the boundary representative, not the original numerical invariant on closed tangles. The entire external full-ADO factor remains unchanged.

For deletion only of A=1, let epsilon_1=(1/N)sum_(j<N)A^j and P=1-epsilon_1. The new color co-source is intrinsically

phi_S(f)=[N phi(f)-f(1)]/(N-1).               (3)

It kills the deleted root’s entire idempotent summand and factors through C_N[(A-1)^(-1)]=P C_N. Thus it removes the previously displayed localization obstruction for this new representative.

2. The actual Ward homotopy, on all matrices

Use the actual root matrices K,F and the split image Ward complex from the Ward contraction:

W_N^0=End(V), W_N^1=im delta_N,
delta_N(C)=([K,C],[F,C]),
h_N delta_N=I-i_N p_N, delta_N h_N=I,
p_N(C)=Tr(C)/N, i_N(c)=c I.

Both Phi and Phi_S take value one on I. Consequently

Phi_S-Phi=(Phi_S-Phi) h_N delta_N.            (4)

This is an explicit all-matrices cochain homotopy, with the known finite denominators in h_N. Every actual closed cut tangle is Ward closed and equals F_N(M)I. Hence

Phi_S(C_K)=Phi(C_K)=F_N(M).                   (5)

This holds wherever that original root-color cut operator is defined, as a meromorphic whole germ in M; no scalar or peripheral factor is replaced by its value at completeness.

The distinction between the two source complexes matters. In the color relative complex R(C_N) alone, phi_S and phi are not homotopic: its top cohomology is C_N, and they have different values on epsilon_1. Equation (4) uses the matrix Ward differential. It must not be presented as a homotopy in the old color relative complex without that additional differential.

3. An explicit localized Ward complex and retraction

Order the retained labels as a_1<...<a_m. Put V_S=P V and K_S=K|_(V_S). If several interior roots were deleted, the ordinary compression of F can be disconnected. Instead use the specified skip-transport matrix

(F_S)_(a_(j+1),a_j)=product_(r=a_j)^(a_(j+1)-1)(1-Q^(r+1)),
                       1<=j<m.             (6)

All these coefficients are units. This is the original F transport along the unique retained linear interval between the two labels. For deletion only of label zero it is ordinary compressed F. For m=1 it is zero, as appropriate to a one-dimensional space.

The distinct K_S eigenvalues and the connected nonzero path (6) give ker delta_S=k I_S. The same entrywise and cumulative formulas give its explicit split image Ward complex W_S and contraction h_S, with normalized row p_S=Tr/m.

Let L:V->V_S be restriction and J:V_S->V be inclusion, so JL=P,LJ=I_S. Define

c_0(C)=L C J,
c_1=delta_S c_0 h_N.                         (7)

Then c_1 delta_N=delta_S c_0: the only omitted term is delta_S c_0 i_N p_N=0. Thus (7) is a genuine chain map, not an assumed rowwise compression of Ward currents. It sends I_N to I_S and induces the identity on scalar cohomology.

A section is equally explicit. Set

s_0(T)=J T L+p_S(T)(I_N-P),
s_1=delta_N s_0 h_S.                         (8)

It is a chain map, sends I_S to I_N, and c s=I in both degrees. With H=(I-s_0 c_0)h_N in degree one, direct multiplication gives

H delta_N=I-s_0 c_0,
delta_N H=I-s_1 c_1.                         (9)

Therefore deleting any s<N roots gives an explicit homotopy retraction of these Ward complexes. The localized cut on W_S composed with c_0 is exactly (2).

This construction transports currents as well as the scalar class. It does not turn V_S into the original quantum-group module. The skip matrix is a Ward transport operator; no coproduct, crossing intertwining, or Hopf-pivotal property is inferred for it.

4. The relative source map is strict after adjoining Ward currents

For a coefficient space E, the existing relative source resolution is R(E)=R(k) tensor E, where

R(E)^1=E[t],
R(E)^2=E[t] direct_sum E direct_sum E,
d_R h=(partial_t h,h(0),h(1)).

Its contraction is natural for every linear coefficient map. Apply it degreewise to W_N and W_S and use the usual total differential d_R tensor I+(-1)^degR I tensor delta. Equations (7)–(9) extend to actual relative-Ward chain maps and homotopies. The two cut rows extend to maps into R(k); their homotopy is explicitly

r tensor x -> (-1)^deg(r) r tensor
                          [(Phi_S-Phi)h_N](x).

Thus the combined relative-Ward complex has the same nonzero pointed line after deletion. This is a precise relative source extension of (4), rather than just equality on one knot value.

5. The whole normal and its branch mark can be retained

Let R=k[[U-1]], and retain the actual finite color normal algebra

C_U=R[A]/(A^N-U).

Mark the unique root alpha(U)^N=U, alpha(1)=1. Its evaluation branches are A=alpha(U)Q^a. The idempotent

epsilon_0(U)=(1/N)sum_(j=0)^(N-1)(A/alpha(U))^j

is regular and projects onto the marked branch reducing to A=1. Put P(U)=1-epsilon_0(U). The exact whole-normal co-source is

phi_S,U(f)=[Tr_(C_U/R)(f)-f(alpha(U))]/(N-1)
         =Tr_(P(U)C_U/R)(P(U)f)/(N-1).        (10)

It is regular, normalized on P(U), and retains every normal coefficient. Since A-1 is a unit on the retained summands, (10) factors through A-1 localization. The marked deleted branch is removed as a whole; its punctured generic remnant is not used to divide away its normal contribution.

For a canonical representative f_tilde=sum_(j<N)f_j A^j, equation (10) reads [N f_0-f_tilde(alpha)]/(N-1). Multiplication still has the exact carry

A f_tilde-(Af)_tilde=f_(N-1)(A^N-U).

The source is linear, and this carry remains necessary. Deletion repairs the excluded-root obstruction; it does not make a trace functional into a ring map or erase the normal U.

The same marked construction works for any subset of branches. In the split evaluation lattice one can use K_aa=x alpha^(-1)Q^(-a) and the interior path entries F_(a+1,a)=1-alpha Q^(a+1). They specialize to the original matrices. Distinct K differences and these interior path entries are units over R. A separately specified cyclic wrap can also be retained; it does not enter the interior entries used by the Ward contraction. The retractions above therefore extend over this whole formal boundary normal family, with their normalization N/(N-s) unchanged. No Hopf or internal-network extension is inferred from this boundary construction.

6. Compression has an exact composition defect

For arbitrary matrices C,D, one has

c_0(CD)-c_0(C)c_0(D)=L C(I-P)D J.             (11)

The right side records an excursion through the deleted root sector. It is generally nonzero. Thus independently compressing each crossing, turn, or cap does not reproduce compression of the whole actual network. Nor is c_0 multiplicative or an ordinary quantum-group intertwiner. The current map (7) is the specific repair in the split Ward complex; it supplies no local R-matrix replacement or strict tensor functor.

The old statement that the original co-source fails to descend is still correct. The new result is that its pointed relative-Ward class has a literal representative supported on the geometric open chart, with an exact normalization and a marked whole-normal extension. A source-compatible internal Gaussian comparison can use this representative and its homotopy. Such a comparison must still transport the excursion currents, carries, and the other retained divisors. Nonzero geometric-source survival and the volume conjecture do not follow just from deleting the root.

Exact audit and results check the source homotopy and explicit chain retraction on matrix units, for every nonempty retained subset at seven levels. They also retain a nonzero compression-excursion defect and the marked whole-normal carry.
