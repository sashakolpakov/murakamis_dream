A nonzero geometric source along one radial leg and its turn

22 November 2026. Research note. Earlier final notes and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The original centered multiplication tensor has a uniformly growing geometric contribution when two parameters have modulus one and the third runs along a strict prism ray, including its zero endpoint. An exact reciprocal identity gives the corresponding original scalar observation after a genuine turn. The coefficient comes from the initialized source, rather than a choice of a formal saddle solution.

These are statements about a universal three-boundary source. They do not assert that arbitrary open outputs remain in this family, or that an assembled knot period satisfies the volume identity.

5. Scope and complete proof sources

This proves the presence and nonzero coefficient of the geometric contribution in two specified original scalar observations, uniformly along the full radial interval and at its signed-crossing endpoint. It is not merely a forward norm maximum or a formal critical equation. Reciprocity itself is already part of the tensor’s exact algebra; the additional estimate is the growing single-leg interval through zero and its correctly oriented turned observation.

The result supplies neither the whole output after the next arbitrary branching nor a compatible scalar observation for an arbitrary short component. Its two unit parameters are a different regime from the compact open bidisks in the cyclic vacuum-gap theorem. No uniform vacuum gap at this unit boundary is inferred here. No new knot-volume class or finite-exception theorem follows from (6) or (19) alone.
