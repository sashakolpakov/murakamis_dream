The even central trivalent packet, with its actual Jordan normal

29 October 2026. The nearest even central channel has its OWN exact source evaluation. It is not replaced by the odd simple Steinberg channel. Its coherent black-vertex scalar is a positive WEIGHTED fourth-power sine-binomial sum, nonzero for every even N>=4, with the same scalar exponential rate and prefactor as the odd anchor. The complete circle’s paired normal normalization and a bounded physical Jordan preimage are explicit. The marked white vertex is retained separately. No joint-color complex packet or global graph survival/volume theorem is asserted.

Use the literal branching vertices and the own finite binomial identities in the odd central proof.

1. Actual even image, ordinary metric and complete-circle normal weight

Put N=2m>=4, d=2m-1, j=m-1, theta=pi/N, q=exp(i theta), Q=q^2, x=qd=-q(-1), F_r=(Q;Q)_r. The central folded Casimir is c=-2cos(theta), a DOUBLE root. Its actual image module has highest weight2j=N-2 and dimensionN-1. Its highest physical degree is m, with vector

h=sum_(a=0)^m h_a |a,m-a>,
h_a=q^(a^2)[m choose a]_Q=q^(ma) beta(m,a).          (1)

The literal Ecal_pair highest-vector equation is the ratio(2) of the odd proof, so (1) is independently valid at the even root. Define

W_r=F_pair^r h/F_r, 0<=r<=2j.                     (2)

These columns give a genuine embedding of the ordinary highest-weight 2j module Q_j into the physical pair, with all arrows before the last nonzero. Its divided-F basis has exact positive single-object metric

G_rr=beta(2j,r)=|1-Q^(r+1)|/|1-Q|.                (3)

This follows from s_r s_(d-r)=N and 2j=N-2, or directly from the actual ladder norms. In particular1<=G_rr<=1/sin(theta), so changing between this ordinary basis and its positive normalized basis costs only a power of N.

The actual CD factor of the complete circle on this central image is

F_central[:,r]=sqrt(h0/G_rr) W_r
              =2sqrt(N/|1-Q^(r+1)|) W_r,
h0=4N/|1-Q|.                                     (4)

To prove (4), at the lowest degree m the full paired CD coefficient is 2s_m^2/s_1=4N/s_1, since s_m^2=2N. The inclusion is a Hopf map; the normalized target ladder then supplies exactly G_rr in(3). Equivalently the full-degree canonical CD vector/weight formula gives (4) entry by entry. This is the POSITIVE coefficient created by the actual first normal of L_N at its double zero. It is not its zero Casimir value. Indeed

W^dagger P W=0.                                   (5)

The image is P-isotropic, unlike the odd simple image. Thus using W^dagger P as an inverse frame or copying the odd P-isometry would delete the paired source normal.

The finite Clebsch formula(5) of the odd proof remains valid with the highest m in(1) and r<=2j. Two exact endpoint evaluations are now

W_(2j)(j+t,d-t)=q^j h_t, 0<=t<=m,
W_j(0,d)=q^(-j^2),
W_j(a,d-a)=q^(-j^2) f_a(c).                        (6)

Here f_a are the actual middle-degree continuants, not distinct-value normal replacements. For the first identity the lowest-vector equation has the same successive ratio as h. Its first scalar, by factorial reflection and finite Vandermonde, is

q^(-j^2) sum_s Q^(s^2)[m choose s]_Q[j choose s]_Q
  =q^(-j^2) F_d/(F_m F_j)=q^j.

The middle first entry is x(-j)F_d/(F_mF_j)=q(-j^2). All denominators have index<N. This proves every phase in(6) directly.

2. Exact coherent scalar and the full central black tensor

Let J_source=p_16 p_23 p_45 be the original nested cup source. For the moment use the unweighted rows W^dagger P at its three pair ports. At the magnetic indices(2j,j,0), its black coefficient is N^3 A_N, where (6) gives

A_N=q^(-j) sum_(k=0)^m x^(k-N)
              conjugate(h_k^2 W_j(j+k,m-k)).        (7)

This includes the conjugate q^(-j) of the LOWEST Clebsch scalar in(6). No extra turn or framing scalar is omitted. The exact evaluation is

A_N=q^[1-m(m-1)] S_N,
S_N=sum_(k=1)^(m-1) sin(k theta)cos(k theta) beta(m,k)^4. (8)

Every term is positive. Thus S_N>0 for every even N>=4. At N=2 the sum is empty, consistently with the vanished three-circle source.

The properly balanced ordinary-channel row is G(-1)Wdagger P, which is a genuine Hopf map. The ordinary triple invariant is one dimensional: its third-color-zero slice is the one-dimensional lowest-vector kernel of F on Q_j tensor Q_j, and the E recurrence uniquely determines every later slice since its third-leg coefficients are nonzero. This is also obtained from its regular generic trivalent vector, whose explicit ladder denominators here all have index<N. Normalize that tensor Theta_j to value1 at(2j,j,0). Therefore the whole balanced black tensor is

U_bal=(N^3 A_N/G_jj) Theta_j.                      (9)

In the actual positive CD coordinates(4) it becomes

U_CD(r1,r2,r3)=h0^(3/2) product_i sqrt(G_ri,ri)
                                  U_bal(r1,r2,r3),
U_CD(2j,j,0)=h0^(3/2) N^3 A_N/sqrt(G_jj).          (10)

Thus the full central black packet is known up to a genuine ordinary 3j tensor, with its scalar and every normal/positive normalization explicit. The ordinary and marked white packets are STILL their distinct full kernels; (9) is not a claim that the marked row is a zero-current invariant covector.

3. Own finite proof of the weighted quartic identity

Use the FULL actual anti-Pascal recoupling mathcal J, reserving J_source for the cups. At this even root its factorial reflection form is

mathcal J_(a,b)=q^(-1) D_a D_b [a+b choose a]_(Q^(-1)),
D_a=(-q^(-1))^a, a+b<=d; zero otherwise.

The own finite inversion proof of the odd note works at this root too: conjugate(mathcal J) P mathcal J=P. Set f=(f_a(c)) and y=mathcal J f. The actual upper-bidiagonal Casimir recurrence gives

y_a=0, a<m;
y_(m+k)=beta(j,k)^2/sin((k+1)theta), 0<=k<=j.       (11)

The repeated diagonal entries occur at j=m-1 and m; their nonzero joining arrow forces the earlier eigenvector entry y_j to vanish. Afterward the ratio is cos^2(k theta)/[sin(k theta)sin((k+1)theta)]. The last entry y_d=f_0=1 fixes the stated constant. This uses the actual repeated-node eigenvector, not two artificial simple channels.

Put b_k=beta(m,k)^2. Its endpoint relation is beta(j,k)=cos(k theta)beta(m,k). On the tail indexed by a=j+k, write w_a=q^(-k)b_k. Equations(6),(7) simplify to

A_N=-q^[j^2-j] conjugate(f)^T w.

Let z=P y and t=P w, extending them by zero through indexm. Then

z_i=cos(i theta)b_i, 0<=i<=m (so z_m=0),
t_i=-i q^i b_i, 0<=i<=m.

Full P-unitarity gives conjugate(f)=P mathcal J P y, hence the source scalar is -q1 z^T mathcal J t. Every nonzero term has indices a<=m-1,b<=m, so a+b<=d: no forbidden top-right matrix entry is added.

For a parameter a define the finite transform

T_k(a)=sum_(i=k)^m b_i(-1)^i a^i [i choose k]_(Q^(-1)).

The finite Durfee factorization used in the odd proof gives

z^T mathcal J t=q^(-1) sum_k Q^(-k^2) Z_k T'_k,
Z_k=[T_k(1)+T_k(Q^(-1))]/2, T'_k=-i T_k(1).        (12)

Put n=m-k. Converting all Gaussian factors to Q gives exactly

T_k(a)=(-1)^k a^k Q^[k^2-mk][m choose k]_Q^2
   *2phi1(Q^(-n),Q^(-n);Q^(k+1);Q,a Q^(1-k)).       (13)

At a=1 the second finite q-Chu applies, because its required argument cQ(2n)=Q(1-k). It gives

T_k(1)=(-1)^k q^[(m-k)^2] sin(k theta)b_k,
                                                1<=k<=m. (14)

Indeed the Pochhammer ratio(-Q;Q)(m-k)/(Q^(k+1);Q)(m-k) has magnitude sin(k theta) and phase q2. At k=0 that ratio has its EXACT zero factor1+Q^m, so T_0(1)=0.

One contiguous step evaluates the second term in(12). For the finite q-Chu polynomial F(z)=2phi1(Q^(-n),b;c;Q,z), at z=cQ^n/b, subtract F(z) from F(z/Q), cancel1-Q^r and shift the index. The remaining length n-1 polynomial is AGAIN second q-Chu, with parameters bQ,cQ. This gives

F(z/Q)/F(z)=1+(1-Q^(-n))(1-b)(z/Q)/(1-z/Q).         (15)

Here b=Q(-n)=-Qk and z/Q=Q^(-k). Including a^k in(13) yields

T_k(Q^(-1))/T_k(1)=(1+3Q^(-k))/(1-Q^(-k)),
Z_k=T_k(1)(1+Q^(-k))/(1-Q^(-k)), 1<=k<=m-1.       (16)

There is no division at k=0: T’_0=0, so its contribution in(12) is zero. At k=m, Z_m=0 directly. For the remaining indices (1+Q(-k))/(1-Q(-k))=-i cot(k theta). Also Q(-k2) T_k(1)2=(-1)m sin^2(k theta)b_k^2. Consequently

z^T mathcal J t=-(-1)^m q^(-1)
               sum_(k=1)^(m-1) sin(k theta)cos(k theta)b_k^2.

Multiplying by -q3 gives(8), since (-1)^m q4=q5 (their exponent difference is 2N(m-1)). This proves the actual coherent positive scalar with the paired normal retained throughout.

4. A bounded actual Jordan preimage, and its nonzero Ward current

At the lowest degree T=m, let v(z) be the actual Jacobi continuant vector with first entry1, so v(c)=h. Its block characteristic polynomial has a DOUBLE zero at c. Differentiating the finite continuant equations therefore gives (Omega-c)v’(c)=h, including the last row. Put

n=v'(c)/L_N'(c),
L_N'(c)=-N/[2sin^2(theta)].                        (17)

Then, as ACTUAL physical vectors,

L_N n=h,
h^dagger P h=0,
h^dagger P n=1/h0.                                (18)

The last identity follows from L_N P=h0 h h^dagger in this degree. This is a genuine Jordan preimage rather than division by the zero value L_N(c). For every polynomial B, its complete local action is

B(Omega)h=B(c)h,
B(Omega)n=B(c)n+[B'(c)/L_N'(c)]h.                  (19)

Thus its first normal is explicitly retained. These two vectors are an analysis frame for this lowest paired block, NOT a new source coupon or a claimed frame for the entire physical projective module.

There is no exponential EXTRA cost in this normal. Write v’_a(c)=h_a H_a, H_0=0. The symmetric Jacobi equation gives its exact discrete current

H_(a+1)-H_a=(sum_(r=0)^a h_r^2)/(t_a h_a h_(a+1)),
t_a=Q^[-m+a](1-Q^(m-a))(1-Q^(a+1)).                (20)

Finite Vandermonde gives sum_(r=0)^m h_r^2=[2m choose m]_Q=0. The magnitudes beta(m,a) increase to their midpoint and then decrease. Use the forward partial sum before that midpoint and its NEGATIVE backward tail afterward. The numerator is then at most N times the smaller appropriate adjacent magnitude squared, yielding

|H_(a+1)-H_a|<=N/|t_a|<=N^2/8,
max_a |H_a|<=N^3/16,
|n_a|<=(pi^2/8)|h_a|.                             (21)

Here |t_a|=4cos(a theta)sin((a+1)theta)>=4sin(theta)>=8/N, and |L_N’(c)|>=N3/(2pi2). The estimates hold also at the endpoints. The factorial/exponential profile already in h is kept; no claim that h itself is polynomially bounded in the physical metric is made.

The source normal is NOT highest. Its exact omitted-if-one-dropped-it Ward current is

Ecal_pair n=[x/(2L_N'(c))] h_previous,
(h_previous)_a=q^(a^2)[m-1 choose a]_Q.             (22)

Indeed Ecal_pair n is the unique c-eigenvector in degree m-1, and its first component follows from n_0=0 and n_1=1/(t_0 L_N’(c)). That vector is a genuine extension direction, not to be set to zero to imitate the odd simple source. The marked vertex likewise remains the exact distinct Vm of the branching theorem; no zero-current covector is substituted for it.

5. Common scalar asymptotic, and its limits

With j0=v8/pi from the odd central proof, the EVEN positive sum in(8) satisfies

S_N=(1/4)N^(-3/2)exp(N j0)(1+O(N^(-1))).           (23)

Here s_m^2=2N exactly, and there is no odd affine shift in m/N=1/2. The local fourth-power density is N^(-2)exp[Nj0-4pi u^2] for k=m/2+sqrt(N)u. Its extra sin(k theta)cos(k theta) tends to1/2. The Gaussian integral is1/2, giving again1/4. Symmetry removes odd terms, and the strict sine-factorial profile gives the same global gap and O(1/N) Laplace error as in the odd proof.

The actual CD highest coefficient in(10) consequently has magnitude

(sqrt(2)/(2pi)) N^4 exp(Nj0)(1+O(N^(-1))),         (24)

with its exact phase q6. Its DIFFERENT polynomial power from the odd coefficient N^3/4 is the retained paired-normal CD normalization, not a new exponential. Equations(8),(23) together with the odd theorem give a nonzero central scalar at BOTH level parities. They do not produce a uniform analytic packet for shifted joint colors, a full projective frame, a tame marked-vertex norm, or a global Casson–Rivin maximum. Those remain additional tasks.
