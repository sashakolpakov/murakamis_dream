A joint physical Gaussian packet for a fixed crossing fan

24 November 2026. Research proof. Earlier finals and manuscripts are unchanged.

A fixed positive fan has one transfer path at each nonzero matrix entry. Its whole physical Hilbert–Schmidt norm and joint Gaussian packet can therefore be calculated before any closing. The result includes a nonzero coefficient between independently specified product Gaussian boundary vectors. It does not estimate the prescribed knot closing. The same bipyramid volume already occurs in the Fourier bridge coordinates of [B]; the new assertions concern the original physical fan matrix, its joint norm, and its prepared physical boundary vectors.
