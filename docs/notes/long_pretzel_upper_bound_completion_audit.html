Completion audit of the original-source upper-bound milestone

Historical intermediate audit. The user correctly clarified that the objective is full hyperbolic-volume equality. That equality is now proved for the same class by the subsequent geometric completion and recorded in the full equality audit. The upper-bound audit below is retained as a proof dependency, not as a substitute for the equality objective.

29 October 2026. The milestone was to prove geometric survival OR a sharp hyperbolic-volume bound for the full original source across a broad class. The assembled theorem meets the upper-bound alternative. Full volume-conjecture equality is not part of this completion claim.

For every fixed odd s>=25 there exists p_s such that, for independently chosen fixed signed integers |p_i|>=p_s, the original pretzel diagram with boxes R^(-(2p_i+1)) satisfies, if its knot complement is hyperbolic,

lim_N (2pi/N)log|<K>_N|=Vol(rho_p)<=Vol(S^3 minus K).

The limit uses the original normalized Kashaev invariant. Both level parities and composite levels are included. The width is unbounded over the class but fixed before N tends to infinity. The slopes are also fixed before that limit; their signs and relative magnitudes are independent. There is no claim of a uniform simultaneous width/level limit, of short-slope coverage, or of all-knot coverage.

Final calibration issues and their resolution

The literal cap traversal is right to left. Generic noncommuting physical boxes were checked to prevent commutativity of the special Casimir source from hiding a reversed order. The complete holomorphic reversal is orientation preserving; it jointly inverts the auxiliary meridian and longitude. The corresponding filling slope is inverted as a whole, consistently with the retained pair of signed source charges.

The representation lift is constructive. It verifies the actual unrotated threaded half-twist group word, the selected peripheral eigenflag and the caps. It does not infer a canonical state graph from nodal R eigenphases or use the trace identity symmetric under U inversion to choose an orientation. No additional unproved U/Ward calibration enters the theorem.

The regulator chain is the developed fundamental triangulation of the actual link complement. Its boundary pairing works for the selected semisimple auxiliary meridians. The separate unipotent five-dilogarithm coefficient identity is not being extended without proof. The spin terms cancel only in the full paired regulator, and the complete anchor fixes the absolute additive constant.

What remains beyond this milestone

The upper inequality does not prove Vol(rho_p)=Vol(K). A positive geometric filling/completion argument, or another equality proof, is still needed for the full volume conjecture for this class. General hyperbolic or alternating knots remain outside the stated theorem.

The user’s Cauchy observation is exact: |bv|2<=(bG(-1)b*)(v*Gv). For the complete augmented base, the explicit weighted Fourier metric has polynomial dual cost, a separately proved sharp geometric energy and polynomially small normalized overlap. The filled-knot theorem above instead uses the whole-source asymptotic and its representation regulator. It does not assert a sharp metric-energy theorem for arbitrary source words.
