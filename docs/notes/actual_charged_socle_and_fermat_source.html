The actual charged source has the same residue on Fermat and nilpotent fibers

28 October 2026. The whole-source coefficient proved in the charged bridge-flow theorem has a canonical finite-flat algebraic realization. At each fixed level, its cyclotomic-grid residue and its nilpotent jet evaluation are exactly the SAME functional, with no unknown scalar or chosen asymptotic branch. The pairing remains nondegenerate when all Fermat centers vanish.

This makes a part of the proposed algebraic-period/jet viewpoint literal. It does not identify residues at different levels, select the geometric regulator, or prove a volume limit. The algebra and the state class both depend on N.

1. A fixed-level finite-flat Frobenius algebra

Fix N>=2, d=N-1, the actual primitive Q and the coefficient field K containing Q, the original half-root and source scalars. For k anchors put

R=K[C_1,...,C_k],
A_N=R[X_1,...,X_k]/(X_i^N-C_i, all i).          (1)

Its basis over R is X^a with 0<=a_i<=d. Reduction by the monic relations is unique, so A_N is free of rank N^k; in particular it is flat even when some or all C_i vanish. Define

lambda_C(P)=[X_1^d ... X_k^d] (P reduced in (1)). (2)

For two basis monomials the EXACT multiplication pairing is

lambda_C(X^a X^b)=product_i delta_(a_i+b_i,d).    (3)

Indeed a_i+b_i<=2d. If reduction by X_i^N occurs, its remaining exponent is at most d-1, so it cannot contribute to (2). Thus the Gram matrix is an anti-diagonal permutation matrix, INDEPENDENT of every C_i, with determinant a unit. This proves

A_N -> Hom_R(A_N,R), f -> (g -> lambda_C(fg))    (4)

is an isomorphism of A_N-modules on every fiber. This is a specified Frobenius functional, not an unspecified nonzero linear form. Generators of this dual module form a torsor under A_N^units; the original source fixes the generator (2).

2. The selected Fermat residue extends to the actual nilpotent jet

When every C_i is nonzero, the roots are simple. The Jacobian of f_i=X_i^N-C_i is N^k product_i X_i^d. Direct evaluation gives

lambda_C(P)
  =Res_(all f_i=0) P(X) dX_1...dX_k/(f_1...f_k)
  =1/N^k sum_(X_i^N=C_i) P(X)/product_i X_i^d.   (5)

The residue means the sum of normalized local product-circle integrals. It agrees with (2) on every basis monomial, hence on all polynomials modulo (1). The coefficient and the Jacobian fix its sign and normalization.

At C=0 the algebra is

A_N(0)=K[X_1,...,X_k]/(X_1^N,...,X_k^N).       (6)

Its socle, the elements annihilated by all X_i, is exactly the one-dimensional line generated by Omega=product_i X_i^d. The same lambda is its top-coefficient functional, lambda_0(Omega)=1, or equivalently the local residue P dX/product_i X_i^N at zero. Formula (3) proves that the source pairing has NOT degenerated, although the fiber has become a nonreduced jet algebra.

For a homogeneous polynomial P of total degree kd there is the stronger exact identity

lambda_C(P)=[X^d]P=lambda_0(P), for EVERY C.     (7)

Any use of one monic relation reduces total X-degree by N; such a term cannot reach the top monomial of total degree kd. This is the algebraic version of the whole-source alias argument, and includes polynomials with large degrees in individual anchors.

The original invariant therefore has the identical descriptions

K_N=chi_N/N sum_(h over-monotone) W_h lambda_C(P_h)
   =chi_N/N sum_(h over-monotone) W_h lambda_0(P_h). (8)

All clock weights, closure adapters and pivots are those already proved in the charged-flow theorem. No raw full-Psi continuation is substituted for this polynomial. Equation (8) is an equality of algebraic periods at each level, rather than an inference from a limit or an arbitrary choice of a quantum source.

3. A general cyclotomic annihilation rule for the actual source

Let i!=j and alpha,beta be nonzero. In the nilpotent fiber,

product_(r=0)^(N-1)(alpha X_i-Q^r beta X_j)
      =alpha^N X_i^N-beta^N X_j^N=0.            (9)

Consequently, if the actual event polynomial P_h contains a complete Q-orbit of linear factors on a pair of anchors, its entire selected coefficient is ZERO. This is an exact rule on the actual class, not an absolute-value estimate or a generic quotient killing a geometric full-function section. By (7) the same selected value vanishes on EVERY original regular Fermat grid.

At nonzero centers the product in (9) equals the scalar alpha^N C_i-beta^N C_j rather than zero. There is no contradiction: its remaining polynomial has degree kd-N, and lambda_C kills that lower degree. Thus applying (9) to the selected period is justified by the whole homogeneous source, not by declaring that scalar zero in an individual grid summand.

For the actual event factors, coefficients are explicit half-root phases. Group factors by their unordered anchor pair and their ratio modulo Q. Covering all N phases of one Q-orbit detects (9) before coefficient expansion. This is a sufficient vanishing test; failure of this test is NOT a nonvanishing theorem. More complicated cycle cancellations and the remaining h sum are still present.

The recently proved two-crossing winding selection in the bridge-phase theorem uses the same full-cycle polynomial 1-t^N in its one-variable coefficient calculation. The multianchor rule (9) does not require that local bigon presentation, but it also does not exhaust the source kernel.

4. What rigidity does and does not establish

The source line is fixed across the finite-flat family (1), and the full original polynomial classes have center-independent periods. This resolves the ambiguity of transporting that source from regular cyclotomic points to its nilpotent jet fiber. It does NOT imply that the original class has a nonzero projection to a geometric period. The perfect pairing (3) allows many nonzero elements with lambda=0; (9) gives source-relevant exact cancellations.

Also N^k, the socle degree k(N-1), the clock domain, and P_h all vary with N. Fixed-level flatness or the source torsor alone therefore supplies no cross-level growth estimate. A comparison with the marked Bloch regulator and a nonzero selected geometric coefficient are still required for the volume conjecture.
