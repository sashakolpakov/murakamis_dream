The odd-level half-charge observation and its normal defect

Research proof, 4 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

The ordinary value interpolation at odd levels has a simple physical endpoint in addition to its double roots. Its angular Cauchy kernel is a sine kernel. The ramified angular pole at zero supplies exactly the original simple-node contribution. With this term retained, the same half-charge saddle as at even levels gives a nonzero observation and a locally dominant normal defect at every positive internal pair.

This note uses the full distinct-node interpolation, not the different weighted odd space on which a positive crossing form was proved. The original trace’s top-degree correction is retained in Section 3.

1. The full-node polynomial and exact angular contour

2. The half-charge packet and the exterior residue

Use the notation of the even-level observation theorem: nt0+2iasinh⁡tan⁡(t0/2)=π,t0=a0−iH0,0<a0<π/3,0<H0<2/5,Sn(z)=Ψ(z)+inz2/(4π)−iz/2,𝒮n=Sn(t0),Mn=ℜ𝒮n,An={sec⁡(t0/2)−in}/(2π).(9) \begin{split} nt_0+2i\operatorname{asinh}\tan(t_0/2)&=\pi,\qquad t_0=a_0-iH_0,\quad 0<a_0<\pi/3,\quad 0<H_0<2/5,\\ S_n(z)&=\Psi(z)+inz^2/(4\pi)-iz/2,\qquad \mathcal S_n=S_n(t_0),\quad M_n=\Re\mathcal S_n,\\ A_n&=\{\sec(t_0/2)-in\}/(2\pi). \end{split} \qquad\text{(9)} Here n≥3n\ge3 is fixed and real, and the square root of AnA_n has positive real part. Theorem 1. Proof. The angular integrand in (8) is odd, so its two horizontal sides add after reflection. On the lower side, 1sin⁡(Nz/2)=2ie−iNz/21−e−iNz. \frac1{\sin(Nz/2)} =\frac{2i\,e^{-iNz/2}}{1-e^{-iNz}} . Consequently the horizontal and vertical upper exponents are exactly the ones already proved in the even-level theorem.

For precision, ℜSn(a−iH0)\Re S_n(a-iH_0) is strictly concave for |a|≤π/2|a|\le\pi/2, with unique maximum at a0a_0. The lower right connector increases in height and is bounded by its smaller horizontal endpoint value. The lower left connector decreases in height and is bounded by Ψ(TN)\Psi(T_N). The upper sides are their reflected copies. All physical omitted values are at most the first omitted complete-kernel value by the actual positive row recurrence. Their rate is Ψ(π/2)\Psi(\pi/2), and Ψ(π/2)<Mn−7/80.(13) \Psi(\pi/2)<M_n-7/80. \qquad\text{(13)} Their weights are bounded by (4), and (w−ca)−1≤N2/(w+1)(w-c_a)^{-1}\le N^2/(w+1). At t0t_0, the original complete-edge packet is ℒN(−2cos⁡z)=N−3/2ae(z,0)eNΨ(z)(1+O(N−1))\mathcal L_N(-2\cos z)=N^{-3/2}a_e(z,0)e^{N\Psi(z)} (1+O(N^{-1})). Let 𝒥n,No(w)\mathcal J^{\mathrm o}_{n,N}(w) denote (8), and 𝒯n,No(w)\mathcal T^{\mathrm o}_{n,N}(w) the omitted physical sum. Corollary 2. At an actual unit-charge positive internal pair of integer powers n,p≥3n,p\ge3, on a fixed neighborhood of the angle tt at its power-nn vertex, one has 𝒟n,No(−2cos⁡z)=hn,N(−2cos⁡z)Qo(−2cos⁡z)(1+O(N−1/2e−δN))=i(−1)mN−3/2ae(z,0)2sin⁡(z/2)eNSn(z)(1+O(N−1))≠0(15) \begin{split} \mathscr D^{\mathrm o}_{n,N}(-2\cos z) &=\frac{h_{n,N}(-2\cos z)}{Q_{\mathrm o}(-2\cos z)} (1+O(N^{-1/2}e^{-\delta N}))\\ &=i(-1)^mN^{-3/2} \frac{a_e(z,0)}{2\sin(z/2)}e^{NS_n(z)} (1+O(N^{-1}))\ne0 \end{split} \qquad\text{(15)} for some δ>0\delta>0, at all large odd levels. Proof. The pair comparison theorem gives H(t)>H0H(t)>H_0 and ℜSn(t)>Mn\Re S_n(t)>M_n. The color is therefore exterior to the rectangle. The contour proof of (11) applies uniformly on a fixed neighborhood of this color, because its Cauchy denominators stay away from zero on the contour and at the physical nodes. It gives ℐn,No/Qo=O(N−2eNMn)\mathcal I^{\mathrm o}_{n,N}/Q_{\mathrm o} =O(N^{-2}e^{NM_n}). 3. The original odd trace still has its top-degree term

The odd full-node normal defect must not be substituted for a weighted positive trace. Let Qj,N(w)=∏a=jm(w−ca),Sj,N=∏r=1j2sin⁡(rπ/N),ℓn,N=[wm]ℐn,No(w). For any contour in the slit plane enclosing the remaining nodes, the exact original trace is ϕN[RnℒNej]=CN,nN2(−1)j2πiSj,N2∮(w+2)𝒟n,No(w)Qj,N(w)dw+𝟏{j=m}CN,nN2ℓn,N(−1)mSm,N2,0≤j≤m.(17) \begin{split} \phi_N[R^n\mathcal L_Ne_j] ={}&\frac{C_{N,n}N^2(-1)^j}{2\pi i S_{j,N}^2} \oint\frac{(w+2)\mathscr D^{\mathrm o}_{n,N}(w)} {Q_{j,N}(w)}\,dw\\ &\quad+\boldsymbol 1_{\{j=m\}}\, C_{N,n}N^2\ell_{n,N}\frac{(-1)^m}{S_{m,N}^2}, \qquad 0\le j\le m . \end{split} \qquad\text{(17)} Indeed the original normal-defect formula gives ∑a<m(4−ca2)Qo′(ca)𝒟n,No(ca)ej(ca)\sum_{a<m}(4-c_a^2)Q_{\mathrm o}'(c_a) \mathscr D^{\mathrm o}_{n,N}(c_a)e_j(c_a), plus the product of the two leading coefficients times N2N^2. For j=mj=m the contour integrand is holomorphic, so this entire term is zero, not an approximation. Since Sm,N2=NS_{m,N}^2=N, ϕN[RnℒNem]=CN,n(−1)mNℓn,N,Nℓn,N=limw→+∞w𝒪noF(w).(18) \phi_N[R^n\mathcal L_Ne_m] =C_{N,n}(-1)^mN\,\ell_{n,N},\qquad N\ell_{n,N}=\lim_{w\to+\infty}w\mathcal O^{\mathrm o}_nF(w). \qquad\text{(18)} The uniform observation theorem gives its explicit nonzero half-charge asymptotic ϕN[RnℒNem]=CN,nN−1bn,∞oeN𝒮n(1+O(N−1)),bn,∞o=2ππcos⁡(t0/2)ae(t0,0)An≠0.(19) \phi_N[R^n\mathcal L_Ne_m] =C_{N,n}N^{-1}b^{\mathrm o}_{n,\infty} e^{N\mathcal S_n}(1+O(N^{-1})), \quad b^{\mathrm o}_{n,\infty} =\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}{\pi} \frac{\cos(t_0/2)a_e(t_0,0)}{\sqrt{A_n}}\ne0 . \qquad\text{(19)} The limit in (18) can be taken directly in the bounded rational amplitudes and finite sums of the contour proof. In particular a two-vertex product of these top-index terms has rate Mn+Mp<2M∞M_n+M_p<2M_\infty. The strict inequality follows by integrating the positive height along each half-charge leaf from charge zero. This does not estimate the other shared indices.

The identities and local packets above retain the actual odd simple node, all first normals, and the full top-degree correction. They do not establish a global signed internal-pair asymptotic or a positive energy identity for this unweighted odd observation.

4. Independent finite normalization checks

The focused checker constructs the crossing and its signed powers by multiplication and division in the original Newton quotient. It compares those traces with (17), including the simple endpoint and the top coefficient. Separately, it integrates the sine contour and checks both exterior and interior evaluation colors with their exact residue correction.
