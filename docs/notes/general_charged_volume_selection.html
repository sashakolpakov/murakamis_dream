What capacity can and cannot select in the actual charged source

28 October 2026. The exact original charged coefficient is a useful place to ask for a general lower bound. Three commonly proposed shortcuts do not supply one: ordinary half-plane stability, Lorentzian positivity after taking magnitudes, and a subexponential comparison with positive capacity. The last failure occurs in an actual nonzero source sector, uniformly in the level, after its already proved parallel-edge fusion. This is an obstruction to these particular methods, not to a further phase-aware fusion or to the volume conjecture.

The source throughout is the specified functional in the global charged-flow theorem, with its Fermat/nilpotent residue. No scalar multiple of the invariant or replacement boundary functional is introduced.

1. The raw root blocks are not half-plane stable

Let q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, d=N-1. A genuine two-anchor block is

B_L(X,Y)=product_(j=0)^(L-1)(alpha X-Q^j beta Y),
alpha beta !=0, 2<=L<=d.                         (1)

A homogeneous linear polynomial aX+bY, with ab nonzero, has no zero with Im X>0 and Im Y>0 if and only if b/a is positive real. Indeed a zero requires X=-(b/a)Y; two open half-planes through zero intersect unless the multiplier maps the upper half-plane exactly to the lower one. A product is stable only when every nonconstant factor is stable.

After ANY nonzero diagonal changes X=cX’, Y=eY’, successive factor ratios in (1) still differ by Q. Since N>=3 here, Q is not positive real. Thus no such change makes (1), or a polynomial containing it, upper-half-plane stable. This also covers arbitrary complex diagonal changes, not just vertex phase gauges. Self-loop factors which have already become scalars or monomials are excluded from this statement.

This does not rule out a different stable object obtained by an exact fusion. It prevents applying stable-polynomial coefficient inequalities directly to the original charged product.

2. Even the positive sine block is not Lorentzian

The exact grouped expansion has positive magnitudes

C_(L,k)=|[L choose k]_Q|
       =product_(j=1)^k sin(pi*(L-k+j)/N)/sin(pi*j/N).

Put a_k=C_(L,k)/binom(L,k). Its successive ratios are

a_k/a_(k-1)
  =sinc((L-k+1)*pi/N)/sinc(k*pi/N),              (2)
sinc(t)=sin(t)/t.

On (0,pi), sinc is strictly decreasing: its derivative has numerator t cos t-sin t, whose derivative is -t sin t<0. As k increases in (2), the numerator increases and the denominator decreases. Hence a_k is strictly log-CONVEX at every interior index when L>=2.

For a binary homogeneous polynomial with positive coefficients, the Lorentzian coefficient condition is log-concavity after division by binom(L,k). Thus the positive-sine block fails this condition. Already L=2 gives X^2+2 cos(pi/N)XY+Y^2: a nonpositive Hessian determinant would require the middle coefficient to be at least 2; here the determinant is positive. Ordinary positive coefficients have not restored the needed real-rooted or Lorentzian structure.

3. Logarithmic torus capacity forgets these root phases entirely

For an actual product P_h of unit-modulus binary linear factors on DISTINCT anchor pairs, its Ronkin average is

R_h(rho)=average_theta log|P_h(exp(rho+i theta))|
         =sum_factors max(rho_tail,rho_head).    (3)

To prove (3), integrate the relative angle of one linear factor. The mean of log|A-Be^(it)| is log max(|A|,|B|); for unequal moduli this follows by expanding log(1-z), and the equal-modulus value follows by an integrable limit. Linearity of the average then gives (3), irrespective of dependencies between the factor angles.

If the charge d at every anchor is feasible, choose an endpoint for each factor giving that total charge. Each maximum in (3) dominates the chosen endpoint, so

R_h(rho)-d sum_a rho_a >=0.

At rho=0 equality holds. Consequently the logarithmic capacity

exp inf_rho [R_h(rho)-d sum_a rho_a]

is EXACTLY ONE in every feasible sector of this type. If self-loop factors occur, first evaluate them as an explicit scalar times a monomial. The same assertion holds after division by that scalar and subtraction of the monomial’s charges; the scalar itself may carry an exponential action and is not omitted from the period. Nonzero selected coefficients in particular have feasible charge. This average is therefore not the exponential volume scale of the original source. It should not be confused with the supremum-over-angles Cauchy bound, or with a capacity which retains further phase information.

4. Positive capacity can exceed a surviving actual period exponentially

Use the already proved uniform original-source family in the anchor-phase and exact-fusion theorem:

word=[1,-2,1,1,-2,-2], N=2r+1,
h=(0,r,r,r,0,r).

Its selected coefficient, INCLUDING every original closure block, is

c_N=[X_0^d ... X_8^d]P_h=N q^(2-r^2).           (4)

The same theorem fuses its two parallel r-blocks into the complete d-block with every coefficient equal to one. Work AFTER this exact fusion. In the remaining flow description choose

a=c=f=0, b=floor(r/2).

These are feasible original source charges. The only nontrivial magnitude in this term is

M_N=|[r choose floor(r/2)]_Q|.                   (5)

Let P_h^+ mean the polynomial obtained from these fused grouped blocks by replacing their coefficient phases by one. It has positive coefficients. Its positive capacity is

Cap_d(P_h^+)=inf_(x_a>0) P_h^+(x)/product_a x_a^d.

The feasible term (5) contributes M_N product x_a^d. All other terms are nonnegative. Therefore, on EVERY radius,

Cap_d(P_h^+) >= M_N.                            (6)

Put J(t)=integral_0^t log(2 sin(pi*s))ds. Elementary endpoint splitting, followed by Riemann summation, gives uniformly for 0<=j<N

log |(Q;Q)_j|=N J(j/N)+O(log N).

Here r/N tends to 1/2 and b/N,(r-b)/N tend to 1/4. Since J(1/2)=0 and J(1/4)=-G/(2*pi), where G is Catalan’s constant,

log M_N=(G/pi)N+O(log N).                       (7)

Combining (4)–(7) proves the genuine actual-source gap

limsup_(odd N) (1/N)log[|c_N|/Cap_d(P_h^+)]
                                  <=-G/pi<0.   (8)

This family is a nonzero sector of a reduced alternating hyperbolic knot. Its phase curvature and feasibility were proved uniformly, not inferred from an isolated numerical level. For this length-one bridge diagram, all the original W_h factors have unit modulus, so an omitted clock normalizer is not causing (8).

Equation (8) rules out a universal subexponential lower comparison between a surviving charged source coefficient and its positive capacity. It does NOT say this particular sector is a leading volume sector: its coefficient is polynomial in N, and its exponential loss is already explained by exact root correlations. It also does not exclude phase-aware capacities after further exact fusions. In particular the new source-exact full-length reproducing contractions and balanced star–triangle moves can change the positive majorant drastically. Equation (8) is not asserted to survive arbitrary such reorganizations.

5. The remaining possibility is genuinely phase-aware

The finite-flat perfect pairing fixes the source functional; it does not force that functional to be large on a specified state. The calculation above gives a quantitative instance using the actual state and the actual source. A useful general theorem would have to retain the root-phase correlations, for example through exact local fusions followed by a source-compatible geometric contour. Taking magnitudes and invoking stability cannot replace that step.

The new defect-jet theorem makes this connection precise: on the unit-radius original anchor torus the source phase frequency is exactly (S_a-2d)/2, where S_a is incident factor degree. Balanced fusion removes a zero-frequency direction, while its bounded-defect extension retains a finite Fourier correction with polynomial cost. Macroscopic defects and the final global coefficient are still present. The audit here independently checks this phase identity on the actual unfused source graphs.

Ordinary dominant-saddle Stokes persistence is consistent with this: in a proper deformation with a strictly highest-action saddle, its top action-graded coefficient is preserved against jumps involving only lower saddles. To use this observation here one still needs a NONZERO original-source coefficient in one chamber and control of singular/boundary competitors throughout the deformation. The positive constrained-lens reference chain supplies neither original cycle comparison by itself. Representation-volume rigidity at complete boundary-parabolic critical points also does not control an entire parameter path or its nonstationary boundaries.

No general geometric lower bound or new knot class follows from this audit. Its constructive consequence is a restriction on methods: the exact phase-aware graph fusions address information which each of the three phase-blind shortcuts above loses.

Audit: actual family metadata/source charges, exact small-level coefficients, the feasible positive term on arbitrary radii, the sine normalization inequalities, and the asymptotic rate in (7).
