The global Green cut tails and the exact wall-transport residual

Research proof and independent source audit, 27 October 2026. Integer-flow conservation excludes a direct bare wall at the final cut before summing the two framing variables. It does not discard internal returning wall excursions or the boundary tails produced by Green shifts. This note records those tails, the exact residual after the new memory contraction, and the source factor a common wall comparison would have to produce.

These are actual source/current tests, not a geometric-period identification or a use of scalarity of the closed knot operator.

1. Primitive flow elimination preserves the unsummed cut grading

Each original positive crossing sends (a,b) to (b+k,a-k), and each negative crossing sends it to (b-k,a+k). Thus every literal crossing term preserves the integer sum of positions, for each transfer separately. All original diagonal pivots, Cartan phases, zero-framing scalars and scalar moment insertions have degree zero.

Close every strand except the marked cut. Equality of input and output positions on the closed strands forces equality at the cut by the same integer sum. This is TERMWISE: it uses neither an internal state sum nor a Schur argument. For a knot, primitive flow elimination uses the unit permutation cofactor already proved in the actual geometric coefficient model. Keep a=cut position and b=one other framing flow unsummed, and denote all remaining free flows by t. The actual kernel therefore has the literal form I(a,b,t)Eaa. I(a,b,t)E_{aa}. \tag{1} Scalar Green moments preserve this diagonal matrix index. The external meridian Ore shifts also preserve it. Hence the direct final-cut image under degree elevation is diagonal and has zero coefficient of the EXTRA root wall E_(N,0). The same statement holds after the full Clebsch word, with all caps and lower excursions retained: its upper final cut is E_N of this original diagonal matrix. It is not a claim about an upper-only word.

The proof survives exact transverse SUMMATION while a,b remain unsummed. A formal Gaussian comparison must still preserve its external shifts, support boundaries and relative source; a bare interior Gaussian sector does not include those boundaries.

There is a useful stronger actual charge rule. Set deg F=+1, deg E=-1 and deg K=0. The coproduct is homogeneous in total degree and the antipode preserves degree. Thus the TRUE Hopf adjoint of a homogeneous h of degree d, applied to a degree-zero word or scalar Green kernel, has degree d. Diagonal pivotal partial trace preserves the remaining cut degree, coefficientwise in EVERY actual q-normal order. This applies to divided powers and their mixed coproduct terms as well.

On the actual high color r=N the fixed-head Fr wall E_(N,0) changes the physical cut position J=NR+S by N^2. Therefore its selected head-00 coefficient is absent for |d|<N^2. In particular a SINGLE divided E^(N) or F^(N) current cannot create that direct cut coefficient. The full wall head block has charges N^2+(S_out-S_in), so is absent whenever |d|<N^2-N+1. Dense source coupons inserted INSIDE a word change this charge budget; an external linear co-source does not. Under a whole source gauge the grading and every endpoint must be conjugated together rather than freezing the old grading on new matrices.

2. The literal cut-window map has two missing boundary rows

Green currents are zero-extended arrays on ALL integer free flows. A cut-index shift can therefore create tails outside 0,…,N-1. For any such finite array retain the matrix and two exterior tails CNf(b,t)=∑a=0N−1f(a,b,t)Eaa,f<0,f≥N. C_Nf(b,t)=\sum_{a=0}^{N-1}f(a,b,t)E_{aa},\quad f_{<0},\quad f_{\ge N}. \tag{2} Put U=sum_(a=0)^(N-2)E_(a+1,a), and use S_+f(a)=f(a+1), S_-f(a)=f(a-1). Exactly, with b,t still UNSUMMED, CN(S+f)=U*CN(f)U+f(N,b,t)EN−1,N−1,CN(S−f)=UCN(f)U*+f(−1,b,t)E00. \begin{aligned} C_N(S_+f)&=U^*C_N(f)U+f(N,b,t)E_{N-1,N-1},\\ C_N(S_-f)&=UC_N(f)U^*+f(-1,b,t)E_{00}. \tag{3} \end{aligned} Here star denotes the transpose partial shift in this basis, not an additional analytic Hermitian choice. General powers have the corresponding finite sums of r endpoint values. In particular 1NTr⁡CN((S+−1)f)=f(N)−f(0)N. \frac1N\operatorname{Tr}C_N((S_+-1)f) =\frac{f(N)-f(0)}N. \tag{4} Thus compression to the finite head is NOT a translation chain map unless its endpoint rows are included.

The correct original relative cut source is ΦNrel(f)=1N[TrCNf+∑a<0f(a)+∑a≥Nf(a)]=1N∑a∈ℤf(a). \Phi_N^{\mathrm{rel}}(f)=\frac1N\left[ \operatorname{Tr}C_Nf+\sum_{a<0}f(a)+\sum_{a\ge N}f(a)\right] =\frac1N\sum_{a\in\mathbb Z}f(a). \tag{5} It annihilates the complete finite difference exactly, with b,t still unsummed. On the original kernel, whose support is in the head window, it is the original normalized-average cut. For a Green current the two tail sums are part of the source. Replacing this by another high-color co-source because closed scalar periods agree would not establish equality of these inserted rows.

Equations (2)–(5) are the explicit cut part of the source for the full finite Green certificate. They explain how a relative wall can arise even though (1) has no bare off-diagonal final-cut wall coefficient. The two statements are compatible; the wall would have to be a TRANSGRESSION of these boundary rows, not an unproved final-matrix projection.

3. Bare wall trace zero does not remove returning wall excursions

The original root pivotal operator is diagonal in the head and identity on the Fr block. Its trace on w=E_(N,0) is zero. But the original divided Fr matrices satisfy eNw=N!E00,weN=N!ENN,Tr⁡Fr(eNw)=N!. e^N w=N!E_{00},\qquad w e^N=N!E_{NN}, \quad\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathrm{Fr}}(e^Nw)=N!. \tag{6} With a literal head insertion H and pivot g_head the corresponding closed value is N! Tr(g_head H). A paired wall can therefore return to the degree-zero channel and survive closure. Termwise charge conservation does not set every internal wall excursion to zero. The strict Clebsch construction supplies actual inverse- crossing examples of exactly such retained excursions.

For true pivotal/Hopf closure one must use the actual ordinary generic pivot and its mixed divided coproduct. The current-level partial-trace theorem and the frozen-pivot correction are retained in the corresponding closure construction. No replacement of that pivotal trace by an unweighted Fr trace is made here.

4. Exact residual after the new cochain contraction

Let (C,d) be the specified memory current complex, with the actual contraction (p,i,h) onto the degree-elevation image plus its retained wall complex: I−ip=dh+hd,pd=dredp,di=idred. I-ip=dh+hd,\quad pd=d_{\mathrm{red}}p, di=id_{\mathrm{red}}. \tag{7} The explicit wall part is the already audited Bockstein contraction. Let O_1,O_2 be consecutive original transported maps (crossings, caps, projectors or source maps), and retain their actual current defects Δj=dOj−Ojd. \Delta_j=dO_j-O_jd. \tag{8} For maps between different coefficient complexes the adjacent differentials and contractions are used; external base shifts also act on their coefficients. Nothing requires a frozen same-base convention.

There is the exact operator identity pO2O1i−(pO2i)(pO1i)=dredH+Hdred−pΔ2hO1i+pO2hΔ1i,H=pO2hO1i. \begin{aligned} pO_2O_1i-(pO_2i)(pO_1i) &=d_{\mathrm{red}}H+Hd_{\mathrm{red}} -p\Delta_2hO_1i+pO_2h\Delta_1i,\\ H&=pO_2hO_1i . \tag{9} \end{aligned} Proof: insert (7) between O_2 and O_1, move d past each O_j using (8), and use the two chain identities in (7). This proves (9) before any summation, Gaussian expansion or scalar source. Iterating gives a finite ordered excursion/current formula for the entire weighted word.

For the ACTUAL relative source/ket Lambda,Psi, its scalar residual is correspondingly (Λdred)HΨ+ΛH(dredΨ)−ΛpΔ2hO1iΨ+ΛpO2hΔ1iΨ. \begin{aligned} &(\Lambda d_{\mathrm{red}})H\Psi+\Lambda H(d_{\mathrm{red}}\Psi)\\ &\quad-\Lambda p\Delta_2hO_1i\Psi +\Lambda pO_2h\Delta_1i\Psi . \tag{10} \end{aligned} The first two terms are the specified endpoint/carry co-sources, not automatically zero. The other two are the inserted actual intertwining currents. True pivotal naturality can remove them for the TRUE Hopf complex with its caps; it does not identify that complex with the MEMORY Cartan/F complex without the mixed head and source comparison. The known Jackson off-diagonal remainder is one explicit such inserted current.

Consequently local contraction to one wall line does not imply that full words factor through that line. Equation (9), with all original operators, is the finite calculation that decides it. It is also more specific than an unspecified appeal to a regular sequence at an isolated gluing point.

5. The exact co-source normalization required of a tail-to-wall map

Take the orientation of the positive boundary (4) and the normalized selected coupled wall w tensor E_00. Its ACTUAL source value is ΦeD(w⊗E00)=2−N. \Phi_{e_D}(w\otimes E_{00})=2^{-N}. If a proposed relative comparison maps the scalar tail t=f(N)-f(0) to c_N(t)(w tensor E_00), preservation of the original co-source therefore FORCES cN(t)=2NNt. c_N(t)=\frac{2^N}{N}t. \tag{11} In the divided generator f^N coordinate it forces instead 2^N t/(N N!). The uniform Fr interval would require (N+1)t/N in the w coordinate. Their ratio is exactly the already measured 2^N/(N+1). This is a necessary coefficient equation, not permission to choose a new normalization of the invariant. A genuine map must derive (11) from its retained source character, or prove that the WHOLE relevant tail class cancels. A bare fixed algebraic unit at the Rivin point cannot account for the exponential factor; the actual weight-N source line is essential.

The marked moment data are essential too. A coupling w^a changes the norm of an ACTUAL translation operator T to w^N T^N. Its finite moment derivatives retain P(a+N)-P(a), the chirp phase, and every memory carry. This does NOT automatically replace the Fr adjoint wall character Q^-N-1 by Q^-N w^N-1: one-sided clock multiplication simply scales the endpoint matrix, leaving its Ad K eigencharacter unchanged. Identifying those translation and memory clutches is another part of the required current map, with its Fourier/relative adapter. It must be checked, not inferred from their equal root numerical values.

The current next test is therefore explicit: transport the two unsummed actual Green currents through (2)–(10), including their true pivotal caps, and evaluate their relative wall/tail pairing at the marked Rivin branch. Its regular sequence and algebraicity can control that evaluation once the map is supplied. They do not determine the inserted Delta_j terms, identify the two clutches, or supply the source factor (11). No common selected global period line follows from the Bockstein character alone.

6. Independent audit: the actual divided Cartan supplies a whole wall row

The subsequent actual divided Ward/cap construction provides a positive addition to this residual test. It passes the following independent audit with all sources retained.

In the fixed actual beta basis the original matrices E(N),F(N) preserve the residue S and move the Fr index by one. Consequently H(q)=τ[E(N)(q),F(N)(q)] H(q)=\tau[E^{(N)}(q),F^{(N)}(q)] is diagonal for EVERY q, with root entries N-2R. On the extreme Fr block its commutator is therefore entrywise multiplication by HN,s(q)−H0,t(q), H_{N,s}(q)-H_{0,t}(q), \tag{12} an original rational unit of root value -2N. Inverting these entries gives a literal WHOLE wall extraction through the actual Cartan commutator. It uses neither delta division nor a guessed source comparison. The selected row remains the original one, composed with this explicit unit inverse; its root coefficient bound1/(2N) is not a bound replacing the wall’s2^-N source.

The global current formula also checks independently. For every actual Hopf element h, [ρ(h),C]=∑(h)Dh1(C)ρ(h2). [\rho(h),C]=\sum_{(h)}D_{h_1}(C)\rho(h_2). This follows directly from the antipode identity. Ordinary pivotal partial trace intertwines these actual adjoints on ALL matrices. Applying the whole weighted crossing-current identity therefore transports (12) through an actual word, with its finite Green difference and source row. Mixed terms of Delta H are essential; this is not a primitive Fr or geometric-Borel assignment.

The ordinary pivot g=K and frozen pivot g_f=K^(1-N) are related EXACTLY by P_f=chi^-1 P_o M_(Z^J). Thus the full frozen cap defect is chi^-1 P_o[M_(Z^J),A_h]. Its first delta coefficient is -chi_0^-1 P_(o,0)[M_J,A_(h,0)]; generator and cap derivatives have not been omitted, since their commutators with M_0=I are zero. The explicit E and F cap signs, the divided-adjoint q powers and the ordered N-term telescope all check. In particular the N small-generator rows together have order at least delta^2 before division by [N]_q!, whose zero is simple. Their individual first normals cannot be substituted for a genuine divided cap.

Finally the construction contains a literal nonzero actual R return, stronger than the formal trace example (6). With m=N(N+1)-1, k=N^2 and the closed coupon E_(N,0)^Fr tensor P_head0, only transfer k contributes to the open entry k<-0. Its first nonzero coefficient is [δN]ptr⁡K[(B⊗I)R]k,0=qNm−2kNNN!ηNm(m−2k)≠0. The symmetric Gaussian is a root unit1, and constant beta phases cancel between the two legs. The frozen leading coefficient has its additional chi_0^-1. All lower orders vanish. The inserted coupon has physical degree N^2, so this returning source is fully consistent with the charge restriction in Section1.

This positive whole Cartan row controls the ACTUAL finite cut wall without changing its co-source. The remaining geometric obligation is still to push this specified mixed Hopf/current row and its relative Green endpoints through the partial geometric contraction. It has not been identified with either of the two internal geometric framing rows merely by its unit inverse.

The later whole Ward-grid theorem extends this to every actual cut matrix. Its two diagonal spectra and actual ordinary/divided F grid give a rational pole-free contraction End to its trace center, with an explicit homotopy for the original source. An independent proof audit checks the whole formula, absence of off-diagonal pollution, improved comb-path norm and two-word reduction. The root selected-source cost is O(N^(3/2)) for r=N. Its degree-one target is the actual image of the four rows, so no unspecified row-compatibility cokernel has been killed. Thus the finite cut target is now completely specified; the remaining map is the pushforward of the two internal geometric Green/source rows into this target, with (2)–(11) retained.
