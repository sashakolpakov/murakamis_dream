Complete volume added by a marked unit chain

31 October 2026. Preserved analytical consequence of the unit-chain closure theorem. No new quantum estimate is claimed.

Let ΦD=Vol⁡(YD)/(2π)\Phi_D=\operatorname{Vol}(Y_D)/(2\pi) be the absolutely normalized complete source capacity. In the marked strip normalization of that theorem, all new curvatures are one, and every old curvature is unchanged. Consequently the ordinary face-target logarithms are unchanged. Set C0=Q(0,1,1)=2G/πC_0=Q(0,1,1)=2G/\pi, with GG Catalan’s constant.

For m≥1m\geq1, the first prefix port contributes C0C_0; the other 2m−12m-1 prefix ports each contribute 2C02C_0. The changed first core port contributes an additional C0C_0. Every other core action stays fixed. Thus

ΦD(m)−ΦD=C0+(2m−1)2C0+C0=4mC0. \Phi_{D^{(m)}}-\Phi_D =C_0+(2m-1)2C_0+C_0=4mC_0.

It follows from the actual complete-source volume calibration that

Vol⁡(YD(m))−Vol⁡(YD)=16mG=4mvoct. \operatorname{Vol}(Y_{D^{(m)}})-\operatorname{Vol}(Y_D) =16mG=4m\,v_{\mathrm{oct}}.

Here voct=4Gv_{\mathrm{oct}}=4G is the regular ideal octahedron’s volume: splitting it into four ideal tetrahedra with angles (π/2,π/4,π/4)(\pi/2,\pi/4,\pi/4) gives 4[Λ(π/2)+2Λ(π/4)]=4G4[\Lambda(\pi/2)+2\Lambda(\pi/4)]=4G. The same difference is zero at m=0m=0. This proves that the added complete source regions increase the actual drilled volume, and are not a redundant presentation of a fixed augmented exterior. It does not assert additivity of the filled volumes or a filling threshold uniform in depth.
