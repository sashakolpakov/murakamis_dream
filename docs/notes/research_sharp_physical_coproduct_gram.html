Sharp physical Grams and a positive history sum for the actual coproduct

24 November 2026. Research proof. Earlier finals and manuscripts are unchanged.

The actual cyclic-source coproduct has a canonical positive invariant Gram. Its optimal distortion in the original physical metric has an explicit sharp exponential rate. A positive sum of squared power norms has a stronger asymptotic with its prefactor, and determines the norms of every cyclic spectral projector exactly. The rate is a regular ideal bipyramid value. It is a metric and projector rate, not a knot-volume rate.

6. Orthogonal powers and corrected spectral packets

Conjugation by the physical unitary KtotK_{\mathrm{ tot}} is unitary on matrix Hilbert–Schmidt space. Its eigenvalues on TkT^k are QkQ^k. Hence the NN powers in (6) are exactly pairwise orthogonal. The polynomial identity TN=IT^N=I has simple roots, so (12) gives its complete spectral projectors. Conjugation by KtotK_{\mathrm{ tot}} cycles their eigenspaces; they all have rank Nb−1N^{b-1}. Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonality of powers proves the norm identity in (13). Finite Fourier orthogonality also proves N∑jPj*Pj=JavN\sum_jP_j^*P_j=J_{\mathrm{ av}} exactly. Thus this canonical positive Gram orthogonalizes the actual spectral packets, with their physical sizes and history sum explicitly known.

At b=1b=1 there is also an exact normalization check. Writing BN=∑a=0dsa−2B_N=\sum_{a=0}^d s_a^{-2}, (15) and reflection give ℋN=(∑asa2)(∑asa−2)=N2BN2\mathcal H_N=(\sum_a s_a^2)(\sum_a s_a^{-2})=N^2B_N^2. Formula (8) gives C1=1/3C_1=1/\sqrt3, consistently with (11).

7. What this supplies to the original source

The theorem computes a positive history sum and a sharp invariant-metric rate for an actual coproduct, including every wrapped physical entry. It does not make the original pivotal tail functional a norm. The original g=K1−Ng=K^{1-N}, marked projection, neighboring crossings, and all their transported interfaces must remain when using this Gram. The source freedom invoked in Section 1 concerns the whole assembled selected knot operator; it does not identify arbitrary local sectors. The intermediate tensor-product Casimir and multiplicity channels are not replaced by one-head scalar eigenvalues.

Already at b=1b=1 this positive metric rate is nonzero, although a normalized unknot cut has value one. Therefore neither (11) nor (13) is a Kashaev nonvanishing statement, a selected geometric coefficient, or a hyperbolic-volume lower bound. General short-component propagation and the finite-exception volume assertion remain open.

[C] already proves conditioning lower bounds for a different actual cyclic Wall operator. [F] already gives sharp positive angle costs for actual divided fusion in its source-fixed factorial metric. The addition here is the matching upper and lower optimal distortion rate in the original physical metric, together with (11)–(13) for this cyclic-source coproduct. No novelty is claimed for finite-group averaging, quantum multinomials, or the bipyramid volume formula.
