Maximal volume fixes the actual marked endpoint configuration

31 October 2026. general concentration theorem. This strengthens the already proved positive endpoint rate. It does not identify a phase-sensitive filling cycle, a genuine-q normal, or a Gaussian contour. All group conjugacies below are in PSL2(C); central SL2 lift signs are not inferred from volume.

1. Equality in the natural-map bound gives actual conjugacy

Let Gamma be the marked complete finite-volume hyperbolic exterior group, with its original inclusion iota into PSL2(C). Let rho be ONE fixed representation with Vol(rho)=Vol(Gamma)>0, in the normalization of the representation-volume proof. Its proved section3 constructs orientation-preserving target isometries g_j and natural maps F_j such that

G_j=g_j F_j -> J locally in C1 on H3,                  (1)

where J is a global orientation-preserving hyperbolic isometry. This convergence is obtained on every compact subset with the SAME g_j. The equivariance is exact:

rho_j(gamma)G_j(x)=G_j(iota(gamma)x),
rho_j(gamma)=g_j rho(gamma)g_j^(-1).                  (2)

For each fixed gamma the right and left input points converge. In particular rho_j(gamma) sends one point converging to J(x) into a bounded set, so these isometries are precompact: the stabilizer of a point of H3 is compact. A limiting isometry sends J(x) to J(iota(gamma)x) for every x, by (1)–(2). Hence it is uniquely J iota(gamma) J^(-1), and

rho_j(gamma) -> J iota(gamma) J^(-1)                 (3)

for every gamma. Equivalently one may first use four noncoplanar points and a finite generating set. This is exactly the equivariance upgrade in the proved peripheral-gap note; it uses no compactness of g_j yet.

We now prove that for this FIXED rho the g_j themselves have a bounded subsequence. Suppose instead that a subsequence escapes every compact subset of PSL2(C). Choose its elementary singular-value decomposition

g_j=k_j diag(exp(t_j/2),exp(-t_j/2)) l_j,
k_j,l_j in PSU(2), t_j -> infinity.

Pass to convergent k_j,l_j, with limits k,l. For each fixed gamma the matrix entries of rho_j(gamma) stay bounded by (3), in either SL2 lift. The upper-right entry of the middle conjugated matrix is

exp(t_j) [l_j rho(gamma) l_j^(-1)]_12.

It follows that [l rho(gamma) l^(-1)]_12=0 for every generator and hence for every gamma. Thus rho has a common fixed point p on CP1. Each rho_j then has the common fixed point p_j=g_j p. Compactness of CP1 gives a further subsequence p_j->p_infinity. Passing to the action in (3) shows that the complete holonomy J iota J^(-1) fixes p_infinity, a contradiction.

Here non-elementarity of the complete exterior is elementary in this setting, not an imported volume-rigidity theorem. For the actual certified exterior, the noncentral based cusp words at distinct vertices of one convex white face already have distinct fixed points. More generally, if a complete cusped finite-volume lattice fixed one point, normalize it to infinity and take a precisely invariant embedded cusp horoball. All its translates are concentric and intersect, so every group element stabilizes the horoball and its horospheres. The positive finite-area Euclidean cusp quotient then extends through all heights; its hyperbolic volume below the horoball is infinite. This contradicts finite volume.

Therefore g_j has a convergent subsequence g. Equation (3) yields

g rho(gamma) g^(-1)=J iota(gamma) J^(-1) for all gamma.
                                                           (4)

In particular rho ITSELF, not just a limit of its characters, is conjugate to the actual marked complete holonomy. This proof does not deduce conjugacy merely from faithfulness and does not assume a closed orbit from outside results.

2. Every endpoint maximizer has the original labeled white circles

Use the original fixed certified flat plat D and the full compact incidence-flow entropy

H_D(q)=sum_e h(q_e), q in Q_D,
W_D=max H_D=Phi_D=Vol(Y_D)/(2pi).                   (5)

The last equality and all boundary reductions are the full endpoint theorem. Let q be ANY maximizer, choosing maximizing triangle allocations at CROSS ports.

No port of q is pure-middle. Otherwise the exact identity reduction and hybrid-piece representation construct a representation of the original pi_1(Y_D) with volume 2pi H_D(q)=Vol(Y_D), killing an essential original crossing-circle meridian. The strict-kernel theorem forbids this. Thus there is no identity contraction at a maximum.

Consequently the source white triangulation is still the original simplicial sphere. Its induced graphs have no parallel-edge digons, so the hybrid tight-set classification gives no proper tight set. The positive hybrid witness and the proved corner expansions then give finite positive ordinary multipliers and only the two original marked zero curvatures. Terminal seams may be tagged CROSS, without changing q or entropy. The resulting signed circle development and original cut-ball representation rho are the regular marked ones, with volume Vol(Y_D). There are no independent pieces or arbitrary closing cusps in this equality case.

Apply (4). It remains to show that conjugacy of the original marked representation actually determines its labeled face geometry. At every original MAIN contact, the based meridian supplied by the literal prefix/turn dictionary is noncentral parabolic and fixes exactly that contact. At every original MATCHING contact, the recorded full ordered product P=BA is the noncentral parabolic preferred longitude, and its unique fixed point is that contact. These are the actual based words of the original cut balls, not just unbased cusp conjugacy classes. There are no deleted ports with free peripheral flags here.

A conjugacy to the actual complete holonomy therefore takes every labeled developed contact to the corresponding original complete contact. Each original white face of the nondegenerate convex certificate has at least THREE DISTINCT such contacts. Three distinct points of CP1 determine a unique generalized circle. Thus every labeled developed white circle/line is carried to its original complete white circle/line. This uses the source dictionary as well as the representation; an unlabeled character alone would not suffice.

Normalize both configurations at their original marked MAIN contact p=infinity. The conjugacy sends p to p because it carries that based noncentral meridian. It is therefore affine, z->a z+b, a!=0. Affine maps preserve bounded disks and oriented center triangles. The two ordered marked lines also determine the strip sides; the ordinary bounded circles lie on the prescribed inward sides. Thus the possible choice of a complementary disk is not an additional freedom.

The complete circle packing has the positive center-triangle signs in the original cyclic order. The reconstructed signed development must have those same signs. A GREEN port would have a negative triangle with an ordinary overloaded middle. Such a triangle cannot be a sign-invisible two-line triangle: on the original marked MAIN edge one of its middles is protected, and an outer matching pair cannot be the two protected vertices. It has either three finite circles or one line and two finite circles. In the latter case the cooriented line and horizontal contact order still distinguish its sign. Both cases contradict the recovered complete configuration. Hence EVERY port of a maximum is CROSS, and every local triangle angle is its actual complete geometric angle.

The common affine scale cancels from all angle formulas. Let q_geom,e=(alpha_x,in+alpha_x,out, alpha_b, alpha_c, alpha_y,in+alpha_y,out), where each alpha is the corresponding complete angle divided by2pi. Then the preceding argument proves

q=q_geom, and the maximizing CROSS allocations are geometric.
                                                           (6)

Thus q_geom is the UNIQUE full incidence-flow maximizer. No unspecified choice of square root, resetting of Gauss flags, or local open-word regulator invariance was used.

3. Compact gaps and the entire one-sided remainder

For every epsilon>0 with a nonempty set in the maximum below, compactness of Q_D and continuity of the full h give

gamma_D(epsilon)
   =Phi_D-max_{q in Q_D, ||q-q_geom||>=epsilon} H_D(q)>0.
                                                           (7)

This is a fixed-D gap; no effective uniformity in diagrams or increasing bridge width is asserted.

There is also a single gap for all one-sided branches. At every original port its two outer vertices form a MATCHING edge. They cannot both be the original protected pair, whose edge is MAIN in the simple labeled certificate. Hence each complete triangle has middle angle strictly less than pi, even when one outer face is marked. Therefore

eta_D=min_e {1/2-q_geom,e,b, 1/2-q_geom,e,c}>0.

The closed set

B_D={q in Q_D: some q_e,b>=1/2 or q_e,c>=1/2}

is separated from q_geom by eta_D in the sup norm. If nonempty, its maximum entropy is Phi_D-gamma_D^one with gamma_D^one>0.

At even N=2m,d=2m-1 every literal LEFT coefficient has c exponent at least m, and every RIGHT coefficient has b exponent at least m. After normalizing by d, any summand with at least one such port lies in B_D. The actual finite one-sided coefficient is bounded by the full positive coefficient at the same exponent. The uniform full coefficient theorem, polynomially many source summands and positivity consequently imply

limsup_(even N) N^(-1)log Z_(D,N;at least one one-sided port)
             <=Phi_D-gamma_D^one.                 (8)

When no such feasible source terms exist the left side is -infinity. The same argument bounds every fixed neighborhood complement by (7). In particular the whole positive source outside its CROSS contribution is exponentially smaller, uniformly over all its finitely many one-sided forest patterns. No subtraction or cancellation estimate is needed for this positive-source statement.

The odd regularized source has the same uniform h and a positive path split with LEFT c>=m+1 or RIGHT b>=m+1, d=2m. Thus the same fixed-D gap applies to its one-sided remainder as well, by the separate exact odd comparison theorem. Its extra visited-central spectral derivative paths stay in the cross support and are not called a one-sided remainder.

These gaps do not themselves give a relative Gaussian asymptotic, a bound on an oscillatory filling transform away from the controlled spectral region, or a nonzero contribution of the original filling cycle. Those remain separate quantum statements.

4. scope

The structural conclusion uses the linked analytic and geometric proofs. Numerical tests do not establish maximal-volume rigidity or uniqueness, and this note does not settle the fixed long-filled Kashaev limit.
