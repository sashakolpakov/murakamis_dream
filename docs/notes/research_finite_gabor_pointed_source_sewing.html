Finite Gabor seams for the original pointed meridian source

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

This note gives a finite packet expansion of the original all-width pointed source, including its two pivotal carrier shifts and its true meridian normals. Every physical occupation, closing factor, and partial-label current remains. The complete seam sum removes the additional packet damping exactly; a selected packet estimate is therefore not a noncancellation estimate for the complete source.

The sole complete proof direct is J, pinned below. The earlier research_gaussian_frame_newton_transport.md already gives a continuous Gaussian resolution, exact Newton-word composition, and original boundary functions. It also retains the inverse frame operator under weights. The frame principle is not new here. The present calculation records the finite all-width pointed closing row, its meridian connection and relative Laurent terms, and its exact seam cancellation. No assertion from that coverage note is used as a proof premise.

5. The complete seam removes the added damping

At any one-head seam the exact contraction is already (5): 1N∑a,sga,s(n)ga,s(k)¯=δnk.(28) \frac1N\sum_{a,s} g_{a,s}(n)\overline{g_{a,s}(k)}=\delta_{nk}. \qquad\text{(28)} The frequency sum first enforces equality of the physical occupations. The center sum then has value one. With the analytic dual in (7), diagonal weights cancel on this diagonal as well. Tensoring proves the same assertion on every wire. Thus summing all packet centers and carriers restores the original capped word, including its original signs and phases. It does not leave four independent positive packet penalties at each previously estimated local kernel.

There is a precise quadratic version of the same fact. Let x∈ℝkx\in\mathbb R^k denote physical seam variations and aa the corresponding free center variations. Suppose the original real quadratic form is 12x𝖳Hx\tfrac12x^{\mathsf T}Hx, and the added packet penalty is −12(x−a)𝖳C(x−a)-\tfrac12(x-a)^{\mathsf T}C(x-a), C>0C>0. The combined Hessian and its Schur complement in aa are (H−CCC−C),(H−C)−C(−C)−1C=H.(29) \begin{pmatrix}H-C&C\\C&-C\end{pmatrix},\qquad (H-C)-C(-C)^{-1}C=H. \qquad\text{(29)} The direction a=xa=x displays the loss of the extra penalty directly. Integrating the center Gaussian gives the same result, up to its normalization. This calculation does not assert a Gaussian approximation to every finite frame mode; (28) proves the cancellation without such an approximation.

A prospective selected source contribution must therefore specify compatible center and carrier labels, including (11), an actual stationary cycle for the enlarged finite sum, and the remaining normal and boundary coupons. It must control all complementary labels, wrapped windows, and Fourier aliases relative to that contribution. A local nonzero packet kernel, or a positive frame operator, supplies none of these comparisons by itself. The frame identity also does not select a geometric relative cycle or make its coefficient nonzero.

The result here is the original finite source expansion (12),(16),(24), its physical caps and boundary data, its true moving-frame normal currents (20)–(27), and the exact seam cancellation (28)–(29). No new volume-conjecture case, source positivity, coherent inverse estimate, or geometric contribution is claimed.
