The literal soft-cut Morse–Bott one-form

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The actual complex-binomial braid source has a nonzero leading one-form on its remaining complete geometric framing curve. With the original factorial and Gaussian square-root marks retained, that form is a representation-dependent constant times dr/sdr/s. This is an amplitude calculation, including the finite N−1N-1 cut correction; it is not an inference from the principal centralizer vector field.

The complete positive gluing certificate remains an explicit hypothesis. No relative framing period or physical cycle coefficient is calculated here. The older two-form/torsion comparison is used in its stated regularity range, and that range is checked below.

1. Source, coordinates, and the one-form being computed

Let the fixed knot braid have bb strands and cc crossings, with

m=c+1,c0=2πi,h=c0/N.(1) m=c+1,\qquad c_0=2\pi i,\qquad h=c_0/N. \qquad\text{(1)}

Use the actual integral chord coordinates and logarithmic occupations ζ=c0x\zeta=c_0x of H and F. The unweighted logarithmic phase is S0S_0, including the original pivotal and framing terms. Use the full correcting integer Fourier covector, including the cut entry. For a complex cut parameter θ\theta near 1/21/2, write

Φθ=S0−c0n⋅ζ+gθ(u),gθ(u)=c0bθ(u/c0),(2) \Phi_\theta=S_0-c_0 n\cdot\zeta+g_\theta(u),\qquad g_\theta(u)=c_0 b_\theta(u/c_0), \qquad\text{(2)} bθ(t)=−tlog⁡t−(1−t)log⁡(1−t)+tlog⁡θ+(1−t)log⁡(1−θ). b_\theta(t)=-t\log t-(1-t)\log(1-t) +t\log\theta+(1-t)\log(1-\theta).

All logarithms are the compatible original ones. The geometric critical curve has

u=uθ=c0θ,s=sθ=e−c0θ−1≠0,(3) u=u_\theta=c_0\theta,\qquad s=s_\theta=e^{-c_0\theta}-1\ne0, \qquad\text{(3)}

and its remaining coordinate is rr. H proves that the Hessian in the actual mm occupation coordinates has rank cc. These coordinates have already removed the common momentum; no further phase variable is quotiented here.

Separate only the explicitly supplied occupation-independent factors of the literal source, such as its common Cartan scalars and any external modified-dimension line. Denote their unchanged product by ENE_N. In equations (4) and (20), the phase is the phase of the quotient by ENE_N: a constant exponential contained in ENE_N is not counted a second time in Φθ\Phi_\theta. Equivalently one may restore that same constant term to the phase and remove its factor from ENE_N. Neither convention changes any Hessian below. The line is never set to one or replaced by an unspecified normalization. In particular this convention does not retain an extra old cut average 1/N1/N: the binomial weight multiplies the unnormalized diagonal partial-trace summand.

In the variable-amplitude convention of D and Q, let alit(ζ)a_{\mathrm{ lit}}(\zeta) be the leading crossing/pivot amplitude of the actual factorial section A, after this separation. F gives

EN−1IN(Nx)=N−m/2alit(c0x)Bθ(u/c0)eΦθ(c0x)/h(1+O(N−1)),(4) E_N^{-1}I_N(Nx)=N^{-m/2}a_{\mathrm{ lit}}(c_0x) B_\theta(u/c_0)e^{\Phi_\theta(c_0x)/h}(1+O(N^{-1})), \qquad\text{(4)} Bθ(t)=1−t(1−θ)2πt(1−t).(5) B_\theta(t)=\frac{1-t}{(1-\theta)\sqrt{2\pi t(1-t)}}. \qquad\text{(5)}

The Fourier character in (4) is included in INI_N for this formula. All fixed derivatives of the error are uniform on smaller regular slit charts. In F’s complete Fourier expansion the selected integral has coefficient one; its relative boundary currents are unchanged.

Choose local coordinates (r,y1,…,yc)(r,y_1,\ldots,y_c) with critical curve y=0y=0, and write dζ1∧⋯∧dζm=J(r,y)dr∧dyd\zeta_1\wedge\cdots\wedge d\zeta_m=J(r,y)\,dr\wedge dy. The logarithmic Gaussian one-form is, by definition,

ωθ=alitBθJdet⁡(−∂y2Φθ)|y=0dr.(6) \omega_\theta= \left. \frac{a_{\mathrm{ lit}} B_\theta J} {\sqrt{\det(-\partial_y^2\Phi_\theta)}} \right|_{y=0}dr. \qquad\text{(6)}

The square root belongs to the transported normal Gaussian orientation line. Under a normal coordinate change the Hessian determinant gains the square of the normal Jacobian, so (6) is independent of that choice with its orientation line retained.

Let 𝒯β\mathcal T_\beta be Q’s actual holonomy torsion, with its specified ordered bases. Then the result is

ωθ2=i1−c(−1)b𝒯β2(drsθ)2.(7) \boxed{\quad \omega_\theta^2= i^{\,1-c}(-1)^b\frac{\mathcal T_\beta}{2} \left(\frac{dr}{s_\theta}\right)^2. \quad} \qquad\text{(7)}

The square does not choose a fresh sign. The actual factorial marks and one oriented normal Gaussian fix the nonzero constant in (6).

2. The fixed-trace regularity needed for the torsion comparison

For completeness, the fixed-trace premise in Q follows here from H’s exact fixed-eigencharacter rigidity; it is not inferred merely by differentiating the trace function at a parabolic.

At a noncentral complete meridian μ\mu, let its retained eigenvalue be ε=±1\varepsilon=\pm1. On every local Artin deformation with tr⁡μ=2ε\operatorname{tr}\mu=2\varepsilon and det⁡μ=1\det\mu=1,

det⁡(μ−εI)=0.(8) \det(\mu-\varepsilon I)=0. \qquad\text{(8)}

A fixed nonzero entry of the original rank-one matrix μ−εI\mu-\varepsilon I stays a unit. Eliminating with that entry shows that its kernel is a unique free rank-one direct summand. It is the exact eigenline for eigenvalue ε\varepsilon, not an arbitrary invariant line with a nilpotently varying eigencharacter. Commuting peripheral matrices preserve this kernel. Thus the unaugmented fixed-trace deformation carries a canonical decoration of precisely the kind used in H’s rigidity argument.

H’s positive-certificate comparison makes the decorated fixed-meridian quotient a point over these Artin rings. Conversely forgetting the canonical exact eigenline recovers the same fixed-trace deformation. Hence the kernel of the linearized braid fixed-point map consists exactly of simultaneous conjugation directions.

In Q’s notation the actual holonomy complex is therefore acyclic:

0→𝔰𝔩2→AV→D=dF−IV→M𝔰𝔩2→0.(9) 0\longrightarrow\mathfrak{sl}_2\mathop{\longrightarrow}^{A}V \mathop{\longrightarrow}^{D=dF-I}V \mathop{\longrightarrow}^{M}\mathfrak{sl}_2\longrightarrow0. \qquad\text{(9)}

Here AA is injective by irreducibility. Surjectivity of MM can also be checked directly: an element annihilating the product differential under the trace pairing commutes successively with every meridian, and is zero. Rank-nullity then proves exactness. The torsion 𝒯β\mathcal T_\beta is nonzero and independent of the framing point. The latter follows either from Q’s determinant ratio or from applying the same simultaneous conjugation to its two middle bases and the same adjoint map to its endpoint bases.

Thus Q’s comparison is applicable to this actual source. It is not an additional acyclicity assumption on an unrelated presentation.

3. The literal subprincipal crossing factors

This step is needed because the chosen root-factorial section has a definite phase and N1/2N^{1/2} normalization. One must not insert only its leading dilogarithm into a torsion formula.

At complete color put A=ea,B=eb,C=evA=e^a,B=e^b,C=e^v for one crossing’s two input log occupations and transfer. A proves that the squared single-factor amplitude is i(1−ez)i(1-e^z). Its positive five-factor ratio therefore has squared amplitude

1i(1−BC)(1−A)(1−B)(1−C)(1−A/C).(10) \frac1i\frac{(1-BC)(1-A)}{(1-B)(1-C)(1-A/C)}. \qquad\text{(10)}

After retaining its common Cartan scalar, the exact monomial in A contributes AB/CAB/C to that squared amplitude: the half-step in x=−q−1x=-q^{-1} gives ABAB, and its last transfer factor gives C−1C^{-1}. Since

1−A1−A/C=C1−1/A1−C/A,(11) \frac{1-A}{1-A/C} =C\frac{1-1/A}{1-C/A}, \qquad\text{(11)}

the literal positive amplitude is

alit,+2=i−1AB(1−BC)(1−1/A)(1−B)(1−C)(1−C/A)=i−1a+2.(12) a_{{\mathrm{ lit}},+}^2=i^{-1} AB\frac{(1-BC)(1-1/A)}{(1-B)(1-C)(1-C/A)} =i^{-1}a_+^2. \qquad\text{(12)}

For the negative crossing the exact monomial has squared subprincipal factor 1/(AB)1/(AB): its qnq^n and last transfer half-step cancel. Applying (11) with BB in place of AA gives

alit,−2=i−1CAB(1−AC)(1−1/B)(1−A)(1−C)(1−C/B)=i−1a−2.(13) a_{{\mathrm{ lit}},-}^2=i^{-1} \frac C{AB}\frac{(1-AC)(1-1/B)} {(1-A)(1-C)(1-C/B)} =i^{-1}a_-^2. \qquad\text{(13)}

The quantities a±a_\pm are exactly D’s balanced principal amplitudes, not amplitudes of a coherent transform. D proves their mixed-Hessian/momentum factorization, including both signs. Multiplying it through the actual word cancels internal momenta and retains the original pivotal multiplier. Consequently

alit2=i−caβ2.(14) a_{\mathrm{ lit}}^2=i^{-c}a_\beta^2. \qquad\text{(14)}

The phases have the same Hessian. To check the reciprocal-factorial convention explicitly, the positive literal monomial has quadratic part ab−bv+πi(v−a−b)ab-bv+\pi i(v-a-b). Inverting its reflected factorial pair adds av−v2/2−πivav-v^2/2-\pi iv, giving ab+(a−b)v−v2/2−πi(a+b)ab+(a-b)v-v^2/2-\pi i(a+b), the balanced phase. Negatively the corresponding two parts are −ab−bv+v2/2+πi(a+b)-ab-bv+v^2/2+\pi i(a+b) and bv−v2/2−πivbv-v^2/2-\pi iv, giving −ab+πi(a+b−v)-ab+\pi i(a+b-v). The continued dilogarithm inversion identity follows by differentiation; its integration constant is independent of the occupations. Other compatible branch marks add their recorded affine Fourier characters and constants. They do not change the Hessian used here. No root phase is replaced by its modulus.

4. The unweighted two-form and the additional binomial Gaussian

Before adding gθg_\theta, the actual phase has its two framing directions. The incidence adapter in H applies with g=0g=0, and the same rigidity argument above gives precisely this smooth two-dimensional critical germ. Its complete-color coordinates are

F(r,s)=(r1−rs−s),u=−Log⁡(1+s),Ωcut=−dr∧dss(1+s)=dr∧dus.(15) F(r,s)=\begin{pmatrix}r&1-r\\s&-s\end{pmatrix},\qquad u=-\operatorname{Log}(1+s),\qquad \Omega_{\mathrm{ cut}}=-\frac{dr\wedge ds}{s(1+s)} =\frac{dr\wedge du}{s}. \qquad\text{(15)}

Q proves, with the actual kernel/cokernel identification and original lattice cofactor, that its raw variable Gaussian two-form obeys

Ωraw2=(−1)b𝒯β2Ωcut2.(16) \Omega_{\mathrm{ raw}}^2=(-1)^b\frac{\mathcal T_\beta}{2} \Omega_{\mathrm{ cut}}^2. \qquad\text{(16)}

In particular this is the raw parity convention, not Q’s separately parity-normalized square. Combining (14) and (16), the two-form from the literal factorial section is a marked constant times dr∧du/sdr\wedge du/s, with squared constant i−c(−1)b𝒯β/2i^{-c}(-1)^b\mathcal T_\beta/2.

Choose (r,u,y)(r,u,y) as local coordinates before the binomial insertion, with yy transverse to the two-dimensional critical surface and uu the actual cut coordinate. The symmetric normal Hessian is invertible. Taylor expansion followed by its holomorphic symmetric matrix square root puts the unweighted phase into a quadratic normal form while keeping r,ur,u fixed. Adding gθ(u)g_\theta(u) therefore adds one independent normal Gaussian. This proves the iterated Gaussian determinant formula here directly; no nonzero normal or tangential Jacobian is suppressed.

The two needed scalar values are

Bθ(θ)2=12πθ(1−θ),gθ″(uθ)=−1c0θ(1−θ).(17) B_\theta(\theta)^2=\frac1{2\pi\theta(1-\theta)},\qquad g_\theta''(u_\theta)=-\frac1{c_0\theta(1-\theta)}. \qquad\text{(17)}

Their quotient is

Bθ(θ)2−gθ″(uθ)=c02π=i.(18) \frac{B_\theta(\theta)^2}{-g_\theta''(u_\theta)} =\frac{c_0}{2\pi}=i. \qquad\text{(18)}

The factor (1−t)/(1−θ)(1-t)/(1-\theta) in (5) is the actual N−1N-1 correction; at this particular stationary value it is one. The calculation retains it before evaluation, including its derivatives. Equations (14), (16), and (18) prove (7).

5. Transport, original normalization, and retained currents

On a connected marked chart, define CβC_\beta by

ωθ=Cβdrsθ,Cβ2=i1−c(−1)b𝒯β/2≠0.(19) \omega_\theta=C_\beta\frac{dr}{s_\theta},\qquad C_\beta^2=i^{1-c}(-1)^b\mathcal T_\beta/2\ne0. \qquad\text{(19)}

Since the square is independent of rr, differentiating it proves ∂rCβ=0\partial_r C_\beta=0. Thus the leading actual one-form is invariant under sθ∂rs_\theta\partial_r. Its constant remains on the original factorial/Gaussian orientation line; independently changing a square-root sign is not the same marked continuation. There are no additional finite-rr poles on this regular source fiber. Boundary degenerations or a changing representation can still affect the torsion and its domain.

For clarity, the original scalar normalization can be restored without choosing it from the torsion. The measure in a Fourier integral is Nmdx=c0−mNmdζN^m dx=c_0^{-m}N^m d\zeta. Formal integration of only its cc normal variables therefore has leading one-form

ENN1/2(2π)c/2c0c/2−meΦθ(ζ*)/hωθ.(20) E_N N^{1/2}(2\pi)^{c/2}c_0^{c/2-m} e^{\Phi_\theta(\zeta_*)/h}\omega_\theta. \qquad\text{(20)}

All powers in (20) use the same normal orientation. A different explicit external normalization multiplies ENE_N by precisely that whole factor. It does not alter (19). Uniformity with a relative O(N−1)O(N^{-1}) error on compact rr-charts follows from A/F’s derivative bounds and H’s holomorphic normal form: on a small descending normal ball the Gaussian tail is exponentially small, odd Taylor terms integrate to zero, and the first remaining Taylor terms have order hh. This constructs a local normal integral, not a deformation of the original global chain to it.

The weighted Ward source has not been replaced by an invariant trace. Put PθP_\theta equal to the actual binomial cut tensor the original pivots. For every one of its ordered weighted Ward terms, and every marked word OO, finite matrix cyclicity gives the exact identity

Tr⁡(Pθ[X,AB]O)=Tr⁡([Pθ,X]ABO)+Tr⁡(PθAB[O,X]).(21) \operatorname{Tr}(P_\theta[X,A B]O) =\operatorname{Tr}([P_\theta,X]A B O) +\operatorname{Tr}(P_\theta A B[O,X]). \qquad\text{(21)}

Summing (21), with the actual weights and any prescribed factor 1/h1/h, retains all cut, pivotal and marked-word currents. Nothing in (12)–(20) sets either term on its right to zero. The leading invariance in (19) was obtained from the actual subprincipal amplitude and determinant, not by discarding those currents. An additional observable with leading symbol o(r)o(r), when its same local expansion is justified, multiplies (19) by o(r)o(r); no invariance or absence of poles is asserted for arbitrary marks.

Finally, on the affine fiber (19) has primitive Cβr/sθC_\beta r/s_\theta. Compact closed loops have zero leading period. A specified finite-endpoint relative chain would pair as Cβ/sθC_\beta/s_\theta times its signed endpoint sum, but the original chain’s transverse pushforward and any infinity regularization remain unproved here. The one-form does not supply their values.
