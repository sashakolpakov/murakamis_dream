Long-twist knot volume from a maximal diagonalization of any simple spherical quadrangulation

31 October 2026. Research theorem assembled from the original-source, geometric, and analytic proofs cited below. The conclusion concerns actual Kashaev invariants and actual filled hyperbolic volumes. The diagram is selected by a finite classical maximization; it is not an arbitrary prescribed diagram.

1. Statement and quantifiers

Let 𝒬\mathcal Q be any finite connected simple spherical quadrangulation with R≥2R\geq2 faces, each bounded by four distinct vertices. Its edges will be called MAIN edges. Choose a diagonal in every quadrilateral, retaining every embedded incidence, to obtain a triangular cellulation 𝒯\mathcal T. There are 2R2^R choices. The chosen diagonals are matching edges: each pairs its two triangles.

Fix a MAIN edge uvuv. Give each triangle total angle π\pi, each vertex other than u,vu,v total angle 2π2\pi, and the two marked vertices angle zero. Write Φ(𝒯;u,v)=12πmaxθ∑t𝒱(θt),𝒱(θ1,θ2,θ3)=2∑j=13[Λ(θj/2)+Λ((π−θj)/2)], \Phi(\mathcal T;u,v) =\frac1{2\pi}\max_{\theta} \sum_{t}\mathcal V(\theta_t),\qquad \mathcal V(\theta_1,\theta_2,\theta_3) =2\sum_{j=1}^3 \left[\Lambda(\theta_j/2)+ \Lambda((\pi-\theta_j)/2)\right], \tag{1} where Λ(x)=−∫0xlog⁡|2sin⁡t|dt\Lambda(x)=-\int_0^x\log|2\sin t|\,dt. The maximum is over these closed angle constraints; an empty fiber has value −∞-\infty. Select any diagonalization 𝒯*\mathcal T_* attaining the maximum of (1) over the finite set of choices.

Theorem. Every such 𝒯*\mathcal T_* is simple. It has an explicitly constructed augmented link Y𝒯*,ϵ⊂S3Y_{\mathcal T_*,\epsilon}\subset S^3, with RR labeled crossing disks, whose complement is complete hyperbolic and whose normalized volume is Vol⁡(S3\Y𝒯*,ϵ)2π=Φ(𝒯*;u,v). \frac{\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus Y_{\mathcal T_*,\epsilon})}{2\pi} =\Phi(\mathcal T_*;u,v). \tag{2} Here ϵe∈{−1,0,1}\epsilon_e\in\{-1,0,1\} records the retained half twist at each disk. There exists a choice of their parities for which the main link has one component. Fix any such choice, with the signs and disk framings recorded. Fill the crossing circles with integer coordinates pep_e, so the actual crossing powers are ne=ϵe−2pen_e=\epsilon_e-2p_e, and call the resulting knot K𝒯*,ϵ(𝒑)K_{\mathcal T_*,\epsilon}(\mathbf p).

There is a finite threshold T(𝒯*,ϵ)T(\mathcal T_*,\epsilon) such that for every fixed integer vector 𝒑\mathbf p with min⁡e|pe|>T(𝒯*,ϵ)\min_e|p_e|>T(\mathcal T_*,\epsilon), the knot complement is hyperbolic and limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(K𝒯*,ϵ(𝒑))|=Vol⁡(S3\K𝒯*,ϵ(𝒑)). \boxed{ \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log\left|K_N\bigl(K_{\mathcal T_*,\epsilon} (\mathbf p)\bigr)\right| =\operatorname{Vol}\!\left(S^3\setminus K_{\mathcal T_*,\epsilon}(\mathbf p)\right).} \tag{3} KNK_N denotes the original, unknot-normalized Kashaev invariant in the complete-color conventions of the source-normalization theorem. The twists are independently signed and fixed before NN tends to infinity. Both parities and composite levels occur in the limit. There is no restriction on ratios of twist magnitudes. The threshold may depend on the whole chosen diagram.

Equivalently, choose a diagonalization with maximal actual polyhedron volume among the simple choices. It is also maximal among all choices: every nonsimple choice has strictly smaller capacity than some simple choice. The maximum and the set of maximizing simple charts do not depend on the MAIN edge used for (1).

2. Selection, geometry, and an actual knot source

The maximal-diagonalization theorem, sections 4–5, supplies three conclusions used here:

  1. Every 𝒬\mathcal Q in the statement has a simple diagonalization.
  2. Every nonsimple diagonalization has capacity strictly below some simple one. Thus every global maximizer is simple.
  3. At each maximizing chart the original closed non-CROSS degree fiber has a uniform strict entropy gap below Φ\Phi.

These assertions include R=2R=2 and allow separating triangles. The combinatorial simplification removes repeated diagonal edges by flips across digons. The strict capacity comparison uses the final flip into a simple sphere, where the angle optimizer is positive at every allowed ordinary corner. It does not assume that a chosen initial chart already has a hyperbolic realization.

For a simple 𝒯*\mathcal T_*, sections 3–5 of the simple-sphere capacity and packing construction give the unique relative-interior angle optimizer, finite positive ordinary curvatures, and the labeled convex ideal right-angled polyhedron P𝒯*P_{\mathcal T_*}. The normalization is exact: Φ=Vol⁡(P𝒯*)/π. \Phi=\operatorname{Vol}(P_{\mathcal T_*})/\pi. \tag{4} This construction supplies the positive geometric certificate, rather than making it an extra unverified hypothesis on 𝒬\mathcal Q.

The general Morse source realization constructs an actual augmented link from this matched sphere. Its physical strands use the specified pairing in every four-terminal disk; they are not the abstract cycles of the cubic graph with its matching removed. Cutting its projection sphere and crossing disks gives precisely the two labeled balls whose boundary polyhedron is P𝒯*P_{\mathcal T_*}. The local shaded maps, including the recorded half twists, are those of the original augmented-link cut and gluing, sections 1–5. The two copies therefore give the complete hyperbolic exterior and (2), with the actual disk-framed peripheral paths.

A spanning tree of the graph of untwisted physical main circles selects odd boxes that merge them to one circle. This proves the existence of a knot parity pattern. Any integer filling of that fixed parity retains one main component. The positive marked filling argument applies to this actual augmented exterior: its local positive gluing and primitive disk slopes give all sufficiently long independent signed fillings. No constant number of heads in a horizontal slice is required by that geometric argument.

The same general Morse source theorem supplies the exact original quantum contraction, for every NN, in the form SN(𝜹)=[∏f≠u,vLfd]∏e=1RHVN(−2cos⁡(2πδe))(Lxe,Lbe,Lce,Lye)|Lu=Lv=0,d=N−1. Thus (5) is a polynomial of the newly constructed link, not a polynomial transported from a different diagonalization. Half twists enter this functional through nen_e once. They are not also inserted in the local VNV_N in (5).

3. The new non-CROSS gap controls the full numerator

Use the exact CROSS/LEFT/RIGHT decomposition of each original local polynomial, at its actual parity-dependent cutoffs. Let CNrawC_N^{\mathrm{raw}} be the all-CROSS coefficient, including its sign (−1)dR(-1)^{dR}, and set EN=SN−CNrawE_N=S_N-C_N^{\mathrm{raw}}. The sharp Green maximum eliminates the real spectra in the local upper functional. 4. The CROSS packet and spectral gap on this general Morse source

Here the extensions of the older flat-plat notation must be checked; the finite source construction alone does not give asymptotics. Sections 1–3 of the original coefficient packet proof use the following data: the exact coefficient (5) and its primitive Cauchy measure; the local full-Hermite cofactor interpolation and relative estimates; a positive simple-white packing with two adjacent marked vertices; and connectivity of the remaining ordinary vertices. All are present on the new source. A simple triangulated sphere remains connected after deleting two vertices. Moreover the protected MAIN edge cannot also be a matching edge, so no local outer pair consists of both protected vertices. The allowed local zero specializations are exactly those in the cofactor proofs.

The angular-complement proof is local on triangles and uses that connectivity. The face saddle uses the positive finite-face Hessian after fixing the common scale. Neither proof uses the order of cups, caps and coupons in a horizontal sweep. Consequently the same finite functions AN,HNA_N,H_N, constructed on this new coefficient, give CNraw(𝜹)=(−1)dRN2ReNAN(𝜹)(2πN)(R−1)/2det⁡HN(𝜹)(1+O(N−1)) C_N^{\mathrm{raw}}(\boldsymbol\delta) =\frac{(-1)^{dR}N^{2R}e^{NA_N(\boldsymbol\delta)}} {(2\pi N)^{(R-1)/2}\sqrt{\det H_N(\boldsymbol\delta)}} (1+O(N^{-1})) \tag{10} on a fixed complex neighborhood of zero. 5. The entire original period and its filled-volume action

Apply the proof of the whole Fourier-period theorem to SNS_N. Beyond H1 and H2, it needs only the exact functional already proved in section 2, separate evenness and period one, the polynomial degree bound, and the original sine/Gaussian endpoint identities. The full source has each property. The proof keeps the second-derivative endpoint currents, bounds the infinite charge tail on whole intervals, and localizes only finitely many charges. Thus no additional cycle selection or cancellation assumption is introduced by this application.

For all sufficiently long independently signed fixed 𝒏\mathbf n, the original unit-charge orbit has a nonzero leading coefficient at both level parities. Its limiting phase is the stationary value of A0(𝜹)+πi∑eneδe2+2πi∑eδe. 6. Relation to the prescribed k-bridge target

The result ranges over every simple spherical quadrangulation of arbitrary size, then every maximizing diagonal choice and every fixed knot parity pattern, with independently long signed twists at every box. It is not a single twist family or a fixed list of examples. Its selection condition is a finite classical optimization, with existence proved above; no quantum gap remains as an extra hypothesis for this selected class.

It does not prove the same result for each preassigned diagonalization. Changing a diagonal can change the actual augmented link. Nor does the general Morse construction preserve a preassigned plat width or prove a particular minimal bridge number. The general prescribed-plat target therefore remains open beyond this and the previously certified subclasses. For that target one still needs a source comparison on the given diagram, or a topology-preserving way to reach a certified chart.
