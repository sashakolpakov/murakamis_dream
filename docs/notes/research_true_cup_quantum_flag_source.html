A quantum flag model for the true cup source

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The complete physical cup source has a unitary matrix-coordinate model. In these coordinates every canonical based quantum meridian acts by an explicit polynomial difference operator. The clock degree rises by at most one per regular meridian entry, at every width and level. The original last-marked closing is exactly one specified cyclic Weyl coefficient of the actual word state, with its original phase and scale.

This identifies a full source and its observation, not a conditional coideal eigenmode. A positive regular-boundary limit gives inverse-pair flag circles for the cup state. The original closing still selects its prescribed coefficient; neither that limit nor the polynomial module gives a lower bound for the coefficient after an arbitrary braid.

5. The original marked scalar is one Frobenius coefficient

Put p=k−1p=k-1 and let 𝒫=∏i=1p𝒬2i\mathcal P=\prod_{i=1}^p\mathcal Q_{2i}, where 𝒬ea=Qaea\mathcal Qe_a=Q^ae_a. 6. A regular holomorphic flag-circle limit

The true cup is a positive vector state. For its fixed holomorphic regular observations it has the following boundary representative, before its original closing is applied. For ww on the unit circle put G0(w)=(0−w−1w−2). G_0(w)=\begin{pmatrix}0&-w^{-1}\\w&-2\end{pmatrix}. It has determinant one, trace −2-2, and a noncentral parabolic flag [w−1:1][w^{-1}:1]. For any fixed regular word WW, ⟨ψ0,W(H1,…,H2k)ψ0⟩=∫𝕋kW(G0(w1),G0(w1)−1,…,G0(wk),G0(wk)−1)∏i=1kdwi2πiwi+OW(N−1).(25) \begin{split} \langle\psi_0,W(H_1,\ldots,H_{2k})\psi_0\rangle =\int_{\mathbb T^k} W(&G_0(w_1),G_0(w_1)^{-1},\ldots,\\ &G_0(w_k),G_0(w_k)^{-1}) \prod_{i=1}^k\frac{dw_i}{2\pi i w_i} +O_W(N^{-1}). \end{split} \qquad\text{(25)} This includes inverse words by (10).

Here is a direct proof from the actual module. For fixed word degree, all shifts and clock degrees are bounded independently of NN. For sufficiently large NN, normalized cyclic trace in (13) therefore becomes ordinary constant term in vi,Tiv_i,T_i. The coefficient formulas (15),(16),(18) are regular Laurent polynomials in qq after their proved divisions. Replacing qq by one costs OW(N−1)O_W(N^{-1}). Formula (25) concerns the holomorphic regular algebra, not its full closure under Hermitian adjoints. The flag curve is not asserted to represent that larger positive moment problem. Formula (22) also has its separate unitary clock insertion and growing selected exponent; neither may be removed to replace the original scalar by (25). The flag variables are relative boundary data, not independently declared global framing gauges or knot meridians chosen after sewing.

7. What this changes and what remains

The coordinate map is unitary on the complete physical source. It retains the entire cup, every coherent input term, actual signed crossings, the original last mark, and the separate χN\chi_N. It does not project to a selected coideal height or a value-only pair-Casimir quotient. The statements are at the fixed quantum root. Parameter normals require their original families; the cyclic Weyl identities are not differentiated as off-root identities.

The exact maps (14)–(20) supply a canonical polynomial module for regular quantum holonomy observations. Equation (22) identifies the original scalar’s precise coefficient in that same full source. A change to these fixed linear coordinates preserves the degree of a polynomial variational formulation; no convexity theorem for an anchored quartic is inferred.

Degree four in auxiliary variational variables is not bounded degree in the regular physical meridian entries. The level-dependent crossing, its original Frobenius coefficient and all remaining coherent sewing must still be estimated. No volume lower bound, nonzero original scalar, or finite-exception theorem is proved here.
