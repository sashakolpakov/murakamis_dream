A source-preserving odd framing period

30 October 2026. A specified difference of the two nearest physical bands, with its exact quantum coefficient, has the SAME normalized value as the original complete invariant. Its normalization is nonzero at every level and costs only O(N) on an interior real coherent cut. In the actual routed physical chart the underlying odd form has an explicit nonzero logarithmic residue; the actual coherent base weight remains part of the source.

This closes the old distinction between the unchanged scalar and an inserted odd observable. It does not determine the original relative cycle’s winding, justify a quantum boundary-scale limit from separate local packets, or supply the missing mixed-color global estimate.

1. The literal source and its normalization

Fix N>=2, d=N-1, q0=exp(pi i/N). Use the original regular generic channel, with epsilon the genuine quantum parameter,

C_e(X)=P_V[A U^-1(X tensor I)U B], C_e(I)=z(e)I.       (1)

Here A:W->V and B:V->W may have different actual Morse widths, U is the recorded braid carrying the chosen W head to FIRST position, and P_V closes the other physical heads with g_e=(-1)^d K_e. All original cups, caps, complete-circle scalars and peripheral lines are retained. These are exactly the hypotheses of the braided source theorem. The statement applies at arbitrary fixed width and to the specified regular complete-source sector sum. It does not make a bare root mark a generic intertwiner.

Let T_n=sum_(r-s=n)E_rs be the UNWRAPPED physical band, and set

beta_a(t)=binom(d,a)t^a(1-t)^(d-a), e_t=beta(t)1^T,
O_N=T_1-q0 T_-1,
kappa_N(t)=1-q0-t^d+q0(1-t)^d.                       (2)

The actual divided-descendant theorem proves C_0(T_n)=z_0 T_n, including both n=+1 and n=-1. Moreover Tr(e_t T_1)=1-beta_d and Tr(e_t T_-1)=1-beta_0. Consequently the exact original scalar is

Tr[e_t C_0(O_N)]/kappa_N(t)=z_0,                    (3)

wherever kappa_N!=0. No second pivotal closure or new state is inserted in (3). The numerator is the original coherent FIRST-cut row applied to two actual neighboring bands of (1).

For real 0<=t<=1 it is nonzero at EVERY N. Indeed put A0=1-t^d, B0=1-(1-t)^d and theta=pi/N. Then A0,B0>=0, A0+B0>=1, and exactly

|kappa_N|^2=(A0-B0)^2 cos^2(theta/2)
                     +(A0+B0)^2 sin^2(theta/2)
              >=sin^2(theta/2)>=N^-2.              (4)

There is no odd/even exception. On any fixed t in [delta,1-delta],

kappa_N=(1-q0)[1+O_delta(N(1-delta)^(N-1))],
kappa_N=-pi i/N+O_delta(N^-2).                      (5)

Thus its inverse has size O(N), without an exponential cost. For complex coherent t, keep the explicit kappa_N and its actual zeros; (4) is not a claim on arbitrary complex t.

The exact finite period in (3) is

kappa_N^-1 [sum_(0<=a,l<d) beta_a
                   C_0(E_(l+1,l))_(a+1,a)
   -q0 sum_(1<=a,l<=d) beta_a
                   C_0(E_(l-1,l))_(a-1,a)].         (6)

These are TWO different physical support sets, with the original internal head sums and their own finite endpoint rows. Neither is wrapped cyclically or identified with the other’s support cycle. Their common primitive charged chart and meromorphic local currents are supplied by the common-band theorem. If the cut is already the first physical slot, U=I is permitted. For a different selected edge, its actual U and U^-1 remain in (6).

2. Genuine quantum normals and the indispensable q coefficient

The ordinary closed source is F_e=D_N(e)z(e), with D_N=(-1)^d[N](q0 exp(epsilon)), D_N(0)=0 and D_N’(0)=nu_N. For example retain the literal regular lift O_e=T_1-q0 exp(epsilon)T-1 and kappa_e=Tr(e_t O_e). Equation (3) implies exactly

Z_e=Tr[e_t C_e(O_e)]/kappa_e,
D_N(e)[Z_e-z(e)]=O(epsilon^2),
[epsilon]D_N Z_e/nu_N=z_0.                          (7)

Every original exterior scalar is the same on both sides. This is a whole-source FIRST-trace-normal identity. It does not assert Z_e=z(e), equality of higher quantum normals, or permission to differentiate (3) as a generic identity. The audit retains nonzero individual normal defects of that false replacement.

There is also a specified eigenkernel lift. With h_s(q) the ACTUAL normalized generic descendants in the adapter theorem, put

O_e^h=(T_1-q T_-1)diag(h_s(q)), q=q0 exp(epsilon).

Both its bands lie in the same generic descendant family, so C_e(O_eh)=lambda(e)O_eh exactly and lambda(0)=z_0. Using its own actual coherent normalization in (7) therefore gives the same first trace normal. This option changes the generic boundary germ; no ordinary coupon or bare mark is silently substituted.

The finite q0 in (2) is essential, not a disposable phase. Exactly

O_N=(1+q0)/2 (T_1-T_-1)
                     +(1-q0)/2 (T_1+T_-1).          (8)

Without it, the coherent normalization would be (1-t)d-td, which is exponentially small in the interior and zero at t=1/2. The actual O(1/N) normalization comes from the retained quantum coefficient. Endpoint terms in (2) also remain exact.

3. The actual routed momentum and its logarithmic form

This section concerns the proved regular geometric chart of the certified complete source. Its local nonzero amplitude and framing form are those of the source Ward density theorem. No geometric assertion for an arbitrary algebraic channel is inferred.

Write A’=A U^-1 and B’=U B. The inserted head is literally the FIRST physical slot at this new level, so its lower-prefix adapter is the identity. Its meridian is the ACTUAL Hurwitz-transformed meridian, not the old unbraided selected-wire matrix. Inserting E_(l+1,l) changes the first exposed output by +1 and the A’-side input by +1. The original boundary generating form gives +Log p_cut-Log p_e. The coherent row used here has p_cut=1. Thus the two actual local principal ratios are

T_1 : p_e^-1,                 T_-1 : p_e.            (9)

This is the same bounded-band source calculation as the physical port theorem, Section 6, now with the route and its physical prefix explicitly fixed.

For completeness a suitable noncommuting first-slot route exists in the certified complete-circle source. Choose a slice entering an actual circle box. Its two main meridians A,B have Tr A=Tr B=Tr BA=-2. They cannot both fix reference infinity: two upper triangular trace-minus-two parabolics have product trace +2. The positive route R_1…R_j makes its new first meridian V_j^-1 mu_j V_j, V_j=mu_(j-1)…mu_1, by the actual Hurwitz rule. If every routed candidate fixed infinity, induction would make every original mu_j upper triangular, a contradiction. Thus some recorded route has nonzero lower-left entry. This uses the original reference, main component and complete-circle geometry, not a free reframing.

In the same trace-two parabolic normalization as the port theorem, write that routed meridian and the cut frame as

mu_e=I+c[[b,-b^2],[1,-b]], c!=0,
F(r,s)=[[r,1-r],[s,-s]], s!=0,
R=r-sb, A_cut=(1+s)^-1.

Directly conjugating by F gives

p_e=(R-1)/R,             k-1=(c/s)R(1-R).            (10)

On its regular chart R!=0,1 and k-1!=0, the underlying original marked scalar density is

Omega_src=(c_D/s) dr wedge dLog A_cut, c_D!=0.        (11)

The factor c_D is independent of the framing point; the original marked source and transverse Gaussian normalization is in it. The full channel Gaussian, when included under its proved hypotheses, changes it by its retained nonzero factor, not an assigned unit.

That original marked row has entries 1/N, whereas (6) uses beta_a(t). Thus the literal new source retains the base weight

B_(N,t)(a)=N beta_a(t)/kappa_N(t).                  (11a)

It multiplies the corresponding two band densities. It is not globally absorbed into the framing-constant c_D: beta_a has its own coherent action as the first cut varies. At a legitimate first-cut localization one instead retains its ACTUAL nonzero local Gaussian factor. The following rational-form calculations concern (11) times the odd symbol; the full coherent source additionally keeps (11a), or that localized factor, and its original first-cut cycle.

The q-retained combination of the two classical ratios is exactly

p_e^-1-q0 p_e=(1-q0)+1/(R-1)+q0/R.                  (12)

At fixed s its product with the residual one-form c_D dr/s has primitive

(c_D/s)[(1-q0)r+Log(R-1)+q0 Log R].                  (13)

Its residues at R=1, R=0 and r=infinity are respectively c_D/s, q0 c_D/s and -(1+q0)c_D/s. On a FIXED first-cut fiber the coherent base weight is independent of r, so it multiplies all three residues unchanged; after a proved first-cut Gaussian the retained factor does the same. These are local meromorphic-form residues. They do not assert that the original period surrounds any of those divisors with a nonzero relative coefficient.

There is an exact noncancellation check for a SPECIFIED sample of this underlying residue. Use the canonical half-index clocks

A_a=q0 Q0^a, s_a=A_a^-1-1, Q0=q0^2, 0<=a<=d,
G_N(t)=sum_a beta_a(t)/s_a.                         (13a)

None of the s_a is zero. Since |A_a|=1 and A_a!=1, Re[A_a/(1-A_a)]=-1/2. Thus for every real 0<=t<=1,

Re G_N(t)=-1/2, |G_N(t)|>=1/2.

At t=1/2 the exact pairing a with d-a has A_(d-a)=A_a^-1 and equal beta weights, so G_N(1/2)=-1/2 exactly at both parities. Retaining the base co-source and its original 1/N averaging explicitly, the sampled fiber residue is

(1/N)sum_a [N beta_a(t)/kappa_N(t)]
                 *[-(1+q0)c_D/s_a]
        =-(1+q0)c_D G_N(t)/kappa_N(t)!=0.           (13b)

This is a finite algebraic sample of the UNDERLYING meromorphic form. It is not an identification with the actual quantum period’s residue, its first-cut pushforward, or a uniform packet outside a fixed chart.

At fixed framing point q0 tends to one. Put s2=k-1. Equations (10)–(11) give the leading odd form exactly as

(p_e^-1-p_e)Omega_src
            =c_D ds wedge ds2/[(1+s)s s2].          (14)

The UNDERLYING form (14) has double logarithmic residue c_D!=0 at s=s2=0, in the displayed order. This is not automatically the full coherent-weighted residue there: (11a) varies with the base clock, and an interior coherent localization is a different operation. Equivalently the physical two-position deck exchanges p_e and p_e^-1; its odd density times this odd observable is the nonzero deck-EVEN form (14). This is a statement about the specified leading form. A formal two-sheet trace cancels an odd-density part only when the actual two-sheet pushforward with its orientations is being taken; it is not automatically the original global cycle.

4. What this replacement does and does not settle

Equation (3) closes the source-preservation gap left in Section 7 of the older port theorem: the q-weighted odd observable is now an EXACT normalized source for the unchanged scalar. It does not replace the relative cycle by a field trace or determine its winding. The original two support cycles, their marked orientations and every endpoint term in (6) are still required in that comparison.

There is a separate uniformity issue at the resonant boundary. For r=N rho, with rho bounded away from zero and s,b bounded, the RATIONAL expression (12) has

p_e^-1-q0 p_e=(2/rho-pi i)/N+O(N^-2),
(p_e^-1-q0 p_e)/kappa_N -> 1+2i/(pi rho)             (15)

for an interior real coherent t. Here both parts of (8) have the SAME order. Separate O(1/N) errors in the two actual quantum band packets can alter the normalized limit (15). Consequently (15) is classical-symbol data, not a proved uniform quantum boundary-period kernel. A source-level joint expansion with sufficient cancellation control remains necessary there.

Finally this complete-source identity supplies neither a nonzero mixed-color packet nor a global contributing-cycle theorem for long fillings. Its exact finite algebra is uniform in fixed width; its geometric residue has the stated certified complete-source scope.
