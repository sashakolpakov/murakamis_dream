Independent audit of actual finite transport and the peripheral normal adapter

Proof audit, 27 October 2026, of the whole two-period transport construction. Its algebraic identities, singular-lattice treatment and actual-source scope pass. This audit does not identify the analytic two-nome period with a selected whole knot period.

1. The free lattice and its actual first-period ratios

On the four-coordinate Laurent domain, the maps sigma(e)=(1-z)e and tau(e)=(1-w)e commute: their base automorphisms commute and each fixes the other’s multiplier. Both positive maps are injective before specialization. Their rational inverses have the specified translated-factor poles. Thus the genuine invertible difference torsor is a localization, whereas the original free line with its vanishing maps is retained at norm zero.

Iteration gives t_n(z)=(z;Q)n for n>=0, and the stated reciprocal product for negative n. Directly splitting the product proves t(m+n)(z)=t_m(z)t_n(Q^m z) for all integers. These ratios depend only on Q,z; every sigma-periodic completion cancels from them. The Gaussian ratio of three factorial transports is its original rational Gaussian polynomial, with cancellation performed before singular evaluation.

Consequently the finite-word substitution changes no matrix entry: the original monomial, Cartan and framing characters, support, cup/cap, pivot, modified dimension, source and moment insertions remain exactly where they were. Products, finite sums and normal derivatives preserve the identity. This verifies an exact rational transport realization, rather than an identity of completed analytic state contractions.

At a primitive N-th root, the polynomial identity product_(j=0)(N-1)(1-Q_Nj z)=1-z^N proves sigmaN(e)=tau(e)=(1-zN)e on the marked commensurate base. The line map is not the identity. A Kummer norm-one gauge divides by nu with nuN=1-zN, and so cannot replace the original lattice on its zero divisor. The actual affine finite Ward source z^N/N is also retained. No primality or odd-N assumption is used here.

2. The whole peripheral source requires its adapter

Set t=M^2 and keep the SAME original phase lift on both dimension factors. The exact quotient is

C_N(Q,M)=d_N^tr/d_N=(1-t^N)/(t;Q)_N.

At a generic regular M it equals one at Q=Q_N. Near M=1 its regular continuation is obtained BEFORE evaluation:

C_N=(1+t+...+t^(N-1))/product_(j=1)^(N-1)(1-tQ^j).

The denominator is N at (Q_N,t=1), so the ratio is a regular unit there with value one. Holding M fixed and using D_Q=Q partial_Q gives

D_Q log C_N=sum_(j=1)^(N-1) j t Q^j/(1-tQ^j).

The sign is positive because this differentiates the reciprocal product. At N=2, Q=-1, M=1 it is -1/2. Therefore the two dimension lifts agree at the root but have different quantum-normal jets. The phase cancels only because its actual whole lift was kept the same on both sides.

Other norm-zero points may involve moving zero/pole intersections; the local regularity statement above is specifically near M=1. The note correctly retains the original rational lattice instead of asserting a global unit adapter across every such point.

Finally, the actual fixed-N power W=M^(2N) satisfies W->Q^N W under M->qM, while sigma fixes the independent dual coordinate w. Their root values agree, but their whole normal dynamics do not. Keeping the original d_N unchanged, as the construction does, avoids imposing a false off-root equivariant identification.

The separate fixed-n exceptional-slope theorem is consistent with this calculation: its rational-slope statement is a telescope over shifted arguments, not an off-root equality R^b=(z;Q)_(aN).
