Every positive-value non-CROSS sharp maximum has an elementary odd MAIN cycle

31 October 2026. Internally reviewed source/combinatorial theorem. Root and geometry completed full independent analytical and original-source scope reviews, PASS. This uses the reviewed full-fiber activation and stationary classification. It does not construct a representation or prove an absolute exponential phase gap. No cap collar is used.

1. Exact hypotheses inherited from the original maximum

Work with the actual certified simple-white marked plat and its original ordinary face targets 11, protected pair u,vu,v, and triangle pairing across MATCHING edges. Every MAIN edge has two incident triangles belonging to different ports. Let qq and the common positive ordinary weights LL be the data supplied by finite-multiplier and stationary classification. In particular every ordinary incidence is positive, the supported port/ordinary-face graph is connected, and each port is one of:

  • strict CROSS at δ=0\delta=0, whose incidence character is constant;

  • strict Green, with one negative half and overloaded ordinary middle oo, qe,o>1/2q_{e,o}>1/2, and

    Lo−Lp=Lx+Lysin⁡2(πδ),0<δ<12; L_o-L_p=\frac{L_x+L_y}{\sin^2(\pi\delta)},\qquad 0<\delta<\tfrac12; \tag{1}

  • a residual cap, with the two protected faces as one outer and the parent middle; its two ordinary incidences are outer aa and overloaded middle oo, with

    qe,a=ν,qe,o=1−ν,0<ν<12,Lo=La. q_{e,a}=\nu,\quad q_{e,o}=1-\nu,\quad 0<\nu<\tfrac12,\qquad L_o=L_a. \tag{2}

For a Green, the literal source character on its negative half is 11 at its overloaded middle and 00 at its outers; its other half has character 00. For a cap, the two supported ordinary characters differ by 11. This is the exact alternating monomer character, up to a constant row gauge. These statements are unchanged by original RIGHT reflection or outer exchange. No globally coherent signs are assumed.

Here is a direct source derivation of the character table. In the exact full-interval source, write αr=cr−σ\alpha_r=c_r-\sigma and retain every unused prefix and suffix factor. On a strict LEFT straddling window the prefix is positive and the lower-half suffix has sign (−1)M−j(-1)^{M-j}. The positive word expansion makes the coefficients of Ki,jK_{i,j} nonnegative for every sufficiently large level at a fixed strict spectrum. Thus, after retaining the common raw sign and omitted-half scalar, the varying sign is (−1)j(-1)^j. The original middle monomial is cd−jc^{d-j}, so this is exactly the character c↦−cc\mapsto-c, up to a degree-independent row sign. The common scalar is not divided out; at a sampled zero the source is zero. At the complete endpoint the same unused factors and tail coefficients are nonnegative, up to the common raw sign, giving the constant CROSS character. Literal row reflection gives RIGHT. The flat-cap formula gives the remaining character directly, including its raw sign at both parities. No geometric collar is needed for this table.

A supported four-cycle e−f−e′−g−ee-f-e'-g-e has sign sum

εe,f+εe′,f+εe′,g+εe,gin 𝑭2. \varepsilon_{e,f}+\varepsilon_{e',f} +\varepsilon_{e',g}+\varepsilon_{e,g}\quad\text{in }\mathbf F_2. \tag{3}

It is odd precisely when the two ports’ edge differences on f,gf,g differ. The test retains actual incidences and is invariant under all row gauges and common face signs.

2. Every strict Green gives an odd MAIN four-cycle

At a strict Green choose an ordinary outer aa. Such an outer exists: the two protected faces form a MAIN pair, whereas the two outers form a MATCHING pair. Write oo for the overloaded middle of the negative half. Both endpoints of the MAIN edge oaoa are ordinary. Equation (1) gives

Lo>Lp+Lx+Ly≥La. L_o>L_p+L_x+L_y\ge L_a.\tag{4}

Thus Lo>LaL_o>L_a also when the other outer or parent is protected. The original edge oaoa has a second triangle at a different port e′e'. The chosen Green has edge-character difference 11.

We show that the second triangle has edge-character difference 00. A positive Green half or a CROSS half has that difference 00. If it is a negative Green half with difference 11, its overloaded middle must be oo or aa: a MAIN edge joins a port middle to an outer. The two possibilities are impossible:

  • overload at oo would put two distinct loads greater than 1/21/2 on the same original demand-one face;
  • overload at aa would require La>LoL_a>L_o by its own version of (4).

If the second triangle belongs to a residual cap and has both ordinary endpoints o,ao,a, it is the cap’s ordinary output edge. Equation (2) would require Lo=LaL_o=L_a, also impossible. These exhaust the classified ports. Thus the actual radial four-cycle e−o−e′−a−ee-o-e'-a-e is odd.

The conclusion needs only (1), the exact sign table, original MAIN typing and face demands. It does not use a Green collar or representation volume.

3. Residual caps also give an odd MAIN four-cycle at positive value

At a cap ee let o,ao,a be its ordinary output pair. They have equal positive weights and opposite incidence characters. Their MAIN edge has its other triangle at e′e'. A noncap triangle cannot have opposite characters on this edge: a negative Green would require one of the equal weights to exceed the other strictly, while positive and CROSS halves have equal characters. Therefore the cycle is odd unless e′e' is itself a cap on the same pair.

The latter case is incompatible with a positive-value maximum. Both caps have all their supply on the same two ordinary faces. The second cap cannot overload oo, since ee already supplies more than 1/21/2 there; it must overload aa. Write the two parameters as ν\nu and ν′\nu'. Face demands imply

1−ν+ν′≤1,ν+1−ν′≤1, 1-\nu+\nu'\le1,\qquad \nu+1-\nu'\le1,

so ν′=ν\nu'=\nu and both demands are saturated by these two rows. Ordinary incidence positivity excludes every other port from both faces. The two cap ports have no other ordinary incidence. They and the two faces form a whole connected component of the supported graph. Connectedness then forces the entire source to consist of these two caps, whose sharp entropy is zero. This contradicts the positive-value hypothesis, in particular a global maximum at or above ΦD>0\Phi_D>0.

There is an independent literal sphere check of the exceptional geometry. After locally naming the protected pair u,vu,v, the first cap triangles are (u,v,a)(u,v,a) and (u,o,a)(u,o,a), paired across uaua. If the other triangle on oaoa is another cap output, its protected vertex must be vv. Its input must then be (u,v,o)(u,v,o): choosing ordinary outer aa would repeat the first input triangle. The four triangles are exactly the boundary of a tetrahedron on u,v,o,au,v,o,a. Every edge is already incident to two of them, so connectedness of the triangulated sphere leaves no other triangle. This is not a claim that the tetrahedral source cannot exist; its all-cap action is simply zero.

Thus every residual cap at the positive-value data in §1 has its own explicit odd MAIN four-cycle as well.

4. Consequence and exact remaining limitation

At every unrestricted global maximum, or competing strict-NC maximum of value at least ΦD\Phi_D under the stationary theorem’s hypotheses, each non-CROSS port has an elementary odd supported four-cycle. In particular there is no common solution

εe,f=γf+κe \varepsilon_{e,f}=\gamma_f+\kappa_e

unless every port is complete CROSS. In the restricted non-CROSS problem, this statement requires at least one middle load strictly greater than 1/21/2; it makes no assertion about a seam-only constrained maximum. A phase-coherent maximum therefore belongs entirely to the original complete endpoint problem. Its value is at most ΦD\Phi_D, and the endpoint unique-max theorem identifies equality when that comparison is used. A competing non-CROSS maximum of high value is necessarily phase-inconsistent.

An odd cycle alone is NOT an absolute upper bound for the oscillatory source. On regular windows the reviewed cycle-packing theorem subtracts an explicit local phase loss from a positive envelope; comparing that result to ΦD\Phi_D remains necessary. Residual flat caps do not automatically satisfy a regular-window exponential angular gap. The exact flat-cap calculation exhibits this limitation. No full invariant or volume theorem is inferred.

Dependencies are the full-fiber boundary reduction, stationary classification, exact source expansion, and original MAIN/MATCHING dictionary. The flat-cap companion retains its exact raw coefficient and phase. Root proposed the general Green argument.
