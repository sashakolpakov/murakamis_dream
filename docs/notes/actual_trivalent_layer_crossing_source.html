Every original crossing has a two-Newton, one-Rogers source layer

28 October 2026. The nine original scalar atoms admit a complete partition into three disjoint trivalent sinks. Two sinks are the PROVED Newton/Barnes template; the third is its actual all-U Rogers polynomial. A positive Gaussian must first be combined with its adjacent reciprocal factorial. A negative Gaussian stays ONCE and is shared by its indicated Newton and Rogers variables.

This yields an exact one-internal-variable crossing kernel WITH its original final nonnegative Hardy barrier, then a simultaneous layer on any signed word and any bridge count. Each Newton kernel can be replaced by its explicit two-full-Psi source plus its retained central current. No positive-factorial edge, Gaussian or original cap is used twice. The resulting current branches are not classified as geometric degeneracies.

1. Fixed kernels and original normalization

Put Q=exp(2pi*i/N), d=N-1, F_a=(Q;Q)_a and

p_a=(-1)^a Q^(a(a-1)/2)/F_a,
U(t)=sum_(a=0)^d t^a/F_a,
V(t)=sum_(a=0)^d p_a t^a,
F(t)=sum_(a=0)^d F_a t^a.

These finite expressions are used to prove the source identity. Their original full-Psi coefficient/relative-cycle realizations may be reinserted exactly; they are not replaced by a new positive invariant. Keep the original color x, not only its complete value. Define

B_N(u,v)=sum_(i+j<N) F_(i+j)/F_i *p_j u^i v^j,
R_N(u,v)=sum_(i+j<N) F_(i+j)/(F_iF_j) u^i v^j.          (1)

B_N is the proved Newton polynomial. R_N is the actual truncated Rogers–Szego generating kernel; it is not assumed to satisfy the same beta theorem. All factorial normalization is already contained in the coefficients in (1). No residual N^3 is to be inserted after the three original factorials have been contracted this way.

Let the two input variables of one crossing be z_0,z_1 and its outputs z_6,z_7. The four original internal head vertices after Hardy-forest contraction are z_2,z_3,z_4,z_5. Write t=z_4. The original external Cartan scalar kappa remains external. Write Pi_+^(6) for the original nonnegative Hardy projection on the first OUTPUT variable z_6. It is independent of N. This barrier is retained in the physical crossing and in every separated branch below.

2. Literal nine-atom table, including the Gaussian placement

The original atom slots, in traversal order, are as follows. The table displays the finite coefficient kernels; every F carries its actual factorial support.

Here a Gaussian is still represented by its ORIGINAL moment

g_s=-x^(-2s)exp(-s h/2)exp(sqrt(pi)(1+i s)xi),
sqrt(N/(2pi))exp(-Nxi^2/2)d xi,
h=2pi*i/N, s=+1 or -1.                                (2)

The original diagonal entries are

d_a=(-1)^a x^(-2a)Q^(a(a-1)/2).

For R_+ the active Gaussian index is the slot-5 reciprocal index h_5, hence its full exponent is g_+^(h_5). For R_- the Gaussian precedes the central transfer; the index is h_5+h_4, hence the two displayed g_- factors are the SAME Gaussian variable. This is a literal gate-order calculation, not two independently integrated Gaussians.

The four internal affine vertex equations are

h_0+h_1+h_3=d,
h_2+h_4+h_6=d,
h_3+h_4+h_5=d,

The first, second and fourth are sinks. Their atomic stars {0,1,3}, {2,4,6}, {5,7,8} are PAIRWISE DISJOINT and partition ALL nine atoms. The third equation is at the remaining source vertex t; its integration is retained.

The table follows either by coefficient conservation in the strict original word or by weighted Hardy-forest residues. At the Gaussian edge z_out=z_in/g_s, logarithmic residue measure has no extra g_s Jacobian. This gives precisely the two placements in the table. Its original caps are unchanged.

3. Positive crossing: absorb one COMPLETE Gaussian cell

In the positive word C D U_+ C^(-1), the Gaussian D is adjacent to the reciprocal second-head factorial in C. Their exact combined diagonal coefficient is

d_a/F_a=p_a x^(-2a).                                 (4)

Thus the COMPLETE Gaussian-plus-slot-5 cell is exactly

V(x^(-2)z_5/t).

It has no remaining Gaussian variable or extra scalar. One first contracts that whole Gaussian cell, then uses the Newton identity. It is not a pointwise deletion of g_+ in an arbitrarily separated full-Psi sector.

Contract the three disjoint sinks in (3). The guaranteed first star {0,1,3} gives B_N(t/z_1,x^(-1)t/z_0). The last star {5,7,8}, after (4), gives B_N(x(-1)z_7/z_6,x(-2)z_7/t). The middle star is R_N(z_6/z_0,x^(-1)z_6/t). Consequently the ENTIRE raw positive crossing kernel is

Ktilde_+=kappa CT_t [
   B_N(t/z_1,x^(-1)t/z_0)
   R_N(z_6/z_0,x^(-1)z_6/t)
   B_N(x^(-1)z_7/z_6,x^(-2)z_7/t)].                   (5)

On the ORIGINAL finite input heads, the PHYSICAL finite-head crossing is K_+=Pi_+^(6) Ktilde_+. All upper support and second-output lower support are already enforced by the three factors; only negative first-output tails remain in the raw expression.

In particular the all-U middle star was not renamed Newton. The original Gaussian is accounted for by the exact identity (4), including its color x^(-2a) and linear root phase.

4. Negative crossing: one shared Gaussian, not two

For the negative crossing, stars {0,1,3} and {2,4,6} have the original U,V,F types and give Newton kernels directly. The last star is all-U and gives the Rogers polynomial. The exact raw negative kernel is

Ktilde_-=kappa^(-1)sqrt(N/(2pi))integral_R exp(-Nxi^2/2)
   CT_t [
   B_N(t/z_1,x^(-1)t/z_0)
   B_N(z_6/z_0,g_-x^(-1)z_6/t)
   R_N(g_-z_7/t,x^(-1)z_7/z_6)] dxi.                 (6)

On those same finite input heads, K_-=Pi_+^(6) Ktilde_-. For example the raw kernels, on input (0,0), have a nonzero output (-1,1) from h_7=1, with coefficient kappa(+/-1)x(-1). The final original Hardy barrier removes it. Thus (5)–(6) are not asserted entrywise finite-head operators without that barrier.

The two appearances of g_- are linked by the SINGLE Gaussian measure (2). Keeping only one or integrating each independently changes the original phase. No remaining Rogers sink is declared a Newton move without a further proved transformation.

One finite coefficient proof checks (5)–(6) directly. On an input (a,b), put h_1=j, h_2=a-j, h_5=b+j-h_4 and output (c,e)=(h_2+h_4-h_7,h_5+h_7). The first Newton coefficient is

F_(b+j)/F_b *p_j x^(-j).

The second chosen Newton and the Rogers factor supply exactly the remaining original F and reciprocal F coefficients. Their Gaussian powers are h_5 for the positive crossing before (4), and h_5+h_4 for the negative crossing. Illegal original indices have zero coefficient. This proves the entire matrix equality, not merely a scalar cap evaluation.

5. Simultaneous exact layer on an arbitrary original word

For m signed crossings, select the TWO Newton stars specified in (5) or (6) at each crossing. They are disjoint inside each crossing and occur at distinct internal sink vertices in the original ordered word. Spectator/Hardy contractions cannot merge one of these sinks with a later crossing: it has only its three atomic incidences and its affine charge -d. Thus there are exactly 2m independent Newton contractions, one remaining Rogers polynomial and one remaining internal t variable per crossing. Every F edge is used exactly once.

Perform the positive Gaussian contraction (4) before expanding any new beta sectors. Retain one Gaussian per negative crossing. Glue the output and input head variables with the SAME original Hardy/source identifications and pair with the SAME original cups, ordinary caps, full marked cap, and external peripheral/framing line. Equations (5)–(6) then give the original whole period. This is valid for any bridge count and the whole original nonzero color x family at the fixed physical root.

At each chosen Newton sink use its exact original-source identity

B_N(u,v)=S_N(u,v)+D_N(u,v)I_N(u,v),
D_N=1-u^N+v^N,
S_N=(N/phi_N)u^N Psi_+(h-Logu)Psi_+(Log(v/u))
                                  exp[-Log(v/u)Logu/h]. (7)

This is the proved beta source with the exact Fermat normalizer, not bare replacement of a factorial coefficient by its symbol. Multilinearity gives at most 2(2m)=4m source branches, with all Rogers kernels, remaining Gaussians and original caps retained. Expanding each D into its three monomials would give at most 4^(2m) terms; keeping it grouped is preferable. The number depends on the word, not N.

The branches live on specified logarithmic/relative source charts. They need not be separately single-valued scalar periods or independently absolutely integrable on an uncontinued original torus. Start on a common regular marked chart, use (7), and continue with its exact beta currents. Equivalently use common finite Gaussian cutoffs and matched pole indentations BEFORE summing the branches. Their sum is the original finite polynomial integrand, so its limiting period is fixed by the original absolute convergence theorem. No independent finite part is chosen for one branch alone. The joint deck/source gluing is part of the retained source extension; deleting current sectors would destroy it.

Thus this is an exact finite-branch whole-source NORMAL FORM. It is not a proof of termwise global growth estimates or of dominance of the all-S branch.

There is an optional whole-source reduction of the last Hardy barrier, but it has a different scope. The COMPLETE raw B/R kernel has only nonnegative incoming index demands, so it annihilates any negative input head. Its only unphysical output is a negative first head. The next crossing touching that head annihilates it; if no crossing touches it again, the original finite final source annihilates it. Therefore the raw complete crossings give the same whole-word source after summing their complete finite kernels. This no-healing argument must NOT be applied separately to the meromorphic S/I branches. The displayed branch normal form retains Pi_+^(6), unless that full-word source reduction has first been performed before splitting.

6. What the actual central monomials do

For the guaranteed first star the literal central function is

A=u^N-v^N=t^N[z_1^(-N)-x^(-N)z_0^(-N)].                (8)

It is a free scalar boundary function, not identically zero or one. At the other positive star it is

A_F=x^(-N)z_7^N/z_6^N-x^(-2N)z_7^N/t^N,

and at the negative central star it is

A_C=z_6^N/z_0^N-g_-^N x^(-N)z_6^N/t^N.                (9)

The original peripheral x^N and the Gaussian norm g_-^N remain. They cannot be evaluated at an ideal point before normal source operations. A separately chosen cyclic boundary-character extension with z_1N=xN z_0^N would make (8) zero, but that is not a specialization theorem for the original finite-head scalar source.

There IS an exact finite-input support statement. The original inverse fusion kernel after the first contraction is

U(z_3/z_0)B_N(t/z_1,x^(-1)t/z_0).                     (10)

Its monomial indexed by i,j,r has incoming exponents (r+j,i) and outgoing (r,i+j), with i+j<N. Multiplication by u^N demands incoming second index i+N; multiplication by v^N demands incoming first index r+j+N. Both are outside the original head. Therefore each inserted WHOLE-kernel tail in (10) is zero on every original finite-head input. This holds before closing a knot, for every original color.

It is a degree-tail annihilation, not a scalar value A=0 assigned inside (7). The separated full-Psi and Barnes kernels are not finite nonnegative head polynomials. They do not inherit this support assertion termwise. The exact constraint after splitting is instead the equal-sum statement

exterior[A S_N]+exterior[A D_N I_N]=0,                (11)

whenever the indicated original finite-head exterior is used. It is invalid to set A=0 in a generic inverse norm or to conclude from (11) that each current vanishes. Inputs outside the original head already detect the nonzero degree shifts.

Likewise the current monomials do not force a degenerate Hurwitz representation. On any regular logarithmic current branch they add to its action respectively

0,
2pi*i(Logt-Logz_1),
2pi*i(Logt-Logz_0-Logx).                              (12)

Their logarithmic momenta change by integer 2pi*i shifts, while their EXPONENTIATED canonical relation is unchanged. Thus they are lifted charge/degree corrections to whatever regular current graph is present, not automatic identity or rank-deficient classical maps. The known full-Psi deck mixing can put the same growing S class into I, so a current-only quotient cannot be imposed without transporting this source extension.

7. Audit and remaining obligation

Run

The audit checks the exact inverse-fusion kernel, both original signed/color crossings, two arbitrary Gaussian dilation values before moment contraction, the positive complete-cell identity, whole original pivotal word sources, the disjoint graph partition and the full-B degree-tail assertion. All original normalization factors are read from the independent original crossing evaluator.

To reach a global volume theorem one still needs to control the remaining Rogers fibers and the combined marked source branches, and show a nonzero geometric exterior coefficient or a suitable global norm gap. Positivity of a single local bipyramid does not give that gap, as the explicit original-source nongrowth chamber already demonstrates. The normal form above states exactly the actual network and currents to which such a proof must apply.
