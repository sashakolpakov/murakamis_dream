Entire adjacent source projectors and their lattice groupoid

Research note, 26 October 2026. On three strands, the two adjacent actual meridian projectors have a joint two-channel polar form. Its overlap is an explicit original pivotal cut moment. The remaining projector terms are regular at completeness. This determines their full local meridian germs, beyond the residue.

There is also a uniform obstruction: these two projectors cannot both preserve one finite meridian lattice. A comparison must retain their changes of lattice. This concerns the original ADO source operators, not a replacement quantum invariant. The selected geometric period and volume conjecture remain unproved.

1. Adjacent cups and caps give opposite source matrix units

Keep N>=2, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, rho=q-q^(-1), and

x=-M/q, u=Log M, R=C[[u]],
delta=rho(M^2-M^(-2)), nu=(-1)^N rho^2.

At M=1 use the actual cup C=vec(D^(-t)) and cap L=vec(gD)^t, where D_(N-1-a,a)=q(a2) and g=K^(1-N). Their two snakes are identities and LC=0. This follows directly by multiplying the two antidiagonal matrices: gD D^(-t)=I and D^(-t)gD=I. The remaining scalar uses x(1)(1-N)q((N-1)^2)=1.

On W_0=V^(tensor 3) put

i_1=C tensor I, i_2=I tensor C,
c_1=L tensor I, c_2=I tensor L.

The snakes and zero loop give

c_1 i_2=c_2 i_1=I, c_1 i_1=c_2 i_2=0.        (1.1)

Consequently (i_1,i_2) and (c_2,c_1)^t split two copies of V, and the two nilpotents Pi_i=i_i c_i are opposite matrix units. In particular

Pi_i^2=0, Pi_1 Pi_2 Pi_1=Pi_1,
Pi_2 Pi_1 Pi_2=Pi_2,
Tr(Pi_1 Pi_2)=N.                             (1.2)

Tensoring the other strands gives the same identities and trace N^(b-2). This is the loop-zero Temperley–Lieb relation derived from the actual cups and caps.

2. Integral numerators and the charged simple block

Let E_1,E_2 be the actual last Casimir projectors on positions 12 and 23 at generic M, and set T_i=delta E_i. They are rational matrices regular at M=1. To see this without evaluating a projector there, let Omega_i be the pair Casimir and let P_i be the integral projector onto the colliding c_0,c_last cluster. That unordered cluster is separated from the other roots at M=1; polynomial Bezout interpolation therefore gives regular P_i. Then

T_i=(Omega_i-c_0)P_i,
T_i^2=delta T_i,
T_i(1)=nu Pi_i.                              (2.1)

All these identities use the exact branch difference delta, not just its linear coefficient 4rho*u.

Use the charged color P=V_z, z=-M^3/q, required by the meridian charge calculation. Let S be the total-Casimir projector of W=V_x^(tensor 3) onto its z block. Its complete Casimir is -2 and its derivative in the central polynomial is (-1)(N+1)N2. Thus S is integral. This block is P with multiplicity N. Each E_i S has rank one on that multiplicity space: the pair image V_(M^2) tensored with V_x contains P once in the explicit highest-vector fusion.

The remaining operators

F_i=E_i(1-S)                                  (2.2)

are integral idempotents. Indeed T_i(1)(1-S(1))=0 since Pi_i factors through the complete color V with Casimir -2. Their integral numerators T_i(1-S) are therefore divisible by delta in R. Their ordinary rank is N(N-1). This already isolates all poles in the charged block, uniformly in N.

3. The exact overlap is an actual cut moment

Normalize the highest vector of the last two-color branch by

h=sum_(a=0)^(N-1) h_a v_a tensor v_(N-1-a), h_0=1,
h_(a+1)/h_a=-e_(N-1-a)(x)Q^(N-a-2)/[e_(a+1)(x)x].

Its coefficients are units near the complete color where needed. The covector vanishing on the F-descendants of all other branches has unnormalized coefficients

lambda_a=(-1)^a x^(-a)Q^(a(a-1)/2).

This follows from lambda_(a+1)+x(-1)Qa lambda_a=0, the two entries of the actual total F at degree N-2. Its normalization is Z=sum_a lambda_a h_a. On the total highest vectors h tensor v_0 and v_0 tensor h, the cross-projection coefficients of E_1 and E_2 are both lambda_(N-1)/Z. Only the component of pair degree N-1 can contribute; the others have zero last-branch projection. Thus the equality of these two coefficients is exact.

The finite calculation in the appendix gives, with r=x^(-2),

Z=(1+r^N)(1-rQ^(-1))/(1-r^2 Q^(-3)).

Using r=Q/M^2 and lambda_(N-1)=(-1)^N q M^(1-N) yields

delta*lambda_(N-1)/Z=A_N(M),
A_N=(-1)^N rho (M+M^(-1))
             *(qM^2-q^(-1)M^(-2))/(M^N+M^(-N)). (3.1)

The complete value is A_N(1)=nu. In particular A_N is a unit of R. Its full MN+M(-N)=M^(-N)(1+U) factor is retained. Comparison with the independently proved original pair moment in Section 7 of the cut-density note shows

A_N(M)=Phi_pair(T_1),
A_N/delta=Phi_pair(E_last).                   (3.2)

where Phi_pair is the original scalar pivotal partial trace, before multiplication by the full modified dimension. Hence the overlap is fixed by the actual cut, with its phase and peripheral function, rather than selected by a model normalization.

4. A joint regular frame for the full polar source

Let theta=x(1)^(1-N). Define two P inclusions by highest vectors thetah tensor v_0 and thetav_0 tensor h and then the original total F descendants. Call them U_0,U_1. They are regular rational intertwiners and at completeness become i_1,i_2. The common theta is necessary to keep the original complete cup frame.

On their images, (3.1) gives the integral matrices

t_1=[delta A_N;0 0], t_2=[0 0;A_N delta].     (4.1)

Here and below scalar matrices act on the two-copy multiplicity factor, with identity on P. There are regular rational rows r_1,r_2 with T_1 S=U_0 r_1 and T_2 S=U_1 r_2. For example, extract them with any coordinate left inverse of the primitive U_i; the factorization makes them H-linear. Their values on U=(U_0,U_1) form

C=[delta A_N;A_N delta].

Its determinant delta2-A_N2 has value -nu^2 at M=1, hence is a unit. Set R=C(-1)(r_1,r_2)t. Then

RU=I, T_i S=U t_i R,
R(1)=(c_2,c_1)^t.                            (4.2)

This proves an exact joint normal form, retaining every meridian coefficient of the actual projectors:

E_1=U [1 A_N/delta;0 0]R+F_1,
E_2=U [0 0;A_N/delta 1]R+F_2.               (4.3)

The regular F_i annihilate S and the common source corner e=UR. Both E_i vanish on the remaining N-2 multiplicity directions of S. Thus no additional polar channel has been discarded. Tensoring the construction with the remaining colors proves it for b>=3, with P replaced by the charged P_b of the preceding note.

There is an intrinsic version of the source corner. On S put

G=A_N^2-delta^2,
e=[T_1T_2+T_2T_1-delta(T_1+T_2)]/G.          (4.4)

This is the same regular projector onto two copies of P. Its matrix units follow from

(t_1t_2-delta t_1)/G=E_11,
(t_2t_1-delta t_2)/G=E_22,
(t_1-delta E_11)/A_N=E_12,
(t_2-delta E_22)/A_N=E_21.                   (4.5)

Thus the integral numerators generate Mat_2(R) on the source multiplicity space. Their joint comparison torsor, with the charged P fixed, is R^times: End_H(P)=R and a simultaneous intertwiner of the full matrix algebra is scalar. This specifies the local torsor and its trace calibration on three strands. After tensoring, the unrestricted comparison group is instead End_H(P_b)^times unless the spectator action is also fixed. Identifying such a torsor with a triangulation realization still requires its actual source operators and overlap to be matched.

The subsequent spectral-source calculation places these formulas in a universal rank-four algebra. For two numerators with t_i^2=delta t_i, the expression z=t_1t_2+t_2t_1-delta(t_1+t_2) is central. On the cover A2=z+delta2, with A,z units, (4.5) are its explicit matrix units. The actual root is exactly (3.1). This is a spectral splitting and scalar-torsor reduction, not nilpotence of the full source group. Genuine pure braid powers have a connected SL_2 quotient; their invariant plane is not generally this particular two-cup corner.

5. Whole sewn cuts now have an exact source decomposition

For any integral whole knot operator B(M), let B_ij=R_i B U_j. The original calibrated rectangular trace identity gives

mathfrak_t_W(B E_1)
  =mathfrak_t_P(B_00)+(A_N/delta)mathfrak_t_P(B_10)
                                  +mathfrak_t_W(B F_1),
mathfrak_t_W(B E_2)
  =mathfrak_t_P(B_11)+(A_N/delta)mathfrak_t_P(B_01)
                                  +mathfrak_t_W(B F_2). (5.1)

These are identities of full meridian germs, not only residues. The P trace uses the original d_N(M^3), including the exact dimension ratio already recorded in the charged-source note. The full affine source coefficient 1+U^(-1) multiplies these entire identities and is retained before specialization.

The regular terms can cancel the source diagonal term. For example, on three strands the full cut of E_i itself is zero, while mathfrak_t_P(I)=d_N(M^3). Consequently

mathfrak_t_W(F_i)=-d_N(M^3).                 (5.2)

No positivity or noncancellation assertion follows from the nonzero source corner or its perfect bilinear trace.

There is a useful exact simplification only after retaining the whole affine coefficient. For c=1+U^(-1), equation (3.1) gives

c A_N/delta
   =(-1)^N M^(1-N)(qM^2-q^(-1)M^(-2))/(M^2-1). (5.3)

Indeed c/(MN+M(-N))=M^(-N). Thus the 1+U factor cancels in the full off-diagonal source, with its phase and monomial left explicit. Its pole is precisely Y-1, the meridian divisor in the actual normal-period construction. This is a rational identity before specialization, not a reset of a numerical factor at M=1. It does not identify the full global normal module or its selected knot period.

6. No one finite lattice can regularize adjacent projectors

From (4.3), on the source corner

Tr(E_1E_2)=N A_N^2/delta^2.

The remaining trace is regular, since it comes from F_1F_2. As delta=4rho*u+O(u^2) and A_N(1)=(-1)^N rho^2,

Tr_(W_b)(E_1E_2)
    =N^(b-2)rho^2/(16u^2)+O(u^(-1)).         (6.1)

The leading coefficient is nonzero in characteristic zero. If a full finite R-lattice were stable under both projectors, their product would be an integral endomorphism and its ordinary trace would belong to R. Equation (6.1), invariant under every meromorphic change of basis, contradicts this. This proof does not require H-linearity of the hypothetical lattice.

In fact the traces of (E_1E_2)^m have pole order 2m, so the algebra of both unscaled projectors is not contained in a finite integral endomorphism order. Multiplying either projector by a meridian unit, including a peripheral factor with nonzero complete value, does not remove this obstruction.

Each projector separately has an elementary stable lattice. For the source pair, replacing P direct_sum P by delta^(-1)P direct_sum P gives the matrices

E_1 -> [1 A_N;0 0],
E_2 -> [0 0;A_N/delta^2 1].                 (6.2)

Thus one elementary change shifts the pole into the adjacent operator with order two. The appropriate retained data form a groupoid of lattices and their source maps. The integral numerators T_i and their polar quotients must stay distinguished. This does not disprove a relative triangulation comparison with different lattices on its charts. It rules out a common finite regularization of these actual source arrows.

7. What remains and exact verification

The entire local adjacent polar source is now explicit, with its charged object, overlap, trace, regular terms and lattice obstruction. This removes the restriction of the earlier sewn calculation to a residue. Its common comparison group is scalar only after the ordered integral numerators have been matched.

These R-unit inversions are fixed-level meridian statements. The value A_N(1)=(-1)^N rho^2 tends to zero as N grows, so they are not automatically unit inversions in the joint quantum–meridian completion S=C[[u,h]] used for the point-source model. A comparison of those two realizations must retain this additional lattice information. No cross-level norm bound is inferred from the frame.

The subsequent uniform Frobenius source base now resolves this coefficient degeneration on a specified meridian chart. It retains w=M^N and the root phases, records the exceptional rho line, and extends the normalized two-channel algebra. Its marked Frobenius period also evaluates the full original cap normalization. This concerns the coefficient algebra; the full color object and selected knot period have not thereby been extended across levels.

The full meridian atlas still contains its specified transitions, and their whole-germ cancellation must be compared with the relative triangulation source. The actual selected section has not been identified with the marked geometric period. Neither the formal normal-block comparison nor (5.1) proves the regulator norm across levels. The volume conjecture remains unproved.

The finite sum in the appendix has existing coverage as the first q-Chu–Vandermonde identity, DLMF 17.6.2. The formula and scope were checked after deriving our independent certificate. No published proof technique is imported. The cup/cap relations and matrix-algebra facts are existing elementary theory; no novelty is claimed for them. The calculation here fixes the actual overlap, charge and pole data in the original conventions.

The audit checks the generic telescoping certificate and the universal two-by-two relations symbolically. Six complete original tensor cases verify the opposite cup/cap units and vanishing of the regular-remainder numerators. Twelve generic pairs of actual full projectors verify the charged inclusions, retractions, overlap, regular idempotents and their ranks and full modified cuts. Forty-eight full tensor-cut decompositions for the identity and figure-eight words check (5.1), with original framing and normalization. Even and odd composite levels are included. Results.

Appendix. An independent finite-sum certificate

From the actual highest-vector recurrence, at the primitive root Q,

Z=sum_(k=0)^(N-1) [(r^(-1)Q^2;Q)_k/(r;Q)_k]
                                  *(r^2 Q^(-3))^k.

For a generic base Q define

f_(n,k)={n choose k}_Q (a;Q)_k(c Q^k;Q)_(n-k)
                                *(-c/a)^k Q^(k(k-1)/2).

Let t=Q^n, z=Q^k. The ratio f_(n+1,k+1)/f_(n+1,k) is

R(z)=-(c/a)(z-Q t)(1-a z)/[(1-Qz)(1-cz)].

Set

H(z)=-Q t(1-z)(1-cz/Q)/[z(1-Q t)(1-c t)].

Direct rational simplification gives

R(z)H(Qz)-H(z)
  =1-(1-(c/a)t)(1-Q t/z)/[(1-Q t)(1-c t)].

Multiplication by f_(n+1,k), followed by summation, telescopes; the two boundary terms vanish. Hence F_n=sum_k f_(n,k) satisfies F_(n+1)=(1-cQ^n/a)F_n and F_0=1. This proves

sum_(k=0)^n [(Q^(-n);Q)_k(a;Q)_k/
                 ((Q;Q)_k(c;Q)_k)](cQ^n/a)^k
   =(c/a;Q)_n/(c;Q)_n.

Specialize n=N-1, a=r(-1)Q2, c=r after proving this rational identity. The factors (Q;Q)_k are nonzero for k<N, and Q^(-n)=Q cancels the matching numerator and denominator. Thus

Z=(r^2 Q^(-2);Q)_(N-1)/(r;Q)_(N-1)
  =(1+r^N)(1-rQ^(-1))/(1-r^2 Q^(-3)).

All cancellations are whole rational identities. At the complete color the displayed final denominator is nonzero and Z has its required simple zero. No infinite product or root asymptotic is used.
