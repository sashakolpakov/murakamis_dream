Independent audit of pointed relative Ward localization

Audit, 27 October 2026. The proof in ward_source_localization.md is sound as a boundary/source homotopy statement. No crossing, coproduct, Hopf-pivotal, or multiplicative compression is claimed or supplied. No new matrix tests were needed.

1. Source normalization and the chain maps

For a nonempty retained set of size mm, the formulas ψS=Pψ,ϕS=(N/m)ϕP\psi_S=P\psi,\ \phi_S=(N/m)\phi P give ϕSψS=1\phi_S\psi_S=1. Hence eSe_S is rank one, idempotent, and has trace one. The new row and the original row both take value one on INI_N. Therefore their difference equals that difference composed with hNδNh_N\delta_N, exactly as claimed. This requires the matrix Ward differential; it does not descend to a homotopy in the color relative complex alone.

The compressed KK eigenvalues are distinct. Every skip coefficient is a product of 1−Qr1-Q^r with 1≤r<N1\le r<N; it is nonzero. Thus the retained path is connected and the split image Ward complex has kernel precisely kISkI_S.

For c1=δSc0hN,s1=δNs0hS, c_1=\delta_Sc_0h_N,\qquad s_1=\delta_Ns_0h_S, the identities c0iN=iS,s0iS=iN,c0s0=Ic_0i_N=i_S,\ s_0i_S=i_N,\ c_0s_0=I give the chain-map equations directly. In particular c1s1=δSc0(I−iNpN)s0hS=δShS=I c_1s_1 =\delta_Sc_0(I-i_Np_N)s_0h_S =\delta_Sh_S=I on the image complex. With the stated order H=(I−s0c0)hNH=(I-s_0c_0)h_N, one gets HδN=I−s0c0,δNH=I−s1c1. H\delta_N=I-s_0c_0,\qquad \delta_NH=I-s_1c_1. These calculations remain valid for m=1m=1, where the retained degree-one image is zero.

2. The relative sign and the whole normal

For total differential dR⊗I+(−1)pI⊗δd_R\otimes I+(-1)^pI\otimes\delta, p=deg⁡Rp=\deg_R, the induced Ward homotopy is (−1)pI⊗H(-1)^pI\otimes H. Its two dRd_R contributions cancel; its remaining terms are I⊗(δH+Hδ)I\otimes(\delta H+H\delta). The displayed relative sign is therefore correct.

The marked root α(U)N=U,α(1)=1\alpha(U)^N=U,\ \alpha(1)=1 exists uniquely over k[[U−1]]k[[U-1]]. Root orthogonality gives the specified regular idempotent and ϕS,U(f)=Tr⁡(f)−f(α)N−1. \phi_{S,U}(f)= \frac{\operatorname{Tr}(f)-f(\alpha)}{N-1}. For a degree-<N<N representative the trace is Nf0Nf_0; the carry fN−1(AN−U)f_{N-1}(A^N-U) is consequently retained.

There is a useful subtlety which the proof handles correctly: localizing CUC_U at A−1A-1 does not literally delete the entire marked branch over the punctured normal base. That branch has α−1\alpha-1 invertible after U−1U-1 is inverted. The new functional instead kills the marked summand as a whole and extends by zero there. It is regular on the retained summands, where A−1A-1 is a unit.

The extended KK-differences and interior FF-entries are units over the formal base, so the boundary Ward retractions extend regularly. An extra cyclic wrap does not change the interior reconstruction on the split image complex. This is not an internal Hopf extension.

3. There is an explicit obstruction to network compression

The composition defect is exactly c0(CD)−c0(C)c0(D)=LC(I−P)DJ. c_0(CD)-c_0(C)c_0(D)=LC(I-P)DJ. For retained i,ji,j and deleted dd, choose C=Ei,dC=E_{i,d}, D=Ed,jD=E_{d,j}. Both separate compressions are zero, whereas c0(CD)=Ei,jc_0(CD)=E_{i,j}. Thus even the most elementary excursion forbids treating the retraction as a local crossing replacement or tensor functor. The proof states this limitation explicitly.

The resulting representative is supported on the A≠1A\ne1 cut chart. Other geometric chart divisors and the internal Gaussian/source map remain separate.

4. Additional check of the off-root product and pivot moment

The two-normal distinction in the complete-color divided Ward note, section 7, also checks directly. In the generic balanced lift, ϵa=MQ−a−1−M−1,Fa+1,a=1−Qa+1. \epsilon_a=MQ^{-a-1}-M^{-1},\qquad F_{a+1,a}=1-Q^{a+1}. Subtracting adjacent diagonal products gives the usual ρ(K−K−1)\rho(K-K^{-1}) at every interior vertex. The absent last edge leaves exactly [ℰ,F]−ρ(K−K−1)=−(1−QN)(MQ−N−M−1)EN−1,N−1. [\mathcal E,F]-\rho(K-K^{-1}) =-(1-Q^N)(MQ^{-N}-M^{-1})E_{N-1,N-1}. Hence the root wall and the integral-color wall are different normals, even though the defect vanishes on both.

On the integral color m=N−1m=N-1, each of the b−1b-1 closed pivot ratios contributes q−Nm+2Najq^{-Nm+2Na_j}. The inverse-writhe twist ratio contributes qNmℓq^{Nm\ell}. Their product is exactly $$ q^{Nm(\ell-b+1)+2N\sum_{\rm closed}a_j}. $$ Knot parity makes its root value one. Its logarithmic derivative still contains the closed-position moment; there is no equality of off-root jets merely from the root scalar equality. The current notes retain that moment and use the genuine generic pivot before divided specialization, so no adapter is omitted.
