Octahedral belt stacks and their marked outer collars

29 October 2026. There is an infinite family of actual augmented link diagrams, with unbounded numbers of complete crossing circles, whose original Kashaev volume limits follow from the complete-circle source packet. The balanced case has a direct positive nodal-energy proof; the general case uses its coherent complex Gaussian. The operation is CYCLIC stacking along one actual twice-punctured crossing disk. It is not branching scalar Frobenius multiplication. The source, the cut collars, and their complete metric are specified below.

The later coherent boundary-packet theorem removes the balance restriction and allows every FIXED integer outer crossing power. Section 3a gives the literal collar certificate for this extension; section 4a links its original-source asymptotic. The filename retains the earlier balanced checkpoint, which remains the elementary positive special case.

1. The two typed pieces, before evaluating any invariant

Let C be the literal auxiliary tangle [2,1,1,2] with its third top/bottom endpoints joined on the right. Its two physical input and two physical output ports stay open. It evaluates to L_N, with the original ordinary pivot and no extra trace denominator. Let X_+ and X_- be the original single checked crossings. Use the genuine quarter-turn F, with the duality adapters of the physical turn theorem.

The positive piece D_+ is the two-strand tangle

D_+ = F(X_+ C) X_- .                                  (1)

It is obtained by opening the OUTER auxiliary circle of the verified two-octahedron source. Its other auxiliary circle remains closed. Its two disk-side boundary collars are the two standard twice-punctured disks used to stack two-strand tangles. Define D_- as the mirror of D_+, followed by the 180-degree rotation reversing its input/output direction. This is a definition of an actual diagram, not a polynomial selected from an invariant. The rotation carries its two physical punctures and the belt-boundary collar along with it.

The mirror of C is the negative crossing word [-2,-1,-1,-2], not a convention that silently keeps all four crossings positive. There is a direct unoriented/typed identification with the same crossing circle: draw its disk horizontally in D^2 times I around the two vertical physical strands. Reflection in the vertical plane containing those strands fixes the disk and its boundary AS SETS, and reverses the auxiliary boundary orientation. There is no curl or self-framing change. The reversed auxiliary is its genuine dual. For the complete balanced color its actual self-duality matrix is

U_(a,b)=x^(-b) delta_(a+b,d),   U=p/N,  U unitary.

Use the original dual cup/cap to identify that reversed closed leg; the two inverse U adapters and their original scalar normalizations cancel on the CLOSED auxiliary. Its pivotal factor is transported by this duality, not replaced by hand while leaving the crossings unchanged. No main-head conjugation remains. Hence the actual closed negative word has the same all-input operator L_N. This is the round crossing-disk reflection with its typed duality, not an inference solely from the real polynomial L_N. The focused audit also closes the negative universal-R word with the literal original pivot and checks its full matrix at five levels.

Here is the all-port source reading. Write z=F(R^-1 L_N), so z#=F(R L_N). The operator/source of the two pieces is EXACTLY

f_+ = z# R^-1,       f_- = R z.                       (2)

For f_+ this is the literal word (1), read in the source’s product order. For f_-, reflection sends R to R^-1, L_N to L_N, and F to the same real unscaled turn. It first gives z R. Input/output reversal is the genuine 180-degree rotation: it REVERSES the stacking order and acts on each Hopf box by F^2. The full physical turn theorem proves F^2 h=h on the complete pair algebra, so it gives Rz. This argument retains all cups, caps, Cartan phases and Casimir normal coefficients. It does not derive the reflected port reading by first commuting two scalar polynomials. The full original matrix audit separately checks the rotations and (2).

For any fixed word epsilon=(epsilon_1,…,epsilon_n), epsilon_i in {+,-}, stack these ACTUAL pieces in that order, retain ONE outer auxiliary circle C around their two physical strands, and take the original denominator/marked closure. Call this link L_epsilon. There is one inner circle per piece and one outer belt. Removing the circles leaves a stack of two-strand full twists: D_+ becomes X_-^2 and D_- becomes X_+^2. Its permutation is the identity. Consequently L_epsilon has n+3 components: two main components, n inner circles and one outer belt. No component identification is being inferred from an invariant value.

2. The literal disk cut and the geometric pieces

Use the labeled cut certificate of the two-octahedron base. Its two regular octahedra P_+,P_- have opposite labeled orientations and opposite vertex pairs

{X0,X1}, {Y+,Y-}, {Z+,Z-}.

The OUTER disk has shaded triangles

A=(X0,Y+,Z+),       B=(X0,Y-,Z-).

The other disk has C=(X1,Y+,Z-) and D=(X1,Y-,Z+). The original white identity face gluings join A+ to A- along all THREE edges. They similarly join B+ to B-. Thus

S_A=A+ union A-,    S_B=B+ union B-                   (3)

are the two actual thrice-punctured sphere boundaries after cutting the outer disk. The original outer A-to-B shaded pairings are the only face identifications removed by this cut. The inner C-to-D pairings remain. The resulting piece is connected and contains exactly two complete regular octahedra.

These pants are TOTALLY GEODESIC, not just intrinsically ideal triangulated surfaces. Every shaded face meets an adjacent white face at pi/2. Reflection in that white face therefore continues the shaded plane to itself. Across each of the three edges in (3) the two shaded triangles develop into a single plane. The metric on (3) is the complete ideal two-triangle pants metric.

This is the geometric reading of the actual collar opening used in (1): a twice-punctured crossing disk has three ideal boundary ends, namely its outer belt and its two physical punctures. Its two sides are S_A,S_B with these same three labeled ends. Restoring the collars is the inverse of the base’s proved disk cut. Therefore stacking the open diagram pieces joins an output copy of these pants to the input copy of the next piece. After the last piece, the single remaining outer circle closes this stack in a cycle. No additional complete circle is inserted at an intermediate seam.

The reflected/reversed piece has the same geometric certificate. On its cut octahedra, reflection exchanges P_+ and P_-. The input/ output reversal exchanges Y+ with Y- and Z+ with Z-, fixing X0,X1. This interchanges A and B, C and D, and the corresponding white faces. This is exactly the collar reversal in the diagram definition of D_-. It is an octahedral symmetry on the vertex labels, and preserves the three actual boundary-end labels after transporting them with the piece. Thus its geometric boundaries are precisely those used by the source reading (2).

3. Complete hyperbolic metric after an arbitrary fixed stack

Glue these geometric pieces by the actual cyclic collar maps. A three-puncture bijection is an isometry of the ideal two-triangle pants. In the present labeled construction it can be checked directly: the corresponding map of a shaded triangle extends to an octahedral symmetry. In normalized hyperboloid coordinates the ideal vertices are (1,+/-e1),(1,+/-e2),(1,+/-e3). The symmetry sends these light vectors EXACTLY, not merely their rays. Therefore it preserves the canonical horospheres and the two-triangle cusp sections. The same holds for the reflected/reversed collar maps above.

Each seam joins two geodesic boundary half-neighborhoods by an isometry, giving an ordinary smooth hyperbolic neighborhood. In the regular-cell certificate each boundary edge has angle pi on its side, so the joined angle is exactly 2pi. There is no edge cone angle or undetermined axis dilation. Matched canonical horospheres give Euclidean torus cusp sections of the actual diagram; shrinking them gives disjoint sections. There are finitely many cells and the remaining truncated core is compact. Thus every end contains a full horoball cusp and the glued finite-volume metric is complete.

This proves, for every fixed word epsilon of length n>=1, that the ACTUAL complement of L_epsilon is hyperbolic and

Vol(S^3 minus L_epsilon)=2n v8.                       (4)

The topology is obtained by reversing the literal disk/collar cuts, not by recognizing a census triangulation or matching a numerical volume. For all plus signs this is also the explicit cyclic cover obtained by taking n copies of the cut base and shifting the two outer A-to-B face pairings by one sheet. The metric proof does not need to identify mixed words with that particular peripheral cover.

3a. Any fixed integer outer crossing power

Let L_(epsilon,a) be the same literal stack, but insert X^a in the OUTER disk collar between the retained C and the first piece. Here a is any fixed signed integer. Its placement is part of the actual diagram, and its source is phi(L_N R^a product_i f_(epsilon_i)). The belt component is RETAINED; none of its cusps is filled.

A single X exchanges the two physical punctures of the standard twice-punctured disk. After passing to its complete ideal pants, this is the isometry that fixes the belt end, exchanges the two physical ends, and EXCHANGES its two ideal triangles. This last exchange is necessary: permuting two vertices in one fixed triangle alone would give the wrong oriented collar map. In the canonical labels of section 2, put the extra power at the last cyclic seam. Its exact face maps are

Parity of a Source to target Vertex map
even A_last^+ to B_first^-, A_last^- to B_first^+ X0 fixed, Y+ to Y-, Z+ to Z-
odd A_last^+ to B_first^+, A_last^- to B_first^- X0 fixed, Y+ to Z-, Z+ to Y-

This is obtained by tracing the two punctures through the actual single collar crossing. It is also the usual two-puncture half rotation of the disk. Its square is a full collar twist: as an ideal pants map it is isotopic to the identity, while the rotations at its ends record a change of the BELT PERIPHERAL MARKING. Thus adding two crossings changes that marking without changing the unmarked complete face quotient. Negative a has the same parity description with its opposite recorded twist. No preferred meridian is silently kept fixed while deleting R^a from the quantum source.

Every displayed triangle map still extends to an octahedral symmetry preserving the normalized light vectors. In the explicit eight-tet subdivision, a last odd seam toggles the target cell P_+ versus P_- while exchanging Y and Z. These two changes cancel in local coordinates: its gluing permutation remains 0132. All tetrahedra keep their positive shape i, all edge products are one and all edge angles are 2pi. The same canonical cusp sections therefore match, without dilation, at either parity.

The two main components of the untwisted stack merge exactly when a is odd: after the circles are removed, the physical two-strand permutation is that of X^(2(n_- - n_+)+a). Here n_+ and n_- count plus and minus pieces. Each of the n inner circles and the single outer belt remains distinct. Consequently

components L_(epsilon,a) = n+3 for even a,
                           n+2 for odd a.            (4a)

The exact cusp links of the modified cell quotient have precisely these counts. They are connected orientable flat tori; for an odd collar the former Y and Z main links become one. The compact-core and matched horoball argument of section 3 applies unchanged. We have proved the ACTUAL geometric statement

Vol(S^3 minus L_(epsilon,a))=2n v8
       for every fixed n>=1, epsilon and a in Z.     (4b)

This is a complete augmented exterior with its marked collar, not a Dehn-filled exterior or a volume-continuity assertion.

4. Balanced words give the full original volume limit

Suppose n=2k and the word has exactly k plus and k minus pieces. By (2), the actual fixed-root source is

Phi_epsilon,N = phi(L_N product_i f_(epsilon_i)).     (5)

The complete two-head Hopf commutant is the FULL algebra C[Omega], including its repeated-root normal directions. Consequently all the f_i commute there, and R^k R^-k cancels in the entire product:

product_i f_(epsilon_i)=(z# z)^k.

In an adjacent +- pair this is also the direct cancellation of the literal factors X_- X_+; the general reordered formula is a proved all-input source identity, not a claim that every different ordering is isotopic. The retained-belt theorem now gives

Phi_epsilon,N=N sum_c m_c |z_N(c)|^(2k)>0,
lim_N (2pi/N) log Phi_epsilon,N=4k v8.                (6)

All N, both parities and all composite levels are included. The complete-source normalization retains only its original unit framing/component phases (or the single recorded 1/N in the one-cut modified convention); it adds no modified dimension per circle. Combining (4),(6), the literal original reduced Kashaev evaluation satisfies

lim_N (2pi/N) log |<L_epsilon>_N|
  =Vol(S^3 minus L_epsilon)=4k v8.                  (7)

This proves a growing complete augmented LINK family. It does not prove arbitrary long fillings of these new bases, fixed knots, or arbitrary-bridge volume limits. The quantum gluing graph here is a cycle along ONE retained belt; a branched tree requires its actual six-port tensor and cannot be substituted into (5).

4a. Coherent source extension to every sign word and outer collar

For n_+ plus and n_- minus pieces put n=n_+ + n_->0 and e=n_- - n_+. The full commutant/source calculation, with the actual typed pieces (2), is

phi(L_N R^a product_i f_(epsilon_i))
              =phi(L_N R^(e+a) (z#)^n_+ z^n_-).      (8)

There is no absolute value inside this unbalanced channel sum. The coherent original belt-stack asymptotic proves its nonzero all-N asymptotic from the actual complex complete-circle boundary packet. It retains the full crossing phase, endpoint multiplicities, ordinary pivots and marked source. Its complex Gaussian has coefficient n-i(e+a), whose real part n is positive, so it never vanishes for these fixed integers. In particular

lim_N (2pi/N)log|phi(L_N R^(e+a)(z#)^n_+ z^n_-)|=2n v8. (9)

Applying the SAME explicit original reduced normalization as above and the geometric collar certificate (4b) gives

lim_N (2pi/N)log|<L_(epsilon,a)>_N|
                   =Vol(S^3 minus L_(epsilon,a))
                   =2n v8.                          (10)

The word, its length n and the signed outer integer a are fixed before N tends to infinity. Both parities and composite levels are included. The balanced positive source in (6) is a special case; the general theorem needs the coherent complex Gaussian and does not follow by taking magnitudes of individual channel terms. Arbitrary fillings of these new bases and genuinely branched multi-belt source graphs remain outside the statement.
