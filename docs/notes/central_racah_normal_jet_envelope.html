A polynomial normal-jet envelope for the central Racah sum

29 October 2026. This proves a quantitative bound for an EXPLICIT seven-factorial finite sum, including both low and high folded partners. The main terms have a common root phase; the terms activated by a second root zero have a uniform strict gap. Consequently four fixed normal jets cost only a polynomial relative to the known positive central marked partition. This is a new analytic estimate, not a numerical inference.

The finite algebraic bridge to the ACTUAL boundary Gram is now proved in the generic source identity: the original double sum equals this normalized Racah sum before root specialization, with all high partners and normal tails. Consequently the estimates here prove polynomial conditioning of the actual correlated boundary Gram on a fixed central corridor. The original mark and all-partner moment formula are those of the generic source theorem. No arbitrary six-port recoupling operator norm or whole-support bound is inferred.

1. Explicit object and the theorem

Write d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), zeta=q(1-Z)^(1/(2N)), with its analytic branch through q. Use symmetric quantum integers and factorials

[n]=(zeta^n-zeta^(-n))/(zeta-zeta^(-1)),
[n]!=product_(k=1)^n[k].

For an ordinary integer triangle a,b,c in 0,…,d put J=a+b+c and r_i=J-2j_i. Define the finite sum

S_abc(zeta)=sum_t (-1)^(d+t) [N+t]!
   /{[d+t-J]! product_i[t-j_i]! product_i[J-j_i-t]!}, (1)

where max(a,b,c,J-d)<=t<=min(a+b,a+c,b+c). Every factorial index is nonnegative. Put

D_abc^2=[r_1]![r_2]![r_3]!/[J+1]!,
D_j^2=[j]!^2[d-j]!/[N+j]!,
Tcal_abc=(-1)^d product_i[2j_i+1]
                  *D_abc^2 product_i D_(j_i)^2 *S_abc^2. (2)

These are rational functions before removable zeros are peeled; no square-root normalization is part of (2).

Take folded labels l_i<=floor(d/2), with sigma_i=(d/2-l_i)/N>=0. Let r be their number of paired axes. An unfolded branch j_i is l_i or d-l_i on a paired axis, and l_i alone on a simple odd axis. Use the actual paired-circle character unit ell_j(Z)=L_N(c_j(zeta))/Z, explicitly defined in the generic source theorem. For I a subset of paired axes put

Hcal_I(Z)=sum_branches Z^r Tcal_abc product_(i in I)ell_(j_i)(Z),
mcal_I=(-1)^d(q-q^(-1))[Z^(r+1-|I|)]Hcal_I(Z).   (3)

There are constants delta_0>0, C,C0, independent of N and of the labels, such that whenever max sigma_i<=delta_0,

|mcal_I|<=C0 N^C tau_N(l),                     (4)

where tau_N(l)=Z_N(l)/N^(3-r)>0 is the PROVED positive marked top moment, with Z_N given by the all-channel marked formula. The estimate includes both parities and arbitrarily close paired neighbors of the odd simple axis. It is asserted for sufficiently large N; finitely many remaining supported labels can be absorbed in C0.

2. One common root phase in the main sum

Choose initially delta<1/20. Every branch has j_i/N in (1/2-delta-O(1/N),1/2+delta). For every term in (1), all SEVEN denominator factorial indices are <N. For instance t-j_i<=N/2+3delta N+O(1), and d+t-J<=N/2+5delta N+O(1); the other differences have the same strict margin. Also J+1<2N.

Separate (1) into t<N and t>=N. In the main part,

([N+t]!/[N])|_(zeta=q)=(-1)^t[N-1]! [t]!.

All denominator quantum factorials are positive at this root. Thus EVERY term of S_main/[N] has the SAME phase (-1)^d. Its absolute sum is exactly

Rcore_N(j)=N sum_(max j_i,J-d<=t<=min pair,d)
   s_t/{s_(d+t-J) product_i s_(t-j_i)s_(J-j_i-t)}, (5)

where s_u=product_(k=1)^u 2sin(pi k/N). This is a genuine positive finite sum for the unfolded as well as folded labels. Its support is invariant under t->J-t. Sine-factorial reflection also gives

Rcore_N=sum_t s_t s_(J-t)
                      /product_i s_(t-j_i)s_(J-j_i-t). (6)

The restriction t,J-t<=d is retained in (5),(6); no alias endpoint has been silently removed.

On the common disk |Z|<=c/N^2, fixed-factorial zero removal and the bounded normal factorial estimate give a uniform constant envelope for each nonzero root product. Here the same fact follows directly: a nonzero factor has size at least a constant/N, while its change is O(|Z|); there are O(N) factors in each fixed factorial ratio. Their accumulated relative cost is bounded on this disk. The normalization zeta-zeta^(-1) and the removed root zeros cost only fixed powers of N.

Because the main root terms have a common phase, summing those envelopes costs at most a polynomial times the POSITIVE value (5). Cauchy’s estimate for any fixed order k<=4 adds at most C_k N^(2k). This step uses no cancellation estimate on the original B double sum.

3. The second-zero tail has a strict central gap

For t>=N, the numerator in (1) contains BOTH [N] and [2N]. After the first is removed, the term still vanishes at Z=0. After both are peeled, its root magnitude has, up to polynomial factors, numerator s_(t-N) instead of s_t. All denominator arguments stay physical by section2. Its continuous summand rate is therefore

Htail(s,a)=L(s-1)-L(1+s-S)
             -sum_i[L(s-a_i)+L(S-a_i-s)],       (7)

where s=t/N, a_i=j_i/N, S=sum a_i and L(u)=integral_0^u log(2sin pi v)dv. The tail has at most N terms.

At a_1=a_2=a_3=1/2 the tail collapses to s=1 and (7)=0. All arguments in (7) stay in [0,1] for its actual nearby support. Since L is continuous there, the supremum of the tail rate tends to zero uniformly as delta->0. This remains true at zero factorial indices; no derivative of L at an endpoint is being bounded.

The main profile from (6) is

H(s,a)=L(s)+L(S-s)
              -sum_i[L(s-a_i)+L(S-a_i-s)].      (8)

At the center it is 4[L(s)-L(s-1/2)], has unique maximum j0=v8/pi at s=3/4, and has strictly negative second derivative throughout its interior. A fixed compact neighborhood of 3/4 has a strict gap from its complement. Uniform continuity first localizes every nearby maximizer to that neighborhood; there every child argument is <1/2 and both parent arguments >1/2, so the cotangent second derivative is strictly negative. Symmetry then fixes the maximum at s=S/2. Its value is

Phi(a)=2[L(S/2)-sum_i L(S/2-a_i)].              (9)

It tends to j0 as delta->0. Choose delta_0 small enough that the tail supremum is <j0/4 and the main maximum >3j0/4. The difference is a UNIFORM positive gap. This argument is effective using the explicit continuous functions, but no numerical choice of delta_0 is needed for the existence theorem.

Fixed normal derivatives of the peeled tail cost only polynomial factors on the same disk. Even after the second zero is activated, the tail is exponentially below the main sum. Squaring S in (2) does not change that conclusion: mixed main/tail terms retain a strict gap from the main square.

4. All eight branch actions equal the folded marked action

Let x_i=S/2-a_i, i=1,2,3, and x_4=1-S/2. These remain in a compact positive half-angle strip in the central corridor. The normalized per-edge factor has the particularly simple root value

([N] D_j^2)|_q=(-1)^j,                         (10)

because [j]![d-j]!=[d]! and ([N+j]!/[N])|_q=(-1)^j[d]![j]!. It has no factorial exponential. An odd simple dimension [2j+1]=[N] supplies its own root zero. The remaining triple factor, after its one zero is removed, has rate sum_i L(2x_i)-L(S-1). Together with the main square (9), the FULL rate of (2) is

E=sum_(i=1)^4 [L(2x_i)-4L(x_i)]
 =-2sum_(i=1)^4[L(x_i)+L(1/2-x_i)].             (11)

Thus none of the dimension/triangle factors may be omitted from this comparison. At finite N set j_i=d/2-N sigma_i; replacing a_i by 1/2-sigma_i only changes the factorial asymptotics by polynomial prefactors, uniformly on this corridor. The four x_i are then the tetrahedral sign rows in the whole-label marked theorem. Its proof shows that (11) is SEPARATELY EVEN in every sigma_i.

An actual high-partner change j_i->d-j_i is EXACTLY sigma_i->-sigma_i. Therefore every unfolded main branch has the SAME exponential rate as the folded positive marked weight. The sine-factorial envelope, the positive interior saddle and at most N summands give

|[Z^k]{Z^r Tcal_abc product ell}|<=poly(N)e^(N E_fold),
                   k<=4.                      (12)

All apparent poles in (2) are removed by Z^r; in fact its main part starts at least at order Z because S has its first zero on every branch. Paired ell units have polynomial jets even at the nearest paired neighbor, since their nonzero root denominator is at least 2sin(pi/N). The second-zero tail is controlled by section3. These observations justify applying the common-disk Cauchy bound to the ENTIRE expressions in (12), not to a bare singular factor.

The proved positive marked partition has uniformly

tau_N(l)>=c N^(-3)e^(N E_fold)                 (13)

on a smaller central corridor. This follows directly from its positive local Laplace prefactor, with the actual odd/even half-step and the paired multiplicities; alternatively an interior nearest saddle term gives a sufficient polynomial lower bound. Combine (12),(13), sum the at most eight branches, and use the coefficient order in (3). This proves (4).

5. The proved finite source bridge and actual conditioning

The proved generic source identity for the B of the original nested cups and ordered duals is

B_abc=(-1)^J zeta^[a^2-b^2+c^2-ab-ac+bc-cd]
               *[a]![b]![c]!/[J+1]! *S_abc.    (14)

It is established by the actual mixed-highest Casimir recurrence, the literal source coefficient COLUMN, the uniquely normalized finite polynomial eigenproblem and an own finite Sears conversion. The exceptional c=0 Jacobi diagonal is computed by the ACTUAL cup/cap projection; it is not obtained by canceling its identically zero Fricke factor. Polynomial continuation in X=Q^N includes the high partners. No published quantum-6j formula is a premise. An independent analytical review and fresh generic/vector certificate replay passed.

Here is explicit normalization bookkeeping when substituting (14) into the proved physical trace. Its F-factor prefactor is

(-1)^d zeta^E/(1-Q) F_r1 F_r2 F_r3 F_(J+1)
      product_j [F_(d-j)(1-Q^(2j+1))/F_(N+j)],
E=d(3a+2b+3c+3)-4a^2-4c^2+2ac-2J.

Write D(a,b,c)=a2-b2+c^2-ab-ac+bc-cd and T_n=n(n-1)/2. The two B prefactors, for (a,b,c) and (c,b,a), have total sign (-1)^(2J)=1. All (1-Q) factors cancel, and the remaining zeta exponent is IDENTICALLY zero:

E+D(a,b,c)+D(c,b,a)+sum_i T_(r_i)+T_(J+1)
         +sum_j[T_(d-j)-T_(N+j)+2j]=0.

The factorials left over are precisely Tcal in (2). This is an identity before specialization, not an equality modulo a root character. Hence the actual marked moments equal (3), including all lower moments and normal tails. Equation (4) proves

max_I |m_I/tau_N|<=poly(N),
K_j=sum_I |coeff_I q_j^(1/2)|^2<=poly(N)        (15)

uniformly throughout a fixed central corridor, at both parities, with simple axes handled by the same formula with their absent paired variables. In particular log K_j=O(log N)=o(N) there. The second implication is immediate from the finite binomial polynomial of degree <=3 in the actual marked boundary frame. Source and co-source costs remain the exact K_j and tau_j^2 K_j from that theorem. No recoupling operator norm or whole-support conditioning follows from this central result.

The diagnostic original moment implementation uses the already proved generic source/cut formula, not (14), to measure the actual moments. For nearest paired central labels K is 1.000043544… at N=128 and 1.000011164… at N=256. Macroscopic central tests remain bounded, while some saturation-boundary tests grow slowly. Those finite measurements guided the proof but are not substituted for the proved finite bridge or a whole-support estimate. The data include polynomially small normal steps and precision growing with N; further paired-precision validation is kept separate from a theorem claim.

6. Actual-source diagnostic and paired precision

The diagnostic audit evaluates the PROVED generic physical double-sum trace, groups every low/high partner before extracting the physical marked normal, and uses no instance of (14) in its measurements. It compares normal steps N^-8 and N^-8/2 while increasing precision by seventy digits. Its saved results are labeled PASS_DIAGNOSTIC, not a proof of (15).

The refined values include:

N folded triple precisions K_j

The maximum difference of normalized moments is below 1.3e-16 in the smallest case and below 3e-23 in every other listed case. The independent positive tau formula checks the top moment at the same normal-step accuracy. The boundary example at N=129 has delta=0; it is outside the central theorem and illustrates why a uniform constant bound is not inferred from nearest-center samples.

These tests establish numerical consistency of the original moment implementation at the stated parameters. The finite transformation (14) is proved separately; the sampling itself does not control all q-jets or establish any whole-support conditioning exponent.
