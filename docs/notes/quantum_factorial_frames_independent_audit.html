Independent audit of the actual quantum-factorial frames

Audit, 27 October 2026. quantum_factorial_frames_and_cyclotomic_source.md passes the uniform frame-period, marked primitive, conditioning and eigenflag-current checks. The exact balanced-source comparison in integral_color_global_q_difference_source.md also checks.

1. Exact frame orientation and actual source

The basis identity pj=Rjfjp_j=R_jf_j implies Ap=R−1AfRA_p=R^{-1}A_fR; the printed coupon transformation has that orientation. The positive factors are the exact ratios of q-falling to ordinary falling factorials.

Relative to the original balanced convention (ρ2E,F)(\rho^2E,F), this is a common Hopf rescaling, or equivalently the indicated weight-dependent change of integral lattice. The ordinary integral generators and the literal balanced generators need not both have the same regular q=1q=1 lattice. This distinction cannot be omitted from a comparison of source jets.

The reciprocal polynomial chart JnJ_n gives the stated global q-difference formulas. A particular old balanced Weyl map, if used, must additionally be conjugated by its actual diagonal frame. It need not equal the bare reciprocal matrix in the new coordinates.

4. Scope of the valid interpolation

The exact ordinary integral-color closure uses KK and its ordinary twist. The frozen root closure has its different whole-clock adapter, explicitly retained in both notes. The root pivot is not an off-root pivotal element merely because the module quotient exists.

The smooth principal shear alone does not identify its literal pullback with finite star transport. A specified diagonal matrix conjugation does give an exact inner star automorphism, with its source transformed too; it does not construct a global geometric A-branch. Exponential frame conditioning, actual inverse-eigenflag marking, endpoint currents and source coupons remain. The nearby-principal-root resonances of the actual scalar color further prohibit a uniformly nonvanishing positive WKB approximation along the whole real path.
