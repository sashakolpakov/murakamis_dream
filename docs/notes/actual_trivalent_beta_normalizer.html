Exact normalizer of the original trivalent source

28 October 2026. The central factor of the original balanced trivalent source is determined exactly. It is not an arbitrary periodic multiplier or an assumed tame normalization. The proof uses its actual finite source, the two root shifts, the zero of the full function, and global analytic rigidity on the central projective line.

With the definitions and logarithmic conventions of the exact beta-source theorem, put h=2pii/N, Q=exp h and

phi_N=Psi_+(h)
     =sqrt(N)exp{i*pi[1/4-(N+N^(-1))/12]}.

The exact residue block in that theorem is

S_N(u,v)=A_N(u) exp[-Log(v/u)Log u/h] Psi_+(Log(v/u)).

Its normalizer is EXACTLY

A_N(u)=(N/phi_N) u^N Psi_+(h-Log u).               (1)

Consequently the complete original sink polynomial satisfies

B_N(u,v)=(N/phi_N) u^N
   exp[-Log(v/u)Log u/h]
   Psi_+(Log(v/u))Psi_+(h-Log u)
             +(1-u^N+v^N) I_N(u,v),                (2)

where I_N is the specified upward convergent Barnes contour in the beta-source theorem. Its uniform O(1) estimate in the positive boundary chamber is unchanged. In particular the factor u^N is compulsory and can have an exponential rate. It is not removed by setting a peripheral variable to a numerical special value.

1. The actual source defines the periodic normalizer

First work away from u=0 and u^N=1. Write u=exp l and choose ANY c_* with

exp(N c_*)=1-u^(-N), w=u exp c_*, u^N-w^N=1.

The original finite balanced polynomial defines

A_N(l)=exp(c_* l/h) B_N(u,w)/Psi_+(c_*).            (3)

Replacing c_* by c_*+h changes w to Qw. The balanced recurrence

(u-w) B_N(u,Qw)=B_N(u,w)

and the exact full-Psi step show that (3) is unchanged: the combined ratio is u(1-w/u)/(u-w)=1. Thus no root choice is suppressed in (3).

The simultaneous boundary recurrence of the SAME original polynomial is

w B_N(Qu,Qw)=(u-1)B_N(u,w)

on the balanced locus. Keep c_* fixed and replace l by l+h in (3). It follows exactly that

A_N(l+h)=(1-u^(-1))A_N(l).                        (4)

Psi_+(h-l) has precisely the same step. Therefore

R_N(l)=A_N(l)/Psi_+(h-l)

has period h and descends to a meromorphic function R_N(z) of

z=exp(N l)=u^N.                                  (5)

This descent is proved on the logarithmic cover by the exact finite source recurrences. It is not inferred from only integer samples of a false constant-monodromy interpolation.

2. No finite central poles except the checked collision

Every zero and pole of the original full Psi_+ has exp(N Z)=1. Indeed its zeros occur at Z=-m h-2piin and its poles at Z=(N+1+m)h+2piin, with m,n>=0; coincident points at a rational root have their actual multiplicities. Only their central location is needed here.

Alternatively this central-location statement follows from the independently checked zero-free open fundamental strip and the exact h shift: all newly introduced zeros or poles come from 1-exp Z and its h translates. No additional global divisor theorem is required for this proof.

For finite z different from zero and one, the central exponentials of the two denominator functions in R_N are

exp(N c_*)=1-z^(-1), exp(N(h-l))=z^(-1).

Neither equals one. The root-independent expression (3) is therefore holomorphic there. The balanced Fourier recurrence also makes B_N(u,w) nonzero, although nonvanishing is stronger than needed for the present pole exclusion. Thus R_N is holomorphic on C minus {0,1}.

At z=1 choose the representative u_0=Q^(-1), l_0=-h. If w tends to zero on the balanced locus, the first nonzero coefficient is

B_N(u_0,w)=[NQ/(1-Q)] w+O(w^2).

For example this coefficient follows by differentiating the finite products, or from their coefficient recurrence and geometric sums. The constant coefficient vanishes to order z-1 and is smaller than w. Also c_=Log(w/u) tends to the left end and Psi_+(c_) tends to one. Since exp(c_* l/h) tends to exp(-c_*) up to a factor tending to one,

A_N -> N/(1-Q).

The exact marked root value is Psi_+(2h)=phi_N/(1-Q). Hence z=1 is removable in R_N and

R_N(1)=N/phi_N.                                 (6)

This argument also covers N=2, using the logarithmic representative l_0=-h; no overlap of two open half-plane charts is required.

3. At the two ends the normalizer has degree at most one

These are FIXED-N asymptotics used for exact rigidity, not a semiclassical interchange of N and an end limit.

As z tends to infinity choose

w=u(1-u^(-N))^(1/N),
c_*=-z^(-1)/N+O(z^(-2)).

The first Newton factor is u-w=O(u^(1-N)); every subsequent factor is O(u). Thus all nonconstant terms of B_N(u,w) are O(1/u) or smaller, and B_N(u,w) tends to one. Moreover exp(c_* l/h) tends to one. The full function has a simple zero at zero, and its exact step gives

Psi_+'(0)=-Psi_+(h)=-phi_N.

Finally Psi_+(h-l) tends to one at the left end. Therefore

R_N(z)/z -> N/phi_N as z -> infinity.             (7)

At z tending to zero choose w tending to w_0=exp(h/2), for which w_0^N=-1. Choose l with bounded IMAGINARY part; its real part tends to minus infinity. Then

c_*=h/2-l+O(z).

B_N(u,w) remains bounded, since it is a finite polynomial with u tending to zero and w tending to w_0. Both c_* and h-l tend to the right end. The EXACT full-Phi horizontal asymptotic is

Psi_+(Z)=zeta_b exp[-Z^2/(2h)+(N+1)Z/2+pi*i*c_b^2]
                  (1+o(1)), Re Z -> +infinity,      (8)

uniformly on any bounded imaginary strip. It follows from the independently proved inversion and the left-end asymptotic. Here c_b=i(sqrt(N)+1/sqrt(N))/2. The bounded error O(z) in c_* changes the logarithm of (8) by O(z Log z), which tends to zero.

Substitute Z_1=h/2-l and Z_2=h-l in the denominator product of

R_N=exp(c_* l/h) B_N(u,w)
                /[Psi_+(c_*)Psi_+(h-l)].

Their logarithms have quadratic/linear part

-l^2/h+(1/2-N)l+constant,

whereas c_*l/h has part -l^2/h+l/2. The log-squared terms cancel and the difference is Nl+constant. Thus

R_N(z)=O(z) as z -> zero.                         (9)

Choosing an Nth-root logarithm of z in any bounded-width strip makes the estimates uniform in its argument. Periodicity (5) then removes that root choice. No central factor has been assumed small.

4. Rigidity fixes the exact answer

The function R_N(z)/z is holomorphic on the punctured central plane, extends across z=1 by Section 2, and is bounded at both zero and infinity by Section 3. It extends to a holomorphic function on P^1 and is constant. Its value is N/phi_N by either (6) or (7). This proves (1), hence (2), with every original source normalization.

The proof first determines a specific actual period and its divisor and end behavior. Periodicity alone would allow arbitrary functions of u^N; the global holomorphy and the two degree bounds are the extra rigidity that eliminate them.

5. The selected two-shape symbol and an actual open source class

Let x=v/u, y=1/u, l=Log u and c=Log x in the positive boundary chamber. BOTH x and y are upper. The exact shift gives

Psi_+(h-l)=Psi_+(-l)/(1-y).

The already proved uniform full-Psi strip symbol consequently yields

S_N(u,v)=(N/phi_N) sqrt[(1-x)/(1-y)]
           exp[W_res(u,v)/h](1+O_K(1/N)),
W_res=Li_2(x)+Li_2(y)-c*l+2*pi*i*l.                (10)

The roots and logarithms are the selected upper-strip ones. This is uniform, with every fixed derivative, on ANY compact positive boundary family, irrespective of the moduli of u,x,y. Its density is explicit and nonzero. No central periodic amplitude remains unresolved.

For comparison with the finite Newton critical action, the lower inversion identity implies

W_res=W_mark+pi^2/3,
W_mark=Li_2(v/u)-Li_2(u)+l^2/2-l Log v+pi*i*l.

The exact phi_N in (10) is retained. Its leading phase accounts for the further real -pi^2/6 shift, giving the marked finite-product critical value W_mark+pi^2/6. No imaginary normalization has been chosen to manufacture a volume.

In particular, on this entire chamber,

log|S_N|=N Lambda(u,v)+(1/2)log N+O_K(1),
Lambda=Im W_res/(2*pi),
delta_norm=max(0,log|u|,log|v|).                  (11)

If a compact family has Lambda>=delta_norm+eta with eta>0, the actual source identity (2) and its uniform Barnes bound prove

B_N(u,v)=S_N(u,v)[1+O_K(N^(-1/2)exp(-N eta))].    (12)

Thus its original geometric residue has coefficient EXACTLY one and its complete normalization current is exponentially smaller. This conclusion uses the original relative source and the exact norm factor, not just nonvanishing of a Hessian or a formal line. It is uniform for the actual O(1/N) Q-tag shifts in the original kernel.

This class is genuinely open and nonempty among positive bipyramids. For |u|=|v|=1, v=1 and Im u<0, the norm rate is zero while

Lambda(u,1)=-Im Li_2(u)/pi>0.

The strict sign follows by integrating -Arg(1-t u) along 0<t<1. The two outputs x=y=1/u are upper. The three-side shapes are p=q=2-u upper and H=u(2-u) lower, so they form a strictly positive signed bipyramid. Every compact subarc has a strictly positive gap, which persists in a full open neighborhood of (u,v). The independent positive Parseval proof in the sink-source theorem gives the same rate on its overlapping |u|<1 chamber.

In particular there is the following genuine volume limit for the original local polynomial, uniform for theta in compact subsets of (0,pi):

lim_(N->infinity) (2*pi/N)log|B_N(exp(-i*theta),1)|
                                     =2 D(exp(i*theta)). (13)

Indeed its gap is positive, the two unit-shape Bloch values equal their dilogarithm imaginary parts, and the logarithmic cross terms in (10) are real on this unit boundary. The identical limit holds with the actual tags B_N(Q^2 exp(-i*theta),Q), by the uniform theorem. This is a continuum of positive bipyramid fibers with the LITERALLY original source. It is not asserted to be a family of knot invariants.

This does not eliminate the current outside the gap or the remaining joint exterior cycle. In particular the positive bipyramid with bounded local source in Section 7 of the beta-source theorem is fully consistent with (2). No broad knot theorem follows from this local normalizer.

There is also an independent compact Fermat-curve proof of the same exact normalizer, including its central factor and phase.

The full-Psi audit and results check twelve exact normalizers including both |u|<1 and |u|>1, the original coefficient tags and the independent Barnes source quadratures. The exact proof above does not infer rigidity from those finite checks.
