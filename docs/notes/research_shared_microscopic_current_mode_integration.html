Integrating a shared microscopic current in the actual endpoint modes

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The original microscopic current integrates one common real parameter at all incidences of its removed vertex. On buffered physical windows, its exact full-jet representatives admit a uniform integrated estimate sharper than the supremum of their separate endpoint factors. The gain is logarithmic. A nonzero integrated coefficient for every incidence number at least three, and a surviving zero-frequency term for two incidences, preclude an exponential improvement from this local integration alone.

These are local source statements. The actual remaining traces, outside rows, interfaces and connecting contours are retained. No whole-current rate or nonzero original closing is inferred.

4. Exact source placement and limits of the estimate

Equations (6)–(7) are a local representation of the original current at one removed vertex, with arbitrary fixed incidence number. The crossing power n=0n=0 makes this removed current zero; it places no restriction on powers at surviving vertices. The cut height A/NA/N is retained exactly, including the term (15). Changing AA or joining adjacent height pieces is a different decomposition whose endpoint terms must be added with their original orientations.

At each surviving traced color, the pinned one-sided construction expresses the original physical-window trace as its two exterior half-contours plus its real interface. Integrating that exact identity in α\alpha commutes with all finite traces and contour integrals. The integrated interface keeps its sharp exponential bound because ∫0A|qN,n(α)|dα≤C\int_0^A|q_{N,n}(\alpha)|\,d\alpha\le C. All original vertical half-sides, rational aliases and physical rows outside the windows remain. The common surviving spectator has not been removed or assigned a norm bound here.

Thus the nonzero limit (19) is a local integrated multiplier; it is not a nonzero coefficient for the original scalar. Nor do (8)–(19) bound the ordinary canonical polynomial at off-grid colors. Its exact local full jets are represented by (6), and that is the scope of the estimate.

Several current vertices may have edges between them. Their factors KN(wN(α),wN(β))K_N(w_N(\alpha),w_N(\beta)), or other parameter-dependent joint sources, are additional shared amplitudes. This proof does not omit them or silently extend (19) to them. Likewise it supplies no bound for a subsequent outer turn. For a parent’s fixed height H>0H>0, the full current ranges over 0≤α≤NH0\le\alpha\le NH; the theorem concerns its bounded microscopic segment only.

The supremum bound for dd separate factors is O(N−d(log⁡N)d)O(N^{-d}(\log N)^d). Integrating their actual common parameter improves this to (8), while (17)–(20) show why the improvement cannot generally be exponential in NN. Shared signs create a zero-frequency sector, and the coalescing layer α≍1/log⁡N\alpha\asymp1/\log N has a nonzero integrated coefficient. Any strict parent-action comparison still requires the complete source and its retained currents.
