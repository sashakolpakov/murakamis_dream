Actual representation and signed volume from hybrid tight-cut pieces

31 October 2026. conditional representation and relative-volume theorem. The hybrid tight-cut theorem supplies the finite-flow input. This note proves the geometric and original-source topology step for its exact output, without an independent Green-block capacity factorization. It concerns the positive endpoint source, not the original phase-sensitive filled-knot period.

1. Precisely the decomposition data required

Start with the actual connected identity smoothing of the original certified flat plat. Its full positive-kernel maximum is the same W_D by the full-kernel identity reduction, §3 and its literal forest contraction. At a chosen maximum, remove every pure-middle port by that reduction and represent terminal one-sided seams as CROSS at the same full-kernel entropy value.

Use the following output of the hybrid tight-cut construction:

  • The remaining loopless white triangular cellulation is cut along actual digon disks into a finite tree of triangular spheres.
  • Each child is marked at the two adjacent vertices of its cut boundary; the root retains the original protected pair. Boundary loads on each mark-free side are zero, so the exact entropy is the SUM of the piece entropies, with the original triangle constants and multiplicities.
  • No GREEN (strict one-sided) port is cut. Every port crossing a matching cut is CROSS. Its entropy is the additive C(alpha)+C(beta), and its two original triangles are retained separately on their actual sides. No new physical auxiliary circle is inferred from closing a piece.
  • A strict relative hybrid witness on each piece, with the existing strict Green row margins, excludes every ordinary zero corner by the proved epsilon-log entropy gains. Finite row-space multipliers then give positive finite ordinary curvatures, protected curvatures zero, and the actual local tangent-circle angles. Each ordinary signed star has total0 or2pi; only the two forced marked lines degenerate.

These are explicit inputs here. The hybrid flow proof, §§4–5 and the corner expansions and multiplier argument, §§2–4 supply their finite support and regularity content. In particular an arbitrary digon decomposition with GREEN ports cut in half would NOT satisfy them.

2. Each piece has one actual signed circle development

Apply the cooriented developing-disk argument, §2 to each piece, with the following explicit multigraph and boundary adaptations. Its new protected edge need not be MAIN. If it is MAIN, the two adjacent triangles have protected middles, so they cannot be negative. If the edge was a MATCHING cut, its two incident triangles are the retained CROSS halves and are again positive. Their new protected outer loads are zero, so each is the fixed two-line triangle (0,0,pi). Thus in both cases the two marked triangles supply the genuine common strip.

At a matching cut, formally pair the two boundary CROSS half-triangles along the new matching edge. This retains the cubic perfect-matching incidence needed for parity; it creates NO physical coupon. A GREEN matching edge between the new protected vertices would have both outer loads zero, hence be a pure-middle port already removed. Every other parallel marked edge gives an allowed MAIN/MAIN or CROSS MATCHING/MATCHING cut. Exhaustive splitting therefore makes the remaining marked line/line edge unique.

Remove one positive marked triangle. The remaining abstract complex is a triangulated disk. A finite/finite edge fixes an orientation-preserving Euclidean gluing by its two distinct ordered centers; a line/finite edge fixes it by its COORIENTED line and center. The unique line/line edge is on the boundary and is never used for an underdetermined gluing. Around each interior finite vertex the holonomy fixes its center and rotates by its signed star angle, either zero or 2pi, so it is the identity. These vertex loops generate the dual disk’s cycles. Boundary fans propagate the SAME two cooriented lines. The final finite circle touches both inward sides, forcing its radius to be half their shared distance and closing the omitted triangle. This argument uses disk topology and nondegenerate edge data, not simplicity of the remaining finite-edge graph. Double-triangle pieces have zero entropy and zero volume and are represented by two lines and one tangent circle.

Every piece therefore gives its ONE family of oriented white planes, with each adjacent pair distinct and tangent. Its developed image may fold. Its signed two-ball chain volume is exactly the sum of sigma V for its original triangles, by the signed cyclic-polygon and contact-triangle identities, §§1–2.

3. Glue the pieces, retaining every original port pairing

Root the actual cut tree. A child’s marked ordered tangent pair is mapped to its already assigned parent pair by an orientation-preserving Mobius map. Explicitly send their common contacts to infinity; both ordered pairs become opposite cooriented parallel planes. Positive scale and translation match their width and position. The remaining horizontal translation is free. Each interior white label belongs to one piece; shared boundary label occurrences form connected subtrees. Recursion therefore gives ONE reflection for every actual smoothed white face. No independent reflection is chosen at different occurrences of a face.

The free translations can be chosen generically to avoid accidental coincidences of distinct nonadjacent white circles. This is not an embedding requirement; all adjacent tangent pairs already remain nondegenerate. The original main prefixes telescope and cups/caps give their literal inverse pairs, exactly as before the cuts.

For a GREEN port both triangles lie in the same piece, so its actual source collar C is already supplied by the literal signed Green formula, §3 and is conjugated by that piece’s ONE gluing map.

For an original CROSS port whose two triangles lie in different pieces, its outer white pair x,y is common. Let P=r_y r_x=-I+H. It is noncentral parabolic. Each ordered triangle gives an actual raw first meridian A with trace-2 and with Tr((P+I)A)=4. After H=[[0,h],[0,0]], h!=0, this says A_21=4/h. Trace and determinant determine the other entries from A_11. The centralizer I+tH acts simply transitively on these pairs. Hence there is a UNIQUE actual C mapping its recorded input first meridian to its recorded output first meridian; it maps the second as well because the whole ordered product P is common.

Equivalently C sends ALL THREE original contact flags: O=x/y is fixed, C1=x/b goes to C0=x/c, and C2=b/y goes to C3=c/y. It is precisely the original untwisted shaded pairing within the first ball. The second ball uses r_u C r_u with ONE global r_u, and the inverse is used on the reverse pairing. This does NOT pair the two artificial halves used to close a child piece into a new physical coupon. The original port may continue to connect different pieces after the geometric cut.

Its raw auxiliary meridian is -C^-1, with the original physical lift C^-1. The actual four-crossing threading word returns it and transports both ordered main ports. Thus every original smoothed Morse relation holds. The two halves’ independent local radii are not asserted to form a regular source-gradient chart at that boundary port; the boundary representation is constructed by its ACTUAL three-point relation and its globally computed entropy, not by selecting an unproved finite-sum or local re-framing branch.

All actual main meridians are noncentral parabolic. At any retained auxiliary cusp, C^-1 and its actual preferred longitude P are in the same parabolic translation centralizer. This remains true if C=I, because P remains noncentral. Thus the entire peripheral subgroup preserves the contact and its canonical horosphere.

4. The original relative fundamental chain has the summed volume

Use the TWO original cut balls of the actual smoothed projection and their literal proper digon-cut disks. Under the common reflection assignment the two ends of each disk are the SAME tangent-pair contact. Their canonical pair horosphere is common. The finite incidence and short-edge construction, §4 places all disk replacements at height T with bounded horizontal coordinates, preserving the actual shaded/white pairings. Added tetrahedra have an edge of length O(1/T), so

|Vol tetra|<=2pi ell cosh^2 ell

makes their total volume vanish. Cut-disk collapse has relative degree one in the abstract domain. It leaves every original shaded triangle uncut and retains its actual C map even when its partner lies in a different block. These are geometric chain operations, not source factorizations.

Each resulting abstract piece is developed by its assigned signed circle family. Cone its white/shaded faces to its own marked boundary contact; the signed cyclic fan identity gives its first-ball volume (1/2)sum sigma V, including winding-zero white polygons. The ONE global reflection r_u develops the second original ball, reversing target and abstract orientations together, so its contribution is the same. Mobius alignment of a piece preserves this geometric volume; no open Gauss regulator is reset or declared invariant. The collapsed disks add zero, and all original boundary faces pair. Therefore

Vol(rho_sm)=sum_pieces sum_(triangles in piece) sigma V =2pi sum_pieces (actual entropy). (1)

There is no missing constant: the input entropy splitting uses the already proved C=V/(2pi) and E=C_input-C_output. Artificial closing triangles are the forced two-line V=0 configurations. The original mark and target degrees were retained in the identity and tight-cut steps; no new quantum mark or 1/N normalization is introduced.

5. Return to the original drilled group

Restore each actual deleted UNTWISTED port. Its middle faces were identified, so its ordered input and output pairs agree. Assign the original auxiliary meridian to identity. The literal threading word is then the identity. Its original outer product may be axial (loxodromic or elliptic), parabolic or central, because the removed matching edge imposed no remaining tangency. Choose its recorded peripheral flag and use the proved zero-volume axis/parabolic filling-cap construction, §4. No artificial outer contact is inserted.

This gives a representation rho of the original drilled group, factoring through the actual meridional identity smoothing, with

Vol(rho)=Vol(rho_sm)=2pi W_D (2)

IF the exact hybrid decomposition/regularity hypotheses in section1 have been established for that maximum. The representation-volume bound and strict-kernel theorem, §§1–3 then give W_D<=Vol(Y_D)/(2pi). If at least one original meridian was killed, the already proved strict-kernel bound is strict; comparison with the known all-cross lower bound would exclude such a maximizing identity-deletion case.

This assembly does not itself prove the hybrid tight-cut classification or the exclusion of every further free KKT stratum. It records exactly what geometry follows from those finite-flow outputs, and preserves all original group, peripheral and chain data needed for the comparison.

Scope

Root, coherent and cluster the complete conditional proof: the matching-mark CROSS boundary rows, cooriented piece development, original three-contact collar maps across pieces, one global reflected copy, actual digon-disk chain collapse and restored identity meridians. The axial restoration explicitly includes elliptic as well as loxodromic outer products. The formal boundary matching preserves combinatorial incidence only; no finite source coupon is inserted there.

This proof retains one original relative fundamental chain and the actual source flags. It does not use the false local assertion that resetting a Gauss reference preserves an open crossing-word regulator; the global chain note explains the replacement. The focused audit checks 270 signed cyclic fans, four repeated-contact fans, four angle sums, 24 actual circle/line tangencies, eight contact-triangle volumes and two oriented two-ball strip chains. Its recorded results use 80-digit arithmetic; root the code and cluster and replayed it. These finite geometric identities supplement the analytic proof; they do not prove general source pairings, relative degree or the cut-disk collapse. The conditional geometric conclusion does not by itself supply an asymptotic for the original phase-sensitive long-filled knot source.
