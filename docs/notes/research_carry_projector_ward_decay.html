Exponential coherent decay of the actual carry-projector Ward currents

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

The low/high pair-degree projectors have exact threshold Ward currents. Their normalized ordinary-binomial coherent kernels are exponentially small on the explicit Γ<1\Gamma<1 chamber of S. The estimate holds at resolved zero coordinates as well as on nonzero-coordinate charts, and retains every fixed true meridian derivative. This is a kernel estimate for the projector currents themselves, not an operator-norm estimate or an estimate after composition with an unspecified crossing or word.

1. Literal arrows, kernels, and the exact currents

Fix N≥2N\ge2, d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, Q=q2Q=q^2, and M=eμM=e^\mu, x=−M/qx=-M/q. The quantum root is held fixed. On two heads put Ep=E1K2+E2,Fp=F1+K1−1F2,J=J1+J2,Π0=𝟏J≤d,Π1=I−Π0.(2) E_{\mathrm{ p}}=E_1K_2+E_2,\qquad F_{\mathrm{ p}}=F_1+K_1^{-1}F_2,\qquad J=J_1+J_2,\qquad \Pi_0=\mathbf1_{J\le d},\quad \Pi_1=I-\Pi_0. \qquad\text{(2)} All thresholds are exact integer occupations. Direct degree counting, including the endpoint zeros in (1), gives S(18a–b): [Ep,Π0]=−Ep𝟏J=N,[Fp,Π0]=Fp𝟏J=d.(3) [E_{\mathrm{ p}},\Pi_0]=-E_{\mathrm{ p}}\mathbf1_{J=N},\qquad [F_{\mathrm{ p}},\Pi_0]=F_{\mathrm{ p}}\mathbf1_{J=d}. \qquad\text{(3)} The high-projector currents have the opposite signs. For a two-head matrix AA, use the actual input-binomial convention KA(z,w)=∑a,b,c,e=0dA(c,e),(a,b)(da)(db)z1cz2ew1aw2b.(5) K_A(z,w)=\sum_{a,b,c,e=0}^{d} A_{(c,e),(a,b)}\binom da\binom db z_1^cz_2^e w_1^aw_2^b . \qquad\text{(5)} Put vi=ziwiv_i=z_iw_i and IN=KI=(1+v1)d(1+v2)dI_N=K_I=(1+v_1)^d(1+v_2)^d. In (8) the factors 1−QN1-Q^N kill precisely the forbidden upper arrows. No occupation cap has been extended.

2. Exact shell bounds, including zero coordinates

Write X=|v1|X=|v_1|, Y=|v2|Y=|v_2|, and Γ(v1,v2)=(X+Y)2|1+v1||1+v2|,Sℓ(X,Y)=∑a+b=ℓ(da)(db)XaYb.(9) \Gamma(v_1,v_2) =\frac{(\sqrt X+\sqrt Y)^2}{|1+v_1|\,|1+v_2|},\qquad S_\ell(X,Y)=\sum_{a+b=\ell}\binom da\binom db X^aY^b . \qquad\text{(9)} For any R>0R>0, positivity of the coefficients gives Sℓ(X,Y)≤R−ℓ(1+RX)d(1+RY)d.(10) S_\ell(X,Y)\le R^{-\ell}(1+RX)^d(1+RY)^d. \qquad\text{(10)} If XY>0XY>0, choose R=(XY)−1/2R=(XY)^{-1/2}. Since (1+RX)(1+RY)/R=(X+Y)2(1+RX)(1+RY)/R=(\sqrt X+\sqrt Y)^2, this proves Sd+1|IN|≤XYΓd,Sd|IN|≤Γd.(11) \frac{S_{d+1}}{|I_N|}\le\sqrt{XY}\,\Gamma^d,\qquad \frac{S_d}{|I_N|}\le\Gamma^d . \qquad\text{(11)} Neither inequality requires XY<1XY<1 or XY>1XY>1. A second estimate removes the zi−1z_i^{-1} factors in (7). The polynomial Sd+1(X,Y)S_{d+1}(X,Y) is divisible by XYXY. The final bounds extend continuously to XY=0XY=0, since the quotients are polynomials. This argument estimates the absolute coefficients of the divided shell, so it applies unchanged with the unit QQ-phases in (7).

3. Genuine meridian and coherent jets

At fixed z,wz,w, every fixed meridian derivative satisfies the same bounds (13)–(14), with CηC_\eta replaced by Cη,kC_{\eta,k}, uniformly in NN. To see this directly, the first coefficient of (7) is xQ−b(MQ−a−M−1)=−q−1(M2Q−a−b−Q−b).(15) xQ^{-b}(MQ^{-a}-M^{-1}) =-q^{-1}\big(M^2Q^{-a-b}-Q^{-b}\big). \qquad\text{(15)} The other coefficient uses only M,M−1M,M^{-1}, while (8) uses only constants and M−1M^{-1}. Thus ∂μk\partial_\mu^k multiplies these finitely many Laurent monomials by powers of 0,±1,20,\pm1,2; one may take Cη,k≤Cη(1+2k)C_{\eta,k}\le C_\eta(1+2^k). The identity kernel is meridian independent. No NkN^k loss is needed for these fixed-coordinate currents.

Let 𝒦\mathcal K be any compact subset of 𝒰={(z,w)∈ℂ4:1+ziwi≠0,Γ(z1w1,z2w2)<1}.(16) \mathcal U=\{(z,w)\in\mathbb C^4: 1+z_iw_i\ne0,\ \Gamma(z_1w_1,z_2w_2)<1\}. \qquad\text{(16)} There is a fixed complex neighborhood of 𝒦\mathcal K, with bounded coordinates, nonzero incidence denominators, and Γ≤γ<1\Gamma\le\gamma<1. Apply (13) and its meridian derivatives on that neighborhood, and then use the ordinary multivariable Cauchy estimate. For every fixed coordinate multi-index α\alpha and fixed k≥0k\ge0, sup(z,w)∈𝒦|Nμ|≤η(|∂z,wα∂μkℰN|+|∂z,wα∂μkℱN|)≤C𝒦,α,k,ηdγd.(17) \sup_{\substack{(z,w)\in\mathcal K\\|N\mu|\le\eta}} \left( |\partial_{z,w}^{\alpha}\partial_\mu^k\mathcal E_N| +|\partial_{z,w}^{\alpha}\partial_\mu^k\mathcal F_N| \right) \le C_{\mathcal K,\alpha,k,\eta}\,d\,\gamma^d . \qquad\text{(17)} In particular every such jet is exponentially small as N→∞N\to\infty. The exponent uses the buffered γ\gamma; it is not a claim that coordinate derivatives preserve the pointwise factor Γ(v)d\Gamma(v)^d. The conclusion includes resolved zero coordinates in 𝒦\mathcal K. It applies to both projector currents by (3), and to (4) for ζ\zeta in a fixed compact set.

Fixed integer powers of the physical K1,K2K_1,K_2 placed on either side of these currents preserve the same conclusion: they only multiply each occupation coefficient by a unit QQ-phase and a fixed Laurent power of MM. Their fixed meridian derivatives change the constants. This assertion does not include arbitrary moving factorial gauges or coordinates.

4. Meaning and limitation

This proves local almost-intertwining in the specific sense of normalized binomial coherent kernels on (16). The exact finite operators (3) are still present; no exponentially small operator norm is asserted. Even the exact identity [Ep,R(Π0+ζΠ1)]=R[Ep,Π0+ζΠ1][E_{\mathrm{ p}},R(\Pi_0+\zeta\Pi_1)] =R[E_{\mathrm{ p}},\Pi_0+\zeta\Pi_1] does not turn (17) into a kernel estimate for that product by pointwise multiplication of normalized symbols.

A contracting coherent chain, its remaining density, and its boundary currents require separate estimates before (17) bounds an inserted current in an original source. No membership of an arbitrary geometric point in (16), localized source/axis correspondence, geometric cycle coefficient, or lower bound for a closed period is inferred.
