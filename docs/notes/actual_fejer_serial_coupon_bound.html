Exact serial Fejer coupons have only a polynomial composition cost

31 October 2026. This concerns the literal SAME ordered pair Casimir and its actual homogeneous composition. A diagrammatic digon may be replaced using this lemma only after its cups, dual turns and port order have been identified with that serial composition. No assertion about an arbitrary matching/matching separating cut is made.

1. Exact finite source and positive paths

Let N be even, d=N-1, theta=pi/N. On the actual polynomial source block with basis z^j, 0<=j<=d, write

Omega z^j=c_j z^j-s_j^2(x+y)z^(j-1)
                    -s_j s_(j-1)xy z^(j-2),
c_j=2cos((2j+1)theta), s_j=2sin(j theta),
a_j=2+c_j=4cos^2((j+1/2)theta)>0.

The endpoint factors s_0=0 make every illegal physical step zero. The even root characteristic polynomial is chi_N(sigma)=2T_N(sigma/2)+2, with chi_N(-2)=4. Hence the FULL positive endpoint coupon is exactly

P_N=-V_N(-2)=4(2+Omega)^(-1).                    (1)

This includes all repeated-node/Hermite data, with the original full numerator convention. Its homogeneous polynomial H_P is the already proved original marked-source coupon; it has no new trace normalization or independently reset half-power.

Put K=(2+Omega)^(-1). For any downward path pi from j to i, with steps one or two, let Vert(pi) be its distinct visited row indices and w(pi) the product of the literal nonnegative lowering weights. Expanding the triangular inverse gives its contribution to K as

w(pi) product_(v in Vert(pi)) a_v^(-1).          (2)

One-steps retain (x+y) and two-steps retain xy. Thus all comparisons below are coefficientwise in the original four homogeneous faces, not only positive-weight evaluations.

2. Composition and the repeated-vertex multiplier

Fix an integer ell>=1. Composing ell copies of K decomposes a complete downward path into ell consecutive subpaths, allowing zero-step subpaths. Each of the ell-1 break vertices is then counted an extra time in the inverse diagonal product. Breaks may repeat; their order along the path is fixed. Therefore the multiplier of (2) is EXACTLY

h_(ell-1)({a_v^(-1):v in Vert(pi)}),             (3)

where h_r is the complete homogeneous symmetric polynomial of degree r. There is one decomposition for every multiset of break vertices. This also proves the formula for a zero-step path and arbitrary ell.

All variables are nonnegative, and h_r(t)<= (sum t)^r. The full inverse diagonal sum has the exact value

sum_(j=0)^d a_j^(-1)=N^2/4.                     (4)

Indeed chi_N’(sigma)/chi_N(sigma)=sum_j(sigma-c_j)^(-1), while chi_N’(-2)=T_N’(-1)=-N^2 at even N and chi_N(-2)=4. This uses every row with its multiplicity, including repeated Casimir eigenvalues.

The P_N^ell-to-P_N path multiplier is 4^(ell-1) times (3). Retaining the term with every break at the initial row j gives the lower bound (4/a_j)^(ell-1)>=1, since a_j<=4. Equations (3)–(4) therefore give the finite, uniform coefficientwise comparison

H_(P_N) <= H_(P_N^ell)
                      <= N^(2ell-2) H_(P_N).    (5)

No exponential asymptotic or fixed-radius comparison enters this bound. For fixed ell the two sides have the SAME exponential rate. It also holds when x=y, or after any original nonnegative variable identification or zero specialization.

For reference the zero-step part satisfies the two-sided comparison

H_1 <= (H_P)_diag=sum_j (4/a_j)b^j c^(d-j)
                    <=N^2 H_1,                 (6)
H_1=sum_j b^j c^(d-j).

The lower bound uses a_j<=4; the upper bound follows from (4) or from min_j a_j=4sin^2(pi/(2N)). It concerns the FULL diagonal range, not one fixed left or right half label.

3. Literal homogeneous and whole-source use

The original homogeneous composition gives, for the SAME pair Casimir,

H_FG(x,b,c,y)=[z^d]H_F(x,b,z,y)H_G(x,z,c,y).    (7)

Thus a literal serial chain with the same ordered outer faces x,y and successive middle faces z_1,…,z_(ell-1) contracts to H_(P_N^ell) by extracting each ordinary internal target d. There is NO additional N, pivot, mark or common crossing phase in (7). Its source normalization has already been included in H, as established by the original physical grid/cup/cap/mark compiler.

If the other remaining factors have nonnegative coefficients, substitute (5) into their product and then extract the unchanged original targets. The resulting whole positive source lies between the source with one P_N coupon and N^(2ell-2) times that source, with its internal middle targets removed by (7). Their exponential rates are identical, including the possibility that both coefficients are zero. All other ordinary and protected targets stay literal. Any actual cup/dual orientation needed to realize (7) is a separate diagrammatic premise, not inferred from the existence of two parallel edges alone.

The exact identity smoothing in (6) and the serial bound (5) do not establish a full block factorization across arbitrary separating digons, nor a bound for the original phase-sensitive long-twist period.

Validation and scope

The focused audit and its results check 30 exact characteristic endpoint identities, 3 original full resolvent identities, and 15 original serial powers through 3,960 physical grid entries, including 1,290 wrong-unwrapped-total zeros. At 75 digits they also check 6,116 path break multipliers and 3,120 independently composed coefficients against BOTH bounds, with maximum relative error below 6.20e-74. Five full inverse-diagonal sums and 40 diagonal bounds retain the actual finite endpoint constants.
