A fixed source sector beyond the shared-radius envelope criterion

The local source polynomial splits into its central, left and right recurrence-path pieces, conventionally labeled CROSS, LEFT and RIGHT (C, L and R). This note gives an obstruction to a sufficient coefficient-estimation method, not to the volume conjecture. It uses one actual fixed marked simple-sphere source, one genuine left label, and central at every other crossing region. There are no unrestricted spectator labels and no mixtures between different genuine spectator sectors.

This is a consequence of the geometric face-star equations and the normalized triangle action in the complete geometric filling period, §§2–4, and the geometric contribution and original filled complement, §§2,4–6. 6. What this changes, and what it leaves open

The earlier 17-crossing marked source in the separation between anchored and refined certificates is a useful sanity test. Its anchored obstruction mixes genuine labels at spectators, while a rigorous 40-branch cover certifies every singleton there. The present search reproduces the expected negative singleton diagnostic for that source and then uses a different simple sphere. No conclusion here contradicts the 40-branch certificate.

For the present 5454-crossing source, every finite cover whose branch costs are formed only by maxima of the same local C/L/R envelopes must include this singleton somewhere. A containing branch has pointwise cost at least GσG_\sigma, so it also cannot have a strict witness below Φ\Phi. Thus arbitrary refinement only among these genuine labels is insufficient on this fixed source and mark.

The obstruction does not exclude another original mark or another presentation of the knot. Nor does it exclude an estimate which keeps additional shared coefficient cancellation, couples the spectral variables, refines the actual finite polynomial by more structure than these three envelopes, or estimates the filled period directly. In particular (3) is not a lower bound for ∥SN,full−SN,C∥\|S_{N,\mathrm{full}}-S_{N,\mathrm C}\|. Cauchy’s bound and the local weighted envelopes can be strictly larger than the shared signed coefficient. No new quantum-volume theorem or counterexample follows.

7. Reproducing the certificate

From the repository root run

python3 repro/run_research_checks.py singleton-envelope

The runner executes both checks and saves their logs. The individual scripts also accept --certificate and --output. The capacity verifier also accepts --repo to locate its rational interval engine. Their defaults resolve from the script location, so the working directory is immaterial. Only the Python standard library is required. Assertions are part of verification; optimized Python execution is rejected.

The certificate contains the exact oriented moves, matching, protected mark, label string, rational corner loads and upper radius vector. The capacity verifier checks those inputs and the entropy inequality; its saved output records rational interval endpoints and hashes. The topology verifier separately checks the oriented sphere, matching frames and original knot pairing; its saved output records the explicit odd crossing set.

These are finite certificates for the theorem proved here. They do not numerically evaluate a quantum invariant, infer its asymptotics from sampled levels, or establish a volume equality for this knot family.
