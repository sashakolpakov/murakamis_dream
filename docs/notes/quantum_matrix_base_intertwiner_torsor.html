The quantum matrix torsor and the transport of its base

Research proof, 27 October 2026. On the dense full-cyclic chart, the actual cyclotomic-normal algebra has an explicit whole quantum-torus matrix cover. An actual crossing algebra map then splits, constructively, into a matrix intertwiner and an automorphism of the quantized leaf. The matrix part has no local lifting obstruction. The base transport and the selected source are separate data, and the latter has an exact comparison residual below.

This extends the root-level regular holonomy line and closed-braid period line. Those notes already construct the root Hom line and its cut. The new issue here is the noncommutative base of the actual full-cyclic Rees module, before setting its normal parameter to zero. No selected-period or volume identity is inferred from smoothness.

1. A commutative base cannot support the full cyclic normal family

Let R be a commutative ring, and let K,E be invertible N by N matrices with KE=Q EK. Taking determinants gives

Q^N=1 in R.                                             (1.1)

In particular a full-cyclic E, for which E^N is a unit, cannot deform as an N-dimensional matrix representation over a flat commutative q-normal base with Q^N!=1. This remains true at a nonsingular point of the classical center. A commutative quantum implicit-function argument for such finite matrices would start with an impossible ambient family.

The actual Rees construction avoids (1.1): its entries are operators on the Fock variable. The base itself is quantized. The construction below identifies the corresponding matrix cover without a formal order-by-order choice of matrix entries.

2. An exact whole quantum-torus matrix cover

Work over a characteristic-zero field containing a marked primitive N-th root zeta. Keep Q variable and put c=Q/zeta. On the open set K Ecal!=0, fix the quantum Casimir Omega. The relation

F Ecal=Omega-qK-q^(-1)K^(-1)

eliminates F. Thus the actual localized algebra is the quantum torus

A_Q=<K^(+/-1),Ecal^(+/-1): K Ecal=Q Ecal K>.              (2.1)

Its Laurent monomials are independent: one may either use their normal-order multiplication directly, or represent K by multiplication and Ecal by the Q-shift. This also proves flatness in Q on this chart.

Choose nonzero root coordinates k_,e_ and marked roots k_^(1/N), e_^(1/N). Let

B_c=<u^(+/-1),v^(+/-1): u v=c v u>.                      (2.2)

Let D,S be constant clock and shift matrices with

D h_j=zeta^j h_j, S h_j=h_(j+1 mod N),
D S=zeta S D, D^N=S^N=I.

There is an exact map, for the whole variable Q,

K=k_*^(1/N) D u,
Ecal=e_*^(1/N) S v,                                    (2.3)

from A_Q to Mat_N(B_c). Indeed the two commutation factors multiply to zeta*c=Q. Distinct K^a Ecal^b have distinct scalar Laurent powers u^a v^b, so the map is injective. Its central powers at the root are

K^N=k_* u^N, Ecal^N=e_* v^N.                            (2.4)

Adjoining these marked quantum N-th roots gives the full matrix cover: D and S are recovered from (2.3), and their finite Fourier projectors and arrows give every matrix unit. Explicitly,

p_0=(1/N)sum_(r=0)^(N-1)D^r,
E_ij=S^i p_0 S^(-j).                                   (2.5)

Conversely (2.3), the quantum roots u,v, and (2.5) generate exactly Mat_N(B_c). This is a noncommutative cover before the root is taken. The marked cross relations include u Ecal=c Ecal u and K v=c v K; choosing arbitrary N-th roots without these cross relations would not specify this cover. It is not an etale cover of a nonexistent commutative generic center. At Q=zeta, B_c becomes the commutative root-coordinate cover of the regular fixed-Casimir leaf, and (2.3) is its ordinary matrix splitting.

This construction is compatible with the dense full-cyclic color chart already obtained from the actual Verma source. It covers its Ecal^N!=0 open set. Another chart is needed at an Ecal^N zero; the identity singularity of the fixed-Casimir leaf has not been removed by declaring this open set dense.

2.1 The exact local logarithmic algebra

For comparison with the actual normal parameter, put

d=N(Q^N-1), H=N Log(Q^N)=N Log(1+d/N).

Thus H=d+O(d^2), and Q=zeta exp(H/N^2) on the marked branch. Set

X=N Log u, P=N Log v.

In the completed local algebra, (2.2) is exactly

[X,P]=H,                                                (2.6)

and

K=k_*^(1/N)D exp(X/N),
Ecal=e_*^(1/N)S exp(P/N).                               (2.7)

One can check (2.6) directly inside the original completed quantum torus. Its logarithms X=Log(K^N/k_), P=Log(Ecal^N/e_) satisfy exp(ad X)P=P+H. The operator ad X raises the complete local filtration, so taking its formal logarithm gives [X,P]=H. The operators

D=k_*^(-1/N)K exp(-X/N),
S=e_*^(-1/N)Ecal exp(-P/N)

commute with X,P, have N-th powers one, and obey DS=zeta SD. This recovers (2.7), including its matrix units, from the actual algebra itself. Completion may be taken with weights X=P=1,H=2; all normal-order products then have finite coefficients at each weight.

In particular the divided commutator has {X,P}=1. Its symplectic form is -dX wedge dP, exactly the fixed-leaf Gauss form -dLog k wedge dLog e in the Rees calculation. On a connected formal chart the center of this quantum base is the constant ring k[[H]]: commuting a normal-ordered series with X and P forces its P and X derivatives, respectively, to vanish. Additional fixed peripheral parameters belong to that constant ring.

3. Constructive matrix Hensel splitting of an actual map

Here is the algebra statement needed for crossing transport. It works over a noncommutative base.

Matrix/base splitting. Let B_s,B_t be complete rings, with a central ideal of definition containing d, and let

alpha:Mat_m(B_s) -> Mat_m(B_t)

be a continuous algebra isomorphism. Suppose a specified root frame G_0 identifies the two systems of matrix units modulo d. Then there is an invertible G and a base isomorphism theta:B_s->B_t such that

alpha(a)=G theta_entry(a) G^(-1).                       (3.1)

This requires the given actual alpha. Classical nonsingularity by itself does not choose theta.

Proof and exact formula: lift G_0 to any invertible G_* and replace alpha by alpha’=Ad_(G_*^-1)alpha. Now alpha’(E_ij)=E_ij modulo d. Define the finite sum

G'=sum_(i=1)^m alpha'(E_i1) E_1i.                       (3.2)

It is I modulo d and therefore invertible. Matrix-unit multiplication gives, for every a,b,

alpha'(E_ab)G'=G'E_ab.

Consequently beta=Ad_(G’^-1)alpha’ fixes all E_ij. The centralizer of these matrix units is exactly the set of scalar matrices bI, even when B_t is noncommutative. Therefore beta(bI)=theta(b)I for an isomorphism theta; multiplying by E_ij proves (3.1) with G=G_*G’. This proves existence and gives the intertwiner explicitly. In particular the base map is also extracted by a finite formula:

theta(b)=[G^(-1)alpha(bI)G]_(1,1).                      (3.3)

The calculation is not restricted to a Taylor truncation: whenever alpha, the marked matrix units, and G_* belong to a specified whole function algebra, (3.2) belongs to that algebra too. Inverting its unit gives the same whole local formula. The formal theorem alone does not prove that this inverse or the quantum-root branches extend globally across their divisors.

At a chosen regular root place the marked quantum roots of Section 2 lift under an actual regular alpha by Hensel functional calculus. For an element with scalar invertible root value, the derivative of W^N-a in the unknown W is multiplication by N W_0^(N-1), a unit. The unique branch obtained this way is the one reducing to the marked scalar N-th root, not one of the non-scalar finite-order roots. Uniqueness enforces the required quantum cross relations as well. For example, writing a=KN,b=EcalN gives ab=c(N2)ba. Conjugating the marked root u by b and taking its N-th power shows b u b(-1)=c(-N)u. Conjugating the marked root v by u and repeating the argument gives u v u^(-1)=c v. The same proof gives u Ecal=c Ecal u and K v=c v K. These equalities use the marked branches with the same scalar root values modulo d. Thus alpha locally lifts to the specified matrix cover, and (3.2) then applies. This constructs a local function germ; it does not replace the branch and localization data of a whole cover.

Apply this to the actual rational R-conjugation on the pair of localized full-cyclic colors, with m=N^2. At an admissible point where its denominators and the chosen input/output cyclic charts are units, it yields an explicit semilinear crossing object (3.5) below. Its classical base map is the actual root holonomy map. The procedure constructs an intertwiner of the actual algebra transport; it does not select the scalar normalization or the original R-source kernel from that algebra transport alone.

3.1 Precisely which torsor is a line?

For fixed alpha and fixed theta, two G in (3.1) differ by a unit of Z(B_t). Indeed their ratio commutes with all matrix units, so is bI; commuting also with all scalar matrices forces b central. If only the matrix-unit equations are fixed, the freedom is B_t^x, not Z(B_t)^x. Changing G to G bI changes theta to

theta' = Ad_(b^-1) theta.                               (3.4)

This distinction matters. At the root the whole base is commutative and the Hom object is an ordinary line over the leaf. Before the root, fixing the full quantum base transport usually reduces its scalar freedom to functions of Q and the fixed peripheral parameters. A nonconstant root rescaling can instead change the subleading base transport through (3.4), while leaving its classical map unchanged.

There is a semilinear module map

R_theta(v)=G theta_entry(v),                            (3.5)

which satisfies R_theta(av)=alpha(a)R_theta(v). This is a genuine linear intertwiner over the constants, with the specified right-base semilinearity. It need not be represented by a regular scalar operator on the original Fock representation.

3.2 No matrix obstruction can hide a base obstruction

If a regular unit in Mat_m(B) implements an inner automorphism, its induced action on the scalar commutative algebra B/dB is the identity: the root scalar matrices are central. Hence a crossing with a nonidentity classical leaf map cannot be implemented by such a regular inner unit on a fixed fiber. It must be understood semilinearly between the transported fibers, or implemented after an irregular enlargement, as the actual quantum factorial does.

The infinitesimal form is equally explicit. An O-linear derivation D of Mat_m(O) is inner, with

H_D=sum_i D(E_i1)E_1i,
D(E_ab)=[H_D,E_ab].                                    (3.6)

The proof differentiates the matrix-unit identities; the term E_a1 D(E_11)E_1b vanishes by differentiating E_11^2=E_11. A general derivation decomposes into its derivation on O and (3.6). Thus a matrix correction can remove a lifting error precisely after its base derivation has been accounted for.

Even a smooth symplectic base does not select the higher corrections. For [P,X]=d, all maps X->X+d a,P->P have the same classical identity. They are distinct exact automorphisms, implemented by exp(aP) on a formal coordinate chart. Classical uniqueness of an algebraic gluing point does not choose among them, or choose a central scalar factor of its intertwiner.

4. Composition, scalar cocycles, and what Hilbert 90 removes

Suppose actual crossing maps alpha_g have been split as in (3.1). For composable g,h write alpha_(gh)=alpha_g alpha_h. The two matrix parts satisfy

G_g theta_g(G_h)=G_(gh) b_(g,h), b_(g,h) in B^x,
theta_(gh)=Ad_(b_(g,h)) theta_g theta_h.                 (4.1)

To prove that b is a scalar matrix, compare the two sides on each matrix unit. This yields (4.1) without assuming a strict scalar cocycle in advance. Associativity gives the exact coherence identity

b_(gh,k) b_(g,h)
   =b_(g,hk) theta_g(b_(h,k)).                          (4.2)

If the base lifts have also been chosen coherently, so that theta_(gh)=theta_g theta_h, the b_(g,h) lie in Z(B)^x. Only then does this become the usual scalar cocycle. In particular equality of classical center maps does not make the two quantum base maps equal or make their relative transport scalar. An actual universal R identity can trivialize the corresponding cocycle; smoothness of the classical place alone cannot do so.

For completeness the generic finite-Galois descent of such a scalar line has an elementary proof. Over a finite Galois field extension L/K let gamma(G)=a_gamma G, with

a_(gamma delta)=a_gamma gamma(a_delta).

Choose t in L such that b=sum_gamma a_gamma gamma(t) is nonzero. Such t exists because distinct field automorphisms are linearly independent: a minimal nontrivial relation, evaluated at tx and subtracted from its value at t times one chosen automorphism of x, would give a shorter relation. Reindexing gives

gamma(b)=a_gamma^(-1)b.

Therefore bG is Galois invariant. This is the needed Hilbert-90 calculation, with its scalar marking explicit. It works over the generic function field, or the constant Laurent-series field of the quantized leaf. It does not prove that b is a unit on an entire integral chart, trivialize a global line bundle, or normalize the actual cut. Those assertions have divisors and source data of their own.

5. The actual dilogarithm moves the quantum base

The separation above locates the resonant factor of the actual crossing. For the uncheck nilpotent factor

Theta=P_Q(q Ecal_1 F_2), P_Q(z)=(z;Q)_infinity,

put X=-e_1 f_2, with e_i=Ecal_iN,f_i=F_iN. The root-normal logarithm has polar part Li_2(X)/d. This follows coefficientwise: in -sum_(m>=1)zm/[m(1-Qm)] only m=N r have poles, and their leading coefficient is z(Nr)/(r2 d). The precise regular matrix factor and its marked source are retained in the explicit crossing regularization.

The actual divided brackets on the two leaves give

{Li_2(X),k_1}=k_1 Log(1-X),
{Li_2(X),k_2}=-k_2 Log(1-X),
{Li_2(X),e_1}={Li_2(X),f_2}=0.                           (5.1)

Since X is constant along this Hamiltonian field, its time-one map is exactly

k_1 -> k_1(1+e_1 f_2), k_2 -> k_2/(1+e_1 f_2),
e_1 -> e_1, f_2 -> f_2,
e_2 -> e_2+e_1/k_2-e_1 k_2/(1+e_1 f_2),
f_1 -> f_1+f_2 k_1-f_2/[k_1(1+e_1 f_2)].               (5.2)

For the last two equations, the vector field is

e_2 dot=(k_2^(-1)-k_2)Log(1-X)/f_2,
f_1 dot=(k_1-k_1^(-1))Log(1-X)/e_1.

Insert k_1(t)=k_1(1-X)t,k_2(t)=k_2(1-X)(-t) and integrate. The formulas are rational and therefore extend from the log chart to the indicated divisor open set.

These are the center formulas for Theta alone. Composing with the actual Cartan automorphism changes the relevant X to -e_1 f_2 k_2/k_1 and gives the full divisor

D_c=1+e_1 f_2 k_2/k_1

of the root holonomy crossing. Thus the resonant function has a specific base action; a finite regular inner matrix factor cannot supply that action by itself. This is a statement about the actual crossing, not an identification of its selected braid closure with the geometric fundamental-class period.

The Rees-to-Gauss transgression is also exact, but has a different role. Its primitives theta_source=u dp and theta_Gauss=-Log(k/a)dLog e are explicitly chosen polarizations. Their dilogarithm difference gauges those prequantum primitives. This does not assert that exp(W/d) is the actual R, the actual source, or a selected horizontal section. The joint-meridian correction there must remain part of any such comparison.

6. The source obstruction is an explicit residual, not a matrix lift

First suppose psi,phi are a column and row over the transported base algebras, so that their coupons are B-linear matrices. Their exact transport under (3.5) is

psi_t=G theta(psi_s),
phi_t=theta(phi_s)G^(-1),                               (6.1)

and phi_t psi_t=theta(phi_s psi_s). For their coupons Gamma=psi phi, the exact comparison with an independently specified target coupon is

Delta_Gamma=G theta(Gamma_s)-Gamma_t G.                 (6.2)

Vanishing of (6.2) is equivalent to preservation of the rank-one source projector. For these B-linear coupons it is unchanged, up to an invertible scalar, by rescaling G through Z(B)^x. It is therefore not removable by the remaining scalar freedom of the intertwiner torsor. Matching the actual source vector and co-source is stronger still: their common rank-one coefficient must be the allowed central unit, with the prescribed normalization.

For the actual finite cut, Gamma contains vacuum evaluation and is not a regular matrix-valued function on the leaf. One must first extend theta to its Fock/source module or construct the corresponding kernel. The source (6.2) is otherwise not even defined in the regular coefficient algebra. For such operator-valued coupons the scalar invariance just proved requires the scalar to commute with the source operator as well. The generic quantum center consists of the constant parameters, which do commute with this actual vacuum coupon. At the root the center enlarges to functions on the leaf, and those functions need not commute with vacuum evaluation. Arbitrary root-leaf units cannot be substituted for the allowed quantum scalars.

In the actual Rees construction its entries are

eta^(i-j) exp(-u/d)/N times evaluation at u=0,
eta^N=d.                                               (6.3)

Every internal finite-head projector also carries exp(-u/d). Their finite normal jets have the recorded pole bounds and ramified flags; replacing them by regular leaf functions would change the period. The matrix splitting proves neither an extension across this irregular source nor the vanishing of (6.2).

There is also a separate scalar evaluation obstruction. For an intertwiner line L and a cut functional ell:L->R, a normalized section ell(J)=1 exists locally if and only if ell of a local frame is a unit. Smoothness and invertibility of the frame do not imply it. An exact two-dimensional example, which can be tensored with any smooth symplectic base, is

J=[[0,1],[-1,0]], Gamma=(1/2)[[1,1],[1,1]].

Here J is invertible and defines a perfectly regular inner algebra transport, but Tr(Gamma J)=0 and J Gamma-Gamma J!=0. This is not a knot counterexample. It disproves the inference from a nonsingular algebraic place and a lifted intertwiner to a nonzero selected source.

7. What is now constructive and what remains for the knot

At an admissible smooth full-cyclic place, the actual rational crossing map and the marked quantum-root cover determine a local semilinear crossing object by (2.3), (3.2), and (3.5). Its matrix deformation is explicit. Its classical leaf transport is forced by the actual algebra map; its actual resonant dilogarithm gives (5.2). When base transport is made coherent the remaining ambiguity is an ordinary central-unit torsor, with the generic descent proved above.

The missing pointed comparison has a precise target: extend that base transport to the actual vacuum/source lattice, retain every internal projector, and prove the corresponding residual (6.2) vanishes with the required scalar evaluation. Nonsingularity of the Rivin algebraic place supplies none of these source equations by itself. Conversely this result does not exclude a whole cyclotomic rigidity theorem after those actual equations and marks are supplied.

The normal direction d at a fixed N remains distinct from the large-level direction H_N=2 pi i/N. The original finite auxiliary state is regular at its fixed root. Irregular factors in (6.3) can cancel polar phases in a whole contraction; regularity does not determine the eventual N-growth or select a geometric saddle.
