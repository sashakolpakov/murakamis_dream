An actual generic four-port Clebsch recoupling kernel

29 October 2026. This gives a finite factorial kernel for a typed four-port recoupling, with the ordered unequal-pair norm and pivotal cyclic turn derived directly. All intermediate channels are retained, including high root partners. The coefficients are bilinear ribbon coefficients, not a unitary or positive 6j matrix. No quoted recoupling formula is a premise.

Single-sum completion: the independent source construction below is now evaluated by the generic all-port single Racah theorem, including its exact seven-factorial prefactor, half-integral-spin cyclic sign and retained root normals. The two-sum formula here is preserved as an independent actual-source proof and audit input; it is no longer the final unresolved summation gate.

The construction supplies an exact local identity before root specialization. Its explicit pole bookkeeping retains the normal data needed in the original marked source. A positive or asymptotic estimate for the primitive cube remains a separate question.

1. Generic unequal Clebsch maps

Write Q=zeta^2, F_r=(Q;Q)_r. The highest-weight L object has the actual balanced matrices

K|u>=zeta^L Q^-u|u>,
F|u>=(1-Q^(u+1))|u+1>,
Ecal|u>=zeta^(-L-1)(1-Q^(L+1-u))|u-1>.

Its canonical cup has first coefficient one:

P_L(u)=(-1)^u zeta^[u(L-u-1)]F_u F_(L-u)/F_L,
p_L(u,L-u)=P_L(u), ell_L=p_L^(-1).             (1)

Take physical highest weights A,B, target C=A+B-2m, with 0<=m<=min(A,B). The first-entry-one highest vector in V_A tensor V_B has entries at (s,m-s)

h_s=(-1)^s zeta^[s(A-2B+2m-s-1)]
                 F_(B-m+s)F_(A-s)/(F_(B-m)F_A),
0<=s<=m.                                      (2)

The direct highest equation gives h_(s+1)/h_s=-zeta(A-2B)Q(m-s-1) (1-Q(B-m+s+1))/(1-Q(A-s)). Let W_ABC[r]=Delta(F)r h/F_r, 0<=r<=C. Its full finite entry is

W_AB^C[r](i,j)=F_i F_j sum_s
    h_s zeta^(-A t) Q^(st)
                 /[F_s F_(m-s)F_t F_(r-t)],
i+j=m+r, t=j-m+s.                             (3)

All indices are required to be physical: 0<=s<=m, 0<=i<=A,0<=j<=B,0<=t<=j,0<=r-t<=i. Otherwise the term is zero. Formula(3) is the direct finite coproduct binomial, with F_r canceled BEFORE specialization. If A,B<N, every denominator in(2),(3) has index<N, even if C or r is >=N. Thus W itself has no root pole.

The final F endpoint follows from the commutator and injectivity of E above the physical middle degree, exactly as for the previously proved equal-pair Clebsch map. This proves generic covariance without assuming a decomposition or a semisimple root replacement.

2. The ordered bilinear norm, by finite convolution

The right dual of W_BA^C is a map on the ORIGINAL A,B order:

D(W_BA)=p_C^T W_BA^T(ell_B^T tensor ell_A^T)P_AB,

where P_AB changes the displayed A,B input to B,A. The actual norm is

D(W_BA^C) W_AB^C=n_AB^C I,
n_AB^C=(-1)^m zeta^[-AB+m(m-1)]
               F_(A+B-m+1)/(F_m F_(C+1)).      (4)

Using D(W_AB) without reversing the object labels is not a typed composition when A!=B. Equation(4) is a rational generic identity; its zeros and any canceled factors are retained.

Here is an own finite derivation. The lowest F recurrence gives

W_BA^C[C](B-m+s,A-s)
  =ell_BA zeta^[(B-A)s] h_AB(s),
ell_BA=zeta^[-AB+3Bm-2m^2]
                  F_(B-m)F_A/(F_(A-m)F_B).    (5)

At s=0 the coproduct entry reduces to

sum_u(-1)^u Q^[u(u-1)/2]
     F_(B-u)/(F_u F_(m-u)F_(B-m-u))=Q^[m(B-m)].

This is the elementary finite second Chu identity, proved by finite binomial expansion in the earlier source calculations; all endpoint powers give exactly(5). The recurrences then prove the entire slice.

Schur’s K,F equations make the composition in(4) scalar. In its highest entry substitute(1),(2),(5). The only remaining convolution, with X=B-m,Y=A-m, is

sum_s Q^[-(X+1)s]
               [X+s choose s]_Q[Y+m-s choose m-s]_Q
   =Q^[-(X+1)m][X+Y+m+1 choose m]_Q.           (6)

It is the coefficient of u^m in 1/[(uQ^(-X-1);Q)(X+1)(u;Q)(Y+1)], a single contiguous reciprocal product. Its elementary finite-product coefficient formula proves(6). The factorials cancel to those in(4); the sign is (-1)^m and the remaining zeta exponent is -AB+m(m-1). The equal case A=B=d is exactly the independently proved physical norm of the generic six-head tetrahedral source.

The generic projectors are consequently

Pi_AB^C=W_AB^C D(W_BA^C)/n_AB^C.               (7)

Different generic Casimir eigenvalues make them mutually orthogonal, and the dimension identity sum_C(C+1)=(A+1)(B+1) proves completeness. This generic identity is not a claim that the individual projectors are regular or orthogonal positive projections at the root.

3. Four-port columns and the actual cyclic turn

Now use integer spin labels a,b,c,d, with external highest weights 2a,2b,2c,2d. Let e range over

I_S=[|a-b|,a+b] intersect [|c-d|,c+d]

in integer steps. Write W_ab^e for (2),(3) with highest weights 2a,2b,2e. Define the actual invariant column

Psi_e^S=(W_ab^e tensor W_cd^e)p_(2e).          (8)

The first canonical cup has to be kept, including its possible root pole. It is not replaced by a Euclidean Bell vector.

For the other pairing let f range over I_T=[|b-c|,b+c] intersect [|d-a|,d+a]. First form Psi_f in the order B,C,D,A. Bring its last A leg to the front by the GENUINE pivotal cyclic map:

Psi_f^T(u,v,w,z)=K_a(u)
          [(W_bc^f tensor W_da^f)p_(2f)](v,w,z,u).
K_a(u)=zeta^(2a-2u).                          (9)

To prove the clock, moving a first highest-L leg to the end by the actual cap/cup has matrix ell_L^T p_L=(-1)^L K_L^(-1). The inverse move multiplies by (-1)^L K_L. Here L=2a is even, giving(9). It is an invariant-column identity; a plain cyclic permutation would give a different kernel.

Let Psi_e^rev be (8) with external order d,c,b,a, and dualize it with those actual four cups and reverse the abstract dual order. The exact diagonal norm is

D(Psi_e^rev) Psi_g^S=delta_(e,g) N_e,
N_e=n_ab^e n_cd^e [2e+1]_zeta.                 (10)

Indeed each ordered pair composition is(4), and the surviving cup and cap give their actual pivotal quantum dimension. No norm square or conjugation is used. All N_e are nonzero generic rational functions.

4. A finite all-entry recoupling kernel

The complete kernel and all-entry source identity are

R_(e,f)=D(Psi_e^rev) Psi_f^T/N_e,
Psi_f^T=sum_(e in I_S) R_(e,f) Psi_e^S.        (11)

Equations(1)–(3),(8),(9) make every entry an explicit terminating factorial sum, with no quoted 6j formula. Weight conservation removes one external magnetic sum. More specifically, for external colors u,v,w,z with u+v+w+z=a+b+c+d, the S column has the single product

Psi_e^S(u,v,w,z)=W_ab^e[r](u,v) P_(2e)(r)
                                  W_cd^e[2e-r](w,z),
r=u+v-(a+b-e).                                (12)

The T column has the analogous product from(9). The dual in(11) uses the reverse complementary external colors and division by their canonical cup coefficients. All those entries are given in(1)–(3).

For completeness, the S columns span the generic four-port invariant space: resolve the first pair by(7). The remaining invariant couples that target to the second pair; its homogeneous highest recurrence allows exactly the matching target e, once. Thus the dimension is |I_S|=|I_T| and the diagonal pairing(10) proves(11). It follows that the local four-port invariant tensor can be replaced by(11) against ANY exterior row, including a source-dependent row. A planar source disk with incoming ports is first bent using its actual cups; the inverse bends are retained when using(11) in the original network.

5. Root partners and the necessary higher normals

Suppose now 0<=a,b,c,d<=M=floor((N-1)/2). All W entries in(3) are holomorphic at q. The cup p_(2e) is regular for e<=M and has at most a simple pole for e>M. The exact norm factors give the following orders, without treating high partners as low ordinary simple modules.

For a PAIRED low node 2e<N-1, including e=M at even N, put e*=N-1-e. Then

ord_q n_ab^e =1 if e* is in [|a-b|,a+b], else0.

The same holds for c,d. At the odd simple 2e=N-1, each n is a unit and [2e+1] has a simple zero. At e>M the zero in the numerator and denominator factorial of(4) cancels, so each n and [2e+1] are units. Thus the low N_e can have order TWO, the odd simple N_e has order one, and high N_e have order zero. These are actual source zeros.

In particular each coefficient in(11), and each summand R_(e,f)Psi_e^S, has a pole of order at most THREE. For low e its dual column is regular and N_e has at most two zeros; the input f has at most one pole. For high e the dual and input have at most one pole each, N_e is a unit, and the extra output column supplies at most one more. The odd simple case is smaller. Therefore a local Laurent identity(11) can be checked through its first normal by retaining Taylor ORDER FOUR (five coefficients, degrees0 through4) after multiplication by the cube of a local root parameter. This is a pole-order bound, not a magnitude bound.

The pair spectral projector also has at most one pole: only its partner eigenvalue can collide, and that eigenvalue gap has a simple zero away from the odd self-paired node. Summing complete colliding clusters, or retaining the Laurent terms in(11), is necessary BEFORE specialization. Dropping high channels, taking only nodal values, or bounding each singular term separately changes the normal source.

The finite kernel does not yet make R unitary or sign-coherent at the principal root. Factorial coefficients and confluence frames can be exponentially large. A primitive cube reduction must retain its entire recoupling sum and original fixed normal, rather than infer positivity from a diagonal norm or real spectral values.

6. An explicit highest-chart kernel with just two terminating sums

The four-port coefficient can also be evaluated without the full four-color magnetic pairing. Put H=a+b+c-d and use the highest chart

w_i=Psi(i,H-i,0,2d),
max(0,H-2b)<=i<=min(2a,H).                     (13)

Bending the last leg by the actual canonical cup makes this the third-color-zero slice of a highest-weight-2d three-object vector. The original highest recurrence recovers all its other slices, then the ordinary F descendants recover the entire four-port column. Thus this chart is injective; it is not an arbitrary packet truncation.

The MIDDLE-pair Casimir on a valid highest chart is the upper-bidiagonal operator

B e_i=lambda_i e_i+b_i e_(i-1),
lambda_i=zeta^(2d-2a+1)Q^i+zeta^(-2d+2a-1)Q^-i,
b_i=-zeta^(2b+2c-2a-1)Q^(i-H)
                (1-Q^(2a+1-i))(1-Q^(H-i+1)).  (14)

To derive it, lift w into the third object using its actual highest recurrence. In the third-color-zero extraction of Omega_bc only third colors0 and1 can contribute. Their F_b E_b terms cancel, leaving -zeta^(2c)F_b E_a K_b plus the two Cartan terms, which is (14). If c=0 the absent color1 is not introduced; the same formula agrees on the valid kernel E_ab w=0. Thus (14) is always used on the actual highest-source subspace.

For f in I_T put i0=a+f-d, and define the generic left eigenrow

ell_f(i)=0 for i<i0, ell_f(i0)=1,
ell_f(i)=product_(j=i0+1)^i[-b_j/(lambda_j-lambda_i0)]. (15)

The row equation directly proves ell_f B=c_f ell_f. For the T_f column, (9) shows its chart is zero when i>i0. Its value at i0 is the following explicit NONZERO normalization:

beta_f=zeta^(2a-2i0) h_bc^f(b+c-f)
      *(-1)^(d+a-f)
      *zeta^[-4da+4d(d+a-f)-(d+a-f)(d+a-f+1)].  (16)

The last factor is the lowest physical endpoint of W_da^f; it follows by substituting s=m in(5). There is no unspecified eigenvector scale. For the S_e chart, put u_e=c+e-d. Its exact scalar is

w_e(i)=chi_e W_ab^e[u_e](i,H-i),
chi_e=(-1)^u_e zeta^[u_e(2e-u_e-1)-2c(d-c+e)]
                  F_(u_e)F_(2d)/(F_(c+d-e)F_(2e)). (17)

This is the unique s=0 coproduct contribution at the external colors (third=0,fourth=2d) in (12). Hence the INVERSE recoupling has the explicit finite formula

Psi_e^S=sum_(f in I_T) L_(f,e)Psi_f^T,
L_(f,e)=chi_e/beta_f
            *sum_(i=i0)^min(2a,H)
                 ell_f(i) W_ab^e[u_e](i,H-i).  (18)

There is one displayed magnetic sum, and each W entry is the single finite factorial sum(3). All remaining factors are finite products. To prove (18), eigenrows with different generic c_f kill the other right channels; the support just noted gives ell_f(w_f)=beta_f. Injectivity of the highest chart then proves the ALL-ENTRY identity. Equivalently the matrices in(11),(18) are inverse. This is a concrete finite two-sum Racah kernel derived from the original operators, not a quoted hypergeometric recoupling coefficient.

It also exposes cancellations hidden in the diagonal norm. At the principal root beta_f is a unit: all its factorial indices are external physical indices <N. Chi_e has at most one pole, precisely from F_(2e) on a high channel. In ell_f there can be at most one colliding denominator, from f*=N-1-f; its zero is simple, and it is absent for a high f or an odd self-paired node. Therefore each coefficient in (18) has pole order at most TWO. The cyclically interchanged formula gives the same bound for(11). Its product with an output column still has at most three poles, so the fixed-normal instruction in section5 is unchanged. No pole is discarded by evaluating an individual partner at the root.

The focused audit passes 44 exact unequal Clebsch/norm channels, 61 four-port external families and all185 recoupling entries, the separate highest-chart two-sum inverse at all61 families/185 entries, and eight full-partner/root families N=2,…,9. The latter construct high-channel W by(3) without dividing by the vanished F_N, check their actual E,F,K covariance, and compare the uncanceled norm-factor zero count with the exact partner condition. Both even central PAIRED and odd central SIMPLE nodes are included. These checks supplement the finite proofs, not a growing-color asymptotic or conditioning assertion.
