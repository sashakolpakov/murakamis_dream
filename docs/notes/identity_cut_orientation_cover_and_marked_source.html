The identity cut has an exact orientation-cover source

27 October 2026. This is a finite whole-source identity for the actual four-port tensor. It replaces neither the state nor its selected cap. It introduces an orientation factor together with its compensating measure, keeps the cap’s marked sheet, and retains every finite band. The comparison with the geometric momentum below is a principal local comparison; a whole Gaussian/source comparison is not inferred.

1. Retain the original cap and every port band

Use the literal tensor and port order of the physical two-cut construction. With zero extension outside all four physical ranges, put

ℱT(x,y;ξ,η)=∑a,b,μ,νTa+μ,b+ν;a,bxaybξμην. \mathcal F_T(x,y;\xi,\eta) =\sum_{a,b,\mu,\nu}T_{a+\mu,b+\nu;a,b} x^a y^b\xi^\mu\eta^\nu.

The first original balanced cut has matrix coefficients 1/N; the second cut closes by its original identity, without a new pivotal weight. Thus, with all original pivots and external scalar lines already inside T,

$$ F(\eta)=\mathcal F_T(1,1;1,\eta),\qquad \Phi_{\rm id}(T)=\frac1N[\eta^0]F =\mathop{\rm Res}_{\eta=0}F(\eta)\frac{d\eta}{N\eta}. \tag{1} $$

The sums over the first output and input remain in F. This is not the normalized trace of a reshaped two-position matrix. In particular, charged current tensors use all their first-port bands as in (1). The same formula works coefficientwise for every whole crossing or source coupling; no specialization of those coefficients is required.

2. An exact orientation factor with the compensating measure

Introduce the quadratic cover

u=η+η−1,v=η−η−1,v2=u2−4. u=\eta+\eta^{-1},\qquad v=\eta-\eta^{-1},\qquad v^2=u^2-4.

Its involution is eta to eta inverse, fixing u and changing v to -v. On this cover the following is a literal equality of rational forms:

F(η)dηNη=[vF(η)]duN(u2−4). \boxed{\quad F(\eta)\frac{d\eta}{N\eta} =[vF(\eta)]\frac{du}{N(u^2-4)}.\quad} \tag{2}

Indeed du=v d eta/eta. Consequently the original source equals the marked residue of the orientation-twisted section G=vF against the new measure. The multiplication by v is the nearest-band difference on the full kernel. Its compensating measure is essential.

There is a precise parity qualification. G is odd if F is even under eta inversion. For arbitrary actual T the correct law is instead

G∘ι=−v(F∘ι). G\circ\iota=-v(F\circ\iota). \tag{3}

Thus (2) is an orientation twist without an assumed involution symmetry of the whole braid word. It does not declare the scalar function G odd when the actual tensor does not have that symmetry.

Multiplication M_v identifies the full Laurent kernel space with its submodule v times that space. On this image every actual linear current A transports as M_v A M_v inverse, and its co-source transports by (2). The inverse is used only on that image. This is a strict source-preserving change of presentation, not division by a possibly vanishing selected period. All endpoint/corner summands are part of the same full kernel.

3. The original cap marks the sheet at infinity

The point eta=0 determines a particular preimage of u=infinity:

v/u→−1,η=(u+v)/2,t=1/u=η1+η2=η+O(η3). v/u\longrightarrow-1,\qquad \eta=(u+v)/2,\qquad t=1/u=\frac{\eta}{1+\eta^2}=\eta+O(\eta^3). \tag{4}

The last equality gives an actual local uniformizer of order one. Residues in eta and t therefore agree, including their signs. On this sheet v is asymptotic to -u. For a constant F, the right side of (2) is asymptotic to -F du/(Nu), whose standard residue at u=infinity is +F/N. The other preimage, eta=infinity and v/u tending to +1, has residue -F/N. Taking an unmarked sum of the two sheet residues would cancel this constant cap. The original tensor fixes the first sheet in (4); there is no additional choice of an odd cycle.

The apparent poles of du/(u^2-4) at u=2,-2 introduce no new poles in the TOTAL form: at eta=1,-1 the factor G=vF cancels them. This uses the actual finite Laurent nature of F. The measure may not be evaluated independently and then assigned its own endpoint terms.

4. A finite tail formula, not just the nearest coefficient

Write F=sum F_j eta^j and G=sum G_j eta^j. Near the marked eta=0,

duNv2=dηN(η2−1)=−1N∑k≥0η2kdη. \frac{du}{Nv^2}=\frac{d\eta}{N(\eta^2-1)} =-\frac1N\sum_{k\geq0}\eta^{2k}d\eta.

Consequently the exact new source is

Ψ(G)=−1N∑k≥0G−2k−1. \Psi(G)=-\frac1N\sum_{k\geq0}G_{-2k-1}. \tag{5}

Only finitely many coefficients contribute for any actual tensor. Since G_j=F_(j-1)-F_(j+1), the partial sum through k=K is

−1N∑k=0KG−2k−1=F0−F−2K−2N. -\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^K G_{-2k-1} =\frac{F_0-F_{-2K-2}}N. \tag{6}

The final coefficient outside the full support vanishes, proving Psi(vF)=F_0/N exactly. Formula (6) is the retained finite endpoint term. Dropping it early, using only -G_-1/N, or replacing (5) by the constant coefficient of vF changes the source.

For the literal two-cut unknot tensor, which is the flip tensor,

$$ F_{\rm unknot}(\eta) =\sum_{j=-(N-1)}^{N-1}(N-|j|)\eta^j. \tag{7} $$

The original cap and (5) both give one. The constant coefficient of vF is zero. Keeping only -G_-1/N gives 2/N for N at least two; the remaining finite tail contributes (N-2)/N. This actual tensor already detects the distinction between an odd insertion at the old source and the source-preserving orientation change (2).

5. Whole clocks and their connection term

Let r be an invertible whole clock parameter. The change eta to r eta acts on the cover by

ur=12(r+r−1)u+12(r−r−1)v,vr=12(r−r−1)u+12(r+r−1)v. \begin{split} u_r&=\tfrac12(r+r^{-1})u+\tfrac12(r-r^{-1})v,\\ v_r&=\tfrac12(r-r^{-1})u+\tfrac12(r+r^{-1})v. \end{split} \tag{8}

It preserves u squared minus v squared and the marked eta=0 point. It also preserves the original logarithmic measure exactly:

durvr=duv,ιτrι=τr−1, \frac{du_r}{v_r}=\frac{du}{v},\qquad \iota\,\tau_r\,\iota=\tau_{r^{-1}}, \tag{8a}

where tau_r is eta to r eta and iota is eta to eta inverse. For the actual whole Q dilation set r=Q in (8). These transports form the multiplicative torus with its inversion involution; the resulting group is dihedral in this precise sense, not automatically nilpotent. At a primitive finite root the dilation itself has finite order. None of these identities drops its whole normal dependence before specialization. For G=vF the actual transformed section representing F(r eta) is

S̃rG=vvrG(ur,vr). \widetilde S_rG=\frac{v}{v_r}G(u_r,v_r). \tag{9}

In particular a clock does not act by an unaccompanied u substitution. Writing D=eta partial_eta=v partial_u+u partial_v, differentiation of (9) gives the exact connection

D̃=D−u/v,MvD=D̃Mv,(D̃G)duNv2=1Nd(G/v). \widetilde D=D-u/v,\qquad M_v D=\widetilde D M_v, \qquad (\widetilde DG)\frac{du}{Nv^2}=\frac1N d(G/v). \tag{10}

Its marked residue is zero. All finite clock derivatives follow before taking any quantum normal or large-N limit. Although (9)-(10) have denominators, the corresponding sections lie in the transported image and the total original form is retained.

Physical port clocks have mixed kernel actions. In the full kernel, input clocks Qa,Qb act by x to Qx,y to Qy; output clocks Q^(a+mu), Q^(b+nu) act by (x,xi) to (Qx,Qxi) and (y,eta) to (Qy,Qeta). Thus (8)-(10) transport precisely the eta component while keeping the simultaneous y rescaling for an output clock. General boundary braiding/prefix adapters still act in their original order. Independent whole crossing couplings commute with M_v, and differentiating them preserves (2), (5) and every finite endpoint in (6).

6. Compatibility with the actual prefix framing cover

The independent proof of the actual prefix chart retains U=mu_j…mu_1, V=mu_(j-1)…mu_1, k=ell_U/ell_V and

z=s(k−1),D=(−1)jz(p−p−1),Ωcut=ℓVBDdlog⁡A∧dlog⁡B. z=s(k-1),\qquad D=(-1)^jz(p-p^{-1}),\qquad \Omega_{cut}=\frac{\ell_V}{BD}d\log A\wedge d\log B. \tag{11}

Here p is the actual balanced local wave momentum and B=1/k at completeness. The two nearest actual output exchange bands have principal ratios p inverse and p, respectively. Distinguish the finite Fourier-band variable eta_F in Sections 1–5 from the geometric parameter eta_geo=p inverse. The boundary action gives the principal Fourier incidence eta_F p=1. This is not a coordinate isomorphism of the original source spaces or a map of their cycles. On this incidence the geometric orientation factor is

vgeo=p−1−p,D=(−1)j+1zvgeo. v_{geo}=p^{-1}-p,\qquad D=(-1)^{j+1}zv_{geo}. \tag{12}

The orientation factor of the exact cap cover consequently has the correct geometric sign line. Retaining the actual inverse-prefix clock, whose whole expression is (q/M)^(j-1) product_(i<j) Q^(J_i), gives the principal equality

ℓV−1(R(+1,−1),0−R(−1,+1),0)Ωcut=(−1)j+1Bzdlog⁡A∧dlog⁡B. \ell_V^{-1}(R_{(+1,-1),0}-R_{(-1,+1),0})\Omega_{cut} =\frac{(-1)^{j+1}}{Bz}d\log A\wedge d\log B. \tag{13}

The density alone is not generally odd: ell_V can differ on the two sheets. Formula (13) includes precisely the prefix factor that would be lost by substituting a developed meridian for the physical one.

Equations (2) and (13) are compatible orientation formulas on that incidence; they are not an equality of their whole selected period complexes. In particular eta_F and the physical position B are independent before that correspondence. Closing the second port by the eta_F constant term does not sum its input position b or identify that sum with a physical B cycle. The marked residue in (4)-(6) involves all finite bands and their endpoint, whereas the existing transverse Gaussian theorem handles fixed bounded port offsets on a regular interior chart. Transporting that full residue/endpoint through the Gaussian comparison remains necessary. In particular (13) alone is not permission to insert a new odd observable into the original knot period.

There is also a direct comparison with the UNINSERTED principal cap measure, including its rational base Jacobian. Write sigma=(-1)^j, t_W=Tr W and

A0=cU+stU,A1=cV+stV. A_0=c_U+s t_U,\qquad A_1=c_V+s t_V.

In the reference frame Tr(JC)=c_C+t_C. In the moving cut frame it is s(beta-gamma)+(1-s)t_C. Using (4.1) of the prefix note gives

ugeo=p+p−1=σ(A0−kA1)s(k−1),∂u∂k|s=σ(A1−A0)s(k−1)2. u_{geo}=p+p^{-1}=\frac{\sigma(A_0-kA_1)}{s(k-1)},\qquad \left.\frac{\partial u}{\partial k}\right|_s =\frac{\sigma(A_1-A_0)}{s(k-1)^2}. \tag{14}

The extra s derivative vanishes after wedging with d log A. Together with D=-sigma s(k-1)v and the density in (11), this proves

ΩcutℓV=k−1A1−A0dlog⁡A∧dugeovgeo=k−1A1−A0dlog⁡A∧dηgeoηgeo. \boxed{\quad \frac{\Omega_{cut}}{\ell_V} =\frac{k-1}{A_1-A_0}\,d\log A\wedge\frac{du_{geo}}{v_{geo}} =\frac{k-1}{A_1-A_0}\,d\log A\wedge\frac{d\eta_{geo}}{\eta_{geo}}. \quad} \tag{15}

This equality is on the further regular chart A_1-A_0 nonzero and k not one. It includes the actual inverse-prefix clock ell_V inverse and an explicit rational Jacobian on the two-position base. Applying (2) to the final one-form in (15) gives the same orientation-twisted section and compensating measure as an algebraic formula. To use it for the period still requires an actual transform of the source and its physical input-position sum. No such transform follows from (15). For a genuine quadratic prefix this Jacobian chart is generically nonempty; a linear prefix or exceptional first-position fiber requires another coordinate rather than division by its vanishing Jacobian.

Formula (15) identifies the geometric density’s algebraic one-form shape with the logarithmic measure appearing in the finite cap. It does not identify their source functionals, transport the marked eta_F=0 cap, sum the independent physical input-position b, or carry the finite tail (6) through the transverse Gaussian. Those remain the precise global comparison data.

6.1. The geometric compactification has a zero-residue boundary

There is an exact algebraic boundary test on the geometric incidence cover, not an image of the original marked finite cap or its cycle. In this subsection eta means eta_geo and u,v are its cover coordinates. Solving (14) for k gives

k=su+σA0su+σA1,k−1=σ(A0−A1)su+σA1. k=\frac{su+\sigma A_0}{su+\sigma A_1},\qquad k-1=\frac{\sigma(A_0-A_1)}{su+\sigma A_1}. \tag{16}

At the geometric eta_geo=0 boundary, u is asymptotic to eta inverse and v to minus eta inverse. Consequently

k−1=σ(A0−A1)sη+O(η2),D→A0−A1. k-1=\frac{\sigma(A_0-A_1)}s\eta+O(\eta^2),\qquad D\longrightarrow A_0-A_1. \tag{17}

At the other preimage eta=infinity the limit is D=A_1-A_0. The two points of this GEOMETRIC compactification reach k=1, or physical B=1, on two algebraic sheets. This does not say that the original eta_F cap maps to either point. At k=1 one has det(U-V)=0, so the exact discriminant is (A_0-A_1)^2. If c_U-c_V is nonzero, the root formula r=(c_C+s h_C-D)/(2c_C) shows that r, and hence ell_V, is finite at (17). On this regular boundary chart,

ℓV(k−1)A1−A0=−σℓV(0)sη+O(η2). \frac{\ell_V(k-1)}{A_1-A_0} =-\frac{\sigma\ell_V(0)}s\eta+O(\eta^2). \tag{18}

Thus the full leading framing density Omega_cut itself is regular at that geometric boundary and has ZERO eta_geo logarithmic residue. The apparent eta logarithm in (15) is canceled by its rational Jacobian. A nonzero cap source cannot be obtained by discarding that Jacobian and declaring a regular constant amplitude there, even if a proposed source map assigned the cap to that boundary. This is a property of the principal prefix chart, not an assertion that such a source map exists or that the selected quantum period vanishes.

For example the genuine figure-eight prefix in Section 6 of the prefix note, at s=1 and u_shape squared plus u_shape plus one equal to zero, has

cU−cV=1,A0−A1=3+ushape,r→−2,ℓV→2+3ushape. c_U-c_V=1,\quad A_0-A_1=3+u_{shape},\quad r\longrightarrow-2,\quad \ell_V\longrightarrow2+3u_{shape}.

All displayed regularity conditions hold there. For a scalar meromorphic amplitude a(eta), the exact residual to compute is

[η0](a(η)ℓV(k−1)A1−A0)dlog⁡A. [\eta^0]\left(a(\eta) \frac{\ell_V(k-1)}{A_1-A_0}\right)d\log A. \tag{19}

A holomorphic amplitude gives zero. If its only negative term is a_-1/eta, the result is -sigma ell_V(0) a_-1/s times d log A; larger poles require the corresponding full finite coefficient sum in (19). The actual finite kernel has negative Fourier-band powers whose order grows with N. They cannot be substituted into a(eta_geo) without the actual source transform. That transform must retain the original source, norm, physical input-position sum, endpoints and any escaping/polar block contribution. The bounded interior Gaussian theorem does not evaluate (19) as the original cap.

The actual boundary-moment and Ward-atom calculation makes this distinction testable. The finite weighted negative-band functional is exactly an original source paired with a K-Ward current and does not equal the identity source on actual signed tensors. An original negative factorial circuit does supply an explicit inverse-norm pole, but its presence in the selected full geometric source is a separate transport question.

7. Reproducible actual tensor checks

The independent audit script uses literal signed braid tensors with their original pivotal, half-Cartan, framing and peripheral scalar lines. It checks (1), (5)-(6), the unknot formula, whole input/output clock rescalings and the connection (10). It also tests the same finite source formula on every port matrix unit with endpoint indices included. The results are recorded in the audit output. These checks validate the finite source change; they are not a global geometric period or volume asymptotic calculation.
