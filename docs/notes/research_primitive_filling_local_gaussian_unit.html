The local primitive-filling coefficient is an algebraic unit

Private written proof, 15 November 2026.

The original circle-tree source determines a particularly simple divisor for its local primitive-charge Gaussian coefficient. On its regular marked chart, that coefficient has no zero except a selected zero-angle factor. This rules out all-order cancellation inside that local Morse block. It does not identify the coefficient with which the original finite source selects the block.

4. The explicit local coefficient theorem

Theorem. Suppose a point on (4), with the retained logarithmic marks, satisfies 𝒫v=−πi\mathcal P_v=-\pi i for all v∈Sv\in S, det⁡J≠0\det J\ne0, and av≠0a_v\ne0 for every selected vertex. The original local primitive-charge block (12) has nonzero leading coefficient. More precisely, A02=(−8)k16m∏v∈Sav2(1+av2)dv−2∏w∉S(1+aw2)dS(w)(∏e∈ESqe)det⁡J.(14) \boxed{ A_0^2=\frac{(-8)^k}{16^m}\, \frac{ \displaystyle\prod_{v\in S}a_v^2(1+a_v^2)^{d_v-2} \displaystyle\prod_{w\notin S}(1+a_w^2)^{d_S(w)} }{ \displaystyle\left(\prod_{e\in E_S}q_e\right)\det J }.} \qquad\text{(14)} The square is a rational unit on that regular primitive critical chart. The coefficient itself is a unit on its retained half-density cover. No restriction on the sizes or signs of the fixed nvn_v is required for this local statement.

Proof. Square (13), use (6), and retain the complex signs: (−1πi)2k(2π)kπk=(−2)k. \left(-\frac1{\pi i}\right)^{2k}(2\pi)^k\pi^k=(-2)^k. Now substitute (9) and sin⁡2tv=4av2/(1+av2)2\sin^2t_v=4a_v^2/(1+a_v^2)^2. Each incident edge supplies one factor 1+av21+a_v^2 at its endpoint. The selected exponent is therefore dv−2d_v-2, and the outside exponent is dS(w)d_S(w). This gives (14). All its numerator and denominator factors are units under the stated hypotheses, including when dv=1d_v=1. ▫\square

On a supplied positive geometric filling with the original core condition Im⁡tv<0\operatorname{Im}t_v<0, the selected zero-angle exclusion follows automatically. The theorem does not establish that an arbitrary hyperbolic short filling admits this chart, a positive decomposition, or the nonsingular Jacobian condition.

5. Formal exactness and the remaining source map

For clarity, at an isolated holomorphic Morse point the complete local stationary series detects formal twisted exactness. Choose local Morse coordinates with phase F*−∑yi2/2F_*-\sum y_i^2/2, put h=N−1h=N^{-1}, and include the coordinate Jacobian in a top density f(y,h)dyf(y,h)\,dy. The normalized Gaussian functional is ℰ(f)=exp(h2∑i∂yi2)f(y,h)|y=0.(15) \mathcal E(f)= \left.\exp\left(\frac h2\sum_i\partial_{y_i}^2\right) f(y,h)\right|_{y=0}. \qquad\text{(15)} It is well defined coefficientwise for holomorphic germs formal in hh. Integration by parts gives ℰ((h∂yi−yi)b)=0\mathcal E((h\partial_{y_i}-y_i)b)=0. Conversely, split the constant coefficient of ff as its value at zero plus ∑iyibi\sum_i y_i b_i. Replacing yibiy_i b_i by h∂yibih\partial_{y_i}b_i modulo these currents and repeating in successive powers of hh gives ker⁡ℰ=∑i(h∂yi−yi)𝒪y[[h]].(16) \ker\mathcal E= \sum_i(h\partial_{y_i}-y_i)\mathcal O_y[[h]]. \qquad\text{(16)} Thus the local top twisted de Rham quotient is the rank-one ℂ[[h]]\mathbb C[[h]] module detected by (15). This statement is formal; it asserts neither convergence of the current primitives nor an equality of analytic global relative periods.

By (14), the local density in (12) has a nonzero class in this quotient, already at leading order. Odd-moment cancellation cannot annihilate its entire Gaussian series. The source’s zero-charge sector illustrates why the trace still matters: (10) is a total derivative, so its zero Fourier mode has zero local stationary series to every order. The factor ℓ\ell in (11) records exactly this cancellation. A positive edge amplitude before taking the trace does not remove it.

Finally, neither (14) nor (16) makes the original finite source a faithful pairing on this local quotient. The selected source still contains the wire lattice modes, all endpoint currents, the other angular charges, the prescribed exterior, and its actual closing. No source-compatible map identifying its geometric contribution with (12) has been proved here. All-order vanishing of a selected scalar therefore cannot be used to infer that this already nonzero local class is exact. That inference would first need the missing comparison with the original relative cycle. The explicit divisor (14) separates the local question from that remaining comparison.
