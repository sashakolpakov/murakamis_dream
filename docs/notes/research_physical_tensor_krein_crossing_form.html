A common physical tensor Krein form for the original crossings

Written research proof, 22 November 2026. The original complete crossing preserves an explicit tensor product of one-head indefinite Hermitian forms. The same form works on overlapping adjacent pairs at arbitrary width. Its full change of coordinates to the prepared flags is proved entrywise, including every cyclic endpoint. The ordinary cup and cap are compatible with this form; the marked cap remains different.

The form is indefinite. These identities give neither a positive whole-source norm nor a scalar noncancellation estimate. In particular, they do not identify the actual paired-face wire with a Hermitian pairing or remove any original pivot or marked boundary factor.

1. Original physical and flag conventions

Use the complete balanced physical heads of [D,W,P,C], with N≥2,d=N−1,q=eπi/N,Q=q2,x=−q−1,λ=x−1=−q,k=(−1)d,Fa=(Q;Q)a.(1) N\geq2,\quad d=N-1,\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad Q=q^2, \quad x=-q^{-1},\quad \lambda=x^{-1}=-q,\quad k=(-1)^d,\quad F_a=(Q;Q)_a . \qquad\text{(1)} Physical indices lie in 0,…,d0,\ldots,d; brackets mean cyclic reduction to this set. Thus Fd=NF_d=N, λ2=Q\lambda^2=Q, and λN=k\lambda^N=k. Every factorial denominator below has index at most dd. Write Z|r⟩=Qr|r⟩Z|r\rangle=Q^r|r\rangle and Rd|r⟩=|d−r⟩R_d|r\rangle=|d-r\rangle. Define on one physical head HN=q−1Z−1Rd,HN|r⟩=qQr|d−r⟩.(2) H_N=q^{-1}Z^{-1}R_d,\qquad H_N|r\rangle=qQ^r|d-r\rangle . \qquad\text{(2)} Directly HN†=HNH_N^\dagger=H_N and HN2=IH_N^2=I. On two heads put 𝒦N=−Nk(HN⊗HN).(3) \mathcal K_N=-Nk(H_N\otimes H_N). \qquad\text{(3)} It is Hermitian and 𝒦N2=N2I\mathcal K_N^2=N^2I.

Let ℬ̃\widetilde{\mathcal B} be the source-fixed invertible flag frame of [D, (27)], with the wraps S1N=1,S2N=kS_1^N=1,S_2^N=k. Its coordinates are |m,s⟩|m,s\rangle. Put a=[−s]a=[-s]. Expanding [D, (25),(27)] gives every physical coefficient: ℬ̃|m,s⟩=∑h=0aAm,a,h|[m+h],a−h⟩,Am,a,h=(−1)hλa−hQma+h(h+1)/2FhFa−h.(4) \begin{split} \widetilde{\mathcal B}|m,s\rangle &=\sum_{h=0}^{a} A_{m,a,h}|[m+h],a-h\rangle,\\ A_{m,a,h} &=\frac{(-1)^h\lambda^{a-h} Q^{ma+h(h+1)/2}}{F_hF_{a-h}} . \end{split} \qquad\text{(4)} Indeed the diagonal rescaling in [D] is λaQma/Fa\lambda^aQ^{ma}/F_a, and λaxh=λa−h\lambda^ax^h=\lambda^{a-h}. This uses the complete physical space, of dimension N2N^2.

In these flag coordinates [D,W] give v=Zs,M=Zm,X|m,s⟩=(1−Q−m)(1−Q1−s)|m−1,s−1⟩,XN=0.(5) \begin{gathered} v=Z_s,\qquad M=Z_m,\\ X|m,s\rangle=(1-Q^{-m})(1-Q^{1-s})|m-1,s-1\rangle, \qquad X^N=0 . \end{gathered} \qquad\text{(5)} Here XX is the physical nilpotent crossing argument of [W], not an additional boundary variable. Define the flag permutation and form ℛ|m,s⟩=|d−m,1−s⟩,J=qℛv−1.(6) \mathcal R|m,s\rangle=|d-m,1-s\rangle,\qquad J=q\mathcal Rv^{-1}. \qquad\text{(6)} All labels in (5),(6) are cyclic. 2. Full physical congruence

The exact relation between the two forms is ℬ̃†𝒦Nℬ̃=J.(8) \boxed{\quad \widetilde{\mathcal B}^{\dagger}\mathcal K_N \widetilde{\mathcal B}=J .\quad} \qquad\text{(8)} We prove all matrix entries. For a left label (m,s)(m,s) and a right label (n,t)(n,t), put a=[−s],b=[−t]a=[-s],b=[-t], and R=a+b−dR=a+b-d. If R<0R<0, (4) and physical complementation in (3) give zero. Otherwise a possibly nonzero term must satisfy h+g=R,m+n+R≡d(mod⁡N).(9) h+g=R,\qquad m+n+R\equiv d\pmod N . \qquad\text{(9)} Every g=0,…,Rg=0,\ldots,R is then valid, with h=R−gh=R-g: the bounds R≤a,bR\leq a,b follow from a,b≤da,b\leq d. For completeness, the only factorial identities needed are Fu¯=(−1)uQ−u(u+1)/2Fu,FuFd−u=N(−1)uQu(u+1)/2.(10) \overline{F_u}=(-1)^uQ^{-u(u+1)/2}F_u,\qquad F_uF_{d-u}=N(-1)^uQ^{u(u+1)/2}. \qquad\text{(10)} The second follows by reversing the last uu factors of FdF_d. In the entry under consideration, (a−h)+(b−g)=d(a-h)+(b-g)=d, so (10) applies without a zero factorial. Also 𝒦N|r,j⟩=−NkQr+j+1|d−r,d−j⟩.(11) \mathcal K_N|r,j\rangle =-NkQ^{r+j+1}|d-r,d-j\rangle . \qquad\text{(11)} Insert (4) into (11), conjugating the left coefficient. Applying (10) to Fa−hFb−gF_{a-h}F_{b-g} yields the following full entry: −kλd−2aQn+b+1−ma+nb+R∑g=0R(−1)gQg(g−1)/2FgFR−g.(12) -k\lambda^{d-2a}Q^{n+b+1-ma+nb+R} \sum_{g=0}^{R} \frac{(-1)^gQ^{g(g-1)/2}}{F_gF_{R-g}} . \qquad\text{(12)} For example the power QR−gQ^{R-g} in this simplification comes from λb−a+h−g=λd−2aQR−g\lambda^{b-a+h-g}=\lambda^{d-2a}Q^{R-g}; hence no wrap sign is being suppressed. The sum in (12) is (1;Q)R/FR(1;Q)_R/F_R, by the finite QQ-binomial theorem. It vanishes for every R>0R>0.

When R=0R=0, (9) implies literally b=d−a,n=d−mb=d-a,n=d-m. The exponent in front of the sum is (n+1)(b+1)−ma≡0(mod⁡N)(n+1)(b+1)-ma\equiv0\pmod N, and −kλd−2a=q−1Q−a=qQb.(13) -k\lambda^{d-2a}=q^{-1}Q^{-a}=qQ^b . \qquad\text{(13)} This is exactly (7), since t=[−b]t=[-b] and s=[1−t]s=[1-t]. Thus (8) holds on every pair of basis vectors, including N=2N=2, even levels, and composite levels.

Both forms in (8) are indefinite. When NN is even their positive and negative indices are both N2/2N^2/2. When NN is odd, those indices are (N2−1)/2(N^2-1)/2 and (N2+1)/2(N^2+1)/2: directly (7) has trace −1-1. In particular (8) is not a positive Gram identity.

3. The original crossing and marked full function

Use the exact original stripped crossing factorization [W, (9)]: W=𝒢ℰN(X),ℰN(X)=∑h=0dXh/Fh=PN(X)−1,PN(X)=∑h=0d(−1)hQh(h−1)/2Xh/Fh,𝒢=∑j=1NgjEj(v),gj=(−1)j−1Qj(j−1)/2,gN=1.(14) \begin{gathered} W=\mathcal G\mathcal E_N(X),\qquad \mathcal E_N(X)=\sum_{h=0}^d X^h/F_h=P_N(X)^{-1},\\ P_N(X)=\sum_{h=0}^d(-1)^hQ^{h(h-1)/2}X^h/F_h,\qquad \mathcal G=\sum_{j=1}^N g_jE_j(v),\quad g_j=(-1)^{j-1}Q^{j(j-1)/2},\quad g_N=1 . \end{gathered} \qquad\text{(14)} The full positive crossing is κW\kappa W, and its negative is κ−1W−1\kappa^{-1}W^{-1}, with the original scalar retained.

4. Cyclic root marks and physical tensor assembly

For the determinant-normalized cyclic function of [D, (14)], denote its two orientations by 𝔡Q,𝔡Q−1\mathfrak d_Q,\mathfrak d_{Q^{-1}}. Their anchored finite products imply 𝔡Q−1(z)𝔡Q(Qz)=1.(19) \mathfrak d_{Q^{-1}}(z)\mathfrak d_Q(Qz)=1 . \qquad\text{(19)} Indeed each factor 1−Qrz1-Q^rz, including r=0r=0, has total exponent −d/N-d/N on the left, while the two central prefactors give (1−zN)d/N(1-z^N)^{d/N}. Continue this identity with its marks.

Put 𝒞t=𝔡Q(tv)\mathcal C_t=\mathfrak d_Q(tv) on the nondegenerate marked cover. When tt and t‾\bar t use conjugate-compatible paths and roots from zero, (19) and the reflection of clock labels give 𝒞t†=J𝒞t‾−1J,𝒞t†J𝒞t‾=J.(20) \mathcal C_t^\dagger=J\mathcal C_{\bar t}^{-1}J, \qquad \mathcal C_t^\dagger J\mathcal C_{\bar t}=J . \qquad\text{(20)} For real tt with a self-compatible mark this is a JJ-isometry. No unrelated choice of a norm root is licensed by (20); singular Fermat values are not evaluated by separating inverse factors.

Conjugate the flat statements (16),(18),(20) by ℬ̃\widetilde{\mathcal B} and use (8). In original physical coordinates they read W†(HN⊗HN)W=HN⊗HN,Fv†(HN⊗HN)Fv=HN⊗HN,𝒞t†(HN⊗HN)𝒞t‾=HN⊗HN.(21) \begin{gathered} W^\dagger(H_N\otimes H_N)W=H_N\otimes H_N,\\ F_v^\dagger(H_N\otimes H_N)F_v=H_N\otimes H_N,\\ \mathcal C_t^\dagger(H_N\otimes H_N)\mathcal C_{\bar t} =H_N\otimes H_N . \end{gathered} \qquad\text{(21)} The scalar −Nk-Nk cancels. At completeness |κ|=1|\kappa|=1, as fixed by the original crossing normalization [C, (15)]. Thus both full signed crossings satisfy the first identity in (21).

For any width bb, set ℋb=HN⊗b\mathscr H_b=H_N^{\otimes b}. On every adjacent pair use its actual signed crossing Ri±1R_i^{\pm1}. Tensoring (21) with the untouched one-head forms proves (Ri±1)†ℋbRi±1=ℋb,𝒲†ℋb𝒲=ℋb(22) (R_i^{\pm1})^\dagger\mathscr H_bR_i^{\pm1} =\mathscr H_b,\qquad \mathcal W^\dagger\mathscr H_b\mathcal W=\mathscr H_b \qquad\text{(22)} for every finite signed word 𝒲\mathcal W, including overlapping pairs. No common positive flag frame or transition-norm estimate is needed: HNH_N is the same physical one-head matrix on every wire.

5. Genuine ordinary turns and the retained marked closing

The actual cup and ordinary cap [D,C] are p=N∑a=0dxa−d|a,d−a⟩,ℓo|a,b⟩=xaNδa+b,d.(23) p=N\sum_{a=0}^d x^{a-d}|a,d-a\rangle,\qquad \ell_o|a,b\rangle=\frac{x^a}{N}\delta_{a+b,d} . \qquad\text{(23)} The second formula uses x−aQ−a=xax^{-a}Q^{-a}=x^a. On degree a+b=da+b=d, HN⊗HNH_N\otimes H_N acts by the literal complementation |a,b⟩↦|d−a,d−b⟩|a,b\rangle\mapsto|d-a,d-b\rangle, because the phase in (11) before its scalar is Qd+1=1Q^{d+1}=1. Conjugating the cup coefficients therefore proves, on the full space, p†(HN⊗HN)=N2ℓo,p†(HN⊗HN)p=0.(24) \boxed{\quad p^\dagger(H_N\otimes H_N)=N^2\ell_o, \qquad p^\dagger(H_N\otimes H_N)p=0 .\quad} \qquad\text{(24)} Both rows vanish off degree dd; on that degree their coefficient is NxaNx^a. The last equality is the original ordinary bubble ℓop=0\ell_op=0. Equivalently p/Np/N and NℓoN\ell_o are dagger compatible for this indefinite tensor form, with their actual order.

The averaged marked row is instead ℓm|a,b⟩=xb/N2\ell_m|a,b\rangle=x^b/N^2, with ℓmp=1\ell_mp=1. Its exact flag row is (ℓmℬ̃)ms=Q−ms/N2(\ell_m\widetilde{\mathcal B})_{ms}=Q^{-ms}/N^2, while ℬ̃−1p=N2|0,1⟩\widetilde{\mathcal B}^{-1}p=N^2|0,1\rangle [D, (29)]. It is not the ordinary row of (24). The original marked cap, pivots, peripheral and framing lines remain attached to every application; none is replaced by a Hermitian adjoint by definition.

This establishes a common indefinite structure for the original crossings and ordinary turns at all levels. It does not make an arbitrary planar source a positive norm square, identify a paired-face seam, bound a general observed period below, or select a geometric integration cycle. The isotropic nonzero cup in (24) already prevents turning these identities into positive reflection by a change of basis.
