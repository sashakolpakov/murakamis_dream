Reflection-compatible original cap metrics, and the remaining leg-order test

29 October 2026. The positive complete-circle form controls the original ordinary and marked boundaries, but with DIFFERENT literal leg flips. This gives an all-width positive boundary estimate with polynomial dual cost. It does not yet identify an arbitrary untwisted fully augmented planar diagram with a Hilbert norm square. A concrete single-leg bend below shows why that last wiring check cannot be omitted.

1. Actual pair sources and the two positive forms

Put N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d. On two original physical heads retain

pa,b=Nx−bδa+b,d,ℓa,b=x−aQ−aδa+b,d/N,ma,b=xb/N2. p_{a,b}=Nx^{-b}\delta_{a+b,d},\quad \ell_{a,b}=x^{-a}Q^{-a}\delta_{a+b,d}/N, \quad m_{a,b}=x^b/N^2.

Let P|a,b>=|b,a> be the literal tensor flip, NOT a crossing. Write m_d=m times the INTEGER pair-degree-d projector. Since x2=Q(-1), the exact row identities are

ℓ=N−2p†P,md=N−3p†=N−1ℓP. \ell=N^{-2}p^\dagger P,\qquad m_d=N^{-3}p^\dagger=N^{-1}\ell P. \tag{1}

The original complete crossing circle satisfies the independently proved full physical positivity theorem:

C=PLN≽0,H=LNP=PCP≽0. C=PL_N\succeq0,\qquad H=L_NP=PCP\succeq0.

The original Hermite source normal t has ell t=1, m_d t=0, L_Nt=2Np. Consequently the already derived ordinary-cap identity is C t=2N^3 ell^dagger and ell C^+ elldagger=1/(2N3).

Conjugating by the TRUE flip P gives the new marked identity

H(Pt)=2Np,mdH+md†=12N5,md†md≼H2N5. H(Pt)=2Np,\qquad \boxed{\displaystyle m_d H^+m_d^\dagger=\frac1{2N^5},\qquad m_d^\dagger m_d\preceq\frac H{2N^5}.} \tag{2}

Indeed H+=PC+P and m_d=ell P/N. Equivalently Pt/(2N^4) is an explicit inverse solution for m_d^dagger, and its pairing with m_d is 1/(2N^5). No inverse of L_N or deletion of its radical is made. Ordinary ell uses C; marked m_d uses H.

Both distinctions matter. Since Cp=0 and m_d p=1, the marked row is not bounded by C. The UNRESTRICTED m is not bounded by H either: H|0,0>=0 while m|0,0>=1/N^2. It is the whole-source degree argument below, not a local marked-row substitution, that permits (2).

2. All-width original marked boundary estimate

Consider the original k-cup plat source p^(tensor k) and a full original signed word W preserving total INTEGER degree. Each cup has degree d. After the k-1 ordinary output caps have fixed their pair degrees d, the remaining marked pair is forced to degree d. Therefore, on the ACTUAL incoming total degree kd,

(ℓ⊗(k−1)⊗m)Wp⊗k=(ℓ⊗(k−1)⊗md)Wp⊗k. (\ell^{\otimes(k-1)}\otimes m)W p^{\otimes k} =(\ell^{\otimes(k-1)}\otimes m_d)W p^{\otimes k}. \tag{3}

Put Lambda=ell^(tensor(k-1)) tensor m_d and

Qtop=C⊗(k−1)⊗H,ck=2−kN−(3k+2). Q_{\mathrm{top}}=C^{\otimes(k-1)}\otimes H,\qquad c_k=2^{-k}N^{-(3k+2)}.

Tensor the actual pair dual norms. This gives EXACTLY

ΛQtop+Λ†=ck,Λ†Λ≼ckQtop. \boxed{\displaystyle \Lambda Q_{\mathrm{top}}^+\Lambda^\dagger=c_k,\qquad \Lambda^\dagger\Lambda\preceq c_k Q_{\mathrm{top}}.} \tag{4}

For the original scalar, with its existing framing/peripheral line chi_N unchanged, one consequently has

|KN|2≤|χN|2ck⟨Wp⊗k,QtopWp⊗k⟩. |K_N|^2\leq|\chi_N|^2c_k \langle Wp^{\otimes k},Q_{\mathrm{top}}Wp^{\otimes k}\rangle. \tag{5}

The estimate extends to arbitrary coefficient Hilbert spaces and keeps every actual pivot already present in ell. It neither assumes W is unitary nor pays a condition number for its unknown state. It is a boundary Cauchy theorem, NOT yet a geometric bound for the positive energy on the right.

On ordinary pairs the original signed same-pair twists are exact C-isometries. On the marked H pair, their REFLECTED conjugates P R^a P are H-isometries. Replacing one by the other without the literal leg interface would not be a source identity.

3. Reflection of original crossings and a genuine bend caveat

The full original equal-color balanced crossing is complex symmetric. In its strict coefficient, reversing input(a,b) and output(b+k,a-k) leaves the full factorial ratio, x power, and Q exponent unchanged. The independently checked actual inverse is Rminus=P conjugate(R)P. Thus

PRminusP=R†. P R_{\mathrm{minus}}P=R^\dagger. \tag{6}

This is the correct reflected-adjoint statement; P remains a leg exchange, not a physical extra braid. Similarly, a reflected ordinary cap is p^dagger P/N^2, whereas the legitimately restricted marked one is pdagger/N3. These are different boundary orientations.

There is a local obstruction to treating PSD as invariant under ARBITRARY planar bending. Regard the actual cup as the matrix N U:

Ua,b=x−bδa+b,d,U†U=I,ℓa,b=(U−1)a,b/N. U_{a,b}=x^{-b}\delta_{a+b,d},\qquad U^\dagger U=I,\qquad \ell_{a,b}=(U^{-1})_{a,b}/N.

Using these very turns to dualize one physical leg of a four-port form B gives

(𝒟2B)ab;cd=∑u,vUbuBav;cu(U−1)vd,𝒟2B=(I⊗U)B𝖳2(I⊗U−1). (\mathcal D_2B)_{ab;cd} =\sum_{u,v}U_{bu}B_{av;cu}(U^{-1})_{vd},\qquad \mathcal D_2B=(I\otimes U)B^{\mathsf T_2}(I\otimes U^{-1}). \tag{7}

The factors N from cup and cap cancel. At N=2 the ACTUAL positive H=L_2P has its sole nonzero block on |01>,|10> equal to

(4−4i4i4). \begin{pmatrix}4&-4i\\4i&4\end{pmatrix}.

Its one-leg transpose, and hence the actually turned form (7), has eigenvalues 4,4,4,-4. Thus a single-leg source duality is NOT in general a positive Hilbert congruence. A bare ordinary bubble gives the related warning ell p=0, whereas replacing ell by pdagger/N2 would incorrectly give N.

This is a statement about a DIFFERENT bipartition of an already formed four-port Gram tensor. It is NOT the genuine antiparallel crossing circle, whose reflection must be transported together with its dual object. The next section verifies the distinction explicitly.

4. Genuine dual objects introduce no local positivity failure

Use standard dual bases for V. With the original antipode conventions, the self-duality isometry from V to V is

T=U𝖳,TS(h)𝖳T−1=h,h∈{H,K,ℰ,F}. T=U^{\mathsf T},\qquad T S(h)^{\mathsf T}T^{-1}=h, \quad h\in\{H,K,\mathcal E,F\}. \tag{8}

For example S(E)=-EK^(-1), S(F)=-KF. Checking the single nonzero entry in each raising/lowering column proves (8), including the endpoints; S(H)=-H and S(K)=K^(-1). The standard coevaluation becomes the original p/N after I tensor T, and standard evaluation becomes N ell after T^(-1) tensor I. Thus this is the actual duality, rather than a guessed rotation of the positive form.

For any choices of the two object orientations, put D=T^(epsilon) tensor T^(eta), epsilon,eta in {0,1}, and transport BOTH the circle and its reflection:

Lϵη=DLND−1,Pϵη=DPD−1,LϵηPϵη=DHD†≽0. L_{\epsilon\eta}=D L_ND^{-1},\qquad P_{\epsilon\eta}=D P D^{-1},\qquad L_{\epsilon\eta}P_{\epsilon\eta}=DHD^\dagger\succeq0. \tag{9}

All D are unitary. In particular no negative eigenvalue in (7) disproves the antiparallel instance of (9).

This transport can be chosen consistently along EVERY oriented diagram wire: assign the appropriate object isometry to that wire, and transform a coupon B by D_out B D_in^(-1). Each contracted internal port cancels a D with its inverse. This elementary tensor identity holds for arbitrary exteriors and all leg orderings. It does not require an extra sign or an arbitrary new crossing.

If left and right self-duality conventions are instead identified, their exact discrepancy is the original pivot. Namely, with g_a=x(-d)Q(-a),

U−1U𝖳=g,U𝖳U−1=g−1,UgU−1=g−1. U^{-1}U^{\mathsf T}=g,\qquad U^{\mathsf T}U^{-1}=g^{-1},\qquad UgU^{-1}=g^{-1}. \tag{10}

These identities are direct antidiagonal multiplication. They show which unitary clocks must remain when a turn changes sides. Their existence permits a consistent genuine-dual description; it does NOT license deleting a pivotal holonomy on a closed component.

The unresolved global check is therefore more specific than an orientation-sign problem. One must exhibit the projection-plane cut of the ACTUAL fully augmented tensor graph, with its remaining wire contractions and original turns, as a common ket/bra pairing of the transported positive forms (9), or another positive boundary pairing of polynomial cost. A positive coupon on each face is not itself that graph identity. The original ordinary bubble, for example, is ell p=0, whereas replacing its pivotal contraction by p^dagger p/N^2 would give N.

No proof that a correctly cut reduced fully augmented diagram must use the bad bipartition (7) is asserted. Conversely no arbitrary global norm-square theorem follows here merely from (9). Until the complete wiring identity is proved, (5) and (9) are the supported all-width source and oriented-local results; the global geometric energy estimate remains open.

It also checks the literal full crossings, all source inverse solutions, original degree restriction, and both left/right pivotal discrepancies.
