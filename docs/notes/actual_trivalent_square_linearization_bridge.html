A short finite square-linearization proof of the positive source scalar

29 October 2026. This proves the remaining source-coefficient-to-short-series identity by finite product linearization and root cancellation. It is independent of the Jackson-Leibniz/Newton derivation. The input is the already proved original-source coefficient theorem, not a proposed 6j formula. All root denominators and zero coefficients are retained.

Put N>=2, d=N-1, Q^N=1 with Q primitive, F_k=(Q;Q)_k. Take retained triangle labels a,b,c<=floor(d/2), let J=a+b+c, delta=J-d>=0, r=b+c-a, and H=2c-r=a+c-b=d-2b+delta. In particular

0<=delta<=min(a,b,c,H), 0<=r<=2min(b,c), 2b,2c<N.

The reversed coefficient in the actual root-Fourier theorem is

C_delta=[U^delta](UQ^-b;Q)_b^2
   *sum_(e=0)^H [V^e](V;Q)_c^2
                     F_(2c-e)/F_(H-e) U^e.       (1)

The first three sections prove exactly

C_delta=T_0 H_c,
T_0=Q^[delta(delta-1-2b)] F_b^2 F_(2c)
                     /[F_H F_delta F_(b-delta)^2],
H_c=sum_(k=0)^delta
  (Q^-delta,Q^-c,Q^-c,Q^-H;Q)_k Q^k
              /(Q,Q^-2c,Q^(b-delta+1),Q^(b-delta+1);Q)_k. (2)

Every denominator here is nonzero. No ordering such as a=max(a,b,c) is required. Section 4 completes the finite Sears transformation and explicit phase conversion to the positive factorial partition.

1. An elementary finite product linearization

For generic v and nonnegative m,n,

(U;v)_m(U;v)_n
  =sum_(k=0)^min(m,n) A_(m,n,k) U^k(U;v)_(m+n-k),
A_(m,n,k)=(-1)^k v^[k(k-1)/2]
             (v;v)_m(v;v)_n
          /[(v;v)_k(v;v)_(m-k)(v;v)_(n-k)].      (3)

Here is a finite induction. The m=0 case is immediate. With S=m+n,

(1-v^m U)U^k(U;v)_(S-k)
  =U^k(U;v)_(S+1-k)
     +(v^(S-k)-v^m)U^(k+1)(U;v)_(S-k).

Consequently the next coefficient is

A_(m,n,k)+(v^(m+n-k+1)-v^m)A_(m,n,k-1)
                                          =A_(m+1,n,k).

Substitution of the displayed factorials proves this equality; the interior ratio is (1-v(m+1))/(1-v(m+1-k)). Endpoints follow by clearing denominators and setting out-of-range coefficients to zero. Thus (3) is a polynomial identity, and specializes at Q with m=n=c because c<N. The largest product length 2c is also below N here.

Apply the coefficient multiplier

V^e -> F_(2c-e)U^e/F_(H-e), 0<=e<=H,

to (3) with m=n=c, discarding coefficients above H. In its k-th term the finite binomial coefficient [2c-k choose e-k]Q cancels the factor F(2c-e). The remaining finite binomial theorem gives

sum_(e=0)^H [V^e](V;Q)_c^2 F_(2c-e)U^e/F_(H-e)
  =sum_(k=0)^min(c,H) (-1)^k Q^[k(k-1)/2]
      F_c^2 F_(2c-k)
      /[F_k F_(c-k)^2 F_(H-k)] U^k(U;Q)_(H-k).  (4)

No coefficient beyond the genuine derivative range H is introduced.

2. The exact root coefficient cancellation

For 0<=n<=b put

I_n=[U^n](UQ^-b;Q)_b^2(U;Q)_(d-2b+n).

Since (U;Q)_N=1-U^N and n<N, coefficient extraction gives exactly

I_n=[U^n] (UQ^-b;Q)_b
                    /(UQ^(n-1-2b);Q)_(b+1-n).  (5)

Indeed the missing factor in the length-N product runs from exponent n-1-2b through -1. Its last b factors cancel one of the two numerator products. Formal division is valid because every Euler factor has constant coefficient one. The discarded numerator term U^N cannot affect the coefficient n.

Set h=b+1-n>=1 and z=Q^(n-1-2b). The reciprocal finite-product coefficient is zl(Qh;Q)_l/F_l: it follows either by multiplying the factors, or from the recurrence obtained by shifting U to QU. Expanding the numerator in (5), and dividing by its v=0 term, gives

I_n=z^n (Q^h;Q)_n/F_n
                   *sum_(v=0)^n (Q^-n;Q)_v Q^[(b+1)v]/F_v.

The factors (Q^-b;Q)_v cancel in numerator and denominator; they are nonzero since v<=n<=b<N. The finite binomial theorem evaluates the sum as (Q(b+1-n);Q)_n=(Qh;Q)_n. Therefore

I_n=Q^[n(n-1-2b)] F_b^2/[F_n F_(b-n)^2].         (6)

This proof includes n=0 and uses no analytic limit or infinite series.

3. Consecutive terms give the exact short series

Insert (4) into (1). Only k<=delta contributes, and put n=delta-k. Since H-k=d-2b+n, equation (6) applies term by term. The result is

C_delta=sum_(k=0)^delta T_k,
T_k=(-1)^k Q^[k(k-1)/2+(delta-k)(delta-k-1-2b)]
   *F_b^2 F_c^2 F_(2c-k)
   /[F_k F_(c-k)^2 F_(H-k) F_(delta-k) F_(b-delta+k)^2]. (7)

Every factor is a physical factorial with index in 0,…,d. The initial term is precisely T_0 in (2). Its consecutive ratio is

T_(k+1)/T_k
  =Q * (1-Q^(k-delta))(1-Q^(k-c))^2(1-Q^(k-H))
       /[(1-Q^(k+1))(1-Q^(k-2c))
                              (1-Q^(b-delta+k+1))^2]. (8)

The apparent extra power in this ratio is Q(H-delta+2b+2)=Q(N+1)=Q. This is exactly the root relation that is needed, rather than a generic-q continuation of the root source. Equations (7),(8) prove (2).

All denominator ranges are safe: k+1<=delta<=c,b; 2c-k is positive and below N for every ratio used; b-delta+k+1<=b<N. Empty sums and delta=0 give the same formula without a ratio. This closes the finite source coefficient bridge. Its remaining finite normalizations are evaluated next. Physical CD and magnetic tensor normalizations remain distinct from this scalar calculation.

4. The symmetric positive scalar, with its full original phase

Put r_1=a+b-c, r_2=H=a+c-b. In (2), use the root equality Q(b-delta+1)=Q(-a-c), and apply the own finite Sears identity with length delta, retained parameter Q^-c, and both selected denominators Q^(-a-c). It gives

H_c=K_1 H_sym,
K_1=(Q^-a,Q^(-a-b);Q)_delta
                    /(Q^(-a-c),Q^-r_1;Q)_delta,
H_sym=4phi3(Q^-delta,Q^-a,Q^-b,Q^-c;
                       Q^(-a-b),Q^(-a-c),Q^(-b-c);Q,Q). (9)

Every descending denominator index is at least delta, and every upper factorial index is below N. Thus this is a direct finite specialization. No division by H_sym is used. Its kth term is strictly positive at the principal root: the sine factors are positive, and their residual phase is q1=q(Nk)=(-1)k, cancelling the denominator’s remaining (-1)^k. The k=0 term is one.

Here is a short explicit normalization of the ACTUAL source. Let

E_F=3a^2+b^2+c^2-2ab-2ac+2bc-2c-2,
E_src=2a^2-ab-ac+bc-J-3,
tau_u=(-1)^u q^[u(u+1)], F_u F_(d-u)=N tau_u.

The original Fourier theorem says

A=N q^[E_F+2b(b+1)-2c(c-1)]
                     C_delta/(F_a F_b F_c F_r). (10)

Converting K_1 to factorials introduces NO phase: the sums of its two numerator and denominator descending parameters agree. Explicitly

K_1=F_a F_(a+b) F_(d-b) F_(d-2c)
        /[F_(a-delta) F_(d-c) F_(a+c) F_(r_1)].

Insert T_0 from (2) into (10) and reflect complements. Its remaining factorial prefactor is exactly

N^2 tau_b tau_(2c) tau_(a+b)/(tau_c tau_(a+c))
  /[F_r F_(r_1) F_(r_2) F_delta
                       F_(a-delta) F_(b-delta) F_(c-delta)]. (11)

The tau signs multiply to (-1)^(2b)=1. The seven denominator indices sum to 2d, so F_u=(-i)^u q2s_u contributes the net sign (-1)^d. If E_total is the remaining q exponent, direct expansion gives

E_total-E_src+N(J+1)=2N(2b+1).

Thus the exact phase of (11), including (10), is (-1)(N+J)qE_src. Its positive modulus proves

A=(-1)^(N+J)q^E_src D_sym H_sym,
D_sym=N^2/[s_r s_(r_1)s_(r_2)s_delta
                     s_(a-delta)s_(b-delta)s_(c-delta)]>0. (12)

This proves nonvanishing on EVERY supported triangle, at both parities.

For completeness the same symmetric series is the positive R_N. Choose a maximal label a for this SYMMETRIC positive object. Its first term and the first finite Sears prefactor are

R_0=s_a s_(b+c)/[s_(a-b)s_(a-c)s_b s_c s_r],
K_0=(Q^(a+1),Q^-2b;Q)_r
                          /(Q^(a-b+1),Q^-b;Q)_r.

The long series is obtained by the consecutive ratio of the seven sine-factorial summand. The proved Sears identity changes it to H_b, and the same identity changes H_b to K_2 H_sym, where

K_2=(Q^-c,Q^(-a-c);Q)_delta
                     /(Q^(-b-c),Q^-r_2;Q)_delta.

The finite denominator ranges are safe since r<=b,c and delta<=r_2. The phases in K_0 and K_2 cancel internally, so both are positive. Their sine-factorial products and reflection s_u s_(d-u)=N give

R_0 K_0 K_2
  =N^3/[s_a s_b s_c s_r s_(r_1)s_(r_2)
                     s_(a-delta)s_(b-delta)s_(c-delta)]. (13)

Both sides are symmetric; the choice of a maximal chart places no restriction on the source ordering. Combining (12),(13) proves the original all-channel identity

A=(-1)^(N+J)q^E_src [s_a s_b s_c/(N s_delta)]R_N.

The source is zero for J<d by the independent Fourier degree theorem. This entire finite proof concerns the actual black scalar. It does not bound its magnetic tensor or replace the marked white co-source.
