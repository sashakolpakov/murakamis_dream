A geometric source block and a bounded current on the prism tube

21 November 2026. Research note. Earlier finals and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The original centered tensor is an explicit nonzero geometric block plus an absolutely bounded correction on the whole strict prism-angle tube, with independent moduli up to the unit boundary on its three legs. Wherever the geometric height is nonnegative this gives its nonzero leading asymptotic. In particular it transports the initialized packet along every strict positive-prism radial path, on compact families away from the origin. The coefficient is determined by the original finite source.

The proof uses a canonical Gamma interpolation of its exact wall residues. A norm-corrected finite rectangle removes both outer pole families before any estimate. Its moving Cauchy pole gives an explicit nonzero geometric term. The remaining complete vertical current is uniformly bounded after its stated normalization. No absolute gap between the geometric action and an outer-facet action is assumed.

This is a theorem about one universal multiplication tensor. It does not move the assembled Conway coefficient cycle or prove a knot volume identity after arbitrary short fillings.
