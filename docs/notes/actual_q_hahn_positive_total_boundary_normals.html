Genuine numerator normals at positive-total framing boundaries

30 October 2026. A uniform spectral gap for the SUM of the two original positive matrices controls the actual generic numerator in both the202 and222 boundary regimes. The actual total square root and every logarithmic head lift remain in the source normalization. Near the fold, all fixed genuine quantum derivatives have subexponential relative cost when the partner separation satisfies s log N=o(N).

This is a numerator theorem. It does not assign a generic amplitude to a root collision or prove a global source period. The separate root222 determinant action and the root202 coefficient packet retain their own normalizations and hypotheses.

1. An exact uniform gap, including the folded endpoint

Let theta=pi/N, N>=3, and 1<=a<=floor((N-1)/2). Use the actual Hermitian H_-,H_+ from the source determinant, with diagonal d_r=1/[cos(r theta)cos((r+1)theta)] and upper entries

sqrt(B_n)[sin(n theta) +/- i cos(n theta)],
B_n=sin^2(n theta)/[cos^2(n theta)
                  sin((2n-1)theta)sin((2n+1)theta)].

Then EXACTLY

H_-+H_+ >= cos(theta) I >= I/2.                      (1)

Here is a direct weighted-path proof. Put J=(H_-+H_+)/2 and w_r=sqrt(sin((2r+1)theta)), 0<=r<a. Alternating-sign conjugation turns its real offdiagonal e_n=sqrt(B_n)sin(n theta) negative. For an interior row r, subtracting its two weighted neighbors from the diagonal gives

d_r-e_r w_(r-1)/w_r-e_(r+1)w_(r+1)/w_r
   = [sin(r theta)cos(r theta)/(1+sin(r theta))
      +sin((r+1)theta)cos((r+1)theta)
                                  /(1+sin((r+1)theta))]
                                  /sin((2r+1)theta)
   >= cos(theta)/2.                                 (2)

For the equality, use sin((2r+1)theta)=sin(r theta)cos((r+1)theta) +sin((r+1)theta)cos(r theta). For the inequality, bound both denominators 1+sin by 2 and use sin(r theta)cos(r theta)+sin((r+1)theta)cos((r+1)theta) =sin((2r+1)theta)cos(theta).

The r=0 formula has its left term zero. At the final row r=a-1, subtracting the finite positive quantity sin^2(a theta)/[cos(a theta)sin((2a-1)theta)] from the ACTUAL one-neighbor residual gives exactly the expression in (2). Thus that actual residual is larger. This last comparison does not introduce a virtual B_a or w_a: at the maximal odd fold those separate quantities would be singular or zero. All weights and coefficients actually used in the matrix are positive. For a=1 the same finite comparison applies with no actual neighbor. Finally, for x_r=w_r y_r the quadratic form is the sum of e_n w_(n-1)w_n |y_n-y_(n-1)|^2 and the weighted residual diagonal terms. Thus (2) proves J>=cos(theta)I/2 and hence (1). No limiting matrix or unproved eigenvalue asymptotic is used.

2. The actual total phase and the two boundary domains

Fix the actual logarithmic heads sigma,u and put, GENERICALLY,

q=q0 exp(epsilon), Q=q^2, B=Q^-N,
U=q^(2u), V=q^(2(sigma-u)), b=q^sigma,
X=qU, Y=qV, T=XY=q^2UV,
w=qb=q^(sigma+1), z=b/U.

In particular T=w^2 and z/w=1/X exactly. The square root w is the original exponential, not a freely chosen branch. At the root, the exact normalized source is

H_n^1(U,V)=(qUV)^n K_n det M_n,
M_n=X^-1 I-H_-/2-H_+/(2T),                         (3)

where K_n=[2n choose n]_Q for the small channel n and the matrices are its n-row truncations. Indeed the original monic determinant is R_n(z)=det[zI-(wH_-+w^-1H_+)/2]; divide its matrix by w and use H_n=b^n K_n R_n. Thus the factor w^n, including its sign and every integer logarithmic character, is retained in (qUV)^n.

For physical half-index heads write X=exp(2pi i x), Y=exp(2pi i y), sigma=N(x+y)-1. Then w=exp(pi i(x+y)) exactly. On the canonical physical logarithms, when T is positive and both arguments lie strictly between 0 and 2pi, their sum is 2pi and w is the NEGATIVE square root of T. The same sign persists in that marked neighborhood. If another actual integral lift changes this sign, (3) retains the change; none of the estimates below replaces it by the canonical sign.

Let T at epsilon=0 range in a sufficiently small fixed complex neighborhood of a compact subset of (0,infinity). Two domains are allowed:

  • 202: X/N^2 belongs to a fixed compact subset of C*.
  • 222: X belongs to a fixed compact neighborhood of a negative real compact subset of C*.

Require the marked heads to satisfy |sigma|+|u|<=C N(1+log N). These are conditions on the actual arguments, not on an arbitrarily reset logarithm. In202, Y=T/X is small; in222 both raw finite clocks are near negative reals. The actual prefix-sign theorem supplies precisely these limiting signs where its dictionary applies.

For all sufficiently large N, on both domains the matrix in (3) satisfies

Re(-M_n)>=cI,     ||M_n^-1||<=C,                   (4)

uniformly for all small n through the fold. Indeed Re(1/T)>=eta>0, and (1) bounds H_-+eta H_+ below by a fixed positive multiple of I. For202 the term 1/X is O(N^-2), so it cannot close that gap. For222, Re(1/X)<0 adds a positive diagonal term. Thus the root numerator is nonzero. This proof retains the additive identity term and does not infer an inverse bound from positivity alone.

3. A genuine generic disk and its cofactor

The all-degree original generic recurrence, with B=Q^-N retained, is

R_(n+1)=(z+alpha_n)R_n+beta_n R_(n-1).

Its coefficients and rational all-degree proof are in the actual paired-normal theorem. Here the generic scalar is K_n(q)=(B2Q(n+1);Q)_n/(BQ;Q)_n, whose root value is the Gaussian binomial in (3). Its generic derivatives are not those of that root simplification. Set G_n=R_n/w^n. As exact generic identities,

G_(n+1)=(X^-1+alpha_n/w)G_n+(beta_n/w^2)G_(n-1),
H_n^B(U,V)=(qUV)^n K_n G_n.                         (5)

For n<=a the nonzero original spectral differences are bounded below by cN^-2 on a small polynomial disk. Here b and w are bounded above and away from zero; their derivatives have size O(N log N). The quotient formulas for alpha_n,beta_n therefore give

(alpha_n/w)'=O(N^5(1+log N)),
(beta_n/w^2)'=O(N^7(1+log N)).                       (6)

The original b_n,d_n in those formulas are O(1), with derivatives O(N(1+log N)); the preceding u_n and v_n have sizes O(N2),O(N4). This gives (6) directly, with no generic specialization of a root reflection. The derivative of X^-1 is no larger than O(N log N) in either domain and is smaller in202.

For each edge of the actual root matrix M_a, freeze whichever of its upper and lower entries has larger absolute value. Their SUM is

-sqrt(B_n)sin(n theta)(1+T^-1).

Since sqrt(B_n)>=1/2 and |1+T^-1| is bounded below, the frozen entry has absolute value at least c/N for every n=1,…,a-1. The first bound follows directly from sin((2n-1)theta)sin((2n+1)theta)<=sin^2(2n theta) and the displayed formula for B_n. Keep it in its original orientation and put the other generic entry equal to -beta_n/(w^2 frozen_entry). The diagonal is X^-1+alpha_n/w. At the root this is exactly M_a, with all edge phases intact. At generic q its determinant and every leading principal minor are exactly the recurrence in (5), since each adjacent product is the original one. No constant choice of upper versus lower is required, and no parameter-dependent similarity is silently dropped.

By (6) this generic matrix differs from its root by O(N^8(1+log N)|epsilon|) in operator norm. Choose

|epsilon|<=rho_N=cN^-10.                            (7)

Equations (4),(6) give uniformly bounded inverse and two-sided determinant ratio. Indeed the logarithm of the determinant ratio costs at most O(N^9(1+log N)rho_N), which is bounded and tends to zero. The last diagonal inverse entry is the actual cofactor, so

|G_(a-1)/G_a|<=C.                                  (8)

The exact source scalar K_a has logarithmic derivative O(N^3), and (qUV)^a has logarithmic derivative O(N^2(1+log N)). Their two-sided ratios are bounded on (7). Thus the small H_a itself has bounded two-sided relative variation on this disk.

4. The actual large partner and every fixed normal

Let m=d-a, s=m-a, with 0<=s<=eta N and eta sufficiently small. The literal root identity is H_m1=P_s(UV)H_a1, where P_s(UV)=product_(r=a)(m-1)(UV-Qr). Since UV=q0^-2T is near a positive real compact and these r lie near N/2, every added factor is bounded above and away from zero. In particular H_m^1 is nonzero.

On the boundary of (7), the original folded spectral differences are at least c rho_N; all others are at least cN^-2. The original generic recurrence coefficients alpha_n/w and beta_n/w^2 are therefore at most C rho_N^-1 and C rho_N^-2. Starting from (8), propagating the s rows costs at most (CN20)s.

The exact generic K_m/K_a ratio has 2s numerator factors and 2s denominator factors. Those denominator indices stay near N/2, so the ratio costs only C^s. The factor (qUV)^s also costs C^s relative to the retained root P_s(UV). Thus on the circle, and then throughout its disk,

|H_m^B(U(q),V(q))/H_m^1(U(q0),V(q0))|
                    <=C(CN^20)^s.                  (9)

The passage inside uses the original finite H_m sum: all its generic factorial denominators have indices below N and are units on this disk. Singular monic R_m factors were never assigned a holomorphic root value. Cauchy’s formula now gives, for each fixed k,

|partial_epsilon^k H_m^B|_0
   <=C_k N^(10k)(CN^20)^s |H_m^1(U(q0),V(q0))|.     (10)

For s=0 this follows directly from the small determinant argument. For bounded s the cost is polynomial, covering both maximal folded parities. For s log N=o(N) it is subexponential. This does not cover a fixed nonzero scaled partner separation and does not give a nonzero leading normal amplitude. The source factor (qUV)^n, the integer characters and every marked head are retained before taking the derivative.

The result applies to the individual actual202/222 numerator factors. Any surrounding white monomials, cups, pivots, marks and finite support cycles must still use their literal source identities and their own estimates. Neither the positive gap nor finite-jet control selects the original global relative period.

Focused audit: positive-total source and retained-normal checks, with saved results. The 120-digit run checks both exact trigonometric identities, 389 actual weighted rows including 24 finite last-row comparisons, 24 folded matrix gaps, 12 original root phase/determinant identities, 48 original generic determinants and 48 cofactors, 48 actual large-partner generic-circle values and 24 first/second normals. Four cases have one root edge EXACTLY zero and retain the opposite edge. Twelve tests keep identical root clocks but change sigma to sigma+N; their genuine first normals differ, verifying why marked lifts cannot be reset. Maximum source error was below 3.3e-119. These finite checks supplement the proof; they do not establish its uniform estimates by numerical sampling.

The result remains the stated individual-numerator, shrinking-channel normal bound; no leading normal amplitude, whole-network contraction estimate or global relative-cycle coefficient is inferred.
