Long-filling volume for one-end-layer plats at every bridge width

theorem, 31 October 2026. This is a right-end extension of the reduced-layer construction. It retains the original flat-plat source, first-cap mark, all half twists and all independently signed full twists. The proof covers every k≥2k\geq2; it does not assert balance for every certified plat.

All 3k−33k-3 twist parameters can have independent signs and magnitudes. The result holds at both level parities. No comparability of twist magnitudes or joint growing-twist/level limit is asserted. Moreover, in the congruence subfamily where every exponent in (2) is divisible by 33, the minimal bridge number is exactly kk. The congruence is used only for that lower bound, not for (3).

The analytic implication is the general complete-radius balance theorem. It remains to prove its hypotheses for the actual source (1), uniformly in kk, which occupies Sections 2–5 below.

6. Full source, knot parity and exact bridge subfamilies

The general complete-radius balance filling theorem now proves (3), including the full original one-sided remainder, the entire all-charge period, the original normalization and both parities. Its threshold can depend on the fixed diagram and prescribed parity, and no claim is made for arbitrary certified diagrams failing balance.

The first O layer has odd exponent on the zero-based slots 1,3,…,2k−31,3,\ldots,2k-3. The resulting transpositions (12),(34),…,(2k−32k−2)(1\ 2),(3\ 4),\ldots,(2k-3\ 2k-2) join the initially kk cap components successively. All remaining boxes have even exponent. Therefore the original endpoint-pairing graph is one cycle and the plat in (2) is a knot. This argument includes k=2k=2 and does not confuse zero-based slots with conventional generator indices.

The Fox-coloring certificate applies without change: if every actual exponent in (2) is divisible by 33, every full crossing box acts as the identity on ordered 𝔽3\mathbb F_3 colors, and the standard cup/cap register has exactly 3k3^k colorings. Any bb-bridge knot has at most 3b3^b colorings. Together with the supplied kk-bridge plat this gives minimal bridge number exactly kk. Each required exponent congruence is compatible with its prescribed parity and independent arbitrarily large signed full twists. This identifies unbounded actual bridge complexity, without putting a congruence restriction on the volume theorem.

No finite search over k or numerical packing values are used to prove this uniform statement. The reduced-family proof supplies the recurrence algebra, comparison principle and source Q normalization; this note proves the changed right boundary and the literal all-width incidence explicitly.

Validation and scope

The proof applies to every fixed width, but does not provide a single filling threshold independent of the diagram or width. It proves this explicit family, not a universal balance condition for every certified plat.

The focused exact audit and recorded results check the original right-end box incidences, first mark, reflection and knot parity at 39 widths, six changed-boundary identities, and all 36 exact circle-support pairs and nine port tests of the width-two and width-three seeds. This supplements the analytical proof; the old interior recurrence audit is not repeated, and finite tests are not used to infer the uniform or asymptotic conclusion.
