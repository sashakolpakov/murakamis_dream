Riccati transport for the original physical edge rows

Research note, 3 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

The original physical-row recurrence has a useful contraction that survives complex free colors. It gives a uniform exponential asymptotic for ratios of actual kernel values, with a bounded nonzero transport factor. The phase is the original edge-phase difference. Only the free-color endpoint is complex in this statement; the other endpoint remains on the physical grid.

The argument uses the exact positive-real ratios from the Jacobi boundary problem. It does not infer nonzero transport from a separation of two formal characteristic roots.

1. The original recurrence and its contracting domain

3. The logarithmic error and the nonzero transport factor

On ℜw<c0\Re w<c_0, define the logarithm of the actual normalized ratio by Li,j,N(w)=∑r=i+1jLog⁡ρr,N(w),exp⁡Li,j,N=Fj,N/Fi,N.(19) L_{i,j,N}(w)= \sum_{r=i+1}^{j}\operatorname{Log}\rho_{r,N}(w), \qquad \exp L_{i,j,N}=F_{j,N}/F_{i,N}. \qquad\text{(19)} This is the holomorphic branch that is real on the real free-color interval. 5. Why the determinant formula does not replace the contraction

The existing Jacobi representation gives the exact Stieltjes formula Fj,N(w)=det⁡(𝒥j−wI)∏r<jAr,N,−∂wlog⁡Fj,N(w)=Tr⁡(𝒥j−wI)−1.(26) F_{j,N}(w)= \frac{\det(\mathcal J_j-wI)}{\prod_{r<j}A_{r,N}}, \qquad -\partial_w\log F_{j,N}(w) =\operatorname{Tr}(\mathcal J_j-wI)^{-1}. \qquad\text{(26)} It proves zero-freeness and bounds free-color derivatives. By itself it does not compare neighboring row sizes at the required precision. The Riccati map is the exact Schur-complement recursion for these determinants. Its contraction (7) supplies the quantitative attraction and the bounded logarithmic error, without a separate assumption about spectral-measure convergence.
