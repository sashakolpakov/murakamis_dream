The retained Pachner ratio in the original physical flag algebra

Research note, 22 November 2026. The residual cyclic factorial in the first source-preserving nine-cell beta move has an explicit realization on the original two quantum-group heads. It is a commutator of the existing cyclic dilogarithm with a completed physical flag shift. The completion retains its missing corner, and the change of coordinates retains the full original exterior covector.

The oscillator preparation used below is already proved in [P]. The new identification is with the two particular boundary clocks of [R, (31)]. It does not identify the other factors of that source with the nilpotent factors of the mixed pentagon.

3. The cyclic factor and its finite commutator

7. Scope

The physical operator construction (1)–(29) is valid for all N≥2N\geq2, even or composite. The existing literal nine-cell source occurrence (17)–(19) is proved in [R,E] for odd N>2N>2; nothing here extends that charged triangulation definition to even levels. The physical space throughout has dimension N2N^2, including all original head coordinates and their original crossing endpoint data.

This realizes the specific retained ratio as a coupon in the actual flag algebra. It does not show that the remaining WW-factors in [R, (31)] are the neighboring P(aΔ)P(a\Delta) factors in [A, (24)]. The negative adapter [R, (18)] uses a mixed input/output pair of boundary indices; (23) does not assert the same left-output endomorphism formula for it without a further typed dual transport. Nor does it justify cancelling adjacent commutators without checking their full shared frames and inverse-dual exterior. No further Pachner move, geometric source identification, whole-word norm bound or scalar noncancellation follows from (23) alone. At a singular Fermat wall, the relative expressions and their source lattices must be retained; separate inverse factors in (14)–(23) are not evaluated there.
