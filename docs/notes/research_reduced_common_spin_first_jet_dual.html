The actual reduced first jet in one hybrid spin polarization

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

Both Cartan terms of the actual reduced pointed source have one and the same finite spin-coherent integrand. Their difference is carried by explicit rational scalar densities at one interface. This remains exact with arbitrary cyclic diagonal basis frames, including physical and factorial choices on different heads. The whole meridian pole, its specified integrated residue, and its first finite part are retained.

The scalar first-normal density is a uniform unit on compact complex charts whose total spin occupation is nonreal. This supplies a source dual in the same polarization as hybrid spin kernels. It does not estimate the angular period or its geometric relative-cycle coefficient.

The finite spin resolution is existing coverage in compact_coherent_source_period.md. The beta normalization and rotation used here are proved directly. The complete proof directs are R and G, pinned at the end.

5. Scope of the exact adapter

All formulas use one finite spin resolution and one cyclic list of actual frames. In particular hybrid choices of physical and factorial heads need no asymptotic passage through a Bargmann kernel to use (16) or (21). Every finite occupation cap is already present in (4).

The rotated incidence divisors 1+Q±1zjwj=01+Q^{\pm1}z_jw_j=0 of (11) remain part of the exact complex density. Although they avoid each fixed compact nonincident chart for large NN, their possible global contour currents are not discarded. The beta rotation proves the original cycle identity; it does not transport an arbitrary chosen geometric window through these divisors.

Likewise (19) need not hold on an angular subchain. Its restricted pole coefficient and all frame-connection currents must then be retained. A separately prescribed scalar line multiplies the whole original expression, before its required finite part or derivative. No rate or nonvanishing is assigned to that line here.

The conclusion is an exact common-spin source dual and a local scalar normal unit. Neither the angular pairing, its geometric coefficient, nor the full original-source lower bound is proved by this adapter.
