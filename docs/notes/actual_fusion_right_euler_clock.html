Exact fusion relates the right Euler operator to physical clocks

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The actual two-leg fusion kernel gives an exact polynomial operator relation between the RIGHT lowering coefficient and physical/relative clocks. All nilpotent endpoints are retained. This provides a concrete coordinate relation for the Euler cap rather than guessing that its argument is a physical Fourier momentum. It does not yet supply a geometric period map or a selected geometric sector.

1. The actual finite kernel

Use the actual Borel fusion on two nilpotent heads. Their highest K eigenvalues x,y are invertible. Set F_j=(Q;Q)_j, with Q of exact order N. Relative basis vectors are f^a e_j, where f is the TOTAL physical lowering operator acting on the LEFT. Their images under the actual fusion C are f^a(e_0 tensor e_j). In the physical basis (u,v), the kernel is exactly

Cu,v;a,j={xu−aFaFvFa−uFj,0≤u≤a,v=j+a−u<N,0,otherwise. C_{u,v;a,j}= \begin{cases} x^{u-a}\displaystyle\frac{F_aF_v}{F_{a-u}F_j}, &0\leq u\leq a,\quad v=j+a-u<N,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \tag{1}

To prove (1), write f=A_0+B_0 with A_0=F_1 and B_0=K_1 inverse F_2. Then B_0 A_0=Q A_0 B_0. In the q-binomial expansion of f^a, the term with u first-leg lowering steps has coefficient F_a/[F_u F_(a-u)]. Acting on the source gives F_u from the first leg, x^(u-a) from the K_1 inverses, and F_v/F_j from the second leg. Terms exceeding the second nilpotent head vanish. This gives (1) with no asymptotic step.

In the ordering by a on the relative side and u on the physical side, C is upper block triangular. Its diagonal blocks are F_a times the identity. They are units, so C is invertible. The kernel depends on x, but not on y.

Let D_a and D_j be the relative diagonal clocks Q^a and Q^j. Let U,V be the physical diagonal clocks Q^u and Q^v. Equation (1) immediately gives

CDaDj=UVC. C D_aD_j=UV C. \tag{2}

2. Right lowering and its exact clock equation

Right multiplication R_f in the coefficient algebra is

Rf(faej)={fa+1ej−1mod⁡N,a<N−1,0,a=N−1. R_f(f^a e_j)= \begin{cases}f^{a+1}e_{j-1\bmod N},&a<N-1,\\0,&a=N-1. \end{cases} \tag{3}

This is different from physical total lowering, which is LEFT multiplication in the fused module. Define the transported operators

A=CDaC−1,J=CDjC−1,fR=CRfC−1. A=C D_a C^{-1},\qquad J=C D_j C^{-1},\qquad f_R=C R_f C^{-1}.

Then the following are exact finite matrix identities:

x(1−U−1A)fR=(1−QA)(1−J),AJ=UV. \boxed{\quad x(1-U^{-1}A)f_R=(1-QA)(1-J),\qquad AJ=UV. \quad} \tag{4}

They are not assertions merely about interior matrix entries. Before conjugating, the first identity reads

xCRf−xQU−1CRfDa=C(1−QDa)(1−Dj). x C R_f-xQ U^{-1}C R_fD_a=C(1-QD_a)(1-D_j). \tag{5}

For j>0 and a<N-1, use (1) at consecutive relative labels:

Cu,v;a+1,j−1Cu,v;a,j=x−1(1−Qa+1)(1−Qj)1−Qa+1−u. \frac{C_{u,v;a+1,j-1}}{C_{u,v;a,j}} =x^{-1}\frac{(1-Q^{a+1})(1-Q^j)}{1-Q^{a+1-u}}. \tag{6}

The polynomial equation obtained by clearing the denominator in (6) is valid even when that denominator is zero. At the new support u=a+1, the left coefficient vanishes and the old kernel entry is zero. At any other excluded row both sides vanish.

For j=0, R_f sends the second label to N-1. Its new kernel support is ONLY u=a+1,v=N-1. The left coefficient 1-Q^(a+1-u) is then zero, and the right side is zero because 1-Q^j=0. This is an actual wrapped endpoint, not a discarded row. For a=N-1 the left side is zero by (3), while the right side has factor 1-Q^N=0. These observations establish (5) on every matrix entry.

Finally R_f D_a=Q^(-1)D_a R_f, so conjugating (5) gives the ordered equation in (4). Also, exactly,

[A,J]=0,AfR=QfRA,JfR=Q−1fRJ. [A,J]=0,\qquad A f_R=Q f_R A,\qquad J f_R=Q^{-1}f_R J. \tag{7}

These follow directly from the relative basis, including its cyclic j endpoint, at the root Q^N=1.

3. The apparent denominator is nilpotent at the actual root

It would be incorrect to divide the first equation in (4) by 1-U inverse A as an actual finite operator. In fact

(1−U−1A)N=0. (1-U^{-1}A)^N=0. \tag{8}

Indeed C is upper block triangular with scalar diagonal blocks F_a. Conjugating D_a by C yields an upper block triangular A with diagonal blocks Q^u. Therefore U inverse A is upper block triangular with every diagonal block equal to one. Its difference from one is strictly upper block triangular, which proves (8).

Thus (4) is an exact polynomial transport identity through this endpoint, not a rational inverse formula for f_R. It retains a singular clock divisor that a prospective period transform must handle. No scalar normalization removes (8).

4. A useful prospective principal relation, with its scope

The actual cap uses the Euler argument x f in the RIGHT coefficient algebra. On a commutative principal-symbol chart associated with (4), that argument would satisfy

z(1−A/U)=(1−A)(1−J),AJ=UV. z(1-A/U)=(1-A)(1-J),\qquad AJ=UV. \tag{9}

Where 1-A/U is invertible this can be written

z=(1−A)(1−J)1−A/U. z=\frac{(1-A)(1-J)}{1-A/U}. \tag{10}

Equations (9)-(10) specify the algebraic symbol relation forced by the exact kernel. They do not prove that a particular semiclassical symbol chart exists for the full selected word, that it is symplectic, that z equals a geometric Fourier momentum, or that its divisor is traversed by the original source class. In particular (10) is NOT the finite root matrix equation, because of (8).

This still makes a concrete step beyond the former unspecified right-Euler/physical-clock relation: the actual finite kernel and all its endpoint terms are now explicit. A geometric comparison must use this relation and the original cap, rather than silently replacing the cap argument by another momentum.

5. Truncated generic-Q kernel exposes a top-degree defect

For a Q not satisfying Q^N=1, retain the SAME finite formula (1), provided F_1,…,F_(N-1) are units, and the SAME nilpotent shift (3). These are well-defined matrices, without claiming they form the actual generic-q word module. Let Pi_top project to a=N-1. The exact finite-kernel identity is now

xCRf−xQU−1CRfDa=C(1−QDa)(1−Dj)−(1−QN)C(1−Dj)Πtop. \begin{aligned} x C R_f-xQ U^{-1}C R_fD_a &=C(1-QD_a)(1-D_j)\\ &\quad -(1-Q^N)C(1-D_j)\Pi_{\mathrm{top}}. \tag{11} \end{aligned}

The same entry proof applies away from a=N-1, including j=0. At a=N-1 the left side vanishes by truncation. The correction in (11) subtracts exactly the nonzero right side there.

Consequently the root identity has a visible normal endpoint term before root specialization. Dividing (11) by 1-Q^N and discarding its last term would change the kernel calculation. This is only a statement about this explicit finite kernel; it does not provide a moving-q extension of actual braid intertwining, fusion of divided powers, or the original whole q-normal source. Those further comparisons remain necessary.

6. Exact checks

The audit and saved output pass at N=2,…,9 with unequal x=3,y=7. At each actual root they compare (1) to the literal coproduct fusion matrix and check (2), (4), (7), (8). Eight additional generic-Q kernel cases check the full nonzero endpoint correction (11). The all-level entry proofs above establish the theorem; these exact checks verify its conventions and endpoints.
