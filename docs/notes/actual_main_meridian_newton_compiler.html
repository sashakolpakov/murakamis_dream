Computing oriented tangles as the main meridian varies

1 November 2026.

A two-strand tangle is a piece of a knot diagram inside a box, with four ordered boundary points. Its quantum operator can be encoded by a polynomial in the two-strand Casimir. Turning or joining boxes then becomes a finite calculation on polynomial coefficients. The main meridian parameter is the eigenvalue assigned to a small loop around the knot itself; it is distinct from any auxiliary crossing-circle color.

There are two distinct operations in a main-meridian deformation: changing the polynomial coordinates of a coupon, and changing the multiplication and turn tensors of the physical source. A bound for the first operation does not by itself control the second: products that vanish at completeness can acquire nonzero coefficients. This note proves the polynomial basis bound, constructs an exact formal compiler for a fixed oriented Conway tree, and retains the actual primitive closing trace. The separate quarter-turn comparison now computes all six physical turn types in fixed polynomial coordinates and bounds them relative to the complete turn. The closing normalization still supplies only one factor HNH_N; it does not force the remaining regular contraction to tend to one.

6. What support and normalization do not prove

For opposite colors, the fixed abstract multiplication retains the complete Newton support. For equal colors, a formerly forbidden product need not vanish. One exact way to see the change is to keep the complete basis as a vector-space basis and write Ps=P0+Δs,Δs=YN+Y−N−2=2cos⁡(2πs)−2. P_s=P_0+\Delta_s,\qquad \Delta_s=Y^N+Y^{-N}-2=2\cos(2\pi s)-2. If i+j≥Ni+j\ge N, then the complete-basis product has a polynomial factorization ei0ej0=P0hije_i^0e_j^0=P_0h_{ij}, with deg⁡hij≤N−2\deg h_{ij}\le N-2. Therefore in the moving algebra ei0ej0≡−Δshij(mod⁡Ps). e_i^0e_j^0\equiv-\Delta_s h_{ij}\pmod{P_s}. \tag{25} For fixed microscopic ss, Δs\Delta_s is of order one, not of order N−1N^{-1}. A polynomially controlled basis change does not suppress this new term. The exact turn comparison retains the distinction between equal-color and opposite-color objects; its fixed polynomial coordinates do not identify their multiplication algebras. The additional products in (25) therefore remain in the physical calculation.

At first order there is a useful distinction: Δ0′=0\Delta'_0=0, so the multiplication of fixed degree-<N<N polynomials has zero first derivative at completeness. The moving Newton coefficients can nevertheless have nonzero first derivatives. The multiplication note computes the exact connection derivative and shows that these first derivatives are precisely its basis-change contribution; they do not by themselves give a coordinate-independent first-order obstruction.

Finally write the even reduced state as FN(M)=fN(Y)F_N(M)=f_N(Y), with Y=M2Y=M^2. The canonical ordinary-color subtraction gives the exact decomposition FN(ez/(2N))KN=HN(z)ρN(z)+πN(z),πN(z)=PNord(ez/N)KN. \frac{F_N(e^{z/(2N)})}{K_N} =H_N(z)\rho_N(z)+\pi_N(z),\qquad \pi_N(z)=\frac{P_N^{\mathrm{ord}}(e^{z/N})}{K_N}. \tag{26} Thus the coefficient contraction of (23) is precisely AK,N=ρN+πNHN. For knots with positive complete exponential rate, πN\pi_N is exponentially small on compact sets, but its quotient by HNH_N still has the actual finite poles. After subtracting it, a bounded signed contraction gives normality of ρN\rho_N and subsequential limits g(z)=H(z)a(z),H(z)=2sinh⁡(z/2)/z,a(0)=1. g(z)=H(z)a(z),\qquad H(z)=2\sinh(z/2)/z,\qquad a(0)=1. They are not new actual knot sources; they show why the weaker data cannot imply the missing estimate or identification.

The result is an exact whole-tangle compiler with a polynomially controlled Newton basis deformation and the retained boundary factor. Its application to separated bare product junctions now gives an exact supremum-volume limit for the auxiliary-colored source on M=qsM=q^s, |s|≤R<1|s|\le R<1, at both parities. A bare junction has no distinguished original crossing-circle operator; the condition is that no two such junctions are adjacent. The comparison includes the channels opened in (25) after summing them inside the actual junction. The geometric constant is the volume with the auxiliary circles unfilled, and the lower witness is the complete value. Adjacent bare junctions, the moving filled-knot profile, and identification of its normalized regular limit still need further estimates. Polynomial spectral stability of an arbitrary turn does not itself preserve a localized jet profile.
