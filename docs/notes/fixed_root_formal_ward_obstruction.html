Fixed-root Ward data do not admit a literal scalar formalization

Research note, 27 October 2026. This isolates an obstruction to one proposed shortcut from the exact all-matrices Hopf-Ward diagram to the geometric Gaussian period. It also specifies what a homological perturbation argument would have to supply. The obstruction applies to a fixed-root, composition-preserving formalization. It does not rule out a linear source-compatible comparison in a larger all-level category, and does not invalidate the partially summed support theorem.

1. The fixed-N action cannot become a nontrivial near-identity flow

Fix N>=2, a characteristic-zero cyclotomic coefficient field, and the actual matrices. Since

K^N=x^N I,

their conjugation action satisfies

(ad_K)^N=I.                                  (1.1)

The same relation holds on every tensor Hopf-adjoint matrix space.

Finite-order lemma. Let E be a separated, complete, H-torsion-free module in characteristic zero, with N invertible. If a continuous endomorphism U satisfies

U=I+O(H),       U^N=I,

then U=I.

Proof: if U=I+H^s D mod H^(s+1), expansion of the fixed finite power gives U^N=I+N H^s D mod H^(s+1), hence D=0. Induction and separatedness give the result. Equivalently the H-adic logarithm exists and N log U=0.

Consequently a comparison preserving (1.1) cannot send the actual fixed-N adjoint action to a regular operator with principal form

U=I+H A(h_sl/2)+O(H^2)

and a nonzero conjugation field on a framing coefficient algebra. The same applies to the two cut-stabilizer fields obtained from actual Hopf-adjoint combinations in the finite Ward calculation.

If an equivariant linear map has only an invariant image, this conclusion applies only on that image: U acts as identity there. An invariant rank-one scalar quotient is therefore not excluded. The obstruction concerns simultaneously retaining the full fixed-N action and realizing its nontrivial classical framing transport on that image.

2. All matrices cannot map multiplicatively into scalar symbols

Let A_H be an H-adically separated, H-torsion-free complete algebra whose special fibre is an integral domain. Then A_H is a domain. Indeed two nonzero elements have finite H-valuations, and the product of their first nonzero symbols cannot vanish. The same is true after inverting H.

There is no unital algebra map

End(k^N) -> A_H[1/H],       N>1.              (2.1)

The matrix algebra is simple, so a unital map is injective, whereas its nontrivial orthogonal idempotents cannot inject into a domain. Thus the exact all-matrices finite diagram cannot be mapped multiplicatively to a scalar deformation quantization with a reduced integral geometric special fibre.

Even on a smaller subalgebra, the literal nilpotent relation gives a concrete symbol obstruction. The actual raising matrix obeys Ecal^N=0. Any composition-preserving scalar principal-symbol map therefore sends it to e with e^N=0, so e=0 on the reduced geometric chart. This is incompatible with the nonzero interior moment-map symbol used for the geometric conjugation fields.

Matrix-valued formal targets evade the domain statement: they can retain matrix idempotents and nilpotents. They do not thereby evade the finite-order lemma if the adjoint action is required to become near identity on the image. Linear maps of current complexes need not preserve products and are not ruled out by (2.1).

3. Why this is a fixed-root obstruction, not a contradiction to bulk support

For fixed N, there is no scalar base change sending the primitive root Q_N to exp(H) in a characteristic-zero formal field while retaining its defining equation: it would require

exp(NH)=1,

which is false as a formal power series for nonzero fixed N. Equivalently the formal neighbourhood of Q=1 in Q^N=1 is the single reduced point H=0.

The geometric scaling instead has N growing and positions of order 1/H, with A=Q^a and Q^N=1 retained through an independent Frobenius parameter before specialization. It is an all-level unfolding, not the formal completion of one finite matrix algebra. This is exactly why the proper-ratio construction in the partial support theorem is legitimate: it first proves a rational contiguity identity in independent variables and only then specializes its central parameter. It does not map every fixed-N matrix and every finite relation into the scalar formal coefficient algebra.

The source itself makes the distinction visible. In the finite color algebra C_N=k[A^+/-1]/(A^N-1), canonical representatives obey

A f_tilde-(Af)_tilde=f_(N-1)(A^N-1).

The cut of (Af)_tilde contains f_(N-1), even though ordinary multiplication by A followed by the zero residue does not. This degree-near-N carry is part of the exact source. A bounded-degree scalar formal coefficient expansion cannot silently replace it. The relative source construction retains it explicitly and is linear rather than multiplicative.

4. What homological perturbation would prove, if its hypotheses were supplied

Here is a precise sufficient formal statement. Work in a common H-complete source-unfolded category, with all endpoint and carry components included. Suppose a complex (C,d0) has a contraction onto a one-degree geometric line G, with maps p,i,k satisfying

p i=I, d0 k+k d0=I-i p, k^2=k i=p k=0.

The line may carry its retained exponential regulator factor; scalar coefficients and formal units are taken after that factor is fixed. Suppose d=d0+Delta is a differential, and k Delta and Delta k raise the H filtration by at least one. Define

i_D=(I+k Delta)^(-1)i,
p_D=p(I+Delta k)^(-1),
k_D=k(I+Delta k)^(-1),
d_G=p Delta(I+k Delta)^(-1)i.

All inverse operators here are the convergent geometric series in the stipulated filtration, and p_D=p+O(H). These signs use d0 k+k d0=I-i p. The identities follow by substituting i_D=i-k Delta i_D and d0 Delta+Delta d0+Delta^2=0; for example they give d i_D=i_D d_G. The corresponding identities for p_D,k_D follow by the same multiplication, yielding the perturbed contraction. Since G is concentrated in one degree, d_G=0.

If the original marked closed source c is a d-cocycle and

p(c mod H)!=0,

then p_D c is nonzero: its first coefficient is the displayed nonzero projection. A compatible diagram of actual partial traces and Ward complexes would carry this class to a scalar Ward line. If its leading scalar map is nonzero, the perturbed scalar map is a unit and the class survives. The exact finite cut homotopy Phi-p=b delta would then identify its prescribed cut with that scalar projection. This would be a formal source-survival theorem; it would still assert no convergence or selected rootwise asymptotic.

The compatibility requirement is substantial. Given chain maps in a diagram, perturbation transports them to p_D' f i_D, but their nonzero leading action must be checked. Chain-map identities and normalization of a different class do not force it.

5. Which hypotheses are already available

The current notes supply the following separate exact inputs:

  • A nonzero transverse geometric Gaussian contraction with two actual framing coordinates left external, including exact contiguity.
  • A literal finite relative source complex and the original psi,phi,e,D, with all canonical carries retained.
  • An exact finite Hopf-adjoint partial-trace chain map for every matrix, and a finite cut Ward contraction onto k I.
  • The uniform nonzero classical normal density in the marked cut determinant line.

They do not yet supply a common source-unfolded complex C, its unperturbed source-compatible contraction, the perturbation Delta realizing the actual Ward currents after transverse elimination, or the nonzero leading scalar map on the geometric class. In particular the auxiliary relative coordinate of the finite source has not been identified with the internal Gaussian framing mode.

The finite Ward homotopy cannot merely be inserted into the formal geometric series and declared small. Its coefficients contain

1/(k_a-k_b)=1/[x(exp(-Ha)-exp(-Hb))]
           =1/[x H(b-a)]+O(1)

for fixed a!=b, and the cumulative F inverse likewise contains 1/(1-exp(H(r+1))). Thus an O(H) correction need not make k Delta topologically nilpotent. A suitable rescaled classical complex may repair this, but its source and endpoint compatibility must be constructed. Polynomial bounds in N do not establish the required H-adic smallness.

There is therefore no justified HPL shortcut from the current separate inputs to scalar source survival. A literal multiplicative fixed-root-to-scalar formalization is impossible by Sections 1–2. A linear all-level comparison, retaining the Frobenius and source normal directions and explicitly transporting the currents, remains possible and is the appropriate next algebraic object. The finite all-matrices chain map alone does not construct it.
