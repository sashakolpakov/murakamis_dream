Genuine braid transport on the retained source charts

Research note, 26 October 2026. This constructs a groupoid of the existing source presentations with actual moving semicyclic centers, tree changes and source-root charts. All braid and mixed comparison identities retain the basic carry lattice, actual source sublattice, dual source and selected cut. The meridian appears in the normalized center map as U=M^(2N), with its raw K^N root still retained.

This is a construction of operator transport on the prescribed state. It does not identify strand braids with tetrahedron vertex permutations or prove the required global triangulation comparison.

1. Fixed face adapters are not repaired just by root charges

The oriented-source note already disproved the bare S4 tensor prescription with its actual face metrics and corrected cyclic maps. There is a stronger finite diagnostic for one proposed repair.

In the shared standard frames replace the local kernel by every root translate L0(uQa,vQb), a,b modulo N, retaining its actual relative-input clock/shift. Compare all root choices for the reference order (0,1,2,3) with all choices for (1,2,0,3), using those same actual face adapters.

At N=3, p=397, the two projective orbits are disjoint: all 9*9=81 pairs have nonzero proportionality minors. The complete reduction certificate records the q-root, flags, difference roots, index pairs, coefficient values and all 81 nonzero minors. Every required denominator is a unit in this construction.

This refutes a rational identity for this particular repair in characteristic zero. All odd-level integer-charge choices are included projectively: their roots give these N^2 translates, while their full charged prefactors are nonzero scalars. Such prefactors cannot repair nonproportionality. They are not deleted from an actual period by this diagnostic.

The same diagnostic at N=2 and 5 also fails. Its variants removing the relative mark or using raw cyclic rotation likewise fail. The even-level test concerns the existing fusion expression, not a new even charged definition.

This does not disprove the published decorated matrix symmetries, whose full adapter prescription has not been identified with this one. It shows that naming the charged kernel while retaining these face maps is insufficient. No geometric state is substituted for the selected actual cut.

2. Moving centers and the correct total Borel coordinate

Keep the genuine semicyclic modules V(x,phi) and their highest-weight-normalized crossings from the semicyclic braid proof. Use the same color x=-M/q on b strands and put

k=x^N=(-1)^(N-1)M^N, k^2=U=M^(2N),
phi_j=epsilon a_j, j=0,...,b-1.

The raw checked positive center map is

(a_j,a_(j+1)) -> (k a_(j+1),
                     k a_j+(1-k^2)a_(j+1)),      (2.1)

with the previously proved inverse for the negative crossing. The uncheck wrap correction phi_2/k and the original twist are retained.

For this variable-center construction choose endpoint factors

c_j=x^(-j), S_j v_n=c_j^n v_n,
X_j=x K_j^(-1), Y_j=c_j F_j.                    (2.2)

This choice is made for a specific reason. The normalized Borel total is exactly the actual iterated coproduct:

sum_j X_0...X_(j-1)Y_j
             =sum_j K_0^(-1)...K_(j-1)^(-1)F_j
             =Delta^(b-1)F.                    (2.3)

After the basis change S_j the normalized Y has standard shift arrows and central wrap epsilon mu_j, with

mu_j=k^(-j)a_j.

These inverse endpoint factors serve a different coordinate choice from the positive powers used to make the earlier common fixed line have equal wraps. Both choices must be written explicitly; they are not interchangeable unmarked normalizations.

Equation (2.1) now becomes

L_-(U)=[[0,U],[1,1-U]],
L_+(U)=[[1-U^(-1),1],[U^(-1),0]],              (2.4)

on (mu_j,mu_(j+1)). Their sum is preserved. Define normal flags

delta_0=0, delta_(j+1)=delta_j-mu_j.             (2.5)

Then delta_b, the total central parameter, is preserved by every crossing. Require distinct delta_i along the finite path in use. This excludes finitely many linear divisors; it does not require one finite Zariski chart to work for every possible braid word simultaneously.

At U=1 the two classical matrices (2.4) are ordinary swaps. The quantum pure-braid action is still nontrivial, as the actual cup/cap identity in the companion meridian note proves. The raw even-level sign in k has not been erased by (2.4).

3. All tree and source charts carry the same genuine arrows

Use the full frames of the source flag carry extension,

Ical_(T,a,s)=J_epsilon M_(T,a)(s).

At a root branch over source infinity write M=t^(-w)Mbar, keeping the source line t^w and its dual. Let B_i(a;epsilon) be the genuine crossing with the centers updated by (2.1), and include the endpoint changes (2.2). It maps both the original leaf lattice and the common carry lattice to their target lattices. A wrap lowers the integer residue degree by N and has one factor epsilon; its inverse has the same property.

For an arrow from (T,a,s) to (T’,a’,s’), set

Bcal_i=M_(T',a')(s')^(-1)
       J_epsilon^(-1) B_i(a;epsilon) J_epsilon
                M_(T,a)(s).                    (3.1)

This is an integral isomorphism of the retained sources. At infinity its coordinate matrix on the regular frames has the explicit source-line factor t^(w’-w). The dual uses the inverse transpose and the reciprocal line.

For zero braid length (3.1) is exactly the strict tree/source transition. For a sequence of crossings and chart changes, all intermediate M matrices cancel. Therefore the direct genuine braid identity implies the same identity through any intermediate trees and source charts, with the actual intermediate centers. Positive/negative inverse paths likewise close exactly. Source-line orders telescope.

This proves the mixed transport identities for source presentations, rather than assuming a new cyclic braided category from the Borel modules alone.

There is an additional actual module statement. The direct coproduct intertwining in the holonomy construction and the correct wrap imply that (3.1) intertwines the same total F and K on both sides, by (2.3)–(2.4). At generic epsilon the total normalized Borel algebra is a full N-by-N matrix algebra. Its commutant forces

Bcal_i=I_total tensor b_i,                      (3.2)

with the entire relative operator b_i and its scalar fixed. The identity extends to the source fiber by integral specialization. It is not merely a trace comparison.

For the actual sublattice, substitution in Ical^(-1)L_leaf gives

Bcal_i B_(T,a,s)=B_(T',a',s').

The inverse transpose preserves the full dual, so the perfect linking pairing and the lost normal directions are transported as well. No ordinary specialization image replaces them.

4. The actual cut and its nontrivial braid holonomy remain

Closing all but the marked strand with the original pivot still gives F_N(M)I. The residue-degree argument in the carry theorem rules out every wrapped closed path. Under (3.1), the full cut insertion is transported with the operators. Its source and dual line factors cancel exactly; the original ADO dimension is retained at the boundary.

On a center fixed point a braid gives a genuine loop in this source groupoid. Its operator is not generally the identity. The explicit identity

e_0(B_N(1)^2-I)=(-1)^(N+1)(N-1)(q-q^(-1))Pi_N

in the actual meridian source is nonzero. It has determinant-one unipotent local monodromy, fixes the cup, and is detected by the actual pivotal closing functional. Scalar source lines and reduced central data do not contain that information.

This is why ordering paths cannot simply be forgotten in a comparison using the actual strand braidings. It is not a proof that all tetrahedron permutations are strand braids, or that a differently decorated tetrahedron cannot have its own S4 symmetry.

5. Exact checks, coverage and the remaining global task

The groupoid audit checks ten variable-center cases, N=2,3,4,5,6 at generic and Kashaev colors. The two three-letter braid paths use different intermediate trees and source-root branches. Their full matrices agree and both retain total line order one. Inverse paths close; every arrow preserves the actual specialization image and factors as (3.2). Thirty original whole cut operators agree in the two source charts. The Tor dimensions are retained.

The companion spectral/meridian checks in the same audit test the actual meridian source and nontrivial full twist. The separate charge audit provides the fixed-adapter counterexample in Section 1.

These are the existing cyclic recouplings and holonomy crossings covered by Kashaev’s cyclic 6j framework and Kashaev–Reshetikhin’s root holonomy framework. Only their abstracts are used for coverage; all operator, lattice and source identities here follow from the direct calculations already specified. No new knot invariant or published global comparison theorem is assumed.

The subsequent intrinsic cut theorem assembles the two-copy meridian maps through a generic even-strand braid using centralizer transitions. It proves the full selected cut is intrinsic under fusion changes and every module comparison, with the dimension and phase retained. A perfect complete two-factor residue pairing also fixes its integral Casimir order and dual. The complete geometric triangulation presentation, its geometric quantum-A component and selected regulator norm remain unproved. The E^N=0 center family here is still the reducible-character locus; it is not substituted for the complete irreducible hyperbolic character.
