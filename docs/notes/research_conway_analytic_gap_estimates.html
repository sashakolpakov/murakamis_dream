Analytic gap estimates for Conway tangle sources

23 November 2026. Research proof; frozen papers and earlier notes remain unchanged.

The two scalar comparisons used in the Conway source bound admit analytic proofs with their original margins: 1/1001/100 for each two-leaf alias comparison and 1/25001/2500 for a proper single attachment. No finite comparison table is a proof input here. In the attachment argument we use its actual proper parameter region. We do not assert the stronger auxiliary comparison on the entire artificial slope square. The result replaces the finite comparison checks needed by those two lemmas. It supplies no new filled-source lower bound or volume identity.
