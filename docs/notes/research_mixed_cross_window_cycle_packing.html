Mixed whole-CROSS and regular-window phase losses

31 October 2026. Internally reviewed research theorem and conditional source-rate criterion. Root and a separate reader completed full analytical reviews. This combines the original whole-CROSS cofactor angular bound with regular moving windows on the same face torus. The majorants below are exact finite source quantities. No comparison of their product with the complete volume is assumed.

1. The whole-CROSS polynomial, with both parities retained

Put d=N−1d=N-1, θ=π/N\theta=\pi/N, u=−4sin⁡2(πδ)u=-4\sin^2(\pi\delta), and ar=4cos⁡2((r+12)θ),sr=2sin⁡(rθ),A=x+y,P=xy. a_r=4\cos^2((r+\tfrac12)\theta),\qquad s_r=2\sin(r\theta),\qquad A=x+y,\quad P=xy. A zero variable stays exactly zero. In particular this includes protected middle and protected outer patterns allowed by the original marked source. It excludes two zero outer faces.

2. A finite majorant and the whole-port angular loss

The cofactor interpolation theorem gives, coefficient by coefficient, |Rj(u)|coef≤Clog⁡(N+1)Rj(0),−4≤u≤0,0≤j≤⌊d/2⌋. |R_j(u)|_{\mathrm{coef}} \le C\log(N+1)R_j(0),\qquad -4\le u\le0, \quad 0\le j\le\lfloor d/2\rfloor. \tag{6} Together with the exact central comparisons in that theorem, it implies |PN×(Leiϑ;u)|≤MN×(L):=C0log⁡2(N+1)BN(L) |P_N^\times(L e^{i\vartheta};u)| \le M_N^\times(L):=C_0\log^2(N+1)B_N(L) \tag{7} for every face phase and every real δ\delta. The constant can be chosen uniformly over the fixed radius compact and all sufficiently large levels of both parities. Protected zero variables need no assigned phase.

For the supported incidences define the distance to common phase by d×(ϑ)=minφ∈𝕋maxf∈supp⁡(x,b,c,y)|ϑf−φ|𝕋. d_\times(\vartheta) =\min_{\varphi\in\mathbb T} \max_{f\in\operatorname{supp}(x,b,c,y)} |\vartheta_f-\varphi|_{\mathbb T}. \tag{10} Incidences are retained separately if two arguments happen to be the same original face. To prove this, choose any positive outer incidence pp, which exists because A>0A>0. Taking φ=ϑp\varphi=\vartheta_p in (10) shows that some supported incidence has circular distance at least η\eta from pp. If it is bb, the supported phases of (x,b,y)(x,b,y) are separated; if it is cc, those of (x,c,y)(x,c,y) are separated. If it is the other outer incidence, both sides are separated. The selected side therefore has at least two positive entries. Thus a whole CROSS port has alignment label zero at every supported incidence. This is a whole-port conclusion from the exact central factorization, not an assumption that the two cofactor phases can be chosen independently.

3. Endpoint normalization and its rate

Let Q(x,b,y)Q(x,b,y) denote the original absolute complete triangle potential at δ=0\delta=0, with homogeneity degree 1/21/2. Then, on the fixed radius compact and fixed zero pattern, log⁡BN(L)=N{Q(x,b,y)+Q(x,c,y)}+O(log⁡N). \log B_N(L)=N\{Q(x,b,y)+Q(x,c,y)\}+O(\log N). \tag{13} In particular the exact majorant in (7) has a polynomial upper bound MN×(L)≤CNKexp⁡[N{Q(x,b,y)+Q(x,c,y)}]. M_N^\times(L)\le C N^K \exp\!\left[N\{Q(x,b,y)+Q(x,c,y)\}\right]. \tag{14} This polynomial normalization is not needed for (11), but identifies its source-rate interface without an unspecified exponential scalar.

For completeness, (13) follows from the existing endpoint cofactor packets with a quantitative product estimate. For b>0b>0, including exactly one zero outer weight, the interior-start packet has its starting saddle in a fixed compact subset of (0,1/2)(0,1/2). The finite prefix diagonal is bounded away from zero before that saddle, and the positive tail root is bounded away from zero from there to the terminal row. Their logarithms have uniform bounded derivatives on these intervals. The finite-row shift is bounded, and the finite tail root differs from its limiting root by O(N−1)O(N^{-1}). Elementary Euler–Maclaurin estimates therefore give NϕN(tN)=NQ(x,b,y)+O(1)N\phi_N(t_N)=NQ(x,b,y)+O(1). The packet amplitude is a bounded unit times N\sqrt N; hence log⁡RJ−k(b;0)=NQ(x,b,y)+O(log⁡N)\log R_{J-k}(b;0)=NQ(x,b,y)+O(\log N), for fixed kk.

For b=0b=0 and xy>0xy>0, the protected-middle packet is a bounded nonzero amplitude times ∏j=1nsjλj,+\prod_{j=1}^n s_j\lambda_{j,+}. The positive λj,+\lambda_{j,+} is uniformly bounded away from zero even at the lower endpoint and differs from a smooth limiting root by O(N−1)O(N^{-1}). Its logarithmic sum has bounded Riemann error. The remaining sine product has only the endpoint logarithm: comparison with log⁡t\log t gives Riemann error O(log⁡N)O(\log N). The absolute marked-action identity identifies the resulting integral with the same Q(0;x,y)Q(0;x,y). 4. Mixed cycles on the one original face torus

Take a fixed original source and one radius Lf>0L_f>0 per ordinary face. Protected face variables are specialized to zero before coefficient extraction. Select a set JJ of ports consisting of either:

  • whole CROSS coupons satisfying the hypotheses above, with majorant ℰe,N=Me,N×\mathcal E_{e,N}=M_{e,N}^\times and zero incidence labels;
  • literal regular moving windows satisfying the four-positive theorem or the one-protected-outer theorem, with ℰe,N=Ee,N\mathcal E_{e,N}=E_{e,N} and their actual endpoint-sign labels.
  • fixed-start b=0b=0 or reflected fixed-stop c=0c=0 windows satisfying the protected-middle theorem, again with their literal positive envelopes and only their three supported incidences. Their hypotheses require both outer weights and the other middle weight positive.

Each selected port now has the two bounds |Pe,N|≤ℰe,N,|Pe,N|≤Cee−ceNℰe,Nif de≥η, |P_{e,N}|\le\mathcal E_{e,N},\qquad |P_{e,N}|\le C_e e^{-c_eN}\mathcal E_{e,N} \quad\text{if }d_e\ge\eta, \tag{16} where ded_e is distance to its supported alignment. For CROSS this is (10); for a window subtract its prescribed 0/π0/\pi incidence labels before minimizing over a common phase. All constants are obtained at the same fixed threshold η\eta.

Use the original supported incidence multigraph, retaining repeated incidences. For a nonempty finite collection 𝒞\mathcal C of odd-label cycles, put η=π/(4rmax)\eta=\pi/(4r_{\max}) and let κ𝒞=max⁡{∑C∈𝒞λC:λC≥0,∑C∋eλC≤cefor every e∈J}. \kappa_{\mathcal C}= \max\left\{\sum_{C\in\mathcal C}\lambda_C: \lambda_C\ge0,\quad \sum_{C\ni e}\lambda_C\le c_e\ \text{for every }e\in J\right\}. \tag{17} The fractional cycle-packing proof uses only (16), so applies unchanged. CROSS vertices can carry paths between moving-window vertices, including across a protected middle whose zero incidence is absent. A cycle containing only CROSS vertices has even label and supplies no such obstruction.

At unselected ports let Me,NM_{e,N} be any certified phase-uniform majorants for their actual polynomials. For the unchanged original target exponents tf(N)t_f(N), Cauchy extraction on the one shared torus gives |[∏fzftf(N)]∏ePe,N(z)|≤Ce−κ𝒞N∏e∈Jℰe,N(L)∏e∉JMe,N(L)∏fLftf(N). \boxed{\displaystyle \left|\left[\prod_f z_f^{t_f(N)}\right] \prod_e P_{e,N}(z)\right| \le C e^{-\kappa_{\mathcal C}N} \frac{\prod_{e\in J}\mathcal E_{e,N}(L) \prod_{e\notin J}M_{e,N}(L)} {\prod_f L_f^{t_f(N)}}.} \tag{18} Raw numerator signs, any external source character and all additional source prefactors retain their actual moduli. There is no new power of NN, a changed face target, or an independent radius per incidence in (18).

5. Scope of the rate criterion

The whole-CROSS contribution to the logarithmic majorant in (18) is exactly Q(x,b,y)+Q(x,c,y)Q(x,b,y)+Q(x,c,y) by (13). The moving-window contribution is its own original positive envelope. They are not identified with each other. If the normalized logarithmic limsup of the entire positive quotient in (18) is BLB_L, its actual shared coefficient has upper rate BL−κ𝒞B_L-\kappa_{\mathcal C}. Thus BL−κ𝒞<ΦD, B_L-\kappa_{\mathcal C}<\Phi_D, \tag{19} is a conditional strict source-rate certificate whenever the original complete rate ΦD\Phi_D is known.

No uniform margin in (19), no exhaustive window cover, and no universal bound by ΦD\Phi_D have been proved here. A family of polynomially many pieces still needs the uniform finite-level errors and constants specified in the cycle-packing criterion. Compatible phase systems, uncovered irregular windows, and the remaining source sectors require their own estimates. The statement here is for real spectral variables; a complex-strip version must also budget the unconditional Bernstein growth of all noncontracting factors.

The finite central identities and cofactor angular theorem used above have their own original-source audits. This note composes their proved estimates analytically; it makes no claim to a new physical-matrix or asymptotic numerical audit.
