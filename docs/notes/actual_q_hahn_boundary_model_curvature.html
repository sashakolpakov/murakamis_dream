Finite local curvature of the original boundary models

30 October 2026. The actual 220/222 determinants and the actual 202 positive polynomial have logarithmic curvature bounded above and below by constant multiples of N, uniformly through the fold. The other retained factors have nonlinear derivatives of size O(s+1), where s is the channel deficit. These are finite local source statements. No graph-rank, stationary-point, Gaussian reduction or original-period assertion is made.

1. Actual clocks and the finite core factors

Put N>=3, d=N-1, theta=pi/N, q=exp(i theta), Q=q^2. Keep an ACTUAL integer channel a with

a0 N<=a<=floor(d/2),  a0>0,
m=d-a, s=d-2a.

All estimates are for sufficiently large N, including either maximal folded parity. Coefficients below range in fixed positive compact sets. Use the exact physical half-index clocks

U=q^-1 X, V=q^-1 Y, QUV=XY.                       (1)

The half-index is not dropped, and a is not replaced by a limiting continuous color. The actual source determinant is

H_a^1(U,V)=V^a K_a det[I-(qU/2)H_--(q^-1/(2V))H_+],
K_a=[2a choose a]_Q.                              (2)

The a-by-a Hermitian positive matrices H_-,H_+ have diagonal

d_n=1/[cos(n theta)cos((n+1)theta)], 0<=n<a,

and upper adjacent entries

h_n^+/-=sqrt(b_n)[sin(n theta)+/-i cos(n theta)],
b_n=sin^2(n theta)/[cos^2(n theta)
                  sin((2n-1)theta)sin((2n+1)theta)], 1<=n<a.

We take H_+ to have the plus upper entry and H_- the minus entry, as in the source determinant. These are the original finite coefficients, including the fold endpoint.

For negative raw clocks X,Y set

alpha=-X/2>0, beta=-1/(2Y)>0,
M=I+alpha H_-+beta H_+.

The finite nonlinear core factors of the six models are:

  • 220: det(I+alpha H_-), depending on the first raw log clock;
  • 022: det(I+beta H_-), after the literal reversed reciprocity, depending on the second raw log clock with opposite sign;
  • 222: det M, retaining BOTH actual finite clocks;
  • 202: P_a(w)=sum_(k=0)^a beta_(a,k)^2 w^k, w=QUV=XY>0, beta_(a,k)=S_a/(S_k S_(a-k)), S_j=product_(r=1)^j 2sin(r theta);
  • 200 and 002: no order-N nonlinear core is extracted here.

Every source scalar, affine phase, monomial and large-partner product remains in the finite models of the correlated-band theorem. The word “core” separates a derivative calculation, not a change to the source. Positivity below refers to real LOGARITHMIC RADII. A change of normalized magnetic head nu changes its clock by exp(2pi i nu), so pullback to those heads retains the factors 2pi i and the literal incidence signs. No positivity in real magnetic-angle directions is asserted.

2. An exact noncommutative determinant Hessian

Write Xmat=alpha H_-, Ymat=beta H_+, and

A=M^-1/2 Xmat M^-1/2, B=M^-1/2 Ymat M^-1/2.

For real u,v let D differentiate alpha->alpha exp(tu), beta->beta exp(tv). The finite matrix differentiation identity gives

D^2 log det M
  =Tr[u^2 A+v^2 B-(uA+vB)^2]
  =u^2 Tr(Xmat M^-2)+v^2 Tr(Ymat M^-2)
                     +(u-v)^2 Tr(AB).              (3)

Indeed A+B=I-M^-1. Substitute this in Tr(A-A^2) and Tr(B-B^2) and use cyclicity of trace. No commutativity of H_- and H_+ is assumed. Each term on the last line is nonnegative: for example Tr(AB)=Tr(A^1/2 B A^1/2)>=0. Since A,B are positive and A+B<I, the first line also gives

D^2 log det M<=a(u^2+v^2).                         (4)

For actual raw log-clock directions (u,v), equation (1) instead changes alpha by exp(tu) and beta by exp(-tv). Thus the last square in (3) is (u+v)^2, while the first two squares are unchanged. This sign is part of the original second-clock normalization.

The one-variable beta=0 specialization of (3) is

(alpha partial_alpha)^2 log det(I+alpha H_-)
           =Tr[alpha H_-(I+alpha H_-)^-2].         (5)

3. A finite bulk argument gives the lower bound through the fold

The actual Cholesky pivots are

p_(n-1)=sin(n theta)/[cos(n theta)sin((2n-1)theta)],
1<=n<=a.

Consequently, for h_n the upper entry of either H matrix,

z*Hz=sum_(n=1)^(a-1) p_(n-1)
         |z_(n-1)+(h_n/p_(n-1))z_n|^2
              +p_(a-1)|z_(a-1)|^2.               (6)

This is the original square decomposition; it follows by multiplying its factors and the displayed finite trigonometric pivot identity. Only ACTUAL rows appear. No virtual endpoint or singular extension beyond the maximal folded matrix is used.

Select every other adjacent square whose two indices lie in a fixed bulk interval, for example a0 N/4<=n<=a0 N/2. Their supports are disjoint, and their number is at least cN. The pivots, h_n, row norms and reciprocal row norms are bounded on this interval independently of N and a. If w_j are the normalized column vectors of those squares, they are orthonormal, and (6) gives

Xmat>=c P,  P=sum_j w_j w_j*, rank P>=cN.          (7)

On the support of each w_j all entries of M are uniformly bounded, since it is wholly inside that same bulk interval. Thus <w_j,Mw_j><=C. For the spectral probability measure of M at w_j, convexity of lambda->lambda^-2 gives

<w_j,M^-2 w_j>>=<w_j,Mw_j>^-2>=C^-2.

Taking the trace of (7) against the positive M^-2 proves

Tr(Xmat M^-2)>=cN.                                (8)

Apply the same argument separately to the squares of H_+ to prove Tr(Ymat M^-2)>=cN. Their selected projections need not coincide. The uniform lower and upper compact bounds on alpha,beta are used here. Together with (3),(4), this proves

cN(u^2+v^2)<=D^2 log det M<=CN(u^2+v^2).           (9)

The same proof with beta=0 and (5) proves cN<=log-radius curvature of the 220 core<=CN. Exact reciprocity gives the 022 statement with its recorded inverse clock. These estimates require a/N bounded below, but no upper gap from the fold. Large final entries do not affect the selected bounded bulk squares or their expectations.

4. Exact 202 curvature is a uniformly nondegenerate variance

For positive w define the probability weights

pi_k=beta_(a,k)^2 w^k/P_a(w), 0<=k<=a.

Direct differentiation, with all finite coefficients retained, gives

(w partial_w)^2 log P_a(w)=Var_pi(k).              (10)

Here is a uniform two-sided estimate without differentiating a limiting Laplace formula. The EXACT consecutive weight ratio is

R(k)=w[sin((a-k)theta)/sin((k+1)theta)]^2.

Extend its logarithm to real 0<=x<=a-1. It satisfies

(log R)'(x)
  =-2theta sin((a+1)theta)
      /[sin((a-x)theta)sin((x+1)theta)].           (11)

Since a0 N<=a<=floor(d/2), this is at most -c/N throughout its interval. For w in a fixed positive compact the crossing of R through one is a distance cN from both ends: for x<=cN the numerator sine is bounded below while the denominator is at most Cc, and the reverse holds for x>=a-cN. Choose c small using the two bounds on w. Thus any modal integer k_* lies in [cN,a-cN]. On a fixed smaller bulk neighborhood of k_*, (11) has absolute value at most C/N. The adjacent modal logarithmic ratios are consequently O(1/N).

Multiplying the exact ratios away from k_* now yields, for every k,

weight(k)/weight(k_*)<=C exp[-c(k-k_*)^2/N].       (12)

The bulk upper bound on the ratio drops also gives a fixed positive lower bound for that ratio whenever |k-k_*|<=2sqrt(N), for large N. It follows by summing (12) and those central terms that

c sqrt(N) weight(k_*)<=P_a(w)<=C sqrt(N) weight(k_*).

The upper Gaussian bound gives E[(k-k_*)^2]<=CN, hence Var(k)<=CN. Each of the two integer shells k_-2sqrt(N)<=k<=k_-sqrt(N) and k_+sqrt(N)<=k<=k_+2sqrt(N) has probability at least c>0. For independent samples K,K’, the identity Var(K)=E[(K-K’)^2]/2 therefore gives Var(K)>=cN. This proves

cN<=(w partial_w)^2 log P_a(w)<=CN.                (13)

In the actual two raw log-clock directions this is the quadratic form Var(k)(u+v)^2, because w=QUV=XY EXACTLY. The affine phase (-1)^a Q1 contributes no curvature.

5. Fixed holomorphic logarithmic derivatives are O(N)

The preceding finite bounds have uniform complex neighborhoods. For a positive reference M and complex z_1,z_2 near zero,

M(z)=I+exp(z_1)Xmat+exp(z_2)Ymat.

Conjugating its increment by M^-1/2 gives (exp(z_1)-1)A+(exp(z_2)-1)B, whose norm is less than one half on a fixed polydisc, because A,B are positive with A+B<I. The matrix logarithm series is therefore convergent and

|Log(det M(z)/det M)|<=C a.

Cauchy’s formula on a smaller fixed polydisc proves that every fixed positive-order logarithmic derivative is O(N), including mixed derivatives and both maximal-fold parities. The same proof covers 220 by setting Ymat=0. The normalization of the logarithm is relative to its positive reference determinant; no absolute O(N) bound on unrelated scalar logs is needed.

For 202 the proved original negative-root theorem factors P_a(w)=C_a product_j(w+rho_j), rho_j>0. For w in a positive compact and small complex z, every factor w exp(z)+rho_j stays a fixed relative distance from zero. Its marked logarithmic ratio is O(1) uniformly in rho_j; summing over a factors and using Cauchy gives the same O(N) bound for every fixed positive-order derivative.

In particular, changing the logarithmic base clocks by O(N^-1) changes each core Hessian by O(1), by the third-derivative bound and integration along that short path. This controls the ACTUAL finite-r clock correction to the real signed limiting clocks. It does not discard the order-one transport term in the determinant or its source phase.

6. The retained noncore factors have O(s+1) nonlinear cost

Use the EXACT finite models from the correlated-band theorem on its fixed complex clock polydisc. Let z_1,z_2 denote logarithmic clock variations; marked heads are pulled back by their actual affine lifts. All statements below concern fixed derivatives of total order at least two. A function linear in these logarithms has zero such derivatives, even when its value or first derivative is large.

The root cup identity is exactly

p_(d-s)(x)=F_s exp(pi i x) q^[(s-1)x]
                /product_(j=1)^s(1-Q^(x+j)).       (14)

Its character is retained. At finite auxiliary clocks every denominator is uniformly separated from zero, so each logarithmic derivative of order at least two costs O(s). At an escaping clock of size N^2 or N^-2, first factor its full linear monomial if necessary; the remaining derivatives cost O(s/N^2). Physical cups have s=0 and are EXACT linear characters, as are their inverse caps. Literal dual reversals and their Chevalley scalar are affine substitutions with their recorded signs.

For 202/222 the retained partner product P_s(T)=product_(r=a)(m-1)(T-Qr) has fixed logarithmic derivatives O(s): its positive total neighborhood is separated from the forced arc. For 220/022 its total has size N^2 or N^-2; after retaining the exact leading linear monomial its nonlinear derivatives cost O(s/N^2). These statements hold uniformly in a sufficiently small near-fold neighborhood s<=eta N, including both folded parities.

The 200 model retains (-1)^m Q{m(m+1)/2}(UV)m product_(r<m)(1-Q^r/U) times its zero-free Bessel factor. The monomial has zero nonlinear derivatives. The product contributes O(m/N2)=O(N-1), and the Bessel logarithm contributes O(1) on its fixed zero-free complex compact. For 002, extract the exact constant product of -Q^r from product_(r<m)(V-Q^r); its remaining product has the same O(N^-1) cost, and its Bessel factor has O(1) cost. No Bessel factor or phase is discarded. The 022 reciprocity prefactor is also exactly linear in the logarithmic heads.

All other original white monomials, half-twist channel weights, fixed root gamma/h0 factors and physical pivotal characters are affine in the varying logarithmic heads, or independent of them. Their actual values and first derivatives remain part of the source; this theorem does not assert that they satisfy a stationary equation. An exposed coherent binomial row, including its actual first-head normalization, is not one of these local tensor factors. Its separate nonlinear action and curvature are not removed by this decomposition.

Consequently, for one actual local boundary model, its logarithm decomposes locally as an AFFINE retained source term, the applicable core from section 1, and a remainder whose every fixed nonlinear logarithmic derivative is O(s+1). For any fixed collection of these local factors with the literal bounded affine head substitutions, the correction is O(S+1), S=sum deficits. This is just a derivative bound on a finite product; it asserts no rank of that affine substitution.

Finally the actual root numerator/model ratio has a holomorphic O(N^-1) remainder on this same fixed normalized-head neighborhood. The logarithm and all its fixed derivatives are O(N^-1) on a smaller polydisc. Thus it adds only that error to the local curvature estimates. Generic quantum normals are a separate calculation and are not obtained by differentiating this ROOT identity.

These facts identify the finite local sources of order-N curvature and bound their retained corrections. They do not prove a transverse rank, stationarity of the complete geometric sample for the actual labels, a nonzero Gaussian coefficient, or an original relative-cycle pairing.

This is a finite local theorem. Rank and stationarity remain separate questions.
