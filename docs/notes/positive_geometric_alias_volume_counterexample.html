A positive geometric triangulation with an oversized integer-alias face

Research note, 28 October 2026.

Universal wrong-edge alias-face volume domination is false, even for a positive complete ideal triangulation of a hyperbolic knot, with the complete marked meridian retained and the integer alias lattice enforced.

This is a counterexample to a proposed intermediate geometric bound, not to the volume conjecture. No nonzero coefficient of the face in the original quantum cycle is asserted. Precisely that source-selection question is indispensable.

All numerical inequalities below are proved by outward-rounded Arb enclosures. Angle and lattice equations are checked by exact rational arithmetic. Complete shapes and holonomy products are verified in an explicit cubic field. The six topological moves are independently legal in Regina; the initial triangulation is isomorphic to our previously audited three-tetrahedron 525_2 triangulation.

1. The actual triangulation

Start with the standard three-tetrahedron 525_2 triangulation. With indices starting at zero, perform the following 2→32\to3 moves, each specified by (tetrahedron, opposite-face vertex):

(1,0),(2,1),(2,0),(1,2),(5,3),(1,0). (1,0),\ (2,1),\ (2,0),\ (1,2),\ (5,3),\ (1,0). \tag{1}

All face permutations before and after each move are in the fixture. The resulting nine-tetrahedron triangulation has isomorphism signature

For every step, the audit reconstructs the edge-angle matrix from the face permutations, checks one ideal vertex and torus vertex link, and verifies complete positive shapes. Thus positivity is not a numerical assumption about a large triangulation reached by randomization.

The field generator is the upper-half-plane root of

2. A rational wrong-edge face point

Write the three angles of tetrahedron (j), in units of π\pi, as ($ (a_j,b_j,c_j) $). Consider

jajbjcj001/2019/20102/53/5201/109/1031/23/101/541/101/2017/20513/203/201/561/23/101/5717/201/101/20813/203/201/5 \begin{array}{c|ccc} j&a_j&b_j&c_j\\\hline 0&0&1/20&19/20\\ 1&0&2/5&3/5\\ 2&0&1/10&9/10\\ 3&1/2&3/10&1/5\\ 4&1/10&1/20&17/20\\ 5&13/20&3/20&1/5\\ 6&1/2&3/10&1/5\\ 7&17/20&1/10&1/20\\ 8&13/20&3/20&1/5 \end{array} \tag{4}

Each triple sums to one. Let (E) be the actual edge-angle incidence matrix saved in the fixture, in its recorded edge ordering. Direct exact multiplication gives

Eα=2k,k=(1,1,1,1,1,1,2,0,1)T. E\alpha=2k,\qquad k=(1,1,1,1,1,1,2,0,1)^T. \tag{5}

The zero-winding edge meets exactly tetrahedra (0,1,2); they are flat. The other six tetrahedra are strictly positive.

The marked meridian row is

μ(α)=−c1+c2−c6−b7. \mu(\alpha)=-c_1+c_2-c_6-b_7.

At (4) it is exactly zero, its complete marked value. The second recorded peripheral row also evaluates to exactly zero; the audit checks both. Thus the example does not merely satisfy edge windings while dropping the peripheral condition.

3. The defect belongs to the actual integer alias lattice

For each tetrahedron choose the cyclic quad rotation

r=(2,2,0,2,2,1,0,0,0). r=(2,2,0,2,2,1,0,0,0). \tag{6}

This permutes positive geometric shape types cyclically and preserves their positivity. Use edges (0,1,2,3,5,6,7,8), together with the marked meridian, as the nine gluing rows. If their rotated incidence blocks are (G_0,G_1,G_2), set

A=G0−G1,B=G2−G1. A=G_0-G_1,\qquad B=G_2-G_1.

In the recorded ordering,

B=(01000000−1000111−11000−10−1000010−10001−100000010001−1110−20−11−1−10000000−100−100000001000−110),det⁡B=−2. The actual full marked defect is

d=(0,0,0,0,0,1,−1,0,0)T. d=(0,0,0,0,0,1,-1,0,0)^T.

It has the integer preimage

Bm=d,m=(0,1,0,0,0,0,−1,−1,1)T∈ℤ9. \boxed{\quad Bm=d,\qquad m=(0,1,0,0,0,0,-1,-1,1)^T\in\mathbb Z^9.\quad} \tag{8}

The audit also checks ABT=BATAB^T=BA^T. In the notation of the positive Legendre chart, C=B−1AC=B^{-1}A is symmetric, and with the original complete mark κ\kappa, point (4) satisfies exactly

γ=κ−Cθ+2m,θ,β,γ≥0,θ+β+γ=𝟏, \gamma=\kappa-C\theta+2m,\qquad \theta,\beta,\gamma\geq0,\qquad \theta+\beta+\gamma=\mathbf1, \tag{9}

where angles are measured in units of π\pi. It therefore belongs to the weak nonnegative alias angle locus of the genuine integer-shifted potential WmW_m, not just an unrestricted edge-defect assignment or a rational shift on a larger unrecorded phase cover.

The strictly positive alias locus is empty: a zero-winding edge forces zero angles. Accordingly, this weak locus is not the topological closure of a nonempty positive alias cell. No limiting chain or source coefficient is inferred from (9).

4. Its volume is strictly greater than the hyperbolic volume

The three flat tetrahedra contribute zero. Summing the other six gives

Valias=2[Λ(3π/10)+2Λ(π/5)+Λ(π/10)+Λ(π/20)−Λ(7π/20)]. \begin{split} V_{\mathrm{alias}}=2\big[&\Lambda(3\pi/10)+2\Lambda(\pi/5) +\Lambda(\pi/10)\\ &+\Lambda(\pi/20)-\Lambda(7\pi/20)\big]. \end{split} \tag{10}

Arb proves

3.76403<Valias<3.76404,2.82812<Vg<2.82813. 3.76403<V_{\mathrm{alias}}<3.76404, \qquad 2.82812<V_g<2.82813. \tag{11}

In fact Valias>3v3V_{\mathrm{alias}}>3v_3, whereas the initial complete triangulation has only three tetrahedra. The failure of domination is therefore robust; it does not depend on a delicate comparison of critical values or on trusting a numerical face optimizer.

5. Consequence for the critical path

This example rules out the general assertion

supweak nonnegative integer-alias lociV≤Vg \sup_{\text{weak nonnegative integer-alias loci}}V\leq V_g

under geometricity, positivity, ideal topology, complete marked-meridian compatibility, and integer-lattice membership alone. It also rules out a universal retraction of such faces to the original angle polytope that preserves or increases volume.

The compact clamped Legendre construction does not fix this obstruction. On the weak alias locus it minimizes Im⁡Wm\operatorname{Im}W_m, whose radial infimum is that cell’s Rivin volume. Restricting the radial minimization to a finite box can only increase the infimum. At (4), that lower bound already exceeds VgV_g. Compactness of the contour cannot supply the missing comparison between different marked potentials.

For a proof of the original invariant’s volume limit, the excessive face must consequently be absent from the selected source, cancel as a relative Ward/sewing charge, or be excluded by a further structural hypothesis on the actual supported quantum chart. Its coefficient has not been computed here. The result does not assert that the original state acquires a growth rate larger than hyperbolic volume.

The positive complete geometry still controls the original angle polytope. What fails is automatic domination of all other winding cells by that one geometric maximum.

The subsequent last-move trace finds a precise local obstruction: this face fails the very first inverse Pachner replacement. Its new edge has winding zero rather than one, and the exact five-term inverse meets a simultaneous norm wall with retained root-frame monodromy. That calculation identifies the source theorem needed; it does not supply the missing quantum coefficient.
