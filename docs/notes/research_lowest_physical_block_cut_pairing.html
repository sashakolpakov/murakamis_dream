The lowest physical block and the original cut pairing

Private written proof, 19 November 2026. Author-final for independent review.

The operator dual to physical highest-block multiplication has a literal endpoint interpretation: it takes an intertwiner to its lowest diagonal block. This endpoint is another regular coordinate on the whole intertwiner space. The original cut pairing is therefore perfect in these coordinates, including at the complete meridian.

The abstract perfect cut form is existing coverage in integral_projective_cut_duality.md. That result is not a premise below. The present proof identifies the finite duality operator and proves its invertibility directly, without projective-module or fusion arguments. Its sole complete direct is H, the physical highest-block reconstruction, pinned below.

This is a bilinear statement. It supplies neither a positive inner product nor a lower bound for the closing of a specified knot word.

2. The explicit duality operator

Define a finite linear operator on 𝒜\mathcal A by ℒJ=∑a=0dx−afa∏j=0a−1(Q−1RF−QjLF)Ca(J).(8) \mathscr L J= \sum_{a=0}^d\frac{x^{-a}}{f_a} \prod_{j=0}^{a-1}(Q^{-1}R_F-Q^jL_F)\,C_a(J). \qquad\text{(8)} The factors act on the displayed Ca(J)C_a(J). Each summand has total root weight zero. The formula also preserves exact integer degree zero when that restriction is imposed.

The weighted ordinary trace gives the exact identity τ0(H⋆J)=Tr⁡U(gHℒJ).(9) \boxed{\qquad \tau_0(H\star J)=\operatorname{Tr}_U(gH\mathscr L J). \qquad}\qquad\text{(9)} Indeed gF=Q−1FggF=Q^{-1}Fg. Their factors commute, so applying (10) to every factor of (6), and then (7), proves (9). No complex conjugation has been taken.

4. Invertibility from the opposite endpoint

The lowest-block map is injective on the total intertwiner space. To see this, let Wdd=0W_{dd}=0. The physical raising equation gives (1−Qa)Wa−1,d=x−1(QdWadF−QaFWad),a=d,d−1,…,1.(18) (1-Q^a)W_{a-1,d} =x^{-1}\bigl(Q^dW_{ad}F-Q^aFW_{ad}\bigr), \qquad a=d,d-1,\ldots,1. \qquad\text{(18)} All displayed denominators are nonzero, so the entire last column vanishes. The maps EΣbid:U→V1⊗U,0≤b≤d,(19) E_\Sigma^bi_d:U\longrightarrow V_1\otimes U, \qquad 0\le b\le d, \qquad\text{(19)} jointly form an invertible triangular coordinate system: their lowest first-head row is d−bd-b, with coefficient (ed⋯ed−b+1)Kb(e_d\cdots e_{d-b+1})K^b, an invertible operator. Commutation with EΣE_\Sigma and the vanishing last column now give WEΣbid=0WE_\Sigma^bi_d=0 for every bb; hence W=0W=0.

The highest-block map already identifies the whole intertwiner space with 𝒜\mathcal A. The lowest-block map has the same target and is injective, so it is an isomorphism. Its matrices depend holomorphically on MM. Since its determinant is nonzero wherever e1⋯ed≠0e_1\cdots e_d\ne0, its inverse is holomorphic on that same domain. The degree-zero argument is identical using H’s exact integer-degree restriction.

Together with (11), this proves that ℒ\mathscr L is an actual holomorphic linear automorphism at M=1M=1, not just an invertible principal symbol.

5. The original cut is a perfect bilinear pairing

The ordinary form (H,T)↦Tr⁡(gHT)(20) (H,T)\longmapsto\operatorname{Tr}(gHT) \qquad\text{(20)} is perfect on 𝒜\mathcal A. Indeed the actual Cartan is diagonal, so its commutant is a direct sum of full matrix algebras on its weight spaces. On each, gg is a nonzero scalar. The ordinary matrix trace pairing on each block is perfect. The same reasoning works for the finer blocks of exact total occupation degree.

Equations (9) and (11), and invertibility of the lowest-block map, therefore prove that B(H,J)=τ0(H⋆J)(21) B(H,J)=\tau_0(H\star J) \qquad\text{(21)} is perfect. It remains perfect over the holomorphic meridian germ and after specialization to M=1M=1. It is associative in the Frobenius sense, since associativity of ⋆\star gives B(H⋆J,T)=B(H,J⋆T)B(H\star J,T)=B(H,J\star T). No separate symmetry theorem is needed for these conclusions.

The conclusion is concrete: cut duality is realized by the opposite physical endpoint, with the original pivot and every finite coefficient retained. Nondegeneracy says a nonzero element has some nonzero pairing. It does not say that its prescribed pairing with the identity is nonzero. In particular it supplies no positive norm, no subexponential conditioning statement, and no surviving geometric contour coefficient for an actual knot word.
