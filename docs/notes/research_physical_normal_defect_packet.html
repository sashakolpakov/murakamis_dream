A uniform packet for the physical normal-defect rows

Research proof, 4 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

The half-charge observation determines the actual interpolation-normal defect at every physical row, not only at complex geometric colors. Rows outside the shifted contour require an exact cancellation of two Cauchy poles. The remaining regular part has a smaller exponential rate, by the original first-normal bound and polynomial collocation estimates. This gives one uniform source packet at both parities.

The result concerns the specified complete-end source. It does not justify applying an exponentially ill-conditioned shared-index transform to a relative O(N−1)O(N^{-1}) row error.

3. Exact cancellation and a uniform contour packet

Let 𝒥n,N(w)\mathcal J_{n,N}(w) be the exact shifted cosine or sine contour, without its omitted physical rows. At an inside physical node the interior residue identity gives Dn,N(ca)=−𝒥n,N(ca)+𝒯n,N(ca)N.(10) D_{n,N}(c_a) =-\frac{\mathcal J_{n,N}(c_a)+\mathcal T_{n,N}(c_a)}{N}. \qquad\text{(10)} The tail sum has no pole there. Its node gaps (8), weights bounded by two, and (7) bound it by CNAeNΨ(π/2)CN^A e^{N\Psi(\pi/2)}.

At an outside node use the exterior identity and (5) before taking the limit: Dn,N(w)=hn,N(w)−In,Ntail(w)QN(w)−𝒥n,N(w)N. D_{n,N}(w) =\frac{h_{n,N}(w)-I^{\mathrm{ tail}}_{n,N}(w)}{Q_N(w)} -\frac{\mathcal J_{n,N}(w)}N . The numerator vanishes at that node. Hence the exact regular value is Dn,N(ca)=hn,N′(ca)−(In,Ntail)′(ca)QN′(ca)−𝒥n,N(ca)N.(11) D_{n,N}(c_a) =\frac{h'_{n,N}(c_a)-(I^{\mathrm{ tail}}_{n,N})'(c_a)} {Q_N'(c_a)} -\frac{\mathcal J_{n,N}(c_a)}N . \qquad\text{(11)} Equations (7)–(9) bound the first term by CNAeNΨ(π/2)CN^A e^{N\Psi(\pi/2)}. Thus the same conclusion holds at every node: Dn,N(ca)=−𝒥n,N(ca)N+O(NAeNΨ(π/2)).(12) D_{n,N}(c_a) =-\frac{\mathcal J_{n,N}(c_a)}N +O(N^A e^{N\Psi(\pi/2)}). \qquad\text{(12)}

The contour proof of the observation packet is uniform at all these real evaluation colors. Here is the point requiring care. On the two horizontal sides, −2cos⁡z-2\cos z is a compact curve disjoint from [−2,2][-2,2], so the Cauchy denominator is uniformly bounded away from zero. On each midpoint vertical side, |−2cos⁡τa+2cos⁡(TN+iv)|≥c/N(|v|≤H0)(13) |-2\cos\tau_a+2\cos(T_N+iv)|\ge c/N \qquad(|v|\le H_0) \qquad\text{(13)} for all physical nodes and all sufficiently large levels. 4. What can be transported from this estimate

Equation (4) gives a nonzero source coefficient for every actual physical normal-defect row, with one common exponential phase and the explicit rational observation profile. It can be used inside identities that retain the whole original finite source.

It does not license replacing the rows by their leading terms inside an arbitrary oscillatory shared-index sum. Such a sum can magnify the O(N−1)O(N^{-1}) row error exponentially relative to its signed value. The exact Cauchy or reduced-node expression must be retained until the relevant collective contour has been controlled. Likewise the odd top-degree trace term remains the one displayed in the linked odd-level note.

5. A fixed complex neighborhood of the retained real interval

The physical-row estimate has a holomorphic extension away from the spectral endpoint 22. This matters for the reduced-node Cauchy formula: its contour can be kept a fixed distance from the nodes. The extension still uses the literal low complete-end source in (1). It does not assert an estimate for the full even off-grid kernel.

Theorem 2. Fix real n≥3n\ge3 and 0<δ<40<\delta<4.
