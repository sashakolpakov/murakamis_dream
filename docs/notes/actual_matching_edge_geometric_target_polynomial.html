The actual geometric target channel and its finite Casimir polynomial

Follow-up: the actual primitive-word theorem now proves c<=1 for EVERY circle in the certified complete class, including half twists. The c>1 algebraic case classified below is therefore formal and cannot be realized by that complete marked quotient. Equality c=1 remains a genuine parabolic target.

30 October 2026. The literal four-port bend fixes the recoupled channel; it is not a freely chosen geometric diagonal. In the certified right-angled geometry its complete trace is 2-4/c^2, where c is the actual crossing-circle rectangle ratio. In particular, an entire already constructed class has a target outside the real physical channel range at EVERY matching edge.

There is nevertheless an exact source polynomial that can be evaluated at that trace. The fully normalized original Racah scalar is a degree-a polynomial in the target Casimir, generically before specialization. Consequently the completed target operator is that polynomial of the ORIGINAL Casimir. This retains all its normal derivatives. Its scalar evaluation below trace -2 is positive and has a direct variational limit.

This is not yet an asymptotic for that operator in the surrounding coherent contraction. In particular a polynomial of degree proportional to N cannot be replaced by its value at a classical symbol without an additional argument. No global volume equality follows here.

1. The typed bend fixes the geometric target

Use the exact bend of the marked four-port source:

B(K)_(a,b,c,e)=sum_(u,v) K_(a,b;u,v) pV(u,e)pV(v,c).

Its four external objects occur in the order (a,b,c,e). The target tensor in that theorem couples the middle ports (b,c) and then applies the ACTUAL pivotal last-to-first turn to the other pair. It is not a different permutation of these objects.

Here is the corresponding based-holonomy calculation. For an original physical head write

Lminus(X,S)=[[1,0],[S(1-X)/X,-1/X]],
G(X,S)=[[-1/X,(1-X)/(XS)],[-S(1-X)/X,-2+1/X]].

The original cup gives the inverse-head relation (X,S) -> (X^-1,-S). Its two Lminus matrices multiply to the identity, and prefix transport makes its two based meridians inverses. Thus the nested cups in B(I) have ordered based meridians

(A_in, B_in, B_in^-1, A_in^-1).                    (1)

For example, the middle two local Lminus factors cancel; the remaining prefix at the fourth head is the first Lminus, which gives precisely the inverse of the first based meridian. This proves the nesting order, including its basing, directly from the original cups.

The actual critical character map preserves both total F and total K. Therefore it preserves the ENTIRE matrix Lminus_2 Lminus_1, not only the trace of the pair product. Applying the coupon to the first two heads of (1) consequently leaves the prefixes and based meridians at the last two heads unchanged. The bent tensor has the ordered tuple

(A_out, B_out, B_in^-1, A_in^-1),
A_in^-1 B_in^-1 B_out A_out=P_in^-1 P_out=I.        (2)

The original target middle Casimir is consequently

lambda_target=Tr(B_in^-1 B_out).                   (3)

The pivotal turn does not change (3): it is the actual cyclic basepath change for the OTHER pair, already retained in the finite tensor formula. It does not replace a meridian by its negative or insert a longitude.

At completeness normalize the incoming pair by simultaneous conjugacy:

A=-[[1,1],[0,1]], B=-[[1,0],[-4,1]],
P=BA, H=P+I, H^2=0,
C=I+bH, B_out=C B C^-1.

Direct matrix multiplication gives

Tr(B^-1 C B C^-1)=2+16b^2.                        (4)

All these traces use the original trace-minus-two lifts. No common choice of framing flags changes (3) or (4).

2. Classification in the certified complete geometry

The general matching-graph geometry puts the crossing-circle rectangle at (1,E,-1,-E), with E=exp(2pi i x), 0<x<1/2, and c=cot(pi x)>0. The actual primitive collar, calibrated to the quantum half-twist sign in the channel-Hessian theorem, is

tau_epsilon=2ic/(1-epsilon ic),
b-epsilon/2=1/tau_epsilon, epsilon in {0,+1,-1}.

Hence the apparent shear cancels in the physical pair transport:

b=-i/(2c), lambda_target=2-4/c^2.                  (5)

This is independent of the retained half-twist sign; its primitive auxiliary meridian still depends on that sign. The source target eigenvalue is zeta^(2g+1)+zeta^(-2g-1), whose real scaled limit is 2cos(2pi xi_target). Thus (5) has the classification

c>1: xi_target=asin(1/c)/pi in (0,1/2), up to partner;
c=1: lambda_target=-2, the folded parabolic point;
0<c<1: xi_target=1/2 +/- i arcosh(1/c)/pi.          (6)

The last case is a fixed nonzero imaginary channel displacement. Neither the ordinary packet, which keeps the channel real and discrete, nor the shrinking REAL-fold norm theorem is a continuation to it.

There is no general remedy by selecting another matching edge. The strict-quarter theorem, together with the actual marked Conway geometry, proves x_leaf>1/4 for EVERY leaf of EVERY retained robust cylindrical Conway factor. Those are its measured primitive rectangle parameters, as follows also from the proved cusp shape 2i cot(pi x_leaf). Thus every matching edge in that broad class has c<1. In C(h^3), all three ratios are c=1/2, so all three target traces are -14. This is an obstruction to a proposed real-target-channel rescue, not to recoupling or to the conjecture.

3. The original scalar is a generic polynomial in the target Casimir

Put d=N-1, use generic zeta, Q=zeta^2, and symmetric quantum integers [n]=(zeta^n-zeta^-n)/(zeta-zeta^-1). Let S_ag,G_ag,D_a be the ACTUAL seven-factorial sum and face factors of the positive matching-edge scalar:

G_ag=(-1)^d S_ag/[N],
D_a=(-1)^a[N][a]!^2[d-a]!/[d+a+1]!,
H_ag=D_a G_ag D_g.

Define lambda_r=zeta^(2r+1)+zeta^(-2r-1) and

p_a(z)=sum_(k=0)^a (-1)^k/[k]!^2
   *product_(r=0)^(k-1)
      {[a-r][a+r+1]/([d-r][d+r+2])
                *(z-lambda_r)/(zeta-zeta^-1)^2}.
                                                           (7)

For every generic label 0<=a,g<=d, the exact identity is

H_ag=p_a(lambda_g).                               (8)

In particular this is a degree-a polynomial in the ACTUAL target Casimir, with p_0=1 and p_a(lambda_0)=1. Its finite basic sum is

4phi3(Q^-a,Q^(a+1),Q^-g,Q^(g+1);
                     Q,Q^-d,Q^(d+2);Q,Q).        (9)

There are no omitted powers of zeta in (7). Indeed each numerator pair in (9) contributes -(zeta-zeta^-1)^2 zeta^(2r+1)[a-r][a+r+1], or its g version. The four denominator factors and the summand’s Q^k leave exactly the sign (-1)^k in (7). Also [g-r][g+r+1]=(lambda_g-lambda_r)/(zeta-zeta^-1)^2.

Here is an own finite proof from the source sum. By symmetry assume a<=g. Apply the already proved finite Sears identity to (9), with length a, retained parameter Q^(g+1), and d_S=Q^-d,e_S=Q. Its new numerator and denominator EXPONENTS are

(-a,g+1,g-d,-d-a-1); (-d,g-a+1,g-d-a),

with prefactor

F1=(Q^-g,Q^(g-d-a);Q)_a/(Q,Q^(-d-a-1);Q)_a.

Apply the same finite identity again, retaining Q^(g-d) and taking d_S=Q^(g-a+1), e_S=Q^(g-d-a). This gives

4phi3(Q^-a,Q^-a,Q^(g-d),Q^(d+g+2);
                      Q,Q^(g-a+1),Q^(g-a+1);Q,Q),
F2=(Q^-a,Q^(g-a+1);Q)_a/
                      (Q^(g-d-a),Q^(d-a+1);Q)_a.

The intermediate denominator Q^(g-d-a) first vanishes AFTER length a, including g=d. Multiplying and reversing the negative products gives

F1 F2=(-1)^a[N][d-a]![g]!^2/
                      ([d+a+1]![g-a]!^2).         (10)

The right side is precisely the original z=d+g Racah summand, multiplied by (-1)^d D_a D_g/[N]. Its successive summand ratio is

-[d+g+k+2][d-g-k][a-k]^2 /
                          ([g-a+k+1]^2[k+1]^2),

which is also the final series ratio. It terminates at min(a,d-g), exactly the original range. This proves (8) by finite products and the own finite transform, including all generic zeros. Both sides of (9) are symmetric in a,g, which covers a>g.

4. Exact completed-operator identity, with every normal retained

Let Omega and Pi_g be the ORIGINAL generic two-head Casimir and its generic projectors in the completed target theorem. Its scalar is F_ag=G_ag D_g. Equation (8) gives the operator identity

sum_(g=0)^d F_ag Pi_g = D_a^-1 p_a(Omega).          (11)

Indeed the projectors exhaust the actual generic tensor product and Omega has eigenvalue lambda_g on their images. This is a GENERIC identity, so it includes the true first normal and every fixed generic jet. At a colliding pair its root Hermite data are exactly

F_ag(q)=D_a(q)^-1 p_a(lambda_g(q))=R_N(a,g),
H_ag^div(q)=D_a(q)^-1 p_a'(lambda_g(q)).            (12)

The prime in (12) is the spectral-variable derivative; generic-q derivatives are still taken in (11). This distinction is essential. In particular the old nilpotent term has not been removed: (12) computes its actual coefficient rather than inferring that coefficient from equality of the two root scalar values.

The full typed replacement is therefore exactly

sum_g G_ag h_g T_(2g)=beta_N R B[D_a^-1 p_a(Omega)]. (13)

Here beta_N, the physical bend B and the pivotal turn R are the unchanged maps of the completed target theorem. All original row weights, half-twist phases and the exposed whole-source normal remain outside (13) with their original conventions.

For the original range 0<=a<=floor(d/2), every denominator in (7) and D_a is a root unit after its displayed common zero is canceled. Thus the right side of (11) is holomorphic directly, without forming individual singular projectors. Equation (11) is not an assertion that Omega is Hermitian or that p_a(Omega) has polynomial operator norm.

5. Positive evaluation at the actual nonphysical geometric trace

At q=exp(pi i/N) put theta=pi/N, x_r=2cos((2r+1)theta), s_k=product_(v=1)^k 2sin(v theta). Converting the generic products in (7) at the root gives

p_a(z)=sum_(k=0)^a s_k^-4
             product_(r=0)^(k-1)(x_r-x_a)(x_r-z). (14)

For the original folded row range, x_r>x_a whenever r<a. Hence EVERY term in (14) is positive at every real z<-2. This is a genuine finite source-polynomial evaluation, not interpolation in a fabricated complex integer color. At z=x_g it equals the previously proved positive squared-trinomial Racah sum, but (14) remains defined away from all those physical nodes.

Fix compact subsets xi in (0,1/2], c in (0,1), and let a/N->xi, with actual geometric trace z=2-4/c^2. Write

A=sin^2(pi xi), kappa=c^-2,
f_(xi,c)(v)=integral_0^v
   log[(A-sin^2(pi s))(kappa-sin^2(pi s))/sin^4(pi s)] ds.
                                                           (15)

Then the actual finite polynomial satisfies

N^-1 log p_a(2-4/c^2) -> max_(0<=v<=xi) f_(xi,c)(v).
                                                           (16)

If the parameters on the right are taken at their actual values xi=a/N, the logarithmic error is O(log N), uniformly on the stated compacts. This includes the folded limit. More explicitly, its k-th term has EXACT logarithm

sum_(v=a-k+1)^(a+k) log sin(v theta)
   -4 sum_(v=1)^k log sin(v theta)
   +sum_(r=0)^(k-1) log[kappa-sin^2((r+1/2)theta)].

The first line follows from x_r-x_a=4 sin((a-r)theta)sin((a+r+1)theta); all powers of two cancel. The last summand is smooth uniformly because kappa>1. The elementary endpoint harmonic bound for log sin gives an O(log N) error for its Riemann sums, even when a+k approaches N-1 or a-k=0. Finally sin(pi(xi-s))sin(pi(xi+s))=A-sin^2(pi s) identifies the integral with (15). Thus log term_k=N f(k/N)+O(log N), including either endpoint. The polynomial has at most N positive terms; the upper bound is their maximum times N, and a nearest integer to its interior maximum gives the reverse bound with the same logarithmic loss.

The maximizing point is unique. Set t=sin^2(pi v). The derivative exponential is (A-t)(kappa-t)/t^2, strictly decreasing from infinity to zero as t runs from 0 to A. Consequently

sin^2(pi v_*)=A kappa/(A+kappa).                  (17)

At the folded row xi=1/2, this becomes

v_*=atan(1/c)/pi=x,                              (18)

the ACTUAL measured crossing-circle rectangle parameter. Thus the positive finite polynomial selects the correct local rectangle in this precise scalar variational sense.

For z in a fixed compact subset of (-infinity,-2), all fixed generic-q and spectral derivatives of the scalar in (14) cost only a polynomial relative to p_a(z). This follows from (7), not from differentiating a root-only formula: each root term is positive; the nonzero quantum integer factors have total logarithmic derivative O(N^2(1+log N)), and z-x_r is uniformly separated from zero. On a disk of that reciprocal radius every summand remains in a fixed narrow sector. Cauchy gives the fixed-jet bound. This controls scalar evaluation at that trace, not the norm of the operator in (11).
