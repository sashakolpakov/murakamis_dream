A direct positive ideal triangulation and the full source angle critical point

29 October 2026. The ACTUAL half-twisted augmented pretzel polyhedra admit a positive geometric ideal triangulation with compatible face pairings. The proof below checks every homogeneous face map; no general Epstein–Penner existence theorem is used. This removes an optional dependency from the Casson–Rivin presentation. The already proved finite-center/equivariant-map geometric completion does not need it.

1. An explicit homogeneous section of every original ideal cell

Use alpha=pi/s, rho^2=sec(alpha)+tan(alpha), and the actual P_s vertices

Z_i=rho exp(2ii alpha),
U_i^+=rho^-1 exp((2i+1)i alpha),
U_i^-=rho^3 exp((2i+1)i alpha).

The face cycles and their literal half-twist pairings are those of the geometric cellulation and the complete port anchor. Put O=(0,rho) in upper half-space.

The rotation R(z)=exp(2i alpha)z and the holomorphic half-turn

F_i(z)=rho^2 exp(4ii alpha)/z                          (1)

are genuine global P_s isometries: substitution permutes its vertices, and hence its convex hull. In particular

F_i: (Z_i,U_(i-1)^+,U_i^+)
                   ->(Z_i,U_i^-,U_(i-1)^-).         (2)

The finite dihedral group generated by R and F_0 fixes O. Conversely a point fixed by the rotation lies on the vertical axis, and F_0 fixes only height rho on that axis. Averaging any finite interior point in the future hyperboloid over this finite group and normalizing stays inside every strict supporting halfspace. The average is O. Thus O is STRICTLY interior to P_s, not merely a guessed symmetry center.

In the Lorentz model use the ideal light lift

l(z)=(|z|^2+1,2Re z,2Im z,|z|^2-1),
V(z)=rho l(z)/(|z|^2+rho^2).                         (3)

Then -<O,V(z)>=1, using the unit hyperboloid representative of O. All lifted ideal vertices of this cell lie in the SAME affine three-plane A_O={v:-<O,v>=1}, which does not contain the origin. Since F_i fixes O, its Lorentz action carries V(z) EXACTLY to V(F_i z), without an additional scalar on the light ray.

2. Every actual face map preserves these lifts

Let r_+ be the reflection in the white W_+ plane. Develop the second cell as r_+ P_s and give it center r_+ O and light lifts r_+ V(z). These satisfy its analogous affine normalization.

For a white face Q or W_-, the literal transition from the second cell to the first is r_face r_+. Applied to its face vertices it gives

(r_face r_+) r_+ V(z)=r_face V(z)=V(z),               (4)

because a Lorentz plane reflection fixes the ENTIRE plane, including the normalized light vectors, not only their projective endpoints. For W_+ the transition is identity and r_+ itself fixes those vectors. Thus every white face preserves the homogeneous lifts exactly.

For shaded faces, the complete port proof gives actual orientation- preserving transitions gamma_i^+ and gamma_i^- with the ordered maps

gamma_i^+ r_+ : (Z_i,U_i^-,U_(i-1)^-)
                          ->(Z_i,U_(i-1)^+,U_i^+),
gamma_i^- r_+ : (Z_i,U_(i-1)^+,U_i^+)
                          ->(Z_i,U_i^-,U_(i-1)^-).  (5)

They agree on those three ideal points with the GLOBAL isometry F_i in(1). This statement checks all three vertices, not only a fixed flag. The maps gamma_i^plusminus r_+ reverse ambient orientation, whereas F_i preserves it. Consequently their precise difference is reflection in the source triangle plane: composing with that plane reflection makes an orientation-preserving Mobius map fixing three distinct points, which is identity. Such a plane reflection fixes each V(z) exactly. It follows that on the actual face vertices

gamma_i^plusminus r_+ V(z)=F_i V(z)=V(target z).       (6)

Equation(6) is the needed scalar-normalization check. It does NOT assert that gamma_i maps the second CELL CENTER to the first center. Only its homogeneous face section is required, and that has now been proved. The literal reverse-cap traversal is the same holomorphic F_0 combined with an index reversal; it preserves(3) and is not a mirror gluing.

Thus the two cells, every face pairing and all their translates have Gamma-equivariant ideal lifts with affine face maps. The complete face-pairing identities make this assignment consistent. This proof uses the actual retained half twists; it does not replace the source by its untwisted partner.

3. A finite compatible subdivision, proved directly

Here is the elementary affine subdivision argument for these cells. For a three-dimensional convex cell with v vertices, vertex heights modulo affine functions have dimension v-4. For a paired n-gonal face, requiring the two height restrictions to differ by an affine function imposes at most n-3 linear conditions. Under such a condition the two lower-face subdivisions match, since adding an affine function does not change a regular subdivision.

For a finite paired affine cellulation, the total remaining dimension is at least

sum_cells(v-4)-sum_facepairs(n-3)
               =(1/2)sum_cells(f-4).                (7)

Use twice the edge count for the sum of face side counts and v-e+f=2 for each convex three-cell. Every cell has f>=4, and equality means a tetrahedron. If some cell is not tetrahedral, (7) is positive. Choose a nonzero compatible class of heights and take its lower convex subdivision. At least one cell refines nontrivially; all new cells are three-dimensional, use only old vertices and have matched affine faces. Every original vertex is extreme in projection, so the lower subdivision does not discard an original boundary vertex.

Repeat. This terminates: assign each resulting three-cell one affine- independent quadruple of its original vertices. Two cells with disjoint interiors cannot contain the same nondegenerate tetrahedron. Within each original cell only finitely many quadruples exist, while every nontrivial refinement increases the cell count. If a non-tetrahedral cell remained, (7) would permit another refinement, contradicting this finite bound. Thus a finite compatible tetrahedral subdivision results.

Apply this argument in the affine planes from(3)–(6). Four affine- independent light vectors in A_O are LINEARLY independent because A_O does not contain zero. Hence their ideal hyperbolic tetrahedron is nonflat. Convex subdivision gives its positive orientation. The result is a finite POSITIVE GEOMETRIC IDEAL triangulation of the actual complete augmented manifold. No literature triangulation existence theorem is needed for this class.

At the selected sufficiently long filling, move its ideal vertex orbits by the actual bounded framed representation and its marked peripheral flags. Every one of the finitely many shapes stays in the upper half-plane by continuity. Contractible edge transitions are identity, so positive edge angle sums remain exactly2pi. This gives a positive source angle point on the ACTUAL ideal triangulation.

4. The full edge/peripheral tangent space, not just the source-family tangent

For t tetrahedra and h cusp tori let E be the edge-Hamiltonian space in the 2t-dimensional real shape symplectic space. Its rank is t-h. The rank statement can be checked directly: an edge-row relation has equal sums on opposite edge pairs in every abstract tetrahedron. Its six local coefficients therefore have the form lambda_ij=a_i+a_j. The formula a_i=(lambda_ij+lambda_ik-lambda_jk)/2 shows that a_i agrees across matched faces. Connected ideal vertex links propagate it to one constant per cusp. Conversely every cusp constant gives a relation. The ideal Euler count gives t global edges, so precisely h independent relations leave rank t-h.

The edge rows are isotropic: the local skew corner counts cancel across paired faces. The peripheral rows belong to E^perp; the same local normal-curve count pairs a primitive meridian and longitude by -2, with different cusp tori pairing zero. Thus their 2h rows span the ENTIRE symplectic quotient E^perp/E. This is the actual marked incidence calculation underlying the boundary pairing proof, not a claim that stationarity on a small source family exhausts the angle tangent.

Use centered projective logs U_i,V_i, so the reduced form is

Omega_red=-(1/2)sum_i dU_i wedge dV_i.                (8)

At any positive full complex gluing solution, its real shape-log vector lies in E^perp. EVERY affine angle tangent satisfying the edge equations lies there too. The sine rule for a positive tetrahedron gives

dVol(delta theta)=Omega(Re shape,delta theta)
  =(1/2)sum_i[Re V_i delta Im U_i
                                  -Re U_i delta Im V_i]. (9)

Consequently an incomplete gluing point is critical on its FULL fixed- peripheral-ANGLE slice, even though it need not maximize the larger unfilled cusp angle domain. No nonlinear gluing condition is imposed on the tangent delta theta in(9).

For the actual filled source, Re(U_i+p_iV_i)=0. On the larger affine slice fixing only Im(U_i+p_iV_i)=the actual primitive2pi angle, its contribution in(9) is

(1/2)Re V_i delta Im(U_i+p_iV_i)=0.

Every unfilled main cusp has U_i=V_i=0, so it contributes zero as well. Thus the source is stationary on the FULL filled-slope angle slice, not merely on its selected critical representation curve.

5. Concavity, boundary maxima and the actual hyperbolic value

For angles alpha,beta,gamma>0 with sum pi, the negative Hessian of Lambda(alpha)+Lambda(beta)+Lambda(gamma) in (alpha,gamma) is

[[cot alpha+cot beta,cot beta],
 [cot beta,cot gamma+cot beta]].

Its determinant is1 and its first diagonal entry is sin(gamma)/(sin(alpha)sin(beta))>0. It is strictly positive definite. Thus total ideal volume is strictly concave on every affine positive angle slice. At the positive source point its full constrained derivative vanishes by(9). Every straight segment from it to a different point in the closed angle polytope stays positive before its final endpoint; its volume is strictly decreasing after its initial zero derivative. Continuity at zero/pi angles then makes EVERY different boundary value strictly smaller as well. The source point is the unique GLOBAL maximum.

The value is the marked source regulator because the shapes develop the same actual representation and oriented fundamental class. The primitive-angle proof and general geometric completion identify it with the complete filled metric and its hyperbolic volume. The complete augmented maximum V_s and the finite filled maximum V_src(p) are different: each uses its OWN affine peripheral constraints.

This proves the optional Casson–Rivin certificate for the current explicit augmented family, with all existence/rank premises checked. For an arbitrary base, the general geometric completion remains valid without an ideal triangulation. A universal Casson–Rivin presentation still needs a verified positive ideal angle chart; it does not follow from an arbitrary original source critical point or a matching action alone.

It compares FULL Lorentz/Hermitian light matrices in the normalization(3), not projective point images alone. It also checks the analytical identity F_i(O)=O to that precision. The finite compatible-subdivision, rank and strict-concavity arguments above are analytical and do not rest on these samples.
