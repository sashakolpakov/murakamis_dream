Complementary root arcs preserve the source, but change its relative cycle

28 October 2026. A long actual root arc can be replaced exactly by the inverse complementary arc on a regular Fermat fiber. The central norm is compulsory. For a homogeneous original source, the resulting complementary period has an explicitly determined central divisor. Its scalar numerator is the SAME original selected coefficient.

There is also an exact global cycle check: after any nonempty set of these complements, its bare ordinary product-torus integral, enclosing all the Fermat roots, is ZERO. The original nonzero source is carried by additional short-arc relative residue cycles. Thus center freedom does not justify substituting a bare convex torus for the original selected cycle. A construction retaining those relative cycles remains possible; it is not supplied by the complement identity.

1. Exact complementation with its original source normalizer

Use the actual charged polynomial source with d=N-1,

I_N(h)=lambda_C(P_h),
K_N=chi_N/N sum_h W_h I_N(h),
deg P_h=k d, lambda_C=[X_1^d ... X_k^d] mod(X_i^N-C_i).

All W_h, pivots and chi_N remain. The equality between lambda_C and the specified Fermat residue, and its center independence at this degree, were proved in the actual source theorem. Let a genuine two-anchor block be

B_L(X_i,X_j;a)=product_(r=0)^(L-1)(X_i-Q^r a X_j).

For every 0<=L<=N, set

D=B_(N-L)(X_i,X_j;aQ^L),
F_i=X_i^N-C_i,
K_ij=C_i-a^N C_j.

Then the ordinary polynomial identity is

B_L D=X_i^N-a^N X_j^N=K_ij+F_i-a^N F_j.       (1)

If K_ij!=0, D has no zero on the Fermat fiber and is a UNIT in its finite algebra. Its inverse is exactly B_L/K_ij. Thus replacing B_L by K_ij/D is a source-exact identity. There is no numerical normalization chosen by the value of a knot invariant. A nonunit tail coefficient alpha contributes its retained factor alpha^L; loops have already been evaluated as scalar monomials instead.

Choose any fixed collection S of block slots, with r=|S|>=1, and write P_h=E_h product_(e in S)B_e. Define the rational representative

R_h=E_h product_(e in S)D_e^(-1),
K_S=product_(e in S)K_e.

On the regular central domain where all K_e!=0,

lambda_C(R_h)=I_N(h)/K_S.                       (2)

The left means the SAME selected sum of local grid residues, not an unspecified contour integral of the rational representative. Equation (2) follows from (1) in the finite algebra and the original center independence of lambda_C(P_h). It fixes its central divisor exactly; its remaining scalar is the original period I_N(h). Knowing that divisor does not establish that this scalar is nonzero.

For the actual bridge blocks a is a diagram-dependent power of x times an integer power of Q: the h-dependence enters only the latter. Consequently aN=(xN)^integer is a signed diagram datum, independent of the clocks. For a fixed choice of potential block slots, even when a length is zero, the same K_S can be retained through the full clock sum. The normalized complementary whole period is then exactly K_N/K_S. Choosing only the long blocks separately in each sector is also legitimate, but its norm product must remain inside that sum.

2. The bare source torus of the complemented form is exactly zero

Define the rational k-form

Omega_comp=K_S R_h(X) dX_1...dX_k/product_i F_i. (3)

Take any ordinary product torus T with each radius larger than |C_i|^(1/N), and assume that no D_e vanishes anywhere on T. Such radii always exist: for unit arc coefficients any unequal endpoint radii avoid that edge’s roots. The normalization of integrals is (2pii)^(-k).

Then EXACTLY

integral_T Omega_comp=0.                        (4)

Proof. Complementing one block changes its homogeneous X-degree from L to -(N-L), a decrease of N. Thus

deg R_h=k(N-1)-rN.

Dilate all radii of T by the same positive factor t>=1. No F_i pole is crossed, since all its roots were inside the original coordinate circle. The D_e are homogeneous, so their zero set cannot cross this common dilation either. Holomorphic top-form Stokes therefore makes the integral constant in t. On the dilated torus the k-form has size O(t^(-rN)): the numerator, dX and the Fermat denominators contribute respectively t(k(N-1)-rN),tk and t^(-kN). Its integral tends to zero. This proves (4), without an asymptotic exchange in N.

For comparison, the original polynomial form

Omega_poly=P_h dX/product_i F_i

integrates on this same torus to lambda_C(P_h)=I_N(h). Thus the chosen sum of small grid-residue tori is not homologous to T in the complement of the NEW short-arc divisors unless that source value happens to vanish. Every nonzero original source requires an additional relative residue cycle in this presentation.

The statement includes complementing a zero-length block: in the finite algebra 1=K_ij/(X_iN-aN X_j^N). This exact unit insertion still creates new off-fiber poles and a zero bare-torus integral. One cannot infer a selected geometric cycle merely from the poles of a chosen rational representative.

3. The missing source currents are explicit

For one complemented block let P_h=E B_L and write F_other=product_(ell notin {i,j})F_ell. Equation (1) gives the EXACT identity of meromorphic forms

Omega_poly-Omega_comp
  = E/D *[1/(F_j F_other)-a^N/(F_i F_other)] dX. (5)

Each term has lost one Fermat denominator. It has zero local product residue at every original grid point because D is regular there. That explains why (1) preserves lambda_C. It does NOT make these currents zero on an enlarged torus: the short-arc divisors can supply the missing pole. Combining (4)–(5) gives

I_N(h)=integral_T E/D
             *[1/(F_j F_other)-a^N/(F_i F_other)]dX. (6)

Thus these explicitly retained currents carry the ENTIRE selected source on that torus. They cannot be declared subleading from the complement identity or from central regularity. With several complements, expanding each K_e+F_i-a^N F_j gives the corresponding finite collection of mixed source currents; dependent intersections must remain jointly regulated if they are converted to contour residues.

A literal unit source cell

The actual full closure/reproducing block is

B_d(X,Y;Qt)=sum_(j=0)^d X^(d-j)(tY)^j.

With the permitted exterior Y^d its original selected coefficient is EXACTLY one. Its complement is D=X-tY and K=C_X-t^N C_Y. On every product torus enclosing both Fermat root grids and avoiding X=tY, its complemented bare integral is zero by (4), while the two currents in (5) have sum one. This is the original reproducing source normalization, not an auxiliary quantum invariant. Both unpivoted t=1 and actual pivotal t=Q^(-1) cases retain these values.

4. A constructive finite forest residue formula

The additional relative cycles have an exact algebraic decomposition. Choose ANY nonempty forest of actual two-anchor block slots and orient each edge from a child i to its parent p(i). Reverse a block when necessary using its exact identity

B_L(X,Y;a)=(-a)^L Q^[L(L-1)/2]
                    B_L(Y,X;a^(-1)Q^(1-L)).

Every such scalar and the original gamma,W_h,chi_N remain. Write the oriented selected blocks as B_(L_i)(X_i,X_p;a_i), and put

m_i=N-L_i,
S_i=B_(m_i)(X_i,X_p;a_i Q^(L_i)),
H_i=F_i S_i                   for a nonroot,
H_r=F_r                       for a forest root,
R=(product_i K_i) E.

Here P_h=E product_i B_(L_i) includes the actual orientation scalars. Assume generic nonzero centers so ALL path norms

C_i-(product_(edges on i->ancestor) a_e^N) C_ancestor

are nonzero. This is stronger than merely checking each adjacent K_i. For the actual unit-root phases, a_e^N=+1 or -1, so pairwise distinct centers up to sign suffice. The result extends rationally wherever the corresponding joint residue is defined, but colliding residues must not be split independently.

The triangular monic system H=0 has simple zeros. At a nonroot choose either F_i=0, or a short root

X_i=a_i Q^(L_i+r_i)X_p, 0<=r_i<m_i.             (7)

Every component of chosen short edges has one root satisfying its original Fermat equation. This lists ALL zeros of H, not only an assumed geometric branch. With standard Grothendieck orientation,

sum_(all H zeros) R(X)/det(partial H/partial X)=0. (8)

Here is a direct proof of the zero. H_i is monic in its child variable, of degree N+m_i; a root equation has degree N. Their coefficients depend only on parents. Consecutive monic division in child-to-parent order gives a basis with child exponent <N+m_i and root exponent <N. Every reduction preserves or decreases TOTAL degree: the highest part is homogeneous and the central term decreases degree by N. The global sum of residues equals the coefficient of the top basis monomial. This follows inductively from the elementary one-variable identity that, for monic h, sum_(h(x)=0)A(x)/h’(x) is the highest coefficient of the remainder of A modulo h. The required top degree is

sum_i(deg_(X_i)H_i-1)=k(N-1)+sum_nonroot m_i,

whereas deg R=k(N-1)-sum_nonroot L_i. Their difference is exactly N times the number of forest edges, which is positive. The top coefficient therefore vanishes, proving (8). Equivalently the leading homogeneous system has no projective zero: start with each root and propagate zero to its children. There is no omitted residue at infinity.

For the EMPTY choice of short edges every F_i vanishes. At those points R/product S_i=P_h, so their sum of residues is EXACTLY I_N(h). Consequently

I_N(h)=-sum_(nonempty selected-short subsets)
            sum_(their explicit zeros) R/J_H.     (9)

All relative cycle coefficients in this complete decomposition are fixed, with no unknown knot scalar. For fixed diagram size its number of points is polynomial in N: it is at most product_roots N product_nonroots(N+m_i). This is a finite canonical residue presentation once the actual forest and orientations have been specified, not a positivity assertion about its terms.

The triangular Jacobian is completely explicit. Its factor at i is

N X_i^d S_i(X)                 if F_i=0,
(X_i^N-C_i) partial_(X_i)S_i   if S_i=0,

and at an original forest root it is N X_r^d. At the short root (7),

partial_(X_i)S_i
  =(a_i Q^(L_i)X_p)^(m_i-1)
    *(-1)^r_i Q^[r_i(m_i-1)-r_i(r_i+1)/2]
    *F_(r_i) F_(m_i-1-r_i), F_j=(Q;Q)_j.       (10)

This is obtained by splitting the other roots at r_i. Every factor is nonzero under the stated genericity; its possible exponential size is retained.

For an entirely selected SPANNING tree, X_i=beta_i X_r. Homogeneity of E cancels all powers of X_r against (10) and the root Jacobian. The N identical root labels cancel its 1/N exactly, leaving an explicit sum of products of the factorials in (10), the evaluated remaining actual blocks E(beta), and the retained central path norms. This is an exact full-source channel, but it is generally only ONE part of (9). The mixed subsets must not be discarded.

More generally, grouping a selected component into one root gives source charge d+sum_selected_internal(d-L_i), which can exceed N-1. Its remaining regular-Fermat root sum has genuine winding sectors. It cannot be called the ordinary top coefficient, or simplified by dropping cyclic aliases, before the stated component sums or a further source identity have been performed.

5. An arbitrary-block contraction, with its changed source charge

The one-edge case also has a direct ordinary-coefficient formulation. Put m=N-L, b_r=aQ^(L+r), and

w_r=1/product_(s!=r)(b_r-b_s), 0<=r<m.

For ANY polynomial exterior E and all other original charges equal to d, there is the exact source identity

[X_i^d X_j^d X_other^d] B_L E
  =sum_(r<m) w_r
       [X_j^(2d-L) X_other^d] E(X_i=b_r X_j).  (11)

Its ordinary parent charge is 2d-L=d+m-1, and must be retained. The root sum is essential; its individual regular-Fermat channels need not have the same ordinary coefficient.

Proof. The Lagrange sums sum_r w_r b_r^p vanish for p<m-1 and equal h_(p-m+1)(b) for p>=m-1. Their generating function is

sum_(k>=0)h_k(b)t^k
     =product_(s=0)^(L-1)(1-aQ^s t)/(1-a^N t^N).

Only p<=2d-L can contribute to the coefficient on the right, hence k<=d. In that range h_k is exactly the kth coefficient of the displayed numerator, including its zero range L<k<N. These are precisely the coefficients of B_L needed on the left. This proves (11), without choosing an unknown normalization or a geometric cycle.

For subsequent charges larger than d, the same short-root formula cannot simply be iterated: the next complete homogeneous index may reach N, where the denominator above produces aliases. For example N=3,L=2,E=X_i^3, with tail charge 2 and parent charge 3, gives zero on the original side but a nonzero short-root evaluation. The full forest formula (9) retains the necessary mixed channels; further charge/jet reductions require an additional explicit source identity.

6. Consequence for a geometric cycle construction

Complementation itself is fully authorized by the existing actual source and preserves every normalization. It can replace long arcs by shorter inverse factors and may provide useful geometric charts. But its selected relative cycle has to be transported as well. Equations (4)–(6) show precisely why a bare torus replacement loses the original source, even with arbitrary regular Fermat centers.

Large N-dependent centers need extra care: K_e can itself carry an exponential action. It is retained in (1)–(6), and fixed-center tame Fourier-frame estimates do not extend automatically to it. Conversely on fixed compact central domains these factors may be tame, but the relative currents still need not vanish.

A successful Rivin comparison would therefore have to map the FULL grid/short-arc relative cycle, or the explicit currents (5), to a source-compatible convex chain and compute its geometric coefficient. Its starting value is not determined by the known central divisor (2). No broad volume upper or lower bound follows from the present complement identity alone.

Audit: actual charged polynomials after closure contraction, ALL triangular forest zeros and mixed short subsets, original scalar/orientation/path norms, factorial Jacobians and the spanning-tree root cancellation, a whole original signed pivotal scalar, and the original unit closure cell’s zero bare integral versus nonzero source currents.
