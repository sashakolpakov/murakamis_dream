Exponential cancellation of the actual low and central high endpoint bands

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The low endpoint polynomial and the central portion of its original high Newton band cancel exponentially after division by the distinct-node polynomial, on fixed lower angular compact sets. This holds at both level parities. It follows from the exact original kernel difference equation and a holomorphic iteration with a fixed domain buffer. No formal asymptotic series is used to infer the exponential error.

The result combines the actual low term with the half-character term before estimating them. It extends to arbitrary fixed products of endpoint coupons at a shared surviving vertex. It does not discard the vertical sides or the remaining physical rows. The positive exponential constant obtained here is not compared with a filled action deficit, and no full-source asymptotic or nonzero cycle coefficient follows.
