A protected middle with strict outer loads cannot maximize at the spectral cap

The middle-cap calculus does not supply a uniform finite-coefficient limit at the cap, a relative-chain comparison, or a cycle selection.

1. The exact protected-start functional

Take a LEFT interval with its original protected input middle, so its start is exactly μ=0\mu=0. Fix 0<ν<12,0<ξ<ν,ζ=ν−ξ>0. 0<\nu<\tfrac12,\qquad 0<\xi<\nu,\qquad \zeta=\nu-\xi>0. The original four face degrees (ξ,0,1−ν,ζ)(\xi,0,1-\nu,\zeta) stay fixed. Put k=cos⁡(πδ)>0k=\cos(\pi\delta)>0 and β(t)=4sin⁡2(πt),αδ(t)=4[k2−sin⁡2(πt)], \beta(t)=4\sin^2(\pi t),\qquad \alpha_\delta(t)=4[k^2-\sin^2(\pi t)], ρη(x)=1+x+(1−x)2+4ηx2,Jk(x)=∫0νlog⁡ρk2/sin⁡2(πt)(x)dt. \rho_\eta(x)=\frac{1+x+\sqrt{(1-x)^2+4\eta x}}2,\qquad J_k(x)=\int_0^\nu\log\rho_{k^2/\sin^2(\pi t)}(x)\,dt. Normalize y=1y=1 only inside the coefficient Legendre transform. The original sharp functional is Hk(0,ν,ξ)=Ik(ν)+Jk*(ξ),Ik(ν)=∫0νlog⁡β(t)|αδ(t)|dt,Jk*(ξ)=infx>0{Jk(x)−ξlog⁡x}. \begin{aligned} H_k(0,\nu,\xi)&=I_k(\nu)+J_k^*(\xi),\\ I_k(\nu)&=\int_0^\nu\log\frac{\beta(t)}{|\alpha_\delta(t)|}\,dt,\\ J_k^*(\xi)&=\inf_{x>0}\{J_k(x)-\xi\log x\}. \end{aligned}\tag{1} This is the actual unused-product plus positive-word normalization. The identities ∫01/2log⁡|αδ(t)|dt=∫01/2log⁡β(t)dt=0 \int_0^{1/2}\log|\alpha_\delta(t)|\,dt =\int_0^{1/2}\log\beta(t)\,dt=0 incorporate both unused intervals. No omitted-half scalar is divided out. The logarithmic singularities are integrable. At the cap k=0k=0, ρ0(x)=max⁡(1,x),I0=J0*=H0=0. \rho_0(x)=\max(1,x),\qquad I_0=J_0^*=H_0=0.\tag{2} This equality holds for every closed outer split. The strict improvement below requires both outer loads positive.

2. A constant-transition lower bound

Since k2/sin⁡2(πt)≥k2k^2/\sin^2(\pi t)\ge k^2 and ρη(x)\rho_\eta(x) increases with η\eta, for every x>0x>0, Jk(x)≥νlog⁡ρk2(x). J_k(x)\ge\nu\log\rho_{k^2}(x).\tag{3} Define the constant-transition outer entropy by Fk(p)=infℓ∈ℝ{log⁡ρk2(eℓ)−pℓ},0≤p≤1. F_k(p)=\inf_{\ell\in\mathbb R} \{\log\rho_{k^2}(e^\ell)-p\ell\},\qquad 0\le p\le1.\tag{4} It is concave in pp, as an infimum of affine functions. This uses only outer-degree concavity at fixed interval and transition; it makes no row-concavity or shared minimax assertion. The inequality ρk2(x)≥max⁡(1,x)\rho_{k^2}(x)\ge\max(1,x) gives Fk≥0F_k\ge0, and the limits x→0,∞x\to0,\infty give Fk(0)=Fk(1)=0F_k(0)=F_k(1)=0. No new finite-level coefficient theorem is needed here.

3. The monomer cost is quadratic

For k<sin⁡(πν)k<\sin(\pi\nu), the exact half-interval integral identities give Ik(ν)=−∫ν1/2log⁡sin⁡2(πt)sin⁡2(πt)−k2dt. I_k(\nu)=-\int_\nu^{1/2} \log\frac{\sin^2(\pi t)}{\sin^2(\pi t)-k^2}\,dt.\tag{7} Thus Ik≤0I_k\le0. If k2≤sin⁡2(πν)/2k^2\le\sin^2(\pi\nu)/2, the inequality −log⁡(1−z)≤2z-\log(1-z)\le2z for 0≤z≤1/20\le z\le1/2 gives Ik(ν)≥−2k2∫ν1/2csc⁡2(πt)dt=−2πk2cot⁡(πν). H_k(0,\nu,\xi)\ge2a\log(1+k)-\frac2\pi k^2\cot(\pi\nu).\tag{9} For any positive kk satisfying k≤min⁡{1,sin⁡(πν)2,πa4tan(πν)}, k\le\min\left\{1,\frac{\sin(\pi\nu)}{\sqrt2}, \frac{\pi a}{4}\tan(\pi\nu)\right\},\tag{10} use log⁡(1+k)≥k/2\log(1+k)\ge k/2 to conclude Hk(0,ν,ξ)≥ak2>0=H0(0,ν,ξ). Each fixed admissible pair has its own positive range (10). Fixed k>0k>0 satisfies the actual finite positive-word condition at all sufficiently large NN; no joint kNk_N limit is asserted.

The terminal half-interval has a separate immediate conclusion. If ν=1/2\nu=1/2 and 0<ξ<1/20<\xi<1/2, (7) is empty, so Ik=0I_k=0 exactly and Hk(0,12,ξ)≥2min⁡(ξ,12−ξ)log⁡(1+k)>0,0<k<1. This calculus observation asserts no stationarity in the terminal row, change of sector label, or finite support relabeling.

4. Reflection and the shared maximum

The exact finite reflection takes (i,j)(i,j) to (d−j,d−i)(d-j,d-i) and exchanges the middle variables while preserving the tail and all unused rows. Hence the same conclusion holds for a protected output middle, with interval [1−ν,1][1-\nu,1], strict outer loads ξ,ν−ξ\xi,\nu-\xi, and 0<ν≤1/20<\nu\le1/2. The finite endpoint is j=dj=d, not NN; the recorded one-row shift in the limiting functional does not change a protected target or the reflected RIGHT support.

At a shared sharp-functional maximum with free real spectra, change only this port’s δ\delta from 1/21/2 to arccos⁡(k)/π\arccos(k)/\pi. Every original face load, the fixed mark, and every other local term remain unchanged. Equations (11)–(12) therefore exclude this cap.

The strict outer hypothesis is essential. If ξ=0\xi=0 or ξ=ν\xi=\nu, the word is monomial, Jk*=0J_k^*=0, and (7) is nonpositive. No exclusion of that joint protected-outer corner is claimed. Zero-length intervals, simultaneous two-terminal intervals [0,1][0,1], and cap-uniform finite coefficient bounds remain separate questions. The lemma gives no source growth lower bound or original-period contribution.
