The original odd pretzel and its complete mixed-circle transfer source

29 October 2026. The literal original turns and disjoint twist boxes give an exact cyclic transfer formula for EVERY odd number of odd pretzel boxes. It keeps one unwrapped integer charge, the entire marked row and all original crossing-circle normal channels. It is not a replacement by a Jones polynomial, a TV state, or an AK integral.

The formula is a concrete source for the arbitrary-region augmented pretzel survival question. Its complete-color base does not become entrywise positive by a diagonal phase gauge, even after retaining the half twists. A positive reflected network or an original relative-cycle calculation still needs proof.

1. Diagram, signs and literal original source

Use N≥2,d=N−1,q=eπi/N,Q=q2,x=qdN\geq2,d=N-1,q=e^{\pi i/N},Q=q^2,x=q^d. Label 2s2s vertical heads from left to right. Box ii acts on heads (2i−1,2i)(2i-1,2i). At BOTH ends the planar matching is

(1,2s),(2,3),(4,5),…,(2s−2,2s−1). (1,2s),(2,3),(4,5),\ldots,(2s-2,2s-1). \tag{1}

The outer bottom turn is the original pa1,bsp_{a_1,b_s}; the other bottom turns are pbi,ai+1p_{b_i,a_{i+1}}. At the top the inner turns are ℓdi,ci+1\ell_{d_i,c_{i+1}}, and the outer turn is the sole original marked row mc1,dsm_{c_1,d_s}. All port orders here are part of the definition, including that of the nested outer turn. The original formulas are

puv=Nx−v𝟏u+v=d,ℓuv=xu/N𝟏u+v=d,muv=xv/N2. p_{uv}=N x^{-v}\mathbf1_{u+v=d},\quad \ell_{uv}=x^u/N\,\mathbf1_{u+v=d},\quad m_{uv}=x^v/N^2. \tag{2}

In particular mm has NO degree indicator before the contraction. For degree-preserving physical boxes BiB_i, define the RAW marked source 𝒜s,N(B1,…,Bs)\mathcal A_{s,N}(B_1,\ldots,B_s) by this tensor diagram. Its original peripheral/framing line χN\chi_N remains outside it.

For odd box integers nin_i, the usual odd pretzel diagram is (1) with Bi=R−niB_i=R^{-n_i}, with this explicit crossing convention. Choosing RniR^{n_i} instead gives the global mirror. This fixes the sign by the one-crossing boxes: rotating their original cup/cap diagrams and applying the two snake identities concatenates them into the literal central ss-crossing four-plat, with no curl. Thus the all-ni=1n_i=1 raw source agrees with the original four-plat σ2s\sigma_2^s. This is an equality of the specified tangle sources, not an identification inferred from a colored polynomial.

There is one component when ss is odd. Indeed each odd box has the swap permutation of its two endpoints. Following the two planar matchings advances the box index by two modulo ss. For odd ss this visits every box; for even ss there are two components. This proof is independent of the odd twist magnitudes and signs. We use only odd s≥3s\geq3, so the sole marked source is an admissible original one-component cut. Reduction, hyperbolicity and long filling are further topological hypotheses, not consequences of this source formula.

2. Exact unwrapped-charge transfer theorem

For −d≤h≤d-d\leq h\leq d put

Ih={max⁡(0,h),…,min⁡(d,d+h)},Dh=diag⁡a∈Ihx2a, I_h=\{\max(0,h),\ldots,\min(d,d+h)\},\quad D_h=\operatorname{diag}_{a\in I_h}x^{2a}, Mi(h)[a,a′]=x−2a+h(Bi)(a−h,d−a′+h);(a,d−a′),a,a′∈Ih. M_i^{(h)}[a,a'] =x^{-2a+h} (B_i)_{(a-h,\,d-a'+h);\,(a,\,d-a')}, \qquad a,a'\in I_h. \tag{3}

These are finite physical matrix entries; no cyclic index is allowed. Then

𝒜s,N(B1,…,Bs)=x(s−1)dN∑h=−ddTr⁡(DhM1(h)⋯Ms(h)). \boxed{\displaystyle \mathcal A_{s,N}(B_1,\ldots,B_s) =\frac{x^{(s-1)d}}N \sum_{h=-d}^d\operatorname{Tr} \bigl(D_hM_1^{(h)}\cdots M_s^{(h)}\bigr).} \tag{4}

The statement holds for arbitrary degree-preserving boxes on their specified physical pairs, not merely for one eigenchannel or for root-sampled continuations. In particular it includes every actual signed power of RR, every literal mixed-color circle, and their products.

Proof. Write the inputs of box ii as (ai,bi)(a_i,b_i), its outputs as (ci,di)(c_i,d_i). The bottom source forces bi=d−ai+1b_i=d-a_{i+1}, cyclically, where the exceptional outer source has the ordered coefficient pa1,bsp_{a_1,b_s}. The inner caps force di=d−ci+1d_i=d-c_{i+1}, i<si<s. The total input degree is sdsd; each BiB_i preserves its actual integer degree. Therefore the remaining outer pair has c1+ds=dc_1+d_s=d, including when evaluated against the FULL marked row (2). Only now may that forced equality be used to write ds=d−c1d_s=d-c_1.

Each box conservation equation is now

ai−ai+1=ci−ci+1. a_i-a_{i+1}=c_i-c_{i+1}.

Thus h=ai−cih=a_i-c_i is a common UNWRAPPED integer, and each aia_i lies exactly in IhI_h. No mod-NN aliases have been introduced or discarded. The total turn coefficient before using this conservation law is

1Nx(s−1)d+2a1−∑iai−∑ici=x(s−1)dNx2a1∏ix−2ai+h. \frac1N x^{(s-1)d+2a_1-\sum_i a_i-\sum_i c_i} =\frac{x^{(s-1)d}}N x^{2a_1} \prod_i x^{-2a_i+h}. \tag{5}

The N−1N^{-1} in (5) is exactly Ns/Ns+1N^s/N^{s+1} from the cups, ordinary caps and single marked cap. Summing the resulting cyclic product gives (4). This proves the literal source normalization; there is no new trace average of size NsN^{s} or 2N2N.

3. Simultaneous original full-twist filling

For ni=2pi+1n_i=2p_i+1, keep the half twist R−1R^{-1} and use the already proved ALL-INPUT circle kernel on R−2piR^{-2p_i}:

R−ni=κ−2pi∑j=1NβN,−2pi,jR−1Lj,∑j|βN,−2pi,j|≤2N(1+|pi|). R^{-n_i}=\kappa^{-2p_i}\sum_{j=1}^N \beta_{N,-2p_i,j}\,R^{-1}L_j, \qquad\sum_j|\beta_{N,-2p_i,j}|\leq2N(1+|p_i|). \tag{6}

Every LjL_j is the ordinary jj-dimensional mixed auxiliary circle with its original pivot, not an extra complete component identified by a quotient lemma. Define

𝒜s,N(j1,…,js)=𝒜s,N(R−1Lj1,…,R−1Ljs). \mathcal A_{s,N}(j_1,\ldots,j_s) =\mathcal A_{s,N}(R^{-1}L_{j_1},\ldots,R^{-1}L_{j_s}).

Equations (4) and (6) give the exact original pretzel scalar

KN=χN∏iκ−2pi∑j1,…,js(∏iβN,−2pi,ji)𝒜s,N(j1,…,js). K_N=\chi_N\prod_i\kappa^{-2p_i} \sum_{j_1,\ldots,j_s}\left(\prod_i\beta_{N,-2p_i,j_i}\right) \mathcal A_{s,N}(j_1,\ldots,j_s). \tag{7}

The χN\chi_N here is the original filled pretzel’s line. It is not replaced by a guessed normalization or framing of the augmented diagram. For each fixed ss the coefficient mass is at most ∏i2N(1+|pi|)\prod_i2N(1+|p_i|). The formula treats arbitrary odd ss, so it is not restricted to bounded braid width or one variable slope. Its large-NN constants may depend on the fixed diagram ss.

4. Exact complete-base channel formula and a positivity test

At complete circle colors every box is the SAME B*=R−1LNB_*=R^{-1}L_N. Hence (4) becomes a sum of weighted powers Tr⁡(Dh(M*(h))s)\operatorname{Tr}(D_h(M_*^{(h)})^s). The positive reflected-circle theorem gives a complete all-degree expansion of B*PB_*P. In a low total degree block TT, its coefficients are

B*P=∑jhT,j(κνj)−1vT,jvT,j*,hT,j>0, B_*P=\sum_jh_{T,j}(\kappa\nu_j)^{-1}v_{T,j}v_{T,j}^*, \quad h_{T,j}>0, \tag{8}

including the odd simple node and every even-level normal channel. Thus the transfer (3) retains the HALF-TWIST phase (κνj)−1(\kappa\nu_j)^{-1}; it is not the plain PSD matrix LNPL_NP. Its two physical ports can belong to different total degrees T=d+a−a′T=d+a-a'. A partial transpose or reshuffling of the PSD matrix is not assumed positive.

For an exact illustration, at N=3,h=0N=3,h=0 the literal transfer is

M*(0)=(92+332i−32−332i03212+932i−32+332i0−32−332i9). M_*^{(0)}= \begin{pmatrix} \tfrac92+\tfrac{3\sqrt3}2i&-\tfrac32-\tfrac{3\sqrt3}2i&0\\ 3&\tfrac{21}2+\tfrac{9\sqrt3}2i&-\tfrac32+\tfrac{3\sqrt3}2i\\ 0&-\tfrac32-\tfrac{3\sqrt3}2i&9 \end{pmatrix}. \tag{9}

Its diagonal entries do not have a common phase. No scalar and diagonal similarity can therefore make this matrix entrywise nonnegative or Hermitian positive. This is a failure of that specific Perron/diagonal-gauge shortcut, not an obstruction to a coherent reflected-source formula or geometric survival.

The complete raw base scalar at N=3,s=3N=3,s=3, including ALL charges in (4), is exactly

𝒜3,3(3,3,3)=−189+813i. \mathcal A_{3,3}(3,3,3)=-189+81\sqrt3\,i. \tag{10}

Its nonzero value is an exact source check. It is not an all-level nonvanishing theorem, an asymptotic volume calculation, or a proof that its geometric coefficient is nonzero. Establishing an original positive reflected base or a selected relative cycle in an open cusp-color corridor remains the geometric step.
