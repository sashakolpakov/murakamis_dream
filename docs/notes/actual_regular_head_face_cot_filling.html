Exact two-neighbor filling of regular physical head faces

28 October 2026. Grouping the TWO actual crossings neighboring a physical head before estimating its normal contour gives a source-faithful outward filling at every regular physical head endpoint. This uses the complete original scalar state, not a discarded tail of a low-band Hardy kernel, a new boundary state, or an independent output projection.

The theorem is uniform for every fixed signed braid and bridge count. Its scope is a head face whose other head colors AND all transfers remain in a fixed bulk chamber. Simultaneous head/transfer endpoint clusters are excluded. The estimate does not by itself give the sharp whole-knot volume bound or geometric noncancellation.

1. Exact signed scalar and the head-factor composition identity

Retain N>=2, d=N-1, Q=exp(2pii/N), x=-exp(-pi*i/N), the original kappa, all pivots and the original external chi_N. Let Fhat_+ be the actual factorial interpolant and Fhat_- its conjugate-root partner. Their exact relation is

Fhat_-(s)=g_N(s) Fhat_+(s),
g_N(s)=exp[i*pi*s-i*pi*s*(s+1)/N].               (1)

Indeed both factorials have the SAME real meromorphic sine-factorial part in their independent Barnes definition; only their displayed quadratic chirps differ. This proves (1) as a whole meromorphic identity, including every normal coefficient. It is not just equality at integer states. In particular g_N(n)=(-1)^n Q^(-n(n+1)/2).

For positive transfer k, the actual signed checked coefficients are

r_+(a,b;k)=kappa x^(-a-b+k) Q^(b*(a-k))
                Fhat_+(b+k)Fhat_+(a)
                 /[Fhat_+(k)Fhat_+(b)Fhat_+(a-k)],

r_-(a,b;k)=kappa^(-1) x^(a+b-k) Q^(-a*(b-k))
                Fhat_-(a+k)Fhat_-(b)
                 /[Fhat_-(k)Fhat_-(a)Fhat_-(b-k)]. (2)

Their unwrapped outputs are respectively (b+k,a-k) and (b-k,a+k). The physical OVER strand always loses k, and the physical UNDER strand always gains k; the sign determines which tensor slot has that role.

Replace each Fhat_- in (2) using (1), retaining its entire factor g_N. At EITHER crossing sign the head incidences are now exactly

OVER input: +1, OVER output: -1,
UNDER input: -1, UNDER output: +1,              (3)

and its transfer has exponent -1. Thus, on any closed physical knot, the complete ordered scalar product has the exact meromorphic form

A_N(u)=E_N(u) product_(physical arcs e) Fhat_+(h_e(u))^(c_e)
                     product_j Fhat_+(k_j)^(-1), (4)

where

c_e=+2 for UNDER output -> OVER input,
c_e=-2 for OVER output -> UNDER input,
c_e= 0 for a continued OVER or UNDER role.       (5)

All original kappa, half-Cartan, g_N conversion chirps and closure pivots are retained in the specified nonzero entire exponential E_N; the original external chi_N/N stays outside unchanged. The logarithm of E_N is an explicit quadratic polynomial with quadratic coefficients O_word(1/N) and linear coefficients O_word(1); its external state-independent factor is not normalized away. Formula (4) is an EXACT two-neighbor composition identity. If different physical arcs have the same state linear form, their factors are multiplied, not declared independent.

2. Which endpoint can occur with nonzero adjacent transfers?

Consider a physical arc of color h. At h=0 with both adjacent transfers strictly positive, its previous crossing cannot have UNDER output: the UNDER input h-k would be negative. Its next crossing cannot have OVER input: the OVER output h-k would be negative. Therefore this is necessarily an OVER-output -> UNDER-input local MINIMUM and c_e=-2.

At h=d with both adjacent transfers strictly positive, its previous crossing cannot have OVER output (its input would be d+k), and its next crossing cannot have UNDER input (its output would be d+k). It is a local MAXIMUM, UNDER output -> OVER input, and c_e=+2.

These statements use the actual nonnegative transfer convention at both crossing signs. They are physical state inequalities, not a positivity claim about crossing coefficients. If one of the adjacent transfers is zero, this deduction is unavailable and that joint corner is deliberately retained.

3. The exact whole source and its normal cotangent contour

Use the cardinal completion of the COMPLETE original averaged marked source, before splitting any head bands. For a one-component diagram its coordinates are exactly u=(t,k_1,…,k_m), and every arc h=t+sum_j epsilon_j k_j has epsilon_j in {0,+1,-1}. Hence

(t,k) -> (h,k), determinant 1,                  (6)

is an INTEGRAL bijection. All original valid states have 0<=h,k_j<=d. Choose a fixed O(N) complex box in these coordinates, and sufficient fixed cardinal powers at each original factorial occurrence. The completed scalar is holomorphic throughout the box, equals the original scalar on valid integer states, and vanishes at invalid integer states, including simultaneous scalar poles.

Consequently the ORIGINAL scalar is exactly chi_N/N times the product cotangent residue integral of that completed scalar in (h,k). Every picot(pih) and picot(pik_j) factor is retained. This assertion follows by the finite residue sum, not by dropping a transformed source factor: a pointwise pullback of the old cotangent in t would instead be picot(pi(h-sum epsilon_j k_j)). Taking the k residues first gives integer k and its exact periodic equality with picot(pih). Thus the new source representation and the old one have the SAME complete integer residues, with no Jacobian, grid factor, or omitted side current.

For a regular lower head face the original left normal edge Re h=-1/2 can now move to Re h=-M-1/2. All negative integer cotangent poles have zero residue because the completed scalar vanishes there. The two finite-height horizontal connecting pieces are retained in this closed rectangle identity. At an upper face move the right edge from Re h=d+1/2 to Re h=d+M+1/2, retaining the same connecting pieces and all cotangent factors. No physical integer state is crossed or deleted.

The tangent variables are fixed, not replaced by independent off-grid outputs. Their actual unwrapped arc forms shift with h according to (6). The bounds below hold uniformly while every other head and every transfer stays in its stated bulk chamber.

4. Uniform normal estimate, including all original phases

Fix an imaginary-height bound T and a compact marked BULK chamber for the remaining factorial arguments. For a literal fixed-height source contour one may require their scaled real parts in [eta,1-eta], with bounded scaled imaginary parts and consistent factorial branches. Let B_N be the unwindowed original scalar at the endpoint h=0 or d, with that head factorial replaced by its actual finite value. All other factorials, pivots and kappa remain in B_N; the same original external chi_N/N multiplies both sides of every estimate.

There are constants C,B,delta>0 depending only on the diagram and this chamber such that, for 1<=M<=delta*N, the WHOLE windowed scalar on the outward normal edge satisfies

|Atilde_N| <= N^B exp(C(M+1))
              [C(M+1)/N]^(2M) |B_N|.          (7)

The same estimate with the appropriate integer floor of the normal distance holds on the horizontal connecting pieces. The powers of N absorb the finite number of fixed cardinal windows and the bounded normal factorial base. On a macroscopic nonreal tangent chamber one may expand the BULK windows by their exact finite-mode packet and apply (7) to each specified bulk mode with its original integer-marked N-order phase retained: its right-hand B_N is then the MODE-WEIGHTED bulk amplitude, including that exponential, rather than the literal unwindowed B_N used on the fixed-height contour. The HEAD windows stay whole: their separated modes have poles at outside-band integers and their matched currents are essential to the same contour identity. No wrong-side bulk-window exponential or normal cancellation current is erased.

Here is a direct uniform proof. At a lower face write h=nu-M with nu in the fixed strip -1<=Re nu<=0, |Im nu|<=T. The exact factorial step gives

1/Fhat_+(h)=product_(r=0)^(M-1)(1-Q^(nu-r)) / Fhat_+(nu). (8)

The reciprocal base on that compact strip is O_T(N^C): shift once to 0<=Re(nu+1)<=1, where the bounded-order Gamma expansion gives the usual polynomial base, and retain 1-Q^(nu+1). Moreover

|1-Q^(nu-r)| <= C_T(r+1)/N

for M<=delta*N. The gathered head factor is its SQUARE by (5). At the upper face write h=d+nu+M with 0<=Re nu<=1. Exactly,

Fhat_+(h)=Fhat_+(d+nu)
                  product_(r=1)^M(1-Q^(nu+r-1)),
Fhat_+(d+nu)=N exp[i*pi*nu*(nu+d)/N]/Fhat_+(-nu). (9)

The second identity is the actual factorial reflection, including its chirp. It proves a polynomial upper base through both endpoints; the first proves the same small-factor product as (8).

For every OTHER bulk factorial, a normal displacement of size M+1 has relative modulus at most exp(C(M+1)). This follows either from the uniform complex factorial symbol with bounded logarithmic derivative on the marked compact chamber, or by the exact unit step followed by the bounded fractional Gamma base. The logarithmic derivative of E_N in (4) is O_word(1) on the fixed O(N) box, so ALL Cartan, conversion, pivot and Gaussian phases cost exp(C(M+1)) too. Finally the exact cardinal bound is sqrt(N)exp(2pi|Im ell|). On a fixed-height joint contour every Im ell is bounded by a diagram constant, independent of N and M. This proves (7), also at coincident negative integer poles, since all completed factors are holomorphic.

For M=N^alpha, 0<alpha<1, the entire collar has exp(o(N)) cost, and its far edge relative to B_N is at most

exp[-2(1-alpha)N^alpha log N+O(N^alpha+log N)]. (10)

One may also take M=floor(deltaN) with a sufficiently small fixed delta. Then its far edge has a strict exponential drop exp[-cdeltaN*log(1/delta)] relative to the endpoint bulk amplitude. The whole intervening collar has only polynomial normal overhead: choose delta so C+2log(Cdelta)<0 in (7), and treat bounded M with the polynomial base. This is a NORMAL estimate; it does not bound the unrestricted tangent exterior amplitude B_N by volume.

5. What has and has not been removed

The zero-side direction comes from composing the two original neighboring scalar factors FIRST. It is absent from an isolated low-head kernel K_R, whose Hardy residues cancel at its apparent matching pole. No such pole residue was asserted here. The whole head bands remain together in the original finite source, and the outer cotangent edge is filled with its exact scalar continuation.

This removes a regular head endpoint as a compulsory scalar POLE exit. It supplies a quantitative outward contour with the original normalization and unwrapped routing. It does not prove that the remaining whole cycle reaches the geometric thimble, or that every simultaneous endpoint cluster has this direction. In particular a head endpoint next to a zero transfer, a repeated head face, and escaping tangent bulk coordinates require their own compensated whole-source treatment. The full global growth estimate remains open.

Audit: literal signed head incidence, original statewise composition, exact negative-root conversion and both normal small-factor identities.
