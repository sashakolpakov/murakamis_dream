The full singleton clock phase and its algebraic stationary equations

Written proof, 14 November 2026. This note gives the complete quadratic phase of the original charged source when every crossing is retained as one event. It includes all understrand histories, all closure degrees, and the original scalar normalization. The clock variables can then be eliminated exactly by the finite binomial theorem, or rationally from the regular classical stationary equations. Its reduced primitive agrees exactly with the physical crossing action on a specified analytic branch. The exact phase derivatives have a local polar-angle interpretation with explicit history terms.

The result is an explicit refinement of the existing integer feedback operator, not a positivity or selected-period theorem. In particular, the exact clock elimination returns the original physical crossing network, with its phase. No knot-complement face pairing is assumed.
