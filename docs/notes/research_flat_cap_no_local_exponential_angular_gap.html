A flat cap has no uniform local exponential angular mismatch bound

31 October 2026. Internally reviewed exact source observation. Root and geometry completed full independent analytical and original-source scope reviews, PASS. This is a limitation of one local phase estimate, not a lower bound for the original shared source or its filling period.

The actual cap character is aligned only when the cc and yy phases differ by π\pi. The retained alternating terms leave endpoint currents of at least polynomial size; there is no strict local stop-action gap at the cap.

Therefore the regular-window angular theorem cannot be extended to a flat cap merely by assigning it the same opposite-sign incidence labels. An odd MAIN cycle involving such a cap does not by itself supply the product-envelope exponential loss used by the regular cycle-packing argument. This does not exclude cancellation after original shared coefficient extraction, a combined analytic block estimate with other ports, or a different source-preserving treatment of the cap boundaries.
