Two boundary rows retain the full coideal period

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof.

The full actual coideal crossing has a two-row boundary presentation with its original forcing, both sorted folds and literal observation. The reconstruction is polynomial, even at every Dirichlet pole. At physical neighboring heights each raw boundary evaluation needs at most four spectral derivatives; a raw full-period evaluation needs at most eight. A three-row variant isolates the controlled positive cooriented interiors without making the whole boundary operator positive. These are initialized source identities, not an arbitrary Schur model.

The matrices and their polynomial degrees still grow with the level. A fixed number of boundary rows does not give a fixed-dimensional source, close a whole-word displacement rank, or bound its marked scalar below.

3. Two boundary rows and exact polynomial reconstruction

4. Every pole has a uniformly finite local evaluation

Removing 0 and d removes both folded edges. The remaining matrix is the direct sum of two ordinary parity paths. Detailed balance gives μtct+=μt+2ct+2−, \mu_tc_t^+=\mu_{t+2}c_{t+2}^-, so diag⁡(μ)LIIdiag⁡(μ)−1\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt\mu)L_{II} \operatorname{diag}(\sqrt\mu)^{-1} is real symmetric. Negative or zero off-diagonal coefficients are retained. Its only possible zero edges are at t=N−v−2t=N-v-2 and t=N−s−2t=N-s-2, when these are interior edges. Consequently there are at most four irreducible Jacobi components. Each has simple real spectrum: an eigenvector is determined by its first coordinate, which cannot vanish, and the symmetric matrix is diagonalizable. Thus every root of D is real with multiplicity m≤4m\le4.

At such a root η\eta, polynomial division gives componentwise vB(η)=NB(m)(η)D(m)(η),vI(η)=NI(2m)(η)(D2)(2m)(η).(11) v_B(\eta)={\frac{N_B^{(m)}(\eta)}{ D^{(m)}(\eta)}},\qquad v_I(\eta)={\frac{N_I^{(2m)}(\eta)}{(D^2)^{(2m)}(\eta)}}. \qquad\text{(11)} These formulas require raw jets of order at most four and eight. Sequential exact division also works, but does not justify a raw full-state bound of four. At N=2 there is no D-pole. These are auxiliary ζ\zeta-jets, not replacements for original Casimir/Hermite normals or moving-color derivatives.

5. Three rows expose the controlled positive interiors

6. What is retained in a composition

The result gives the actual full crossing and prescribed observation from small boundary matrices, with initialized interior forcing and exact finite parts. More generally let η\eta be any prescribed row independent of ζ\zeta, including η=ℓA\eta=\ell A for a fixed actual short-component composition AA of compatible size. Equations (10),(11),(14) compute its original period with the same raw jet bounds. The full AA, complementary channels and external factors remain in η\eta; no bound for η\eta or its period is inferred.

The polynomial entries, Dirichlet spectra and path sizes still depend on N and the actual neighboring heights. The real-spectrum and multiplicity bounds require those physical root parameters; the algebraic frame continuation in P does not extend them to complex heights. The positive interior response is not a common positive braid metric. A seam must transport source, boundary data, complementary channels and marked row. No constant boundary-rank closure for overlapping faces, phase alignment, global period lower bound or finite-exception theorem is asserted. Full Casimir-normal data remain in the invertible source maps.

P supplies the initialized source and marked unit residue; N supplies the actual sparse difference equation; G supplies its cooriented signs; J supplies the common positive Jacobi response. The new content is the full folded boundary presentation, source-preserving polynomial reconstruction, and uniform local auxiliary pole order. No novelty for abstract Schur complements or Jacobi spectral theory is claimed.
