The divided adjoint pairs nontrivially with the actual missing edge

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The genuine complete integral Weyl color gives a divided Hopf-adjoint operator on its matrix space. Applied to the literal missing cyclic edge, it has an exact nonzero pairing with the prescribed marked cut. This yields a cohomologous divided-normal realization of the original knot cut functional. It does not prove survival of the geometric Gaussian period.

1. The two normal directions are distinct

Keep Q=q^2, x=-M/q. In the balanced basis the generic truncated raising entries are

Ecal_(a-1,a)=M Q^(-a)-M^(-1),       1<=a<N.

The absent cyclic entry is the specified coefficient

w(q,M)=M Q^(-N)-M^(-1),
W=E_(N-1,0).

It vanishes identically on the genuine integral Weyl color M=-q^N. At the fixed root Q^N=1 it becomes w=kappa=M-M^(-1). These two restrictions are different normal directions. If delta=1-Q^N, their exact relation is

w=kappa+M delta/(1-delta).

At the complete intersection M=1,delta=0, one has dw=d kappa+d delta; the Weyl curve is w=0, not kappa=0. No tensor divided power below is identified with the generic-M cyclic-edge normal by replacing one parameter with the other.

2. The actual divided Hopf adjoint

Take the genuine Weyl family V(q)=D_(N-1)(q) on M=-q^N, as in the factorial fusion proof. For a matrix T its actual generic Hopf adjoint is

A(T)=ad_Ecal(T)=[Ecal,T]K^(-1).

Write A=X+Y, with

X(T)=Ecal T K^(-1), Y(T)=-T Ecal K^(-1).

Then XY=Q YX and X^N=Y^N=0 for every generic q. Hence the same q-binomial cancellation as for the tensor divided power defines a regular actual operator on the root matrix space:

J_ad=[A^N/(1-Q^N)]_(q=q_N)
    =sum_(j=1)^(N-1) c_j Y^(N-j)X^j,
c_j=N/[(Q;Q)_j(Q;Q)_(N-j)].                  (1)

This is the divided Hopf adjoint. It need not equal ordinary commutation by the divided power on V; that latter power is zero on the individual color. Formula (1) retains the mixed antipode terms acting through V tensor V*.

3. Exact action on the missing edge

At the actual root set

epsilon_a=Q^(-a)-1, P_a=product_(s=1)^a epsilon_s,
P_(N-1)=(-1)^(N-1)N,
K^N=(-1)^(N-1)I.

Let l=N-j. The j-th term of (1) applied to W=E_(N-1,0) is supported only at matrix entry (l-1,l). Its coefficient is

c_j (-1)^l Q^(-l(l-1)/2-jl)
                   P_(N-1) epsilon_l K^(-N).

Using

c_j=c_l=(-1)^(l-1)Q^(-l(l-1)/2)/(1-Q^l),
epsilon_l=(1-Q^l)Q^(-l),

and Q^N=1, every coefficient simplifies to -N. Therefore

J_ad(W)=-N sum_(l=1)^(N-1) E_(l-1,l).          (2)

This holds for every N>=2, with the original balanced root basis. For the literal marked source and co-source,

e_(a,b)=1/N, Phi(T)=Tr(eT)=Tr(g D T),

equation (2) gives the exact nonzero pairing

Phi(J_ad(W))=-(N-1).                          (3)

The normalized ordinary trace of (2) is zero. Thus the marked normal pairing is visible before the ordinary scalar Ward quotient. Its degrees are explicit: W raises matrix index by N-1, J_ad lowers it by N, and the marked cut supplies the remaining one-step pairing. This is a mixed divided-normal/missing-edge cochain calculation, not a nonzero closed geometric class.

4. An actual cohomologous divided-normal cut

Define the literal all-matrices row

Phi_div(T)=-Phi(J_ad(T W))/(N-1).              (4)

Equation (3) says Phi_div(I)=1. Let delta_W(T)=([K,T],[F,T]) and let h_W be the explicit actual Ward contraction in the finite Ward note, so

h_W delta_W=I-i p,
p(T)=Tr(T)/N, i(c)=c I.

Since (Phi_div-Phi)i=0, one obtains the exact cochain homotopy on all matrices,

Phi_div-Phi=(Phi_div-Phi)h_W delta_W.          (5)

In particular every actual closed cut tangle is Ward closed, hence equals F_N(1) I. Equations (4)–(5) give

Phi_div(C_K)=Phi(C_K)=F_N(1)=Kashaev_N(K).     (6)

Thus (4) is an explicit divided-normal source realization of the original invariant, with its source and normalization fixed. It is not a new invariant or a redefinition of its numerical values. The full ADO value retains the entire external marked line; at completion it multiplies (6) by i^(N+3)/N. That scalar is not absorbed into (4).

5. Pivotal partial trace commutes with the genuine divided adjoint

For the genuine generic integral colors use the generic pivotal partial trace

ptr_K(T)=Tr_closed[(I tensor K_closed)T].

The generic Hopf adjoint and this trace commute on every matrix. This follows from the ordinary antipode cancellation; equivalently the generic pivotal cap is a Hopf map. Apply the generic identity to the N-th adjoint power and divide both sides by 1-Q^N. The divided operators are regular by the q-binomial argument on the corresponding tensor-and-dual colors. Specialization gives

ptr_K J_ad,total=J_ad,cut ptr_K.               (7)

At the root, the actual pivot satisfies g_closed=K_closed^(1-N)=chi_closed^(-1)K_closed, where the entire scalar chi_closed=K_closed^N is retained. Thus (7) holds also for the original actual pivotal partial trace ptr_g.

The order of the marked edge matters. For an arbitrary full tensor matrix T, the cut edge lifts as W_cut tensor I_closed, so the exact all-matrices identity is

Phi_div(ptr_g T)
  =-Phi(ptr_g[J_ad,total(T(W_cut tensor I))])/(N-1). (8)

Here ptr_g(T(W_cut tensor I))=(ptr_g T)W_cut was used. There is no license to replace the argument of J_ad,total by T and move the edge unchanged outside it. The mixed Hopf-adjoint terms are essential. Equations (7)–(8) transport the new source through the actual contraction while keeping its insertion order.

6. What this proves about cohomology and HPL

The action J_ad lowers matrix weight by N, so its square is zero on End(V_N). It annihilates I and has zero normalized ordinary trace on every matrix. It consequently induces a chain map on the split image Ward complex: set

J_ad^1(delta_W T)=delta_W(J_ad T).

This is well defined because the kernel of delta_W is k I and J_ad(I)=0. Its action on scalar Ward cohomology is zero. Likewise the marked row Phi J_ad is Ward exact, by composing the old cut homotopy with this chain map. This is consistent with the nonzero cochain pairing (3): the missing edge is not Ward closed.

The normalized row (4), in contrast, has the same nonzero cohomology class as the original cut, by (5). It is now represented using a genuine divided adjoint and the literal missing edge. This gives concrete source data for a future source-preserving Gaussian chain comparison. It does not construct that comparison or show which geometric formal class has nonzero image. Nilpotence of an inserted operator and exact scalar Ward normalization alone do not supply the missing HPL map or an asymptotic volume theorem.

Exact audit and results check (2)–(5) on every matrix unit at seven levels and verify the all-matrices pivotal and ordered-insertion identities (7)–(8).
