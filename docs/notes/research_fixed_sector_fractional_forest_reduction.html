Fractional one-sided sectors: the forest and the missing reduction

31 October 2026. Open research interface. The structural lemmas below concern the convex weighted comparison for one fixed actual LEFT/CROSS/RIGHT pattern. They do not prove its universal capacity bound. The comparison perspective is distinct from the original Green coefficient entropy.

The finite-sector cover theorem explains the desired implication for the original invariant. The certificate separation shows why unrestricted spectator mixtures cannot be assumed harmless.

1. Exact network and perspective

Use the original simple-white plat, ordinary demands one and protected demands zero. At a LEFT port put p=b,o=cp=b,o=c; at a RIGHT port put p=c,o=bp=c,o=b. The two outer faces are x,yx,y. Its exact comparison flow is

qo=1−t2,(qx,qp,qy)=tα,0≤t≤1,α≥0,∑α=12,henv=tC(α). q_o=1-\frac{t}{2},\qquad(q_x,q_p,q_y)=t\alpha,\qquad 0\le t\le1,\quad\alpha\ge0,\quad\sum\alpha=\frac{1}{2}, \qquad h_{\mathrm{env}}=tC(\alpha).\tag{1}

Here CC is the original half-degree triangle entropy. CROSS retains its two separate half-supply triangle rows. Equation (1) is equivalently a supply-one node on the four original faces whose arc to oo has lower bound 1/21/2. Thus the fixed-sector feasible polytope is an ordinary bounded bipartite flow polytope, with protected arcs removed.

Scaling by two makes its supplies, demands and lower bounds integral. The incidence matrix is totally unimodular, so all vertices are half-integral before scaling. Every one-sided vertex has t=0t=0 or t=1t=1. In particular the polytope is the convex hull of the feasible pure-atom patterns. This is a statement about flow vertices, not maximizers of a concave entropy. Concavity has the wrong direction for an atomwise maximization argument.

2. The strict-overload middle graph is an actual rooted forest

Select the ports with t<1t<1, orient their original middle edges p→op\to o, and retain their endpoints. Each selected child has qo>1/2q_o>1/2. Two such edges therefore cannot enter one ordinary face, and none can enter a protected face.

In the literal gap presentation, middle edges in an odd gap form a path. Those in an even gap form a cycle based at the protected exterior uu. Different gap paths meet only at uu. An undirected selected cycle would be one of these exterior cycles. Since its edges each have one head and every indegree is at most one, every cycle vertex would have indegree one, including uu, a contradiction. Hence every selected component is a tree.

Its indegrees sum to one less than its vertex count, so it has exactly one root and all arrows point away from that root. If it contains a protected face, that face is the root. Gap parity makes a component monochromatic, so it contains at most one protected face and its outer x,yx,y faces lie outside the component.

For a component TT with κ∈{0,1}\kappa\in\{0,1\} protected roots, define

ℓe=te(αx,e+αy,e). \ell_e=t_e(\alpha_{x,e}+\alpha_{y,e}).

The selected edges contribute |E(T)|−∑eℓe|E(T)|-\sum_e\ell_e to its vertices. Their total demand is |V(T)|−κ|V(T)|-\kappa. Therefore all remaining original rows contribute exactly

1−κ+∑e∈E(T)ℓe. 1-\kappa+\sum_{e\in E(T)}\ell_e.\tag{2}

A pure identity contraction would leave target 1−κ1-\kappa. The extra term in (2) is the outer-load compensation required by a fractional leaf elimination. It cannot be omitted or absorbed into a protected zero target.

3. Finite positive KKT equations

Assume a relative-interior maximizer has finite positive ordinary dual weights LfL_f. Protected coordinates remain zero. Each active retained triangle is the unique maximizer α(L)\alpha(L) in the original triangle Fenchel formula. A genuinely fractional row satisfies

Q(Lx,Lp,Ly)=12log⁡Lo,0<t<1. Q(L_x,L_p,L_y)=\frac{1}{2}\log L_o, \qquad 0<t<1.\tag{3}

The triangle entropy inequality then gives

Lo=exp⁡(2Q(Lx,Lp,Ly))≥Lx+Lp+Ly. L_o=\exp\bigl(2Q(L_x,L_p,L_y)\bigr) \ge L_x+L_p+L_y.\tag{4}

This is strict when at least two input weights are positive. Thus every nonzero input curvature is strictly below the overloaded curvature, except for a monomial retained triangle. Such a triangle has one positive input face ff, zero entropy and Lo=LfL_o=L_f. It can occur only in an original triangle containing both protected vertices.

The endpoint inequalities have different directions:

t=0:Lo≥exp⁡(2Qinput),t=1:Lo≤exp⁡(2Qinput). \begin{array}{ll} t=0:&L_o\ge\exp(2Q_{\mathrm{input}}),\\ t=1:&L_o\le\exp(2Q_{\mathrm{input}}). \end{array}\tag{5}

Consequently a maximal-curvature fractional overload cannot be an input of another nonmonomial fractional row. It can nevertheless meet CROSS rows and terminal t=1t=1 fixed-triangle rows: (5) does not give an ascending-curvature conclusion for those terminal rows. Pure t=0t=0 rows carry no retained-triangle load. A claim that such a sink has only CROSS neighbors needs an additional argument.

4. The exact condition for lossless elimination

Strict concavity of CC gives an equality criterion for its perspective. Between two states of positive mass, equality in concavity of tC(α)tC(\alpha) holds precisely when their normalized α\alpha’s agree. For CROSS, both triangle allocations must agree. Along an affine path in the mass and weighted-allocation variables, its second variation is

d2[tC(α)]=t(dα)TD2C(α)(dα). \mathrm d^2[tC(\alpha)] =t\,(\mathrm d\alpha)^T D^2C(\alpha)(\mathrm d\alpha).\tag{6}

It is strictly negative when an allowed normalized allocation changes. A monomial allowed triangle has no normalized variable. The same equality criterion extends to a mass-zero endpoint by continuity.

At a constrained maximizer, another feasible point of equal or greater entropy must therefore keep every positive-mass normalized triangle allocation fixed along their connecting segment. If all endpoint masses t=0,1t=0,1 are also kept fixed, the changes he=dteh_e=\mathrm dt_e at fractional rows must solve the exact original face system

∑eheDe=0,De=αx,eex+αp,eep+αy,eey−12eo. \sum_e h_eD_e=0,\qquad D_e=\alpha_{x,e}e_x+\alpha_{p,e}e_p+\alpha_{y,e}e_y -\frac{1}{2}e_o.\tag{7}

Thus an angle-changing CROSS compensation incurs a strict entropy cost at this maximizer. This does not compare the maximizer’s value with ΦD\Phi_D; it identifies the condition a lossless same-sector argument would have to satisfy.

The columns in (7) are usually independent. Their overloaded children are distinct. Restrict to those face rows and order by LoL_o. A nonmonomial fractional column has diagonal entry −1/2-1/2, while every off-diagonal positive entry lies at a strictly smaller curvature. Without monomial dependency cycles, triangular elimination gives full column rank.

More generally the kernel can only come from directed cycles of monomial fractional rows with equal curvature. Indeed, interpret 2α2\alpha as an input probability vector and reverse the arrows from child to inputs. Each nonmonomial step strictly decreases curvature; a monomial step preserves it and has a single successor. A recurrent class of the restricted substochastic matrix is therefore a deterministic equal-curvature cycle. Equivalently, eliminate the strict curvature blocks; the remaining equal-level blocks are deterministic partial transition matrices: several columns may enter one face. Pruning their transient trees leaves exactly the recurrent directed cycles. A cycle has zero entropy and allows a constant mass circulation until one mass reaches zero or one. After these harmless cycles are removed, (7) is injective.

The rooted middle forest and KKT order therefore do not, by themselves, provide a lossless leaf reduction. Half-integrality of the flow vertices does not repair this issue.

5. The remaining sufficient lemma

For a fixed sector write PsP_s for its original incidence-flow polytope and HsH_s for the sum of its perspectives and triangle entropies. The universal comparison sought is

maxq∈PsHs(q)<ΦDfor every nonempty one-sided pattern s. \max_{q\in P_s}H_s(q)<\Phi_D \quad\text{for every nonempty one-sided pattern }s.\tag{8}

The existing pure-pattern, identity and fixed-triangle boundary theorems control atom patterns. The original endpoint Green theorem does not control the intervening perspective pointwise.

A sufficient new step would map a fractional maximizer into the original complete or source-permitted smoothed flow spaces while comparing total entropy and preserving the original face demands. Equation (2) requires transport between actual opposite-parity outer components. Equations (6)–(7) show why this cannot simply be declared a feasible lossless motion inside the same strict sector. A direct quantitative upper inequality could also suffice, provided it retains the fractional masses and shared targets.

The network, forest and leakage statements hold on the whole closed polytope. The KKT and curvature claims assume the stated finite positive multipliers; their existence is not asserted. This research note proves no new group representation, relative fundamental class, universal sector bound, or original-invariant asymptotic.

Scope

Deterministic transition blocks need not be permutation blocks. Transient monomial trees are pruned before recurrent cycles are identified. The reduction does not establish inequality (8).
