A relative asymptotic for the original undamped reciprocal source

24 November 2026. Research proof. Earlier finals and manuscripts are unchanged.

The original physical reciprocal integral has relative error tending to zero, without damping its closing row or changing its budgets. The additional input is specific to the original source: either closing after a product has bilinear norm at most 1/N1/N. This bounds every nonlinear residual coefficient more strongly than the generic bilinear budget does. Fixed leaf degree then controls its Gaussian moments and the full reciprocal integral.

The antichain expansion and its orthogonality were already available in [O]. The reciprocal construction and the shrinking physical closing row were established in [U]. The result below combines these with the source-specific coefficient estimate, not a geometric evaluation of the integral or a lower bound for the knot scalar.

2. The two physical closing forms after a product

The original rows of [P,H] satisfy λ0(AB)=λ0(A)λ0(B),∥λ0∥*≤(2N)−1/2,ϕ(AB)=1NTr⁡[(I⊗g)AB],(7) \lambda_0(AB)=\lambda_0(A)\lambda_0(B),\qquad \|\lambda_0\|_*\le(2N)^{-1/2},\qquad \phi(AB)=\frac1N\operatorname{Tr}[(I\otimes g)AB], \qquad\text{(7)} where gg is the actual unitary pivot. The first identity is evaluation at the physical Casimir node q+q−1q+q^{-1}, on the full algebra, including its derivative directions. The row bound is proved in [U, Section 2] from the actual pair characteristic polynomial and multiplicity 2N2N. The trace identity is [H, (11)]. Matrix Cauchy–Schwarz therefore gives, for either λ∈{λ0,ϕ}\lambda\in\{\lambda_0,\phi\}, |λ(AB)|≤1N∥A∥ph∥B∥ph.(8) |\lambda(AB)| \le \frac1N\|A\|_{\mathrm{ ph}}\|B\|_{\mathrm{ ph}}. \qquad\text{(8)} For λ0\lambda_0, (7) in fact gives the constant 1/(2N)1/(2N). The genuine turn obeys λ0𝒯=ϕ\lambda_0\mathcal T=\phi, ϕ𝒯=λ0\phi\mathcal T=\lambda_0. Consequently any top unary corridor carries the original row to a scalar of modulus at most one times one of these two rows. Its residual terms are linear.

Thus the first binary gate below that corridor, if one exists, is observed through the bilinear form (8), with all its normalized scalar factors having modulus at most one. This is stronger than estimating multiplication and the closing separately.

3. Complete antichain coefficients and their physical saving

An antichain AA is a set of proper vertices with no member an ancestor of another. The exact recursion (2) gives f(r)=k+∑A≠⌀,AantichainCA((rv)v∈A).(9) f(r)=k+\sum_{A\ne\varnothing,\ A\ {\mathrm{ antichain}}} C_A((r_v)_{v\in A}) . \qquad\text{(9)} Here CAC_A is complex multilinear in its |A||A| packet inputs; the signs from the terms −rv-r_v are included. To obtain it, replace the subtrees at AA by their free inputs and evaluate every other branch with the actual normalized maps and prescribed constants. This is the antichain expansion already used in [O, (20)]. A choice of a residual stops its subtree, so an admissible set contributes once. There is no repeated occurrence or hidden combinatorial multiplicity. Distinct literal child subtrees have disjoint variables. An antichain has at most LL members.

For every nonlinear term, with a=|A|≥2a=|A|\ge2, we claim ∥CA∥HS2≤Na−3.(10) \|C_A\|_{\mathrm{ HS}}^2\le N^{a-3}. \qquad\text{(10)} All coefficient norms here use physical orthonormal packet bases. After fixing some inputs of an antichain coefficient to unit basis vectors, each remaining subtree linear slice has norm at most one. This follows inductively from the normalized product and turn bounds, and from the forward constants of norm at most 1/161/16.

Consider the first binary gate below the top unary corridor. Every nonlinear antichain lies below that gate. If it meets both children, fix all but one free input in each child. The resulting scalar bilinear form is (8) composed with two linear contractions. Its operator norm is at most 1/N1/N. An NN-by-NN coefficient matrix with that operator norm has squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm at most 1/N1/N. Summing over the Na−2N^{a-2} fixed input choices proves (10).

If the antichain lies in one child only, the other child supplies its true forward constant, of norm at most one. Formula (8) then gives a row of norm at most 1/N1/N on the active child. Fix a−1a-1 inputs. The remaining row still has norm at most 1/N1/N. Summing its squared norm over Na−1N^{a-1} choices again gives (10). This uses the actual signed constants before any norm; it is not a positive replacement of the source. If there is no binary gate, ff is affine and p=0p=0.

4. Dimension-independent fixed Gaussian moments

6. Scope and complete inputs

The theorem uses fixed leaf degree through the antichain expansion and fixed Wick moments. The closing estimate (8) is essential: a generic scalar polynomial with small separate operator bounds does not supply the physical coefficient saving (10). Neither smallness of a real minimizer nor a Hessian determinant enters the argument.

This improves the universal constant-error undamped comparison of [U] to a relative error tending to zero for the unchanged quartic and budget. It does not evaluate 𝒫N\mathcal P_N, remove the signed source from its denominator, construct a geometric trial field, or prove eventual nonvanishing. The arbitrary-short- filling volume theorem and finite-exception assertion remain open. No novelty is claimed for Gaussian moments or Laplace integration.
