A relative finite Fourier transition for a vanishing prefix minor

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The complementary entry at a simple classical prefix zero has an explicit finite-source transition. A triangular tensor-prefix operator, a diagonal factorial gauge and an actual finite Fourier matrix turn its ordered quantum lift into a nonzero diagonal clock minus an explicit carry. The carry has rank Nm−1N^{m-1} on a prefix of mm heads. It cannot be dropped: the original complementary entry is nilpotent, whereas the diagonal clock is invertible.

The construction retains the original probes, finite projectors and all quantum/meridian normal terms. It also gives an exact partial inverse and a two-endpoint source-current identity. It is a relative prefix chart, not an invertible Bruhat factorization of the unchanged finite head or a calculation of its geometric period.

The complete directs are E, the actual finite head; M, the classical marked prefix correspondence; and V, the whole finite Fourier source. The tensor-prefix splitting and the identities below are proved inline.

5. Original source, projectors and a finite endpoint current

Insert 𝒜,𝒜−1\mathcal A,\mathcal A^{-1} on the two sides of the actual prefix interface. Every original matrix WW, source vector, covector, pivot and occupation projector transforms by that same map. For example W′=𝒜W𝒜−1W'=\mathcal A W\mathcal A^{-1} and P′=𝒜P𝒜−1P'=\mathcal A P\mathcal A^{-1} satisfy Tr⁡(P′W′)=Tr⁡(PW).(34) \operatorname{Tr}(P'W') =\operatorname{Tr}(PW). \qquad\text{(34)} This applies to the literal highest or soft cut and arbitrary marked defects. The SS-conjugated coupled masks in (34) must be retained before making any simplification like (35).
