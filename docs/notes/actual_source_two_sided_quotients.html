The actual multi-leg source has no nonzero two-sided null ideal

Research proof, 27 October 2026. For three or more strands, the original pivotal highest-input source on the FULL Borel-relative word algebra does not factor through any proper algebra quotient. Every path coefficient can be detected after actual relative matrix coupons. For two strands its maximal null ideal is instead explicit, and the minimal algebra quotient is the triangular path algebra of a line.

This is a boundary on algebraic reductions of the entire relative algebra, not an obstruction to a linear relative-period transform, a current-complex quotient, a singular geometric comparison, or a quotient of a smaller actual-word/observable algebra. In particular it does not disprove the volume conjecture. It specifies what a source-preserving attempt to discard the nilpotent shell would need to avoid.

1. The actual algebra and the relevant notion of source quotient

Fix an original root Q of order N>=2. Let

A_0=<H,f | H^N=1, Hf=Q^-1 fH, f^N=0>,
z_(a,j)=f^a e_j, 0<=a,j<N.

The exact multiplication is the one in the actual Borel fusion theorem. Think of z_(a,j) as an oriented path of length a starting at j and ending at j+a modulo N. A product whose total length is at least N is zero.

On b strands that theorem identifies the actual tensor module with a free LEFT A_0 module of rank r=N^(b-2). Consequently the full Borel-relative endomorphism algebra is

B_b=Mat_r(A_0^op).

The original source is the actual pivotal partial trace on all but the first strand, followed by first input e_0 and output ell=ones^t. In coefficient form write

Psi(T)=sum_(t,s) Lambda_(t,s)(a_(t,s)).            (1)

Here T_(t,s)=R_(a_(t,s)) acts by right multiplication in the regular algebra. All original external factors may be included or retained externally. At a regular complete fiber they are nonzero, so they do not change any null ideal below.

Define the maximal two-sided source-null ideal by

I_Psi={T : Psi(U T V)=0 for every U,V in B_b}.    (2)

It is the largest two-sided ideal contained in ker(Psi). If an algebra homomorphism pi:B_b->C preserves the source, meaning Psi=lambda composed with pi, then ker(pi) is contained in (2). This formulation does not assume that an arbitrary geometric relative-period map is an algebra homomorphism.

2. Literal physical top rows detect all residue classes

Use the ORIGINAL full fusion basis, not the later tail-head decomposition. Its tail labels are

s=(s_3,...,s_b), 0<=s_i<N.

The seed associated with f^a e_k in copy s is

f_total^a(e_0 tensor e_(k-sum(s) mod N) tensor e_s).

Let X=product_i x_i be the product of the actual highest K roots. The source of the diagonal relative top path is exactly

Lambda_(s,s)(f^(N-1)e_k)
   =gamma_s delta_(k,sum(s) mod N),
gamma_s=N X^(1-N) Q^(1-sum(s)), gamma_s!=0.       (3)

This is a direct physical cap calculation. Returning every tail output to its input s forces zero lowering steps on those legs. For the remaining N-1 steps to return the second output to the required pivotal index, all of them must go to the second leg, whose initial index must be zero. Thus k=sum(s) modulo N; any other k would require N first-leg lowering steps, impossible in total degree N-1. The coefficient of the second-leg steps is F_(N-1)=N, and their K_1 inverses supply x_1^(1-N). The second pivotal entry contributes x_2^(1-N)Q, while the tail pivots contribute product_(i>=3) x_i(1-N)Q-sum(s). This is (3).

The all-ones first output row remains the actual one; only its zero-index entry occurs in this particular top-row calculation. No normalized trace or geometric co-source is substituted.

3. For b>=3, no relative algebra ideal can be discarded

Theorem. For b>=3, I_Psi=0.

Take a nonzero element T of a two-sided ideal I in B_b. Some coefficient a_(t,s) has a nonzero coefficient c of f^a e_j. Relative matrix units first move that entry to any chosen diagonal copy; left/right coefficient idempotents isolate it, because

e_(j+a) a_(t,s) e_j=c f^a e_j.                  (4)

Only one degree between zero and N-1 has that start and endpoint. Multiplication by f^(N-1-a) then gives

f^(N-1-a) [c f^a e_j]=c f^(N-1)e_j.            (5)

All these operations are allowed relative matrix coupons, so the result stays in I. For b>=3 choose a diagonal tail copy u with sum(u)=j modulo N, for instance u_3=j and all later u_i=0. Equation (3) evaluates the resulting element to c gamma_u!=0. Thus no nonzero ideal is contained in ker(Psi), proving the theorem.

The coefficient algebra is opposite to physical operator composition; this only exchanges which of the two matrix-coupon sides realizes the left or right coefficient factor in (4)-(5). Both sides are available in (2), so no commutativity assumption enters the proof.

In particular ANY source-preserving algebra quotient of B_b is injective. This includes nilpotent ideals: the entire radical cannot be removed, nor can a nonzero subideal of it. The result is stronger than an example of one nonzero radical contribution.

It does not say Psi(UT) alone is a nondegenerate bilinear pairing; the proof uses both coupon sides. Nor does it say that every relative coupon is an actual braid word or commutes with the full quantum-group E action. The algebra in the theorem is the full actual Borel commutant furnished by fusion. A reduction on a strictly smaller operator algebra needs its own analysis.

4. The two-leg contrast: the exact quotient is triangular

For b=2, the actual cap is

Lambda(f^a e_k)=x_2^(1-N) x_1^-a Q^-(k+a)
                     F_(k+a)/F_k,  if k+a<N,
               0,                 if k+a>=N.    (6)

Define the wrapped-path span

I_cut=span{f^a e_k : k+a>=N}.                    (7)

It is a two-sided ideal: further path concatenation cannot undo crossing the cut from N-1 to zero, and paths of total length at least N vanish. The source (6) kills it. Conversely every nonwrapped path has a nonzero source value, and the two endpoint idempotents isolate its coefficient as in (4). Thus no larger two-sided ideal lies in the source kernel, so

I_Lambda=I_cut,
dim I_cut=N(N-1)/2.                             (8)

The quotient is the lower triangular N-by-N matrix algebra under

f^a e_k -> E_(k+a,k) if k+a<N, and zero otherwise.

This respects the path multiplication and gives all triangular matrix units. Its dimension is N(N+1)/2. In these coordinates the ORIGINAL cap is the matrix coefficient

Lambda(T)=x_2^(1-N) r^t T c,
r_i=x_1^-i Q^-i F_i, c_k=x_1^k/F_k.             (9)

Every r_i and c_k is nonzero. The effective triangular nilpotence length still grows with N. Its existence does not provide a bounded-rank critical algebra or a subexponential coefficient bound.

The b>=3 theorem explains why one cannot apply this two-leg quotient independently to intermediate diagonal blocks of a multi-strand word. Off-diagonal tail transitions expose all cyclic residue classes before the final selected contraction. Only the completed word may be passed to the diagonal-source formula of the all-strand Euler/Cauchy theorem.

5. Consequence for the geometric comparison

The exact endpoint functional is a tractable finite linear source, but its full algebra source does not descend to a smaller algebra by deleting a null ideal once b>=3. A fixed geometric critical algebra cannot be obtained as such a quotient of B_b while retaining all sourced relative matrix coupons. Morita equivalence may remove the matrix factor, but A_0 still has N simple modules and Loewy length N, so it is not Morita equivalent to one fixed finite etale critical algebra as N grows.

This specifies the kind of comparison still available: a linear relative-period or current-complex map, a separately constructed singular/nonlocal transform, or a restriction to an actual smaller meridian/word algebra with a proved invariant source quotient. Zero-source linear current spaces need not be ideals, so they are not excluded by the theorem. The result does not establish a nonzero selected geometric class or a volume norm.

6. Exact checks

The audit and its saved results check (3) against the literal original physical source after the actual coproduct fusion for braid indices three and four. They also check all relative matrix-basis coefficient recovery certificates in (4)-(5), and the two-leg wrapped ideal at levels two through eight. The proof above is all-level and does not depend on a finite rank fit or on an assertion about untested knots.

The saved run reports PASS on seven physical-source cases, 125 literal top rows, 3067 relative matrix-basis recovery certificates, and seven two-leg levels. A separate agent independently inspected the all-level source calculation, path isolation and quotient argument, finding no issue. The distinction between the full Borel commutant and the smaller actual full-quantum-group word algebra is essential to the conclusion’s scope.
