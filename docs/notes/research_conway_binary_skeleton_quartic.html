A quartic source minimum on the internal Conway edges

21 November 2026. Earlier finals and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

Retaining both inputs of each binary product permits every other packet to be removed exactly. The reduced objective is still a polynomial of degree at most four. Its coefficients require only constant linear-corridor metrics and one scalar root response; there is no linear solve depending on the trial field.

If there are p≥1p\ge1 binary products, this first step leaves exactly 2p2p packets and preserves the minimum exactly. Fixing the p+1p+1 known leaf inputs then leaves only p−1p-1 full packets, one on each connection between binary products. The latter minimum may change, but satisfies the same original source comparison. In the literal Conway compiler all corridor weights are explicit positive scalars. This is a simplification of the finite variational problem, not a new geometric lower bound.

1. Which original variables are retained

Use the normalized full source from the convex theorem. At level NN, every proper packet belongs to the complete Newton space HN≅ℂNH_N\cong\mathbb C^N, including its repeated-Casimir directions. There are ss proper vertices. A vertex is a prescribed leaf, a constant linear gate, or a bilinear product. The original normalized closing is the row cc. The energy is E(x)=∑vproper∥Φv((xj)j child of v)−xv∥2+|cxtop|2.(1) E(x)=\sum_{v\ {\mathrm{ proper}}} \|\Phi_v((x_j)_{j\text{ child of }v})-x_v\|^2 +|cx_{\mathrm{ top}}|^2 . \qquad\text{(1)} Every trial packet lies in its unit ball. Each normalized leaf, linear or bilinear map and closing row has norm at most σ=1/16\sigma=1/16. A constant linear gate may depend on NN and the fixed source data, but not on any trial packet.

The original budgets and their product BtotB_{\mathrm{ tot}} are fixed. The complete source theorem gives I≤Dℝ2E≤3I,(x−12∇E(x))v∈33256𝔹¯,(2) I\le D_{\mathbb R}^2E\le3I,\qquad \left(x-\tfrac12\nabla E(x)\right)_v \in\frac{33}{256}\overline{\mathbb B}, \qquad\text{(2)} and, with γ=197/196\gamma=197/196, Btot2min⁡E≤|KN|2≤γBtot2min⁡E.(3) B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2\min E\le |K_N|^2 \le\gamma B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2\min E . \qquad\text{(3)} All gradients use the real inner product Re⁡∑vhv*kv\operatorname{Re}\sum_vh_v^*k_v.

Let pp be the number of binary vertices. Retain the output packet at each of their two immediate child vertices. Call this list uu, and call every other proper packet zz. These 2p2p child occurrences are distinct: a vertex has exactly one parent. If a child is itself binary, its output and its parent’s input are the same variable and are counted once. The top proper packet is never retained. Thus #u=2p≤s−1(p≥1).(4) \#u=2p\le s-1\quad(p\ge1). \qquad\text{(4)} When p=0p=0, retain no variable.

Its eliminated minimizer is unique, strictly interior, and polynomial of degree at most two in the retained real coordinates. On the retained product of balls, I≤D2F≤3I.(6) I\le D^2F\le3I . \qquad\text{(6)} Its half-gradient step stays in the 33/25633/256 packet balls and contracts by 1/21/2. The exact minimum in (3) may be replaced by min⁡F\min F.

For p=0p=0, the reduced problem is a constant with no optimization. The explicit formulas below evaluate FF without a trial-dependent matrix inverse.

2. Why the inner quadratic system has constant coefficients

By construction, both inputs of every bilinear gate are retained. Thus no omitted variable occurs as an input of a bilinear map. Every full residual in (1), including the closing component, is jointly affine in zz, with constant coefficient matrix: E(u,z)=∥Az+b(u)∥2.(7) E(u,z)=\|Az+b(u)\|^2 . \qquad\text{(7)} The vector bb is polynomial of degree at most two. For example an omitted binary output contributes its prescribed bilinear forcing Φv(uL,uR)\Phi_v(u_L,u_R) and minus that output variable. A retained binary output has the same forcing but its negative output is already part of bb. All remaining gates are linear or constant.

The principal Hessian bounds in (2) imply 12I≤A*A≤32I.(8) \tfrac12I\le A^*A\le\tfrac32I . \qquad\text{(8)} These bounds hold for the entire unconstrained inner quadratic problem, because its Hessian is independent of zz. For fixed feasible uu, the map z↦z−12∇zE(u,z)(9) z\longmapsto z-\tfrac12\nabla_zE(u,z) \qquad\text{(9)} sends the omitted product of balls into its strict 33/25633/256 contraction, by the omitted components of (2). Its derivative has norm at most 1/21/2, by (8). Iteration within those balls therefore has a unique fixed point. It is stationary for the unconstrained quadratic, so z*(u)=−(A*A)−1A*b(u)(10) z_*(u)=-(A^*A)^{-1}A^*b(u) \qquad\text{(10)} is that point and is feasible. This proves the inactive-ball assertion and its uniform interior margin.

Since AA is constant, z*z_* has degree at most two, and F(u)=b(u)*[I−A(A*A)−1A*]b(u)(11) F(u)=b(u)^*\! \left[I-A(A^*A)^{-1}A^*\right]b(u) \qquad\text{(11)} has degree at most four. Equation (11) proves the degree claim; it need not be used to assemble a dense projection.

The difference from retaining only one child per product is important. In that reduction the omitted child enters a product with a retained child, so the inner matrix depends on the retained field. Here the inner matrix is constant.

The stationary inner solution (10) is smooth on a neighborhood of the retained balls. Equation (2) puts every component in its 33/25633/256 ball. Averaging (6) along a segment gives the same contraction factor 1/21/2 as in the full problem. 5. Only the internal connections need remain free

Suppose p≥1p\ge1. Suppress the unary corridors only for counting. The resulting rooted binary tree has pp binary vertices and p+1p+1 prescribed leaves. Of its 2p2p inputs, exactly p+1p+1 come from a leaf through unary gates; the other p−1p-1 come from a nontop binary output through unary gates.

For each leaf block, fix its retained endpoint to its known exact center y=Pay=Pa, using (12)–(15). These are prescribed leaf transports, independent of every free packet. They have norm at most σ\sigma, so the restriction is feasible. Each such block now contributes exactly zero. No output of a binary product is fixed at its unknown true value.

Let F̃\widetilde F be this restriction of FF, and write e*=min⁡E,ẽ*=min⁡F̃.(26) e_*=\min E,\qquad \widetilde e_*=\min\widetilde F. \qquad\text{(26)} It is quartic on precisely p−1p-1 full packets, or 2N(p−1)2N(p-1) real coordinates. Its Hessian remains between II and 3I3I, by taking the corresponding principal restriction of (6). Its free half-gradient components are those of the full step used in (24), with the leaf inputs fixed. They therefore remain in their 33/25633/256 balls and contract by 1/21/2.

The minima need not be equal. The restricted feasible set gives e*≤ẽ*e_*\le\widetilde e_*. On the other hand, the true normalized field satisfies every prescribed leaf transport. Using its internal connection packets in the restricted problem, and then minimizing the omitted packets, gives e*≤ẽ*≤|KN/Btot|2.(27) e_*\le\widetilde e_* \le |K_N/B_{\mathrm{ tot}}|^2. \qquad\text{(27)} Combining this with (3) yields the unchanged source sandwich Btot2ẽ*≤|KN|2≤γBtot2ẽ*.(28) \boxed{\qquad B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2\widetilde e_*\le |K_N|^2 \le\gamma B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2\widetilde e_* . \qquad} \qquad\text{(28)} This includes zeros. The comparison uses the same normalization budget and the same γ=197/196\gamma=197/196; it does not multiply a separate loss for each clamped leaf.

For p=1p=1, there is no remaining minimization: the two known leaf transports enter the sole product, followed by (19). For p=0p=0, the entire source is the single linear chain already evaluated by (19). These statements evaluate the stated finite source expressions; they assert no asymptotic estimate.

6. Scope and complete proof sources

Every retained packet is still the full HNH_N. Omitted packets were minimized over all their physical coordinates; their effect remains in (15) and (19). This is not a semisimple quotient or a deletion of Hermite directions.

The smaller one-child reduction retains pp packets, but its exact least-squares objective generally has variable coefficients and is rational. The corresponding unweighted compressed objective may have degree above four. The present exact partial minimum retains 2p2p packets to preserve the quartic degree and constant corridor metrics. The additional prescribed-leaf restriction reduces this to p−1p-1 packets while preserving the source comparison, rather than preserving the numerical minimum.

All linear maps in the corridors must be independent of trial packets. A bilinear gate cannot be reclassified by silently fixing one of its variational inputs at its true value. Nor does algebraically expanding a crossing power preserve a previously chosen normalization budget if it changes the compiler tree.

The reduction evaluates no hyperbolic exponential rate and supplies no additional geometric lower certificate. An exponentially small minimum still requires relative control; uniform conditioning alone does not supply it. Degree-four exactness of a new sum-of-squares or semidefinite relaxation is not inferred from the reduced objective’s convexity. The original lifted certificates remain available.
