Current scope and direct geometric input. The whole-color improvement lowers the odd-width threshold to s>=9. 29 October 2026. This records the variational equality argument requested by the user. The relevant volume is Vsrc_s(p), not the larger complete augmented volume V_s. The angle constraints must be those of the actual filled slopes. A maximum for the unfilled cusp problem would not prove the desired equality.

The geometric completion inputs are the positive-core construction, the primitive angle certificate and the log-link/tube lemma. Stationarity does not replace smooth end completion. The completion argument gives the full source equality and its scope.

1. The essential established quantum inputs

For each fixed odd s>=9 and independently sufficiently large fixed signed integers p_i, the whole original source theorem with the subsequent width improvement gives

Kraw_N=N^(3/2) exp[-is*pi*(N-1)/4] kappa^(-sum p_i)
               D_s(p) exp[N A_s(p)] (1+O_(s,p)(1/N)),
D_s(p)!=0.

The original reduced Kashaev invariant has the same absolute value. Thus its limit already exists and equals Vsrc_s(p)=2pi Re A_s(p). The steps that matter for this conclusion are:

  1. Exact source transformations retain the original marked derivative channels, all colors, peripheral factors and Cartan phases.
  2. The inner sums are combined coherently before estimating them. The global alias/core gap controls every color outside the cusp corridor.
  3. The corridor has an exact complex analytic packet, not merely a pointwise real-grid approximation. This permits the contour estimates.
  4. The exact filling transform retains every endpoint current. Its zero charge vanishes and its minimal nonzero signed charges add. This establishes the nonzero coefficient for the entire source.
  5. The literal port lift and marked regulator comparison supply an actual representation rho_p of the filled knot group and the absolute identity Vsrc_s(p)=Vol(rho_p), with the complete oriented augmented holonomy as anchor.

Neither Cauchy alone nor existence of a classical stationary point establishes steps2–4. They are already supplied by the linked analytic proofs. The present task is the classical equality for this selected representation.

2. Obtain a positive ideal angle point from the selected source

Let Y_s be the original complete augmented link complement. Choose a fixed positive geometric ideal triangulation T of Y_s. The direct subdivision certificate, sections 1–3, supplies it from the actual face-labeled polyhedra: normalized light lifts are preserved by every white and shaded face pairing, and a finite compatible affine subdivision gives nonflat ideal tetrahedra. This uses no general triangulation existence theorem. Here “positive” means every oriented tetrahedron has shape in the upper half-plane; it is stronger than a topological ideal triangulation. The direct finite-center completion proof does not depend on this all-ideal existence input, but it supplies the customary Casson–Rivin angle presentation used in this note.

Transport the finitely many developed ideal vertices of T using the actual source representation rho_z and its selected peripheral flags. At z=0 this is exactly the geometric developing configuration. A peripheral flag tends continuously to its noncentral parabolic flag: in a bounded matrix frame the eigenspace kernel has rank one at the limit, so it admits a local continuous, indeed holomorphic, choice. Every vertex orbit is well defined because its peripheral stabilizer fixes the chosen flag.

All cross-ratios of the finitely many tetrahedra therefore vary continuously from strictly positive shapes. Shrink the parameter neighborhood once so every shape remains positive and nondegenerate. The actual representation identities telescope to the complex edge gluing equations. The edge angular sums are continuously close to 2pi and are integral multiples of 2pi, hence are exactly 2pi. The marked peripheral products are the actual eigenvalue multipliers.

The selected filling points approach z=0 uniformly as max_i |p_i|^(-1) tends to zero. One threshold therefore puts ALL independently signed sufficiently long tuples in this fixed positive shape neighborhood. No comparability between different slopes is used. Denote their positive angle points by theta_p.

3. The correct affine volume problem includes the filled slopes

For each tetrahedron write its positive angles as alpha,beta,gamma, with alpha+beta+gamma=pi. Let

V_T(theta)=sum_t [Lambda(alpha_t)+Lambda(beta_t)+Lambda(gamma_t)],
Lambda'(a)=-log(2sin a).

The edge constraints are the usual linear equations with sum2pi at each edge. Fix their original angle and peripheral log lifts, continued from the complete triangulation. In projective geometric coordinates the actual auxiliary logs are

U_i=Log(M_i^2)=-z_i,
V_i=Log(lambda_i^2), centered at completeness,=-2ell_i.

Their imaginary parts are affine real-linear functions of the tetrahedral angles in this fixed marking. The old SL longitude sign is retained in the quantum/regulator comparison; it is not an extra geometric rotation here. By the primitive-angle certificate the selected all-positive unit charge has

U_i+p_i V_i=-2pi i.                                  (1)

Other minimal signed charges replace the right side by -2pi i m_i, |m_i|=1, on their corresponding eigenflag marks. It suffices to use the all-positive charge, whose nonzero contribution is already proved.

Define A_p to be the positive angle polytope satisfying the tetrahedral and edge equations, the REAL linear filling-angle constraints

Im U_i+p_i Im V_i=-2pi,  i=1,...,s,                 (2)

and Im U_0=0 on a chosen primitive meridian of the remaining main cusp. The latter cusp is actually fully parabolic in rho_p, so U_0=V_0=0. The source point theta_p belongs to the INTERIOR of this affine slice.

The full nonlinear holonomy equations are not being imposed as convex constraints. Only their imaginary, angle-linear parts define A_p. The real parts will give stationarity of volume.

4. The source equations imply stationarity on the entire angle slice

Set x_t=Log z_t and y_t=Log z_t’‘, z_t’’=1-z_t^(-1), with Im x_t=alpha_t and Im y_t=gamma_t. The sine rule gives

Re x_t=log(sin beta_t/sin gamma_t),
Re y_t=log(sin alpha_t/sin beta_t).

For EVERY infinitesimal angle variation, not merely a variation through geometric solutions, direct differentiation gives

dV_T(delta theta)=sum_t [Re x_t delta gamma_t
                                  -Re y_t delta alpha_t]. (3)

Let E be the real space of edge Hamiltonians in the standard shape symplectic space Omega=sum dx_t wedge dy_t. Both the real logarithmic shape vector of a gluing solution and any tangent satisfying the edge angle equations lie in E^perp. The already established boundary pairing, summed over the boundary tori, reduces Omega there to

Omega_red=-(1/2)sum_i dU_i wedge dV_i.

Distinct tori have zero intersection pairing; on each torus the primitive peripheral rows have pairing -2. Thus at theta_p equation(3) becomes the boundary formula for arbitrary angle tangents

dV_T(delta theta)
  =(1/2)sum_i [Re V_i delta Im U_i
                                  -Re U_i delta Im V_i]. (4)

This is not obtained by restricting the derivative to the smaller variety of full complex gluing solutions. The coisotropic pairing justifies its use for every tangent of A_p.

For each filled cusp, the real part of(1) says

Re U_i=-p_i Re V_i.

Its contribution to(4) is therefore

(1/2)Re V_i [delta Im U_i+p_i delta Im V_i]=0

by(2). The main cusp contributes zero because U_0=V_0=0. Consequently

dV_T(theta_p)|_(T_theta_p A_p)=0.                    (5)

This is the full constrained critical-point condition requested in the Casson–Rivin argument. It uses the actual filled slopes and their centered integer angles, rather than the unfilled cusp maximum.

5. Strict concavity makes this the unique global maximum

On one tetrahedron, in coordinates alpha,gamma with beta=pi-alpha-gamma, the negative Hessian is

[[cot alpha+cot beta, cot beta],
 [cot beta, cot gamma+cot beta]].                    (6)

Its determinant is1. Its first diagonal entry equals sin(gamma)/(sin(alpha)sin(beta))>0. It is positive definite on the entire positive angle simplex. Summing proves strict concavity of V_T on every affine angle slice, including A_p.

For any other point of the closure of A_p, the straight segment from theta_p stays positive until its possible final endpoint. Along it the first derivative starts at zero by(5), and the second derivative is strictly negative unless the point is unchanged. Continuity of Lambda at the boundary then gives

V_T(theta)<V_T(theta_p),  theta!=theta_p.             (7)

Thus the source point is the UNIQUE global maximizer of the full filled-slope angle polytope, including its boundary. No gradient-flow convergence assumption or numerical maximum is required.

6. Smooth completion identifies the maximum with hyperbolic volume

The value of the positive developed fundamental triangulation is

V_T(theta_p)=D(beta(rho_p))=Vsrc_s(p).                (8)

Here the absolute marked regulator comparison fixes the constant; one is not defining an unknown source action to be volume.

For completeness, the geometric content behind the generalized Casson–Rivin conclusion is proved directly by the companion notes. The positive finite-center deformation supplies a local hyperbolic metric on the same compact core. Its auxiliary cusp links are positive Euclidean similarity tori. After placing their chosen flags at infinity, the second common affine fixed point q escapes to infinity near the complete structure. The finite link development avoids q. Therefore Log(dev-q) gives a nonsingular translation structure on a COMPACT torus. Its flat metric is complete, so its universal developing map is the whole complex plane, with its actual rank-two period lattice.

The end contains a full punctured hyperbolic tube. The primitive-angle certificate proves that its actual meridian period is exactly -2pi i, not that integer merely modulo2pi. Hence the tube completes to a smooth solid torus with the specified primitive slope. The main cusp stays complete. The tube lemma proves both the degree-one statement and the finite-volume completion.

The resulting complete oriented metric g_p is on the ACTUAL filled knot complement. Its holonomy is rho_p. This supplies the geometric equality certificate that strict concavity or scalar PSL holonomy alone would not provide.

The user’s Gromov-norm observation gives a short final comparison. Apply the independently proved sharp representation-volume inequality with the specified hyperbolic metric g_hyp as domain and g_p holonomy as target. Then reverse their roles using the same oriented topological marking. Fundamental chains have degree one in both directions, so

Vol(g_p)<=Vol(g_hyp),  Vol(g_hyp)<=Vol(g_p).

No separate unproved equality case of the norm inequality is invoked. These two inequalities prove

Vsrc_s(p)=max_(theta in A_p) V_T(theta)
                     =Vol(g_p)=Vol(S^3 minus K).     (9)

7. Quantum conclusion and quantifiers

Combining(9) with the whole-source asymptotic and the independently audited positive-core, primitive-period and tube certificates proves the requested original-invariant equality:

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N)log|<K>_N|=Vol(S^3 minus K).

With the later whole-color improvement, the class is each fixed odd width s>=9, all independently sufficiently large fixed signed p_i, the original boxes R^(-(2p_i+1)), and the standing hyperbolicity assumption. The common threshold may increase to place the selected source points inside the fixed geometric neighborhood. There is no upper bound on width across the class, no comparability or common-sign condition on the slopes, and no restriction to prime levels or one parity. This note does not assert the quantum limit for all hyperbolic knots or for slopes growing with N.

8. Literature coverage, not a proof premise

The Futer–Gueritaud survey describes Casson–Rivin’s critical-volume characterization of hyperbolic structures. That classical variational framework is established territory; this note does not claim to invent it. The contribution needed for this project is verifying its hypotheses for the point selected by the ENTIRE original quantum source, with the actual filled slopes and marked logarithms. Equations(3)–(7) and the companion geometric completion arguments are proved here rather than imported from the survey. The geometric ideal-triangulation input used for this angle presentation is the previously audited argument of Ge, version1; the independent finite-center completion route avoids making that input necessary for the final volume equality itself.
