Main-color preserving metrics in a bounded regularized source problem

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof.

Main-color preserving gauges already suffice at the leading exponential scale for the actual all-main-color source. The earlier proof establishes an infimum over those gauges. Here that infimum is approximated inside an explicit finite metric window, including when individual color values vanish. The restricted regularized minimizer consequently has logarithmic metric radius O(N)O(N) and the same leading rate as the unrestricted problem. The proof retains the original closing in every color.

This removes color mixing from that bounded variational formulation at the leading scale. It does not identify the remaining within-color metrics with geometric data or prove a volume lower bound.

1. The actual sectors and a known input bound

Use the actual all-main-color compiler of [C, Section 5]. Fix a completed braid word whose prescribed marked closure is one knot, with its original zero-framing factors and partial pivotal trace. Let bb be its fixed braid width; each proper edge in color jj is the full operator space End⁡(Vj,N⊗b)\operatorname{End}(V_{j,N}^{\otimes b}). Compile multiplication of its signed crossing operators as a tree with m≥2m\ge2 vertices, including the root readout. Select any set 𝒥⊂{1,…,N}\mathcal J\subset\{1,\ldots,N\} containing 11, with 2≤r:=|𝒥|≤N2\le r:=|\mathcal J|\le N. Keep those original color blocks and their original readouts. An internal edge is an orthogonal direct sum of these rr physical color spaces. Every local tensor has blocks Tv,jT_{v,j}, j∈𝒥j\in\mathcal J, and a contraction pairs only equal color labels. The root output is exactly Y𝒥,N=(cj)j∈𝒥,c1=1,∥Y𝒥,N∥≥1,cN=KN.(1) Y_{\mathcal J,N}=(c_j)_{j\in\mathcal J},\qquad c_1=1, \qquad \|Y_{\mathcal J,N}\|\ge1,\qquad c_N=K_N. \qquad\text{(1)} The last identity refers to the original complete-color readout, whether or not NN was selected. Every selected physical space is retained in full, including its reducible structure. No nonvanishing of cjc_j for j>1j>1, or of KNK_N, is assumed.

Orient edges toward the root. Write ae,ja_{e,j} for the actual open subtree message in sector jj, before its parent is contracted. These proper messages are products of invertible signed physical crossings. They do not include the final closing. Fix a known B=BN≥2B=B_N\ge2 which bounds ∥Tv,j∥HS,∥Tv,j∥HS−1,∥ae,j∥,∥ae,j∥−1for every v,e,j.(2) \|T_{v,j}\|_{\mathrm{ HS}},\quad \|T_{v,j}\|_{\mathrm{ HS}}^{-1}, \quad \|a_{e,j}\|,\quad \|a_{e,j}\|^{-1} \quad\text{for every }v,e,j. \qquad\text{(2)} Vector norms on the edge spaces are their physical Hilbert–Schmidt norms. Norms of maps between those spaces below are induced operator norms. Local tensor norms are coefficient Hilbert–Schmidt norms.

For every fixed actual word, such a BNB_N satisfies log⁡BN=O(N),log⁡DN=O(log⁡N),DN:=∑edim⁡He.(3) \log B_N=O(N),\qquad \log D_N=O(\log N),\qquad D_N:=\sum_e\dim H_e. \qquad\text{(3)} Here DND_N includes all selected colors. To verify the first assertion, [M, (10)–(12)] bounds both signed crossings and their inverses uniformly over the entire main-color orbit. The inverse of a fixed proper word is the reversed word of inverse crossings. Thus its operator norm and inverse operator norm are exp⁡(O(N))\exp(O(N)); tensoring spectator identities adds polynomial factors. Its Hilbert–Schmidt norm has the same upper bound and is at least the reciprocal of its inverse operator norm. In every selected sector the multiplication and original readout blocks have nonzero unit-modulus coefficients and polynomial norms. Their reciprocal norms are therefore bounded too, even if that readout evaluates to cj=0c_j=0. These observations prove (2)–(3) without any division by a closed color value.

Fixed signed powers or fixed short word blocks may be contracted first. All constants can depend on that fixed presentation. The gauges below use the complete open messages, not their principal symbols.

2. A finite sector-preserving competitor

3. Scalar balancing and the metric radius

Within sector jj, prescribe the logarithmic vertex rescalings λv,j=1mlog⁡pj−log⁡av,j.(11) \lambda_{v,j} =\frac1m\log p_j-\log a_{v,j}. \qquad\text{(11)} Their sum is zero and |λv,j|≤2mℒ|\lambda_{v,j}|\le2m\mathcal L. The incidence identity on a rooted tree makes the resulting logarithmic change at each vertex exactly λv,j\lambda_{v,j}, including at the root. Each edge scalar and its inverse are bounded by exp⁡(2m2ℒ)\exp(2m^2\mathcal L). No scalar is defined from cj−1c_j^{-1}.

4. The restricted regularized minimum

5. Two actual colors suffice for a positive target rate

In particular take the two original main colors 𝒥={1,N}\mathcal J=\{1,N\}. Their selected output is exactly Y{1,N},N=(1,KN),C2=(1+|KN|2/m)m/2,1+|KN|2≤C2≤2(m−1)/21+|KN|2.(20) Y_{\{1,N\},N}=(1,K_N),\qquad C_2=(1+|K_N|^{2/m})^{m/2},\qquad \sqrt{1+|K_N|^2}\le C_2 \le2^{(m-1)/2}\sqrt{1+|K_N|^2}. \qquad\text{(20)} Equations (16)–(17) become minblockJN,N−2=log⁡C2+O(N−1),supe∥log⁡He,Nblock∥op=O(N).(21) \min_{\mathrm{ block}}J_{N,N^{-2}}=\log C_2+O(N^{-1}),\qquad \sup_e\|\log H^{\mathrm{ block}}_{e,N}\|_{\mathrm{ op}}=O(N). \qquad\text{(21)} For every V>0V>0 this gives the exact target equivalence limN2πNminblockJN,N−2=V⇔limN2πNlog⁡|KN|=V.(22) \lim_N\frac{2\pi}{N}\min_{\mathrm{ block}}J_{N,N^{-2}}=V \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \lim_N\frac{2\pi}{N}\log|K_N|=V . \qquad\text{(22)} Indeed log⁡C2\log C_2 differs from max⁡(0,log⁡|KN|)\max(0,\log|K_N|) by at most (m/2)log⁡2(m/2)\log2. A strictly positive limiting rate removes that maximum and implies eventual nonvanishing. This argument needs no estimate for the other ordinary-color remainder.

Each internal edge here is two copies of the raw physical operator space End⁡(Vj,N⊗b)\operatorname{End}(V_{j,N}^{\otimes b}), of total dimension 2N2b2N^{2b}. These are two actual main-color sectors, with the original crossings and root readouts. They are not a newly appended constant coordinate. The orthogonally anchored Conway construction [A] already has output (1,KN)(1,K_N) in a different, smaller boundary presentation. No new anchoring principle or improvement of its dimension is claimed. The new assertion is finite controlled-window near-attainment of the exact sector infimum and its regularized consequence.

There is also a direct comparison with [A]’s fixed-degree quartic minimum QNQ_N for the same original Conway source. That theorem gives 1+|KN|2≤QN≤(1+1/N)(1+|KN|2)1+|K_N|^2\le Q_N\le(1+1/N)(1+|K_N|^2). With j2,N=min⁡blockJN,N−2j_{2,N}=\min_{\mathrm{ block}}J_{N,N^{-2}}, equations (20)–(21) imply −12log⁡(1+1/N)≤j2,N−12log⁡QN≤m−12log⁡2+O(N−1).(23) -\tfrac12\log(1+1/N) \le j_{2,N}-\tfrac12\log Q_N \le\tfrac{m-1}{2}\log2+O(N^{-1}). \qquad\text{(23)} This is a bounded additive difference of logarithmic values. It identifies neither minimizers nor pointwise objectives.

For all NN colors the output remains the one in [C], ∥Y{1,…,N},N∥2=|KN|2+RN\|Y_{\{1,\ldots,N\},N}\|^2=|K_N|^2+R_N, with its original polynomial ordinary-color remainder. No original source value changes when that larger selected set is used instead.

6. Scope of the reduction

The new conclusion is a finite-window realization and regularized version of the sector infimum already proved in [M, Section 5]. The normalizing directions are exact open source packets, and the balancing scalars use actual finite local tensor norms. This is not a construction of those directions from a classical angle structure. The original cap stays at the root, and a zero complete-color value is permitted throughout.

The restricted minimizer is not asserted to equal the full minimizer. Pinching an arbitrary gauge is not asserted to improve the objective. Restricted stationarity controls only the permitted block variations; it does not imply the full marginal balance of [C,R]. No within-color dimension reduction, principal-symbol limit, interchange of an infimum and a limit, or geometric noncancellation follows.

All statements concern the raw multiplication tree of a fixed completed one-component knot. Proper packets are invertible word operators; arbitrary Conway packets or components containing internal partial traces need not have this property. Constants may depend on the fixed word, filling assignment and braid width. No uniformity in unbounded word length, or anchor for an unfilled auxiliary link, is asserted. There is no new volume identity or finite-exception theorem.
