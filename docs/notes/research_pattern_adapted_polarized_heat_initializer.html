A contour-polarized heat family with an actual small-charge initializer

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

Expand the original finite source by its removed physical-row subsets before choosing contour heights. Freeze for each subset the heights used in its small-charge estimate. Splitting only the retained lower horizontal cosine then gives exact polarized entire families. Their sign average is the original charged source, and every polarization has the original unit-charge endpoint. The all-plus family has a proved nonzero complex initializer and exact signed heat evolution.

These families depend on the prescribed polynomial source and frozen contours. They are different from the finite Hermite coupons QN,±Q_{N,\pm}. No polarized finite-coupon initializer is inferred. Nor does the construction supply the angle-label coverage or global action-relative bounds needed to reach arbitrary unit-charge targets.

3. Exact heat evolution and the full-source growth bound

Each integrand in (4)–(6) obeys ∂nv=−i4πN∂χv2(12) \partial_{n_v} =-\frac{i}{4\pi N}\partial_{\chi_v}^2 \qquad\text{(12)} when applied to its Gaussian times its cosine or exponential. In (4), the physical angular derivative commutes with this identity. All finite sums and fixed compact contour integrals commute with the parameter derivatives. Consequently (10) is entire in powers and charges and satisfies (12) in every coordinate. No integration by parts or missing moving-endpoint term is involved.

For fixed real powers in a bounded box and free boundaries in a fixed compact set, there are constants A,p,CA,p,C such that |ℨN,𝝈(𝒏,𝝌;𝒖)|≤ANp∏v(1+|χv|)exp⁡N{C+∑v[π|ℑχv|+H*|1−ℜχv|]}.(13) |\mathfrak Z_{N,\boldsymbol\sigma}(\mathbf n,\boldsymbol\chi;\mathbf u)| \le A N^p\prod_v(1+|\chi_v|) \exp N\left\{C+\sum_v \bigl[\pi|\Im\chi_v|+H_*|1-\Re\chi_v|\bigr]\right\}. \qquad\text{(13)} Every angular argument used in (10) has |ℜt|≤π|\Re t|\le\pi and |ℑt|≤H*|\Im t|\le H_*. Its charge exponential is therefore bounded by the corresponding factor in (13); cosine is their mean. Physical normals add polynomial factors and 1+|χv|1+|\chi_v|. The original Newton polynomial envelopes on this fixed angular compact give NpeCNN^p e^{CN} for the kernels and their fixed mixed normals, as in the local heat theorem. The horizontal WNW_N factors are bounded at the fixed positive heights; the vertical denominators in (6) are bounded by one. The finitely many patterns and finite small levels can be absorbed in the constants. This proves (13), also with any fixed number of ordinary free-boundary derivatives.

For δ∈ℝ\{0}\delta\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\} and vδ=e−isgn⁡(δ)π/4v_\delta=e^{-i\operatorname{sgn}(\delta)\pi/4}, the exact heat formula is ℨN(𝒏+δ𝒆v,𝝌)=N|δ|∫ℝe−πNr2/|δ|ℨN(𝒏,𝝌+vδr𝒆v)dr.(14) \mathfrak Z_N(\mathbf n+\delta\mathbf e_v,\boldsymbol\chi) =\sqrt{\frac N{|\delta|}} \int_{\mathbb R}e^{-\pi Nr^2/|\delta|} \mathfrak Z_N(\mathbf n, \boldsymbol\chi+v_\delta r\mathbf e_v)\,dr. \qquad\text{(14)} Complete the square on each charge exponential before the compact angular integrations. Bound (13), with the Gaussian quadratic decay, justifies Fubini and all derivatives. It also permits any finite translation of the entire charge line. Thus (14) retains every original current. 4. A genuine holomorphic initializer for the all-plus family

Throughout this section the outside angles are specialized to zero, so their spectral variables equal −2-2. The initializer below is not asserted for arbitrary free-boundary values.

Let Φ𝒃,𝝌\Phi_{\mathbf b,\boldsymbol\chi}, its small critical point, and AΓ(𝝌)≠0A_\Gamma(\boldsymbol\chi)\ne0 have exactly the original normalization in the small-charge theorem, with carriers removed. If ee is the number of kernel edges in that theorem, then on a fixed complex neighborhood UU of the base point (8), ℨN,+(𝒃,𝝌)=2mN3(m−e)/2AΓ(𝝌)eNΦ𝒃,𝝌(𝒕*)(1+Ohol(N−1)).(15) \boxed{\quad \mathfrak Z_{N,\mathbf+}(\mathbf b,\boldsymbol\chi) =2^m N^{3(m-e)/2} A_\Gamma(\boldsymbol\chi) e^{N\Phi_{\mathbf b,\boldsymbol\chi}(\mathbf t^*)} (1+O_{\mathrm{ hol}}(N^{-1})).\quad} \qquad\text{(15)} The amplitude is a holomorphic unit after shrinking UU, and there is an expansion to every fixed inverse-power order. Through both level parities, every other sign vector obeys, on a possibly smaller UU, |ℨN,𝝈(𝒃,𝝌)|≤ANpeN(ℜΦ𝒃,𝝌(𝒕*)−γ)(𝝈≠+)(16) |\mathfrak Z_{N,\boldsymbol\sigma}(\mathbf b,\boldsymbol\chi)| \le A N^p e^{N(\Re\Phi_{\mathbf b,\boldsymbol\chi}(\mathbf t^*)-\gamma)} \quad(\boldsymbol\sigma\ne\mathbf+) \qquad\text{(16)} for some γ>0\gamma>0.

Here is the source estimate, including the role of the frozen heights. At the real base point, a summand with S≠⌀S\ne\varnothing uses precisely the reduced heights required by the original outer-row proof. Its centered kernel bound retains the losses at every removed incidence and every mixed physical normal. Replacing a remaining horizontal cosine by either exponential does not increase its exponential majorant: at height −h-h the plus choice gives charge χ\chi, and the minus choice gives the larger decaying charge 2−χ2-\chi. Thus the archived outer-row gap applies to that summand. Likewise every summand with a vertical coordinate has the original mixed-current gap, with the side factor in (6) retained.

For S=⌀S=\varnothing and no vertical coordinate, WN(t)eiN(1−χ)t=e−iNχt(1−ηe−iNt)2,e−iN(1−χ)teiN(1−χ)t=e−2iN(1−χ)t.(17) W_N(t)e^{iN(1-\chi)t} =\frac{e^{-iN\chi t}}{(1-\eta e^{-iNt})^2},\qquad \frac{e^{-iN(1-\chi)t}}{e^{iN(1-\chi)t}} =e^{-2iN(1-\chi)t}. \qquad\text{(17)} On the fixed lower horizontals at the base point, the second factor has modulus e−2N(1−ξv)hv⌀e^{-2N(1-\xi_v)h_v^\varnothing}. Every height is positive. Hence any minus sign gives a strict gap, while all plus signs give exactly the original selected phase and Gaussian density. The only normalization change is the absence of the cosine’s factor 1/21/2 at each vertex, proving the factor 2m2^m in (15).

This argument is valid at real nonlattice charges: the exact coupon identities use the same even entire functions and full Hermite data, and all quoted estimates use real charge inequalities rather than integrality of NχN\chi. Real powers are handled by the canonical crossing extension in the local heat theorem. Its physical values have unit modulus and its normals have polynomial cost.

To obtain the complex neighborhood, keep every contour in (10) fixed. A change |δχv|≤ε|\delta\chi_v|\le\varepsilon changes each of the preceding absolute exponential bounds by at most eC1Nεe^{C_1N\varepsilon}, uniformly over all the compact contour pieces and physical angles. The physical derivatives add only polynomial factors; explicitly their coupon bounds are eπN|ℑχ|e^{\pi N|\Im\chi|} and N|1−χ|eπN|ℑχ|N|1-\chi|e^{\pi N|\Im\chi|}. Choose ε\varepsilon below the finitely many strict gap margins divided by the resulting constants. The continued action changes by O(ε)O(\varepsilon), so the gaps remain uniform relative to it. This is an exponential estimate, not pointwise continuity in NN.

The all-plus saddle moves holomorphically inside the same pole-free thin rectangle. Its local contour can be displaced through that saddle, keeping the ends fixed and the connecting pieces in the existing strict-gap region. The original holomorphic kernel symbols, sine factors and Gaussian determinant stay nonzero. Their standard Taylor expansion gives (15) with uniform holomorphic errors and all fixed orders. The same gap perturbation gives (16).

5. What local heat transport now does, and what it does not do

Equations (13)–(15) supply an actual initialized family to which the quantitative local heat proof applies. Its growth function is now C+π|ℑχ|+H*|1−ℜχ|C+\pi|\Im\chi|+H_*|1-\Re\chi| in one coordinate. Along a Gaussian line, its extra linear cost is at most (π+H*)|r|(\pi+H_*)|r|. Replacing the constant π\pi by π+H*\pi+H_* in that proof’s linear tail bound gives the same sufficient small-step theorem, with its excess constant also replaced by the supremum of C+π|ℑζ|+H*|1−ℜζ|−ℜS(ζ)C+\pi|\Im\zeta|+H_*|1-\Re\zeta|-\Re S(\zeta) on the certified initial domain. In particular the characteristic, new action and density remain χ=ζ+iδ2πS′(ζ),Sδ=S(ζ)+iδ4πS′(ζ)2,aδ=a(ζ)1+iδS″(ζ)/(2π),(18) \chi=\zeta+\frac{i\delta}{2\pi}S'(\zeta),\qquad S_\delta=S(\zeta)+\frac{i\delta}{4\pi}S'(\zeta)^2, \qquad a_\delta=\frac{a(\zeta)} {\sqrt{1+i\delta S''(\zeta)/(2\pi)}}, \qquad\text{(18)} with the square root continued from one and with the actual eroded-domain and tail requirements of that theorem.

The frozen family is not redefined after a step. Rechoosing its heights would require retaining its exact height-change currents. Iteration still carries the same initially certified classical angle labels; the present construction does not prove that they cover a prescribed arbitrary short filling at unit charge. Nor does (13) replace the whole-source growth by the geometric action. The remaining problem is coverage and a certified chain of action-relative tail bounds for this now initialized family. It is no longer an internal charge-reflection obstruction, since the all-plus family is not itself even about charge one.
