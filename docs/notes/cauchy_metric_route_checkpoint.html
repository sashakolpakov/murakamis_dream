The special inner product: what Cauchy proves and what must be constructed

Whole-support update, 29 October. The actual correlated boundary Gram now has polynomial source cost on EVERY supported folded triple, including saturation boundaries, at both level parities. The current generalization strategy keeps the proved pretzel argument as the main route. Genuine changes of pairing and the sharp global energy/overlap still need control. The filled pretzel threshold is now odd s>=9 by the whole-color improvement; older threshold and status paragraphs below are historical.

Full long-pretzel equality, 29 October. The geometric completion theorem and Casson–Rivin proof now identify the selected full-source rate with the actual filled hyperbolic volume for fixed odd widths s>=25 and independently sufficiently long fixed signed twists. The equality uses the separately proved quantum survival and smooth geometric completion; it is not inferred from Cauchy’s upper inequality. Earlier status paragraphs below retain their historical scope.

Current result, 29 October. The requested inequality is weighted Cauchy–Schwarz. With the unchanged scalar K=bv and a specified positive Hermitian G, it is exactly |K|^2<=(b G(-1)b)(v^Gv). A subexponential dual norm and an independent geometric estimate of the energy are the two required bounds. The complete augmented-source Fourier metric below has both, including all marked normal derivatives. Its lower overlap estimate is an additional survival theorem, not a consequence of Cauchy.

The full long-pretzel upper theorem also now bounds the original invariant by the actual filled hyperbolic volume for fixed odd widths s>=25 and independently sufficiently long fixed signed twists. It uses the whole-source asymptotic and the actual representation regulator, not a newly assumed metric-energy estimate for arbitrary filled sources. Full volume equality and a general-knot sharp metric theorem remain unproved. Older status paragraphs follow.

Whole-source update, 29 October. The full original long-pretzel source now has a nonzero asymptotic for fixed odd s>=25 and independently sufficiently large fixed signed twists. Its proof bounds the entire color complement and all filling Fourier currents, then evaluates the surviving coherent charges. Its selected source rate still requires comparison with the filled hyperbolic volume. Complete augmented-base result, 29 October. For every fixed odd pretzel width s>=3 the question now has a sharp constructive answer. Write the original coherent Laurent polynomial z_N(w+w(-1))s as sum a_k w^k, and use G_(kk)=R^(2(k-N)) at the geometric radius R=exp(2asinh(tan(pi/s))). Crucially, the full-period proof now independently establishes log Z=N V_s/pi-(1/2)log N+O(1). The subsequent Gaussian calculation proves original-period survival as well. The normalized squared Riesz overlap is explicitly asymptotic to a nonzero constant times N^(-1/2), so the Cauchy estimate loses only a polynomial factor here; see equations (23)–(24) of that proof. This is the complete augmented link base with s+1 components; it does not give the required metric bound for its fixed filled knots. The arbitrary-source estimates and obstructions below retain that broader unresolved scope.

Mixed-source progress, 29 October. The mixed-cusp period proof now gives a nonzero uniform leading term on a fixed small real-color corridor for every fixed odd s>=5. Latest concrete metric, 29 October. The original complete crossing circle now supplies a positive semidefinite form on the entire physical pair: C=PL_N and H=L_NP. |K_N|^2 <= |chi_N|^2 2^(-k)N^(-(3k+2)) v_N^*G_top v_N,
G_top=C^(tensor(k-1)) tensor H,  v_N=W p^(tensor k).

This is a proved special-inner-product Cauchy inequality for the actual source, with every framing/peripheral factor retained. The cap kills the metric’s radical, so the inverse is the Moore–Penrose inverse on its positive quotient. The unrestricted marked row cannot be substituted locally. Same-pair twists preserve C; overlapping crossings need not.

For the arbitrary odd-pretzel augmented port graph there is also an exact coherent factorization into TWO positive source packets, with their retained half twists unitary between them. Neither result currently bounds its energy by the geometric Rivin partition. The boundary Cauchy step is complete; the sharp whole-source energy comparison and a survival/lower bound remain unproved.

Direct answer to the Cauchy question. Yes. For the original row b and state v, any positive Hermitian G gives

|bv|^2 <= (b G^(-1) b^*) (v^* G v).

The explicit complete-source metrics below already make the first factor polynomial. The remaining estimate is an INDEPENDENT bound of the second factor by the geometric angle partition, including the original external factors. Calling that second factor Z does not establish the geometric comparison.

Even a metric specified without knowing the numerical period can merely repackage it. Indeed G=b^*b+epsilon I gives exactly

b G^(-1)b^*=||b||^2/(||b||^2+epsilon)<=1,
v^*Gv=|bv|^2+epsilon ||v||^2.

Thus explicitness and a cheap boundary alone are insufficient. The useful content must be an independent geometric energy estimate.

The complete Fourier–Hermite filling metric gives another exact Cauchy realization with ALL normal jets retained: its dual cost for a full twist 2p is 1+p2(N2-1)/(3N^2), independent of N up to the displayed correction. The same proof gives an actual hyperbolic-knot normalization insertion on which its energy is excessive. Polynomial dual cost, full rank, and coherent off-diagonal terms alone therefore do not establish the sharp geometric comparison; the cancellations required by the whole source must be reflected in the energy estimate.

29 October 2026. The proposed comparison is precisely weighted Cauchy–Schwarz. Its substantive input is a positive metric on the original state, with controlled dual source and geometric energy.

Write the unchanged invariant as K_N=a_N^*v_N, including its actual peripheral and normalization factors in this pairing. For any positive Hermitian matrix G_N,

|K_N|^2 <= (a_N^*G_N^(-1)a_N)(v_N^*G_Nv_N).       (1)

Indeed a_N^*v_N=(G_N^(-1/2)a_N)^*(G_N^(1/2)v_N). Thus the requested bound follows from

a_N^*G_N^(-1)a_N <= exp(o(N)),
v_N^*G_Nv_N <= exp(o(N)) Z_N.                    (2)

The positive angle partition already has the independently proved geometric estimate log Z_N=N Vol(M)/pi+O(log N) for a fixed complete positive ideal triangulation with the specified bounded true edge defects. Equation (2) would therefore prove the sharp UPPER bound for the original invariant. A matching lower bound requires geometric survival as well.

Rescaling G_N by a positive scalar scales the two factors of (1) reciprocally. It cannot improve their product. Likewise defining a metric using the unknown complete contracted period does not establish (2). A useful metric must come from explicit packet, source and geometric data whose estimates can be checked independently.

An explicit metric already works on the original figure-eight packet

Put Q=exp(2pi i/N), F_n=(Q;Q)_n, and let

P_(n,k)=[n choose k]_Q, k<=n; otherwise 0,
H=diag(|F_n|^2), G=P^*HP,
v_k=(-1)^k Q^(k(k+1)/2), a=P^*F.

The literal original-source calculation proves Pv=F and K_(4_1),N=a^*v, with the original turns and Cartan factors retained. P is invertible and G is positive definite; its entries depend only on the explicit quantum-factorial packet. Consequently

a^*G^(-1)a=N,
v^*Gv=sum_(n=0)^(N-1)|F_n|^4,
|K_(4_1),N|^2 <= N sum_n |F_n|^4.               (3)

The last positive sum has logarithm N Vol(4_1)/pi+O(log N). This is the requested Cauchy mechanism in a specified non-diagonal inner product. The positive formula for this particular original period also proves its lower bound. Neither fact alone generalizes the volume conjecture to other knots.

What now extends beyond that example

The complete original multiplicity reduction now preserves EVERY signed braid generator on an entire even-plat invariant space. For a fixed 2k-head plat it gives exactly K_N=chi_N lambda B_word w_0, with

||w_0||=N^[3(k-1)/2], ||lambda||=N^[-(k-1)/2].

Thus ordinary Cauchy already gives |K_N|^2<=|chi_N|^2 N^[-(k-1)] ||B_word w_0||^2 in that specified full multiplicity space. The original external factor chi_N is retained, not assumed small. The representation is complete, so there is no unresolved preservation assumption for a compressed cap channel. What remains is the sharp estimate of the coherent norm, potentially in a different positive metric. The full Casimir/Hermite packet supplies all frame and inverse coefficients, including their derivative normalizers, for constructing and checking such a metric.

For the complete four-strand plat representation, this now gives an explicit additional positive metric. Let J be the full anti-Pascal recoupling, V the complete degree-(N-1) Casimir/Hermite frame, and set

S=V^(-1)J, G=(1/N)S^*S.

Both matrices are invertible and are fixed before the braid is chosen. The exact original source calculation in Section 4a of the multiplicity note gives, with n_distinct=ceil(N/2),

||ell||_(G^(-1))^2=n_distinct/N<=1,
||p||_G^2<=8N^2.

Here the first expression means the dual norm of the row ell. All Casimir derivative directions and the original full marked closure are included through the complete invariant-tensor reduction. Thus for v=chi_N B_word p we have EXACTLY

|K_N|^2<=||v||_G^2.

This constructs a special inner product and controls its original dual source on the complete module. However the desired estimate ||v||_G^2<=exp(o(N)) Z_N for THIS metric is now disproved even for the figure-eight, by the exact channel witness linked above. A different coherent metric is necessary; the terminal packet construction supplies one with the sharp benchmark rate on this same full module. No smallness of chi_N or of the whole recoupling matrix is assumed. The all-width reduction remains available, with general terminal energy still open.

The next source calculation performs an entire contraction coherently. The original pivotal cap on the actual reassociation (C'_23)^(-1)C'_12 supplies the finite sum sum_r Q^r [b+r choose r]_Q=[N choose b+1]_Q. It annihilates every middle input except b=N-1. In the source-fixing quarter metric the remaining squared coefficient is exactly w_N(a,c,N-1-a-c), with its full phase retained. The inverse cup identity is also exact. The unrestricted marked row has its separately recorded current packet and is not replaced by this ordinary cap.

Arbitrary binary trees of these original cap packets now have a complete quantitative contraction theorem. With l active leaves of degrees a_i and root n=sum a_i, their squared kernel is N^(2-l)/[s_(N-1-n) product_i s_(a_i)]. Their row Gram is exactly diagonal, with explicitly known entries E_(l,n), and

log E_(l,n)=(N/pi)[l Lambda(pi*n/(l*N))-Lambda(pi*n/N)]
                +O_l(log N), uniformly 0<=n<N.

Their normalized coherent overlaps are contractive at arbitrary fixed width. Actual source phases, separator channels and cap powers remain. This proves boundary-tree energy control, not preservation of those packets by a general interior braid.

The marked Casimir source channel also now has a quantitative positive frame. Its original cup p and canonical generalized vector t_N are orthogonal, with

||p||^2=N^3, N^3/4<=||t_N||^2<=pi^2 N^3/6,
condition([p,t_N])<=2.

The proof is an exact finite Fourier–Plancherel calculation. The original marked and ordinary cap rows are the two dual coordinates, so each has dual norm one in the Hermite Gram. The full crossing, including its nilpotent part, has norm at most 1+pi*|ell| for twist exponent ell in that frame. This controls the selected degree-d two-plane, not transitions into other channels on overlapping pairs.

The Pascal matrices are finite Euler polynomials in the original nilpotent current. The same note factors EVERY original signed same-color crossing, in every integer total-degree block, into Pascal matrices, their inverses, explicit factorial diagonals and Gaussian phases. High-degree blocks and actual marked sources remain. This supplies a known local packet alphabet for arbitrary braid width. It does not give a bound on its overlapping recouplings.

Two independent estimates supply controlled interfaces:

  • The source-balanced Fermat metric H_a=|L_a|/|P_a| makes both original source costs the explicit chord sum |1-u| sum_a |1-uQ^a|^(-1). At every normalized original cap, retaining its exact Gamma factors gives cost O(1+|epsilon|log N), uniformly in the cap index.
  • The physical factorial metric controls the retained two-head fusion current and its inverse by 1+|t|^N H_floor(N/2)/2. Fixed words of these adapters, including overlapping pairs, therefore cost polynomially when their metric interfaces agree and |t|^N is polynomially bounded.

There is also a boundary projector theorem: on the actual doubled corner sector, the cap metric is scalar for real cap parameters. All flat orthogonal projectors there are contractive; through the complex cap disk their norms remain polynomially bounded. The full regularized cap has other sectors, and interior transport of this particular sector has not been proved.

These are actual source and current estimates. Larger fused-block currents carry positive angle energy and cannot simply be declared tame. Transporting one packet metric through a neighboring packet also retains its full off-diagonal terms.

For one exact three-head reassociation, the retained matrix entry is |[u choose a]_Q|=N w_N(a,u-a,N-1-u). Its three nonnegative angle indices sum to N-1 exactly. Thus an exponential interface cost already has a concrete geometric weight; its squared norm contains the square of that weight. The global metric must account for the required power and the actual edge incidence, as well as the source compensation.

The remaining upper-bound problem is now concrete: compose the explicit packet alphabet and its dual sources into a positive Gram whose coherent energy satisfies (2), including the true geometric edge constraints and retained current channels. There is no proof of that statement for a broad knot class yet.

The raw positive path model obtained by dephasing the shear factors has a uniform excessive closed route even with the original trace constraints. It is not the target geometric partition. Complete source and interior packets must be contracted coherently before a sharp positive comparison is made.

The metric must preserve cancellations across suitable packets. Every diagonal metric on the complete figure-eight clock sectors has an excessive Cauchy budget. Even arbitrary individual-wire matrix gauges cannot improve the finest crossing-by-crossing norm bound for the specified alternating-sign words. These statements leave coupled packet metrics such as (3) available; they specify why choosing an inner product separately on each wire is insufficient.

The critical path is therefore the geometric bound on the composed coherent norm, followed by a lower/survival argument. Cauchy itself is the final elementary step, not another unproved analytic principle.
