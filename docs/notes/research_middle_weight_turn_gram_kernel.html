The exact middle-weight turn and its positive boundary Gram

21 November 2026. Research note. Earlier final notes and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The faithful middle-weight model has an explicit triangular change of coordinates: it is the transpose of the already known Newton crossing, with its common scalar removed. This identifies the genuine turn with a finite Euler kernel. Its four boundary rows and columns collapse by finite binomial identities, including the exact rows for both original closings. The turn also has a direct positive factorization: its product with index reversal is precisely the inverse of its boundary Gram.

The cyclic intertwiner itself is already present in the canonical-graph calculation. The purpose here is to identify it with the cyclic-column matrix of the middle-weight model and derive the resulting turn kernel and endpoint factors. No geometric contour or volume estimate follows from the change of coordinates alone.

6. The remaining analytic duty

The packet model is faithful to all repeated-root normals. The turn in (11)–(15) is the actual fixed-root turn, and (10) retains both original source rows. Its Newton version is unitary reversal; its physical version includes the explicit Euler and factorial frames. No bound for their changes of norm is inferred from that reversal.

The previously proved modular relation already reduces redundant twist/turn words. The regular-bend calculation already specifies the actual parameter-dependent cups and caps. Neither should be replaced by a new convention here. In particular, differentiating a generic quantum parameter requires its actual bend family; these fixed-root formulas alone do not give such derivatives.

What remains for the geometric application is to transport the complete marked contour through (12) or (13), including the strict shift endpoints and factorial frames, and prove the required estimate for the resulting actual packet and prescribed closing. An isolated endpoint norm or the finite sum does not prove that the selected geometric contribution survives an arbitrary short component.

Individual non-Hopf face factors still act as full middle-block matrices, in their original order. Only a whole Hopf box is identified with its polynomial packet. The source rows in (10) are not an extension of the all-array averaged physical trace to arbitrary inserted matrices.

The turn studied here acts on cyclic middle-weight packets. A recoupling matrix in a highest-weight multiplicity frame is a different typed operator until its boundary and source adapters have been supplied.
