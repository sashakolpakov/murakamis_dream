Independent audit of the relative whole-word Green map

Audit, 27 October 2026. The proof in relative_word_green_transport.md passes its weighted-source, Green-current and relative-map checks. No test rerun was required.

Transfer and cut number moments are exactly Euler derivatives of these weights.
