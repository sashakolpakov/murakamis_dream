A strict relative transport for the whole weighted word and its Green currents

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The actual complete finite word, with every intermediate head projector, has a strict Fock-relative extension. It carries the whole weighted generating family and hence every explicit least-moment Green current, rather than only the unweighted cut coupon. Its endpoint operators are specified exactly. Under Mellin and Rees transport those endpoints and all irregular projectors remain present.

This constructs an actual current/source map. It does not identify its coefficient class with the internal geometric Gaussian class. The final section isolates why the universal relative homotopy does not by itself prove that additional nonzero image.

1. Whole crossing couplings realize the actual index moments

For each crossing in a fixed arbitrary b-strand word introduce a unit coupling w_t. Multiply its literal transfer-k coefficient by w_t^k, with its original positive or negative crossing convention. Equivalently use the actual factorial P_Q(w_t Z_t) or the appropriate inverse factorial, in its fixed Cartan/flip order. At w_t=1 this is the original crossing. No braid identity away from that specialization is needed.

Introduce w_0^(n_cut) at the cut. All these coupled crossings still conserve the integer sum of positions. Thus the cut matrix is diagonal term by term, including before its remaining finite internal sums. Consequently the original normalized-average cut equals normalized ordinary trace for the entire weighted family:

F_N(w)=framing_line*(1/N)Tr_(H^tensor b)
   [(w_0^ncut tensor g_2 tensor ... tensor g_b)
              R_m^H(w_m)...R_1^H(w_1)].                   (1)

The original pivot, Cartan line, zero-framing scalar and full external modified dimension are retained. This is an exact finite polynomial in the weights. It is not an assumption of Ward closure of the coupled word.

The compact coherent source period therefore supplies its exact fixed compact cycle and star-trace calculus. Every weighted array and Green-current insertion below uses that same cycle. No identification of its coordinates with the internal geometric framing is assumed.

Put E_i=w_i partial_(w_i). Every actual moment polynomial p(k_0,k_1,...,k_m) is exactly p(E_0,E_1,...,E_m) applied to this family. In particular the actual Green binomials are product_i binom(E_i,alpha_i). These are whole-function coupling identities, not guessed extensions of a Taylor jet.

Here the closure is a knot: its braid permutation has one cycle. Following that cycle expresses every initial position as the cut position plus an integral linear combination of the crossing transfers. The intermediate positions are then integral affine functions of those same variables. Thus these weights account for every actual free-flow moment in the knot problem. For a link with several permutation cycles, retain one additional initial-position weight per other component before making that claim.

2. The exact Green currents are weight differential operators

Use the actual Green currents of the full certificate formula. For any finite zero-extended array f, the generating transform is

Z_w[f]=sum_k w^k f(k),
Z_w[S_i f]=w_i^(-1)Z_w[f],
Z_w[p(k)f]=p(E_w)Z_w[f].                    (2)

The first equality includes the shifted support and its endpoints. For the explicit Green word i_1,...,i_n representing D^beta, let p_(m-1) be its backward-difference moment polynomial. Then the generating current is exactly

J_i(w)=-sum_beta A_beta sum_(m:i_m=i)
   p_(m-1)(E_w-e_i)
      product_(l=m+1)^n(w_(i_l)^(-1)-1) F_N(w).            (3)

The Euler operator in (3) acts on the whole following product, including its weight coefficients. Moving it to act on F_N alone would drop the actual index-shift correction.

The global Green identity becomes the whole source identity

(-1)^h A_alpha F_N(w)
                   =sum_i(w_i^(-1)-1)J_i(w).              (4)

Thus the Koszul current complex of the actual zero-extended index differences maps strictly to the weight-current complex with differential given by the commuting factors w_i^(-1)-1. Equation (1) supplies its actual finite-word/source realization. Evaluation at w=1 gives the original full scalar annihilation.

3. A relative functor for actual maps, including nonintertwining projectors

Let an actual completed source space V carry its fixed Fock multiplication T_V. Define its relative source complex by

delta_V f=((partial_sigma-T_V)f,f(0),f(1)).

Its coefficients are completed so that exp(sigma T_V) is defined coefficientwise, as in the actual Mellin source theorem. For a continuous actual linear map O:V->W define the strict relative lift by

O_rel(sigma)=exp(sigma T_W) O exp(-sigma T_V)

in degree one and in the bulk of degree two. Its endpoint maps are O at zero and exp(T_W)Oexp(-T_V) at one. Direct differentiation proves

delta_W O_rel=O_rel delta_V.                 (5)

No commutation of O with the Fock operators is assumed. In particular this applies to every actual crossing and every head inclusion, projection and marked coupon.

For composable maps one has exactly

(O_2 O_1)_rel=(O_2)_rel(O_1)_rel.            (6)

Take T_(V tensor W)=T_V tensor I+I tensor T_W. The exponential then factors, so this transport also respects tensor products. Finite head objects and the scalar target have T=0; their actual pivotal caps and ordinary evaluation maps can be included without introducing an infinite trace. All finite head inclusions and projections remain explicit maps in this diagram.

The actual transgression obeys the exact naturality formula

L_W O_rel=O L_V.                            (7)

It follows either by multiplying the integrand by exp(-sigma T_W) or directly from its two endpoint terms. Thus this is a strict relative source transport through a complete arbitrary word.

4. Actual moments and head projectors have explicit endpoint lifts

On a block edge the actual number is n=j+N T B. Since [n,T]=N T, its relative lift is exactly

n_rel=n-N sigma T.

For every degree-h polynomial the lift is

p(n)_rel=p(n-N sigma T),

with the actual noncommutative operator order. It has sigma degree at most h, and its endpoint operators are p(n) and p(n-NT). Multiedge polynomials and the actual transfer differences follow by the tensor product and composition laws. Hence this construction carries all the polynomial moments in (3), not only their constant part or a top-degree projection.

The whole cut clock also has an exact formula. For a=w^N,

(w^n)_rel=w^j exp[sigma(1-a)T] D_a,
(D_a f)(t)=f(a t).                          (8)

This includes every coupling derivative and its finite endpoint carry. Differentiating (8) at w=1 recovers the displayed number lift, including its -N sigma T term.

For the actual head projection P=i pi_0, P T=0. Therefore

P_rel(sigma)=exp(sigma T)P,
P_rel(0)=P, P_rel(1)=exp(T)P.                (9)

It is idempotent at every sigma because P exp(sigma T)=P. Every intermediate projector in a word has its own factor (9). It is not replaced by identity and is not asserted to be a quantum-group intertwiner.

An explicit Cartan homotopy records its and every map’s change:

O_rel(sigma)-O
  =integral_0^sigma exp(t T_W)
                  (T_W O-O T_V)exp(-t T_V)dt.            (10)

This retains the inserted current rather than declaring a source invariant. For polynomial moments its normal-ordered expression is finite; the crossings and projector exponentials remain whole functions in the specified completion.

5. Mellin, whole-R and Rees transport commute with this construction

Under the actual Fock Mellin map T_V becomes multiplication by u=Log U; hence the endpoint in (9) is exactly UP. All co-source conjugations and normal rows in the Mellin theorem remain in (5)–(10). The exact normal-jet complex and its binomial endpoint terms are obtained by taking jets of these whole formulas.

The Rees maps have the form C=D_eta exp(-p u/d), so they commute with multiplication by u. They are therefore coefficientwise maps of these relative complexes. For an actual transformed internal projector, the lift is

(C P C^(-1))_rel(sigma)=exp[(sigma-p/d)u]P,                (11)

with the head eta flags retained in non-diagonal coupons. Its endpoint is exp[(1-p/d)u]P, not a regular vacuum.

The whole coupled crossings are exactly those of the whole-R source factorization, with its parameter t now the actual transfer weight. Both its regular cyclic factor and its specified resonant/Cartan period are kept; no separate root value of an infinite factor is taken.

Because the chosen source operators do not depend on the coupling weights, (5)–(7) commute with all Euler weight derivatives and Laurent weight factors in (3). Thus equations (1)–(4) transport to this actual relative/Fock/Mellin/Rees source diagram as exact cochain identities for a complete word, including every projector.

This does not discard the actual two-wall word curvature. It is carried as an ordinary coefficient map in the same functor. The constrained-word source construction supplies a finite algebraic realization of those coefficients; this relative lift adds their endpoint/current coherence rather than replacing them. Its differentials, inclusions and retractions can all be lifted by (5), degree by degree. Their exact chain identities remain exact by (6); no commutation with the original Fock multiplication is presumed.

6. An exact whole-source resummation shows what remains

Before taking any root limit, let the fully head-projected word be O_beta(w)=i_b B_beta^H(w) p_b. Under a common Rees conjugation its whole operator is exactly

C_b O_beta(w) C_b^(-1)
  =exp[-sum_i p_i u_i/d]
       D_eta B_beta^H(w) D_eta^(-1) P_b.     (12)

Its relative version has sigma added to every displayed source exponent. This resums the complete product of cyclic factors, canonical periods and all internal projectors. It does not leave an independently adjustable regular cyclic vacuum.

Applying the actual final source and co-source recovers the original weighted finite period, and hence every Green moment, exactly. But the coefficient in (12) is the original finite head word. The source exponent is irregular and the head flags remain. This identity does not evaluate the coefficient of its geometric stationary block.

There is a precise homological limitation. The relative complex is equivalent by its explicit transgression to its original coefficient space in degree two. Therefore the functor (5)–(7) preserves coefficient classes; it does not turn an unknown map from the geometric Gaussian module into a nonzero one. To preserve the geometric horizontal generator, that coefficient map still has to be constructed through the irregular projectors (11)–(12).

At the geometric end the exact two framing currents already computed in the full Green formula still have their marked endpoint/carry defect. The actual source diagram above specifies its own corresponding co-sources completely. What is not proved is that the Gaussian pushforward sends one set to the other with nonzero horizontal image. This is now a comparison of specified whole current/source objects, rather than an unspecified boundary normalization. No full scalar geometric factor or selected volume growth is concluded from this relative transport alone.

The compact source construction supplies the actual global cycle for the weighted family (1) and currents (3). The next geometric pushforward must commute with its exact star product and current maps while controlling degeneration or escape of the critical family at the compactified polar divisor. That statement has not been proved here.
