A common complex endpoint baseline with its original window currents

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The unturned microscopic kernel has a common polynomially bounded analytic baseline on every buffered bounded subdomain of 0<ℜx<1/20<\Re x<1/2. Exact representatives on the two half-domains retain both physical channels and differ from that baseline exponentially. Their real interface and every exterior current remain explicit. No extra complex characteristic-separation hypothesis is needed: the original balanced two-component step is already I+O(N−1)I+O(N^{-1}).

There is also a direct estimate for the entire positive-spectral-end window of a filled-leaf trace, including the derivative of the outgoing kernel. That window has arbitrarily small exponential cost. Neither result supplies the still-missing generic-parameter scalar seed for the filled leaf: the opposite endpoint and the coherent interface remain to be estimated at the terminal action.
