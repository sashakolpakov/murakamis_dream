A flat pointed torsor lifts; an etale classical optimizer need not

Independent proof and audit, 27 October 2026. This adds a constructive comparison formula and a flatness criterion to whole cyclotomic reconstruction. It also gives rational quantum objects for which a unique reduced classical pointed comparison fails to lift. Thus it distinguishes an actual sufficient theorem from an implication that classical Rivin rigidity alone does not supply.

The fixed-meridian Rees Poisson map and its new whole Heisenberg primitive are checked in Section 5. Section 6 gives a concrete actual crossing obstruction to a naive positive weighted-norm proof. None of the statements below asserts the selected volume asymptotic.

1. A flat marked comparison has exactly one lift

Let R=k[[ϵ]]R=k[[\epsilon]], with kk of characteristic zero. Suppose a whole, bounded algebraic comparison problem has been specified, including its sources, cuts and lattice. Complete its comparison algebra at a chosen classical point and call the resulting RR-algebra BB. Assume

B/ϵB=k,⋂n≥0ϵnB=0,ϵ is a nonzero divisor on B. B/\epsilon B=k,\qquad \bigcap_{n\geq 0}\epsilon^nB=0, \qquad \epsilon\text{ is a nonzero divisor on }B. \tag{1}

Then R→BR\longrightarrow B is an isomorphism. In particular the marked comparison exists uniquely to every formal quantum order.

Here is a direct proof. Given b∈Bb\in B, choose its residue c0∈kc_0\in k and write b=c0+ϵb1b=c_0+\epsilon b_1. Repeat this for b1b_1, obtaining b−∑j<ncjϵj∈ϵnB. b-\sum_{j<n}c_j\epsilon^j\in\epsilon^nB. The element f=∑cjϵj∈Rf=\sum c_j\epsilon^j\in R therefore maps to bb, by separatedness. Thus R→BR\to B is surjective. A nonzero element of its kernel would be ϵm\epsilon^m times a unit, contradicting the last condition of (1) and B/ϵB≠0B/\epsilon B\ne0. This proves the assertion.

Over this DVR, the last condition is exactly RR-flatness. A finite separable marked classical residue field is handled after its specified unramified base extension. With a finite algebraic presentation, this is a statement about its selected algebraic formal germ; it does not assert that an arbitrary formal period is algebraic.

The same result retains several normal directions. Let RR be any complete Noetherian local ring, with maximal ideal 𝔪\mathfrak m and residue field kk. If BB is separated for 𝔪B\mathfrak mB, is RR-flat, and B/𝔪B=kB/\mathfrak mB=k through the specified residue map, then B=RB=R. Choose generators of 𝔪\mathfrak m and repeat the residue argument by total degree; completeness of RR and separatedness of BB give B=R/IB=R/I, I⊆𝔪I\subseteq\mathfrak m. Flatness, applied to 0→I→R0\to I\to R and tensoring with BB, forces I/I2=0I/I^2=0. Since II is finitely generated and I=I2⊆𝔪II=I^2\subseteq\mathfrak mI, Nakayama gives I=0I=0. Thus quantum and meridian normals can be retained together.

There is a useful converse formulation. The first two conditions already imply B=R/IB=R/I. If comparisons exist modulo ϵn\epsilon^n for every nn, the maps R/I→R/ϵnR/I\to R/\epsilon^n give I⊆(ϵn)I\subseteq(\epsilon^n) for all nn; hence I=0I=0. No additional choice of compatible lifts is needed once the pointed comparison is unique. Comparisons at several different cyclotomic levels become such a test only after a fixed whole presentation and the reconstruction hypotheses have been supplied.

This is a genuine existence theorem for a flat whole pointed comparison. Flatness of the two objects separately is a different condition.

2. Cyclic pointing constructs the only possible comparison

Here the comparison need not be guessed. Let AA be a coefficient algebra with an automorphism σ\sigma. Let Vi=ArV_i=A^r carry the semilinear transport Ti(v)=Aiσ(v)T_i(v)=A_i\sigma(v), source sis_i, and cut ℓi\ell_i, for i=1,2i=1,2. Form the cyclic matrices

Vicyc=[si,Tisi,…,Tir−1si]. V_i^{\mathrm{cyc}}=[s_i,T_i s_i,\ldots,T_i^{r-1}s_i].

Assume their determinants are units on the marked lattice chart. Define

C=V2cyc(V1cyc)−1,ai=(Vicyc)−1Tirsi,mi=ℓiVicyc. C=V_2^{\mathrm{cyc}}(V_1^{\mathrm{cyc}})^{-1},\qquad a_i=(V_i^{\mathrm{cyc}})^{-1}T_i^r s_i,\qquad m_i=\ell_i V_i^{\mathrm{cyc}}. \tag{2}

Then CC and its inverse are regular on that chart, and

CT1=T2C,Cs1=s2,ℓ2C=ℓ1⇔a1=a2,m1=m2. CT_1=T_2C,\quad Cs_1=s_2,\quad\ell_2C=\ell_1 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a_1=a_2,\quad m_1=m_2. \tag{3}

Moreover CC is the only pointed transport comparison.

Indeed every such comparison must send each T1js1T_1^j s_1 to T2js2T_2^j s_2, which forces (2). Its transport residual vanishes on the first r−1r-1 cyclic vectors. On the last vector it vanishes exactly when their last cyclic relations aia_i agree. Equivalently, Aiσ(Vicyc)=VicycComp⁡(ai); A_i\sigma(V_i^{\mathrm{cyc}})=V_i^{\mathrm{cyc}}\operatorname{Comp}(a_i); substituting (2) proves the transport identity. The cut identity is exactly equality of the two rows mim_i.

Consequently, near a regular cyclic classical comparison, its whole comparison algebra is B=R/I,I=(entries of a1−a2,m1−m2), B=R/I,\qquad I=(\text{entries of }a_1-a_2,\ m_1-m_2), \tag{4} with additional supplied connection residuals included if required. This makes the flatness defect an explicit finite obstruction ideal. Classical agreement says only I⊆(ϵ)I\subseteq(\epsilon); flatness says I=0I=0.

If the objects and these finitely many iterates have a fixed bounded algebraic presentation, the cyclotomic jet theorem tests the residuals in (3), after denominators and algebraic norms are handled in the stated lattice. Thus a regular cyclic source gives a canonical comparison and a finite exact certificate. There is no unknown unbounded family of levelwise gauges in this construction.

The hypotheses are substantive: the rank must be fixed for a uniform test, both cyclic determinants must be units, and the whole transports and pointings must already exist. Generic cyclicity away from a source boundary does not imply these unit conditions. The full finite NN-dimensional module and a rank-one local geometric block are also not interchangeable objects.

For compatible differential period connections, horizontality can follow from this uniqueness. If the connections commute with transport, and the two source connection laws agree under CC, then E=∇2C−C∇1E=\nabla_2C-C\nabla_1 commutes with transport and kills the source. It kills every cyclic vector, so E=0E=0. For the mixed quantum and meridian law, the extra meridian intertwining in the whole comparison theorem, Section 5 is required. This argument does not construct either connection.

3. Two explicit failures of classical-to-quantum lifting

Flat objects with a nonflat pointed comparison

Take rank-one free RR-modules, with transports T1=1T_1=1 and T2=1+ϵT_2=1+\epsilon, and sources s1=s2=1s_1=s_2=1. Both objects are flat. A source-preserving comparison is forced to be C=1C=1, so its remaining equation is ϵ=0\epsilon=0. Its comparison algebra is R/(ϵ)R/(\epsilon).

There is one reduced classical pointed comparison and no quantum lift. The same example works for meridian-difference transports T1=σT_1=\sigma, T2=(1+ϵ)σT_2=(1+\epsilon)\sigma over k(Y)[[ϵ]]k(Y)[[\epsilon]], with σ(Y)=eϵY\sigma(Y)=e^\epsilon Y. It has the same classical graph L=1L=1, etale over YY. Thus etaleness of that graph and flatness of the objects do not imply flatness of their pointed comparison.

Even the unpointed algebraic gauge can fail

Put Q=eϵQ=e^\epsilon, σ(Y)=QY\sigma(Y)=QY, and compare the rank-one transport multipliers a1(Q,Y)=1,a2(Q,Y)=1+Q−1Y. a_1(Q,Y)=1,\qquad a_2(Q,Y)=1+\frac{Q-1}{Y}. \tag{5} They are rational whole functions, regular formally at Q=1Q=1, with the same classical etale graph L=1L=1. An algebraic formal unit solving the gauge equation σg=a2g\sigma g=a_2g, with leading term g0g_0, would satisfy Y∂Yg0=g0/Y,dlog⁡g0=dY/Y2. Y\partial_Yg_0=g_0/Y,\qquad d\log g_0=dY/Y^2. \tag{6} This is impossible on every finite algebraic cover. A logarithmic derivative of an algebraic meromorphic function has only simple poles. At a place Y=teY=t^e, the right side of (6) has pole order e+1>1e+1>1. The analytic solution g0=exp⁡(−1/Y)g_0=\exp(-1/Y) demonstrates precisely the excluded irregularity.

Multiplying both multipliers by any common nonzero rational a0(Y)a_0(Y) gives the same failure over the graph L=a0(Y)L=a_0(Y). The issue is therefore not the particular constant classical graph. At first quantum order, the difference of subprincipal transport defines the necessary logarithmic connection (6); classical etaleness does not trivialize that connection.

The full cyclic norm detects the failure exactly: ∏j=0N−1a2(QN,QNjY)=1−(−QN−1Y)N≠1. \prod_{j=0}^{N-1}a_2(Q_N,Q_N^jY) =1-\left(-\frac{Q_N-1}{Y}\right)^N\ne1. \tag{7} For fixed Y≠0Y\ne0, its difference from one is smaller than every power of 1/N1/N. Thus even this rational transport produces a curvature error invisible to its asymptotic 1/N1/N expansion. It is visible to the exact whole cyclotomic test.

In the opposite direction, the independently proved full rank-one descent theorem does give a positive result: a comparison multiplier in ℚ(Q,Y)*\mathbb Q(Q,Y)^*, with exact whole norm one at every sufficiently large root, has one rational gauge. Other coefficient fields require the whole-root marking in that theorem. Its source anchor fixes the residual scalar, provided the gauge and inverse are regular on the prescribed source lattice. Equations (5)–(7) explain why the whole norm hypothesis cannot be replaced by a classical optimizer or an all-orders asymptotic norm identity.

4. The actual Rees source has a concrete regular-lifting obstruction

The actual Rees construction has block clock DZf(u)=f(Zu)D_Zf(u)=f(Zu), Z=QNZ=Q^N, and G−1DZG=eu/NDZ,G=eu/[N(Z−1)]. G^{-1}D_ZG=e^{u/N}D_Z,\qquad G=e^{u/[N(Z-1)]}. \tag{8} The original vacuum is fixed by DZD_Z.

There is no regular clock intertwiner sending this vacuum to a nonzero regular limiting vector. For such an intertwiner CC, applying (8) to the vacuum and reducing at Z=1Z=1 would give (eu/N−1)C0(1)=0in k[[u]], (e^{u/N}-1)\,C_0(1)=0 \quad\text{in }k[[u]], so C0(1)=0C_0(1)=0. Allowing mixing among head clocks does not help: the equations become (QNjeu/N−1)fj(u)=0(Q_N^j e^{u/N}-1)f_j(u)=0, and each factor is a nonzero divisor. The exact irregular gauge instead sends the source to e−u/[N(Z−1)]e^{-u/[N(Z-1)]}, as retained in that note.

This is not a claim that two identical whole-center families are inequivalent: their normal central characters differ, and the source gauge is explicitly irregular. It is a concrete warning that a regular generator-module limit and matching closed-point data do not produce a regular pointed lift through the source boundary. The newly regular Rees module has not silently made the comparison algebra flat.

6. Scalarity does not make a positive weighted norm sharp

There is an actual crossing example, requiring no speculative analytic sector. Close one leg of a positive checked crossing at complete color M=1M=1. This is the framed unknot kink. Write Q=e2πi/N,q=eπi/N,x=−q−1,Pa(Q)=(Q;Q)a. Q=e^{2\pi i/N},\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad x=-q^{-1}, \qquad P_a(Q)=(Q;Q)_a. The exact finite identity ∑b=0aQb2Pa(Q)2Pb(Q)2Pa−b(Q)=1 \sum_{b=0}^{a} Q^{b^2}\frac{P_a(Q)^2}{P_b(Q)^2P_{a-b}(Q)}=1 \tag{10} can be proved without importing a knot formula. Partitions with largest part at most aa have generating function 1/Pa1/P_a. Separate them by their Durfee square of size bb. This proves (10) as a formal rational identity, hence at every root with a<Na<N. The exact kink scalar is x1−Nx^{1-N}, of modulus one.

Nevertheless, if 4∣N4\mid N, a=N/2,b=N/4a=N/2,b=N/4, the modulus of this one closed term is P(N/2)2P(N/4)3,P(m)=∏r=1m2sin⁡(πr/N). \frac{P(N/2)^2}{P(N/4)^3},\qquad P(m)=\prod_{r=1}^{m}2\sin(\pi r/N). Every internal diagonal basis weight cancels around this closed term; a normalized cut basis vector and its dual cancel their external weight too. Thus any bound obtained by replacing all entries by absolute values, including products of induced coordinate weighted norms, is at least this exponentially large term. It cannot be sharp for the unit scalar (10).

This does not rule out a specially chosen wavepacket or a phase-sensitive propagation theorem. For this kink, the cut a=0a=0 is already cancellation-free. It does show why scalarity alone and strict concavity of a classical shape functional do not supply the required sharp positive norm for an arbitrary normalized source. A useful coherent-state argument must prove its phase-sensitive propagation and stability through the actual crossings and caps, rather than infer them from the algebraic optimizer.

7. The concrete whole-torsor task

There is now a precise positive route: construct the two whole pointed objects, prove regular cyclicity on their marked lattices, and evaluate the finite residuals (3). A flat comparison algebra, or sufficiently deep exact cyclotomic tests in the fixed presentation, then gives the unique comparison; a compatible marked connection gives horizontality.

Rivin rigidity supplies the unique algebraic geometric place. The new Rees–Gauss identity supplies a fixed algebraic period connection on actual source coordinates. Neither presently implies the comparison-flatness or removes the irregular projectors. Those are explicit equations and source conditions to prove. Even after that algebraic comparison, the physical large-level norm requires a calibrated nonzero selected contribution and its growth estimate.
