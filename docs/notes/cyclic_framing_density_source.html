The prefix-adapted framing density has an exact cyclotomic source factor

Research calculation, 27 October 2026. This computes an actual finite root-filter functional on the rational density supplied by the prefix-adapted framing chart. The result keeps an explicit power character and its cyclic norm. It is not a proof that this root filter is the original source after geometric Gaussian pushforward. That interchange remains unproved.

1. The density, with the physical prefix retained

Fix the complete parabolic meridian mark. Use signed developed prefix products U,V from the actual braid-to-Gauss dictionary; the physical inverse position is k=ell_U/ell_V. On the framing torsor write

P = [[r, r(1-r)/s], [s,1-r]],
ell_W = Tr(W P),
A=1/(1+s), B=1/k.

For a matrix W write c_W=W_21 and h_W=W_11-W_22. The equation ell_(U-kV)=0 is quadratic in r. In the regular quadratic field put

D = partial_(r/s) ell_(U-kV).

The actual framing two-form is

Omega = ell_V/(B D) dlog A wedge dlog B.        (1.1)

Its field trace over the two-position base is

Tr Omega = T(B) dlog A wedge dlog B,
T(B) = W B/(c_U B-c_V)^2,
W = c_V h_U-h_V c_U.                          (1.2)

In particular it need not be zero. The earlier special case V=I had W=0 and an odd unweighted density; that case cannot be used for an arbitrary physical edge. The chart note proves (1.1)–(1.2) from the actual prefix dictionary.

For completeness the elementary trace calculation is short. Put tau=r/s, C=U-kV, and write

ell_C = -s c_C tau^2+(c_C+s h_C)tau+d_C+s b_C.

At its two simple roots the values of D are opposite. Reducing the quadratic ell_V modulo ell_C, or subtracting its two root values, gives

Tr(ell_V/D) = (c_V h_C-h_V c_C)/c_C^2
            = W/(c_U-k c_V)^2.

Multiplication by 1/B gives (1.2). Notice that the trace coefficient has no remaining s dependence.

The coefficients in (1.2) are intrinsic marked character functions. With the reference cut meridian J=[[1,1],[0,1]], direct multiplication gives

c_W = Tr(J W)-Tr(W),
W = Tr(J(VU-UV)).                             (1.3)

Thus c_U,c_V,W, and their ratios below are rational trace-word functions of the marked representation. Conjugating while retaining J does not change them. Equivalently its residual unipotent centralizer fixes c_W and changes h_W by a common multiple of c_W, which cancels from W. They live on the marked character cover, not in the free framing-coordinate field. On a geometric component generically finite over the peripheral curve, they are algebraic over its A-curve field. The identities here concern the complete parabolic slice; no independent full holonomy deformation is imposed.

2. Exact root averaging is a derivative of a cyclic norm

Suppose c_U!=0, W!=0, and set

lambda=c_V/c_U, gamma=W/c_U^2,
T(B)=gamma B/(B-lambda)^2.                    (2.1)

Let Q be a primitive N-th root and assume lambda^N!=1. Define the finite algebraic functional

A_N(T) = (1/N) sum_(b=0)^(N-1) T(Q^b).

Then, EXACTLY,

A_N(T) = gamma N lambda^(N-1)/(1-lambda^N)^2.  (2.2)

No limiting operation or quantum expansion is involved in (2.2). To prove it, first use the literal cyclic norm

product_(b=0)^(N-1)(1-lambda Q^(-b))
                   = 1-lambda^N.             (2.3)

With theta=lambda partial_lambda, direct differentiation gives

T(B)=-(gamma/lambda) theta^2 log(1-lambda/B).

Applying theta twice to log (2.3) proves (2.2). Logarithms are only notation for logarithmic derivatives of rational functions here; no branch of a logarithm is needed. Equivalently the ordinary partial-fraction identity for B^N-1 proves the same formula.

The case lambda=0 is taken directly: T(B)=gamma/B and A_N(T)=0 for N>1. Formula (2.2) is then interpreted by its polynomial limit. The displayed rate assertions below assume lambda!=0.

This also has a WHOLE off-root representative. Before setting Q^N=1 retain

P_N(Q,lambda)=product_(b=0)^(N-1)(1-lambda Q^(-b)).

For arbitrary nonzero Q, away from the specified poles,

(1/N)sum_b T(Q^b)
    = -gamma/(N lambda) theta^2 log P_N.       (2.4)

Only after taking the actual whole derivatives may P_N specialize to (2.3). Its Q derivative is

Q partial_Q log P_N
  = sum_b b lambda Q^(-b)/(1-lambda Q^(-b)).   (2.5)

Specializing (2.3) first and differentiating it would incorrectly erase this current. The exact finite product retains the quantum normal, its moments, and its source factors.

3. A nonzero rational density can acquire an exponential source weight

At any fixed complex lambda with 0<|lambda|!=1 and gamma!=0, (2.2) implies

lim_(N->infinity) (1/N)log|A_N(T)|
                     = -|log|lambda||.        (3.1)

For |lambda|<1 the denominator tends to one, and the numerator has rate log|lambda|. For |lambda|>1 factor lambda^(2N) out of the denominator; the resulting rate is -log|lambda|.

There is no contradiction with the polynomial bounds for a fixed algebraic coefficient on one controlled arc. A_N has a growing cyclic norm and power character; it is not evaluation of one fixed algebraic function of Q near one. Nor does (3.1) prove that the actual geometric coefficient is exponentially small. The source pairing has not been shown to commute with this field trace and principal-density sampling. It does show that such an interchange cannot be declared harmless merely because T is rational and pointwise nonzero.

Ordinary B residues do not replace this functional either. The one-form T(B)dlog B=gamma dB/(B-lambda)^2 is exact and has zero ordinary residues. Its finite cyclic average (2.2) is nevertheless nonzero. The marked source and its cyclic norm supply information which absolute de Rham exactness would lose.

4. A calibration on the actual figure-eight representation chart

The prefix chart note constructs a fixed triple for the actual mixed braid (1,-2,1,-2), over u^2+u+1=0. For its third physical strand the signed developed prefix products reduce to

V = [[1,1],[-u,1-u]],
U = [[u+1,2u+1],[1-u,3-u]].

These are determinant-one matrices. The chart computation gives

W=2u+1,  lambda=1/(u+2),  gamma=-lambda.

Consequently (2.2) becomes

A_N(T)=-N lambda^N/(1-lambda^N)^2,             (4.1)
|lambda|=1/sqrt(3).

At either marked geometric embedding its rate is therefore -log(3)/2. This is a calibration of the exact density functional, not a recomputation of Kashaev’s figure-eight invariant. In particular it must not be inserted as a new prefactor in that invariant. A source-preserving comparison must instead explain how this character and its dual/source normalization are transported. The example tests a general prefix formula; the conjecture target remains unrestricted hyperbolic knots.

The symbolic audit checks the cyclic norm, an independent companion-matrix trace, the whole off-root current, and the exact figure-eight field values.
