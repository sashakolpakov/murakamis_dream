A fixed interior cut for the original physical scalar

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The original unmarked invariant can be evaluated at one interior cut occupation, instead of at the highest vector. The exact Ward current between the two probes has polynomial size in the level, including every fixed meridian derivative on a specified shrinking disk. In the existing classical cut chart, an interior occupation selects a noncentral complete meridian. This provides a possible regular presentation of the same scalar. It does not identify its stationary scheme or cycle with that of the highest-cut Borel source.

1. Exact finite cut identities

2. A polynomial bound for the current and its meridian jets

Declare the factorial basis orthonormal for this estimate. A matrix with one weighted subdiagonal has operator norm the largest weight. At M=1M=1, |ϵr(1)|=2|sinπ(r+1)N|≥4N.(7) |\epsilon_r(1)|= 2\left|\sin\frac{\pi(r+1)}N\right|\ge\frac4N . \qquad\text{(7)} Here the minimum occurs at an end of 1,…,N−11,\ldots,N-1, and sin⁡x≥2x/π\sin x\ge2x/\pi on [0,π/2][0,\pi/2]. For |M−1|≤1/(4N)|M-1|\le1/(4N), one has |M|≥7/8|M|\ge7/8 and |ϵr(M)−ϵr(1)|≤|M−1|+|M−1−1|<3|M−1|≤34N.(8) |\epsilon_r(M)-\epsilon_r(1)| \le |M-1|+|M^{-1}-1| <3|M-1|\le\frac{3}{4N}. \qquad\text{(8)} Thus all denominators in (3) are bounded below by 3/N3/N on that disk, independently of aa, and ∥BN,a(M)∥≤N/3.(9) \|B_{N,a}(M)\|\le N/3 . \qquad\text{(9)} On the smaller disk |M−1|≤1/(8N)|M-1|\le1/(8N), the Cauchy formula therefore gives, for every fixed j≥0j\ge0, ∥∂MjBN,a(M)∥≤CjNj+1.(10) \|\partial_M^jB_{N,a}(M)\| \le C_j N^{j+1}. \qquad\text{(10)} The constants do not depend on aa or NN. The estimates concern the current, not a relative error in a scalar pairing. In particular they do not bound (5) relative to the possibly small value Tr⁡(T0CNmark)\operatorname{Tr}(T_0 C_N^{\mathrm{ mark}}).

3. One fixed bulk choice has a regular complete meridian

Choose once and for all 0<θ<10<\theta<1, and take aN=⌊θN⌋(11) a_N=\lfloor\theta N\rfloor \qquad\text{(11)} for sufficiently large NN. The midpoint choice θ=1/2\theta=1/2 is available at both parities. The exact identities (2)–(6) apply for every such level.

The discrete position is AN=QaNA_N=Q^{a_N}. In the actual regular classical cut chart of [M,J], the position and framing coordinates satisfy Acut=MM+s,AN→Aθ=e2πiθ.(12) A_{\mathrm{ cut}}=\frac{M}{M+s},\qquad A_N\longrightarrow A_\theta=e^{2\pi i\theta}. \qquad\text{(12)} Thus the selected classical position is Acut=AθA_{\mathrm{ cut}}=A_\theta. This statement does not drop any finite Weyl shift from the matrices in Section 1. At the complete value M=1M=1, it selects sθ=e−2πiθ−1≠0.(13) s_\theta=e^{-2\pi i\theta}-1\ne0 . \qquad\text{(13)} On the existing pcut=1p_{\mathrm{ cut}}=1 framing chart, its meridian is μsθ=(1+sθ−sθsθ1−sθ).(14) \mu_{s_\theta} =\begin{pmatrix}1+s_\theta&-s_\theta\\ s_\theta&1-s_\theta\end{pmatrix}. \qquad\text{(14)} This is noncentral parabolic: its determinant is one, its trace is two, and μsθ−I≠0\mu_{s_\theta}-I\ne0. The frame F(r,s)=(r1−rs−s),μs=F−1(1101)F(15) F(r,s)=\begin{pmatrix}r&1-r\\s&-s\end{pmatrix},\qquad \mu_s=F^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}F \qquad\text{(15)} has determinant −s-s. With ss near sθs_\theta and rr in a fixed compact set, both this matrix and its inverse are bounded independently of the level.

Thus the selected noncentral geometric framing itself need not escape merely because the complete eigenvalues coincide. This is an explicit change of the finite cut presentation, not a regularization of all matrices in the old highest-cut chart. It does not put every source configuration or the remaining centralizer coordinate rr in a compact set.

4. What remains to justify in the new presentation

Equations (2)–(11) justify using the fixed interior probe to compute the original unmarked invariant. Its cut occupation grows with NN. It is therefore a new semiclassical source presentation; it is not the fixed-occupation hh-expansion of the old highest-cut germ.

At a known geometric critical configuration, (12) fixes the coadjoint framing parameter ss, leaving the centralizer parameter rr. Fixing a cut occupation also removes a stationarity equation. The equivalence of the entire new critical scheme with the original geometric representation scheme has not followed from (2). The exact interior Ward recurrence is available, but turning it into that scheme or a relative-chain map requires its actual source analysis.

Likewise (5) retains every marked defect. It cannot be used to carry a previously defined marked Borel period unchanged into the new presentation. A new phase, amplitude, endpoint analysis and normalization must be derived from its literal coefficient extraction. No geometric coefficient or volume limit is claimed.
