Actual ordinary-color q-jets: one-volume growth and a macroscopic tame source

28 October 2026. The ordinary-color VALUES are polynomial at q_N, but their q-normal derivatives need not be. For the actual figure-eight state, the first derivative at color N-1 has the sharp volume exponent, with an explicit positive formula and leading constant. Nevertheless EVERY fixed-order q-jet, uniformly over all ordinary colors, has at most that ONE-volume exponent, not two volumes.

A separate positive-plat argument gives a genuinely broad source filtration: every fixed q-jet is polynomial for colors k(j-1)+1<=N in a fixed k-bridge presentation. This includes the entire figure-eight half-orbit and its midpoint. It permits restarting the actual fixed-budget pointed recurrence at a macroscopic color, with polynomial data and no input from the complete value.

1. The actual whole-source cyclotomic polynomial

Use the independently proved ordinary-color identity

C_j(q)=sum_(k=0)^(j-1) T_(j,k)(q),
T_(j,k)=product_(r=1)^k
                (q^(2j)+q^(-2j)-q^(2r)-q^(-2r)). (1)

Its reduced unknot normalization, crossing Cartan phases and zero framing are the original ones. At roots (1) is the actual Newton state of the pointed Green theorem; for fixed j the generic Laurent identity follows from the independently identified ordinary quotient at infinitely many levels. Thus (1) is not fitted to K_N.

Put q_N=exp(pi i/N), Q=q_N^2, F_k=(Q;Q)_k and

J(s)=integral_0^s log(2sin(pi t))dt,
gamma=max J=J(5/6), Delta=2gamma=Vol(4_1)/(2pi).

All q-derivatives below keep the integer color j FIXED. They are not meridian derivatives or derivatives along a changing root level.

2. A sharp exact first jet at the top ordinary color

Write D_q=q d/dq. For every N>=3,

(D_q C_(N-1))(q_N)
   =-2N(Q-Q^-1) sum_(k=1)^(N-2)
                     |F_(k-1) F_(k+1)|/|F_2|.     (2)

Every summand is POSITIVE real before the displayed common phase. Indeed all nonconstant T_(N-1,k) contain the root-zero factor r=1. Its derivative is -2N(Q-Q^-1). Every remaining factor is

Q+Q^-1-Q^r-Q^-r
   =4sin(pi(r+1)/N)sin(pi(r-1)/N)>0,
                2<=r<=N-2.

Multiplication gives precisely the factorial quotient in (2), with the k=1 empty product equal to one. This is a derivative of the COMPLETE original ordinary state, rather than an individual large summand. Its value C_(N-1)(q_N)=1 does not control this jet.

The exact leading asymptotic, through ALL integer levels, is

(D_q C_(N-1))(q_N)
    =-i N^(7/2)/(pi 3^(1/4))
                     exp(N Delta)(1+o(1)).        (3)

Proof. Uniformly on compact 0<s<1 the elementary factorial estimate is

|F_k|=sqrt(2Nsin(pi k/N)) exp(NJ(k/N))(1+O(1/N)),

and its all-index version has logarithmic error O(log N). Also |F_2|=8pi2/N2 (1+O(N^-2)). Thus the summand in (2), at s=k/N, is

[N^3 sin(pi s)/(4pi^2)] exp(2NJ(s))(1+O(1/N)).

The exponent has its unique global maximum at s=5/6 and second derivative -2pi sqrt(3). Endpoint-uniform estimates discard its complement exponentially. Rescale k=5N/6+sqrt(N)xi. The lattice Gaussian sum has integral 3^-1/4, with arbitrary fractional part of 5N/6. Since sin(5pi/6)=1/2, its sum is

N^(7/2)/(8pi^2 3^(1/4)) exp(NDelta)(1+o(1)).

Finally -2N(Q-Q^-1)=-8pi i(1+O(N^-2)). This proves (3). At N=2 the only ordinary color is trivial and the first jet is zero.

3. Uniform ONE-volume upper bound for every fixed q-jet

For every fixed nonnegative integer r there are constants A,C_r independent of N and j such that

|[t^r] C_j(q_N(1+t))|
               <=C_r N^(A+4r) exp(NDelta), 1<=j<N. (4)

The same assertion holds for logarithmic derivatives D_q^r, with a different fixed C_r. It controls the ENTIRE fixed-order ordinary jet vector, up to polynomial dimension factors. In particular fixed jets do not accumulate a two-volume rate in this example.

Here is a direct all-color proof, including activated root zeros. For each factor in (1), at q_N,

f_(j,r)=-4sin(pi(j+r)/N)sin(pi(j-r)/N).

For r<j<N it is zero only when j+r=N, and there is at most ONE such factor in a product. Every nonzero factor has magnitude at least 16/N^2. Every fixed-order derivative of a factor is bounded by C_r N^r. Repeated Leibniz differentiation therefore costs at most C_r N^(4r) times the product of all NONZERO root factors: there are at most N^r choices of differentiated factors, at most r removed nonzero factors, and derivative powers cost N^r. An undifferentiated zero simply makes that term zero.

It remains to bound these root products, not to assume them tame. If k<min(j,N-j), their exact magnitude is

|T_(j,k)(q_N)|
    =|F_(j+k)|/[|1-Q^j| |F_(j-k-1)|].             (5)

Its logarithm is at most N(max J-min J)+O(log N)=NDelta+O(log N). If j>N/2 and k>=m=N-j, the zero at r=m is removed; the remaining absolute product is EXACTLY

|F_(k+m) F_(k-m)|/[|1-Q^m| |1-Q^(2m)|].          (6)

All factorial indices are in 0,…,N-1 and 2m<N. Since each J is at most gamma and the two denominators are bounded below by 4/N, (6) has the same exponential upper bound. Equations (5)–(6), the all-index factorial estimate and the additional sum over k prove (4). This proof does not rely on cancellations or an ordinary-jet bound which is being inferred from the desired volume theorem.

4. A broad polynomial window, including the midpoint

The independent positive-plat/source proof in the macroscopic normal-jet window gives, for ANY fixed knot with a k-bridge plat and every fixed r,

|[t^r]C_j(q_N(1+t))|<=C_(K,k,r) N^(c_(K,k,r))
                 whenever k(j-1)+1<=N.           (7)

The inclusive endpoint is important. Its elementary inputs are:

  • The actual endpoint-zero TL path space for the 2k(j-1)-strand cable has maximum height k(j-1). On the one-sided arc theta in [pi/(2N),pi/N), all required sine weights are positive.
  • Actual colored cups have squared norm [j]^k, and the reduced original closure divides by exactly [j], giving the generic-arc bound |C_j(exp(i theta))|<=[j]_theta^(k-1).
  • The actual zero-framed finite cut is Laurent in q^(1/2) of degree O_K(j^2). Clearing its denominator after a tangent-half-angle substitution makes an ordinary polynomial on an interval of length comparable to 1/N; the clearing factor varies by O_K(1). Elementary Chebyshev coefficient/derivative bounds give polynomial fixed derivatives, including at the one-sided endpoint.

The proof takes the invariant polynomial to the endpoint, rather than differentiating a singular path basis or a zero quantum dimension. For k>=2 the relevant ordinary [j] is nonzero.

For the figure-eight, k=2. Thus every fixed q-jet is polynomial uniformly through j=floor((N+1)/2). In particular the midpoint has NO positive exponential jet rate. No matching positive lower rate is claimed there: a particular derivative may vanish. On the other hand (3) proves sharp one-volume growth near the far endpoint. The polynomial source filtration therefore separates actual tame half-orbit data from exponentially large far normal response.

5. Restart the ACTUAL pointed system without using C_N

Assume k>=2; the one-bridge unknot is already identically one. Let an actual all-level annihilator have order d and pole budget B as in the pointed Gram theorem. Define

J_N=1+floor((N-1)/k),
j0=J_N-d+1.

For large N, j0 is beyond the fixed exceptional starting colors. Prescribe the actual B-jets of C_(j0),…,C_(j0+d-1), all inside the polynomial window (7), and impose the SAME all-level recurrence through color N. This produces an exact tail pointed system with polynomial source supported in its initial d blocks.

The original valuation proof still makes its constant values unique: the sum of leading-coefficient zero orders over this TAIL is no larger than the original uniform budget B. Subtraction of any two solutions with zero prescribed initial jets therefore leaves every constant coefficient zero. Nothing in that induction requires a starting color fixed independently of N; the bounded source was the missing quantitative input, now supplied by (7).

After the same jet scaling the tail bulk symbol and all normal resonance data are unchanged. Its starting coordinate tends to 1/k, rather than zero. This is an actual source-preserving improvement, not a definition using an unknown target value. A tail Green/transfer estimate still needs its singular-step and spectral control. In particular a general sharp Vol conclusion does not follow merely from this restart, nor from the figure-eight calculation (3)–(4).

Audit: actual cyclotomic Taylor jets, all-color product identities, the positive endpoint formula and leading constant, and midpoint/tail growth diagnostics. Finite checks supplement the all-level proofs.
