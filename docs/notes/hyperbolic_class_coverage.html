Coverage audit for the hyperbolic-knot target

29 October: fixed long fillings. A further primary-source coverage check distinguished our fixed-twist, N-to-infinity statement from Lee, 2307.09903: its abstract first passes to a skein/categorical limit as twist counts tend to infinity and then relates the limiting evaluation to octahedral volumes. The abstract of Galakhov et al. concerns a conjectured explicit formula for colored polynomials of arbitrary pretzels. 1101.2844 concerns noncommutative A-polynomial computations for the restricted (-2,3,n) family and numerical volume-conjecture work. These scope checks supply no proof premise to the present full-source estimates or marked representation-volume comparison. They also do not justify a claim of novelty or an exhaustive absence of overlapping results.

29 October: augmented-base coverage check. The new complete augmented pretzel period proof is an independent original-source calculation, not a novelty claim. The abstract of van der Veen’s augmented knotted trivalent graph paper states volume-conjecture coverage for augmented KTGs and an arithmetic containing-link corollary. The abstract and introductory scope of Ibarra–McQuire–Purcell concern OCTAHEDRAL fully augmented links and a Turaev–Viro result without half twists; the published introduction also records earlier Kashaev coverage for octahedral fully augmented links. These are coverage statements, not proof inputs to the new finite-source, recurrence or Gaussian arguments. They do not by themselves classify the exact overlap with every non-octahedral s-antiprism base here. No claim that this base family was previously uncovered is made. Its complete augmented links must also be distinguished from the fixed-slope filled knots, which the new base theorem does not settle.

Research record, 25 October 2026. Hyperbolicity is a standing assumption. The user permits an unbounded, sufficiently broad knot class, and requests literature checks for coverage rather than proof techniques. Accordingly, the external statements below are not hypotheses accepted on the authority of their proofs. No proof section of these papers is needed for this audit.

1. Keep the quantum object fixed

The target is Kashaev’s original invariant and volume conjecture, approached through a literature-defined quantum A-module and its distinguished period. The later Andersen–Kashaev meridian state and Andersen–Malusa b-shift annihilator are comparison objects. An annihilating equation alone does not select a solution or its normalization. An AK volume theorem does not settle the original conjecture without the corresponding comparison.

The independent local and analytic arguments in the existing notes may duplicate stated literature coverage. That is independent verification, not evidence of a new theorem about previously uncovered knots.

For the principal algebraic direction, Guilloux–Marche, 1804.01395, already studies exact polynomials, a class containing knot A-polynomials, and their volume functions. Its definitions and stated coverage identify classical regulator exactness as existing theory. A quantum theorem still has to identify the distinguished period and its normalization. The intrinsic norm-torsor note reconstructs the required elementary descent arguments independently.

All-level state and torsor coverage

The following are definitions and stated scope, not imported proofs:

  • Murakami–Murakami, math/9905075, states the Kashaev/colored-Jones comparison for all levels. Our crossing note reconstructs the needed matrix and period identities directly. This is independent verification of known coverage.
  • Baseilhac–Benedetti, 1101.1851, states an odd-level quantum-hyperbolic/Kashaev comparison. Its link specialization uses the trivial ambient character. It must not be silently replaced by a fixed hyperbolic character of the complement.
  • Willetts, 2003.09854, states an all-knot completion unifying ADO and colored Jones, with the Alexander factor retained. That unification and its recursion-ideal theorem have not been adopted here. Our modified-dimension gauge is proved from the defining finite matrices instead. The later Frobenius Verma calculation independently proves the stated root normalization ADO/Delta by untruncated crossing coefficients and the polynomial-block trace. It also proves equality of the actual root annihilator ideals under this central factor. It does not identify the generic completion or adopt a colored-Jones recursion comparison.
  • Garoufalidis–Scholze–Wheeler–Zagier, 2412.04241v2, introduces proposed K3-graded Habiro modules. The user subsequently requested a separate independent audit. It confirms counterexamples to the printed unrestricted Theorems 1 and 2: the local span need not be finitely generated, and the global set need not be additive. It also verifies formula errors in (114) and (165). Theorem 5 restricts to specified good root orders; the supplied examples do not directly refute its restricted membership conclusion, which remains independently unverified here. No theorem from this paper is a premise of the A-curve torsor construction or its requested volume comparison.

The direct ADO pointing proof also identifies a structural limitation of a fixed-level approach: rational root states have a central shift sector v=1. Retaining the original quantum family and its pointing is necessary before comparing with the geometric classical A-curve. A finite splitting torsor alone is insufficient.

The subsequent finite cut-state proof now independently establishes a nonzero actual all-level meridian annihilator for every knot. It does not adopt the literature holonomicity or recursion-ideal comparison theorems. The triangulation bridge specifies the remaining geometric-symbol, relative-source, and regulator-norm comparison. These existence results do not establish the volume conjecture for a broad knot class.

3. What alternatingness supplies and what it does not

Menasco’s diagrammatic checkerboard decomposition is useful combinatorial data. It must not be identified automatically with a decomposition into right-angled cells in the complete hyperbolic metric.

Gan, 2005.07904v3, classifies alternating links with both checkerboard surfaces totally geodesic as three links, with octahedral, cuboctahedral and icosidodecahedral projection graphs, and characterizes them as right-angled completely realisable. Thus the strong geometric interpretation is highly restrictive. This coverage statement does not invalidate the combinatorial Menasco decomposition.

An augmented link and a knot obtained by filling its crossing circles are also different manifolds. A right-angled augmented-link realization, or a TV volume theorem for it, cannot be transferred to the filled knot without a separate quantum filling argument.

4. What would count as closing a broad class here

The present independent theorem covers every globally branched complete strictly positive geometric knot triangulation, for its specifically normalized tensor-derived meridian state. The remaining requirements are not additional finite rank scans:

  1. Uniform membership of a stated unbounded knot class in the hypotheses, established independently if a literature existence proof is not used.
  2. Identification of the selected literature state across the required realizations, with its peripheral functions, phase and cycle retained.
  3. Application of the independently proved volume and annihilator results to this same state.

Hyperbolic two-bridge knots beyond twist knots and suitable highly twisted alternating families are possible class targets. The sources checked here do not state an unconditional all-two-bridge or all-alternating AK theorem. This search result is not a claim that no such theorem exists elsewhere. Selecting a new matrix condition on knots already in a stated census does not satisfy the uniform-class requirement.

5. Subsequent steering

The user explicitly rejected further reduction of the main objective to FAMED or other chart-by-chart AK hypotheses. The principal direction is the intrinsic period torsor and general algebraic properties of the A-polynomial. The geometric existence input is audited separately in ge_geometric_triangulation_audit.md.
