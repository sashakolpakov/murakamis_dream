Homogeneous face kernels for the original Casimir coupons

30 October 2026. This stays within the original fixed flat-plat presentation. No arbitrary matched graph-to-plat adapter is being assumed.

1. One homogeneous local polynomial

Let N>=3,d=N-1,q=exp(pi i/N),Q=q^2, and let Omega be the actual balanced pair Casimir. At a box retain the literal face order (x,b,c,y): input middle b, output middle c. If F=f(-T), where T is the source-normalized small-circle face operator, write for 0<=j<N

F z^j=sum_(a,beta>=0; a+beta<=j)
   M_f(j;a,beta) x^a y^beta z^(j-a-beta).

Define a HOMOGENEOUS polynomial in the four original faces by

H_f(x,b,c,y)=sum_(j=0)^d sum_(a,beta)
   M_f(j;a,beta) x^a b^(j-a-beta)c^(d-j)y^beta.  (1)

All exponents are nonnegative and its total degree is d. This uses the whole polynomial f(Omega), with all repeated-node data; it is not a spectral-value interpolation. The operator T preserves polynomials and degree and commutes with z^N, so the N input monomials determine the exact local cyclic shift operator.

Examples, with all scalar conventions explicit:

H_1=sum_(j=0)^d b^j c^(d-j).
H_(L_h)=T_h^(b) H_1,
H_(L_h R^epsilon)=lambda0^epsilon T_h^(b) H_(r_N^epsilon),
lambda0=q^(d^2/2), epsilon=+/-1.                (2)

Here T_h=P_(h-1)(T), and the superscript means that T acts on the variable b with outer variables x,y. The original square-root lift in lambda0 is retained. This is root algebra, not a genuine-q germ.

The normalized crossing polynomial r_N and its inverse have the literal Hermite data in the signed one-port theorem. Write M=floor(d/2); the quantities k_a, beta_a, D_a and Ptilde_s below are the exact factors of the positive-circle theorem.

The complete circle has the exact identity

H_(L_N)=N sum_(a=0)^M k_a beta_a^2
         D_a(x,b,y)D_a(x,c,y)Ptilde_(d-2a)(x,y). (3)

It follows either by applying the already proved original complete spectral identity to (1), or by the finite Newton formula. With a retained half twist, insert the actual omega_a^epsilon in (3). For each fixed complete channel, its H is N times that literal local positive polynomial, including k_a beta_a^2 and its phase.

2. Exact local physical face-grid identity

Keep ALL formal Casimir step words and group them by p modulo N only after inserting the source phase q^(d p). Write the resulting normalized q-difference operator

F g(z)=sum_(p mod N) A_p(x/z,y/z) g(Q^-p z).

Its inverse finite Fourier transform is exact:

A_p(X,Y)=N^-1 sum_(j=0)^d P_j(X,Y) Q^(p j),
P_j=sum M_f(j;a,beta)X^aY^beta.                 (4)

On the actual face grid, c=Q^-p b. Consequently

c^-d H_f(x,b,c,y)=sum_j P_j(x/b,y/b)(b/c)^j
                =N A_p(x/b,y/b).               (5)

For the physical input heads u,v and output heads r,s, the literal clocks are U=-q^-1 x/b,V=-q^-1 b/y, and p=r-u. The original matrix element is therefore

f(Omega)_(r,s;u,v)
    =N^-1 q^(-d p)c^-d H_f(x,b,c,y)            (6)

when the output clocks are represented by c. This includes zero when the modular output has the wrong UNWRAPPED total: every Casimir word has the true vanishing physical endpoint coefficient, so an illegal wrap has zero coefficient before it can become a physical path. The finite R/R^-1 are the actual polynomials in this same Casimir, with the specified lambda0 and Hermite derivative. Equation(6) is not an extension obtained by discarding a physical endpoint.

3. Original mark, integer lifts and even-level carries

Use the actual connected flat plat with R boxes and R+2 white faces. Retain the original cups and every ordinary cap except its ONE original unrestricted marked cap. There are R ordinary face targets and two protected faces u,v. All coupons f(Omega) preserve the literal integer sum of the two main-head indices. Thus after the other caps the two indices at the marked cap necessarily sum to d, exactly as in the original marked face-coefficient proof. Its ratio to the ordinary cap is still the actual N^-1 (L_v/L_u)^d, not a new trace functional.

For a nonzero physical state, the centered main heads define an integer conserved flow on the actual planar four-valent Morse graph, including the cups and caps. The elementary planar face-potential argument gives integer lifted potentials, unique up to a common constant, with the literal half-index offsets. Equivalently, the main heads have

i=d/2+n_left-n_right+(t_left-t_right)/2.

Here t=0 for odd N and t is the original gap parity for even N. Adjacent main regions have opposite t at even N; the two middle faces of a box have the same t. In particular the ACTUAL physical p=r-u equals n_b-n_c, not just its residue modulo N.

Use these lifted logarithms in the intermediate half powers. Then

q^(-d p)(bc)^(d/2)=c^d,                        (7)
f(Omega)_(r,s;u,v)=N^-1 H_f(x,b,c,y)/(bc)^(d/2). (7a)

The second identity follows DIRECTLY from (6),(7); it does not use a complete-box factorization as a black box.

Here is the entire remaining turn calculation. It is the literal matrix calculation in the original marked face-coefficient theorem, sections 2–3. For a physical complementary pair with first head i and second j=d-i,

cup(i,j)=N q^(-d^2+di),
cap(i,j)=N^-1 q^(di),
mark(i,j)=N^-2 q^(dj).

The centered first head on a cup/cap has outside region o and inside region z, so q(di)=q(d2/2)(L_o/L_z)(d/2). Thus each cup constant is N q(-d2/2), each ordinary cap constant is N^-1 q(d2/2), and their k pairs cancel EXACTLY. The mark-to-ordinary-cap ratio is N^-1 q(-d2)Q(dj)=N-1(L_v/L_u)^d, with the original SECOND head j. This uses the whole-source complementary constraint already proved above, rather than restricting the original mark in advance.

Let T_f count +1 for each outside cup/cap corner and -1 for each inside corner. The turn product is therefore

N^-1 (L_v/L_u)^d product_f L_f^[(d/2)T_f].

Let M_f count the input/output middle incidences at ALL original boxes, independently of which f(Omega) is inserted. The flat-plat sweep gives M_f-T_f=2 at every bounded face and M_u-T_u=-2 at the exterior. Every bounded gap segment has two middle-or-inside-turn ends; the exterior has 2(k-1) middle ends and 2k outside turns. This argument uses only the original projection, not a smoothed graph.

Multiplying (7a) over the R boxes now gives the literal total summand

N^(-R-1) product_e H_(f_e)
      *(L_v/L_u)^d product_f L_f^[(d/2)(T_f-M_f)]
  =N^(-R-1) product_e H_(f_e)
                          /product_ordinary L_f^d. (7b)

All half powers have disappeared and every face exponent in the final expression is integral. Changing any lifted n_f by N leaves it unchanged. This is why both parities have the same final formula; local lift signs have not been reset independently. The original cup, cap, mark and index formulas have all been displayed, and none depends on using a complete coupon at a box.

Conversely each original finite face-grid point specifies canonical physical main heads. If an unwrapped conservation equation fails, its actual matrix element (6) vanishes. If all are nonzero, the conserved flow gives the lifted potentials just used, so the equality applies. The original positive complete-channel support zeros are included when such coupons are retained. Thus this is an equality of the actual finite source sums, not an unconstrained off-grid continuation.

4. Whole-source polynomial coefficient, with no smoothing hypothesis

The original finite face grid has N^(R+1) points, after fixing the exterior potential. The original marked scalar is N^-1. Each of the R local inverse Fourier transforms contributed exactly N^-1. Therefore the actual source is the normalized face-grid average of

product_(boxes e) H_(f_e)(L_x,L_b,L_c,L_y)
                  /product_(ordinary faces) L_f^d. (8)

Equivalently it is

Z_N=[product_(ordinary faces) L_f^d]
           product_e H_(f_e)(L_x,L_b,L_c,L_y),
          with both protected exponents equal0.  (9)

The primitive grid normalization is exactly accounted for: there is NO extra N^R in (9), because H_f itself includes the N in (3). If r boxes remain complete and the others are general, extracting their N factors gives N^r times the original remaining spectral scalars. All external Cartan and framing factors stay exactly where specified.

To prove that the grid average is exactly (9), the numerator is a polynomial with nonnegative exponents (not necessarily positive coefficients), homogeneous of degree dR. The grid selects exponents d mod N at all R ordinary faces and0 mod N at the protected face v. Since d=N-1, the former have minimal nonnegative representative d. The total degree forces them ALL to equal d, and the protected exponents to equal zero. The exterior face exponent then also equals zero. In the even coset, the resulting phase is one since every exponent equals its target. This excludes ALL aliases on the original grid. It does not use a merge forest, an elimination order, or the connectedness of any identity-smoothed projection.

In particular (9) applies to disconnected small-port subsets and to sets with outer-variable dependency cycles. The fixed original projection remains connected, which is the stated flat-plat hypothesis.

5. Exact signed magnitude and all-subset local norm certificate

For the geometric rate and corridor statements below, assume the original flat plat has the same actual labeled nondegenerate right-angled certificate as the complete-source volume theorem. The finite algebra in sections 1–4 needs only the stated connected original flat-plat source.

Put S_0=1 and S_j=product_(v=1)^j 2sin(pi v/N). For i+k+l+t=d, the normalized positive crossing polynomial has exact coefficient on b^i x^k y^l c^t

(-1)^(k+l+1) q^[kl-(i+1)(t+1)]
                 N/(S_i S_k S_l S_t).          (10)

For the inverse, invert q in the displayed phase; all sine factors remain positive. The original lambda0^epsilon multiplies this whole polynomial. Formula(10) follows from the finite q-multinomial kernel: j=d-t, and S_j S_t=S_d=N. In particular its weighted coefficient norm is EXACTLY the positive homogeneous polynomial

A_N(b,x,y,c)=sum_(i+k+l+t=d)
              N b^i x^k y^l c^t/(S_i S_k S_l S_t). (11)

Uniform sine-product estimates give N^-1 log A_N -> G(b,x,y,c), the four-angle potential of the signed one-port theorem. This follows directly from a single degree-d simplex, not from a maximum over operator blocks. At fixed positive radii, (2) therefore implies

||H_(L_h R^epsilon)|| <=C_b^(h-1) A_N,
||H_(L_h)|| <=N C_b^(h-1) max(b,c)^d,          (12)
C_b=3+4(x+y)/b+4xy/b^2.

The second inequality retains the exact leading rate of H_1,

I(b,c)=log max(b,c),
max(b,c)^d <= ||H_1|| <=N max(b,c)^d.

In particular I<=G, since the b and c corners occur in (11). This keeps the actual cancellations within each local f before taking absolute values. Multiplying (12) and taking the SINGLE coefficient (9) gives the independent-old-face bound without any dependency-order issue.

The strongest direct finite certificate is allowed to use different SHARED old-face radii for different subsets. For a nonempty reflected subset S, choose ONE positive radius l_f for each original face, and set

C_S(l)=sum_(e notin S) [F(l_x,l_b,l_y)+F(l_x,l_c,l_y)]
       +sum_(e in S, epsilon_e!=0) G(l_b,l_x,l_y,l_c)
       +sum_(e in S, epsilon_e=0) I(l_b,l_c)
       -sum_(ordinary f) log l_f.               (13a)

Here F is the actual fold triangle rate, including its beta scalar, from the all-channel rate theorem and its absolute endpoint normalization. All local factors use the SAME l_f wherever that face occurs. The complete half-twist phases have modulus one and remain in the exact source before applying this bound. The sufficient test is

for every nonempty S subset E, there exists l^(S)>0 with
      C_S(l^(S)) < Phi_D=Vol(Y_D)/(2pi).        (13b)

No uniformity over a changing graph or infinitely many subsets is needed: the original graph and E are fixed. An infimum certificate strictly below Phi_D suffices, because then some finite positive radii realize a smaller strict margin. This formulation retains I for unsigned deleted ports; replacing I by G is a weaker optional bound. The I-only capacity is the same smoothed positive-source capacity underlying the already proved untwisted corridor. No universal existence of mixed signed/unsigned certificates is asserted.

Here is a direct justification of that I-only identification. Use the literal merge-forest/exterior-cycle classification in the simultaneous source theorem, including its disconnected case, and the finite angle-entropy duality of the smoothed-source capacity theorem. The dual of I(b,c)=max(log b,log c) assigns nonnegative loads sigma_b,sigma_c with sum one to the edge endpoints, with zero entropy. A connected merge tree has total remaining triangle load one if its root is ordinary, and zero if its root is protected. Given a feasible merged angle flow, its lift to the original vertices is unique: at an edge oriented parent-to-child, let L be the remaining triangle load in the entire child subtree, and set sigma_child=1-L, sigma_parent=L. All these loads are in [0,1], since the subtree load is nonnegative and at most the whole component load. Subtracting neighboring subtree balances verifies every original face target; the root balance follows from the component total. Conversely, summing an original feasible flow over each component gives the merged flow. The edge entropy is zero, so both maximized entropies, hence both capacities, agree, whether or not a finite radius minimizer exists. If smoothing disconnects the projection, the exterior component has beta>=1 actual cycles and would require remaining triangle load -beta: its number of original ordinary targets minus its number of deleted edges. Nonnegative angle loads make this impossible; the dual capacity is -infinity. No analogous identification is made for a signed G edge.

One simpler sufficient test uses the original marked packing for all S. At those radii put

g_e=F(x,b,y)+F(x,c,y)-G(b,x,y,c).                (13)

If g_e>0 for every port in E, then (13b) follows, because I<=G. Every original ordinary target remains d, so this is the literal original target subtraction and it also covers disconnected replacements. Readjusting the shared radii in (13b) can certify a subset even when its original-packing local g_e is nonpositive.

For a boundary quartet with some marked face variables zero, this criterion is understood by the LITERAL polynomial and its continuous rate. In (11), zero variables require the corresponding exponents to vanish. The entropy maximum is over that simplex face; G is continuous on every nonzero nonnegative quartet. The same is true of the actual triangle potential F. Hence if (13) is strictly positive there, one may set the marked zeros to sufficiently small FIXED positive radii before applying (12). The C_b constants can grow, but are then fixed; delta is chosen afterwards. No positivity of every boundary g_e is assumed. The local criterion is sufficient, not universal.

6. Whole mixed-source corridor under finite shared-radius certificates

Let E be any set of varied original circle ports, with their actual epsilon_i in {0,+1,-1}. Suppose the finite shared-radius test (13b) holds. In particular it holds under the simpler local test (13), with the literal marked-zero convention above. No assumption on the identity smoothing of E or of its subsets is needed. All external colors outside E remain N, with arbitrary original complete half twists retained.

There are constants delta,gamma>0 and K such that the original mixed source A_N and its EXACT empty-subset complete-image core F_N^E obey

|A_N(N-h_E,N_else)-F_N^E(h)|
         <=N^K exp[N(Phi_D-gamma)]              (14)

for every sufficiently large N of both parities and every PHYSICAL integer 0<=h_i<=delta N. Indeed the exact reflection expands over finitely many nonempty subsets. A term selecting a zero deficit vanishes because L_0=0; apply (12) only to the positive deficits in the remaining terms. Equations(9),(12) bound each original subset at its chosen shared old-face radii l^(S). The strict margins in (13b) have a positive minimum over the finitely many subsets. If using marked-zero data, first fix positive regularizations preserving those margins. Choose delta afterwards so that all the finite small-color costs C_b^(h_i-1) consume less than half the smallest margin. No N-dependent optimization of radii is used. The uniform all-channel upper estimate handles all remaining complete labels, and their number and measures are polynomial. The original reflection signs and half-twist phases are retained before this bound.

Shrink delta further inside the zero-free complex-core packet. Its uniform lower rate is at least Phi_D-gamma/2 there. Thus

A_N(N-h_E,N_else)/F_N^E(h)=1+O(exp(-cN))         (15)

uniformly on that integer corridor. For h_N/N -> chi real in this cube, the actual marked geometric assembly then gives

lim (2pi/N)log|A_N(N-h_(N,E),N_else)|
                                =Vol(rho_chi).  (16)

The regulator boundary correction vanishes because the selected peripheral U_i=-2pi i chi_i have zero real part. Finite-N critical actions are kept in the core’s relative asymptotic; only their proved uniform limit is used here. This is a SUFFICIENT shared-radius corridor. The local-G test is only one way to satisfy it. Some actual boundary quartets fail (13); no universal certificate or positivity, far-color bound, genuine-q normal identity, or filling transfer follows.

7. A certified actual local instance and validation

The criterion is not merely an open locus of unrelated curvature data. The already used actual width-three flat plat with box word [1,3,0,2,4,1,3], protected faces 0,8, has an exact marked strip packing with finite curvatures (25,9,16,4,1,9,1). At its zero-based port 3 the actual quartet (x,b,c,y) is (4,9,1,1), with distinct unmarked middle faces. Here G=log9 by the dominant-side case, and

F(4,9,1)+F(4,1,1)-log9 > 0.45.                (17)

The audit gives rational centers/radii, verifies every white contact/noncontact and shaded supporting-circle inequality on the original face chart, and certifies (17) by a finite Lobachevsky series with its explicit analytic tail. This supplies an actual one-port instance of (13b), not a claim that all actual diagrams satisfy (13). For the stronger simultaneous theorem, (13b) must be verified for every nonempty subset of the chosen E.

Focused audit: original local matrices, full sources and certificate, with saved results. The local checks retain arbitrary polynomial coupons, both crossing signs with lambda0, wrong unwrapped totals, and complete-channel phases. The whole checks use original cups/caps/unrestricted mark, compare coefficient extraction with full physical contractions, and independently average the original even/odd face grids on selected fixtures. They include all-deleted and disconnected substitutions. These finite checks support the exact proof; they do not numerically prove universal shared-radius certificates or a volume limit.

The source audit compares complete and signed polynomial coefficients, retained crossing phases, physical entries, whole sources and face-grid points, including illegal-total zeros. It also checks exact white and shaded support. The geometric gap is certified by a 256-bit outward-rounded Arb evaluation of a 100-term Lobachevsky series, with total tail below 0.01910 and lower bound above 0.46643723013, stronger than (17).

The conclusion retains the sufficient-certificate, physical-root and fixed-presentation scope.
