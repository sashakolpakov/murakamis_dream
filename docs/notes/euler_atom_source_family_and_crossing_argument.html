Euler-factor source families and the literal crossing argument

27 October 2026. Multiplying the actual primitive gauge period by one Euler factor gives an exactly solvable inverse-factor family. At the resonant shifts a=Q^r it transfers the selected local period from the Gaussian point 2 to the boundary point 0. This is a real source effect of an N-dependent unit, rather than an inference from its classical symbol. A further forward Euler factor has a nonreal formal critical pair; its selected asymptotic is not proved here.

The Euler polynomial is literally the one in the original R-matrix. Its ARGUMENT must also be retained: the physical R atom and the right source variable are different operators. We prove this distinction, and give the exact literal signed crossing coefficients in the actual two-leg Borel fusion. No family below is substituted for the original knot invariant.

1. The actual atom and its right-source argument are distinct

Fix q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, rho=q-q^-1, N>=2. Write

F_k=(Q;Q)_k,
u_N(z)=sum_(k=0)^(N-1) z^k/F_k,
v_N(z)=sum_(k=0)^(N-1) (-1)^k Q^(k(k-1)/2)z^k/F_k.

In the normalized actual unchecked crossing the nilpotent factor is

Theta=v_N(X), X=q rho^2 E_1 F_2,
Theta^-1=u_N(X).                              (1)

This follows coefficient by coefficient from the original coefficient q(k(k-1)/2)rhok/[k]_q!, since

[k]_q!=q^(-k(k-1)/2)F_k/(1-Q)^k,
rho(1-Q)=-q rho^2.

It agrees with the existing actual-atom proof. Every Cartan, flip, framing and peripheral line remains its original factor. Formula (1) does not replace that whole crossing by one scalar Euler function.

The actual source gauge instead uses z=xf in the RIGHT coefficient algebra of Borel fusion. On the physical two-leg regular module its right operator has rank N(N-1). At the complete color, both E_1 and F_2 have rank N-1, so the physical X in (1) has rank (N-1)^2. Hence these two nilpotent operators cannot be similar for any N>=2. Their Euler units minus identity have the same respective ranks, because their linear coefficients are units and the remaining factor is invertible.

Thus u_N(a z) below is an explicit actual-source COUPON using the same Euler polynomial as the literal inverse R atom. It cannot be called a complete crossing insertion by equating its argument with X. A whole crossing comparison has to carry its actual fusion, weight-diagonal factor and finite-path support as well.

2. Exact signed crossing coefficients in the actual Borel fusion

In the actual two-leg fusion C, with z_(a,k)=f^a e_k, remove only the recorded common Cartan scalar kappa from the positive crossing. Its unique RIGHT coefficient is exactly

a_+=sum_(a,k>=0,a+k<N)
   (-1)^k x^(-a-2k) Q^(k(k-1)/2) f^a e_k/F_a. (2)

The inverse crossing, with scalar kappa^-1 recorded, has coefficient

a_-=sum_(a,k>=0,a+k<N)
   (-1)^k x^(a+2k) Q^(-k(k-1)/2-ka) f^a e_k/F_a. (3)

Here x is the actual same-color highest K eigenvalue and M=-qx; the formulas hold at fixed root for generic M and at completeness. They are not obtained by a numerical fit at M=1.

For a direct proof of (2), the actual positive crossing sends e_0 tensor e_j to x^-j e_j tensor e_0. The fusion kernel is

C_(u,v;a,k)=x^(u-a) F_a F_v/[F_(a-u)F_k],
0<=u<=a, v=k+a-u<N.

It preserves the unwrapped total degree a+k=u+v. Fixing that degree j, solve its triangular system for the right coefficient. The finite Euler inverse convolution gives

F_a [f^a e_k]a_+=(-1)^k x^(-a-2k)Q^(k(k-1)/2),
a+k=j<N,

and there are no coefficients with a+k>=N. This proves (2). The nonwrapped paths form a triangular subalgebra. Its diagonal weights are d_k=(-1)^k x^-2k Q^(k(k-1)/2); inversion of its Euler factor gives (3). It also follows by direct multiplication of (2) and (3) in the full actual coefficient algebra.

The completed expression u_N(x^-1 f) D(H), D e_k=d_k e_k, has the same coefficients as (2) on nonwrapped paths. Its wrapped terms require their own source comparison; they may not be erased from a general inserted word simply because one two-leg cap kills them. At M=1 its Euler argument is

x^-1 f=x^-2 z=Q z.                             (4)

Thus the actual crossing’s right Euler profile is related to the resonant a=Q case below, rather than an unmarked replacement of its argument by z. The diagonal D and the source-supported finite path information remain essential for the whole crossing.

3. The full inverse-factor family has a retained norm source

Let

g_N(z)=v_N(z)v_N(Qz) modulo z^N,
w^-_(N,a)(z)=g_N(z)u_N(a z) modulo z^N,
J^-_N(a)=[z^(N-1)]w^-_(N,a)(z).                (5)

All use their canonical degree-<N representatives. The exact parameter polynomial is

J^-_N(a)=(1/N)sum_(k=0)^(N-1)
                        product_(r=0)^(k-1)(a-Q^r). (6)

To prove (6), the finite q-binomial coefficient identity gives

[z^k]v_N(z)u_N(a z)
      =a^k(a^-1;Q)_k/F_k
      =product_(r=0)^(k-1)(a-Q^r)/F_k.

It is polynomial at a=0 as well. The reflected coefficient of v_N(Qz) at degree N-1-k is F_k/N. Convolution cancels the remaining factorial and yields (6).

Writing P_k(a)=product_(r=0)(k-1)(a-Qr), the identity P_k(Qa)=Qk(a-Q-1)P_(k-1)(a) and P_(k+1)(a)=(a-Q^k)P_k(a) yield the exact parameter transport

J^-_N(Qa)=(Qa-1)(a-1)J^-_N(a)
                          +(Qa/N)(2-a^N).      (7)

The complete norm P_N(a)=a^N-1 enters its derivation. The source 2-a^N cannot be replaced by its value at a marked point before transporting the parameter.

The entire canonical wave in (5), before setting z^N=0, obeys

(1-z)(1-Qz)w^-_(N,a)(Qz)-(1-a z)w^-_(N,a)(z)
       =[(a^N-2)/N]z^N+J^-_N(a)z^(N+1).     (8)

All lower coefficients follow from nilpotent Euler cancellation. For the degree-N coefficient, use the ordinary polynomial identities u_N(aQz)=(1-a z)u_N(a z)+a^N z^N/N and the full gauge Ward equation. Their source gives (a^N-2)/N. Truncating a product’s degree-N tail does not change that coefficient because Q^N-1=0. The degree-N+1 coefficient is the displayed top period. This proves (8) without treating evaluation as a nilpotent character.

For moments j_m=[z^(N-1)]z^m w^-_(N,a), (8) implies exactly

j_(m+2)=(1+Q-aQ^(m+2))j_(m+1)
                            +(Q^(m+2)-Q)j_m,
j_0=J^-_N(a),
j_1=(1+Q-aQ)J^-_N(a)+Q(a^N-2)/N.            (9)

The generic cleared classical source support is z(z+a-2)=0. That polynomial alone does not select its branch.

4. A proved source branch transfer at resonant inverse atoms

For fixed r>=0 and N>r, take a=Q^r. Exact Euler transport gives

w^-_(N,Q^r)(z)=v_N(Qz)(z;Q)_r modulo z^N.     (10)

For a fixed polynomial P, the BASE period on v_N(Qz) is

L_N(P)=[z^(N-1)]P(z)v_N(Qz)
       =(1/N)sum_m [z^m]P(z) F_m,            (11)

once N exceeds its degree. Since F_0=1 and F_m=O_m(N^-m) for every fixed m>=1, (10)-(11) prove

J^-_N(Q^r)=N^-1[1+O_r(N^-1)],
[z^(N-1)]P(z)w^-_(N,Q^r)(z)/J^-_N(Q^r)
                           -> P(0),          (12)

with error O_(P,r)(1/N). Its original period is nonzero for all sufficiently large N and has zero exponential rate. In particular

J^-_N(1)=1/N, J^-_N(Q)=Q/N                  (13)

exactly. Before adding this inverse factor, the gauge period is c_N~omega_N/sqrt(N), and its fixed polynomial observables select 2. After the factor the Gaussian leading component disappears and the evaluated boundary source selects 0. The actual finite period has not vanished; its leading source class has changed.

At a=1 this can also be seen from the minimal Ward equation: w^-=v_N(Qz), so

(1-Qz)w^-(Qz)-w^-(z)=-z^N/N.

Its classical source support is only z=0. The extra root z=1 of the generic cleared polynomial z(z+a-2) at a=1 comes from an unremoved factor 1-z. It cannot be used as a selected critical point. Source saturation is necessary at the resonant atom, even though the multiplying Euler polynomial is a unit in the nilpotent algebra.

Thus fixed-degree source selection cannot simply be extended to all Euler units. This is an exact family of source probes built from the actual atom polynomial. It is not a statement that a literal complete R crossing, including D(H), always destroys a Gaussian branch.

5. Adding a forward factor gives a different critical problem

For completeness define the other actual-polynomial source probe

w^+_(N,a)=g_N(z)v_N(a z) modulo z^N,
J^+_N(a)=[z^(N-1)]w^+_(N,a),
b^+_N(a)=[z^(N-2)]w^+_(N,a).

Its full unreduced Ward equation is

(1-z)(1-Qz)(1-a z)w^+_(N,a)(Qz)-w^+_(N,a)(z)
  =-[(2+a^N)/N]z^N
   +[(1+a+aQ^-1)J^+_N(a)-aQ^-1 b^+_N(a)]z^(N+1)
   -aJ^+_N(a)z^(N+2).                       (14)

The degree-N source follows by multiplying the exact Euler and gauge Ward equations; it retains 2+a^N. The remaining two coefficients are read from the cubic factor and the top two canonical coefficients. This is an exact recursion/source statement, not an assertion about which critical class survives.

At a=1 its classical equation is (1-z)^3=1. Besides the boundary root zero it has the two nonreal points

z_+=3/2+i sqrt(3)/2, z_-=conjugate(z_+).

On the marked upper branch, the coefficient action S_+(z)=3Li_2(z)-2pii Log z is stationary at z_+, since Log(1-z_+)=-2pii/3. Its imaginary part is exactly

Im S_+(z_+)=3D(z_+)=2D(exp(pi*i/3)).           (15)

For example the cross-ratio triangle has angles pi/6,pi/6, 2pi/3, so D(z_+)=2Lambda(pi/6)-Lambda(pi/3)=2Lambda(pi/3). The elementary duplication identity gives (15). This is the same positive regulator number calibrated for the original figure-eight source in its independent Bloch calculation.

No selected asymptotic for J^+_N(1) is proved here. Numerical rate samples are saved as diagnostics only. Neither a nonreal formal critical point nor the equality of the regulator number in (15) identifies this universal source probe with a knot invariant or supplies its nonzero thimble coefficient.

6. Verification and critical-path consequence

The exact audit and saved results pass 23 levels, 115 parameter cases, both unreduced Ward families, the exact parameter transport and resonant fixed-observable selection formulas. Twelve literal crossing tests at complete and generic meridians verify (1)-(3) directly from the original color matrices and actual fusion. The rank mismatch of the two atom arguments is checked at every complete test.

The inverse-family result gives a real source-branch transfer and identifies the exact resonant saturation that any full-word theorem must handle. The signed crossing formulas specify what is additionally needed to pass from the Euler function family to a literal crossing. Nothing here replaces the original state by a positive model, asserts new move invariance, or proves the general volume conjecture.
