The actual Gauss kernel’s rational collective graph

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The fixed-width coherent estimate K has a specific canonical graph. After fixing total occupation and dividing the common diagonal frame, that graph is a rational collective conjugation of the actual dressed meridians. We identify it by its initialized coherent boundary and the proved analytic uniqueness in K. We do not infer it by substituting classical symbols into a high-degree matrix function.

The rational map extends farther than the estimated kernel. In particular it has an algebraic continuation to the regular geometric opposite-frame problem. Its generating projection, its marked primitive, and the coefficient of an original relative cycle are separate issues. The complete directs K, R, L are pinned below.
