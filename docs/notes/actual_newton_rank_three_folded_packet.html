A rank-three actual source: two endpoint envelopes give a sharp norm

29 October 2026. The next repeated Newton twist/turn word has a genuine cubic spectral cover and an interior forward dominant-modulus switch. Nevertheless its ENTIRE positive original-source norm has a sharp limit. The proof keeps its exact source currents, uses a gapped backward branch only for the lower bound, and overlaps two full spectral-radius envelopes for the upper bound. The required profile comparisons have finite outward-rounded interval/Rouche certificates. No hyperbolic volume or link identity is inferred from the resulting constant.

1. The actual source and both of its boundary vectors

Use T=T_1,P=P_rev and the unchanged original Newton metric. By the universal endpoint identity, (T2P)4 1 has, up to a unit scalar, coefficients

v_i=kappa^(-2)(-q)^(i-d)K_i,
K_i=sum_(j=0)^i nu_j [i choose j]_Q conjugate(P_(d-j)). (1)

Here P_i is the original Green sequence, not the turn matrix. Its rank-three forced Ward, with G_i=K_i/F_i and M_i=F_i^3G_i, is proved in the endpoint theorem, section5. Write its coefficients as B_i,C_i,D_i there. The exact monic equation is

M_i+B_i M_(i-1)+C_i(1-Q^(i-1))^3 M_(i-2)
   -Q^3(1-Q^(i-1))^3(1-Q^(i-2))^4 M_(i-3)
                                         =Q^2 delta_(i,1). (2)

Negative-index terms are absent. Its literal data are M_0=conjugate(P_d), M_1=(1-3Q+Q2)conjugate(P_d)+Q2; D_2=0 fixes the next datum. In particular the source forcing has not been deleted.

There is a regular exact backward equation. Put

g_k=conjugate(F_k)^4 M_(d-k), Z_k=Q^(-k).

For every3<=k<=d,

g_k=a_k g_(k-1)+b_k g_(k-2)+c_k g_(k-3),             (3)
a_k=1+Q^(-1)+Q^(-2)-(4+Q^(-1))Z_k,
b_k=[-Q^(-3)-Q^(-2)-Q^(-1)
             +(3+4Q^(-1))Z_k-3Q Z_k^2](1-Q Z_k),
c_k=Q^(-3)(1-Q Z_k)(1-Q^2 Z_k)^4.

All original currents are encoded in its INITIAL VECTOR. Let S_0=sum_(j=0)^d conjugate(P_j). Exactly, for N>=3,

g_0=N^2 S_0,
g_1=N^2 Q^(-1)[(Q-3)S_0+2N],
g_2=N^2 Q^(-4)[(Q^4-3Q^3-3Q^2+6Q+3)S_0
                              +2(Q^3+Q^2-3Q-1)N].   (4)

For a direct proof of(4), root reflection gives

K_(d-h)=nu_h sum_(j=h)^d [j choose h]_Q conjugate(P_j).

Writing S_m=sum_j Q^(mj)conjugate(P_j), the original finite Green equation yields

(1-Q^m)S_m+2Q^(m-1)S_(m-1)-Q^(m-2)S_(m-2)
                                  =N delta_(m,1 modN).

The actual inverse-crossing last row also gives S_(-1)=NQ: equivalently sum_j QjP_j=NQ(-1). These evaluate S_1,S_2 from S_0,N and give(4). Reversing(2) and multiplying by the displayed fourth factorial proves(3).

The independently evaluated original endpoints give

|M_0|~N^(3/2),
S_0=N^(3/2)exp[-i pi(3-N)/4]+O(N).

Consequently (g_2,g_1,g_0)/g_0=(4,-2,1)+O(N^(-1/2)). This is the nonzero dominant eigenvector at the backward endpoint. The N terms in(4) are retained source currents, not arbitrary choices.

2. Spectral envelopes, and a source-selected backward lower bound

Put Y=e(2iu),Z=e(-2iu). The forward and backward principal cubics are

f_u(lambda)=lambda^3+(-3+7Y-3Y^2)lambda^2
                +(3-5Y)(1-Y)^3lambda-(1-Y)^7,
b_u(lambda)=lambda^3+(5Z-3)lambda^2
                +(3-7Z+3Z^2)(1-Z)lambda-(1-Z)^5.    (5)

Let rho_f,rho_b be their largest root moduli. They are continuous and bounded below by1/3,2/3 respectively, by their traces. Set I_e(u)=pi(-1)integral_0u log rho_e(t)dt, e=f,b.

Uniformly for all indices in any indicated compact u interval, the actual matrix product has the spectral-radius upper bound

log|M_i|<=N I_f(pi i/N)+o(N),
log|g_k|<=N I_b(pi k/N)+o(N).                        (6)

This remains valid at modulus switches and the initial double fast zero. Here is an elementary proof avoiding an ill-conditioned full eigenframe. A bounded3x3 matrix of spectral radius at most1 has ||Al||<=C(l+1)2, uniformly in repeated roots: Newton interpolation of z^l bounds its order0,1,2 confluent differences by the corresponding complete homogeneous polynomials, of size at most (l+1)^2. The matrix factors in that interpolation are uniformly bounded. Freeze the exact smooth companion over blocks of length L=floor(N^(1/8)). Its step error is O(L/N). Expanding the product in those errors bounds one block by rho^L C L^2 exp(C L^4/N). Multiplying the blocks costs O(N log L/L+L^3)=o(N) in its logarithm. Uniform continuity of log rho gives the Riemann integral, uniformly also for partial final blocks. Polynomial initial data add only O(log N). This proves(6).

The upper-bound mechanism is valid for ANY FIXED companion rank r, not only this cubic. A uniformly bounded rank-r matrix with spectral radius at most one has powers bounded by C(l+1)^(r-1), by the same order-(r-1) confluent divided-difference formula. Freezing L steps therefore costs at most rho^L C L^(r-1) exp(C L^(r+1)/N). Over O(N/L) blocks the extra logarithm is O(N log L/L+L^r). Taking L=floor(N^(1/(2r))) makes this o(N). This requires a fixed-rank uniformly smooth actual recurrence, a positive lower spectral-radius bound, and subexponential initial data; it permits eigenvalue collisions and modulus switches. It is a FULL spectral-radius upper envelope, not a lower bound or a license to replace the radius by an arbitrarily selected single branch.

For every strict backward prefix0<=u<=u_0<pi/2 there is also a lower:

log|g_k|=N I_b(pi k/N)+O_(u0)(log N).                 (7)

The independent exact dominant-root certificate proves a unique simple dominant branch of b_u beginning at-2 on the ENTIRE strict half-circle. Its two fast roots may coalesce at0. Its regular dominant projection and a uniformly stable complement power-Gram give source transport without diagonalizing those fast roots. Equation(4) supplies the required actual nonzero projection with relative O(N^(-1/2)) error. The exact backward equation is homogeneous for k>=3, so no interior forcing is omitted in applying that certificate. Summed dominant logarithms prove(7). This argument does not transport a branch through the forward modulus switch.

3. One algebraic profile maximum and two finite comparisons

Let Lambda be Lobachevsky and define

J_e(u)=I_e(u)+2Lambda(u)/pi, e=f,b.

Since M_i=F_i^2K_i and s_i s_(d-i)=N, (6) gives the two original coefficient envelopes J_f(pi i/N) and J_b(pi(d-i)/N). The lower(7) gives J_b at every fixed strict backward prefix.

The backward profile has exactly one first-half maximum u_*. Put

p_6(z)=z^6-9z^5+29z^4-38z^3+10z^2+2z+1.

Its unique root z_* in[1,4] lies in(19/5,77/20), and u_=arcsin(sqrt(z_)/2), so 2pi/5<u_*<pi/2. Indeed

J_b'(u)=pi^(-1)log[rho_b(u)/(2sin u)^2].

Imposing |lambda|=z=4sin^2u in b_u and its conjugate and eliminating lambda,Z gives z^24 p_6(z)^2=0. Exact Sturm counts give just two positive roots of p_6 in[0,4]: one in(2/3,7/10) and z_* above. The smaller cannot give rho_b=z because the product of the three root moduli gives rho_b>=z^(5/6)>z when0<z<1. At u=pi/2 all roots of lambda3-8lambda2+26lambda-32 have modulus<4: its unique real root lies in(2,3), and the conjugate pair has modulus sqrt(32/r)<4. Continuity now proves the claimed maximum. These eliminations use full polynomials, not a sampled eigenvalue branch.

The forward profile is increasing: its root trace has modulus1+3z, so rho_f>=z+1/3>z. Two further finite inequalities suffice for a global overlap:

integral_0^(2pi/5)log(rho_f/rho_b)du<0,
integral_(2pi/5)^(pi/2)log[rho_b/(2sin u)^2]du<0.      (8)

The audit supplies rigorous interval bounds on WHOLE parameter cells. For each cubic it chooses rational complex root centers and verifies three disjoint disks by

|p'(c)|_lower r > |p(c)|_upper+|p''(c)|_upper r^2/2+r^3.

Rouche therefore encloses all roots for EVERY parameter in that cell. Their moduli bound rho and its logarithm. The small initial interval uses a monic Cauchy bound and rho_b>=2/3. At4096 outward-rounded cells the two certified upper bounds in(8) are respectively less than -.0327 and-.0114. This is a finite proof of the comparisons, not extrapolation from samples.

The first inequality makes J_f(2pi/5)<J_b(2pi/5). The second makes J_b(pi/2)<J_b(2pi/5). Root-conjugation symmetry gives J_b(pi-u)=2J_b(pi/2)-J_b(u), so J_b(3pi/5)<J_b(pi/2). The only profile criticals before3pi/5 are u_* and its reflected minimum. Therefore the maximum of J_b on[0,3pi/5] is J_b(u_*). Use the forward envelope for i/N<=2/5 and the backward envelope for i/N>=2/5. They cover EVERY physical index and neither exceeds this backward maximum.

4. Whole original-source conclusion and scope

The polynomial number of positive norm terms gives the upper rate; a rounded backward index near u_* and(7) give the matching lower. For the literal original halfword A=(T_2P_rev)^4,

lim_N N^(-1)log||A1||_Newt^2=2J_b(u_*).              (9)

Its original adjoint-word marked closure equals this positive norm exactly. All Cartan lines and all fixed-root Casimir normals remain. No value-only trace or arbitrary diagonal Newton coupon was substituted. The assertion concerns fixed word depth before N tends to infinity.

This extends the source-aware folded Ward method to a genuine cubic with an interior modulus switch. Its inputs are explicit resets, the full opposite source vector, finite algebraic dominant-mode tests, and finite certified envelope comparisons. It is not a general theorem that an arbitrary scalar A-polynomial selects its largest root, and it is not a new broad bridge-family or hyperbolic-volume claim.
