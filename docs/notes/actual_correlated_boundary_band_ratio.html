Correlated neighboring bands of the original boundary source

30 October 2026. The exact ratio of two neighboring charged source products is rational in their actual clocks. On the original six-type resonant boundary chart, their root packet errors are correlated strongly enough to divide by the odd-source normalization of size N^-1. The resulting model is an explicit FINITE quotient. Its classical limit, a transverse Gaussian reduction, and its coefficient in the original period are not evaluated here.

1. One original product and one integral band path

Use the labeled right-angled matching polyhedron, actual cut and original white-prefix dictionary of the common chart. There is one main component. Let N>=3, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2. Fix the actual folded labels a_i, m_i=d-a_i and deficits s_i=d-2a_i. The number of original tensors is fixed. Initially one can require sum s_i<=S0, independently of N; the proof below is uniform also for 0<=s_i<=eta N, with a sufficiently small fixed eta.

After the two ACTUAL cuts, use the primitive common head chart of the charged-current theorem. The component charge conditions must hold. Fix its free coordinates and the two input heads at the common boundary assignment. Its three bands are restrictions of the SINGLE affine family

h(delta)=h_N+delta v,                               (1)

where v is the recorded integral charge path. Its entries are bounded independently of N. All original vertex equations, reversed dual heads and cup complements hold for complex delta. In particular delta=+1 and -1 are not independent head assignments or independently selected asymptotic branches. Their original physical supports remain different.

Let S_N(delta) be the actual charged ROOT product s_delta of the common-band theorem: original whites, literal pi duals, the remaining cups/caps, turns and fixed root channel weights, before any internal head sum or exposed coherent functional. The ordinary scalar cap at the exposed cut has been REMOVED. Its replacement is the literal off-diagonal coherent row after the two bands are assembled. Keeping that old diagonal cap would annihilate a nonzero charged band. For all remaining local tensors we use the specified continuation in the original source envelope. This is not its closed charge-zero product S_F. In particular its charge-zero complementary mark/cap formula Xi_N is NOT used for delta=+/-1. The coherent binomial row is applied after this statement; its input head is fixed along (1), so its common factor can also be retained on both sides of the pointwise ratio.

The hypothesis is on this actual source presentation: each numerator must be one of the original six types 200,002,220,022,202,222. Unspecified routed R or U factors can introduce 000 or 020. Their inclusion is NOT justified by the theorem, even if their contracted root channel has the same scalar. No off-grid equality between such presentations is assumed.

Take r_frame=N rho, rho in a positive real compact, and the first-cut angle in a fixed sufficiently small positive compact of the common chart. For some c>0 independent of N, (1) extends to |delta|<cN: each clock is multiplied by exp(2pi i v_e delta/N), a small bounded complex perturbation. The scaled escaping clocks remain in compact subsets of C*, the finite negative clocks stay in a small neighborhood of the negative ray, and 202 totals stay near their positive ray. The 200/002 Bessel factors stay uniformly away from zero. Shrink c once for the finite network. The exact head lifts and all half-index offsets are kept; this extension does not assert finite-a stationarity.

2. Rational source quotients before asymptotics

The actual large white is

W_a(i,j;r)=(-1)^m q^[-dr+di-m(m-1)] H_m^1(U,V)/F_m,
i+j=m+r, U=Q^i, V=Q^j, F_m=(Q;Q)_m.

For ANY integral u,v its exact meromorphic quotient is

W_a(i+u,j+v;r+u+v)/W_a(i,j;r)
   =q^(-dv) H_m^1(Q^u U,Q^v V)/H_m^1(U,V).        (2)

The same identity holds before specialization with q replaced by zeta and the actual H_m(Q-N). Thus (2) is not obtained by differentiating a root collision. It retains the internal Casimir label m.

For the specified cup, L<N fixed,

p_L(x)=exp(pi i x) q^[x(L-x-1)]
                   Fhat_N(x)Fhat_N(L-x)/F_L,

the factorial recurrence gives, for u>0,

p_L(x+u)/p_L(x)
  =(-1)^u q^[u(L-2x-1)-u^2]
     product_(j=1)^u(1-Q^(x+j))
     /product_(j=0)^(u-1)(1-Q^(L-x-j)).            (3)

Negative shifts are the reciprocal formula at the shifted base head. Caps use the literal complementary argument and inverse. Physical cup scales are constant in x. Formula (3) also holds for the specified generic moving-period factorial, before taking the root.

For estimates at the ROOT, an exact simplification is essential. Write L=d-s and use the PROVED root reflection with the moving-factorial section specialized only now. It gives the meromorphic identity

p_(d-s)(x)=C_(N,s) exp(pi i x) q^[(s-1)x]
               /product_(j=1)^s(1-Q^(x+j)),
C_(N,s)=N(-1)^s Q^[s(s+1)/2]/F_(d-s).             (4)

Explicitly, the reflection is Fhat_N(x)Fhat_N(d-x)=N q1. Apply the unit recurrence s times to its second factor. The removed product is product_(j=1)s(1-Q(-x-j)); factoring each negative power gives exactly (4). Also C_(N,s)=F_s by the finite root factorial identity.

In particular p_d(x)=exp(pi i x)q^-x, and

p_(d-s)(x+1)/p_(d-s)(x)
  =-q^(s-1)(1-QU)/(1-Q^(s+1)U), U=Q^x.          (5)

The exp(pi i x) in (4) is literal. It is not set to one off the integer lattice. The physical scale N q-d2 and every other source character remain. Equation (4) cancels the quadratic factorial gauges BEFORE estimating derivatives; estimates for their separate absolute logs, which can be of size N log N, are unnecessary.

For clarity, the literal dual is still

pi_a(r;i,j)=gamma_a^-1 p_(2a)(2a-r)
   W_a(d-j,d-i;2a-r)/[pV(d-j)pV(d-i)],            (6)

with FIXED root gamma_a and the actual reversal. Equations (2),(3) applied to (6), together with the recorded turn and peripheral characters, prove that S_N(+1)/S_N(-1) is an explicit rational function of the base clocks. No norm pi_a W_a is inverted. Apparent denominators are kept, or canceled as meromorphic factors; the boundary chart below avoids them. This proves the rational identity BEFORE any asymptotic replacement or normalization by the odd source.

3. The finite model and a bounded neighboring quotient

Here are explicit choices of numerator model B_(m,N)(U,V). All finite clocks in the formulas co-vary along (1). Put a=d-m, s=m-a,

P_s(T)=product_(r=a)^(m-1)(T-Q^r),
K_a=[2a choose a]_Q,
M(U,V)=I-(qU/2)H_--(q^-1/(2V))H_+,
M0(U)=I-(qU/2)H_-.

Below S_j=product_(r=1)^j 2sin(pi r/N), S_0=1, is the sine factorial; it is distinct from the channel deficit s.

The matrices are the actual positive Hermitian source matrices from the complementary packet. The following are finite expressions, including their source phases:

  • 200: E_m(U,V) J_0(2sqrt(K)), where E_m=(-1)^m Q{m(m+1)/2}(UV)m product_(r<m)(1-Q^r/U), K=sin^2(pi m/N)(V-1)/(pi2(U/N2)V). The Bessel series specifies this expression without a square-root ambiguity.
  • 002: product_(r<m)(V-Q^r) J_0(2sqrt(K0)), where K0=N^2 V sin^2(pi m/N)(1-U)/pi^2.
  • 220: V^a K_a det M0(U) P_s(UV). The actual large-partner product is retained exactly.
  • 022: (UV/A0)^m times the 220 model at (A0/V,A0/U), A0=Q^d. This is the literal generic reciprocity restricted to the root.
  • 202: A_a(UV)P_s(UV), where A_a(T)=(-1)^a Q2 sum_(k=0)^a [S_a/(S_k S_(a-k))]2(QT)k.
  • 222: V^a K_a det M(U,V) P_s(UV). This model is the EXACT root numerator; no determinant is frozen at its limiting clocks.

The first two and the relative 202 estimate are proved in the root hard-edge theorem. The 220 comparison is proved in the complementary packet; multiplying both sides by the SAME P_s changes no relative error. The 022 model uses its exact reciprocity. The 222 zero exclusion and its uniform finite-clock neighborhoods are proved by positive determinant domination.

Replace each numerator of S_N(delta) by its displayed model, and keep EVERY other factor exactly. Call the resulting nonzero product B_N(delta). Uniformly on |delta|<cN,

S_N(delta)=B_N(delta)(1+R_N(delta)),
|R_N(delta)|<=C/N,                                 (7)

where R_N is holomorphic. This follows by the finite product of the uniform holomorphic root estimates on the indicated complex compact domains, not by extending estimates known only on a real ray. The first two limiting Bessel factors are nonzero on the common chart; continuity gives the required smaller complex neighborhoods. For 202 and 222, P_s has roots on the unit arc near -1 when eta is small, whereas the total lies near a positive compact. For the mixed outer-degree types its total tends to zero or infinity, and the reciprocity above retains the corresponding nonzero partner factors.

We also need the two-sided bound

|B_N(+1)/B_N(-1)|+|B_N(-1)/B_N(+1)|<=C.            (8)

Here is a direct proof, with no assumption on an absolute logarithm of B_N. Write D=partial_delta; each varying clock Z obeys D Z=(2pi i n/N)Z for a fixed bounded integer n.

For (4), all factors Q^(x+j), 1<=j<=s, either remain uniformly separated from 1 in a compact negative-clock neighborhood, or tend uniformly to zero or infinity. Thus

D log p_(d-s)(x)
  =v_x[pi i+(s-1)pi i/N]
   +(2pi i v_x/N) sum_(j=1)^s Q^(x+j)/(1-Q^(x+j))

is O(1+s/N). Physical cups have s=0. This also controls inverse cups and the reversed arguments in (6). The actual common-chart factorial-domain proof ensures these assertions for the auxiliary heads, including 202/202 and both maximal-fold parities. No escaping Gamma factor remains to estimate after the EXACT identity (4).

Each original white/turn/peripheral character has a linear head exponent with coefficient O(N) in a power of q or Q (and the retained bounded integer character in (4)); therefore its logarithmic delta derivative is O(1). No arbitrary added character with an unbounded logarithmic delta derivative is covered by (8). Fixed root weights, factorials, gamma and K_a have derivative zero. In the 200/002 models the degree-m monomials and endpoint products have bounded logarithmic derivative: each finite linear factor has logarithmic derivative O(1/N), while m=O(N). The Bessel factor has derivative O(1/N), since its argument varies by O(1/N) and it stays away from zero on a fixed complex compact.

For 220, H_- is positive. Its eigenvalue product proves |U partial_U log det M0(U)|<=C a on the negative-clock neighborhood. For 202 the negative real roots of its positive-coefficient polynomial give |T partial_T log A_a(T)|<=C a near the positive ray. Each partner factor gives an additional O(1/N) delta derivative, uniformly under the separation just stated. The 022 expression has the same bounds after its literal reciprocal substitutions and exact monomial.

Finally, for 222 fix the nearby negative raw clocks as a reference. For M_ref=I+alpha H_-+beta H_+, alpha,beta>0, conjugation by M_ref^-1/2 puts both positive summands below I. A small complex clock neighborhood leaves the normalized inverse bounded and bounds M_ref^-1/2 (D M) M_ref^-1/2 by C/N. Hence |tr(M^-1 D M)|<=C a/N. This controls the exact finite determinant uniformly at the fold. Combining the finitely many factors yields |D log B_N|<=C on |delta|<=2, with its continuous local logarithm. Integration between -1 and +1 proves (8).

4. Correlation survives the small odd-source denominator

Apply Cauchy’s formula to the SAME R_N in (7) on the delta disk. For |delta|<=2 it gives |D R_N|<=C/N^2. Therefore

S_N(+1)/S_N(-1)
   =[B_N(+1)/B_N(-1)](1+O(N^-2)).                 (9)

The improved error is a correlation statement. Two unrelated relative O(N^-1) estimates would not prove it. All source and model quotients are nonzero here for sufficiently large N.

Let t range in a fixed real compact subset of (0,1), and retain the EXACT normalization of the source-preserving odd insertion,

kappa_N(t)=1-q-t^d+q(1-t)^d.

Its proved lower bound is |kappa_N|>=1/N. Equations (8),(9) give

[S_N(+1)-q S_N(-1)]/[kappa_N S_N(-1)]
   =[B_N(+1)/B_N(-1)-q]/kappa_N + O(N^-1).        (10)

In particular this theorem retains the exact q, rather than replacing it by one before a cancellation of order N^-1. The entire finite model quotient, every cup character, internal channel label, clock correction and half-index offset remains on the right of (10).

The constants are uniform for bounded total deficit, both parities, and also in a sufficiently small fixed near-fold label neighborhood 0<=s_i<=eta N. Thus they apply to the shrinking window sum_i s_i log N=o(N). This is a ROOT ratio result and does not expand the previously proved generic-normal window.

No assertion is made that the right side of (10) has a nonzero limit, or even stays bounded. In particular replacing its finite label dependence by the complete parabolic stationary value would require an additional argument. Formula (10) supplies an explicit model with an absolute O(N^-1) error relative to S_N(-1), even when its leading finite quotient has not been evaluated.

5. What contraction would additionally require

This is a theorem about the original uncontracted products on a common affine band path. Their coherent first-cut weight, the original internal head measure and the two original support cycles have not been removed. A ratio of sums or periods is not the sum or period of the pointwise ratio in (9). Uniform pointwise relative errors alone do not control the relative error of a complex integral with cancellation.

A Gaussian-reduced version would need one common holomorphic transverse reduction, its full coarea/Hessian factor, controlled common cycles and ends, and a nonzero model pushforward with a uniform relative remainder. If such a reduction itself supplies (7) on a cN disk and (8), the same Cauchy proof gives (9),(10) for that reduced family. This implication is conditional; those analytic and cycle hypotheses are not proved by the present uncontracted calculation. Nor is a routed source with uncontrolled 000/020 factors covered by removing its routing only after contraction.

Focused audit: original shifts and correlated local packets, with saved results. It checks 649 actual white neighboring quotients and 649 literal dual neighbors through the first genuine quantum jet, 68 generic cup shifts, 58 root reflection-reduced cups and two symbolic source exponents. At 160 digits it compares 48 original finite Newton sums with the six model formulas, then checks 384 packet values, 96 paired W-pi source quotients and their normalized odd errors, 192 complex head-neighborhood values and 48 exact escaping-cup logarithmic derivatives. Both level parities and fixed neighboring folded labels are retained. The local signed fixtures cover all six types, including an explicitly supplemental 202/202 face pair; they are not a numerical global-network proof.

The theorem concerns an uncontracted source in the specified controlled presentation. It supplies no Gaussian reduction or period coefficient.

The charged product has its exposed scalar cap removed, and the charge-zero Xi_N formula is omitted. No contracted, arbitrarily routed, or model-limit conclusion follows.
