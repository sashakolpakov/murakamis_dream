What rational jet rigidity fixes about a global period

Independent function-level audit, 27 October 2026. The all-level Ward unfolding and norm-root transfer have the stated local uniqueness. That uniqueness does not by itself identify the selected full knot period. The distinction is precise: an analytic function, its formal asymptotic germ, and its period functional are different data.

This note gives sharp uniqueness statements, explicit counterexamples, and a minimal algebraic source criterion. No literature proof is used.

1. Exact coefficient rigidity really is uniqueness of a function germ

Let QN=exp⁡(2πi/N)Q_N=\exp(2\pi i/N). Suppose F(Q,z)=∑j≥0fj(Q)zj,G(Q,z)=∑j≥0gj(Q)zj F(Q,z)=\sum_{j\ge0}f_j(Q)z^j,\qquad G(Q,z)=\sum_{j\ge0}g_j(Q)z^j have rational QQ-coefficients, and for every fixed jj, fj(QN)=gj(QN)f_j(Q_N)=g_j(Q_N) for every sufficiently large N>jN>j. Then F=GF=G coefficientwise. For each fixed jj, a nonzero rational function cannot have these infinitely many distinct zeros. The exceptional bound can depend on jj.

Rationality can be weakened: it suffices that each difference has a convergent finite Puiseux–Laurent germ at Q=1Q=1, on the marked branch. Indeed a nonzero such germ is ctm(1+O(t))c\,t^m(1+O(t)), c≠0c\ne0, after Q−1=teQ-1=t^e, and has no zeros sufficiently near the puncture.

If the resulting power series are holomorphic in zz near zero on an open QQ-domain, equality of coefficients is equality of actual analytic germs. On any connected continuation domain, with the same paths and logarithmic sheets marked, the functions are equal. There is no extra holomorphic function vanishing to all orders at an interior point. Thus the literal factorial function in the unfolding theorem has no hidden local rational-coefficient freedom.

The regularity condition matters. On a punctured neighborhood of Q=1Q=1, choose the local logarithm and put $$ U(Q)=\exp\!\left(\frac{4\pi^2}{\Log Q}\right). $$ It is a nonzero holomorphic unit there and U(QN)=1U(Q_N)=1 for all large NN, but U≠1U\ne1. It has exponential growth of order 1/|Q−1|1/|Q-1|. For example 1+(U(Q)−1)z1+(U(Q)-1)z preserves the constant coefficient one and specializes to one at every QNQ_N, while being a different generic formal unit. Its coefficient is not rational or Puiseux meromorphic at Q=1Q=1, so it is excluded by the actual theorem. This example does not change a fixed exact finite root contraction; it shows why punctured holomorphy and exponential-type growth alone do not replace coefficient rigidity.

2. Growing finite jets are not a continuity theorem for a global value

Equality of a coherent local function fixes any specified continuous formal contraction of it: finite sums and products, substitutions of positive formal order, and finite-jet functionals preserve equality. For a zz-adic linear functional into a field with the discrete topology, continuity means that it kills (zB)(z^B) for some finite BB. This is an elementary consequence of continuity at zero.

Without such a property, agreement of growing truncations has no uniform evaluation implication. For fixed a>1a>1, AN(z)=1,BN(z)=1+aNzN A_N(z)=1,\qquad B_N(z)=1+a^Nz^N agree modulo zNz^N, but BN(1)−AN(1)=aNB_N(1)-A_N(1)=a^N. Each is polynomial, with algebraic coefficients; the added term is proper hypergeometric in NN. This family is not a second rational coefficient unfolding of section 1. It is a level-dependent tail which that theorem never claims to determine.

In a nilpotent matrix algebra zN=0z^N=0 the tail is literally zero and cannot change any fixed finite matrix contraction. In a whole normal order zNz^N can instead be a nonzero normal parameter; a divided source can see it. If a selected contraction requires at most BB normal jets, knowing those jets fixes that contraction. What must be proved is that the comparison uses the same functional, with its boundary carries and finite jet budget. The local Ward unfolding alone does not construct that global comparison.

This is exactly why the source and first-normal terms retained in the norm-root period theorem and in the divided-power calculations are necessary. Their equality as local operators does not license replacing the full contraction by a different cycle.

3. All formal WKB jets do not fix an analytic period at an irregular point

This is a different issue from exact rational coefficient uniqueness. Let c=1−ic=1-i, and on a punctured hh-neighborhood consider P0(h)=1,P1(h)=1+exp⁡(−c/h). P_0(h)=1,\qquad P_1(h)=1+\exp(-c/h). On a sufficiently small sector around the positive real ray, exp⁡(−c/h)=O(hm)\exp(-c/h)=O(h^m) for every mm. Thus both have the same normalized formal power series, to every order. Both are holomorphic on the punctured neighborhood and have exponential-type growth exp⁡(O(1/|h|))\exp(O(1/|h|)). Both even solve the same rational-coefficient equation (h2∂h)(h2∂h−c)P=0. (h^2\partial_h)(h^2\partial_h-c)P=0. Yet on h=iϵh=i\epsilon, ϵ>0\epsilon>0, exp⁡(−c/h)=exp⁡((1+i)/ϵ)\exp(-c/h)=\exp((1+i)/\epsilon). Therefore ϵlog⁡|P0(iϵ)|=0,ϵlog⁡|P1(iϵ)|→1. \epsilon\log|P_0(i\epsilon)|=0,\qquad \epsilon\log|P_1(i\epsilon)|\longrightarrow1. Formal jets in one chamber do not control another marked root direction without an analytic continuation and sectorial estimate.

Even on the same ray, adding a subdominant exponential changes the global period while preserving all normalized dominant WKB jets. It then leaves the leading rate unchanged, but is still an exact global ambiguity invisible to perturbative jets.

Consequently “coefficients lie in the algebraic critical field” is not a convergence assertion about a formal hh-series. The series ∑n!hn\sum n!h^n already has rational coefficients and zero radius. Nor does an algebraic optimizer rule out irregular-singular exponentials or determine their global coefficients.

4. The missing source coefficient has a sharp linear-algebra criterion

Let VV be a finite-dimensional global period space over a characteristic-zero field. Suppose the retained normalizations fix a functional ℓ\ell only on a subspace U⊂VU\subset V, and let g∈Vg\in V be the geometric period vector.

Source criterion. The value ℓ(g)\ell(g) is forced by those data if and only if g∈Ug\in U.

If g∈Ug\in U, this is immediate. If g∉Ug\notin U, extend a basis of UU by gg. Its dual gives a functional μ\mu with μ(U)=0,μ(g)=1\mu(U)=0,\ \mu(g)=1. Then ℓ+cμ\ell+c\mu preserves every given normalization and assigns any desired value to gg, including zero. This is an exact criterion, without an asymptotic hypothesis.

Here is a function-level counterexample with a unique rigid optimizer. Take the rigid algebra k×kk\times k, action S=(V,W)S=(V,W), V>WV>W, and period vector Π(ϵ)=(eV/ϵ,eW/ϵ). \Pi(\epsilon)=(e^{V/\epsilon},e^{W/\epsilon}). Every normalized local amplitude is exactly 11, with all higher jets zero; both points and the unique maximizing point are algebraic. The two positive normalized functionals ℓ+(a,b)=a,ℓ−(a,b)=b,ℓ±(1,1)=1 \ell_+(a,b)=a,\qquad \ell_-(a,b)=b,\qquad \ell_\pm(1,1)=1 give leading rates VV and WW, respectively. All local functions, actions, and normalized jets are identical. The differing datum is the global source coefficient, which vanishes on the optimizer for ℓ−\ell_-. A nonzero formal geometric quotient is compatible with this example.

Strict positivity without quantitative control is also insufficient when the source depends on the level. The normalized functional ℓϵ(a,b)=e−a*/ϵa+(1−e−a*/ϵ)b,a*>0, \ell_\epsilon(a,b)=e^{-a_*/\epsilon}a+ (1-e^{-a_*/\epsilon})b,\quad a_*>0, is strictly positive on both points, but its period rate is max⁡(V−a*,W)\max(V-a_*,W). What matters is nonzero subexponential geometric weight, rather than positivity at each separate level.

A strictly convex/concave Rivin optimizer therefore supplies the unique geometric critical point and its local nonzero Gaussian block. Algebraicity supplies its critical field and local period identities. Neither assertion by itself constrains ℓ(g)\ell(g). For a positive integral on a specified real domain, an ordinary uniform density bound can supply that missing constraint; it cannot be inferred for an unspecified complex quantum cycle.

Even strict convexity on one connected algebraic domain does not choose that domain’s integration class. For the same entire integrand e−x2/ϵe^{-x^2/\epsilon}, compare the normalized positive functionals 12∫−11\frac12\int_{-1}^1 and ∫12\int_1^2. Both send 11 to 11, while their period rates ϵlog⁡Pϵ\epsilon\log P_\epsilon are respectively 00 and −1-1. The action x2x^2 is strictly convex with the unique algebraic optimizer 00. The first rate follows by bounding below on [−ϵ,ϵ][-\sqrt\epsilon,\sqrt\epsilon]; the second by bounding below on [1,1+ϵ][1,1+\epsilon] and above by e−1/ϵe^{-1/\epsilon}. The changed datum is the marked relative cycle, not the function.

5. A useful sufficient global regularity hypothesis

Suppose a constructed global comparison gives a nonzero geometric coefficient c(Q)c(Q) which is meromorphic Puiseux at Q=1Q=1. Then c(QN)=CN−α(1+o(1)) c(Q_N)=C\,N^{-\alpha}(1+o(1)) on its marked branch, with C≠0C\ne0; in particular 1Nlog⁡|c(QN)|→0. \frac1N\log|c(Q_N)|\longrightarrow0. \tag{5.1} Proof: substitute QN−1=2πi/N+O(N−2)Q_N-1=2\pi i/N+O(N^{-2}) in the first nonzero Puiseux–Laurent term. Thus a nonzero rational or algebraic-meromorphic global comparison coefficient cannot hide an exponential suppression or infinitely many zero levels.

This is a practical target for torsor descent: construct the global geometric projection and prove its coefficient belongs to this field and is nonzero. Proving uniqueness of such a comparison if it exists does not prove existence or nonvanishing.

For clarity, the elementary rate-transfer statement is as follows. If PN=cNGN+RN,2πNlog⁡|GN|→V,log⁡|cN|=o(N),|RN|≤exp⁡(N2π(V−δ)) P_N=c_NG_N+R_N,\quad \frac{2\pi}{N}\log|G_N|\to V,\quad \log|c_N|=o(N),\quad |R_N|\le \exp\!\left(\frac N{2\pi}(V-\delta)\right) for some δ>0\delta>0, then (2π/N)log⁡|PN|→V(2\pi/N)\log|P_N|\to V. Indeed RN/(cNGN)→0R_N/(c_NG_N)\to0; upper and lower triangle bounds then give the conclusion. This needs a bound for the total remainder, not only for individual local critical points.

An algebraic alternative to Puiseux meromorphy suffices for (5.1). If cN≠0c_N\ne0 satisfies an integer polynomial of degree dNd_N and coefficient maximum HNH_N, with log⁡(dNHN)=o(N)\log(d_NH_N)=o(N), then log⁡|cN|=o(N)\log|c_N|=o(N). After removing powers of XX, its constant and leading coefficients are nonzero integers. For |cN|≤1|c_N|\le1, 1≤dNHN|cN|1\le d_NH_N|c_N|; for |cN|≥1|c_N|\ge1, the elementary Cauchy bound gives |cN|≤1+HN|c_N|\le1+H_N. This condition is not automatic from algebraicity at each level: cN=aNc_N=a^N already has exponentially large height.

The later critical-source theorem strengthens this criterion: log⁡HN=o(N)\log H_N=o(N) alone suffices, with no degree bound, by applying the geometric-series Cauchy bound to an integer polynomial and its reciprocal. More substantively, a fixed whole algebraic source on the actual exponential root arc has polynomial upper and lower bounds without an absolute height estimate. Irreducible field descent and a retained nonzero source pairing then force the geometric coefficient to be nonzero; neither source descent nor a total remainder bound is inferred from the classical optimizer.

6. Consequences for the current repository claims

  • The unfolded Ward theorem has exact rational-coefficient uniqueness, a marked local cyclic gauge, and a controlled norm on its stated compact chart. Its derivations and scope do not assert a global source coefficient.
  • The norm-root theorem transports the actual missing-edge class to the marked peripheral period. Its norm-one kernel and the selected internal knot contraction remain additional data; the full peripheral period does not determine a knot Bloch-period coefficient.
  • The partial support theorem constructs a nonzero formal geometric quotient before the final two-index contraction. The source criterion above explains exactly why this need not survive that contraction.
  • The divided-power drafts make operator/source statements. Their positive ghosts lower total index by NN, so ordinary full weight-zero closure kills them. Their present stated scope does not claim a selected global relative pairing.

There is no free ambiguity in the already specified actual period: the all-level pointing theorem determines it from its actual operator and finite initial colors. Changing its global functional, or adding a flat solution without preserving that pointing, gives a different period and is not a counterexample to that theorem.

The remaining logical step is consequently concrete. One must map the actual selected source, including its divided normal row, into the geometric quotient and prove a nonzero coefficient with subexponential control, or construct an exact global period comparison preserving that source and metric. Local jet uniqueness then removes local freedom. It cannot substitute for this source map or prove its value by itself.
