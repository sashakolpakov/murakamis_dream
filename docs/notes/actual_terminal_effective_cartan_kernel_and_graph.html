The effective terminal Cartan coupon: an exact kernel and its two critical channels

29 October 2026. This continues the literal terminal Ward calculation. The complete Steinberg projection can be expanded before taking a classical symbol. Its kernel has an exact root-orbit completion. There are consequently two coherent channels, rather than just the phase-one Hurwitz graph. The regular graph of the first channel is explicit below. The second has an explicit coefficient integral and saddle equations. Neither channel is independently substituted for the original source.

This note does not prove a full selected-cycle energy bound, or a closed-knot stationary counterexample. In particular its last section keeps the normal equation of the outgoing cap: the finite character cap is at an escaping physical Fourier coordinate. A character intersection alone does not settle that normal equation.

3. The regular constant-channel canonical graph

Here is a precise principal-phase calculation for the constant channel of (5). It applies where all indicated shapes are regular and the ss-sum has an interior saddle. It describes that critical correspondence; it does not assert that this saddle meets the selected source cycle.

Write (h=Q), (I=e{hi},J=e{hj},S=e^{hs}), and set

B=S/J,V=SI/J. B=S/J,\qquad V=SI/J.

The exact adjacent summand ratio in ss tends to one precisely when

tIJ(1−S/J)2=(1−S)(1−SI/J). tIJ(1-S/J)^2=(1-S)(1-SI/J). \tag{7}

Input and output canonical momenta of (4), after taking the constant NN channel, are

zin=(1−S)/S,zout=SI/J1−SI/J. z_{\mathrm{in}}=(1-S)/S,\qquad z_{\mathrm{out}}=\frac{SI/J}{1-SI/J}. \tag{8}

These follow by the exact jj and ii ratios, respectively; input momentum is the inverse input ratio. Solving (7)–(8) gives

zout=zint[J(1+zin)−1]2,I=J(1+zin)zout1+zout. z_{\mathrm{out}}=\frac{z_{\mathrm{in}}} {t[J(1+z_{\mathrm{in}})-1]^2},\qquad I=\frac{J(1+z_{\mathrm{in}})z_{\mathrm{out}}}{1+z_{\mathrm{out}}}.

At (t=1) this is the original graph of (L^{-1}), consistently with (3). Composing it with (L), the regular constant-channel graph of the effective coupon itself is therefore

(J,z)↦(J(1+z)t+z,zt). \boxed{\quad (J,z)\longmapsto \left(\frac{J(1+z)}{t+z},\frac zt\right). \quad} \tag{9}

It preserves (dJdz). The recorded physical clock is (B=z/[J(1+z)]), invariant under (9).

To express (9) on the ACTUAL character cubic, the symbols of the literal complete Casimir matrices give

X=A−2=−(1−J)2(1+z)2z,Y=BCas−2=(J−1)2(1+z)J,Z=C−2=(J−1)2(1+z)Jz. X=A-2=-\frac{(1-J)^2(1+z)^2}{z},\quad Y=B_{\mathrm{Cas}}-2=\frac{(J-1)^2(1+z)}J,\quad Z=C-2=\frac{(J-1)^2(1+z)}{Jz}.

They satisfy ((X+Y+Z)^2+XYZ=0). In these coordinates (9) is

(X,Y,Z)↦λ(X,Y,tZ),λ=(X+Y+tZ)2t(X+Y+Z)2,Bclock=−Y/X. \boxed{\quad (X,Y,Z)\longmapsto\lambda(X,Y,tZ),\qquad \lambda=\frac{(X+Y+tZ)^2}{t(X+Y+Z)^2},\qquad B_{\mathrm{clock}}=-Y/X.\quad} \tag{10}

This is a nontrivial algebraic canonical action. Parseval matching of the recorded clocks in its two copies does not algebraically set t=1t=1. On the true irreducible terminal R232R_{23}^2-cap component one must impose

(X′,Y′,Z′)=(c2,c,−c), (X',Y',Z')=(c^2,c,-c),

not just its unsaturated equation (Y’+Z’=0). In particular the condition (Y+tZ=0) alone cannot replace the full cap source.

4. The second, coherently retained critical correspondence

Put

Aij=[zN](Q1−iz;Q)i(Q1−jz;Q)j. A_{ij}=[z^N](Q^{1-i}z;Q)_i(Q^{1-j}z;Q)_j.

Its exact Cauchy coefficient integral supplies a second phase. At a regular nonzero coefficient saddle (w), its leading action is

Φ=Li⁡2(w/I)+Li⁡2(w/J)−2Li⁡2(w)−2πilog⁡w, \Phi=\operatorname{Li}_2(w/I)+\operatorname{Li}_2(w/J) -2\operatorname{Li}_2(w)-2\pi i\log w,

with the inherited logarithmic sheets retained. Its stationary equation is

(1−w)2=(1−w/I)(1−w/J),w=2IJ−I−JIJ−1. (1-w)^2=(1-w/I)(1-w/J),\qquad w=\frac{2IJ-I-J}{IJ-1}. \tag{11}

zin=1−SS(1−R),zout=V(1−U)1−V,Bclock=S/J. z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{1-S}{S(1-R)},\qquad z_{\mathrm{out}}=\frac{V(1-U)}{1-V},\qquad B_{\mathrm{clock}}=S/J. \tag{12}

One then composes with the SAME original (L). At (t=1), away from excluded denominators, (7) implies (S=R^{-1}); (12) gives both momenta −1-1. Equations (11)–(12) retain precisely the orbital completion missing from (9).

Endpoint saddles of the finite sums and logarithmic/coordinate boundary strata still require their own source analysis. This note does not identify their union with a selected global canonical cycle.

5. An exact source/cap intersection and its remaining normal test

The endpoint issue is visible without arbitrary exterior data. Take the literal one-component four-plat word [−2,1,2,2][-2,1,2,2], so its prefix is B=R12R23−1B=R_{12}R_{23}^{-1}. The actual source gives

R23−1p=Nκ−1ed,LBp=NL2ed. R_{23}^{-1}p=N\kappa^{-1}e_d, \qquad L Bp=N L^2e_d.

(L2ed)j=N2xj−dFjFd−j∑v=0j(−1)vQv(v+1)/2−jvFj−vFv3. (L^2e_d)_j=N^2x^{j-d}\frac{F_j}{F_{d-j}} \sum_{v=0}^j \frac{(-1)^vQ^{v(v+1)/2-jv}}{F_{j-v}F_v^3}. \tag{13}

Its interior saddle, writing (V_0=e^{hv}), satisfies

JV02−3JV0+3J−1=0,zsource=(1−J)2J(V0−J). JV_0^2-3JV_0+3J-1=0,\qquad z_{\mathrm{source}}=\frac{(1-J)^2}{J(V_0-J)}. \tag{14}

The source curve before the final LL is exactly (X,Y,Z)=(s,s2,−s)(X,Y,Z)=(s,s^2,-s), by the literal Hurwitz maps. Formula (14) gives its image under that final LL, not an independently chosen boundary phase.

6. What conjugate Parseval matching says on the first channel

The doubled stationary equation must keep the opposite-root convention. If m+m_+ is the recorded clock in the first copy and m‾−\bar m_- the clock in the conjugate-root copy, differentiation in the retained phase gives

log⁡m++log⁡m‾−=0 \log m_++\log\bar m_-=0

on the inherited sheets (equivalently equality of the two integer recorded colors before taking a symbol). On a conjugation-fixed real critical stratum this implies |m|=1|m|=1. A real retained phase also has |t|=1|t|=1. It does not say that every complex stationary point of a deformed doubled period lies in that stratum.

For the first correspondence, the irreducible terminal cap condition in (10) is t=−Y/Zt=-Y/Z, with its full component retained. Therefore its conjugation-fixed stationary characters satisfy

|X|=|Y|=|Z|,m=−Y/X,t=−Y/Z. |X|=|Y|=|Z|,\qquad m=-Y/X,\qquad t=-Y/Z. \tag{19}

This is a concrete condition on the incoming prefix character curve. It is weaker than t=1t=1. Conversely, if m,tm,t are unit numbers and the denominators are nonzero, the cubic and terminal cap determine

X=t(1−m+m/t)2m2,Y=−mX,Z=mX/t. X=\frac{t(1-m+m/t)^2}{m^2},\qquad Y=-mX,\qquad Z=mX/t.

The outgoing cap parameter is c=−1/mc=-1/m. Thus this first channel has a particularly explicit angular locus; the alias channel (15) has a different relation between recorded clock and cap parameter. They cannot be conflated in a doubled-energy estimate.

As a check with the literal figure-eight prefix R12−1R23R_{12}^{-1}R_{23}, its transported source curve is

(X,Y,Z)=(s,s2,−s(1+s)2). (X,Y,Z)=(s,s^2,-s(1+s)^2).

Substituting in (19) gives |s|=|1+s|=1|s|=|1+s|=1, hence s=e±2πi/3s=e^{\pm2\pi i/3} and t=s/(1+s)2=1t=s/(1+s)^2=1. This is an algebraic transparency check for that channel and that actual prefix. It does not control the alias, escaping cap normal, or non-real doubled critical strata. It is therefore not a substitute for the exact figure-eight packet proof or a broad knot-energy theorem.

7. Shrinking the phase does not bound all character ratios

The condition |m|=1 above comes from stationary matching in the phase variable. An endpoint of a restricted real phase arc does not supply that equation. Bounded actual character coordinates do not replace it, since m is a ratio.

There is an exact escape in (15)–(16), using the SAME original prefix and cap as Section 5. Put tau=t-1 and write m=1+w/tau. The actual source equations Y=X^2 and Z=-X are satisfied exactly whenever

w^3-w^2+2tw-t=0.                                      (20)

This follows by direct substitution in their common factor, not by choosing independent exterior data. At t=1 the polynomial has discriminant -23, so all three roots are simple and nonzero. Each has a local analytic continuation w(t), including along t=exp(i theta) as theta tends to zero. The real root at one lies in (0.56,0.58).

Along each branch the incoming character and outgoing cap parameter are

(X,Y,Z)=(w^2/t,w^4/t^2,-w^2/t),
c=w^2/(w+tau).                                        (21)

They remain bounded and tend to finite irreducible characters. In particular the incoming limit has X=w(1)^2 != 0. Nevertheless |m| is comparable to |t-1|^(-1). The fixed-t cap normal calculation suggests I comparable to 2w(1)/h, a polynomial-radius physical chart. Its uniform validity when |t-1| is smaller than |h| is a separate joint-limit question; the fixed-t expansion does not prove it. Thus t near one and bounded character coordinates alone do not exclude the reduced channel, but do not prove its full normal lift.

With v=w(1), the limiting incoming coordinates may equivalently be written (v2,v(v-1)2,-v(v2-v+1)2). Equation (20) makes this exactly (v2,v4,-v^2). This also checks the unscaled limit of (16), including its genuine cap component.

This is an algebraic escape certificate, not a proof that an extra contribution survives in the original quantum arc integral. The normal sectors may still cancel this channel, as the exact identity M_1=I requires at phase one. Its calibrated action and coefficient must be retained to decide suppression for |t-1|=N^(-A). Polynomiality alone does not assign that action or its selected intersection number. No shrinking-arc sharp-energy theorem is claimed here.

There is a useful action test before asserting suppression. On any genuinely stationary branch with the original t-dependence retained, the envelope identity gives

d S / d log(t) = log(m),

with the inherited integer color lift. All original scalar factors are t-independent; the identity follows directly from t^n in (1). For m=1+w/tau with w tending to a nonzero limit, it gives on a fixed logarithmic sector

S(t)=S_* - tau log(tau) + O(tau).                       (22)

An additional bounded 2pii color lift changes only the O(tau) term. Hence this phase differential does not itself yield a positive Nlog|tau| vanishing factor. It also does not calculate S_, prove that the branch is stationary in every retained normal variable, or determine its selected coefficient. Those are essential to any actual suppression or survival claim. The exact cancellation at t=1 must not be replaced by assigning an unproved value to S_.

E_a=sum_(n,m) f_n conjugate(f_m) sinc(a(n-m)), a=N^-3.
