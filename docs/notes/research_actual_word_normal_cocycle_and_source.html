Actual braid words, the normal cocycle, and the original source

Private written proof, 19 November 2026. Author-final for independent review.

The first normal layer of the actual root braid algebra has an explicit generator transport. In a common frame that makes the remaining lowering operator constant, every internal crossing has zero quotient-normal class. The boundary crossing has a finite formula from its actual capped prefix coefficients. The original closing of any word is then a sum of Levi traces, diagonal normal terms, and specified quadratic couplings of off-diagonal blocks.

This is a finite source theorem. It does not replace the closing by a positive norm or prove a geometric coefficient is nonzero. The complete directs are F, the reciprocal flag classification; C, the actual prefix Ward equations; and R, the actual reduced braid representation and its source. All additional normal, frame, generator, and word calculations are proved below.

5. The normal class in the physical flag blocks

Choose F’s fixed complements, so the pair spaces have dimensions (k,l)(k,l) and (l,k)(l,k), and the root word has the form Xβ=(aβbβoff0dβ),Yβ=(dβfβ0aβ).(22) X_\beta=\begin{pmatrix}a_\beta&b_\beta^{\mathrm{ off}}\\0&d_\beta\end{pmatrix}, \qquad Y_\beta=\begin{pmatrix}d_\beta&f_\beta\\0&a_\beta\end{pmatrix}. \qquad\text{(22)} The complements identify AA and BB with their identity arrows as in F. The common crossing scalar is still included in every root matrix in (20),(22). A logarithmic derivative of a scalar multiple of a generator adds a scalar multiple of bβb_\beta, which lies in VV, so it has zero quotient-normal class. This observation does not remove the scalar’s root value. Nor does it remove its derivative when differentiating the whole closed source rather than computing its root value.

6. The exact closing of an arbitrary signed word

Write the literal word as β=gL⋯g1\beta=g_L\cdots g_1, with each letter an actual signed generator. To prove (27), the actual first derivative of the matrix product is a sum with one (Ci,Di)(C_i,D_i) insertion. Cyclicity of each ordinary block trace changes its complementary factors to (25). Expanding Tr⁡(CiXγi−DiYγi)\operatorname{Tr}(C_iX_{\gamma_i}-D_iY_{\gamma_i}) gives exactly its four displayed terms. Each Bi,FiB_i,F_i in (26) is a sum with exactly one root off-diagonal letter. For an ordered upper-triangular product this follows by choosing the unique step from its lower block to its upper block. Thus the last two terms in (27) are the actual quadratic couplings between a root off-diagonal and a normal off-diagonal. Trace removes either one in isolation; it does not remove their products. No adjoint, complex conjugation, or norm has replaced these bilinear contractions.

For a self weight χ=±1\chi=\pm1, the source contribution is χTr⁡(bβ|Uχ)/ε\chi\operatorname{Tr}(b_\beta|_{U_\chi})/\varepsilon, counted once. Summing these and (27) over all inversion orbits gives the entire original pointed source. Separately prescribed exterior scalar lines multiply that full result with their actual values. They retain their product-rule derivatives on the meridian germ. Equation (27) computes the original root value from the reduced first normal. For a fixed full root operator, the full reduced first derivative is lift-dependent, although its quotient class is not. A derivative of the original closed source generally also needs the reduced second coefficient against the simple pole in (10); no such extra jet is discarded or supplied by the root formula (27).

7. One boundary occurrence and the exact remaining duty

Suppose a word has a single σ1ϵ\sigma_1^{\epsilon}, ϵ=±1\epsilon=\pm1, and all other letters are internal. R’s cyclic source identity permits rotating it to σ1ϵη\sigma_1^{\epsilon}\eta, with η\eta internal. The same cyclicity holds in each paired block since DμD_\mu is a scalar there on each weight. By (11), only the boundary letter remains in the normal sum of (27). All extensive internal spectators remain in the actual root word in (28).

For the positive left stabilization this is consistent with an independent whole-meridian source contraction. If Wη=I⊗WoldW_\eta=I\otimes W_{\mathrm{ old}}, R’s zero-transfer row and the highest cut force the new first and old first occupations both to zero. The retained pivotal factor there is x1−Nx^{1-N}. Hence Φ0(R1(I⊗Wold))=κ(M)x1−NΦ0(Wold).(29) \Phi_0(R_1(I\otimes W_{\mathrm{ old}})) =\kappa(M)x^{1-N}\Phi_0(W_{\mathrm{ old}}). \qquad\text{(29)} The actual positive zero-framing compensation cancels this same scalar, as in R. Equation (29) retains its true meridian derivative; it is not a new nonzero-source theorem.

The result completes the finite normal transport of actual signed words and their prescribed closing. It reduces a one-boundary presentation to an explicit boundary covector against an actual internal root representation, rather than an unspecified normal matrix. It supplies no analytic lower bound for that contraction, no positive Levi metric, and no selected geometric coefficient. The remaining coefficient and relative-cycle estimates cannot be deduced from radical nilpotence or invertibility of the word.
