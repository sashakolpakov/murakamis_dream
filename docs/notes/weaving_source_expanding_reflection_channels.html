The weaving reflection source has expanding nonordinary channels

28 October 2026. A bounded negative correction would be enough to evade the exact indefinite-Gram obstruction at the leading exponential scale. This note tests a specific way to obtain that correction: ordinary-color unitary traces and finitely many of their source jets. The ORIGINAL five-strand weaving source does not admit that particular reflection-positive decomposition. Its retained projective source has nonreal EXPANDING moment channels, not only the constant channel seen at N=2.

This is a fixed-root, word-growth theorem. It is NOT a disproof of a polynomial-in-N correction for each fixed knot, an asymptotic positive formula valid only at large N, or a word-dependent scalar norm. Those would require additional arguments. In particular nothing below identifies the negative source terms with the bounded ordinary-color tail of the regular meridian period.

1. The exact arbitrary-width reflection pairing

At complete color the ORIGINAL balanced crossing matrices are symmetric:

R_plus^t=R_plus, R_minus^t=R_minus.

For example the positive coefficient with input (a,b), output (c,j), c=a+b-j, is

kappa x^(-b-j) Q^(jb) F_c F_a/(F_(a-j)F_bF_j).

Transposing exchanges (a,b) with (c,j); c-b=a-j makes this coefficient identical. The negative statement follows by inversion. The proved complete antiunitary identity therefore gives R_minus=P R_plus^dagger P, including kappa.

Let the width p be odd. Put U=product_(i odd)R_i^+, and let J reverse the p head factors. Since the odd crossings are disjoint,

V=product_(i even)R_i^-=J U^dagger J,
B=VU=J U^dagger J U,
B^dagger J=J B.                               (1)

The actual first-cut source is F=P_0 tensor g^(tensor(p-1)); the equivalent averaged marked row may be used only through its already proved whole-source identity. With the original writhe-zero framing and its external line retained, the reduced weave moments are

k_n=Tr(F B^n),
h(X,Y)=Tr(F J X^dagger J Y),
h(B^i,B^j)=k_(i+j).                           (2)

Thus a definite reflection subspace containing these half-states would give a positive Hankel form on the sequence (k_n). Equations (1)–(2) are an actual source pairing. They do not assert that the involution J, or its original marked co-source, is positive.

For p=5 the time-ordered braid block is [1,3,-2,-4]. Its physical over and under encounters alternate, and its strand permutation is a five-cycle. In particular the closures at n=5m+2 are knots. The scalar is the original reduced Kashaev value. Restoring the full ADO dimension line multiplies every moment at this fixed N by the same known nonzero scalar; it changes none of the poles below.

2. An exact selected-source computation at N=3

Put omega=Q=exp(2 pi i/3). Then x=omega and g_a=omega^(1-a). The two positive and two negative common Cartan factors cancel in B. Its entries lie in Z[omega]. The audit constructs this literal operator and its original source exactly, rather than sampling its spectrum numerically.

The resulting rational generating function is

G(z)=sum_(n>=0) k_n z^n=A(z)/D(z),             (3)

where A and D are COPRIME integer polynomials and deg D=43. The full coefficient lists are saved in the audit certificate. The denominator is

D=-(z-1)^3 P4a^2 P4b P8^2 P12,

P4a=z^4-17z^3+55z^2-17z+1,
P4b=z^4-5z^3+7z^2-5z+1,

P8=z^8-19z^7+126z^6-380z^5+557z^4
                  -380z^3+126z^2-19z+1,

P12=z^12-57z^11+1032z^10-8306z^9+34071z^8
      -75492z^7+95323z^6-75492z^5+34071z^4
      -8306z^3+1032z^2-57z+1.                 (4)

The first moments are

0,1,217,15349,707317,26955828,936961969,... .

This is not an inferred finite recurrence. After discovering its coefficients, the audit verifies the reversed denominator polynomial as an EXACT annihilating matrix identity on every source-supported integer-degree block. It then verifies the numerator and coprimeness. Cayley–Hamilton-style multiplication of that displayed identity proves (3) at ALL n, independently of any finite fit.

The ordinary source includes only first-head index zero, hence supports total integer degrees 0,…,8. The exact arithmetic represents a+b omega by the integer matrix [[a,-b],[b,a-b]], so the calculation uses neither floating eigenvalues nor approximate cancellation. The local matrices and pivot are also checked directly against the existing independently assembled ORIGINAL balanced crossing.

3. The retained source couples to nonreal expanding modes

Exact rational root isolation places a root z_0 of P8 in

.41<Re z_0<.42, .11<Im z_0<.13.              (5)

In particular |z_0|<1. Numerically z_0 is approximately .4133686+.1189402i, giving |1/z_0| approximately 2.324825. The numerical values are explanatory only; (5) proves expansion. Since gcd(A,P8)=1, this is a genuine DOUBLE pole of the selected whole source. Its moment contribution has the form (a+b n)lambda^n with b!=0 and lambda=1/z_0.

It survives on the all-knot subsequence n=5m+2. Indeed the exact fifth-power resultant is

Res_z(P8(z),w-z^5)
  =w^8-58634w^7+98866981w^6-2511273890w^5
     +449769171292w^4-2511273890w^3
     +98866981w^2-58634w+1.                   (6)

This polynomial is squarefree and coprime to the corresponding resultant of D/P8^2. Thus no two relevant fifth powers collide and cancel the selected double pole. The box (5) also proves Im(z_0^5)>0: for z_0=x+iy it is y(5x4-10x2y2+y4), whose lower bound on that box is positive. Consequently the generating series of k_(5m+2) has a NONREAL pole strictly inside the unit disk.

This selected-source assertion is stronger than noticing nonreal eigenvalues in the ambient transfer matrix. Such eigenvalues could have been invisible to the marked cap; coprimeness in (3) proves that these are visible.

4. What this rules out, and what it leaves open

An ordinary-color braid action in the positive path model is unitary. At fixed N, its closing trace is therefore uniformly bounded as the weave repetition varies. Finitely many fixed-order analytic jets of those finite matrices have at most polynomial growth in the repetition: differentiating a product chooses only polynomially many factors, with the remaining zero-order factors unitary. Their generating functions are analytic in |z|<1.

Suppose the reflection form (2), restricted even to nonempty half-states B^(1+5i), split into a positive form plus such an ordinary-color/finite-unitary-jet correction. Then the sequence k_(5m+2) would differ by a polynomially bounded sequence from a positive Hankel moment sequence. This is impossible because of the nonreal pole just proved.

Here is an elementary justification, avoiding a spectral-positivity assumption about B. A positive Hankel sequence a_m of exponential size defines the inner product <Xi,Xj>=a_(i+j) on polynomials. Multiplication T by X is symmetric. Iterated Cauchy–Schwarz gives

||Tf||<=||f||^(1-2^(-j)) ||T^(2^j)f||^(2^(-j)).

The exponential moment bound makes ||T^n f||<=C_f R^n, so letting j tend to infinity proves ||Tf||<=R||f||. Thus T extends to a bounded self-adjoint operator on the Hilbert completion. Its scalar resolvent <1,(I-zT)^(-1)1> is analytic at EVERY nonreal z. One may verify the usual resolvent fact directly from ||(T-w)f||>=|Im w| ||f|| and the same inequality for its adjoint. It agrees near zero with the moment series. Subtracting a polynomially bounded sequence changes that series by a function analytic in the unit disk, which cannot remove the nonreal pole in (5)–(6). This is the contradiction.

The conclusion concerns a source-compatible REFLECTION/HANKEL decomposition with a correction uniformly polynomial in repetition. The nonordinary source radical cannot be bounded in that way just by invoking ordinary-color unitarity. It retains expanding channels.

It remains possible that, for each fixed knot as N tends to infinity, a different marked extension has a polynomially bounded negative part; this fixed-N computation does not decide that question. Likewise a nonlinear or word-dependent norm formula need not give the Hankel form (2), and is not excluded. The positive cyclic norm already known for all knots is a different construction. No broad volume theorem, asymptotic counterexample, or positivity failure for the scalar values themselves follows here.
