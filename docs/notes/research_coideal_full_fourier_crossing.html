A full Fourier compiler for the actual coideal crossing

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The actual coideal graph crossing has an explicit triangular matrix between two unitary Fourier bases. Its entries are finite Gaussian binomials and finite products. A full initialized Toeplitz adapter then gives every pair of neighboring heights and the complete arbitrary-width operator, with all deeper channels still coherent. Both crossing signs and the original cup source are retained.

This is a finite source compiler. It neither replaces a coherent input by independent column norms nor estimates the original marked scalar below.

6. Sewing, coverage and remaining scope

In a composition the full adapter, Fourier interfaces, source column and literal closing row are all transported. If FF is an invertible coordinate frame, they become F−1uF^{-1}u and ℓF\ell F, with the full pulled-back Gram retained. Different faces share their actual tail variables; no independent resummation or probability is inserted. The original external χN\chi_N and every marked or framing scalar remain in their prescribed positions.

T already gives the full two-parameter crossing and both signs. P already gives initialized Toeplitz neighbor adapters, and the bidiagonal-height note refines those adapters on the physical grid. B already identifies the matched geometric packet and its legal charged observation. None of these general comparisons is claimed new. The addition is the explicit entire Fourier matrix (5)–(6), its finite Newton proof, and its coherent full-tail use (19)–(22).

The equations are linear maps on full packets, so retaining them as auxiliary source equations does not increase a quartic variational objective’s degree. They compare representations of the same packet; they impose no equation between independently summed physical heights. They do not identify this simple coideal module with the confluent pair-Casimir compiler or remove its bilinear Conway gluing gates.

This note makes no assertion about canonical quantum holonomy observations, geometric contour coefficients, or the propagation of a positive packet through an arbitrary word. In particular (15),(21) are complete coherent Gram identities, not lower bounds for the original marked observation in (22). No global scalar lower bound or arbitrary-short-filling finite-exception theorem follows.
