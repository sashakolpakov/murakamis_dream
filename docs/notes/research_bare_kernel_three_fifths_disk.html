The full original edge kernel on a three-fifths angular disk

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The full same-index kernel has its original nonzero geometric expansion on the complex disk of radius 3/53/5 in each angle. This extends the available height range near completeness. The proof uses the existing split-cotangent contour mechanism, with new disk-specific phase, omitted-index and lattice-mode estimates. No numerical certificate is used. This is an edge-source theorem; an angular complement estimate for a subsequent network contraction is separate.

All inequalities have strict reserves on compact sets, so the proof works on a slightly enlarged parameter neighborhood. Cauchy estimates retain every fixed angular derivative. Separate evenness of the actual sum and of the uniquely continued phase and amplitude gives holomorphic descent to the ordinary colors near the ramified complete angles; away from ramification it is immediate. Ordinary-color Cauchy estimates give all fixed spectral derivatives. No artificial odd-level normal is introduced. This completes the theorem.

5. Scope and pinned direct sources

The existing rectangular kernel theorem, its exact interpolation, normalization and split-cotangent mechanism are prior coverage. The contribution here is the larger angular disk, with fully written uniform estimates for its high half, low tails and lattice modes. The disk contains the small positive saddles relevant to a possible degree-twelve network extension, but the theorem does not bound the whole angular complement or prove that network extension.
