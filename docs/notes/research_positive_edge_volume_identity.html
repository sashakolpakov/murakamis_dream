The volume of a positive edge and its primitive action

For every positive configuration of the eight original edge tetrahedra, their volume is Ve(t,u)=2πℜψ(t,u)−2{ℑtℑrt+ℑuℑru}.(1) \boxed{\quad V_e(t,u)=2\pi\Re\psi(t,u) -2\{\Im t\,\Im r_t+\Im u\,\Im r_u\}. \quad} \qquad\text{(1)} Here ψ\psi is the original edge phase, normalized at completeness, and rt,rur_t,r_u are its original marked meridian increments. The identity holds throughout the positive edge domain. It is not restricted to a filling path.

At a positive solution of the primitive filling equations, the correction terms combine exactly with the quadratic crossing terms and the linear filling charges. Thus the stationary action has the absolute volume normalization, without a separate integration along a common-charge cone path. One must still establish that the positive cells with the prescribed marking realize the intended filled manifold. This calculation does not replace that geometric assembly, nor does it establish a quantum asymptotic.

1. The original shapes and branches

Use the positive edge domain and its strip coordinates P,QP,Q. The branches are fixed at P=Q=0P=Q=0. The positive domain is biholomorphic to the product of two unit disks, so the original closed holomorphic period form has a single-valued primitive there. The ordered original shapes are (A,B,−1/A,−1/B),(C,D0,−1/C,−1/D0).(4) (A,B,-1/A,-1/B),\qquad (C,D_0,-1/C,-1/D_0). \qquad\text{(4)} The symbol D0D_0 denotes a tetrahedron shape; D(z)D(z) always denotes its volume function. 3. Converting to primitive boundary coordinates

Write t=st+iht,u=su+ihu,rt=kt+igt,ru=ku+igu.(17) t=s_t+ih_t,\quad u=s_u+ih_u,\qquad r_t=k_t+ig_t,\quad r_u=k_u+ig_u. \qquad\text{(17)} The exact linear period identities give 4d(EP+EQ)=ktdst+kudsu+htdgt+hudgu.(18) 4\,d(E_P+E_Q) =k_t\,ds_t+k_u\,ds_u+h_t\,dg_t+h_u\,dg_u. \qquad\text{(18)} Consequently (13) and (15) imply dVe=−2(ktdst+kudsu+htdgt+hudgu).(19) dV_e=-2(k_t\,ds_t+k_u\,ds_u +h_t\,dg_t+h_u\,dg_u). \qquad\text{(19)} On the other hand, (3) gives 2πdℜψ=−2(ktdst+kudsu−gtdht−gudhu).(20) 2\pi\,d\Re\psi =-2(k_t\,ds_t+k_u\,ds_u-g_t\,dh_t-g_u\,dh_u). \qquad\text{(20)} Subtracting 2d(htgt+hugu)2\,d(h_tg_t+h_ug_u) produces (19). 5. The geometric and analytic obligations left by this identity

The result calibrates the action whenever the specified positive configuration is available. If its original face attachments and primitive peripheral curves realize a complete filling, the cell volume in (25) is the hyperbolic volume of that filled manifold: the tetrahedron interiors partition its nonsingular part, and the filled cores have volume zero. This is an implication with the geometric realization stated as a hypothesis.

For example, the negative-corridor criterion gives a positive primitive solution at unit charges when all selected crossing magnitudes are at least four, independently of the path length. Formula (25) now gives its absolute cell-volume anchor directly. The existence of a common-charge cone path is not needed for that calculation. The separate pointwise marked-completion theorem verifies the face assembly, full primitive periods and solid-torus completion for every positive critical point with the required strict core signs. It therefore supplies the actual marked hyperbolic-volume interpretation for this corridor class.

Neither this identity nor existence and uniqueness of the positive solution supplies the coefficient of its saddle in the original root-of-unity period. The whole-source estimate and noncancellation problem remain separate.
