Actual sewing shifts are divided-Ward images of the wrap source

28 October 2026. The unit shifts in the exact cyclic sewing have an explicit relation to the ORIGINAL integral divided Hopf adjoint. The relation holds on the actual balanced head, including an exact whole-q source profile. The large scalar powers cancel between the two ends of a transfer. Thus these shifts need not be assigned an auxiliary unipotent action.

This does not yet give a geometric centralizer chain map. There is a first explicit obstruction to the simplest proposed extension: independent head adjoints are not the full mixed coproduct adjoint. Their difference has a nonzero ORIGINAL crossing-cap pairing. The internal geometric interval cannot be inferred by omitting it.

1. The literal integral head and its two wrap coupons

Fix N>=2 and put m=N-1, L=N(N-1)/2, rho=q-q^-1. Use the actual integral color of highest weight m, with basis v_j,

E^(a)v_j=[m-j+a choose a]_q v_(j-a),
F^(a)v_j=[j+a choose a]_q v_(j+a),
K v_j=q^(m-2j)v_j.                           (1)

Terms outside the head are zero. Symmetric Gaussian coefficients are their Laurent-polynomial values, not quotients evaluated at a root. Let B_+=|m><0|, B_-=|0><m|. These are the literal missing cyclic-wrap matrix units. Write J_+,J_- for the unit truncated raising/lowering shifts and J v_j=j v_j.

Use the FULL integral Hopf adjoints

A_(E^(n))(C)=sum_(k=0)^n (-1)^k q^(-k(n-1))
                     E^(n-k) C E^(k) K^-n,
A_(F^(n))(C)=sum_(k=0)^n (-1)^k q^(k(n-1))
                     F^(n-k)K^-k C K^k F^(k). (2)

The mixed terms in (2) remain even when E(N),F(N) individually vanish on the N-dimensional head. These are linear adjoint images; no assertion that a single A_h is a square-zero differential is made.

2. Exact root identities in the ORIGINAL balanced basis

At q=exp(pi*i/N) one has [m choose k]_q=1 for every k. Indeed each numerator factor [N-s]_q equals [s]_q, with all factors below N nonzero. Direct substitution in (2) gives

A_(E^(N))(B_+)=(-1)^m J_- q^J,
A_(F^(N))(B_-)=-q^-1 J_+ q^-J                (3)

in the integral basis. For example only the k-th term contributes to entry (k-1,k) of the first identity. Its coefficient is (-1)^k q^(-km) q^(-N(m-2k))=(-1)^m q^k. For the second identity, entry (j+1,j) comes from k=m-j and has coefficient -q^(-j-1).

The original balanced crossing basis is the diagonal transport

D_j=(-rho)^j q^(j(j+1)/2).                   (4)

It gives exactly the original raising coefficients 1-Q^(j+1) and balanced Ecal=rho^2 E coefficients Q^-j-1, with Q=q^2. Transport the actual adjoint and the wrap coupons by this same D, keeping their scalars. Equations (3)-(4) imply

A^bal_(E^(N))(B_+)=-rho^(-N) q^-L J_-,
A^bal_(F^(N))(B_-)=(-1)^m rho^N q^L J_+.     (5)

Hence on the actual two-head sewing edge,

J_+ tensor J_- =(-1)^N
      A^bal_(F^(N))(B_-) tensor A^bal_(E^(N))(B_+). (6)

The rho^N and quadratic phase factors cancel EXACTLY. The same statement with exchanged heads gives the other transfer. The marked x^-1 coefficient in each actual transfer remains attached. Neither endpoint wrap is reset or replaced by a new vacuum.

Thus an actual divided-current description of every local sewing shift exists, at every N, before Gaussian contraction. It uses the mixed Hopf adjoint, rather than an ordinary commutator with the vanishing divided matrix.

3. The whole normal profile is explicit

The root formulas cannot simply be differentiated as constants. Keep q generic and the same integral head and balanced frame. Put

M_col=-q^N,
G_j(q)=M_col^j [m choose j]_q,
C_E(q)=(-rho)^(-N)q^(-Nm-L),
C_F(q)=(-1)^m(-rho)^N q^(L+1-m^2).

Then the exact WHOLE-q identities are

A^bal_(E^(N))(B_+)=C_E(q) J_- diag(G_j(q)),
A^bal_(F^(N))(B_-)=C_F(q) J_+ diag(G_j(q)).   (7)

To verify the first, its integral (k-1,k) coefficient is (-1)^k q^(k(N+1)-Nm)[m choose k]_q. The balanced-frame ratio and the wrap scalar in (4) change this into C_E M_col^k[m choose k]_q. For the second, put k=m-j in (2); its integral coefficient is (-1)^(m-j)q^(-(m-j)m)[m choose j]_q. The same frame calculation gives the displayed C_F G_j.

At the root G_j=1 and C_E C_F=(-1)^N. Off the root the diagonal profile is nonconstant and may have source divisors. It is tied to the already computed actual color-source profile:

G_j=M_col^(2j) V_j,
V_j=[m choose j]_q/M_col^j.                  (8)

Thus the whole-head Gamma/source comparison can transport this precise profile, with its extra character retained. Equation (7) itself does not invert G_j at a zero. Fractional source and dual lattices, all normal derivatives, and the original peripheral line remain necessary.

The actual moment carry at these endpoints is also explicit. Let S=J_++B_- be the cyclic raising completion. For every polynomial p (indeed every function on the displayed finite arguments), entrywise multiplication proves

p(J)S-S p(J+1)=[p(0)-p(N)]B_-,
p(J)S^-1-S^-1 p(J-1)=[p(m)-p(-1)]B_+.       (8a)

In particular [J,S]=S-NB_- and [J,S^-1]=-S^-1+NB_+. Before taking moments, the whole position-clock identity is

z^J S z^-J=z S+(z^(1-N)-z)B_-,
z^J S^-1 z^-J=z^-1 S^-1+(z^(N-1)-z^-1)B_+. (8b)

Euler derivatives in z recover every polynomial/logarithmic moment with its actual endpoint. The factors z^N are retained before specialization. For the two-head cyclic transfer A,

[J_1+J_2,A]
    =Na[-B_-,1 tensor S_2^-1+S_1 tensor B_+,2]. (8c)

The two corner contributions cancel where both wrap edges overlap. The nilpotent transfer has zero total-index commutator. Thus its sewing sources have exactly the N-step moment carry required to repair the cyclic completion. Equations (7)-(8c) retain that carry; they do not turn it into an ordinary derivation after cyclic specialization.

4. The first global mismatch has an actual nonzero cap row

Equation (6) contains INDEPENDENT adjoints on the two heads. The original crossing intertwines the actual coproduct, whose divided formulas have all mixed terms. Those are different operations. The difference is already visible at N=2 without an asymptotic argument.

Take q=i, Q=-1, M=1, x=i. In the original balanced basis,

E=(1/2)E_01, F=2E_10, K=diag(i,-i),
Delta E^(2)=(1/4)E_(00,11),
Delta F^(2)=4E_(11,00).

Let C=B_- tensor B_+. Direct use of (2) with the full coproduct matrices gives

A^tot_(F^(2)E^(2))(C)
   =[[-i,0,0,0],
     [0,-i,1,0],
     [0,1,i,0],
     [0,0,0,i]].                            (9)

The independent product in (6) is J_+ tensor J_-, whose only nonzero entry is (row2,column1)=1. Denote their difference by D_mix. It is not a suppressed scalar or a zero source current. Keep the original stripped crossing

R_0=[[1,0,0,0],[0,0,-i,0],
                     [0,-i,2,0],[0,0,0,1]],

and its original normalized average first cut with closed pivots (-i,i). Exactly,

Phi(D_mix R_0)=1/2,
Phi(R_0 D_mix)=-1/2.                         (10)

The actual common Cartan factor kappa multiplies these by kappa and is retained; restoring the other external factors does not make them zero. Thus the proposed replacement of the independent sewing image by a diagonal divided Ward generator drops an actual nonzero endpoint contribution.

This rules out that replacement, not every corrected current construction. A valid tensor/chain fusion must carry D_mix and its counterparts, rather than use independent adjoint identities as if they already had coproduct naturality.

5. The geometric endpoint condition is now specific

There is also a precise target on the ORIGINAL whole word, not only on a separate source cone. The already proved actual Borel Ward calculation gives the complete principal conjugation fields

(ad_Ktot-I)/H -> A(h/2),
ad_Ecal,tot/H -> A f_sl,
ad_Ftot/H -> A e_sl,

where H=Log Q. On the original inverse-eigenflag framing chart the compensated cut-Borel fields are xi_1=2(r-1)partial_r+2s partial_s and xi_2=(2r-1)partial_r+2s partial_s. Define the literal finite operator, with the specified LEFT multiplication,

S_cut=-q K_1-1,
W_cut=ad_Ftot-2(ad_Ktot-I)-ad_Ecal,tot,
V_N(C)=S_cut W_cut(C).                      (11a)

Its cut symbol is S_cut -> A_cut^-1-1=s, so V_N/H -> s(xi_2-xi_1)=s partial_r=V_J. It vanishes exactly on the unweighted actual braid word. The target classical ODE consequently has endpoint r->r+s at time one. This gives the principal generator; it is not a proof that an arbitrary finite exponential of V_N equals the retained quantum meridian endpoint.

The target co-source row is explicit as well. For Phi_A(C)=Tr(A C) set B=A S_cut, in that order. Then

Phi_A(V_N(C))=Tr[W_cut^vee(B) C],
W_cut^vee(B)=B Ftot-Ktot Ftot B Ktot^-1
          -2(Ktot^-1 B Ktot-B)
          -(Ktot^-1 B Ecal,tot-Ecal,tot Ktot^-1 B). (11b)

Each term follows by cyclicity of the trace, so the original cut row and pivot need not be declared invariant. The whole weighted Ward formula transports (11a)-(11b) with the actual lower-moment Green currents: a moment polynomial p leaves its specified forward difference Delta_t p, not a zero endpoint. The normal clock/pivot adapters in the divided Ward theorem remain attached. These target data already exist; (6) does not by itself supply their source map.

The actual paired Frobenius source already supplies a precise target generator. In the marked variables of the unprojectivized source torsor, let s=C alpha^2, r=(1+m_src)/2+D alpha^2. The actual divided-source generator e-h-f has field 2 partial_msrc; it fixes alpha. Hence its source-dependent multiple

s(e-h-f) -> s partial_r=V_J.                (11c)

At fixed alpha its actual standard-vector group is I+b s [[-1,1],[-1,1]]. Its square-zero matrix gives an exact additive group law, and the paired dual parameter changes by m_src -> m_src+2bs. At b=1 the frame changes by r -> r+s, precisely the complete marked meridian endpoint. Thus the desired endpoint is not an unspecified abstract additive action.

Equation (11c) is a theorem on that actual paired source family. Equations (6)-(8) identify the literal sewing currents and their whole normal profiles. The missing map must fuse the INDEPENDENT images in (6) into the FULL ordinary/divided coproduct current, carry the correction detected in (10) and all its Green-moment rows, and then identify its transverse Gaussian image with (11a)-(11c).

The generic principal density c_beta Omega_cut and its nonzero logarithmic residue do not perform that fusion. No nonzero selected geometric image, equality of the relative coordinates, or volume lower bound is claimed from the two separate endpoint constructions.
