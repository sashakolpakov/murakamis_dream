An exact Gamma section and affine extension of the original crossing source

22 November 2026. Written research proof. Earlier author-final notes and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The original crossing source has an exact canonical Gamma solution of its homogeneous boundary equation. The physical polynomial period is a different, explicitly sourced solution: its first-order defect is a known polynomial in the cyclic boundary characters. Keeping that defect gives a flat affine extension and its full cyclic monodromy. It does not identify the physical period with the Gamma section.

The constructions below concern solution and source channels over a boundary-parameter difference field. They do not replace an original Conway leg by a two-dimensional vector space.

5. The full cyclic norm and affine monodromy

8. What can and cannot be sewn from this extension

Affine source extensions and their tensor face and corner terms are already present in the earlier factorial cut-source treatment. The new information here is the explicit original-crossing multipliers g,J,Fg,J,F, their exact norm, and their calibration to the physical polynomial source. No novelty is claimed for upper-triangular extensions or variation of constants as general methods.

Tensoring (31) keeps all source terms. These are solution/source channels over the boundary difference field.
