The double-protected spectral cap does not admit the naive finite collar

The obstruction concerns finite continuation in the specified chart. It does not exclude other blowup coordinates or prove a global group, killed-meridian, or volume obstruction.

1. The original cap coefficient and the remaining parameter

Use the original ordered face quartet (x,b,c,y), with protected x=b=0. For a positive-length LEFT row the limiting degrees are

q=(0,0,1-nu,nu), 0<nu<=1/2.

At delta=1/2, sigma=2, the sharp coefficient action is zero. Its free nu stationarity with finite positive ordinary multipliers gives c=y. It does not fix nu. The proposed full-fiber boundary reduction is a separate theorem; the present obstruction does not require it. In particular it applies to the interior range 0<nu<1/2 if subsequent reductions exclude the terminal value.

The exact original scalar has not lost its phase. Put theta=pi/N, d=N-1 and take i=0, 0<=j<=floor(N/2)-1. The original source identity is

(-1)^d H_V = sum b^i c^(d-j)
               (prod_(a<i)alpha_a)(prod_(a>j)alpha_a) K_(i,j),
alpha_a=2cos((2a+1)theta)-sigma.

At x=0, K_(0,j)=y^j prod_(a=1)^j 4sin^2(a theta). At sigma=2 every unused alpha_a=-4sin^2((a+1/2)theta)<0. Consequently the RAW coefficient of c(d-j)yj in H_V is (-1)^j A_j, where

A_j=csc^2(theta/2)
        prod_(a=1)^j sin^2(a theta)/sin^2((a+1/2)theta)>0. (1)

Indeed prod_(a=0)^d 4sin^2((a+1/2)theta)=4. This is valid at both parities and retains the original omitted half. The signed output clock c_tilde=-c makes (-1)^j c_tilde(d-j)=(-1)d c^(d-j), a common recorded sign; it is precisely the post-pole sign choice, not deletion of the j-dependent phase. Thus c=y corresponds to the signed middle q=-y in the existing reflection chart. This does not by itself impose a global face-sign solution.

2. Literal raw reflections at the cap

Use the matrices in the literal signed line flags, section1. An anti-Mobius reflection is represented by J with det J=-1; ordered products are J_2 conjugate(J_1). Put s=sin(pi delta), k=cos(pi delta). The protected outer line and ordinary outer circle are

J_x=-i I,
J_y=[[i cos(2pi delta),sin^2(2pi delta)/y],
     [y,i cos(2pi delta)]].

For signed middle q and its actual root T_q2=qy+q2 s^2,

J_q=[[i-2kT_q/y,-4k^2(y+q s^2)/y^2],
     [q,i+2kT_q/y]].                              (2)

At the protected input b=0, T_b=0 and J_b=[[i,-4k^2/y],[0,i]]. These are the actual two marked line lifts; their height is 2k^2/y. Define, with the original head order,

A_q=J_q conjugate(J_x), B_q=J_y conjugate(J_q),
P=B_q A_q=J_y conjugate(J_x).                     (3)

Now set delta=1/2, c=y, q=-y directly in the polynomial matrices (2). All entries are finite, without any division by T or k:

J_x=-iI, J_b=iI,
J_y=[[-i,0],[y,-i]], J_q=[[i,0],[-y,i]]=-J_y,
P=[[1,0],[iy,1]]!=I.

Direct multiplication in (3) gives

(A_b,B_b)=(-I,-P),
(A_q,B_q)=(-P,-I).                               (4)

Thus there is NO finite invertible C with

C A_b C^(-1)=A_q, C B_b C^(-1)=B_q.              (5)

The first equality would conjugate the central matrix -I to the noncentral matrix -P. This obstruction remains in PSL2: identity cannot be conjugated to a nontrivial parabolic. Changing central lifts or adding decorating flags does not alter that central/noncentral distinction.

The coincident marked lines therefore do not give a finite specialization of the proved original collar. The negative output circle is the same projective circle as y, and the central MAIN head has moved to the other ordered leg. Merely listing matching traces or free fixed flags cannot repair the required simultaneous ordered conjugations.

3. An exact family exposes the singular collar

There is a useful diagnostic family with the SAME protected faces, even though its terminal row need not be among the surviving global maxima. For 0<delta<1/2 set c=y/s^2 and take q=-c,T_q=0. The local endpoint equations then have nu=1/2, and the existing centralizer has R0=-1. Write h=sin(2pi delta)>0. In the original frame its projective matrix is

C=[[0,-h/y],[-y/h,0]], C^2=I.                   (6)

For example,

A_b=[[-1,-4ik^2/y],[0,-1]],
A_q=[[-1,0],[-iy/s^2,-1]],

and (6) conjugates A_b to A_q since h2=4s2k^2. It commutes with the whole ordered P, so it conjugates B_b to B_q as well. Its projective determinant is -1; choosing a determinant-one central lift does not change any conclusion.

As delta approaches1/2, (6) has no invertible projective limit: multiply by h/y to obtain a matrix tending to [[0,0],[-1,0]], of rank one. This is the explicit mechanism behind (4), not an arbitrary failure of continuity at collided ideal vertices. The marked-strip height collapses while the noncentral outer product remains noncentral in this frame.

Imposing the free-spectral critical endpoint equations with x=b=0 forces nu=1/2, outside the strict nonterminal stratum. At the cap the exact monomer permits arbitrary nu. Hence (6) does not approximate arbitrary cap loads by actual regular marked critical data. In the direct cap circle matrices the parameter nu has disappeared; any recovery of it needs additional boundary data rather than an unproved assignment of angles to coincident centers.

4. Exact limitation

This refutes only the naive finite-collar extension of the existing line/reflection chart, with its original ordered heads and retained signed clock. It does not refute a different blowup, a global block reduction, or cancellation of the original cap source. A parameter-dependent common conjugation may change which limiting heads remain noncentral, and needs its own whole-source flags and sewing analysis. No such operation is assumed here.

In particular (4) is NOT a global group representation: its required local relation already fails. The central input therefore cannot be called a killed meridian in an established representation, and no representation-volume gap follows from it. The existing regular fundamental-chain theorem must not be applied at this cap merely because the local sharp action is zero or the marked lines coincide.

Dependencies: the original four-state source for the exact finite sign, the literal signed line flags for (2)–(3), and the regular stationary reduction for the terminal strict-critical restriction.

The finite-collar calculation retains the stated chart and ordered pair. No global representation or volume conclusion follows.
