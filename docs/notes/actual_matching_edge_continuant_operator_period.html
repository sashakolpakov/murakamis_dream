The original exceptional operator as a continuant period

30 October 2026. The completed matching block has an exact generic contour representation whose entries are original forward and backward continuants. It retains the paired double poles, moving-q normals, physical target order and finite propagation. This separates an actual operator period from evaluation of its scalar polynomial at a classical trace.

No geometric saddle contribution or sharp complementary contraction estimate is asserted.

1. The original generic matrix and its actual spectrum

Put d=N-1 and use zeta=q exp(h), q=exp(pi i/N). In a physical total-degree block 0<=t<=d, the original Casimir J_t(zeta) has diagonal A_r, upper U_r and lower L_r, r=0,…,t:

Q=zeta^2,
A_r=zeta^-1 Q^(r-t)(1-Q^(N-r))(1-Q^r)
   +zeta^(-2d-1)Q^r(1-Q^(N-t+r))(1-Q^(t-r))
   +zeta^(2d+1)Q^-t+zeta^(-2d-1)Q^t,
U_r=zeta^-N Q^(2r+1-t)(1-Q^(N-r-1))(1-Q^(t-r)),
L_r=zeta^-N(1-Q^(N-t+r))(1-Q^(r+1)).             (1)

The offdiagonals are indexed 0<=r<t. These are the actual entries proved in the physical generic-jet theorem. In particular U and L are NOT identified away from the root. All internal offdiagonals are nonzero at q. The actual generic high-degree adapter identifies total degree 2d-t with this same matrix, with the translated physical indices; no new norm or complex conjugation is imposed.

Its generic characteristic polynomial is

chi_t(zeta,z)=product_(m=0)^t (z-lambda_m(zeta)),
lambda_m(zeta)=zeta^(2N-2m-1)+zeta^(-2N+2m+1).    (2)

This follows from the original normalized Clebsch columns for m=0,…,t and their distinct generic eigenvalues. Their generic source identity then extends (2) to q. The target label used in the recoupling is g=d-m, so (2) is exactly the target Casimir zeta(2g+1)+zeta(-2g-1), with its original moving-q derivative.

At q the only repeated eigenvalues are the pairs m and d-m when both occur in this block. Their multiplicity is two. An odd-level middle label is simple. An unreduced tridiagonal matrix has one-dimensional eigenspaces: its first coordinate determines an eigenvector recursively and cannot vanish. Therefore every paired node here has a genuine Jordan block of size two, rather than two independent eigenspaces.

2. Exact Green entries with both continuants retained

Define the prefix and suffix determinants

P_r(z)=det(zI-J_t[0,...,r-1]), P_0=1,
S_r(z)=det(zI-J_t[r,...,t]),   S_(t+1)=1.         (3)

All quantities retain their dependence on zeta. They satisfy the original forward and backward determinant recurrences. For example

P_(r+1)=(z-A_r)P_r-U_(r-1)L_(r-1)P_(r-1).

For z outside the actual spectrum, set R_t(zeta,z)=(zI-J_t)^-1. The exact entries are

(R_t)_(u,v)=
   (product_(r=u)^(v-1) U_r) P_u S_(v+1)/chi_t,  u<=v,
   (product_(r=v)^(u-1) L_r) P_v S_(u+1)/chi_t,  v<=u.      (4)

Empty products are one, so the two formulas agree on the diagonal. Expanding a tridiagonal cofactor proves (4): the intervening chain uses exactly the indicated upper or lower entries, and the two remaining blocks supply the prefix and suffix. Equivalently direct multiplication by zI-J_t verifies (4) using (3). No root-only reflection is differentiated to obtain the backward factor.

3. The exact generic operator period and its bandwidth

Let p_a(zeta,z) and D_a(zeta) be the original polynomial and face factor in the generic target identity. For the original folded range 0<=a<=floor(d/2), their coefficients are holomorphic near q after the displayed common face zero is canceled. The completed operator before the fixed physical bend and pivotal turn is

E_a(zeta)=D_a(zeta)^-1 p_a(zeta,Omega(zeta)).      (5)

Fix one contour Gamma enclosing ALL the root spectral nodes, for example the positively oriented circle |z|=3. For zeta in a sufficiently small neighborhood of q, the same contour encloses every moving node and meets none. On each physical block,

E_(a,t)(zeta)
  =D_a(zeta)^-1/(2pi i)
      integral_Gamma p_a(zeta,z) R_t(zeta,z) dz.              (6)

This follows either by Cauchy’s formula on the generic eigenspaces, then holomorphic continuation, or by expanding the resolvent at infinity and taking the coefficient of z^-1. Thus (6) is valid at the paired root WITHOUT deleting its double poles. Substitution of (4) is an exact single spectral-period representation of every native magnetic matrix entry.

The original polynomial has degree a. Since J_t is tridiagonal,

(E_(a,t)(zeta))_(u,v)=0 if |u-v|>a.             (7)

This is a generic identity, so every fixed moving-q derivative has the SAME bandwidth. Equivalently (6) has exact cancellation outside this band. This assertion is about the actual target physical coordinates BEFORE the recorded bend and pivotal turn; it does not silently permute the original outside ports.

The full source substitution retains beta_N times the original bend and turn, the row factor and half-twist, and the whole-source marked normal, exactly as in the completed target theorem. Those factors have not been absorbed into a chosen resolvent norm.

4. Genuine moving-q normals and the reduced pole budget

Differentiate in h with z FIXED. The exact identity is

partial_h R_t=R_t (partial_h J_t) R_t.

Consequently the first normal of (6) is

partial_h E_(a,t)
  =-(partial_h D_a)/D_a *E_(a,t)
   +D_a^-1/(2pi i) integral_Gamma
      [(partial_h p_a)R_t+p_a R_t(partial_h J_t)R_t] dz.     (8)

The partial derivative of p_a retains every generic-q coefficient. The derivative of J_t is the derivative of (1), including the distinct generic upper and lower entries. Formula (8) therefore includes moving spectral nodes and moving generalized eigenspaces, not merely a derivative of the scalar root value.

There is a useful exact reduction at a paired node lambda. Let Q(h) be its analytic two-dimensional cluster projector, and let its two actual analytic eigenbranches be lambda_1(h),lambda_2(h). At h=0 put

Q=Q(0), M=(J_t(q)-lambda)Q, M^2=0.

Then the root resolvent on this cluster is

R_cluster=Q/(z-lambda)+M/(z-lambda)^2.           (9)

The apparent fourth-order pole in R(partial_h J)R vanishes. Indeed differentiate the exact cluster identity (J-lambda_1)(J-lambda_2)Q(h)=0 and sandwich it by Q. It gives

Q J' M+M J' Q=(lambda_1'+lambda_2')M,
M J' M=0.                                     (10)

The second equation follows by multiplication by M. Thus the same-cluster contribution to R J’ R is exactly

Q J' Q/(z-lambda)^2
   +(lambda_1'+lambda_2')M/(z-lambda)^3.         (11)

Terms between different spectral clusters remain in (8); they must not be discarded. They contain the actual motion of the cluster projectors. The source derivative of the paired gap is lambda_j’-lambda_(d-j)’=-2N(q(2j+1)-q(-2j-1)), in convention (2).

More generally the r-th moving-q derivative of the resolvent has pole order at most r+2 at a paired node and at most r+1 at a simple node. To see this WITHOUT assuming diagonalizability at the root, the exact analytic cluster resolvent is

R_cluster(h,z)=
   [(z-lambda_1(h))Q(h)+(J(h)-lambda_2(h))Q(h)]
   /[(z-lambda_1(h))(z-lambda_2(h))].            (12)

Each h derivative increases the specialized denominator pole order by at most one. All numerators and cluster projectors in (12) are holomorphic. The corresponding scalar spectral derivatives needed in an r-th operator normal consequently have order at most r+1, with the actual q derivatives still retained. In particular the first normal needs scalar spectral derivatives only through order two, consistent with the prior Taylor-two calculation. These are exact pole budgets, not size bounds on the cluster projectors.

5. Scalar continuation is distinct from contour contribution

The source-polynomial root theorem gives a canonical Log p_a and a leading scalar action throughout C minus [2,infinity). This is a statement about p_a, not about R_t. The latter has the actual nodes of (2), all in [-2,2] at q, including their double poles.

In particular a target below -2 is off the root spectrum. A general target in (-2,2) lies in its spectral interval, among nodes that become dense as N grows. Any deformation of (6) must avoid those actual poles or account for their residues. The scalar’s zero-free continuation does not permit deleting them.

The exact numerator in (4) is where selected forward/backward boundary estimates can enter. A proof of a geometric contribution must still analyze that numerator together with chi_t, the fixed spectral contour, and the actual surrounding coherent state. It cannot replace p_a(Omega) by p_a(lambda_geometric) pointwise. Equations (4),(6),(8),(12) expose that remaining operator problem while preserving its original source and every required normal.
