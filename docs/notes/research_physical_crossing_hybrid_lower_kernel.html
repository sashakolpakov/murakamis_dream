A hybrid lower-donor kernel for the full physical crossing

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

The literal positive physical crossing and its literal inverse have nonzero ordinary-binomial kernels on a fixed neighborhood of a resolved lower donor. The donor is in its factorial frame. The recipient may be physical on any compact incidence-free chart, or factorial near its lower row. The Cartan factor, flip, finite caps, and actual meridian family are included. The inverse is estimated from its own finite recurrence, not from invertibility of the positive matrix.

The new finite recurrences below have the same activity-triangular structure as the Euler-star estimates in H. We verify their analytic and cap hypotheses explicitly. W supplies the actual physical matrices and their finite intertwining identities. These are the only complete proof directs. This is a local crossing theorem, not a claim that every crossing of a given geometric braid lies in the displayed chart.

The scalar κ±1\kappa^{\pm1}, original pivotal insertions, and all adjacent frame changes remain as in (7).
