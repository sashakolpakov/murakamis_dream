Balanced anchors and the actual alternating-knot source

28 October 2026. In the exact charged source of an alternating one-component diagram, vanishing of every individual anchor defect forces all overcrossing clocks to be equal. This is a topological incidence statement, not a knot census.

For the ORIGINAL figure-eight state the resulting diagonal clock sectors are ALL annihilated, at every level. More strongly, every nonzero clock sector has an anchor defect of size at least (N-1)/2. Thus individual sublinear-defect star estimates, or a positive balanced-anchor chamber alone, cannot cover this actual source. Further clustering/fusion may transport these defects; no statement that such a global method is impossible is made.

1. Alternation determines the defect equations

Use the exact charged source and source closure contraction. Put d=N-1. In a strictly alternating diagram, each maximal overbridge is a single crossing. After contracting the original closure-adapter forest, its anchors are the physical oriented segments between successive OVER encounters. Each such segment contains exactly ONE UNDER encounter.

Let crossing i be its first over encounter and j its next over encounter. If its under encounter has clock h_l, the three contributions to the anchor’s incident linear-factor count are

h_i             from the first over-output block,
d-h_j           from the final over-input block,
(d-h_l)+h_l=d   from the spectator under encounter.

A self-loop is counted twice, since it contributes a factor of that anchor at both ends. Phase rescalings from the retained pivots do not change these counts. Hence the EXACT incidence and defect are

S_(i->j)=2d+h_i-h_j,
delta_(i->j)=S_(i->j)-2d=h_i-h_j.              (1)

The successor relation on over encounters is one cycle for a one-component knot. Therefore

all delta=0  iff  all crossing clocks h_i=H.    (2)

For a link the same conclusion is componentwise. Formula (1) is valid independently of crossing signs. It uses physical over/under roles and survives the exact signed source gluing, rather than identifying successive braid positions with successive physical strand encounters.

2. The actual figure-eight cut forces its first clock to zero

Take the original signed word [1,-2,1,-2], with clocks (h_0,h_1,h_2,h_3). We retain the original averaged pivotal scalar. The initial use of INTEGER degree conservation to write it as

(1/N)Tr[(I tensor g tensor g)W]                (3)

is made before decomposing into clock sectors, as in the charged source theorem. No Schur lemma or marked-row substitution is applied to an individual h-sector afterward.

For the support calculation choose fixed cyclic Fourier grids on head i with root x^(i-1), x=-exp(-pi*i/N). At a positive adjacent crossing these roots differ by x. The outgoing first propagated root is Q^(-1) times its fixed root; the other outgoing root is unchanged. Thus if (a,b;c,e) are its fixed-grid input/output colors, the actual overclock at the first +sigma_1 crossing is

h_0=[b-c-1],                                  (4)

where brackets mean the representative in 0,…,d. The same-grid crossing is the exact finite Fourier transform of the ORIGINAL R, even where its old raw four-Psi presentation was singular. The closed polynomial port formulas extend there; their normalizer is a nonzero monomial.

These clock dictionaries are unchanged by the propagated prefixes. If a positive under-input root has offset Q^v from its fixed position root, its under-output root has offset Q^(v-1), since it is x times the input root. The difference of the two label offsets therefore still gives b-c-1. For a negative understrand the offsets are Q^v and Q^(v+1), giving e-a-1. Thus resetting the grid labels does not silently replace the actual clock variables.

Here is its elementary support dictionary. Put

u=[b-a-1], v=[a-c], t=[c-e], z=[e-b].

The two positive Pochhammers are nonzero exactly when

u+v+t+z=d,
h_0=u+v.                                      (5)

For example the first Pochhammer says u<=h_0 and then v=h_0-u; the second says z<=d-h_0 and then t=d-h_0-z. The negative table gives the same support equation, with its overclock u+z instead. Equivalently supported crossing colors have cyclic lifts

b<=e_lift<=c_lift<=a_lift=b+[a-b].              (6)

For clarity, the cut-support argument is recalled here. For fixed sentinels L,R define

V(x)=[L-x_1]+sum_i[x_i-x_(i+1)]+[x_b-R].

The cyclic interval (6) makes V nonincreasing at every supported crossing: the two outside triangle inequalities have defects which are nonnegative multiples of N, and their middle intervals sum to [a-b]. The trace source (3) has input (A_1,[A_2+1],…) and output (A_1,A_2,…), because the actual pivot is a nonzero scalar times the cyclic shift. Choose L=A_1 and R=[A_b+1]. The endpoint difference of V is zero if A_2=A_1 and -N otherwise. Thus A_2=A_1 and V is constant along every supported source path.

Translate colors by -A_1; this changes none of the support tests. The first head begins and ends at zero. The left triangle equality at position1 gives [0-c]=[0-a]+v. Its nonnegative increments must sum to zero, so v=0 at every position1 crossing and that head stays zero. Before the FIRST such crossing the second head stays one: its distance [0-x_2] is nondecreasing and begins at its maximum d. Thus at that first crossing a=0,b=1, so u=v=0.

This proves pointwise, for EVERY cut color in the averaged source,

nonzero fixed-clock source sector implies h_0=0. (7)

This is the same support argument independently proved in the original cyclic-cut theorem, with its mapping (4)–(5) to the present clocks made explicit. It does not require intertwinement of separately split sectors. The center-independent homogeneous source theorem transports this support calculation back to its original regular frames.

3. A second support inequality in the actual reduced polynomial

There is also a direct regular-Fermat test of the full reduced four-anchor polynomial. Up to a nonzero scalar, rescale its two anchors B,D by x^(-1), and call the four new variables A,B,C,D. Choose all four new variables to be Nth roots of one. This is permitted by the exact finite-flat source theorem, not a change of invariant or a pointwise replacement of the old raw kernel.

The eight actual blocks have the following lengths and root starts:

tail -> head length start exponent p in (tail-Q^p head;Q)_L
B -> C d-h_0 0
C -> A h_0 1
D -> A d-h_1 h_1+1
A -> B h_1 -h_1+h_2+1
C -> B d-h_2 h_0+h_2+2
B -> D h_2 -h_2-h_3-1
A -> D d-h_3 -h_1
D -> C h_3 0

This table includes all original closure pivot substitutions. It is a direct application of the actual graph metadata and full-block source contractions. No new graph or exterior coupon is introduced.

Let a,b,c,e be the root-grid color indices of A,B,C,D. If a grid summand is nonzero, the D->A block forces

r=[e-a] in [0,h_1].

The A->D block forces [a-e+h_1] in [d-h_3,d]. Since r<=h_1, the left representative is the ordinary integer h_1-r. Hence

h_1-r>=d-h_3,
h_1+h_3>=d.                                    (8)

The endpoint cases with an empty block give the same (possibly vacuous) inequalities. If (8) fails, EVERY point on this regular Fermat grid has polynomial value zero. Its exact source coefficient is therefore zero. Thus (8) is necessary for a nonzero h-sector, not merely an absolute-envelope or stationary-point condition.

There is an independent pathwise check of (8), in the same cyclic coordinates as Section2. With first cut color zero, the source-cut support forces the four intermediate states to have the form

initial (0,1,B+1),
after first  (0,D,B+1),
after second (0,E,F),
after third  (0,0,F),
final        (0,0,B),

all colors interpreted cyclically. The fourth negative crossing has current clock h_3=[B-1]. If B=0 it is d, proving (8). If B>0, its cyclic interval forces 1<=F<=B. At the second negative crossing h_1=u_2+z_2 with

u_2=[B-D]>=0,
z_2=[F-B-1]=N+F-B-1>=N-B.

Thus h_1+h_3>=(N-B)+(B-1)=d. This argument uses only actual supported paths before summation. The grid offsets just checked show that h_1,h_3 here are the present propagated overclocks, not renamed old-corner variables.

4. Balanced diagonal sectors vanish, and defects must be macroscopic

For this alternating one-component diagram, (2) makes every balanced clock sector (H,H,H,H). But (7) would force H=0, while (8) would require 2H>=d. As d>=1, these are incompatible. Therefore

lambda(P_(H,H,H,H))=0,
   EVERY N>=2 and EVERY H in 0,...,d.           (9)

There is no asymptotic qualifier: the WHOLE fixed-H coefficient vanishes exactly in the actual original source.

More generally, any nonzero sector has h_0=0 and max_i h_i>=d/2 by (8). Its cyclic clock range is therefore at least d/2. Along the physical four-overencounter cycle, total variation is at least twice the range. In fact the actual physical successor is (3,0,1,2), so the four defects are exactly

(-h_3,h_1,h_2-h_1,h_3-h_2).

Using (8) gives the stronger explicit bound

sum_anchors |delta_i|>=d,
max_anchors |delta_i|>=max(h_1,h_3)>=(N-1)/2.    (10)

Thus no sequence of nonzero figure-eight clock sectors can have all individual anchor defects o(N) in this representation. The positive balanced-anchor maximum principle and the retained sublinear local defect jets are consequently insufficient by themselves for its actual volume contribution.

This does not rule out cluster reductions: full-kernel fusion can add opposite defects, and exact phase-aware fusions can change the remaining source variables. Equation (10) must be respected before such a further operation; it does not prove that a macroscopic defect core can never be reduced or analyzed geometrically.

5. Reproducible audit

python3 experiments/audit_actual_alternating_balanced_source.py

The audit verifies the sign-independent physical successor and incidence formula on actual alternating signed words after all original closures, the local cyclic-support dictionary directly against both ORIGINAL R matrices, the general reduced figure-eight block table, and exact zero of every diagonal sector at multiple levels. It separately checks the regular-grid support implications without replacing them by coefficient-modulus estimates.
