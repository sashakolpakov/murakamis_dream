The original braid period has an exact finite Euler/Gaussian Cauchy network

Subsequent exact simplification, 28 October: the Hardy support theorem proves that, in this COMPLETE ordered factorization, every internal upper-projector branch is killed by an existing factorial barrier. The cyclic-function completion then replaces the finite Euler kernels by the anchored literature cyclic functions without changing the original whole source. The endpoint warnings below remain valid for the separate finite scalar kernels and arbitrary inserted coupons; they are not an obstruction to those proved complete-word replacements. The new representation still requires its selected geometric-cycle theorem.

28 October 2026. The all-word Euler completion gives a strict factorization of the ORIGINAL crossing into scalar Euler transfer operators, factorial diagonals and a marked Gaussian diagonal. The factorial kernels themselves are Euler polynomials. Consequently the entire original period is an explicit Laurent-polynomial Cauchy integral with a number of variables depending on the fixed braid, not on N. Its original cap and peripheral line are retained.

This is useful for the proposed global contour route: it gives an actual finite-polynomial integrand, not a different state integral or an assumed sum over critical points. The finite-head projectors have endpoint branches with their own exponential scale. The construction does not prove that the geometric saddle is selected.

The existence of an exact finite-variable source period and coherent composition was already proved in the compact coherent calculus. The additional content here is the explicit factorization of its actual crossing kernels into scalar u_N/v_N, Gaussian and finite projection atoms, with their precise arguments and original source. This exposes the functions whose GLOBAL asymptotics a contour proof must control; it is not a new abstract kernel-composition theorem.

1. Strict factorization after taking the zero-weight part

Use q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, x=-M/q and F_a=(Q;Q)_a. Let

u_N(t)=sum_(a=0)^(N-1)t^a/F_a,
v_N(t)=sum_(a=0)^(N-1)(-1)^aQ^(a(a-1)/2)t^a/F_a.

On one head let S^+ and S^- be the truncated unit raising and lowering shifts. Define on two heads

X_-=S_1^- S_2^+, X_+=S_1^+ S_2^-,
U_-=u_N(x^-1 X_-), V_-=v_N(x^-1 X_-),
U_+=u_N(x^-1 X_+), V_+=v_N(x^-1 X_+).

Both transfer arguments have Nth power zero, so V_- and V_+ are the exact inverses of U_- and U_+. Let F_i be the diagonal whose entry on head i is F_a, and let

D e_(a,k)=d_k e_(a,k),
d_k=(-1)^k Q^(k(k-1)/2)x^(-2k).

Then the ACTUAL fusion and positive/negative crossings obey

C=F_2 U_- F_1 F_2^-1,
C^-1=F_2 F_1^-1 V_- F_2^-1,
R_+=kappa C D U_+ C^-1,
R_-=kappa^-1 C V_+ D^-1 C^-1.              (1)

Here kappa is the original common Cartan scalar, kept unchanged. The formula for C follows entrywise: X_-^h sends (a,k) to (a-h,k+h), giving exactly

x^-h F_a F_(k+h)/[F_h F_k],

the proved actual fusion coefficient. For R_+, start from the source-equivalent completion, whose right Euler uses S_1^+ times CYCLIC lowering on head two. Its physical positive-N-weight part is precisely the part where that cyclic shift wraps. Replacing it by S_2^- takes the degree-zero part. Since C and D preserve integer degree, the resulting matrix is the actual R, not only an equivalent scalar period. This gives the third formula in (1); ordinary matrix inversion gives the fourth.

Thus each crossing is an ordered product of ten elementary factors. All factors in (1) now preserve total unwrapped degree. This strict factorization still has finite-head projections; those projections are encoded explicitly below, not silently removed.

2. Scalar Laurent-polynomial kernels

Identify a head vector with a polynomial in z of degree<N. For Laurent inputs define Pi_N to retain exponents 0,…,N-1 and put

K_N(t)=sum_(a=0)^(N-1)t^a=(1-t^N)/(1-t).

The apparent pole is removable: K_N is a polynomial. For b heads the exact projection formula is

(Pi_N f)(z)=CT_w product_i K_N(z_i/w_i) f(w).

Consequently the transfer factors in (1) have the kernels

K_(U_-,N)(z,w)=product_i K_N(z_i/w_i)
                                  u_N(x^-1 w_2/w_1),
K_(U_+,N)(z,w)=product_i K_N(z_i/w_i)
                                  u_N(x^-1 w_1/w_2), (2)

with v_N replacing u_N for the inverses. These formulas are exact on finite-head inputs: Pi_N of a monomial transfer is precisely the corresponding product of truncated shifts. In a full kernel composition every preceding output already lies in that head.

The diagonal factor on a head has kernel sum_a c_a(z/w)^a. In particular its nontrivial scalar factors are

F^-1: u_N(z/w),
F:    N(z/w)^(N-1) v_N(Qw/z),
D^s: Gamma_(s,N)(z/w), s=+1,-1,
Gamma_(s,N)(t)=sum_(a=0)^(N-1)d_a^s t^a.    (3)

Every other spectator head contributes K_N(z_i/w_i). The factorial identity in (3) follows from

F_a=N Q^(-a-1)[t^(N-1-a)]v_N(t),
sum_(a=0)^(N-1)F_a t^a=N t^(N-1)v_N(Q/t).

It retains its full factor N and monomial t^(N-1). All kernels in (2)-(3) are finite Laurent polynomials. Their only coordinate singularities are zero/infinity; the notation with 1-t in K_N does not create an additional pole in the complete kernel.

3. The full original source and a fixed-dimensional integral

Let beta have b heads and m signed crossings, and expand (1) in its original order. There are L=10m elementary factors A_l. Let K_l(zl,z(l-1)) be their scalar kernels in (2)-(3), extended by spectator K_N factors to b variables. Keep the original pivots g_a=(xQ-a)(1-N). At the actual root their head kernel is

G_N(t)=sum_a g_a t^a=x^(1-N)K_N(Q^-1 t).

For the original normalized-average first-cut source, its scalar kernel is EXACTLY

S_N(z^0,z^L)=(1/N)K_N(z_1^0)K_N((z_1^L)^-1)
              product_(i=2)^b G_N(z_i^0/z_i^L). (4)

For the proved fixed-first-input/all-ones-output source at index zero, simply replace its first factor by K_N((z_1L)-1). These are the specified original source matrices, not a preferred new contour or solution. The known root scalar-closure identity equates their uninserted original periods.

If chi_(beta,N)(M) is the ORIGINAL external modified-dimension, framing and common-Cartan line, then

P_(beta,N)(M)=chi_(beta,N)(M)
   CT_(z^0,...,z^L) S_N(z^0,z^L)
                     product_(l=1)^L K_l(z^l,z^(l-1)). (5)

In (5), the factors kappa^sign from (1) are included once in chi, if they were not already retained in the local kernels. They are the original factors. One may leave (3)’s factors N inside their kernels rather than collect them; no numerical multiplier is fixed after specialization.

The proof is elementary coefficient contraction: each CT at an internal head identifies its outgoing and incoming finite index. The source kernel (4) performs exactly the original first-cut pairing and all original pivotal closures. Thus (5) is the literal actual tensor contraction written in scalar variables.

Equivalently every CT in (5) is an integral dz/(2piiz) over ANY nonzero circle. There are b(10m+1) variables, independent of N. Since the full integrand is a finite Laurent polynomial, all these radii can be varied freely without crossing a noncoordinate pole. The equality holds for the whole root meridian family. It is not an assertion about moving-q normal jets.

There are only m Gaussian kernels Gamma. Expanding those alone gives a sum over m indices k_j in 0,…,N-1, with explicit weights product_j d_(k_j)^(sign_j), whose remaining coefficient integral contains only u_N,v_N, finite projection kernels and source monomials. This is the requested fixed-number-of-indices Euler/Gaussian realization of the ORIGINAL scalar.

4. The endpoint branches that a global deformation must retain

Formula (5) makes contour deformation possible, but does not remove the finite-head endpoint actions. For fixed t with |t|!=1,

(1/N)log|K_N(t)| -> max(0,log|t|),             (6)

uniformly on compact sets away from the unit circle. This follows directly from (1-t^N)/(1-t). Thus discarding its numerator is an exponentially wrong replacement in the |t|>1 chamber.

If the kernel network has nu projection factors, expand their numerators simultaneously:

product_e K_N(t_e)
  =sum_(epsilon in {0,1}^nu) (-1)^|epsilon|
              product_e t_e^(N epsilon_e)/(1-t_e). (7)

There are at most 2^nu branches, where nu<=bL+b+1 is independent of level. In each radius chamber avoiding t_e=1, (7) is an exact identity on the SAME torus. Its branch actions retain the linear terms 2pii sum epsilon_e Log(t_e)/h, h=2pii/N. Individual branches have artificial poles that cancel in their marked sum; deforming them across those poles separately requires the corresponding residue correction.

In particular, K_N(z/w) dw/w=[1-(z/w)^N]dw/(w-z) is regular at the proposed reproducing face w=z. For any other factor regular there, the two split residues are +f(z) and -f(z). Thus a reduction keeping only the first reproducing residue loses the accompanying tail chain. This is visible to the original source even in degree zero: with the literal transfer arguments X_-=S_1^- S_2^+, X_+=S_1^+ S_2^-, X_-X_+=(I-E_top) tensor (I-E_bottom). The averaged original two-head pivotal cut evaluates this as -(N-1)g_0/N, whereas ordinary multiplication of the reciprocal Laurent arguments gives I, whose pivotal cut is zero. These walls are not in the proved positive-N-weight source-null ideal. Quantitative interior conditions under which the tails really are exponentially small are given in the analytic bulk projection theorem.

This counts ONLY the projection-numerator choices. It does not count or control every semiclassical branch of u_N,v_N or the Gaussian polynomials Gamma. Their own finite truncations and Stokes transitions also have to be analyzed. In particular the proved small-disk Euler expansions cannot be continued uniformly to arbitrary geometric radii merely because the finite polynomials are entire in their arguments.

These endpoint choices are not new knot states or independently normalizable periods. They are components of one prescribed source. Around a closed chain made only of projection ratios, the product of their moduli is one, so they cannot ALL lie in the decaying |t_e|<1 chamber. Other diagonal factors in an actual network can change that feasibility problem; no universal infeasibility assertion for every connected knot is made here.

Likewise the monomials in (3) and the marked Gaussian factors must remain in the global phase. Strict concavity of a bulk volume functional alone does not bound the full upper envelope (6)-(7) or prove cancellation cannot occur between its branches.

The concrete next problem is therefore well defined: choose a torus through the complete geometric point, prove uniform actual atom asymptotics there INCLUDING the marked endpoint branches, and establish the global real-part gap and nonzero derived source. The present note supplies that exact integrand and its finite branch bookkeeping, not those three analytic/geometric assertions.

5. The single general comparison still to prove

For clarity, a sufficient general comparison proposition for THIS literal network is the following. It is an OPEN target, not a consequence claimed here.

For the proposed broad class of positive geometric ideal triangulations, construct a source-preserving reduction of (5) and a contour deformation of its ORIGINAL marked cycle to a relative Rivin-angle chain such that:

  1. Every auxiliary integration, cap, projection-numerator branch and peripheral normal row is carried by an exact identity or by a contour deformation with its matched residue terms.
  2. The resulting chain projects to the positive angle domain with oriented local degree plus or minus one at its complete Rivin optimizer. The full derived source coefficient at that point is nonzero, with its normalization bounded above and below by powers of N.
  3. The actual finite atoms have a UNIFORM comparison on that chain to the calibrated marked action. Its unique maximal real exponent is Vol(X)/(2pi) at the complete point; all other stationary or escaping boundary pieces have a strict exponential gap or a proved smaller contribution.

After redundant source variables are eliminated, a nondegenerate Gaussian contraction (or the corresponding sourced clean-critical calculus), with its local remainder controlled RELATIVE to the full nonzero leading source coefficient, and the global gap would then give

log|P_(beta,N)|=N Vol(X)/(2pi)+o(N),

with the original polynomial peripheral factors still attached. The leading source coefficient may vary with N; the stated upper and lower polynomial bounds do not assert a fixed power or a convergent prefactor. A formula N^alpha(c+o(1)) would require an additional coherent density expansion. The logarithmic statement already proves the original volume limit. It is not enough to match one local saddle of one atom or show that the positive angle optimizer exists.

The currently unproved assertion is the whole marked chain/source comparison above, particularly its degree-one/nonzero-source property and uniform control of all atom endpoint sectors. Rivin strict concavity controls the optimizer ON an already supplied angle chain; it does not determine the homology or intersection degree of the original cycle (4). Formula (5) now makes that cycle and every required function explicit for the comparison.

The audit and saved output check (1) against the original positive and negative crossing, the actual fusion matrix, transfer projection kernels, factorial reflection kernels and full original word/source contractions. These are exact finite-field convention checks; (1)-(7) are direct all-level identities. No lower bound for the hyperbolic volume exponent is claimed.
