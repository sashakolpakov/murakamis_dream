Coherent radial capacity of the original augmented pretzel source

29 October 2026. These diagnostics evaluate the exact polynomial and original source in the complete-base theorem. They retain all nodal derivatives and all true winding channels. They do not prove an asymptotic limit, a global circle supremum, or geometric survival.

1. A genuine full-source finite Cauchy bound

Let zNz_N be that explicit half-degree polynomial and let R>1R>1. The exact enclosing residue, with the original source normal terms, immediately gives

|𝒜s,N|≤N2R+R−1(1−R−N)2R−Nmax|w|=R|zN(w+w−1)|s. |\mathcal A_{s,N}| \leq N^2\frac{R+R^{-1}}{(1-R^{-N})^2} R^{-N}\max_{|w|=R}|z_N(w+w^{-1})|^s. \tag{1}

No inner factorial sum is replaced by absolute values. Its only maximum is the coherent actual polynomial on the specified circle. For each fixed R,sR,s the prefactor is polynomial in NN.

The classical candidate circle for the primitive winding is

cs=2−4sec⁡2(π/s),Rs=−cs+cs2−42. c_s=2-4\sec^2(\pi/s),\qquad R_s=\frac{-c_s+\sqrt{c_s^2-4}}2.

The compared classical action rate is

Vs/(2π)=4sπ[Λ(π4+π2s)+Λ(π4−π2s)]. V_s/(2\pi)=\frac{4s}{\pi} \left[\Lambda\left(\frac\pi4+\frac\pi{2s}\right) +\Lambda\left(\frac\pi4-\frac\pi{2s}\right)\right].

The identification and accessibility of that classical critical point are separate from the finite inequality (1).
