A polynomial-cost Fourier kernel for every original twist block

29 October 2026. Every signed power of the original equal-color crossing has an explicit Chebyshev expansion whose coefficient mass is O(N(1+|ell|)). It retains the full Casimir Hermite jets, including the odd simple node. For a full twist ell=2p this supplies the coefficient kernel for the entire ordinary circle-color packet, rather than an inversion of a single complete-color circle.

The literal mixed-color circle calculation identifies the displayed operators with the original pivotal encircling sources. This is therefore an exact quantitative source transformation at arbitrary width, with the marked cut unchanged. Polynomial coefficient mass does not prevent cancellation in the resulting filling sum, and does not prove geometric survival.

1. The complete local spectral data

Use N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, kappa=q(d2/2). The original pair Casimir Omega has polynomial

P_N(z)=2T_N(z/2)+2,
c_k=w_k+w_k^(-1), w_k=q^(2k+1), 0<=k<N.

Its distinct nodes have multiplicity two, except c=-2 when N is odd. The original full checked crossing is R=kappa r_N(Omega), with the original, already derived values and jets

nu_k=r_N(c_k)=(-1)^k q^[k(k+1)],
r_N'(c_k)/nu_k=-(d-2k)/[2(w_k-w_k^(-1))]             (1)

at double nodes. The possible simple node has only the value condition. These are the complete physical spectral data; there is no semisimple projection. For a negative power use the actual inverse crossing, not its adjoint.

For any integer ell let f_(N,ell) be the unique polynomial of degree less than N representing r_N^ell modulo P_N. Negative powers exist because every nu_k is nonzero. Define finite data

h_k=nu_k^ell,
d_k=-(ell/2)(d-2k) h_k.                             (2)

At double nodes, d_k=(w_k-w_k^(-1))f’(c_k). At the odd simple node d-2k=0 and w_k-w_k^(-1)=0, so d_k=0 without prescribing an additional derivative. All |h_k|=1 and |d_k|<=|ell|N/2.

2. Exact Fourier-Hermite interpolation

Take the marked finite Fourier transforms

H_n=(1/N)sum_k h_k w_k^(-n),
D_n=(1/N)sum_k d_k w_k^(-n), 0<=n<N.                (3)

Then the COMPLETE polynomial is

f_(N,ell)(z)=a_0+sum_(n=1)^d a_n 2T_n(z/2),
a_0=H_0,
a_n=[D_n+(N-n)H_n]/N, 1<=n<N.                     (4)

Here is a direct proof, avoiding a conditioned confluent Vandermonde inverse. Set g(w)=f(w+w^(-1)). Its Laurent coefficients are a_0 and the symmetric pair a_n at exponents n and -n. Since w_k^N=-1, the node values and logarithmic derivatives have Fourier aliases

H_n=a_n-a_(N-n),
D_n=n a_n+(N-n)a_(N-n), 1<=n<N,
H_0=a_0, D_0=0.                                   (5)

Solving the two equations gives (4). The paired nodes k,d-k have the same h and opposite d, so the compatibility conditions for (5) are automatic. At n=N/2, when present, H_n=0 and the second equation still gives the correct coefficient. At the odd simple node the logarithmic derivative of every such g is zero. Thus (4) imposes exactly the N original Hermite conditions, with no extra endpoint constraint, at every even and composite level too.

Equation (4) immediately gives the uniform coefficient bounds

|a_0|<=1, |a_n|<=1+|ell|/2.                        (6)

No separation bound for nearby Casimir nodes is needed.

3. Full circle-color coefficients and their norm

Let U_j denote the second-kind Chebyshev polynomial, U_0=1, U_1=z when its argument is z/2. Put a_N=a_(N+1)=0 and define

beta_j=(-1)^j(a_j-a_(j+2)), 0<=j<N.                (7)

The identities 2T_n=U_n-U_(n-2), n>=2, and 2T_1=U_1 give

f_(N,ell)(Omega)
   =sum_(j=0)^d beta_j (-1)^j U_j(Omega/2),
R^ell=kappa^ell sum_(j=0)^d beta_j L_(j+1),
L_(j+1)=(-1)^j U_j(Omega/2).                       (8)

All phases, including kappa^ell, are retained. Summing (6) gives

sum_(j=0)^d |beta_j| <= 2N(1+|ell|/2).             (9)

For ell=2p, (8) is an exact full-twist filling kernel of mass at most 2N(1+|p|). The separate physical circle proof realizes L_j as the actual ordinary pivotal j-color encircling operator, including its original framing and trace. A single L_N is not inverted or declared sufficient; the whole degree-<N basis occurs in (8).

This bound holds uniformly in N and p, including negative twists and all gcd/parity cases. There is no division by p or by a Gauss sum. For ell=0 the expansion reduces exactly to L_1=I.

For full twists the data in (3) are particularly explicit:

h_k=Q^[p k(k+1)], d_k=-p(d-2k)h_k,
H_n=(1/N)sum_k Q^[p k(k+1)]q^[-n(2k+1)].            (10)

The corresponding D_n is the SAME finite quadratic sum with its retained linear normal weight -p(d-2k). Dropping this weight would erase the derivative data of the original crossing and generally change the invariant. Formula (10) specifies the filling phases before any large-N or long-filling analysis.

4. Simultaneous filling and the actual remaining theorem

Replace a fixed collection of disjoint twist blocks R^(2p_i) by (8) BEFORE any exterior contraction. Distributivity gives the exact sum of the same original tensor diagram with circle operators L_(j_i) in those slots. All spectator tensors, the original marked cut and peripheral/framing line retain their order and values. The total coefficient mass is at most

product_i [2N(1+|p_i|)].                            (11)

For a fixed number of blocks this is polynomial when the p_i are fixed; more generally its logarithm is o(N) whenever sum_i log(1+|p_i|)=o(N). Thus no exponential coefficient cost is introduced by retaining the complete circle-color data.

Together with the physical circle identification, this supplies an original-source input for simultaneous crossing-circle fillings. It does not identify a fixed complete-color augmented-link scalar with this packet, nor replace its trace by a different invariant.

The next geometric survival question is substantive: obtain a nonzero selected geometric period for that actual MIXED-COLOR augmented source and control the coherent filling sum (10). Taking absolute values in (11) gives only a maximum over the base packet; it does not automatically yield the smaller filled-manifold volume. No broad original-knot volume theorem is claimed by this expansion.

The complete Fourier–Hermite Gram turns these coefficients into an explicit positive Cauchy metric. Its exact dual cost is 1+ell2(N2-1)/(12N^2), uniformly bounded in N for a fixed twist. A literal original normalization insertion also proves that this metric’s energy can be excessive even when the final knot is hyperbolic. A sharp geometric energy estimate requires more than the controlled filling-source norm.

Audit: PASS 81 signed full-physical crossing-power comparisons at N=2,…,10 and 64 complex full-Hermite/coefficient-mass cases through N=48. Independent proof review checked both Fourier aliases, the even middle index, the odd simple node, and the circle-basis conversion.
