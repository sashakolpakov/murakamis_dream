Recent advances and the remaining conjecture

29 October 2026. This records the current internal research status. The general volume conjecture remains open.

A general local source-to-volume dictionary

The actual four-port kernel now has a proved single-sum formula and seven-factorial conversion, including its physical normalization, phases and bounded normal order. The supported scalar theorem supplies common phase and the positive face normalization. The hyperideal geometry theorem identifies its normalized action with an actual truncated-tetrahedron volume and proves all six length derivatives.

This is a local geometric input to the general Cauchy route. The gauge can be singular on paired channels, and high partners retain extra normal currents. Moreover the local support inequalities alone maximize at the octahedral boundary. Full marked transport and the actual global edge/boundary constraints still need to be established.

A whole-source limit for positive Conway expressions

The variational theorem proves a full-sequence limit for every fixed robust positive Conway expression, with every original circle color and normal retained. It equals twice a specified concave ideal-angle maximum. An additive integer-support argument supplies the lower bound at every level, including boundary maxima. The angle problem has explicit algebraic critical equations, but its identification with the actual complement is not yet proved in general. Some expressions have zero growth.

Exact positivity in the original source

The Newton multiplication theorem gives an exact nonnegative multiplication table in the full root algebra. It retains the repeated-root normal terms. The actual turn reverses the Newton coordinates, and the actual closures extract coordinates. Thus the covered two-head circuits become positive finite sums within the original source, without replacing it by an auxiliary positive model.

The actual source now has a single Racah formula

The generic identity expresses the trivalent source as one finite Racah sum, retaining its normalization, high fold partners and normal tails. At the exceptional endpoint, uniqueness is established by a direct cup projection.

Together with the whole-support normal-jet envelope, this establishes polynomial costs for the actual correlated boundary Gram on EVERY supported folded triple at both level parities, including saturation boundaries. It does not bound physical frame changes, arbitrary recoupling operators or the global graph energy.

General geometric gluing is available

For the certified ideal cores in the graph geometry theorem, the actual complement volume is 4bv8+2Vol⁡(Q)4b v_8+2\operatorname{Vol}(Q). The three-port sewing theorem also gives an exact whole-source gluing identity; the six-head pruning theorem eliminates unsupported seam channels before the exposed mark or exterior acts. These supply geometric and algebraic gluing, without yet proving the required asymptotics for arbitrary graphs.

What remains on the critical path

The remaining obstacle is global control of the original growing-color sum: the unfixed cycle channels, possible phase cancellation, behavior outside the geometric corridor, and the even-level cube source. The general graph problem also needs the resulting global energy to recover the certified core volume. These are substantive missing estimates.

The earlier all-level results for the specified sufficiently long fillings of signed octahedral belt stacks remain in force in the notes. The frontier states their quantifiers and the other established families. Finite checks alone do not establish the asymptotic theorems.
