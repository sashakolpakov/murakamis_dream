Intrinsic probe geometry for unflagged Conway components

31 October 2026. geometric and classical-action theorem. It supplies the exact parameter range and absolute pressure used by the separate full-normal coefficient-cone bound. The inputs are the original strict-mask complete geometry, the marked quarter-bound theorem, the rigid-spine action identity, and the exact reduced-hub source. The appendix proves a separate universal pressure gap for an unturned product hub, with an explicit constant greater than 1/101/10.

5. Scope of the probe

Equations (1)–(9) concern the original positive profiles and their complete selected states. The effective leaves carry their exact additive constants. Their signed coupons and independent physical colors are retained by the separate coefficient-cone theorem.

The old block and attachment certificates therefore have no missing geometric parameter range at this probe. The unturned product condition is essential to the proof. Applying an outer turn reverses the output derivative, and does not preserve (A9) or the claimed orientation of (A14). This note makes no corresponding claim for an arbitrary type-AA expression with a top turn.

A.5. Exact scope for the pending signed estimate

The result proves the requested classical inequality for the high Newton-index part of a candidate unflagged-turn estimate, if that part has already been bounded by the original lower-half profile. It does not itself establish that quantum coefficient bound or replace the original signed coefficient by its positive profile. The separate coefficient-cone theorem supplies its own exact finite adapter.

The low Newton part uses a separate source-exact alias or spectral estimate; it is not inferred from this pressure inequality. Likewise (A14) does not allow the exponential norm of an arbitrary turn to be charged to a new physical leaf. There is no new source-circle insertion, isotopy, geometric volume replacement, or full mixed-hub theorem here.
