The complete crossing Green source has explicit ground and carry endpoints

28 October 2026. The exact two-Newton/one-Rogers crossing layer gives a coupled source equation normal to its geometric holonomy. After the ORIGINAL physical finite boundary restrictions, its entire right side is explicit: a ground-output block, a ground-input block, and one rank-one carry on pair degree N. Its ORIGINAL cup/marked-cap value is the nonzero known number (Q(N-1)-1)/N, with the same external Cartan line retained.

This is an inhomogeneous source theorem, not a claim that the currents vanish. It also computes the exact shared full-Psi face fusion and the remaining one-function Fourier kernel. The original relative Fourier cycle is not replaced by a standard real contour.

Subsequent constructive resolution of the LOCAL comparison: the balanced finite Fourier kernel uses an adaptive internal Fermat orbit and independent external finite frames. All three source norms then vanish on that actual sampling map, and an ORIGINAL E-current proves its four-full-Psi kernel is EXACTLY the original crossing operator. Its uniform local density is nonzero and polynomial. The global sewn source and geometric noncancellation remain separate.

1. The actual positive crossing and paired Ward source

Use the proved physical crossing layer. Let z_0,z_1 be the original inputs, z_6,z_7 the outputs, and t its remaining internal variable. Put

u=t/z_1, v=x^(-1)t/z_0,
f=x^(-1)z_7/z_6, r=z_6/z_0, w=x^(-1)z_6/t,
B_0(t)=B_N(u,v), F(w)=B_N(f,fw)R_N(r,w).

Then the original normalized positive core is the physical finite block of CT_t B_0F, with its final original Hardy barrier. Its common kappa, writhe/peripheral line and original source are unchanged. All statements below are at the original fixed Q root for the whole nonzero color x family unless completeness is explicitly specified.

Define the exact central sources

D_0=1-u^N+v^N,
D_B=1-f^N+f^Nw^N,
D_R=1-r^N-w^N.

The finite Newton and Rogers recurrences give the FULL paired Ward

F(w)-fr F(Qw)=H_pair(w),
H_pair=D_B R_N(r,w)+f D_R B_N(f,Qfw).                  (1)

This is a polynomial identity, with every source endpoint retained. In particular D_B+f^N D_R=1-(fr)^N is a boundary norm identity, not a reason to discard both currents.

2. A source Green equation for the complete crossing

Multiply (1) by v B_0(t), integrate the COMPLETE polynomial CT, and reindex t->Qt in its shifted term. The simultaneous-Q source recurrence is

v B_0(Qt)=(u-1)B_0(t)+D_0.

Because v(Qt)=Qv(t), the result is exactly

CT_t[(v-Qfr(u-1))B_0F]
   =CT_t[v B_0 H_pair]+Qfr CT_t[D_0F].                (2)

The original q factor in the reindexing is compulsory. Its left coefficient is the bounded-degree LINEAR polynomial

v-Qfr(u-1)=x^(-1)/z_0
                   [t(1-Qz_7/z_1)+Qz_7].            (3)

At Q=1 its zero is precisely the regular stationary gluing equation of the all-homogeneous Fourier kernel in Section 5. Thus (2) is an actual sourced quantum normal equation, without localizing a nilpotent face or dividing by an unknown period.

Equation (2) holds before restricting any boundary coefficient. Apply the SAME original finite input/output boundary blocks to both sides. Below L denotes this physical block of its left side. This restriction is important for boundary monomials which can shift a physical output past its original top; it is not an additional reset inserted into one separated source sector.

3. Exact finite endpoint filtration

Write d=N-1, F_a=(Q;Q)_a and p_j=(-1)jQ(j(j-1)/2)/F_j. On original input |a,b>, 0<=a,b<N, the physical form of (2) is

L=G_out+G_top+G_in.                                    (4)

The ground-SECOND-INPUT term is particularly simple:

G_in|a,b>=delta_(b,0) Q sum_(e=1)^a
                                  x^(-e)|a-e,e>.       (5)

It has rank N-1. To prove it, the two nonconstant terms of D_0 demand an incoming index at least N, and vanish on the ORIGINAL finite inputs. The constant term gives CT F=K_N(f)K_N(r). Multiplication by Qfr and the actual finite boundary block yields exactly (5). In particular the D_0F term is NOT zero.

The ground-SECOND-OUTPUT block has rank at most N-1:

G_out|a,b>=0 unless a>=1 and a+b<N,

G_out|a,b>=x^(-b-2) F_(a+b)/F_b
   *sum_(j=0)^(a-1)
     p_j x^(-2j)/[F_(a-j-1)(1-Q^(b+j+1))]
   *|a+b,0>.                                          (6)

All denominators in (6) are ORIGINAL nonzero factorial units: b+j+1<=a+b<N. No singular determinant is inverted.

The remaining term is one rank-one carry on pair degree N:

G_top|a,b>=0 unless b=N-a, 1<=a<N,

G_top|a,N-a>=-x^(-N-a) F_d/F_(N-a) p_(a-1)
             *sum_(e=1)^d x^(-e)|N-e,e>.              (7)

The rank is one because the output vector in (7) is fixed and nonzero. It is not a rank-one assertion about the whole Green operator or about an arbitrary crossing.

Here is a direct proof of the filtration. In the first term vB_0 D_B R of the right side of (2), the -f^N summand has negative first output, and the (fw)^N summand has second output at least N. The original barriers remove both. Its constant summand has second output zero; coefficient expansion gives (6).

In the second term vfB_0 D_R B(f,Qfw), the constant summand has negative first output, and -r^N demands first input at least N. The -w^N term survives only when the B_0 total order is EXACTLY d and the V-index of B(f,Qfw) is zero. The resulting inputs and outputs have total degree N; their coefficient is precisely (7). This also explains why multiplying a primitive ground-cell Ward by a monomial may expose an additional top carry. One must apply the physical barriers AFTER the specified weighting, not beforehand.

4. The ORIGINAL complete cap detects a known nonzero boundary

At completeness x=-q^(-1), Qx^2=1. Keep exactly

p=N sum_(a=0)^d x^(a-d)|a,d-a>,
m|c,e>=x^e/N^2,

Then

m G_in p=Qd/N,
m G_top p=0,
m G_out p=-1/N,
m Lp=(Qd-1)/N!=0.                                    (8)

The first equality follows immediately from the one surviving input |d,0> in (5). The second follows because the original cup has total degree d, while (7) requires degree N.

For the third, reorganize the finite coefficient sum in (6) by k=b+j+1. Its inner coefficient is

sum_(b+j=k-1) p_j/F_b=delta_(k,1),

the exact inverse Euler identity U(t)V(t)=1 modulo t^N. Thus for the same literal vectors at general x this pairing is -Q(-1)x(-2)/N; completeness makes it -1/N. This proves (8) by finite algebra at EVERY level. The original unstripped crossing carries its SAME external kappa multiplying this source value; it has not been reset.

Equation (8) is a concrete boundary normalization of the actual Green source. It cannot be replaced by an assertion that every endpoint/current vanishes. It is also not a global knot lower bound. Transporting the nonintertwining ground/carry coupons through an arbitrary exterior needs their actual Ward/source equations; bare cut relocation or Schur scalarity does not justify that step.

The latter is the proved opposite-root Rogers source. It is NOT obtained by substituting the balanced norm rNwN off its balancing divisor. Its equivalent plus-oriented form contains (1-rN)(1-wN).

Put

All four remaining full functions and both central norm factors are explicit. Since f_log+rho=D-A-X, its internal tau dependence separates as

boundary_constant *(1-(x^(-1)z_6/t)^N)

The boundary constant, including phase, is

(N^2/phi_N)exp[N(f_log-B)](1-r^N)

In particular the affine 2piitau term from u^N is retained. The remaining integral is a one-full-Phi Fourier FUNCTIONAL with a two-term central source; it is not a bare transform.

The principal growth chambers of the two original local factors on this face have opposite signs. Their exact sewing (9) must be done before choosing their joint symbol or discarding a central factor. Separate local norm-gap theorems cannot be simultaneously asserted on this shared face.

8. The top carry is a controlled idempotent and a normal Ward source

At completeness the coefficient of (7) simplifies completely:

F_d/F_(N-a) *p_(a-1)=Q^(1-a).

This is ordinary root-factorial reflection. Put

w=sum_(e=1)^d x^(-e)|N-e,e>.

Since Qx^2=1, equation (7) becomes

G_top=-Q w w^t=Q E, E=-w w^t,
w^t w=-1, E^2=E.                                      (16)

The transpose here is BILINEAR, not a Hermitian adjoint. Indeed sum_(e=1)^d x(-2e)=sum_(e=1)d Q^e=-1. Thus the carry is not a nilpotent error. Its normalized rank-one projector has operator norm N-1 in the original Euclidean metric, since ||w||^2=N-1 and its row has the same norm. It is polynomially controlled without selecting an exponentially conditioned full flag basis.

There is also an exact algebra for repeated carry contractions. Let P_* be the one-head projection onto indices 1,…,d, and let W be the coefficient matrix of w. Then

W^2=x^(-N)P_*, x^(-2N)=1,
E_12 E_23 E_12=E tensor P_*,
E_23 E_12 E_23=P_* tensor E.                            (17)

To verify the first identity, W has its sole entry in row a at column N-a, equal to x^(a-N); the two opposite entries multiply to x^(-N). Expanding three rank-one contractions uses W^2 twice and gives the other two identities. Disjoint E_i commute. Hence on all nonzero heads these are idempotent Temperley–Lieb relations. Globally the ground projections P_* in (17) must remain; no quotient of the original source or preservation by R is inferred. In particular this small endpoint algebra does not prove that an arbitrary interleaved braid word is polynomially bounded.

The carry VECTOR also has an actual meridian-normal Ward description. Retain the original balanced coproduct

F_tot=F_1+K_1^(-1)F_2, K_1=x Z_1^(-1),
D_M=x partial_x at fixed physical Q.

Direct application to the original complete p gives

(D_M F_tot)p=N(1-Z_1)w.                                (18)

Only the K_1^(-1)F_2 term differentiates. Its output |a,N-a>, 1<=a<N, has coefficient N x(a-N)(1-Qa), proving (18) entrywise. On this degree-N subspace every first index is nonzero, so 1-Z_1 IS a unit there. Thus

w=N^(-1)(1-Z_1)^(-1)(D_M F_tot)p

is a valid restricted identity. It does not invert the singular operator 1-Z_1 on the entire finite module. Nor does it replace a full normal co-source by this one operator-normal insertion. It identifies the carry line which the global endpoint/current transport must retain.

These relations give an explicit finite endpoint calculus: ground heads, a rank-one degree-N projector and its actual normal Ward source. Sliding them through other crossings still requires the original coproduct/current equations; the relations alone supply no global geometric lower bound.

9. Exact flat-metric norm of the COMPLETE Green source

At completeness, the ground-output coefficient (6) is just a unit phase:

G_out|a,b>=x^(-b-2)|a+b,0>,
                a>=1, a+b<N.                         (19)

This is a simplification of the FULL weighted endpoint, not of the unweighted ground cell of the primitive clock Ward. To prove it, use the partial-fraction identity

1/(z;Q)_a=sum_(j=0)^(a-1)
   (-1)^j Q^(j(j+1)/2)
      /[F_j F_(a-1-j)(1-zQ^j)].                      (20)

The residues at each of the a simple poles agree; both sides vanish at infinity, proving (20) without an asymptotic step. Set z=Q^(b+1). Since x^(-2)=Q, the sum in (6) is F_b/F_(a+b), which proves (19). No denominator vanishes: b+a<N. The support filtration in Section 3 is unchanged; there is no additional ground block.

Use the original orthonormal finite-head basis and let d=N-1. Equations (5), (16), and (19) imply

||G_in||=||G_out||=sqrt(d), ||G_top||=d,

||L||=max[d,sqrt(d+sqrt(d))]
     =d for N>=3, and sqrt(2) for N=2.                (21)

Here the norm is the actual operator norm, not a norm after an exponentially conditioned gauge. To see sharpness, fix total degree t with 1<=t<N and write |b>=|t-b,b>. The complete lower-degree block is

L_t=Q[|0>r_t^t+u_t<0|],
(r_t)_b=x^(-b) for 0<=b<t, (r_t)_t=0,
(u_t)_0=0, (u_t)_e=x^(-e) for 1<=e<=t.

The two displayed output ranges are orthogonal. Therefore L_t^*L_t=conjugate(r_t) r_t^t+t|0><0|. Its possibly nonzero eigenvalues are t+sqrt(t), t-sqrt(t), since ||r_t||^2=t and |(r_t)_0|=1. The degree-N block is Q E; all other degree blocks vanish. This proves (21) including the small-level exception. The unstripped crossing retains the SAME external kappa, of modulus one at completeness.

Thus the actual normal Green source of a crossing is only linearly large in N in the original metric, even though its individual unweighted clock ground kernel may be exponentially large. In a tensor word this gives an explicit local defect bound. Controlling its contraction through arbitrary exterior crossings still requires that exterior; (21) alone gives no global scalar lower bound or cancellation theorem.

10. The carry is fixed by its SAME original crossing

Let B=R_+/kappa=C D U_+ C^(-1) at completeness. The vector w in Section 8 obeys the exact two-sided identities

B w=w, w^t B=w^t, B E=E B=E.                          (22)

Consequently the same holds for B^(-1). The original R_+ has eigenvalue kappa on this line, and R_- has kappa^(-1). These are identities in the ORIGINAL physical finite heads; no arbitrary cut relocation is asserted.

Here is an all-level proof directly from the actual fusion. On total degree N its coefficient is

C_(u,v;a,b)=x^(u-a) F_a F_v/[F_(a-u)F_b],
                   u<=a, v=b+a-u.

Hence, with b=N-a, the component of w^t C is

x^(-b) F_a sum_(h=0)^(a-1) Q^h [b+h choose h]_Q
   =x^(-b)F_a [N choose a-1]_Q.

It is zero unless a=1. The surviving row is x^(-d)F_1<1,d|. This row is fixed by D U_+: D_d=1 and only transfer order zero can reach its output from this finite total-degree sector. This proves the left equality.

For the right equality, the original inverse-fusion Euler convolution gives z=C^(-1)w with

z_(N-v,v)=-x^(-v)(-1)^v Q^(v(v-1)/2)/F_(N-v),
                                      1<=v<N.

Indeed its convolution is the full inverse-Euler coefficient zero minus its missing final term. Put m=N-v. Applying D U_+ to this vector multiplies its common output prefactor by

D_v sum_(h=0)^(m-1)
   (-1)^h Q^(h(h-1)/2+(v+1)h)/[F_h F_(m-h)].

The sum extended through h=m is (Q^(v+1);Q)m/F_m=0. Subtract its last term and use D_v=(-1)^v Q^(v(v+1)/2); the displayed factor is 1/F_m, because -(-1)^N Q^(N(N+1)/2)=1 for every N, including even N. Thus D U+z=z. Together with the left identity and E=-ww^t, this proves (22).

Equation (22) is compatible with (17), but it does not say that an adjacent crossing preserves the carry sector. Transport through neighboring heads remains a separate question with the ground projections retained.

11. An exact resonant Fourier source, including its closure current

The remaining one-function field admits an exact finite-orbit comparison with (12), with no undetermined central scalar. Write h=2pi*i/N, retain the previously fixed phi_N, and put

Here I_- is precisely the conjugate opposite-root Newton Barnes current of the Rogers source theorem, with its inherited DOWNWARD orientation and its continued logarithmic marks. Equation (24) is a meromorphic identity; at a collision it means the SAME continued combined source. Neither lambda nor z is reduced modulo 2pi*i.

To prove (24), use the full-function shift exactly j times:

  =Psi_+(z) product_(k=0)^(j-1)

Psi_+(z) B_(Q^(-1))(exp lambda,

Psi_-(-h-lambda)
   =(1-exp(-Nlambda)) zeta^(-1)

      *Psi_+(lambda+2pi*i+h).

Substitute (12) and zeta_0^2 zeta exp(piic_b^2)=1. This is exactly (24), including its affine exponential, phase, and two norm factors.

In the actual field (11), take

tau_j=tau_0+hj, z=h+B-tau_0,
lambda=D-B+2pi*i.

Its extra factor 1-exp[N(C-X-tau_j)] is constant on this finite orbit and is retained outside (23). The scalar A in (24) is EXACTLY the full-turn multiplier in (15). Consequently the remaining Barnes current is multiplied by the precise closure defect 1-A of the actual field. This is a computed source, not a normalization imposed on it.

On the balanced divisor A=1, that current vanishes and T_N=H_N F_ref exactly. On its regular finite part H_N is nonzero: 1-exp(Nz)=exp(-Nlambda), and exp(Nlambda)-1 cannot vanish there. This proves a normalized, nonzero resonant reference coefficient on THAT divisor. The original whole source has NOT been shown to be supported on A=1. Off it the current remains compulsory and may contain the same continued homogeneous block.

Why this finite orbit is compatible with the original source

There is a separate exact finite-source fact, before any homogeneous/current split. The COMPLETE raw positive kernel K(t)=B_0(t)F(t) is a Laurent polynomial with exponents in [-2d,d]. For any fixed nonzero scalar t_0,

(1/N)sum_(j=0)^d K(t_0 Q^j)
   =CT_t K+t_0^(-N)[t^(-N)]K.                       (26)

There is only one alias, since 2d<2N and d<N. The sampling point may also depend on degree-zero auxiliary parameters, including the retained Gaussian variables. It must not carry nonzero total head degree; otherwise its power in (26) would change the grading argument below. For a monomial indexed by the actual nonnegative atom orders, minus its t exponent is the output total head degree minus the input total head degree. Thus the alias in (26), after the ORIGINAL finite head restrictions, raises integer degree by N. For the negative crossing the same statement follows from its exact two-Newton/Rogers layer, with its ONE shared continuous Gaussian retained; the Gaussian changes coefficients and not these degrees.

Replace ANY collection of complete internal CTs in any original signed word by (26). Each resulting error raises total degree by a positive multiple of N; all original crossings preserve total degree. In the ORIGINAL one-cut pointed trace every traced head has identical input/output index. The remaining cut index would have to increase by at least N, impossible for indices 0,…,d.

For the ORIGINAL k-pair pointed plat, the cups have total degree k d and its k-1 ordinary caps require (k-1)d. The marked pair would therefore have degree at least d+N=2N-1>2d, impossible for its two finite heads. This uses the actual marked row, not an assertion that this row is itself fixed-degree: it has coefficients xe/N2 on all |c,e>. Thus every nonempty alias selection vanishes. Hence this finite root-orbit replacement preserves the entire original whole source for arbitrary word length and bridge count. It also preserves its original scalar normalization. It is not asserted for arbitrary degree-lowering coupons.

This is a source-level circle-to-finite-orbit map. One may apply (24) to its all-homogeneous summand, while retaining EVERY other B/R current sector with the SAME logarithmic marks. Neither (26)’s alias deletion nor its degree proof can be reapplied separately to a meromorphic S/I sector, whose Fourier support is different. Thus (24)–(26) do not yet identify the WHOLE original Fourier functional with the bare reference chain plus a polynomially bounded endpoint operator. The missing bound concerns the joint continued B/R currents, not the exact coefficient H_N.

This last limitation concerns the INTERNAL-orbit map alone. The subsequent balanced external Fourier theorem supplies an additional source-preserving polarization that makes all three original norms vanish together. Its exact current rigidity eliminates the remaining physical alias errors. That result resolves the local operator comparison, while retaining every finite adapter and the original global co-source.
