A quartic source minimum at the branching products

21 November 2026. Research note. Earlier finals and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

After the prescribed leaf transports have been fixed, many remaining products are linear in the unknown boundary packets. They can be eliminated exactly with constant coefficients. Only a product whose two inputs both come from other products needs nonlinear variables.

If there are qq such products, the quartic minimum needs only 2q2q full boundary packets. Its minimum and original source comparison are unchanged. Its Hessian still lies between II and 3I3I, and its half-gradient step is an inward contraction. The eliminated linear responses require no solve depending on a trial packet. Their coherent action remains part of the coefficients.

2. Count the products that remain bilinear

Classify a product by the number of its inputs represented by internal variables:

  • With no internal input, its output expression is a known constant.
  • With one internal input, its output expression is a known linear function of that packet, because its other input is prescribed.
  • With two internal inputs, its output expression remains bilinear.

Let uu count the middle class and qq the last class. Every nontop product contributes exactly one internal connection to one input of a product above it. Therefore p−1=u+2q.(4) p-1=u+2q. \qquad\text{(4)} In particular 2q≤p−12q\le p-1.

Retain the input packet on each of the two incoming internal connections of every product in the last class. These 2q2q packets are distinct, since a product output has only one parent. Call their list ww, and call the other p−1−2qp-1-2q packets zz. The classification is made from the expression tree and the already prescribed leaf inputs. No unknown internal product output is set to its true value.

The only restriction of a previous feasible set was the earlier, explicit leaf-transport clamp in the direct source. This note adds elimination, not further clamping. No normalization budget is reassigned to a fused linear map.

When p=0p=0, the source has only its prescribed leaf and linear transport; the direct source already evaluates its scalar response. When p≥1p\ge1 but q=0q=0, all remaining maps after the known leaf inputs are inserted are linear or constant. Equation (12) then contains the whole finite problem without an outer minimization. This does not simplify the geometric asymptotics of that coherent linear expression.

5. What this does not remove

Every retained packet is still the full HNH_N. Eliminated coordinates contribute through the exact constant response; no Casimir derivative or original marked closing is quotiented out. The result changes neither the original tangle nor its finite source.

The smaller number of nonlinear packets does not make a source coefficient positive, or give it the hyperbolic-volume rate. Known linear maps can still carry strong phases and cancellation. A geometric trial certificate must control those actual responses and the original closing. Nor does convexity alone imply an exact degree-four semidefinite relaxation of the newly reduced objective; no such assertion is made.
