An exact generic boundary gauge for the original marked source

30 October 2026. Independently reviewed. A symmetric analytic basis change removes the nonmonomial part of every ordinary cup and preserves the entire original marked source, including its retained trace normal. Its transformed white coefficients are given below in terms of the ORIGINAL generic q-Hahn numerator. This is a finite tensor identity on the actual modules, including the paired nilpotent sectors. It is not a complex magnetic continuation theorem.

1. A root-normalized symmetric basis

Fix N>=3, d=N-1, q0=exp(pi i/N), zeta=q0 exp(epsilon), Q=zeta^2, and F_n=(Q;Q)_n. For any actual module V_L with 0<=L<N set

b_L(r;zeta)=zeta^[r(L-r)] F_r F_(L-r)/F_L,
b_L^0(r)=b_L(r;q0)=s_r s_(L-r)/s_L,
s_n=product_(k=1)^n 2sin(pi k/N),
g_L(r;zeta)=[b_L(r;zeta)/b_L^0(r)]^(1/2),
G_L=diag(g_L(0),...,g_L(L)).                           (1)

The square root is the analytic one equal to 1 at epsilon=0. Every factorial index is below N, so all these functions are units on a neighborhood of the actual root. In particular

G_L(q0)=I,  g_L(r)=g_L(L-r),  b_d^0(r)=1.              (2)

The last identity is s_r s_(d-r)=s_d=N. In the actual ordinary cup normalization, including its ordering,

p_L(r,L-r)=(-1)^r zeta^(-r) b_L(r;zeta).

The physical cup is pV=N zeta(-d2) p_d. Replace each module basis by its columns G_L and transform both covariant and contravariant tensor indices. Thus

p'_L=G_L^(-1) p_L G_L^(-T),
ell'_L=G_L^T ell_L G_L,  ell_L=p_L^(-1).

Then, EXACTLY before root specialization,

p'_L(r,L-r)=(-1)^r zeta^(-r) b_L^0(r),
pV'(r,d-r)=N zeta^(-d^2-r)(-1)^r.                    (3)

The generic factorial factors have moved to the adjacent tensors; they have not been deleted from the source.

The gauge has polynomial cost on physical indices. Indeed |1-q0^(2k)|>=c min(k,N-k)/N and direct logarithmic differentiation of (1) gives O(N^2 log N), uniformly in L,r. The same product estimate on a sufficiently small disk proves

|g_L(r;zeta)|+|g_L(r;zeta)^(-1)|<=C
   for |epsilon|<=c/[N^2(1+log N)].                  (4)

Consequently every fixed epsilon derivative of g_L or its inverse is bounded by C_m[N^2(1+log N)]^m. These are estimates at the actual integer module indices only.

2. The actual white map, dual, and cyclic order

Let W_a:V_(2a)->V_d tensor V_d be the original regular generic Clebsch map, 0<=a<=floor(d/2), with the normalization in the full matching-graph source. Keep its exact dual

pi_a=gamma_a^(-1) p_(2a)^T W_a^T
                     (ellV^T tensor ellV^T) P,
gamma_a=q0^(2-a^2)/N^2.                               (5)

Here gamma_a is the fixed root scalar in that regular lift; P reverses the two physical heads. Put G_pair=G_d tensor G_d and

W'_a=G_pair^(-1) W_a G_(2a),
pi'_a=G_(2a)^(-1) pi_a G_pair,
K'_a=W'_a pi'_a=G_pair^(-1)(W_a pi_a)G_pair.            (6)

Substitution of (3) into (5), using P G_pair=G_pair P, proves the SAME categorical dual formula with every tensor primed. This verifies compatibility with the actual bent edges, rather than replacing the white vertex by a Hermitian adjoint.

All generic intertwining identities remain true after conjugating the module matrices by G_L. No division by pi_a W_a occurs. In particular its root is still zero on a paired sector and the identity on the odd simple sector. The nilpotent source has not been replaced by a semisimple quotient.

The exact generic reversal J_L|r>=zeta1|L-r> commutes with G_L by (2). Therefore the original Chevalley/high- degree adapter also holds for the primed white maps. Likewise the actual four-physical-head cyclic turn, with its coefficient (-1)^d zeta^(d-2u), commutes with the product of four G_d factors: the diagonal coefficient commutes and the cyclic permutation permutes identical factors. No unproved cyclic-module adapter is being applied to the original nilpotent module.

3. The actual LARGE-channel numerator

The generic source distinction is essential here. W_a takes values in the channel of highest weight 2a, so its generic Casimir eigenvalue is zeta(2a+1)+zeta(-2a-1). In the original compiler indexing its channel is m=d-a, not a. These two generic eigenvalues coincide at the root but have different derivatives.

Use the actual generic polynomial from the collision/source theorem:

H_m^B(U,V)=sum_(k=0)^m (-1)^k B^k Q^[k(k+1)/2]
   [m choose k]_Q (BQ^(m-k+1);Q)_k/(BQ;Q)_k
   V^k product_(j<k)(U-Q^j) product_(j<m-k)(V-Q^j),
B=Q^(-N).

For 0<=r<=2a put m=d-a, sigma=m+r and i+j=sigma, with 0<=i,j<=d. Then the original white coefficient is EXACTLY

W_a(i,sigma-i;r)
  =(-1)^m zeta^[-dr+di-m(m-1)]
     H_m^B(Q^i,Q^(sigma-i))/F_m,                      (7)

and its transformed coefficient is

W'_a(i,sigma-i;r)
  =g_(2a)(r)/[g_d(i)g_d(sigma-i)] * right_side_of_(7). (8)

For the low half r<=a, the original endpoint and normalized LARGE column are

W_a(0,sigma;r)=zeta^(-dr) F_sigma/(F_m F_r),
C_m(zeta;sigma,i)=zeta^(di) H_m^B(U,V)/D_m(Q^sigma),
D_m(T)=product_(k=0)^(m-1)(T-Q^k),
D_m(Q^sigma)=(-1)^m zeta^[m(m-1)]F_sigma/F_r.

The actual finite Clebsch sum divided by its endpoint is C_m; cancellation gives (7). For ALL r one can verify (7) term by term, without extending that nonexistent physical endpoint into high degrees. In the original Clebsch sum use highest index b and set k=j-m+b. Its denominator is F_b F_(m-b) F_k F_(r-k), with r-k=i-b. Its complete q exponent simplifies to

-dr+di-2db+2bj+b(b-1),

exactly the converted finite-H summand. The factorial numerator is F_(a+b)F_(d-b)/(F_a F_d) on both sides. Terms outside their finite support vanish by their Newton products, so the same identity holds in the high half r>a. All denominators in H_m and F_m are root units because m<N. Thus (7) is regular BEFORE taking its generic normal and specifies the full original white tensor, without a root-only high adapter.

There is a decisive check already at a=0. The original coefficient is

W_0(i,d-i;0)=(-1)^i zeta^(-i)b_d(i;zeta),
W'_0(i,d-i;0)=(-1)^i zeta^(-i),

whereas the SMALL normalized compiler column is C_0=zeta^(di). Their root values agree, but their generic derivatives do not. Thus substituting the small-channel generic R_a into W_a merely because the root columns collide would be incorrect.

The precise possible bridge to the small numerator is the exact collision identity, with B temporarily independent of Q:

H_m^B-H_a^B product_(k=a)^(m-1)(UV-Q^k)
      =(1-B^2 Q^N) T_(a,m)^B(U,V),  m+a=d.           (9a)

It holds in the localized generic coefficient ring by the finite terminating identity proved in the collision note. Along the ACTUAL source B=Q^(-N), the derivative of 1-B^2 Q^N at q0 is 2N. Thus the transverse term in (9a) contributes to the original first normal. A bound or packet for the small R_a alone does not control that term. Equation (8), not a differentiated root simplification, is the transformed source requiring continuation.

4. Exact cancellation on the whole marked source

Apply (1)–(6) at every physical and channel occurrence of the original typed matching graph. Every contracted wire has one G_L and one G_L^(-1); cups and caps transform as in (3). Consequently the ordinary closed scalar satisfies

F'_Delta(epsilon)=F_Delta(epsilon)                   (9b)

as an analytic identity, for any fixed original diagram and every fixed choice of its folded channel labels. This also holds after the finite label sum and retained signed half-twist factors. No coupon is commuted through another coupon. It is the exact coordinate invariance of this specified finite tensor contraction.

Keep the SAME exposed original physical head. Its open endomorphism transforms by G_d^(-1) H G_d. For the original Hopf-covariant contraction H=z_Delta I, so its scalar is unchanged. The physical ordinary dimension remains

D_N(epsilon)=(-1)^d[N]_(q0 exp(epsilon)),
nu_N=D_N'(0)=2N(-1)^N/(q0-q0^(-1)).                   (10)

This can also be checked directly from (3): the ratio of the two ordered complementary cup entries is still (-1)^d zeta^(d-2r). Since G_d(q0)=I, the original unrestricted marked row on that exposed arc is unchanged at the root, including its exact normalization m pV(q0)=1. Thus the source’s genuine trace-normal extraction is preserved:

Z_Delta=F_Delta'(0)/nu_N=F'_Delta{}'(0)/nu_N.          (11)

If one represents the mark off root as a row, it must transform covariantly too; retaining an untransformed generic row while changing its cup is not (9b). The proof of (11) uses the original ordinary closure and its first normal, so it requires no invented off-root mark and no movement of the bare root mark.

For the complete-circle decomposition this applies to the regular Hopf lift in the full-source theorem. As there, that lift is not asserted to be the original complete circle’s entire moving-q germ. The root scalar and its normalized ordinary trace derivative are independent of that choice. For an actual specified generic coupon (for example the original exceptional polynomial operator), basis covariance (9b) applies to that very coupon and all its generic jets. It does not replace its moving-q germ by the complete-circle lift.

5. What finite gauge cancellation does not prove

The physical estimates (4) do NOT imply estimates at complex magnetic coordinates. There is an exact elementary obstruction to such an inference. Given any proposed holomorphic interpolation g(epsilon,u) of a physical gauge, replace it by

g(epsilon,u) exp[epsilon exp(N^2) sin^2(pi u)].         (12)

For every integer u this changes neither its value nor ANY of its epsilon derivatives. For integer L it preserves the symmetry u<->L-u. Yet its first generic derivative at a fixed noninteger u can be arbitrarily large. Even fixing all finite physical jets and the reversal symmetry does not determine the needed off-grid gauge.

One may cancel specified factors algebraically in an exact whole- source continuation BEFORE estimating them. Alternatively one may prove bounds for an explicitly chosen continuation of the factors in (8). Neither operation follows from the physical cancellation (9b). In particular the existing joint deficient-degree theorem controls the actual R numerators, while the remaining boundary factors, the full generic operator lift in that chart, and the global degree period still require their own exact constructions.

6. What can nevertheless be bounded in the deficient corridor

The exact numerator in (7) gives a useful positive statement without inventing a continuation of G_L. Define the unprimed white continuation by the RIGHT SIDE of (7), with m=d-a fixed, sigma=N Sigma complex, r=sigma-m, and a specified magnetic lift i of size O(N). It is a literal finite generic source expression, agreeing with every physical white entry as just proved. Denote it by mathscr W_a(zeta;sigma,i). It is not defined by interpolation of physical samples.

Fix a compact K in the joint deficient corridor of the actual joint numerator theorem, using z=q0^(sigma-2i) and its marked logarithmic lift. Put

s=m-a=d-2a.

There are eta,c,C>0, depending on K, such that for 1<=s<=eta N, every fixed k>=0, and all sufficiently large N,

|partial_epsilon^k H_m^B(Q^i,Q^(sigma-i))|_0
   <=C_k N^(10k) (C N^20)^s |H_a^1(U0,V0)|,          (13)

|partial_epsilon^k mathscr W_a|_0
   <=C_k N^(10k) (C N^20)^s |mathscr W_a(q0)|.        (14)

The root quantities on the right are nonzero on K. The simple odd midpoint s=0 is covered directly by the small-numerator theorem. Thus the relative generic cost is subexponential in every window s log N=o(N), and polynomial for bounded s. This is an upper bound, not a nonzero leading normal amplitude. It concerns the specified unprimed white continuation; no bound for the unspecified complex gauge in (8) is asserted.

Here is a proof retaining all large-channel factors. The exact generic normalization is

H_n^B(Q^i,Q^(sigma-i))=zeta^(n sigma) K_n R_n(zeta;sigma,z).

The joint deficient theorem supplies the actual small determinant M_a(epsilon), a bounded inverse, and a bounded determinant ratio on |epsilon|<=rho=c N^(-10). Its last diagonal cofactor is R_(a-1), so R_(a-1)/R_a is bounded on this disk too. The exact all-degree generic recurrence and its source justification are recorded in the near-fold partner theorem. On the circle |epsilon|=rho its only spectral denominators satisfy

|Lambda_n-Lambda_(n-j)|>=c rho, j=1,2, n<=m,

with the j=2 equation used only when defined. At the unique folded resonance the factor is 1-Q^(-N); its modulus is comparable to N rho, and the other factor is comparable to N^(-1). All other required gaps are at least cN^(-2). The finite rational coefficient formulas therefore give |alpha_n|<=C/rho, |beta_n|<=C/rho^2. Propagating the ACTUAL recurrence exactly s steps gives

|R_m/R_a|<=(C N^20)^s.                               (15)

The accompanying exact scalar quotient is

K_m/K_a=product_(j=2a+1)^(2m)(1-B^2 Q^j)
   /[product_(j=a+1)^m(1-B^2 Q^j)
      product_(j=a+1)^m(1-BQ^j)].                    (16)

Its denominator indices stay uniformly near N/2, so their root values are units with a fixed lower bound; its 2s numerator factors are bounded above. Consequently |K_m/K_a|<=C^s. Also |zeta^(s sigma)|<=C_K^s, since the scaled marked parameters remain in a fixed compact. These estimates and the small-numerator bound prove (13) on the circle. The ORIGINAL finite H_m is holomorphic inside it: all denominator Pochhammer indices are below N. Thus Cauchy’s estimate applies to H_m, not to its singular monic R_m.

At the root the collision identity (9a) gives

H_m^1=P_s(T0)H_a^1,
P_s(T)=product_(j=a)^(m-1)(T-Q^j),
c_K^s<=|P_s(T0)|<=C_K^s.                             (17)

The last bounds hold because the deficient total Sigma is separated from the near-fold interval [a/N,m/N]. In particular no D_a(T0) is divided out, even when Sigma N is a deficient integer. Finally the monomial and F_m^(-1) in (7) have bounded relative variation on the same disk, by the fixed marked-lift bound and the root-unit factorial estimate in section 1. This proves (14).

One can retain the actual transverse numerator as well. With the original compiler eigenvalues

lambda_n=zeta^(2N-1-2n)+zeta^(-2N+1+2n),
lambda_m-lambda_a=-(zeta^s-zeta^(-s))
                                  (zeta^N-zeta^(-N)),

the function

mathscr T_a=[H_m^B-P_s(T)H_a^B]/(lambda_m-lambda_a)    (18)

is holomorphic through epsilon=0 by (9a). On the same circle the denominator has modulus at least c s rho. Applying the preceding estimates and then Cauchy’s formula gives

|partial_epsilon^k mathscr T_a|_0
   <=(C_k/s) N^[10(k+1)](C N^20)^s |H_a^1|.          (19)

This is the original divided numerator with the actual gap sign. It does not divide by D_m(T), so it has none of the spurious deficient-degree column poles. It does not assert that the full exceptional operator’s unspecialized boundary factors have already been continued or bounded. Nor can these nonreal local estimates turn an identically zero forbidden physical degree slice into a nonzero global period.
