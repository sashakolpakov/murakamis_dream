Sine-factor endpoint modes with the sharp truncation rate

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The normalized factorial interpolation gives exact first-order sine-ratio factors for the unturned endpoint equation. Their ordinary lowering relation produces a finite approximate homogeneous basis. On a buffered bulk rectangle of any fixed height this basis is bounded, and its optimally truncated residual has the sharp real product rate J*J_*. Its two coefficients can be fixed by an actual physical value packet. The independent normal packet is retained separately; no sharper source-normal compatibility is inferred.

6. Coverage and direct sources

The continuum sine-power modes and their leading value connection constants were already known. The exact factorial interpolation was also available. The additional step here is its ordinary descending index expansion (12), the uniform tall-rectangle estimate for that specific finite sum, and the identity of its optimal residual rate with J*J_*. The actual two-value normalization (31) retains the normal datum that the sharp residual alone does not determine.
