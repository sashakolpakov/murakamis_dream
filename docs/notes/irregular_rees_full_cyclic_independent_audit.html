Independent audit of the irregular Rees cyclic bridge

Audit, 27 October 2026. The proof in irregular_rees_full_cyclic_module.md is sound. I checked its operator order, wrap sign, centers, Casimir, dense-chart coverage, transformed source and pivot. No test rerun was needed. The Green moment/source formulas audited in section 5 below are consistent with it.

1. The genuine block operators and the regularized wraps

On ur/r!u^r/r!, the wrap B(MDZ−1−M−1) B(MD_{Z^{-1}}-M^{-1}) has coefficient MZ−r−M−1MZ^{-r}-M^{-1} into block r−1r-1. Reversing the displayed order would change that coefficient. Similarly T1−ZL+1L+1 T\frac{1-Z^{L+1}}{L+1} has the required coefficient 1−Zr+11-Z^{r+1} into block r+1r+1. The integral expression in the proof is exactly the same operator, not a convergence approximation.

For d=N(Z−1)d=N(Z-1), the scalar conjugation gives G−1DZG=eu/NDZ,G−1DZ−1G=e−u/(NZ)DZ−1,G−1BG=B+1/d. G^{-1}D_ZG=e^{u/N}D_Z,\quad G^{-1}D_{Z^{-1}}G=e^{-u/(NZ)}D_{Z^{-1}},\quad G^{-1}BG=B+1/d. The head factors ηj\eta^j, followed by the Hopf scaling, cancel on the nonwrap entries. They give dd on the EE-wrap and d−1d^{-1} on the FF-wrap. Consequently the EE-wrap is exactly (1+d∂u)[Me−u/(NZ)DZ−1−M−1], (1+d\partial_u) [Me^{-u/(NZ)}D_{Z^{-1}}-M^{-1}], with that order. The completion convention is appropriate: GG belongs to the uu-adic completion with Laurent η\eta-coefficients, not to a ring requiring one pole bound for its entire series. The regular operator lattice and the irregular selected source are therefore genuinely different objects.

3. The original source and its ramified flags

Since π0G=π0\pi_0G=\pi_0, transforming the actual source pair gives $$ \psi_{\rm new}=D_{\rm head}e^{-u/d}\psi,\qquad \phi_{\rm new}=\phi D_{\rm head}^{-1}\pi_0. $$ Their pairing is one. The corresponding coupon has entries ηi−je−u/d/N\eta^{i-j}e^{-u/d}/N followed by evaluation at zero. Its uhu^h term has worst pole η−Nh−(N−1)\eta^{-Nh-(N-1)}, exactly the stated bound. It remains idempotent, but is not a regular source on the limiting lattice.

The coefficientwise head projector commutes with the scalar exponential; the finite vacuum coupon does not. The proof correctly preserves that distinction instead of inferring source regularity from unchanged vacuum normalization.

4. The literal pivot and the actual scope

Conjugation preserves the literal identity g=K1−Ng=K^{1-N}. The regular root clock therefore selects $$ g_{\rm lit}=U g_{\rm red},\qquad g_{\rm red}=x^{1-N}A^{-1}. $$ The paired inverse defect has the opposite U−1U^{-1} factor. This agrees with the revised Mellin source adapters and does not identify their separate normal derivatives.

The proof supplies a genuine full-cyclic module limit and an explicitly irregular marked source. It does not claim regularity of a conjugated RR-network, survival of that source in the limit, or a geometric regulator norm. Those scope statements are mathematically necessary.
