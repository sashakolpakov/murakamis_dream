An exact source repair for correlated coherent windows

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

The original pointed source admits an exact correlated coherent cutoff after an exponentially small correction in one explicit coupon direction. A polynomially bounded projection onto the actual lowering commutant also gives a stronger Neumann repair at matrix level. Both amplitudes are close to the original matrix in the whole-operator norm budget. The source equalities are exact, even when that scalar is zero.

The complete directs C, F, and X are pinned below. C supplies a literal root radical coupon and its pivotal partial trace; F supplies the actual meridian frame; X supplies the capped Euclidean off-matching estimate. The projection, its calibration, and the convergent source repair are proved here. The earlier raw averaged co-source lift was checked for coverage; it is not an input to these proofs.
