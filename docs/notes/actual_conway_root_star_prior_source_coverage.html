Root-star sources: exact overlap with prior belt and pretzel results

31 October 2026. This is a source and coverage audit, not a new asymptotic theorem or a classification of tangle isotopy types. It compares the literal mixed-color source of C(h0T(h1⋯hs))C(h_0T(h_1\cdots h_s)) with the earlier notes, including all first Casimir normals and the original marked closure.

1. The mixed-color identity and the belt alphabet

Work in the full physical pair algebra ℋN=ℂ[z]/(2TN(z/2)+2)\mathcal H_N=\mathbb C[z]/(2T_N(z/2)+2), with its original turn TT, crossing RR, ordinary circle LjL_j, and marked functional ϕ\phi. Put

hjϵ=T(RϵLj),ZN(𝒋)=ϕ(hj0ϵ0T(∏i=1shjiϵi))=ϕ(Lj0Rϵ0∏i=1shjiϵi). h_j^\epsilon=T(R^\epsilon L_j),\qquad Z_N(\mathbf j)=\phi\!\left(h_{j_0}^{\epsilon_0} T\!\left(\prod_{i=1}^s h_{j_i}^{\epsilon_i}\right)\right) =\phi\!\left(L_{j_0}R^{\epsilon_0} \prod_{i=1}^s h_{j_i}^{\epsilon_i}\right). \tag{1}

The last equality is the Frobenius turn identity, proved with the full Newton basis in the root-character contraction, §1. Every color 1≤ji≤N1\le j_i\le N remains independent.

The literal octahedral belt construction, §1 and §4a, reads its two typed pieces as f+=T(RLN)R−1f_+=T(RL_N)R^{-1} and f−=RT(R−1LN)f_-=RT(R^{-1}L_N). The mixed-circle packet, §§1 and 6, retains the same physical reflection and turn with every ordinary color. Thus their mixed versions are exactly

f+(j)=hj+1R−1,f−(j)=Rhj−1. f_+(j)=h_j^{+1}R^{-1},\qquad f_-(j)=Rh_j^{-1}. \tag{2}

Here the superscript −1-1 on hj−1h_j^{-1} denotes the negative half-twist sign, not a multiplicative inverse. If all inner signs are nonzero, let n+n_+ and n−n_- count them and choose the fixed outer crossing power a=ϵ0+n+−n−a=\epsilon_0+n_+-n_-. Commutativity within the full pair algebra gives

ϕ(Lj0Ra∏i=1sfϵi(ji))=ϕ(Lj0Ra−n++n−∏i=1shjiϵi)=ZN(𝒋). \begin{aligned} \phi\!\left(L_{j_0}R^a\prod_{i=1}^s f_{\epsilon_i}(j_i)\right) &=\phi\!\left(L_{j_0}R^{a-n_++n_-} \prod_{i=1}^s h_{j_i}^{\epsilon_i}\right)\\ &=Z_N(\mathbf j). \end{aligned}\tag{3}

This is equality of the entire mixed-color scalar, not only its complete value or its leading action. The physical turn/reflection formulas establish (2) before commutativity is used; no normal directions or Cartan factors are discarded.

The long-filled belt theorem already supplies the whole-source packet and all-color estimates for every fixed two-type word and every fixed integer aa, at both parities and composite levels. It also proves independently long signed fillings of any auxiliary subset for its literal belt diagram. Consequently the all-inner- ±1\pm1 scalar in (1) overlaps that prior analytic theorem. A pure root ϵ0=0\epsilon_0=0 is included by the choice of aa. An inner pure leaf ϵi=0\epsilon_i=0 is outside the recorded two-type alphabet; its inclusion in the current source estimate should be stated separately.

Equation (3) alone proves neither isotopy of the two diagrams nor a correspondence of their peripheral curves. The current root-star geometry and filling calibration use the marked-mask construction for the literal current presentation. No new topological family or priority claim follows from this comparison.

2. The missing Fourier mode was already proved

The outer squared-kernel theorem, §§1–4, already retains values and first normals and gives

ϕ(LN−hF)=N2∑ℓ∈ℤ(1−ℓ)(−1)(N−1)ℓF̂ℓN−h, \phi(L_{N-h}F)=N^2\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb Z} (1-\ell)(-1)^{(N-1)\ell}\widehat F_{\ell N-h}, \tag{4}

where its angular coordinate is z=−2cos⁡θz=-2\cos\theta. The potentially small winding ℓ=1\ell=1 has coefficient zero. Its finite double-pole contour keeps all four boundary pieces and proves a whole-color gap from a Gaussian angular packet and a strict complementary-node gap.

The present coordinate z=2cos⁡ϑz=2\cos\vartheta differs by a shift of π\pi. Writing F(2cos⁡ϑ)=∑areirϑF(2\cos\vartheta)=\sum a_re^{ir\vartheta}, symmetry and m=−ℓm=-\ell turn (4) into

ϕ(LN−hF)=(−1)hN2∑m∈ℤ(−1)m(m+1)ah+mN. \phi(L_{N-h}F)=(-1)^hN^2\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z} (-1)^m(m+1)a_{h+mN}. \tag{5}

Thus the missing m=−1m=-1 mode is the same cancellation. The current residue derivation, calibrated chirp and periodic endpoint repair give an alternative proof and an application to the stated child estimate; they do not establish a previously absent Fourier identity.

3. Distinct nearby source families

The pretzel source reduction, §1, identifies the ordinary mixed odd-pretzel scalar with a product of rotated boxes, hence with ϕ(∏ihji−1)\phi(\prod_i h_{j_i}^{-1}) in these conventions. It has no outer Lj0L_{j_0}. Its long-filling theorem and width extension concern that different marked source. The root-star argument therefore does not, merely by (3), remove the recorded pretzel width restriction.

The balanced alternating-source note instead treats anchor defects in the charged source of an alternating diagram, with an exact figure-eight obstruction. It is not a Conway root-star or belt-source theorem.
