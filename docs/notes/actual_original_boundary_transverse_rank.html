The original boundary source has exactly two flat face directions

30 October 2026. Independently reviewed. On the labeled original right-angled matching graph, the finite boundary models detect every homogeneous magnetic direction except two explicit face potentials. Two neighboring ORIGINAL physical heads give a primitive slice for those directions. This statement includes the closed source and the common fixed-charge two-cut lattice; arbitrary routed extra factors are excluded.

Together with the separate local curvature estimates, the result gives a uniformly invertible transverse Hessian for the actual nonzero ROOT source product at the resonant boundary sample, in a shrinking folded-channel window. It does NOT say that this sample is stationary, perform a Gaussian contraction, or assign a coefficient in the original relative period.

1. The literal graph and its homogeneous lattice

Use the labeled certificate and original reference meridian of the common boundary chart. Let Delta be its plane cubic graph, with R matching edges D and 2R vertices. The full original source, section 2, identifies this SAME graph with the actual W/pi contraction: matching edges carry V_(2a_i), and all other edges carry V_d, d=N-1. Cups, caps, reversals, and the cyclic order at every vertex are retained. No new routing braid is inserted.

For an oriented typed edge of highest weight L_e center its head by z_e=h_e-L_e/2. The actual degree equation at each vertex is exactly

sum_out z_e-sum_in z_e=0.                            (1)

Changing an edge orientation replaces z_e by -z_e. A literal cup or cap replaces h by L_e-h, which makes precisely this sign change in centered variations. Thus degree-two bends can be contracted without changing the integral HOMOGENEOUS lattice. Half-integral centered basepoints when L_e is odd do not change that lattice: its variations remain integral.

For fixed channel labels, the retained half twist multiplies the original circle term by its exact scalar omega_a^epsilon. The full-source identity already includes the physical reversal in pi. Consequently half twists do not add a magnetic constraint, remove a graph edge, or add a free magnetic variable to (1). Their scalar phases remain in the source.

Write B for the oriented incidence matrix of Delta. Its homogeneous lattice is

V_Z=ker_Z B,  dim_C V=|E|-|V|+1=R+1.                (2)

The certificate makes Delta connected. Its white faces are the actual projection regions, not freely identified copies of developed faces. There are R+2 of them. Let P_f be one additive potential per white face, modulo a common additive constant. Assign to an oriented edge the difference of its left and right face potentials, with the sign chosen to agree with (1). This gives an integral isomorphism

Z^(white faces)/Z(1,...,1)  = V_Z.                   (3)

Here is an elementary proof of surjectivity, including primitivity. Given an integral divergence-free edge assignment, integrate it along paths in the dual graph from a fixed face. Around a dual face, the sum is the original vertex divergence, hence zero. Every closed dual walk on the sphere is a sum of these oriented face boundaries; cutting it at repetitions and filling its simple components proves this directly. The potential is therefore well defined and integral. Its only ambiguity is the initial constant. Conversely differences have zero divergence because the consecutive faces around a vertex telescope. This proves (3) over the integers, not only by a dimension count.

At an ordered shaded triangle with white regions (f0,f1,f2), the physical variations are, up to the fixed orientation,

P_f0-P_f1, P_f1-P_f2,

and the auxiliary variation is their sum P_f0-P_f2. This is also the differential of the actual prefix clocks X=-L_f0/L_f1, Y=-L_f1/L_f2. For a literal dual white, its heads are (d-j,d-i), so the two physical variations are reversed and negated and the auxiliary variation is negated. No dual row is incorrectly identified with the physical row.

2. The exact finite-clock rank

In the common reference normalization, precisely two white faces contain the marked ideal vertex p: call them u,v. Their prefixes have zero lower-left entry. Every other white face has strictly positive normal coefficient and a quadratic prefix diagonal. This follows from the strict supporting inequalities of the labeled certificate. A right-angled ideal vertex has exactly two incident white and two incident shaded faces.

The white-normal polyhedron, sections 2–3, is a convex three-dimensional polyhedron C whose vertices are these white faces and whose facets are the original shaded triangles. Its boundary is a triangulated sphere. An edge of C corresponds to an edge of Delta; u,v are joined by the original marked MAIN edge e0.

Define L on (3) from each literal numerator by the following head rows:

Numerator type Rows of L
200, 002 none
220 first physical variation
022 second physical variation
202 sum of the two physical variations
222 both physical variations

Pull these rows back through the actual dual reversal when the numerator is in pi. Row signs or invertible local changes of these rows do not change their common kernel. All six types, including 202/202 pairs, are allowed. No exclusion at rectangle parameter c=1 is made.

The equations L P=0 say exactly that every pair of QUADRATIC white-face potentials belonging to one shaded triangle is equal. The induced graph on all vertices of C other than u,v is connected. To prove this without an external connectivity theorem, start with any path in the connected 1-skeleton. Remove occurrences of u by rerouting through its cyclic vertex link. In the resulting graph, occurrences of v can be rerouted through its link with u removed. This link is a cycle or a path and is connected. The replacement never returns to u. Thus any two surviving vertices remain joined.

Every surviving edge is in a shaded triangle and its endpoints are both quadratic, so its equality is among the equations of L. Consequently all quadratic-face potentials coincide. After subtracting that common value,

ker L={P_u,P_v arbitrary; P_f=0 for f!=u,v},
dim ker L=2,  rank L=R-1.                           (4)

This is a theorem about independent original source variables. It does not extend the critical-scheme/representation inverse off criticality.

The two directions are precisely the LIMITING geometric framing tangents. At r=N rho the quadratic prefix logarithms have common varying part 2 Log rho-Log s. Write the two constant prefixes as e_u(1+z_u s), e_v(1+z_v s). The original marked primitive meridian makes z_u-z_v=+1 or -1: in the reference convention these are I and -J or its recorded reciprocal. After subtracting one quadratic face potential, the two remaining variable parts are

P_u=-2 Log rho+Log s+Log(1+z_u s),
P_v=-2 Log rho+Log s+Log(1+z_v s).                   (4a)

The exact constant signs, face coefficients, and common 2 Log N are retained additive constants and disappear only on differentiation. The Jacobian of (4a) in (Log rho,s) has determinant

2(z_u-z_v)/[(1+z_u s)(1+z_v s)] != 0.               (4b)

It and its inverse are uniformly bounded on the specified compact chart. Thus these two complete-geometry tangent directions span (4). This is not a stationarity claim for the actual finite auxiliary labels.

3. A neighboring physical-head slice is primitive

The marked main edge e0 separates u,v, so its homogeneous head is P_u-P_v up to sign. Choose either shaded triangle adjacent to e0 and let w be its third white vertex. It is different from u,v and hence quadratic. Each shaded triangle has exactly one matching edge and two main edges. Since e0 is main, exactly one of its other two edges is also MAIN. Call that edge e1. It joins w to u or to v.

On ker L, the two actual head functionals at e0,e1 therefore have matrix, up to the recorded row and column signs,

[[1,-1],[1,0]]  or  [[1,-1],[0,1]].                 (5)

Their determinant is +1 or -1. Thus fixing these two actual physical heads removes exactly the two flat directions, with a primitive, diagram-independent integer minor. This chooses source integration coordinates; it does NOT authorize a new single-port co-source, move an observable through a braid, or claim that e1 is first in an existing Morse slice.

The slice W={head_e0=head_e1=0} in homogeneous variations has dimension R-1, and L|_W is injective. Because the diagram is fixed, choose an integral basis of W once. In that basis its smallest singular value is a positive diagram-dependent constant, independent of N and the frame. Alternatively fixing the two constant-face potentials directly is a primitive complementary slice with the same conclusion.

An arbitrary second physical head need not work. If both its adjacent white faces are quadratic, its restriction to ker L is zero. In general the exact test is the rank of the two cut-head rows restricted to (4). The corresponding kernel dimension after fixing the heads is 2 minus that rank. No generic-cut transversality is silently assumed.

4. Closed, marked-open, and fixed-charge two-cut sources

For the original marked source, equality of the two exposed heads is the permitted WHOLE-SOURCE consequence of integer degree conservation. It is not a local replacement of the unrestricted mark by an invariant cap. After that equality, restoring the exposed edge gives exactly (2), with its actual marked scalar weight still attached. The marked-open chart including the one free exposed head therefore has the same homogeneous space V. Fixing that head imposes its one displayed linear functional.

For two cuts on DISTINCT original edges use the actual port orientations of the common charged chart: the heads are (a,a+delta) and (l,l+delta). At a FIXED charge delta, subtract one recorded affine particular solution. Variations then have equal heads across each cut and restore exactly a cycle flow on Delta. Thus, when a,l are included as coordinates, the homogeneous space is again V and (4) applies unchanged. Fixing a,l gives the slice obtained by setting their two cut-head functionals to zero, so (5) is the promised sufficient condition for a full transverse slice.

The deleted graph is connected for two distinct edges of this certified Delta. Indeed it is the dual of the triangulated sphere boundary of C. If deleting at most two dual edges disconnected its triangles, the boundary between a nonempty proper set of triangles and its complement would be a nonempty edge cycle with at most two edges. A simplicial sphere has no such cycle. This also proves that a single original edge is not a bridge. If two cut positions lie on the SAME edge, or a routed source uses a different graph, retain the component conditions of the common charged-chart theorem instead; the preceding conclusion need not apply.

Varying delta itself adds an affine relative-boundary direction. It is not a closed cycle flow and is NOT counted as a third flat framing variable by (4). The neighboring-band quotient fixes delta to +1 or -1 after its single analytic interpolation. The two physical support cycles still differ; (3)–(5) do not identify them or erase an endpoint current.

5. Assembly of the finite transverse curvature bounds

This section uses the separately proved local estimates in the finite boundary curvature theorem. Retain the exact models of the correlated-band theorem, their scalar phases, the literal pi reversal, and every cup and mark. The statement concerns the ORIGINAL uncontracted root product. It does not include the nonlinear binomial co-source or replace its actual weight.

Use logarithmic face coordinates, so a physical clock is multiplied by exp(P_f-P_g). Equivalently the finite head changes by N(P_f-P_g)/(2pi i); the integral lattice in sections 1–4 is the same after this fixed scalar normalization. At the real limiting boundary clocks, the local 220/022 scalar curvature, 202 positive-total curvature, and 222 two-variable curvature are bounded below by cN and above by CN on their displayed rows of L. Their non-affine phases have already been retained in the local source formulas; the positive reference Hessians are taken in the corresponding real logarithmic clock coordinates.

Thus their sum is a real symmetric matrix H0_N with

cN ||L P||^2 <= P^T H0_N P <= CN ||L P||^2          (6)

for real P, with a fixed finite number of local rows. Sign changes from 022 or the literal dual are included in L. In a fixed basis of the primitive slice W of section 3, (6) gives

c_D N I <= H0_N|_W <= C_D N I.                     (7)

Now retain the exact finite-r clocks at r=N rho. Their relative errors from the finite nonzero limiting clocks are O(1/N). The uniform complex logarithmic derivative bounds through order three in the local curvature theorem make their Hessian correction O(1). The 200/002 zero-free Bessel factors have O(1) Hessian on the common compact neighborhood; their endpoint-product corrections have Hessian O(1/N). Linear head monomials and all fixed channel scalars contribute zero Hessian.

Let S=sum_i(d-2a_i). The retained 202/222 partner products and auxiliary root cups have Hessian O(S), since their factors are uniformly separated from zero. Mixed-type cup/partner residuals are smaller after their linear monomials are removed. These are estimates of the actual root identities; no root reflection or partner collision is differentiated as a generic-q identity. Physical cup/cap characters are linear.

The nonzero actual product and finite model satisfy a relative O(1/N) estimate on a fixed source polydisc. Cauchy’s formula gives an O(1/N) Hessian error for their local logarithms. Consequently the actual root source Hessian H_N at the boundary sample satisfies, on W,

||H_N-H0_N|| <= C_D(1+S).                          (8)

For S=o(N), (7),(8) imply invertibility and

||(H_N|_W)^-1|| <= C_D/N.                          (9)

This is a complex Hessian statement; positivity is asserted for the reference matrix H0_N, not for an arbitrarily perturbed complex point. All constants are uniform in the stated alpha/rho compacts and through both maximal-fold parities. The shrinking s-window is an INTERNAL channel window, not the external auxiliary-color h-window needed for the eventual filling theorem. Genuine q normals are not estimated by (8),(9).

6. What this does not yet prove

The complete geometric framing sample is not automatically stationary for the finite labels a_i. Even at the folded limit the actual linear source characters and retained Poisson sector must be checked against the boundary action before invoking a stationary reduction. A Hessian bound alone does not bound the gradient or the displacement of a nearby critical point. The binomial base localization, transverse amplitude, Jacobian, square-root branch and common descending cycle would all have to be retained in a Gaussian theorem.

The exact critical-scheme inverse remains a statement on the critical representation germ. No off-critical flat representations were used to obtain (4) or (9). In particular a generic unframed perturbation of a zero lower-left prefix is not assigned the same scale as a primitive source-head perturbation.

Finally, selecting the primitive neighboring-head slice does not expose those heads as two first slots without additional source algebra. The original relative cotangent periods, their support/carry boundaries, the global nonzero coefficient and the mixed-color/alias estimates remain separate. No hyperbolic-volume equality follows here.
