Exact finite-band unit-turn conjugation and the branching forcing

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

Conjugating spectral multiplication by an original unit turn gives an explicit tridiagonal operator. A fixed number of turns therefore preserves a finite-band operator presentation, with polynomial operator cost for the conjugated multiplication itself. This is not a polynomial bound for the turn operator. The ordinary branching product has an additional mixed-difference forcing, retained below with its original full normal data. Its growth after turning remains uncontrolled by the present calculation.
