Actual complementary heads and the outgoing large-clock packet

30 October 2026. Two exact source identities extend the proved boundary packets. Generic Chevalley complementation transports the retained 0F1 normals to the small second-clock regime. A separate positive-matrix comparison controls the outgoing large-second-clock numerator at the root, through the fold. The original scalar factors and large partner remain explicit. This does not yet assemble every cup factor or select a global period.

1. An exact generic reciprocity, before any root collision

Let H=H_m^B(U,V) be the actual generic white polynomial, and put A0=Qd=(BQ)(-1), d=N-1 fixed. Then

H(A0/V,A0/U)=A0^m(UV)^(-m)H(U,V).                   (1)

Here U=Qx,V=Qy use their marked logarithms, and the complementary heads are EXACTLY d-y,d-x. In particular d does not move with the quantum period, and (1) may be genuinely differentiated.

There is a direct independent-variable proof from the actual white Ward equations. Set Htilde=(UV/A0)^m H(A0/V,A0/U). It is polynomial of degree at most m in each variable. Substituting X=A0/V,Y=A0/U shows that the E equation for H becomes the F equation for Htilde, and the F equation becomes its E equation, with the original coefficients. For example multiply the E equation at X,Y by UV/A0^2; it reads

U(1-V)H(QX,Y)+(1-U)H(X,QY)=(Q^m-UV)H(X,Y),

which is precisely the transformed F equation.

These two equations determine the polynomial up to one scalar. To see this without a finite-grid continuation assumption, expand in the Newton basis P_b(U)=product_(r<b)(U-Q^r). After removing the first b basis terms, evaluate the F equation at U=Q^b. The coefficient h_b(V) must satisfy

Q^b(1-V)h_b(V/Q)=(1-VQ^(b-m))h_b(V).

Among polynomials of degree at most m its solutions are exactly the multiples of V^b P_(m-b)(V), as follows by coefficient comparison, starting at its lowest power. Induction gives the actual Newton basis used in the source. The E equation then fixes its adjacent coefficients by the original generic ratio. This is a rational-function proof for generic Q,B; specialization follows after clearing denominators.

Finally H(0,0)=(-1)^m Q1. The coefficient of UmVm in Htilde is A0(-m)H(0,0)=(-B)m Q2, exactly the top coefficient of H. This proves (1) with scalar ONE, and in particular retains the actual Chevalley normalization.

2. The genuine small-second-clock packet

Suppose at the root U_N is in a fixed compact subset of C*, V_N=N^(-2)v_N with v_N in such a compact, and the fixed marked heads have size O(N log N). Retain t=N^2 epsilon and all moving quantities from the generic resonant hard-edge theorem. The transformed clocks U’=A0/V,V’=A0/U satisfy its hypotheses. At t=0 define

kappa'_N=A0(0)/v_N, V'_N=A0(0)/U_N,
K'_N=sin^2(pi m/N)(V'_N-1)/(pi^2 kappa'_N V'_N).

Equation (1) transports the exact normalization as well:

Z_low(t)=c_m(t)A0(t)^m
             product_(r<m)(1-Q(t)^r V(t)/A0(t))
        =(-1)^m Q(t)^[m(m-1)/2]
             product_(r<m)(1-B(t)Q(t)^(r+1)V(t)).     (2)

Thus, uniformly on each fixed |t|<=rho<pi,

H_m^B(U,V)/Z_low(t)
  =0F1(;1-t/(pi i);-K'_N)+O((1+log N)/N).            (3)

All fixed scaled genuine quantum jets have the same convergence. The transformed heads d-y,d-x are fixed as epsilon varies; neither the A0 derivative nor the marked character is suppressed. Equation (2) notably cancels the B powers in c_m A0^m exactly. The estimate remains absolute across zeros of the limiting function.

3. A uniform comparison of the two actual positive matrices

At the ROOT let a<=floor((N-1)/2), theta=pi/N, and use the actual Hermitian matrices H_+,H_- from the full physical determinant theorem. Their diagonal is

d_n=1/[cos(n theta)cos((n+1)theta)],

and their adjacent upper entries are

sqrt(B_n)[sin(n theta)+/-i cos(n theta)],
B_n=sin^2(n theta)/[cos^2(n theta)
                  sin((2n-1)theta)sin((2n+1)theta)].

They satisfy, uniformly in N,a including the maximal folded a,

H_+ <= 2H_-+8I,       H_- <= 2H_++8I.                (4)

Here the inequalities are Hermitian quadratic-form inequalities. The proof retains their exact endpoint coefficients. Both matrices have the same Cholesky pivots as the positive D matrix, namely

p_(n-1)=sin(n theta)/[cos(n theta)sin((2n-1)theta)].

Write their quadratic forms as sums of squared adjacent Cholesky terms and the final positive pivot. Flipping an offdiagonal phase changes its term by 2i sqrt(B_n)cos(n theta)/p_(n-1) times the next coordinate. The inequality |x+y|2<=2|x|2+2|y|^2 gives an additional diagonal cost

8 B_n cos^2(n theta)/p_(n-1)
             =4sin(2n theta)/sin((2n+1)theta)<=8.

Indeed sin(2n theta)=2cos(theta)sin((2n+1)theta) -sin((2n+2)theta), and the last sine is nonnegative for every actual offdiagonal n<=a-1. This proves the first inequality in (4), and complex conjugation proves the second. No lower eigenvalue estimate or inverse channel norm is introduced.

4. The actual root numerator when the second clock is large

Put K_a=[2a choose a]_Q. The exact source determinant, with its retained scalar, gives

H_a^1(U,V)/V^a
   =K_a det[I-(qU/2)H_--(q^-1/(2V))H_+].           (5)

For example derive this from R_a(z;-cos gamma) =det[zI-(e^(i gamma)H_-+e^(-i gamma)H_+)/2], with z=q^t/U, e^(i gamma)=q q^t and (qt)2=UV, before canceling the displayed powers. This proves (5) as a polynomial identity and does not make a square-root choice in its final formula.

Let qU range in a fixed compact subset of Re z<0, and let V=N^2 kappa_N with kappa_N in a fixed compact subset of C*. Set M0=I-(qU/2)H_-. Then

H_a^1(U,V)=V^a K_a det(M0)[1+O(N^-1)].               (6)

This is a UNIFORM RELATIVE estimate, including folded a. To prove it put D0=I+(eta/2)H_-, where -Re(qU)>=eta>0. The conjugated M0 has inverse norm at most one, while (4) bounds D0(-1/2)H_+D0(-1/2) uniformly. Thus the relative perturbation from the last term in (5) has norm O(N^-2). Expanding Log det costs at most a O(N-2)=O(N-1). This also proves nonvanishing and a marked relative logarithm on the stated domain. At qU negative real, det M0 is strictly positive. K_a and V^a retain their original phases.

For the actual LARGE root channel m=d-a, the exact root partner identity supplies

H_m^1(U,V)=H_a^1(U,V)
                  product_(r=a)^(m-1)(UV-Q^r).

Consequently its corresponding packet is

H_m^1(U,V)=V^m U^(m-a) K_a det(M0)[1+O(N^-1)].       (7)

The added product differs from (UV)^(m-a) by 1+O(N^-1). This is explicitly a ROOT identity: it is not differentiated as a generic large/small collision. Generic normals in this second large-clock regime remain separate from (6),(7).

5. The genuine paired 200/220 circle

There is an exact geometric use of the negative ray in (6). Take the root theorem’s 200 box with B=-J, J=[[1,1],[0,1]], and write

A=[[a,-(a+1)^2/4],[4,-2-a]], P=BA,
C=I+b(P+I), Aout=C^-1 A C,
Q1=e[[1,z],[0,1]], W=A^-1Q1, e=+/-1.

Direct multiplication, with the ACTUAL inverse collar transport, gives

W_21=-4e,       (Aout W)_21=16e b^2,
U_geom,out=-W_21/(Aout W)_21=1/(4b^2).               (8)

Here C names the auxiliary collar, so its inverse gives the displayed transport. Conventions naming the transport itself C write C A C^-1 instead. The lower-left entry and ratio in (8) are unchanged under b->-b, so this squared-ray certificate is identical in those two notations; it does not change any primitive peripheral mark.

The marked complete rectangle collar has b=-i/(2c), c>0, including its retained half-twist convention. Thus U_geom,out=-c^2. The physical half-index argument is U=q^-1 U_geom,out up to the retained finite-r correction, so qU approaches precisely this negative ray. Formula (7) therefore gives the nonzero outgoing 220 numerator paired with the incoming 200 Bessel packet. No independent phase of U has been chosen.

The actual white monomial, F_m denominator, categorical dual, channel cup, physical caps and exterior source scalar still have to be retained when assembling the full box. Their exact lifts and logarithmic-escape estimates remain available; they are not set equal to one by (8). Other prefix patterns, original boundary currents and the global relative-cycle coefficient are not resolved by this local paired-numerator statement.
