Actual cap residues at the Pachner norm wall

28 October 2026. The actual source and co-source give an explicit residue functional at the simultaneous norm wall of the last-move calculation. For a full holomorphic product it retains its original matrix corner. For split meromorphic terms it retains a finite list of source jets, with bounds uniform in the level. The cap is not a projector onto geometric edge winding one.

The results below use exactly the source frames and residue constants of the cap-wall theorem. They do not replace an invariant or assign an arbitrary source to the geometric triangulation. The original geometric exterior still needs its typed comparison with these local matrix coordinates.

The exact audit checks 148 direct finite-product identities in cyclotomic fields, the generic first and second central jets, and the uniform-bound constants. Its saved results separate central-meromorphic, fixed-cover-pole, and global-source scope.

For the original word, its Ward corner is used only after the full intertwining contraction has been assembled. These residue formulas do not authorize applying that Ward replacement separately to a cyclic/sewing sector whose full original marked row is different.

1. Coincident residues reproduce the original corners

Fix N≥2N\ge2, d=N−1d=N-1, Q=e2πi/NQ=e^{2\pi i/N} and put

ε=aN,δ=(1−ε)1/N,u0=δ,ud=Qδ, \varepsilon=a^N,\quad \delta=(1-\varepsilon)^{1/N},\quad u_0=\delta,\quad u_d=Q\delta,

Pns(a)=an∏j=1n(1−usQj),Lmt(a)=a−m∏j=0m−1(1−utQj). P_n^{s}(a)= \frac{a^n}{\prod_{j=1}^{n}(1-u_sQ^j)},\qquad L_m^{t}(a)= a^{-m}\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}(1-u_tQ^j). \tag{1}

Their original residue constants are

α0=β0=1,αd=1−Q,βd=N. \alpha_0=\beta_0=1,\qquad \alpha_d=1-Q,\qquad\beta_d=N.

Write σ0=τ0=0\sigma_0=\tau_0=0, σd=1\sigma_d=1, τd=d\tau_d=d, and Dts=σs+τtD_{ts}=\sigma_s+\tau_t. For any matrix O(a)O(a) holomorphic at zero,

Indeed Pdd=(1−Q)a−1+O(aN−1)P^d_d=(1-Q)a^{-1}+O(a^{N-1}), while Pnd=O(an)P^d_n=O(a^n) for n<dn<d. Also Ldd=Na−d+O(a)L^d_d=Na^{-d}+O(a) and Lmd=O(a−m)L^d_m=O(a^{-m}) for m<dm<d. At the zero corner P00=L00=1P^0_0=L^0_0=1, whereas Pn0=O(an)P^0_n=O(a^n) and Lm0=O(aN−m)L^0_m=O(a^{N-m}) for m,n>0m,n>0. Thus exactly (m,n)=(t,s)(m,n)=(t,s) has the maximal required pole; every other term has at least one additional power of aa. Multiplication by a holomorphic O(a)O(a) cannot create another residue.

For s=t=ds=t=d, (2) is specifically

1N(1−Q)Res⁡aN−1Ld(a)O(a)Pd(a)da=Odd(0). \frac1{N(1-Q)} \operatorname{Res}a^{N-1}L^d(a)O(a)P^d(a)\,da =O_{dd}(0). \tag{3}

This equals the actual independent input/output cap residues. It is an equality of residue functionals on the stated class, not a pullback of two differential forms to their diagonal. For individually singular factors the order of regularization must therefore still be proved; it cannot be inferred by substituting the same parameter into two residue formulas.

Taking O=IO=I in (3) gives exactly one. The marked wall has its original unit augmentation.

2. Exact source jets for meromorphic split terms

O(ε)=∑r=−p∞Orεr. O(\varepsilon)=\sum_{r=-p}^{\infty}O_r\varepsilon^r.

The second-order coefficient includes the cubic expansion of δ\delta before the removable denominator is divided.

3. Uniform bounds: no exponential source-jet cost

Take ρ=1/16\rho=1/16. On |ε|≤ρ|\varepsilon|\le\rho,

|δ−1|≤2|ε|N. |\delta-1|\le\frac{2|\varepsilon|}{N}.

For a nontrivial root QrQ^r, put ℓ=dist⁡(r,Nℤ)≥1\ell=\operatorname{dist}(r,N\mathbb Z)\ge1. The elementary sine bound gives |1−Qr|≥4ℓ/N|1-Q^r|\ge4\ell/N, and hence

|1−Qrδ|≥3ℓN,|1−Qδ1−Q|<2. |1-Q^r\delta|\ge\frac{3\ell}{N},\qquad \left|\frac{1-Q\delta}{1-Q}\right|<2. \tag{8}

All nonexceptional entries of the table are therefore bounded by (4/3)|ε|/ℓ(4/3)|\varepsilon|/\ell. The two removable quotients use

A(ε)=εN(1−δ)=1+δ+⋯+δN−1N,|A|≤1+ρ. A(\varepsilon)=\frac{\varepsilon}{N(1-\delta)} =\frac{1+\delta+\cdots+\delta^{N-1}}N, \qquad |A|\le1+\rho.

In particular

Kd=A+εQN(1−Q),|Kd|≤1+ρ+ρ/4<2. K_d=A+\frac{\varepsilon Q}{N(1-Q)},\qquad |K_d|\le1+\rho+\rho/4<2.

The remaining wrap value (1−Qδ)/(1−Q)(1-Q\delta)/(1-Q) is also bounded by two. Consequently, for all four actual cap channels, all N≥2N\ge2, and all indices,

sup|ε|≤1/16|Hnts(ε)|≤2,|Hr,nts|≤2⋅16r. \sup_{|\varepsilon|\le1/16}|H^{ts}_n(\varepsilon)|\le2, \qquad |H^{ts}_{r,n}|\le2\cdot16^r. \tag{9}

For example, in the original flat Euclidean matrix basis,

|ℛts(O)|≤2N∑r=0p16r∥O−r∥op. \boxed{\quad |\mathcal R_{ts}(O)| \le2N\sum_{r=0}^{p}16^r\|O_{-r}\|_{\mathrm{op}}. \quad} \tag{10}

The factor NN can be replaced by a trace-norm bound on each selected cyclic diagonal. Independent source variables tensor: for a fixed word and fixed pole orders their combined extraction has polynomial cost in NN.

This is a bound relative to the actual Laurent coefficient norms. It does not turn exponentially large input currents into small ones without their exact cancellation. Nor is the norm asserted unchanged under an exponentially conditioned fusion frame.

The actual sewing theorem retains at most second-order poles per Euler variable. When such an actual matrix term is expressed in the stated central local coordinate, (5)–(10) give its exact cap correction and a bound for the finite source-jet pushforward. They do not authorize dropping the sewing poles or their finite-part prescription.

Fixed pole order on the Fermat cover, without central descent

There is a complementary statement for an actual matrix that is meromorphic in aa itself. Put

P̂s=aσsPs/αs,L̂t=aτtLt/βt,Jts(a)=P̂s(a)L̂t(a). \widehat P_s=a^{\sigma_s}P^s/\alpha_s,\qquad \widehat L_t=a^{\tau_t}L^t/\beta_t,\qquad J_{ts}(a)=\widehat P_s(a)\widehat L_t(a).

These are the regular original cap frames. The uniform cap-wall estimate gives an absolute CC such that their Euclidean norms are at most CC on |a|≤1/(16N)|a|\le1/(16N). Consequently the rank-one kernel has trace norm at most C2C^2 there. If O(a)=∑ℓ=−p∞AℓaℓO(a)=\sum_{\ell=-p}^{\infty}A_\ell a^\ell, then exactly

ℛts(O)=∑r=0ptr⁡(A−rJts,r),∥Jts,r∥1≤C2(16N)r. \mathcal R_{ts}(O)= \sum_{r=0}^{p}\operatorname{tr}(A_{-r}J_{ts,r}), \qquad \|J_{ts,r}\|_1\le C^2(16N)^r. \tag{10a}

This follows from the original formula Res⁡L̂tOP̂sda/a\operatorname{Res}\widehat L_tO\widehat P_s\,da/a and Cauchy’s coefficient estimate in trace norm. Thus

|ℛts(O)|≤C2∑r=0p(16N)r∥A−r∥op. |\mathcal R_{ts}(O)| \le C^2\sum_{r=0}^{p}(16N)^r\|A_{-r}\|_{\mathrm{op}}. \tag{10b}

The first central correction to δ\delta has degree NN, which proves this table directly from (1). These are genuine source and co-source derivatives, with all original normalizations.

A fixed pole order on the cover and a fixed pole order in the central variable are different hypotheses: a central pole of order pp has cover order NpNp. Estimate (10) is strong precisely because it uses central descent and exact cyclic-diagonal telescoping. Estimate (10b) applies without descent only when the cover pole order is bounded. Neither is a uniform assertion for arbitrary separated Puiseux factors with growing pole order.

4. Monodromy selects the physical cap charge

Under a↦Qaa\mapsto Qa, δ\delta is fixed and

Ps(Qa)=ZPs(a),Lt(Qa)=Lt(a)Z−1,Zen=Qnen. P^s(Qa)=ZP^s(a),\qquad L^t(Qa)=L^t(a)Z^{-1},\qquad Ze_n=Q^ne_n.

The total residue weight is QDtsQ^{D_{ts}}. Deck averaging keeps precisely matrix entries with

m−n≡t−s(mod⁡N). m-n\equiv t-s\pmod N.

The zero jet of (4) further selects the actual corner (t,s)(t,s). For d→0d\to0 and 0→d0\to d this is the WRAPPED entry of the corresponding cyclic diagonal, with coefficient one. Thus actual cap residues require, rather than delete, a retained cyclic boundary charge.

In particular the compatible root monodromy (Q,Q−1,Q)(Q,Q^{-1},Q) of the last Pachner wall cannot by itself be read as a projector onto geometric edge winding one. It gives a finite root/deck charge. The geometric integer lift has not been identified with the original integer-degree/carry data.

5. The literal primitive cap retains the generic local wall

There is also a direct local nonvanishing statement. Express the primitive normalized pentagon in a common clock/shift basis

X=μZ,Y=ηS,ZS=QSZ,μ(0)=Q,ηN=x0, X=\mu Z,\quad Y=\eta S,\quad ZS=QSZ, \quad \mu(0)=Q,\quad \eta^N=x_0,

and take the actual dd input and output cap corners in that basis. At a generic exceptional direction, x0∉{0,1}x_0\notin\{0,1\} and y0=1−x0y_0=1-x_0. The regular two-atom side is

O0=D(Y;b)D(bX;h),bN=y0,hN=x0. O_0=D(Y;b)\,D(bX;h),\quad b^N=y_0,\quad h^N=x_0.

The second factor is diagonal with every entry nonzero. Write the first as ∑n=0dcnSn\sum_{n=0}^{d}c_nS^n. Its exact cyclic difference equation gives

(Qn−b)cn=ηQn−1cn−1, (Q^n-b)c_n=\eta Q^{n-1}c_{n-1}, \tag{11}

with indices cyclic. Since η≠0\eta\ne0 and bN=1−x0≠1b^N=1-x_0\ne1, every coefficient is nonzero: one zero would propagate around the entire cycle and make the invertible matrix zero. Therefore

(O0)dd=c0D(bX;h)dd≠0. \boxed{\quad (O_0)_{dd}=c_0\,D(bX;h)_{dd}\ne0.\quad} \tag{12}

Formula (3) says that the actual paired cap retains this local wall. It is not killed by operator regularity, by a norm-root zero, or by deck averaging.

This does not identify this primitive cap placement with the exterior of the specific oversized 525_2 face. The actual crossing’s atom order and commutator orientation must be matched; an equation between central sums alone does not establish that embedding. No global excessive-face coefficient is computed here. The result is the explicit original-cap local charge and the finite uniformly bounded extraction map that an eventual typed exterior comparison must preserve.
