Literal pretzel coefficient phase, marked geometric saddles, and their action

Subsequent global proof. The full coherent period asymptotic now establishes complete augmented-source survival by a finite inner transformation, a global two-branch bound and real Gaussian estimates. The local symbol audit below remains a separate verification; it is not being promoted to a survival proof by itself.

29 October 2026. This independently checks the leading phase from the EXACT finite factorial coefficients, its branch marks, all regular stationary equations, and the nonzero full Hessian. The geometric action has an explicit Lobachevsky formula. These are symbol and local saddle statements only: no deformation of the original finite source, nonzero thimble coefficient, or asymptotic dominance is proved here.

1. The actual finite term fixes the phase

Use the original complete-base polynomial with its source normalization:

κzN(w+w−1)N2=∑0≤r≤v≤Mtv,r(w),tv,r=F2vFv4(Q−v;Q)rQrFr3(qw;Q)r(q/w;Q)r,M=⌊(N−1)/2⌋. \frac{\kappa z_N(w+w^{-1})}{N^2} =\sum_{0\leq r\leq v\leq M}t_{v,r}(w),\quad t_{v,r}=\frac{F_{2v}}{F_v^4} \frac{(Q^{-v};Q)_rQ^r}{F_r^3} (qw;Q)_r(q/w;Q)_r, \qquad M=\lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor. \tag{1}

Here F_a=(Q;Q)_a, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, and the original kappa=q((N-1)2/2) is retained. On the interior physical index triangle put X=Q^v, Y=Q^r. In a regular product-log chart the leading exponent of the normalized term is N S/(2pi i), where

S=−6Li⁡2(1)−Li⁡2(X2)+4Li⁡2(X)+Li⁡2(X−1)−Li⁡2(Y/X)+Li⁡2(w)+Li⁡2(w−1)−Li⁡2(wY)−Li⁡2(Y/w)+3Li⁡2(Y). \begin{split} S={}&-6\operatorname{Li}_2(1)-\operatorname{Li}_2(X^2) +4\operatorname{Li}_2(X)+\operatorname{Li}_2(X^{-1}) -\operatorname{Li}_2(Y/X)\\ &+\operatorname{Li}_2(w)+\operatorname{Li}_2(w^{-1}) -\operatorname{Li}_2(wY)-\operatorname{Li}_2(Y/w) +3\operatorname{Li}_2(Y). \tag{2} \end{split}

For example log F_v has leading N[Li2(1)-Li2(X)]/(2pi i). The first quotient in (1) gives -3Li2(1)-Li2(X^2)+4Li2(X). The terminating Pochhammer gives Li2(X^(-1))-Li2(Y/X), F_r^(-3) gives -3Li2(1)+3Li2(Y), and the two remaining products give the four w terms in (2). This is the product-log primitive continued from the physical triangle, not a separately chosen favorable potential. The factor Q^r is subleading in this scaling; the half-step q in the last two products affects subleading amplitudes. The full original z_N additionally contains kappa^(-1), whose unwrapped leading contribution to S is +pi^2, a REAL constant per box. No imaginary action or stationary equation is lost by recording it separately.

There is a check avoiding all primitive choices. The EXACT adjacent finite term ratios are

tv+1,rtv,r=(1−Q2v+1)(1−Q2v+2)(1−Qv+1)41−Q−v−11−Qr−v−1, \frac{t_{v+1,r}}{t_{v,r}}= \frac{(1-Q^{2v+1})(1-Q^{2v+2})}{(1-Q^{v+1})^4} \frac{1-Q^{-v-1}}{1-Q^{r-v-1}}, tv,r+1tv,r=Q(1−Qr−v)(1−qwQr)(1−qQr/w)(1−Qr+1)3. \frac{t_{v,r+1}}{t_{v,r}}= Q\frac{(1-Q^{r-v})(1-qwQ^r)(1-qQ^r/w)}{(1-Q^{r+1})^3}. \tag{3}

Their regular limits are exactly exp(S_x), exp(S_y), for x=log X,y=log Y below. These identities retain the root phases before taking a limit. They do not extend an interior assertion to the terminating boundary r=v or to any collision divisor.

2. All regular sheets and the inner source marks

In logarithmic coordinates x,y,t=log w, differentiating (2) gives

eSx=(1+X)2(X−1)(X−Y),eSy=(1−Y/X)(1−wY)(1−Y/w)(1−Y)3,eSt=wY−1w−Y. e^{S_x}=\frac{(1+X)^2}{(X-1)(X-Y)},\qquad e^{S_y}=\frac{(1-Y/X)(1-wY)(1-Y/w)}{(1-Y)^3},\qquad e^{S_t}=\frac{wY-1}{w-Y}. \tag{4}

A regular dilogarithm sheet change adds 2pi i times an INTEGER linear form in x,y,t, plus a real multiple of 4pi^2. This follows directly by continuing dLi2(z)=-Log(1-z)dLog z; every argument in (2) is an integer monomial. Thus (4) and the Hessian are sheet independent. A stationary PHASE also retains the corresponding integer Fourier/Poisson marks; exponentiating alone forgets them. When a sheet is changed those marks change by the same integer linear form. The combined marked phase has unchanged imaginary action. No choice of a different sheet may silently delete this compensating source mark.

For an s-fold power in the actual positive winding m from the finite enclosing residue, the explicit regular geometric branch below uses PRINCIPAL dilogarithms. There one has

S_x=0, S_y=2pi i, S_t=2i alpha, alpha=pi m/s.

Consequently its locally stationary marked phase is

ℱ=∑i=1s[S(Xi,Yi,w)−2πilog⁡Yi]−2πimlog⁡w. \mathcal F=\sum_{i=1}^s [S(X_i,Y_i,w)-2\pi i\log Y_i] -2\pi i m\log w. \tag{5}

The inner y mark is compulsory on this sheet. It happens to change only the REAL action at the geometric point because |Y_i|=1; that does not justify dropping it before stationarity or continuation. On the conjugate/reciprocal branch its y mark is -1 instead. The exact finite winding weights, their double-pole multiplicities, and the original common Cartan are still those of the source formula. Equation (5) does not select a winding or a cycle for it.

3. Nondegenerate geometric solutions and the excluded factor

In the regular locus, the first two equations e(S_x)=e(S_y)=1 imply

X=Y−1Y+3,w+w−1=6−16Y(1+Y)2. X=\frac{Y-1}{Y+3},\qquad w+w^{-1}=6-\frac{16Y}{(1+Y)^2}. \tag{6}

Factors Y=0, Y=1, X=0, X=1 and all zero denominators must be excluded before using these rational equivalences. For the outer condition put U=exp(2pi i m/s). Then Y=(Uw+1)/(w+U), and exact substitution in the second equation of (6) yields

(w−1)2w(w+U)2{(1+U)2(w2+1)−2(U2−6U+1)w}=0. \frac{(w-1)^2}{w(w+U)^2} \{(1+U)^2(w^2+1)-2(U^2-6U+1)w\}=0. \tag{7}

For U!= -1, the factor w=1 gives Y=1, a collision rather than a regular geometric saddle. It may not be retained as a nondegenerate stationary branch. On the regular branch,

w+w−1=2−16U(1+U)2=2−4sec⁡2α. w+w^{-1}=2-\frac{16U}{(1+U)^2} =2-4\sec^2\alpha. \tag{8}

For 0<m<s/2, define

η=2arsinh⁡(tan⁡α),β=arctan⁡(sec⁡α),w=−eη,Y=e2iβ,X=isec⁡α2−isec⁡α. \eta=2\operatorname{arsinh}(\tan\alpha),\quad \beta=\arctan(\sec\alpha),\quad w=-e^\eta,\quad Y=e^{2i\beta},\quad X=\frac{i\sec\alpha}{2-i\sec\alpha}. \tag{9}

These satisfy (4)–(8) and the marked derivatives in section 2. All their dilogarithm arguments remain regular for fixed alpha in (0,pi/2). This is the outside-|w| branch appropriate as a candidate for the actual enclosing-circle representation. The other regular solution for this U is obtained by w->1/w and (X,Y)->(conjugate X,conjugate Y); its imaginary action is the negative one. Neither statement proves a contour reaches either point. For fixed odd s, the positive finite-source windings are m=1,…,(s-1)/2 once N is sufficiently large, all within this range.

The principal integer marks can be verified without numerical argument tracking. Put tau=sec(alpha)>1, A=atan(1/tau), B=atan(2/tau). The four principal arguments in S_x are respectively pi/2+A-2B, A-B, A, pi-A, with coefficients 2,-4,1,-1; their sum is exactly zero. For S_y, Arg(1-Y/X)=pi-A and Arg(1-Y)=-A. Both 1-wY and 1-Y/w lie in the upper half-plane, the latter in its right half. Their product, using (4), has principal argument pi-2A. Their argument sum is in (0,3pi/2), so it equals pi-2A without a full turn. Thus S_y has argument (pi-A)+(pi-2A)+3A=2pi. Finally S_t has imaginary part the difference of these last two positive arguments, which is in (-pi/2,pi). Equation (4) fixes it to 2alpha in (0,pi). The real parts vanish by the same rational equations. These prove the asserted 0,1,m/s marks on the entire connected branch.

At s=3,m=1, (9) is exactly

w=-7-4sqrt3, X=(-1+i)/2, Y=(-3+4i)/5.

4. The FULL saddle Hessian is nonzero

Write the one-box Hessian in the logarithmic variables as

H3=(ab0bde0ef), H_3=\begin{pmatrix}a&b&0\\b&d&e\\0&e&f\end{pmatrix},

where

a=2X1+X−XX−1−XX−Y,b=YX−Y, a=\frac{2X}{1+X}-\frac X{X-1}-\frac X{X-Y}, \quad b=\frac Y{X-Y}, d=−YX−Y−wY1−wY−Yw−Y+3Y1−Y,e=−wY1−wY+Yw−Y,f=−1−wY1−wY−Yw−Y. d=-\frac Y{X-Y}-\frac{wY}{1-wY}-\frac Y{w-Y} +\frac{3Y}{1-Y}, \quad e=-\frac{wY}{1-wY}+\frac Y{w-Y}, \quad f=-1-\frac{wY}{1-wY}-\frac Y{w-Y}. \tag{10}

After the first relation in (6), its determinant is exactly

det⁡H3=−Y(Y−1)(Y+3)2(w2−6w+1)4(w−Y)(Y+1)2(Yw−1). \det H_3=-\frac{Y(Y-1)(Y+3)^2(w^2-6w+1)} {4(w-Y)(Y+1)^2(Yw-1)}. \tag{11}

On the second relation of (6), the inner two-by-two block K_2 has

det⁡K2=−Y2(Y+3)2(w−1)2(w−Y)(Y+1)3(Yw−1). \det K_2=-\frac{Y^2(Y+3)^2(w-1)^2} {(w-Y)(Y+1)^3(Yw-1)}. \tag{12}

Both are NONZERO at (9). In particular checking only three collective coordinates has not missed the antisymmetric modes of the s actual copies. The full (2s+1)-variable Hessian of (5) has

det⁡Hess⁡ℱ=s(det⁡K2)s−1det⁡H3≠0. \boxed{\det\operatorname{Hess}\mathcal F =s(\det K_2)^{s-1}\det H_3\ne0.} \tag{13}

This follows by its exact block Schur complement. Its effective outer entry, before summing the s copies, is

det H3/det K2=i cos(alpha)

on the expanding branch. It changes sign on the reciprocal branch. This is a local saddle fact, not a global maximum or a statement about the original integration-cycle intersection.

For the FULL power z_N^s there are also mixed regular inner branches, which must not be suppressed. At a common negative w<-1 the two inner solutions are the plus solution (9) and its conjugate. Their outer characters are U and U^(-1), respectively, and their inner y marks are +1 and -1. With k plus copies, put n=2k-s. Since s is odd, n cannot vanish. On the principal product chart the positive winding condition is

n>2m, alpha=pi m/n

for the outside-|w| solutions. These are all regular stationary possibilities in this chart for a positive source winding: the inner quadratic has only its reciprocal Y pair; the outer product is U^n=1, and the regular elimination (7) then fixes negative real w. The zero-winding possibility U=1 is not part of the exact source sum, whose windings start at 1. In particular it need not be treated as a selected saddle of that source. The reciprocal-|w| solutions have a majority of conjugate copies and negative action.

The exact mixed Hessian has outer Schur entry n i cos(alpha) and

det⁡Hess⁡ℱ=(det⁡K2)k(det⁡K2¯)s−knicos⁡α≠0. \det\operatorname{Hess}\mathcal F =(\det K_2)^k(\overline{\det K_2})^{s-k} n i\cos\alpha\ne0. \tag{13a}

This retains every one of the 2s independent inner variables. The binomial number of arrangements is finite for a fixed source width; it is not an additional exponential multiplicity as N grows.

5. The real imaginary-action formula

Define Lambda(theta)=-integral_0^theta log|2sin t| dt. For (9), the fully marked phase has

Im⁡ℱ=8s{Λ(π4+α2)+Λ(π4−α2)}. \boxed{\operatorname{Im}\mathcal F =8s\{\Lambda(\tfrac\pi4+\tfrac\alpha2) +\Lambda(\tfrac\pi4-\tfrac\alpha2)\}.} \tag{14}

Here is a derivation that does not assume a known antiprism volume or the survival of this saddle. Set V(alpha)=Im S(X,Y,w)-2alpha eta. Along (9), y=2i beta and t=eta+i pi. The derivatives in section 2 give

d(Im S)/dalpha=2alpha eta',
V'=-2eta=-4arsinh(tan alpha).

The right side of (14), divided by s, has the SAME derivative, since

sin(pi/4+alpha/2)/sin(pi/4-alpha/2)
   =sec alpha+tan alpha,

whose logarithm is arsinh(tan alpha). The common integration constant is zero at alpha->pi/2. To check the latter directly, X,Y tend to -1 and all bounded dilogarithms have vanishing imaginary limits. The only unbounded pair is Li2(w)-Li2(wY). The elementary inversion identity, proved by differentiation and evaluation at -1,

Li2(z)+Li2(1/z)=-pi^2/6-(1/2)Log(-z)^2,

shows that its imaginary part is 2beta eta+o(1). As beta-alpha=O(pi/2-alpha), 2(beta-alpha)eta tends to zero. Hence V tends to zero, as does the Lobachevsky expression. This proves (14).

For s=3,m=1, triplication of Lambda gives the exact value 4v8, where v8=8Lambda(pi/4). The same formula for other s is the formal four-antiprism action with the stated winding alpha. This identification of a REAL formula does not assume that the original augmented link’s invariant has this growth, or identify a filled knot from that action. The original-cycle/boundary and possible multi-winding cancellation questions remain outside this theorem.

For completeness, the mixed branch with effective n in (13a) has the SAME formula with s replaced by n and alpha=pi m/n. This follows since the two inner actions are complex conjugates at negative real w; their imaginary parts subtract. All such mixed outside actions are strictly below the all-plus n=s action at the same m. Indeed V(alpha)>0 and V’(alpha)<0 for 0<alpha<pi/2, so

ddn{nV(πm/n)}=V(α)−αV′(α)>0. \frac d{dn}\{nV(\pi m/n)\}=V(\alpha)-\alpha V'(\alpha)>0. \tag{15}

The all-plus action in turn strictly decreases with the positive winding m. Hence m=1 and n=s give the UNIQUE largest regular imaginary action among these actual positive-winding stationary systems, apart from identical labeling of equal copies. This is an interior critical-value gap, NOT a contour-height or boundary gap. In particular the equal-height diagonal collision boundary in the independent boundary analysis is not ruled out here.

The audit checks the literal finite ratios, exact elimination and Hessian identities, every positive winding at several odd widths, both reciprocal branches, the actual integer inner marks, and the full Lobachevsky action. It does not numerically estimate a selected cycle coefficient.
