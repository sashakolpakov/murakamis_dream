A whole-interval upper rate for turned microscopic endpoint products

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The exact divided-difference product module, including its polynomial channel gauge and forced recovery, gives a regular finite physical recurrence. Its full value/normal transfer has the terminal maximal characteristic rate. The original fixed-row initialization can itself have that rate. Keeping both costs gives a uniform upper bound of twice the terminal integral for every fixed ordinary endpoint product.

This is an upper bound, not a nonzero coefficient or a filled-action comparison. The conclusion for one unit turn is subexponential stability of the whole product. It does not say that its image is another endpoint product, or that arbitrary repeated tree elimination stays in this class.
