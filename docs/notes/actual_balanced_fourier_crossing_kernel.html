A regular balanced full-Psi Fourier kernel for the original crossing source

28 October 2026. At the complete original color, independent finite Fourier frames turn the entire two-Newton/one-Rogers crossing into a regular, explicitly normalized full-Psi Fourier kernel. All three finite source norms vanish on this frame’s Fermat multisection. The resulting operator is EXACTLY the original physical crossing. The proof first bounds every possible alias error to degree +N, then eliminates it with one exact original E-coproduct Ward identity. Consequently all original words, currents, caps and sources are retained.

This is stronger than setting a central scalar to one inside the old co-source. The external frames and internal Fermat sampling are part of one specified finite source map. Every intermediate frame change, original cup/cap, pivot and scalar line remains. The theorem does not prove a nonzero global geometric intersection or scalar volume limit.

1. Original kernel and the finite frames

Use q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, d=N-1, x=-q^(-1), s=x^N, so s^2=1. Retain the original common Cartan scalar kappa. The exact complete positive layer is the original physical finite block of

kappa CT_t K(t),
K=B_N(t/z1,x^(-1)t/z0)
    R_N(z6/z0,x^(-1)z6/t)
    B_N(x^(-1)z7/z6,x^(-2)z7/t).                    (1)

The complete positive Gaussian cell has ALREADY been contracted in (1); this is its exact finite coefficient identity, not a pointwise discard of a continuous Gaussian. The finite polynomials are

B_N(u,v)=sum_(j=0)^d product_(k=0)^(j-1)(u-Q^k v),
R_N(u,v)=sum_(i+j<N) F_(i+j)/(F_i F_j) u^i v^j.

For nonzero r define the normalized finite frame

F(r)_(k,a)=N^(-1/2) (r Q^k)^a,
F(r)^(-1)_(a,k)=N^(-1/2) (r Q^k)^(-a).             (2)

Choose nonzero central values A,B with B!=s A, and fixed roots

r0^N=A, r1^N=B, r6=x r1, r7=x r0,
t0^(-N)=r1^(-N)-r7^(-N).                           (3)

The last quantity is nonzero. For each external pair of input/output Fourier labels evaluate z_i=r_i Q^(label_i), and set t_j=t0 Q^j. Define the TWO-HEAD Fourier matrix

M_(k,l;i,j)=kappa/N^3 sum_(m=0)^d K(t0 Q^m),        (4)

at these four external grid points. Define its decoded physical operator by

Rhat_+=(F(r6)^(-1) tensor F(r7)^(-1))
                 M (F(r0) tensor F(r1)).            (5)

The factor N^(-3) in (4) is exact: N^(-2) is the two-head Fourier kernel normalization and N^(-1) is internal root averaging.

Exact-operator theorem. Rhat_+=R_+, including its original Cartan line. Section 2 first proves that their difference can only have degree +N in the original finite index basis. Section 5 then proves that this difference is zero, using the actual E current.

2. The exact degree/carry proof

Expand the three factors of (1) with indices (i,j),(r,k),(h,l), respectively, each nonnegative pair having sum<N. A term has external input/output exponents

a=j+r, b=i, c=r+k-h, e=h+l,
t-power p=i+j-k-l,
c+e-a-b=-p.                                        (6)

Consequently

a in[0,2d], b,e in[0,d], c in[-d,d], p in[-2d,d].

Internal root averaging keeps precisely p=0 and p=-N. Its latter term is t0(-N)[t(-N)]K. For p=-N, the same inequalities show that a<N and c>=0: a>=N would force output total>=2N, while c<0 would force output total<d. Neither is possible. Thus these terms already have valid original head indices and change degree by N.

External inverse Fourier extraction folds a,c modulo N. On p=0, an input a>=N folds to a-N and adds degree N; an output c<0 folds to c+N and also adds degree N. These two cases cannot coincide: the former has original input total>=N, the latter original output total<d. All remaining p=0 terms are exactly the original physical finite block of (1). This proves the theorem. All root radius factors are retained in the folding; they are nonzero scalars and do not alter the degree calculation.

On b physical heads the ideal of strictly positive changes N,2N,… has b-th power zero. Every original signed crossing preserves degree. Thus a word in these completed blocks differs from its original word only by that source-null ideal. The full original pointed partial trace kills it entrywise, and the pointed plat source kills it by its actual ordinary-cap constraints. The marked row alone is not assumed fixed-degree: cups have total k d, ordinary caps require (k-1)d, and an error would force the last pair to degree d+N>2d. This is the same proved integer-degree source mechanism.

3. ALL three original source norms vanish on this actual grid

Write T=t0^N, and at a grid point put

u=t/z1, v=x^(-1)t/z0,
f=x^(-1)z7/z6, r=z6/z0, w=x^(-1)z6/t.

Their central powers are independent of all the finite labels:

u^N=T/B, v^N=s T/A,
r^N=s B/A, w^N=B/T,
f^N=s A/B, (fw)^N=s A/T.

Equation (3) implies exactly

1-u^N+v^N=0,
1-r^N-w^N=0,
1-f^N+(fw)^N=0.                                    (7)

All factors are regular on the stated torus: r^N and w^N are nonzero and different from one; the same holds for the central arguments of the Newton full-Psi factors, 1/u and v/u (and 1/f and w). The bare v^N itself need not avoid one. Hence each original finite polynomial equals its FULL source-normalized homogeneous term on this multisection:

B_N(u,v)=S_B(u,v), R_N(r,w)=T_R(r,w),
B_N(f,fw)=S_B(f,fw).                               (8)

These are consequences of the independently proved finite source identities and their exact Fermat normalizers, including the opposite-root Rogers current. No current is thrown away before the complete sampling map (1)–(5). The regular restrictions in (3) ensure that the currents in those identities are regular; at a collision their combined finite expression must instead be continued, as usual.

4. Explicit normalized full-Psi Fourier evaluation

Use the exact notations and constants of the complete Fourier source, Sections 5–6 and 11. Let a,b,c,d0 be the retained logarithms of z0,z1,z6,z7, respectively, X=Log x and tau0=Log t0. Put

beta=b-a-X, f_log=d0-c-X, rho=c-a,
z=h+b-tau0, lambda=d0-b+2pi*i.

The original affine 2pi*i mark is retained. Define

C_boundary=(N^2/phi_N) exp[N(f_log-b)](1-exp(Nrho))
   *Psi_+(beta)Psi_+(h-f_log)/Psi_+(rho)
   *exp{[beta*b-(c-X)(f_log+rho)]/h},

H_bal=i sqrt(N) phi_N zeta_0 (1-exp(-Nlambda))
                             exp[(N+z/h)lambda].     (9)

Then EVERY entry of (4) is exactly

M=kappa/N^3 C_boundary
    *(1-exp[N(c-X-tau0)]) exp(lambda*tau0/h)
    *H_bal*F_ref(lambda).                          (10)

F_ref is the independently normalized full-Phi Fourier function

F_ref(lambda)=zeta_0/(i sqrt(N))
   *exp[i sqrt(N)lambda c_b-iNlambda^2/(4pi)]
   *Psi_+(lambda+2pi*i+h).                         (11)

Thus (10) has FOUR variable full-Psi factors and its explicitly known Gaussian and central norm factors. It is an evaluation of the continued Fourier function with its stated logarithmic marks; an unrecorded move to a convergent real contour is not asserted.

For the proof, exact shared-face sewing first gives Psi_-(sigma-h)Psi_+(sigma)=1-exp(Nsigma). After separation the only internal full function is Psi_+(z-hj). Its orbit is

sum_j Psi_+(z-hj)exp(lambda*j)
   =Psi_+(z)B_(Q^(-1))(exp lambda,
                                exp(lambda+z-h)).

Here the second Newton balance is automatic:

exp(Nlambda)(1-exp(Nz))=1.                         (12)

The fully normalized resonant identity of Section 11 therefore has zero closure current and equals H_bal F_ref. Substitution gives (10). Every prefactor and variable full-Psi value in (10) is finite and nonzero on the regular chart. Alternatively the finite balanced Newton Fourier recurrence proves directly that the final orbit sum is nonzero. Hence every entry of this actual FOUR-LEG Fourier kernel is nonzero.

A root-independent four-function kernel and its controlled density

All internal-root dependence in (10) cancels. In the same retained external logarithms set

G=d0-a-X, R_c=exp(Nrho)=s B/A,
E=N f_log+(N+1)lambda/2
      +[G(b-c+X)+lambda^2/2]/h.

Then (10) is the particularly simple exact expression

M=kappa zeta_0^2/N *R_c(1-R_c)^2 exp(E)
     *Psi_+(beta)Psi_+(h-f_log)
        Psi_+(lambda+2pi*i+h)/Psi_+(rho).           (14)

To verify it, the powers in (10)–(11) multiply to kappa zeta_0^2/N. Balance gives 1-exp(Nrho)=1-R_c, 1-exp(Nsigma)=R_c, and 1-exp(-Nlambda)=1-R_c. In the exponent use z+tau0=h+b, i sqrt(N)c_b=-(N+1)/2, and beta+lambda=G+2pii. The term (2pii b)/h=N b cancels the -N b from the boundary prefactor. The remaining exponent is exactly E. No affine mark is removed.

For central A,B in a fixed compact subset of A B(B-s A)!=0, with fixed bounded root logarithms, the entire density outside the FOUR full-Psi functions has sharp polynomial size:

c_K/N <= |kappa zeta_0^2 R_c(1-R_c)^2 exp(E)/N|
                                                   <=C_K/N, (15)

uniformly for EVERY choice of the four finite labels and every N. Indeed each external logarithm has real part O_K(1/N) and imaginary part O_K(1). X is purely imaginary. Products in E/h therefore have bounded real parts, as do N f_log and (N+1)lambda/2. Also |kappa|=|zeta_0|=1 and R_c,1-R_c are uniformly bounded above and below. This proves (15).

Thus the reference kernel has an explicit nonzero original-source local density, with no hidden exponential gauge or periodic normalizer. Its dilogarithmic action is in its four full functions and recorded Gaussian. This is a local normalization theorem; sewing and applying the original global source can still cancel.

A genuine positive octahedral chart of this ACTUAL kernel

The four classical shapes in (14), with their signed orientations, are

u=-z1/z0, v=-z6/z7, w=z7/z1, and -(uvw),

where the last minus denotes its coefficient in the Bloch sum, not multiplication of its shape by -1. Thus the signed regulator is D(u)+D(v)+D(w)-D(uvw). On the unit-angle chart let

0<theta1,theta2,theta3<pi,
pi<theta1+theta2+theta3<2pi,
theta4=2pi-theta1-theta2-theta3.

Then u=exp(i theta1), v=exp(i theta2), w=exp(i theta3), and the negative fourth tetrahedron has positive quad shape 1/(uvw)=exp(i theta4). These are four positive ideal tetrahedra around an octahedral diagonal, with total angle 2pi.

On every compact subset of this chart, the independently proved full-Psi strip estimate and (15) give the actual kernel a nonzero analytic leading density. Recorded whole-turn shifts contribute their exact nonzero central factors; they are bounded on the stated central compacta. Its leading logarithmic modulus obeys the uniform two-sided estimate

log|M|=N V(theta)/(2pi)-log N+O_K(1),                (15a)

with V equal to the volume of those four positive tetrahedra and theta the sampled angles in that compact chamber. The O(1/N) root-modulus and half-step changes alter the logarithm by O_K(1). This is a statement about the literal original finite Fourier kernel, not a newly chosen packet.

The volume is strictly concave on this convex angle chamber. Indeed for L(theta)=D(exp(i theta)), L’’(theta)=-cot(theta/2)/2, and

Hess V=-diag(cot(theta_i/2))/2
         +cot((theta1+theta2+theta3)/2) 11^t/2.

Both terms are negative definite or negative semidefinite here, with the first strictly negative. Its unique maximum is theta1=theta2=theta3=theta4=pi/2, the regular octahedron. The existence of this local chart does not place the complete global geometric critical point on its sewn original source.

5. An exact original E-current eliminates every possible alias

The degree +N allowance in Section 2 can be removed. Retain the original complete scaled head current and coproduct

Ecal|a>=(Q^(-a)-1)|a-1>, Ecal|0>=0,
K|a>=x Q^(-a)|a>,
E_total=Ecal_1 K_2+Ecal_2.                          (16)

The actual R_+ commutes with E_total. In the finite Fourier frame, put (Tf)(z)=f(Qz). Then EXACTLY, for every nonzero frame radius,

Ecal=z^(-1)(T^(-1)-I), K=x T^(-1).

There is no missing wrap: the coefficient of the input constant in the first expression is zero, and all other powers lower without crossing the finite top.

Use z0,z1,z6,z7 for the four grid values, and let M denote (14). Its exact one-step full-Psi identities give the ratios

R1=M(z6/Q,z7/Q)/M
   =-z6/z0 *(1-z7/z1)/(1-z6/(Qz0)),
R2=M(z6,z7/Q)/M
   =q z6/z7 *(1-z7/z1)/(1-Qx z6/z7),
M(z0 Q,z1 Q)/M=R1,
R4=M(z0,z1 Q)/M
   =q z1/z0 *(1-z7/z1)/(1-z1/(xz0)).                (17)

All untouched arguments in each ratio are held fixed. Their denominators are nonzero on the regular central chart. Here q=-1/x and Q=x^(-2). Substituting (17) proves the rational identity

x/z6*(R1-R2)+(R2-1)/z7
   =x/z0*(Q^(-1)R1-R4)+(Q^(-1)R4-1)/z1.             (18)

This is precisely the matrix relation E_total,out M=M E_total,in. The factors Q^(-1) on the right come from transposing the finite input difference: z_j^(-1) at its shifted row becomes Q(-1)z_input(-1). Thus (18) is the original quantum-group current, not merely its commuting symbol. Decoding the four finite frames proves [Rhat_+,E_total]=0.

For completeness, (17) can be checked without an operator claim. Under logarithmic shifts (delta a,delta b,delta c,delta d0) =h(Ai,Bi,Ci,Di), put dl=Di-Bi, dG=Di-Ai, dJ=Bi-Ci. The Gaussian in (14) has ratio

x^(-dl) exp[dG(b-c+X)+dJ(d0-a-X)+dl lambda]
                      *Q^(dG*dJ+dl^2/2).

Apply Psi(z+h)=Psi(z)/(1-exp z) and its inverse to the four full functions. For the four shifts in (17) the displayed ratios result directly. Equation (18) is then an elementary rational identity; cross-multiplication uses only Qx^2=1.

Finally E_total is INJECTIVE on each physical total-degree subspace t>=N. Its inputs have first index a=t-d,…,d, all positive. The first output (t-d-1,d) sees only the leftmost input, with nonzero coefficient (Q^(-(t-d))-1)K_d. Remove it and continue: the bidiagonal matrix forces every remaining input coefficient to vanish.

Let Delta=Rhat_+-R_+. It has degree +N by Section 2 and commutes with E_total. On input degree zero, E_total Delta=0; injectivity at degree N implies Delta=0 there. Induct on input degree j: Delta E_total=0 follows from the preceding degree, hence E_total Delta=0, and injectivity at j+N implies Delta=0. At j>=N-1 the target degree exceeds 2d and Delta already vanishes. This proves Rhat_+=R_+ for EVERY level, including N=2, composite and even levels.

6. Signed words, adapters, source, and scope

The negative block is obtained by the exact complete-color antiunitary mirror R_-=P overline(R_+)P, with its original Cartan factor and all frames/marks transported. Applying the same mirror to (5) gives the EXACT original negative operator. This does not balance the raw negative continuous Gaussian at a fixed value of its integration variable; that shortcut would be false. The literal inverse of (5) is consequently the original negative crossing as well.

Independent frames at consecutive gates must be joined by their EXACT finite change of frame. On one shared head it is

F(r')F(r)^(-1)_(j,k)
   =(1/N)sum_(a=0)^d (r'/r)^a Q^((j-k)a)
   =[1-C]/[N(1-(r'/r)Q^(j-k))], C=(r'/r)^N,        (13)

with its removable finite-sum values. If the central values and their fixed logarithmic marks stay in a compact nonzero set, r=exp(Log A/N), these frames and their inverses have uniformly bounded norms. The same holds for (13). All original cups/caps, pivots and scalar lines are transformed by those same frames. Their polynomial norms at the complete color remain polynomial.

No matching of independent central values is required. In fact forcing direct same-grid closure on a one-component braid drives this local regular chart to t0=infinity; the adapters in (13) are what avoid that artificial restriction. Their off-diagonal tails are generally O(1/N), not exponentially small, and are part of the retained global gluing and co-source.

After inserting these adapters, decoding the whole finite network recovers the EXACT original physical word, by Section 5. This retains arbitrary inserted original currents and coupons when they are transformed by the same finite frames. The global construction and its source are recorded in the regular balanced word theorem.

This theorem resolves the local circle/current normalization by an actual finite polarization. It supplies a nonzero local Fourier kernel with the original closed scalar retained. Global selected geometric survival, source-angle bounds, and dominance still require control of the sewn finite Fourier sum and the retained adapters.
