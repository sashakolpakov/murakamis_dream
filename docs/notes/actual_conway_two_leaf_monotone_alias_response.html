Exact monotone alias response of a two-leaf Conway block

31 October 2026. analytical and original-source statement. The one- and two-alias conjugate gaps of an actual two-leaf seed increase with its complete output slope throughout the proper-port range. The proof retains the original multiplication constraints and the core plateau. It also gives a restriction on where a leafwise failing seed can occur in the selected complete geometry.

The independently proved uniform block certificate supplies positive gaps on the entire interval, including its lower endpoint. That proof does not depend on the monotonicity derived here. Conversely, the present response proof does not use that certificate until its final consequence. There is no circular dependency.

Use the original functions J(t)=∫0tlog⁡(2sin⁡(πv))dv,g(a)=J(2a)−4J(a),g¯(a)=g(max⁡(a,1/4)),0≤a≤1/2,k(a)=−2J(a/2),0≤a≤1,m(x,y,z)=J(x+y)+J(z)−J(x)−J(y)−J(z−x)−J(z−y)−J(x+y−z). \begin{aligned} J(t)&=\int_0^t\log(2\sin(\pi v))\,dv,\\ g(a)&=J(2a)-4J(a),\\ \overline g(a)&=g(\max(a,1/4)),\qquad 0\le a\le1/2,\\ k(a)&=-2J(a/2),\qquad 0\le a\le1,\\ m(x,y,z)&=J(x+y)+J(z)-J(x)-J(y)\\ &\quad-J(z-x)-J(z-y)-J(x+y-z). \end{aligned} The product domain is D={x,y≥0:max⁡(x,y)≤z≤x+y≤1}D=\{x,y\ge0:\max(x,y)\le z\le x+y\le1\}. These are the original core/alias envelopes and seven-factorial multiplication action. They are upper bounds for the actual full Newton source, including its normal directions; they are not replacement coupons or asserted coefficient lower rates.

1. A failing seed must face a turn or the deleted closing product

At a proper A2A_2 seed, let X=cos⁡αX=\cos\alpha, where 0<α<π/30<\alpha<\pi/3. Its selected port is B=X−12X=sin⁡αcot⁡(2α),Y=12X=sin⁡αcsc⁡(2α). B=X-\frac1{2X}=\sin\alpha\cot(2\alpha), \qquad Y=\frac1{2X}=\sin\alpha\csc(2\alpha). Suppose this seed enters a retained macro product without an intervening odd turn. The other proper child has the same XX and angle β>α\beta>\alpha. The proper parent has Yparent<1Y_{\mathrm{parent}}<1, so its product angle satisfies 2α+β<π−α. 2\alpha+\beta<\pi-\alpha. Hence 4α<π4\alpha<\pi, or X>1/2X>1/\sqrt2. Such an A2A_2 already passes the old leaf interval. An even number of intervening turns has the same effect on these exact port variables; this is not an isotopy assertion about the physical tangle.

More generally, an unturned completed top product at X≤1/2X\le1/\sqrt2 has two input angles greater than α\alpha. Its output angle exceeds 2α≥π/22\alpha\ge\pi/2, so its BB is negative. This includes an unturned seed AsA_s, s≥2s\ge2, expanded into products.

A seed failing the algebraic leaf cutoff, X<2/3X<2/3, has B<−112,Y>34. B<-\frac1{12},\qquad Y>\frac34. It cannot directly enter a retained macro product without an odd turn. If the unturned A2A_2 itself is a closed root child, its reflected sibling has positive BB and the same X<2/3X<2/3. That sibling must have an odd top turn after consecutive turns are reduced in the port formulas. These statements follow from the actual two-leaf geometry and its strict proper-port inequalities. Alone, they do not prove an alias entropy gap.

2. Fixed-output alias blocks are strictly interior

Here is the boundary justification. Each fixed-output domain has a strict point: take x=yx=y between b/2b/2 and min⁡(b,1/2)\min(b,1/2). Put p=b−xp=b-x, q=b−yq=b-y, r=x+y−br=x+y-b, w=1−x−yw=1-x-y. 4. The conjugate gap increases on the entire proper-port range

This permits boundary maximizers; it does not assume a strictly interior conjugate maximizer.

In particular, ΔJ(λ)≥ΔJ(−log⁡4). \Delta_J(\lambda)\ge\Delta_J(-\log4). \tag{13}

This is an exact all-proper-port reduction. The independent uniform block certificate proves ΔAC(−log⁡4),ΔAA(−log⁡4)>1/100\Delta_{AC}(-\log4),\Delta_{AA}(-\log4)>1/100; in fact its rational supporting planes prove the entire interval directly.
