One shared face frame for pointed fusion and the standard matrix kernel

Research note, 26 October 2026. The preceding standard matrix comparison has four explicit face factors for a tetrahedron. This note makes them compatible on a shared affine flag chart: there is one frame for each ordered triangular face. In that frame the actual dual pairing and corrected cyclic map are fixed Gaussian matrices times their retained source-normalized scalars. Every ordered tetrahedron is the standard kernel with an explicit clock/shift insertion and a specified scalar.

This is a local gluing result for an already specified pointed network. It does not assert invariance of the bare uncolored tetrahedron under vertex permutations. The earlier counterexample in the oriented-source note still applies. Equivariance on a knot’s developing cover, global literature charge conditions, the actual source/cut comparison and the regulator norm remain to be established.

1. Shared roots and a face frame

Fix N>=2, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, and lambda_0=-q. Thus

lambda_0^2=Q, lambda_0^N=(-1)^(N-1).

On a finite affine flag chart choose distinct nonzero p_i=A_i^N. Orient each edge once and choose B_ij^N=p_i-p_j on that orientation. Define the reverse root by B_ji=q B_ij. Consequently, for an arbitrary ordered edge,

r_ij=B_ji/B_ij is q or q^(-1), r_ji=r_ij^(-1).     (1.1)

The reversal prescription is made once per edge, not afresh on each triangle. It does not prescribe B_ji=q B_ij in both directions.

For f=(i,j,k), the actual relative endpoint module has generators

D_f^N=lambda_f=(p_i-p_k)/(p_j-p_k),
Y_f=(v_f-u_f D_f)sigma^(-1),
u_f=A_j/A_k, v_f=A_i/A_k.

Here sigma(D_f)=Q D_f. Put

beta_f=B_ik/B_jk,
rho_f=(A_j/A_i) beta_f,
a_f=(A_k/A_i) B_ji/B_jk,
nu_f=B_ji/B_jk.                                  (1.2)

Then rho_f^N=t_f=(p_j/p_i)lambda_f and a_f^N=1-t_f. All these quantities and their indicated factorials are nonzero. Define

phi_f(n)=(Q rho_f;Q)_n/a_f^n,
H(beta)_(a,b)=(beta Q^a)^b,
G_f=diag(phi_f^(-1)) H(beta_f).                    (1.3)

The factorial is periodic in n. Let Z e_n=Q^n e_n and S e_n=e_(n+1). Direct evaluation gives

G_f D_f G_f^(-1)=beta_f Z,
G_f Y_f G_f^(-1)=nu_f S.                          (1.4)

Indeed H(beta) evaluates polynomials at beta Q^a. In that basis Y_f is (v_f-u_f beta_f Z)S. The adjacent ratio phi_f(a)/phi_f(a-1)=(1-rho_f Q^a)/a_f cancels this coefficient and leaves v_f a_f=nu_f. Thus the same G_f works regardless of which tetrahedron contains the ordered face.

2. The actual face pairing is a fixed Gaussian times a fixed scalar

Write bar f=(k,j,i). Let M_f be the actual pairing induced from the shared endpoint caps and pointed fusion maps, as in the relative pivotal proof. Its scalar is already fixed by those caps. In the new frames its matrix is

M_f^st=G_f^(-t) M_f G_(bar f)^(-1).                (2.1)

The proved reversal anti-map on the raw relative generators is

a(D)=-Y^(-1)D, a(Y)=QY^(-1).

Substitute (1.4), retaining transpose and generator order. With c_f=-r_ik^(-1), the resulting two equations are

M_f^st S=Q S M_f^st,
M_f^st Z=c_f Z S M_f^st.                         (2.2)

They determine every matrix entry from its (0,0) entry. Namely

M_f^st=mu_f Mcal_f, mu_f=(M_f^st)_(0,0),
Mcal_f(a,b)=Q^b c_f^n Q^(n(n+1)/2),
n=a-b mod N.                                    (2.3)

For a direct proof, the first equation gives M_(a,b)=Q^b F_(a-b). The second gives F_n=c_f Q^n F_(n-1). Its cyclic consistency is c_f^N Q^(N(N+1)/2)=1. This proves (2.3) at every parity, without dividing by two modulo N. The Gaussian is a product of two invertible diagonal matrices and the Fourier matrix (Q^(-ab)). Since the original pairing is invertible, mu_f is nonzero.

If r_ik=q, (2.3) is equivalently

Mcal_f(a,b)=Q^b lambda_0^((a-b)^2).

If r_ik=q^(-1), it is the transpose of this matrix. Thus its entire matrix dependence on the flag parameters has disappeared; mu_f is the actual remaining scalar function. It is not set to one.

3. The corrected cyclic map has the same property

Write r f=(j,k,i). The preceding oriented-source proof constructed the actual cyclic map

U_f=tau_N lambda_f L_f D_f^(-1), tau_N^3=Q,

with exact cyclic and pairing coherence. Define

U_f^st=G_(r f) U_f G_f^(-1).

Its raw generator equations are D_(r f) U_f=-Q U_f D_f^(-1)Y_f and Y_(r f) U_f=Q U_f D_f^(-1). Therefore (1.4) gives

Z U_f^st=crot_f U_f^st Z^(-1)S,
S U_f^st=drot_f U_f^st Z^(-1),
crot_f=-Q r_ik, drot_f=Q r_ik/r_jk.               (3.1)

Solving the entry recurrences yields

U_f^st=xi_f Ucal_f, xi_f=(U_f^st)_(0,0),
Ucal_f(a,b)=drot_f^(-a) crot_f^(-b)
                        Q^(ab+b(b+1)/2).         (3.2)

The second equation of (3.1) first gives U_(a,b)=drot_f(-a)Q(ab) U_(0,b). The first then gives U_(0,b+1)=crot_f(-1)Q(b+1)U_(0,b). These are precisely (3.2). Again Ucal_f is a diagonal–Fourier–diagonal product and xi_f is nonzero. The chosen tau_N is retained in xi_f.

Conjugating the already proved identities gives

U_(r^2 f)^st U_(r f)^st U_f^st=I,
(U_f^st)^t M_(r f)^st (U_(i,k,j)^st)^(-1)=M_f^st.
                                                    (3.3)

Thus the scalar functions in (2.3) and (3.2) obey the exact coherence relations forced by the Gaussian products. They are not independent normalizations assigned at the end of each move.

4. A standard kernel for every ordered tetrahedron

For an ordered tetrahedron (i,j,k,l), name its faces

A=(i,k,l), B=(i,j,k), C=(i,j,l), D=(j,k,l).

Use their shared roots from (1.2). They satisfy rho_A=rho_C rho_D. Put

alpha=rho_C, beta=rho_D, eta=rho_A,
gamma=a_A/a_D, v=a_C/a_A, u=alpha/gamma.

Then uN+vN=1. Define the marked integers e,d modulo N by

Q^e=gamma/rho_B=r_ik/r_jk,
Q^d=-lambda_0 gamma beta v/a_B=q/r_jk.            (4.1)

The first root defect uses the edges ik and jk; it does not use ij. Since every r is q or q^(-1), e is 0,1 or -1 and d is 0 or 1 modulo N. These are explicit orientation data.

The central standard shape has the projective expression

u^N=(p_i-p_l)(p_k-p_j)/((p_j-p_l)(p_k-p_i)).       (4.1a)

With the convention r(infinity,0,1,z)=z used in the crossing fundamental-cycle note, (4.1a) is r(p_i,p_j,p_k,p_l)^(-1). The reciprocal and the corresponding oriented tetrahedron order must be carried into any regulator comparison. They cannot be removed by calling both coordinates the geometric shape. The formula concerns the root center at fixed N; it does not specify the quantum-parameter trajectory of a selected period across N.

Let R_raw be the actual pointed fusion of the raw face modules C and D, with total face A and relative face B. Let P exchange the total and relative input factors. The exact shared-frame formula is

(G_C tensor G_D) R_raw
          (G_A^(-1) tensor G_B^(-1)) P
   =kappa phi_B(e) L0(u,v)(Z^d S^(-e) tensor I),

kappa=(1/N)sum_(b=0)^(N-1)
                 gamma^(-b)(Q eta;Q)_b/(Q beta;Q)_b.
                                                    (4.2)

Here kappa is the nonzero unit-fixed scalar from the preceding local comparison. At odd N, L0 is exactly the published basic matrix kernel without its h(u) prefactor. At every N it is the same existing pointed-fusion expression

L0_(k,l)^(i,j)=Q^(kj)lambda_0^(k^2)
                Omega(u,v|i-k) delta_(i+j,l).

This all-level expression does not extend the literature’s odd-level charge definition by dividing by two at even N.

Here is a direct derivation of (4.2). Normalize each raw endpoint module by X_tilde=(u_f/v_f)D_f and Y_tilde=Y_f/v_f. The polynomial basis change diag((u_f/v_f)^n) sends this normalized module to the raw one. These changes intertwine both the total and relative generators of the fusion, so the preceding four-frame comparison applies with parameters s=rho_CN,t=rho_DN.

The total and two output factors then cancel against the shared face frames. The natural relative evaluation root is gamma rather than rho_B, so its evaluation map differs by S^(-e). Its remaining diagonal entry at k is

D_gamma(k) lambda_0^(-k^2) phi_B(k+e),
D_gamma(k)=(beta v)^k /
                product_(j=1)^k(1-gamma^(-1)Q^(-j)).

Periodicity and finite-product cancellation give

phi_B(k+e)=phi_B(e)(Q gamma;Q)_k/a_B^k,
product_(j=1)^k(1-gamma^(-1)Q^(-j))
     =(-gamma)^(-k)Q^(-k(k+1)/2)(Q gamma;Q)_k.

Consequently that entry is exactly

phi_B(e)(-lambda_0 gamma beta v/a_B)^k
   =phi_B(e)Q^(dk).

This proves the whole clock/shift factor and its scalar, including at even and composite levels. If the tetrahedron order agrees with all its edge orientations, e=d=0 and (4.2) is kappa L0.

5. The scalar line and source evaluation are retained

The scalar functions can be evaluated by the specified (0,0) entries; their determinant equations also give exact norm data:

mu_f^N=det M_f /
              (det G_f det G_(bar f) det Mcal_f),
xi_f^N=(det G_(r f) det U_f) /
              (det G_f det Ucal_f).                 (5.1)

These equations do not authorize new phase choices. The original caps and trivalent tensors determine mu_f and xi_f themselves. For the normalized literature kernel L=h(u)L0, the multiplier in (4.2) is kappa phi_B(e)/h(u), with exactly the same Weyl insertion. Full integer-charge lifts and their non-phase multipliers remain as in the preceding comparison note.

For any already specified finite contraction, (1.3), (2.1) and (3.2) are honest changes of bases. All interior inverse frames cancel. A source column p changes to G_f p; its cut row changes to ell G_f^(-1). A raw unit is therefore the generally nonconstant column (phi_f(n)^(-1))_n, not a new unit in the standard coordinates. At a source wall, the lattice and its normal quotient must move with the frame, using the retained carry/linking constructions.

These observations prove preservation of a given contraction. They do not construct that contraction on an arbitrary positive geometric triangulation. Projective/deck equivariance, the global charge constraints, its actual source extension and selected cut must still be matched. In particular the fixed Gaussian matrices above have not been declared equal to a published choice with its scalar normalizations omitted.

The audited ideal triangulation theorem supplies the desired geometric developing flags and marked fundamental-class period. This note supplies shared face algebra for a proposed quantum comparison. Equality of the actual and triangulation source classes, integral pointings and regulator norms does not follow from either existence statement alone.

The subsequent source-flag proof makes the origin an extra marked point. Its finite-source transports are fixed by actual edge duality and preserve fusion, caps and cyclic maps exactly. It also expresses the normal chart and its non-scalar first coefficient as jets of that same source family. This supplies more of the local source comparison; the genuine degenerate edge lattice and selected global norm remain necessary.

6. Independent finite-field audit

The audit constructs four distinct affine flags and roots of all six edge differences in exact finite fields. For each N=2,3,4,5,6,9, it checks the 24 ordered face pairings and cyclic maps against (2.3),(3.2), retaining their actual scalars, and all 24 ordered tetrahedron identities (4.2), including the two root-defect formulas (4.1). Thus 144 pairings, 144 cyclic maps and 144 full ordered kernels passed. Results. The uniform proofs above are separate from these finite tests.
