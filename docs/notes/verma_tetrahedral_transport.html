Pointed tetrahedral transport of the Verma root period

Research note, 26 October 2026. This factors the actual untruncated Verma crossing into four compact quantum dilogarithms, derives a scalar-one algebraic tetrahedral pentagon, and proves that finite formal pentagon rewrites preserve its selected root period after compression to the Verma lattice. It retains the boundary frame, its entire norm character and its poles. Individual compact products are not evaluated at roots of unity.

The resulting root period is still the one independently identified in the Frobenius Verma note:

V_N(M)=F_N(M)/Delta_K(M^(2N)).

This supplies local operator moves for the proposed triangulation comparison. It does not establish that an arbitrary positive geometric triangulation carries the required colored network and pointing, or identify its selected quantum period with the Bloch regulator. The volume conjecture remains unproved.

1. A pointed compact pentagon

For generic Q put

P_Q(t)=(t;Q)_infinity
  =sum_(n>=0)(-1)^n Q^(n(n-1)/2)t^n/(Q;Q)_n.

Use it as a formal series. Its constant term is one and P_Q(Qt)=P_Q(t)/(1-t). If AB=QBA, Gaussian expansion gives

P_Q(A+B)=P_Q(A)P_Q(B).                         (1.1)

For XY=QYX, conjugation by P_Q(Y) sends X to X(1-Q^(-1)Y)=X-YX. The two summands X and -YX satisfy the commutation required in (1.1). Consequently

P_Q(Y)P_Q(X)=P_Q(X)P_Q(-YX)P_Q(Y).            (1.2)

This proves the pentagon directly from the series and its shift equation. It is an equality with scalar one, fixed by the constant term. The ordered middle argument -YX, including its Q phase in an X-before-Y monomial basis, is part of the identity.

To make completions unambiguous, attach formal variables u,v to X,Y. The middle argument has tag uv. Every coefficient is finite in the (u,v)-adic completion of the quantum Laurent algebra. Inverses of P are defined in the same completion. No analytic branch or root-level value of an individual infinite product is assumed.

2. A tetrahedral operator, including its shear and phase

On Laurent monomials in z_1,…,z_b let Z_i multiply by z_i and S_i f(z)=f(…,Qz_i,…). Put x_i=Z_i, y_i=S_i^(-1), so x_i y_i=Q y_i x_i. Define a monomial shear

(C_ij f)(z)=f(...,z_i z_j,...,z_j,...),
a_ij=-Q x_i x_j^(-1)y_j,
T_ij(t)=C_ij P_Q(t a_ij)^(-1).                 (2.1)

The shear conjugates x_i to x_i x_j and y_j to y_i^(-1)y_j. Its exact monomial action gives C_12 C_13 C_23=C_23 C_12. Move the dilogarithms past these shears in the two products below. On the left their arguments become A,-AC,C; on the right they become C,A, where

A=u a_12, C=v a_23, CA=QAC.

Indeed the transformed a_13 has coefficient -Q uv, whereas AC has coefficient Q uv. Inverting (1.2) therefore proves

T_12(u)T_13(uv)T_23(v)=T_23(v)T_12(u).        (2.2)

This is the algebraic three-to-two tetrahedral operator identity with its exact shear and scalar. It is not a claim that the uncolored geometric triangulation theorem supplies the extra tensor data needed to apply it globally.

For a direct coefficient check, T_ij(t) sends a Laurent monomial of exponent vector n to vectors with

n'_i=n_i+m, n'_j=n_j+n_i,
coefficient=t^m(-1)^m Q^(m(m+1)/2-m n_j)/(Q;Q)_m,

summed over m>=0. Tagged coefficients are finite. Omitting the Q in a_ij already violates (2.2); it is not a removable scalar normalization.

3. Four factors for the actual Verma crossing

Use the same infinite Verma basis v_a=z^a, x=q^alpha, Q=q^2, rho=q-q^(-1). On one factor put S v_a=Q^a v_a and Z v_a=v_(a+1). Then

K=xS^(-1), F=Z,
rho^2 E=qx Z^(-1)(1-S^(-1))(1-Q^(-1)x^(-2)S).

The right side kills v_0 and preserves the polynomial Verma module. Let G=P_Q(x^(-2)S), initially as a tagged formal diagonal operator. The functional equation gives

rho^2 E=qx G[Z^(-1)(1-S^(-1))]G^(-1).         (3.1)

For a pair set A=Z_1^(-1)Z_2, S=S_1 and

W=x^(-2)S, U=Qx A, V=-Qx A S^(-1).

Here AS=QSA and VU=QUV. The formal universal uncheck R, with its common Cartan scalar removed, is D times its nilpotent series, where

D(v_a tensor v_b)=x^(-a-b)Q^(ab)v_a tensor v_b.

Using the independently derived R coefficients and (3.1) gives

R^0=D P_Q(W)P_Q(V)P_Q(U)P_Q(W)^(-1).          (3.2)

Indeed the middle product is P_Q(U+V), with U+V=Qx A(1-S^(-1)); its action terminates on each polynomial input before the diagonal conjugation. Equation (3.2) is therefore the actual Verma operator, not a replacement cyclic crossing.

Two applications of (1.2) remove the diagonal G:

P_Q(W)P_Q(V)=P_Q(V)P_Q(-WV)P_Q(W),
P_Q(W)P_Q(U)P_Q(W)^(-1)=P_Q(U)P_Q(-WU).

Since -WV=x^(-1)A and -WU=-x^(-1)AS, the four-monomial formula is

R^0=D P_Q(-Qx AS^(-1))P_Q(x^(-1)A)
                P_Q(Qx A)P_Q(-x^(-1)AS).      (3.3)

The checked crossing is the usual flip times (3.3). Its inverse is the inverse word, with the same external conventions. All Cartan, flip and zero-framing factors remain in the period.

4. Compression fixes the boundary lattice

The individual factors in (3.3) can send a polynomial input to infinitely many negative powers of z_1. The complete product has an elementary cancellation. Introduce a color tag h and a transfer tag d in (3.2): replace W by hW and U,V by dU,dV. In (3.3) this tags the second and fourth arguments by hd, and the first and third by d.

After grouping the whole product, the coefficient sending (a,b) to (a-n,b+n), before the checked flip, is exactly

d^n x^(-a-b+n)Q^((a-n)b){a choose n}_Q
                   (h x^(-2)Q^(a-n);Q)_n, 0<=n<=a,       (4.1)

and is zero for n>a. Here the Gaussian coefficient is a polynomial, not a divided-factorial value at a root.

For a uniform proof, first expand P_Q(d(U+V)). Its n-th power acts by the factor product_(j=0)(n-1)(1-Q(-a+j)), which vanishes for n>a. Combining this factor with the Euler coefficient gives d^n x^n Q^(-n(a-n)){a choose n}_Q. The diagonal ratio from G is (h x(-2)Q(a-n);Q)_n. Applying D at the output gives (4.1). This is also the direct untruncated crossing coefficient of the preceding note at h=d=1.

Every fixed homogeneous total-index sector of the combined operator is thus a finite matrix of Laurent polynomials in q,x and polynomials in h,d. The inverse has the same property, by the terminating inverse R series. Its determinant is the invertible Cartan monomial times that of a unipotent triangular matrix. The combined operator preserves the original polynomial Verma lattice. Neither this fact nor its root specialization is obtained by specializing its four compact factors separately.

5. A precise pointed transport theorem

Start with the Verma braid operator and a fixed cut, pivot and grading insertion as in the graded trace construction. Expand its crossings and inverses by (3.2) or (3.3), with independent auxiliary tags for the occurrences. Permit a finite sequence of the formal pentagon identities, their inverse identities, exact monomial/shear identities and cancellations of adjacent inverse factors, with their argument tags and external leg identifications transported throughout. These are equalities of the external operator, before taking its trace.

Then compress the entire rewritten operator to the original Verma lattice, set its tags to one, and specialize q=q_N. The resulting graded cut period and its rational continuation at tau=1 are exactly the original ones:

rewritten V_beta(q_N,x;tau)=V_beta(q_N,x;tau),
rewritten V_N(M)=F_N(M)/Delta_K(M^(2N)).       (5.1)

Here is the proof, including the otherwise missing specialization step. All factors have positive auxiliary tag degree, so every rewrite is defined in a common formal completion of the Laurent module. The initial complete operator has the polynomial matrix entries of Section 4. Formal operator equality forces the rewritten complete entries to equal those polynomials, including the vanishing of every forbidden negative-index transfer. Thus setting the tags to one and specializing q use a known regular polynomial entry. No root limit of an individual factor is used.

Each coefficient of the cut grading tau sums only finitely many input and intermediate indices after this compression. The coefficientwise period identity follows. At a root the initial period has the rational formula proved in the preceding note. Two rational functions with the same series at tau=0 are equal; hence their entire rational continuations, poles and regular whole specializations agree. This proves (5.1), including the Kashaev fiber and its marked normal coordinate. Multiplying by the full d_N(M) retains the full ADO normalization as before.

This theorem transports the actual selected period under the specified formal operator moves. It does not assert that every Pachner or face-permutation operation on arbitrary ideal triangulations has already been assigned such an operator identity. For such an application the coloring, polarization, turns and boundary identifications must be constructed and checked.

6. The relative norm torsor at the Kashaev boundary

Normalize the diagonal operator in (3.2) on v_0. Its finite ratio is

g_a=P_Q(x^(-2)Q^a)/P_Q(x^(-2))
              =1/(x^(-2);Q)_a, g_0=1.

The finite expression specifies its meromorphic root frame even though the two infinite products individually have no such value. For q=q_N and U=M(2N)=x(2N),

g_(a+1)/g_a=(1-x^(-2)Q^a)^(-1),
g_(NA+r)=(1-U^(-1))^(-A)g_r, 0<=r<N.          (6.1)

This is an explicit rank-one cyclic norm character

Norm((1-x^(-2)Q^a)^(-1))=(1-U^(-1))^(-1).

It is invertible away from its divisor; at U=1 its meromorphic filtered lattice, with pole order A on the A-th polynomial block, must be retained. Every finite total-index filtration has finite pole order. At the actual Kashaev color x=q_N^(N-1), x^(-2)=Q, so the first N frame entries are

g_r=1/(Q;Q)_r, 0<=r<N.

They are exactly the factorial frame of the already fixed actual cut boundary. The next entry has a pole. The combined crossing is regular nevertheless by (4.1), and the complete rational trace is regular there because Delta_K(1)=1. Evaluating this diagonal frame separately at its pole would lose the prescribed boundary data.

The middle arguments in pentagons also retain nontrivial central norms. If XY=QYX and Q has order N, direct ordered multiplication gives

(-YX)^N=-X^N Y^N                              (6.2)

at every parity. Thus its binomial norm factor is 1+X^N Y^N, which can specialize to a numerical multiplier. In the tetrahedral operator (2.1), a_ijN=-Z_iN Z_j(-N)S_j(-N), with the same sign. Such factors must be carried as functions before a whole boundary specialization. The strict formal identities above do not justify discarding them or repair a different numerical state model by fiat.

The subsequent factorial source-torsor calculation identifies this frame directly with the literature cyclic primitive. For h=Q(-1)x(-2), write p_a(h)=(Qh;Q)_a=nu^a phi_a(h), nuN=1-U(-1). Then the exact Verma frame is

g_a=1/p_a(h)=nu^(-a) phi_a(h)^(-1).

Its block pole character in (6.1) is the N-step transport of this same singular cyclic frame. The new two-direction affine extension and cut-root adapters retain its source channel and the full endpoint factors. They do not supply the remaining global colored/polarized network or its selected regulator norm.

The subsequent four cyclic-atom proof now constructs a finite root counterpart for the genuine semicyclic crossing: each atom is the literature alternate cyclic dilogarithm, acting between explicitly transported centers. Two independent Fermat norm roots and their inverses restore the external clocks. Their determinant normalization, an external root and the actual source anchor fix the whole crossing scalar. This is not a termwise evaluation of (3.3); the finite cyclic atoms include the required center transport and boundary frame. Recombination has exactly the original regular crossing and full cut period.

The subsequent root tetrahedral pentagon constructs the corresponding finite cyclic move with monomial shears and changing centers. Compatible roots give the same final clock/shift frame on both paths; a determinant and formal zero-tag anchor fix the scalar to one on the stated lifted component. This supplies an exact root move for specified exterior contractions, rather than a separate specialization of the compact infinite factors. The global geometric tensor presentation and selected regulator norm are still missing.

7. Role in the regulator comparison

There are now actual four-factor crossing data, an algebraic tetrahedral pentagon and a pointed root-period transport theorem for finite formal operator rewrites. The cyclic norm character and its relative lattice are explicit. These are local algebraic inputs for a triangulation presentation of the source period.

The positive geometric triangulation supplies the marked classical Bloch fundamental class, as already audited. To apply Section 5 to it requires a compatible colored/polarized tensor presentation and a move transport of the cut and its relative gluing data. Existence of positive tetrahedra alone does not supply that presentation or a global branching. Nor does the operator move identity show that a chosen hyperbolic character contribution is the selected quantum period or has its regulator norm. The original distinction between root-central and semiclassical shape coordinates remains in force.

In particular (5.1) preserves the actual state and its source; it does not equate its abelian interior root character with the different fixed hyperbolic character separated in the threading note. The needed comparison remains a relative marked-period theorem. Its norm statement and the volume conjecture are not proved here.

The subsequent pointed tetrahedron gluing construction does resolve local order coherence and nonvanishing at the root module level. Integral edge primitivity gives a nonzero saturated rank-N boundary block for S^3 knots at all N. It also specifies the projected tetrahedron generator, without proving that vector nonzero or identifying it with the selected relative Verma trace. The full tensor-face presentation and marked regulator norm are still additional comparisons.

8. Coverage and exact diagnostics

Kashaev, q-alg/9503005 already covers a canonical Heisenberg-double pentagon and reconstruction of Yang–Baxter solutions. The abstract of Suzuki, 1612.08262 covers a Heisenberg-double construction on colored ideal triangulations and colored moves. These are coverage of the framework, not proof premises or an unrestricted geometric-triangulation comparison. The earlier consulted four-dilogarithm holonomy-R coverage also prevents a novelty claim for the general factorization idea. The formulas and polynomial-specialization proof above are reconstructed directly in the fixed actual Verma conventions.

The audit checks the compact pentagon symbolically, the tagged tetrahedral operator on Laurent monomials, forbidden-transfer cancellations in the four-factor product, root-specialized compressed entries and their actual ADO restrictions, the full meromorphic frame, and the period through the grading using independent finite ADO and classical Burau data. See the results. These diagnostics verify the stated constructions and not the remaining global geometric or regulator-norm theorem.

The saved checks include 28 symbolic compact-pentagon coefficients, 81 Laurent inputs and 1,215 coefficients for the tagged tetrahedral operator, 49 symbolic four-factor compressions with 21 forbidden transfers canceled, 3,360 compressed root entries, nine full pole lattice levels through 12, and 210 graded coefficients in 30 transported trace cases. Even and composite levels and the exact Kashaev boundary are included.
