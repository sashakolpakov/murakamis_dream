Quantitative inward propagation for the actual pointed-A boundary response

28 October 2026. A uniformly off-characteristic color interval has a controlled whole-line Green extension and an explicit finite-interval observability estimate. Weighted energy entering from its retained boundary packets decays exponentially inward. The constants remain polynomial when the spectral gap shrinks slower than N^-1/2.

This upgrades bulk Agmon coercivity to a resolvent and a maximum estimate for the actual fixed-band source system. It accounts for nonnormality without diagonalizing transfers. Both boundary packets remain in the general case. It is not a claim that an arbitrary square finite compression has a tame inverse, or that the required right-hand source packet is already bounded by volume.

1. Broad operator class and actual inputs

Let the block size r and shift radius d>=1 be fixed. On a lattice interval J=[a,b] of length O(N), consider the ACTUAL weighted recurrence

(L_N u)_j=sum_(i=-d)^d C_(i,N)(j) u_(j+i).         (1)

The forward-only case has zero coefficients for negative i. Assume uniform boundedness of the coefficients, and that they are within C/N of matrices A_(i,N)(j/N) having a uniform Lipschitz bound on J/N. The functions A may depend on N. Suppose

sigma_min(sum_i A_(i,N)(x) exp(i i theta))>=eta_N>0
                          for x in J/N, theta real. (2)

In exp(i i theta), the first i is sqrt(-1), the second the integer shift index. The notation is solely the Fourier symbol of (1). Require eta_N>=4C_0 N^-1/2, where C_0 depends on those uniform bounds and on fixed r,d. Eventually strict inequality suffices.

These are independently checkable coefficient hypotheses. For the actual pointed jet matrix, after its proved jet scaling and color weight exp(N phi_N(x)), its symbol is

A_B(x,exp(-phi_N'(x))exp(i theta)).

The hypotheses above follow from the actual finite monomial formula whenever phi_N’ and phi_N’’ are uniformly bounded and this radius has gap eta_N. No unknown period is used. All original jet components and source rows remain. A shrinking radius tilt with a fixed smooth profile is allowed; an unbounded derivative of that profile is not.

2. A controlled whole-line extension and inverse

Extend each coefficient sequence constantly beyond the two ends of J. Equivalently clamp the symbol path at its endpoint values; the actual coefficients keep their O(1/N) error there. This defines an auxiliary operator L_ext on ell2(Z,C^r), agreeing with the actual recurrence on J. The extension is part of the PROOF; it replaces neither the original outside word nor its source.

The sqrt(N)-window Plancherel argument gives

||L_ext v|| >=(eta_N-C_0 N^-1/2)||v||
                                >=eta_N||v||/2. (3)

The same proof applies to L_ext^dagger. Its coefficients are shifted adjoints of those in (1), hence have the same slow variation, and its frozen symbol is the adjoint of (2), with the same singular-value gap. Thus its kernel is zero too. The range of L_ext is closed by (3) and dense by the zero adjoint kernel. Therefore it is onto and

G_N=L_ext^-1 exists, ||G_N||<=2/eta_N.             (4)

This is a two-sided, source-independent resolvent theorem, with no assumption of normal matrices or distinct eigenvalues. The constants do not grow with interval length. The proof uses full output rows; it does not discard the leakage rows at an interval endpoint.

3. Exponential Green decay, proved by bounded conjugations

Put K_1=sum_i |i|sup_j||C_(i,N)(j)|| and K_1^+=max(1,K_1). Choose

mu_N=min(1/d, eta_N/(8K_1^+)).                    (5)

For a fixed source color k, multiply by the BOUNDED weights

W_R(j)=exp(mu_N min(|j-k|,R)).

Their neighboring ratios differ from one by at most exp(mu_N|i|)-1<=2mu_N|i|. Consequently

||W_R L_ext W_R^-1-L_ext||<=2mu_N K_1<=eta_N/4.

Neumann inversion using (4) bounds the conjugated inverse by 4/eta_N, uniformly in R. Applying it to a block point source at k and then letting R increase proves the explicit kernel bound

||(G_N)_(j,k)||<= (4/eta_N)exp(-mu_N|j-k|).        (6)

No unbounded conjugation or imported decay theorem is used. Block sources and multiplicities are included in the operator norm.

4. Exact finite-interval boundary observability

Suppose u is the ORIGINAL response on J and satisfies (1)=f on all rows whose stencil is inside J. Let J_in=[a+d,b-d]; retaining this slightly smaller row set works for both shift orientations. Zero extend u from J and call it u_J. Then exactly

L_ext u_J=1_(J_in)f+B_left u_left+B_right u_right. (7)

The two u-side packets contain the first and last 2d physical color blocks of J. The two B operators are simply the corresponding boundary rows of L_ext; they are explicit, have fixed support and uniformly bounded norm. Their row supports lie within distance d of the respective endpoint. If J is shorter than 4d the analogous finite-dimensional estimate uses all its blocks as boundary data.

Uniqueness of (4) gives an EXACT representation by applying G_N to (7). In particular

||u_J||_2 <=C eta_N^-1
               (||f_(J_in)||_2+||u_left||_2+||u_right||_2). (8)

More precisely, for any j in J, (6) gives

||u_j|| <=C eta_N^-1 [
   sum_(k in J_in) exp(-mu_N|j-k|)||f_k||
   +exp(-mu_N dist(j,left))||u_left||_2
   +exp(-mu_N dist(j,right))||u_right||_2 ].       (9)

The fixed-width ambiguity in dist is absorbed into C. Summing the geometric series proves the uniform maximum/observability estimate

sup_J||u_j|| <=C eta_N^-1(1+mu_N^-1)
                     (sup_(J_in)||f_j||+||u_left||+||u_right||). (10)

Thus a regular homogeneous bulk transmits only the retained boundary packets, with exponential distance suppression. On a compact bulk interval of the actual pointed jet system, its initial identity rows lie outside J, so f is zero in (9); this is NOT a replacement by an arbitrary homogeneous solution. Those initial rows remain original boundary data. More general actual affine sources may lie inside a regular interval; their full row and column weighting remains in f. An initial identity row is not silently assigned the bulk symbol (2).

For eta_N=N^-gamma with 0<=gamma<1/2, the inverse and maximum constants are polynomial and the decay length is O(N^gamma). Thus the loss is exp(o(N)), even for a shrinking off-characteristic tilt. This also applies to the full normal-jet blocks, not only their constant-color projection. The latter can be selected afterwards.

5. Which boundary data are genuinely necessary

The general estimate needs BOTH sides. Spectral gap alone does not make a recurrence stable in the forward direction. The elementary scalar recurrence u_(j+1)=2u_j has a fixed unit-circle gap, yet left data u_0=1 grow to 2^j. Its reciprocal recurrence demonstrates the opposite direction. These examples concern the broad operator class, not a proposed replacement knot state.

Nor does (4) prove a tame inverse for any chosen square compression. For example I-2T has min_(|L|=1)|1-2L|=1, but the n-by-n upper bidiagonal compression has inverse sum_(k=0)(n-1)2k T^k, of norm at least 2^(n-1). It has dropped an output leakage row and imposed the wrong boundary condition. The full-output estimate and (7) retain precisely the boundary data which prevent that inference.

There is a genuine one-sided improvement under an EXTRA checkable stability condition. Suppose the monic actual companion equals T(j/N) up to O(1/N), with T uniformly bounded and Lipschitz, and has uniformly rho(T(x))<=1-delta on the interval. Then its positive Lyapunov metric sum_(k>=0)(Tdagger)k Tk/(1-delta/2)(2k) is uniformly bounded and Lipschitz. Its neighboring metric changes are 1+O(1/N), so for large N the actual transfer contracts by 1-c_delta. Variation of constants gives a uniform one-sided bound from the full LEFT initial packet and its actual sources. If the inverse companions have that property, the analogous theorem uses the RIGHT packet. Mixed stable/unstable spectra do not satisfy either premise.

For an actual geometric radius between spectral branches, mixed spectra are the usual case. The missing incoming packet is then genuine source data, not an error that the local gap controls. The pointed initial rows, cyclic root matching and normal resonance must determine it with a quantified bound. This is exactly the boundary-response problem retained by the positive Schur reduction.

For the ACTUAL bounded-jet problem the initial fixed-color packet is known polynomially, including all its retained jets. The far packet is different: the ordinary-color theorem bounds only CONSTANT values C_j(q_N), j<N, not their q-normal jets. Those jets, and the complete coordinate itself, may carry exponential size. Replacing the full right-hand packet in (8)–(10) by the ordinary-color bound would therefore drop source data. Bounding it by an unknown multiple of the target period would not close the estimate either. An actual cyclic/Weyl or source-Schur certificate is needed there.

6. Consequence for a sharp volume estimate

The theorem gives controlled inward propagation on every certified regular interval. It accounts for repeated roots away from the chosen circle, nonnormal jet blocks and shrinking gaps. A regulator primitive is a legitimate weight there only on a genuinely identified peripheral branch. It must also retain its SOURCE anchor: normalizing the small-color source changes the recovery factor to R(x)-R(0), which may be as large as twice the knot volume.

It follows that a sharp Vol upper bound needs a quantified connection of the actual endpoint/turning packets, with that source anchoring. Equations (7)–(10) remove uncontrolled regular-interval amplification from this question; they do not supply those remaining boundary values or assume them equal to the desired invariant.

Audit: actual full-source figure-eight intervals with full leakage rows, explicit nonnormal Green kernels and the finite-compression caution. The all-operator proof is (2)–(10); the finite checks do not assert a new sharp knot class.
