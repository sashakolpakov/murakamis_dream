The exact even CROSS endpoint is not real stable, and the remaining parity adapter

31 October 2026. Research result: exact-source reduction and obstruction. The local nonstability and stable-reference comparison are proved in the stated scope; the actual global even negative-axis adapter remains open. The finite counterexample uses the ORIGINAL even CROSS projection at sigma=-2. It is not a nearby complete-circle model. The obstruction is local; no failure of stability or of an absolute bound for a globally extracted original exterior is inferred from it.

1. Literal even source and an exact N=6 counterexample

Use the central factorization in actual cofactor factorization, sections 1–2. For N=2m, theta=pi/N, put

aj=2+2cos⁡((2j+1)θ),sj=2sin⁡(jθ),A=x+y,R0(b)=1,Rj(b)=bj∏0≤r<jar+sj2ARj−1(b)+sjsj−1aj−1xyRj−2(b). \begin{aligned} a_j&=2+2\cos((2j+1)\theta),\qquad s_j=2\sin(j\theta),\qquad A=x+y,\\ R_0(b)&=1,\\ R_j(b)&=b^j\prod_{0\le r<j}a_r+s_j^2 A R_{j-1}(b) +s_js_{j-1}a_{j-1}xyR_{j-2}(b). \end{aligned}\tag{1}

with absent negative-index terms zero. Set

Db=Rm−1(b),Eb=am−1Rm−2(b). D_b=R_{m-1}(b),\qquad E_b=a_{m-1}R_{m-2}(b).

The ACTUAL polynomial, with its fixed common raw sign removed, is

Pcross=sm2ADbDc+smsm−1xy(DbEc+EbDc). P_{\mathrm{cross}}=s_m^2 A D_bD_c +s_ms_{m-1}xy(D_bE_c+E_bD_c).\tag{2}

All three central path types remain. No unused factor, spectral derivative, or CROSS-support condition has been replaced.

Take N=6, m=3 and x=y=1. Write r=sqrt3. Then

a0=2+r,a1=2,a2=2−r,s1=1,s2=r,s3=2,L(z)=R1(z)=(2+r)z+2,D(z)=R2(z)=2(2+r)z2+6(2+r)z+12+2r. \begin{aligned} a_0&=2+r,\quad a_1=2,\quad a_2=2-r,\\ s_1&=1,\quad s_2=r,\quad s_3=2,\\ L(z)&=R_1(z)=(2+r)z+2,\\ D(z)&=R_2(z)=2(2+r)z^2+6(2+r)z+12+2r. \end{aligned}\tag{3}

Thus (2), exactly, is

Pcross(1,b,c,1)=4[2D(b)D(c)+k(D(b)L(c)+L(b)D(c))],k=2r−32>0. \begin{aligned} P_{\mathrm{cross}}(1,b,c,1) &=4\bigl[2D(b)D(c)+k(D(b)L(c)+L(b)D(c))\bigr],\\ k&=\frac{2r-3}{2}>0. \end{aligned}\tag{4}

Specialize the remaining real variable c=-2. Since D(-2)=4-2r and L(-2)=-2-2r, the original source becomes

Pcross(1,b,−2,1)=(8−8r)b2+(12−16r)b+72−48r. P_{\mathrm{cross}}(1,b,-2,1) =(8-8r)b^2+(12-16r)b+72-48r.\tag{5}

Its discriminant is

Δ=−6000+34563<0. \Delta=-6000+3456\sqrt3<0.\tag{6}

For a fully rational sign certificate, sqrt3<125/72 because 3*722=15552<15625=1252; multiplying gives (6).

A real-stable polynomial remains stable or zero after real specializations. The nonzero quadratic (5) has one upper-half-plane zero, contradicting that condition. Equivalently, perturb x,y,c to 1+i epsilon,1+i epsilon,-2+i epsilon. The simple nonreal root b of (5) continues to an upper-half-plane root for all sufficiently small positive epsilon, providing a zero with ALL four imaginary parts positive. Therefore:

The original even CROSS endpoint polynomial is not real stablein its four face variables, already at N=6. \begin{gathered} \text{The original even CROSS endpoint polynomial is not real stable}\\ \text{in its four face variables, already at }N=6. \end{gathered}\tag{7}

This is an exact source counterexample, not a numerical root plot. The symbolic expansion of (1)–(4) independently reproduces (5)–(6).

2. Why the simplest auxiliary-cofactor pencil cannot repair this

The candidate joint pencil R_(m-1)(b)+t R_(m-2)(b) also fails at the same original level and the same positive x=y=1. Indeed

D(b)−4L(b)=2(2+3)(b2+b+1). D(b)-4L(b)=2(2+\sqrt3)(b^2+b+1).\tag{8}

A real-stable polynomial in b,t could not have this nonstable specialization t=-4. Multiplying the t coefficient by the original positive a_(m-1) only rescales the real counterexample t and does not repair it. Thus a Hermitian PSD pencil realizing this exact auxiliary cofactor pair is impossible. This does not exclude a different source-level decomposition or a globally extracted stability theorem.

For comparison, the operator (x+y)+kxy partial_t DOES preserve real stability when applied to a polynomial stable jointly in all its indicated variables: after division by xyP, both 1/x+1/y and partial_t log P have nonpositive imaginary part, with the first strictly negative. The failure here is the required actual input pencil, not that elementary operator fact. No substitute complete-node pencil is inserted.

Even the proposal to treat the three central summands separately as stable products fails: an individual cofactor appearing in them need not be stable. At N=8, M=3, set x=y=1 and put

r=2,u=2+2,v=2−2,uv=r. r=\sqrt2,\qquad u=\sqrt{2+\sqrt2},\qquad v=\sqrt{2-\sqrt2},\qquad uv=r.

The literal recurrence (1) gives

R2(b)=(2+u)(2+v)b2+4(2+u)b+v2(8+2u+r). R_2(b)=(2+u)(2+v)b^2+4(2+u)b+v^2(8+2u+r).\tag{8a}

since s_1=v, s_2=r, a_0=2+u and a_1=2+v. Its discriminant is

Δ2=4(2+u)[4(2+u)−(2+v)v2(8+2u+r)]<0. \Delta_2=4(2+u)\bigl[4(2+u)-(2+v)v^2(8+2u+r)\bigr]<0.\tag{8b}

The sign requires no root computation: u<2, u>3/2, v>3/4 and r>1 give the bracket strictly less than 16-(11/4)(9/16)12=16-297/16<0. The bound v>3/4 follows from sqrt2<23/16, whose square is a rational inequality. Thus the ACTUAL E_b=a_3 R_2(b) in (2) is not stable. A constant-size stable decomposition does not follow by viewing the three displayed central terms as its components. This does not exclude a different decomposition of the original polynomial.

3. The exact stable even comparison polynomial survives globally

There IS a genuine positive stable comparison, but its logical scope must be kept exact. The original even half-block theorem, section 5, defines the actual nearest complete-channel lower cofactors

Db*=RM(b;u*),M=m−1,u*=−2+2cos⁡(π/N). D_b^*=R_M(b;u_*),\qquad M=m-1,\qquad u_*=-2+2\cos(\pi/N).

and proves the coefficientwise inequalities

Bcross=4(x+y)Db*Dc*≤coefPcross(−2)<coef3Bcross,Db*=CfrontDM(x,b,y),Cfront=N2sin⁡(π/N). \begin{aligned} B_{\mathrm{cross}}&=4(x+y)D_b^*D_c^* \le_{\mathrm{coef}} P_{\mathrm{cross}}(-2)<_{\mathrm{coef}}3B_{\mathrm{cross}},\\ D_b^*&=C_{\mathrm{front}}D_M(x,b,y),\qquad C_{\mathrm{front}}=\frac{N}{2\sin(\pi/N)}. \end{aligned}\tag{9}

Here D_M is the original positive Hermitian determinant. The polynomials in (9) retain the original level N and the ONE forced x-or-y unit. The shift u_* is the actual closest complete folded node; (9) does not assert it equals the full endpoint coupon.

Since x+y and both determinant factors are real stable, B_cross is real stable. For an original one-cap exterior with R-1 CROSS coupons, let Q_N(A,C) be obtained by using these B_cross coupons and performing the SAME protected specialization and EVERY same exterior face coefficient extraction as for P_N(A,C). Then

QN is zero or nonnegative real stable,deg⁡QN=d=N−1when QN≠0,QN≤coefPN≤coef3R−1QN. \begin{gathered} Q_N\text{ is zero or nonnegative real stable},\qquad \deg Q_N=d=N-1\quad\text{when }Q_N\ne0,\\ Q_N\le_{\mathrm{coef}}P_N\le_{\mathrm{coef}}3^{R-1}Q_N. \end{gathered}\tag{10}

The exact coefficient inequalities follow under products and extractions; no face target changes. Under the standing nonzero complete-source hypotheses Q_N is nonzero at all sufficiently large levels. Its degree is d because B_cross has the same exact degree d as P_cross, including the forced outer unit.

Consequently P_N and Q_N have the SAME normalized pressure at every positive boundary fugacity, whenever either limit exists. In particular, using the original both-parity positive coefficient theorem near the strict stationary exterior, that common limit is the same classical F(z) used at odd levels.

The compact-measure argument requires only convergence on a small REAL interval here. The root measures of Q_N have bounded mass on [0,1]. Any weak subsequential limit mu gives integral log(1+p(e^z-1)) dmu(p)=F(z)-F(0) for real z in that interval, since its kernel is bounded continuous in p. The integral is analytic near z=0; equality on the real interval determines all moments. Thus every subsequential limit is the SAME odd-level mu_D.

It follows rigorously that the stable REFERENCE exterior has the same Mahler and negative-axis supremum rates as the odd original exterior:

1Nlog⁡Mahler⁡(QN)→Mcap−ℓD,1Nlog⁡sup0≤t≤1|QN(−t,1)|→Mcap+m−,1Nlog⁡sup0≤t≤1|QN(−1,t)|→Mcap+m+. \begin{aligned} \frac1N\log\operatorname{Mahler}(Q_N)&\longrightarrow M_{\mathrm{cap}}-\ell_D,\\ \frac1N\log\sup_{0\le t\le1}|Q_N(-t,1)|&\longrightarrow M_{\mathrm{cap}}+m^-,\\ \frac1N\log\sup_{0\le t\le1}|Q_N(-1,t)|&\longrightarrow M_{\mathrm{cap}}+m^+. \end{aligned}\tag{11}

This uses the reviewed logarithmic-supremum continuity theorem. The passage from Q_N back to P_N at negative fugacity is NOT supplied by (10).

4. Even exponentially sharp positive comparison is insufficient

Here is a concise general warning, not an original-source example. For d>=1, j=floor(d/2) and fixed 0<a<log2, set

Qd(A,C)=(A+C)d,Pd(A,C)=Qd(A,C)+eadAjCd−j. Q_d(A,C)=(A+C)^d,\qquad P_d(A,C)=Q_d(A,C)+e^{ad}A^jC^{d-j}.\tag{12}

All coefficients are nonnegative. The central binomial coefficient is at least 2^d/(d+1), so even the coefficientwise comparison is

Qd≤coefPd≤coef[1+(d+1)e−(log⁡2−a)d]Qd. Q_d\le_{\mathrm{coef}}P_d \le_{\mathrm{coef}}\bigl[1+(d+1)e^{-(\log2-a)d}\bigr]Q_d.\tag{13}

The normalized positive pressure, and all coefficient exponential rates, are unchanged. On any fixed real z interval, the pressure also has the same locally uniform complex continuation in a small neighborhood, because the added term is exponentially smaller than (1+ez)d there.

Nevertheless for BOTH negative-axis intervals,

ead≤sup0≤t≤1|Pd(−t,1)|≤1+ead,ead≤sup0≤t≤1|Pd(−1,t)|≤1+ead. \begin{aligned} e^{ad}&\le\sup_{0\le t\le1}|P_d(-t,1)|\le1+e^{ad},\\ e^{ad}&\le\sup_{0\le t\le1}|P_d(-1,t)|\le1+e^{ad}. \end{aligned}\tag{14}

by evaluation at t=1 and the triangle inequality. The corresponding two suprema of Q_d are exactly1. Thus neither positive pressure nor even an exponentially sharp coefficientwise comparison transfers negative-axis exponential rates. In particular the genuine constant-factor source comparison (10) cannot by itself do so.

This example does not assert that the correction in (12) is realized by an original CROSS exterior. It identifies the precise extra signed/structural estimate that an even-parity adapter would need.

5. Current conclusion and remaining original-source question

The direct all-parity local stability extension is false by (5)–(7). The stable nearest-fold comparison Q_N is an EXACT original-level, same-target reference and has the desired limiting root measure. What remains is a negative-axis/source contraction for the ACTUAL difference P_N-Q_N, or a different exact positive decomposition with controlled signed boundary values. A proof that the globally extracted P_N itself is stable remains possible despite the local failure; no such proof or counterexample is given here.

The original even and odd signed-cap finite moment identities remain valid independently of stability. This note supplies no spectral neighborhood, no signed-source lower rate, and no general filled volume theorem.
