The controlled Fourier gauge sends the geometric cut clock to a conormal shift

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The controlled source gauge has an explicit quadratic kernel. It gives exact transformed head Ward operators, hence an actual crossing/current Hamiltonian dictionary. It also distinguishes two clocks which have identical-looking coherent coordinate descriptions but are different actual insertions. The difference survives the normalized source pairing. The relevant first-normal coupling is computed from the whole clock, not inferred from its reduced root representative.

The result does not yet send both actual framing-current equations to the full sourced geometric period. It identifies the required polarization/normal adapter and rules out a source-preserving identification which simply equates the coupled position clock with the old geometric cut clock.

1. Exact finite kernel and observables

Use the actual high-color basis and source of the cyclotomic wall construction, with r=N. Write R=0,…,N for the Frobenius block index, S=0,…,N-1 for the head index, A=Q_N^S and B=Q_N^R. Let V increase S modulo N. Take the explicitly normalized Fourier matrix

$$ {\cal F}_{S',S}=N^{-1}Q_N^{S'S},\quad {\cal F}^{-1}_{S,S'}=Q_N^{-SS'},\quad H_0=G(I\otimes{\cal F}),\quad G=\sum_R P_R\otimes V^R. $$

The actual matrix kernel is exactly

(H0)R,S′;R,S=N−1QNS(S′−R). (H_0)_{R,S';R,S}=N^{-1}Q_N^{S(S'-R)}. \tag{1}

The reduction of S’-R modulo N changes no power of Q_N. Finite geometric-series multiplication gives

$$ \begin{split} H_0 A H_0^{-1}&=V^{-1},& H_0 V H_0^{-1}&=B^{-1}A,\\ H_0 B H_0^{-1}&=B,& H_0e_{\rm Fr}H_0^{-1}&=e_{\rm Fr}V^{-1},& H_0f_{\rm Fr}H_0^{-1}&=f_{\rm Fr}V. \end{split} \tag{2} $$

The Fr matrices in (2) are the actual divided matrices with their root sign normalization, not an independent classical copy. Their last two conjugates are exactly the endpoint-completed generators constructed from divided powers and actual normal wrap entries.

In particular the OLD head clock is the NEW head shift. The new position clock restricted to the correlated source satisfies A|P=B|P, but this inserted A is not H_0 A_old H_0^(-1). Conjugating an entire word transforms its old observables as well as its sources; it does not replace this distinction by a choice of terminology.

2. Actual transformed Ward operators, including the endpoints

First keep the ORIGINAL INTEGRAL head frame of the wall source. Let M^2=1 and x=-M/q_N. The sign M is the retained meridian lift; in the high r=N color it is (-1)^N before its specified one-dimensional lift adapter. The genuine Hopf scaling is Ecal=rho^2 E. With C_q=diag(q_N^S), the actual root matrices are

$$ K=xA^{-1},\quad {\cal E}=M\rho V^{-1}(C_q-C_q^{-1}),\quad F=V(q_NC_q-q_N^{-1}C_q^{-1})/\rho. \tag{3} $$

These are Ecal=M rho^2 E_St and F=F_St. Their E edge at S=0 and F edge at S=N-1 vanish exactly, since [0]=[N]=0. Define the whole finite polynomial matrix

Ψw(V)=1N∑ℓ=0N−1∑s=0N−1(wQNℓ)sVℓ,Ψ±=ΨqN±1. \Psi_w(V)=\frac1N\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1} \sum_{s=0}^{N-1}(wQ_N^\ell)^sV^\ell, \qquad\Psi_\pm=\Psi_{q_N^{\pm1}}. \tag{3a}

It is exactly Fourier w^S Fourier^(-1). The controlled shift commutes with this circulant matrix, so H_0 C_q(+/-1)H_0(-1) =Psi_(+/-). Hence the exact transformed actual current operators, in the SAME wall source frame, are

$$ \begin{split} K^{H_0}&=xV,\\ {\cal E}^{H_0}&=M\rho BA^{-1}(\Psi_+-\Psi_-),\\ F^{H_0}&=B^{-1}A(q_N\Psi_+-q_N^{-1}\Psi_-)/\rho. \end{split} \tag{4} $$

These are original head Ward currents for the ACTUAL conjugated crossing: conjugate the input/output coproducts with the same endpoint H_0’s. The original exact R intertwining equation then gives its exact transformed equation, with no omitted Cartan, twist, cap, source or internal projector. Thus (4) is a concrete crossing/current identity, not only an identification of orbit dimensions or groups.

It has a useful negative consequence. The transformed Fr generators in (2) commute with all three transformed head operators (4), because the two original factors commute. They are distinct commuting actions at the root. Calling the Fr generators the old geometric head currents without a normal comparison would contradict this exact operator fact.

For comparison there is a simpler BALANCED-frame formula, but its frame adapter must not be erased. The explicit integral-to-balanced diagonal matrix is

Ds=(1−QN)sqNs(s−1)/2. D_s=(1-Q_N)^s q_N^{s(s-1)/2}.

Direct adjacent entries give D F_St D^(-1)=V(1-Q_NA) and D(M rho^2 E_St)D(-1)=V(-1)(MA(-1)-M(-1)). If one defines a FRESH unit shift and unit Fourier gauge H_B in those balanced coordinates, its formulas are

$$ {\cal E}^{H_B}=BA^{-1}(MV-M^{-1}),\quad F^{H_B}=B^{-1}A(1-Q_NV^{-1}),\quad K^{H_B}=xV. \tag{4a} $$

The transported original integral shift is instead D V D^(-1), which is weighted. Thus H_B is not obtained by silently retaining the same unit V while calling it the transported wall H_0. To compare the two actual words one must include D (and its actual whole q-germ when normal jets are compared), with both sources, duals and every internal projector. The logarithm of this diagonal can contribute to the phase. No Gaussian/large-level phase estimate is inferred merely from its invertibility at fixed N.

The actual whole source gauge H(q)=K_src(q)H_0 of the wall note has K_src(q_N)=I and retains its compression unit. Equation (4) is its root dictionary. If K_src=I+delta K_src,1+O(delta^2), every first-normal transformed operator has the additional term [K_src,1,O^(H_0)]. That known source-germ term must be included rather than treating constant H_0 as the entire whole source comparison.

3. The exact quadratic generating family and its conormal

Put H_N=2pi i/N and mark logarithmic positions

QR=HNR,q=HNS,q′=HNS′. Q_R=H_NR,\quad q=H_NS,\quad q'=H_NS'.

The kernel (1) is N^(-1) exp(W/H_N), with the exact quadratic generating function

W=q(q′−QR). W=q(q'-Q_R). \tag{5}

Use dW=p’_h dq’+(p’_R-p_R)dQ_R-p_h dq. The canonical transformation is therefore

QR′=QR,q′=QR−ph,ph′=q,pR′=pR−q. Q'_R=Q_R,\quad q'=Q_R-p_h,\quad p'_h=q,\quad p'_R=p_R-q. \tag{6}

It preserves dQ_R wedge dp_R+dq wedge dp_h. Logarithmic marks are required: a cyclic index wrap adds its specified integral 2pi i period to the quadratic generator. It is not discarded while composing marked phases. The root Frobenius parity remains its separately recorded character.

The Fr index R is UNWRAPPED: R=0 and R=N are distinct endpoint states even though B has the same eigenvalue there. No cyclic Fr wrap is added to its nilpotent divided matrices. Formula (6) is the local marked canonical map of the exact quadratic kernel; the finite endpoint constraints and the Fr O(N) source clutch remain in the finite operator problem. No analytic stationary-phase comparison or global flat Weyl quantization of that source line has been assumed.

For a normally ordered original head observable h(A,V), (2) is an exact finite difference Egorov formula. Its leading Hamiltonian is the old Hamiltonian under

q=ph′,ph=QR−q′. q=p'_h,\qquad p_h=Q_R-q'. \tag{7}

For example IN THE BALANCED FRAME the principal Hamiltonians are e^p(M e(-q)-M(-1)) and e(-p)(1-eq). Under (7) they give precisely the principal symbols of (4a). In the original integral frame the functions Psi_+/- in (4) are retained instead; converting their marked phase requires the displayed diagonal adapter. The quadratic Hamiltonian dictionary itself follows directly from the actual finite Fourier kernel, without importing a general semiclassical Egorov theorem or suppressing this basis change.

The original uniform head source has p_h=0. Its image is the actual constraint q’=Q_R, exactly P from the wall construction. The Fr source psi(t)=sum_R t^Rv_R has p_R=Log t, so its correlated image has the conormal relation

pR′+ph′=Log⁡t. p'_R+p'_h=\operatorname{Log}t. \tag{8}

Thus the actual OLD cut position A_old=e^q is e^(p’_h), or t/e^(p’_R), on this marked conormal. It is not e^(Q_R). Equation (8) is a concrete relative-Fr-momentum adapter, including the source parameter. It is a relation on a source conormal, not a replacement of two independent momentum operators by ordinary functions before contraction.

4. The normalized source detects the clock distinction

For every pair of Fr source flags, e_D is supported on the selected states z_j of the wall theorem. A head shift changes S while fixing R. Hence

PV−1P=0,Tr⁡(eDV−1)=0. PV^{-1}P=0,\qquad \operatorname{Tr}(e_DV^{-1})=0. \tag{9}

On the same source the other insertion has the exact, nonconstant character

Tr⁡(eDA)=(1+QNtu1+tu)N,T=tu1+tu. \operatorname{Tr}(e_D A) =\left(\frac{1+Q_Ntu}{1+tu}\right)^N,\qquad T=\frac{tu}{1+tu}. \tag{10}

Equations (9)–(10) are actual finite matrix pairings. Thus there cannot be a source-preserving whole-observable comparison which identifies these two clocks. The polynomial Fr-clock normal graph in the parent’s finite-clock construction is an actual INSERTED clock image and a useful source cycle; it is not yet the image of the old cut observable under (1). An additional polarization and current comparison is necessary.

The same distinction appears in the original geometric directions. In the old complete critical frame, both cut-Borel generators obey

$$ \delta p_h=0,\qquad \delta q=-2(1-A_{\rm old}). \tag{11} $$

Here delta p_h=0 is exactly ker(ell), the fixed inverse-cut-flag condition, rather than a new gauge assumption. Lift these old geometric directions without changing the ancillary Fr variables. Equation (6) carries their CUT components to

$$ \delta q'=\delta Q_R=0,\quad \delta p'_h=-2(1-A_{\rm old}),\quad \delta p'_R=2(1-A_{\rm old}). \tag{12} $$

They lie in the relative conormal direction (8). Their independence as the two full geometric framing fields is carried by the other actual flow coordinates; it is invisible in these identical cut components. One source clock or one cut current does not determine that independent internal coordinate.

5. The whole clock has an exact first-normal Fr coupling

The normal coupling does exist, but it uses more data than a root clock value. Before specializing let Q=q^2, Z=Q^N, delta=1-Z, and retain the actual unwrapped color clock

$$ C_Q=Q^{J_{\rm un}}=Q^S Z^R. \tag{13} $$

Let e(q)=tau E^(N)(q) and f(q)=F^(N)(q) be the actual generic integral divided matrices; the sign tau is constant on the marked root. They lower and raise R by one, respectively, while preserving S. Thus matrix grading alone proves the exact whole identities

[e(q),CQ]=(Z−1)CQe(q),[f(q),CQ]=(Z−1−1)CQf(q). [e(q),C_Q]=(Z-1)C_Qe(q),\qquad [f(q),C_Q]=(Z^{-1}-1)C_Qf(q). \tag{14}

Regular division and the root evaluation give the actual normal cut-current insertions

[[e,CQ]/δ]0=−Ae0,[[f,CQ]/δ]0=Af0. [\,[e,C_Q]/\delta\,]_0=-A e_0,\qquad [\,[f,C_Q]/\delta\,]_0=A f_0. \tag{15}

The same identities hold for the true tensor divided coproduct and the TOTAL clock: every mixed coproduct term lowers or raises the unwrapped total index by N. Hence (14)–(15) retain those mixed terms, rather than replacing a divided Hopf action by a primitive single-factor commutator. Actual R intertwining and total-index conservation transport this normal identity through every crossing; closing strands still requires its actual pivotal and relative co-source rows.

The full head resolvent in the correct polarization

The whole transformed clock can itself be written explicitly. Write zeta=Q_N while Q remains the variable in the ORIGINAL q-family, and keep H_0 fixed at zeta. Equation (3a) gives

CQH0=ZRΨQ(V),ΨQ(V)=∑ℓ=0N−11−QNN(1−Qζℓ)Vℓ. C_Q^{H_0}=Z^R\Psi_Q(V),\qquad \Psi_Q(V)=\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1} \frac{1-Q^N}{N(1-Q\zeta^\ell)}V^\ell. \tag{15a}

Every quotient in (15a) is the polynomial N^(-1) sum_(s=0)^(N-1)(Q zetaell)s. In particular its resonant 0/0 at ell=N-1 is not separately evaluated or removed. This identity follows from the exact finite Fourier sum; it retains the entire whole function before restricting its normal jets.

Its first normal is

CQH0=V−1+δ[−RV−1−N−12NV−1+∑ℓ≠N−1VℓN(1−ζℓ+1)]+O(δ2). \begin{split} C_Q^{H_0}=V^{-1}+\delta\bigg[ -RV^{-1}-\frac{N-1}{2N}V^{-1} +\sum_{\ell\ne N-1}\frac{V^\ell}{N(1-\zeta^{\ell+1})} \bigg]+O(\delta^2). \end{split} \tag{15b}

Thus the needed head pseudoinverse coefficients arise from the actual Fourier-clock operator; they are not guessed to make two classical vector fields agree. Equivalently the head part is -V^(-1) Shat/N, where Shat=Fourier S Fourier^(-1). Its operator norm is (N-1)/N in the root unit metric, since Fourier has condition number one. The individual nonresonant coefficients and their resonant diagonal term must be kept together.

Let e_t(q)=H_0 e(q)H_0^(-1), f_t(q)=H_0 f(q)H_0^(-1). Their root values are the compensated generators e_Fr V^(-1) and f_Fr V. They commute with the reduced whole head function Psi_Q(V) for ALL q, because e(q),f(q) preserve S. Consequently the head terms in their differentiated commutator cancel exactly. The extra R term of (15b) alone supplies the remaining normal interaction:

$$ [e_t(0),-RV^{-1}]=-e_{\rm Fr}V^{-2},\qquad [f_t(0),-RV^{-1}]=f_{\rm Fr}. \tag{15c} $$

These are precisely -C_0 e_t(0) and C_0 f_t(0) from (15), with C_0=V^(-1). This is an explicit actual normal crossing/current Hamiltonian identity in the CORRECT shift polarization. It shows where the head resolvents cancel and where the surviving Fr normal current originates, rather than identifying it from orbit names.

The whole source gauge does not invalidate the calculation. If K_src=I+delta K_1+…, it adds [K_1,C_0] and [K_1,e_t(0)] to the two first coefficients. Their extra commutator contributions sum to [K_1,[e_t(0),C_0]]=0 by Jacobi; similarly for f. Thus the normal coupling is preserved with the actual compression unit and source germ retained. Nothing here removes the finite endpoint or relative normal co-source when the word is closed.

A nonzero actual old-clock normal map to the unprojectivized source line

Keeping the source line, rather than only its normalized projector, provides a concrete additional comparison. Let r=N. On each actual residue string use the homogeneous divided source

ψs(q;a,b)=aNexp⁡((b/a)F(N)(q))w0,s,Us(q;a,b)=Λs(q)ψs(q;a,b), \psi_s(q;a,b)=a^N\exp((b/a)F^{(N)}(q))w_{0,s},\quad U_s(q;a,b)=\Lambda_s(q)\psi_s(q;a,b), \tag{15d}

where Lambda_s is the original actual divided co-source. The first formula extends polynomially at a=0, since the terminating degree-R term is a(N-R)bR(F(N))R/R!. At the root

Us(qN;a,b)=((a+b)/2)N=:U*. U_s(q_N;a,b)=((a+b)/2)^N=:U_*.

On a+b!=0 the U_s are units as whole q-germs. Keep the exact root-one ratios and define the actual source/co-source

ψ=∑sψs,Λ=1N∑sU0UsΛs,Λψ=U0. \psi=\sum_s\psi_s,\qquad \Lambda=\frac1N\sum_s\frac{U_0}{U_s}\Lambda_s, \qquad\Lambda\psi=U_0. \tag{15e}

This is the whole retraction with a COMMON UNNORMALIZED source line. Normalizing by U_0 returns the original scalar high-color period; retaining it records the actual weight-N scale character. The ratios U_0/U_s, with all their normal derivatives, have not been set equal to their root value one.

For the actual old whole clock (13), finite matrix multiplication gives the exact whole formula

ΛCQψ=U0(q;a,b)N∑s=0N−1QsUs(q;a,bZ)Us(q;a,b). \Lambda C_Q\psi =\frac{U_0(q;a,b)}N\sum_{s=0}^{N-1}Q^s \frac{U_s(q;a,bZ)}{U_s(q;a,b)}. \tag{15f}

Its root value is zero, but its regular normal quotient is

[ΛCQψ1−QN]qN=((a+b)/2)NN(1−QN). \boxed{\left[\frac{\Lambda C_Q\psi}{1-Q^N}\right]_{q_N} =\frac{((a+b)/2)^N}{N(1-Q_N)}.} \tag{15g}

Here is a proof retaining the source derivatives. Put D_Q=Q d/dQ. The derivative of the ratio in (15f) is, at the root, N mu with mu=b partial_b log U_*=Nb/(a+b), independent of s. The explicit q-derivatives of the two U_s cancel in the ratio; they were not assumed zero. This Fr mean term and the derivative of U_0 multiply sum_s Q_N^s=0. The remaining head term is

DQ(ΛCQψ)|0=U*N∑ssQNs=U*QN−1,DQ(1−QN)|0=−N, \left.D_Q(\Lambda C_Q\psi)\right|_0 =\frac{U_*}N\sum_s sQ_N^s=\frac{U_*}{Q_N-1}, \qquad D_Q(1-Q^N)|_0=-N,

which proves (15g) by regular division at the simple normal zero. The entire expression (15f), not independent Taylor choices at each string, specifies the lift being divided.

Thus the known head unit N(1-Q_N) extracts the actual source character U_* from a literal OLD-cut-clock normal co-source. Its inverse has uniformly bounded modulus: 1/(2pi)<=|1/[N(1-Q_N)]|<=1/4. No exponential adapter is hidden in this factor. For an actual scalar long-knot matrix the same calculation gives U_* K_N/[N(1-Q_N)] at the root, with the original external peripheral line separately retained.

There is an exact root Borel-character statement as well. The actual divided source is the degree-N coherent vector of (a,b), and its fixed root row is the N-th symmetric power of (1,1)/2. If (1,1)g=chi(g)(1,1), then U_(g(a,b))=chi(g)^N U_(a,b). Equation (15g) is a nonzero equivariant map to this weight-N source line. At the equal-flag anchor the generator e+f, which fixes the normalized projector, acts on the unnormalized source with weight N. That previously invisible scale has therefore been retained by an ACTUAL clock-normal map, rather than inserted after identifying two abstract Borel groups.

This is a map of the cut/normal source CHARACTER. It does not equate the old clock value with a function of a+b, identify its two full internal framing currents, or prove a geometric summand survives the original finite cut. Dividing by U_0 at the outset would discard exactly the scale which (15g) detects. At this SELECTED SCALAR order the reduced clock Q^S gives the same answer: its omitted Fr-mean term already vanishes in the root head sum. Thus (15g) alone does not distinguish the whole and reduced normal actions. Their distinction is the operator/current identity (14)–(15), not an inferred independent scalar Fr current.

By contrast e(q) and f(q) commute with the reduced clock Q^S for ALL q. Consequently (15) depends on the chosen whole lift (13). Two observables with the same numerical root value give different normal currents. This is exactly the whole-variable datum needed before quantum jets are restricted.

There is also a precise limitation. The operation delta^(-1)ad(e) is not a regular derivation of the entire Fr source algebra: [e_0,f_0]=h_0 is generally nonzero. On delta times a lifted matrix g its root value is [e_0,g_0], not zero. It therefore does not descend to the root quotient independently of the lift. Equations (14)–(15) define a normal/source-complex insertion with a specified whole clock, not an ordinary classical vector field on every root observable. The normal connection, source unit, and endpoints are essential to extending it.

6. What the current dictionary now requires

The actual old head Hamiltonians and fields are explicitly given by (4), (7), and (12), while (14)–(15) are an actual Fr-normal coupling with every tensor mixed term retained. These are the two concrete inputs to the missing framing-current comparison. Simply mapping the Fr coherent quadric to the physical meridian graph does not intertwine them, as (9)–(10) proves at the selected source.

In particular the bare Fr commutator fields cannot be substituted for the two cut-Borel fields while preserving a clock A=e^(cT). In x=Log v,T coordinates their actual coherent values are

Xe=v∂x+v(1−T)∂T,Xf=−v−1∂x−Tv−1∂T,Xh=2∂x. X_e=v\partial_x+v(1-T)\partial_T,\quad X_f=-v^{-1}\partial_x-Tv^{-1}\partial_T,\quad X_h=2\partial_x.

A fixed linear combination has T velocity alpha v(1-T)-beta T/v. This can be a function of T alone only if alpha=beta=0, whereas (11) requires a nonzero such function. Head/source-dependent coefficients and the normal lift are therefore necessary. Their existence as rational or logarithmic functions is not itself the missing network-current identity.

No claim is made that this dictionary already kills the two full Green boundaries or produces a nonzero full geometric period. It specifies which physical observable, normal insertion and relative conormal class the required map must preserve.
