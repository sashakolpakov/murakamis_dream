Every actual root braid period has a source-equivalent Euler/Gaussian completion

27 October 2026. The original checked crossing admits an explicit Euler/Gaussian completion whose error raises the TOTAL INTEGER head degree by N. On b strands all positive multiples-of-N errors form a source-null ideal with bth power zero. Consequently every SIGNED braid word in the completed generators and their literal inverses has exactly the original full one-cut pivotal matrix. This holds at every root and as the whole original meridian family.

The bounded nilpotence concerns the error between two realizations, not the full braid action. The completed crossings need not obey the braid relation as matrices. They obey it modulo the proved source-null ideal, and their original closed scalar is exactly the original knot invariant. No quantum factorial model is substituted on asymptotic grounds and no numerical peripheral factor is chosen afterward.

The construction uses the existing original root R-matrices and actual Borel fusion. It removes a specific finite-support wall for CLOSED braid periods. It does not yet prove their hyperbolic growth, nor preserve arbitrary inserted currents or moving-q normal jets.

1. The original positive crossing coefficient in the relative algebra

Fix Q=q^2 of exact order N>=2 and write F_a=(Q;Q)a. The nilpotent balanced heads have F e_a=(1-Q^(a+1))e(a+1), K e_a=xQ^-a e_a. Let A_0=<H,f | HN=1,Hf=Q-1fH,f^N=0> with its usual right idempotents e_k. For two equal colors, the actual triangular fusion is C(f^a e_k)=f_total^a(e_0 tensor e_k). Its physical kernel is

C_(u,v;a,k)=x^(u-a) F_a F_v/[F_(a-u)F_k]
   if 0<=u<=a, v=k+a-u<N; otherwise zero.       (1)

Retain the original common Cartan scalar kappa=R_(00,00). It can equally be recorded in its prescribed external Hom/normalization line; it is not reset in this calculation. The positive crossing R commutes with the actual diagonal Borel action, so C^-1 R C is RIGHT multiplication by a unique element a_R. That coefficient is exactly

a_R=kappa sum_(a,k>=0,a+k<N)
       (-1)^k Q^(k(k-1)/2) x^(-a-2k) f^a e_k/F_a. (2)

Here all a,k lie in 0,…,N-1. Formula (2) is an all-level coefficient identity for the actual R, not its semiclassical symbol.

To prove it, apply the word to the algebra unit. Its physical seed is e_0 tensor ones, and the literal original crossing has

R(e_0 tensor e_j)=kappa x^-j e_j tensor e_0.

For total degree n<N, invert the upper-triangular block (1) on this vector. If its relative coefficients are h_a on f^a e_(n-a), put d_a=F_a h_a/[x^a F_(n-a)]. The equations are

sum_(a=u)^n d_a/F_(a-u)=delta_(u,n) x^(-2n).

Their reversed sequence is the inverse Euler series: d_a equals x^(-2n) times (-1)(n-a)Q((n-a)(n-a-1)/2)/F_(n-a). Undoing the change gives precisely (2), with k=n-a. There is no component of degree n>=N because the physical vector above has none and fusion preserves the total unwrapped degree a+k.

2. The completion is a genuine Euler times a marked Gaussian

Define

U_x(f)=sum_(a=0)^(N-1)x^-a f^a/F_a,
D_x(H)=sum_(k=0)^(N-1)
           (-1)^k Q^(k(k-1)/2)x^(-2k)e_k,
a_tilde=kappa U_x(f)D_x(H),
R_tilde=C R_(a_tilde) C^-1.                         (3)

The Euler factor has constant term one and nilpotent argument. Every Gaussian entry is nonzero. Thus (3) is an invertible actual finite matrix, with no support projector or division by an unknown period. Its negative crossing is its LITERAL inverse,

R_tilde^-1=C R_(kappa^-1 D_x^-1 U_x^-1) C^-1.

Right multiplication reverses composition, so the order of these factors is intentional. In (2), a_R is precisely the NONWRAPPED part of (3); the missing terms are f^a e_k with a+k>=N.

This formula also has a local unequal-color version. For a checked crossing from colors (x_1,x_2) to (x_2,x_1), use input fusion C_in and output fusion C_out. Then its relative coefficient is

a_R=kappa sum_(a+k<N)
      (-1)^k Q^(k(k-1)/2)x_1^-a(x_1x_2)^-k f^a e_k/F_a,

and its completion is obtained by dropping a+k<N, namely kappa U_(x_1)(f)D_(x_1x_2)(H). The same triangular inversion applies, replacing its top right side by (x_1x_2)^-n. The equal-color case already covers every ordinary knot braid.

At the complete color x=-q^-1, the right Euler argument in (3) is x^-1 f=Qz for z=xf. Thus this is exactly u_N(Qz), the Euler orientation appearing in the correctly transported primitive source sector of the whole-word gauge theorem. That observation identifies the actual local factor, without equating the full invariant with a single Fourier sector.

The finite Gaussian is marked, not a periodically extended scalar Gaussian: its unwrapped entries obey d_(k+N)=-x^(-2N)d_k. For example its exact coefficient-algebra conjugation contains

D_x f D_x^-1=-x^-2 fH^-1
              +Q^-1 x^-2(1+x^(2N))f e_(N-1).       (4)

The second term is the original wrap correction. A numerical factor such as its complete value two has not been silently erased. Below we prove which closed-source operations kill its positive weight; outside that algebra (4) must retain the entire factor.

3. Every wrapped error raises physical integer degree by N

Let D_b=sum_i J_i be the ordinary INTEGER head degree, J_i e_a=a e_a. Fusion preserves this degree: (1) has u+v=a+k as an equality of integers, not modulo N. Consider right multiplication by a wrapped path f^a e_k, a+k>=N. On a basis input f^c e_j it is nonzero only when j=k+a modulo N and c+a<N. Its output is f^(c+a)e_k. Therefore

output degree-input degree
   =(c+a+k)-(c+j)=N.                           (5)

No additional wrap can occur because 0<=a,k<N. Conjugating by C or by the two color versions of C preserves (5). In particular

Delta=R_tilde-R raises total integer degree exactly N.

The original R and R^-1 preserve integer degree exactly. Locally the two-head degree range is 0,…,2N-2, so

Delta R^-1 Delta=0,
R_tilde^-1=R^-1-R^-1 Delta R^-1.                (6)

Thus the negative completion also differs from the original inverse only by an operator of degree +N. The explicit inverse in (6) agrees with the inverse of the Euler/Gaussian factors in (3).

4. A level-uniform nilpotent error ideal for every braid index

On b heads let F_b be the algebra of operators having only degree changes 0,N,2N,…, and let n_b be the subspace with only strictly positive such changes. It is a two-sided ideal of F_b. Since all physical degrees lie between zero and b(N-1),

n_b^b=0.                                         (7)

More precisely its power floor(b(N-1)/N)+1 vanishes. The degree-zero projection pi_0 is an algebra homomorphism, with kernel n_b. It splits by embedding the degree-preserving operators themselves. On unit groups its kernel is 1+n_b; every fiber over a prescribed degree-zero unit is a torsor under that unipotent group. Inversion is the finite series in n_b, and

[1+n_b^r,1+n_b^s] lies in 1+n_b^(r+s).

Hence its nilpotence class is at most b-1, independently of N. This is a bounded error transport, not a nilpotence assertion for the whole actual braid group or its degree-zero semisimple sector.

Embed (3) and (6) at every crossing of an arbitrary SIGNED braid word beta. Each completed factor belongs to F_b and has original degree-zero part. Consequently

W_tilde(beta)-W(beta) belongs to n_b,             (8)
pi_0(W_tilde(beta))=W(beta).

This includes arbitrarily long words; the nilpotence bound in (7) depends on braid index, not crossing number or level. The completed generators satisfy every actual braid relation MODULO n_b. They need not satisfy the braid relation as matrices: the exact audit finds nonzero positive-weight braid defects. No unrestricted new braided tensor functor is claimed.

5. The original full first-cut matrix kills the ideal

Use the actual pivotal closure on all heads except the first, with the original diagonal pivots g_i=K_i^(1-N). In a matrix entry from first input a to first output c, every closed head returns to its original index. A term of degree change lN would therefore require c-a=lN. But

-(N-1)<=c-a<=N-1.

No strictly positive l is possible. Thus the entire original pivotal partial trace vanishes on n_b, entry by entry. Equation (8) proves the ALL-LEVEL, WHOLE-WORD identity

ptr_g W_tilde(beta)=ptr_g W(beta).               (9)

Every original first-input row/column, highest-input source and averaged source is preserved. The full original Cartan lines, writhe/framing scalar and modified dimension d_N(M) multiply the same two matrices, so they remain IDENTICAL without a new normalization. In particular the reduced completed root period at M=1 is exactly the prescribed original Kashaev invariant.

The formula holds over the original meridian coefficient field x=-M/q. In the usual rational ADO frame it is an identity of rational meridian families, not only their complete values. It therefore preserves their M-normal germs, all meridian derivatives, all defined orbit values M->q^j M, and the full peripheral rational map/source-lattice product. Any specified root-specialized annihilator of the original meridian period also annihilates the completed period. No assertion of a generic-q annihilator or a new ordering of the classical A-polynomial is needed for this consequence.

There is also a matrix meridian-transport statement. If sigma(M)=qM, then wherever the actual braid matrix is invertible define

K_j(M)=sigma^j(W(M)) W(M)^-1,
K_tilde_j(M)=sigma^j(W_tilde(M)) W_tilde(M)^-1.

The original K_j preserves integer degree; the completed one belongs to F_b. Because pi_0 is an algebra homomorphism, commutes with sigma and preserves inverses,

pi_0(K_tilde_j)=K_j.                            (9a)

The analogous identities hold for the local crossing transports and for logarithmic M-derivative connections. Thus root meridian matrix transports and their sourced word insertions are preserved in this filtered quotient even if they are not Borel-linear. Pivotal changes and the original scalar dimension/framing ratios are degree preserving and retain their assigned factors. This is not a claim that every chosen internal transfer-ratio coupon is unchanged as an uncontracted tensor; it is an exact two-fiber matrix/source statement about the actual root meridian family.

Any additional degree-preserving coupons may be retained anywhere in (8)-(9). This covers unwrapped head clocks and diagonal weights. It does NOT cover arbitrary coupons, Fourier projectors on an opened second wire, or all E/F Ward insertions.

There is a literal warning at N=2. With x=i, eta=exp(pi*i/4) and the original full positive crossing,

Delta=eta^-1 E_(11,00),
Ecal_(0,1)=-2,
Lambda_phys[(I tensor Ecal)Delta]=2eta!=0.         (10)

The inserted lowering degree -1 lets total change N-1 appear in the all-ones first output. Thus (9) is a proved closed-word source equivalence, not an equivalence of every inserted current complex. It must not be promoted to that stronger statement.

6. What has moved on the critical path

The original root state at arbitrary braid index now has an exact realization using explicit Euler and marked Gaussian factors with no local nonwrapped-path cutoff, modulo a SOURCE-NULL ideal of uniformly bounded nilpotence. This is a genuine extension beyond the fixed-polynomial observable window: it carries completed actual crossing words and their original meridian period exactly. The source no longer has to be guessed for this completion.

It is compatible with the earlier full Borel source-faithfulness theorem: that theorem permits arbitrary relative coupons, including negative N-degree maps. Here the algebra is restricted to nonnegative physical degree changes, which the completed words genuinely satisfy.

What remains is to analyze the full Euler/Gaussian word with its original closure and prove that its selected period carries the marked geometric A-branch and the required regulator norm. No positivity, thimble intersection, geometric survival or global cancellation estimate is inferred from (7). The degree-zero part remains the entire actual braid operator. Bounded nilpotence of the completion error is not a bound for that operator’s norm.

The theorem is at the actual roots and the whole M family. It does NOT by itself identify moving-q normal jets: off root the actual truncated coproduct has divided-power defects, and the free nilpotent fusion algebra used here needs its own extension. Those quantum-normal terms cannot be discarded by a root identity. The original volume conjecture remains unproved.

7. A natural off-root continuation fails the actual quantum normal

The restriction to actual root fibers is substantive. At N=2, remove only the common Cartan line from the actual matrices and keep Q,x as independent variables. The specified actual finite crossing and the natural finite-kernel continuation of (3) are

R(Q,x)=[[1,0,0,0],
        [0,0,x^-1,0],
        [0,x^-1,1-x^-2,0],
        [0,0,0,Qx^-2]],

R_tilde(Q,x)=[[1,0,0,0],
              [0,0,x^-1,0],
              [0,x^-1,1-x^-2,0],
              [-x^-3,0,0,-x^-2]].             (11)

At Q=-1 their difference has only degree +2, as proved above. Off root it also has the degree-zero term -(Q+1)x^-2 E_(11,11). On the original three-strand figure-eight word (1,-2,1,-2), use the actual pivot diag(x^-1,Q/x), the literal negative inverses and the highest first-input cut. Exact symbolic multiplication gives

C_00(W_tilde-W)=Q(Q+1)(x^2-1)/x^2.           (12)

Its root Q-normal derivative is

[Q partial_Q C_00(W_tilde-W)]_(Q=-1)
                              =(x^2-1)/x^2. (13)

At completeness x^2=-1 this equals two. Differentiating along x=-M/q instead gives the same value: the x-derivative of (12) is multiplied by Q+1 and vanishes on the root. The word has writhe zero, so its common positive/negative Cartan lines cancel; the original nonzero peripheral factor multiplies this nonzero normal defect rather than removing it.

Thus even an actual knot word distinguishes these two q-normal families. This is a VERIFIED failure of the natural continuation (11), not merely an absence of a proof. It does not preclude constructing a different completed quantum-normal family with its missing divided-power terms retained. It does preclude inferring that family from the root equality (9) alone.

8. Independent exact checks

The audit and saved results pass 26 root/meridian/braid-index cases and 88 complete signed words, including the original figure-eight word. They check:

  • the explicit actual relative R coefficient (2), independently against the original physical crossing;
  • the Euler/Gaussian factorization and literal negative inverse;
  • the physical +N weight of local and full-word errors;
  • equality of the ENTIRE original pivotal first-cut matrix;
  • original full dimension, Cartan and framing factors;
  • positive-weight braid defects and their zero source closure.

Six additional unequal-color cases check the local Hom formula against the literal original balanced crossing coefficient. The audit separately checks four whole figure-eight two-fiber meridian matrix transports and their entire original first-cut matrices. It also verifies the negative-weight E coupon in (10) and the characteristic-zero q-normal counterexample (11)-(13).

There are 74 nonzero full-word errors, so the source equality is not merely equality of the two tensor matrices. The proofs above are all-level. Finite exact checks verify conventions and scope.
