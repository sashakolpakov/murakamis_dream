Exact meridian frames for the actual opposite source

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The actual lowering coproduct is diagonally conjugate to its complete root value. A different, purely Cartan frame conjugates the raising coproduct and the entire prefix quotient. Their combination cancels the factorial meridian dependence of the middle turnover and, apart from its retained scalar, puts the remaining non-Cartan dependence of the opposite kernel in one explicit diagonal insertion. The true first meridian jet follows by the product rule. Both frames have polynomial conditioning on a fixed scaled meridian disk.

The complete direct J supplies the literal finite matrices, quotient, turnover, and source normalization; B supplies the ordered balanced crossings. Every new conjugation, normal formula, and bound is proved below. These are changes of presentation of the same finite source, not a simultaneous trivialization of its quantum-group representation or a relative estimate for a closed scalar.

In coherent resolutions these diagonal transformations act on both sides of the finite kernels and on the retained source coupons.
