A positive supported Racah factorial, its metric and retained partner current

29 October 2026. This is an independent finite FACTORIAL theorem. It does not assume that a proposed four-port source coefficient is a Racah sum. On the full folded supported chamber it proves a common phase, a positive root normalization, a uniform positive Laplace principle, and polynomial relative bounds for every fixed generic-q normal jet. Complementary high partners have the same positive value core but an additional explicitly retained normal current.

Connecting these formulas to the actual source requires its separate typed coefficient identity. A positive root value is not by itself a bound on a complete Hermite recoupling operator.

1. Seven factorials, with a root unit at every denominator

Fix N>=3, D=N-1, M=floor(D/2), q=exp(pi i/N), and six INTEGER folded spins 0<=a,b,c,d,e,f<=M. Define four faces and three quads

F=(a+b+e, c+d+e, a+d+f, b+c+f),
Q=(a+b+c+d, a+c+e+f, b+d+e+f).              (1)

Assume every F_i>=D. Every face is an ordinary triangle: its two smaller entries sum to at least D minus its largest, which is at least its largest because D>=2M. Moreover EVERY face deficit F_i-2ell lies in [0,D]. Every difference Q_k-F_i is one such deficit of a complementary face. Therefore

z_-=max F_i <= z_+=min Q_k,
D<=z_-<=z_+<=4M<=2D,
0<=z-F_i<=D, 0<=Q_k-z<=D.                   (2)

Use the GENUINE generic symmetric factorial [n]!_zeta, with [u]_zeta=(zetau-zeta-u)/(zeta-zeta^-1). Set

S(zeta)=sum_(z=z_-)^(z_+) (-1)^z [z+1]!
              /[product_i [z-F_i]! product_k[Q_k-z]!]. (3)

No negative factorial is assigned a value: the displayed interval is the true finite range. By (2), every denominator is a unit at q. The numerator contains EXACTLY ONE factor [N]. Factoring it BEFORE specialization defines the analytic function

G(zeta)=(-1)^D S(zeta)/[N]_zeta.              (4)

Let s_r=product_(u=1)^r 2sin(pi u/N), s_0=1. The exact positive root value is

R_N:=G(q)=N sum_z s_(z-D)
                /[product_i s_(z-F_i) product_k s_(Q_k-z)]>0. (5)

Indeed the signs of [N+1],…,[z+1] multiply to (-1)^(z+1-N). Together with (-1)^(D+z) this is +1. Also s_D=N. The powers of 2sin(pi/N) cancel, since

sum_i(z-F_i)+sum_k(Q_k-z)=z,
sum_i F_i=sum_k Q_k=2(a+b+c+d+e+f).

Thus (5) is a finite exact identity, including boundary faces F_i=D, collapsed z intervals, both level parities and composite levels.

2. The exact positive face metric

For a face (x,y,z) of sum F, define the generic squared triangle factor and its regular normalization by

Delta_F^2=[F-2x]![F-2y]![F-2z]!/[F+1]!,
D_F(zeta)=(-1)^(F-D)[N]_zeta Delta_F^2.

It is analytic and nonzero at q, with positive root value

d_F=D_F(q)
       =s_(F-2x)s_(F-2y)s_(F-2z)/(N s_(F-D))>0. (6)

The natural positive normalized scalar is

W_N=R_N sqrt(product_faces d_F).              (7)

This metric is not optional in a geometric interpretation. The branch-independent generic squared identity is EXACTLY

[N]^2 S(zeta)^2 product_faces Delta_F^2
                  =G(zeta)^2 product_faces D_F(zeta). (8)

The root of its right side is W_N^2>0. There is no residual sign, because sum_faces(F-D)=2(a+b+c+d+e+f)-4D is even. Equation(8) defines a finite factorial normalization; it is not a claim that an as-yet-unproved physical recoupling matrix is unitary in this metric.

The ordered physical Clebsch norms, source gamma factors and any intermediate quantum dimensions must still be matched by an actual coefficient theorem. A quantum dimension can vanish at the odd simple endpoint. It is not canceled by evaluating (7) alone; its generic source limit must be retained before interpreting this as a physical Gram.

3. A uniform positive action on the ENTIRE folded chamber

Put L(t)=integral_0^t log(2sin(pi u))du on [0,1], continuously at both endpoints. Write x=(a,b,c,d,e,f)/N, D_N=D/N, and use the same linear forms F_i(x),Q_k(x). On

I_N=[max_i F_i(x),min_k Q_k(x)]

define

A_N(x,Z)=L(Z-D_N)-sum_i L(Z-F_i(x))
                            -sum_k L(Q_k(x)-Z),
V_N(x,Z)=A_N(x,Z)
   +(1/2)sum_faces[sum_(ell in face)L(F(x)-2x_ell)
                                       -L(F(x)-D_N)].       (9)

Uniformly over ALL allowed integral labels,

log R_N=N max_(Z in I_N) A_N(x,Z)+O(log N),
log W_N=N max_(Z in I_N) V_N(x,Z)+O(log N).   (10)

Proof. Every factorial index is in [0,D], so the uniform elementary sine-factorial estimate log s_r=N L(r/N)+O(log N) applies to every term, including endpoints. There are at most 2N positive terms. Their maximum gives the upper bound and one term gives the lower. The modulus of continuity of L is O(epsilon log(1/epsilon)); rounding Z to a feasible integral index changes N times the action by at most O(log N). All constants are independent of the labels. The same estimate applied to the four explicit factors (6) proves the second line. Thus no cancellation or selected complex saddle is required.

For any convergent scaled-label sequence, (10) yields the corresponding max-action limit, with D_N replaced by 1. This is an action theorem; identification with an actual geometric tetrahedron is a separate task.

Two calibrations expose the metric’s role. At all six scaled labels 1/3, the bare action maximum is 3v3/pi, whereas the face metric adds -2v3/pi and the normalized maximum is v3/pi. At all six labels 1/2, the metric action is zero and the normalized maximum is v8/pi. Here v3=3Lambda(pi/3), v8=8Lambda(pi/4). The maxima occur respectively at Z=7/6 and Z=7/4, by direct differentiation of (9).

The independently proved local hyperideal geometry theorem now identifies this normalized maximum with actual truncated-tetrahedron volume. Its exact Casimir angle coordinates are a_ell=(ell+1/2)/N. Using Z=(z+7/4)/N shifts the numerator factorial argument by 3/(4N), each denominator argument by 1/(4N), and every face-metric argument by 1/(2N). The same uniform endpoint modulus gives the SHARP scalar bound

log W_N=(N/pi)Vol_ext(T(a_ell))+O(log N)        (10a)

on the entire supported chamber, with a universal error constant. Vol_ext denotes the continuous boundary-volume extension when an odd simple channel has zero dihedral angle. This is a local scalar energy calibration, not a proof that a source graph supplies the corresponding cell gluing or a completed Hermite metric.

4. Polynomial relative generic normal jets, not just root values

Take zeta=q exp(t), with ALL integer labels fixed, and cancel the single displayed [N] in each term of (3). This is the actual generic factorial germ, not a formal root recurrence continued off the root. There is a universal c>0 such that on

|t|<=r_N=c/[N^2(1+log N)]                     (11)

the functions G and each D_F are holomorphic and nonzero, and their ratios to their positive root values differ from one by less than 1/4. The canonical analytic square roots of D_F, positive at t=0, therefore define H(t)=G(t) product_F sqrt(D_F(t)), with H(0)=W_N. Shrinking the SAME universal c if necessary gives |H/H(0)-1|<1/4.

Here is a uniform proof. After cancellation, all factors zeta-zeta^-1 cancel too, by the factorial degree equality used in section1. Every remaining factor is zetau-zeta-u, 1<=u<2N, u!=N. At t=0 its absolute value is at least 4 dist(u,N Z)/N. For |t|<=N^-2, its derivative has absolute value at most 30N. Thus each unit factor’s logarithmic change is bounded by 15N^2|t|/dist(u,N Z) when that relative change is small. Across a summand there are at most eighteen copies of the harmonic sum sum_(k=1)^N 1/k; the four face factors have the same fixed bound. Consequently each product ratio has logarithmic change at most C N^2(1+log N)|t|, with universal C. Choosing, for example, a sufficiently small c<=10^-6 makes every summand stay in a small common sector around its POSITIVE value in (5). A positive weighted sum stays in that sector as well. This proves (11), including the nonzero lower bound and analytic square roots.

Cauchy’s inequality now gives, for every fixed k>=0 and F=G or H,

|F^(k)(0)|/k! <=2 r_N^-k F(0),
|(1/F)^(k)(0)|/k! <=2 r_N^-k/F(0).           (12)

Thus finite normal jets and inverse scalar jets have polynomial relative cost UNIFORMLY throughout this folded supported chamber. This scalar assertion does not estimate a change of physical frame. Together with (10a), it bounds squared fixed normal jets by a polynomial times exp[(2N/pi)Vol_ext(T(a_ell))], with all joint labels still shared.

5. Complementary partners: positive value core and a real missing current

Now replace ANY subset of the six folded labels ell by D-ell. At each face, its new deficits are old deficits, their complements D-old_deficit, the old excess F-D, or its complement D-(F-D). They all lie in [0,D]. Its new sum lies in [D,2D]. Therefore (2)’s denominator-unit assertion survives at EVERY one of the up to 64 partner assignments, and the new z range is nonempty with z_-<=2D.

Also the sum of its six labels is at most 4D, because every new face has sum at most 2D. Hence z_+<=8D/3<3N-1. The generic sum still contains [N] in every term, but some terms now contain [2N] too. The EXACT decomposition is

(-1)^D S(zeta)/[N]_zeta=C(zeta)+[2N]_zeta T(zeta), (13)

where C sums the TRUE terms z<=2D after deleting the numerator [N], and T sums the terms z>=2N-1 after deleting BOTH [N] and [2N]. All their denominators are units. At q the nonempty core is exactly the positive expression (5), with z truncated at 2D. The positive metric (6) and squared identity (8) apply at every partner value too. The core C has the same polynomial scalar-jet bound as section4.

The term [2N]T is NOT zero as a generic normal. In particular

((-1)^D S/[N])'(0)=C'(0)+(4N/(q-q^-1))T(0).   (14)

Writing z=2N-1+k, its retained root value is explicitly

T(0)=-2sin(pi/N)N^2 sum_k (-1)^k s_k
          /[product_i s_(2N-1+k-F_i)
                     product_j s_(Q_j-2N+1-k)]. (15)

Every index in (15) is physical. The alternating sum must remain coherent. Its absolute-value envelope is not a substitute for it. For example N=4, partner labels (2,2,2,2,2,1) arise by five flips from the supported folded labels (1,1,1,1,1,1). Their faces are (6,6,5,5), quads (8,7,7). Core z=6 gives C(0)=sqrt(2), while the single tail term z=7 gives T(0)=-1. The EXTRA first normal in (14) is exactly 8sqrt(2)i. Deleting the tail loses actual data.

Accordingly (12) is a theorem for the complete LOW-fold scalar germ and for partner value cores. It is NOT asserted for the full partner germ without a further coherent estimate for T. Nor are the partner columns deleted from a physical Hermite cluster. This is the remaining normal interface for using the positive factorial in a full recoupling energy argument.

A research lead, originally unproved, is preserved by the 64-partner value diagnostic and its recorded root images. For three specified folded tuples per level when distinct, every one of the 64 complement bit patterns was checked at N=3,…,10: 1408 comparisons showed no failure of W_squared invariance. These are exact finite cyclotomic-root images, not a uniform complex identity proof. No symmetry of generic germs, bound on the [2N] current, completed physical Gram or volume theorem is inferred from that observation. The subsequent actual partner-reflection theorem now proves the root-value symmetry uniformly from the actual reciprocal source and analytic projector residues. Its exact first-normal counterexample shows why this does not establish generic-jet symmetry.

6. An exact valuation interface to the separately proved physical kernel

The preceding factorial theorem has not used a source identity. One can now APPLY the independently proved actual inverse four-port theorem, equations (23),(24). Put its external highest weights (A,B,C,D_ext)=(2a,2b,2c,2d), E=2e and G=2f. It states

L_(G,E)=chi_E X_E/(b_G J_n S0 V_n) *S,
p=min(a+d,b+c), q=max(a+d,b+c), s=p+q,
n=p-f, t0=q-p,
V_n=zeta^n (Q^(-p-f-1);Q)_n
              /[F_n(Q^(t0+1);Q)_n(Q^(-s-1);Q)_n]. (16)

Throughout the fully supported folded chamber, chi_E X_E, b_G and J_n are root units, while S0 has exactly one [N] zero. This follows directly from the source product formulas: all their physical factorial indices are <=D, whereas the numerator of S0 is [s+1]! with D<=s<=2D. For X_E at its endpoint, its numerator index is <=A when that endpoint is H, and <=B when it is A. Hence no endpoint root zero has been hidden in that assertion.

Both f faces imply p+f>=D and q+f>=D. The sole possible numerator zero of V_n is at index k=p+f-D. It is INSIDE 0,…,n-1 exactly when 2f<D. Its denominator has no root zero: t0+n=q-f<=D, and the first potential zero in (Q^(-s-1);Q)_n has index s-D>=n. Consequently the actual inverse coefficient has exact order

ord_q L_(G,E)=-1 if 2f<D,
ord_q L_(G,E)= 0 if 2f=D.                    (17)

Its leading coefficient is nonzero by the positive finite sum (5). Thus a positive root factorial does NOT turn the uncompleted physical coefficient matrix into a finite positive operator: paired output channels have genuine simple poles and must be combined with their partners in a regular Hermite frame.

The same bookkeeping in the actual ordered invariant norm

N_E=n_(ab)^e n_(cd)^e [2e+1]

gives order two for paired e and order one for odd simple e. These are source-frame orders, not optional quantum-dimension factors. Equations (16),(17) supply an explicit local interface for matching the positive metric (6) to a completed physical recoupling energy; they do not complete that matching or bound the partner current (15).

Audit: true root factorials, partner ranges and retained Taylor coefficients. The common phase, metric, uniform action and jet arguments above are independent of the proposed actual four-port source identity.
