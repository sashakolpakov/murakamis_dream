Independent audit of the actual universal resonant cut

27 October 2026. The construction in the universal-cut theorem passes under its stated complete discrete-faithful and original regular geometric-chart hypotheses. It is an actual presentation/cut change, not only a conjugation of an abstract representation. The complete principal pole and port half-character become universal. The global selected-period comparison remains unproved.

1. The unchanged meridian can be moved by the specified actual word

The initial braid meridians generate the knot group. If they all had lower-left entry zero, the representation would be reducible. Choose the first U with c=U_21 nonzero. A preceding upper parabolic is conjugate to J within the discrete faithful group. Its conjugator must fix infinity. An upper word with diagonal ratio not one either gives nontrivial translations tending to identity under conjugation, or has nontrivial finite projective order. Discreteness and torsion freeness exclude both. Its ratio is one, so it commutes with J and the preceding meridian is EXACTLY J.

In the actual Hurwitz convention the NEGATIVE adjacent move is

(J,U) -> (U,U J U^-1).

Thus the indicated sequence carries U LEFT UNCHANGED. Placing this sequence P and then its inverse BEFORE the old action word means the literal operator R_beta R_P^-1 R_P. This order matters. Opening the first physical identity wire between the two words supplies the claimed prefix V=I and signed first-slot index -1. The original cut, its input/output source and all existing caps remain in place.

Actual R inverses, including their full Cartan/scalar marks, give identity reclosure at every level. The added writhe is zero. The original d_N(M) line is unchanged as a rational map/lattice, not replaced by its complete value. The same inverse identity holds in the supplied whole finite auxiliary lift, hence with all its normal derivatives. None of these identities requires extending an auxiliary source-frame deformation to a knot representation.

The cut is at slot ell=0, so the actual open meridian endpoint factor q^(-ell nu) is one. This removes that PORT character only. The original x=-M/q, gamma_N framing power, full g_N and every independent peripheral power remain, just as stated in the note.

2. Every added atom is regular on a nonempty common open

Write

U=I+c[-beta;1] * (1,beta), t=r+beta s,
a_h=1+hs,
b_h=a_h+(c/s)(1-t)(t+hs).

The displayed b_h equals (F^-1 U J^h F)_11 by direct multiplication. Before each negative move the prefixes are Jh,J(h+1),U J^(h+1), so its actual clocks and transfer are exactly

A=a_h/a_(h+1), B=a_(h+1)/b_(h+1),
C=a_(h+1)b_h/[b_(h+1)a_h].

The five atoms are A,C,1/B,AC,C/B. Besides their nonzero numerator and denominator factors, their differences from one are controlled by

1-A=s/a_(h+1),
1-C=c(1-t)^2/[b_(h+1)a_h],
b_h-a_h=(c/s)(1-t)(t+hs),
b_(h+1)-a_(h+1)=(c/s)(1-t)(t+(h+1)s),
b_(h+1)-b_h=s+c(1-t).

Each excluded function is nonzero because c is nonzero and t,s are independent framing coordinates. Their finite product therefore defines a nonempty Zariski open. The framing surface is irreducible, so it intersects the original nonempty regular chart. This is a general nonemptiness proof, not inference from the figure-eight tests.

For the inverse positive move, A’=B/C,B’=AC,C’=1/C. Its atoms are AC,1/C,C/B,A,1/B. Thus it adds no further conditions. All inverse paths are actual reversed graphs of these same moves.

For a two-POSITION transverse chart also exclude 1-2t, since the new clock has r derivative (c/s)(1-2t). This is another nonzero factor and leaves a nonempty open. The Fourier (A,p) chart itself has derivative 1/t^2 and resolves that position caustic.

3. The Morse normal block persists on this open

The existing actual phase/critical dictionary is an isomorphism of the exponentiated critical scheme with the framed representation germ on its unit-atom charts, including local Artin rings. Adding the crossing graphs and their inverses eliminates their intermediate states uniquely and recovers the OLD fixed representation equations. Thus the new critical germ still has precisely its two B_1 tangent modes. The added 2 length(P) flow variables increase the Hessian rank by that same number. Every VARIABLE principal amplitude is a unit on the constructed open, with its original base-only line retained separately, so its transverse formal Gaussian block remains nonzero.

The generating-family stabilization wording has an explicit twist unit, not an additional unproved determinant hypothesis. In the original input/output position coordinates the exact mixed Hessians from the actual coefficients are

V_-=C/(1-C), V_+=1/(1-C').

For C’=1/C these satisfy V_+=-V_-. They are units on the atom open C!=0,1. Consequently the extra cross block has the indicated form [[0,mu],[mu,*]], with mu a unit up to its unit coordinate factor, and determinant -mu^2. No additional excluded function or singular quantum-gap division is required here.

More explicitly, the inverse-pair generating phase can be written F(y,q)-F(y,q+delta)+S_open(z,delta), with its actual inverse marks. The y equation forces delta=0 because F_yq is a unit; the delta equation then fixes y from the old momentum. Its reduced critical phase is S_open(z,0)=S_old(z), and the added (y,delta) Hessian has determinant -F_yq^2. The intermediate position and net displacement are unimodular integer coordinates in the two added transfers. This gives the direct stabilization proof recorded in the separate word/source audit.

The original marked action calibration continues to apply to the new admissible diagram. The inverse path cancels its canonical graph and relative contribution, with the fixed phase/base line retained. This does NOT prove equality of every old/new formal coefficient in arbitrary frames, nor a global Gaussian/source pushforward. Finite matrix inversion alone would not justify those stronger statements.

4. The new complete prefix and density are genuinely universal

Direct multiplication gives

F^-1 U F=I+(c/s)[1-t;-t] * (t,1-t),
k=1+(c/s)t(1-t).

With the ACTUAL balanced/factorial momentum and first-slot sign,

p=-(F^-1UF)_12/(k-1)=1-1/t,
r=1/(1-p)-beta s.

Thus at completeness the framing map is birational, without a new quadratic cover, and

Omega_cut=-dr wedge ds/[s(1+s)]
   =-p/[s(1-p)^2]dlog A_cut wedge dlog p.

The pole p=1 belongs to the INVERSE framing map and density; the momentum p(r) itself has its pole at t=0. The universal density pole is at r=infinity and lies outside the proved fixed regular framing chart. At xi=p^-1 it is the fixed Fourier node xi=1, present at every N. This exact principal formula has not been promoted to a generic-M double-pole formula or a substitute for the full quantum source.

The regular-node sum (N^2-1)/(12s) is correct. It excludes precisely the singular node and is not the selected knot period. Higher coefficients, original shape-chart exclusions and relative current endpoints still need their whole completion. The source-preserving word change therefore gives a useful uniform critical target, but does not close gates C, D or E.
