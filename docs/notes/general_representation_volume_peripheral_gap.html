A uniform representation-volume gap for fixed peripheral words

30 October 2026. This extends the supplied natural-map proof to a varying sequence of representations. No quantum source estimate follows from this geometric statement alone.

Let X=Gamma^3 be the fixed oriented finite-volume complete hyperbolic exterior, with its fixed marked complete PSL2 holonomy iota. For each n let rho_n:Gamma->SL2(C), followed by its PSL2 action, and let Vol(rho_n) have the same canonical peripheral-regulator normalization as in that note. Then

Vol(rho_n) -> Vol(X)

implies, after passing to a subsequence and conjugating by orientation-preserving target isometries g_n, that

g_n rho_n(gamma) g_n^-1 -> iota(gamma)             (1)

for every fixed gamma in Gamma, with one common conjugation and the actual marking. A fixed final conjugation is absorbed into g_n. For absolute volumes tending to Vol(X), pass to a constant-sign subsequence; the limit is either iota or its orientation-reversed conjugate.

Proof with nonuniform cusp data

Choose any s_n>2 tending to2. For rho_n choose its own cusp-adapted map f_n and its natural map F_n=F_(s_n,f_n). The cusp estimates are used separately at the fixed pair (s_n,rho_n), and prove exactly

integral_X Jac F_n = Vol(rho_n).

No uniform cusp constants, uniform cusp truncation, or exchange of a cusp limit with n is needed. The radial construction, however, gives uniform INTERIOR estimates independent of f_n:

|Jac F_n| <= (s_n/2)^3,
AA^T <= H/3,     (I-H) DF_n=s_n A,
||D_x nu_n||_TV <= s_n,
||D_x^2 nu_n||_TV <= 3s_n^2/2.

The latter bounds differentiate only the domain radial kernel. Therefore

integral_X [(s_n/2)^3-Jac F_n] ->0.             (2)

This is a nonnegative deficit. On any fixed compact lifted ball it tends to zero in L1 as well: the projection has bounded finite multiplicity on that ball. Choose points with Jacobians tending to1. The strict matrix inequality forces H->I/3 and all singular values of DF_n to1 there. Normalize their target images by orientation-preserving isometries g_n.

The same fixed-radius Hessian bootstrap as in the frozen note now gives uniform C2 bounds near those points: positivity of I-H controls DF_n; its first variation is bounded by C(1+||DF_n||), and the uniform second measure derivative controls D2F_n. A C1 subsequential limit has Jacobian1 everywhere by (2). The strict matrix inequality forces its derivative to be an orientation-preserving isometry. This convergence propagates along finite chains of overlapping fixed-radius balls; target values in the new ball stay bounded by the known overlap and derivative bound. A diagonal exhaustion gives

G_n=g_n F_n -> G_infinity locally in C1 on H^3,

where G_infinity is a global orientation-preserving hyperbolic isometry. This construction does not assume compactness or irreducibility of the representations and does not claim that the normalizing g_n are bounded.

It remains to use the equivariance, rather than only kernel invariance. Set rho’_n=g_n rho_n g_n^-1. For every fixed gamma and x,

rho'_n(gamma) G_n(x)=G_n(gamma x).              (3)

Choose four fixed source points whose G_infinity-images are noncoplanar. Both sets of points in (3) converge. The isometries rho’_n(gamma) are precompact: their values at one fixed target point are bounded, because that point is o(1) from G_n(x), and the stabilizer of a point is compact. Every limiting isometry sends the four G_infinity(x)-points to their G_infinity(gamma x)-images. These determine an isometry uniquely, so

rho'_n(gamma) -> G_infinity iota(gamma) G_infinity^-1.

Absorb G_infinity^-1 in the common g_n. This proves (1) simultaneously for every fixed gamma; it also suffices to check a finite generating set. The SL2 central signs need not stabilize, and are immaterial for trace squared. No assertion about their lift is made.

Uniform peripheral gap in the marked finite-dimensional problem

Fix finitely many actual peripheral words m_1,…,m_b and delta>0. Suppose each iota(m_i) is parabolic or central, so tr^2 iota(m_i)=4. Then there is g=g(X,{m_i},delta)>0 such that EVERY actual SL2 representation satisfying

max_i |tr^2 rho(m_i)-4| >= delta

has

|Vol(rho)| <= Vol(X)-g.                         (4)

Otherwise a sequence violating every positive gap would have absolute volumes tending to Vol(X). Apply the proved compactness statement, passing to a constant-sign subsequence. Trace squared is continuous and conjugacy-invariant; its value at every selected word would tend to4 in either orientation, contradicting the fixed delta. This proof supplies a positive gap, not an effective numerical value.

For a physical peripheral eigenvalue m=e^(pi i chi) with real chi,

|tr^2 rho(m_i)-4|=4 sin^2(pi chi).

Thus a selected real color parameter a fixed distance from the integer aliases meets (4), IF it is the eigenvalue of an actual representation whose volume controls the relevant source period. The latter implication is NOT proved here: it requires the source action, its cycle, its upper bound, and cancellation of all ordinary complementary terms to be established separately. This statement is not a bound on F_N or L_h.

The fixed finite set is essential. One cannot take a supremum over ALL peripheral words whose exponents are allowed to grow with n: powers of a near-parabolic element may have trace far from ±2. The actual finite set of marked crossing-circle meridians is within the theorem’s scope.

Validation and scope

Root full analytical review: PASS. The review checked the uniform interior measure estimates despite varying cusp maps, the fixed-pair volume identity, normalized isometry propagation, the use of exact equivariance to recover the marked character, and the finite-fixed-word quantifier. No numerical test substitutes for this analytic argument. The source action/period upper bound and the complementary-color cancellation remain separate requirements.

Independent full analytical review: PASS. The reviewer checked the fixed-pair cusp normalization versus representation-independent interior estimates, the propagated isometric limit, convergence of the conjugated isometries from four noncoplanar points, and the fixed finite peripheral quantifier. The absolute-volume orientation alternative and the source limitations remain explicit. The theorem is frozen after these reviews.
