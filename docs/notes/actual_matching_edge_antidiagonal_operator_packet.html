The original physical operator kernel and its positive anti-diagonal packet

30 October 2026. The actual pivotal reshuffle turns an anti-diagonal entry of the completed exceptional Casimir polynomial into the squared modulus of ONE original ordinary column, with an explicit positive factor. The same exact identity factors every entry within its bandwidth into two ordinary columns. It supplies a literal holomorphic root-kernel continuation and its nonzero relative amplitude. Both level parities are included, without a spectral contour argument. The surrounding coherent contraction and its global selected frame remain additional requirements; genuine q normals are bounded here only at physical rows.

1. Exact original root identity

Put d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), s_n=product_(r=1)^n 2sin(pi r/N), and 0<=a<=floor(d/2). Let J_t be the ORIGINAL physical Casimir block in total degree 0<=t<=d, in its original order u=0,…,t. Let p_a be the original exceptional polynomial. Choose u<=v with u+v=t and set h=v-u. If h>a, then

[p_a(J_t)]_(u,v)=0.                              (1)

If 0<=h<=a, write f_(d-h,a)(u) for the original normalized physical column with first entry one, in degree d-h and compiler channel a. Then the exact finite identity is

[p_a(J_t)]_(u,v)
   =s_(a+h)/(s_h^2 s_(a-h)) |f_(d-h,a)(u)|^2.    (2)

No projectors or target channels have been discarded in (2). The polynomial is the completed operator, including its root nilpotent parts. The root face factor is D_a(q)=1. The opposite anti-diagonal entry equals (2), since the original root J_t is symmetric under transpose. The high physical total uses the already proved exact generic translated adapter, and hence the same formula in its low coordinates.

Here is the full source and phase proof. Use the actual maps of the completed projector theorem:

B(K)_(a,b,c,e)=K_(a,b;d-e,d-c) pV(d-e)pV(d-c),
(R Psi)_(a,b,c,e)=s(a) Psi_(b,c,e,a),
s(j)=(-1)^d q^(d-2j), A=B^-1 R B.

In the first line pV(j)=pV(j,d-j), and dummy outside labels are unrelated to the channel a. Direct inversion, retaining the nested cup order, gives for every physical operator K

(A^-1 K)_(a,b;x,y)
  =s(d-x)^-1 pV(d-b)/pV(x)
                      K_(d-x,a;y,d-b).           (3)

The original measure cancellation is h0_a K_a=k_a N A p_a(Omega), where h0_a=k_a N s_(2a)/s_a^2 and K_a=W_a pi_a. On the chosen anti-diagonal, (3) therefore gives

[p_a(J_t)]_(u,v)
   =s_(2a)/s_a^2 s(d-v)^-1 pV(d-v)/pV(v)
                       K_a(d-v,u;u,d-v).          (4)

At the root the actual physical cup is

pV(j)=N q^(-d^2+dj).

Consequently s(d-v)^-1 pV(d-v)/pV(v)=1 EXACTLY: its remaining exponent is N(d-2v), and its sign cancels (-1)^d. The original white map, in channel row r=a-h, is

pi_a(r;x,y)=G_(a,r)^-1 conjugate(W_a(y,x;r)),
G_(a,r)=s_(2a)/(s_r s_(2a-r)).

Thus (4) is s_(a-h)s_(a+h)/s_a^2 times |W_a(d-v,u;a-h)|^2. The channel row is absent if h>a, proving (1) independently of polynomial bandwidth.

When h<=a, this Clebsch column has low degree d-h. Its actual first endpoint has modulus s_a/(s_h s_(a-h)). The root reflected recurrence gives |f_(d-h,a)(d-v)|=|f_(d-h,a)(u)|, because those two indices add to d-h. Substitution proves (2). In particular d-h>=d-a automatically lies in the original complete-column range; no unsupported ordinary formula at a different degree is invoked.

2. A uniform nonzero relative operator amplitude

Write the ACTUAL scaled parameters

alpha=a/N, delta=h/N, nu=u/N,
tau_prime=(d-h)/N=1-delta-1/N.

Consider any fixed compact inside

0<delta<alpha<=1/2, 0<nu<(1-delta)/2.            (5)

As usual alpha denotes an admissible discrete sequence; its endpoint 1/2 is interpreted by the actual folded sequences a/N. Physical u+v<=d is retained. The transformed ordinary arguments stay in a fixed compact of the full physical tube, since tau_prime>1/2 and 0<nu<tau_prime. The original total (u+v)/N may be BELOW 1/2.

The proved ordinary full-domain packet has the actual, nonzero amplitude B_alpha and phase Phi_alpha:

f_(d-h,a)(u)=N^-1/2 B_alpha(tau_prime,nu)
      exp[N Phi_alpha(tau_prime,nu)](1+O(N^-1)).

Equation (2) now gives directly

[p_a(J_t)]_(u,v)
  =s_(a+h)/(N s_h^2 s_(a-h)) |B_alpha(tau_prime,nu)|^2
     exp[2N Re Phi_alpha(tau_prime,nu)](1+O(N^-1)).           (6)

The error is uniform on (5), and the leading factor is positive and nonzero. Both integer offsets remain in tau_prime and in B; there is no replacement of tau_prime by 1-delta inside the N-scaled exponential.

For an entirely smooth amplitude, put J(x)=integral_0^x log(2sin(pi s)) ds. The uniform sine-factorial formula s_(Nx)=sqrt(2N sin(pi x)) exp[N J(x)](1+O(N%5E-1)) on compact subsets of 0<x<1 rewrites (6) as

[p_a(J_t)]_(u,v)=N^-2 C_alpha(delta,tau_prime,nu)
                     exp[N H_alpha(delta,tau_prime,nu)]
                     (1+O(N^-1)),                         (7)

H_alpha=J(alpha+delta)-2J(delta)-J(alpha-delta)
                     +2Re Phi_alpha(tau_prime,nu),
C_alpha=|B_alpha(tau_prime,nu)|^2/(2sin(pi delta))
                 *sqrt(sin(pi(alpha+delta))/sin(pi(alpha-delta))).

All scalar arguments in (7) remain strictly between zero and one on the stated compact. This is an actual local matrix-entry asymptotic, rather than a scalar-symbol substitution or a spectral stationary-phase conjecture.

3. Genuine q jets come from the generic reshuffle

One MUST NOT differentiate the absolute square in (2). The original generic identity is instead

p_a(Omega)=D_a/(rho_a beta_N) B^-1 R^-1 S_(2a),
S_(2a)=W_a p_(2a) W_a^T.                         (8)

Every anti-diagonal entry of the right side has exactly ONE channel row r=a-h and its complement 2a-r. The two Clebsch entries, original ordinary cup, physical inverse cups, turn, and scalar in (8) retain their genuine q dependence. There is no sum in this entry which could hide cancellation between exponentially larger summands.

The physical all-q-jet theorem bounds each fixed jet of the normalized physical columns by a polynomial times its root modulus. Their original Clebsch endpoint factors and the cups in (8) are root-unit products with harmonic logarithmic derivative bounds. The generic high adapter is exact, so it applies to the second, high-degree Clebsch entry as well. The scalar D_a/(rho_a beta_N) is a nonzero canceled root-unit product; its fixed relative jets have polynomial cost by the original normalization theorem.

Therefore, for each fixed m, on (5) there are constants C_m,L_m independent of the physical labels and level parity such that

|partial_epsilon^m [p_a(Omega(q exp(epsilon)))]_(u,v)|_0
      <=C_m N^L_m [p_a(J_t(q))]_(u,v).            (9)

All derivatives in (9) include the q dependence of the polynomial AND of the original matrix. The completed face-normalized operator has the same bound. This is an upper bound for genuine normals, not their nonzero amplitude. The analogous entry remains identically zero outside |u-v|<=a at generic q, by bandwidth.

4. The entire physical kernel, with its original phase

The same proof also gives every entry. Let 0<=u<=v<=t<=d, h=v-u, and 0<=h<=a. Then

[p_a(J_t)]_(u,v)
  =q^[-d(t-u-v)] s_(a+h)/(s_h^2 s_(a-h))
         *conjugate(f_(d-h,a)(u)) f_(d-h,a)(t-v).           (10)

Here (3) uses the original entry K_a(d-v,u;t-v,d-t+u), in degree d-h. Its white reversal gives W_a(d-v,u) times conjugate(W_a(d-t+u,t-v)), divided by the same G_(a,a-h). The root reflected recurrence is

f_(d-h,a)(d-h-r)=f_(d-h,a)(d-h)
                                  conjugate(f_(d-h,a)(r)).

The two endpoint factors cancel because the final normalized endpoint has modulus one. The remaining cup and turn ratio in (3) is exactly q1. This proves (10), including its phase. Entries below the diagonal follow by transpose symmetry of the ROOT J_t.

Both arguments u and t-v are actual physical arguments in the same complete column. The generic tensor corresponding to (10) still has only one channel row. Consequently the genuine-jet bound (9) holds for every strict physical entry covered below, with the positive right side replaced by the modulus of (10). This uses (8), not differentiation of the conjugation or root reflection in (10).

There is a useful finite product for its entire scalar. The exact root identity s_h s_(d-h)=N gives

s_(a+h)/(s_h^2 s_(a-h))
   =P_(N,a)(h)
   :=N^-1 product_(r=1)^(d-a) 2sin[pi(h+r)/N]
            *product_(r=1)^a 2sin[pi(h+r)/N].               (11)

For h<=a, cancel the two products using s_(d-a+h)=N/s_(a-h). For each physical integer a<h<=d, the first product instead contains its factor with h+r=N, hence vanishes. Thus (11) is a literal finite entire function, not an interpolation inferred from the leading sine-factorial rate.

5. A source-matching complex root kernel and relative amplitude

Use independent scaled variables delta,nu,lambda, corresponding on the physical grid to

h=N delta, u=N nu, t-v=N lambda,
v=N(nu+delta), t=N(nu+lambda+delta).

For fixed actual alpha=a/N, take compact subsets of the convex tube

0<Re delta<alpha, Re nu>0, Re lambda>0,
                   Re(nu+lambda+delta)<1.                   (12)

The channel remains an integer. Put tau_prime=1-delta-1/N. For all large N, (tau_prime,nu) and (tau_prime,lambda) lie in fixed compacts of the proved full physical ordinary tube. If fhat denotes that ORIGINAL finite continuation, define

fhat_a^#(tau,nu)=conjugate(fhat_a(conjugate(tau),conjugate(nu))),

where fhat_a(tau,nu) is understood to have the scaled arguments (Nnu,N(tau-nu)). This is a holomorphic function, not pointwise conjugation at complex arguments. Define the finite root kernel

K_(N,a)(delta,nu,lambda)
  =exp[-pi i(N-1)(lambda-nu)] P_(N,a)(N delta)
        fhat_a^#(tau_prime,nu) fhat_a(tau_prime,lambda).      (13)

Equation (10) proves EXACT equality with the original physical matrix entry at every grid point in (12). All factors of (13) are explicit original finite functions or the finite product (11). No noninteger target channel or arbitrary interpolation is used. For large N the ordinary factors are nowhere zero on these compacts. Every sine argument in (11) has real part strictly between zero and one there, so the scalar is nonzero as well.

Let J be the holomorphic continuation of the real primitive of log(2sin(pi z)) in 0<Re z<1. Use the branch real on (0,1). Write Phi_alpha^# and B_alpha^# with the same holomorphic-conjugate convention. Put

H=J(alpha+delta)-2J(delta)-J(alpha-delta)
     +Phi_alpha^#(tau_prime,nu)+Phi_alpha(tau_prime,lambda)
     -pi i(lambda-nu),
C=exp[pi i(lambda-nu)] B_alpha^#(tau_prime,nu)
        B_alpha(tau_prime,lambda)/(2sin(pi delta))
         *sqrt(sin(pi(alpha+delta))/sin(pi(alpha-delta))).   (14)

The square root is its positive real branch continued on (12). Then the ACTUAL finite function (13) satisfies

K_(N,a)=N^-2 C exp(N H)(1+O(N^-1)).             (15)

The amplitude is holomorphic and nowhere zero. The relative error and all its fixed derivatives are uniform on smaller compacts. The actual value alpha=a/N and the offset tau_prime=1-delta-1/N are retained throughout (14),(15).

For completeness, no complex factorial interpolation is needed to prove the scalar part. Apply the elementary trapezoidal estimate to the TWO literal sums of log sine in (11). The first upper index is d-a=N(1-alpha)-1; the second is a=N alpha. Their integrals add to J(alpha+delta)-2J(delta)-J(alpha-delta), using J(1-z)=-J(z). Their endpoint terms give exactly

P_(N,a)(N delta)
  =N^-1 sqrt(sin(pi(alpha+delta))/sin(pi(alpha-delta)))
               /(2sin(pi delta))
    *exp{N[J(alpha+delta)-2J(delta)-J(alpha-delta)]}
                                        *(1+O(N^-1)).

All derivatives of the summands are bounded on the compact enlarged strip, so the error is uniform and holomorphic. Multiplying the TWO proved actual ordinary packets proves (15), with its exact monomial phase. The real anti-diagonal formula (7) is its lambda=nu slice.

This complex continuation is for the ROOT kernel. It does not by itself continue the genuine generic-q normals of (8) to complex magnetic rows, nor identify (13)’s branch at a particular geometric frame. Those are separate requirements.

6. What this does and does not resolve

The anti-diagonal completed operator has a source-exact positive formula and its own nonzero relative local packet. In this chamber one no longer needs to prove that a chosen off-spectrum saddle contributes merely to compute this individual matrix entry.

An arbitrary surrounding tensor is not anti-diagonal. Placing the actual geometric boundary data in these coordinates, matching its selected action and derivatives, and proving the coherent global contraction still require their own arguments. Nor does the root positivity in (2) replace the original whole-network first normal by a positive root-value state sum. In particular, compatibility of a local real target frame with the selected frame on every other original factor is not supplied by this kernel identity.

The exact global tube obstruction makes this limitation concrete. Under the presently used global shaded-exterior flags and controlled ordinary complement, undoing the actual turn maps all of (12) back to two strictly physical original factors. The resulting all-physical frame is excluded by the proved white-normal obstruction. Thus this valid root-kernel packet does NOT contain that prescribed global geometric point. A larger continuation domain, another coupled grouping, or an exact local frame-transition construction is still required.
