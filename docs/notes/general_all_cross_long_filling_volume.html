The entire original all-CROSS Fourier period from its local packet and global spectral gap

Independently reviewed analytical theorem, 31 October 2026. This concerns the actual all-CROSS shared numerator and its whole original Fourier/Hermite period. The two source estimates used below have separate proofs. All twists are independently signed, sufficiently long, and fixed before the level tends to infinity. The remaining moving one-sided sectors of the full numerator are not estimated here.

1. Exact source, hypotheses, and conclusion

Fix the original certified marked flat plat DD with RR ports, d=N−1d=N-1 and q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}. In this note write CN(𝜹)C_N(\boldsymbol\delta) for the raw shared all-CROSS coefficient

where CNpositiveC_N^{\mathrm{positive}} is the sign-adjusted coefficient formed from Pcross=(−1)dHVN,crossP_{\mathrm{cross}}=(-1)^dH_{V_N,\mathrm{cross}} in the preceding cofactor/global-gap notes. The spectral arguments are σi=−2cos⁡(2πδi)\sigma_i=-2\cos(2\pi\delta_i), with both parity definitions of CROSS retained. The absolute global gap is unchanged by this exact sign. The raw source is an entire, separately even, one-periodic function of each δi\delta_i, obtained from a polynomial of degree at most dd in each σi\sigma_i. It is not an independently optimized product of local norms. In particular the sign (−1)dR(-1)^{dR} in (2) belongs to this raw convention, not to the preceding positive CNC_N.

Every ni=ϵi−2pin_i=\epsilon_i-2p_i is its actual nonzero fixed integer crossing power. 2. Basic bounds, endpoint identities, and parameter choices

The local packet and real concavity bound near zero, together with (H2) outside a smaller fixed cube, imply

sup𝜹real|CN(𝜹)|≤eN(ΦD+eN),eN→0. \sup_{\boldsymbol\delta\ \mathrm{real}}|C_N(\boldsymbol\delta)| \leq e^{N(\Phi_D+e_N)},\qquad e_N\longrightarrow0.\tag{5}

Polynomial Bernstein on each cosine variable yields, for any fixed TT,

|CN(𝜹)|≤exp⁡[N(ΦD+eN)+2πd∑i|Imδi|],|Im⁡δi|≤T. |C_N(\boldsymbol\delta)| \leq\exp\!\left[N(\Phi_D+e_N)+2\pi d\sum_i|\operatorname{Im}\delta_i|\right], \qquad |\operatorname{Im}\delta_i|\leq T.\tag{6}

This is a bound for the whole shared polynomial; no factorwise positive-radius majorant is used. For source derivatives, apply Cauchy to (6) in the required variables. Gaussian derivatives are evaluated separately on REAL spectator coordinates, avoiding any unwanted large-twist exponential cost.

If a source factor ZZ is an arbitrary entire polynomial in the σi\sigma_i, its product with ∏isin⁡(2πδi)\prod_i\sin(2\pi\delta_i) and the fixed Gaussian satisfies

B|δj=±1/2=0,∂jB|δj=1/2=∂jB|δj=−1/2. \left.B\right|_{\delta_j=\pm1/2}=0,\qquad \left.\partial_j B\right|_{\delta_j=1/2} =\left.\partial_j B\right|_{\delta_j=-1/2}.\tag{7}

The first derivative is solely the derivative of the sine: the two cosines are both −1-1, the spectral argument is 22 at both ends, and the Gaussian endpoint value is eπiNnj/4e^{\pi iNn_j/4}. These facts remain true after arbitrary derivatives in other variables, including when one different coordinate is held complex. Thus three integrations by parts give exactly

∫−1/21/2Be2πiNℓjδjdδj=(2πiNℓj)−3{[∂j2Be2πiNℓjδj]−1/21/2−∫−1/21/2∂j3Be2πiNℓjδjdδj}. \begin{aligned} \int_{-1/2}^{1/2}B e^{2\pi iN\ell_j\delta_j}\,d\delta_j =(2\pi iN\ell_j)^{-3}\biggl\{ &\left[\partial_j^2B e^{2\pi iN\ell_j\delta_j}\right]_{-1/2}^{1/2}\\ &-\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}\partial_j^3B e^{2\pi iN\ell_j\delta_j}\,d\delta_j \biggr\}. \end{aligned}\tag{8}

The second-derivative endpoint difference is retained. Repeating (8) over any spectator subset gives at most 3R−13^{R-1} boundary/integral terms. Fixed source Cauchy bounds and real Gaussian derivatives cost at most C𝒏N3C_{\mathbf n}N^3 per differentiated variable; these powers cancel the N3N^3 denominators. After the actual charge factors, every spectator coordinate supplies |ℓj|−2|\ell_j|^{-2}.

Choose a small fixed outer cube KB=[−B,B]RK_B=[-B,B]^R inside (H1). Choose a>0a>0 much smaller than BB so the localized contribution theorem applies uniformly for all positive charges ℓi/|ni|≤a\ell_i/|n_i|\leq a. Next choose 0<b<a/40<b<a/4, with KbK_b inside the same chart. All these constants depend only on DD. Below n0n_0 will be enlarged finitely many times, independently of ratios among the |ni||n_i|.

3. The infinite far-charge union is exponentially small

Use (H2) at η=1/4\eta=1/4. Choose τ>0\tau>0 sufficiently small that the source and all required spectator derivatives have a strict bound eN(ΦD−γe)e^{N(\Phi_D-\gamma_e)} at

δi=±12+iv,0≤v≤τ. \delta_i=\pm\frac12+iv,\qquad 0\leq v\leq\tau.

with every other coordinate real or in a fixed Cauchy neighborhood of radius τ\tau. This uses the separated endpoint coordinate, regardless of the other real coordinates. Shrink τ\tau and γe\gamma_e once if needed.

For a positive far charge ℓi\ell_i, shift its whole interval upwards by iτi\tau, keeping all other integrations on their whole real intervals. Write

Bi(z;𝜹other)=sin⁡(2πz)eπiniNz2CN(z,𝜹other). B_i(z;\boldsymbol\delta_{\mathrm{other}}) =\sin(2\pi z)e^{\pi i n_iNz^2}C_N(z,\boldsymbol\delta_{\mathrm{other}}).

The exact pair of endpoint currents is

Bi(1/2+iv)−Bi(−1/2+iv)=2isinh⁡(2πv)sinh⁡(πniNv)×eπiniN(1/4−v2)CN(1/2+iv,𝜹other). \begin{aligned} B_i(1/2+iv)-B_i(-1/2+iv) ={}&2i\sinh(2\pi v)\sinh(\pi n_iNv)\\ &\quad\times e^{\pi i n_iN(1/4-v^2)} C_N(1/2+iv,\boldsymbol\delta_{\mathrm{other}}). \end{aligned}\tag{9}

The other sine/Gaussian factors remain outside this equality. Multiplying the actual Fourier character leaves the endpoint parity (−1)ℓi(-1)^{\ell_i}. Its modulus is one. Both vertical sides were retained; the paired factor vanishes quadratically at v=0v=0.

For the paired vertical contribution, apply (8) in every spectator coordinate. The strict endpoint strip bound and source Cauchy estimates then give, after all original charge weights, an upper bound

C𝒏|ni|NℓieN(ΦD−γe)(∏j≠i|ℓj|−2)∫0τv2e−2πN(ℓi−|ni|/2)vdv. C_{\mathbf n}|n_i|N\ell_i\,e^{N(\Phi_D-\gamma_e)} \left(\prod_{j\ne i}|\ell_j|^{-2}\right) \int_0^\tau v^2 e^{-2\pi N(\ell_i-|n_i|/2)v}\,dv.\tag{10}

Here |sinh⁡(2πv)sinh⁡(πniNv)|≤C|ni|Nv2eπ|ni|Nv|\sinh(2\pi v)\sinh(\pi n_iNv)|\leq C|n_i|Nv^2e^{\pi|n_i|Nv}. No oscillatory-sine substitution is made. For ℓi>|ni|/2+D\ell_i>|n_i|/2+D, with D≥1D\geq1, the integral is at most C/[N3(ℓi−|ni|/2)3]C/[N^3(\ell_i-|n_i|/2)^3]. Consequently summing (10) over this far coordinate and all nonzero spectator charges is bounded by a polynomial factor times eN(ΦD−γe)e^{N(\Phi_D-\gamma_e)}. The exact estimate

∑ℓ>Mℓ(ℓ−|n|/2)3≤C[D−1+(|n|+1)D−2]. \sum_{\ell>M}\frac{\ell}{(\ell-|n|/2)^3} \leq C\left[D^{-1}+(|n|+1)D^{-2}\right].\tag{11}

makes the summability explicit. Constants may depend on the fixed 𝒏\mathbf n.

For the upper horizontal contribution, apply the same spectator integrations by parts. Source Cauchy from (6), in a fixed radius-τ\tau neighborhood of the spectators, has exponential cost at most 2πRτN2\pi R\tau N. The shifted sine is bounded, while the bad-coordinate Gaussian and Fourier factors cost exactly at most

eπ|ni|Nτ−2πℓiNτ. e^{\pi|n_i|N\tau-2\pi\ell_iN\tau}.

Thus the weighted horizontal contribution is bounded by

C𝒏ℓi(∏j≠i|ℓj|−2)exp⁡{N[ΦD+eN+2πRτ−2πτ(ℓi−|ni|/2)]}. C_{\mathbf n}\ell_i\left(\prod_{j\ne i}|\ell_j|^{-2}\right) \exp\!\left\{N\left[\Phi_D+e_N+2\pi R\tau -2\pi\tau(\ell_i-|n_i|/2)\right]\right\}.\tag{12}

Choose a fixed DD large enough that 2πτD>2πRτ+2γe2\pi\tau D>2\pi R\tau+2\gamma_e, and set

Mi=⌈|ni|2+D⌉. M_i=\left\lceil\frac{|n_i|}{2}+D\right\rceil.\tag{13}

The geometric tail of ℓi\ell_i times (12) is exponentially bounded by ΦD−γe\Phi_D-\gamma_e after a harmless decrease of γe\gamma_e and all large N. Both signs of the far charge obey the same estimate, either by downward shifts or the exact separate-evenness/reflection identity.

Therefore, for some fixed γ∞>0\gamma_\infty>0,

∑ℓ∈(ℤ\{0})R∃i:|ℓi|>Mi|ℐN,ℓcross|≤C𝒏poly⁡(N)eN(ΦD−γ∞). \sum_{\substack{\boldsymbol\ell\in(\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\})^R\\ \exists i:\ |\ell_i|>M_i}} \left|\mathcal I_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}^{\mathrm{cross}}\right| \leq C_{\mathbf n}\operatorname{poly}(N)e^{N(\Phi_D-\gamma_\infty)}.\tag{14}

Here ℐN,ℓcross\mathcal I_{N,\boldsymbol\ell}^{\mathrm{cross}} denotes the full weighted charge term in (1). Use the union bound over ii; multiple far coordinates cause no issue. Every other coordinate was summed on its whole original domain after (8). Finite-N absolute convergence and the displayed dominating series justify all summations. No partial-far exterior with uncontrolled RR powers appears: the hypothesis used is the shared all-CROSS endpoint gap, valid for arbitrary real spectator spectra. This is the stronger input missing from the local operator-tail argument.

4. The finite charges outside the local scaled box

The remaining set 0<|ℓi|≤Mi0<|\ell_i|\leq M_i is finite after 𝒏\mathbf n is fixed. Suppose at least one coordinate ii has |ℓi|/|ni|>a|\ell_i|/|n_i|>a. Reflect charge signs if necessary; the source is separately even, so absolute estimates are unchanged and assume ℓi>0\ell_i>0.

On the original real domain outside KbK_b, (H2) gives rate ΦD−γb\Phi_D-\gamma_b. The Gaussian and Fourier factors there have modulus one. No contour estimate is needed for this finite-charge portion.

Inside KbK_b, shift only coordinate ii upward by

ti=b|ni|. t_i=\frac{b}{|n_i|}.\tag{15}

All actual side currents of this small rectangle are retained. The packet and its real-action Taylor bound give, uniformly for xx in KbK_b and 0≤v≤ti0\leq v\leq t_i,

Re⁡AN(x+ivei)≤AN(0)−c|x|2+Cv2. \operatorname{Re}A_N(x+iv e_i) \leq A_N(0)-c|x|^2+Cv^2.\tag{16}

Reality makes the first Taylor term purely imaginary. The Gaussian-plus-Fourier contribution in this coordinate is

−2πnixiv−2πℓiv≤−2π|ni|(a−b)v≤0. -2\pi n_ix_iv-2\pi\ell_iv \leq-2\pi|n_i|(a-b)v\leq0.\tag{17}

On the horizontal translate, v=tiv=t_i, this costs at least 2πb(a−b)2\pi b(a-b), while the source error is at most Cb2/n02Cb^2/n_0^2. On either vertical side |xi|=b|x_i|=b, (16) gives at least cb2cb^2 loss, and (17) is nonpositive. Enlarge n0n_0 so C/n02C/n_0^2 is small. Then the horizontal and both sides have a fixed strict gap γbad>0\gamma_{\mathrm{bad}}>0 below ΦD\Phi_D, independently of slope ratios. The packet’s amplitudes contribute only polynomial factors.

Together with the exterior KbK_b bound, every such bounded bad charge is exponentially small. There are finitely many of them, so their whole sum obeys the same strict rate with a fixed-n prefactor. No infinite sharp-cube series has been split.

5. Finite local charges and the actual unit contribution

It remains to consider the finite set with all |ℓi|/|ni|≤a|\ell_i|/|n_i|\leq a. On the real domain outside the larger KBK_B, (H2) gives a fixed loss γB\gamma_B. Inside KBK_B apply the localized original contour theorem to the packet (2). It retains the exact side currents, the finite action, the Gaussian determinant branch and all sine/charge/parity factors.

For positive charges the finite critical values satisfy

Re⁡SN,𝟏−Re⁡SN,ℓ≥∑iCi2ni2(ℓi2−1). \operatorname{Re}S_{N,\mathbf1}-\operatorname{Re}S_{N,\boldsymbol\ell} \geq\sum_i\frac{C_i}{2n_i^2}(\ell_i^2-1).\tag{18}

All sign-unit choices contribute exactly equally. Every other charge in this finite local box has a positive fixed-n gap. The all-unit local contribution is precisely (4), with its O𝒏(N−1)O_{\mathbf n}(N^{-1}) relative error and nonzero limiting amplitude. The half twists are included once through ni=ϵi−2pin_i=\epsilon_i-2p_i.

The limiting unit loss from ΦD\Phi_D is OD(∑i|ni|−2)O_D(\sum_i|n_i|^{-2}). Enlarge the diagram-dependent n0n_0 until this loss is less than one quarter of the minimum of γ∞\gamma_\infty, γbad\gamma_{\mathrm{bad}}, γb\gamma_b and γB\gamma_B. Also require an0≥1a n_0\geq1 so the unit charge lies in the local box. Since SN,𝟏S_{N,\mathbf1} tends to its limiting value, all complementary bounds above are then exponentially smaller than eNRe⁡SN,𝟏e^{N\operatorname{Re}S_{N,\mathbf1}} for large N. The smallest nonunit gap in (18) may depend on the fixed vector 𝒏\mathbf n, but remains positive. Combining the finite local sector, bounded bad charges and the absolutely bounded infinite union proves (4).

The exact critical volume identity identifies

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|Icross|=Vol⁡(the actual smooth filled exterior). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log|I_{\mathrm{cross}}| =\operatorname{Vol}(\text{the actual smooth filled exterior}).\tag{19}

There is no uniform joint growing-twist/level error assertion: the twists are fixed first, while the threshold applies to arbitrary independent signs and magnitudes.

6. Scope of the transfer

The argument proves a contribution theorem and all-charge control for the entire original all-CROSS spectral sector from the proved inputs (H1)-(H2). The original full numerator also contains moving one-sided sectors. The already established diagonal/CROSS and short-moving sector gaps can be retained separately, but they do not exhaust that remainder. Formula (19) is therefore not yet the general filled-knot invariant theorem.

The crucial new analytic point is (14): a uniform shared endpoint gap makes partial far-charge tails summable without changing the actual source or creating infinite sharp-cut subseries. The old polynomial +2+2 operator bound alone would not control the spectator environment. No generic-qq normal theorem or unproved choice of a Lefschetz coefficient is used.

Validation and scope

The full analytical transfer proof received independent root and geometry reviews. The review checked the raw versus sign-adjusted source convention, the paired hyperbolic-sine endpoint current, all spectator second-derivative endpoint terms, absolute summability of the partial-far union, the two distinct finite-charge cubes, and the original nonzero prefactor. No numerical audit is substituted for this analytic consequence. The theorem evaluates the entire all-CROSS sector; the moving one-sided remainder of the original full numerator remains outside its conclusion.
