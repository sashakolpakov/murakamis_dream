The full even Fejér endpoint source has the actual complete volume

31 October 2026. general endpoint-volume theorem. This is an endpoint theorem for the original positive polynomial numerator. It does not rename that numerator Kashaev’s invariant, prove its Gaussian filling transform, or assert the fixed long-filled knot conjecture.

1. Statement and normalization

Fix a connected original flat plat D, at any fixed bridge width, with R ports and its literal ordered face incidences (x_e,b_e,c_e,y_e). Retain the original two protected faces u,v and their MAIN edge. Assume the existing labeled convex ideal right-angled certificate for its actual untwisted augmented exterior Y_D. Thus Y_D is hyperbolic and its two original cut balls, white/shaded face pairings, crossing-circle matching and primitive markings are specified. No realization of an unrelated triangulation is sufficient for this hypothesis.

For even N put d=N-1 and use the original pair Casimir Omega_N and its full characteristic polynomial

chi_N(sigma)=2T_N(sigma/2)+2,
V_N(sigma)=chi_N(sigma)(sigma-Omega_N)^(-1),
P_N=-V_N(-2)=4(2+Omega_N)^(-1).

The middle expression is the actual polynomial numerator, including its repeated-node data. H is the original homogeneous coupon compiler. Set

Z_(D,N)=[prod_(f ordinary)L_f^d]
             prod_(e=1)^R H_(P_N)(x_e,b_e,c_e,y_e)
             |_(L_u=L_v=0).                       (1)

All coefficients in (1) are nonnegative. All source scalars and the marked coefficient targets are retained; no additional N^R is inserted. Then

lim_(even N->infinity) (2pi/N) log Z_(D,N)
                          =Vol(Y_D).              (2)

The limit runs through all even levels, including composite levels. Its hypotheses impose no bound on the fixed bridge width or number of ports. The geometric certificate and original flat-plat incidence are essential stated assumptions. This theorem concerns the complete augmented exterior, not a metric obtained by long filling.

2. One continuous entropy on the full local simplex

The closed local coefficient theorem gives the one-sided entropy

E(mu,nu,xi)=inf_l {integral_mu^nu g(t,l)dt-xi*l},
g(t,l)=l/2+log tan(pi*t)
                    +asinh(tan(pi*t)cosh(l/2)),
0<=mu<=nu<=1/2, 0<=xi<=nu-mu.

Its triangular endpoint is C(alpha)=E(alpha_b,1/2,alpha_x), for nonnegative alpha of sum1/2, and its ABSOLUTE normalization is

C(alpha)=V(2pi alpha)/(2pi),
V(theta)=2sum_j[Lambda(theta_j/2)+Lambda((pi-theta_j)/2)].

On q=(X,B,Cm,Y)>=0 of sum1 define h by the two E branches when Cm>=1/2 or B>=1/2, and by max[C(alpha)+C(beta)] over the actual cross split when B,Cm<=1/2. The branches agree at both terminal seams; h is continuous on the ENTIRE closed simplex and h(0,B,1-B,0)=0.

Every degree-d monomial is present in the full positive H_P. Uniformly over those monomials, its coefficient p_N(n) satisfies

N^-1 log p_N(n)=h(n/d)+o(1).                      (3)

This is the uniform full-kernel theorem, proved from the closed half-block estimates, positive cross convolution, the coefficientwise cross comparison and continuous seam values. It retains the finite forced outer factor. It does not require assigning a fixed half label to a limiting seam point.

Let Q_D be the compact nonnegative incidence-flow polytope: each port supplies1, each ordinary face demands1, and protected demands are0. Repeated local incidences remain distinct arcs. Scaling a flow by d and fractional-cycle rounding preserves exact integer balances with at most one unit of error per incidence. Every rounded local vector is in the full support. The number of summands is polynomial in N. Thus (3), positivity and compactness prove

lim_(even N) N^-1 log Z_(D,N)
   =W_D:=max_(q in Q_D) sum_e h(q_e).              (4)

The all-cross comparison and the complete source volume theorem give W_D>=Phi_D:=Vol(Y_D)/(2pi). It remains to prove the opposite inequality for a maximizer of (4).

3. Remove pure-middle maximizing ports by exact source identities

Choose a maximizer q, including maximizing triangle allocations at its cross ports. Let J consist of ALL ports with both outer loads zero. This includes one-sided diagonals and the pure-middle cross seam. The literal identity coupon is

H_1(b,c)=sum_(j=0)^d b^j c^(d-j).

It has entropy zero on the whole pure-middle segment, and coefficientwise H_1<=H_P. The mixed source with H_1 on J therefore has rate at most W_D. The SAME maximizing q is feasible for its mixed flow problem and has the same entropy. The full-kernel flow theorem with identity nodes gives the reverse inequality, so these rates are EXACTLY equal. No finite cross monomial with zero outer degree is invented.

The selected middle edges form a forest in the original gap graph. Every possible original middle cycle contains the protected exterior; its unit edge supplies would exceed its vertices’ total demand. A tree cannot contain both protected vertices either, by the same load count. For each remaining monomial, its identity degrees along a tree are uniquely determined by subtree balances and lie between0 and d. Consequently exact coefficient extraction contracts each identity tree to its ordinary/protected merged face, with coefficient one and no multiplicity. This is the actual connected identity smoothing.

The inherited maximizing local vectors at the remaining ports still have positive total outer load. A loop in the smoothed white cellulation would enclose v ordinary vertices but only v-1 whole ports and one half port. Its demand forces that half port’s middle load1, making it pure-middle, a contradiction. A retained matching edge between the two protected roots would also force a pure-middle port. Thus the remaining white triangular cellulation is loopless, with distinct adjacent protected roots. Multiple edges are retained. The empty source has rate zero and is handled separately; it cannot attain W_D>=Phi_D>0.

4. Hybrid Hall theory resolves all remaining forced boundary degrees

At the inherited maximum tag each B,Cm<=1/2 port CROSS, retaining its two triangle allocations. Every other port is GREEN, in a strict one-sided chamber with 0<=mu<nu<1/2. A CROSS port supplies two triangle nodes of mass1/2. A GREEN port supplies one node of mass1 incident to its four original faces. Its entropy is E, not two independent triangle entropies. A shared outer incidence is aggregated once for this node.

For an ordinary vertex set S, merging a triangle pair changes its Hall capacity by +1/2 exactly when one triangle touches S and the other does not. It never decreases capacity. The hybrid Hall theorem proves:

  • Every proper tight set is a union of actual digon interiors, after adjoining neighboring holes joined by a GREEN matching edge.
  • Their surviving cuts are MAIN/MAIN, or MATCHING/MATCHING with BOTH boundary ports CROSS. No GREEN pair is split.
  • These tight sets explain exactly the structurally forced zero arcs. Every other ordinary arc is positive in one common feasible witness.

For completeness, the counting behind this classification is exact. On the induced graph of U=V, let p count original triangle faces and n=|U|. Euler and boundary-walk length at least2 give p<=2n-4; equality forces a connected induced graph whose other faces are genuine digons. Hybrid equality additionally forbids a GREEN edge from separating a triangle touching S from one not touching S. Adjacent such holes merge along the same parallel-edge pair. For proper S these mergers are paths, not cycles. Thus the surviving cuts have precisely the types above. The reserve-epsilon Hall test then proves the positive-witness assertion.

Split along these actual permitted cuts, iterating on the inherited marked pieces. The child boundary loads are zero. At a matching cut, the separated CROSS triangles are the original additive C rows; their pure-middle closing caps have entropy zero. Formal re-pairing of two boundary C rows is only bookkeeping and creates no physical coupon. The resulting hybrid flow and entropy constraints are Cartesian across pieces. Every piece perturbation reassembles an original cross split, whose full h is at least the corresponding C+C value. Hence the inherited point is maximizing on each piece.

Mix that point slightly with its strictly positive relative hybrid witness. CROSS allocations stay within their triangle simplexes, so no terminal seam is pushed into a losing one-sided chamber. The strict GREEN row margins persist. The proved corner expansions give only nonnegative epsilon log(1/epsilon) terms and a strictly positive one at every activated ordinary zero. All other changes are O(epsilon). Maximality therefore excludes every ordinary zero except the two marked faces and the zero-dimensional closing rows. Diagonal losses are absent: all pure-middle ports were removed in section3.

Finite-dimensional row-space Lagrange multipliers now exist on each piece, including when the balance equations are redundant. Exponentiating them gives finite positive ordinary curvatures. The local equations are the actual tangent-circle formula for each C row and the actual Green equations L(mu;x,y)=b, L(nu;x,y)=c for each E port, with their proved one-line/two-line limits. Every ordinary signed star sums to0 or2pi.

5. The entropy is the volume of a representation of the original group

The hybrid-piece representation theorem applies to exactly these data. Its essentials are included here to specify the volume being bounded. Each piece develops ONE signed circle family by finite-center star holonomy. The marked edge may be MAIN or MATCHING. In the latter case its two incident rows are the positive zero-entropy CROSS closing halves, so the same two-line strip construction applies. All permitted parallel marked chords have been split. No unproved translation holonomy remains.

Along the actual cut tree, map each child’s ordered tangent boundary planes to the parent’s pair by ONE Mobius map. This gives one reflection for each occurrence of an actual shared white face. A GREEN port remains within one piece and retains its entire local source collar. At an original CROSS port spanning pieces, the common outer product P is noncentral parabolic. Its two raw first meridians have trace-2 and Tr((P+I)A)=4. The parabolic centralizer acts simply transitively on them, so there is a unique C mapping the WHOLE ordered input pair to the output pair and all three recorded contact flags. This is the original shaded pairing, even when its two triangles lie in different pieces.

Use the two original topological cut balls and the original face maps. One global reflection develops the second ball. Their proper digon-cut disks have both ends at the same tangent-pair contact. Compatible finite incidence horospheres and the short-edge tetrahedron bound make the added cut-disk volume tend to zero. Signed cyclic polygon coning, including winding-zero faces and repeated contact images, then computes the actual relative fundamental chain:

Vol(rho_sm)=sum_pieces sum_triangles sigma V(theta)
          =2pi*(inherited maximizing entropy).     (5)

Each piece uses its own genuine marked contact in this chain calculation. Mobius invariance is used for geometric tetrahedron volumes, not for an open crossing expression with a reset Gauss flag. The failed local re-framing argument is neither needed nor reinstated.

Finally restore the deleted original auxiliary meridians as identities. The ordered physical pairs coincide across each identity smoothing. Their outer product need not be parabolic; the established axial (loxodromic or elliptic), parabolic, or central zero-volume filling cap retains its actual peripheral flag. Thus rho_sm extends through the actual quotient to a representation rho of pi_1(Y_D), with the SAME relative volume. Equations (4)–(5) give

2pi W_D=Vol(rho)<=Vol(Y_D).

The last inequality is the independently proved representation-volume bound for the original hyperbolic exterior. Together with the all-cross lower bound this proves (2), including the absolute volume constant.

6. A strict identity-insertion consequence

The same argument starts with any fixed nonempty set I of original identity coupons and P coupons elsewhere. If its mixed incidence polytope is empty, its coefficient is zero at every even level and its rate is -infinity. Otherwise its original identity graph is a forest with at most one protected vertex per tree, and its coefficient contracts exactly. The full-kernel theorem gives a compact maximum. Any further pure-middle maximum ports can be added to I: lifting the identity degrees gives a feasible pure-middle assignment in the original graph, so the union is again a permitted forest. The hybrid argument and actual representation assembly then apply unchanged.

The resulting representation kills each specified original auxiliary meridian. Those are essential in Y_D. The already proved strict-kernel volume bound therefore gives

lim_(even N) N^-1 log Z_(D,N;H_1 on I)
                          <Vol(Y_D)/(2pi).         (6)

The empty remaining source has rate zero, also strictly below the positive complete hyperbolic volume. This is a strict statement about the actual positive identity-inserted source. It is not a phase-sensitive estimate for all small colors or a replacement for their normal data.

7. What is still required for the requested knot conjecture

The theorem closes the COMPLETE even positive endpoint estimate, at arbitrary fixed bridge width under the labeled geometric certificate. It does not control the full Gaussian-resolvent contour away from this endpoint, its nonzero Fourier charges, the selected geometric cycle or its nonzero contribution. The odd regularized numerator theorem now supplies the same rate for its actual value-plus-spectral-derivative coupon, and fixed spectral jets have an explicit polynomial bound. These are spectral derivatives at the fixed root, not genuine-q normals. Independently long fixed signed fillings still require those quantum inputs, with the original peripheral normals and scalars, before the filled hyperbolic volume can be concluded.

The general original Kashaev volume conjecture remains OPEN. Its target has not been replaced by (2).

8. Finite verification

The focused structural audit and recorded results check 41 literal loopless identity smoothings at widths two, three and four; 918 hybrid patterns; 121004 exact Hall cuts; 716 tight-set covers by independently constructed actual dual digon disks; and 816 independent positive-arc max-flow tests. These finite tests supplement the general proof; they do not numerically prove the asymptotic or the relative fundamental-class identification.
