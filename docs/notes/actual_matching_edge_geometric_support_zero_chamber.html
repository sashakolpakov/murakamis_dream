The two geometric defects lie across the complete-channel support wall

30 October 2026. Independently reviewed. For the prescribed-pair frame on any actual certified right-angled matching cut, the two failed ordinary factors at one matching edge have a precise common chart. Their normalized total degree has real part BELOW 1/2; their magnetic rows are strictly inside that degree. After the actual inverse turn, the target rows are physical and cross-central, but their separation has real part ABOVE 1/2. This identifies exactly which inequality in the controlled exceptional-kernel tube fails.

The statement is geometric and algebraic. It neither continues an actual finite kernel across that wall nor supplies an asymptotic there.

1. A strict failed triangle has a deficient normalized degree

Let (a0,a1,a2) be an original prefix triple in a generic regular frame. Assume zero is strictly inside its real convex hull, and Im(a0 conjugate(a2))!=0. Multiplying all entries by one nonzero scalar, put a0=1 and arg a2=theta in (-pi,pi), theta!=0. The strict positive relation among the three diagonals implies

a1=-b0-b2 a2, b0,b2>0.

Conversely such a relation makes zero an interior point of the triangle. Thus the argument beta of a1 lies strictly between pi and pi+theta, with these arguments lifted to the same interval of length less than pi.

The actual wire exponentials and their canonical logarithms are

X1=-a0/a1, X2=-a1/a2,
xj=Log_(0,2pi)(Xj)/(2pi i), 0<Re xj<1.

If theta<0, the two wire arguments are pi-beta and beta-theta-pi. They are positive and their sum is -theta. If theta>0, the arguments are 3pi-beta and pi+beta-theta, with sum 4pi-theta. Hence

theta<0: 0<Re(x1+x2)=-theta/(2pi)<1/2,
theta>0: 3/2<Re(x1+x2)=2-theta/(2pi)<2.         (1)

Use the ORIGINAL degree adapters even though the complete channel is no longer in their physical support. Define

low:  sigma=x1+x2, r=x1;
high: sigma=2-x1-x2, r=1-x2.

In both cases

0<Re sigma<1/2, 0<Re r<Re sigma.               (2)

No wire endpoint has been crossed. It is the complete-channel total degree bound, rather than a magnetic-row endpoint, that fails.

2. The two exceptional ends share this same complex degree

The prescribed-pair theorem chooses the polarization line to leave the white-normal polyhedron through the interiors of the two shaded facets at one matching edge. For a generic transverse line, the induced affine map on each triangular facet is nonsingular. Its strict barycentric relation therefore gives the strict convex-hull hypothesis of section 1, not merely a boundary relation.

The input and output triples at this edge have the SAME outer prefix diagonals a0,a2. They consequently have the same theta and the same adapter in (1). Their canonical wire sums have equal real parts, and their exponentials are both a0/a2. Their imaginary parts therefore agree as well. They share an EXACT complex normalized total sigma. Write their two normalized magnetic rows as r,s. Then

0<Re r,Re s<Re sigma<1/2.                     (3)

The generic family can additionally avoid Re(r-s)=0. The relevant target-total ratio is the holomorphic ratio of the two middle prefix diagonals. It is nonconstant: otherwise the two transported first meridians would be proportional as linear trace functions on every rank-one projector, hence equal after using their common trace -2. The actual nontrivial complete collar does not fix that meridian. A nonconstant holomorphic ratio cannot take only positive-real values on the open prescribed-pair family. Its real seam is thus a proper excluded set, compatible with all the existing strict margins.

3. The actual target chart has exactly the opposite support defect

First suppose the original adapter is low. Write O=exp(2pi i sigma), R=exp(2pi i r), S=exp(2pi i s). The original local prefix traces are (-1/R,-1/S,1/O). Undoing their actual inverse-turn relation gives target exponentials

T_target=R/S, V_input=1/S, U_output=O/S.        (4)

Their canonical physical logarithms have total 1+r-s and rows 1-s, sigma-s. If Re r<Re s, this target is low. If Re r>Re s, apply its ORIGINAL high adapter: the normalized total is 1-r+s and the rows are 1-r, sigma-r. For an original high adapter, the exponentials are instead V_input=O/S,U_output=1/S; this only reverses the target input/output order in the same calculation.

In a fixed component choose m,M from r,s by their smaller and larger real parts. This does not take a nonholomorphic minimum inside an action: the choice is fixed on that component. The normalized ordered target chart is

delta=1-sigma,
nu=sigma-M, lambda=m,
tau=nu+lambda+delta=1+m-M,
u=nu, v=nu+delta=1-M.                          (5)

It follows immediately from (3) that

1/2<Re delta<1,
Re nu>0, Re lambda>0, Re(nu+lambda+delta)<1,
1/2<Re tau<1,
0<Re u<Re tau/2<Re v<Re tau.                 (6)

For the two midpoint inequalities, the positive differences are

Re(tau/2-u)=[1+Re(r+s)-2Re sigma]/2>0,
Re(v-tau/2)=[1-Re(r+s)]/2>0.

Thus EVERY inequality of the proved exceptional-kernel tube is satisfied except its bandwidth condition Re delta<alpha<=1/2. This description is uniform in bridge width and uses the actual typed turn and degree adapters, not a freely selected target frame.

4. What continuation is actually required

The controlled root kernel has delta=h/N, where h is the row separation, and requires 0<Re delta<a/N. At generic quantum parameter the original polynomial p_a(J_t) has bandwidth a. Consequently ALL real physical integer entries with h>a vanish identically, together with every retained generic-q jet.

The geometric chamber (6) is beyond that support wall at the complete fold a/N->1/2. This does not make its complex geometric coordinates meaningless. It specifies what an analytic period or new transition kernel must justify; it cannot be supplied by a nonzero relative estimate merely extended from the present tube. In particular the normalized ordinary factors used inside that tube are outside their established complete-degree range after (2). Their apparent endpoint factors, zeros or poles must be retained in any proposed continuation rather than canceled after specialization.

A block estimate or contraction tree that factors EVERY grouped patch back into ordinary packets on their old physical domains cannot remove this obstruction under the common K-frame hypothesis: it would make every original factor strictly physical and selected, contrary to the white-normal theorem. A genuinely coupled packet or source-exact frame transition remains a different possibility.

This is a direct structural corollary of the audited prefix, adapter, prescribed-facet and inverse-turn identities. No new finite-kernel asymptotic, nonvanishing contribution or global equality is claimed.
