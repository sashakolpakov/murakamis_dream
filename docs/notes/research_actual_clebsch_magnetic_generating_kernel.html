Actual Clebsch generating legs and their binomial dual

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The ordinary generating function of an actual normalized Clebsch column admits an exact magnetic summation. A contracting finite recurrence then gives a holomorphic unit on a full small activity disk. Its literal ordinary-binomial dual has a selected coefficient-circle saddle with nonzero amplitude. A uniform-Casimir extension then estimates both literal confluent partner legs by Cauchy differentiation. The phases, finite support and full Hermite channel row are retained. No subsequent Casimir-channel contraction is estimated.

Complete direct H fixes the physical column and its full Hermite inverse. Complete direct C supplies the finite Chu identity used below. The recurrence, analytic estimates, and dual contour proof are given here. Complete direct U supplies the stronger uniform-Casimir magnetic unit; Section 5 proves its actual binomial-dual and partner extension.
