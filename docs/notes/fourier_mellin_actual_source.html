An actual bounded source in the Fourier–Mellin polarization

Research construction, 27 October 2026. A fixed rational inhomogeneous source need not be assumed as a special factorization of the original meridian operator. It can be constructed in the Fourier–Mellin polarization directly from any actual all-level operator and finitely many fixed colors. The original Kashaev invariant is retained as an exact coefficient period. It is not replaced by point evaluation or by a new state.

This gives an actual sourced quantum-A object for arbitrary knots. Its classical geometric support and selected regulator norm still require proof; the volume conjecture is not proved here.

1. Actual input and the Q convention

Choose the nonzero rational operator supplied by the Q-only construction, clear its coefficient denominators, and remove a common shift:

P=sum_(j=0)^d a_j(Q,Y) T^j,
a_j=sum_m a_(j,m)(Q) Y^m,
TY=QYT, a_0 a_d!=0.                          (1.1)

Here d>=1, the m support is finite, and the coefficients belong to the rational function field ℚ(Q). The associated fixed-color periods are C_n(Q), normalized by C_n(1)=1. They are the actual finite reduced periods of the existing color-pointing theorem. The exact identities are

F_N(Y=Q_N^n)=C_n(Q_N), 1<=n<=N,
C_N(Q_N)=F_N(1)=<K>_N,
Q_N=exp(2pi*i/N).                            (1.2)

There is no large-color asymptotic premise in (1.2).

For completeness these fixed-color functions descend to Q=q^2 as well. On the ordinary n-dimensional color x=q^(n-1), use the same stripped crossing coefficients, ordinary pivot K and residual twist x. The total x-power on a closed state is

E_ord=b-1-w+sum_positive(-a-c)+sum_negative(a+c)
     =-kappa_D+2sum_negative a-2sum_positive c,

because sum(c-a)=0. It is even for a knot. All remaining factors are expressed in Q and x^2. At x=q^(n-1) this is a rational function of Q. The known Laurent regularity and normalization of C_n are those of the original finite color construction, not an additional asymptotic or Jones-identification assumption.

Applying the actual root equation to (1.2), then using infinitely many distinct Q_N for every fixed n, gives the generic recurrence

sum_(j=0)^d a_j(Q,Q^n) C_(n+j)(Q)=0, n>=1.   (1.3)

This is the same elementary cross-level rigidity used in the all-level period theorem.

2. Choose a nonsingular fixed starting color

Let A be the Q-exponent span in the collected Laurent support of a_d(Q,Y). For every integer r>A, the substitution a_d(Q,Q^r) is nonzero. Indeed distinct monomials Q^u Y^v become Q^(u+rv), and a collision with v!=v’ would imply r<=|u-u’|<=A. Equal-v terms were already collected.

Choose the fixed integer

n_0>=d+A+1.                                 (2.1)

Then a_d(Q,Q^(n_0-d))!=0, and also a_d(Q,Q^(r-d))!=0 for every r>=n_0. Every C_n is nonzero since C_n(1)=1. These facts will give both a nonzero source and unique formal propagation in the chosen source lattice.

3. The exact Fourier–Mellin operator and its boundary source

Form the actual formal generating period

Z(Q,X)=sum_(n>=n_0) C_n(Q) X^n
       in X^(n_0) ℚ(Q)[[X]].                (3.1)

Let S act by S f(X)=f(QX), so SX=QXS. The quantum-torus Fourier map is the exact algebra map

Y |-> S, T |-> X^(-1).

It respects TY=QYT because X(-1)S=QSX(-1). In the recorded left-coefficient ordering its image is

P^M=sum_(j,m) a_(j,m)(Q) Q^(-mj) X^(-j) S^m. (3.2)

Negative m are allowed; they are invertible shifts. A fixed left power of S makes the displayed operator have nonnegative order, transporting its source by the same power, if that convention is desired. No factor of the source is discarded by doing so.

Multiplying (1.3) by X^n and summing n>=n_0 gives

P^M Z=B(Q,X),                               (3.3)

where the source is the explicit fixed Laurent polynomial

B=sum_(j,m) a_(j,m)(Q) Q^(-mj) X^(-j)
      sum_(r=n_0)^(n_0+j-1) C_r(Q) Q^(mr) X^r. (3.4)

The empty sum for j=0 is zero. The identity follows termwise from

sum_(n>=n_0) Q^(mn) C_(n+j) X^n
  =Q^(-mj) X^(-j)
       [Z(Q,Q^m X)
            -sum_(r=n_0)^(n_0+j-1) C_r(Q) Q^(mr) X^r].

Every coefficient of every X power involves only finitely many terms; convergence is not assumed.

The source uses exactly the d fixed colors C_(n_0),…,C_(n_0+d-1). Its X powers lie between n_0-d and n_0-1. Its lowest coefficient is

[X^(n_0-d)]B=C_(n_0)(Q) a_d(Q,Q^(n_0-d))!=0. (3.5)

Thus it is a genuine nonzero bounded source, not a possibly vanishing right-hand side inferred from formal elimination.

4. The pointing and the original period are retained

Equation (3.3), together with the lattice condition Z in X^(n_0)[[X]], specifies Z uniquely. A nonzero homogeneous difference W in this lattice, of lowest power r>=n_0, would contribute at power r-d only through j=d. Its coefficient would be

a_d(Q,Q^(r-d)) [X^r]W,

which is nonzero by (2.1), a contradiction. Thus the source and specified lattice retain the actual finite-color pointing.

For every N>=n_0 the original invariant is exactly

<K>_N=[ [X^N]Z(Q,X) ]_(Q=Q_N)
      =Res_(X=0) Z(Q_N,X) X^(-N-1)dX.        (4.1)

The first expression means whole-germ specialization of the coefficient C_N(Q). Even if coefficients of a recurrence or a companion solution have poles at Q_N, C_N itself is the original regular Laurent period. No division by such a pole precedes its cancellation. The residue notation means coefficient extraction; it does not assert an analytically convergent contour realization.

The full ADO period is still d_N(M)F_N(M), with its marked pole, residue, first jet and sign retained in the original meridian realization. This change of polarization constructs the same reduced Kashaev scalar in (4.1); it does not silently evaluate or remove d_N. A comparison of full-state realizations must transport that specified normalization line as well.

5. Classical support and the affine extension

At Q=1 the symbol of (3.2) is exactly

P_cl(S,X^(-1)).                              (5.1)

Thus the classical Fourier transformation exchanges the meridian coordinate and the inverse longitude coordinate in the specified ordering. If a geometric factor occurs in the original actual symbol, its transformed geometric factor occurs here. No such factor is inferred merely from the transform.

The marked regulator action is compatible with this polarization. Let y=Log Y, lambda=Log(L/L_c) and use H=Log Q, centering the longitude logarithm at the chosen complete lift L_c. Use the corresponding centered WKB action dW=lambda dy. The original constant character L_c is retained separately, rather than dropped from the period. In centered coordinates x=Log(X/X_c)=-lambda, X_c=L_c^(-1), with new momentum y. Hence

W^M=W-y lambda,  dW^M=y dx.                 (5.2)

Its symmetric regulator coordinate is unchanged:

R=W-(1/2)y lambda
  =W^M-(1/2)x y.                            (5.3)

Indeed dR=(lambda dy-y d lambda)/2, exactly the marked regulator differential after Y=m and L^2=l are substituted. At the chosen complete lift y=0 the Legendre correction vanishes, including a retained longitude sign. Thus the previously calibrated complete imaginary action is preserved. This checks the classical marked action, not a normalization or asymptotic theorem for the quantum coefficient period (4.1).

Grouping the m powers of S and using its usual companion realization turns (3.3), when its S-order is positive, into an affine difference extension with rational coefficients and the explicit source (3.4). One can homogenize it by the exact source annihilator

(S-B(Q,QX)/B(Q,X)) P^M Z=0.                 (5.4)

The source quotient and the chosen formal lattice must remain marked; (5.4) is not permission to replace the original object by an arbitrary homogeneous solution. If the transformed order is zero, (3.3) is instead its rational algebraic source equation; no artificial order is inserted to manufacture a geometric block.

This removes the need to assume a special inhomogeneous right factor in order to obtain some canonical generic sourced polarization of the actual A-period. It does not prove that this extension is generically nonsplit, identify its geometric source class, or bound the selected coefficient norm in (4.1).

6. The precise comparison residual

For two supplied affine systems sigma v_i=C_i v_i+c_i, a proposed map v_act=P v_geo+b is horizontal precisely when

sigma(P) C_geo=C_act P,
(sigma-C_act)b=c_act-sigma(P)c_geo.           (6.1)

Thus after a homogeneous comparison P, the remaining obstruction is the explicit class

[c_act-sigma(P)c_geo]
          in F^d/(sigma-C_act)F^d.           (6.2)

An affine cut ell_i v_i+k_i is preserved additionally when

ell_act P=ell_geo,
k_act+ell_act b=k_geo.                       (6.3)

The second equation retains the dual affine constant. Generic matrix invertibility does not by itself transport the boundary lattice.

The new construction supplies the actual side of (6.1)–(6.3) in this polarization. It does not supply the geometric side or assert that their class (6.2) vanishes. Multiplicative rank-one curvature descent alone does not solve this additive source problem.

The actual Mellin normal-source theorem now uses this very source quotient to exhibit a dual cocycle with value one on the selected section. Its normal symbol is therefore nonzero for every knot, without a semisimplicity assumption. Whole first-normal compatibility removes central scalar freedom on that selected line. The cyclotomic jet theorem additionally fixes arbitrary root sections in the exact pole lattice using a bounded number of source-compatible quantum levels. Neither theorem constructs the geometric side of (6.1)–(6.3) or estimates the selected residue’s norm.
