A global alias bound and the full original colored-source gap

29 October 2026. The entire original RPRP band has the uniform faithful-Hermite upper rate

B=12log⁡(4+15)<j0=v8π. B=\tfrac12\log(4+\sqrt{15}) <j_0=\frac{v_8}{\pi}. \tag{1}

The proof uses the exact short-band coefficients, monic physical continuants, and an elementary spectral ellipse for their recurrence. It retains the value AND first normal coefficient at every double Casimir node. It requires no ordinary-color unitarity, Laurent positivity conjecture, or discarded endpoint sector.

Combining this bound with the proved global low-core bound gives a strict original-source gap outside every fixed complete-color corridor, over the ENTIRE auxiliary-color cube, for every fixed odd width s≥25s\geq25. More generally the exact sufficient condition is As>(s−1)j0+BA_s>(s-1)j_0+B. This supplies a global-color input for long fillings; the selected cusp-corridor filling asymptotic and its peripheral identification remain separate.

1. Rewrite the exact short band before estimating it

Use d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, Q=q2Q=q^2, M=⌊d/2⌋M=\lfloor d/2\rfloor. Let plp_l be the MONIC physical middle-degree continuant:

p0=1,p−1=0,pl+1(z)=(z−Dl)pl(z)−al−12pl−1(z), p_0=1,\quad p_{-1}=0,\qquad p_{l+1}(z)=(z-D_l)p_l(z)-a_{l-1}^{\,2}p_{l-1}(z), \tag{2} Dl=4qQl−q(1+Q)Q2l,al−1=−(1−Ql)2. D_l=4qQ^l-q(1+Q)Q^{2l},\qquad a_{l-1}=-(1-Q^l)^2.

Thus pl=(a0⋯al−1)flp_l=(a_0\cdots a_{l-1})f_l. A bar on a polynomial denotes coefficient conjugation at this root. For 1≤h≤⌊N/2⌋1\leq h\leq\lfloor N/2\rfloor, define the literal short polynomial by the same finite formula as the cusp split:

PN,h=∑t=1h(−1)N−t−hst−12st+h−1s2t−1sh−tf‾t−1. P_{N,h}= \sum_{t=1}^{h}(-1)^{N-t-h} \frac{s_{t-1}^2s_{t+h-1}}{s_{2t-1}s_{h-t}} \bar f_{t-1}. \tag{3}

The endpoint h=N/2h=N/2 at even NN is allowed in (3): the largest factorial index is 2h−1=N−12h-1=N-1. It is needed for the actual middle auxiliary color, which has no low core.

All small denominators in (3) cancel in the following exact polynomial identity:

(−1)N+h−1PN,h(z)=∑l=0h−1(−1)lq3l2+4l+1−2hl−h[h+l2l+1]Qp‾l(z). \boxed{\quad (-1)^{N+h-1}P_{N,h}(z)= \sum_{l=0}^{h-1}(-1)^l q^{3l^2+4l+1-2hl-h} {h+l\brack 2l+1}_Q\,\bar p_l(z). \quad} \tag{4}

Indeed sa=iaq−a(a+1)/2Fas_a=i^a q^{-a(a+1)/2}F_a. In the term t=l+1t=l+1, the factorial ratio not containing sl2s_l^2 becomes its Gaussian coefficient with the indicated phase. Also Fl2f‾l=(−1)lQl(l+1)p‾lF_l^2\bar f_l=(-1)^lQ^{l(l+1)}\bar p_l. These two phase simplifications give (4), including its sign. No factorial index in (4) reaches NN, and the Gaussian can equivalently be regarded as its finite polynomial.

2. A uniform monic-continuant bound with all source endpoints

The required estimate is

limsupN→∞max0≤l≤M1Nlog⁡(6−lmaxz∈[−2,2]|pl(z)|)≤0. \limsup_{N\to\infty}\ \max_{0\leq l\leq M}\ \frac1N\log\left(6^{-l} \max_{z\in[-2,2]}|p_l(z)|\right)\leq0. \tag{5}

The initial vector in (2) is exactly (p0,p−1)=(1,0)(p_0,p_{-1})=(1,0). Its coefficients have no singular endpoint. They are uniformly smooth functions of u=πl/Nu=\pi l/N, 0≤u≤π/20\leq u\leq\pi/2, with uniform O(1/N)O(1/N) error from their frozen classical values.

Put V=e2iuV=e^{2iu}. The eigenvalues of the frozen companion are VλV\lambda, where

λ2−dzλ+a2=0,dz=zV−1−4+2V,a=4sin⁡2u. \lambda^2-d_z\lambda+a^2=0, \qquad d_z=zV^{-1}-4+2V,\qquad a=4\sin^2u. \tag{6}

Both roots lie STRICTLY inside |λ|=6|\lambda|=6, uniformly for 0≤u≤π/20\leq u\leq\pi/2, −2≤z≤2-2\leq z\leq2. Here is an elementary verification, avoiding a chosen eigenbasis.

For fixed a≤4<6a\leq4<6, a root on |λ|=6|\lambda|=6 makes dz=λ+a2/λd_z=\lambda+a^2/\lambda, the ellipse with semiaxes 6+a2/66+a^2/6 and 6−a2/66-a^2/6. Both roots at dz=0d_z=0 are inside that disk. The same segment-continuity argument used for the mixed-source ellipse therefore puts both roots inside whenever dzd_z is in the interior of this ellipse.

The point dzd_z is affine in real zz, so convexity reduces the check to z=±2z=\pm2. For z=2z=2, d2=−2ad_2=-2a, and 2a<6+a2/62a<6+a^2/6 because a<6a<6. For z=−2z=-2, put y=1−cos⁡(2u)∈[0,2]y=1-\cos(2u)\in[0,2], so a=2ya=2y, d−2=−4+4isin⁡(2u)d_{-2}=-4+4i\sin(2u). The ellipse condition is

36(9+y2)2+36(2y−y2)(9−y2)2<1. \frac{36}{(9+y^2)^2} +\frac{36(2y-y^2)}{(9-y^2)^2}<1. \tag{7}

After multiplication by (81−y4)2>0(81-y^4)^2>0, its positive slack factors exactly as

(y4+12y2−36y+27)(y4+24y2−36y+135). (y^4+12y^2-36y+27)(y^4+24y^2-36y+135).

The first factor equals (y−1)2(y2+2y+3)+12(y−4/3)2+8/3>0(y-1)^2(y^2+2y+3)+12(y-4/3)^2+8/3>0. The second equals y4+24(y−3/4)2+243/2>0y^4+24(y-3/4)^2+243/2>0. This proves the uniform strict root bound, including both index endpoints.

For completeness, this spectral statement genuinely bounds the varying product in (2). Divide each frozen companion by 6. On the compact (u,z)(u,z) set the matrices are uniformly bounded and their eigenvalues have modulus at most 1. Cayley–Hamilton gives ∥BL∥≤L∥B∥+L−1≤CL\|B^L\|\leq L\|B\|+L-1\leq C L, including double roots. An exact block of fixed length LL differs from the frozen power by OL(1/N)O_L(1/N), uniformly in u,zu,z. Choose LL large to make log⁡(CL)/L\log(CL)/L arbitrarily small, then choose NN large and concatenate the blocks. The two incomplete blocks have bounded cost. This proves (5) uniformly in l,zl,z, without adiabatic eigenvector transport or an uncontrolled endpoint factor.

3. A global bound for the entire alias, including the normals

At a unit root the finite Gaussian satisfies |[h+l2l+1]Q|≤(h+l2l+1)|{h+l\brack2l+1}_Q|\leq{h+l\choose2l+1}, by its elementary Pascal polynomial expansion with nonnegative integer coefficients. Consequently (4)–(5) give a sequence ϵN→0\epsilon_N\to0, independent of hh, with

max[−2,2]|PN,h|≤eϵNN∑l=0h−1(h+l2l+1)6l=eϵNNUh−1(4). \max_{[-2,2]}|P_{N,h}| \leq e^{\epsilon_NN} \sum_{l=0}^{h-1}{h+l\choose2l+1}6^l =e^{\epsilon_NN}U_{h-1}(4). \tag{8}

The identity follows from the generating function ∑h≥1∑l<h(h+l2l+1)Klth−1=[1−(K+2)t+t2]−1\sum_{h\geq1}\sum_{l<h}{h+l\choose2l+1}K^l t^{h-1} =[1-(K+2)t+t^2]^{-1}.

The actual short polynomial has degree h−1≤Mh-1\leq M. Complex Markov on [−2,2][-2,2] therefore bounds its derivative by (h−1)2/2(h-1)^2/2 times its supremum. In the original faithful Hermite norm, with normal scale N−2N^{-2}, this costs at most 9/89/8. The literal crossing also has norm at most 9/89/8. Thus the whole original alias obeys

∥RPN,h∥H≤8164eϵNNUh−1(4)≤8112815eϵNN(4+15)h. \boxed{\quad \|R P_{N,h}\|_{\mathrm H} \leq\frac{81}{64}e^{\epsilon_NN}U_{h-1}(4) \leq\frac{81}{128\sqrt{15}}e^{\epsilon_NN} (4+\sqrt{15})^h. \quad} \tag{9}

It includes every value and every first normal coefficient. In particular, uniformly for 1≤h≤⌊N/2⌋1\leq h\leq\lfloor N/2\rfloor,

limsupN1Nlog⁡∥RPN,h∥H≤B=12log⁡(4+15). \limsup_N\frac1N\log\|RP_{N,h}\|_{\mathrm H} \leq B=\tfrac12\log(4+\sqrt{15}). \tag{10}

This is a source-compatible upper bound, not a claim that the alias is polynomial, nor an asserted sharp asymptotic for it.

4. Every original auxiliary color is covered

For j>N/2j>N/2, put h=N−j≤Mh=N-j\leq M. The exact box is zN,h=cN,h+RPN,hz_{N,h}=c_{N,h}+RP_{N,h}, with a zero alias at h=0h=0. For 1≤j≤⌊N/2⌋1\leq j\leq\lfloor N/2\rfloor, factorial complementation in the literal high band gives

ℛ(R−1Lj)=(−1)NRPN,j. \mathscr R(R^{-1}L_j)=(-1)^N RP_{N,j}. \tag{11}

This includes j=N/2j=N/2 at even NN. Thus no middle color, normal channel or color endpoint is absent.

The global core theorem, followed by complex Markov and the limit of its fixed radius R↓1R\downarrow1, gives the uniform bound

limsupN1Nlog⁡max0≤h≤M∥cN,h∥H≤j0. \limsup_N\frac1N\log \max_{0\leq h\leq M}\|c_{N,h}\|_{\mathrm H} \leq j_0. \tag{12}

Apply (9),(12) to the original source |Φ2(f)|≤3N3∥f∥H|\Phi_2(f)|\leq3N^3\|f\|_{\mathrm H}. Any product term with a nonempty alias set of size mm has uniform upper rate

(s−m)j0+mB≤(s−1)j0+B, (s-m)j_0+mB\leq(s-1)j_0+B, \tag{13}

since B<j0B<j_0. This applies across the ENTIRE original color cube, including low colors whose boxes are pure aliases.

Let As=Vs/(2π)A_s=V_s/(2\pi). Whenever

gs:=As−(s−1)j0>B, \boxed{\qquad g_s:=A_s-(s-1)j_0>B,\qquad} \tag{14}

Condition (14) holds for EVERY odd s≥25s\geq25. Indeed the previous elementary bound is gs≥j0−π/[scos⁡(π/s)]g_s\geq j_0-\pi/[s\cos(\pi/s)]. 5. The full off-cusp original-source theorem

Fix an odd s≥25s\geq25 and any 0<δ<1/20<\delta<1/2. There is γs(δ)>0\gamma_s(\delta)>0 such that, uniformly over ALL actual auxiliary colors 1≤ji≤N1\leq j_i\leq N with at least one ji≤N−δNj_i\leq N-\delta N,

limsupN1Nlog⁡|𝒜s,N(j1,…,js)|≤As−γs(δ). \boxed{\quad \limsup_N\frac1N\log \left|\mathcal A_{s,N}(j_1,\ldots,j_s)\right| \leq A_s-\gamma_s(\delta). \quad} \tag{15}

The limsup includes the maximum over those color tuples. To prove it, if some color is at most N/2N/2, every term contains an alias, so (13)–(14) give the gap. Otherwise all boxes have the exact core-plus-alias split. Alias terms have the same gap; the all-core term has a strict source gap by the global core theorem because at least one deficit is at least δN\delta N. Taking the smaller of the two positive gaps proves (15).

In the exact original simultaneous filling sum, each fixed signed twist kernel has polynomial total coefficient mass ≤2N(1+|pi|)\leq2N(1+|p_i|). Thus its ENTIRE off-cusp color contribution is bounded by

|χN|∏i2N(1+|pi|)exp⁡{N[As−γs(δ)+o(1)]}. |\chi_N|\prod_i2N(1+|p_i|) \exp\{N[A_s-\gamma_s(\delta)+o(1)]\}. \tag{16}

All original κ\kappa factors are unit phases, and the original χN\chi_N is displayed rather than absorbed. The estimate is before any asymptotic deletion of filling normal data; their exact Fourier–Hermite kernel is used with its proved total mass.

Equation (16) does not yet prove a filled-knot asymptotic. One must still analyze the original coherent cusp-corridor sum, identify its peripheral marking, and prove its selected contribution. The present theorem removes the global-color obstruction for the stated broad fixed-width family; it does not replace those remaining tasks by a positive comparison invariant.
