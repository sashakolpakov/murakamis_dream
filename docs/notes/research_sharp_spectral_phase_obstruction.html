A sharp spectral maximum does not by itself define the original torsor

Workspace research draft, 31 October 2026. Exact local result. This concerns the actual LEFT coefficient, including its omitted-half scalar and unused rows. It exhibits a strict real spectral maximum of its absolute leading rate for which the usual positive-clock physical adapter cannot transport the original ordered meridian pair. A missing sign character is identified explicitly. This is NOT a global shared-source growth counterexample, nor an obstruction to a properly constructed signed-clock/phase adapter.

1. A literal coefficient with one protected outer face

Use the original even-level notation

N=2m, d=N-1,
alpha_r(delta)=4cos²(pi(r+1/2)/N)-4sin²(pi delta),
s_r=2sin(pi r/N).

Set y=0. Then every dimer vanishes, so the actual tail is the single monomer product

K_(i,j)=x^(j-i) product_(r=i+1)^j s_r².

Consequently the coefficient of x(j-i)bi c^(d-j) in the original LEFT factor, up to its recorded overall sign, is EXACTLY

a_N(delta)=A_rest(delta)
           product_(r<i) alpha_r(delta)
           product_(j<r<m) alpha_r(delta)
           product_(r=i+1)^j s_r².                          (1)

This follows from actual_global_left_weighted_spectral_envelope.md (1); no absolute recurrence or endpoint replacement has been introduced.

Take N divisible by12, i=N/6, j=N/3 and delta=1/4. The omitted-half Chebyshev scalar has modulus2, since m is divisible by6 and sigma=0. No alpha_r vanishes: its zero would require r+1/2=N/4, with an integer right side. Thus (1) is nonzero at every one of these levels.

More generally, write mu=i/N, nu=j/N and assume

0<mu<t_c=1/2-delta<nu<1/2.

The unused intervals avoid the moving zero. Their absolute Riemann rate, with the same monomer interval and zero-rate omitted half, is

h_delta(mu,nu)
  = integral_0^mu log|alpha(t,delta)|dt
    +integral_nu^(1/2) log|alpha(t,delta)|dt
    +integral_mu^nu log(4sin²(pi t))dt,                     (2)

alpha(t,delta)=4[cos²(pi delta)-sin²(pi t)].

At the specified point (2) is the ACTUAL coefficient limit N^(-1)log|a_N(1/4)| along N divisible12. The smoothly varying rate functional (2) is not asserted to approximate through every dense zero of A_rest on a fixed spectral interval.

2. A strict free-spectral maximum and all row equations

Hold mu=1/6 and nu=1/3 fixed. At delta=1/4,

alpha(t)=2cos(2pi t), alpha_delta=-4pi,
alpha_(delta delta)=0.

Reflection t ->1/2-t interchanges the two unused intervals and negates alpha. Hence direct differentiation of (2) gives

partial_delta h=0,
partial_delta² h
   =-4pi² [integral_0^(1/6) sec²(2pi t)dt
            +integral_(1/3)^(1/2) sec²(2pi t)dt]
   =-4pi sqrt(3)<0.                                        (3)

This is a strict interior maximum in the FREE real spectral coordinate. No branch-contour assertion is involved.

At shared positive face multipliers x=b=1,c=3, add the original degree terms mu log b+(1-nu)log c to (2). The x term is zero because x=1. Both row equations hold as well. Indeed, with y=0,

L_delta(t)=sin²(pi t)/|cos²(pi delta)-sin²(pi t)|,
L_(1/4)(1/6)=1=b, L_(1/4)(1/3)=3=c.                      (4)

Thus partial_mu=log b-log L(mu)=0 and partial_nu=log L(nu)-log c=0. The outer-degree constraint is literal: xi=nu-mu=1/6, since all outer degree lies at x and y is protected.

These are exactly the local real equations one would encounter when using (2) as an absolute-rate functional with common face multipliers. The local datum need not extend to a globally maximizing shared flow; no such extension is claimed.

3. The original physical Casimir fails on one leg

For the original physical adapter H/(bc)^(d/2), the endpoint derivatives of the absolute stationary action give

p_in=exp(-2pi i mu), p_out=exp(-2pi i nu).                  (5)

This is the LEFT sign convention of the calibrated endpoint Green calculation, including its integer c character; inversion of either p does not change the following scalar test. The original crossing Casimir in the same clock chart is

Sigma(z,p)=2+[(p-1)²/p] (z+x)(z+y)/z².                     (6)

It is the actual principal formula in notes/actual_cross_cofactor_marked_action.md (12), not a freely chosen trace convention. Equal whole ordered products necessarily have equal traces, independently of the common scale or outer momentum.

At the exact datum (3)–(4),

p_in=(1-i sqrt3)/2, p_out=(-1-i sqrt3)/2,
Sigma(1,p_in)=0=sigma,
Sigma(3,p_out)=-2 != sigma.                               (7)

Thus these positive-clock absolute equations cannot supply the original pair-preserving centralizer. The discrepancy is not repaired by choosing the reciprocal spectral eigenline. In contrast

Sigma(-3,p_out)=0.                                       (8)

The output’s row equation after the pole is the conic with c replaced by -c. This is a genuine clock sign, not a zero spectral boundary or a discarded protected-face singularity.

An all-positive companion exists: x=y=b=1,c=5, delta=1/4, sin²(pi mu)=1/8, sin²(pi nu)=25/32, and xi=(nu-mu)/2. Symmetry gives the unique outer tilt x/y=1. The absolute stationary spectral derivative vanishes, with fixed-row second derivative -2pi sqrt7. Its two positive-clock traces are0 and -5/2, and replacing c by -5 gives0. The protected monomer example above is sufficient and does not need a general coefficient Legendre theorem.

4. The exact character that the modulus discarded

In the strict straddling regime every unused prefix row r<i has alpha_r>0, while every unused suffix row j<r<m has alpha_r<0. Its EXACT sign is

(-1)^(m-1-j)=(-1)^(d-j) (-1)^(m-1-d).                     (9)

The first factor depends on the physical c exponent. It is precisely the change c -> -c in that summand; the second is a retained scalar. The omitted-half scalar A_rest is still present and has its own two oscillatory Chebyshev modes. Its zero exponential modulus does not license deleting its phase or its derivatives.

At even levels these modes can be printed exactly:

A_rest=2(-1)^m cos(N pi delta)
      =(-1)^m[exp(i pi N delta)+exp(-i pi N delta)].        (10)

For odd N=2m+1 the exact formula from the same source is

A_rest=4(-1)^m sin(pi delta) sin(N pi delta).

On a fixed strict spectral interior its two exponential modes have the same derivatives +/-i pi; the displayed sine prefactor has zero principal derivative after division by N. No endpoint continuation of that prefactor is assumed.

Within the strict straddling chamber the suffix sign in (9) is independent of delta. Thus the principal spectral derivative of each of these two RETAINED modes is

partial_delta h + i pi,  or  partial_delta h - i pi.        (11)

At (3) it equals +i pi or -i pi, not zero. A consistent face-character absorption changes only the recorded integer monomial phase and does not remove (10). If a corresponding actual longitude-polarized representation were constructed, its normalization U=partial_delta A would therefore give U=+/-i pi, hence M²=-1, rather than a killed meridian. This last sentence is a conditional interpretation using the existing primitive-log convention, not a representation constructed from the absolute datum.

Therefore partial_delta h=0 asserts stationarity of a REAL absolute rate only. It does not imply that the full complex original phase has zero primitive auxiliary meridian log, nor that a formally written ratio H_c/E_b=1 is its actual primitive collar.

The endpoint identity/hybrid construction at delta=0 does not see this post-pole character, because no pole lies inside a strict lower interval. Extending that construction requires a consistent shared face-character assignment and the original omitted-half/peripheral phases. It cannot follow from the absolute row and spectral equations alone.

This identifies a precise missing adapter, not an impossibility theorem. A signed-clock assignment could restore the two conics locally; whether those signs sew at every original face, cup, cap and unrestricted mark is a separate incidence and phase problem.
