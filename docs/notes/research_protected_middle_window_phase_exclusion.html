Regular stop-window exclusion with a protected input middle

31 October 2026. research local theorem. This keeps the original specialization b=0b=0 exactly, hence the start row is i=0i=0. It assumes a strict regular maximum only for the stop. There is no regular start maximum, inverse zero-face weight, or inverse initial-row gauge. The conclusion is a phase loss relative to the original window envelope, not a comparison with complete volume.

The reflected protected-output case is proved by an exact finite row reflection in §6. No double-zero specialization is included.

5. The protected target and supported incidence graph

The original bb-face target is zero. Nonnegative polynomial exponents make the substitution b=0b=0 exactly its constant-term restriction, also when that face occurs at other ports. No new integration variable or phase is assigned to it.

These ports supply the same two local inequalities used by the cycle-packing criterion: the unconditional positive-envelope bound and (15). 6. Exact reflection and the protected output

For every finite level and every spectral value, αd−r=αr,βN−r=βr,Ki,j(x,y)=Kd−j,d−i(x,y). \alpha_{d-r}=\alpha_r,\qquad \beta_{N-r}=\beta_r,\qquad K_{i,j}(x,y)=K_{d-j,d-i}(x,y). \tag{16} For the last identity reverse a monomer/dimer tiling of the interval i+1,…,ji+1,\ldots,j. A monomer at rr, of weight βr(x+y)\beta_r(x+y), goes to N−rN-r. A dimer ending at rr, of weight srsr−1αr−1xys_rs_{r-1}\alpha_{r-1}xy, goes to one ending at N+1−rN+1-r; its weight is unchanged because sN−r=srs_{N-r}=s_r and αN−r=αr−1\alpha_{N-r}=\alpha_{r-1}. The reversed interval is d−j+1,…,d−id-j+1,\ldots,d-i. This proves (16), including empty intervals, without a physical-turn or unrecorded duality.

In the full original path expansion, the substitution (i,j,b,c)↦(d−j,d−i,c,b) (i,j,b,c)\longmapsto(d-j,d-i,c,b) preserves every monomial, unused row factor and the raw sign. This is an internal local polynomial identity, not a relabeling of shared source faces. Hence the c=0c=0 specialization, which forces j=dj=d, is the exact reflection of the b=0b=0 specialization. A regular start window reflects to a regular stop window, with the explicit mesh shift j′/N=1−N−1−i/Nj'/N=1-N^{-1}-i/N. Such an O(N−1)O(N^{-1}) boundary shift is covered by the same boundary-gap and one-row comparisons above.

The genuine RIGHT half-row sector has i≥⌈N/2⌉i\ge\lceil N/2\rceil. Under reflection this is precisely j′≤⌊N/2⌋−1=Mj'\le\lfloor N/2\rfloor-1=M, at both parities. Thus (15) applies to protected-output FULL and RIGHT windows, with supported alignment ϑx=ϑy=ϑb+arg⁡α(μ*)\vartheta_x=\vartheta_y=\vartheta_b+\arg\alpha(\mu_*). The output-zero incidence is absent. This is an exact source reflection, not an assertion that a general turned operator is multiplicative.

Both outer weights must remain positive, and the other middle must remain positive. No double-zero extension, irregular stop/start maximum, spectral cap, or pole-touching window is included. The theorem controls these specified source windows only; global window coverage and the complete-volume comparison remain separate.

The conclusion retains every strict-window and positive-supported-weight hypothesis stated above.
