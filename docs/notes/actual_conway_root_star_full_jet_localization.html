Full-Hermite localization for an actual Conway root star

31 October 2026. This proves an absolute whole-source upper bound and strict child-alias/child-color gaps for the literal scalar

ZN(j0,…,js)=ϕ(hj0ε0𝖳(∏i=1shjiεi)). Z_N(j_0,\ldots,j_s)= \phi\!\left(h_{j_0}^{\varepsilon_0} \mathsf T\!\left(\prod_{i=1}^s h_{j_i}^{\varepsilon_i}\right)\right).

Here hjε=𝖳(RεLj)h_j^\varepsilon=\mathsf T(R^\varepsilon L_j), εi∈{0,1,−1}\varepsilon_i\in\{0,1,-1\}, and s≥1s\ge1 is fixed. It preserves every repeated Casimir root and its first normal. It does NOT prove the remaining root-only far-color gap, a full filling asymptotic, or a norm estimate preserving the optimized action of an arbitrary turned child.

The inputs are the exact color source algebra, the full Newton half-twist transport, and the uniform coherent-core recurrence bound. The root-character Fourier contraction uses the angular estimate below to control root aliases and root-only far colors. The general all-core packet theorem supplies the complementary original wire-window statement. Neither companion is needed to infer the estimates proved here.

6. Boundary of the full-jet estimate

The estimates prove a useful root-independent contraction criterion: if an arbitrary actual child ENE_N has a full-jet rate at most FEF_E, then all the literal root-color contractions (5) have rate at most FEF_E, with the same strict losses whenever present in the child norm. They do not prove that FEF_E equals the optimized closed action for every child.

In particular the turn is not polynomially bounded in this fixed norm: LNL_N has polynomial jets but 𝖳LN=hN\mathsf T L_N=h_N has rate 2G02G_0. An arbitrary turned child’s internal action cannot be preserved by simply declaring this Hilbert or trace norm invariant.

For the product star, the ONLY color case not given a strict loss by (16) is one in which all children remain in the complete corridor but the root color is macroscopic. Formula (4) estimates that root character in absolute value and cannot produce its oscillatory loss. The exact Fourier/Hermite contraction is still required for that case; it is supplied by the independently proved root-character theorem. In particular this note alone does not restore the full filling period at its action below the complete value, and asserts no new long-twist volume equality.
