A finite original-network source envelope at the resonant boundary

30 October 2026. Fix the labeled positive right-angled matching-polyhedron certificate, actual cut and original white-prefix dictionary of an augmented diagram with one main component. On its single common boundary chart, the actual complete ROOT tensor summand is nonzero. Its specified regular complete-circle Hopf lift has fixed-quantum-jet upper bounds of polynomial cost times (CN^C) to the sum of the channel deficits. This includes all six original numerator types, both folded parities, 202/202 pairs and c=1.

A positive triangulation alone does not supply the required polyhedron and prefix data.

This is an UNCONTRACTED finite-network theorem. It retains the exact first-normal identity for the original complete source, but supplies no bound or noncancellation statement for its contracted finite sum or relative period. The generic lift is the regular W pi lift already proved for that identity, not the exceptional K/E germ and not the entire moving-q germ of a complete crossing circle.

1. Specify the original generic tensor summand

Fix the original marked 2k-plat D with R complete-circle boxes and retained half-twist signs epsilon_i. Put N>=3, d=N-1, q0=exp(pi i/N), zeta=q0 exp(epsilon), Q=zeta^2. Fix actual folded labels a_i, 0<=a_i<=floor(d/2), and write

m_i=d-a_i, s_i=d-2a_i, S=sum_i s_i.

Use EXACTLY the regular Hopf lift from the full matching-graph source, sections 3–4. Its channel weights are held at their original root values

h0_a=k_a N s_(2a)/s_a^2,
omega_a=q0^(d^2/2)(-1)^a q0^[a(a+1)],
k_a=1 if 2a=d and k_a=2 otherwise,
gamma_a=q0^(2-a^2)/N^2.                            (1)

They are all nonzero, including both maximal folded parities. In particular h0_M=N at odd N and h0_M=2N/sin(pi/N) at even N. Gamma_a is a FIXED root constant, not a generic norm to invert.

The original generic white has the specified finite continuation

W_a(i,j;r)=(-1)^m zeta^[-dr+di-m(m-1)]
          H_m^(Q^-N)(Q^i,Q^j)/F_m,
i+j=m+r, F_m=(Q;Q)_m.                              (2)

It is the actual LARGE source, m=d-a, in both physical degree halves. For a fixed highest weight L<N define the original continued cup

p_L(x)=exp(pi i x) zeta^[x(L-x-1)]
                Fhat_P(x)Fhat_P(L-x)/F_L,
pV(x)=N zeta^(-d^2)p_d(x), P=pi i/Log zeta.          (3)

Here Fhat_P is the specified moving-period Gamma product, agreeing with the exact generic finite factorial at its admissible integer arguments. The ordered cap is 1/p_L(L-x), with the same physical scale when L=d. The complete-circle retraction is literally

pi_a=gamma_a^-1 p_(2a)^T W_a^T
                       (ellV^T tensor ellV^T)P_rev. (4)

An explicit coefficient, useful for retaining every orientation, is

pi_a(r;i,j)
  =gamma_a^-1 p_(2a)(2a-r)
        W_a(d-j,d-i;2a-r)/[pV(d-j)pV(d-i)].         (5)

The exact generic Chevalley identity also gives W_a(d-j,d-i;2a-r)=zeta1W_a(i,j;r). Thus (5) retains the original channel cup, TWO physical inverse cups, reversed tensor order and gamma_a. It divides by no paired root norm: pi_a W_a=0 at a paired node is not a denominator in this theorem.

Expand every original coupon with (2),(4), and use the original physical cups/caps and cyclic turns. Impose their literal integral affine head constraints on an admissible stratum, with any primitive free-lattice chart h=h0+A x. Let S_F(epsilon,h) be this ordinary CLOSED tensor summand, including product_i h0_(a_i)omega_(a_i)^epsilon_i and an ordinary cap at the exposed marked arc. At admissible integer heads it is the actual finite summand of this specified regular Hopf lift. No head is wrapped, and no support inequality is removed. Constant forbidden strata retain their original zero value rather than being assigned a meromorphic 0/0 continuation.

2. The one common geometric head assignment

Use the original common boundary chart, with r_frame=N rho, rho in a positive real compact, and s_frame=exp(-2pi i alpha)-1, alpha in a fixed sufficiently small positive compact. Every physical head has its ACTUAL canonical lift

i=Nx-1/2, X=exp(2pi i x), 0<Re x<1.

The input/output two-head argument sums lie in the SAME interval (pi,3pi), so their totals agree exactly, not just modulo N. Cups have complementary lifts summing to d. Auxiliary heads are defined by r0=i+j-(d-a), and both ends of each matching edge agree after the recorded cup/dual reversal. Therefore h_N lies in the original complex affine head chart. These are samples of the complete geometric representation, not assertions of finite-a stationarity.

Require the shrinking-fold range S log N=o(N). For the estimates it is enough first to assume each 0<=s_i<=eta N, for a sufficiently small fixed eta. Then a_i/N stays in a fixed interior neighborhood of one half and all the following margins are uniform.

Every physical i and d-i has real part divided by N in a fixed compact subset of (0,1), with imaginary part divided by N of size O(log N). For auxiliary heads put sigma=i+j. Exactly,

r0=a+N(x+y-1),    2a-r0=a-N(x+y-1).                (6)

For 202/222, the canonical total gives x+y=1-i Log(T_infinity)/(2pi)+O(1/N), T_infinity>0. Both auxiliary real parts are consequently a+O(1), within an interior factorial strip. For mixed outer degrees 200/220 and 002/022, the two auxiliary arguments have opposite imaginary parts with absolute value divided by N equal to (log N)/pi+O(1); their real parts divided by N remain bounded. They lie in the nonreal exterior factorial domain. Fixed integer offsets and all actual reversed arguments preserve these margins. The only fixed integer factorials are F_m,F_(2a),F_d and their original finite products, with indices<N.

Thus every factor and inverse in (2)–(5) satisfies the logarithmic-factorial theorem. The actual root and nearby generic factors avoid its poles and zeros. This includes 202/202 and either folded parity; no physical-head half-index offset is assigned to an auxiliary head.

3. Nonzero root product, with every exact factor retained

At the common chart every original white numerator has one of the six proved boundary models. The source-normalized 200/002 Bessel factors are nonzero: their common argument is s_frame sin(pi m/N)/(pi rho), which is nonreal. The 202 whole polynomial lies near its positive total ray, with its actual large-partner product retained. The 220/022 determinant packets are nonzero on their actual negative finite-clock rays. The 222 positive determinant retains its finite clock perturbation and order-one trace correction. No202/202 pair or c=1 case is excluded.

Let B_v,N denote the exact scalar times normalized root model specified by the applicable numerator theorem, evaluated at the ACTUAL finite clocks. Keep every non-numerator factor of S_F(0,h_N) exactly, and replace each H_m only by its B_v,N. Denote the resulting finite product by B_F,N. Then

S_F(0,h_N)=B_F,N[1+O_D(N^-1)],  B_F,N!=0.          (7)

In particular the uncontracted original root summand is nonzero for large N. The original h0_a, gamma_a, omega_a, white monomials, factorials, cups, pivotal clocks, half-index shifts and every integral character remain in B_F,N. They are not normalized to modulus one. Some have exponential or N log N size individually. Equation (7) retains their exact joint product rather than bounding them separately. The finite-clock determinant in 220 and the trace correction in 222 cannot be replaced by their limiting-clock values without their specified order-one factors.

At the original exposed root mark, degree conservation after the other caps forces complementary indices (d-x,x). The literal ratio of that marked row to the ordinary cap is

Xi_N(x)=N^-1 q0^(-d^2) Q0^(d x), Q0=q0^2.          (8)

Consequently the actual marked root summand on this same affine chart is S_M(0,h_N)=Xi_N(x_N)S_F(0,h_N), and has the corresponding nonzero packet Xi_N(x_N)B_F,N. The character in (8) is retained as written: Q0^(dx)=exp(2pi i x)Q0^-x on a literal complex head, not merely Q0^-x. No bare root mark is moved through a cap or promoted to a generic covariant tensor.

For the chosen original first-cut chart its exposed physical clock is A=(1+s_frame)^-1=exp(2pi i alpha), or the recorded complementary clock according to the cap orientation. Thus x_N is real, equal to N alpha-1/2 or N(1-alpha)-1/2, and

|Xi_N(x_N)|=1/N.                                   (9)

This modulus statement is specific to that chosen real-alpha boundary chart. It is not a claim that the marked character is harmless on an arbitrary complex framing chart.

4. Every fixed generic jet of the specified lift

There are constants c_D,C_D and, for each fixed k, C_(D,k) such that on |epsilon|<=c_D N^-10,

|S_F(epsilon,h_N)/S_F(0,h_N)|
                 <=C_D(C_D N^40)^S,                (10)

and hence

|partial_epsilon^k S_F(0,h_N)|
      <=C_(D,k) N^(10k)(C_D N^40)^S
                                   |S_F(0,h_N)|.   (11)

The derivative holds EVERY marked head h_N fixed. Its common geometric construction is not rerun as epsilon varies.

Here is the proof from the actual factors. The genuine 200/002 theorem gives bounded relative numerator variation on this smaller disk when the nonzero Bessel factor is retained. The paired220 theorem gives cost C(CN20)s for 220; exact GENERIC reciprocity gives the same for 022, including its marked complement and scalar. The positive-total theorem gives that bound for 202/222, including possible zero single root edges. Each matching edge has TWO original white numerators after (5) is expanded. Their total cost is at most C_D(C_D N20)(2 sum_i s_i)=C_D(C_D N40)S.

The factorial domain verification in section 2 gives bounded two-sided relative variation for every cup factor, its inverse and fixed factorial on the larger disk c/[N^2(1+log N)^2]. All monomial exponents have size O_D(N^2(1+log N)^2), so their ratios are bounded on our smaller disk. Gamma_a, h0_a and omega_a are the fixed root constants of the specified lift and have relative ratio one. There are only finitely many factors for fixed D. This proves (10) and its holomorphy directly in the original regular factors. Cauchy’s formula proves (11).

Thus the cost is polynomial for bounded total deficit S, including maximal folded channels at both level parities, and subexponential whenever S log N=o(N). No fixed nonzero scaled channel-width estimate is asserted. Nor is a nonzero leading normal amplitude inferred from this relative UPPER bound.

5. The unchanged original first-normal identity and its limit

Let A_D,N be the original complete root source before its separately recorded exterior framing line. On each label sector the specified regular Hopf lift has ordinary closed value F_a(epsilon)=D_N(epsilon)z_a(epsilon), with

D_N(epsilon)=(-1)^d[N]_(q0 exp(epsilon)),
nu_N=D_N'(0)=2N(-1)^N/(q0-q0^-1)!=0.

The exact source theorem therefore remains

A_D,N=nu_N^-1 sum_a sum_(h in original support(a))
                              partial_epsilon S_F(0,h). (12)

All sums in (12) are finite and retain their original affine constraints, support, parity and mark. The full paired-circle first normal is already contained in h0_a W_a pi_a at the root. No paired norm is inverted, and no Hermite sector is removed. The regular lift need not be the whole moving-q circle: any two regular generic Hopf lifts with the same root coupon give the same marked scalar, because their closed difference is D_N times a holomorphic open scalar vanishing at zero.

Combining (9),(11) with |nu_N|=N/sin(pi/N) gives the local bound

|nu_N^-1 partial_epsilon S_F(0,h_N)|
   <=C_D N^9(C_D N^40)^S |S_M(0,h_N)|.              (13)

This retains the actual first-normal normalization and the actual mark ratio. It is still a bound at the sampled complex head assignment. Equation (12) is NOT evaluated by replacing its finite supported summands by (13), moving their cycles to this chart, or identifying the regular lift with the exceptional K/E germ. A contraction can vanish although every local boundary factor in (7) is nonzero. The original coherent band cycles, their boundary residues and the global relative framing coefficient remain to be determined.
