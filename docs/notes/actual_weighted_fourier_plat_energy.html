Source-preserving weighted Fourier energy for the original plat

28 October 2026. There is an exact positive factorial gauge which fixes every original plat turn. It gives a concrete family of weighted Fourier norms and a finite convex upper-bound problem for the original scalar. Its gauges can be exponentially conditioned; no such condition number is paid at the paired source. Whole inverse crossing pairs can be contracted before taking positive norms.

This does not give the sharp hyperbolic-volume estimate. In particular, same-frame diagonal scaling cannot reduce the local crossing’s flat Hilbert–Schmidt norm. A short exact doubled-source calculation below identifies the coherent sewing probability which still needs an estimate.

1. The physical factorial gauge fixes the original paired source

Put Q=exp(2 pi i/N), d=N-1, x=-exp(-pi i/N), F_a=(Q;Q)_a. The exact root reflection identity is

F_a F_(d-a)=N (-1)^a Q^(a(a+1)/2).                 (1)

Consequently the actual middle-certificate frame and its positive part,

Gamma_a=(-1)^a Q^(-a(a-1)/2) F_a^2
        =Q^a |F_a|^2,
L_a=|F_a|^2/N,

obey at EVERY index

Gamma_a Gamma_(d-a)=N^2 Q^(-1),
L_a L_(d-a)=1.                                    (2)

The second relation is an exact equality of positive real numbers. It is not an asymptotic bound near a selected cap index.

Use the original physical cup, ordinary cap, and full marked row:

p_(a,b)=N x^(-b) delta_(a+b,d),
ell_(a,b)=x^b g_a/N delta_(a+b,d),
m_(a,b)=x^b/N^2,
g_a=x^(1-N)Q^(-a).

For EVERY real t, positive powers defined by their real logarithm give

(L^t tensor L^t)p=p,
ell(L^(-t) tensor L^(-t))=ell.                    (3)

The marked row has the same identity on pair degree d. It is not an identity of the unrestricted row m. In an assembled original k-cup plat the exact integer-degree argument forces the final marked pair to have degree d after the ordinary caps are taken. Diagonal gauges preserve that argument. Thus (3) holds for the full marked source in its legitimate whole-network scope.

More generally, choose arbitrary positive numbers A_(r,a) on the odd head of pair r and set the even-head entries to A_(r,d-b)^(-1). The product gauge G fixes p^(tensor k) and its original ordinary/marked co-source exactly. L^t on both heads is the special case (2). These are physical-head transformations; they do not identify a fusion-relative index with a physical one.

For clarity, retain the proved original whole-source normalization from the actual plat Gram theorem:

P_N=chi_N N^(k-1) <phi,W uhat>,
uhat=p^(tensor k)/||p^(tensor k)||,
phi=G_top^dagger uhat, ||phi||=||uhat||=1.           (4)

Here G_top is the original unitary product of ordinary pivots; the marked pair has none. The external chi_N, framing and peripheral line are exactly those in that theorem. The gauge G just defined commutes with G_top and fixes uhat and phi. Therefore, for ANY two such endpoint gauges G_in,G_out,

<phi,G_out W G_in^(-1) uhat>=<phi,W uhat>.           (5)

This provides a genuine source-preserving positive gauge freedom. It does not declare one isolated Gamma in a local five-point word removable; such a local replacement still needs its typed interfaces.

2. Exact weighted Plancherel and the dual metric

Let F be the normalized unitary finite Fourier matrix, F_(r,a)=Q^(ra)/sqrt(N). For a physical head vector v put

vhat_t=F L^t v,
H_t=F L^(-2t) F^dagger.

Then exactly, for arbitrary v,w,

<v,w>=<vhat_t,H_t what_t>,
(H_t)_(r,s)=N^(-1)sum_a Q^((r-s)a)(N/|F_a|^2)^(2t). (6)

H_t is positive definite. These are the compulsory dual norm weights of this factorial Fourier transformation. Flat Fourier energy of L^t v is a different norm from that of v. Equation (2) pairs the weights reciprocally on the original cap charge, explaining the source cancellation in (3); it does not make H_t the identity.

The usual unitary Fourier changes can now be made at every physical wire, with all turns transported. In the G=L^t frame their original turn arrays are unchanged before the unitary Fourier transform. All interior inverse frames cancel literally. Thus this weighted duality extends to any signed original word and its doubled norm, including the marked pair, with no exponential source renormalization.

The size of the weights remains explicit. Uniformly in a,

log L_a=2N J(a/N)+O(log N),
J(s)=integral_0^s log(2 sin(pi u))du.                (7)

Hence (6) carries the full factorial dilogarithm action. It cannot be replaced by a flat positive trace merely because its source normalization is exact.

3. A computable positive energy upper-bound hierarchy

Partition a fixed original word into consecutive actual blocks

W=B_l ... B_1.

Choose arbitrary positive diagonal tensor gauges G_j on all live physical heads between blocks; only the endpoint gauges are required to satisfy the paired-source condition of Section 1. Define

C_j=G_j B_j G_(j-1)^(-1).

All factors, pivots, and sources in (4) remain original. From (5), ordinary Cauchy–Schwarz and submultiplicativity give the finite-N bound

|P_N| <= |chi_N|N^(k-1) product_j ||C_j||_op
       <= |chi_N|N^(k-1) product_j ||C_j||_HS.       (8)

This is not a largest-root or a favorable-source prescription. The right side is an explicit optimization problem in known original block matrices. At the finest partition the blocks are literal crossings, whose entries are given by the finite factorial formula. There is no unknown knot scalar in that objective.

Write a diagonal gauge entry as exp(s_j(I)), where s_j(I) is the sum of its head log entries. Each summand in its logarithmic HS objective is

(1/2)log sum_(I,J) |(B_j)_(I,J)|^2
                       exp(2s_j(I)-2s_(j-1)(J)).   (9)

It is convex in every gauge logarithm: the Hessian of log-sum-exp is the positive covariance matrix of its exponent vectors. The paired endpoint conditions are linear equations in these same logarithms. No minimum-attainment hypothesis is needed to take the infimum in (8). The factorial choices in Section 1 are legitimate points of this feasible set, even when they depend exponentially on N.

Merging neighboring blocks never worsens the best HS bound, since ||AB||_HS <= ||A||_HS ||B||_HS with the intervening gauge cancelled. In particular an actual neighboring R R^(-1) block is exactly the identity, of polynomial HS norm N^k. Its exponential cost is zero. Thus one can perform exact signed-pair and other verified word reductions before taking norms, rather than charging their separate positive crossing energies. This is a constructive renormalization of those pairs with the original source retained.

The same block principle applies to a local contraction containing an actual turn. In particular the defining complete ADO Markov calculation gives, for either sign with its own original zero-framing correction,

Tr_2[Rhat_+ (I tensor g)]=I,
Tr_2[Rhat_- (I tensor g)]=I.                       (9a)

For arbitrary one-head exterior matrices A,B it follows that

Tr_2[(A tensor I) Rhat_sigma (B tensor I)
                     (I tensor g)]=AB, sigma=+,-. (9b)

No division by AB or by a knot period occurs. Thus a genuine curl subnetwork, including its original cap, can be replaced by the identity before forming its doubled positive block energy. Its doubled map is then the identity trace-preserving channel and has zero exponential cost. This works inside any larger knot word. It does not replace an unclosed crossing by a trace-preserving channel. More generally any verified exact local tensor contraction can be used as a block, with its original boundary source retained, before applying (8).

The finest flat bound already proves an unconditional whole-period estimate. For a word of m crossings, the independently proved actual crossing norm theorem gives

log||R_+||_HS=log||R_-||_HS
                =N v_8/(2pi)+O(log N).

Tensoring with a fixed number of spectator identities adds only O(log N). All complete external lines in chi_N have polynomial magnitude. Therefore

limsup_(N->infinity) (2pi/N)log|P_N| <= m v_8.       (10)

The same holds for the original reduced Kashaev scalar, since its complete modified-dimension factor is a known root phase divided by N. This is a crossing bound, not the desired sharp Vol(S^3-K) bound. The convex bound (8)–(9), including exact block reductions, is an operative way to improve it; no assertion is made that it has already reached the hyperbolic volume for a broad knot class.

4. The remaining positive coherent sewing probability

The precise cost of closure can be stated before any scalar-cut reduction. Let W act on one open head tensor an environment of dimension D=N^(b-1), and keep the original unitary environment pivot g. Group vec(W) by its open input/output pair and environment pair. Put

h_g=vec(g^dagger)/sqrt(D),
P_g=I_(open pair) tensor |h_g><h_g|,
C=Tr_env[W(I tensor g)].

P_g is an orthogonal positive projector. Direct expansion of the matrix indices proves

||C||_HS^2=D <vec(W),P_g vec(W)>.                   (11)

This identity holds for nonscalar C. Under any unitary Fourier transform of the half-edge indices, BOTH vec(W) and h_g transform, and the right side is unchanged. It is not the same as replacing the projector by an identity or dephasing its channels.

If the original cut is C=K_N I, the normalized projection probability on the free vectorized word is

p_g=N|K_N|^2/(D||W||_HS^2), 0<=p_g<=1.             (12)

The positive source opening Pi=N g_1 E_d(Z_1Z_2)E_0(X) in the exact cap Fourier theorem satisfies ||(C tensor I)Pi||_HS2=N||C||_HS2 for arbitrary C. Thus it supplies a normalized local positive realization of (11), but leaves its exterior coherent projection in place.

For a normalized single positive crossing (the unknot curl), C=I exactly. With b=2, (12) becomes the all-N identity

p_curl=||R_+||_HS^(-2),
log p_curl= -N v_8/pi+O(log N).                     (13)

This uses the original residual twist and pivotal cap; they have not been reset. It explains why local positive energies can have a larger rate than the closed period. The block renormalization in Section 3 is one exact way of contracting such source effects before bounding; it is not permission to discard the coherent probability for a general knot word.

There is also a sharp restriction on improving a local block by a same-frame positive diagonal gauge. The actual balanced crossing is symmetric, R^t=R. For ANY positive diagonal D,

||D R D^(-1)||_HS^2
   =sum_(i,j)|R_(i,j)|^2 D_i^2/D_j^2
   >=sum_(i,j)|R_(i,j)|^2=||R||_HS^2,              (14)

by pairing i,j and j,i and using r+r^(-1)>=2. Fourier changes do not alter this norm. Thus isolated same-frame factorial scaling cannot remove the regular octahedral rate; an improvement must use actual coupled block contraction or a controlled whole-source estimate.

5. Audit and current scope

The audit passes N=2,…,12. It checks exact factorial reflection, preservation of the original cup/cap and the correctly restricted marker for positive and negative real gauge powers, the non-flat dual Fourier metric, the nonscalar source projector identity, unitary transport of its coherent source, actual crossing symmetry, the local norm minimum, and the normalized unknot-curl probability. Eight complete original four-head plats additionally test the literal ordinary plus full marked source, its whole-degree reduction, unequal paired endpoint gauges, arbitrary internal gauges and the positive block energy bound. The extra external Cartan/framing lines are common unit phases on both sides of those source comparisons. Saved results.

The new constructive point is the original-source-preserving paired factorial gauge and its explicit positive convex energy hierarchy. The sharp geometric estimate for that hierarchy, or a direct bound on its remaining coherent sewing probability, has not been proved. No global geometric coefficient or hyperbolic-volume lower bound is inferred here.
