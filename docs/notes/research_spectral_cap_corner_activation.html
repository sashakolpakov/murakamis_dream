A positive ordinary-zero barrier at the remaining monomial cap corner

The cap-corner variation changes both degree and spectrum at a free maximum. It is not a shrinking-neighborhood finite-NN estimate.

1. The cap corner and the actual feasible direction

At delta=1/2 take the positive-length monomial corner

mu=0, 0<nu0<1/2, xi=0, zeta=nu0.

The original face loads in order (x,b,c,y) are (0,0,1-nu0,nu0). Let an admissible original affine direction give

mu_eps=eps B, xi_eps=eps X, nu_eps=nu0+O(eps),
B,X>=0.

Fix K>0 and choose the open real spectrum by

k_eps^2=cos^2(pi delta_eps)=eps K.                  (1)

Then

h_(delta_eps)(q_eps)
  =(B+X)eps log(1/eps)+O(eps).                     (2)

The base cap value is zero. Thus any activation of the parent middle or the zero outer degree gives a strictly positive singular gain. The other outer-zero case is the literal x/y symmetry; exact finite row reversal gives the protected-output/RIGHT version. These operations do not change original shared targets.

2. Monomer baseline, including the initial removed interval

Put g_k(t)=log[sin^2(pi t)/|k2-sin2(pi t)|]. The sharp action is

h_delta=I_k(nu)-int_0^mu g_k(t)dt+J_k^*,
I_k(nu)=int_0^nu g_k(t)dt,
J_k^*=inf_(x>0)[int_mu^nu log rho_k(t;x)dt-xi log x],
rho_k(t;x)=[1+x+sqrt((1-x)^2+4k^2 x/sin^2(pi t))]/2.

The exact total half integral of g_k is zero. Since nu stays a fixed positive distance from0, the suffix is regular for small k, giving

I_k(nu)=-k^2 cot(pi nu)/pi+O(k^4)=O(eps).          (3)

On 0<=t<=eps B, sin^2(pi t)=pi^2 t2[1+O(t2)] and k^2=eps K. The entire removed interval lies before the small pole when B is fixed. With t=eps s,

g_k(eps s)=log eps+log(pi^2 s^2/K)+O(eps s^2),
int_0^(eps B)g_k(t)dt=B eps log eps+O(eps).         (4)

The logarithm at s=0 is integrable. If B=0 the interval is empty. Equation (4) has one log eps, not two: the unused spectral scale k^2 is itself proportional to eps.

3. Uniform tilted-root estimates at this moving cap scale

Suppose X>0. Let u denote the positive x/y tilt in this section, and put

D=sqrt((1-u)^2+4eta u), eta=k^2/sin^2(pi t).

The exact derivative is

partial_(log u)log rho
    =1/2+(u-1)/(2D)=2eta u/[D(D+1-u)].             (5)

For 0<u<=1/2 it is uniformly comparable to min(1,eta u), with constants independent of k. On the interval under consideration sin(pi t) is comparable to t. With a=k sqrt u, integration therefore gives

partial_(log u)J(mu,nu,u) asymp a^2/(mu+a).       (6)

At any fixed small u0>0, the left side is comparable to k~sqrt eps, which is much larger than the required xi=eps X. Strict tilt convexity thus puts the unique minimizing tilt below u0. Apply (6) at the minimizer: since mu=eps B,

a asymp eps, u=a^2/k^2 asymp eps.                (7)

These constants depend on the fixed B,X,K and nu0, but not eps. There is no reuse of a bound proved only for k bounded away from zero.

Furthermore J>=0 and J<=Ca. To verify the upper bound, for eta u<=1 use log rho<=Ceta u. Where eta u>1 use log rho<=C+log(a/t). Split the integral at t=a; the lower part is O(a), and the upper part is bounded by C int_a^nu a2/t2 dt=O(a). Thus

J=O(eps),
J_k^*=-X eps log eps+O(eps).                     (8)

If X=0 the normalized word is exactly monomial and J_k^*=0. Combining (3), minus (4), and (8) proves (2), including B=0 or X=0.

4. The flat terminal monomial cases

At nu0=1/2, mu=xi=0, the monomer interval is a whole half interval, so h_delta=0 for EVERY delta. At the full interval mu=0,nu=1 with one outer degree zero, the same is true by reflection. At a global free-spectrum maximum one may therefore retune these base ports to ANY fixed strict delta before perturbing their degrees, with no base cost. The fixed-spectrum corner barrier applies to the half-terminal case even if the full-source perturbation extends nu through1/2.

There is a stronger half-terminal conclusion on the full original flow fiber when the perturbation is toward its actual geometric all-CROSS flow. Retune the zero-valued half-terminal monomial base to delta=0. In its exact latent two-triangle split the output triangle is the pure-middle vertex. The geometric output middle is strictly less than 1/2, so dot_nu>0 and that triangle gains

2 dot_nu eps log(1/eps)+O(eps).

The input triangle has no negative singular contribution. The gain persists even when its parent middle and zero outer incidence are both protected. Thus a half-terminal monomial at any initial spectrum cannot occur at the global maximum covered by the full-fiber activation theorem. Exact reflection gives the upper half-terminal case. This uses the full original fiber and its actual latent split, not a relabeling inside a fixed LEFT or RIGHT support.

For completeness the full two-terminal corner has the following exact fixed-spectrum extension. Set mu=eps B, 1-nu=eps C and keep delta in a strict compact. If both base outer loads are positive, use xi=xi0+eps dot_xi with 0<xi0<1. The endpoint expansions Lambda~constant times t and constant times(1-t) give coefficient B+C of eps log(1/eps). At the monomial outer corner instead set xi=eps X. Then the beta baseline contributes 2(B+C), and the tilted mean is comparable to

u/(eps B+sqrt u)+u/(eps C+sqrt u).

For X>0 this forces u~eps^2; the word action is O(eps) and its Legendre term contributes 2X. For X=0 it is exactly zero. The resulting coefficient is 2(B+C+X), or its outer reflection. Thus these flat monomial corners also have a positive activation barrier whenever an ordinary zero is activated. This two-terminal statement is about the full original interval, not a simultaneous use of two independently chosen half rows.

5. Scope of a shared use

Equation (2) can be applied at every remaining monomial cap corner in the same original feasible segment. If B=X=0, both zero incidences stay zero; one can simply keep the flat cap value zero, or use the uniform cap-retuning lower bound to make its cost negligible. When a zero incidence is ordinary and the actual geometric all-CROSS direction is used, its activation slope is strictly positive. If both zeros are protected, both slopes vanish and there is no ordinary zero to remove.

This lemma does not, by itself, classify every possible cap maximum. It must be combined with the separate fixed-load cap exclusions and the other local directional estimates. In particular the negative pole-start singularity at a different non-diagonal port cannot be ignored.

For each fixed eps>0 the new delta lies strictly inside the real spectral interval. Evaluate the sharp continuum functional at that fixed eps, then let eps tend to zero in the variational contradiction. If a separate coefficient identification is used, its N limit is taken with eps fixed. No simultaneous N-dependent retuning, nonzero bound through an omitted-half zero, sharp Fourier-cycle contribution, or volume comparison is asserted.

Scope

The monomial corner and strict-base outer pattern are distinct. The comparison uses the actual sharp functional, not the fractional replacement.
