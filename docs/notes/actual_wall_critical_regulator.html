Every regular full wall critical action has the actual representation regulator

28 October 2026. The nine-atom full-Psi scalar crossing has the SAME Bloch–Wigner regulator as the original five-dilogarithm ADO crossing, for both signs, on its entire regular rational graph. This is proved without inferring a regulator from holonomy alone: their Bloch wedges are identical, the parameter chart is connected, and a real chart fixes the regulator constant.

Integer norm/carry marks add 2pii times logarithms to the action. At every regular FULL interior critical point these terms cancel from the imaginary critical value by an exact real-quadratic identity. Consequently the critical value is the regulator of the actual boundary-parabolic knot representation, after the existing marked fundamental-cycle comparison. This controls critical VALUES, not the original cycle’s selected coefficient or its singular/escaping contributions.

The audit and results verify both rational crossing maps in all six Gauss coordinates, factor the Bloch wedges identically over Q(A,B,z,w), check the real normalization and complex regulators, and verify the off-critical integer-mark identity. No literature proof is used in these calculations.

1. The actual nine atom graphs

Use physical clocks A,B and head coordinates z,w at complete color x=-1. At a current pair (a,b,p,r), the elementary principal maps are:

Gate Bloch contribution Position/clock update
U diagonal on i -[1-B_i] z_i -> z_i(1-B_i)
F diagonal on i +[1-B_i] z_i -> z_i/(1-B_i)
U_- -[t], t=-r/p a -> a/(1-t), b -> b(1-t)
V_- +[t], t=-r/p a -> a(1-t), b -> b/(1-t)
U_+ -[t], t=-p/r a -> a(1-t), b -> b/(1-t)
V_+ +[t], t=-p/r a -> a/(1-t), b -> b(1-t)
D_+ 0 r -> -r/b
D_- 0 r -> -br

Read the original strict factorization in traversal order:

C^(-1): U_2, V_-, U_1, F_2;
positive center: U_+, D_+;
negative center: D_-, V_+;
C: U_2, F_1, U_-, F_2.                         (1)

The nine nonzero Bloch entries define beta_9^+ or beta_9^-. All are explicit rational functions of A,B,z,w. This is the actual scalar full-Psi action, with its reflected monomials and Gaussian retained.

The physical generators are

K=-1/a, e=(a^(-1)-1)/p, f=(1-a)p.              (2)

Direct rational substitution in (1) gives EXACTLY the independently proved actual crossing maps (4.6)–(4.7) of the marked braid calculation. This is checked algebraically for every one of the six output Gauss coordinates; equality of maps is not inferred from equality of finite root-level matrices or from a presumed saddle selection.

2. Regulator equality with the original coefficient symbol

Let C_+=(1-e_1 f_2)^(-1), and C_-=1-(B/A)e_2 f_1. The original coefficient Bloch symbols are

beta_5^+=[B]+[C_+]+[1/A]-[BC_+]-[C_+/A],
beta_5^-=[A]+[C_-]+[1/B]-[AC_-]-[C_-/B].       (3)

On the common regular rational crossing graph,

delta(beta_9^sign-beta_5^sign)=0
           in Lambda^2(Q(A,B,z,w)^* tensor Q).    (4)

There are two independent ways to check (4). For a signed dilog generating expression, its wedge is its incoming symplectic boundary minus the outgoing boundary; the internal monomial coordinates cancel, and symmetric real quadratic/Gaussian terms contribute zero. Both graph maps in Section 1 are the same actual map. In the physical position convention the common boundary is

z wedge A+w wedge B-z_out wedge A_out-w_out wedge B_out.

In the original coefficient convention it is A wedge p_in,1 plus B wedge p_in,2 minus the two outgoing terms, with p=z^(-1), so these are identical. The complete color -1 is torsion and disappears rationally. Alternatively factor every rational shape and its complement into irreducibles; the audit verifies the full antisymmetric coefficient matrix of (4) is identically zero for BOTH signs. Rational constant factors are retained in this check, not silently discarded.

The differential of the Bloch–Wigner regulator D is a homomorphism of the multiplicative wedge: for one shape its differential is log|a| dArg(1-a)-log|1-a| dArg a. Thus (4) gives

d D(beta_9^sign-beta_5^sign)=0.                 (5)

The regular parameter space is a nonempty complex Zariski-open subset of (C*)4, obtained by removing finitely many rational zeros, poles and shapes equal to 1. It is connected: connect generic points by complex affine lines avoiding the finitely many excluded points on each line. Hence (5) is a single real constant on this space.

Every rational argument in (1)–(3) has REAL coefficients. A generic real input avoids the finite excluded algebraic sets. Every shape is then real, so EVERY D value is zero. Therefore that constant is zero:

D(beta_9^+)=D(beta_5^+),
D(beta_9^-)=D(beta_5^-).                       (6)

This fixes exactly the regulator information needed here. No claim is made that wedge equality alone identifies an integral Bloch class, an integral flattening, or a complex period torsor.

3. Exact cancellation of integer wall marks at full critical points

Use logarithmic coordinates Z=rho+i theta. Consider

W(Z)=sum_j epsilon_j Li_2(A_j(Z))
                   +1/2 Z^t C Z+i kappa^t Z+c,
A_j(Z)=exp(a_j^t Z+i*pi*nu_j),                 (7)

where a_j,nu_j,C,kappa are real, C is symmetric, and Im c=0. Branches of Li_2 and Log(1-A_j) are matched; D remains its single-valued Bloch–Wigner value on the recorded local sheet. On its ordinary unflattened local branch the exact identity is

Im W-sum_j epsilon_j D(A_j)
             =rho^t Im(gradient W).           (8)

Indeed Im Li_2(A)=D(A)-log|A| Arg(1-A), and log|A_j|=a_j^t rho. Also Im(1/2 Z^t C Z)=rho^t C theta, while Im(i kappa^t Z)=kappa^t rho. The right side of (8) is precisely this difference. Consistent alternative dilog branches carry their explicit integer logarithmic marks into kappa and the real constant; one must not change the Li_2 sheet without its mark.

A simple atomic negative-ray source has its retained factor A_j^N. In exp(W/h), h=2pii/N, it adds 2pii Log A_j. Transverse iterated wall charges add integer sums of such logarithms. They only change kappa by real integer multiples of 2pia_j and add a REAL constant from ipinu_j. Equation (8) remains true. At a FULL critical point,

gradient W=0  ==>  Im W=sum_j epsilon_j D(A_j). (9)

The integer norm charges therefore do not create an additional critical exponential rate log|A_j| after the critical equations are imposed. Dropping full stationarity would make this conclusion false: the residual term on the right side of (8) is then present.

The actual scalar action is of the form (7). Put eta_s=sqrt(2piis)xi_s at each Gaussian. Its original Gaussian term -piixi_s^2 becomes -seta_s^2/2, a REAL quadratic. The actual atomic arguments are signed monomials in the head positions and exp(eta_s). For each coupled cap put p=2piis_cap; its interval term is p Log r, a REAL bilinear form in logarithmic coordinates. Original reflected monomials and complete-color signs give the ikappa terms. Thus Gaussian and cap variables must be retained in the FULL stationarity condition of (9).

4. What regular wall stationary points represent

An integer logarithmic wall mark shifts derivatives by multiples of 2pii but leaves the EXPONENTIATED canonical equations unchanged. At a finite regular full interior critical point these are the same actual rational crossing/Hurwitz maps as in Section 1 and the same inverse-meridian cap equations proved in the coupled-cap note. They define a representation of the original oriented knot group, with the original marked source and preferred peripheral convention.

For a noncentral complete meridian the peripheral longitude commutes with a nontrivial unipotent matrix, whose centralizer in PSL(2,C) is the unipotent one-parameter group. Thus this is a genuinely boundary-parabolic representation. A cut wall is not generally a physical clock B=1: its DILOG argument A_j is negative real, and an auxiliary flat atom can occur over a noncentral representation.

The restriction “full” includes regular wall chains whose remaining real tangent directions span the full complex cotangent space. Their interior complex stationary equation is gradient W=0. It excludes endpoint extrema, rank-deficient/nontransverse corner strata, escaping flags, and singular sources where a constrained stationary equation does not imply the full equation.

5. The marked fundamental-cycle comparison extends beyond faithfulness

The proof in the crossing fundamental-cycle note uses unipotence, the actual Hurwitz words, oriented crossing balls, face cancellation and a degree-one peripheral reconstruction. None of its determinant/five-term certificates requires the DEVELOPING representation rho to be faithful. The group used in its relative configuration complex is the original hyperbolic knot group Gamma; its peripheral malnormality concerns Gamma, independently of rho.

For any boundary-parabolic rho with noncentral meridians, choose its fixed peripheral flags and generic auxiliary flags. Forced repeated DEVELOPED vertices map to zero in the normalized alternating configuration complex. This is a chain map: repeated-vertex face terms cancel in pairs. The same explicit three five-term boundaries therefore give the rho-developed relative fundamental cycle. Unipotent cancellation uses a nonzero translation because the meridians are noncentral; central representations are handled separately and have trivial PSL image in the one-component case.

Consequently, summing (6) over the actual word and keeping the original turn orientations gives

sum_crossings D(beta_9)=Vol(rho).              (10)

Caps contribute their original pairing/marking, not an additional independently assigned tetrahedral regulator. The preferred-longitude correction is the same determinant-one writhe shear as in the fundamental-cycle proof. When a finite developed shape approaches 0,1 or infinity, D extends continuously by zero, so normalized degenerate tetrahedra do not add an unknown constant. This continuity does not justify a quantum source limit at a singular chart.

Combining (9)–(10), EVERY regular FULL interior wall critical action in the recorded actual source has its actual representation regulator. The separately proved sharp representation-volume bound therefore gives |Im W_critical|<=Vol(M) for all these regular full critical values. This statement alone gives neither a global contour bound nor a selected geometric lower bound. Normalization ray cells can have the full geometric rate, as the retained source calculations already show; they must be grouped exactly rather than called negligible.
