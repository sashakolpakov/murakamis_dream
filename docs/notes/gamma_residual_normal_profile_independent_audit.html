An exact pointed unit comparison for the factorial source factor

Proof and independent audit, 27 October 2026. The normal quantum factorial residual has the stated whole Gamma profile. More strongly, its middle component is exactly a holomorphic unit times the actual root Weyl splitting after the correct normal-coordinate change. The unit preserves all finite resonance zeros, is normalized at the complete point, and tends uniformly to one. This is an actual scalar source-factor comparison, not a comparison of the full knot period.

Use the exact factorial frames of the integral-color source and the marked root splitting of the balanced Weyl cocycle.

5. Independent check of the full-head extension

The stronger full-head theorem has also been checked directly. With n=N−1n=N-1, L=⌊n/2⌋L=\lfloor n/2\rfloor, and partial βj(M)=(M2Q0;Q0)j/(Q0;Q0)j\beta_j(M)=(M^2Q_0;Q_0)_j/(Q_0;Q_0)_j, its complement is βj(M)βn−j(M−1)=M2j−nSN(M2)NMn. \beta_j(M)\beta_{n-j}(M^{-1}) =M^{2j-n}\frac{S_N(M^2)}{NM^n}. \tag{12} Indeed reversing the n−jn-j suffix indices sends each ratio to M−2(1−M2Q0r)/(1−Q0r)M^{-2}(1-M^2Q_0^r)/(1-Q_0^r). Multiplying that suffix by the prefix gives M−2(n−j)SN(M2)/NM^{-2(n-j)}S_N(M^2)/N, proving (12). Thus the upper source section is defined by the canceled complementary product; no inverse is evaluated at a zero.

For j≤Lj\le L, the exact unit is (8) with product length jj and no even-level scalar prefactor. Its O(1/N)O(1/N) estimate is uniform over all prefix lengths by the same double difference. For j>Lj>L, symmetric q-binomial reflection gives VN,j=Mcoln−2jVN,n−j; V_{N,j}=M_{\mathrm{col}}^{n-2j}V_{N,n-j}; the remaining upper comparison character has logarithm (H−H0)b(n−2j)/2=O(1/N)(H-H_0)b(n-2j)/2=O(1/N), uniformly in jj. This proves the claimed whole-head diagonal unit estimate.

The retained chirp comparison has the exact exponent log⁡(Ucol/U0)j=ca(j2N−j(j+1)4N2). \log (U_{\mathrm{col}}/U_0)_j =ca\left(\frac{j}{2N}-\frac{j(j+1)}{4N^2}\right). It and its inverse are uniformly bounded in every common diagonal metric on a fixed compact normal set. The same conclusion holds for the whole source comparison TNT_N.

The coupon identities in the full-head theorem are exact coordinate statements. Both frames act on the same original balanced column (1,…,1)t(1,\ldots,1)^t and dual row (1,…,1)/N(1,\ldots,1)/N; their respective coordinate maps are Ucoldiag⁡VU_{\mathrm{col}}\operatorname{diag}V and U0diag⁡BU_0\operatorname{diag}B. Their quotient is TNT_N, hence the columns, rows and rank-one coupons transform by TNT_N, TN−1T_N^{-1}, and conjugation. Position moments and endpoint projectors commute with this diagonal comparison. At a resonance this statement uses the paired fractional lattices, whose abstract tensor pairing remains one; their singular coordinate arrays cannot be specialized separately.

The root chart is a meromorphic gauge of the same actual root head. All root operators must therefore conjugate with the coupon. No equality with the moving-q crossing follows: the independent spectral proof rules out even arbitrary scalar-adjusted same-fiber similarity.
