Positive angular coordinates for the octahedral edge blocks

1 November 2026.

Each edge in an ordered tree of crossing circles contributes two octahedra to the cut link exterior, one in each projection copy. This note gives their vertices and tetrahedron shapes as explicit functions of the two endpoint longitude parameters. The shapes remain positive on a fixed complex disk, independently of the size of the tree. The two-triangle disk boundaries depend only on their own circle parameter, which is what allows incident blocks with different other endpoints to be joined.

The ordered decomposition identifies these cells and all their labels with the original link exterior. The completion proof then follows their actual logarithmic periods to the smooth filling. These are the geometric inputs to the quantitative volume theorem, whose angular path is supplied by the analytic criterion.

3. Uniform positive tetrahedra on an explicit complex bidisk

6. Assembly in the original ordered exterior

The ordered decomposition constructs the following finite assembly. Associate the two octahedra above to each physical edge of the circle tree. At a circle vertex, list its incident edges in the actual cyclic order and join the corresponding outgoing and incoming disk-side pants cyclically. Insert the prescribed retained half twist at its specified disk page. Table (14) supplies geometric seam matching for a common angular variable at that circle. The complete model has two octahedra per edge and DD meridional sectors at a degree-DD circle.

The topological proof cuts each original circle neighborhood into ordered disk pages and uses terminal-vertex insertion to identify the two-leaf block at each physical quarter-turn, including its four marked main segments. The two-circle endpoint pairs are interlaced by the actual quarter-turn. The proof retains that matching and every left or right insertion in the original product order. It also proves that the primitive meridian crosses the pages once in their cyclic order, with only the single recorded half-twist shear. These facts justify the actual paths used in (17).

At a distinguished odd seam one uses the original puncture-and-copy exchange, rather than an arbitrary permutation of the three pants punctures. The original matrices and their three full projective edge-return words are given in Section 3 of the completion proof. Table (14) transports that specified map between incident blocks with a common longitude parameter.

Together, (10), (14), and the actual period equations provide the positive cells and link data used by that completion. The analytic criterion keeps every angular parameter below 0.001110.00111, within the radius 0.010.01 proved here. The complete conclusion and its precise class of diagrams are stated in the quantitative volume theorem.
