The geometric contribution for positive and signed circle paths

Research proof, 3 November 2026. This note belongs to the continuing research project; the frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

For an original circle-tree presentation whose graph is a path, the geometric point contributes with a nonzero coefficient whenever every selected crossing power has magnitude at least eight. Signs may differ and all auxiliary vertices may be selected. Positive powers at least seven also suffice when the stipulated complete outside endpoint is retained. The path and its powers are fixed before the quantum level tends to infinity.

Section 7 proves these bounds and the resulting original Kashaev knot limit. Sections 1–5 retain the source normalization and the earlier threshold-sixty-four argument. The improved comparison is still local: completing selected vertices costs less than one tenth per incidence, whereas the corresponding original removed rows lose more than 21/20021/200 per incidence.

This does not settle the finite-exception problem. A power below the stated threshold can remain fixed while other powers grow through infinitely many assignments.
