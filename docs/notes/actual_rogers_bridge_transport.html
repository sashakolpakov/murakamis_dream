Actual Rogers blocks and the full marked source at arbitrary bridge number

28 October 2026. This note connects the newly proved finite Rogers transform to the ORIGINAL two-head fusion kernel and folds all the actual plat turns through that kernel. The result is an exact recursion for any admissible colored 2k-plat, with one explicit marked output row, all other outputs fixed corners, and no restriction on bridge number. Every total integer degree is retained. This is a root-family identity in the full meridian, not a moving-q extension or a volume theorem.

The all-strand regular fusion theorem, Euler word completion, and regular canonical fusion graph already give much of the underlying matrix transport. The new assertions here are the exact Rogers realization on every actual degree block, the ordinary-turn corner formulas, the original marked-cap row and its degree-restricted closed form, their simultaneous arbitrary-plat recursion, and the exact final Ward-corner reduction. They do not turn a basis-change loop into a positive tetrahedron or a source-preserving geometric 2↔︎3 theorem.

1. Every actual degree block is q-Pascal

Fix a primitive Nth root Q, d=N-1, and F_a=(Q;Q)_a for 0<=a<N. The original balanced heads have raising coefficients 1-Q^(a+1) and clock x Q^-a. For two heads the actual fusion is

C_x(u,v; a,b) = x^(u-a) F_a F_v / [F_(a-u) F_b],
0<=u<=a, v=a+b-u<N,                                  (1)

and zero otherwise. In particular it preserves the TOTAL INTEGER degree, not only its value modulo N. Its diagonal entries are F_a, so it is invertible over the whole Laurent field in x.

For total degree s put

I_s = {max(0,s-d),...,min(d,s)}, 0<=s<=2d.

Using a for the first input and u for the first output, the block is

C_(x,s)(u,a)
   = [x^u F_u F_(s-u)] {a choose u}_Q
                                  [x^-a/F_(s-a)].     (2)

The binomial is zero when u>a. Thus EVERY degree block is an exact row/column gauge of the corresponding contiguous principal q-Pascal block. The inverse Pascal block is explicit:

P_s^-1(u,a)=(-1)^(a-u)Q^((a-u)(a-u-1)/2)
                                  {a choose u}_Q.    (3)

To prove (3), multiply the two triangular matrices and use {a choose v}{v choose u}={a choose u}{a-u choose v-u}. The remaining sum is (1;Q)_(a-u), which is zero unless a=u. The same proof applies to each I_s because the intermediate indices between u and a all belong to that interval. No factorial of index N is divided out.

In particular the ACTUAL inverse block, with row u and column a, is

C_(x,s)^-1(u,a)
   =x^(u-a)(-1)^(a-u)Q^((a-u)(a-u-1)/2)
      F_(s-u)/[F_u F_(a-u) F_(s-a)]  for u<=a,

and zero otherwise. This follows by reversing the two diagonal gauges in (2), and specifies every inverse kernel used below.

On the cap block s=d, let

R(k,n)=Q^(n-k+1){n choose k}_Q, 0<=k,n<N.

This is the matrix in the arbitrary-x Rogers convolution proved in the finite whole-source transform. It is literally an actual fusion block with the following gauges:

R(k,n) = L_k C_(x,d)(k,n) B_n,
L_k=Q^(1-k)x^-k/[F_k F_(d-k)],
B_n=Q^n x^n F_(d-n).                                 (4)

Thus that finite transform is available as a change of actual fusion coordinates; it is not merely an unrelated operator on Newton coefficients. Equation (4), however, realizes one degree block, not an invariant subspace for an arbitrary plat word.

2. Actual ordinary turns become corners

Write xvee=x^-1 Q^-1 for the actual dual color. All formulas below use the original balanced turns from the bridge source calculation, including their factors N and 1/N and the original pivot.

For a physical pair (V(x),V(xvee)),

coev_L(x) = C_x |d,0>,
ev_R(x) C_x = (1/N)<0,d|.                            (5)

For the opposite order (V(xvee),V(x)),

coev_R(x) = C_(xvee) |d,0>,
ev_L(x) C_(xvee) = (1/N)<0,d|.                       (6)

For the cups, (1) at (a,b)=(d,0) gives the coefficient N x^(u-d). This is exactly the original left cup. With the first color xvee it is exactly the original right cup, because (xvee)^(u-d)=x^(d-u)Q^(d-u).

For completeness, a supported physical row ell(u,d-u)=kappa lambda^u has

(ell C_x)(a,d-a)
  =kappa N x^-a (Q lambda x;Q)_a/F_(d-a).            (7)

Indeed use

F_u F_(d-u)=N(-1)^u Q^(u(u+1)/2)

in (1), and then the finite q-binomial theorem. The actual right cap has kappa=1/N, lambda=xvee, so Q lambda x=1. All a>0 entries vanish and the a=0 entry is 1/N. This proves (5), and the same argument proves (6). The vanished Pochhammer is the WHOLE polynomial factor, rather than a numerical value selected after specialization.

3. The original marked cap is an explicit nonconstant row

The actual reduced source is represented by the marked right cap

ev_R^D(u,v)=x^v/N^2,                                 (8)

with no restriction on u+v. This is the original averaged source; it is not an ordinary intertwining cap. Pulling (8) through (1) gives the following exact row on the ENTIRE relative two-head space:

m_x(a,b) := (ev_R^D C_x)(a,b)
   = x^b F_a/N^2
       sum_(h=0)^min(a,d-b) {b+h choose b}_Q.         (9)

To check (9), substitute v=a+b-u in (8). The x powers become x^(v+u-a)=x^b. Set h=a-u in the remaining factorial quotient; then F_(b+h)/(F_h F_b)={b+h choose b}. This calculation includes every source wall and every total-degree block.

There is a useful further simplification in a whole plat. All k cups start in total degree kd, every actual crossing preserves that integer degree, and each of the k-1 ordinary output caps in (5)–(6) forces degree d. Therefore the marked pair is automatically in degree d. There (9) is the closed row

mu_x(r) := m_x(r,d-r)
  = x^(d-r)(-1)^r Q^(-r(r+1)/2) F_r^2/N^2,
  0<=r<N.                                           (10)

For a direct proof of its inner sum, reflect the Gaussian:

{d-r+h choose h}_Q
   =(-1)^h Q^(-rh+h(h-1)/2){r choose h}_Q.

Summing h=0,…,r gives (Q^-r;Q)_r=(-1)^r Q^(-r(r+1)/2)F_r, yielding (10). In particular every mu_x(r) is nonzero for x!=0, and

mu_x(r+1)/mu_x(r)
   = -x^-1 Q^(-r-1)(1-Q^(r+1))^2.                  (11)

No normal direction in x, and hence no meridian derivative at fixed Q, has been discarded. For example x d/dx mu_x(r)=(d-r)mu_x(r). At the canonical unit root Q=exp(2pi i/N),

mu_x(r)=x^(d-r)/N^2
              product_(j=1)^r [2 sin(pi j/N)]^2.      (12)

Put w_N(r)=|F_r|^2/N^2>0. There is an explicit coherent gauge of the WORD implementing the positive boundary row, rather than merely an observation about its coefficients. Choose the same designated pair at every horizontal slice and let

G_boldx |a_1,b_1;...;a_k,b_k>
               = x_*^(b_*) |a_1,b_1;...;a_k,b_k>.

Here x_* is that pair’s current first color, updated after crossings. For every letter replace (14) below by

B_i^(epsilon,G)=G_boldx' B_i^epsilon G_boldx^-1.

The input cup corners have b_*=0, so G_initial fixes them. Therefore the final scalar can use weights w_N(r) and the entries of B_beta^G=G_final B_beta G_initial^-1 exactly. All intermediate gauges cancel. On |x_j|=1 this is a unitary diagonal change at every slice; for general nonzero x_j it remains an invertible Laurent change retaining the whole meridian. The crossing kernels remain complex, so positivity of this actual SOURCE row is not a no-cancellation theorem for the whole contraction.

The empty one-bridge word has r=d as its sole input/output entry. The full identity (-1)^d Q^(d(d+1)/2)=1 gives mu_x(d)=1. Hence (10) reproduces the original unknot normalization at both parities without replacing the marked cap by an ordinary cap.

4. Strict actual crossing cores, including both signs and colors

Let J_+|a>=|a+1> and J_-|a>=|a-1>, with their actual top and bottom arrows zero. Put X_+=J_+ tensor J_-; this preserves the integer total degree and has X_+^N=0. Set

u_N(t)=sum_(h=0)^d t^h/F_h,
v_N(t)=sum_(h=0)^d (-1)^h Q^(h(h-1)/2)t^h/F_h,
D_(x,y)|a,b>=(-1)^b Q^(b(b-1)/2)(xy)^-b |a,b>.

Then u_N(X)v_N(X)=I for every nilpotent X of order at most N. The ORIGINAL checked crossings satisfy

R_+^(x,y) = kappa_(x,y) C_y
                  D_(x,y) u_N(x^-1 X_+) C_x^-1,
R_-^(x,y) = kappa_(y,x)^-1 C_y
                  v_N(y^-1 X_+) D_(x,y)^-1 C_x^-1.   (13)

Here every common Cartan scalar kappa is the original unrolled one, or its prescribed external Hom-line factor; it is not reset to one. The negative formula is the inverse of the positive formula with input colors reversed. Formula (13) is STRICT for these noncyclic head shifts. It differs from extending the coefficient algebra by cyclic right multiplication, whose positive-degree-N completion is only source-equivalent on the closed-braid algebra.

One proof of (13) is to multiply its triangular kernels and use the finite q-binomial theorem. Equivalently, the already checked actual right coefficient is

kappa sum_(h+b<N)
   (-1)^b Q^(b(b-1)/2)x^-h(xy)^-b f^h e_b/F_h.

Its degree-preserving action on a relative input (a,b) has output (a+h,b-h), coefficient x^-h/F_h followed by the displayed Gaussian at b-h. These are precisely D u_N(x^-1 X_+). The omitted cyclic paths raise physical integer degree by N; the literal actual crossing contains exactly the degree-preserving part. This proves (13) on every total-degree block, including s>=N and both truncated endpoints. Taking the inverse proves the negative formula with its stated operator order.

5. An exact recursion for every admissible 2k-plat

At a horizontal slice with colors bold x=(x_1,...,x_(2k)), define

C_boldx = C_(x_1) tensor C_(x_3) tensor ... tensor C_(x_(2k-1)).

This is the actual pair-fusion frame. At a crossing i let boldx’ be the color order after swapping i and i+1, and set

B_i^epsilon(boldx)
   = C_boldx'^-1 R_i^epsilon(boldx) C_boldx.          (14)

All entries of C and C^-1 are explicit in (1)–(3); all entries of R are explicit in (13). Equation (14) is therefore a fixed finite convolution rule, not an unspecified intertwiner.

For a crossing within one fused pair, (14) is exactly the triangular Euler/Gaussian core in (13). A crossing between consecutive pairs acts on ONLY four relative heads. If their current physical colors are (x,y,z,w), its rule is

B_middle^epsilon
  = (C_x^-1 tensor C_y^-1)
       (I tensor R_epsilon^(y,z) tensor I)
                                  (C_x tensor C_z). (15)

Thus it consists of a fixed number of the actual Pascal, inverse Pascal, Euler and Gaussian factors, independent of N and of k. It retains the whole set of total-degree channels I_s. Other pairs are unchanged. No source-dependent adjustment is introduced at an interior crossing.

For any word beta update colors after EACH letter and iterate (14). The frames at consecutive slices cancel exactly, giving

B_beta=C_final^-1 R_beta C_initial.                  (16)

In particular these kernels satisfy every colored braid relation EXACTLY, with the original scalar lines retained: both words in such a relation have the same boundary frames in (16). This is stronger than claiming braid relations modulo the completion’s positive-degree error ideal. The actual noncyclic Euler cores and all the degree blocks in (2) are what make the present statement strict. Transporting an extra coupon means conjugating that coupon by its slice frame as well; it does not mean leaving its old relative matrix in place.

At every cup the initial state is |d,0>. At each ordinary cap the final row is (1/N)<0,d|. Designate one final pair with first color x_* for the original marked right cap; by choosing the standard orientation of that marked pair, (8) applies. Let e_in=|d,0> tensor ... tensor |d,0> and let e_out(r) put (r,d-r) at the marked pair and (0,d) at all other pairs. The ORIGINAL reduced scalar is exactly

P_(beta,N) = chi_beta N^(-(k-1))
        sum_(r=0)^d mu_(x_*)(r)
                          <e_out(r)|B_beta|e_in>.    (17)

The factor chi_beta retains the original common Cartan and framing lines if they were recorded externally rather than in R. Any original peripheral modified-dimension germ multiplies both sides unchanged. There are k cups, k-1 ordinary caps, and exactly ONE marked cap, as in the original reduced closure. An ordinary cap has not been placed at the marked pair.

The proof of (17) is (5)–(10) together with (16). It holds for arbitrary bridge number, arbitrary signed word with admissible output pairing, and the whole original meridian family. Reversing an ordinary pair uses (6); no further scalar is generated. Its full moments and meridian jets follow by differentiating the same Laurent identities at fixed Q. Moving-q normal jets are not asserted. Explicitly, the same truncated matrices obey C_(q x)(u,v;a,b)=q^(u-a) C_x(u,v;a,b). Thus meridian half-step transport is an exact frame change on the fixed-root family; its dual color changes by q^-1. None of the whole factors 1-x^N or 1+x^N in the original cyclic boundary descriptions has been inverted to obtain this recursion.

The zero-current quotient is one corner, with no genericity hypothesis

For actual plat words without extra nonintertwining coupons, the source can be reduced further. Contract the k-1 ordinary final caps and leave the designated pair open. The resulting physical two-head vector v is invariant under the actual coproduct, because the input cups, crossings and the contracted ordinary caps are actual Hopf intertwiners. In particular,

F_total v=0,       K_total v=v.                      (18)

On the remaining ordered dual pair (x,xvee), the literal actual fusion identities are

C_x^-1 F_total C_x=S_a,
C_x^-1 K_total C_x|a,b>=Q^(-1-a-b)|a,b>,

where S_a|a,b>=|a+1,b> for a<d and is zero for a=d. The first identity follows from the definition C_x|a,b>=F_total^a(|0> tensor |b>), including its top endpoint; F_total^N=0 is the actual root coproduct identity. Therefore

ker S_a intersect ker(Q^(-1-a-b)-1)
                     = span{|d,0>}.                 (19)

Indeed the first kernel has a=d. The second equation then becomes Q^-b=1, which forces b=0 in the actual head range. This uses neither Schur’s lemma nor irreducibility of the color module. It holds on the whole meridian family, including reducibility divisors.

Consequently the amplitudes in (17) satisfy the exact Ward zeros

<e_out(r)|B_beta|e_in>=0  for 0<=r<d.                (20)

There is no sum over hidden intermediate states in (20): the other output pairs are already fixed to their ordinary cap corners. Since mu_x(d)=1, the original scalar is the SINGLE original fused corner

P_(beta,N)=chi_beta N^(-(k-1))
                     <e_out(d)|B_beta|e_in>.         (21)

The diagonal source gauge above also fixes e_out(d), because its marked b coordinate is zero. Thus (21) is unaffected by that gauge. The original marker is still necessary: an ordinary cap evaluates the remaining invariant cup to zero, whereas the marker evaluates it to one. Equations (19)–(21) describe the quotient by the actual zero-current equations; they do not authorize replacing a marker in a network containing nonintertwining current coupons. In that more general network the full row (9), not (21), is required.

Equation (21) removes the final source-row cancellation problem at the exact finite level. It does not say that the remaining corner coefficient has positive summands, or that its geometric critical coefficient cannot vanish in an asymptotic decomposition.

6. Why the cap-block Rogers rule alone cannot unwind every bridge

The degree-d block in (4) is NOT stable under crossings between pairs. For every N>=2, take the actual cup-supported physical input (0,d,0,d). The zero-raising route of the positive middle crossing takes its middle pair (d,0) to (0,d) with a nonzero original coefficient. The resulting four-head state is

(0,0,d,d),

whose pair degrees are 0 and 2d, rather than d and d. This is an all-N literal coefficient statement; it uses no semiclassical model. Later crossings can return these channels to degree d. Discarding them does not define an exact iterative actual-plat transform. Equations (14)–(17) supply the necessary larger rule.

This explains the distinction from the auxiliary Newton operators in the whole-source Newton note: one actual cap block realizes their Rogers matrix, but arbitrary actual bridge transport requires the full physical channel system.

7. The source meets a fusion boundary, not the regular fusion torus

The already proved regular fusion graph has relative clocks A,J, physical clocks U,V, and momenta satisfying

AJ=UV,  H=A/U,
p_U=(1-A)p_A,
p_V=(1-V)p_J/(1-J),
H=1-(1-A)(1-J)/(x p_J/p_A).                          (22)

It is the actual Borel multiplication chart: in particular physical total lowering has symbol f_total=1/p_A. The physical ordinary bridge source has f_total=0, because its two Borel holonomies multiply to I. Therefore it is outside this chart with finite nonzero p_A. A regular interior symplectic map alone cannot move the bridge source into a chosen positive geometric chart.

The same issue is visible directly in the ORIGINAL marked row, without assigning a symbol to a finite nilpotent matrix. Fix total degree d, a=r, and multiply the physical-u summand of (1) by (8). Apart from a factor independent of u, this summand is F_(d-u)/F_(r-u). Its consecutive ratio is exactly

[1-Q^(r-u)]/[1-Q^(d-u)].                            (23)

On an interior principal chart its limit is (U-A)/(U-1). The stationary equation setting this ratio to one forces A=1, which is a divisor excluded from (22). Thus the exact nonzero row (10) is a boundary/resonant transport calculation. It is not an interior critical point of the regular fusion kernel with the original marker inserted.

This is a specific constraint on the route to a positive quad or Rivin chart. The source-boundary contribution must be included in an actual compactified/relative chart transform; merely composing the interior canonical graphs would lose it. The exact row (9)–(10), its endpoint recurrence (11), and its exact Ward corner (19)–(21) give that transform a specified source to carry. They do not prove that its selected geometric coefficient is nonzero after the full word contraction.

8. Verification and precise remaining comparison

The independent original-matrix audit and saved output pass fourteen root/color families at N=2,…,8, all total-degree Pascal blocks, the Rogers gauges, all four turn corners, the full marked row, the degree-d simplification, and six mixed signed crossings per family. Six whole pointed plats with two and three bridges compare literal original contractions with (17), verify all the zero-current channels (20), and verify the single corner (21). Seven all-parity leakage witnesses verify Section 6. These checks fix conventions; the finite identities above prove the all-N formulas.

What is now explicit is the actual arbitrary-bridge kernel system AND its marked source. A further source-preserving local transform must turn these all-channel kernels into positive quad/2↔︎3 kernels while carrying (9), its boundary (23), the Ward corner (19), and all central factors. Neither a Pascal basis change nor the ordinary matrix pentagon by itself performs that comparison. No broad hyperbolic growth or geometric-cycle selection claim follows from (17) alone.
