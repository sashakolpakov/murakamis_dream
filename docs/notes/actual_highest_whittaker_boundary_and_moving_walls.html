The actual highest-input / Whittaker-output boundary retains two walls

Research proof, 27 October 2026. Fixing the highest INPUT column and retaining the original output row gives an exact source for every original root knot period. Its whole finite matrices admit a strict cyclic presentation with two separate endpoint defects. The selected pair sees a nonzero first-normal wrap class, so those defects cannot be discarded when proposing a boundary-torsor comparison.

The classical Springer boundary map is explicit, but is only a candidate principal incidence for this source. A whole network and relative-endpoint map is not proved by its existence. In particular the one-sided uninserted Fourier polynomial is not a subcomplex for all actual port currents.

1. The genuine highest-input source and its remaining Fourier sum

Use the scalar root closure and whole source homotopy in the one-index theorem. With psi=e_0 and ell the all-ones ROW, the original root period is

PN(M)=λ0(CN)=ℓCNe0. P_N(M)=\lambda_0(C_N)=\ell C_N e_0. \tag{1}

There is no first average in (1); only the INPUT has been fixed. The output row still sums all c. For the literal two-cut tensor, at a=0 total-index conservation says c=-nu, hence

F0(ηF)=∑b,c,dTc,d;0,bηFd−b=∑ν=−(N−1)0fνηFν. F_0(\eta_F)=\sum_{b,c,d}T_{c,d;0,b}\eta_F^{d-b} =\sum_{\nu=-(N-1)}^0 f_\nu\eta_F^\nu.

Its actual Fourier partial family and final source are exactly

F̃N(0,j)=1N∑ν=−(N−1)0QNjνfν,PN=∑j=0N−1F̃N(0,j)=f0. \widetilde F_N(0,j)=\frac1N\sum_{\nu=-(N-1)}^0 Q_N^{j\nu} f_\nu,\qquad P_N=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\widetilde F_N(0,j)=f_0. \tag{2}

The 1/N inside (2) belongs to the inverse Fourier matrix. The original pivots, framing lines and complete d_N(M), if included, remain in T. Whole q-normal identities use the actual Vandermonde inverse and the source-current correction of the one-index theorem, not an off-root assertion that (1) is scalar for every auxiliary lift.

The one-sided property applies to the charge-zero uninserted tensor. A homogeneous port-current tensor of total integer charge d instead has c+nu=d, so nu can range from d-(N-1) to d. This distinction matters even for ordinary generators; it is not a large-band issue.

2. Exact whole finite generators and boundary equations

Work before jets with B=diag(1,Q,…,Q^(N-1)), J_head=diag(j), U e_j=e_(j+1) with its last arrow zero, and Uflat=U transpose. The actual balanced compressed matrices are

F=U(I−QB),ℰ=Uflat(MB−1−M−1I),K=xB−1,x=−M/q,q2=Q. F=U(I-QB),\qquad \mathcal E=Uflat(MB^{-1}-M^{-1}I),\qquad K=xB^{-1},\quad x=-M/q,\quad q^2=Q.

Put Z=Q^N, delta=1-Z, kappa=M-M inverse and define the constant cyclic repair and its two wrap arrows

P=U+W,W=E0,N−1,P−1=Uflat+W−,W−=EN−1,0,PN=I. P=U+W,\quad W=E_{0,N-1},\quad P^{-1}=Uflat+W_-,\quad W_-=E_{N-1,0},\quad P^N=I.

Direct matrix multiplication proves the WHOLE identities

BP−QPB=δW, BP-QPB=\delta W, \tag{3} F=P(I−QB)−δW,ℰ=P−1(MB−1−M−1I)−κW−. F=P(I-QB)-\delta W,\qquad \mathcal E=P^{-1}(MB^{-1}-M^{-1}I)-\kappa W_-. \tag{4}

Thus at the complete root delta=kappa=0 these are the literal cyclic quantum-torus formulas. Away from that fiber (3)-(4) retain the actual finite walls. P is a presentation device with the displayed defects; the actual U is still nilpotent.

The right highest-input equations hold WHOLE:

Bψ=ψ,Kψ=xψ,ℰψ=0,Fψ=(1−Q)e1. B\psi=\psi,\qquad K\psi=x\psi,\qquad \mathcal E\psi=0,\qquad F\psi=(1-Q)e_1. \tag{5}

The output row obeys ell P=ell P inverse=ell and

ℓF=ℓ(I−QB)−δeN−1t,ℓℰ=ℓ(MB−1−M−1I)−κe0t. \boxed{\quad \ell F=\ell(I-QB)-\delta e_{N-1}^t,\qquad \ell\mathcal E=\ell(MB^{-1}-M^{-1}I)-\kappa e_0^t. \quad} \tag{6}

These are the actual Whittaker-type boundary equations. At the complete root their two endpoint rows vanish. Their first normals are independent: the Q-normal row is supported at the top head, and the M-normal row at the bottom. Neither may be discarded by replacing the source with a joint ordinary character beforehand.

The source matrix Q_0=psi ell has trace one. The commuting left B action and right P action have the exact joint eigenspace

{A:BA=A,AP=A}=RQ0. \{A:BA=A,\ AP=A\}=R Q_0. \tag{7}

Here invert Q^j-1 for 0<j<N, as in the actual head ring. BA=A forces only its zeroth row, and AP=A makes that row constant. Thus (7) is a whole pointed rank-one paired-source line. Left and right actions commute; it does NOT assert a one-dimensional character for the noncommutative algebra generated by B,P.

3. The selected pair detects the missing wrap at first normal order

Define only for comparison the cyclic expressions

Fcyc=P(I−QB),ℰcyc=P−1(MB−1−M−1I). F_{cyc}=P(I-QB),\qquad \mathcal E_{cyc}=P^{-1}(MB^{-1}-M^{-1}I).

The actual F is nilpotent, so F^N=0. One full cyclic traversal visits each head position once, giving the exact whole equation

FcycN=(Q;Q)NI=δ(Q;Q)N−1I. F_{cyc}^N=(Q;Q)_N I =\delta (Q;Q)_{N-1}I.

Consequently the original highest/Whittaker pair satisfies

ℓFNψ=0,[ℓFcycNψ1−QN]QN=N. \ell F^N\psi=0,\qquad \left[\frac{\ell F_{cyc}^N\psi}{1-Q^N}\right]_{Q_N}=N. \tag{8}

For the other wall, already one generator gives

ℓℰψ=0,ℓℰcycψ=κ. \ell\mathcal E\psi=0,\qquad \ell\mathcal E_{cyc}\psi=\kappa.

At the root its full cyclic central product is

ℰcycN=(−1)N−1(MN−M−N)I. \mathcal E_{cyc}^N=(-1)^{N-1}(M^N-M^{-N})I. \tag{9}

Away from the root the product is the original finite product product_(j=0)^(N-1)(M Q^(-j)-M inverse), rather than its root norm. This retains the same peripheral boundary and central factors as the original norm-root/modified-dimension construction. Their coalescing zeros must be evaluated as their whole ratios.

There is a sharp algebraic obstruction to dropping these walls: an invertible B and P with exact BP=QPB and P^N=I would imply (1-Q^N)B=0. It cannot be a whole family over a ring where 1-Q^N is a genuine nonzero parameter and B remains invertible. Equation (3) is the required concrete finite correction. At fixed roots a cyclic quantum torus is legitimate; (8) shows why its bare root specialization loses a selected first-normal class.

4. A strict polynomial realization of the source before jets

Identify the actual head with polynomials f(z) of degree below N, e_j corresponding to z^j. Then psi=1, ell(f)=f(1), and

Bf(z)=f(Qz),ℰf(z)=Mf(Q−1z)−M−1f(z)−κf(0)z, Bf(z)=f(Qz),\quad \mathcal E f(z)=\frac{M f(Q^{-1}z)-M^{-1}f(z)-\kappa f(0)}z, Ff(z)=z[f(z)−Qf(Qz)]−δfN−1zN. Ff(z)=z[f(z)-Qf(Qz)]-\delta f_{N-1}z^N. \tag{10}

The first correction removes the actual north pole and the second removes the south degree overflow. Formula (10) is an exact global finite-section model on H^0(P1,O(N-1)), including its whole two endpoint operators. Its dual row is the explicit Serre cochain

cℓ(z)=∑j=0N−1z−j−1,Res⁡z=0cℓ(z)f(z)dz=f(1). c_\ell(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}z^{-j-1},\qquad \operatorname{Res}_{z=0}c_\ell(z)f(z)dz=f(1). \tag{11}

It therefore carries the actual source and all normal derivatives without replacing functions by their endpoint jets. To transport arbitrary products or relative word maps, carry the full operators and their endpoint rows, as in the established whole Cech model.

5. What its Springer boundary incidence does and does not supply

The classical complete physical cut chart, with common momentum temporarily restored, is

s=A−1−1,μ(A,p)=(1+s−sps/p1−s),v=p−1,ηS=−sp. s=A^{-1}-1,\quad \mu(A,p)=\begin{pmatrix}1+s&-sp\\s/p&1-s\end{pmatrix}, \quad v=p^{-1},\quad \eta_S=-sp.

If a principal source transform carries the exact left/right characters B-input=1 and P-output=1 into these physical variables, its candidate corner is A=1,p=1, with eta_S=0 and the RETAINED Springer flag v=1. This is a conditional principal incidence, not an identification of the Fourier-band cap or of a uniform endpoint symbol map. The highest input is an endpoint of the original finite head, outside the previously proved regular good-A Gaussian chart.

On the geometric side itself the associated centralizer resolution has the exact local map

v=1,ηS=−s,aB=1−r,b=(1−r)/s. v=1,\quad \eta_S=-s,\quad a_B=1-r,\quad b=(1-r)/s.

The renormalized a_B is regular while the raw additive centralizer b has its genuine pole. Pulling back the actual geometric density gives

Ωcut=−dr∧dss(1+s)=−dηS∧daBηS(1−ηS),Res⁡ηS=0Ωcut=−daB=dr. \Omega_{cut}=-\frac{dr\wedge ds}{s(1+s)} =-\frac{d\eta_S\wedge da_B}{\eta_S(1-\eta_S)},\qquad \operatorname{Res}_{\eta_S=0}\Omega_{cut}=-da_B=dr. \tag{12}

This is a ONE-form on the exceptional fiber v=1, not the full compact two-form or its already-computed compact period. It is exact on that affine fiber, so a nonzero relative value requires its actual endpoints and is their difference in r. The remaining finite Fourier-label sum must supply those data through a genuine source map; its existence is not inferred from (12).

There is also a geometric obstruction to an ordinary regular raw matrix extension. For an irreducible complete representation some other word has nonzero lower-left entry c. In the actual r,s conjugator its appropriate entry has a c/s term; equivalently the nontrivial commutator trace cannot approach the identity trace while all matrices remain regular. Thus the endpoint comparison must use the resolved/meromorphic relative framing object. One Springer flag alone cannot regularize the full irreducible knot family. This is consistent with the actual moving walls (3)-(9), not their removal.

5.1. The actual second momentum is rational on a regular exceptional fiber

There is a more precise geometric boundary chart, independent of an unproved quantum source map. Retain the ACTUAL signed prefixes U,V of the second physical edge and suppose c_V and c_U-c_V are nonzero. Let r differ from zero and one, and take s to zero with r fixed. The original expression k=ell_U/ell_V gives

k→k0=cU/cV,C0=U−k0V=(a0b00d0),a0d0=(k0−1)2. k\longrightarrow k_0=c_U/c_V,\qquad C_0=U-k_0V=\begin{pmatrix}a_0&b_0\\0&d_0\end{pmatrix}, \qquad a_0d_0=(k_0-1)^2.

Write C(s)=U-k(s)V. Its fiber equation ell_C=0 implies

cC/s→−d0+(a0−d0)rr(1−r). c_C/s\longrightarrow-\frac{d_0+(a_0-d_0)r}{r(1-r)}.

For the actual F(r,s) conjugation the off-diagonal entries of F inverse C F are beta,gamma. Since its first diagonal is zero, direct multiplication now gives

β→−d01−rr,γ→a0r1−r. \beta\longrightarrow-d_0\frac{1-r}{r},\qquad \gamma\longrightarrow a_0\frac r{1-r}.

The actual balanced momentum definition beta=(-1)^j(k-1)p_e therefore has the rational limit

pe→γ01−rr,γ0=−(−1)jd0k0−1≠0. p_e\longrightarrow\gamma_0\frac{1-r}{r},\qquad \gamma_0=-\frac{(-1)^j d_0}{k_0-1}\ne0. \tag{12a}

Thus the remaining momentum is birational on this regular part of the exceptional fiber, with inverse r=gamma_0/(p_e+gamma_0). The boundary density is exactly

dr=−γ0(pe+γ0)2dpe. dr=-\frac{\gamma_0}{(p_e+\gamma_0)^2}dp_e. \tag{12b}

Its genuine pole is at p_e=-gamma_0 and its ordinary logarithmic residue is zero. A relative endpoint pairing cannot be inferred by discarding that pole. The raw representation escape and the local regularity of this particular physical Gauss coordinate are distinct.

For the genuine figure-eight third prefix in the prefix theorem, u squared plus u plus one is zero and k_0=2+u, a_0=-1, d_0=-u, gamma_0=u squared. All conditions of this chart hold. Equation (12a) specifies a candidate coordinate for the last Fourier-label source at the resolved boundary. The whole finite sum, its possible passage through the pole in (12b), the internal crossing coefficients and moving normal factors must still be transported before it is a selected period identity.

6. One-sided scalar bands are not a current subcomplex

The literal two-cut unknot is the flip tensor. Fix a=0 and insert the actual second OUTPUT F generator. The input b=0 term has c=0,d=1 and coefficient 1-Q. It contributes

(1−Q)ηF+1, (1-Q)\eta_F^{+1}, \tag{13}

which is nonzero at every physical N greater than one. Thus even this actual port current exits the nonpositive-band subspace in (2). It must retain its positive tail in the whole Fourier/current complex. This statement concerns a literal port row, not an assumed untwisted two-leg coproduct on the reshaped tensor. Actual total Hopf rows still use their correct ordered port/prefix dictionary.

7. Exact audit and remaining map

The audit and its output check whole matrix boundary formulas, both cyclic norms and their selected wrap normal, actual signed R-word highest-input Fourier sources, and the positive current tail (13).

The strongest exact conclusion is the whole source line, polynomial realization and its retained normal endpoints, together with a concrete candidate geometric relative fiber. It does not construct the internal R/Gaussian transform to that fiber, prove nonzero selected geometric survival, or determine the hyperbolic-volume growth. Such a transform must preserve (6), (8), (11), the full peripheral zero ratios and every current tail, not only (12).
