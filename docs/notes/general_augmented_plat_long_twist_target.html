Current critical path: the selected complex packet and the original period

The actual completed matching edge is the generic operator D_a^-1 p_a(Omega). Its entire finite root kernel retains every physical support zero. The fixed deficient factorization separates forced zeros from a positive-root Jacobi determinant, and the joint deficient packet now proves a selected action and nonzero relative amplitude in a complex neighborhood of the ACTUAL geometric frame. Degree and magnetic coordinates may vary together. This reaches the chamber beyond physical bandwidth at every fixed width under the marked geometric certificate.

The generic boundary-source theorem retains the actual LARGE channel m=N-1-a and proves genuine numerator bounds of subexponential relative cost when (N-1-2a) log N=o(N). The small and large channels coincide at the root but their quantum normals differ. The full generic operator’s boundary lift remains a separate step; finite physical gauge cancellation alone does not bound an unspecified complex interpolation.

The absolute reflected regulator has no uncomputed constant. Exact source reflection has scalar one; the endpoint Bloch symbol preserves the actual diagram volume. Literal turn and degree-log characters must stay in the action and in its marked winding sectors. At FULL source-marked stationarity the critical action has real part Vol(Y_D)/(2pi).

The main unresolved theorem is GLOBAL: transport the original degree and magnetic cycle, retaining finite-head boundary terms and crossed poles; prove a nonzero geometric coefficient and control competing colors. A forbidden physical integer degree slice is identically zero, including genuine generic normals. Its magnetic contour alone cannot acquire a nonzero period just because the continued local packet is nonzero. The general long-twist volume equality remains OPEN.

30 October 2026. The target is an original Kashaev volume equality for arbitrary fixed bridge number and arbitrary fixed essential twist skeleton. A Conway seed grammar is an established application, not the definition of the intended general class. The statement below remains a CONJECTURE until the full source estimates and selection of the geometric period are proved for its general skeleton.

1. Quantifiers and the actual diagram

Fix k>=2 and an ordered 2k-strand plat diagram with r designated two-strand twist boxes. Every crossing belongs to one of the boxes; all other wires, cups and caps are those of this fixed diagram. For example its braid word can be written

beta(p)=sigma_(i_1)^(epsilon_1-2p_1) ...
         sigma_(i_r)^(epsilon_r-2p_r),
epsilon_s in {0,+1,-1}, p_s in Z.

The index word, box ordering, endpoint marking and epsilon are fixed. Adjacent powers of the same generator are combined before treating their twist parameters as independent. The fixed upper and lower pairings together with the permutation of the epsilon word must give one component. This is a finite permutation/matching test: adding a full twist does not change it. Thus every p gives a knot with a k-bridge presentation; k need not be its minimal bridge number.

Drill one actual crossing circle around each box, remove the full twists, and retain its specified half twist. Denote the resulting marked augmented exterior by Y_D. Require Y_D to be hyperbolic, with the actual primitive crossing-disk markings retained. This is a geometric hypothesis on the supplied diagram, not an assertion that every presentation of a hyperbolic knot has hyperbolic augmentation.

General long-twist conjecture. For every such fixed diagram D there is T(D)<infinity such that, for every fixed integer vector p with all |p_s|>T(D),

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N) log|K_N(K_D(p))|
                  =Vol(S^3 minus K_D(p)).                 (1)

Here N is the quantum level. Twist numbers are fixed BEFORE this limit. The conjecture includes both level parities and composite levels, arbitrary signed twist vectors, and no comparability condition on their magnitudes. It asks for the volume of the FILLED knot, not the complete drilled volume or a limit in which p grows with N. For a partial filling the analogous statement is about the remaining link exterior with its original single marked normalization.

The quantifier is every fixed k and every admissible fixed skeleton; there is no proposed universal threshold depending on k alone.

Under an actual positive marked gluing certificate, the geometric long-filling corollary now proves a threshold T_geom(D) for complete hyperbolic geometry of every independently long signed filling. Existence of the quantum threshold T(D) in (1) is the remaining conjectural assertion for a general skeleton. These two thresholds must not be conflated.

2. Why twist length needs a diagram hypothesis

Raw box counts do not measure geometric twisting. For the actual crossing operator, including its Cartan factor,

R^(2p) R^(-2p+2c)=R^(2c).                               (2)

Both displayed blocks can have arbitrarily many crossings while the diagram remains the fixed bounded-twist diagram on the right. The identity holds inside ANY 2k-strand source and before its marked cap. It is an exact algebraic cancellation, not an asymptotic example. Likewise a nugatory twist can change a presentation without adding an essential drilled cusp. Thus independent long parameters must refer to actual essential crossing disks. Certifying Y_D directly is a uniform way to impose this requirement without restricting k or assuming that a visually complicated plat is geometrically essential.

3. The exact reduction already works for every fixed k

The all-input circle identity gives the original full-twist expansion on each pair of strands. Let A_(D,N)(h_1,…,h_r) be the actual augmented source with circle color j_s=N-h_s, including all its specified half twists, cups, caps, pivots and the exposed first cut. Then the general source transfer has the exact identity

K_N(K_D(p))=chi_N kappa^(-2 sum_s p_s)
   sum_(j_1,...,j_r=1)^N
   product_s beta_(j_s-1)^(-p_s) A_(D,N)(N-j).            (3)

The source A is defined before the displayed recorded outer factor chi_N; it is not normalized using the unknown answer. All repeated Casimir-root normals are part of beta. Multilinearity applies each substitution in its ORIGINAL operator order. Physical crossings at different boxes need not commute. Only the independent color-register functionals commute.

Consequently the long-twist problem does not require a new surgery formula for each bridge number. The same exact filling map is already available. What is missing generally is control of its input A_D.

4. The three tasks on a general augmented skeleton

Full quantum source. Establish an exact holomorphic color packet for A_D near the complete colors, with a uniform complex asymptotic, and an exponential gap on the ENTIRE physical color complement. The estimate must include all aliases and normal currents. A bound for one positive sector or for independently maximized vertices does not give this input. The marked multiport Chebyshev/Parseval packet is exact at every fixed k, but its physical source-to-coefficient energy is still the quantity to estimate.

Actual geometry. Supply the marked complete augmented exterior and its absolute volume anchor. A positive ideal gluing certificate on a triangulation of the actual exterior is one sufficient route. Another starts from its actual disk/projection cut: the cubic graph of shaded triangles and its distinguished crossing-circle matching describe the whole polyhedron, and a positive right-angled realization supplies the complete metric. A triangulation of an unrelated manifold or a numerically matching volume does not identify this labeled topology.

The general marked gluing certificate proves the geometric implication directly. It also proves the Casson–Rivin equivalence with an interior critical point of the actual angle-volume function, whose unique maximum is Vol(Y_D). Positive phases and positive multiplier variables give a finite algebraic certificate; the selected optimum has algebraic shapes. None of these geometric facts implies the quantum packet estimate.

Same selected action. Prove that the critical source action is the calibrated primitive on the ACTUAL peripheral branch, retaining the selected square roots and logarithms. The required equations are

G=2pi i phi, U_s=-2pi i x_s,
V_s=-2 partial_s phi,
Im G(0)=Vol(Y_D).                                      (4)

The filled source saddle then gives the primitive equation U_s+p_s V_s=-2pi i. The existing geometric completion theorem constructs the actual smooth filled metric and identifies its volume with the boundary-corrected source action. It is this last equality, not an unnamed source volume, that yields the right side of (1).

The already proved long-filling transfer is independent of k once these inputs are available. It must not be cited as having supplied them for an arbitrary plat.

The new critical-scheme equivalence and marked multi-cusp rigidity now supply a nearby geometric stationary branch under an actual positive gluing certificate. The only magnetic Hessian null directions are the two framing directions; a transverse slice removes them. The branch has the actual peripheral differential and absolute volume anchor. The quantum statement still requires that the original finite source select and contribute to this branch.

The new actual local character map proves the primitive differential on any correctly marked stationary branch. The absolute endpoint regulator theorem proves the complete-volume anchor at a regular correctly marked geometric critical point. A generic framing theorem supplies a regular unfolded geometric candidate, but does not select its physical logarithmic sheet or a contributing quantum cycle. Thus the identities in (4) have a general classical proof with these hypotheses; their realization by the actual selected source remains part of the quantum problem.

The actual channel Hessian also has a general primitive normalization: H_(xi_i,xi_j) is -delta_ij 4pi i/tau_i, and the channel/magnetic mixed block vanishes. Its negative real part gives a local channel elimination at the geometric endpoint, while holding the magnetic variables as parameters. It supplies no nonzero cycle coefficient or magnetic asymptotic.

5. What the current general work is changing

The complete source compiler and all-color compiler give explicit finite factorial sums, phases and generic confluence normals for every fixed plat. Their inverse measures retain their exponential profiles. No unknown local recoupling inverse remains. The entire core function also supplies an exact separately-even finite Fourier interpolation, including the odd endpoint. It does not supply a uniform complex asymptotic. If the required nonzero analytic corridor packet exists, its periodicity mirrors the same rate near the opposite cube face. The actual physical alias split therefore matters for the global gap.

For each actual local q-Hahn column, the negative-root theorem now proves a canonical slit-plane logarithm, and the complex-action theorem proves its actual leading action on the physical Fourier arc. The uniform amplitude theorem adds a nonzero prefactor with relative error O(1/N). The magnetic corridor theorem continues the actual finite kernel in one magnetic variable while the pair degree and channel stay real and discrete. The subsequent joint determinant and joint amplitude theorem now continue pair degree and magnetic coordinate together on a fixed central domain, including folded channels, with an explicit selected local sheet. The channel remains an integer. The common-framing criterion states finite strict inequalities for simultaneous use on a plat; the right-angled obstruction now proves that an all-central selected frame cannot exist for the actual certified right-angled cut. Generic geometric strip flags localize the weaker strict physical-support failures to exactly TWO factors, for arbitrary bridge width. Those facets can be prescribed, including the pair adjacent to the original marked physical edge. The full physical packet and selected-factor corollary now put EVERY other ordinary factor in its actual selected physical packet, with no central-support hypothesis. The two exceptions can instead be placed together at one crossing-circle matching edge. The selected local certificate remains a conditional local theorem; it does not establish a whole-source cycle or sum estimate. The small-channel generic-normal estimate and exact partner collision retain the original derivatives. The latter exposes additional individual normal poles, so a full-domain paired-normal estimate cannot be assumed. The physical paired-normal theorem does prove polynomial relative cost on the whole complete physical grid, retaining the actual large partner and both parities. The complex near-fold theorem now supplies subexponential relative cost on a fixed complex compact when (l-j) log N=o(N). A fixed nonzero scaled channel shift and a whole-source asymptotic remain open. Every fixed generic q jet of all physical columns and completed divided pairs is also controlled by the physical generic-jet theorem. Its forward/backward proof does not supply a completed boundary tensor norm. The full physical packet extends the complex near-fold bound to all fixed physical compacts, still with a shrinking scaled channel window. These local results are not a whole-source asymptotic. Outside the specified marked domain the endpoint quadratic has monodromy, so an unspecified analytic continuation alone cannot identify the selected geometric root. A controlled simultaneous continuation and a global cycle estimate remain necessary.

Choosing the exceptional pair at a crossing-circle matching edge also gives the exact marked four-port rewrite of the COMPLETE-circle source. Its original row measure cancels the exponential factorial gauge, and the positive scalar theorem controls every recoupling target and fixed generic jet. The completed-projector formula also cancels the target scalar gauge and combines the collision pairs before taking the first normal, retaining their nilpotent part. The remaining native projector/complement costs are explicit but not yet sharply bounded by the geometric action. This is not the general mixed-color packet required for fixed long fillings.

The native-projector energy theorem now bounds every fixed generic operator derivative by a polynomial times its explicit magnetic energy, uniformly at both parities and all physical degrees. A simple midpoint channel proves that energy can itself be exponential. The remaining joint variational estimate must keep the actual shared wire variables and these energy terms; independent local maxima do not supply it.

The full folded boundary rate is now computed by exact Plancherel and the actual selected endpoint polynomial. It remains valid in all shrinking real fold windows. For arbitrary fixed skeletons, the shared-cycle compiler gives an exact bounded-flow source with coupled Fourier congruences and the original one-unit mark. Its positive coefficients have a uniform interior variational rate. These source reductions retain every shared label and phase. They do not supply the general mixed-color packet, sharp joint geometric estimate or lower bound needed for the fixed-long-filling theorem.

The explicit positive partition now gives a polynomial-loss Cauchy upper bound retaining every shared flow and Fourier charge. Its sharp rate is not yet the hyperbolic volume: the conditional radial-frame obstruction prevents one proposed all-ordinary geometric reconstruction, without proving a numerical gap. The exact coherent source and the retained exceptional matching block remain the objects to estimate.

The full graph identity puts all folded labels and normals on the actual matching graph. The reflection-double identity removes the final gluing cancellation for specified three-port doubles, retaining the full source. Neither identity already bounds the internally coherent half-packets for arbitrary graphs.

The matching-graph geometry constructs the actual complete complement and primitive markings from a labeled positive right-angled certificate. It also constructs a positive ideal triangulation. The geometric endpoint theorem locates the complete disk character at c=-2, the folded-channel endpoint, and retains its marked peripheral square root. This is necessary geometric information, not a proof of quantum selection. The endpoint phase calculation also shows that a universal separate-leg positive normalization already fails at N=3. It leaves coherent resummation available.

The source-divisor extension allows a nonzero analytic source-density germ to vanish at the complete point. With the other packet, gap and geometric hypotheses proved, it yields the filling equality away from an analytic exceptional divisor, including density one of long slopes in each signed box. A peripheral monomial times a unit gives EVERY independently long signed slope. The general nonzero-germ version does not by itself prove the all-slopes conjecture (1).

These results remove finite-source and geometric ambiguities. The principal remaining theorem is still a coherent asymptotic with the required global gap and the actual selected geometric period.

The arbitrary-width Conway extension is retained as an application and a calibration of these interfaces. It does not settle (1) for all k-bridge diagrams. The full cube source, which lies beyond that grammar, remains one useful branching test, not the definition of the general target.

6. Literature coverage only

Purcell’s highly twisted volume paper states hyperbolicity and geometric volume bounds under diagrammatic hypotheses; those are not an original Kashaev volume-limit theorem. Van der Veen’s augmented KTG paper states a volume conjecture result for its augmented knotted trivalent graphs and a containing-link corollary. Its abstract does not assert (1) for all independently long fillings of arbitrary fixed plats. These are coverage observations, not imported proofs or an exhaustive novelty claim. The definitions and source normalizations must still be compared before identifying any covered family.
