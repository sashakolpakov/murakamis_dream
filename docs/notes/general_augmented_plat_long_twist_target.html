The general long-twist target at arbitrary bridge number

30 October 2026. The target is an original Kashaev volume equality for arbitrary fixed bridge number and arbitrary fixed essential twist skeleton. A Conway seed grammar is an established application, not the definition of the intended general class. The statement below remains a CONJECTURE until the full source estimates and geometric source comparison are proved for its general skeleton.

1. Quantifiers and the actual diagram

Fix k>=2 and an ordered 2k-strand plat diagram with r designated two-strand twist boxes. Every crossing belongs to one of the boxes; all other wires, cups and caps are those of this fixed diagram. For example its braid word can be written

beta(p)=sigma_(i_1)^(epsilon_1-2p_1) ...
         sigma_(i_r)^(epsilon_r-2p_r),
epsilon_s in {0,+1,-1}, p_s in Z.

The index word, box ordering, endpoint marking and epsilon are fixed. Adjacent powers of the same generator are combined before treating their twist parameters as independent. The fixed upper and lower pairings together with the permutation of the epsilon word must give one component. This is a finite permutation/matching test: adding a full twist does not change it. Thus every p gives a knot with a k-bridge presentation; k need not be its minimal bridge number.

Drill one actual crossing circle around each box, remove the full twists, and retain its specified half twist. Denote the resulting marked augmented exterior by Y_D. Require Y_D to be hyperbolic, with the actual primitive crossing-disk markings retained. This is a geometric hypothesis on the supplied diagram, not an assertion that every presentation of a hyperbolic knot has hyperbolic augmentation.

General long-twist conjecture. For every such fixed diagram D there is T(D)<infinity such that, for every fixed integer vector p with all |p_s|>T(D),

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N) log|K_N(K_D(p))|
                  =Vol(S^3 minus K_D(p)).                 (1)

Here N is the quantum level. Twist numbers are fixed BEFORE this limit. The conjecture includes both level parities and composite levels, arbitrary signed twist vectors, and no comparability condition on their magnitudes. It asks for the volume of the FILLED knot, not the complete drilled volume or a limit in which p grows with N. For a partial filling the analogous statement is about the remaining link exterior with its original single marked normalization.

The quantifier is every fixed k and every admissible fixed skeleton; there is no proposed universal threshold depending on k alone.

2. Why twist length needs a diagram hypothesis

Raw box counts do not measure geometric twisting. For the actual crossing operator, including its Cartan factor,

R^(2p) R^(-2p+2c)=R^(2c).                               (2)

Both displayed blocks can have arbitrarily many crossings while the diagram remains the fixed bounded-twist diagram on the right. The identity holds inside ANY 2k-strand source and before its marked cap. It is an exact algebraic cancellation, not an asymptotic example. Likewise a nugatory twist can change a presentation without adding an essential drilled cusp. Thus independent long parameters must refer to actual essential crossing disks. Certifying Y_D directly is a uniform way to impose this requirement without restricting k or assuming that a visually complicated plat is geometrically essential.

3. The exact reduction already works for every fixed k

The all-input circle identity gives the original full-twist expansion on each pair of strands. Let A_(D,N)(h_1,…,h_r) be the actual augmented source with circle color j_s=N-h_s, including all its specified half twists, cups, caps, pivots and the exposed first cut. Then the general source transfer has the exact identity

K_N(K_D(p))=chi_N kappa^(-2 sum_s p_s)
   sum_(j_1,...,j_r=1)^N
   product_s beta_(j_s-1)^(-p_s) A_(D,N)(N-j).            (3)

The source A is defined before the displayed recorded outer factor chi_N; it is not normalized using the unknown answer. All repeated Casimir-root normals are part of beta. Multilinearity applies each substitution in its ORIGINAL operator order. Physical crossings at different boxes need not commute. Only the independent color-register functionals commute.

Consequently the long-twist problem does not require a new surgery formula for each bridge number. The same exact filling map is already available. What is missing generally is control of its input A_D.

4. The three tasks on a general augmented skeleton

Full quantum source. Establish an exact holomorphic color packet for A_D near the complete colors, with a uniform complex asymptotic, and an exponential gap on the ENTIRE physical color complement. The estimate must include all aliases and normal currents. A bound for one positive sector or for independently maximized vertices does not give this input. The marked multiport Chebyshev/Parseval packet is exact at every fixed k, but its physical source-to-coefficient energy is still the quantity to estimate.

Actual geometry. Supply the marked complete augmented exterior and its absolute volume anchor. A positive ideal gluing certificate on a triangulation of the actual exterior is one sufficient route. Another starts from its actual disk/projection cut: the cubic graph of shaded triangles and its distinguished crossing-circle matching describe the whole polyhedron, and a positive right-angled realization supplies the complete metric. A triangulation of an unrelated manifold or a numerically matching volume does not identify this labeled topology.

The general marked gluing certificate proves the geometric implication directly. It also proves the Casson–Rivin equivalence with an interior critical point of the actual angle-volume function, whose unique maximum is Vol(Y_D). Positive phases and positive multiplier variables give a finite algebraic certificate; the selected optimum has algebraic shapes. None of these geometric facts implies the quantum packet estimate.

Same selected action. Prove that the critical source action is the calibrated primitive on the ACTUAL peripheral branch, retaining the selected square roots and logarithms. The required equations are

G=2pi i phi, U_s=-2pi i x_s,
V_s=-2 partial_s phi,
Im G(0)=Vol(Y_D).                                      (4)

The filled source saddle then gives the primitive equation U_s+p_s V_s=-2pi i. The existing geometric completion theorem constructs the actual smooth filled metric and identifies its volume with the boundary-corrected source action. It is this last equality, not an unnamed source volume, that yields the right side of (1).

The already proved long-filling transfer is independent of k once these inputs are available. It must not be cited as having supplied them for an arbitrary plat.

5. What the current general work is changing

The complete source compiler and all-color compiler give explicit finite factorial sums, phases and generic confluence normals for every fixed plat. Their inverse measures retain their exponential profiles. No unknown local recoupling inverse remains. The entire core function also supplies an exact separately-even finite Fourier interpolation, including the odd endpoint. It does not supply a uniform complex asymptotic. If the required nonzero analytic corridor packet exists, its periodicity mirrors the same rate near the opposite cube face. The actual physical alias split therefore matters for the global gap.

The matching-graph geometry constructs the actual complete complement and primitive markings from a labeled positive right-angled certificate. It also constructs a positive ideal triangulation. The geometric endpoint theorem locates the complete disk character at c=-2, the folded-channel endpoint, and retains its marked peripheral square root. This is necessary geometric information, not a proof of quantum selection. The endpoint phase calculation also shows that a universal separate-leg positive normalization already fails at N=3. It leaves coherent resummation available.

The source-divisor extension allows a nonzero analytic source-density germ to vanish at the complete point. With the other packet, gap and geometric hypotheses proved, it yields the filling equality away from an analytic exceptional divisor, including density one of long slopes in each signed box. A peripheral monomial times a unit gives EVERY independently long signed slope. The general nonzero-germ version does not by itself prove the all-slopes conjecture (1).

These results remove finite-source and geometric ambiguities. The principal remaining theorem is still a coherent asymptotic with the required global gap and the actual selected geometric period.

The arbitrary-width Conway extension is retained as an application and a calibration of these interfaces. It does not settle (1) for all k-bridge diagrams. The full cube source, which lies beyond that grammar, remains one useful branching test, not the definition of the general target.

6. Literature coverage only

Purcell’s highly twisted volume paper states hyperbolicity and geometric volume bounds under diagrammatic hypotheses; those are not an original Kashaev volume-limit theorem. Van der Veen’s augmented KTG paper states a volume conjecture result for its augmented knotted trivalent graphs and a containing-link corollary. Its abstract does not assert (1) for all independently long fillings of arbitrary fixed plats. These are coverage observations, not imported proofs or an exhaustive novelty claim. The definitions and source normalizations must still be compared before identifying any covered family.
