Two source constants for the true bulk endpoint normal defect

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

On a buffered physical-index interval, the true scalar normal defect is intrinsic up to exponentially small errors among polynomially bounded analytic value interpolants. Its exact discrete transport is determined, to that accuracy, by two actual anchor coefficients. The transport has an explicit two-dimensional continuum limit, regular also at the coalescing parameter α=0\alpha=0.

No coefficient is set to zero or assumed small. This reduces the compatibility question to two source constants; it does not answer that question or estimate a filled source.

5. Exact criterion for a moderate scalar Hermite representative

The two coefficients characterize, rather than merely accompany, the missing local scalar interpolation. All intervals in this statement retain fixed buffers about the anchor; a smaller fixed strip is allowed. Exponential estimates may include a polynomial factor in NN.

Corollary 3. The following are equivalent:

  • The actual anchor vector 𝒂Nf\mathbf a_N^f is exponentially small.
  • On a fixed smaller bulk interval containing the anchor there is a polynomially bounded holomorphic scalar hN(x,α)h_N(x,\alpha) matching both physical channels: hN(xj,α)=Fj(α),∂xhN(xj,α)=Nc′(j)Gj(α).(28) h_N(x_j,\alpha)=F_j(\alpha),\qquad \partial_xh_N(x_j,\alpha)=Nc'(j)G_j(\alpha). \qquad\text{(28)}

For the converse it suffices to have (28) on any fixed neighborhood of the anchor, with an analytic strip and a polynomial bound there. Uniformity in a fixed parameter set is retained when the hypotheses are uniform on a larger parameter neighborhood. The condition is independent of the chosen admissible fNf_N in the exponential sense of (11).

Proof of necessity. On the given neighborhood, hN−fNh_N-f_N is polynomially bounded, holomorphic, and zero at every physical value node. Proof of sufficiency. Exact transport (12), not just the continuum approximation, makes all DjfD_j^f exponentially small for xj∈I0x_j\in I_0. Let Δj\Delta_j denote their first components. On a sufficiently narrow bulk strip the map x↦c(Nx)=2cos⁡(2πx+π/N)x\mapsto c(Nx)=2\cos(2\pi x+\pi/N) is one-to-one with nonzero derivative. This equivalence does not prove its smallness premise for real α\alpha. It supplies no full-period representative or endpoint extension, and no boundary current has been discarded.

6. Scope and direct sources

The first component of this transported defect is the actual missing spectral normal, multiplied by Nc′(j)Nc'(j). Its contribution to the original trace is the exact current in the pinned normal-defect source, with the original crossing, remaining spectator, shared colors, and all other traces retained. A polynomial upper bound for the two coefficients is not a filled-action bound for that current.

Neither transport theorem says that 𝒂Nf\mathbf a_N^f is nonzero, zero, exponentially small, or of a specified algebraic order at real parameters. Section 4 gives only its physical-parameter calibration and divisible correction. Establishing its actual real leading coefficients remains a separate source calculation.
