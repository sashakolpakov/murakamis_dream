Independent audit of the quantum matrix/base torsor

Audit, 27 October 2026. Sections 2–4 of quantum_matrix_base_intertwiner_torsor.md pass this independent proof check. The noncommutative base and weak-composition conventions are essential. No tests were rerun.

For DS=ζSDDS=\zeta SD, uv=(Q/ζ)vuuv=(Q/\zeta)vu, the two commutation factors give exactly KE=QEKKE=QEK. Distinct Laurent monomials retain distinct u,vu,v degrees, proving injectivity of the proposed map. Adjoining the marked quantum roots with their stated cross relations recovers D,SD,S, and their Fourier projectors give every matrix unit. This is a noncommutative matrix cover, rather than a commutative generic finite-dimensional representation.

The logarithmic scale is correct: H=Nlog⁡(QN)=N2log⁡(Q/ζ),[X,P]=H,X=Nlog⁡u,P=Nlog⁡v. H=N\log(Q^N)=N^2\log(Q/\zeta),\qquad [X,P]=H,\quad X=N\log u,\ P=N\log v. For an independent derivation, the original NN-th powers give exp⁡(ad⁡X)P=P+H\exp(\operatorname{ad}X)P=P+H. Since HH is central, applying the formal logarithm leaves only the first difference and gives [X,P]=H[X,P]=H. The recovered D,SD,S commute with both logarithms, have order NN, and obey DS=ζSDDS=\zeta SD. The resulting Poisson form is −dX∧dP-dX\wedge dP, as stated.

The local root-lifting argument also preserves the quantum cross relations. To make this explicit, suppose ab=cN2ba,uN=a,vN=b,c=Q/ζ, ab=c^{N^2}ba,\quad u^N=a,\quad v^N=b,\quad c=Q/\zeta, with the roots marked by their scalar values at the regular root place. Conjugation and uniqueness of these marked roots imply bub−1=c−Nu,uvu−1=cv. b u b^{-1}=c^{-N}u,\qquad u v u^{-1}=cv. The first identity follows by taking the marked NN-th root of bab−1=c−N2ab a b^{-1}=c^{-N^2}a; the second follows by taking that of ubu−1=cNbu b u^{-1}=c^Nb. The same calculation gives the mixed relations with K,EK,E. Thus the Hensel lift extends the whole quantum cover, not merely its two power equations.

For the matrix splitting, G′=∑iα′(Ei1)E1i G'=\sum_i\alpha'(E_{i1})E_{1i} satisfies α′(Eab)G′=α′(Ea1)E1b=G′Eab\alpha'(E_{ab})G'=\alpha'(E_{a1})E_{1b}=G'E_{ab}. Its unit reduction makes it invertible. Conjugating by its inverse therefore fixes every matrix unit, whose centralizer consists exactly of scalar matrices over the possibly noncommutative base. This proves the entrywise base isomorphism and the printed semilinear module map. Holding that base map fixed leaves only central units; holding only the matrix-unit transport fixed leaves arbitrary base units. These two torsors must remain distinct.

The order in the coherence formulas checks directly. From Ggθg(Gh)=Gghbg,h G_g\theta_g(G_h)=G_{gh}b_{g,h} one gets θgh=Ad⁡bg,hθgθh\theta_{gh}=\operatorname{Ad}_{b_{g,h}}\theta_g\theta_h. Associating a triple matrix product in the two ways gives bgh,kbg,h=bg,hkθg(bh,k), b_{gh,k}b_{g,h} =b_{g,hk}\theta_g(b_{h,k}), with exactly the printed order. Central scalar cocycles arise only after coherent base maps have been specified. The stated Hilbert-90 sum transforms by aγ−1a_\gamma^{-1}, so it descends the generic line with the given cocycle convention.

The result constructs a local whole matrix/base transport from an actual regular algebra isomorphism. It does not infer that isomorphism, its coherent quantum base lift, source preservation, or a nonzero selected evaluation from classical smoothness. The separate source qualification in Section 6 is necessary.
