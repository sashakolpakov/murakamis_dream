Physical conditioning of the complete upper Gauss normal

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The upper Fourier coefficient sector of the actual balanced Gauss source has polynomial bounds, including its first meridian normal. Those bounds do not survive evaluation on the original physical Cartan–Casimir matrices. This note proves an exponential norm lower bound for the complete prescribed upper first-normal sum, not merely for one interpolation coupon or for an arbitrary nonnormal matrix. The lower bound survives every positive diagonal physical metric, including the binomial coherent metric.

The proof uses an actual two-tail-head block at every even level. An exact finite harmonic cancellation computes its whole radical multiplier. The product of its nonzero singular values then prevents cancellation from making the full matrix polynomially bounded. The original tail pivot on this block is one. A marked pivotal matrix-entry test is therefore explicit, but it is not an unmarked knot contraction or a claim about a fixed short-tangle exterior.

The only complete proof directs are C and H, pinned at the end. No numerical tests, mathematical programs, or imported asymptotic theorems are used.

6. Source meaning and limits

C bounds the complete upper coefficient array, its fixed scalar Fourier jets on thin annuli, and its fixed meridian jets by polynomials. Equations (33),(37) show that evaluation on the specified original physical representation can nevertheless cost an exponential. This holds after every positive diagonal factorial or coherent change of metric.

There is an exact marked pivotal test. In any such orthonormal physical coordinates, one entry of UNU_N has modulus at least ∥UN∥D/N\|U_N\|_D/N. The corresponding reversed matrix-unit mark has norm one. Since the actual total tail pivot is one on (7), its pivotal trace with UNU_N is exactly that entry. Applying (9) retains the actual HdKdH_dK^d first-normal factor as well. The mark can depend on the level and the entry attaining this bound. It is not asserted to be the image of a fixed braid or a fixed short filling.

The complete opposite source remains C’s identity T=ℬm(Wτ−1)𝒜NDtop𝒞r(M),J=DN(MK)−1T.(38) T=\mathcal B_m(W_\tau^{-1})\,\mathcal A_N\, D_{\mathrm{ top}}\mathscr C_r(M),\qquad J=D_N(MK)^{-1}T. \qquad\text{(38)} None of these factors, the original cut, its framing scalar, or an exterior contraction is removed. Even though the upper factor vanishes at the root, its normal (9) must be transported through all factors in (38). The present lower bound for that factor does not give a lower bound after those compositions. Their coherent cancellations remain possible.

Thus the proposed general polynomial-cost evaluation of the upper Fourier carry is false already on this actual fixed-width physical block. A proof that its contribution is small in a particular original closed source would require additional source-specific cancellation or a weighted non-diagonal estimate with its boundary costs retained. No knot noncancellation, geometric occurrence, or volume conclusion is inferred.
