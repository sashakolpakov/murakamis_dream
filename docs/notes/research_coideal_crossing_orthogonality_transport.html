Exact channel transport for the first coideal crossing

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The first crossing in the actual coideal graph has an explicit finite transform. It decouples the neighboring total coideal into a one-head operator, with every original phase retained. Its action between two independently specified joint Haar metrics is diagonal in the channel labels, with an explicit positive gain. The weight in that gain is symmetric and has a uniform two-label ideal-octahedron volume profile.

This gives an exact isometry after specified positive reweightings, and sharp exponential crossing costs before those reweightings. The two interface metrics differ. The original closing remains a prescribed complex row; no word-energy or scalar lower bound follows.
