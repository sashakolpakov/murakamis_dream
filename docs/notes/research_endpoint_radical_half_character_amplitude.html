The actual endpoint radical has a regular half-grid character amplitude

Private research note, 16 November 2026. Written proof only.

This note groups the entire radical part of the original endpoint coupon before estimating it. Near the complete physical color its alternating weights have an exact analytic interpolation, with the parity required for a full-grid Fourier argument. The interpolation also handles endpoint heights of order N−1N^{-1}. The low part of the coupon and the original spectator remain present; no filled-source rate or integration-cycle assertion is made.

Half-charge sampling contours are already used for the distinct-node Cauchy observation in the odd-level observation note, Sections 1–2, and its even-level counterpart, Section 5. Those sections were checked for coverage. The new input here is the exact analytic amplitude of the actual endpoint’s entire high coupon, including its microscopic-height normalization and terminating physical-row interpolation. Section 5 derives its own contour formula; the older observation theorems are coverage references, not additional proof dependencies or assertions about this different coupon.

4. The actual character, both parities, and the retained low part

The trigonometric factors in (25), before multiplication by FF, are in fact polynomials in cos⁡τ\cos\tau. This parity is essential. A half-charge cosine alone vanishes on the even physical grid; its normal data cannot simply be dropped. Equations (7) and (25) instead start with the full original trace, consume the radical normal exactly, and only then express its surviving values as a half-grid character.
