An actual upper Euler column with a controlled complex resolvent chart

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

A diagonal change on the donor alone turns the actual top column of the finite Euler star into a finite resolvent of the original recipient raising matrix. The recipients keep their binomial metric, compact coherent kernels, and endpoint arrows. On every fixed compact recipient chart away from incidence, sufficiently large donor coordinate gives an actual holomorphic kernel with nonzero amplitude and relative error O(N−1)O(N^{-1}).

Every donor input and dual factor is explicit. In the opposite kernel, full turnover moves the remaining inverse-factorial coupon to the last head; it does not disappear against the first-head quotient. The result estimates an actual source factor, not the complete opposite kernel or a geometric relative cycle.

The sole complete direct J is pinned below. It supplies the actual finite prefix, turnover, and compact source conventions. The column identity, metric estimate, all-power bounds, and interfaces are proved here.
