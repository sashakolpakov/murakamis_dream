The literal middle five-point configuration and its actual crossing factor

Research note, 28 October 2026. The middle five-point certificate in the classical crossing cycle, Section 3 is

+partial(a,b,p,p_1,s_M), p_1=a p, s_M=a s_0.  (1)

This note puts those actual shared quantum flags in an explicit canonical frame, identifies one of its tetrahedral arguments in an exact factorization of the original crossing, and gives a corrected finite reciprocal-coordinate transition with its original source. The transition has an unavoidable spectral projector. Its omitted term has a nonzero pairing in an actual one-crossing cup network.

This is not a full five-factor quantum realization of (1). It does give a literal configuration, factor and correction against which such a realization can be checked. In particular, scalar auxiliary region flags cannot remove the determinant wall: a rank identity below proves this for every generic choice of that flag.

1. A finite two-factor decomposition of the original crossing

Use the original balanced matrices from the shared quantum flags note. Let P be the original finite Euler polynomial

P(A)=sum_(h=0)^(N-1)(-1)^h Q^(h(h-1)/2)A^h/F_h,
F_h=(Q;Q)_h, Q=exp(2pi*i/N).

The original uncheck crossing is R_0=D P(e_1 f_2), including its original Cartan diagonal D. Its common scalar Cartan line kappa remains external throughout; the full checked crossing is kappa times flip R_0. Neither its framing nor its peripheral normalization is changed.

Choose any cyclic completion S_2 of the actual raising shift. Since f_2=(1-Z_2)S_2 is already the original nilpotent F_2,

R_0=D P(-e_1 Z_2 S_2) P(e_1 S_2)             (2)

is an exact identity at every level N and every pair of nonzero colors. Indeed put A=e_1S_2, B=-e_1Z_2S_2. Then BA=QAB, so the finite binomial identity gives P(A+B)=P(B)P(A). Every apparent product term of total degree >=N contains e_1^N and vanishes. Thus no root specialization of separate infinite compact products is involved. The cyclic wrap in S_2 cancels in A+B and (2) is the original crossing, not a cyclic replacement.

The individual factors in (2) are nilpotent Euler kernels with exact finite coefficients. They are not asserted to be single nondegenerate basic matrix dilogarithms.

2. An exact canonical frame for the actual five flags

Now take complete colors x=-q^(-1), lambda=x^(-1)=Qx, k=xN=(-1)(N-1), and the typed marks

S_1^N=mu, S_2^N=k mu.

As in the shared-flag note let

G_1=[[K,e],[fK,2lambda-QK]],
a=(1,S_1)^t, b=(1,t_2)^t,
t_2=f_1+K_1^(-1)S_2.

These are the fixed flags of the actual shared meridians H_1 and H_2. Choose the same Gauss auxiliary flags as the classical calculation: p=(0,1)^t and s_0=(1,0)^t. Keep their images as homogeneous columns:

p_1=G_1p=(e_1,2lambda-QK_1)^t,
s_M=G_1s_0=(K_1,f_1K_1)^t.                  (3)

No inverse of e_1 is used. Set

Z=Z_1, Y=-ZS_1, X=x S_2S_1^(-1),
L=[[Y,0],[-S_1,1]].                          (4)

All these frame factors are units, and

XZ=QZX, X^N=Z^N=1.

The five original columns have the following exact form:

Original flag F Canonical column F_c Retained right tag R_F in LF=F_c R_F
a (1,0)^t Y
b (1,1-X)^t Y
p (0,1)^t 1
p_1 (1-Z,1)^t lambda
s_M (1,1)^t YK_1

For example (t_2-S_1)Y^(-1)=1-X, Y e_1=lambda(1-Z), and (2lambda-QK_1)-S_1e_1=lambda. These prove the table by multiplication. Thus it is the literal middle configuration of (1), with every right normalization retained. A projective coordinate such as (1-Z)^(-1) is not asserted to exist in the finite module: its homogeneous column is what the table gives.

This frame is uniformly controlled at unit marks. If |mu|=1, then S_1,Y,K_1 and lambda are unitary. The norm of L and L^(-1) is the golden ratio (1+sqrt(5))/2; each right tag has norm and inverse norm one. The frame neither diagonalizes all five flags nor uses the generally exponential full coherent Fermat basis. These assertions therefore do not contradict the previously calculated exponential coherent-frame norms.

3. Its classical boundary and an actual tetrahedron factor

In the commutative limit the five flags of the table are

(a,b,p,p_1,s_M)=(0,1-X,infinity,(1-Z)^(-1),1).

Put H=X+Z-XZ. In the exact cross-ratio convention of the classical certificate, the omitted-vertex tetrahedra have

Omitted vertex j Cross-ratio r_j Boundary sign
a H/[X(1-Z)] +
b 1/(1-Z) -
p X/H +
p_1 X -
s_M H +

This is a direct determinant computation on those five points, so it is precisely the middle certificate (1). The first tetrahedron has positive quad coordinate

1/(1-r_0)=-X(1-Z)/Z.

But the last factor of the original crossing in (2) has the exact argument

n=e_1S_2=(Q-Z^(-1))X, n^N=0.                (5)

Its classical symbol is exactly -X(1-Z)/Z. Hence a genuine tetrahedron of this specific certificate, not a freely chosen auxiliary atom, occurs in (2). The tetrahedron omitting b has positive quad coordinate Z. The boundary is equivalently

[n_cl]-[Z]+[X/H]-[X]+[H]=0.                 (6)

Equation (6) is the classical statement. At the quantum level neither X, Z, nor the homogeneous columns alone authorize replacing the whole right factor in (2) by five uncorrected cyclic dilogarithms.

4. Two explicit issues in a naive quantum lift

For the usual cyclic pentagon center pattern, a classical parameterization of (6) is U=1-Z,V=X, with

1-U+UV=H,
-UV/(1-U)=n_cl,
V/(1-U+UV)=X/H.

Its left centers are X and (1-X)(1-Z)=1-H. Applying the ordinary complement relation gives exactly (6). But its literal quantum candidates do not form a quantum torus:

X(1-Z)-Q(1-Z)X=(1-Q)X !=0.                 (7)

Thus simply substituting the classical five-point coordinates in the generic cyclic pentagon is false. A scalar root phase or retained numerical multiplier does not remove (7). This does not rule out a nonlinear quantum change of coordinates with additional face factors; it locates the change that is needed for this actual configuration.

The reciprocal coordinate also has a precise finite correction. The would-be expression (1-QZ)^(-1)X cannot exist as a right solution to (1-QZ)U=X, since 1-QZ has a kernel and X is a unit. Let E_d be the Z=Q^(-1) spectral projector, and define the finite group inverse

H_Q(Z)|j>=1/(1-Q^(j+1))|j> (j<d),
H_Q(Z)|d>=0,
U=H_Q(Z)X.

Then exactly

UZ=QZU, U^N=0,
(1-QZ)U=X-E_dX.                              (8)

The projector in (8) is compulsory. It is not permission to replace the generic reciprocal coordinate by U without transporting the right-hand side as well.

5. An explicit corrected transition in the original crossing

There is a closed factorial gauge, rather than an unknown similarity, connecting (5) to the regularized primitive (8):

Gamma|j>=g_j|j>,
g_j=(-1)^j Q^(-j(j-1)/2)F_j^2,
Gamma n Gamma^(-1)=U.                       (9)

Here Gamma acts on the first actual head. To prove (9), use X|j> proportional to |j-1> on the first head. For 1<=j<N, the coefficient of n is Q(1-Q^(-j)) times that of X, whereas the coefficient of U is 1/(1-Q^j) times that of X. The required ratio is precisely

g_j/g_(j-1)=-Q^(1-j)(1-Q^j)^2.

Both operators have zero arrow at j=0. This proves every entry, including the wrap, independently of parity and mu. Consequently the original crossing has the exact refactor

R_0=D P(-e_1Z_2S_2) Gamma^(-1)P(U)Gamma.    (10)

Equations (8)–(10) give a corrected finite coordinate change at a tetrahedron of the literal middle five-point configuration, with every factor of the original R specified. They do not yet give the entire five-factor quantum identity for (6).

Gamma is not a negligible unit. At complex roots,

g_j=Q^j|F_j|^2.                              (11)

Its absolute weights are positive, but generally exponential in N. For example the already proved factorial estimate in the coherent-frame note gives log||Gamma||=2gamma_N+O(log N) and log||Gamma^(-1)||=2gamma_N+O(log N), with gamma_*=Vol(4_1)/(4pi)>0. Thus the explicit frame contributes at the scale being studied. It must stay in the period.

The same factorial square appears numerically in the existing degree-d Rogers cap row: at completeness its entries satisfy mu_r=x(d+r)g_r/N2. This does not cancel Gamma here. The Rogers index belongs to the relative fusion frame, whereas (9) acts on the original physical clock. A fusion comparison must carry the actual matrix and all of its sources before identifying those two diagonals.

6. Original source and the nonzero omitted wall

Keep the original complete cup and full marked cap

p=N sum_(a=0)^d x^(a-d)|a,d-a>,
m|a,b>=x^b/N^2, mp=1.

The unstripped common scalar line is kappa. The checked crossing B with kappa external obeys Bp=p. Directly,

Xp=Q^(-1)p,
m E_d X Bp=Q^(-1)/N.                        (12)

For the full crossing the second value is kappa Q^(-1)/N, with its same exterior framing and peripheral factors. Thus (8)’s correction is visible in an actual one-crossing source contraction, not only in an arbitrary matrix entry.

The five-point canonical frame also has its literal right source tags. At the a and b vertices put p’=Yp and m’=mY^(-1). Then

Xp'=p', m'p'=1, m'E_dXp'=1/N.               (13)

The Q phase in (12) has therefore not been discarded; (13) is the correctly typed changed source. Under the further factorial transition (9), use Gamma p and mGamma^(-1). Their pairing is again one. Neither vector can be replaced by the old vector individually in (10).

The cap still has its original affine charge (J_1+J_2+1)p=Np. The corrected clock transition (8) has not been identified with the internal integer edge winding of a positive Pachner move. Its projector and source tags are exactly part of what that identification must transport.

Endpoint wall removal preserves every original knot exponent

There is an unconditional, quantitative boundary consequence. Let C_N be the actual cut operator of any knot, with its original exterior scalar normalization retained. Its Hopf intertwining implies C_N=K_N I: commuting with the original K forces a diagonal matrix, and commuting with the original F, whose consecutive arrows are nonzero, makes all diagonal entries equal. This argument holds at every N and at the complete color, without an asymptotic or a Schur hypothesis. Consequently

m E_dX(C_N tensor I)p=Q^(-1)K_N/N,
m(1-E_dX)(C_N tensor I)p=(1-Q^(-1)/N)K_N.    (15)

The scalar in the second line satisfies

1/2 <= |1-Q^(-1)/N| <= 3/2  (N>=2).

Thus removal of this projector at the marked endpoint preserves the limsup and liminf of N^(-1)log|K_N|; the normalized logarithms change by at most (log 2)/N. This removes this specific boundary normalization issue without assuming a volume conjecture or a noncancellation statement. The wall itself has exactly the same exponential rate as K_N, with the polynomial factor 1/N, and cannot be declared exponentially suppressed.

The boundary sewing algebra is equally explicit. For any integers r,s and clock indices j,k,

E_jX^r E_kX^s=delta_(j,k-r) E_jX^(r+s),
m E_jX^r(C_N tensor I)p=Q^(-r)K_N/N.         (16)

Thus a fixed specified endpoint sewing word has an exact finite coefficient multiplying the original scalar. A claim that an interior five-point wall has moved to that endpoint must still prove its current/source transport. Equation (15) alone supplies neither that transport nor a comparison with a separately selected geometric saddle.

7. A generic scalar auxiliary flag cannot remove this wall

The obstruction is not caused by choosing p=infinity. Let P=(r,s)^t have scalar entries and ell_P=(-s,r). The original complete meridian has the factorization

G-lambda=(1,S)^t(K-lambda)(1,-S^(-1)).

Since ell_P P=0, it follows exactly that

ell_P G P=(rS-s)(K-lambda)(r-S^(-1)s).       (17)

For a generic scalar flag both outer factors are units; for example, r=1 and s^N!=mu. The middle factor has rank N-1. Hence the determinant between P and GP is singular of rank N-1 for every such generic scalar auxiliary choice. Inverting it inside the original finite module is impossible. This rules out repairing the naive generic five-point field construction merely by moving a scalar auxiliary flag.

This is a specific obstruction to that construction, not to a relative quantum five-point period. A valid continuation can retain a normal variable and the projector/source in (8), or add a genuine quantum auxiliary module with its originally specified reduction. It must then prove that the resulting combined five-factor product gives (10) with its typed source. Neither (7) nor (14) excludes that possibility. No such complete product or geometric saddle coefficient is asserted here.

8. The actual unwrapped-period coordinates match these flags

The actual cyclic/Hardy graph uses S_i as multiplication by z_i and Z_i=B_i as the shift f(z_i)->f(Qz_i). In that realization the same dictionary is literal:

X=x z_2/z_1, Z=B_1,
n=(Q-B_1^(-1))X=q Ecal_1 z_2.               (18)

Here Ecal is the actual rho^2 E. Indeed at completeness Ecal_1=z_1(-1)(B_1(-1)-1), and -xQ=q. All homogeneous frame identities of Section 2 and (5) use only these Weyl relations and the complete color. They therefore hold on that actual graph as well, before selecting its root center.

The central powers must be distinguished. On the unwrapped graph, XN=xN z_2N/z_1N is a variable central multiplication operator. On the typed finite heads used in Sections 2 and 6, S_2^N=k S_1^N gives X^N=1. That is a source/period restriction, not an identity in the unwrapped Laurent algebra. Equation (18) supplies the operator dictionary but does not prove its source pushforward or equate a central root value with a semiclassical shape.

9. Exact audit

Run

python3 experiments/audit_actual_middle_five_point_wall.py

This checks 28 original-R factorizations (2), 21 typed five-point frames and corrected factorial transitions, and 21 generic scalar-flag rank obstructions, over exact prime fields for N=2,…,8. It includes (12)–(13), both wraps, the actual original coefficient evaluator, and the full input cup. The all-level proofs above are finite identities; these tests are independent diagnostics.
