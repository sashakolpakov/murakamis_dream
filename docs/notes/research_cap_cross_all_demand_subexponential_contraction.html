Scope

Every degree in the original nonempty J_n has been covered, with both finite boundary currents retained. The proof asserts neither a polynomial all-demand factor nor estimates on a spectral neighborhood. It does not identify independent overlapping Casimirs, introduce a semisimple coupon adapter, or compare arbitrary exterior face targets. A whole-source or filling consequence would require its own assembly argument.

The new ingredients in this draft are the exact general contiguity (3)–(6), the even forced-unit lower check (9)–(12), and the seam-current comparison (13)–(14). No numerical growth experiment is used as proof.

scope and finite source verification

No finite monotonicity of individual coefficients is assumed.

The portable exact-field audit with recorded results checks N=3 through 10: 164 literal coefficient-contiguity identities and 52 original projected flux recurrences, including both parity cutoffs and empty terminal ranges. It separately checks 52 full raw physical three-port scalars, 52 full auxiliary recurrences, 24 actual low-cutoff identifications and 52 Newton resolvent entries. All calculations are exact in the chosen finite fields, with conservative integer-overflow bounds. These are finite source-identity checks, not an asymptotic proof. The subexponential estimates are established analytically above. Root completed a fresh redirected replay of this combined finite audit with identical recorded results.
