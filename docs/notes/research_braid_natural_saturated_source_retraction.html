A braid-compatible retraction onto the original saturated source

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The divided-current source equation has a retraction that commutes with every original regular braid. In the coherent corner coordinates this retraction is an explicit finite polynomial in the actual middle divided currents. Its source and complementary spaces therefore remain separate through overlapping crossings and through regular linear cup/cap maps.

A positive metric makes this separation orthogonal, agrees with the physical metric on the source, and retains the original polynomial input/closing budget. A second specified metric gives a uniform divided-current repair gap for the entire source equation. These metrics have different budget statements, made explicit below. Neither estimates the scalar carried by the restricted word nor identifies a geometric contributing saddle.

8. What the retraction settles

[D,K] already identify the saturated source as an actual divided-current kernel and provide its finite polynomial projector. [M] already gives complete source coordinates and exact original boundaries. [C] already identifies the coherent corner projection and its polynomial boundary product. The additional points are the full-small-invariant construction (11), braid and linear-gluing naturality, its signed current transport (15), explicit commuting form (17), compatible metric budget (27), and the full-source repair estimate (35) in the specified finite Gram (38). The original corner row is itself divided-current invariant (24a), so the complementary part is invisible to every word closing (25).

These statements concern fixed-root values. Differentiated generic families retain their full inhomogeneous Ward equations and the derivatives of any transported projector; the root formula does not silently make those equations homogeneous. Arbitrary root-only coupons without the specified regular generic lift are not covered by (20).

The construction imposes the complete value source restriction before estimating a coherent word. Its repair gap is in 𝔊\mathfrak G, not a claimed polynomial physical singular gap for JEJ_E. It gives no sharp word energy or positive lower bound for the original marked scalar. The remaining estimate concerns the source-projected word recursion or gradient and its original closing. Formula (35) quantifies distance when comparing with an unprojected trial; exact source projection itself requires no small-current-residual premise. The finite-exception volume statement after arbitrary short partial fillings is unchanged by this compatibility result alone.
