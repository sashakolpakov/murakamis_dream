31 October 2026. Independently reviewed conditional stationary-stratum lemma. This extends the real local Green regulator to the FORCED two marked zero curvatures. It does not remove additional free row/outer endpoint strata or prove that a maximum of the compact entropy problem satisfies the hypotheses below.

1. Exact hypotheses from the original source

Let C be the actual simplicial white triangulation of the certified connected flat plat, with the two ORIGINAL protected vertices u,v joined by their MAIN edge. Give every other vertex f a finite positive curvature l_f and set l_u=l_v=0. There are no additional zero or infinite radii. At each source triangle use the usual tangent-circle angle vector

theta_j=2 atan(l_j/sqrt(l_0 l_1+l_1 l_2+l_2 l_0)). (1)

A one-line triangle has angle zero at that line and strictly positive angles summing pi at its finite vertices. A two-line triangle uvf has angles (0,0,pi). Assign each triangle a sign sigma=+1 or -1. At cross ports both signs are positive. At a left Green port the input triangle (x,b,y) is positive and output (x,c,y) is negative; a right port reverses these roles. The negative triangle’s middle is the overloaded face.

Assume the actual shared degree equations hold. Every ordinary vertex has demand1 and protected vertices have demand0. The exact signed-angle formula for E gives, at every finite vertex f,

sum_(triangles at f) sigma theta_(triangle,f) =2pi(1-o_f), (2)

where o_f is the number of its overloaded-middle incidences. Original finite feasibility gives o_f in {0,1}; protected faces have o_f=0. No independent angle optimization has replaced the source constraints.

The two uv triangles necessarily have POSITIVE sign. Indeed uv is a MAIN edge, so in either triangle one of u,v is its middle. A negative triangle would overload that protected middle, which is impossible. These two triangles have no other degeneracy than their two line faces.

At a constrained source stationary point, equations (1) hold with ONE shared vector of finite positive multipliers whenever all remaining row/outer equations are strict. Forced cases l_b=0, l_x=0 or l_y=0 are retained as the corresponding one-step/endpoint limits; no derivative in a protected zero variable is imposed. This clause is a hypothesis on the stationary stratum, not a claim about every maximizer.

2. A cooriented developing disk closes the SAME marked strip

Remove the open uvf_R triangle from the sphere. The remaining complex is a triangulated closed disk, whose boundary vertices are u,v,f_R. Start with the other uv triangle uvf_L. Realize u,v as the outward halfplanes Y<=0 and Y>=H and f_L as a circle of radius r_fL=1/l_fL tangent to both, with H=2r_fL. Fix its horizontal center at zero. This uses one common Euclidean normalization, not separate normalizations at different ports.

Every all-finite triangle of prescribed positive radii is a rigid configuration of three EXTERNALLY tangent circles. Choose the orientation of its center triangle to be its prescribed sigma relative to the actual cyclic source order. A one-line triangle is likewise rigid: its two finite centers are on the inward side of that COORIENTED line, at normal distances r_f,r_g, and their horizontal separation is 2sqrt(r_f r_g). The two choices of its sign reverse that horizontal displacement.

Glue these rigid configurations along their actual shared edges. Along a finite/finite edge two distinct ordered centers determine the unique orientation-preserving Euclidean motion. Along a line/finite edge the cooriented line and finite center determine that motion. The only line/line edge uv is on the boundary of the disk, so it is never used as an underdetermined interior gluing. Thus every dual path develops the same radius data by actual Euclidean motions.

Here is the complete holonomy check. A dual cycle surrounding an interior finite vertex fixes its developed center. Its rotation is the signed sum of the center angles in its star, which is 2pi or zero by (2). It is therefore the identity Euclidean motion, including its translation part. This remains true at f_L: its uv corner contributes the recorded pi and the two cooriented line normals are opposite. Cycles of the dual disk are generated by these interior finite-vertex stars. The vertices u,v are BOUNDARY vertices, so no unproved translation loop around an infinite vertex remains. Development is path-independent.

The incident triangles of each boundary vertex u or v form a connected fan. They therefore propagate the SAME cooriented line, not an independent parallel translate at each occurrence. The final boundary circle f_R is one circle tangent on the inward side to both already fixed lines. Its center consequently has Y=r_fR and H-Y=r_fR. Hence r_fR=H/2. This closes the omitted positive uv triangle and proves the shared-strip condition.

The result is a globally assigned family of oriented white circles and the original two opposite lines. Adjacent circles are externally tangent. Nonadjacent circles may overlap, and the developed triangular surface may fold or have winding zero at overloaded vertices. Embeddedness, convexity and local degree one are NOT used or asserted.

3. Explicit boundary reflection and source-collar adapters

Write an anti-Mobius reflection by a determinant -1 matrix J; products of reflections use J_2 conjugate(J_1). For x,y>0, normalize the outer circles to center0/radius1/x and center (x+y)/(xy)/radius1/y. Put A=x+y, T_z=sqrt(z A+xy), and use

J_x=[[0,1/x],[x,0]], J_y=[[A/x,-(2x+y)/x^2],[y,-A/x]], J_z^eta=[[z/x+(y-x)/A+2i eta T_z/A,-z/x^2-2/x], [z,-z/x-(y-x)/A+2i eta T_z/A]]. (3)

For z>0 this is the circle tangent to both outer circles on the eta side. At z=0 the SAME matrix is a line reflection; all entries stay finite and its determinant stays -1. Thus a protected middle parent requires no division by its vanished clock or a newly chosen flag.

When x=0,y>0 choose instead the actual lower line and tangent y circle:

J_x=-iI, J_y=[[i,0],[y,i]], J_z^eta=[[-2eta sqrt(z/y)+i,-4/y], [z,2eta sqrt(z/y)+i]]. (4)

The finite z circle has center -2eta/sqrt(zy)+i/z and radius1/z. At z=0, (4) is the second line reflection [[i,-4/y],[0,i]], namely Y=2/y. In particular the double-zero corner x=b=0 retains the ACTUAL two-line strip and its selected contact, while T_b=0 is never divided out. For y=0,x>0 the explicitly ordered version is

J_x=[[i,0],[x,i]], J_y=-iI, J_z^eta=[[2eta sqrt(z/x)+i,-4/x], [z,-2eta sqrt(z/x)+i]]. (4b)

It follows by reversing the outer pair in (4) AND changing eta to -eta; P is inverted and H changes sign, so the displayed source coefficient in (5) remains exactly the same. This is the recorded ordering adapter, not a free independent reversal of a source port.

In either chart put P=J_y conjugate(J_x), A_z=J_z conjugate(J_x), B_z=J_y conjugate(J_z), so B_z A_z=P. These are the original ordered raw meridians, all of trace -2. Direct multiplication gives

H=P+I, H^2=0, H!=0, C=I+i(eta_c T_c-eta_b T_b)H/[2(x+y)], C A_b C^-1=A_c, C B_b C^-1=B_c. (5)

For (4), T_z=sqrt(zy), so (5) holds verbatim with x=0. These are the SAME source signs and entire-pair transport as the full physical half-power derivative calculation. They are not selected from trace alone. The actual threading word [2,1,1,2] takes raw auxiliary -C^-1, closes it and transports both physical ports. The original cup/cap/mark and all original prefix orders remain those of the source.
