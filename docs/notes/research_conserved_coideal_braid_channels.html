Conserved coideal channels with the original braid source

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof.

A particular conserved combination of the actual total generators has an exact degree-NN polynomial on every complete-color tensor power. Its full matrix is a repeated companion matrix, including the Jordan directions at the exceptional parameters. For a stable exterior range of the auxiliary parameter, its conserved channels can be made orthogonal with metric logarithm of order at most the level. Pinching the original closing into these channels is exact against the original intertwining word. The closing is retained and is not made positive.

The corresponding classical Hamiltonian is an explicit simultaneous conjugation flow. Its zero level gives the residual parabolic framing motion. These facts do not identify a selected quantum channel with a geometric period or establish a nonzero scalar coefficient.

1. Complete finite matrices and the conserved element

Fix N≥2N\ge2, width b≥1b\ge1, and q=eπi/N,Q=q2,h=2πi/N,x=−q−1,γ=xN=(−1)N+1,q=eπi/(2N).(1) q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad Q=q^2,\quad h=2\pi i/N,\quad x=-q^{-1},\quad \gamma=x^N=(-1)^{N+1},\quad \sqrt q=e^{\pi i/(2N)}. \qquad\text{(1)} Use the factorial basis of W, declared orthonormal for the estimates below. At the complete meridian M=1M=1, its actual generators are Kea=xQ−aea,Eea=(Q−a−1)ea−1,Fea=(1−Qa+1)ea+1.(2) K e_a=xQ^{-a}e_a,\quad E e_a=(Q^{-a}-1)e_{a-1},\quad F e_a=(1-Q^{a+1})e_{a+1}. \qquad\text{(2)} The absent arrows at a=0,N−1a=0,N-1 are zero. Here EE is W’s scaled ℰ\mathcal E, not an unscaled unrolled generator. Retain the ordered coproducts K12=K1K2,E12=E1K2+E2,F12=F1+K1−1F2.(3) K_{12}=K_1K_2,\qquad E_{12}=E_1K_2+E_2,\qquad F_{12}=F_1+K_1^{-1}F_2. \qquad\text{(3)} Write their width-bb iterates as 𝖪,𝖤,𝖥\mathsf K,\mathsf E,\mathsf F. Every literal unmarked checked braid word RR commutes with all three, including its original signed crossing scalars.

For an auxiliary scalar c∈ℂc\in\mathbb C, define Bc(b)=𝖥−(𝖤−cI)𝖪−1,Hc(b)=Bc(b)−2I.(4) B_c^{(b)}=\mathsf F-(\mathsf E-cI)\mathsf K^{-1},\qquad H_c^{(b)}=B_c^{(b)}-2I. \qquad\text{(4)} These commute with RR. The order in (4) is fixed. In particular Hc𝖪=−(2𝖪+𝖤−𝖥𝖪−cI)H_c\mathsf K=-(2\mathsf K+\mathsf E-\mathsf F\mathsf K-cI); replacing 𝖥𝖪\mathsf F\mathsf K by 𝖪𝖥\mathsf K\mathsf F would change the finite operator. Direct substitution in (3) gives Bc(b)=(F1−E1K1−1)⊗I+K1−1⊗Bc(b−1),Bc(0)=c.(5) B_c^{(b)}=(F_1-E_1K_1^{-1})\otimes I +K_1^{-1}\otimes B_c^{(b-1)},\qquad B_c^{(0)}=c. \qquad\text{(5)} Equivalently Yc=Bc−cIY_c=B_c-cI satisfies ΔYc=Yc⊗1+K−1⊗Yc\Delta Y_c=Y_c\otimes1+K^{-1}\otimes Y_c. Since KK is unitary and ∥E∥,∥F∥≤2\|E\|,\|F\|\le2, ∥𝖤∥,∥𝖥∥≤2b,∥Bc(b)∥≤4b+|c|.(6) \|\mathsf E\|,\|\mathsf F\|\le2b,\qquad \|B_c^{(b)}\|\le4b+|c|. \qquad\text{(6)}

2. A finite cyclic determinant with all zero arrows retained

Define the monic polynomial pN(z)=∏sN=1(z−s−q/s)=2qN/2TN(z2q),qN/2=i.(7) p_N(z)=\prod_{s^N=1}(z-s-q/s) =2q^{N/2}T_N\!\left(\frac{z}{2\sqrt q}\right), \qquad q^{N/2}=i. \qquad\text{(7)} Here TNT_N is Chebyshev of the first kind. These NN values of zz are distinct: a collision of two different s,ts,t would require st=qst=q, impossible after taking NN-th powers. Thus the monic polynomials agree.

On one head let Sea=ea+1mod⁡NS e_a=e_{a+1\bmod N} and Cea=QaeaC e_a=Q^ae_a. Formula (2) gives the exact identity Bc(1)=A(S)−Tc(S)C,A(s)=s+q/s,Tc(s)=Qs+q/s+cq.(8) B_c^{(1)}=A(S)-T_c(S)C,\qquad A(s)=s+q/s,\qquad T_c(s)=Qs+q/s+cq. \qquad\text{(8)} Indeed F=S(1−QC)F=S(1-QC), −EK−1=qS−1(1−C)-EK^{-1}=qS^{-1}(1-C), and cK−1=−cqCcK^{-1}=-cqC. Diagonalize the unitary cyclic shift SS. In that basis CC is a single cyclic permutation. The determinant of z−A(S)+Tc(S)Cz-A(S)+T_c(S)C has only its diagonal permutation and its complete-cycle permutation. Since Tc(s)/q=A(s−1)+cT_c(s)/q=A(s^{-1})+c and qN=−1q^N=-1, this proves det⁡(zI−Bc(1))=pN(z)+(−1)N−1∏sN=1Tc(s)=pN(z)+pN(−c).(9) \det(zI-B_c^{(1)}) =p_N(z)+(-1)^{N-1}\prod_{s^N=1}T_c(s) =p_N(z)+p_N(-c). \qquad\text{(9)} The argument is polynomial in cc; no coefficient is assumed nonzero. It includes N=2N=2. For example then p2(z)=z2−2ip_2(z)=z^2-2i and (Bc(1))2=(4i−c2)I(B_c^{(1)})^2=(4i-c^2)I.

Cayley–Hamilton and pN(−z)=(−1)NpN(z)p_N(-z)=(-1)^Np_N(z) yield pN(Bc(1))=γpN(c)Ip_N(B_c^{(1)})=\gamma p_N(c)I. This is a polynomial matrix identity in the scalar parameter. Substitute the commuting tail matrix from (5) for that parameter and induct. The result is pN(Bc(b))=γbpN(c)I,qN,b,c(z):=pN(z)−γbpN(c).(10) \boxed{\quad p_N(B_c^{(b)})=\gamma^b p_N(c)I,\qquad q_{N,b,c}(z):=p_N(z)-\gamma^bp_N(c).\quad} \qquad\text{(10)} This proof applies to the entire tensor power, without semisimplifying its quantum-group module.

3. Exact free companion form and its cost

Put db=Nb−1d_b=N^{b-1} and 𝔣k=(Q;Q)k=∏a=1k(1−Qa)\mathfrak f_k=(Q;Q)_k=\prod_{a=1}^k(1-Q^a), 𝔣0=1\mathfrak f_0=1. Consider the block Krylov matrix 𝒮b=[e0⊗Idb,Bc(e0⊗Idb),…,BcN−1(e0⊗Idb)].(11) \mathcal S_b=[\,e_0\otimes I_{d_b},\ B_c(e_0\otimes I_{d_b}), \ldots,B_c^{N-1}(e_0\otimes I_{d_b})\,]. \qquad\text{(11)} To reach first-head index kk from zero in kk steps, every step must use F1F_1. Consequently (11) is block triangular with diagonal blocks 𝔣kIdb\mathfrak f_k I_{d_b}, all nonzero. Relation (10) now gives 𝒮b−1Bc(b)𝒮b=Comp⁡(qN,b,c)⊗Idb.(12) \boxed{\quad \mathcal S_b^{-1}B_c^{(b)}\mathcal S_b =\operatorname{Comp}(q_{N,b,c})\otimes I_{d_b}.\quad} \qquad\text{(12)} The companion convention is the basis 1,z,…,zN−11,z,\ldots,z^{N-1}. Thus qN,b,cq_{N,b,c} is the minimal polynomial, and the characteristic polynomial is qN,b,cdbq_{N,b,c}^{\,d_b}, for every cc. Every Jordan block of the scalar companion occurs dbd_b times. No normal channel is lost at a collision.

Equivalently the full head space is free of rank dbd_b over AN,b,c=ℂ[t]/qN,b,c(t)A_{N,b,c}=\mathbb C[t]/q_{N,b,c}(t), and the full commutant of BcB_c is End⁡AN,b,c(VN⊗b)≃Mat⁡db(AN,b,c).(12a) \operatorname{End}_{A_{N,b,c}}(V_N^{\otimes b}) \simeq\operatorname{Mat}_{d_b}(A_{N,b,c}). \qquad\text{(12a)} Every original braid word lands in this algebra. If γbpN(c)=−2i\gamma^bp_N(c)=-2i, the substitution t=qzt=\sqrt q\,z identifies AN,b,cA_{N,b,c} with F’s full Conway quotient ℂ[z]/(2TN(z/2)+2)\mathbb C[z]/(2T_N(z/2)+2), retaining its double-root normals. This does not identify the transported original closing with that quotient’s canonical Frobenius functional or with a knot A-polynomial.

There are controlled inverse bounds for (11), not merely a nonzero determinant. For T=Bc(b−1)T=B_c^{(b-1)}, let the monic polynomials with commuting operator coefficients satisfy D0=I,D−1=0,Dk+1(z;T)=(z+qQkT)Dk(z;T)−q(1−Qk)2Dk−1(z;T).(13) D_0=I,\quad D_{-1}=0,\qquad D_{k+1}(z;T)=(z+qQ^kT)D_k(z;T) -q(1-Q^k)^2D_{k-1}(z;T). \qquad\text{(13)} The first-head tridiagonal entries give Dk(Bc;T)(e0⊗I)=𝔣k(ek⊗I)D_k(B_c;T)(e_0\otimes I)=\mathfrak f_k(e_k\otimes I). For fixed width and |c|≤C*|c|\le C_*, (6) and (13) bound the sum of coefficient norms of DkD_k by C1kC_1^k. Also sine complementation gives |𝔣N−1|=N,|𝔣k|−1=|𝔣N−1−k|/N≤2N−1−k/N.(14) |\mathfrak f_{N-1}|=N,\qquad |\mathfrak f_k|^{-1} =|\mathfrak f_{N-1-k}|/N\le2^{N-1-k}/N. \qquad\text{(14)} Expanding the identities after (13) expresses each coordinate block in the Krylov columns. Together with (6), this proves ∥𝒮b∥+∥𝒮b−1∥≤exp⁡(Cb,C*N).(15) \|\mathcal S_b\|+\|\mathcal S_b^{-1}\| \le \exp(C_{b,C_*}N). \qquad\text{(15)} All these bounds include the exceptional parameters. The recurrence (13) is an Al-Salam–Chihara specialization; no new recurrence or orthogonal-polynomial technique is claimed.

The roots of (10) are simple except when its right side is a critical value of pNp_N. For N≥3N\ge3 these values are ±2i\pm2i; for N=2N=2 only −2i-2i is critical. At a critical value all multiple roots have multiplicity exactly two. Formula (12) therefore specifies the whole exceptional Jordan form, with block length at most two, rather than replacing it by its value quotient. These normal channels are generalized eigenspaces; this does not transport meridian or off-root quantum-parameter derivatives.

4. An orthogonal channel metric on a stable parameter range

Assume 4≤|c|≤C*4\le|c|\le C_*. Choose ww with w+w−1=c/qw+w^{-1}=c/\sqrt q and |w|>1|w|>1, and choose εN=γb\varepsilon^N=\gamma^b, |ε|=1|\varepsilon|=1. The roots are λj=q(εwQj+ε−1w−1Q−j),0≤j<N,|w|≥2+3.(16) \lambda_j=\sqrt q(\varepsilon wQ^j+ \varepsilon^{-1}w^{-1}Q^{-j}), \qquad 0\le j<N, \qquad |w|\ge2+\sqrt3. \qquad\text{(16)} They are distinct. For j≠kj\ne k, factoring their difference gives |λj−λk|≥(|w|−|w|−1)|Qj−Qk|,∏k≠j|λj−λk|≥N(|w|−|w|−1)N−1.(17) |\lambda_j-\lambda_k| \ge(|w|-|w|^{-1})|Q^j-Q^k|, \quad \prod_{k\ne j}|\lambda_j-\lambda_k| \ge N(|w|-|w|^{-1})^{N-1}. \qquad\text{(17)} Use the full spectral idempotents Πj=∏k≠jBc−λkIλj−λk,Gc=∑jΠj*Πj.(18) \Pi_j=\prod_{k\ne j} \frac{B_c-\lambda_kI}{\lambda_j-\lambda_k}, \qquad G_c=\sum_j\Pi_j^*\Pi_j. \qquad\text{(18)} Equations (6), (16)–(17) prove ∥Πj∥≤eCb,C*N\|\Pi_j\|\le e^{C_{b,C_*}N}. Their sum is the identity, so Cauchy–Schwarz also proves N−1I≤Gc≤NeCb,C*NI,∥log⁡Gc∥=Ob,C*(N).(19) N^{-1}I\le G_c\le N e^{C_{b,C_*}N}I,\qquad \|\log G_c\|=O_{b,C_*}(N). \qquad\text{(19)} Indeed GcΠj=Πj*Πj=Πj*GcG_c\Pi_j=\Pi_j^*\Pi_j=\Pi_j^*G_c. The NN full eigenspaces have dimension dbd_b and are exactly orthogonal in GcG_c. Nothing inside a conserved eigenspace has been projected away.

The estimates survive the explicit factorial coordinate adapter. If va=𝔣aeav_a=\mathfrak f_a e_a, then Fva=va+1Fv_a=v_{a+1}, Eva=(1−Qa)(Q−a−1)va−1Ev_a=(1-Q^a)(Q^{-a}-1)v_{a-1}, and KK is unchanged. This is the undivided complete module with its scaled EE. The diagonal change and its inverse have norms eO(N)e^{O(N)} by (14) and |𝔣a|≤2a|\mathfrak f_a|\le2^a. On fixed tensor width, transporting (19) to those raw physical coordinates still has logarithmic radius O(N)O(N). This is an exponential controlled adapter, not a polynomial equivalence of the two Hilbert metrics. The original diagonal pivot and cut commute with that adapter.

For M’s operator packets use the induced Hilbert–Schmidt metric ∥A∥HS(Gc)=∥Gc1/2AGc−1/2∥HS\|A\|_{{\mathrm{ HS}}(G_c)}=\|G_c^{1/2}AG_c^{-1/2}\|_{\mathrm{ HS}}. Its column-vectorization Gram is Gc¯−1⊗Gc\overline{G_c}^{-1}\otimes G_c, with logarithmic radius at most 2∥log⁡Gc∥=O(N)2\|\log G_c\|=O(N). Simultaneous conjugation transports multiplication and its original root row exactly. Putting this packet metric on the complete-color block and the identity metric on M’s ordinary anchor block therefore lies in a known color-preserving window of the same O(N)O(N) order, enlarged if necessary. The head metric and the operator-packet metric have different dimensions. The polynomial (10) itself is asserted only at the complete meridian. At other colors the deleted lower arrow does not equal the corresponding cyclic coefficient; no ordinary-color or whole-meridian polynomial identity is inferred from (8).

5. The original closing and its exact channel sum

Retain W’s whole original source at this complete root: P=T0⊗g⊗(b−1),T0=e0e0*,g=K1−N,Φ(O)=𝜘βTr⁡(PO).(20) P=T_0\otimes g^{\otimes(b-1)},\qquad T_0=e_0e_0^*,\quad g=K^{1-N},\qquad \Phi(O)=\varkappa_\beta\operatorname{Tr}(PO). \qquad\text{(20)} The framing/peripheral scalar 𝜘β\varkappa_\beta is unchanged. For a simple spectrum define Pdiag=∑jΠjPΠjP^{\mathrm{ diag}}=\sum_j\Pi_jP\Pi_j. Whenever [O,Bc]=0[O,B_c]=0, cyclicity and ΠjΠk=0\Pi_j\Pi_k=0 give the exact identity Tr⁡(PO)=∑jTr⁡(ΠjPΠjO)=Tr⁡(PdiagO).(21) \boxed{\quad \operatorname{Tr}(PO)=\sum_j\operatorname{Tr}(\Pi_jP\Pi_jO) =\operatorname{Tr}(P^{\mathrm{ diag}}O). \quad} \qquad\text{(21)} In particular this applies to the literal unmarked braid word. All its conserved channels and every compressed closing remain. At exceptional parameters the primary idempotents, rather than individual eigenvalue quotients, give the same identity. The size-two blocks from (12) stay inside them. No near-collision version of (19) is claimed without a separate separation bound.

For (19), the Πj\Pi_j are orthogonal projections in the indicated head metric. Thus pinching also has the exact observation-norm consequences ∥Pdiag∥Gc→Gc≤∥P∥Gc→Gc,∥Pdiag∥HS(Gc)≤∥P∥HS(Gc).(22) \|P^{\mathrm{ diag}}\|_{G_c\to G_c}\le\|P\|_{G_c\to G_c},\qquad \|P^{\mathrm{ diag}}\|_{{\mathrm{ HS}}(G_c)}\le\|P\|_{{\mathrm{ HS}}(G_c)}. \qquad\text{(22)} The second inequality is the corresponding contraction of root-row dual norms. For the original row it is usable on the commuting subalgebra in (21), where the two rows agree. Neither matrix is asserted positive. General inserted clocks need not commute with BcB_c; their off-diagonal channel terms must then be retained. Equation (21) is not an equality of all marked-current functionals. This distinction is essential at the original cut.

In the simple-spectrum case there is nevertheless an exact source advantage: [Pdiag,Bc]=[Pdiag,Hc]=0[P^{\mathrm{ diag}},B_c]=[P^{\mathrm{ diag}},H_c]=0. Thus its inner Ward boundary vanishes, while its pairing with every unmarked original braid is unchanged. For a general inserted OO, the exact correction is still Tr⁡(PO)=Tr⁡(PdiagO)+Tr⁡((P−Pdiag)O).(22a) \operatorname{Tr}(PO)=\operatorname{Tr}(P^{\mathrm{ diag}}O) +\operatorname{Tr}((P-P^{\mathrm{ diag}})O). \qquad\text{(22a)} At a collision, primary pinching alone need not commute with the nilpotent part. The invariant-probe assertion is not extended to it.

7. Coverage and remaining comparison

Coideal minimal-polynomial and semisimplicity questions are already treated at odd-order roots by Shimizu–Sugitani, Coideal subalgebras of quantum SL2 at roots of unity, introduction and Theorem 4.10 (primary text). No formula identification or proof technique from that paper is a premise here. The standard recurrence also appears in a different Casimir problem in F. The source-specific assertions proved here use the literal complete matrices at every NN, their free companion form with controlled cost, and the unchanged braid closing.

G already constructs a different collective trace-Hamiltonian graph, with an initialized factorial kernel. W already supplies the weighted centralizer field, and C supplies a finite additive E-flow and relative endpoints. The present autonomous Hamiltonian does not identify those different finite currents or import their selected coefficients.

In particular (26) is a principal interior statement, not a whole finite Egorov estimate through the highest-cut wall. It neither transports the central highest-cut augmentation to a noncentral parabolic point nor identifies a relative geometric framing chain. The auxiliary cc, the classical level aa, and a quantum eigenvalue λj\lambda_j are distinct data. No projection to ker⁡Hc\ker H_c is made. The full sum (21) can cancel. The metric cost is controlled exponential, not asserted negligible at the volume scale. No original scalar lower bound or finite-exception theorem is inferred.
