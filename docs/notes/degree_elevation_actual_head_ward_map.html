Degree elevation preserves the cut and transports the actual head Borel complex

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The uniform co-source obtained by integrating the paired Fr dual has N+1 states, whereas the original head has N. A cyclic identification duplicates its endpoint and does not preserve the head row. The explicit matrix degree elevation below corrects this on ALL head matrices. Together with retained whole arrow gauges it gives an actual cut-Ward cochain map, including the original co-source homotopy. It is not a multiplicative R-network map or a proof of Gaussian/source descent.

The earlier degree-elevated wall note constructed the binomial wall row and its fixed-head equivariance obstructions. It did not construct the full matrix degree elevation, its uniform-cut identity, or the actual head-current map proved here.

1. Full matrix elevation, with both endpoint entries

Put n=N-1. In the actual Fr symbol convention sigma_r(C)=ell_r(u) C psi_r(t)/(1+tu)^r, where psi_r(t)_j=t^j and ell_r(u)_i=binom(r,i)u^i, define

(ℰNC)ij=N−iNCij+iNCi−1,j−1,0≤i,j≤N. (\mathcal E_NC)_{ij} =\frac{N-i}{N}C_{ij}+\frac{i}{N}C_{i-1,j-1}, \qquad 0\le i,j\le N. \tag{1}

Every out-of-range old entry is zero. Both terms are required. Binomial cancellation gives the exact polynomial identity

ℓN(u)(ℰNC)ψN(t)=(1+tu)ℓn(u)Cψn(t),σN(ℰNC)=σn(C). \ell_N(u)(\mathcal E_NC)\psi_N(t) =(1+tu)\ell_n(u)C\psi_n(t), \qquad\sigma_N(\mathcal E_NC)=\sigma_n(C). \tag{2}

The map is injective because the symbol numerator determines every matrix entry. It is unital and preserves BOTH actual uniform rows:

Tr⁡(ℰNC)N+1=Tr⁡CN,∑ij(ℰNC)ijN+1=∑ijCijN. \frac{\operatorname{Tr}(\mathcal E_NC)}{N+1} =\frac{\operatorname{Tr}C}{N},\qquad \frac{\sum_{ij}(\mathcal E_NC)_{ij}}{N+1} =\frac{\sum_{ij}C_{ij}}N. \tag{3}

Indeed each old entry C_ij occurs with total coefficient ((N-i)+(i+1))/N=(N+1)/N, and the same holds for diagonal entries. The second identity is the original average rank-one coupon, not only the ordinary trace.

For a diagonal C the elevated entries are

C̃R=N−RNCR+RNCR−1,C̃0=C0,C̃N=CN−1. \widetilde C_R=\frac{N-R}{N}C_R+\frac RN C_{R-1}, \quad\widetilde C_0=C_0,\quad\widetilde C_N=C_{N-1}. \tag{4}

Thus the actual paired-Fr interval, whose integrated dual is uniform 1/(N+1), gives the original head average exactly after (4). By contrast the uncorrected cyclic P_diag insertion repeats C_0 at R=N; it gives weights 2/(N+1),1/(N+1),…,1/(N+1). For C_R=Q_N^R its value is 1/(N+1), while the old head average is zero. Formula (4) is the required endpoint correction, not an identification of these different rows. The binomial wall B_N remains its separate co-source with its original factor 1/N.

2. Exact classical and whole Cartan covariance

The actual divided Fr matrices on degree r satisfy e psi=(rt-t^2 partial_t)psi, f psi=partial_t psi and h psi=(r-2t partial_t)psi, with their corresponding dual formulas. The normalized commutator fields are independent of r. Applying them to (2) proves

ℰN([xn,C])=[xN,ℰNC],x=e,f,h. \mathcal E_N([x_n,C])=[x_N,\mathcal E_NC], \qquad x=e,f,h. \tag{5}

This is also checked directly from (1); it is not an assumed published quantization result. Since both terms of (1) have the same i-j, for EVERY nonzero whole parameter Q,

ℰN(Ad⁡Q−JnC)=Ad⁡Q−JNℰNC. \mathcal E_N(\operatorname{Ad}_{Q^{-J_n}}C) =\operatorname{Ad}_{Q^{-J_N}}\mathcal E_NC. \tag{6}

The image therefore is a global P1 operator/co-source submodule under the ordinary divided Fr f and whole Cartan dilation. Its two extreme entries (0,N),(N,0) are identically zero. It is not the entire End(Sym^N); in particular an arbitrary degree-N wall current need not belong to it.

3. Actual whole head arrows give a strict cut-current map

In a specified original head basis let K_j=xQ^(-j), F_(j+1,j)=f_j(q), 0<=j<N-1, with all f_j actual nonzero arrow units near q_N. This includes the original balanced f_j=1-Q^(j+1) and the integral f_j=[j+1]_q, with their actual source adapters carried. Define the WHOLE diagonal gauge

D0=1,Dj+1/Dj=(j+1)/fj(q). D_0=1,\qquad D_{j+1}/D_j=(j+1)/f_j(q).

Then DFD^(-1)=f_n, the classical degree-n lowering matrix, and DKD^(-1)=K. Every q-factorial, source and dual in this gauge is retained; invertibility at fixed N is not an asymptotic norm bound. Put T_0(C)=E_N(DCD^(-1)). Equations (5)–(6) give the LITERAL whole matrix cochain identities

T0((Ad⁡K−I)C)=(Ad⁡Q−JN−I)T0(C),T0([F,C])=[fN,T0(C)]. T_0((\operatorname{Ad}_K-I)C) =(\operatorname{Ad}_{Q^{-J_N}}-I)T_0(C),\qquad T_0([F,C])=[f_N,T_0(C)]. \tag{7}

Thus the actual two-row cut-Ward complex maps isomorphically onto its image subcomplex by T_0 in degree zero and the displayed two row maps in degree one. This is an explicit current map, not a declaration that two Borel groups are abstractly isomorphic.

The actual Hopf F row is transported too, with its explicit ordered K-row correction retained:

ad⁡F(C)=[F,C]+K−1[K,C]F,[K,C]=((Ad⁡K−I)C)K. \operatorname{ad}_F(C)=[F,C]+K^{-1}[K,C]F, \quad [K,C]=((\operatorname{Ad}_K-I)C)K. \tag{8}

Use this invertible row operation before (7). Its product term is sent by the LINEAR map T_0 with its full order; T_0 is not applied to its factors separately.

The target may use the ACTUAL whole divided Fr lowering matrix on any fixed residue string s. If its arrow is beta_(R,s)(q), set B_(0,s)=1 and B_(R+1,s)/B_(R,s)=beta_(R,s)/(R+1). These are regular actual units with root one, and F^(N)|_s=B_s f_N B_s^(-1). Consequently

Ts=Ad⁡BsT0 T_s=\operatorname{Ad}_{B_s}T_0 \tag{9}

has the same strict properties with actual F^(N)|_s in place of f_N. B_s commutes with the whole memory Cartan Q^(-R). This Cartan is the specified Fr memory observable; it is not silently relabeled as the original high-color quantum K, whose root action is on the head factor. The actual opposite divided generator and mixed tensor coproduct still require their specified normal rows.

4. Original cut co-source and its exact transported homotopy

Conjugations preserve trace, so (3) gives tau_Fr T_s=tau_head. Before D,B are inserted, (3) also preserves the original average coupon on ALL matrices. After those gauges the actual source and dual are transported; a binomial or uniform row cannot simply be substituted for the conjugated row.

There is nevertheless an explicit comparison of the original head average Phi_h and the uniform Fr interval row Phi_Fr on the image. Let B_h be the actual head matrix [K,B_h]=e_h-I/N. In the target let K_Fr=Q^(-J_N) and put

$$ (B_{\rm Fr})_{ij}=\frac1{(N+1)(Q^{-i}-Q^{-j})} \quad(i\ne j,\ \{i,j\}\ne\{0,N\}), $$

with the diagonal and the two extreme corners zero. Every displayed denominator is a unit near Q_N. The omitted corners of T_s(C) are IDENTICALLY zero, so the actual trace homotopies give

$$ \Phi_h(C)-\Phi_{\rm Fr}(T_sC) =-\operatorname{Tr}(B_h[K,C]) +\operatorname{Tr}(B_{\rm Fr}[K_{\rm Fr},T_sC]). \tag{10} $$

The last commutator equals T_s((Ad_K-I)C) K_Fr. Thus (10) is an explicit cochain homotopy on the actual cut-Ward complex. It holds with the whole D and B_s units present, not only at their numerical root values. On actual diagonal Green arrays these conjugations do nothing and (3)–(4) preserve the original cut exactly. On Ward-closed cut matrices the homotopy is zero as well. The raw Green 1/N normalization remains the one in the original certificate.

The excluded extreme target corners and the complementary operator subspace cannot be discarded if an arbitrary Fr-valued wall or normal current leaves the image. This is where an additional endpoint/mixed-normal source may still occur.

5. Scope of this actual map

The source and coefficient complex at the FINAL finite cut is now explicitly related to global Fr P1 operators, the integrated uniform Fr co-source, and the actual head Hopf rows. It does not turn T_s into an algebra homomorphism: already N=2, E_2(P_0)=diag(1,1/2,0), so E_2(P_0)^2!=E_2(P_0). Products, R crossings and mixed coproducts must therefore be transported as whole ordered operators, not factor by factor.

Nor has an internal remaining flow index been identified with this open cut matrix index. The geometric Gaussian/source comparison must still map its two retained currents and normal endpoint classes to the explicit finite complex. The result here is a genuine cut cochain map and co-source homotopy, not a selected geometric period or volume limit.

Exact matrix and coherent-symbol audit. The results pass fourteen actual generic-q/root cases N=2,…,8 and 406 full matrix units. They check the complete symbols and both uniform rows, whole arrow gauges, the actual Fr divided string, Hopf row correction and co-source homotopy. These are actual finite cut identities, not a Gaussian or selected-cycle test.
