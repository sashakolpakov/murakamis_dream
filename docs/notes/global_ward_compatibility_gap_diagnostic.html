The simultaneous many-head Ward gap is a separate problem

28 October 2026. The proved polynomial inverse for an actual two-head lowering current does not yet give an inverse for arbitrary many-head constraints. This note records a reproducible test and an exact identity identifying the full system worth studying. It proves no new asymptotic spectral bound and reports no counterexample to such a bound.

1. The actual physical operators

At the canonical complete color, put

Q=exp(2pi i/N), x=-exp(-pi i/N),
F_one e_a=(1-Q^(a+1))e_(a+1),
Ecal_one e_a=-(1-Q^(-a))e_(a-1),
K_one e_a=x Q^-a e_a.

Here Ecal=rho^2 E in the original balanced physical frame. This polynomial scalar rescaling retains the actual Ward equation. For b heads use the ACTUAL coproducts

F_b=sum_j K_1^-1...K_(j-1)^-1 F_j,
Ecal_b=sum_j Ecal_j K_(j+1)...K_b.

Let P reverse the physical heads. Since K is unitary and Ecal_one=-F_one^*, directly

Ecal_b=-P F_b^* P.                                  (1)

In total-degree blocks this is

Ecal_s=-P_(s-1) F_(s-1)^* P_s.

Thus the simultaneous Ward normal operator is exactly

F_s^*F_s+P_s F_(s-1)F_(s-1)^*P_s.                   (2)

This identity uses the original physical metric, not a nonunitary fusion similarity. It holds in every head number at this complete color. No claim that (2) has a uniform spectral gap follows from the identity alone.

There is also an exact Hermitian formulation. On the full physical tensor space put D=F_b+Ecal_b. Equation (1) gives

(P D)^*=-P D.

Thus iP D is Hermitian. For a vector in a single total degree, the F and Ecal outputs are orthogonal, so ||Dv||^2 is precisely the simultaneous Ward energy (2). This is a folded Hermitian Dirac formulation in the original metric. Its spectral estimate remains to be proved; Hermiticity alone does not bound a gap.

There is a further exact kernel issue: the full Dirac kernel need not be graded. Already at one complete physical head, N=5, write c_r=1-Q^r and

v=e_0+[c_1/conjugate(c_2)]e_2+e_4.

Then (F+Ecal)v=0 by the two odd-row recurrences, whereas neither F e_2 nor Ecal e_2 vanishes. The degree-2 component is nonzero and obeys K=1, since x=Q^2. Thus even on the required K=1 slice, projection of the FULL Dirac kernel is not the joint Ward kernel. A gap for the global Hermitian Dirac would require an additional kernel-angle estimate before yielding the fixed-degree stacked inverse. This is why the target remains the actual Gram (2).

2. The exact Gram matrix has nontrivial magnetic triangle flux

Write H_s=F_s*F_s+Ecal_s*Ecal_s, and let a be a physical head state of total degree s. Its diagonal is exactly

H_s[a,a]=4 sum_i [sin^2(pi(a_i+1)/N)+sin^2(pi a_i/N)].

For i<j, a_i<d and a_j>0, set a’=a+e_i-e_j and M=sum_(i<h<j)a_h. Direct multiplication of the two actual coproducts gives the off-diagonal entry

H_s[a',a]=8 q^-1 sin(pi(a_i+1)/N)sin(pi a_j/N)
   *cos((j-i)phi-pi(a_i+a_j+2M)/N),                 (3)

where q=exp(pi i/N) and phi=Arg x=pi-pi/N. All other off-diagonal entries vanish except the Hermitian reverse edges. Equivalently the real cosine factor in (3) is

(-1)^(j-i) cos[pi(a_i+a_j+2M+j-i)/N].

For clarity, the F^*F contribution before combining phases is

x^(j-i)Q^(-a_i-M-1)
                     (1-Q^(a_i+1))(1-Q^-a_j),

and the Ecal^*Ecal contribution is

x^(i-j)Q^(a_j+M-1)
                     (1-Q^(a_i+1))(1-Q^-a_j).

Their sum is exactly (3). No asymptotic symbol or K=1 approximation is used in this calculation.

Consequently a diagonal unitary vertex gauge CANNOT in general make this Gram matrix an ordinary real weighted graph Laplacian. There is an explicit countertest inside the actual K_total=1 sector: take b=4, N=5, s=8, and the triangle

a=(1,1,2,4), b=(2,0,2,4), c=(2,1,1,4).

Put k=(5-sqrt(5))/2. Equation (3) and the elementary golden-angle sine identities give exactly

H[b,a]=k q^-1, H[c,b]=k q^-1, H[a,c]=-sqrt(5)q,
H[b,a]H[c,b]H[a,c]=-sqrt(5)k^2 q^-1.               (4)

The triangle product is nonreal. It is invariant under every diagonal unitary gauge, while any matrix with real off-diagonal entries has real products around all such triangles. All three states obey K_total=1 since x4Q-8=1. Thus this obstruction is present on the required actual invariant-weight fiber, not only on another degree or a hypothetical kernel.

This does not refute a polynomial simultaneous Ward bound, a magnetic/Hodge comparison, or a nonlocal unitary decomposition. It rules out the direct real-path argument obtained by simply gauging away all global phases. The two-head path proof has no triangles and does not encounter this obstruction.

There is nevertheless an EXACT norm-preserving realification at all head numbers. The entry formula and reversal of the interval i,…,j give

P_s H_s P_s=conjugate(H_s).

Define P_+=(I+P_s)/2, P_-=(I-P_s)/2, and

U=P_++iP_-=[(1+i)/2]I+[(1-i)/2]P_s.

Then U is unitary, mixes only a state and its physical reversal, and

U^* H_s U=Re H_s-P_s Im H_s                            (5)

is REAL SYMMETRIC. Indeed the real part commutes with P_s and the imaginary part anticommutes with it. This proves (5) directly. It is a sparse two-state mixing with condition number one, not an exponentially conditioned fusion transformation.

The resulting real matrix has signed interactions and no automatic diagonal-dominance or Laplacian comparison. Thus the triangle test rules out a diagonal phase gauge, while (5) supplies a valid more general unitary reduction. Any use on the original source must transform the currents and source by this same specified U.

3. Four-head diagnostic at the actual two-cup degree

For b=4 choose s=2(N-1), exactly the total integer degree of two original cups. The regular Borel decomposition has full length-N strings, so the exact rank of F_s is

rank F_s=h_4(s)-h_3(s-(N-1)),

where h_b(s) is the coefficient of t^s in (1+…+t(N-1))b. This fixes which singular values are nonzero without numerical thresholding. The original physical matrices give the following resolved nonzero F gaps:

N= 4: 1.08239220
   6: 0.43260052
   8: 0.18973845
  10: 0.08478187
  12: 0.03780617
  14: 0.01676550
  16: 0.00740054
  20: 0.00142953.

The exact zero cluster remains below 9e-15 in these diagnostics. This rapid decay warns against carrying the two-head estimate over to F_b by an unproved iteration. These finite values alone DO NOT prove exponential decay or disprove every polynomial estimate.

Crucially, keeping BOTH actual Ward currents changes the picture. For [F_s; Ecal_s] the computed smallest nonzero values are

N= 4: 1.20597689
   6: 0.62788204
   8: 0.39963573
  10: 0.28269084
  12: 0.21347310
  14: 0.16854041.

The observed joint kernel dimension is N at these levels. The joint gap is consistent with a polynomial bound, which is not proved here. In particular the F-only diagnostic is not an obstruction to a polynomial simultaneous Ward contraction.

4. The remaining constructive target

A useful many-head theorem must solve the compatible simultaneous current system with its joint kernel and cokernel retained. Its exact Gram is (2)–(3), (4) rules out a diagonal phase removal, and (5) gives a condition-one sparse realification. It must not impose a separate invariant-pair equation at an intermediate slice where interbridge crossings have mixed the pair channels. Equations (1)–(2) provide an actual fixed-metric formulation of that target. The local two-head bounds and the cyclic Ward-wall contraction are valid inputs; compatibility and a simultaneous spectral estimate are further work.

Even a proved polynomial simultaneous inverse would transport exact currents, not identify a positive geometric kernel or prove the selected scalar’s volume rate by itself.

Reproduce the diagnostic. The default levels stop at N=12; --larger adds N=14,16. Larger resolved F-only values above were separately computed in the same physical basis; a contemplated N=24 dense SVD was interrupted.
