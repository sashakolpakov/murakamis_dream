The actual Mellin source has a rigid selected quantum-normal line

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. For every knot, the actual bounded Fourier–Mellin source gives an observable monodromy covector which detects its selected rational root section. Its first quantum-normal symbol is therefore nonzero without a simple-root or semisimplicity hypothesis. The actual coefficientwise whole-Q lift makes that selected section horizontal for the rational kernel-to-cokernel normal operator. Together these facts exclude nonconstant central scalar multipliers compatible with the same whole operator.

The retained scalar is exactly the Kashaev residue at infinity. This does not estimate that residue, identify its regulator norm, or transfer rigidity to a different meridian source object.

1. The specified actual source and its admissible roots

Retain the actual finite-color construction:

Z(Q,X)=∑n≥n0Cn(Q)Xn,A(Q,X,S)Z=B(Q,X),Sf(X)=f(QX). Z(Q,X)=\sum_{n\ge n_0}C_n(Q)X^n,\qquad A(Q,X,S)Z=B(Q,X),\qquad Sf(X)=f(QX). \tag{1.1}

The coefficients Cn(Q)C_n(Q) are the original reduced color periods, Laurent polynomial in QQ, with Cn(1)=1C_n(1)=1. The specified fixed starting color makes BB a nonzero fixed Laurent polynomial in XX, with rational Q-coefficients, built from finitely many actual colors. Its lowest coefficient is explicitly nonzero. The formal lattice Xn0ℚ(Q)[[X]]X^{n_0}\mathbb Q(Q)[[X]] and (1.1) specify the actual series uniquely. No generic rationality of ZZ is assumed.

Apply any fixed left S-power needed to make A’s shift support 0,…,r0,\ldots,r, and apply the same shift to B. Write

A=∑j=0rajSj,a0ar≠0. A=\sum_{j=0}^{r}a_jS^j,\qquad a_0a_r\ne0. \tag{1.2}

Order zero is allowed. For all sufficiently large N, at ζ=exp⁡(2πi/N)\zeta=\exp(2\pi i/N), all coefficients required below are regular over E=ℂ(X)E=\mathbb C(X), and a0,ar,Ba_0,a_r,B remain nonzero in E. Indeed each possible failure says that one of finitely many fixed nonzero rational functions of Q vanishes or has a pole. Thus only finitely many Q-values, hence finitely many levels, are excluded. An X-dependent zero or pole is retained as an element of E; it is not removed by choosing a numerical X-point.

By the independently proved actual all-color root periodicity, the specialization ZN=Z(ζ,X)Z_N=Z(\zeta,X) is rational. Explicitly,

ZN=∑j=1N−1Cj(ζ)Xj+KNXN1−XN−∑j=1n0−1Cj(ζ)Xj. Z_N=\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}C_j(\zeta)X^j+K_NX^N}{1-X^N} -\sum_{j=1}^{n_0-1}C_j(\zeta)X^j. \tag{1.3}

This is equality with the same formal expansion at zero. It holds even if KN=0K_N=0. The rational source equation is still ANZN=BNA_NZ_N=B_N.

2. An observable source quotient detects the selected symbol

Use the canonical homogeneous operator attached to this source:

Â=(S−σQ(B)/B)A,σQf(X)=f(QX). \widehat A=(S-\sigma_Q(B)/B)A,\qquad \sigma_Q f(X)=f(QX). \tag{2.1}

It has order d=r+1d=r+1, with both endpoint coefficients nonzero. Let C be its forward solution companion, so σQv=Cv\sigma_Qv=Cv for v=Jdf=(f,Sf,…,Srf)tv=J_df=(f,Sf,\ldots,S^rf)^t, and put T=C−1σQT=C^{-1}\sigma_Q. There is a literal observable row

ℓ=1B(a0,…,ar),ℓJdf=Af/B. \ell=\frac1B(a_0,\ldots,a_r),\qquad \ell J_df=Af/B. \tag{2.2}

The coefficient identity (2.1), with no chosen solution, implies

σQ(ℓ)C=ℓ,ℓT=σQ∘ℓ. \sigma_Q(\ell)C=\ell,\qquad \ell T=\sigma_Q\circ\ell. \tag{2.3}

For example, the first equality says that the source quotient of any homogeneous solution of (2.1) is shift-invariant; its coefficient of the highest jet is precisely the companion relation. The second follows by right multiplication by C−1C^{-1}.

At the root let T0=T|Q=ζT_0=T|_{Q=\zeta}, H0=T0NH_0=T_0^N, and v0=JdZN∈Edv_0=J_dZ_N\in E^d. Since σζN=1\sigma_\zeta^N=1, (2.3) gives

T0v0=v0,ℓ0H0=ℓ0,ℓ0v0=1. T_0v_0=v_0,\qquad \ell_0H_0=\ell_0,\qquad \ell_0v_0=1. \tag{2.4}

Set L0=H0−IL_0=H_0-I, K=ker⁡L0K=\ker L_0, 𝒞=coker⁡L0\mathcal C=\operatorname{coker}L_0, and let ι:K→𝒞\iota:K\to\mathcal C be inclusion followed by quotient. The row ℓ0\ell_0 kills im⁡L0\operatorname{im}L_0. Consequently

ℓ0ι(v0)=1,ι(v0)≠0. \ell_0\iota(v_0)=1, \qquad \iota(v_0)\ne0. \tag{2.5}

This is a dual source certificate, not a characteristic-polynomial guess. No assertion about other eigenvalue-one Jordan blocks is needed. In fact p=v0ℓ0p=v_0\ell_0 is a root difference-equivariant projection onto Ev0Ev_0: (2.3)–(2.4) give pT0=T0ppT_0=T_0p and p2=pp^2=p. Thus this selected line is a direct summand of the root module even if the complementary module is nonsemisimple.

The quotient is observable in the original scalar state and its shifts. It is not an unused unit line adjoined to force (2.5). For another supplied original annihilator, including a left multiple, one may rebuild its finite-color source B and apply the same proof. One may not erase that source and apply the conclusion to an arbitrary homogeneous left multiple.

For order zero, (1.1) itself already determines a rational function Z=B/a0Z=B/a_0 generically. The same rank-one homogenization and observable row remain valid for this selected normal statement; they assert no geometric block or additional geometric order.

3. The actual formal lift kills the rational normal obstruction

Put Q=ζ(1+ϵ)Q=\zeta(1+\epsilon), DX=X∂XD_X=X\partial_X, and retain every moving argument in the specified whole companion. As in the first quantum-normal construction,

T(Q)N−I=L0+ϵL1+O(ϵ2),L1=H1+NH0DX,DN:K→𝒞,DN(v)=[L1v]/N2. \begin{split} T(Q)^N-I&=L_0+\epsilon L_1+O(\epsilon^2),\\ L_1&=H_1+NH_0D_X,\\ D_N:K\longrightarrow\mathcal C,\qquad D_N(v)&=[L_1v]/N^2. \tag{3.1} \end{split}

Every CnC_n is regular at the root. Coefficientwise differentiation therefore gives an actual first lift Z=ZN+ϵZ1+O(ϵ2)Z=Z_N+\epsilon Z_1+O(\epsilon^2) with Z1∈F=ℂ((X))Z_1\in F=\mathbb C((X)) and the same fixed lower X-bound. No convergence in X, uniform Q-radius, or bound on color derivatives is needed. Rational coefficients of the companion and of B−1B^{-1} expand in E[[ϵ]]⊂F[[ϵ]]E[[\epsilon]]\subset F[[\epsilon]]; the orders of their X-poles may depend on the epsilon coefficient.

The actual formal whole solution of (2.1) has jet v=v0+ϵv1+O(ϵ2)v=v_0+\epsilon v_1+O(\epsilon^2), v1∈Fdv_1\in F^d, satisfying

L0v1+L1v0=0. L_0v_1+L_1v_0=0. \tag{3.2}

Both L0L_0 and L1v0L_1v_0 are over E. For a matrix over any field E and any extension F/E,

im⁡(L0:Fd→Fd)∩Ed=im⁡(L0:Ed→Ed). \operatorname{im}(L_0:F^d\to F^d)\cap E^d =\operatorname{im}(L_0:E^d\to E^d). \tag{3.3}

To prove it, row-reduce over E. The zero rows and the corresponding augmented consistency conditions stay zero or nonzero under field extension. Thus (3.2) implies that its right-hand side lies in the rational image, and

DN(v0)=0. D_N(v_0)=0. \tag{3.4}

This proves vanishing of a rational obstruction class. It does not assert that the actual Z1Z_1, actual jet v1v_1, or normal cut functional is rational, and does not replace them by a rational lift.

The usual cyclic descent applies to K, 𝒞\mathcal C, and this normal map. Its constant field is ℂ(U)\mathbb C(U), U=XNU=X^N, and its symbol on that field is U∂UιU\partial_U\,\iota.

4. Whole first-normal compatibility removes central scalars

Let c(U)∈ℂ(U)c(U)\in\mathbb C(U), or more generally ℂ((U))\mathbb C((U)). Suppose c(U)v0c(U)v_0 has a first-order lift for the SAME specified whole homogeneous companion (2.1). Then

0=DN(cv0)=cDN(v0)+Uc′(U)ι(v0)=Uc′(U)ι(v0). 0=D_N(cv_0)=cD_N(v_0)+Uc'(U)\iota(v_0) =Uc'(U)\iota(v_0). \tag{4.1}

After scalar extension if necessary, applying the source covector (2.5) gives Uc′=0Uc'=0. In characteristic zero, c is constant. The actual source value one or a fixed nonzero color-prefix anchor then fixes that constant to one. For all sufficiently large N a fixed anchor Cn0(ζ)C_{n_0}(\zeta) is nonzero, since the fixed Laurent polynomial Cn0C_{n_0} is nonzero and has finitely many roots.

There is a direct scalar check which avoids choosing a rational normal lift. For any compatible whole first-order solution W set h=AW/Bh=AW/B. Equation (2.1) says σQh=h\sigma_Qh=h modulo ϵ2\epsilon^2. If W0=c(U)ZNW_0=c(U)Z_N, then h0=c(U)h_0=c(U). Iterating N times yields

(σQN−I)h=ϵNDXc(U)+O(ϵ2)=ϵN2Uc′(U)+O(ϵ2)=0. (\sigma_Q^N-I)h=\epsilon N D_Xc(U)+O(\epsilon^2) =\epsilon N^2Uc'(U)+O(\epsilon^2)=0. \tag{4.2}

Thus this rigidity uses only the actual source quotient and the first whole quantum jet. It excludes a central scalar change of the selected section, not arbitrary source-kernel additions.

5. Actual presentations distinguish the selected criterion

The actual unknot has Cn(Q)=1C_n(Q)=1. With start one, Z=X/(1−X)Z=X/(1-X), g=X−1−1g=X^{-1}-1, its Mellin source is gZ=1gZ=1. The canonical homogeneous operator is (S−1)g(S-1)g. After the exact gauge h=gfh=gf, its selected solution is h=1h=1, with visible source row one and nonzero selected symbol.

The equally actual annihilator (S−1)2g(S-1)^2g has, in h coordinates, forward companion

C=(01−12)=I+J,J2=0,J≠0. C=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}=I+J, \qquad J^2=0,\quad J\ne0.

At a level-N root, H0=C−N=I−NJH_0=C^{-N}=I-NJ. Its actual selected vector (1,1)t(1,1)^t lies in both ker⁡(H0−I)\ker(H_0-I) and im⁡(H0−I)\operatorname{im}(H_0-I), so its selected symbol vanishes. Indeed (σQ−1)2c(XN)=O(ϵ2)(\sigma_Q-1)^2c(X^N)=O(\epsilon^2) for EVERY c. The unnecessary left factor destroys the first-normal scalar test if the observable source is forgotten. This is a diagnostic on the actual unknot, rather than a replacement knot state.

Conversely, simplicity is stronger than needed. Still for the actual unknot, use the supplied original operator (Y−1)(T−1)(Y-1)(T-1) and start two. Its exact Mellin pair is

A=(S−1)g,Z=X2/(1−X),B=(Q−1)X. A=(S-1)g,\quad Z=X^2/(1-X),\quad B=(Q-1)X.

Canonical homogenization is (S−Q)(S−1)g(S-Q)(S-1)g. In h coordinates its companion has distinct eigenvalues 1 and Q. At every primitive level-N root its monodromy is I2I_2, so the eigenvalue one is double and semisimple, while the observable selected symbol is nonzero. A simple norm root is not an all-knot premise of Sections 2–4, even for canonically rebuilt nonminimal source objects.

6. A genuine affine nonresonance criterion and an actual input

For a supplied rational affine equation σv=CAv+c\sigma v=C_Av+c, its augmented monodromy has the form

$$ H_{\rm aug}=\begin{pmatrix}H_A&s\\0&1\end{pmatrix}. \tag{6.1} $$

If I−HAI-H_A is invertible, its characteristic polynomial satisfies

$$ \Delta_{\rm aug}(t)=(t-1)\det(tI-H_A),\qquad \Delta'_{\rm aug}(1)=\det(I-H_A)\ne0. \tag{6.2} $$

Thus it has a simple eigenvalue one. Translation by the unique rational particular solution block-diagonalizes (6.1). This uses a genuine supplied source equation; it cannot be inferred merely from an arbitrary homogeneous root annihilator.

The actual quantum extremal-coefficient criterion proves I−HAI-H_A invertible whenever the homogeneous scalar operator has a unique strictly least Y-order coefficient (or unique greatest degree at infinity). Its proof already includes cyclic descent; no growing determinant or classical-symbol inference is needed.

There is a concrete actual input here. The independently proved whole figure-eight identity and its explicit root source operator give, at every level N,

(c−S−1+c0+c+S)FN=D(Y)(3−U−U−1),U=YN,D=Y(Y+1)(Q−Y2)(QY2−1),g0=Q2Y2−QY4+QY3+QY−Q+Y2,c−=Y2(Q−Y)(QY2−1),c0=−(Y−1)2(Y+1)g0,c+=Y2(QY−1)(Q−Y2). \begin{split} (c_-S^{-1}+c_0+c_+S)F_N&=D(Y)(3-U-U^{-1}),\\ U&=Y^N,\\ D&=Y(Y+1)(Q-Y^2)(QY^2-1),\\ g_0&=Q^2Y^2-QY^4+QY^3+QY-Q+Y^2,\\ c_-&=Y^2(Q-Y)(QY^2-1),\\ c_0&=-(Y-1)^2(Y+1)g_0,\\ c_+&=Y^2(QY-1)(Q-Y^2). \end{split} \tag{6.3}

Here c0c_0 alone has Y-order zero, with coefficient Q; both neighbors have order two. This persists at EVERY primitive root, including level two. Hence the homogeneous order-two monodromy has no eigenvalue one. The genuine rank-three source homogenization has a simple eigenvalue one for every N over ℂ(Y)\mathbb C(Y). Its selected observable source is 3−U−U−1≠03-U-U^{-1}\ne0; one may normalize the covector by that central factor generically.

This is an actual root-module statement. The known off-root finite cyclotomic calibration is not thereby the original auxiliary finite-Verma Q-lift. No assertion identifying those whole normal connections is made, and no intrinsic norm is identified with the classical geometric A-polynomial. At a special U-value where the source factor vanishes, its marked lattice must be retained instead of dividing by it.

7. The original period and the remaining comparison

For the SAME section (1.3),

KN=Res⁡X=∞ZN(X)dX/X. K_N=\operatorname{Res}_{X=\infty} Z_N(X)\,dX/X. \tag{7.1}

The full ideal ADO factor remains ηNKN/N\eta_NK_N/N, with its specified parity and meridian germ. Nothing in the normal argument replaces that factor by a number before cancellation or estimates the exponential growth of (7.1).

The central coordinate in Sections 3–4 is X^N in the Mellin polarization. A comparison to an original meridian profile in Y^N requires an actual source/co-source map. In particular, for the earlier regularized equation 𝒜R=bN\mathcal A R=b_N, a meridian change of the original F acts as

(R,1)↦(cR+(c−1)p,1), (R,1)\longmapsto(cR+(c-1)p,1),

rather than multiplying the full augmented section by c. It is a homogeneous source-kernel addition, so the scalar theorem above does not remove it automatically. The selected regulator norm, geometric source comparison, and asymptotic residue bound remain additional problems.

The stronger cyclotomic jet reconstruction also handles arbitrary homogeneous source-kernel additions in this canonical Mellin realization. It retains the exact pole lattice and a bounded number of quantum levels of the same inhomogeneous source equation. Under those additional conditions the entire rational root function is unique, not just its selected scalar line. This remains a uniqueness theorem rather than a regulator or residue-growth estimate.
