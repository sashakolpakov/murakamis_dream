Independent audit of the two physical cuts, bands and selected source

27 October 2026. The two-cut route gives a genuine two-position source/current model. It escapes the one-cut diagonal obstruction, has a unimodular flow chart, and supplies canonical nonzero LOCAL Gaussian blocks for bounded port offsets. The global selected-class comparison still needs its specified endpoint/projector and sheet data. The assertions below distinguish these operations explicitly.

1. Integral physical coordinates and the original source

The existence/lattice proof in the actual two-cut construction passes independently. Edge differentials here mean logarithmic physical-clock differentials on the classical critical scheme; integer labels define the underlying flow lattice. If all edge differentials lay in the span of the first cut differential, so would all transfer differentials, contradicting the two-dimensional critical framing tangent space. A second independent physical edge therefore exists on an open chart.

For a connected graph G with circulation lattice L, the kernel of evaluating a set S of edge flows is the circulation lattice of G minus S. Incidence ranks give exactly rank⁡evS=|S|+1−c(G\S). \operatorname{rank}\mathrm{ev}_S=|S|+1-c(G\setminus S). Rank two for the chosen pair implies that its complement is connected. A spanning tree avoiding both edges gives an integral fundamental-cycle basis with these two flows as literal coordinates. Thus there is no finite lattice index, congruence loss, extra cover or Jacobian factor. All zero-extension support conditions move with that integer change of variables.

Opening the second identity wire means replacing it by the literal matrix unit E_bd. Summing E_bb restores its identity. Every original pivotal/turn/framing/peripheral tensor stays in its original place; no second pivotal weight is added. The reconstruction T_(a,b)^(c,d) and its double diagonal J_ab therefore have the exact source FN=1N∑a,bJab. F_N=\frac1N\sum_{a,b}J_{ab}. \tag{1} Use RAW coefficients for J. If the old Green array already includes 1/N, multiply its partial reshape by N before applying (1). There is no second normalization 1/N.

J is a linear reshaping, not the two-leg operator algebra. For example diagonal extraction of the identity on H tensor H is the all-ones matrix, whose source value in (1) is N, while the actual two-cut unknot is the swap tensor and gives J=I, value one. A trace-center or multiplicative statement about J cannot silently be imported from the original four-port operator.
