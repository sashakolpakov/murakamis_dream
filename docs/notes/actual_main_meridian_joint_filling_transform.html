Joint filling contours for independently long signed twists

The first Fourier term of one filled crossing is not enough for a whole filled source. Its relative error has size e−cN/n2e^{-cN/n^2}, whereas other fillings can produce a much larger change from the complete positive exponent. The proof here retains every positive integer Fourier frequency, called a charge, until after the common wire variables have been integrated. It compares their joint critical actions, so it permits independently long and arbitrarily unequal fixed signed integers.

The grouped wire packet is an input stated precisely below and proved from the finite source. Its construction retains the original positive binary resolutions. The direct crossing trace supplies the exact local contour, and the general component theorem supplies the grouped error bound for arbitrary bare components. Together these yield the filled-volume theorem for the specified class. The moving filling functional here is the polynomial crossing power itself.

7. Scope of the transfer

The analytical conclusion is conditional only on the explicit finite packet and grouped global-error statements, not on a conjectural moving full-twist coefficient expansion. The local operator RsnvR_s^{n_v}, translated grid contours, all endpoint currents, and the first charge coefficient are exact.

The grouped wire packet, together with the general component comparison, supplies these interfaces for every fixed strict ordered Conway tree. It constructs the original primitive lattice and positive resolutions, proves (2), (7) and (8) with ν=3(1−m)/2\nu=3(1-m)/2, and proves the fixed grouped error (6).

There is no additional valence, adjacency or path condition on the bare junctions. For the fixed original knot and parity pattern, (37) and (41) therefore hold for every independently sufficiently long fixed signed filling. The original marked geometric calibration identifies the volume. The nonzero quantum lower bound is supplied by (34). The turn deletions used to estimate correction terms do not change this packet, its scalar factors or its filling action.

The lower bound on |nv||n_v| is a single source-dependent constant. It can be maximized over the finitely many retained parity/sign conventions if the desired theorem quantifies over them. It is independent of the chosen fixed radius R<1R<1: the action, the wire and angular windows, the Gaussian constants in the exponent, and the structural high-state/bare-error margins come from the complete source. The radius enters only polynomial or bounded prefactors, the convergence of the geometric kernel tails for large NN, and the uniform remainder constants. Accordingly the threshold for NN, but not the threshold for the filling integers, may depend on 𝒏\mathbf n and RR. There is no same-sign assumption, no bound on ratios of different |nv||n_v|, no prime-level restriction, and no limit in which the filling integers grow with NN.

The distinction between the two uses of an exponential gap is essential. Fixed global window and contour-tail losses can be compared with the total complete-to-filled volume drop after all fillings are sufficiently long. The charge-dependent losses in (28) are instead compared with the same joint filled critical action. Replacing this second comparison by a smallest local gap against the total drop would not establish the stated independent-slope result.
