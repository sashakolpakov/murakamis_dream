What fixed algebraic rigidity can force for the actual selected period

28 October 2026. Exact cyclotomic pointing already determines the actual quantum period. It does not automatically make its geometric coefficient nonzero. Two precise facts sharpen the torsor target:

  1. The SAME original figure-eight family has exponential growth on its complete meridian arc and bounded values on every fixed-color meridian arc approaching the same complete classical point. A uniform dominant geometric comparison regular on the raw central fibre is therefore impossible. The divided normal source is essential, rather than an optional choice of coordinates.
  2. The explicit whole-word Kummer period has a fixed number of variables, but growing cover genus and growing character homology. It has not yet supplied the fixed finite-rank algebraic object required by the existing whole-comparison rigidity theorem.

These are proofs about the original anchored state and its actual cover. No rescaled invariant or adjustable levelwise source is used.

1. The source is already fixed; uniqueness is not the missing datum

Use the actual family and conventions of the all-level operator theorem and the normal-period theorem:

q_N=exp(pi*i/N), Q_N=q_N^2, Y=M^2,
F_N(1)=K_N,
F_N(Q_N^j)=C_j(q_N), 1<=j<N.

For each FIXED j, C_j(q) is the specified Laurent-polynomial ordinary-color period, C_j(1)=1. The exact all-level recurrence, its actual finite initial window and its bounded quantum jets determine K_N uniquely. The Mellin theorem strengthens this to the entire rational root section with its pole lattice.

There is consequently no freedom to alter the knot period while keeping those exact data. The unresolved assertion is that this unique anchored object has the required selected regulator character. Uniqueness cannot be converted into existence of a regulator comparison without producing a candidate satisfying the original source, lattice and quantum-normal equations.

2. Fixed algebraic coefficients have polynomial size on a fixed-color arc

Put t=1/N and retain the root source coordinates (t,Q,Y) on the central slice U=Y^N=1. Let E be a fixed finite FIELD extension of k(t,Q,Y), k a fixed embedded characteristic-zero field. A specified finite residue-field mark and an etale selected place can be retained. Let a be a nonzero element of E, regular on the marked chart where the arc is tested.

For all sufficiently large FIXED positive integers j, every specialized conjugate of a on

gamma_j(t)=(t,exp(2pi*i*t),exp(2pi*i*j*t))      (1)

has upper and lower polynomial bounds at t=1/N, for all large N. The required lower bound on j depends on the fixed presentation of a, not on N. In particular it cannot be exponentially small or zero at infinitely many large levels.

Here is a direct proof, including the possible vanishing issue. Choose a polynomial relation for a with nonzero constant and leading coefficients, clear all denominators, and consider each nonzero coefficient P(t,Q,Y). Write

P=sum_(r,s) p_(r,s)(t) Q^r Y^s.

Let A be the span of its Q exponents. For j>A the integers r+js belonging to different collected pairs (r,s) are distinct. Thus

P(gamma_j(t))=sum_(r,s) p_(r,s)(t)
                                  exp(2pi*i*(r+js)t)

is not identically zero. Independence follows by successively applying the constant-coefficient operators partial_t-2pii(r+js), with powers exceeding the polynomial degrees, to isolate one exponential. A nonzero resulting analytic germ has finite vanishing order at t=0. Apply this to the finitely many relation coefficients. Cauchy’s root bound and the same bound for the reciprocal polynomial give

c N^(-B)<=|a_(i,N)|<=C N^B.                  (2)

The proof is uniform over all roots of the selected relation. It does not assume bounded absolute cyclotomic degree or heights.

In particular, the complete figure-eight scalar itself cannot be a fixed algebraic function of (t,Q) on a finite cover: (2) would give zero exponential rate, contradicting (3) below. This does not exclude a fixed algebraic connection or torsor with a transcendental marked horizontal period. Its irregular regulator character must be retained before applying algebraic coefficient rigidity.

For algebraic sections on a finite integral shape cover, apply the argument to a fixed nonzero norm or a polynomial relation on the selected irreducible component. Clearing actual poles must precede specialization. A statement about a fixed algebraic section is much stronger than algebraicity at each separate N.

3. An original-source obstruction to a regular central-fibre comparison

Take the ORIGINAL figure-eight knot. The independently proved whole-source identity gives

lim_(N->infinity) N^-1 Log|F_N(1)|
                          =Vol(4_1)/(2pi)>0. (3)

For every fixed j>=1, the SAME family and SAME original cap give

F_N(Q_N^j)=C_j(q_N)->1.                       (4)

No normalization, operator or source was changed between (3) and (4). Both arcs approach (Q,Y,U)=(1,1,1), and U=1 on both. More literally, the actual pivot has g_k(M)=M^(1-N)g_k(1). On these unit-meridian arcs its change is a specified common unit for every closed head. Moving that unchanged factor into its retained external line leaves the same numerical cut functional. Thus this growth contrast does not hide an independently chosen co-source.

Suppose a proposed whole geometric comparison on the raw central fibre had the form

F_N(Y)=a_N(Y) G_N(Y)+R_N(Y),                  (5)

where a is a nonzero section of the fixed algebraic type in Section 2. Suppose on every fixed-color arc (1) its geometric period and remainder satisfied, for a common V>0,

N^-1 Log|G_N(Q_N^j)|->V,
|R_N(Q_N^j)|<=exp(N(V-delta_j)), delta_j>0.   (6)

Then (5) is impossible. Choose a fixed j larger than the presentation bound of Section 2. Its a_N(Q_N^j) is nonzero and polynomially bounded above and below. Equations (5)–(6), by the reverse triangle inequality, give positive exponential growth on that arc, contradicting (4).

Equivalently, if bounded ordinary-color values force the coefficient to vanish on EVERY fixed-color arc, the exponential separation proof in Section 2 forces its algebraic restriction to the raw central fibre to vanish identically. It cannot then give the nonzero complete value by ordinary regular evaluation.

This excludes a tempting shortcut: a uniform dominant geometric block with a regular algebraic scalar coefficient on that fibre. It does NOT exclude a source-preserving normal extension, a nonregular meridian lattice, or cancellation between blocks with the same exponential size. The latter possibilities are precisely data which raw algebraic point rigidity does not fix.

The original divided source explains the necessary distinction

The exact actual source is already known to use the normal extension. With U=Y^N and t_mu=U-1, set

Y_r(t_mu)=Q_N^r(1+t_mu)^(1/N),
v_N(t_mu)_r=(Y_r(t_mu)-1)F_N(Y_r(t_mu)).

Then the unconditional original state identity is

K_N=N e_0^*[t_mu^1]v_N,

whereas the central state v_N(0) contains only the polynomially bounded ordinary-color row. The source has degree minus one; it is not ordinary evaluation of the lowest state degree. See the source-adapted filtration.

The elementary expression

(U-1)/(Y-1), U=Y^N,

likewise vanishes at every proper root-orbit point and has complete whole-germ value N. It is not a regular function on the raw two-variable fibre U=1 at Y=1. This illustrates the required divided lattice; it does not define a replacement invariant or prove its regulator coefficient is nonzero.

Thus the actual source-anchored example supports the user’s normal-torsor viewpoint, while ruling out its regular scalar version on the unextended central fibre.

4. The explicit Kummer period has growing ambient rank

For an odd prime p the actual one-variable cyclic cover is

C_p: y^p=product_(j=1)^(p-1)(1-Q_p^j t)^j.

It is connected. Its p-1 finite branch points have ramification index p, since each j is coprime to p. Infinity is unramified: the polynomial degree p(p-1)/2 is divisible by p. Counting the Euler characteristic of the punctured p-sheeted cover and filling its ramified points gives

2-2g=p*2-(p-1)*(p-1),
g=(p-1)(p-3)/2.                               (7)

The growth persists in the particular nontrivial deck character used by y^-1 dt. Remove the finite branch points from the base sphere. It retracts to a graph with one vertex and p-2 loops. In any nontrivial deck-character component the covering graph chain groups have dimensions 1 and p-2, and its boundary map has rank one: a branch generator acts by a nontrivial p-th root. Hence that character’s H_1 has dimension p-3. Filling the ramification points changes only the trivial character, because each such point is deck-fixed. Therefore

dim H_1(C_p)_chi=p-3                           (8)

for every nontrivial character chi, including the selected one.

The small-circle source has an additional important feature. Removing the p preimages of t=0 adds one boundary-circle dimension to each nontrivial character. The original selected circle projects into that canonical one-dimensional boundary subspace. Thus growing ambient rank DOES NOT rule out a smaller selected period object. It does show that fixed variable count alone has not constructed such an object uniformly in p.

In particular the compact C_p cannot all be the smooth proper fibres of one fixed finite-type algebraic curve family on finitely many strata: their genus is unbounded. A fixed selected torsor must be a genuinely constructed reduction or comparison, not an automatic consequence of the displayed Kummer equations.

5. What a successful rigidity certificate would now have to contain

The existing whole-comparison rigidity theorem can promote bounded algebraic quantum-jet identities to an exact whole comparison. The existing critical-source theorem can then force every geometric coefficient nonzero from an irreducible descended critical line and a nonzero exact source moment, or from a faithful trace realization. Fixed algebraic coefficients automatically have polynomial norms on the root arc.

Those theorems would remove a separately assumed nonzero geometric weight. Their inputs are substantive and finite:

  • a fixed algebraic comparison space or an explicitly bounded selected quotient of the actual Kummer/quantum object;
  • the source-normal pairing, its meridian pole lattice and its quantum-normal connection retained by that comparison;
  • an actual descended critical line/observable with its exact source moments, rather than only a classical shape field;
  • control of the TOTAL compact remainder after that projection.

The first three have not been supplied for the selected knot period. The new exact cut construction supplies the original cycle, and its infinity bound removes a real global escape obstacle. It does not make their fixed algebraic reduction or critical source moment automatic. Equations (3)–(4) show why that reduction must retain the divided source before passing to a central or associated-graded fibre.
