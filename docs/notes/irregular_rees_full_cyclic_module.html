An irregular Rees bridge from the actual Verma source to full cyclic colors

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. The genuine balanced infinite module admits an explicit irregular and ramified transformation whose operator coefficients extend regularly to the root. Its limit is a full cyclic root color, with both E and F central powers generically nonzero. After one root scaling it covers a dense chart of the fixed-Casimir root conjugacy class.

The transformed source is specified as well. Its co-source vacuum is unchanged by the exponential gauge, but its source vacuum is irregular and its head lattice is ramified. Thus regularity of the module limit does not prove regularity or survival of the original selected period.

1. Exact block operators of the genuine common module

Use the actual balanced infinite module from the two-wall construction:

K v_a=-(M/q)Q^(-a)v_a,
Ecal v_a=(M Q^(-a)-M^(-1))v_(a-1), a>=1,
Ecal v_0=0,
F v_a=(1-Q^(a+1))v_(a+1), Q=q^2.

Write a=rN+j, 0<=j<N, and identify

v_(rN+j)=h_j u^r/r!, B=partial_u, T=u,
L=T B, Z=Q^N,
D_Z f(u)=f(Zu).

These formulas are exact before specializing Q. The clock is Q^j D_Z, and K is -(M/q)Q(-j)D_(Z-1). For Ecal, the nonwrap and wrap entries, respectively, are

E_j=M Q^(-j)D_(Z^-1)-M^(-1), 1<=j<N,
E_0=B(M D_(Z^-1)-M^(-1)).                    (1.1)

The first maps h_j to h_(j-1), the second h_0 to h_(N-1). For F the entries are

F_j=1-Q^(j+1)D_Z, 0<=j<N-1,
F_(N-1)=T (1-Z^(L+1))/(L+1)
        =u integral_Z^1 f(xu)dx on f.         (1.2)

For example (1.2) on u^r/r! has the required coefficient 1-Z^(r+1) into the next block. The apparent inverse L+1 is just division by the nonzero integer r+1 on that basis.

2. The exact irregular transformation and head gauge

Put epsilon=Z-1, d=N epsilon, and choose the marked local ramification eta^N=d. Define

G=exp(u/d), D_head h_j=eta^j h_j,
C=D_head G^(-1).

The exponential belongs to the u-adic completion with Laurent eta coefficients. Each u coefficient has a finite pole; there is no claim of a single uniform pole bound for the whole function. Conjugate first by G^(-1)(-)G, then by D_head(-)D_head^(-1), and apply the genuine Hopf scaling Ecal->eta Ecal,F->eta^(-1)F:

Ehat=eta C Ecal C^(-1),
Fhat=eta^(-1) C F C^(-1), Khat=C K C^(-1).   (2.1)

The direction of the head conjugation is essential. It cancels eta on every nonwrap E entry and contributes d on its wrap; it cancels eta^(-1) on every nonwrap F entry and contributes d^(-1) on its wrap.

There is no unresolved irregular expansion in the transformed operators. Since

G^(-1)D_ZG=exp(u/N)D_Z,
G^(-1)D_(Z^-1)G=exp(-u/(NZ))D_(Z^-1),
G^(-1)B G=B+1/d,

the exact entries become

Ehat_j=M Q^(-j)exp(-u/(NZ))D_(Z^-1)-M^(-1), j>0,
Ehat_0=(1+d partial_u)
               [M exp(-u/(NZ))D_(Z^-1)-M^(-1)],
Fhat_j=1-Q^(j+1)exp(u/N)D_Z, j<N-1,
Fhat_(N-1) f
   =-(u/N) integral_0^1 exp(tu/N)f((1+epsilon t)u)dt,
Khat_j=-(M/q)Q^(-j)exp(-u/(NZ))D_(Z^-1).     (2.2)

The F wrap formula follows by x=1+epsilon t in (1.2), retaining the reversed integration endpoints. Each coefficient of (2.2) is a regular formal series in epsilon,u. Integration is polynomial antiderivation coefficientwise, not a convergence assumption.

The generic quantum-group relations hold because (2.1) is conjugation and Hopf scaling of a genuine module. Their regular specializations are therefore exact root identities.

3. The full cyclic root module and its central chart

Fix q=q_N, Q=Q_N and put y=exp(u/N), U=y^N=exp(u), gamma=(-1)^(N+1). Taking the regular value of (2.2) gives

K_j=-(M/q_N)y^(-1)Q_N^(-j),
Ecal_j=M y^(-1)Q_N^(-j)-M^(-1),
F_j=1-y Q_N^(j+1),                         (3.1)

with j read modulo N, including both wraps. All entries act by multiplication. For F the wrap limit is -(u/N)integral_0^1 exp(tu/N)dt=1-y, including its regular u=0 value. The resulting entries are Laurent algebraic functions of M,y. Thus the module formulas extend from the marked formal y=1 neighborhood to y in G_m. This extends the module chart, not a selected irregular source or its global continuation data.

Direct multiplication, or specialization of the regular relations, gives

[Ecal,F]=rho_N(K-K^(-1)),
F Ecal+q_N K+q_N^(-1)K^(-1)=-M-M^(-1).

The exact central powers are

k=K^N=gamma M^N/U,
e=Ecal^N=gamma(M^N/U-M^(-N)),
f=F^N=1-U.                                  (3.2)

Consequently

e f=k+k^(-1)-gamma(M^N+M^(-N)).              (3.3)

For U!=1 the F arrows are all units. Clock projectors followed by their cyclic F arrows give every matrix unit, so this is a genuine split regular root color, rather than a Borel-only model.

Apply the additional genuine root Hopf scaling Ecal->lambda Ecal,F->lambda^(-1)F. Given an admissible central triple (k_t,e_t,f_t) satisfying (3.3), with all entries nonzero, choose

U=gamma M^N/k_t,
lambda^N=e_t/[gamma(M^N/U-M^(-N))].           (3.4)

The denominator is nonzero because its product with 1-U is e_t f_t. On the indicated N-th-root cover, (3.4) realizes e_t, and (3.3) forces the realized f to be f_t. Thus the family covers a dense full fixed-Casimir central chart. It is not restricted to the triangular source locus F^N=0. It does not itself prove that an actual knot’s selected source reaches a chosen point of that chart.

4. The selected source is irregular and its lattice flags remain

Let the original finite head source be i psi, with psi_j=1, and its co-source phi pi_0, phi_j=1/N. Its finite-rank coupon is Gamma=i e pi_0. Under the actual transformation C the exact source and co-source are

psi_new=D_head exp(-u/d)psi,
phi_new=phi D_head^(-1)pi_0,
phi_new psi_new=1.                          (4.1)

Here pi_0G=pi_0, but G does not preserve the source vacuum. In particular

G^(-1)Gamma G=exp(-u/d)Gamma,

not Gamma. The full transformed coupon has entry

(Gamma_new)_(i,j)
  =eta^(i-j)exp(-u/d)/N times evaluation at u=0. (4.2)

It is exactly idempotent by (4.1). On u jets of order at most h, its eta pole order is bounded by Nh+N-1, equivalently its d pole order by h+(N-1)/N. The head lattice has the explicit flags eta^j h_j, with the opposite dual flags eta(-j)h_j*. The normalized pairing does not make those flags or the essential source exp(-u/d) disappear.

The coefficientwise head projector e tensor I is different: G commutes with it and only the ramified D_head changes it. It cannot be substituted for the original finite Gamma.

The same issue occurs at every internal finite-head compression, not just at the final cut. For the whole-head projector P=i pi_0 (identity on H), one has exactly

C P C^(-1)=exp(-u/d)P.                       (4.3)

On b legs the scalar is exp(-(u_1+…+u_b)/d). The actual off-root finite word is the product with its intermediate head projectors retained. Each becomes this irregular source under the transformation. In particular the pre-gauge two-wall word correction deltawH_beta from the common-Verma note cannot be discarded after taking module Rees symbols: those source poles and normal co-sources can compensate its vanishing factors.

Thus the module has a regular full-cyclic limit while the source is an explicitly recorded irregular section. Equality of finite pairings before degeneration does not provide a regular limiting selected period or a Stokes coefficient.

5. The literal pivot lift is selected, with its U adapter

Conjugation preserves the identity g=K^(1-N). Since Khat has a regular root value, its literal power has that same regular limit:

g_lit=K_U^(1-N)=U g_red,
g_red=x^(1-N)A^(-1), A=yQ_N^j, x=-M/q_N.

Therefore this construction selects the literal whole-clock lift, not the reduced root representative. Their paired defects satisfy D_lit=U^(-1)D_red and both products give the same cut projector; their separate normal derivatives differ. The exact distinction is retained in the Mellin comparison, Section 2. The whole external modified-dimension line d_N(M), Cartan half-character, and zero-framing phases remain marked.

6. The actual cyclotomic-normal Poisson form

There is a fixed-N normal Planck parameter already present in this construction: d=N(Q^N-1). The generic weight relations give

[K^N,Ecal^N]=(Q^(N^2)-1)Ecal^N K^N,
[K^N,F^N]=(Q^(-N^2)-1)F^N K^N.

The two scalar ratios after division by d have root values +1 and -1. Thus the actual divided-commutator Poisson bracket on the root center satisfies

{k,e}=ke, {k,f}=-kf.                         (6.1)

It is independent of the choice of lifts: changing a lift by d times an operator adds a commutator with a central root element. The quantum Casimir is central before specialization. Its root Chebyshev relation (3.3), on a fixed-M leaf, consequently forces

{e,f}=k^(-1)-k.                              (6.2)

This follows first where e!=0 by bracketing (3.3) with e, then extends as the polynomial bracket identity. The Casimir relation is not being declared central merely because it vanishes in one representation; it is the specialization of the actual universal central Casimir.

Vary the actual irregular phase to G_p=exp(pu/d), with p!=0. The Rees momentum is Pi=d partial_u, so [Pi,u]=d and G_p^(-1)Pi G_p=Pi+p. This fixes {p,u}=1. In (2.2) replace u/N in the exponent by pu/N and (1+d partial_u) on the E wrap by (p+d partial_u). The F wrap limit is (1-y)/p, y=exp(pu/N). Other entries remain the corresponding nonwrap entries. Hence its central functions are exactly

U=exp(up),
k=gamma M^N/U,
e=p gamma(M^N/U-M^(-N)),
f=(1-U)/p.                                  (6.3)

Direct differentiation with {p,u}=1 gives (6.1)–(6.2). The exact source symplectic form is

Omega=du wedge dp=dlog U wedge dlog p
      =-dlog k wedge dlog e,                 (6.4)

holding M fixed. The last equality follows from e=p(k-gamma M^(-N)). This is precisely the fixed-meridian Gauss leaf form used in the actual holonomy/moment-map proof, not just an unrelated symplectic parameterization. To match its usual balanced center coordinates set e_G=e/rho_N^N, f_G=-rho_N^N f. Then

G=[k e_G;k f_G k^(-1)+e_G f_G]

has trace gamma(MN+M(-N)); the physical gamma*G has trace MN+M(-N). Constant root scalings do not change (6.4). Varying M requires its peripheral boundary terms and is not part of this fixed-leaf identity.

7. A regular chart at the parabolic source boundary

At M=1 set v=1/p and w=e/gamma. The same central chart is

U=(1+wv)^(-1),
k=gamma(1+wv), e=gamma w,
f=w v^2/(1+wv),
Omega=dw wedge dv/(1+wv).                    (7.1)

These are algebraic identities. The form is regular and nonzero at v=0, including w=0. The locus w=0 maps to the identity central point; its v coordinate retains the exceptional direction. Thus (7.1) is a regular resolving chart at that collapsed point, not a claim of a complete global resolution without its other chart.

The module entries also extend across v=0 on the marked y cover. Here y=(1+wv)^(-1/N), and its E wrap is

[(1+wv)^(1/N)-1]/v -> w/N,

while its F wrap is v[1-(1+wv)^(-1/N)] ->0. The nonwrap entries tend to the actual head values. Consequently the v=0 module is exactly the previously constructed E-semicyclic source with mu=w/N, and w=0 is the actual complete head. This is a concrete source boundary of the full conjugacy-class chart. It does not prove that the selected knot period reaches a chosen geometric point in that chart.

8. What has and has not been bridged

The full cyclic module is obtained from the genuine actual Verma module by an explicit irregular Rees transformation. This supplies a module-level route from the original head lattice to dense root color charts with both central powers nonzero. Its construction does not assume that a generic typical finite head extends to a Lusztig module, nor identify the two regular wall normals.

At U=1 its generic-M value is the E-semicyclic s=1 fiber, with wrap coefficient kappa=M-M^(-1), rather than the nilpotent s=0 head. They coincide at M=1. The previously proved whole E-semicyclic cut independence is relevant, but is a separate word/source comparison and is not a consequence of this module specialization.

No regular limit of the transformed source has been asserted. The generator module limit alone also does not prove that a whole conjugated R network has a regular scalar limit with this selected source. The remaining tasks include its ramified flags, irregular source class, physical phase adapters, and actual geometric/cut comparison. No global regulator period or volume limit follows from the central-chart coverage alone.

The parameter d is a local root normal for fixed N, not the physical large-N parameter H_N=2pi*i/N. If H=Log Q on the marked root sheet, then d=N^2(H-H_N)+higher order. The actual finite auxiliary state is regular at that fixed root. A nonzero polar geometric phase exp(S/d) is therefore not automatically its selected phase: polar pieces in a full source-preserving contraction must be accounted for or canceled. The root regular part can still have N-dependent growth. The exact module and Poisson maps above do not settle that separate norm problem.

The uncompressed universal Verma braid has its own already proved root Alexander-normalized period in the earlier Verma note. It is not identified with the compressed selected period by dropping the projectors (4.3); their full normal adapter remains necessary.
