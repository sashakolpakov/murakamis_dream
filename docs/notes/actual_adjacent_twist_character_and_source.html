Exact adjacent-twist character coupling with its physical source

28 October 2026. Adjacent original twist blocks have an exact coupled quantum-character algebra, not independent spectral phases. The identities below give a denominator-free braid recursion, fix the nearest-neighbor spectral phase ratios, and impose a strict integer spectral window on the original generic-meridian family. An actual cup port restricts the total Casimir without changing the marked co-source. These are reusable local identities at arbitrary width.

They do not estimate the original whole-source q-Racah contour or prove a sharp volume upper bound or noncancellation. The exact phase coupling is a step beyond estimating separate repeated-crossing norms.

1. Physical trace coordinates

Use the actual balanced generators K_i,F_i,Ecal_i=rho^2E_i on three physical heads, with q=exp(pi i/N), rho=q-q^(-1), delta=q+q^(-1). Let e_i=q Ecal_i and form the literal matrices

L_(+,i)=[[K_i,e_i],[0,1]],
L_(-,i)=[[1,0],[-K_iF_i,K_i]],
G_i=L_(-,i)^(-1)L_(+,i).

Keep the actual prefix-based meridians H_i from the shared quantum flags: H_1=G_1 and H_i=P_(i-1)^(-1)G_i P_(i-1), where P_(i-1)=L_(-,i-1)…L_(-,1). Define tr_q(T)=qT_11+q^(-1)T_22 and

A=tr_q(H_2H_1)=Omega_12,
B=tr_q(H_3H_2)=Omega_23,
C=tr_q(H_3H_1),
D=tr_q(H_3H_2H_1)=Omega_123.                       (1)

The two displayed intermediate Casimirs are the actual coproduct Casimirs of the original physical heads. The prefix conjugation in B drops out of tr_q with its required q-ordering, not by ordinary trace cyclicity. Each head has central Casimir a_i; for common original meridian M, a_i=-(M+M^(-1)); at completeness a_i=-2.

Set central coefficients

alpha=a_1a_2+a_3D,
beta=a_2a_3+a_1D,
gamma=a_1a_3+a_2D.                                (2)

The full original common Cartan cancels in conjugation statements, but remains on the actual crossing operators and their powers.

2. Exact quantum Fricke identities

The following are finite polynomial identities, including N=2:

q^(-1)AB-qBA=rho(delta C-gamma),
q^(-1)BC-qCB=rho(delta A-alpha),
q^(-1)CA-qAC=rho(delta B-beta),                    (3)
[D,A]=[D,B]=[D,C]=0.

They retain the coupled cubic

q^(-1)ABC+q^(-2)A^2+q^2B^2+q^(-2)C^2
   -q^(-1)A alpha-qB beta-q^(-1)C gamma
 =delta^2-a_1^2-a_2^2-a_3^2-D^2-a_1a_2a_3D.       (4)

No division by delta, rho, a clock determinant, or a nilpotent face is needed to state (3)–(4).

Here is a reproducible algebraic proof, separate from finite-level numerical checks. Take independent quantum tori Z_i S_i=q^2S_i Z_i, and independent formal meridians M_i. Substitute

K_i=-M_i q^(-1)Z_i^(-1),
F_i=S_i(1-q^2 Z_i),
Ecal_i=S_i^(-1)(M_i Z_i^(-1)-M_i^(-1)).            (5)

Their single-head Casimir is -(M_i+M_i^(-1)). Expand (1) using the two-by-two triangular inverse matrices, then put every product into the order S_1^u Z_1^v S_2^w Z_2^x S_3^y Z_3^z. Multiplication supplies only the scalar q^(2sum v_i u_i’). The accompanying certificate uses integer Laurent coefficients in q,M_1,M_2,M_3 and verifies that every coefficient of (3)–(4) is zero. A,B,C,D have respectively 14,14,62,34 Laurent terms before those identities are expanded.

This is an exact generic-q polynomial certificate, not a sample at roots or an imported q-Racah theorem. It applies to the original finite nilpotent heads too. Indeed the single-head algebra with fixed Casimir has normal forms KaFb and KaEcalc; the mixed product FEcal is its fixed nonzero Laurent polynomial in K. The torus realization (5) is faithful on those normal forms: distinct F/Ecal degrees have distinct S powers, and the remaining nonzero Laurent factor cannot kill a Laurent polynomial in Z. Thus the certified identities hold in that central quantum-group quotient and then in its finite-head representations. This argument does not add the generic cyclic E endpoint to the original module.

3. Actual braid action and the retained twist phases

The already directly verified original Hurwitz transport acts on H_1,H_2,H_3. Taking its ordered traces gives the exact identities, for equal colors (a_1=a_2=a_3=a, alpha=beta=gamma=a^2+aD),

Ad(R_12):(A,B,C) -> (A,q alpha-qBA-q^2C,B),
Ad(R_23):(A,B,C) -> (C,B,q alpha-qCB-q^2A).          (6)

The trace reductions in (6) are also included in the exact Laurent certificate, using H_i(-1)=q(-1)a_i I-q^(-2)H_i. Thus (6) does not infer a trace identity from a classical mnemonic. For distinct colors, the same proof retains the permuted a_i and the appropriate alpha, beta,gamma in (2); the present common-color formulas suffice for the original knot family. Literal inverse crossings give the inverse polynomial maps; no new scalar is supplied.

Put B_t=R_12^t B R_12^(-t). The complete adjacent-block transport is

B_(t+1)=-q B_t A-q^2 B_(t-1)+q alpha,
B_0=B, B_(-1)=C.                                  (7)

This formula holds at every signed integer t and every N, with the whole physical boundary and original source unchanged. It retains the q-phase; replacing it by a real modulus recurrence is not valid.

On the genuine generic-meridian family the first pair has distinct Casimir nodes and original eigenvalues

c_j(Y)=q^(-1)YQ^(-j)+qY^(-1)Q^j,
lambda_j(Y)=nu_jY^(-j),
nu_j=(-1)^j q^(j(j+1)), 0<=j<N.                    (8)

The normalized physical fusion/projector formulas in the factorial-frame theorem realize these operators with their actual normalizers. In particular the two homogeneous multipliers of (7) at A=c_j(Y) are

-Q^(j+1)Y^(-1)=lambda_(j+1)/lambda_j,
-Q^(-j)Y=lambda_(j-1)/lambda_j.                    (9)

These are precisely the neighboring ratios of the ORIGINAL twist phases, not independently selected semiclassical characters.

4. A strict spectral window, rather than a cyclic replacement

Let E_i(Y) denote the meromorphic actual pair-Casimir projectors, extended by the third head. For i!=j, project (7) between E_i and E_j. The central forcing disappears, and its scalar characteristic equation is

(lambda_i/lambda_j)^2
    +q c_j(Y)(lambda_i/lambda_j)+q^2=0.

By (9), equality as a meromorphic function of Y forces the exponent of Y in lambda_i/lambda_j to be +1 or -1. Therefore

E_i(Y) B E_j(Y)=0 unless i=j or i=j+1 or i=j-1     (10)

as INTEGER indices in [0,N-1]. For N>=3 the apparent cyclic neighbor (0,N-1) is excluded. Casimir nodes alone are cyclic, but the original braiding eigenvalues (8) have a nontrivial endpoint norm character; discarding it would incorrectly allow that extra edge.

The diagonal is fixed as well:

(delta+c_j(Y)) E_j B E_j=E_j alpha E_j.             (11)

This follows from Ad(R_12)C=B and the diagonal part of (6). It is stated without dividing at a zero of the factor on the left. Equations (10)–(11) are the actual finite three-term spectral coupling underlying a q-Racah overlap. They remain identities of whole meromorphic germs at completeness; the colliding projectors cannot be independently evaluated or replaced by a semisimple root basis.

For an adjacent sequence R_12^ell R_23^m, its exact kernel is obtained by composing the actual C_T E_i C_T^(-1) projector matrices with their lambda_i^ell and lambda_j^m factors, then applying the original co-source. Formula (10) supplies the coupling recurrence rather than treating those spectral labels as independent or modulo N. It does not yet evaluate the resulting multidimensional contour.

5. The original cup port gives the parabolic total character

At completeness let p be the ORIGINAL cup on heads 1,2. Its actual Hopf identities E_tot p=F_tot p=0 and K_tot p=p imply that both two-head coproduct triangular matrices are identity on p. Consequently

(D+2I)(p tensor v)=0 for EVERY third-head vector v. (12)

Every actual word in R_12 and R_23 commutes with D, so (12) remains true on the entire transported cup-generated subspace. Spectator heads may be included. An original ordinary cap and full marked row can be left untouched: inserting D+2 into that original whole network gives zero by (12), with no change of co-source or division by a knot period. This is a forward source restriction, not an assertion that the unrestricted marked row is an invariant covector.

One may NOT impose D=-2 on an arbitrary three-head port which has already interacted with outside heads. At such a port the full D in (2)–(7) must be retained and sewn to its actual exterior source.

On a commutative principal chart, (4) at a_i=-2 becomes the usual four-punctured character cubic. On the actual cup slice D=-2 it is

A^2+B^2+C^2+ABC-8(A+B+C)+28=0,
(X+Y+Z)^2+XYZ=0, X=A-2,Y=B-2,Z=C-2.               (13)

This identifies the coupled trace support and its original parabolic source. It does not establish that the original spectral cycle has a nonzero intersection with its geometric place, or bound every complex boundary phase by knot volume.

7. A specific obstruction to leaving the spectral contour real

At completeness the original spectral nodes are

c_j(1)=2cos((2j+1)pi/N),

and their real-angle limit lies in [-2,2]. On a regular physical classical chart, A is the actual trace of the product of its two based parabolic meridians, by (1). A nontrivial element in a discrete faithful hyperbolic manifold group cannot have real trace strictly between -2 and 2: such a matrix is elliptic, whereas the manifold’s deck group acts freely and discretely on hyperbolic space. Thus a regular discrete-faithful pair-product trace cannot occur in the interior of this real spectral interval. A loxodromic pair product requires either a nonreal trace or a real trace outside that interval. Parabolic/central pair products belong to the turning values +/-2 and need their own retained boundary analysis.

Consequently a proposed real-spectral-angle stationary proof cannot simply identify its real optimizer with a regular geometric pair whose product is loxodromic. The q-Racah spectral variable must undergo a complex deformation, or one must perform another actual polarization change with its source. This criterion is local and applies at any width; it is not a knot census or a disproof of a complex-contour route. Real eigenvalues of a nonnormal finite Casimir do not exclude complex principal characters in the original physical polarization. The claim concerns leaving THIS diagonal spectral contour real.

This is why the coupled trace identities above still do not turn the local block bound into a sharp volume estimate: the actual complex spectral contour and its endpoint/normal source currents remain to be transported and bounded. They have not been selected by the algebraic cubic alone.
