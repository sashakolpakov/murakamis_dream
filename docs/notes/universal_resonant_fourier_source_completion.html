Exact complete localization of the original first-slot Fourier source

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. At an actual root, the first-slot cut in the universal resonant presentation has the exact original finite Fourier array

J_(a,j)(1)=P_N(1) delta_(j,0)                     (1)

for EVERY physical first input a. This includes the original all-ones output row, the original pivotal caps, twists and full peripheral dimension line. No division by P_N and no nonvanishing assertion are used. The result is a consequence of the actual Hopf rows, rather than an approximation by the geometric principal density. In fact the entire channel obeys L(X_j)=P_N X_j at completeness, as proved below; this is stronger than the selected scalar rows.

Consequently the regular Fourier labels j!=0 have zero selected finite value at completeness. A nonzero local geometric formal block at those labels does not establish that it contributes to the original period. The resonant value P_N remains to be evaluated. In particular, (1) supplies neither its volume exponent nor a new scalar formula independent of the original word.

1. The actual channel and its source

Fix N>=2, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, and retain the original meridian M. In the balanced head put

B=diag(Q^a), K=x B^-1, x=-M/q,
F_(a+1,a)=1-Q^(a+1),
Ecal_(a-1,a)=M Q^(-a)-M^-1.                       (2)

All missing entries are zero. Ecal is the fixed scalar multiple of E used in the actual balanced crossing conventions. Its coproduct and antipode have the same form as those of E.

Cut a braid at its FIRST physical slot between two actual words A and Bword; both words intertwine the true iterated coproduct. The original second-cut coupon X defines the actual linear channel

L(X)=d_N theta^(-w) ptr_g[ A (X tensor I) Bword ]. (3)

Here ptr_g closes every strand except the original first open strand using g=K^(1-N), in the original order. The displayed external factors may instead be recorded in their original external line; the SAME convention must then be used for P_N and J throughout. The universal presentation inserts a word P and its literal inverse, so A Bword is exactly the original braid operator. It changes none of the factors in (3), including their actual rational-map/lattice specializations.

The root scalar-closure proof, Section 1, gives

L(I)=P_N(M) I.                                   (4)

There is an elementary version of that proof: the cut commutes with K and F; the distinct K eigenvalues make it diagonal, and its F commutator makes successive diagonal entries equal because 1-Q^a!=0 for 0<a<N. Thus no generic irreducibility or division by an unknown scalar period is needed. With the full line in (3), P_N=d_N F_N; with that line recorded externally, P_N=F_N. At M=1, F_N(1) is the fixed original Kashaev invariant.

Set ell=1^t, and use the actual root Fourier projectors

X_j=V^-1 E_(j,j) V,
(X_j)_(b,d)=Q^[j(d-b)]/N,
V_(j,b)=Q^(jb), 0<=j<N.

The original one-index Fourier source is EXACTLY

Y_j=L(X_j),  J_(a,j)=ell Y_j e_a.                 (5)

This follows by expanding the second-wire conjugation and retaining the all-ones first output row. Since sum_j X_j=I, (4) gives

sum_j J_(a,j)=P_N                                (6)

for every a. The 1/N in X_j is the Fourier inverse. There is no additional exterior factor in (6).

2. Why the first-slot channel intertwines the actual Hopf adjoint

For the actual coproduct convention,

Delta Ecal=Ecal tensor K+1 tensor Ecal,
Delta F=F tensor 1+K^-1 tensor F,
S(Ecal)=-Ecal K^-1, S(F)=-K F.

The induced action on an endomorphism is

ad_K X=K X K^-1,
ad_Ecal X=(Ecal X-X Ecal)K^-1,
ad_F X=F X-K^-1 X K F.                           (7)

For the FIRST tensor factor, direct substitution in the coproduct gives

ad_h^(total)(X tensor I)=(ad_h X) tensor I,
                     h=K,Ecal,F.                (8)

For example the Ecal commutator is [Ecal,X] tensor K_other, which becomes [Ecal,X]K^-1 tensor I after multiplying by the total K^-1. For F the extra term cancels as

K^-1 X tensor F_other
  -K^-1 X K K^-1 tensor F_other=0.

This verifies the position requirement. An arbitrary later-slot embedding is not substituted for (8).

Multiplication on either side by an actual braid intertwiner commutes with (7). The original pivotal partial trace is also an adjoint intertwiner. One can check the latter directly, without assuming scalarity: on the closed tensor factor write K_o,E_o,F_o for its total generators and g_o for its original product pivot. At the actual root,

[g_o,K_o]=0,
g_o E_o g_o^-1=K_o E_o K_o^-1,
g_o F_o g_o^-1=K_o F_o K_o^-1.

Weighted partial-trace cyclicity cancels the closed-factor terms in (7), using precisely these identities. The remaining terms are ad_h of the partial trace. Equivalently these are the direct evaluation/coevaluation identities checked in the closed-braid construction, Section 2. Scalars d_N and theta^(-w) commute with this calculation. Consequently

L(ad_h X)=ad_h L(X), h=K,Ecal,F.                  (9)

Equations (8)–(9) apply to the literal cut of any word of the specified equal original color, not only to an inverse-pair example. The inverse-pair construction is what simultaneously provides this first slot and the desired noncommuting geometric meridian while preserving the original selected period.

Everything so far is an ACTUAL root identity. It makes no off-root q scalarity or compressed auxiliary Hopf assertion.

3. Complete cyclic generator formulas

Let S be the cyclic raising shift S e_a=e_(a+1 mod N). The Fourier projectors satisfy

S X_j=X_j S=eta_j X_j, eta_j=Q^j,
B^-1 X_j B=X_(j+1).                              (10)

All Fourier labels in this note are cyclic. At M=1 the missing physical endpoints in (2) have zero coefficients. Therefore, as exact finite matrices,

F=S(I-QB), Ecal=S^-1(B^-1-I).                    (11)

There is no replacement of a nonzero endpoint by a cyclic arrow. Substituting (10)–(11) into (7) gives

ad_K X_j=X_(j+1),
ad_Ecal X_j=x^-1 eta_j^-1
                     (Q^-1 X_(j+1)-X_j),
ad_F X_j=eta_j X_j-eta_(j-1) X_(j-1).           (12)

For instance [S-1(B-1-I),X_j]B has its two X_j B terms cancel; the remaining terms are eta_j-1(Q-1 X_(j+1)-X_j). For the F row, the two S B terms cancel by B X_j=X_(j-1)B. These prove all formulas in (12) within the original finite balanced head.

The first row of (12) and (9) imply

Y_(j+1)=B^-1 Y_j B.                              (13)

In particular their diagonal entries are independent of j. Summing (13) and using (4) proves (Y_j)_(a,a)=P_N/N. This latter identity also holds for generic M and will retain the full scalar period in the endpoint equation below.

4. Highest-input localization, followed by all-input propagation

First take the actual input a=0. Since Ecal e_0=0 and ell Ecal=ell(B^-1-I) at M=1, (13) gives

ell (ad_Ecal Y_j)e_0
              =x^-1[J_(0,j+1)-J_(0,j)].

Apply (9) to the Ecal row of (12) and compare the two expressions. The resulting exact scalar recurrence is

(eta_j-Q^-1)J_(0,j+1)
                   =(eta_j-1)J_(0,j).           (14)

At j=0 its right side is zero and 1-Q^-1 is a unit, so J_(0,1)=0. For j=1,…,N-2 the coefficient eta_j-Q^-1 is nonzero, and induction gives J_(0,j)=0 for every j!=0. Equation (6) then gives J_(0,0)=P_N. This proof never divides by P_N and remains valid when the original invariant vanishes.

For every physical input a, use (13), the F row of (12), and F e_a=(1-Q^(a+1))e_(a+1). They give

(1-eta_j)J_(a,j)
  =(Q^(a+1)-Q^-1 eta_j)J_(a,j-1)
              +(1-Q^(a+1))J_(a+1,j-1).          (15)

At a=N-1 the last term is zero; it is not an added cyclic physical-input column. The candidate P_N delta_(j,0), constant in a, satisfies (15). Subtract it and call the difference D. The result just proved is D_(0,j)=0 for all j. If D_(a,j)=0 for all j and a<N-1, (15) forces

(1-Q^(a+1))D_(a+1,j-1)=0.

Every coefficient is a unit. Induction in a proves D=0, which is the stated theorem (1). The proof also works at M=-1, since then Ecal=-S-1(B-1-I); its extra common sign cancels in (14).

There is a useful full-matrix corollary. Define the Fourier rows r_k=ell B^-k, which form a basis of the dual head. By (13),

r_k Y_j=ell Y_(j+k) B^-k
       =P_N delta_(j+k,0) r_k.

Directly from (5), r_k X_j=delta_(j+k,0)r_k as well. Hence

L(X_j)=P_N X_j,                                  (19)
L(h(S))=P_N h(S)

for EVERY element h(S) of the finite cyclic-shift algebra. This identity also uses no nonzero-period assumption. It concerns this commutative subalgebra of coupons, not the full endomorphism algebra or the generic-M channel.

5. The meridian endpoint and word data are retained off completeness

Keep the actual root Q but allow M to vary. Put

kappa=M-M^-1, W=E_(N-1,0).

The exact finite Ecal matrix is

Ecal=S^-1(MB^-1-M^-1 I)-kappa W.                 (16)

The last term subtracts the cyclic arrow at the original physical endpoint. It is nonzero off completeness. The F row, K row, (13), (15) and (Y_j)_(0,0)=P_N/N remain exact. Define the ACTUAL inserted endpoint source

R_j=ell L([W,X_j]B)e_0.                          (17)

Repeating the preceding Ecal calculation with (16) gives

M(eta_j-Q^-1)J_(0,j+1)
  +(M-M^-1 eta_j)J_(0,j)
             =kappa eta_j(P_N/N-R_j).           (18)

Indeed the cyclic part of ad_Ecal X_j is x^-1 M eta_j-1(Q-1 X_(j+1)-X_j), while its missing-arrow correction is -x^-1 kappa[W,X_j]B. On the output side, ell Ecal=ell(MB-1-M-1 I)-kappa e_0^t. This proves (18), including both its original scalar source and its actual inserted endpoint source. Deleting (17) before differentiation would alter the original meridian normal equation.

There is no universal generic-M profile determined by P_N alone for all original words. The exact finite-field audit includes the same first-slot inverse-pair cut on the one-positive-crossing unknot and positive trefoil. At N=3, M=3 over F_157, their highest rows divided by their respective NONZERO full periods are

unknot:  [117,62,136],
trefoil: [52,122,141].

Both sums are one modulo 157. The different rows disprove such a universal rational identity across these original words; this is not a separate claim about all hyperbolic knots or about a particular complex asymptotic boundary profile. It records why the whole word/source in (17)–(18) cannot be replaced by a knot-independent cap simply because (1) holds on the complete fiber.

6. Critical-path consequence and verification

On this actual source-preserving cut ALL of the selected complete finite period is at j=0. Thus the nonzero geometric normal block on a regular p!=1 chart is not yet a nonzero image of the final selected scalar source: the complete finite rows at j!=0 vanish exactly. Sampling their nonzero geometric principal coefficient and summing it is not an evaluation of the actual finite array.

The remaining issue is a source-compatible geometric transform of the resonant row and its whole endpoint/peripheral completion, not proving that an unspecified regular Fourier row survives. Its regulator exponent, subexponential coefficient and global norm estimate are still unproved in general. The current theorem does not close C, D or E of the critical-path audit.

The exact original-matrix audit checks every matrix unit against all three Hopf-adjoint generators, the generic-M endpoint equation (18), all-input equation (15), complete delta localization and full matrix projector identity (19). Its saved results record 36 signed-word/cut cases at N=2,…,6, comprising 598 matrix input units and three generator checks per unit. The full d_N, original pivots and twists are retained in every case. These finite tests support the direct proof above; they are not used as a proof for untested knots or as an asymptotic estimate.
