Every certified crossing-circle rectangle has ratio at most one

30 October 2026. For every crossing circle in an ACTUAL complete right-angled matching-polyhedron certificate, the disk-framed rectangle ratio satisfies

0<c<=1.                                        (1)

Thus its actual recoupled target trace is at most -2. This holds for both retained half-twist signs, not just the pure or Conway subclass. The equality case remains a nontrivial parabolic target; no strict inequality or operator asymptotic is inferred.

1. The geometric and marked input

Assume the labeled actual cut, complete cusp metric and primitive crossing-circle paths of the general matching-polyhedron certificate. Its actual crossing-circle rectangle has vertices (1,E,-1,-E), with E=exp(2pi i x), 0<x<1/2, and c=cot(pi x)>0. An arbitrary abstract ideal rectangle is not asserted to satisfy (1). The conclusion concerns one realized in this complete manifold with these actual disk and meridian identifications.

The original based pair and its transport may be normalized as

A=-[[1,1],[0,1]], B=-[[1,0],[-4,1]],
P=BA, H=P+I, H^2=0,
C=I+bH, B_out=C B C^-1.

The actual primitive collar and quantum half-twist calibration give

tau_epsilon=2ic/(1-epsilon ic),
b-epsilon/2=1/tau_epsilon,
b=-i/(2c), epsilon in {0,+1,-1}.                (2)

The literal inverse-cup bend fixes the target word

W=B^-1 B_out,
Tr W=2+16b^2=2-4/c^2,                          (3)

as proved in the actual geometric target theorem. The only possible concern for a discreteness argument is whether C itself is a group element when a retained half twist occurs. The next calculation removes that concern without assuming it.

2. The half-transport still gives actual based meridian words

Put S=I+H/2. The original primitive-collar reconstruction in the actual critical scheme, at the complete endpoint J=-1, says PROJECTIVELY

mu=C^-1 S^epsilon.                             (4)

Here mu is the actual based auxiliary meridian. Indeed its generic factor (P-1-I)epsilon specializes using

P^-1-I=-2S,

and the recorded scalar h_J(-epsilon)=(-2)(-epsilon) cancels that constant. The remaining lift scalar is central and has no effect on the conjugation below. Thus (4) retains the primitive meridian; it does not replace it by a half-longitude.

Direct multiplication in the original pair gives

S B S^-1=A, S^-1 B S=B A B^-1.                 (5)

Since C=mu^-1 S^epsilon projectively, the three physical outputs are

epsilon=0:  B_out=mu^-1 B mu,
epsilon=+1: B_out=mu^-1 A mu,
epsilon=-1: B_out=mu^-1 B A B^-1 mu.            (6)

Every expression in (6) is an ACTUAL based holonomy word in the same complete manifold. The meridians A,B,mu have the common basepaths fixed by the original threading word. In particular W=B^-1 B_out is a group element for every retained parity. The matrix S need not itself be a group element; none of (6) assumes that it is. Changes of lift sign cannot affect the ensuing absolute trace classification.

3. The complete metric excludes the forbidden trace

The certified metric is complete and its universal developing cover is hyperbolic three-space, as in the complete gluing theorem. Its holonomy acts by deck transformations and hence freely. A nonidentity element of this group cannot fix an interior point.

If c>1, equation (3) gives -2<Tr W<2. Its determinant-one matrix therefore has distinct eigenvalues exp(+i theta), exp(-i theta), with 0<theta<pi. After conjugacy its Möbius action is a nontrivial rotation about the geodesic joining its two ideal fixed points. It fixes every interior point on that geodesic. This contradicts the free deck action. Consequently c<=1, proving (1).

This is an elementary group consequence of the ACTUAL primitive word (6). No unproved packing inequality or exterior cusp-shape estimate is used. It also explains why the same assertion could not be made about an abstract rectangle without the marked quotient.

At c=1 the target is genuinely parabolic, not the identity. In the normalization above its matrix is

W=[[8b^2-4b+1,4b^2],[16b^2,8b^2+4b+1]],

and at b=-i/2, W+I=[[2i,-1],[-4,-2i]] is nonzero and squares to zero. Nothing here rules out that parabolic word in the manifold. A strict bound would require an additional topological or geometric argument about that word, which is not supplied by this theorem.

4. Consequence and scope

Every actual complete target in this certified class is therefore

c<1: Tr W<-2, a real off-spectrum target;
c=1: Tr W=-2, the parabolic spectral endpoint.

The formal c>1 branch of the local algebraic four-parabolic relation still exists, but cannot be the complete crossing-circle geometry with the marked certificate used here. This removes the in-spectrum case for these actual geometric targets. It does not show that a given common frame places the target’s physical rows in the strict cross-central domain, identify a surrounding continuant state, or prove contour contribution or equality of source and volume.
