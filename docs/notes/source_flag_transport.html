The affine origin is an auxiliary source flag

Research note, 26 October 2026. The selected vacuum in the existing cyclic endpoint module depends on an affine origin. Keeping that origin as an extra point s in P^1 gives an intrinsic parameterization of the same modules. It gives exact unit-preserving projective transport when s moves with the geometric flags, and an explicit flat adapter when s changes. For two finite source flags the adapter can be normalized by the actual edge caps so that pointed fusion, face pairings and cyclic maps all commute exactly.

The normal chart is also explicit: its extra source point is sigma=(s-p_*)/epsilon. The first normal coefficient of an adapter is usually a non-scalar matrix. This realizes a concrete affine change of normal lifts, with a corresponding change of the selected cut row.

These are statements about the already defined quantum modules and their selected data, not a replacement invariant or a proof of the volume conjecture. The global ADO/geometric source identification and the marked regulator norm remain unproved.

1. The source flag and the existing triangle module

Use N>=2, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2 and the shared difference roots B_ij from the shared-face proof. For distinct flags p_i and a finite s avoiding them, choose

A_i(s)^N=p_i-s.

Only ratios of the A_i occur in the relative triangle module, so a common rescaling of all A_i has no effect. On f=(i,j,k), use

D_f^N=lambda_f=(p_i-p_k)/(p_j-p_k),
Y_f=(v_f-u_f D_f)sigma_Q^(-1),
u_f=A_j(s)/A_k(s), v_f=A_i(s)/A_k(s).             (1.1)

Here sigma_Q(D)=QD; it is unrelated to the normal source coordinate introduced in Section 6. Direct subtraction gives Y_f^N=1-lambda_f. Thus the full relative matrix algebra is independent of s. Its pointed unit line for Y_f+u_f D_f-v_f varies with s.

The normalized Steinberg parameter is now the four-point function

t_f(s)=(p_j-s)(p_i-p_k)/((p_i-s)(p_j-p_k)),
1-t_f(s)=(p_k-s)(p_j-p_i)/((p_i-s)(p_j-p_k)).
                                                    (1.2)

In particular it is invariant under simultaneous projective change of p_i,p_j,p_k,s. The values s=p_i,p_j,p_k correspond to t=infinity,0,1. The Nth-root cover for its normalized parameters is the existing punctured Fermat curve rhoN+aN=1. No new quantum function has been defined.

At s=infinity take the endpoint ratios A_i/A_j=1. Then (1.1) is the usual Steinberg triangle module and t_f=lambda_f. This chart concerns the relative triangle modules. The original edge modules and their caps can degenerate there; their retained lattice and source terms cannot be obtained by substituting these ratios into a separately singular edge formula.

Define, as before,

beta_f=B_ik/B_jk, nu_f=B_ji/B_jk,
rho_f(s)=(A_j(s)/A_i(s)) beta_f,
a_f(s)=(A_k(s)/A_i(s)) nu_f,
phi_(f,s)(n)=(Q rho_f(s);Q)_n/a_f(s)^n,
G_(f,s)=diag(phi_(f,s)^(-1)) H(beta_f).           (1.3)

It follows that G D G^(-1)=beta_f Z and G Y G^(-1)=nu_f S. The matrices H, clock Z and shift S are exactly those of the preceding note.

2. Moving the source restores pointed projective covariance

Let F(p)=(a p+b)/(c p+d), Delta=ad-bc. Choose compatible roots

E^N=Delta, D_i^N=c p_i+d, D_s^N=c s+d,
B_ij'=E B_ij/(D_i D_j),
A_i'=E A_i/(D_i D_s).                            (2.1)

When F(s)=infinity the ratios in the last formula are read as A_i’/A_j’=(A_i/A_j)(D_j/D_i); its common factor is unnecessary. The same ratio interpretation covers other source charts.

Substitution gives the exact identities

beta_f'=(D_j/D_i)beta_f,
nu_f'=(D_k/D_i)nu_f,
rho_f'(F(s))=rho_f(s), a_f'(F(s))=a_f(s).         (2.2)

Set kappa_f=D_j/D_i, eta_f=D_k/D_i and, in the raw polynomial bases, K_f=diag(kappa_f^(-n)). Then

K_f 1=1, G_(Ff,F(s)) K_f=G_(f,s),
D_f' K_f=K_f kappa_f D_f,
Y_f' K_f=K_f eta_f Y_f.                          (2.3)

For a tetrahedron with faces A=ikl,B=ijk,C=ijl,D=jkl, these rescalings commute with both total and relative algebra embeddings. For example kappa_A=kappa_C kappa_D and eta_A=kappa_C eta_D=eta_C. Consequently

R_(F p,F(s))(K_A tensor K_B)
              =(K_C tensor K_D)R_(p,s).         (2.4)

A proof including the scalar is immediate from the pointed uniqueness theorem: both sides intertwine the same full matrix algebra and send the unit tensor to the unit tensor. Therefore the scalar is one. No determinant root or inverse of two modulo N is needed. This does not assert that arbitrary vertex permutations give equal bare tensors.

Holding the origin fixed while applying F changes the source flag data. The nonconstant Hilbert-90 factor in the earlier projective-frame construction records exactly that distinction.

3. Changing the source: an explicit flat connection

For fixed geometric flags define the source-change adapter

H_f(s',s)=G_(f,s')^(-1)G_(f,s)
   =H(beta_f)^(-1)
      diag(phi_(f,s')/phi_(f,s)) H(beta_f).        (3.1)

It intertwines both raw generators, has value one at the marked spectral root beta_f and satisfies the strict composition law

H_f(s'',s')H_f(s',s)=H_f(s'',s).                 (3.2)

This is the explicit normalized Hilbert-90 map already present in the affine transport proof, now written on the intrinsic source-flag cover. The adapter is usually not unit-preserving. Indeed a scalar image of 1 would make H_f a scalar, since 1 is cyclic for D_f. Intertwining Y would then force both endpoint ratios in (1.1) to coincide; distinct source flags and distinct p_i rule that out.

Equivalently (3.1) is parallel transport for the algebraic flat connection, with p_i fixed,

nabla_f=d+G_(f,s)^(-1)dG_(f,s)
   =d-H(beta_f)^(-1)diag(d log phi_(f,s))H(beta_f).
                                                    (3.3)

This is a logarithmic connection on its root cover. It describes the finite-level source frame, not the dilogarithmic regulator connection or a norm comparison across levels.

4. The full source scalar is a cocycle with an explicit cone

Write K_t(s) for the retained scalar kappa_t(s)phi_(B,s)(e_t) in equation (4.2) of the shared-face proof. The standard shape u_t,v_t and the clock/shift insertion depend on B_ij and edge orientations, and are independent of s. Therefore exactly

R_(s')(H_A tensor H_B)
   =c_t(s',s)(H_C tensor H_D)R_s,
c_t(s',s)=K_t(s')/K_t(s).                         (4.1)

All factors are the actual nonzero finite sums/products of the pointed fusion; no source value has been independently normalized.

Compare the ordered pentagon at s and s’. The standard kernel operators in its two presentations are identical, so its scalar part gives delta c=1 on every nondegenerate five-flag chart. Thus c is a configuration three-cocycle. This proof uses the previously proved algebraic pointed pentagon, not a published state-sum invariance theorem.

Choose a further flag o distinct from those in use, with all root data on the same cover, and put

b_f(s',s;o)=c_(o,i,j,k)(s',s), f=(i,j,k).
delta b_(i,j,k,l)=b_(j,k,l)b_(i,j,l)
                     /(b_(i,k,l)b_(i,j,k)).       (4.2)

Expanding delta c(o,i,j,k,l)=1 gives delta b=c. Hence Hhat_f=b_f H_f obeys the tetrahedron comparison with scalar one. Its source composition is strict because b itself is a ratio of K values.

The anchor dependence is also explicit. For another anchor o’,

b_f(o')/b_f(o)=a_(i,k)/(a_(j,k)a_(i,j)),
a_(i,j)=c_(o,o',i,j).                            (4.3)

Thus changing the anchor gives an edge coboundary with its full value retained. These are identities of actual algebraic functions; they do not discard a central multiplier or a phase.

5. The actual caps fix a different, compatible scalar

The cone correction by itself does not preserve the already selected cap and cyclic normalizations. Write the shared-frame actual pairings as mu_f(s) Mcal_f and cyclic maps as xi_f(s) Ucal_f. Direct substitution gives the retained defects

Hhat_f^t M_f(s') Hhat_(bar f)
      =d_f M_f(s),
d_f=b_f b_(bar f) mu_f(s')/mu_f(s),

U_f(s') Hhat_f
      =u_f Hhat_(r f) U_f(s),
u_f=(b_f/b_(r f)) xi_f(s')/xi_f(s).               (5.1)

They are not generally one. At N=2 and the regular reduction p=241 in the audit, face (1,2,3) has d_f=152 and u_f=45. This refutes a claim that the cone scalar alone supplies the actual pivotal comparison; it does not obstruct the following repair.

For two finite source flags use the actual edge modules, with fixed g_ij=(1,p_i-p_j). On each chosen edge orientation define T_ij by the normalized Hilbert-90 product

h_0=1,
h_n=product_(r=1)^n
      (A_i(s')-A_j(s')x_ij Q^r)/
      (A_i(s)-A_j(s)x_ij Q^r),
T_ij=H(x_ij)^(-1)diag(h_n)H(x_ij).              (5.2)

The cyclic norm of the ratio is one. Its marked evaluation fixes the scalar and makes composition strict. Let J_ij(s) be the actual source-normalized cap, already shared on the whole edge. Define the reverse adapter by

T_ji=J_ij(s')^(-1) T_ij^(-t) J_ij(s).            (5.3)

It is an intertwiner of the reverse edge modules and preserves the cap exactly. The double-dual identity J_ij(-t)J_ji=X(N-1), together with [T_ij,X]=0, gives the same naturality on the reverse orientation. Equations (5.2)–(5.3) also compose strictly.

The actual face adapter S_f is now fixed by

(T_ij tensor T_jk) C_(f,s)
       =C_(f,s') (T_ik tensor S_f).              (5.4)

To prove existence, conjugate the left side by C_(f,s’)^(-1) and remove T_ik. The remainder commutes with the total full matrix algebra, so it is I tensor S_f. Its partial trace divided by N computes S_f explicitly. The relative generators show

S_f=theta_f H_f(s',s), theta_f nonzero.           (5.5)

The scalar theta_f is fixed by (5.2)–(5.4), not chosen to force a period. Tree naturality, cap naturality and the trivalent definition of the corrected cyclic map prove, respectively,

R_(s')(S_A tensor S_B)=(S_C tensor S_D)R_s,
S_f^t M_f(s') S_(bar f)=M_f(s),
U_f(s') S_f=S_(r f) U_f(s).                     (5.6)

These assertions include every scalar and hold at all N>=2. They also compose strictly in the source flag. In particular

delta theta=c=delta b.

Thus theta/b is a configuration two-cocycle: the actual edge data supply the further scalar correction needed by (5.1). This extends the earlier affine triangle transport to the specified pivotal and cyclic face data. It does not specialize a separately singular edge cap at s=infinity.

6. The normal chart is the same source family

Let the geometric flags collide as

p_i=p_*+epsilon delta_i,
B_ij=epsilon^(1/N) b_ij, b_ij^N=delta_i-delta_j.

If the source also approaches the collision, write

s=p_*+epsilon sigma,
A_i(s)=epsilon^(1/N)(delta_i-sigma)^(1/N).        (6.1)

All common fractional powers cancel from (1.1) and (1.3). The resulting triangle module and face frame are exactly the source-flag family for (delta_i,sigma), independent of epsilon. This is an algebraic identity on the marked root chart, not an estimate.

A source s staying away from p_* is the chart sigma=infinity. More precisely its local coordinate is

tau=1/sigma=epsilon/(s-p_*),
(A_i(s)/A_j(s))^N=(1-tau delta_i)/(1-tau delta_j).
                                                    (6.2)

Thus the normal source and its transverse coefficients are ordinary algebraic jets of this same family at the source flag infinity. A full projective transformation of the normal flags also moves that source flag. Fixing infinity selects the affine subgroup; replacing the transported source by the default unit would change the data.

At this stage (6.1)–(6.2) identify the local triangle source chart. Identifying its epsilon with the actual marked meridian normal coordinate and carrying the whole cut/source lattice are additional requirements.

7. The non-scalar first coefficient and the affine source class

For fixed finite s avoiding p_, put w_s=(p_-s)^(-1), and w_infinity=0. Set beta=b_ik/b_jk and let H=H(beta). The frame is regular and invertible in epsilon: the shared fractional edge factor has canceled, and t_f(0) differs from zero and one. Its source adapter has expansion

H_f(s',s)=I+epsilon B_f+O(epsilon^2),

B_f=(w_(s')-w_s)/N H^(-1)diag(J_f(n))H,
J_f(n)=-(delta_j-delta_i)
          sum_(r=1)^n beta Q^r/(1-beta Q^r)
                  -n(delta_k-delta_i).           (7.1)

This follows by differentiating the finite product phi, using rho=beta[1+epsilon w_s(delta_j-delta_i)/N+…] and a=nu[1+epsilon w_s(delta_k-delta_i)/N+…]. In particular B_f commutes with D_f. The linear intertwining identity is

[B_f,Y_0]=(w_(s')-w_s)/N
   [(delta_i-delta_k)I-(delta_j-delta_k)D_f]
                                  sigma_Q^(-1). (7.2)

Its right side is nonzero for distinct flags and distinct sources. Hence B_f is not scalar and B_f*1 has a nonconstant part. A scalar cone or edge correction cannot erase that part. For Hhat its first coefficient is B_f plus the retained scalar derivative of b.

This is the promised affine normal-lift interpretation. For a fixed ordinary vector v_0, an adapter I+epsilon B sends

v_0+epsilon v_1 -> v_0+epsilon(v_1+Bv_0).
                                                    (7.3)

Suppose the difference automorphism sigma_diff fixes epsilon, and v_0 is horizontal for C_0. If its lift obeys sigma_diff(v_1)=C_0 v_1+c, (7.3) changes the source by

c -> c+sigma_diff(Bv_0)-C_0 Bv_0.               (7.4)

Thus the local source-flag adapter supplies an explicit translation in the same difference cokernel as the affine quantum-A extension. This is a conditional comparison formula; no identification with the actual knot source has been assumed.

In particular (7.4) changes a representative by a difference coboundary. It does not make a nonzero actual extension class split by passing to a finite cover; the trace obstruction in the affine extension proof still applies.

The cut row must also move. For ell=ell_0+epsilon ell_1, inverse transport gives ell_1 -> ell_1-ell_0 B. Consequently the selected first coefficient ell_0 v_1+ell_1 v_0 is preserved exactly. At higher retained orders the usual finite convolution of Taylor coefficients gives the complete jet action. Keeping the ordinary fiber while dropping these normal changes would lose the selected period data.

8. Coverage, audit and the remaining global comparison

These are the existing cyclic Weyl/Borel modules underlying Kashaev’s cyclic 6j definition. The odd-level standard matrix definition compared in the previous note is Baseilhac–Benedetti’s. Their abstracts were checked for coverage; their invariance, symmetrization and state-comparison proofs are not premises. No novelty is claimed for normalized Hilbert-90 transport. The contribution here is the source-flag parameterization, the explicit full-scalar/actual-dual adapters and their normal-jet interpretation for the specified period problem.

The independent audit checks symbolic projective invariance and the two normal-chart identities. At N=2,3,4,5,6,9 it checks 60 triangle adapter compositions, 60 actual edge-induced pivotal face adapters, their cap and cyclic naturality, 24 full ordered tetrahedron comparisons and moving-source inversions, the scalar cocycle, six anchor-change formulas and 60 nonzero first normal shears. The anchor cap/turn defects are recorded before their exact edge-induced repair. All checks pass, with every scalar retained. Results.

The ideal triangulation supplies the geometric developing flags and marked Bloch class. This note gives a more explicit local source family and finite-source dual transport on those charts. It still does not construct the full equivariant colored presentation of the actual ADO cut on that triangulation, prove its relevant geometric A-block, or identify its selected all-level norm with the Bloch-regulator norm. The source flag at infinity must retain the genuine edge degeneration and normal lattice. The original volume conjecture remains unproved.

The actual threaded-character separation also remains in force. The source-flag family does not identify the actual rational root state with a fixed hyperbolic central eigenline. A successful global comparison must keep its relative source and selected evaluation, as required by that test.

The subsequent carry extension now supplies the full integral tree/source comparison at the marked infinity. The total source frame cancels the fractional edge weights to the integer carry, giving the same carry lattice and its actual sublattice in all those source charts. Their full duals and the original whole cut evaluation extend as well. This resolves the ordered-tree lattice issue identified above; it does not identify the full geometric tensor presentation, the marked meridian normal coordinate or the regulator norm.
