Independent audit of the normal Mellin and full Green maps

Audit, 27 October 2026. The normal Mellin map and relative connection are sound. The revised Mellin section 2 and residual normal-current section 6 explicitly retain both pivot lifts and both projector extensions, with their correct normal rows. There is no outstanding correction. The full discrete Green/current calculation is sound and does not assert cancellation of the remaining geometric boundary terms. No test reruns were needed.

1. The strict normal Mellin map

For AN=UA^N=U, $$ D_{\rm phys}=A\partial_A+NU\partial_U $$ preserves the relation, and $\nabla=D_{\rm phys}/N$ has $\nabla\Log U=1$. With the marked αN=U\alpha^N=U, the elements A/αA/\alpha, hence every ϵj\epsilon_j, are horizontal. The map $$ v_{rN+j}\longmapsto\epsilon_j(\Log U)^r/r! $$ is therefore a topological linear isomorphism carrying BB to ∇\nabla, TT to multiplication by $\Log U$, and the coherent kernel to ϵjUs\epsilon_jU^s. The averaged trace and its U=1U=1 value give precisely the stated original vacuum cut.

There is a useful projector distinction. The coefficientwise extension eH⊗Ie_H\otimes I maps to f↦τ(f)1f\mapsto\tau(f)1. The finite-rank ambient coupon ieHπ0i e_H\pi_0 instead maps to f↦[τ(f)]U=11f\mapsto[\tau(f)]_{U=1}1. Both recover the same head cut; their whole-normal extensions are different. The revised finite-vacuum row is exactly [∇,ΓU]f=−[τ(∇f)]U=11: [\nabla,\Gamma_U]f=-[\tau(\nabla f)]_{U=1}1: the derivative of the constant output of ΓU\Gamma_U is zero, while ΓU∇\Gamma_U\nabla is its displayed evaluated trace row. The coefficientwise projector commutes with ∇\nabla.

For ∂σ−T\partial_\sigma-T, the compatible normal direction is B−σB-\sigma: [B−σ,∂σ−T]=0. [B-\sigma,\partial_\sigma-T]=0. The two scalar commutators cancel with opposite signs. Endpoint evaluation gives the degree-two corrections (B−σ,B,B−1)(B-\sigma,B,B-1), and direct differentiation of e−σThe^{-\sigma T}h proves the transgression formulas. Under Mellin transport the endpoint is exactly UU; no specialization to one occurs before this identity.

The algebraic primitive obstruction is also valid. A vacuum-preserving intertwiner on an algebraic finite cover would give df=cdU/Udf=c\,dU/U, c≠0c\ne0. On the compact normalized curve the right side has nonzero residues at zeros/poles of UU, whereas an exact algebraic differential has zero residues. Ramification multiplies rather than kills the residue. The formal logarithmic torsor avoids, rather than contradicts, this global algebraic obstruction.

2. Two whole-normal lifts of the finite pivot

The displayed formulas in the Mellin note are correct for the root-reduced lift $$ g_{\rm red}=x^{1-N}A^{-1},\quad D_{\rm red}=g_{\rm red}^{-1}e. $$ They have adapters α−1\alpha^{-1} and α\alpha, and physical derivatives $-g_{\rm red}$ and $+D_{\rm red}$, respectively.

The revised note separately retains the literal power of KU=xA−1K_U=xA^{-1}: $$ g_{\rm lit}=K_U^{1-N} =x^{1-N}A^{N-1}=U g_{\rm red}. $$ Its matching defect is $D_{\rm lit}=U^{-1}D_{\rm red}$. The adapters are then αN−1\alpha^{N-1} and α1−N\alpha^{1-N}, and $$ D_{\rm phys}(g_{\rm lit})=(N-1)g_{\rm lit},\qquad D_{\rm phys}(D_{\rm lit})=(1-N)D_{\rm lit}. $$ Both choices give gD=egD=e and the same finite source at U=1U=1, but their individual normal jets differ. The revised proof now states this U±1U^{\pm1} adapter explicitly.

Residual normal-current section 6 is consistent with these formulas. The divided Frobenius shift is $-D_{\rm phys}$, the averaged trace satisfies $\tau D_{\rm phys}=NU\partial_U\tau$, and its paired matrix row has the same covariance for either lift. Its coefficientwise extension is explicitly separated from ΓU\Gamma_U, so the latter’s extra finite-vacuum normal derivative has not been dropped.

The physical 2πi2\pi i caveat is correct: $\tau_{\rm geo}\Log A/H_N=\tau_{\rm geo}\Log U/(2\pi i)$. The bare kernel $U^{s_{\rm Fock}}$ therefore needs $s_{\rm Fock}=\tau_{\rm geo}/(2\pi i)$; its unit endpoint cannot silently be identified with the geometric unit meridian interval.

These are identities on entire zero-extended arrays. The polynomial moment does not lose a support boundary, and all currents remain finite. External Ore operators commute with the index polynomials and internal shifts.

4. Transverse pushforward and the unresolved relative boundary

The formal current identity follows from the already established interior identity by the same algebraic product rule, with ki=ui/Hk_i=u_i/H. No separate density argument for polynomial logarithms is needed. The transverse Gaussian translation identity kills the transverse differences even with their insertions; external coordinate/base shifts commute with the recentered Wick construction. Exactly the two stated framing differences remain.

For a translated chain, ∫Γ(FH*J−J)=∫FHΓ−ΓJ\int_\Gamma(F_H^*J-J)=\int_{F_H\Gamma-\Gamma}J; the Cartan homotopy therefore has the stated boundary sign. Its logarithmic loop changes the index lift by 2πi/HN=N2\pi i/H_N=N, preserving the polynomial-current jump. The complete residual Ward ideal does not set these relative endpoints or carries to zero.

Finally, on the actual diagonal one-cut array, CW=CN−1,N−1WCW=C_{N-1,N-1}W. Thus the ordered divided row is exactly that endpoint value, as claimed. This does not create an additional independent framing index.

The Green note correctly presents a full scalar certificate with a two-current geometric defect, not a full geometric annihilation or survival theorem.
