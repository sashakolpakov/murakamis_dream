The odd endpoint source is the actual matching-graph first normal

30 October 2026. The complete geometric matching graph Delta is also an exact quantum-source graph at every ODD level. Splitting the actual simple complete-color projectors yields a typed trivalent pivotal network on Delta itself. Its ordinary closure has a simple quantum- dimension factor, and the original exposed marked endpoint contribution is its specified first normal. This works for every fixed actual plat, without a Conway-tree, bipartite, or NC/Fisher assumption.

This is a source-compatible global graph interface. It is not a positive resummation theorem or a volume limit. It isolates exactly which global network must be compared with the geometric angle polytope; it does not replace its tensors by independently maximized local scalars.

1. The literal endpoint and the matching graph

Fix an original augmented 2k-plat with r designated crossing circles, all outside wires/cups/caps recorded, and one exposed original physical mark. Let N=2m+1, d=2m, q=exp(pi i/N), and x=q^d. The genuine endpoint c_m=-2 is simple. The already proved original Clebsch formulas give

W:V_N -> V_N tensor V_N,
pi=W^dagger P,    pi W=I,
E_St=W pi,
L_endpoint=N E_St,
R^epsilon E_St=omega_m^epsilon E_St,                    (1)

where

omega_m=q^(d^2/2) (-1)^m q^(m(m+1)).

P in (1) is the physical two-head reversal. It is part of the actual pivotal dual, not a replacement of a bilinear contraction by a norm. All formulas apply to the full physical pair, including every degree.

Split each E_St into its ordered W and pi factors. Its intermediate V_N edge is the crossing-disk edge d_i. The other legs follow precisely the original outside main segments. Thus the resulting trivalent ribbon graph is Delta of the actual matching construction: its marked perfect matching D consists of these intermediate edges, and its other edges are the original main segments. Its vertex cyclic orders are the original disk-side sectors, not a subsequently chosen abstract graph isomorphism.

All edges now carry the SAME object V_N. At a vertex one retains the actual W or pi arrow and any cups required by the drawn physical orientation. There is no need to color all vertices black/white or put them in two horizontal rows. General Delta need not be bipartite. Bending a leg uses its literal cup/cap and pivotal clock; it is not plain tensor-index permutation.

Consequently the marked all-endpoint contribution is

Z_endpoint=N^r omega_m^(sum_i epsilon_i) Z_Delta,       (2)

where Z_Delta is the original marked contraction of these typed W/pi vertices and original outside cups/caps. The independently recorded external framing line remains external. This formula includes its complete normalization and allows Z_Delta=0.

2. A regular generic lift, including the original trace quotient

Keep N fixed and let zeta=q exp(t), before root specialization. Use the original balanced highest-weight-d representation V_d(zeta), its actual generic physical cup pV and inverse cap ellV. In particular

pV_a=N zeta^(-d^2) (-1)^a zeta^[a(d-a-1)]
                (Q;Q)_a (Q;Q)_(d-a)/(Q;Q)_d,
Q=zeta^2.                                           (3)

All displayed denominator indices are below N, so the physical cup and cap are regular. Their root values are the original ones. Their genuine turn retains the full ratios in (3), not just root monomials.

The central generic projector e_m onto the highest-weight-d summand of V_d tensor V_d is REGULAR near q. One direct formula is

e_m(t)=product_(0<=j<=d, j!=m)
   [Omega(t)-c_j(t) I]/[c_m(t)-c_j(t)],
c_j(t)=zeta^(2j+1)+zeta^(-2j-1).                       (4)

Every denominator at q is nonzero: the central node is isolated even though the other nodes pair with one another. Thus (4) also retains its full zero first normals on those other paired nodes.

Equivalently, use the generic first-entry-one W:V_d->V_d tensor V_d and its genuine ordered bilinear dual D(W). Then

e_m=W D(W)/eta_m,
eta_m=(-1)^m zeta^[-m(3m+1)]
             (Q;Q)_(3m+1)/[(Q;Q)_m (Q;Q)_(2m+1)].    (5)

The common root zero in the ratio cancels before specialization; its remaining product tends to one. Hence eta_m is a unit and this is another regular lift of the original E_St. Formula (4) avoids having to choose a root frame to see regularity.

Let F_Delta(t) be the ordinary closed physical plat with e_m in every circle position, all original outside cups/caps, and an ordinary cap also at the eventually marked arc. After splitting e_m as in (5), it is the same typed trivalent network Delta with its genuine generic pivotal contractions. No high recoupling channel has been discarded.

Leave the chosen exposed physical arc open. Generic Hopf covariance makes the resulting endomorphism of V_d a scalar z_Delta(t) I: its K eigenvalues are distinct and its interior F arrows are nonzero, so commuting with K and F forces this scalarity. All physical entries are regular, hence z_Delta is holomorphic. Closing that arc gives

F_Delta(t)=D_N(t) z_Delta(t),
D_N(t)=(-1)^d [N]_(q exp(t)),
D_N(0)=0,
nu_N=D_N'(0)=2N(-1)^N/(q-q^-1) !=0.                  (6)

The original marked root row satisfies m pV(0)=1 and therefore extracts precisely z_Delta(0). It follows that

Z_endpoint=N^r omega_m^(sum_i epsilon_i)
                        F_Delta'(0)/nu_N.            (7)

This is the full scalar derivative of the ordinary closure. It must not be replaced by first specializing that closure to its root zero, or by differentiating one guessed local coefficient. A different regular lift of the same root coupons gives the same marked root endomorphism, so the normalized derivative in (7) is lift independent. It is legitimate to use (4), but this does not call (4) the original moving-q germ of the entire complete-circle operator.

3. Why this is the geometric graph, and what the global polytope is

Suppose the actual matching graph Delta has the labeled positive right-angled ideal polyhedron Q_Delta. Its triangular shaded faces are exactly the just-identified source vertices, and its ideal vertices are exactly the source graph edges. Thus the geometric and quantum constructions share the actual labeled incidence, not merely a cubic vertex count. The complete augmented exterior has volume 2Vol(Q_Delta).

Cone an ideal vertex of Q_Delta to compatible triangulations of its nonincident faces, and use the identical subdivisions in the second copy. Keep the actual white and shaded face identifications, including all retained half twists. This gives an actual positive ideal triangulation T of the augmented exterior. Its global angle polytope is

alpha_(t,0)+alpha_(t,1)+alpha_(t,2)=pi for every t,
sum_(tetrahedral incidences at e) alpha_(t,e)=2pi for every e,
alpha>=0.                                           (8)

The incidence matrix is calculated from these FULL face-pairing words; it is not one independent simplex per source vertex. The proved general positive gluing theorem gives its unique positive geometric maximum

max_(8) sum_t V_ideal(alpha_t)=2Vol(Q_Delta).          (9)

It also supplies an algebraic optimizer and its cusp-decoration gauge. A separately certified marked positive triangulation can replace Q_Delta.

Equations (7)–(9) identify the precise source and geometric endpoints of a proposed coherent resummation. They do not yet identify the variables or weights BETWEEN them. To do that, an actual recoupling or face-state expansion must retain its finite physical coefficient, all pole/normal terms, and a proved map to the angle incidences (8). Its factorial action must equal the SINGLE constrained functional in (9), with the source’s phases/cycle and absolute normalization. Triangle inequalities for ordinary spin colors, by themselves, are not the global edge-sum equations (8). Matching the counts of labels or tetrahedra is not an incidence proof.

This also explains why locally maximizing each trivalent scalar is insufficient. The source tensors remain contracted through every physical magnetic edge. The exact six-entry phase obstruction already rules out making them all positive by independent leg gauges. A positive resummation has to use those contractions coherently.

4. What is actually established beyond local vertices

The primitive cube supplies one nontrivial calibration. Its original odd central sector was reduced by a generic, clocked, all-input rung identity to a positive Newton period. The exact first normal survives that reduction, and its exponential rate contains the shared geometric core term, not just the product of the local source scalars. This is a concrete example of the kind of global resummation required by (7). It is not a generic identity for arbitrary Delta.

For an NC source graph Gamma, the present graph is its Fisher expansion Delta=Fisher(Gamma). The original trivalent-source contractions reduce its facial triangles to the known local coefficients and leave the ordinary Gamma-network first normal. This is consistent with (7), but does not extend that special facial-triangle reduction to an arbitrary matching graph. General Delta can have nonmatching cycles of other lengths and need not satisfy the original NC ordering.

At even N the endpoint node is double; the circle is nilpotent on that sector. W pi with pi W=I is NOT its all-input replacement. A full even normal or paired-channel source theorem is still required. Even at odd levels, (7) alone neither proves endpoint dominance in the whole color sum nor a nonzero endpoint value. Independently long fillings additionally require the actual mixed auxiliary-color packet, whole-color gap, marked peripheral branch and unit logarithmic charge.

5. Literature coverage only, with the actual scope retained

Van der Veen, arXiv:0805.0094v2, Definitions 1–2 and Theorem 2, uses a specified sequence of KTG moves. Its main theorem gives a sufficiently large augmentation multiplicity for that sequence. Its invariant has the stated formula at ODD N and is zero at EVEN N; its conclusion is explicitly the so(3) version. The singly augmented outside is built from regular ideal octahedra. Thus its theorem is not an all-level result for every singly augmented matching graph, nor a theorem for all independently long fillings. Only those definitions and statements are used here for coverage.

Ibarra–McQuire–Purcell, arXiv:2506.09296 addresses OCTAHEDRAL fully augmented links. Its stated TV result is for links without half twists and concerns a different invariant/root; its introduction explicitly sets Dehn fillings aside. It does not supply the original marked all-level mixed packet needed here. Belletti, arXiv:2002.01904 formulates a Yokota upper-bound conjecture, proves it for a stated large family, and derives TV consequences. That also does not identify the original first normal (7) with a positive state sum for every Delta. No proof technique or unreviewed asymptotic theorem from these papers is an input to sections 1–4.

6. Exact source audit

The executable audit checks six original plat families at odd N=3,5,7 and widths k=2,3,4. It constructs the original pair Casimir and circle, checks the central projector against the independent Clebsch W/pi split, and contracts all physical wires with the original unrestricted mark. Its separate generic first-jet calculation uses (3),(4), before root specialization, and verifies the normalized trace derivative (6),(7). Retained signed half twists are checked against the original R matrices. The outside interval compiler independently checks every oriented Delta face.

Recorded results. These are source-identity tests; no asymptotic or positivity conclusion is inferred from their finite values.
