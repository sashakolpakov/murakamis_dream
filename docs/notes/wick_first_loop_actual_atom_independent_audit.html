Independent first-loop audit for the actual compact coefficient model

Proof audit, 27 October 2026. This checks the universal Wick coefficient and the original crossing, color-half-step and pivot contributions used in the figure-eight cap calculation. The coefficient is a transverse FORMAL block in the stated physical slice. Its geometric coordinate eta_geo=p_e^(-1) is independent of the finite band-Fourier variable eta. Transport of the latter’s entire marked contour/endpoint is a separate assertion; see the exact Fourier audit.

1. Wick signs and multiplicities

Write the local integral formally as exp(S/H) a_0(1+H a_1+…), put G=(S’’)^(-1), and use Gaussian covariance -H G. Repeated indices below are summed. Dividing the first coefficient by its leading amplitude gives

z_1=a_1-G_ij a_(0,ij)/(2a_0)
    +(a_(0,l)/a_0)S_ijk G_ij G_kl/2
    +S_ijkl G_ij G_kl/8
    -S_ijk S_lmn G_il G_jm G_kn/12
    -S_ijk S_lmn G_ij G_lm G_kn/8.            (1)

The amplitude second derivative contracts one covariance, giving the minus sign. The amplitude-linear/cubic and quartic vertices contract two covariances, giving the positive signs. Two cubic vertices contract three, giving the negative signs. Of the 15 six-leg pairings, six have all three edges between vertices and nine have one self-pair at each vertex; the expansion coefficient 1/(2(3!)^2) gives respectively 1/12 and 1/8. These prove every coefficient in (1).

In one dimension (1) reduces independently to

z_1=a_1-a_0''/(2a_0 S'')
   +a_0'S'''/(2a_0(S'')^2)+S''''/(8(S'')^2)
   -5(S''')^2/(24(S'')^3).

The exact audit script contracts expanded polynomials by an independent recursive pairing rule in dimensions one, two and three, using non-diagonal Hessians in the latter cases. All identities pass over the rationals.

2. The actual atoms have the stated first variable correction

For P_Q(z)=(z;Q)infinity and H=Log Q, its original logarithm is -sum(m>=1) z^m/[m(1-exp(mH))]. Therefore

log P_Q(exp(alpha H)z)
   =Li_2(z)/H+(1/2-alpha)Log(1-z)
      +H B_2(alpha)Li_0(z)/2+O(H^2).          (2)

Here B_2(alpha)=alpha^2-alpha+1/6. In particular alpha=0 and alpha=1 both give +H Li_0(z)/12; the exact functional equation P_Q(Qz)=P_Q(z)/(1-z) gives the same conclusion directly. A fractional half shift would instead contribute -H Li_0(z)/24, so the original color cancellation must be used rather than assuming half shifts do not matter.

For the actual balanced coefficient x=-M exp(-H/2), Qx2=M2=Y. The five variable arguments and signs are

positive: (B,C,Y/A,BC,YC/A),
negative: (A,C,Y/B,AC,YC/B),
epsilon=(+1,+1,+1,-1,-1).

The original compact factors have shift alpha=1 at arguments one, two and four, and alpha=0 at arguments three and five. Thus their log-a_0 coefficients are (-1,-1,+1,+1,-1)/2 and their VARIABLE a_1 is exactly

a_1^var=(1/12)sum_atoms epsilon Li_0(z_atom).  (3)

Every fixed P_Q(Q) normalization stays in the recorded base line. No individual fixed compact product is evaluated at a root.

Put xi_a=Ha, xi_b=Hb, xi_k=Hk. The exact original positive Gaussian/color monomial has H^0 coefficient (xi_a+xi_b)/2; the negative one has (xi_k-xi_a-xi_b)/2. Neither has a variable H^1 coefficient. This follows by substituting Log x=Log M+pi*i-H/2 in the original monomial, including the positive k(k+1) term, BEFORE expansion. The audit checks this symbolically.

The closed pivot/framing source contributes -sum_closed xi to log a_0, in addition to M/base factors. On the physical level mark n=2pi*i/H-1, its remaining H^1 term is kappa_D/2, independent of framing variables. Common Cartan and unrolled color scalars also belong to the base line. A base-only addition to z_1 times a framing form regular at a cap has zero logarithmic cap residue. This does not authorize discarding that line in a whole period.

3. Implementation and cap scope

The computation in the actual figure-eight first-loop script uses external physical positions (a_0,a_2), with k_2=a_2-a_0+k_3 and transverse variables (k_1,k_3,k_4). Its matrix is the exact integral complement in the original flow lattice. Both closed pivot rows enter the amplitude gradient. All logarithmic flow derivatives are taken before substituting s=1.

The implementation’s Hessian, third and fourth derivatives use the rank-one tensors of the actual monomial shape arguments. Its amplitude second term is precisely -Tr(G Hess log a_0)/2-(grad log a_0)^T G(grad log a_0)/2; the remaining terms agree with (1). Combining opposite vector classes flips the cubic tensor sign and preserves the quartic sign, as required. No erroneous s=1 differentiation or omitted pivot gradient appears in that construction.

Individual a_1 or Wick terms may have poles at the marked cap. Only their FULL sum determines the residue of the transverse first-loop form. Identifying that form with the literal finite Fourier cap first needs a source-compatible Fourier/canonical pushforward. The bounded-band principal relation eta p=1 is an incidence relation, not an ordinary base substitution transporting the cap. An all-order comparison also needs its ordered band/cap and half-density data. The exact computation therefore concerns the stated actual transverse-formal cap, and does not by itself prove survival of the selected finite period.

4. Exact characteristic-zero cap certificate on the stated slice

The saved characteristic-zero calculation now supplies z_1=a(r)+t b(r) over Q(r)[t]/(t^2-t+1), with t=(1-sqrt(-3))/2. Independent rational simplification of its two components gives the denominators

den a=216(r^2+3)^4(3r^2+6r+7)^4,
den b=108(r^2+3)^4(3r^2+6r+7)^4.              (4)

At the candidate geometric boundary r=-2, both variable factors equal seven. At the other k=1 point r=1-t, the relation r^2-r+1=0 reduces them to r+2 and 9r+4, both nonzero. Thus BOTH components, and therefore the entire first-loop correction, are regular at both caps on the stated s=1 slice. This certifies more than cancellation of the simple residue. As a separate check, all six exact residues printed for the individual double-pole terms sum identically to zero in Q(sqrt(-3)).

Since Omega_cut=-dr wedge ds/[s(1+s)] is regular there, the relative first-loop form z_1 Omega_cut has zero logarithmic residue in the SEPARATE coordinate eta_geo on this slice. This does not identify either point with the finite Fourier cap. Retaining only the variable compact-factor term would give a false nonzero answer. This calculation does not prove zero residue identically in s, all-order cap regularity, or the missing subprincipal finite-cap comparison.

5. Absolute exactness retains nonzero relative endpoint data

The updated characteristic-zero JSON includes a rational Hermite primitive. This audit reconstructs it independently in the native field Q(sqrt(-3))(r) from its polynomial part and the six displayed coefficients multiplying

r/J^3, 1/J^3, r/J^2, 1/J^2, r/J, 1/J.

The literal coefficient table and polynomial part in the first-loop proof note, equation (5.1), agree with this reconstruction, including every sign after substituting sqrt(-3)=1-2t.

Here t=(1-sqrt(-3))/2, and the actual prefix minors give

V=2+t-tr^2, U=4-t-(t+1)(r+r^2),
J=U'V-UV'.

Direct rational differentiation proves EXACTLY G’=z_1. The two simple zeros of J are 2t-1 and -(1+4t)/3. At each, the coefficient of J^(-3) in G is nonzero, so G has order-three poles and z_1 has order-four poles. In particular z_1 dr has zero logarithmic residue at both caustics. This is an absolute rational exactness certificate, including the nonconstant first-loop correction.

It does not discard endpoints. Direct substitution in the SAME primitive gives

G(1-t)-G(-2)=(889-125t)/216,
G(-1)-G(-2)=(1076-991t)/432.                 (5)

Both are nonzero. Adding an arbitrary base-only scalar kappa to z_1 changes G to G+kappa r+constant. It changes these two gaps by respectively kappa(3-t) and kappa. Their combination is fixed:

[G(1-t)-G(-2)]/(3-t)-[G(-1)-G(-2)]
                =(295t-138)/112 != 0.         (6)

Thus no base-only first-loop normalization can eliminate BOTH relative gaps. Absolute exactness of the current is compatible with a genuine relative endpoint class. The primitive, original endpoint marks and source line must travel together in any framing comparison. Equations (5)–(6) concern this actual formal block on s=1; they are not an identification of the marked finite cap or its selected global cycle.
