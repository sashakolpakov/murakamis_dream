Independent audit of the actual horizontal meridian source transport

27 October 2026. Detailed examination of the revised SECOND-wire construction in the actual open-meridian theorem. The main scaled Vandermonde is a whole source-preserving basis change on arbitrary finite tensors. The optional first-wire construction has the stated Ward correction; it is not needed to justify the main map. The regulator/selected-period comparison remains an additional theorem.

1. The actual local and open-word half-character has the stated sign

For the positive actual coefficient the monomial x power changes by q^(-i-j-k), while the Pochhammer ratio is (1-z Q^k)/(1-z), z=Y/A. The displayed tau_+ inverse contributes that ratio times x/(BC). Multiplying x inverse and q^(j+k-i) gives exactly q^(-i-j-k). The negative coefficient similarly gives q^(i+j-k): its tau_- inverse contributes A/x and its first output is j-k. These are canceled whole finite-product identities, not separate evaluation of their zero factors.

For either sign the integer potential -sum_i i a_i changes by first output minus first input. The opened matrix unit changes it by ell nu. Every old closed strand returns to its input and the first cut has coefficient zero in the potential. Thus the crossing contribution is -ell nu exactly, including unwrapped integer values. This establishes q^(-ell nu), not its inverse.

Each old closed pivot contributes q^(1-N) under the common meridian step and the residual framing line contributes q^(w(N-1)). Hence gamma_N=q^((N-1)(w-b+1)) is correct. For a knot w-b+1 is even; its root simplification does not justify discarding the whole q^N character before normal derivatives. Opening the internal identity adds no pivotal cap.

2. The second-wire gauge preserves the source for every finite tensor

Use D_2=diag(M^(-ell b)) and

Th=(I⊗D2−1)T(I⊗D2),W=V(Q)D2−1,Wj,b=(MℓQj)b. T_h=(I\otimes D_2^{-1})T(I\otimes D_2),\qquad W=V(Q)D_2^{-1},\quad W_{j,b}=(M^\ell Q^j)^b.

Its coefficient is M(ell(d-b))=M(ell nu) with NO charge qualification. This cancels q^(-ell nu) in the actual open-word meridian relation. Since the second closure is its identity trace and every original pivot is already inside T, partial trace of this conjugation is EXACTLY unchanged on all first-wire entries. The first input/output source can therefore remain literally the original one on arbitrary finite current tensors too.

No Schur scalarity argument is needed. It would be incorrect to leave a separately untransformed pivotal matrix on that second trace; the literal opened-identity tensor already fixes its location. The current theorem uses the correct tensor convention.

The scaled node differences are Mell(Qi-Q^j). Thus W introduces only the old Vandermonde head units and a meridian monomial, not a new peripheral norm denominator. Its whole inverse is D_2 V inverse. The identity W(qM)D_rho=W(M), rho=q^(-ell), proves the revised whole matrix meridian equation with its original L insertion.

Consequently arbitrary ordered operators and source/current maps transport by this basis change before any jets. The separate pure L path identity concerns the stated actual word and its covariantly transported insertion complex; it is not permission to hold an arbitrary M-dependent inserted coupon fixed under a meridian step.

3. Whole matrix connection and packet connection are different objects

Direct differentiation gives

ΓM=(DMW)W−1=ℓVJheadV−1,ΓQ=(DQV)V−1(M fixed), \Gamma_M=(D_MW)W^{-1}=\ell VJ_{head}V^{-1},\qquad \Gamma_Q=(D_QV)V^{-1}\quad(M\text{ fixed}), DMTW=W(DMT)W−1+[ΓM,TW]. D_M T^W=W(D_MT)W^{-1}+[\Gamma_M,T^W].

Thus the covariant derivative has sign D_M minus ad Gamma_M. The commutator’s partial trace is zero ENTRYWISE on the first wire. Every mixed/normal derivative of the exact source identity is preserved by the product rule, including the actual M(q) chain rule. At the root V divided by sqrt N is unitary, so the full matrix connection has norm ell(N-1). This is a source frame estimate, not a bound on the actual crossing word.

For a charge-zero fixed-input a the scalar packet instead has unwrapped representatives nu=a-c, c=0,…,N-1. Its matrix is

Ha=Fadiag⁡(Mℓν)Fa−1,(Fa)j,c=QNj(a−c). H_a=F_a\operatorname{diag}(M^{\ell\nu})F_a^{-1},\quad (F_a)_{j,c}=Q_N^{j(a-c)}.

The nu=0 column is the all-ones vector. It follows exactly that H_a times ones is ones and Sigma H_a=Sigma. Cyclic shifts, normalized differences and their centered primitive are diagonal in that same root Fourier basis, so H_a commutes with them. For absolute M one the map is unitary; the distance to the zero-source cyclic current space is therefore unchanged AT THE SAME M. This does not compare the norms of different meridian states or turn the L insertion into a scalar longitude.

The packet connection is A_a=ell F_a diag(a-c)F_a inverse, with zero source and constant mode and norm at most ell(N-1). It is not the full Gamma_M above. More explicitly, on the common Fourier-label space, for a<N-1,

Aa+1−Aa=ℓNΠa+1,Ha+1Ha−1=I+(MℓN−1)Πa+1, A_{a+1}-A_a=\ell N\Pi_{a+1},\qquad H_{a+1}H_a^{-1}=I+(M^{\ell N}-1)\Pi_{a+1}, \tag{1}

where Pi_(a+1) is the Fourier-mode projector with residue a+1. Only one unwrapped representative has changed by N. This proves why packets for different a cannot be silently identified through their residues. Charged/unwrapped tail modules retain their own independent alias powers and the full tensor map, as the note states.

4. Peripheral and alias powers remain whole

The complete ratio is exactly

dN(qM)dN(M)=qN−1q2M2−1M2−1M2N−1q2NM2N−1. \frac{d_N(qM)}{d_N(M)} =q^{N-1}\frac{q^2M^2-1}{M^2-1} \frac{M^{2N}-1}{q^{2N}M^{2N}-1}.

The formula follows from the original finite S_N ratio, with the same phase on the two sides. It is a canceled whole meromorphic germ; norm-zero numerator and denominator are not evaluated separately. At a root its generic-M reduction agrees with the known -q inverse peripheral gauge, but that reduction does not give its whole q-normal adapter.

The independent alias V_eta=eta^N transforms by V_eta to (qN)(-ell)V_eta. At the actual root this is the sign (-1)^ell. A head window has only its zero multiple of N, so its constant coefficient is unchanged even on the antiperiodic grid. Unwrapped current tails can have other multiples, whose retained characters and normal derivatives cannot be omitted.

The optional first-wire source formula also passes. Its coefficient M^(ell(a-c))-1 is precisely the quotient by the genuine head Cartan gap Q^(a-c)-1 times the actual D_K entry. The packet derivative signs, wrap M^(ell N), and polynomial source-row bounds are correct. They are an alternative source homotopy, not a substitute for the exact all-tensor invariance of the main second leg.

5. The geometric filter formulas have their exact conditional scope

For p_hor=alpha p, alpha=M^ell, the coordinate density transforms as

Gp(p−λ)2dlog⁡p=αGphor(phor−αλ)2dlog⁡phor. G\frac p{(p-\lambda)^2}d\log p =\alpha G\frac{p_{hor}}{(p_{hor}-\alpha\lambda)^2} d\log p_{hor}.

This extra factor alpha is necessary. It makes the finite-filter ratio alpha^N times [(1-lambdaN)/(1-alphaN lambda^N)] squared, with the printed M derivative at one. The finite sampling identity on p^N=S has numerator N S lambda^(N-1); antiperiodic S=-1 therefore retains its minus sign.

The proven Möbius/single-pole framing theorem is complete-M only. The horizontal gauge alone does not extend that theorem to general M. The current note correctly makes a marked lambda(M) branch conditional and does not identify its rational filter with the actual quantum source. Its singularities must be accounted for by the actual whole word/source correction, not hidden by this gauge.

6. The remaining Green residual is typed and nonzero in general

With sigma_M I_h=L I_h and R_i=S_i I_h/I_h, one obtains sigma_M R_i/R_i=S_i L/L. Therefore

LdσR,i(f)=dR,i(Lf) L\,d_{\sigma R,i}(f)=d_{R,i}(Lf)

with exactly the displayed order. This is a genuine semilinear connection on the proper-array insertion/current complex. Full zero extension retains its boundary strata. After source pairing, multiplying an ordinary difference by L leaves the finite residual sum_s [L(s-e_i)-L(s)]H(s). It is not generally zero and does not become zero by substituting the critical longitude into the sum.

Thus the theorem removes a real endpoint half-character and supplies a tame actual source transport. It does not prove a nonzero selected geometric quotient, kill actual zero-source currents in that quotient, or bound the selected regulator norm. Those are correctly stated as remaining source/word comparisons rather than normalization choices.
