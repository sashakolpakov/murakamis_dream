An exact analytic color packet carrying the original mixed pretzel source

29 October 2026. Fix an odd s>=5. There is an EXACT finite, holomorphic, separately even color packet C_N(chi_1,…,chi_s), defined using the original factorial coefficients and the full marked Hermite functional. It approximates the physical mixed-color source with a uniform, explicitly retained exponential gap in a fixed cusp corridor. The packet ITSELF, rather than merely its leading expression, satisfies a complex Gaussian growth bound. This is the source input needed before applying a Fourier/cotangent endpoint estimate.

The theorem does not remove the rest of the physical color cube or identify a filled-knot volume. Those remain separate source terms.

1. Definition by actual finite coefficients and the original mark

Use N,d,q,Q,kappa,M and the original f_v,P_r,Omega as in the exact complete polynomial. Let eta_s=2asinh(tan(pi/s)), R_s=exp eta_s, u_s=atan(sec(pi/s)), and F_0=V_s/(2pi). Choose FIXED numbers

1/4<a<u_s/pi<b<1/2,
I_N={ceil(aN),...,floor(bN)}.

For sufficiently large N this is a nonempty physical index window. Shrink a fixed complex chi disk so that |Re chi|/pi<a/2 and all arguments below lie in regular compact scaled factorial substrips. Put h=N chi/pi, and define the finite holomorphic function

Ahat_(N,r)(chi)=sum_(v=r)^M
  Q^(-vr)F_(2v)
   /[F_v F_(v-r) Fhat_N(v-h)Fhat_N(v+h)], r in I_N.

Here F_a=(Q;Q)_a at the physical integers, and Fhat is the canonical whole factorial completion with its original marks. Define the polynomial

c_N^win(z,chi)=N^2 sum_(r in I_N) b_(N,r)(chi) B_r(z),

b_(N,r)(chi)=kappa^(-1)q^(h^2)(-1)^r q^(2r^2+r)
                                     Ahat_(N,r)(chi)/F_r^3,
B_r(z)=product_(v=0)^(r-1)(c_v-z),
c_v=q^(2v+1)+q^(-2v-1).                            (1)

The powers q(h2)=exp(iN chi^2/pi) and the full kappa are KEPT. They are not replaced by absolute values or a fitted period scale. This polynomial is a finite explicit function, not an asymptotic definition. It has degree at most floor(bN)<M+1 and is even in chi. Its coefficients are holomorphic on a fixed complex disk, uniformly in the sense of domains, since each denominator Fhat(v+/-h) is nonzero there.

Use the original full-Hermite marked functional

Phi_2(f)=(-1)^d sum_(c double)
              [(4-c^2)f'(c)-c f(c)]
                    +1_(N odd)f(-2),

where the double and possible simple nodes are those of P_N(z)=2T_N(z/2)+2. The exact packet is

C_N(chi_1,...,chi_s)=Phi_2(product_i c_N^win(z,chi_i)). (2)

It is holomorphic and even in EACH color. Every original Casimir normal derivative remains in (2). No positive quotient, ordinary semisimple trace, or modified dimension for auxiliary components is substituted. An external original normalization, if required, is still multiplied separately with its already proved value.

Equivalently, for ANY fixed R>1, the SAME exact packet is

C_N=(-1)^d N^2/(2pi i) integral_(|w|=R)
    product_i c_N^win(w+w^(-1),chi_i)
               w^N(1-w^(-2))/(1+w^N)^2 dw.          (3)

This is the established residue formula for the actual marked source. It keeps all finite windings, including the double-pole normal weight. Formula (2), or (3), is the definition of C_N; no unknown scalar knot period defines its metric or amplitude.

2. Exact relation to the physical mixed source and its residual

At an actual integer deficit 0<=h<aN/2, Fhat(v+/-h)=F_(v+/-h) for every v>=r in I_N. Thus (1) is EXACTLY the indicated Newton window of the physical low core c_(N,h). The finite source identity proves

z_(N,h)=c_(N,h)+R P_(N,h),

with the actual nonzero upper band retained separately. Put A_N(h)=Phi_2(product_i z_(N,h_i)). The exact residual is

A_N(h)-C_N(pi h/N)
  =Phi_2(product_i z_(N,h_i)-product_i c_(N,h_i))
   +Phi_2(product_i c_(N,h_i)
                     -product_i c_N^win(pi h_i/N)). (4)

There is a fixed corridor 0<=h_i<=delta_s N and gamma_s>0 such that, uniformly on this physical color grid,

|A_N(h)-C_N(pi h/N)|
                     <=poly(N)exp[N(F_0-gamma_s)].  (5)

Here are the two bounds, so neither residual in (4) is silently discarded. The first is the proved uniform original RP-band source gap for odd s>=5. For the second, use the REAL-color all-index bound from the mixed-source theorem and the original circle estimate at R_s. The complete positive Newton profile J_R+E has its unique maximum at u_s, so

g_I=j(eta_s)-max_(u/pi outside(a,b))(J_R(u)+E(u))>0.

Every removed r-summand has circle height at most j(eta_s)-g_I+C chi^2, and every retained or full core has height at most j(eta_s)+C chi^2, with polynomial prefactors. Telescope the product of s cores and use the EXACT source-circle bound from (3). This gives

|second residual|<=poly(N)
             exp[N(F_0-g_I+C sum_i chi_i^2)].

First fix the index window and its gap, then shrink delta_s to preserve at least half this gap and the independent RP gap. This proves (5). The underlying sums in (4) remain the actual source terms. The estimate is absolute, so it remains usable before a subsequent filling sum with its known polynomial coefficient mass. It is not an assertion that those terms vanish.

No complex continuation of the FULL physical low-index or upper-band source is needed for (5). Only the EXACT packet (2) is continued.

3. The exact packet has a uniform complex-color asymptotic

After shrinking its fixed chi disk, the packet ITSELF satisfies

C_N(chi)=N^(3/2)exp(-i s pi d/4)
          A_s(chi)exp[N Phi_s(chi)](1+O(1/N)),       (6)

UNIFORMLY on a fixed closed complex polydisk. The analytic functions are those in the mixed-cusp construction, and A_s is nonzero there. This statement is stronger than a pointwise approximation to the original real-grid full source. It follows by applying that construction to (2) as follows.

First, the high-window coefficients are the natural COMPLEX-color factorial source. The literal endpoint wall makes their continued fractions exact on every physical r-grid, even when chi is complex. The source theorem supplies a uniformly analytic action interpolant, and its original-grid residual is relatively O(exp(-cN)), uniformly in that complex color disk. The analytic Newton factor has its proved prefactor ONE with all the original logarithmic marks.

Second, use the EXACT finite half-grid cotangent rectangle for the r-window. Its vertical sides have |cot(Nu)|<=1 and a fixed endpoint gap. Its horizontal remainders are O(exp(-2Nd)); their complete phase grows only O(d^2) under that fixed vertical displacement. Choose d first, then the fixed complex eta,chi neighborhoods. All these remainders, INCLUDING the retained grid residual, are exponentially below the complex saddle. The two constant cotangent parts give the same analytic saddle integral with total density N/pi. Uniform analytic Gaussian expansion gives the nonzero box amplitude a(eta,chi), with the original exp(-i pi d/4) factor. This proves the local box asymptotic for the EXACT high-window polynomial at complex eta,chi.

Third, control the rest of the w circle WITHOUT invoking real-color estimates on an off-grid source. The high-window WKB and canonical factorial ratio imply, directly on the physical r-grid,

|b_(N,r)(chi)|<=poly(N)[s_(2r)/s_r^6]
                                  exp(CN|chi|^2).

The finite Newton factor B_r(w) has the old strict angular envelope away from w=-R_s, uniformly for a<=r/N<=b. Thus the exact packet’s noncentral circle parts have a fixed exponential gap for all small COMPLEX chi. These estimates concern only the explicit finite packet, whose domain contains that entire complex color polydisk.

Finally insert the analytic box asymptotic into the original source contour (3). The nearby eta saddle has nonzero Hessian, stays in Re eta>0, and is reached by a local contour shift with gapped end connectors. No pole of (3) is crossed. The original sign and normal kernel give exactly N^(3/2) and the amplitude A_s in (6). The exact packet’s exponential interpolation remainders stay below this saddle after a fixed further shrink of the color disk. This proves (6) uniformly in complex colors; it is NOT inferred by analytic continuation of a pointwise real-grid error estimate.

4. A genuine complex Gaussian growth estimate for C_N itself

Write u_s=atan(sec(pi/s)) and

a_0=sin(2u_s)/(2pi)>0,
b_0=(1+cos(2u_s))/(2pi).

The actual source curvature is

Phi_s(chi)=F_0+(-a_0+i b_0)sum_i chi_i^2+O(||chi||^4).

For chi=x+i y, the real quadratic part is

-a_0||x||^2+a_0||y||^2-2b_0 sum_i x_i y_i
   <=-(a_0/2)||x||^2
                     +(a_0+2b_0^2/a_0)||y||^2.

Shrink the fixed complex polydisk until the quartic remainder is at most (a_0/4)(||x||2+||y||2). Then (6), with bounded A_s, proves an ACTUAL uniform inequality

|C_N(x+i y)|<=C_s N^(3/2)
   exp{N[F_0-a_s||x||^2+B_s||y||^2]},              (7)

a_s=a_0/4,
B_s=5a_0/4+2b_0^2/a_0.

The constants and the color domain are independent of N. The uniform relative O(1/N) error in (6) changes only C_s. In particular (7) is a bound on the EXACT source function (2), not on A_s exp(N Phi_s) after discarding a polynomially small error.

For Fourier estimates in the physical variable h/N, substitute chi=pi h/N; both quadratic constants are multiplied by pi^2. Because the packet is exactly even in every color, the actual complete-color coefficient retains its physical half weight under the proved Weyl fold. No extra 2N average, ordinary trace, or one-sided exp(pi i h) completion is introduced.

5. Precisely what can now be combined with filling

The Fourier endpoint lemma may be applied to the exact packet C_N, or to a slice of it with the other colors retained, using (7). The physical error (5) is still a separate explicit source term and must be multiplied by the actual filling coefficient mass before estimating it. For fixed twists that mass is polynomial, so its fixed gap is preserved.

This handles only the displayed small cusp-color window. The complement of that physical color window, its upper RP bands, other Fresnel charges and the geometric identification of the filled critical action require their own proofs. Neither (5) nor (7) licenses deletion of those parts of the original filling source.

Audit checks the literal finite polynomial/source definition, its marked phases, exact evenness and retained physical source decomposition; complex growth diagnostics supplement the uniform proof above.

The finite audit checks six original physical source families N=5,…,10, each with five genuine boxes, retained color deficits, all E/F/R normalizations and the full marked transfer. The same packet is independently evaluated by the full physical pivotal trace and the Hermite coefficient-residue functional. Its low-index and RP residuals are checked as exact separate source terms. Four further canonical complex-color families verify separate exact evenness and the unchanged derivative source. These diagnostics do not establish an exponential estimate from finite samples; bounds (5),(7) follow from the proofs above.
