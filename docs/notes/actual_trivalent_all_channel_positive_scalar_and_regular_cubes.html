All-channel positive black scalar and regularity of every surviving source cube

29 October 2026. This is an ALL-LEVEL result for the original trivalent source, at both parities and composite levels. Every allowed three-circle block has a nonzero scalar with an explicit positive factorial evaluation. Consequently the actual saturated source on each surviving block is the regular cube module, cyclically generated by the original nested cups. The previously conditional three-circle rank bound is now an equality.

This is a local source theorem. It does not identify a sewn graph with a hyperbolic manifold, bound the full black magnetic tensor by its scalar, or replace the sole marked white co-source by an ordinary cap.

1. Exact source scalar at every retained triple

Put N>=2, d=N-1, M=floor(d/2), q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, s_u=product_(v=1)^u 2sin(pi v/N), s_0=1. The retained pair labels are 0<=a,b,c<=M. Use the original coproduct columns

H^(j)_k=q^(k^2)[d-j choose k]_Q,
W^(j)_r=F_pair^r H^(j)/(Q;Q)_r.

At a triangle triple, the black coefficient extracted by the rows W^dagger P with magnetic indices (2a,b+c-a,0) is N^3 A_N(a,b,c). Its physical pair degrees are (d+a,d-a+c,d-c). These are the exact columns and cup ordering of the general source-corner formula.

Write J=a+b+c, delta=J-d and

e=2a^2-ab-ac+bc-J-3.

The actual scalar vanishes for J<d. For J>=d, the triangle inequalities are automatic because a,b,c<=M, and

A_N(a,b,c)=(-1)^(N+J)q^e C_N(a,b,c) R_N(a,b,c),
C_N=s_a s_b s_c/(N s_delta),                     (1)

where

R_N=N sum_(max(a,b,c)<=t<=min(a+b,a+c,b+c))
   s_t/[s_(t-a)s_(t-b)s_(t-c)s_(d+t-J)
                     s_(a+b-t)s_(a+c-t)s_(b+c-t)]. (2)

All summands and C_N are STRICTLY POSITIVE. Every factorial index lies in 0,…,d, and the summation interval is nonempty. In particular

A_N(a,b,c)!=0 if and only if J>=d                 (3)

within the triangle range; the full black tensor also vanishes outside the triangle range. The phase in (1) belongs to the stated ordered corner; the modulus is symmetric in a,b,c.

There are two own finite proofs of (1). Both begin with the exact root-Fourier source coefficient, including the low-mode cancellation that proves its zero branch. The short square-linearization proof uses finite product induction and (U;Q)_N=1-U^N. The independent Jackson/Newton proof uses finite Leibniz, Newton interpolation and Vandermonde. Each reaches the same symmetric terminating series through an independently proved finite Sears transformation. Its terms have one exact phase, which cancels to positive sines. The proofs retain all normalizing factors; (1) is not inferred from finite tests or from a published 6j formula.

2. General regular-cube theorem for the actual saturated source

Let S_N be the ACTUAL six-head saturated invariant source and let A_1,A_2,A_3 be its three disjoint pair Casimirs. For a folded joint cluster j=(a,b,c), write E_j for the regular cluster idempotent and S_j=E_j S_N. Let r be the number of DOUBLE axes; an odd central Steinberg axis is simple. On a double axis put epsilon_i=A_i-c_(j_i). The previously proved saturated support theorem gives

epsilon_i^2=0,
R_r=C[epsilon_1,...,epsilon_r]/(epsilon_1^2,...,epsilon_r^2),
dim S_j=2^r when a+b+c>=d.                       (4)

The complete ordinary circle acts by a nonzero multiple of epsilon_i on a double axis, and by N on a simple axis. Outside a+b+c>=d, the product of the three complete circles is zero by the cleared missing-corner identity on the ACTUAL torsion-free source lattice.

For EVERY surviving cluster, put

v_j=E_j(p_16 p_23 p_45).

Then the map

R_r -> S_j, f -> f v_j                           (5)

is an isomorphism. Thus the original nested cup is a cyclic generator of every surviving regular cube; no choice of a semisimple root frame or a guessed generic lattice is involved.

Proof. The exact all-degree circle factorization is L_j=F_j F_j^dagger P. Its columns F_j are independent, with the actual first-normal CD weight already included. The joint image of v_j under the three circles is (F_a tensor F_b tensor F_c)U_j. Equation (1) supplies a nonzero actual coefficient of U_j. Tensor products of the injective F_j maps are injective, so this image is nonzero. Equivalently,

epsilon_1 ... epsilon_r v_j !=0.                 (6)

Every nonzero ideal of R_r contains its one-dimensional socle C epsilon_1…epsilon_r: multiply a nonzero ideal element by a maximal product of the epsilon_i that leaves a nonzero result, and then use the monomial basis to identify the common annihilator of all epsilon_i. Consequently Ann(v_j)=0 by (6). The map (5) is injective, and the equal dimensions in (4) make it an isomorphism. For r=0 this is simply the nonzero one-dimensional simple block. This proves the theorem.

3. Exact three-circle rank

On a surviving regular cube the top product has rank ONE. Let

K=3M-d.

The number of surviving folded triples is the number of nonnegative h_1,h_2,h_3 with sum h_i<=K, by h_i=M-j_i. Since K<=M, their individual upper bounds impose no additional restriction. Therefore

rank[(L_N(A_1)L_N(A_2)L_N(A_3))|S_N]
   =binom(K+3,3) if K>=0, and 0 if K<0.          (7)

Equivalently the rank is binom(m+3,3) at odd N=2m+1, and binom(m+1,3) at even N=2m (zero at N=2). This closes the former interior/even nonvanishing gap in the support theorem. It does NOT make the three-ordinary-circle quotient faithful on the whole marked source: the previously proved marked radical obstruction is unchanged.

4. Actual scalar packet and its sharp color maximum

The joint scalar partition theorem now applies to the original scalar, through (1). With j0=v8/pi,

|A_N(j)|<=poly(N)exp(Nj0).

For every fixed eta>0, labels with max_i(1/2-j_i/N)>=eta satisfy a strict gap poly(N)exp[N(j0-gamma_eta)]. Unsupported labels are exactly zero. The normalizer C_N is at most 1 (cos(pi/N) at the even maximum), so it preserves the global maximum but cannot be omitted from the local action.

More precisely let j_i=(N-1)/2-N sigma_i. On a fixed small complex polydisk, the EXACT positive factorial window, after its known source phase has been factored, has

N^(-3/2)exp[N(Phi(sigma)+Gamma(sigma))]
               /[4sqrt(prod_i cos(pi sigma_i))] *[1+O(1/N)]. (8)

The source phase is exactly exp(i pi(N+J)+i pi e/N); it must be restored before any coherent sewing. Here L(u)=integral_0^u Log(2sin pi t)dt, a_i=1/2-sigma_i, S=sum a_i,

Phi=2[L(S/2)-sum_i L(S/2-a_i)],
Gamma=sum_i L(a_i)-L(S-1).

Their real central maximum is j0, their joint Hessian is -2pi I, and the completed packet has the known nonzero amplitude in (8). Phi is the four-angle Lobachevsky functional; Gamma is the necessary source normalizer action. Neither is by itself an identified graph complement volume. These sigma_i are INTERNAL pair labels, not automatically peripheral meridian coordinates.

5. What remains after this local theorem

The full magnetic normalization recovers the whole black tensor from (1) and its ordinary 3j tensor. Its coefficients can have EXTRA exponential growth: an actual odd central coefficient already has rate 5j0/4. Thus (8) is not a bound for the whole CD vertex norm.

The all-channel marked contraction now contracts that extra magnetic growth coherently for EVERY supported triple in the specified two-vertex wiring, including all paired normals. Its whole-label signed asymptotic then sums all physical labels with a nonzero Gaussian coefficient. A general graph still needs its actual ordinary/marked white tensors, paired normals, ordered sewing, noncancellation and complete geometric face/peripheral data. The regular-cube theorem supplies the correct local source lattice; it does not replace these global requirements.

The scalar identities for all channels do not imply a general volume-conjecture theorem.
