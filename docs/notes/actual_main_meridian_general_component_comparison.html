Main-meridian estimates for arbitrary Conway product regions

1 November 2026.

A fixed Conway construction joins two-string tangles by ordered products and physical quarter-turns. Consecutive products with no turn between them form one product junction. A junction is bare when it carries no original crossing-circle operator. This note treats an arbitrary finite connected region of such junctions: their number and their valences are unrestricted, and both are fixed before the level tends to infinity.

The main meridian moves by an amount of order 1/N1/N. That deformation changes the polynomial relation in some orientation types, so a whole-junction trace can acquire terms absent at completeness. A comparison with the complete trace at the same indices is false in general. The proof instead separates the trace difference according to its high indices. With one high index, an exact Fourier bound reflects that index and an exact Frobenius pairing fuses two junctions in a positive comparison tree. With several high indices, a half-label projector supplies a boundary comparison. In each case the full neighboring branches remain coupled, and the comparison pressure is bounded by the pressure of the original construction.

The clean part uses complete bare tensors with the original deformed edge factors, boundary arrays and typed carriers retained. It is not the invariant evaluated at the complete meridian. The grouped remainder has a strict exponential loss. Sections 1–7 prove this source statement and its passage through the actual circle operators. Sections 8–10 verify the original wire-packet and filling interfaces. Together with those established proofs, the result removes the bare-component restriction from the filled meridian-profile and volume theorem. All its strictness, knot, framing, fixed-parity and sufficiently-long fixed-twist hypotheses remain.

The fused trees occur only in upper estimates. No isotopy or equality of knot complements is inferred from the fusion identity, and the final volume and preferred cusp always belong to the original filled knot.

9. What is needed to pass to the filled invariant

The new component estimate supplies the missing global source error bound. To obtain the filled nonzero asymptotic one must still use all of the established interfaces, in their original order:

  1. The regular physical circle trace and its canonical Gamma wire interpolant retain the exact type, Cartan scalar, ordinary-circle normalization and the chosen entire continuation. They are the proofs in regular physical circle trace and direct crossing trace.
  2. The finite original wire packet, its strict complex saddle, and its real-spectator angular Gaussian use the unchanged local window described in Section 8. All hidden unturned variables and physical-edge insertions remain in that packet.
  3. The joint filling transform retains every charge.
