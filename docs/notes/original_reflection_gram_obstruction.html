The original source has no automatic positive reflection form

27 October 2026. A universal reflection/Gram argument for the ORIGINAL Kashaev source fails at every level: its identity value is zero while an actual normalized unknot word has nonzero value. A two-channel reflection square has source -2. The standard alternating three-strand weaving transfer also cannot be made a positive Hermitian operator, at any level N>=3, by a positive metric or a scalar phase.

These are obstructions to AUTOMATIC positivity of the original word source or transfer. They do not rule out a separately proved positive formula for a particular knot family, a word-dependent source elimination, an explicitly projected positive channel, or the existing positive squared norm of the original invariant. They do not disprove the volume conjecture.

1. The original source is not a positive functional

Fix q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, N>=2, complete color x=-q-1=q(N-1), and retain the original pivot

K e_a=x Q^-a e_a, g=K^(1-N).

For b>=2 the original reduced one-cut source on actual braid intertwiners is

t_b(A)=Tr[(P_0 tensor g^(tensor(b-1)))A].      (1)

The all-ones first output row gives the same value on these integer-weight-preserving words. The full source is its original nonzero dimension factor d_N(1)=i^(N+3)/N, with the original zero-framing line. No ordinary quantum dimension is divided out.

Since Q has exact order N,

Tr(g)=x^(1-N)sum_(a=0)^(N-1)Q^-a=0,
t_b(I)=0.                                    (2)

Let B_i be the original checked crossing with its own common Cartan factor recorded, and theta its original twist factor. Use the actual zero-framing normalized generator Bhat_i=theta^-1 B_i. The ordinary positive Markov chain

W_b=Bhat_(b-1)...Bhat_1

closes to the unknot and has

t_b(W_b)=t_b(W_b^-1)=1.                       (3)

This can be checked directly without a knot-polynomial formula. At each last-strand pivotal closure the actual positive crossing has partial trace theta I; its inverse has theta^-1 I. For the positive crossing evaluate on the first highest vector: the closure forces both local indices zero, leaving g_0. For the inverse evaluate on the first lowest vector: only its zero transfer survives, leaving theta^-1. The root scalar-closure identity makes those the whole partial traces. Repeating the closures proves (3), with every original factor retained.

No-go theorem. There is no involution dagger on a unital complex algebra containing these actual words, and no nonzero scalar multiple of t_b, which makes it a positive linear functional on all A^dagger A.

Indeed a positive functional obeys Cauchy–Schwarz. Its value on the identity is zero by (2), so its value on every element would be zero. Equation (3) contradicts this. Equivalently, if the reflection Gram matrix on the channels I,W_b were Hermitian, its upper-left entry would be zero and its off-diagonal entry nonzero. Its determinant is negative.

For the usual anti-linear inverse-word reflection, W_b^dagger =W_b^-1. The explicit formal braid combination A=I-W_b gives

A^dagger A=2I-W_b-W_b^-1,
t_b(A^dagger A)=-2.                           (4)

This is an actual source evaluation of a linear combination of original braid words. It is not an invented negative test functional. The full dimension/phase line multiplies both positive and negative directions by the same fixed nonzero scalar; no phase can turn their indefinite Gram form positive.

2. The original cups and caps have the same obstruction

The actual nonzero cup and cap obey

cap cup=Tr(g)=0.

They therefore cannot be Hermitian adjoints in a positive-definite reflection structure: that identity would be the squared norm of the nonzero cup. This is the actual isotropic cup/cap channel already exhibited in the sewn-source construction. One marked cap changes its specified channel and gives the normalized unknot value, but it does not convert ALL ordinary cup/cap pairings in a multi-bridge plat into positive adjoint pairings.

Thus drawing a braid or plat as two reflected halves is not by itself a proof that its original scalar is a sum of absolute squares. A valid restricted formula must prove how its actual co-source becomes the required adjoint on the particular image subspace, with the marked cap and all other turns retained.

3. Even the basic alternating weaving transfer is not positive

Let T_N=B_12 B_23^-1 on three original same-color strands. Its two crossing scalars and zero-framing factors cancel, since the word has writhe zero. It is the block repeated in the alternating weaving words (sigma_1 sigma_2-1)m.

Consider its invariant physical total-index-one subspace with basis |100>,|010>,|001>. Literal crossing coefficients give

T_N|_1=[[1-Q,0,1],
         [-q,0,0],
         [0,-q^-1,1-Q^-1]].                  (5)

For example the local index-one crossing block is [[1-Q,-q],[-q,0]] in the ordered pair |10>,|01>. The two coefficients are rho/x=1-Q and x^-1=-q at x=-q^-1. Multiplying the two adjacent blocks gives (5). This is a computation in the original head, without a proposed positive replacement crossing.

Its exact characteristic polynomial is

(lambda-1)
  [lambda^2-(1-Q-Q^-1)lambda+1].             (6)

At N=3 it is (lambda-1)^3 but the matrix in (5) is not identity. Thus it is nontrivially unipotent and cannot be self-adjoint in any positive-definite Hermitian metric.

At every N>=4 the two other roots are exp(+/-i theta_N), where

cos(theta_N)=[1-2cos(2pi/N)]/2,
pi/3<=theta_N<2pi/3.

They are nonreal. Every positive-metric self-adjoint operator has real spectrum. Consequently T_N cannot be a positive Gram operator C^dagger C after any similarity. Multiplying it by a scalar phase cannot help: its eigenvalue 1 forces that phase to be positive real, leaving the nonreal pair unchanged.

Nor does a fixed power remove the obstruction. For any fixed m>=1, the positive eigenvalue condition for T_N^m would require m theta_N to be an integer multiple of 2pi. The angle theta_N is strictly increasing to 2pi/3, so this cannot hold for all sufficiently large N. Indeed each admissible multiple can occur at most once. A fixed alternating weave block therefore cannot be converted into a positive transfer at all levels by taking a fixed number of repetitions.

This statement concerns the transfer as an operator. A positive scalar identity for a particular closure could still result from source cancellation and a nontrivial finite-sum identity, as the actual figure-eight proof demonstrates. Operator positivity is stronger than that scalar conclusion.

4. What remains possible for a positive high-weight channel

The unit-circle eigenpair in (6) does not exclude a separately constructed positive spectral channel elsewhere in the tensor module. Such a route would require at least:

  1. An explicit source-compatible projection or relative source reduction on the actual word, rather than deleting the nonpositive channels from the invariant by definition.
  2. A proved positive representation of the retained scalar with its original marked cap, phases and peripheral line.
  3. A bound showing that omitted channels cannot cancel the desired leading contribution, plus a nonzero coupling to the calibrated geometric critical class.

The existing positive trace for ordinary colors does not automatically give these facts on the Kashaev projective color. Its quantum dimension is precisely zero, while the modified source detects its nonsemisimple data. Nor does Rivin uniqueness establish positivity of this quantum source. No new positive high-weight channel satisfying these conditions has been constructed here.

There IS already a valid positive formulation for all knots: the original cyclic norm satisfies H_N=|K_N|^2/N+O(poly(N)), and the pointed A-Gram theorem expresses its equivalent pointed norm by positive polynomial coefficients. Those constructions retain the actual source and target. They are not an ordinary reflection-positive word trace, and their geometric exponential estimate and source overlap remain open. The present obstruction does not invalidate them.

5. Exact checks and scope

The audit and saved results check 17 literal original-source cases, braid indices two through four, including the normalized positive unknot chain, its actual inverse and (4). Seven exact root tests check the literal invariant block (5). Its characteristic polynomial is also checked symbolically. The complex positivity conclusion comes from the all-level proof, not from an inference about finite-field signs.

This rules out the automatic Gram/reflection route and its basic alternating-transfer version. It neither rules out a separately proved positive family nor supplies a hyperbolic volume lower bound. A genuine selected geometric-cycle or source-compatible positive-channel theorem is still needed.
