The complete source has a nonzero local marked density and folded Gaussian

30 October 2026. Fix a certified augmented plat, its original exposed mark, and the complete geometric chart with one exceptional matching edge. The proved ordinary and joint deficient packets give a nonzero LOCAL density of the actual complete root source. The paired-circle normal is already in this statement. Its folded channel sum has a nonzero local Gaussian coefficient at BOTH level parities.

This is separate from the coefficient of a geometric saddle in the original global period. The two framing directions, the physical support cycles, crossed divisors and competing sectors remain. It is also a complete-color statement, not a mixed auxiliary-color or filling packet.

1. Start with the actual mark, rather than differentiating one coupon

Write d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), x=q^d, M=floor(d/2), and let R be the fixed number of original complete-circle coupons. In a low physical degree t, the EXACT fixed-channel coefficient of the circle is

C_(N,a)^epsilon(t;u,v)
  =kappa^epsilon x^(-t) nu_a^(epsilon-1)
      h_(t,a) f_a(t,u) f_a(t,v),
h_(t,a)=k_a s_t^2/[s_(t-a)s_(t+a-d)],
nu_a=(-1)^a q^[a(a+1)],
k_a=1 if 2a=d, and k_a=2 otherwise.                 (1)

Use the literal translated high block when needed. Equation (1) is the complete root compiler, including the full first-normal circle coefficient; it is not a value-only spectral projector. In particular the original scalar and its endpoint values are

h0_a=k_a N s_(2a)/s_a^2,
h0_M=N                       (N odd),
h0_M=2N/sin(pi/N)             (N even).             (2)

The latter formula follows from s_(N-2)=N/[2sin(pi/N)] and s_(N/2-1)^2=N/2. The vanishing paired retraction pi_a W_a=0 does NOT make (1) zero. The original nilpotent circle is h0_a W_a pi_a, whose displayed scalar already contains that circle’s normal.

In a 2k-head plat, keep all k original bottom cups, k-1 ordinary top caps, and the unrestricted original marked top row. Degree conservation forces the last pair to have degree d only AFTER the other caps act. Their product then has scale

N^k N^(-(k-1)) N^-2=N^-1,                          (3)

and an explicitly retained nonzero monomial in x. Thus the marked ROOT compiler is a finite sum of products of (1) and this monomial. It contains no further local q derivative. This direct use of the original mark is essential: nonzero E_a at one block would not by itself evaluate

F_Delta'(0)/nu_N,

the alternative whole-network ordinary-trace normal. That identity is proved only after the complete original closure. We do not differentiate one factor, assign a normal to each vertex, or move the bare mark.

2. The full normalization gives N^-1 per circle at the fold

Put tau=t/N, alpha=a/N, eta=tau+alpha-1, zeta=tau-alpha. First work on a fixed ordinary physical compact, with alpha in a fixed real interval ending at 1/2. The finite source keeps its actual alpha. The original sine-factorial continuation G_N, on its marked strip, gives

hhat_(t,a)=k_a G_N(N tau)^2/
               [G_N(N zeta) G_N(N eta+1)].          (4)

This is (1) on its supported physical grid. The endpoint +1 comes from d=N-1. The elementary uniform expansion

G_N(Nw)=sqrt(2N sin(pi w)) exp[N J(w)](1+O(N^-1))

and the exact sine recurrence at N eta+1 give

hhat_(t,a)=k_a b_h(tau,alpha) exp[N H(tau,alpha)]
                                            (1+O(N^-1)),
H=2J(tau)-J(zeta)-J(eta),
b_h=sin(pi tau)/[2sin(pi eta)
                     sqrt(sin(pi zeta)sin(pi eta))]. (5)

All sine factors here are units on the compact; their branches are the actual continued ones. In particular b_h remains nonzero and smooth at alpha=1/2. The large even value in (2) has canceled against the actual CD/column normalization. It is not an extra N in the coupon density.

The full ordinary packet satisfies fhat_a=N^-1/2 B_alpha exp(N Phi_alpha)(1+O(N^-1)), where B_alpha is nowhere zero, uniformly through alpha=1/2. Hence (1) has the actual relative packet

C_(N,a)^epsilon
   =kappa^epsilon k_a N^-1 b_epsilon
                  exp[N Theta_epsilon^lit](1+O(N^-1)),
Theta_epsilon^lit=H+Phi_alpha(tau,u/N)+Phi_alpha(tau,v/N)
                    +pi i[-tau+(epsilon-1)(alpha+alpha^2)],
b_epsilon=b_h B_alpha(tau,u/N) B_alpha(tau,v/N)
                      exp[pi i(tau+(epsilon-1)alpha)]. (6)

The exact kappa and retained outside framing line remain outside this formula. The -tau in (6) uses literal x-t=q-dt; the alternative +tau differs by the recorded degree character exp(-2pi i t). No character is discarded when comparing (6) to the marked stationary action. Every factor of b_epsilon is nonzero. Errors and fixed derivatives are uniform on smaller compacts, with smooth dependence on real alpha.

The exceptional circle has the SAME assertion. Indeed its correctly restored entire coefficient is exactly

C_a(t;r,s)=k_a beta_a^2 V_a(d-t) q^[d(r+s-t)]
                  R_a(q^(t-2r);c_t) R_a(q^(t-2s);c_t).

Its scalar-one reflection sends (t,r,s) to (N-t-2,r+N-t-1,s+N-t-1). At negative determinant arguments the reflected values lie in the ordinary physical packet (6). The joint deficient theorem then supplies a nowhere-zero relative packet for each ratio R_a(z)/R_a(z_*), uniformly for the actual real alpha through the fold. The remaining ratio is the EXACT nonzero monomial q1. Multiplying these ratios propagates (6)’s absolute N^-1 scale and nonzero amplitude to the actual exceptional geometric compact. Different bounded marked logarithmic lifts retain their exact monomials; no root of a squared character is chosen afresh.

This argument includes both beta factors, the residual sine product, the finite reflection offset -2, and the original fold weight k_a. There is no assertion that the individually large beta factors have a smooth nonzero endpoint asymptotic before that cancellation. The literal generic lift has the additional root character exp(-2pi i v); restoring the actual physical turn cancels it, giving precisely C_a above. Thus it does not introduce an additional zero or unknown scalar in the restored density.

3. A nonzero fixed-channel marked density

Use the proved actual geometric frame, slightly deformed if needed to make the exceptional degree nonreal. All ordinary factors are on their selected physical sheets, and the exceptional pair is in the joint deficient corridor on its reflected selected sheet. These conditions are strict and hold on a common neighborhood. The internal q-Hahn roots have already been summed by their actual finite relative packets.

Choose the original free magnetic coordinates y after the affine wire constraints. Retain every finite d=N-1 offset and every original cup, cap, degree and Poisson character. For a fixed vector of actual channels alpha=(a_1/N,…,a_R/N) near (1/2,…,1/2), the continued marked ROOT summand therefore has

D_N(y,alpha)=U_N N^(-R-1) product_i k_(a_i)
      b_N(y,alpha) exp[N F(y,alpha)](1+O(N^-1)).   (7)

Here U_N is only the explicitly recorded head-independent unit phase. The original external line, if any, is kept separately. The functions b_N are holomorphic in y, smooth in the real alpha, nowhere zero, and bounded above and away from zero with fixed derivatives on smaller compacts. They include the nonzero order-one exponentials from the finite affine offsets. Such offsets may be kept literally instead; expanding them changes only b_N and its relative O(N^-1) error.

The proof is multiplication of the FIXED number R of packets (6), their exceptional continuation, and the exact marked factor (3). This proves nonzero local density for the complete fixed-channel source, not merely nonzero separate polynomial values. It uses a direct root marked compiler, rather than an unjustified inference about the derivative of an ordinary closed density. The specified meromorphic extension agrees with the original source on its supported grid; its local use does not move any support-dependent residue cycle.

4. The actual endpoint lattice has a nonzero local folded Gaussian

Fix a point y0 of the complete geometric framing orbit in this chart. Use the FULL marked channel stationarity: the channel derivatives of the unadjusted phase are 2pi i n_i with fixed integers n_i. Subtract these exact characters. This changes no actual channel summand because exp(2pi i n_i a_i)=1. In these marked conventions,

F_alpha(y0,1/2)=0,
F_(alpha_i,alpha_j)(y0,1/2)=delta_ij H_i,
H_i=-4pi i/tau_i, Re H_i<0,                        (8)

where tau_i are the actual oriented primitive cusp shapes. Half twists are included: H_i=-2pi/c_i+2pi i epsilon_i in the rectangular convention. The channel Hessian theorem also proves F_(alpha,y)=0 at the endpoint. Negative real part alone would not substitute for the first equality in (8).

The selected branch here is fixed as a neighborhood germ by the full ordinary anchor and the scalar-one deficient reflection, not merely by matching endpoint magnetic momenta. Thus its channel derivatives are the derivatives of the same actual marked principal phase. Explicitly, F=F_raw-2pi i sum_i n_i alpha_i is unchanged in the exponential at every actual grid point; at alpha=1/2 its exponential includes the known unit exp(-pi i N sum_i n_i). Alternatively use the centered F_c=F_raw-2pi i sum_i n_i(alpha_i-1/2), and place that compensating unit in U_N. These conventions give the same (9); the unit is not erased.

Let 0<delta<1/6, and sum (7) only over the actual folded labels with 0<N/2-a_i<=N^(1/2+delta). This is a sum of SPECIFIED local continued density values at y0, not the original magnetic period. It has

sum_local D_N(y0,a/N)
  =U_N N^(-R/2-1) b_N(y0,1/2) exp[N F(y0,1/2)]
         *(2pi)^(R/2)/product_i sqrt(-H_i)
                              *(1+O(N^-1/2)).       (9)

Each square root is selected by continuation from H_i<0 real; Re H_i<0 makes it nonzero. This coefficient is nonzero at both parities.

Here is an elementary lattice proof retaining the endpoint weights. For odd N, the numbers w=N/2-a are 1/2,3/2,5/2,…; their weights are 1 at w=1/2 and 2 thereafter. For even N they are 1,2,3,…, all with weight 2. In either case, for Re H<0,

N^-1/2 sum_w k_a exp[H w^2/(2N)]
    =2 integral_0^infinity exp(H z^2/2) dz+O(N^-1/2)
    =sqrt(2pi)/sqrt(-H)+O(N^-1/2).                 (10)

The half-step shift and the one exceptional odd weight affect only the O(N^-1/2) error. A direct interval Riemann estimate, using the integrable derivative of the Gaussian, proves this uniformly on compact subsets of Re H<0. The omitted tail is exponentially small in N^(2delta). Taylor’s formula gives the quadratic diagonal in (8); the error in N F is O(|w|3/N2). On the stated window it tends uniformly to zero. Its Gaussian-weighted absolute sum, and the corresponding variation of b_N, are O(N^-1/2) relative to N^(R/2). This proves (9), including complex H_i; no positivity of the complex terms is assumed.

The actual half-twist phase has an independent exact lattice check. Set s=d-2a, s_min=0 at odd N and 1 at even N, and a_max=M. Then

nu_a/nu_(a_max)
        =exp[pi i(s^2-s_min^2)/(4N)].              (11)

Indeed the integer character in (-1)^a cancels the linear term in a(a+1) along this parity-restricted lattice. Thus a retained signed half twist contributes its specified quadratic phase, with no alternating nearest-channel factor. Formula (9) does not require an exact even extension of the finite density across the folded endpoint; its one-sided Riemann proof uses the actual two parity lattices directly.

No finite noninteger-channel interpolation has been used. Only the proved uniform packet at ACTUAL discrete a_i, its smooth coefficient functions, and the original channel spacing enter. The window has s_i=d-2a_i=O(N^(1/2+delta)), so it is also inside the already proved s_i log N=o(N) generic-normal upper-bound window. That latter bound is not needed to obtain the root local coefficient (9).

5. What the magnetic quotient Hessian does and does not add

At the certified geometric point the actual critical scheme, section 7, identifies the magnetic Hessian radical with exactly the two-dimensional T_diag framing tangent space. On a holomorphic slice transverse to this orbit the magnetic Hessian is nondegenerate. Together with (7) and a nonsingular coordinate Jacobian, this gives a nonzero transverse Gaussian density PER UNIT local framing volume: its coefficient divides the nonzero numerator by the chosen square root of the transverse Hessian determinant. A small local descending Morse cycle for those transverse variables has that nonzero coefficient.

This does not integrate the framing orbit or assign its measure to the finite source. Nor does a local Morse cycle establish its coefficient in the original physical cycle. In particular every real forbidden integer degree slice of the exceptional source has zero original period, despite the nonzero continued density away from its real lattice. The degree/cotangent transport and its residue terms cannot be omitted.

Thus the unresolved question is not an unspecified zero of a local complete-circle amplitude: fixed-channel density (7) and the marked local folded sum (9) are nonzero. The remaining global work includes the actual degree/magnetic cycle and framing integral, its boundary terms and competing contributions, and any mixed-color extension. No nonzero individual genuine q-normal density is claimed or required by this direct root marked-density theorem.

Focused audit: source normalizations and actual folded lattices, saved results. It checks 49 original h0 endpoint values, 674 exact fold half-twist ratios, five literal unrestricted marked plat contractions, eighteen signed full-coupon relative packets at both parities, and eighteen complex-Gaussian parity calibrations. The source comparisons use the literal terminating columns independently of their determinant formulas. The Gaussian calibrations test the elementary lattice lemma, not a global source period. Uniformity and the exceptional continuation follow from the proofs above and their already audited source dependencies.

The conclusion is a nonzero complete local density. It does not supply a global period, framing integral, mixed-color packet, or an individual nonzero generic-normal amplitude.

The magnetic transverse statement is per unit local framing volume. No original cycle coefficient or mixed-color theorem is inferred.
