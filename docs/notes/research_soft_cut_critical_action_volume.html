Every regular soft-cut critical action has its representation volume

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The regular principal critical scheme of the actual binomial-cut state is already identified in S. This note calibrates every one of its boundary-parabolic critical actions, with the integer Fourier correction made before taking its imaginary part. The result applies to competing representations, not only the complete geometric one. Copies of the same projective holonomy have the same exponential rate and are not separated by this result.

The complete proof directs are S, Q, C and G, pinned below. The general crossing-chain and logarithmic regulator arguments are already present in C and Q. Their extension beyond faithful representations is also explicit in the earlier coverage note actual_wall_critical_regulator.md, Section 5. The new adapter here is the soft-cut entropy and its integral characters.

1. The actual principal action and its lattice

Fix the literal finite braid of an oriented hyperbolic knot KK, with all signs, tail pivots, writhe compensation and reduced normalization retained. Work at the physical root h=2πi/N,c0=2πi,M=1.(1) h=2\pi i/N,\qquad c_0=2\pi i,\qquad M=1. \qquad\text{(1)} Use independent integral coordinates kik_i on the incidence and occupation lattice of its finite array, and unwrapped logarithms ζi=hki\zeta_i=h k_i. Integral affine offsets, if used in choosing coordinates, remain part of these original integer occupations. They are not discarded by taking cyclic residues.

Write ν=log⁡M\nu=\log M for the meridian logarithm. Q supplies the actual leading action S0(ζ,ν)=G0(ζ,ν)+∑jϵjLi⁡2(zj),(2) S_0(\zeta,\nu)=G_0(\zeta,\nu) +\sum_j\epsilon_j\operatorname{Li}_2(z_j), \qquad\text{(2)} where the shapes are monomials with modulus-one constants. The polynomial G0G_0 is a real quadratic form plus a purely imaginary linear form and a real constant. It includes the Cartan, pivot, original framing and Li⁡2(1)\operatorname{Li}_2(1) terms; it is not an unspecified primitive of the gluing equations. The logarithms and dilogarithms are continued compatibly.

Let uu be the cut logarithmic occupation, an integral affine linear function of the ζi\zeta_i. S proves that the soft action is Ssoft=S0+g(u),g(u)=c0[−(u/c0)log⁡(u/c0)−(1−u/c0)log⁡(1−u/c0)−log⁡2].(3) S_{\mathrm{ soft}}=S_0+g(u),\qquad g(u)=c_0[-(u/c_0)\log(u/c_0) -(1-u/c_0)\log(1-u/c_0)-\log2]. \qquad\text{(3)} Its distinguished local sheet near u=c0/2u=c_0/2 has g(c0/2)=g′(c0/2)=0.(4) g(c_0/2)=g'(c_0/2)=0. \qquad\text{(4)} The finite mean is (N−1)/2(N-1)/2, not N/2N/2; all its finite shifts and normal corrections remain in the exact source of S. Equation (3) is its principal coefficient action.

In this note a regular critical point means a point in S’s localized canonical graph, with the internal and soft cut rows imposed. S proves, as an equality of ideals, that these rows are the full braid fixed-point rows together with u=c0/2.(5) u=c_0/2. \qquad\text{(5)} This uses the conserved full ordered K,E,FK,E,F coproduct and the stated unit chart. It is not inferred from a peripheral eigenvalue equation alone. After a common classical momentum gauge the cut is A=−1,p=1A=-1,p=1, with noncentral parabolic meridian. The corresponding projective representation is denoted ρ\rho.

2. The Fourier correction preserves every finite summand

At an exponentiated critical point, on its compatible logarithmic lift, write ∂ζiSsoft=c0ni,ni∈ℤ.(6) \partial_{\zeta_i}S_{\mathrm{ soft}}=c_0 n_i, \qquad n_i\in\mathbb Z. \qquad\text{(6)} The integers are locally constant on a continued critical chart. Define the corrected action Scorr=Ssoft−c0∑iniζi.(7) S_{\mathrm{ corr}}=S_{\mathrm{ soft}}-c_0\sum_i n_i\zeta_i. \qquad\text{(7)} At every original finite lattice state, exp⁡((Scorr−Ssoft)/h)=exp⁡(−2πi∑iniki)=1.(8) \exp((S_{\mathrm{ corr}}-S_{\mathrm{ soft}})/h) =\exp(-2\pi i\sum_i n_i k_i)=1. \qquad\text{(8)} Thus (7) is a permitted Fourier character of the same discrete source, with no new coefficient or endpoint factor. It does not specify a contour or select the charge of a contributing saddle. On an integral affine parametrization, its integer offsets give the same identity, including their retained level dependence.

The entropy sheets can be checked explicitly. If the two logarithms in (3) change by c0j0,c0j1c_0j_0,c_0j_1, respectively, then Δg=−c0(j0−j1)u−c02j1,j0,j1∈ℤ.(9) \Delta g=-c_0(j_0-j_1)u-c_0^2j_1,\qquad j_0,j_1\in\mathbb Z. \qquad\text{(9)} For the actual cut occupation u=hau=ha, c0=Nhc_0=Nh, its exponential multiplier is exp⁡(Δg/h)=exp⁡[−2πi((j0−j1)a+Nj1)]=1.(10) \exp(\Delta g/h) =\exp[-2\pi i((j_0-j_1)a+Nj_1)]=1. \qquad\text{(10)} At (5), Δg=−12c02(j0+j1)∈ℝ,Δg′=−c0(j0−j1).(11) \Delta g=-\tfrac12c_0^2(j_0+j_1)\in\mathbb R,\qquad \Delta g'=-c_0(j_0-j_1). \qquad\text{(11)} The derivative change is absorbed in (6). These are genuine integer characters, not arbitrary complex shifts of the action. The real constant in (9) is retained.

3. Calibration of the imaginary critical value

Let β=∑jϵj[zj]\beta=\sum_j\epsilon_j[z_j] be the actual signed five-shape crossing sum. For compatible branches Q proves D(β)=Im⁡S0−∑iRe⁡ζiIm⁡∂ζiS0−Re⁡νIm⁡∂νS0.(12) D(\beta)=\operatorname{Im}S_0 -\sum_i\operatorname{Re}\zeta_i\, \operatorname{Im}\partial_{\zeta_i}S_0 -\operatorname{Re}\nu\, \operatorname{Im}\partial_\nu S_0. \qquad\text{(12)} This identity is algebraic in the logarithmic differential data: Im⁡Li⁡2(z)=D(z)−log⁡|z|Im⁡log⁡(1−z)\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Li}_2(z) =D(z)-\log|z|\operatorname{Im}\log(1-z), while the imaginary part of a real quadratic polynomial with imaginary linear part is the real-coordinate pairing with its imaginary gradient. Compatible dilogarithm monodromy contributes its integer logarithms and a real constant, so the same identity persists.

On the distinguished entropy sheet, (4)–(5) imply that Im⁡g=0\operatorname{Im}g=0 and that every internal derivative of gg vanishes. Since Re⁡ν=0\operatorname{Re}\nu=0 at M=1M=1, substitution of (6)–(7) into (12) gives Im⁡Scorr=D(β).(13) \operatorname{Im}S_{\mathrm{ corr}}=D(\beta). \qquad\text{(13)} For another entropy sheet, (11) gives a real value of gg at the same point. Its internal derivative changes by the integer covector already corrected in (7). Also Re⁡u=0\operatorname{Re}u=0; hence the pairing of that derivative with the real internal coordinates is zero. Any retained affine offset in uu is purely imaginary and has the same property. Thus (13) holds on every compatible entropy sheet.

There is no freely adjustable imaginary constant in (13). The original Gaussian/framing constants in Q and the normalized binomial constant in (3) have already fixed it. The preferred longitude and its inverse eigenflag are the ones in Q; their spin signs have not been recentered.

The same conclusion holds for the other complete central lift when carried with the full source conventions. One can either apply (12) with Re⁡log⁡M=0\operatorname{Re}\log M=0, or twist a determinant-one representation by the knot’s meridian sign character. Then xx and each KjK_j change sign together, so Aj=x/KjA_j=x/K_j, transfer ratios and x2x^2 do not change. The crossing Bloch sum is unchanged. This is a comparison of retained classical lifts, not a deletion of quantum signs.

If the full ADO normalization is used, its exact factor remains dN(M)=iN+3MN−11+M2+⋯+M2N−2.(14) d_N(M)=i^{N+3}\frac{M^{N-1}}{1+M^2+\cdots+M^{2N-2}}. \qquad\text{(14)} At M=1M=1 it is iN+3/Ni^{N+3}/N, and at M=−1M=-1 it is iN+3(−1)N−1/Ni^{N+3}(-1)^{N-1}/N. These phases and powers must be kept, and their contribution to the scaled logarithmic modulus is −(2π/N)log⁡N-(2\pi/N)\log N, tending to zero. No off-root normal or generic-meridian gauge is discarded.

4. The crossing regulator is the competing representation volume

C states its final comparison for complete faithful holonomy. Its chain proof applies to every representation obtained above. Here are the ingredients needed to check that extension.

At M=1M=1, the noncentral meridians are unipotent projectively. Their fixed flags and the actual Hurwitz words supply exactly C’s region decorations. Its determinant identities, three five-term boundaries and signed four-tetrahedron crossing balls use unipotence, not faithfulness of ρ\rho. The paired faces and original strand closures are the same abstract oriented relative fundamental cycle, with the degree-one cusp marking and preferred-writhe shear proved in C.

The abstract group in that construction is the knot group, independent of whether its image under ρ\rho is faithful. Choose free auxiliary vertex flags generically. If developed vertices are forced to repeat, use the normalized alternating configuration complex: that tetrahedron is zero, and its repeated-vertex faces cancel in pairs. The five-term map therefore still identifies the signed crossing sum with the ρ\rho-developed fundamental chain. Equivalently one can subdivide and add generic free interior vertex types; their stars are balls and change no fundamental regulator. Continuity D(0)=D(1)=D(∞)=0D(0)=D(1)=D(\infty)=0 gives the same value for any normalized degenerate developed tetrahedra.

The longitude commutes with the nonidentity unipotent meridian. Its projective centralizer consists of the unipotents fixing that flag, so the peripheral image is boundary-parabolic. The regulated idealized chain is consequently the signed representation-volume normalization used in C and G. We obtain D(β)=Vol⁡S3\K(ρ).(15) D(\beta)=\operatorname{Vol}_{S^3\setminus K}(\rho). \qquad\text{(15)} A trivial meridian would force a trivial knot representation by normal generation and give volume zero, but it is outside the noncentral unit midpoint chart used here.

Combining (13) and (15) proves the actual-source calibration Im⁡Scorr=Vol⁡S3\K(ρ),2πNlog⁡|eScorr/h|=Vol⁡S3\K(ρ).(16) \boxed{\displaystyle \operatorname{Im}S_{\mathrm{ corr}} =\operatorname{Vol}_{S^3\setminus K}(\rho),\qquad \frac{2\pi}{N}\log|e^{S_{\mathrm{ corr}}/h}| =\operatorname{Vol}_{S^3\setminus K}(\rho).} \qquad\text{(16)} The second equality evaluates the exponential of the principal action. It is not an asymptotic assertion about the sum (1) in S.

5. What the actual filled-group gap now compares

Fix a hyperbolic marked core and any earlier fixed short partial filling that is already part of that core, in G’s scope. Fill the remaining designated cusps along sufficiently long primitive slopes σi=(ai,bi)\sigma_i=(a_i,b_i), and suppose the resulting physical exterior is the knot exterior under consideration. Use a literal braid of that actual filled knot in S, rather than an unfilled-parent source equipped only with peripheral eigenvalue equations.

Every regular midpoint critical point of this full braid already gives a representation of its actual knot group, hence of the actual filled group. Its remaining main cusp is boundary-parabolic by Section 4. G therefore applies directly. For every such point whose underlying projective representation is not conjugate to the oriented filled geometric holonomy, Im⁡Scorr≤Vol⁡(M(σ))−min⁡{δ2,3c1ℓmax⁡2},ℓmax=maxiai2+bi2.(17) \operatorname{Im}S_{\mathrm{ corr}} \le \operatorname{Vol}(M(\sigma)) -\min\left\{\frac{\delta}{2}, \frac{3c_1}{\ell_{\max}^2}\right\}, \qquad \ell_{\max}=\max_i\sqrt{a_i^2+b_i^2}. \qquad\text{(17)} G’s positive constants depend only on the fixed core and certificate. This is a uniform comparison of the regular principal critical rates of these actual full-word presentations. It needs no matching of a quantum Fourier label to a centered geometric slope charge: G uses the full filled-group relations of the representation.

In contrast, a critical configuration of an unreduced parent partial-filling expression has not been shown by this argument to satisfy those full matrix relations. Applying (17) to such an expression still requires that source-to-group map and its action normalization. The equality of its final invariant with a knot invariant does not identify the separate critical configurations.

6. Equal-rate aliases and remaining source boundaries

Different compatible logarithm or entropy sheets, central lifts, regular framing points, or retained flag descriptions of the same projective representation all have the same value in (16). Their corrected actions can have different real parts. The associated oscillatory phases, multiplicities and cycle coefficients have not been added or shown nonzero. In particular (17) does not separate copies of the geometric holonomy or prevent cancellation among them.

The original compact source cycle of S is retained. Neither its nonstationary boundary contributions, its nonunit/support-wall strata, escaping decorations nor its centralizer relative current is controlled by (16)–(17). No integral upper bound, geometric intersection number, scalar lower bound or new volume-conjecture filling case is inferred.
