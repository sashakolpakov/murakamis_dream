The original-source error as a Euclidean boundary-kernel norm

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

The polynomial Ward primitive sees only a specified vacuum-input block of the marked current. This note gives an exact positive coherent resolution for that block in the physical coefficient Euclidean metric, and an explicit bound for the original-source error by its unnormalized mixed-kernel L2L^2 norm. No change of metric is hidden in the bound.

The sole complete direct W supplies the actual primitive, source identity, and whole-meridian polynomial bounds. The support compression, coherent resolution, exact Hilbert–Schmidt constant, and metric comparison are proved here. They do not estimate the required kernel at a geometric window or select a contributing relative cycle.

5. Why the metric and the mixed kernel matter

The other common coherent metric has ∥wa∥bin2=(da)−1\|w_a\|_{\mathrm{ bin}}^2=\binom da^{-1}, so its orthonormal basis is ua=(da)wau_a=\sqrt{\binom da}\,w_a. It is not the metric of (12)–(22). This distinction is visible in this very primitive. On the root input (a,d−a−1)(a,d-a-1) and output (0,d)(0,d), its matrix entry in the binomial orthonormal basis has modulus (da)(da+1)|1−Q−a−1|.(24) \frac{\sqrt{\binom da\binom d{a+1}}}{|1-Q^{-a-1}|}. \qquad\text{(24)} Take a=⌊(d−1)/2⌋a=\lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor. The largest binomial coefficient is at least 2d/(d+1)2^d/(d+1), its indicated adjacent coefficient is at least half as large, and |1−Q−a−1|≤2|1-Q^{-a-1}|\le2. Thus (24) is at least 2d/(22(d+1))2^d/(2\sqrt2(d+1)). This particular primitive has exponential binomial-metric norm. This does not claim the same lower bound for every possible Ward primitive.

The top source is the mixed corner ⟨d,0|C|0,d⟩\langle d,0|C|0,d\rangle, with spectators closed by their literal pivot. In (12), w0w_0 and wdw_d are the two antipodal coherent endpoints. Their identity overlap is zero for N≥2N\ge2. Consequently (15) uses the unnormalized mixed kernel and never divides by an identity overlap such as (1+wz)d(1+wz)^d.

Local normalized symbol estimates, estimates in the binomial metric, and estimates on a small output disk do not by themselves bound the full integral in (15). The bridge retains the original source and all its true normals, but supplies no geometric window, mixed-kernel decay estimate, opposite-polarization return, or nonzero closing coefficient. No new volume-conjecture case is asserted.
