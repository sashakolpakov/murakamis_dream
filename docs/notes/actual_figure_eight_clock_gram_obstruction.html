Complete figure-eight clock sectors and diagonal Gram metrics

29 October 2026. The exact factorial length frame has a polynomial original clock co-source. Nevertheless one fully anchor-contracted clock sector of the original figure-eight knot has twice the desired volume rate. Consequently every positive DIAGONAL clock metric has an exponentially excessive Cauchy budget on this actual source.

This concerns a COMPLETE clock sector, after its anchor sums and original closure contractions. It is not an expanded crossing state or an arbitrary inserted coupon. It does not disprove Cauchy–Schwarz, the volume conjecture, or a coherent non-diagonal special inner product.

1. The original source and its exact reduced table

Use q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, d=N-1, x=-q^-1, F_a=(Q;Q)_a, s_a=|F_a|. The actual word is [1,-2,1,-2]. Its clocks are the original overclocks (h_0,h_1,h_2,h_3). The whole charged source and actual closure contractions give

K_N=sum_h K_(N;h),
K_(N;h)=(chi_N/N) zeta_(N;h) C_(N;h), |zeta|=1.   (1)

chi_N is the unchanged original external dimension/framing line; at completeness its modulus and inverse have polynomial size. Every common Cartan, pivot and source factor stays in (1). zeta consists only of the explicitly retained powers of x,Q, the crossing Cartan phases and the closure-rescaling monomials. No unknown knot scalar has been divided out.

The full source support gives h_0=0, before any separate-sector Schur argument. In the rescaled anchors of the actual alternating-source table, write B_L(U,V;Q^p)=product_(r<L)(U-Q^(p+r)V). Then

C_(N;h)=[A^d B^d C^d D^d] product of these EIGHT blocks:
tail -> head length p
B -> C d-h_0 0
C -> A h_0 1
D -> A d-h_1 h_1+1
A -> B h_1 -h_1+h_2+1
C -> B d-h_2 h_0+h_2+2
B -> D h_2 -h_2-h_3-1
A -> D d-h_3 -h_1
D -> C h_3 0

This table includes the original pivotal closure substitutions. For auditability, zeta in (1) is obtained without any scalar choice by: the charged_data weight; the normalized_graph tail monomials; the contract_edge closure factors; and the substitutions of anchors B,D by x^-1 B,x^-1 D. Each factor is an original root of unity. Those exact operations are implemented in the audit linked below.

The normalized blocks

b_L=B_L/D_L, D_L=s_L^(1/2)/N^(1/4)

have D_(d-h_i)D_(h_i)=1 at each crossing. Thus the product of all EIGHT table normalizers is exactly one. The coefficient C_(N;h) is also the completely contracted normalized-b clock STATE in this closure-contracted presentation. The co-source remains polynomial, as in the exact clock-source theorem.

2. A uniform family with no internal cancellation

Let

N=3k+1, d=3k, h=(0,2k,k,2k), k>=1,
beta_(L,r)=|[L choose r]_Q|=s_L/(s_r s_(L-r)),
S_k=sum_(r=0)^k beta_(2k,r) beta_(2k,k-r)>0.         (2)

Then the COMPLETE table coefficient is exactly

C_(N;(0,2k,k,2k))=N q^(3k^2-2k) S_k/F_k.          (3)

In particular the original sector modulus is

|K_(N;(0,2k,k,2k))|=|chi_N| S_k/s_k.              (4)

Here is a direct coefficient proof. The full B_d(B,C;1) contracts B=Q^-1 C. Its C -> B loop is C^(2k)N/F_k. Reversing the D -> A interval and concatenating it with the A -> D interval produces B_(2k)(A,D;Q) with reversal factor

xi_1=(-Q^(2k+1))^k Q^[k(k-1)/2].

The replacement in B -> D contributes Q^-k. For the two remaining C/D intervals use

B_k(C,D;Q^(-3k)) B_(2k)(D,C;1)
   =xi_J (C^N-D^N)/(C-Q^(-2k)D),
xi_J=(-1)^(2k)Q^[(2k)(2k-1)/2].                   (5)

For 0<=r<=k its coefficient of C^(k-r)D^(2k+r) is xi_J Q^[-2k(2k+r)]. The remaining A/D extraction is therefore the D^k coefficient of B_(2k)(A,D;Q)^2, times the displayed common root factors. Indeed the second interval has effective start Q^(k-2k-2k)=Q^(-3k)=Q.

The coefficient of A^(2k-r)D^r in B_(2k)(A,D;Q) is

(-q^(2k+1))^r beta_(2k,r).

Consequently every summand of its D^k coefficient has the SAME phase. Combining this phase, (5), xi_1 and Q^-k gives precisely q^(3k^2-2k), proving (3). All finite sums, including the loop factor, have been evaluated; no uncontracted anchor is left.

3. Its exact exponential rate

Put Lambda(theta)=-integral_0^theta log(2sin u)du and v_3=3 Lambda(pi/3). The successive ratios of beta_(2k,r) are

beta_(2k,r+1)/beta_(2k,r)
   =sin((2k-r)pi/N)/sin((r+1)pi/N),

which decrease with r because (2k+1)pi/N<pi. Hence the symmetric summands in S_k are maximal at the integer(s) nearest k/2. The sum lies between that maximum and (k+1) times it. The uniform factorial estimate, including its endpoints, is

log s_a=-(N/pi)Lambda(pi*a/N)+O(log N).

It follows directly that

lim_(k->infinity) (1/N)log(S_k/s_k)
   =[3Lambda(pi/3)+2Lambda(pi/6)]/pi
   =2v_3/pi=Vol(4_1)/pi.                        (6)

The elementary Lobachevsky duplication identity gives 2Lambda(pi/6)=3Lambda(pi/3)=v_3. The identification Vol(4_1)=2v_3 uses its two regular ideal tetrahedra, rather than an asymptotic volume formula for the invariant.

Thus the actual complete sector has

lim (2pi/N)log|K_(N;h)|=2 Vol(4_1).               (7)

This is larger than the desired full-knot rate Vol(4_1). The full original figure-eight scalar does have that latter rate by its separately proved positive finite sum. There must therefore be cancellation between COMPLETE clock sectors in this exact frame.

4. What this forces on a special inner product

For the flat clock energy G_N=sum_h |K_(N;h)|^2, (7) gives

liminf_(N=1 mod3) (pi/N)log G_N >=2 Vol(4_1).       (8)

The same exponent holds for the normalized-b state energy, because the original co-source coefficient in (1) has only polynomial norm. This rules out identifying that flat energy, up to subexponential factors, with a positive geometric angle partition of rate Vol/pi.

More generally let K=sum_h a_h f_h. For EVERY positive diagonal metric G=diag(g_h) the exact Cauchy budget is

B(G)=(sum_h g_h|f_h|^2)(sum_h |a_h|^2/g_h)
     >=(sum_h |a_h f_h|)^2.                      (9)

The infimum over all positive g_h is the right side, obtained by g_h proportional to |a_h|/|f_h|; zero entries follow by limits. In particular (7) makes ALL diagonal-clock Cauchy budgets too large for the desired geometric upper exponent, even when the metric depends arbitrarily on N and on the entire diagram.

For a general positive definite, possibly non-diagonal G, the Riesz vector of the linear co-source sum_h a_h f_h is conj(a). Consequently its budget is

B(G)=(f^*Gf)(a^T G^(-1)conj(a))
   >=||f||^2 ||a||^2/kappa(G).                   (10)

In the original singleton source ||a|| has polynomial upper and lower bounds, while this fixture gives ||f||^2>=exp[2N Vol(4_1)/pi-o(N)]. Therefore a sharp budget B(G)<=exp[N Vol(4_1)/pi+o(N)] requires

log kappa(G)>=N Vol(4_1)/pi-o(N)                  (11)

along this subsequence. Exponential conditioning is allowed: the factorial metrics already exhibit it. Equation (11) is not a nonexistence theorem. It says that a successful coherent Gram has to retain genuine phase cancellation; a diagonal reweighting cannot replace it.

Audit: the literal original reduced table and its exact root-field coefficient, the common-phase formula (3), positive-sum maximum bounds and the large-level rate. The proof above is uniform in all k.
