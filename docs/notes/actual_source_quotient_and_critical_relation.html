The actual source cannot descend by a raw finite critical-algebra quotient

28 October 2026. There is a precise algebraic obstruction to using the new exact sewing as an exact multiplicative quotient onto a fixed geometric critical algebra while retaining every coupon. The original cut pairing is faithful. Moreover an insertion which vanishes on the geometric critical scheme can still have the FULL volume exponent in an actual hyperbolic-knot period.

The latter is proved below for the original figure-eight source: its geometric polynomial p(Z)=Z^2-Z+1 has

N * mu_N(p(Z)p(Z^-1))/K_N -> 2pi sqrt(3).      (1)

Thus the critical relation costs a power of N, not a smaller exponential action. This changes the needed construction: a geometric critical algebra must arise through a specified formal or derived reduction with Gaussian counterterms and source degree, rather than by an exact raw source-preserving algebra quotient or by discarding all insertions of smaller exponential order.

1. A perfect cut excludes every proper exact algebra quotient

Let C be a finite algebra over a field and let tau:C->k have nondegenerate pairing (x,y)->tau(xy). If an algebra quotient pi:C->A carries the same co-source, tau=lambda pi, then pi is injective. Indeed for x in ker pi and every y in C,

tau(xy)=lambda(pi(x)pi(y))=0,

and nondegeneracy gives x=0. The same proof works over a ring when the displayed pairing is perfect.

The actual projective cut theorem applies this hypothesis to

C_(b,R)=End_(E,F,K)(V_M^(tensor b)), R=k[[M-1]],
tau=d_N(M) times the original pivotal first cut.

Its pairing is perfect over R and on the complete fibre, with rank N^(2b-3) for b>=2. Thus there is no proper exact quotient of this full coupon algebra through which that whole source factors. Exact sewing and basis changes do not alter the conclusion, since they retain the original cut.

The statement also survives the normal direction. Modulo u^(J+1), u=M-1, the R-linear trace pairing remains perfect. Even the scalar top-normal co-source

lambda_J(c)=[u^J]tau(c)

has nondegenerate k-pairing lambda_J(cd): choose the lowest nonzero u coefficient of c, pair it nontrivially by tau’s special-fibre perfect form, and multiply the other input by the complementary power of u. Hence an exact algebra quotient preserving this co-source and all normal coupon products is again injective. Retaining divided sources does not justify semisimplifying their algebra.

This excludes a SPECIFIC strong construction. It does not exclude a derived pushforward, a source-compatible corner or restricted insertion algebra, or an action-normalized associated graded. Such constructions must state which operations and currents they retain. Boundary Ward homotopies need not be algebra quotients.

2. An actual complete-colour nilpotent source survives

There is a small exact model inside the original crossing. At N=2 take q=i, Q=-1, x=i in the original balanced frame:

K=diag(i,-i), F=[[0,0],[2,0]], E=[[0,1/2],[0,0]].

Use the original coproduct and normalized Casimir Omega. On two heads it is exactly

Omega=[[0,0,0,0],
       [0,4i,-4,0],
       [0,-4,-4i,0],
       [0,0,0,0]],   Omega^2=0, Omega!=0.

The full two-head centralizer is k[Omega], of dimension two. With the original averaged first-cut Phi and closed pivot (-i,i), direct contraction gives

Phi(1)=0, Phi(Omega)=-4,
Gram_(1,Omega)(Phi(xy))=[[0,-4],[-4,0]].      (2)

Multiplying by the original modified dimension d_2(1)=i/2 retains perfection and makes the offdiagonal entries -2i. The reduced classical Casimir point Omega=0 forgets exactly the nonzero source in (2). For the normalized actual crossing R_0=1+(i/4)Omega one has Phi(R_0)=-i. Its nilpotent term is essential, even though all characters of k[Omega] send it to zero.

This persists in a full original hyperbolic-knot check. On three heads form the original figure-eight word

W=R_(1)^+ R_(2)^- R_(1)^+ R_(2)^-

in its operator-action order. Its writhe is zero, so the crossings’ common Cartan factors cancel. With both original closed pivots and the original first-cut average, exactly

Phi_3(W)=5, Phi_3(1)=0.                       (3)

Replacing each local crossing by its value at Omega_i=0 would give zero instead of five. This replacement is not a proved algebra map of the global word; (3) shows precisely why that local-character substitution cannot represent the original source. No arbitrary source or renormalized model was introduced.

There is a second exact local-to-global warning. At every N, Tr(g)=0 for the complete-colour pivot. Therefore, for an actual local coupon X,

Phi_(b+1)(X tensor I)=Phi_b(X) Tr(g)=0.        (4)

A nonzero local sourced class can be killed by the original global pivotal closure. Nonzero local sine jumps, nonzero Fourier coefficients or nonzero local radial symbols do not by themselves imply a nonzero global geometric image.

3. The exact original figure-eight insertion algebra has rank N

Use its proved whole-source identity:

K_N=sum_(n=0)^(N-1)w_(N,n),
w_(N,n)=|(Q_N;Q_N)_n|^2>0.

On this EXACT reduced whole-source expression retain the literal insertion Z=Q_N^n. Define

mu_N(f)=sum_n w_(N,n) f(Q_N^n),
A_N=C[Z,Z^-1]/(Z^N-1).                      (5)

No change of the unweighted period is made: mu_N(1)=K_N. Every weight in (5) is nonzero. Root evaluation identifies A_N with C^N and its pairing mu_N(fg) with a diagonal pairing whose entries are those weights. It is nondegenerate. Consequently Section 1 applies: no proper multiplicative quotient of A_N retains all these literal inserted moments. In particular no quotient of uniformly bounded rank can do so.

This remains true if the proposed relation has N-dependent coefficients. If mu_N(p q)=0 for every Laurent polynomial q, then p(Q_N^n)=0 for every n. Its representative in A_N is zero, or equivalently p is divisible by Z^N-1. For degrees less than N a nonzero relation is impossible.

This is an exact whole hyperbolic-knot source calculation, stronger than checking an unrelated identity insertion or changing a normalized source functional. It does not assert that (5) is already the geometric critical observable of a different polarization.

4. The geometric relation retains the full exponent

Let p(Z)=Z^2-Z+1. Its two roots are the complete regular ideal shapes. The actual positive source has its unique bulk maximum at x_=5/6, Z_=exp(5pi*i/3), also a root of p. Nevertheless

mu_N(p(Z)p(Z^-1))>0                           (6)

at every N, and its exponential rate is the SAME as K_N. Since p has real coefficients and |Z|=1, the insertion in (6) is |p(Z)|2=(2cos(2pi*n/N)-1)2.

Here is a quantitative proof. Put

Phi(x)=integral_0^x Log(4sin^2(pi*t))dt,
lambda=-Phi''(5/6)=2pi sqrt(3).

The endpoint-aware factorial estimate gives the uniform central expansion

w_(N,n)=2N sin(pi*x) exp(N Phi(x))(1+O(1/N)),
                                            x=n/N,

on every compact subinterval of (0,1). Away from x_* the already proved strict maximum gives an exponential gap. Under xi=sqrt(N)(x-x_*), the normalized sums therefore converge, including their second moments, to the real Gaussian of variance 1/lambda. This follows directly by Taylor expansion, a Gaussian bound on a fixed central neighborhood, and the outside exponential estimate.

For g(x)=|p(exp(2piix))|^2,

g(x_*)=g'(x_*)=0, g''(x_*)/2=12pi^2.

Thus discrete Laplace evaluation gives the exact limit

N mu_N(g)/K_N ->12pi^2/lambda=2pi sqrt(3).    (7)

In particular

lim N^-1 Log mu_N(g)=Vol(4_1)/(2pi),

although g belongs to the ideal of the geometric critical polynomial. A vanishing of finite order at the saddle loses only powers of N. The same argument gives the full exponent for every fixed nonzero polynomial insertion |f(Z)|^2, even when it vanishes at Z_*.

Therefore an associated graded which discards ONLY smaller exponential rates cannot impose p(Z)=0 while retaining this source. The ordinary semiclassical H-adic leading symbol does impose that relation, but its next Gaussian contractions and normal/divided co-source remain necessary: (7) records one of the terms it would otherwise erase. Here H=Log Q is the quantum semiclassical direction; it is not the distinct meridian normal U-1. A source comparison must retain both rather than identify their filtrations.

5. A constructive exact current module in the SAME original source

The correct relation can be exhibited without guessing a critical quotient or dividing by the unknown scalar. Put

A(Z)=2-Z-Z^-1=(1-Z)(1-Z^-1),
S_Q f(Z)=f(QZ), B_Q=A(QZ) S_Q.

The actual weights in (5) satisfy

w_(n+1)=A(Q^(n+1))w_n, w_0=1, w_N=0.

Consequently telescoping the original finite sum gives, for every Laurent polynomial insertion f, EXACTLY

mu_N((I-B_Q)f)=f(1).                         (8)

The endpoint value on the right is compulsory. For example the left side for f=1 is one, rather than zero. This is an affine q-difference/Green identity with the original endpoint source, not an ideal relation for the multiplication algebra.

There is also a unique completely algebraic realization. On the finite clock A_N one has S_Q^N=I and

B_Q^N=product_(l=1)^N A(Q^l Z)
     =(1-Z^N)(1-Z^-N)=0.

It follows that

mu_N=ev_(Z=1)(I-B_Q)^-1
    =ev_(Z=1)sum_(k=0)^(N-1)B_Q^k.           (9)

Evaluation of the k-th term is exactly w_k f(Q^k), proving that (9) uses the original period, not an adapter normalized by K_N. Equation (8) uniquely determines this functional.

At the classical limit Q=1 its operator has multiplication symbol

1-A(Z)=Z+Z^-1-1=p(Z)/Z.                     (10)

Thus the finite geometric algebra really does appear, but as the principal symbol of a sourced DIFFERENCE OPERATOR. Away from the root set Q=exp(H). Its next formal term contains the Euler derivative, so integration by parts produces higher Gaussian contractions. It is precisely those terms that make (7) compatible with the classical critical relation (10).

Equations (8)–(10) supply an actual nonzero affine source object with fixed operator data and exact endpoint pointing. They do not prove a general geometric trace descent. They show concretely which category permits the desired reduction: relative twisted difference/de Rham cohomology, with its boundary current, rather than the raw multiplicative algebra.

6. The resulting next construction target

The irreducible trace nonvanishing theorem requires an ACTUAL descended critical algebra and source. The exact sewing and its regularized periods do not yield it by taking a raw multiplicative quotient: Sections 1–3 forbid that operation on the full faithful source objects. Taking the final matrix Ward cohomology first instead leaves only its scalar pointed line, rather than a new nontrivial geometric field action.

The appropriate test is therefore on the partly integrated weighted/current module, BEFORE final scalar Ward closure: construct its action-normalized formal or derived critical observable, with the required Gaussian counterterms, and map the original relative sewing and meridian-normal co-source through that reduction. A literal flow observable need not satisfy its critical polynomial there; (7) shows the required counterterms are already visible at order 1/N.

The existing formal insertion construction provides such a renormalized critical observable on the transverse FORMAL block. The still-missing step is its source-compatible whole/derived realization from the exact sewn period. This is a sharper requirement than finding another local nonzero unit. The lemma and original-source calculations above do not prove that realization, but rule out two tempting constructions and specify which source information it must retain.
