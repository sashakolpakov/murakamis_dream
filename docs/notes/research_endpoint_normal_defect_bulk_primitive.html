The actual endpoint normal defect and its coherent bulk sine primitive

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The full first normal of a microscopic endpoint kernel is not obtained by differentiating an arbitrary interpolation of its physical values. This note gives the exact difference, including its original trace current. The difference is a sum of the derivative of the physical-value interpolant and a sine transform of the actual high Fourier coefficients. The checkerboard cancels inside this transform. On a fixed bulk frequency interval, the transform has an explicit discrete primitive with exponentially small remainder. Its two terminal terms remain.

The separate value/normal interpolation theorem supplies polynomially bounded analytic representatives of both channels. It does not make their difference zero. No filled-action estimate, contour cancellation, or source nonvanishing follows here.

5. Scope and direct-source readbacks

The note isolates the exact scalar compatibility question. It adds no claim that a polynomially bounded pair of channels is a single moderate scalar Hermite representative. The bulk sine primitive removes no external connector, physical endpoint, fold band, odd simple value, original crossing, or spectator. In particular it does not give a filled-action estimate for the joined endpoint current.
