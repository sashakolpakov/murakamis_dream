Independent tangle depth at every initial bridge gap

Independently reviewed theorem, 31 October 2026. The one-end plat family admits arbitrarily deep, independently chosen tangles at every first-layer bridge gap. The construction preserves its original marked cap and all core circle radii. It is checked on the literal source; it does not assert a theorem for arbitrary stacking or arbitrary plat diagrams.

1. The all-width, all-depth family

Fix k≥2k\geq2, put L=k−1L=k-1, and choose any fixed depth vector 𝒎=(m1,…,mL)\mathbf m=(m_1,\ldots,m_L) with mj≥0m_j\geq0. Number adjacent box slots from zero and set sj=2j−1s_j=2j-1. Replace the jjth box of the first OO layer by

(sj,sj+1)mjsj. (s_j,s_j+1)^{m_j}s_j.\tag{1}

Process j=1,…,Lj=1,\ldots,L in its original order, retaining the middle ErightE^{\mathrm{right}} and final OO layers. The resulting literal word is

[∏j=1L(2j−1,2j)mj(2j−1)](2,4,…,2L)(1,3,…,2L−1),R=3k−3+2∑j=1Lmj. \left[\prod_{j=1}^{L}(2j-1,2j)^{m_j}(2j-1)\right] (2,4,\ldots,2L)(1,3,\ldots,2L-1), \qquad R=3k-3+2\sum_{j=1}^{L}m_j.\tag{2}

Products in (2) mean concatenation of slot lists in bottom-to-top order. A slot ss is conventional braid generator σs+1\sigma_{s+1}. Each added pair therefore means two independently powered boxes σ2j−2pσ2j+1−2q\sigma_{2j}^{-2p}\sigma_{2j+1}^{-2q}. Retain the standard adjacent cups/caps and mark the first original top cap.

The retained original first-OO boxes have fixed half twists ϵj∈{1,−1}\epsilon_j\in\{1,-1\}. All added boxes and the original middle/final layers have ϵ=0\epsilon=0. Every box has its own independent signed integer full-twist parameter. No region is merged, removed or assigned a dependent parameter.

Theorem. Every word (2) is an original certified simple-white marked plat, and its actual complete packing satisfies QQ-balance at every port. Its prescribed parity gives a knot. For every fixed kk, depth vector and sign pattern, there is a threshold T(k,𝒎,𝝐)T(k,\mathbf m,\boldsymbol\epsilon) such that every independently signed fixed full-twist vector with all |pi|≥T|p_i|\geq T satisfies

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(Kk,𝒎(𝒑))|=Vol⁡(S3\Kk,𝒎(𝒑)). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log\left|K_N\!\left(K_{k,\mathbf m}(\mathbf p)\right)\right| =\operatorname{Vol}\!\left(S^3\setminus K_{k,\mathbf m}(\mathbf p)\right).\tag{3}

This is the full original normalized invariant, both level parities and the entire charge sum, through the full-source balance theorem. Depths and twists are fixed before NN grows. No comparison between twist magnitudes, or threshold uniform in widths or depths, is asserted. Requiring every actual exponent to be divisible by 33 gives a subfamily of minimal bridge number exactly kk; that congruence is not required for (3).

More generally, the same closure works for any balanced certified core with the initial OO layer, no slot 00, the first-cap mark, and ordered actual initial curvatures

0=A0≤A1≤⋯≤AL. 0=A_0\leq A_1\leq\cdots\leq A_L.

The one-end cores satisfy strict inequalities, proved below. Neither this order nor balance is asserted for arbitrary initial-layer diagrams.

2. Exact simultaneous source dictionary

The original first-OO port jj is

(aj−1,u,ej,aj), (a_{j-1},u,e_j,a_j),\tag{4}

where uu is exterior, a0a_0 is the protected first-cap face, and aja_j is ordinary for j≥1j\geq1. Set A=aj−1A=a_{j-1} and identify the eventual last face of the local prefix with the old core face B=ajB=a_j.

For mj=m>0m_j=m>0, let f0=uf_0=u and let b(0)b^{(0)} be the current bottom odd-gap face. Prefix pair tt has the exact original quartets

slot sj:(A,ft−1,ft,b(t−1)),slot sj+1:(ft,b(t−1),b(t),u),1≤t≤m. \begin{aligned} \text{slot }s_j &: (A,f_{t-1},f_t,b^{(t-1)}),\\ \text{slot }s_j+1 &: (f_t,b^{(t-1)},b^{(t)},u), \qquad1\leq t\leq m. \end{aligned}\tag{5}

The retained old box becomes

(A,fm,ej,b(m)). (A,f_m,e_j,b^{(m)}).\tag{6}

Identify old BB with b(m)b^{(m)}. If mj=0m_j=0, retain (4) unchanged.

These formulas hold simultaneously. Before processing bridge jj, earlier blocks have changed only gap positions up to 2j−12j-1. The face there is already the identified old AA; the current bottom odd gap 2j+12j+1 has not changed. The even gaps 2j2j and 2j+22j+2 remain exterior uu, the latter being exterior outright when j=Lj=L. The two local slots change only their own gaps. Thus (5) is the original gap sweep; after (6), the current middle gap contains old eje_j. Induction on jj proves the entire first-layer dictionary. Every later core quartet is unchanged under these face identifications. The first marked face is never crossed because slot 00 is absent.

Introduce symbols Cj,nC_{j,n} with Cj,0=ajC_{j,0}=a_j. For depth mm,

b(t)=Cj,2(m−t),ft=Cj,2(m−t)+1,b(0)=Cj,2m,f0=u. \begin{aligned} b^{(t)}&=C_{j,2(m-t)},& f_t&=C_{j,2(m-t)+1},\\ b^{(0)}&=C_{j,2m},& f_0&=u. \end{aligned}\tag{7}

Every positive-nn symbol is a distinct new source face. In particular the old core disk aja_j is preserved as b(m)b^{(m)}; the original bottom face is now the outermost new chain disk. The same indexing is used by the actual packing.

3. An exact chain inside a finite triangular gap

Consider one original empty interstice (A,u,B)(A,u,B), with uu the baseline halfplane Y≤0Y\leq0. First assume its actual curvatures satisfy A,B>0A,B>0. Put the AA-contact at X=0X=0 and the BB-contact on the positive side. The old radii are 1/A,1/B1/A,1/B, and the BB center is (2/AB,1/B)(2/\sqrt{AB},1/B).

Inside this actual bounded curvilinear interstice define, for n≥0n\geq0,

Cn=(B+nA)2,rn=Cn−1,Xn=2A(B+nA),Yn=rn. \begin{aligned} C_n&=(\sqrt B+n\sqrt A)^2,& r_n&=C_n^{-1},\\ X_n&=\frac{2}{\sqrt A(\sqrt B+n\sqrt A)},& Y_n&=r_n. \end{aligned}\tag{8}

The n=0n=0 disk is old BB. In the positive coordinate C/A\sqrt{C/A}, this is the affine translation Cn/A=B/A+n\sqrt{C_n/A}=\sqrt{B/A}+n. That identity describes the circle-chain construction; it is not an identification with a global quantum torsor or a theorem about its periods.

Every disk is tangent to uu and old AA. Consecutive disks obey

Xn−Xn+1=2rnrn+1, X_n-X_{n+1}=2\sqrt{r_nr_{n+1}},

so their tangencies are exact. For nonconsecutive indices, the squared-distance support slack is

4rnrℓ[(n−ℓ)2−1]>0. 4r_nr_\ell\bigl[(n-\ell)^2-1\bigr]>0.\tag{9}

Each next disk occupies the bounded gap toward the A/uA/u contact, with 0<Xn+1<Xn0<X_{n+1}<X_n. This actual contact order selects the sum of square roots in (8); the other algebraic branch is not used. The finite chain remains within the original empty interstice. Other old disks lie outside that face, and the new contact points avoid its three old vertices. Hence the only old finite contacts are with AA, and with BB for n=1n=1.

These coordinates describe the existing original gap. They are not an independent conjugation of a local quantum word. In the common marked plane, if the AA-contact is XAX_A and σ=sign⁡(XB−XA)\sigma=\operatorname{sign}(X_B-X_A), the actual new horizontal centers are XA+σXnX_A+\sigma X_n. Every old disk stays fixed.

For j=1j=1, old A=a0A=a_0 is the upper protected line and its curvature is zero. Use the exact parallel-line construction: normalize the lines to Y=0,2Y=0,2, so old BB has radius 11, and add 2m2m unit disks in the empty complementary component. Here Cn=B=1C_n=B=1. No finite-circle expression is divided by A=0A=0.

Insert 2mj2m_j circles in each original first-OO triangular gap using (7)–(8). The original gap interiors are disjoint, including when their boundaries share old edges. The finite new disks remain in those gaps and avoid their old vertices, so different patches cannot overlap or acquire extra contacts. Each old triangular disk is replaced by the literal triangulated disk in (5)–(6), with the same boundary. The new sphere is simple, with exactly the source contact graph and cyclic order. 4. Balance at all new and modified ports

The only order needed is B≥A≥0B\geq A\geq0. For A>0A>0, put s=B/A≥1s=\sqrt{B/A}\geq1, so Cn/A=(s+n)2C_n/A=(s+n)^2.

A new odd port has outer curvatures A,CnA,C_n for even n≥2n\geq2. Its positive middles are Cn−1,Cn+1C_{n-1},C_{n+1}, except that the first prefix port replaces the latter by zero. Thus

max⁡{middles}A+Cn≤(s+n+1)21+(s+n)2≤2, \frac{\max\{\text{middles}\}}{A+C_n} \leq\frac{(s+n+1)^2}{1+(s+n)^2}\leq2,\tag{10}

because 2(1+t2)−(t+1)2=(t−1)2≥02(1+t^2)-(t+1)^2=(t-1)^2\geq0. A new even port has outers Cn+1,0C_{n+1},0 and middles Cn,Cn+2C_n,C_{n+2}, for even n≥0n\geq0. Hence

max⁡{middles}sum of outers=(s+n+2s+n+1)2≤94. \frac{\max\{\text{middles}\}}{\text{sum of outers}} =\left(\frac{s+n+2}{s+n+1}\right)^2\leq\frac94.\tag{11}

Here s+n+1≥2s+n+1\geq2. Equivalently,

94−(t+1t)2=(t−2)(5t+2)4t2≥0(t≥2). \frac94-\left(\frac{t+1}{t}\right)^2 =\frac{(t-2)(5t+2)}{4t^2}\geq0\qquad(t\geq2).

The original QQ is nondecreasing in that middle, so (10)–(11) give strict balance at every new port, with gap >1/2500>1/2500. For A=0A=0, the parallel-line chain has all positive curvatures equal to 11 and the same conclusion follows directly.

6. Full source, independent twists and bridge number

Every inserted even exponent −2p-2p has identity endpoint permutation. All original odd first-OO boxes and other core boxes remain in their original order with their original half twists. The first marked cap is unchanged. Thus the original knot parity persists for arbitrary neighboring depth choices.

The total framing character is that of the extended original diagram. Its exact normalization is retained; it is not identified numerically with the core’s character, and no equality of old and new invariants is claimed.

For each fixed width and depth vector, the certificate and balance meet the existing full-source theorem. Its entire spectral and charge argument proves (3) for all independent sufficiently long fixed signed twists, including the 2∑jmj2\sum_jm_j new parameters. This is not an inference from local angle volume or a selected contribution alone.

The same proof permits arbitrary fixed added half twists ϵ∈{0,1,−1}\epsilon\in\{0,1,-1\} provided the resulting actual endpoint permutation gives a knot. Their extended framing and peripheral conventions are retained. The white source dictionary and complete packing do not change under these retained half twists. The concrete even additions in (1) are the convenient subfamily that automatically preserves the old knot parity.

If every full exponent is also a multiple of 33, the Fox certificate counts exactly 3k3^k colors on the standard cup/cap register. The supplied plat then has minimal bridge number exactly kk. These congruences allow independent large parameters of either sign and preserve the prescribed parity. They are required only for the bridge lower bound.

Validation and scope

Root and independent source/geometry reviewers checked the simultaneous gap induction, finite and parallel-line branches, exact support slack, disjoint original gaps, both ratio inequalities, the two separate modified-port targets, both curvature-order proofs, extended framing, parity and all fixed-parameter quantifiers. The construction gives arbitrary independent depth at every initial bridge gap; it does not cover arbitrary multilayer plat diagrams or prove a universal balance criterion.

The focused audit and recorded results compare the closed dictionary against the original Morse compiler on 413 nonuniform charts, checking 11880 ports, 23760 triangles, 35640 edges, all marks and core permutations. Seven symbolic identities check tangent chains, support and inequalities. Twenty-five exact simultaneous seed packings supply 2333 circle/line supports, 168 new-port bounds and 32 modified targets. Independent code review and a redirected replay reproduced the counts. These checks supplement the general proof; finite samples do not establish its uniform or quantum conclusion.
