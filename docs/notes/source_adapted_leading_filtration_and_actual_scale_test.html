Which leading filtration preserves the actual selected period?

28 October 2026. There is an exact, globally defined normal-jet source functor with polynomial norm. It is compatible with the whole-word Euler/prefix comparison. It retains the source’s marked degree; taking only the lowest state jet does not preserve it.

Two actual-source calculations determine what may be stripped. First, a single crossing’s square-zero completion correction has an exponential norm in the specified flat Borel metric. Second, the original figure-eight period loses its entire positive volume rate if one retains only a sublinear factorial-index window. These are different filtrations. Neither bounded nilpotent depth nor small normal order is an exponential-order estimate.

The surviving global quantum period has not been carried to a proved nonzero marked hyperbolic regulator class. This note does not assert that remaining step or a new volume conjecture proof.

1. An exact source-adapted normal-jet functor

Use the ORIGINAL reduced state and marks of the normal-period theorem:

q_N=exp(pi*i/N), Q_N=q_N^2, Y=M^2,
F_N(1)=K_N,
F_N(Q_N^r)=C_r(q_N), 1<=r<N.

On the complete cyclic meridian fiber put

t=Y^N-1,
Y_r(t)=Q_N^r(1+t)^(1/N),
v_N(t)_r=(Y_r(t)-1)F_N(Y_r(t)).                 (1)

This t is the MERIDIAN Frobenius normal, not the separate quantum normal delta=1-Q^N. They must not be identified. Write v_N=v_0+t v_1 modulo t^2. Direct differentiation gives

(v_0)_0=0,
(v_0)_r=(Q_N^r-1)C_r(q_N), r!=0,
(v_1)_0=K_N/N.                                 (2)

No rank hypothesis on an annihilator is needed for (1)-(2). The operator B_0 rank conditions elsewhere concern recovering this coefficient from a homogeneous kernel, not its identity with the actual state.

Thus the selected scalar is the explicit normal-period pairing

lambda_N(v)=N e_0^*[t^1]v,
K_N=lambda_N(v_N)
   =N Res_(t=0) e_0^*v_N(t) dt/t^2.           (3)

On the flat coefficient norm of (v_0,v_1), ||lambda_N||=N. Its quotient norm therefore satisfies exactly

dist(v_N,ker lambda_N)=|K_N|/N.                (4)

This is a global actual source quotient with polynomially controlled closure. It is not a definition of an unknown geometric amplitude or a division by K_N. The quotient line does not acquire a hyperbolic interpretation merely from (4).

The correct graded contraction pairs the degree-one state coefficient with the source of degree minus one. More generally, for a marked Laurent co-source and a jet state, the degree-zero scalar is the sum of ALL terms whose total source/state degree is zero. It need not be the pairing of their separately lowest terms. If those terms cancel, a formal nonzero leading state does not imply a nonzero leading scalar.

This construction commutes with the whole prefix Euler transport: its identity Tr(D^E A^E)=Tr(D A) holds as a meridian family BEFORE taking the marked residue. It therefore gives the same closed F_N, v_N and scalar (3). No action of the physical prefix matrix on the already closed N-vector v_N is asserted.

More generally, on a vector source presentation of matching dimension, a gauge G(t) transports a derivative source to

N e_0^*G(t)^-1 dt/t^2.

If G^-1=H_0+tH_1+… and the new vector is w^E=Gw, its actual contraction is

lambda_N(w)=N e_0^*(H_0 w_1^E+H_1 w_0^E).     (5)

The analogous matrix formula uses D^E=D G_m^-1 before taking the residue. The second term in (5) cannot be discarded. This is why the source, not just the state, must pass to the associated graded. At fixed normal depth the prefix correction is finite; the theorem does not bound the size of H_0,H_1 in an arbitrary changed metric.

2. The lowest nonzero state jet can miss all actual volume

The proved ordinary-color bound for any fixed b-strand knot braid is

|C_r(q_N)|<=sin(pi/N)^(1-b)<= (N/2)^(b-1).

Consequently v_0 in (2) has polynomial norm. It is NONZERO: the ordinary one-color anchor is C_1(q_N)=1, so its r=1 coordinate is Q_N-1. Its normal valuation is therefore zero.

For the ORIGINAL figure-eight knot, the independently proved whole source identity and volume evaluation give

log|K_N|=N Vol(4_1)/(2pi)+O(log N), Vol(4_1)>0.

Its formally subleading jet (v_1)_0=K_N/N has that positive exponential rate, whereas the nonzero formally leading state jet v_0 has rate at most zero. Thus projecting to the lowest nonzero normal state jet before applying (3) loses ALL the volume in an actual hyperbolic knot, not just in an unsummed formal saddle or an arbitrarily chosen zero-source current.

Keeping the marked derivative source in (3) avoids this loss. It does not, by itself, prove a positive rate for another knot.

There is also a useful global removal of genuinely bounded parts already available. The actual regularized cyclotomic period satisfies

t_N=(1/N)Tr_(C(Y)/C(Y^N))(Y F_N(Y))|_(Y^N=1),
K_N=N t_N+O(N^B)                                (6)

for a fixed B depending on the braid. The trace is taken in its specified regular lattice before the fiber value. Thus for any V>0 the limits N^-1 log|K_N|->V and N^-1 log|t_N|->V are equivalent: the polynomial difference is negligible relative to either positive exponential lower bound. Equation (6) constructs the same leading class modulo subexponential scalar sequences, without assuming it is nonzero. Its exact family identity composes with the prefix comparison. To retain meridian operators one must keep the whole orbit and normal extension, not just the single scalar t_N.

3. A square-zero ACTUAL prefix correction has exponential norm

Here is a norm test specific to the new crossing completion. Work at |x|=1 in A_0 with FLAT basis f^a e_k. Put F_a=(Q;Q)_a, lambda=x^-1 and d_k=(-1)^k x(-2k)Q(k(k-1)/2). The actual positive crossing’s right coefficient and its completion are

a=sum_(a+k<N)lambda^a d_k f^a e_k/F_a,
a^E=sum_(all a,k)lambda^a d_k f^a e_k/F_a.

Their original common Cartan scalar cancels in the relative prefix. Since right multiplication reverses composition,

G=R_(a^E)R_a^-1=R_(a^-1 a^E)=I+R_h.

The coefficient h is supported exactly on wrapped paths a+k>=N, and it has the explicit formula

h_(a,k)=x^(a-2N) Q^a F_k
                  /[N(1-Q^a)F_(a+k-N)].        (7)

In particular h^2=0. To derive (7), the actual nonwrapped inverse is

[f^a e_k]a^-1=d_(a+k)^-1 (-1)^a
                  Q^(a(a-1)/2)lambda^a/F_a,
a+k<N.

For a wrapped final path put r=a+k-N. The product a^-1 a^E then has coefficient

lambda^a(d_k/d_r)/F_a
    *sum_(l=0)^r (-1)^l Q^(l(l-1)/2){a choose l}_Q.

The partial binomial sum is (-1)^r Q^(r(r+1)/2){a-1 choose r}Q. This follows directly from the Gaussian Pascal identity by telescoping. Reflecting F(N-k-1)=N(-1)kQ(k(k+1)/2)/F_k gives (7).

Let M_N=max|h_(a,k)|. Every interval 0<=r<k<N occurs in (7), with a=N-k+r, and its magnitude is

product_(j=r+1)^k 2sin(pi*j/N)
                /[2N sin(pi*(k-r)/N)].        (8)

The denominator is bounded between a fixed positive constant and 2N. Every interval product is bounded by the product of all factors exceeding one. Taking exactly that interval gives the matching lower bound up to a polynomial factor. Hence

log M_N=N integral_(1/6)^(5/6)
                    log(2sin(pi*t))dt+O(log N)
       =N Vol(4_1)/(2pi)+O(log N).            (9)

The final equality uses the already calibrated Lobachevsky value Vol(4_1)=4 Lambda(pi/6). It is a scale in THIS UNIVERSAL local correction, not the volume of the knot in whose word it occurs.

Every h coefficient appears as an entry of the right-regular matrix: apply it to the suitable degree-zero idempotent and read its f^a e_k row. Therefore

M_N<=||R_h||<=N^2 M_N.

Both G=I+R_h and G^-1=I-R_h consequently have the positive exponential rate in (9), despite (G-I)^2=0. This is the actual one-crossing prefix correction, not an arbitrary unipotent matrix chosen for a counterexample. The metric is the flat regular Borel metric; no unestimated transfer to the physical head metric is made.

The bare original cut annihilates this positive carry exactly. Thus its algebraic associated-graded quotient is source-faithful on the restricted uninserted network. It is NOT an exponential small-error filtration. Norm or source-jet estimates cannot follow merely from its bounded nilpotent depth.

4. The exact original figure-eight source decides the bulk scale

For the actual invariant, not a substitute positive model, the whole-source identity is

K_N=sum_(n=0)^(N-1)t_(N,n),
t_(N,n)=product_(r=1)^n 4sin^2(pi*r/N)>0.

Put Phi(x)=integral_0^x log(4sin^2(pi*t))dt. The direct endpoint-aware rectangle estimate already proved there gives

log t_(N,n)=N Phi(n/N)+O(log N)                (10)

uniformly in n. For ANY nonempty retained index set A_N, positivity and at most N summands imply the exact scale test

(1/N)log sum_(n in A_N)t_(N,n)
   =max_(n in A_N)Phi(n/N)+O(log N/N).        (11)

Phi has its unique global maximum at x_*=5/6, with value Vol(4_1)/(2pi). By compactness and strict uniqueness, the retained source has the FULL volume rate if and only if

dist(5/6,A_N/N)->0.                            (12)

Thus even a single properly chosen bulk index retains the exponent; recovering the Gaussian coefficient needs a stronger window statement. In contrast a retained initial window n<=d_N=o(N) has rate zero. The elementary upper bound t_(N,n)<=4^n already proves this, while its n=0 term is one.

More precisely an initial window n<=alpha N has rate

0,                         0<=alpha<=1/2,
Phi(alpha),                1/2<alpha<5/6,
Vol(4_1)/(2pi),             5/6<=alpha<=1.      (13)

The sign changes of log(4sin^2(pi*x)) occur at 1/6 and 5/6; Phi(0)=Phi(1/2)=Phi(1)=0, giving (13).

This is an original-network obstruction to discarding high factorial/color degree as if it were a subleading jet. Fixed normal depth zero still retains the whole sum in (10); it is not the same truncation as fixing n. The scale n/N is a plausible bulk coordinate for a whole period. In this positive source, the exponential associated graded is strict and picks x_*=5/6. For a general signed braid the source sum need not be positive, so its maximal action supplies an upper bound but not a proved nonzero leading scalar. The marked cycle/cancellation question must still be solved.

5. What leading functor is justified now?

The source-adapted normal contraction (3), or the regular-period class (6), is a genuine GLOBAL quantum leading object. It commutes with the original scalar closure and with its pointed Euler/prefix realization. Its original source norm is polynomial. It is not yet a quotient carrying a proved nonzero geometric Bloch period. Its nonzero positive exponential class is exactly the missing lower-bound question for a general knot.

The carry associated graded removes source-invisible completion artifacts but returns the full original degree-zero word. The normal associated graded must retain the co-source’s degree. Neither operation strips the macroscopic factorial data in (10), and neither supplies a norm estimate by nilpotence alone. An action-normalized leading period functor would additionally need a whole source-compatible geometric realization and a nonzero graded contraction there. That is not constructed by declaring a formal geometric block to be the leading class.

The audit and saved output check (7) against direct products of the actual crossing’s relative coefficients, test the square-zero correction, and diagnose the scale tests (9)-(13). Exact matrix tests are finite; the coefficient, grading, interval-product and positivity proofs above establish the all-level statements.
