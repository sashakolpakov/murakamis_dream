Archived diagnostic: a coherent all-high Racah tail and a cotangent sum

Current status, 29 October 2026: the cotangent identity recorded here is now proved by the own finite Fourier argument in the coherent-tail residue theorem. That theorem also proves polynomial first-normal bounds whenever the maximal face sum is repeated. General unique-maximal-face residuals and higher generic normal jets remain open.

The text below is the preserved HISTORICAL DIAGNOSTIC, written before that proof. Its question marks and statements that a finite proof was missing describe that earlier checkpoint. The eleven-level numerical record has not been relabeled as a proof.

29 October 2026, earlier checkpoint. RESEARCH LEAD ONLY at that stage. The finite identity proposed below had been checked numerically at eleven specified levels. No finite proof, certified error bound, uniform normal estimate, or general partner-transport theorem was asserted at that checkpoint.

1. The actual generic normal being observed

Fix an integer m>=1 and

N=3m+1, D=N-1=3m, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2,
theta=pi/N, s_r=product_(u=1)^r 2sin(u theta).

Start with the supported folded sextuple (m,m,m,m,m,m), and change ALL six spins to their partners D-m=2m. Use the SAME generic symmetric quantum integers [u]=(zetau-zeta-u)/(zeta-zeta^-1) and seven-factorial sum S(zeta) as in the proved common-phase and retained-normal theorem. For this all-high branch, every face sum is 6m and every opposite sum is 8m. Its true summation range is therefore 6m<=z<=8m.

Remove the actual numerator zeros, before root evaluation:

(-1)^D S(zeta)/[N]=C(zeta)+[2N]T(zeta).       (1)

Here C is the SINGLE term z=6m=2D, and T consists of the terms z=6m+1,…,8m with BOTH [N] and [2N] omitted from the numerator. All seven true denominator indices are <N. The functions C,T are analytic at q, and the elementary factorial reflection gives

C(q)=N^2/s_(2m)^3=s_m^3/N,
T(q)=-2sin(theta)N^2 sum_(k=0)^(2m-1)
          (-1)^k s_k/[s_(k+1)^4 s_(2m-1-k)^3]. (2)

The tail is really present. In the coordinate t=log(zeta/q),

dot[(-1)^D S/[N]](q)=dot C(q)+4N/(q-q^-1) T(q). (3)

Consequently T(q) cannot be deleted merely because [2N](q)=0. The all-partner value theorem does not identify the derivatives in (3).

2. The observed formula and its exact checked scope

The proposed finite identity is

T(q)/C(q) ?= -3sin(theta) sum_(u=1)^m cot(u theta). (4)

The question mark is intentional. The portable diagnostic checks EXACTLY the following previously probed values:

m=1,2,3,4,6,8,12,16,24,32,48,
N=4,7,10,13,19,25,37,49,73,97,145.

Thus it checks both parities and several composite levels, but not every m<=48. Its working precision is 3N+60 decimal digits. At these levels the two sides agree to far more than forty decimal digits. This is numerical evidence, not an exact cyclotomic certificate.

Some rounded values of the coherent ratio are

N=4:   -2.1213203435596,
N=25:  -7.5419628808584,
N=49:  -9.5541461519503,
N=145: -12.801479315575.

Taking absolute values INSIDE the alternating sum in (2) loses the cancellation. The diagnostic records this absolute-tail comparison separately. The observed large overestimate supplies no no-go theorem for the coherent sum and no proof of its growth rate.

If (4) is eventually proved, elementary cotangent estimates would bound THIS T(q)/C(q) polynomially (in fact logarithmically). That would still be a special all-high family, not a theorem for every supported partner branch or for every normal order.

3. Higher-normal observations and a possible finite-proof route

The same existing probe records i dot T(q)/C(q) and ddot T(q)/C(q), with no derivative of C in these ratios. These are computed from the actual omitted-zero factorial terms, not by differentiating (4). For a unit quantum integer the exact logarithmic derivatives at q are

dot log[u]=-i[u cot(u theta)-cot(theta)],
ddot log[u]=u^2 csc^2(u theta)-csc^2(theta).   (5)

Sum (5) over the numerator indices except N,2N and subtract the seven denominator sums. If the resulting real quantities for a tail term are L1,L2, its first derivative is -i L1 times the term and its second derivative is (L2-L1^2) times the term. The recorded values grow moderately at the checked levels; no asymptotic bound or all-order conclusion is inferred from that observation.

A possible own finite-proof route is a parameter-differentiated cyclic terminating summation. The proved central cotangent identity in the spectral/normal transport note comes from the OWN balanced Sears calculation followed by a finite product derivative. It motivates looking for an analogous finite transformation for (2). That analogy is a research direction, not a missing step silently supplied by a named summation formula.

The current established scope remains the polynomial root spectral estimate and the tail-free generic-normal criterion. General bounds on live [2N] currents, their fixed jets, and coherent partner sewing remain open.

Portable diagnostic: script and recorded results. Only mpmath is required. Status PASS_DIAGNOSTIC means the finite numerical comparisons passed, not that (4) is proved.
