Two actual flow cuts give a strict matrix and coherent Green complex

Research construction, 27 October 2026. Once two actual physical edge positions a,b have been retained, their zero-extended array is one matrix with independent row and column clocks. Both finite Green currents, all boundary rows/columns/corners, and the original 1/N co-source have an exact Laurent/coherent realization. This does not identify a physical single-port Hopf action with matrix multiplication, and does not assume that the geometric Gaussian commutes with the construction.

The physical existence, four-port ordering and integral flow-chart choice are supplied by the actual two-cut reconstruction. The second cut is an INTERNAL IDENTITY edge: no new pivot is inserted there. All original pivots, caps and scalar peripheral factors remain in the actual array. Only a diagonal transport ALREADY present on that edge may be absorbed in its definition.

1. The actual array, source and independent clocks

Let f(a,b) be a finite zero-extended array on Z^2 over the original coefficient algebra, after the specified other finite sums. All flow constraints and their zero entries are retained. On the head window 0<=a,b<N put J_ab=f(a,b), and n=N-1. The actual selected state is head-supported, and its period is

Φ(J)=1N∑a,b=0nJab. \Phi(J)=\frac1N\sum_{a,b=0}^{n}J_{ab}. \tag{1}

There is no second factor 1/N. In particular this is the original rank-one average matrix row Tr(eJ), e_ab=1/N, rather than Tr(J)/N. The matrix is a reshaped DOUBLE-DIAGONAL four-port array, not an ordinary open-knot endomorphism. No scalarity condition J=cI is imposed on it.

Let A=diag(1,Q,…,Q^n). The two actual characters are exactly

A1J=AJ,A2J=JA. A_1J=AJ,\qquad A_2J=JA. \tag{2}

Both remain independent before the final source pairing. Their whole q-germs, including Q^N, are retained before root evaluation.

2. Exact shifts and every boundary term

Let Ue_j=e_(j+1), j<n, and Ue_n=0. Define endpoint rows and columns r_+(b)=f(N,b), r_-(b)=f(-1,b), c_+(a)=f(a,N), c_-(a)=f(a,-1), restricted to the other head index. Then literal coordinate translations have the matrix formulas

ΠSa+f=UtJ+enr+,ΠSa−f=UJ+e0r−,ΠSb+f=JU+c+ent,ΠSb−f=JUt+c−e0t. \begin{split} \Pi S_a^+f&=U^tJ+e_n r_+,& \Pi S_a^-f&=UJ+e_0r_-,\\ \Pi S_b^+f&=JU+c_+e_n^t,& \Pi S_b^-f&=JU^t+c_-e_0^t. \end{split} \tag{3}

Here Pi is head-window restriction; these are coordinate-array translations, not physical single-port raising/lowering actions. The endpoint rows themselves translate and have endpoints. For example

ΠSa+Sb+f=UtJU+enr+U+Utc+ent+f(N,N)enent. \Pi S_a^+S_b^+f=U^tJU+e_n r_+U+U^tc_+e_n^t +f(N,N)e_ne_n^t. \tag{4}

More generally choose L_+=U^t,L_-=U, R_+=U,R_-=U^t, e_+=e_n,e_-=e_0 and t_+=N,t_-=-1. Then

ΠSaϵSbηf=LϵJRη+eϵrϵRη+Lϵcηeηt+f(tϵ,tη)eϵeηt. \Pi S_a^\epsilon S_b^\eta f =L_\epsilon JR_\eta+e_\epsilon r_\epsilon R_\eta +L_\epsilon c_\eta e_\eta^t +f(t_\epsilon,t_\eta)e_\epsilon e_\eta^t. \tag{5}

The corner term is required for the two translations to commute. Longer words keep the corresponding finite strips and corners; there is no fixed finite head matrix representation of all translations without this tail data.

The co-source versions of (3) are equally explicit:

Φ(Π(Sa+−1)f)=1N∑b=0n[f(N,b)−f(0,b)], \Phi(\Pi(S_a^+-1)f)=\frac1N\sum_{b=0}^n[f(N,b)-f(0,b)], Φ(Π(Sa−−1)f)=1N∑b=0n[f(−1,b)−f(n,b)], \Phi(\Pi(S_a^--1)f)=\frac1N\sum_{b=0}^n[f(-1,b)-f(n,b)], \tag{6}

with the analogous two column formulas. A clock on an endpoint uses Q^N or Q^(-1), not one substituted from its root value. The exact Weyl relation S_a^+ A_1=Q A_1 S_a^+, and its three counterparts, includes these endpoint coefficients.

3. A whole Laurent kernel retains the tails strictly

Define the finite Laurent polynomial

ℱf(x,y)=∑a,b∈ℤf(a,b)xayb. \mathscr F_f(x,y)=\sum_{a,b\in\mathbb Z}f(a,b)x^ay^b. \tag{7}

This map is injective and has the exact operator dictionary

Sa+↔x−1,Sa−↔x,Sb+↔y−1,Sb−↔y, S_a^+\leftrightarrow x^{-1},\quad S_a^-\leftrightarrow x, \quad S_b^+\leftrightarrow y^{-1},\quad S_b^-\leftrightarrow y, A1↔[x↦Qx],A2↔[y↦Qy],a↔x∂x,b↔y∂y. A_1\leftrightarrow[x\mapsto Qx],\quad A_2\leftrightarrow[y\mapsto Qy],\quad a\leftrightarrow x\partial_x,\quad b\leftrightarrow y\partial_y. \tag{8}

Consequently arbitrary ordered clock/shift words and every retained polynomial Green moment are transported exactly. External meridian Ore operators act on the coefficient algebra with their original order. Q derivatives of a dilation include its Euler chain-rule term; the source’s normal terms are not frozen before differentiation. If an external Ore shift changes a chosen source/basis frame L, its transported operator is L_after O sigma(L_before^(-1)), with the specified semilinear coefficient action. It is not ordinary conjugation by a frame which the Ore shift has changed.

For head-supported states (1) is exactly

Φfull(f)=ℱf(1,1)N=1NRes⁡x=1Res⁡y=1ℱf(x,y)dxdy(x−1)(y−1). \Phi_{full}(f)=\frac{\mathscr F_f(1,1)}N =\frac1N\operatorname{Res}_{x=1}\operatorname{Res}_{y=1} \frac{\mathscr F_f(x,y)\,dx\,dy}{(x-1)(y-1)}. \tag{9}

The same expression on currents sums ALL their finite tails. It equals the head row plus the explicit outside-window sum divided by N. Thus it kills both full zero-extended differences, while (6) records exactly the boundary removed by restricting to the head. No cyclic interpretation of either actual coordinate is used.

4. Explicit two-current complex and its contraction

Write d_x=x(-1)-1,d_y=y(-1)-1. The actual two-flow Green complex on finite Laurent kernels has

d1(P,Q)=dxP+dyQ,d2(H)=(dyH,−dxH),Φfulld1=0. d_1(P,Q)=d_xP+d_yQ,\qquad d_2(H)=(d_yH,-d_xH),\qquad\Phi_{full}d_1=0. \tag{10}

All actual finite certificate identities map to this complex coefficientwise. Define P_xF=F(1,y), P_yF=F(x,1), and

HxF=F−PxFx−1−1,HyF=F−PyFy−1−1. H_xF=\frac{F-P_xF}{x^{-1}-1},\qquad H_yF=\frac{F-P_yF}{y^{-1}-1}. \tag{11}

The quotients are Laurent POLYNOMIALS: their numerator vanishes at the indicated source point. In the original array, for example, H_af(a,b)=-sum_(k>=a)[f(k,b)-delta_(k,0)sum_u f(u,b)]; this is a finite sequence. Put

h0(F)=(HxF,PxHyF),h1(P,Q)=−HxQ. h_0(F)=(H_xF,P_xH_yF),\qquad h_1(P,Q)=-H_xQ. \tag{12}

Direct substitution gives

d1h0F=F−F(1,1),h0d1+d2h1=I,h1d2=I. d_1h_0F=F-F(1,1),\quad h_0d_1+d_2h_1=I,\quad h_1d_2=I. \tag{13}

Thus both currents and their common corner/syzygy have a specified finite contracting homotopy. There is no inferred Gaussian or Stokes operation in this identity.

If the section is chosen as the literal matrix identity, let E_N(x,y)=sum_(a=0)n(xy)a, so Phi(E_N)=1, and let Gamma=h_0(E_N-N). Replace h_0 by

h0I(F)=h0(F)−ΓΦfull(F). h_0^{I}(F)=h_0(F)-\Gamma\Phi_{full}(F). \tag{14}

Then d_1h_0^I=I-E_N Phi_full, while the other identities remain unchanged. The 1/N in the original source is therefore preserved by the pointed complex; it has not become 1/N^2. This section is a specified homotopy anchor, not a claim that every physical two-cut tangle has identity matrix.

5. Global P1 kernel and the literal source/co-source

The core polynomial (7) has bidegree at most (n,n), hence is a section of O(n) external_tensor O(n) on P1_x times P1_y. A single matrix operator kernel uses the dual coordinate w=y^(-1):

𝒦J(z,w)=∑a,b=0nJabzaw−b−1dw. \mathcal K_J(z,w)=\sum_{a,b=0}^nJ_{ab}z^a w^{-b-1}\,dw. \tag{15}

In the monomial identification of the actual head basis with H^0(P1,O(n)), its input factor is the explicit Serre-dual Cech class in H^1(P1,O(-n-2)). The ACTUAL head ket and row are psi(w)=sum_(b=0)^n w^b and evaluation at z=1 divided by N. Thus

Φ(J)=1NRes⁡w=0𝒦J(1,w)ψ(w). \Phi(J)=\frac1N\operatorname{Res}_{w=0} \mathcal K_J(1,w)\psi(w). \tag{16}

This keeps the original uniform ket/dual, rather than replacing them by binomial coherent weights or compact ordinary trace. If a binomial-normalized coherent frame is desired, its diagonal source and dual adapter must be carried explicitly. No positivity, metric isometry or asymptotic phase is inferred from this vector space identification.

Both clocks and the shifts act on (15) as in (8); for the input clock its action is the ONE-FORM pullback w->w/Q, which includes the differential factor. This avoids a spurious factor Q in the right clock. On the coefficient of dw the input index b is -w partial_w-1; the missing -1 would give the wrong moment. Matrix composition, when it is actually used, is exactly

𝒦JH(z,w)=Res⁡u=0𝒦J(z,u)𝒦H(u,w), \mathcal K_{JH}(z,w)=\operatorname{Res}_{u=0} \mathcal K_J(z,u)\mathcal K_H(u,w),

with the input/output differential factors retained. It is not pointwise multiplication of symbols, nor a claim that the physical four-port tangle composes by the reshaped matrix product.

The full tails in (7) are meromorphic sections with possible poles only at zero and infinity in each coordinate. Keep them as such, or as the corresponding relative Cech cochains with their explicit boundary rows. Projecting them to the finite H^0/Serre quotient would discard the terms (3)–(6). The whole residue (9) is regular on these finite Laurent sections and preserves them. This gives one global meromorphic matrix/coherent Green complex for both actual coordinate currents, with no hypothetical extra Fr flag.

Here is an explicit principal-part/Cech description of the tails. For O(n) on U_0 and U_infinity use the transition z^n and write every finite meromorphic section uniquely as

p(z)=p<0(z)+p[0,n](z)+p>n(z). p(z)=p_{<0}(z)+p_{[0,n]}(z)+p_{>n}(z).

The first and last summands are respectively the finite principal parts at zero and infinity; the middle one is H^0(O(n)). This is the global-section form of 0->O(n)->O(n)(0+infinity)->principal_parts->0. Exactness is immediate from the displayed Laurent splitting. Two coordinates give N-by-N core, four strips and four corner principal parts. The two one-step rules are, for a core polynomial,

Π(z−1p)=p−p(0)z,Π(zp)=zp−pnzn+1. \Pi(z^{-1}p)=\frac{p-p(0)}z,\qquad \Pi(zp)=zp-p_nz^{n+1}. \tag{16a}

The discarded monomials are precisely the outgoing principal parts; the incoming ones give the last row or first row in (3). Tensoring these splits gives the explicit corner formula (5). Thus this is a strict global meromorphic/relative-Cech lift of the actual finite tail data, rather than an appeal to a different cohomology class with the same scalar value. Its source evaluates ALL nine regions at (1,1) with the one factor 1/N.

6. One-cut restriction and the physical Hopf distinction

Closing the second identity cut gives the original one-cut array p_a=sum_b J_ab, hence C_one=diag(p_a) and

Φ(J)=Tr⁡(Cone)/N. \Phi(J)=\operatorname{Tr}(C_{one})/N. \tag{17}

The second clock and current cannot in general be reconstructed from C_one: matrices with zero row sums can have nonzero weighted row sums after right multiplication by A. Keeping J avoids exactly that loss. Neither its two clocks nor its arbitrary off-diagonal entries are subject to an ordinary open-knot Schur condition.

The ACTUAL physical single-port Hopf actions of the four-port tangle need not descend to J. They can create off-diagonal port bands, whose source uses the corresponding original cut pairing. Equations (2)–(8) concern simultaneous coordinate translations of the scalar/charge-zero flow array, not that separate Hopf diagram. The necessary port-band current extension is therefore retained as an additional map; it is not asserted by this matrix reshape. Their exact bands and current audit are recorded in the physical port-band calculation.

The finite two-flow certificate now has an exact global coherent and sourced relative complex. Establishing that the nondegenerate geometric Gaussian pushforward is a map into this complex, with the same marked endpoint/carry data, remains a theorem to prove. No such commutation, nonzero selected geometric contribution or volume limit is inferred here.

7. An exact band involution and the identity-cut residue sign

A nontrivial geometric deck which fixes BOTH position clocks cannot be an exact source-preserving involution on the bare matrix J. Indeed the two operators L_A,R_A have jointly simple eigenvectors E_ab. A linear map commuting with both is a Schur multiplier E_ab->w_ab E_ab. Since Phi(E_ab)=1/N, preserving the original source forces every w_ab=1. An anti-source map similarly gives only -I. This is a fixed-level obstruction, not a prohibition on a transform with nontrivial internal/band multiplicity or only semiclassically fixed clocks.

The retained PHYSICAL PORT BANDS have such multiplicity. In the actual band convention put

Ja,bμ,ν=Ta+μ,b+ν;a,b,(ℐJ)a,bμ,ν=Ja+μ,b+ν−μ,−ν. J^{\mu,\nu}_{a,b}=T_{a+\mu,b+\nu;a,b},\qquad (\mathcal I J)^{\mu,\nu}_{a,b} =J^{-\mu,-\nu}_{a+\mu,b+\nu}. \tag{18}

This is simply input/output transpose of the full four-port tensor. It is exactly involutive and preserves the actual full source N^(-1) sum_(mu,a,b) J^(mu,0)_(a,b), with zero-extended bounds. It is not asserted to be a symmetry of the actual knot word. It exchanges input/output clocks; the CENTERED clocks Q^(a+mu/2), Q^(b+nu/2) are fixed exactly. Their square-root mark is the original q with q^2=Q. They restrict to the physical position clocks on the zero band. Simultaneous coordinate translations in a,b commute with this involution.

The finite Laurent band kernel and its exact transformation are

FT(x,y;ξ,η)=∑Ja,bμ,νxaybξμην,FℐT(x,y;ξ,η)=FT(x,y;x/ξ,y/η). F_T(x,y;\xi,\eta)=\sum J^{\mu,\nu}_{a,b}x^ay^b\xi^\mu\eta^\nu, \qquad F_{\mathcal IT}(x,y;\xi,\eta) =F_T(x,y;x/\xi,y/\eta). \tag{19}

The original second identity-cut co-source is the eta constant term of F_T(1,1;1,eta)/N. Its period form is

θT=FT(1,1;1,η)dηNη. \theta_T=F_T(1,1;1,\eta)\frac{d\eta}{N\eta}.

For inversion iota(eta)=eta^(-1) the natural ONE-FORM identity is

ι*θT=−θℐT. \iota^*\theta_T=-\theta_{\mathcal IT}. \tag{20}

Thus the scalar coefficient source is even, but its Cech residue orientation line is canonically odd: inversion exchanges zero and infinity, and Res_infinity(theta_T)=-Res_0(theta_T). This sign follows from the literal identity-cut residue, rather than from an assigned choice of geometric orientation.

Equations (18)–(20) give a candidate source sign line for a geometric deck with anti-invariant density. To use it one must still prove that the geometric deck lifts to this actual port involution and transport the whole R/word and source frames. Transposition swaps left and right insertions and reverses operator order; no unchanged single-port Hopf row or unproved Weyl symmetry is inferred. The fixed-clock bare-matrix obstruction and the full-band candidate are distinct, exact statements.

Exact shifts, source, global-kernel and two-current audit. The results pass fourteen root/whole-Q families N=2,…,8 and 742 head-plus-tail matrix units. They check every signed shift/corner, whole boundary clock, literal 1/N source and both current homotopies. An additional 1956 physical four-port matrix units check the candidate band involution, centered clocks and source; the residue orientation sign is checked symbolically. These tests do not identify that involution with the geometric deck or establish a Gaussian map.
