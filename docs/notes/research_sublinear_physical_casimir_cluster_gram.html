Sublinear clusters of the actual physical Casimir frame

Written proof, 24 November 2026.

Any sublinear number of distinct physical Casimir blocks has a subexponentially conditioned joint frame after each individual block is Hermitian orthonormalized. The selected blocks may be anywhere in the spectrum and need not be consecutive. Every actual first normal in a double block is retained. The estimate holds at every physical pair degree, including its recorded high-degree mirror.

The interpolation estimate behind the proof is already present in [S]. The source conclusion here is a bound for arbitrary coefficients in a growing sum of actual physical invariant spaces. It is stronger than an entrywise overlap estimate. It is not an ambient spectral projection bound, and raw column norms and inverse measures remain.

For near-fold blocks on a strict real degree compact, the positive energy estimate in [F] supplies a further conclusion. The whole orthonormalized cluster is exponentially isotropic for the original bilinear pairing, while its conjugate adjoint-Casimir cluster gives an asymptotically orthonormal doubled frame and a bilinear block swap. No arbitrary original exterior is asserted to produce that adjoint carrier or a nonzero marked closing.

2. The whole selected-cluster estimate

Select any m≥1m\ge1 distinct nodes occurring in this one physical block, with every column of each selected simple or double block. The nodes need not form a consecutive window. Recovery on the actual selected invariant sum

For arbitrary coefficient packets ai∈ℂνia_i\in\mathbb C^{\nu_i}, put v=∑iEiaiv=\sum_iE_i a_i. The uniform degree estimate also gives a full pair-boundary version. Its logarithmic conditioning costs at most Cmax⁡tℒN,mtC\max_t{\mathcal{ L}}_{N,m_t}, with no factor for the number of degrees; empty blocks are omitted. It controls arbitrary coherent coefficient tensors, not only separable inputs. Disjoint pairings are essential to this tensor statement: changing to an overlapping pairing still requires the actual recoupling map and its source estimate.

The filters (14) are not the ambient spectral projectors. Their action on unselected blocks is not zero in general. Equation (19) applies to the selected invariant sum, not to an arbitrary original vector before its unselected part is controlled. At m=1m=1 the filter is the identity, even when the actual ambient projector onto that one block has exponential norm. This distinction prevents an inference about the cost of selecting a physical source sector.

The doubled frame and its bilinear pairing have condition numbers tending to one.

The selected range of UU is JtJ_t-invariant. Since the physical matrix is complex symmetric, Jt*=Jt¯J_t^*=\overline{J_t}, so the range of U¯\overline U is invariant under the adjoint Casimir. Whole-cluster orthonormalization can mix the selected nodes; no individual column of UU is newly declared an eigenvector. The conjugates are an adjoint carrier, not extra eigenchannels of the original Casimir. Formula (26) applies only when those two stated boundary types are actually paired.

4. Source meaning and remaining gluing problem

Theorem 1 controls arbitrary coherent coefficient vectors in any selected sublinear cluster. It requires neither a local Gaussian approximation nor separation between its selected nodes beyond the exact physical cosine spacing. The first-normal directions, simple endpoints, both level parities and all physical degrees remain.

This does not remove the raw per-block value norms, the inverse Christoffel–Darboux measures, framing phases, pivots, or the original marked closing. Those known factors must be transported explicitly when source coefficients are expressed in this frame. A component outside the selected invariant sum is not bounded by (19). Neither the ambient spectral complement nor an arbitrary turn or short component is asserted to preserve the selected cluster.

Likewise (26) supplies a stable pairing for an actual carrier and its adjoint conjugate. It does not show that an arbitrary original exterior supplies that adjoint carrier. The original ordinary cap can annihilate a selected cluster omitting its distinguished source channel, as [H] records. The marked row is not replaced by an ordinary cap or by a Hermitian norm. No lower bound for its projection onto this growing cluster is inferred.

The closest prior estimate [S] is the same factorial Hermite bound for analytic spectral repair. The fixed-window result [R] instead gives polynomial recovery for a fixed number of analytic source rows on a nonzero-minor chart. It is not the unconditional growing-block statement (10). The new conclusions here concern their source application to the actual bounded physical Casimir and, using [F], the operator-level doubled adapter for a sublinear cluster. No original knot-volume lower bound or finite-exception theorem is claimed without the further source, exterior and closing estimates.
