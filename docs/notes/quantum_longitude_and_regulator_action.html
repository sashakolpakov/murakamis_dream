Exact longitude insertion and the regulator action of the actual state

Research note, 26 October 2026. This continues the marked braid correspondence and complete fundamental-cycle comparison. It proves an exact meridian-shift insertion formula in the actual finite state, identifies its classical coordinate with the preferred longitude, and fixes the norm of a compatible geometric logarithmic action. The selected finite period’s geometric contribution is still unproved; this is not a proof of the volume conjecture.

The construction uses the literature ADO state already fixed in the normalization audit. It introduces neither an ordering of an arbitrary classical polynomial nor a replacement state.

1. The meridian shift is an exact finite insertion

Fix a b-strand braid closing to a knot, with writhe w. Put

q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^alpha, M=-qx,
kappa_D=w-b+1.

The permutation of a knot braid is a b-cycle, hence kappa_D is even. The actual reduced zero-framed state F_N(M) is the cut scalar. In the balanced factorial basis it can equally be written with a constant cut vector and normalized average. Every nonzero term then has matching cut input and output indices: total integer weight is conserved at every crossing. Denote the resulting closed state set by S_N and its full weight, including trace, framing, and 1/N, by W_N(x;s).

For a crossing with input a,b and shift k use A=Qa,B=Qb,C=Qk,X=x2. Define the rational functions

tau_+=BC/x * (1-QX/A)/(1-QXC/A),
tau_-=x/A * (1-QX/B)/(1-QXC/B).                 (1.1)

Then, exactly at every level N>=2,

F_N(qM)=sum_(s in S_N) L_D(q,x;s) W_N(x;s),
L_D=q^(-kappa_D) x^(-w)
                          product_crossings tau_sign^(-1). (1.2)

The insertion is a fixed rational expression in q,x and the internal monomials. Its formula does not grow with N. Its meaning at a zero or pole is the cancelled whole term, as specified in Section 2, not evaluation of separate quotients.

Direct proof, including the square-root and framing factors

The exact coefficients in the preceding notes give

r_+(qx)/r_+(x)
  =q^(-a-b-k)(1-QXC/A)/(1-QX/A),
r_-(qx)/r_-(x)
  =q^(a+b-k)(1-QXC/B)/(1-QX/B).                 (1.3)

If c is the first output index, respectively b+k or b-k, both equations become

r_sign(qx)=x^(-sign)q^(c-a) r_sign(x)/tau_sign. (1.4)

The factor q^(c-a), sometimes written as a square root of a shape monomial, telescopes without a sign ambiguity. Let

J(a_1,...,a_b)=-sum_j j*a_j.

For either crossing, c+d=a+b as an equality of integers, and J_out-J_in=c-a. Every closed state therefore has

product_crossings q^(c-a)=1.                   (1.5)

This uses the actual unwrapped indices and their finite support; it does not infer a sign by taking a square root after cyclic specialization.

The pivotal trace contributes q^((1-N)(b-1)) under x->qx, and the residual zero-framing factor contributes q^(w(N-1)). Their product is

gamma_N=q^((N-1)kappa_D)=q^(-kappa_D),           (1.6)

because q^N=-1 and kappa_D is even. Combining (1.4)–(1.6) proves (1.2). No summation index was shifted, so no endpoint term is omitted. Away from the roots the auxiliary finite cut lift has the same identity with gamma_N in place of q^(-kappa_D), or with z^kappa_D q^(-kappa_D) when z=q^N. That central factor must be retained before q^N=-1.

There is a useful cancellation in (1.2). Put Y=M^2=QX. Then

L_D=q^(-kappa_D)
   product_positive (1-YC/A)/(BC(1-Y/A))
   product_negative A(1-YC/B)/(1-Y/B).          (1.7)

Thus the insertion uses the squared meridian, with no residual x or square root. Its N-fold cyclic norm is exactly one on every closed state. The squared-meridian descent proves F_N(-M)=F_N(M), descends the actual minimal operator to Y, and retains the full ADO state’s even-level sign. Its natural root norm extension has degree N rather than 2N.

2. Cancellation at the ideal color and higher shifts

Write r_sign=J_sign(z_sign;Q)k, where J_sign denotes the explicit monomial times Gaussian binomial in the actual formula, defined without dividing by the Pochhammer. Put z+=QX/A, z_-=QX/B. Adjacent product cancellation proves

r_+/tau_+=(x/(BC)) J_+ (Qz_+;Q)_k,
r_-/tau_-=(A/x) J_- (Qz_-;Q)_k.                 (2.1)

These are finite Laurent expressions in x, even when the individual ratios in (1.1) are singular. For k=0 both Pochhammers are one; for k>0 cancel the first factor and add the last. This proves the extension of (1.2) to M=1 while retaining its whole meridian germ.

For j>=0 there is the exact ordered-insertion identity

F_N(q^j M)=sum_s W_N(x;s)
                    product_(r=0)^(j-1)L_D(q,q^r x;s).      (2.2)

The local cancelled forms are

r_+ product_r tau_+(q^r x)^(-1)
  =J_+ (Q^j z_+;Q)_k (x/(BC))^j q^(j(j-1)/2),
r_- product_r tau_-(q^r x)^(-1)
  =J_- (Q^j z_-;Q)_k (A/x)^j q^(-j(j-1)/2).     (2.3)

They follow by telescoping a finite rectangular product. The external multiplier for the right side of (2.3), when summed with the old trace and framing weights, is

gamma_N^j x^(-jw)q^(-w j(j-1)/2).

All these formulas preserve even and composite levels and all finite support endpoints.

For a specified Ore operator P=sum_j a_j(q,M)T^j, TM=qMT, its action on F_N is consequently the same actual period with the inserted expression

sum_j a_j(q,M) product_(r=0)^(j-1)L_D(q,q^r x;s).             (2.4)

Thus T is an actual quantum peripheral insertion in the literature state. Equation (2.4) neither assumes that its classical polynomial annihilates each individual state nor identifies the minimal operator’s classical components.

The full ADO normalization remains explicit

For N_N(M)=d_N(M)F_N(M), use the already proved gauge

g(q,M)=d_N(qM)/d_N(M)
      =-q^(-1)(q^2M^2-1)/(M^2-1).

Its insertion is g(q,M)L_D. This retains the entire factor d_N=i(N+3)M(N-1)/(1+M2+…+M(2N-2)). The full state may have poles elsewhere on the root orbit, so its identities are meromorphic whole-germ identities. At a generic classical meridian g->-1; the full and reduced classical shift variables therefore differ by a sign. No numerical specialization erases that marked distinction.

3. Its classical symbol is the preferred longitude

Let q->1 in the rational insertion, with the internal coordinates held fixed. Then x=-M and

ell_intr=x^(-w)/T_over,
T_over=product t_sign,
t_+=BC/x*(1-x^2/A)/(1-x^2C/A),
t_-=x/A*(1-x^2/B)/(1-x^2C/B).                   (3.1)

The exact cancellation (1.5) fixes the sign of this symbol. There is no remaining arbitrary square root.

Here is a vector proof of its marking. In the balanced physical coordinates of the previous note,

K=x/A, e=(x^(-1)-K)p, f=(1-A)/p.

The raw meridian matrix G=b_-^(-1)b_+ obeys

G (p,1)^t=x^(-1)(p,1)^t.

Thus the canonical momentum p is the raw projective eigenflag. After the prefix transport B_(-,j-1), use the global vectors

v_j=B_(-,j-1)^(-1)(p_j,1)^t.

Their actual meridians mu_j=-H_j have eigenvalue M^(-1) on these vectors. Direct substitution in both crossing maps gives

v'_under=-mu_over^(-sign) v_under,
v'_over=t_sign v_over.                         (3.2)

For example at a positive crossing, H_1(-1)b_(-,1)(-1)=b_(+,1)^(-1), whose second row is (0,1). Hence its under-vector is already normalized with scalar one before replacing H_1 by -mu_1. The over scalar is p’2/p_1, exactly t+ in (3.1). The inverse crossing gives t_- and the same minus sign. This proves (3.2) as a rational identity, with the prefix transports included.

Follow the single knot through its braid and closure. Let W be the left-ordered product of mu_over^(-sign) encountered at the underpasses. Each crossing supplies one minus sign, and each supplies one factor t_sign at its overpass. Since the critical tuple closes, (3.2) gives

1=(-1)^c T_over * eigenvalue(W),                (3.3)

where c is the crossing number. The positively oriented blackboard parallel has holonomy W^(-1): its Wirtinger word lists mu_over^(sign) in encounter order, the inverse of this left-ordered vector transport. Its linking number is w. Therefore the preferred longitude is

lambda=W^(-1)mu^(-w).                          (3.4)

This also follows by pushing the parallel curve along the diagram: an underpass contributes its over-meridian with the crossing sign, and removing the writhe removes mu^w.

On the selected M^(-1)-flag, (3.3)–(3.4) give

eigenvalue(lambda)=(-1)^c M^w T_over
                  =x^w T_over=ell_intr^(-1),    (3.5)

using c congruent to w modulo two. On the other peripheral flag, with meridian eigenvalue M, the eigenvalue is ell_intr. Consequently

(M,ell_intr) is the marked preferred peripheral pair.       (3.6)

The source’s cut flag and the flag in (3.6) are explicitly different at generic M. At completeness they coalesce, and the rational identity extends on the augmented chart. This fixes the Weyl inversion that a wedge calculation alone could not detect.

The geometric peripheral image of the actual classical critical correspondence is therefore the marked geometric A-curve in these variables. This is a statement about that critical correspondence. It is not a claim that every minimal annihilator of its selected quantum period retains this component.

4. A source-compatible logarithmic action

Put h=Log Q=2pi*i/N and u=Log M. Use independent integral state variables k_i, with t_i=Q^(k_i), xi_i=Log t_i. The actual incidence/weight-conservation lattice is integral; its kernel has an integral basis. Choose these coordinates before passing to logarithms, retaining any N-dependent purely imaginary affine offsets.

The leading action derived from the actual coefficients has the form

S=G(xi,u)+sum_j eps_j Li_2(z_j),                (4.1)

where z_j are monomials with modulus-one constants, and G is a quadratic form with real coefficients, a purely imaginary linear term, and a real constant. The real constant includes the retained Li_2(1) and framing constants.

For example, in a positive crossing with a=Log A,b=Log B, c=Log C, the balanced expression uses

G_+=ab+(a-b)c-c^2/2-u(a+b+c)-pi*i(a+b),
Phi_+=Li_2(B)+Li_2(C)+Li_2(M^2/A)
                   -Li_2(BC)-Li_2(M^2C/A)-Li_2(1).

The negative expression uses

G_-=-ab+u(a+b-c)+pi*i(a+b-c),
Phi_-=Li_2(A)+Li_2(C)+Li_2(M^2/B)
                   -Li_2(AC)-Li_2(M^2C/B)-Li_2(1).

These signs follow from Log x=u+pii in the actual formulas. The pivotal term is 2pii sum_(closed legs)Log A_j and the remaining framing term is 2piikappa_Du-2pi^2kappa_D. Subleading terms remain in the exact quantum weights; (4.1) only describes their already specified leading logarithmic action.

On an exponentiated critical branch,

partial_(xi_i)S=2pi*i*n_i, n_i in Z.

The integers are locally constant on its logarithmic cover. Define

S_flat=S-2pi*i sum_i n_i xi_i.                  (4.2)

This is compatible with the same actual discrete period: at every actual state, on its unwrapped lift xi_i=h k_i,

exp((S_flat-S)/h)=exp(-2pi*i sum_i n_i k_i)=1.   (4.3)

No coefficient or boundary factor is changed. Equation (4.3) is a discrete character identity; it is not a claim that this character is an ordinary rational gauge for generic q. It identifies permissible logarithmic action sheets of the same finite data. In particular it does not choose a global steepest descent cycle or its coefficient.

After restriction to this log-critical branch,

dS_flat=2v du, v=Log ell_intr.                  (4.4)

The factor two is forced by the actual meridian half-step u->u+h/2 and by (1.3), not inferred from an analogy with a classical polynomial.

5. An exact logarithmic identity fixes the geometric action norm

For the same compatible logarithms and dilogarithms,

D(beta)=Im S-sum_i Re xi_i Im(partial_(xi_i)S)
                      -Re u Im(partial_u S).   (5.1)

Here beta is the actual crossing Bloch sum. To prove this, write Log z_j=sum_i m_ji xi_i+m_ju u+i theta_j. The Bloch–Wigner identity gives

D(beta)=Im Phi+sum_j eps_j Re Log z_j Im Log(1-z_j).

Differentiation of Phi gives minus the corresponding linear combinations of Log(1-z_j). Finally a real quadratic G with purely imaginary linear term and real constant satisfies

Im G=sum_i Re xi_i Im(partial_i G)+Re u Im(partial_u G).

Substitution proves (5.1). Compatible continuation of Li_2 and Log(1-z) preserves the identity: their monodromy terms cancel in D, and their remaining constants are real.

Apply (4.2). All internal derivatives vanish on the branch, and (4.4) yields

Im S_flat=D(beta)+2 Re u Im v.                  (5.2)

At the complete color u=0 the previous fundamental-cycle proof gives the fully calibrated value

Im S_flat=Vol(M).                              (5.3)

Thus an admissible geometric action sheet of the actual coefficients has the required exponential: for h=2pi*i/N,

(2pi/N)log|exp(S_flat/h)|=Vol(M)                (5.4)

at the complete point. This evaluates the specified geometric exponential. It is not an asymptotic formula for F_N(1).

6. The action period is the marked regulator norm

Define

R_intr=S_flat-u v, U=2u, V=2v.

Equations (4.4) and (5.2) give exactly

dR_intr=-(U dV-V dU)/4,
Im R_intr=D(beta)+(Re U Im V-Re V Im U)/4.       (6.1)

Hence the period exp(2R_intr/(pi*i)), with the invariant Heisenberg norm already fixed in the regulator note, satisfies

log||P_intr||=2D(beta)/pi.                      (6.2)

There is no free imaginary additive constant: the retained action and the exact identity (5.1) fix it. A real phase constant remains part of the full complex-period comparison.

The functions in the geometric triangulation’s full peripheral rows are m=M2,l=ell_intr2. Indeed a peripheral matrix diag(M,M^(-1)) fixing infinity acts on its horospherical coordinate by multiplication by M^2, and similarly for the longitude. Thus both the actual crossing sum and the positive triangulation satisfy

delta beta=-2M wedge ell_intr.

Their regulator difference has zero differential on the connected geometric branch. It is zero at completeness by the fundamental-cycle comparison. Therefore D(beta) is the marked geometric regulator on that branch, on compatible nondegenerate chart overlaps. Equations (6.1)–(6.2) identify the connection and norm of its logarithmic action period.

At a complete longitude value -1, v may equal pi*i rather than zero. Centering it requires the corresponding explicit character in S_flat; the regulator line’s deck multiplier and invariant norm account for that change. It is not silently replaced by a longitude value +1. Likewise the full ADO gauge in Section 2 changes the classical shift sign; its meridian germ remains a separate piece of the exact quantum pointing.

7. What this establishes, and the remaining theorem

There are now three actual-state comparisons:

  1. The root-level meridian shift is the exact rational longitude insertion (1.2), with all endpoints, roots, and framing retained.
  2. Its classical critical coordinate is the preferred longitude on the M-eigenflag, with the cut’s inverse flag recorded.
  3. The compatible geometric logarithmic action has the marked regulator connection and norm, and its complete imaginary value is Vol(M).

The selected finite period still needs a theorem showing a nonzero geometric contribution of subexponential normalization, and controlling the other contributions. An exponentiated critical point, an allowed logarithmic character, and (5.3) do not prove its coefficient in that period is nonzero. They also do not prove a geometric factor in every minimal annihilator of the actual sum. The root-level rational V=1 sector is not being equated with the geometric central curve.

The unchanged target is

(4/N)log|F_N(1)|-log||P_hyp|| ->0.

No version of the original volume conjecture is asserted here.

8. Coverage and verification

Classical meridian/longitude potential interpretations already have coverage in, for example, Sawabe, potential functions with arbitrary colors. The geometric action/regulator framework is also within the previously recorded optimistic-limit coverage. The calculations above were derived independently from the fixed actual weights; no proof from those sources is a premise, and no novelty claim is made for the classical conclusion.

The audit and results check both rational eigenvector transports and the exact integer telescoping identity; 53,262 local cancelled shift identities at generic q and roots; and 80 inserted periods against the independently assembled actual dense matrices. These include the Kashaev color, both crossing signs, two successive shifts, and even/composite levels. An exact trefoil calculation fixes the longitude sign, inversion, and framing: the M-flag value is -M^(-6), and the inverse flag has -M^6. It is a peripheral calibration, not a torus-knot volume argument.

The actual positive and negative potentials satisfy (5.1) in high-precision checks. A figure-eight complete/deformed calibration in three frames checks the preferred-longitude word, the five integer stationary windings, (5.2)–(6.2), and the complete action value. The proofs are the rational and logarithmic calculations above, not inference from these samples.
