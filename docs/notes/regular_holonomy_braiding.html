A regular holonomy-braiding line through the actual ADO color

Research note, 26 October 2026. This supplies local quantum data for the triangulation comparison, continuing the marked braid calculation. It constructs the unrestricted quantum holonomy automorphism, a regular splitting module through the actual Kashaev color, and an explicitly anchored intertwiner line. Its restriction to the nilpotent meridian section gives the original zero-framed knot evaluation with its full peripheral factor. It does not construct the global relative source extension or identify its selected period with the Bloch regulator.

Holonomy braidings have existing literature coverage; Section 8 records it. The algebra calculations below reconstruct the needed local facts without using a published holonomy-braiding proof.

The subsequent semicyclic carry-braiding theorem now gives a global explicit formula on E^N=0: its uncheck finite Euler sum retains the wrap phi_2/K_1^N. Four elementary conjugations, the Casimirs and the center powers prove all six generator equations. A common highest-weight flag fixes strict braid coherence without a determinant ambiguity on that locus. The transported center maps are the Frobenius block matrices; their common fixed line gives genuine braid actions on all integral recoupling charts and the original entire scalar cut operator. This is a special reducible-character source, not an extension of that highest-weight argument to E^N!=0 or an identification with the geometric regulator.

1. Rational conjugation survives when a universal matrix does not

Put q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, rho=q-q^(-1), N>=2. Use the unrestricted algebra with

KE=QE K, KF=Q^(-1)FK, [E,F]=(K-K^(-1))/rho,
Delta E=E_1 K_2+E_2, Delta F=F_1+K_1^(-1)F_2.

There is no unrolled H on a general cyclic module. To derive rational formulas, first use a generic parameter and the formal series

R=D Theta, D=q^(H_1 H_2/2),
Theta=sum_(n>=0) c_n (E_1 F_2)^n,
c_n=q^(n(n-1)/2)rho^n/[n]_q! .                (1.1)

Writing P_Q(t)=(t;Q)_infinity as a formal series, elementary coefficient comparison gives

Theta=P_Q(q rho^2 E_1 F_2),
P_Q(Qt)=P_Q(t)/(1-t).

Thus conjugation on generators has rational expressions even though the series (1.1) cannot itself be specialized on general cyclic modules. Set, with the displayed multiplication order,

u=q rho^2 E_1 K_1^(-1) K_2 F_2,
H=(1-u)^(-1), G=(1-Q^(-1)u)^(-1).

The automorphism r is

K'_1=K_1(1-Q^(-1)u),       K'_2=K_2 H,
E'_1=E_1 K_2,             F'_2=K_1^(-1)F_2,
E'_2=K_1 E_2+E_1-E_1 K_2^2 H,
F'_1=F_2+F_1 K_2^(-1)-G K_1^(-2)F_2.          (1.2)

For K, commute K through P_Q and use its functional equation. E_1 and F_2 commute with Theta, so their images follow from D. For the other two images use r(Delta a)=Delta^op a. This last identity is independently checked from

c_(n+1)[n+1]_q=q^n rho c_n,
[E,F^n]=[n]_q F^(n-1)
          (q^(1-n)K-q^(n-1)K^(-1))/rho.

Substitution cancels adjacent series coefficients; the F equation is the same calculation with E,F and K,K^(-1) interchanged. No root-level infinite series is used in (1.2).

There is an explicit rational inverse. From the primed generators set u=q rho^2 E’_1 F’_2, with H,G as above. Then

K_1=K'_1 G, K_2=K'_2(1-u),
E_1=E'_1 K_2^(-1), F_2=K_1 F'_2,
E_2=K_1^(-1)(E'_2-E_1+E_1 K_2^2 H),
F_1=(F'_1-F_2+G K_1^(-2)F_2)K_2.              (1.3)

These equations also specify the necessary localizations.

Here is a direct coherence proof. If AB=QBA, expansion of (A+B)^n gives the ordered Gaussian coefficients and hence

Theta(A+B)=Theta(A)Theta(B).

After commuting the Cartan factors, the two instances needed are (A,B)=(E_1 K_2 F_3,E_2 F_3) and (E_1 K_2^(-1)F_3,E_1 F_2). They give

(Delta tensor id)R=R_13 R_23,
(id tensor Delta)R=R_13 R_12.

Applying the intertwining identity to the first equation proves R_12 R_13 R_23=R_23 R_13 R_12. Consequently the rational conjugations (1.2) satisfy the corresponding three-factor identity. The identities involve only rational functions of the generators and q after conjugation. Clear their denominators and specialize: they hold at each root where the indicated denominators are invertible. This proves coherence of the automorphisms rather than assuming an infinite universal matrix exists at that root.

2. Exact root center and its peripheral marking

Write k_i=K_i^N, e_i=E_i^N, f_i=F_i^N and

Omega=rho^2 FE+qK+q^(-1)K^(-1),
C_0=2, C_1=z, C_(n+1)=z C_n-C_(n-1).

Multiplying the N shifted FE factors proves

C_N(Omega)=rho^(2N)ef-k-k^(-1).                (2.1)

For example the polynomial identity behind this calculation is product_(j=0)(N-1)(z-qKQj-q(-1)K(-1)Q^(-j)) =C_N(z)+KN+K(-N), using q^N=-1. Both individual Casimirs are fixed by r.

Ordered multiplication gives uN=-rho(2N)e_1 f_2 k_2/k_1. Retain the entire central divisor

D_c=1-u^N=1+rho^(2N)e_1 f_2 k_2/k_1.           (2.2)

All factors 1-Q^j u become invertible after D_c is inverted: their product is D_c. No parity-dependent scalar is discarded. The center map of (1.2) is

k'_1=k_1 D_c,                k'_2=k_2/D_c,
e'_1=e_1 k_2,                f'_2=f_2/k_1,
e'_2=k_1 e_2+e_1-e_1 k_2^2/D_c,
f'_1=f_2+f_1/k_2-f_2/(k_1^2 D_c).             (2.3)

For the last two formulas, take the N-th powers of the coproduct intertwining equations. The mixed Gaussian-binomial coefficients vanish because Q has order N, including composite N.

Use balanced center coordinates

epsilon=rho^N E^N, varphi=-rho^N F^N.

Then k+k^(-1)+epsilon*varphi=-C_N(Omega) and D_c=1-epsilon_1 varphi_2 k_2/k_1. Equations (2.3) have the same form in these balanced variables. For the checked algebra map Ad_(PR)=tau composed with r, its point map applies (2.3) to swapped inputs, by contravariance. For inputs (k,e,f),(l,g,h), with c=1-k g f/l, that point map is

((l c, k g, f+h/k-f/(l^2 c)),
 (k/c, l e+g-k^2 g/c, f/l)).                  (2.4)

This is exactly the inverse holonomy map (4.7) in the marked braid note. The inverse is required by pullback; replacing it by the positive critical map changes the convention. The previously derived Borel-factorization/prefix change therefore identifies (2.4) with inverse Hurwitz transport.

In those coordinates put

G(k,e,f)=[[k,e],[kf,k^(-1)+ef]].

At the actual ideal color a=N-1, x_0=q^a, k_0=x_0N=delta=(-1)(N-1), Omega_0=-2, e=f=0. The marked meridian is mu=delta G. It is the identity at this augmentation. On the actual nilpotent color section x=x_0 M it has eigenvalues MN,M(-N); its squared eigenvalue coordinate is U=M^(2N), as in the actual threading calculation. This fixes the sign rather than silently changing the root character.

3. A regular three-parameter splitting module

Work near (x,epsilon_0,phi_0)=(x_0,0,0), where epsilon_0 and phi_0 now denote the raw central powers EN,FN, not the balanced variables of Section 2. Choose the unique algebraic Hensel branch omega near -2 satisfying

C_N(omega)=rho^(2N)epsilon_0 phi_0-x^N-x^(-N).

It exists because C_N’(-2)=delta N^2 is a unit in characteristic zero. Thus the full center is smooth on this cover at the actual color; fixing omega=-2 first would instead produce a singular quadratic cone at the augmentation.

For 1<=j<N define

a_j=(omega-qxQ^(-j)-q^(-1)x^(-1)Q^j)/rho^2,
P=product_(j=1)^(N-1)a_j.

Every a_j specializes to [j]q^2, a unit. On a basis v_0,…,v(N-1) set

K v_j=xQ^(-j)v_j,
F v_j=v_(j+1) (j<N-1), F v_(N-1)=phi_0 v_0,
E v_j=a_j v_(j-1) (j>0), E v_0=(epsilon_0/P)v_(N-1).
                                                        (3.1)

The product identity (2.1) gives the missing FE diagonal at j=0. The differences of the other diagonals give [E,F], and K gives the remaining relations. Thus (3.1) has the specified Casimir and central powers. At epsilon_0=phi_0=0 the chosen omega is qx+q(-1)x(-1), so these are exactly the actual nilpotent ADO E,F,K matrices. At x=x_0 they give the Kashaev color. General cyclic members have no H satisfying the unrolled weight rule; none is asserted.

This is a splitting module, including at the nilpotent point. Indeed the distinct K eigenvalues give spectral projectors p_j. For adjacent nonwrapping indices, F and a_j^(-1)E give inverse matrix units. Their products give all matrix units. Conversely reduce any returning path using FE, EF and the specified full E and F cycles. Its p_0 corner is a scalar. Hence the central quotient on this chart is End of the free module (3.1). This proves the needed splitting directly, including the special fiber.

4. A concrete Hom line and its anchor

For a pair of source modules use (2.3) for the target center and choose

x'_1=x_1 D_c^(1/N), x'_2=x_2 D_c^(-1/N),

with D_c^(1/N)=1 at the ideal pair. Keep the individual Casimirs. The target modules are again (3.1). Source matrices transformed by (1.2), and these target matrices, are two splitting representations with the same center. Their intertwiners form a rank-one Hom module.

There is an explicit generator. For each transformed K’_i form

P_i=(1/N)sum_(j=0)^(N-1)(K'_i/x'_i)^j.

Let lambda_0=q((N-1)2/2), using the already specified square root of q, and v=P_1 P_2(lambda_0 v_0 tensor v_0). Define the columns of I by

I(v_r tensor v_s)=(F'_1)^r(F'_2)^s v.           (4.1)

The matrix-unit description proves all six intertwining equations. At the ideal pair the transformed algebra is conjugated by the actual full R, and R(v_0 tensor v_0)=lambda_0(v_0 tensor v_0). Consequently (4.1) equals that actual R at this point. In particular I is invertible on the formal neighborhood. This constructs the Hom frame using the K cover and ordinary units; no roots of E^N or F^N are required.

At the base det I=1. Set

I_bar=(det I)^(-1/N^2) I,                     (4.2)

using the root with value one there. This is a specified finite algebraic cover and a pointed line, not a free scalar choice.

For a triple, the rational Yang–Baxter identity identifies the two target splitting modules. The two products of the framed intertwiners differ by a scalar. Each pair map has determinant one, so its embedding on a triple also has determinant one. Thus the scalar has N^3-th power one. It equals one at the ideal triple, where the actual finite R satisfies Yang–Baxter (the generic highest-weight identity specializes without denominators [N]_q). In the formal neighborhood a root of unity with this value is one. This proves strict local holonomy coherence on the specified cover. Global continuation must retain its root cover and phases; this argument alone gives no globally normalized tangle invariant.

5. Exact restriction to the original knot period

For the same nilpotent color on both strands, write R^0 for the actual uncheck R with its common Cartan factor q(alpha2/2) removed. The formula (4.1) restricts to I=lambda_0 R^0. Its diagonal entries before the common factor are x(-r-s)q(2rs), whence

det R^0=x^(-N^2 a)q^(N^2 a^2/2), a=N-1.

Writing x=x_0 M, the root of det I selected above is M^(-a). Therefore

I_bar=x^a q^(-a^2/2) R^0.                     (5.1)

At M=1 this is the actual full R. For the residual normalization R^0 the actual same-color twist is theta_0=x^(-a). For (5.1) take theta_bar=q(-a2/2). Their ratio is exactly the crossing scalar x^a q(-a2/2). On a knot braid of writhe w, multiplying crossings contributes this scalar to the power w; the zero-framing twist correction contributes its inverse. The pivot and cut pairing are unchanged. Thus the reduced and full zero-framed knot periods are exactly the original ones throughout this nilpotent meridian germ. In particular F_N(1)=<K>_N still holds.

This statement concerns the same-color cut evaluation. It does not assert that the constant theta_bar is a ribbon twist on every mixed cyclic object. Nor does it change the full ADO factor

d_N(M)=i^(N+3) M^(N-1)/(1+M^2+...+M^(2N-2)).

The rank-one meridian wall and its whole ratio at M=1 must still be retained in a global source construction.

6. What this contributes to the ideal-triangulation route

The audited ideal-triangulation theorem supplies a complete positive geometric representative beta_T and its marked Bloch regulator. The crossing fundamental-cycle calculation already identifies the complete classical crossing class with beta_T. The construction here supplies actual local quantum holonomy data and a Hom line regular through the color used by the original invariant. It therefore provides a local object to transport when comparing a diagram decomposition and an ideal triangulation.

It supplies neither the relative gluing source nor its chosen global contraction. In particular the actual root augmentation mu=I and the complete hyperbolic meridians are different central data. The threaded separator rules out simply equating their homogeneous eigenlines while preserving the interior observables. A regular local family of crossing modules does not prove that the corresponding closed knot representations lie on one component, or identify their evaluations.

The required comparison is of pointed relative source extensions, including their cut/cap data, full meridian wall, peripheral factors, and selected evaluation. It must also specify the trajectory between fixed semiclassical shapes and root-central coordinates. Positivity and local Yang–Baxter coherence alone do not prove nonzero geometric contribution, exclude cancellation, or bound the norm of the unknown comparison period. The volume identity

(4/N)log|F_N(1)| -> log||P_hyp||=2Vol(M)/pi

remains unproved.

The subsequent closed-braid note constructs the admissible global braid Hom line and a literal scalar cut functional from these local data. A grading calculation identifies its entire upper triangular reducible period with the actual ADO meridian value. It also proves that the formal central augmentation neighborhood is abelian and identifies the Alexander attachment divisor. This advances the global braid construction; the relative triangulation source, opposite-weight geometric coupling and regulator norm remain unproved.

7. Independent exact diagnostics

The audit checks 14 generic cyclic automorphism cases, including every commutator, the rational inverse, coproduct transport, Casimirs and central powers. Six three-factor tests check the quantum Yang–Baxter maps. Six tests construct the matrix-unit intertwiner and check all six generators. Fourteen actual nilpotent/ideal cases check exact conjugation, the anchored frame and determinant root; thirty knot cut evaluations check the compensating twist. Nine third-order deformation tests, through level 15, check the Hensel module and both central powers. The balanced-center sign and inverse holonomy convention are also checked symbolically. Even and composite levels are included. See the results. These diagnostics support the algebraic proofs, not a global period identification or volume estimate.

8. Literature coverage, not proof premises

Kashaev–Reshetikhin, math/0202212 already constructs root-of-unity holonomy R-matrices. The abstract of McPhail-Snyder–Reshetikhin, 2509.02354v2 announces an explicit four-dilogarithm factorization and holonomy Yang–Baxter equation. The abstract of McPhail-Snyder, 2509.02365v3 announces invariants with flat-connection input and a phase-free geometric twist of Kashaev’s invariant. These establish prior coverage of the general framework, not the selected original-state period comparison needed here. Their proofs, factorization and global normalization claims are not premises of Sections 1–6.

The fixed-Casimir polynomial in the older recoupling note is a specialization/lattice lift; that note does not assert a generic braiding for it. Equations (1.2)–(2.3) use a different construction: the individual unrestricted central characters change by the holonomy map. The coherence proof here gives no generic Yang–Baxter identity for that fixed polynomial extension.
