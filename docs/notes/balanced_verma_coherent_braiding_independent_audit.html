Independent audit of balanced Verma coherent braiding

Audit, 27 October 2026. I checked the balanced Verma proof against the displayed actual coefficients, the earlier Verma period, and the F-semicyclic chart. The proof is sound as stated. No additional matrix test was needed, and no geometric-source or volume conclusion is inferred.

1. The lattice, divided shift, and exact resonance

Substituting ℰ=ρ2E\mathcal E=\rho^2E into the original Euler coefficient gives qk(k−1)/2ρk[k]q!ρ−2k=(−1)kqk2(Q;Q)k, q^{k(k-1)/2}\frac{\rho^k}{[k]_q!}\rho^{-2k} =\frac{(-1)^kq^{k^2}}{(Q;Q)_k}, because (1−Q)/ρ=−q(1-Q)/\rho=-q. The displayed balanced crossing entry follows by multiplying the EE-arrows and canceling the FF-product against (Q;Q)k(Q;Q)_k. Its Gaussian coefficient is polynomial, so the root specialization is regular on each finite total-position sector, including transfers k≥Nk\ge N.

On root blocks, the literal EE-wrap is κB\kappa B, while the FF-edge at every block boundary is zero. The full EE-cycle is ℰN=(−1)N+1κNB. \mathcal E^N=(-1)^{N+1}\kappa_N B. The canceled coefficient of FN/(Q;Q)NF^N/(Q;Q)_N is ((r+1)N+a‾N)Q=r+1\binom{(r+1)N+\bar a}{N}_Q=r+1. Thus B=∂t,T=tB=\partial_t,\ T=t on tr/r!t^r/r!, with [B,T]=1[B,T]=1. The coherent kernel has the stated BB-eigenvalue and TT-derivative identities. Those are kernel identities, not a commutator-preserving substitution B↦s,T↦∂sB\mapsto s,T\mapsto\partial_s.

For the logarithm of PQ(qℰ1F2)P_Q(q\mathcal E_1F_2), the only extra surviving term is the resonance m=Nm=N: −qNNNℰ1N⊗[F2N1−QN]root=ℰ1N⊗T2. -\frac{q_N^N}{N}\mathcal E_1^N \otimes\left[\frac{F_2^N}{1-Q^N}\right]_{\mathrm{root}} =\mathcal E_1^N\otimes T_2. Here qNN=−1q_N^N=-1 at every level. For m=jNm=jN, j≥2j\ge2, the numerator has jj vanishing factors and the denominator one; nonresonant m>Nm>N has a vanishing numerator and a unit denominator. The low-degree part is the logarithm of the nilpotent finite factorial. Since T2T_2 commutes with F2F_2, it commutes with the resonance. Exponentiating therefore proves the exact factorization, with its sign. All operations are finite on each total-position sector; the stated product completion is defined sectorwise.

2. Changing centers and the inverse

The resonance translates (s1,s2)↦(s1,s2+(−1)N+1κ1,Ns1). (s_1,s_2)\mapsto (s_1,s_2+(-1)^{N+1}\kappa_{1,N}s_1). Its finite head coefficient depends on s1s_1, not s2s_2, so no derivative of that coefficient is lost. The Cartan block ratio is exactly x2−Nrx1−Nsx_2^{-Nr}x_1^{-Ns}; its QQ-powers are full NN-cycles. It rescales the coherent parameters exactly as in the note.

For equal colors, with χ=xN=(−1)N+1MN\chi=x^N=(-1)^{N+1}M^N, (−1)N+1κN=χ−χ−1. (-1)^{N+1}\kappa_N=\chi-\chi^{-1}. After the checked flip this gives L+=(1−χ−2χ−1χ−10),L−=(0χχ1−χ2). L_+= \begin{pmatrix}1-\chi^{-2}&\chi^{-1}\\ \chi^{-1}&0\end{pmatrix}, \quad L_-= \begin{pmatrix}0&\chi\\ \chi&1-\chi^2\end{pmatrix}. They are literal inverses and fix the stated ordered line. The inverse finite Euler coefficient is qNk/(Q;Q)kq_N^k/(Q;Q)_k; it has no additional (−1)k(-1)^k. The recovered original source parameter must be used after the flip and inverse Cartan, as the proof does.

The regular infinite braid identity restricts through injective coherent kernels, whose head projection is the identity. This proves scalar-one changing-source coherence. It does not identify the new E-semicyclic chart with the older F-semicyclic chart; their positive center transports are oppositely oriented, consistently with their formulas.

3. Source independence of the whole original cut

In every finite positive crossing, an EE-wrap raises integer residue position by N−1N-1 in place of a lowering by one. Its net change from the ordinary crossing is therefore +N+N. The FF-factor has no wrap. The negative inverse uses the same EE- and head-FF arrows, so it has the same nonnegative wrap-degree rule. Flips and Cartan/pivotal factors do not change this degree.

After tracing b−1b-1 strands, a term with rr wraps would have cut change rNrN. The open index range is 0,…,N−10,\ldots,N-1, forcing r=0r=0 and equal cut endpoints. Thus the entire cut matrix, not only a trace, agrees with the original nilpotent one. The argument keeps the full pivot, zero-framing scalar and external dN(M)d_N(M) line.

On the fixed source line this is a genuine endomorphism evaluation. Before source closure, the same degree argument remains a coordinate-matrix contraction; it does not define a new topological invariant.

4. The boundary extension and earlier Verma period

Replacing κs\kappa s by the independent μ\mu makes every finite crossing coefficient polynomial in μ\mu. The center transport remains regular and linear for equal colors. The selected-cut identity holds for κ≠0\kappa\ne0, hence extends as a whole polynomial identity to M=1M=1, with μ\mu fixed.

The cycle product is exactly ℰμN=(−1)N+1uN(M)μ,uN(1)=N. \mathcal E_\mu^N =(-1)^{N+1}u_N(M)\mu, \quad u_N(1)=N. No singular coherent kernel with s=μ/κs=\mu/\kappa is evaluated separately at the boundary.

Finally, the generic factorial basis change has poles on the tail root blocks. It cannot identify the two root infinite modules. It does preserve diagonal entries on every finite total-position sector as generic whole identities, which continue to the root. This is compatible with the earlier graded Verma trace and its full Alexander divisor. The finite coherent cut is a different selected contraction, and the proof does not delete that divisor or replace the earlier period formula.

The resulting source centers remain triangular/reducible. The proof therefore supplies an exact coherent source realization of the actual cut, with the regular boundary chart, but no irreducible geometric A-component or selected volume asymptotic.
