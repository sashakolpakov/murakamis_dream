Arbitrary-depth marked unit-chain extensions of balanced plats

structural theorem, 31 October 2026. A balanced original plat can admit arbitrarily many additional independently twisted regions at the same fixed bridge width. The construction below preserves its original marked cap and every old circle radius. It is a literal source operation, not a claim about arbitrary stacking of plats or abstract gluing of matched graphs.

1. Hypotheses and full-invariant conclusion

Let DD be a fixed original flat 2k2k-plat, k≥2k\geq2, with RR ordered crossing regions. Assume:

  • Its first zero-based box slot is 11.
  • No box uses slot 00.
  • The first original top cap, on slots (0,1)(0,1), is marked.
  • Its white sphere is simple, carries the actual labeled right-angled certificate, and its one complete marked packing satisfies the complete-radius balance condition at every port.

Retain every original half-twist sign and the original cup/cap conventions. For any integer m≥0m\geq0, form the literal bottom-to-top word

D(m)=(1,2)mD. D^{(m)}=(1,2)^mD.\tag{1}

Each new box has half-twist ϵ=0\epsilon=0 and an independent signed integer full-twist parameter pp, hence actual braid exponent −2p-2p. Slot jj is conventional braid generator σj+1\sigma_{j+1}, so the added braid is

∏j=1mσ2−2pj,1σ3−2pj,2. \prod_{j=1}^{m}\sigma_2^{-2p_{j,1}}\sigma_3^{-2p_{j,2}}.

No old box is combined, deleted, moved or reinterpreted. The new number of crossing regions is R+2mR+2m.

Theorem. The original marked diagram D(m)D^{(m)} is again certified and simple-white, and every port satisfies complete-radius balance. If the original parity gives a knot, the extended diagram still gives a knot. For each fixed mm, there is a threshold T(D,m)T(D,m) such that every independently signed fixed filling vector with all parameters above that threshold in absolute value satisfies

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(KD(m),𝒑)|=Vol⁡(S3\KD(m),𝒑). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log\left|K_N\!\left(K_{D^{(m)},\mathbf p}\right)\right| =\operatorname{Vol}\!\left(S^3\setminus K_{D^{(m)},\mathbf p}\right).\tag{2}

This is the full original normalized invariant, both level parities and the entire original charge sum, through the general balance theorem. Every twist is fixed before N→∞N\to\infty; no comparison of their magnitudes is required. The threshold is not asserted to be uniform in mm.

In particular, the one-end-layer cores OkEkrightOkO_kE_k^{\mathrm{right}}O_k satisfy these hypotheses. Thus for every fixed k≥2k\geq2 and every m≥0m\geq0, (1) supplies 3k−3+2m3k-3+2m independently long signed twist regions. This is arbitrary depth at fixed width. The added alternating tangle acts on the first four physical strands; the claim does not say that each added layer spans all 2k2k strands.

The case m=0m=0 is unchanged. Assume m≥1m\geq1 in the source patch and balance arguments of Sections 2–4.

2. Exact original gap dictionary

The absence of slot 00 means the bottom odd-gap face vv on slots (0,1)(0,1) remains the first top-cap face. It is the protected face paired with the exterior uu. Before the first box, every internal even gap belongs to uu. Hence the first port of DD is

(v,u,e,a), (v,u,e,a),\tag{3}

where aa is the bottom odd-gap face on slots (2,3)(2,3). Simplicity makes ee an ordinary face distinct from u,v,au,v,a; aa is also ordinary. All other regions and cyclic port orders are those of DD.

For the prefix, let a(0)a^{(0)} be the original bottom face in gap 33, set f0=uf_0=u, and let fj,a(j)f_j,a^{(j)} be the new faces created by the jjth pair (1,2)(1,2). The literal sweep gives

slot 1j:(v,fj−1,fj,a(j−1)),slot 2j:(fj,a(j−1),a(j),u),1≤j≤m. \begin{aligned} \text{slot }1_j &: (v,f_{j-1},f_j,a^{(j-1)}),\\ \text{slot }2_j &: (f_j,a^{(j-1)},a^{(j)},u), \qquad1\leq j\leq m. \end{aligned}\tag{4}

The right outer region at slot 22 is still uu: the prefix never crosses the next even gap, whether it is internal for k>2k>2 or exterior for k=2k=2. Identify the old core face aa with a(m)a^{(m)}. The first core port becomes

(v,fm,e,a(m)). (v,f_m,e,a^{(m)}).\tag{5}

Every later core quartet is unchanged under that one face relabeling. The mark remains u,vu,v. This is an exact instance of the original Morse face dictionary, without isotopy, stabilization or a change to the quantum marked cap.

3. The actual empty gap and complete packing

Normalize the old marked disks as the outside halfplanes below Y=0Y=0 and above Y=2Y=2. The original triangle (v,u,a)(v,u,a) implies that aa is tangent to both lines and has radius 11. Translate its center to (0,1)(0,1).

This triangle is one of the two marked-edge triangular interstices. Choose the one global horizontal reflection so it is the empty right complementary component of the marked strip. The unit disk aa touches both strip boundaries and separates the strip into left and right components. Every other old finite disk lies in the left component: otherwise the actual triangular interstice would contain another disk or the embedded contact graph would cross its boundary. The right component is therefore empty. This uses the actual labeled embedded packing, not an assumed gap in an arbitrary developed configuration.

Leave every old disk unchanged. For 0≤j≤m0\leq j\leq m, set

center⁡(a(j))=(4(m−j),1),radius⁡(a(j))=1, \operatorname{center}(a^{(j)})=(4(m-j),1),\qquad \operatorname{radius}(a^{(j)})=1,

where a(m)a^{(m)} is the old disk aa. For 1≤j≤m1\leq j\leq m, set

center⁡(fj)=(4(m−j)+2,1),radius⁡(fj)=1. \operatorname{center}(f_j)=(4(m-j)+2,1),\qquad \operatorname{radius}(f_j)=1.\tag{6}

The 2m2m new disks form a chain in the actual empty right component. Consecutive disks are tangent, and nonconsecutive disks have disjoint interiors and no contact. Every new disk touches both marked lines. Only fmf_m touches an old finite disk, namely a(m)a^{(m)}. Thus no unintended old/new contact occurs. More explicitly, if another old disk has center (X,Y)(X,Y), then X<0X<0. The triangles in (4)–(5) are exactly the gaps above and below this chain. At the far right, (v,u,a(0))(v,u,a^{(0)}) replaces the old unbounded triangle (v,u,a)(v,u,a). The other triangle (v,a,e)(v,a,e) of the old first port is unchanged. Equivalently, one old triangular disk has been replaced by a triangulated disk with 2m2m additional vertices and the same original boundary. The new source sphere is simple, and the displayed packing realizes precisely its contact graph and cyclic order.

The proved simple-white packing construction, Sections 4–5, supplies its actual labeled right-angled cut balls. Marked uniqueness and contact-center development identify this packing with the normalized complete source packing. Every old ordinary curvature is unchanged; every new curvature equals 11. No separate radii are chosen for different ports.

4. Exact balance at every port

Use the original half-degree triangle action and put

C0=Q(0,1,1)=2Gπ>0,Q(0,0,1)=0, C_0=Q(0,1,1)=\frac{2G}{\pi}>0,\qquad Q(0,0,1)=0,\tag{7}

where GG is Catalan’s constant. These constants have the original source normalization.

The first new slot-11 port has outer weights (0,1)(0,1) and middle weights (0,1)(0,1), hence balance gap C0C_0. Every later new slot-11 port has outer weights (0,1)(0,1) and middle weights (1,1)(1,1), hence gap 2C02C_0. Every new slot-22 port has outer weights (1,0)(1,0) and middle weights (1,1)(1,1), again gap 2C02C_0.

For the modified first core port, the outer curvatures are v=0v=0 and a=1a=1. Its old middles were 00 and E=curvature⁡(e)>0E=\operatorname{curvature}(e)>0. Every finite disk inside the height-22 strip has radius at most 11, so E≥1E\geq1. Before and after the modification, the largest positive middle logarithm is therefore log⁡E\log E. The old action sum was Q(0,E,1)Q(0,E,1); the new sum is

Q(0,1,1)+Q(0,E,1), Q(0,1,1)+Q(0,E,1),\tag{8}

larger by exactly C0C_0. The old strict balance is preserved. All other core ports retain their weights and balance gaps exactly.

The minimum local balance margin of D(m)D^{(m)} is at least the smaller of the old minimum margin and C0C_0, independently of mm. This does not make the analytic filling threshold uniform in mm: the number of ports, source constants and geometric filling data still depend on the extended diagram.

5. Retained phases, knots and bridge number

Every new exponent −2p-2p is even, of either sign, so its endpoint permutation is the identity. The new boxes do not change the original component graph. Every original half twist remains in its original box, and the marked cap remains fixed. These even exponents are independent genuine crossing regions; their trivial endpoint permutation is not an identity on the finite quantum source.

For each fixed extended diagram, its certificate and proved balance let the full-source theorem control the entire one-sided remainder and apply the original all-charge filling theorem. This proves (2), including the original normalization and sufficiently long smooth hyperbolic filling. The conclusion is not inferred from the packing picture alone.

If the original parity gives a knot and all old and new full exponents are additionally divisible by 33, the Fox-coloring certificate gives exactly 3k3^k colorings. The supplied 2k2k-plat then has minimal bridge number exactly kk. For the new boxes, this condition is p≡0(mod⁡3)p\equiv0\pmod3. Every required congruence allows independent arbitrarily large positive and negative parameters. It is used only for the bridge lower bound, not for the volume theorem.

Validation and scope

The operation applies to every core satisfying the stated hypotheses, not only the one-end template. It does not prove balance for arbitrary multilayer plats or arbitrary marks. It preserves the original protected edge and all old disk coordinates; no generic re-framing invariance is used.

The focused audit checks finite literal core/depth charts and exact width-two/width-three packing extensions. These checks supplement the general source patch and empty-gap proof; they do not infer the uniform or quantum theorem by sampling.

The focused exact audit and recorded results reproduce 56 original extended charts, 1176 quartets, 2352 triangles, 3528 edges and 56 knot-parity checks, together with 12 exact seed extensions, 1084 circle/line pair supports, 84 new unit-port tests and 12 changed core-port tests.
