Actual Casimir pair localization of the reduced closing source

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The reciprocal Cartan pairs in the reduced first-jet formula have actual analytic central projectors on the original tensor module. Each pair cancels its own source residue. For a braid word the literal color turnover also reflects the two reduced block traces, including their meridian derivatives.

These are finite source identities. The projectors retain the generalized Casimir eigenspaces at the root. They neither select a geometric state inside a multiplicity space nor prevent cancellation between different pairs. The complete directs are H, R and Y, pinned below.

1. The exact reduced blocks and the total Casimir

Fix N≥2N\ge2, d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, Q=q2Q=q^2. Use m≥2m\ge2 actual heads with common meridian MM near one, at this fixed qq. Write r=m−1r=m-1, x=−M/qx=-M/q, x0=−q−1x_0=-q^{-1}, and Kr(M)=MrK0,ε=(−1)(N+1)r,K0N=εI.(1) K_{\mathrm{ r}}(M)=M^rK_0,\qquad \varepsilon=(-1)^{(N+1)r},\qquad K_0^N=\varepsilon I. \qquad\text{(1)} Let 𝒳\mathcal X be the NN distinct eigenvalues of K0K_0, with their constant occupation-space projectors PχP_\chi and spaces UχU_\chi. Inversion preserves 𝒳\mathcal X. For an analytic family W(M)W(M) of total intertwiners, R gives the actual algebra map B(W)=p0Sm−1Wi0,Sm−1WSm=∑n=0dF1n⊗Bn(W),[B(W),Kr]=0.(2) B(W)=p_0S_m^{-1}W i_0,\qquad S_m^{-1}WS_m=\sum_{n=0}^{d}F_1^n\otimes B_n(W), \qquad [B(W),K_{\mathrm{ r}}]=0. \qquad\text{(2)} Set tχW(M)=Tr⁡UχB(W(M)).(3) t_\chi^W(M)=\operatorname{Tr}_{U_\chi}B(W(M)). \qquad\text{(3)} The derivative of (3) always includes the dependence of the actual Sm(M)S_m(M). Use the scaled lowering operator EE in H. 2. Analytic paired projectors and their internal residue

At M=1M=1, equality λχ(1)=λψ(1)\lambda_\chi(1)=\lambda_\psi(1) is equivalent to ψ=χ\psi=\chi or ψ=χ−1\psi=\chi^{-1}. Let 𝔬\mathfrak o run over inversion orbits in 𝒳\mathcal X. Around the corresponding distinct number −χ−χ−1-\chi-\chi^{-1}, choose disjoint small positively oriented circles Γ𝔬\Gamma_{\mathfrak o}. For MM sufficiently close to one define 𝒬𝔬(M)=12πi∫Γ𝔬(zI−ΩΣ(M))−1dz.(8) \mathcal Q_{\mathfrak o}(M) =\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{\Gamma_{\mathfrak o}} (zI-\Omega_\Sigma(M))^{-1}\,dz. \qquad\text{(8)} All contours and neighborhoods here may depend on N,mN,m. The finite resolvent is holomorphic in MM along them. Its elementary spectral integral is an idempotent, the projectors are disjoint and sum to II, and they commute with all total generators and all total intertwiners. For example these assertions follow by integrating the resolvent identity; on a Jordan block the integral is II or zero according to whether its eigenvalue lies inside the contour. Thus no root Jordan directions have been removed.

Triangularity in (5) applies also to the resolvent. Its constant diagonal block is (z+MKr+M−1Kr−1)−1(z+MK_{\mathrm{ r}}+M^{-1}K_{\mathrm{ r}}^{-1})^{-1}. It follows exactly that B(𝒬𝔬)=P𝔬:=∑χ∈𝔬Pχ,B(𝒬𝔬W)=P𝔬B(W).(9) B(\mathcal Q_{\mathfrak o})=P_{\mathfrak o} :=\sum_{\chi\in\mathfrak o}P_\chi,\qquad B(\mathcal Q_{\mathfrak o}W)=P_{\mathfrak o}B(W). \qquad\text{(9)} In particular 𝒬𝔬W\mathcal Q_{\mathfrak o}W is an analytic actual intertwiner family, with its original highest cut and tail pivot. These projectors localize that source, not just an auxiliary scalar polynomial.

H’s exact residue equation, applied to (9), gives ∑χ∈𝔬(χ−χ−1)tχW(1)=0.(10) \sum_{\chi\in\mathfrak o} (\chi-\chi^{-1})t_\chi^W(1)=0. \qquad\text{(10)} For a two-element orbit this says tχW(1)=tχ−1W(1).(11) t_\chi^W(1)=t_{\chi^{-1}}^W(1). \qquad\text{(11)} It holds for every analytic total intertwiner, without a braid or reflection assumption. A singleton orbit χ=±1\chi=\pm1 gives no additional constraint.

There is also a purely polynomial proof of (10). Apply H’s residue to p(ΩΣ)Wp(\Omega_\Sigma)W, using (7). The result is ∑χ∈𝒳(χ−χ−1)p(−χ−χ−1)tχW(1)=0for every p.(12) \sum_{\chi\in\mathcal X} (\chi-\chi^{-1})p(-\chi-\chi^{-1})t_\chi^W(1)=0 \quad\text{for every }p. \qquad\text{(12)} Interpolation on the distinct numbers indexed by 𝔬\mathfrak o isolates one orbit. Formula (8) adds the actual holomorphic projector through the collision, which polynomial interpolation only at M=1M=1 would not by itself specify.

3. Literal turnover of the reduced braid traces

For this section W=WβW=W_\beta is the actual unmarked same-color braid word, with its common crossing scalars. On one balanced head put C(M)wa=ca(M)wd−a,ca(M)=∏j=1aMQj−M−11−Qj,c0=1.(13) C(M)w_a=c_a(M)w_{d-a},\qquad c_a(M)=\prod_{j=1}^a\frac{MQ^j-M^{-1}}{1-Q^j}, \qquad c_0=1. \qquad\text{(13)} The map goes from the head at M−1M^{-1} to the head at MM with its Chevalley action. It is holomorphic and invertible near one: ca(1)=(−1)ac_a(1)=(-1)^a. Directly from the literal arrows, C(M)E(M−1)=F(M)C(M),C(M)F(M−1)=E(M)C(M),C(M)K(M−1)=K(M)−1C(M).(14) \begin{split} C(M)E(M^{-1})&=F(M)C(M),\\ C(M)F(M^{-1})&=E(M)C(M),\\ C(M)K(M^{-1})&=K(M)^{-1}C(M). \end{split} \qquad\text{(14)} For instance the consecutive ratio in (13) is (MQa−M−1)/(1−Qa)(MQ^a-M^{-1})/(1-Q^a); substitution verifies both nonzero arrows. The forced extreme arrows also match. With the branch α=d+log⁡M/log⁡q\alpha=d+\log M/\log q, the weight operator is sent to its negative, since the reflected color has α′=2d−α\alpha'=2d-\alpha.

The Chevalley map E↔FE\leftrightarrow F, K↔K−1K\leftrightarrow K^{-1} reverses the coproduct order and fixes the Casimir (4). Let 𝖩\mathsf J reverse the mm tensor factors, and put 𝒞(M)=C(M)⊗m𝖩\mathscr C(M)=C(M)^{\otimes m}\mathsf J. The actual universal crossing in Y has its Cartan factor unchanged by changing both weight signs; its nilpotent term En⊗FnE^n\otimes F^n becomes Fn⊗EnF^n\otimes E^n with the same coefficient. Tensor reversal restores the checked crossing. This proves, for both signs, 𝒞(M)Wβrev(M−1)𝒞(M)−1=Wβ(M),𝒞(M)ΩΣ(M−1)𝒞(M)−1=ΩΣ(M),(15) \begin{split} \mathscr C(M)W_{\beta^{\mathrm{ rev}}}(M^{-1}) \mathscr C(M)^{-1}&=W_\beta(M),\\ \mathscr C(M)\Omega_\Sigma(M^{-1}) \mathscr C(M)^{-1}&=\Omega_\Sigma(M), \end{split} \qquad\text{(15)} where βrev\beta^{\mathrm{ rev}} replaces each σi±1\sigma_i^{\pm1} by σm−i±1\sigma_{m-i}^{\pm1}, without reversing the order of the word. This is a finite matrix identity, not ordinary symmetric-power covariance.

For explicit scalar bookkeeping, the full common crossing factor is qα2/2q^{\alpha^2/2}. The full Cartan operator is qH1H2/2q^{H_1H_2/2}, so (15) retains that factor rather than stripping it. The zero-framing twist θ(M)=q(α2−2dα)/2(16) \theta(M)=q^{(\alpha^2-2d\alpha)/2} \qquad\text{(16)} obeys θ(M−1)=θ(M)\theta(M^{-1})=\theta(M). The original modified-dimension line ηN/DN(M)\eta_N/D_N(M) is likewise reciprocal. Thus their prescribed external products introduce no missing multiplier in the stated unmarked turnover. Any other external line remains explicit.

To extract reduced traces, first take generic MM where the values (6) are distinct, and let Πχ(M)\Pi_\chi(M) be the individual total-Casimir spectral projector. From (5) and (2), every first occupation diagonal block of Sm−1ΠχWSmS_m^{-1}\Pi_\chi W S_m is PχB(W)P_\chi B(W). Positive powers of F1F_1 cannot return to that diagonal. Therefore Tr⁡full(ΠχW)=NtχW(M).(17) \operatorname{Tr}_{\mathrm{ full}}(\Pi_\chi W) =N\,t_\chi^W(M). \qquad\text{(17)} This auxiliary full trace computes a reduced block trace; it does not replace the highest-cut pivotal source by an ordinary trace.

Since λχ−1(M−1)=λχ(M)\lambda_{\chi^{-1}}(M^{-1})=\lambda_\chi(M), (15)–(17) imply tχWβ(M)=tχ−1Wβrev(M−1).(18) t_\chi^{W_\beta}(M) =t_{\chi^{-1}}^{W_{\beta^{\mathrm{ rev}}}}(M^{-1}). \qquad\text{(18)} The Artin half twist Δm\Delta_m conjugates σi\sigma_i to σm−i\sigma_{m-i}. 4. The original closing row on one pair

Define the actual localized source by Φ𝔬(M)=Φ0(𝒬𝔬(M)W(M)).(21) \Phi_{\mathfrak o}(M) =\Phi_0(\mathcal Q_{\mathfrak o}(M)W(M)). \qquad\text{(21)} It sums exactly to the original source. H gives on a punctured meridian disk Φ𝔬(M)=DN(M)ε(MmN−M−mN)∑χ∈𝔬(Mmχ−M−mχ−1)tχ(M).(22) \Phi_{\mathfrak o}(M) =\frac{D_N(M)} {\varepsilon(M^{mN}-M^{-mN})} \sum_{\chi\in\mathfrak o} (M^m\chi-M^{-m}\chi^{-1})t_\chi(M). \qquad\text{(22)} Its removable value is the corresponding portion of H’s first-jet row: Φ𝔬(1)=12ε∑χ∈𝔬(χ+χ−1)tχ(1)+12εm∑χ∈𝔬(χ−χ−1)tχ′(1).(23) \Phi_{\mathfrak o}(1) =\frac1{2\varepsilon}\sum_{\chi\in\mathfrak o} (\chi+\chi^{-1})t_\chi(1) +\frac1{2\varepsilon m}\sum_{\chi\in\mathfrak o} (\chi-\chi^{-1})t_\chi'(1). \qquad\text{(23)} For an actual braid and a two-element orbit, (20) reduces this to Φ{χ,χ−1}(1)=χ+χ−1εtχ(1)+χ−χ−1εmtχ′(1).(24) \boxed{\quad \Phi_{\{\chi,\chi^{-1}\}}(1) =\frac{\chi+\chi^{-1}}{\varepsilon}t_\chi(1) +\frac{\chi-\chi^{-1}}{\varepsilon m}t_\chi'(1). \quad} \qquad\text{(24)} A singleton is counted only once and contributes χtχ(1)/ε\chi t_\chi(1)/\varepsilon. The exact normal coefficient and the derivative of the entire reduced word have both been retained.

There is a useful elementary insertion consequence. If a(M)a(M) is a holomorphic scalar, then Φ0(a𝒬𝔬W)(1)=a(1)Φ𝔬(1).(25) \Phi_0(a\,\mathcal Q_{\mathfrak o}W)(1) =a(1)\Phi_{\mathfrak o}(1). \qquad\text{(25)} In the first-jet calculation the extra term would be a′(1)∑χ∈𝔬(χ−χ−1)tχ(1)/(2εm)a'(1)\sum_{\chi\in\mathfrak o} (\chi-\chi^{-1})t_\chi(1)/(2\varepsilon m); it vanishes by the internal residue (10). This does not say that an arbitrary short tangle acts as a scalar on this multiplicity space.

5. Exact scope of the localization

The weights group total occupations modulo NN. If k=∑ajk=\sum a_j, then χk=x0rQ−k\chi_k=x_0^rQ^{-k}; complementing every occupation gives χrd−k=χk−1\chi_{rd-k}=\chi_k^{-1}. Nevertheless (19) is a trace identity on the whole UχU_\chi, not an asserted equality separately for each integer degree kk. Nor does the actual turnover become simple occupation complementation after the triangular reduction.

For inserted coupons, literal conjugation by 𝒞(M)\mathscr C(M) still gives their exact turnover, with its meridian dependence. To identify the result with the same unmarked braid in (19), those coupons would also need the corresponding covariance. Nonintertwining marks retain their original Ward defects and are not put into H’s homogeneous first-jet formula by definition.

The finite projectors in (8) need not be uniformly bounded as NN grows. They retain whole generalized Casimir blocks, not a geometric line within them. All actual χ\chi’s lie on the unit circle; these identities alone do not continue a physical weight to an off-circle loxodromic eigenvalue. Even different internally regular pair contributions can cancel in their final sum. For instance the identity has tχ=dim⁡Uχt_\chi= \dim U_\chi, derivative zero, and its complete pivotal source is zero. No positivity or nonzero geometric coefficient is inferred from (24).

The result is an exact source-compatible finite localizer and an actual sector turnover, with the highest cut, pivotal line, root Jordan data and first meridian normal preserved. Quantum normals and ordinary-color closed jets still require their whole original adapters.
