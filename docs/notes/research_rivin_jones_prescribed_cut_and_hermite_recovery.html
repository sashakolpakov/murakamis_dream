The external highest-weight cut and full Hermite boundary recovery

Private written comparison, 14 November 2026. The external rivin-jones formula fixes one highest-weight input and output and traces the remaining heads with its specified KK. This note transports that actual functional, rather than replacing it by an unmarked trace. It then recovers every coefficient of a canonical Newton boundary polynomial by explicit inserted versions of the same functional. The recovery includes the first derivatives at all double Casimir roots, at every level N≥2N\ge2.

The boundary polynomial recovered here has degree below NN in each independent pair coordinate. Products of several free incidences at one vertex must be retained as ordinary polynomials after those incidences have been recovered separately. A single pair operator does not retain a multiple of PNP_N.

2. When the lowest-head observation equals the original cut average

Suppose YY commutes with the total physical E,F,KE,F,K. Let the original physical all-head cut average be Φb(Y)=1NTr⁡[(I⊗g⊗(b−1))Y],g=ηK.(7) \Phi_b(Y)=\frac1N\operatorname{Tr} [(I\otimes g^{\otimes(b-1)})Y], \qquad g=\eta K. \qquad\text{(7)} Equations (5)–(7) now give Lext,b(X)=ηb−1Φb(Y).(8) L_{{\mathrm{ ext}},b}(X)=\eta^{b-1}\Phi_b(Y). \qquad\text{(8)} For b=1b=1, an invariant operator is already scalar and the same identity holds. This is the same distinction retained by the exact source formulas in the complete multiplicity module, Section 4: its scalar identity is proved on actual intertwiners, while its unrestricted marked contraction remains a matrix.

3. Exact framing of a closed knot braid

Let ww denote the writhe of an external bb-strand word, and let YY be its physical sign-reversed word under (3). 6. Scope of the recovered boundary

Equations (14), (19), and (20) recover all canonical pair data, including every first normal, from explicit finite contractions. They use Casimir coupons, not only crossing powers. At composite levels crossing eigenvalues can coincide at different Casimir nodes; recovery from crossing powers alone is not asserted.

If a free vertex has several incident canonical boundary factors, its retained ordinary polynomial is their product before reduction. Its degree can exceed N−1N-1. Passing first to one pair matrix identifies this polynomial with its remainder modulo PNP_N, and none of the observations above can undo that loss. The incidence factors, or their full independent coefficient tensor (15), must be retained before their free coordinates are identified. This is an exact requirement on the boundary representation, not an asymptotic estimate.

The external scalar routine alone does not return the inserted observations (14). The equations specify the additional boundary contractions required for an exact mathematical correspondence; no claim of an implemented boundary API is made here. No volume asymptotic or geometric saddle selection is inferred from the finite correspondence.
