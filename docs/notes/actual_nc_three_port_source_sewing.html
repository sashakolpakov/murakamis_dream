Exact three-port source sewing before and after the root normal

29 October 2026. An actual NC/Morse vertex substitution has a generic three-port elimination, with an explicitly normalized theta denominator. At a supported boundary it preserves the original FIXED first normal: the new raw marked scalar is the product of the two old raw scalars divided by the positive actual marked theta scalar. This is not root multiplication of circle coupons. Unsupported boundaries retain their explicit open-column/source jets before an actual six-head support identity proves their complete-circle contribution zero.

The theorem is channelwise at arbitrary N, with all three correlated boundary labels and original normal factors retained. It supplies a survival transfer when the two actual input sources are known. It does not prove a new all-N asymptotic for an unsolved primitive cube block, identify arbitrary six-pants boundary maps, or complete the graph-volume problem.

1. A literal NC vertex substitution, including the pointing

Let A,B be ordered NC/Morse graphs of the corrected exposed-source theorem. A has an interval black vertex u on edge slots (a,a+1,a+2), and an EXPOSED white marked cap elsewhere. B has an outer white vertex w on edge slots (0,k,E_B-1), so every other white block is in one of its two gaps. Give B the exposed mark at w. Match the three boundary labels in their displayed order; call them (a0,b0,c0).

Remove u and w and glue their three edge ends IN ORDER. Concretely replace A’s three-slot interval by B’s whole edge row, map the three old A contacts to a+(0,k,E_B-1), move subsequent A slots by E_B-3, and map every B slot e to a+e. Keep the other vertices. Black A blocks surround or avoid the replacement interval; the inserted black blocks are noncrossing within it. The old white blocks are mapped monotonically, and new white blocks lie strictly in the two previously empty contact gaps. Thus both new partitions remain noncrossing.

The original p/ell matchings give a literal new Morse word. A’s exposed marked cap remains exposed; no inserted white block can surround its contacts. All statements below use that point, not a bare mark moved to a nested cap. The two merged edge exponents ADD. The new edge has ONE coupon R^(e_A+e_B)L_j, not the product of the two old circle coupons. No L_j^2 has been substituted or inverted at the root.

2. A genuine invariant output column, not a guessed scalar exterior

Fix generic zeta near q=exp(pi i/N), and the canonical ordinary Q_j tensors, duals and actual edge constants of the first-normal theorem. Assume first that every local ordinary triple is triangular. Remove ONLY B’s outer white row D(Theta_c0,b0,a0). Contract every other ordinary vertex in B. The resulting column is

Y_B(zeta) in Q_a0 tensor Q_b0 tensor Q_c0.       (1)

It is a genuine homogeneous invariant. For a direct proof, the noncrossing black source is built by inserting interval invariant triples, hence is globally invariant. Every white block other than w can then be removed in its innermost interval order, by a genuine ordinary invariant covector. All blocks are in w’s two gaps. What remains are its three ordered output ports, with no intervening cap, braid, or free tensor permutation.

The ordinary invariant space has dimension ONE at generic zeta, and its normalized Theta_a0,b0,c0 has corner (2a0,b0+c0-a0,0) equal to one. Every local coefficient is regular at the root. Consequently

Y_B=alpha_B Theta_a0,b0,c0,
alpha_B=(Y_B)_(2a0,b0+c0-a0,0),                 (2)

with alpha_B a HOLomorphic finite function, computed by the open magnetic contraction. This is an all-entry identity of that source column and can be paired with an arbitrary exterior row. No invariant or regularity assumption about A’s exterior row is needed.

Let Xi_A,Xi_B,Xi_new be the complete ordinary networks. The fully normalized ordinary theta is

Xi_theta=D(Theta_c0,b0,a0)Theta_a0,b0,c0.

Then (2) gives exact generic identities

Xi_B=alpha_B Xi_theta,
Xi_new=alpha_B Xi_A,
Xi_theta Xi_new=Xi_A Xi_B.                     (3)

The scalar alpha_B is not defined by contracting the unknown final invariant: it is the explicit open corner in (2). In particular its regularity is proved BEFORE division by Xi_theta. If the boundary triple is nontriangular, the actual invariant column (1) is zero and the new source is identically zero. Nontriangular interior vertices are likewise zero; they are not omitted as a semisimple convention.

3. The denominator has an exact root order and source normalization

Write a,b,c for the boundary labels in this section and set

J=a+b+c, r=b+c-a, r1=a+b-c, r2=a+c-b,
F_l=(zeta^2;zeta^2)_l.

The independently proved finite Vandermonde evaluation is

Xi_theta=(-1)^r1 zeta^[2c-r1(r-1)]
   F_(2b)F_r2 F_(J+1)
         /[(1-zeta^2)F_r F_(2a)F_r1 F_(2c)].    (4)

All denominator factorials have index <N. Thus

ord_q Xi_theta=1 if J>=d, and 0 if J<d, d=N-1.  (5)

The numerator contains at most one factor 1-zeta^(2N), since J+1<2N. Formula (4) applies to odd simple and even/odd paired axes without separating away their original source factors.

Let F_A,F_B,F_new be the ordinary GENERIC physical closures in the chosen regular Hopf lift, and use C,S as in the first-normal theorem. The new constants are exactly

C_new=C_A C_B/C_theta,
C_theta=product_(boundary j)h0_j gamma_j,
S_new=product_(vertices of A except u)s_v
                          *product_(vertices of B except w)s_v. (6)

Boundary edge twists are added, so their omega factors occur in C_new with the correct sum. Both removed source factors are the actual s_abc and s_cba. In particular the cross-multiplied identity is

F_theta F_new=F_A F_B,
F_theta=C_theta s_abc s_cba Xi_theta.           (7)

This keeps ALL phases, G factors, h0 first normals and fixed gamma units. Each remains present exactly once on a new edge. The source identity is generic and does not divide by nilpotent root operators.

For J>=d the actual s_abc(0),s_cba(0) are nonzero, Xi_theta has a simple zero, and

F_theta'(0)=nu_N Z_theta!=0,
nu_N=2N(-1)^N/(q-q^(-1)),
Z_theta=N^2 k_a k_b k_c
                     s_r s_r1 s_r2/s_(J-d) *R_N(a,b,c)^2>0. (8)

Here R_N is the already proved positive finite source partition, not an assumed quantum 6j. Equation (8) is a fully explicit boundary weight. It may be exponential and must not be called a polynomial normalization cost.

4. Exact fixed-normal gluing and order addition

Each exposed physical closure is F_G=D_N z_G, where D_N=(-1)^d[N]_zeta has its simple zero at q and z_G is the actual regular open scalar. For a SUPPORTED boundary (J>=d), equation(7) therefore gives a holomorphic identity

z_new=z_A z_B/z_theta,

because z_theta(q)=Z_theta>0. In particular the original raw marked scalars, with no transported or repeated mark, obey

Z_new=Z_A Z_B/Z_theta.                          (9)

This formula holds even if one of the pieces has a zero raw scalar or a retained unsaturated marked-white current. Those pieces are the complete exposed original sources, not just a selected leading saddle. All such terms are included in their z_G.

For nonzero generic ordinary spin networks, (3) gives

v(Xi_new)=v(Xi_A)+v(Xi_B)-v(Xi_theta),           (10)

where v is the exact order at q. The regular alpha_B proves this without assigning a first-nonzero convention to the original source. For supported seams and nonzero physical closure germs one similarly has v(F_new)=v(F_A)+v(F_B)-1. Thus two genuine simple physical zeros give a simple zero after sewing. If a piece has higher order, its fixed first-normal value vanishes and (9) retains that zero.

Any original external lines remain explicit. If K_G=chi_G Z_G, then the corresponding relation has the extra factor chi_new chi_theta/(chi_A chi_B); it cannot be suppressed without the actual framing/normalization certificate.

5. All boundary channels, including the unsaturated current

The ordinary column (2) and identity (3) hold at EVERY triangular folded boundary label. For J<d, Xi_theta(q) is a unit. The two removed source factors s_abc(q),s_cba(q) are zero; it is not valid to divide root marked scalars by Z_theta=0. Instead the exact formula is

Z_new=C_new/nu_N *d/dt|0
      {S_new(t) Xi_A(t) alpha_B(t)}.             (11)

All local source factors in S_new are defined by actual finite Clebsch/cup contractions and their explicit first jets. Thus (11) retains the local unsaturated source normals, including a local marked-white current inherited from A. It does not infer them from the zero closed values of the removed boundary vertices. Using alpha_B=Xi_B/Xi_theta here is harmless because this particular ordinary denominator is a root unit.

For full circles, the shared boundary triple must be summed ONCE, and its three labels remain correlated. If Z_A(a,b,c),Z_B(a,b,c) denote the complete coherent interior sums with those labels fixed, the whole new scalar is exactly

sum_(a+b+c>=d) Z_A(a,b,c) Z_B(a,b,c)/Z_theta(a,b,c). (12)

There is no omitted unsaturated correction in this COMPLETE-circle operation. The exposed six-port pruning theorem proves it before the exterior or mark acts: remove B’s three boundary coupons and every ordinary cap other than its omitted outer vertex. The remaining physical six-head column is a regular generic invariant, so its root lies in the actual saturated S_N. The three boundary coupons commute with those disjoint cap removals and can be applied last. Their full complete-circle product annihilates S_N when J<d. This proves that the exact expression(11) vanishes as a WHOLE source; it does not set its local source derivatives to zero. Nontriangles are zero as well. The positive denominator in (12) does not make its numerator a square: the two actual packets need not be conjugates. A physical mirror/reflection identity would still be needed before asserting a positive graph norm.

6. An explicit primitive cube corner, with three finite sum variables

For the genuine ordered cube use black blocks

(0,1,2),(3,4,11),(5,9,10),(6,7,8)

and ordinary white blocks (1,8,9),(2,3,7),(4,5,6), leaving outer white (0,10,11) OPEN. Put j_0=a,j_10=b,j_11=c, fix boundary magnetic colors r_0=2a,r_10=b+c-a,r_11=0, and write J_v for the sum of edge labels at the indicated vertex. Define

B0=j_1+j_2-a,
B1=j_3+j_4+c,
B2=j_5+j_9+a-c.

For free magnetic integers u,v,w set

r_1=u, r_2=B0-u,
r_3=v, r_4=B1-v,
r_5=w, r_9=B2-w,
r_8=J_(1,8,9)-B2-u+w,
r_7=J_(2,3,7)-B0+u-v,
r_6=J_(4,5,6)-B1+v-w.                          (13)

The remaining black magnetic equation holds identically by boundary weight conservation. Therefore alpha_cube is exactly the triple sum over (13) of the FOUR canonical black Theta entries and the THREE genuine white dual Theta entries. Require 0<=r_e<=2j_e on EVERY edge; otherwise the term is zero. Each individual entry is the explicit finite ordinary coefficient(6),(7) of the first-normal theorem, including its phase. There is no extra closed physical source or unknown inverse frame in this formula.

This is a usable all-N primitive open-column scalar. The full column is alpha_cube Theta_boundary by (2), so the triple sum is not just one arbitrarily chosen bad entry. At spin one its exact evaluation is g3(D3-9D^2+29D-32), recovering the independently proved generic flow model. A positive high-fold evaluation or a large-N nonzero estimate for the general triple sum remains open; absolute values of its summands would discard precisely the coherence to be proved.

7. An exact order-two counterexample to a graph-only criterion

At N=5, d=4 and M=2, take two actual ordered cubes. In A give its interval black edges (0,1,2) labels (1,1,1); every other edge has label2. In B give its outer white edges (0,10,11) labels (1,1,1); every other edge has label2. Perform the typed substitution. The new graph has fourteen vertices and twenty-one edges and is three-edge-connected by the vertex-sum proof. Its marked cap remains exposed. Every remaining vertex triple has sum FIVE or SIX, hence is fully supported and has the strictly positive local source scalar. The separating three-edge triple itself has sum3<d.

The new ordinary network nevertheless has order EXACTLY TWO. Here is a compact exact certificate for the only additional zero needed. Evaluate B’s triple sum(13) in Q(q), with

Phi_10(q)=q^4-q^3+q^2-q+1=0.

There are fifty nonzero terms. Group them by u=r_1=0,1,2,3. Their reduced coefficients in the basis (1,q,q2,q3) are

u=0: (  58, -58,   0,  -92),
u=1: (-386, 236, -92,  476),
u=2: ( 476,-234, 150, -528),
u=3: (-148,  56, -58,  144).

Each column sums to zero. This is an exact reduction of the explicit finite canonical Clebsch sum, using only polynomial arithmetic and inversion of units modulo Phi_10. It proves alpha_B(q)=0 over the complex numbers, not just in one finite field.

Its first derivative is nonzero. The exact rational first-jet formulas reduce modulo the prime 1021 at q->432, a primitive tenth root, to

alpha_B'(q)->909,
Xi_A'(q)->775.

Every denominator is a unit in this reduction. A zero algebraic derivative would reduce to zero, so these nonzero images certify nonzero derivatives. Xi_A(q)=0 independently: A’s exposed outer triple is (1,2,2), whose regular outer-column factor contains the simple Xi_theta zero. Thus v(Xi_A)=1 and v(alpha_B)=1. Equation(3) gives v(Xi_new)=2; its second Taylor coefficient has nonzero image 775*909=1006 modulo1021.

All NEW vertex source factors are nonzero by their supported sums. Therefore the new physical ordinary closure also has order two, and the original fixed-first-normal marked sector is ZERO. Its higher normal is nonzero. This is not a zero of the full channel sum or a counterexample to a volume statement. It proves that connectivity, absence of a two-edge neck, and positive supported VERTEX scalars do not by themselves ensure simple source order. The separating source constraint matters. It agrees with the all-N six-port pruning theorem rather than deleting its higher jet.

The focused audit checks open-column invariance and all entries, exact local theta normalization, NC/pivotal pointing, ordinary and full marked sewing, the cube triple-sum corner, the physical divided-current pruning, and the exact cyclotomic certificate above. No graph-volume or unconditional all-N cube survival claim is made.

The exact exposed statements do not give an all-N primitive-core estimate.
