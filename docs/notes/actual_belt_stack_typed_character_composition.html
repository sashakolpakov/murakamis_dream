Typed belt-stack character composition and marked peripherals

1. Regular pair frame at the actual complete source

Use the regular local port chart. Set c=w+w^(-1), w=-exp eta, and

a=-[[1,1],[0,1]], b=-[[1,0],[c-2,1]],
A=ba=[[1,1],[c-2,c-1]].                              (1)

These main meridians have trace -2. The central signs do not affect their PSL classes; both signs in their product cancel. They form an irreducible pair near c=-2, including that point.

For the source-selected local z=mathscrR(R^(-1)L_(N-h)) branch, let X,Y,H,L,U be the literal stationary functions of the mixed local source lift, continued through the quarter point using the newly proved actual auxiliary-color packet. They satisfy

U=(wY-1)/(w-Y), L=(H-X)/(1-HX),
(1+YL)/(1+Y/L)=L^2/H.                               (2)

The regular centralizer K_z of A constructed in the linked chart has determinant1, eigenratio U on the ordered A-eigenvalues (w,w^(-1)), and gives the rotated map

(a,b) -> (K_z a K_z^(-1),K_z b K_z^(-1)).           (3)

Put P=aK_z a(-1)K_z(-1)=b^(-1)K_z bK_z^(-1). Then Tr P=-L-L^(-1). With Delta=(1+YL)(1+Y/L),

J=(I-YP)/sqrt(Delta), C=(YI-P)/sqrt(Delta), JC=-P.   (4)

The branch of sqrt is its regular complete branch. The ACTUAL unrotated ports are incoming (K_z bK_z(-1),b(-1)) and outgoing (K_z a(-1)K_z(-1),a). Formula(4) gives exactly the threaded negative half-twist group relations. On the P-eigenflag with eigenvalue lambda=-L^(-1), C has the source-prescribed positive auxiliary meridian eigenvalue M=H^(-1/2). This is the local literal port/flag theorem, not a trace-only assertion.

3. The sharp box has its own actual positive-half meridian
