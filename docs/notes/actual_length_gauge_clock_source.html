The original clock source under the factorial LENGTH gauge

29 October 2026. The full original clock co-source has polynomial Riesz norm in the proposed factorial length frame, provided every crossing is first kept as a singleton over-event. Each crossing then supplies a complementary pair of lengths. This is an exact source embedding for arbitrary signed braid width, including the original cut, pivots, clock feedback and external scalar.

After an overbridge has been unwound, its factorial Gamma is no longer a harmless phase. In the same length frame its exact norm is a positive angle-simplex partition. The two descriptions are consistent: unwinding has moved a genuine weight from the tensor network into the clock co-source. It must be retained there or assigned back to a specified bulk tensor.

This note concerns the LENGTH registers, not a replacement metric on physical heads. It does not establish a positive geometric state or a sharp knot bound.

1. The actual singleton presentation

Put q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, d=N-1, x=-q^-1 and

F_a=(Q;Q)_a,  s_a=|F_a|,
L_a=s_a^2/N,
A_a=L_a^(1/8),
D_a=A_a^2=s_a^(1/2)/N^(1/4).                              (1)

The exact reflection s_a s_(d-a)=N gives

D_a D_(d-a)=1,  D_d=N^(1/4).                            (2)

For a binary block B_l(X,Y;a)=product_(r<l)(X-Q^r aY), use the proposed normalization b_l=B_l/D_l. Its coefficients, phases and spectator dependence are otherwise unchanged. All original LENGTH SLOTS are retained, including l=0: D_0=N^-1/4 and b_0=N^1/4. A raw empty factor B_0=1 can be omitted, but after normalization its scalar must then be recorded. The complementary products below include those zero-length slots.

In the original charged source, one may choose EVERY crossing as a singleton over-event: no identity requires compressing a maximal overbridge. For a word with m crossings on b heads there are then m+b anchor events. At crossing j its two binary blocks have lengths

(d-h_j,h_j), 0<=h_j<=d.                                 (3)

All m clocks are independent. Their feedback into spectator ports is the same exact signed integer map as in the global source theorem; it is not suppressed or untwisted. The m crossing Gamma factors are now just

kappa^(sigma_j)(-1)^h_j
              Q^[sigma_j h_j(h_j+1)/2],

of modulus one. Every other factor in W_h is a power of x,Q or an original pivotal phase. Thus |W_h|=1. The original complete dimension/framing line is denoted chi_N and remains outside.

Every closure block has length d. Therefore the whole block normalizer is EXACTLY, and independently of every clock,

product_edges D_(length)=N^(b/4).                       (4)

There is no factorial action hidden in (4): each pair in (3) cancels by (2). If f_h is the ACTUAL normalized b-network coefficient, the exact scalar is

K_N=(chi_N/N) N^(b/4) sum_(h in{0,...,d}^m) W_h f_h.      (5)

The original cut factor1/N has not been absorbed into an invented boundary normalization. The f_h can be exponentially large and oscillatory. Equation(5) makes no assumption about their size.

2. Exact source norm and a real full-source Cauchy inequality

Give the clock space C({0,…,d}m) its standard orthonormal basis. The Riesz norm of the co-source in (5) is EXACTLY

||ell_N||=|chi_N| N^(b/4+m/2-1).                        (6)

Indeed there are N^m entries, all of the same modulus. This gives the genuine original-source inequality

|K_N|^2 <= |chi_N|^2 N^(b/2+m-2) sum_h |f_h|^2.          (7)

For fixed word and the known complete scalar line, the source factor is polynomial. This is an exact Cauchy–Schwarz theorem, not a proposed positive pivotal trace or a favorable cut.

Equivalently, the length-register inclusion

|h_1,...,h_m> -> tensor_j |d-h_j,h_j>
                       tensor |d>^(tensor b)           (8)

is isometric before the diagonal D frame. Applying D on every length leg leaves every clock pair fixed and multiplies the closure legs by N^(b/4), exactly as in (4). Arbitrary diagonal unit-phase clock/flow feedback commutes with this length frame; its actual phases remain in the network. It supplies no additional Riesz cost at the boundary. No relation between a length leg and a physical head index is presumed.

There is a slightly cleaner equivalent source. Contract the b original closure blocks by the exact source reproducing law BEFORE taking a norm. Each normalized full block contributes the retained scalar D_d-1=N-1/4, canceling (4). The remaining clocks are still independent and the new weights W’_h have modulus one. For a one-component presentation with at least one crossing, the closure edges form the proved forest, so no unexplained full-loop factor is introduced. The resulting literal co-source has

K_N=(chi_N/N) sum_h W'_h f'_h,
||ell'_N||=|chi_N|N^(m/2-1).                             (9)

The original pivots survive as their exact root phases in f’_h and W’_h. Equations(6),(9) use their own typed bulk families; one must not keep (6)’s family while deleting its closure scalar.

These polynomial SOURCE norms do not estimate the positive energy sum|f_h|^2. In particular they do not identify that energy with the geometric angle partition. A large energy is compatible with an exact Cauchy inequality. Global angle support, edge characters and coherent normalized-block gluing are additional requirements.

3. The exact cost after overbridge unwinding

An m_e-encounter bridge has r=m_e+1 composition lengths

n_0=d-h_1, n_j=h_j-h_(j+1), n_(r-1)=h_(m_e),
n_i>=0, sum_i n_i=d.                                    (10)

The original Gamma in the bridge theorem satisfies EXACTLY

|Gamma_e|=s_(h_1)/(s_(h_m) product_j s_(h_j-h_(j+1)))
         =N/product_(i=0)^(r-1) s_(n_i).                (11)

No normalization is chosen by dividing by the knot value. Under the SAME b=B/D convention its transformed co-source coefficient has modulus

|Gamma_e product_i D_(n_i)|
    =N^(1-r/4) product_i s_(n_i)^(-1/2).                (12)

Thus its exact squared Riesz norm, on the orthonormal composition basis, is

N^(2-r/2) Z_(r,N),
Z_(r,N)=sum_(n_i>=0,sum n_i=d) product_i s_(n_i)^(-1).    (13)

For a collection of compressed bridges, after the closure forest has been contracted, independence of their monotone clock domains gives the WHOLE original co-source norm

||ell_comp||^2=|chi_N|^2/N^2
               product_e [N^(2-r_e/2) Z_(r_e,N)].       (14)

The feedback phases can couple the bulk coefficients but do not change this norm: their moduli are one. Keeping a normalized coefficient family and declaring its Gamma source unit would change the original source.

For r=2, reflection gives Z_(2,N)=1 EXACTLY. Consequently (13) is N, agreeing with the singleton source norm in (9). For r>=3, the source weight really can have exponential scale. If Lambda(theta)=-integral_0^theta log(2sin u)du, then

log Z_(r,N)=N r Lambda(pi/r)/pi+O_r(log N).                (15)

In particular r=3 is the positive tetrahedral angle energy of rate v_3/pi. This is useful geometric weight, not an unrecorded error or a claim of geometric knot gluing.

Proof of(15). The uniform factorial estimate gives log s_a=N J(a/N)+O(logN), with J(t)=-Lambda(pit)/pi, including a=0,d. There are polynomially many compositions and changing their total d/N to1 changes the maximum by O(logN/N), using the integrable logarithmic endpoint modulus of continuity. It remains to maximize sum_i Lambda(pix_i), x_i>=0,sum x_i=1.

For r>=3 a maximum cannot lie on the boundary: adding a small positive coordinate increases the sum by a positive -delta log delta term, except at a pure vertex; there, distributing delta equally among two zero coordinates gives a positive delta log2 term. At an interior maximum no coordinate can be at least1/2: each of the other coordinates is strictly smaller than its complement, so shifting mass to one of them increases the function by the derivative -pi log(2sin(pi*x)). On the remaining region all coordinates are below1/2, where this function is strictly concave. Its unique maximum is therefore the uniform point x_i=1/r. Rounding that point to a composition of d gives the matching lower bound with O(logN) loss. This proves(15).

An isometric complementary-pair encoding cannot erase the Riesz norm(13): isometries preserve it. It may instead KEEP the positive simplex weight as a bulk/source tensor. That is exactly the weight(12), and its global geometric incidence must still be proved. The polynomial singleton source and the weighted compressed source are two exact organizations of the SAME state.

4. What this settles for the proposed special inner product

The original clock source is not an uncontrolled exponential boundary factor in the uncompressed length frame: (6)–(9) give its exact polynomial norm at arbitrary width. The original clocks, phases, closure and full external line are explicit.

This does not mean that a contracted normalized b-network scalar has polynomial norm, nor that its anchor coefficient functional has polynomial dual norm in every proposed polynomial metric. For example, with the half-factorial metric ||Xa||2=s_a, a valence-v anchor’s exact dual norm squared is sum_(sum a_i=d, a_i<=l_i) product_i s_(a_i)^(-1). Complementary valence2 is tame; higher valency carries another positive simplex weight when its capacity constraints allow it. Those gluing weights must be retained in a global Cauchy/angle calculation. These are typed source and dual norms. They do not replace every trivalent tensor by a positive sum-d vertex with complementary pairings on every edge: a closed such network would require both Vd=Ed and 3V=2E, which are incompatible for d>0,V>0. In the actual binary blocks the two flow powers sum to the BLOCK LENGTH l, not d; the remaining length registers and oriented dual source charges are essential. A geometric edge-incidence theorem cannot be inferred by forgetting those types.

No geometric coefficient or lower bound follows from this source norm alone. The useful next question is whether the COMPLETE normalized state energy, with these exact source and gluing weights, has the desired geometric edge support and volume bound.
