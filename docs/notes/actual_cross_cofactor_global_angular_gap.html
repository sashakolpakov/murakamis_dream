A global angular gap for the actual cleared cross cofactors

The whole-source local packet now assembles these results for the actual full numerator. The original global spectral filling period remains open.

31 October 2026. Internally reviewed. All matrices, paths and spectral nodes are at the ORIGINAL level N. This is a local cofactor estimate on a thin spectral cover strip, not a Gaussian-cycle or whole-source theorem.

1. Statement and exact source inputs

Put d=N-1, J=floor(d/2), theta=pi/N,

a_r=4cos^2((r+1/2)theta), s_r=2sin(r theta), A=x+y, P=xy, p_r=s_r^2 A, q_r(u)=s_r s_(r-1)(a_(r-1)+u)P.

For j=J-k, with 0<=k<=k0 fixed, let R_j(b,u) be the actual cleared cofactor with recurrence

R_j=b^j product_(r<j)(a_r+u)+p_j R_(j-1)+q_j(u)R_(j-2). (1)

This includes D,E’s R factor, and odd G. A zero face remains zero. Assume the positive entries of (x,b,y) range in a fixed positive compact, A>0, and at least TWO entries are positive. Let epsilon>0. If the circular phases of the positive face entries have diameter at least epsilon, then there exist gamma>0,C,K and a FIXED strip height tau>0 such that, uniformly for |Im delta|<=tau and all real Re delta,

|R_j(x exp(i theta_x),b exp(i theta_b),y exp(i theta_y); -4sin^2(pi delta))| <=C N^K exp(-gamma N) R_j(x,b,y;0). (2)

The statement includes b=0 and either single protected outer face. For b=0,xy=0 there is only one positive variable and the cofactor is an exact monomial; there is no relative-angle condition to assert. The case x=y=0 is excluded by the actual marked matching-edge dictionary.

The exact node adapter, proved independently from the original matrices, is, for 0<=a<=j,

R_j(b,-a_a)=K_(a,j)(-a_a) S_(2a)D_a(x,b,y), (3) R_a(b,-a_a)=S_(2a)D_a(x,b,y).

Here K_(a,a)=1, K_(a,a-1)=0, and K follows p_v,q_v above. Also

0<=R_a(b,-a_a)<=R_a(b,0), 0<=K_(a,j)(-a_a)<=K_mono(a,j):=product_(v=a+1)^j p_v. (4)

These are COEFFICIENTWISE statements. For completeness the latter normalizes K by product s_v^2, giving

Q_r=A Q_(r-1)-gamma_r xy Q_(r-2), gamma_r=1-s_a s_(a+1)/(s_(a+r)s_(a+r-1)) in [0,1].

Induction on Q_r-xQ_(r-1)=y[Q_(r-1)-xQ_(r-2)] +(1-gamma_r)xyQ_(r-2) proves Q_r>=0, then Q_r<=A^r. Thus no absolute value of a sign-changing tail is assumed positive. The nodal positivity proof is in the reviewed cross-cofactor handoff §8.

2. A strict gap at all far nodes

Choose a fixed small delta0>0, for example delta0<1/8. Call a node far if a/N<=1/2-delta0. This definition only separates nodes for this proof. It does not alter the physical folded label or its half-index. We prove, whenever at least two faces are positive,

R_j(b,-a_a)<=C exp(-gamma_far N)R_j(b,0) (5)

uniformly over ALL these far nodes. This estimate needs no angular separation. Polynomial factors are harmless and can be absorbed by slightly reducing gamma_far.

First suppose xy>0; b may be zero. The fixed interval

I=[1/2-delta0/2, 1/2-delta0/4]

lies AFTER every far row a and BEFORE j for all sufficiently large N. On it every ratio

q_v(0)/(p_v p_(v-1)) =(a_(v-1)xy)/(s_v s_(v-1)(x+y)^2)

is bounded below by a positive compact-set constant. Choose cN disjoint pairs of adjacent steps in this interval. Starting with the all-monomer path from j to a, either retain each selected pair or replace it by its original dimer. The choices are independent and all paths are physical. Their sum therefore gives

K_(a,j)(0)>=exp(c’ N)K_mono(a,j). (6)

On the other hand, K_(a,j)(0)R_a(b,0) is precisely the subset of paths in R_j(b,0) whose starting row is at most a and which visit a. There is no multiplicity in splitting a path at that row. Combining (4),(6) proves (5). All selected rows stay in a fixed strict bulk interval, so neither a near-zero initial row nor the actual folded endpoint affects the constants.

Now suppose xy=0 and b>0. Write A for the sole positive outer face. The recurrence is first order and its actual zero-spectral starting terms are

W_i=b^i product_(r<i)a_r product_(v=i+1)^j [s_v^2 A], R_j(b,0)=sum_(i=0)^j W_i.

For a far node, all starts i>a vanish exactly, and for i<=a the ratio to W_i is product_(r<i)(1-a_a/a_r). Uniformly for sufficiently large N, a_a>=c_delta0>0 and a_r<=4. Hence starts i>=eta N have ratio at most (1-c_delta0/4)^(eta N).

The remaining short starts have an independent exact gap. Choose fixed eta>0 small enough that, on the given positive compact,

W_(i+1)/W_i=b a_i/(s_(i+1)^2 A)>=1+c for all 0<=i<=2eta N

and all sufficiently large N. Such eta exists from the displayed original sine/cosine entries. Each i<=eta N is then exponentially smaller than W_floor(2eta N), by at least eta N consecutive ratios. Summing at most N terms gives

sum_(i<=eta N) W_i<=CN exp(-c’eta N)R_j(b,0).

Combining the two ranges proves (5), including x=0 and y=0. The only remaining case b=0,xy=0 is the excluded one-variable angular case.

3. The near nodes have the original determinant angular gap

For a/N>=1/2-delta0, a>=a0N for a fixed a0>0. Apply the EXACT PSD contraction of actual_positive_face_coefficient_saddle.md §2 to

D_a=det[bI+(x/2)H_-+(y/2)H_+].

Its finite pair-trace lower bounds hold for every a0N<=a<=floor(d/2), not only a deficit o(N): this is the explicit scope and bulk-square proof in actual_q_hahn_boundary_model_curvature.md §§1–4.

When x,b,y are all positive, the b–x and b–y pair traces are at least cN. If the positive-phase diameter is at least epsilon, one of these two phase differences is at least epsilon/2. When one outer face is zero, its single remaining pair has the same bound (220/022). When b=0 and x,y>0, the original 202 variance/pair trace is at least cN. Consequently in every applicable zero pattern,

|D_a(x exp(i theta_x),b exp(i theta_b),y exp(i theta_y))| <=exp(-gamma_ang N)D_a(x,b,y). (7)

This is a determinant modulus inequality on the ENTIRE phase torus, not a local complex-log estimate. The positive polynomial K in (3) has modulus at most its positive-radius value. Equations (3),(4),(7) therefore prove the same exponential nodal bound relative to R_j(0). For far nodes, (5) and the positive coefficient statement (4) already prove the bound at arbitrary phases. Thus ALL nodes a=0,…,j satisfy

|R_j(faces with phases;-a_a)| <=C exp(-gamma0 N)R_j(positive faces;0). (8)

4. Exact interpolation, including the omitted last nodes

The actual spectral degree of R_j is at most j. The full physical node family a=0,…,J is the even Chebyshev-root or odd Chebyshev-Radau family after the affine change z=-1-u/2. Its exact interpolation Lebesgue norm on [-4,0] is O(log(N+1)); this follows from discrete cosine inversion on the original N-point odd-angle grid and the bound |D_J(t)|<=min(2J+1,1/|sin(t/2)|). This is the same exact interpolation used in the reviewed cofactor handoff, not interpolation of full V.

If j=J, apply it directly to (8). If j<J, DO NOT use an unavailable D_a adapter at a>j. Instead put

p(u)=R_j(faces with phases;u), Q(u)=p(u) product_(a=j+1)^J (u+a_a).

The degree of Q is at most J. At the retained nodes its absolute value is bounded by 4^k times the right side of (8); at the omitted nodes it is EXACTLY zero. Full-grid interpolation gives

||Q||_[-4,0]<=C_k log(N+1) exp(-gamma0 N)R_j(0). (9)

Division by these k fixed-in-number factors costs only a polynomial in N, even though their roots cluster at the endpoint. Here is an elementary bound with no spacing assumption. A polynomial p of degree j and interval supremum M has derivative supremum at most C(j+1)^3 M: expand in Chebyshev polynomials, whose coefficients have magnitude at most2M, and use ||T_l’||<=l^2. Around a point attaining M, a one-sided interval of length c/(j+1)^3 has |p|>=M/2. Remove small neighborhoods of the k roots occupying less than half that interval. At a remaining point every factor has magnitude at least c_k/(j+1)^3. Hence

||p||[-4,0]<=C_k(j+1)^(3k)||Q||[-4,0]. (10)

This also holds for complex p, by the same cosine coefficient bound. Thus every required literal terminal cofactor satisfies an exponential angular gap on the WHOLE real spectral interval, with a polynomial prefactor. No last row or parity node has been silently replaced.

Finally the elementary polynomial Bernstein bound on [-4,0] gives

|p(-4sin^2(pi delta))| <=exp(2pi j |Im delta|)||p||_[-4,0]. (11)

Indeed z=-1-u/2=-cos(2pi delta)=(w+w^-1)/2 with |w|=exp(2pi|Im delta|); apply the exterior maximum principle to the polynomial of degree j after this substitution. Since j<=N/2, choosing pi tau<gamma0/2 makes (9)–(11) imply (2), with a possibly reduced gamma. The estimate is uniform for the full real fold interval and its periodic copies, not only near delta=0.

5. Scope

This proof uses exact original node identities, finite positive paths, actual PSD angular bounds and finite polynomial interpolation. No complex starting-row contour or generic off-node PSD representation is required. Every physical starting row and the protected-middle i=0 contribution remains. The one-variable monomials are explicitly exempt because their sole phase is a common homogeneous phase.

The local factors can serve a whole shared-face angular argument only after checking the actual connected phase graph, keeping every other factor under a uniform spectral bound, and retaining the original marked coefficient and finite central prefactors. The local theorem alone is not a full shared coefficient asymptotic or a selected Gaussian period.
