Fractional boundary data with an original crossing leaf

22 November 2026. Written research proof. Earlier author-final notes and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

We put one coherent input exactly at zero, where it is the original positive crossing element divided by N\sqrt N. The fractional boundary packet then has two numerator families instead of three. Its infinite upper end has a nonzero limiting rate. We retain that end and prove a relative estimate only where it has a strict gap. The result supplies two adjacent initial values on every fractional boundary orbit in this region.
