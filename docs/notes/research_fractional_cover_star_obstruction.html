Fractional rows and the obstruction to entropy-preserving covers

31 October 2026. Open research interface. The lemmas below concern the convex weighted LEFT/CROSS/RIGHT comparison on the original marked source. They disprove an automatic entropy-preserving cover construction. They do not disprove the desired strict fixed-sector capacity bound by complete volume, or any statement about the original quantum invariant.

The fractional forest reduction gives the exact flow network and stationary equations. This note isolates what an unbranched cover would additionally have to achieve. The finite-sector cover theorem uses “cover” in a different sense: a finite cover of label patterns by valid shared-radius estimates, not a topological covering space.

1. Projected flows and their entropy

Every original ordinary face has demand one; the two protected faces have demand zero. CROSS retains its two original triangle rows, each with supply one half. For a triangle allocation a≥0a\ge0, ∑a=1/2\sum a=1/2, the exact entropy is

C(a)=∑jf(aj),f(s)=Λ(πs)+Λ(π/2−πs)π,f″(s)=−2πsin⁡(2πs)≤−2π. C(a)=\sum_j f(a_j),\qquad f(s)=\frac{\Lambda(\pi s)+\Lambda(\pi/2-\pi s)}{\pi}, \qquad f''(s)=-\frac{2\pi}{\sin(2\pi s)}\le-2\pi.\tag{1}

Consequently, including closed boundary allocations by continuity,

C(a¯)−C(a)¯≥π∥a−a¯∥2¯. C(\overline a)-\overline{C(a)} \ge \pi\,\overline{\|a-\overline a\|^2}.\tag{2}

This is the normalization of the fixed-triangle capacity lemma. At a selected LEFT or RIGHT port let oo be the overloaded ordinary middle, pp the opposite middle, and x,yx,y the original outer faces. The fractional comparison row is

qo=1−t2,(qx,qp,qy)=tα,0≤t≤1,∑α=12,henv=tC(α). q_o=1-\frac{t}{2},\qquad(q_x,q_p,q_y)=t\alpha, \qquad0\le t\le1,\quad\sum\alpha=\frac{1}{2}, \qquad h_{\mathrm{env}}=tC(\alpha).\tag{3}

At t=0t=0 this is the pure identity endpoint eoe_o. At t=1t=1 it is one retained triangle α\alpha and the pure-middle half triangle eo/2e_o/2. All protected incidences remain zero; the four original faces are distinct, so o∉{x,p,y}o\notin\{x,p,y\}.

For a rational feasible flow choose qq with all qteqt_e integral. Clearing denominators specifies qteqt_e retained half-triangle atoms and q(1−te)q(1-t_e) identity atoms at each selected port. It does not specify a cover with closed face stars.

2. A necessary cover-realization condition

Allow qq lifts of every face and qq copies of every original coupon and triangle incidence. At each lifted face require exactly one copy of each original incident corner. Copies may be assigned to lifts by arbitrary permutations: no common sheet labeling or trivial monodromy is assumed. An actual unbranched source cover must also retain the cyclic order, but we do not impose that additional restriction here.

Require the following conditions:

  • Each ordinary lifted face has total load one; every protected lift has load zero.
  • Every CROSS triangle copy and every retained triangle copy has supply one half.
  • The mean allocation of a CROSS row is its prescribed ara_r. Among the qteqt_e retained copies of a selected row, the mean is its prescribed normalized allocation αe\alpha_e.
  • The two atom counts at every selected port are the prescribed counts above.

These are necessary for an unbranched original-source cover representing the given projected flow. We permit more incidence permutations than a genuine geometric cover; a failure here cannot be repaired solely by choosing different source monodromy.

Let HH be the fractional entropy and HcovH_{\mathrm{cov}} the sum of the triangle entropies on such a realization. Equation (2) gives

qH−Hcov≥π∑rCROSS∑s=1q∥ar,s−ar∥2+π∑e∑sretained∥αe,s−αe∥2. \begin{aligned} qH-H_{\mathrm{cov}}\ \ge{}& \pi\sum_{r\ \mathrm{CROSS}}\sum_{s=1}^q \|a_{r,s}-a_r\|^2\\ &+\pi\sum_e\sum_{s\ \mathrm{retained}} \|\alpha_{e,s}-\alpha_e\|^2. \end{aligned}\tag{4}

Therefore entropy preservation—or the entropy increase needed to deduce H≤Vol⁡/qH\le\operatorname{Vol}/q from a cover-volume bound—requires every positive-mass triangle allocation to be identical on all its copies. Varying angles across sheets cannot increase entropy while preserving the projected flow.

The original bounded bipartite flow becomes integral after scaling by two, so it can be decomposed into feasible pure-atom flows. This does not supply the desired entropy comparison: (4) has the opposite direction. Such a decomposition can strictly lose entropy.

3. Quantitative obstruction at a fractional sink

Suppose a port ee has 0<te<10<t_e<1, overloaded face oo, and no other fractional row uses oo as a retained-triangle input. Distinct strict overloads have distinct children, because each contributes more than one half to a demand-one face. All other load at oo therefore comes from CROSS triangles or retained triangles of terminal t=1t=1 rows. Let MM be their number of corner incidences at oo. Feasibility forces M>0M>0.

The prescribed mean of these corners sums to te/2t_e/2. At a lift whose selected atom has state s∈{0,1}s\in\{0,1\}, exact face closure requires their sum to be s/2s/2. Their total deviation from their projected values is (s−te)/2(s-t_e)/2. Cauchy–Schwarz on these MM corners and (4) yield

qH−Hcov≥πM∑lifts of o(s−te2)2=qπte(1−te)4M>0. \begin{aligned} qH-H_{\mathrm{cov}} &\ge\frac{\pi}{M}\sum_{\text{lifts of }o} \left(\frac{s-t_e}{2}\right)^2\\ &=q\,\frac{\pi t_e(1-t_e)}{4M}>0. \end{aligned}\tag{5}

There are exactly qteqt_e state-one copies. The estimate uses only each lifted star, and permits arbitrary permutations between different face lifts. Its loss per sheet is independent of qq; even asymptotically lossless atomization is unavailable under these hypotheses.

In particular this applies to a single fractional row. More generally, draw a dependency arrow from a fractional child used as an input to the child of the row using it. An acyclic dependency graph has a sink to which (5) applies.

At a finite positive stationary point, the exact KKT equations give, for every genuinely fractional row,

Lo=exp⁡(2Q(Lx,Lp,Ly))≥Lx+Lp+Ly. L_o=\exp\bigl(2Q(L_x,L_p,L_y)\bigr) \ge L_x+L_p+L_y.\tag{6}

The inequality is strict when the input has at least two positive weights. Nonmonomial dependency arrows strictly increase curvature. If there are no equal-curvature monomial cycles, the fractional dependency graph is acyclic and (5) applies. Terminal t=1t=1 rows need not obey this ordering; their triangle corners are already included among the MM corners.

This does not assert that a global sector maximizer is fractional or has value above complete volume. It shows that a fractional stationary point’s forest and curvature order do not supply the proposed lossless cover.

4. An exact original five-port flow

Use the literal width-three word, in the original compiler’s zero-based slots,

[1,3,2,1,3],marked top cap 0. [1,3,2,1,3],\qquad\text{marked top cap }0.

The protected faces are {0,1}\{0,1\}, the ordinary faces are {2,3,4,5,6}\{2,3,4,5,6\}, and the original quartets are

0(1,0,4,2)1(2,0,5,3)2(4,2,6,5)3(1,4,0,6)4(6,5,0,3) \begin{array}{c|c} 0&(1,0,4,2)\\ 1&(2,0,5,3)\\ 2&(4,2,6,5)\\ 3&(1,4,0,6)\\ 4&(6,5,0,3) \end{array}\tag{7}

The ten triangle rows are distinct, all fifteen edges occur twice, and the seven vertices form the original simple-white sphere. This is the exact reduced width-three source in the shared-radius theorem, not an abstract graph requiring a new plat adapter.

Set only port 3 to RIGHT, with the other four CROSS. For 0≤β≤1/20\le\beta\le1/2, take t3=2βt_3=2\beta and α3=e6/2\alpha_3=e_6/2. Its loads and entropy are

q4=1−β,q6=β,h3=0. q_4=1-\beta,\qquad q_6=\beta,\qquad h_3=0.\tag{8}

The two CROSS rows of a port are its original (x,b,y)(x,b,y) and (x,c,y)(x,c,y), in that order. Their nonzero face loads are

portfirst rowsecond row0{2:12}{4:β,2:12−β}1{2:β,3:12−β}{3:12}2{5:12}{6:12}4{5:12}{6:12−β,3:β} \begin{array}{c|c|c} \text{port}&\text{first row}&\text{second row}\\ \hline 0&\{2:\tfrac12\}&\{4:\beta,\ 2:\tfrac12-\beta\}\\ 1&\{2:\beta,\ 3:\tfrac12-\beta\}&\{3:\tfrac12\}\\ 2&\{5:\tfrac12\}&\{6:\tfrac12\}\\ 4&\{5:\tfrac12\}&\{6:\tfrac12-\beta,\ 3:\beta\} \end{array}\tag{9}

Every row has supply one half and is supported on its original triangle. Equations (8)–(9) give load exactly one at each ordinary face and zero at both protected faces. Thus the full shared constraints hold. The entropy is

H(β)=3C(β,12−β,0). H(\beta)=3C(\beta,\tfrac12-\beta,0).\tag{10}

It is strictly concave, vanishes at both endpoints, and has a unique maximum on this particular feasible segment at β=1/4\beta=1/4. There,

t3=12,H(14)=6Gπ>0, t_3=\frac{1}{2},\qquad H(\tfrac14)=\frac{6G}{\pi}>0,\tag{11}

where GG is Catalan’s constant. This segment is not asserted to contain the full fixed-sector maximizer.

At face 4 the other three incidences are the face-4 corners of port 0’s second row and both rows of port 2. Their projected loads are 1/4,0,01/4,0,0. Equation (5) gives at least π/48\pi/48 entropy loss per sheet, for every cover degree and every incidence permutation.

In this sparse example the loss is even stronger: every admissible pure-atom realization preserving the projected flow has zero entropy. A nonnegative corner with projected mean zero vanishes on every copy. Thus both port-2 face-4 corners stay zero, and face-4 closure forces port 0’s second row to a triangle vertex. Face-2 closure then forces port 1’s first row to a vertex; face-3 closure forces port 4’s second row to a vertex. All other rows were already monomial. This reasoning is local at each lifted star and does not require global sheet labels.

Atomization is not empty: the β=0\beta=0 and β=1/2\beta=1/2 flows form a feasible two-sheet atomization with average β=1/4\beta=1/4. Their total entropy is zero, rather than twice (11). Integral decomposition therefore exists, but loses entropy in exactly the direction that the proposed cover-volume comparison would need to preserve.

5. The remaining general question

The desired universal statement is still

maxoriginal fixed-sector flowHenv<ΦD. \max_{\text{original fixed-sector flow}} H_{\mathrm{env}}<\Phi_D.

The example has neither a certified fixed-sector capacity excess nor an original quantum asymptotic excess. It rules out automatic unbranched cover atomization that preserves the projected flow without entropy loss. A different construction must change the projected flow and prove a compensating global entropy inequality, or allow branching and prove an appropriate new volume comparison. Summed angles 2πq2\pi q alone do not provide either condition.

The focused exact check used the original Morse compiler and rational arithmetic: ten triangle incidences, fifteen paired edges, five rational flows, forty row sums, thirty-five face targets, and the exact two-endpoint average. The coefficient 1/481/48 is exact. The entropy and covering inequalities are analytical; these finite checks are not a proof of a general covering topology or of the open universal sector bound.
