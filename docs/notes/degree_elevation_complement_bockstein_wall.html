The degree-elevation complement is one marked quantum-normal wall line

Research theorem and actual crossing/source calculation, 27 October 2026. The complement omitted by actual cut degree elevation has dimension 2N+1, but its whole memory-Borel complex contracts to ONE two-term wall complex. The contraction retains the two extreme corners and uses no division by the wall parameter. Its root norm is O(N) in the natural unitary spin metric.

The source on that wall is essential. The original coherent cut assigns it 2^(-N); the uniform interval assigns 1/(N+1). Their exponential ratio cannot be discarded as a Ward homotopy. An explicit charged component of the FIRST ACTUAL universal R normal hits the wall generator in an ordered current calculation. No full-network cancellation or selected geometric contribution is inferred from these local statements.

1. The actual missing subspace and its global boundary polynomial

Use actual matrix degree elevation ℰN:End⁡Sym⁡N−1→End⁡Sym⁡N\mathcal E_N:\operatorname{End}\operatorname{Sym}^{N-1} \to\operatorname{End}\operatorname{Sym}^{N}. It is injective, unital, cut-preserving and equivariant for the ACTUAL divided classical commutators. Write

ℐN=im⁡ℰN,End⁡Sym⁡N=ℐN⊕𝒲N. \mathcal I_N=\operatorname{im}\mathcal E_N,\qquad \operatorname{End}\operatorname{Sym}^{N} =\mathcal I_N\oplus\mathcal W_N.

The summands have adjoint spins 0,…,N-1 and N respectively; dim⁡𝒲N=2N+1\dim\mathcal W_N=2N+1. A concrete quotient map is

ob⁡N(C)(u)=uNℓN(u)CψN(−1/u)=∑i,j(−1)j(Ni)CijuN+i−j. \operatorname{ob}_N(C)(u) =u^N\ell_N(u)C\psi_N(-1/u) =\sum_{i,j}(-1)^j{N\choose i}C_{ij}u^{N+i-j}. \tag{1.1}

Its kernel is exactly I_N: the symbol numerator is divisible by 1+ut precisely when its restriction t=-1/u vanishes. The degree bound then leaves a numerator of bidegree at most N-1, giving the unique old matrix. Its image is every polynomial of degree at most 2N. Thus W_N is the GLOBAL H0(O(2N)) boundary module, rather than an arbitrary missing collection of matrix entries. Its two endpoint corners are e^N=N!E_(0,N) and f^N=N!E_(N,0).

For complete direct verification, (1.1) takes ad e,ad f,ad h to partial_u, 2Nu-u^2 partial_u, 2N-2u partial_u, and takes Ad(Q^(-J)) to g(u)->Q^N g(u/Q). The independent incidence/source calculation records this quotient and its one-sided clock residuals.

One may project onto W_N algebraically without choosing a split matrix basis: the adjoint Casimir 𝒞=(ad⁡h)2/2+ad⁡ead⁡f+ad⁡fad⁡e\mathcal C=(\operatorname{ad}h)^2/2+ \operatorname{ad}e\operatorname{ad}f+ \operatorname{ad}f\operatorname{ad}e has eigenvalues 2l(l+1). Its polynomial projector onto eigenvalue 2N(N+1) is ∏l=0N−1(𝒞−2l(l+1))/[2N(N+1)−2l(l+1)]\prod_{l=0}^{N-1}(\mathcal C-2l(l+1))/ [2N(N+1)-2l(l+1)]. All denominators are nonzero constants.

2. The exact whole Borel complex

Let R be the actual coefficient field completed in delta=1-Q^N, retaining Q=Q_N(1-delta)^(1/N). Work first in the untwisted target frame, with memory K=Q^(-J), rho=Ad K and F=ad f. The ACTUAL whole arrow gauge B_s(q) sends f to the original F^(N)|_s and commutes with memory K. Conjugating the complex below by B_s carries all its units and source marks. The memory K is not the old physical high-color head clock.

On any invariant spin module define

d0C=((ρ−I)C,FC),d1(X,Y)=(ρ−Q−1)Y−Q−1FX. \begin{split} d_0C&=((\rho-I)C,FC),\\ d_1(X,Y)&=(\rho-Q^{-1})Y-Q^{-1}FX. \tag{2.1} \end{split}

The identity rho F=Q^(-1)F rho proves d_1d_0=0 exactly over R. No root specialization has occurred.

For spin l choose a basis v_0,…,v_L, L=2l, satisfying Fv_j=v_(j+1) and rho v_j=Q^(l-j)v_j. Each j<L gives a separate three-term fork

R→a↦(Aja,a)R2→(x,y)↦Q−1(−x+Ajy)R,Aj=Ql−j−1. R\ \xrightarrow{a\mapsto(A_ja,a)}\ R^2 \xrightarrow{(x,y)\mapsto Q^{-1}(-x+A_jy)}\ R, \quad A_j=Q^{l-j}-1. \tag{2.2}

It contracts using h_1(x,y)=y and h_2(z)=(-Qz,0), whether or not A_j vanishes at the root. The only remaining terms are

[R→Q−l−1R]0,1,[R→Ql−Q−1R]1,2. [R\xrightarrow{Q^{-l}-1}R]_{0,1},\qquad [R\xrightarrow{Q^l-Q^{-1}}R]_{1,2}. \tag{2.3}

This is a literal direct decomposition, not a numerical rank argument. In particular it keeps both possible resonance lines.

For W_N take l=N and normalize the chain by

vj=(−1)N(2N)!(ad⁡f)jeN,v2N=fN. v_j=\frac{(-1)^N}{(2N)!}(\operatorname{ad}f)^j e^N, \quad v_{2N}=f^N. \tag{2.4}

The last equality follows by expanding the commutator: only its middle term survives, giving ad(f)(2N)eN=(-1)N(2N)!fN. The second arrow in (2.3) is a unit since its root is 1-Q_N^(-1). The entire complement therefore contracts to

[RfN→,Q−N−1,RfN]0,1,Q−N−1=δ1−δ. \boxed{\ [R f^N\xrightarrow{,Q^{-N}-1,}R f^N]_{0,1}\ }, \qquad Q^{-N}-1=\frac\delta{1-\delta}. \tag{2.5}

The coefficient has a SIMPLE zero, with derivative one. At the root H0 and H1 are each the one-dimensional marked wall line, and H2=0. Over the whole formal DVR, H0=0 and H1=R/(delta). Its root kernel and its first-normal Bockstein must therefore be retained together; no free whole horizontal source line is being asserted.

3. Explicit projection and contraction retaining every unit

For x=sum x_jv_j, y=sum y_jv_j and z=sum z_jv_j, projection to (2.5) is pi_0(C)=C_(2N) and pi_1(x,y)=x_(2N); inclusion uses v_(2N) in degree zero and (v_(2N),0) in degree one. Put

h1(x,y)=∑j=02N−1yj+1vj,h2(z)=(−Q∑j=12Nzjvj−1,(QN−Q−1)−1z0v0). \begin{split} h_1(x,y)&=\sum_{j=0}^{2N-1}y_{j+1}v_j,\\ h_2(z)&=\left(-Q\sum_{j=1}^{2N}z_jv_{j-1},\ (Q^N-Q^{-1})^{-1}z_0v_0\right). \tag{3.1} \end{split}

Direct substitution gives h_1d_0=I-iota_0pi_0, d_0h_1+h_2d_1=I-iota_1pi_1, and d_1h_2=I. Only QN-Q(-1), a unit, is inverted. The wall delta is never inverted. Conjugating all maps by the ACTUAL B_s(q) gives the same contraction on the actual whole target complement Ad(B_s)W_N. The original source/co-source must be conjugated or evaluated with those same B_s units; root B_s=I does not erase their derivatives.

In the unitary Fr metric ||v_k||^2=binom(N,k), e and f are adjoints. The highest-spin Hilbert–Schmidt module has an orthonormal chain with F-arrow coefficients sqrt((j+1)(2N-j)). Its anchored F inverse has norm 1/sqrt(2N). Using those coefficients in (3.1), its fork inverses have that same bound; the remaining unit inverse has root norm 1/[2 sin(pi/N)]. The orthogonal W_N projector has norm one. Hence the root complement contraction has norm O(N), explicitly at most max(1/sqrt(2N),1/[2 sin(pi/N)]). This is a bound on the COMPLEX contraction, not on every source functional or on a uniform off-root neighborhood.

4. The actual global co-source on the extra module

Under (1.1), the basis (2.4) is ob⁡N(vj)=N!uj/(2N−j)!\operatorname{ob}_N(v_j)=N!u^j/(2N-j)!. Thus any ACTUAL cut functional Phi restricts to the global dual Čech class

cΦ(u)=∑j=02N(2N−j)!N!Φ(vj)u−j−1in H1(O(−2N−2)). c_\Phi(u)=\sum_{j=0}^{2N} \frac{(2N-j)!}{N!}\Phi(v_j)u^{-j-1} \quad\text{in }H^1(O(-2N-2)). \tag{4.1}

Its residue pairing with ob_N(C) equals Phi(C) on W_N, by the explicit two-chart source pairing. The two corner components occupy different north/south Laurent modes. Neither is a zero Čech class merely because it was absent from the degree-elevation image.

For the normalized wall generator w=f^N/N!=E_(N,0), ||w||_HS=1, the ACTUAL paired coherent source gives

Φm(w)=(1−m2)N,Φ+(w)=2−N(m=0). \Phi_m(w)=\left(\frac{1-m}{2}\right)^N, \qquad \Phi_+(w)=2^{-N}\quad(m=0). \tag{4.2}

The uniform interval row gives instead

$$ \Phi_{\rm uni}(w)=\frac1{N+1}. \tag{4.3} $$

The ratio is 2^N/(N+1), GENUINELY exponential. For the unnormalized divided generator f^N the two values are N!/2^N and N!/(N+1). Their actual normalization has not been hidden by declaring its matrix unit to be the source value one.

The coupled original source makes the head endpoint explicit: $\Phi_{e_D}(w\otimes H_{\rm head})=2^{-N}(H_{\rm head})_{00}$. The Fr endpoints 0 and N are the SAME cyclic head endpoint. The uniform interval row is not that original coherent coupon. Since w is root Ward-closed, no root co-source homotopy on the full target can equate (4.2) and (4.3).

There is still an exact relative-endpoint interpretation: Phi_m(w)dm/2=-d[((1-m)/2)^(N+1)/(N+1)]. Its interval integral from -1 to +1 is 1/(N+1), while its endpoint-coherent value at m=0 is 2^(-N). Exactness of a primitive does not eliminate its marked endpoint jump. The global class (4.1), actual normal units, and relative endpoint density remain together.

The OTHER omitted corner w_high=E_(0,N) has response Phi_m(w_high)=((1+m)/2)^N. It is carried by the contracted forks, not a second H0 wall: Fw_high!=0. Its interval primitive is ((1+m)/2)^(N+1)/(N+1), with a nonzero POSITIVE-endpoint jump. Thus the two corners have different cochain roles even though their uniform integrals and their coherent values at m=0 agree. Contracting the highest corner requires its recorded Ward/source homotopy, rather than deleting its relative endpoint pairing.

5. An unavoidable charged FIRST ACTUAL R normal sees the wall

In the constant actual integral frame, put I_1=R_0^(-1)R_1. For r=N its first resonant transfer, including every head entry, is exactly

$$ (I_1)_{(-1,+1)}=N e\otimes f \otimes I_{\rm heads}. \tag{5.1} $$

This is the original universal R tail, not an appended operator: the first-normal coefficient is kappa_N E^(N) tensor F^(N), kappa_N=N(-1)^(N-1), and E(N)=(-1)(N-1)e. All nonresonant normal terms change at most ONE Fr block, while Cartan terms change neither. Thus no other first-normal term can cancel the charged component (-1,+1) of (5.1). The actual whole diagonal arrow gauges B_s(q) add first-normal gauge terms of ZERO Fr block charge. They therefore cannot erase this charged channel; their units remain in all other rows.

Let n=N-1 and choose actual old-head matrix coupons P_0=E_(0,0) and C_low=E_(n,0). Put X=E_N(P_0), Y=E_N(C_low). Their entries are

X=diag⁡(1,1/N,0,…),Y=N−1EN−1,0+EN,1. X=\operatorname{diag}(1,1/N,0,\ldots),\qquad Y=N^{-1}E_{N-1,0}+E_{N,1}.

Here fY=Yf=w. Consequently the charged (-1,+N) component of the ACTUAL adjoint normal current is

$$ \Pi_{(-1,+N)}[I_1,X\otimes Y] =N[e,X]\otimes w\otimes I_{\rm heads}. \tag{5.2} $$

Its first factor is [e,X]=−(N−1)E0,1−(N−1)E1,2/N[e,X]=-(N-1)E_{0,1}-(N-1)E_{1,2}/N, an elevated old head current. The second factor is precisely the wall generator, not an element of I_N. At a generic root the first factor is a memory-Cartan Ward term, since its charge has unit Q_N-1. Thus (5.2) is a concrete LOCAL Ward/carry source with a wall-valued coefficient; it is not a closed scalar knot invariant by itself.

Its two legitimate paired values are different:

$$ (\Phi_{\rm uni}\otimes\Phi_{\rm uni})(5.2) =-\frac{N-1}{N+1},\qquad (\Phi_+\otimes\Phi_+)(5.2) =-\frac{N(N-1)}{2^{2N-1}}. \tag{5.3} $$

Both are nonzero. The relative co-source can therefore detect this actual omitted-corner channel; it cannot be dropped as a zero Čech boundary or replaced by its uniform value.

Under the controlled coupled-source gauge G, the e and f factors also acquire their literal head shifts V^(-1) and V. The source coupon must be conjugated WITH the operator. Leaving a head shift behind while changing the source would give a different pairing, potentially zero. No such change is made in (5.3).

For the relative interaction-commutator used here, I(0)=I and its root commutator is zero. Thus its first coefficient depends only on (5.2), even when the input coupons and paired source have their actual whole lifts. This does NOT remove the separate lift terms in the whole Euler/current formula (8d) of the endpoint-completed clock theorem. That formula still uses the prescribed H_1 and its charged head neighbors. No general current descent follows by replacing it with this one isolated interaction channel.

6. Low raw PBW order versus ordered products and clock gauges

A PBW operator of degree d<N has normalized coherent numerator divisible by (1+ut)^(N-d), so ob_N vanishes. In particular raw interaction coefficients of order j lie in the elevation image on every Fr leg whenever N>2j, by the actual all-normal bound. The first possible order for a raw PBW complement is ceil(N/2). This is an exclusion bound; attainment by a particular actual crossing or closed word is a separate assertion.

This does NOT say multiplication by such a coefficient preserves the image: (5.2) is already a first-normal counterexample on elevated input coupons. Nor does it apply to a controlled source gauge containing genuine whole clock dilations. Their one-sided symbols can have nonzero ob_N even at root order. Their actions must be kept as whole functions with their source characters.

The Clebsch strictification keeps its complementary memory sector for precisely this reason; neither that construction nor (2.5) declares the full actual R network image-invariant. Every actual offimage current must be projected by (1.1) and then checked in the explicit complex (2.1). A CLOSED residual reduces to its ONE wall component; its value there uses the ORIGINAL source row, not automatically (4.3).

The global geometric Ward/current map, its charged head neighbors and the selected regulator norm remain to be proved. This result replaces an arbitrary 2N+1-dimensional missing source by an exact single marked Bockstein wall line and exhibits an actual R channel which makes its source normalization unavoidable.
