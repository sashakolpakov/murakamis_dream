The half-charge observation with a free outside color

Research proof, 4 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

The half-charge observation and its physical normal-defect rows retain their nonzero packets when the complete outside color is replaced by a fixed small complex neighborhood. The input is the literal full Newton kernel. A polynomial interpolation estimate controls it on the whole observation contour; the local free-edge theorem supplies the coefficient only near the saddle. Actual first normals control the omitted physical rows.

All outside derivatives below are derivatives in the ordinary spectral color, including at the complete color −2-2. No conclusion about a global coupled pair coefficient is inferred.

1. A whole-contour bound from the actual physical rows

5. Whole outside-period decay, with a complex strip

The complete half-charge contour also gives an upper bound on the whole outside period. This assertion concerns the exact observation and the exact defect values; it does not continue the local relative expansion (20) to distant outside colors.
