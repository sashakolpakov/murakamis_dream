The actual local source phase generates ordered character transport

30 October 2026. The explicit augmented-plat phase has a direct algebraic map to the original ordered pair character. This note proves that map, including the full factorial normalization, its centralizer transport, and its regular parabolic limit on the longitude cover. It is a local critical-phase theorem, not a claimed full-source asymptotic or a new choice of quantum invariant.

The remaining global issues are stated in section 6. In particular the local calculation does not assign the source action an absolute manifold regulator merely because its critical equations have the right character.

Subsequent comparison: the regular geometric chart supplies an unfolded complete geometric candidate, and the endpoint regulator theorem fixes its absolute volume anchor under full marked stationarity. Those additional results do not supply the physical cycle or global source estimate left open in this local note.

1. Retain the complete normalized coupon phase

Use the low physical degree chamber of the actual compiler. Let tau be the scaled pair degree, u and v its output and input first magnetic indices, xi the folded Casimir index, and alpha,beta the two internal q-Hahn variables. Set

J0(s)=integral_0^s Log(2 sin(pi w)) dw,
B(s,r)=J0(s)-J0(r)-J0(s-r),
E(tau,xi,u,alpha)=B(xi,alpha)+B(u,alpha)
                     +B(tau-u,xi-alpha)-B(tau,xi),
Phi(tau,xi,u,alpha)=E(tau,xi,u,alpha)
              +pi i [u-xi u+(tau+xi-1)alpha],
H(tau,xi)=2J0(tau)-J0(tau-xi)-J0(tau+xi-1).

All logarithms have the compiler’s actual factorial marks. The complete coupon, with retained half twist epsilon in {0,+1,-1}, has leading phase

Theta_epsilon=H+Phi(tau,xi,u,alpha)+Phi(tau,xi,v,beta)
                  +pi i[tau+(epsilon-1)(xi+xi^2)].       (1)

Its already recorded external kappa^epsilon is not removed or replaced; its leading constant is a unit phase and has no derivative in these variables. The finite offsets and normal-derived coefficients remain those of the exact compiler. Equation (1) is their leading phase, not an equality of finite quantum germs.

Write

T=e^(2pi i tau), U=e^(2pi i u), V=e^(2pi i v),
J=e^(2pi i xi), Z=e^(2pi i alpha), W=e^(2pi i beta).

On a regular logarithmic chart the two internal equations are exactly

P(U,Z)=0, P(V,W)=0,
P(X,Y)=T(J-Y)^2(X-Y)+(Y-1)^2(TY-XJ).                    (2)

Every formula below is localized only where its displayed denominators are nonzero. In particular these equations have not licensed deleting boundary strata. Their leading quadratic coefficients may be assumed nonzero for the symbolic certificate; the rational identities themselves hold wherever their displayed denominators are defined.

2. The actual Casimir equation, with its magnetic momentum

Direct differentiation of Phi gives

Y(X,Z)=e^(Phi_u)=(1-X)(TZ-XJ)/[J(X-Z)(T-X)].            (3)

The principal symbol of the original symmetric Jacobi block is

a(X)= (X-T)(1-X)/T,
d(X)=2X(1+T-X)/T,
Omega_cl=a(X)(y+y^-1)+d(X).

These follow directly from the physical tridiagonal entries, with q->1 and q^(N-1)->-1. Equation (2) implies

a(X)[Y(X,Z)+Y(X,Z)^-1]+d(X)=J+J^-1.                  (4)

This is an exact rational identity modulo P. For example its difference from the right side equals

-X(J-T)P(X,Z)/[JT(X-Z)(JX-TZ)].

Thus the critical phase yields the actual ordered pair trace, not just an independently chosen quadratic with the same number of roots.

The inverse change is explicit:

Z=XJ[y(T-X)+(1-X)]/[yJ(T-X)+T(1-X)].                  (5)

On its regular chart, the Jacobi equation (4) conversely implies (2). The local root is therefore selected by the actual magnetic momentum y; one does not choose a root by its numerical size.

3. Full normalization preserves the ordered product

Differentiating ALL terms of (1), including H and pi i tau, gives

D=e^(Theta_tau)
  =JZW(T-J)(T-U)(T-V)/[(TJ-1)(TZ-UJ)(TW-VJ)].          (6)

Omitting the inverse-measure factorials or their phase would change (6). Introduce an arbitrary nonzero common magnetic scale g. The actual incoming and outgoing raising-shift symbols are

(S1_in,S2_in)=(g Y(V,W),g),
(S1_out,S2_out)=(g/[D Y(U,Z)],g/D).                   (7)

The signs in (7) follow from a kernel: right multiplication by a raising shift differentiates its input positively, and left multiplication differentiates its output negatively. Total-degree conservation supplies the common scale g. No Hermitian conjugation has been inserted.

For a physical head define its original classical flag matrices by

K=-1/X, F=S(1-X), E=(X^-1-1)/S,
Lplus=[[K,E],[0,1]], Lminus=[[1,0],[-KF,K]],
G=Lminus^-1 Lplus=[[K,E],[FK,FE+K^-1]].                (8)

They have determinant one and trace -2. Prefix conjugation gives the ordered meridians A=G1 and B=Lminus1^-1 G2 Lminus1, exactly as in the proved quantum character identity. Their product is

P=B A=[[T^-1,E_tot],[F_tot/T,F_tot E_tot+T]],
F_tot=F1+K1^-1 F2, E_tot=E1 K2+E2.                   (9)

Equations (2),(3),(6),(7) give

F_tot,out=F_tot,in,
K_tot,out=K_tot,in=T^-1,
Tr P_in=Tr P_out=J+J^-1.                             (10)

For F_tot nonzero, (9) then gives E_tot,out=E_tot,in as well. Hence the ENTIRE ordered matrix P is preserved. This is stronger than trace matching. The identity continues wherever the full rational expressions are defined; the present proof uses the stated regular F_tot chart.

For reproducibility set g=1 and write

f=(1-V)Y(V,W)+T-V.

The first equality in (10) is the following explicit certificate:

[(1-U)/Y(U,Z)+T-U]-D f
  = VZ(J-T)(T-U)P(V,W)
     /[(V-W)(JT-1)(JU-TZ)(JV-TW)].                   (11)

The scale g merely conjugates the common matrices and costs no source normalization.

The HIGH-degree chamber has an equally literal adapter. If s is the physical pair degree divided by N, put

tau=2-s, u=a-s+1, v=c-s+1,

where a,c are its physical output/input first indices. These are the leading versions of the exact compiler’s d=N-1 index translation; the finite offsets remain in its amplitude. The physical exponentials are (U/T,U^-1) at the output and (V/T,V^-1) at the input. Chain-rule differentiation of the SAME phase gives

Y_out,high=Y(U,Z), Y_in,high=Y(V,W),
D_high=1/[D Y(U,Z)Y(V,W)].                           (11a)

Use (7) with D_high and these PHYSICAL head exponentials. This is the actual Chevalley-and-flip map (X,S)->(X-1,-S-1) on both low heads, with the common scale consistently transported. It exchanges the total F and E and inverts total K. Therefore the whole high ordered product is preserved as well. There is no independent high frame gauge or differentiation of a root-only identity involved. The high coupon has the SAME channel derivative (12), and the four centralizer identities (14) are also checked symbolically in these physical high coordinates. Thus the local principal map covers BOTH original degree chambers, while retaining their separate integer lifts.

4. The channel derivative is a centralizer eigenratio

Let R0=e^(partial_xi Theta_0). Direct differentiation yields

R0=ZW(J-1)^4(TZ-UJ)(TW-VJ)
    /[JUV(J-Z)^2(J-W)^2(T-J)(TJ-1)].                 (12)

For J!=+/-1 define the matrix

C=I+(R0-1)/(J-J^-1) (P-J^-1 I).                     (13)

Its eigenvalues on the P-eigenlines J and J^-1 are R0 and 1, respectively, so det C=R0 and C commutes with P. Moreover

A_out=C A_in C^-1,
B_out=C B_in C^-1.                                 (14)

These are exact rational identities on (2). An entirely polynomial certificate is obtained by putting

Ctilde=(J-R0/J)I+(R0-1)P.

Clear only its displayed denominators in Ctilde A_in-A_out Ctilde. Every one of the four resulting entries has zero remainder modulo the two explicit quadratics P(U,Z),P(V,W). The accompanying executable certificate performs this calculation over Q(T,U,V,J), not at sampled roots or numerical shapes. Equations (8),(9) supply all matrices used. The second identity of (14) follows from the first and preservation of P.

Thus R0 is the eigenratio of a genuine ordered-pair conjugator. It is not merely a scalar Casimir value. To compare with determinant-one peripheral lifts one must retain the selected square root of R0.

For the retained half twist, the FULL derivative is

e^(partial_xi Theta_epsilon)=R0(-J)^epsilon.           (15)

The physical conjugator in (14) is independent of epsilon at fixed magnetic/channel variables. Equation (15) removes the explicit half twist from its peripheral marking. Indeed positive original Hurwitz transport is (A,B)->(A^-1 B A,A); with P=BA it is simultaneous conjugation by P^-1-I, whose eigenratio is -J^-1. Therefore internal stationarity in the pure spectral phase, R0(-J)^epsilon=1, gives exactly that recorded signed Hurwitz factor. It would be incorrect to call R0 itself the unchanged auxiliary meridian for every epsilon.

For the entire core’s cosine branch exp[+pi i N(1-2xi)chi], the additional xi derivative is -2pi i chi. Hence its channel equation is

R0(-J)^epsilon=e^(2pi i chi).                        (16)

The opposite Fourier branch inverts the right side. The external color deficit chi is distinct from the internal channel xi.

The primitive collar is fixed by the original threading word, not by centralization alone. Apply the positive Hurwitz rule chronologically at [2,1,1,2] to the three meridians (A,B,C_aux), and put K=P^-1 C_aux P. The exact three outputs are

(K^-1 A K, K^-1 B K, K).

Closing the auxiliary sets K=C_aux. Thus the pure physical transport is conjugation by C_aux^-1. The actual zero-self-linking disk longitude is P=BA, with no meridian factor, as proved in the literal mixed-loop marking, sections 4–5. Its argument uses the two underpasses and the absence of an auxiliary self-crossing; it is independent of plat width.

On the chosen P eigenflag J=-ell, the primitive auxiliary meridian has eigenvalue M_aux. Hence the pure transport eigenratio is M_aux^-2. The retained main half twist adds the already computed factor (-J)^(-epsilon) to the physical transport and adds no auxiliary longitude detour. Consequently the exact marked relation is

R0(-J)^epsilon=M_aux^-2=e^(-U_aux),
M_aux=-q^(N-h) -> e^(-pi i chi), U_aux=-2pi i chi.    (16a)

This calibrates (16) and the primitive collar in the original source convention. Opposite eigenflag choices are retained by the Fourier sign, rather than replacing the projective multiplier by an unmarked square root.

5. The endpoint retains additive transport, rather than losing it

At the geometric endpoint J=-1, both quadratics (2) have simple roots on any chart where their discriminants are nonzero. Equation (12) obeys

R0=1                                                 (17)

identically modulo those two endpoint quadratics. Thus complete internal channel stationarity is automatic EXPONENTIALLY; a Poisson/logarithmic sheet is still to be recorded. It does not force C to be identity.

Continue the two chosen roots analytically with J. The quotient in (13) has a removable singularity and

C=I+b(P+I), b=(1/2) dR0/dJ at J=-1.                  (18)

The derivative in (18) is along the chosen roots of (2), not a derivative with Z,W incorrectly frozen. The matrix H=P+I is nonzero and H^2=0 at an essential complete crossing disk. For common-trace parabolic A,B, Tr(H A)=4. Consequently the same coefficient is recoverable without any limit by

b=Tr([H,A_in] A_out)/32.                             (19)

To verify (19), C A C-1=A+b[H,A]-4b2 H, while Tr([H,A]^2)=32 and Tr([H,A]H)=0. The positive-certificate disk has distinct puncture flags, so this is its regular additive centralizer, not the central matrix P=-I.

On the selected longitude cover set J=-ell=-exp(V_long/2). Then

d_(V_long) log R0 at completeness=-b,
log[R0(-J)^epsilon]=log R0+epsilon V_long/2.           (20)

With the calibrated auxiliary source convention R=e^(-U_aux), (20) is precisely the centered shear U_epsilon=U_0-epsilon V_long/2. The factor of two and inverse eigenratio convention are both retained.

6. The peripheral differential on a selected stationary branch

There is a useful conditional consequence for the FULL assembled core phase, rather than just one local coupon. Choose one of the two original cosine signs sigma_i in {+1,-1} at each auxiliary region. Its added leading phase is exactly

sum_i sigma_i pi i (1-2xi_i) chi_i.                    (22)

The finite -1 in m_j=N-2j-1 changes the amplitude, not (22). Assume there is a holomorphic stationary branch of the assembled phase in all its internal variables on a specified logarithmic/Poisson sheet. This is a hypothesis here, not inferred from the local character equations. Call its critical action phi(chi), retaining the original source phases and fixed internal Poisson characters. Envelope differentiation gives

partial_i phi=sigma_i pi i(1-2xi_i).                  (23)

On the marked longitude branch centered at xi_i=1/2 put

V_i=sigma_i 2 Log(-J_i)
   =sigma_i 4pi i(xi_i-1/2), U_i=-2pi i chi_i.

These are equalities on the specified CENTERED logarithmic sheet. An integer change of a xi lift, or an additional recorded exterior color character, changes the corresponding linear term of phi and the marked peripheral logs together. Such terms must be kept; (24) is not a rule for resetting them to zero.

Then (23) proves, without an unidentified proportionality constant,

V_i=-2 partial_i phi,
d(2pi i phi)=(1/2) sum_i V_i dU_i.                   (24)

The local channel equations are R_epsilon,i=e^(sigma_i 2pi i chi_i). The actual collar comparison above identifies its selected multiplier with R_epsilon,i(-sigma_i)=e(U_i), while the selected longitude multiplier is J_i(2sigma_i)=e(V_i). An inverse choice of collar orientation or eigenflag must be changed explicitly on BOTH sides.

Consequently the primitive differential is forced by the original Fourier-color coupling ON ANY such correctly marked stationary branch. It is not evidence that the branch exists globally, contributes to the finite source, or has the required complex estimate. Moreover (24) does not determine the additive value phi(0). Even after the marked representation comparison, its complete-volume anchor still needs an actual fundamental-chain or absolute period proof.

7. What can be sewn, and what is still missing

The original cup has exact affine degree a+b=N-1 and phase x^(a-d); the ordinary cap has phase x^a, and the original marked cap has phase x^b after the allowed WHOLE-SOURCE degree restriction. Their principal equations all give

X1 X2=1, S1/S2=-1, P=I.                             (25)

This is the actual inverse-meridian cap relation. It does not identify the ordinary and marked amplitudes or discard their different normals. Their leading phases pi i u and pi i(1-u) have derivatives differing by an integral 2pi i character. Exponentiation hides precisely that original marked Poisson/logarithmic datum; it must remain in the global action and cannot be recovered from P=I alone. Shared physical wires identify the same X and S symbols, so (10),(14), and (25) are source-compatible local character equations at every width.

Conversely, on a regular magnetic Borel chart an ordered parabolic pair with its actual P determines X,S, hence Y; equation (5) reconstructs its internal q-Hahn root. The full normalization (6) then reconstructs the outgoing scale. In this sense the local map covers the regular ordered-pair centralizer relation, including its parabolic limit.

A global marked representation must still be compared with its source degree-chamber choices and integer carries, and the original logarithmic/ Poisson sheets. The local primitive collar is fixed above; the rational map alone does not prove that every positive complete gluing certificate lands in one physical source chart or has the required global lift. Nor does it supply existence of a nearby marked deformation merely from a complete point.

Most importantly, the local generating phase and its correct character relation do NOT fix its absolute Bloch/regulator class. Section 6 proves the primitive differential on a correctly marked stationary branch; its existence and its actual degree-one complete-volume anchor remain necessary. Matching a character variety cannot fix an additive action constant or a different period class. No absolute comparison, cycle selection, nonzero amplitude, or global source gap is asserted here.

The advance is the explicit local source-to-character generating map, with its full normalization and additive parabolic transport preserved. It is a reusable algebraic input to those remaining comparisons.
