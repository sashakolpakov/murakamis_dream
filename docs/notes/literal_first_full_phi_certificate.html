The literal FIRST five-point operator word and its original full source

28 October 2026. This realizes the FIRST classical certificate

-partial(b,p,p_1,s_M,s_R)

of the crossing fundamental cycle, with its actual flag order. It uses the original two physical heads, one compulsory second-head strict cut, the original factorial frame, and its marked full-Psi function. No auxiliary head or unrelated quantum-torus coordinates are introduced.

The resulting two-to-three operator identity is embedded in the ENTIRE original crossing. Every finite projector, marked scalar, Cartan factor and original cup/cap remains. This closes this literal local certificate; it does not by itself supply a positive geometric face atlas or a global selected-cycle degree.

1. The actual FIRST five flags, in the same bounded frame

Keep the complete original conventions

Q=exp(2pi*i/N), d=N-1, x=-q^(-1), lambda=x^(-1)=Qx,
S_1^N=mu, S_2^N=x^N mu,
Z=Z_1, T=Z_2, X=x S_2 S_1^(-1).

Thus XZ=QZX and XT=Q^(-1)TX. Use the actual balanced Gauss generators e_j,f_j,K_j of the shared quantum meridians. In the bounded homogeneous frame

Y=-ZS_1, L=[[Y,0],[-S_1,1]],

the FIRST flags have the following canonical columns and retained right tags. This is the SAME frame as in the literal middle configuration.

Flag Canonical column Right tag in L flag = column times tag
b (1,1-X)^t Y
p (0,1)^t 1
p_1 (1-Z,1)^t lambda
s_M (1,1)^t YK_1
s_R (1,R)^t YK_2K_1

Here

R=1-(1-T)X.                                  (1)

For the last row, the exact coproduct/Gauss first column is

s_R=(K_2K_1,F_tot K_2K_1)^t,
F_tot=f_1+K_1^(-1)f_2.

Since f_1-S_1=Y and

K_1^(-1)f_2Y^(-1)=-(1-T)X,

one has F_tot-S_1=RY, proving the row without dividing by any nilpotent flag determinant. Every right tag is retained. At unit wrap marks these tags are unitary, as is the previous bounded-frame convention up to its fixed two-by-two condition.

Classically the ordered tuple is therefore

(b,p,p_1,s_M,s_R)
    =(1-X,infinity,1/(1-Z),1,1-(1-T)X).       (2)

The reciprocal in (2) is only a commutative chart notation; the actual finite flag is the homogeneous column in the table.

2. Its five literal cross-ratios

Put

n_cl=-X(1-Z)/Z, G=1-n_cl+n_cl T.

Using exactly the determinant convention

r(v0,v1,v2,v3)
   =det(v3,v1)det(v2,v0)/(det(v3,v0)det(v2,v1)),

the FIRST face table is

Omitted flag Cross-ratio r_j Sign in -partial
b G -
p G/T +
p_1 1/T -
s_M (1-1/n_cl)/T +
s_R 1-1/n_cl -

These are direct determinants of the five columns in (2), not an arbitrary renamed five-term pattern. The independent symbolic audit checks all five rational identities.

Define the convenient quad coordinates

a_cl=n_cl(1-T),
y_cl=-n_cl T/(1-n_cl),
J_cl=T/G.

The rational oriented pre-Bloch rules [1-z]=-[z], [1/z]=-[z], [1-1/z]=[z], and [z/(z-1)]=-[z] convert that literal table to

-partial=[a_cl]-[J_cl]+[T]-[y_cl]-[n_cl].     (3)

In particular (3) compares the two signed lists

left:  [n_cl]-[T],
right: -[y_cl]-[J_cl]+[a_cl].                 (4)

The signed right list minus the left list is the specified FIRST negative boundary. These signs and quads are fixed below by the actual finite operators.

3. The genuine finite cut and the ORIGINAL crossing atom

Let E_d(T) be the original second-head index-d projector and put

n=e_1 S_2=(Q-Z^(-1))X,
c=n(1-E_d(T)),
a=e_1 f_2.                                  (5)

The classical n is exactly n_cl from Section 2. Its second-head cut is indispensable. It gives, at the actual finite level,

cT=Q^(-1)Tc, c^N=0,
a=c(1-QT).                                  (6)

Indeed c=e_1 tensor J_(+,2) uses the literal strict second-head raise. Acting on input index j, its multiplication by 1-QT supplies 1-Q^(j+1), exactly the coefficient of f_2. The possible wrap of n is killed by that same original zero.

Use the original finite Euler function

P_Q(A)=sum_(h=0)^d p_h A^h,
p_h=(-1)^h Q^(h(h-1)/2)/(Q;Q)_h.

Let F_j=(Q;Q)_j and retain the marked scalar

phi_N=sqrt(N) exp{i*pi[1/4-(N+N^(-1))/12]}.

The original marked full-Psi root clock is

mathcal F_T=marked Psi(QT)=phi_N diag_j(1/F_j).

The logarithmic lift at index j is 2pii(j+1)/N. In particular the index-d value uses the regular TOP lift, not the bottom zero. Set

F=mathcal F_T^(-1)=phi_N^(-1)diag_j(F_j).       (7)

Consecutive factorial ratios, including the strict top edge, prove

F c F^(-1)=c(1-QT)=a.                         (8)

Thus the ENTIRE original uncheck crossing is

R_0=D P_Q(a)=D F P_Q(c) F^(-1).               (9)

D is its original Cartan diagonal. Its nonzero common Cartan scalar kappa, flip, writhe correction and peripheral normalization remain the original external lines. Equation (9) is a matrix identity, not a change of invariant or a replacement of the original source.

4. Exact two-to-three sequence and all developed arguments

Abbreviate P=P_Q(c) and define the transported marked clock

H=P^(-1)TP, F_H=P^(-1)FP,
y=-Q cH, Y=P_Q(y).                            (10)

All displayed inverses exist. In particular H^N=1 and yN=cN=0. The relation cH=Q^(-1)Hc follows by conjugating (6). Conjugating (8) gives

F_H c F_H^(-1)=c(1-QH)=c+y.                  (11)

Moreover yc=Qcy. The ordered finite binomial theorem therefore gives

P_Q(c+y)=P_Q(y)P_Q(c).

Every term of degree >=N vanishes because it contains that degree of c. Thus (11) yields the exact FIRST operator sequence

F_H P=Y P F_H,
P F_H=Y^(-1)F_H P.                           (12)

This is a two-to-three identity at every level, including even and composite N. Its one marked full-Psi factor is the SAME transported F_H on both sides; its scalar phi_N^(-1) has not been reset.

The left word P F_H develops to c and the negative clock T, since PHP^(-1)=T. The right word develops to y with negative orientation, the negative marked clock

J=Y^(-1)HY=H(1+cH)^(-1),                     (13)

and the final positive argument

Y^(-1)F_H c F_H^(-1)Y=a.                      (14)

Here (13)–(14) are exact ordered identities, not only symbols. For a proof, the finite Euler recurrences give

(1-c)H=T,
Y^(-1)cY=c(1-y),
Y^(-1)HY=H(1-Q^(-1)y)^(-1).

Since (1-y)H=H(1-Q^(-1)y), substitution in (11) gives

Y^(-1)c(1-QH)Y
   =c(1-y)-QcH
   =c-Qc(1-c)H=c(1-QT)=a.

The denominator 1+cH in (13) is a unit because cH is nilpotent; there is no inverse of a singular clock determinant. J is a conjugate of H, so J^N=1 with its integer clock mark transported by the same Y, not chosen afresh.

Classically H=T/(1-n_cl), and (10), (13), (14) give precisely y_cl,J_cl,a_cl of Section 2. Thus the five developed signed arguments of (12) are the literal FIRST table (3)–(4).

There is also a useful fixed-degree ordered Ptolemy form, involving the ORIGINAL unsplit atom a rather than a guessed scalar quotient:

G_first=1-a=1-c+Tc,
J=G_first^(-1)T,
r=Jc=-y(1-y)^(-1),
1-r=G_first^(-1)(1-c).                       (14a)

For (14a), invert (13):

J^(-1)=H^(-1)+c=T^(-1)(1-c)+c
       =T^(-1)(1-c+Tc)=T^(-1)(1-a).

Also Jc=QcH(1+QcH)^(-1), which is the displayed quad of y. All inverses in (14a) are units because a,c,y are nilpotent. The first literal face complements G_first and 1-c, the clock J, and the fourth-face quad r therefore satisfy exact ordered equations on the original physical heads, with the strict c-wall retained. They are not products of separately compressed coordinate ports.

Combining (9)–(12) embeds the identity in the ORIGINAL crossing:

R_0=D P F_H F^(-1)
    =D Y^(-1)F_H P F^(-1).                   (15)

The terminal F^(-1) is a compulsory interface. Deleting it changes the operator and original period. The inverse checked crossing is the inverse of (15), with the SAME original scalars; the exact audit compares it independently with the original negative formula.

The operator proof (5)–(15) actually extends over the whole original color parameters at this fixed physical q root: use the original e_1(x_1), the same strict J_(+,2), and the original mixed Cartan D(x_1,x_2). Equations (6), (8), and the finite binomial proof are unchanged. Thus it also preserves the whole meridian color germ and its normal derivatives. The literal five-flag table of Sections 1–2 and the explicit source evaluations below are stated at completeness. No off-root Hopf or generic-q invariant is being asserted.

5. The complete strict wall and its current

Every power of the cut is explicit:

c^h=n^h(1-sum_(j=N-h)^(N-1)E_j(T)), 0<=h<N.  (16)

For h=0 the sum is empty. The forbidden second-head wrap inputs are exactly those j with j+h>=N. The first-head nilpotence is retained independently. Thus (16) supplies the whole finite source wall, not merely its principal approximation.

Write mu_2=S_2N=xN mu and

W=nE_d(T)=mu_2 e_1 tensor |0><d|,
F_tot=f_1 tensor I+K_1^(-1) tensor f_2.

Its actual total-current commutator is

[F_tot,W]=mu_2(
  [f_1,e_1] tensor |0><d|
  +(1-Q)K_1^(-1)e_1 tensor |1><d|
  -(1-Q^(-1))e_1K_1^(-1) tensor |0><d-1|).    (17)

This follows by applying f_2 before or after the missing wrap. It is retained in (5), (16), and all conjugated interfaces. No separate wall or radial summand is asserted Ward-invariant. Applying the ordinary typed commutator product rule to the exact operator equality (15) transports every other original current as well. At an interior position of a word the full W remains; one does not replace it by a scalar source value.

6. Original cup, marker, and uncanceled marked scalar

Keep the ORIGINAL balanced cup and full marker

p=N sum_(j=0)^d x^(j-d)|j,d-j>,
m|a,b>=x^b/N^2, mp=1.                        (18)

On this whole degree-d cup, any forbidden second-head input in (16) has first index <h and is already killed by e_1^h. Thus

c^h p=n^h p, m c^h p=1, 0<=h<N.             (19)

For the moment in (19), summing the explicit complete e_1 coefficients reduces it to

(1/N)sum_(j=h)^d (Q^(-j);Q)_h=1.

Extend the sum to all j modulo N: its omitted terms are zero, its constant coefficient contributes N, and all positive powers Q^(-ell j), 0<ell<=h<N, sum to zero. This proves (19) directly from the original source.

The finite hockey-stick contraction already used for the original middle factor now gives

Pp=N|d,0>, Hp=p, F_Hp=phi_N^(-1)p.           (20)

The transported T-index here is zero, hence the nonzero marked Psi argument is Q with its recorded index-one logarithmic lift. Consequently the two words of (12) have the exact source value

mP F_Hp=mY^(-1)F_H Pp=1/(N phi_N).           (21)

Its modulus is N^(-3/2), with no adjustable normalization.

Equation (21) evaluates the unadorned local word on the literal cup. In the full original embedding (15), its actual incoming source is F^(-1)p and its actual outgoing row retains flip D. Those interfaces are kept as operators. The full marked original one-crossing source therefore remains exactly its old value:

m flip D [P F_H] F^(-1)p
  =m flip D [Y^(-1)F_H P] F^(-1)p=1,          (22)

before the unchanged common scalar kappa. This is also checked against the original E/Cartan coefficient evaluator. No unknown knot period enters the source or any adapter.

7. Full-Psi coefficient cycles and scope

Every factor of (15) has the same exact full-Psi coefficient realization as in the full-function source theorem. For an explicit contractive core let J_(minus,1) and J_(+,2) be the original strict shifts and use the previously proved factorial/Gaussian frame

H_e|j>=lambda^j(-1)^jQ^(j(j-1)/2)|j>/F_j.

Then

c=(H_e tensor I)(J_(minus,1) tensor J_(+,2))
                                  (H_e^(-1) tensor I).

The core has norm <=1. Also

y=P^(-1)(-QcT)P,

so its Euler factor and inverse reduce, through the explicitly retained P interfaces, to the same kind of strict shift times a unit clock. F and F^(-1) are the marked second-head factorial diagonals with the phases (7); their moment realizations retain the original reflected factorial factors N and phi_N.

Every coefficient moment is kept as its FULL circle-minus-ray source, or its equivalent exact atomic-window resolution. The inverse Euler factor Y^(-1) uses the original inverse polynomial on its nilpotent argument, not an unmarked inverse of a scalar full-Psi function. All root marks, finite cuts and radial terms remain in their actual operator order. The common Cartan/writhe/ peripheral lines in (15) are unchanged.

This proves the FIRST literal finite five-point operator identity and its full original source, uniformly for the crossing. It does not prove that every displayed framed face is already in a common positive geometric polarization, that separated radial pieces have a gap, or that the global geometric cycle is selected.
