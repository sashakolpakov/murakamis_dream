An actual Gauss coherent kernel from its finite Goursat problem

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The two-head Gauss operator has an actual nonzero local kernel in the strict upper-half degree chamber. This follows from its exact Jacobi commutator and its initialized first column. The proof controls the finite logarithmic recurrence and the entire remaining coefficient cap; it does not infer an analytic kernel from a formal characteristic equation. The literal ordinary-binomial input is then composed on its original coefficient circle. Its selected saddle has coefficient one and a nonzero mixed derivative.

This is a fixed-root statement for this full operator block. The exterior factors of the opposite source remain, as do other degree blocks. No meridian-normal estimate, geometric-point membership, or closed-source coefficient follows. Complete directs H, G, B and V are pinned below.
