Exponential real characteristic concentration of the actual edge source

Private written proof, 23 November 2026.

The exact same-index edge equation gives exponential, rather than only algebraic, concentration away from a fixed real symbol gap. For a graph with arbitrary coupled channel weights, the bound is CEN2e−cNδ2CE N^2e^{-cN\delta^2} times its full Fourier energy. Normalized angular jets satisfy the same estimate as a complete vector.

The proof iterates the adjoint equation on explicit Gaussian test functions. Only those known test functions are continued to fractional Weyl displacements. No continuation or asymptotic expression for the unknown source is used. All incidence variables remain separate until the original vertex and marked-closing operations are applied.

7. Scope and complete sources

The exponential bound applies to the real unitary phase space of the full free incidence variables. There is no alias identification, discarded channel endpoint, or artificial finite output compression. The full marked vertex operations and their normal coordinates remain the original closing ΛN\Lambda_N of A, with its full phases, turns, reductions and scalars. The proved closing estimate is (27) for that specific compiler and its canonical incidence norm; a different compiler requires its actual adapter. This Fourier norm is not identified with the original ordinary-color Plancherel energy.

The estimate is an exponential real complementary-region bound for the actual pre-gluing source class, with the source-relative marked remainder (27). The retained contribution still needs a nonzero coherent lower estimate, and the pre-gluing norm still needs its geometric rate. Neither (5) nor (27) selects a complex hyperbolic characteristic or a nonzero geometric-cycle coefficient, or proves a lower bound after arbitrary short fillings. Moving-quantum-parameter jets and conversions to Casimir normals at parabolic angles are not inferred from normalized angular jets.
