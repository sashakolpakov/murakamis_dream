The whole original source as an exactly charged bridge-flow coefficient

28 October 2026. After the exact simultaneous overbridge reduction, ALL remaining understrand anchor sums can also be performed exactly. The result is a finite charged coefficient, with one charge N-1 at every anchor. There are no extra cyclic alias sectors in this whole coefficient. This follows from nonnegative polynomial degree and global homogeneity, with the original closed-braid source retained.

This is a larger closed algebra than the isolated complementary Pochhammer class. Its coefficients remain coupled and oscillatory. It does not prove a positive state, geometric selection or scalar noncancellation.

1. Use the original scalar source before splitting

Put N>=2, d=N-1, Q=exp(2pii/N), x=-exp(-pi*i/N), s=x^N. Let W be ANY original complete signed braid word on b heads, in the balanced factorial basis. Every actual crossing preserves total INTEGER head degree. The original closed-head pivots are diagonal,

g_a=(x/s)Q^(-a).

Therefore the original one-cut partial trace is diagonal on its remaining head. The original averaged marked row, with every entry 1/N, has exactly the same scalar pairing as I/N on this already assembled diagonal matrix. Hence the actual scalar is

K_N=chi_N/N *Tr[(I tensor g tensor ... tensor g) W].  (1)

The external original dimension/framing line chi_N remains. This uses integer-degree conservation BEFORE any Fourier or bridge decomposition. It is not a sectorwise replacement of the marked row, a claim about arbitrary inserted currents, or an extra pivotal trace.

Put W in the exact regular propagated frames. This removes every interior Fourier adapter. At closure head i retain its exact adapter, including the pivot when i>1. If Z is its final-grid input and Y its initial-grid output, the kernel is

E_i(Y,Z)=eta_i/N *Z^(-d) C_i(Z,Y),
C_i(Z,Y)=sum_(a=0)^d Z^(d-a)(t_i Y)^a,
(eta_1,t_1)=(1,1),
(eta_i,t_i)=(x/s,Q^(-1)), i>1.                  (2)

This is the finite Fourier sum, including its removable values. Its numerator is a homogeneous d-form. Equivalently

C_i(Z,Y)=product_(r=1)^d (Z-Q^r t_i Y).         (3)

Every nontrivial boundary adapter stays in (2). Matching incompatible central grids or replacing it by an identity is unnecessary.

2. Each reduced overbridge is a homogeneous d-form

Use the exact signed overbridge theorem. An overbridge e has m encounters in physical strand order, signs sigma_j, clocks d>=h_1>=…>=h_m>=0, and spectator UNDER outputs u_j. Its conditional crossing parameters can be written

a_j=alpha_j/u_j, b_j=beta_j u_j,
alpha_j=Q^(-h_j)/x, beta_j=Q^(h_j+1), sigma_j=+1,
alpha_j=Qx,         beta_j=Q,           sigma_j=-1.

In either case alpha_j beta_j=Q^2x. Write Delta_j=h_j-h_(j+1), and f_sigma(h)=(-1)^h Q^(sigma*h(h+1)/2). Define the explicit factor

Gamma_e=kappa^(sum_j sigma_j)
   *product_j f_(sigma_j)(h_j)
   *product_(j<m) [(-alpha_j)^(h_(j+1))
                     Q^(h_(j+1)(h_(j+1)-1)/2)]
   *F_(h_1)/[F_(h_m) product_(j<m)F_(Delta_j)],
F_r=(Q;Q)_r.                                    (4)

Every factorial index in (4) is at most d. All crossing normalizers are now explicit. In particular the powers N from the internal strand sums and the factors 1/N at crossings leave precisely 1/N for this ENTIRE bridge.

Let Zin and Zout be its overstrand endpoint variables. Directly clearing the denominators in the exact bridge formula gives

B_e=Gamma_e/N *Zin^(-d) P_e(Zin,Zout,u_1,...,u_m), (5)

P_e=
  product_(0<=r<d-h_1)(Zin-Q^r beta_1 u_1)
  *product_(j<m) product_(0<=r<Delta_j)
                     (u_j-Q^(r-1)alpha_j beta_(j+1)u_(j+1))
  *product_(0<=r<h_m)(u_m-Q^r alpha_m Zout).      (6)

The three collections have lengths d-h_1, sum Delta_j, h_m. Their total is EXACTLY d. Thus P_e is a homogeneous degree-d polynomial with no negative powers, even when spectator variables later coincide or particular coefficients vanish. Equation (5) has only ONE denominator, at the overbridge input.

For example each link denominator u_j^(Delta_j) is canceled by the corresponding spectator powers of the closed crossing scalar and link monomial. The last spectator power clears the last output Pochhammer; the input endpoint becomes Zin^(-d). This is an exact polynomial identity, not a modulus estimate.

3. Understrand clocks and a global bijection

At a signed crossing the overclock from the actual port table is

label_under,out=label_under,in-sigma*h (mod N),
z_under,out=x^sigma z_under,in Q^(-sigma*h).     (7)

The label equation holds for both signs, with the table’s physical over/under conventions. The root equation also uses the actual propagated roots, not an independently imposed meridian value.

Regard every compressed overbridge and every retained closure adapter (2) as an event with one physical input and one output. Cut the oriented strand at these events. Between successive events the remaining encounters are exclusively UNDER encounters. Untouched head identities have already been suppressed. If there are B overbridge events, there are

k=B+b

events and exactly k intervening directed segments. The closure adapter events break any directed cycle of under encounters, even on a link component which is always under in the open braid.

Assign one anchor variable X_a to the first grid port on each such segment. Starting there, (7) gives EVERY port on the segment as

z_v=zeta_v(h) X_a,
zeta_v=x^(sum_previous sigma) Q^(-sum_previous sigma*h). (8)

For fixed clocks the map from the segment anchor label to its port labels is a cyclic translation. Conversely a collection of the old segment labels determines each h by (7) and its initial anchor label. Thus the change from all remaining port labels to the crossing clocks h and k anchor labels is a BIJECTION, of counting Jacobian one. No congruence sectors are thrown away.

Every anchor segment ends at the input of exactly one event. Thus, after (8), it acquires exactly ONE denominator X_a^d, from (2) or (5). The phase zeta_event,input^(-d) is retained in the event factor. All event output and spectator variables in their numerators are also substituted by (8). No selected-overbridge variable reappears.

Write P_h(X) for the product of these k substituted event numerator polynomials, and

W_h=product_overbridges Gamma_e
      *product_closure eta_i
      *product_events zeta_event,input^(-d).    (9)

The root substitutions in each event polynomial preserve degree, so P_h has nonnegative exponents and TOTAL degree k d. Its coefficients and W_h are fully explicit functions of Q,x,kappa,h; they do not depend on an unknown knot period or on the initial Fermat centers. The latter only specify the anchor sampling grids.

4. Exact disappearance of EVERY cyclic anchor alias

The complete source (1), after the bijection and simultaneous overbridge reduction, is

K_N=chi_N/N *N^(-k)
   sum_(h over-monotone) W_h
   sum_(X_a^N=C_a, all a) [product_a X_a^(-d)] P_h(X). (10)

Each C_a is the actual fixed nonzero central value at its segment anchor. It need not be one. Every event contributes its exact 1/N; there are k events.

Expand a monomial X^e in P_h. Root sampling can keep it only if

e_a-d=N l_a, for integers l_a, all a.

But e_a>=0 and d=N-1 imply l_a>=0. Global homogeneity gives

sum_a N l_a=sum_a e_a-kd=0.

Therefore ALL l_a are zero. The argument includes arbitrarily large single-variable degrees: there is no omitted high-degree positive alias, because it would require a negative alias elsewhere, which the lower bound -d excludes. Consequently, termwise in h,

sum_(X_a^N=C_a) [product X_a^(-d)]P_h(X)
   =N^k [X_1^d ... X_k^d]P_h(X).               (11)

There are no powers of the C_a on the right. This proves exact frame-center independence of the WHOLE anchored coefficient at each clock assignment, without deleting a separate current sector. Substituting into (10) gives the final original-source identity

K_N=chi_N/N *sum_(h over-monotone)
                W_h [X_1^d ... X_k^d]P_h(X).   (12)

All k finite anchor sums have been evaluated. Their N^k cancels the N^(-k) event normalizations EXACTLY. The remaining 1/N is the original averaged first-cut source in (1), not a guessed normalization.

5. The resulting charged flow algebra and its scope

Every event polynomial is a product of d explicit linear differences of anchors; closure uses (3). Expanding these factors chooses an endpoint anchor for each factor. The coefficient in (12) keeps exactly d choices at EACH anchor. Thus it is a finite nonnegative integer-charge flow problem, with a complex unit-phase weight on each choice. The overbridge clock domain is the independent product of monotone simplexes, equivalently the compositions

(d-h_1,Delta_1,...,Delta_(m-1),h_m), all >=0, sum=d.

The polynomial coefficient algebra is closed under further anchor elimination: taking one degree-d coefficient preserves nonnegative polynomial exponents and lowers total degree by d. It does not preserve the isolated two-Pochhammer factorization. It is in this larger EXACT charged algebra that the understrand elimination closes; no tuned Vandermonde identity or discarded boundary term is required.

Exact anchor-cycle transport and charged source currents

For every FIXED h the period in (12) is the specified Laurent polynomial form

(1/(2*pi*i)^k) integral P_h(X)
                           product_a dX_a/X_a^(d+1). (13)

It has no finite noncoordinate poles. Consequently the product circle radii can be changed to ANY finite positive radii, with the original coefficient and source unchanged. This is a literal torus homotopy in (C*)^k; it does not use the raw off-grid four-Psi continuation. Its linear-form zeros are zeros, not residue divisors. No discarded Phi seam, Cauchy collision or unretained cyclic carry is needed for this anchor-radius transport. Escape to zero/infinity is not part of the assertion.

The exact charged Ward current at each anchor is

[X^d](X_a partial_(X_a)-d)P_h=0.                (14)

More generally the integral of X_a partial_(X_a)[F P_h product_b X_b^(-d)] against product_b dX_b/X_b is zero, for a Laurent-polynomial test F. After multiplying by the FULL W_h and summing h it is still zero. This is a current of the proved charged representation, not a claim that arbitrary new matrix coupons preserve its previous root-grid support theorem.

If none of the linear forms L_r vanishes, the finite-N stationary anchor equations of (13) in logarithmic coordinates are explicitly

sum_r c_(r,a) X_a/L_r(X)=d, all a,
L_r(X)=sum_a c_(r,a)X_a.                        (15)

Thus the stationary current is a rational charge-balance equation. Each form contributes total one, so the sum of (15) is the known identity kd=kd. One common scale is redundant. Exactly, without an asymptotic argument,

[X_1^d...X_k^d]P_h(X)
   =[Y_1^d...Y_(k-1)^d]P_h(Y_1,...,Y_(k-1),1). (16)

This follows from total degree kd. It removes the common C* scale from the actual source period. Neither (15) nor (16) by itself identifies its stationary scheme with the knot A-curve; the remaining clock equations and their actual source selection must also be proved.

The exact grouped graph connection

Let q=exp(pi*i/N). A block of length 0<=L<=d has the form

product_(r=0)^(L-1)(alpha X_i-Q^r beta X_j).

Its coefficient with j-charge a is

alpha^L S_(L,a) w^a X_i^(L-a)X_j^a,
w=-(beta/alpha)q^(L-1),
S_(L,a)=product_(r=1)^L sin(pi*r/N)
   /[product_(r=1)^a sin(pi*r/N)
      product_(r=1)^(L-a) sin(pi*r/N)] >0.     (17)

This follows from [L choose a]Q=q^(a(L-a))S(L,a), together with the q-binomial expansion. Thus the phase is LINEAR in the block charge after a fixed block phase is removed; it is not a quadratic approximation.

For the actual blocks in (6), after the undertransport (8), the connection phases are exactly

input:   w=q^(sigma_1*h_1) zeta_(u_1)/zeta_(Zin),
link j:  w=q^(sigma_(j+1)*h_(j+1)-sigma_j*h_j)
                           zeta_(u_(j+1))/zeta_(u_j),
output:  w=q^(-sigma_m*h_m) zeta_(Zout)/zeta_(u_m),
closure: w=t_i zeta_(Zout)/zeta_(Zin).          (18)

For closure the first argument in its length-d block starts at Q, as in (3), which makes its residual phase exactly the actual pivot t_i=1 or Q^(-1). No extra half-turn is hidden in (18).

Within one bridge, (18) telescopes using the local potentials q^(sigma_j*h_j)zeta_(u_j) and the unshifted endpoint potentials. That fact alone is not global flatness: distinct spectator ports u_j can belong to the SAME under-anchor segment and have different local potentials. A gauge test must use the actual grouped graph and differences of FEASIBLE charge flows, including its capacities, rather than an unsupported abstract cycle.

The audit exposes this graph through graph_blocks(H,S,h). Its metadata records every block’s anchor endpoints, length, event type, and exact integer q-exponents for alpha,beta,w, modulo 2N. It also records the baseline outgoing degree at each anchor. If a block chooses head charge a_e in [0,L_e], the exact charge equations are

sum_(head=i) a_e-sum_(tail=i) a_e
   =d-sum_(tail=i)L_e.                         (19)

These metadata reproduce the whole polynomial coefficient, with (17) and all fixed block phases retained. They do not assert that the connection has trivial holonomy on the feasible-flow lattice.

Equation (12) is stronger than retaining a generic higher hypergeometric remainder at every underbridge. It supplies the complete source charge at all anchors and removes all cyclic carry ambiguity from those sums. It does not make the remaining h/flow sum positive. The phases in (4), (8), and the linear forms remain; they can produce cancellation. Identifying a global geometric critical branch and its selected coefficient is still required. No broad scalar volume limit follows solely from (12).

For literature COVERAGE only, quantum-determinant presentations of the Kashaev invariant were already reported by Huynh–Le, On the Colored Jones Polynomial and the Kashaev invariant. The abstract also proposes comparing that determinant with a determinant expression for hyperbolic volume. No determinant formula or analytic proof from that paper is used here, and no novelty claim for coefficient/determinant reformulations in general is intended. The independent statement proved above is the exact original-source clock bijection, event charge and absence of cyclic anchor aliases.

The proof is for the original complete braid word and its stated scalar source. An inserted degree-changing coupon need not permit (1) and would need its own charged source form. The theorem does not discard such a coupon or claim the same charge automatically.
