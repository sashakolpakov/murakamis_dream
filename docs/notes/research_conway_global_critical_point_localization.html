Global critical-point localization for the original Conway quartic

Written proof, 23 November 2026. The normalized Conway quartic has one real critical point on the entire packet space. A single sweep of real scalar path minimizations brings any field into the radius-1/161/16 packet balls without increasing the energy. The known contracting iteration then applies. A gradient test and continuous-flow convergence are also proved. These finite-level statements concern the literal uncompressed tree, not the minimum’s hyperbolic-volume rate.

1. Complete physical packets and the original closing

Use [P]’s full NN-dimensional physical pair algebra HNH_N with its uniform physical Hilbert metric, for every N≥2N\ge2. All independent first normals at repeated Casimir roots remain. The proof also applies in [C]’s normalized Newton metric, keeping that metric’s own maps and budgets together.

Fix a finite rooted literal Conway tree with distinct child occurrences. Every proper vertex is a prescribed constant leaf, a homogeneous complex-linear turn, or a complex-bilinear product. Every proper packet has one parent. There are no shared variables or compressed affine gates. The closing root is the original complex-linear row LL, with all its recorded scalar factors. Equal prescribed leaf data still have distinct trial variables.

4. One real critical point on the whole space

The causal residual map is a polynomial automorphism. Given any residual field rr, solve successively from leaves to root by xv=Φv((xc)c)−rv.(9) x_v=\Phi_v((x_c)_c)-r_v. \qquad\text{(9)} This is its polynomial inverse. Consequently EE is coercive: an energy sublevel is a closed subset of the compact image under G−1G^{-1} of a closed residual ball. A global minimum therefore exists and is a real critical point.

This is not a statement about critical points of a complexification of the real polynomial.

6. Continuous gradient flow from arbitrary initial data

No uniform flow entry time, global Polyak–Lojasiewicz inequality, or global flow rate is asserted.

7. Scope, coverage, and complete inputs

Fixed signed twists and fixed short fillings remain their full leaf packets in the unchanged literal tree, with the original cap and all physical normals. A component with unknown incoming packets is not replaced by a known constant leaf.

[J] already proves coercivity and the unique global minimum. The path identity and initialization add global critical-point control. The original comparison (3) is unchanged; its minimum can still be zero or exponentially small. Uniqueness here concerns the real quartic, not complex source-phase saddles or geometric thimbles. No source growth rate or finite-exception volume theorem is proved.
