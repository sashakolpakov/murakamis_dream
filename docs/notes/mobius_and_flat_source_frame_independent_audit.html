Independent audit of the Möbius framing fiber and flat source frame

27 October 2026. Detailed examination of the Möbius framing and norm-pole theorem and the flat cyclic source-frame theorem. The derivative and base-ring qualifications below should be retained. No new selected-period comparison follows merely from these source-frame identifications.

1. The actual prefix Möbius formula and its orientation

Factor U-V=u v, with v=(a,b) and w=v V inverse=(c,d). Since U,V have determinant one and UV inverse is nontrivial trace two, w u=0 and U-V has rank exactly one. In the actual F(r,s) frame, the first row of F inverse(U-V)F is proportional to vF. Using the inverse of the determinant-one matrix F inverse V F gives

pσ=(vF)2ℓV−(vF)1(V′)12(vF)1=(wF)2(vF)1=c(1−r)−dsar+bs. \frac p\sigma =\frac{(vF)_2\ell_V-(vF)_1(V')_{12}}{(vF)_1} =\frac{(wF)_2}{(vF)_1} =\frac{c(1-r)-ds}{ar+bs}.

This verifies the full prefix adapter and its sign directly, without a position-discriminant square root. Solving gives the displayed inverse, and differentiating gives

rp=σ[s(ad−bc)−ac](ap+σc)2. r_p=\frac{\sigma[s(ad-bc)-ac]}{(ap+\sigma c)^2}.

The two-form orientation also passes. Since Omega_cut=-dr wedge ds/[s(1+s)] and d log A=-ds/(1+s), the coefficient of d log A wedge d log p is -p r_p/s, exactly the negative sign in the theorem. In the finite-pole chart the projective pole is lambda=-sigma c/a, independent of s. On c_U-c_V nonzero its trace-word expression follows from c_U-c_V=u_2 a and c_(UV inverse)=u_2 c. This is intrinsic to the MARKED prefix/cut data, not an asserted diagram-independent topological invariant.

The actual figure-eight prefix factorization gives v=(1,2), w=(1+u,1), sigma=-1, and the theorem’s numerator, inverse and derivative follow. Its pole lambda=1+u=-u squared has order six at either geometric embedding. This agrees with the independently computed exceptional-fiber limit gamma_0=u squared: its pole p=-gamma_0 is the same point. The finite- and infinite-pole charts in the proof retain their nonvanishing hypotheses correctly.

2. The finite cyclic norm and the required derivative qualification

The rational identity

1N∑pN=1p(p−λ)2=NλN−1(1−λN)2 \frac1N\sum_{p^N=1}\frac p{(p-\lambda)^2} =\frac{N\lambda^{N-1}}{(1-\lambda^N)^2}

follows by differentiating the finite partial-fraction expansion of 1/(1-lambda^N), or directly from the companion matrix. It has the theorem’s sign and exponent. The pole-at-zero and pole-at-infinity averages vanish for N greater than one. For fixed nonzero G and absolute lambda not one, (1/N) log absolute value of G N lambda(N-1)/(1-lambdaN)^2 tends to minus the absolute value of log absolute lambda. Hence a rational fiber coordinate does not itself force a subexponential source weight.

For the WHOLE product P_N(Q,lambda), the derivative must be written

DQlog⁡PN=∑j=0N−1jλQ−j1−λQ−j,DQPN=PNDQlog⁡PN. D_Q\log P_N =\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\frac{j\lambda Q^{-j}}{1-\lambda Q^{-j}}, \qquad D_QP_N=P_N D_Q\log P_N. \tag{1}

The sum alone is the logarithmic derivative, not D_Q P_N. This minor wording correction was reported to the author. At a norm-zero fiber neither the apparent logarithmic poles nor their product with P_N should be evaluated separately: use the original finite product derivative. The root relation P_N=1-lambda^N is not an identity of its Q-normal extension.

For N divisible by six the actual figure-eight principal-density sampling encounters its pole. The actual finite tensor remains regular. This is an explicit reason the rational principal-density average cannot be declared the whole selected source. It is not a statement that the original invariant or its selected geometric coefficient is singular or zero.

3. The source-cyclic classification is scheme-theoretic on its chart

The flat-frame argument is valid over COMMUTATIVE base algebras, including those with nilpotents. The orbit map from N copies of the source line is its explicit open-chart hypothesis. In that line-valued frame AP=A fixes A to the summing source and P to the cyclic shift; BA=A fixes the zeroth column of B. The remaining rows of the aligned defect give B_(i,j+1)=Q B_(i-1,j). Their wrap equations force every off-diagonal zeroth-row entry to zero, and hence B=diag(1,Q,…,Q^(N-1)). This uses no delta division.

The line-bundle groupoid and the removal of its automorphisms by a nonzero marked source vector follow from this canonical frame. They do not classify unrelated noncyclic components. Distinct root eigenvalues prove the orbit hypothesis in a neighborhood of the actual primitive-root source, as stated.

The additional aligned defect projector is e_0 rho with rho_0=1. The whole multiplicative equation becomes exactly delta rho_j=0. If delta is a nonzero divisor on the base, its flat saturation is the actual e_00 projector and no extra row parameters remain. The statement that saturation is JUST the original base has this necessary scope. On a base with delta-torsion it also removes base torsion: for example delta=epsilon in k[epsilon]/epsilon squared makes delta-power saturation the zero ring. This qualification was reported and is now explicit in the current note. It does not affect the actual regular normal coefficient ring or the preceding classification over arbitrary commutative bases.

4. The whole corrected algebra and the nonaligned family

Evaluation at Q^j makes tau(X)=Q inverse times (X-delta e_0(X)) the predecessor cyclic permutation of the actual clock nodes. Thus tau(e_j)=e_(j+1), with the theorem’s orientation. In its crossed product the elements e_i P^(i-j) multiply as E_ij and give exactly Mat_N of the base. In particular its whole correction retains the finite wrap rather than imposing the inconsistent generic-Q relation BP=QPB with P^N=I.

The explicit N=2 nonaligned family also passes. Set h=Q-Q inverse. Its conditions reduce to t squared=x(1+x); then its multiplicative commutator entries agree exactly with the displayed R. Replacing delta by delta^k in x and t preserves this identity and makes the tilt invisible to any prescribed fixed jet, while changing B’s second eigenvalue. The moving projector image fails alignment with the actual source line. This is a valid counterexample to omitting WHOLE alignment, not to the positive aligned theorem.

The scalar-intertwiner conclusion is exact. Root actual closure with generic meridian M is scalar; the auxiliary whole q-normal lift need not be, as the earlier literal unknot calculation shows. The current note explicitly retains that scope when mentioning an actual scalar knot transport. A central scalar is not fixed by source-frame classification. Strictly requiring the FINAL transport to fix the source vector would set that scalar to one and would be an extra normalization, not a property of the original period.

5. The central loop identity is exact but needs a typed endpoint map

The actual framed loop/source identity, equation (5a), follows from Delta_mom S_P=Q(Z inverse minus one)Q_0 and Q_0=v ell. It is an exact whole rank-one insertion factorization, with selected divided-normal insertion Q_N Q_0. Its orbit path and source mark are necessary.

It does not by itself identify a geometric endpoint crossing. Its normal divisor is 1-Q^N. The Möbius framing pole has norm divisor 1-lambda^N, which is generally NONZERO at that same actual root. Even when both vanish at a resonant level, their extensions and normal orders are independent until an actual word/source map compares them. No such identification follows from (5a) or from the rigidity of the finite source frame.

Algebraically (5a) is homogeneous under arbitrary central rescalings of C_1 and C_2; their scalar periods rescale on both sides. With C_2=I it prescribes the inserted period in terms of the already unknown uninserted one, rather than determining that unknown scalar. A genuine endpoint comparison must additionally transport the actual crossing word, co-source, orbit path and normal divisor into the geometric relative endpoint complex. The audited notes correctly leave this selected comparison and the volume limit open.
