Actual fixed-width Gauss monomial kernels on initialized sectors

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The exact suffix-Casimir equations propagate the actual initialized column to a full monomial kernel at any fixed width. The initial output domain is a separated winning sector of W, not a disk about the vacuum. A finite input-degree estimate, with an explicit output-shift budget, pays the entire remaining input cap. Compatibility of the transport equations is obtained from the actual finite solutions.

The ordinary-binomial input is not transformed in this theorem. Inactive input phase slopes vanish. Its Cauchy interfaces can therefore carry new exponential phases, and cannot be replaced by repetitions of the two-head Bessel saddle.

5. Why the ordinary-binomial input remains a separate step

Equation (26) is a full matrix kernel in its stated monomial polarization. It has only one active principal input slope: Sζi(ρ,0)=0(i≠j).(27) S_{\zeta_i}(\rho,0)=0\quad(i\ne j). \qquad\text{(27)} Thus L’s nonzero-slope coefficient circle does not apply in all input variables. The distinction is substantive. Already the finite identity kernel in one such variable has ∑a=0d(ρζ)a=1−(ρζ)N1−ρζ; \sum_{a=0}^d(\rho\zeta)^a =\frac{1-(\rho\zeta)^N}{1-\rho\zeta}; on a small product disk its principal phase is zero, while its literal binomial input is ∑a(da)(ρη)a=(1+ρη)d\sum_a\binom da(\rho\eta)^a=(1+\rho\eta)^d. Its exponential phase is recovered at the Cauchy interface, not from a nonzero small-circle slope. An O(N−1)O(N^{-1}) monomial relative estimate alone does not justify that further transform.

The inactive dependence of 𝒜r{\mathcal{ A}}_r, whose initial row (8) contains explicit Cauchy factors, is therefore retained in full. No pole is selected or deleted here. A later binomial theorem would have to keep those interfaces, their caps and competing branches on the actual extraction cycles.

Finally the original factors in (5), the exact a00(r)a_{00}^{(r)}, and G’s surrounding quotient braid, top Cartan and turnover remain unchanged. Fixed real occupation sectors do not establish a chart for a loxodromic geometric total moment. No meridian normal, geometric-source coefficient, or closed-source lower bound is asserted.
