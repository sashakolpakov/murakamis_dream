Coherent cuts retain a zero mode; an exact relative E-Ward class

This note proves that a Gaussian rank-one cut probe cannot remove both framing zero modes at an unchanged geometric critical point. It then constructs an exact finite Ward current and relative class using the actual nilpotent EE-action. The latter is genuine quantum boundary transport, but has not been identified with the internal geometric centralizer flow or its sourced Gaussian pushforward.

1. Conservation leaves one cut position, even for a rank-one probe

Every actual positive or negative crossing has position update

(a,b)↦(b+k,a−k),(a,b)↦(b−k,a+k), (a,b)\longmapsto(b+k,a-k),\qquad (a,b)\longmapsto(b-k,a+k),

respectively. Both conserve the total integer position. Close all strands except one by the actual pivotal trace. Along every surviving state, the other output positions equal their input positions. Conservation then forces

aout=ain. a_{\mathrm{out}}=a_{\mathrm{in}}. \tag{1.1}

This is an exact support equation, before taking a critical point or a root specialization. The auxiliary off-root cut matrix is diagonal too. Diagonal factorial changes of frame do not alter this fact.

Thus the rank-one probe ϕCNψϕψ \frac{\phi C_N\psi}{\phi\psi} has only one cut index in its actual state sum. Taking ψa=zaqca2,ϕa=waqda2 \psi_a=z^a q^{ca^2},\qquad \phi_a=w^a q^{da^2} gives exactly

∑a=0N−1(zw)aq(c+d)a2(CN)aaGN,GN=∑a=0N−1(zw)aq(c+d)a2. \frac{\sum_{a=0}^{N-1}(zw)^a q^{(c+d)a^2}(C_N)_{aa}} {G_N},\qquad G_N=\sum_{a=0}^{N-1}(zw)^a q^{(c+d)a^2}. \tag{1.2}

The denominator is retained and must be nonzero. At the actual root, scalarity of the cut operator makes (1.2) exactly FNF_N. There are still m+1m+1 independent flow variables for an mm-crossing knot braid, rather than m+2m+2. Introducing two symbols for the cut endpoints does not make them independent.

2. Exact rank-one obstruction at the geometric critical point

Let H=Log⁡QH=\operatorname{Log} Q, q=exp⁡(H/2)q=\exp(H/2), q1=Haq_1=H a, and κc=c+d\kappa_c=c+d. The added leading boundary phase in (1.2) is

g(q1)=Log⁡(zw)q1+κc2q12. g(q_1)=\operatorname{Log}(zw)\,q_1+\frac{\kappa_c}{2}q_1^2. \tag{2.1}

Its normalization GNG_N is independent of all internal variables. Even when z,wz,w depend on the external meridian, it has no internal Hessian contribution.

The original geometric critical phase has a two-dimensional radical, the actual framed B1B_1-torsor. On its regular chart,

Acut=MM+s,q1=log⁡Acut,dq1=−dsM+s. A_{\mathrm{cut}}=\frac{M}{M+s},\qquad q_1=\log A_{\mathrm{cut}}, \qquad dq_1=-\frac{ds}{M+s}. \tag{2.2}

In particular it is independent of the residual centralizer coordinate rr, whose fundamental vector is VJ=Δ∂r,Δ=s+(M−M−1)r. V_J=\Delta\partial_r,\qquad \Delta=s+(M-M^{-1})r.

For the same geometric point to remain critical after (2.1), one must have g′(q1*)=0g'(q_1^*)=0 on the chosen logarithmic sheet. Indeed dq1≠0dq_1\ne0 there. At that point the full Hessian changes by

Hnew=Hold+κcdq1⊗dq1. H_{\mathrm{new}}=H_{\mathrm{old}}+\kappa_c\,dq_1\otimes dq_1. \tag{2.3}

On the two-dimensional original radical, its matrix in the framing coordinates is therefore

(000κc/(M+s)2). \begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&\kappa_c/(M+s)^2\end{pmatrix}. \tag{2.4}

Consequently ∂r\partial_r remains in the full Hessian kernel. If κc≠0\kappa_c\ne0, the old Hessian has exactly that two-dimensional kernel, and dq1dq_1 is nonzero on it, the new corank is exactly one. To check the last assertion, project Hnewv=0H_{\mathrm{new}}v=0 to the old cokernel; it forces dq1(v)=0dq_1(v)=0, then Holdv=0H_{\mathrm{old}}v=0.

Thus a coherent Gaussian cut can generically remove one framing mode, but cannot remove both at an unchanged geometric critical point. The exact Gauss-sum normalization does not change this obstruction. This result does not assume that a differently moved coherent critical scheme has already been identified with a holonomy scheme.

3. An explicit finite E-Ward current

Use the actual factorial boundary frame of the marked cut calculation. Write ℰ=ρ2E,ρ=q−q−1,x=−M/q. \mathcal E=\rho^2E,\qquad \rho=q-q^{-1},\qquad x=-M/q. It is upper bidiagonal:

ℰa,a+1=ϵa=q−1x−1(1−x2Q−a)=MQ−a−1−M−1,0≤a<N−1, \mathcal E_{a,a+1}=\epsilon_a =q^{-1}x^{-1}(1-x^2Q^{-a}) =M Q^{-a-1}-M^{-1},\qquad 0\le a<N-1, \tag{3.1}

and its last wrap is zero. At M=1M=1, ϵa=Q−a−1−1, \epsilon_a=Q^{-a-1}-1, which is nonzero for every interior edge 0≤a<N−10\le a<N-1. Hence these coefficients are units in the whole meridian germ near completion. The exact actual intertwining identity gives [ℰ,CN]=0. [\mathcal E,C_N]=0. Since CNC_N is diagonal by (1.1), this reads

ϵa((CN)a+1,a+1−(CN)aa)=0. \epsilon_a\big((C_N)_{a+1,a+1}-(C_N)_{aa}\big)=0. \tag{3.2}

It independently connects all its diagonal entries.

Let w,w′w,w' be any two diagonal probe weights with ∑awa=∑awa′=1\sum_a w_a=\sum_a w'_a=1, and put ra=wa−wa′,ja=∑b=0arb(0≤a<N−1). r_a=w_a-w'_a,\qquad j_a=\sum_{b=0}^a r_b \quad(0\le a<N-1). Define the strictly lower-bidiagonal current Ba+1,a=jaϵa. B_{a+1,a}=\frac{j_a}{\epsilon_a}. \tag{3.3} Direct matrix multiplication gives the exact identity

[ℰ,B]=diag⁡(r0,…,rN−1). \boxed{[\mathcal E,B]=\operatorname{diag}(r_0,\ldots,r_{N-1}).} \tag{3.4}

Indeed its diagonal is ja−ja−1j_a-j_{a-1}, with j−1=jN−1=0j_{-1}=j_{N-1}=0; both products have no other entries. It follows that

∑a(wa−wa′)(CN)aa=Tr⁡([ℰ,B]CN)=Tr⁡(B[CN,ℰ])=0. \sum_a(w_a-w'_a)(C_N)_{aa} =\operatorname{Tr}([\mathcal E,B]C_N) =\operatorname{Tr}(B[C_N,\mathcal E])=0. \tag{3.5}

This is an exact source transgression for the finite cut, including its endpoints. For the average wa=1/Nw_a=1/N and the endpoint probe wa′=δa0w'_a=\delta_{a0}, it is explicitly ja=(a+1)/N−1. j_a=(a+1)/N-1.

Equivalently, the actual weighted interval difference (Df)a=ϵa(fa+1−fa) (Df)_a=\epsilon_a(f_{a+1}-f_a) gives an exact complex 0→k→kN→DkN−1→0. 0\longrightarrow k\longrightarrow k^N \xrightarrow{D}k^{N-1}\longrightarrow0. \tag{3.6} Surjectivity is solved by the cumulative sum f0=0,fa=∑b<a(Df)b/ϵbf_0=0,\ f_a=\sum_{b<a}(Df)_b/\epsilon_b. Its dual says that normalized probes differ by a unique edge current. No endpoint term is discarded.

The cyclic coefficient that was removed in (3.1) is also exact: ϵN−1cyc=q−1x−1(1−M2)=M−M−1. \epsilon_{N-1}^{\mathrm{cyc}} =q^{-1}x^{-1}(1-M^2)=M-M^{-1}. \tag{3.7} It is the original whole-meridian nilpotent endpoint defect, rather than an arbitrary choice of cyclic representative. Its vanishing at M=1M=1 does not permit deleting its meridian normal derivative. In particular its scalar is exactly the regular-meridian eigenvalue gap; this coincidence alone does not identify the two group actions.

6. What has not been identified

The geometric centralizer JMJ_M is multiplicative away from M=1M=1 and additive at M=1M=1; the finite flow (4.1) remains additive for all MM. They cannot be equated as whole meridian group schemes. In addition, unscaled etℰe^{t\mathcal E} is an amplitude-scale flow in a semiclassical expansion. A finite canonical flow requires a generator divided by H=Log⁡QH=\operatorname{Log} Q.

There is a useful exact candidate at the special fiber: −log⁡(1+ℰ)-\log(1+\mathcal E) is a finite nilpotent polynomial. Its principal cut symbol at M=1,pcut=1M=1,\ p_{\mathrm{cut}}=1 is −log⁡(1+ecut)=−log⁡(1+s)=log⁡Acut. -\log(1+e_{\mathrm{cut}})=-\log(1+s)=\log A_{\mathrm{cut}}. The already established geometric form obeys ιVJΩcut=dlog⁡Acut. \iota_{V_J}\Omega_{\mathrm{cut}}=d\log A_{\mathrm{cut}}. This matches a scalar Hamiltonian on the framed chart. It does not prove that the full internal Ward action becomes VJV_J, because the reduced density form and the bulk internal shift complex still need their marked chain comparison.

Thus (5.4) supplies actual finite source transport and endpoints. It does not yet supply the weighted internal-shift covariance or external-meridian compatibility required in section 4 of the sourced telescoping criterion. Neither geometric support nor a selected volume asymptotic follows from the coherent probe or this boundary relative class alone.

The cyclic Ward norm adapter completes the literal missing edge kappa and proves an exact determinant character at the Frobenius peripheral value M^N. Its finite quantum-factorial gauge has a scalar cyclic source, with the full modified-dimension ratio retained. This resolves the norm-character part of the generic-versus-nilpotent comparison. It does not make the additive flow here and the geometric centralizer isomorphic.
