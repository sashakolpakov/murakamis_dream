The physical THIRD five-point word with its original source

28 October 2026. The THIRD certificate

-partial(a,b,p,p_1,s_R)

has a physical two-head realization. The construction uses the inverse of the actual shared-flag braid transport, followed by the already proved FIRST identity at the opposite root. Finite factorial reflection embeds its inverse word in the ENTIRE original crossing. All clock marks, strict cuts, scalar lines and original endpoint sources remain.

This replaces the proposed use of the auxiliary vacuum pentagon for this face table. That auxiliary formula remains an exact original-R port, but its contracted source has a forced degenerate clock face. The physical construction below does not use those compressed ports.

This closes the literal local operator/source certificate at the same level as FIRST and MIDDLE. It does not identify its framed factors with a positive charged geometric state or transport the original global cycle to the positive reference cycle.

1. The actual third flags and their useful order

Keep the complete conventions of the shared actual flags:

Q=exp(2pi*i/N), x=-q^(-1), lambda=x^(-1)=Qx,
S_1^N=mu, S_2^N=x^N mu,
Z=Z_1, T=Z_2, X=x S_2S_1^(-1),
a_u=TX, n_+=e_1S_2, n_-=-e_1Z_2S_2,
P_+=P_Q(n_+), M=P_Q(n_-),
A=M P_+=P_Q(e_1f_2), R_0=D A,
B=flip D A.

The unchanged common scalar kappa is external to B and R_0; all original framing and peripheral factors are retained there. Define

H=P_+^(-1)ZP_+=Z(1-n_+),
R=1-(1-T)X,
X_R=a_u R^(-1), Z_R=1-R(1-Z).                (1)

Here H is a root clock. R is a unit since ((1-T)X)^N=0. Neither is an inverse of a missing-arrow projector.

In the SAME bounded homogeneous frame used by FIRST and MIDDLE, the actual THIRD tuple has columns and original right tags

The fifth row is the actual total-lowering first column, as proved in FIRST. No flag determinant has been inverted to obtain this table.

At the commutative regular chart, rescaling s_R to one gives

(a,b,p,p_1,s_R)=(0,1-X_R,infinity,1/(1-Z_R),1),
H=X_R+Z_R-X_R Z_R.                          (2)

The original homogeneous columns, rather than the displayed scalar reciprocals, remain the finite definitions.

2. Inverse actual flag transport gives the missing polarization

Put

a_eff=Q a_u Z^(-1),
C=A^(-1)a_eff A.                            (3)

The following are exact physical matrix identities:

Z_R=A^(-1)ZA,
C=H^(-1)X_R,
CZ_R=QZ_R C, C^N=Z_R^N=1.                   (4)

Thus X_R and Z_R are not renamed as a quantum torus. The actual coupled torus is C,Z_R, and its required polarization is X_R=HC, with the displayed order. There is no auxiliary compression.

Here is an all-level proof of the nontrivial middle identity in (4). Use t_2,Delta,v,t_3,H_1,H_2 from the shared-flag note. Its independently proved forward relations are

BS_1B^(-1)=t_2, B t_2 B^(-1)=t_3,
BG_1B^(-1)=H_2,
t_2=F_tot+xK_tot^(-1)S_2,
t_3=t_2-QDelta v^(-1).

Direct multiplication of those original Gauss matrices gives

H_2(1,S_1)^t=(1,t_3)^t x v.                 (5)

For clarity, its upper-right entry is beta=e_2K_1, its lower-right entry is lambda+t_2 beta, and its upper row on (1,S_1) is x v. The second component is consequently t_2 x v-lambda Delta, which is the second component in (5).

The rank-one expression for G_1-lambda and G_1^(-1) gives

G_1^(-1)(1,t_2)^t=(1,t_0)^t lambda^(-1)QH,
t_0=S_1+Q^(-1)Delta H^(-1)=xS_2H^(-1).      (6)

The first tag in (6) follows by expanding 1-lambda^(-1)e_1Delta=QH. Its last equality follows from S_1H+Q^(-1)Delta=xS_2; S_2 commutes with H. Conjugating the ENTIRE column (6) by B and using (5) proves

B^(-1)S_1B=xS_2H^(-1).                     (7)

Both first tags are units. No uniqueness of a nilpotent eigenline is presumed. Because B preserves the original F_tot and K_tot, the formula for t_2 also gives

B^(-1)S_2B=x^(-1)K_tot(S_1-F_tot).           (8)

Cartan and flip conjugation give, with x2=Q(-1),

flip D a_eff D^(-1) flip=X^(-1).

Hence C=B(-1)X(-1)B. Substitute (7)–(8), together with

S_1-F_tot=RZS_1,
K_tot=x^2 Z^(-1)T^(-1), [K_tot,R]=0.

In order, this gives

C=H^(-1)X Z^(-1)R^(-1)K_tot^(-1)
  =H^(-1)TXR^(-1)=H^(-1)X_R,

proving the missing polarization identity without a finite-level guess.

For the first identity in (4), A=P_Q(e_1f_2) and the Euler recurrence give A^(-1)ZA=Z(1-e_1f_2). The actual relation Z e_1f_2=-(1-T)X(1-Z) identifies this with (1). Finally a_eff Z=QZ a_eff and a_eff^N=1 give the last two identities in (4).

Define the actual nilpotent

nu=C(1-Z_R)=A^(-1)n_-A=P_+^(-1)n_-P_+.

The original Euler recurrence then proves the ordered relation

H=Z_R(1-nu)^(-1),
X_R=HC,
H=Z_R(1-C+CZ_R)^(-1).                       (9)

All inverses in (9) exist since nu^N=0. In particular these are full coupled physical operators, not products of distinct typed ports.

is even. Computing determinants of the columns in Section 1 gives the following table, with c_cl=C and t_cl=Z_R at the classical symbol:

These are EXACTLY the FIRST table with parameters C,Z_R, on this evenly reordered ACTUAL third configuration. The calculation uses the specified five flags, not equality of two arbitrary zero Bloch sums.

Put at this classical chart

nu=C(1-Z_R), y=-CZ_R/(1-C).

The table’s signed sum is

-partial_THIRD=[nu]-[H]+[Z_R]-[y]-[C].       (10)

Its explicit remaining quad relations are

Expansion of its negative alternating boundary gives exactly (11). The cross-ratios and both quads are checked independently by symbolic determinants in the accompanying audit.

4. Opposite-root FIRST on the physical transported clock

Set r=Q^(-1). Its original finite Euler function is P_r; this is not a replacement invariant. It is an explicit auxiliary factor inside the following identity for the ORIGINAL P_Q crossing.

Let E_Q(Z_R) denote the actual spectral projector at Z_R=Q, transported by the SAME A in (4). Put

c_3=C(1-E_Q(Z_R)),
a_3=c_3(1-rZ_R)=C(1-rZ_R)=C^(-1)nu C.       (12)

Then c_3Z_R=r^(-1)Z_Rc_3 and c_3N=a_3N=0. The missing arrow is at Z_R=Q, not Z_R=1. The inverse-root clock index is j_new=-j modulo N, so that this is precisely its top index d.

Keep the actual negative-orientation full function and scalar

phi_-=conjugate(phi_+),
phi_+=sqrt(N)exp{i*pi[1/4-(N+N^(-1))/12]},

F_3=[marked Psi_-(r Z_R)]^(-1).

In the transported clock frame its value at old index j is

F_3(j)=phi_-^(-1)(r;r)_((-j) mod N).         (13)

The logarithmic lift is -2pii(j_new+1)/N. In particular the cut index j_new=d uses the regular negative TOP value -2pi*i, not the bottom zero. Consecutive factorial ratios prove

F_3 c_3 F_3^(-1)=a_3.

All these factors can be constructed without knowing A: use the explicit rational formulas (1), (4) for C,Z_R and the root-clock projectors E_(Qj)(Z_R)=N(-1)sum_k Q(-jk)Z_Rk. Equations (3)–(4) prove which ORIGINAL transported clock mark those explicit factors carry; they do not define a new factor by an unknown conjugated crossing.

Define

P_3=P_r(c_3), H_3=P_3^(-1)Z_RP_3,
F_H3=P_3^(-1)F_3P_3,
y_3=-r c_3H_3, Y_3=P_r(y_3).

The independently proved FIRST identity now applies on these physical heads. In inverse-word form it is

F_H3^(-1)P_3^(-1)
   =P_3^(-1)F_H3^(-1)Y_3.                  (14)

The exact developed FIRST arguments before word inversion are c_3, Z_R, y_3, J_3, a_3, with

J_3=Y_3^(-1)H_3Y_3=(1-a_3)^(-1)Z_R.

Their classical values are precisely C,Z_R,y,H,nu of (10). At finite N one keeps J_3, a_3, their marks and their displayed q-shifts; one does not replace them by H and nu as exact matrices.

Here is the exact common-port convention, including a common-output realization if preferred. For a product acting right to left, develop an argument at the right/input port by conjugating it by the inverse of all factors to its right. The left word of (14) then develops H_3 to P_3H_3P_3^(-1)=Z_R and leaves c_3 unchanged. The right word develops its three arguments to

Y_3^(-1)F_H3 c_3 F_H3^(-1)Y_3=a_3,
Y_3^(-1)H_3Y_3=J_3,

Thus this is the forward FIRST common-left/output table read in reverse at the inverse word’s right/input port. It is not the unconjugated output table of the raw inverse word.

To realize the SAME table in a common-left/output frame, put

W_3=P_3F_H3=F_3P_3,
hat(O)=W_3 O W_3^(-1).

Conjugate EACH individual factor and its current in (14) by W_3. Its total product remains exactly the original inverse word, because W_3 commutes with W_3^(-1). The framed equality is

hat(F_H3^(-1)) hat(P_3^(-1))
   =hat(P_3^(-1)) hat(F_H3^(-1)) hat(Y_3).   (14a)

The developed arguments at its common-left/output port are (Z_R,c_3) on the left and (a_3,J_3,y_3) on the right, exactly. For example hat(H_3)=Z_R, hat(c_3)=a_3, and developing the second left argument gives c_3. All W_3/W_3^(-1) face frames in (14a) are retained; no claim of a tame size or trivial action is made.

There are two orientation changes in (14): the Euler/full-Psi root is r=Q^(-1), and the FIRST word is inverted and read with its original input/output face types reversed. Relative to the original h=2pi*i/N these changes cancel. Its signed face boundary is therefore the specified negative THIRD boundary (10), with the above even flag order. All inverse-word interface marks are transported from (13), not reselected independently.

5. Exact reflection embeds the entire word in the ORIGINAL R

Set B_3=P_r(a_3). Nilpotent factorial reflection gives exactly

P_r(t)=P_Q(Qt)^(-1)=(1-t)P_Q(t)^(-1)

for every t^N=0. Indeed (r;r)_h=(-1)^h Q^(-h(h+1)/2)(Q;Q)_h, so the P_r coefficient is Q^h/(Q;Q)_h, the inverse Euler coefficient of Qt. Apply this to (12):

B_3=C^(-1)(1-nu)P_Q(nu)^(-1)C,
A=P_+ C B_3^(-1) C^(-1)(1-nu).             (15)

Here P_Q(nu)=P_+^(-1)MP_+, proving the second equality. The factor 1-nu is a compulsory original source adapter; it is not set to one.

Also B_3=F_3P_3F_3(-1)=P_3F_H3F_3(-1). Substitution of (14) into (15) gives the COMPLETE physical THIRD refactorization

R_0=D P_+ C F_3 [F_H3^(-1)P_3^(-1)]
                     C^(-1)(1-nu)
   =D P_+ C F_3 [P_3^(-1)F_H3^(-1)Y_3]
                     C^(-1)(1-nu).        (16)

Either bracket in (16) may equivalently be written with its fully framed factors from (14a); its product is identical. That is the common-output face version whose literal determinants were used above. There is no unstated conjugation of a single face alone.

All factors are explicit original physical matrices or their specified finite Euler/full-Psi frames. The inverse actual checked crossing is obtained by inverting the ENTIRE displayed word, with the same external scalar and closing conventions. There is no auxiliary index left to suppress.

6. The complete source and every cut remain

Keep the original p=N sum_j x^(j-d)|j,d-j> and full marker m|a,b>=xb/N2. In the local identity (14), its literal input and output from (16) are

psi_3=C^(-1)(1-nu)p,
ell_3=m flip D P_+ C F_3.                   (17)

The ENTIRE input adapter has the useful exact cancellation

C^(-1)(1-nu)=R (TX)^(-1) Z_R.               (17a)

Indeed C(-1)=X_R(-1)H=R(TX)^(-1)H and H(1-nu)=Z_R. Thus the original incoming source requires no inverse of H or R. On unit wrap marks, X,T,Z are unitary, so ||R||<=3 and ||Z_R||<=1+||R||||1-Z||<=7. Consequently the adapter in (17a) has operator norm at most 21, uniformly in N. In the original Euclidean head metric this gives ||psi_3||<=21 N^(3/2). This is a bound on the actual input adapter, not on its inverse, the outgoing factorial frame, or the entire geometric cycle comparison.

Thus both sides have exactly the original normalized one-crossing source value 1. With the unstripped original crossing that value has the same original kappa line. In a longer tangle, replace p and m by its actual exteriors in (17); equality holds before any contraction. The scalar phi_- in F_3 and F_H3 is retained and cancels in the full word because those precise interfaces have opposite powers. No selected scalar is divided out.

The full transported strict cut is

c_3^h=C^h[1-sum_(j=1)^h E_(Q^j)(Z_R)],
                     0<=h<N.              (18)

This follows by following C’s decreasing old clock indices: its deleted input is j=1, and the forbidden inputs for h arrows are exactly j=1,…,h. It includes the wrapped index-zero path and every parity. Neither the sum nor the individual projectors is deleted on source grounds. Every actual Ward current is transported by applying its ordinary typed commutator product rule to the full equality (16).

Each nilpotent Euler factor in (16) has the exact original full-Psi circle-minus-radial moment realization, using the appropriate root orientation. The new clock frame is explicitly (13) transported by A and P_3. Thus all corresponding radial relative-source terms and factorial frame factors stay in the identity. This statement uses the finite matrix theorem and the already proved moment theorem; it does not replace a generalized coefficient functional by bare evaluation of Psi.

The theorem is at the physical complete root for all N and all typed wrap marks mu. An off-root Hopf identity or a complete meridian-normal version of the flag table is not asserted here.

7. Why the earlier auxiliary port did not supply this table

In the auxiliary construction, C_0=C_B|0> and rho=<0|P_0 satisfy exactly

(B_aux-1)C_0=0,
rho(H_0-1)=0,
<0|W=<0|.

Consequently its auxiliary pentagon has a face forced to one on every regular fully contracted stationary configuration. Simultaneous controlled or Gaussian similarities only transport these eigenconstraints. They do not give the generic regular THIRD configuration by multiplying its differently typed ports. That construction remains a valid exact R formula. Equations (4), (12)–(17) instead realize THIRD on the physical coupled flags and keep its true polarization and full source.
