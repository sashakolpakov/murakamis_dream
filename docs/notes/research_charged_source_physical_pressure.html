Physical charge pressure and its geometric excess

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The whole original charged source has a graph-dependent exponential envelope sharper than a constant plus its exponential type. All physical first normals are included. The envelope is the pressure of the positive physical kernel values, and does not depend on the real crossing powers. At a supplied positive complex-charge geometric point its excess over the selected zero-power action is an explicit positive convex-energy difference. The resulting Fresnel-tail bound retains that excess rather than replacing the physical pressure by the filled action.
