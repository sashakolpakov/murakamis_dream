Root-star compositions and the knot complement volume

31 October 2026. assembly. This note concerns the literal composition C(h0T(h1⋯hs))C(h_0T(h_1\cdots h_s)), with s≥2s\ge2. The analytic argument treats the correlated root character together with its child. It does not replace the original signed source by a positive alias envelope. The theorem records all physical pure/odd patterns in this literal presentation. Its all-odd source overlaps an existing belt-stack theorem; no new knot isotopy classification is asserted. The published completed-root manuscript is unchanged.

Fix a half-twist pattern whose literal terminal pairings have one main component and no internal closed component. Such patterns exist by the endpoint-pairing construction in the general marked geometry theorem, §7.

At leaf ii, insert a fixed signed full twist R−2piR^{-2p_i}. Filling that circle replaces the leaf by the rational tangle T(Rϵi−2pi)T(R^{\epsilon_i-2p_i}). Write Ks,ϵ(𝒑)K_{s,\epsilon}(\mathbf p) for the resulting knot in this retained presentation.

Theorem. For every fixed s≥2s\ge2 and knot half-twist pattern there is P(s,ϵ)<∞P(s,\epsilon)<\infty such that, for all fixed signed integers |pi|>P(s,ϵ)|p_i|>P(s,\epsilon), the knot complement is hyperbolic and limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(Ks,ϵ(𝒑))|=Vol⁡(S3\Ks,ϵ(𝒑)). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log\left|K_N(K_{s,\epsilon}(\mathbf p))\right| =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K_{s,\epsilon}(\mathbf p)). \tag{1} The level runs through both parities and all composite levels. Here KNK_N is the original reduced enhanced invariant. The normalization comparison keeps its outer framing character of modulus one. 5. What this adds and what remains open

The root-star expression has a bare root leaf and is outside the literal completed-root grammar of the published manuscript. This is a statement about that grammar, not a claim that its knot types were previously unknown or cannot admit another covered presentation. The prior-source coverage comparison records two concrete overlaps. The missing Fourier mode already appears in the outer squared-kernel theorem. When every inner sign is nonzero, the entire mixed-color scalar (5) is the earlier octahedral-belt scalar with outer power a=ϵ0+n+−n−a=\epsilon_0+n_+-n_-. Its full source estimate was already available. The present child argument also allows inner ϵi=0\epsilon_i=0, outside that recorded two-type belt alphabet. A source identity alone supplies no isotopy or correspondence of peripheral curves. The marked-mask proof in §4 establishes the geometry of the actual current presentation independently. The ordinary pretzel source has no outer LjL_j and is not the same literal source.

For arbitrary strict closed trees, the core estimate and actual marked filling calibration are now available. The remaining source requirement is a nonempty-alias bound at the complete geometric rate, or an equally sufficient bound after exact filling. The present Fourier theorem gives such a bound when the turned child is a product of physical leaves. It does not prove the necessary spectral localization for an arbitrary child with internal turns. Presentation coverage and a generic main-meridian quantum A-operator theorem remain separate open tasks.
