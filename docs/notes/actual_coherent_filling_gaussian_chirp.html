The original filling kernel is an exact centered Gaussian with a normal source

29 October 2026. The full original twist, including every projective Casimir jet, is an exact centered Gaussian in the pair eigenlogarithm. Its finite Fourier coefficients have an exact Poisson/Fresnel formula with a retained INTEGER LINEAR charge weight. No coprimality or parity restriction is needed. The representation gives actual nonzero local Gaussian charge channels near real complete color for fixed |p|>=2. It does not prove that their exterior pairing survives in a filled knot.

1. Full-Hermite Gaussian, not a value-only Gauss sum

Use the original N,d=N-1,q=exp(pi i/N),Q=q^2, kappa=q(d2/2), and hbar=2pi i/N. The node eigenlogs are UNWRAPPED:

x_k=pi i(2k+1)/N, w_k=exp(x_k), 0<=k<N.

For an integer P, the actual twist2P data in the complete Fourier-Hermite theorem are h_k=nu_k^(2P), d_k=-P(d-2k)h_k. They satisfy EXACTLY

hk=κ−Pexp⁡{P(xk−πi)2/ℏ},dk=∂x[κ−PeP(x−πi)2/ℏ]|x=xk. \boxed{h_k=\kappa^{-P} \exp\{P(x_k-\pi i)^2/\hbar\},\qquad d_k=\left.\partial_x\left[\kappa^{-P} e^{P(x-\pi i)^2/\hbar}\right]\right|_{x=x_k}.} \tag{1}

Indeed the derivative multiplier is P(2k+1-N), and expanding the square gives precisely Q^(P k(k+1)). Its difference is a full integer turn, not an omitted even-level sign. The centered square is invariant under x->2pi i-x, so it has the compatible paired values, opposite derivatives, and zero derivative at the odd simple node x=pi i. Every original normal jet is present.

There is also a source-independent matrix form avoiding any sum over auxiliary colors. The function

K(z)=arccosh(-z/2)^2

has the branch analytic on C minus[2,infinity), with its removable continuation at z=-2. On the nodes, K(c_k)=(x_k-pi i)^2. The actual finite Casimir has all its eigenvalues below2. Full holomorphic/Hermite functional calculus therefore gives

R2P=κPexp⁡{PK(Ω)/ℏ}. \boxed{R^{2P}=\kappa^P\exp\{P K(\Omega)/\hbar\}.} \tag{2}

This equality uses BOTH (1) conditions and the FULL polynomial P_N=2T_N(z/2)+2. It is not an assertion that R is Euclidean unitary; its nilpotent exponential is retained. For the filled odd box the exact formula is

ℛ(R−(2p+1))=κ−pℛ{R−1e−pK(Ω)/ℏ}. \mathscr R(R^{-(2p+1)}) =\kappa^{-p}\mathscr R\{R^{-1} e^{-pK(\Omega)/\hbar}\}. \tag{3}

The actual turn map mathscrR is linear, not multiplicative. Formula (3) does not move the exponential through a turn as a new local operator or discard a source-normal direction.

2. A coherent all-color finite Gaussian kernel

The exact marked transforms are

H_n=N^(-1)sum_k h_k w_k^(-n),
D_n=N^(-1)sum_k d_k w_k^(-n),
a_n=[D_n+(N-n)H_n]/N, 0<=n<N.                     (4)

At n=0, D_0=0 so (4) includes a_0=H_0. These a are the actual first-kind Chebyshev coefficients, not freely chosen Fourier values. For an arbitrary original mixed-circle exterior A_j, where A_j multiplies L_(j+1), put

eta_n=(-1)^n(A_n-A_(n-2)), A_negative=0.

Then the FULL filling/source identity is

κ2P∑j=0N−1βjAj=κ2PN2∑n,k=0N−1hkwk−n[N−n−P(d−2k)]ηn. \kappa^{2P}\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\beta_j A_j =\frac{\kappa^{2P}}{N^2} \sum_{n,k=0}^{N-1}h_kw_k^{-n} [N-n-P(d-2k)]\,\eta_n. \tag{5}

It holds for scalar, Hilbert-valued or operator-valued exteriors in their original order. Taking a difference on the exterior retains the two finite color endpoints; no periodic substitute for that finite difference is made.

For completeness the normal transform can be recovered coherently from the value transform alone, with its compulsory full finite Hilbert kernel:

Dn=−iP∑m≠nHmsin⁡(π(m−n)/N). D_n=-iP\sum_{m\ne n}\frac{H_m}{\sin(\pi(m-n)/N)}. \tag{6}

Derive this by the elementary DFT of k: its nonzero-frequency coefficients are -1/(1-Q^(-l)), and its mean is d/2. That mean cancels the -PdH_n term exactly. The matrix in (6) is Hermitian and has norm |P|d. This does NOT allow one to discard D when H_n vanishes at a particular color: it mixes all retained colors.

All gcd/parity behavior is already exact in (4). For P!=0 put g=gcd(|P|,N), L=N/g. The value H_n is zero unless g divides n. After that reduction it has modulus L^(-1/2) for odd L. For even L it is zero unless

(P-n)/g congruent to (P/g)L/2 modulo2,

and otherwise has modulus sqrt(2/L). These statements follow by grouping the quadratic sum into g cosets and expanding its squared modulus; the latter leaves only the shifts0, and L/2 if L is even. They include L=1 by its trivial sum. Their PHASES remain the actual finite Gaussian sum in (4); none is divided out as a nonzero normalizer. For P=0, H_n=delta_(n,0),D_n=0, exactly.

In particular arithmetic zeros of the value Gauss sum do not determine support of the full-Hermite filling kernel.

3. Exact finite-window Poisson representation and endpoint current

Set t_k=(k+1/2)/N. For P real integer define the entire finite coefficient interpolation

a(n)=κ−PN∑k=0N−1[2P(tk−12)+1−n/N]e2πiPN(tk−1/2)2−2πintk. a(n)=\frac{\kappa^{-P}}N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} [2P(t_k-\tfrac12)+1-n/N] e^{2\pi i PN(t_k-1/2)^2-2\pi i n t_k}. \tag{7}

It is exactly (4) on the physical n=0,…,N-1. It is a specified interpolation of the FILLING COEFFICIENT, not a new assertion about an arbitrary nonintegral ordinary-color exterior.

Define

IP,N(b)=∫01e2πiPN(t−1/2)2−2πibtdt,JP,N(n)=eπiPN/2(1−e−2πin)2πiN. I_{P,N}(b)=\int_0^1 e^{2\pi i PN(t-1/2)^2-2\pi i bt}\,dt, \quad J_{P,N}(n)=\frac{e^{\pi i PN/2}(1-e^{-2\pi i n})}{2\pi i N}.

The all-N, all-parity exact formula is

a(n)=κ−P∑ℓ∈ℤsymmetric(−1)ℓ{(ℓ+1)IP,N(n+Nℓ)−JP,N(n)}. \boxed{a(n)=\kappa^{-P} \sum_{\ell\in\mathbb Z}^{\mathrm{symmetric}}(-1)^\ell \{(\ell+1)I_{P,N}(n+N\ell)-J_{P,N}(n)\}.} \tag{8}

The SUM IS PAIRED symmetrically in ell. Each paired tail is O(ell^(-2)) on compact n sets for fixed N,P; the series is then locally uniformly convergent. The endpoint current J is essential off the integer lattice. No unsupported absolute summation of its constant pieces is intended.

Here is a direct proof. Apply midpoint Fourier sampling to the periodic extension of the smooth finite-window integrand in (7). Its Fourier coefficient at frequency N ell acquires (-1)^ell. The periodic extension may jump at its endpoints, but its Fourier series converges to each interior t_k. Its paired Fourier aliases converge as stated, by two integrations by parts; their leading odd1/ell terms cancel. In that coefficient put b=n+N ell. The amplitude equals

[2P(t-1/2)-b/N]+(ell+1).

The first bracket is the phase derivative divided by 2pi i N. Its integrated endpoint is EXACTLY -J_(P,N)(n). This proves (8) and specifies the original side current rather than assuming it vanishes under a later complex-color deformation.

For physical INTEGER n, J=0 and (8) simplifies to

an=κ−P∑ℓsymmetric(−1)ℓ(ℓ+1)IP,N(n+Nℓ). a_n=\kappa^{-P}\sum_\ell^{\mathrm{symmetric}} (-1)^\ell(\ell+1)I_{P,N}(n+N\ell). \tag{9}

Thus the integer linear charge weight is exactly the retained NORMAL data in (4), not an optional phase-space amplitude. In particular ell=-1 is killed on the original integer color grid. Off that grid its term is -J and may NOT be dropped. The zero charge is therefore not deleted from a deformed source by fiat.

When P!=0, each integral in (8) is explicitly a finite Fresnel difference. With b arbitrary complex,

IP,N(b)=e−πib−πib2/(2PN)∫−1/2−b/(2PN)1/2−b/(2PN)e2πiPNv2dv. I_{P,N}(b)=e^{-\pi i b-\pi i b^2/(2PN)} \int_{-1/2-b/(2PN)}^{1/2-b/(2PN)}e^{2\pi i PN v^2}\,dv. \tag{10}

The integration segment is the translated original interval, and the integrand is entire. For P=0 use its elementary Fourier integral instead. Thus no division by a Gauss sum or a coprimality assumption has entered any formula.

4. Exact odd-filling operator and its classical charge equation

Put B_j=mathscrR(R^(-1)L_(j+1)), and eta_nB=(-1)n(B_n-B_(n-2)), B_negative=0. Substituting P=-p into (5),(9) gives an ALL-INPUT identity for the filled box:

ℛ(R−(2p+1))=κ−p∑n=0N−1ηnB∑ℓsymmetric(−1)ℓ(ℓ+1)I−p,N(n+Nℓ). \boxed{\mathscr R(R^{-(2p+1)}) =\kappa^{-p}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\eta_n^B \sum_\ell^{\mathrm{symmetric}}(-1)^\ell(\ell+1) I_{-p,N}(n+N\ell).} \tag{11}

All exteriors and original modified/peripheral factors are kept when this is inserted in a diagram. Multiple boxes can be replaced simultaneously before their original marked closure, by ordinary linearity. Formula (11) is the coherent surgery kernel, rather than an absolute sum over independently normalized circle invariants.

To state its local symbol precisely, let a=n/N, b/N=a+ell, y=2pi i(t-1/2), mu=2pi i(a-1), and c=ell+1. Stationarity of the Gaussian integral in (9) gives

2Py−μ=2πic. 2P y-\mu=2\pi i c. \tag{12}

For the actual negative odd filling, P=-p, this is mu+2p y=-2pi i c. It is the canonical FULL-TWIST charge relation with the half twist already in B_j. The exact physical auxiliary color is j=n+1, whose squared-meridian log near completeness is mu_phys=2pi i((n+1)/N-1)=mu+hbar. Thus the finite equation has the corresponding retained Weyl shift, and (12) is its leading classical form. Identifying y with a chosen topological longitude also requires the actual peripheral chart, not an inference from a classical A-polynomial alone.

For the actual low mixed pretzel core the sign can be checked directly. Put H=Q^h for auxiliary color j=N-h. Its literal coefficient has the identity

a_(h,v)x^v/N^2=q^(h^2)F_(2v)/[F_v^2F_(v-h)F_(v+h)].

Thus its phase replaces 4Li2(X) by 2Li2(X)+Li2(X/H)+Li2(XH), and includes +(Log H)^2/2. Differentiation at a regular internal saddle gives

dG/dLogH=LogH+Log(1-X/H)-Log(1-HX)
        =Log[(H-X)/(1-HX)]=Log L_H,

with its continued integer mark. Here mu_phys=-LogH, rather than LogH. In the circle kernel the factor (-1)^(j-1) cancels the Gaussian linear action -pi i mu_phys, leaving

(LogH-2pi i c)^2/(4p)

up to real constants for P=-p. Its combined stationary equation is therefore LogH+2p LogL_H=2pi i c. This is a calculation from the original factorial and normal-source phases. It still does not assert the actual exterior contour selects that stationary point.

5. Exact first-order transport of the FULL filling coefficient

There is an additional useful source Ward identity for a(n). Shifting the original periodic chirp index k->k+1 gives, on integer n,

Hn−2P=Qn−PHn,Dn−2P=Qn−P(Dn−2PHn+2Pq−n). H_{n-2P}=Q^{n-P}H_n, \quad D_{n-2P}=Q^{n-P}(D_n-2P H_n+2Pq^{-n}).

The boundary term 2P is the retained jump of the original LINEAR normal weight at k=N-1. Substitution in (4) cancels the homogeneous H terms and proves

a(n−2P)=Qn−Pa(n)+2PNqn−2P,n∈ℤ. \boxed{a(n-2P)=Q^{n-P}a(n)+\frac{2P}{N}q^{n-2P}, \quad n\in\mathbb Z.} \tag{12a}

Outside 0<=n<N this uses the specified finite interpolation (7), not an extension of missing Chebyshev coefficients by zero. For complex n the EXACT retained current is

a(n−2P)−Qn−Pa(n)−2PNqn−2P=(e−2πin−1)qn−2PN2[N−n+P(N+1)]. a(n-2P)-Q^{n-P}a(n)-\frac{2P}{N}q^{n-2P} =\frac{(e^{-2\pi i n}-1)q^{n-2P}}{N^2} [N-n+P(N+1)]. \tag{12b}

Thus the physical coefficient recurrence is affine, not an illicit homogeneous theta recurrence after complex-color deformation.

For P!=0, define g_n=q(n2/(2P))a_n, with the original log q. Equation (12a) says

g_(n-2P)-g_n=(2P/N)q^(n^2/(2P)-n).

Every physical coefficient packet is therefore a sum of at most 2|P| homogeneous quadratic chirps and explicit INCOMPLETE finite quadratic sums. For instance along n_j=n_0+2Pj in the actual window, j>=0,

anj=q−nj2/(2P)[qn02/(2P)an0−2PN∑t=1jqnt2/(2P)−nt]. a_{n_j}=q^{-n_j^2/(2P)} \left[q^{n_0^2/(2P)}a_{n_0} -\frac{2P}{N}\sum_{t=1}^j q^{n_t^2/(2P)-n_t}\right]. \tag{12c}

The anchors are explicit finite Gaussian data (4), bounded by 1+|P|. No anchor is a knot period. Equation (12c), or the exact Fresnel representation (8), supplies the endpoint function with its actual source rather than approximating D by a Gaussian value. Polynomially small errors in this endpoint function cannot simply be discarded against an exponentially smaller filled rate.

6. The actual circle difference cancels its current and folds EXACTLY

The current J in (8) is periodic in n with period1. It therefore cancels TERMWISE in the original circle-basis difference. Define the canonical ENTIRE extension

b_ext(j)=exp(pi i(j-1))[a(j-1)-a(j+1)],
K_P(h)=b_ext(N-h).

The physical circle coefficient for color j is beta_(j-1). For 1<=j<N it equals b_ext(j); for the last color,

βN−1=12bext(N). \beta_{N-1}=\tfrac12 b_{\mathrm{ext}}(N). \tag{12d}

The half is compulsory: the original aliases give a(N)=0 and a(N+1)=-a(N-1), while the missing actual coefficient a_(N+1) was extended by ZERO in the finite circle-basis conversion.

There is an exact ENTIRE Weyl symmetry, not only an equality on the color lattice:

KP(h)=KP(−h),h∈ℂ. \boxed{K_P(h)=K_P(-h),\qquad h\in\mathbb C.} \tag{12e}

To prove it without discarding a current, put y_k=x_k-pi i and use the finite Gaussian expression (7). Directly

KP(h)=2(−1)N−1N2∑khkehyk[(h+2Pyk/ℏ)sinh⁡yk+cosh⁡yk]. K_P(h)=\frac{2(-1)^{N-1}}{N^2}\sum_k h_k e^{hy_k} [(h+2Py_k/\hbar)\sinh y_k+\cosh y_k]. \tag{12f}

The k,d-k pair has y mapped to -y and h_k unchanged. Its sum is even in h. The odd simple y=0 term is even as well. Equivalently, the exact alias identities H(-n)=e^(2pi in)H(n), D(-n)=-e^(2pi in)D(n), together with antiperiodicity at n+N, give (12e), including its necessary exp(pi i(j-1)) factor.

For ANY even core exterior C_h=C_(-h), scalar or operator-valued, and any integer H with 0<=H<N, the literal physical-color sum therefore obeys

∑h=0HβN−h−1Ch=12∑h=−HHKP(h)Ch. \boxed{\sum_{h=0}^H\beta_{N-h-1}C_h =\frac12\sum_{h=-H}^H K_P(h)C_h.} \tag{12g}

This keeps the physical half weight at h=0. In the actual pretzel core, C_h is even on the physical grid by its finite Chebyshev relation. Its symmetric finite-factorial continuation keeps the pair F_(v-h)F_(v+h) and q(h2), and is even as well; a new one-sided exp(pi i h) continuation must not be substituted for it. Thus the fold applies before the original marked closure and with arbitrary spectator boxes. It iterates over several color ports. The remaining high bands/aliases and any cutoff at +/-H remain their actual source terms; (12g) makes no global tail assertion.

The current-inclusive Poisson formula becomes especially simple for this folded kernel. With c=ell+1 and b=Nc-h,

KP(h)=κ−Peπi(N−h−1)∑ℓsymmetric(−1)ℓc[IP,N(b−1)−IP,N(b+1)]. K_P(h)=\kappa^{-P}e^{\pi i(N-h-1)} \sum_\ell^{\mathrm{symmetric}}(-1)^\ell c [I_{P,N}(b-1)-I_{P,N}(b+1)]. \tag{12h}

There is NO omitted J in this equation: J(j-1)=J(j+1) cancels exactly for all complex j. The charge c=0 is thus truly absent from the full circle kernel, even off lattice. This stronger conclusion holds after the actual circle difference, not for an individual a(n) in section3.

Reflection (h,c)->(-h,-c) preserves each paired contribution in (12h). Indeed I(-b)=e^(2pi ib)I(b) by t->1-t; its factor e^(-2pi ih) cancels the reflected prefactor and the two odd signs cancel. Thus +/-charge folding keeps the EXACT endpoint Fresnel functions too. The h=0 endpoint is removed by a genuine whole-source identity, not by comparing two formal saddle heights.

7. An actual nonzero LOCAL Gaussian charge, and the remaining gate

Fix |P|>=2 and 0<=h/N<=delta<1/2, with physical n=N-1-h. For each c=+1 or -1, the stationary point of the labeled integral with ell=c-1 stays strictly inside [0,1], uniformly for large N. The exact Fresnel integral therefore has

IP,N(n+Nℓ)=eisgn⁡(P)π/42|P|Ne−πi(n+Nℓ)−πi(n+Nℓ)2/(2PN)+OP,δ(N−1). I_{P,N}(n+N\ell)= \frac{e^{i\,\operatorname{sgn}(P)\pi/4}}{\sqrt{2|P|N}} e^{-\pi i(n+N\ell)-\pi i(n+N\ell)^2/(2PN)} +O_{P,\delta}(N^{-1}). \tag{13}

This follows by the elementary full real Gaussian integral and one integration by parts on its two nonstationary omitted tails. It is uniform and NONZERO for sufficiently large N. The original normal charge factor c is nonzero on both channels. No unknown cycle coefficient occurs INSIDE this labeled real Gaussian integral. For P=0 or |P|=1, the exact formulas remain valid; uniform endpoint asymptotics are not substituted for (13).

For the circle difference itself the corresponding leading charge term is, with b=Nc-h and ell=c-1,

2iκ−Peisgn⁡(P)π/42|P|N(−1)(N+1)ℓcsin⁡πbPNexp⁡{−πi(b2+1)/(2PN)}. \frac{2i\kappa^{-P}e^{i\operatorname{sgn}(P)\pi/4}} {\sqrt{2|P|N}} (-1)^{(N+1)\ell}c\sin\frac{\pi b}{PN} \exp\{-\pi i(b^2+1)/(2PN)\}. \tag{13a}

This follows by subtracting the two actual Gaussians at b-1,b+1. Its c=+/-1 amplitudes are nonzero in a fixed sufficiently small real complete-color corridor for |P|>=2. Under (h,c)->(-h,-c), the sine and c both reverse and all remaining factors agree. Thus the two geometric-charge halves ADD at h=0; the apparent half-line endpoint is not an independent positive-height error. One must still retain exact endpoint corrections until their exterior pairing is estimated to the needed exponential accuracy.

This is a local source-channel result, not yet nonzero filled-knot pairing. The complete sum in (11) retains all charges; arithmetic Gauss cancellations can and do occur. A complex geometric color deformation must retain the full current-inclusive kernel before using the proved difference cancellation, all exterior/source data, and its endpoint transport. Neither (13) nor the formal relation (12) proves survival after that original exterior contraction.

The subsequent exact source Fourier estimate retains those endpoint currents and proves a uniform high-charge gap. Combined with the original analytic mixed source it now yields nonzero survival of the entire truncated cusp-color sum for independently large fixed signed fillings. That theorem still retains the complementary physical color region as an open whole-source gate; it does not infer its absence from the local Gaussian above.

8. The wrapped normal current is an explicit original source

The endpoint function also has a finite operator Ward identity. It identifies its forcing term without discarding it. Let f_P be the degree-<N Casimir polynomial for r_N^(2P), so that R(2P)=kappa(2P)f_P(Omega). Define a LINEAR map on Casimir polynomials by

(𝒯f)(w+w−1)=f(Qw+Q−1w−1)+f(Q−1w+Qw−1). (\mathcal T f)(w+w^{-1}) =f(Qw+Q^{-1}w^{-1})+f(Q^{-1}w+Qw^{-1}). \tag{14}

The symmetric Laurent expression is a polynomial: its constant Chebyshev coefficient is doubled and its nth coefficient is multiplied by Qn+Q(-n). Products on the following left side are evaluated in the FULL Casimir algebra, with reduction modulo P_N where necessary. Write rho=q-q^(-1). Then, for every integer P and every N>=2,

𝒯fP(Ω)−QP2T2P(Ω/2)fP(Ω)=2PNρΞ0(Ω)=2PN(−1)Nρpℓ. \boxed{\mathcal T f_P(\Omega) -Q^P\,2T_{2P}(\Omega/2)f_P(\Omega) =\frac{2PN}{\rho}\,\Xi_0(\Omega) =2PN(-1)^N\rho\,p\ell.} \tag{15}

Here T_(-n)=T_n and Xi_0 has value zero at every node, derivative one at c_0=q+q^(-1), and derivative zero at the other double nodes. The literal original cup and ordinary cap p,ell have precisely the normalizations used throughout this note.

To prove (15), put G(x)=kappa^(-P)exp(P(x-pi i)^2/hbar). Its exact shifts satisfy G(x+/-hbar)=Q^P exp(+/-2Px)G(x). The interpolating Laurent polynomial g(x)=f_P(ex+e(-x)) has the same value and x derivative as G at every original node x_k. Consequently both sides of (15) have zero node values. All their normal derivatives agree except when a shift crosses the original logarithmic window. At x_0 the derivative defect is +2PN, and at x_d it is -2PN; the shifted endpoint value is one in both cases. Dividing by dc/dx=rho and -rho, respectively, gives the SAME Casimir derivative 2PN/rho at the paired node c_0. At the odd simple node no extra normal condition is imposed. Full Hermite uniqueness proves the first equality, including even and composite levels.

For the second equality use the normalized original source observable, Xi_0=(-1)^N rho^2 p ell on the degree-d block. This identity extends to the entire physical pair: both sides are Hopf intertwiners, and the degree-d block generates the whole pair by the actual E/F maps, as proved in the full pair commutant theorem. There is no unrecorded projector onto that block.

Most usefully, the exact endpoint forcing becomes a scalar when placed in its ACTUAL odd-box turn. Indeed R(-1)p=kappa(-1)p and the genuine unscaled turn obeys mathscrR(p ell)=I. Therefore

ℛ[R−1{𝒯fP−QP2T2P(Ω/2)fP}]=2PN(−1)Nρκ−1I. \boxed{\mathscr R\!\left[ R^{-1}\{\mathcal T f_P-Q^P 2T_{2P}(\Omega/2)f_P\}\right] =2PN(-1)^N\rho\,\kappa^{-1}I.} \tag{16}

Its scalar modulus is at most 4pi|P|, uniformly in N. This is an all-input original-source reduction, not just a formal critical value statement. It can be inserted with arbitrary spectators in this typed slot. In a composed source the displayed current removes that box and leaves its explicitly known scalar factor.

Equations (15)–(16) do not bound the inverse Ward transport, nor identify a general Fresnel endpoint remainder with this one term. The map mathcalT is the specified linear Casimir q-shift, not a physical gate that may be relocated through other boxes. A global filled-knot estimate must still control the resulting actual source and any iteration of this identity.
