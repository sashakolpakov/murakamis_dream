Independent audit of the exponential compact-current detour

Audit, 27 October 2026. The revised proof in compact_exponential_cech_current_detour.md passes its whole-form, relative-source and normal-pole checks. The algebraic-to-holomorphic domain qualification added during this audit is necessary.

The exponential fibre map has the correct south expression, fixes the base, and is holomorphic on the whole compact source for the stated nonresonant parameters. The affine-fibre difference transforms linearly, so BC,λ=−[∫aaλfC(v,b)db]dv B_{C,\lambda}= -\left[\int_a^{a_\lambda}f_C(v,b)\,db\right]dv is global and differentiates to Φλ*(fCdv∧da)−fCdv∧da\Phi_\lambda^*(f_C\,dv\wedge da)-f_C\,dv\wedge da. The sign follows from d(−Fdv)=Fadv∧dad(-F\,dv)=F_a\,dv\wedge da. The identity matrix formula and normalized compact trace agree.

The interpolation a+τ(aλ−a)a+\tau(a_\lambda-a) is global in the affine torsor, fixes the deleted base fibre, and gives the printed Cartan operator on both XX and its affine complement. In the support cone with differential D(α,β)=(dα,α|−dβ)D(\alpha,\beta)=(d\alpha,\alpha|-d\beta), Φλ*(αC,βC)−(αC,βC)=D(BC,λ,−KλβC) \Phi_\lambda^*(\alpha_C,\beta_C)-(\alpha_C,\beta_C) =D(B_{C,\lambda},-K_\lambda\beta_C) has the correct second-component sign: it is BC,λ+dKλβCB_{C,\lambda}+dK_\lambda\beta_C. This retains the explicit polar-fibre primitive and source residue.

Near resonance, aλ=ηphys/k+ae−cT+O(k)a_\lambda=\eta_{\mathrm{phys}}/k+a e^{-cT}+O(k) and the printed Jacobian expansion follow from e−cλT=e−cT(1−k)Te^{-c\lambda T}=e^{-cT}(1-k)^T. Since fCf_C has degree at most nn in aa, its antiderivative and the relative homotopy have pole order at most n+1=Nn+1=N. The chosen affine primitive has the same degree bound. Comparing Laurent coefficients proves that every negative coefficient is relatively exact and the finite part retains the original source class. This assumes the matrix coefficients are fixed or regular in this auxiliary normal, as the note states. Meridian-coupled coefficient poles require their additional chain-rule and divisor contributions.

The important domain repair is explicit in the revised note: Φλ\Phi_\lambda is holomorphic exponential, so it maps the algebraic support complex into a holomorphic or specified whole exponential-period complex. It is not an algebraic endomorphism of the original complex. Its normal coefficients and finite-part forms can be nonalgebraic. Consequently this identity does not invoke the bounded algebraic cyclotomic reconstruction theorem.

The note correctly also keeps the two clocks distinct. The physical clock at the pulled-back compact point is ecvaλe^{cva_\lambda}, while the meridian detour clock is hλ/uλh_\lambda/u_\lambda. Their equality, a star-product intertwiner, and actual quantum dressing are not proved by the current homotopy. Nor does it identify the Gaussian coefficient map or select a nonzero full geometric period.
