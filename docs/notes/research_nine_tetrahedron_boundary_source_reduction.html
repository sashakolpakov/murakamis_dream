The literal nine-tetrahedron source and its paired-face quotient

Private written proof, 17 November 2026. This records the complete incidence, a charge lift, and an exact six-variable reduction of the nine-cell crossing source specified in [K, Section 4]. In the explicit charge lift, for every crossing sector, the actual boundary tensor lies in the paired-face adapter image proved in [A]. Its inverse loses no boundary data. The recovered four-index tensor is not yet identified with Kashaev’s crossing weight. Neither the printed identity (4.25) nor a closed contraction of it is a premise. An ordered-decoration limit also proves a normalization obstruction: with the explicit 1994 scalars used here, one actual boundary entry has leading coefficient qq, whereas the unit-coefficient printed crossing expression has N2qN^2q. This does not evaluate the recovered array globally.
