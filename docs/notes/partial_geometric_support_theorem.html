A geometric A-factor for an actual partially summed quantum state

Research note, 27 October 2026. This proves a bulk geometric-support theorem for the original finite coefficient model. Leave two actual integer flow coordinates unsummed, and eliminate all remaining flow coordinates. The resulting finite family has a specifically constructed nonzero all-level meridian annihilator whose first nonzero classical symbol contains the marked geometric A-factor. The construction does not append a known classical factor.

This partial family retains its two framing indices. Pairing them with the selected full finite source is a further operation. The theorem does not assert survival under that operation, a statement about the minimal scalar annihilator, or a volume limit.

1. The actual partial family

Use a knot braid with b strands and m crossings, the balanced factorial boundary frame, and the original normalized cut average. Set

q_N=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, x=-M/q, Y=M^2.

All crossings, the original pivot, the full zero-framing correction, the cut factor 1/N, lift characters and base-only compact factors are retained. If the full ADO state is wanted, its complete original marked factor d_N(M) remains an external line; it is not absorbed into a formal unit or replaced by its value at completeness.

Closing strands 1,...,b-1 gives a cofactor of the knot permutation matrix minus identity, with determinant +1 or -1. Solve these closures integrally. The independent actual flow coordinates are

k=(a_0,k_1,...,k_m),       d=m+1.

The remaining closure follows from total-index conservation. The summand is the actual product of the finite crossing and boundary coefficients, zero outside its finite integral support.

On the regular geometric critical chart the Hessian has a two-dimensional radical equal to the tangent space of the framing torsor. This is the Morse–Bott statement for the actual coefficient phase proved in the normal-period note. Select two members chi_1,chi_2 of the displayed actual coordinate list whose differentials are independent on this radical. Such a pair exists because the radical is a two-dimensional subspace of the coordinate tangent space. No integral change of variables is made.

Keep chi=(a,b) external and sum only over the remaining r=d-2=m-1 coordinates j. The actual partial array is

F_N^chi(M;a,b)=sum_(j in Z^r) I_N(q_N,-M/q_N;a,b,j).

The sum is finite and includes its exact boundary. Its final contraction over the two external indices is the original reduced scalar state. The auxiliary off-root lift uses the same normalized cut trace and the same partial array, with generic q; topological invariance of that off-root lift is not a premise.

For coefficient bookkeeping put

z=q^N, Ahat=q^a, Bhat=q^b, t_i=q^(j_i).

The physical phase coordinates are A=Ahat^2=Q^a and B=Bhat^2=Q^b. Keeping Ahat,Bhat avoids suppressing the genuine square-root characters in a meridian half-step. Alternatively one may work on their stated finite etale cover. Let T send M to qM (equivalently x to qx) while holding q,Ahat,Bhat fixed.

2. A global partial-array certificate

The proof in the finite-array annihilator note extends with the two external multiplication variables. Its summand ratios are proper and aligned rational functions in

q,x,z,Ahat,Bhat,t_1,...,t_r.

For 0<=e_x,e_z,e_A,e_B,e_T,beta_i<=R, consider

x^e_x z^e_z Ahat^e_A Bhat^e_B T^e_T S^beta I,

where S_i shifts j_i by one and never shifts a,b. These are (R+1)^(r+5) independent formal Ore monomials.

Their image span has dimension O(R^(r+4)) over Q(q). In the interior, clear the rational shift ratios by one common denominator whose Laurent degrees are O(R) in the r+4 multiplication variables x,z,Ahat,Bhat,t. For the boundary proof, stratify every support inequality according to whether it is far inside, far outside, or has one specified integer value in an interval of length O(R). If the near-boundary forms have rank c on (N,a,b,j), there are O(R^c) consistent sets of their labels. On each resulting stratum, the associated c binomial relations leave O(R^(r+4-c)) Laurent monomials after the fixed Smith reductions. The total contribution is again O(R^(r+4)). Supported shifts have nonzero common denominators on the stratum, exactly as in the original proof. Empty and lower-dimensional supports are included.

Finite linear algebra therefore constructs a nonzero certificate

P(x,z,Ahat,Bhat,T,S) I=0                       (2.1)

on the whole zero-extended array, with no multiplication by any internal t_i. Separate summand components, if present, can be retained in a direct sum, giving a common certificate.

The full flow support has a nonempty open cone of strict inequalities: take all transfers small and positive compared with N and the free cut position near N/2. Every position is then N/2+O(epsilon*N). This is unchanged by choosing a subset of the coordinates as external. Consequently (2.1), divided by a nonzero interior reference term, is a rational identity in all independent multiplication variables. The precise lattice-cone density proof is in the sourced-telescoping audit, Section 2. This step is needed: a relation only on isolated discrete frame values would not suffice for the formal geometric argument.

3. Root specialization and transverse moment repair

Clear fixed coefficient denominators and divide the largest common central power (z+1)^s from the whole certificate, obtaining P0. For each fixed generic N, q^N+1 is a nonzero rational function of q, so the global array identity survives this division. The finite summands are regular at q_N; their crossing factorial denominators have indices below N. Thus

P1(x,Ahat,Bhat,T,S)=P0(x,-1,Ahat,Bhat,T,S)      (3.1)

is nonzero and annihilates the actual root partial arrays for all but finitely many levels. The same specialization is valid for the independent-variable interior contiguity identity.

Expand in the commuting transverse shifts only:

P1=sum_alpha D_alpha(x,Ahat,Bhat,T)(S-1)^alpha.

Let h0 be the least total degree with a nonzero coefficient, and choose any nonzero D_alpha of that degree. Give the finite internal sum independent weights exp(v dot j). Exact translation of the whole finite zero-extended sum sends S_i to exp(-v_i). At degree v^alpha, every positive moment multiplies a coefficient of degree below h0, and hence is zero. Therefore

D_alpha(q_N,-M/q_N,q_N^a,q_N^b,T)
                      F_N^chi(M;a,b)=0.       (3.2)

This is a genuine nonzero all-level operator for the actual partial family. All external labels and all finite boundary strata are kept.

4. The same certificate acts on the exact formal transverse period

Write H=Log Q=2 Log q. In logarithmic physical flow coordinates, the actual interior coefficient has a formal realization

I_form=C_beta(H) exp(S/H) a(u,H),

with the original characters, a(u,H)=a_0(u)(1+O(H)), and a_0 a unit on the chosen geometric chart. The retained base line C_beta(H) includes the cut average and the fixed compact factors. It is not evaluated at an individual root.

This formal realization has the exact rational contiguity ratios, not merely their leading symbols. One construction uses the original monomials and the compact-product series, for variable arguments different from 0,1,

log P_Q(z)=Li_2(z)/H +(1/2)log(1-z)
  +sum_(n>=1) B_(2n) H^(2n-1)/(2n)! Li_(2-2n)(z).

Its formal functional equation is P_Q(Qz)=P_Q(z)/(1-z). This follows either coefficientwise from the Bernoulli generating function or by expanding the defining geometric-series logarithm. Thus its ratios agree to all orders with cancellation of the original finite factors. Signs and logarithm sheets are the ones fixed by the actual critical-correspondence calculation. The fixed singular argument P_Q(Q) stays in the base-only line. Pivotal monomials and the framing correction have their literal exact shift ratios as well.

Now fix the two external coordinates chi. The remaining coordinate space complements the radical of the symmetric Hessian. The restricted r by r Hessian is therefore nondegenerate. The critical point of S in the remaining variables is unique in the chosen formal germ. The full geometric critical surface is etale over chi, so its unique point at each nearby external chi supplies a solution of these transverse equations. By uniqueness this is the same partial critical germ, and all of the full critical equations hold there. Its unweighted Gaussian contraction is nonzero, with leading amplitude

a_0/sqrt(det(-H_perp)).                       (4.1)

Introduce independent weights by replacing the phase with

S_v(u)=S(u)+sum_(i=1)^r v_i u_i.

The formal implicit-function theorem gives a unique critical point u(v) with u(0)=u_*, because H_perp is invertible. Formal stationary contraction there defines Z_chi(v). It is a formal power series in v whose coefficients lie in the original WKB line times Laurent series in H. Its value at zero is nonzero. The action difference divided by H can produce increasingly negative finite H orders as the v degree grows; this is allowed.

Formal stationary contraction is invariant under a translation of an internal integration coordinate. Explicitly, for the unit shift u_i -> u_i+H, substituting y_i=u_i+H in the formal Gaussian gives

G_v(S_i f)=exp(-v_i) G_v(f).                  (4.2)

This is an identity of formal stationary expansions at their corresponding critical points, with an elementary termwise proof. For one shifted coordinate its weighted phase is

S(u+H e_i)+v dot u = S_v(u+H e_i)-H v_i.

Its critical point is u(v)-H e_i. Centering at that point gives exactly the same Hessian, higher vertices, amplitude Taylor series and coordinate volume as centering S_v at u(v). Only the constant phase changes, by -H v_i. Thus every Wick coefficient is multiplied by exp(-v_i). No integration-by-parts theorem is a premise of this argument. No finite-array endpoint or framing endpoint is being discarded here: this is a formal contraction of precisely the r nondegenerate transverse variables. Both framing coordinates remain external throughout.

The same formal contraction commutes with the meridian step T. The shifted critical point is the unique nearby one with the same external Ahat,Bhat; the formal construction and its retained half-density are natural under that base shift. Multiplication by external rational coefficients commutes as well.

Apply this contraction to the exact interior identity (3.1). By (4.2),

P1(x,Ahat,Bhat,T,exp(-v)) Z_chi(v)=0.

The same least-degree moment coefficient used for the finite partial sum therefore gives

D_alpha Z_chi(0)=0.                          (4.3)

Unlike full framed integration, this implication has no missing relative-source covariance assumption. Nondegeneracy of the transverse block and leaving both framing coordinates external provide it directly.

The subsequent full Green-certificate calculation also transports the actual fully summed scalar operator to this transverse block. Its image includes two explicitly computed framing boundary currents. Their final endpoint and logarithmic-carry functional is retained; neither the formal residual Ward ideal nor the source torsor identities currently prove its cancellation. Thus the distinction between the present partial theorem and the full scalar claim is now an explicit source defect, not an omitted ordinary Gaussian variable.

5. The classical symbol has the geometric factor

Divide D_alpha by its common scalar (q-1) valuation and clear remaining rational denominators. Call the resulting nonzero operator Dhat. Scalar division is valid both in the actual root identities (for all large N) and in the formal WKB line. Let

d_cl(M,Ahat,Bhat,L)

be its first nonzero classical symbol in the stated meridian and lift gauge. The exact preferred-longitude calculation for the actual phase gives

leading [T Z_chi(0)/Z_chi(0)]=L_geom(M).

Indeed the full critical gradient vanishes on the framing torsor, so the critical action has zero derivative in both frame directions. Its meridian derivative at fixed chi is therefore the same marked longitude derivative as on the unframed geometric branch. The transverse amplitude is a unit and does not alter this leading ratio. Equation (4.3) now gives

d_cl(M,Ahat,Bhat,L_geom(M))=0.                (5.1)

The two selected flow coordinates are etale coordinates on the framing torsor. Consequently their values range over a dense open part of a two-dimensional frame space over the geometric branch. Equation (5.1) is therefore a relation over the purely transcendental frame coefficient field, not only at one frame.

Let A_geo(M,L) denote the irreducible rational polynomial defining the Zariski closure of the marked geometric longitude branch, in the same sign, inverse and preferred-longitude convention. Its irreducibility is preserved by the independent transcendental frame variables. Clearing peripheral denominators in (5.1) proves

A_geo(M,L) divides d_cl(M,Ahat,Bhat,L)

over Q(Ahat,Bhat), up to the recorded monomial and lift gauges. This is the claimed geometric-support theorem. In particular the constructed operator has positive meridian-shift order; a nonzero order-zero rational multiplier cannot annihilate this nonzero formal period on a dense open chart.

The whole construction of Dhat uses actual finite coefficients, global boundary linear algebra, central specialization, and transverse moment extraction. It never multiplies an operator by an independently supplied A_geo.

The actual certified module has a formal geometric line quotient

There is a corresponding precise formal torsor statement. Let K_geo be the algebraic function field of the marked geometric gluing branch and its two retained framing coordinates, with the recorded half-density and sign covers. Put h=Log q, and let Z_geo^chi be the nonzero transverse block just constructed. On this field the shift is the formal automorphism sigma=exp(h delta), where delta=M partial_M fixes both framing coordinates and extends uniquely to the algebraic and half-density covers in characteristic zero. Its shift ratio

b_geo=T(Z_geo^chi)/Z_geo^chi
      in K_geo[[h]]^*, b_geo|_(h=0)=L_geom

has algebraic coefficients. The meridian-independent base-only line cancels in this ratio. The exponent difference has leading Log L, and its higher derivatives are algebraic after exponentiating that leading logarithm; the transverse amplitudes and all their meridian derivatives are algebraic as proved in the normal-period calculation.

Right division in the meridian Ore ring over K_geo((h)) gives

Dhat=Q_geo (T-b_geo)+r_geo.

Applying this to Z_geo^chi gives r_geo Z_geo^chi=0. This period is nonzero in its formal exponential line, so r_geo=0. Thus T-b_geo is a right factor of an operator constructed from actual partial arrays. Equivalently its cyclic module has a formal rank-one quotient on the marked geometric branch. It is not asserted to be a rational generic factor or a quotient of the minimal full scalar operator.

The action of this line is the marked actual crossing regulator action: in the h convention its complete imaginary value is Vol/2, with the original peripheral and base-only lines retained. The existing formal Gaussian comparison transports it to the positive triangulation’s marked regulator line by an algebraic formal unit. No convergence, rootwise value of that formal unit, or nonzero coefficient in the selected scalar period follows from this factor.

In fact every nonzero moment operator obtained from a whole partial-array certificate by this procedure annihilates the same formal block. Their generated left ideal in the localized meridian Ore ring over Q(q,M,Ahat,Bhat) is nonzero. Every element annihilates both the actual partial arrays and the formal block. Its unique monic least-order generator consequently also has a geometric classical factor after taking its first nonzero (q-1) symbol. This defines a minimal certified partial operator; it is not asserted to equal the minimal annihilator of one selected scalar solution. Clear its coefficient denominators in a polynomial Ore combination before evaluating discrete frame labels; this retains identities also at labels where the localized formula itself has a pole.

The full literature normalization is transported exactly, if desired. For the full partial array N_N^chi=d_N(M)F_N^chi, retain

d_N(M)=i^(N+3)M^(N-1)/(1+M^2+...+M^(2N-2)),
g_N(q,M)=d_N(qM)/d_N(M)
   =q^(N-1)(q^2 M^2-1)/(M^2-1)
                     * (M^(2N)-1)/(q^(2N)M^(2N)-1).

At the ACTUAL root q^N=-1 its meromorphic meridian-germ restriction is

g_bar(q,M)=-q^(-1)(q^2 M^2-1)/(M^2-1).

The constructed full ROOT operator is the Ore gauge

Dhat_full=Dhat|_(T -> g_bar^(-1) T).

This notation means ordered Ore substitution, not commutative substitution of powers. Its all-level identity is a meromorphic whole MERIDIAN-germ identity ON THE ROOT FIBERS, with the complete displayed d_N retained. For a whole quantum-normal source use g_N, or its actual independent powers z=qN,w=M(2N), before specializing. The expression g_bar is only its root restriction; their q-normal derivatives are not identified. Equivalently,

g_N=q^(-1)z (q^2 M^2-1)/(M^2-1)
                                    * (w-1)/(z^2 w-1).

Here the actual meridian step sends w to z^2 w. It is not a constant dual-nome coordinate off the root. Also g_N has limit +1 at q=1 with N FIXED, whereas the root-character-specialized g_bar below has limit -1. These are different classical regimes; the root-symbol statement is not a claim that their fixed-N whole quantum-normal symbols agree.

The independent peripheral-shift audit checks this full ratio, the ordered gauge direction and its retained-character normal discrepancy explicitly.

At generic classical M, g_bar->-1, so its geometric factor is A_geo(M,-L_full) in the full shift convention. The regular completion at M=1 uses the original source lattice and complete normalization germ; the separate pole of the localized g_bar is not evaluated there.

6. Scope of the result

The proof applies to every knot for which the audited regular geometric critical chart and Hessian-radical statement hold. In particular its braid complexity is unrestricted: it is not confined to two-bridge, twist, alternating, or a fixed bridge number. The geometric input and the retained SL2 sign cover are exactly those of the actual normal-period theorem; no literature asymptotic or creative-telescoping theorem is a premise.

The finite partial state and its operator depend on the braid and the selected two flow coordinates. They have been constructed from the original coefficient model; no topological invariance of this two-index extension is asserted. Summing its remaining two indices with the original selected source requires further elimination. The geometric factor can be lost in that pushforward. The theorem does not identify the full scalar minimal quantum-A operator, the selected global geometric cycle, Kashaev’s volume growth, or the value of any individually normalized compact product at a root.

7. A source-preserving Fourier partial family has the same geometric factor

There is now a second actual partial family with a different and useful framing polarization. It requires the FULL four-port tensor, not only its double diagonal. Use the original first-cut rank-one source and Fourier-transform the second identity wire as in the whole polarization construction. At a primitive root put V_jb=Q^(jb), with V(-1)_bj=Q(-bj)/N, and T^V=(I tensor V)T(I tensor V^(-1)). Define the RAW partial family by

Ftilde_N(M;a,j)=sum_c (T^V)_(c,j;a,j)
   =(1/N)sum_(b,nu,u) Q^(j nu) W_N(M;a,b,nu,u). (7.1)

Here W_N is the original raw open coefficient on its integral flow coset, T_(a-nu,b+nu;a,b)=sum_u W_N, and u has m-1 internal chord coordinates. All four actual port bounds and every original phase/pivot/peripheral line remain in W_N. The Fourier factor 1/N in (7.1) is not the first-cut source normalization. The original selected scalar is exactly

F_N(M)=(1/N)sum_(a,j) Ftilde_N(M;a,j).         (7.2)

Indeed the sum over j kills every nonzero offset nu in its actual range |nu|<N and restores the identity second closure. It does not replace the physical b sum by evaluation of a momentum function. Equivalently, partial trace on that wire is invariant under the whole invertible basis change. If its first cut were also made diagonal BEFORE this operation, charge conservation would force nu=0, and the resulting array would be independent of j. The sum over c in (7.1) is essential.

The following assertions hold under the regular geometric input of this note.

Fourier partial support theorem. There is a nonzero all-level meridian operator constructed from (7.1) by finite coefficient linear algebra and moment repair, whose first nonzero classical symbol contains the same marked geometric A-factor. On a dense geometric framing chart its nonzero normal block is parametrized by the original cut position and the second physical wave momentum. At the discrete-faithful complete point these are birational framing coordinates; no new quadratic framing cover is required. This does not assert a geometric factor after the final selected sum (7.2).

Here is the certificate and specialization proof, including the distinction between root data and the whole q-normal source.

First keep a,j external and b,nu,u internal. Their number is r_F=m+1. Removing both physical cuts leaves the same connected graph, so the integral tree particular flow makes W_N proper with linear finite support, exactly as in the two-cut proof. The added monomial Q^(j nu) is also proper: shifting nu multiplies it by the external character Q^j, and shifting any remaining flow has its stated rational coefficient. There is a nonempty strict cone: take all original transfers small and positive, the original positions near N/2, and the open offset nu in a sufficiently small two-sided interval about zero. The Fourier index j may independently range through a strict subinterval of its original head.

The formula on the right of (7.1), with the same monomial Fourier kernel at generic q, is a convenient AUXILIARY whole coefficient lift. At every root it is the literal actual family. Its common factor 1/N can be kept as a source line or removed from a homogeneous coefficient certificate, since internal and meridian shifts fix it. The finite support-stratum count of Section 2 applies with r replaced by r_F and the second external character replaced by q^j, retaining its square-root mark when required. Thus a nonzero whole certificate with no internal multiplication characters exists. Strip its largest common q^N+1 power from the ENTIRE certificate before root evaluation. The least internal-shift moment then gives a nonzero meridian operator annihilating the actual root arrays (7.1), by the exact same zero-extended summation identity as Sections 2–3.

This auxiliary lift is not identified with the actual generic-q inverse Fourier matrix. The source-preserving whole basis change uses the Vandermonde V(Q) and its Lagrange inverse, with their explicit connection and endpoint carries. Its inverse equals Q^(-bj)/N at the root but generally has different normal jets. Thus the argument proves an operator identity for the literal ALL-LEVEL ROOT partial family; it does not discard the source normal terms in the whole polarization theorem. All M-dependent identities at a fixed root still agree, since the two Fourier bases are independent of M.

The geometric normal block comes from the macroscopic open coefficient phase, rather than extrapolating a bounded-band Taylor formula to offsets of order N. Put t=H nu, v=Hj. Its phase is

Phi=S_open(a,b,t,u)+v t.                      (7.3)

On the original geometric critical manifold t=0, the old internal equations hold and partial_t S_open=Log p_e, in the actual balanced boundary frame. Therefore (7.3) is stationary precisely on the selected geometric germ with

v=-Log p_e,   eta=exp(v)=p_e^(-1).

The original cut position together with p_e is etale on a nonempty geometric open chart. The bordered-Hessian proof in the Fourier normal-block theorem then gives a nondegenerate (m+1)-dimensional internal Hessian. Its mixed entry along the old fixed-cut framing tangent is the nonzero derivative of Log p_e. The added Fourier pair has its literal factor 1/N and supplies no new exponential action. The geometric critical value is still S_geo, since t=0.

The auxiliary coefficient realization has the same exact proper ratios and full marked formal phase (7.3). It consequently has a nonzero formal Gaussian block. Translate its internal variables in the whole certificate and extract the least weighted moment as in Section 4. The operator obtained from the finite root arrays annihilates that SAME normal block. Section 5 now applies: its first nonzero classical symbol vanishes at the marked geometric longitude and has the geometric A-factor. The formal Ore right-factor statement follows by the same division argument.

The original peripheral dimension line is transported using the ordered Ore gauge already displayed above. Its separate whole normal adapter is not replaced by a root norm in this proof. Nor is the sum over j interpreted as the old cap point eta=0: the correct new source is the finite j sum in (7.2), with every tail/carry retained on inserted currents. Survival and the volume norm after that last sourced pushforward remain unproved.

8. The original root period has a one-exterior-index Fourier presentation

One of the two exterior sums can be removed EXACTLY, without replacing the first-cut source by a double diagonal. The fixed-column/Ward theorem proves that the fully reclosed one-cut word C_N(M) is scalar on the actual root family. This follows directly from its true K,F intertwining: K has distinct eigenvalues and F connects all successive head basis vectors with nonzero coefficients. Hence every first column sum is the same selected scalar. In the RAW Fourier presentation above,

sum_j Ftilde_N(M;a,j)=sum_c(C_N(M))_(c,a)=F_N(M)
                   for EVERY a in [0,N-1].                (8.1)

The full peripheral factor, if present, multiplies both sides as its original line. Equation (8.1) keeps the sum over c in (7.1). Replacing it by c=a before Fourier polarization would destroy the momentum coordinate and is a different operation.

This statement also has a controlled WHOLE source defect. The whole actual balanced K,F Ward contraction gives

h delta C=C-I Tr(C)/N,
delta C=((Ad K-I)C, F C-K^(-1)C K F).

If lambda_a(C)=sum_c C_(c,a) and Phi is the original rank-one average, then (lambda_a-Phi)(I)=0 and therefore

lambda_a-Phi=(lambda_a-Phi) h delta.            (8.2)

Its primitive and the original source adapters are explicit in the fixed-column theorem. Thus (8.1) is not extended to an arbitrary off-root lift by setting its Ward defects to zero. The actual whole normal derivatives act on every term of (8.2).

There is a uniform way to fix a good interior sequence of first input indices while retaining the geometric normal block. The bad first-position fibers of its nonempty algebraic open chart form a FINITE set: after writing its finitely many shape/Jacobian units as rational functions of the first position and momentum, an entire fiber can fail only at a common zero of their nonzero coefficient functions. Also exclude the finitely many first half-phases where the already constructed operator’s nonzero leading symbol becomes identically zero in M, the second exterior character and L. Clearing the specified denominators first makes this an ordinary polynomial coefficient test.

Choose a rational theta=k/d in (0,1) such that

A_*=exp(2pi*i*k/d), alpha_*=exp(pi*i*k/d),

avoid these finite bad sets, including A=1. Such a choice exists because these algebraic phases are infinite. Put

a_N=floor(kN/d), r_N=kN modulo d.

At the original root its physical and half clocks are EXACTLY

A_N=A_* q_N^(-2r_N/d),
Ahat_N=alpha_* q_N^(-r_N/d).                  (8.3)

These indices are in the strict interior for all sufficiently large N. For each fixed residue class r_N=r the right side is one fixed algebraic q germ on a marked d-th-root cover, and it tends to the chosen good geometric framing phase. The same nondegenerate normal block therefore persists there, with its original geometric action.

Substitute Ahat=alpha_* q^(-r/d) in the operator of Section 7, after its stated coefficient localization. This is an Ore homomorphism because the meridian shift fixes q and both exterior frame characters. By the choice of alpha_* its first nonzero classical symbol stays nonzero and still has the SAME geometric A-factor. On N residue class r the specialized operator annihilates the actual one-index partial family

G_(N,r)(M;j)=Ftilde_N(M;a_N,j),
F_N(M)=sum_j G_(N,r)(M;j).                    (8.4)

Its formal geometric line is now over the marked geometric representation field and ONE momentum coordinate. No restriction to a knot family or bridge number has been introduced. The values G_(N,r) still contain the original bulk finite contractions; this is one exterior source index, not an asserted closed single-product formula for the knot invariant.

For a WHOLE quantum-normal source calculation, (8.3) must not freeze the underlying power coordinates. Before root restriction, the actual fixed-N clocks are

A_N=(Q^N)^(k/d) Q^(-r/d),
Ahat_N=(q^N)^(k/d) q^(-r/d),                  (8.5)

with the logarithmic/finite-cover marks Log(Q^N)=N Log Q and Log(q^N)=N Log q. Their root values give (8.3), but their normal derivatives retain the N terms. The operators in (8.4) are certified ALL-LEVEL ROOT operators, as in Section 7; no whole q-normal identification is inferred from (8.3).

If one wants a single operator across these finitely many residue classes, take a common LEFT multiple of their nonzero specialized operators in the localized meridian Ore ring and then clear the original denominators. A naive ordered product is not justified. The per-residue statement (8.4) already suffices for the present source reduction. It still does not prove that the final one-index sum retains its geometric block with the regulator norm. That is the remaining selected-period theorem.

The actual Fourier current theorem now provides a rigorous scope test for this last step. The literal second-port Cartan current has zero whole second partial trace, yet on the good-A chart its formal geometric block is a nonzero derivative of the block above with the SAME action. Its own partial certificate still has a geometric classical factor, while its selected scalar is identically zero. Thus a geometric partial factor is not a certificate of survival under the last source. The final theorem must control the actual class in the cyclic source/current quotient, including the polar and normal data.
