A uniform representation-volume gap along centered long cone paths

Research note, 18 November 2026.

For a fixed complete hyperbolic core, sufficiently long primitive filling equations remain in one complete-holonomy chart throughout the cone-angle cube. Near-maximal representation rigidity then gives a uniform volume gap to every other representation satisfying the same centered equations. The comparison is along the whole path, rather than only at its endpoints.

The conclusion concerns marked representations and their volumes. It neither compares different logarithmic lifts of the same representation nor transports the original quantum cycle.

1. Complete proof sources and the comparison data

The only proof sources are:

  • N, A uniform volume gap away from geometric holonomy, complete text. Its Theorem 1 and Corollary 2 give a fixed positive volume deficit outside any conjugacy-invariant neighborhood of the oriented complete holonomy. Its Sections 5–6 explain why every nearby peripheral decoration lies in the supplied positive gluing chart.
  • F, A finite positive certificate supplies every sufficiently long filling branch, complete text. It supplies the actual centered peripheral germ, the uniform contraction for all primitive slope directions, and the absolutely normalized local volume expansion.

Let MM be one fixed oriented complete finite-volume hyperbolic three-manifold, with cc torus cusps and volume V>0V>0. Supply the actual positive complete marked triangulation required by these sources. In an application, MM may already be a fixed hyperbolic short partial filling. Its primitive cusp paths and every earlier filling are fixed.

On cusp ii, choose the positively oriented primitive basis (μi,λi)(\mu_i,\lambda_i). In the decorated complete-holonomy chart write Ui,ViU_i,V_i for the centered logarithms of the projective similarity multipliers. Thus these are twice eigenvalue logarithms; no central SL2\mathrm{SL}_2 sign is inserted in them. Source F proves Vi(U)=Uiτi(U),ℑτi(0)>0.(1) V_i(U)=U_i\tau_i(U),\qquad \Im\tau_i(0)>0. \qquad\text{(1)} All these are the actual peripheral words of MM.

Choose a subset II of cusps and primitive slopes (ai,bi)∈ℤ2(a_i,b_i)\in\mathbb Z^2, i∈Ii\in I. Set ℓi=(ai2+bi2)1/2,ℓmin=mini∈Iℓi. \ell_i=(a_i^2+b_i^2)^{1/2},\qquad \ell_{\min}=\min_{i\in I}\ell_i. For the empty subset take ℓmin=+∞\ell_{\min}=+\infty; its branch is the complete representation and the same proof below gives the gap in N. Choose signs ϵi∈{1,−1}\epsilon_i\in\{1,-1\} and real charges λ=(λi)i∈I∈[0,1]I\lambda=(\lambda_i)_{i\in I}\in[0,1]^I. The prescribed equations are Uj=0(j∉I),aiUi+biVi=2πiϵiλi(i∈I).(2) U_j=0\quad(j\notin I),\qquad a_iU_i+b_iV_i=2\pi i\epsilon_i\lambda_i\quad(i\in I). \qquad\text{(2)} On the constructed branch the first condition keeps every unfilled physical main cusp complete. At a coordinate λi=0\lambda_i=0, equation (1) will also make that selected cusp parabolic on the constructed branch.

Here is a precise convention for competitors. Each peripheral group has a retained common fixed flag, and is parabolic, trivial, or fixes both endpoints of an axis individually, as in N. On the selected cusps its multipliers lie in a fixed sufficiently small neighborhood of one; take their single-valued centered logarithms there and impose (2). On all other cusps impose the stated boundary-parabolic condition. These conditions describe actual decorated representations, modulo simultaneous conjugation. They do not allow an arbitrary deck translate of a centered logarithm to be substituted into (2).

Only competitors near complete holonomy need these small-multiplier conditions in the proof: all representations outside the fixed neighborhood already satisfy the stronger volume deficit supplied by N.

2. The uniform centered branch and its volume

There are constants L0,C>0L_0,C>0, depending on the fixed marked certificate, such that, whenever ℓmin>L0\ell_{\min}>L_0, equation (2) has one solution in a fixed centered chart for every λ∈[0,1]I\lambda\in[0,1]^I. Denote its representation by ρg(λ)\rho_{\mathrm g}(\lambda).

To see the uniformity directly, take the polydisk from F on which ℑτi≥m>0,|τi|≤T,∑j|∂jτi|≤L. \Im\tau_i\ge m>0,\qquad |\tau_i|\le T,\qquad \sum_j|\partial_j\tau_i|\le L. With c0=m/1+T2c_0=m/\sqrt{1+T^2}, the real two-column estimate in F gives |ai+biτi(U)|≥c0ℓi.(3) |a_i+b_i\tau_i(U)|\ge c_0\ell_i. \qquad\text{(3)} Keep unfilled coordinates zero and use the fixed-point map 𝒯λ,i(U)=2πiϵiλiai+biτi(U).(4) \mathcal T_{\lambda,i}(U)= \frac{2\pi i\epsilon_i\lambda_i}{a_i+b_i\tau_i(U)}. \qquad\text{(4)} Since 0≤λi≤10\le\lambda_i\le1, exactly the estimates of F give ∥𝒯λ(U)∥∞≤2πc0ℓmin,∥D𝒯λ(U)∥≤2πLc02ℓmin.(5) \|\mathcal T_\lambda(U)\|_\infty \le\frac{2\pi}{c_0\ell_{\min}},\qquad \|D\mathcal T_\lambda(U)\| \le\frac{2\pi L}{c_0^2\ell_{\min}}. \qquad\text{(5)} One fixed long-slope threshold makes the image lie in the interior half-polydisk and the second bound smaller than 1/21/2. This proves existence and uniqueness throughout the closed charge cube. It also gives invertibility of the normalized filling Jacobian I−D𝒯λI-D\mathcal T_\lambda, with inverse norm at most two. The branch is holomorphic in a neighborhood of each charge value; uniqueness patches these neighborhoods along the whole cube.

Coordinatewise, |Ui|≤2πλic0ℓi,Ui=2πiϵiλiai+biτi(0)+O(λiℓiℓmin).(6) |U_i|\le\frac{2\pi\lambda_i}{c_0\ell_i},\qquad U_i= \frac{2\pi i\epsilon_i\lambda_i}{a_i+b_i\tau_i(0)} +O\!\left(\frac{\lambda_i}{\ell_i\ell_{\min}}\right). \qquad\text{(6)} The constants do not depend on signs, directions, relative slope lengths, or charges. If λi=0\lambda_i=0, (4) gives Ui=Vi=0U_i=V_i=0 exactly. Such a cusp remains nontrivial parabolic in the fixed chart.

The absolutely normalized volume expansion proved in F is Vol⁡(ρU)=V−14∑iℑτi(0)|Ui|2+O(∥U∥∞∑i|Ui|2).(7) \operatorname{Vol}(\rho_U) =V-\frac14\sum_i\Im\tau_i(0)|U_i|^2 +O\!\left(\|U\|_\infty\sum_i|U_i|^2\right). \qquad\text{(7)} Substitution of (6) gives, uniformly on the entire charge cube, Vol⁡(ρg(λ))=V−π2∑i∈Iℑτi(0)λi2|ai+biτi(0)|2+O(ℓmin⁡−1∑i∈Iλi2ℓi2).(8) \operatorname{Vol}(\rho_{\mathrm g}(\lambda)) =V-\pi^2\sum_{i\in I} \frac{\Im\tau_i(0)\lambda_i^2} {|a_i+b_i\tau_i(0)|^2} +O\!\left(\ell_{\min}^{-1} \sum_{i\in I}\frac{\lambda_i^2}{\ell_i^2}\right). \qquad\text{(8)} In particular its volume loss is at most C∑iℓi−2C\sum_i\ell_i^{-2}. At unit charges, F identifies this branch with the actual smooth hyperbolic filling. At intermediate charges the argument here constructs the actual representation path. If an actual cone deformation with these centered periods is supplied in the chart, uniqueness identifies its holonomy with this path. No separate metric-existence assertion at every intermediate angle is needed for the volume comparison.

3. Uniform dominance over centered competitors

Theorem. There are L*L_* and g>0g>0, depending only on the fixed marked core and certificate, such that for every subset II, every independently signed primitive slope tuple with ℓmin>L*\ell_{\min}>L_*, and every λ∈[0,1]I\lambda\in[0,1]^I, each competitor specified in Section 1 obeys ρ≁ρg(λ)⇒Vol⁡(ρ)≤Vol⁡(ρg(λ))−g.(9) \rho\not\sim\rho_{\mathrm g}(\lambda) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Vol}(\rho) \le\operatorname{Vol}(\rho_{\mathrm g}(\lambda))-g . \qquad\text{(9)} The relation ∼\sim is conjugacy of the underlying projective representation. The assertion is signed, with the chosen orientation. It does not separate central lifts or source decorations of one underlying representation.

Proof. First choose a sufficiently small representation neighborhood of the oriented complete holonomy, before choosing the slopes. At each cusp retain a primitive element with nonidentity parabolic complete image. Its nearby images remain nonidentity. Every fixed flag of these nearby matrices lies near the unique complete flag: otherwise a convergent subsequence of flags in ℂℙ1\mathbb{CP}^1 would produce a second fixed flag of the nonidentity parabolic.

As explained in N, Sections 5–6, developing the supplied triangulation from any such peripheral flags therefore gives positive shapes in its fixed gluing chart. The normalized decorated representation and gluing constructions are inverse there. Shrink this representation neighborhood so every centered peripheral vector lies in the contraction polydisk of Section 2. Its conjugacy saturation 𝒰\mathcal U is open.

Consequently any competitor in 𝒰\mathcal U, with any retained flags, satisfying the centered equations (2), is the unique fixed point (4). It is conjugate to ρg(λ)\rho_{\mathrm g}(\lambda). This uses centered similarity logs of all the actual selected primitive words, rather than traces or exponential filling equations alone.

N, Corollary 2, now gives a fixed δ>0\delta>0 such that every admitted representation outside 𝒰\mathcal U satisfies Vol⁡(ρ)≤V−δ.(10) \operatorname{Vol}(\rho)\le V-\delta. \qquad\text{(10)} This estimate requires no bound on shape coordinates, chosen conjugations, or peripheral translation lengths. Increase the slope threshold until (8) gives Vol⁡(ρg(λ))>V−δ/2(11) \operatorname{Vol}(\rho_{\mathrm g}(\lambda))>V-\delta/2 \qquad\text{(11)} for all charges, subsets and directions under consideration. There are only finitely many cusps and subsets, so the same threshold works for all of them. Every nonconjugate competitor lies outside 𝒰\mathcal U; (10)–(11) prove (9) with g=δ/2g=\delta/2. ▫\square

In particular (9) holds along every path in the charge cube, including the straight cone path λi=t\lambda_i=t, 0≤t≤10\le t\le1. For a fixed hyperbolic short partial filling, the constants depend on that entire short-filled core. There is no uniformity assertion over varying short cores.

4. Exact volume ordering on the larger centered charge box

There is also an exact comparison of different nearby real charges. It does not require that their magnitudes be at most one. Choose a sufficiently small fixed ρ*>0\rho_*>0. The same fixed-point map, with numerator 2πiqi2\pi iq_i, is a contraction for every q∈ℝI,|qi|≤ρ*ℓi.(12) q\in\mathbb R^I,\qquad |q_i|\le\rho_*\ell_i. \qquad\text{(12)} Indeed its image norm is at most 2πρ*/c02\pi\rho_*/c_0, and its derivative norm is at most 2πLρ*/c022\pi L\rho_*/c_0^2. Choose ρ*\rho_* so these are within the fixed chart and below the contraction threshold. Write 𝒱(q)\mathcal V(q) for the volume of the resulting representation.

Choose an integral complementary primitive curve on each selected cusp: aidi−bici=1,si=aiUi+biVi=2πiqi,Wi=ciUi+diVi.(13) a_i d_i-b_i c_i=1,\qquad s_i=a_iU_i+b_iV_i=2\pi iq_i,\qquad W_i=c_iU_i+d_iV_i. \qquad\text{(13)} The exact volume differential F(10) is invariant under this determinant-one change of peripheral basis. Along the real charge box it therefore gives d𝒱=12∑i[ℜWidℑsi−ℜsidℑWi]=π∑iℜWidqi.(14) d\mathcal V =\frac12\sum_i [\Re W_i\,d\Im s_i-\Re s_i\,d\Im W_i] =\pi\sum_i\Re W_i\,dq_i. \qquad\text{(14)} Unfilled terms vanish since their Ui,ViU_i,V_i are identically zero. By (1), (3) and (13), Wi=2πiqici+diτi(U)ai+biτi(U),ℜWi=−2πqiℑτi(U)|ai+biτi(U)|2.(15) W_i=2\pi iq_i \frac{c_i+d_i\tau_i(U)}{a_i+b_i\tau_i(U)},\qquad \Re W_i= -\frac{2\pi q_i\Im\tau_i(U)} {|a_i+b_i\tau_i(U)|^2}. \qquad\text{(15)} Here the last identity uses ℑ[(c+dτ)/(a+bτ)]=ℑτ/|a+bτ|2\Im[(c+d\tau)/(a+b\tau)]=\Im\tau/|a+b\tau|^2; the determinant in (13) fixes its sign. Consequently ∂𝒱∂qi=−2π2qiℑτi(U(q))|ai+biτi(U(q))|2.(16) \frac{\partial\mathcal V}{\partial q_i} =-\frac{2\pi^2q_i\Im\tau_i(U(q))} {|a_i+b_i\tau_i(U(q))|^2}. \qquad\text{(16)} This is exact, including all coupling between cusps. There is no uncontrolled derivative of the remainder in (8).

The volume is unchanged by independently reversing the sign of any qiq_i. For qi≠0q_i\ne0, change only that cusp’s retained common eigenflag to the other fixed flag of the same representation. This negates Ui,ViU_i,V_i and keeps all other cusp logs unchanged. The nearby-flags argument in Section 3 puts this decoration in the same positive chart. Its centered charge is the sign-reversed tuple, so uniqueness identifies it with that tuple’s constructed representation. The underlying representation has not changed. At qi=0q_i=0 the assertion follows directly, or by continuity.

Thus 𝒱(q)\mathcal V(q) is a function of the coordinatewise absolute charges and is strictly decreasing in each qi2q_i^2 on (12). More quantitatively, if q,q̃q,\widetilde q lie in that box and |qi|≥|q̃i||q_i|\ge|\widetilde q_i| for every ii, integration of (16) one coordinate at a time gives 𝒱(q̃)−𝒱(q)≥c1∑iqi2−q̃i2ℓi2,c1=π2m1+T2>0.(17) \mathcal V(\widetilde q)-\mathcal V(q) \ge c_1\sum_i\frac{q_i^2-\widetilde q_i^2}{\ell_i^2}, \qquad c_1=\frac{\pi^2m}{1+T^2}>0. \qquad\text{(17)} We used |ai+biτi|2≤(1+T2)ℓi2|a_i+b_i\tau_i|^2\le(1+T^2)\ell_i^2. Equality of all absolute charges gives the same underlying representation by the flag argument.

This box includes every sufficiently near-complete representation whose centered primitive slope logs are purely imaginary. In fact |qi|ℓi≤1+T22π|Ui|.(18) \frac{|q_i|}{\ell_i} \le\frac{\sqrt{1+T^2}}{2\pi}|U_i|. \qquad\text{(18)} Shrink the representation neighborhood in Section 3 so the right side is below ρ*\rho_*. This shrink is independent of the slopes. Representations outside it still have the fixed gap from N.

At unit charge, (17) compares every nonzero centered integer-charge tuple q∈(ℤ\{0})Iq\in(\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\})^I in the neighborhood with the tuple of unit absolute charges. If some |qi|≥2|q_i|\ge2, its volume deficit is at least 3c1/ℓmax⁡23c_1/\ell_{\max}^2, where ℓmax=max⁡i∈Iℓi\ell_{\max}=\max_{i\in I}\ell_i. All tuples with |qi|=1|q_i|=1 give the same underlying representation. Outside the neighborhood, combine N with (11). Thus the geometric comparison over all such nonzero integer charges has a positive gap for each fixed long slope tuple: gσ=min⁡{δ/2,3c1/ℓmax⁡2}.(19) g_\sigma=\min\{\delta/2,\,3c_1/\ell_{\max}^2\}. \qquad\text{(19)} This statement assumes that the centered primitive charges are those integers; it does not infer this from an exponentiated filling equation.

More generally, if qi=λiniq_i=\lambda_i n_i with nonzero integers nin_i and λi∈[λ0,1]\lambda_i\in[\lambda_0,1], λ0>0\lambda_0>0, the same argument gives a gap min⁡{δ/2,3c1λ02/ℓmax⁡2}\min\{\delta/2,3c_1\lambda_0^2/\ell_{\max}^2\} to every different underlying representation. This is a geometric statement for that specified charge family, not an assertion that a deformation of the original quantum kernel has been constructed.

For other charge families, the comparison across different classes need not give a fixed positive gap up to every endpoint. For example, (λ−2)2−λ2=4(1−λ)(\lambda-2)^2-\lambda^2=4(1-\lambda) tends to zero as λ↑1\lambda\uparrow1; the two minimal signs then represent the same underlying representation. Their source coefficients still need to be combined with their actual signs and peripheral factors.

5. Why exponentiated projective data alone are insufficient

The same actual gluing germ shows why exponentiated slope equations would be insufficient. Fill just one cusp, and fix 1/2<λ<11/2<\lambda<1. Formula (4) also defines the nearby actual representation for the bounded signed charge λ−1\lambda-1. The two branches have the same exponentiated projective slope multiplier: e2πiλ=e2πi(λ−1).(20) e^{2\pi i\lambda}=e^{2\pi i(\lambda-1)}. \qquad\text{(20)} Nevertheless their volumes, by the same uniform expansion (8), satisfy Vol⁡(ρλ−1)−Vol⁡(ρλ)=π2ℑτ(0)(2λ−1)|a+bτ(0)|2+O(ℓ−3)>0(21) \operatorname{Vol}(\rho_{\lambda-1}) -\operatorname{Vol}(\rho_\lambda) =\frac{\pi^2\Im\tau(0)(2\lambda-1)} {|a+b\tau(0)|^2}+O(\ell^{-3})>0 \qquad\text{(21)} for all sufficiently long slopes. The last inequality follows from |a+bτ(0)|≤Cℓ|a+b\tau(0)|\le C\ell and ℑτ(0)>0\Im\tau(0)>0. These are actual representations of the same fixed core, constructed by its supplied positive germ. Equation (21) makes no assertion that the second branch occurs in the original quantum cycle.

The two charges in (20) differ by an odd integer: the unsquared SL2\mathrm{SL}_2 slope multiplier changes sign. Thus this comparison does not apply to a source that separately fixes that sign. In the latter case the possible charge shifts are even, and the geometric comparison in Section 4 is the relevant one. Determining the actual congruence class, including integer-spin channels and Weyl factors, is a literal source calculation, not a consequence of projective volume rigidity.

Thus near-maximal rigidity does not select a logarithmic sheet. Even within the complete neighborhood, the full source equations must be matched to the centered primitive equations before (9) applies. Representations outside that neighborhood already satisfy (10).

6. The remaining source question

This assembly replaces an arbitrary-cone maximality premise by a proved geometric comparison for sufficiently long centered fillings of a fixed core. It supplies a gap throughout the deformation and local isolation of the marked representation branch. It does not identify every original critical action with that volume, eliminate equal-action decorations, or prove a quantum Hessian nondegenerate from the representation Jacobian.

In particular, an initially nonzero thimble coefficient is not transported by this note. Applying (9) to that question still requires the actual centered source marking and action normalization, the original relative cycle with both endpoint currents, and control or exact cancellation of source divisors and ends. Compactness of near-maximal representations does not imply compactness of their highest-cut source coordinates.
