Exact top-cap mark transport for the original arbitrary-Casimir source

The equality concerns full complete-colored sources. It does not transport individual sectors or their positive envelopes.

1. Statement with the permitted cuts fixed

Fix N>=3, d=N-1, q_N=exp(pi i/N), and one actual ordered 2k-strand flat plat. At each original box insert ANY finite polynomial

f_e(Omega) R_e^(epsilon_e), epsilon_e in {0,+1,-1},

in its original order, with the full physical crossing/Cartan scalar. Keep all main colors equal to V_d, all original bottom cups, and all original top caps except that the single unrestricted mark is placed at one selected top pair r. Let S_r be that literal raw contraction. The same fixed exterior framing line may multiply every S_r.

Then S_r=S_s for ANY two of the original top-cap positions. In particular this holds for two permitted cuts on the same main component. It also holds for different top-cap components when they carry this SAME complete color and these exact source conventions; that stronger statement is proved below, not inferred from half-twist independence.

Coefficients of the polynomials may depend on N and on arbitrary spectral variables. All original Hermite data are retained: this is an identity of full matrices, not a simple-node interpolation. The result applies in particular to the full cleared resolvent numerator V_N(sigma) at every real or complex sigma, including repeated spectral nodes.

2. A regular finite-dimensional lift, not a generic ADO normalization

Hold N fixed and introduce a variable q near q_N, on the original local square-root branch for the Cartan factors. On the ordinary highest-weight module of highest weight d use

K v_a=q^(d-2a)v_a, F v_a=v_(a+1),
E v_a=[a]_q[N-a]_q v_(a-1), 0<=a<=d.

Endpoints are zero. The literal finite universal R has summation indices less than N; all factorial denominators [h]_q!, h<N, are units at q_N. Its inverse, the actual duality matrices, and all finite Casimir polynomials are therefore regular there. No denominator [N]_q is used in a matrix entry.

For clarity the self-duality is regular too. In the standard basis set

J(q)v_a=c_a(q)v_(d-a)^*,
c_a(q)=(-1)^a q^(a(a-N)).                         (1)

Using S(F)=-KF and S(E)=-EK^(-1), the F equation is c_(a+1)=-q^(-d+2a)c_a, and the E and K equations give the same intertwiner. At q_N, c_a=q_N(a2), the actual complete-color duality. Change to the literal balanced basis v_a/F_a(q), F_a(q)=prod_(h=1)a(1-q(2h)); every F_a with a<N is a unit at q_N. The actual cup and cap formulas are thus regular specializations of these generic finite matrices, with the root pivot correction below.

Extend each fixed root polynomial f_e by keeping its coefficients fixed and replacing Omega by the generic pair Casimir Omega(q). Retained R_e^(epsilon_e) is extended by its ACTUAL finite universal crossing, not by a guessed generic interpolation of its root Hermite polynomial. These are Hopf intertwiners in the actual oriented/dual frames.

For the cleared numerator an explicit choice avoids any inverse:

f_sigma(z)=[chi_N(sigma)-chi_N(z)]/(sigma-z),
chi_N(z)=2 T_N(z/2)+2.                            (2)

Equation (2) is a polynomial in z and sigma. Only AFTER specializing q=q_N use chi_N(Omega)=0 to identify it with V_N(sigma). No generic meromorphic resolvent or cancellation at a repeated node is assumed.

This construction is an auxiliary finite-dimensional polynomial specialization. It does not identify the generic standard pivot with the genuine-q ADO pivot or assert an equality of their transverse jets.

3. Scalar open caps and one generic closed contraction

Use the standard generic pivot K and its actual oriented evaluation and coevaluation. After the boxes and all top caps except r, the network is a Hopf map from the unit object to V_d tensor V_d^* at that pair. Through the explicit cup identification this is an endomorphism C_r(q) of V_d commuting with the generators. It is scalar: K has distinct weights generically, and the consecutive nonzero F arrows force all diagonal entries equal. Write C_r(q)=a_r(q)I.

Equivalently the remaining two-head vector is a_r(q) times its original cup. The scalar is regular at q_N: it is one matrix entry of the open network in the regular frames just constructed. This argument needs no ordinary quantum dimension at the root and introduces no new mark.

Close the last pair with the ordinary K-pivotal cap. The result is the SAME fully closed tensor contraction Z(q), independent of which cap was temporarily left open. The explicit trace of K is

Tr_(V_d)K=sum_(a=0)^d q^(d-2a)=[N]_q,
Z(q)=[N]_q a_r(q) for every r.                   (3)

The dual has the same trace, by reversing this finite weight sum. For generic q, [N]_q is nonzero, so all a_r(q) agree. They are regular at q_N, hence their equality specializes. The division in (3) occurs ONLY generically; no zero quantum dimension is divided at the root. This uses literal finite tensors and a finite weight sum, not an imported modified-trace cyclicity or ambidexterity theorem.

4. Return to the exact root pivot and unrestricted mark

At q_N every main weight has parity d, so the ACTUAL pivot is

g=K^(1-N)=(-1)^d K.                              (4)

In the flat-plat convention all bottom turns are left cups and all ordinary top turns are right caps. The left cups are unchanged; each of the k-1 ordinary top caps therefore acquires the SAME factor (-1)^d relative to the K-pivotal network. This is independent of r.

The balanced source formulas are, with x_0=q_N^d and Q=q_N^2,

p_(i,j)=N x_0^(-j) delta_(i+j,d),
ell_(i,j)=x_0^j g_i/N delta_(i+j,d),
m_(i,j)=x_0^j/N^2.                              (5)

The mark m is unrestricted. Equations (1) and

F_a(q_N)F_(d-a)(q_N)=N(-1)^a Q^(a(a+1)/2)

give the cup and ordinary cap in (5) directly. The original rank-one source e=psi phi, psi_a=1, phi_a=1/N, has Tr(e)=1, and its marked insertion is D=g^(-1)e. Thus Tr(gDC)=Tr(eC), and DIRECTLY mp=1. For the scalar open vector in section 3, the original marked evaluation is consequently

S_r=(-1)^(d(k-1)) a_r(q_N),                     (6)

with no additional N, quantum-dimension division or component-dependent factor. All full Cartan factors and retained half twists were already inside the same a_r. A fixed original framing/peripheral character multiplies both sides of (6) equally.

This proves the stated equality for all permitted top caps, at either parity and composite levels. If the one-cut modified normalization is used, its complete-color factor i^(N+3)/N is the same on any such complete-colored cut. Deformed unequal main colors, unrestricted moves of the nonintertwining defect D, and generic-q ADO derivatives are NOT asserted to satisfy this identity.

5. Consequences and the remaining cap question

The exact homogeneous face-source identity therefore gives equal marked coefficients for the different original top-cap targets, for EVERY choice of the full Casimir polynomials. In particular the full S_N(sigma) is identical as a polynomial in all spectral variables. This is stronger than merely equality of the final knot scalar at one twist vector.

Corollary: a certificate at any permitted original top mark

Fix a width k≥2k\geq2, an original ordered 2k2k-strand flat-plat skeleton DD, and fixed retained half twists ϵe∈{0,±1}\epsilon_e\in\{0,\pm1\}. Suppose the endpoint permutation gives a knot and that, at some original top mark rr, the diagram has the actual labeled simple-white geometric certificate and satisfies all hypotheses of at least one of these proved full-source criteria:

  • complete-radius balance;
  • the anchored shared-radius criterion;
  • a finite cover of the actual sectors.

The protected faces, complete packing, target extraction, and any finite shared-radius witnesses in the selected criterion are those for mark rr. Choose this mark and certificate once, independently of the quantum level and filling vector. Then there is T(D)<∞T(D)<\infty, with the chosen certificate included in the fixed data, such that for every independently signed integer vector 𝒑\mathbf p satisfying |pe|>T(D)|p_e|>T(D), fixed before N→∞N\to\infty, and for every original top mark ss,

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(s)(KD(𝒑))|=Vol⁡(S3\KD(𝒑)). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N} \log\left|K_N^{(s)}\bigl(K_D(\mathbf p)\bigr)\right| =\operatorname{Vol}\bigl(S^3\setminus K_D(\mathbf p)\bigr). \tag{7}

Here the actual crossing powers are ne=ϵe−2pen_e=\epsilon_e-2p_e and KN(s)K_N^{(s)} uses the original full normalization, with only the permitted top mark changed. There is no comparability condition on the magnitudes of the independent twists. Both level parities and composite levels are included. The volume is that of the same actual filled knot exterior; no claim of exact bridge number kk, a joint growing-twist/level limit, or a threshold uniform in different diagrams is made.

Proof. Let SN,r(𝝈)S_{N,r}(\boldsymbol\sigma) be the full marked numerator with the polynomial coupons (2), without an extra retained RR factor. Sections 1–4, applied with ϵe=0\epsilon_e=0, give the exact polynomial identity SN,r=SN,sS_{N,r}=S_{N,s} for every NN. The original Fourier/Hermite transform is a linear functional of this full polynomial. Its sine factors, charge weights and parity, Gaussian powers ne=ϵe−2pen_e=\epsilon_e-2p_e, and scalar qNd2∑ene/4(−i)Rq_N^{d^2\sum_e n_e/4}(-i)^R are the same for both marks. Apply that same exact transform to the polynomial identity. Multiplying by the same recorded final-diagram framing/component character and complete-color normalization therefore gives KN(r)=KN(s)K_N^{(r)}=K_N^{(s)} at every level. The retained half twist appears exactly once, through nen_e in this Fourier description; the alternative direct coupon description retains its actual ReϵeR_e^{\epsilon_e} as in section 1.

At mark rr, the selected theorem supplies the full original invariant limit and a threshold depending on its fixed geometric and finite certificate data. The exact scalar equality transfers that conclusion to each ss, with the same filled topology and threshold. In particular, we do not transport its all-CROSS summand or its remainder decomposition. This proves (7).

The width-three mark-dependence example separates two sufficient tests on the same literal diagram: one mark passes balance while another fails. With the present corollary the successful test suffices for either marked evaluation. This does not prove strict enlargement of the underlying unmarked knot classes, and does not show that every original diagram has a successful mark.

The identity does NOT transport an individual cap window or a chosen LEFT/CROSS/RIGHT term. Those row projections are not arbitrary Casimir polynomials/intertwiners. Their absolute coefficient arrays, protected targets, signed-incidence cycles and envelope maxima change with the mark. Equality of the summed polynomial does not identify those maxima or guarantee that a new mark has no residual cap. The earlier finite- collar obstruction remains unchanged. A direct termwise mark-slide or an absolute cancellation estimate still needs its own proof.

The source inputs are the original all-coupon identity, literal duality, turns and rank-one mark, and root Casimir relation and regular highest-weight construction. The mark-dependence example is separate geometry. The sufficient applications use the existing complete-radius balance theorem or anchored shared-radius theorem. The finite-collar obstruction is unchanged. No literature proof theorem is used here.

6. scope and finite verification

The preserved portable audit and its results check original physical coproduct matrices, complete divided-polynomial numerators at sigma=-2,2,0,3, arbitrary Casimir polynomials, and the actual positive and negative crossings. They verify the remaining two-head vector entrywise against its scalar times the original cup before comparing the original top marks.

The exact fixtures use compatible finite fields and check the stated integer overflow bounds. They verify the full source identity at the tested levels, not projected-sector identities or an asymptotic theorem.
