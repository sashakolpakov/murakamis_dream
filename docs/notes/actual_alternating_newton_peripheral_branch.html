The retained Newton sign and the actual local peripheral branch

30 October 2026. The actual alternating low-color Newton sum has a localized critical scheme whose eliminated quadratic is exactly the polynomial closure of the original untwisted primitive-collar trace relation. Its selected complete germ is fixed by the already proved collar flags and meridian convention. For positive face variables, however, every real critical Newton index lies OUTSIDE the physical terminating range. The identity does not make that branch a contribution to the original finite sum.

This is a local marked algebraic comparison, valid at an actual original port with positive outer and middle face variables. It is independent of bridge width. It does not cover a marked zero-face degeneration, a retained half twist at this port, the discrete endpoint analysis, or a global low-color representation/period. The nearby actual geometric family is already constructed separately; no new global gluing theorem is inferred from this scalar relation.

1. Keep the literal Newton coefficient and its sign

The exact Newton source formula, section0, gives

T_h=sum_(r=0)^(h-1) (-1)^(h-1+r) b_(h,r) Bhat_r(-T),
b_(h,r)=S_r^2 S_(h+r)/[S_(2r+1)S_(h-r-1)],
S_j=product_(k=1)^j 2sin(pi k/N).

Here 1<=h<=floor(N/2), and the coefficient of x^i y^j c^(m-i-j) in Bhat_r(-T)c^m is exactly

[m r]_s [m+r-i-j r]_s [r i]_s [r j]_s.

Write a_r for this four-binomial coefficient multiplied by the LITERAL signed b_(h,r). With s_j=2sin(pi j/N), direct factorial telescoping gives, when both terms are in the strict physical support,

a_(r+1)/a_r =
-s_(r+1)^2 s_(h+r+1) s_(h-r-1) s_(m+r-i-j+1) s_(m-r)
----------------------------------------------------------------.
      s_(2r+2)s_(2r+3)s_(r-i+1)s_(r-j+1)             (0)

In particular r+1<h. Its limit on each strict normalized compact is the NEGATIVE of the product on the left of (2). Thus (2) is the actual finite-ratio characteristic; it is not inferred by differentiating only a logarithmic asymptotic with an uncontrolled error. The three other shared-face characteristics follow by the same factorial telescoping in m,i,j. All finite offsets remain in these identities.

The port and original q^(dp) character are those of that source identity. Its unwrapped physical support and its actual finite +1 shifts are retained; the calculation below concerns their limiting logarithmic critical equations, not an off-grid finite equality.

Use the original Newton variables alpha=h/N, rho=r/N, mu=m/N, xi=i/N, eta=j/N. For 0<rho<alpha<1/2 and the strict nonzero Newton polytope, the genuine positive coefficient b_(h,r) has logarithmic rate

B_alpha(rho)=2J(rho)+J(alpha+rho)-J(2rho)-J(alpha-rho),
J(t)=integral_0^t log(2sin(pi z)) dz.

The strict nonzero Newton polytope means

rho<mu<1, 0<xi,eta<rho, xi+eta<mu,
mu+rho-xi-eta<1.

The coefficient’s logarithmic rate is

K=J(mu)+J(mu+rho-xi-eta)-J(mu-rho)-J(mu-xi-eta)
                   -J(xi)-J(rho-xi)-J(eta)-J(rho-eta).

Its shared-face critical action contains

K+B_alpha+pi i rho+mu Log(b/c)
                    +xi Log(x/b)+eta Log(y/b),

with the fixed pi i alpha character and the rest of the original source still retained. Differentiating in mu,xi,eta gives the first three equations of section2; differentiating in rho gives (2).

The finite +1 shifts affect this rate by o(1) on fixed strict compacts. They are not removed from the finite source. Differentiating gives

exp(partial_rho B_alpha)
  =sin(pi(alpha+rho)) sin(pi(alpha-rho))/cos(pi rho)^2
  =[sin(pi alpha)^2-sin(pi rho)^2]/cos(pi rho)^2.    (1)

The exact sign (-1)^(h-1+r) contributes pi i rho to the leading rho action. Its pi i alpha part must ALSO remain in the complete marked action; it is simply independent of rho. With K the actual four-tetrahedron Newton entropy, its retained rho critical equation is

sin(pi(mu+rho-xi-eta)) sin(pi(mu-rho))
------------------------------------------------- *
sin(pi(rho-xi)) sin(pi(rho-eta))
[sin(pi alpha)^2-sin(pi rho)^2]/cos(pi rho)^2 = -1. (2)

All logarithms use a specified continuation from the positive physical domain before adding the retained pi i character. There is no real interior solution in that domain, since every factor there is positive. This is precisely why its positive capacity maximum cannot be used as an actual saddle.

Put Z=exp(2pi i mu), A=exp(2pi i xi), B=exp(2pi i eta), W=exp(2pi i rho), and kappa=4cos(pi alpha)^2. The two rational factors in (2) become

(ZW-AB)(Z-W)/[Z(W-A)(W-B)],
(W-exp(2pi i alpha))(W-exp(-2pi i alpha))/(W+1)^2.

After multiplying their RECORDED denominators and bringing the -1 across, the constant and degree-four terms cancel. Divide only by the exponential unit W. The result is the quadratic

(Z-A)(Z-B)(W+1)^2
      +kappa (W-Z)(ZW-AB)=0.                      (3)

This identity follows by expansion and contains no asymptotic approximation. The physical source fixes which component, logarithm and contour could be relevant; (3) does not choose them. The parameter kappa has the form of a meridian trace square under the established color marking, but this calculation alone does not identify the remaining variables with actual holonomy coordinates.

Equation (3) is valid only off the denominators cleared from (2), including W=A,B,-1 and the original sine factors. At alpha=1/2 the second factor in (1) is identically 1 after cancellation. Then the uncleared equation reduces to

W (Z-A)(Z-B)=0.

Since W is an exponential unit, it has no generic finite solution. The double root W=-1 of (3) at kappa=0 is therefore extraneous, not an endpoint geometric saddle. Any treatment at that color must use the literal discrete endpoint/pole structure or an independently justified continuation. The coefficient’s exact finite offsets and all other original phases remain essential.

2. The localized shared-face critical scheme

Write the four positive face variables as x,b,c,y, retaining the source input b/output c convention from the Newton coefficient extraction. Let U=exp(2pi i alpha), kappa=(U+1)^2/U. Over the Laurent ring in U,Z,A,B,W and the four face variables, the UNSATURATED equations are

b(Z-1)(ZW-AB)-c(Z-W)(Z-AB)=0,
x(Z-AB)(W-A)-b(ZW-AB)(A-1)=0,
y(Z-AB)(W-B)-b(ZW-AB)(B-1)=0,
(Z-A)(Z-B)(W+1)^2+kappa(W-Z)(ZW-AB)=0.              (4)

The actual regular critical scheme is their localization by the following product of recorded nonzero factors. The saturation ideal defines its closure and has the same restriction on D_reg != 0:

D_reg=xybc UZABW (Z-1)(Z-W)(Z-AB)(ZW-AB)
         (A-1)(B-1)(W-A)(W-B)(W-1)(W+1)
         (W-U)(W-U^-1).                            (5)

These factors come from the strict sine-product arguments of the literal coefficient and its weight. Some cancel in particular formulas, but are not silently removed from the original regular chart. The choice of the continued logarithms is additional marked data, not encoded by this algebraic saturation alone. At endpoint arguments the unsaturated equations are merely a closure; they are not the actual critical scheme without a separate boundary analysis.

The discriminant of the quadratic in W is exactly

kappa {kappa(Z^2-AB)^2
      -4(Z-A)(Z-B)(Z+1)(Z+AB)}.                    (6)

At leading-coefficient or discriminant zeros it need not have two distinct finite branches. No branch or integration cycle follows from this discriminant.

3. Exact elimination using the three shared-face equations

The first three equations admit the following direct reduction. Set

t=b(ZW-AB)/(Z-AB), L=b+c+x+y,
D=xy-bc, E=bc(x+y)+(b+c)xy, A0=x+y.

On the regular chart (4) implies

A=(xW+t)/(x+t), B=(yW+t)/(y+t),
Z=(t-cW)/(t-c),
Lt^2+(1+W)Dt-WE=0.                                 (7)

The denominators x+t,y+t,t-c are units on (5): if one vanishes, its corresponding unreduced equation forces W=1. For positive faces, LE+D^2=(b+x)(b+y)(c+x)(c+y)>0. Thus the simultaneous exceptional case E-Dt=Lt+D=0 cannot occur, and

W=t(Lt+D)/(E-Dt).

For this positive-face elimination chart and its nearby complex continuation, additionally localize at E-Dt and (c+x)(c+y). For the reverse reconstruction below also retain L and kappa as units. These are units on the positive locus; they are not consequences of (5) on every complex component. Retain the full pullback of (5) under these substitutions. In particular t, W, t-c and t-cW stay nonzero; Z=0 is not an allowed solution. The first Newton sine ratio has the exact expression

exp(K_rho)=-1+W(c+x)(c+y)/[(t-c)(t-cW)].

Its correctly signed remaining equation is

kappa(t-c)(t-cW)
   +(c+x)(c+y)[(W+1)^2-kappa W]=0.                 (8)

After substituting W, the resultant is the unit (c+x)(c+y) times

(Lt^2+E)^2-kappa t(t+x)(t+y)(E-Dt)=0.              (9)

This is another compact polynomial closure. It is used only with the same pulled-back exclusions, not as an unconditional off-grid identity. Eliminating t instead and putting zeta=(W+1)^2/W gives the factor (c+x)2(c+y)2 W^2 times

(L^2+kappa D)zeta^2
  -kappa(2L A0+kappa xy)zeta+kappa^2 A0^2=0.

For kappa nonzero, z=zeta/kappa satisfies

(L^2+kappa D)z^2-(2L A0+kappa xy)z+A0^2=0,          (10)

with discriminant kappa[4A0 E+kappa x2y2]. Extra roots on excluded divisors are not restored by the calculation. There is an explicit reverse reconstruction on the recorded open chart. If Q1,Q2 denote the left sides of (7)’s last equation and (8), then

L Q2-kappa Q1
 =(c+x)(c+y)[-kappa(1+W)t+L(W+1)^2-kappa A0 W].

Consequently

t=L(W+1)/kappa-A0 W/(W+1)
  =W(Lz-A0)/(W+1).                               (10a)

If P(z) denotes the left side of (10), direct substitution gives Q1=L W^2 P(z)/(W+1)^2 whenever (W+1)^2=kappa z W. Thus a root of (10), either W satisfying (W+1)^2=kappa z W, and (10a), followed by (7), reconstruct the original four equations whenever all recorded units remain nonzero. This proves equivalence on that open chart, not on its polynomial closure. The extra L unit is explicit; it cannot be inferred from (5) on an arbitrary complex component.

4. The real critical indices lie outside the terminating sum

Assume x,b,c,y>0 and 0<alpha<1/2, so 0<kappa<4. The leading coefficient L^2+kappa D is positive, the middle coefficient has positive magnitude, and the discriminant in (10) is positive. Hence its two roots are real, distinct and positive. Let P be its left side. Exactly

P(1)=(b+c)^2-kappa bc>0,
2(L^2+kappa D)-(2L A0+kappa xy)
    =2(b+c)^2+2A0(b+c)+kappa(xy-2bc)>0.

The second inequality follows from kappa<=4, leaving 2(b-c)^2+2A0(b+c)+kappa xy>0. Thus the vertex lies below1; since P(1)>0, BOTH roots are strictly less than1. On a real unit-circle branch W=exp(2pi i rho), equation(10) reads

z=cos(pi rho)^2/cos(pi alpha)^2 in (0,1).

Its first-half real representative therefore has alpha<rho<1/2; the reflected representative lies in (1/2,1-alpha). Neither belongs to the ACTUAL terminating Newton range 0<r/N<h/N=alpha. This holds for every positive quartet, not only close to alpha=1/2.

In fact these give ALL FOUR regular algebraic critical points over this positive base (before choosing logarithmic windings). For each of the two z roots, the two W roots are distinct, nonreal and on the unit circle. Also

P(A0/L)=-kappa A0 E/L^2<0,

so Lz-A0 is nonzero. Formula(10a) makes both t and t/W nonreal. It follows that x+t,y+t,t-c,t-cW and E-Dt are nonzero, as are A,B,Z from (7). Their differences A-1,B-1,W-A,W-B,Z-1,Z-W are nonzero multiples of W-1 or t(W-1). The first three equations then exclude Z-AB=0: that would force ZW-AB=0, contradicting Z(W-1)!=0. They also exclude ZW-AB=0. Finally W=exp(+/-2pi i alpha) is impossible because z<1. Every factor in (5) is therefore a unit. No extra positive-base branch is created by (10); all four are reconstructed by (10a). This does not choose the actual source logarithms or cycle.

At alpha=1/2, (8) reduces on the saturated chart to the impossible condition (c+x)(c+y)=0. Thus the double W=-1 root of its polynomial closure is extraneous for positive faces, as already noted. At kappa=4 a root of (10) reaches1 only when b=c; the physical endpoint needs its own source treatment.

This proves a selection exclusion, not exponential cancellation or an endpoint estimate. A deformation of the actual finite sum or cotangent chain must still account for its endpoints, crossed divisors and marks. No representation of the original group or peripheral branch is inferred from equations(4)–(10) alone.

5. The actual primitive-collar polynomial closure and selected germ

Use the actual primitive collar, sections1–3 and its marked-strip orientation in section5. Its original order is A_in=r_b r_x, B_in=r_y r_b, P=r_y r_x; its corner-preserving sidepairing carries the input b triangle to the output c triangle. This is not the doubled shaded-reflection word. Write

u=sin(pi delta)^2, A0=x+y,
Dg=A0^2-4xy u,
Tb^2=b A0+xy+b^2u, Tc^2=c A0+xy+c^2u,
Eb=A0+2bu+2sqrt(u)Tb, Ec=A0+2cu+2sqrt(u)Tc.

The literal untwisted primitive collar has R0=Eb Ec/Dg on its selected flag. Write M for its selected auxiliary meridian EIGENVALUE, so the actual determinant-one meridian matrix has eigenvalues M,M^-1. Its marking is M=exp(-pi i alpha), R0=M^-2=exp(2pi i alpha), as in the geometric assembly, sections1–2. Therefore its trace square is

kappa=R0+2+R0^-1.                                 (11)

Both square roots Tb,Tc are the positive ones at completeness, then continued on the actual marked germ. Eliminating them from (11) gives

[kappa Dg-2Dg-2(A0+2bu)(A0+2cu)]^2
                 -64u^2 Tb^2 Tc^2
=Dg {16[(b+c)^2-kappa bc]u^2
      -4(kappa-4)[2A0(b+c)+kappa xy]u
      +(kappa-4)^2 A0^2}.                          (12)

Retain Dg != 0 and the actual selected square roots; squaring does not make the other sign combinations into the selected collar. For kappa != 4 and z != 1 put

v=4u/(kappa-4), v=z/(1-z).

After dividing (12) by (kappa-4)^2 and multiplying by (1-z)^2, its quadratic is EXACTLY (10). Equivalently

u=(kappa-4)z/[4(1-z)]
  =-sin(pi alpha)^2 z/(1-z).                       (13)

Thus the first three shared-face equations do identify the remaining Newton polynomial closure with the original untwisted peripheral trace relation, through the explicit rational change (13). This does not identify every critical point or its logarithmic lift with that representation: all earlier units, signs, and actual source support remain necessary. At kappa=4 equation(13) is interpreted by the selected germ limit, not by division at that divisor.

The selection is visible already at the complete point. Set Tb0=sqrt(b A0+xy), Tc0=sqrt(c A0+xy). On the selected collar,

v -> A0^2/(Tb0+Tc0)^2,
z -> A0^2/[A0^2+(Tb0+Tc0)^2].                     (14)

This is the smaller z-root at kappa=4. The other squared branch uses (Tb0-Tc0)^2 instead; it is not the same selected flag continuation. For b=c it reaches z=1, where the displayed rational transformation is singular. For small real alpha the selected collar has delta = i alpha A0/(Tb0+Tc0)+O(alpha^2), with the stated M convention. Trace square alone would not fix this sign, distinguish a reciprocal collar, or retain the central SL lift. The selected choice is explicitly M_aux=R0^(-1/2) tending to1. Both low external color h and reflected high color N-h have the same limiting trace square; this does not identify their source periods or their selected contribution coefficients. In contrast the REAL Newton representative of either z-root has rho>alpha by the previous section. This gives an explicit separation between the algebraic peripheral branch and the physical terminating Newton support.

There is one further marked selector: the two reciprocal W roots of the selected z do not have the same external momentum. Keeping the pi i alpha character, the stationary Newton action has derivative

partial_alpha Psi=Log[sin(pi(alpha+rho))/sin(pi(alpha-rho))]+pi i,
exp(partial_alpha Psi)=-sin(pi(alpha+rho))/sin(pi(alpha-rho)). (15)

This also follows from the literal finite coefficient identity a_(h+1,r)/a_(h,r)=-s_(h+r+1)/s_(h-r), before taking its strict-compact limit. On the first-half branch alpha<rho<1/2 put delta=i beta, beta>0. Equation(13) implies EXACTLY

sin(pi(rho+alpha))/sin(pi(rho-alpha))=exp(2pi beta).

Indeed sinh(pi beta)=sin(pi alpha)cos(pi rho)/ sqrt[sin(pi(rho+alpha))sin(pi(rho-alpha))], and this equals the hyperbolic sine of half the logarithm of the displayed ratio. Hence (15) is exp(+2pi beta) for this W and exp(-2pi beta) for its reciprocal (rho replaced by1-rho). The already selected complete-core action has partial_alpha S=2pi i delta=-2pi beta. Therefore the RECIPROCAL W branch is the one whose exponentiated external momentum agrees with the actual primitive marking. The first-half W has the opposite external momentum. Both lie outside the terminating Newton range.

This comparison is exact on the local selected germ and retains logarithmic winding ambiguities. It does not establish equality of the full Newton and core actions, an additive or monomial normalization, or their original contributing cycles. It provides no contribution coefficient or exponential estimate for the finite alternating sum.

6. Verification and remaining source question

The focused audit checks the exact cleared identities and discriminants, the original finite signed Newton coefficient ratios against their logarithmic limits, reconstruction of the uncleared shared-face equations, and the UNSQUARED selected collar R0 with its literal meridian orientation, and the finite external ratio selecting the reciprocal W momentum. The finite-coefficient checks retain all original offsets and compare (0) with an independently assembled literal signed factorial coefficient.

The calculation isolates a concrete next requirement: continue the actual finite Newton/cotangent chain to the selected marked branch, retaining its terminating endpoints and every crossed divisor. Neither a root of the polynomial closure nor the absence of a real physical critical point proves exponential cancellation, nonvanishing, a regulator equality for the low-color period, or a global volume bound.

The peripheral-branch identities do not supply full-action normalization, endpoint analysis, a contribution to the finite sum, or a global low-color volume bound.
