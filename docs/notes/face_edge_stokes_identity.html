The edge part of face/edge duality

Research note, 25 October 2026. This is an independent algebraic argument for globally branched, oriented ideal face pairings. It does not use a quantum-invariant theorem. Its conclusion concerns all edge rows; the row for a specified peripheral curve requires a separate argument.

1. Statement and conventions

Let a triangulation have N ordered tetrahedra with signs epsilon_j. Face gluings preserve the induced order of their three vertices. Let X_i be the N by 2N incidence matrix for the face opposite vertex i, and put

D = X_0-X_1+X_2,     R = X_2-X_3,
E = diag(epsilon_j), script-A = [D;R],
S = script-A^(-1)[0;E],     r = X_0 S,
Q_f = -(r+r^T)/2,         P_+ = (E+I)/2.

Assume script-A is invertible. For a global edge e let a_e,b_e,c_e be the N-component incidence rows for local opposite edge pairs (01,23), (02,13), (03,12), respectively. Thus an edge can occur twice in one opposite pair. The literature shape convention gives

A_e = a_e-c_e, B_e = b_e-c_e     if epsilon_j=+1,
A_e = a_e-b_e, B_e = c_e-b_e     if epsilon_j=-1,

where this formula is applied column by column. Define

G = -2Q_f+P_+ = r+r^T+P_+.

Proposition. Every edge row satisfies

A_e = B_e G.                                      (1.1)

There is no geometricity assumption, and no restriction to twist knots.

2. A face assignment produced by an edge assignment

Assign an arbitrary number lambda_e to each global edge. Its pullbacks to a tetrahedron are denoted lambda_01,…,lambda_23. On an ordered face with vertices i<j<k define the single-valued global face assignment

phi(lambda)_ijk = lambda_jk-lambda_ik.

Its four local values are

phi_0=lambda_23-lambda_13,
phi_1=lambda_23-lambda_03,
phi_2=lambda_13-lambda_03,
phi_3=lambda_12-lambda_02.

Consequently D phi=0, whereas R phi=b(lambda)-c(lambda). If B(lambda)=sum_e lambda_e B_e, then columnwise B(lambda)=epsilon*(b(lambda)-c(lambda)). Therefore

script-A phi = [0;E B(lambda)^T],
S B(lambda)^T = phi,
r B(lambda)^T = lambda_23-lambda_13.               (2.1)

These equalities follow from invertibility and the displayed local formulas, without a homology assertion.

3. The second half is a discrete Stokes identity

Let v be arbitrary, and let x=S v be its global face assignment. Then D x=0 and R x=E v. On an ordered triangle i<j<k put

psi(lambda)_ijk = lambda_ik-lambda_ij.

Both x and psi are single-valued on identified faces. Their oriented face sum cancels in pairs:

sum_j epsilon_j sum_i (-1)^i x_(j,i) psi_(j,i) = 0. (3.1)

Within one tetrahedron substitute x_1=x_0+x_2. The summand becomes

(lambda_02+lambda_13-lambda_03-lambda_12)x_0
   -(lambda_01-lambda_02)(x_2-x_3).

Equation (3.1) therefore says

B(lambda) r v = sum_j (lambda_01-lambda_02)_j v_j.

Since v is arbitrary,

r^T B(lambda)^T = lambda_01-lambda_02.             (3.2)

Adding (2.1) and (3.2) gives

(r+r^T) B(lambda)^T = a(lambda)-b(lambda).

Finally P_+ B(lambda)^T adds b-c in a positive tetrahedron and zero in a negative tetrahedron. The result is A(lambda)^T. Symmetry of G now gives (1.1).

4. A rank consequence

Suppose the face pairings give a manifold with V ideal vertices and m global edges. Write A_ed,B_ed for all edge rows. Then

rank B_ed = m-V.                                 (4.1)

Indeed, lambda in ker(B_ed^T) also belongs to ker(A_ed^T) by (1.1). The two conditions imply a(lambda)=b(lambda)=c(lambda) in every tetrahedron. These two local equations are equivalent to the existence of four numbers t_0,t_1,t_2,t_3 such that

lambda_ij=t_i+t_j.

For example t_0=(lambda_01+lambda_02-lambda_12)/2, with the other values recovered similarly; the equal opposite-pair sums are exactly the remaining consistency conditions. The values t at the three vertices of a face are uniquely determined by that face’s three edge values. They therefore agree under every face gluing, and descend to one t per global ideal vertex. Conversely such global vertex values give a=b=c and B_ed^T lambda=0. This parametrization is injective: on each triangle its three equations t_i+t_j=0 force all three t’s to vanish. Thus the kernel has dimension V, proving (4.1).

For a one-cusped knot triangulation, Euler characteristic gives m=N and V=1, so B_ed has rank N-1. Its row dependence is the sum of all edge rows, since a=b=c=2 for the constant global edge assignment.

5. What remains for a peripheral curve

The subsequent preferred_longitude_cochains.md supplies the independent preferred-longitude proof left open in this section, including the singular relation. Its consequence for boundary fibers and exact framing corrections is proved in general_boundary_state_and_framing.md.

This proof establishes neither

A_l=B_l G

for the preferred longitude l, nor invertibility after adding that row to N-1 independent edge rows. A boundary Stokes formula identifying the induced peripheral line with the kernel of H_1(boundary M)->H_1(M) would address the former. That formula has not been proved here.

The exact diagnostic in audit_two_bridge.py recomputes face matrices and all edge/peripheral rows from canonical face pairings and SnapPy’s integral peripheral marking. It is a finite check, not an independent proof of the numerical canonicalization or a uniform two-bridge theorem. Its first nine examples find proper compatible branchings for 4_1, 5_2, 6_3 and 7_6. For 6_2, 7_3, 7_4, 7_5 and 7_7, all compatible branchings of the supplied canonical face pairing have singular script-A (16,16,32,24,32 branchings respectively). In those cases the unconstrained finite search exhausts its tree before the 10,000-candidate cap. These singularities do not obstruct other triangulations, other coordinate descriptions, or oscillatory state representations. They do invalidate an argument that assumes the canonical layered triangulation is automatically proper.

For the four proper examples the exact preferred-longitude row and rank-N completed B tests also pass. Those peripheral successes are separate certificates rather than consequences silently inferred from (1.1).
