An actual high integral color carries the degree-N Frobenius wall row

Research construction, 27 October 2026. The actual integral color of dimension N(r+1), specialized at the level-N root, has a classical Sym^r multiplicity space. Its generators are literal divided powers of the original quantum matrices, not an auxiliary classical source. For r=N a marked coherent co-source gives exactly the binomial wall row with N+1 entries. The original reduced knot scalar is retained by exact all-color periodicity.

This constructs the wall functional as an actual color insertion. It does not yet intertwine both framing-current complexes, identify whole quantum jets of different colors, or prove selected geometric source survival or a volume norm.

1. The original integral matrix family

Let N>=2, r>=0, m=N(r+1)-1, and let D_m(q) have its integral Weyl basis u_j, 0<=j<=m:

Huj=(m−2j)uj,K=qH,Euj=[m−j+1]quj−1,Fuj=[j+1]quj+1,[a]q=(qa−q−a)/(q−q−1). \begin{split} H u_j&=(m-2j)u_j,\quad K=q^H,\\ E u_j&=[m-j+1]_q u_{j-1},\qquad F u_j=[j+1]_q u_{j+1},\\ [a]_q&=(q^a-q^{-a})/(q-q^{-1}). \end{split} \tag{1.1}

Out-of-range vectors are zero. These are the ACTUAL integral color matrices used in all-color periodicity, not the generic-M typical module. Put q_N=exp(pi i/N), Q_N=q_N^2, and write j=kN+s with 0<=k<=r and 0<=s<N. Make the unit sign change

wk,s=βk,sukN+s,βk,s=(−1)Nk(k−1)/2+ks. w_{k,s}=\beta_{k,s}u_{kN+s},\qquad \beta_{k,s}=(-1)^{Nk(k-1)/2+ks}. \tag{1.2}

At the root, both ordinary block edges vanish: F at s=N-1 has coefficient [(k+1)N]=0, and E at s=0 has coefficient [(r+1-k)N]=0. With D_(N-1) denoting the Steinberg head, direct adjacent entries give

$$ \begin{split} E&=I_{r+1}\otimes(-1)^rE_{\rm St},\qquad F=I_{r+1}\otimes F_{\rm St},\\ K&=I_{r+1}\otimes(-1)^rK_{\rm St},\\ H&=I_{r+1}\otimes H_{\rm St} +N\operatorname{diag}(r-2k)\otimes I_N. \end{split} \tag{1.3} $$

For example the F coefficient before gauging is (-1)^k[s+1], and the consecutive beta ratio is (-1)^k. The E coefficient is (-1)^(r-k)[N-s], whose beta ratio gives (-1)^r[N-s]. The K and H identities follow from m-2j=N-1-2s+N(r-2k).

Thus the ordinary small quantum action has r+1 invariant Steinberg blocks. The unrolled H is NOT constant on this multiplicity space. A map mixing k is not automatically an unrolled intertwiner.

2. Divided powers have a nonzero classical block action

Keep the original generic matrices before specializing and define

𝖤=[E(q)N/[N]q!]q=qN,𝖥=[F(q)N/[N]q!]q=qN. \mathsf E=\left[E(q)^N/[N]_q!\right]_{q=q_N},\qquad \mathsf F=\left[F(q)^N/[N]_q!\right]_{q=q_N}. \tag{2.1}

These ratios are regular matrix Laurent polynomials. On u_j their coefficients are the symmetric Gaussian polynomials

𝖥:uj↦[j+NN]qNuj+N,𝖤:uj↦[m−j+NN]qNuj−N. \mathsf F:u_j\longmapsto {j+N\brack N}_{q_N}u_{j+N},\qquad \mathsf E:u_j\longmapsto {m-j+N\brack N}_{q_N}u_{j-N}. \tag{2.2}

Here boundary maps are zero. This is a regular integral division before specialization, not division of the zero root matrices.

The needed Gaussian evaluation has an elementary proof. The identity

∏j=0a−1(1+Qjz)=∑hQh(h−1)/2[ah]Qzh \prod_{j=0}^{a-1}(1+Q^jz) =\sum_h Q^{h(h-1)/2}{a\brack h}_Q z^h

follows by coefficient induction. For a=AN+s, 0<=s<N, at Q_N the product is (1-(-z)N)A times a product of degree s. Its z^N coefficient, divided by Q_N(N(N-1)/2)=(-1)(N-1), gives [AN+sN]QN=A{AN+s\brack N}_{Q_N}=A. The symmetric Gaussian convention is [aN]q=q−N(a−N)[aN]q2{a\brack N}_q=q^{-N(a-N)}{a\brack N}_{q^2}. Therefore before the beta gauge the signs and coefficients are exactly

𝖥ukN+s=(−1)kN+s(k+1)u(k+1)N+s,𝖤ukN+s=(−1)N(r+1−k)−1−s(r+1−k)u(k−1)N+s. \begin{split} \mathsf F u_{kN+s}&=(-1)^{kN+s}(k+1)u_{(k+1)N+s},\\ \mathsf E u_{kN+s}&=(-1)^{N(r+1-k)-1-s} (r+1-k)u_{(k-1)N+s}. \end{split} \tag{2.3}

The beta ratios then give

𝖥wk,s=(k+1)wk+1,s,𝖤wk,s=τ(r−k+1)wk−1,s,τ=(−1)Nr−1. \mathsf F w_{k,s}=(k+1)w_{k+1,s},\qquad \mathsf E w_{k,s}=\tau(r-k+1)w_{k-1,s},\quad \tau=(-1)^{Nr-1}. \tag{2.4}

Consequently

$$ e_{\rm cl}=\tau\mathsf E,\quad f_{\rm cl}=\mathsf F, \quad h_{\rm cl}w_{k,s}=(r-2k)w_{k,s} \tag{2.5} $$

satisfy [e_cl,f_cl]=h_cl, [h_cl,e_cl]=2e_cl and [h_cl,f_cl]=-2f_cl. They are the ordinary irreducible Sym^r action on k and commute with the root E,F,K head action in (1.3). Their nilpotence order is r+1, so r=N has the degree-N string unavailable in a fixed number of original small-head factors.

This is a SINGLE-COLOR divided action. Divided powers on tensor products have mixed coproduct terms. Its classical multiplicity action must not silently be substituted for that entire tensor action or for a Hopf action on all matrix observables.

3. The actual first-normal block edges recover the same generators

Extend the sign frame (1.2) constantly in q, put Z=QN=q(2N), delta=1-Z, and retain the actual generic matrices. Let F_cross and E_cross be just their between-block entries. They are divisible by delta. With rho=q_N-q_N^(-1), the exact regularized root values are

$$ \begin{split} [F_{\rm cross}/\delta]_{q_N} &=\rho^{-1} f_{\rm cl}\otimes E_{0,N-1},\\ [E_{\rm cross}/\delta]_{q_N} &=(-1)^{r+1}\rho^{-1}e_{\rm cl}\otimes E_{N-1,0}. \end{split} \tag{3.1} $$

To check every sign, the F edge has coefficient (-1)^k[(k+1)N]_q and the E edge has coefficient (-1)^(k-1)[(r+1-k)N]_q. For every positive integer a,

[[aN]q1−QN]qN=(−1)a+1aρ. \left[\frac{[aN]_q}{1-Q^N}\right]_{q_N} =\frac{(-1)^{a+1}a}{\rho}. \tag{3.2}

This follows by factoring Q^(aN)-1 by Q^N-1 before evaluating; no derivative of a singular quotient is inferred from root values. Equations (3.1) follow immediately. In the normal variable h=Log(q/q_N), delta=-2Nh+O(h^2), so the same entries are respectively -2N f_cl/rho and 2(-1)^r N e_cl/rho at first h-order.

Thus the classical source is encoded canonically in the actual quantum-normal block edges. The root decomposition is not a product decomposition of the generic high-color module.

4. A coherent divided source gives exactly the binomial wall row

On the multiplicity space write v_k for its basis. The actual divided operators in (2.5) construct the finite source and co-source

$$ \psi_+=\exp(f_{\rm cl})v_0=\sum_{k=0}^rv_k,\qquad \lambda_+=2^{-r}v_0^*\exp(e_{\rm cl}), \quad \lambda_+(v_k)=2^{-r}{r\choose k}. \tag{4.1} $$

Both exponentials terminate. Direct multiplication gives lambda_+(psi_+)=1. The rank-one coupon e_+=psi_+lambda_+ is idempotent and has trace one. These are Frobenius/divided coherent operators on the ACTUAL high integral color, not an ordinary Sym^r action postulated on the old fixed-N head.

Let J=(J_0,…,J_r) be any array over a characteristic-zero commutative coefficient algebra, and insert its literal diagonal block operator diag(J_k) tensor I_N. Let P_s be any rank-one head coordinate projector and tau_high=Tr/[N(r+1)]. Then

$$ (r+1)\tau_{\rm high} [(e_+\otimes P_s)(\operatorname{diag}J\otimes I_N)] =\frac1{N2^r}\sum_{k=0}^r{r\choose k}J_k. \tag{4.2} $$

For r=N this is EXACTLY the actual wall row B_N(J), including its 1/N and the k=N endpoint. The coupling to the original Green array is explicit: the multiplicity block k takes the original entry J_k, equivalently the Kronecker coupling delta_(k,j). Formula (4.2) is valid pointwise for all remaining flow variables and hence after their finite sums. It applies to polynomial or retained logarithmic moment coefficients as a linear functional; no multiplication or Toeplitz-symbol identification is used.

If P_s is replaced by I_N, the coherent functional has value one on the identity and the right side is N times (4.2). The marked rank-one head accounts for the wall normalization; it must not be dropped while claiming equality of source rows.

This realizes B_N as a marked co-source on an actual larger color. It does not prove that the delta_(k,j) coupling intertwines the second framing current or its entire divided Hopf coproduct. Those equations must still be checked, with their endpoint carries.

The coherent source has a canonical actual whole quantum germ

There is no need to extend the root binomial row arbitrarily. The generic integral divided matrices E_N(q)=E(q)^N/[N]_q! and F_N(q)=F(q)^N/[N]_q! are regular, and preserve every residue s while moving the block index k. On the fixed s-string define

Ψs(q)=exp⁡(FN(q))w0,s,Λs(q)=2−rw0,s*exp⁡(τEN(q)),us(q)=Λs(q)Ψs(q),es(q)=Ψs(q)Λs(q)/us(q). \begin{split} \Psi_s(q)&=\exp(F_N(q))w_{0,s},\\ \Lambda_s(q)&=2^{-r}w_{0,s}^*\exp(\tau E_N(q)),\\ u_s(q)&=\Lambda_s(q)\Psi_s(q),\qquad e_s(q)=\Psi_s(q)\Lambda_s(q)/u_s(q). \end{split} \tag{4.3}

The finite exponentials terminate. At q_N, u_s=1 by (4.1), so its whole germ is a unit, with no root pole canceled by hand. Equation (4.3) is a regular idempotent of trace one. Different s-strings are disjoint, hence E_coh(q)=sum_s e_s(q) is an idempotent of trace N. For the ACTUAL generic high-color scalar long-knot operator,

$$ \frac1N\operatorname{Tr}[E_{\rm coh}(q)C_{\rm high}(q)] =C_{N(r+1)}(Q) \tag{4.4} $$

identically as a whole germ. The marked string e_s(q) gives the exact whole wall weights N−1Λsdiag⁡(Jk)Ψs/usN^{-1}\Lambda_s\operatorname{diag}(J_k)\Psi_s/u_s. At the root these reduce to (4.2). Their quantum derivatives are determined by the ORIGINAL divided matrices and the retained unit u_s, rather than inferred from the numerical binomial row.

Equation (4.4) compares this color with itself. It is not an off-root equality with C_N(Q), and does not make e_s(q) an unrolled intertwiner or prove a current-complex map.

5. The reduced scalar is the original one; whole Q-jets are different

The actual zero-framed long-knot operator on D_m(q) is C_(m+1)(Q) times identity, by the original integral color construction. Its regular root specialization is still scalar. The independently proved all-color identity gives

CN(r+1)(QN)=CN(QN)=KN. C_{N(r+1)}(Q_N)=C_N(Q_N)=K_N. \tag{5.1}

Thus the normalized coherent functional with unmarked head identity returns K_N. Its marked wall version returns K_N/N, as expected from B_N(1)=1/N. The full original ADO normalization is still the old marked line eta_N K_N/N, with its peripheral meridian germ; one does NOT replace it by an imagined eta_(N(r+1))/[N(r+1)] line.

The high-color meridian path before specializing is

$$ M_{\rm high}=-q^{N(r+1)},\quad Y_{\rm high}=M_{\rm high}^2=Z^{r+1},\qquad M_{\rm low}=-q^N,\quad Y_{\rm low}=Z. \tag{5.2} $$

At the root M_high=(-1)^r. In particular r=N uses the other lift at odd N. The one-dimensional shift and its zero-framing phase must be retained in comparing it with the original ideal lift. For the external modified-dimension line the exact sign-chart law is dN(σeu)=σ1−NdN(eu)d_N(\sigma e^u)=\sigma^{1-N}d_N(e^u), with sigma=(-1)^r. Its retained adapter sigma(N-1)=(-1)(r(N-1)) is identically one when r=N. This does not install a new modified trace on the large ordinary Weyl module: the original external d_N line is retained. The fixed-color whole functions C_(N(r+1))(Q) and C_N(Q) have not been identified away from Q_N. Their normal meridian slopes already differ in (5.2), and (3.1) is the actual block-extension normal data. For a nontrivial adequate knot their different quadratic degree spans prove that the two whole Laurent polynomials are unequal.

Consequently this source elevation is an exact ROOT and first-normal MODULE construction, not yet a source-preserving equality of the whole Q-jets needed by Mellin reconstruction. It supplies the missing degree-N wall representation while retaining the precise additional normal comparison which remains to be established.

6. Verification and remaining network map

The following audit checks (1.3), every divided coefficient including both boundaries, the full classical commutators, coherent source and co-source normalization, the literal wall row, and both normal block edges at even, odd and composite levels. The remaining network question is whether the explicit block/Green coupling in (4.2), together with (3.1), transports the FULL actual two-current relative source complex. 7. The actual braiding retains a definite mixed half-Cartan sign

Here is an independent direct crossing and closure proof, with no tensor-product theorem used as a premise. Put n=N(r+1), m=n-1, and let W be the N-dimensional root color

W=D_(N-1) tensor C_(Nr),
H_W(i)=m-2i, 0<=i<N.

In the gauged basis of Section 1 the high color has the same ordinary E,F,K as W on every block, while

H_high(w_(a,i))=H_W(i)-2Na.

The generic universal R specializes integrally: on integral Weyl vectors its kth factorial coefficient is a product of two Gaussian polynomials and [k]_q!. Thus every k>=N term vanishes at q_N; there is no division by [N] at the root. For k<N its nilpotent part preserves the two block indices and is exactly that of W.

The remaining Cartan ratio is

q_N^(-N[a H_W(j)+b H_W(i)]+2N^2ab)
   =epsilon^(a+b), epsilon=(-1)^m.             (7.1)

All head weights have parity m, so this sign is independent of i,j and the transfer. The same calculation applies whether the Cartan is evaluated before or after the nilpotent term, since the head weights change by even integers. Inversion retains the same sign. Consequently, for BOTH checked crossing signs,

Rcheck_high^(+/-)(w_(a,i) tensor w_(b,j))
  =epsilon^(a+b) sum_(k,l)(Rcheck_W^(+/-))_(ij)^(kl)
                                  w_(b,k) tensor w_(a,l). (7.2)

The common highest-weight half-Cartan scalar is the original one on the marked exponential sheet. It is the same for high and W in (7.2); it is not set to one. The sign in (7.1) is genuinely nontrivial for some even/odd levels and block pairs. Thus the factorization is not an unmarked flip on arbitrary inserted arrays. Mixed source or current coupons must carry this sign.

The original root pivot has no additional block factor:

q_N^((1-N)(H_W(i)-2Na))=q_N^((1-N)H_W(i)).       (7.3)

The twist is also the same on every block. Shifting a highest weight m by -2Na changes the ordinary twist exponent h(h+2)/2 by -2Na(m+1)+2N2a2, whose q_N-power is one.

For a CLOSED BRAID whose closure is one knot, the multiplicity index is transported by the permutation without changing along the component. Closure therefore makes it a single value a. Every mixed crossing sign in (7.2) is then epsilon^(2a)=1. Equations (7.3) and the twist calculation show that its reduced zero-framed head scalar is precisely the scalar of W.

To compare W with the original Steinberg color, retain the one-dimensional shift h=Nr. With b braid strands and writhe w, the actual crossing constant is

s_0=q_N^(h^2/2)(-1)^(r(N-1)),
g_h=q_N^((1-N)h)=(-1)^(r(1-N)).

The crossing index coboundaries telescope around the knot. Crossings contribute s_0^w, closed pivots g_h^(b-1), and the specified zero-framing twist contributes (s_0 g_h)^(-w). Their product is g_h^(b-1-w)=1, since the parity of a one-cycle braid permutation gives b-1-w even. This is the same direct phase calculation used in the all-color periodicity proof. It proves for the ACTUAL high-color long-knot operator

C_high(q_N)=K_N I_(N(r+1)).                    (7.4)

In particular a normalized Frobenius source/co-source and the original head cut retain K_N exactly. The full ordinary normalized trace corresponds instead to the normalized trace on the multiplicity factor; both evaluate (7.4) to K_N. The wall coupon with a rank-one head projector has normalization 1/N, precisely as in (4.2), rather than normalization one. The old external modified-dimension line and its sign law remain those of Section 5.

This proof compares actual ROOT periods. Whole generic colors and their quantum-normal slopes are different as (5.2) records. Nor does (7.4) identify inserted Green networks: their block-changing normal arrows and mixed signs require their literal couplings.

8. Coverage

Divided-power quantum groups and Steinberg/Frobenius tensor structures are established representation-theoretic constructions. For coverage, Sutton–Tubbenhauer–Wedrich–Zhu, 2105.07724 studies SL2 tilting modules, quantum-number root conventions and integral divided-power models, including the complex root-of-unity case. No tensor-product, trace, or braiding proof from that paper is a premise here. The calculations above prove the explicit all-parity module signs, actual first-normal edge, coherent wall coupon, its whole high-color source germ, and the original marked scalar adapter in the conventions used by the actual knot state.
