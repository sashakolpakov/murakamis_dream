Closed open-port kernel of the original terminal positive packet

29 October 2026. This is an all-input formula for the terminal map in the complete-module positive Gram. It does not substitute the figure-eight prefix. Both the recorded physical color and the complete multiplicity input remain open. The answer consists of finite factorial/Newton transforms and an explicit first-order factorial Green register. Its kernel is retained.

1. Conventions and the open matrix

Put

q=eπi/N,Q=q2,d=N−1,x=qd,Fa=(Q;Q)a,sa=|Fa|,κ=qd2/2. q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad Q=q^2,\quad d=N-1,\quad x=q^d,\quad F_a=(Q;Q)_a,\quad s_a=|F_a|,\quad \kappa=q^{d^2/2}.

All factorial indices below lie between 0 and d. In particular none of the displayed denominators vanishes. We use the original balanced heads, coproducts, full checked crossing R, ordinary cap ell, and complete map I_w of the multiplicity theorem. The basis of W is e_j=|j,d-j>. Thus

D(w)=Iw|d⟩,Tn(w)=(ℓ12⊗⟨d|)(I⊗R2)Πmiddle=nD(w). D(w)=I_w|d\rangle,\qquad T_n(w)=(\ell_{12}\otimes\langle d|)(I\otimes R^2) \Pi_{\mathrm{middle}=n}D(w). \tag{1}

The cap is ℓa,d−a=x−aQ−a/N\ell_{a,d-a}=x^{-a}Q^{-a}/N, and R includes kappa. This definition uses the genuine whole Hopf closure. It does not replace an arbitrary marked coupon by an ordinary cap.

Define the finite scalar register

P0=1,Pt=(1−Qt)2Pt−1+Qt,Pt=Ft2∑v=0tQvFv2. P_0=1,\qquad P_t=(1-Q^t)^2P_{t-1}+Q^t, \qquad P_t=F_t^2\sum_{v=0}^t\frac{Q^v}{F_v^2}. \tag{2}

The following is the closed answer for EVERY input column j:

Tn,j=(−1)nxj−dQ−n(n+3)/2FnFd−j∑t=0min⁡(n,j)Qt(Q−n;Q)t(Q−j;Q)tFt4Pt. \boxed{\displaystyle T_{n,j}=(-1)^n x^{j-d}Q^{-n(n+3)/2} \frac{F_n}{F_{d-j}}\, \sum_{t=0}^{\min(n,j)} \frac{Q^t(Q^{-n};Q)_t(Q^{-j};Q)_t}{F_t^4}\,P_t .} \tag{3}

There is no unknown source value in this formula. In particular it specifies the map before a preceding braid is supplied. It holds at even, odd, prime, and composite levels.

Let V be the lower triangular finite Newton matrix Vn,t=(Q−n;Q)tV_{n,t}=(Q^{-n};Q)_t, set

Dn,nrow=(−1)nQ−n(n+3)/2Fn,Dj,jcol=xj−d/Fd−j. D^{\mathrm{row}}_{n,n}=(-1)^nQ^{-n(n+3)/2}F_n,\qquad D^{\mathrm{col}}_{j,j}=x^{j-d}/F_{d-j}.

Then (3) is the exact coherent congruence factorization

T=DrowVdiag⁡(QtPtFt4)V𝖳Dcol. T=D^{\mathrm{row}}\,V\, \operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{Q^tP_t}{F_t^4}\right) V^{\mathsf T}D^{\mathrm{col}}. \tag{4}

The transpose is a bilinear transpose, not an adjoint. Consequently the diagonally rescaled kernel is symmetric. Positivity is supplied by T†TT^\dagger T, not by declaring (4) positive.

2. A lowest-column crossing identity

Let A be the physical pair Casimir on W, and put L=κ−1R12|WL=\kappa^{-1}R_{12}|_W. Its exact entries are

Li,j={x−d+hQ(d−j)(d−i)FiFjFhFd−jFd−i,h=i+j−d≥0,0,i+j<d. L_{i,j}=\begin{cases} x^{-d+h}Q^{(d-j)(d-i)} \dfrac{F_iF_j}{F_hF_{d-j}F_{d-i}},&h=i+j-d\geq0,\\ 0,&i+j<d. \end{cases} \tag{5}

The full lowest intertwiner column is

D(w)=∑k=0dF12kFkLw⊗|d−k⟩. \boxed{\displaystyle D(w)=\sum_{k=0}^d\frac{F_{12}^{\,k}}{F_k}\,Lw \otimes|d-k\rangle .} \tag{6}

Here the operator F12 is distinguished from the scalar factorial F_k. For a proof, first extract the last-color-d component of D and call the resulting map L0. Since D is lowest for F123, its other components are fixed by L0: on pair degree d+k+1,

K12−1(1−Qd−k)=−(1−Qk+1). K_{12}^{-1}(1-Q^{d-k})=-(1-Q^{k+1}).

Thus the successive coefficients in (6) are exactly 1. There is no omitted endpoint term: F12N=0F_{12}^N=0.

We next identify L0 without assuming a spectral basis. It commutes with A because A commutes with E12, F12, and K12 in the defining highest and lowest constructions. Let J be the actual highest-chart anti-Pascal matrix. The already proved original source identity is

J(xj)j=0d=ed,J𝖳=J. J(x^j)_{j=0}^d=e_d,\qquad J^{\mathsf T}=J. \tag{7}

On first physical color 0 the highest column is Jw. The divided F23dF_{23}^d coefficient from |b,d-b> to |d,d> is x^b: expand the two q-commuting terms and use FbFd−b=N(−1)bQb(b+1)/2F_bF_{d-b}=N(-1)^bQ^{b(b+1)/2}. The additional first-head factor in F123^d is x^(-d). Hence

e0𝖳L0=x−d(xb)b𝖳J=x−ded𝖳. e_0^{\mathsf T}L_0=x^{-d}(x^b)_b^{\mathsf T}J =x^{-d}e_d^{\mathsf T}. \tag{8}

The original R commutes with A and its strict transfer formula gives the identical first row for kappa^(-1) R. Every off-diagonal Jacobi coefficient of A is nonzero: it is −(1−Qj)2-(1-Q^j)^2, 1<=j<=d. Therefore the rows e0𝖳Are_0^{\mathsf T}A^r, 0<=r<=d, span all rows. Two commuting maps with the same first row are equal. This proves L0=L and (6), including all Jordan channels.

For reference, direct divided-power extraction also gives the finite Newton/Euler expression

L=∑c=0d(−1)cqc(c+2)Fc2F12cE12c. L=\sum_{c=0}^d \frac{(-1)^c q^{c(c+2)}}{F_c^2}\,F_{12}^{\,c}E_{12}^{\,c}. \tag{9}

It agrees with (5); it is not a completion of the nilpotent currents.

3. Forced terminal transfers and squared Pascal factorization

The total physical degree of D(w) is 2d. At recorded middle color n, write its physical states as

(d−r,n,d−n+r),0≤r≤n. (d-r,n,d-n+r),\qquad 0\leq r\leq n.

The two terminal crossings and final cap force the first crossing’s transfer to n-r and the second crossing’s transfer to 0. Substituting the original signed-color formula, with both common Cartan factors, gives the terminal coefficient

cn,r=x−nN(−1)n−rQ−n(n+1)/2−n+r(r+1)/2FnFr. c_{n,r}=\frac{x^{-n}}N(-1)^{n-r} Q^{-n(n+1)/2-n+r(r+1)/2}\frac{F_n}{F_r}. \tag{10}

In simplifying it we used exactly κ2=xd\kappa^2=x^d, not a deleted Cartan scalar. This is the only path admitted by the original finite support for these fixed physical colors.

For a low-pair input i=d-t, the coefficient of |d−r,n⟩|d-r,n\rangle in F12n−r/Fn−rF_{12}^{n-r}/F_{n-r} is

x−(n−t)Q(n−t)(d−t)Fd−rFnFt−rFn−tFd−tFt,0≤r≤t≤n. x^{-(n-t)}Q^{(n-t)(d-t)} \frac{F_{d-r}F_n} {F_{t-r}F_{n-t}F_{d-t}F_t}, \qquad 0\leq r\leq t\leq n. \tag{11}

Multiplying (10) and (11), reflecting F_(d-r), and summing r leaves the finite convolution

∑r=0tQr(r+1)Fr2Ft−r=∑v=0tQvFv2=PtFt2. \sum_{r=0}^t\frac{Q^{r(r+1)}}{F_r^2F_{t-r}} =\sum_{v=0}^t\frac{Q^v}{F_v^2} =\frac{P_t}{F_t^2}. \tag{12}

A self-contained proof of the first equality is as follows. Let E(z)=sum_r Q{r(r+1)}zr/F_r^2 and U(z)=sum_k z^k/F_k, as formal series modulo z^N. Their coefficient recurrences imply

E(z)−2E(Qz)+(1−Q2z)E(Q2z)=0,U(Qz)=(1−z)U(z). E(z)-2E(Qz)+(1-Q^2z)E(Q^2z)=0,\qquad U(Qz)=(1-z)U(z).

For J(z)=E(z)U(z), compare coefficients through degree d to obtain

Pt=(1+Q−2Qt+Q2t)Pt−1−Q(1−Qt−1)2Pt−2. P_t=(1+Q-2Q^t+Q^{2t})P_{t-1} -Q(1-Q^{t-1})^2P_{t-2}.

Here P_t=F_t2[zt]J, P0=1, P1=1-Q+Q^2. The recurrence factors exactly: Zt=Pt−(1−Qt)2Pt−1Z_t=P_t-(1-Q^t)^2P_{t-1} satisfies Z_t=Q Z_(t-1) and Z0=1. This proves (2) and (12) at every required index.

Let Rev e_i=e_(d-i), and define

Un,t(2)=Q−ntFn−t[nt]Q2(t≤n), U^{(2)}_{n,t}=Q^{-nt}F_{n-t}{n\brack t}_Q^2\quad(t\leq n), Dn,nout=(−1)nQ−n(n+3)/2,Dt,tin=(−1)txtQt(t+1)/2. D^{\mathrm{out}}_{n,n}=(-1)^nQ^{-n(n+3)/2},\qquad D^{\mathrm{in}}_{t,t}=(-1)^t x^tQ^{t(t+1)/2}.

Both D’s are unitary diagonal matrices. Equations (6), (10)–(12) give the second useful all-exterior factorization

T=1NDoutU(2)Dindiag⁡(Pt)RevL. \boxed{\displaystyle T=\frac1N D^{\mathrm{out}}U^{(2)}D^{\mathrm{in}} \operatorname{diag}(P_t)\,\mathrm{Rev}\,L.} \tag{13}

To recover (3), use the support t<=j in (5), reflect F_(d-t), and use

(Q−n;Q)t(Q−j;Q)t=Q−(n+j)t+t(t−1)FnFjFn−tFj−t. (Q^{-n};Q)_t(Q^{-j};Q)_t =Q^{-(n+j)t+t(t-1)}\frac{F_nF_j}{F_{n-t}F_{j-t}}.

This also proves every entry of (4), rather than only its row sum.

4. Kernel, open Gram, and exact angle incidence

All matrices flanking diag(P) in either (4) or (13) are invertible. Consequently

rank⁡T=#{t:Pt≠0},ker⁡T=L−1Revspan⁡{et:Pt=0}. \operatorname{rank}T=\#\{t:P_t\ne0\},\qquad \ker T=L^{-1}\mathrm{Rev}\, \operatorname{span}\{e_t:P_t=0\}. \tag{14}

For N=6, Q^2-Q+1=0, the successive values P0,…,P5 are

1, 0, Q-1, 4Q-5, -4Q-12, 17-13Q.

Thus the actual complex packet has rank 5. The positive completion in the terminal Gram note is essential; an inverse of T would be invalid. No lower bound on nonzero P_t is assumed here. The elementary uniform upper bound |P_t|<=(4^(t+1)-1)/3 follows from (2), but is not sharp.

For every input w, not only a specified knot prefix, (13) gives the SOURCE-EXACT positive energy

∑n|Tnw|2=1N2∑n|∑t≤nUn,t(2)Dt,tinPt(Lw)d−t|2. \sum_n|T_nw|^2=\frac1{N^2}\sum_n \left|\sum_{t\leq n}U^{(2)}_{n,t} D^{\mathrm{in}}_{t,t}P_t(Lw)_{d-t}\right|^2. \tag{15}

All interference between distinct t is retained. Equivalently the open Gram is

T†T=1N2L†Revdiag⁡(P‾)(Din)†(U(2))†U(2)Dindiag⁡(P)RevL. T^\dagger T=\frac1{N^2}L^\dagger\mathrm{Rev}\, \operatorname{diag}(\bar P) (D^{\mathrm{in}})^\dagger (U^{(2)})^\dagger U^{(2)}D^{\mathrm{in}} \operatorname{diag}(P)\mathrm{Rev}\,L. \tag{16}

This is an explicit finite matrix on the original complete module. Neither dephasing the t register nor dropping its P_t multiplier preserves (15) in general.

There is also a genuine local angle weight, with its metric fully specified. For k=n-t and wd(a,b,c)=1/(sasbsc)w_d(a,b,c)=1/(s_as_bs_c), define the THREE-PORT tensor

Ũn;t,k=δn,t+ksn−3/2st3/2sk1/2Un,t(2). \widetilde U_{n;t,k}=\delta_{n,t+k}\, s_n^{-3/2}s_t^{3/2}s_k^{1/2}U^{(2)}_{n,t}.

The exact reflection identity gives

1N|Ũn;t,k|2=wd(t,k,d−n),t+k+(d−n)=d. \boxed{\displaystyle \frac1N|\widetilde U_{n;t,k}|^2 =w_d(t,k,d-n),\qquad t+k+(d-n)=d.} \tag{17}

Its phase is the ACTUAL phase of Q−ntFn−t[nt]Q2Q^{-nt}F_{n-t}{n\brack t}_Q^2. The factors sn−3/2st3/2sk1/2s_n^{-3/2}s_t^{3/2}s_k^{1/2} are recorded length metrics; they cannot be applied to the two matrix ports while silently deleting the k port or the adjacent source metric. In terms of the existing length gauge A_a=(s_a2/N)(1/8), this factor is N1/4An−6At6Ak2N^{1/4}A_n^{-6}A_t^6A_k^2. Length zero has s0=1 and is retained.

Thus the open packet contains a precise angle-simplex tensor and a coherent original factorial Green register. This is not a proof that every full packet energy is a geometric angle partition function: the complete prefix Lw, the P register, and the exterior length metrics still have to be controlled together.

5. Scope and audit

The construction applies to arbitrary original prefixes and to arbitrary vectors in the complete W at the selected cut. It is not restricted to the one Casimir source plane. It proves a closed formula and a coherent energy identity; it does not prove a sharp broad-knot upper estimate. The known identity T_n(R12^(-1)R23 p)=|F_n|^2 is an additional prefix-specific cancellation, not a definition of this T.

Audit constructs H, D, the two terminal original crossings, and their full physical projectors directly from the original generators. It compares every input column with (3), (6), (9), and (13); checks the exact rank and shifted-Durfee/Green identities; and verifies the literal angle metrics separately over the complex principal roots.

The finite audit checks ten complete original open matrices at N=2,…,10,12, including every input column, original row-sum source and benchmark packet; eleven complex-root three-port angle-metric families.

The quantitative Green companion combines the Pascal phases into the simpler exact form T=N(-1)diag(xn)R diag(P_(d-j))R. It uses the actual polynomial endpoint P_d to control all Green colors, and proves a one-crossing capacity bound for T R^(-1) with an arbitrary preceding input. It does not establish a broad sharp knot-volume bound.
