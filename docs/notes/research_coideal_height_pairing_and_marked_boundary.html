A signed height pairing gives a positive metric with polynomial source costs

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The actual tensor Krein form, after an explicit half-Cartan twist, is diagonal in the complete nested coideal frame. Its diagonal entries are signed cosecants independent of neighboring heights. Taking the absolute values of this coordinate diagonal gives a specified positive metric. In that metric the actual crossing coefficients have their sewn volume rate without additional graph weight corrections, and both original boundary packets have polynomial norms.

An original marked cap followed by a crossing between its two legs has a further exact all-channel formula: a known quadratic phase times a positive weight between polynomial bounds. This is a boundary coefficient estimate, not a lower bound for its contraction with an arbitrary coherent exterior.

This explicit positive path metric has polynomial original input and marked-output costs on the globally conserved source channel.
