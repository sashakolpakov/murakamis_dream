The original marked boundary source and its transverse stationary point

30 October 2026. This concerns the specified original six-type boundary source, at fixed real integer channel labels approaching the fold. Its actual marked cap cancels the limiting linear magnetic force. This gives a nearby transverse stationary point and a constructed local period. It does not identify their coefficient in the original support cycle. An independent coning calculation below identifies its absolute leading real rate with Vol(Q)/pi, where Q is the actual right-angled half-polyhedron; it does not use a finite-r sheet assertion.

The logarithmic source coordinates, exact scalar factors and original mark are part of the statement. No additional winding character is introduced in the marked-source conclusion.

1. The literal regular circle lift has no extra root character

Put d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, 0<=a<=floor(d/2), m=d-a, s=d-2a, and F_j=(Q;Q)j. Write S_j for the positive sine factorial and beta_a=S(2a)/S_a^2. Retain the original constants

h0_a=k_a N beta_a, gamma_a=q^(2-a^2)/N^2,
P_s(T)=product_(j=a)^(m-1)(T-Q^j).

All heads in this section use the specified moving-factorial continuation of the regular original source. Let sigma=i+j, r=sigma-m, x=2a-r=d+a-sigma, T=Q^sigma. At the root its ordinary channel cup is exactly

p_(2a)(x)=F_s q^[2m(d+a)] T^(-a)/P_s(T).           (1)

This is a meromorphic identity, including its removable specializations. Indeed the fixed-root factorial reflection first gives

p_(d-s)(x)=F_s exp(pi i x) q^[(s-1)x]
                  /product_(j=1)^s(1-Q^(x+j)).

At x=d+a-sigma its denominator is T^(-s)P_s(T), and exp(pi i x)q1=q^(2mx). This proves (1) with its literal logarithmic character.

The actual generic reversal, specialized only after it is used, gives

W_a(d-j,d-i;2a-r)=q^[s(a-r)] W_a(i,j;r),
pV(d-j)pV(d-i)=N^2 q^(-d sigma).

Thus the literal categorical dual is retained in the product. For an output row R and input row S at the same total sigma, substitute

W_a(R,sigma-R;r)
   =(-1)^m q^[d(R-sigma)+m(a+1)] H_m(Q^R,Q^(sigma-R))/F_m

and the same formula for S. Use the exact root collision H_m=P_s(T)H_a and H_a=q^(a sigma)K_a R_a, where K_a=F_(2a)/F_a^2 and R_a has its actual monic normalization. The remaining head dependence of h0_a W_a pi_a is

q^[d(R+S)-2m sigma] P_s(T)
   R_a(q^(sigma-2R);c_sigma)R_a(q^(sigma-2S);c_sigma).

The entire coefficient C_a in the absolute regulator normalization has precisely this dependence, because its residual sine product obeys

V_a(d-sigma)=q^(-sd-s sigma)P_s(T).

The ratio of the two scalars, retaining gamma_a and h0_a, reduces by F_j=(-i)^j q2S_j and S_j S_(d-j)=N to

q^[-2N(-2N+a+3)]=1.

Consequently, as the SPECIFIED root continuations,

h0_a W_a pi_a = C_a.                               (2)

This uses both actual LARGE numerators and the actual dual cups. It does not differentiate a root collision, identify moving-q germs, or replace the separately recorded bare canceled/turned continuation by this one. The retained half twist is a fixed-channel scalar on both sides and changes no magnetic derivative.

There is a useful exact homogeneous form, valid for EVERY folded label; the positive face-polynomial theorem gives its independent local proof, both-parity carry convention, full coefficient positivity and the physical support-zero audit. Define

D_a(x,b,y)=det[bI+(x/2)H_-+(y/2)H_+],
Ptilde_s(x,y)=product_(j=a+1)^(d-a)(x-Q^j y).

For positive formal face diagonals x=L0, y=L2 and the two middle diagonals b,c, use the marked raw clocks -x/b,-b/y and -x/c,-c/y. Then (2) is exactly

h0_a W_a pi_a
  =k_a beta_a^2 D_a(x,b,y)D_a(x,c,y)Ptilde_s(x,y)
                                        /(bc)^(d/2). (2a)

Indeed H_a=(-q-1)a K_a D_a/L2^a and P_s(Qsigma)=q(-2s)L2^(-s)Ptilde_s. Substitute these into the calculation above. Its remaining scalar phase is (-1)^d q3=(-1)^(d+s)=1. In this computation qsigma=-q-1 sqrt(x/y) and the two negative physical raw clocks have their canonical argument pi. The identity extends with those marked half powers; at odd N the powers are integral. This is not a replacement of an even-level marked square root by an unrecorded principal branch. The numerator in (2a) has degree 2a+s=d.

2. Source coordinates and the local angle-force input

Fix the labeled certified diagram and the common boundary sample r_frame=N rho, s_frame=exp(-2pi i alpha)-1. The parameters range in the compact sets of the common boundary chart. Keep the actual physical half-index shifts, auxiliary integer offsets, all original turns, the ordinary or marked exposed cap, and fixed labels with S=sum_i(d-2a_i)=o(N).

Use LOGARITHMIC CLOCK potentials Z_f, not normalized head potentials. Their homogeneous physical variations are

delta h_e=N(delta Z_left-delta Z_right)/(2pi i).    (3)

The literal face-lattice theorem proves that the integer face differences give the original primitive homogeneous lattice. A common additive constant is immaterial. Exactly two faces have constant prefixes; write Qcal for the other, quadratic faces. The actual sample has Z_f=Log L_f with all source marks retained.

For clarity the local analytic input needed here is stated explicitly. At an ordered white triangle with faces f0,f1,f2, let theta_j be the angle at the center of its j-th circle in the tangent-circle triangle. A constant-prefix line has limiting angle zero. At the common sample, the normalized logarithmic derivative of its literal W numerator and white monomial tends uniformly to

sum_(j=0)^2 theta_j dZ_fj/(2pi) - dZ_f1/2.          (4)

The theta sum is pi. This statement is obtained from the actual finite cores, not from a finite-r identification of their sheets with the geometric critical branch. For 222, with alpha_=a_f0/(2a_f1), beta_=a_f2/(2a_f1), its derivatives are

theta_0/(2pi)=atan(2alpha_*/sqrt(2alpha_*+2beta_*+4alpha_*beta_*))/pi,
theta_2/(2pi)=atan(2beta_*/sqrt(2alpha_*+2beta_*+4alpha_*beta_*))/pi.

The actual H Ward equations and positive finite determinant select these angles. The 220 and 022 formulas are their one-line limits. The 202 positive polynomial gives theta_0=2atan(sqrt(T_raw)) and theta_2=pi-theta_0. The 200 and 002 top terms give respectively (theta_0,theta_1,theta_2)=(pi,0,0) and (0,0,pi). All root partner products and near-fold cup corrections are retained; their normalized first-derivative errors tend to zero when S=o(N).

Here are source proofs of the force limits, including the boundary cases. Put p=N^-1 partial_logU Log H and r=N^-1 partial_logV Log H. The exact two white Ward equations, the O(N) second-log-derivative bounds, and Q=exp(2pi i/N) give at the fold

(1-U)t_U+U(1-V)t_V=1+UV,
(1-U)/t_U+U(1-V)/t_V=1+UV,
t_U=exp(2pi i p), t_V=exp(2pi i r).               (4a)

The errors before taking the limit are O((s+1)/N) on a 222 compact. Its positive matrix gives p,r in a compact subset of (0,1/2): each relevant positive trace has a bulk lower bound, as does its complement. For U=-u,V=-v the unique upper-semicircle solution is

p=atan(u sqrt(v)/sqrt(1+u+uv))/pi,
r=atan(sqrt(v(1+u+uv)))/pi.

Its triangle discriminant is positive and separated from zero on the specified compacts. Thus it gives uniform derivative convergence, not just an identity satisfied by a putative geometric saddle. The elementary circle cosine law makes these exactly the angles in (4).

For 220 the determinant-rate theorem extends to its zero second coefficient uniformly using H_+<=2H_-+8I. Its fixed holomorphic logarithmic bounds give derivative convergence. At the fold the derivative integral is

integral_0^(1/2) [1-cos(pi t)/sqrt(cos^2(pi t)+u)]dt
   =atan(sqrt(u))/pi.

The last equality follows by y=sin(pi t). The other H force tends to 1/2. Exact reciprocity gives 022. For 202 the positive coefficient ratios give the mode equation

tan(pi kappa)=sqrt(T_raw)sin(pi a/N)
                         /[1+sqrt(T_raw)cos(pi a/N)].

The already proved Gaussian weight bound and variance O(N) imply mean/N-kappa=O(N^-1/2), uniformly. This tends to the stated atan formula. Finally the exact top terms of 200/002 have H forces (m/N,m/N)+O(N^-1) and (0,0)+O(N^-1), respectively. Their nonzero Bessel remainders have bounded logarithmic derivatives. Multiplication by the original white monomial subtracts d/(2N) from the second H force. The large partner and root cup corrections cost O((s+1)/N) in normalized first derivatives, using the actual separated factors. These arguments prove (4), locally uniformly with its marked phases. No uniform quantitative rate beyond o(1) is needed below.

3. The integer covector is computed from the original turns

At a literal physical cup or cap write its first-head variable as its actual signed face difference plus the affine offset. The exact root cup pV(x)=N q(-d2)q^(dx), and its ordered inverse cap, contribute in the normalized limit one half of a SIGNED face difference. Let epsilon_(b,f) be that signed incidence for the original turn b. This is read from the cup or cap entry; it is not chosen afterward. Let chi_f be the integer face-incidence vector of the retained marked versus ordinary cap character. For the specified mark this follows directly from Xi_N(x)=N^-1 q(-d2)Q^(dx). Here x is the SECOND ordered cap head: the original cap and mark are q^(di)/N and q(dj)/N2 at (i,j)=(d-x,x). If u is the cap’s outside face and v its inside face, the raw second clock is -L_v/L_u; the mark therefore contributes +1 at v and -1 at u. A retained literal cyclic turn is treated by its actual monomial in the same way.

Let M_f count the middle white corners at the trivalent vertices. Then the limiting normalized logarithmic derivative of the original full root product S_N is the explicit covector

nu_f=1_(f in Qcal)-M_f/2
                +(1/2)sum_b epsilon_(b,f)+chi_f.    (5)

The angle contribution is 1 on a bounded circle because the consecutive tangent-circle triangles sweep its full 2pi angle. It is zero at each of the two constant-prefix lines. This is the actual circle-packing incidence, not a sum of independently optimized vertices.

Every nu_f is an INTEGER. To prove this in the original uncontracted Morse graph, traverse the closed boundary of a white face and record whether its direction agrees with the source-edge orientation. At a middle corner the two main legs are both incoming or both outgoing, so this agreement flips. At a main-matching corner one leg is incoming and one outgoing, so it does not flip. It flips at a literal cup or cap. Returning to the start implies that M_f plus the number of turns at f is even. The sign of a turn is irrelevant modulo two, proving the integrality in (5).

This argument is unchanged by the literal pi reduction. Before that reduction dualization reverses all THREE ports; each adjacent face meets two of these local turns. Deleting them preserves turn parity. Algebraically its cup/physical-inverse-cup ratio has no leading magnetic derivative at the fold: when 2a=d its q exponent is

d[(d-r)-(2d-i-j)]=d(m-d),

which is label-only. The exact near-fold remainder is not deleted. Thus the parity calculation and the reduced two-W calculation of (4) refer to the same source.

Also sum_f nu_f=0. There are 2R white vertices and R quadratic faces; the total middle count is 2R, while every turn and mark incidence has sum zero. Hence (5) defines an integral covector on face potentials modulo their common constant.

For the original flat-plat presentation the signed count is stronger. At a horizontal slice let the gaps between its 2k ordered heads carry the actual white-region labels. A box in slot a removes the old middle gap a+1 at its pi vertex and introduces a new one at its W vertex. Each odd gap begins at a bottom cup and ends at a top cap. Every bounded segment of such a region has exactly two endpoints: middle vertices or inside cups/caps. The internal even gaps begin and end in the exterior. Each must be met by at least one box: otherwise the plat separates across that gap, contrary to the one-main-component hypothesis. Its bounded intermediate segments have two middle endpoints. Thus, with the LITERAL cup signs, every bounded face has

M_f-turn_f=2,  turn_f=sum_b epsilon_(b,f).

The exterior has M_out=2(k-1) and turn_out=2k, hence index -2. An inside cup/cap contributes -1 to turn and its outside contributes +1; this follows directly from its raw first-head ratio -L_out/L_in. Consequently the exact physical monomial after (2a) is

L_out^d product_(bounded f) L_f^(-d).             (5a)

The two constant faces are precisely this exterior and the inside face of the marked cap. The mark contributes -d at the former and +d at the latter. In fact its scalar is exactly

Xi_N=N^-1 (L_in/L_out)^d,                         (5b)

since q(-d2)(-q-1)d=(-1)^d q^(-Nd)=1. This proves

nu_f=0 for EVERY face of the original marked source. (5c)

The ordinary closed source instead has the two charges +1 at the exterior and -1 at the other constant face. They are canceled by the actual mark, not by an invented winding choice.

For the specified compact parameter sets, (4) therefore implies

(1/N)d Log S_N(Z_N) = o(1)                         (6)

uniformly for shrinking folded deficits. Fixed-channel h0, gamma and half-twist phases have zero magnetic derivative but stay in S_N.

4. Retained normalization and the distinction from a winding change

The preceding signed count also records the complete formal-face normalization. On the positive face-log branch each original bottom cup has scalar N q(-d2/2), and each ordinary top cap has scalar N^-1 q(d2/2); their k pairs cancel. Equation (5b) supplies the remaining 1/N. Hence the uncontracted original marked sector is

N^-1 product_i[k_(a_i) beta_(a_i)^2 omega_(a_i)^epsilon_i]
   * product_i[D_(a_i,in)D_(a_i,out)Ptilde_(s_i)]
   /product_(quadratic f)L_f^d.                   (7)

The original exterior framing line remains separate and unchanged. The identities extend with the specified source marks. Formula (7) does not itself convert a physical finite sum to an unrestricted face torus, or settle its support and alias conditions.

For comparison, if one keeps the ordinary cap, or uses another already proved presentation with the integer cochain (5), an exact lattice-one winding change can be specified as follows. This comparison is NOT used to obtain (5c).

Choose an INTEGER affine source origin h^0 for the fixed labels and support stratum. Its corresponding potential origin Z^0 includes the actual half-index constants. Every original integer source state has

Z-Z^0=(2pi i/N)n,  n integral modulo constants.

The entire character

C_nu,N(Z)=exp[-N sum_f nu_f(Z_f-Z_f^0)]

equals one at every such state. Multiplying the original summand by this displayed character preserves its original finite sum, and all residues on its original small node tori. It does not equate differently transported support chains. The affine-origin factor exp(N nu dot Z^0) is retained, even when its real logarithm has leading size.

Define the particular sector logarithm

g_N(Z)=Log S_N(Z)-N nu dot(Z-Z^0).                  (8)

For the original MARKED source nu=0 and g_N=Log S_N exactly. For the comparison presentation its normalized gradient at the boundary sample tends to zero after the displayed explicit correction. The subtracted character can change the real off-grid action. Accordingly (8) is a specified winding representative, not an assertion that the original global cycle selects this representative or that its critical value is the marked geometric volume. The exact identity (2) prevents an additional accidental circle character; it does not compute an unproved global winding coefficient.

5. Local transverse critical point and a constructed local period

Fix the two neighboring original physical heads from the primitive slice theorem. Equivalently take its fixed integral complementary log-clock basis C and write Z=Z_N+Cw, w in C^n, n=R-1. The existing rank theorem and finite core curvature give, in this basis, a real positive reference Hessian bounded between cN I and CN I. The exact finite clocks and near-fold corrections change it by o(N). On a fixed complex neighborhood all fixed derivatives of order at least two are O(N). These statements include both parities.

Set e_N=|N^-1 partial_w g_N(0)|, so e_N tends uniformly to zero by (6). The Hessian divided by N has inverse bounded uniformly, and its derivative is bounded on a smaller fixed ball. Newton’s contraction, using that Hessian at zero, therefore gives a unique zero w_N in a ball of radius C e_N. Explicitly the Newton remainder is at most C|w|^2, so the map sends that ball into itself and has contraction constant at most C e_N for sufficiently large N. Thus

partial_w g_N(w_N)=0,    |w_N|<=C e_N=o(1).         (9)

This is a transverse sector-critical point; it does not assert that the finite-label geometric sample itself is stationary. Nor does it assert that the two remaining finite framing forces vanish. Without a quantitative rate for (6), the head displacement is only o(N).

Let H_N be the w Hessian at w_N. Its real part remains at least cN I. For fixed sufficiently small eta>0 the oriented local contour w=w_N+i x, x in [-eta,eta]^n, obeys

Re[g_N(w_N+i x)-g_N(w_N)]<=-cN|x|^2.

Taylor’s theorem proves this from the Hessian and O(N) third derivative. The standard rescaling x=N^-1/2 y can here be verified directly: the cubic correction is odd and integrates to zero on the symmetric box against the even complex Gaussian; the quartic correction and square of the cubic have relative size O(N^-1), and the remaining tails are exponentially small. Uniform higher derivative bounds give

integral_(w_N+i[-eta,eta]^n) exp(g_N(w)) dw
  = i^n (2pi)^(n/2) exp(g_N(w_N))/sqrt(det H_N)
                                       *[1+O(N^-1)]. (10)

The square root is the continuation from the positive reference matrix. All source scalars and the affine-origin character remain in the displayed exponential. If the chosen flat source measure is the actual unscaled free-head measure, the exact coordinate Jacobian contributes (N/(2pi i))^n. Any cotangent kernels or additional boundary currents must be retained separately; they are not implicitly replaced by that flat measure.

Equation (10) is a nonzero explicitly constructed LOCAL period. No intersection with the original finite-support cycle, residue transport, mixed-color comparison, or asymptotic for the fully contracted source is inferred.

6. The absolute rate from the actual coned polyhedron

This section supplies an absolute geometric normalization without identifying the finite-r selected sheets with a geometric critical branch. The potential and coning identities themselves require only the connected certified right-angled graph and a marked main edge; they do not require that all main strands form one component. The one-main-component hypothesis remains in the earlier finite-r six-type boundary-chart and local-period assertions. Let a_f>0 be the actual curvatures of the bounded white circles in the marked coordinate p=infinity, and set a_f=0 at its two white lines. A common positive scale is immaterial. For one shaded triangle write its three curvatures as (x,b,y), and let

F(x,b,y)=lim_(a/N->1/2) N^-1 Log D_a(x,b,y).

The limit and its first derivatives follow from the actual finite determinant rate and the preceding boundary limits. It is homogeneous in the logarithmic sense F(tx,tb,ty)=F(x,b,y)+(1/2)log t. For three positive curvatures set

Delta=sqrt(xb+by+yx), theta_j=2atan(a_j/Delta).

These are the center-triangle angles, their sum is pi, and (4) says partial_log a_j F=theta_j/(2pi). Consequently

V_tri=2pi F-sum_j theta_j log a_j

has differential -sum_j log(tan(theta_j/2))dtheta_j. The common log Delta disappears because sum_j dtheta_j=0. Define the usual Lobachevsky function by Lambda(t)=-integral_0^t log|2sin u|du. Direct differentiation now gives

V_tri=2 sum_j[Lambda(theta_j/2)
                    +Lambda((pi-theta_j)/2)] + constant. (11)

The constant is ZERO, with the source normalization still retained. Indeed at (x,0,0), D_a=(x/2)^a det H_-. The exact original LDL pivots give

det H_-=product_(n=1)^a
       sin(n pi/N)/[cos(n pi/N)sin((2n-1)pi/N)].

Their normalized logarithm tends to (1/2)log2, by the elementary log-sine Riemann sum. Thus F(x,0,0)=(1/2)log x and V_tri=0. This is also the continuous boundary value of the determinant rate: set the second matrix coefficient to zero by H_+<=2H_-+8I, then let the identity coefficient tend to zero in its explicit rate integral. After subtracting (1/2)log alpha its integrand is dominated by C+2|log cos(pi t)| and tends to -log2-2log cos(pi t). The right side of (11) tends to zero at (pi,0,0). This fixes the absolute constant and includes all one-line degenerations.

The actual facets identify the sum in (11) with volume. Cone the marked ideal vertex p=infinity to every nonincident facet of the convex Q. These positive pyramids partition Q, as in the existing positive polyhedron triangulation. For a WHITE ideal polygon on a finite circle, let delta_j be its consecutive central arcs. Its cone volume is sum_j Lambda(delta_j/2). To verify this, triangulate the actual cyclic polygon from one of its vertices. Each ideal tetrahedron with fourth vertex infinity contributes the three Lobachevsky values of its Euclidean triangle angles. Internal diagonals occur with complementary half-arcs t and pi-t, and cancel by Lambda(pi-t)=-Lambda(t). Only the boundary half-arcs remain. No new ideal vertex or unsigned overlapping decomposition is introduced.

At a shaded triangle the consecutive contact arcs on a neighboring white circle are exactly its center angle theta_j. Orthogonality of the white and shaded circles gives two right angles in the quadrilateral of their centers and their two intersections. Hence the shaded arc between those intersections is pi-theta_j. The shaded ideal triangle coned to infinity therefore has volume sum_j Lambda((pi-theta_j)/2). Summing the white pyramids and shaded tetrahedra over their ACTUAL facet incidences proves

Vol(Q)=sum_(shaded triangles) sum_j
      [Lambda(theta_j/2)+Lambda((pi-theta_j)/2)]
      =(1/2)sum_(shaded triangles) V_tri.          (12)

The two incident white lines have zero cone volume. Incident shaded triangles give the (pi,0,0) limiting angles and contribute zero as well. Thus (12) retains the marked cusp and every actual facet; it is not a separately maximized local-polyhedron comparison.

Evaluate the homogeneous source polynomial at these curvatures, with the two constant faces set to zero. Its limiting normalized real log, including the target monomial of (7), is

Phi=sum_(shaded triangles)F(a_f0,a_f1,a_f2)
                                  -sum_(quadratic f)log a_f.

The angle sums at every bounded white circle are 2pi. Equations (11),(12) therefore give the ABSOLUTE identity

2pi Phi=sum_tri V_tri=2Vol(Q),
Phi=Vol(Q)/pi=Vol(actual augmented complement)/(2pi). (13)

The remaining original fixed-channel scalars have not been discarded. For shrinking folded deficits, N^-1 log beta_a^2 tends to 2J(2a/N)-4J(a/N), hence to zero because J(1)=J(1/2)=0. The positive root forced product has logarithmic size O(s) on these curvature compacts, including a zero endpoint curvature; its normalized rate also tends to zero. The factors k_a and 1/N are polynomial, and the retained half-twist powers have modulus one at actual integer labels. Their exact values stay in (7).

The original finite-r face diagonals have one common quadratic scale (b_ref/s_frame)N^2 rho^2 and relative error O(N^-1); their two constant diagonals divided by that scale tend to zero. Total homogeneity of degree dR cancels this common complex scale against the target monomial EXACTLY. The six source packets, with their retained top monomials and nonzero Bessel factors, then give the same normalized real rate (13). Their finite transport factors are not reset to one; they have zero normalized logarithmic rate. The nearby critical displacement in (9) changes N^-1 Re g_N by o(1), so it has the same value (13).

This proves the absolute rate of the original marked boundary sample and of the constructed local transverse period. It still does not prove that an original finite-support period contains that local contour with nonzero coefficient, or control cancellation in the full auxiliary-label sum or in external filling colors.

7. Focused source and absolute-normalization checks

The focused audit, with recorded results, checks 34 literal marked-cap ratios and 34 physical cup/cap scalar pairs, 60 actual Morse charts with 810 signed face indices and zero marked forces, 48 original LARGE-H Ward identities in all six boundary regimes, 40 original LDL products, nine reciprocal-radius circle-angle identities, three absolute determinant-rate/Lobachevsky comparisons, and seven cyclic-polygon fan cancellations. The arithmetic is at 90 digits. Twenty-four finite force errors are retained as convergence diagnostics, not substituted for the uniform derivative proof.

The Morse region compiler is shared with the independently audited full marked coefficient transformation. The local all-label circle identity has its separate original-white/dual/cup audit in the positive face-polynomial theorem. No new source-cycle assertion is attached to these numerical checks.
