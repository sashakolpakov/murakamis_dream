A whole-interval upper rate for the original terminal Jordan pair

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

Polynomial initialization at finitely many original physical rows, crude propagation through short endpoint intervals, and the compact-interior transfer theorem give a whole-real-interval upper rate for every fixed signed terminal power. Both the terminal eigenvector and its original normal companion are included. This is an upper bound only, and does not control ordinary branching products.

4. Joining the estimates and recovering the ordinary polynomial

5. Both original anchors with a polynomially bounded spectator

Corollary 4. Fix an integer nn, and let pNp_N be any sequence of canonical polynomials with ∥pN∥ℝ≤CNa\|p_N\|_{\mathbb R}\le C N^a, for fixed C,aC,a. Proof. For any two canonical polynomials F,pF,p, the physical values of their product are bounded by ∥F∥ℝ∥p∥ℝ\|F\|_{\mathbb R}\|p\|_{\mathbb R}. Markov’s inequality and the full product rule bound its angular first derivatives by CN2∥F∥ℝ∥p∥ℝC N^2\|F\|_{\mathbb R}\|p\|_{\mathbb R}. Applying (27) to the canonical representative of this product therefore gives, with a deliberately coarse power, ∥can⁡(Fp)∥ℝ≤CN3∥F∥ℝ∥p∥ℝ.(30) \|\operatorname{can}(Fp)\|_{\mathbb R} \le C N^3\|F\|_{\mathbb R}\|p\|_{\mathbb R}. \qquad\text{(30)} The first-normal terms are part of this estimate; values alone would not justify the canonical multiplication. No signs or normal channels in the original traces were replaced; only their absolute size is bounded here. ▫\square

The second anchor uses Hn=T(R−nQ0)H_n=T(R^{-n}Q_0) exactly; it is not obtained by substituting VnQ0V_nQ_0. No lower bound is asserted.

6. Scope and direct sources

This theorem closes the physical endpoint initialization and whole-interval upper propagation for the specified terminal pair Gn,HnG_n,H_n. It does not show that a maximal-modulus characteristic has nonzero coefficient in either polynomial. No lower bound or equality of rates follows. It does not compare ℐn\mathcal I_n with a filled geometric action, control an ordinary product after branching, or identify a relative-cycle coefficient. All original crossing phases and normal traces were retained in (12) and the exact system (16).
