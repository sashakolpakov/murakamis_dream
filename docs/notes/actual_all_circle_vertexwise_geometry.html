A vertexwise criterion for positive geometric fillings

The global sum used to control the finite quantum source is not needed for geometric completion. For an ordered all-circle tree, the nonlinear charge equations admit a contraction whose norm is independent of the number of vertices. This gives a sufficient condition at each vertex, including vertices with zero or small crossing power and large degree. The conclusion below concerns the actual hyperbolic metric and its marked volume and cusp. It makes no new assertion about dominance in the finite quantum sum.

1. Statement

Use the original ordered all-circle construction and its actual octahedral decomposition. There are at least two circle vertices. Every graph edge is an original physical quarter-turn, and each vertex carries an original crossing circle. Keep the original cyclic orders, disk frames, retained half twists ϵv∈{0,±1}\epsilon_v\in\{0,\pm1\}, and primitive auxiliary curves (μv,λv)(\mu_v,\lambda_v). Fix the integers pvp_v and the physical powers nv=ϵv−2pvn_v=\epsilon_v-2p_v. Write dv≥1d_v\ge1 for the graph degree. Assume the original component test gives the required knot, when a knot-complement conclusion is desired.

The following condition suffices for positive geometric completion: dv2+nv2≥630for every vertex v. \sqrt{d_v^2+n_v^2}\ \ge630 \qquad\text{for every vertex }v. \tag{1} There is a unique solution in the closed polydisk |τv|≤10−2|\tau_v|\le10^{-2} of Ψv(𝝉)+inv2πτv=ic,Ψ(𝝉)=∑vwψ(τv,τw),0≤c≤1. \Psi_v(\boldsymbol\tau)+\frac{i n_v}{2\pi}\tau_v=ic, \qquad \Psi(\boldsymbol\tau)=\sum_{vw}\psi(\tau_v,\tau_w), \qquad 0\le c\le1. \tag{2} The solution continues holomorphically in cc on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk, is zero at c=0c=0, and satisfies Im⁡τv(c)<0(0<c≤1),|τv(c)|≤2πcdv2+nv2−dv/39000. \operatorname{Im}\tau_v(c)<0\quad(0<c\le1),\qquad |\tau_v(c)| \le\frac{2\pi c} {\sqrt{d_v^2+n_v^2}-d_v/39000}. \tag{3} The denominator is positive. The original primitive periods obey Uv=−2πΨv−iϵvτv,Vv=2iτv,Uv+pvVv=−2πic. The constants in (1) are deliberately tied to the already established radius 10−210^{-2} of the positive octahedral chart. The substantial difference from the earlier condition is that there is no sum over vertices. The number and relative sizes of the degrees and powers are unrestricted.

The geometric inputs are the ordered octahedral decomposition, the positive angular charts, and the primitive filling completion. Their conclusions about the actual ordered topology, positive tetrahedra, primitive periods, and completion are used below. No source comparison diagram is used.

4. Actual metric and absolute volume

The octahedral chart depends only on the parameters at the ends of one physical edge. Conditions (3) and (14) keep every such pair in its proved positive disk. The actual ordered disk seams match their full complex ratios, including the single prescribed odd seam. The original cut proof retains every primitive meridian and longitude. Thus the geometric completion theorem applies to the path just constructed, rather than to an unrelated solution of exponentiated gluing equations.

In particular, the actual logarithmic periods start at zero as c↓0c\downarrow0: in a fixed complete peripheral frame the second fixed point escapes to infinity, and the increments of log⁡(z−qv)\log(z-q_v) along the fixed generator paths tend to zero. Their differences from (4) are continuous integers times 2πi2\pi i, and hence vanish along the whole path. The slope in (4) makes one full primitive turn at c=1c=1. This is the winding information that exponentiated filling equations alone would not supply.

Every complementary primitive λv\lambda_v represents the core, of length ℓv=−2Im⁡τv\ell_v=-2\operatorname{Im}\tau_v. 5. A finite criterion with different radii at different vertices

One can keep the local graph data instead of using the common threshold (1). Choose radii 0<rv≤10−20<r_v\le10^{-2}. Put hv=∑w∼vαvwh_v=\sum_{w\sim v}\alpha_{vw}. The following finite inequalities suffice: 2π+hvrv<Lvrv,hv<dv,maxv[hvLv+∑w∼vβvwrwLvrv]<1. \begin{split} &2\pi+h_vr_v<L_vr_v,\qquad h_v<d_v,\\ &\max_v\left[ \frac{h_v}{L_v} +\sum_{w\sim v}\frac{\beta_{vw}r_w}{L_vr_v}\right]<1. \end{split} \tag{25} The first line makes (13) preserve the product of the vertex disks and gives the sign in (17). More generally, the second line of (25) can be replaced by a positive-vector certificate for the derivative matrix with diagonal entries hv/Lvh_v/L_v and off-diagonal entries βvw/Lv\beta_{vw}/L_v. If a positive vector qq and a number κ<1\kappa<1 satisfy Mq≤κqMq\le\kappa q, contraction holds in the qq-weighted norm. The invariant polydisk is still supplied separately by the first line of (25). Thus choosing the contraction norm does not change the geometric parameter region.

6. What this extends, and what remains separate

The global condition S≤2−25S\le2^{-25} implies (1), but the converse fails on arbitrarily large trees. For instance on paths with powers chosen from 630,631630,631, every degree is at most two and (1) holds, whereas SS grows linearly with the number of vertices. Choose the original parity pattern by the finite knot-component test when a knot is required. The vertexwise criterion also permits a zero power at any vertex of degree at least 630630, together with other powers satisfying their own local inequalities.

These examples concern the arithmetic hypotheses and the supplied actual diagram. They do not assert an unrestricted classification of small-slope fillings. The explicit positive radius is a sufficient chart, not the maximal positivity domain of the octahedra.

The earlier quantum proof discards some parts of the source with a fixed absolute loss and then compares that loss with the total change of the critical action. The total change in (21) can grow with the size of a tree satisfying (1). Consequently this geometric theorem alone does not supply the earlier source dominance or a new volume-conjecture conclusion. The local subtree argument provides such a quantum comparison under the stronger condition max⁡vdv/(dv2+nv2)≤2−120\max_v d_v/(d_v^2+n_v^2)\le2^{-120}. The geometric condition Lv≥630L_v\ge630 alone does not prove that quantum conclusion.
