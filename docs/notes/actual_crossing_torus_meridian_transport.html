The original crossing torus transports the actual meridians

31 October 2026. The full physical phase of the original crossing torus induces the actual signed Hurwitz transport, including its common magnetic scale. Full regular critical points of the layered direct-power source therefore map to representations of the actual filled diagram. This is a forward morphism on the recorded localized chart, not an isomorphism or a selected-cycle theorem. No global coefficient bound is asserted. Use N>=3, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N) and the original scalar lambda0=q(d2/2), with its recorded exponential lift.

Put A0=x+y, P0=xy, f(b)=(b+x)(b+y). Distinguish the frozen crossing note’s COEFFICIENT momentum r_H=exp(2pi i I) from the PHYSICAL momentum p=r_H^-1. This inverse must be proved from the full physical adapter, not chosen using the symmetric Casimir. We first do that calculation.

Full physical phase, all derivatives and the original half powers

Write the physical input heads as (v,t-v) and output heads as (u,t-u), all divided by N at principal order. The exact finite homogeneous identity is Repsilon_entry=N-1 H_(Repsilon)/(bc)(d/2), with its recorded log lifts; this is (7a) of the homogeneous-source theorem. There is no new cap or pivot in this identity. Let Psi_H be the ACTUAL homogeneous kernel phase (including lambda0^epsilon). At a regular local critical point of its three simplex variables, the envelope derivatives with respect to (Log x,Log b,Log c,Log y) are (K,I,T,L), where K+I+T+L=1. All integer characters from its original signs remain in the critical equations. The physical principal phase is therefore

Theta=Psi_H-(Log b+Log c)/2.                      (0a)

Take a common face-scale gamma=Log y and the literal head clocks

Log x=gamma+2pi i t,
Log b=gamma+2pi i(t-v)+pi i,
Log c=gamma+2pi i(t-u)+pi i,
Log y=gamma.                                   (0b)

These are precisely X_in,1=-x/b, X_in,2=-b/y and the corresponding output clocks -x/c,-c/y. Here b/c are PHYSICAL input/output middle clocks after the exact Fourier adapter. The frozen crossing note’s LEFT output b / RIGHT input c instead describes its polynomial monomial operator; these are dual uses of the kernel, not competing physical orientations. The finite half-index and d/N offsets in the exact identity are retained in the original amplitude; (0a,b) are the explicitly stated PRINCIPAL symbol. Homogeneity cancels the common gamma derivative exactly at this order. Differentiation gives

Theta_v=-2pi i(I-1/2),
Theta_u=-2pi i(T-1/2),
Theta_t=2pi i(K+I+T-1)=-2pi i L,
Theta_gamma=K+I+T+L-1=0.                        (0c)

For the original kernel convention, input raising-shift symbols differentiate positively and output symbols negatively. Restoring a nonzero common input scale g gives

S_in=(g exp Theta_v,g),
S_out=(g exp[-Theta_t-Theta_u],g exp[-Theta_t]). (0d)

Indeed its generating one-form is Log(S1_in)dv+Log(S2_in)d(t-v)-Log(S1_out)du-Log(S2_out)d(t-u); comparison of dv,du,dt yields exactly (0d).

Thus, independently of the crossing sign,

p=exp(-2pi i I), p_out=exp(2pi i T),
S_in=(-g p,g),
S_out=(-g exp(2pi i L)p_out,g exp(2pi i L)).     (0e)

The outer derivative is ESSENTIAL: it fixes the common scale transport and not just the first/second-head ratio. No arbitrary scale is assigned after examining a trace. For epsilon=+/-1 set E_j=exp(2pi i epsilon j) as in the crossing-torus theorem, section2. Equations (0e) read

p=E_I^(-epsilon), p_out=E_T^epsilon,
g_out/g=E_L^epsilon.                           (0f)

Now (4) of that note supplies E_I=(P0-bc)/f(b), E_T=(P0-bc)/f(c), E_L=-(b+y)(c+y)/(P0-bc). Therefore positive physical chronology has (c,p_out)=F_-(b,p), and negative chronology has F_+(b,p). Equivalently it is the coefficient graph F_epsilon conjugated by p=1/r_H. This explicitly explains the inverse and its full normalization; it is not inferred from (2) below.

The actual meridian matrices

The resulting ACTUAL physical Gauss data are

X1=-x/b, X2=-b/y, S1=-g p, S2=g.                    (1)

The sign in S1/S2=-p is compulsory by (0c–f). As a separate check, the original Jacobi symbol at X=X1,T=X1X2=x/y has

a(X)=-f(b)/b^2,  d(X)=-2A0/b-2P0/b^2.

Thus a(X)(Y+Y^-1)+d(X), with the original magnetic Y=S1/S2=-p, is exactly the source-torus invariant

sigma=2+((p-1)^2/p)f(b)/b^2.                       (2)

For each leg define the original matrices

K=-1/X, F=S(1-X), E=(X^-1-1)/S,
G=[[K,E],[FK,FE+K^-1]], Lminus=[[1,0],[-KF,K]].

Then the ordered physical meridians are

A=G1, B=Lminus1^-1 G2 Lminus1, P=B A.              (3)

They have determinant one and trace minus two. Direct multiplication gives P11=y/x and Tr(P)=sigma; this is the original ordered pair Casimir, not just an abstract first integral. These formulas are on the same Gauss units as the original compiler; any additional scale or total-F denominator needed below is explicitly localized.

Actual signed Hurwitz moves

Positive physical chronology is

(A,B) -> (A^-1 B A,A),

and negative chronology is its inverse (A,B)->(B,B A B^-1). The first new physical clock is recovered from the new first meridian by b_new=x*(A_new)11. After setting g=1 for display, multiplication gives

positive: c=(P0-f(b)/p)/b,
  S1_new=b p/(-b+p x-x),
  S2_new=-(-b+p y-y)/b,
  p_new=-S1_new/S2_new=(P0-bc)/f(c);

negative: c=(P0-p f(b))/b,
  S1_new=-(b p+p x-x)/b,
  S2_new=b p/(b p+p y-y),
  p_new=-S1_new/S2_new=f(c)/(P0-bc).              (4)

For general g multiply both new S values by g. Rebuilding the second leg from X2_new=-c/y and these S values gives exactly the SECOND Hurwitz meridian as well, so the WHOLE ordered matrix P is preserved. This is stronger than trace matching. The displayed extra denominators are already factors of the regular source chart after the corresponding F_+ or F_- substitution; they must remain excluded.

Substitution of (0f) gives exactly the S values in (4), including S2_new/g=E_L for positive chronology and E_L^-1 for negative chronology. Consequently the matrices checked here come from ALL physical phase derivatives, not only the Casimir. No crossing sign or original scalar is changed by the coefficient-to-physical inversion.

Fixed product, centralizer and the spectral sign

The full product has the actual form

P=[[T^-1,E_tot],[F_tot/T,F_tot E_tot+T]], T=x/y.

On F_tot!=0, fixing sigma and T fixes E_tot once F_tot is fixed. As (b,p) varies on one conic, choose the scale g so that F_tot has that fixed value. The common magnetic scale is thereby restored, and the conic is a rational chart of ACTUAL ordered trace-minus-two pairs with that fixed P. Conversely the original regular Gauss inverse recovers (b,p,g) from such a pair. This is a local birational statement, with the same recorded open units; not every completed-conic point is an allowed source point.

If J is the selected eigenvalue of P, then

J+J^-1=sigma, lambda=-J or -J^-1.

On the chosen source spectral sheet lambda=-J. Hence physical positive crossings multiply the norm coordinate by lambda^-1, while negative crossings multiply by lambda. The central minus sign cannot be removed for an odd power. In particular the square of positive Hurwitz is simultaneous conjugation by P^-1:

H_+^2(A,B)=(P^-1 A P,P^-1 B P),

and the torus multiplication is lambda-2=J-2, as required. The opposite spectral sheet interchanges the reciprocal coordinate.

At sigma=2 and f(b)!=0, equation(2) is the double equation (p-1)^2=0. Its reduced locus p=1 has the two meridians sharing their fixed line; positive Hurwitz swaps this commuting pair. This explains why the completed double line is not an additional regular source saddle. At sigma=-2 the already derived additive coordinate advances by -2A0 under positive physical chronology, the inverse of the pullback advance.

All of this is rational matrix algebra on the source-defined classical chart. The exact finite kernels still retain their original Hermite data, scalar lambda0^n, punctures and original marked target. A global representation follows only after the actual wire/cup constraints are imposed; none of these identities assigns a coefficient to that branch.

Full regular direct-power critical points and the filled group
