A uniform comparison of the whole finite source-factor lattice

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The middle-component Gamma comparison extends to every color in the actual finite head. A complementary Weyl chart is essential in the upper half: using the same partial product there would introduce extra periodic zero divisors.

The result is an exact diagonal unit comparison, uniformly I+O(1/N) in the full head, including all fixed normal resonance divisors. It compares source factors and their dual fractional lattices. It does not identify the two crossing representations or the selected knot periods. The surviving crossing response is studied separately.

1. Actual factors and the two root Weyl charts

Use the actual frame of the integral-color source and its whole Gamma normal. Put

c=2pi*i, n=N-1, L=floor(n/2), H_0=c/N,
H=H_0+ca/N^2, b=a/(1+a/N), Hb=ca/N,
M_col=exp(Hb/2)=-q^N, q=exp(H/2),
M_+=exp(H_0b/2), M_-=M_+^(-1), Q_0=exp(H_0).

For j=0,…,n, the retained source-frame residual is

V_(N,j)={n choose j}_(q,sym)/M_col^j.               (1)

Every q-binomial in (1) means its Laurent-polynomial value, including parameters where a factorial quotient is 0/0. At such parameters V need not be a unit.

The partial root Weyl factors are the actual functions

beta_j(M)=(M^2 Q_0;Q_0)_j/(Q_0;Q_0)_j.              (2)

Their denominators are nonzero for j<N. They are the factors in the diagonal matrix of the exact root Weyl intertwiner, whose entries are M^j/beta_j(M), with the original meromorphic and source-lattice interpretation. The marked full half-adapter is beta_L at odd N, and [(1+iM)/(1+i)]beta_L at even N. The extra even factor is retained when passing to that scalar.

Define the complementary target source-factor section by

B_(N,j)=beta_j(M_-),                         j<=L,
B_(N,j)=M_+^(n-2j)beta_(n-j)(M_-),           j>L.    (3)

These are concrete root Weyl factors and their retained character, not values of an arbitrary replacement invariant.

The two formulas are the actual norm/complementary charts. If

H_N(cb)=S_N(M_+^2)/(N M_+^n),

the exact identity is

beta_j(M_+) beta_(n-j)(M_-)
                =M_+^(2j-n) H_N(cb).               (4)

Consequently the upper expression in (3) obeys

beta_j(M_+) B_(N,j)=H_N(cb).                        (5)

Thus B_(N,j) can also be written H_N/beta_j(M_+) after canceling common factors. Formula (3), rather than division at a zero, defines its holomorphic value.

To prove (4), transform the last n-j factors of (M_+^2 Q_0;Q_0)n using r->N-r. Their ratios become M+(-2)(1-M_+2Q_0r)/(1-Q_0r). The complete product is S_N(M_+^2)/N. Multiplying its character gives exactly (4). This also retains the unpaired even residue; replacing the marked even half-adapter by beta_L alone would lose its scalar splitting, although (2)–(5) remain valid partial-factor identities.

2. Exact unit comparison at every color

Let J(x)=2sinh(x/2)/x, J(0)=1. For 0<=k<=L set

u_(N,k)=exp[-(H-H_0)bk/2]
    product_(r=1)^k
      J(H(r-b))J(H_0r)/[J(Hr)J(H_0(r-b))].           (6)

There is no even prefactor in this partial-product formula. Define a diagonal matrix C_N(a) by

C_(N,j)=u_(N,j),                                      j<=L,
C_(N,j)=exp[(H-H_0)b(n-2j)/2]u_(N,n-j),               j>L.

Then, exactly for every j,

V_(N,j)=C_(N,j) B_(N,j).                             (7)

For each compact complex a-set K, all sufficiently large N have a neighborhood of K on which every C_(N,j) is a holomorphic unit, C_(N,j)(0)=1, and

sup_(a in K,0<=j<N)|C_(N,j)-1|<=A_K/N.               (8)

This assertion concerns the comparison unit, not invertibility of V or B at their common zeros.

For k<=L the exact factorization in the Gamma theorem is

V_(N,k)=exp(-Hbk/2) product_(r<=k)
      (1-b/r) J(H(r-b))/J(Hr).

The corresponding beta_k(M_-) has H replaced by H_0. Their common zero polynomial is kept before canceling, and the remaining factors give (6). All J arguments remain in a fixed zero-free neighborhood of c[0,1/2] for a in K and large N. In particular the unit extends through integer b.

Uniformity in k is the same double-difference estimate as in the middle-component proof. With F=Log J and DeltaH=H-H_0, the logarithm of one cross ratio is

DeltaH[(r-b)F'(H_0(r-b))-rF'(H_0r)]
                                  +O_K(DeltaH^2 r^2).

The bracket is O_K(1), because its two arguments differ by b, F’ and F’’ are bounded, and H_0r is bounded. Since DeltaH is O_K(N^-2), summing at most N/2 terms gives O_K(1/N), uniformly in the prefix length. The phase in (6) has the same bound.

For the upper half use the exact symmetric q-binomial identity

V_(N,j)=M_col^(n-2j)V_(N,n-j).                       (9)

Combining (9) with the lower comparison gives (7); its additional character differs from the root character by the displayed unit in C_(N,j). Its logarithm is again O_K(1/N). This proves (8) uniformly in the entire head, without a bulk-color restriction.

For any common diagonal coordinate or coherent metric, (8) gives

||C_N-I||<=A_K/N,
||C_N||,||C_N^(-1)||<=1+O_K(1/N).                    (10)

The bound is independent of the dimension N. It holds for every fixed normal jet as well, on a smaller compact set, by Cauchy’s formula. No sum over colors is needed to obtain these operator norm estimates.

3. The resonance divisors are matched, not inverted away

At an integer b=r with 1<=r<=L and N>2r, the exact vanishing pattern of the residual is

V_(N,j)=0 precisely for r<=j<=n-r.                  (11)

The same pattern holds for B, with the same simple divisors in the parameter. Indeed beta_k(M_-) vanishes first at k=r, and there is no other root-periodic zero in k<=L in a fixed normal compact set for large N. The complement gives (11) in the upper half. The unit in (7) preserves these divisors.

The exact parameter centers are a=Nr/(N-r), rather than a=r. No quotient V/B is evaluated there to define C_N. Defining V by a raw q-factorial quotient at an upper index would miss its removable cancellation at these lower-order roots; its Laurent-polynomial or complementary value in (1) is essential.

For the paired source and co-source one may work first over the meromorphic parameter field. Equation (7) identifies the fractional source lattices generated by V_j and B_j, and also their inverse dual lattices. The dual comparison is C_(N,j)^(-1). The tensor pairing is preserved before specialization. A zero source coordinate and a pole in its dual cannot separately be discarded: their abstract pairing is still one.

4. The whole source chirp costs no exponential factor in N

The exact balanced frame satisfies

D_col^(-1)=U_col diag(V),
(U_col)_j=q^[Nj-j(j+1)/2].                           (12)

For a root chirp (U_0)_j=exp[H_0(Nj-j(j+1)/2)/2], use the concrete root-chart source frame U_0 diag(B). Its comparison to the actual column frame is

U_col diag(V)=T_N(a) U_0 diag(B),
T_N=diag(U_col/U_0) C_N.                            (13)

The chirp ratio is exactly

(U_col/U_0)_j
   =exp{ca[ j/(2N)-j(j+1)/(4N^2)]}.                 (14)

For a in K, T_N and its inverse have uniform operator bounds independent of N in every common diagonal metric. At j/N->T, its chirp tends to exp[ca(T/2-T^2/4)]. Thus the normal source transport has a controlled whole quadratic character, and the remaining unit tends uniformly to one.

Equations (12)–(14) transport the original source column and dual row, including their fractional lattices and endpoint coordinates. They do not replace either by a constant vacuum after a change of frame. At a resonance, specialize the paired lattices, not their separately singular coordinate arrays.

More explicitly, U_0 diag(B) is a meromorphic coordinate frame of the same literature-defined root head. All its root operators must be conjugated in that frame, together with the original column psi and row phi. If Gamma_root is the resulting cut coupon, then its comparison with the actual color-frame coupon is exactly

psi_col=T_N psi_root,
phi_col=phi_root T_N^(-1),
Gamma_col=T_N Gamma_root T_N^(-1).                  (15)

The diagonal T_N commutes with J and both endpoint projectors. Thus (15) also transports every finite position-moment insertion and the original endpoint source probes. This does not identify Q_col^J with Q_0^J; the two quantum parameters and the crossing operators remain distinct. No new state or closure is defined.

5. What is and is not closed

The source-factor comparison now covers the entire head and both endpoint ranges with a dimension-independent unit bound. It retains the actual root Weyl norm, even-level mark, all finite normal zero divisors and the quadratic chirp.

This is not a full crossing similarity. The q-normal and fixed-root crossings can have different spectral multiplicities and different periodic alias zeros. Their nontrivial bulk response and the normal base must also be transported. Nor do the source bounds bound a cancellation-prone selected knot sum. A source-preserving comparison of the actual full knot period with its marked geometric regulator, and its selected exponential norm, remains necessary.

The all-level crossing spectra rule out even an arbitrary scalar-adjusted nondiagonal similarity. The compatible constructive statement is the closed Euler/regulator response, which compares the actual normal geometric blocks with their full sources. Neither statement is replaced by (15).

Verification: audit and results. The independent source audit also verifies the whole-head unit, chirp, zero divisors and paired coupon/lattice identities.
