A combinatorial geometric certificate for actual plats at every fixed width

31 October 2026. Combinatorial sufficient class and arbitrary-width construction. The main class is ALL actual flat plats with a simple white triangulated sphere. The explicit words below prove nonvacuity at every supplied width; they do not replace the general long-twist target by one family.

1. The existing simple-sphere construction applies to the actual source

Start with an ORIGINAL ordered 2k-strand flat plat, k>=2, whose boxes cover all crossings, retaining the original cups, caps, marked top cap, cyclic port order and crossing-disk matching. Build its actual plane cubic matching graph and dual white triangular cellulation by the literal construction in the actual matching geometry, sections1–2, or equivalently by the original middle-gap sweep.

Require that this white cellulation is a SIMPLE triangulated sphere: its vertices and edge occurrences are the actual regions and incidences, there are no loops or parallel edges, and every triangular cell has three distinct vertices. This is a finite combinatorial test on the supplied diagram, not an abstract graph chosen to imitate it. The nondegenerate sphere has at least four white vertices. Each source triangle still has exactly one designated matching edge.

The already proved construction in the simple-block construction, sections4–5, applies with this WHOLE sphere as its single simple block. Its fixed-boundary Hall argument gives an interior angle maximizer; Euclidean disk development is an embedding of degree1; the strict sector argument excludes unintended contacts. The contact circles of the shaded triangles then give all supporting halfspaces. Therefore it constructs a convex three-dimensional ideal right-angled polyhedron with exactly these LABELED white faces, shaded triangles, ideal contacts and cyclic orders. Separating triangles are allowed by that proof.

Sections3–6 of the actual matching geometry identify this labeled medial sphere with the TWO actual projection/crossing-disk cut balls. They glue white faces across the copies and shaded triangles by the original retained half-twist permutations. The original meridians, primitive crossing-disk longitudes and source mark are retained. Their right-angle edge cycles and cusp decorations prove completeness for every fixed half-twist pattern. In particular the ACTUAL augmented exterior Y_(D,epsilon) is hyperbolic, with volume2Vol(Q). Its positive ideal triangulation supplies the general marked gluing certificate.

Thus the simple-white test is a SUFFICIENT combinatorial geometric certificate for the exact class used by the right-angled source results. It is not a claim that every hyperbolic or prime twist-reduced plat satisfies this test. The older matching-geometry existence-gap paragraph can now be sharpened for this class using the later simple-block proof. No external alternating-link existence theorem is being imported.

2. Explicit literal words at all widths k>=3

Number adjacent strand slots from0 to2k-2. Put

O_k=(1,3,...,2k-3), E_k=(0,2,...,2k-2),
w_k=O_k E_k O_k, R=3k-2.                            (1)

These are the ORIGINAL bottom-to-top box slots; no isotopy or abstract matching-to-plat adapter is needed. Denote the exterior by u, the k bottom odd-gap regions by a_0,…,a_(k-1), the k top odd-gap regions by b_0,…,b_(k-1), and the intermediate bounded even-gap regions by e_1,…,e_(k-1). Set e_0=e_k=u for formulas only. All the other named vertices are distinct.

The literal gap sweep gives exactly these ordered four-face incidences:

first O:  (a_(j-1),u,e_j,a_j),       1<=j<=k-1;
middle E: (e_j,a_j,b_j,e_(j+1)),      0<=j<=k-1;
final O:  (b_(j-1),e_j,u,b_j),        1<=j<=k-1.      (2)

The two cells of a quartet (x,b,c,y) are (x,b,y) and (x,y,c). The MAIN edges of its white sphere are exactly

u-a_i, u-b_i                         (0<=i<=k-1),
e_j-a_(j-1), e_j-a_j,
e_j-b_(j-1), e_j-b_j                 (1<=j<=k-1),

and its MATCHING edges are exactly

a_(j-1)-a_j, b_(j-1)-b_j             (1<=j<=k-1),
u-e_1-e_2-...-e_(k-1)-u             (the k cycle edges). (3)

For k>=3 every listed edge has distinct endpoints and occurs only once in (3) and the MAIN list. In particular the matching cycle has length at least3. The triangles in (2) are distinct and every listed edge occurs in precisely two of them. These are the actual embedded sphere cells from the gap sweep, with

vertices=3k, edges=9k-6, triangles=6k-4,
vertices-edges+triangles=2.

For example the MAIN edge e_j-a_(j-1) appears in the first-O output triangle and the preceding middle-E input triangle; the other three e_j MAIN incidences pair in the same way. The end u-a_0,u-a_(k-1) incidences close against the first and last middle-E cells, and the interior u-a_i incidences pair between consecutive first-O cells; the b incidences are reflected. The matching edges each pair the two triangles of their own original box. This checks the full labeled sphere, not just its Euler count. The protected mark can be the original first top cap: its two adjacent white faces are u,b_0, joined by MAIN. Consequently every w_k, k>=3, meets the simple-white certificate.

3. Width two and the knot parity

For k=2 take the ORIGINAL slot word (1,0,1), with R=3. Its actual quartets are

(1,0,3,2), (0,1,4,3), (4,3,0,2).

The six triangles form the triangular bipyramid on vertices0,…,4, with equatorial triangle(0,2,3) and apices1,4. Thus its white sphere is simple and nondegenerate. The superficially similar word (1,0,2,1) must NOT be used for this argument: its two middle boxes produce parallel matching edges between the same white pair.

For k>=3 put epsilon=+1 or -1 on EVERY box of the FIRST O layer, and zero on the other boxes. For k=2 use one nonzero epsilon on the first slot1 box and zero on the other two. Signs do not affect permutation. Starting with k disjoint cap components, the successive first-layer transpositions on slots1,3,…,2k-3 join component j to the already joined components0,…,j-1. At that step their two strands are in DIFFERENT components; cutting and cross-rejoining them merges those two cycles. Induction therefore gives ONE main component. Equivalently, the exact permutation is the product of the disjoint transpositions (1 2)(3 4)…(2k-3 2k-2), and the lower standard pairing together with the upper pairing transported by this permutation form a single alternating cycle.

All full twists have trivial strand permutation. Thus for each such fixed parity pattern EVERY fixed integer vector p gives a knot with box exponents epsilon_i-2p_i. The geometric long-filling theorem gives a threshold T_geom(D,epsilon) for independently long signed p_i, with no comparability requirement. Each resulting knot has a k-bridge PRESENTATION, hence minimal bridge number at most k. No equality b(K)=k or growth of the minimal bridge number is proved here.

4. Exact coverage and threshold dependence

The main geometric assertion applies to EVERY actual simple-white flat plat, not just (1). Its width and number/order of boxes may be arbitrary but are fixed before the quantum limit. Each fixed retained half-twist pattern and original primitive marking is part of D. The geometric threshold can depend on that full D, including cusp moduli, the positive-shape and framed-completion neighborhoods, and the common inverse-slope IFT polydisk. It is not asserted to depend only on k. A maximum over the finitely many cusp subsets gives one threshold for partial fillings as well, with unfilled inverse slopes0.

The endpoint/complete-source results apply with their separately stated source and parity scopes. The desired original-invariant theorem for ALL independently long fixed signed twists still additionally requires the phase-sensitive Gaussian period, all complementary Fourier charges, the actual contributing cycle and its nonzero coefficient, and the required genuine-q normals. This class construction does not prove those quantum inputs, does not identify all essential hyperbolic plats with the simple-white class, and does not certify an arbitrary abstract matched graph as an original plat.

5. Focused verification

The literal-source audit and recorded output check the original gap-sweep incidences, distinct triangle and edge occurrences, sphere counts and one-component parity at widths 2 through 30. At width 30 the source has 88 ports and 90 white vertices. These finite checks support the source dictionary; the symbolic formulas and construction above prove the arbitrary-width statement. They do not prove minimal bridge number or the phase-sensitive quantum filling limit.

Root and an independent internal reviewer checked the entire class argument, the all-width incidence formulas, geometric dependencies and parity proof. The portable source audit was read and replayed by root.
