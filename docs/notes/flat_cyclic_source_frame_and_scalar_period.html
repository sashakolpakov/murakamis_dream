The flat cyclic source frame is rigid; its scalar period is separate

Independent algebraic theorem, 27 October 2026. The actual highest-input clock, cyclic repair and paired source have a whole matrix-algebra description BEFORE quantum jets. On the source-cyclic chart their scheme-theoretic classification is elementary. A rank-one defect must be aligned with the actual source line; rank one alone does not imply this. The rigid frame does not fix the scalar of a closed transport.

1. The actual source frame and its coefficient ring

Fix N>=2, Q a unit, and invert only 1-Q^k for 1<=k<N. Write

delta=1-Q^N,
B_0=diag(1,Q,...,Q^(N-1)),
P_0 e_j=e_(j+1 mod N),
E_0=E_00, A_0=e_0 1^t.

Here 1^t is the original all-ones output row. These are the literal highest-input data of the actual two-wall boundary, not an additional state. Direct entries give

B_0 P_0-Q P_0 B_0=delta E_(0,N-1),
B_0 P_0 B_0^-1 P_0^-1
                =Q[I+(Q^-N-1)E_0],
B_0 A_0=A_0, A_0 P_0=A_0, A_0^2=A_0,
P_0^N=I, E_0 A_0=A_0, A_0 E_0=E_0.          (1.1)

The last two identities express equality of the two IMAGE lines. They do not identify the source row with the defect row: E_0 is e_0 e_0^t, whereas A_0 is e_0 1^t. That distinction is retained. The normalization A_0^2=A_0 is the specified primitive pair 1^t e_0=1. Any original external d_N(M) line remains attached as that exact separate source factor; it is not divided away at one of its zeros/poles to obtain this primitive matrix statement. Without the specified normalization, BA=A and AP=A allow every scalar multiple a A_0, so even the source scale would be free.

Work with rank-N vector bundles over this coefficient ring or any specified COMMUTATIVE base algebra. Source and defect projectors have locally free image of rank one. No delta inverse is adjoined.

2. An explicit classification on the cyclic source chart

Let E have rank N, let P be invertible with P^N=I, and let A be an idempotent rank-one source. Put L=im A. Suppose

BA=A, AP=A,
im(BP-QPB) contained in L,                    (2.1)

and suppose the source orbit map

Theta: direct_sum_(j=0)^(N-1) L -> E,
Theta(l_0,...,l_(N-1))=sum_j P^j l_j          (2.2)

is an isomorphism. Then in this CANONICAL line-valued frame

P=P_0 tensor I_L, B=B_0 tensor I_L,
A=A_0 tensor I_L.                            (2.3)

Proof. AP^j=A makes A act by summing the N input copies into the zeroth copy. P cycles them. BA=A makes the first column of B equal to (I_L,0,…,0)^t. Every entry of B in this frame is an endomorphism of L, hence a scalar over the base. Since the image in (2.1) lies in the zeroth copy, the other rows give

B_(i,j+1 mod N)=Q B_(i-1,j), 1<=i<N.         (2.4)

For i>=j this recurrence and the first column yield B_(i,j)=Q^j delta_(i,j). For i<j they yield B_(i,j)=Q^i B_(0,j-i). The wrap case j=N-1 in (2.4) has left side zero, forcing every B_(0,k)=0 for 1<=k<N. This proves (2.3). In particular B is automatically invertible. No wall cancellation, eigenvalue argument or flatness theorem was needed for this proof.

This is a classification over arbitrary base algebras on the specified open chart, including algebras with nilpotents. Its groupoid is the groupoid of line bundles L, by E=L^direct_sum N. If E is required to be globally free, retain the corresponding trivialization condition on that direct sum. Over a local base L is free. Choosing a source vector v trivializing L, with Av=v, makes Theta a canonical ordinary basis and removes all its automorphisms. Without that vector marking the remaining automorphisms are precisely multiplication by a central unit on L.

The cyclic hypothesis is automatic near the actual root source. Indeed at a primitive Q_N the actual multiplicative relation reduces to BP=Q_N PB. If Bv=v, the N vectors P^jv have distinct eigenvalues Q_N^j. They are nonzero and independent over every residue field. Rank N makes them a basis. In a local deformation of that root point their determinant stays a unit, proving (2.2). This does not assert cyclicity on unrelated global components.

3. The multiplicative defect and its explicit flat saturation

Suppose also that R is a rank-one idempotent with

AR=R, RA=A,
BPB^-1P^-1=Q[I+(Q^-N-1)R].                  (3.1)

The image of its commutator lies in im R=L, so Section 2 applies. In its canonical frame R=e_0 rho with rho_0=1. Equations (1.1) and (3.1) reduce EXACTLY to

delta (R-E_0)=0,
delta rho_j=0, 1<=j<N.                     (3.2)

If the base is flat over the normal parameter, or more generally delta is a nonzero divisor on it, (3.2) forces R=E_0. This is the actual normalized defect, recovered before taking jets.

The unsaturated presentation has the explicit coordinate algebra

R_base[rho_1,...,rho_(N-1)]/(delta rho_j).   (3.3)

When delta is a nonzero divisor on R_base, this presentation is not flat at the wall. Its delta-saturation kills the rho_j and is just R_base. If the supplied base already has delta-torsion, saturation also removes that base torsion. At delta=0 alone the multiplicative relation cannot see these projector-row parameters. A rank-N module can be locally free over this nonflat comparison base; that is not a substitute for flatness/saturation of the base over the normal parameter. If the module itself is flat over that normal base, the nonzero-divisor hypothesis above applies. If R is marked to be the actual E_0, these excess parameters never arise.

4. The whole endpoint-corrected algebra is a matrix algebra

Set

chi(X)=product_(j=0)^(N-1)(X-Q^j),
D=R[X]/chi(X),
e_j(X)=product_(k!=j)(X-Q^k)/(Q^j-Q^k).

The node differences are units in the stated head localization. Evaluation at them identifies D with R^N: the e_j are orthogonal idempotents summing to one. The polynomial map

tau(X)=Q^-1[X-delta e_0(X)]                  (4.1)

cycles these evaluations, so it is an algebra automorphism with tau^N=I and tau(e_j)=e_(j+1 mod N). Form the explicit algebra with basis f P^a, f in D, 0<=a<N, and multiplication

(f P^a)(g P^b)=f tau^a(g)P^(a+b mod N).     (4.2)

Associativity follows from tau being an automorphism. Define

E_(i,j)=e_i P^(i-j mod N).

These satisfy E_(i,j)E_(k,l)=delta_(j,k)E_(i,l), sum_i E_(i,i)=I. They form a basis: fixing i,a recovers e_iP^a uniquely. Therefore (4.2) is EXACTLY Mat_N(R), with no rank comparison or unverified quiver theorem as a premise. The original source is recovered by

A_0=e_0 sum_(j=0)^(N-1)P^j,
Tr(A_0)=1.                                  (4.3)

Thus the original paired-source matrix can be recovered from the flat normalized defect and cyclic transport; the defect and source rows remain different. Every coefficient, root norm and endpoint term is retained in (4.1), rather than imposing an impossible whole BP=QPB with P^N=I.

This adds the explicit finite ALIGNED SOURCE classification and whole matrix-algebra presentation to the boundary note. It does not duplicate or contradict the whole clock function theorem. That theorem concerns the UNCORRECTED infinite-order shift on a coalescing clock and requires its endpoint tower. Here the nodes Q^j are distinct at each fixed primitive root, and (4.1) changes the last shift by its actual finite endpoint defect. A fixed-N head localization is not a common neighborhood of Log Q=0 as N grows. This finite algebra does not discard whole functions, unwrapped Green tails or their separate source extension.

5. Rank one without source alignment is insufficient

There are flat rational families through the actual N=2 root which satisfy every OTHER condition but are not the canonical clock. Put near Q=-1

delta=1-Q^2, h=Q-Q^-1,
t=delta/(1-delta^2), x=delta^2/(1-delta^2),
P=[[0,1],[1,0]], A=[[1,1],[0,0]],
B=[[1,t h],[0,Q+x h]],
R=[[1+x,-t],[t,-x]].                         (5.1)

All entries are rational and regular there; B is invertible. Since t^2=x(1+x), direct multiplication proves

R^2=R, Tr R=1, rank R=1,
BA=A, AP=A, P^2=I,
BPB^-1P^-1=Q[I+(Q^-2-1)R].                 (5.2)

At Q=-1 these are EXACTLY B_0,P_0,A_0,E_0. Nevertheless the second eigenvalue Q+xh of B is generically different from Q, so no conjugation can identify it with B_0. The rank-N module and every displayed rational family are flat. What fails is AR=R, namely alignment of the moving defect with the actual source line. Even correct classical source/defect markings and flatness of the objects do not impose that whole relation. Taking delta replaced by delta^k in x,t makes the tilt invisible to an arbitrarily long fixed jet, without changing this conclusion. The ACTUAL fixed E_00 defect is aligned and is covered by the positive theorem, not by this counterexample.

6. The scalar closed period is not fixed by this frame

Over any base the commutant of the matrix units in Section 4 consists of scalars: commuting with E_ii makes a matrix diagonal, and commuting with E_ij makes all its diagonal entries equal. Consequently a closed frame-intertwiner is

C=c I,    Tr(A_0 C)=c,                      (6.1)

with c an ARBITRARY scalar (a unit if C is invertible). Adjoining c, or an invertible c, gives a flat finite-type extension with the exact same frame, defect and normalized source. Source-frame classification alone imposes no equation on that period. It also does not supply a regulator action or fix a leading norm.

This is a statement about insufficient structural conditions, not permission to change the original actual knot state. Once the complete actual crossing word and every scalar/cap/peripheral normalization are supplied, that word fixes c. A comparison of the FULL word evaluations through the canonical source frame preserves it automatically. Merely identifying their clock/source matrix algebras does not compare their selected word sections.

There is no contradiction with strict vector-pointed rigidity. An intertwiner C preserving a specified nonzero v must satisfy cv=v and hence c=1. A generic actual knot period C=cI is NOT such a source-vector-preserving automorphism; it preserves the coupon A by conjugation while its cut pairing is c. Demanding strict preservation at that final step would assume the desired normalization and exclude the actual nontrivial period.

Here the actual scalar-knot example is on its original ROOT fiber with generic M. Its whole auxiliary q-normal lift need not be scalar, as the explicit unknot in the one-index source audit shows. No extension of root scalarity is used in this argument.

The exact audit and saved results check the universal recurrence equations, all matrix units/source moments, arbitrary conjugate frames, wall parameters and the explicit nonaligned rational family. No geometric selected-cycle or hyperbolic-volume theorem follows from this finite-frame result.

The independent proof audit checks the commutative-base classification, corrected crossed product, saturation scope and nonaligned counterexample. It also distinguishes the actual root scalar closure from its auxiliary whole q-normal lift.
