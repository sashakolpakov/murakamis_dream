Central resolvent, inverse Jacobian, and compact source symmetry

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The original highest-cut pivotal functional is an explicit central resolvent inside an ordinary full trace. This gives a compact coherent period with no cut coupon to reset under Weyl turnover. The actual turnover and half-twist return yield a specified rational exact-form current, including every meridian pole and finite-part term. A symmetric representative has a literal orientation-preserving involution on the original product of compact spheres.

These are exact finite-source identities. They do not identify the two geometric eigenline contributions of the reduced trace. The opposite polarization and local relative boundary currents remain.

The complete directs H, P, C and G are listed at the end. H fixes the pointed functional, P the whole-meridian turnover, C the compact kernel calculus, and G the resolved reduced geometric chart. All new Chebyshev, inverse-Jacobian, and compact-current calculations are below.
