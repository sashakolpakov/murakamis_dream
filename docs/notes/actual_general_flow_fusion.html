General source-preserving fusion of the original charged network

28 October 2026. Two exact operations apply to the WHOLE original charged source at arbitrary valency and arbitrary braid width:

  1. A full length-N-1 block is the source reproducing kernel. It can be contracted through ANY polynomial exterior, not merely through one complementary Pochhammer.
  2. A balanced trivalent charged star is exactly a triangle of three root intervals, with an explicit nonzero q-binomial scalar.

The first operation contracts every original closure adapter of a nontrivial knot at the SOURCE level, retaining the actual pivots. The second is a genuine higher-valency fusion. Its proof is a root-orbit divisibility argument and degree rigidity, not a borrowed analytic pentagon or an assumed positive source.

These operations do not yet reduce every original network or prove its selected geometric coefficient. A degree obstruction below explains why arbitrary charge stars cannot simply be declared balanced.

1. The source in which the operations are exact

Use N>=2, d=N-1, Q=exp(2pii/N), q=exp(pi*i/N). The whole charged bridge theorem has already proved, with ALL original scalar lines retained,

K_N=chi_N/N sum_h W_h lambda(P_h),
lambda(P)=[X_1^d ... X_k^d]P,
deg P_h=k d.                                    (1)

All h are the actual over-monotone clocks. The canonical source theorem permits the SAME lambda to be evaluated in

A_0=K[X_1,...,X_k]/(X_1^N,...,X_k^N).           (2)

This is an exact equality of the original selected period. It is not a rule setting every separate regular-grid factor to zero. The perfect multiplication pairing is lambda(X^a X^b)=delta_(a+b,d). In particular arbitrary polynomial exteriors are admissible in the algebraic identities below. No original coupon or scalar is removed.

2. Full blocks reproduce arbitrary exteriors

Write B_L(X,Y;a)=product_(r=0)(L-1)(X-Qr aY). Root complementation gives

B_d(X,Y;a)=sum_(j=0)^d X^(d-j)(aQ^(-1)Y)^j.    (3)

For ANY polynomial F(X,Y,U), with U denoting all other anchors,

[X^d] B_d(X,Y;a)F(X,Y,U)
   =F(aQ^(-1)Y,Y,U) mod (Y^N,U_i^N).           (4)

There is no valency or individual-degree restriction on F. Indeed the left side is sum_(j<N)(aQ(-1)Y)j [X^j]F. Every missing term of the substitution has a factor Y^N and vanishes in (2). Pairing with the remaining original source proves (4) for the whole scalar. A tail coefficient alpha contributes its actual factor alpha^d.

The reverse contraction, which may retain a preferred anchor, is

[Y^d] B_d(X,Y;a)F(X,Y,U)
   =(aQ^(-1))^d F(X,(aQ^(-1))^(-1)X,U)
                                                mod (X^N,U_i^N). (5)

All displayed monomial factors in (4)–(5) remain. If the two anchors already coincide, then

B_d(X,X;a)=X^d G_N(aQ^(-1)),
G_N(t)=sum_(j=0)^d t^j.

Thus its source contraction is G_N(aQ^(-1)) times evaluation of the remaining polynomial at X=0, NOT a variable identification. For an actual root phase t=q^m,

G_N(t)=N                         if m=0 mod 2N,
       0                         if m even, not0 mod2N,
       2/(1-q^m)                 if m odd.      (6)

Every nonzero value has modulus between 1 and N. Its fixed-order logarithmic derivatives are bounded by N^(p+1), directly from the finite sum. This is a general tame fusion of a saturated source cycle, including its resonant value; no central norm is silently inverted at resonance.

All original closure adapters can be contracted

The closure numerator in the actual source is

C(Z,Y)=sum_(j=0)^d Z^(d-j)(tY)^j=B_d(Z,Y;Qt),
t=1 on the unpivoted cut, t=Q^(-1) on a pivotal head.

Therefore its SOURCE relation is Z=tY, with the exact factors of (4) or (5). The original pivot scalar x^(1-N) also remains. This is the nilpotent-source contraction (4), not the forbidden pointwise matching of distinct regular Fermat grids or a bare Cauchy pole substitution.

In the anchored graph the closure edges have in-degree and out-degree at most one. A closure-only cycle would follow a physical component through only UNDER encounters and closures, without any overbridge event. A one-component knot with at least one crossing has no such component: each of its self-crossings has an over encounter on the same component. Thus its closure edges form a forest. Contracting them by (4)–(5) removes ALL b boundary-adapter anchors, leaving exactly B anchors, one per open overbridge event (or their canonically identified representatives).

This proves an exact globally sewn original source at the jet fiber. It retains chi_N/N and every pivot phase. It does not claim the raw four-Psi continuation has become periodic away from its sampling grid. For links with a closure-only component, the remaining cycle is instead evaluated by (6) and its accompanying zero-anchor substitution.

3. Balanced star–triangle identity

Let k_1,k_2,k_3>=0, k_1+k_2+k_3=d, and L_i=d-k_i. For arbitrary nonzero a_i, the exact polynomial identity is

[Z^d] product_(i=1)^3 B_(L_i)(X_i,Z;a_i)
   =C a_1^(k_2)a_2^(k_3)a_3^(k_1)
      *B_(k_3)(X_1,X_2;Q^(k_2+1)a_1/a_2)
      *B_(k_1)(X_2,X_3;Q^(k_3+1)a_2/a_3)
      *B_(k_2)(X_3,X_1;Q^(k_1+1)a_3/a_1),      (7)

C=(-1)^(k_1+k_3)
   Q^[k_1(k_1-1)/2+k_3(k_3-1)/2-k_2]
   *[d-k_2 choose k_3]_Q.                       (8)

All lengths and factorials are at most d; the scalar is NONZERO. It must be retained. In particular its modulus need not be tame when all k_i are proportional to N. No half-volume or unknown period factor is absorbed into C.

The balance condition is exactly L_1+L_2+L_3=2d. The three new lengths are k_i=d-L_i. The identity remains valid when some are zero, and after substituting coinciding outer variables.

Proof by root covering and degree

First set every a_i=1. The left side F has total outer degree d. For a cyclic pair (i,j) and the remaining index l, specialize

X_i=Q^p X_j, p=k_j+1,...,k_j+k_l.

The Z-root arcs of the two corresponding blocks are

{Q^(p-r)X_j:0<=r<L_i},
{Q^(-s)X_j:0<=s<L_j}.

Their union contains a FULL orbit of N roots. Equivalently their missing intervals, of lengths k_i+1 and k_j+1, are disjoint at precisely the displayed k_l positions. Hence the complete product of all three blocks is divisible, as a polynomial in Z, by X_jN-ZN. Its residual Z-degree is 2d-N=N-2=d-1. The coefficient of Z^d is therefore zero: the first term cannot reach degree d and the Z^N term already exceeds it.

Thus F is divisible by all three displayed families of linear outer factors on the right of (7). Their total degree is k_1+k_2+k_3=d, exactly deg F, so their quotient is a scalar. The coefficient X_1(L_1)X_2(k_1) at X_3=0 fixes that scalar. It is

(-1)^d Q^[k_3(k_3-1)/2+L_3(L_3-1)/2]
                     [L_2 choose k_3]_Q

on the left, and

(-1)^(k_2) Q^[k_2(k_1+1)+k_2(k_2-1)/2]

in the proposed product. Their ratio simplifies to (8). Finally rescale X_i by a_i and extract the factors a_i^(L_i). The remaining scalar powers are a_1(k_2)a_2(k_3)a_3^(k_1), which proves the full formula (7).

Because (7) is an ordinary polynomial identity, it can be inserted inside ANY original exterior source pairing. The removed central anchor carries exactly its original charge d; every other anchor keeps its original charge. No scalar knot value is used to choose the normalization. Reversing any incident block is permitted with its exact monomial coefficient, so all strand orientations are covered.

Connection and reverse move

For a block of length L, its positive sine-binomial connection is w=-a q^(L-1). If the three star edges are oriented outer-to-center, then the new triangle edges obey exactly

w_(i->j,new)=w_i/w_j.                           (9)

Indeed the extra exponent is k_1+k_2+k_3+1=N and q^N=-1. Thus the new triangle is flat and has the same boundary path phases as the old star. Other nonflat cycles of the complete graph are not erased or declared positive.

Conversely (7), divided by its explicit NONZERO scalar, inserts a charged anchor into a matching flat triangle. This is an exact source-preserving inverse move, including zero-length degeneracies.

More explicitly, orient the triangle cyclically with lengths (k_3,k_1,k_2), sum d, and normalized root parameters (lambda_12,lambda_23,lambda_31). Its connection is flat precisely when

lambda_12 lambda_23 lambda_31=Q^2.              (10)

Choose a_1=1, a_2=Q^(k_2+1)/lambda_12, and a_3=Q^(k_3+1)a_2/lambda_23. Equation (10) supplies the remaining edge. Thus the inverse has no unsolved normalizer or extension-field choice. A common nonzero rescaling of all a_i is simply the scale of the introduced anchor and is canceled by the retained scalar.

This has a genuinely nonzero, nondegenerate typed application inside the original source, rather than only in an auxiliary graph. For the figure-eight word [1,-2,1,-2], its actual clocks

h=(0,a,b,a+b), a>0, b>0, a+b<d,

give the three bulk blocks of lengths (a,b,d-a-b) and holonomy one. The inverse move therefore has all three positive triangle lengths. At N=4, a=b=1, the complete original clock-sector coefficient is exactly 4(q^2-1), hence nonzero. The audit inserts the (2,2,2) star, retains its original exterior and inverse scalar, and checks the same coefficient. This example checks applicability of the general source identity; it is not a proof of nonvanishing for all clocks in the displayed family or a knot-volume asymptotic.

4. What simple source splitting can and cannot balance

The full kernel (3) is also the canonical coproduct kernel of the source pairing. It permits splitting an anchor into two charged anchors joined by a full length-d edge, with an arbitrary partition of the old incident polynomial factors. This is exact by (4).

Let the old incident lengths sum to S. Replace its neighborhood by r charged anchors and a TREE of r-1 such reproducing edges, without changing its external factors. The total incident length, counted at all new vertices, is

S+2d(r-1).

If every new vertex were a balanced star, that sum would be 2dr. Necessarily S=2d. Arc subdivision does not change S. Thus an unbalanced anchor cannot be converted to nothing but balanced stars merely by inserting source identity kernels and partitioning its old factors. A two-factor piece with lengths summing to d does give a balanced star after such a split, but its third edge is full length d and the triangle move has a zero-length edge. This alone is not a nondegenerate geometric triangulation.

The local defect S-2d is additive under full-edge contraction and is unchanged at outer vertices by the balanced star–triangle move. Defects can be transported or combined; they cannot be silently discarded. Additional exact channel sums or a genuinely deficient-star identity would be needed for a universal reduction.

5. Scope of the general advance

The full-kernel contraction performs the original boundary gluing at arbitrary valency. The balanced star–triangle move is an exact higher-valency rule inside the actual charge algebra, with its normalizer fixed and its root-orbit proof given above. Both commute with arbitrary exterior original source pairing and allow repeated application wherever their algebraic hypotheses hold.

They do not prove that every charged knot graph admits an all-balanced trivalent resolution. Nor do they turn a general remaining cycle sum into positive weights or prove its geometric contribution survives. In particular no quantum determinant or general volume theorem is inferred from the existence of these local rules alone.

For coverage only, root-of-unity Fermat and restricted star–triangle identities have substantial precedent, including Faddeev–Kashaev, Quantum Dilogarithm. No claim to the first such identity is intended. The independently proved implementation here is the ordinary polynomial identity with its exact scalar and the actual selected socle-source contraction; no proof or analytic contour from that literature is imported.
