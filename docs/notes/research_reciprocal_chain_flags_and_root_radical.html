Reciprocal lowering flags and the actual root radical

Private written proof, 19 November 2026. Author-final for independent review.

The reciprocal Cartan-pair image of the actual prefix reduction is exactly the endomorphism algebra of a two-periodic exact lowering complex. This identifies its admissibility condition with explicit physical flags. Together with the original first-normal source order, it proves that the Jacobson radical of the entire actual root intertwiner algebra has cube zero, uniformly in the level and width. Only the units congruent to one modulo this radical form the resulting two-step nilpotent group.

The complete directs are P, the first-jet source order, and C, the actual prefix Ward equations. The lowering-chain, division-free Ward, flag, and radical arguments are proved below. This is a statement about the full Cartan-preserving source algebra. Its integer-degree-preserving subalgebra is retained where a braid word requires it, but no separate classification of that subalgebra is asserted.

2. Full physical lowering chains

The operator EE on UU has only Jordan chains of length NN. This follows directly from the actual tensor structure. For one head, its coefficients ea=Q−a−1,1≤a≤d,(8) e_a=Q^{-a}-1,\quad 1\le a\le d, \qquad\text{(8)} are nonzero, and its two outward arrows are zero. For 0≤b≤d0\le b\le d, consider Eb(wd⊗v),v∈Ut.(10) E^b(w_d\otimes v),\qquad v\in U_{\mathrm{ t}}. \qquad\text{(10)} Their least first-head occupation is d−bd-b, with coefficient (ed⋯ed−b+1)Ktb(e_d\cdots e_{d-b+1})K_{\mathrm{ t}}^b. 4. Exact flag classification and the first-normal quotient

Choose complements in the exact complex (11): Uχ=P⊕Q*,P=im⁡A=ker⁡B,Uχ−1=R*⊕T*,R*=im⁡B=ker⁡A.(18) U_\chi=P\oplus Q_*,\qquad P=\operatorname{im}A=\ker B, \qquad U_{\chi^{-1}}=R_*\oplus T_*,\qquad R_*=\operatorname{im}B=\ker A. \qquad\text{(18)} The dimensions are (k,l)(k,l) and (l,k)(l,k), respectively. 5. Cube-zero radical of the whole actual root algebra

On a nonself pair, (5) and (24) identify the entire actual root factor with the trivial extension T(V𝔬)=V𝔬⊕V𝔬*,(b,ϕ)(b′,ϕ′)=(bb′,bϕ′+ϕb′).(25) T(V_{\mathfrak o})=V_{\mathfrak o}\oplus V_{\mathfrak o}^*, \qquad (b,\phi)(b',\phi')=(bb',b\phi'+\phi b'). \qquad\text{(25)} Define 𝔯𝔬=J⊕V𝔬*.(26) \mathfrak r_{\mathfrak o} =J\oplus V_{\mathfrak o}^*. \qquad\text{(26)} This is an ideal. Its quotient is (21). Its cube is zero: a product containing two dual factors is zero, as is a product with two adjacent JJ factors. The only other possible three-factor product is jϕj′j\phi j'. For every v∈Vv\in V, (jϕj′)(v)=ϕ(j′vj)=0,(27) (j\phi j')(v)=\phi(j'vj)=0, \qquad\text{(27)} because JJ is an ideal and J2=0J^2=0. The central pair projectors from P split (5): they are central in the ambient 𝒜\mathcal A and therefore also in its quotient bimodule. Each self-Cartan factor has no normal quotient and is the full semisimple matrix algebra. Consequently, if ℜ\mathfrak R is the Jacobson radical of the actual full root intertwiner algebra on all mm heads, then ℜ3=0.(29) \boxed{\quad\mathfrak R^3=0.\quad} \qquad\text{(29)} The bound does not depend on NN or mm. It does not claim that passing to an arbitrary subalgebra preserves this radical classification or these rank formulas.

6. Finite radical transport and the original source

The radical unit group 1+ℜ1+\mathfrak R has nilpotence class at most two. For X,Y∈ℜX,Y\in\mathfrak R, (1+X)−1=1−X+X2,(1+X)(1+Y)(1+X)−1(1+Y)−1=1+[X,Y],(30) (1+X)^{-1}=1-X+X^2, \quad (1+X)(1+Y)(1+X)^{-1}(1+Y)^{-1}=1+[X,Y], \qquad\text{(30)} and the last commutator is central within 1+ℜ1+\mathfrak R. Equivalently, eX=1+X+12X2,log⁡(1+X)=X−12X2,log⁡(eXeY)=X+Y+12[X,Y].(31) e^X=1+X+\tfrac12X^2,\quad \log(1+X)=X-\tfrac12X^2,\quad \log(e^Xe^Y)=X+Y+\tfrac12[X,Y]. \qquad\text{(31)} These follow by expansion and (29). For any element TT of the actual root algebra, its conjugation is also exact at second order: eXTe−X=T+[X,T]+12[X,[X,T]].(32) e^XTe^{-X}=T+[X,T]+\tfrac12[X,[X,T]]. \qquad\text{(32)} Every omitted term has three radical factors, with ordinary algebra factors between them, and vanishes because ℜ\mathfrak R is an ideal and ℜ3=0\mathfrak R^3=0. The full unit group contains the nonnilpotent matrix groups of (21); it has not been declared nilpotent.

All these coordinates keep the original source. P’s exact formula for a root class (b,[c])(b,[c]) is Φ0(b,[c])=Tr⁡(d0b)+Tr⁡(d−1c),d0=K+K−12ε,d−1=K−K−12εm.(33) \Phi_0(b,[c])=\operatorname{Tr}(d_0b) +\operatorname{Tr}(d_{-1}c), \quad d_0=\frac{K+K^{-1}}{2\varepsilon},\qquad d_{-1}=\frac{K-K^{-1}}{2\varepsilon m}. \qquad\text{(33)} On the pair in (19), this becomes χ+χ−1ε(Tr⁡a+Tr⁡d*)+aχ[Tr⁡k(C11−(D*)22)+Tr⁡l(C22−(D*)11)].(34) \frac{\chi+\chi^{-1}}{\varepsilon} (\operatorname{Tr}a+\operatorname{Tr}d_*) +a_\chi\bigl[ \operatorname{Tr}_k(C_{11}-(D_*)_{22}) +\operatorname{Tr}_l(C_{22}-(D_*)_{11})\bigr]. \qquad\text{(34)} In particular the normal layer is not discarded by the flag classification. Crossings and framing scalars included in an actual word remain in its two jet coefficients; any prescribed outside scalar retains its own product-rule derivatives.

The theorem gives explicit physical flags for admissibility and uniform algebraic nilpotence in the actual finite source. It does not estimate the condition numbers of the chosen flag complements, identify these Cartan pairs with selected geometric eigenflags, or prove positivity or nonvanishing of the closing of a prescribed braid. No geometric Regge or framing group is identified with the radical unit group.
