A sharp complete-circle nodal energy from the original source

29 October 2026. The actual complete-circle packet has the sharp physical-node exponent v8/πv_8/\pi. This follows from an exact discrete Chebyshev identity and the already proved NONZERO finite-source asymptotic on each fixed Bernstein ellipse. It needs no assertion about the phases of all Newton or monomial coefficients.

Consequently a retained complete belt gives a positive original source, with an exact rate for every fixed reflected power. This is an all-channel source theorem. Its interpretation as a family of geometric belted sums requires a separate literal disk-gluing certificate; it is not inferred from the rate.
