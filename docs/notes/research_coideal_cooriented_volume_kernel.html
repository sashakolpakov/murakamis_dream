Every cooriented coideal crossing has a positive volume amplitude

24 November 2026. Research note. Written proof.

The actual mixed coideal coefficient is nonzero throughout the strict cooriented domain, at both index parities. Its calibrated phase is the same quadratic height phase as before, and its logarithmic magnitude is the volume of the sewn ideal octahedron, with a universal logarithmic error over all allowed labels. The unresolved parity is settled by a positive Dirichlet problem for the actual crossing recurrence. Its lower boundary is an exact finite quadrature and its upper boundary is a balanced finite Sears sum with positive terms. A finite forest comparison transfers the already proved neighboring-parity rate.

This is a local source-kernel theorem. The original input, marked closing, external scalar and coherent intermediate sums are still required for an assembled word.
