A regular actual mixed-circle port chart at the belt boundary

29 October 2026. The old diagonal pair chart for the actual mixed circle is singular at w=-1, precisely where the new belt packet is concentrated. This note resolves that local chart with bounded meridian matrices and preserves the FULL threaded-circle equations. It does not select a bare-crossing state/observable convention, nor identify the complete global stack representation from vertex flags.

The later typed global composition now completes the outer closed-leg/peripheral checks kept separate in this local note, and the full filling assembly records the resulting any-subset volume equality. The local calculations below remain the independent input to that global proof.

The local all-port convention is the one in the original literal port lift: incoming rotated pair (g(z),h(z)), outgoing (g(Uz),h(Uz)), with

h(z)g(z)=diag(w,w^(-1)),
U=(wY-1)/(w-Y).                                    (1)

Here Y is the SOURCE-selected inner critical parameter. In the new physical-color packet it is holomorphic near w=-1,Y=i,H=1. It is not the physical meridian.

1. A bounded irreducible pair

Put c=w+w^(-1) and use

a=-[[1,1],[0,1]],
b=-[[1,0],[c-2,1]],
A=ba=[[1,1],[c-2,c-1]].                            (2)

Both main meridians have trace -2 and determinant one. Their product has trace c. At c=-2 it is a NONCENTRAL trace-minus-two parabolic. The fixed flags of a,b are infinity and zero, so the pair stays irreducible for c near -2. This avoids diagonalizing the parabolic product and then calling a diverging diagonal frame bounded.

For w!=+1,-1 this pair is simultaneously conjugate to the original g,h pair: their product eigenvalues and the two parabolic traces agree, and their distinct flags determine the conjugacy on the irreducible chart. The following formulas also give an explicit regular continuation of its rotated action.

Choose sqrt(U) through 1 near w=-1,Y=i, and set

beta=w(Y-1)/[(w-1)(w-Y)sqrt(U)],
alpha=[sqrt(U)+1/sqrt(U)-beta*c]/2,
K=alpha I+beta A.                                 (3)

All displayed denominators are nonzero in a fixed neighborhood. For w!=+1,-1 this equals

beta=[sqrt(U)-1/sqrt(U)]/[w-w^(-1)].               (4)

Indeed U-1=(w+1)(Y-1)/(w-Y). Thus K has determinant one and eigenvalues sqrt(U),1/sqrt(U) on the A-eigenflags w,w^(-1). It is exactly the conjugate of diag(sqrt(U),1/sqrt(U)), which sends g(z) to g(Uz) in the old chart. Consequently the ACTUAL rotated mixed-circle map is

(a,b) -> (K a K^(-1), K b K^(-1)).                 (5)

This is a local all-port statement, not an inference solely from a trace identity. Under w->w^(-1) one has U->U^(-1), and (4) and alpha are unchanged. The regular K therefore descends holomorphically to c near -2 when the selected Y is even in eta for w=-exp(eta). This is exactly the evenness supplied by the physical-color packet.

2. Original unrotated ports and the auxiliary meridian

The literal bends, including their inverses, give the unrotated ports

(a_orig,b_orig)=(K b K^(-1), b^(-1)),
(r_orig,s_orig)=(K a^(-1)K^(-1), a).               (6)

Their common disk product is

P=b_orig a_orig
 =b^(-1)K b K^(-1)
 =a K a^(-1)K^(-1).                               (7)

The last equality uses [K,A]=0, with A=ba. Direct multiplication gives the REGULAR trace formula

D=Tr P
 =2+(w-1)^2(Y-1)^2/[(w-Y)(wY-1)].                (8)

For w!=-1 this is exactly the old formula ((w-1)/(w+1))2(U+U(-1))+8w/(w+1)^2; its apparent pole is removable on the SOURCE relation(1).

Put Delta=1-YD+Y^2, choose sqrt(Delta) through 1+i, and define

J=(I-YP)/sqrt(Delta),
C=(YI-P)/sqrt(Delta).                             (9)

They commute with P, have determinant one, and JC=-P. The original threaded half-twist equations remain EXACTLY

r_orig=C^(-1)b_orig C,
s_orig=C^(-1)b_orig a_orig b_orig^(-1) C.           (10)

One can verify them without roots by using I-YP and YI-P; their common scalar cancels from conjugation. Equivalently Ja_orig=aJ, together with(7) and JC=-P, proves(10). They are rational identities on the old nonsingular chart and both sides are regular here, so their continuation includes w=-1. No group relation is replaced by only its trace.

On the full stationary mixed source, the previously proved identities give D=-(L+L^(-1)), L=(H-X)/(1-HX), and the marked auxiliary meridian C has eigenvalue H^(-1/2) on the P-eigenflag with eigenvalue -L^(-1). This is the same ACTUAL circle and preferred disk-word marking as the typed longitude theorem. The eigenflag may move through the parabolic limit; the matrices in (2)–(10) remain bounded. Nothing here asserts a moving-q continuation.

3. Exact complete matrices and the six octahedral flags

At w=-1,Y=i,H=1,

A=[[1,1],[-4,-3]],
K=I-(i/2)(A+I),
P=[[-1+2i,-1],[-4,-1-2i]],
C=I-(P+I)/(1+i).                                 (11)

The outer flag of A is -1/2; the inner flag of P and C is -i/2. The four actual rotated port flags are

a:infinity, b:0,
KaK^(-1):-(1+i)/2, KbK^(-1):-(1+i)/4.

The HOLomorphic Möbius map

M(zeta)=(zeta+1/2)/(zeta+i/2)                      (12)

sends, in that order, the outer/inner flags to 0/infinity and the four port flags to 1,-i,i,-1. These are the six ideal vertices of a regular octahedron. In this exact frame one obtains

M A M^(-1)=[[-1,0],[2-2i,-1]],
M P M^(-1)=[[-1,-2+2i],[0,-1]],
M C M^(-1)=[[1,-2i],[0,1]],
M J M^(-1)=[[1,2],[0,1]].                         (13)

In particular the actual inner meridian translates by -2i, while the preferred disk product translates by 2-2i (its central sign has no projective effect). Their ratio is 1+i. This is a literal matrix/peripheral calculation, not a ratio guessed from a Hessian. The source prediction agrees: at eta=chi=0 its physical color action has j_chi_chi=(-1+i)/pi; with U_geom=-2i chi and the marked longitude log V_geom=-2 partial_(Log H)(2pi i j), the same ratio is 1+i.

4. The separate global geometric lift

The old local source-to-character construction now extends regularly through the ACTUAL belt saddle and retains every port and auxiliary meridian. Its six complete ideal flags and the inner peripheral word are also explicit. These remove a genuine singular-chart problem.

They do NOT alone identify the full global stack holonomy with its two-octahedron face quotient. 5. The opposite retained half twist, with its actual circle meridian

There is an additional exact all-port statement using the SAME K and P. The positive unrotated half-Hurwitz rule with an auxiliary centralizer Q is

r_orig=Q^(-1) a_orig^(-1)b_orig a_orig Q,
s_orig=Q^(-1) a_orig Q.

It is solved by Q=J^(-1). Indeed Ja_orig=aJ gives the second equation; the first then reduces to a^(-1)P=K a(-1)K(-1), which follows from(7). Thus the opposite retained half twist has circle meridian J^(-1), rather than silently using C unchanged. At completeness this meridian translates by -2 in the frame(12), whereas C translates by -2i. The SAME preferred disk product P translates by 2-2i. Its two primitive cusp ratios are consequently

(2-2i)/(-2i)=1+i,   (2-2i)/(-2)=-1+i.             (14)

These are the actual two half-twist meridians. On the full mixed branch the positive-circle color condition is imposed on J^(-1), not on C. Matching that flag condition to the typed reflected source is still required; one must not reuse the negative-half mixed parameter H without this check. This addition proves both complete typed matrices, not an unproved global sharp/reflection adapter.

6. Every complete white and inner shaded face map

Use the normalized frame(12), now with the PRECISE labels

The white faces of the existing literal two-octahedron cut table are

Their reflections are the following antiholomorphic maps, with c0=(1+i)/2:

r0(z)=c0+(1/2)/(conjugate(z)-conjugate(c0)),
r1(z)=-c0+(1/2)/(conjugate(z)+conjugate(c0)),
r2(z)=i conjugate(z)+1-i,
r3(z)=i conjugate(z)-1+i.                         (15)

Direct Gaussian-rational matrix multiplication gives the FULL PSL isometries (hats denote conjugation by M):

a_hat=r2 r0, b_hat=r1 r2,
a'_hat=r3 r0, b'_hat=r1 r3,
A_hat=r1 r0.                                    (16)

Thus all white-face holonomies are matched, not only the fixed flags of the meridians. Develop the second octahedron as r0(P_+), using W0 as the face-tree pairing.

The inner shaded faces are

C_face=(infinity,1,i), D_face=(infinity,-1,-i).

The literal threaded-port equations give the two shaded pairing maps gamma_+=J a’, gamma_-=b’J(-1)=J(-1)a. In the M frame they are EXACTLY

gamma_+=[[3i,1-i],[1+i,-i]],
gamma_-=[[i,1-i],[-1-i,i]].                       (17)

They have the following complete three-vertex actions:

gamma_+ r0(infinity,-1,-i)=(infinity,1,i),

These are r0(D_face)->C_face and r0(C_face)->D_face, precisely the TWO cross-copy shaded gluings of the actual retained half twist. They check the third vertex as well as the circle flag and one port. A holomorphic Mobius map is uniquely determined by three distinct ideal points; therefore these are the entire face isometries in the existing positive regular-octahedron certificate. 7. The oriented outer-pants boundary word

There is a concrete boundary-path check that is useful for that last peripheral proof. The incoming pants has shaded triangle (X0,Y+,Z+)=(0,1,-i). The holomorphic map

T(zeta)=((1+i)/2)(zeta-1)/zeta

sends its three punctures to infinity, zero, one. Conjugating the EXACT white-face meridians in(16) gives

a_T=[[-1,0],[-2,-1]],
b_T=[[-3,2],[-2,1]],
A_T=[[-1,-2],[0,-1]].                             (19)

Use the upper-half-plane orientation for its doubled ideal triangle. At punctures zero and one, the upper-half-plane cusp coordinates -1/zeta and -1/(zeta-1) make a_T and b_T each translation by -2. At infinity, A_T^(-1) is translation by -2 as well. They are therefore the consistently oriented boundary loops of a compact truncation of the pants: its oriented top horocycle goes to the left. Their based boundary relation is EXACTLY

(A^(-1)) b a=I.                                  (20)

Thus the oriented OUTER boundary word of these actual puncture paths is A^(-1), not A. Reversing the pants orientation reverses all three paths together. This is an oriented face/path certificate, independent of any computed source Hessian.

To call(20) the preferred POSITIVE auxiliary longitude, the actual closed auxiliary orientation and its original ordinary cup/pivot are transported to this pants boundary in the linked typed-composition proof.
