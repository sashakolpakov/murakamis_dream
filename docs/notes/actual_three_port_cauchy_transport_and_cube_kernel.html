Exact three-port Cauchy transport and a nontrivial projected cube kernel

29 October 2026. The whole-support correlated Gram bound removes a local marked normalization cost. It does not by itself prove sharp transport through a graph. Complete-circle three-port sewing observes only the socle of each regular cube and hence reduces to a scalar weighted Cauchy pairing. This note makes that limitation precise, then gives an ACTUAL nontrivial positive cross-color transfer for the cube packet already proved, with its sharp norm and iterated source rate. The eight cycle projections remain in one cube; there is no full-color cube or arbitrary-graph volume-conjecture assertion.

1. What the correlated Gram does under complete-circle observation

On a supported source cell use the exact boundary frame

R_r=C[eta_1,...,eta_r]/eta_i^2,
lambda(fv)=tau[eta_all](q f), q(0)=1,
w_I=q^(-1/2)eta_Iv, <w_I,w_J>=delta_IJ.

The whole-support theorem proves polynomial source cost K and exact dual cost tau^2 K in this frame. But the complete paired-circle product itself is simply

eta_all w_empty=w_all,
eta_all w_I=0 for I!=empty.                    (1)

Thus it has operator norm EXACTLY ONE and rank one. The simple axes contribute their actual scalar N, so the full three-circle product has norm N^(3-r). On its image the original row is

lambda(w_all)=tau,
lambda(eta_all f v)=tau f(0).                  (2)

Equations (1),(2) keep the original marked row; they do not replace it by an ordinary cap. They also show that the complete observation does not measure the lower nilpotent coordinates of f. After this observation, a Cauchy estimate on one cell is an estimate of its one surviving scalar. Polynomial K supplies no independent geometric upper bound for that scalar’s exterior-dependent energy.

2. The exact weighted packet and its available transport

Let D_N be the supported folded triples and put

theta_l=Z_theta(l)>0,
a_G(l)=Z_G(l)/theta_l,
F_G(l)=sqrt(theta_l)a_G(l)=Z_G(l)/sqrt(theta_l).

Here Z_G(l) is the COMPLETE coherent interior source with the three boundary labels fixed, not an individual black vertex. For the exact typed vertex substitution, including the exposed mark and one circle per merged edge, the source sewing theorem gives

Z_new=sum_(l in D_N) theta_l a_A(l)a_B(l)
     =sum_l F_A(l)F_B(l),
|Z_new|^2<=E_A E_B,
E_G=sum_l |Z_G(l)|^2/theta_l.                 (3)

The bilinear sewing map has norm one in this explicitly prescribed Hilbert space. Its Cauchy proof conjugates the first numerical vector; it does NOT assume that one physical graph is the mirror of the other. No extra K or generic inverse-frame factor occurs in (3). The theta weight can be exponential and must not be called a polynomial cost.

Grafting a fixed piece B onto an unsummed boundary is multiplication by a_B. Its norm is EXACTLY max_l |a_B(l)|. Thus sharp control of (3) requires the actual boundary packet or this multiplier, not just an estimate of the closed scalar sum_l Z_B(l). For example, for two supported labels l_1,l_2 the numerical packet a(l_1)=T/theta_(l_1), a(l_2)=-T/theta_(l_2) has closure zero and unbounded energy as T grows. This is a finite-space obstruction to deducing energy from one closure; it is not asserted to be a realizable knot counterexample.

The proved signed-theta boundary has a_theta,e(l)=product_i omega_(l_i)^e_i. It is consequently an exact unitary multiplier in (3). These are actual source-preserving twist operations; theta substitution itself leaves the graph skeleton unchanged. The one-variable complete-pretzel circle metric is a different actual cut and polarization. A bound on its one closed functional does not identify a three-variable F_G or bound E_G without an explicit port-opening identity. No such general identity is inferred here.

3. An actual two-boundary cube transfer beyond a scalar closure

Now take N=2m+1 and the original ordered cube of the four-rung packet. Fix its eight cycle edges to m. Keep the outer-white rung (edge 10) at j, the interval-black rung (edge 1) at l, and sum the other two rung labels (edges 4,7). Use zero rung twists. Set

p_j=P_(N,j)>0, k_j=2 except k_m=1,
h_j=Rturn L_j/(N k_j),
f=sum_(l=0)^m k_l p_l^2 h_l,
theta_j=Z_theta(m,j,m)=N^3 k_j p_j^2.          (4)

The last normalization is exact: in the actual theta formula s_j s_(d-j)=N and the one-leg source has R_N(m,j,m)=p_j. All paired rung nilpotents and the original unrestricted mark remain.

By the proved four-rung SOURCE formula and linearity, the actual two-boundary cube contribution is

B_(j,l)=N^12 k_j k_l p_j^2 p_l^2
                         phi(h_j h_l f^2).    (5)

This is a full coherent magnetic contraction, not a product of unsewn black coefficients. The exposed white boundary is (m,j,m) and the interval black boundary is (m,l,m), with their original orders. Both are supported at every j,l. For a typed graft, the three shared contacts are summed once using the exact denominator theta_l, so its relative-packet transport is

a_new(j)=sum_l B_(j,l)/theta_j *a_old(l).

Equivalently in F=sqrt(theta)a coordinates its matrix is

A_(j,l)=B_(j,l)/sqrt(theta_j theta_l)
    =N^9 sqrt(k_j k_l)p_j p_l phi(h_j h_l f^2). (6)

This is an actual cross-color kernel, not the product of two known closed scalar values. It is real symmetric and ENTRYWISE strictly positive. Indeed all h_j have positive Newton coefficients through their degree j, while f^2 has strictly positive coefficients at every degree 0,…,d. The constant coefficient of h_jh_l is positive, so the actual phi in (6) is strictly positive. The Newton theorem retains the full root algebra and its first normals in this argument.

Entry positivity does not make A a positive semidefinite Gram. At N=3, h_0=1,h_1=1+Bhat_1, p_0=1,p_1=2 and f=6+4Bhat_1. The exact matrix of the phi factors in (6) is

[phi(h_j h_l f^2)]_(j,l=0,1)
        = [[16,80],[80,244]], determinant=-2496. (7)

Thus A itself has determinant -19968*3^18. This is a genuine correlated transfer with both eigenvalue signs; no Hilbert norm square or physical reflection identity is being smuggled into it.

4. Its operator norm has the sharp primitive-core increment

Put h=h_m, S=p_m, W=phi(h^4)>0. Coefficientwise Newton monotonicity and the positive multiplication law give

h_j<=h, p_j<=S,
S^2 h<=f<=2(m+1)S^2 h.

Consequently, with R_N=N^9 S^6 W,

R_N<=A_(m,m)<=[2(m+1)]^2 R_N,
0<A_(j,l)<=2A_(m,m),
A_(m,m)<=||A||_2<=2(m+1)A_(m,m).              (8)

The last lower bound is a diagonal Rayleigh quotient. The upper bound is the maximum row sum for the absolute-entry matrix, or sqrt(||A||_1||A||_infty); symmetry makes those norms equal. It does not require A to be positive semidefinite. Thus (8) is a direct source-preserving weighted Cauchy transport estimate whose exponent is computed independently from positive factorial periods.

The proved actual central scalar and cuboctahedral period theorem give, on all odd levels,

lim (1/N)log||A||_2=6v8/pi+Vol(Q_cube)/pi.

In hyperbolic-volume units this is

lim (2pi/N)log||A||_2
    =12v8+2Vol(Q_cube)
    =Vol(augmented cube)-Vol(augmented theta). (9)

The equality with these ACTUAL complements is the independently proved labeled rectified-core geometry, not a volume-constant fit. It is the precise primitive-core increment in this projected packet.

5. Iteration is a genuine but still projected graph-source family

Start with theta, keeping its two cycle contacts at m and its third label j summed. Repeatedly insert the ordered cube at the interval black vertex (0,1,2), with outer white boundary (0,10,11). Preserve the original exposed mark. At each stage the two cycle contacts remain m and the middle contact is summed as an actual full circle. The exact NC substitution supplies the new Morse word and one circle per merged edge. Equations (3),(5),(6) give, for fixed k>=0,

Z_k^proj=u^T A^k u, u_j=sqrt(theta_j).          (10)

This is the raw original partial source of the resulting graph. It retains ALL 1+3k rung labels and fixes the 2+6k cycle labels to m. The graph has b=1+3k vertices of each color. The one exposed physical mark is never replicated; the formula comes from the already normalized fixed-first-normal quotient at each graft.

Every matrix entry in (10) is positive. Choosing only the central index at each step supplies the lower bound, while (8) and u_j<=sqrt(2)u_m supply the upper bound

N^(3+9k) S^(2+6k) W^k <= Z_k^proj
   <=[2(m+1)]^(3k+1) N^(3+9k) S^(2+6k) W^k.  (11)

For k=1 this is the exact four-rung packet bound; k=0 is the one-rung theta face. Therefore for every fixed k,

lim_(N odd) (2pi/N)log Z_k^proj
      =(4+12k)v8+2k Vol(Q_cube).              (12)

The actual graph geometry is the same sequence of triangular core grafts: its core is k cuboctahedra glued along triangles, with additive volume k Vol(Q_cube). The rectified-core theorem therefore identifies (12) with its ACTUAL augmented complement volume. All cycle twists can be kept as one common unit character; different rung-twist phases do not preserve this positivity argument.

The proved one-leg boundary gap also transfers, without a new stationary-phase assertion. For fixed delta>0, p_j/S<=poly(N)exp(-gamma_delta N) whenever m-j>=delta N. Resolve each f in (5) into its actual complete-circle color sum and iterate the source quotient. Every one of the 1+3k rung labels occurs with its factor k_jp_j^2 ONCE, while every surviving phi factor is bounded above by W using h_j<=h. Therefore

sum_(some rung m-j>=delta N) Z_(k,labels)^proj
   <=poly_(k,delta)(N)exp(-2gamma_delta N)

These are the whole-label one-leg estimates of the actual theta source transported through a specified branching kernel.
