Even-twist Ward transport with the original half crossing retained

Private written proof, 15 November 2026.

Every original integer crossing power can be split into its even twist and a retained half crossing. Applying the Ward operation to the even factor gives scalar endpoint deletion on an arbitrary circle tree, without the odd-power derivative coupons. The retained half crossing has a canonical zero-free analytic representative, so its conjugated Ward operator has an explicit local symbol with the original total power. A rank-two endpoint formula records exactly what changes if the canonical spectral sheet is replaced by an unwrapped Gaussian.

This organizes an available even-twist factorization into an all-exterior operator and simultaneous-current statement. It does not establish a global contour deformation or the original geometric cycle coefficient.

5. The canonical-sheet seam is an explicit endpoint operator

In particular, applying (26) to fnf_n yields 𝒯1,unwϵfn−2qmTm(z/2)fn=−2Π0+m−ϵρΞ0.(29) \mathcal T_{1,\mathrm{unw}}^\epsilon f_n -2q^mT_m(z/2)f_n =-2\Pi_0+\frac{m-\epsilon}{\rho}\Xi_0. \qquad\text{(29)} Thus changing sheets reintroduces a value projector and a normal. There is no contradiction with (18): equality of original residues does permit replacing the polynomial half crossing by its canonical analytic unit. It does not permit replacing its values at translated arguments by the unwrapped Gaussian after a logarithmic endpoint has been crossed. Equations (26)–(29) quantify precisely that distinction.

6. Coverage and scope

The even Gaussian, the retained-half-crossing factorization, the even scalar current, and its full-turn cancellation are prior results in the sources below. The added organization is their exact conjugated all-exterior operator, simultaneous scalar deletion on arbitrary circle trees, zero-free canonical analytic lift, and explicit seam operator.

This removes the odd-current matching coupons from this particular Ward implementation. It does not make every contour boundary a split Ward current, prove that deleted-forest rates are smaller, or give a nonzero original geometric coefficient. The canonical analytic lift does not bound the original polynomial representative away from the spectrum; such a bound is unnecessary for its exact initial residue identity but cannot be assumed during a different contour comparison. The common-chart symbol statement likewise supplies no global contour across the cut or the endpoint seams.
