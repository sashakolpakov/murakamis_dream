The selected amplitude of the exact finite clock contraction

Private written proof, 15 November 2026. No mathematical code, numerical calculation, test, or saved numerical evidence is used. Frozen notes and public manuscripts are unchanged.

The complete singleton clock contraction has an all-orders relative asymptotic on a complex domain containing the physical interior colors. Its leading amplitude is explicit and nonzero. With the canonical Fourier character retained, that amplitude is precisely the normalized Gaussian amplitude of the clock critical point, with coefficient one. This is proved for the actual finite contraction, including both endpoints, by evaluating its exact Pochhammer product.

The conclusion concerns the clock contraction conditional on the physical colors. It does not assert that the remaining physical color sum of an arbitrary short tangle has a nonzero geometric contribution.

6. Composition and the remaining short-component problem

For any fixed braid, the exact joint clock theorem first fixes a conserved physical flow and then gives independent clock intervals (2) at all its crossings. The products (3) may therefore be performed simultaneously, with the original cut, pivots, and exterior scalar unchanged.

If those conditional physical colors range in a compact set satisfying the local hypotheses at every crossing, multiplication of (22) gives a uniform all-orders expansion for the product of the complete rows. The leading amplitudes multiply and are nonzero. This holds for any fixed number of signed crossings, including any fixed short signed component; the constants may depend on that component and on the chosen compact set.

The remaining operation is the coherent sum or integral over physical flows with the prescribed boundary observation. Its neighboring factors share those variables. The theorem supplies no lower bound for that exterior pairing, no automatic localization to the compact set, and no estimate for physical endpoint strata. In particular termwise relative errors cannot be summed as relative errors of a potentially cancelling whole period.

What has now been established unconditionally is the actual local clock contraction’s selected nonzero amplitude, full relative expansion, specified residue-period normalization, and Gaussian coefficient one. The general partial filling theorem still requires the geometric source pairing and complementary estimates for the remaining original physical-flow period.
