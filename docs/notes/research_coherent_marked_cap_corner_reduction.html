The original marked cap becomes a corner in coherent coordinates

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The original transformed closing restricts to one physical corner in the coherent coordinates used to bound the shared boundary resolvent. The ordinary caps remain literal. Its intrinsic dual norm is bounded above and below by explicit powers of the level. No new closing functional is chosen.

There is a further source-faithful reduction. The central integer-degree projection of the coherent packet retains the entire invariant tensor at every width. The original input and closing in this smaller packet have exact polynomial norm product. Earlier complete source modules already have central-degree coordinates; the new statements identify this particular orthogonal projection and this coherent marked row. They do not bound the remaining coherent word or prove its volume rate.

7. Scope and earlier complete reductions

[M] already gives complete all-width central-degree source modules, bounded-degree Casimir formulas and polynomial original boundaries. At four heads, the coordinate f|d,0⟩f|d,0\rangle is exactly (xd/N)Jw(x^d/N)Jw in its opposite highest chart, where w,Jw,J are [M]’s original multiplicity vector and recoupling matrix. [O] already proves faithful observations with explicit reconstruction and actual source constraints. [P] already retains every repeated-root Casimir normal and gives the original endpoint functional. [T] already gives the coherent metric, its full input cost and the literal physical closing row. [D] already proves the regular divided-current kernel and its exact source dimension.

The additions are the exact source slice (11), the resulting cheap extension of the original coherent closing, the actual-source dual sandwich (23), and the identification of the faithful orthogonal degree projection (24), with the actual generators on that projected packet. The finite Euler tools, the existence of central multiplicity coordinates and the earlier source kernel are not claimed new.

All statements concern the actual fixed-root source. Differentiated source families keep their original inhomogeneous normal equations. No geometric saddle, arbitrary coherent scalar noncancellation, or finite-exception volume theorem after general short partial fillings follows from the polynomial boundary budget alone.
