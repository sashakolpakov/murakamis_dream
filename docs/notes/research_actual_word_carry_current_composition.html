Carry-projector Ward decay through an actual fixed braid word

Private written proof, 19 November 2026.

The normalized coherent estimate for a carry-projector current can be propagated through a literal finite braid word on an explicit open domain. The proof sums the actual crossing transfers before estimating the current. It does not multiply normalized coherent symbols or assume a crossing amplitude is a unit.

The propagation cost is a positive linear map on output moduli. Its finite composition gives a computable exponential base for the complete word. On the domain where that base is less than one, all fixed coherent derivatives and genuine meridian jets of the composed current decay exponentially. The physical caps, crossing scalars, and original zero-framing line remain explicit. No assertion places an arbitrary geometric critical point or closing cycle in this domain.
