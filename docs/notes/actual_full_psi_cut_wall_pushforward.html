Exact pole pushforward with the original scalar cut-wall current retained

28 October 2026. The absolutely convergent full-Psi flow cycle can be pushed to a bare full-function head period PLUS an explicit scalar current on the negative-ray atomic cut walls. The current is an oriented tubular boundary of a specified radius homotopy; all wall intersections are retained with a common regularization. It is not an unknown matrix normalization or an asserted zero correction.

This supplies an allowed exact cycle deformation and locates its additional chains. It still does not prove geometric survival or a sharp global bound on those chains. A cycle passing through a regular critical point does not determine its Lefschetz intersection number.

The audit and results check full-function Cauchy pushforwards for both signs and a coupled two-atom source with two distinct angular cuts. The latter explicitly has a nonzero correction; bare-circle substitution fails.

1. A scalar full-function Cauchy identity

Use the exact moment chain M_(epsilon,R) of (3) in the flow note. For 0<|A|<R off the negative real ray,

M_(epsilon,R)[1/(1-A/t)]
                  =Psi_epsilon(Log_principal A).        (1)

This is an ordinary cut-disk Cauchy formula, not a formal coefficient expansion on its radial piece. For epsilon=+1 integrate Psi(Log t)dt/(t-A) around the disk cut on the negative axis, with an inner circle of radius delta. Its inner-circle integral tends to zero since Psi tends to 1 and A is fixed nonzero. On the two cut lips, Psi_lower=(1-t^N)Psi_upper. Their difference gives exactly the negative-ray term in M. The residue at t=A is Psi(Log A). Conjugate reflection gives epsilon=-1, including its orientation.

Equation (1) cannot be applied to the original flow cycle with fixed head variables without moving that cycle: its head radii depend on the moment radii. That move is the source of the current below.

2. A global target head torus with no hidden large source

First perform the exact HEAD Hardy-forest contraction from Section 8 of the flow note. Its continuous flow polytope is unchanged, and its reduced whole-source period is absolutely convergent. The remaining head-dependent poles are ONLY

D_j: 1-A_j(z,xi)/t_j=0.                         (2)

Every A_j is a primitive ratio of distinct retained head segments, times its explicit color/Gaussian coefficient. In particular it is not a constant self-loop.

Choose finite outer moment radii R_j>=1 and target head log radii lambda_*(xi), independent of all t_j, such that

|A_j|<=a_j^max<R_j,
|lambda_*|<=C(1+sum|xi|).                      (3)

Such a target exists by the original reduced small-argument radius construction with moment radii set to one. More generally it may be chosen to have ANY prescribed finite head radii at a fixed Gaussian tuple xi_g: use that prescribed vector in a compact xi neighborhood, the small-argument construction outside it, and a log-convex smooth interpolation. Enlarge the finitely many R_j to cover the compact modified neighborhood. All bounds in (3) remain uniform in xi.

The flow proof extends verbatim from R_j=1 to any fixed finite R_j: replace -log|t_j| in its bound by its positive part; on 1<=|t_j|<=R_j all extra radial powers are bounded by R_j^N. Thus the original source still has an absolute Gaussian majorant with explicit exp(C_word N) constants. No discarded exterior-radius estimate is being substituted for the exact ray chain.

At the target, integrate each full moment chain first. Equation (1) gives the bare product of Psi_epsilon(Log_principal A_j), with exactly the original monomials, Gaussian and coupled K_N sources. This target period is well-defined as an ordinary angular cut period. Its moment form is locally absolutely integrable even where a radial pole meets the head torus: integrate its real radial distance first to obtain at most a logarithm of the angular distance to Arg A_j=pi. Products of finitely many such logarithms are integrable. Indeed Holder’s inequality reduces this to finite powers of one logarithm of a nonzero torus character, whose Haar integral is finite. Each radial variable is separate even if some atomic angular characters coincide. Condition (3) prevents circle-pole intersections.

The head source contributes at most exp(CN(1+sum|xi|)) times a fixed power of N. All A_j stay in a bounded disk, where the selected principal-log full function is bounded at each N, including its continuous limit at zero. Therefore the target period is jointly absolutely convergent with its Gaussian variables as well.

3. The exact global homotopy and its correction current

Let lambda_0(t,xi) be the reduced flow-dual radii, and use the single angle-INDEPENDENT homotopy on the FULL head-angle torus

lambda_s=(1-s)lambda_0+s lambda_*, 0<=s<=1.      (4)

There is no restriction to an isolated angular box whose side prisms would be omitted. The form is the original scalar full-function form, first with the exact K_N cap kernels integrated as polynomials. Source interval versions can be reinserted using their exact relative derivative on logarithmic seams.

On every moment circle, both endpoints of (4) have |A_j|<R_j. Log-convex interpolation preserves this, so no D_j is crossed on circle pieces. On a moment ray t_j=-r_j, a crossing of D_j requires

A_j=t_j<0.                                    (5)

These are the only extra pole loci in the head-radius homotopy. All head angular boundaries are paired globally. If one instead partitions into angular chambers, their side prisms MUST be included; they cancel pairwise off (5), with the remaining pole tubes retained.

For complete precision, truncate every radial variable at a common delta>0, truncate the Gaussian box, and remove a common small tubular neighborhood of the union of (2) from the homotopy (4). Apply Stokes to its complement. Retain the ENTIRE induced tubular boundary, including all its matched intersections/corners; do not replace it by independent unsupported simple residues. Denote its integral, with the complement’s boundary orientation, by W_(delta,epsilon). The matched full-Psi logarithmic lips are exactly those in M, so their outer endpoints cancel using the original monodromy. On a fixed radial end face the full form has dt_j=0. Gaussian end faces vanish in the limit by its majorant. One obtains

P_original=P_bare+W,
W=lim_(delta->0,epsilon->0) W_(delta,epsilon),    (6)

with the SAME common regularization for all tube pieces. The sign is fixed by the complement’s boundary orientation. This aggregate limit exists because the initial and target periods just proved absolute have their corresponding limits and Stokes gives their difference. Separate periods for individual intersecting or divergent tube pieces are not assigned.

The non-pole source-prefactor bound during (4) follows from convexity of its support function J:

J(lambda_s)<=(1-s)J(lambda_0)+s J(lambda_*)
      <=d sum_j max(0,-log r_j)+CN(1+sum|xi|).

Away from a fixed logarithmic pole tube the rational kernels are bounded. This justifies the finite-cutoff homotopy and its Gaussian limit, while the common tube regularization retains every pole term. No statement that W is small or zero is part of (6).

4. The simple-wall formula fixes the retained source and sign

For one primitive head coordinate u=A and a Laurent test F(u), assume no other pole is crossed. If its initial radius obeys |u|<rho|t| and the target radius is a<R, the negative-ray pole u=t is crossed for 0<r<a. The head kernel has residue

Res_(u=t) F(u)du/[u(1-u/t)]=-F(t).

The resulting correction in (6) is exactly

W=-epsilon/(2*pi*i) integral_0^a
     Psi_epsilon(log r+i epsilon pi)
                       (-r)^N F(-r) dr/r.      (7)

Taking F(u)=u^(-b), 0<=b<N, gives precisely the original coefficient identity v_b=C_b-J_b. Thus this pushforward does not silently discard the low Cauchy moments of t^N Log t. For multiple atomic poles (6) retains their common tubular boundary, with (7) as its simple stratum.

5. Actual wall geometry, and what remains on the critical path

A simple wall retains the explicit charge A_j^N. Its leading action is W_0+2pii Log A_j. Regular transverse iterations give integer sums of these marks. The exponentiated actual canonical/Hurwitz equations and the original caps are unchanged. The independently proved regular critical-regulator theorem shows that EVERY regular FULL interior critical point of such a wall action has its actual boundary-parabolic representation volume. The atom argument on the cut is flat; it need not be a physical degenerate meridian clock. Normalization ray cells can still carry the full geometric rate and must be grouped exactly.

There is also an exact branch formula on the specified principal angular domain. If epsilon Arg A<0, then

Psi_epsilon(Log A)
  =(1-A^N)Psi_epsilon(Log A+epsilon*2*pi*i).     (8)

The shifted logarithm is in the selected epsilon strip. Otherwise the principal logarithm already is in that strip. On compact angular cells away from the real rays, the uniform full-Psi symbol therefore applies to an EXACT finite integer-mark expansion. This does not assert that arbitrary Stokes branches in other domains are exhausted; positive-ray interfaces and all cut/corner chains remain part of the source.

The Gaussian contour may also be shifted by any bounded constant imaginary vector, or a bounded endpoint-fixed deformation through a prescribed finite complex tuple. Apply the exact scalar source identity first: its remaining Gaussian integrand is a finite sum of entire exponential moments, with Gaussian decay on horizontal lines. The adapted flow radii satisfy the same bound with bounded imaginary parts, so this is an allowed full scalar cycle deformation, not an assumption that a fixed-circle Gaussian interchange is valid.

Consequently a regular target head/Gaussian/cap chart can be specified through a given finite geometric point. The exact price is the current W in (6), not an unspecified comparison factor. Neither passing through that point nor the unit normal residue proves that the resulting whole cycle has nonzero geometric-thimble coefficient. Global growth near singular real-axis interfaces, non-full endpoint strata, approximate critical escape and the selected geometric lower bound still require proof. Equation (6) locates these issues in explicit scalar chains; it does not resolve them by notation.
