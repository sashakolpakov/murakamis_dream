Exact root-character contraction and a correlated spectral localization lemma

31 October 2026. Internally reviewed original-source theorem. The exact root-character contraction retains the full repeated-root algebra and all first normal directions. A correlated spectral estimate proves the whole-source alias and physical-color gaps for every fixed root star C(hT(hs))C(hT(h^s)), s≥2s\ge2, with independent colors and arbitrary retained half-twist signs. This is a source theorem, separate from the marked geometric and whole-period steps needed for a filled-knot conclusion. No corresponding spectral estimate for an arbitrary turned child is assumed.

The missing winding in the exact Fourier identity was already proved in the outer squared-kernel theorem, §§1–4. The residue calculation and periodic endpoint repair below provide an alternative proof and retain the same normal-coordinate cancellation. The prior-source comparison also records the exact all-inner-odd belt-source overlap; no novelty or topological classification is asserted.

Put d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, κ=qd2/2\kappa=q^{d^2/2}, and PN(z)=2TN(z/2)+2P_N(z)=2T_N(z/2)+2. The physical pair algebra is ℋN=ℂ[z]/(PN)\mathcal H_N=\mathbb C[z]/(P_N). The actual marked closure and normalized Newton basis obey

ϕ(f)=(−1)dN2[zd](fmod⁡PN),ϕ(B̂iB̂j)=𝟏i+j=d,TB̂i=B̂d−i. \begin{aligned} \phi(f)&=(-1)^dN^2[z^d](f\bmod P_N),\\ \phi(\widehat B_i\widehat B_j)&=\mathbf1_{i+j=d},\\ T\widehat B_i&=\widehat B_{d-i}. \end{aligned}

These are the original source identities in the exact source compiler, §1, and the finite invariant normalization. In particular T is a linear involution, not an algebra automorphism. Coefficient reversal gives, for all F,G∈ℋNF,G\in\mathcal H_N,

ϕ((TF)G)=ϕ(F(TG)),ϕ((TF)(TG))=ϕ(FG). \phi((TF)G)=\phi(F(TG)),\qquad \phi((TF)(TG))=\phi(FG).\tag{1}

The actual circle is Lj=(−1)j−1Uj−1(z/2)L_j=(-1)^{j-1}U_{j-1}(z/2), 1≤j≤N1\le j\le N. A retained half twist is the original RεR^\varepsilon, ε∈{0,+1,−1}\varepsilon\in\{0,+1,-1\}. Consequently the literal root-leaf contraction is

All child colors, all half twists in E, the root color j, the marked closure and the normal coefficients remain unchanged. Equation (2) is a scalar source identity. No tangle isotopy or new framing follows from it.
