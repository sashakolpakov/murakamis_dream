Independent audit of the constrained common-Verma word

Audit, 27 October 2026. The proof in constrained_verma_word_source_extension.md is sound, with the source-comparison qualification now explicit in Section 4. No new tests were needed.

Each crossing preserves total integer index. Thus the chosen finite ambient module has dimension (bNb)\binom{bN}{b}, contains the entire head, and is invariant under every crossing of either sign. The two opposite first-exit/first-return factors give the printed δ,w\delta,w blocks. Only the m−1m-1 internal word times require tail variables; discarded final tail output cannot affect the head cut. This is a sufficient construction, not a minimal-rank claim.

The tail matrix F=I−DF=I-D has strictly lower word-time DD, so its inverse is a finite sum. Direct ordered block multiplication gives UFLVF=diag⁡(E−δwBF−1C,F). U_FLV_F=\operatorname{diag}(E-\delta wBF^{-1}C,F). The memory entry bjdj−1⋯di+1cib_jd_{j-1}\cdots d_{i+1}c_i records exactly one tail visit. Each such visit advances time by at least two; therefore the claimed finite excursion expansion and the bound ⌊m/2⌋\lfloor m/2\rfloor are correct. The original finite word is its zero-excursion coefficient, not the unrestricted ambient word.

For the constrained extension, the printed unipotent matrices satisfy, over any associative ring and in the displayed order, U(AB0CDI0I0)V=diag⁡(A,(0II0)). U\begin{pmatrix}A&B&0\\C&D&I\\0&I&0\end{pmatrix}V =\operatorname{diag}\!\left(A, \begin{pmatrix}0&I\\I&0\end{pmatrix}\right). This does not invert a wall, the head operator, or the tail operator. The source map h↦(h,0,δCh)h\mapsto(h,0,\delta Ch), residual map f↦(f,0,0)f\mapsto(f,0,0), and retraction (f,g,k)↦f+wBk(f,g,k)\mapsto f+wBk satisfy all stated chain identities. The preserved cut is literally the original head functional.

Differentiating those whole chain identities gives the first-normal cokernel formula. The terms involving the derivative of the embedding lie in the special-fiber image, as required; the exact whole identity also preserves all higher jets and the normal co-source. A gauge carries the tail inclusion and projection themselves, so their transformed projector retains its actual irregular exponential. Keeping a regularized generator module while deleting that projector would change the complex.

An important presentation distinction was clarified during this audit. The cited scalar-to-companion construction has target M=TN−IM=T^N-I. That target is not automatically the word-time head operator EE. Applying the conditional formula $K_{\rm con}(I_0J)=(I_1R)P$ to this actual word still requires a supplied chain map EJ=RPEJ=RP; alternatively the general algebraic extension theorem can be applied directly with A=MA=M. The revised note states this distinction explicitly and does not claim to construct the missing actual quantum-A/network map.

The result is therefore a genuine whole pointed presentation of the constrained finite word. It neither identifies the unconstrained period with that word nor proves a geometric source, source-flatness, or a physical large-level norm.
