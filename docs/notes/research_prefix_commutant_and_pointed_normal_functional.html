Prefix commutants and the original pointed normal functional

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The actual total-prefix change of coordinates reduces an intertwiner to a polynomial in one nilpotent raising operator. The remaining lowering equation is an explicit finite recursion. The original highest-cut, pivotal source retains every coefficient of that polynomial; it does not in general factor through its constant coefficient at the complete root.

There is nevertheless a constructive reduction through the whole meridian germ of that constant coefficient. It is a universal meromorphic trace functional whose value on an actual analytic intertwiner is removable. Its pole order is at most N−1N-1, so a meridian jet of order at most N−1N-1 suffices. No minimal-depth or uniform norm assertion is made.

A universal two-head Casimir coupon detects the distinction. Its constant coefficient vanishes at the root, whereas its original pivotal partial trace is NIN I. It is square-zero, and adding any multiple of it to the identity gives an invertible intertwiner with the same constant coefficient and a varying pointed source. This is an obstruction inside the actual intertwiner/coupon algebra, not a counterexample about a knot or the image of the braid group.

The complete directs are P, the actual relative prefix transition, and M, the original pivotal and cut conventions. All additional algebra and source evaluations are proved below.

7. Retained source scope and quantum normals

The statements above use the actual root-quantum meridian family. They do not identify it with a fixed-NN ordinary-color quantum normal path. On the whole q,Mq,M germ, P proves instead S−1FΣS=F1+JN,JN=fd−1S−1FΣN(T0d⊗I).(42) S^{-1}F_\Sigma S=F_1+J_N,\qquad J_N=f_d^{-1}S^{-1}F_\Sigma^N(T_{0d}\otimes I). \qquad\text{(42)} Its quantum normal generally survives.
