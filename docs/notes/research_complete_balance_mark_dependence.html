Complete-radius balance depends on the original marked cap

PRIVATE exact observation, 31 October 2026. This concerns the finite geometric sufficient condition, not invariance or failure of the actual knot invariant. It uses TWO legitimate original top-cap marks on the SAME literal source word. No arbitrary local re-framing or new quantum mark is introduced.

Use the already proved width-three word D=(1,3,2,4,1,3). Its face labels are u,a0,a1,a2,e1,e2,b1,b2 =0,…,7, and its original quartets are

(1,0,4,2),(2,0,5,3),(4,2,6,5), (5,3,7,0),(1,4,0,6),(6,5,0,7). (1)

With the FIRST top cap marked the protected pair is(0,1). Its normalized packing is exactly

a1=(sqrt2,1;1), a2=(2-sqrt2,r;r), e1=(0,3/2;1/2), e2=(0,1/2;1/2), b1=(-sqrt2,1;1), b2=(sqrt2-2,r;r), r=3-2sqrt2,

between linesY=0,2. This original mark passes Q-balance at all six ports.

The SECOND original top-cap mark has protected pair(0,6), as the literal Morse compiler confirms. Its contact on Y=0 is p=-sqrt2. The ONE global Möbius transformation

                     w=-4/(z+sqrt2)                   (2)

sends that contact to infinity, keeps the lower halfplane as u, and sends the b1 disk to the upper halfplaneY>=2. It maps the entire same packing, retaining all source labels and contact orders. Its actual curvatures are

(u,a0,a1,a2,e1,e2,b1,b2) =(0,1,2,3+2sqrt2,2,1,0,1). (3)

Indeed a circle(X,Y;R) not through p has transformed curvature [((X-p)2+Y2-R^2)/(4R)]; the old lineY=2 becomes curvature1. The original b1 disk becomes the zero-curvature upper line. Every other listed value follows directly. This formula is a common whole-packing mark change, not independent modification of triangle weights.

At the fourth port(5,3,7,0), the new quartet is

              (x,b,c,y)=(1,3+2sqrt2,1,0).

Let F(r)=Q(1,r,0)=(2/pi)Ti2(sqrt r), with the original source constant. Put t=sqrt2-1 and G=Ti2(1), Catalan’s constant. The balance gap at this port is exactly

F((1+sqrt2)2)+F(1)-log((1+sqrt2)2) =(2/pi)[Ti2(t)+G]-log(1+sqrt2). (4)

It is strictly NEGATIVE, with an elementary rational certificate:

Ti2(t)<t<29/70, G<1-1/9+1/25=209/225, pi>31/10, log(1+sqrt2)>log(12/5) >2[7/17+(7/17)3/3+(7/17)5/5]. (5)

For completeness, sqrt2>140/99 and <99/70 follow by squaring rational bounds. Thus t>41/99, and pi=8atan(t)>8[41/99-(41/99)^3/3] =9092816/2910897>31/10. The Ti2 and Catalan estimates are their elementary integral/alternating series bounds; the logarithm bound is the positive atanh series. The exact difference of the lower logarithm and upper first term in(4) is

2[7/17+(7/17)3/3+(7/17)5/5] -(20/31)(209/225+29/70) =114290888/13864903605 >1/200.

Therefore the second-cap marked source fails complete-radius balance by at least1/200 at this actual port, whereas the first-cap marked source is balanced. In particular a legitimate different protected edge CAN remove a failed balance test for the same literal diagram (choose the first cap instead of the second).

The conclusion is only presentation/mark dependence of this sufficient geometric test. It does not show a failure of any source volume equality, and it does not assert that every failing diagram has a successful mark. Applying a quantum theorem after changing a mark must still retain that original cap’s exact source conventions; no generic open-word regulator invariance is used here.
