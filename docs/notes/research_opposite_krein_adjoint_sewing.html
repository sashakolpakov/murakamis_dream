The opposite source kernel sews by its actual Krein adjoint

Written research proof, 23 November 2026.

The actual opposite-polarization kernel has an explicit adjoint after the prefix Krein descent. One frozen Cartan factor converts it into a single kernel whose reflected meridian value is its Krein adjoint. The original pointed source is consequently an exact adjoint sewing of that kernel with the actual reduced word. Every scalar, meridian pole and root finite part is retained.

The form is indefinite. This is not a positive trace or a lower bound for the marked scalar. No geometric window or complementary current is removed by the adjoint identity.

4. Source normalization gives one kernel and its adjoint

Retain exactly [O]’s notation for the scalar normalization: 𝒟N(z)=zN−z−Nz−z−1,γN(M)=(−1)d𝒟N(M)/N,aN(M)=(−1)dNγN(M)m,ℋM=𝒟N(MKr(M)),JM=ℋM−1TM.(15) \begin{gathered} \mathcal D_N(z)=\frac{z^N-z^{-N}}{z-z^{-1}},\quad \gamma_N(M)=(-1)^d\mathcal D_N(M)/N,\quad a_N(M)=(-1)^dN\gamma_N(M)^m,\quad \mathcal H_M=\mathcal D_N(MK_{\mathrm{ r}}(M)),\quad J_M=\mathcal H_M^{-1}T_M . \end{gathered} \qquad\text{(15)} The Laurent-polynomial continuation defines 𝒟N\mathcal D_N at z=±1z=\pm1. It is not an additional factor NN, a reset cap, or an unidentified opposite word. The density identity holds for any remaining matrix BB; identifying it with Φ0(W)\Phi_0(W) uses the actual intertwiner and original source formula just stated.

For real positive MM, the scalar in (20) is exactly 𝒟N(M)aN(M)=σ(N𝒟N(M))m−1,σ=(−1)d(m+1).(21) \frac{\mathcal D_N(M)}{a_N(M)} =\sigma\left(\frac{N}{\mathcal D_N(M)}\right)^{m-1}, \qquad\sigma=(-1)^{d(m+1)}. \qquad\text{(21)} It is real; the sign is retained. 5. Root finite parts and scope

For the meridian germ about completeness put M=euM=e^u. The holomorphic reflected adjoint means A♮♯(u)=ℋrA(u‾)¯𝖳ℋrA^{\natural\sharp}(u)= \mathscr H_r\overline{A(\bar u)}^{\mathsf T}\mathscr H_r, not a pointwise conjugation of an unrelated complex meridian. Polarization of (19) gives A♮♯(u)=A(−u)A^{\natural\sharp}(u)=A(-u). Thus (20) continues meromorphically with the literal product A(−u)Bβ(eu)A(u)A(-u)B_\beta(e^u)A(u) and the even scalar (21). All root finite parts are taken after this whole product. The original source is analytic there; its individual kernel factors need not be.

In particular [O] gives at most a simple pole. If A(u)=∑j≥−1AjujA(u)=\sum_{j\ge-1}A_ju^j, then exactly Aj♯=(−1)jAj,A−12=0.(23) A_j^\sharp=(-1)^jA_j,\qquad A_{-1}^2=0. \qquad\text{(23)} The second statement follows because the right side of (19) has at most a simple pole, so the coefficient of u−2u^{-2} in A(u)A(−u)A(u)A(-u) vanishes. Neither residue nor its normal terms is discarded.

For the ACTUAL unwindowed braid there is a stronger matrix cancellation. Put B+(M)=Bβ(M)B_+(M)=B_\beta(M), and let B−(M)B_-(M) be the word with reflected head labels of [O, (11)]. That exact intertwining and commutation of B−B_- with K0K_0 give B+(M)AM=AMB−(M̂)B_+(M)A_M=A_MB_-(\widehat M). Thus AM̂B+(M)AM=aN(M)ℋM̂−1B−(M̂).(24) A_{\widehat M}B_+(M)A_M =a_N(M)\mathcal H_{\widehat M}^{-1}B_-(\widehat M). \qquad\text{(24)} The right side has at most a simple meridian pole as a MATRIX. Consequently A−1Bβ(1)A−1=0A_{-1}B_\beta(1)A_{-1}=0 as a matrix. This uses the actual word intertwining, before taking any trace.

An arbitrary holomorphic middle insertion C(u)C(u), without that intertwining, can instead give a nonzero matrix coefficient −A−1C(0)A−1-A_{-1}C(0)A_{-1} at u−2u^{-2}. Its complete unwindowed trace is zero by cyclicity and (23). A further outer window, or separate windows on the two kernel factors, need not retain that trace cancellation; their actual relative defects must be kept. The first-meridian-jet source remains the original one reconstructed in [O] and [D]. Quantum-parameter normals are different and are not differentiated in this proof.

The gain is a genuine adjoint sewing of the original source, using a specified single kernel and its reflected meridian value. No assertion is made that a partial coherent resolution commutes with that kernel, that its relative-window defect vanishes, or that the actual geometric cycle is selected. The two tails retain all NrN^r physical coordinates and every root Jordan direction. All exterior rows, pivots, framing and modified-dimension factors retain their original positions and their own conjugate factors. No positive norm, two-eigenline noncancellation, or marked scalar lower bound follows from (20).
