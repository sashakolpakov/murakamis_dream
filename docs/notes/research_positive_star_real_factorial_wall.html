A real negative factorial argument at a positive primitive star

Private written proof, 15 November 2026.

Positive primitive geometry does not force the canonical Newton factorial arguments into their original strip. For every integer central power n≥5n\ge5, a sufficiently large fixed degree admits an original positive star whose two-leaf multiplication gate has a real argument x+y−zx+y-z strictly between −1-1 and 00. The outside angles are prescribed and noncomplete. This is not an exhibited knot exterior or a complete global filling.

All leaves in the construction are identical. Consequently every binary multiplication tree in the resolution that leaves one factor for the final pairing and puts all crossings after the products contains this gate. No assertion is made about redistributing crossings among proper subproducts.

1. An exact positive star with its primitive period

Fix n∈ℤn\in\mathbb Z, n≥5n\ge5, and put θ=2πn∈(0,π/2),t=θ−iH,a=tan⁡(t/2),r=nH2d,H>0.(1) \theta=\frac{2\pi}{n}\in(0,\pi/2),\qquad t=\theta-iH,\qquad a=\tan(t/2),\qquad r=\frac{nH}{2d},\qquad H>0. \qquad\text{(1)} Here d≥3d\ge3 will be a fixed integer. In fact ℜa>0,ℑa<0\Re a>0,\ \Im a<0, so ℜw>0,ℑw<0\Re w>0,\ \Im w<0; the square root in (2) has positive real part and negative imaginary part. All these functions are regular near any fixed H>0H>0.

2. The actual two-leaf prefix labels

Allocate no crossing to the first products. The original prefix dictionary gives the two identical input labels and their output: x=y=1πarctan⁡(tanh⁡ra),z=1πarctan⁡(tanh⁡(2r)a).(8) x=y=\frac1\pi\arctan\!\left(\frac{\tanh r}{a}\right), \qquad z=\frac1\pi\arctan\!\left(\frac{\tanh(2r)}{a}\right). \qquad\text{(8)} For large dd, these are the analytic inverse-tangent branches near zero; no integer lift is reset. They correspond to the returns r,r,2rr,r,2r, respectively. The two-leaf set is proper when d≥3d\ge3. Their branch also agrees with continuation from positive real labels: for R>0R>0 and ℜa>0,ℑa≤0\Re a>0,\ \Im a\le0, the argument tanh⁡R/a\tanh R/a has positive real part, and the inverse tangent is analytic there with its value fixed on the positive real axis. This gives the stated lift without crossing either inverse-tangent pole.

3. A transverse real negative value

Theorem. For every fixed integer n≥5n\ge5, and every sufficiently large fixed integer dd, there is Hd=H0+On(d−2)(16) H_d=H_0+O_n(d^{-2}) \qquad\text{(16)} for which the original positive primitive star (1)–(7) has Δd(Hd)∈(−1,0),Δd(Hd)=−n3H03cos⁡θ4πsin⁡3θd−3+On(d−5).(17) \boxed{\quad \Delta_d(H_d)\in(-1,0),\qquad \Delta_d(H_d)= -\frac{n^3H_0^3\cos\theta}{4\pi\sin^3\theta}\,d^{-3} +O_n(d^{-5}). \quad} \qquad\text{(17)}

Proof. Regard δ=1/d\delta=1/d temporarily as a real parameter, with r=nHδ/2r=nH\delta/2. The quotient F(δ,H)=πΔd(H)2r3(18) F(\delta,H)=\frac{\pi\Delta_d(H)}{2r^3} \qquad\text{(18)} has a removable extension at δ=0\delta=0. At that solution ℜF<0\Re F<0 for small positive δ\delta, by (14). Substituting r=nH(δ)δ/2r=nH(\delta)\delta/2 and (14) in (10) proves (17). Positivity and the complete central period were already proved exactly in (5)–(7). ▫\square

All the other arguments of this multiplication gate remain nonreal with positive imaginary part. Indeed xx, zz, 2x2x, and z−xz-x have respective leading terms rπ(A+iB),2rπ(A+iB),2rπ(A+iB),rπ(A+iB),(19) \frac r\pi(A+iB),\quad \frac{2r}\pi(A+iB),\quad \frac{2r}\pi(A+iB),\quad \frac r\pi(A+iB), \qquad\text{(19)} with B(H0)>0B(H_0)>0. The only real argument among its seven factorial arguments is the negative Δd(Hd)\Delta_d(H_d). It is noninteger, so this does not contradict the proper-prefix sine exclusions.

4. Source and resolution scope

The original gate is μN(Nx,Ny,Nz)=GN(N(x+y))GN(Nz)GN(Nx)GN(Ny)GN(N(z−x))GN(N(z−y))GN(N(x+y−z)).(20) \mu_N(Nx,Ny,Nz)= \frac{G_N(N(x+y))G_N(Nz)} {G_N(Nx)G_N(Ny)G_N(N(z-x))G_N(N(z-y)) G_N(N(x+y-z))}. \qquad\text{(20)} Its last argument at (17) belongs to the real chamber (−1,0)(-1,0), with denominator multiplicity −1-1. The exact factorial chamber formula therefore retains the factor 1−eτ2πiNΔd(Hd)(21) 1-e^{\tau2\pi iN\Delta_d(H_d)} \qquad\text{(21)} for either indicated continuation side τ=±1\tau=\pm1, together with the corresponding marked action and nonzero local remainder. This factor is not an asymptotically constant unit on a neighborhood of that real point. Equation (17) by itself does not assert that it vanishes at that fixed point for any particular level.

For the leave-one-factor resolution with all crossing layers after the products, the d−1d-1 multiplication inputs are identical copies of (8). Every full binary tree with at least two leaves has an internal vertex whose two children are leaves: take a deepest internal vertex. Hence every such binary parenthesization contains exactly the two-leaf gate analyzed above. Reassociation within this specified resolution does not remove it. Redistributing crossings before products changes the prefix returns and is not covered by this assertion.

The example concerns an original positive edge star with a primitive central filling and prescribed noncomplete outside angles. It does not exhibit an actual knot exterior realizing those angles, or a globally filled knot. It rules out inferring the original factorial strip solely from positive primitive geometry, even in this simple boundary problem. The chamber formula retains the relevant Fourier factor; no obstruction to a complete corrected contour proof or to the volume identity is inferred.
