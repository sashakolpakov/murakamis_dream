Selected physical factors outside one exact matching-edge block

30 October 2026. This note assembles two separate inputs: the actual source’s full physical packet and the proved right-angled geometric localization. It does not identify a contributing global finite cycle.

1. Inputs and statement

Fix an actual augmented plat of arbitrary fixed width, with its retained half twists, literal Morse word, original exposed physical mark, and a positive convex ideal right-angled matching-polyhedron certificate as in the actual matching geometry. The actual source is the full marked matching-graph source; its folded labels, partner normals and generic lifts are not replaced by a root-only semisimple network. Choose any one crossing-circle matching edge e.

Use the following two established inputs, with their distinct scopes:

  • The prescribed-pair geometric theorem supplies a common regular frame with strip flags in the common shaded exterior K. Its only two strict physical-support failures are the two shaded facets at the ends of e. All other ordinary input/output factors have their actual canonical low or high physical coordinates.

  • The full physical complex packet concerns the ORIGINAL finite q-Hahn column. Its limiting domain is

    P={ (tau,nu) in C^2 : 1/2<Re tau<1, 0<Re nu<Re tau }.       (1)

    On each compact of P, for sufficiently large N and actual integer folded channels j with j/N near 1/2, it gives the selected ordinary packet with nonzero amplitude. Its selected characteristic extends from the previously proved central domain. No complex continuation of the finite index j is inferred from this statement.

Then the common frame can be chosen so that EVERY ordinary factor outside the two ends of e belongs to the source-selected branch on P. The two exceptions comprise ONE original matching-edge operator, whose four exterior objects all have highest weight d=N-1. That operator has the exact generic recoupling and retained first-normal formula of the marked four-port block. The original physical mark remains where it was.

The finite collection of good factors lies in a common compact subset of P. Consequently their ordinary finite-column packets can be multiplied there, with the product of their nonzero amplitudes and relative error 1+O(1/N). This is a local product statement at the framed geometric germ, not an estimate of the complementary tensor contraction, its summation, or the completed marked source.

2. The selected flag branches continue throughout P

Here is the additional proof needed beyond the old central-domain statement. Write

T=exp(2pi i tau), U=exp(2pi i nu), zeta=T^(1/2)/U,
W=1-(T+1)/U, V=W+zeta^2,                          (2)

where T^(1/2)=exp(pi i tau) retains its marked argument. The full packet proves W and V nonzero on P at the endpoint channel xi=1/2. Its square root of W is the analytic continuation from the positive real center. It need not be the pointwise principal square root outside the central domain. Since P is convex and W is nonzero, that continued square root is single valued.

Normalize the actual incoming puncture matrices as

A=-[[1,1],[0,1]], B=-[[1,0],[-4,1]], P_disk=B A.

The exact original head formulas in the selected local certificate give, for image flag z and kernel flag w,

U_phys=(z-w)/(z-w+1),
T_phys=(z-w)/(4wz+3w+z+1),
Y_in=-w/(z+1), Y_out=Y_in^(-1).                   (3)

First use the low adapter U_phys=U,T_phys=T. Directly solving (3), with delta=U/(1-U), gives the two pairs

Delta=U(T+1-U)/T=-U^2 W/T,
w=(-1 +/- sqrt(Delta))/(2(1-U)), z=w+delta.        (4)

The marked exponential arguments in (1) imply U!=1 and U!=T. Equation (2) gives Delta!=0. Thus (4) defines two distinct global analytic pairs of finite flags on P. Their actual normalized prefix triple is

(a_0,a_1,a_2)=(1,-1/U,1/T).                       (5)

The strict physical criterion applies to this triple throughout (1): the canonical physical wire coordinates are nu and tau-nu. The proved real determinant-pencil argument therefore puts z,w on the same OPEN side of the real disk circle. Neither flag can reach that circle along either analytic branch. In particular no unrecorded passage through infinity changes its side; infinity itself belongs to the forbidden circle.

For the actual high adapter one instead has

T_phys=1/T, U_phys=U/T.

Its physical wires are 1+nu-tau and 1-nu. Both are in the canonical strip, and the total has the required high degree. Crucially,

U_phys(T_phys+1-U_phys)/T_phys=Delta.              (6)

The same discriminant gives the two analytic flag pairs, now using 1-U/T in the denominator. This denominator is nonzero. Their physical triple (1,-T/U,T) is, up to a common nonzero factor and exchange of the outer terms, the triple (5). Hence the same open-side conclusion holds.

The full tube P is connected. At any central base point, the original source calibration already proves selected input means both flags below the incoming real circle, and selected output means both flags above its own real circle, in BOTH adapters. The source characteristic has no collision on P, and the flag branches have no collision or circle crossing. Continuing that calibration proves throughout P

selected input  iff Im z<0 and Im w<0,
selected output iff Im z>0 and Im w>0.             (7)

Each line uses that factor’s own actual disk normalization. This proof continues the selected root, not the old pointwise test Re sqrt(W)>0.

3. From actual strips to all good factors

At a complete coupon, the primitive collar gives C=I+b(P_disk+I), with Im b<0 and b-epsilon/2=1/tau_cusp. In eta(s)=1/(2s+1),

eta(C s)=eta(s)-2b.

Consequently (7) is equivalent, at any STRICTLY PHYSICAL input/output pair, to the same two actual strip tests as before:

0<Im eta(w)<-2 Im b,
0<Im eta(W_prefix v)<-2 Im b.                     (8)

The exact based-prefix dictionary of the right-angled localization identifies these with its common shaded-exterior flags. Choose its prescribed failed facets to be the two ends of e. Every other factor then has strict physical support AND the required oriented strip side. Equations (7),(8), proved on the larger tube, make each of those factors source-selected. This is the extra conclusion that the earlier central-domain theorem could not provide.

There are finitely many factors, so their strict margins have a common positive lower bound after fixing this frame. Small neighborhoods of their actual low/high coordinates have compact closure in P. Apply the full packet on those neighborhoods, uniformly for its allowed actual integer channels, and multiply a fixed finite number of estimates.

For an algebraic geometric certificate the frame may be chosen algebraic, as in the prescribed-pair theorem. This does not turn the selected logarithmic action into a root-only polynomial evaluation: its original logarithmic characters and finite-source normalization remain specified.

4. Exact exceptional block and limits of the conclusion

Both exceptional factors are kept together on e. The marked block theorem bends its actual operator K_a by the original physical cups, recouples through EVERY target g=0,…,d, and writes its whole-source first normal as Taylor coefficients of order at most two after clearing the possible simple poles. It retains the original circle weight and half-twist phase. The native row weight and physical cup scale introduce no exponential loss. Its explicit target and complement costs J_g,J_C are still part of that theorem’s bound; they are not removed by the present geometric localization.

The full packet also supplies the actual small-channel fixed generic jet bounds and the near-fold partner-normal upper bound on these SAME complex compacts. In its notation, for fixed r and s=l-j,

|D^r C_j/C_j| <= C_r N^(8r),
|w_(j,l)/C_j| <= C N^8(C N^16)^s, s<=eta N.       (9)

Here D=q d/dq, and the derivatives in the numerator are generic-q derivatives evaluated at the root; the denominator is the ordinary root column. Constants depend on the chosen compact, and every channel is still an actual integer. For a fixed number of good factors, the additional partner-normal loss is exp(O(sum s_i log N)). It is subexponential when each s_i log N=o(N). A fixed nonzero scaled separation is not covered by that inference. These are upper bounds, not a nonzero confluent amplitude. The separate all-physical lattice jet theorem is not by itself a theorem for complex geometric totals.

No global cycle, lower bound, nonzero completed saddle coefficient, or sharp estimate of the complementary contraction has been proved here. Nor have we made the entire factorization physical: its two necessary support defects remain, now in one exact block. The useful reduction is that all other ORDINARY factors have an actual selected physical packet, at arbitrary fixed plat width, while the exceptional block has an exact source-preserving expression with its boundary costs displayed.

Focused audit: exact new low/high flag identities. It checks both adapters and all four flag branches: eight original-head identities, twelve actual prefix traces, three discriminant identities and four sheet-involution identities. These are new exact algebraic checks only; the outside-central finite-source comparisons belong to the separate full-physical packet audit.

Independent analytical reviews: PASS. The root reader independently solved the low flag quadratic and checked the high-adapter discriminant and triple reversal. A second reader checked the actual prefix and open-circle-side continuation; a third checked the entire corollary against the full physical packet and its precise complex normal ranges. All checked the continued, rather than pointwise principal, square root, the prescribed matching-edge pair, and the distinction between an ordinary local product and the uncontrolled complementary contraction. The proof also uses the existing prescribed-pair audit and the separately reviewed full-physical packet; no numerical fixture is used to infer the global frame or selected branch.
