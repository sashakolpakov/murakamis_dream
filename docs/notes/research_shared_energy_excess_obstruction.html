The shared spectral energy can exceed the complete volume rate

31 October 2026. On one certified original seven-port source, the endpoint-conditioned energy exceeds the complete rate by more than 0.50, already at one fixed real spectral corner. This disproves the stronger proposed bound

sup𝝈∈[−2,2]RENC,N≤exp⁡{N(ΦD+o(1))}. \sup_{\boldsymbol\sigma\in[-2,2]^R}E_{\mathrm{NC},N} \le \exp\{N(\Phi_D+o(1))\}.

It does not refute the weaker endpoint-mass budget or the joint shared-flow criteria in the energy theorem. It is not a lower bound for the original oscillatory invariant.

1. An exact original shared flow

Take the width-three word

[1,3,0,2,4,1,3] [1,3,0,2,4,1,3]

with the first cap marked. The original compiler gives protected faces 0,60,6, ordinary faces 1,2,3,4,5,7,81,2,3,4,5,7,8, and the seven ordered quartets

(1,0,4,2),(2,0,5,3),(0,1,6,4),(4,2,7,5),(5,3,8,0),(6,4,0,7),(7,5,0,8). \begin{split} &(1,0,4,2),\ (2,0,5,3),\ (0,1,6,4),\ (4,2,7,5),\\ &(5,3,8,0),\ (6,4,0,7),\ (7,5,0,8). \end{split}\tag{1}

Select only port 22 as RIGHT; the other six ports are CROSS. At that port the outer x=0x=0 and opposite middle c=6c=6 are protected. Its normalized exponents have the form

qx=qc=0,qy=w,qb=1−w,0<w<12. q_x=q_c=0,\qquad q_y=w,\qquad q_b=1-w,\qquad 0<w<\tfrac12. \tag{2}

By the literal reflection of the protected-outer coefficient theorem, its actual coefficient and reference entropies satisfy

k*(q)=0,h0(q)=−2f(w),2k*(q)−h0(q)=2f(w)=C(0,12−w,w). k^*(q)=0,\qquad h_0(q)=-2f(w),\qquad 2k^*(q)-h_0(q)=2f(w)=C(0,\tfrac12-w,w). \tag{3}

Here C(α)=∑jf(αj)C(\alpha)=\sum_j f(\alpha_j), ∑jαj=1/2\sum_j\alpha_j=1/2, and

f(t)=Λ(πt)+Λ(π/2−πt)π. f(t)=\frac{\Lambda(\pi t)+\Lambda(\pi/2-\pi t)}\pi.

Equation (3) is an actual energy rate, not merely the general local upper bound. Its weighted energy potential is

ΨR=12log⁡L1+Q(0,L1,L4). \Psi_R=\tfrac12\log L_1+Q(0,L_1,L_4). \tag{4}

The flow therefore consists of the original input triangle (0,1,4)(0,1,4) of supply 1/21/2, and a fixed half-unit at face 11. This is entropy bookkeeping for (2); it introduces no new finite coupon or changed mark.

The registered certificate contains 30 exact rational corner values on the 13 original triangle rows other than port 22’s output row (0,6,4)(0,6,4). Every row sums to 1/21/2. With the fixed half-unit at face 11, every ordinary face receives exactly one and both protected faces receive zero. At all six CROSS ports, both middle degrees are strictly less than 1/21/2; at port 22, the bb-degree is strictly greater than 1/21/2. All these assertions are checked over the rationals against the original compiler.

Put

Ecert=∑13 registered rowsC(αrow). E_{\mathrm{cert}}=\sum_{\text{13 registered rows}}C(\alpha_{\mathrm{row}}). \tag{5}

At a CROSS port its two registered rows give one allowed split of the outer degrees, so their entropy is at most the actual coefficient entropy. At the selected port its one row gives exactly (3). No numerical optimizer is used to justify either assertion.

2. Actual integer arrays and a fixed spectral corner

Let qe,fq_{e,f} be the aggregated rational port-face flow above. For each even N=2mN=2m, set d=N−1d=N-1. On the original port-face transportation network give each incidence the integer bounds

⌊dqe,f⌋≤ve,f≤⌈dqe,f⌉. \lfloor d q_{e,f}\rfloor\le v_{e,f}\le\lceil d q_{e,f}\rceil. \tag{6}

The fractional flow dqdq satisfies these bounds and all integer port supplies dd and ordinary face demands dd. Integrality of the bipartite transportation network gives an integer flow vNv_N between the bounds. All zero incidences, including the protected ones, remain exactly zero, and vN/d→qv_N/d\to q.

The fixed strict middle slack gives vb,vc≤m−1v_b,v_c\le m-1 at each CROSS port for all sufficiently large even NN, and vb≥mv_b\ge m at the RIGHT port. These are the actual finite supports with the true target d=N−1d=N-1. The full positive endpoint kernel contains every degree-dd monomial, and its CROSS part has precisely these middle inequalities. Thus the original central factor is retained by classification of the actual coefficient. No separate rounding of formal half-triangle rows or removal of a forced outer factor is used.

At the six CROSS ports choose σe=−2\sigma_e=-2. At even levels the original reference is pN(v)=−aN(v;−2)>0p_N(v)=-a_N(v;-2)>0, and hence

|aN(v;−2)|2pN(v)=pN(v) \frac{|a_N(v;-2)|^2}{p_N(v)}=p_N(v) \tag{7}

exactly. At the selected port use the fixed value σ2=+2\sigma_2=+2. After literal row reflection its monomer starts at i=0i=0. Writing θ=π/N\theta=\pi/N, the exact unused-row formula and χN(2)=4\chi_N(2)=4 give

|aN(v;2)|=csc⁡2(θ/2)∏r=1j[sin⁡(rθ)sin⁡((r+1/2)θ)]2,j≤m−1. |a_N(v;2)|= \csc^2(\theta/2) \prod_{r=1}^{j} \left[\frac{\sin(r\theta)}{\sin((r+1/2)\theta)}\right]^2, \qquad j\le m-1. \tag{8}

4cos⁡2(θ/2)≤|aN(v;2)|≤csc⁡2(θ/2). 4\cos^2(\theta/2)\le |a_N(v;2)|\le\csc^2(\theta/2). \tag{9}

Its rate at this fixed spectral value is zero. The uniform endpoint entropy therefore yields the selected-port energy rate in (3), at the actual rounded exponents.

Let

𝝈*=(−2,−2,+2,−2,−2,−2,−2). \boldsymbol\sigma^*=(-2,-2,+2,-2,-2,-2,-2).

The positive shared energy contains the summand indexed by vNv_N. The uniform closed endpoint coefficient theorem, (3), and (7)–(9) imply

liminfN→∞Neven1Nlog⁡ENC,N(𝝈*)≥Ecert. \boxed{\displaystyle \liminf_{\substack{N\to\infty\\N\ \mathrm{even}}} \frac1N\log E_{\mathrm{NC},N}(\boldsymbol\sigma^*) \ge E_{\mathrm{cert}}.} \tag{10}

All spectral coordinates belong to the same original shared numerator. No spectral optimization, limit/supremum exchange, or independent local face contraction is used.

3. A certified excess over the complete rate

For an independent complete upper bound use the fixed ordinary radii

(L0,…,L8)=(0,16,9,25,1,4,0,1,9). (L_0,\ldots,L_8)=(0,16,9,25,1,4,0,1,9). \tag{11}

The actual complete capacity formula gives, simply by evaluating one positive ordinary vector,

ΦD≤U=∑14 original trianglesQ(Ltriangle)−∑fordinarylog⁡Lf. \Phi_D\le U= \sum_{\text{14 original triangles}}Q(L_{\mathrm{triangle}}) -\sum_{f\ \mathrm{ordinary}}\log L_f. \tag{12}

This vector is also the original complete packing, but its optimality is unnecessary for the upper bound (12).

The verify-only audit uses the fixed rational certificate and Arb arithmetic at 192 bits. Each Lobachevsky Fourier series has 8192 terms and the explicit absolute tail 1/(2M)1/(2M). The registered results prove

Ecert>7.51209890188,U<6.99858850313,Ecert−ΦD>0.50. E_{\mathrm{cert}}>7.51209890188,\qquad U<6.99858850313,\qquad E_{\mathrm{cert}}-\Phi_D>0.50. \tag{13}

The certified excess lower endpoint is greater than 0.51351039. Equations (10)–(13) refute the stronger energy-at-most-complete-rate proposal on an actual certified original source. The even subsequence already suffices to refute an all-level upper bound.

4. The weaker criteria are unaffected

For a sector with endpoint mass rate zsz_s and energy rate ese_s, the weaker Cauchy condition is zs+es<2ΦDz_s+e_s<2\Phi_D. This certificate does not compare the energy excess with the endpoint mass loss and does not disprove that condition.

The joint score must retain both terms on the same original flow. At the selected doubly protected port, (3) gives exactly

h0(q)+g(q)2=0=k*(q). \frac{h_0(q)+g(q)}2=0=k^*(q). \tag{14}

The positive energy rate compensates an exponentially small reference coefficient; it is not positive growth of the original spectral coefficient. The universal joint-score and combined-envelope inequalities remain open. No lower bound for the original oscillatory source, no failure of its volume limit, and no obstruction to the weaker energy budget follows from this counterexample.

5. verification scope

The portable verifier preserves the verify function’s complete syntax tree and the registered certificate bytes; only imports and output/default paths were changed. It has no optional numerical builder or optimizer dependency.

Its finite checks establish the original incidence, rational feasibility, support slack, and certified entropy inequalities. The asymptotic step uses the proved uniform coefficient theorems and integer transportation argument; no sampled large-level rate is presented as proof.
