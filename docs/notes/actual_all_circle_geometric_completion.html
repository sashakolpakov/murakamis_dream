Completing the crossing-circle fillings and calibrating their volume

1 November 2026.

The octahedral cells of an ordered tree of crossing circles stay positive while their angular longitude parameters vary in a fixed small disk. This note proves that the parameter path selected by the analytic filling equations completes those cells to the actual filled link exterior. It follows the full logarithmic period of each primitive filling curve, so the completed meridian makes exactly one turn at the endpoint. It then identifies the absolute volume with the continued critical action.

The ordered decomposition supplies the original topology and primitive curves. The angular charts supply the positive cells and their exact face maps. The analytic criterion supplies the path under the explicit bound in (4). Together these establish the geometric part of the quantitative volume theorem. Only the original crossing circles are filled; the main knot cusp remains complete and unfilled.

1. Exact topological and local inputs

Fix a finite circle-only Conway tree with at least two circle vertices. Every edge is a physical quarter-turn and every vertex is an original crossing circle. Keep its actual ribbon orders, projection-copy labels, half twists and primitive disk curves.

The ordered cut theorem supplies three marked facts.

  • The original projection and disk-page cuts give two octahedra per edge, with the six vertices and face maps of the literal two-leaf angular chart. Any transported corner permutations and projection-copy changes are retained.
  • At each original circle, outgoing and incoming pages are joined in their actual cyclic order. The retained half twist occurs at its distinguished original seam. The face maps keep their full three-point labels.
  • The disk longitude is the boundary loop with period Vv=2iτvV_v=2i\tau_v. The original meridian concatenates the local meridional page paths, with the single half-longitude shear −ϵvVv/2-\epsilon_vV_v/2. These are the original primitive curves.

Physical T−1T^{-1}, TT, and retained planar half-turns are not identified by source involutivity. Reversing a disk frame reverses both its paths and its peripheral coordinate. All formulas below use the fixed original disk basis.

The local positive eight-tetrahedron chart is valid on |τv|,|τw|≤10−2. |\tau_v|,|\tau_w|\le10^{-2}. \tag{1} Its incoming pants edge ratios are (−Q−1,−Q,−Q−1)(-Q^{-1},-Q,-Q^{-1}), and its outgoing ratios are their inverses, where Q=eiτvQ=e^{i\tau_v}. Thus the marked seam cancels the full complex shears, and its two interior-angle branches sum to 2π2\pi. This includes both projection copies. At zero these are the two regular octahedra in the actual two-leaf cut.

7. Scope of the completion

The metric argument permits large global meridian logs while every edge block remains uniformly close to its regular geometry. It does not require a globally small framed-representation norm.

The conclusion applies to the ordered all-circle trees of Section 1 under (4), or more generally whenever their actual analytic path satisfies (3). It does not assert that every bounded crossing power gives a hyperbolic filling. The degree-weighted bound permits some small powers because the resulting local angular changes remain small while the primitive meridian is assembled from many sectors.

The preferred-cusp continuation also follows the actual main longitude through this family. Its marking is established by the original face words and surgery homology. The complete geometric and analytic conclusions are collected in the quantitative volume theorem.
