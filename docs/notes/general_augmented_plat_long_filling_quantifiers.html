Fixed-skeleton long fillings: the proved geometric quantifiers

30 October 2026. The new multi-cusp rigidity result and the existing geometric completion theorem have a useful joint corollary: an actual positive marked augmented-link certificate supplies ALL independently sufficiently long signed integral fillings, without requiring a quantum source branch to construct those geometric fillings. This is an assembly of the repo’s established geometric arguments, not a claim of a new general hyperbolic Dehn filling theorem or of a volume-conjecture proof.

1. Fix the topology before either parameter limit

Fix k>=2 and one ordered 2k-strand plat skeleton D. Its r designated crossing-circle disks, outside wires, upper/lower pairings, marked cut, and retained half twists epsilon_i in {0,+1,-1} are part of D. The corresponding filled word has exponents epsilon_i-2p_i. Combine adjacent powers of the same generator before calling the disks independent. Let Y_D be the actual drilled augmented exterior, with its primitive crossing-disk pairs (mu_i,lambda_i).

Supply a positive complete ideal gluing certificate for this ACTUAL marked Y_D, as in the general positive certificate. A certified right-angled matching-graph realization is one existing way to supply it. The conclusion below applies at every fixed k and fixed r; neither k nor r is sent to infinity, and constants may depend on the entire fixed diagram D.

A parity test decides whether filling all auxiliary circles gives knots: the fixed plat pairings composed with the permutation of the retained half-twist word must have one component. Full twists have trivial strand permutation. Consequently the number of surviving main components is independent of every p_i. Long twisting cannot repair a failed knot connectivity test. A 2k-plat gives a k-bridge presentation; minimal bridge number is not being asserted.

2. Geometric corollary with independent signed slopes

There exists T_geom(D)<infinity with the following property. Choose ANY subset I of the r auxiliary cusps and ANY fixed signed integer slopes p_i, i in I, satisfying

|p_i|>T_geom(D) for every i in I.

Fill precisely those cusps along the original primitive curves mu_i+p_i lambda_i. Then the actual filled exterior has a positively oriented complete finite-volume hyperbolic metric. All main cusps and all auxiliary cusps outside I remain complete. There is no common-sign or slope-comparability condition. One threshold works for every subset. The empty subset recovers the supplied complete augmented geometry.

Here is the direct proof, including the existence step that the older completion theorem did not assert on its own. By multi-cusp marked rigidity, with main meridian logs fixed to zero, the auxiliary PROJECTIVE meridian logs U=(U_1,…,U_r) are holomorphic coordinates on the decorated geometric germ. The actual preferred longitude logs obey

V_i(U)=U_i t_i(U),
t_i(0)=tau_i, Im tau_i>0.                            (1)

This divisibility follows from the exact commuting-similarity identity, not a guessed source potential. Choose a sufficiently small geometric neighborhood where all t_i are nonzero and where the framed representations lie in the fixed neighborhood required by the existing geometric completion theorem. Normalize a developed triple to obtain a bounded holomorphic representative of the framed family there.

Introduce complex parameters e_i and solve

V_i(U)+e_i U_i=-2pi i e_i,  i=1,...,r.               (2)

At (U,e)=(0,0) the U-Jacobian is diag(tau_i), hence invertible. The holomorphic implicit-function theorem gives one solution U(e) in a fixed polydisk around zero. Shrink that polydisk so the framed family stays in the above completion neighborhood and

|t_i(U(e))+e_i|>=c_D>0.

Using (1), equation (2) becomes the exact coordinatewise identity

U_i(e)=-2pi i e_i/[t_i(U(e))+e_i].                   (3)

In particular |U_i(e)|<=2pi|e_i|/c_D, even when the e_i have arbitrarily unequal magnitudes. If e_i=0, then U_i=V_i=0 EXACTLY. That cusp is unfilled and parabolic; it has not been assigned a filling coefficient p_i=0.

Now put e_i=1/p_i for i in I and e_i=0 otherwise. For sufficiently large independently signed |p_i| this is in the same polydisk and (2) gives the ACTUAL small-log equations

U_i+p_i V_i=-2pi i, i in I.                         (4)

The curve mu_i+p_i lambda_i is primitive because its meridian coefficient is one. Equation (4) has unit charge, with its original orientation. All unfilled groups are parabolic and nontrivial nearby. These are precisely the inherited hypotheses of the geometric completion theorem: actual nearby framed representation, correct primitive marking, parabolic unfilled ends, and unit-charge filling logarithms. That theorem constructs the complete smooth metric on the correctly marked filled topology. This proves the corollary.

The argument makes no rational-surgery quantum claim. Retained half twists belong to Y_D and its own primitive collar. A hidden half-integer slope on a different pure link is not substituted for (4).

3. The strongest honest general volume statement is still conditional

For this SAME D, suppose the original mixed-color marked source satisfies all hypotheses of the general original-source long-filling transfer: an exact analytic color packet, uniform complex asymptotic with nonzero leading density, the full complementary-color/alias/normal-current gap, and selection of the actual marked geometric action and its complete regulator anchor. Then that transfer theorem gives a threshold T_vol(D)>=T_geom(D) such that, for every FIXED signed vector p with all |p_i|>T_vol(D),

lim_(N -> infinity) (2pi/N) log |K_N(K_D(p))|
                  =Vol(S^3 minus K_D(p)).           (5)

Here the parity test in section 1 is assumed, and all auxiliary circles are filled. Every quantum level is included, both parities and composite levels. The order of quantifiers is

for each fixed k and admissible fixed D,
  there exists T_vol(D),
    for every fixed signed long vector p,
      take the full sequence N -> infinity.

The general quantum hypotheses have NOT yet been proved for every D. Thus T_geom(D) is established under the positive marked certificate, whereas existence of T_vol(D) for arbitrary certified D remains the active volume-conjecture target. The exact compiler, classical geometric critical scheme and local column amplitudes do not by themselves supply that missing full-source estimate or period coefficient.

If only a nonzero analytic density germ is proved, rather than a unit or a controlled peripheral monomial times a unit, the source-divisor extension allows an exceptional analytic divisor of slopes. Its density-one conclusion must not be restated as the all-long-slopes assertion (5).

There is no assertion of uniformity when p depends on N. The transfer errors and their constants are proved with p fixed. Likewise no threshold depending ONLY on k follows from these arguments: bridge width bounds the number of simultaneous heads, while the number/order of boxes and all geometric and source constants remain part of D. This is a limitation of the proved quantifiers, not a proof that a universal k-only threshold cannot exist within a more restrictive certified class.

4. What long twisting does and does not provide

Given the positive marked base certificate, long integral twists provide the hyperbolic filled geometry by section 2. They preserve the already fixed knot/link connectivity. They do not imply the base certificate: Y_D is independent of p, as are its cusp labels, essential crossing disks and actual cut topology. Nor does hyperbolicity of a filled knot imply that every chosen augmented presentation has a hyperbolic base.

There is an elementary obstruction to using raw crossing counts in place of essential marked disks. Two adjacent same-pair blocks can satisfy

R^(2p) R^(-2p+2c)=R^(2c)

with fixed c while both apparent twist counts grow. This identity holds in the original source, with its Cartan factor and marked closure. It motivates merging redundant regions and certifying the drilled topology; it does not disprove a uniform threshold inside the certified essential class.

The present target is therefore broad but precise: every fixed certified essential augmented 2k-plat skeleton, for every k>=2, with the required parity and independently long signed full twists. It is not a claim that every k-bridge knot already lies in that long-twist class, nor that the full volume conjecture for that class has now been proved.

The geometric threshold assumes the marked positive certificate. The quantum threshold also requires the stated source estimates.
