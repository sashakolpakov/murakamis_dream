Historical research lead: generic four-port AW parameters (now completed)

29 October 2026. Completion update: the general actual source kernel and its exactly normalized seven-factorial conversion are now PROVED in the generic all-port theorem. This historical research lead records the original parameter proposal; it is not an independent proof and does not add a positivity claim.

The original proposal preserved a possible extension of the completed physical tetrahedral source identity. At that stage it was not a source formula or an asymptotic input; the generic gauge and normalization were still missing. They are now established in the linked theorem, with root positivity and energy estimates still explicitly separate.

Use generic q, Q=q^2, and ordinary four-port spin labels a,b,c,d. The actual left and right intermediate ranges are

e in [|a-b|,a+b] intersect [|c-d|,c+d],
f in [|b-c|,b+c] intersect [|d-a|,d+a].

In the chamber c-d>=|a-b| put e_min=c-d, e_max=min(a+b,c+d), f_max=a+d, and n=f_max-f. A candidate for the finite operator parameters is

A=q Q^(c-d), B=q Q^(b-a),
C=q^-1 Q^(-c-d), D=q^-1 Q^(-a-b),
z_e=q Q^e, kappa=q Q^(a+d), P=ABCD=Q^(-2(a+d)). (1)

For the finite symmetric polynomial operator

Lg(z)=u(z)[g(Qz)-g(z)]+u(z^-1)[g(Q^-1z)-g(z)],
u(z)=product_(T=A,B,C,D)(1-Tz)/[(1-z^2)(1-Qz^2)],

the eigenvalue polynomial is Lambda_n=(Q-n-1)(1-PQ(n-1)). There is an elementary EXACT arithmetic identity

kappa[Lambda_n+1+P/Q]
             =q Q^f+q^-1 Q^-f, n=a+d-f.       (2)

The candidate forward coefficient vanishes at e_max, and the backward coefficient vanishes at e_min. This explains the expected support but does not identify the actual source recurrence.

The specialization a=b=d_phys/2, c=a_source, d=b_source, f=d_phys/2 gives precisely the parameters and physical Casimir eigenvalue in the already PROVED generic six-head theorem. In that specialization the polynomial degree is b_source, and the affine operator scale is q(d_phys+1)Qb_source. This consistency check is not a general proof.

At the proposal stage the remaining concrete steps were: prove the actual general Jacobi diagonal and off-diagonal product certificate from the typed Fricke relations; derive its diagonal source gauge from the true first-component-one Clebsch columns; and evaluate the actual endpoint coefficient to fix normalization. The existing four-port kernel already provides a finite two-sum formula with the correct cups and cyclic clock, which would be the independent source to compare. All generic high channels and pole-canceling root normals must be retained before any specialization. Distinct spectra, formal matching of (2), or named polynomial symmetry alone do not prove this bridge.

Those generic source/gauge/normalization steps, including the seven-factorial comparison and finite normal budget, are now supplied by the linked completion. The next open tasks concern coherent high-partner metric/jet transport and actual global source incidence, not this finite identity. This historical proposal remains available to distinguish the initial heuristic from its later proof.
