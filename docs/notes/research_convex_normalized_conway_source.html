A strongly convex minimum for the complete Conway source

21 November 2026. This note concerns the original reduced Kashaev source of a fixed literal Conway construction. Previous final notes and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

After an explicit normalization of its local tensors, the source admits an unweighted quartic minimum on a product of unit balls. Its real Hessian lies between II and 3I3I, uniformly in the quantum level and the number of vertices. The minimum has a unique interior point, and the corresponding value satisfies |KN|2≤QN≤γ|KN|2,γ=197196.(1) |K_N|^2\le Q_N\le\gamma|K_N|^2,\qquad \gamma=\frac{197}{196}. \qquad\text{(1)} This includes zeros. An explicit degree-six polynomial gives an exact lower-certificate maximum for the minimum. These statements concern the original marked source; no reference coordinate or ordinary-color sum is introduced. Their geometric volume rate remains to be proved.

For each local physical tensor TvT_v, including leaves and the root, choose the known budget bv=16max⁡(1,∥Tv∥HS),σ=116. b_v=16\max(1,\|T_v\|_{\mathrm{ HS}}),\qquad \sigma=\frac1{16}. Use one trial packet xv∈HNx_v\in H_N at each proper vertex and put 𝒟N=∏v≠root{xv:∥xv∥≤1},ℛv(x)=Φv((xc)c)−xv. \mathcal D_N=\prod_{v\ne\mathrm{root}}\{x_v:\|x_v\|\le1\}, \qquad \mathcal R_v(x)=\Phi_v((x_c)_c)-x_v. 6. An exact polynomial lower certificate

For any feasible field, define DN(x)=EN(x)−12∥∇EN(x)∥2.(16) D_N(x)=E_N(x)-\frac12\|\nabla E_N(x)\|^2. \qquad\text{(16)} This is a real polynomial of degree at most six. The lower Hessian bound gives DN(x)≤min⁡EN≤EN(x).(17) D_N(x)\le\min E_N\le E_N(x). \qquad\text{(17)} Indeed, for feasible yy, EN(y)≥EN(x)+⟨∇EN(x),y−x⟩ℝ+12∥y−x∥2. This argument uses convexity only on the feasible balls. At x*x_* the gradient is zero, so min𝒟NEN=max𝒟NDN.(18) \min_{\mathcal D_N}E_N =\max_{\mathcal D_N}D_N. \qquad\text{(18)} The polynomial DND_N need not be concave.

Combining (11) and (17) gives, at every feasible trial field, Btot2max⁡(0,DN(x))≤|KN|2≤γBtot2EN(x).(19) \boxed{\quad B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2\max(0,D_N(x))\le|K_N|^2 \le\gamma B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2E_N(x). \quad} \qquad\text{(19)} This lower certificate includes every real and imaginary boundary coordinate, including the physical normal directions.

For example, suppose feasible fields xNx_N satisfy ∥∇EN(xN)∥2≤2(1−ηN)EN(xN),0<ηN≤1,log⁡ηN=o(N).(20) \|\nabla E_N(x_N)\|^2\le2(1-\eta_N)E_N(x_N),\qquad 0<\eta_N\le1,\qquad \log\eta_N=o(N). \qquad\text{(20)} Then DN(xN)≥ηNEN(xN)D_N(x_N)\ge\eta_NE_N(x_N). Thus a single controlled geometric trial field would suffice for a volume lower bound. Neither moment stationarity nor angle stationarity alone establishes (20), which uses the full real gradient. 7. Scope and an optional exact reduction

All coefficients come from the complete original source. For a fixed literal construction, log⁡Btot=O(N)\log B_{\mathrm{ tot}}=O(N); the sine-factor bounds, crossing norms and closing normalization are recorded in Section 7 of the earlier quartic note. Two exact presentations have logarithmic QNQ_N values differing by at most log⁡γ\log\gamma whenever KN≠0K_N\ne0, and both values vanish at every zero. Fixed short proper subtrees may be contracted with their full open boundary packets before applying this construction.

There is an optional variable-projection reduction. Retain one child variable for each binary product and eliminate every other variable. Sibling pairs are disjoint, so if n×n_\times is the number of product vertices this retains exactly n×n_\times packets. Every residual is jointly affine in the eliminated variables when the retained ones are fixed. Their inner minimization is therefore a strictly convex quadratic problem on a product of balls. Its interaction graph consists of linear corridors: every eliminated vertex has at most one eliminated child and one parent. Its minimizer exists uniquely. Partial minimization preserves strong convexity in the retained variables: interpolate the two inner minimizers, then apply the original strong-convexity inequality and drop the nonnegative squared displacement of the inner variables. This gives an exact reduction to 2Nn×2Nn_\times outer real variables. The reduced objective need not be polynomial.

Finally, the fixed comparison factor in (1) makes the exponential rate of QNQ_N equivalent to that of the original source. Convexity has provided a unique finite minimum, a uniformly contracting iteration, and a lower witness at one trial field. Its asymptotic comparison with hyperbolic volume remains necessary, including after the fixed short partial assignments in the finite-exception program.
