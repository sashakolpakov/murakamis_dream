The actual cyclic head has a rank-one moment relation and an orbit co-source

Research proof, 27 October 2026. The proposed multiplicative relation is exact for the actual repaired head. Its natural moment covector differs from the original Whittaker row. Retaining their finite orbit path gives the original source and an explicit whole Ward homotopy. Actual R coefficients transport the moment framing, but do not make the orbit row an eigenrow. Thus the relation yields an exact source sum rule rather than survival of the geometric line in the last sum.

This is a direct finite-matrix calculation. No classification or published multiplicative-moment proof is used.

1. Exact framed multiplicative relation before root specialization

Use the actual matrices from the highest/Whittaker boundary construction:

B=diag⁡(1,Q,…,QN−1),P=∑j=0N−2Ej+1,j+E0,N−1,PN=I. B=\operatorname{diag}(1,Q,\ldots,Q^{N-1}),\quad P=\sum_{j=0}^{N-2}E_{j+1,j}+E_{0,N-1},\quad P^N=I.

Write Z=Q^N, delta=1-Z, v=e_0, w=e_0^t, e=vw=E_00, so wv=1. Since W=eP=E_(0,N-1), the exact relation BP-QPB=delta W gives

BPB−1P−1=Q[I+(Z−1−1)vw]. \boxed{\quad BPB^{-1}P^{-1}=Q[I+(Z^{-1}-1)vw].\quad} \tag{1}

For example W B inverse P inverse=Q^(-(N-1))e, proving every coefficient and the sign in (1). With the actual framing vector v and scaled covector what=(Z inverse minus one)w, it is the framed equation Q inverse BPB inverse P inverse=I+v what. Its determinant forces and satisfies 1+what v=Z inverse. The inverse equation is equally exact:

PBP−1B−1=Q−1[I+(Z−1)e]. PB P^{-1}B^{-1}=Q^{-1}[I+(Z-1)e]. \tag{2}

It is the rank of the Q-NORMALIZED commutator minus I that is at most one. The factor Q cannot be dropped: at a primitive N-th root Q is not one. Generically Z not one makes that rank exactly one. At Z=1 it drops to zero, but the divided normal retains

[Q−1BPB−1P−1−I1−QN]QN=e. \left[\frac{Q^{-1}BPB^{-1}P^{-1}-I}{1-Q^N}\right]_{Q_N}=e. \tag{3}

The actual whole lift specifies w through what=(Z inverse minus one)w. A bare root moment relation with what=0 does not reconstruct that divided covector or the selected source.

2. Both actual endpoint walls use this same marked projector

Let kappa=M-M inverse, e_top=P inverse eP. The cyclic expressions and actual finite generators factor WHOLE as

Fcyc=P(I−QB),ℰcyc=P−1(MB−1−M−1I), F_{cyc}=P(I-QB),\quad \mathcal E_{cyc}=P^{-1}(MB^{-1}-M^{-1}I), F=Fcyc(I−etop)=Fcyc−δeP,ℰ=ℰcyc(I−e)=ℰcyc−κP−1e. \boxed{\quad F=F_{cyc}(I-e_{top})=F_{cyc}-\delta eP,\qquad \mathcal E=\mathcal E_{cyc}(I-e) =\mathcal E_{cyc}-\kappa P^{-1}e. \quad} \tag{4}

The first equation deletes the last raising arrow and the second deletes the first lowering arrow. Thus both actual walls have the same rank-one framing transported to the appropriate end. The multiplicative commutator itself only sees the Q-wall delta; the independent meridian character kappa must also be retained.

In particular F^N=0 while F_cyc^N=delta(Q;Q)_(N-1)I, and the original pair detects the divided wrap value N. At a primitive root E_cycN=(-1)(N-1)(M^N-M inverse N)I. These are the actual central norms and endpoint factors, including the ones entering the full modified-dimension ratio. Neither (1) nor its root rank drop replaces these norms by numerical units or equates their different normal directions.

3. The actual co-source is a required finite orbit path

The natural moment covector is w. The original output row is ell=ones transpose. It is recovered exactly by the finite path

SP=∑r=0N−1Pr,ℓ=wSP,Q0=vℓ=eSP. S_P=\sum_{r=0}^{N-1}P^r,\qquad \ell=w S_P,\qquad Q_0=v\ell=e S_P. \tag{5}

Thus the exact highest-input period is ell C v=w S_P C v, not just w C v on arbitrary current matrices. Both anchors equal one on I, but their rows are not proportional for N greater than one. Any simultaneous change of basis carries w,ell and S_P together; it cannot erase their relative orbit path.

There is a source-preserving framed period identity, not just the unpointed matrix relation. Write Delta_mom=BPB inverse P inverse minus QI and lambda(C)=ell C v. Combining (1) and (5) gives

ΔmomSP=Q(Z−1−1)Q0, \Delta_{mom}S_P=Q(Z^{-1}-1)Q_0, λ(C1ΔmomSPC2)=Q(Z−1−1)λ(C1)λ(C2). \boxed{\quad \lambda(C_1\Delta_{mom}S_P C_2) =Q(Z^{-1}-1)\lambda(C_1)\lambda(C_2). \quad} \tag{5a}

This holds for arbitrary actual whole matrices C_1,C_2 with their coefficient lines consistently retained. It is the literal rank-one cut/source factorization: Q_0=v ell. Dividing the already specified whole equation by delta and evaluating at the root gives coefficient Q_N on the right. Equivalently the selected divided moment insertion is Q_N Q_0. Without S_P the right factor would have the different moment row w. Thus (5a) is an actual whole loop/source identity with its required orbit path, not permission to discard that path or to assign a new value to the uninserted period.

The norm of S_P in the original flat unitary head metric is N, and the norm of ell is sqrt N. No exponential source scale is introduced by (5). Conversely these elementary source bounds are not a bound on the actual R word or its period.

There is an exact WHOLE source-current comparison. Put D_K C=K C K inverse minus C with K=xB inverse, and define

βK(X)=∑c=1N−1Xc,0Q−c−1. \beta_K(X)=\sum_{c=1}^{N-1}\frac{X_{c,0}}{Q^{-c}-1}.

Every displayed denominator is a unit at a primitive N-th root. Entrywise multiplication gives

ℓCv−wCv=βK(DKC). \boxed{\quad \ell C v-wCv=\beta_K(D_K C).\quad} \tag{6}

This is the K-only specialization of the established actual Ward source homotopies. It holds before jets on arbitrary C, so all derivatives of beta_K, K, C and the original peripheral line are retained. At the physical root its exact flat-metric row norm is

∥βK∥2=∑c=1N−114sin⁡2(πc/N)=N2−112. \|\beta_K\|^2 =\sum_{c=1}^{N-1}\frac1{4\sin^2(\pi c/N)} =\frac{N^2-1}{12}. \tag{7}

For a direct proof of the last identity, apply two logarithmic derivatives at one to (t^N-1)/(t-1), whose roots are the nontrivial N-th roots. This gives sum Qc/(1-Qc)2=-(N2-1)/12; each negative summand is the corresponding reciprocal squared sine.

4. Exact consequence for the actual one-index Fourier source

Use the whole Vandermonde change on the SECOND wire and put A_j[c,a]=T^V_(c,j;a,j). Define

JjWhit=ℓAjv,Jjmom=wAjv. J^{Whit}_j=\ell A_j v,\qquad J^{mom}_j=w A_j v.

Equation (6) gives the WHOLE coefficientwise relation

JjWhit−Jjmom=βK(DKAj). J^{Whit}_j-J^{mom}_j=\beta_K(D_K A_j). \tag{8}

At the original root, charge conservation and the ordinary scalar closure prove

Jjmom=PN/N,JjWhit=PN/N+βK(DKAj),∑jβK(DKAj)=0. J^{mom}_j=P_N/N,\qquad J^{Whit}_j=P_N/N+\beta_K(D_K A_j),\qquad \sum_j\beta_K(D_K A_j)=0. \tag{9}

Indeed setting c=a=0 forces the old second offset to zero; Fourier transformation then contributes precisely 1/N and no j dependence. The first formula is not an off-root scalarity claim. The whole sum of (8) is beta_K(D_K C); it vanishes at the root for the uninserted fully reclosed scalar C, while inserted currents and normal derivatives retain their actual defects.

Equation (9) is a genuine constraint on the final finite j source. It also explains a limitation: replacing the Whittaker orbit by the natural moment row loses ALL nonconstant Fourier geometry. The geometric formal block may occur in this Ward sector, whose sum is controlled only after closure. A nonzero local block or a rank-one moment relation does not prove its selected survival.

5. Actual R transports the framing but not the orbit row trivially

For any actual invertible crossing R, conjugating ALL B_i,P_i,e_i and their vector/covector maps preserves (1)-(4) algebraically. This is a strict identity of the actual finite matrix algebra; it is not a claim that individual generators remain fixed. For two highest inputs the literal actual coefficients give

R(v⊗v)=r00(v⊗v),(w⊗w)R=r00(w⊗w). R(v\otimes v)=r_{00}(v\otimes v),\qquad (w\otimes w)R=r_{00}(w\otimes w).

The full scalar r_00, including its half-Cartan mark, is retained. Thus the highest moment framing is compatible up to this actual scalar. For two copies of the original boundary object, its tensor orbit row has the exact residual

(ℓ⊗ℓ)R−r00(ℓ⊗ℓ)=(w⊗w)[SP⊗SP,R]. (\ell\otimes\ell)R-r_{00}(\ell\otimes\ell) =(w\otimes w)[S_P\otimes S_P,R]. \tag{10}

It is generally nonzero. At N=2, q=i, eta_half=exp(pi i/4), the literal positive crossing matrix displayed in the actual boundary-moment audit has r_00=eta_half. The input (0,1) component of (10) is eta_half inverse minus eta_half=-sqrt(2)i, not zero. Multiplying the crossing by its retained scalar normalization multiplies both sides; it does not remove this defect. Actual word, cap and prefix transport must carry the orbit path and this residual. This is a local boundary-object covariance test. A full original braid still has its pivotal caps on the other strands; their source is not replaced by ell tensor ell. The actual fully sourced one-index identity is (8)-(9), with those original caps retained.

Even the naive tensor product of the moment pairs is not another rank-one pair: for B_tot=B_1B_2,P_tot=P_1P_2 its normalized commutator is (I+a e_1)(I+a e_2), a=Z inverse minus one. For generic Z not plus or minus one its defect has rank 2N-1, preserved by every simultaneous crossing conjugation. The true Hopf/coprod and source fusion therefore cannot be replaced by that naive product if a rank-one target is desired.

6. Exact audit and scope

The independent script and its output check the whole framed relation, endpoint projectors, root divided class, orbit path, current homotopy, actual Fourier sum rule and literal R residual, including a characteristic-zero example.

The usable whole object is the framed pair B,P,v,w WITH its original orbit co-source S_P, both actual endpoint deletions and the meridian characters. This is more precise than a bare root commutator relation. It supplies exact source-current identities; it does not identify a global geometric cycle, make the final source horizontal by itself, or prove a volume limit.

7. Existing terminology and the root specialization

The defining rank-one multiplicative-commutator relation occurs in Chalykh–Fairon, Section 3, page 10. Their invertible-coordinate presentation has rank(XZX inverse Z inverse minus q_0 I)=1. Our actual generic pair has X=B, Z=P and q_0=Q. At Q^N=1 its rank drops to zero, so the root belongs to the rank-at-most-one closure, with the divided marking (3) retained. Their Proposition 3.3 assumes q_0 is not a root of unity. Their subsequent regular coordinate chart also excludes x_i=q_0 x_j, which the actual clock spectrum violates. No smoothness or coordinate theorem there is applied to this resonant root/source section; only the defining coverage is used.

The additional aligned source-frame theorem proves the whole matrix-algebra classification directly, including its necessary alignment and flat-saturation hypotheses. The actual Fourier cancellation theorem then constructs a nonzero geometric Ward block whose selected cyclic sum vanishes. These distinguish frame rigidity from the remaining selected-period problem.
