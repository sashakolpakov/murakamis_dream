The actual pivotal cap is a two-sided Euler coefficient functional

Research proof, 27 October 2026. This is an exact root-level statement about the original two-leg source after the actual Borel fusion, valid at every level. Its coefficient functional has a rational kernel with two adjacent quantum poles. The full central-F extension has explicit affine and wrapping corrections. None of these variables is identified with the physical Fourier momentum or the geometric A-curve.

The starting fusion, actual cap and all external normalization lines are those in the actual Borel-relative period. In particular, no new invariant, cap normalization or preferred solution is introduced below. A scalar normalization multiplying the original word multiplies both sides of every period identity below.

1. Algebra and the original cap

Let Q have exact order N, with N at least two, over a characteristic-zero coefficient field. Put F_j=(Q;Q)j and F_0=1; F(N-1)=N. Retain the actual common highest K-eigenvalue x, assumed invertible. The actual coproduct algebra is

AΦ=⟨H,f∣HN=1,Hf=Q−1fH,fN=Φ⟩. A_\Phi=\langle H,f\mid H^N=1,\quad Hf=Q^{-1}fH, \quad f^N=\Phi\rangle.

Here f is the actual TOTAL lowering operator, not an individual meridian clock. For two legs with actual F_i^N=phi_i,

Φ=ϕ1+x−Nϕ2. \Phi=\phi_1+x^{-N}\phi_2. \tag{1}

The idempotents e_j have H-eigenvalue Q^(-j). The free basis is f^a e_j, with 0<=a,j<N, and e_j f=f e_(j-1), cyclically. Let

τ(faej)=δa,N−1,ρ0(b)=[fN−1e0]b. \tau(f^a e_j)=\delta_{a,N-1},\qquad \rho_0(b)=[f^{N-1}e_0]b. \tag{2}

The functional tau is Frobenius, but is not an ordinary cyclic trace. It does satisfy tau(h(f)b)=tau(b h(f)) for every polynomial h(f), because its Nakayama map fixes f. Also

τ(eN−1b)=ρ0(b). \tau(e_{N-1}b)=\rho_0(b). \tag{3}

To verify (3), left multiplication by e_(N-1) selects f^a e_j only when j+a=N-1 modulo N; tau then selects a=N-1 and hence j=0.

Under the actual triangular fusion, the original highest-input, all-ones-output pivotal cap has the following exact values:

$$ \Lambda_{\rm act}(f^a e_k)= x^{1-N-a}Q^{-b}\frac{F_b}{F_k} \begin{cases} 1,&k+a<N,\\ \phi_2,&k+a\geq N, \end{cases} \qquad b=k+a\pmod N. \tag{4} $$

Indeed the source projector requires output weight b. Returning the second output to its original pivotal closure forces all a lowering steps onto that leg. Its nonwrapping coefficient is F_b/F_k; a second-leg wrap supplies phi_2. The factors x(1-N-a)Q(-b) are the original pivotal weight and K_1 inverse factors. This is the same literal cap as before fusion. The first-leg central parameter enters the relative algebra through (1), not through a replacement for this second-leg wrap.

2. Exact two-sided Euler form on the nilpotent fiber

Write z=xf and define the finite polynomials

u(z)=∑j=0N−1zjFj,v(z)=∑j=0N−1(−1)jQj(j−1)/2zjFj,v2(z)=v(Q2z),γ=Nx2−2NQ. u(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\frac{z^j}{F_j},\qquad v(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1} \frac{(-1)^jQ^{j(j-1)/2}z^j}{F_j},\qquad v_2(z)=v(Q^2z), \quad \gamma=Nx^{2-2N}Q. \tag{5}

On phi_1=phi_2=0, the ORIGINAL cap is exactly

$$ \boxed{\quad \Lambda_{\rm act}(a)=\gamma\rho_0\big(v_2(z)\,a\,u(z)\big). \quad} \tag{6} $$

The shift Q^2 is necessary. Replacing v_2 by v is false in general; level two conceals the error since Q^2=1 there.

Here is a direct all-level proof. The cap density from the actual fusion factors as L R, where

L=u(z)eN−1,R=∑k=0N−1x1−N−kQ−kFkfN−1−kek. L=u(z)e_{N-1},\qquad R=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}x^{1-N-k}Q^{-k}F_k f^{N-1-k}e_k.

The elementary product identity

FN−1−j=N(−1)jQj(j+1)/2Fj F_{N-1-j}=N\frac{(-1)^jQ^{j(j+1)/2}}{F_j}

gives R=gamma e_(N-1)v_2(z), coefficient by coefficient. Thus the exact density is C=gamma u(z)e_(N-1)v_2(z). Moving u through tau, which is permitted for polynomials in f, and then using (3) proves (6). This derivation uses the actual cap density, rather than prescribing a desirable co-source afterward.

The finite Euler equations on this nilpotent fiber are

u(Qz)=(1−z)u(z),v(z)u(z)=1. u(Qz)=(1-z)u(z),\qquad v(z)u(z)=1.

For degrees below N the first follows by subtracting consecutive coefficients; its remaining degree-N term vanishes because z^N=0. The second follows either by the finite q-binomial formula or by the same recurrence and constant term one. Consequently

v2(z)u(z)=1(1−z)(1−Qz)=:JQ(z), v_2(z)u(z)=\frac1{(1-z)(1-Qz)}=:J_Q(z), \tag{7}

and (6) has the equivalent exact form

$$ \boxed{\quad \Lambda_{\rm act}(a)=\gamma\rho_0 \left[J_Q(z)\,u(z)^{-1}a\,u(z)\right].\quad} \tag{8} $$

All inverses in (7)-(8) exist in the truncated algebra: their arguments have constant term one and z is nilpotent. Formula (8) is a coefficient identity with a rational kernel; it does not introduce a convergent contour integral or an asymptotic cycle. In particular J_Q must be applied AFTER the indicated actual-word Euler conjugation. Inserting it as an extra weight in the old unconjugated word would change the period.

For any actual two-leg word W that commutes with the diagonal Borel action, its fused action is right multiplication by the unique element a_W. The original period, with its original external line gamma_W, is therefore exactly

PW=γWγρ0[JQ(z)u(z)−1aWu(z)]. P_W=\gamma_W\gamma\rho_0 \left[J_Q(z)u(z)^{-1}a_Wu(z)\right]. \tag{9}

This applies to words, their signed inverses and their exact compositions. It is not limited to a chosen critical branch. There is a relevant left/right distinction. Physical total F acts by LEFT multiplication on this free fused module, whereas a_W is its RIGHT-multiplication coefficient. Thus the conjugation a_W -> u^(-1)a_W u in (8) is in the right coefficient algebra. It is not conjugation of the physical word by u(physical total F): the latter commutes with a Borel-linear word and would do nothing. For more than two legs the actual free relative module has matrix-valued word coefficients and its original entry-dependent cap rows; those rows must be retained. No assertion that all of them equal this single two-leg cap is being made.

3. The whole central-F extension retains its wrap correction

Equations (5) still define polynomials on the full flat A_Phi family. Define

CE=γu(z)eN−1v2(z),ΛE(a)=τ(CEa)=γρ0(v2(z)au(z)), C_E=\gamma u(z)e_{N-1}v_2(z),\qquad \Lambda_E(a)=\tau(C_Ea)=\gamma\rho_0(v_2(z)a u(z)),

and the explicit element

W=∑a=1N−1∑b=0a−1x1−N−aQ−bFbFN+b−afN−1−aeb. W=\sum_{a=1}^{N-1}\sum_{b=0}^{a-1} x^{1-N-a}Q^{-b}\frac{F_b}{F_{N+b-a}} f^{N-1-a}e_b. \tag{10}

Then the exact ORIGINAL whole cap is

$$ \boxed{\quad C_{\rm act}=C_E-x^N\phi_1 W,\qquad \Lambda_{\rm act}(a)=\Lambda_E(a)-x^N\phi_1\tau(Wa). \quad} \tag{11} $$

This correction is essential even when Phi=0: opposite nonzero leg centers can have zero total center.

To prove (11), evaluate the Euler density on f^a e_k. The idempotent in u e_(N-1)v_2 forces the coefficient of u to have index k and that of v_2 to have index N-1-b, where b=k+a mod N. If k+a<N, their total f degree, including a, is N-1. The value is the first case of (4). If k+a>=N, that degree is 2N-1 and one factor Phi occurs. The resulting value is

ΛE(faek)=x1−N−aQ−bFbFk{1,k+a<N,xNΦ,k+a≥N. \Lambda_E(f^a e_k)=x^{1-N-a}Q^{-b}\frac{F_b}{F_k} \begin{cases}1,&k+a<N,\\ x^N\Phi,&k+a\geq N.\end{cases} \tag{12}

The Frobenius dual-basis formula makes tau(W f^a e_k) zero in the first case and x(1-N-a)Q(-b)F_b/F_k in the second. Now use x^N Phi=x^N phi_1+phi_2. Equations (4), (11) follow on every basis element, hence on the whole flat family.

The Euler density itself has a useful exact central scalar:

eN−1v2(z)u(z)eN−1=deN−1,d=1+N−32NxNΦ,CE2=γdCE. e_{N-1}v_2(z)u(z)e_{N-1}=d\,e_{N-1},\qquad d=1+\frac{N-3}{2N}x^N\Phi,\qquad C_E^2=\gamma d C_E. \tag{13}

Only total degrees zero and N contribute in this corner. The degree-N coefficient of v_2(z)u(z) is

−1N∑j=1N−1Q2j1−Qj=N−32N. -\frac1N\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}\frac{Q^{2j}}{1-Q^j} =\frac{N-3}{2N}.

For the last equality use sum 1/(1-Q^j)=(N-1)/2, sum Q^j=-1 and t^2/(1-t)=1/(1-t)-1-t. This proves (13), including composite N. If phi_1=0, C_act=C_E and the same quadratic relation holds for the actual cap. In general its explicit W correction cannot be discarded. In particular d is not the multiplicative Euler norm 1-x^N Phi.

4. Exact dressed source Ward equation and its affine completion

Let sigma fix H,x and send f to Qf. This is an algebra automorphism, with sigma=Ad(H inverse). Put

t=1−z,t2=1−Q2z,c=xNΦ/N. t=1-z,\qquad t_2=1-Q^2z,\qquad c=x^N\Phi/N.

Direct comparison of consecutive coefficients in (5), including the last degree, gives the WHOLE polynomial equations

σu=tu+c,t2σv2=v2−c. \boxed{\quad \sigma u=t u+c,\qquad t_2\sigma v_2=v_2-c.\quad} \tag{14}

The last coefficients are respectively +z^N/N and -z^N/N. They are not zero away from the nilpotent source.

On the open chart 1-x^N Phi invertible, t_2 is invertible since its cyclic product is 1-x^N Phi. For any a put

V=t2−1σ(a),T(a)=Vt,δ(a)=T(a)−Q−1a. V=t_2^{-1}\sigma(a),\qquad T(a)=Vt,\qquad \delta(a)=T(a)-Q^{-1}a. \tag{15}

Then the exact actual co-source equation is

$$ \boxed{\begin{aligned} \Lambda_{\rm act}(\delta(a)) &=\gamma c\rho_0\big(Vtu+cV-v_2V\big) -x^N\phi_1\tau(W\delta(a)). \end{aligned}} \tag{16} $$

For a proof, put b=v_2 a u. Equation (14) gives, with the noncommutative order retained,

v2T(a)u−σ(b)=c(Vtu+cV−v2V). v_2T(a)u-\sigma(b)=c\big(Vtu+cV-v_2V\big).

Since rho_0(sigma b)=Q(N-1)rho_0(b)=Q(-1)rho_0(b), applying gamma rho_0 and then (11) proves (16).

On the actual nilpotent source phi_1=phi_2=0, both right-hand terms vanish. Thus this is the exact dressed Ward identity

$$ \Lambda_{\rm act}(T(a)-Q^{-1}a)=0. \tag{17} $$

It is a statement about the ORIGINAL cap, not just a homogeneous formal solution. At nonzero centers (16) specifies the affine defect and the source’s first-leg wrap term. Treating (17) as homogeneous on that whole family would erase actual source data.

5. The source connection in x is also explicit

At phi_1=phi_2=0 define

A=∑r=1N−1xr−1fr1−Qr,A2=∑r=1N−1Q2rxr−1fr1−Qr. A=\sum_{r=1}^{N-1}\frac{x^{r-1}f^r}{1-Q^r},\qquad A_2=\sum_{r=1}^{N-1}\frac{Q^{2r}x^{r-1}f^r}{1-Q^r}.

The finite Euler equation gives u^(-1)partial_x u=A and partial_x v_2=-A_2 v_2. For example, take the nilpotent logarithm in u(Qz)=(1-z)u(z): in degree r<N its coefficient is 1/[r(1-Q^r)], which proves these formulas by differentiation. Differentiating (6) therefore gives exactly

$$ \partial_x\Lambda_{\rm act}(a) =\frac{2-2N}{x}\Lambda_{\rm act}(a) +\Lambda_{\rm act}(\partial_xa+aA-A_2a). \tag{18} $$

The scalar gamma character has been retained. In particular (18) agrees with the x^(1-N-a) dependence of every original basis cap value in (4). This is an x-connection at the fixed root, not an identification with the moving-q normal direction.

6. What this does and does not resolve on the critical path

This result supplies an exact actual-word/source transform with a rational coefficient kernel. It removes the need to guess an unsourced double-pole weight for the two-leg Borel cap. It also locates all central-F affine and wrapping defects before taking jets. These are useful prerequisites for a selected-period map.

The equality z=xf is an operator in the TOTAL lowering algebra. It has not been identified with the physical second-meridian Fourier variable p, nor with a coordinate on the geometric critical variety. Similarity between J_Q and a geometric density with a double pole is not such an identification. The actual word is Euler-conjugated in (8), and the central correction in (11) is not optional. The fixed-root central-F family also does not contain the moving-q divided-power normal automatically.

Thus the next required bridge is an actual source-compatible symbol/period transform from the dressed word and cap in (8) (or its full relative matrix source) to the geometric meridian/A-period object. That map must carry actual source currents to zero and send the original selected class to a proved nonzero geometric class. This note constructs no such map, and proves neither its subexponential coefficient nor the global volume norm. Gates C, D and E remain open.

7. Exact checks

The audit and saved output were rerun successfully at N=2,…,7. The checks use the literal original physical cap after triangular fusion, not a cap defined by the answer (6). They cover 139 nilpotent basis Cauchy/connection identities and 139 explicit rational-kernel coefficient identities, 556 whole cap basis values across four central specializations per level, 24 whole Euler/wrap cases, 500 affine co-source Ward identities on their invertible charts, and 24 literal signed-word period tests. The proofs above are all-level; these exact finite-field tests are independent convention and coefficient checks, not a substitute for those proofs.
