Two-leaf seeds in the actual complete Conway geometry

31 October 2026. geometric extension and dependency audit. Keep the original retained crossing-circle leaf hh, its literal turns and identity stacks. Allow seeds As=hsA_s=h^s for every fixed s≥2s\ge2 and the grammar E::=As∣T(E)∣EE. E::=A_s\mid T(E)\mid EE. Consider the actual cylindrical closure C(FG)C(FG), where both FF and GG are expressions in this grammar. Isolated C(A2)C(A_2) is excluded.

The strict complete source optimizer builds the actual complete finite-volume right-angled two-ball link metric. Every fixed retained half-twist partner has the same complete volume. The marked deformed representation and its actual filled-volume action also extend. The old restriction s≥3s\ge3 is unnecessary for these geometric statements.

This note does not assert a universal mixed-color or alias gap. The two-leaf source theorem treats the finite source, rounding and packet separately; the algebraic leaf certificate supplies an additional sufficient alias condition. A full quantum limit requires those analytic hypotheses.

5. The two exclusions and the source interface
