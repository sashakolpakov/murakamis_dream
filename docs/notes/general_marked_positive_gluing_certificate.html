A general marked positive gluing certificate, without a convex polyhedron

30 October 2026. A finite positive ideal gluing certificate is enough for the geometric anchor in the general augmented-plat program. No convex right-angled polyhedron, checkerboard decomposition, Conway expression, or bridge-width restriction is needed. There is also a useful simplification: for an actual ideal triangulation with torus links, positivity and the MULTIPLICATIVE edge equations already force unit edge winding. One need not impose an additional logarithmic angle equation at every edge.

This is a direct sufficient certificate theorem. It does not establish that every augmented diagram has such a solution, or select the original quantum source’s geometric period. The existing general source-regulator completion theorem requires still less geometric structure: an actual complete marked hyperbolic base and a nearby framed representation. The certificate below supplies that base and its absolute volume by finite algebraic data.

1. Exact hypotheses and the marked topological certificate

Let Y be the interior of a compact oriented three-manifold with nonempty torus boundary. In the intended application Y is the ACTUAL augmented plat exterior, with its given crossing disks, main components, half twists and primitive peripheral paths.

Supply a finite oriented ideal triangulation T with all of the following:

  • Every tetrahedron face is paired. There are no finite vertices, unpaired boundary faces, material boundary faces or extra cone points.
  • Away from the deleted ideal vertices, the face quotient is a three-manifold; every ideal vertex link is a compact torus. The orientation is coherent across the face pairings.
  • The quotient has a specified orientation-preserving homeomorphism to Y, taking its labeled ideal ends and peripheral paths to the ACTUAL marked ones. A triangulation of an unrelated link, the same number of cusps, or the same numerical volume does not supply this datum.

This topology condition is finite and reviewable: for example a common subdivision with the literal disk/projection cut and its labeled boundary maps gives a simplicial certificate. It is separate from solving shape equations. One can instead begin with a triangulation constructed directly on the specified exterior.

Give each oriented tetrahedron t a shape z_t with Im(z_t)>0, and set

z'_t=1/(1-z_t),   z''_t=1-1/z_t.                     (1)

The tetrahedral ordering assigns these parameters to its three opposite edge pairs. Use precisely those orderings in every equation. Require

product_(tetrahedral edge incidences at e) w_(t,e)=1 (2)

for every global edge e, where w is its assigned z,z’,or z’’. Incidences are counted with multiplicity, including identified edges of one tetrahedron. These are full complex equalities, not modulus equations.

Finally require completeness of every cusp. One explicit algebraic version is that the complex linear similarity multipliers of both recorded primitive peripheral paths are one:

a_mu=1,   a_lambda=1.                                (3)

They are computed from the actual face-path words in the corner triangles, with their literal signs. They are rational monomials in the tetrahedral shapes in a fixed convention. They are PROJECTIVE similarity multipliers; SL eigenvalues require their selected square roots and central signs. An equivalent finite horosphere certificate is given in section 4.

THEOREM. These data construct a positively oriented complete finite- volume hyperbolic metric on the actual Y, with

Vol(Y)=sum_t D(z_t)>0.                                (4)

Here D is the Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm, once for each oriented fundamental tetrahedron. There is no unrecorded factor two. The geometric holonomy, its peripheral paths and its fundamental-class regulator are those of the given marked topology.

2. Why multiplicative edge equations force winding one

Write n for the number of tetrahedra, e for the number of global edges, and h for the number of ideal vertices. There are 2n paired faces. End-compactify Y by coning each boundary torus to its deleted ideal vertex. Since chi(Y_compact)=0 and each torus has Euler characteristic zero, the end compactification has Euler characteristic h. The tetrahedral cell count gives

h-e+2n-n=h,   hence e=n.                              (5)

For completeness, chi(Y_compact)=0 follows by doubling the oriented compact three-manifold: its double has Euler characteristic zero by pairing complementary cells, and the gluing boundary has Euler characteristic zero. Alternatively count the triangulated cusp links: there are 4n link triangles, 6n link edges, and 2e link vertices, with multiplicity at the two ends of every global edge. Their total Euler characteristic is 2e-2n=0. The actual manifold/link hypothesis excludes an edge reversal that would invalidate this count.

All three parameters in (1) have positive imaginary part. Their arguments lie in (0,pi), and zz’z’’=-1 gives

Arg z+Arg z'+Arg z''=pi.                             (6)

Indeed the sum lies strictly between zero and 3pi and is pi modulo 2pi. Thus the sum of all six dihedral angles of one ideal tetrahedron is 2pi. At an edge e, equation (2) implies that its positive total angle is 2pi m_e for a positive integer m_e. Summing over ALL edges,

sum_e m_e=n=e.                                       (7)

Every summand is at least one and there are e summands. Consequently m_e=1 for EVERY edge. This is the promised unit-winding proof; it uses positivity and torus links globally, not an arbitrarily chosen branch of Log(2).

The same edge product also removes longitudinal translation. To see both parts directly, develop the edge to the vertical line from 0 to infinity. Consecutive incident faces determine rays in its transverse complex plane. The next tetrahedral sector changes their ratio by its assigned w. Multiplying all sectors gives exactly (2). The modulus records translation along the edge and the argument records rotation. Thus translation is zero, while (7) says the sectors fill one full turn. The local edge development is an ordinary ball neighborhood, not a branched 4pi-or-higher neighborhood with the same PSL word.

Ideal faces are all isometric triangles, so their specified vertex maps glue by actual isometries. Face interiors, tetrahedron interiors and the just-checked finite edge neighborhoods therefore carry a nonsingular oriented hyperbolic metric.

3. Complete cusps from the actual similarity links

A horospherical corner of an ideal tetrahedron is an oriented Euclidean triangle, defined up to positive scale. The face maps glue these as oriented Euclidean SIMILARITIES on the compact topological cusp torus. Section 2 makes every link vertex nonsingular: its incident angles sum to 2pi and its transverse edge holonomy is identity. The similarity structure consequently has a well-defined linear holonomy character of the torus group.

By (3) this character is trivial on its actual two generators and hence on the whole group. Choose one corner’s scale and transport it along link paths. The scale returns to itself around every loop, so the corner triangles acquire compatible Euclidean metrics. The compact link is a nonsingular flat Euclidean torus, and is complete. Its lifted local development covers the whole Euclidean plane: lift finite line segments using completeness, and use that the plane is simply connected. Its deck group is therefore a discrete rank-two translation lattice. In particular the two primitive paths have nonzero, real-linearly independent translation periods; these are a CONSEQUENCE, not an extra numerical assumption hiding in (3).

Realize these compatible corner scales by actual horospheres in each tetrahedron. A uniform shrink makes all the chosen corner neighborhoods disjoint. Their face maps agree on the horospheres, and each assembled end has the standard metric

dt^2+exp(-2t) ds_link^2.

There are finitely many tetrahedra, so the part left after truncating these ends is compact. The metric on all of Y is complete and has finite volume. Its universal developing cover is H3, by the same complete-local-isometry segment-lifting argument. Thus its actual holonomy is discrete and faithful. Summing the oriented volumes of its disjoint tetrahedral interiors proves (4).

4. A finite real-algebraic test and a local decoration alternative

Write z_t=x_t+i y_t. Positivity is y_t>0. Equations (1)–(3), after clearing only their specified nonzero denominators, are real polynomial equalities and equalities in finitely many variables. Every denominator in (1) is nonzero automatically by y_t>0. Literal peripheral paths must still be evaluated in the same marked convention. If an exact algebraic point is supplied, isolating intervals distinguish its real embedding and certify all strict inequalities. A numerical residual alone is not an exact solution certificate.

One may replace (3) by finite COMPATIBLE HOROSPHERE data. Give each corner triangle a positive scale and require equality of the corner segment lengths under every paired face. These equations are algebraic: canonical squared side lengths are rational expressions in x_t,y_t; positive scale squares remove square roots. All transitions are then Euclidean isometries. The resulting nonsingular compact torus is flat and complete as above. Its orientation-preserving deck isometries must be translations, since a nontrivial rotation of the Euclidean plane has a fixed point and cannot act as a deck transformation. Hence its full complex linear holonomy is one, recovering (3).

This decoration version is sometimes easier to audit than long peripheral monomials. It does NOT remove the primitive paths: those remain needed to identify a filling slope and source peripheral logs.

A strictly positive angle assignment alone is weaker than this certificate. It controls angle sums, but does not supply the complex edge modulus equations or complete cusp scales. Conversely an arbitrary nondegenerate complex solution with negatively oriented tetrahedra cannot be used in (7): its winding terms need not be positive. Such data need a different nonsingular developing-map certificate.

5. Absolute regulator and the source comparison that remains

The actual tetrahedral quotient is an oriented degree-one fundamental chain. Its developed ideal shapes therefore give

beta_T=sum_t [z_t],    D(beta_T)=Vol(Y).                (8)

This is the absolute geometric anchor. If all shapes lie in a common number field, (8) is its actual marked algebraic Bloch chain; the complete peripheral boundary vanishes after tensoring with Q, by the finite fundamental-chain boundary calculation already proved in the repo. No assertion about integral flattenings or an identification of different fields is needed for the real regulator identity (8).

For a nearby actual framed representation, develop the SAME marked ideal vertices using its peripheral fixed flags and take cross-ratios. Positivity and nondegeneracy persist in a small neighborhood. This constructs shapes from an existing representation; it does not assume existence of a representation for arbitrary specified peripheral values. The marked peripheral and regulator calculations of the general source-regulator theorem then apply on that connected branch.

In particular a proposed source action G must still satisfy

dG=(1/2) sum_s V_s dU_s,
Im G(0)=sum_t D(z_t),                                  (9)

with the ACTUAL primitive projective logs. Then Im G-(1/2)sum_s Re U_s Im V_s equals the actual representation volume. At nearby unit-charge primitive filling points the existing general completion theorem identifies this with the filled hyperbolic volume. Neither solving (2) nor knowing (8) proves (9) for the original quantum source. Whole-color control, normal sectors, selected cycles, nonzero amplitudes and the source’s branch selection remain additional tasks.

6. The exact variational criterion, in both directions

Let A_T be the angle polytope of this ACTUAL triangulation. Its variables are three opposite-pair angles per tetrahedron, each nonnegative; their tetrahedral sums are pi and their global edge sums are 2pi. Write A_T^+ for its strictly positive part and define

V(theta)=sum_t [Lambda(alpha_t)+Lambda(beta_t)+Lambda(gamma_t)],
Lambda(a)=-integral_0^a log(2 sin s) ds.                  (10)

The following equivalent criteria give the same complete metric:

  • There is a positive complete gluing certificate of section 1.
  • V has a critical point in A_T^+.
  • The maximum of V on the closed angle polytope is attained in A_T^+.

When these hold, the critical point is UNIQUE, including as a maximizer of the closed polytope, and its value is the actual Vol(Y). This is the needed general Casson–Rivin optimization statement. Here is a direct proof of both directions and the normalization; no geometric existence theorem is being inserted into the implication.

The elementary horocycle and length identity

For one positive ideal tetrahedron, choose an arbitrary horosphere at each of its four corners. At corner a, its Euclidean triangle has angles theta_ab, theta_ac, theta_ad. The sine law gives one positive number C_a such that its side in the ideal face abc has length

s_(a;bc)=C_a sin(theta_ad).

In an ideal triangle, the signed truncated length of its edge ab is

ell_ab=-log[s_(a;bc) s_(b;ac)].

To check the constant directly, send that triangle to (infinity,0,1). The corner segment at infinity on height H has length 1/H; the segment at zero on a horocycle of Euclidean diameter d has length d. Their edge distance is log(H/d), proving the formula. Overlapping horospheres give its signed version, with the same identity.

Put S=sin(alpha)sin(beta)sin(gamma) and

u_a=-log C_a-(1/2)log S.

The two opposite angle pairs other than theta_ab occur in the product of the two corner segments. Consequently

ell_ab=log sin(theta_ab)+u_a+u_b.                       (11)

Conversely ANY four real values u_a are realized by positive corner horospheres: take C_a=exp(-u_a)/sqrt(S).

On tetrahedral angle variations, the sum of the three angles at every corner is fixed. Equation (11) and opposite-pair equality therefore give the differential identity

dV_t=-(1/2) sum_(six tetrahedral edges ab) ell_ab dtheta_ab.
                                                              (12)

Indeed the four u terms cancel by the corner angle sums, and each opposite pair supplies -log sin(alpha) d alpha; the missing log 2 also cancels because d alpha+d beta+d gamma=0. This derives the ideal Schlaefli formula used here directly from (10) and actual horocycle lengths.

Complete gluing implies a critical point and the unique maximum

At the complete metric choose the compatible cusp horospheres of section 3. Each global edge e has ONE truncated length ell_e in all incident tetrahedra. Summing (12) gives

dV=-(1/2) sum_e ell_e d(total angle at e)=0              (13)

on the tangent space of A_T. Thus the geometric point is critical.

The Hessian of V_t on gamma=pi-alpha-beta is negative definite. The negative Hessian matrix is

[[cot alpha+cot gamma, cot gamma],
 [cot gamma, cot beta+cot gamma]].

Its first diagonal entry is sin(beta)/(sin(alpha)sin(gamma))>0, and its determinant is 1, by the cotangent addition identity when alpha+beta+gamma=pi. Hence V is strictly concave on the product of positive tetrahedral angle simplices and on A_T^+. It extends continuously to the compact closed angle polytope. Along a segment from its critical positive point to ANY other closed point, all intermediate points are positive, the initial derivative is zero, and the second derivative is strictly negative. The volume strictly decreases, so the endpoint also has smaller value. This proves the unique global maximum assertion, including boundary points.

A positive critical point reconstructs complete gluing

Conversely let theta in A_T^+ be critical. Since its constraints are linear, elementary row-space/kernel duality supplies real multipliers lambda_t for the tetrahedral sums and eta_e for the global edge sums:

-log(2 sin alpha_t)=lambda_t+eta_e+eta_f,                (14)

where e,f are the two opposite edge incidences carrying alpha_t. The same formula holds for beta and gamma, with multiplicity if e=f. Constraint dependencies do not affect this statement.

Construct the positive ideal tetrahedron with these angles, for example

z_t=[sin(beta_t)/sin(gamma_t)] exp(i alpha_t).

Put L_e=-2 eta_e. Equation (14) gives

L_e+L_f-2log sin(alpha_t)=2lambda_t+2log 2,

the SAME value for all three opposite pairs in t. Therefore there exist four real numbers u_a, one per tetrahedral corner, such that

L_(ab)=log sin(theta_ab)+u_a+u_b                       (15)

for all six incidences. Here is the entire linear algebra: set b_ab=L_(ab)-log sin(theta_ab). The three opposite-pair sums of b are equal, which is precisely the two compatibility conditions for the six equations b_ab=u_a+u_b. They have a unique solution, starting with u_a=(b_ab+b_ac-b_bc)/2 and then u_b=b_ab-u_a, etc.

By (11), choose the actual tetrahedral corner horospheres realizing these u_a. Thus EVERY incident tetrahedron now has the same prescribed truncated length L_e on a global edge. The three side lengths of an ideal face determine its three corner horocycle sizes uniquely: if s_a s_b=exp(-L_ab), then

log s_a=(-L_ab-L_ac+L_bc)/2.

Consequently all face pairings match all corner horospheres. Around each global edge these maps preserve the two endpoint horospheres, so its dilation is zero. Its total angle is 2pi by the angle-polytope constraint, so its full edge holonomy is identity and the local development is nonsingular. The compatible corner decorations give complete flat cusp tori by section 4. Thus these shapes satisfy the edge and complete peripheral equations and construct the actual complete geometric metric. This proves the converse.

In particular, a nonempty strict angle polytope alone is NOT the criterion. Its volume maximum must lie in the strict interior. The proof above explains exactly what that interior optimum supplies.

7. A purely algebraic certificate for that strict optimum

The variational criterion has a finite real-algebraic form with no logarithms. For each opposite angle pair take a unit phase w_(t,a) in the upper half-plane. Require

|w_(t,a)|^2=1, Im w_(t,a)>0,
product_(three pairs in t) w_(t,a)=-1,
product_(incidences at global edge e) w_(t,a)=1.        (16)

The tetrahedral argument is pi by the same range check as (6), and the global arguments are exactly 2pi by (5)–(7). Thus these are an actual strict angle structure, with no missing angle winding.

Add positive real variables A_t and B_e and require, at every opposite pair whose global edges are e,f,

2 Im(w_(t,a)) A_t B_e B_f=1.                          (17)

Taking real logarithms gives precisely (14), with lambda_t=log A_t and eta_e=log B_e. Therefore (16)–(17) imply a positive critical point, hence actual complete geometry and the unique volume maximum. Conversely every such complete positive geometric point supplies these multipliers by the preceding proof. Equations (16)–(17) and strict positivity are finite polynomial conditions in real and imaginary parts. Repeated edge incidences retain the corresponding factor B_e^2.

This is a simple algebraic sufficient criterion on ANY actual marked ideal triangulation, without first finding a convex Q_Delta. It is not a purely combinatorial classification: proving nonemptiness of this strict semialgebraic system for a chosen broad diagram class is still an existence problem. Its distinguished strict point is an honest geometric optimum, rather than an arbitrary branch of the exponentiated equations. Failure for one supplied triangulation is not a proof that Y is nonhyperbolic; a different marked triangulation may admit the certificate.

The unique positive optimum also has ALGEBRAIC phases and shapes. Normalize its angles by pi. The closed angle polytope is rational, and its atoms are the rational coordinate angle triples, with weight one. The already proved rational angle-optimizer theorem therefore applies directly. More explicitly, choose a rational tangent basis, exponentiate stationarity after clearing its denominators, and use the strictly negative Hessian to obtain an invertible complex Jacobian at the selected phase point. Differentiating those polynomial equations forces its coordinates to have zero transcendence degree over the coefficient number field. Formula z=exp(i alpha) sin(beta)/sin(gamma) then gives algebraic positive shapes. This proves algebraicity of the marked optimum; it does not make its dilogarithm value algebraic or identify any cyclotomic quantum source with that period.

There is also a precise DECORATION TORSOR in this certificate. With the strict phases fixed, all positive multiplier solutions (A_t,B_e) correspond exactly to compatible choices of cusp horospheres. Indeed the reconstruction above converts each solution to corner horospheres; between two solutions their corner scale ratios agree across every paired face, and hence are constant on each actual cusp. If changing the horosphere at cusp v changes its corner term u by s_v, then L_e changes by s_v+s_w at an edge with endpoints v,w. Put t_v=exp(s_v/2). The exact action is

A_t -> A_t product_(four corners v of t) t_v,
B_e -> B_e/(t_v t_w),        t_v>0.                    (18)

It preserves (17) because the two opposite edges together use all four corners. The preceding horosphere comparison proves transitivity. It is free: a fixed B would give s_v+s_w=0 on every edge; the three edge equations of any tetrahedral face force each of its corner values to be zero, even when some global cusp labels coincide. Thus the positive solution fiber is a torsor for one positive scale per cusp. This is the actual geometric cusp-decoration gauge, NOT the still-unidentified quantum-period selection torsor. No assertion that the same monomial action is free over C is made; its sign kernels and square-root choices would have to be retained there.

8. The weakest useful general class and what was removed

There are three different levels of geometric input:

  1. An actual complete finite-volume marked hyperbolic base Y is enough for the already proved general geometric completion/regulator implication. It needs no ideal triangulation at all.
  2. An actual marked positive complete gluing certificate as above gives that base and its regulator by finite explicit equations, uniformly for arbitrary plat width and arbitrary triangulation incidence.
  3. A convex positive right-angled Q_Delta certificate gives such a triangulation by the coning construction in the matching-graph theorem.

Thus (2) is a broader usable certificate than requiring (3). We do not claim a new existence theorem for positive ideal triangulations of all hyperbolic manifolds, nor a combinatorial characterization of all graphs admitting Q_Delta. Neither is needed for the implication proved here. For the general long-twist target, one can retain its existing hypothesis that the ACTUAL augmented exterior is hyperbolic, and use ANY independently certified geometric base instead of first reconstructing a right-angled convex polyhedron. A common marked positive triangulation is a convenient way to compare a source Bloch chain with its actual fundamental class.

The broad quantum theorem remains about the original augmented source on this geometric base; the certificate has not replaced that source by a state sum defined from T.

9. Coverage and independent exact checks

For coverage, the antecedent is explicit in Futer–Gueritaud, From angled triangulations to hyperbolic structures: its abstract states the Casson–Rivin theorem that a critical point of the angle-structure volume functional gives a complete hyperbolic structure. No novelty of that theorem is claimed. Sections 2–7 above give the direct proof and exact marked-certificate form used here; they do not import an existence theorem for arbitrary augmented plats.

The exact audit constructs an eight-tetrahedron face quotient from two regular ideal octahedra with the original two bowtie pairings. It checks orientation of every face map, three torus vertex links, all eight complex edge products, and angle sum exactly 2pi at every edge. Ninety-six paired corner-segment checks give compatible horospheres. The same exact data satisfy the critical-point multiplier equations independently; the restricted Hessian determinant and four-corner compatibility identity are checked symbolically. These test the stated geometric and variational certificate, not a quantum source selection theorem.
