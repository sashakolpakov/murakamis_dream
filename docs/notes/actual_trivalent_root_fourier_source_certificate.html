An independent root-Fourier certificate for the trivalent black scalar

29 October 2026. This derives an EXACT Euler-polynomial coefficient formula from the actual highest/descendant source. It proves its integer saturation support J>=d by cancellation of a complete Fourier mode. The positive C R identity is not assumed in this derivation; its remaining finite transformation is now separately proved. This coefficient certificate retains all original phases and root endpoint currents.

Let N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d, F_a=(Q;Q)_a. Assume integer 0<=j_i<=floor(d/2) satisfying the triangle inequalities, and put r=j_2+j_3-j_1 and J=j_1+j_2+j_3. Thus 0<=r<=2min(j_2,j_3). Use the original balanced coproduct and highest vectors h(j)_k=q(k^2)[d-j choose k]_Q. The selected actual black coefficient is N^3 A, with

A=q^[-j_1(d-2j_1)] sum_k x^[k+j_1+j_3-2d]
   conjugate[h^(j_1)_k h^(j_3)_k
      W^(j_2)_r(j_3+k,d-j_1-k)].                 (1)

Here k=0,…,min(d-j_1,d-j_3), W(j)_r=F_pairr h^(j)/F_r. This is the literal endpoint extraction of the all-port vertex, including its lowest-column phase; no generic 6j formula is used.

1. Exact coefficient theorem

Define the unnormalized Jackson difference

D_Q g(T)=[g(T)-g(QT)]/T,
D_Q T^h=(1-Q^h)T^(h-1),
f(T)=(TQ^(1-j_3);Q)_(j_3),
g(T)=(TQ;Q)_(j_2).

The source-exact identity is

A= N q^E/[F_(j_1)F_(j_2)F_(j_3)F_r]
                     *[T^d] g(T)^2 D_Q^r f(T)^2,
E=3j_1^2+j_2^2+j_3^2-2j_1j_2-2j_1j_3
                          +2j_2j_3-2j_3-2.       (2)

Every polynomial and denominator is finite; all denominator indices are below N. In particular deg(g^2 D_Q^r f^2)=J, so

J<d implies A=0 EXACTLY.                         (3)

This is an original-source cancellation before taking absolute values, not an admissibility rule imposed on a guessed 6j symbol.

2. Own finite Fourier proof

The original coproduct binomial gives, with b=d-j_1-k,

W^(j_2)_r(j_3+k,b)=F_(j_3+k)F_bF_(d-j_2)
  *sum_z q^(z^2)x^[-r+j_3+k-z]
   Q^[z(r-j_3-k+z)]
   /[F_z^2 F_(d-j_2-z)^2 F_(j_3+k-z)
                                  F_(j_2-j_1-k+z)].

The range is exactly where every displayed denominator index is nonnegative and <N. Substitute this and the two highest vectors in (1). Use only

conjugate(F_a)=(-1)^a Q^[-a(a+1)/2]F_a,
F_(d-a)=N(-1)^a Q^[a(a+1)/2]/F_a,
q^N=-1.

After the factors N cancel, the result is

A=(-1)^(j_3)q^B/[F_(j_1)F_(j_2)F_(j_3)]
  *sum_(k,z) (-1)^(k+z)
   q^[(k-z)^2+(1+2j_1-2j_2)k+(1-2j_3)z]
   [(Q^(k+1);Q)_(j_3)(Q^(z+1);Q)_(j_2)]^2
   /[F_(j_3+k-z)F_(j_2-j_1-k+z)],
B=3j_1^2-2j_1j_2+j_2^2-j_3-2.                  (4)

Equation (4) displays all phase bookkeeping; no root sign is left in an unspecified scalar. Put l=k-z and p=j_3+l. The two last factorials force p=0,…,r. For fixed p the z-dependent factor is

Q^[(1-r)z]
   (Q^(z+p-j_3+1);Q)_(j_3)^2(Q^(z+1);Q)_(j_2)^2.

Its sum extends EXACTLY to z=0,…,N-1. For z>d-j_2 the second Euler factor contains 1-Q^N. If k=z+p-j_3<0 then k>=-j_3 and the first factor contains 1-Q^0; if k>d-j_3 it contains 1-Q^N. There is no k>=N wrap because z<=d-j_2 and p-j_3<=j_2-j_1 imply k<=d-j_1. Thus all added states are killed by their literal factorial zeros.

For a polynomial P(T) of degree <=2(j_2+j_3)<2N, complete root Fourier summation consequently selects precisely

sum_(z=0)^(N-1) Q^[(1-r)z]P(Q^z)
            =N([T^(r-1)]P+[T^(N+r-1)]P).         (5)

Absent or negative coefficient indices mean zero. The p sum in (4) is, after its explicit constant phase is extracted,

H(T)=sum_(p=0)^r (-1)^p Q^[p(p-1)/2+(1-r)p]
               [r choose p]_Q f(Q^pT)^2.

If f(T)^2=sum_h c_h T^h, the finite binomial theorem gives

H(T)=sum_h c_h T^h(Q^(1-r+h);Q)_r
     =T^r D_Q^r f(T)^2.                         (6)

In fact for h<r the product contains 1-Q^0, while for h>=r it equals F_h/F_(h-r). Therefore the LOW Fourier coefficient r-1 in (5) vanishes EXACTLY. The surviving high coefficient is [T^(N+r-1)]g2H=[Td]g2D_Qr f^2. The p-independent phase in (4) is q^E, with E as in (2), and its factorial denominator is F_r. This proves (2) and (3), including r=0 and all level parities.

3. A finite low-degree version at saturation depth delta

For delta=J-d>=0, put c_e=[U^e](U;Q)_(j_3)^2. Reversal of the two finite Euler products in (2) gives

[T^d]g^2D_Q^r f^2
  =Q^[j_2(j_2+1)-j_3(j_3-1)] [U^delta]
   (UQ^(-j_2);Q)_(j_2)^2
   *sum_(e=0)^(2j_3-r) c_e
                   F_(2j_3-e)/F_(2j_3-e-r) U^e. (7)

Thus only coefficients <=delta need be computed. All denominator indices in (7) are physical: delta<=j_i and delta<=2j_3-r follow from the bounds on the other two j’s. For any fixed saturation depth this is a fixed-length source formula, with no unspecified boundary data or q-normal derivative. It is not claimed that its summands have a common phase.

4. Two entire saturation layers are manifestly nonzero

Let

Lead=Q^[j_2(j_2+1)-j_3(j_3-1)]
                          F_(2j_3)/F_(2j_3-r).

This is the NONZERO leading coefficient of g2D_Qr f^2. Therefore on the entire layer delta=0, equation (2) gives

A=Nq^E Lead/[F_(j_1)F_(j_2)F_(j_3)F_r]!=0.      (8)

For delta=1, so J=N, the next-to-leading coefficient divided by Lead is

-2/(1-Q) [Q^(-j_2)(1-Q^(j_2))
         +(1-Q^(j_3))(1-Q^(2j_3-r))/(1-Q^(2j_3))]
  =2Q^(-2j_2)(1-Q^(j_2))(1-Q^(j_2+j_3))
                                  /[(1-Q)(1+Q^(j_3))]. (9)

The first equality follows by taking the next coefficient in the two Euler squares before the Jackson derivative: their ratios are -2sum_(a=1)(j_2)Q-a and -2sum_(a=0)(j_3-1)Qa; the derivative multiplies the latter ratio by (1-Q(2j_3-r))/(1-Q(2j_3)). For the second equality use 2j_3-r=J-2j_2=N-2j_2 and cancel the factor 1-Q^(j_3) in 1-Q^(2j_3).

Every factor on the last line of (9) is nonzero. Indeed J=N and 2j_i<=d force j_i>=1; also j_2+j_3=N-j_1 lies strictly between 0 and N, and j_3<N/2 excludes 1+Q^(j_3)=0. Multiplying (9) by the nonzero prefactor in (2) proves A!=0 on the ENTIRE delta=1 layer, at both level parities. Empty small-N layers are simply absent. These are genuine all-triple source nonvanishing statements, not deductions from a presumed positive formula.

5. The subsequent exact transformation

The complete positive formula from this independent route equates the finite coefficient in (2) or (7), including its displayed phase, to

(-1)^(N+J)q^[2j_1^2-j_1j_2-j_1j_3+j_2j_3-J-3]
   *[prod_i s_(j_i)/(N s_delta)] R_N(j).

Equation (3) already proves its saturation-zero branch. The one-central-channel result supplies a proved nonzero boundary line. The finite coefficient-to- short-series and Sears bridge is now proved in the linked finite Euler-to-Racah transform, with an independent square-linearization derivation. Finite experiments alone do not prove it. No sewn-graph energy, magnetic norm or hyperbolic-volume claim is made from this coefficient formula.
