The resonant framing blowup and the actual peripheral line

27 October 2026. This note proves exact identities on the geometric framing chart of the original first-slot inverse-pair cut. The full ADO dimension cancels the chart’s apparent first-order pole before specialization. The paired principal Gaussian line also extends. Neither statement constructs the required map from the actual finite source to a selected geometric period.

One correction matters: the generic physical inverse-pair atom is AC=M*b_h/b_(h+1). Its first boundary coefficient is s*zeta. The coefficient s*zeta+a/2 belongs to the RAW prefix quotient b_h/b_(h+1), which omits its physical factor M. Those expressions cannot be substituted for each other in a quantum-product boundary calculation.

1. Work on a geometric formal branch, before imposing level N

Let R be a characteristic-zero formal discrete valuation ring with uniformizer epsilon, containing the marked geometric coefficients. Fix a meridian deformation with

kappa=M-M^(-1)=epsilon*a,  M(0)=1,
p=1-epsilon*zeta,
d=1-kappa*beta,
t=r+beta*s=d/(epsilon*zeta).

Here M is the unique formal root near one of M-M inverse=epsilona. The coefficients c,beta,s may vary regularly with epsilon; c and s are units at zero. All leading formulas below use their values at epsilon=0. We work where zeta and szeta+a are units. These are classical geometric coordinates, not a newly chosen finite source.

The actual generic-M first-slot matrices, as fixed in the checked meridian-germ appendix, are

F=[[r,1-r],[s,-kappa-s]],
U=[[M-c*beta,kappa*beta-c*beta^2],
                     [c,M^(-1)+c*beta]].

Set Delta=s+kappa*r. Exact substitution gives

Delta=d*(s*zeta+a)/zeta.                         (1)

In particular Delta is a unit on this chart, despite r having valuation minus one. Direct conjugation gives, for k=(F inverse U F)_11,

k=M^(-1)+a*(epsilon*a+s)/(s*zeta+a)
          -c*d*p/[epsilon^2*zeta*(s*zeta+a)].    (2)

For completeness, U-M inverse I is the rank-one product

[kappa-c*beta;c]*(1,beta).

Its conjugated (1,1) entry is

[kappa*(kappa+s)+c*(d-t)]*t/Delta,

which proves (2), without discarding any meridian term. The actual physical momentum is still

-(F inverse U F)_12/(k-M^(-1))=p.

The SECOND balanced physical position, denoted A_e to distinguish it from the reference cut coordinate A_cut, is

A_e=M/k,
epsilon^(-2)*A_e -> -zeta*(s*zeta+a)/c.          (3)

Thus its valuation is two on this unit chart. The reference coordinate remains A_cut=M/(M+s); the two coordinates must not be conflated.

2. The framing lattice extends although the scalar clock escapes

The matrix F has determinant -Delta, a unit, and at least one entry of valuation minus one. Over R its Smith exponents are therefore (-1,+1). Equivalently

Lambda=F^(-1)R^2

is a free rank-two lattice inside the framed representation space. For a geometric representation defined over R, every conjugated holonomy F inverse rho(g) F preserves Lambda. Also

F*(1,1)^t=(1,-kappa)^t,

so the marked inverse flag remains primitive. This proves flatness of the underlying geometric representation lattice, not a comparison with a quantum source lattice.

There is a useful precise restriction on that comparison. The actual finite second position operator has relation B_e^N=1 on the complete root fiber. A multiplicative scalar specialization carrying B_e to the geometric A_e in (3) cannot preserve that relation: A_e^N has valuation 2N, whereas 1 has valuation zero. Multiplication by a unit does not change this discrepancy. Dividing A_e by epsilon squared changes the N-th-power relation by epsilon^(2N), which is precisely a factor that must be accounted for, rather than a harmless unit gauge.

This excludes a regular scalar-clock substitution with the original norm relation. It does not exclude a linear period transform, a singular change of lattice with its determinant retained, or an irregular/nonlocal source comparison. Such a construction would still need to carry the actual word and selected source.

3. The actual dimension cancels the framing-form pole exactly

At fixed M the invariant form already specified by the marked centralizer calculation is

Omega_cut=-dr wedge ds/[Delta*(M+s)].

Treat epsilon and a as base parameters. Using (1) and dlog A_cut=-ds/(M+s), its exact expression is

epsilon*Omega_cut
  =dlog A_cut wedge dzeta/[zeta*(s*zeta+a)].      (4)

This is an algebraic identity, not a claim about a selected integration cycle or a uniform Gaussian expansion.

Now retain the original dimension line, including its phase:

d_N(M)=eta_N/u_N(M),  eta_N=i^(N+3),
u_N(M)=(M^N-M^(-N))/(M-M^(-1)).

The quotient u_N is a Laurent polynomial with u_N(1)=N. With B=M^N its exact fraction-field expression is

d_N/eta_N=kappa*B/(B^2-1).

Consequently, before any limiting argument,

(d_N/eta_N)*Omega_cut
  =a*B/(B^2-1)
       *dlog A_cut wedge dzeta/[zeta*(s*zeta+a)]. (5)

The epsilon inverse pole in (4) cancels against the original peripheral line. The factor B squared minus one remains in (5). It cannot be deleted at a torsion value or used to replace the whole regular lattice by two separately undefined quotients. At M=1 the original polynomial formula gives d_N/eta_N=1/N.

On the physical power graph epsilon=1/N and fixed a, the branch is

M_N=exp(asinh(a/(2N))),
B_N=exp(N*asinh(a/(2N))) -> exp(a/2).

Thus the scalar in (5) tends to a/[2*sinh(a/2)] away from its remaining norm divisor. At a=0 its regular continuation is one, in agreement with the exact value (1/N)/epsilon=1. This verifies the peripheral normalization at this boundary. It does not identify the rest of a geometric density with the original quantum period.

4. Physical inverse-pair atoms and the first-order correction

Let mu_M=[[M,1],[0,M inverse]] and

u_h=(M^h-M^(-h))/kappa,
a_h=(F inverse mu_M^h F)_11=M^h+s*u_h,
b_h=(F inverse U*mu_M^h F)_11.

The power identity gives (mu_M^h)’_21=s*u_h in the framed chart. Since U’_12=-p*(k-M inverse), we get the exact formula

b_h=k*[M^h+(1-p)*s*u_h]+p*M^(-1)*s*u_h.         (6)

For the negative move carrying U left across the next reference meridian, the actual physical dictionary A_j=M*ell_previous/ell_next gives

A=M*a_h/a_(h+1),
B=M*a_(h+1)/b_(h+1),
C=a_(h+1)*b_h/[b_(h+1)*a_h],
AC=M*b_h/b_(h+1).                              (7)

This is the generic-M extension of the complete formulas; the factors M in A,B and AC are forced by the signed Gauss/position dictionary. At the boundary M=1+epsilon*a/2+O(epsilon squared) and u_h=h+O(epsilon squared). Equation (2) has common leading coefficient -c/[zeta*(s*zeta+a)] at order epsilon minus two. From (6),

b_h/b_(h+1)=1-epsilon*(s*zeta+a/2)+O(epsilon^2),
AC=1-epsilon*s*zeta+O(epsilon^2).                (8)

The physical atom therefore has no additional a/2 term. Keeping only the raw prefix ratio would introduce a false half-shift into a proposed product or Gamma-profile normalization. No such quantum profile is established in this note.

At completeness the five negative coefficient atoms A,C,1/B,AC,C/B have valuations (0,0,-2,0,-2). The same valuations hold on this generic blowup chart when their leading coefficients are units. The atom AC approaches one at order epsilon. Consequently the ordinary fixed-atom Gaussian expansion is not uniform here, even though the inverse-pair principal normal line below is regular.

5. The paired principal normal ratio extends

The squared PRINCIPAL variable amplitudes from the actual balanced coefficients, with Y=M squared and all fixed phases retained separately, are

a_-^2=C/(AB)*(1-AC)*(1-Y/B)
                     /[(1-A)*(1-C)*(1-YC/B)],
a_+^2=AB*(1-BC)*(1-Y/A)
                     /[(1-B)*(1-C)*(1-YC/A)].

The inverse move uses (A’,B’,C’)=(B/C,AC,1/C). Direct cancellation, valid before putting Y=1, proves

a_-^2(A,B,C)*a_+^2(A',B',C')=-C^2/(1-C)^2.      (9)

The mixed Hessians are V_-=C/(1-C) and V_+(1/C)=-V_-. The added normal Hessian has determinant -V_- squared. Thus its squared principal amplitude divided by that determinant is EXACTLY one. On the unit chart C tends to A(0) inverse and V_- tends to 1/[A(0)-1], a unit. The inverse-pair PRINCIPAL normal line extends through this blowup after pairing.

This does not say that the individual amplitudes, all higher coefficients, the open source, or the full quantum normal lattice extend in this Gaussian frame. Equation (8) explicitly shows an atom colliding with one. An all-order completed source transform would have to control that collision with its actual word data.

6. Critical-path implication and exact checks

The actual full dimension supplies the precise first peripheral pole cancellation; no extra numerical normalization is available to choose here. The inverse-pair principal determinant also causes no additional unknown scalar. What remains is a source-compatible linear/irregular geometric period map at the resonance, proving that the original selected class has a nonzero geometric image, with subexponential coefficient and a sharp global norm estimate. The displayed cancellations do not prove those three assertions.

The symbolic audit checks the raw conjugation, the whole dimension/form identity, the physical-versus-raw first-order correction, the generic inverse principal-amplitude identity, and the dimension polynomial through level twelve. Its saved output is a verification of these identities, not evidence of a volume limit or a finite-source/geometric identification.
