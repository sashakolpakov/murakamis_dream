Full physical-row products and strictly signed normal support

Private written proof, 17 November 2026.

The missing normal sector in an ordinary product of physical rows has a particularly simple support and sign. Its coefficient at the double node cℓc_\ell is (−1)N−1(-1)^{N-1} times a strictly positive quantity exactly when j+k+ℓ≥N−1j+k+\ell\ge N-1. That quantity is a symmetric triple trace with an explicit positive sine-factorial sum. This holds for every physical pair j,kj,k, including products above the value-row degree range.

The positive Newton multiplication table and the general confluent coordinate formula are prior results. We use them to obtain this strict support statement and explicit coefficients; we do not present coordinate inversion as a new theorem. The value coefficients retain a separate alternating derivative tail. No scalar lower bound after crossing phases is inferred.
