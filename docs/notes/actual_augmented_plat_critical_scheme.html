The actual Casimir plat phase and its marked representation germ

30 October 2026. On a regular chart at a complete geometric point, the constrained critical scheme of the original augmented-plat phase is isomorphic to the actual marked representation germ modulo one diagonal framing scale. This includes local Artin test rings, rather than only complex points or tangent dimensions. The proof reconstructs shared wire momenta from the event-incidence equations and supplies an inverse which is regular at the parabolic channel endpoint.

The phase is the actual normalized Casimir/q-Hahn phase. This is not an application of the older braid-cut scheme claim with its different boundary state. Every original cup, ordinary cap, the unrestricted marked cap after its permitted whole-source degree restriction, and both physical degree adapters occur below. No quantum source quotient or asymptotic selection is asserted.

1. Objects and the precise local statement

Fix a literal augmented plat, its retained half twists epsilon_v, and its actual outside wires. Its event graph has a vertex for each cup, cap or two-input/two-output circle coupon, and one directed edge for each physical wire between successive events. Identity segments are included in that wire. Let B be its oriented incidence matrix, with +1 at an incoming half-edge and -1 at an outgoing half-edge. The leading degree constraints are

B x=b,  b_cup=-1, b_cap=1, b_coupon=0.               (1)

Each connected component has equally many cups and caps, by counting its incoming and outgoing half-edges, so its entries of b sum to zero. The graph can have arbitrary cycles and width. We state the representation quotient for a CONNECTED event graph. The incidence construction below works componentwise without that assumption. A disconnected projection is a split diagram; contracting the connected local crossing-circle boxes does not change that connectivity. Thus the connected case includes an essential nonsplit augmented-plat diagram.

Choose one of the actual low/high degree adapters at each coupon and a regular complete geometric point as in the generic magnetic chart. Fix its affine logarithmic lift of (1), its simple internal q-Hahn roots, and all logarithmic characters. All displayed local-map denominators, prefix diagonals, main K+1, and the chosen total F charts are units. We work in the corresponding formal neighborhood over C. The internal roots are eliminated by the exact marked critical reduction in the channel-Hessian theorem, not by substituting roots into an unadjusted phase.

For each auxiliary circle retain its longitude eigenvalue J_v near -1 and prescribe its physical meridian eigenvalue m_v near 1. The channel equation is the original one

R_(epsilon_v)=m_v^(-2).                              (2)

The complete fiber has m_v=1. Equivalently the positive cosine branch uses m_v=exp(-pi i chi_v) and adds 2 Log(m_v)(xi_v-1/2) to the phase, with J_v=exp(2pi i xi_v).

Let Crit be the formal scheme given by magnetic criticality on ker B and (2), with the retained integer characters. Let Rep be the actual Morse/ crossing-disk representation germ with the same peripheral marking: main meridians have their exact unipotent eigenvalue +1, auxiliary meridians have selected eigenvalue m_v, and the raw ordered disk product has selected eigenvalue J_v. All the same regular chart conditions hold. The main flag means the kernel of mu-I, NOT an arbitrary invariant line of a trace-two Artin matrix. The auxiliary flag is the selected J_v eigenline of its noncentral disk product.

Then there is an isomorphism of formal functors

Crit = Rep / T_diag,                               (3)

where T_diag acts by simultaneous projective diagonal conjugation. The quotient has a concrete local slice: set the momentum S on one chosen physical wire to 1. Every such S is a unit. No reductive-quotient existence theorem or reduction of a nonreduced ring is needed for (3). Sections 2–6 give mutually inverse constructions over every local Artin C-algebra with the specified residue point.

2. Incidence criticality reconstructs all wire momenta

Use physical half-edge variables before imposing (1). At a low coupon take the following ambient extension of its root-reduced phase:

s=x_in,1+x_in,2,  u=x_out,1, v=x_in,1,
f_v=Theta_bar(s,u,v,xi).

The second output is recovered by degree conservation. Write D, Y_in, Y_out for the ACTUAL derivatives in the local character note. The four exponentiated half-edge derivatives of this extension are

incoming: (D Y_in,D),  outgoing: (Y_out,1).          (4)

For a high coupon use the actual affine function Theta_bar(2-s,u-s+1,v-s+1,xi). Formula (4) remains true with D_high=1/(D Y_out Y_in) and the physical high head coordinates. Thus there is no separate high-degree gauge assumption.

Let p_(v,e) be the local derivative of the chosen ambient extension and c_e=exp(sum_(v incident e) p_(v,e)). Magnetic criticality means

product_e c_e^(l_e)=1 for every l in ker_Z B.         (5)

The integer cycle lattice is the full kernel: an oriented graph incidence matrix is saturated. A direct construction is enough here. Choose a spanning tree and one vertex value t_v0=1. Solve along its edges

c_e=t_tail(e)/t_head(e).                            (6)

Every non-tree edge satisfies (6) precisely because its fundamental cycle satisfies (5). This constructs unit vertex values over ANY commutative test ring. They are unique up to one common unit per connected component; no extraction of roots is involved.

Now define the momentum at each incident half-edge by

S_(v,e)=t_v exp(B_(v,e) p_(v,e)).                   (7)

Equation (6) says exactly that the two definitions of S on a wire agree. At a coupon (4),(7) give

S_in=(t_v D Y_in,t_v D),
S_out=(t_v/Y_out,t_v).                             (8)

This is the original local kernel formula with g=t_v D. Conversely shared momenta satisfying that formula give (7), hence (6),(5). Choosing another ambient extension changes its derivatives by a local multiple of the degree row and merely changes t_v, leaving S unchanged.

In additive logarithmic language, (5) is grad F=B^t lambda after keeping the fixed integral character, and t_v=exp(-lambda_v). On a formal Artin neighborhood the exponential is injective on nilpotents. Thus the exponentiated equations and the exactly marked critical equations have the same infinitesimal scheme, not just the same complex zero set. The spanning-tree construction also proves the asserted saturation and the absence of hidden finite scale choices.

3. The actual boundary maps have precisely the inverse-pair relation

On their actual forced degree fibers, the leading cup, ordinary cap and marked cap phases are respectively

pi i(x_1-1),  pi i x_1,  pi i x_2.                 (9)

The marked restriction x_1+x_2=1 follows only after all other actual caps and degree-preserving coupons act; it is not a local replacement of an unrestricted mark in a degree-changing source.

Apply (7) with B=-1 at a cup and B=+1 at a cap. The two momenta are (-t,t) for the cup and ordinary cap, and (t,-t) for the marked cap. All three therefore give

X_1 X_2=1,  S_1/S_2=-1.                            (10)

For an actual physical head use

K=-1/X, F=S(1-X), E=(X^(-1)-1)/S,
Lminus=[[1,0],[-KF,K]],
G=[[K,E],[FK,FE+K^(-1)]].                         (11)

It has determinant one and trace -2. Its exact -1 eigenline is spanned by (1,S). Since K+1 is a unit, this kernel line is unique over the Artin ring; no nilpotent invariant-flag freedom is silently added.

Equation (10) is equivalent to the actual based pair product P=I. For the converse, K_total=1 gives X_2=X_1^(-1), while

F_total=(1-X_1)(S_1+S_2)=0

forces S_1=-S_2 because 1-X_1 is a unit. These are identities over the test ring. The ordinary and marked phases differ on the degree fiber by pi i-2pi i x_1; this retained integral character has the same exponentiated relation (10), but must not be deleted from the action.

4. A regular parabolic inverse for each actual coupon

The local critical map reconstructs actual ordered pair matrices from (X,S), preserves their ENTIRE product P, and fixes

Tr P=J+J^(-1).

Conversely, given regular incoming and outgoing heads with that product, their ratios S_in,1/S_in,2 and S_out,2/S_out,1 determine their internal q-Hahn roots by the rational inverse (5) of the local map. The Jacobi equation is the actual trace equation, so these roots satisfy the two internal critical equations. Total-F preservation gives the exact D and therefore (8); the regular high adapter gives the same conclusion in the other physical chamber. The chosen simple roots eliminate any additional local root choice in this formal neighborhood.

At J=-1 one MUST NOT divide by J-J^(-1) in an Artin ring. Here is an explicit replacement for the local conjugator formula. Put

Q=P-J^(-1)I,  A=A_in, A'=A_out,
c=Tr([Q,A](A'-A)) / Tr([Q,A](Q A-A' Q)),
C=I+cQ.                                            (12)

At the complete point Q=H=P+I and A’=A+b[H,A]-4b^2H. The denominator in (12) is

Tr([H,A]([H,A]-4bH))=32,                          (13)

using Tr([H,A]^2)=32 and Tr([H,A]H)=0. It is a unit throughout the formal neighborhood. The original generic conjugator identity now extends in this regular expression and gives

C A C^(-1)=A', [C,P]=0,
det C=R0=1+c(J-J^(-1)).                            (14)

For precision, these rational identities hold on the smooth localized internal-root chart before imposing the global critical equations; their specialization therefore holds on nonreduced Artin test rings as well. This is not cancellation by a nonunit in such a test ring.

Specify the selected auxiliary eigenvalue m and let h_J=J^(-1)-1, which is a unit near J=-1. The actual physical auxiliary meridian is

C_aux=m R0 h_J^(-epsilon) C^(-1)(P^(-1)-I)^epsilon.  (15)

It has selected J-flag eigenvalue exactly m and

det C_aux=m^2 R0(-J)^epsilon=1                     (16)

by (2). All inverses in (12),(15) are units. At completeness (15) is I+(epsilon/2-b)(P+I), exactly the original primitive collar matrix. Its inverse followed by the retained Hurwitz half twist induces C on the main pair. Thus the auxiliary is the literal disk-framed meridian, not a centralizer chosen only from its eigenvalue ratio.

Conversely an actual auxiliary meridian with that eigenvalue commutes with P. Its inverse, composed with the retained half twist, transports the pair. Normalize this projective conjugator to have eigenvalue one on the J^(-1) flag. This divides by a unit even at the endpoint, because the commutant of the noncentral P is the free span of I,P. The normalized matrix is (12), and its eigenratio gives (2). The normalization and the conjugator are unique: at the complete pair a matrix commuting with P=-I+H has form aI+bH; commuting also with A forces b=0 since [H,A] has a unit entry. The same unit linear minor proves this assertion in the whole formal neighborhood.

5. Assemble the actual marked representation, and invert it

From a critical point, section 2 supplies shared physical heads (11). At every horizontal slice prefix-transport them by their actual lower triangular matrices. The local coupon preserves P, and each cup/cap has P=I, so prefixes outside the event remain unchanged. Physical wires therefore carry the same based meridian at both ends. At each circle, (15) satisfies exactly the original threading relation [2,1,1,2] and the retained main Hurwitz move. These are the literal van Kampen relations of the drilled box; inverse-pair caps close the outside plat. They construct a representation of the actual marked augmented link.

For the reverse construction start with that representation and its recorded Morse basepaths. If H_1,…,H_m are the raw trace -2 main meridians at a slice, let P_i=H_i … H_1, P_0=I and use

Bminus(P)=[[1,0],[-P_21,P_11]],
G_i=Bminus(P_(i-1)) H_i Bminus(P_(i-1))^(-1),
K_i=(P_i)_11/(P_(i-1))_11,
X_i=-1/K_i, S_i=(G_i)_21/(K_i+1).                  (17)

All denominators are units by the same regular chart. This is the explicit inverse of the actual prefix factorization. It remains valid over the Artin ring. The selected channel eigenvalue fixes the actual trace equation and its unique regular eigenline; the rational inverse from section 4 recovers the internal roots. The local maps then recover (8),(10), and section 2 gives magnetic criticality. No root is chosen by an order, norm, or independent semiclassical convention.

Both constructions are inverse before quotienting, except for the one common scale in (6). Multiplying all momenta by t conjugates every matrix by diag(1,t) projectively: upper entries divide by t and lower entries multiply by t. The prefix construction respects that same conjugation. Setting one wire momentum to 1 removes exactly this freedom and proves (3) over all local Artin test rings.

6. Consequences and limits of the statement

On the complete fiber m=1, (2) fixes J=-1 locally, scheme-theoretically. Indeed R_epsilon-1 vanishes identically on J=-1 and its J derivative there is 2b-epsilon=2/tau_geom, a unit at the actual complete cusp. The formal implicit-function theorem therefore gives J+1=0. This uses the primitive cusp normalization of the channel-Hessian theorem, not a semisimple endpoint quotient.

At an irreducible geometric representation, changing the common projective frame supplies the PGL2 orbit; the diagonal scale above has already been quotiented. With the convention rho_g=g rho g^(-1), that scale acts on g by LEFT multiplication. Its two-dimensional local T_diag framing directions therefore remain in the critical scheme. Equation (3) identifies all its additional infinitesimal directions with actual marked representation deformations. This source equivalence alone does not exclude those additional deformations. The following corollary uses a separately checked geometric input.

7. With an actual positive certificate, the magnetic radical is exactly framing

Assume additionally an ACTUAL marked positive complete ideal gluing certificate for this augmented exterior. The multi-cusp marked rigidity theorem identifies its decorated representation germ modulo PGL2 with a smooth germ whose coordinates are the primitive peripheral eigenvalue logs. It proves this identification on Artin rings, with the exact main eigenvalue conditions used above. Fix all main eigenvalues to +1 and fix the auxiliary eigenvalues m. The unframed germ is then a point. The complete geometric representation is irreducible, so its framed germ is a smooth PGL2 orbit. Equation (3) and its explicit one-scale slice therefore prove

Crit at fixed m is smooth of dimension two.          (18)

This assertion holds formally over nearby auxiliary eigenvalue parameters as well. The dimension comes from the actual T_diag framing quotient, not from deleting two presumed redundant equations or counting variables and equations.

At completeness write F for the root-reduced phase after its affine degree restriction, in free magnetic coordinates y and channels xi. The scheme Crit is the zero scheme of its EXACTLY marked derivatives. Consequently its Zariski tangent space is the kernel of Hess F, even before any reducedness assertion. Equation (18) now proves that this kernel is precisely the two-dimensional framing tangent space. The independently proved channel calculation gives

Hess F = [[F_yy,0],[0,diag(-4pi i/tau_geom,i)]].     (19)

The channel diagonal is invertible. Hence the two framing directions are purely magnetic, and

ker F_yy = tangent(T_diag\PGL2), dim ker F_yy=2.    (20)

There are no further magnetic infinitesimal null directions on this regular positive geometric germ. A local slice transverse to these actual frame directions has a nondegenerate holomorphic quadratic form. This supplies a classical normal form; it is not a computation of an actual source density, a finite quantum gauge factor, or a Gaussian coefficient on the physical summation cycle.

A nearby classical stationary branch and its absolute action

There is now also a holomorphic CLASSICAL stationary branch. Choose a local slice transverse to the two framing directions and keep the geometric sheet’s integer Poisson characters fixed. The marked geometric implicit-function theorem solves the representation uniquely on that slice for m_i=exp(-pi i chi_i) near 1. The inverse in (17), the regular internal-root maps, and the fixed local logarithms reconstruct all the actual principal-phase variables holomorphically. Equation (3) proves that these variables satisfy every exactly marked stationary equation. This proves existence; it is no longer an assumption about an unspecified classical critical branch.

Its critical value phi(chi) is independent of the transverse framing slice. Along the connected local framing orbit all internal derivatives vanish, so the value of the same marked phase is constant. This statement does not reset any additive period or change an integer mark.

The auxiliary-color normalization can be checked directly in the exact entire core. Its full-algebra identity centers (-1)^h T_h into cos[pi(N-2j-1)chi]. On the positive cosine branch the order-N coupling is EXACTLY pi i(1-2xi)chi. The finite -1 contributes exp(-pi i chi) to the amplitude, not a new term in the principal phase. The actual main cups/caps, kappa^epsilon factors and original exterior line have no auxiliary chi dependence in this packet. Their known leading constant phases are retained. In particular there is no additional order-N exterior color character to discard or calibrate by the volume.

Envelope differentiation and the proved literal peripheral marking give

partial_i phi=pi i(1-2xi_i),
U_i=-2pi i chi_i, V_i=4pi i(xi_i-1/2),
d(2pi i phi)=(1/2) sum_i V_i dU_i.                 (21)

Here U,V are the centered projective meridian and preferred-longitude logs. The negative cosine sign requires the simultaneous reciprocal eigenflag convention stated in the local character note; it gives the same primitive differential with those consistently marked logs. An independently changed exterior color character would change the corresponding linear action/log mark and is not covered by silently reusing (21).

The absolute endpoint regulator comparison now applies to the complete point of this FULL stationary branch and fixes its value:

2pi Re phi(0)=Vol(Y_D).                             (22)

Finally (21) and dV_i=tau_geom,i dU_i at completeness, or equivalently the channel elimination calculation, give

phi_(chi_i,chi_j)(0)=delta_ij pi i tau_geom,i.       (23)

Thus the general transfer’s parameter is tau_packet=tau_geom/2. These are statements about the actual marked PRINCIPAL action on its geometric sheet. They do not prove continuation from the locally lattice-selected q-Hahn roots, a nonzero finite-source cycle coefficient, or a uniform quantum packet with this action. The branch is called geometric here, not source-selected.

8. Scope

This is a formal classical equivalence. It neither quotients the finite quantum coupon algebra nor replaces its co-source. It does not locate the point in a physical support chamber, continue the source-selected local branches to it, choose its Poisson coefficient, compute a nonzero amplitude, or bound any competing source. The original marked action, peripheral factors and all finite-normal data remain unchanged.
