Exact cyclic variation and peeling the original Kashaev cut

Research note, 28 October 2026. This is an exact support theorem for the original enhanced crossing in the independently derived crossing convention. It uses neither a Fourier gauge nor an asymptotic estimate. It applies to every braid position word and every assignment of crossing signs.

1. The local cyclic interval

Write Q=exp(2pii/N), q=exp(pi*i/N), and [x] for the representative in {0,…,N-1}. For a checked crossing with input (a,b) and output (c,d), put

u=[b-a-1], v=[a-c], t=[c-d], h=[d-b].

For either crossing sign the support condition is exactly

u+v+t+h=N-1,          [a-b]=v+t+h.                  (1)

Thus, after lifting b to an integer, the output is in the cyclic interval

b <= d_lift <= c_lift <= a_lift=b+[a-b].           (2)

This follows directly from the original four-index weight and the actual inverse, including its Q^(-1) normalization. Negative crossings permute the four factorial indices, so they have the same support.

Given colors x=(x_1,…,x_b) and fixed sentinels L,R, define the integer cyclic variation

V_(L,R)(x)=[L-x_1]+sum_(i=1)^(b-1)[x_i-x_(i+1)]
                                       +[x_b-R]. (3)

Every supported adjacent crossing makes V nonincreasing. Indeed, with neighboring colors p,s, its three affected summands satisfy

[p-c] <= [p-a]+v,       [d-s] <= h+[b-s],
[c-d]=t,               v+t+h=[a-b].               (4)

The inequalities are the elementary triangle inequality for nonnegative cyclic distance. Their defects are nonnegative multiples of N.

2. The actual source forces a face at every cut crossing

The original enhancement is mu=-q S, where S e_j=e_(j+1 mod N). Fix the first cut color to zero. Each summation path in the actual weighted partial trace runs from

x_in =(0,[A_2+1],...,[A_b+1])
x_out=(0,A_2,...,A_b).                             (5)

This is a statement about an individual nonzero path, before summing coefficients. Choose L=0 and R=[A_b+1]. The interior tail distances in (3) agree at the two endpoints. Direct subtraction gives

V(x_in)-V(x_out) = 0   if A_2=0,
                      -N  otherwise.             (6)

Monotonicity therefore forces A_2=0, and V must be constant throughout the path. Both inequalities in (4) are consequently equalities at every crossing.

At any crossing in position 1, its left neighbor is the sentinel 0, so

[0-c]=[0-a]+v.                                    (7)

Only these crossings change x_1. Telescoping (7) from x_1=0 back to x_1=0 shows that the sum of their nonnegative v’s is zero. Therefore

x_1=0 at EVERY stage; every position-1 crossing has v=0. (8)

In particular, no original source path can lie in a chamber with all four crossing angles positive at every vertex. Formal strict concavity on such a chamber is not an assertion that the original source occupies that chamber.

Before the first position-1 crossing, only position-2 crossings can change x_2. Their left equality in (4) makes [0-x_2] nondecreasing. It starts at N-1, because A_2=0. Hence x_2 stays 1 until the first position-1 crossing. After the last position-1 crossing the same quantity ends at zero, so x_2 is already zero and remains zero. Consequently:

first position-1 crossing: u=v=0;
last  position-1 crossing: v=t=0.                 (9)

If there is only one such crossing, both statements hold. If there are none, the original source has no supported paths. These statements remain valid for links; no scalar-endomorphism assertion is required.

The original absolute crossing weight is N/|F_u F_v F_t F_h|, with F_j=product_(r=1)j(1-Qr). The reflection |F_j F_(N-1-j)|=N shows that BOTH end crossings in (9) have absolute weight exactly one. Every other cut crossing is on the tetrahedral three-corner face v=0. This is an actual source-support reduction.

3. Exact q-Pascal boundary operators

Set

T^sigma_(k,j)=(B_sigma)_(0,k ; 0,j).

All indices below lie in {0,…,N-1}. Directly from the original crossing formula,

T+_(0,j)=Q^(1-j),        T+_(k,0)=0 for k>0,

T+_(k,j)=(-1)^(j-1) Q^[k+(j-1)(j-2)/2]
                     {k-1 choose j-1}_Q,
                     1<=j<=k<=N-1,               (10)

and all remaining entries vanish. The actual inverse crossing gives

T-_(k,j)=Q^(k-j) conjugate(T+_(k,j)).              (11)

Thus T-_(0,j)=Q^(-1), and, for 1<=j<=k<=N-1,

T-_(k,j)=(-1)^(j-1) Q^[-j-(j-1)(j-2)/2]
                     {k-1 choose j-1}_(Q^-1).    (12)

For example, (10) follows by replacing N/F_(N-k) with bar_F_(k-1) and bar_F_r with (-1)^r Q1F_r. The wrap row k=0 is kept; it is not an optional endpoint correction.

In fact T-=(T+)^(-1). To prove this without compressing an inverse by fiat, let P be the positive-index block of T+, indexed r,i=0,…,N-2:

P_(r,i)=Q^(r+1)(-1)^i Q^[i(i-1)/2]{r choose i}_Q.

The finite q-binomial theorem gives the inverse q-Pascal matrix

({r choose i}_Q)^(-1)
   =(-1)^(r-i)Q^[(r-i)(r-i-1)/2]{r choose i}_Q.

The resulting inverse of P is exactly (12). For the wrap row, the finite q-hockey-stick identity and the root specialization imply

sum_(r=i)^(N-2) P_(r,i)=-Q^(-i).

This gives the constant wrap row Q^(-1) in the inverse of T+. The formula is valid at every N>=2, without a prime-level assumption.

4. The full original scalar after peeling

Let W be the braid word in its actual operator order, let w be its writhe, and let alpha=-q. Construct a word W_peel on b-1 heads by replacing every position-1 crossing by T+ or T-, and every crossing in position i>=2 by the same original B+ or B- in position i-1. No sign, ordering, Cartan scalar, or framing factor changes.

For b>=2 the exact fixed-cut scalar is

K_N(W)=(-q) alpha^(-w)
  [ Tr_(heads 3,...,b)
      ( W_peel (I tensor mu^(tensor(b-2))) ) ]_(0,1). (13)

The trace in (13) is over the remaining heads except its first one. For b=2 this is just (-q)alpha^(-w)(W_peel)_(0,1). The outside factor -q is the retained pivotal coefficient on the removed ordinary trace of original head 2. The original writhe w is unchanged, including crossings replaced by T.

Proof: expand the original weighted partial trace into paths. Section 2 forces every first-head label to zero and A_2=0. Restrict each crossing coefficient accordingly and perform the remaining sums. This gives (13) term by term, before using cancellation. It is stronger than a formal compression identity for arbitrary sources.

The first-strand shell is therefore removed uniformly for every original braid. This differs from the classical arbitrary-bridge deformation reduction: (13) is an identity of the actual finite quantum scalar. Iterating it without preserving the new T boundary operators is not justified. The remaining T/B word is complex, and this theorem supplies no geometric saddle coefficient or volume lower bound.

5. Reproducible checks

experiments/audit_original_source_cyclic_variation_peeling.py checks the original matrices and their actual inverses over cyclotomic prime fields, both q-Pascal formulas and their product, every supported local cyclic-variation inequality at small levels, full original signed-word scalars against (13), and the forced faces path by path. The same-position trefoil and figure-eight words have identical support and absolute local weights, while their actual selected scalars differ. The source reduction therefore does not remove the need to control the original phases.
