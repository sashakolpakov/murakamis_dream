Actual marked-theta link, four octahedra and its source angle slice

29 October 2026. The marked two-vertex source is literally a three-box fully augmented pretzel diagram, with its crossing circles retained. This gives the missing source-to-topology dictionary. Its complement has volume 4v8 for ANY fixed signed twists on the three physical boxes. The independently proved whole-source limit is therefore its actual hyperbolic volume. An explicit sixteen-tetrahedron angle assignment also realizes the full untwisted scalar rate, not just its value at the maximum. This is a local three-circle result, not a general graph theorem.

1. Literal original source, before any channel decomposition

Let C be the actual auxiliary [2,1,1,2] three-braid, closed on its third head with the ORIGINAL pivot. Its complete-color value is L_N by the all-input circle theorem. It is an actual additional link component, with no division by its zero ordinary quantum dimension.

On six ordered physical heads place R^(e_1)L_N on heads12, R^(e_2)L_N on heads34, and R^(e_3)L_N on heads56. Use the matching

(16),(23),(45)

at the bottom and the top. At the bottom use the ORIGINAL cups; at the top use ordinary caps on23 and45, and the sole original unrestricted marked row on16. Explicitly they are

p_ab=N x^(-b)delta_(a+b,d),
ell_ab=x^a delta_(a+b,d)/N, m_ab=x^b/N^2.

This is EXACTLY the original s=3 pretzel source

A_(3,N)(R^(e_1)L_N,R^(e_2)L_N,R^(e_3)L_N)        (1)

in the literal source and transfer theorem. The black matching is precisely p_16 p_23 p_45, and its white caps and marked arc are precisely those used in the all-channel marked contraction. Thus its raw scalar is the WHOLE channel sum Theta_N(e) in the whole-label theorem. There is no additional trace average, magnetic projection or state replacement in (1).

Keeping the original marked row first is essential: the total physical degree later forces its missing degree equation. The three cup factors N^3, two ordinary cap factors N^-2 and marked factor N^-2 leave exactly the original one-cut factor N^-1. That factor is already inside (1).

The auxiliary circles remain ordinary closed components. When e_i is even, its two physical strands do not swap endpoints; when it is odd, they do. Following the displayed matchings determines all main components. At e=(0,0,0) there are three main components and three crossing circles, hence six components. Other parity choices change the main component count, not the retained three circles.

2. Reversible actual face cut and the hyperbolic complement

The explicit projection-plane and crossing-disk cut of the augmented pretzel geometry applies to (1), including its untwisted diagram. At s=3 each of the two cut projection cells P_3 has ideal vertices Z_k,U_k+,U_k-, k mod3, and faces

W_+=(U_2^+,U_1^+,U_0^+), W_-=(U_0^-,U_1^-,U_2^-),
Q_k=(Z_k,U_k^+,Z_(k+1),U_k^-),
T_k^+=(Z_k,U_(k-1)^+,U_k^+),
T_k^-=(Z_k,U_k^-,U_(k-1)^-).                    (2)

Here the k-th retained crossing disk is the bowtie T_k+,T_k-, with common crossing-circle vertex Z_k. The planar arc between two disks is Q_k. The two other projection regions are W_+,W_-. These are the faces traced from the actual ordered pretzel matching; restoring the disk collars reverses the cut.

The two projection copies p=0,1 have opposite orientations. White faces W_+,W_-,Q_k glue across p by the identity label map. For e_k even, the shaded gluing is WITHIN each projection copy,

T_k^+ -> T_k^-, Z_k -> Z_k,
                U_(k-1)^+ -> U_(k-1)^-, U_k^+ -> U_k^-.

For e_k odd it is ACROSS the projection copies, with the two outer indices exchanged:

T_k^+(p) -> T_k^-(1-p), Z_k -> Z_k,
                U_(k-1)^+ -> U_k^-, U_k^+ -> U_(k-1)^-. (3)

The cross-copy change is part of the oriented half-twist gluing. A swap within the same oriented copy would be a different map.

Each P_3 is the double of the directly constructed right-angled ideal triangular antiprism across its triangular base. That antiprism is a regular ideal octahedron. Thus (2),(3) use FOUR actual regular ideal octahedra. The supporting planes and Lorentz-normal proof are given in the cited geometry construction; no polyhedron recognition is being inferred from the quantum growth constant.

Every edge cycle in (2),(3) has four right angles. The regular octahedral symmetries and the displayed reflections preserve the canonical horospheres. Consequently there is no edge dilation, and the ideal links are connected nonsingular flat tori. Shrinking their horospheres gives full complete finite-volume cusp ends and a compact core. This constructs the complete hyperbolic metric on the literal complement, with

Vol(S^3 minus L(e))=4v8.                         (4)

All choices of the three parities have the same metric volume. A further full twist is the actual crossing-disk collar twist. With the crossing circle RETAINED, it changes the boundary marking by an integral disk-longitude shear. After the disk boundary is treated as a cusp, the induced pure three-punctured-sphere map is isotopic to the identity: one can undo the collar rotation allowing that cusp boundary to rotate. Thus the unmarked complementary gluing remains the one in (3). This is an unmarked-complement homeomorphism, not invariance of the quantum source or its preferred filling slope. Positive versus negative odd crossings differ by just such full twists. Hence (4) holds for all fixed signed integer triples e.

3. An actual positive ideal angle slice for the scalar rate

Here take the UNTWiSTED gluing, e=(0,0,0). Split each P_3 across its internal triangle (Z_0,Z_1,Z_2). The four octahedra are O_(p,s), p,s in {0,1}; s records the plus/minus half. After forgetting that sign in the abstract labels, each octahedron has vertices Z_0,Z_1,Z_2,U_0,U_1,U_2 and faces

Z=(Z_0,Z_1,Z_2), U=(U_0,U_1,U_2),
q_k=(Z_k,U_k,Z_(k+1)), t_k=(Z_k,U_(k-1),U_k).

All maps fix the abstract vertex indices. Faces Z,t_k flip s; faces U,q_k flip p. Every octahedral boundary-edge cycle therefore visits all four (p,s) cells with the same abstract edge label. There are twelve such cycles. Ideal vertex classes have the six labels Z_0,Z_1,Z_2,U_0,U_1,U_2, the actual six cusps.

In EVERY octahedron choose the opposite-vertex axis

A=Z_0, B=U_1,
(v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3)=(Z_1,Z_2,U_2,U_0).

Triangulate it by (A,B,v_i,v_(i+1)), i mod4, with the appropriate positive orientation. This is a genuine triangulation of the regular octahedron; all boundary faces are its existing triangles. The resulting triangulation of the actual link has sixteen positive ideal tetrahedra and four distinct internal axis edges.

For ANY 0<x_i<1/2 assign to the i-th tetrahedron in cell (p,s)

alpha_(p,s,i)=pi x_(i+2p+s mod4),
beta_(p,s,i)=pi/2-alpha_(p,s,i).

Put dihedral angle pi/2 on AB and its opposite v_i v_(i+1), alpha on A v_i and B v_(i+1), and beta on the two other opposite edges. Each tetrahedron is a positive ideal angle triple. Internal axis edges have four right angles. The four equatorial boundary edges also contribute one right angle per cell, hence 2pi per cycle. A radial boundary edge at v_i has, in one octahedron, angle

pi/2+alpha_i-alpha_(i-1) at A,
pi/2-alpha_i+alpha_(i-1) at B.

For each column i the four cells use each of x_0,…,x_3 exactly once. Summing either radial expression over its actual four-cell edge cycle is EXACTLY 2pi. This proves all edge angle equations symbolically on the entire open four-cube. It is not a test only at the regular point.

Each of the four right-tetrahedron angle triples (pi x_i,pi/2-pi x_i,pi/2) occurs four times. Thus the actual angle volume on this slice is

V_angle(x)=4 sum_(i=1)^4
                 [Lambda(pi x_i)+Lambda(pi/2-pi x_i)]. (5)

Let a_i be the continuum limits of j_i/N and S=sum a_i. On the supported folded source set S>=1, a_i<=1/2, put

x_i=S/2-a_i, i=1,2,3, x_4=1-S/2.

These lie in the closed half-angle cube and sum to one. Interior labels give its positive slice; physical boundary labels are limits. The exact finite half-steps and prefactors stay in the whole-label source proof, rather than being called continuum shape equations. Its fully magnetic-contracted factorial rate is

E=sum_i[L(2x_i)-4L(x_i)]
 =-2 sum_i[L(x_i)+L(1/2-x_i)],
L(t)=integral_0^t log(2sin pi u)du.

Since L(t)=-Lambda(pi t)/pi, (5) gives the PRECISE calibration

V_angle(x)=2pi E(x).                            (6)

Hence the source rate is the volume functional of an explicit positive angle slice in the ACTUAL triangulation. Each summand in (5) is strictly concave in x_i, with its unique maximum at 1/4. At this point every tetrahedron has shape i and the entire face and cusp certificate of section2 applies. It is the actual complete metric, not a merely lower-dimensional critical point of the source. In particular its full fixed-periphery criticality follows from the complete gluing and the already proved full-slice variational certificate. This distinguishes the actual geometric anchor from stationarity only along the displayed source family.

Off-center angles in this slice are NOT asserted to solve the real modulus gluing equations or to be actual link representations. For example the complex shape product around one internal axis is, up to the chosen reciprocal convention, product_i tan(pi x_i). The equation sum x_i=1 does not force that product to one. This is a precise additional geometric equation, not a missing angle sum. At the maximum it is one. No peripheral meridian meaning is assigned to the INTERNAL labels x_i.

4. Original external line and the actual volume equality

The complete augmented-source normalization applies to this multicomponent diagram. It proves scalarity on the chosen original cut without using link connectivity. At complete color the physical twist is theta_d=kappa^(-1), hence all prescribed framing/peripheral characters have modulus one. Thus for the original one-cut enhanced Kashaev evaluation

|<L(e)>_N|=|Theta_N(e)|.                         (7)

For the full one-cut modified convention the additional entire dimension specializes to i^(N+3)/N, so its absolute value is |Theta_N(e)|/N. There is one such factor, not one per component. All auxiliary circle traces and all Cartan phases were already inside (1). No division by [N]=0 is introduced in either statement.

The whole-label theorem proves for every FIXED signed e and both level parities, including composites,

Theta_N(e)=N^(3/2)e^(2Nj0) Omega_N^(sum e)
              /[16 product_i sqrt(2-i e_i)] (1+O_e(1/N)),
j0=v8/pi.

Each square root has positive real part, so its leading coefficient is nonzero. Combining this actual source result with (4),(7) proves

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N)log|<L(e)>_N|
              =4v8=Vol(S^3 minus L(e)).          (8)

The modified one-cut version has the same rate. The half-twist and full-twist source phases have been retained throughout; their geometric volume invariance has not been used to delete crossings from the quantum tensor diagram.

5. Reusable geometry and the remaining graph gate

If only the white faces of the two P_3 projection cells are glued, the remaining shaded faces form six totally geodesic pairs of ideal triangles, one pair for each T_k^+ or T_k^-. Each is a genuine three-punctured sphere: its three sides are glued by the original white identity maps, and its two corners at each ideal puncture join to a cusp. Orthogonality of white and shaded faces makes the seam across every boundary edge smooth and totally geodesic. Thus this is a concrete four-octahedron geometric block with six geodesic pants boundary components. Matching boundary pants by any orientation-compatible puncture permutation preserves their canonical equal horocycles and gives a reusable complete geometric gluing operation. The theta link is obtained by the three recorded pairings of those pants.

This does NOT yet identify a quantum six-port block for arbitrary graph sewing. Formula (1) closes all three circles and the entire magnetic tensor with a particular white/marked partner. A larger graph does not in general contain that closed local contraction. Its open kernel must retain the three paired normals, ordered boundary sources and the unique physical mark. One cannot replace that kernel by the positive scalar in (1), or apply the Latin angle assignment across a different graph without its actual edge table. The four-octahedron block and (6) are explicit local geometric data; the open-source identification is the remaining substantive gate.
