Coverage check for the belt-stack filling route

29 October 2026. This is a check of stated scope in primary sources, not an audit or use of their proofs. No novelty claim follows from this limited search.

  • Roland van der Veen, Proof of the volume conjecture for Whitehead chains, states a volume-conjecture result for an infinite link family containing the Whitehead link and Borromean rings. The complete belt stacks may be covered by this family. An exact tangle/peripheral dictionary remains necessary before claiming either inclusion or a new topological class.
  • Dionne Ibarra, Emma N. McQuire and Jessica S. Purcell, Augmented links, shadow links, and the TV volume conjecture: a geometric perspective, describe an isometry between octahedral fully augmented link exteriors and fundamental shadow-link exteriors. Their abstract states a Turaev–Viro volume result for the case without half-twists. An isometry of exteriors does not itself identify our marked original quantum source, its root convention or its filling kernel.
  • Ce Shen, The Chen–Yang volume conjecture for long integral fillings of fundamental shadow links, states a theorem for sufficiently long integral fillings of any fixed marked fundamental shadow-link exterior, allowing mixed signs and unrelated magnitudes. Its stated invariant is SO(3) Turaev–Viro on odd levels, with a further signed WRT expansion. This directly overlaps the geometric long-filling theme; it does not, by its statement alone, establish our original marked Kashaev source on the full sequence of levels.

The present source calculation independently retains the actual ordinary auxiliary-color operators, the full Hermite normal trace, and the original marked closure. Its squared Fourier kernel and global physical-color estimate are proved in the outer-color theorem. That theorem does not claim the deformed source/regulator comparison from a literature theorem, a shared cusp shape or matching volumes. The actual marked comparison is proved separately in the typed global lift, and is included in the full equality assembly.
