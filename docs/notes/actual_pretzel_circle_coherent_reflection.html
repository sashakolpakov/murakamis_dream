Actual pretzel circle sewing: a coherent positive factorization and a half-twist test

29 October 2026. For the specified arbitrary-width pretzel port graph, the complete circle has an exact source-preserving Kraus/Cauchy factorization. The half twists remain diagonal unitary channel phases. The original marked cap produces TWO explicit boundary states; local positivity alone does not identify them. A level-three exact calculation rules out a literal positive norm-square identity for the half-twisted base, even after multiplying by any unit root framing phase. A separate untwisted calculation shows that the two source states remain different in the complete positive-circle quotient itself. This excludes their automatic identification by a choice of positive factor or consistent unitary duality gauges. It does not rule out another global reflection pairing for the genuinely untwisted base or a sharp positive majorant.

1. Specified original port graph

Let s>=3 be odd, with boxes on adjacent head pairs (1,2),(3,4),…,(2s-1,2s). The bottom matching and top matching are

S={(1,2s),(2,3),(4,5),...,(2s-2,2s-1)}.

The outer top pair (1,2s) has the original marked row m; all other top pairs have the original ordinary cap ell. Put d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d, and retain the original common Cartan kappa=q(d2/2). Use the original physical pair operators R and L=L_N. Each box considered here is

Bi=ReiL,ei∈ℤ. B_i=R^{e_i}L,\qquad e_i\in\mathbb Z. \tag{1}

The literal all-width port/source calculation fixes this graph and its signs. Its augmented odd-pretzel base has e_i=-1. The genuinely untwisted circle graph has e_i=0. These are different graphs; removing the half twists is not a scalar framing operation.

Let p_S be the tensor product of the genuine cups p_ab=Nx^(-b) delta_(a+b,d), placed on S in its displayed order. Let P_o swap each ordinary-cap pair (2,3),(4,5),… and leave the marked outer pair alone. Let P_b swap every box pair. Both are literal permutations of tensor legs, not added physical crossings.

All boxes preserve total INTEGER physical degree. Thus the total incoming degree sd and the ordinary caps force the final marked pair to degree d. Only on this assembled source the top row is

ΛS=N−(2s+1)pS†Po. \Lambda_S=N^{-(2s+1)}p_S^\dagger P_o. \tag{2}

Indeed ell=p^dagger P/N^2 and m_d=pdagger/N3 on their respective pairs. The unrestricted marked row has not been replaced locally on an arbitrary exterior.

Consequently the full raw source scalar, before its existing external framing/peripheral factor chi_N, is

ZN=N−(2s+1)pS†Po(B1⊗⋯⊗Bs)pS. Z_N=N^{-(2s+1)}p_S^\dagger P_o (B_1\otimes\cdots\otimes B_s)p_S. \tag{3}

This graph identity is the source input; no geometric triangulation or hyperbolic volume formula is assumed.

2. Full physical coherent channel factorization

Use the canonical full physical positive resolution of the original complete circle. For every physical degree T and admitted distinct Casimir node j, let v_(T,j) have first component one, embedded in its actual degree block, and let h_(T,j)>0 be its exact positive CD coefficient. Form a rectangular matrix F with columns sqrt(h_(T,j)) v_(T,j). Then

FF†=H=LP,RF=Fdiag⁡(κνj),νj=(−1)jqj(j+1). FF^\dagger=H=LP,\qquad R F=F\,\operatorname{diag} (\kappa\nu_j),\quad \nu_j=(-1)^j q^{j(j+1)}. \tag{4}

Every high physical block is retained by the explicit low-block mirror; both even paired nodes and the odd simple node are included. This is not a value-only replacement of a root Jordan block. The original circle has already selected its entire image, with its actual normal source, before (4) is used.

Since |kappa nu_j|=1, define the diagonal unitary U_i=diag((kappa nu_j)^(e_i)). Equations (1) and (4) prove the ALL-INPUT factorization

Bi=FUiF†P. \boxed{B_i=F U_i F^\dagger P.} \tag{5}

This retains every half twist and its full Cartan phase. It does not use the false assertion that the original R is an H-isometry; the isometry on H would instead involve the correctly leg-exchanged R.

Put F_s=F^(tensor s), U=U_1 tensor…tensor U_s, and define the two source packets

u=Fs†PbpS,v=Fs†PopS. u=F_s^\dagger P_b p_S,\qquad v=F_s^\dagger P_o p_S.

Inserting (5) in (3) gives the exact coherent formula and its positive Cauchy estimate:

ZN=N−(2s+1)v†Uu,|χNZN|2≤|χN|2N−(4s+2)∥v∥2∥u∥2. \boxed{Z_N=N^{-(2s+1)}v^\dagger Uu,\qquad |\chi_N Z_N|^2\leq |\chi_N|^2N^{-(4s+2)} \|v\|^2\|u\|^2.} \tag{6}

The two energies are explicit positive original-source contractions:

∥u∥2=pS†C⊗spS,∥v∥2=pS†PoH⊗sPopS. \|u\|^2=p_S^\dagger C^{\otimes s}p_S,\qquad \|v\|^2=p_S^\dagger P_oH^{\otimes s}P_op_S. \tag{7}

All original integer labels, cap constraints and CD factors are kept before these contractions. The local once-power angle coefficient is the exact h_(T,j), not its independent maximum. Formula (6) works at arbitrary s and for arbitrary signed e_i; neither packet is defined by the unknown closed scalar.

For e_i=0, the middle U is the identity, but v and u are still different specified source packets. A global reflection theorem must supply an additional relation between them, or another controlled pairing. This does not follow from FF^dagger>=0 or from a consistent dual-object basis change. In particular (6) is a structural positive comparison, not a sharp geometric energy estimate.

For clarity, the equivalent literal transfer entries retain the phases as follows. If h is the conserved input-left minus output-left charge, I_h=[max(0,h),min(d,d+h)], T=d+a-a’, then

Mih[a,a′]=x−2a+h∑jadmittedatThT,j(κνj)eivT,j(a−h)vT,j(d−a′)¯. M_i^h[a,a']=x^{-2a+h} \sum_{j\ {\mathrm{admitted\ at}}\ T} h_{T,j}(\kappa\nu_j)^{e_i} v_{T,j}(a-h)\overline{v_{T,j}(d-a')}. \tag{8}

The total physical degree T varies with the two open transfer indices. It cannot be replaced by a common fixed degree before contraction.

3. Exact half-twisted source certificate at N=3

Take N=3, s=3 and every e_i=-1. Build the literal original factorial R, the actual coproduct Casimir Omega, and L=Omega^2-I. The complete source contraction (3), or equivalently the already specified transfer trace, gives exactly

Z3=−189+81i3=27(−7+3i3). \boxed{Z_3=-189+81i\sqrt3=27(-7+3i\sqrt3).} \tag{9}

This calculation includes kappa=q^2 and the original marked N factors. The exact transfer trace used to verify it is

Z3=x43∑h=−22Tr⁡{Dh(Mh)3},Dh=diag⁡a∈Ihx2a. Z_3=\frac{x^4}{3}\sum_{h=-2}^2 \operatorname{Tr}\{D_h(M^h)^3\},\qquad D_h=\operatorname{diag}_{a\in I_h}x^{2a}.

The five traces, in order h=-2,-1,0,1,2, are

270+243 i sqrt3,
27-351 i sqrt3,
2781/2+(189/2) i sqrt3,
-540+162 i sqrt3,
-999/2+(27/2) i sqrt3.

Moreover

Z3/Z3¯=(11−21i3)/38. Z_3/\overline{Z_3}=(11-21i\sqrt3)/38. \tag{10}

This cannot be a root of unity: its rational field trace is 11/19, whereas a root of unity is an algebraic integer and a rational trace of an algebraic integer must be an integer. Thus multiplying (9) by ANY unit root framing phase cannot make it real. In particular the original half-twisted scalar is not a positive norm square times the retained framing phase and a positive scalar normalization.

This is an obstruction only to that literal norm-square identity. The positive comparison (6) remains valid. The certificate is not an upper-volume counterexample and does not identify a hyperbolic knot or link from a numerical invariant. The graph without the half twists (all e_i=0) instead has Z_3=270, consistent with the still-open genuinely untwisted reflection question. No extrapolation from this calculation settles arbitrary untwisted fully augmented diagrams.

3a. The untwisted source mismatch survives the positive-circle quotient

There is a stronger, source-exact check than merely observing that the two permutations in Section 2 are different. Set N=3, s=3, and every e_i=0, with the original cups and the one marked cap as in Section 1. For ANY factor F satisfying FF^dagger=H, the actual packet Gram is

(u†uu†vv†uv†v)=310(181010119). \boxed{\quad \begin{pmatrix} u^\dagger u&u^\dagger v\\v^\dagger u&v^\dagger v \end{pmatrix} =3^{10}\begin{pmatrix}18&10\\10&119\end{pmatrix}. \quad} \tag{11}

This is an exact quadratic-field calculation directly in the original physical modules. One can verify it without choosing square roots or CD columns: put

pa,b,c=27x−(d−a+d−b+d−c),(i1,…,i6)=(a,b,d−b,c,d−c,d−a), p_{a,b,c}=27x^{-(d-a+d-b+d-c)},\qquad (i_1,\ldots,i_6)=(a,b,d-b,c,d-c,d-a),

for a,b,c in {0,1,2}. These are precisely the 27 nonzero entries of p_S. Permute them by P_b or P_o, and contract the two resulting vectors against H tensor H tensor H. The literal coproduct Casimir gives L=Omega^2-I and H=LP; their entries, and all the displayed source coefficients, lie in Q(sqrt(-3)). The three resulting finite contractions are respectively 18,10,119 times 3^10. The audit performs these operations in that exact number field, separately from the numerical CD tests.

In particular

∥u−v∥2=117310>0,det⁡Gram⁡(u,v)=2042320>0. \|u-v\|^2=117\,3^{10}>0,\qquad \det\operatorname{Gram}(u,v)=2042\,3^{20}>0. \tag{12}

The difference therefore survives the actual image quotient: it is not a radical vector discarded by the positive complete circle. The packets are not even collinear. The original closed value and the two proposed canonical packet norms are

Z3=3−7v†u=270,3−7∥u∥2=486,3−7∥v∥2=3213. Z_3=3^{-7}v^\dagger u=270,\qquad 3^{-7}\|u\|^2=486,\qquad 3^{-7}\|v\|^2=3213. \tag{13}

Every equality (11)–(13) is independent of the Kraus factor: its Gram depends only on H. Replacing a minimal F by another such factor applies a common isometry to its channel space and cannot change it. Consistent unitary physical duality changes, with the original sources transformed at their ports, likewise leave these closed contractions unchanged. Consequently removing the half-twist phases and transporting the true dual objects do NOT turn formula (6) into either canonical norm square.

This is deliberately a limited obstruction. It does not exclude a different projection-plane cut, a nontrivial global pairing with its own proved source normalization, or coherent elimination before taking a norm. The untwisted value in (13) is positive. What fails is the specific proposed inference that the original marked source identifies the two positive-circle boundary states after quotienting. In particular this calculation does not replace a genuine six-port junction by a two-port Frobenius multiplication.

4. Validation and remaining estimate

The audit constructs the full original generators, crossings, complete circle, actual marked/cup source and canonical CD columns. It compares (3) directly to (6), with all physical box ports still open before their literal source contraction. A separate exact quadratic-field calculation reconstructs (9) from the original matrices rather than from guessed channel phases. It also computes (11) exactly from H and the literal 27-term original cup state; positivity, noncollinearity, and the quotient-level mismatch are consequences of that exact Gram matrix.

The remaining sharp task is a bound for the ACTUAL constrained packets (7), or a stronger coherent pairing than their product. Independent local capacities do not establish this. Neither polynomial duality transport nor the complete-circle local PSD theorem supplies the missing global geometric incidence or selected-source energy estimate.
