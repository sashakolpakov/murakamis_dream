An exact oblique contour decomposition through the geometric point

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

For any fixed finite braid of a hyperbolic knot, the literal root state has an exact contour decomposition with a distinguished translated real sheet passing through the regular geometric point. Its local coefficient is +1+1. The construction retains an exact physical boundary band, every initial mixed rectangle current, every translated side current and all other horizontal sheets. No factorial divisor occurs in the constructed bulk prisms.

This supplies a whole-source domain and a relative-current decomposition, not a thimble coefficient or a scalar lower bound. The geometric centralizer remains a null direction. The retained boundary terms and other sheets have not been bounded by smaller actions. Their number or their geometric mass alone cannot provide that estimate.

The complete proof directs are A, the literal factorial amplitude, and L, the local geometric period for any braid. All new coordinate, cell, translation and orientation calculations are given below.

1. Primitive cut-and-transfer coordinates and a strict interior

Let the fixed braid have cc crossings, let m=c+1m=c+1, and put d=N−1,h=2πi/N,c0=2πi.(1) d=N-1,\qquad h=2\pi i/N,\qquad c_0=2\pi i. \qquad\text{(1)} Use as independent integer coordinates the cut occupation aa and every signed-crossing transfer n1,…,ncn_1,\ldots,n_c. At a positive crossing the two oriented strand visits change occupation by −ni,+ni-n_i,+n_i; at a negative crossing they change it by +ni,−ni+n_i,-n_i. Traverse the single oriented knot from the cut. Every wire occupation is thereby an integer linear form ℓe(k)=a+∑iϵeini,ϵei∈{−1,0,1}.(2) \ell_e(k)=a+\sum_i\epsilon_{ei}n_i, \qquad \epsilon_{ei}\in\{-1,0,1\}. \qquad\text{(2)} Each transfer is encountered with both signs before returning to the cut, so the final occupation is exactly aa. Conversely the cut and transfers of a matching state recover all its wires by this traversal. Thus these coordinates identify the whole matching lattice with ℤm\mathbb Z^m, with no index factor.

Equivalently, split each crossing into its two strand visits and join the donor to the receiver by a transfer edge. The wire edges form one directed knot cycle. That wire cycle with the cut edge removed is a spanning tree; the cut and cc transfer edges are its chords. The traversal above is the direct integer reconstruction on this expanded graph.

The actual support is exactly SN={k∈ℤm:0≤ℓe(k)≤d for every wire,ni≥0}.(3) S_N=\{k\in\mathbb Z^m:0\le \ell_e(k)\le d \text{ for every wire},\quad n_i\ge0\}. \qquad\text{(3)} The positive inequalities ni≤ainn_i\le a_{\mathrm{ in}} and ni≤d−binn_i\le d-b_{\mathrm{ in}}, and the analogous negative inequalities, are the nonnegativity and upper bounds of the output wires. In particular 0≤ni≤d0\le n_i\le d follows from (3). All five factorial arguments of each literal crossing in A are wire occupations or transfers. No support value is extended cyclically or assigned an artificial negative factorial.

Let ℒ\mathscr L be the finite list consisting of every wire form (2) and the coordinate forms nin_i. It has nonzero integer rows, and the support is equivalently 0≤ℓ(k)≤d0\le\ell(k)\le d for all ℓ∈ℒ\ell\in\mathscr L. This list may contain repetitions. Set L0=maxℓ∈ℒ∑j|ℓj|,B∈ℤ,B>L0/2+2.(4) L_0=\max_{\ell\in\mathscr L}\sum_j|\ell_j|, \qquad B\in\mathbb Z,\qquad B>L_0/2+2. \qquad\text{(4)}

There is a strict real normalized interior independent of NN. Take xcut0=12,xni0=ε0=18(c+1).(5) x^0_{\mathrm{ cut}}=\tfrac12,\qquad x^0_{n_i}=\varepsilon_0=\frac1{8(c+1)}. \qquad\text{(5)} During the knot traversal each transfer has coefficient 00 or ±1\pm1, so every wire lies strictly between 3/83/8 and 5/85/8. Every transfer equals ε0>0\varepsilon_0>0. Consequently all ℓ(x0)\ell(x^0) lie in a fixed compact subinterval of (0,1)(0,1). This also tracks the upper endpoint d=N−1d=N-1: for all sufficiently large NN, a fixed real neighborhood of Nx0Nx^0 of radius proportional to NN has every form in [B,d−B][B,d-B]. The required threshold may depend on the fixed braid.

2. The geometric chart and the literal holomorphic domains

Use L’s complex-binomial cut with parameter θ\theta, and its original scalar normalization. Its actual interpolated summand will be denoted IN(z)I_N(z), now in the coordinates of Section 1. It includes the exact binomial Gamma factor, signed crossing monomials, Cartan line, tail pivots and framing.

The change from L’s primitive chord coordinates to these coordinates is integral and unimodular. The cut clock and every transfer clock at its chosen geometric point are off the unit circle. Hence in the new coordinates the same point x*x^* satisfies ℑxj*≠0(1≤j≤m),ℑℓ(x*)≠0(ℓ∈ℒ).(6) \Im x^*_j\ne0\quad(1\le j\le m),\qquad \Im\ell(x^*)\ne0\quad(\ell\in\mathscr L). \qquad\text{(6)} The wire assertions follow from the same common frame. All logarithmic flow relations remain exact under the coordinate change. The corrected integer character is correspondingly an integer vector q∈ℤmq\in\mathbb Z^m. Fix it once and put JN(z)=e−c0q⋅zIN(z).(7) J_N(z)=e^{-c_0q\cdot z}I_N(z). \qquad\text{(7)} At every original integer state the new character is one. Near x*x^*, L gives the actual nonzero local amplitude, additive principal stationarity, and geometric action rate. No character is changed during the contour construction.

We use two elementary consequences of A’s exact marked product. The root factorial F̂N(ζ)\widehat F_N(\zeta) is holomorphic and nonzero for −1<ℜζ<N,(8) -1<\Re\zeta<N, \qquad\text{(8)} and also at every nonreal ζ\zeta. For (8), every Gamma argument in A’s product has positive real part, and its normally convergent logarithm is finite. For nonreal ζ\zeta, ζ/N\zeta/N lies in A’s slit domain; its product there is holomorphic and nonzero. These are the same marked function.

The binomial interpolant Γ(N)Γ(zcut+1)Γ(N−zcut)θzcut(1−θ)d−zcut(9) \frac{\Gamma(N)} {\Gamma(z_{\mathrm{ cut}}+1)\Gamma(N-z_{\mathrm{ cut}})} \theta^{z_{\mathrm{ cut}}}(1-\theta)^{d-z_{\mathrm{ cut}}} \qquad\text{(9)} is entire in zcutz_{\mathrm{ cut}}, with its fixed logarithms; it introduces zeros but no poles. All remaining exact monomials in INI_N are entire nonzero exponentials. Therefore JNJ_N is holomorphic on any region where every factorial form is either in (8) or nonreal. This conclusion concerns the actual full summand, not only its principal action.

3. Retain the whole boundary band

Define the bulk states and their indicator by RN={k∈ℤm:B≤ℓ(k)≤d−B for every ℓ∈ℒ},χN=𝟏RN.(10) R_N=\{k\in\mathbb Z^m:B\le\ell(k)\le d-B \text{ for every }\ell\in\mathscr L\}, \qquad \chi_N={\mathbf{1}}_{R_N}. \qquad\text{(10)} They are a subset of SNS_N. Keep the complementary states SN\RNS_N\setminus R_N exactly as their original positively oriented small product tori. Denote their sum by ΓNband\Gamma_N^{\mathrm{ band}}. Their radii can be chosen small and fixed in unscaled coordinates so that every factorial form has real part in (−1,N)(-1,N) and no other integer pole is enclosed. Equation (8) verifies this at every supported state. The band is not discarded or estimated.

There are O(Nm−1)O(N^{m-1}) band states. Indeed every such state has some fixed nonzero integer form within a fixed distance of one of its two physical supporting hyperplanes. All independent coordinates lie in [0,d][0,d]. Fix the other m−1m-1 coordinates and one nonzero coefficient of that form; only O(1)O(1) choices of the remaining coordinate lie in the band. Sum over the finite list of forms. This count gives no bound on their weighted sum.

For k∈RNk\in R_N, the closed unit cube Ck=∏j[kj−12,kj+12],ΩN=⋃k∈RNCk(11) C_k=\prod_j[k_j-\tfrac12,k_j+\tfrac12],\qquad \Omega_N=\bigcup_{k\in R_N}C_k \qquad\text{(11)} has every factorial form in [2,d−2][2,d-2] on its real points, by (4). The union is an oriented real polyhedral mm-chain, with the standard dx1∧⋯∧dxmdx_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx_m orientation. It contains a real neighborhood of Nx0Nx^0 of radius proportional to NN, for all sufficiently large NN. This holds even if Nx0Nx^0 lies on a unit-cell boundary: all cells meeting a sufficiently small proportional neighborhood are bulk cells by the strict margin in (5).

4. Polarized residue kernels and initial rectangle currents

Put ϵj=sgn⁡(ℑxj*),Kϵj(zj)=πcot⁡(πzj)−iπϵj, \epsilon_j=\operatorname{sgn}(\Im x^*_j),\qquad K_{\epsilon_j}(z_j)=\pi\cot(\pi z_j)-i\pi\epsilon_j, ωN=1(2πi)mJN(z)∏jKϵj(zj)dz1⋯dzm.(12) \omega_N=\frac1{(2\pi i)^m}J_N(z) \prod_jK_{\epsilon_j}(z_j)\,dz_1\cdots dz_m. \qquad\text{(12)} Every kernel has residue one at every integer. Thus the original small-torus residue identity is still exactly ∫ΓNωN=KN.(13) \int_{\Gamma_N}\omega_N=K_N. \qquad\text{(13)} The subtraction in (12) is recorded off the lattice, not treated as equality of meromorphic integrands. Replacing one cotangent by its subtracted constant removes that coordinate’s lattice-pole family and integrates to zero on each original supported torus.

Choose a positive vector a=(a1,…,am)a=(a_1,\ldots,a_m) sufficiently close to (|ℑx1*|,…,|ℑxm*|)(|\Im x^*_1|,\ldots,|\Im x^*_m|) that sgn⁡ℓ(ϵa)=sgn⁡ℑℓ(x*)(ℓ∈ℒ).(14) \operatorname{sgn}\ell(\epsilon a) =\operatorname{sgn}\Im\ell(x^*) \quad(\ell\in\mathscr L). \qquad\text{(14)} Here signs act coordinatewise, and the quantities are real. Require in addition ℓ(σa)≠0(ℓ∈ℒ,σ∈{±1}m).(15) \ell(\sigma a)\ne0 \quad(\ell\in\mathscr L,\ \sigma\in\{\pm1\}^m). \qquad\text{(15)} This is possible: (14) is an open condition at the original positive vector, while (15) excludes only finitely many proper real hyperplanes. Every row of ℒ\mathscr L is nonzero. Fix a small positive number η\eta.

For each coordinate and integer ll, let Hj,σlH_{j,\sigma}^{\,l} be the interval [l−12,l+12]+iησaj[l-\tfrac12,l+\tfrac12]+i\eta\sigma a_j, oriented in increasing real direction. Let VjlV_j^{\,l} be the interval l+12+i[−ηaj,ηaj]l+\tfrac12+i[-\eta a_j,\eta a_j], oriented upward. The positively oriented rectangle boundary about ll is Bjl=∑σ=±1(−σ)Hj,σl+Vjl−Vjl−1.(16) B_j^{\,l}=\sum_{\sigma=\pm1}(-\sigma)H_{j,\sigma}^{\,l} +V_j^{\,l}-V_j^{\,l-1}. \qquad\text{(16)} Every product rectangle over a bulk cell is a valid residue cycle: on its whole product box every factorial real part stays in [2,d−2][2,d-2], and each coordinate rectangle encloses only its original integer. Its period is the corresponding literal summand.

Expand ∑kχN(k)∏jBjkj\sum_k\chi_N(k)\prod_jB_j^{\,k_j} in the coordinate order 1,…,m1,\ldots,m. For J⊆{1,…,m}J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}, define ΔJχN(l)=∑δ∈{0,1}J(−1)|δ|χN(l+δ).(17) \Delta_J\chi_N(l)= \sum_{\delta\in\{0,1\}^{J}}(-1)^{|\delta|} \chi_N(l+\delta). \qquad\text{(17)} Finite index shifts in (16), with no integration by parts in the source, give the exact chain identity ∑kχN(k)∏jBjkj=∑σ∈{±1}mcσ(ΩN+iησa)+ℳN,cσ=∏j(−σj),(18) \sum_k\chi_N(k)\prod_j B_j^{\,k_j} =\sum_{\sigma\in\{\pm1\}^m}c_\sigma (\Omega_N+i\eta\sigma a) +\mathcal M_N,\qquad c_\sigma=\prod_j(-\sigma_j), \qquad\text{(18)} where the complete mixed current is ℳN=∑⌀≠J⊆[m]∑lΔJχN(l)∑σ∈{±1}Jc(∏j∉J(−σj))∏j=1m{Vjlj,j∈J,Hj,σjlj,j∉J.(19) \mathcal M_N= \sum_{\varnothing\ne J\subseteq[m]}\ \sum_l \Delta_J\chi_N(l) \sum_{\sigma\in\{\pm1\}^{J^c}} \left(\prod_{j\notin J}(-\sigma_j)\right) \prod_{j=1}^m \begin{cases} V_j^{\,l_j},&j\in J,\\ H_{j,\sigma_j}^{\,l_j},&j\notin J . \end{cases} \qquad\text{(19)} Products in (19) always have the stated coordinate orientation. These are explicit integer coefficients, including all intersections and cancellations; no mixed face is suppressed.

Whenever ΔJχN(l)≠0\Delta_J\chi_N(l)\ne0, at least one adjacent cube is a bulk cube, and the displayed face lies in its boundary. Its factorial real parts therefore remain in the physical strip. Each vertical coordinate is half-integral in real part; each horizontal coordinate has nonzero imaginary part. Thus every cotangent factor is regular on the whole mixed current.

Furthermore such a coefficient occurs only within a bounded unscaled distance of a facet of (10). If all defining indicators were constant on the cube l+{0,1}Jl+\{0,1\}^{J}, the alternating sum would vanish. A changing indicator has its defining form within ∑j∈J|ℓj|\sum_{j\in J}|\ell_j| of its threshold. These currents are consequently in fixed-width physical facet bands, with all their exact multiplicities retained. They have O(Nm−1)O(N^{m-1}) cells; several difference directions may lie on a single slanted facet, so higher codimension is not asserted.

5. Simultaneous oblique translation of each horizontal sheet

Let vσ=σav_\sigma=\sigma a and δ=x*−x0\delta=x^*-x^0. For the preferred sign vector ϵ\epsilon, choose the path bϵ(t)=(1−t)iηvϵ+tNδ,0≤t≤1.(20) b_\epsilon(t)=(1-t)i\eta v_\epsilon+tN\delta, \qquad 0\le t\le1. \qquad\text{(20)} For every other sign vector choose bσ(t)=i((1−t)η+tN)vσ.(21) b_\sigma(t)=i\bigl((1-t)\eta+tN\bigr)v_\sigma. \qquad\text{(21)} All coordinates of x+bσ(t)x+b_\sigma(t), with x∈ΩNx\in\Omega_N, have fixed nonzero imaginary signs σj\sigma_j. In (20) this uses sgn⁡ℑδj=ϵj\operatorname{sgn}\Im\delta_j=\epsilon_j. Hence no coordinate lattice pole occurs in any prism.

Every factorial form on these prisms has a fixed nonzero imaginary sign as well. For (21) this is (15). For (20), ℑℓ(x+bϵ(t))=(1−t)ηℓ(vϵ)+tNℑℓ(x*),(22) \Im\ell(x+b_\epsilon(t)) =(1-t)\eta\ell(v_\epsilon) +tN\Im\ell(x^*), \qquad\text{(22)} whose two terms have the same nonzero sign by (14). For all sufficiently large NN its absolute value is bounded below by a positive constant independent of NN. The same holds for (21). Section 2 proves that the entire literal summand is holomorphic on a neighborhood of every closed prism. Thus no additional factorial/source-divisor current is hidden in these translations.

Give [0,1]×ΩN[0,1]\times\Omega_N the orientation dt∧dxdt\wedge dx, and map it by (t,x)↦x+bσ(t)(t,x)\mapsto x+b_\sigma(t). Define 𝒮σ,N\mathcal S_{\sigma,N} as the image of [0,1]×∂ΩN[0,1]\times\partial\Omega_N, with the dtdt-then-boundary orientation. Since ∂([0,1]×ΩN)={1}×ΩN−{0}×ΩN−[0,1]×∂ΩN, \partial([0,1]\times\Omega_N) =\{1\}\times\Omega_N-\{0\}\times\Omega_N -[0,1]\times\partial\Omega_N, holomorphic Stokes gives exactly ∫ΩN+iηvσωN=∫ΩN+bσ(1)ωN−∫𝒮σ,NωN.(23) \int_{\Omega_N+i\eta v_\sigma}\omega_N =\int_{\Omega_N+b_\sigma(1)}\omega_N -\int_{\mathcal S_{\sigma,N}}\omega_N. \qquad\text{(23)} This is a finite sum of ordinary polyhedral Stokes identities; internal cell sides cancel with opposite orientations. There is no appeal to a contour at infinity.

Combining (13), (18), and (23) gives the full original scalar: KN=cϵ∫ΩN+NδωN+∑σ≠ϵcσ∫ΩN+iNvσωN+∫ΓNbandωN+∫ℳNωN−∑σcσ∫𝒮σ,NωN.(24) \boxed{\displaystyle K_N= c_\epsilon\int_{\Omega_N+N\delta}\omega_N +\sum_{\sigma\ne\epsilon} c_\sigma\int_{\Omega_N+iNv_\sigma}\omega_N +\int_{\Gamma_N^{\mathrm{ band}}}\omega_N +\int_{\mathcal M_N}\omega_N -\sum_\sigma c_\sigma\int_{\mathcal S_{\sigma,N}}\omega_N .} \qquad\text{(24)} Every term and every sign in (24) has been specified. The band tori use the same polarized form (12); their residues remain the original boundary-state summands. No complementary real band has been set to zero.

All constructed bulk currents have polynomial geometric mass in NN for the fixed braid and chosen frame. The top sheets have O(Nm)O(N^m) volume; their boundary prisms have O(Nm−1)O(N^{m-1}) unit facets and translation length O(N)O(N). The initial mixed currents have O(Nm−1)O(N^{m-1}) fixed-size cells and bounded integer multiplicities. Every kernel factor on the latter and on the translated prisms is bounded independently of NN. These are geometric and kernel bounds, not bounds for JNJ_N or the full weighted periods.

6. The distinguished sheet and what its coefficient means

The preferred sheet contains Nx*Nx^*, since Nx*=Nx0+Nδ,(25) Nx^*=Nx^0+N\delta, \qquad\text{(25)} and ΩN\Omega_N contains a proportional neighborhood of Nx0Nx^0. After scaling by NN, it therefore contains a fixed real neighborhood of the geometric point. All chord and factorial imaginary parts there are nonzero. L’s actual local expansion and corrected geometric action apply to this same form.

For z=Nxz=Nx on this neighborhood, the polarized kernel satisfies Kϵj(Nxj)=−2πiϵj+O(e−γN).(26) K_{\epsilon_j}(Nx_j) =-2\pi i\,\epsilon_j+O(e^{-\gamma N}). \qquad\text{(26)} Consequently its coefficient, including the rectangle orientation and residue normalization, is cϵ(2πi)−m∏j(−2πiϵj)=1.(27) c_\epsilon(2\pi i)^{-m} \prod_j(-2\pi i\epsilon_j)=1. \qquad\text{(27)} Thus the preferred top has the literal nonzero local amplitude of JN(Nx)NmdxJ_N(Nx)N^m\,dx, multiplied by 1+O(e−γN)1+O(e^{-\gamma N}). Its coefficient in this explicit current decomposition is exactly the recorded +1+1, not an unknown local contour weight.

For a nonpreferred top the kernel bound is instead |∏jKϵj(xj+iNvσ,j)|≤Cexp⁡{−2πN∑σj≠ϵjaj},x∈ΩN.(28) \left|\prod_j K_{\epsilon_j}(x_j+iNv_{\sigma,j})\right| \le C\exp\!\left\{-2\pi N \sum_{\sigma_j\ne\epsilon_j}a_j\right\}, \qquad x\in\Omega_N. \qquad\text{(28)} This follows from the elementary exponential formula for cotangent. The character in (7) and the entire factorial action remain outside this kernel estimate. Their growth can outweigh (28). No source gap or alias suppression is deduced from this penalty alone.

The principal critical family on the preferred top retains the geometric rr-centralizer null direction. A real sheet through one of its points is not thereby a specified transverse Gaussian cycle or a centralizer period. Other critical points, equal-holonomy aliases, all facets and the band may compensate its contribution. Formula (24) gives no noncancellation estimate and no lower bound for KNK_N.

7. Exact character differences and the remaining reduction

For the unpolarized cotangent form of L, an integer-character step has the exact identity (e−2πizj−1)πcot⁡(πzj)=−iπ(1+e−2πizj).(29) (e^{-2\pi iz_j}-1)\pi\cot(\pi z_j) =-i\pi(1+e^{-2\pi iz_j}). \qquad\text{(29)} Iterating independent character differences cancels precisely the selected coordinate lattice-pole families. This does not cancel factorial divisors or source endpoints. Each difference has zero period on each original supported torus, not merely on their sum. It therefore supplies no finite Fourier lower bound for the original scalar: all those integer-character periods are the same KNK_N.

The polarized replacements used in (12) are the corresponding one-coordinate pole-free changes with fixed constants. The substantive additional control in (24) is its verified whole-source domain and its complete finite relative boundary: the original boundary band, initial mixed facet currents, translated staircase side prisms and the other top sheets. There is no unidentified interpolation-divisor crossing in their bulk construction, and no discarded infinity end. What remains is to estimate these actual terms and resolve the residual critical family; their existence and coefficients are no longer hypothetical.

The older factorial-core contour compiler already has exact cell telescoping and finite-facet rules on retained holomorphic tubes. Those calculations are coverage, not a proof premise here. The new ingredient is that the common nonreal framing gives the required oblique holomorphic prisms for the full literal braid bulk in arbitrary fixed diagrams, while the entire complementary physical band remains in (24). This does not evade the individual-evaluation homology obstruction by claiming a low norm for the undecomposed source. Its potentially large boundary terms are explicit.
