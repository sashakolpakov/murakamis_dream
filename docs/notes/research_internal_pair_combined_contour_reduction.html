The original quantum source for positive internal pairs

Research proof, updated 4 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

For an internal pair with powers n,p≥3n,p\ge3, the complete outside kernels now have an independently normalized complex phase. This allows one to retain those kernels as whole factors. The exact remaining contour has three variables: the shared Newton index and the two trace angles. No separate asymptotic for either one-vertex Fourier source is required.

Sections 1–5 give a contour reduction for every pair of powers at least three. Sections 6–7 complete the estimate when both powers are at least seven. In that range the original two-boundary source has a nonzero geometric leading coefficient at every sufficiently large level, with a uniform expansion near the complete boundary colors. The proof retains the original first-normal traces and their endpoint terms.

This is a theorem for a specified partially filled three-edge circle-tree exterior. It does not establish stability under every short partial filling, arbitrary tangle composition, or finitely many exceptional assignments per template.

There is a second scope distinction. The packet in the small outside-color bidisk is already proved locally. In (4) the global compact-contour bound is presently used at complete outside colors. Section 7 supplies those bounds from the physical-row interpolation and the uniform free-boundary defect theorem; it does not deduce them from (25) at a single boundary point.
