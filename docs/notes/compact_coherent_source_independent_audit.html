Independent audit of the compact coherent source period

Audit, 27 October 2026. Sections 1–5 of compact_coherent_source_period.md pass this independent check. The finite centered star derivatives, global primitive, both walls and physical Poisson scale are correct. No test rerun was needed.

1. Fixed compact cycle and exact products

The degree n=N−1n=N-1 kernel is a section of O(n,n)O(n,n). Its quotient by the identity kernel is regular on the fixed surface X=(ℙ1)2\DX=(\mathbb P^1)^2\setminus D; its form has the correct canonical degree and no poles outside DD. The real sphere w=v,z=v‾w=v,z=\bar v is disjoint from DD, including infinity. Its orientation gives −12πidw∧dz(1+wz)2=d(area⁡v)π(1+|v|2)2. -\frac1{2\pi i}\frac{dw\wedge dz}{(1+wz)^2} =\frac{d(\operatorname{area}v)} {\pi(1+|v|^2)^2}. Angular integration and the printed beta integral therefore yield ∫ΓωA=Tr⁡(A)/N\int_\Gamma\omega_A=\operatorname{Tr}(A)/N. The factor NN in kernel composition precisely cancels the middle beta pairing; no intermediate binomial remains.

The centered star formula is exact. At the origin, its two derivatives are r!(nr)A0rr!\binom nr A_{0r} and r!Br0r!B_{r0}, so the coefficient (n−r)!/(r!n!)(n-r)!/(r!n!) produces A0rBr0A_{0r}B_{r0}. For the general point, put d=1+wzd=1+wz and g=d−1/2(1−wz1). g=d^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}1&-w\\z&1\end{pmatrix}. Its column action sends bb to (z+b)/(1−wb)(z+b)/(1-wb); the dual row action sends aa to (w+a)/(1−za)(w+a)/(1-za). The symmetric-power basis with the printed column binomials therefore gives exactly the stated two centered derivatives. Both differentiated expressions are finite polynomials of degree at most nn. The square-root sign cancels in conjugation.

2. The global rational primitive

The commutator vector fields check directly: σ[J0,B]=(w∂w−z∂z)σB,σ[J−,B]=(∂w+z2∂z)σB. \sigma_{[J_0,B]}=(w\partial_w-z\partial_z)\sigma_B,\qquad \sigma_{[J_-,B]}=(\partial_w+z^2\partial_z)\sigma_B. For the second identity, acting on the numerator produces ∂w+z2∂z−nz\partial_w+z^2\partial_z-nz; differentiating its denominator gives exactly the same correction. Both fields are global and preserve the identity two-form.

The off-diagonal matrix is recovered by division by i−ji-j. For the diagonal primitive, the jj-th commutator entry is (j+1)bj−jbj−1(j+1)b_j-jb_{j-1}; the last entry is correct because the remaining diagonal has trace zero. Cartan’s formula then gives d(σBιVωI)=(VσB)ωI d(\sigma_B\iota_V\omega_I)=(V\sigma_B)\omega_I with the printed sign. Hence the rational primitive is global on XX, and Stokes on the actual compact sphere proves its zero period. The identity form itself has period one and is not being declared exact.

3. Whole walls and the physical Gauss scale

Summing the truncated binomial kernels gives all three formulas in Section 4, including the lower-vacuum subtraction −κ(1−t)n-\kappa(1-t)^n and upper-head subtraction −δtn-\delta t^n. The latter uses n+1=Nn+1=N, which fixes its coefficient and sign. Their normalized beta moments and falling-factorial number moments are exact with the stated factors of NN.

The physical principal clock is A=e2πitA=e^{2\pi it}, and p=1/wp=1/w gives the printed balanced Gauss symbols. Direct differentiation gives dlog⁡A∧dlog⁡p=2πidw∧dz(1+wz)2. d\log A\wedge d\log p =2\pi i\,\frac{dw\wedge dz}{(1+wz)^2}. On the real sphere this is 2πdt∧dϕ2\pi\,dt\wedge d\phi. Thus the trace measure is precisely the normalized Liouville measure Ω/(4π2)=dtdϕ/(2π)\Omega/(4\pi^2)=dt\,d\phi/(2\pi). The first star commutator has sign (1+wz)2(azbw−bzaw)/n(1+wz)^2(a_zb_w-b_za_w)/n; division by hN=2πi/Nh_N=2\pi i/N gives the inverse tensor of this same form. No fixed-root normal has been substituted for hNh_N.

4. Source and asymptotic scope

The original coupon functional equals normalized trace on the diagonal closed arrays and their number-moment weights, as stated. It does not equal it on arbitrary off-diagonal Ward insertions. For those, the exact compact expression would retain the coupon: Φ(A)=Tr⁡(eA)=N∫Γ(σe⋆σA)ωI. \Phi(A)=\operatorname{Tr}(eA) =N\int_\Gamma(\sigma_e\star\sigma_A)\,\omega_I. This follows immediately from the exact product and trace formulas. No such replacement is needed for the diagonal arrays considered in the note.

The fixed algebraic surface and compact cycle are genuine. The symbol degree and allowed pole order still grow with NN; fixed dimension alone does not provide the bounded presentation required by the separate cyclotomic reconstruction theorem. Likewise the finite star formula does not imply a uniform semiclassical expansion for an arbitrary growing full-word matrix. The note correctly restricts its Poisson expansion to a controlled symbol class and leaves the global geometric source coefficient and its norm unproved.

5. The full top cohomology and uniform primitive bounds

The added global cohomology statement also checks. Normalize the Segre matrix to have trace one: (abcd),a+d=1,ad=bc. \begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},\qquad a+d=1,\quad ad=bc. Then h=2a−1,e=2b,f=2ch=2a-1,e=2b,f=2c identifies XX with the smooth affine split quadric h2+ef=1h^2+ef=1. Every regular algebraic top form on XX has finite pole order mDmD on the compactification. Since Ωℙ1×ℙ12(mD)=O(m−2,m−2), \Omega^2_{\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1}(mD) =O(m-2,m-2), it has exactly the displayed kernel form with n=m−2n=m-2, after increasing the allowed pole order if necessary. The primitive therefore proves HdR2(X)=k[dw∧dz(1+wz)2]. H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(X) =k\left[\frac{dw\wedge dz}{(1+wz)^2}\right]. Its period is −2πi-2\pi i, so it is nonzero. This is the fixed source’s rank-one algebraic cohomology; it is not the full knot’s Bloch-regulator period.

For Gjj=(nj)−1G_{jj}=\binom nj^{-1}, the symbol on Γ\Gamma is the normalized GG-expectation in the column cj=(nj)v‾jc_j=\binom nj\bar v^j. Hence |σA|≤∥A∥G|\sigma_A|\le\|A\|_G. Both primitive matrices actually have bounds independent of NN. For the off-diagonal part the exact integral Boff=12π∫02πi(θ−π)eiθJ0Ae−iθJ0dθ B_{\mathrm{off}} =\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} i(\theta-\pi)e^{i\theta J_0}Ae^{-i\theta J_0}\,d\theta has entry Aij/(i−j)A_{ij}/(i-j) off the diagonal and zero diagonal. The conjugations are GG-unitary, so ∥Boff∥G≤π2∥A∥G. \|B_{\mathrm{off}}\|_G\le\frac\pi2\|A\|_G. For the raising part, let sj=∑k=0j(Akk−τ)s_j=\sum_{k=0}^j(A_{kk}-\tau). Using both the prefix and its trace-zero complementary suffix gives |sj|≤2min⁡(j+1,n−j)∥A∥G. |s_j|\le2\min(j+1,n-j)\|A\|_G. Its weighted shift entry is sjj+1j+1n−j, \frac{s_j}{j+1}\sqrt{\frac{j+1}{n-j}}, whose modulus is at most 2∥A∥G2\|A\|_G. Therefore ∥Bdiag∥G≤2∥A∥G\|B_{\mathrm{diag}}\|_G\le2\|A\|_G. These strengthen the polynomial estimates without changing the primitive or source.

The qualification is the same-metric one: GG itself is exponentially conditioned against the original Euclidean basis. These are uniform bounds for the source contraction in the coherent metric, not an unproved polynomial comparison with another metric or a bound on the actual crossing-word norm.

6. Fixed-order actual A-period and removal of its normal poles

The subsequent fixed-matrix-size application is also valid. Use an actual order-rr generic color recurrence and its companion matrices, fixed initial column v0v_0, and last-coordinate row ℓ\ell. For HN=AN−r⋯Aj0,RNleft(q)=v0(q)ℓHN(q) H_N=A_{N-r}\cdots A_{j_0},\qquad R_N^{\mathrm{left}}(q)=v_0(q)\ell H_N(q) the all-level color theorem gives Tr⁡RNleft=ℓHNv0=CN(q)\operatorname{Tr}R_N^{\mathrm{left}}=\ell H_Nv_0=C_N(q). Applying the coherent calculus at matrix size rr, rather than color size NN, therefore gives ΩN=rω(r,RNleft),∫ΓΩN=CN(q). \Omega_N=r\,\omega_{(r,R_N^{\mathrm{left}})},\qquad \int_\Gamma\Omega_N=C_N(q). The surface, cycle and pole-order bound r+1r+1 are independent of the level. The case r=1r=1 is simply the scalar n=0n=0 coherent formula.

The whole companion product has poles of order at most the uniform total leading-germ budget. Write its outer product at the marked root as RNleft(q)=∑j=−B∞RN,jϵj. R_N^{\mathrm{left}}(q)=\sum_{j=-B}^{\infty}R_{N,j}\epsilon^j . Because CN(q)C_N(q) is regular, every negative coefficient has trace zero. For each such coefficient the global primitive gives ω(r,RN,j)=dextηRN,j\omega_{(r,R_{N,j})}=d_{\mathrm{ext}}\eta_{R_{N,j}}. Subtracting dext(r∑j=−B−1ϵjηRN,j) d_{\mathrm{ext}}\left( r\sum_{j=-B}^{-1}\epsilon^j\eta_{R_{N,j}}\right) therefore removes every normal pole, leaves the period unchanged, and introduces no division by CNC_N. In particular this works at a level where KN=0K_N=0. The regularized representative remains in the same fixed pole-order top-form space.

The order matters for interpreting this pole removal. The alternative matrix RNright=HNv0ℓ R_N^{\mathrm{right}}=H_Nv_0\ell is already regular entrywise: its column HNv0H_Nv_0 consists of actual Laurent-polynomial colors. The earlier ordering used in this audit was therefore valid for the period identity but made polar removal vacuous. In the revised left ordering, nonzero traceless polar coefficients can occur. The exact difference RNleft−RNright=[v0ℓ,HN] R_N^{\mathrm{left}}-R_N^{\mathrm{right}} =[v_0\ell,H_N] has trace zero, and its global primitive identifies the two whole period classes without dividing by the selected scalar. This supplies an explicitly regular representative as well as the coefficientwise regularization above.

This is a local regularization at the specified root, not a uniform bounded-height presentation of the growing companion product. Its rational coefficient degrees and heights can still grow with NN. Conversely, since the coherent matrix size is now fixed, its metric and primitive bounds do not acquire the exponential-in-color-size distortion mentioned in Section 5. Neither fact determines the physical norm or the geometric comparison.
