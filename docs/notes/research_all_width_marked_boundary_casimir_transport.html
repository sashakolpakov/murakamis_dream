Explicit boundary Casimirs in the marked observation coordinates

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The all-width marked observation has an explicit inverse on the actual source. This note replaces that full reconstruction, for each of the two outer pair Casimirs, by one shared linear Borel resolvent on the middle physical packet. All other pair Casimirs remain literal middle operators. The resolvent has an exact finite inverse with denominator two, at every level, and norm at most eCbNe^{C_bN} at fixed width. In a specified coherent Borel metric its normalized multiplier has norm at most three.

The formulas transport the actual braid representation by its original Casimir polynomials, including every Jordan derivative. The braid polynomials themselves are not asserted to have bounded order. All source constraints remain, and the original marked closing has an explicit physical row with polynomial norm. These identities do not lower-bound the final coherent scalar.

7. The complete braid representation and its source
