Signed half twists and long fillings of arbitrarily branching Conway factors

29 October 2026. The original marked quantum invariant has its full hyperbolic-volume limit for every fixed signed half-twist pattern on C(FG), where both children are robust, after independently sufficiently long fixed signed integral fillings of any auxiliary subset. Every such expression has a constructible pattern giving a KNOT when all auxiliary circles are filled. No fixed diagram width, branching depth, turn depth, level parity, or prime-level condition is imposed.

This research proof assembles the independently checked full-source gap, finite complex packet, actual marked representation, and primitive geometric completion. It is not a theorem for arbitrary hyperbolic knots. The exact grammar and the excluded minimal case are below.

1. The class and theorem

Start with the original retained crossing-circle leaf h and seed A=h^3. Let F and G be any fixed finite expressions generated by A, literal quarter-turns, and stacking. Use the actual cylindrical closure C(FG). At each individual leaf l choose epsilon_l in {0,+1,-1}, replacing h_j by the actual T(R^epsilon_l L_j). Keep all original auxiliary circles, marks, and normalization. This includes arbitrary finite branching, arbitrary turn depth, and independent half-twist signs.

Let Y^epsilon be this augmented-link exterior. Choose any subset I of auxiliary circles and independently sufficiently large fixed signed integers p_i. Fill by the original disk-framed full twists R^(-2p_i). The resulting literal crossing power at a filled leaf is epsilon_i-2p_i. Unfilled auxiliary colors remain complete; p_i=0 is not used to represent an unfilled cusp.

Then Y^epsilon(p) is the actual complete hyperbolic filled exterior and

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N) log|K_(E,N)^epsilon(p)|
                      =Vol(Y^epsilon(p)).                (1)

Both level parities and composite levels are included. The diagram, half twists, filling subset, and slopes are fixed before N tends to infinity. The threshold may depend on the fixed diagram and signs; it does not require the slopes to have the same sign or comparable magnitudes. The invariant is the original marked one-cut scalar, with its explicit nonzero enhancement factor of subexponential modulus. For the reduced enhanced Kashaev invariant this outer factor has unit modulus. For the full one-cut modified normalization it has modulus 1/N, once rather than once per auxiliary circle. These facts follow from the original normalization comparison and the independently derived Kashaev crossing comparison.

The minimal C(A) is excluded from this mixed-color filling statement. Its complete half-twist volume equality is a separate proved result. No equality for all alternating, arborescent, or hyperbolic knots is claimed simply from the name of this expression grammar.

2. The actual complete source and the whole-source gap

The full Newton transport theorem retains every repeated-root normal coordinate and the original Cartan phase. Its complete-source leading coefficient is nonzero for every fixed epsilon pattern. The complete critical point is the pure one; every complete leaf lies strictly above one quarter.

The supporting-plane theorem proves a uniform gap for every alias subset and the entire physical color complement on C(FG). Its mechanism is uniform across this grammar: each proper-seed leaf lies between 1/4 and atan(sqrt(2))/pi, and the tangent to its complete action dominates the full alias profile by a fixed positive amount. Product tangents cancel by stationarity under the actual affine wire relations. No separate estimate is needed for each expression or for each number of aliases.

The half-partner geometry theorem constructs the actual complete augmented complement by changed shaded face pairings. All pointwise edge words and complete horosphere links are checked. Its volume is 2pi times the same complete source action.

The finite complex-color packet is separately even and has a uniform complex asymptotic with power N^(3/2) and nonzero leading amplitude. The fixed finite grid, all D=N-1 offsets, and the exponentially small interpolation residual are retained. The marked odd deformation constructs the actual near-complete link-group representation and the primitive peripheral coordinates of that SAME selected action. Together these results supply every hypothesis of the general original-source filling theorem.

3. The global peripheral shear

Let Phi_0(t) be the already proved pure critical action, with all auxiliary colors allowed to vary near completeness. Write

a_i(t)=partial_i Phi_0(t),
U_i^0=-2pi i t_i, V_i^0=-2a_i(t),
G_0(t)=2pi i Phi_0(t).                                  (2)

The exact changed-source/geometry correspondence is

chi_i=t_i-epsilon_i a_i(t)/(2pi i),
Phi_epsilon(chi)=Phi_0(t)
                     -sum_i epsilon_i a_i(t)^2/(4pi i). (3)

At zero, Phi_0’’=-2pi diag(c_i), c_i=cot(pi x_(i,*))>0. The Jacobian of t->chi is diag(1-epsilon_i i c_i), hence invertible. Equation (3) defines a unique holomorphic branch t(chi) near zero.

The local selected-source identity is proved in the complex packet by its exact Ward quadratic and the zero-action endpoint x=1/2. Its global extension is elementary. At fixed wire coordinate x the implicit local relation is chi=t-epsilon*g_t(x,t)/(2pi i), and

f_epsilon(x,chi)=g(x,t)-epsilon*g_t(x,t)^2/(4pi i).

Differentiation at fixed chi gives f_epsilon,x=g_x; differentiation at fixed x gives f_epsilon,chi=g_t. Thus the complete network wire equations are exactly the old pure equations at the hidden colors t. At their critical point g_t=partial_i Phi_0. This proves (3), including all interactions between distinct cusps and all turns and products.

The same computation gives

partial_i Phi_epsilon=a_i(t),
U_i^epsilon=U_i^0-epsilon_i V_i^0/2=-2pi i chi_i,
V_i^epsilon=V_i^0=-2 partial_i Phi_epsilon,
G_epsilon=G_0-sum_i epsilon_i (V_i^0)^2/8,
dG_epsilon=(1/2)sum_i V_i^epsilon dU_i^epsilon.          (4)

Its packet cusp ratios are

tau_i^epsilon=i c_i/(1-epsilon_i i c_i),
Im tau_i^epsilon=c_i/(1+epsilon_i^2 c_i^2)>0.           (5)

For epsilon=-1 this is (1+exp(2pi i x_(i,)))/2; for epsilon=+1 it is -(1+exp(-2pi i x_(i,)))/2. The geometric primitive cusp ratio is twice the packet ratio, as required by the original full-twist convention.

Equations (3)–(5) are used together with the actual selected finite packet and the original odd-link primitive paths. Neither the algebraic shear alone nor a match of Hessians is used to infer the asymptotic.

4. Filling stationarity and an exact action simplification

The general original-source filling theorem uses the unique small critical point chi_*(p) of

S_epsilon,p(chi)=Phi_epsilon(chi)
                  +(pi i/2)sum_(i in I)(chi_i-1)^2/p_i. (6)

In the original normalization it proves the stronger asymptotic

K_(E,N)^epsilon(p)=Xi_N eta_N kappa^(-sum_(i in I)p_i)
    N^(3/2) D_(E,epsilon)(p) exp[N S_epsilon,p(chi_*)]
                                  *[1+O_p(N^-1)],
eta_N=exp[(i pi (N-1)/4)sum_l epsilon_l],
D_(E,epsilon)(p)!=0.                                  (6a)

Here Xi_N is the already recorded enhancement/framing factor, not another copy of a factor inside the raw source. The nonzero D is the explicit Gaussian-and-sinh prefactor of the general transfer. Its minimal signed charge sectors add. Every original color, alias, normal term, endpoint current, and nonminimal charge is controlled before taking this limit. In particular (6a) is a full-sequence statement and is not obtained by taking the modulus term by term.

Its saddle equation gives the primitive filling condition

U_i^epsilon+p_i V_i^epsilon=-2pi i.                   (7)

The regulator anchor is the actual complete volume, and the marked volume identity is

Vol(rho_chi)=Im G_epsilon(chi)
                   -(1/2)sum_i Re U_i^epsilon Im V_i^epsilon.

At (7) this equals 2pi Re S_epsilon,p(chi_*). The general geometric completion proof applies to the actual nearby bounded framed representation, with all unfilled cusps parabolic and the primitive small logs in (7). It constructs the smooth complete hyperbolic metric on the intended filled topology. Combining its volume with (6a) proves (1).

There is also a useful exact simplification. At the saddle put P_i=p_i-epsilon_i/2 and substitute (3). Then

a_i(t)+(pi i/P_i)(t_i-1)=0,
S_epsilon,p(chi_*)=Phi_0(t_*)
                      +(pi i/2)sum_(i in I)(t_i-1)^2/P_i. (8)

For an unfilled cusp, chi_i=0 implies t_i=0 on the local branch: separate evenness factors a_i=t_i Q_i(t), and its coefficient remains nonzero near completeness. Thus it contributes no extra term in (8).

Equation (8) is an identity of selected actions, not an assertion that the odd link is the pure link with a rational filling. At an odd leaf multiplying U_i0+(p_i-epsilon_i/2)V_i0=-2pi i by two produces the primitive pure-cusp slope (2,2p_i-epsilon_i) with total logarithmic angle -4pi i. Ordinary smooth pure-link Dehn filling therefore cannot replace the marked odd-link completion in (7).

5. The constructive knot subfamily

The literal connectivity theorem gives a linear-time parity assignment for EVERY robust expression. The allowed endpoint matchings are I,U,X; a seed realizes U or X without internal circles, a turn swaps I and U, and stacking has only one circle-creating pair, U times U. Every subtree retains at least two feasible matching types, so the root can be chosen U or X. Its cylindrical closure has one main component.

Either sign can be used at every odd leaf. Full-twist fillings preserve the resulting endpoint permutation. Filling all auxiliary circles therefore gives knots for arbitrary fixed branching and turn depth. Equation (1) applies to this entire constructible knot subfamily. No fixed diagram width or turn depth is imposed; a statement about the knots’ intrinsic minimal bridge numbers is not needed here.

6. A-structure scope

The relevant marked branch is algebraic. With E=exp(2pi i x), T=exp(2pi i t), H=exp(2pi i chi), and Q=(E-T)/(ET-1), the local equations following from (3)–(4) are

H=T Q^(-epsilon), M=H^(-1), L=Q^(-2),
kappa=-(1-E/T)(1-ET)/(1+E)^2.                        (9)

Together with the actual product and turn equations these describe the selected auxiliary peripheral branch. All multipliers here are projective similarity multipliers (squared SL eigenvalues), with the original centered logarithms retained. The pole divisors are excluded and the complete oriented branch is specified.

The calibrated primitive G_epsilon and the source-selected nonzero period carry information beyond the elimination ideal. No whole classical knot A-polynomial, generic-main-meridian quantum operator, or automatic dominant-period selection is inferred from elimination. Those general extensions remain part of the larger active objective.

The half-integral notation has a simple algebraic explanation. Over the peripheral torus with coordinates M,L, retain ell with ell^2=L. This finite etale double cover is a mu_2 torsor in characteristic zero. On it the change is the INTEGRAL monomial map

(M^0,ell) -> (M^0 ell^(-epsilon),ell).              (10)

The source branch supplies ell=Q^(-1)=exp(V/2), continued from ell=1. Thus the internal algebraic data trivialize the pulled-back torsor locally. With several cusps the cover has group (mu_2)^b. Forgetting this marked square root loses the information needed to distinguish the physical odd meridians. It does not justify a half-integral change of primitive basis on the same cusp torus.

On the logarithmic cover, the primitive changes by G_epsilon=G_0-sum epsilon_i V_i^2/8, as in (4). This is an explicit algebraic correspondence together with its calibrated action change. Neither the elementary torsor nor its trivialization alone proves the asymptotic: selection and survival of the period are supplied by the finite-source packet and the whole-color bound.

7. Verification and limits

The independent six-leaf certificate checks C(A A) beyond the old three-leaf examples. It proves the nine stationary gradient equations and positive Hessian exactly, retains the D=N-1 multiplier 4/3, and gives the pure leading amplitude 3^(5/4)/32768. Its 36 signed complete-source and 18 mixed-color finite-level comparisons are explicitly numerical diagnostics; they are not premises of the general convergence proof.

Literature coverage is kept separate from the own proofs. The result retains its literal grammar, long-slope quantifiers, and original invariant. The active general objective remains open beyond classes with these source and geometry certificates, including arbitrary knot diagrams and the full generic quantum A-operator formulation.
