Independent audit of the unfolded Ward factorial

Research audit, 27 October 2026. The formulas in the unfolding theorem pass an independent coefficientwise proof check. This audit does not assert a global network-period identity.

1. All-level uniqueness

Fix a coefficient index j. For all sufficiently large N>j, the actual nilpotent jet prescribes the value c_j(Q_N)=1/(Q_N;Q_N)_j; neither denominator vanishes there. A nonzero rational function has finitely many zeros, while the Q_N are infinitely many distinct points. Thus the coefficient is uniquely 1/(Q;Q)_j. The exceptional set can depend on j. No common bound in j on degrees or exceptional levels is required. This proves uniqueness in the specified rational coefficient class, and the displayed universal factorial supplies existence in this case.

2. Cyclotomic pole and constant

The formal logarithm is

log F(Q,z)=sum_(m>=1) z^m/[m(1-Q^m)].

Put Q=zeta exp(h), with zeta primitive of order N and X=z^N. For m=kN,

1/[m(1-exp(mh))]=-1/(m^2 h)+1/(2m)+O(h).

The pole is therefore -Li_2(X)/N^2. The resonant constant is Li_1(X)/(2N)=-Log(1-X)/(2N).

For D_N=product_(j=1)^(N-1)(1-zeta^j z)^(j/N), the coefficient of z^m in its logarithm is

-1/(mN) sum_(j=1)^(N-1) j zeta^(jm).

For nonresonant m, this equals 1/[m(1-zeta^m)], exactly the constant of log F. For m=kN, it is -(N-1)/(2kN), so the whole resonant part of log D_N is (N-1)Log(1-X)/(2N). Subtracting Log(1-X)/2 leaves precisely the resonant constant of log F. This checks the sign and every factor of N in the pole and regular part.

The parameter h=Log(Q/zeta) here is a fixed-root deformation parameter. It is not the earlier large-level parameter Log q with q_N=exp(pi*i/N).

3. Norm-root gauge and its uniqueness

The marked cyclic product satisfies

D_N(zeta z)/D_N(z)=(1-z)/(1-X)^(1/N),
product_(a=0)^(N-1) D_N(zeta^a z)
                      =(1-X)^((N-1)/2).

Both follow directly by rearranging the marked logarithms, hence hold for even and composite N as well. With nu=(1-X)^(1/N), the gauge

g_D=D_N/nu^((N-1)/2)

has norm one and the required normalized cyclic transport. Its nilpotent specialization has constant one and solves the same coefficient recurrence as the literal actual factorial. It therefore equals that finite factorial modulo e^N.

The ratio of this gauge and the previously constructed norm-root gauge is sigma invariant, hence scalar in the completed base: the basis 1,e,...,e^(N-1) has distinct sigma eigenvalues. Its norm is its N-th power. The identity marking forces this scalar to be one. This proves equality on the completed common marked cover, rather than merely equality of norms. The even-N square root is fixed by the same identity mark.

For the actual cyclic sign e^N=(-1)^(N+1)kappa_N, substitution z=-te gives X=-kappa_N t^N and 1-X=1+kappa_N t^N, with no remaining parity sign.

4. Period and source conventions

For S=Li_2(X)+UV, U=Log X, V=Log(1-X), direct differentiation gives dS=U dV. Thus the stated Heisenberg matrix has Pi^(-1)dPi with zero central entry; equivalently its right equation is dPi=Pi Omega. The opposite Maurer–Cartan convention would require a different central coordinate.

Since d kappa_N/d kappa=N at completion,

partial_kappa[-Li_2(-kappa_N t^N)/N^2]_(kappa=0)=t^N/N.

The actual divided affine source is -t^N, exactly minus N times this normal derivative. The local Bloch–Wigner logarithmic metric term is retained by the displayed regulator formula.

5. Identity-meridian formal rigidity is already available

The closed-braid period note, Section 4, already proves that an Artinian representation with every meridian congruent to identity factors through knot abelianization. Its induction is valid: if all meridians agree modulo m^r and lie in I+m, their pairwise commutators lie in m^(r+1). A Wirtinger crossing then equates consecutive understrand meridians modulo m^(r+1). A knot has one connected understrand cycle, so all agree at the next order. Nilpotence terminates the induction.

For matrix groups this argument does not require characteristic zero. More generally it applies whenever the identity congruence filtration satisfies [G_r,G_s] subset G_(r+s). For the present SL_2 application it proves the stated Artin functor equality and the claimed formal inability to reach nonabelian geometry from the identity representation. It says nothing analogous about a noncentral parabolic meridian or a different residual representation.
