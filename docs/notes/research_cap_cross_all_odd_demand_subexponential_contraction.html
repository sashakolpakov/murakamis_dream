Scope

The proof uses the raw odd projected recurrence, a genuine positive endpoint coefficient LOWER theorem, and a uniform two-sided comparator rate. It does not replace the original cutoff sum by its full auxiliary slice above M, use a norm bound, or infer finite monotonicity of the individual comparator coefficients.

The even source requires its own boundary term, which is not covered by this all-odd statement. Nearby spectra, the full source and filling asymptotics remain separate; the all-parity extension is cited above.
