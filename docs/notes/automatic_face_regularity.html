The Fourier rank conditions follow from knot-exterior homology

Research note, 25 October 2026. This supplies a uniform independent proof of the two rank conditions in singular_faces_and_six_two.md. It applies to every globally branched oriented ideal triangulation of a knot exterior in S^3. No geometricity, quantum-invariant existence theorem, or finite census assertion is used.

1. Statement

Let X be the ideal compactification of an oriented one-cusped manifold M, with its single ideal vertex. Assume H^1(X;R)=0. For ordered face pairings put

D=X_0-X_1+X_2, R=X_2-X_3, E=diag(epsilon).

Theorem. Rank D=N. For any basis F of ker D, set

T=E R F, U=X_0 F,
K=basis of ker T, P=basis of im T,
C=K^T U^T P.

Then rank C=dim ker T. Thus the Fourier reduction of the existing charged tensor always has a well-defined convergent residual integral. The full stacked face matrix [D;R] can still be singular.

For knot exteriors in S^3 the cohomology assumption is automatic, as proved in Section 4. The theorem is also valid whenever the stated ideal-compactification cohomology vanishes.

2. A local relation is exactly an ideal edge cocycle

Introduce three numbers (a,b,c) in an ordered tetrahedron of sign epsilon. Define four local momentum coefficients and four position coefficients

p=(b,b+c,c,c),
q=(a,-a,a+epsilon*b,-epsilon*b).                     (2.1)

Suppose across every glued face pair i,j they satisfy

p_(t,i)=p_(u,j), q_(t,i)+q_(u,j)=0.                 (2.2)

The vector space of such relations is naturally isomorphic to the space of cellular real 1-cocycles on the ideal triangulation X.

Proof. In one tetrahedron give its vertices potentials

n_0=0, n_1=c, n_2=b+c, n_3=b+c+epsilon*a.

For the face opposite i, list its three vertices as u<v<w and let omega_i=epsilon*(-1)^i. Direct substitution gives

p_i=n_v-n_u, q_i=omega_i*(n_w-n_v).                 (2.3)

The same face has opposite omega on its two occurrences. Because face gluings preserve the three-vertex order, (2.2) is exactly equality of the two independent vertex differences on that face. Consequently all local differences lambda_ij=n_j-n_i descend to a single number on each global oriented edge. They sum to zero around every triangular face, so define a cellular edge cocycle.

Conversely, a global edge cocycle is locally a gradient on each tetrahedron: set n_0=0 and n_i=lambda_0i. The triangular cocycle relations give every other edge difference. Then recover

c=n_1-n_0, b=n_2-n_1, a=epsilon*(n_3-n_2).

Equations (2.3) prove the face matching conditions. The constructions are inverse. This proves the claimed isomorphism, including injectivity.

There is only one ideal vertex, so the cellular coboundary map from 0-cochains to 1-cochains is zero. Hence H^1(X;R)=0 says that the cocycle space itself is zero. All relations (2.1)–(2.2) must therefore vanish.

3. Applying the local lemma to the matrices

First, if D^T a=0, use (2.1) with b=c=0 in every tetrahedron. Its position coefficients match because their paired sums are exactly D^T a; the momentum coefficients are zero. The lemma forces a=0. Thus D has full row rank N.

Next suppose C has a nonzero left-null vector z. The corresponding global face assignment k=F K z obeys

Dk=0, Rk=0.

Let b=X_0 k. Since P spans E R(ker D), the condition z^T C=0 is

b^T E R F=0.

Therefore R^T E b annihilates ker D and is in im D^T. Choose a with

D^T a+R^T E b=0.                                   (3.1)

Set c=X_2 k. The equations Dk=Rk=0 say that the four occurrences of k in a tetrahedron are (b,b+c,c,c), exactly the momentum coefficients in (2.1). They match on paired faces since k is a global face assignment. The local position coefficients in (2.1) sum over faces to the left side of (3.1). Thus they match as well. The lemma forces a=b=c=0, hence k=0. Since F K has independent columns, z=0, a contradiction. C has full row rank as asserted.

This argument uses only real finite-dimensional linear algebra and the explicit cocycle lemma. In particular it does not infer convergence from a general TQFT theorem with an unaudited analytic proof.

4. Why the cohomology vanishes for knot exteriors

Let M be the compact exterior of a knot in S^3. Its ideal compactification is M with its connected boundary torus collapsed to one point. Thus

H^1(X;R)=H^1(M,boundary M;R).

In the cohomology exact sequence of this pair, H0(M)->H0(boundary M) is an isomorphism. Hence H^1(M,boundary M) injects into H^1(M), with image the kernel of restriction to H^1(boundary M).

Mayer–Vietoris for S^3=M union a solid torus gives H_1(M;Z)=Z and shows that the boundary meridian maps to its generator. For example H_1(boundary M)->H_1(M) direct-sum H_1(solid torus) is an isomorphism; the meridian is zero in the solid torus, and the other boundary generator generates its H_1. The meridian therefore generates H_1(M). Restriction H1(M)->H1(boundary M) is injective, so its kernel and H^1(M,boundary M) vanish. This proves the required hypothesis.

5. Consequence and the remaining distinction

The full-tensor volume theorem in singular_faces_and_six_two.md now applies to every globally branched complete positive geometric ideal triangulation of a knot exterior in S^3. Its Fourier rank assumptions have a uniform topological proof, rather than being additional census conditions. The independent geometry-to-saddle and analytic proofs remain necessary and are supplied in that note.

This is still a statement about the full charged tensor at complete angles. A knot quantum A-state additionally needs its specified boundary fiber, phase, normalization, and peripheral identification. The general preferred-longitude/fiber construction and equivalence of arbitrary realizations are not consequences of the rank theorem alone. For 6_2 these are supplied by the separate finite certificates.

The subsequent preferred_longitude_cochains.md supplies the uniform preferred-longitude relation. The ordinary-fiber construction and then fourier_boundary_state.md supply the normalized meridian state for both possible boundary ranks, preserving the complete geometric volume limit. Geometric realization and global triangulation identification are still separate obligations. The subsequent preferred_longitude_cochains.md now gives the uniform preferred-longitude relation. general_boundary_state_and_framing.md constructs the meridian state whenever the longitude increases the B rank, with an exact correction for any rational longitude shear. The other boundary-rank case remains separate.

There is also no assertion here that every ideal triangulation admits a global branching or that every hyperbolic alternating knot has the required positive geometric realization of the chosen combinatorics. Those are separate uniform construction questions.

The script reduce_singular_faces.py checks (2.3) symbolically and recomputes the ideal edge-cocycle matrix in each finite example. As a useful boundary check, the two-component word RLRLRL has a nonzero cocycle and local-relation space; the one-vertex hypothesis should not be dropped without a replacement argument.
