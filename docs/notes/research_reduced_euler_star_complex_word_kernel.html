The lower-donor Euler star as a complex word kernel

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

After the actual factorial normalization, a full reduced Euler star is a finite word polynomial with an explicit inversion weight. Its inverse has one additional bounded pair interaction. On a compact chart where the ordinary linear characteristic is nonzero, an elementary connected-graph argument proves that both unprojected kernels are nonzero for a small normalized lower-donor interval. On the a=O(N)a=O(\sqrt N) scale it gives a relative quadratic-exponential approximation with error O(N−1/2)O(N^{-1/2}), not an absolute multinomial majorant.

The exact upper projectors remain. The result concerns the lower donor, not its opposite endpoint or a closed contraction. The sole complete proof direct is G, the actual reduced correspondence. All finite-word and analytic estimates are proved below.

6. Capacity, normal, and source scope

Equations (7)–(12) are exact finite polynomial identities. The actual kernel is still (4). Formula (28) is therefore an actual kernel estimate in the untruncated capacity region (5); elsewhere it is an estimate before the original upper projectors. Those projectors can change its value and cannot be estimated by the nonzero unprojected factor.

The result is explicitly a lower-donor theorem. It does not apply when aa is near dd. Even a two-head full-circle product retains a factor of the form 1−uN1-u^N, which can vanish or grow exponentially. No such factor is replaced by a unit here.

The recipient clocks in the original integer source are Bj=QbjB_j=Q^{b_j}. Allowing their coefficients in (3) to vary holomorphically is useful for uniform estimates, but does not construct an exact interpolation in complex occupations. Neither does the auxiliary variable ζ\zeta transport an original contour or a root carry. Fixed-qq meridian derivatives in (28) are of the exact retained finite family; no closed ordinary-color quantum-normal identity is inferred.

All factorial changes of basis, neighboring coupons, Cartan lines, pivots, scalar normalizations and final first-jet source functional must remain in a closed word. The original flow propagation may force additional boundary sectors outside (5). This theorem does not cancel them, prove an endpoint-to-interior source propagation, or infer noncancellation of a cyclic trace. It supplies the stated relative local kernel estimate only.
