The capped Gauss scalar near the complete meridian

23 November 2026. The meridian parameter is scaled as M=exp⁡(v/N)M=\exp(v/\sqrt N).

The actual capped Gauss initializer admits a scalar-relative asymptotic on the meridian scale M=exp⁡(v/N)M=\exp(v/\sqrt N). Its ordinary quantum-dimension zeros are retained and divided removably. The remaining transport factor has an explicit nonzero Gaussian limit, jointly with the original binomial cap on its complex Gaussian activity collar.

The quantum root and the physical Fourier frames are fixed throughout. This is a statement about the specified initialized period, not the original marked knot closing or an arbitrary Conway exterior.
