The actual whole source for the 7/3 five-crossing plat

Research note, 27 October 2026. This derives an exact finite source formula for 5_2 from the same oriented mixed-color four-plat method used for 4_1. All turns, the cut coupon, full Cartan scalar and zero-framing correction are retained. Both the three-index Gaussian/Pochhammer formula and its cyclotomic coefficient transform are proved for the whole meridian family. At completeness the transform gives an explicit two-index formula for the original state.

This is a diagnostic for arbitrary-bridge source transport, not a restriction of the main volume target to the twist family. No broad selected-period or hyperbolic-volume theorem is asserted here.

1. The marked plat and exact framing

Set q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, x=-M/q, Y=M2=Qx2, d=N-1 and xvee=x(-1)Q(-1). Use

beta=sigma_2^2 sigma_1^(-2) sigma_2.          (1.1)

Its action order is sigma_2, sigma_1^(-1), sigma_1^(-1), sigma_2, sigma_2. With the same rational-tangle marking as in the figure-eight proof,

T=[1 1;0 1], U=[1 0;1 1],
T^2 U^2 T (0,1)^t=(7,3)^t.                 (1.2)

This is the two-bridge 7/3 plat, with continued fraction [2,2,1] or [2,3], giving the five-crossing twist-knot diagram 5_2 in the handedness fixed by (1.1). The actual three-braid word used in the earlier prototype is retained as an independent finite evaluation check in the audit; no Jones formula is used.

The top colors are [V(x),V(xvee),V(xvee),V(x)] and their orientations are [+, -, -, +]. Every crossing has positive oriented writhe, so the framing correction is the inverse fifth power of the actual color twist. Put A=alpha, B=2d-alpha, so q^A=x, q^B=xvee. The full mixed Cartan contribution is q((3B2-2AB)/2). Including the twist q(-(5/2)(A2-2dA)) gives exactly

q^(3dB)=xvee^(3d).                           (1.3)

Formula (1.3) is included in the contraction. Omitting either its Cartan or framing part would change the whole meridian identity.

2. The same literal source has different bottom cap types

Use the exact balanced mixed R coefficients and turns from the figure-eight proof. Cut the fourth top leg at index zero. Because this cut is a left pivotal trace, its coupon is D_0=g E_00, and Tr(g^(-1)D_0 C)=C_00. Thus it is the original reduced scalar cut.

The top source indices are

(a,d-a,d,0), 0<=a<N,

and their coefficient, including the two cups and the coupon, is N^2 x^(a-2d). The final colors are now [V(x),V(xvee),V(xvee),V(x)]. Thus the first bottom cap is ev_R(x), while the second is ev_L(x). This differs from the figure-eight contraction and is essential.

If the surviving first output index is b and its dual partner is d-b, then the two cap coefficients are

x^(d-b)g_b/N=x^(-b)Q^(-b)/N,
x^d/N,
g_b=x^(-d)Q^(-b).                            (2.1)

All cap and cup factors are literal finite matrices. No ordinary quantum dimension is divided out, and no separate modified trace is assigned to each bridge.

3. Forced transfer elimination

Write the five transfers in action order as k_1,…,k_5. The first positive crossing has second input index d, so k_1=0. After the next two negative crossings write their transfers as k and ell. The successive states are

(a,d,d-a,0),
(d-k,a+k,d-a,0),
(a+k-ell,d-k+ell,d-a,0),

with

a+k<N, 0<=ell<=k.                            (3.1)

The fourth positive crossing has transfer j<=a. The last bottom cap forces the fifth transfer to be j-a, hence

k_4=a, k_5=0.                               (3.2)

The final state is

(a+k-ell,d-a-k+ell,d,0).

The first cap relation follows automatically. Thus the exact surviving routes are (0,k,ell,a,0), with support (3.1). This elimination uses only the actual unwrapped finite support.

Set j=k-ell, n=a+k and m=a+j=n-ell. The whole boundary factor from the cups, cap types in (2.1), and the coupon is

x^(-d-j) Q^(-a-j).

Multiplying by the five mixed crossing entries and (1.3), then reflecting the two terminal Gaussian binomials, gives

W_(a,k,ell)=(-1)^ell Y^(2d-2n+ell)
  Q^(a(k+1)+k^2+2k+2-ell*j-ell(ell+1)/2)
  {n choose k}_Q {k choose ell}_Q
  (Q/Y;Q)_k (YQ^(-n);Q)_ell
  (Q^(j+1)/Y;Q)_a.                          (3.3)

The reflection identities used here are

{d choose a}_Q=(-1)^a Q^(-a(a+1)/2),
{d-j choose ell}_Q
    =(-1)^ell Q^(-ell*j-ell(ell+1)/2)
                                  {j+ell choose ell}_Q.

They hold because Q has primitive order N and all upper indices involved are less than N.

4. An exact whole-source Gaussian formula

Invert the ell factors of (YQ^(-n);Q)_ell in (3.3):

(YQ^(-n);Q)_ell
   =(-Y)^ell Q^(-n*ell+ell(ell-1)/2)
                          (Q^(m+1)/Y;Q)_ell.

The signs cancel. The remaining products and binomials simplify by

{n choose k}_Q {k choose ell}_Q
               ={m+ell choose ell}_Q {m choose j}_Q,

(Q/Y;Q)_k (Q^(j+1)/Y;Q)_a (Q^(m+1)/Y;Q)_ell
               =(Q/Y;Q)_(m+ell)(Q^(j+1)/Y;Q)_ell.

Their monomial exponent becomes j(m+1)+m+ell+2. We obtain the following proved identity for the actual reduced whole period:

F_(5_2),N(Y)
  =sum_(m,ell>=0,m+ell<N) sum_(j=0)^m
      Y^(2(N-1-m)) Q^(j(m+1)+m+ell+2)
      {m+ell choose ell}_Q {m choose j}_Q
      (Q/Y;Q)_(m+ell)(Q^(j+1)/Y;Q)_ell.     (4.1)

Every factor is finite. This is a Laurent-polynomial identity before setting Y=1 or Y^N=1. In particular the displayed Y^(2(N-1-m)) is retained, including all of its meridian jets. The exact original full ADO factor d_N(M), if desired, multiplies both sides as its unchanged whole germ.

At N=2 the formula gives, exactly,

F_(5_2),2(Y)=-2Y-3-2/Y,
<5_2>_2=-7.

The sign is fixed by the above framing and source, rather than chosen from the knot determinant.

5. Reciprocal Newton space and two proved boundary rows

Formula (4.1) has minimum Y exponent at least -d, term by term: the minimum in a term is 2d-3m-2ell, and m+ell<=d. The already proved actual Weyl symmetry F(Y)=F(Y^(-1)) therefore bounds the maximum exponent by d as well. Consequently there are unique coefficients C_n^(N), 0<=n<=d, in

F_N(Y)=sum_(n=0)^d C_n^(N) T_n(Y),
T_n(Y)=product_(r=1)^n(Y+Y^(-1)-Q^r-Q^(-r)). (5.1)

This is a statement about whole reciprocal Laurent polynomials, not interpolation of a few root values. The T_n are monic Newton polynomials of degree n in Y+Y^(-1), including at a root Q.

The actual anchor at Y=Q gives C_0^(N)=1. The coefficient of Y^(-d) in (4.1) comes only from m=d,ell=0. Since (-1)^d Q^(d(d+1)/2)=1, the top row is exactly

C_d^(N)=Q sum_(j=0)^d {d choose j}_Q.         (5.2)

There is a useful source-basis simplification valid at every n:

(1-Y)T_n(Y)=-Y^(n+1)(Q^(-n)/Y;Q)_(2n+1).   (5.3)

Thus the normal source has one contiguous Pochhammer rather than two separated products. Equation (5.3) follows by inverting (Y;Q)_(n+1) in (1-Y)T_n and joining its factors with (Q/Y;Q)_n. It may be useful when transporting sources through arbitrary bridge convolutions. It does not itself prove the remaining coefficient transform.

6. The proved whole-source cyclotomic transform

For every primitive Nth root Q, every N>=2, and arbitrary nonzero Y, the exact simplification is

C_n(Q)=(-1)^n Q^(-n(n+3)/2)
                sum_(j=0)^n Q^(-j(n+1)){n choose j}_Q,
F_N(Y) =sum_(n=0)^(N-1) C_n(Q) T_n(Y).     (6.1)

Equivalently its inner summand is Q^(-j(j+1)){n choose j}_(Q^(-1)). The direct finite proof starts from (4.1), applies an elementary terminating three-factor q-Chu transform and q-Vandermonde, and then uses a generic Newton connection and a finite root Rogers-polynomial convolution. Every parity sign and source factor is retained. The proof is a Laurent-polynomial identity in Y; repeated ordinary-color nodes are not used as interpolation data. Thus it includes all fixed-root meridian jets and the normal complete-color source.

At Y=1, use

T_n(1)=(-1)^n Q^(-n(n+1)/2)(Q;Q)_n^2.

Writing F_n=(Q;Q)_n, the original reduced Kashaev state is therefore

<5_2>_N=sum_(0<=j<=n<N)
      Q^[-n(n+2)-j(n+1)] F_n^3/[F_j F_(n-j)]. (6.2)

This is the exact double sum used by the source-preserving inner quadrature. Neither (6.1) nor (6.2) asserts saddle selection, a noncancellation estimate, or a volume limit. The full modified-dimension function remains its original separate factor when the full ADO period is used.

7. Independent checks

The literal-path audit starts with the original E/Cartan mixed crossing entries, the two exact cups, the fourth-leg coupon, and the differently oriented bottom caps. It sums all paths and checks the forced route pattern and each surviving weight in (4.1). Twenty-seven whole contractions at levels 2 through 10 and three meridians per level pass; eighteen are also compared with the original six-crossing three-braid cut. The audit checks (1.2)–(1.3), the normal-basis product identity, the top Newton row, the proved cyclotomic values and (6.2).

The uniform proof of (4.1) is the source and transfer calculation in Sections 1–4. The proof of (6.1) is the separate finite transform, whose audit also compares whole-Y Laurent coefficients and checks the reusable generic and root convolution identities. The finite checks validate the formulas; they do not establish their all-level scope.
