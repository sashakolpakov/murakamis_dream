Independent audit of the Gauss-wall finite current map

27 October 2026. Both the actual wall/cut-current theorem and the elevated-source restrictions pass after the explicit raw-to-matrix factor NN is retained. This audit checks their finite identities and scope; it supplies no internal Gaussian source comparison.

3. The elevated source restrictions are exact

Its row has binomial multiplicities and gives the desired BNB_N with the stated extra factor 1/N1/N, not 1/(N+1)1/(N+1). Irreducibility in characteristic zero proves the claimed obstruction to any nonzero full-SL2 intertwiner into this space or a subquotient with the same bound.
