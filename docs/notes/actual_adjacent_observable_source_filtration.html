The genuine adjacent observable algebra and its macroscopic source filtration

29 October 2026. Two ACTUAL adjacent interval Casimirs already generate the full matrix algebra on the complete three-head source multiplicity. This narrows the older arbitrary-coupon quotient obstruction to a small physical moment-map algebra. Its proof is constructive and retains the original nilpotent normal source.

The associated filtration is equally important: bounded-degree source tests see zero until an integer traversal of length N-1. A single original crossing has precisely that macroscopic observable degree. Thus full matrix generation does not disprove a filtered or microlocal geometric reduction, and low-degree source-null identities do not by themselves control its norm.

1. Original matrices, sources and charts

Let N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi*i/N), Q=q^2, x=q^d, rho=q-q^(-1). On the COMPLETE original W=(V tensor V)_(degree d), use e_j=|j,d-j>. The actual interval Casimirs A=Omega12 and B=Omega23, in the left highest chart, are

Aj,j=q+q−1−q(1−Qj)2−q−1(1−Qj+1)2,Aj−1,j=Aj,j−1=−(1−Qj)2, A_{j,j}=q+q^{-1}-q(1-Q^j)^2-q^{-1}(1-Q^{j+1})^2, \qquad A_{j-1,j}=A_{j,j-1}=-(1-Q^j)^2, \tag{1} Bej=cjej+bjej−1,cj=q2j+1+q−2j−1,bj=Qj+Q−j−2. Be_j=c_je_j+b_je_{j-1},\qquad c_j=q^{2j+1}+q^{-2j-1},\quad b_j=Q^j+Q^{-j}-2. \tag{2}

All off-diagonal coefficients in (1) are nonzero. Put c*=q+q^(-1). The original cup and ordinary cap are

pj=Nxj−d,ℓj=x−jQ−j/N=xj/N. p_j=Nx^{j-d},\qquad \ell_j=x^{-j}Q^{-j}/N=x^j/N.

The complete anti-Pascal chart J satisfies the exact source identities

JB=AJ,JAJ−1=B𝖳,Jp=Nx−ded,ℓJ−1=(xd/N)e0𝖳. JB=AJ,\quad JAJ^{-1}=B^{\mathsf T},\quad Jp=Nx^{-d}e_d,\quad \ell J^{-1}=(x^d/N)e_0^{\mathsf T}. \tag{3}

These are the original coproduct and actual cap identities from the complete source theorem, not a semisimple replacement. In particular all N multiplicities, both members of every colliding Casimir pair, and even levels remain.

2. A tame nilpotent source operator generates all channels

The monic characteristic polynomial of A is

𝒫N(z)=∏j=0d(z−cj)=2TN(z/2)+2. \mathcal P_N(z)=\prod_{j=0}^d(z-c_j)=2T_N(z/2)+2.

Its roots have multiplicity two, except the simple root -2 when N is odd. At N=2 it is z^2, so the following construction also applies. The irreducible Jacobi matrix A has cyclic endpoint vectors; hence its minimal polynomial is this full degree-N polynomial.

Set Q0(z)=P_N(z)/(z-c*)^2. Define the explicit degree-d observable

Ξ0(A)=(A−c*)Q0(A)Q0(c*). \Xi_0(A)=\frac{(A-c*)Q_0(A)}{Q_0(c*)}.

Then

Ξ0(A)=θpℓ,θ=(−1)Nρ2≠0. \boxed{\displaystyle \Xi_0(A)=\theta p\ell,\qquad \theta=(-1)^N\rho^2\ne0.} \tag{4}

Here is a proof not assuming a Jordan basis. At all other Casimir nodes the polynomial has the full required vanishing multiplicity. At c* it has value 0 and derivative 1. The eigenspace of an irreducible Jacobi matrix is one-dimensional, so its range is the original eigenline Cp and its row is the original eigenrow Cell. The coefficient is fixed by its (0,d) entry. Lower matrix powers cannot connect d to 0, and

(Ad)0,d=∏j=1d[−(1−Qj)2]=(−1)dN2. (A^d)_{0,d}=\prod_{j=1}^d[-(1-Q^j)^2]=(-1)^dN^2.

The elementary Chebyshev differential equation at the double zero gives Q0(c*)=N2/(4-c*2)=-N2/rho2. Since p0 ell_d=1, the coefficient in (4) is theta. This proves its normalization, including the exceptional N=2 case.

In the original positive physical norm,

∥Ξ0(A)∥op=N|ρ|2≤4π2/N. \|\Xi_0(A)\|_{\mathrm{op}}=N|\rho|^2\leq4\pi^2/N. \tag{5}

Indeed ||p||2=N3 and ||ell||^2=1/N. This rank-one normal source is polynomially controlled; it is not an exponentially conditioned projector. It is nonzero even though it is square-zero, because ell p=0.

For 0<=r,s<=d,

BrΞ0(A)Bs=θ(Brp)(ℓBs). B^r\Xi_0(A)B^s=\theta(B^rp)(\ell B^s). \tag{6}

By (3), the columns B^r p are a basis: their J images are a nonzero constant times A^r e_d, which are triangular endpoint Krylov columns. The rows ell B^s are also a basis, since their products with J^(-1) are a nonzero constant times e0^T A^s. Therefore the N^2 operators in (6) are a basis of End(W). In particular

ℂ⟨A,B⟩=Mat⁡N(ℂ). \boxed{\displaystyle \mathbb C\langle A,B\rangle =\operatorname{Mat}_N(\mathbb C).} \tag{7}

No arbitrary Borel-relative matrix coupon is needed for (7): A and B are the two actual adjacent interval observables.

3. Consequence for an exact positive or invariant quotient

Suppose a linear quotient q0:W->H is compatible with these two observables: q0 A=A’ q0 and q0 B=B’ q0. Its kernel is invariant under (7), and is therefore either 0 or all of W. If it preserves the original source and its inserted observable values it cannot be the zero quotient, since

ℓBdp=(−1)dN2≠0. \ell B^d p=(-1)^dN^2\ne0. \tag{8}

Thus it is injective. Equivalently, a positive semidefinite packet Gram whose nullspace is invariant under BOTH actual observables cannot remove a source channel: its nullspace is 0 or it kills the entire original source representation.

A multiplicative quotient imposing a nonzero relation on this actual observable algebra is likewise impossible while retaining all source insertions. In particular setting the rank-one normal (4) to zero generates the whole matrix ideal by (6). This is a restriction on an EXACT observable-compatible quotient, not a no-go theorem for a nonmultiplicative Ward pushforward, a current-complex reduction, or a geometric comparison after taking semiclassical asymptotics. Nor does it preclude a faithful embedding of W into a larger positive angle space satisfying additional edge constraints. Such an embedding need not be a smaller quotient of this observable representation.

The same statement applies to a reflected double IF both copies of the actual observable algebra are retained independently: (7) then gives the full matrix algebra on W tensor conjugate(W). It is not asserted for a construction that retains only synchronous diagonal braid action and no independent copy observables.

4. The exact source filtration: degree N-1 is essential

Define

ℱm=span⁡{p,Bp,…,Bmp},0≤m≤d. \mathcal F_m=\operatorname{span}\{p,Bp,\ldots,B^mp\}, \qquad0\leq m\leq d.

The endpoint Jacobi calculation and (3) give EXACTLY

Jℱm=span⁡{ed−m,…,ed},dim⁡ℱm=m+1,Aℱm⊂ℱm,Bℱm⊂ℱm+1. J\mathcal F_m=\operatorname{span}\{e_{d-m},\ldots,e_d\}, \quad\dim\mathcal F_m=m+1, \quad A\mathcal F_m\subset\mathcal F_m, \quad B\mathcal F_m\subset\mathcal F_{m+1}. \tag{9}

The A statement uses JAJ(-1)=BT, which is LOWER bidiagonal, and therefore preserves the displayed terminal coordinate subspace. Also

ℓ|ℱd−1=0,ℓBmp=0(m<d),ℓBdp=(−1)dN2. \ell|_{\mathcal F_{d-1}}=0,\qquad \ell B^m p=0\ (m<d),\qquad \ell B^d p=(-1)^dN^2. \tag{10}

More generally EVERY mixed word with fewer than d occurrences of B has zero original pairing ell w(A,B)p, independently of the number of A factors. In the J chart each B can lower the integer index by at most one, each A preserves or raises it, and the source travels from d to 0. Thus there is no cyclic shortcut.

At the first detected degree the complete associated-graded source is explicit. For any nonnegative integers a0,…,ad,

ℓAa0BAa1B⋯BAadp=(−1)dN2∏j=0dcjaj. \boxed{\displaystyle \ell A^{a_0}B A^{a_1}B\cdots B A^{a_d}p =(-1)^dN^2\prod_{j=0}^d c_j^{a_j}.} \tag{11}

With exactly d lowering opportunities, every B must use its lowering Jacobi entry and every A its diagonal entry. This proves (11) as a unique finite path, with all original source constants cancelling as (Nx(-d))(xd/N)=1. In particular, for N>=3 and 1<=r<=d-1,

ℓBd−r(A−c*)Brp=(−1)dN2(cr−c*)≠0. \ell B^{d-r}(A-c*)B^rp =(-1)^dN^2(c_r-c*)\ne0. \tag{12}

The local cup relation (A-c*)p=0 therefore cannot be reimposed after an arbitrary legitimate exterior. Its first detectable exterior has macroscopic observable degree, not bounded degree. For the selected normal (4), the exact factorization (6) implies that an exterior on BOTH sides needs at least d B occurrences on each side before the original scalar can detect it.

Its retained response is nevertheless explicitly local, rather than an unspecified lost normal direction. For ANY allowed exteriors U,V,

ℓUΞ0(A)Vp=θ(ℓUp)(ℓVp). \ell U\Xi_0(A)Vp=\theta(\ell Up)(\ell Vp). \tag{12a}

This is the actual cup–cap rank-one surgery (4), with its known normalization; it does not define a metric from an unknown closed period. The individual factors still require their own estimates. With r such normals the exact correction is ℓU0Ξ0U1⋯Ξ0Urp=θr∏j=0r(ℓUjp)\ell U_0\Xi_0U_1\cdots\Xi_0U_rp =\theta^r\prod_{j=0}^r(\ell U_jp). All factors are in the SAME original cap/cup normalization; converting them to separately normalized knot periods retains their individual known external lines. No inequality between their volumes is implied.

5. A fixed braid already reaches the macroscopic degree

This degree distinction is not permission to ignore it for fixed knot diagrams. The actual adjacent crossing is R23=kappa r_N(B), a polynomial of degree<N. Its coefficient at B^d has EXACT modulus N^(-2):

[Bd]R23=κ(−1)dx−dN2. \boxed{\displaystyle [B^d]R_{23}=\frac{\kappa(-1)^d x^{-d}}{N^2}.} \tag{13}

To prove it directly from the original crossing, highest-chart extraction forces one finite transfer and gives (R23 p)j=kappa p_j F(d-j). Therefore

ℓR23p=κx−d∑k=0dQk+1Fk=κx−d. \ell R_{23}p=\kappa x^{-d} \sum_{k=0}^d Q^{k+1}F_k=\kappa x^{-d}. \tag{14}

The last sum telescopes, Q^(k+1)F_k=F_k-F_(k+1), with F_N=0. All lower polynomial degrees pair to zero by (10), proving (13). In particular the original crossing really has degree d, at every level, and its first detected source coefficient is polynomially large rather than exponentially suppressed.

A fixed number of actual crossings can therefore expose the normal even though low-degree observable tests cannot. From (12a),(14),

ℓR23Ξ0(A)R23p=θκ2x−2d=θx−d≠0, \ell R_{23}\Xi_0(A)R_{23}p =\theta\kappa^2x^{-2d}=\theta x^{-d}\ne0, \tag{14a}

of exact modulus |rho|^2. This is a polynomial-sized normal current between TWO original crossings. It must not be set to zero in an exact source identity, but it is compatible with a filtered construction that explicitly retains and estimates polynomial normal counterterms. These statements concern the specified complete source sector; no rank-one formula is asserted on an unrestricted larger multiplicity space without its actual charge projector.

For any polynomial f of degree<d, (14) also gives the elementary operator-norm separation

∥R23−f(B)∥op≥1/N, \|R_{23}-f(B)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\geq1/N, \tag{15}

since the original source has dual product ||ell|| ||p||=N. This does not preclude a polynomial-error approximation at a specified order or an exponentially normalized upper comparison. It does show that fixed-degree truncation does not preserve even the first nonzero original source value automatically.

The viable filtered alternative must therefore state how its fixed-degree Ward equations act on these degree-N crossing packets, including the retained normal source and the norm of the resulting counterterms. Low-degree tests against the bare cup are identically zero, so they cannot alone certify a nonzero geometric component or a polynomial-cost positive reduction. No sharp global energy estimate or obstruction to a properly controlled microlocal quotient follows from (7) alone.

6. Original braid algebra at prime levels

For completeness there is an actual-word strengthening along every prime level. At an odd prime N, the nodal eigenvalues nu_j=(-1)^j q1 coincide only at the paired indices j+l=d. Indeed equality implies N divides (j-l)(j+l+1); for distinct indices primality forces j+l+1=N. At every double Casimir node r_N’ is nonzero. Finite Hermite interpolation therefore recovers A as a polynomial in R12, and B as the same polynomial in R23. Hence

ℂ⟨R12,R23⟩=Mat⁡Nfor every prime N. \mathbb C\langle R_{12},R_{23}\rangle=\operatorname{Mat}_N \quad\text{for every prime }N. \tag{16}

N=2 has a single double node and nonzero derivative, so it also satisfies (16). For composite levels additional braid-phase coincidences can occur; (16) is not asserted there. The all-level observable statement (7) has no such restriction. The interpolation degrees and inverse norms in (16) are not claimed uniform or tame.

It does not infer a geometric norm from algebra dimension or from a finite numerical rank fit.

The finite audit checks twelve literal coproduct/source modules at N=2,…,12,14, every rank-one observable generator and strict source flag, the retained normal between two actual crossings, and actual-braid recovery at the tested prime levels.
