Exact Green responses and an original MAIN-network instability certificate

31 October 2026. analytical research continuation of the constrained-Hessian reduction. No universal instability, globally realized stable counterexample, or absolute source/volume bound is claimed.

1. Scope and the exact Green response

Use the actual full protected-zero transport fiber and the classified stationary data of the constrained-Hessian reduction. Each ordinary face has demand one. Local rows have supply one and positive load on every ordinary incidence. Complete CROSS spectra are held at zero; strict Green spectra vary; residual cap spectra are held at 1/2. For a constrained non-CROSS maximum retain the inherited high-value hypothesis. Its strict overload is automatic by the closed CROSS comparison and seam-gap theorem. No enlarged angle fiber is used here.

Let v denote local row-simplex coordinates, K the positive fixed-spectrum row stiffness, and B the row/spectral derivative. The preferred local row displacement of a Green under a unit spectral change is K_e^{-1}B_e. Let r_e be its vector of original face-load changes.

For LEFT, write A=x+y, P=xy, z=sin^2(pi delta), D_0=A^2-4Pz and C_0=A+2bz. At a strict critical Green,

(c-b)z=A,  Delta=sqrt(C_0^2-D_0)>0.

The original normalized row-optimized derivative is

partial_delta F=log(T_c/E_b),
E_b=C_0+sqrt(C_0^2-D_0),
T_c=2cz-A+sqrt((2cz-A)^2-D_0).

Differentiate this identity in the logarithm of each actual face weight, with delta fixed. At T_c=E_b, the common-discriminant derivatives cancel. The gradient of F in log weights is the original row allocation, so mixed derivatives give exactly

r_e=(2/Delta)[zc e_c-zb e_b-x e_x-y e_y],          (1)
W_e=(2/Delta)(c-b) pi sin(2pi delta)>0.           (2)

Here e_f denotes the coordinate of an ordinary face, and a protected zero-weight term is absent. The derivative commutation is on the strict smooth stationary branch; it is not a coefficient-limit argument. For RIGHT reverse the original middle orientation and use its overloaded face as c in (1).

The coefficients in (1) sum to zero by (c-b)z=A. Exactly one is positive, at the overloaded child c; every supported input coefficient is negative. Moreover L_c>L_b+L_x+L_y, so every such input has STRICTLY smaller weight than the child. The actual response is thus a directed redistribution from the three (or two) supported inputs toward its original overloaded middle. No sign/phase compatibility has been assumed.

2. Independence survives actual cap elimination

Distinct Green ports have distinct overloaded children: two loads strictly greater than 1/2 cannot occupy the same demand-one face. With no caps, the response columns (1) are therefore linearly independent. Indeed choose a nonzero coefficient in a putative relation whose child has maximal common weight. At that child coordinate, no other selected column can have a negative entry: such an entry would require an even larger child. No other column has that positive child. Its coefficient must vanish, a contradiction.

The same proof survives residual caps. Form the finite multigraph on ordinary faces whose edges are the actual residual cap rows. A cap has two distinct ordinary faces, equal common weights, and a load greater than 1/2 at its child. Orient its edge toward that child. Demand one implies indegree at most one. A Green child has cap indegree zero.

A connected cap component on n vertices has at least n-1 edges. Since every edge contributes one to total indegree and no indegree exceeds one, the component has at most one indegree-zero vertex. It therefore contains at most one Green child. This argument allows parallel edges and does not assume the component is a tree. A cyclic component has no Green child.

Let U sum the original face loads in each cap component (isolated faces are components). The span of actual cap row displacements is exactly ker U. Hence U is an exact set of quotient face constraints after the zero-action cap degrees are eliminated. All weights in a component are equal. The column U r_e has one positive child component, distinct for different Green ports, and negative entries only at strictly lower weight components. The same maximal-child argument proves

the columns of R_bar=(U r_e)_e are independent.    (3)

This agrees with the orthogonal projection P onto ker(T_cap^T) used in the earlier note: U and P encode the same quotient, and their response columns have the same kernel. Consequently no nonzero spectral combination can cancel its entire preferred-load defect for free, even after actual cap displacements. This is an injectivity obstruction to one proposed whole-circuit shortcut, NOT a sign theorem for S=W-R^T C^dagger R. A positive repair cost may still be smaller than W.

3. Build the repair network from actual supported MAIN edges

For a noncap spectator port p and a MAIN edge fg in one of its two original triangles, suppose f,g are ordinary. Let v_{p,fg} be the unique local row-coordinate vector whose original incidence displacement is +1 at f, -1 at g and zero at its other incidences. Define

kappa_{p,fg}=v_{p,fg}^T K_p v_{p,fg}>0.          (4)

This is an actual admissible infinitesimal row displacement; positive ordinary loads permit both signs for sufficiently small magnitude. It is evaluated with that spectator’s spectrum fixed. There is no independent face torus and no change in the protected targets.

Choose nonnegative numbers tau_{p,fg} with

sum_(selected MAIN edges fg at p) tau_{p,fg}<=1. (5)

If the same port is encountered on several boundary edges, it is still counted only once in (5). Put d_{fg}=U(e_f-e_g), and form the ordinary quotient-graph Laplacian

L_net=sum_(p,fg) [tau_{p,fg}/kappa_{p,fg}]
                                  d_{fg} d_{fg}^T. (6)

Edges collapsed by actual caps have d_{fg}=0 and need not be included. In terms of the original face map A_p on port p, the elementary rank-one Cauchy inequality is

U A_p K_p^{-1} A_p^T U^T
       >= d_{fg}d_{fg}^T/kappa_{p,fg}.

Taking the convex combination (5), then summing ports, gives

C>=L_net,                                      (7)

where C is the exact face compliance in these quotient coordinates. This is why one cannot simply add every edge’s full conductance when several of them use the same coupon.

The support of each Green r_e is connected by a network of this kind using only OTHER ports. The four original MAIN boundary edges of its quartet form a cycle. A strict Green has at most one protected incident face: an overloaded protected face vanishes, two protected outers are forbidden by the actual mark, and the remaining double-protected input case is excluded by the stationary classification. Deleting its possible protected vertex leaves a connected three-vertex MAIN path; with no protected vertex the original cycle remains. Each of these MAIN edges has its other incident triangle at a port different from e. The spectator ports need NOT be pairwise distinct across those edges. If the other port is a cap, that edge is already collapsed at zero cost. Otherwise (4) provides a finite positive conductance. Assign, for example, equal total weight one among the finitely many selected edges at each spectator. The resulting path/cycle connects the supported quotient faces of r_e.

It follows that U r_e belongs to range(L_net). The same holds for any linear combination of columns if the chosen network contains such a path/cycle for every selected Green. Thus the network energies below are finite and concern the original target defect, not a guessed unconstrained electrical matrix.

4. A concrete general instability certificate

For r in range(L_net), the variational formula for resistance and (7) give

r^T C^dagger r <= r^T L_net^dagger r.            (8)

Indeed each side is the supremum over quotient face potentials p of 2p^T r-p^T C p, or respectively 2p^T r-p^T L_net p. Redundant total-load constraints cause no problem because r is in the indicated range.

Consequently, if there is a spectral vector s such that

(R_bar s)^T L_net^dagger (R_bar s)
                                  < sum_e W_e s_e^2, (9)

then the ACTUAL constrained Hessian has a positive direction and the stationary datum is not a maximum. Equivalently it suffices that

W-R_bar^T L_net^dagger R_bar

have a positive eigenvalue. All coefficients of this sufficient test are explicit local derivatives, shared weights and original MAIN incidences; its proof is independent of the diagram’s width.

There is a useful one-port version: build the network from spectators of one chosen Green and test its r_e against W_e. Holding that Green at its preferred row response and repairing only through these spectators is an actual feasible variation. If its repair energy is less than W_e, the variation has positive second derivative. The full network version may be stronger because it permits distributed repair and a simultaneous choice of several spectral directions.

What remains OPEN is whether common-weight dominance and the exact unit face demands ALWAYS force (9), or the corresponding sharper test with C, at a high-value non-CROSS stationary configuration. The response independence (3) specifically prevents proving this by setting the preferred defect equal to zero. The earlier forced MAIN-cycle example specifically prevents proving it by assigning a positive curvature to each elementary odd cycle. No globally feasible stable stationary counterexample has been obtained here.

5. The maximal child component

Use the classified common-weight stationary data and exact cap quotient of sections 1–2 above. Let a Green port e have maximal overloaded-child weight among ALL Green ports. Let S be the connected cap component containing its child c; an isolated ordinary face is permitted. All weights on S agree.

Every other noncap port incident to S is CROSS. Indeed another Green cannot have its child in S, by the already proved at-most-one-child count. If one of its inputs were in S, its child would have strictly larger common weight, contradicting the choice of e. The chosen Green itself has no input in S, because each input weight is strictly smaller than its child weight.

The component S is automatically a tree; no assertion about other cap components is needed. Orient each cap edge toward its greater-than-one- half load. Every vertex has indegree at most one, while c has indegree zero because its Green load already exceeds one half. If S has n vertices and m cap edges, then m<=n-1. Connectedness gives m>=n-1. Thus m=n-1, including the isolated-vertex case. Parallel edges have not been discarded; a component containing such a cycle cannot contain c.

Its ordinary demands total n. Its n-1 internal cap rows each supply one unit entirely to S. The remaining noncap supply into S is therefore exactly one. Consequently

sum of all CROSS incidence loads in S = 1-q_(e,c) < 1/2.       (10)

This is an exact original-target identity, not a collapsed-source interpretation.

6. Exact triangle compliance

A complete triangle has loads alpha_i>=0, sum alpha_i=1/2, and entropy C_tri=sum_i f(alpha_i), where -f’’(a)=2pi/sin(2pi a). For positive weights w_i its unique weighted stationary loads are

alpha_i = (1/pi) atan(w_i/T),
T=sqrt(w_1 w_2+w_1 w_3+w_2 w_3).                            (11)

Let s_i=sin(2pi alpha_i). Differentiating its constrained Legendre problem, or inverting the diagonal stiffness on sum d alpha_i=0, gives the exact Hessian in logarithmic weights:

C_tri = (1/(2pi)) [diag(s)-s s^T/(sum_i s_i)].               (12)

Thus it is the three-vertex graph Laplacian with conductances

g_ij = s_i s_j/[2pi sum_r s_r]
     = w_i w_j/[2pi(w_i+w_j)T].                           (13)

For the second equality use

s_i=2w_i T/[(w_i+w_j)(w_i+w_k)],
sum_i s_i=4T^3/[(w_1+w_2)(w_1+w_3)(w_2+w_3)].

One protected-zero weight is the literal limit of (12)–(13). A half with only one positive weight has fixed load 1/2 and zero compliance; the indeterminate all-zero expression in (12) is not used for that half. A complete CROSS port contributes the sum of its two actual triangle matrices, pushed to its four original incidence coordinates. Shared outer coordinates are added, not treated as independent faces.

For any subset I of a triangle’s faces, its component-charge diagonal is

1_I^T C_tri 1_I
  = (1/(2pi)) [s_I-s_I^2/(sum_i s_i)]
  <= sum_(i in I) s_i/(2pi)
  <= sum_(i in I) alpha_i.                                (14)

This includes I empty or the whole triangle and all permitted protected limits. The last inequality is sin(2pi a)<=2pi a on [0,1/2].

Let C_sp be the exact face compliance from all noncap spectator ports, written in the component-sum coordinates U of section 2. At S all such incidences are CROSS. Equations (10) and (14) give

(C_sp)_(S,S) <= 1-q_(e,c) < 1/2.                          (15)

Other spectators may be Green away from S; they contribute zero to this diagonal because they have no incidence in S.

7. The resulting lower bound on spectator repair cost

Hold e at its preferred row response r_e under a unit spectral change, and repair the original face targets only through spectators and the zero-cost cap rows. Write r_bar=U r_e. If repair is possible, the exact minimum cost is r_bar^T C_sp^dagger r_bar. Its dual variational formula, restricted to potentials supported at S, gives

r_bar^T C_sp^dagger r_bar
    >= r_bar_S^2/(C_sp)_(S,S)
    >= r_(e,c)^2/[1-q_(e,c)].                              (16)

If the relevant diagonal vanishes while r_bar_S is nonzero, repair is impossible; this strengthens, rather than reverses, the obstruction. The exact response has

r_(e,c)=2 z c/Delta,
W_e=2(c-b)pi sin(2pi delta)/Delta,
z=sin^2(pi delta),
Delta=sqrt((x+y+2bz)^2-[(x+y)^2-4xyz]).

At the protected-outer local datum

(mu,nu,delta)=(1/6,1/3,1/4), (x,b,c,y)=(1,1,3,0),

these are q_(e,c)=2/3, r_(e,c)=sqrt(3), W_e=4pi/sqrt(3). If this datum occurs with the maximal-child hypotheses above, every spectator-only repair therefore costs at least

3/(1/3)=9 > 4pi/sqrt(3)=W_e.                              (17)

The strict inequality is elementary, for example from pi<22/7. Thus the one-port preferred-response/spectator-network criterion cannot prove instability for ANY embedding satisfying those local and maximal-child assumptions. No such global embedding is asserted here. The full Schur complement also allows e’s own row response to change, and may combine several spectral directions. Equations (15)–(17) give no sign theorem for that full matrix.

8. What comparison with the geometric weights does establish

Let L^G be the complete marked geometric weights and let ordinary rho_f=L_f/L_f^G. At a face f maximizing rho, every incident COMPLETE triangle contributes at least its geometric load at f. This follows directly from (11): every neighbor ratio w_j/w_f is no larger than its geometric counterpart, so T/w_f is no larger. Equality is possible only when all supported neighboring ratios agree (apart from a fixed one-positive-weight monomial half).

This is a valid triangle maximum principle. It is not yet a maximum principle for the full Green/CROSS source. A Green input can have a strict load deficit compared with its complete triangle allocation; for instance the datum in section 7 has input-middle load 1/6 while the complete same-weight load is 1/4. Its strictly larger child weight does not imply a larger ratio rho_c: the geometric denominator L_c^G is different. The present argument therefore does not propagate a ratio maximum from a deficient Green input to a still larger ratio. Any such propagation or full-Hessian inequality needs an additional global identity, not just the absolute child-weight ordering.

No numerical search or source asymptotic claim enters this addendum.

Internal validation and remaining scope

The network gives a conditional spectator-repair test. No universal inequality for the full constrained Hessian follows from it.

The original MAIN/MATCHING incidence used here is that of the certified augmented plat dictionary. The odd MAIN-cycle theorem remains a qualitative phase obstruction; it is not substituted for (9). The universal resistance comparison and any global stable counterexample remain open.
