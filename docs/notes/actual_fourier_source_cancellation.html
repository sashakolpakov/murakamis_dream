An actual zero-source Fourier current retains a geometric formal block

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. The last Fourier-label source has an explicit, uniformly tame cyclic current complex. A literal Cartan port current of the ORIGINAL knot tensor lies in its zero-source subspace, retains the actual highest-input one-sided support, and on the proved regular geometric chart has a NONZERO formal block with the SAME regulator action. Thus nonzero geometric partial support, root scalarity, the pointed boundary line and one-sided support do not by themselves prove survival in the selected sum.

This is an actual current/source obstruction, not a replacement state or a proposed positive model. The geometric formal assertion below uses the regular good-A chart; it does not assert that chart extends to the highest-input boundary A=1.

1. A literal whole Cartan current has zero selected source

Let T_(c,d;a,b) be the original two-cut tensor, with every original cap, pivot, scalar and peripheral line retained. Put B=diag(Q^b) on the SECOND physical wire and define

W(T)=[(I tensor B)T(I tensor B^-1)-T]/(Q-1).   (1)

Its literal band coefficient is

W(T)_(c,b+q;a,b)=[q]_Q T_(c,b+q;a,b),
[q]_Q=(Q^q-1)/(Q-1).                         (2)

For every integer q this is a Laurent polynomial in Q, including negative q; it has a regular Q=1 continuation. Formula (1) is a specified actual position/Cartan PORT current. It makes no assumption about an untwisted total Hopf action on a reshaped tensor.

Partial trace on the second identity wire is invariant under this conjugation. Therefore the ENTIRE reclosed first-wire matrix obeys

ptr_second W(T)=0                             (3)

as a WHOLE finite-matrix identity, including all q-normal derivatives. No root scalarity is used in (3). The first input/output source, highest vector, Whittaker row and external dimension line remain exactly the originals.

The multiplier (2) changes no index or support. If T is charge zero and the first input is a, its band interval is [a-(N-1),a]. At the highest input a=0 it remains the original nonpositive interval. In particular the actual endpoint defects 1-Q^N and M-M^-1 of the highest boundary have not been discarded or replaced by cyclic off-root formulas.

2. The exact root Fourier current complex

Let J_j be the RAW Fourier partial array with fixed first input, including its inverse-Fourier 1/N. Its selected source is Sigma J, where Sigma J=sum_j J_j, with NO outside 1/N. At Q=Q_N the actual Fourier transform sends (1) to

J_W,j=(J_(j+1)-J_j)/(Q_N-1),                  (4)

with cyclic j modulo N. This follows from (2) and Q_N^(Nq)=1, or by the actual cyclic companion matrix at the root. Thus Sigma J_W=0, in agreement with the stronger whole identity (3).

Put D=(S-I)/(Q_N-1), S J_j=J_(j+1), and P J=(Sigma J/N)1. Over the root coefficient field,

ker D=constant vectors, im D=ker Sigma.        (5)

There is an explicit centered inverse h on the zero-source space. For f put g=f-Pf and

(h f)_j=(Q_N-1){sum_(k<j)g_k
                  -(1/N)sum_(ell=0)^(N-1)sum_(k<ell)g_k}.

Direct telescoping, including the cyclic last endpoint, proves

D h=h D=I-P,   h P=0, Sigma h=0.              (6)

This is a strict finite cyclic source/current contraction, with section i(c)=c1/N. It is not an assertion that the actual port clock remains a cyclic shift off root. At whole Q its Vandermonde companion has the previously proved last-row carry, and (3), not an uncorrected cyclic formula, is the normal-source identity.

This image statement also lifts to literal highest-input port tensors, rather than only abstract sequences. For any g with Sigma g=0 put f_(-b)=sum_j g_j Q_N^(jb), 1<=b<N, and take

R_(b,0;0,b)=f_(-b)/[-b]_(Q_N),

with every other entry zero. All arrows are in the original head, the tensor is charge zero, its highest band support is one-sided, and its reclosed matrix is zero. The highest Fourier family of W(R) is EXACTLY g. Every divided coefficient here is a nonzero head unit. Thus every zero-source highest Fourier array has an explicit actual-port Cartan primitive. In the flat root metrics, Parseval and |[-b]_(Q_N)|>=1 give the tame bound ||R||_HS<=sqrt(N)||g||_2. This concerns the linear actual port/ current category; it does not assert that arbitrary R is a new uninserted knot word.

In the flat complex root metric the cyclic Fourier basis is unitary. For nonzero mode k the inverse h has multiplier (Q_N-1)/(Q_N^k-1), whose modulus is at most one. Hence EXACTLY

||h||_2<=1,
||J||_2^2=|Sigma J|^2/N+||(I-P)J||_2^2,
|Sigma J|=sqrt(N) dist(J,im D).               (7)

Thus the selected norm measures distance from actual zero-source currents, rather than the size of the partial array. There is no positive lower bound for that distance from ||J|| alone. These are norms of this specified finite source complex, not estimates for the full knot word or a uniform q-normal neighborhood.

For a literal ACTUAL example, the two-cut unknot is the flip tensor. At first input zero it has

F_0(eta_F)=sum_(b=0)^(N-1) eta_F^-b,
J_j=delta_(j,0),
J_W,j=[delta_(j+1,0)-delta_(j,0)]/(Q_N-1).

The last array is nonzero, one-sided before Fourier transformation, and its selected source is zero at every N>1. Its source and actual moving endpoint operators are precisely the originals.

3. The same actual current has nonzero geometric normal support

Now use the proved good first-position phase, not the unproved highest-boundary extension. The actual proper root lift and its nondegenerate Fourier Gaussian give

Z(v,H)=C(H) exp(S_geo/H)[A_0(v)+H A_1(v)+...],
eta=exp(v), p_e=eta^-1.

Here S_geo is independent of v; all original base, peripheral, half-density and Fourier phase marks stay in C and the chosen line. Multiplication of the actual open coefficient by (2) is EXACTLY the external shift identity

Z_W(v,H)=[Z(v+H,H)-Z(v,H)]/(exp(H)-1).         (8)

Equation (8) uses the specified proper root lift of the partial family; it does not equate that lift’s q-normal jets with the whole-Vandermonde family. It follows coefficientwise, rather than by a growing-band analytic limit. Hence

Z_W=C(H) exp(S_geo/H)[partial_v A_0+O(H)].      (9)

The leading derivative is NOT identically zero. Indeed the uniform actual Gaussian/holonomy density and its Fourier Jacobian show at fixed good A that

A_0(v)=c(M,A) partial_v r(eta),                (10)

up to the retained fixed ordered Fourier phase. Here is the needed frame-independence justification, rather than a free normalization: the actual uniform density theorem gives Omega_beta2=(-T_beta/2)Omega_cut2. Its determinant ratio T_beta is invariant under simultaneous representation framing; the frame-change determinants in its two chain maps cancel and det Ad(g)=1. On the marked half-density cover the square of Omega_beta/Omega_cut is therefore frame-constant. Differentiating that square in characteristic zero makes the marked ratio itself frame-constant. The exact Fourier Jacobian then gives (10), with a nonzero c depending only on M,A and the retained base mark. At completeness r(eta) is the nonconstant rational inverse of the actual birational (A,p_e) chart. If partial_v A_0 vanished, eta partial_eta r would be a constant d. Then dr=d deta/eta. A derivative of a rational function has zero logarithmic residue at eta=0, so d=0, forcing r constant. This contradicts that birational chart. Thus partial_v A_0 is nonzero on a dense regular open. The result persists on the marked meridian deformation germ.

Equations (3) and (9) prove the advertised obstruction: an ACTUAL zero-source port-current family has a nonzero formal geometric block with the same marked action as the knot.

For its certificate use a common LEFT multiple, rather than assuming this insertion has unit principal amplitude: its multiplier vanishes at t=0 and contains H^-1. Let D(eta) be the already constructed meridian certificate for the original partial array and its SAME formal block, with eta the physical Fourier character Q^j. Then D(Q eta) annihilates the externally shifted array J(Q eta) and formal block Z(Q eta). The meridian shift fixes Q,eta. A nonzero common left multiple of D(eta) and D(Q eta), over the indicated localized Ore field, consequently annihilates their difference divided by Q-1, BOTH for actual root arrays and for the exact formal difference (8). Clear its denominators and remove its common q-1 power. Its leading symbol has the geometric factor because (9) is a nonzero formal block with the same longitude action. This avoids applying the unit-amplitude coefficient theorem directly to a vanishing insertion. The original certificate theorem supplies D and its formal identity.

The selected scalar of this current family is nevertheless identically zero, whose minimal scalar annihilator is one. This is an explicit mechanism by which geometric partial support is lost in the final source pushforward.

This does NOT say the uninserted knot’s selected geometric contribution is zero, nor establish an actual exponential norm for the current arrays. It says that taking a nonzero local WKB line alone is not a faithful map on the actual source quotient. Any global comparison must carry the current/relative class and its marked cycle, not merely its action or leading local solution.

4. Compatibility with the first-cut highest moment decomposition

There is a complementary exact first-port description in the rank-one moment/orbit theorem. With A_j[c,a]=T^V_(c,j;a,j), it proves

J_whit,j=P_N/N+beta_K(D_K A_j),
beta_K(X)=sum_(c=1)^(N-1) X_(c,0)/(Q_N^-c-1).

The equality is entrywise: D_K multiplies that entry by Q_N^-c-1. The highest moment row c=0 forces q=0 in the uninserted conserving tensor and is constant in j at the root. Its exact beta norm is sqrt((N^2-1)/12). This decomposition retains the ORIGINAL orbit row and its Ward sector; it does not compute the unknown scalar P_N or move the regular good-A geometric block to A=1.

Together with (5)–(9), it identifies the extra requirement sharply: the selected scalar class must be determined in the cyclic/current quotient, including boundary and normal data. Scalar root closure, pointing, algebraicity of the geometric optimizer and a nonzero partial A-factor do not supply that determination by themselves. The flat cyclic source-frame theorem is consistent with this: it fixes the aligned whole clock/source frame before jets, but the scalar of the supplied closed word is separate. Here its actual cyclic-source kernel and a nonzero geometric formal current in that kernel are both explicit.

Exact current/source and cyclic-complex audit and saved results. The checks pass seven root levels with all 8,771 port units, 119 highest-input conserving units, 35 cyclic homotopy basis vectors, eight checks of the proved norm bound, and the exact whole-Q two-cut unknot. The latter detects the nonzero off-root carry missed by treating the root cyclic difference as a whole companion formula.

The intrinsic Mobius-pole calculation makes r(eta) and its pole explicit. Its root norm factor 1-lambda^N explains why sampling the rational principal density is not automatically a uniform source comparison. In particular the actual figure-eight principal pole meets the sampling grid at N divisible by six, while its finite arrays remain regular.
