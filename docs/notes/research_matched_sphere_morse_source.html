A matched spherical triangulation has the original general Morse source

31 October 2026. This is a finite topology/source extension, not a volume asymptotic and not a claim that a new presentation has a prescribed flat-plat width.

1. Input, conclusion, and the local ordering

Let 𝒯\mathcal T be a finite connected triangular cellulation of the oriented sphere, with a perfect matching DD of its cubic dual Δ\Delta. Assume every paired pair of triangles has a four-sided disk as its union, with four distinct white vertices. Equivalently, begin with a connected spherical quadrangulation 𝒬\mathcal Q, choose one embedded diagonal in each quadrilateral, and triangulate along those diagonals. Parallel edges produced by a choice of diagonals are not silently identified. They are allowed as cellulation data here. A positive geometric certificate, when needed, is a separate hypothesis.

There are 2R2R triangles, RR matched pairs, and R+2R+2 white vertices. For each quadrilateral choose the oriented cyclic order (x,b,y,c)(x,b,y,c), with chosen diagonal xyxy. The two triangles are (x,b,y)(x,b,y) and (x,c,y)(x,c,y). The local box frame is input middle bb, output middle cc, left outer xx, right outer yy. Reversing this frame later would require its actual duality adapter; no such unrecorded reversal is part of the construction.

The construction below gives a genuine augmented link in S3S^3, with RR labeled twice-punctured crossing disks, whose original cut graph is (Δ,D)(\Delta,D). It has a general Morse diagram containing exactly these RR recorded two-strand boxes and no other crossing. In the fixed frames, any full original coupons fe(Ω)Rϵef_e(\Omega)R^{\epsilon_e} have exactly the homogeneous source P(L)=∏e=1RHfeRϵe(Lxe,Lbe,Lce,Lye). P(L)=\prod_{e=1}^R H_{f_eR^{\epsilon_e}} (L_{x_e},L_{b_e},L_{c_e},L_{y_e}). \tag{1} For any MAIN edge between faces a,ba,b, the original raw one-mark contraction is exactly IN=[∏f≠a,bLfN−1]P|La=Lb=0. The formula uses the original turns, reduced mark, all full crossing scalars, and complete root Hermite data. No level factor is suppressed.

2. Inverting the actual crossing-disk cut

Embed the cubic dual Δ\Delta on the sphere. Choose disjoint small regular-neighborhood disks around the matched edges and their endpoint vertices. Because DD is a perfect matching, these disks are disjoint and meet the other edges in four boundary terminals each. Contracting each disk gives a connected plane four-valent graph GG, with RR vertices and 2R2R edges.

At one disk label the four terminals cyclically 0,1,2,30,1,2,3 so that the two original matched vertices group terminals (1,2)(1,2) and (3,0)(3,0). These are the two shaded half-disk sectors. Draw the physical untwisted main strands with the other planar pairing (0,1),(2,3). (0,1),\qquad(2,3). \tag{3} These arcs are disjoint. Join the terminals outside the disks along the original nonmatching dual edges. The result is a finite union of embedded circles in the projection sphere. Each circle meets a box, because every piece came from the finite four-terminal construction.

One must not identify these circles with the abstract cycles of Δ\D\Delta\setminus D: the latter uses the shaded grouping (1,2),(3,0)(1,2),(3,0), whereas the physical strands use (3).

In a disjoint three-ball neighborhood of each disk put the standard perpendicular crossing circle and its twice-punctured spanning disk, meeting exactly the two strands in (3). All its labels and its collar orientation are inherited from the chosen local box frame. This is an explicit link embedded in S3S^3; no abstract three-manifold realization theorem is needed.

Cut it along the projection sphere and open each original upper and lower half disk. The two shaded triangles at the box have the original labels S0=(O,C1,C2),S1=(O,C3,C0). S_0=(O,C_1,C_2),\qquad S_1=(O,C_3,C_0). The untwisted within-copy gluing fixes OO and sends C1↦C0C_1\mapsto C_0, C2↦C3C_2\mapsto C_3. Thus the cut is literally the inverse of sections 1–4 of original matching-graph cut. Its medial boundary graph is the medial graph of the supplied Δ\Delta, with every original white face and matching edge retained. The proof there that opening the disjoint half disks gives two balls is local and does not use a plat sweep; it therefore applies to this construction.

Insert a recorded half twist ϵe∈{0,1,−1}\epsilon_e\in\{0,1,-1\} in a box when desired. Its original shaded maps are precisely the cross-copy maps in that same cut theorem. Both nonzero signs have the same terminal permutation but retain their different disk-framed peripheral paths. The external graph and the local polynomial frame are unchanged.

3. A knot parity pattern exists, without a bridge-width assertion

Start with the untwisted physical circles of section 2. Form a finite graph whose vertices are these circles and whose edge at a box joins the two circles passing through that box (a loop is allowed). This graph is connected: otherwise the union of boxes and outside arcs, and therefore the connected four-valent graph GG, would split.

Choose a spanning tree. Give its boxes odd parity and all other boxes even parity. Switching a tree box from (3) to the odd pairing (0,2),(1,3)(0,2),(1,3) joins two distinct current circles into one. Process the tree edges in any order: after any subset, its connected tree components are exactly the merged circles, so the next edge still joins two different circles. Thus the final main link has one component. Each odd sign may independently be chosen +1+1 or −1-1.

Filling box ee with any integer power nen_e of that parity preserves this one-component property. The original convention is ne=ϵe−2pen_e=\epsilon_e-2p_e, where pep_e is the integer full-twist filling coordinate in the recorded local disk framing. Arbitrarily large independent positive or negative powers of the chosen parity are available. This says nothing about minimal bridge number or a specified original flat width.

4. Producing the original finite Morse tensor network

Choose a point outside the disks as infinity. In each disk use its given orientation-preserving parametrization to draw two vertical strands, with terminals 1,21,2 at the input and 0,30,3 at the output. The local quadrilateral is then exactly (x,b,y,c)(x,b,y,c) in section 1. The finitely many disjoint disks can be placed in such plane charts independently; this fixes, rather than transports or resets, their ordered four-leg frames. More explicitly, straighten each chosen disk parametrization in its own disjoint collar, and extend those collar changes to an orientation-preserving plane isotopy. The remaining outside arcs are then drawn between the resulting boundary endpoints. Thus the local vertical charts belong to one global plane drawing; no ribbon normal twist or permutation of a coupon’s legs is introduced.

Smooth the outside arcs and perturb their height functions away from the disks. There are finitely many ordinary nondegenerate maxima and minima, all at distinct heights after another small perturbation. No new crossing or ribbon twist is required. This is a general Morse diagram, with arbitrary cups/caps between recorded box heights. It need not be a plat with all cups below and all caps above the boxes.

Evaluate it using exactly the original complete module VdV_d, its regular self-duality JJ, all four original turns, the original checked crossings and Casimir coupons, and one exposed original reduced cap. Upward/downward arcs carry the corresponding actual module or dual; their physical frames are related by JJ, not by an independently reset diagonal insertion. Since the complete object is self-dual, these are the same original finite matrices in the ordered balanced head frames. This is the usual original ribbon tangle evaluation on the explicitly constructed diagram.

There is no new normalization ambiguity. For fixed NN, the regular generic lift uses g̃=(−1)HK\widetilde g=(-1)^HK, with J−tJ=g̃J^{-t}J=\widetilde g, which specializes literally to g=K1−Ng=K^{1-N}. Its two traces agree on every endomorphism in the tensor-generated integral-weight category. The full proof, including duals and regular root specialization, is in sections 2–3 of all-MAIN mark and general Morse compiler; it is independent of a flat layout. Its snakes prove invariance under the Morse interchanges just used. Its spherical trace exchange exposes any selected MAIN edge as a top cap while keeping every coupon disk fixed.

The cut network is a regular generic intertwiner on VdV_d or its dual. Its KK weights are distinct and its internal FF arrows nonzero, so it is scalar. Its ordinary closure is (−1)d[N]q(-1)^d[N]_q times that scalar. Equality of closed spherical readings, followed by regular specialization, proves independence of the reduced marked scalar from the MAIN edge. No root quantum dimension is divided by zero. This argument works on any component because all the complete colors agree.

7. Scope needed by a diagonal-choice argument

This theorem constructs an actual source for each diagonal choice on the fixed quadrangulation. The constructed links for different choices need not be isotopic, their marked coefficient polytopes need not coincide, and no projected sector or physical operator is moved from one choice to another. The common quadrangulation supplies a common finite combinatorial indexing only.

Every original target, half-index coset, retained half-twist and disk-framed signed filling coordinate has now been supplied for that choice. Consequently a proposed complete-capacity-maximizing choice can refer to a genuine original source rather than a formal entropy model. This does not by itself prove its global source estimate, local filling packet, nonzero period, hyperbolicity, or volume limit. Those hypotheses must be checked on the chosen diagram. In particular no old flat-plat gap forest or prescribed bridge width has been transported merely by constructing a general Morse source.

scope

All finite matrix ingredients are the original duality, homogeneous source, all-MAIN compiler, and local full-Hermite filling functional. No new numerical audit is asserted for this constructive proof. No global spectral comparison, filling asymptotic, prescribed plat width, or equality between sources from different diagonal choices is claimed.
