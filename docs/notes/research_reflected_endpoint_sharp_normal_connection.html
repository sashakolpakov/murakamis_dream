Reflection gives the sharp physical normal connection

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The sine-factor approximate modes admit a regular divided-color frame through the middle fold. Exact reflection of that frame, together with the original recurrence, determines the derivative of the actual connection coefficients to the sharp truncation accuracy. Thus the same two-value normalization now approximates both physical values and first spectral normals at that rate. The result concerns the actual physical jets; it does not compare an unrelated off-grid baseline or estimate a filled current.

6. Scope and complete direct sources

The scalar in (8) is the explicitly normalized approximate homogeneous basis combination, anchored by two actual physical values. The new estimate (36) supplies its previously retained normal mismatch. It gives a moderate representative with sharply small full physical-jet error; it does not make that error zero, remove an exterior or interface current, or estimate a scalar closing with arbitrary spectators.

The construction does not compare this scalar to the old modified-differential-equation baseline on fractional grids. Such a comparison is unnecessary for (9), and no statement about the old Green boundary packet is inferred. Exact one-sided Hermite repair of the new physical-jet error remains a subsequent source operation with its own retained interface and sides.
