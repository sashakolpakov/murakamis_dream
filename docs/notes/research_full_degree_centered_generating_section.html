A full-degree generating section with its original source normalization

21 November 2026. Research note. Earlier finals and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

The centered Conway basis has a generating polynomial whose finite quantum-difference equation includes the exact characteristic-polynomial forcing. At every physical Casimir node it factors into finite products. This gives a nonzero dilogarithmic approximation on the entire open unit disk, uniformly through both ends of the physical index range.

The same approximation retains the actual first Casimir normals. Their proof uses an exact cyclic primitive with a necessary central correction, rather than differentiation of values interpolated between the physical nodes. An explicit analytic section is exponentially accurate in all these physical Hermite coordinates. A separate small-disk estimate reaches fixed off-spectrum Casimir labels too. Neither statement identifies the original closing of a general assembled short component.
