Coherent full-normal traces of physical row products

Private written proof, 17 November 2026.

The positive coefficient contour for a product of physical value rows also controls its original boundary normals. The source-exact Jacobi derivative replaces each normal by a polynomial cofactor and the Ward endpoint coupon. The coupon has a cut, which is retained below. Its jump is polynomial, and the entire uncharged cut lies strictly below the positive coefficient saddle.

This proves a polynomial relative upper bound for every mixed first normal, uniformly over all physical rows with a fixed support margin. In a strict bulk degree chart the leading mixed normal is nonzero. A single holomorphic scalar packet then approximates all those physical jets with exponential accuracy. No charged closing, arbitrary-height continuation, or complete network lower bound follows from this local packet.
