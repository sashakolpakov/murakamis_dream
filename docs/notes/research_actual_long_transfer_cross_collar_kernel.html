The actual long-transfer cross-collar kernel

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The opposite endpoint blocks excluded from the short Euler transition have an exact finite kernel with a genuine factor NN. This note gives both signed factors, their checked-crossing order, their endpoint gauges, and their finite Fourier kernels. The root reflection identity is used only after specialization. The full unreflected products are retained for every quantum/meridian normal.

The sole complete direct E supplies the literal balanced matrices, crossing order, gauges and two-wall convention. All new reflection, phase and Fourier calculations are given below. No neighboring packet, contributing cycle or scalar noncancellation is assumed.

3. Exact endpoint gauges and the symmetric kernel

Use E’s GF|a⟩=Fa|a⟩G_F|a\rangle=F_a|a\rangle. On both heads, the uncheck transformed entry is the original one multiplied by Fd−uFv/(FwFd−z)F_{d-u}F_v/(F_wF_{d-z}).
