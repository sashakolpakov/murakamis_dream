Completion audit: full hyperbolic-volume equality for the original source

29 October 2026. The objective is EQUALITY with the actual hyperbolic volume. An upper bound alone does not satisfy it. The earlier upper-bound checkpoint is an intermediate result; this audit concerns the complete equality on the same broad class.

1. Statement and unchanged quantifiers

For each fixed odd integer s>=25 there is P_s such that, for every independently chosen FIXED signed tuple |p_i|>=P_s, the original pretzel knot with boxes R^(-(2p_i+1)) has

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N)log|<K>_N|=Vol(S^3 minus K).

The invariant and its cut normalization are the original reduced Kashaev invariant. Both parities and all composite quantum levels are included. The slopes may have different signs and arbitrarily different sizes. The width is unbounded across the class, but both it and the slopes are fixed before N tends to infinity. The threshold may depend on the fixed width. No statement for arbitrary knots, short slopes, or widths/slopes growing with N is included.

The standing hyperbolicity assumption can be retained. In fact the constructed smooth complete metric proves hyperbolicity in this sufficiently long range. This strengthening is not needed to reduce or change the original equality target.

3. The actual equality chain

The completed and variational proofs give

lim_N (2pi/N)log|<K>_N|
  =Vsrc_s(p)
  =D(beta(rho_p))
  =max_(theta in A_p) V_T(theta)
  =Vol(S^3 minus K).

Here A_p uses the filled rotational constraints Im(U_i+p_iV_i)=-2pi, not the complete UNFILLED cusp constraints. At the source point ReU_i+p_iReV_i=0, so for every tangent of A_p,

dV_T=(1/2)sum_i ReV_i d(ImU_i+p_iImV_i)=0.

The main cusp term is zero because its peripheral image is parabolic. The negative tetrahedral Hessian is positive definite with determinant1. Thus the source is the unique constrained maximizer. The separate primitive tube proof ensures that this maximum is realized on the actual smoothly filled knot, rather than on a branched local structure.

For the customary all-ideal angle presentation, the prior audited positive ideal-subdivision input is used. The independent finite-center metric completion proves the final geometric equality without relying on that optional all-ideal presentation. These dependencies are not conflated.

4. Verification and limits of the checks

The completion argument combines the positive core, primitive period, complete log-link tube and Casson–Rivin variation on all angle tangents. Both the source and geometric thresholds allow independently signed unequal slopes.

The finite audit experiments/audit_actual_long_pretzel_primitive_filling_angle.py checks the exact spin/angle algebra,120 signed core families,32 small-log calibrations and5 Fermi metric checks. These checks calibrate formulas; the uniform proof of smooth completion is in the notes, not inferred from those finite examples. The previous full-source audits and their precise analytical scope are recorded in the whole-source theorem and the earlier upper-bound completion audit.

In particular, the new proof does not rely on any of the following insufficient substitutes: a scalar killed peripheral word; convergence of filled volumes to V_s; a positive quantum norm without survival; an arbitrary topological triangulation; or strict concavity without a verified source point in the correct constrained angle domain.

The evidence establishes full equality for the class in section1. The general volume conjecture for arbitrary hyperbolic knots remains outside this theorem, rather than being declared complete by this audit.
