The complete Fourier core and the actual marked geometric deformation

30 October 2026. The exact complete-image Fourier core has the volume rate of the actual nearby marked geometric representation when its small external deficits are real. For complex deficits the explicit peripheral boundary correction is retained. The proof uses the established complex-core packet, common marked circle reconstruction and literal primitive-collar theorem. This note does not estimate the ordinary-color subset terms of the full mixed source or prove the long-filled knot limit.

1. The selected source critical point and its local peripheral data

Fix any connected original certified augmented plat, with its actual main mark, all matching circles and retained half twists epsilon_i. No one-main-component restriction is imposed. Use the exact entire complete-image core and its complex packet. Let chi_i=h_i/N denote its external deficit coordinates, and let S(chi) be the limiting selected critical action, with the explicit chi-independent exterior and maximal-channel phases still outside this normalized action. Set delta_i=1/2-mu_i for its internal channel coordinates. Its phase is

sum_i[Q_bi(delta_i)+Q_ci(delta_i)]
  -sum_(finite faces) log l_f
  +pi i sum_i epsilon_i delta_i^2
  +2pi i sum_i chi_i delta_i.                         (1)

All Q terms retain their actual sine and forced-factor normalization. The marked circle reconstruction proves that its face-stationary radii are the curvatures of one common analytic line-circle pattern, with the original two marked lines and all actual triangle incidences. Thus this is not a collection of independently framed local solutions.

For each circle use its actual ordered input/output pairs and product P_i. The selected disk eigenvalue is

J_i=-exp(-2pi i delta_i).                            (2)

It is exactly the continuation of the actual internal node and selected line supplied by the marked-pattern theorem, including the two-line triangles. Let C_i transport the incoming pair to the outgoing pair, with C_i A_in C_i^-1=A_out and [C_i,P_i]=0. Normalize its eigenvalue on the J_i^-1 line to one, and denote its eigenratio by R0_i.

The primitive-collar theorem proves the ACTUAL primitive, corner-preserving identity

R0_i=E_bi E_ci/D_i, Log R0_i(0)=0,                  (3)

with the source-selected roots and the literal line-face limit. A doubled reflection between the two middle faces or the reciprocal flag is not a substitute for (3). The theorem identifies this C_i with the recorded even collar, including its common marked-line limit.

Since partial_delta(Q_b+Q_c)=-Log(E_b E_c/D), channel stationarity of (1) is precisely

Log R0_i-2pi i epsilon_i delta_i=2pi i chi_i.       (4)

With m_i=exp(-pi i chi_i), this says m_i^2 R0_i(-J_i)^epsilon_i=1. Every logarithm is its germ at the complete point, with the existing primitive marking; no integer shear or new linear action character is chosen.

2. All actual group relations, not only the local eigenvalues

For the global assembly, for each actual white face f use its reflection R_f in the common circle pattern. For complex parameters this means the holomorphic complexification of the two reflection matrices R_f+,R_f-, with R_f-R_f+=I. It agrees with ordinary reflection at real parameters; one does not conjugate the complex deformation variables.

At every original Morse slice let F_0,…,F_(2k) be its ACTUAL region sequence. Define the original prefix and raw physical heads by

Q_j=R_(F_j)^+ R_(F_0)^-,
H_j=R_(F_j)^+ R_(F_(j-1))^-.                       (5)

These telescope: H_j…H_1=Q_j. Every main incidence remains tangent, so H_j has determinant one and trace -2; the physical main meridian is its recorded trace +2 lift. The same region labels on an unchanged physical wire give the same based head at its two ends. At an actual cup or cap the first and last regions agree, so the ordered pair product is I. This is the inverse-pair relation of the literal ordinary and marked closures, without replacing the unrestricted quantum mark. The two closure phases can differ by their retained integer character; that character has not been deleted from the action.

Across a box the outer regions agree on its two sides. Thus the input and output products are the same P_i. The collar C_i in (3) gives their actual simultaneous conjugacy. For a retained half twist put h_J=J_i^-1-1, a unit near completeness. The original physical auxiliary meridian is the regular matrix

M_i=m_i R0_i h_J^(-epsilon_i)
                 C_i^-1(P_i^-1-I)^epsilon_i.       (6)

This is exactly the original collar/Hurwitz expression from the literal Morse presentation, section 4. Its selected J_i-line eigenvalue is m_i, while

det M_i=m_i^2 R0_i(-J_i)^epsilon_i=1.

It commutes with P_i. Its inverse followed by the recorded Hurwitz half twist induces C_i on the main pair. At the complete point it is the original primitive crossing-circle meridian, with its recorded SL lift; it is not a separately chosen centralizer eigenvalue.

The relations just verified are ALL the original Morse/van Kampen relations: shared physical wires, inverse-pair cups/caps, the drilled box threading word [2,1,1,2], and the retained signed Hurwitz move. Their presentation was proved in section 5 of the same critical-scheme note from the actual diagram. It applies at arbitrary fixed width and with every actual matching-graph cycle. Thus (5),(6) construct a representation of the ACTUAL augmented link group, with every main meridian parabolic and all auxiliary meridians prescribed by m_i. No additional global cycle or generic frame is needed for this group identity. A regular common conjugate may be used to compare with the old critical chart, but no independently chosen local conjugates are introduced.

At chi=0 this is the complete geometric representation by the original certificate and primitive collar calibration. The complete geometric representation is irreducible. The already proved positive marked gluing rigidity says that its nearby decorated representation germ, modulo one common conjugation, is uniquely parameterized by the actual cusp meridian logs. Fixing every main cusp log to zero leaves precisely the auxiliary coordinates in (4). Hence the constructed family is the selected marked geometric deformation, not another character component.

3. The exact peripheral action differential

The primitive projective logs, centered at completeness, are

U_i=2Log m_i=-2pi i chi_i,
V_i=2Log(-J_i)=-4pi i delta_i.                     (7)

Here P_i is the original ordered disk longitude, with the preferred peripheral convention already proved for the threading word. A half-longitude or an independently inverted flag would change (7) and is not permitted. The actual half-twist shear has already entered (4),(6).

Envelope differentiation of the selected critical value in (1) gives

partial_i S=2pi i delta_i,
d(2pi i S)=(1/2)sum_i V_i dU_i.                   (8)

There is no new order-N exterior color character in the original core: the exact color dependence is its prescribed finite cosine transform. The retained exterior framing unit and maximal-channel half-twist phases are chi-independent and stay in the analytic packet. Their constant phase must not be mistaken for a change of (8).

At completeness the actual positive face coefficient and facet coning already prove

2pi Re S(0)=Vol(Y_D).

The old correctly marked geometric action has the same differential (8) and the same absolute regulator anchor. Their holomorphic difference is therefore a chi-independent constant whose real part is zero. This fixes the exponential rate without assigning an unproved Chern– Simons constant. All actual constant source phases remain explicit.

For genuinely complex meridian logs, the boundary correction is retained. The marked regulator identity with U=2Log M,V=2Log ell gives

2pi Re S(chi)=Vol(rho_chi)
                    +(1/2)sum_i Re U_i Im V_i.     (9)

Main-cusp terms vanish because their U logs are identically zero. Equation (9) uses the continuously selected longitude arguments and the complete anchor; it does not say that the bare real action equals the incomplete representation volume at arbitrary complex chi.

4. An exact finite-polynomial volume limit

Equations (1)–(9) identify the action of the ACTUAL fixed-complex-neighborhood core packet with the actual marked geometric deformation. The finite face torus and finite channel residue rectangle already specify that core period and its nonzero amplitude. No unknown geometric intersection coefficient remains for this complete-image term.

In particular, for each fixed labeled certified diagram and retained half-twist vector there is a rho>0 such that, locally uniformly for REAL |chi_i|<rho,

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N)log|F_N(N chi)|
                                  =Vol(rho_chi).    (10)

Here F_N is the EXACT finite cosine polynomial of the original complete-image source, not an interpolation chosen from its values. The limit includes both parities and all sufficiently large quantum levels. The right side is the volume of the selected actual geometric representation of the original marked exterior. No cone-manifold completion is asserted. Indeed U_i=-2pi i chi_i is purely imaginary on this real slice, so the correction in (9) vanishes. On the fixed complex polydisc the same limit is the right side of (9), including its correction. This follows directly from the locally uniform rate of the complex-core theorem; its nonzero relative packet supplies the needed absence of nearby zeros.

5. The selected algebraic peripheral germ

There is also a finite algebraic description of the representation germ, distinct from its analytic action. Normalize the actual marked strip by H=1 and the fixed finite center by X_ref=0. For each finite circle retain its coordinates (X_f,Y_f,r_f). The marked reconstruction equations are polynomial in these variables and in

I_i=-(J_i+J_i^-1)/2.                               (11)

For example, a finite/finite edge has equation (X_f-X_g)2+(Y_f-Y_g)2-r_f2-r_g2-2I_e r_f r_g=0; the line equations are Y_f-r_f I_e=0 or 1-Y_f-r_f I_e=0. Main edges have I_e=1. These are equations over the rational Laurent field Q(J_1,…,J_R), with 3R-1 normalized circle variables and 3R-1 equations.

The proved circle-variable Jacobian is invertible at the actual complete point. Hence its selected local algebraic component is smooth of dimension R, projects analytically locally isomorphically to the J coordinates, and is dominant and generically finite over them. Equivalently its function field is a finite extension of Q(J_1,…,J_R). This assertion is about the selected component; it does not assert that every fiber of the global polynomial system is finite.

The source-selected E roots are algebraic as well. With x,y,b the actual curvatures, write z=sin^2(pi delta)=(2+J+J^-1)/4. Then

E_b^2-2(x+y+2bz)E_b+[(x+y)^2-4xy z]=0.           (12)

Adjoin the selected square roots on their recorded germ and the roots

M_i^2=[R0_i(-J_i)^epsilon_i]^-1, M_i(0)=1.         (13)

The nonvanishing denominators are localized at the units already proved for the actual collar. The displayed expression for C also contains the physical divisor J-J^-1, which is NOT inverted here: its divided difference is the regular germ proved by the literal three-point collar map. In particular R0-1 vanishes when J=-1 with the other parameters free, and the selected quotient extends through that divisor. Thus (5),(6) give an algebraic family of actual marked representations on a finite algebraic extension of the selected circle component. Its peripheral image is the selected algebraic germ with all main meridians M=1. The internal parameters are auxiliary longitude coordinates; they are not external finite channel labels.

At completeness the eliminated circle solution is even in each delta, and the actual meridian-log Jacobian is

partial U_i/partial delta_j
    =-2(c_i-pi i epsilon_i) delta_ij,
c_i=pi(T_bi(0)+T_ci(0))/(x_i+y_i)>0.              (14)

Here c_i is a scalar radial coefficient, distinct from the collar matrix C_i. The Jacobian is invertible, so the auxiliary meridian logs are local coordinates on this same actual marked peripheral germ. This is a local A-variety statement with a specified branch and marking. It does not compute a global eliminated A-polynomial, give a scalar quantum A-operator, or assert that S is an algebraic number. The function S is the regulator primitive with differential (8).

6. The remaining full-source and filling distinction

The exact finite packet still uses its N-dependent selected critical phase S_N and unit Hessian factor. Holomorphic convergence S_N->S does not by itself justify replacing N S_N by N S in a relative formula. The geometric identification here concerns the limiting action and its actual peripheral marking.

The full external-color source also contains ordinary-color subsets. Their source-exact transport, possible degeneracies, exponential gaps and far-end cancellation have not been supplied by this identification. Consequently the arbitrary independently long signed-filling theorem remains separate. No knot family or allowed slope set is narrowed in order to replace those remaining requirements.
