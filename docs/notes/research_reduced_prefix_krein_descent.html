Krein adjoints descend through the actual prefix reduction

Written research proof, 23 November 2026.

The actual triangular prefix change is an isometry for the fixed physical tensor Krein form on positive real meridians. Its constant-coefficient reduction preserves adjoints. Consequently the literal reduced braid matrices, including their boundary Euler generator and full Cartan scalar, preserve the same tensor form on the remaining heads. This is an algebraic descent through a star-stable nilpotent ideal, not restriction of the form to the first-head vacuum, which is isotropic.

All statements are at the actual fixed quantum root, including even and composite levels. The form is indefinite. No marked scalar lower bound or identification of geometric eigenline contributions follows.

1. Actual matrices and the fixed tensor form

Fix N≥2,d=N−1,q=eπi/N,Q=q2,Fn=(Q;Q)n(0≤n<N).(1) N\ge2,\quad d=N-1,\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad Q=q^2, \quad F_n=(Q;Q)_n\quad(0\le n<N). \qquad\text{(1)} Keep this quantum parameter fixed. The common meridian is M>0M>0, with x=−M/qx=-M/q and the real branch of Log⁡M\operatorname{Log} M. No cyclic completion is made. Put HN=q−1Z−1Rd,Z|a⟩=Qa|a⟩,Rd|a⟩=|d−a⟩,ℋb=HN⊗b.(3) H_N=q^{-1}Z^{-1}R_d,\quad Z|a\rangle=Q^a|a\rangle, \quad R_d|a\rangle=|d-a\rangle,\quad \mathscr H_b=H_N^{\otimes b}. \qquad\text{(3)} Thus HN|a⟩=qQa|d−a⟩H_N|a\rangle=qQ^a|d-a\rangle, HN†=HNH_N^\dagger=H_N, and HN2=IH_N^2=I. The direct head identities are K♯=K,F♯=Q−1F,L♯=QL,x‾=Qx.(4) K^\sharp=K,\qquad F^\sharp=Q^{-1}F,\qquad L^\sharp=QL,\qquad \bar x=Qx . \qquad\text{(4)} The first two are [H, (4)]. 3. Constant coefficients preserve adjoints

Let 𝒞Σ\mathcal C_\Sigma be the full matrix commutant of FΣF_\Sigma, at a fixed positive real MM. It is stable under ♯m\sharp_m, since FΣ♯=Q−1FΣF_\Sigma^\sharp=Q^{-1}F_\Sigma. The column on |0⟩|0\rangle determines all columns by commutation with F1F_1, proving (10). This is the same commutant calculation used in [R, (3)].

Define ℬm(W)=B0(W)\mathcal B_m(W)=B_0(W). This is the actual prefix map, not a different compression: for i0v=|0⟩⊗vi_0v=|0\rangle\otimes v, [R] gives Smi0=i0S_mi_0=i_0 and B0=p0Sm−1Wi0B_0=p_0S_m^{-1}Wi_0. In particular, if an invertible actual total intertwiner satisfies W♯mW=IW^{\sharp_m}W=I, then B(W)†ℋrB(W)=ℋr,B(W)−1=ℋrB(W)†ℋr.(13) \boxed{\quad B(W)^\dagger\mathscr H_rB(W)=\mathscr H_r,\qquad B(W)^{-1}=\mathscr H_rB(W)^\dagger\mathscr H_r .\quad} \qquad\text{(13)} Here and below B(W)=ℬm(W)B(W)=\mathcal B_m(W). Thus the vacuum is isotropic, while its pairing with the opposite end is nondegenerate. The algebra quotient proof, rather than a nonexistent positive vacuum restriction, gives (13).

5. Meridian germs and the original pointed source

6. Scope and complete sources

The new operation is the actual prefix star descent (9)–(13). It gives one common nondegenerate form for the complete reduced braid representation and its meridian germs. It does not remove any remaining head, root Jordan direction, or source coefficient. The physical dimension is still Nm−1N^{m-1}. No positive metric, geometric packet estimate, nonzero marked closing, comparison of off-circle geometric eigenlines, or general volume lower bound is asserted. Moving-qq normal jets retain their separate original prefix adapters and are outside this fixed-root meridian proof.
