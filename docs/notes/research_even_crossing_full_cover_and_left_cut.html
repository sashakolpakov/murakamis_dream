Even crossing representatives on the full cover and their left-cut current

Written research proof, 23 November 2026.

The entire microscopic crossing function has an explicit partial-fraction expansion. It gives uniform bounds on the full physical logarithmic cover, an exact finite-level left-cut jump, and a uniform integrable bound at the cut endpoint. The original polynomial spectator and genuine turn kernel remain in the resulting finite-contour identity. No bound for the rest of that contour or for a general short-component closing is inferred.
