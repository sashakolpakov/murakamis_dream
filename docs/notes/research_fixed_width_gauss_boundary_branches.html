Actual fixed-width Gauss boundary kernels, their branches and vacuum layer

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The initialized first column of the universal Gauss operator admits an exact width recursion and an exact finite divided-difference lift. For every fixed number of tail heads these determine its vacuum normalization, its microscopic limit, and actual exponential branches on explicitly separated sectors. No spectral channel sum is introduced. The original vacuum forcing remains in the recursion.

A fixed zero-free disk at the origin does not persist to three tail heads: the actual normalized column has zeros at distance O(N−1)O(N^{-1}). Thus the wider initializer must retain its branches and their confluent derivatives. This is a boundary-kernel theorem, not a higher-dimensional Goursat theorem or a closed-source estimate.

2. Exact width recursion with its vacuum source

For a<Ta<T, the finite Gaussian identity (1−Qn−a)[T−an−a]Q=(1−QT−a)[T−a−1n−a−1]Q (1-Q^{n-a}){T-a\brack n-a}_Q =(1-Q^{T-a}){T-a-1\brack n-a-1}_Q and (5) give, with VT+1(r)=0V_{T+1}^{(r)}=0, Va(r+1)=QaVa(r)−Qa(1−QT−a)Va+1(r).(9) V_a^{(r+1)} =Q^aV_a^{(r)}-Q^a(1-Q^{T-a})V_{a+1}^{(r)}. \qquad\text{(9)} The boundary case a=Ta=T follows directly from (5). 4. Quantitative competing bound and a nonzero sector branch

Shrink the seed disk in (7) once. On it put YG=ec0Dϕ=Y1−ρ,W(ρ;C)=C−1−Y(ρ;C)ρ.(19) Y_G=e^{c_0D\phi}=\frac{Y}{1-\rho},\qquad W(\rho;C)=\frac{C^{-1}-Y(\rho;C)}{\rho}. \qquad\text{(19)} Its numerator is b+O(ρ)b+O(\rho), uniformly; hence it has no zeros on a sufficiently small punctured disk. It has a pole at zero. It is not a holomorphic unit on the full disk.

This proof applies to compact subsets of the sector; it does not claim uniformity as ρ→0\rho\to0 or as the positive gap closes.

6. Scope and complete directs

This is an actual arbitrary-fixed-width initialization result: (4),(10),(22) preserve all physical coefficients and endpoint support; (15) pays the vacuum normalization; (20) controls every competing boundary term; (21),(26) give nonzero local branches where the stated quantitative gaps hold. The microscopic and confluent rows are retained when these hypotheses fail. No replacement positive packet is used.

The universal operator also commutes with its consecutive partial Casimirs: each commutes with the total E,F,KE,F,K by the ordered coproduct and centrality on its own interval, hence with (1). This note does not solve their multi-variable kernel equations. In particular it makes no higher-width full-kernel, meridian-normal, inverse-frame, geometric-chart, relative-cycle or volume assertion. All exterior factors in [G]’s original opposite kernel remain.
