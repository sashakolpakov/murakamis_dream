A uniform closed route in the original Pascal-cell source

29 October 2026. The exact positive angle cells of the actual divided fusion maps have a simultaneous, source-admitted closed route at every crossing of ANY signed braid. Its energy is exponential. In particular, the phase-free path partition of these cells cannot itself be the sharp geometric angle partition for the figure-eight knot. Coherent contraction of the physical maps is essential before such a geometric comparison.

This is about the original averaged-trace presentation AFTER its authorized whole-word degree reduction. It is not a statement about every possible pointed presentation or a surviving coherent clock sector.

1. Exact physical alphabet and original source

Use N>=2, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, and x=-q^(-1). The exact physical fusion and crossing theorem gives C=C’_12 and its literal inverse with entries

C_(a-r,b+r;a,b)=x^(-r)[b+r choose r]_Q,
C^(-1)_(a-r,b+r;a,b)
   =(-1)^r Q^(r(r-1)/2) C_(a-r,b+r;a,b),
0<=r<=min(a,d-b).                                      (1)

Write C_21=Flip C Flip and

D_2|a,b>=(-1)^b Q^(b(b-1)/2)x^(-2b)|a,b>.

The FULL original checked crossings are

R_+=kappa C D_2 C_21 C^(-1),
R_-=kappa^(-1) C C_21^(-1) D_2^(-1) C^(-1).             (2)

No physical cutoffs or common Cartan scalars have been removed. All displayed diagonals, kappa and original closing pivots have unit modulus at completeness. Both signs are included below with their actual order.

For the whole original knot word W, integer total-degree conservation first gives the exact source

K_N=chi_N/N Tr[(I tensor g tensor ... tensor g)W].         (3)

This is the original charged-source theorem. It is used BEFORE expanding W into fusion paths. It is not a sectorwise replacement of the full marked row, nor a rule for arbitrary insertions. The original dimension/framing line chi_N remains outside (3).

The physical quarter gauge G_a=N^(1/4)/sqrt(s_a), s_a=|(Q;Q)_a|, commutes with the closing pivots. Thus its tensor similarity preserves (3) exactly. The squared entry of either C or C^(-1) in this gauge is

|Chat_entry|^2=N^2 w_d(r,a-r,d-a)w_d(r,b,d-b-r),
w_d(i,j,k)=1/(s_i s_j s_k), i+j+k=d.                     (4)

The operator C_21 has the same formula with the two physical heads exchanged. The inverse phases in (1) remain in the coherent word.

2. One closed route works for all signs and all braid positions

Put N=3r+1, d=3r, r>=1, and choose the PHYSICAL initial trace state

a_star=(2r,r,2r,r,...).                                  (5)

Every adjacent pair is either (2r,r) or (r,2r). At each crossing use the following transfers, listed in operator ACTION order:

input pair right C^(-1) middle C_21 or C_21^(-1) left C output pair
(2r,r) r r 0 (2r,r)
(r,2r) 0 r r (r,2r)

For a negative crossing its D_2^(-1) is between the right and middle maps; for a positive crossing D_2 is between the middle and left maps. It is diagonal and never changes this route or its nonzero modulus. Each intermediate pair is (2r,r) or (r,2r), so all physical bounds in (1) hold as ORDINARY integers. Inverse maps have exactly the same support and modulus as the forward maps.

Each row therefore returns the pair to its initial value. Applying the indicated row at every crossing keeps the complete physical state (5) fixed after EVERY complete R. It supplies a closed route for any braid width and any signed word, regardless of crossing order. Its original pivotal factor in (3) is nonzero and of modulus one, and its single cut factor is 1/N. This is an admitted expanded route of the complete original source, not an isolated entry without boundary gluing.

3. Exact energy and its uniform growth

Every active transfer in the table sees a high input head 2r and a low input head r in the orientation of that C. Both triples in (4) are EXACTLY (r,r,r). Each zero transfer contributes the two triples (0,a,d-a) and (0,b,d-b), with

w_d(0,a,d-a)=1/N.

Thus its squared entry is exactly one. There are two active C maps per crossing, giving four regular cells. For a word with m crossings, the product of squared, quarter-gauged path entries is exactly

E_route=N^(4m) w_d(r,r,r)^(4m)=N^(4m)/s_r^(12m).           (6)

All internal gauge factors telescope, since the route is closed. Equation (6) is therefore also its ungauged squared modulus; no endpoint gauge or source normalizer is missing. Including the literal cut coefficient changes (6) by N^(-2), and including chi_N multiplies it by |chi_N|^2. These known external factors remain explicit.

Let Z_path be the positive sum of squared entries over all closed physical paths in the alphabet (2), with the original trace boundary conditions. Then

Z_path>=N^(4m)w_d(r,r,r)^(4m).                             (7)

With Lambda(theta)=-integral_0^theta log(2sin t)dt and v_3=3Lambda(pi/3), the uniform endpoint factorial estimate gives

log s_r=-(N/pi)Lambda(pi*r/N)+O(log N),
lim_(N=3r+1) (1/N)log E_route=4m v_3/pi.                  (8)

This is an exact all-r family with a proved rate, not extrapolation from a few levels. Every cell has the correct affine sum d and every physical head-flow/source constraint is satisfied.

For the original figure-eight word [1,-2,1,-2], m=4 and its geometric volume is 2v_3 (two regular ideal tetrahedra). The lower rate in (8) is 16v_3/pi, whereas a sharp angle ENERGY partition has rate 2v_3/pi. Hence neither a polynomial normalization nor bounded polynomial multiplicity can identify the complete phase-free Z_path with that sharp positive geometric fiber. In particular the actual shared-spin relations alone do not imply the required geometric edge equations.

4. What has to remain coherent

The route is NOT claimed to survive summing its internal C channels. For example, for R_+ at input (r,2r), the diagonal physical entry is zero: the literal positive R requires output first head b+k with k>=0, so returning it to r is impossible. Its nonzero expanded routes cancel exactly. For R_- the analogous statement holds at (2r,r). This is a constructive reason to perform the available complete crossing contractions before assigning an angle partition, not a new obstruction to doing so. It also prevents misidentifying (6) with an already coherently summed, nonzero original clock sector.

The exact positive cells and coherent Gram remain useful. What fails is the shortcut of imposing the original physical head/source support on their phase-free path expansion and inferring a sharp geometric edge fiber from that support alone. An additional source-preserving coherent regrouping, such as complete-channel recoupling with its full mixed Gram, is necessary. This theorem does not rule out that route or a different global inner product.

Focused audit: original coproduct-derived C and inverse, full positive and negative R, both path orders, quarter metric and all regular/boundary cells, original pivotal trace conversion, and nonzero closed routes at arbitrary adjacent positions.
