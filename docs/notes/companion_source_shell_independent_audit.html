Independent audit of the whole pointed companion source

27 October 2026. The classification and polynomial comparison in the pointed source theorem hold over the stated commutative coefficient rings, including nonreduced bases. They require no quantum-gap division or imported multiplicative-quiver classification. The full actual word and selected regulator norm remain additional data.

1. Exact scheme classification in the source orbit basis

The cyclic source basis e_j=Y^jv fixes v=e_0 and makes Y the companion with last column t_i. Its determinant is (-1)^(N-1)t_0. The identities ell v=1 and ell Y=ell give ell=(1,…,1) and sum_i t_i=1, without division. The multiplicative moment equation is equivalent to requiring that XY-QYX have image in the source line, with its row then uniquely determined by the two invertible matrices.

Let Xv=v. In a nonzero-index row, for column j<N-1 the companion equation is

X_(i,j+1)=Q[X_(i-1,j)+t_i X_(N-1,j)].

Induction on j gives X_(N-1,j)=0 there and

X_(i,j)=Q^i x_(j-i) for i<=j, and zero for i>j.

Only x_0=1 and the still undetermined zeroth-row coefficients occur. In the last column put m=N-i. Dividing by the UNIT Q^i gives exactly

x_m=(1-Q^m)t_(N-m)
             +sum_(l=1)^(m-1)t_(N-m+l)x_l.

This recursively determines every x_m and proves the converse at the same time: all nonzero-index moment rows vanish. Thus det X=Q^(N(N-1)/2), and its inverse requires only powers of Q. Y inverse requires t_0 inverse, exactly the indicated principal open. The row w is unique. Its determinant character is

wv=Q^-N-1,

by det(I+v w)=1+w v, which follows directly from determinant multilinearity and also holds over rings with nilpotents.

Every inverse map and classification step is over the stated coordinate ring, so this is an isomorphism of schemes, not merely a classification of field-valued points. The source chart is the principal open 1-sum_(i>0)t_i!=0 in affine (N-1)-space. With a chosen free ambient basis the representation chart is GL_N times that open: the source orbit basis gives the unique gauge, and its stabilizer is trivial.

2. The polynomial coboundary and its retained co-source

Let chi(z)=z^N-sum t_i z^i and h(z)=z^N chi(1/z). Since sum t_i=1, ordinary monic division gives

chi=(z-1)chi_red, h=(1-z)h_red,
h_red=z^(N-1)chi_red(1/z), h_red(0)=1.

The recurrence is precisely

h(z)x(z)=h(Qz) modulo z^N,

where x(z)=sum_(m<N)x_m z^m. On the finite lowering shift L, L^N=0 and B L B-1=Q-1 L. Thus

G=h(L), G B=B h(QL)=X G.

G is upper unipotent and its inverse is the finite nilpotent geometric series. This proves X=G B G^-1 over the WHOLE ring. No factors 1-Q^m or 1-Q^N are inverted, canceled or evaluated. The formula remains valid at Q=1; at that value it is a canonical choice from chi, not a uniqueness assertion for every clock conjugator.

Since L v=0, Gv=G inverse v=v. Since ell(I-L)=e_0^t,

ell G=e_0^t h_red(L).

Hence the source pairing identity

ell C v=[e_0^t h_red(L)] [G inverse C G] v

has the printed orientation. Trivializing the clock has not made the co-source constant. The new Y, actual crossing/cap/current matrices and source must ALL be conjugated. Parameter-dependent connections also carry the forced gauge term G inverse dG. No fixed original peripheral/dimension line is normalized away.

3. Whole mixed moments recover the retained polynomial

For 0<=j<N, Y^jv=e_j, so the mixed moments are exactly

m_j=ell X Y^jv=sum_(l=0)^j Q^(j-l)x_l,
m_0=1, x_j=m_j-Q m_(j-1).

The second identity is ordinary telescoping. On the head-unit localization, the already proved recurrence then recovers t_(N-m) successively, by dividing only by 1-Q^m. Thus these N-1 mixed source/clock moments determine the WHOLE polynomial, not merely a normal Taylor approximation. Ordered in the reverse tail variables (t_(N-1),…,t_1), their polynomial Jacobian is lower triangular with diagonal 1-Q^m. Its determinant is

product_(m=1)^(N-1)(1-Q^m)=N at primitive Q_N.

The last equality follows by evaluating (zN-1)/(z-1)=product_(m=1)(N-1)(z-Q_N^m) at z=1. It is an exact root unit, not a bound for the global inverse map. At other Q, including Q=1, those moment coordinates can be singular although the companion chart and G remain regular.

Pure Y moments do NOT recover this data: ell Y^jv=1 for all integer j, by the normalized output equation and invertibility of Y. On the actual section the mixed moments are Q^j, so the reconstruction gives the original cyclotomic chi=z^N-1. This does not identify chi with a knot A-polynomial or compute an actual closed-word scalar.

4. The actual section and the remaining period

On the source orbit chart, Y^N=I implies Y^Nv=v. Expanding this single equation in its basis forces t_0=1 and t_i=0 for i>0. Conversely those equations make Y the cyclic shift. This is valid over every supplied commutative base, not only at a root or in characteristic zero. Then

chi=z^N-1, h=1-z^N, G=I,
h_red=1+z+...+z^(N-1),
w=(Q^-N-1)e_0^t.

Thus the original finite source is a regular SECTION of the unfolding, while its two finite endpoint deletions remain the actual operators from the previous boundary theorem. This is not a change of the actual knot state to a deformed companion.

The theorem determines a whole algebraic source chart and an exact comparison for any GIVEN full word. It does not determine that word’s scalar closed period, identify chi_red with A_K, select a global geometric cycle, or give a growing-N norm bound for G. Its retained co-source polynomial is precisely why clock conjugacy alone is insufficient.

The exact independent script and saved results pass symbolic N=2,…,7 checks of every lower moment row, characteristic/pointing factor, determinant, polynomial coboundary, co-source, mixed-moment inverse/Jacobian and original section. Twelve additional primitive/generic finite-field fibers check the FULL multiplicative moment row and its determinant character, not only the linearized relation.
