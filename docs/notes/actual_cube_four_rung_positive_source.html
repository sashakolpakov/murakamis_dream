A positive original cube packet with all four rung colors retained

29 October 2026. Fix the EIGHT cycle channels of the original ordered cube to the odd Steinberg channel, while retaining EVERY physical complete-circle channel on its FOUR rungs. The original exposed marked source has an explicit positive Newton contraction. This includes all paired nilpotents, gives its exact global support, controls the entire four-color sum by the central term at polynomial cost, and proves a strict gap for macroscopic rung-color deficits.

This is a specified sector of the original cube source, with its eight cycle projections still present. The remaining eight color sums and even levels are not evaluated. No conclusion about the full cube invariant or arbitrary cubic graphs is inferred.

1. The exact marked packet

Keep N=2m+1>=3, d=2m, q=exp(pi i/N), the literal ordered cube and its EXPOSED marked white vertex from the central cube theorem. Its two cycles have edge orders

top (0,11,3,2), bottom (9,5,6,8),
rungs (1,10,4,7).

Every cycle edge has label m. The four rung labels j_0,…,j_3 vary independently over 0,…,m. For j<=m put

k_j=2 if j<m, k_m=1,
z_j(v)=s_(j+v)/[s_(j-v)s_v^2], 0<=v<=j,
h_j=sum_(v=0)^j z_j(v) Bhat_v,
P_(N,j)=sum_(v=0)^j z_j(v) beta(m,v)^2,
S_N=P_(N,m),
omega_j=kappa(-1)^j q^[j(j+1)], kappa=q^(d^2/2).

Here the Newton basis and functional are the FULL root algebra, not the distinct-node quotient. In particular h_m is the genuine turned isolated projector from the central cube theorem. The actual complete circle sector L_j, paired or simple, obeys

Rturn L_j=N k_j h_j.                            (1)

Let Z_jvec denote the RAW original marked cube source with these twelve specified labels and no edge twists. Then

Z_jvec=N^12 product_(r=0)^3[k_(j_r)P_(N,j_r)^2]
                           phi(h_(j_0)h_(j_1)h_(j_2)h_(j_3)). (2)

All factors and the functional in (2) are the actual ones. For any fixed signed edge exponents multiply (2) by exactly

omega_m^(sum_cycle e_l) product_r omega_(j_r)^(e_(rung r)). (3)

Thus a twisted individual label sector has a specified unit phase; a sum of differently phased rung sectors is not declared positive.

The finite support of the untwisted source is particularly simple:

Z_jvec>0 iff j_0+j_1+j_2+j_3>=d;
Z_jvec=0 otherwise.                            (4)

Every LOCAL vertex already has total label 2m+j>=d. The additional constraint (4) is a genuine global contraction constraint; it cannot be deduced by keeping only the local black scalar supports.

2. The full normal sector turns to a positive polynomial

Let E_j be the FULL Hermite projector of the pair Casimir cluster c_j; L_j=L_N E_j. At a double node it retains the actual nilpotent circle normal. The complete Frobenius trace identity gives

phi(L_j f)=N Tr(E_j m_f)=N k_j f(c_j).           (5)

It follows from the complete polynomial identity for L_N and the trace of multiplication; the trace in the j cluster has its actual algebraic multiplicity k_j. No spectral derivative has been removed from L_j before this identity is used. The Newton turn and pairing then give the v-th coefficient of Rturn L_j as

phi(L_j Bhat_v)=N k_j Bhat_v(c_j).

Direct paired-sine factorization gives Bhat_v(c_j)=z_j(v) for v<=j, and zero for v>j. This proves (1) as a FULL algebra identity. Its polynomial degree is j, and hence it also specifies its first normal at every other double node. The paired nilpotent has become an ordinary low-degree turned polynomial by the actual nonmultiplicative turn, not by treating it as a positive projector.

3. Use the actual physical source’s cyclicity

For the finite generic derivation retain the physical cup

pV(u,d-u)=cV P_d(u), cV=N zeta^(-d^2),
P_L(u)=(-1)^u zeta^[u(L-u-1)]F_uF_(L-u)/F_L.

All factorials in this generic cup are retained. The root monomial formula is not substituted off the root. At the root

gamma_j=q^(2-j^2)/N^2,
h0_j=k_j N s_(2j)/s_j^2,
pi_j=gamma_j^(-1)D_phys(W_j),
L_j=h0_j W_j pi_j.

Choose the regular generic W_j and pi_j lift of the original first-normal theorem. The latter identity is asserted at the root; its chosen lift is h0_j W_j(t)pi_j(t). Every denominator in W_j and pi_j is a unit at this odd root. Paired generic projectors are NOT used.

There is an important simplification for unequal labels: rotate the actual black source U_abc, rather than assuming that every differently normalized canonical Theta has the same cyclic scalar. The physical nested-cup source has entries

J_((a,b),(c,e),(f,g))=pV(a,g)pV(b,c)pV(e,f).

The ratio to its pair-cyclic permutation is exactly K(a)K(b), because P_d(d-u)=K(u)^(-1)P_d(u), with d even. Covariance of each pi therefore proves the generic all-entry identity

U_abc(alpha,beta,gamma)
   =K_a(alpha) U_bca(beta,gamma,alpha).         (6)

This is valid before specialization and therefore for its first normal as well. White source tensors have the corresponding genuinely dual identity. The scalar product of the three fixed gamma factors is symmetric and does not change (6). No plain leg permutation or same-order Hermitian dual is introduced.

Use (6) to put each rung in the last black and first white slot, as in the explicit four-rung table of the central cube theorem. The two outer cycle objects are both Q_m=V_d, and the rung object is Q_j. Let W_j:Q_j->V_d tensor V_d be its ordinary Clebsch map and let D_can use the canonical cups on these two V_d objects. Set

a_j=(-1)^(d-j) zeta^[-j(j+1)-d],
Theta_mmj=(W_j tensor I)p_(2j)/a_j,
s_j=the actual black corner s_(m,m,j),
U_mmj=(s_j/a_j)(W_j tensor I)p_(2j).            (7)

To verify the anchor a_j, the proved lowest-column identity gives

W_j[2j](d,j)
   =zeta^[j(d-2j)] h_(d-j),
h_(d-j)=(-1)^(d-j)zeta^[-(d-j)(j+1)],
P_(2j)(2j)=zeta^(-2j).

Multiplication gives exactly (7), with no endpoint division by a zero factorial. Every a_j is a nonzero monomial.

Contracting one rung is now the ALL-SIDE-LEGS-OPEN identity

(s_j/a_j)^2 W_j D_can(W_j).                    (8)

The two Q_j cup/cap matrices cancel in their actual order. Since the cycle object’s physical cup is cV times its canonical cup,

D_can(W_j)=cV^2 D_phys(W_j)=cV^2 gamma_j pi_j,
W_j D_can(W_j)
       =cV^2 gamma_j L_j/h0_j at the root.      (9)

Equations (8),(9) retain the entire paired root operator. In fact for j<m this rung map is nonzero and nilpotent, with square zero. Splitting it into a vanishing generic norm times a singular generic projector would obscure this regular map; no such split is made.

The four rung maps all act on V_d tensor V_d. Their genuine full-cup turn, cyclic four-box trace and mark are consequently exactly the entrywise identities (9a),(9b),(10) of the central cube theorem, now with four different self-dual regular maps. Projector idempotence was never needed in that index substitution. The exposed physical mark remains on a cycle V_d strand. It is not transported through any paired Q_j image. Generic ordinary closure is [N] times the root-open scalar; taking its first normal and dividing by nu_N is therefore the original marked value phi of the product of turns.

4. All source phases cancel, with paired factors retained

Before simplifying, the preceding contraction gives at the root

Z_jvec=(N gamma_m)^8 product_r[
     h0_j gamma_j (s_j/a_j)^2]
         phi(product_r Rturn[W_j D_can(W_j)]).

This follows from the original one-gamma-per-edge identity. Its eight cycle edge factors are N gamma_m; each of the four rung factors is h0_j gamma_j. The eight actual black/white source scalars are those in (8), not inferred from a closed theta norm. The mark’s single quantum-dimension normal is treated only after this full contraction.

Insert (9) and then (1). The remaining scalar is

(N gamma_m cV)^8 N^4
    product_r[k_j gamma_j^2 s_j^2/a_j^2].       (10)

There is no missing h0_j: its occurrence in the edge factor cancels its explicit reciprocal in the regular map (9).

The proved actual ordered scalar formula gives

s_j=N^3 A_N(m,m,j),
A_N(m,m,j)=(-1)^(j+1)
            q^[m^2-2m-j-3] P_(N,j).

Its positive magnitude P_(N,j) is the finite one-leg source, and its displayed ordered phase is from the full source theorem, not a permutation guess. Squaring and using (7) yields exactly

gamma_j^2 s_j^2/a_j^2
          =N^2 q^[2m^2-2]P_(N,j)^2,            (11)

INDEPENDENT of j apart from P_(N,j). Also N gamma_m cV=q1. The complete phase in (10),(11) is

q^[8-32m^2]=q^[8N(2-N)]=1.

The powers of N are N^4 times N^8, and this proves (2). Full Cartan and all fixed signed crossing powers give precisely (3), as follows from the all-normal eigenvalue equality R^e L_j=omega_j^e L_j.

5. Exact global support and a whole four-color bound

The positive Newton multiplication theorem applies to every h_j in (1). Its coefficients are strictly positive at all indices 0,…,j. If sum j_r<d, the product has degree below d, so phi=0. If sum j_r>=d, choose integers 0<=v_r<=j_r with sum v_r=d. Their product has positive coefficient at output d: at each multiplication choose its upper index, the current partial sum. These partial sums stay <=d<N, so none of the allowed products vanishes. All other coefficients are nonnegative. Thus phi(product h_j)>0, proving (4) without a norm or stationary-phase approximation.

For v<=j<m the exact sine quotient is

z_(j+1)(v)/z_j(v)
   =sin[pi(j+v+1)/N]/sin[pi(j-v+1)/N]>=1.

The difference of these sines is 2cos[pi(j+1)/N]sin(pi v/N)>=0. The new coefficient at v=j+1 is positive. In the actual Newton cone this proves

0<=h_j<=h_m,
0<P_(N,j)<=S_N.                                (12)

Multiplication and phi preserve the cone. Equations (2),(12), and k_j<=2 consequently give

0<=Z_jvec<=16 Z_(m,m,m,m).                      (13)

Let Z_four-rung be the actual source obtained by replacing the four rung sectors by their FULL complete circles, while retaining the eight central cycle sectors and taking zero rung twists. Linearity of the original source makes it the sum of (2) over all four labels. It follows that

Z_St<=Z_four-rung<=16(m+1)^4 Z_St.              (14)

The same statement holds after removing the common unit phase of arbitrary fixed cycle twists. It is not a triangle-inequality bound on an oscillating rung sum: the summed source itself is positive.

There is also a strict full-source color gap. For every fixed delta>0, the proved one-leg factorial estimate supplies gamma_delta>0 with

max_(m-j>=delta N) P_(N,j)/S_N
             <=poly_delta(N)exp(-gamma_delta N).

Using (12) in (2) gives the entire four-color complement bound

sum_(max_r(m-j_r)>=delta N) Z_jvec
   <=poly_delta(N)exp(-2gamma_delta N) Z_St.    (15)

All finite source values, not only a selected term or a value-only Casimir symbol, occur in (14),(15). The bounds are uniform over the whole four-rung physical color cube and include j=0 and paired axes. The empty range for an oversized delta is understood literally.

The proved positive central period rate and (14) therefore give

lim_(N odd) (2pi/N)log Z_four-rung
   =16v8+2Vol(Q_cube)=Vol(actual augmented cube link).

The eight fixed central cycle projections are still a substantive restriction; (14) does not allow their removal or control interference from the remaining cycle colors. This partial-source statement is not a full cube volume-conjecture theorem.

Audit: all-rung actual marked contractions and full local normals, with recorded results. It compares every rung tuple at N=3,5,7, further physical tuples at N=9,11, and a signed original edge word at each level. Every actual U cyclic identity is checked including its first normal; paired rung nilpotents and the full half-degree turn are checked as operators. The complex positivity and global support follow from the analytical proof above, not a sign or nonvanishing interpretation in a finite field.

The four-rung source result is not an equality for the full cube invariant.
