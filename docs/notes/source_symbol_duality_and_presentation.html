Source-symbol certificates and dependence on the presentation

Independent audit and direct proofs, 27 October 2026. A nonzero ordinary-color anchor does not imply simple root monodromy. The correct selected-source condition has an exact dual certificate, weaker than simplicity. The canonical actual Fourier–Mellin source supplies that certificate without deleting Jordan blocks.

This complements the source-corrected normal connection. It does not identify the Mellin source line with the meridian regular-profile line or prove the selected regulator norm.

1. The exact source/dual certificate

Let E=ℂ(Y)E=\mathbb C(Y), T0=B0σζT_0=B_0\sigma_\zeta be invertible, H=T0NH=T_0^N, L=H−IL=H-I, and let 0≠v∈ker⁡L0\ne v\in\ker L be the selected root section. Then ι(v)≠0 in coker⁡L⇔rank⁡[L∣v]>rank⁡L⇔∃w:wL=0,wv≠0. \iota(v)\ne0\text{ in }\operatorname{coker}L \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \operatorname{rank}[L\mid v]>\operatorname{rank}L \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists w:\ wL=0,\quad wv\ne0. \tag{1} These are equivalent elementary vector-space statements: ι(v)=0\iota(v)=0 means v∈im⁡Lv\in\operatorname{im}L, and the annihilator of im⁡L\operatorname{im}L is the left kernel of LL. Thus the needed witness is a dual monodromy cocycle that sees the actual section. A physical cut with nonzero value need not be such a cocycle.

There is a direct minors certificate at every rank. Choose a nonzero rr-minor A=LI,JA=L_{I,J}, where r=rank⁡Lr=\operatorname{rank}L. For i∉Ii\notin I, put wi=eit−Li,JA−1eIt. w_i=e_i^t-L_{i,J}A^{-1}e_I^t . The rank assumption gives wiL=0w_iL=0, and det⁡(AvILi,Jvi)=det⁡(A)wiv. \det\begin{pmatrix}A&v_I\\L_{i,J}&v_i\end{pmatrix} =\det(A)\,w_iv. \tag{2} At least one such minor is nonzero exactly when (1) holds. The rank-zero case uses the empty determinant convention: the certificate is simply a nonzero coordinate of vv.

In corank one, any source cut ℓ\ell with ℓv≠0\ell v\ne0 gives the single bordered certificate det⁡(Lvℓ0)=−ℓadj⁡(L)v. \det\begin{pmatrix}L&v\\\ell&0\end{pmatrix} =-\ell\,\operatorname{adj}(L)v. \tag{3} As ker⁡L=Ev\ker L=Ev, the rank-one adjugate satisfies adj⁡(L)v=tr⁡(adj⁡L)v\operatorname{adj}(L)v=\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{adj}L)v. Therefore (3) is nonzero exactly when ι(v)≠0\iota(v)\ne0. If Δ(t)=det⁡(tI−H)\Delta(t)=\det(tI-H), its exact normalization is det⁡(Lvℓ0)ℓv=(−1)d∂tΔ(1),d=dim⁡EM. \frac{\det\left(\begin{smallmatrix}L&v\\\ell&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)} {\ell v} =(-1)^d\partial_t\Delta(1),\qquad d=\dim_E M. \tag{4} This supplies the requested adjugate/source test. A simple norm root is sufficient, but (1) remains valid in higher corank, where an adjugate or a first characteristic derivative can vanish despite a visible selected section.

There is also an exact splitting interpretation. If T0v=vT_0v=v, then (1) holds if and only if EvEv has a T0T_0-equivariant retraction from EdE^d. For a witness normalized by wv=1wv=1, the projection p=vwp=vw commutes with HH. Average its conjugates: p‾=1N∑j=0N−1T0jpT0−j. \bar p=\frac1N\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}T_0^jpT_0^{-j}. Since T0NpT0−N=HpH−1=pT_0^NpT_0^{-N}=HpH^{-1}=p, this is a T0T_0-equivariant retraction onto EvEv. Conversely any such retraction has a row ww with wL=0,wv=1wL=0,wv=1. Thus an invariant source line can fail to split even when it is an ordinary saturated vector subspace.

2. Actual polynomial pointing does not imply simplicity

The actual unknot already distinguishes the statements. Its color functions are exactly Cj(Q)=1C_j(Q)=1, including their full polynomial pointing, and its minimal meridian operator is S−1S-1. Use instead the equally valid actual annihilator P2=(S−1)2. P_2=(S-1)^2 . Its solution companion is A=(01−12)=I+J,J2=0,J≠0. A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}=I+J,\qquad J^2=0,\ J\ne0 . For T=A−1σζT=A^{-1}\sigma_\zeta, H=A−N=I−NJ,ker⁡(H−I)=im⁡(H−I)=E(1,1)t. H=A^{-N}=I-NJ,\qquad \ker(H-I)=\operatorname{im}(H-I)=E(1,1)^t . The actual source v=(1,1)tv=(1,1)^t has ι(v)=0\iota(v)=0, even though its first-coordinate cut and every color anchor equal one. The cut (1,0)(1,0) does not annihilate H−IH-I. Thus neither explicit polynomial colors nor their nonzero anchor forces the source-symbol condition in an arbitrary actual presentation.

Even failure of a simple norm root does not imply failure of the selected symbol. The actual unknot annihilator (S−Q)(S−1) (S-Q)(S-1) has a companion with distinct eigenvalues 1,Q1,Q at every root of level N≥2N\ge2. Its monodromy is H=IH=I; hence ι\iota is an isomorphism although Δ(t)=(t−1)2\Delta(t)=(t-1)^2 has zero derivative at one. The dual certificate (1), rather than simplicity alone, is the appropriate invariant for the selected direction.

Removing a known extra left factor is legitimate only with its source comparison. Scalar-operator module quotients induce inclusions of solution modules, so left multiplication is not a regular gauge of the same normal module. In the squared example, the inclusion of the true rank-one source line into the enlarged Jordan module sends its cokernel symbol to zero. This explains exactly why the first-normal construction was not claimed invariant under arbitrary annihilator left multiples.

3. Root saturation does not choose the whole normal quotient

The exact diagnostic 𝒜=(S−1)(S−Z),Z=QN,GN=1+YN \mathcal A=(S-1)(S-Z),\qquad Z=Q^N,\qquad G_N=1+Y^N from the source-corrected note makes the issue explicit. The minimal scalar operator of its root section 1+U1+U is S−1S-1, but (S−1)GN=(Z−1)YN,(S−Z)GN=1−Z. (S-1)G_N=(Z-1)Y^N,\qquad (S-Z)G_N=1-Z. Neither whole factor annihilates this source. Replacing the whole problem by its minimal root factor loses the source-normal rule.

Given the full actual source, one can make a rank-one whole quotient: S−1+ZYN1+YN. S-\frac{1+Z\,Y^N}{1+Y^N}. But its first-normal source-corrected connection is U∂U−U1+U, U\partial_U-\frac{U}{1+U}, so the quotient has retained the chosen source ratio itself. For the other exact source A+BYNA+B Y^N, the corresponding quotient and connection instead contain A+BZYNA+BYN,U∂U−BUA+BU. \frac{A+B ZY^N}{A+B Y^N},\qquad U\partial_U-\frac{BU}{A+BU}. All have the same minimal root factor S−1S-1. When A+B=2A+B=2, all root color values agree as well. Consequently a root source-generated or saturated quotient can restore a rank-one symbol but does not canonically determine its whole normal lift from the root operator and anchor.

This does not deny the canonical quotient determined by a supplied full marked source. It prevents using that quotient as an operator-only proof of the very source comparison still needed.

4. The canonical actual Mellin source supplies a visible dual

There is a constructive way to obtain the certificate for every knot, in the actual sourced Fourier–Mellin polarization. Retain the canonical finite-color source: Z(Q,X)=∑n≥n0Cn(Q)Xn,A(Q,X,S)Z=B(Q,X),Sf(X)=f(QX). Z(Q,X)=\sum_{n\ge n_0}C_n(Q)X^n,\qquad A(Q,X,S)Z=B(Q,X),\qquad Sf(X)=f(QX). Here A=PMA=P^M, and B≠0B\ne0 is a fixed Laurent polynomial built from finitely many fixed actual colors. Its nonzero lowest coefficient was proved in that construction.

Assume positive S-order and retain the canonical homogenization Ph=(S−σQB/B)A. P_h=(S-\sigma_QB/B)A . \tag{5} On its companion vector v=(f,σf,…)tv=(f,\sigma f,\ldots)^t, the literal observable source row is ℓv=Af/B. \ell v=Af/B . The defining coefficient identity of (5) gives the quotient dynamics σQ(Af/B)=Af/B\sigma_Q(Af/B)=Af/B. Equivalently its companion matrix satisfies σQ(ℓ)Ah=ℓ. \sigma_Q(\ell)A_h=\ell . Thus, for its T=Ah−1σQT=A_h^{-1}\sigma_Q, the row intertwines TT with the scalar shift. At a level-N root, ℓH0=ℓ,ℓv0=AZN/B=1. \ell H_0=\ell,\qquad \ell v_0=AZ_N/B=1. \tag{6} Equations (1) and (6) prove ι(v0)≠0\iota(v_0)\ne0, whether or not the rest of H0H_0 is semisimple. This is an observable quotient of the same actual scalar state, not an unused adjoined unit.

This construction is robust under the original operator presentation. If the original actual annihilator is replaced by a left multiple, one rebuilds its canonical Fourier–Mellin source BB and the homogenization (5). The same finite-color construction makes that BB nonzero and (6) holds again. No deletion of Jordan blocks and no minimal-annihilator assertion is needed. If the transformed S-order is zero, AZ=BAZ=B is its exact rational algebraic equation; there is no need to manufacture a normal companion.

5. Formal first lifts suffice for the rational obstruction

The root rationality of the cyclotomic Mellin fiber gives ZN∈E=ℂ(X)Z_N\in E=\mathbb C(X). The first derivative of the actual whole series is only asserted to lie in F=ℂ((X))F=\mathbb C((X)). This is enough.

Each fixed Cn(Q)C_n(Q) is Laurent polynomial and hence regular at the root. Coefficientwise differentiation gives a formal Z1Z_1 with the SAME fixed lower X bound n0n_0. No common analytic q-neighborhood, derivative growth bound in nn, or convergent X-series is required. Rational coefficients of the fixed operator and of B−1B^{-1} are regular over E[[ϵ]]E[[\epsilon]] for every sufficiently large N: nonzero fixed rational Q-coefficients have only finitely many exceptional root values. A possible moving X-pole order in their epsilon expansion causes no problem in F[[ϵ]]F[[\epsilon]].

Negative Laurent S-powers must be normalized by a fixed left shift applied to BOTH AA and BB; the observable source becomes its same shifted quotient. The proof then applies without changing its pointing. No pole coefficient is discarded before specialization.

The theorem removes central scalar freedom on this canonical selected Mellin line. It does not exclude arbitrary homogeneous source-kernel additions, identify another supplied module with that line, or estimate this residue.

There is a necessary directional qualification for other affine objects. In the root regularization ARN=bNAR_N=b_N, the earlier central transformation acts as (RN,1)↦(cRN+(c−1)pN,1), (R_N,1)\longmapsto(cR_N+(c-1)p_N,1), rather than multiplying the augmented vector by cc. The Mellin argument above works because the tested comparison is a central scalar multiple of the SAME actual scalar ZNZ_N, whose observable quotient is then multiplied by that scalar.
