The original undamped Conway quartic has a reciprocal source integral

24 November 2026. Research proof. Earlier finals and manuscripts are unchanged.

The original physical quartic needs no damping of its closing row for a reciprocal-integral comparison at exponential scale. Its positive integral approximates the reciprocal squared source with a universal relative error, independent of the level, tree size and leaf packets. No minimizer, temperature, or Hessian determinant is input.

The addition to [R] is removal of its level-dependent damping and compensating budget. The original objective and budgets are retained. The comparison here has a fixed error, whereas [R]’s damped formula has a relative error tending to zero. Neither formula evaluates its integral from geometry.

5. Logarithmic consequence and scope

Let SNrec=ℬN2/𝒫NS_N^{\mathrm{ rec}}=\mathcal B_N^2/\mathcal P_N, with reciprocal zero at infinity. For every nonzero source, (5) gives |πNlogSNrec−2πNlog|KN||≤−πNlog⁡(1−δ)≤π2047N.(18) \left|\frac\pi N\log S_N^{\mathrm{ rec}} -\frac{2\pi}N\log|K_N|\right| \le-\frac\pi N\log(1-\delta) \le\frac\pi{2047N}. \qquad\text{(18)} Zeros coincide exactly. The same bound holds between πN−1log⁡(1+SNrec)\pi N^{-1}\log(1+S_N^{\mathrm{ rec}}) and πN−1log⁡(1+|KN|2)\pi N^{-1}\log(1+|K_N|^2), including at zeros. For any fixed positive target rate, convergence to that rate for the reciprocal expression is therefore equivalent to convergence for the original squared source. No eventual nonvanishing was assumed.

The comparison constant is universal, not chosen from any source value or filling packet. The closing row, full physical algebra, normal channels, objective and original budgets have not changed. The integral is not claimed to be a separately topological quantity at each finite level. Its signed source remains inside the denominator. No geometric energy estimate, contributing-cycle coefficient, arbitrary-short-filling volume theorem or finite-exception theorem is supplied by this representation.
