Orthogonal elimination of the linear Conway packet variables

21 November 2026. Research note. Earlier final notes and public manuscripts remain unchanged.

After retaining one child packet at each binary gate, every other packet can be eliminated by an unconstrained, uniformly positive definite linear solve. The original unit-ball constraints on those variables are automatically inactive. The resulting objective is the norm of a genuine orthogonal projection, with its full Gram matrix retained.

A second form uses only the retained gate equations and the original scalar closing row. Its boundary Gram matrix lies between II and (226/225)I(226/225)I, uniformly in the level and tree size. This is a reduction of the actual finite variational problem, not a geometric evaluation of its minimum.

1. The original energy and the variables to retain

Use the complete normalized compiler of the convex source theorem. At level NN, there are ss proper packet variables in the full complex Newton space HNH_N, including the repeated-root directions. Each prescribed leaf, linear turn and bilinear product has recorded Hilbert–Schmidt norm at most σ=1/16\sigma=1/16. The original normalized closing row is LL, with ∥L∥≤σ\|L\|\le\sigma. Its real Hessian lies between II and 3I3I. The complete original marked source satisfies Btot2e*≤|KN|2≤γBtot2e*,e*:=min⁡E,γ=197/196.(2) B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2e_*\le |K_N|^2 \le\gamma B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2e_*,\qquad e_*:=\min E,\quad\gamma=197/196. \qquad\text{(2)} The normalization budget BtotB_{\mathrm{ tot}} is retained throughout.

Fix uu in its product of unit balls. At each product, one child is retained and the other is eliminated. 6. Scope of the simplification

The exact reduced minimum in (9) satisfies the unchanged source bounds (2). Equation (20) also permits replacing its boundary Gram by the identity with an explicitly proved constant comparison. That step is valid because (17)–(20) control this particular Gram matrix. It is not a rule for discarding arbitrary quantum boundary metrics.

This construction concerns the budget-normalized recursion errors. It is distinct from a quotient metric on the physical tangle synthesis map, which can have exponential condition number. All original normalization factors remain in BtotB_{\mathrm{ tot}}. No actual crossing or product is asserted to be unitary in an unscaled physical metric.

Elimination has removed the inner constrained optimization, exposed a true orthogonal projection and reduced the number of nonlinear packet variables. It has not supplied a positive geometric trial value at the volume rate. The original coherent closing remains one component of FF, and exact source cancellation remains possible. The finite degree-four formulation can be retained by keeping the linear variables and the original gate equations; explicit elimination instead produces the rational function (11), or the higher-degree polynomial vector in (18). Neither representation alone gives the missing hyperbolic lower bound after arbitrary fixed partial fillings.
