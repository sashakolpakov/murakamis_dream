A literal middle five-point operator identity with its complete finite source

28 October 2026. This realizes the middle classical certificate

+partial(a,b,p,p_1,s_M)

from the crossing fundamental cycle as an exact finite relative operator identity inside the original crossing factorization. Its developed arguments are the five cross-ratios of that specific configuration, with the required quad/orientation changes. The X face is a strict cut of a cyclic unit; its full projector correction is explicit. The original cup selects a nonzero top marked full-Phi value.

This is an identity with marked full-Phi and nilpotent Euler operators, including their factorial face frames and circle/radial sources. It does not assert that each factor separately is an already assigned positive charged tetrahedron in a regular geometric face frame. That further face/Fourier assignment, the other two certificates, and the global selected cycle remain separate work.

1. Fixed actual coordinates and the original crossing factor

Use complete original heads with

Q=exp(2pi*i/N), d=N-1, x=-q^(-1), lambda=x^(-1)=Qx,
S_1^N=mu, S_2^N=x^N mu,
Z=Z_1, X=x S_2S_1^(-1), XZ=QZX, X^N=Z^N=1.

The literal five flags were calculated in the middle-configuration note. With every right flag tag retained, an explicit bounded homogeneous frame sends them to

(a,b,p,p_1,s_M)=(0,1-X,infinity,(1-Z,1)^t,1). (1)

The fourth entry is a homogeneous column; no inverse of 1-Z is taken. Put

P(A)=sum_(h=0)^d (-1)^hQ^(h(h-1)/2)A^h/F_h,
F_h=(Q;Q)_h,
n=e_1S_2=(Q-Z^(-1))X, P=P(n),
n_-= -e_1Z_2S_2.

The exact original uncheck crossing is

R_0=D P(n_-)P.                               (2)

D is the original Cartan diagonal. Its common scalar line kappa, framing and peripheral factors remain external and identical throughout. The checked crossing is flip R_0 with that same line; none of these factors is reset.

2. The fifth clock is an actual quantum cross-ratio

Define

H=P^(-1)ZP=Z(1-n)=Z+(1-QZ)X.                (3)

The finite Euler recurrence proves the middle equality. Thus H is invertible, H^N=1 and nH=QHn, without taking any singular reciprocal of 1-Z. Its commutative symbol is

H_cl=X+Z-XZ,

the H appearing in the five cross-ratios of (1).

Let Psi be the full literature function with the exact normalization in the modular moment theorem. Retain

phi_N=sqrt(N) exp{i pi[1/4-(N+N^(-1))/12]}.

The elementary marked root evaluation, independently proved in the marked-flag note, gives

F_Z=marked Psi(QZ)
    =phi_N diag_j[1/F_j], 0<=j<N.           (4)

The argument at clock index j is on logarithmic lift 2pii(j+1)/N. Thus j=d uses the regular TOP value, not the zero on the bottom lift. The finite missing arrow of n lies at input j=0. The exact conjugation is

F_Z n F_Z^(-1)=n(1-Z).                      (5)

Transport the entire clock mark, rather than choosing a new one, by

F_H=P^(-1)F_ZP.                              (6)

It is the marked full-Phi function of QH in this transported integer frame. Equations (3), (5), and commutation of P with n give

F_H n F_H^(-1)=n(1-H).                      (7)

All matrices in (3)–(7) are invertible where claimed at the physical root. The inverse of 1-H is not used.

3. The exact two-to-three word and the original R

Since n and -nH Q-commute and share nilpotent degree, the finite ordered binomial theorem proves

F_H P=P P(-nH)F_H.

Equivalently the literal two-to-three sequence is

P(n)F_H^(-1)=F_H^(-1)P(n)P(-nH).            (8)

Multiplying by the displayed interface F_H gives an exact refactorization of the original crossing, including every exterior operator:

R_0=D P(n_-) F_H^(-1)P(n)P(-nH)F_H.         (9)

The final F_H is a retained face/source adapter; deleting it would change the original period. In a network one can use (8) on that specified subword or (9) as a full crossing replacement with its adapter. These are not definitions of a new invariant.

4. All five developed arguments and the exact X-wall

Use the actual preceding factors to transport each argument to the common input frame. The two arguments on the left of (8) are n and the negative clock Z, since PHP^(-1)=Z. The three on the right are the negative clock H and

u=F_H^(-1)nF_H,
w=-F_H^(-1)P nH P^(-1)F_H
  =-F_H^(-1)nZF_H.                           (10)

These are explicit finite matrices, and they simplify to

K=X(1-E_0(Z)),
u_Z=-Z^(-1)K,
u=P^(-1)u_ZP,
w=QK=QX(1-E_0(Z)).                          (11)

Here E_0 is the original first-clock zero-index projector. It is the required finite source wall, not an omitted exceptional case.

For a proof, F_Z^(-1)nF_Z=u_Z follows from consecutive factorial ratios. Also

n=u_Z+QK, u_Z(QK)=Q(QK)u_Z,
P(n)=P(u_Z)P(QK).                            (12)

Every term of degree >=N vanishes because K is a strict cut shift. Conjugation by P(u_Z) sends QK to QK(1-u_Z)^(-1). Substitution of (6) into w in (10) then gives

w=P^(-1)[-u_Z Z(1-u_Z)^(-1)]P
  =P^(-1)[QK(1-u_Z)^(-1)]P=QK,

proving the last identity of (11) at all levels.

The corresponding exact ordered quad relation is

r=wH^(-1)=-u(1-Q^(-1)u)^(-1), r^N=0.        (13)

The inverses in (12)–(13) exist because u is nilpotent. They are not inverses of a clock determinant with a nontrivial kernel.

Classically, away from the boundary divisors, (10)–(13) become

u_cl=n_cl/(1-H_cl)=-X/[Z(1-X)],
w_cl=X,
r_cl=X/H_cl,
u_cl=r_cl/(r_cl-1).

Thus u is the negative-quad coordinate of the tetrahedron with cross-ratio X/H, while w is the precisely cut X tetrahedron. The developed signed list of (8) is therefore

left:  [n_cl]-[Z],
right: -[H_cl]-[X/H_cl]+[X].                 (14)

This is exactly the middle certificate of (1), since n_cl=-X(1-Z)/Z is the positive-quad coordinate of its omitted-a tetrahedron. It is not a five-point relation for freely chosen unrelated flags.

Statement (14) identifies the rational geometric arguments and their quantum cuts. It does not discard the nonlinear factorial frames in assigning a positive tetrahedral kernel or its complete phase. Those frames remain in (4), (6), and the period construction below.

5. The complete original source is nonzero and polynomial

Keep the actual cup and full marker

p=N sum_(a=0)^d x^(a-d)|a,d-a>,
m|a,b>=x^b/N^2, mp=1.

Then exactly

P(n)p=N|d,0>,
Hp=Q^(-1)p,
F_Hp=(phi_N/N)p.                            (15)

To prove the first identity, the coefficient of |u,d-u> is N x^(u-d) times

sum_(h=0)^(d-u) Q^(-h(u+1))
              {u+h choose h}_Q
         ={N choose u+1}_(Q^(-1)).

The equality is the finite Gaussian hockey-stick identity. At the primitive root it is zero for u<d and one for u=d. The remaining identities in (15) follow from (3), (4), and (6). In particular the actual cup selects the TOP marked QH argument, with value phi_N/N.

Consequently both sides of (8) have the exact source value

mP(n)F_H^(-1)p=phi_N^(-1).                  (16)

This is nonzero and has modulus N^(-1/2). In (9) the retained final adapter has factor phi_N/N on the cup. The full checked original crossing therefore has exactly its old source value, including kappa and all original framing/peripheral lines. The scalar has not been reset to one in (8).

The complete cut correction is known at every order, not just to first order:

w^a=Q^a X^a(1-sum_(j=0)^(a-1)E_j(Z)),
mw^ap=1-a/N, 0<=a<N.                        (17)

These formulas follow from the strict shift and the original uniform marked clock distribution. Thus the projector correction can be of order one at bulk a; it is not legitimate to remove it because an individual endpoint weight is 1/N.

At an interior position of a longer word one must keep F_H as an operator and every projector of (17). The scalar simplification (15) applies to this actual input cup. The earlier counterexamples to untransported interior cut relocation are unaffected.

6. Full-Phi/Fourier frames and all radial components

Every operator in (8)–(9) has an explicit fixed-complexity full-Phi/Gaussian relative-period realization. Let

T=J_(minus,1) tensor S_2,
D_n|j>=lambda^j(-1)^jQ^(j(j-1)/2)|j>,
H_n|j>=D_n(j)|j>/F_j.

Then the exact frames are

n=H_n T H_n^(-1),
u_Z=D_n T D_n^(-1),
w=lambda T,
H_n=phi_N^(-1)D_nF_Z.                       (18)

D_n is a unitary linear/quadratic Gaussian at unit marks. T is the literal strict compression of a unit shift; its missing Fourier/Hardy endpoint is exactly the projector in (11), not a new boundary convention. For unit S_2 all these cores have norm at most one. Their inverses as Euler operators use the opposite root/orientation, retaining its phase.

For each Euler polynomial in a contractive core use the exact circle-minus-radial operator

P_epsilon(T)=B_epsilon(T)-E_epsilon(T)

of the modular-circle theorem. For diagonal frames use

(F_Z)_j=phi_N(-1)^jQ^(-j(j-1)/2)
                     [B_+(j)-E_+(j)],
(F_Z^(-1))_j=(N/phi_N)Q^(-j-1)
                     [B_+(d-j)-E_+(d-j)].    (19)

All phases and the factors phi_N,N are retained. For example P(-nH)=P^(-1)P(-nZ)P, and -nZ=H_n(-TZ)H_n^(-1), another contractive core. Equations (6), (18), and (19) therefore expand the full word (9) into finitely many explicit Phi/Gaussian integrals and their original coefficient cycles.

Every bracket in (19), and every transfer bracket, is kept as B-E in its original order. Expanding their product includes ALL circle/radial components with their signs; no individual component is identified with the whole original scalar. The radial kernel alone has the proven harmonic or diagonal polynomial bound, while other full-Phi factors can still give an exponential period. No global domination estimate is inferred from those local bounds.

The exact operator identity also transports every actual Ward current: apply the ordinary typed product rule to (8) or (9). In particular, the X-wall is QXE_0=QE_dX, whose original current is explicitly

[F_tot,E_dX]=(Q^(-1)-1)mu^(-1)
                          |d><0| tensor S_2^2.

It remains in (11), (17), and (19). The other current terms are finite commutators of the displayed P, F_Z and their inverses; differentiating the exact operator word gives their equal full sum. This is not a claim of sectorwise Ward vanishing.

7. Scope and exact audit

This provides the literal middle five-point relative operator sequence, its original R embedding, its marked full-Phi clocks, every strict projector moment, and the nonzero original cup coefficient. It is an actual finite identity at every N, including even N, with no hypothetical source replacement.

To obtain a complete positive geometric tensor presentation one must still assign these framed operators to the proper oriented geometric face kernels and transport the actual relative cycle. The other two classical certificates are not declared proved by (8). A local operator equality does not give a global geometric intersection degree.
