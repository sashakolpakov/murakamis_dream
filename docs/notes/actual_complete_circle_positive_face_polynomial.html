The actual complete circle as a positive face polynomial

30 October 2026. The complete ROOT circle, with its original weight and literal W/pi cup normalization, has an exact homogeneous polynomial factorization in its four face diagonals. For EVERY original folded channel that polynomial has nonnegative coefficients. Its local zeros enforce the original unwrapped channel support on a modular physical-head grid. Both level parities are included.

This is local source algebra. The global face-grid measure, marked coefficient, stationary angle balance and original relative period are separate statements.

1. Exact local variables, lifts and scalar

Put N>=3, d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, and keep an actual integer 0<=a<=floor(d/2). Write m=d-a, s=d-2a and

beta_a=S_(2a)/S_a^2, S_j=product_(r=1)^j 2sin(pi r/N),
k_a=1 if s=0, otherwise k_a=2.

Let x,b,c,y be the outer, incoming-middle, outgoing-middle and final face diagonals, respectively. Choose their logarithms and use the literal centered physical heads

S=d/2+N(Log x-Log b)/(2pi i),
R=d/2+N(Log x-Log c)/(2pi i),
sigma=d+N(Log x-Log y)/(2pi i).                    (1)

The other physical heads are sigma-S and sigma-R. The auxiliary row is r=sigma-m, with its actual complement 2a-r. Thus

Q^S=-q^-1 x/b, Q^(sigma-S)=-q^-1 b/y,
Q^R=-q^-1 x/c, Q^(sigma-R)=-q^-1 c/y.

The physical half-index and the auxiliary finite color are not confused. Define, using the original Hermitian matrices H_-,H_+,

D_a(x,z,y)=det[z I+(x/2)H_-+(y/2)H_+],
Ptilde_s(x,y)=product_(j=a+1)^(d-a)(x-Q^j y).       (2)

The empty determinants and products are one. Then the full coefficient of the specified regular root complete-circle source is EXACTLY

h0_a W_a pi_a
  =k_a beta_a^2
     D_a(x,b,y) D_a(x,c,y) Ptilde_s(x,y)/(bc)^(d/2). (3)

This is the fixed-root Hopf lift from the original source envelope, not a newly chosen off-grid continuation of a canceled exceptional kernel. A retained half twist multiplies (3) by its original fixed scalar omega_a^epsilon. Every power in (3) uses the logarithms in (1).

If one adds literal N-lifts to R,S,sigma, the multiplier is (-1)1. The degree lift n_sigma cancels. It is identically one at odd N; at even N it must be retained or canceled by a proved global source-character calculation. Formula (3) makes no independent wrapping of an out-of-basis head.

2. Proof from the literal large white and cups

At the root the actual white and dual are

W_a(i,j;r)=(-1)^m q^[-dr+di-m(m-1)] H_m^1(Q^i,Q^j)/F_m,
i+j=m+r, F_m=(Q;Q)_m,
pi_a(r;i,j)=gamma_a^-1 p_(2a)(2a-r)
   W_a(d-j,d-i;2a-r)/[pV(d-j)pV(d-i)].

Here gamma_a=q(2-a2)/N^2 is FIXED, h0_a=k_a N beta_a, pV(z)=N q(-d2+dz), and no paired norm pi_a W_a is inverted. The exact Chevalley factor is W_a(d-j,d-i;2a-r)=q2W_a(i,j;r).

Let T=Q^sigma and P_s(T)=product_(j=a)(m-1)(T-Qj). The exact root reflection of the SPECIFIED factorial section gives

p_(2a)(d+a-sigma)
      =F_s q^[2m(d+a)] T^-a/P_s(T).                (4)

Indeed p_(d-s)(z)=F_s exp(pi i z)q3 /product_(j=1)s(1-Q(z+j)); substitute z=d+a-sigma and retain exp(pi i z)=q^(Nz) literally. This proves (4) before canceling its possible poles. The root identities H_m=H_a P_s and

H_a(U,V)=V^a K_a det[I-(qU/2)H_--(q^-1/(2V))H_+],
K_a=q^(a^2) beta_a,

are source-exact finite polynomial identities.

For comparison with the previously normalized entire circle C_a, substitution in the preceding literal W/pi expression gives the same head factor q4/P_s(T) as in C_a. Its remaining scalar ratio is one: use

F_j=(-i)^j q^[j(j+1)/2] S_j, S_(d-j)=N/S_j.

The ratio reduces to q5=1. This proves h0_a W_a pi_a=C_a as the specified meromorphic root functions, not just at physical nodes. It does not identify their moving-q germs with a different generic lift.

Now use the exact original normalized coefficient

C_a=k_a V_a(d-sigma) q^[d(R+S-sigma)-2a^2]
              H_a(U_o,V_o)H_a(U_i,V_i)/T^a,
V_a(h)=product_(j=a+1)^m 2sin(pi(h+j)/N).

On the logarithms (1), direct factorization gives

V_a(d-sigma)=Ptilde_s(x,y)/(xy)^(s/2),
H_a(-q^-1 x/z,-q^-1 z/y)=(-q^-1)^a K_a y^-a D_a(x,z,y),
q^[d(R+S-sigma)]=(xy/(bc))^(d/2).                 (5)

For the first identity, the scalar in the sine product is i^s q6=i^s q^(-sN/2)=1. The remaining powers of xy cancel because d=2a+s, and K_a^2 q(-2a2)=beta_a^2. This proves (3) with its exact scalar. The same formulas show that additional N-lifts contribute (-1)7=(-1)8, since s-d=-2a.

3. Coefficient positivity and homogeneity

D_a is homogeneous of degree a in x,z,y and has NONNEGATIVE REAL coefficients. This follows directly from the actual positive matrices, without assuming their entries are real. Factor H_-=B_-B_-* and H_+=B_+B_+*, and apply Cauchy–Binet to the rectangular matrix

[sqrt(z) I, sqrt(x/2) B_-, sqrt(y/2) B_+].

Its determinant expansion is a sum of squared absolute minors times the indicated monomials, hence has the claimed coefficients. Since all three source matrices I,H_-,H_+ are positive definite, every homogeneous monomial of degree a in the three variables in fact has positive coefficient: subtract a sufficiently small positive multiple of I from each matrix, then select the three identity parts in the same Cauchy–Binet expansion. This is a coefficient statement for each fixed N,a; no uniform lower coefficient bound is claimed.

More strongly, the retained forced product has the exact positive expansion for EVERY original folded label:

Ptilde_s(x,y)=sum_(j=0)^s [S_s/(S_j S_(s-j))] x^(s-j)y^j. (7)

To prove it, multiply its finite linear factors, or induct on their number, to obtain the coefficient (-1)^j Q9 [s choose j]_Q. The exact finite Gaussian coefficient is [s choose j]Q=q10 S_s/(S_j S(s-j)). The remaining q exponent is j[2a+2+j-1+s-j]=j(2a+1+s)=jN. Its factor q(jN)=(-1)j cancels the displayed sign. This proves (7), whose coefficients are strictly positive because s<N. It also retains both maximal-fold parities: Ptilde=1 at odd simple fold and Ptilde=x+y at even maximal fold. No quarter-channel restriction is needed.

The numerator in (3) is consequently a homogeneous polynomial of degree 2a+s=d with nonnegative coefficients in the four face variables. The monomial denominator and all finite logarithmic carries are still part of the source. Evaluating some face variables at complex values does not preserve positivity of the resulting specialized coefficients.

4. Local modular support retains one forced zero

Take physical heads i,j in {0,…,d}, sigma=i+j. In the original white the unwrapped auxiliary row is r=sigma-m, so its actual allowed range is 0<=r<=2a, equivalently

m<=sigma<=d+a.                                    (6)

At U=Qi,V=Qj, every Newton summand of H_a has factors forcing k<=i and a-k<=j. Therefore H_a(Qi,Qj)=0 if sigma<a. Exact root complementation sends the pair to (d-j,d-i), proving the same vanishing if sigma>2d-a.

The complete circle retains ONE factor P_s(Q^sigma) after (4) cancels one of the two factors from its large numerators. Hence:

  • If sigma<m, either sigma<a and the small numerator vanishes, or a<=sigma<m and the retained P_s vanishes.
  • If sigma>d+a, either sigma>2d-a and the complementary small numerator vanishes, or sigma-N belongs to [a,m-1] and P_s vanishes.

These are zeros of the canceled entire root coefficient, not an assignment of a value to an unexplained 0 times infinity. Thus a modular auxiliary residue outside its original support is killed locally even though its individual continued channel cup may have a pole.

If both physical pairs of a circle survive and their totals agree modulo N, both totals lie in the SAME interval (6), containing only 2a+1<=N integers. They therefore agree exactly. The auxiliary row is then the original unwrapped row. The complementary physical cup also adds no modular states: i+j congruent to d modulo N with 0<=i,j<=d forces i+j=d, since d-N<0 and d+N>2d.

This local check supplies the missing support input for a possible global face-grid transformation. The global primitive measure, even-level coset and carry phases, original marked-cap monomial and coefficient-extraction identity still require their own proof.
