A common head chart for the original charged Ward currents

30 October 2026. This makes the off-diagonal Ward construction on a specified tensor source explicit before its finite head sums. The charge-zero and neighboring charge-one bands use one primitive head lattice. Cutting a spanning tree reduces the proof to the independent-head identities of the actual tensors. Reclosing the auxiliary cuts gives explicit difference currents. The original support cycles and their endpoint differences remain part of the result; a common affine chart does not make those cycles equal.

1. Typed incidence equations and their component obstruction

Fix one channel assignment in the original generic source, including the full generic channel expansion of every routed R and R inverse. Keep all small and large partners, scalar denominators, physical turns and ordinary caps. Intermediate scalar poles are treated meromorphically before root specialization; to take a finite normal one first clears their known finite pole order and retains the required higher Taylor coefficients. No root projector replaces this generic expansion.

Each wire e carries its fixed integral highest weight L_e and an actual head h_e. At a typed vertex the K equation is

sum_out h_e-sum_in h_e
            =(sum_out L_e-sum_in L_e)/2.             (1)

These are ordinary signed INCIDENCE equations. A contracted wire is an output at one end and an input at the other. Cups and caps are vertices, not an unrecorded change of orientation. Their constants are respectively L and -L. At W:V_(2a)->V_d tensor V_d the constant is d-a; the dual has the opposite constant. Thus every constant in (1) is an integer in the actual source.

Delete the original exposed cut and the routed first-head cut. For their band delta put the first input/output heads a,a+delta and the second input/output heads l,l+delta. Let B be the incidence matrix of the remaining INTERNAL graph. Its equation is

B h=b(a,l)+delta q.                                 (2)

The signs of q come from the literal four port orientations. They are opposite unit charges at the two shifted boundary ends; they are not an assigned independent color. Integer degree conservation is retained, without reduction modulo N.

Check connectedness after THESE cuts on the actual typed graph. For a connected graph the only incidence relation is the sum of its rows, and the source makes the right side of (2) have sum zero. A spanning tree therefore gives an integer-affine solution for arbitrary complex a,l,delta. With x the heads on the non-tree edges,

h=h_*(a,l)+delta w+A x,                             (3)

where h_* and w have zero non-tree coordinates, w is the signed unit tree path for q, and A is the integral fundamental-cycle basis. The non-tree rows of A are the identity. All three bands delta=0,1,-1 therefore have the SAME primitive free coordinates x. No division by a quantum number or passage to an index-N cover occurs.

The internal CURRENT terms also have a specified chart: place the compensating charge on the auxiliary edge carrying the generator, instead of on the second actual cut, and use its signed unit tree path w_e in place of w. Its non-tree coordinates are again zero. Thus all possible placements have the same h_* and A and differ only by their recorded integral charge-path offsets. We do not mistake a charged intermediate tensor for a zero-band density.

For a disconnected graph the exact criterion is zero total charge in EACH component. Components containing neither cut are retained as their original scalar factors. If a charged right side fails the component criterion, its affine band has no original states; one must not invent a path joining those components. If the component conditions constrain a,l, keep those original affine strata rather than asserting a two-dimensional chart. On a geometric two-position chart the required connected deleted graph is the explicit criterion in the two-cut construction. For a chosen route U the graph is checked after its actual expansion; connectedness of a different uncut diagram is not a substitute.

The physical inequalities 0<=h_e<=L_e remain separate from (3). In particular a dimension-one channel can give a thin or empty physical support even though the incidence graph is connected.

2. The specified meromorphic bands and local identities

Use the actual large finite white polynomial and the specified moving-period factorial, with d and every L_e fixed as zeta varies. The independent-head W identities, proved in the source Ward calculation, are polynomial identities, not equalities only on root samples. Its cup/cap equations follow from the same exact unit-step factorial recurrence. They apply meromorphically also to fixed L_e>=N: the finite scalar factorial can then have a root zero and its pole is retained as just specified. Integer-affine source characters are kept; their unit-step multipliers are the actual recorded ones.

One may instead keep an original R as a four-port vertex only when using its specified literal factorial continuation. Its exact independent-variable Ward ratios are those of the original crossing currents. This is a second permitted primitive presentation, not an assertion that two different off-grid presentations coincide merely because their physical matrices agree. The full spectral W/cup presentation above already suffices for the routed source.

In (3), let s_delta(a,l,x) be the product of these actual factors with the indicated band charge, BEFORE applying the exposed coherent or root functional. It includes the fixed scalar, turn and peripheral characters of that convention; any extra generic boundary row must be separately specified and transported covariantly. No bare root mark is moved through this generic calculation. On the allowed physical lattice its original finite period is the actual coefficient of C(E_(l+delta,l)) from first input a to output a+delta. The zero band gives J_(a,l).

For the next displayed interior identity use UNTRUNCATED analytic coefficients

f(y)=1-Q^(y+1),
k(y)=zeta^(d-2y),
gamma(y)=zeta[Q^(y-N)-1]=e(y)/k(y).                 (4)

For example f(d)=1-Q^N here, whereas the finite matrix arrow f_d is zero. The distinction is indispensable at a genuine quantum normal. The physical endpoint will be restored by its source cycle, not by substituting a root simplification in (4).

3. The tree construction of the common difference currents

In addition to the two actual cuts, cut each non-tree edge of the internal graph. What remains is a typed TREE with auxiliary pairs of boundary heads. Its internal heads are uniquely determined by its boundary heads and (1). Its tensor entry is consequently a literal product of the specified factors, with no internal head sum. Propagating the original Hopf generator through this tree using the local identities proves its meromorphic Ward identity. Every coproduct prefix K factor and every ordered dual map stays in that propagation.

Now identify the two auxiliary heads of edge e with its common coordinate x_e, using the original identity contraction. The two insertions on this cut give a discrete product rule. With their ACTUAL coefficient and tensor factors denoted j,c, it is

j T_e c-(T_e^-1 j)c
                     =(T_e-I)[(T_e^-1 j)c],         (5)

where T_e shifts x_e by one. The shift acts on the WHOLE indicated product, including its insertion and all prefix characters. A reverse shift is put in this form by (T_e-1-I)u=-(T_e-I)T_e-1 u. These are actual array translations, not new single-port matrix actions.

This specifies the current algorithm: cut the stated tree, propagate the original generator in its original tensor order, and for each auxiliary cut collect the literal product on the right of (5), with its sign. Tree edges cancel pointwise. Each non-tree edge gives its own current j_(F,e) or j_(E,e). Equivalently the charge paths for the two placements of its insertion differ by exactly its fundamental cycle, which is x_e->x_e+1 in (3). This proves that the construction uses the same head chart, rather than silently comparing different charge lattices. The currents are finite sums of specified source products and shifts, not solutions chosen by interpolation.

At the two remaining cuts the result is the explicit interior band identity

f(l)s_+(a,l,x)-f(l-1)s_+(a,l-1,x)
   -f(a)[s_0(a,l,x)-s_0(a+1,l,x)]
                     =sum_e (T_e-I)j_(F,e),        (6)

gamma(l)s_-(a,l,x)-gamma(l+1)s_-(a,l+1,x)
   -gamma(a)[s_0(a,l,x)-s_0(a-1,l,x)]
                     =sum_e (T_e-I)j_(E,e).        (7)

The boundary head changes do not shift x: the latter are the literal heads of the fixed internal non-tree edges. All tree heads do change by their exact affine formulas in (3). Equations (6),(7) are meromorphic identities in independent complex a,l,x and generic zeta on every regular source chart, and hence after clearing their denominators. All genuine quantum derivatives are retained.

Their signs can also be checked directly at the finite matrix boundary. For a diagonal C(E_ll), ad_F has its (a+1,a) entry f_a[J_(a,l)-J_(a+1,l)]; C(ad_F E_ll) has the other two terms in (6). For ad_E the (a-1,a) entry is gamma_a[J_(a,l)-J_(a-1,l)], and C(ad_E E_ll) has the other two terms in (7). The interior construction proves these same equations modulo the displayed actual internal currents BEFORE finite contraction.

4. An explicit two-crossing interior current

Here is a source-level calculation with an internal cycle. Let r(a,b,k) be the literal positive crossing coefficient, sending (a,b) to (b+k,a-k), and write K(y)=x Q^-y, f(y)=1-Q^(y+1). Retain its common full Cartan scalar. For two consecutive crossings take external target c+d=a+b+1 and internal transfer k. Put

T1=f(a) r(a+1,b,k) r(b+k,a+1-k,c-a-1+k),
T2=K(a)^-1 f(b) r(a,b+1,k) r(b+1+k,a-k,c-a+k),
T3=f(c-1) r(a,b,k) r(b+k,a-k,c-a-1+k),
T4=K(c)^-1 f(d-1) r(a,b,k) r(b+k,a-k,c-a+k).

Then for S_k:k->k+1 the exact independent-head equation is

T1+T2-T3-T4=(S_k-I)J_F,
J_F=r(b+k,a-k+1,c-a+k-1)
      *[K(a)^-1 f(b)r(a,b+1,k-1)
                        -f(b+k-1)r(a,b,k-1)].       (8)

Apply the first crossing’s literal F identity at k and the second one at k, keeping the middle insertion. Their shared middle terms cancel except that the first-head middle insertion is evaluated at k and k+1. This gives (8) with its displayed sign. It is not an identity obtained by summing a finite row. The source’s proper ratios verify it rationally in independent Q,x,Qa,Qb,Qc,Qk. For the E calculation use c+d=a+b-1 and the actual analytic e(y)=q-1[x-1-Q x Q^-y]. The four boundary terms are

U1=K(b)e(a)r(a-1,b,k)r(b+k,a-1-k,c-a+k+1),
U2=e(b)r(a,b-1,k)r(b-1+k,a-k,c-a+k),
U3=K(d)e(c+1)r(a,b,k)r(b+k,a-k,c-a+k+1),
U4=e(d+1)r(a,b,k)r(b+k,a-k,c-a+k).

Its exact current is

U1+U2-U3-U4=(S_k-I)J_E,
J_E=r(b+k-1,a-k,c-a+k)
      *[K(a-k)e(b+k)r(a,b,k)-e(b)r(a,b-1,k)].       (8a)

The middle first-input insertion now appears at k+1 in the first local equation and at k in the second. This proves (8a), including the displayed K prefix and its sign. Both identities keep the same two original crossing scalars on their two sides.

The coefficients in (8) are interior functions. Its physical finite support and truncated endpoint arrows must still be retained when it is summed. In particular a generic f(d) is not set to zero as a meromorphic function merely because the physical matrix has no arrow there.

5. Original period chains, endpoints and the remaining global step

For every charged band keep its original support polytope in (3) and its ordered product-cotangent tori Gamma_delta. These supports are different at neighboring bands and at adjacent a,l. A constant forbidden polar stratum remains its original zero state. When a separately proved source-null torus is added, its proof and ordered residue convention are retained.

Equations (6),(7) may be integrated against any common regular test cycle. To replace the resulting band periods by the original ones, keep the differences between each Gamma_delta and that test cycle. For every internal current its translated-cycle correction is exactly

integral_Gamma c w(T_e-I)j
  =integral_Gamma c(T_e^-1 w-w)j
      +integral_(Gamma+e-Gamma)c(T_e^-1 w)j,
c=product_i pi cot(pi x_i).                         (9)

The source normalization is (2pi i)^(-dim x). A chain filling the last cycle difference contributes ALL its crossed source residues and its remaining ends. Coincident cotangent/source poles require their actual finite parts and complement derivatives. The specified moving-P factorial and fixed highest weights remain in this process. No periodicity of the summand identifies the support cycles.

On the original finite arrays the actual endpoint arrows and support rules recover the already proved braided coherent flux. Pairing the first head with beta_a requires only its exact finite sum; no new meromorphic interpolation of beta is used. The first-head and second-head ends of a selected finite window remain the interval terms in that theorem. The individual meromorphic continuations of an outside-head term are not set to zero; its original source cycle is empty, and transport out of that source domain is accounted for by these chain differences.

Thus the local meromorphic Ward equation and its actual charged source lattice are now specified together. This still does not identify the original cycle with a geometric descending cycle or compute the coefficient of the framing-diagonal residue. The stationary pushforward must carry the currents (6),(7) and the explicit chain differences (9), including all resonant characters and generic normals. A zero closed finite flux alone cannot make that relative coefficient nonzero.

The focused audit and results check the actual expanded two- through five-bridge plat graphs, including routed inverse pairs and all fixed channel weight types: 92 primitive cycles, 196 integral charge paths, and the disconnected component test. The two composed currents (8),(8a) are verified rationally in independent head exponentials. A separate comparison with the original generic crossing matrices checks 539 value/first-jet entries, including their full Cartan scalar, and seven nonzero untruncated endpoint-normal defects. These checks do not establish equality of the different charged support cycles or a geometric-cycle coefficient.

The reduction requires the routed graph to be connected, the primitive chart to be fixed, and the endpoint divisors to be retained. It does not identify the charged cycles or a geometric period.
