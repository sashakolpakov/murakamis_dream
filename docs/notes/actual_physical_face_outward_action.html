A nonzero outward action gradient at every physical state face

28 October 2026. In the original one-component scalar source, adjacent head factorials can be combined before estimating a boundary. Their exponents are even, whereas every independent transfer has exponent minus one. This elementary parity forces a nonzero radial logarithmic coefficient at EVERY proper physical face. Each face admits an outward direction lowering the complete marked action. Individual factors need not go to their separate preferred sides.

The statement is uniform over the finitely many faces of any fixed NONEMPTY braid word with one-component closure, at arbitrary width. The empty one-strand unknot has constant density and is excluded. It is not yet a deformation of the whole original cycle or a sharp bound for its scalar. In particular, local changes of integer cotangent charts have their own side currents.

1. The exact gathered factorial source

Use the original averaged cut before splitting the state, as justified in the scalar completion. Follow the one physical component once, listing its 2m crossing visits. Visit i has transfer k_(j(i)) and increment eta_i k_(j(i)), eta_i=+/-1. Each crossing appears twice, with opposite increments. Thus

h_0=t, h_(i+1)=h_i+eta_i k_(j(i)), h_(2m)=t.       (1)

The variables (t,k_1,…,k_m) are independent integer coordinates. Every arc form h_i has coefficient one on t. Its other coefficients are 0,+1,-1. The physical source is exactly

0<=t,k_j<=d, 0<=h_i(t,k)<=d, d=N-1.               (2)

The original signed coefficients at one visit contribute a factorial exponent -eta_i at its input and +eta_i at its output. This is true for both crossing signs after the EXACT conversion

Fhat_-(s)=exp[i*pi*s-i*pi*s*(s+1)/N] Fhat_+(s).   (3)

Equation (3) follows from the common real Gamma product, not just from equality at integer s. Its quadratic factor is retained. Therefore the full scalar has gathered factorial part

product_i Fhat_+(h_i)^(c_i) / product_j Fhat_+(k_j),
c_i=eta_(i-1)-eta_i in {-2,0,2}.                 (4)

All cyclic indices in c_i refer to the physical component. Coincident arc forms may be kept as separate occurrences; their exponents add. Every original Cartan, pivotal and framing scalar and the chirps (3) remain outside (4). The combined quadratic factor has unit modulus on real indices, but is not discarded on complex contours.

2. Integral face coordinates and vertices

Scale (2) by d and call the resulting polytope P in R^(m+1). It has strict interior: t=1/2 and all k_j sufficiently small and positive put every h_i strictly between zero and one. Its defining integer matrix is totally unimodular. Here is a direct proof.

In traversal order, each transfer column of the arc matrix is +1 or -1 on ONE contiguous interval between its two visits and zero elsewhere. The t column is all ones. Negate negative columns. For any square minor, selected arc rows retain this interval property. Successive row differences turn each column into at most one +1 and one -1. This row operation has determinant one. Such a square matrix has determinant 0,+1,-1: expand a column with at most one nonzero; if none exists, all column sums vanish and the determinant is zero. Adjoining unit transfer rows preserves the assertion by expansion along those rows. Upper inequalities only negate rows; duplicate opposite rows give zero minors. This proves the claim for (2).

At a vertex, m+1 independent active rows consequently have determinant +/-1. Their right sides are integers, so the vertex is integral. Since t,k_j are all in [0,1], EVERY vertex lies in {0,1}^(m+1). All its physical arc forms are also zero or one.

Any r independent active rows at a face have a nonzero r-by-r minor of determinant +/-1. Adjoin the complementary original coordinate rows to obtain an affine GL_(m+1)(Z) chart. Its r normal coordinates are actual physical indices h, k, d-h or d-k. There is no fractional lattice, root-grid multiplicity or unrecorded counting Jacobian. At a nonsimple face the other active inequalities remain explicit.

A finite unimodular simplex atlas of the physical polytope

Every physical facet has lattice width ONE on P. Its primitive defining form takes only the values zero and one on the vertices, by the preceding binary-vertex result. Every face inherits the same property: a facet of a face is cut out by one of the original physical inequalities, and its defining form takes both values on that face’s vertices. Their integer difference evaluates to one, so the restricted form is primitive on the face lattice as well.

Fix an order on the finitely many vertices and form the pulling triangulation. Inductively triangulate all opposite facets of a chosen vertex, using the same order on every shared face, and cone their simplexes to that vertex. This covers the polytope with disjoint relative interiors and compatible intersections. The assertion is elementary by following each ray from the chosen vertex to its first opposite boundary face.

All resulting simplexes are unimodular. Dimension zero is the base. At the inductive step the chosen vertex has primitive lattice height one over the opposite facet. Appending its edge to a determinant-one facet basis therefore gives determinant +/-1 in the face lattice. Thus P has a finite unimodular triangulation depending only on the fixed word. No imported triangulation theorem or N-dependent refinement is needed for this statement.

For the ACTUAL source polytope dP, each cell is consequently

u=d v_0+B n, n in d*Delta, B in GL_(m+1)(Z),

where Delta is the standard simplex and B is independent of N. The cell d*Delta itself has N-dependent size; it is not asserted to have lattice volume one. Its change of source coordinates has counting Jacobian one. Shared cell faces must be assigned once by a fixed half-open convention if their finite lattice sums are split. This finite real simplex atlas supplies controlled integral charts. It does not identify or glue their COMPLEX cotangent residue cycles.

3. The radial logarithmic coefficient never vanishes

Let F be a nonempty proper face of P. Let H(F) list its active head occurrences h_i=0 or1, and J(F) its active transfers k_j=0 or1. The row vector governing the radial logarithmic term is

q_F=sum_(i in H(F)) c_i grad h_i
                       -sum_(j in J(F)) grad k_j. (5)

The SAME sign applies at the lower and upper endpoint. Indeed, for I(s)=integral_0^s log|2sin(pi u)| du, either real endpoint a=0,1 satisfies

I(a+delta v)-I(a)=delta v log delta+O_v(delta)    (6)

on a continuously marked side. At the upper endpoint this also follows from I(1-s)=-I(s). Full-function branch changes add recorded integer linear terms; along a real normal displacement they do not change the real logarithmic coefficient.

If J(F) is nonempty, choose j in it. Every head contribution to the k_j coefficient of (5) is even, while its own transfer contributes -1 and other transfers contribute zero. That coefficient is ODD. Hence q_F!=0, regardless of simultaneous head collisions.

If J(F) is empty, all transfers are positive and below one on the relative interior of F. A head h_i=0 must be a strict local minimum along the component: its preceding increment is negative and its following increment is positive. Otherwise a neighboring head would be negative. Thus c_i=-2. Similarly a head h_i=1 is a strict local maximum, so c_i=+2. Writing a_f for the active INWARD inequalities h_i>=0 or 1-h_i>=0, we obtain

q_F=-2 sum_f grad a_f.                         (7)

This cannot vanish. At a real relative-interior point x of F, all a_f(x)=0; at a strict interior point x_* all a_f(x_*)>0. If their gradient sum vanished, their sum would be constant and these two values would coincide, a contradiction. This completes the proof for EVERY proper face.

4. An outward direction with strict action decrease

For every F there is a fixed integer vector v_F and an active inward inequality a_g such that

a_g'(v_F)<0, q_F(v_F)>0,
a_f'(v_F)!=0 for every active a_f.              (8)

For a facet the verification is explicit. A transfer k_j=0 facet has q_F=-grad k_j. A k_j=1 constraint cannot be a facet: its crossing forces physical head endpoints as independent equalities. A head endpoint facet has q_F a negative positive-integer multiple of its inward normal, by the local-minimum/maximum argument above. Choose v outward, then perturb within its open halfspace if needed.

For codimension at least two, active inward normals span a space of dimension at least two. Since q_F!=0, at least one normal a_g’ is not a positive multiple of q_F. There is then a real vector v with a_g’(v)<0 and q_F(v)>0: these two strict halfspaces are incompatible only for positive proportional normals. Avoid the finitely many other normal hyperplanes, choose v rational, and clear denominators. This gives (8) with integer v_F. All its constants depend only on the fixed word and face, not on N or the tangential source values.

The direction need not make EVERY active inequality negative. It remains outside the physical polytope because a_g<0. This is why it also handles the all-zero corner, where shifting every transfer outward negatively was uphill. At that corner one can instead increase all transfers and decrease t sufficiently: all heads move outside while q_F(v_F)>0. The COMPLETE product descends.

5. Uniform local phase estimate

Write the original scalar density as exp(N S) times its retained amplitude, with the gathered factorials (4), and include any fixed integer character sector from the actual cardinal/norm packets. Let y_F be a point of the COMPLEXIFIED affine face: its active indices equal 0 or1 exactly. Assume its inactive factors are regular on the chosen marked tube. If G is the nonsingular part, suppose

|grad G|<=C_0+C_1 epsilon log N.                (9)

The original shrinking-strip hypotheses supply (9); it is not a bound on an unknown knot scalar. Choose delta=N^(-alpha), with alpha>C_1 epsilon/q_F(v_F), allowing fixed norm-comparison constants. The finitely many endpoint expansions (6) give

Re S(y_F+delta v_F)-Re S_face(y_F)
   =delta log delta q_F(v_F)
                   +O_word(delta(1+epsilon log N))
   <=-c_F delta log N                           (10)

for all sufficiently large N. Choose alpha<1 and epsilon small. The estimate is uniform on the regular tangential tube. Any fixed integer mark has exactly constant modulus under this real displacement. The active full-function resets change only those marks, so (10) holds for all retained outgoing marked sectors. A pole in a separated cardinal coefficient is not crossed independently of its full packet.

Small complex offsets of size delta’=N^(-beta), beta>alpha, change the active-factor height by O(delta’|log delta’|), and the regular height by O(delta’(1+epsilon log N)). They therefore do not remove the far-edge decrease in (10). The shift of the quantum upper endpoint d/N from1 costs O(log N/N), also smaller than the stated drop. This is a local inner-to-outer normal estimate, not a monotone flow assertion at every intermediate point of every small corner.

There are finitely many physical faces for a fixed word. Thus the vectors, charts and admissible exponents can be chosen uniformly over this entire finite list. At every vertex Re S_face=0: all factorial arguments are 0 or1 and all retained polynomial phases are pure imaginary on the real vertex. This agrees with the independent subexponential all-transfer-endpoint operator bound.

6. Exact source scope and what remains

The cardinal-completed scalar is holomorphic in any prescribed fixed O(N) box and has the exact original values at every valid integer state, zero at every invalid one. Therefore its COMPLETE finite residue sum can be represented in any affine unimodular chart from Section 2. This is equality of residue functionals on holomorphic scalars, with orientation retained. It is not a pointwise identity between the different products of cotangents.

Consequently (8)–(10) identify a descending normal for every physical face without assuming independent output positions or individual-atom zero sides. They do not by themselves glue those charts into one global deformed cycle. Partial chart changes retain diagonal cotangent walls and side currents; a sweep through valid physical integers retains their original residues. Nonphysical integer poles of separated window modes must still cancel in their complete packet.

The remaining global claim must control this entire assembled source, the cumulative transport mass and any escaping tangential charts. Neither geometric survival nor a sharp whole-source volume upper bound follows merely from nonvanishing q_F or from the existence of the local vectors (8). These are constructive boundary action estimates for the proposed boundary-to-interior transport, with the exact source gluing still required.

7. A uniform quantum bound for the complete active cluster

The preceding real radial estimate has an exact finite-N counterpart. It concerns the WHOLE cardinal-completed active factorial packet, not separate polar Gamma or cardinal modes. All constants below may depend on the fixed face, word, chosen integer direction and bounded normal-height set, but not on N or the bulk amplitude.

7.1 A primitive outward direction with a literal source coordinate

One can strengthen (8) to choose

grad a_g(v_F)=-1, q=q_F(v_F)>0,
grad a_f(v_F)!=0 for every active a_f.         (11)

Every physical row a_g is primitive. If q_F is a negative multiple of grad a_g, any integer solution of grad a_g(v)=-1 has positive q_F(v); perturb it within the integer kernel to avoid the finitely many other normal hyperplanes. Otherwise q_F is nonzero on its integer kernel. Start with one integer solution v_0 and add Lw, where w lies in that kernel, q_F(w)>0, and avoids all active normal kernels except those forced by proportionality to a_g. Such w exists by an open rational halfspace argument; choose L sufficiently large and avoid the finitely many exceptional values. This proves (11). The vector v_F is automatically primitive, since its pairing with an integer row is -1.

Use a_g as the FIRST affine integer source coordinate and complete the others by an integer basis dual to v_F and ker a_g. The resulting chart has determinant +/-1. With its tangent coordinates fixed, the outward normal path is

u=u_F+z v_F, a_g(u)=-z EXACTLY.                (12)

This selected zero offset is important. Other active factorial arguments may have bounded complex offsets nu_f in a small face tube, but the selected a_g has zero offset for every value of those tangent coordinates. Every positive integer z in (12) is therefore an INVALID physical state through this selected head or transfer. The complete cardinal factor for that physical form vanishes there, even when the remaining tangent coordinates are complex. This is stronger than an assertion just on integer tangent slices.

Re-present the COMPLETE residue source in this affine integer chart before taking the normal homotopy. Its first factor is picot(pia_g) da_g=picot(piz) dz with induced orientation. Moving the original outward edge from Re z=1/2 to Re z=M+1/2 crosses only these zero-residue nonphysical integers. Both finite-height horizontal connectors remain. This source statement uses the full holomorphic completion, not a pointwise substitution of one cotangent product for another. No valid-state cotangent residue is swept away.

7.2 All upper normalizers and window reflections are exact

Write an active argument at its lower endpoint as s, and one at its upper endpoint as d+s. Its exponent c is one of the actual head exponents -2,0,2 or a transfer exponent -1. The exact identities are

Fhat(d+s)/N=exp[i*pi*s*(s+d)/N]/Fhat(-s),
w_N^+(d+s)=w_N^-(-s),
w_N^-(d+s)=w_N^+(-s).                        (13)

The first identity retains the complete reflected chirp and the factor N=F_d. The other two follow from S_N(d-s)=-S_N(s) in the literal cardinal sum. Thus the complete active packet, including its windows, becomes

N^(C_upper) exp(P_upper(z))
   product_i [Fhat(s_i)^(r_i)
                w_N^+(s_i)^(p_i^+) w_N^-(s_i)^(p_i^-)],
s_i=nu_i+beta_i z, beta_i integer nonzero,
C_upper=sum_(active upper occurrences) c,
sum_i r_i beta_i=q_F(v_F)=q>0.               (14)

Here lower occurrences have r_i=c and their original slope; upper occurrences have r_i=-c and the reflected slope. P_upper is the explicit sum of the chirps in (13). The powers are those of the original scalar cardinal completion, with orientations exchanged under (13). They are never reset to one. Total window powers are strictly larger than every positive Gamma pole order; in particular p_i++p_i->max(r_i,0). An active physical window with r_i=0 is kept as well. It may be precisely the selected window in (12).

The integer power N^(C_upper) is the ACTUAL upper-endpoint factor, not a tame normalization chosen by the proof. It may be negative. It will remain explicit in the bound below. On bounded normal imaginary height the chirp exp(P_upper) has at most exp(O(M+1)) modulus for Re z of size M.

7.3 Uniform regularized Gamma estimate through all collisions

Put h=2pii/N and use the fixed branch Log(-h)=log(2pi/N)-pii/2. The actual Barnes product gives EXACTLY

Fhat(s)=(-h)^s Gamma(s+1) A_N(s),
A_N(s)=exp[i*pi*s*(s+1)/(2N)]
   product_(k>=1) Gamma(Nk+s+1)
                /[Gamma(Nk-s)(Nk)^(2s+1)].    (15)

For |s|<=delta_0*N with any fixed sufficiently small delta_0, A_N is holomorphic and zero-free, including at the negative integers. The common Gamma divisor in (15) has been canceled BEFORE evaluating there. Uniformly in this disk,

|A_N(s)|+|A_N(s)^(-1)|
               <=2 exp[C(|s|+1)^2/N].        (16)

For completeness, apply the elementary uniform Stirling expansion to t+s+1 and t-s, t=Nk. Its 1/t term cancels exactly:

LogGamma(t+s+1)-LogGamma(t-s)-(2s+1)log t
                 =O((|s|+1)^3/t^2).

Both arguments have positive real part, uniformly away from zero. The error sums absolutely over k. Its total is O((|s|+1)3/N2), bounded by C(|s|+1)^2/N on the disk. The explicit quadratic chirp in (15) has the same latter bound. This proves (16) without a periodic ambiguity or a choice of a replacement factorial.

Let Re z=M+theta with theta in a fixed bounded interval, |Im z|<=T, and let all nu_i range over a fixed bounded complex set. For M sufficiently large the sign of Re s_i is the sign of beta_i. For negative Re s_i use the exact reflection

Gamma(s_i+1)=-pi/[sin(pi*s_i) Gamma(-s_i)].

The COMPLETE window is

w_N^+/- (s)=exp(+/-pi*i*s) sin(pi*s) S_N(s)/pi.

On Re s<=-1/2, |S_N(s)|<=C log(N+1). The retained window powers cancel every negative-integer Gamma pole in the same product, and leave only nonnegative powers of sin(pi*s). These are uniformly bounded on the stated fixed-height strip. For positive Re s_i, the original Gamma is regular and the whole-plane cardinal bound is polynomial in N on that strip. Stirling for the remaining Gamma or reciprocal Gamma factors now gives, for the complete product,

log |active packet/N^(C_upper)|
   <=-q M log[N/(M+1)]
                +C M+C M^2/N+C log N.         (17)

Offsets nu_i contribute only O(log N), since they are bounded; all constants beta_i are fixed. For bounded M the same assertion, with enlarged C, follows from (15) after the already-canceled Gamma poles. Thus (17) is uniform through dependent and coincident active collisions. No estimate is made on individual polar modes.

7.4 Coupling to the actual bulk and the quantitative collar

Let G_N(u_F) contain all INACTIVE factorials and the full original Cartan, conversion, pivot, kappa and external chi_N/N factors. Do not divide by a completed face amplitude, which may vanish. The explicitly retained N^(C_upper) stays outside G_N. Assume the inactive marked tube gives the normal allowance

|G_N(u_F+zv_F)|
   <=exp(CM) N^(C_1*epsilon*M+C_2) |G_N(u_F)|. (18)

This is the quantitative form of (9). On a regular marked bulk branch with uniform factorial-symbol control, inactive clocks and their complements bounded above and below by N^(+/-epsilon), and a displacement smaller than their singularity distance give the stated O(1+epsilon log N) leading logarithmic derivative. In a continued chart the EXACT allowance (18) additionally retains and controls its central norm factors and full symbol; it does not follow merely from bounds on B and 1-B if an outside-band integer factorial divisor is approached. Finite bulk-window modes keep their actual integer-marked exponential in G_N; the HEAD/active windows remain whole as in (14). Their bounded-height polynomial factors are already included in (17).

Combining the exact active bound and (18) gives the following bound for each retained bulk-window mode, with the active packet WHOLE:

|Atilde_N(u_F+zv_F)|
   <=|N^(C_upper) G_N(u_F)| N^C
      exp[CM+CM^2/N]
      N^(C_1*epsilon*M) [(M+1)/N]^(qM).      (19)

For a general nonreal tangent chamber the COMPLETE scalar is the fixed finite sum of these modes. Its bound uses the SUM of the absolute reference amplitudes on the right of (19), not the absolute value of their potentially canceling sum. On a fixed-height joint contour the inactive windows themselves cost only a polynomial; there the unwindowed original bulk G_N suffices directly.

Choose M=floor(N^(1-alpha)), with

max(epsilon,C_1*epsilon/q)<alpha<1.

Then the far normal edge has the strict bound

|Atilde_N|<=|N^(C_upper)G_N(u_F)|
   exp[-(q*alpha-C_1*epsilon+o(1))M log N].   (20)

Throughout that collar the additional normal cost is exp(o(N)); finite-height connector lengths and cotangent bounds add only polynomial factors. Estimate (20) compares to the retained bulk source itself, not to an assumed volume bound for it. Arbitrarily fixed bounded normal jets obey the same kind of bound on slightly smaller contours by Cauchy’s formula after pole cancellation.

One may allow offset size L=o(M), at the additional cost exp[O((L+1)log N)]: the same regularized Gamma calculation and bounded-height estimate use |Im s|=O(L+1). In particular the small thickenings in Section 5 with beta>alpha do not remove (20). This extension concerns a single common radial normal and its specified small offsets, not arbitrary nested scales.

The actual conclusion is a uniform active-cluster OUTWARD quantum collar for every physical face, whenever the inactive bulk allowance is available. The original residues and all upper normalizers are retained, and the selected outside cotangent integers have exactly zero residue. It does not glue different face collars, prove a global mass bound, or classify every nested tangential escape.

Section 7 supplies the finite-N whole-packet estimate; no scalar noncancellation or full volume theorem is inferred.

Independent face audit: 790 proper physical faces of seven original signed words, 4877 exact maximal minors, primitive outward vectors and 28 radial phase checks. The all-word conclusions use the proofs above, not this finite census.

The supplementary complex checks in the two-neighbor audit also pass nine normalized Gamma-factor checks, eighteen exact upper window reflections, and three mixed-direction pole cancellations. The latter use a cluster with q=2(-1)+(-1)(-3)=1, so an individual factor goes toward a pole while the COMPLETE product has the stated descending logarithmic coefficient. These are checks of exact local identities, not substitutes for the uniform proof or global source gluing.
