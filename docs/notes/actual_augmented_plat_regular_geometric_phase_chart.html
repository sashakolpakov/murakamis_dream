A regular magnetic chart for the complete geometric plat character

30 October 2026. A complete geometric augmented-plat representation can be put simultaneously into the regular magnetic charts of the actual local critical-character map. This is a finite generic-framing theorem, not an assumption that the geometric flags are already in a convenient chart. At the leading exponentiated level its original Morse degree constraints also have an integral logarithmic lift.

The statement constructs a regular candidate for the UNFOLDED principal equations. It does not prove that its logarithms lie in a nonempty physical support chamber, choose its Poisson sector in the original finite cycle, establish a nondegenerate reduced critical branch, or supply a quantum asymptotic. Those distinctions matter at the folded channel endpoint.

1. The actual prefix factorization

Fix the finite ordered Morse word of the augmented plat and its actual complete geometric representation. Use common trace -2 lifts of the main meridians. At each circle box its two puncture flags are distinct and their ordered product P is noncentral parabolic with trace -2, as in the geometric endpoint theorem. The same statements hold after its retained Hurwitz half twists.

These hypotheses do not require a right-angled cell decomposition. In any actual complete geometric augmented exterior, the disk boundary is a nontrivial peripheral longitude of its auxiliary cusp. If its two main puncture parabolics had the same fixed point, their nontrivial parabolic product would have that point too. This would identify a main cusp lift with an auxiliary cusp lift. Distinct cusp lifts have disjoint sufficiently small horoballs; two horoballs with the same ideal center intersect. This is impossible for different components. The elementary common-trace calculation then forces Tr P=-2.

At any horizontal cut let H_1,…,H_m be its ordered based meridians and Q_i=H_i … H_1, Q_0=I. For a determinant-one matrix Q with Q_11!=0 put

Bminus(Q)=[[1,0],[-Q_21,Q_11]],
Bplus(Q)=[[Q_11,Q_12],[0,1]].

Then Q=Bminus(Q)^-1 Bplus(Q). Consequently the original physical head at slot i is

G_i=Bminus(Q_(i-1)) H_i Bminus(Q_(i-1))^-1,
K_i=(Q_i)_11/(Q_(i-1))_11,
X_i=-1/K_i, S_i=(G_i)_21/(K_i+1).                     (1)

For K_i!=0,-1 these are finite nonzero magnetic coordinates. The determinant-one and trace -2 equations give exactly the original head matrix (8) of the local critical map. Thus (1) does not introduce an independent flag realization.

Change the ONE common frame by g in SL2(C), so every word Q changes to gQg^-1. There are finitely many heads and boxes. The following sections show that each excluded condition is a proper algebraic subset of this same frame group. Their finite union cannot cover it.

2. Every required endpoint chart divisor is avoidable

A nonzero 2-by-2 matrix R has a nonzero (gRg^-1)_11 for some g. Indeed otherwise every vector/covector pair v,l with l(v)=1 would have l(Rv)=0, forcing R=0. Thus a forbidden diagonal of any NONZERO fixed word combination is a proper condition.

This handles all prefix diagonals. It also handles K_i=-1, since

Q_i+Q_(i-1)=(H_i+I)Q_(i-1)

is nonzero for a nontrivial trace -2 parabolic.

At a local input pair put R=Q_(i-1), A=H_i, B=H_(i+1), P=BA and Q_0=R,Q_1=AR,Q_2=PR. Write a_r=(gQ_r g^-1)_11. Its low physical coordinates obey

U=-a_0/a_1, T=a_0/a_2.                               (2)

The exclusions T=+/-1 and U=T are diagonal conditions for (I-/+P)R and (I+B)AR. These matrices are nonzero. The endpoint q-Hahn quadratic leading coefficient is U(T+1)-4T. Its vanishing, away from the nonzero a_r, is exactly

a_0+a_2+4a_1=0.

The corresponding fixed matrix is (I+P+4A)R. It is nonzero because Tr(I+P+4A)=-8.

The discriminant is proportional to T+1-U. Here its vanishing is the quadratic expression

a_0a_1+a_1a_2+a_0a_2=0.                              (3)

To prove this is PROPER for every prefix R, conjugate just for this nonvanishing proof to the canonical pair

A=-[[1,1],[0,1]], B=-[[1,0],[-4,1]],
R=[[a,b],[c,d]], ad-bc=1.

The normalization follows from the two distinct flags and Tr(BA)=-2. For the test frames g_r=[[1,r],[0,1]], direct multiplication gives

a_0a_1+a_1a_2+a_0a_2=-( -a+c r-c )^2.                (4)

This polynomial is not identically zero, since the first column (a,c) of R is not zero. Therefore (3) is avoidable by a generic common frame. A different test frame can prove propriety for each box; the final choice avoids ALL of their finitely many bad loci.

Both total F and total E may also be required nonzero. In the local Borel frame E_tot=P_12/a_0, so its zero is proper because P is noncentral. For F_tot the numerator, up to nonzero diagonal factors, is det(Rv,PRv), where v=g^-1 e_1. This quadratic in v is not identically zero for a noncentral P. Thus this exclusion is proper too.

These conditions exclude every pole in the endpoint local formulas. For example P(U,Z) at J=-1 cannot have Z=0,1,-1,U or TZ+U=0: substitution gives a nonzero product of T,U,T+1,U-1,U-T. Its leading coefficient is nonzero and its discriminant is nonzero, so both roots are finite and simple. The actual magnetic momentum selects its root by the rational inverse (5) of the local map.

For the HIGH chamber use T_low=T_phys^-1 and U_low=U_phys/T_phys. The discriminant divisor transforms by

T_low+1-U_low=(T_phys+1-U_phys)/T_phys,

and the leading coefficient by

U_low(T_low+1)-4T_low
  =[U_phys(T_phys+1)-4T_phys]/T_phys^2.

All other excluded head and total-coefficient divisors are the same ones above, with F and E exchanged. Thus the single generic frame works for either actual degree adapter at every box.

If the marked geometric representation is algebraic, the good frame may be chosen algebraic (in fact rational after fixing its matrix coordinates). To see this use a rational big-cell parametrization of SL2 and clear the finitely many nonzero denominators. A nonzero polynomial over a number field cannot vanish on all rational tuples, by the elementary one-variable root count and induction. Equations (1) and the local inverse then give algebraic magnetic and internal phases. This does not make their logarithms algebraic.

3. The unconstrained integral degree lift

The leading Morse degree equations are sum_out-sum_in=0 at a coupon, sum_out=1 at a cup, and sum_in=1 at a cap. Regard every physical wire as an oriented edge between successive events. Their coefficient matrix is the oriented incidence matrix of this finite graph: each wire occurs once positively and once negatively.

Choose any logarithms of the magnetic X_e. Since the exponentiated degree equations hold, each defect from the desired affine equation is an integer. On each connected component the sum of these defects is zero. The wire terms cancel, and that component has equally many cups and caps, by counting incoming and outgoing half-edges.

Every integral vertex vector of total zero is the incidence image of an integral edge vector: choose a spanning tree, remove its leaves one by one, and put the leaf defect on its unique remaining edge. The last defect is zero by the total-sum condition. Adding these integer corrections to the wire logarithms solves all affine degree equations exactly.

This does NOT enforce 0<=Re u_e<=1, select the finite low/high support chambers, or erase a recorded phase. The original ordinary and marked cap characters differ by an integral Poisson character and must still be kept. The result is an unrestricted logarithmic lift, not a claim about the support of the physical finite sum.

4. Consequence and precise remaining gap

In the good common frame reconstruct the local roots from the actual geometric momenta. Both q-Hahn internal equations hold. The local map preserves the whole ordered product and recovers the actual inverse auxiliary transport; at completeness its channel equation is R_epsilon=1. Shared wires carry the SAME X,S, and original cups/caps give the inverse-meridian relation P=I. Thus the local derivatives match on the tangent lattice of the Morse degree constraints, and their exponentials are one. Section 3 supplies an affine degree lift.

This is a regular geometric solution of the UNFOLDED exponentiated principal equations, with the original marked representation. It is a genuine candidate input to the regulator comparison. The result does not identify a physical support chamber with a nonempty selected cycle, or show that the required logarithmic/Poisson sector contributes. It makes no claim of a reduced nondegenerate Hessian, nearby holomorphic critical family, nonzero source amplitude, or volume asymptotic.

The frame construction is not an argument that one may change the original quantum source by choosing a favorable co-source. It is a coordinate choice on its classical marked character, retaining the original cup/cap equations. The dimension of its framing orbit after the phase-coordinate reductions is not asserted here.

5. The remaining bridge to the source-selected local branch

The actual q-Hahn logarithm theorem now selects the local internal branch for REAL strict physical parameters, including the folded endpoint. At that endpoint, if

1/2<tau<1, 0<u<tau, w=exp[pi i(tau-1/2)],

its selected logarithmic root satisfies

max(0,u-tau+1/2)<Re alpha<min(u,1/2), Im alpha>0.

Consequently Z=exp(2pi i alpha) lies in the upper half of the unit disk. The strict inequality tau>1/2 matters: at w=1 the selected alpha is real instead. The implicit function theorem also gives joint complex parameter germs near these regular physical points. These are source-selected local data, not yet a global continuation to the geometric candidate constructed above.

The exact endpoint quadratic is

[U(T+1)-4T] Z^2+2U(T-1) Z+U(T+1)=0,
discriminant=16TU(T+1-U).

A small loop about U=T+1 at a generic fixed T exchanges its two roots. Thus unspecified algebraic or holomorphic continuation can change the branch. What is still needed is a common admissible continuation domain, or an explicitly controlled path, which carries the ACTUAL selected local kernels and their original logarithmic marks from a nonempty physical support chamber to the geometric point at every coupon simultaneously. Generic avoidance of the discriminant and the integral affine lift in section 3 do not supply that statement. Even after such a branch comparison, the remaining whole-source cycle and noncancellation estimate would be separate work.

Long fillings perturb the auxiliary color parameters chi by O(1/p) near the complete source, with p the filling slope. They do not by themselves resolve this base magnetic branch-selection problem. The source-divisor extension permits a finite-order zero of an already constructed analytic amplitude; it does not construct the missing selected branch or prove that its amplitude germ is nonzero.
