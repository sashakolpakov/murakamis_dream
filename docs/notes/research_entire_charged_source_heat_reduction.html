An exact heat reduction of the entire charged original source

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The canonical real-power crossing and the full-Hermite charge coupon give a scalar heat equation for the original source. Its initial datum is the entire zero-power charged source, with every original kernel, trace and outside polynomial retained. A convergent Gaussian integral implements every fixed signed real power, and its charge contours can be translated through any prescribed finite point without crossing a pole. These are exact finite-source statements. They do not replace the initial datum by one of its asymptotic branches.

The real-power semigroup and the analytic charge coupons were already proved separately. The Gaussian-window Newton frame also already gives exact crossing integrals. The reduction here acts on the prescribed charged scalar itself, uses its faithful finite exponential-polynomial space, and retains the nilpotent normal through the heat operator. It does not use a positive norm in place of the original closing.

2. The charge space is faithful, and the heat equation is exact

Write y=1−χy=1-\chi, and let ℰN\mathcal E_N be the span of cos⁡(Nτay),ysin⁡(Nτay)(0≤a<⌊N/2⌋),1(Nodd).(10) \cos(N\tau_a y),\qquad y\sin(N\tau_a y) \quad(0\le a<\lfloor N/2\rfloor), \qquad 1\quad(N\ {\mathrm{ odd}}). \qquad\text{(10)} These are functions of χ\chi, with the indicated y=1−χy=1-\chi.

Theorem 1. The map 𝒪N:𝒜N→ℰN,f↦ϕN(f𝒞N,χ)(11) \mathcal O_N:\mathcal A_N\longrightarrow\mathcal E_N,\qquad f\longmapsto\phi_N(f\mathcal C_{N,\chi}) \qquad\text{(11)} is an isomorphism. Consequently (9) is entire in all its parameters and satisfies ∂nvZ̃N=−i4πN∂χv2Z̃N(1≤v≤m).(12) \boxed{\qquad \partial_{n_v}\widetilde Z_N =-\frac{i}{4\pi N}\partial_{\chi_v}^2\widetilde Z_N \qquad(1\le v\le m). \qquad} \qquad\text{(12)} Its initial datum is exactly Z̃N(𝟎,𝝌;𝒖)\widetilde Z_N(\mathbf0,\boldsymbol\chi;\mathbf u). The equations uniquely determine it in ℰN⊗m\mathcal E_N^{\otimes m}, coefficientwise in the free variables.

Proof. From (3) and (7), the observation (11) is 𝒪N(f)=∑a{Aa(f)cos⁡(Nτay)+Ba(f)ysin⁡(Nτay)}+𝟏Noddf(−2),(13) \mathcal O_N(f)=\sum_a\{A_a(f)\cos(N\tau_a y) +B_a(f)y\sin(N\tau_a y)\} +\mathbf1_{N\ {\mathrm{ odd}}}f(-2), \qquad\text{(13)} where Aa(f)=η{4sin⁡2τaf′(ca)−caf(ca)},Ba(f)=−2ηNsin⁡τaf(ca).(14) A_a(f)=\eta\{4\sin^2\tau_a\,f'(c_a)-c_af(c_a)\},\qquad B_a(f)=-2\eta N\sin\tau_a\,f(c_a). \qquad\text{(14)} The positive τa\tau_a are distinct, and none has zero sine. Exponential polynomials at distinct frequencies are independent; expanding the sine and cosine in their two frequencies also proves independence of the two functions at each aa. The optional constant has a different frequency. Thus (10) has dimension NN. Equations (14) recover each value first and then each derivative, while the constant recovers the odd simple-node value. This proves the isomorphism, including the nilpotent coordinates.

The equality ∂χ2𝒞N,χ=−N2𝒯N𝒞N,χin 𝒜N(15) \partial_\chi^2\mathcal C_{N,\chi} =-N^2\mathscr T_N\mathcal C_{N,\chi} \quad\text{in }\mathcal A_N \qquad\text{(15)} can be checked on the angular representatives ∂χ2cos⁡(N(1−χ)t)=−N2t2cos⁡(N(1−χ)t)\partial_\chi^2\cos(N(1-\chi)t)=-N^2t^2\cos(N(1-\chi)t). Taking their spectral first derivatives gives exactly the product rule in the double local factors. The odd simple value is zero on both sides. This is a full-Hermite identity, not just a nodal one. It proves the intertwining assertion. Differentiating (5) and using (15) inside (9) proves (12).

The space ℰN\mathcal E_N is invariant under ∂χ2\partial_\chi^2. For example, if λ=Nτa\lambda=N\tau_a, then ∂χ2cos⁡(λy)=−λ2cos⁡(λy),∂χ2(ysin⁡(λy))=−λ2ysin⁡(λy)+2λcos⁡(λy).(16) \partial_\chi^2\cos(\lambda y)=-\lambda^2\cos(\lambda y),\qquad \partial_\chi^2(y\sin(\lambda y)) =-\lambda^2y\sin(\lambda y)+2\lambda\cos(\lambda y). \qquad\text{(16)} These are the actual size-two normal blocks; the odd constant is the remaining simple block. Faithfulness here concerns the whole function of the charges. A single value of that function, in particular its value at 𝝌=𝟏\boldsymbol\chi=\mathbf1, is still one prescribed observation. The NN-dimensional initial datum has not been compressed to a geometric line.

3. Convergent Gaussian contours and arbitrary translations

All attached spectators, mixed normals and ordinary free-boundary derivatives remain in the initial function.

Proof. For f(χ)=eλχf(\chi)=e^{\lambda\chi}, elementary completion of the square in the real Gaussian gives 𝔊n[f](χ)=eλχexp⁡(|n|vn2λ24πN)=eλχ−inλ2/(4πN).(23) \mathfrak G_n[f](\chi) =e^{\lambda\chi} \exp\!\left(\frac{|n|v_n^2\lambda^2}{4\pi N}\right) =e^{\lambda\chi-i n\lambda^2/(4\pi N)}. \qquad\text{(23)} The identity holds for every complex λ\lambda by absolute convergence and the entire Gaussian identity. Differentiating in λ\lambda also proves it for χeλχ\chi e^{\lambda\chi}. Every function in (10) is a linear combination of these functions, so (20) follows, with the normal blocks included. Changing variable y=χ+vnry=\chi+v_n r proves the prefactor and orientation in (21).

For a compact set of free-boundary parameters, every initial function in ℰN⊗m\mathcal E_N^{\otimes m} has a bound of the form |ZN(𝟎,𝒚;𝒖)|≤AN∏v(1+|yv|)exp⁡(πD∑v|ℑyv|).(24) |Z_N(\mathbf0,\mathbf y;\mathbf u)| \le A_N\prod_v(1+|y_v|) \exp\!\left(\pi D\sum_v|\Im y_v|\right). \qquad\text{(24)} The finite number ANA_N retains the actual full source and can grow exponentially with NN; no uniform estimate for it is asserted. The same form of bound holds for fixed derivatives, with their actual constants. Formula (24) follows directly from (10)–(14).

On the two connectors y=χ±vnR+sby=\chi\pm v_n R+s b, 0≤s≤10\le s\le1, the real Gaussian exponent is at most −πN|n|R2+ON(R+1).(25) -\frac{\pi N}{|n|}R^2+O_N(R+1). \qquad\text{(25)} The source in (24) contributes only another exponential linear in RR, times a polynomial. This argument is at each fixed NN; it does not replace (24) by a subexponential estimate.

In several coordinates the Gaussian quadratic dominates the sum of the linear growth bounds. Fubini’s theorem and successive one-variable translations are therefore legitimate, proving (22). Coefficientwise polynomial dependence, or uniformity on a compact set of ordinary boundary variables, proves the derivative statements. ▫\square

In particular the new charge contours have no factorial poles or physical grid poles. Those finite-source contributions have already been combined into the entire initial function. Passing through a target point by translation is an exact contour statement; it is not yet a descent estimate for the full integrand.

5. What the reduction leaves to estimate

The initial datum in (22) is the full function ZN(𝟎,𝝌;𝒖)Z_N(\mathbf0,\boldsymbol\chi;\mathbf u), not its value at 𝝌=𝟏\boldsymbol\chi=\mathbf1, and not a local formal geometric unit. For example, (8) gives the exact identity ZN(𝟎,𝟎;𝒖)=ηmZN(𝟎,𝟏;𝒖).(38) Z_N(\mathbf0,\mathbf0;\mathbf u) =\eta^m Z_N(\mathbf0,\mathbf1;\mathbf u). \qquad\text{(38)} The known small-charge initializer is on a box separated from zero; the original coupon source at zero is not a fresh complete-edge initial value. Its companion phases and full Hermite aliases cannot be replaced by one chosen primitive branch inside the Gaussian integral.

The new entire contours remove pole avoidance as a condition on charge translations. They do not remove the initial source’s exponential growth, its competing actions, or cancellation among its branches. To use (33) for the complete integral still requires a controlled expansion of that actual initial function on the chosen charge contours and a bound on their complement. Formula (34) proves local nondegeneracy, not that the straight translated contour is a descent contour or that its geometric contribution survives the rest.

The earlier Gaussian Newton frame resolves coefficient vectors by a positive family and expresses the crossing of a frame vector by a Gaussian integral of a finite Pochhammer product. It retains separate cup and closing observations. Here the heat operator acts directly on the original entire charged observation, its initial datum is the actual zero-power compiled source, and the size-two charge blocks in (16) preserve the normal data. Neither representation by itself proves a filled-action upper bound or noncancellation for arbitrary short components.
