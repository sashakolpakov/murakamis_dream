The deficient packet retains the diagram regulator and its source character

30 October 2026. The absolute reflected anchor of the deficient packet introduces no new Bloch–Wigner regulator constant. The fully normalized original circle coefficient has an exact degree-reflection identity. Its endpoint Bloch symbol is invariant under the corresponding rational involution, with absolute normalization fixed on the real chart. The physical turn nevertheless contributes an explicit integer-linear character. It is one on the original lattice, and cannot be discarded in a continued stationary action.

Consequently the existing endpoint-volume theorem applies to the source-marked full critical action after this character is retained. This does not assign the hyperbolic volume to a bare polynomial-kernel value, or prove that the original degree cycle contributes there.

1. An exact reflection of the normalized circle coefficient

Put d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), 0<=a<=floor(d/2), beta_a=s_(2a)/s_a^2, and let k_a be the original fold multiplicity. Use the actual monic determinant R_a(z;c) and define

V_a(h)=product_(j=a+1)^(d-a) 2sin(pi(h+j)/N),
c_t=-cos(pi(t+1)/N), h=d-t.

After the removable ordinary-column denominators are canceled, the ORIGINAL circle-channel coefficient has the entire continuation

C_a(t;r,s)=k_a beta_a^2 V_a(h) q^[d(r+s-t)]
             *R_a(q^(t-2r);c_t) R_a(q^(t-2s);c_t).       (1)

Here t,r,s may be complex, while N,a remain integers. To verify the normalization, start with the actual complete-circle coefficient

k_a N P_a(h) x^-t nu_a^-1 fhat_a(t,r) fhat_a(t,s),
x=q^d, nu_a=(-1)^a q^[a(a+1)],
P_a(h)=N^-1 product_(j=1)^(d-a)2sin(pi(h+j)/N)
                *product_(j=1)^a2sin(pi(h+j)/N).

The exact ordinary normalization is

fhat_a(t,r)=beta_a q^[dr+(a^2-da)/2]
              R_a(q^(t-2r);c_t)/S_a(t),
S_a(t)=product_(j=0)^(a-1)2sin(pi(t-j)/N).

Thus N P_a/S_a^2=V_a, and the remaining channel unit is (-1)^a q1=1. This proves (1), first away from the apparent poles and then by its removable continuation. The retained half twist multiplies both sides by the same original kappa^epsilon nu_a^epsilon, and does not change any comparison below.

Set

t_H=N-t-2, r_H=r+N-t-1, s_H=s+N-t-1.

Then, EXACTLY as entire finite functions,

C_a(t;r,s)=C_a(t_H;r_H,s_H).                            (2)

Indeed c_(t_H)=-c_t and q(t_H-2r_H)=-q(t-2r). The monic recurrence gives R_a(-z;-c)=(-1)^a R_a(z;c). Reindexing j by N-j in the residual product gives V_a(d-t_H)=(-1)^(d-2a)V_a(d-t), while the monomial in (1) changes by q(dN)=(-1)d. These signs cancel. This proves (2) with scalar ONE, not an unspecified function of the degree or channel.

The joint deficient packet uses precisely this reflection for its two absolute anchors. At the negative-real determinant anchor the reflected columns lie in the already selected ordinary packet. The offset -2 and all factors above are retained. Formula (2) then continues the same normalized coefficient to the large complex arguments of the geometric frame. No independent normalization is chosen there.

2. The reflected endpoint symbol has exactly the same regulator

Work at the complete channel endpoint xi=1/2. Use the symbol from the endpoint regulator theorem:

B(T,U,Z)=-[U]-[T/U]+2[Z]+2[-1/Z]+[U/Z]+[-TZ/U].          (3)

The rational endpoint-root chart is

U=T(a-1)^2/(Ta^2+1), Z=(a-1)/(a+1).

The degree reflection sends T to T_H=1/T and U to U_H=U/T. Its SAME magnetic multiplier determines

a_H=(2Ta-T+1)/((T-1)a+2),
Z_H=(T+1)(a-1)/(3Ta-T-a+3).                            (4)

These identities follow by substitution in the original inverse momentum formula. Direct substitution also gives U_H=T_H(a_H-1)^2/(T_H a_H^2+1). The map (T,a)->(1/T,a_H) is a rational involution. This is the degree reflection of (2), not a replacement for the separate physical high-degree adapter.

There is an exact rational exterior-square identity

delta B(T_H,U_H,Z_H)=delta B(T,U,Z),
delta[z]=z wedge (1-z).                                  (5)

Here is a short factor certificate. Put

A=2Ta-T+1, B0=(T-1)a+2, F=Ta^2+1.

Expanding (3) gives

delta B = T wedge (T+1)+A wedge T-A wedge F
   +2A wedge (a-1)+B0 wedge F-2B0 wedge (a-1)
   +F wedge a+2a wedge (a-1).

Under (4) the corresponding factors become

T_H=1/T, a_H=A/B0, a_H-1=(T+1)(a-1)/B0,
A_H=(T+1)^2 a/(T B0), B0_H=(T+1)^2/(T B0),
F_H=(T+1)^2 F/(T B0^2).

Bilinearity and antisymmetry now give (5), retaining every positive rational constant; only the torsion sign is removed in the rational exterior square. The focused audit expands the two symbols separately.

The regular complex chart is connected, and contains generic real points. All maps in (3),(4) have real coefficients. Therefore the differential formula for the Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm makes the regulator difference constant, and the generic real point makes it zero:

D B(T_H,U_H,Z_H)=D B(T,U,Z).                              (6)

This is an ABSOLUTE regulator comparison; equality of magnetic momenta alone would not prove it.

The sine normalization has not disappeared from this argument. A single normalized column has symbol

B(T,U,Z)+[T]-[-T].

The exact reflection factor S_a(t_H)/S_a(t), at xi=1/2, contributes

-[T_H]+[-T_H]+[T]-[-T].

It therefore converts the reflected column normalization to the original one. The complete inverse measure has symbol -2[T]+2[-T], canceling the two column additions. Its remaining symbol is exactly the sum of the two terms (3). Thus (6) accounts for both columns AND the original measure, including the residual sine product in (1). There is no uncomputed additive regulator constant from the absolute reflected anchors.

3. The exact physical turn supplies a Poisson character

Let u<=v be the target rows, h=v-u, l=t_target-v, and put

t=d-h, r=t-u=d-v, s=l.

The literal inverse turn and nested physical cups give the original W_a pi_a entry from the polynomial entry with multiplier

beta_a^-1 q^[(d+2)u+d l+2N h].                            (7)

This is the actual cup convention pV(j)=N q(-d2+dj) and turn s(j)=(-1)^d q^(d-2j). Substituting the entire polynomial kernel and h0_a=k_a N beta_a gives the EXACT continued identity

h0_a (W_a pi_a)_continued(t;r,s)
      =exp(-2pi i r) C_a(t;r,s).                         (8)

Equivalently the character is exp(2pi i v), since r=d-v. It is one on every physical integer entry. Off the grid it is a genuine exponential, and adds -2pi i r/N to the action normalized by N (where r itself is the unscaled index). For the other physical degree chamber use the already proved exact high-block index translation. The displayed r is then its normalized low coordinate; its complete integer affine expression in the physical indices must be pulled back along with the character. The high adapter adds no independent coefficient scalar.

The compiler also records the choice of the degree logarithm: x-t=q(-dt), whereas the equal INTEGER expression (-1)^t q^t with the positive product logarithm differs off the grid by exp(-2pi i t). In scaled coordinates tau=t/N, using the marked inverse logarithm of nu_a, the literal coefficient action is Theta_epsilon-2pi i tau; (8) supplies the additional -2pi i r/N. Changing the logarithm of a fixed integer channel unit adds its explicit imaginary constant; the channel is real and held at the endpoint, so such a constant contributes no regulator. Neither degree nor magnetic characters may be silently removed.

These are exact reindexings of EXISTING source sectors. In the shared Fourier compiler, the actual wire has r=epsilon x_c+B_c with B_c an integer affine function of the retained matching labels. Since X_c=Q^x_c, the factor in (8) is X_c^(-N epsilon) exp(-2pi i B_c). At each original matching state the latter factor is one, and

[X_c^b]_N X_c^(-N epsilon)L_c=[X_c^b]_N L_c.

Thus the integer winding is reindexed and the marked charge b=1 is UNCHANGED. The same fact on the original product-cotangent cycle is equality of all lattice residues. On a continued degree cycle the affine B_c character must also remain. This is not a guessed correction fitted to the hyperbolic volume.

The subsequently proved specified generic boundary lift has root value exp(-2pi i v) K_ent. Its own character therefore cancels exp(2pi i v) from the actual physical turn, restoring C_a exactly. Thus (8) describes the bare canceled-root continuation; it is not an additional uncanceled magnetic character of the restored specified generic lift. The two off-grid continuations remain distinct, and the original degree logarithm and global source marks remain retained. This consequence uses the proved generic lift, not variation of q in the canceled root formula.

4. Exactly when the existing volume theorem applies

Equations (2),(6) show that the actual deficient packet carries the same endpoint regulator as the original crossing-circle diagram, for either physical adapter and every retained half twist. The exact typed turn changes only the explicitly recorded monomial characters, not its Bloch symbol. Summing these symbols at the complete geometric representation therefore gives the ACTUAL augmented-link volume by the degree-one diagram theorem.

To identify this regulator with a numerical critical action one must still use the full marked stationary equations. With all the characters above retained, the existing identity is

Im G-sum D(B)=rho dot Im(gradient G), G=2pi i phi.

It applies to the reflected expression too: its normalized dilogarithms, sine primitives, and exact monomials have the same real-quadratic/imaginary-linear form, with real constant in G. At FULL correctly marked stationarity the right side vanishes; the held endpoint channel has zero real logarithm. Hence

2pi Re phi=Vol(actual augmented link).

This is the volume of the source-marked critical candidate, not the rate of the bare p_a(J) entry or of an absolute envelope. The required winding reindexing fixes action bookkeeping; it does not prove a nonzero coefficient of that critical point in the original degree period.
