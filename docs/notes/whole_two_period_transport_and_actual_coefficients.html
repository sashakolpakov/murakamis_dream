A whole two-period transport lattice preserves the actual finite coefficients

Research construction, 27 October 2026. The standard two-nome product already constructed in the repository defines a flat rank-one DIFFERENCE object over four independent coordinates. Its finite first-period transports are exactly the Pochhammer factors in the original crossing matrices. Consequently every actual finite word and its original source have a realization in this whole transport algebra before quantum jets are taken.

This statement concerns transport ratios, not replacement of the selected knot state by a value or integral of the completed function. The singular transport lattice, its central norm and the full peripheral factor are retained. No meromorphic differential connection at H=0 or selected volume asymptotic is asserted.

1. The algebraic difference object precedes any analytic restriction

Work over a characteristic-zero constant field k_0 and put K=k_0(Q,Qtilde,z,w), with FOUR independent coordinates. The commuting base automorphisms are

sigma(z)=Qz, sigma(w)=w,
tau(z)=z, tau(w)=Qtilde^(-1)w,

and both fix the two nomes. On the free line L=K e define

sigma_L(e)=(1-z)e, tau_L(e)=(1-w)e.              (1.1)

They commute: sigma(1-w)=1-w and tau(1-z)=1-z. This is the flat two-period difference presentation. It is a fixed finite-type rational/birational presentation, not an assertion that its period section is an algebraic function of the base.

The integral lattice matters. Over R=k_0[Q+-1,Qtilde+-1,z,w], retain R e. This also retains the z=w=0 source mark and nilpotent jets; its torus localization is available when needed. The positive maps in (1.1) are injective semilinear maps over this domain; their coefficients may vanish on a divisor. Their inverses are rational maps with the specified poles. One may invert all translated factors to obtain a genuine Z^2 difference torsor, but that localization is NOT the original lattice at a zero of one of those factors. At such a point keep the line, its actual vanishing transport maps and their normal/source data.

One analytic marked period, on Re H<0, is the existing product

Q=exp H, Qtilde=exp(4pi^2/H),
z=exp x, w=exp(2pi i x/H),
Psi(H,x)=(Qtilde*w;Qtilde)_infinity/(z;Q)_infinity. (1.2)

Both nomes have modulus less than one. Product cancellation gives

Psi(x+H)=(1-z)Psi(x),
Psi(x+2pi i)=(1-w)Psi(x).                     (1.3)

The extra Qtilde in the numerator is essential for the second equation. In the two-nome atom note our w is its y, and its numerator argument is Qtilde*y. That note supplies and marks (1.2) by exact products. Nothing here derives a unique completion from the first Ward equation.

2. Every finite transport is the original finite product

Define t_n(z) by sigma_L^n(e)=t_n(z)e, first over K. Direct iteration gives for EVERY integer n

t_n(z)=product_(j=0)^(n-1)(1-Q^j z), n>=0,
t_(-n)(z)=1/product_(j=1)^n(1-Q^(-j)z), n>0.    (2.1)

They satisfy the exact cocycle identity

t_(m+n)(z)=t_m(z)t_n(Q^m z).                   (2.2)

For any nonzero marked period of (1.1), in a domain where its values are defined meromorphically,

Psi(H,x+nH)/Psi(H,x)=t_n(exp x).               (2.3)

The right side gives the rational continuation of the RATIO, even when neither individual period has a root value. A sigma- periodic completion factor cancels in (2.3) exactly.

In particular

(z;Q)_n=t_n(z), (Q;Q)_n=t_n(Q),
{a choose k}_Q=t_a(Q)/[t_k(Q)t_(a-k)(Q)].       (2.4)

These are identities of whole finite products before evaluation. The physical q with Q=q^2 and the original half-Cartan root characters are retained on their specified covers when actual coefficients are pulled back; the new notation does not erase them. For instance the actual balanced positive crossing is, with its original unwrapped support and full Cartan line retained,

r_+=(-1)^k x_1^(-b-2k)x_2^(-a+k)
       Q^(ab+(a-b)k-k(k+1)/2)
       {b+k choose k}_Q t_k(Qx_1^2 Q^(-a)),

and the negative one is

r_-=x_1^b x_2^(a-k)Q^(-ab)
       {a+k choose k}_Q t_k(Qx_2^2 Q^(-b)).     (2.5)

Their derivation, original Cartan factors and exact cups/caps are in the actual crossing calculation, Section 2. Here (2.4) realizes every factor in (2.5) by the universal period transport. It does not omit their common scalar.

3. Universal finite-word preservation, with the actual lattice

Take the original finite coefficient array at fixed level, with all actual crossing coefficients, cup/cap factors, pivots, framing and meridian lifts, cut row and full modified dimension. Replace each finite Pochhammer by (2.1), and each Gaussian by (2.4), on its specified support. Keep every monomial, phase and original normalization factor unchanged.

The resulting transport word equals the original coefficient as a whole rational function in its actual q/M variables and clock characters. Its value is independent of the extra two-period completion: each local first-period ratio already descends from the four-coordinate period object to Q,z.

This equality is preserved by finite products, sums, matrix contractions and literal insertion of all actual index moments. It therefore gives EXACTLY the original finite state, including its source and every whole normal derivative. This is a uniform finite-word identity for arbitrary braids/cups/plats; it does not assume a triangulation move or a new state integral identity.

At a singular factor evaluate the CANCELLED WHOLE coefficient in the original lattice. Do not evaluate the numerator and denominator period separately. The ordinary actual root support has factorial denominators of length below N, hence those factors are nonzero there. A vanishing norm or a supplied source ratio may require the original relative/normal lattice instead.

For example the actual finite Ward gauge is

f_N(z)=sum_(j=0)^(N-1) z^j/t_j(Q_N).

Its EXACT affine source is

f_N(Q_N z)-(1-z)f_N(z)=z^N/N.                  (3.1)

Indeed adjacent coefficients cancel and t_(N-1)(Q_N)=N. The nonzero source must not be replaced by a homogeneous cyclic equation. Nor does the transport equality mean that f_N itself is the scalar function Psi_N; the latter has additional central period/source data, explicitly computed in the atom note.

The new conclusion compared with rational-state folding is this actual finite-word preservation and singular-lattice statement. The two-nome definitions and their rational-point factorization are already part of the repository and established literature.

4. Commensurate periods keep the cyclic norm as central transport

At the marked physical H_N=2pi i/N the analytic power restriction is

Q=Q_N, Qtilde=1, w=z^N.                       (4.1)

The two periods obey NH_N=2pi i. On the base sigma^N and tau are both identity after (4.1), but their LINE maps are

sigma_L^N(e)=t_N(z)e=(1-z^N)e=tau_L(e).        (4.2)

The equality t_N=1-z^N uses primitiveness, with no primality or odd-level assumption. It is NOT sigma_L^N=I. The nontrivial central tau transport is exactly the finite cyclic source norm. Off the divisor 1-z^N=0, the commensurability loop sigma_L^N tau_L^(-1) is trivial, as it must be.

If nuN=1-zN with the original mark nu=1 at z=0, the normalized small-step transport (1-z)/nu has cyclic norm one. This is the finite Kummer/Hilbert-90 part. It is obtained only after adjoining nu and inverting its zero divisor. At z^N=1, the original transport in (4.2) is noninvertible, while the normalized map requires division by zero. The original line lattice and its vanishing/normal source must remain; the norm-one gauge is not a regular replacement there.

5. The original peripheral norm and its whole quantum adapter

The full original peripheral factor is

d_N(M)=epsilon_N (M-M^(-1))/(M^N-M^(-N))
      =epsilon_N M^(N-1)(1-M^2)/(1-M^(2N)).     (5.1)

Keep epsilon_N and any specified whole phase lift exactly. At the root, (4.2) identifies its denominator with t_N(M^2). Thus its central norm is a transport factor of the same object, including all factors such as 1+M^n on their actual marked substitutions. No such factor is replaced by its numerical complete-point value.

There is a necessary WHOLE qualification. The actual fixed-N power coordinate W=M^(2N) is independent before restriction. Under the actual half-meridian step M->qM it obeys

W -> Q^N W,

whereas the independent dual-nome coordinate w in (1.1) is fixed by sigma. The two markings agree at (4.1); they are not an off-root equivariant identification. The actual source and its q/M power-graph derivatives must therefore be retained.

In particular the alternative first-period norm lift

d_N^tr(Q,M)=epsilon_N M^(N-1)t_1(M^2)/t_N(M^2)

agrees with (5.1) at Q_N but is generally DIFFERENT as a whole q-germ. Their exact ratio is

C_N(Q,M)=d_N^tr/d_N=(1-M^(2N))/(M^2;Q)_N.      (5.2)

The whole regular continuation near M=1 is obtained by canceling their common 1-M^2 factor before evaluation. At a generic regular M and Q=Q_N,

D_Q log C_N=sum_(j=0)^(N-1)
                   j M^2 Q_N^j/(1-M^2 Q_N^j).  (5.3)

For N=2 this is already visible:

d_2=epsilon_2 M/(1+M^2),
d_2^tr=epsilon_2 M/(1-QM^2).

Their values agree at Q=-1, but (5.3) at M=1 is -1/2. The phase lift cancels from this comparison if it is the SAME actual lift on both sides. Thus replacing the original peripheral denominator by a cyclic Pochhammer before differentiating loses an actual normal source term.

More generally the cancelled whole function at M=1 is C_N=N/product_(j=1)(N-1)(1-Qj). Since Re[Q_Nj/(1-Q_Nj)]=-1/2 for 1<=j<N, its actual physical-root normal satisfies

Re[D_Q log C_N]_(Q_N,M=1)=-N(N-1)/4.           (5.4)

Thus an adapter whose root value is exactly one can still have quadratically growing quantum-normal data. This does not change its root value; it explains why that value cannot replace its whole marked source in a quantum-jet comparison.

One may record (5.1) in the universal TWO-period base as the formal transport ratio epsilon_N M^(N-1)(1-z)/(1-w), with z=M^2 and w=W. This records its whole rational source factor; it does not identify its fixed-N power-graph dynamics with the analytic modular restriction away from the root. Alternatively keep (5.1) separately, which is what finite-word preservation in Section 3 does. Either way, no original factor has been changed.

6. Fixed-n exceptional slopes are compatible, but not a global blowup

The atom note proves independently, for n FIXED, Q=zeta_n exp(eps) in a closed negative-real eps sector and U=exp(t n eps),

F(Q,zU)/F(Q,z) -> (1-z^n)^t,                  (6.1)

with its logarithm marked from z=0, uniformly on |z|<=r<1 and compact t sets. The resonant terms m=kn in the exact logarithm give -t z^(kn)/k; nonresonant terms vanish. Exponential bounds and a slightly larger r<1 give the stated uniform remainder.

For t=a/b the EXACT telescope uses shifted arguments,

product_(j=0)^(b-1) R(Q,zU^j,U)=F(Q,zU^b)/F(Q,z).

On Ub=Q(an) its root value is (1-zn)a, and the marked individual exceptional period has nub=(1-zn)^a. It is NOT the assertion R^b equals that finite product away from the exceptional restriction. Nor is (6.1) a regular holomorphic extension across an entire complex blowup neighborhood, where other cyclotomic divisors still accumulate. No n-growing limit is interchanged with this fixed-n statement.

This local exceptional/source result, the whole difference presentation and the actual finite transport identities fit one period object. They do not prove that its analytic completion preserves the selected global knot contraction. The latter still requires its original period/cycle, all internal projectors and the source/co-source comparison, rather than only equal nilpotent jets or equal cyclic norms.
