The actual pointed commutant is scalar on its selected Mellin state

Research obstruction and constructive formal adapter, 27 October 2026. The canonical actual Mellin companion cannot directly provide a nontrivial irreducible critical-field observable through an endomorphism which commutes with whole quantum transport and preserves its source lattice. Such endomorphisms have a scalar character on the selected state. A concrete critical observable instead exists on the actual partial Gaussian insertion module; it has a covariant, rather than horizontal, transport rule.

No geometric candidate for the final selected cyclotomic residue is constructed here. The distinction identifies a necessary change of object before applying the critical trace nonvanishing theorem.

1. The entire admissible horizontal lattice is one-dimensional

Use the original actual Mellin source, with F=k(Q,X), R=k(Q)[[X]], sigma(X)=QX, and Q an indeterminate:

AZ=B≠0,Z∈Xn0R,Â=(S−σ(B)/B)A. AZ=B\ne0,\qquad Z\in X^{n_0}R,\qquad \widehat A=(S-\sigma(B)/B)A.

All fixed left-shift conventions, source coefficients and finite color pointing are those of that construction. Its exact affine equation has exactly one solution in this lattice. Let d be the order of Â\widehat A, C its forward companion, T=C−1σT=C^{-1}\sigma, v=JdZv=J_dZ, and ℓJdf=Af/B\ell J_df=Af/B.

If w∈k(Q)((X))dw\in k(Q)((X))^d satisfies Tw=wTw=w, the companion’s first rows force w=JdWw=J_dW for its first component W. If also W∈Xn0RW\in X^{n_0}R, then

h=ℓw=AW/B,σh=h. h=\ell w=AW/B,\qquad \sigma h=h.

The sigma invariants of k(Q)((X)) are exactly k(Q): its coefficient of X^j satisfies (Qj−1)hj=0(Q^j-1)h_j=0, which forces h_j=0 for j!=0. Thus h=c(Q). The source-lattice uniqueness gives

W=c(Q)Z,w=c(Q)v. W=c(Q)Z,\qquad w=c(Q)v. \tag{1.1}

No minimal-annihilator, semisimplicity or rationality of Z is assumed. This statement concerns the supplied homogeneous SOURCE object and its lattice, not arbitrary solutions in a larger formal lattice.

2. A whole admissible endomorphism has a scalar character

Let Theta be an F-linear companion endomorphism with

ΘT=TΘ. \Theta T=T\Theta. \tag{2.1}

Require that Theta preserves the specified formal source lattice, or just that the first component of Theta v lies in X^(n_0)R. For instance a matrix regular at X=0 preserves X(n_0)Rd and has this property. Rational poles at X=0 do NOT automatically preserve it; the actual lattice condition must be checked.

The vector Theta v is horizontal. Equation (1.1) therefore gives

Θv=χ(Θ)v,χ(Θ)∈k(Q). \Theta v=\chi(\Theta)v, \qquad \chi(\Theta)\in k(Q). \tag{2.2}

On the algebra of lattice-preserving endomorphisms, chi is a unital algebra character. It is exactly computable by

χ(Θ)=[X0]ℓΘJdZ. \chi(\Theta)=[X^0]\,\ell\Theta J_dZ. \tag{2.3}

Only finitely many fixed color coefficients enter this constant coefficient for a fixed rational Theta. Thus this is a consequence of the ACTUAL finite pointing, not an unmarked eigenvalue choice.

In particular Theta cannot satisfy an irreducible polynomial p(z)∈F[z]p(z)\in F[z] of degree greater than one: applying p(Theta) to v gives p(chi(Theta))=0, a rational root of that polynomial. Hence the full actual pointed companion does not carry the irreducible critical observable proposed in the critical trace theorem through such an admissible horizontal endomorphism.

The same obstruction is visible in exact moments:

ℓΘjv=χ(Θ)j,rank⁡(mi+j)i,j=1. \ell\Theta^jv=\chi(\Theta)^j,\qquad \operatorname{rank}(m_{i+j})_{i,j}=1. \tag{2.4}

At any admissible root, monodromy H_0 itself satisfies H_0v_0=v_0, so ℓ0H0jv0=1\ell_0H_0^jv_0=1. Functional calculus of that monodromy sees only this source-one direction, regardless of the other spectrum. It cannot manufacture higher critical moments or identify its characteristic polynomial with the classical A-polynomial.

Neither (2.2) nor (2.4) proves that the knot has no geometric period quotient. They exclude this particular construction on the raw pointed object. Endomorphisms which do not preserve the lattice, root-only operators without a whole lift, relative correspondences, and observables with a nonzero covariant derivative are different objects.

3. Actual inserted partial moments provide a different observable

There is a concrete associated-graded adapter before final framing contraction. Use the ACTUAL two-index partial family and nondegenerate transverse formal contraction of the partial support theorem. Keep both framing indices external and the full base, cut, pivot, half-density and peripheral normalization lines. Put H=Log Q.

Let F_0 be the rational function field of the external meridian and framing variables on the marked chart, and K/F_0 its finite separable geometric critical field, of degree d. Keep any Gaussian line separate from K; a square-root sign is not an extra critical point. The field K is generated by the critical values of the actual internal flow monomials z_i=Q^(j_i). A fixed rational linear combination θ=∑cizi\theta=\sum c_i z_i can be chosen primitive: each pair of distinct embeddings excludes one proper linear hyperplane of choices c, and there are finitely many pairs.

The insertions theta^j, 0<=j<d, are literal finite sums of monomial weights on the SAME finite array. They change no underlying invariant or selected source. Their unweighted member j=0 is the original partial array. Let G_sigma be its transverse formal contraction at the conjugate critical place sigma and form

Mσ,j(H)=Gσ(θjI)Gσ(I),0≤j<d. M_{\sigma,j}(H)= \frac{G_\sigma(\theta^j I)}{G_\sigma(I)}, \quad 0\le j<d. \tag{3.1}

The denominator is nonzero by the actual transverse support theorem. The common exponential and base line cancel in this ratio. Termwise formal Gaussian contraction gives algebraic coefficients and

Mσ,j=σ(θ)j+O(H). M_{\sigma,j}=\sigma(\theta)^j+O(H). \tag{3.2}

Thus M is a unit matrix over the normal closure’s formal H-ring: its leading determinant is the nonzero Vandermonde. Define

Dθ=diag⁡(σθ),ΘH=M−1DθM. D_\theta=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma\theta),\qquad \Theta_H=M^{-1}D_\theta M. \tag{3.3}

This is an explicit formula from d actual inserted partial moments, at every formal order. Galois conjugation permutes both the rows of M and the entries of D_theta, so Theta_H descends to F_0[[H]]. It satisfies the exact irreducible critical relation

p(ΘH)=0, p(\Theta_H)=0, \tag{3.4}

where p is the minimal polynomial of theta. Its leading operator is multiplication by theta in the power basis. The distinguished source e_0 has M e_0=(1,…,1)^t; it has not been replaced by a favored critical point. The matrix in (3.3) is a completed formal insertion operator, not an actual finite-N companion endomorphism or a claim that the raw multiplication theta commutes with quantum integration. Generally Theta_H e_0 is NOT e_1: the raw first insertion column is sigma(theta)+O(H), while M Theta_H e_0 is exactly sigma(theta). Powers of Theta_H therefore correspond to explicitly renormalized combinations of insertion columns, including their formal counterterms, rather than to the uncorrected higher raw moments.

4. Its covariant rule explains the escape from the obstruction

Let delta be the external meridian derivation, extended uniquely to the separable critical field. In the normalized branch frame the insertion connection is ordinary differentiation. In the actual moment frame it is

∇=M−1δM=δ+M−1(δM). \nabla=M^{-1}\delta M =\delta+M^{-1}(\delta M).

Its commutator with the critical observable is exactly

[∇,ΘH]=M−1diag⁡(δσθ)M. [\nabla,\Theta_H] =M^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(\delta\sigma\theta)M. \tag{4.1}

The analogous formal meridian difference rule uses the actual transverse block ratios b_sigma=T G_sigma(I)/G_sigma(I), already constructed in the partial support theorem. In branch coordinates transport is diag(b_sigma) sigma_H. Pulling this back by M gives the exact conjugation rule

𝒯HΘH𝒯H−1=M−1diag⁡(σH(σθ))M. \mathcal T_H\Theta_H\mathcal T_H^{-1} =M^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_H(\sigma\theta))M. \tag{4.2}

Equations (4.1)–(4.2) are Leibniz/covariant laws, not the zero commutator (2.1). If theta varies on the geometric branch, the commutator (4.1) and the conjugation DIFFERENCE T_H Theta_H T_H^(-1)-Theta_H in (4.2) are nonzero. In addition the coefficient field and lattice are those of the completed critical insertion module, not the original formal X-lattice. Both changes are essential.

This constructs the correct formal shape observable from actual insertions rather than guessing an extra monodromy eigenvalue. It still leaves an explicit gap: the final finite two-index co-source, its endpoint carries and logarithmic moments have not been mapped to the trace pairing on this insertion module. Hence (3.3) gives no selected critical trace weight or global volume norm. The exact boundary currents in the full certificate must still be transported through that map. No q^N, M^N, parity, or peripheral factor has been discarded in making the partial formal adapter.

5. The raw actual compact co-source has infinite moment rank

There is a second concrete obstruction to extracting a finite critical field directly from the unmodified cut. The exact actual centralizer Toeplitz row has on the identity insertion the moments

μ(bh)=1N∑j=0N−1(−1)h(j+1)h¯(N+1)h¯=(−1)hh+1. \mu(b^h)=\frac1N\sum_{j=0}^{N-1} (-1)^h\frac{(j+1)^{\overline h}}{(N+1)^{\overline h}} =\frac{(-1)^h}{h+1}. \tag{5.1}

The equality follows by summing (j+1)^(rising h)=h! binom(j+h,h). These moments are the same at EVERY N and equal the integral of (-T)^h on 0<=T<=1. Every finite Hankel matrix (mu(b^(i+j))) is nonsingular: after removing the alternating signs its quadratic form is

∫01|∑iciTi|2dT>0 \int_0^1|\sum_i c_iT^i|^2dT>0

for a nonzero coefficient vector. Thus this raw co-source cannot factor through any finite algebra k[b]/p with nonzero p. Such a factorization would force mu(p q)=0 for every polynomial q; taking q to have the conjugate coefficients gives a strictly positive integral. The same argument can be made after any generic complex specialization of the remaining rational parameters.

This is compatible with the finite matrix size: the actual Toeplitz maps T_N(b^h) are NOT powers of T_N(b). Their multiplication is a star product. Consequently a finite initial trace-Gram calculation does not certify a polynomial relation for the raw b insertion. The full relation moments must be proved after the proposed action-normalized or relative reduction. The renormalized observable in Section 3 and the raw compact coordinate are different objects.
