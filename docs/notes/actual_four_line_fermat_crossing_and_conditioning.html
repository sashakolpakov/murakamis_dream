The actual four-line Fermat crossing and its unavoidable exponential action

28 October 2026. The compactified cap sources form an explicit full head frame with an exact inverse and the original basis as its normalized cap value. This gives a four-Fermat-line formula for the actual crossing. The full frame and EACH selected corner have a sharp positive exponential norm on fixed-log root charts, although the separate quad-line coefficients are polynomial. That action must be retained in a geometric comparison.

The cyclic conjugation and existence of dilogarithmic gauge action are already established in the actual Hilbert-90 gauge and controlled cyclic source. They are not counted as new here. The new content is the full dual frame, its cap-compatible four-leg actual kernel, the selected sharp norm scale, and an all-N support obstruction for independent frames. No positive geometric crossing identity or volume limit is claimed.

1. The full coherent frame has an explicit dual

Use N>=2, d=N-1, q,Q,Z,S,P,L from the cap-residue theorem. Thus uN+vN=w^N, uvw!=0, and

P_n=v^n/product_(r=1)^n(w-uQ^r),
L_n=v^-n product_(r=0)^(n-1)(w-uQ^r).

The N-column coherent frame is

M_(n,j)=P_n(uQ^j,v,w), 0<=n,j<N.                     (1)

It has the EXACT inverse

(M^-1)_(j,n)
  =v^N Q^n L_n(uQ^j,v,w)
              /[N(uQ^j)^(N-1)(w-uQ^j)].             (2)

Indeed the columns are eigenvectors of A=Z^-1(wI-vS), with distinct eigenvalues uQ^j. The corresponding left eigenvectors are L(uQ^j,v,w)Z. Different eigenvalues are orthogonal, while their diagonal pairing is

L(uQ^j,v,w)Z P(uQ^j,v,w)
   =N(uQ^j)^(N-1)(w-uQ^j)/v^N.                     (3)

To compute it, sum (w-uQj)Qn/(w-uQ^(j+n)) and use the logarithmic derivative of wN-uN. Every factor in (3) is a unit on the chart. This proves (2), including its output clock.

2. A cap-normalized circulant, with exact inverse

Set w=1 and take u=(1-vN)(1/N), u(0)=1. Order the columns by k=-j modulo N. Multiply column k>=1 by v^(N-k)/F_(N-k), and leave column k=0 unchanged. Call the result K(v). The all-cusp residue formulas prove

K(0)=I.                                              (4)

Extend p_n=P_n(u,v,1) periodically. Put

H_(n,k)=p_(n-k), r_0=1,
r_k=(uQ;Q)_(N-k)/F_(N-k), 1<=k<N.

Then exactly

K=H diag(r_k).                                      (5)

For P_n(uQ^-k)=p_(n-k)/p_(-k) and p_(-k)=v^(N-k)/(uQ;Q)_(N-k), proving (5). All r_k tend to one, with r_k-1=O(v^N). Thus the FULL head frame, not only one vector, extends invertibly through the actual cap wall.

The circulant has an especially simple inverse. For general homogeneous u,v,w, define

kappa=v^N/[N u^(N-1)(w-u)],
h_n=kappa (v/Q)^n/product_(r=1)^n(w-uQ^-r).

Then

H^-1_(n,k)=h_(n-k).                                 (6)

For a proof, (2) at j=0 gives the zeroth row of the inverse circulant; finite-product reflection gives exactly h_n. Thus (6) is a finite cyclic beta-convolution identity with its WHOLE rational scalar. At the cap branch kappa→1 and H→I. The apparent 0/0 has this prescribed regular extension.

The associated known cyclic Weyl shear is exactly

H^-1 S H=S,
H^-1 Z H=u^-1(wI-vS)Z.                              (7)

It follows from u H(QS)=(w-vS)H(S) and ZH(S)=H(QS)Z. The new clock’s Nth power is one precisely because wN-vN=u^N. Its compensating root u must not be dropped.

3. The original crossing in four Fermat lines

Let R be either original mixed crossing, with its original Cartan scalar inside R or in the specified external line. Choose Fermat triples f_i on its two incoming and two outgoing heads. Define R_F=(M_3^-1 tensor M_4^-1)R(M_1 tensor M_2). Equations (1)–(3) give its explicit four-line kernel:

(R_F)_(j3,j4;j1,j2)
  =v3^N v4^N /
    [N^2 (u3Q^j3)^(N-1)(w3-u3Q^j3)
         (u4Q^j4)^(N-1)(w4-u4Q^j4)]
   *sum_(n1,n2,n3,n4) Q^(n3+n4)
      L_n3(u3Q^j3,v3,w3)L_n4(u4Q^j4,v4,w4)
      R_(n3,n4;n1,n2)
      P_n1(u1Q^j1,v1,w1)P_n2(u2Q^j2,v2,w2).          (8)

The original crossing support reduces this to three independent finite indices. There is no asymptotic substitution or new cap. One can instead use the cap-normalized K frames, or H with the explicit r factors in (5). The four external kernels are then p and the reflected cyclic sources h in (6). At all four cap walls this identity specializes back to the original crossing MATRIX by (4). In a whole plat every interior inverse frame cancels; actual rows, columns, coupons and scalar lines all move.

There are two distinct applications. For the original PHYSICAL head R in (8), cups and caps remain their original balanced arrays, transported by these frames; they are not replaced by corner vectors. The literal cup is C_x|d,0>, so the physical transformed cup keeps (K_1^-1 tensor K_2^-1)C_x|d,0>. Alternatively first use the proved pair-fused corner presentation, then apply these head frames to its RELATIVE heads. Its within-pair crossing has four such lines, while its between-pair kernel acts on four relative heads and has eight external lines. The formulas apply to both presentations, but they must not be conflated.

The transported Hopf currents are also explicit. On a balanced head of color x, K_head=xZ^-1 and F_head=S(1-QZ); their H images follow by (7). At completeness the original scaled E is the exact cyclic expression S-1(Z-1-1). At other colors its original bottom projector must also move. No bare current is left in the old relative frame.

Equation (8) is an exact four-line ACTUAL identity. Arbitrary independent f_i are not thereby geometric shared-face data.

4. Sharp exponential norms include every selected corner

Take canonical unit roots and compatible fixed logarithms

u=exp(a/N), v=exp(b/N), w=1,
exp(a)+exp(b)=1.

Assume the central values stay away from zero and the logarithms in a fixed compact set. Define

gamma_*=-integral_0^(1/6)log(2sin(pi t))dt >0.

Then

lim (1/N)log||H||_2=lim (1/N)log||H^-1||_2=gamma_*,
lim (1/N)log cond_2(H)=2gamma_*.                     (9)

The same holds for the cap-normalized frame K. For EVERY corner j=j(N), uniformly in that choice,

lim (1/N)log||K e_j||_2=gamma_*,
lim (1/N)log||e_j^t K^-1||_2=gamma_*.                (10)

In the existing figure-eight normalization, gamma_*=Vol(4_1)/(4pi); the integral definition alone suffices. These are the standard Euclidean norms of the explicitly chosen head basis. When used on the actual relative corner presentation, they concern its original corner sources. A further nonunitary physical fusion change needs its own comparison and may not be ignored in a whole physical norm estimate.

Here is a complete proof. The elementary marked root-product comparison gives, uniformly in n,k,

N^-C/|F_n| <= |p_n|, |h_n| <= N^C/|F_n|,
N^-C <= |r_k| <= N^C.                               (11)

For each product the relative logarithmic errors are O(1/min(j,N-j)); their sum is O(log N). The finitely many endpoint ratios have nonzero limits, because the marked central value of u differs from one. For h use (6), the identical inverse-Q factorial magnitude, and the nonzero bounded kappa. The r assertion is the same comparison in (5).

The elementary uniform factorial estimate proved in the full-Euler estimate is

log|F_n|=N J(n/N)+O(log N),
J(s)=integral_0^s log(2sin(pi t))dt.

Its minimum is J(1/6)=-gamma_* and its maximum is J(5/6)=gamma_. Consequently max_n 1/|F_n| has rate gamma_. The norm of an N by N matrix is between its maximum entry magnitude and N times that magnitude. Applied to H and H^-1, this proves (9). Every circulant column is a permutation of p, and every inverse row a permutation of h, so their Euclidean norms have the same rate. The polynomial r factors in (5) prove both claims for K and (10).

In particular e_j^t K^-1 K e_j is EXACTLY one, while the product of its selected row and column norms has rate 2gamma_*. This is selected source cancellation, not merely poor conditioning of an unrelated auxiliary module. The polynomial quad-LINE coefficients do not make the FULL frame subexponential. This dilogarithmic action has to enter the geometric phase calculation. There is no contradiction with the cap theorem’s fixed-log line bounds: a full raw frame includes uQ^j with bulk j, whose marked logarithms need not stay bounded. Even for fixed-log selected lines, a polynomial transition COEFFICIENT compares two vectors which can both have exponential norm.

For comparison, the UNNORMALIZED coherent frame M in (1) is even worse:

lim (1/N)log||M||_2
  =lim (1/N)log||M^-1||_2=2gamma_*,
lim (1/N)log cond_2(M)=4gamma_*.

Its largest entry is exactly max|p_n|/min|p_n|, since M_(n,j)=p_(n+j)/p_j. In (2), the inverse entries equal p_j/p_(n+j) times a uniformly polynomial factor, using L_n P_n=(w-u)/(w-uQ^n). The factorial profile proves the displayed limits. The cap column factors in (5) therefore remove part, but not all, of the exponential condition number. Both normalizations must remain explicit in a four-face comparison.

5. What tetrahedral variable the principal shear needs

For fixed-log roots u,v→1, let the input clock be A, output clock U, and let p be their common wavefunction momentum. S has symbol p^-1. Equation (7) has the principal graph

t=U/A=1-p^-1.                                      (12)

Its generating action is Li_2(t)-Li_2(1), with the branches inherited from the cyclic product. Differentiation gives p_out=p_in=(1-t)^-1. If p is in the upper half-plane, t is its positive quad rotation. The variable needing a geometric mark is the BULK ratio U/A, not the central Fermat value u^N.

The actual momentum has a holonomy meaning. In the existing Gauss coordinates

K=x/U, e=(1-x^2/U)p/x, f=(1-U)/p,
G=[[K,e],[Kf,fe+K^-1]],

direct multiplication gives

G(1,p^-1)^t=x^-1(1,p^-1)^t.                         (13)

Thus p^-1 is the selected meridian eigenflag in that frame. The other three reference flags must still be identified with the actual shared developing flags to make (12) a geometric tetrahedron. A single eigenflag/source ratio does not establish that four-face comparison. The action’s existence is already known; its exact four-leg source and scale (9)–(10) are the new comparison information here.

6. Independent line frames cannot be one basic tetrahedron

There is an all-N support obstruction to a naive reading of (8). Take four INDEPENDENT cap parameters z_i with u_i=(1-z_iN)(1/N), v_i=z_i, w_i=1 and H frames. Every entry of (H_3^-1 tensor H_4^-1)R(H_1 tensor H_2) is a NONZERO algebraic germ.

For input (a,b) and output (c,e), let r=-a mod N and s=-b mod N in the head range. The coefficient of z_1^r z_2^s z_3^c z_4^e is uniquely the original route with both intermediate R indices (0,0). It equals

R_(00,00) (-1)^(c+e)Q^[c(c-1)/2+e(e-1)/2]
                       /[F_r F_s F_c F_e],          (14)

which is nonzero with the original Cartan line retained. Each degree is less than N, so no central correction to u_i enters. The four cyclic shifts fix both intermediate indices uniquely, which proves (14) with no possible cancellation.

The basic matrix tetrahedron kernel instead has strict support delta i+j=l modulo N, hence only N^3 potential nonzero entries. Independent diagonal face gauges and Weyl index permutations preserve that support count. Therefore this generic four-line actual kernel cannot be a SINGLE such tetrahedron under those changes. Coupled shared-face parameters, additional shears, or several tetrahedra are necessary.

This does not exclude a genuine four-face geometric identity. It states precisely what (8) alone lacks. Proving actual shared developing-flag relations, with their full source and phase, remains the next comparison; no broad volume claim follows here.

7. Reproducible checks

The audit and saved output pass 56 full coherent/circulant frames at N=2,…,8, including the dual inverse, exact nonlinear clock transport and all column normalizations. They check 56 mixed positive/negative original crossing entries by an independent sum using the literal E and Cartan coefficients, with the full four-line kernel retained. The 8,771 support witness coefficients are nonzero at every matrix position in these levels. Five norm-scale diagnostics through N=256 approach the limits in (9)–(10); the limits are proved by the uniform product estimates, not those samples.
