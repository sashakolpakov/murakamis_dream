Full crossing spectra distinguish the two actual normal paths

Independent proof and audit, 27 October 2026. The whole Gamma comparison transports actual scalar source factors. It cannot be extended to a scalar-adjusted similarity of the two checked crossing matrices on the same finite tensor fiber. This failure holds for every level, including arbitrary nondiagonal gauges. It does not exclude transport of the base, a relative kernel, or an enlarged normal module.

The local coefficient comparison is the actual crossing calculation. The compatible constructive statement is the whole bulk response.

1. Exact spectra and the general obstruction

Write n=N−1n=N-1. On the highest-weight basis use Kui=qα−2iui,Fui=ui+1,Eui=[i]q[α−i+1]qui−1. K u_i=q^{\alpha-2i}u_i,\qquad Fu_i=u_{i+1},\qquad Eu_i=[i]_q[\alpha-i+1]_q u_{i-1}. The actual full positive checked crossing, including its common Cartan factor, has the following generic eigenvalues: rj(q,α)=(−1)jqα2/2−j(2α−j+1),0≤j<N. r_j(q,\alpha)=(-1)^j q^{\alpha^2/2-j(2\alpha-j+1)},\qquad 0\le j<N. \tag{1} Here generic means away from the explicitly finite eigenvalue collision and highest-weight degeneracy loci.

There are two different multiplicity formulas:

  • For the ordinary integral NN-color, α=n\alpha=n and generic moving qq, the multiplicity of rjr_j is 2(n−j)+12(n-j)+1.
  • At q0=eπi/Nq_0=e^{\pi i/N}, for a generic typical NN-color with α=n+b\alpha=n+b, the multiplicity of every rjr_j is NN.

These multiplicity lists are different for every N≥2N\ge2. Therefore the moving-qq integral crossing and the fixed-root typical crossing are not similar, even after either is multiplied by any nonzero scalar function. The conclusion survives meromorphic or ramified changes of matrix basis: an equality of such matrices would imply equality over their common generic fraction field, where the eigenvalue multiplicities disagree. Retaining an additional common Cartan, zero-framing, or peripheral scalar cannot change this argument.

Here is a direct proof of both formulas. At total index j<Nj<N the vector vj=∑a=0jcaua⊗uj−a,c0=1, v_j=\sum_{a=0}^j c_a u_a\otimes u_{j-a},\qquad c_0=1, in the kernel of ΔE=E⊗K+1⊗E\Delta E=E\otimes K+1\otimes E is unique for generic parameters. Successive coefficient cancellation gives cj=(−1)jq−j(α−j+1). c_j=(-1)^j q^{-j(\alpha-j+1)}. \tag{2} The checked crossing preserves this highest vector line. To compute its eigenvalue, inspect the coefficient of u0⊗uju_0\otimes u_j. In the actual PqH⊗H/2∑k(⋯)Ek⊗FkPq^{H\otimes H/2}\sum_k(\cdots)E^k\otimes F^k, only the input uj⊗u0u_j\otimes u_0 with k=0k=0 can contribute. Its coefficient is cjqα2/2−αjc_jq^{\alpha^2/2-\alpha j}, which is (1).

For generic moving qq and integral α=n\alpha=n, the string generated from vjv_j by ΔF\Delta F has highest weight 2n−2j2n-2j and length 2(n−j)+12(n-j)+1. Indeed EFrvj=[r]q[2n−2j−r+1]qFr−1vj E F^r v_j=[r]_q[2n-2j-r+1]_qF^{r-1}v_j proves nonvanishing through its stated length. The distinct generic Casimir eigenvalues separate these strings. Their dimensions sum to N2N^2.

At q0q_0 and generic α=n+b\alpha=n+b, each string instead has exactly length NN. 3. Exact verification and the constructive scope

The independent script assembles the literal balanced checked crossing in its conserved total-index blocks. It computes all power traces and recovers the characteristic polynomial by Newton identities over finite fields and their dual numbers. It compares generic integral, generic root-meridian, integral first-normal, and root first-normal matrices at N=2,…,8N=2,\ldots,8: 840 exact characteristic coefficients pass.
