Opposite-polarization kernels and the original closing source

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The actual total lowering power, balanced turnover, and prefix quotient give a finite operator between the two polarizations. Its normalization is exactly the inverse-Jacobian coupon of the original pointed source. Two normalized kernels sew to that source, on the original compact coherent cycles. Their root Laurent coefficients recover the first meridian jet and give an explicit contracting homotopy on every nonself reciprocal physical-weight pair.

This is also a relative-source construction: restricting a resolution to a subchain produces a specified operator defect and its boundary transgression. Neither the defect nor the complementary chains are discarded. No geometric contribution is selected by generic invertibility or by the contracting homotopy.

The complete directs R, P, and Z are pinned below. They supply the actual quotient, balanced turnover, and original source normalization. The power product, normalized sewing, Laurent algebra, and relative kernel statements are proved here.

7. The geometric loxodromic argument remains a separate continuation

The factorization (10) explains exactly which polarization change is implemented at the finite source level. The first turnover takes FΣ=0F_\Sigma=0 to EΣ=0E_\Sigma=0 on controlled classical charts, as Z records. The subsequent EΣN−1E_\Sigma^{N-1} and quotient are retained operators, not a presumed classical coordinate change of those cycles. Their full kernels occur in (33). No uniform large-NN symbol for them is asserted here.

For a scalar argument zz on a compact loxodromic branch |z|>1+δ|z|>1+\delta, the normalization has the exact formula DN(z)−1=(z−z−1)z−N(1−z−2N)−1.(39) D_N(z)^{-1}=(z-z^{-1})z^{-N}(1-z^{-2N})^{-1}. \qquad\text{(39)} The last factor is 1+O((1+δ)−2N)1+O((1+\delta)^{-2N}), with fixed derivatives bounded by the corresponding polynomial powers of NN. Thus the character −NLog⁡z-N\operatorname{Log} z and the nonzero factor z−z−1z-z^{-1} remain. The reciprocal branch uses the same DND_N. This is a scalar statement; it neither evaluates JJ’s coherent kernel at a geometric point nor turns it into a unit-amplitude transport.

All physical root weights χ\chi in (20) lie on the unit circle. A loxodromic geometric total-product eigenvalue is not one of those physical eigenvalues at M=1M=1. Continuing a source phase or a relative cycle to that argument still needs its actual analytic comparison. In particular neither (27) nor compact source equality makes the window defect (37) zero.
