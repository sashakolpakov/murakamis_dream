An explicit coideal kernel with the original cup calibration

24 November 2026. Written research proof.

The actual conserved coideal admits polynomial kernel and cokernel maps, including its collision parameters. A trailing continuant removes the apparent inverse in their comparison. The original cup is the image of an explicit endpoint seed; at the source-selected parameter its spectral projection loses only a subexponential factor. The original marked closing remains a specified row on this kernel. No geometric cycle coefficient or scalar lower bound is inferred.

1. Actual matrices and continuants

Use the complete balanced physical basis of C and S. Put N≥2,d=N−1,b=2k≥2,q=eπi/N,q=eπi/(2N),Q=q2,x=−q−1,Fa=(Q;Q)a,F0=1,Fd=N.(1) N\ge2,\quad d=N-1,\quad b=2k\ge2,\quad q=e^{\pi i/N},\quad \sqrt q=e^{\pi i/(2N)},\quad Q=q^2,\quad x=-q^{-1},\quad F_a=(Q;Q)_a,\quad F_0=1,\quad F_d=N . \qquad\text{(1)} The basis is orthonormal. The actual one-head generators have entries Kea=xQ−aeaKe_a=xQ^{-a}e_a, Eea=(Q−a−1)ea−1Ee_a=(Q^{-a}-1)e_{a-1}, and Fea=(1−Qa+1)ea+1Fe_a=(1-Q^{a+1})e_{a+1}, with both missing endpoint arrows zero. For the ordered total generators define, as in C, Bc=𝖥−(𝖤−cI)𝖪−1,T=Bc(b−1),W=(ℂN)⊗(b−1).(2) B_c=\mathsf F-(\mathsf E-cI)\mathsf K^{-1},\qquad T=B_c^{(b-1)},\qquad W=(\mathbb C^N)^{\otimes(b-1)} . \qquad\text{(2)} Here cc is an auxiliary parameter and BcB_c without a superscript acts on V=ℂN⊗WV=\mathbb C^N\otimes W. Its first-head tridiagonal action is (Bcu)a=(1−Qa)ua−1+q(1−Qa+1)ua+1−qQaTua.(3) (B_cu)_a=(1-Q^a)u_{a-1} +q(1-Q^{a+1})u_{a+1}-qQ^aTu_a . \qquad\text{(3)} Only indices 0,…,d0,\ldots,d occur. Write ιa:W→V\iota_a:W\to V and πa:V→W\pi_a:V\to W for coordinate insertion and extraction.

Let Da(λ;T)D_a(\lambda;T) be the leading continuant on indices 0,…,a−10,\ldots,a-1 of λI−Bc\lambda I-B_c, with TT held fixed: D−1=0,D0=I,Da+1=(λ+qQaT)Da−q(1−Qa)2Da−1.(4) D_{-1}=0,\quad D_0=I,\qquad D_{a+1}=(\lambda+qQ^aT)D_a-q(1-Q^a)^2D_{a-1}. \qquad\text{(4)} All its coefficients commute. Let Ed(λ;T)E_d(\lambda;T) be the trailing continuant on indices 1,…,d1,\ldots,d, and DmidD_{\mathrm{ mid}} the one on 1,…,d−11,\ldots,d-1; the empty determinant is II. The monic polynomial and exact identity from C are pN(z)=2qN/2TN(z2q),DN(λ;T)=pN(λ)−γpN(T),γ=(−1)N+1.(5) p_N(z)=2q^{N/2}T_N\!\left(\frac{z}{2\sqrt q}\right),\qquad D_N(\lambda;T)=p_N(\lambda)-\gamma p_N(T),\quad \gamma=(-1)^{N+1}. \qquad\text{(5)} Here TNT_N is Chebyshev of the first kind. Since bb is even, pN(T)=γb−1pN(c)p_N(T)=\gamma^{b-1}p_N(c), so DN(c;T)=0D_N(c;T)=0. Below, an omitted argument means λ=c\lambda=c, T=Bc(b−1)T=B_c^{(b-1)}.

2. A polynomial inverse valid at collisions

The tridiagonal determinant identity gives DdEd−DNDmid=∏a=1dq(1−Qa)2=qdN2I.(6) D_dE_d-D_ND_{\mathrm{ mid}} =\prod_{a=1}^dq(1-Q^a)^2=q^dN^2I . \qquad\text{(6)} For clarity, the two cross-minors in the determinant identity are the products of the upper and lower arrows. Their signs cancel and give the displayed factor, including at N=2N=2. Thus, on this actual tail module, DdEd=qdN2I,Dd−1=EdqdN2,Ed−1=DdqdN2.(7) D_dE_d=q^dN^2I,\qquad D_d^{-1}=\frac{E_d}{q^dN^2},\qquad E_d^{-1}=\frac{D_d}{q^dN^2}. \qquad\text{(7)} These are polynomial matrix identities in cc. No tail spectral inverse or exclusion of collision parameters is needed.

Define the explicit rectangular maps Lc=∑a=0dιaq−aDaFa,Mc=∑a=0dDaFaπa,Qc=EdMc.(8) L_c=\sum_{a=0}^d\iota_a\,\frac{q^{-a}D_a}{F_a}, \qquad M_c=\sum_{a=0}^d\frac{D_a}{F_a}\,\pi_a,\qquad Q_c=E_dM_c . \qquad\text{(8)} The recurrence proves (Bc−cI)Lc=0,Mc(Bc−cI)=0,π0Lc=I,Mcι0=I.(9) (B_c-cI)L_c=0,\quad M_c(B_c-cI)=0,\quad \pi_0L_c=I,\quad M_c\iota_0=I . \qquad\text{(9)} In particular LcL_c is a graph parameterization of the entire cc-eigenkernel, not a selection of particular eigenvectors.

There is an exact sequence at every cc: 0→W→LcV→Bc−cIV→McW→0.(10) 0\longrightarrow W\xrightarrow{L_c}V \xrightarrow{\,B_c-cI\,}V\xrightarrow{M_c}W \longrightarrow0 . \qquad\text{(10)} One can prove exactness without a dimension argument. The equations in rows 0,…,d−10,\ldots,d-1 determine a kernel vector from its first coordinate, using the nonzero upper arrows in (3). For Mcw=0M_cw=0, set z0=0z_0=0 and solve (cI−Bc)z=w(cI-B_c)z=w successively in those rows. The remaining residual is supported at dd and is killed by McM_c. Its last coefficient Dd/FdD_d/F_d is invertible by (7), so that residual is zero. The sign change to Bc−cIB_c-cI does not change its image.

This proof already works over 𝒪N[c]\mathcal O_N[c], where 𝒪N=ℤ[q,1/N]\mathcal O_N=\mathbb Z[\sqrt q,1/N]. Every 1−Qa1-Q^a, 1≤a<N1\le a<N, is a unit there because their product is NN. Equation (7) supplies the remaining unit. Thus the kernel and cokernel are free of tail rank over this ring; no field-rank argument or inversion of pN′(c)p_N'(c) enters (10). Replacing McM_c by QcQ_c gives the same exact sequence.

3. The finite kernel and its natural comparison

Put rc(z)=pN(z)−pN(c)z−c.(11) r_c(z)=\frac{p_N(z)-p_N(c)}{z-c}. \qquad\text{(11)} This is a monic polynomial of degree dd. The cofactor identity for the tridiagonal matrix gives rc(Bc)=LcEdMc=LcQc.(12) \boxed{\qquad r_c(B_c)=L_cE_dM_c=L_cQ_c.\qquad} \qquad\text{(12)} Here is an algebraic verification including collisions. The adjugate of cI−BccI-B_c, computed in the commutative coefficient ring generated by TT, equals rc(Bc)r_c(B_c) by (5) and Cayley–Hamilton. Its columns lie in the kernel in (9), and its first row is a left kernel row. Their first-coordinate normalizations are L0=M0=IL_0=M_0=I. The 0000 cofactor is EdE_d, proving (12).

The exact normalization is McLc=pN′(c)DdqdN2,QcLc=pN′(c)I.(13) M_cL_c=\frac{p_N'(c)D_d}{q^dN^2},\qquad Q_cL_c=p_N'(c)I . \qquad\text{(13)} To check the derivative, first allow λ\lambda to vary with TT fixed. The recurrence gives (λI−Bc)L(λ)=ιdDN(λ;T)qdFd.(14) (\lambda I-B_c)L(\lambda) =\iota_d\,\frac{D_N(\lambda;T)}{q^dF_d}. \qquad\text{(14)} Differentiate in λ\lambda, evaluate at cc, and multiply by McM_c. The term with L′L' vanishes by (9); Mcιd=Dd/FdM_c\iota_d=D_d/F_d and DN′(c;T)=pN′(c)D_N'(c;T)=p_N'(c) give (13), using (7). This derivative is not differentiation of the tail T(c)T(c).

Consequently rc(Bc)2=pN′(c)rc(Bc).(15) r_c(B_c)^2=p_N'(c)r_c(B_c). \qquad\text{(15)} When pN′(c)≠0p_N'(c)\ne0, rc(Bc)/pN′(c)r_c(B_c)/p_N'(c) is the spectral projector onto ℰc=ker⁡(Bc−cI)\mathcal E_c=\ker(B_c-cI). At a collision pN′(c)=0p_N'(c)=0, (12) is instead a nonzero square-zero map with image ℰc\mathcal E_c and kernel im⁡(Bc−cI)\operatorname{im}(B_c-cI). Indeed LcL_c is injective and QcQ_c is surjective by (7)–(10). The natural map from the kernel to the cokernel, using Lc,QcL_c,Q_c, is multiplication by pN′(c)p_N'(c). It vanishes at a collision. The graph parameterization LcL_c itself remains valid there.

4. The actual endpoint seed and the unchanged closing

Retain the literal cup and the full top rows from S and H: p=N∑a=0dxa−d|a,d−a⟩,ℓo|a,b⟩=xaNδa+b,d,ℓm|a,b⟩=xbN2.(16) p=N\sum_{a=0}^d x^{a-d}|a,d-a\rangle,\quad \ell_{\mathrm{ o}}|a,b\rangle=\frac{x^a}{N}\delta_{a+b,d}, \quad \ell_{\mathrm{ m}}|a,b\rangle=\frac{x^b}{N^2}. \qquad\text{(16)} There is no degree delta in the marked row. Set u0=p⊗k,ℓ0=ℓo⊗(k−1)⊗ℓm,y=|0⟩2⊗p⊗(k−1),ξ=ι0y.(17) u_0=p^{\otimes k},\quad \ell_0=\ell_{\mathrm{ o}}^{\otimes(k-1)}\otimes\ell_{\mathrm{ m}},\quad y=|0\rangle_2\otimes p^{\otimes(k-1)},\quad \xi=\iota_0y . \qquad\text{(17)} The actual source is invariant, so Bcu0=cu0B_cu_0=cu_0 for every cc. Its explicit seed calibration is u0=rc(Bc)ξ=LcEdy,Edy=Nx−d|d⟩2⊗p⊗(k−1).(18) \boxed{\quad u_0=r_c(B_c)\xi=L_cE_dy,\qquad E_dy=Nx^{-d}|d\rangle_2\otimes p^{\otimes(k-1)} . \quad} \qquad\text{(18)} To prove the first equality, use C’s monic block Krylov basis. In that basis the kernel of multiplication by z−cz-c, modulo pN(z)−pN(c)p_N(z)-p_N(c), consists exactly of rc(z)⊗y′r_c(z)\otimes y'; this remains true at a multiple root. Its first-head dd component is Fdy′=Ny′F_dy'=Ny'. The same component of the literal cup u0u_0 is NyNy, fixing y′=yy'=y. Equation (12) and Mcι0=IM_c\iota_0=I give the second expression. Extracting first-head coordinate zero from the literal cup proves the last formula, with its phase retained. Also (13) gives Qcu0=pN′(c)EdyQ_cu_0=p_N'(c)E_dy; hence the natural kernel-to-cokernel comparison kills this nonzero cup at a collision.

For any original unmarked signed braid word RR, define Rc=π0RLc,z0=Nx−d|d⟩2⊗p⊗(k−1),ℓc=ℓ0Lc.(19) R_c=\pi_0RL_c,\quad z_0=Nx^{-d}|d\rangle_2 \otimes p^{\otimes(k-1)},\quad \ell_c=\ell_0L_c . \qquad\text{(19)} Since RR commutes with BcB_c, RLc=LcRcRL_c=L_cR_c. Thus the entire original paired-plat scalar, with its prescribed external factor χN\chi_N, is exactly 𝒦N=χNℓcRcz0.(20) \mathcal K_N=\chi_N\ell_cR_cz_0 . \qquad\text{(20)} This graph reduction is source-faithful even at collisions. It does not replace ℓc\ell_c by an adjoint row. The saturated generic source 𝒮N\mathcal S_N lies inside ℰc\mathcal E_c; the latter, of dimension Nb−1N^{b-1}, is not identified with 𝒮N\mathcal S_N or with the smaller complete invariant multiplicity. All original crossing factors and Casimir derivative channels remain. An inserted coupon not commuting with BcB_c needs its actual defect.

5. Uniform costs and the source-selected projection

For fixed bb and |c|≤C*|c|\le C_*, the actual tail satisfies ∥T∥≤4(b−1)+C*\|T\|\le4(b-1)+C_*. Recurrence (4) bounds the leading and trailing continuants by Ca+1C^{a+1}. Moreover |Fa|−1=|Fd−a|N≤2d−aN.(21) |F_a|^{-1}=\frac{|F_{d-a}|}{N}\le\frac{2^{d-a}}N . \qquad\text{(21)} Consequently all maps in (8), Dd,EdD_d,E_d, and their inverses in (7) have operator norm at most eCb,C*Ne^{C_{b,C_*}N}. There is no Nlog⁡NN\log N cost or loss of uniformity at a collision. The forward representation and the original closing in (19)–(20) retain these frame costs.

At c=2c=2, S proves pN′(2)≠0p_N'(2)\ne0 for every N≥2N\ge2. Write Π2=r2(B2)/pN′(2)\Pi_2=r_2(B_2)/p_N'(2). Since ∥p∥=N3/2\|p\|=N^{3/2}, (18) gives the exact source-leg estimate ∥r2(B2)ξ∥∥ξ∥=N3/2,∥Π2ξ∥∥ξ∥=N3/2|pN′(2)|.(22) \frac{\|r_2(B_2)\xi\|}{\|\xi\|}=N^{3/2},\qquad \frac{\|\Pi_2\xi\|}{\|\xi\|} =\frac{N^{3/2}}{|p_N'(2)|}. \qquad\text{(22)} The coefficient has a sharp subexponential asymptotic: N3/2|pN′(2)|=2πe−πN/2(1+O(N−1/2)).(23) \boxed{\quad \frac{N^{3/2}}{|p_N'(2)|} =2\sqrt\pi\,e^{-\sqrt{\pi N/2}}\bigl(1+O(N^{-1/2})\bigr). \quad} \qquad\text{(23)} Indeed, with θ=π/(2N)\theta=\pi/(2N), take w=exp⁡(arsinh⁡sin⁡θ−iarcsin⁡sin⁡θ)w=\exp(\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{\sin\theta} -i\arcsin\sqrt{\sin\theta}). S gives |pN′(2)|=N|wN−w−N|22sin⁡θ.(24) |p_N'(2)|=\frac{N|w^N-w^{-N}|}{2\sqrt{2\sin\theta}} . \qquad\text{(24)} Here Nlog⁡|w|=πN/2+O(N−1/2)N\log|w|=\sqrt{\pi N/2}+O(N^{-1/2}) and |wN−w−N|=|w|N(1+O(e−cN))|w^N-w^{-N}|=|w|^N(1+O(e^{-c\sqrt N})). Substitution proves (23). Thus the specified seed-to-cup leg has no exponential loss at the volume scale, although the global projector and graph frames need only satisfy exponential bounds.

A quantum correction of the auxiliary parameter makes this coefficient polynomial while retaining the same principal parabolic generator. Set cN=1+q,pN(cN)=0,λj=Qj+qQ−j(0≤j<N).(25) c_N=1+q,\qquad p_N(c_N)=0,\qquad \lambda_j=Q^j+qQ^{-j}\quad(0\le j<N). \qquad\text{(25)} The identity pN(s+q/s)=sN−s−Np_N(s+q/s)=s^N-s^{-N} proves these formulas. The roots are distinct: a collision for different s,ts,t with sN=tN=1s^N=t^N=1 would require st=qst=q, which contradicts qN=−1q^N=-1. Differentiating that identity at s=Qjs=Q^j gives pN′(λj)=2NQjQ2j−q,pN′(cN)=2N1−q.(26) p_N'(\lambda_j)=\frac{2NQ^j}{Q^{2j}-q},\qquad p_N'(c_N)=\frac{2N}{1-q}. \qquad\text{(26)} In particular all these derivative moduli are at least NN. The product formula for the spectral projectors, together with ∥BcN∥≤4b+2\|B_{c_N}\|\le4b+2 and |λj|≤2|\lambda_j|\le2, bounds them by eCbNe^{C_bN}. The actual seed-to-cup coefficient is now exactly ∥ΠcNξ∥∥ξ∥=N3/2|pN′(cN)|=Nsin⁡π2N=π2N(1+O(N−2)).(27) \boxed{\quad \frac{\|\Pi_{c_N}\xi\|}{\|\xi\|} =\frac{N^{3/2}}{|p_N'(c_N)|} =\sqrt N\sin\frac{\pi}{2N} =\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt N}(1+O(N^{-2})). \quad} \qquad\text{(27)} Here ΠcN=rcN(BcN)/pN′(cN)\Pi_{c_N}=r_{c_N}(B_{c_N})/p_N'(c_N) is asserted over ℂ\mathbb C; an integral projector requires localizing at pN′(cN)p_N'(c_N). The exact zero-level generator satisfies (BcN−cNI)−(B2−2I)=(q−1)(𝖪−1−I),∥(BcN−cNI)−(B2−2I)∥≤2|q−1|=O(N−1).(28) (B_{c_N}-c_NI)-(B_2-2I) =(q-1)(\mathsf K^{-1}-I),\qquad \|(B_{c_N}-c_NI)-(B_2-2I)\|\le2|q-1|=O(N^{-1}). \qquad\text{(28)} The actual source and closing remain precisely (20). This correction does not calculate a geometric relative framing period or identify the finite projector with a geometric local cycle.

There is a concrete finite cyclic realization at this corrected parameter. Define C0(z)=2C_0(z)=2, C1(z)=zC_1(z)=z, and Ca+1(z)=zCa(z)−qCa−1(z)C_{a+1}(z)=zC_a(z)-qC_{a-1}(z). Then Ca(s+q/s)=sa+qas−aC_a(s+q/s)=s^a+q^as^{-a}, so VN=BcN−qCN−1(BcN)2,VNN=I,BcN=VN+qVN−1,ΠcN=1N∑a=0N−1VNa.(29) V_N=\frac{B_{c_N}-qC_{N-1}(B_{c_N})}{2},\qquad V_N^N=I,\qquad B_{c_N}=V_N+qV_N^{-1},\qquad \Pi_{c_N}=\frac1N\sum_{a=0}^{N-1}V_N^a . \qquad\text{(29)} Indeed VNV_N has eigenvalue QjQ^j at λj\lambda_j. For the full spectral idempotents Πj\Pi_j, character orthogonality gives 1N∑a=0N−1(VNa)*VNa=∑j=0N−1Πj*Πj.(30) \frac1N\sum_{a=0}^{N-1}(V_N^a)^*V_N^a =\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\Pi_j^*\Pi_j . \qquad\text{(30)} This positive cyclic-average Gram retains all channels and has the controlled exponential cost above. The operator average in (29) fixes the actual source; it is not identified with a bounded-time geometric flow.

6. Coverage and the remaining source comparison

C already proves the free companion form and controlled Krylov recurrence; H already gives a smaller exact invariant-source module. S selects c=2c=2 for the original cup and proves its stable projector bound. The additional assertions here are the collision-regular polynomial kernel (12), its free exact sequence over 𝒪N[c]\mathcal O_N[c], and the literal seed calibration with coefficients (23) and (27).

The cut and soft-cut texts keep different finite currents and the actual geometric framing form. The present finite coefficient is not identified with that form’s relative period or with a geometric local kernel. In particular (20) retains a closing kernel and complex word phases; no nonzero lower bound for its particular scalar follows. No geometric cycle, new volume class, or finite-exception theorem is asserted.
