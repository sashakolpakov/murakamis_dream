A positive tail-free Racah scalar at one actual matching edge

30 October 2026. This is a specialization of the actual generic four-port coefficient theorem to the two halves of ONE crossing-circle matching edge. Its four outside objects are all the original physical V_(N-1). The scalar is positive after the retained single root zero is removed, including every new high recoupling channel and both level parities. All fixed generic scalar jets have polynomial relative cost. Each full recoupled tensor term has at most one pole, so its first normal needs Taylor order two after clearing that pole.

This theorem controls the explicitly stated scalar and Laurent budget. It does not estimate the native boundary change-of-frame costs, the completed Hermite operator, or the surrounding coherent contraction.

1. The full actual channel range is uniformly saturated

Put d=N-1, q=exp(pi i/N), and fix

0<=a<=floor(d/2),
(A,B,C,D)=(d,d,d,d), E=2a,
G=2g, 0<=g<=d.                                      (1)

The G range in (1) is the ENTIRE generic four-port recoupling range. No high partner is discarded. These are the four-port highest-weight conventions of the actual coefficient theorem. The corresponding six spins are (d/2,d/2,d/2,d/2,a,g). Its faces and opposite sums are

F=(d+a,d+a,d+g,d+g),
Q=(2d,d+a+g,d+a+g).                                 (2)

Every face is at least d, and all its triangle deficits are among k,k,d-k for k=a or g. They lie in [0,d]. All four faces and three opposite sums are INTEGERS, also when N is even and the four physical spins d/2 are half-integral. Consequently the true Racah range is

d+max(a,g)<=z<=min(2d,d+a+g),                        (3)

and all seven denominator factorial indices are in [0,d]. Every numerator [z+1]! contains exactly one [N] and contains NO [2N]. Thus this special physical block is tail-free even when g>d/2. This is not a claim that arbitrary high recoupling partners are tail-free.

For the genuine generic symmetric factorial at zeta define

S_ag(zeta)=sum_(z in (3)) (-1)^z [z+1]! /
      ([z-d-a]!^2 [z-d-g]!^2 [2d-z]!
                          [d+a+g-z]!^2),
G_ag(zeta)=(-1)^d S_ag(zeta)/[N]_zeta.               (4)

The division in (4) is performed in every original summand before root specialization. It is an analytic germ at q, not a derivative of a root-only replacement.

2. An exact positive squared-trinomial formula

Set s_k=product_(v=1)^k 2sin(pi v/N), s_0=1. The elementary identity s_(d-k)=N/s_k gives the explicit root scalar

R_N(a,g)=G_ag(q)
  =sum_(r=max(0,a-g))^(min(a,d-g))
        [s_(g+r)/(s_(g+r-a)s_r s_(a-r))]^2 >0.     (5)

To verify every factor, put k=z-d. The removed-numerator formula is

N sum_k s_k /
   [s_(k-a)^2 s_(k-g)^2 s_(d-k) s_(a+g-k)^2].

Replace s_(d-k) by N/s_k and then set r=k-g. All indices in (5) are between 0 and d. The signs of the factors [N+1],…,[z+1] combine with (-1)^(d+z) to give +1. No half-spin phase was omitted: A+B+C+D+E+G=4d+2a+2g is even.

The full high partner has the SAME root scalar:

R_N(a,g)=R_N(a,d-g).                                (6)

This is termwise: replace g by d-g and r by a-r, and use s_(d-k)=N/s_k in the numerator/first-denominator ratio. The actual ranges in (5) are carried onto one another. Equation (6) is proved at both parities and does not discard any generic normal.

The face metric is especially simple. For a face (d/2,d/2,k), put

Delta_k^2=[k]!^2[d-k]!/[d+k+1]!,
D_k(zeta)=(-1)^k [N]_zeta Delta_k^2.                 (7)

All D_k are analytic and nonzero at q, and

D_k(q)=s_k s_(d-k)/N=1.                             (8)

There are two faces of each kind k=a,g. The generic normalized scalar therefore has the single-valued form

H_ag(zeta)=G_ag(zeta)D_a(zeta)D_g(zeta),
H_ag(q)=R_N(a,g).                                   (9)

Its branch-independent identity is the exact actual scalar convention

H_ag^2=[N]^2 S_ag^2 product_(four faces) Delta^2.    (10)

All powers and signs in (10) are the original ones. A scalar diagonal gauge relating (10) to the physical recoupling matrix is still required; root value 1 of the face factors alone does not make that matrix unitary.

3. Every fixed generic scalar jet has polynomial relative cost

There are universal c,C>0 such that, for ALL labels in (1), on

zeta=q exp(t), |t|<=c/[N^2(1+log N)],               (11)

G_ag/G_ag(q), D_a, D_g and H_ag/H_ag(q) are holomorphic and remain in a fixed small disk about 1. In particular they are nonzero. For every fixed m>=0 this implies

|partial_t^m G_ag(0)|+|partial_t^m H_ag(0)|
   <=C_m [N^2(1+log N)]^m R_N(a,g).                (12)

The same relative estimate holds for their inverses.

For clarity, the proof applies to the COMPLETE generic germ in (4). After the one [N] factor is canceled, each summand and each face metric is a quotient of factors zetau-zeta(-u), 1<=u<2N, u!=N, with fixed factorial multiplicities. Its logarithmic change on (11) is bounded by C N^2(1+log N)|t|, using |sin(pi u/N)| bounded below by a constant times dist(u,NZ)/N and the finite harmonic sum. At the root every summand of G_ag is positive by (5). On a sufficiently small common disk these summands remain in one narrow sector, so their sum has the same uniform relative bound. Equations (7),(8) give it for D_a,D_g. Cauchy’s estimate proves (12). This is the direct supported-scalar argument, with (2),(3) proving its hypotheses for the entire present range, including half-integral physical spins and high g.

In particular no missing [2N]T normal survives here: such a numerator factor is absent from every term before differentiation. The result does not say that a normal equals the derivative of the positive root expression (5); (4) remains the differentiated source.

4. The actual physical coefficient has only a simple pole

Let L_(2g,2a) be the actual inverse Racah coefficient, with its original pivotal turn and first-highest-entry-one Clebsch bases. The exact source formula is

L_(2g,2a)=chi_E X_E/(b_G J_n S0 V_n) S_ag,
n=d-g,
S0=[2d+1]!/([d-a]!^2[a]!^2),
V_n=zeta^n (Q^(-d-g-1);Q)_n /
            [(Q;Q)_n^2 (Q^(-2d-1);Q)_n],
Q=zeta^2.                                          (13)

The four prefactors chi_E,X_E,b_G,J_n are root units. This follows from their actual boundary product formulas: E=2a<N; their physical endpoint factorial indices are all in [0,d]. No first-normal channel norm is divided out.

S0 has exactly one root zero. The numerator of V_n has a single root zero exactly when

2g<d.                                              (14)

Indeed its negative exponents run from -(d+g+1) to -(2g+2), and the only possible root multiple is -N. Its denominator factors are all units: the negative product starts at -(2N-1) and has length d-g, ending strictly before -N. The two (Q;Q)_n factors have n<N. By the strict positivity in (5), S_ag has exactly one root zero. Thus the ACTUAL coefficient orders are

ord_q L_(2g,2a)=-1 for 2g<d,
ord_q L_(2g,2a)= 0 for 2g>=d.                      (15)

This includes a=0 and all endpoints. It does not assert positivity or regularity of the uncompleted physical coefficient matrix.

Let T_(2g) be the actual generic target invariant tensor. Its Clebsch entries have the regular canceled physical coproduct formulas; the only possible pole is the canonical intermediate cup, with denominator (Q;Q)(2g). Hence T(2g) is regular for 2g<N and has at most a simple pole for 2g>=N. These two ranges never overlap the pole range in (15). Consequently

pole_order [L_(2g,2a) T_(2g)] <=1                 (16)

for EVERY channel and level parity. The fixed source tensor S_(2a) is regular, so the simple poles on the right of its exact recoupling identity cancel in their full sum.

After multiplication by a local parameter t, each recoupled term is holomorphic. Its contribution to the first normal of the regular whole contraction is extracted from Taylor coefficient t^2. Thus this special block has a uniform first-normal budget TWO, improving the general all-port budget four. If a regular surrounding contraction is present, it must be included when extracting that coefficient; its derivatives are not discarded.

5. Relation to the geometric pair localization

The prescribed-facet corollary allows the two physical-support exceptions to be placed at the two halves of any chosen crossing-circle matching edge. Their four outside objects are then all V_d, so the present specialization applies regardless of the ribbon order of the other matching edges. A physical MAIN edge does not have that automatic property: its two channel legs can be adjacent instead of opposite in the actual cyclic order.

The exact typed bend from the original W_a pi_a block to S_(2a), its fixed gamma factor, and the unchanged physical mark belong to the separate source interface. The normal must be extracted from the complete generic contraction, with all channels in (1) retained.

Even after that interface, this is not a sharp completed block norm or a bound on the full source. Its scalar gauge factors can carry nontrivial boundary cost; the other local columns are not automatically in the central domain; and a global selected cycle and coherent bound remain necessary. The theorem makes the exceptional matching block an explicit tail-free scalar/finite-jet problem, without declaring those remaining tasks solved.

Audit: exact matching-edge scalar, original recoupling and normal budget. It passes 21,772 support/phase/pole cases, ten independently reconstructed generic source tensor families with all 47 recoupling coefficients, 529 retained root germs, 1,257 termwise partner checks, and 1,058 exact face-unit jets. In 406 comparisons the partners have the same root value but DIFFERENT generic first derivatives. The audit retains that distinction; its finite cases test conventions, while the written harmonic-product argument proves the uniform jet bound.

The positive scalar Racah model does not supply a norm for the native matrix source or control a whole source from root-face values.
