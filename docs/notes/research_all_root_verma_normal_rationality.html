Rational quantum normals of the original graded Verma source

Private written proof, 17 November 2026. The same untruncated, ordinarily pivoted, graded cut source as [V] has rational coefficients at every quantum-normal order and every prescribed root q_N. At order j its only possible denominator is the (2j+1)-st power of the original deleted classical Burau determinant. The proof retains closed head-carry cycles, not just the root associated graded.

On genuine integral-color curves this source is the original finite reduced, zero-framed period. Its order-j root jet at color NR is polynomial in R of degree at most j. The grading-one completion is uniquely fixed by those finite-color germs, is presentation independent, and satisfies every supplied actual generic color recurrence.

All-root rational loops and recurrence compatibility have existing literature coverage, discussed at the end. No literature proof is a premise here. This is an independent proof for the specified original graded source, with an explicit determinant exponent and normal path. It proves no convergence of the whole quantum expansion, large-level estimate, geometric-cycle coefficient, or volume lower bound.

4. The cut trace and its unchanged determinant

An elementary trace fact is needed. If D is a degree-zero polynomial differential operator of order at most d, then Tr⁡Sym(DΓ(B))has denominator at mostdet⁡(I−B)d+1.(16) \operatorname{Tr}_{\mathrm{ Sym}}(D\Gamma(B)) \quad\text{has denominator at most}\quad \det(I-B)^{d+1}. \qquad\text{(16)} This is a formal identity when B has positive formal degree and requires no inverse of B.

The order-zero trace is det⁡(I−B)−1\det(I-B)^{-1}, proved coefficientwise in [V, (3.1)]. Each homogeneous Weyl monomial Zα∂βZ^\alpha\partial^\beta, with |α|=|β|≤d|\alpha|=|\beta|\le d, is a linear combination of products of at most d vector fields Zi∂ZjZ_i\partial_{Z_j}. Normal ordering their product gives the desired leading monomial plus smaller-order terms; induction subtracts the latter. A vector field on Gamma(B) is the infinitesimal left substitution B↦(I+sEij)BB\mapsto(I+sE_{ij})B. Thus the trace is obtained by at most d polynomial directional derivatives of the determinant inverse. Each raises its denominator power by at most one. These identities hold on each finite homogeneous polynomial space, proving (16).

Put T0=diag⁡(0,1,…,1)T_0=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,\ldots,1), the ACTUAL first-block-zero projector, and z=tau^N. Giving z to every block variable gives the original grading on this projector. Equations (14)–(17) prove (6) over 𝕂(x)\mathbb K(x). Their numerator is polynomial in tau: head grading is nonnegative and the trace differentiations have polynomial matrix-entry numerators. No negative grading powers have been introduced.

6. Integral colors before any specialization

For ANY positive integer L, put x=q^(L-1) before taking a root. The actual subspace W_L=span{v_a:a>=L} is a Verma submodule; the quotient is the ordinary L-dimensional color. This follows also directly from (2). If a>=L>a-n, the positive color product contains 1−x−2QL−1=01-x^{-2}Q^{L-1}=0; its other strand rises b to b+n. Both signs therefore propagate any high flag along its physical strand, in the actual swapped order.

A high flag in any contributing matrix path, even one first created internally, cannot disappear. At tau=1 this is the original reduced zero-framed C_L(q). The ordinary closed pivots contribute x^(b-1). Restoring the common Cartan scalar of each crossing and dividing by the ordinary twist for zero framing contributes x^(-w). Their product is exactly x^c in (3). The highest cut introduces no additional dimension factor. This is the finite source convention in [A], and uses no frozen root pivot.

8. Canonical completion, uniqueness, and actual recurrences

Define V̂N,K(q,x)=∑j≥0δjVβ,j(x,1).(26) \widehat V_{N,K}(q,x)= \sum_{j\ge0}\delta^j V_{\beta,j}(x,1). \qquad\text{(26)} It is a formal series with rational meridian coefficients. This does not assert generic convergence of (3) at tau=1.

The following elementary rigidity suffices. In eta=log(1-delta), u=log(x/x*), a formal germ H(eta,u) vanishing on every line u=s_R eta is zero. Its homogeneous degree-n coefficient restricts to a polynomial of degree at most n in s_R. Infinitely many distinct slopes force every coefficient of that polynomial to vanish.

Two presentations of the same zero-framed knot have the same C_NR(q) in (20), hence the same restrictions on all these lines after (21). Equation (23) makes their coefficients regular germs. Rigidity identifies the full germs; equality of a rational meridian germ identifies the rational function. Thus (26) is presentation independent.

It is also unique among formal rational-meridian series regular at x*, even in x, and with the prescribed integral-color germs. Without evenness, use one parity of R, still infinitely many slopes, for the analogous local uniqueness at each root point.

Take any SUPPLIED actual generic color recurrence, in the original meridian convention M=-q^L, ∑i∈Iai(q,−qL)CL+i(q)=0,(27) \sum_{i\in I}a_i(q,-q^L)\,C_{L+i}(q)=0, \qquad\text{(27)} for all sufficiently large positive L and a fixed finite integer shift set I. Clear coefficient denominators first. Its action on the completion is H(q,x)=∑i∈Iai(q,−qx)V̂N,K(q,qix).(28) H(q,x)=\sum_{i\in I}a_i(q,-qx) \widehat V_{N,K}(q,q^i x). \qquad\text{(28)} All terms are regular at x, by (23). On the genuine curve x=q^(NR-1), the shifted colors NR+i are positive for large R. The ANY-L statement (20) makes (28) exactly (27). Restrict here to odd positive R: the genuine curve then has root x itself, and its logarithmic slope is s_R, with no sign gauge applied to the possibly odd recurrence coefficients. These are still infinitely many distinct slopes. Rigidity gives H=0 as a germ and coefficientwise as rational functions.

Thus every supplied actual generic color annihilator acts on this same selected all-root completion. No existence theorem for a recurrence, minimality, equality of annihilator ideals, or geometric-factor identification is inferred.

9. Coverage and scope

The general all-root rational-loop and recurrence statements have existing literature coverage. Garoufalidis–Wheeler, “A lift of the colored Jones polynomial of a knot” (2026), Theorem 1.3 and Corollaries 1.5 and 1.9, state all-root loop expansions via an etale Habiro ring and recurrence compatibility. Their abstract and theorem/corollary statements were consulted for coverage only. Willetts’s unified invariant already gives the root ADO/Alexander normalization recorded in [V]. These are statement-level coverage, not proof premises. No novelty is claimed for all-root rationality itself.

The internal addition here is the independent, literal proof for (3): its bounded differential insertion, all closed carry cycles, exact projector and determinant power, cancellation of apparent poles, and original finite-color normal restrictions. The finite high-color PBW theorem cannot simply replace Section 3: at generic Verma meridian, unbounded block transfers survive already at order zero.

Fixed-x, fixed-M=-qx, and integral-color normals are different. The latter paths require their original chain rule. No generic analytic completion, coefficient-size estimate, geometric cycle comparison, or selected scalar lower follows.
