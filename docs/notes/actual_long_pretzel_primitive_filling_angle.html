The selected long-pretzel filling has primitive angle 2pi

29 October 2026. The actual unit-charge equation gives a primitive 2pi geometric meridian, not merely scalar PSL holonomy. The uncentered SL longitude log would give the WRONG angle. This note fixes the small geometric logs from the actual cusp development, proves their exact oriented core-length formula for independently signed slopes, and identifies the normal rotation without a hidden integer. The positive compact-core deformation and the resulting global tube completion are supplied by their separate geometric lemmas; scalar holonomy alone would not supply them.

1. Center the projective geometric logs before forming the slope

The literal mixed-circle certificate fixes

M_i=exp(u_i)=H_i^(-1/2), u_i=-z_i/2,
lambda_i=-L_i^(-1), v_i=Log lambda_i=pi i-ell_i,
z_i=Log H_i, ell_i=Log L_i,

with all z_i,ell_i zero at the complete geometric point. The preferred longitude has SL eigenvalue -1 there. Its PROJECTIVE multiplier is nevertheless one. In a coordinate whose infinity is the selected peripheral eigenflag, the actual affine holonomy multipliers are M_i^2 and lambda_i^2. Their continuously small logs are therefore

Ui=2ui=−zi,Vi=2(vi−πi)=−2ℓi. U_i=2u_i=-z_i,\qquad V_i=2(v_i-\pi i)=-2\ell_i. \tag{1}

Both vanish at completeness. This centering is fixed ONCE by the complete affine translation cusp, independently of the slope. It is not an opportunistic logarithm reset at the filling point. The positive original diagonal marking is

mu_i=W_i(1+i c), lambda_i=2ic W_i,
c=cos(pi/s), W_i>0,

in its complete Euclidean cusp coordinate. Thus

(dV_i/dU_i)(0)=2tau_s=2ic/(1+ic), Im(2tau_s)>0.     (2)

The basis is primitive: it is the actual meridian and boundary of the crossing disk, not half or twice that boundary. The retained R^(-1) is already in this mu_+ marking; no additional meridian shear is allowed.

For the ACTUAL source equation of charge m_i,

z_i+2p_i ell_i=2pi i m_i,

equation (1) gives EXACTLY

Ui+piVi=−2πimi. \boxed{U_i+p_iV_i=-2\pi i m_i.} \tag{3}

In contrast u_i+p_i v_i=pi i(p_i-m_i) is an UNCENTERED SL eigenlog. Its p_i*pi i term is the old longitude spin, not geometric winding about a new core. Doubling that expression and calling it a cone angle would be incorrect. Likewise using undoubled eigenlogs would incorrectly give pi instead of 2pi.

2. A uniform exact core-length and orientation identity

Separate evenness of the original analytic source action implies

ell_i=z_i a_i(z_1^2,...,z_s^2), a_i(0)=tau_s.

Hence the ratio

t_i(z)=V_i/U_i=2a_i(z_1^2,...,z_s^2)

is holomorphic through z_i=0. On a fixed sufficiently small complex source neighborhood, Im t_i>=b>0. This remains true for the simultaneous selected fillings, regardless of the signs or relative magnitudes of their large p_i.

Combining V_i=t_iU_i with (3), for REAL nonzero p_i, gives the exact formulas

Ui=−2πimi1+piti,Vi=−2πimiti1+piti,Re⁡Vi=2πmiIm⁡ti|1+piti|2. U_i=\frac{-2\pi i m_i}{1+p_it_i},\qquad V_i=\frac{-2\pi i m_i t_i}{1+p_it_i},\qquad \boxed{\operatorname{Re}V_i =\frac{2\pi m_i\operatorname{Im}t_i} {|1+p_it_i|^2}.} \tag{4}

The denominator cannot vanish because Im t_i>0. Thus each nonzero charge gives U_i,V_i nonzero and a genuine LOXODROMIC core generator: Re V_i has the sign of m_i. In particular it cannot be a nontrivial elliptic with an accidentally killed eigenvalue. At the selected unit charges the positive core length is

length_i=|Re V_i|=2pi Im t_i/|1+p_i t_i|^2>0.

There is no comparison between different slope sizes in this proof. The oriented logarithmic lattice is also fixed:

Im⁡(U¯iVi)=|Ui|2Im⁡ti=2πmiRe⁡Vi>0. \operatorname{Im}(\overline U_iV_i) =|U_i|^2\operatorname{Im}t_i =2\pi m_i\operatorname{Re}V_i>0. \tag{5}

Thus the two period directions are linearly independent over R, with the SAME orientation as the positive complete cusp. Reversing the unit charge jointly reverses U_i,V_i and the core orientation; it does not mirror the hyperbolic structure or change the slope.

3. The small source logs ARE the geometric angular lifts

Here is the precise local lemma needed to exclude a hidden extra 2pi winding. Its input is the genuine positive similarity cusp-link deformation supplied by the positive-core construction, not an abstract representation with prescribed eigenvalues.

Choose the moving peripheral eigenflag as infinity by a frame continuous at the complete cusp. Let the developed similarity cross-section have holonomy

mu: w -> exp(U)w+A,
lambda: w -> exp(V)w+B.

On the complete limit A->W(1+ic), B->2icW, both nonzero. Develop the two primitive generator paths using a fixed finite cusp triangulation and a fixed basepoint. Their images and their Euclidean lengths stay uniformly bounded near completeness: each comes from finitely many continuously varying positive triangles. Call a common image bound R and length bound L. These are bounds in the continuous cusp frame, not a singular diagonalizing frame.

For U!=0 sufficiently small, the common finite affine fixed point is

k=A/(1-exp(U)).

Commutativity makes it the fixed point for lambda too. Since A has a nonzero limit, |k| tends to infinity as U tends to zero, uniformly in all the other small parameters. In particular |k|>2R. Along either generator path the analytic logarithmic coordinate

T=Log(dev-k)

can be continued in the ball of radius R, which misses k. Its variation satisfies the elementary bound

|ΔT|≤L/(|k|−R)→0. |\Delta T|\le L/(|k|-R)\longrightarrow0. \tag{6}

The endpoint affine holonomy gives exp(Delta T)=exp(U) or exp(V). Therefore Delta T-U or Delta T-V is an integer multiple of 2pi i. Both quantities tend to zero. For all sufficiently small parameters the ONLY possible integer is zero:

ΔμT=U,ΔλT=V. \boxed{\Delta_\mu T=U,\qquad\Delta_\lambda T=V.} \tag{7}

This is pointwise quantitative; no connectedness of a punctured parameter domain or arbitrary lift choice is used. It rules out an added integer for EACH primitive generator before multiplying one by the large p. Because the finite source/port frame and the cusp triangles vary on one fixed neighborhood, the argument is uniform for independently signed large slopes. If only a PSL character were given without this geometric cusp-link development, the conclusion (7) would not follow from eigenvalues alone.

The log-link/tube theorem uses precisely these periods. Its compact translation torus has no extra wrapping index because the primitive generators have periods U,V generating its full rank-two lattice. One must not infer a period lattice solely from the multiplier equations without the small-increment argument (6).

4. Primitive slope, actual angle, and the absence of branching

The filled primitive curve is sigma=mu+p lambda: its first coefficient is one, so it is primitive in the ACTUAL torus basis. Take lambda as the complementary core curve. This is an integral basis change of determinant one. From (3),(7), its exact developed displacements in T are

Delta_sigma T=-2pi i m,
Delta_lambda T=V, Re V!=0.                         (8)

For the angle normalization use H3 around the vertical axis above k. In upper-half-space coordinates set

horizontal=k+exp(t)tanh(r)exp(i theta),
height=exp(t)sech(r).

Direct differentiation gives

ds^2=dr^2+sinh(r)^2 dtheta^2+cosh(r)^2 dt^2.

At fixed r, T=t+Log tanh(r)+i theta, so its periods ARE the axial/rotational displacements (Re T,Im T). Equation (8) has zero axial translation and angular displacement -2pi m. A small normal circle therefore has circumference

2pi |m| sinh(r)=2pi |m| r+O(r^3).                  (9)

For |m|=1 the axis has the ordinary smooth 2pi neighborhood. For |m|=2 it is a 4pi branched neighborhood, although both cases have scalar PSL meridional holonomy. This is the rigorous discriminant that the scalar group relation alone cannot supply. In particular the selected |m|=1 channels give a smooth primitive filling.

For m=1 the displayed meridian angle is -2pi; reversing the orientation of the SAME primitive filling curve gives +2pi. This is the usual orientation-reversing boundary attachment of a solid torus, not a reversal of ambient volume. Formula (5) keeps the positive cusp lattice orientation for either choice of signed slope. More explicitly, for either unit sign choose

meridian_tube=-m sigma, core_tube=m lambda.

Their angular period and axial length are respectively +2pi and |Re V|. Relative to the original oriented (sigma,lambda) basis the change has determinant -m^2=-1, exactly the boundary-orientation reversal required to glue an ORIENTED solid torus. This works for both signs of p without changing the orientation of H3. The exact holomorphic reversed-cap isometry jointly inverts both peripheral generators; it interchanges m=+1 and -1 and preserves (9). No mu_+ -> mu_- framing shear occurs.

The original coherent source has no zero charge, and all 2^s unit signed charges were proved to add. Thus it is exactly the quantum selected channels, rather than an imposed primitive filling, that meet the smooth-angle condition.

5. Scope and the direct geometric lemmas used for completion

The result proves the actual peripheral lift, loxodromic core orientation and primitive smooth-angle calibration, using the positive geometric cusp-link deformation near the established complete anchor. It does not infer a hyperbolic metric from a bare PSL representation. The independent finite-center positive-core proof now supplies the face-compatible local metric and these cusp links. The log-link/tube completion proof identifies the entire end with a standard punctured tube before adding its smooth core.

Straightening the primitive curve μ+pλ\mu+p\lambda uses ϕ′=ϕ−pθ\phi'=\phi-p\theta and inserts −p-p full turns. Thus the retained R−1R^{-1} crossing becomes R−(2p+1)R^{-(2p+1)}. Smooth completion also requires the full identity edge holonomy, including zero axis dilation; the filling angle alone is insufficient.

Combined with the independently proved complete log-link and whole-source/regulator theorems, this is a FULL equality certificate on the unchanged class: each fixed odd s>=25 and independently sufficiently large FIXED signed p_i, with the full N sequence, both parities and composite levels. No slope comparability or all-ideal triangulation assumption is needed for this completion. It makes no assertion for small widths, short slopes, N-dependent slopes/width, or arbitrary hyperbolic knots.
