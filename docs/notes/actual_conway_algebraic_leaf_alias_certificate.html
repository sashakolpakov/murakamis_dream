The exact alias-tangent threshold and a rational port certificate

31 October 2026. Internally reviewed analytic and original-source certificate. The exact algebraic port condition below gives a uniform mixed-source gap; it is a sufficient condition on the actual complete optimizer, not an automatic property of every two-leaf expression. This enlarges the sufficient leaf interval in the existing whole mixed-source Conway argument. It does not assert that every expression using h^2 has the required strict geometry, finite lattice packet, or passing leaf labels. Those source and geometric hypotheses are supplied separately by the two-leaf source interface and actual geometric interface.

The actual functions are those of the full original mixed-source theorem:

J(x)=∫0xlog⁡(2sin⁡πt)dt,g(a)=J(2a)−4J(a),0≤a≤12,k(u)=−2J(u/2),0≤u≤1. \begin{gathered} J(x)=\int_0^x\log(2\sin\pi t)\,dt,\\ g(a)=J(2a)-4J(a),\quad 0\le a\le\tfrac12,\qquad k(u)=-2J(u/2),\quad 0\le u\le1. \end{gathered}

The coefficient bounds for the full original core and alias, including Hermite/normal data and both parities, are retained from that source theorem. Nothing below replaces them by a semisimple or nodal estimate.

1. Exact minimization of the leaf tangent deficit

For 1/4<=a<1/2, put s(a)=g’(a)=2log cot(pi a) and

I(a)=min0≤u≤1[g(a)+s(a)(u−a)−k(u)]. I(a)=\min_{0\le u\le1}\left[g(a)+s(a)(u-a)-k(u)\right].\tag{1}

The exact derivatives are

g″(a)=−4πsin⁡(2πa)<0,k′(u)=−log⁡(2sin⁡(πu/2)),k″(u)=−π2cot⁡(πu/2)<0(0<u<1). \begin{gathered} g''(a)=-\frac{4\pi}{\sin(2\pi a)}<0,\qquad k'(u)=-\log(2\sin(\pi u/2)),\\ k''(u)=-\frac\pi2\cot(\pi u/2)<0\quad(0<u<1). \end{gathered}\tag{2}

Let a0=atan(sqrt(2))/pi. For a in [1/4,a0], the unique maximizer of k(u)-s(a)u is

u(a)=2πarcsin⁡tan⁡2(πa)2. u(a)=\frac2\pi\arcsin\frac{\tan^2(\pi a)}2.\tag{3}

with u in [1/3,1]. At a=a0 it is the endpoint 1. On the strict interior the envelope derivative is

I′(a)=g″(a)(u(a)−a)<0. I'(a)=g''(a)(u(a)-a)<0.

The continuous extension has the same monotonicity at both endpoints. The already proved exact lower bound is

I(a)≥I(a0)≥c0,c0=2Catalanπ−34log⁡2>1613960>0. I(a)\ge I(a_0)\ge c_0,\qquad c_0=\frac{2\operatorname{Catalan}}\pi-\frac34\log2 >\frac{161}{3960}>0.\tag{4}

For a>=a0, s(a)<=-log2. Since k’ decreases to -log2, the function s(a)u-k(u) is decreasing on [0,1], strictly except possibly at its right endpoint. Thus its minimum is EXACTLY at u=1. Since k(1)=0,

I(a)=H(a):=g(a)+(1−a)g′(a),a0≤a<12. I(a)=H(a):=g(a)+(1-a)g'(a),\qquad a_0\le a<\tfrac12.\tag{5}

In particular H’(a)=(1-a)g’’(a)<0. No sampling of the alias range or unknown optimizer is involved in (5).

2. The exact threshold exists and lies below one third

Introduce the real inverse-tangent integral

Ti⁡2(x)=∫0xarctan⁡ttdt,x≥0. \operatorname{Ti}_2(x)=\int_0^x\frac{\arctan t}{t}\,dt,\qquad x\ge0.

If x=cot(pi a), integration from g(1/2)=0 gives

g(a)=−2π∫0xlog⁡t1+t2dt=2π[Ti⁡2(x)−arctan(x)logx]. \begin{aligned} g(a)&=-\frac2\pi\int_0^x\frac{\log t}{1+t^2}\,dt\\ &=\frac2\pi\left[\operatorname{Ti}_2(x)-\arctan(x)\log x\right]. \end{aligned}

Since 1-a=1/2+atan(x)/pi, this yields the exact simplification

H(a)=2πTi⁡2(x)+log⁡x,x=cot⁡(πa). H(a)=\frac2\pi\operatorname{Ti}_2(x)+\log x,\qquad x=\cot(\pi a).\tag{6}

At a0, H is strictly positive by (4). At a=1/3, x=1/sqrt(3), and atan(t)<t gives Ti2(x)<x. Using pi>3 and log3>1,

H(13)<233−12<0. H(\tfrac13)<\frac{2}{3\sqrt3}-\frac12<0.\tag{7}

For the elementary log inequality, exp(1)<3 follows directly by bounding its factorial series. The last inequality in (7) follows from 16<27. Strict decrease and continuity therefore prove a UNIQUE

acrit∈(a0,13),H(acrit)=0. a_{\mathrm{crit}}\in(a_0,\tfrac13),\qquad H(a_{\mathrm{crit}})=0.\tag{8}

For the whole interval 1/4<=a<1/2, the exact supporting-plane condition I(a)>0 holds if and only if a<a_crit. At equality it has zero margin; above it, the genuine alias endpoint u=1 lies above the tangent. That is a failure of this sufficient tangent method, not a lower bound for an actual mixed source or a volume obstruction. No decimal approximation defines or proves (8).

3. A sharper reusable algebraic certificate: cot(pi a)>=2/3

Set

a*=1πarctan⁡32. a_* =\frac1\pi\arctan\frac32.

It lies strictly above a0 because 3/2>sqrt(2). At x=2/3 the alternating series for Ti2 is valid, with decreasing terms. Four terms give

Ti⁡2(23)>23−(2/3)39+(2/3)525−(2/3)749=17087622679075. \operatorname{Ti}_2(\tfrac23) >\frac23-\frac{(2/3)^3}{9}+\frac{(2/3)^5}{25}-\frac{(2/3)^7}{49} =\frac{1708762}{2679075}.\tag{9}

The positive series log((1+y)/(1-y))=2sum_(n>=0)y^(2n+1)/(2n+1) at y=1/5, bounding all remaining denominators by 7, gives

log⁡32≤2[15+13⋅53+15⋅55+17⋅57(1−1/25)]=177391437500. \begin{aligned} \log\frac32 &\le2\left[\frac15+\frac{1}{3\cdot5^3}+\frac{1}{5\cdot5^5} +\frac{1}{7\cdot5^7(1-1/25)}\right]\\ &=\frac{177391}{437500}. \end{aligned}\tag{10}

Since pi<22/7, equations (6),(9),(10) imply the exact rational margin

H(a*)>71117087622679075−177391437500=57269136687500>12500. H(a_*)>\frac7{11}\frac{1708762}{2679075}-\frac{177391}{437500} =\frac{57269}{136687500}>\frac1{2500}.\tag{11}

Every inequality here is analytic or rational arithmetic; no asymptotic computation or numerical sign test is used. Combining (4), strict decrease in (5), and (11) proves

k(u)≤g(a)+g′(a)(u−a)−12500,0≤u≤1,14≤a≤a*. \begin{gathered} k(u)\le g(a)+g'(a)(u-a)-\frac1{2500},\\ 0\le u\le1,\qquad \frac14\le a\le a_*. \end{gathered}\tag{12}

It also sharpens the exact bracket to

1πarctan⁡32<acrit<13. \frac1\pi\arctan\frac32<a_{\mathrm{crit}}<\frac13.

The certificate (12) is algebraic in the positive actual port variable:

X=cot⁡(πa)≥23⇔9X2≥4. X=\cot(\pi a)\ge\frac23 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 9X^2\ge4.\tag{13}

The positivity of X and the retained a>=1/4 hypothesis matter in the equivalence and source application. No claim is made that the true transcendental threshold itself is an algebraic port condition.

For comparison, the simpler bound X>=7/10 also follows without the Ti2 series. For a0<=a<=atan(10/7)/pi, concavity gives g(a)>=g(a0)+g’(a)(a-a0). Hence

H(a)≥c0+(1−a0)[g′(a)+log⁡2]>1613960−log⁡5049>1613960−149=3929194040>0. \begin{aligned} H(a)&\ge c_0+(1-a_0)[g'(a)+\log2]\\ &>\frac{161}{3960}-\log\frac{50}{49} >\frac{161}{3960}-\frac1{49}=\frac{3929}{194040}>0. \end{aligned}

The cutoff (13) admits the larger interval and will suffice below.

4. Exact conditional mixed-source transfer

Consider a FIXED actual Conway source made from the original leaves T(R^epsilon L_j), actual Newton products and literal turns, with the original final cylindrical closure. Suppose its complete pure source has a unique strict optimizer, every unforced gate is strict, and every actual complete leaf label satisfies

14<aℓ<acrit. \frac14<a_\ell<a_{\mathrm{crit}}.\tag{14}

Retain all finite source, strict lattice, nonzero complete packet and geometric assumptions required by the existing assembly. The present statement supplies the mixed-source inequality; it does not reprove those other assumptions for a new grammar.

For a finite set of leaves define c=min_l I(a_l)>0. The original all-color core has uniform upper rate

g̃(u)=g(max⁡(u,14)),0≤u≤12. \widetilde g(u)=g(\max(u,\tfrac14)),\qquad 0\le u\le\tfrac12.

This function is concave: it is constant up to 1/4, its right derivative there is zero, and it is strictly concave thereafter. Thus, for every leaf in (14),

g̃(u)≤g(aℓ)+g′(aℓ)(u−aℓ). \widetilde g(u)\le g(a_\ell)+g'(a_\ell)(u-a_\ell).\tag{15}

with equality only at u=a_l. Its pure-core counterpart obeys the same upper bound. For an alias, (1) gives on its ENTIRE physical range

k(u)≤g(aℓ)+g′(aℓ)(u−aℓ)−I(aℓ),0≤u≤1. k(u)\le g(a_\ell)+g'(a_\ell)(u-a_\ell)-I(a_\ell), \qquad0\le u\le1.\tag{16}

The exact original multiplication action remains concave on max(x,y)<=z<=x+y<=1. Add its supporting planes at the SAME complete state to (15),(16). All linear terms cancel under the original affine turn and closure equations by complete-source stationarity. For any nonempty alias subset S the resulting rate is at most

F0−∑ℓ∈SI(aℓ)≤F0−c. F_0-\sum_{\ell\in S}I(a_\ell)\le F_0-c.\tag{17}

This argument uses neither a vanishing product of aliases after turns nor covariance of a projected coupon. It keeps the full original Hermite/normal terms in the known source bounds. At finite level the actual wire affine right sides are 1-1/N. Fixed tangent slopes therefore cost only O(1) in the exponent, while endpoint-uniform factorial and transport errors are o(N). The fixed circuit has polynomially many wire states and finitely many alias subsets. Reducing c if necessary gives the whole mixed-source radical gap uniformly over all physical auxiliary colors, both parities and composite levels.

The physical-color complement argument also transfers. The concave all-core majorant is bounded above by the sum of the same supporting planes. Equality forces every leaf to equal its original a_l by (15) and every retained strict gate to equal its original state. It therefore has the same unique maximum. Compactness gives a gap outside a fixed complete-state neighborhood. Inside that neighborhood, choose beta<1/2 larger than every leaf label; the existing uniform far-color core loss applies with unchanged terminal source and recurrence branches. Combining it with (17) gives the existing complete-color corridor gap.

If every leaf obeys the algebraic condition (13), one can take the uniform tangent margin 1/2500 in this argument. Under merely (14) the positive margin depends on the fixed actual optimizer, as permitted by the original fixed-expression quantifiers.

This is a CLOSED-source argument. It does not assert an open, coefficientwise error comparison for an h^2 seed at every prescribed output, and does not fill a missing finite top coefficient by changing the exact source lattice.

5. Interface with h^2 and other actual seeds

The existing complete port formulas for A_s=h^s are

X=cot⁡(πa),Bs=Ts(X)Us−1(X),Ys=1Us−1(X). X=\cot(\pi a),\qquad B_s=\frac{T_s(X)}{U_{s-1}(X)},\qquad Y_s=\frac1{U_{s-1}(X)}.

Whenever the independently checked closed strict geometry gives the proper-seed inequalities, s>=3 already forces X>1/sqrt(2)>2/3. Thus all old admissible seeds retain the new certificate automatically. For a proper h^2 seed the known port relation is Y_2=1/(2X). The new test becomes X>=2/3, equivalently Y_2<=3/4, in addition to the actual strict source/geometric hypotheses. The weaker proper h^2 inequality X>1/2 by itself does NOT imply (13) or (14).

Thus a fixed mixed grammar with h^2 seeds can inherit the old mixed-source proof by checking the algebraic inequality (13) at its actual selected complete gluing branch, provided its other source, packet and geometric interfaces have been proved independently. This does not cover all h^2 expressions.

When those separately proved packet and actual marked-geometry hypotheses hold, the original-source long-filling transfer applies with all its original scalar, Cartan, phase and peripheral normalizations. Fix the expression and its retained half-twist signs, then fix independently sufficiently long signed integral full-twist fillings. The threshold may depend on this fixed datum. If the chosen main-component parity gives a knot and ALL auxiliary circles are filled, the conclusion is the actual knot complement volume:

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(K)|=Vol⁡(S3\K). \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log|K_N(K)| =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K).

The limit retains both parities and composite levels. With some auxiliary circles unfilled, the corresponding conclusion concerns the actual remaining cusped link complement. This conditional transfer does not assert that every two-leaf expression passes (13) or (14), and does not replace the original signed knot by a different diagonal or pure partner.
