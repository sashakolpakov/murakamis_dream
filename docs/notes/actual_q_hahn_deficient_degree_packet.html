The actual q-Hahn numerator at a deficient integer degree

30 October 2026. Fix an actual integer degree t below the small channel a. The original generic numerator has an exact forced factor, which persists BEFORE root specialization. Its residual root polynomial is a positive-spectrum real Jacobi determinant. This gives a nonzero relative root amplitude at complex magnetic coordinates away from its explicit cuts, and polynomial cost for genuine q derivatives of this original numerator.

The degree t stays an integer. A joint complex-degree estimate and the full exceptional operator’s generic boundary factors are not asserted. Nor does a nonzero off-grid local packet change the fact that every original physical entry in the forbidden degree slice, including its q normals, is zero.

1. The forced factor is an identity of the original generic source

Use the exact generic source and recurrence of the small-channel normal theorem. Thus N>=3, d=N-1, q varies near q0=exp(pi i/N), Q=q^2, B=Q^-N, and H_a^B is the original finite Newton numerator. Its monic reverse polynomial is

R_a(q;t,z)=z^a S_a^B(q^t/z,Q^t)/(Q^(ta) K_a),
S_a^B(U,T)=U^a H_a^B(U,T/U),
K_a=(B^2 Q^(a+1);Q)_a/(BQ;Q)_a.                  (1)

Throughout 0<=t<a<=floor(d/2). All coefficients and K_a are holomorphic root units where required; in particular K_a(q0)!=0. At U=Q^r, 0<=r<=t, every kth summand of H_a^B contains

product_(j<k)(U-Q^j)
   *product_(j<a-k)(Q^t/U-Q^j).

Either k>r or a-k>t-r, so it vanishes. This is true at GENERIC q, with all the original B factors retained. Consequently

F_t(q,z)=product_(r=0)^t(z-q^(t-2r))
          divides R_a(q;t,z).                            (2)

The nodes are distinct near q0. At a=t+1 monicity gives R_(t+1)=F_t exactly. Write the genuine generic recurrence as

R_(n+1)=(z+alpha_n(q;t))R_n+beta_n(q;t)R_(n-1).

When a>=t+2, its next step, together with (2), forces

beta_(t+1)(q;t)=0 IDENTICALLY.                            (3)

Indeed a nonzero scalar times the lower-degree monic R_t cannot be divisible by F_t. It follows that the quotient

V_m(q;t,z)=R_(t+1+m)(q;t,z)/F_t(q,z)

has its own exact tail recurrence

V_0=1, V_1=z+alpha_(t+1),
V_(m+1)=(z+alpha_(t+1+m))V_m
                        +beta_(t+1+m)V_(m-1).             (4)

When a=t+1 the quotient is simply V_0=1, and no recurrence at a larger degree is needed. No q derivative couples a nonempty tail back across the vanished bridge (3). This differs from differentiating a factorization that holds only at a root of unity.

2. Exact root locations in the deficient chart

Put theta=pi/N and c=-cos((t+1)theta)<0. The root recurrence is

alpha_n=c/[cos(n theta)cos((n+1)theta)],
beta_n=B_n[cos^2(n theta)-c^2],
B_n=sin^2(n theta)/[cos^2(n theta)
                   sin((2n-1)theta)sin((2n+1)theta)].      (5)

For n>=t+2, n<a, beta_n<0. Hence V_(a-t-1) is the characteristic polynomial of a real symmetric Jacobi matrix: its diagonal entries are -alpha_n for n=t+1,…,a-1, and its adjacent entries are sqrt(-beta_n), n=t+2,…,a-1.

Its roots are in fact POSITIVE, not merely real. The exact root recurrence has R_a(z;-c)=(-1)^a R_a(-z;c). Set

t_hi=N-t-2,  -cos((t_hi+1)theta)=-c.

Then t_hi>=d-a lies in the original complete-column chamber. Its proved root factorization has forced LEFT unit-arc roots and a negative-real residual. Reflection z->-z gives precisely the forced roots in (2) and positive-real roots for V. Their simplicity also follows from the irreducible real Jacobi matrix in (4).

Thus every zero of the ACTUAL root polynomial R_a is on

{q^(t-2r):0<=r<=t} union (0,infinity).                    (6)

Choose compact real parameter ranges

0<sigma_0<=sigma=t/N<=sigma_1<1/2,
sigma+eta<=alpha=a/N<=1/2, eta>0.                         (7)

Take the common slit domain

Omega=C\([0,infinity)
          union {exp(i phi):|phi|<=pi sigma_1}).          (8)

It is connected and simply connected: in the logarithm plane with 0<Arg z<2pi the removed unit arc is two finite vertical slits attached to the two edges of the strip. In particular negative real z belongs to Omega. The root polynomial is nowhere zero there, uniformly for the levels and parameters in (7).

3. The actual selected leading phase and relative amplitude

Fix z0=-1. There are functions S_(sigma,alpha),A_(sigma,alpha), holomorphic in z on Omega, with S(z0)=0,A(z0)=1 and A nowhere zero, such that on each fixed compact in Omega

R_a(q0;t,z)/R_a(q0;t,z0)
   =A_(sigma,alpha)(z) exp[N S_(sigma,alpha)(z)]
                                        *(1+O(N^-1)).     (9)

The error and all its fixed z derivatives are uniform. The ACTUAL ratios sigma=t/N and alpha=a/N are used. No uniform continuation in complex sigma is claimed.

Here is a proof and a normalization that determines the functions. From (6), logarithmic derivatives of N^-1 Log[R_a(z)/R_a(-1)] are uniformly bounded on compact subsets of Omega; the logarithm is zero at -1. Its higher derivatives have the same local bounds. For z=-r<0 use the exact reflection

R_a(q0;t,-r)=(-1)^a R_a(q0;t_hi,r),
                      t_hi=N-t-2.                        (10)

The right side is the already proved ACTUAL high-degree packet. Consequently every normal-family limit agrees there with

S_(sigma,alpha)(-r)
   =Phi_alpha(1-sigma,v_r)-Phi_alpha(1-sigma,v_1)
                                  -pi i(v_r-v_1),
v_r=(1-sigma)/2+i log(r)/(2pi), v_1=(1-sigma)/2.            (11)

The identity theorem fixes its continuation uniquely on Omega. The finite offset -2 in (10) is retained for the absolute anchor; only its limit is used in (11). Sequential compactness gives the claimed uniform leading convergence over (7), even when the actual parameters approach their limits slower than 1/N.

For an explicit transport proof of the relative error, put

D=z partial_z, kappa=2pi i,
U=exp(pi i sigma)/z, T=exp(2pi i sigma), J=exp(2pi i alpha).

The SAME root finite polynomial satisfies exactly

(QU-1)(U-T) R(z/Q)+(QT-U)(1-U) R(Qz)
   +[2U+2QTU-(1+Q)U^2-T(Q^-a+Q^(a+1))]R(z)=0.            (12)

This is the original generic Casimir identity for S, with the monic reverse substitution (1); it is valid at these deficient degrees too. Write

A0=(U-1)(U-T),
C0=2U(1+T-U)-T(J+J^-1),
H(p,z)=A0(exp(kappa p)+exp(-kappa p))+C0.

For p=D S the selected characteristic is H(p,z)=0. Its derivative H_p is nonzero on Omega. Indeed

C0^2-4A0^2=-16 T^2 sin^2(pi alpha) W/z^2,
W=1-2cos(pi sigma)z+cos^2(pi alpha)z^2.                    (13)

When sigma<alpha<=1/2, all finite roots of W are positive real: for alpha<1/2 its quadratic has positive sum and product and cos^2(pi sigma)>cos^2(pi alpha); at alpha=1/2 it is linear with positive root. Also A0 vanishes only at the excluded unit-arc endpoints. This proves uniform simplicity on the stated compacts.

The first coefficient of (12), after its exact shifts are expanded, is

H1=kappa[U(U-T)exp(-kappa p)
           +T(1-U)exp(kappa p)+2TU-U^2-TJ],
rho=-(H1+(D p)H_(p,p)/2)/H_p.                            (14)

Define A by D Log A=rho and A(-1)=1. Simplicity and the larger- compact Cauchy bounds first give D(N^-1 Log R)-p=O(N^-1). Cauchy gives that bound for its fixed derivatives too. Substituting a second time into (12) then gives

D(N^-1 Log R)=p+rho/N+O(N^-2).

Integrate along compact paths from -1. The exact normalized ratio at the starting point is one, proving (9) and its error. This is an actual finite-shift bootstrap, not a formal transport ansatz. The coefficients are smooth in the real parameters (7), by the simple characteristic and the actual anchor (11).

The absolute anchor in (9) is explicitly known from (10) and the original ordinary normalization:

R_a(q0;t,-1)=(-1)^a b_a^-1
  product_(r=0)^(a-1)2sin[pi(t_hi-r)/N]
  *q0^[-d t_hi/2-(a^2-da)/2]
  *fhat_a(t_hi,t_hi/2),
b_a=s_(2a)/s_a^2.                                      (15)

The fhat on the last line is the proved nonzero ordinary packet evaluated at its ACTUAL high total t_hi=N-t-2, including a half-integer magnetic center when necessary. Thus (9) has no unfixed multiplicative phase or scale. One may apply that packet and the elementary sine-product estimate directly to (15).

4. Polynomial cost of genuine generic-q numerator derivatives

Let z=q^(t-2u), with the complex physical coordinate u held fixed while q=q0 exp(epsilon) varies. Require a FIXED logarithmic lift: the root z lies in a fixed compact of Omega and |u|<=C N. For example, take u=(t-Log(z)/(pi i/N))/2 with the marked branch of Log on Omega. The fixed integer t and a obey (7). Then for each fixed m

|partial_epsilon^m R_a(q;t,q^(t-2u))|_(epsilon=0)
       <=C_m N^(10m) |R_a(q0;t,q0^(t-2u))|.               (16)

Here the polynomial is the original generic R from (1), with B=Q^-N. It is not obtained by differentiating the root recurrence (5) while discarding B. A root value z alone does not fix this jet: replacing u by u+N L preserves z(q0) but changes its q derivative by -2N L z. The bounded marked lift is therefore necessary.

To prove (16), first split off the genuine factor (2). On a small generic disk its explicit logarithmic derivative is O(N^2), since all root distances from the compact to the unit arc are bounded below. For the residual determinant (4), freeze each upper entry at its positive root value sqrt(-beta_n(q0)); put the generic lower entry equal to -beta_n(q)/sqrt(-beta_n(q0)). Its diagonal is z(q)+alpha_n(q). The determinant is precisely the actual V.

At the root this matrix is the alternating-sign unitary conjugate of zI minus the positive-spectrum real Jacobi matrix in section 2: the frozen positive offdiagonals differ from its negatives by that diagonal sign gauge. Its inverse is bounded by the inverse distance of the compact from [0,infinity). Moreover, for n>=t+2,

cos^2((t+1)theta)-cos^2(n theta)
  =sin((n+t+1)theta)sin((n-t-1)theta)>=c/N,

uniformly on (7). Since B_n>=1/4, every frozen upper entry has size at least c N^-1/2. The original generic recurrence estimates give alpha_n’=O(N^5), beta_n’=O(N^7), uniformly for all required n<=a. Thus the perturbed determinant matrix differs from its root matrix by at most C N^(15/2)|epsilon| in operator norm.

On |epsilon|<=c N^-10 its determinant ratio is bounded uniformly above and below: apply det(I+M0^-1(M-M0)) with dimension at most N. The forced product has the same property there. Cauchy’s formula proves (16). The same argument bounds the exact scalar K_a and monomial factors in (1), whose relative logarithmic derivatives are polynomial. Therefore (16) also controls the original unnormalized Newton numerator at these complex magnetic arguments. It does not divide by D_a(Q^t), which vanishes here.

5. The local kernel application and the remaining period problem

The entire exceptional source uses two copies of these root R polynomials at the SAME deficient integer degree. If both determinant arguments lie in fixed compacts of Omega, (9),(15) give their actual nonzero root packet, with the residual sine product and original normalization retained. This includes large genuinely nonreal determinant arguments; the forbidden physical nodes on the unit arc remain outside Omega.

This is the chart supplied by the actual global real-deficient frame. For each level choose t_N near N/6 and set d_N=tan(pi t_N/N) in that construction. Its two failed factors then have the EXACT real normalized degree t_N/N, with the determinant arguments in the upper and lower half-planes. Fixing one sufficiently large geometric X and an interior midpoint keeps these arguments in fixed compact subsets of Omega as N grows. The marked magnetic lifts stay bounded after division by N.

In the folded limit alpha->1/2, the original magnetic momentum associated to this numerator is Y=-exp(-2pi i D S). Its selected branch is

Y(z)=(z-i sqrt(1-2cos(pi sigma)z))^2
                   /[1-2cos(pi sigma)z+z^2],              (17)

where the square root is positive at z<0 and continued through the respective half-plane. To check the sign, (10) sends z=-r to high z=r>0, whose proved selected characteristic has the PLUS square root. Replacing r by -z gives exactly (17). Characteristic simplicity continues it uniquely. This is the actual geometric branch established in the frame theorem, with its output disk normalization retained. It does not identify a contributing global period or the absolute action with the link volume.

Equation (16) controls the actual generic q-Hahn NUMERATORS. A full generic exceptional-kernel continuation still has its original boundary factors; it is not proved merely by replacing q in the root entire formula. Nor is this a joint complex-degree theorem.

Finally, each real integer forbidden separation h>a gives an IDENTICALLY ZERO original physical operator slice, including every generic-q normal. Deforming only its magnetic finite sums cannot turn that zero slice into a nonzero original period. A local geometric contribution in such a representation must cancel or fail selection there. Actual transport of the degree period, or another source-preserving grouping, remains necessary for a global nonzero contribution. No general long-twist volume theorem is inferred from this local chart result.
