What a finite algebraic source criterion can certify

29 October 2026. This audits the proposed A-polynomial/gluing-ideal route. It separates a finite certificate for the ACTUAL hyperbolic value from the remaining estimate on the original quantum period. It supplies a specific obstruction using the actual source’s value/normal pair, not a claim that knot A-polynomials never contain enough information.

1. A finite classical certificate is available

For a specified actual decorated triangulation/port diagram, the useful algebraic object is its FULL marked gluing ideal on the regular locus, saturated by every inverted shape/frame denominator. A peripheral elimination polynomial alone is weaker. The following are checkable finite data:

  • The actual oriented ideal manifold triangulation: face identifications, circular edge links and the intended torus vertex links, with its degree-one marked identification with the original exterior. Retain actual integer gluing/peripheral incidence and all multiplicities.
  • An algebraic complete solution with every positively ordered shape in the upper half-plane. Its sign inequalities can be certified by real-algebraic isolating intervals, rather than by a fitted shape. Require true complex edge products1, unit unwrapped edge winding, and both marked peripheral linear products1. Unit winding itself is a finite sign test: along ordered edge factors w_j with Im w_j>0, put p_0=1,p_j=product_(k<=j)w_k. Count indices for which Im p_(j-1)<0 and Im p_j>=0; the count must be1. Each factor turns through strictly less than pi, so this counts exactly the positive real-ray crossings, including a landing on that ray. Positive cusp link triangles and unit edge winding give a nonsingular similarity torus; peripheral linear products1 make it a complete flat torus.
  • Full edge rank and the marked peripheral symplectic pairing. The explicit ideal certificate proves these for the actual current cellulation; the general stationarity audit makes the required rank conditions explicit.
  • The marked gluing Jacobian is then nonzero, rather than an extra assumed analytic input. Positive polarization proves it after choosing independent edge and marked peripheral constraints: if (A B) has full row rank n and its n-dimensional row space is real Lagrangian in R^(2n), and tau_j=1/(z_j-1), then Im tau_j<0 and (A+B diag tau)v=0 would put (v,diag(tau)v) in its complexified Lagrangian kernel, contradicting sum Im(tau_j)|v_j|^2<0 unless v=0.
  • An actual localized inverse between the selected source critical scheme and this decorated group/gluing scheme, retaining the original meridian, preferred longitude, flags and central signs.

These certify an etale geometric germ and identify the selected regular critical representation there. They are stronger than finding the same peripheral polynomial or matching a cusp derivative.

At a positive full gluing solution, the coisotropic identity gives the volume derivative for EVERY affine angle tangent:

dV=(1/2)sum_i(Re V_i dIm U_i-Re U_i dIm V_i).

At a complete point this vanishes on the whole edge-angle domain. At a filled point, Re(a_i U_i+b_i V_i)=0 makes it vanish on the FULL fixed-slope-angle domain. Strict concavity makes that positive point the unique global maximum, including against boundary points. Thus these data identify V_source with hyperbolic volume once the marked source action is calibrated on the same branch.

The exponentiated filling equation is not enough: the actual primitive period must be +/-2pi i, not zero or a higher multiple. The general geometric completion proves the needed degree-one statement from the bounded near-complete framed representation and these small log periods. Its equivariant construction avoids a universal ideal-triangulation existence premise. When an angle presentation is used, its positive chart must be supplied separately; a topological triangulation alone is not that certificate.

2. Why A or a localized quantum module does not specify the actual source

There is an exact example INSIDE the existing original-source theory. Let F_N(Y) be the actual reduced meridian period and put

f_N(Y)=(1-Y)F_N(Y).

This is the normalized actual function used in the whole Newton source transform. Both are meromorphic sections of gauge-equivalent q-difference modules over the localized meridian field. If P=sum a_k(q,Y)T^k annihilates F, Tg(Y)=g(QY), then

P_f=(1-Y)P(1-Y)^(-1)

has coefficients a_k(q,Y)(1-Y)/(1-Q^kY) and annihilates f. Away from Y=1 its classical symbol is EXACTLY the same as P’s. Clearing and saturating apparent meridian factors does not restore the source row.

Nevertheless at the complete physical point

f_N(1)=0,
-partial_Y f_N(1)=F_N(1)=K_N.                       (1)

For the actual figure-eight, the already proved exact K_N=sum_r|F_r|^2 is positive at every level. Thus the full nonzero geometric growth is in the NORMAL coefficient in(1), while the value is identically zero. This is not two different knot invariants with the same A-polynomial. It proves the narrower necessary statement: the same localized peripheral curve/module does not tell one which actual value or normal functional computes the original invariant.

A criterion must therefore retain the integral pointed lattice and its specified normal co-source, not just a meromorphic gauge class or a saturated classical ideal. Regular-singular language on a punctured meridian disk does not by itself restore this lost lattice datum. The bounded-normal-factorial theorem gives estimates for prescribed jets of explicit kernels; it does not choose which jet the source uses.

There is a second independent loss. The peripheral differential fixes an action only up to a meridian-independent constant. Bloch lifts of the same boundary wedge can differ by a closed Bloch class with nonzero regulator. The Bloch-period audit, sections3–4, shows precisely why the actual fundamental-class lift and complete volume anchor must accompany the A-curve. They are not recovered from the peripheral equation’s Jacobian or degree.

3. Exact algebraic support does not yet give the whole quantum estimate

The actual coefficient normal-period theorem proves a nonzero local formal geometric normal block and a formally unit gauge to the calibrated geometric line. It explicitly leaves the two framing modes and their original relative pushforward unevaluated. The algebraic local ring and nonsingular Hessian are therefore real progress, but they do not prove that the original whole period retains that block.

This is visible in an exact endpoint calculation, rather than a generic appeal to Stokes phenomena. The sourced telescoping audit, section5, has for the actual relative interval

J_v(S_eta f)=exp(-v)J_v(f)
   +exp(-v)[integral_1^(1+eta)-integral_0^eta]
                                  exp(vt/eta)f(t)dt.

Already f=1 excludes the naive homogeneous shift-covariant replacement. The endpoint source must be transported or canceled exactly. A proof of interior ideal equality cannot delete this term by saturation.

Similarly, the saturated critical compactification bounds all TRUE full critical limits, but explicitly does not put every endpoint of the original relative cycle into that closure. Its exact Pachner counterexample shows cleared equations can create false boundary volume; full saturation removes that false component, not an arbitrary physical boundary current.

The algebraic-clock conversion example also warns against replacing whole transport by selected principal root actions: a polynomial offdiagonal perturbation changes the leading exponential rate despite nonzero distinct roots and bounded eigenframes. It is not a knot operator or a counterexample to a knot-specific theorem. It rules out deriving the needed propagation solely from that generic algebraic/spectral regularity.

Thus a DECORATED POINTED quantum module could in principle supply the missing source theorem, but it must supply more than its regular local ring: actual normal evaluation, relative boundary/shift covariance, and a quantitative nonzero geometric pushforward. No impossibility theorem for such a source-specific algebraic construction is asserted.

The general original-source filling transfer provides one concrete successful bridge from base data: an exact even complex color packet, global complementary source gap, marked actual representation and calibrated action imply full nonzero long-filling asymptotics. Those analytic base estimates have been proved for the current explicit source class, not for every ideal satisfying the classical tests in section1. This distinction keeps the active broader equality objective from being replaced by a principal-curve criterion.
