Real filling powers and a uniform bound for the outer shared indices

Research proof, 4 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

The angle bound for a positive internal pair was previously proved only at integer powers. This matters when comparing two actions by differentiating with respect to a filling power. We first fill that gap for the geometric problem. We then use the actual physical normal-defect estimate to bound the outer shared indices in the finite quantum sum. The estimate holds for every fixed positive integer pair of powers at least three, through both level parities.

The two outside endpoints remain complete throughout. Nothing here asserts a nonzero coefficient for the remaining middle integral.

1. The continuous geometric family

Give two adjacent selected vertices real powers n,p≥3n,p\ge3, unit charges, and one complete outside endpoint at each vertex. The real-power variational theorem gives the unique positive interior solution. Its angles depend smoothly on the powers: the minimum is interior and its constrained Hessian is positive definite. Write one angle as t=a−iHt=a-iH. The selected branches have 0<a<π0<a<\pi, H>0H>0, and the original period equation is nt+2i{r0(t)+r1(t,u)}=2π,r0(t)=asinh⁡tan⁡(t/2),r1(t,u)=asinh⁡{tan⁡(t/2)cos⁡(u/2)}.(1) nt+2i\{r_0(t)+r_1(t,u)\}=2\pi, \quad r_0(t)=\operatorname{asinh}\tan(t/2),\quad r_1(t,u)=\operatorname{asinh}\{\tan(t/2)\cos(u/2)\}. \qquad\text{(1)} Each return has positive real part and imaginary part between −π/2-\pi/2 and π/2\pi/2. 5. Uniform geometry when both powers are at least seven
