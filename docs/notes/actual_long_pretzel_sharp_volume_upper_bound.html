The hyperbolic-volume upper bound for the full original long-pretzel invariant

Subsequent equality, 29 October. The geometric completion proof now proves rho_p is the holonomy of a complete positively oriented metric on the actual filled knot. The upper inequality below is therefore an equality on the SAME sufficiently long fixed signed class. See the Casson–Rivin equality proof and full completion audit. This note retains the intermediate upper-bound argument as a dependency.

29 October 2026. This assembles the whole-source asymptotic, the literal representation/peripheral lift, and the marked regulator comparison. The upper bound uses the ACTUAL filled hyperbolic volume, with coefficient one. The equality required by the full volume conjecture is distinguished explicitly below.

1. Statement and scope

For every fixed odd integer s>=25 there is an integer p_s>0 with the following property. Let p_1,…,p_s be independently chosen fixed signed integers satisfying |p_i|>=p_s, and let K be the original odd pretzel knot with boxes R^(-(2p_i+1)). Assume its complement is hyperbolic, as in the standing project hypothesis. Then the ORIGINAL normalized Kashaev invariant satisfies

lim_(N->infinity) (2pi/N)log|<K>_N|
    =Vol(rho_p) <= Vol(S^3 minus K).                  (1)

Here rho_p is an explicitly constructed boundary-parabolic PSL2(C) representation of the ACTUAL filled knot group. The limit exists, and its leading source coefficient is nonzero. Both level parities and all composite levels are included. The fixed slopes need not have comparable sizes or the same signs.

The class has no upper bound on its number of twist regions, and each sufficiently long twist parameter varies independently. It is not a fixed-width twist family or a finite list of tested knots. No assertion for small widths or short slopes is inferred from (1).

The hyperbolicity assumption is explicit. The proof below does not use an unproved claim that every displayed diagram is hyperbolic, or require a faithfulness theorem for rho_p in order to establish the upper bound.

2. The full original source supplies a nonzero limit

The whole-source survival theorem proves, including the ENTIRE original color cube,

Kraw_N=N^(3/2) exp[-i s pi(N-1)/4]
        kappa^(-sum p_i) D_s(p) exp[N A_s(p)]
                               (1+O_(s,p)(1/N)),
D_s(p)!=0.                                          (2)

Its global bound includes all ordinary color bands, the even middle color, and every Casimir normal derivative. Its coherent filling estimate retains the zero-charge cancellation, all nonzero charges, paired endpoint currents and the exact original half-color weights. The unit charges add rather than being assigned an assumed cycle coefficient. All these statements are quantitative source estimates, not consequences inferred from classical stationary equations.

The actual framing/cut normalization gives |<K>_N|=|Kraw_N|. The optional full one-cut modified convention contributes the kept factor i^(N+3)/N, with the same exponential rate. It follows that the left side of (1) exists and equals 2pi Re A_s(p).

3. Its selected point is an actual filled-knot representation

The source-selected mixed lift derives the regular internal coordinates from the literal finite recurrence and verifies their stationary rational identities. The full port and geometric-anchor theorem then constructs actual parabolic main meridians and auxiliary matrices C_i,P_i, with

[C_i,P_i]=1,
M_i=H_i^(-1/2), lambda_i=-L_i^(-1)

on the same eigenflag. It verifies every original cap in the actual right-to-left multiplication order. The common w and the outer equation product_i U_i=1 close the full original diagram. This is a representation of that diagram’s group, not a trace variety introduced independently of the source.

The preferred-longitude certificate proves that P_i is the disk-framed longitude, with no unrecorded meridian power. The minus in lambda_i is forced by the original auxiliary pivot. With continuous z_i=Log H_i and ell_i=Log L_i, the actual unit-charge filling equation is

z_i+2p_i ell_i=2pi i.

It gives C_i P_i(p_i)=(-1)(p_i-1)I, as a MATRIX identity on the regular nearby branch. Thus it kills the primitive filled slope in PSL2 and defines rho_p on the actual knot group. A particular SL2 lift may require a mod-two character change; no SL2 descent is needed for the PSL2 representation-volume bound. The main meridian remains noncentral parabolic, so its commuting peripheral image is boundary-parabolic.

At the complete augmented point the same representation agrees with every white and shaded face pairing of the four-antiprism geometric construction. The absolute auxiliary translations also match the primitive disk longitude and positive diagonal meridian. The reversed cap traversal is accounted for by an explicit orientation-preserving holomorphic isometry, not by taking a mirror. This supplies the oriented geometric anchor V_s.

4. The source action is the absolute regulator of rho_p

The marked regulator comparison keeps the preferred SL sign before centering. If G is the source action with G_(z_i)=ell_i, put

u_i=-z_i/2, v_i=pi i-ell_i,
Gbar=G-pi i sum_i z_i.

Then dGbar=2 sum v_i du_i, and the marked regulator is

Dsrc=ImGbar-2 sum Reu_i Imv_i
    =ImG-sum Rez_i Imell_i.                          (3)

The actual fundamental Bloch boundary is -2 sum M_i wedge lambda_i. Its elementary differential agrees with (3), and the complete geometric anchor fixes their SAME absolute constant V_s. Thus Dsrc is the regulator of the representation constructed above throughout the selected connected local branch.

The ORIGINAL Gaussian filling action is

W=G+sum_i (z_i-2pi i)^2/(4p_i).

At its critical point, Re z_i=-2p_i Re ell_i. Consequently

2pi Re A_s(p)=ImW
   =ImG+2 sum_i p_i Reell_i Imell_i
   =Dsrc.                                           (4)

No peripheral or endpoint correction is omitted in (4): the quadratic is precisely the one in the source-preserving filling formula, and the spin terms cancel only in their complete paired regulator expression.

Finally compare the augmented and filled fundamental chains using one finite compatible triangulation of the truncated core and the attached solid tori. The proved axis-cusp homotopy first replaces the OLD filled-boundary data by an axis-valued map without changing the augmented regulator. Assign those boundary vertices and every new solid-torus vertex equivariantly to that SAME invariant axis. The killed primitive slope makes the assignment descend through the solid-torus fundamental group. Every added tetrahedron is collinear and has zero volume. The core plus solid-torus chain therefore computes the filled boundary-parabolic representation volume with no new constant. This proves

2pi Re A_s(p)=Vol(rho_p).                            (5)

The boundary replacement uses the explicit O(Y^(-2)) homotopy estimate in the general peripheral volume proof, sections1–2. Merely assigning NEW vertices an axis flag while leaving arbitrary old boundary data would not justify (5).

5. Apply the independent sharp representation-volume estimate

For an oriented complete finite-volume hyperbolic manifold X and a boundary-parabolic representation rho, the independently proved representation-volume theorem gives

|Vol(rho)|<=Vol(X).                                 (6)

Its proof averages finite regular tetrahedra, cancels all four face-homotopy errors by Haar invariance, and lets their volumes increase to the maximal ideal-tetrahedron volume. It does not assume a quantum volume conjecture or faithfulness of rho. Apply it to the actual hyperbolic filled complement and rho_p. Equations (2),(5),(6) prove (1).

Thus the requested sharp full-original-source UPPER-BOUND milestone holds for the stated broad class. Equality in (1), and hence the full volume conjecture for that class, additionally requires proving that rho_p is the positive geometric Dehn-filling representation (or an equivalent equality argument). This theorem does not count that remaining geometric completion as already proved. It also makes no assertion for all hyperbolic or all alternating knots.
