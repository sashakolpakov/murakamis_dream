The actual reduced braid network and its resolved critical scheme

Private written proof, 18 November 2026.

The exact constant-prefix quotient has a finite Euler network. Its regular exponentiated generating correspondence is the Hamiltonian reduction of the original braid correspondence at total lowering coordinate zero. The central first head on that level retains its eigenflag. Discarding that flag would give a different reduction.

For a regular semisimple total product, a fixed point of the reduced correspondence lifts to an actual braid fixed point: there is no additional upper-unipotent closing seam. Under the positive complete gluing certificate specified below, the geometric fixed germ, after the common momentum quotient, is a reduced point. For complete discrete faithful holonomy, the needed resolved charts exist after the source-preserving presentation changes in R.

These are statements about the actual finite coefficients and their principal rational correspondence. A uniform Fourier integral description on the first-head vacuum, a nonvanishing local amplitude there, and an original-cycle coefficient are not asserted. The three complete proof directs are R, M and H, pinned at the end.

5. Fixed schemes and the single common momentum

Assume KΣ2≠1K_\Sigma^2\ne1 on this level. A reduced fixed point lifts to an equation Rβ(z)=U(s)⋅z.(28) R_\beta(z)=U(s)\cdot z . \qquad\text{(28)} The original braid preserves the exact ordered total EE and KK. Equations (20)–(22) consequently imply s(KΣ−1−KΣ)=0.(29) s(K_\Sigma^{-1}-K_\Sigma)=0. \qquad\text{(29)} The second factor is a unit, so s=0s=0. Conversely any original fixed point on this level gives a reduced fixed point. The same calculation works over every local Artin algebra. Thus the reduced fixed scheme is precisely the original fixed scheme on FΣ=0F_\Sigma=0, divided by the upper-unipotent action. It has no additional closing seam.

For clarity, passing to independent occupation variables removes one further, specified common momentum. Regard an SrS_r factor as a vertex with rr inputs and rr outputs. Its positions have the sole linear conservation equation; inputs and r−1r-1 transfers give its 2r−12r-1 coordinates. Equations (9) recover all its momentum ratios. The physical crossings have the same property by M; a DD vertex has one incoming and one outgoing wire and (12).

The resulting closed network is connected for a braid whose closure is a knot. For m>2m>2, the word must use R1R_1 or its inverse, since otherwise its first strand closes as a separate component. Its Sm−1S_{m-1} vertex joins all remaining heads. For m=2m=2 there is one remaining wire. Identity pieces may be omitted. The conservation matrix LL of this network therefore has rank one less than the number of vertices.

On the vector space of conserved wire positions, the same linear argument as H gives 0→ℂ→ℂV→LtℂE→(ker⁡L)*→0.(30) 0\longrightarrow\mathbb C\longrightarrow\mathbb C^{V} \mathop{\longrightarrow}^{L^t}\mathbb C^{E} \longrightarrow(\ker L)^*\longrightarrow0. \qquad\text{(30)} The differential of the sum of the actual local primitives is the list of momentum mismatches restricted to ker⁡L\ker L. Vertex constants can remove that list exactly when the derivative vanishes; their only ambiguity is one global constant. A spanning tree supplies a fixed right inverse, so this is an equality of local schemes, not just tangents. The Fourier integer covector is extended from chord edges and assigned to incident local primitives, as in H. This retains its additive derivative while changing no exponentiated graph. Original pivotal turns are treated in the same fashion.

Thus, on a regular logarithmic coefficient chart, the actual occupation gradient scheme is the reduced fixed scheme divided by the single common momentum pj↦λpjp_j\mapsto\lambda p_j. 6. The geometric resolved chart and its local rigidity

R permits at most three source-preserving positive stabilizations and an actual braid conjugation so that the complete total product PP is regular semisimple. Suppose first the holonomy is complete, discrete and faithful. In a torsion-free discrete group a nonparabolic noncentral semisimple element is loxodromic: a finite-order elliptic would be torsion, and the powers of an infinite-order elliptic accumulate at the identity. A loxodromic element cannot share a fixed point with a nontrivial parabolic element. In coordinates putting that point at infinity, conjugating the nontrivial translation by appropriate powers of the loxodromic multiplier produces distinct elements converging to the identity. This contradicts discreteness.

Choose an eigenline vv of PP and the selected eigenline ww of the first meridian. They are distinct. In the basis (v,w)(v,w), the total PP is upper triangular and the first meridian has the form (19), with Fr≠0F_{\mathrm{ r}}\ne0. Every stage prefix PjP_j has nonzero upper-left entry. Otherwise PjvP_jv would be proportional to ww, making vv the fixed point of a conjugate of the first parabolic meridian, which was just excluded. Likewise a physical fj=0f_j=0 would make vv an eigenline of Pj−1−1HjPj−1P_{j-1}^{-1}H_jP_{j-1}, again a conjugate meridian. This proof applies to every prefix in the finite braid and to each reselected first-meridian flag used in (19).

At the section (19), the full prefixes satisfy Pjfull=h0Pj−1rdiag⁡(x,1).(31) P_j^{\mathrm{ full}} =h_0P_{j-1}^{\mathrm{ r}}\operatorname{diag}(x,1). \qquad\text{(31)} In particular their (1,1)(1,1) entries are xx times those of the remaining prefixes. The new remaining physical heads are exactly the old heads numbered 2,…,m2,\ldots,m on this section. All their KK’s and ff’s are therefore units at every quotient stage. For an Sm−1S_{m-1} generator the input and output KK’s in (11) are units. Starting at Tr+1=1T_{r+1}=1 shows successively that all its TjT_j’s are units. Its recipients have unit fjf_j. The donor may have pj=0,Aj=1p_j=0,A_j=1; its chart is then (16), with tj=−fjt_j=-f_j retained. This proves existence of a regular resolved correspondence chart at the geometric point.

It does not prove that every new three-factorial shape in (8) avoids one. In particular repeated parabolic flags can force zero transfers. Such points remain resolved correspondence points, not automatically ordinary nonzero-factorial amplitude charts.

Now additionally supply the actual exterior’s positive complete gluing certificate in H. Its exact selected meridian-eigenvalue theorem makes the unframed geometric representation germ a reduced point. Before quotienting frames, the germ is therefore a smooth conjugation orbit of dimension three. Its projective stabilizer is trivial: a lift of a projective centralizer must conjugate each lifted meridian to itself or its negative; the nonzero parabolic trace excludes the negative. It then centralizes all generators and is scalar by irreducibility, as in H. The exact eigenflag is retained, as in (16), including over Artin rings.

The equation P21=0P_{21}=0, equivalently FΣ=0F_\Sigma=0, is transverse to that orbit when PP is regular semisimple: infinitesimal lower-unipotent conjugation changes P21P_{21} by a nonzero multiple of KΣ−KΣ−1K_\Sigma-K_\Sigma^{-1}. It leaves a smooth two-dimensional germ. Quotienting the free upper unipotent action (20) leaves the diagonal framing direction, and the common momentum in (30) is exactly that diagonal conjugation. This action is free locally: if every remaining pjp_j were zero, their unit fjf_j would give every Aj=1A_j=1 and hence KΣ2=1K_\Sigma^2=1, contrary to the stated unit condition. Consequently the geometric resolved reduced fixed germ modulo the common momentum is a reduced point, on each choice of the eigenline vv. There are two discrete eigenline choices, not an additional continuous gauge.

Whenever an actual regular logarithmic generating chart represents this point, (30) identifies its gradient scheme with that reduced point. Its Hessian is therefore invertible: the kernel is the Zariski tangent of the gradient scheme. This last holomorphic-phase statement requires that chart; the resolved rational result does not manufacture it at singular factorial/projector faces.

7. Source and action scope

All finite support inequalities in (6), the actual negative coefficients, the prescribed pivots, framing scalars and meridian derivatives remain in (2)–(3). The proof does not differentiate the root reduction as an off-root quantum-normal identity. Such normals retain the prefix carry from R’s complete sources.

The original scalar source is still the specified Cartan-weighted first-meridian-jet functional of the finite reduced network. The geometric correspondence does not estimate that matrix or its spectral radii. In particular a physical-basis reduced matrix norm is not identified with representation volume.

Equation (8) records the literal principal primitive, its three-factorial part, and the inverse’s marked quadratic term. Equality of the rational canonical maps proves no equality of marked primitive constants. No reduced regulator/volume calibration is claimed here. Nor does a reduced isolated geometric germ determine a nonzero local amplitude, a thimble coefficient, or the original relative period.
