Exact central black, ordinary-white and marked contractions

29 October 2026. At the ODD simple Steinberg triple the actual ordinary white covector is a genuine pivotal dual of the black tensor, with an explicit scalar. It is nonzero, yet its closed magnetic pairing with black is ZERO. The original marked closure instead gives an explicit positive scalar after all magnetic indices are contracted. This is a coherent source calculation, not a norm-square replacement of the vertex. It explains why the full-black CD norm obstruction does not determine the growth of a sewn scalar.

Only this odd central sector and the specified actual two-vertex contraction are evaluated. No paired/even analogue, arbitrary graph recoupling estimate or global volume statement is inferred.

1. Original duality fixes the ordinary white tensor

Use N=2m+1, d=2m and the exact central W,pi,Theta,A_N of the actual odd source. Thus pi=W^dagger P, pi W=I, and the unscaled black tensor is

U=pi^tensor3 J_source=s Theta,
s=N^2 q^(-m^2) A_N,
Theta=(W tensor I)p.                              (1)

The ORIGINAL physical cup/cap matrices are

p_(a,b)=N x^(-b)delta_(a+b,d),
ell_(a,b)=x^a/N delta_(a+b,d), p ell=ell p=I.

For a map T:V->V tensor V its genuine right dual, after identifying the dual objects by these SAME maps, is

D(T)=p^T T^T(ell^T tensor ell^T)P_ord.             (2)

P_ord reverses abstract dual tensor order; it is not an inserted R. For an invariant column v in V^tensor3 the right-dual covector is

D(v)=v^T(ell^T)^tensor3 Rev_3.                    (3)

These are bilinear pivotal duals. Complex conjugation is not part of D. Both formulas follow by the ordinary evaluation index sums, with every N factor retained.

The actual simple-channel Hom space is one-dimensional, so D(W) is a scalar multiple of pi. Evaluate row0,column(m,0) in(2), using W_d(d,m)=q(m2). It gives EXACTLY

D(W)=gamma pi, gamma=q^(3m^2)/N.                  (4)

In particular gamma is nonzero. Dualizing pi W=I and using the same one-dimensional Hom space gives D(pi)=gamma^(-1)W. The nested matching (16),(23),(45) is preserved by reversal, and its right dual is precisely the ORIGINAL three ordinary caps ell^S. Therefore, with Lambda=ell^S W^tensor3,

D(U)=gamma^(-3)Lambda,
Lambda=gamma^3 s D(Theta).                        (5)

Both U and Lambda are nonzero at every odd N, since A_N is the proved nonzero quartic scalar. The original ordinary white packet V in the unscaled W coordinates satisfies conjugate(V)=N^6 Lambda. This proves its precise dual relation; it does not assume V=U or V=Vm.

2. The ordinary closed pairing is zero, despite nonzero vertices

Write Theta_c and lambda_c for the third magnetic slices of Theta and D(Theta). Let

T_(s,t)=sum_(a,b) Theta_(a,b,s)D(Theta)_(a,b,t).

Integer H=0 support makes T diagonal. The ordinary E equations for the invariant column and covector imply, for 1<=r<=d,

k_r lambda_r E_12=-e_r lambda_(r-1),
k_(r-1) E_12 Theta_(r-1)=-e_r Theta_r.

Here k_r=xQ^(-r) and every e_r is nonzero. Pairing these identities gives T_rr/T_(r-1,r-1)=k_(r-1)/k_r whenever interpreted as the cleared equality. Hence K T is scalar, with NO division by T_rr.

Its scalar can also be computed finitely. The top-edge Clebsch identity is

W_(d-a)(d,m-a)=q^(m^2)h_a, 0<=a<=m.              (6)

To prove it, start at W_d(d,m)=q(m2), apply the actual E relation at first physical color d, and use factorial reflection. The resulting factor is Q^(ma)F_m F_(d-a)/(F_(m-a)F_d); its root phase converts it exactly to h_a. There is no new scalar connection constant.

Equations(2),(4),(6) give

T_dd=gamma x^(-d)q^(-m^2) sum_a x^a beta(m,a)^2
     =gamma x.

The last sum is the finite Vandermonde sum N/F_m^2. Since k_d=x^-1,

K T=gamma I,
D(Theta)Theta=Tr T=gamma Tr(K^-1)=0.              (7)

Consequently Lambda U=0 exactly. This is the ordinary q-dimension zero in its actual magnetic contraction, not a claim that either vertex vanishes. Replacing it by a positive Euclidean contraction would destroy this cancellation.

The open tangle obtained by cutting that third edge is K T in these left/right duality conventions. The K factor is the recorded pivotal clock from the same snakes; (7) also verifies directly that the open map is a Hopf endomorphism. Its normalized marked closure is gamma, since the original marked unknot has value one.

3. Transfer the physical mark through the actual simple projector

Let E_St=W pi be the FULL isolated -2 Casimir projector. The actual central circle is L_St=N E_St. Its appropriately oriented ordinary partial traces are

sum_b K_b (E_St)_(a b;c b)=delta_(a,c),
sum_a K_a^(-1)(E_St)_(a b;a e)=delta_(b,e).         (8)

Here is a root-safe proof, rather than a guessed positive resolution. Lift only this projector to the ordinary generic highest-weight-d pair. Its central summand is again the highest-weight-d module. The Casimir projector is regular at the odd root: its eigenvalue -2 collides with no other pair eigenvalue, so every projector denominator is a unit there. Its typed partial trace is a Hopf endomorphism of V_N, hence scalar. Taking the generic full pivotal trace gives

scalar [N]_q=[N]_q,

so the scalar is one away from the root and remains one by regular specialization. This proves(8) with the original root duality. The same argument shows equality of the root scalar obtained by cutting a physical V_N component or its embedded central V_N edge: the generic quantum dimensions are IDENTICAL, and all relevant coupons have a regular lift. No arbitrary modified-trace axiom is assumed.

At odd root the usual generic pivotal maps specialize to the recorded ones, since K^N=I on V_N and K^(1-N)=K. Root marked closure of an open scalar t I is exactly t, by the direct identity m p=1 with m_(a,b)=xb/N2. This is the actual unrestricted marked source, not a replacement by an ordinary cap.

This generic lift proves a FIXED-ROOT identity only. It does not identify the original moving-q normal germ of the complete circle with a value-only projector or authorize differentiation of it. Other Casimir channels and their original normals remain separate.

4. Exact marked two-vertex central scalar

Use the actual two-vertex, three-edge source of the branching note, with one original marked cap and all three edge circles projected to their odd simple Steinberg summand. Contract EVERY magnetic index before estimating. The open effective scalar of U and Lambda is, from(1),(5),(7),

gamma^4 s^2=q^(10m^2)A_N^2=|A_N|^2.              (9)

Indeed A_N=q1S_N with S_N positive, and the remaining phase is q2=q^(4mN)=1. Each of the three actual central circles contributes N, while(8) transports the original marked cut without an extra dimension. Thus the ORIGINAL raw marked central contribution is EXACTLY

Z_St=N^3 |A_N|^2
     =(1/16)exp(2Nj0)[1+O(1/N)].                (10)

For edge twists R^(e_i)L_St multiply(10) by the literal full-Cartan unit phase omega_m^(e_1+e_2+e_3). Any recorded external line chi_N stays outside. Equation(10) is not the full graph source with the other joint channels removed by fiat: it is the explicitly specified central sector of an original source decomposition.

This is a concrete coherent contraction that removes the extra magnetic growth of the black CD norm. It does not make the original marked WHITE tensor equal to the black tensor, or furnish a sharp norm bound for arbitrary six-port sewing. The paired/even analogue still needs its own normal-current calculation. A complete graph also retains all other channels, phases and possible cancellations.
