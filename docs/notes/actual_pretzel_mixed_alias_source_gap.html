A uniform original-source alias gap near complete pretzel colors

29 October 2026. For every fixed odd number of boxes s≥5s\geq5, the high-band terms in the exact cusp-color split are exponentially smaller than the complete augmented geometric period on a sufficiently small, fixed cusp-color corridor. This is a bound on the original source, including all Casimir normal coefficients. The complete-color circle estimate is used coherently before passing to its Hermite norm.

The assertion is an error bound relative to the complete-color geometric rate. To obtain a mixed-color asymptotic one must still prove the selected core asymptotic, with a comparable rate, throughout that corridor. No fixed-filling volume theorem follows here. For s=3s=3 the proof below has zero limiting margin; a separate cancellation is needed.

Use d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, Q=q2Q=q^2, x=qd=−q−1x=q^d=-q^{-1}, and M=⌊d/2⌋M=\lfloor d/2\rfloor. All boxes, turns and the source Φ2\Phi _2 are exactly those in the original complete-base polynomial theorem. The exact two-band split is

zN,r=cN,r+RPN,r,0≤r≤M,∥RPN,r∥H≤56r(r>0), z_{N,r}=c_{N,r}+R P_{N,r},\qquad 0\leq r\leq M, \qquad \|RP_{N,r}\|_{\mathrm H}\leq56^r\quad(r>0), \tag{1}

where r=N−jr=N-j is the actual auxiliary-color deficit and the remainder is zero for r=0r=0. The faithful norm and all source normalizations are retained from the two-band theorem.

1. A bounded dual Casimir on a coherent circle

Let ℛ\mathscr R be the literal quarter-turn, and define 𝒟=ℛ∘(Ω⋅)∘ℛ−1\mathcal D=\mathscr R\circ(\Omega\cdot)\circ\mathscr R^{-1} on the physical pair commutant. In the physical continuant basis,

𝒟fv=cvfv+bvfv−1,cv=q2v+1+q−2v−1,bv=Qv+Q−v−2. \boxed{\quad \mathcal D f_v=c_v f_v+b_v f_{v-1},\qquad c_v=q^{2v+1}+q^{-2v-1},\quad b_v=Q^v+Q^{-v}-2.\quad} \tag{2}

Here b0=0b_0=0. This operator preserves polynomial degree, including in the full degree-dd algebra, so it preserves the low core.

For completeness, (2) follows from an actual Hopf Ward identity. For a pair intertwiner BB, write βv=Bd,v;d,v\beta_v=B_{d,v;d,v}. The original coproduct of FF gives

Bd−1,v+1;d,v=x−1Qd1−Qv+11−Qd(βv+1−βv). B_{d-1,v+1;d,v}= x^{-1}Q^d\frac{1-Q^{v+1}}{1-Q^d} (\beta_{v+1}-\beta_v). \tag{3}

The last row of the physical Ω\Omega block of total degree d+vd+v has off-diagonal Q−1(1−Q)(1−Q−v−1)Q^{-1}(1-Q)(1-Q^{-v-1}) and diagonal 2q−1+(q−q−1)Q−v−12q^{-1}+(q-q^{-1})Q^{-v-1}. Inserting (3) gives

(ΩB)d,v;d,v=cvβv+q−1(1−Qv+1)(1−Q−v−1)βv+1. (\Omega B)_{d,v;d,v} =c_v\beta_v+ q^{-1}(1-Q^{v+1})(1-Q^{-v-1})\beta_{v+1}.

The first row of the rotated box is xv−dβvx^{v-d}\beta_v; its adjacent phase converts the second coefficient to bv+1b_{v+1}. The cyclic first row of the middle Casimir block proves (2) for the entire commutant, not just its semisimple quotient.

In the symmetric Laurent variable z=w+w−1z=w+w^{-1}, (2) is the explicit rational difference operator

(𝒟F)(w)=a(w)F(Qw)+a(w−1)F(Q−1w)+c(w)F(w), (\mathcal D F)(w)=a(w)F(Qw)+a(w^{-1})F(Q^{-1}w)+c(w)F(w), \tag{4} a(w)=(qw−1)3q(w2−1)(qw+1),a(w−1)=(w−q)3q(w+q)(w2−1),c(w)=8qw(w+q)(qw+1). a(w)=\frac{(qw-1)^3}{q(w^2-1)(qw+1)},\qquad a(w^{-1})=\frac{(w-q)^3}{q(w+q)(w^2-1)},\qquad c(w)=\frac{8qw}{(w+q)(qw+1)}.

Here F(w)F(w) means f(w+w−1)f(w+w^{-1}). This formula is exact at the specified root and retains every normal coefficient. Its apparent poles are removable on symmetric Laurent polynomials.

An all-degree algebra check of (4) is short. Put Pr(w)=(qw;Q)r(q/w;Q)rP_r(w)=(qw;Q)_r(q/w;Q)_r. The two shifted product ratios give

𝒟Pr=crPr−(Qr−1)4qQrPr−1. \mathcal D P_r=c_rP_r- \frac{(Q^r-1)^4}{qQ^r}P_{r-1}. \tag{5}

Expand the physical continuant by its finite identity

fv=xv∑r=0v(Q−v;Q)rQrFr3Pr. f_v=x^v\sum_{r=0}^v \frac{(Q^{-v};Q)_rQ^r}{F_r^3}P_r.

The ratios of adjacent coefficients are (1−Qr−v)Q/(1−Qr+1)3(1-Q^{r-v})Q/(1-Q^{r+1})^3 and x−1(1−Qr−v)/(1−Q−v)x^{-1}(1-Q^{r-v})/(1-Q^{-v}). Substitution in (5) proves (2) coefficient by coefficient. All denominators FrF_r here have r<Nr<N; no root-zero division or omitted self-pair is used.

For every fixed radius 𝜚>1\varrho>1, let ∥f∥𝜚=max⁡|w|=𝜚|f(w+w−1)|\|f\|_{\varrho}=\max_{|w|=\varrho}|f(w+w^{-1})|. Since |Q|=1|Q|=1, the shifts preserve that circle, and (4) gives

∥𝒟f∥𝜚≤K𝜚∥f∥𝜚,K𝜚=2+16𝜚(𝜚−1)2. \boxed{\quad \|\mathcal D f\|_{\varrho} \leq K_{\varrho}\|f\|_{\varrho},\qquad K_{\varrho}=2+\frac{16\varrho}{(\varrho-1)^2}.\quad} \tag{6}

Indeed each shifted coefficient is at most (𝜚+1)2/(𝜚−1)2(\varrho+1)^2/(\varrho-1)^2, and the central one at most 8𝜚/(𝜚−1)28\varrho/(\varrho-1)^2. The constant is independent of NN, polynomial degree and color deficit.

2. All low cores are generated from the complete core

Let 𝖳r(z)=2cos⁡(rarccos⁡(z/2))\mathsf T_r(z)=2\cos(r\arccos(z/2)), including 𝖳0=2\mathsf T_0=2. The elementary sine addition identity, reduced modulo the FULL 𝒫N=𝖳N+2\mathcal P_N=\mathsf T_N+2, gives

LN−r=(−1)NLr+(−1)r2𝖳r(Ω)LN. L_{N-r}=(-1)^N L_r+ \frac{(-1)^r}{2}\mathsf T_r(\Omega)L_N. \tag{7}

Multiply by the literal half twist R−1R^{-1}, then rotate. For 1≤r≤M1\leq r\leq M, the rotated R−1LrR^{-1}L_r has only the high continuant band v≥N−r>Mv\geq N-r>M; this is the same finite Gaussian support computation used in the two-band theorem. The second term of (7) has degree at most MM after rotation, by (2). Consequently the low-degree projection is exactly

cN,r=(−1)r2𝖳r(𝒟)zN. \boxed{\quad c_{N,r}=\frac{(-1)^r}{2} \mathsf T_r(\mathcal D)z_N.\quad} \tag{8}

The Chebyshev recurrence and (6) imply, also for r=0r=0,

∥cN,r∥𝜚≤(K𝜚+1)r∥zN∥𝜚. \|c_{N,r}\|_{\varrho} \leq (K_{\varrho}+1)^r\|z_N\|_{\varrho}. \tag{9}

This keeps the coherent complete-base polynomial. Bounding its continuant summands separately is unnecessary.

The interval [−2,2][-2,2] lies inside the ellipse w+w−1w+w^{-1}, |w|=𝜚|w|=\varrho. By the maximum principle and the ordinary degree-MM Markov inequality, applied to a real part after a unit phase rotation at each derivative evaluation,

∥cN,r∥H≤(1+M2/N2)∥cN,r∥𝜚≤54(K𝜚+1)r∥zN∥𝜚. \|c_{N,r}\|_{\mathrm H} \leq(1+M^2/N^2)\|c_{N,r}\|_{\varrho} \leq\frac54(K_{\varrho}+1)^r\|z_N\|_{\varrho}. \tag{10}

Thus the passage to the faithful normal-source norm costs only a constant. It does not drop the source derivative.

3. A strict source alias gap for every odd s≥5s\geq5

The proved coherent-circle theorem in the complete-base asymptotic gives, for each fixed η>0\eta>0,

limsupN→∞1Nlog⁡∥zN∥eη≤j(η),j(0)=v8π,j′(η)=1πarctan⁡sinh⁡(η/2). \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac1N \log\|z_N\|_{e^\eta}\leq j(\eta),\qquad j(0)=\frac{v_8}{\pi},\qquad j'(\eta)=\frac1\pi\arctan\sinh(\eta/2). \tag{11}

In particular j(η)−j(0)=O(η2)j(\eta)-j(0)=O(\eta^2). Uniformly over every integer color choice 0≤ri≤δN0\leq r_i\leq\delta N, a source term with a nonempty alias set SS, |S|=m|S|=m, has upper rate

1Nlog⁡|Φ2(∏i∈SRPN,ri∏i∉ScN,ri)|≤(s−m)j(η)+δ[(s−m)log⁡(Keη+1)+mlog⁡56]+o(1). \frac1N\log\left|\Phi_2\left( \prod_{i\in S}RP_{N,r_i} \prod_{i\notin S}c_{N,r_i}\right)\right| \leq (s-m)j(\eta) +\delta\big[(s-m)\log(K_{e^\eta}+1)+m\log56\big]+o(1). \tag{12}

The o(1)o(1) is uniform in these colors. This is (1), (10), submultiplicativity of the faithful norm, and the exact estimate |Φ2(f)|≤3N3∥f∥H|\Phi_2(f)|\leq3N^3\|f\|_{\mathrm H}. The finite sum of at most 2s−12^s-1 such terms has the same upper rate. All original circle, half-twist and source phases remain in the exact terms being bounded.

Let

As=Vs2π=4sπ[Λ(π4+π2s)+Λ(π4−π2s)]. A_s=\frac{V_s}{2\pi} =\frac{4s}{\pi} \left[\Lambda\left(\frac\pi4+\frac\pi{2s}\right) +\Lambda\left(\frac\pi4-\frac\pi{2s}\right)\right].

The complete-base theorem proves the original source has rate AsA_s. Its margin over the worst alias envelope at δ=0\delta=0 is

gs=As−(s−1)j(0)>0(s≥5). g_s=A_s-(s-1)j(0)>0\qquad(s\geq5). \tag{13}

Here is an elementary uniform proof. With A(α)=Λ(π/4+α/2)+Λ(π/4−α/2)A(\alpha)=\Lambda(\pi/4+\alpha/2)+ \Lambda(\pi/4-\alpha/2), A(0)=GA(0)=G (Catalan’s constant), A′(0)=0A'(0)=0, and A″(α)=−1/(2cos⁡α)A''(\alpha)=-1/(2\cos\alpha). Hence

gs≥4Gπ−πscos⁡(π/s)>0(s≥5). g_s\geq\frac{4G}{\pi}- \frac{\pi}{s\cos(\pi/s)}>0 \qquad(s\geq5).

Indeed the second summand is less than 1 for s≥5s\geq5, while 4G/π>14G/\pi>1, for example from G>1−1/9G>1-1/9.

Fix η>0\eta>0 with (s−1)(j(η)−j(0))<gs/4(s-1)(j(\eta)-j(0))<g_s/4, and then fix δ>0\delta>0 so that sδ[log⁡(Keη+1)+log⁡56]<gs/4s\delta[\log(K_{e^\eta}+1)+\log56]<g_s/4, and also δ<1/4\delta<1/4. Equations (12)–(13) prove the genuine uniform original-source bound

|Φ2(∏izN,ri−∏icN,ri)|≤exp⁡{N(As−gs/2+o(1))},0≤ri≤δN,s≥5 odd. \boxed{\quad \left|\Phi_2\left(\prod_i z_{N,r_i} -\prod_i c_{N,r_i}\right)\right| \leq \exp\{N(A_s-g_s/2+o(1))\}, \quad 0\leq r_i\leq\delta N, \quad s\geq5\text{ odd}.\quad} \tag{14}

One may choose η\eta of order δ\sqrt\delta as δ↓0\delta\downarrow0; (12) then loses Os(δlog⁡(1/δ))O_s(\delta\log(1/\delta)) above (s−1)j(0)(s-1)j(0). This optimization is not needed for the fixed-corridor theorem.

The proof does not estimate the low-core selected period from below, does not supply a gap for all colors outside the corridor, and does not sum the filling kernels. Those are separate questions.

4. The other color-cube corners are also controlled

For an actual small ordinary color 1≤j≤M1\leq j\leq M, the same finite Gaussian support computation gives a pure high band. Exact sine-factorial complementation gives the particularly simple identity

zN,N−j=(−1)NRPN,j,∥zN,N−j∥H≤56j. \boxed{\qquad z_{N,N-j}=(-1)^N R P_{N,j},\qquad \|z_{N,N-j}\|_{\mathrm H}\leq56^j.\qquad} \tag{15}

For example j=1j=1 is exactly the original crossing RR. To see that the uniform constant does not acquire an extra NO(j)N^{O(j)} factor, the apparent upper-band ratio is

sN−j+t−1s2t−1sN−j−t=sj+t−1s2t−1sj−t,1≤t≤j. \frac{s_{N-j+t-1}}{s_{2t-1}s_{N-j-t}} =\frac{s_{j+t-1}}{s_{2t-1}s_{j-t}},\qquad1\leq t\leq j.

It is the SAME short Gaussian ratio as in the 56j56^j proof. All large factorial indices are complemented before estimating.

Consequently let every actual color jij_i lie either in the low corner 1≤ji≤δN1\leq j_i\leq\delta N or the complete corner N−δN≤ji≤NN-\delta N\leq j_i\leq N. If at least one color is in the low corner, expand only the complete-corner colors by (1). Every resulting term has at least one short crossing sector. The proof of (14) therefore gives uniformly

|Φ2(∏izN,N−ji)|≤exp⁡{N(As−gs/2+o(1))},s≥5 odd. \left|\Phi_2\left(\prod_i z_{N,N-j_i}\right)\right| \leq\exp\{N(A_s-g_s/2+o(1))\},\qquad s\geq5\text{ odd}. \tag{16}

Thus all the OTHER binary corners of the original color cube have a rigorous gap. This does not cover its intermediate-color slabs.

5. The three-box endpoint is genuinely source-sensitive

For s=3s=3, A3=2j(0)A_3=2j(0), so (13) has zero margin. The full source Φ2(RzN2)\Phi_2(Rz_N^2) is a useful diagnostic: its nodal maximum has rate approaching 2j(0)2j(0), but direct evaluation of the complete derivative-plus-value source at levels N=16,32,64,128N=16,32,64,128 gives respectively

N−1log⁡|Φ2(RzN2)|=1.2930259621,1.2612009276,1.2297272136,1.2060473620. N^{-1}\log|\Phi_2(Rz_N^2)|= 1.2930259621,\ 1.2612009276,\ 1.2297272136,\ 1.2060473620.

These finite values suggest an additional source cancellation. They neither prove its rate nor justify a uniform mixed-color gap. The accompanying audit retains the exact crossing derivative in this diagnostic and replays the high-level value at doubled precision.

6. The global intermediate-color test remains a diagnostic

The separate exact-source diagnostic evaluates all equal-color and all single-changed-color periods at N=24,48,72N=24,48,72, s=3,5s=3,5, using the literal all-color two-band coefficients and the complete derivative-plus-value functional. It also checks 544 sorted mixed-color samples at N=48N=48, and replays the selected source values at doubled precision. The sampled maximum is complete color; the largest noncomplete sampled tuples are (0,0,4)(0,0,4) and (0,0,0,0,4)(0,0,0,0,4) in color deficits. This supplies no uniform asymptotic upper bound for intermediate colors or for the unsampled mixed-color cube. In particular it is not an input to (14) or (16).
