A free outside neighborhood for the actual pair-edge kernel

Research proof, 3 November 2026. Frozen manuscripts are unchanged.

The complete-end logarithmic estimate can be strengthened to the full Gaussian edge packet, with a fixed small complex neighborhood in the outside color. The proof below uses the original finite Newton sum. Its contour is a ray in the tangent coordinate, rather than a horizontal line. All low-index endpoints, reciprocal-factorial terms, cotangent aliases, and the original radical are retained.

1. Domain and statement

Write t=a−iHt=a-iH, and put C=cos⁡(t/2),A=C2,𝒟={0<a<π/2,H>0,|C|<1}.(1) C=\cos(t/2),\qquad A=C^2,\qquad \mathcal D=\{\,0<a<\pi/2,\ H>0,\ |C|<1\,\}. \qquad\text{(1)} On this domain CC is in the first quadrant, ℜA>1/2\Re A>1/2, and 0<arg⁡C<π/40<\arg C<\pi/4. 5. A fixed complex outside neighborhood and the full kernel

All contour and tail inequalities just proved are strict on compact sets. The real upper exponent of the exact reciprocal factorial is continuous across zero and across either side of the negative real axis. Therefore the same bounds persist uniformly for tt in a slightly larger complex neighborhood and |u|<ϵ|u|<\epsilon, after decreasing ϵ>0\epsilon>0. For the real omitted indices this uses the continuous factorial upper exponent, not an analytic continuation of their errors from u=0u=0.

On the retained contour the outside factorial arguments have positive real part if ϵ\epsilon is small enough. The selected saddle moves holomorphically. Locally deform the principal ray through this saddle; outside a fixed small saddle neighborhood all gaps persist, and inside it the nonzero quadratic derivative (7) gives the ordinary holomorphic Gaussian expansion. This proves (3) for LN(t,u)L_N(t,u), uniformly with every fixed inverse-power order. The exact contour and normalized errors are holomorphic, so Cauchy estimates retain fixed derivatives on smaller neighborhoods.

Finally the original radical Fourier estimate and its polynomial degree give |KN(t,u)−LN(t,u)|≤N2e(N−1)(|ℑt|+|ℑu|).(22) |K_N(t,u)-L_N(t,u)| \le N^2 e^{(N-1)(|\Im t|+|\Im u|)}. \qquad\text{(22)} By the complete-edge gap and continuity, ℜψ(t,u)>|ℑt|+|ℑu|+c\Re\psi(t,u)>|\Im t|+|\Im u|+c on a sufficiently small fixed neighborhood, for some c>0c>0. Thus (22) is exponentially small relative to the packet, with all fixed derivatives after shrinking the neighborhoods. This establishes Theorem 1 for the full original kernel. In particular the extension preserves the outside ordinary normal: even holomorphy in uu descends through −2cos⁡u=−2+u2+O(u4)-2\cos u=-2+u^2+O(u^4).

No joint-cycle lower bound is asserted. The conclusion supplies an independently normalized free-boundary edge factor for a subsequent original trace or Fourier contraction.

For a fixed integer pair, the selected charge-cube image is compact. Its nonzero angles satisfy (1), while its zero angles have neighborhoods covered by the thin-rectangle kernel theorem. A finite cover therefore supplies one common outside neighborhood for this fixed pair’s entire charge cube. This conclusion still concerns the individual edge kernel.

7. Whole outside-period decay and a complex strip

The same actual contours give a bound useful before a subsequent trace or Fourier contraction. This requires a global factorial estimate, not merely positivity of the finite physical samples.
