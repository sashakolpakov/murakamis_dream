Whole-domain large Fourier charges: exact paired currents and a vanishing-rate operator tail

, 31 October 2026. This concerns the actual fixed-root operator coupons and their original marked contraction. It does not split an infinite charge series at an artificial real cutoff. Twists are fixed before NN. The resulting all-far-sector bound is proved; a partial-far mixed-source estimate is isolated explicitly in Section 5.

1. Original one-port Fourier coupons

Put d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}, Ω\Omega equal to the original pair Casimir, χN(z)=2TN(z/2)+2\chi_N(z)=2T_N(z/2)+2 and VN(z)=χN(z)(z−Ω)−1V_N(z)=\chi_N(z)(z-\Omega)^{-1}, interpreted as its degree-dd polynomial. Work in the original balanced physical basis used by the full endpoint-resolvent theorem, including its high-total adapter. All physical degrees form its orthogonal direct sum. For a fixed integer nn define

BN,n(δ)=sin⁡(2πδ)eπinNδ2VN(−2cos⁡(2πδ)),JN,n,ℓ=∫−1/21/2BN,n(δ)e2πiℓNδdδ,FN,n,ℓ=qnd2/4(−i)ℓ(−1)(N+1)ℓJN,n,ℓ. \begin{aligned} B_{N,n}(\delta)&=\sin(2\pi\delta)e^{\pi i nN\delta^2}V_N(-2\cos(2\pi\delta)),\\ J_{N,n,\ell}&=\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}B_{N,n}(\delta)e^{2\pi i\ell N\delta}\,d\delta,\\ F_{N,n,\ell}&=q^{nd^2/4}(-i)\ell(-1)^{(N+1)\ell}J_{N,n,\ell}. \end{aligned}

The exact full-Hermite formula gives

Rn=∑ℓ∈ℤ\{0}FN,n,ℓ. R^n=\sum_{\ell\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}}F_{N,n,\ell}.\tag{1}

The series is absolutely convergent for each fixed N,nN,n. Its endpoint regularity is that of the WHOLE interval. Since BB is odd, J−ℓ=−JℓJ_{-\ell}=-J_\ell; therefore F−ℓ=FℓF_{-\ell}=F_\ell, including the original parity character. There is no ℓ=0\ell=0 term. For integer M≥1M\geq1 put

TN,n;M=∑|ℓ|>MFN,n,ℓ. T_{N,n;M}=\sum_{|\ell|>M}F_{N,n,\ell}.\tag{2}

This is a literal operator in the actual Casimir quotient, not an auxiliary scalar source or a truncated physical head sum.

2. Exact contour shift and the paired endpoint current

For positive ℓ\ell shift the WHOLE interval vertically UP to height τ>0\tau>0. The integrand is entire. If HN,n,ℓ;τH_{N,n,\ell;\tau} denotes the upper horizontal integral, the two actual side currents give

JN,n,ℓ=HN,n,ℓ;τ−i(−1)Nℓ∫0τe−2πℓNv[BN,n(12+iv)−BN,n(−12+iv)]dv. \begin{aligned} J_{N,n,\ell}={}&H_{N,n,\ell;\tau}\\ &-i(-1)^{N\ell}\int_0^\tau e^{-2\pi\ell Nv} \left[B_{N,n}\!\left(\tfrac12+iv\right)-B_{N,n}\!\left(-\tfrac12+iv\right)\right]dv. \end{aligned}\tag{3}

Both endpoints have the SAME spectral value z(v)=2cosh⁡(2πv)z(v)=2\cosh(2\pi v). Their sine values are −isinh⁡(2πv)-i\sinh(2\pi v), whereas the two Gaussian values are eπinN(1/4−v2)e∓πnNve^{\pi i nN(1/4-v^2)}e^{\mp\pi nNv}. Consequently EXACTLY

B(12+iv)−B(−12+iv)=2isinh⁡(2πv)sinh⁡(πnNv)eπinN(1/4−v2)VN(2cosh⁡(2πv)). B\!\left(\tfrac12+iv\right)-B\!\left(-\tfrac12+iv\right) =2i\sinh(2\pi v)\sinh(\pi nNv)e^{\pi i nN(1/4-v^2)}V_N(2\cosh(2\pi v)).\tag{4}

In particular the paired current vanishes to order TWO at v=0v=0. The second factor is a hyperbolic sine, not a bounded oscillatory sine. Its growth eπ|n|Nve^{\pi|n|Nv} is kept in every estimate. Substitution in (3) gives the plus-sign version

J=H+2(−1)Nℓ∫0τe−2πℓNvsinh⁡(2πv)sinh⁡(πnNv)eπinN(1/4−v2)VN(2cosh⁡(2πv))dv. J=H+2(-1)^{N\ell}\int_0^\tau e^{-2\pi\ell Nv} \sinh(2\pi v)\sinh(\pi nNv)e^{\pi i nN(1/4-v^2)}V_N(2\cosh(2\pi v))\,dv.\tag{5}

Thus multiplication by the original Fourier character in FF leaves the vertical character (−1)ℓ(-1)^\ell, not a level-dependent endpoint sign. No individual vertical side is set to zero. Formula (3) is an equality before any summation or norm bound.

3. Uniform original operator bound for the tail

There are absolute τ0>0\tau_0>0 and constants c,C,C0c,C,C_0 such that the following holds for every fixed nn, 0<τ≤τ00<\tau\leq\tau_0 and integer MM with

D=M−|n|2−1≥1. D=M-\frac{|n|}{2}-1\geq1.

After increasing the constants when needed,

∥TN,n;M∥≤Cn,τNCeC(log⁡N)2{(M+1)e−N(2πτD−C0)+[D−1+(|n|+1)D−2]eNε(D)},ε(D)=Ce−cD→0(D→∞). \begin{aligned} \|T_{N,n;M}\|\leq{}&C_{n,\tau}N^Ce^{C(\log N)^2} \Bigl\{(M+1)e^{-N(2\pi\tau D-C_0)}\\ &\qquad+\left[D^{-1}+(|n|+1)D^{-2}\right]e^{N\varepsilon(D)}\Bigr\},\\ \varepsilon(D)&=Ce^{-cD}\longrightarrow0\quad(D\longrightarrow\infty). \end{aligned}\tag{6}

Only fixed-nn uniformity in NN is asserted. In particular, for every η>0\eta>0 there is LηL_\eta, independent of nn, such that choosing

M≥|n|2+1+Lη, M\geq\frac{|n|}{2}+1+L_\eta,

(with an integer ceiling) makes

limsupN→∞1Nlog⁡∥TN,n;M∥≤η. \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log\|T_{N,n;M}\|\leq\eta.\tag{7}

The constants multiplying (6) may depend on the fixed nn. The cutoff in (7) has the natural Gaussian threshold |n|/2|n|/2 and an additional fixed allowance. This is a bound for the original FULL-V Fourier coupon, including semisimple data.

Proof of the horizontal part. In the original balanced root basis ∥F∥,∥ℰ∥≤2\|F\|,\|\mathcal E\|\leq2 and KK is unitary. The literal coproduct gives ∥Ftot∥,∥ℰtot∥≤4\|F_{\mathrm{tot}}\|,\|\mathcal E_{\mathrm{tot}}\|\leq4, hence ∥Ω∥≤18\|\Omega\|\leq18 on the ENTIRE physical tensor product. This includes high totals without changing the norm by a basis adapter. Equivalently the actual blocks have fixed bandwidth and uniformly bounded entries. The exact divided-Chebyshev identity is

VN(z)=UN−1(Ω/2)+2∑h=1N−1TN−h(z/2)Uh−1(Ω/2). V_N(z)=U_{N-1}(\Omega/2)+2\sum_{h=1}^{N-1}T_{N-h}(z/2)U_{h-1}(\Omega/2).

The scalar recurrences imply ∥Uj(Ω/2)∥≤C*j+1\|U_j(\Omega/2)\|\leq C_*^{j+1} for an absolute C*>1C_*>1. On δ=x+iτ\delta=x+i\tau, |x|≤1/2|x|\leq1/2,

|Tk(−cos⁡(2πδ))|≤e2πkτ. |T_k(-\cos(2\pi\delta))|\leq e^{2\pi k\tau}.

Thus ∥VN(−2cos⁡(2πδ))∥≤CNeN(C0+2πτ)\|V_N(-2\cos(2\pi\delta))\|\leq CN e^{N(C_0+2\pi\tau)} for a fixed C0C_0. The sine is bounded, the Gaussian modulus is at most eπ|n|Nτe^{\pi|n|N\tau}, and the Fourier modulus is e−2πℓNτe^{-2\pi\ell N\tau}. The resulting geometric sum of ℓ\ell times horizontal integrals is bounded by the first term of (6), after a harmless adjustment from M+1M+1 to MM. It introduces no false coefficientwise real-spectral bound.

Proof of the vertical part. Use the already proved original closing-region estimate on the right real ray, with r=2cosh⁡(2πv)−2r=2\cosh(2\pi v)-2:

∥(2cosh⁡(2πv)−Ω)−1∥≤CN3eCNf(r)+C(log⁡N)2,f(r)=rlog⁡(2+1/r). \|(2\cosh(2\pi v)-\Omega)^{-1}\| \leq CN^3e^{CNf(r)+C(\log N)^2},\qquad f(r)=\sqrt r\log(2+1/r).

It applies at v=0v=0 by the polynomial endpoint bound as well. Since χN(2cosh⁡(2πv))=2cosh⁡(2πNv)+2\chi_N(2\cosh(2\pi v))=2\cosh(2\pi Nv)+2, and rr is comparable to v2v^2,

∥VN(2cosh⁡(2πv))∥≤CN3exp⁡{N[2πv+Cvlog(C/v)]+C(logN)2}. \|V_N(2\cosh(2\pi v))\| \leq CN^3\exp\!\left\{N\left[2\pi v+Cv\log(C/v)\right]+C(\log N)^2\right\}.\tag{8}

All constants are uniform for 0≤v≤τ00\leq v\leq\tau_0, and vlog⁡(C/v)=0v\log(C/v)=0 at v=0v=0. Also

|sinh⁡(2πv)sinh⁡(πnNv)|≤C|n|Nv2eπ|n|Nv. |\sinh(2\pi v)\sinh(\pi nNv)|\leq C|n|Nv^2e^{\pi|n|Nv}.

For Dℓ=ℓ−|n|/2−1D_\ell=\ell-|n|/2-1, (5) therefore gives, up to polynomial factors,

|n|N4ℓeC(log⁡N)2∫0τv2exp⁡{N[−2πDℓv+Cvlog(C/v)]}dv. |n|N^4\ell e^{C(\log N)^2}\int_0^\tau v^2 \exp\!\left\{N\left[-2\pi D_\ell v+Cv\log(C/v)\right]\right\}dv.\tag{9}

Reserve half the damping for the integral. Elementary one-variable optimization yields

supv≥0[Cvlog(C/v)−πDv]≤C′e−cD=ε(D). \sup_{v\geq0}\left[Cv\log(C/v)-\pi Dv\right] \leq C'e^{-cD}=\varepsilon(D).

Since Dℓ≥DD_\ell\geq D, the remaining integral is at most

eNε(D)∫0∞v2e−πNDℓvdv=2eNε(D)(πNDℓ)3. e^{N\varepsilon(D)}\int_0^\infty v^2e^{-\pi ND_\ell v}\,dv =\frac{2e^{N\varepsilon(D)}}{(\pi ND_\ell)^3}.

Finally

∑ℓ>MℓDℓ3≤C[D−1+(|n|+1)D−2]. \sum_{\ell>M}\frac{\ell}{D_\ell^3} \leq C\left[D^{-1}+(|n|+1)D^{-2}\right].\tag{10}

This proves (6), with both charge signs included using their exact symmetry. The bound is an ABSOLUTE summation of paired whole-domain currents. It never uses convergence of an infinite sharp-cube series. Choose DD so 2πτD−C0>02\pi\tau D-C_0>0 and ε(D)<η\varepsilon(D)<\eta to obtain (7). The n=0n=0 case has zero vertical current; its finite-band Fourier formula in fact has no charges |ℓ|>1|\ell|>1, consistent with this proof.

4. A genuine source consequence when ALL ports are far

Keep the original diagram order, the original physical cups/caps and its single mark. Replacing every RiniR_i^{n_i} by TN,ni;MiT_{N,n_i;M_i} gives EXACTLY the subseries of the original whole-domain Fourier period with |ℓi|>Mi|\ell_i|>M_i for EVERY port. Finite-NN absolute convergence and multilinearity justify this identity; physical coupons are never commuted. Call this marked contraction ZallfarZ_{\mathrm{allfar}}.

In the same balanced physical basis, the finitely many actual turns, closures and single marked cap have polynomial operator/Euclidean norms in NN. Their entries are the original NN times unit-phase cup, N−1N^{-1} ordinary cap and N−2N^{-2} marked cap. More explicitly, cups and ordinary caps have a+b=da+b=d; the marked cap mab=xb/N2m_{ab}=x^b/N^2 is UNRESTRICTED. Their Euclidean norms are respectively N3/2N^{3/2}, N−1/2N^{-1/2} and N−1N^{-1}, and the retained right-turn pivots are unit phases. The number of wires and contractions is fixed by the diagram. Hence

|Zallfar|≤poly⁡D(N)∏i∥TN,ni;Mi∥,limsupN→∞1Nlog⁡|Zallfar|≤∑iε(Di). |Z_{\mathrm{allfar}}|\leq\operatorname{poly}_D(N)\prod_i\|T_{N,n_i;M_i}\|, \qquad\limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log|Z_{\mathrm{allfar}}|\leq\sum_i\varepsilon(D_i).\tag{11}

once each horizontal term has been made exponentially decreasing. This is an actual shared-source estimate, not a replacement of its coefficient by arbitrary face radii. All nin_i are fixed before NN.

The geometric local unit theorem gives Φfill≥ΦD/2\Phi_{\mathrm{fill}}\geq\Phi_D/2 for ALL independently sufficiently long fixed twists, after increasing a threshold depending only on the original diagram. Choose fixed DiD_i so ∑iε(Di)<ΦD/4\sum_i\varepsilon(D_i)<\Phi_D/4 and all horizontal exponents in (6) are negative. These DiD_i depend on the fixed diagram, not on ratios or magnitudes of the long slopes. The actual cutoffs can therefore be chosen as Mi=⌈|ni|/2+1+Di⌉M_i=\lceil|n_i|/2+1+D_i\rceil. Then (11) gives a uniform strict RATE gap from the proved local contribution throughout that general long-filling class. Prefactor and relative-error constants may still depend on the fixed twists; no joint growing-twist/level limit follows. This does not cover the much larger UNION of sectors in which only one or some charges are far.

5. Partial far subsets: the exact missing mixed-source estimate

Let ℰD,S,N\mathcal E_{D,S,N} be the literal original marked exterior functional with RjnjR_j^{n_j} retained, in their original order, on every jj outside a nonempty set SS. Define its norm on the remaining tensor product of operator spaces by

∥ℰD,S,N∥=sup∥Ai∥≤1i∈S|ℰD,S,N(⨂i∈SAi)|. \|\mathcal E_{D,S,N}\|= \sup_{\substack{\|A_i\|\leq1\\ i\in S}} \left|\mathcal E_{D,S,N}\!\left(\bigotimes_{i\in S}A_i\right)\right|.

Then the immediate correct bound for that subset is

|ℰD,S,N(⨂i∈STi)|≤∥ℰD,S,N∥∏i∈S∥Ti∥. \left|\mathcal E_{D,S,N}\!\left(\bigotimes_{i\in S}T_i\right)\right| \leq\|\mathcal E_{D,S,N}\|\prod_{i\in S}\|T_i\|.\tag{12}

There is no established reason for the first factor to have rate less than Φfill\Phi_{\mathrm{fill}}. The remaining signed RR powers are NOT uniformly unitary in this basis. Their individual exponential norm bounds only supply a crude upper rate depending on the retained twists. Equation (7) alone therefore cannot remove a partial-far sector.

The exact finite inclusion-exclusion identity for the union is

Zfull−Zalllow=∑⌀≠S⊆ports(−1)|S|+1ℰD,S,N(⨂i∈STi). Z_{\mathrm{full}}-Z_{\mathrm{all\,low}} =\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq\mathrm{ports}}(-1)^{|S|+1} \mathcal E_{D,S,N}\!\left(\bigotimes_{i\in S}T_i\right).\tag{13}

It retains the original full RR powers on the complement of SS, rather than replacing them by an uncontrolled collection of infinitely many spectator charge pieces. A direct sufficient new input would be a strict bound on these actual mixed exteriors, or a narrower estimate on the paired-current families

ℰD,S,N(⨂i∈SVi(2cosh(2πvi))),0≤vi≤τ. \mathcal E_{D,S,N}\!\left(\bigotimes_{i\in S}V_i(2\cosh(2\pi v_i))\right), \qquad0\leq v_i\leq\tau.\tag{14}

with a rate strictly below Φfill\Phi_{\mathrm{fill}} as vv tends to zero, uniformly on a small fixed closing box, including simultaneous endpoint subsets. Every sine, Gaussian and charge character in (4)-(5) would then remain explicit and summably controlled. Horizontal pieces can be made as small as needed by increasing the fixed charge cutoffs, using a crude finite exponential norm for the fixed remaining coupons. The difficult input is thus the SHARED endpoint environment in (14), not a missing local +2 resolvent estimate.

Neither the positive Fejer endpoint theorem at σ=−2\sigma=-2 nor the noncross local gap proves (14) at σ=+2\sigma=+2 with arbitrary remaining RR powers. Nor does a local norm estimate prove the needed shared rate. This distinction is preserved in (11)-(14). No full invariant or partial-far complement theorem is claimed here.

Validation and scope

The focused audit and its results check paired endpoint entries, whole-interval contour identities and charge-sign identities from the physical blocks. These are finite normalization checks, not proofs of an asymptotic rate or a mixed-source gap.
