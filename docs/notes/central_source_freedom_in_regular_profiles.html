A same-operator central freedom in the whole regular profile

Independent algebraic audit, 27 October 2026. The actual root operator, its bounded affine source, all integer-color values, reciprocity, and qualitative finite exponential type do not by themselves force a constant centered regular profile. They leave a central source freedom. This is not a modification of the literature-defined invariant: it identifies precisely which proposed uniqueness premises omit its full marked source.

The starting objects are the actual regularized period and scaled meridian profile. Fix QN=e2πi/NQ_N=e^{2\pi i/N}, U=YNU=Y^N, and write FN=SNRN+PN,SN=(U−1)/(Y−1),KN=FN(1). F_N=S_NR_N+P_N,\qquad S_N=(U-1)/(Y-1),\qquad K_N=F_N(1). \tag{1} The specified root operator PP annihilates FNF_N. After the fixed actual gauge, its affine operator AA satisfies ARN=bN,ApN=−bN,pN=PN/SN. A R_N=b_N,\qquad A p_N=-b_N,\qquad p_N=P_N/S_N. \tag{2} All coefficients and clearing factors of these actual operators are retained.

1. An exact transformation preserving the operator and source

Let h(U)h(U) be any Laurent polynomial satisfying h(1)=1,h(U−1)=h(U), h(1)=1,\qquad h(U^{-1})=h(U), and define an auxiliary comparison family F̃N(Y)=h(YN)FN(Y). \widetilde F_N(Y)=h(Y^N)F_N(Y). \tag{3} The same hh is used at every level. The following statements are exact:

  • Every specified root annihilator of FNF_N annihilates F̃N\widetilde F_N, because T(U)=UT(U)=U for T(Y)=QNYT(Y)=Q_NY.
  • Every ordinary and complete orbit value is unchanged, since U=1U=1 at Y=QNjY=Q_N^j. Thus PNP_N, KNK_N, and the given ordinary-color polynomial family are unchanged.
  • Laurent polynomiality and actual Weyl reciprocity are preserved.
  • The canonical regular quotient remains Laurent polynomial and satisfies the same affine equation with the same bNb_N.

To prove the last assertion set Lh(U)=h(U)−1U−1∈ℂ[U,U−1]. L_h(U)=\frac{h(U)-1}{U-1}\in\mathbb C[U,U^{-1}]. Division is in the Laurent polynomial ring and is regular at U=1U=1. The exact quotient is R̃N=h(U)RN+(Y−1)Lh(U)PN=h(U)RN+(h(U)−1)pN. \widetilde R_N =h(U)R_N+(Y-1)L_h(U)P_N =h(U)R_N+(h(U)-1)p_N. \tag{4} Since AA commutes with every central h(U)h(U), (2) gives AR̃N=hbN−(h−1)bN=bN. A\widetilde R_N=h b_N-(h-1)b_N=b_N. \tag{5} In particular both regular meridian lattices used to fix the complete value are preserved, and R̃N(1)=RN(1)\widetilde R_N(1)=R_N(1). This agrees with the pointed complete-value uniqueness statement: it does not imply uniqueness of the whole meridian function.

This construction is rootwise and all-level. It does not supply an off-root quantum-normal lift with the same q-jets, nor a second actual knot invariant.

2. The freedom survives regularization and finite type

Assume the precise tail domination used in the scaled-profile theorem: εN=NDN|KN|→0. \varepsilon_N=\frac{ND_N}{|K_N|}\longrightarrow0. Put Y=ez/NY=e^{z/N} and use the original centered full-state monomial: gN(z)=FN(ez/N)/KN,ρN(z)=e(N−1)z/(2N)NRN(ez/N)/KN. g_N(z)=F_N(e^{z/N})/K_N,\qquad \rho_N(z)=e^{(N-1)z/(2N)}\,NR_N(e^{z/N})/K_N . Equation (3) gives exactly g̃N(z)=h(ez)gN(z). \widetilde g_N(z)=h(e^z)g_N(z). \tag{6} By (4) the difference between ρ̃N\widetilde\rho_N and h(ez)ρNh(e^z)\rho_N is e(N−1)z/(2N)N(ez/N−1)Lh(ez)PN(ez/N)/KN. e^{(N-1)z/(2N)}N(e^{z/N}-1) L_h(e^z)P_N(e^{z/N})/K_N. \tag{7} On every fixed compact z-set, N(ez/N−1)N(e^{z/N}-1) is bounded uniformly and PN/KN=O(εN)P_N/K_N=O(\varepsilon_N). Hence (7) tends uniformly to zero. Whenever the original whole regular profile exists, ρN→ρ⇒ρ̃N→h(ez)ρ. \rho_N\longrightarrow\rho \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \widetilde\rho_N\longrightarrow h(e^z)\rho . \tag{8} Convergence is locally uniform, including the exact resonance centers, by (7) or by the proved uniform removable division.

For the concrete choice hm(U)=12(Um+U−m),m∈ℤ>0, h_m(U)=\tfrac12(U^m+U^{-m}),\qquad m\in\mathbb Z_{>0}, the multiplier is cosh⁡(mz)\cosh(mz): an even entire function, normalized to one at zero, of finite exponential type. If ρ\rho has finite exponential type, so does cosh⁡(mz)ρ\cosh(mz)\rho. Since ρ(0)=1\rho(0)=1, the latter cannot coincide identically with ρ\rho. If a nonconstant ρ\rho already exists the proposed constancy inference has failed; if ρ=1\rho=1, this same-operator construction gives the explicit nonconstant comparison profile cosh⁡(mz)\cosh(mz).

Thus even the actual root operator and its actual bounded affine source do not make qualitative finite exponential type a constancy criterion. A sharp numerical type or Laurent-degree window can impose additional constraints; none is being discarded by this argument.

3. What the marked source still contributes

The comparison changes the full source and its normal jets. For example hm(U)−1h_m(U)-1 has a second-order zero at U=1U=1; the first meridian jet is preserved, but the second generally is not. More generally h(U)=1+λ(U+U−1−2)r h(U)=1+\lambda(U+U^{-1}-2)^r preserves all orbit meridian jets of order below 2r2r, while changing the whole profile by a finite-type multiplier. Every prescribed finite jet budget can therefore be preserved by choosing a larger fixed rr. This does not preserve all analytic jets: the identity theorem would force an analytic multiplier with every jet equal to one to be identically one.

The Laurent degree window also grows by O(N)O(N), and an exact source lattice may prohibit that change. The result therefore isolates a genuine missing premise rather than refuting uniqueness for the fully pointed actual state. The next comparison must retain its source lattice or its whole normal connection; operator support, ordinary-color values and an unspecified finite growth type do not replace it.

There is a constructive first-normal remedy. The source-corrected normal connection uses the same whole preoperator to turn its normal source into a linear rule RN(v)R_N(v). If the actual root section has ι(v)≠0\iota(v)\ne0, simultaneous lifting of vv and h(U)vh(U)v forces Uh′(U)ι(v)=0Uh'(U)\iota(v)=0, hence hh is constant and the anchor fixes it to one. That nonzero-symbol condition is not automatic; the linked note gives an exact Jordan counterexample and the actual factorial atom with its corrected connection.
