Local spectral adapters for the original Newton gates

Private written proof, 15 November 2026.

The exact Newton multiplication and signed crossing gates admit explicit one-variable spectral generating families. Their critical values, nonzero fiber Hessians and Gaussian densities agree with the original factorial formulas. The multiplication statement includes the high indices of the actual source. The crossing statement records the integer Fourier character needed to compare the two analytic lifts.

These identities give a local comparison of the complete Newton and angular phases when a specified factorization has regular intermediate labels. They do not prove that an arbitrary positive angular point has such labels, or that the original integration cycle contains its local Gaussian class. In particular, they do not transport the entire inverse-period descent contour through a stationary elimination.
