An exact cusp-color split with a controlled original normal-source remainder

29 October 2026. For every j=N−rj=N-r with 0≤r≤⌊(N−1)/2⌋0\leq r\leq\lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor, the literal rotated pretzel circle box has a positive half-degree core plus an original crossing times a degree-r−1r-1 polynomial. The entire second band, including every Casimir normal jet, has norm at most 56r56^r in a faithful source-compatible Hermite norm. Thus it costs exp⁡(O(δN))\exp(O(\delta N)) on a fixed cusp-color corridor r≤δNr\leq\delta N, and subexponentially when r=o(N)r=o(N). No term is deleted.

This is not yet a geometric survival theorem. The low core still requires its selected asymptotic analysis; estimates of a remainder do not prove that the whole original coefficient is nonzero.

1. The entire rotated polynomial, with its two support bands

Use the original conventions and actual rotation ℛ\mathscr R of the complete-base polynomial theorem. Let fvf_v be the physical middle-degree continuant polynomials, sa=|(Q;Q)a|s_a=|(Q;Q)_a|, M=⌊d/2⌋M=\lfloor d/2\rfloor, d=N−1d=N-1. Then

ℛ(R−1LN−r)=zN,r(Ω),zN,r(z)=κ−1∑v=0dar,vfv(z), \mathscr R(R^{-1}L_{N-r})=z_{N,r}(\Omega),\qquad z_{N,r}(z)=\kappa^{-1}\sum_{v=0}^d a_{r,v}f_v(z), ar,v=sd−v2qr2−v2[2vv−r]Q. \boxed{\quad a_{r,v}=s_{d-v}^2q^{r^2-v^2} {2v\brack v-r}_Q.\quad} \tag{1}

The Gaussian coefficient is its finite polynomial, not an invalid factorial ratio at a root. A negative lower index gives zero. For the displayed cusp corridor its support and all phases are exactly

ar,v=sd−v2s2vsv−rsv+r>0,r≤v≤M, a_{r,v}=\frac{s_{d-v}^2s_{2v}}{s_{v-r}s_{v+r}}>0, \quad r\leq v\leq M, \tag{2} ar,N−t=(−1)N−t−rst−12st+r−1s2t−1sr−t,1≤t≤r, a_{r,N-t}=(-1)^{N-t-r} \frac{s_{t-1}^2s_{t+r-1}}{s_{2t-1}s_{r-t}}, \quad 1\leq t\leq r, \tag{3}

and it is zero elsewhere. In particular the middle band is exactly zero. For r=0r=0, (2) is the positive complete-base coefficient bv=N2s2v/sv4b_v=N^2s_{2v}/s_v^4, and there is no upper band.

Proof by a finite resolvent, not a semiclassical approximation. In the low block of degree K=d−vK=d-v, the first-to-last entry of (I−tJK+t2I)−1(I-tJ_K+t^2I)^{-1} is

tKBK∏k=0K(1−q2k+1t)(1−q−2k−1t),BK=(−1)KsK2. \frac{t^K B_K}{\prod_{k=0}^K(1-q^{2k+1}t)(1-q^{-2k-1}t)}, \qquad B_K=(-1)^Ks_K^2.

Its tj−1t^{j-1} coefficient is the first-to-last entry of Uj−1(JK/2)U_{j-1}(J_K/2). The full two-copy root orbit factors the inverse denominator as

(q1−2vt;Q)2v(1+tN)2. \frac{(q^{1-2v}t;Q)_{2v}}{(1+t^N)^2}.

The needed remaining coefficient has degree v−r<Nv-r<N, so the denominator contributes exactly 1. The finite q-binomial theorem, the original circle sign (−1)j−1(-1)^{j-1}, and the literal R−1R^{-1} last-row coefficient give precisely (1). This derivation retains both the actual pivotal circle normalization and its half twist.

If 2v<N2v<N, the Gaussian phase in (1) cancels, giving (2). If 2v≥N2v\geq N and v−r<Nv-r<N, split one whole root orbit from the finite q-binomial product. Its factor has no coefficient in degrees 1,…,N−11,\ldots,N-1. Thus [2vv−r]Q=[2v−Nv−r]Q{2v\brack v-r}_Q={2v-N\brack v-r}_Q, which is zero unless v+r≥Nv+r\geq N. Taking its explicit phase and complementing the remaining sine factorials gives (3). This proves the exact bands without division by a vanished factorial.

2. The upper band is an original crossing times a SHORT polynomial

Let f‾l\bar f_l mean coefficient conjugation at the specified principal root. Reversing the physical Jacobi recurrence gives

fd−l(Ω)=fd(Ω)f‾l(Ω),fd(Ω)=xdrN(Ω). f_{d-l}(\Omega)=f_d(\Omega)\bar f_l(\Omega), \qquad f_d(\Omega)=x^d r_N(\Omega).

Indeed e0Tfd(A)=edTe_0^Tf_d(A)=e_d^T and edTf‾l(A)=ed−lTe_d^T\bar f_l(A)=e_{d-l}^T; the cyclic first row proves the polynomial identity modulo the FULL 𝒫N\mathcal P_N, including every double root. Since xd=κ2x^d=\kappa^2, define

The exact ALL-INPUT identity is

zN,r(Ω)=cN,r(Ω)+RPN,r(Ω). \boxed{\quad z_{N,r}(\Omega)=c_{N,r}(\Omega) +R P_{N,r}(\Omega).\quad} \tag{4}

For r=1r=1 its remainder is (−1)NR(-1)^N R. Thus the short sector is not identically zero, and it cannot be removed by restricting to the original cup or by retaining only the complete-circle quotient.

3. Uniform quantitative bound WITH every original normal jet

On ℂ[z]/(𝒫N)\mathbb C[z]/(\mathcal P_N) use the faithful Hermite norm

∥f∥H=max⁡{maxcdouble(|f(c)|+N−2|f′(c)|),|f(−2)|ifNisodd}. \|f\|_{\mathrm H}= \max\left\{\max_{c\ \mathrm{double}} (|f(c)|+N^{-2}|f'(c)|),\quad |f(-2)|\ \mathrm{if\ }N\mathrm{\ is\ odd}\right\}. \tag{5}

It includes values and ALL first-normal coefficients; the simple node has only its value. It is submultiplicative. The literal crossing has ∥R∥H≤9/8\|R\|_{\mathrm H}\leq9/8, by its original unit nodal values and |rN′/rN|≤N2/8|r_N'/r_N|\leq N^2/8. The original source has

|Φ2(f)|≤3N3∥f∥H. |\Phi_2(f)|\leq3N^3\|f\|_{\mathrm H}. \tag{6}

This follows immediately from the exact source values and derivatives in the complete-base theorem. In particular this norm does not discard the nilpotent source as an ordinary semisimple quotient would.

There is a constant independent of N,rN,r, explicitly

∥RPN,r∥H≤56r,1≤r≤M. \boxed{\quad \|R P_{N,r}\|_{\mathrm H}\leq56^r, \qquad 1\leq r\leq M.\quad} \tag{7}

Here is a direct proof of the bound, including the cancellation of small factorial denominators. Write the MONIC physical continuant as pl=(a0⋯al−1)flp_l=(a_0\cdots a_{l-1})f_l, so |a0⋯al−1|=sl2|a_0\cdots a_{l-1}|=s_l^2. On |z|≤2|z|\leq2, its recurrence has |z−Dl|≤12|z-D_l|\leq12 and |al−12|≤16|a_{l-1}^2|\leq16. Induction gives

|pl(z)|≤14l,|pl′(z)|≤l14l−1. |p_l(z)|\leq14^l,\qquad |p_l'(z)|\leq l14^{l-1}.

The coefficient in (3) divided by st−12s_{t-1}^2 has magnitude

st+r−1s2t−1sr−t=|[t+r−12t−1]Q|≤(t+r−12t−1)≤4r. \frac{s_{t+r-1}}{s_{2t-1}s_{r-t}} =\left|{t+r-1\brack 2t-1}_Q\right| \leq {t+r-1\choose 2t-1}\leq4^r.

The first inequality follows from the nonnegative integer coefficients of the finite Gaussian polynomial (or its elementary Pascal induction), since its argument has modulus one. Therefore the st−12s_{t-1}^2 CANCELS the full denominator of f‾t−1\bar f_{t-1} before estimating any normal derivative. Summing the geometric series in 14t−114^{t-1}, and multiplying by 9/89/8, proves (7) with room to spare. No NO(r)N^{O(r)} loss occurs.

There is also a full physical operator bound

∥PN,r(Ω)∥op≤120r,∥RPN,r(Ω)∥op≤120r∥R∥op. \|P_{N,r}(\Omega)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq120^r, \qquad \|R P_{N,r}(\Omega)\|_{\mathrm{op}} \leq120^r\|R\|_{\mathrm{op}}. \tag{8}

Indeed every physical Ω\Omega block has norm at most18; the same monic recurrence is bounded by 30l30^l when evaluated at this operator. The preceding factorial cancellation proves (8). The factor ∥R∥\|R\| cannot be discarded: already r=1r=1 the remainder is the whole original crossing. Thus (7), which is the faithful normal-source bound needed here, is stronger than a claim of uniform physical contraction would be and has a different scope.

4. Exact simultaneous original-source error accounting

For arbitrary fixed ss, substitute (4) at every augmented pretzel box BEFORE applying the original source. The difference between the complete mixed-color period and its low-core product is exactly the sum over nonempty subsets of the retained remainder boxes. Equations (5)–(7) give the explicit bound, with ϵ0=0\epsilon_0=0 and ϵr=56r\epsilon_r=56^r for r≥1r\geq1,

|Φ2(∏izN,ri−∏icN,ri)|≤3N3∑⌀≠S⊂{1,…,s}∏i∈Sϵri∏i∉S∥cN,ri∥H. \left|\Phi_2\left(\prod_i z_{N,r_i} -\prod_i c_{N,r_i}\right)\right| \leq3N^3\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subset\{1,\ldots,s\}} \prod_{i\in S}\epsilon_{r_i} \prod_{i\notin S}\|c_{N,r_i}\|_{\mathrm H}. \tag{9}

For ri≤δNr_i\leq\delta N the individual retained source remainders have rate at most δlog⁡56\delta\log56; for ri=o(N)r_i=o(N) that rate is zero. This is an independently quantified actual source term. It does not make the TOTAL right side negligible relative to the geometric period without estimates of the coherent core and its selected action gap. No open-corridor noncancellation or long-filling survival is asserted from (9) alone.
