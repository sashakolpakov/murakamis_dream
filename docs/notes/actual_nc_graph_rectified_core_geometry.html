The actual graph complement from one rectified core and octahedra

29 October 2026. This is a general GEOMETRIC theorem for the literal ordered noncrossing (NC) cubic sources. It gives the actual complete hyperbolic volume, rather than a scalar-vertex volume surrogate. Its input is a finite positive right-angled ideal core certificate. It does not prove the growing-color quantum asymptotic.

Let Gamma be a connected plane cubic graph in the original NC source class, with b black and b white vertices and E=3b edges. Keep a crossing circle on each physical edge box. Suppose Q is a convex right-angled ideal polyhedron with the following labeled medial incidence:

  • Its ideal vertices x_e are the edges e of Gamma.
  • At every vertex v of Gamma there is a triangular face T_v with the three incident edge labels.
  • At every face f of Gamma there is a polygon F_f following its edge boundary. Every polyhedral edge separates a T_v from an F_f.

The certificate includes the labeled oriented face cycles and positive supporting-plane inequalities. In particular a matching unlabeled polyhedron or cusp count is not sufficient. Such a Q will be called the ideal rectified core; this term means the ideal medial polyhedron, not the untruncated projective polyhedron used in some literature.

Then the ACTUAL retained-circle source link L_Gamma(e), with any fixed signed edge twist integers e, has a complete hyperbolic metric and

Vol(S^3 minus L_Gamma(e)) = 4b v8 + 2 Vol(Q).       (1)

The topology and peripheral crossings in this assertion are those of the original cups, caps and edge boxes. No quantum twist has been removed. Equation (1) is the target for an eventual full-source limit, not a proof of that limit.

1. A finite explicit octahedron on an ideal triangle

Use the Lorentz product of signature (-,+,+,+). Normalize the three future null lifts a,b,c of a triangular face so that their pairwise products are -1. This is possible uniquely by positive rescaling: the three scale products are prescribed positive numbers. Let n be its outward unit spacelike normal. The regular ideal octahedron on the OTHER side of this face has three further vertices

a' = b+c+sqrt(2)n,
b' = c+a+sqrt(2)n,
c' = a+b+sqrt(2)n.                               (2)

These are future null vectors. Opposite pairs (a,a’), (b,b’), (c,c’) have product -2 and every other pair has product -1. Thus the six vectors are Lorentz isometric to the standard regular ideal octahedron: four independent vectors determine an isometry by their Gram matrix, and the remaining two satisfy the same linear equations. The choice of +n places its interior outside Q.

The three octahedral faces adjacent to the attaching face merge with the corresponding F_f faces. Indeed their third vertices have the form a+b+sqrt(2)n. If m is the normal of F_f along the edge ab, then <m,a>=<m,b>=<m,n>=0, the last equality being the right-angle condition. Thus all three vertices lie in that same supporting plane.

The remaining boundary faces are an opposite WHITE triangle (a’,b’,c’) and three SHADED triangles

S_(v,e_a)=(a,b',c'), and cyclically.              (3)

Their outward unit normals are respectively

n_white=2n+(a+b+c)/sqrt(2),
n_a=n+sqrt(2)a, and cyclically.                 (4)

Their incident products vanish. Equations (2)–(4) prove directly that all the surviving boundary dihedral angles are right angles.

Attach an octahedron along EVERY T_v. This is an abstract gluing of convex cells along full ideal faces. The union is topologically a ball: the attaching triangles have disjoint interiors, and the same statement holds after small cusp truncation. We do not need to assume that all developed octahedra in a chosen global drawing have disjoint interiors. The intrinsic cell metric and the actual face identifications below are enough to construct the complete manifold. Flat boundary seams are merged only as intrinsic coplanar faces.

Each original F_f is an ideal polygon. An attached coplanar triangle lies outside one of its sides, with its third ideal vertex on the empty ideal-boundary interval between that side’s endpoints. Different sides have disjoint such intervals. These attachments therefore form an embedded ideal polygon with twice as many vertices. This also justifies the merged white-face isometries used below.

2. The complete labeled bowtie cell

Call the resulting ball P. It contains Q and exactly 2b octahedra, with disjoint interiors in its intrinsic cell decomposition. Hence

Vol(P)=Vol(Q)+2b v8.                             (5)

It has 3E ideal vertices: E original x_e and three new vertices for each of the 2b original graph vertices. Its shaded faces are the 2E triangles S_(v,e). Its white faces are the 2b opposite octahedral triangles and the b+2 expanded old face polygons, hence E+2 in total. Every boundary edge separates a white and a shaded face. The boundary has 6E edges and Euler characteristic 2.

For each edge e=vw, the two shaded triangles S_(v,e),S_(w,e) meet at x_e. They are precisely the two sides of its retained crossing disk. Their other vertices correspond to the two physical strand arcs at that end of the box. At each trivalent graph vertex the three original nested cup/cap arcs connect the three incident disks in a triangle. Consequently the white-face and cusp incidence is exactly the Fisher expansion T(Gamma), as independently traced in the actual boundary construction.

This is a reversible topological cut: shrink each opposite white triangle toward the original vertex cup/cap matching; trace each expanded F_f along the original planar region; restore each pair of shaded triangles as its crossing disk. It recovers the literal NC diagram. In particular it does not use an arbitrary incidence permutation in place of the original ordered ports.

Take two copies P0,P1 with opposite orientations. Glue each WHITE face across the copies by its labels. At e=vw an even box twist glues S_(v,e) to S_(w,e) within each copy, fixing x_e and matching the actual two strand positions. An odd twist uses the opposite copy and exchanges these two outer strand positions. These are exactly the disk maps from the original two-strand box; the same orientation rule is written explicitly for theta in the marked-theta geometry.

Because every shaded face is an ideal triangle, each prescribed vertex bijection is an isometry. Around every actual boundary edge the alternating white and disk maps give four right angles. At an old flat seam the two copies supply two angles pi. Internal attaching faces already have smooth neighborhoods. Thus all finite edge and face neighborhoods in the glued cell metric are hyperbolic.

3. Simultaneous horospheres and completeness

Completeness requires more than the angle sums. At an original ideal vertex x_e the Q link is a Euclidean rectangle. Its two opposite sides belong to T_v and T_w, and have equal length on EVERY horosphere. Normalize its horosphere so that this length is 1. Do this independently at each original ideal vertex. On each triangular T_v, all three corner horocycle segments now have length 1: they are its canonical mutually tangent decoration. Equality of opposite rectangle sides shows there is no incompatibility between the two triangles at x_e.

Equip each attached regular octahedron with its canonical decoration having side length 1 at every cusp square. Its three old vertex horospheres then agree with those just chosen on Q. Its new vertices use that same canonical decoration. Every S_(v,e) consequently has the canonical decoration at ALL THREE corners. Every shaded face pairing, including an outer-vertex exchange, preserves it. White pairings preserve decorations because they join identical copies.

After uniformly shrinking these horospheres, they are disjoint and the complement of their ends is compact. The ideal links glue as compact nonsingular Euclidean surfaces. They are orientable, and the reversible link cut identifies them as the link’s cusp tori. There is no dilation holonomy. Their ends are complete standard hyperbolic cusps. This proves completeness and finite volume of the literal link complement, with volume twice (5), establishing (1).

Further full twists are crossing-disk collar homeomorphisms while the circle remains drilled. They change the preferred peripheral marking by the actual integral shear. They do not change the unmarked complete volume. The quantum twist phases and any later filling slopes must still retain that shear.

4. Algebraic input and an explicit nondegenerate example

The input is finite real-algebraic data: future null vertex lifts, spacelike outward face normals, incidence equalities, strict supporting inequalities, the labeled convex face cycles, and vanishing Lorentz products at adjacent faces. A finite convex-hull/incidence check certifies the stated Q. Formulae (2)–(4) use only arithmetic and positive square roots. No limiting geometric realization or a choice of formal peripheral series is needed for this complete anchor.

For the cube graph, the core is explicitly the convex hull in the Klein ball of

(±1,±1,0)/sqrt(2) and their coordinate permutations. (6)

Its six square planes are ±X_i=1/sqrt(2). Its eight triangular planes are ±X_1±X_2±X_3=sqrt(2). The corresponding Lorentz normals have norms squared 1/2 and 1, respectively; each incident square/triangle pair has product zero. Every vertex is ideal, and the strict nonincident supporting inequalities check directly. Thus it is a positive ideal right-angled cuboctahedron with the required cube labels. Here b=4 and

Vol(actual augmented cube link)=16v8+2Vol(Q_cube). (7)

The exact audit checks this certificate and all eight octahedral attachments, their merged supporting planes, right angles and the actual resulting 36-vertex/72-edge/38-face bowtie incidence. The general proof is the cell construction above, not extrapolation from this example.

The theta graph has a degenerate triangular core rather than a positive three-dimensional Q. Its separately proved four-octahedron construction has volume 4v8; it is not being passed through the strict hypothesis here.

The input class is closed under actual vertex grafts

Two such positive cores can be glued along triangular faces by the specified three-port bijection, putting their interiors on opposite sides of the common plane. The adjacent white polygons merge because their dihedral angles add to pi. The resulting union is convex. For an explicit proof, the two convex cores lie in opposite closed halfspaces and intersect exactly in the common triangle T. If x,y are in different cores, minimize d(x,z)+d(z,y) over z in T. This function tends to infinity when z escapes to an ideal end, so a minimum is attained at a finite point. Every finite seam point is locally convex because the right angles add to pi; T has no finite vertices. The minimizing broken path therefore straightens locally across the seam to the ambient geodesic. It is a single geodesic, contained in the union. Points in the same core are already joined there by a geodesic. This proves convexity. Its ideal end vertices and the remaining right-angled boundary faces have exactly the medial incidence of the planar three-edge vertex sum of the two graphs.

The canonical unit decorations agree at the seam. Volumes add, and the finite certificate is transported by the explicit face isometry. This gives a constructive closure operation on the geometric input class, not an existence assumption for every planar graph. When the operation uses the ordered interval/outer-face graft of the NC cube insertion, it also retains the actual original source topology.

In particular, start with the literal cube and repeatedly insert an ordered cube at any allowed interval vertex. Equivalently take the theta source followed by k>=1 such cube insertions. The resulting genuinely branching family has b=1+3k and a core formed from k copies of Q_cube. Its actual hyperbolic volume is

Vol(S^3 minus L_Gamma(e))
       =(4+12k)v8+2k Vol(Q_cube).                (7a)

The choices include arbitrary finite ordered binary branching trees. This establishes positive core existence and actual geometry throughout that family. The fixed-level spin-one theorem does not yet provide its all-N quantum limit.

5. The precise remaining quantum target

For every graph satisfying the input certificate, the desired equality now has a geometrically specified right side:

lim_N (2pi/N)log |K_N(L_Gamma(e))|
                =4b v8+2Vol(Q).                 (8)

The exposed-mark first-normal source formula is exact, but it does not yet prove (8). The shared Q contribution is a global graph term; independent theta scalars cannot supply it. Growing-label control of the full magnetic first normal, coherent noncancellation, and the outer-color packet needed for fillings remain to be proved.

For coverage only, the ideal medial polyhedron appears as the truncated rectification in Belletti’s paper. Fully augmented right-angled cell decompositions and octahedral subclasses are discussed in Purcell’s exposition. No proof or quantum-invariant identification from those sources is used in the construction above, and no novelty of the geometric operation is asserted.

The cube audit determines the six square faces from the source’s opposite black/white ribbon orders. In the vertex-graft argument, minimizing paths use the exact common triangle and cannot escape to an ideal end.
