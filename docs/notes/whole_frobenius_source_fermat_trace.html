An actual whole Frobenius source is a Fermat-cover trace

Research proof, 27 October 2026. Integral divided powers give a whole algebraic source polynomial whose root value is the actual binomial coherent source. Its Kummer-cover trace, norm and first quantum-normal flag transport are explicit. This supplies a source object before taking jets, with every power and phase retained.

This is a choice of whole coupons in the ORIGINAL integral color. An explicit root-identity pair gauge compares it with the previously specified divided-exponential coupons. Operators and all partial sources must be transported by that gauge. No equality with a new state model, controlled growing-level norm, or selected geometric period is inferred.

1. Actual divided powers and the whole polynomial

Fix N>=2, m=N(N+1)-1 and a residue string 0<=s<N in D_m(q). Use the constant actual sign frame wk,s=βk,sukN+s,βk,s=(−1)Nk(k−1)/2+ks,0≤k≤N. w_{k,s}=\beta_{k,s}u_{kN+s},\qquad \beta_{k,s}=(-1)^{Nk(k-1)/2+ks},\quad 0\le k\le N. Symmetric Gaussian coefficients below are integral Laurent polynomials, with the original divided-power formulas F(r)uj=[j+rr]quj+r,E(r)uj=[m−j+rr]quj−r. F^{(r)}u_j={j+r\brack r}_q u_{j+r},\qquad E^{(r)}u_j={m-j+r\brack r}_q u_{j-r}. Put L_s=m-s and epsilon_s=(-1)^(N-1+s). Define Ak,s(q)=ϵsk[LskN]q,Ps(q,t)=∑k=0NAk,s(q)tk. A_{k,s}(q)=\epsilon_s^k{L_s\brack kN}_q,\qquad P_s(q,t)=\sum_{k=0}^N A_{k,s}(q)t^k. \tag{1} Here t is a SOURCE-FLAG coupling. It is not the knot meridian, the classical longitude, or a quantum-A spectral coordinate. The literal constant-frame ket psi_s=sum_k w_(k,s) is made from actual divided powers as ψs=∑kβk,s[s+kNkN]qF(kN)us. \psi_s=\sum_k\frac{\beta_{k,s}}{{s+kN\brack kN}_q} F^{(kN)}u_s. \tag{2} Every denominator is a unit at q_N: its root value is (-1)^(ks). The homogeneous dual is also an actual divided-power row: λ̃s(q;c,d)=∑kϵskβk,scN−kdkus*E(kN). \widetilde\lambda_s(q;c,d)= \sum_k\epsilon_s^k\beta_{k,s}c^{N-k}d^k u_s^*E^{(kN)}. Its value on w_(k,s) is A_(k,s)c(N-k)dk. Thus its pairing with the ket is the homogeneous polynomial Bs(q;c,d)=∑kAk,s(q)cN−kdk,Bs(q;1,t)=Ps(q,t). B_s(q;c,d)=\sum_k A_{k,s}(q)c^{N-k}d^k, \quad B_s(q;1,t)=P_s(q,t). \tag{3} Normalize by this ORIGINAL pairing unit on its marked chart. At c=d=1 the normalized dual has coefficients A_k/P_s(q,1). The root value P_s(q_N,1)=2^N is retained, not set to one before normalization. Scaling the ket by alpha^N and the dual inversely retains the unprojectivized source line.

2. Exact trace and norm on the root cover

Let zeta_N be the marked N-th root of unity, independent of the variable q. Work on the Kummer cover x^N=epsilon_s t and set Πs(q,x)=∏r=0Ls−1(1+qLs−1−2rx). \Pi_s(q,x)=\prod_{r=0}^{L_s-1} (1+q^{L_s-1-2r}x). Multiplication of the factors gives the symmetric Gaussian identity Pi_s=sum_j {L_s choose j}_q x^j. Averaging the deck transformations x->zeta_N x kills exactly the degrees not divisible by N. Hence Ps(q,t)=1NTr⁡xN=ϵstΠs(q,x). \boxed{\ P_s(q,t)=\frac1N\operatorname{Tr}_{x^N=\epsilon_st} \Pi_s(q,x).\ } \tag{4} This is an exact polynomial identity before normal specialization. It is valid even at t=0 as an identity of coefficients; the cover is etale on t!=0. The 1/N is part of the source construction.

The full norm is equally explicit. Put Z=QN=q(2N) and theta_s=(-1)^s q1. The elementary deck product gives 𝒩s(q,t):=Norm⁡Πs=∏r=0Ls−1(1+θsZ−rt). \boxed{\ \mathcal N_s(q,t):=\operatorname{Norm}\Pi_s =\prod_{r=0}^{L_s-1}(1+\theta_s Z^{-r}t).\ } \tag{5} Indeed Norm(1+a x)=1-(-a)^N epsilon_s t, and (-1)^N epsilon_s=-(-1)^s. No peripheral sign or central power has been removed in (5). This is a finite factorial in the WHOLE power coordinate Z, distinct from its value Z=1.

On a marked cover let nu_s^N=mathcal N_s, with nu_s=1 at t=0. Then c_s=Pi_s/nu_s has norm one and Ps=νsTr⁡csN. P_s=\nu_s\,\frac{\operatorname{Tr}c_s}{N}. \tag{6} Thus the source polynomial is a selected trace in a concrete norm torsor. A norm-one statement about c_s does not by itself give the trace in (6); both are specified here. Zeros of the norm or trace remain part of the lattice/domain data.

There is an exact power-parameter law for the SAME source product. Put q_hat=-q^N, so q_hat^2=Z and q_hat=1 at the marked root. Since L_s-1 has parity s, (5) becomes Norm⁡Πs(q,x)=∏r=0Ls−1(1+q̂Ls−1−2rt)=∑j=0Ls[Lsj]q̂tj. \operatorname{Norm}\Pi_s(q,x) =\prod_{r=0}^{L_s-1}(1+\widehat q^{L_s-1-2r}t) =\sum_{j=0}^{L_s}{L_s\brack j}_{\widehat q}t^j. \tag{6a} Thus the Kummer norm is the identical finite Gaussian product at the power parameter -q^N. Its chosen root restriction is a classical binomial product. This is an algebraic identity; it does not assert a modular-group action or equate different knot states.

The norm and selected trace also retain different normal data. Write v=Log Z with v=0 at the marked root. Pairing the symmetric exponents in (6a) gives [∂vlog⁡𝒩s]0=0,[v2]log⁡𝒩s=tLs(Ls2−1)24(1+t)2. [\partial_v\log\mathcal N_s]_0=0,\qquad [v^2]\log\mathcal N_s =\frac{t L_s(L_s^2-1)}{24(1+t)^2}. \tag{6b} Indeed their sum is zero and their squared sum is L_s(L_s^2-1)/12. The source trace in (9) generally has a nonzero FIRST normal on middle residue strings. That information is in its specified cyclic trace, even though the norm’s first derivative vanishes.

3. Its actual root restriction and normalization

Write L_s=N^2+b_s, b_s=N-1-s. At q_N every block of N factors in Pi_s contributes EXACTLY 1+t: the sign from q_N^N=-1 cancels epsilon_s as in (1). There are N full blocks. The remaining polynomial has degree b_s<N, so its deck average is one. Therefore Ps(qN,t)=(1+t)N,Ak,s(qN)=(Nk). P_s(q_N,t)=(1+t)^N,\qquad A_{k,s}(q_N)={N\choose k}. \tag{7} One can also verify (7) by cancelling the root zero factors in the Gaussian product. Its symmetric phase is (-1)2, which epsilon_s^k cancels for every s and every parity of N.

Equations (5)–(6) keep a useful fractional head correction: 𝒩s(qN,t)=(1+t)Ls,νs(qN,t)=(1+t)N+bs/N,Tr⁡cs(qN)N=(1+t)−bs/N. \mathcal N_s(q_N,t)=(1+t)^{L_s},\quad \nu_s(q_N,t)=(1+t)^{N+b_s/N},\quad \frac{\operatorname{Tr}c_s(q_N)}N=(1+t)^{-b_s/N}. \tag{8} All branches have the same mark at t=0. The product is (7). Dropping either factor would change the source character. In particular the original selected dual gives the endpoint E_(N,0) the value 2^(-N), with the full N-th power still visible.

4. First normal equals an explicit flag connection

Use delta=1-Q^N and q=q_N(1-delta)^(1/(2N)). Set ℓs=−12∑a=1sqNa+qN−aqNa−qN−a. \ell_s=-\frac12\sum_{a=1}^{s} \frac{q_N^a+q_N^{-a}}{q_N^a-q_N^{-a}}. Then the actual Gaussian polynomial has the exact first derivative [∂δAk,s]0=kℓs(Nk),[∂δPs]0=ℓsNt(1+t)N−1. [\partial_\delta A_{k,s}]_0=k\ell_s{N\choose k},\qquad [\partial_\delta P_s]_0=\ell_s N t(1+t)^{N-1}. \tag{9} Here is a direct cancellation proof. Split the symmetric Gaussian product into arguments divisible by N and the remaining residues. Ratios of the vanishing q-integers have zero first relative logarithmic derivative: after their simple zeros cancel they are ratios of odd sinh functions. For each of the k nonzero residue blocks, cyclic reindexing leaves N sum_(a<=b_s) cot_q(a) in the q-log derivative. Pairing residues a and N-a makes that sum equal N sum_(a<=s) cot_q(a). Finally q partial_q=-2N partial_delta at the root. This gives (9), including its sign.

At the normalized selected point t=1, the dual’s first derivative is [∂δλs(wk)]0=2−N(Nk)ℓs(k−N/2). [\partial_\delta\lambda_s(w_k)]_0 =2^{-N}{N\choose k}\ell_s(k-N/2). \tag{10} More generally it is ell_s t partial_t of the normalized root dual. Thus the first normal is a concrete source-flag transport. The same ell_s is the original head connection in the whole Jackson current. It vanishes for s=0,N-1; middle strings retain it. Higher normals remain those of the WHOLE polynomial (4), with no assertion that they are all a single flag reparameterization.
