A full-source lattice initializer on the lower half-charge cube

Private written proof, 16 November 2026.

The original zero-power source has a nonzero geometric asymptotic on every compact subset of the lattice charge cube (0,1/2]S(0,1/2]^S. The high Newton sectors have a uniform strict gap, including high boundary edges and both level parities. The proof contracts their actual radical normals first, and then bounds the remaining charged low source with its shared indices intact. It does not use positivity of the charged closing functional.

The archived radical contraction and path comparison already identify physical cut sources. The additional estimate here uses the convex zero-power height minimum to bound their charge cost. The all-low Fourier proof is extended below only at actual lattice charges; no off-lattice complex-charge initializer is asserted.
