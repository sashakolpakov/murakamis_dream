An explicit corridor factor for the quartic Conway minimum

Research proof, 23 November 2026.

The original quartic minimum differs from an explicit multiple of the original squared source by an exponentially small relative error. The factor uses only the known closing row and the turns above the highest product. No unknown internal response appears in it. This does not evaluate the minimum or establish its geometric rate.

1. The original minimum and the known factor

Use the full normalized Conway compiler of [C], with its prescribed leaves, products, turns and original closing. At every level N≥2N\ge2, all packets belong to the full Newton space, including its Hermite directions. Put

σ=116,E(x)=∑v∥Φv(xchildren)−xv∥2+|Lxtop|2,e*=min∥xv∥≤1E(x).(1) \sigma=\frac1{16},\qquad E(x)=\sum_v\|\Phi_v(x_{\mathrm{ children}})-x_v\|^2 +|Lx_{\mathrm{ top}}|^2, \qquad e_* =\min_{\|x_v\|\le1}E(x). \qquad\text{(1)}

∥av∥≤σ,k=Latop=KN/Btot,L=χλε/bρ,ε∈{0,N−1}.(2) \|a_v\|\le\sigma,\qquad k=La_{\mathrm{ top}}=K_N/B_{\mathrm{ tot}},\qquad L=\chi\lambda_\varepsilon/b_\rho, \quad \varepsilon\in\{0,N-1\}. \qquad\text{(2)}

2. Exact elimination of the final linear corridor

Keep every packet below v*v_* as a variable uu. For fixed feasible uu, put f=Φv*(uL,uR)f=\Phi_{v_*}(u_L,u_R). Write y0y_0 for the output of v*v_*, and y1,…,ymy_1,\ldots,y_m for the turn outputs. The part of (1) involving these variables is exactly

∥y0−f∥2+∑i=1m∥yi−Aiyi−1∥2+|Lym|2.(11) \|y_0-f\|^2+\sum_{i=1}^m\|y_i-A_i y_{i-1}\|^2 +|Ly_m|^2. \qquad\text{(11)}

The unconstrained least-squares minimum and its residuals are

The original corridor ball constraints do not alter this minimum. Indeed, with feasible uu fixed, (11) is a strictly positive quadratic in the corridor packets. Its half-gradient step is the corresponding component of the full step of [C]. That step maps every corridor packet ball into its 33/25633/256 ball and contracts by at most 1/21/2. Its fixed point is therefore feasible, interior and stationary for the unconstrained quadratic. It is the unique minimum in (13).

R(ua)=0,DR(ua)=0,g(ua)=k/C,F(ua)=|k|2/C.(15) R(u_a)=0,\qquad DR(u_a)=0,\qquad g(u_a)=k/\sqrt C,\qquad F(u_a)=|k|^2/C. \qquad\text{(15)}

All omitted coefficients have been eliminated, rather than projected to semisimple values. Formula (13) is the familiar root-corridor least-squares formula; the quantitative improvement below concerns how much further internal minimization can lower (15).

3. Further minimization has an exponentially small relative effect

The more explicit Hessian estimate proved in [C] is

D2E≽4132I.(16) D^2E\succeq\frac{41}{32}I. \qquad\text{(16)}

The partial minimum FF inherits the same lower bound. To see this, its eliminated quadratic minimizer is smooth in uu, and its Hessian is the Schur complement of the full Hessian at that minimizer. For every variation hh,

D2F(u)[h,h]=minjD2E(u,y*(u))[(h,j),(h,j)]≥4132∥h∥2.(17) D^2F(u)[h,h] =\min_j D^2E(u,y_*(u))[(h,j),(h,j)] \ge\frac{41}{32}\|h\|^2. \qquad\text{(17)}

The smooth formula extends to the closed feasible balls. Thus the strong-convexity minorant, minimized over all real variations, gives

e*≥F(ua)−1641∥∇F(ua)∥2.(18) e_*\ge F(u_a)-\frac{16}{41}\|\nabla F(u_a)\|^2. \qquad\text{(18)}

Only the two immediate inputs of v*v_* occur in gg. At the true field both have norm at most σ\sigma. Therefore, in the original coefficient metric,

∥Dg(ua)∥2≤∥B∥2C(∥(ua)L∥2+∥(ua)R∥2)≤2σ2ζ2C.(19) \|Dg(u_a)\|^2 \le\frac{\|B\|^2}{C} (\|(u_a)_L\|^2+\|(u_a)_R\|^2) \le\frac{2\sigma^2\zeta^2}{C}. \qquad\text{(19)}

The derivatives and gradients in (16)–(19) are real derivatives for Re⁡∑xv*yv\operatorname{Re}\sum x_v^*y_v. A complex-linear scalar row has the same operator norm in these real coordinates. Using (15),

∥∇F(ua)∥2=∥2(Dg(ua))*g(ua)∥2≤8σ2ζ2CF(ua).(20) \|\nabla F(u_a)\|^2 =\|2(Dg(u_a))^*g(u_a)\|^2 \le\frac{8\sigma^2\zeta^2}{C}F(u_a). \qquad\text{(20)}

4. Prescribed leaves and smaller quartics

Suppose the feasible set for (14) is further restricted to a set containing uau_a, and let ecle_{\mathrm{ cl}} be its minimum. Then the same comparison holds:

(1−ζ282C)|k|2C≤e*≤ecl≤|k|2C.(24) \left(1-\frac{\zeta^2}{82C}\right)\frac{|k|^2}{C} \le e_*\le e_{\mathrm{ cl}}\le\frac{|k|^2}{C}. \qquad\text{(24)}

Indeed restriction gives the middle inequality, and the feasible competitor uau_a gives the last one. Exact elimination of any remaining linear variables preserves that restricted minimum.

This includes the prescribed-leaf restriction in [B]: the fixed inputs are the known leaf packets transported through their turn corridors, and the true field obeys those restrictions. Eliminating the final corridor at the same time is feasible, because none of its packets is one of those fixed inputs. Thus (24) applies to the smaller quartic on the connections between binary products, with its original budgets. No unknown binary output is fixed in making this restriction.

Consequently CBtot2eclC B_{\mathrm{ tot}}^2e_{\mathrm{ cl}} also obeys (10a). This asserts the stated comparison, rather than equality between the unrestricted and restricted numerical minima.

5. What has and has not been reduced

The leading comparison factor CC is an explicit finite sum of known corridor budgets. It requires neither the full forward field nor its backward response. All other internal optimization can change the corresponding value only by the relative amount in (8). The proof may use the true field as a competitor without inserting that unknown field into the definition of CC, EE, or the original source.

This improves the fixed constant source comparison of [C] and removes the unknown response coefficient from the leading comparison in [S]. It does not turn evaluation of e*e_* into a scalar secular equation. The coherent value or a positive geometric lower witness for that minimum is still required. The original marking, scalar χ\chi, budget product, and every physical Hermite direction remain present. No finite-exception theorem or new filling class follows.

The literal product/turn tree and its original endpoint closing are essential. General precomposed unary maps or shared descendants need their own estimates; none is silently treated as a unitary turn.
