Actual Fourier polarization gives a nonzero geometric normal block

Research theorem, 27 October 2026. Fourier-transforming the SECOND physical cut, while retaining the original first-cut rank-one row, gives an exact new partial family for the original root state. Its specified proper finite-array lift has a nonzero geometric formal Gaussian block. The two external coordinates are the first physical position and the second Fourier label. They correspond to (A,p_e), which is birational on the complete discrete-faithful framing torsor.

The displayed source is the ACTUAL finite sum over both external labels. A subsequent root Ward reduction fixes the first input and retains only the Fourier-label sum, as described below. Neither source is the residue eta=0 of the old band variable. No selected global geometric contribution or volume conjecture follows from the existence of this local block.

1. Exact finite polarization and the unchanged selected source

Use the literal four-port tensor T_(c,d;a,b) from the two physical cuts. All original pivots, caps, Cartan/framing lines, meridian marks and the full peripheral factor stay in T or its recorded external line. The second cut is an identity wire, with no new pivot. At level N put Q_N=exp(2pi*i/N) and

V_(j,b)=Q_N^(jb), (V^-1)_(b,j)=Q_N^(-jb)/N,
T^V=(I tensor V)T(I tensor V^-1).

Keep the ORIGINAL first-cut row: define the RAW partial array

Jtilde_(a,j)=sum_c T^V_(c,j;a,j).             (1)

Then finite basis invariance of the identity closure proves EXACTLY

P_N(M)=(1/N)sum_(a,j=0)^(N-1) Jtilde_(a,j). (2)

The first factor 1/N is the original cut average. The inverse Fourier matrix contributes another 1/N INSIDE Jtilde. Neither is silently removed or treated as a second physical pivotal trace. Here P_N denotes the originally selected scalar with its chosen reduced/full external line; the original pointing at completeness and every external d_N factor are unchanged on the two sides. For a conserving tangle c+d=a+b, put q=d-b. Formula (1) becomes

Jtilde_(a,j)=(1/N)sum_(b,q) Q_N^(jq)
                          T_(a-q,b+q;a,b).    (3)

All four original head bounds are retained by zero extension. Crucially c is summed in (1), not set to a. A double diagonal at BOTH cuts would impose q=0 and lose the new Fourier label.

The whole Vandermonde construction retains the actual generic inverse, source, normal connection and endpoint carries. For the stationary construction here specify also the proper ROOT LIFT obtained from (3) by using Q^(jq)/N off root. It equals (1) at every original root. Off root this kernel is generally DIFFERENT from V(Q)^-1, and its total external sum need not equal the original whole q-germ. This distinction is retained; no equality of their higher q-normal source jets is inferred.

2. The open phase is made from the original finite coefficients

Let the braid have m crossings. The old two-position array has external labels (a,b) and r=m-1 remaining cycle flows u. Opening the two ports with balanced offset (-q,+q) adds one boundary-flow parameter. A spanning tree in the connected remaining graph gives an integral particular flow linear in q; the same internal cycle coordinates u remain. No lattice index or hidden determinant occurs.

Every original position/transfer is consequently an integral affine function of (a,b,q,u). Substitute these functions into the original finite factorial/Cartan coefficients. Their compact formal expansion therefore defines a genuine open phase germ

S_open(a_log,b_log,t,u_log), t=Hq,
a_log=Ha, b_log=Hb, H=Log Q,

and the actual nonzero amplitude with all half-step and source lines. The variable compact arguments must stay in the established regular shape chart. At t=0 this is exactly the old closed coefficient model. This construction uses its WHOLE finite-product expressions, not an unjustified summation of fixed-offset asymptotic formulas.

The actual boundary proper-ratio calculation in the two-cut theorem gives on the closed geometric critical surface

partial_t S_open=Log p_e.                     (4)

The balanced exchange lowers the first output and raises the second; the first-cut wave momentum is one in its original constant boundary frame. Hence (4) has the stated sign and contains the original factorial-boundary adapter. Flattened logarithms and peripheral signs are those of the original marked critical correspondence.

Put v=Hj. The proper lift (3) has the exact additional monomial

Q^(jq)=exp(vt/H),
Phi=S_open+vt.                                (5)

The new external variables are (a_log,v). Its internal variables are x=(b_log,u_log), together with t, of dimension m+1=r+2.

3. A complete discrete-faithful framing torsor has birational (A,p_e)

The following proof supplies a nonempty regular polarization chart for EVERY complete discrete-faithful knot representation in the actual geometric model. It does not assert this for arbitrary reducible or nondiscrete representation components.

Normalize the cut meridian J=[[1,1],[0,1]] and retain F(r,s)=[[r,1-r],[s,-s]], s nonzero, A=1/(1+s). For the chosen actual physical second edge retain its developed PREFIX words U,V. The actual inverse clock is

k=ell_U/ell_V,
ell_W=d_W+(a_W-d_W)r+b_Ws+c_W r(1-r)/s.        (6)

An independent second physical position exists by the actual two-position theorem, so k depends nontrivially on r. Here U,V are group words, UV^-1 is a nontrivial parabolic meridian, and det U=det V=1. Let t_W=Tr W, h_W=a_W-d_W and sigma=(-1)^ell for its actual prefix strand position ell; the Fourier sample label is still j. The exact prefix/momentum identities give, with u_p=p+p^-1 and v_p=p^-1-p,

A_0=c_U+s t_U, A_1=c_V+s t_V,
u_p=sigma(A_0-kA_1)/[s(k-1)],
D=-sigma s(k-1)v_p,
D=c_C+s h_C-2c_Cr, C=U-kV.                  (7)

First, every UPPER TRIANGULAR word W of this discrete torsion-free projective group has diagonal entries a,a^-1 with a^2=1, hence h_W=0. Indeed if |a| differs from one, suitable conjugates W^n J W^-n are nontrivial translations converging to the identity. If |a|=1 and a^2 has infinite order, choose a^(2n) tending to one; then W^n J W^-n J^-1 is a nontrivial translation tending to the identity. Both contradict discreteness. If a^2 has finite nontrivial order, the upper triangular W is diagonalizable with projectively finite order, contradicting torsion-freeness. This proves the assertion without a stabilizer classification theorem.

Suppose p were independent of r. Since k_r is nonzero, differentiating the Mobius expression in (7) forces A_0=A_1 as functions of s. Thus c_U=c_V and t_U=t_V. Now C_0=U-V has c=0, trace zero and determinant zero, so C_0=b E_12. It is nonzero because UV^-1 is a nontrivial meridian. Equality of the two determinants forces b c_V=0, hence c_U=c_V=0. Both U,V are upper triangular; the previous paragraph gives h_U=h_V=0. Equation (6) then makes k independent of r, a contradiction. Therefore (A,p) is etale on a nonempty open of the complete geometric framing torsor.

The same upper-word argument shows c_U,c_V are not both zero. On the open set with the displayed denominators units, (7) has the explicit rational inverse

s=(1-A)/A,
k=(s u_p+sigma A_0)/(s u_p+sigma A_1),
C=U-kV, D=-sigma s(k-1)v_p,
r=(c_C+s h_C-D)/(2c_C).                       (8)

Thus the function field of the framing torsor equals its field in (A,p): no new framing cover is introduced by this polarization. Any existing marked representation, SL2-sign or half-density cover is still retained. This is a birational assertion on the indicated open sets, not a global compactification or cycle identification.

At a generic old position-projection caustic p=+1 or -1, k_p=0 but differentiating (8) gives

r_p=-sigma s(k-1)/c_C !=0.                    (9)

Thus the new chart resolves those old two-position caustics when the other displayed units and original shape chart remain regular. The exact geometric two-form is

Omega_cut=-ell_V(k-1)/(A_1-A_0)
                          dlog A wedge dlog p. (10)

This is the prefix density in its new rational coordinates, not a claim about the old finite band cap variable. It is regular at the generic caustics in (9).

4. The ACTUAL new stationary block is nondegenerate

At t=0 let x=(b_log,u_log). Fixing the first physical position in the old geometric Morse–Bott model leaves one framing tangent xi. Hence H_0=partial_x^2 S_open has rank r and radical span(xi). Let c=partial_x partial_t S_open and gamma=partial_t^2 S_open. Differentiating (4) along the critical framing gives

mu=c dot xi=dlog p_e(xi),

which is nonzero precisely on the new etale polarization chart. The internal Hessian of (5) is the actual bordered matrix

H_new=[[H_0,c],[c^t,gamma]].                   (11)

It is invertible. If H_new(delta_x,delta_t)=0, pairing its first row with xi gives mu delta_t=0, hence delta_t=0. Then delta_x lies in the old radical, and the last equation gives delta_x=0. No invertibility of an old two-position transverse minor is used. Thus this proof also applies where that old minor is singular.

For each new external pair with

eta=exp(v), eta p_e=1,

the old closed geometric point at t=0 solves all new internal critical equations. The formal implicit-function theorem for (11) makes it the UNIQUE critical germ there. In particular all the old closed equations still hold, and

Phi_critical=S_geo,                            (12)

with exactly the same marked regulator action and retained base characters: the new term vt vanishes at t=0. The comparison is now an actual Fourier partial-family critical construction, not substitution into the old band-generating cap.

The regular marked representation/gluing branch has the meridian as a local coordinate. Nonzero mu and det H_new persist in that meridian deformation germ. Thus the same construction supplies the geometric formal normal line and action S_geo(M) there. The birational function-field statement of Section 3 was specifically at completeness; it is not promoted to a global statement over every peripheral point.

On an old regular (A,B) chart, choose xi with xi_b=1 and first contract u. If H_perp is the old r-by-r transverse Hessian, its remaining Schur block in (b,t) is

[[0,mu],[mu,gamma_eff]],
det H_new=-mu^2 det H_perp.                  (13)

The formula is independent of gamma_eff. At an old projection caustic xi_b may vanish, so that normalization and old minor are not used; the direct kernel proof of (11) remains valid.

5. Fourier normalization and the remaining actual source

The amplitude at the new critical point is the SAME original nonzero coefficient amplitude at q=0. The Fourier character is one there. Equations (11)–(13) therefore give a nonzero formal normal block, with algebraic coefficients on the new chart and the original square-root/phase mark retained in its Gaussian line.

The two extra discrete sums b,q contribute their logarithmic measure H^-2, while the inverse Fourier matrix contributes 1/N. On the physical sequence H_N=2pi*i/N their product is EXACTLY

(1/N)H_N^-2=1/(2pi*i H_N).                  (14)

This is the normalized two-variable formal Fourier pairing. With the explicit convention that its cross Gaussian exp(zt/H) has pairing one, composition after the old transverse contraction gives numerical leading coefficient a_perp/mu for that declared ordered cross pairing. The oriented external two-form has an additional minus sign: v=-Log p implies db/dv=-1/mu, consistently with (10). This is a FIXED ordered- Fourier phase, not a variable source correction. Another ordered Gaussian square-root mark carries its corresponding fixed phase in the recorded base line. Neither the original physical 1/N in (2) nor any peripheral character is absorbed into an unspecified variable unit.

The resulting amplitude has the same exponential action (12); no new exponential factor is generated by the Fourier pair. This is a formal, coefficientwise statement from the original proper finite-array model. It asserts neither analytic stationary-phase asymptotics for its growing sums nor survival of this block in the final selected contraction.

In particular the CORRECT new final source is still the finite sum in (2). Its Fourier label j takes all original N head values. It has not become eta=0, a choice of one momentum boundary, or an unmarked sum over the old quadratic framing sheets. A global comparison must transport that finite source, its endpoint carries and the actual whole-V versus proper-root-lift distinction above.
