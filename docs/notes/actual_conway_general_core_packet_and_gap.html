A general quarter bound and the full core estimate for strict Conway trees

31 October 2026. research proof. The assertions concern every fixed literal strict cylindrical tree, not only the completed grammar of the published manuscript. The alias part of the original invariant remains separate. No change to that manuscript’s theorem is made here.

1. Exact scope and inputs

Let the original closed expression be C(FG)C(FG), where each of F,GF,G is any finite expression in the physical leaf hh, the actual turn TT, and ordered product. Delete only its forced final gate, whose coefficient is one; retain the exact relation rF+rG=N−1r_F+r_G=N-1. Assume the reduced positive wire polytope has a strict state. Thus all original leaf labels are in (0,1/2)(0,1/2), and every retained gate has max⁡(x,y)<z<x+y<1\max(x,y)<z<x+y<1. Write H≥2H\ge2 for the number of leaves.

The exact compiler, boundary barrier and angle-map proof in the exact source compiler, §§1–3, apply to this arbitrary strict syntax. They give a primitive lattice of dimension K=2H−3K=2H-3, a compact reduced polytope PP, and a unique strict complete maximizer y*y_*. All wire labels are affine in the primitive variables; their finite versions include the original fixed integer offsets from d=N−1d=N-1. Set A(y)=∑i=1Hg(ai(y))+∑v=1H−2m(xv(y),yv(y),zv(y)),F0=A(y*), A(y)=\sum_{i=1}^{H}g(a_i(y))+\sum_{v=1}^{H-2}m(x_v(y),y_v(y),z_v(y)), \qquad F_0=A(y_*), where g(a)=J(2a)−4J(a)g(a)=J(2a)-4J(a) and J(u)=∫0ulog⁡(2sin⁡πt)dtJ(u)=\int_0^u\log(2\sin\pi t)\,dt.

Each original leaf has a fixed physical sign ϵi∈{0,±1}\epsilon_i\in\{0,\pm1\}. At every physical color its exact core/alias split is performed in the original full Hermite–Newton algebra, before any products or turns. Let 𝒜Ncore(𝒉)\mathcal A^{\mathrm{core}}_N(\mathbf h) be the literal scalar with every leaf replaced by its core. This is a summand of the actual source, not a new definition of the knot invariant. A core is zero if its color deficit exceeds the original low-core range.

The additional exact inputs are the port types and product equations of the strict-mask geometry, the high and quarter finite-source packet theorems, and the uniform all-color coefficient bounds proved in the uniform all-color proof. In particular the global core bound is exp⁡N(g̃+o(1))\exp N(\widetilde g+o(1)) with uniform o(1)o(1), not a global polynomial-prefactor estimate.

2. Every complete closed-tree leaf is at or above one quarter

For an optimized open port put B=cot⁡(πb),κ=e−f′(b),X2=1+B21+κ,Y2=κX2,X,Y>0. B=\cot(\pi b),\quad \kappa=e^{-f'(b)},\quad X^2=\frac{1+B^2}{1+\kappa},\quad Y^2=\kappa X^2,\quad X,Y>0. Then X2+Y2−B2=1X^2+Y^2-B^2=1. The exact strict types are L:(B,X,Y)=(B,B,1),H:(B,X,Y)=(−Y,1,Y),A:−Y<B<X. L:(B,X,Y)=(B,B,1),\quad H:(B,X,Y)=(-Y,1,Y),\quad A:-Y<B<X. A turn exchanges X,YX,Y and negates BB. A product has a common child XX, S=B1+B2>0S=B_1+B_2>0, and Bo=B1B2+X2−1S,Yo=Y1Y2S. B_o=\frac{B_1B_2+X^2-1}{S},\qquad Y_o=\frac{Y_1Y_2}{S}.

At the cylindrical root the two BB’s are opposite and their ordered metrics agree, so their X,YX,Y agree. In type AAAA, |B|<min⁡(X,Y)|B|<\min(X,Y), whence the quadratic identity gives X,Y<1X,Y<1. In ALAL, the LL port has Y=1Y=1, and the AA inequality −1<−X-1<-X gives X<1X<1. In AHAH, the HH port has X=1X=1, and the AA inequality Y<1Y<1 gives the other bound. In LHLH the explicit forms force X=Y=1X=Y=1. These exhaust all strict root pairs.

For the downward product step set c=1−X2c=1-X^2. The identity (B12+c)(B22+c)−(B1B2−c)2=c(B1+B2)2 (B_1^2+c)(B_2^2+c)-(B_1B_2-c)^2=c(B_1+B_2)^2 gives Yo2−Bo2=1−X2Y_o^2-B_o^2=1-X^2, hence Xo=XX_o=X. Every child type has Bi≤XB_i\le X; positivity of SS gives Bi>−Bj≥−XB_i>-B_j\ge-X. Consequently Yi2=1+Bi2−X2≤1Y_i^2=1+B_i^2-X^2\le1. Starting with the root bounds, products and turns therefore give X,Y≤1X,Y\le1 at every port. At an original leaf, X=cot⁡πaX=\cot\pi a, so 1/4≤ai(y*)<1/2. \boxed{\quad 1/4\le a_i(y_*)<1/2.\quad} \tag{1} Equality is allowed. It is a regular angle state, even though the two source recurrence roots there have equal modulus. The quarter packet theorem treats that boundary using the actual finite source anchor.

3. Gate curvature controls every primitive direction

Let V(a,b,c)=−π[J(a)+J(b)+J(c)]V(a,b,c)=-\pi[J(a)+J(b)+J(c)], a+b+c=1a+b+c=1, be the ideal tetrahedron volume. Its Hessian is strictly negative on nonzero tangent vectors of the positive angle simplex. For a strict gate set p=z−xp=z-x, q=z−yq=z-y, r=x+y−zr=x+y-z, w=1−x−yw=1-x-y. The exact identity is πm(x,y,z)=V(y,x,w)+V(p,q,2r+w)+V(p+q,r,r+w). \pi m(x,y,z)=V(y,x,w)+V(p,q,2r+w)+V(p+q,r,r+w). \tag{2} All three triples are positive and sum to one. If a tangent has zero quadratic form in the first atom, then δx=δy=0\delta x=\delta y=0. Zero quadratic form in the second then forces δz=0\delta z=0. The third atom has nonpositive Hessian and cannot undo this conclusion. Thus each strict gate has a negative-definite Hessian in its three wire variables, including when one input is above one half.

For H≥3H\ge3, the collection of all retained gate-label maps is injective on the primitive tangent space. To see this, contract the unary turn chains, retaining their affine complements. Every leaf or product output in a nontrivial root subtree occurs at a retained gate. Its variation is therefore zero if all gate variations are zero. The only possible uncovered wire is a root subtree consisting of one leaf and turns; the other subtree is nontrivial, and the root complementary relation sets that leaf variation to zero as well. Both subtrees can be single leaves only when H=2H=2.

It follows that the sum of the retained gate Hessians is negative definite on the full primitive space whenever H≥3H\ge3. This uses the actual wire graph and its unit closing relation; it does not add a spurious root gate or assume leaf curvature where it has disappeared.

4. The all-core majorant has the same unique geometric maximum

Put g̃(a)=g(max⁡(a,1/4)),g‾ϵ(a)={g(a),ϵ=0,g̃(a),ϵ=±1, \widetilde g(a)=g(\max(a,1/4)),\qquad \bar g_{\epsilon}(a)= \begin{cases}g(a),&\epsilon=0,\\ \widetilde g(a),&\epsilon=\pm1, \end{cases} and define on the original closed polytope A‾ϵ(y)=∑ig‾ϵi(ai(y))+∑vm(xv(y),yv(y),zv(y)). \bar A_\epsilon(y)=\sum_i\bar g_{\epsilon_i}(a_i(y))+ \sum_v m(x_v(y),y_v(y),z_v(y)). \tag{3} Both leaf functions are concave. The plateau extension is continuously differentiable at 1/41/4, because g′(a)=2log⁡cot⁡πag'(a)=2\log\cot\pi a vanishes there. By (1), every leaf has the same value and first derivative at y*y_* in (3) as in the complete action. Therefore A‾ϵ(y*)=F0\bar A_\epsilon(y_*)=F_0 and dA‾ϵ(y*)=0d\bar A_\epsilon(y_*)=0. Concavity gives A‾ϵ≤F0\bar A_\epsilon\le F_0 on all of PP.

For H≥3H\ge3, the gate curvature from §3 gives a strictly quadratic local loss. Indeed, in a small compact strict neighborhood the sum of gate terms lies below its tangent by c∥y−y*∥2c\|y-y_*\|^2, c>0c>0, and every leaf term lies below its tangent by concavity. The tangent linear terms cancel by stationarity. Thus A‾ϵ(y)≤F0−c∥y−y*∥2 \bar A_\epsilon(y)\le F_0-c\|y-y_*\|^2 \tag{4} there, without differentiating the plateau twice. A second global maximum would give a constant segment to y*y_*, contradicting (4). Compactness therefore gives a strictly positive gap outside any fixed neighborhood of y*y_*.

For H=2H=2, at least one pure leaf gives the same strictness: after the turns and the strict root relation both original leaf coordinates are one common aa, and the majorant is either 2g(a)2g(a) or g(a)+g̃(a)g(a)+\widetilde g(a), uniquely maximized at a=1/4a=1/4. If both leaves are odd, the majorant is 2g̃(a)2\widetilde g(a) and is flat for a≤1/4a\le1/4. This case is excluded from this core theorem. It has two main components: both odd leaves have endpoint pairing XX, turns preserve XX, and XX=IX X=I closes to two components. Thus every strict two-leaf knot pattern has a pure leaf and is included.

5. Uniform estimates for the literal complete core sum

Fix a small rectangular primitive window BB around y*y_*, with closure in the strict polytope. In exact finite coordinates its wires are rw=NLw(k/N)+δwr_w=NL_w(k/N)+\delta_w, with the original integer offsets. Choose 0<β<1/20<\beta<1/2 larger than every leaf label on this window.

For every physical core color, the global coefficient estimate is |cN,h,rϵ|≤exp⁡N{g‾ϵ(r/N)+eN},eN→0, |c^{\epsilon}_{N,h,r}|\le \exp N\{\bar g_\epsilon(r/N)+e_N\},\qquad e_N\longrightarrow0, \tag{5} uniformly in h,rh,r. The pure case follows from its exact factorial formula and the positive color deficit. The signed case is the collision-safe frozen-block bound, including its terminal source and singular lower endpoint. Gate factorial errors are uniformly O(log⁡N)O(\log N). Fixed affine offsets change continuum rates uniformly by o(1)o(1), even at feasible endpoints, by uniform continuity of JJ on its closed interval.

There are polynomially many states for the fixed tree. Apply the strict compact gap in §4 to (5), then absorb the finitely many eNe_N’s and uniform offset errors into half that gap. The contribution of all core states outside BB is therefore O(NCeN(F0−γ)), O\!\left(N^C e^{N(F_0-\gamma)}\right), \tag{6} uniformly over the whole physical color cube. This bound concerns an absolute sum of those states; no cancellation or discarded channel is needed to obtain it.

For the endpoint comparison, first divide every exact finite wire by d=N−1d=N-1; its full array then lies in PP exactly. Its r/Nr/N coordinates differ by O(1/N)O(1/N), and uniform continuity compares the rates at two admissible arrays. No JJ is evaluated beyond its closed domain. Shrinking or enlarging the fixed window by O(1/N)O(1/N) preserves the same positive outside gap.

Inside BB, the signed recurrence’s strict far-color estimate applies for r≤βNr\le\beta N, including r/N=1/4r/N=1/4. Its proof uses a strict spectral interval above max⁡(β,1/4)\max(\beta,1/4), which every backward path crosses. Hence if one physical deficit is at least δN\delta N, for fixed δ>0\delta>0, its rate loses a uniform positive amount relative to g̃\widetilde g. For a pure leaf the exact loss is Δ(a,t)=J(a−t)+J(a+t)−2J(a)>0(0<t≤a<1/2), \Delta(a,t)=J(a-t)+J(a+t)-2J(a)>0 \quad(0<t\le a<1/2), or the core coefficient is zero when h>rh>r. Compactness of the window and the fixed lower bound on tt give the pure uniform loss too. Together with (6) this proves max∃i:hi>δN|𝒜Ncore(𝒉)|≤NCeN(F0−γδ). \max_{\exists i:\,h_i>\delta N} |\mathcal A^{\mathrm{core}}_N(\mathbf h)| \le N^C e^{N(F_0-\gamma_\delta)}. \tag{7} Colors outside the core’s original support contribute zero, not an uncontrolled analytic continuation.

6. The exact local packet and the remaining alias obligation

On BB, continue only the paired color factorials of the exact finite core coefficients. This gives the finite even holomorphic window 𝒞N(𝝌)\mathcal C_N(\boldsymbol\chi), after removal of the known unit phase ηN=exp⁡(iπ(N−1)∑iϵi/4)\eta_N=\exp(i\pi(N-1)\sum_i\epsilon_i/4). By (1), each leaf lies in a high-index neighborhood or in the proved quarter neighborhood. Use the corresponding exponentially accurate actual-source index interpolant. Their errors, summed over polynomially many window states, are poly⁡(N)eN(F0+C∥χ∥2−c0)\operatorname{poly}(N)e^{N(F_0+C\|\chi\|^2-c_0)}, so shrinking the fixed color polydisk makes them negligible before any complex saddle.

The interpolated phase is the sum of the selected fϵif_{\epsilon_i} and the unchanged gate actions. Its complete wire Hessian is invertible. The original finite cotangent rectangles and Gaussian calculation apply in the same primitive coordinates, retaining all offsets and boundary currents. There are HH leaf factors of order N1/2N^{1/2}, H−2H-2 gate factors of order N−3/2N^{-3/2}, and K=2H−3K=2H-3 Gaussian variables. Consequently 𝒞N(χ)=N3/2aϵ(χ)eNΦϵ(χ)(1+O(N−1)),aϵ(0)≠0. \mathcal C_N(\chi)=N^{3/2}a_\epsilon(\chi)e^{N\Phi_\epsilon(\chi)} (1+O(N^{-1})),\qquad a_\epsilon(0)\ne0. \tag{8} All square roots, offsets and chirps are inherited from the source. At completeness the signed/pure amplitude ratio is ∏ϵi≠0sin⁡πaie−iϵiπai/2\prod_{\epsilon_i\ne0}\sqrt{\sin\pi a_i}\,e^{-i\epsilon_i\pi a_i/2}, which is nonzero also at a quarter leaf. Separate evenness gives ∂i∂jΦϵ(0)=−2πci1−ϵiiciδij,ci=cot⁡πai>0. \partial_i\partial_j\Phi_\epsilon(0)= -\frac{2\pi c_i}{1-\epsilon_i i c_i}\delta_{ij},\qquad c_i=\cot\pi a_i>0. \tag{9} In particular its real part is strictly negative, giving a fixed joint complex Gaussian envelope for the exact finite window. Estimates (6)–(8) give the original all-core source comparison in the small physical color corridor and its far-color complement, at both parities and all sufficiently large levels.

The actual source is 𝒜N=𝒜Ncore+𝒜Nalias\mathcal A_N=\mathcal A_N^{\mathrm{core}}+\mathcal A_N^{\mathrm{alias}}, where the second term is the sum of all nonempty alias sectors with their original signs. Thus for every strict knot pattern the missing global source input has been reduced to supall physical colors|𝒜Nalias(𝒉)|≤NCeN(F0−γ) \sup_{\text{all physical colors}} |\mathcal A_N^{\mathrm{alias}}(\mathbf h)| \le N^C e^{N(F_0-\gamma)} \tag{10} for some fixed γ>0\gamma>0, or to an equally sufficient estimate after the exact filling pairing. A separate bound for every sector is sufficient but not logically necessary. Pointwise positive alias envelopes can fail to prove (10); their failure is not a lower bound on the actual alias.

Once (10) is proved for a class, the general marked-mask construction identifies Ui=−2πiχiU_i=-2\pi i\chi_i, Vi=−2∂iΦϵV_i=-2\partial_i\Phi_\epsilon, and the original primitive filling action with the actual knot complement volume. The proved entire Fourier–Hermite filling transfer then gives the full original-invariant volume limit for independently long fixed signed twists. This is a reduction of the remaining conjectural step, not an assertion that (10) holds for every strict tree.

scope

This argument concerns the literal all-core source for strict knot patterns. A gap for every nonempty alias sector is a separate requirement.

For the root-star family, the full-jet localization and root-character Fourier contraction supply the additional alias estimate. Arbitrary turned children still require that separate global argument. The published completed-root manuscript is unchanged by these companion research notes.
