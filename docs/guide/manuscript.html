Research guide

The Conway composition manuscript

The paper presents the volume identity for knots built from a specified class of finite Conway tangle compositions. It starts with the invariant and tangle conventions, develops the finite source and its geometric optimization, and follows the selected complex action through the crossing-circle fillings.

The theorem’s scope

Starting with a two-leaf block hhhh, completed tangles may be turned, stacked, or extended by a single leaf on either side:

E::=hh∣T(E)∣E1E2∣hE∣Eh.E::=hh\mid T(E)\mid E_1E_2\mid hE\mid Eh.

The cylindrical closure has two completed children. Choose a fixed half-twist pattern giving a knot. For every fixed finite expression, independently sufficiently long signed full twists give

limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(K)|=Vol⁡(S3\K).\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log|K_N(K)| =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K).

The expression and twists are fixed before NN tends to infinity. Both parities and composite levels are included. The threshold may depend on the expression. Earlier isolated seed closures of width at least three are treated separately.

The right side is the knot complement volume. Filling the auxiliary crossing circles inserts twists, and leaves the knot cusp unfilled. The paper does not claim coverage of every arborescent presentation.

Reading the proof

The finite source uses the full Newton algebra, including repeated-root directions. The same leaf and multiplication weights define a concave angle problem. Its unique strict optimizer supplies the complete augmented-link geometry and the local quantum action.

A separate estimate controls every alias and the physical colors away from completeness. The key composition step combines one leaf with its gate and the completed child’s output tangent. The complex packet and the full Fourier filling calculation then give a nonzero leading term. The marked peripheral equations identify its real exponent with the volume of the resulting knot complement.

Reproduction

python3 repro/verify.py --list
python3 repro/verify.py --output repro/results

The suite replays the exact two-leaf, single-leaf and endpoint certificates, and checks their stored rational results. The reproduction guide explains the arithmetic; the proof-input map distinguishes the finite checks from the analytic, geometric and topological proofs.

The older research routes remain accessible through the library. The paper provides a continuous argument for its stated composition class; the archive preserves the calculations and alternative approaches.
