Research guide

The current general frontier

The target is a theorem at every fixed bridge width, with independently long signed twists. The general volume equality remains open. Exact source formulas, the marked geometric action, and much of the local analysis are now available; the unresolved step is the global quantum period.

The class and the order of quantifiers

Fix an essential 2k2k-strand plat diagram DD, k≥2k\geq2, with a hyperbolic augmented exterior YDY_D and its actual primitive cusp markings. The general target asks for a threshold T(D)T(D) such that

mini|mi|>T(D)⇒limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(KD,𝒎)|=Vol⁡(S3\KD,𝒎).\min_i |m_i|>T(D)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log|K_N(K_{D,\mathbf m})| =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K_{D,\mathbf m}).

The diagram and every signed twist number mim_i are fixed before the quantum level NN tends to infinity. The prescribed parity pattern must give a knot. Hyperbolicity of a knot does not automatically certify every chosen augmentation.

Under the actual positive marked gluing certificate, the geometric filling theorem already supplies a geometric threshold, including independently long partial fillings. It is the quantum threshold in the displayed implication that remains unproved for general plats.

The exceptional edge now has an exact algebraic description

Its completed operator is the original normalized polynomial

This is a generic identity before root specialization, so it retains the colliding channels and their true normal derivatives. The source polynomial theorem also fixes which geometric target is required: $z=\operatorname{Tr}(B_{\rm in}^{-1}B_{\rm out})=2-4/c^2$, with the actual primitive rectangle ratio cc.

The marked-word bound proves 0<c≤10<c\leq1 for every circle in the certified complete class, including retained half twists. Otherwise that actual holonomy word would be elliptic in a freely acting complete-manifold group. Thus the actual target is at most −2-2; equality is a genuine parabolic case.

Every zero of pap_a is above 22. Its canonical logarithm selects a scalar action on the slit plane, without inventing a complex finite color. The generating-action theorem proves the general local character relation

$$R+R^{-1}=2-(2-\lambda_{\rm in})\frac{z+2}{z-2},$$

with the branch and additive scalar normalization fixed by the original source. This is a general local A-structure result. It is not yet the global knot quantum A-operator or its selected period.

The exact operator period keeps the distinction explicit:

The closing point +2+2 is distinct from the possible geometric target −2-2, which is outside the current off-spectrum Green theorem.

The geometric point lies across an exact support boundary

The actual operator kernel has a nonzero relative amplitude on its full strict physical complex tube. Its anti-diagonal entries are positive squares. But the geometric support calculation shows precisely why that result does not yet suffice: in the prescribed-pair frame, both exceptional ordinary factors have normalized total 0<Re⁡σ<1/20<\operatorname{Re}\sigma<1/2. The actual change of pairing gives a row separation δ=1−σ\delta=1-\sigma, so Re⁡δ>1/2\operatorname{Re}\delta>1/2. All the other kernel-domain inequalities hold; the bandwidth inequality fails.

This is an exact source constraint. At finite level, pa(Ω)p_a(\Omega) has bandwidth aa, and its physical entries with h>ah>a vanish, including their genuine generic derivatives. Allowing complex coordinates inside the old tube, or assembling more copies of that same packet, does not reach this geometric chamber.

Exact transport retains the boundary terms

The moving-period theorem gives two exact shift equations for the original factorial function, including their scalar normalizations. The source connection goes further: the actual white tensors and cups have an explicit rational gauge, assembled from g(z)=e2πiz/(1−e2πiz)g(z)=e^{2\pi iz}/(1-e^{2\pi iz}). After imposing the original integral head constraints, this conjugates their transport to commuting translations, with the original parity character retained. At any fixed quantum-jet order the normalized operators are unipotent. The gauge’s integer divisors remain in the source; its algebraic trivialization does not determine a global integration cycle.

The degree-cycle boundary theorem now permits a precise enlargement of the original finite-sum cycle. At a forbidden physical point both large white numerators vanish generically. Their product has enough vanishing to make the ordered cotangent residue zero, even though the continued integrand may have a pole. Such points can be added as zero periods whenever the surrounding factors are regular and the degree varies on the actual free lattice chart.

Moving this enlarged cycle requires the computed channel-pole residues and cotangent finite parts, with the actual lattice step and every other crossed divisor retained. An isolated late-wall first-normal residue, divided by the original trace normal, has coefficient ±Nsin⁡(π/N)/π\pm N\sin(\pi/N)/\pi times the root density. Its magnitude tends to one. This is a nonzero local boundary coefficient; the complete cotangent correction also involves the derivative of the actual surrounding source.

The adjoint period connection carries the same transport through the original closed cotangent integral. It differentiates the retained measure as well as GG, giving explicit double-pole corrections. A literal physical cup has a nonzero translated first-normal correction, so these identities do not make the finite physical sum periodic.

The braided two-cut identity now supplies an exact change of exposed cut at arbitrary width, including cuts at different Morse levels. If zz is the original marked scalar, JUJ^U is the two-cut array with its actual braid transport, and gg is the genuine generic pivotal vector, then

Thus either cut gives a specified functional extracting the same whole scalar. The second functional retains its braid bands and pivotal weights; its clock is the transported first-head clock. These identities therefore provide a source-preserving way to study the remaining framing direction, without asserting that both head indices can be fixed independently or that the geometric contribution survives.

The actual residual current uses the two neighboring off-diagonal bands of that transported tensor. It gives an exact finite difference equation and integration by parts on every physical subinterval, retaining both ends and all genuine quantum derivatives. At completeness its Fourier current is supported on three neighboring nodes, with coefficient equal to the unknown whole scalar zz. The conservation equation does not determine zz. Each band also has a specified meromorphic period and a change-of-cycle rule, but a common off-grid Ward identity and the pushforward of its original chain to the framing boundary remain to be established.

How far this is from the general conjecture

The main gap remains a substantial global theorem. There are two concrete tasks:

  1. Compute the framing contribution and transport the original degree and magnetic cycles to the geometric chart with a nonzero controlled coefficient, using the exact boundary corrections. The nonzero local marked density and folded Gaussian do not establish survival of the whole coherent contribution.
  2. Control the remaining physical regions and aliases uniformly in the auxiliary colors, and obtain the full source’s complex color packet, so the general long-filling transfer applies.

The Fourier route is still useful. Its exact positive partition gives a weighted-Cauchy upper bound with all flow and charge constraints retained. Its radial optimizer cannot simply be declared geometric: the radial-frame obstruction prevents assembling all strict ordinary radial saddles into the certified complete representation. No numerical rate gap follows from that obstruction.

Likewise, taking independent local projector maxima pays a genuine exponential boundary cost. The computed folded rate is 5v3/π5v_3/\pi at both parities. The joint contraction must account for that cost rather than discard it as a normalization.

Existing equality results and preserved alternatives

The results chapter states the internally reviewed equality theorems for the specified pretzel, belt-stack and signed robust Conway families. The latter permit arbitrary fixed branching and seed widths within their grammar, with independently sufficiently long fixed signed fillings. They do not exhaust arbitrary plats. The minimal isolated C(h3)C(h^3) mixed-color filling case and arbitrary short fillings remain outside the stated general filling theorem.

The source-divisor transfer can tolerate a leading density that vanishes at completeness if its analytic germ is nonzero. With the other hypotheses supplied, it gives equality outside an analytic exceptional divisor and density one of long slopes in signed boxes; a monomial times a unit gives all independently long slopes. This is still conditional for general plats.

The cube, Fourier, Karamata-inspired estimates, positive sewing and pointed torsor approaches remain in the research library. Their detailed arguments and failed intermediate estimates are preserved in the technical archive. This chapter records the current dependencies rather than requiring the reader to traverse every historical note.
