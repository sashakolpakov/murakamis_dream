The current general frontier

The target remains a volume theorem for broad classes at every fixed bridge width. The new one-end layer family now gives the full original invariant’s limit for every k≥2k\geq2, with 3k−33k-3 independently long fixed signed twist regions and exact-bridge-kk subfamilies. The wider balance theorem applies to every certified diagram passing its finite complete-packing test. The unrestricted conjecture remains open.

A phase-sensitive bound on the original regular windows

The exact source transfer has a before state, a two-state active block and an after state. Its direct before-to-after entry retains the original diagonal path i=ji=j. All prefix and suffix rows, omitted-half scalar modes and raw signs stay in the identity.

The positive active transfer contracts a nonzero outer relative phase. For a mismatched middle phase, the analytic gauge theorem leaves an exponentially small switch residual and both exact boundary terms. The regular-window theorem supplies strict action gaps for those boundaries. It applies to disjoint start/stop windows with all four incident weights positive at each selected port and strict interior maxima, including full-row windows on either side of the original spectral poles.

For the half-row straddling window, the only unsuppressed local alignment is

ϑx=ϑy=ϑb=ϑc−π.\vartheta_x=\vartheta_y=\vartheta_b=\vartheta_c-\pi.

The full-row version uses the actual signs of the unused-row factor at each endpoint. The mixed cycle criterion now includes the proved single-protected-outer and single-protected-middle windows, and whole CROSS coupons with their exact positive endpoint majorants. Only supported incidences enter the 0/π0/\pi constraints. Fractional packing of odd cycles turns the certified local losses into a shared Cauchy improvement e−κNe^{-\kappa N}, with one radius per original face and unchanged targets. The results section groups these four extensions and their precise hypotheses.

The next quantitative step is to prove that the positive majorant rate minus the cycle loss is below ΦD\Phi_D, with uniform estimates for an exhaustive source decomposition. That inequality is not automatic. Compatible phase systems, double-zero specializations, spectral caps and irregular boundary maxima remain separate parts of the general problem. The coefficient-energy criterion below is another proved reduction; neither route currently establishes universal diagram coverage.

Why the coefficient has the actual geometric rate

The proof uses one circle-packing radius for each shared white face. It does not independently maximize the tensor factors.

  1. The rank and curvature theorem leaves exactly two flat framing directions. Removing the two marked faces and fixing the common scale gives a positive Hessian of size NN.
  2. The global coefficient theorem bounds the entire coefficient torus. A finite positive-matrix contraction gives exponential decay away from its unique angular maximum, followed by a nonzero relative Gaussian asymptotic.
  3. The absolute action calculation cones the marked ideal vertex to the actual white and shaded facets. Their Lobachevsky contributions give 2Vol⁡(QD)2\operatorname{Vol}(Q_D), with the additive constant fixed by the original determinant normalization.
  4. The uniform channel-rate theorem controls every internal label at those same radii. Its circle rate increases strictly to the fold. This bounds the whole source from above; one positive folded coefficient supplies the matching lower bound in the untwisted case.
  5. The signed extension proves a uniform relative Gaussian estimate from the original finite continuants. It sums the actual half-twist phases and bounds all remaining labels. Its leading coefficient is nonzero at both level parities.
