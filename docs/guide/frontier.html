Research guide

The current general frontier

The goal is the broad original-source equality with actual hyperbolic volume. The specified families and local identities establish parts of that argument; they do not complete the general conjecture.

The target: arbitrary bridge width and long twist regions

Fix any 2k2k-strand plat, k≥2k\geq2, whose designated crossing circles give a hyperbolic augmented exterior. The general target asks for the original Kashaev volume equality whenever every independent signed twist parameter exceeds a diagram-dependent threshold in absolute value. The twists are fixed before the quantum level NN tends to infinity. A matching test specifies the knot parity patterns. Hyperbolicity of a knot alone does not certify every chosen augmentation.

The geometric long-filling corollary now proves a threshold Tgeom(D)T_{\mathrm{geom}}(D) under the positive marked certificate: every independently long signed filling, including partial fillings, has the actual hyperbolic geometry. No comparability of slope lengths is needed. The quantum threshold giving the volume limit for every such filling remains conditional on the general source estimates.

There are now exact complete-color and all-color formulas for this source at every fixed plat width. They retain the normal terms at repeated roots, all phases, and the factorial rates. The entire core function makes the Fourier proposal concrete: its coefficients and Parseval identity are exact at both level parities.

The matching-graph certificate supplies actual complete geometry, primitive markings, and a positive ideal triangulation when its positive realization is provided. The geometric endpoint theorem locates the complete disk trace at c=−2c=-2 and explains why its marked longitude contains information lost by the trace coordinate.

More generally, the positive gluing and optimization criterion proves directly that an interior volume critical point on an actual ideal triangulation gives complete geometry and the unique maximum. Its algebraic certificate does not require a right-angled polyhedron. It does require the actual topology and a strict critical point.

The full physical packet theorem now gives the actual selected ordinary action and nonzero amplitude on every fixed compact of the full strict physical tube: 1/2<Re⁡τ<11/2<\operatorname{Re}\tau<1 and 0<Re⁡ν<Re⁡τ0<\operatorname{Re}\nu<\operatorname{Re}\tau. Both coordinates may be complex; the channel remains an actual integer and both level parities are included. The amplitude is uniform when j/Nj/N stays in a compact subset of (0,1/2](0,1/2], including the folded endpoint. A second Hermitian determinant places all zeros outside the physical sector. The finite-level correction Re⁡τ<1−1/(2N)\operatorname{Re}\tau<1-1/(2N) is retained, with an explicit counterexample showing why it cannot simply be deleted. Every fixed compact is covered for all sufficiently large levels.

The small-channel generic-normal theorem, with the full-domain extension above, controls every fixed original generic derivative on physical compacts with polynomial relative cost. The partner collision identity treats the large partner separately: its actual first normal is an exact derivative transverse to a generic collision. The surviving tail has additional genuine poles inside parts of the joint domain. The new complex near-fold bound controls the complete paired normal on a fixed complex compact when the partner separation satisfies (l−j)log⁡N=o(N)(l-j)\log N=o(N). Its relative cost is subexponential, including square-root windows. These complex bounds now hold on full physical compacts too. A fixed nonzero scaled displacement and a nonzero leading normal amplitude remain open.

The physical paired-normal theorem now proves a polynomial relative bound for every actual confluent first-normal entry on the complete physical grid, at both parities. It uses the imaginary part of the original Casimir matrix and reflection through a double spectral node. The physical generic-jet theorem also bounds every fixed original q derivative of every admissible physical column, including large channels, and of its genuine divided pair. Integer-grid jets, complex shrinking-window bounds and a global noncancellation theorem have distinct scopes; the last remains open.

The finite framing criterion and selected local certificate remain valid conditional statements. A new general right-angled obstruction proves that their all-central selected common frame cannot exist for the certified right-angled plat decomposition. The proof uses the actual Morse prefixes and outward reflection signs; algebraic regularity cannot supply the missing real inequalities.

There is a useful localization: generic flags satisfying the oriented geometric strip conditions have exactly two input/output factors outside strict physical support, independently of bridge width. Their polarization line crosses the white-normal convex polyhedron through two facets. Those facets can be prescribed, including the two adjacent to the original marked physical edge. The selected-factor corollary now proves that every remaining ordinary factor is on the actual selected full physical packet. Choosing both exceptional facets at one crossing-circle matching edge leaves one exact four-port block. This removes the local domain and sheet gap for the other factors, while the completed contraction and global period remain unproved.

For that crossing-circle block, the positive scalar theorem covers every recoupling channel at both level parities, with polynomial fixed-derivative cost. For the complete-circle source, the exact marked rewrite retains the whole-network first normal and shows that the original circle weight cancels the exponential row normalization. The completed-projector formula cancels the remaining target scalar factor and combines colliding channels before differentiation. Its weighted Cauchy bound leaves the native completed projectors and surrounding contraction, through first derivative order. The retained scalar divided difference includes the required second weight derivatives. Neither remaining operator cost is yet sharply controlled by the geometric action.

The native-energy theorem now controls every fixed generic projector derivative by a polynomial times an explicit original-basis magnetic energy, at every physical degree and both parities. That energy cannot be discarded: one physical-degree block of an odd simple-channel projector has exact exponential rate v8/(2π)v_8/(2\pi), where v8v_8 is the regular ideal octahedron volume. A polynomial bound for all native projectors is therefore false. The remaining estimate must retain this energy and the shared magnetic variables in the joint contraction.

The Plancherel and energy-profile theorem now computes the full folded boundary cost. The actual column norm is an exact finite Fourier norm and differs from its squared canonical midpoint value by only polynomial factors. The full odd simple projector and even completed pair have operator-norm rate 5v3/π5v_3/\pi, where v3v_3 is the regular ideal tetrahedron volume. This retains every physical degree. The local geometric calculation also shows why maximizing a positive energy alone does not finish the proof: the original phase multiplier can remain nontrivial at that maximum, and the measure supplies signed boundary terms.

The same real native rate now holds throughout every shrinking scaled fold window, without extending the complex-normal domain. More directly toward the global problem, the shared-cycle compiler rewrites the complete-circle source as bounded integral flows and coupled Fourier coefficient extractions, retaining the original marked charge. The coefficient variational theorem gives actual local rates with polynomial loss and an explicit selected momentum. Their shared constrained action, phase matching and global contribution still require proof. These are general source reductions, not a new restricted knot family.

There is now an explicit positive Fourier comparison with |AD,N|2≤N4R+2C−2ZF(N)|A_{D,N}|^2\leq N^{4R+2C-2}Z_F(N), retaining all flow and charge constraints and original scalar weights. Its maximum is not yet identified with the complement volume. The radial obstruction rules out assembling all strict ordinary radial saddles into the complete geometric representation under the right-angled certificate. It does not prove a rate gap or exclude boundary states. Controlling the coherent phase or the retained exceptional block remains on the critical path.

The actual local character map retains the ordered meridians and primitive crossing-circle marking. A generic common frame puts the complete geometric representation into its regular charts. The channel Hessian theorem gives a diagonal channel block with entries −4πi/τi-4\pi i/\tau_i and vanishing channel/magnetic cross-block. The new critical-scheme equivalence, combined with marked multi-cusp rigidity, closes the classical magnetic problem under the stated positive gluing certificate: exactly two null directions remain, both framing. A transverse slice gives a nearby holomorphic geometric stationary branch with the actual volume anchor. It does not establish that this branch contributes to the finite source.

The main gap remains: connect the source-selected local branches to a contributing global geometric cycle, prove its nonzero controlled amplitude, and suppress the other regions and physical aliases uniformly. A regular geometric candidate and its correctly normalized action do not prove that the finite sum selects it. The endpoint is an internal channel coordinate; the color deficit is its Fourier-dual parameter. Long twists enter through the subsequent filling argument, whose general packet and gap hypotheses are still missing.

The new full matching-graph source puts all folded labels, including paired nilpotent sectors, on the same actual graph as the geometric cut. There is also an exact full-color reflection-double identity: a specified three-port double is a sum of squared coherent half-packets divided by positive theta weights. This removes the final pairing cancellation for those doubles. Estimating the half-packets remains necessary; the identity alone gives no volume limit or general plat positivity.

One exact shortcut is excluded: an endpoint tensor calculation rules out making all local magnetic entries positive by separate diagonal leg gauges at every level. It does not rule out a positive coherent resummation or a suitable global energy estimate.

The source-divisor theorem weakens one transfer hypothesis: the leading density may vanish at completeness provided its analytic germ is nonzero. With the other hypotheses supplied, it gives equality outside an analytic exceptional divisor, including density one of long slopes in each signed box. A monomial times a unit gives all independently long slopes. This is a conditional general theorem, not a proof for all plats.

1. Full source transport

Available: a general exact four-port Racah formula; a uniform local scalar volume theorem; polynomial local Gram costs throughout the supported folded domain; exact typed sewing identities.

The all-partner theorem now bounds the first normal on all 64 reflected branches, including every genuine nonempty tail. It uses a positive presentation of the full generic source to supply the information missing from the earlier two-current reduction. Fixed higher jets are controlled on face-supported and tail-free branches.

The preceding results include equality of all 64 partner root values, exact weighted cluster-current identities, and a polynomial spectral gap for the folded Racah polynomial. The all-partner first-normal proof supplies additional information: root values and spectral derivatives alone do not control a nonempty generic normal tail.

Missing: arbitrary higher partner normals and a sharp estimate for the completed physical changes of pairing. The first-order scalar estimate does not bound the whole change of physical frame. An exact cluster-projector obstruction rules out making both physical cluster decompositions orthogonal in one positive Hermitian metric; weighted nonorthogonal estimates remain possible.

What would close this step: a source-preserving metric or equivalent global energy estimate whose source and dual costs are subexponential, and whose action retains the true shared color constraints. The corresponding lower bound must establish a nonzero geometric overlap whenever cancellation is possible.

2. The actual global geometric problem

Available: actual full-source hyperbolic volume equality for every retained cylindrical robust Conway factor and every independently sufficiently long fixed signed integral filling of any subset of its auxiliary circles, with arbitrary finite branching and turn depth. The proof gives explicit cells, ordered face matching, the actual crossing-disk cut, four-step edge cycles, and complete cusp horospheres.

The signed extension now includes arbitrary fixed pure or signed odd coupons on C(FG)C(FG), with robust children. Every expression has a constructive pattern giving one main component and hence knots after all auxiliary fillings. Its full quantum rate is proved equal to the actual filled volume, with the changed primitive meridians retained.

The arbitrary-width seed extension permits every seed hsh^s, s≥3s\geq3, mixed within that grammar, and also covers isolated C(hs)C(h^s) for s≥4s\geq4. It is a broader established application of the method; arbitrary plats remain outside it.

The positive source maximum is algebraic and has a rational Bloch lift. For these cylindrical factors, the source atoms now have degree-one geometric cells in the actual complement. For open robust expressions, every interior output has a unique strict optimizer, and its algebraic boundary conjugate is the modulus of a literal ideal quadrilateral port.

Missing: the corresponding theorem for arbitrary knot diagrams and a general comparison with the knot’s quantum A-operator. The new auxiliary-color parameters do have an actual marked peripheral branch, with a calibrated action primitive. This is distinct from the Newton output, which remains a quadrilateral modulus; elimination does not by itself identify an entire classical or quantum A-polynomial.

New quantum progress: the whole mixed-color gap and uniform complex packet now supply the source hypotheses for every retained cylindrical factor. The long-filling source theorem gives a nonzero full-sequence asymptotic for independently sufficiently long signed integral full twists. The new exact face-map construction supplies its actual marked peripheral representation and absolute regulator, proving that the limit is the filled hyperbolic volume.

The changed source is now controlled: the supporting-plane bound handles every alias subset and the entire physical color complement after half twists. The finite complex packet has a nonzero amplitude with every phase and lattice offset retained. Its action matches the actual odd peripheral deformation through an exact half-longitude shear on a marked double cover. The primitive filling equation then gives the smooth filled volume.

The pure component obstruction remains valid for unchanged pure diagrams. The odd parity construction resolves it for the new class. Still excluded are the minimal C(h3)C(h^3) mixed-color filling case, arbitrary short fillings, arbitrary knot diagrams, and a general theorem identifying the entire knot quantum A-operator.

Cap-closed products split as componentwise connected sums, their original quantum sources multiply, and their entire angle polytopes split as Cartesian products. This preserves the actual sum of the exhibited geometric factor volumes without asserting a hyperbolic metric on a connected sum.

Ideas kept in play

The cube and projected-chain constructions test sharp sewing with a shared core. The Fourier and Plancherel approach supplies explicit positive energies. The Karamata-inspired direction seeks cross-level control; its Tauberian formulation is not silently substituted for the direct asymptotic proofs. The pointed A-structure and regulator torsor retain the selected period and peripheral normalization.

These routes can inform each other. Their exact claims, counterexamples, and historical checkpoints remain available in the archive. The detailed frontier and preservation record retain the longer technical history.

Reading status labels

“Full equality” means the exact family and hypotheses in its statement. “Local theorem” stops at the stated local object. “Conditional” means the listed hypotheses still have to be supplied in a new application. “Diagnostic” means a computation that guides research without establishing a general theorem.

An audit marked PASS verifies the checks implemented by that audit. It does not upgrade a conjecture, diagnostic, or restricted theorem to a general proof.
