Research guide

The current general frontier

The target is a theorem at every fixed bridge width, with independently long signed twists. The general volume equality remains open. Exact source formulas, the marked geometric action, and much of the local analysis are now available; the unresolved step is the global quantum period.

The class and the order of quantifiers

Fix an essential 2k2k-strand plat diagram DD, k≥2k\geq2, with a hyperbolic augmented exterior YDY_D and its actual primitive cusp markings. The general target asks for a threshold T(D)T(D) such that

mini|mi|>T(D)⇒limN→∞2πNlog⁡|KN(KD,𝒎)|=Vol⁡(S3\KD,𝒎).\min_i |m_i|>T(D)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log|K_N(K_{D,\mathbf m})| =\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K_{D,\mathbf m}).

The diagram and every signed twist number mim_i are fixed before the quantum level NN tends to infinity. The prescribed parity pattern must give a knot. Hyperbolicity of a knot does not automatically certify every chosen augmentation.

Under the actual positive marked gluing certificate, the geometric filling theorem already supplies a geometric threshold, including independently long partial fillings. It is the quantum threshold in the displayed implication that remains unproved for general plats.

How far this is from the general conjecture

The main gap remains a substantial global theorem. There are two concrete tasks:

  1. Compute the framing contribution and transport the original degree and magnetic cycles to the geometric chart with a nonzero controlled coefficient, using the exact boundary corrections. The nonzero local marked density and folded Gaussian do not establish survival of the whole coherent contribution.
  2. Control the remaining physical regions and aliases uniformly in the auxiliary colors, and obtain the full source’s complex color packet, so the general long-filling transfer applies.

The Fourier route is still useful. Its exact positive partition gives a weighted-Cauchy upper bound with all flow and charge constraints retained. Its radial optimizer cannot simply be declared geometric: the radial-frame obstruction prevents assembling all strict ordinary radial saddles into the certified complete representation. No numerical rate gap follows from that obstruction.

Likewise, taking independent local projector maxima pays a genuine exponential boundary cost. The computed folded rate is 5v3/π5v_3/\pi at both parities. The joint contraction must account for that cost rather than discard it as a normalization.

Existing equality results and preserved alternatives

The results chapter states the internally reviewed equality theorems for the specified pretzel, belt-stack and signed robust Conway families. The latter permit arbitrary fixed branching and seed widths within their grammar, with independently sufficiently long fixed signed fillings. They do not exhaust arbitrary plats. The minimal isolated C(h3)C(h^3) mixed-color filling case and arbitrary short fillings remain outside the stated general filling theorem.

The source-divisor transfer can tolerate a leading density that vanishes at completeness if its analytic germ is nonzero. With the other hypotheses supplied, it gives equality outside an analytic exceptional divisor and density one of long slopes in signed boxes; a monomial times a unit gives all independently long slopes. This is still conditional for general plats.

The cube, Fourier, Karamata-inspired estimates, positive sewing and pointed torsor approaches remain in the research library. Their detailed arguments and failed intermediate estimates are preserved in the technical archive.
