Scope and remaining questions¶

The theorem fixes a finite completed-root expression and a knot half-twist pattern. Every independently chosen signed integral auxiliary filling beyond a source-dependent threshold is then held fixed while \(N\) tends to infinity through all levels. The conclusion is the volume of the knot complement, not the volume of the unfilled augmented link.

The theorem does not claim:

  • coverage of every Conway algebraic or arborescent knot;

  • a threshold uniform over all trees;

  • arbitrary short or rational quantum fillings;

  • a tangle isotopy from equality of source scalars;

  • a general volume bound from a positive envelope alone.

The isolated closures \(C(h^s)\), \(s\ge3\), are separately established cases. The isolated \(C(hh)\) is excluded. More general root-leaf closures require additional global signed-source estimates and marked transfer arguments. A local packet extension at a boundary index is not by itself a full filling theorem.

All review statements in this project describe internal analytical review or reproducible finite checks. They are not claims of external peer review or journal acceptance.
