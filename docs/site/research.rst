Companion research after the manuscript
=======================================

The manuscript retains its completed-root theorem. The following companion notes extend particular inputs and distinguish them from the remaining general conjecture.

For every fixed strict closed Conway tree, the complete leaf indices are at least :math:`1/4`. For at least three leaves, the gate Hessians control all primitive wire directions; in the two-leaf knot case, a pure leaf supplies the needed curvature. For strict knot patterns, this gives the all-core source packet and its uniform physical-color complement gap. The marked-mask construction also supplies the actual primitive peripheral deformation and absolute filling-volume calibration for every strict tree.

A separate whole-source argument treats :math:`C(h_0T(h_1\cdots h_s))`, :math:`s\ge2`, with every fixed pure/odd pattern giving a knot. Independently sufficiently long fixed signed twists have the original invariant's knot complement volume limit through all levels.

The Fourier cancellation was already present in the earlier outer-kernel theorem, and all-odd root-star scalars overlap the octahedral-belt source. Mixed pure/odd leaves extend that recorded source alphabet. No new knot isotopy classification follows from the scalar comparison.

For arbitrary strict knot trees, the remaining source requirement is a nonempty-alias estimate at the geometric rate, or an equally sufficient bound after the exact filling pairing. General presentation coverage and a generic main-meridian quantum A-operator theorem also remain open. These companion notes have internal analytical reviews; they are not externally peer-reviewed publications.
