Companion research after the manuscript
=======================================

The manuscript retains its completed-root theorem. The following companion notes extend particular inputs and distinguish them from the remaining general conjecture.

For every fixed strict closed Conway tree, the complete leaf indices are at least :math:`1/4`. For at least three leaves, the gate Hessians control all primitive wire directions; in the two-leaf knot case, a pure leaf supplies the needed curvature. For strict knot patterns, this gives the all-core source packet and its uniform physical-color complement gap. The marked-mask construction also supplies the actual primitive peripheral deformation and absolute filling-volume calibration for every strict tree.

General presentation coverage and a generic main-meridian quantum A-operator theorem also remain open. These companion notes have internal analytical reviews; they are not externally peer-reviewed publications.


Weighted Conway trees
---------------------

The latest companion theorem fixes any finite rooted ordered tree with at least two vertices. Each vertex carries one integer twist weight. Define nonroot tangles and the root closure by

.. math::

   Q_v=T\!\left(R^{n_v}\prod_wQ_w\right),\qquad
   K=C\!\left(T(R^{n_0})T\!\left(\prod_wQ_w\right)\right).

For each fixed tree there is a finite :math:`B(\mathcal T)`. Every fixed, independently signed weight tuple with :math:`|n_v|>B(\mathcal T)` at each vertex and a loop-free one-component terminal pairing satisfies the original invariant's knot complement volume limit through all levels. The tree and weights are fixed before :math:`N` grows. Branching and unary chains are unrestricted. The knot cusp remains unfilled.
