Scope and remaining questions
=============================

The theorem fixes a finite completed-root expression and a knot half-twist pattern. Every independently chosen signed integral auxiliary filling beyond a source-dependent threshold is then held fixed while :math:`N` tends to infinity through all levels. The conclusion is the volume of the knot complement, not the volume of the unfilled augmented link.

The theorem does not claim:

* coverage of every Conway algebraic or arborescent knot;
* a threshold uniform over all trees;
* arbitrary short or rational quantum fillings;
* a tangle isotopy from equality of source scalars;
* a general volume bound from a positive envelope alone.

The isolated closures :math:`C(h^s)`, :math:`s\ge3`, are separately established cases. The isolated :math:`C(hh)` is excluded. The later companion research extends the core estimate and marked geometry to every strict knot pattern, and proves the whole-source volume theorem for every strict literal Conway tree, including mixed flagged/unflagged junctions and arbitrary finite branching. The exact pointed ADO operator period has the same volume limit. General arborescent presentation coverage and identification of the selected classical geometric factor remain open. These extensions are recorded in :doc:`research` and do not change the theorem stated in this manuscript.

All review statements in this project describe internal analytical review or reproducible finite checks. They are not claims of external peer review or journal acceptance.
