# The whole unselected plateau for words ending in type three

Conditional research assembly, 5 November 2026. The mathematical
deduction below uses the complete four-current first-cap comparison
on its proved actual reference domains. Its final-stage author and
independent saved replay are still pending; they are an explicit
hypothesis here. All ordinary conditional planes and the full
type-three terminal transform are already proved. No new numerical
execution is part of this deduction.

Under that hypothesis, every fixed mixed word of length at least two
ending in three has its original nonzero Gaussian asymptotic after
integration over the **entire unselected plateau**. No retained real
coordinate is restricted to a neighborhood of its reference. This
is a contour-sector statement, not an assertion about the sum of
all sectors of the finite open source.

                   \cap\{-.108\le\Im b\le-.001\}.
 \tag{1}
\]
The original roots, target families and source allocations are those

at \(m=2\), it is the opposite endpoint's attached leg \(s_2\),
not its private leg \(t_2\). The proved ordinary and attached
image bounds give the strict inclusion in each case. Thus Hypothesis
C does not impose a restriction on the actual words considered here.
It makes no claim over the larger auxiliary type-four disks of the

The ordinary-color disk is fixed before \(N\) tends to infinity.

## 2. Exact source and cancellation on the full independent plateau

Fix a word \(3+\epsilon_1,\ldots,3+\epsilon_m\) with
\(m\ge2\) and \(\epsilon_m=0\). Set
\[
 h=m-1,\qquad k_4=\sum_{j=1}^m\epsilon_j,\qquad
 X_j=e^{-2\pi iz_j},\qquad r_j=a_j+ic_j.
 \tag{4}
\]
The \(r_j\) are the actual retained critical legs. All lie strictly
inside the real plateau, and every retained type-four height is
negative. These include the actual attached endpoint ranges; no
narrower cyclic height interval is substituted.

After the exact terminal contraction, the unselected retained
integrand is
\[
 \mathscr J_N(\mathbf z;w_L,w_R)=
 U_{\epsilon_1,N}(w_L,X_1)
 \prod_{j=2}^{h} C_{\epsilon_j,N}(X_{j-1},X_j)
 \mathcal V_{0,N}(w_R,X_h)
 \prod_{j=1}^{h}d_N(X_j)^{\epsilon_j}.
 \tag{5}
\]
Its exact external scalar is
\(\kappa_N^{m+k_4}N^{m+k_4-1}\), by equation (13) of
[the original terminal contraction](research_mixed_reversed_cap_assembly.md).
The terminal coordinate, all its rows and its original alternatives
have been summed into the whole \(\mathcal V_{0,N}\). In the
remaining variables (5) chooses the factor 1, rather than
\(X_j^{-N}\), from each \(1+X_j^{-N}\). At \(m=2\) the
conditional product is empty, and the two caps share one retained
coordinate.

The sector considered here is exactly
\[
                         z_j=x_j+ic_j,
            \qquad (x_1,\ldots,x_h)\in P^h.
 \tag{6}
\]
All original cutoff factors are one on this plateau. Thus the heights
in (6) are the literal unselected hybrid-contour heights, irrespective
of the values of the other real coordinates.

Use each ordinary allocated phase \(\phi_j\) from
[complete ordinary source assembly](research_mixed_three_row_source_assembly.md),
and set \(\Pi=\mathcal P_0-\epsilon_hg/2\) for the final
transform. Original calibration and allocation give locally
\[
 \Phi(\mathbf z)=L_{\epsilon_1}(z_1)
          +\sum_{j=2}^{h}\phi_j(z_{j-1},z_j)+\Pi(z_h).
 \tag{7}
\]
This is the original open action after its hidden variables have
been eliminated. In particular
\(\Phi(\mathbf r)=\mathcal F_m(\text{original critical point})\)
and \(\nabla\Phi(\mathbf r)=0\). Every retained diagonal
weight has exactly two allocated halves: cap and conditional at the
first coordinate, two conditionals internally, and conditional and
terminal at the last. When \(h=1\), the cap and terminal halves
give the single full weight, and (7) has no conditional term.

**Theorem 1, conditional on C.** For every fixed word as above and
every \(\tau>0\), uniformly on (6),
\[
 |\mathscr J_N|
 \le C_{m,\tau}N^{A_{m,\tau}}
       \exp N\left\{M_{\rm open}+\tau
                -2\kappa_*\sum_{j=1}^{h}(x_j-a_j)^2\right\},
 \qquad M_{\rm open}=\Re\Phi(\mathbf r).
 \tag{8}
\]
It holds for every sufficiently large integer \(N\), of either
parity, uniformly on fixed small ordinary-color disks at both ends.
Fixed ordinary-color derivatives on smaller disks satisfy the same
rate with the corresponding polynomial prefactors.

*Proof.* Multiply (3), the complete ordinary source planes, and
the [whole-terminal plane](research_mixed_terminal_three_full_completion.md),
Theorem 2. At the reference, (7) adds their constant terms and
cancels every linear coefficient. Their quadratic losses are
\[
 \kappa_*d_1^2+
 \kappa_*\sum_{j=2}^{h}(d_{j-1}^2+d_j^2)
                   +\kappa_*d_h^2
                  =2\kappa_*\sum_{j=1}^{h}d_j^2.
 \tag{9}
\]
For \(h=1\), the two terms outside the empty sum give the same
identity. This proves (8) with exact halfweights, including Fourier
zeros. For a fixed word, choose the individual source tolerances to
sum to \(\tau\). There is no fixed positive rate error paid at
each edge. The word length may affect the threshold, polynomial
prefactor and color neighborhoods, as is appropriate for a theorem
about each fixed word. Ordinary derivatives follow by Cauchy before
these finite-source bounds are multiplied. \(\square\)

Consequently the part of (6) with
\(\sum_j(x_j-a_j)^2\ge\eta^2\), for fixed \(\eta>0\),
has strict loss \(\kappa_*\eta^2\) after choosing the total
tolerance and then \(N\). The polynomial scalar in the exact
composition does not change this exponential separation.

## 3. The original nonzero coefficient after plateau integration

The local packets near \(\mathbf r\) are already proved:
the [complete first cap](research_mixed_complete_first_cap_packet.md),
the [ordinary conditional packets](research_mixed_reduced_disk_conditional_packets.md),
the original diagonal polynomial, and the
[entire type-three terminal packet](research_mixed_terminal_three_full_completion.md).
Their joint incoming-variable and color germs, including the terminal
one, are justified in Section 4 of the
[local-first plateau slab](research_mixed_terminal_three_plateau_slab.md).
The terminal germ uses its strict **bare** discarded-current gaps,
the joint original interpolant and its simple critical point. It is
not inferred from a value at a single incoming coordinate.

Define the nonzero local leading symbols by
\[
\begin{split}
 U_{\epsilon_1,N}&=N^{-1-\epsilon_1/2}b_U e^{N\mathcal T_{\epsilon_1}}
                                      (1+O(N^{-1})),\\
 C_{\epsilon_j,N}&=N^{-(1+\epsilon_j)/2}b_{C,j}
                    e^{N\varphi_j}(1+O(N^{-1})),\\
 \mathcal V_{0,N}&=N^{-1}b_V e^{N\mathcal P_0}(1+O(N^{-1})),\\
 d_N(e^{-2\pi iz})&=N^{-1/2}a_0(z)^{-1}e^{-Ng(z)}
                                      (1+O(N^{-1})),\qquad
 a_0(z)=\sqrt{2\sin\pi z}\,e^{-i\pi z/2}.
\end{split}
 \tag{10}
\]
Here \(\varphi_j\) is the bare conditional phase, so adding its
two diagonal halfweights gives \(\phi_j\). All symbols and
branches in (10) are the original ones. In particular, if
\((t_*,u_*)\) is the terminal critical pair, then
\[
 b_V(r_h,-2)=
   \frac{2\pi\mathcal A(r_h,t_*,u_*)}
        {\sqrt{\det(-\mathcal T_{(t,u)}''(r_h;t_*,u_*))}}.
 \tag{11}
\]
Thus the terminal two-dimensional Gaussian factor is already
contained in \(b_V\); it must not be inserted a second time.

Put
\[
 b(\mathbf r)=b_U(r_1,-2)b_V(r_h,-2)
       \prod_{j=2}^{h}b_{C,j}(r_{j-1},r_j)
       \prod_{j=1}^{h}a_0(r_j)^{-\epsilon_j},
 \qquad H_{\rm eff}=-\Phi''(\mathbf r).
 \tag{12}
\]
The Hessian is the Schur complement of the original open action
after eliminating all variables hidden in (5). The original
accretivity theorem and the complex Schur identity give
\(\Re H_{\rm eff}\succ0\); its Gaussian square root has the
original accretive branch. Every factor in \(b\) is nonzero.

Let \(\mathcal Z_N^P\) denote the scalar above (5) times the
integral of (5) over (6), with the angular orientation inherited
from the exact torus identity. In increasing real coordinates this
uses the original measure \(dx_1\cdots dx_h\).

**Theorem 2, conditional on C.** At the complete colors,
\[
 \mathcal Z_N^P(-2,-2)=
 \kappa_N^{m+k_4}N^{-3/2}
 \frac{(2\pi)^{h/2}b(\mathbf r)}{\sqrt{\det H_{\rm eff}}}
 e^{N\Phi(\mathbf r)}(1+O(N^{-1})).
 \tag{13}
\]
In particular its coefficient is nonzero. This holds through both
level parities with the original normalization.

*Proof.* A small fixed box around \(\mathbf a\) is inside
\(P^h\). The local packets give its original Gaussian with
phase (7) and coefficient (12). The complement of that box in the
whole plateau is exponentially smaller by (8). Therefore extending
the local integral to all of (6) preserves its nonzero coefficient
and relative \(O(N^{-1})\) error.

The powers, including the exact scalar and the remaining
\(h=m-1\) dimensional Gaussian, are
\[
 (m+k_4-1)-(1+\epsilon_1/2)
 -\frac{m-2+k_4-\epsilon_1}{2}-1-\frac{k_4}{2}
 -\frac{m-1}{2}=-\frac32.
 \tag{14}
\]
This calculation also applies at \(m=2\), with no ordinary
conditional factors. The only extra Gaussian constant outside
(12) is \((2\pi)^{h/2}/\sqrt{\det H_{\rm eff}}\), giving
(13). \(\square\)

For small ordinary-color perturbations the local critical point,
action and coefficient continue holomorphically. A local contour
deformation follows that germ, while the strictly separated plateau
complement remains suppressed on a fixed pair of color disks.
Cauchy applied to the relative error on smaller disks gives, for
fixed ordinary derivative orders \(a,b\), the leading power
\(N^{a+b-3/2}\) and the nonzero multiplier
\[
 \left(-\frac{\tan\pi u_L}{4\pi}\right)^a
 \left(-\frac{\tan\pi u_R}{4\pi}\right)^b.
 \tag{15}
\]
Signed ordinary jets acquire their original additional signs.
No holomorphic halfpower of a Fourier polynomial is needed: the
integrated source (5) has integer diagonal powers throughout.

## 4. What this does and does not complete

Hypothesis C removes the last local restriction on the first retained
real coordinate in the earlier plateau-slab theorem. The deduction
is uniform in the type pattern for each fixed length, and it includes
lengths two and three without a separate numerical word calculation.
All retained real coordinates range independently over the full
plateau, with their actual reference heights. No moving critical
root at an independent plateau target is assumed.

The exact finite open source also contains retained selected Fourier
alternatives and regions where one or more real coordinates leave
the plateau and the cutoff heights change. Those sectors are not
included in \(\mathcal Z_N^P\), and their possible contribution
cannot be discarded by (8). Thus a nonzero plateau coefficient does
not yet prove the nonzero coefficient of the entire ordinary
closure. A terminal type-four version further requires its complete
terminal source theorem and local packet; it is not asserted here.
No arbitrary-short-filling or per-template cofinite theorem follows
from this contour-sector assembly alone.
