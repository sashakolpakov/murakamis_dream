# Closing assessment of the retained results

This assessment ends the audit of the retained, restricted results. It
does not settle the general volume conjecture. The reference manuscript
remains frozen; broader research continues separately.

The agreed boundary was to verify the internal implications while
retaining written analytic and geometric proofs. The
[stopping criterion](MANUSCRIPT_AUDIT.md#where-this-research-phase-ends)
requires an exact invariant formula, a nonzero leading term, a bound for
the whole remainder, the intended completed hyperbolic metric, and an
absolute comparison of its volume with the action. Each application must
supply these inputs with compatible hypotheses and level conventions.

The final reading found no additional missing implication in that chain
for the retained families. This is a statement about the scope of the
review, not a guarantee that no error can remain. The evidence is as
follows.

The two final review directories contain their source hashes. The
whole-sum directory also contains a finite numerical probe and its
output. Those samples test signs and prefactors; they do not prove an
infinite-tail estimate or a volume limit.

One correction needed to be made more visible. The
[errata](../manuscript/ERRATA.md) now explain the unchanged matched pair
after equation (R.39): its outer weights are $(2,0)$, and its middle
weights are $(1,1)$. Quarter-load evaluations preserve the required
strict gap. The corrected companion proof already used those weights.
The archived mathematical sources and PDF were not changed.

The reference account has 374 pages. The current subject drafts have
403 pages across 21 papers, each between 11 and 29 pages. The
[manuscript guide](../book/chapters/manuscript.md) explains the difference.
These are provisional divisions by mathematical subject. Their combined
length is not a measure of compression, proof strength or readiness for
journal publication.

The remaining general source comparison, larger knot classes, and
complete formal models of the actual invariants and manifolds belong to
subsequent work. They are not used to supply a missing premise in the
retained claims reviewed here.
