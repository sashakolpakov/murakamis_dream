# Current internal assembly coverage

This record maps all 23 current papers to exact compiled results and retained written inputs. It compares the main theorem statements with the saved Lean declarations and their explicit assumptions. The record retains exact source locators alongside the saved compilation records. The review concerns those interfaces; it does not reread every local analytic or geometric proof.

The integrated record contains 241 Std theorems and 91 definitions plus 362 theorems in the Mathlib project. Declaration counts do not measure completed paper coverage. Exact declaration names, theorem labels, written source locators and hashes are in [the coverage record](formal_coverage/coverage.json).

## What changed

The Newton calculus and strict stationary profiles now form one paper. Their generic polynomial-reduction results remain the same compiled inputs; the physical formulas, strict optimizer, absolute profiles and positive-source limit retain written proofs. The two earlier editions are excluded from the current-paper count and remain available for existing citations.

The recurrence argument now starts with the original finite equation at actual roots of unity. `characteristic_at_roots` proves the literal sine gauge and shifted denominator exclusions, derives every cleared coefficient, expands the finite sum from its factors, and proves the meridian-factor expansion at $h=π i/N$. Coefficient uniqueness, injective regrading and formal projection then give the classical identity. The original coefficient and actual source-ratio expansions remain explicit analytic inputs.

The uniform-profile module retains the moving $A_N(s)$ and $H_N(s)$, constructs their compact positive bounds from uniform nonzero limits, and proves one nonvanishing threshold and a uniform actual logarithmic rate. The summed first coefficient plus $O(N^{-2})$ error and the directed $O(N^{-1})$ profile both supply its error hypothesis. This closes the elementary uniformity gap identified in the baseline report without assuming the desired uniform rate.

## Exact remaining boundary

The operator application must supply the actual recurrence, exact source normalization, eventual nonvanishing, and the prescribed expansions of its original coefficients and shifted source ratios. The compiled theorem derives the assembled equation’s expansion; it no longer assumes that whole expansion or the elementary meridian-factor estimate. The written $q-1$ normalization, nonzero Laurent symbol, passage to the selected holomorphic germ, and irreducible-factor conclusion remain separate. No operator-existence, full AJ, or full A-polynomial claim follows.

For the uniform profiles, the actual analytic estimates and the literal sine-quotient factor limits remain written. The new theorem proves the subsequent uniform implication, not the saddle or the source. For all volume papers, exact physical normalization, a nonzero full packet, the entire complementary bound and the absolute same-manifold volume calibration remain explicit application inputs. Generic real transfer already handles both parities, fixed signed tuples and common thresholds.

Actual mathematics, conditional assembly and metadata are different: the Lean modules construct operations and prove inequalities/limits; generic assembly theorems use explicit written input estimates; dependency DAGs verify named connections and scope without proving their vertices. No reviewed public statement asserts end-to-end Lean proofs of all 23 papers.
