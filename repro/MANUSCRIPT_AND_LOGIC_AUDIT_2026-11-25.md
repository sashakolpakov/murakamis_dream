# Manuscript and proof-logic audit

25 November 2026

This revision covers the fourteen subject papers outside the [fixed Conway series](../papers/conway-series.json), together with the [reference manuscript](../manuscript/main.tex). The earlier [seven-paper report](CONWAY_SERIES_AUDIT_2026-11-25.md) describes the Conway application and its direct prerequisites. The reference gives another account of overlapping material; it is not an additional prerequisite for every paper.

The work had two stages. First, the active manuscripts were read and rewritten so that objects, conventions and hypotheses precede their use. The proofs now explain why a local estimate applies to the shared finite source, where a nonzero coefficient is proved, and where geometry enters. Second, the retained Lean definitions and proofs were compared with those revised statements. The manuscript sources are now frozen. Final collection-wide verification is being refreshed for that edition; the presence of an older verification file does not certify revised sources.

## Mathematical corrections and removed repetitions

The revisions include corrections of domains and conventions, as well as changes of prose.

- In the moving-source pressure lemma, a boundary label prescribed near one half carries no leaf action \(g\). Adding that action would make the asserted analyticity false because its endpoint derivative is singular. The proof and its applications already used action-free prescribed boundary labels; the source theorems retain their stated scope.
- Reference Appendix F now distinguishes the positive orientation of a geometric tetrahedron from the ordering used in its edge cross ratio. The first two vertices are reversed for that cross-ratio calculation. The eight stated shapes remain \(i\); the volume-sign convention now agrees with the geometric paper.
- Reference Appendix K applies Hermite/supremum/Chebyshev norm equivalence only to canonical polynomials of degree below \(N\). It no longer asserts it for arbitrary degree \(O(N)\): a nonzero multiple of the relation polynomial would contradict that assertion. Higher-degree products are estimated before reduction in their own quotient.
- The standard-library uniqueness and functional-characterization theorems now state degree bounds coefficientwise, rather than by the length of a coefficient list. Trailing zero coefficients therefore do not exclude a polynomial that meets the manuscript degree condition.
- The public Conway assembly theorem in Lean now asks for the physical coefficient identity at \(N\ge3\), rather than unnecessarily at \(N\ge2\). The coefficient mass estimate itself remains valid from level two. This changes an initial-level premise, not the asymptotic conclusion.
- Appendix D uses centered eigenvalue logarithms. A determinant-one lift can have complete eigenvalue \(-1\), so its small similarity logarithm is twice \(\Log(\eta/\eta(0))\), not twice an uncentered logarithm of \(\eta\). The local centered representatives are not asserted to belong to one global lift.
- In Appendix O, convergence of the normalized amplitude is stated after removing the displayed unit scalar phase as well as the level power and exponential. The phase need not converge. The two projection-copy superscripts are retained in the reflection products, making their telescoping cancellation literal.
- The arctangent load formula is restricted to a positive radicand. A singleton row instead has its prescribed load one half and zero Hessian. It is not obtained by substituting a zero denominator into the regular formula.

Redundant arguments were removed rather than archived. Appendix L uses the finite Hermite norm theorem instead of repeating an older Fourier remainder argument. The strict-output detour in Chapter 8a and duplicate strip estimates in Appendix E were removed. The latter now gives one grouped positive-Laurent-coefficient proof with the same retained constants. The preferred-longitude paper derives the cubic-junction formula from its general absolute elimination identity, without a second calibration of the cubic case. The unused convenience lemma `newtonFunctional_from_remainder` was also removed; it had no proof consumer. Earlier tracked versions remain in Git history.

## What the Lean comparison checks

The correspondence records distinguish four relations between a Lean root and a manuscript statement: an exact statement, a specialization, one component of a longer proof, or a conditional assembly with written inputs. Each record gives the symbol dictionary, hypotheses, conclusion and any unformalized step. An exact record has no unformalized part of the cited mathematical statement.

The [analysis record](../formal/correspondence/analysis.json) and [variational record](../formal/correspondence/variational.json) accompany [explicit analysis contracts](../formal-real/AnalysisContracts.lean) and [variational contracts](../formal-real/VariationalContracts.lean). These anonymous examples state expected propositions rather than merely checking declaration names. The variational contracts also check that pooling three boundary destinations preserves their separate fixed coefficients, and that the supported triangle supremum has the correct half-mass scaling law, including singleton rows. The [finite-algebra record](../formal/correspondence/finite.json) and its [standard-library contracts](../formal/StatementContracts.lean) are complete as well. They retain the canonical polynomial degree, repeated-root terms, finite support and certificate-assembly implications.

The manual comparison examined meanings as well as types: ordinary transpose versus adjoint, the normalized shifted Fourier transform, values and derivatives at repeated roots, the exceptional zeroth coefficient, the truncated rather than cyclic shift, the conductance factor \(1/(2\pi)\), free versus grounded equations, and the total-mass condition needed to recover the root equation. Anchors and hashes locate and freeze those claims; they do not establish their mathematical correspondence.

As agreed, the analytic and geometric proofs remain written. Lean proves the specified internal implications, including the scalar asymptotic transfer, finite coefficient estimates and generic supported variational arguments. A theorem whose hypotheses include a source identity, a nonzero limiting amplitude and an action-volume equality does not by itself formalize a knot-volume theorem. Planar counting for a particular disk, contour estimates, smooth metric completion and geometric calibration must still be read in the manuscripts.

The dependency inventory now records the additional comparison made in the reference: an analytic tree meridian correction becomes a preferred cusp shape only after the separate peripheral-path and homology calculation and the marked geometric identification. That comparison has its own reference-only entry. It does not add a preferred-longitude premise to the analytic tree-profile theorem. The pressure entry also states the action-free prescribed half-boundary hypothesis. Classical filling paths and metric completion continue to have no quantum volume-limit premise.

## Verification record and scope

During editing, the paper builds, their finite algebra/certificate checks, and the explicit Lean contracts were exercised. Those checks serve different purposes: types check implications; finite certificates establish their specified inequalities; sampled calculations can detect errors; document builds check presentation. None substitutes for a written analytic or geometric proof.

The final [collection record](results/paper-collection/summary.json) checks all 21 papers, their 524 compiled statement exports and 62 companion links. The papers total 420 pages, each within its 30-page limit; the reference has 210 pages. All current PDFs were rebuilt without typesetting warnings. The finite suite ran all 20 registered groups. The separate dependency-record check validated 49 declared proof steps and rejected 23 invalid substitutions. The three dependency graphs are acyclic, with 154 statement entries, 348 uses and 59 article imports.

The final [Lean replay](results/manuscript-and-lean/lean/summary.json) compiled all 28 retained modules from empty local build directories and checked the axiom dependencies of all 534 public declarations. The 76 selected manuscript roots have 76 explicit expected-type contracts: 15 exact matches, 10 specializations, 41 components and 10 conditional implications. All compiled. Three positive control propositions compiled, while their corresponding false types were rejected: a missing Parseval factor, adjoint in place of holomorphic transpose, and loss of the strict remainder gap. The axiom report contains only Lean's standard propositional extensionality, classical choice and quotient soundness; no admitted proofs or additional axioms occur.

The revised guide, paper index and dependency page have rendered formulas, working local links and image descriptions. The [combined record](results/manuscript-and-lean/verification-summary.json) pins these checks to their actual inputs and PDFs. The seven-paper Conway edition remains fixed at its existing tag; the additional manuscripts and reference are the current revised collection. No external submission was made by this audit.

The established filling results retain their specified constructions and sufficient hypotheses, with every diagram and every twist fixed before the level varies. Sufficiently long twist assignments have density one for each fixed admissible template. This does not imply finitely many exceptional assignments, or a density-one theorem for all knots. The finite-exception problem remains open.
