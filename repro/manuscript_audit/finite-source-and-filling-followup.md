## Appendix N: the finite functional and its limits

The reading followed the original Casimir annihilator and crossing
values and normals through the quotient functional. Put
\[
 d=N-1,\qquad
 \chi_N(z)=2T_N(z/2)+2,\qquad
 V_N(z;\Omega)=\frac{\chi_N(z)-\chi_N(\Omega)}{z-\Omega}.
\]
The fraction denotes polynomial division, not a matrix inverse or an
adjugate. Its spectral degree is \(d\).

For a nonconstant split monic polynomial \(p\), let \(\ell_p\) select
the top coefficient of the canonical remainder modulo \(p\).
Synthetic division constructs \(V_w=(p(X)-p(w))/(X-w)\).
When \(p(w)=0\), the literal identities
\[
 (X-w)V_w=p,\qquad \ell_p(V_w)=1,\qquad
 \ell_p(XgV_w)=w\ell_p(gV_w)
\]
give
\[
                    \ell_p(fV_w)=f(w)
\]
for every polynomial \(f\), including repeated roots and arbitrary
degree. The seven declarations in
[ResolventFunctional](../../formal/VolumeLogic/ResolventFunctional.lean)
formalize these constructed coefficient operations. For a local matrix
application the commutative algebra is the algebra generated by one
\(\Omega\) and scalar matrices; neighboring box operators are not
assumed to commute.

The actual Chebyshev factorization and finite crossing identity remain
written source inputs. The reading checked their confluent
specialization argument: colliding value rows become a value and a
divided-difference row, and the finite crossing matrices remain regular.
Thus the normal is not inferred from endpoint values alone.

At a paired node \(c\), the top-coefficient residue is
\[
 \frac{(4-c^2)F'(c)-cF(c)}{N^2}.
\]
The odd simple node contributes \(F(-2)/N^2\). Folding to the full
midpoint angular grid supplies these multiplicities with one common
factor \(1/2\); no division by the vanishing sine occurs at the simple
node. The Gaussian representative retains the original crossing phase
and its derivative for positive, zero and negative integral powers.

Only \(H(2\cos\phi)\), of trigonometric degree at most \(d\), is
subjected to the Riesz/Bernstein estimate. The Gaussian multiplying it
is not treated as a polynomial of that degree. The resulting scalar
cost is
\[
 \frac1N+\frac{|n|}{2}+\frac dN=1+\frac{|n|}{2}.
\]
The tensor argument separately treats node-hyperplane vanishing and
vanishing only on the full product grid. In the second case mixed
derivative terms remain; they are bounded rather than discarded.

The N report records an exact cyclotomic replay at
\(N=2,3,4,5\), powers \(-2,-1,0,1,2\), and 160 scalar quotient
identities. It also records compilation and an axiom inspection of the
seven new declarations. This curation verified the recorded artifact
hashes but did not rerun those computations or the Lean build. Such

 \partial_eA_N=-2\delta_e B_{e,N}(\boldsymbol\delta^2).

## FaceGridSupport and the finite source boundary

The arithmetic reading covers all six declarations of
[FaceGridSupport](../../formal/VolumeLogic/FaceGridSupport.lean).
Take \(N\ge2\), \(d=N-1\), protected exponents \(a,b\), and
ordinary nonnegative exponents \(m_f\). Ordinary character
selection gives \(m_f\equiv d\pmod N\), hence \(m_f\ge d\).
The total-degree bound
\[
                  a+b+\sum_fm_f\le Rd
\]
forces \(a=b=0\) and every \(m_f=d\). No congruence at the
exterior protected face is assumed. A strictly lower degree
cannot survive all ordinary selections.

For translated grids \(L_f=\xi_f\omega^{p_f}\), the surviving
multiplier is
\(\prod_f\xi_f^{m_f-d}\xi_v^b=1\).
This also explains the even-coset cancellation. Character
orthogonality and this multiplier application are written
steps; the Lean module proves the nonnegative exponent
argument. It is not a polynomial grid evaluator and does not
construct the physical grids or their exact ribbon normalization.
The semantic reviewer did not run a separate Lean compilation.
