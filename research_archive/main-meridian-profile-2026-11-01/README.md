# Meridian deformation and long-twist working proofs

These files preserve the next stage after the published source bounds in commit
`c7f758a`. They are research records; the public statements remain those in the
published guide until a separate publication update is made.

The component argument allows neighboring product junctions without a crossing
circle at each junction. It gives an explicit sufficient condition in terms of
the number of boundary branches and the paths through each component. The
conditioning argument then compares the complete-color signed sum with its
positive complete counterpart. Together with the local meridian formulas, this
gives the proposed whole augmented-source profile, with framing retained.

The direct filled-crossing calculation is a local result. It selects the first
Fourier term of an actual fixed crossing power and identifies its action with
the original marked filling action. Passing to the whole filled knot requires
a joint charge estimate compatible with the finite wire sum. In particular,
independently long twist counts may have arbitrarily different magnitudes; an
estimate using the smallest local error against the total volume drop would
not settle that case.

The diagnostic scripts use the actual finite grids and normal derivatives.
Their numerical outputs support the local calculations but are not substitutes
for the proofs or evidence of a whole filled-knot theorem. Older versions, when
replaced in this archive, are kept in `history/`. Temporary paths in the copied
working notes refer to the files with the same names in this directory.
