A marked nonparabolic chart for the pure two-octahedron piece

1 November 2026. Private mathematical derivation.

This note constructs the face maps of the actual pure two-circle cut. It identifies the two auxiliary peripheral pairs and the four-segment main longitude as rational functions. In particular it identifies the wire variable in the effective action; that variable is not the most convenient white-face coordinate when the main meridian is nonparabolic. The local disk-side sewing calculation is also exact. Arbitrary odd seams and arbitrary oriented tree markings require the further identifications specified in the last section.

The topological inputs are the labeled two-leaf cut in the angular edge chart, the pure white/shaded words in the marked peripheral calculation, and the literal ordered page attachment in the ordered decomposition. The target rational equations are those in the effective-action calculation. No volume asymptotic is used to identify a peripheral word.

6. What is established and what remains to assemble

The actual pure two-octahedron block now realizes the effective edge equation and both primitive auxiliary peripheral pairs. It realizes the local main longitude factor with a specified word, central sign and eigenflag. Formula (18) establishes full pure disk-side seam compatibility, and therefore permits compatible cyclic gluing without an unspecified shear.

For an arbitrary all-circle tree the next step is to trace the two oriented main punctures through each original page attachment. A transported frame can invert a native meridian; it must then transport the selected eigenflag as well. One must show that the local factors (21) concatenate to the preferred global word, with exactly its existing framing monomial. This has not been replaced here by reciprocity of the rational curve.

An odd distinguished seam also requires the actual puncture exchange. A useful regular algebraic candidate is the Hurwitz intertwiner AB−IAB-I for equal-trace puncture matrices, but the positive crossing in the original collar may use its inverse after changing the order of the punctures. Its exact three-point/word correspondence remains separate from the pure sewing calculation above.

The accompanying script checks the local rational identities directly from the matrices, including all six projective returns, the four native main derivatives, both auxiliary peripheral pairs, the source-coordinate equation, all six seam ratios, and the main commutator. These finite identities support the displayed derivation; they do not replace the topological cut or the remaining global oriented-word argument.
