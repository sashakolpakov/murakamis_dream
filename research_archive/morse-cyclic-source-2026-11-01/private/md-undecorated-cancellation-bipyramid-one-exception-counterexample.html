One-exception counterexample

A single unprotected spectral variation already produces a remainder

The odd-level identity at the box adjoining the protected edge does not extend to all positions. The smallest level and equator size in the stated class already give an exact counterexample: N=3N=3, m=6m=6, with the exceptional north box at position 33. In the original raw source normalization, D3(−2,−2,−2,σ,−2,−2)=2⋅95(σ−1)(σ+2). \boxed{ D_3(-2,-2,-2,\sigma,-2,-2) =2\cdot9^5(\sigma-1)(\sigma+2). } \tag{1} In particular the value at σ=2\sigma=2 is 472392472392. This is an exact polynomial identity, not an asymptotic observation. It does not contradict the proved identity for exceptional position 00, and gives no conclusion about a spectral exponential gap.
