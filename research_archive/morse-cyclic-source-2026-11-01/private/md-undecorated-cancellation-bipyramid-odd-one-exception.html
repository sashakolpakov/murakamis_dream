Odd-level support identity

An exact odd-level support identity for the marked bipyramid

This is a finite polynomial identity for the original opposite-offset bipyramid source. It is independent of any limiting spectral estimate. It concerns Newton path indices, not a change of auxiliary colors or the replacement of a source factor by a covariant coupon.
