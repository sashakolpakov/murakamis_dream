4.2 The finite clock fibre cannot have the proposed first-order lift

The obstruction is visible before any knot is chosen. Set Q=QN(1+ϵ)Q=Q_N(1+\epsilon) with ϵ2=0\epsilon^2=0. In the whole quantum torus,

VU=QN2UV=(1+N2ϵ)UV,[V,U]=N2ϵUV. VU=Q^{N^2}UV=(1+N^2\epsilon)UV, \qquad [V,U]=N^2\epsilon UV. \tag{17}

Suppose the finite orbit fibre admitted a free matrix lift over the dual numbers with both central actions reducing to identity. Then U=I+ϵAU=I+\epsilon A and V=I+ϵBV=I+\epsilon B, so their commutator is zero modulo ϵ2\epsilon^2. Equation (17) would instead give N2ϵIN^2\epsilon I, a contradiction in characteristic zero. Equivalently, invertible NN-by-NN clock matrices with TY=QYTTY=QYT would have determinants forcing QN=1Q^N=1, whereas QN=1+NϵQ^N=1+N\epsilon.

This forbids extending the root Fourier intertwiner as an ordinary finite free clock-matrix gauge through the quantum-normal direction. It does not forbid a comparison retaining differential normal operators, relative sources, or a larger module. The derivative term in the first quantum-normal construction records precisely why an off-root shift is not a finite-order linear clock. Any such bridge must retain that term and its source/co-source.

5. The boundary module explains the Mellin selected symbol

The meridian boundary module is

ΔY=ℛQ/ℛQ(Y−1). \Delta_Y=\mathcal R_Q/\mathcal R_Q(Y-1). \tag{18}

Localization of the meridian to ℂ(Q,Y)\mathbb C(Q,Y) kills it, since Y−1Y-1 becomes a unit. Its Fourier image is ℛX/ℛX(SX−1)\mathcal R_X/\mathcal R_X(S_X-1), which survives localization to ℂ(Q,X)\mathbb C(Q,X) as a rank-one trivial difference module. Consequently the two rational localizations discard different boundary information.

There is a stronger actual consequence on the covered positive-volume class. Let P=∑iai(Q,Y)TiP=\sum_i a_i(Q,Y)T^i be any denominator-cleared polynomial reduced-state annihilator. P=B(Y−1),A:=Φ(P)=Φ(B)(SX−1). P=B(Y-1),\qquad A:=\Phi(P)=\Phi(B)(S_X-1). \tag{19}

Thus the solution-companion monodromy of (20) has eigenvalue one of geometric multiplicity at least two, not merely a repeated characteristic root. The argument neither asserts a Jordan block nor determines the dimension of the entire one-sector.

This is a new consequence of combining the existing boundary-factor theorem with the established positive volume rate. It concerns this canonical sourced Mellin presentation, not every minimal meridian operator or every possible inhomogeneous factor. 6. Concrete algebraic comparison criteria, with their missing inputs

There are two useful exact routes. Neither asks for a nonzero WKB formula for the reduced meridian state across M=1M=1.

A. A shared marked normal kernel

A fixed initial-form torsion certificate can prove generic rational kernel rank one at every sufficiently large level. Explicitly, the least YY-coefficient polynomial of a rational-coefficient annihilator must have exactly one occupied root on the grid (qN,QNr)(q_N,Q_N^r); the finite binomial-curve test in torsion_initial_rank certifies this uniformly and permits non-toric factors with finitely many exceptional levels.

Suppose a proposed geometric period is actually placed in that same kernel, the same saturated ramified lattice, and the same Weyl equivariance, with the ordinary anchor equal to one. Then it is h(U)vNh(U)v_N for a central unit with h(1)=1h(1)=1. Weyl symmetry gives h(U)=h(U−1)h(U)=h(U^{-1}), hence h′(1)=0h'(1)=0. Its two marked fibre coordinates and the complete functional (10) therefore agree exactly. This does not require special-fibre injectivity. It is the existing rigid comparison criterion, not an unconditional construction of the second period.

Without generic rank one, there is a precise finite lattice test. Let KNsatK_N^{\mathrm{sat}} be the special fibre of the saturated generic kernel inside (9), let π+\pi_+ record the supplied ordinary data, and let ℓN=(N/2)e0*\ell_N=(N/2)e_0^* on its negative coordinate. Two lifts with those ordinary data have the same scalar if and only if

ℓN(KNsat∩ker⁡π+)=0. \ell_N\bigl(K_N^{\mathrm{sat}}\cap\ker\pi_+\bigr)=0. \tag{23}

This follows by subtracting lifts, and is checkable by ranks or minors after a saturated basis has been computed. Replacing the saturated fibre by the larger special matrix kernel is not justified. No uniform theorem establishing (23) for every actual minimal operator has been found in the inspected notes.

To compare whole central profiles, rather than just their marked complete scalar, the stronger source-corrected first-normal condition is required: the same whole preoperator, its actual linear source rule, and a nonzero selected symbol. The algebraic dual certificate is a row ww satisfying w(H0−I)=0w(H_0-I)=0 and wv≠0wv\ne0. It removes central multipliers by the Leibniz rule, but not arbitrary homogeneous source-kernel additions.

B. A shared sourced Mellin realization

The bounded cyclotomic-jet theorem supplies a stronger uniqueness criterion without any rank assumption. A proposed geometric realization must produce a rational function with the same fixed starting lattice and the same pole numerator of degree at most NN, and a lift through D(s−1)+1D(s-1)+1 quantum levels satisfying the same finite-window source equation with the actual initial colors. It then equals ZNZ_N identically, and its residue is KNK_N.

This criterion is finite in the quantum-normal order and retains every source coefficient. But obtaining that realization is not the same as applying the generic Fourier map to a rational main-meridian section; Sections 4–5 give exact reasons why. The source and cyclotomic lattice must be constructed and carried through the polarization change.
