Summing all such grid tuples costs only a polynomial factor. Thus the compensation is not confined to the exact pair-equality sequence.

The counterexample remains an exponential failure of a local moving/complete trace comparison. What (15) proves is that its loss of that pointwise bound does not overwhelm the actual coupled geometric budget in the specific expression (1). This does not control all index configurations of (1), an arbitrary attached type-A profile, or an arbitrary higher-valence bare component. The moving whole-hub problem therefore remains separate from the proved simultaneous-half comparison for old whole-hub pressures.
