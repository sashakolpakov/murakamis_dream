Global source and filling interfaces for trivalent bare components

Private derivation, 1 November 2026. No repository or public-checkout changes.

The component comparison used here is proved in /private/tmp/md-trivalent-bare-component-simultaneous-clamp.md. It compares every nonempty return sector of an actual typed trivalent component with a boundary stratum of the same complete positive source. The purpose of the present note is to trace the remaining hypotheses of the source, angular packet and joint filling arguments. In particular, a norm bound alone is not substituted for a conditioned local packet, and the local packet is not substituted for a whole-source estimate.

1. The class and the original action

Fix one literal strict ordered Conway construction, with its original physical quarter-turns, auxiliary circles, endpoint orders, one cut and disk framings. Retain the knot and absence-of-internal-component hypotheses in notes/actual_main_meridian_filled_volume.md, Section 1. Only the original forced final flat gate is removed. The strict two-leaf case keeps its existing parity/component qualification.

Collapse consecutive compatible products with no intervening physical turn into the usual reduced product tree. A circle vertex contains one original character or fixed crossing-power operator; an ordinary original leaf is a circle vertex of valence one. A bare vertex contains no such operator. Every edge between reduced vertices is an original physical quarter-turn. Artificial unturned edges used to resolve a higher-valence product are not edges of this reduced tree.

Assume that every maximal connected bare component belongs to at least one of these classes:

  1. it is a singleton of any allowed valence;
  2. it satisfies the existing root-independent path condition in notes/actual_main_meridian_component_extension.md, Section 1;
  3. every one of its vertices is trivalent in the full reduced tree, counting both internal and boundary incidences.

The third alternative is new here. It permits arbitrarily many adjacent trivalent bare vertices in a fixed tree, without a lower bound on their number of rigid boundary branches. It does not permit a mixture of higher-valence vertices inside such a component unless the second alternative is separately satisfied.

7. Exact boundary of this extension

No estimate here applies the trivalent return argument to an artificial binary resolution of a higher-valence hub. Such a resolution has unturned complementary-index edges, and adjacent moving returns can occur. The independent-return hypothesis and the low-arm allocation would then fail. Mixed-valence adjacent components outside the old path condition remain untreated by this argument.

Nor does (6) assert that every old internal high-label sector is negligible. Some are leading, and the clean packet deliberately retains them. The small remainder consists of nonempty return sectors, boundary/probe-high sectors, and the actual compact complement of the original strict window. This distinction is required both for the absolute source comparison and for the complex packet.

All constants are allowed to depend on the fixed presentation, its orientation/parity data, and the chosen compact disk where appropriate. Nothing is uniform for trees or filling slopes growing with the level. No source commutativity has been used to infer a tangle isotopy or a different knot complement.
