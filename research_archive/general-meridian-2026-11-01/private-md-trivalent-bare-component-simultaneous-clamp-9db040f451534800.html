Simultaneous half-boundary clamping for typed trivalent bare components

Private derivation, 1 November 2026. No repository changes.

This proof retains all labels on internal edges of a bare component. The complete maximum need not put such a label below one half. It instead compares every genuine return sector with a boundary stratum of the original complete positive source. The comparison reoptimizes each intervening old region jointly. Its essential source restriction is that every bare vertex is trivalent and every internal edge is an original physical quarter-turn. Actual orientation types then make return vertices nonadjacent.

6. Consequence for the finite source and the remaining scope

At a fixed finite trivalent component there are finitely many old/return assignments and polynomially many integer states. The paired edge factors and all prescribed typed scalars are bounded on |s|≤R<1|s|\le R<1. Uniform closed coefficient rates and (18) therefore bound the entire absolute sum of all nonempty return sectors by eN(F0−δ+o(1)). e^{N(F_0-\delta+o(1))}. \tag{19} No return cancellation is used for this estimate. In sectors without returns, the bounded paired weights are compared termwise with the original positive complete contraction. A high original boundary input or high output probe has the old complete-component strict gap. This is the arbitrary-array theorem in notes/actual_conway_unflagged_core_probe_bound.md; it permits low rates g+cg+c and high rates cc. Thus the component has its original full norm budget, and its clean part retains all old-supported internal labels while keeping its boundary inputs and output low. The remainder has a strict spectral deficit. Inherited full-cone deficits pass by affine homogeneity in the constants, as in the published component refinement.

Near the original strict maximizing state, every cubic has total normalized degree less than 3/23/2. Consequently its raw polynomial trace has degree less than 2N−12N-1 on a fixed window, and the moving trace equals the complete trace exactly there. The regular internal labels may cross half, but stay in a compact subinterval of (0,1)(0,1), where the canonical paired-edge interpolation is regular. Circle-incident labels remain in the original regular low interval. These are the corrected local inputs for the grouped-window argument; no high cutoff is imposed on bare–bare edges.

The theorem established here is a component comparison for arbitrary-size trivalent bare components in the actual typed tree. It is independent of the earlier many-boundary condition. It can coexist with singleton higher-valence components and other already controlled components in the mutual source induction. A full filled theorem still requires stating this enlarged class in the global source-to-window argument and retaining the joint filling contour and exact scalar ledger; those are not republished or modified here.

Higher-valence bare components are not settled by this proof. Their binary resolutions have unturned complementary-label edges, on which adjacent moving return vertices can occur. A raw low higher-valence product can also have a return trace without a high exterior incidence. The independent-return and unique-low-arm accounting above must not be applied to such artificial resolutions.
