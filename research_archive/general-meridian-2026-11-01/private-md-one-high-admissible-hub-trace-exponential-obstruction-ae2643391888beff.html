An exponential obstruction to the complete-admissible whole-hub comparison

Private proof, 1 November 2026. No repository changes.

The proposed pointwise bound is false even for a fixed valence of nine, at both level parities. The counterexample has exactly one high index and satisfies every complete pairwise support inequality. Its complete trace is strictly positive. The proof uses exact polynomial division and real divided differences, rather than a numerical fit or an asymptotic saddle.

5. Scope

This disproves the proposed uniform pointwise comparison |ϕs(∏ei)|≤NCRϕ0(∏ei)|\phi_s(\prod e_i)|\le N^{C_R}\phi_0(\prod e_i) on all complete-admissible tuples, for fixed valence nine. The obstruction is present even with a strictly positive complete denominator and exactly one high input. No numerical evidence is used in the proof.

The family lies on complete pair-equality faces. It does not disprove a comparison restricted to a fixed strict compact interior window. Nor does it compare a whole physical Conway contraction with its original coupled geometric action: the surrounding coefficient profiles and their optimization can supply a different global budget. It therefore gives neither a counterexample to the volume conjecture nor an obstruction to the already proved trivalent theorem. It does rule out using this particular whole-hub pointwise inequality to extend that theorem without an additional boundary argument.
