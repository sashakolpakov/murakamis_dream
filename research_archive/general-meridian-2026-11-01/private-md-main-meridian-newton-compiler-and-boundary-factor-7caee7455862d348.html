A typed Newton compiler and its exact main-meridian boundary factor

Private proof, 1 November 2026. No repository file is changed.

There are two distinct operations in a main-meridian deformation: changing the polynomial coordinates of a coupon, and changing the multiplication and turn tensors of the physical source. The first operation has a uniform polynomial bound in the microscopic color. The second is not controlled by that bound: formerly zero multiplication channels can open. This note proves the basis statement, constructs an exact formal compiler for a fixed oriented Conway tree, and retains the actual primitive closing trace. Its scalar normalization supplies one factor HNH_N, but does not force the remaining regular contraction to tend to one.

6. What support and normalization do not prove

For opposite colors, the fixed abstract multiplication retains the complete Newton support. For equal colors, a formerly forbidden product need not vanish. One exact way to see the change is to keep the complete basis as a vector-space basis and write Ps=P0+Δs,Δs=YN+Y−N−2=2cos⁡(2πs)−2. P_s=P_0+\Delta_s,\qquad \Delta_s=Y^N+Y^{-N}-2=2\cos(2\pi s)-2. If i+j≥Ni+j\ge N, then the complete-basis product has a polynomial factorization ei0ej0=P0hije_i^0e_j^0=P_0h_{ij}, with deg⁡hij≤N−2\deg h_{ij}\le N-2. Therefore in the moving algebra ei0ej0≡−Δshij(mod⁡Ps). e_i^0e_j^0\equiv-\Delta_s h_{ij}\pmod{P_s}. \tag{25} For fixed microscopic ss, Δs\Delta_s is of order one, not of order N−1N^{-1}. A polynomially controlled basis change does not suppress this new term. If one insists on a constant opposite-color algebra at every step, the same issue is transported into the exact typed turns and carrier coefficients, rather than removed.

Finally let PNP_N denote the actual ordinary-color interpolation polynomial and let ρN\rho_N be the canonical regular profile. The already proved exact decomposition is FN(ez/(2N))KN=HN(z)ρN(z)+πN(z),πN(z)=PN(ez/N)KN. \frac{F_N(e^{z/(2N)})}{K_N} =H_N(z)\rho_N(z)+\pi_N(z),\qquad \pi_N(z)=\frac{P_N(e^{z/N})}{K_N}. \tag{26} Thus the coefficient contraction of (23) is precisely AK,N=ρN+πNHN. A_{K,N}=\rho_N+\frac{\pi_N}{H_N}. \tag{27} This identifies the pole in (24) and the required subtraction in the same primitive trace normalization.

For knots with positive complete exponential rate, πN\pi_N is exponentially small on compact sets, but its quotient by HNH_N still has the actual finite poles. After subtracting it, a bounded signed contraction gives normality of ρN\rho_N and subsequential limits g(z)=H(z)a(z),H(z)=2sinh⁡(z/2)/z,a(0)=1. g(z)=H(z)a(z),\qquad H(z)=2\sinh(z/2)/z,\qquad a(0)=1. Boundedness and the boundary normalization do not force a=1a=1. Even reciprocal central multipliers with value one and vanishing first derivative at U=1U=1 can change this limiting function while preserving all root orbit values and the marked ramified normal fibre. They are not new actual knot sources; they show why the weaker data cannot imply the missing estimate or identification.

The concrete advance is therefore an exact whole-tangle formal compiler with a polynomially controlled Newton basis deformation and a fully retained boundary factor. A general source theorem still needs control of the typed operation tensors and the resulting signed contraction, including the channels opened in (25), followed by a condition that identifies its normalized regular limit.
