Polynomial control of moving Casimir twist blocks, and the first resonance obstruction

Private proof, 31 October 2026. No frozen or public file is changed.

This note isolates a genuine local part of the main-meridian problem. Fixed integral twist blocks have polynomially bounded canonical Casimir representatives on every compact disk before the first ordinary-color resonance. Multiplication in the moving Casimir algebra also has polynomial cost. The physical crossing itself gives an exact obstruction to extending that particular norm through the first ordinary resonance. These are statements about the actual crossing, not auxiliary-circle color packets.

3. Products in the moving algebra do not cost an exponential

For a polynomial ff of degree at most 2N−22N-2, write its Chebyshev expansion as f=a0+∑j=12N−2aj𝒯jf=a_0+\sum_{j=1}^{2N-2}a_j\mathcal T_j. Its coefficients are bounded by a constant times ∥f∥[−2,2]\|f\|_{[-2,2]}, by their cosine integral. In the algebra (2), putting c=U+U−1c=U+U^{-1}, 𝒯N≡−c,𝒯N+k≡−c𝒯k−𝒯N−k(1≤k≤N−2). \mathcal T_N\equiv-c,\qquad \mathcal T_{N+k}\equiv-c\mathcal T_k-\mathcal T_{N-k} \quad(1\le k\le N-2). \tag{11} This is the exact product identity 𝒯N𝒯k=𝒯N+k+𝒯N−k\mathcal T_N\mathcal T_k=\mathcal T_{N+k}+\mathcal T_{N-k}. Its derivative costs only another factor O(N2)O(N^2) by the polynomial Markov inequality (or the same cosine expansion).

In particular, for two canonical representatives, multiplication and reduction have polynomial cost in the supremum-plus-derivative norm. The complete Hermite norm is polynomially equivalent to this norm on degree-<N<N representatives: the reverse inequality is the established complete Hermite interpolation bound, and the forward inequality follows by sampling. Thus (12) also gives a polynomial-cost multiplication estimate in that full norm. Iterating a fixed number of products remains polynomial. No positivity, scalar source projection, or deletion of a derivative direction is used here.

This local algebra bound does not supply the physical turn between same-oriented and oppositely oriented color pairs. That turn is the remaining typed source map; the two algebras cannot be identified merely because their complete fibers agree.

4. The first ordinary resonance genuinely breaks the polynomial chart

For the fundamental crossing, at s=1s=1 one has c0=c1=q+q−1,λ0=1,λ1=−1. c_0=c_1=q+q^{-1},\qquad \lambda_0=1,\quad\lambda_1=-1. \tag{13} Consequently no polynomial with coefficients holomorphic through this point can take the prescribed two values. More explicitly, c0−c1=q−3(Q−1)(Y−Q2/Y),λ0−λ1=1+Q/Y. c_0-c_1=q^{-3}(Q-1)(Y-Q^2/Y),\qquad \lambda_0-\lambda_1=1+Q/Y. \tag{14} Since dY/ds=(2πi/N)YdY/ds=(2\pi i/N)Y, dds(c0−c1)|s=1=4πiN(q−q−1)=−8πsin⁡(π/N)N. \left.\frac{d}{ds}(c_0-c_1)\right|_{s=1} =\frac{4\pi i}{N}(q-q^{-1}) =-\frac{8\pi\sin(\pi/N)}N. Integrating the polynomial derivative between them shows that its supremum must be at least the modulus of (15). The polynomial chart thus has a genuine pole at each fixed level, not merely a worsening asymptotic constant.

The physical finite crossing matrix is regular in s, as is every actual knot cut scalar. At this ordinary-color point the Casimir no longer generates the relevant crossing action by a regular polynomial chart. The pole of its generic representative cancels only after evaluation in the degenerating physical realization or after a different source adapter. It cannot be removed by imposing the complete Hermite norm alone. This obstruction concerns this representation and does not assert a pole of FNF_N, a pole of its regular remainder, or failure of their possible scaled profiles.

An even power has equal values at the two nodes in (13); the fundamental-crossing obstruction must therefore not be asserted for every twist exponent. Proposition (4) applies uniformly to all fixed powers on the stated pre-resonance disk without needing such further case analysis.

5. Relation to the paired-sine insertion test

The actual figure-eight identity in figure_eight_whole_source_identity.md is fN(Y)=∑n=0N−1∏r=1n(Y+Y−1−Qr−Q−r). f_N(Y)=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1} \prod_{r=1}^{n}(Y+Y^{-1}-Q^r-Q^{-r}). \tag{16} At Y=ez/NY=e^{z/N}, each term divided by its positive complete value is exactly ∏r=1n(1+sinh⁡2(z/(2N))sin⁡2(πr/N)). \prod_{r=1}^{n} \left(1+\frac{\sinh^2(z/(2N))}{\sin^2(\pi r/N)}\right). \tag{17} Thus a uniform paired-sine bound acts after the cancellations already eliminated by (16). It is qualitatively stronger than applying an absolute bound to the unreduced crossing paths.

The same Newton factors occur in the genuine whole five-crossing source, proved in five_two_cyclotomic_transform_proof.md, equation (1), but with the nontrivial coefficients Cn(Q)=(−1)nQ−n(n+3)/2Hn(Q−n−1)C_n(Q)=(-1)^nQ^{-n(n+3)/2}H_n(Q^{-n-1}). Its source is signed/complex. actual_multichannel_newton_green_transport.md proves exponential growth of some of those coefficients and retains a nonsharp absolute barrier. Consequently (17) alone cannot be pushed through that signed outer sum using positivity. Neither this source identity nor an equivalent paired insertion was found for every strict Conway tree in the current compiler notes.

The concrete local advance here is (4) and (12), with the exact chart obstruction (15). A whole main-meridian theorem still needs the physical typed turn/recoupling and an estimate of the resulting signed source, or an exact elimination analogous to (16) which exposes a controlled paired insertion before taking absolute values.
