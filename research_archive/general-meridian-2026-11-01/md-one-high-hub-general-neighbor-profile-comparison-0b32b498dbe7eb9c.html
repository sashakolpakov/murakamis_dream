2. What coefficient hypothesis permits a quantum conclusion

Suppose the central moving junction has BB low incidences u1,…,uBu_1,\ldots,u_B and one high incidence zz, with v=1−zv=1-z. The fixed-polynomial full-jet and Chebyshev-folding estimates give its absolute tensor rate at most ∑ig(ui)\sum_i g(u_i). If its attached low rigid branches satisfy their original coefficient cones, their additional contribution is at most ∑ig(ui)\sum_i g(u_i), with their constants retained.

To use the classical comparison, the actual neighboring branch must have a coefficient estimate at its prescribed output of the form |[e⌊vd⌋]AN,s|≤exp⁡{N(fA(v)+o(1))}, |[e_{\lfloor vd\rfloor}]A_{N,s}| \le \exp\{N(f_A(v)+o(1))\}, \tag{6} with the appropriate actual rounding, full normals, and uniformity in the parameters under consideration. The existence of the old profile fAf_A, or inequality (2), alone does not imply (6) for a moving subtree.

Under (6), the whole contribution at the prescribed central indices has rate 2∑i=1Bg(ui)+fA(v), 2\sum_{i=1}^B g(u_i)+f_A(v), \tag{7} plus the explicitly retained central boundary constants. It follows from (5) that it is separated from the same original pressure whenever FB,2−f2(v)−2∑i=1Bg(ui)>0. \boxed{\quad F_{B,2}-f_2(v)-2\sum_{i=1}^B g(u_i)>0. \quad} \tag{8} This criterion depends only on the two genuine reference product profiles and the current labels. It is valid for every neighboring AA satisfying the hypotheses; it is not a new valence-only test. A fixed positive margin persists on a closed neighborhood and after summing polynomially many label tuples.

For complete-admissible central tuples, every ui≤vu_i\le v. A more convenient, weaker sufficient condition is FB,2−f2(v)−2Bg(min⁡(v,1/4))>0. A coarse spectral bound can fail to provide the required margin even when the actual contraction is smaller.

There is a useful class where (6) is already a direct exact source consequence. If the neighboring constant-quotient junction is the unturned product of K≥2K\ge2 rigid coefficient-cone inputs, then at output v<1/2v<1/2 the complete positive Newton support forces every input index to be at most the output index. This is a whole positive product estimate at the actual boundary value. Its inputs may carry their independent physical colors and fixed half-twist signs through the established cones.

Thus (8) is an unconditional source criterion for the indicated sectors of the literal two-hub expression C(hBT(hK))C(h^B T(h^K)) in the type where the first junction is moving and the second constant. A more complicated neighboring moving subtree requires a separate proof of (6).
