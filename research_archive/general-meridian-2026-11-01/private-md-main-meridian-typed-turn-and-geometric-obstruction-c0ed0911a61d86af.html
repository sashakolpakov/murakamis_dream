Main-meridian colors, exact typed turns, and the limitation of the present geometric cells

Private working note, 31 October 2026.

The finite-dimensional source can be varied in the main meridian without making a turn singular. The distinction is between the vector-space turn, which is regular, and presenting every coupon in a single moving Casimir algebra, which is incompatible with dual colors. This note gives the turn, its first variation, and a bound uniform in the level on a fixed microscopic color disk. It also identifies why the existing geometric Conway cells cannot acquire a nonparabolic main meridian simply by changing their parameter values.

The geometric normal period already constructed from the actual braid coefficients is an inherited input. No conclusion here identifies its remaining framing period with the full finite sum or proves a new asymptotic volume formula.

4. The exact obstruction in the existing geometric cells

In the marked strict Conway construction, each leaf has white-face maps W0,…,W3W_0,\ldots,W_3, rational in two variables E,RE,R. At its main corner CjC_j, the actual small main meridian is μj=Wj−1−1Wjor its inverse. \mu_j=W_{j-1}^{-1}W_j \quad\text{or its inverse}. \tag{19} The two white maps have equal derivatives in local coordinates at the corner: E−1E^{-1} at C0,C2C_0,C_2, and EE at C1,C3C_1,C_3. Hence (19) has multiplier exactly one for every E,RE,R in the domain. It remains nonidentity near completeness and therefore remains parabolic.

Consequently no change of the auxiliary colors, no holomorphic substitution E=E(u),R=R(u)E=E(u),R=R(u), and no simultaneous change of world frame can make these formulas realize the marked main multiplier M2=e2uM^2=e^{2u}. A genuine extension must change the face maps or matching data. It cannot be obtained solely by changing values of the old parameters.

The marked multiplier is essential to this first-order statement. The trace squared of a meridian with eigenvalues e±ue^{\pm u} is 4cosh⁡2u4\cosh^2u, whose derivative at zero vanishes. A first-order unmarked trace test would miss the obstruction; the logarithmic marked multiplier has derivative 22.

5. The geometric germ that already exists, and the remaining comparison

For a fixed hyperbolic knot in the strict class, the actual geometric normal-period calculation supplies a different route. It uses the original oriented braid coefficients and the positive marked gluing Jacobian. In its coordinates u=Log⁡M,U=2u,V=2Log⁡Lintr, u=\operatorname{Log}M,\qquad U=2u,\qquad V=2\operatorname{Log}L_{\mathrm{intr}}, the unframed geometric germ is unramified over UU, and its cusp derivative is nonzero and nonreal. The actual coefficient critical scheme has two framing directions; its transverse Hessian is nondegenerate. Its formal normal period is nonzero and has the marked geometric connection after the specified gauge comparison.

These are inherited results, not consequences newly proved by the turn formula. One can also convert an oriented tangle into an oriented braid, preserving its exact finite contraction after retaining the actual dualities, pivot, and zero-framing correction. Equality of the finite contractions alone does not identify a selected normal stationary block under that conversion. It does not evaluate the remaining framing period or determine the coefficient of the geometric block in the complete finite sum.

Two concrete obligations remain for a Conway-based extension.

  1. Express both color types, their typed turns, and the first variation (10) in coordinates with controlled large-NN estimates. Equations (8)–(18) provide exact algebraic data, but no spectral-to-physical norm comparison.
  2. Construct new marked white/shaded maps with main multiplier M2M^2, or compare the typed tangle phase to the existing braid critical scheme, retaining the primitive longitude, framing directions, pivot, and absolute action constant. Equation (19) proves that mere reparametrization cannot suffice.

The new conclusions are the regular turn (8), its uniform microscopic bound (9), its exact derivative (10), and the full constant opposite-color order (16) with actual cut (18). They hold at every N≥2N\ge2. They neither rule out a main-meridian extension nor select the finite quantum period from the already existing geometric normal block.
