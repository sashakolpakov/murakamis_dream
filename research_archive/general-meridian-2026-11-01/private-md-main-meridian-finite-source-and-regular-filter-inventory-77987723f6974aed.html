1. What the strict Conway estimates do and do not vary

The strict-tree source theorem does not presently give an analytic family in the main unrolled color. In actual_odd_conway_complex_color_packet.md, Section 1, its variables are the auxiliary-circle deficits ti=hi/Nt_i=h_i/N, and its physical leaves are T(RϵiLN−hi)T(R^{\epsilon_i}L_{N-h_i}). The main strands still carry the fixed module VN−1V_{N-1}. The larger scope of actual_conway_general_core_packet_and_gap.md changes the allowed tree and includes quarter-index states; it does not change that main module. The coefficient-cone theorem in actual_conway_unflagged_core_probe_bound.md still uses the complete quotient, its repeated nodes, the basis reversal Ter=eN−1−rTe_r=e_{N-1-r}, and the complete marked pairing.

There is a geometric check of the distinction: actual_conway_general_mask_marked_filling.md, Section 5, computes the two adjacent white transitions and proves that the main-meridian multiplier remains identically one throughout its auxiliary deformation. It is a parabolic main cusp throughout. The auxiliary filling equations and their complex packet consequently do not provide a main-meridian saddle by changing the name of a parameter. The pointed quantum-A note states the same limitation explicitly near its end.

There is, however, an exact finite source for the main-meridian family, and its regular remainder can also be written as an exact finite source. Sections 2–5 give formulas with their full normalization. Their analytic limitations are stated in Section 7.

3. A useful but strictly precontraction bound

On |s|≤R|s|\le R, set AR=πReπR/2A_R=\pi R e^{\pi R}/2. For 1≤m<N1\le m<N,

|1−Qs−m||1−Q−m|≤1+ARmin⁡(m,N−m). \frac{|1-Q^{s-m}|}{|1-Q^{-m}|} \le 1+\frac{A_R}{\min(m,N-m)}. \tag{6}

Indeed |Qs−1|≤2πReπR/N|Q^s-1|\le2\pi R e^{\pi R}/N, whereas |1−Q−m|≥4min⁡(m,N−m)/N|1-Q^{-m}|\ge4\min(m,N-m)/N. The harmonic sum over these denominators is at most 2(1+log⁡N)2(1+\log N). Every Pochhammer quotient in (5) is consequently bounded by e2ARN2ARe^{2A_R}N^{2A_R}. Its explicit q-power is bounded by a constant on this disk, since the head exponents have absolute value at most 2d2d. The fixed pivot/framing factor has modulus at most eπR|κD|e^{\pi R|\kappa_D|}. Therefore

sup|s|≤R|Wσ(s)|≤CD,RN2LAR|Wσ(0)| \sup_{|s|\le R}|W_\sigma(s)| \le C_{D,R}N^{2LA_R}|W_\sigma(0)| \tag{7}

uniformly over all actual cut paths and both level parities. Cauchy’s formula on a slightly larger disk gives polynomial bounds for every fixed derivative of the relative insertion as well. Zeros at integral shifted colors cause no problem, since only the nonzero complete denominator was divided.

This is not a polynomial comparison with |KN||K_N|. Summing (7) produces the absolute cut-state sum ∑σ|Wσ(0)|\sum_\sigma|W_\sigma(0)|, whose exponential rate need not be the selected knot rate. The strict Conway proof controls a different, recoupled signed source with its own cancellation estimates. A transfer of (7) through that recoupling has not been established.

Also |s|≤R|s|\le R means |log⁡M|=O(1/N)|\log M|=O(1/N). It is not a fixed neighborhood of M=1M=1. The constants in (7) are not uniform for |s||s| proportional to NN.

4. A finite regularization filter, including negative exponents

Write the even meridian family as FN(M)=fN(Y)F_N(M)=f_N(Y). Its evenness is statewise in the finite cut sum: even_meridian_norm_descent.md, Section 2, proves that the total x-exponent

Eσ=dκD+∑+(−a−b0−k)+∑−(a+b0−k) E_\sigma=d\kappa_D+ \sum_+(-a-b_0-k)+\sum_-(a+b_0-k) \tag{8}

is even by the integer-position conservation law. Thus every path is a Laurent monomial in YY, times finite Pochhammers in YY, without first invoking cancellation between paths.

Let INI_N be interpolation on the N−1N-1 nontrivial Nth roots, with values in polynomials of degree at most N−2N-2. The ordinary-color pointing gives INfN=PNordI_N f_N=P_N^{\mathrm{ord}}. Define the linear map

𝒟Nf=f−INfSN. \mathcal D_Nf=\frac{f-I_Nf}{S_N}. \tag{9}

For any integer exponent write its Euclidean division as ℓ=kN+t\ell=kN+t, where k∈ℤk\in\mathbb Z and 0≤t<N0\le t<N, and put U=YNU=Y^N. Then

𝒟N(Yℓ)=Yt(Y−1)Gk(U)+𝟏t=N−1,Gk(U)=Uk−1U−1. \boxed{\quad \mathcal D_N(Y^\ell) =Y^t(Y-1)G_k(U)+\mathbf1_{t=N-1},\qquad G_k(U)=\frac{U^k-1}{U-1}. \quad} \tag{10}

Here GkG_k is a Laurent polynomial, with

Gk(U)={∑j=0k−1Uj,k>0,0,k=0,−∑j=k−1Uj,k<0. G_k(U)= \begin{cases} \sum_{j=0}^{k-1}U^j,&k>0,\\ 0,&k=0,\\ -\sum_{j=k}^{-1}U^j,&k<0. \end{cases} \tag{11}

To prove (10), the root values of YℓY^\ell equal those of YtY^t. If t≤N−2t\le N-2, its interpolant is YtY^t. If t=N−1t=N-1, its interpolant is YN−1−SN=−∑j=0N−2YjY^{N-1}-S_N=-\sum_{j=0}^{N-2}Y^j, which has the required degree. Now use U−1=(Y−1)SNU-1=(Y-1)S_N. This proves every case, with no division by a sampled zero.

For example ℓ=−1\ell=-1 has k=−1,t=N−1k=-1,t=N-1, and (10) gives 𝒟N(Y−1)=Y−1\mathcal D_N(Y^{-1})=Y^{-1}. For ℓ=−N\ell=-N, it gives −Y−N(Y−1)-Y^{-N}(Y-1). For ℓ=N−1\ell=N-1, it gives exactly one. These examples verify both the negative-k sign and the exceptional interpolation row.

At completeness the filter reduces to

RNreg(1)=∑ℓ≡N−1(modN)[Yℓ]fN(Y)=1N∑j=0N−1QjfN(Qj). R_N^{\mathrm{reg}}(1) =\sum_{\ell\equiv N-1\ (\mathrm{mod}\ N)}[Y^\ell]f_N(Y) =\frac1N\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}Q^j f_N(Q^j). \tag{12}

The actual ordinary-color pointing further writes this as RNreg(1)=[KN+∑j=1N−1QjCj(q)]/NR_N^{\mathrm{reg}}(1)=[K_N+\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}Q^jC_j(q)]/N, consistent with PNord(1)=−∑j=1N−1QjCj(q)P_N^{\mathrm{ord}}(1)=-\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}Q^jC_j(q). These are exact signed extraction formulas, not estimates of their individual coefficient contributions.

Equation (12) is already the cyclic-trace formula in regularized_kashaev_period.md, Section 5. It is not being presented as a new complete-point identity. I checked that note, central_source_freedom_in_regular_profiles.md, and scaled_meridian_regular_profile.md, including their division and central-multiplier formulas. They supply the canonical quotient, the complete trace and the whole-profile comparison; I did not find the explicit all-integer monomial rule (10) there. It is recorded as an elementary formula for the existing regularization, not as a new invariant or a novelty claim.

5. The regular remainder is an exact finite cut sum

Each path weight in Section 2 has the form

cσYeσ∏v=1L(YQ−av;Q)kv, c_\sigma Y^{e_\sigma} \prod_{v=1}^{L}(YQ^{-a_v};Q)_{k_v}, \tag{13}

(YQ−a;Q)k=∑r=0k(−1)rQr(r−1)/2−ar[kr]QYr. (YQ^{-a};Q)_k =\sum_{r=0}^{k}(-1)^rQ^{r(r-1)/2-ar} {k\brack r}_QY^r. \tag{14}

There is one extra integer index per crossing, with its original bound 0≤r≤k<N0\le r\le k<N. All factorial denominators remain root units. For the fixed braid the resulting number of states is polynomial in NN; no exponentially large binary expansion is necessary. Write the resulting exact Laurent expansion as a state sum

fN(Y)=∑σ,𝒓cσ,𝒓Yℓσ,𝒓. f_N(Y)=\sum_{\sigma,\mathbf r}c_{\sigma,\mathbf r}Y^{\ell_{\sigma,\mathbf r}}.

Every coefficient in (15) still comes from the literal cut source.

7. The first missing analytic lemma

There is no missing definition of the main-meridian scalar or of its regular remainder: (3)–(5) and (15) are exact finite sources. What is missing is a cancellation-preserving estimate for their contraction, compatible with the geometric complete block.

For a microscopic main-meridian statement, a concrete first target is a bound for ρN(z)\rho_N(z) on a fixed complex disk, in the actual normalization of (17), followed by identification of its selected limit. A termwise bound such as (7), a polynomial bound on each filter in (15), or the existing auxiliary-color packet does not furnish that bound. Even local boundedness alone supplies only subsequences; a source comparison is still needed to select their limit. The existing central-multiplier examples show why the root equation, ordinary values, and a qualitative exponential-type condition alone are insufficient.

For a fixed main-meridian neighborhood, the obligation is stronger. One needs an exact main-color Conway compiler or another source-preserving reduction that retains the moving nodes, the dual-color/cup data, the cutoff currents and the regularization filter; then one needs a uniform nonzero geometric packet and a bound on its complement in that new source. At s=0s=0 the complete module is self-dual; under α=d+s\alpha=d+s, its dual color is d−sd-s. Existing complete basis reversal and first Hermite normals cannot simply be declared to give this full two-color family.

The nearby-zero obstruction for the reduced family prevents replacing this obligation by a uniform nonzero ratio germ for FNF_N itself. It does not prove or disprove such a statement for the regular remainder. The scalar volume theorem at M=1M=1, and the exact pointed operator reconstruction there, remain unchanged.

The actionable continuation is therefore (15) together with the precise cut insertion (5): either recouple this whole filtered source into a geometric packet, or directly control its signed residue classes. No such control has been inferred in this report.
