3. The two central divisors and the finite intersection

4. The dual-number lift obstruction

Set Q=QN(1+ε)Q=Q_N(1+\varepsilon), ε2=0\varepsilon^2=0. For a qq-rational presentation this means choosing the unique local branch

q=qN(1+ε/2). q=q_N(1+\varepsilon/2). \tag{12}

No global QQ-descent is required. In the quantum torus,

VU=QN2UV=(1+N2ε)UV. VU=Q^{N^2}UV=(1+N^2\varepsilon)UV. \tag{13}

If a finite free matrix realization lifted the clock fiber with both U,VU,V reducing to the identity, write U=I+εA1U=I+\varepsilon A_1, V=I+εB1V=I+\varepsilon B_1. Their commutator is zero, whereas (13) makes it N2εIN^2\varepsilon I. This is impossible in characteristic zero. The obstruction concerns a finite free clock-matrix lift. It does not exclude a comparison using differential normal operators, a larger module, or relative source data. Those qualifications in the draft are necessary and correctly stated.
