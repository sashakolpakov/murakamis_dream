Interior-spectrum obstruction for the original pair

The original paired reference does not control the whole cap interval

1 November 2026. Private finite-source theorem. No public files were edited.

The known cap/central contraction keeps the original internal face demand and gains cancellation at the spectral pair (+2,−2)(+2,-2). Its extension to small complex neighborhoods of those two values also retains that demand. The same paired-reference comparison cannot hold on the whole real cap interval. This note proves an explicit exponential violation at the pair (0,−2)(0,-2), without shifting a face target or attaching an exterior to the internal face.

The qualification is substantial: the large values in this example lie between spectral nodes at which both the value and the first normal vanish. It is therefore an obstruction to the whole-interval supremum comparison, not a lower bound for the original filling functional or a closed knot invariant.

1. Existing statements and the exact pair

The inputs read before this calculation were the all-demand contraction, its odd counterpart, the incidence/disjoint-assembly lemma, the cap-star cut, the whole-star spectral-strip theorem, and the two-spectrum neighborhood theorem. Their respective restrictions remain:

  • disjoint pair replacement permits shared exterior variables but requires each extracted internal face to occur only in its own pair;
  • shifting that internal demand already has an exponential counterexample;
  • the whole-star theorem handles all real neighboring central spectra, but the cap remains near +2+2, and the conclusion is a relative weighted-star improvement;
  • the two-spectrum pair theorem controls neighborhoods of (+2,−2)(+2,-2), not the entire real square.

4. The active-node qualification

There is an exact reason not to turn (18) into a claim about the filling functional. At either parity, retain the low range ℓ≤M=⌊N/2⌋−1\ell\le M=\lfloor N/2\rfloor-1, and allow any fixed value of the neighbor’s spectral parameter. The same finite sum has the common factor

Tℓ(σ,τ)=(∏r=ℓ+1d(σ−cr))Pℓ(σ,τ),deg⁡σPℓ≤ℓ. T_\ell(\sigma,\tau)= \left(\prod_{r=\ell+1}^{d}(\sigma-c_r)\right)P_\ell(\sigma,\tau), \qquad \deg_\sigma P_\ell\le\ell. \tag{19}

This follows term by term from (5), so it needs no inverse or divided recurrence. For every distinct double node indexed by a>ℓa>\ell, both rows a,d−aa,d-a occur in the product. Therefore

Tℓ(ca,τ)=∂σTℓ(ca,τ)=0(a>ℓ). T_\ell(c_a,\tau)= \partial_\sigma T_\ell(c_a,\tau)=0\quad(a>\ell). \tag{20}

At an odd level the simple node −2-2 is also a zero. At the remaining double nodes a≤ℓa\le\ell, the reflected row d−ad-a remains, so the value is still zero but the first normal may survive. Consequently the original full-Hermite insertion of any fixed integral power pp reduces exactly to

ℒNpTℓ(⋅,τ)=1N2∑a=0ℓ(4−ca2)fp(ca)∂σTℓ(ca,τ). \mathcal L_N^pT_\ell(\,\cdot\,,\tau) =\frac1{N^2}\sum_{a=0}^{\ell} (4-c_a^2)f_p(c_a) \partial_\sigma T_\ell(c_a,\tau). \tag{21}

The original Gaussian lift in fpf_p, all normal factors, and all remaining face targets are unchanged. No simple-node value or derivative of fpf_p survives because the numerator values are zero.

For the example (18), the active nodes have ca≥cL>0c_a\ge c_L>0. The large value at σ=0\sigma=0 lies outside this active-node band. The nearby spectral nodes are double zeros of the extracted coefficient. Thus the whole-interval supremum comparison fails more strongly than is necessary for evaluating this particular finite functional.

5. Consequence for the next argument, and stopping scope

The existing pair replacement cannot be extended to every cap spectrum with the same coefficientwise endpoint comparator. This failure persists after the correct internal face has been extracted, so it is different from the earlier shifted-demand counterexample.

For the actual filling problem, (21) identifies a narrower unresolved question: control the coherent active normal sum, or its signed assembly with the rest of the original source. Neither (18) nor (21) supplies that estimate. In particular, no lower bound for a filled period, no new knot class, and no contradiction to a volume conjecture is asserted.
