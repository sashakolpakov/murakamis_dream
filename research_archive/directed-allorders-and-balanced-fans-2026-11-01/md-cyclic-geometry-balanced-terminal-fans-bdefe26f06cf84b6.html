Two-sided terminal fans and complete-radius balance

Private working theorem, 1 November 2026. The conclusion concerns an actual augmented diagram and its one marked complete packing. It is not a claim that arbitrary cyclic contractions reduce to the scalar kernels of a tangle-operation tree.

5. Every added port already balances

At an insertion with current middle bb, the new port has outer sum A′=b+b1A'=b+b_1 and middle pair (x,y)(x,y). From (2),

A′≥A=x+y≥max⁡(x,y). A'\ge A=x+y\ge\max(x,y).

Consequently

(A′+x)(A′+y)≥max⁡(x2,y2).(16) (A'+x)(A'+y)\ge\max(x^2,y^2). \qquad(16)

The source-normalized entropy inequality F(a,t,d)≥12log⁡(a+t+d)F(a,t,d)\ge\tfrac12\log(a+t+d), strict when two weights are positive, shows that (16) implies the required strict balance for every positive middle. A zero middle needs no logarithm; its overloaded one-sided finite sector is absent. Since A′>0A'>0, this covers all protected-zero cases too.

Later fan steps subdivide only the retained port’s triangle. No previously added port changes again. Performing the construction at different original ports likewise preserves their already fixed curvatures and all previously settled ports. There are finitely many original ports. Hence the procedure terminates with one actual marked complete packing in which every port balances strictly.

The construction therefore proves a geometric realization theorem: every fixed certified simple-white core has a finite two-sided terminal decoration, with even fan sizes, preserving its knot endpoint pairing and original marked edge, whose entire complete packing satisfies the true radius-balance inequalities. The sizes depend on the original packing; no uniform count or bridge-width assertion is made.

6. What happens to the shared magnetic-flow graph

This refinement does not turn a cyclic source into a scalar tree source. In the cubic dual, the old shaded vertex (x,b,y)(x,b,y), whose matching edge is xyxy, is replaced along its nonmatching physical cycle by

(x,b,w)−−(x,y,w)−−(y,b,w).(17) (x,b,w)\;--\;(x,y,w)\;--\;(y,b,w). \qquad(17)

The old matching edge remains incident to the middle vertex. The new matching edge bwbw joins the first and last vertices. Thus, after collapsing each physical W/π\pi cycle as in actual_matching_graph_shared_cycle_fourier_compiler.md, the new edge is a loop at the old cycle vertex. The existing cycle incidences are unchanged, and the integral flow lattice acquires one independent loop coordinate. Any cups/caps used to draw the local Morse collar remain bivalent and do not change this topological statement.

In particular this is not a bridge attachment. Pair-local decorations do not erase the original cyclic magnetic channels. The potential advantage of this particular construction is its shared-radius bound, not a claim of scalar-kernel factorization.

7. Exact source and the remaining analytic interface

The final matched sphere has an actual general Morse realization and the exact original coefficient source of research_matched_sphere_morse_source.md. In that theorem the whole source is the product of the original four-leg polynomials, extracted at the original ordinary targets, with the two protected variables set to zero; all actual turns, cups/caps, half-index phases and reduced mark are retained. The finite full-Hermite filling functional is also the original one. There is therefore a genuine source for the decorated diagram, not merely an entropy model.

The currently stated complete-radius filling theorem and its local packet inputs begin with a marked flat plat. The terminal insertion can introduce intermediate cups/caps. This note does not silently replace that hypothesis by a general Morse presentation, nor claim that a platification preserves every designated disk and source frame. To conclude the full long-filling volume theorem for these decorated cores, one must verify that the exact Morse coefficient and primitive geometric dictionary supply every hypothesis of the existing packet, global CROSS and whole-period theorems. The local one-sided estimates and the strict balance in (15)–(16) already use only this shared coefficient and its actual complete radii. A proof of the remaining interface would yield the full invariant conclusion for independently long fixed fillings of the decorated diagram, with its own original knot complement as the target.

No volume statement for the undecorated core, no short-filling theorem, and no claim that increasing valence alone forces balance is contained in the construction. In fact a one-sided fan sends one middle curvature to infinity with the other fixed and eventually violates balance.
