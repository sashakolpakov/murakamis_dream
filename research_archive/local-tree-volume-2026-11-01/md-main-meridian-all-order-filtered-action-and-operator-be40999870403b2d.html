The filtered microscopic action and a classical characteristic of the actual operator

Private derivation, 1 November 2026. This report concerns a fixed knot for which the proved filled Conway source has its nondegenerate joint first-charge saddle and a fixed exponential gap: the long-filled strict-tree class, and the quantitative all-circle class under its stated numerical criterion. That further geometric identification is distinguished in Section 7.

7.2. What the existing geometric inputs actually provide

The marked Conway deformation, Section 3, has a leaf function g(x,t)=J(2x)−2J(x)−J(x−t)−J(x+t),K(x,t)=g(x,t)−g(x,0). g(x,t)=J(2x)-2J(x)-J(x-t)-J(x+t), \qquad K(x,t)=g(x,t)-g(x,0). \tag{33} That equality of scalar functions does not identify their peripheral uses. In the existing leaf construction tt is the hidden auxiliary-circle parameter. Section 5 of that note proves that every actual main meridian remains parabolic identically: the two adjacent white maps have the same derivative at their common corner. The primitive collar note likewise identifies an auxiliary meridian and disk longitude. The preferred-cusp note computes a translation in their parabolic main peripheral commutant. None of these statements gives a main meridian with eigenvalue eπite^{\pi it} simply by substituting this tt into its existing cells.

The original marked braid source supplies a different, genuinely nonparabolic construction. Its critical-correspondence theorem, Sections 4–6, identifies the regular exponential critical scheme with framed knot representations with actual meridian eigenvalue MM. The longitude-insertion theorem, Sections 1–4, identifies its preferred longitude, full meridian gauge, and logarithmic action differential. The actual geometric normal-period theorem, Sections 3–5, constructs the local geometric formal block of that braid scheme. Those results do not yet identify the grouped modified equations (30) with that particular critical block.

A sufficient next step is an exact classical adapter: identify the stationary equations of (13), with t=u/(πi)t=u/(\pi i), with the original regular geometric critical scheme of the full marked braid source, retaining its cut flag, framing and preferred longitude insertion. The existing marked braid holonomy theorem identifies that latter scheme with framed knot representations and supplies its rational inverse. An identification of these equations on the complete geometric germ would therefore identify (26) with the geometric eigenvalue germ and prove the desired geometric factor by (25). Agreement of their tangent alone is not sufficient.

The argument also does not claim a physical asymptotic formula for fixed M≠1M\ne1, or for ss growing with NN. The variable tt in Section 4 is an auxiliary analytic parameter used to organize all fixed-ss coefficients. The ordinary-color divisors remain in (2), (18)–(20). The translated charts in Section 2 stay away from those divisors; no uniform nonvanishing state across ordinary-color resonances has been asserted.

The translated-chart extension and its use of the general component error estimates should be checked against the complete source proof before this report is treated as a new theorem. The algebraic filtration and diagonal extraction are independent of that source audit once its stated hypotheses hold.
