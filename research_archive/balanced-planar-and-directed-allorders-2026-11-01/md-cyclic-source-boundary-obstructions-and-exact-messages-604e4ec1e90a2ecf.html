Exact boundary messages for cyclic physical sources

This note separates three finite constructions which have different scopes. A two-strand Conway box has a scalar Casimir description. A general plat has an exact matching-graph source, with coupled magnetic channels and a physical trace normal. An arbitrary finite tensor network can also be evaluated by polynomial boundary messages, provided those messages retain every physical boundary index. These statements do not identify the latter two constructions with the scalar edge kernel of an all-circle tree.

All diagrams, their orders and their integer crossing powers are fixed before NN tends to infinity. No new volume theorem for a cyclic plat is claimed here.

2. A physical obstruction to independent scalar boundary pairs

Let VV be the original NN-dimensional module, and let its Cartan operator be

Kva=xQ−ava,0≤a<N,x=qd. Kv_a=xQ^{-a}v_a,\qquad 0\le a<N,\qquad x=q^d. \tag{2}

For two strands the full Hopf commutant is ℂ[Ω]\mathbb C[\Omega], with the repeated Casimir nodes and their first derivatives retained. On four strands consider the proposed independent-pair algebra

ℬN=ℂ[Ω12]⊗ℂ[Ω34]⊂End⁡(V⊗4). \mathcal B_N=\mathbb C[\Omega_{12}]\otimes \mathbb C[\Omega_{34}] \subset\operatorname{End}(V^{\otimes4}). \tag{3}

Every element of (3) commutes with K12=K1K2K_{12}=K_1K_2. This follows directly from the pair coproduct: Ω12\Omega_{12} commutes with K1K2K_1K_2, and Ω34\Omega_{34} acts on the other two factors. It remains true for any linear combination, including one with arbitrary coefficients in additional spectral variables.

The literal positive checked crossing Ř23\check R_{23} does not commute with K12K_{12}. Indeed the nilpotent part of the physical braid is a sum of Er⊗FrE^r\otimes F^r. On v0⊗v1v_0\otimes v_1, only r=0r=0 contributes. The retained Cartan factor and swap therefore give

Ř23(v0⊗v0⊗v1⊗v0)=qd(d−2)/2v0⊗v1⊗v0⊗v0. \check R_{23}(v_0\otimes v_0\otimes v_1\otimes v_0) =q^{d(d-2)/2} v_0\otimes v_1\otimes v_0\otimes v_0. \tag{4}

Consequently

[K12,Ř23](v0⊗v0⊗v1⊗v0)=x2(Q−1−1)qd(d−2)/2v0⊗v1⊗v0⊗v0≠0 [K_{12},\check R_{23}](v_0\otimes v_0\otimes v_1\otimes v_0) =x^2(Q^{-1}-1)q^{d(d-2)/2} v_0\otimes v_1\otimes v_0\otimes v_0\ne0 \tag{5}

for every N≥2N\ge2, at both parities. The fractional Cartan exponent is the actual fixed highest-weight exponent; no principal matrix logarithm is substituted.

Thus a region containing this actual crossing cannot have an exterior-independent expression in (3). In particular, replacing a four-head separator by two independent scalar Newton variables is not a universal physical source identity. This is an operator obstruction before attaching an exterior, not evidence from numerical asymptotics. It does not say that a particular final scalar contraction cannot simplify after its genuine exterior has acted.

There is also a useful stability statement. If A,B∈ℬNA,B\in\mathcal B_N are invertible, then

[K12,AŘ23B]=A[K12,Ř23]B≠0. [K_{12},A\check R_{23}B]=A[K_{12},\check R_{23}]B\ne0. \tag{6}

Hence invertible pair-local decorations do not erase the coupled channel exactly. This assertion applies when the literal decoration is such an invertible operator. It is not asserted for arbitrary turned terminal tangles, which may be singular at particular levels. An asymptotic damping result would require a separate estimate even when (6) applies.

3. An exact polynomial message which does retain the channels

There is nevertheless a general boundary-message construction without any semisimple reduction. Fix the matching labels in (1). Work in the dual-number ring

𝔻=ℂ[ε]/(ε2),T=T(0)+εT(1) \mathbb D=\mathbb C[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2),\qquad T=T^{(0)}+\varepsilon T^{(1)} \tag{7}

for every original regular local tensor. For a region BB, keep all its uncontracted physical and ordinary-channel half-edges. If their dimensions are m1,…,mbm_1,\ldots,m_b, define

ℳB(z1,…,zb)=∑0≤ij<mjTB(i1,…,ib)∏j=1bzjij∈𝔻[z1,…,zb]. \mathcal M_B(z_1,\ldots,z_b) =\sum_{0\le i_j<m_j} T_B(i_1,\ldots,i_b)\prod_{j=1}^b z_j^{i_j} \quad\in\mathbb D[z_1,\ldots,z_b]. \tag{8}

Here TBT_B is the contraction of the original tensors inside BB. It is a definition by finite tensor contraction, rather than an assumed invariant of a surface. Each variable has degree at most mj−1<Nm_j-1<N, because the matching objects also have dimension 2a+1≤N2a+1\le N.

For a straight physical wire, gluing two exposed variables with coefficient arrays fi,gjf_i,g_j is the exact pairing

∑i=0m−1figi=[zm−1]f(z)zm−1g(z−1). \sum_{i=0}^{m-1} f_i g_i =[z^{m-1}]f(z)z^{m-1}g(z^{-1}). \tag{9}

For a cup or cap with entries pijp_{ij} or ℓij\ell_{ij}, use instead ∑ijpijfigj\sum_{ij}p_{ij}f_i g_j or ∑ijℓijfigj\sum_{ij}\ell_{ij}f_i g_j, with its specified port order. The physical cups have complementary support i+j=di+j=d; their actual nonconstant coefficients remain in this pairing. Reversing a port changes the typed pairing, not merely the name of its variable. Multiple boundary wires are glued by the corresponding multiple finite contraction. A closure within one region is the same operation on two variables of its single message, so cycles are allowed.

Multiplication in 𝔻\mathbb D gives exactly

(AB)(0)=A(0)B(0),(AB)(1)=A(1)B(0)+A(0)B(1). (AB)^{(0)}=A^{(0)}B^{(0)},\qquad (AB)^{(1)}=A^{(1)}B^{(0)}+A^{(0)}B^{(1)}. \tag{10}

Induction over any elimination order proves that the final coefficient of ε\varepsilon is the derivative of the full original ordinary closure. Inserting it into (1) therefore recovers the marked source, including its one physical normal. There is no division by πaWa\pi_aW_a and no separate normal assigned to each separator. If a meromorphic recoupling basis is introduced instead, first jets of those new coordinates need not suffice; this statement uses the regular physical presentation throughout.

The boundary arrays have at most NbN^b entries. Thus a fixed decomposition of bounded separator size gives an exact finite algorithm with a fixed polynomial cost in NN, including a separate finite sum over matching labels. This is a statement about storage and finite arithmetic, not about the magnitude or condition number of its entries. For a fixed diagram, the number of matching labels and the separator size are fixed. No estimate uniform in a growing family is asserted.

The same regular physical compiler applies directly to a filled plat at complete main color: replace its boxes by their literal fixed integer powers of the generic highest-weight crossing, retain the original cups and caps, and use the one exposed physical cut. Negative powers are allowed because that crossing is invertible in the generic neighborhood. The generic NN-dimensional highest-weight module then makes the open knot operator scalar, so its ordinary closure is its scalar times the generic quantum dimension. Differentiating once at the root extracts the original marked complete-color scalar with the same νN\nu_N. This gives a source-exact algorithm for the full filled knot, without first projecting to CROSS. It supplies no asymptotic estimate. At a nonintegral moving main color one must instead use its actual fixed-root unrolled modules and their prescribed modified cut; a generic-qq, fixed-dimension highest-weight continuation is not asserted there.

Unlike (3), (8) records a general four-head coefficient array and therefore contains (4). It is a faithful candidate for a cyclic extension. A nonzero leading contraction and a relative bound for the boundary messages remain analytic questions.

4. The cycle variables which the exact complete source retains

The more economical root compiler in actual_matching_graph_shared_cycle_fourier_compiler.md makes part of the missing data explicit. Restore the ordinary cap to identify the physical cycles, collapse each such cycle to a vertex of Γ\Gamma, and direct matching edge ee from its π\pi cycle to its WW cycle. Set

we=re−ae,|we|≤ae,div⁡Γw=0. w_e=r_e-a_e,\qquad |w_e|\le a_e,\qquad \operatorname{div}_{\Gamma}w=0. \tag{11}

The actual source is

AD,N=q−d2NC−1∑𝒂,𝒘 as in (11)(∏eh0,aeωaeϵe)[Xc*1]Nℒc*(Xc*)∏c≠c*[Xc0]Nℒc(Xc). Here CC is the number of physical cycles, the marked cycle is c*c_*, and [Xj]N[X^j]_N sums coefficients whose exponent is jmod⁡Nj\bmod N. Each ℒc\mathcal L_c contains the original endpoint, cup, cap and Fourier phases. In particular its positive local coefficient factors are not permission to replace the whole Laurent polynomial by a positive polynomial. The mark selects charge one, not charge zero. The source proof also retains the physical affine index lifts before using their first-failure zero lemma; they are not freely wrapped modulo NN.

Choose a spanning tree of a connected Γ\Gamma. Its b1=|E|−|V|+1b_1=|E|-|V|+1 chord variables determine every integral circulation uniquely. In the resulting integral fundamental-cycle basis CeαC_{e\alpha},

we=∑α=1b1Ceαtα,tα∈ℤ,|∑αCeαtα|≤ae. w_e=\sum_{\alpha=1}^{b_1}C_{e\alpha}t_\alpha, \qquad t_\alpha\in\mathbb Z,\qquad \left|\sum_\alpha C_{e\alpha}t_\alpha\right|\le a_e. \tag{13}

To prove integrality and uniqueness, prescribe the flow on the chords and solve the vertex equations successively at leaves of the spanning tree. Each removed tree edge has a prescribed integral flow; the last equation follows because the total divergence is zero. This proof allows loops and parallel edges by treating loops as chords. It does not assert that every feasible circulation has a nonzero Fourier coefficient in (12).

Bridge-attachment lemma. If a tree is attached to Γ\Gamma along a single vertex, every newly attached edge has zero flow, and restriction induces an isomorphism of integral circulation lattices. The proof is leaf removal in the attached tree. The capacities in (11) remain satisfied by its zero flow. Repeating the operation does not remove any original circulation variable.

This lemma is conditional only on identifying the actual induced operation on Γ\Gamma. A terminal tangle insertion in a plat must first be checked in the literal strand compiler; its physical-cycle graph is not determined by the visual operation-tree alone. Even when it is a bridge attachment, the new tensors can change the weights and Fourier constraints in (12). The lemma says that support counting alone cannot establish damping of the old circulation channels.

For the literal stellar terminal insertion, that operation is in fact a loop addition, not a bridge addition. In the matched white triangulation, write the old triangle as (x,b,y)(x,b,y), with matching side xyxy. The new triangle pieces are

(x,b,w),(x,y,w),(y,b,w). (x,b,w),\qquad (x,y,w),\qquad (y,b,w). \tag{13a}

The old matching side xyxy remains incident to the middle triangle; the new matching side is bwbw, joining the two outer triangles. After deleting matching edges in the cubic dual, the old two-leg physical vertex is replaced by the path through these three triangles. Its physical cycle is unchanged up to subdivision. The endpoints of the new matching edge therefore belong to that same cycle. Upon collapsing the cycles, this is a new loop at the old vertex of Γ\Gamma.

Consequently, before capacities and the Fourier coefficients in (12) are imposed, its circulation lattice is

ker⁡div⁡Γnew=ker⁡div⁡Γold⊕ℤ,−anew≤wnew≤anew. \ker\operatorname{div}_{\Gamma_{\mathrm{new}}} =\ker\operatorname{div}_{\Gamma_{\mathrm{old}}}\oplus\mathbb Z, \qquad -a_{\mathrm{new}}\le w_{\mathrm{new}}\le a_{\mathrm{new}}. \tag{13b}

The old cycle’s Laurent coefficient also acquires the two actual new vertex factors. Thus the new loop flow is not an independent scalar multiplier after Fourier extraction. This explains why terminal fans can alter and potentially improve a geometric weight balance while enlarging, rather than eliminating, the source’s magnetic circulation space. The triangle-to-cycle calculation uses the W/π\pi source cycles; it must not be confused with the link components obtained from an untwisted pairing of strands.

5. A separate algebraic warning about closing scalar Frobenius diagrams

Let 𝒜=ℂ[z]/P0\mathcal A=\mathbb C[z]/P_0, with

P0(z)=𝒯N(z)+2,ϕ(f)=κ[zN−1](fmod⁡P0),κ=(−1)N−1N2. P_0(z)=\mathcal T_N(z)+2,\qquad \phi(f)=\kappa[z^{N-1}](f\bmod P_0),\qquad \kappa=(-1)^{N-1}N^2. \tag{14}

Its Newton basis is exactly anti-diagonally dual: ϕ(eiej)=δi+j,N−1\phi(e_i e_j)=\delta_{i+j,N-1}. The contraction of a multiplication with its bilinear dual therefore produces the Euler element

𝔢=∑i=0N−1eieN−1−i=P0′(z)κmod⁡P0. \mathfrak e=\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}e_i e_{N-1-i} =\frac{P_0'(z)}{\kappa}\quad\bmod P_0. \tag{15}

Here is a proof retaining the repeated nodes. First use a monic polynomial PP with distinct roots. The primitive idempotent at a root cc has trace κ/P′(c)\kappa/P'(c); its dual is therefore P′(c)/κP'(c)/\kappa times that idempotent. This gives (15) at every root. Both the bilinear inverse matrix and polynomial reduction are polynomial expressions in the coefficients of monic PP, apart from the fixed nonzero scalar κ\kappa: the monomial trace matrix is anti-triangular with anti-diagonal κ\kappa, so its determinant is a fixed nonzero constant. Polynomial continuation proves (15) at the repeated-root P0P_0.

When NN is even, every root of P0P_0 is double. Then (P0′)2(P'_0)^2 is divisible by P0P_0, while P0′P'_0 itself is a nonzero polynomial of degree N−1N-1. Hence

𝔢≠0,𝔢2=0,ϕ(𝔢)=N. \mathfrak e\ne0,\qquad \mathfrak e^2=0, \qquad \phi(\mathfrak e)=N. \tag{16}

For odd NN, the root −2-2 is simple and the other roots are double; the same Euler element has both its simple-node part and its retained nilpotent data. A closed scalar Frobenius contraction can therefore depend strongly on its internal cycle topology. In particular one cannot replace its handles by an invertible constant or discard its normals. This algebraic observation is not an assertion that a physical cyclic plat equals this Frobenius diagram. Establishing that equality is precisely what is missing from a naive scalar-kernel extension.

6. Why neither bounded separators nor long local powers complete the estimate

Bounded separator size gives polynomially many coefficients in (8), but those coefficients can have different exponential rates and phases. Polynomial dimension does not bound the ratio of an absolute contraction to its signed value. The original matching graph has a concrete example: the full numerator contains LEFT and RIGHT path coefficients whose ratio to the complete positive endpoint can be exponentially large at a fixed real spectral point. At NN divisible by 2424, the literal LEFT coefficient recorded in actual_cross_cofactor_factorization.md, equation (32), has ratio at least

(3+23)N/12. (3+2\sqrt3)^{N/12}. \tag{17}

That is a local coefficient obstruction, not a counterexample to the coupled invariant. It does rule out using the all-CROSS coefficientwise bound for the full cyclic message.

Likewise a signed crossing power on one fixed pair is not an absolute spectral filter: its complete Casimir eigenvalues have modulus one. Its effects include phase and normal data. An inserted turned terminal tangle is a different physical operator and can have a useful magnitude profile, but that profile must be established in the coupled boundary frame of (8) or (12). Equation (6) also shows why exact pair-local diagonalization cannot be assumed to erase a crossing between boundary pairs.

The first concrete analytic target for attached-tree filters is therefore a bound for the actual phase-bearing cyclic contraction after the literal local decorations have been inserted, with the shared flows (11), marked Fourier charge and LEFT/RIGHT coefficients retained. One can compute that object using (8), or, at complete circle colors, using (12). A projected scalar edge model or a count of added leaves is insufficient. The already proved signed complete-source asymptotic provides the complete anchor; extending its full moving numerator beyond the local packet remains the substantive missing estimate.

The conclusions established here are finite and unconditional: the counterexample (5), the faithful first-jet compiler (8)–(10), the circulation parameterization and bridge lemma, and the Euler-element calculation (15)–(16). They exclude several shortcuts while preserving a source-exact route for future coupled estimates. No conclusion about the hyperbolic volume of a cyclic comparison graph is substituted for the original complement.

7. The separate balanced-fan route

The subsequent files md-cyclic-source-terminal-fan-morse-adapter and md-cyclic-source-general-morse-period-audit-and-theorem provide a different route for suitably decorated certified cores. They retain the full shared face coefficient, including all magnetic loop channels, and use the actual complete-radius balance established by the two-sided geometric fan construction to control the full one-sided remainder. The resulting complete-main-color long-filling theorem does not require the false scalar-pair reduction excluded here. It does not prove the same global estimate for an undecorated arbitrary core.
