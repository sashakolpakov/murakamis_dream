Signed grade blocks for the original bipyramidal source

This note continues the exact cyclic transfer without changing its source or taking absolute values of local coefficients. It gives an exact polynomial functional-calculus factorization of the north rows, a coefficient formula for the south rows, and the precise shared remainder estimate that would be sufficient for long filling once the separate all-CROSS asymptotic applies. The estimate itself remains unproved.

The fixed diagram is the opposite-offset bipyramid of even size m≥6m\ge6, with the original orientations, protected mark and knot-parity data of the cyclic source note. The quantum level is NN, d=N−1d=N-1, q=eπi/Nq=e^{\pi i/N}. The north variable is denoted XX, to distinguish it from NN, and the south variable is SS. The original ordered factors are Fj={Hfj(0,zj−1,zj+1,zj),j odd,Hfj(S,zj+1,zj−1,zj),j even. 4. A genuine but limited positive form

Let JJ reverse the full index range 0,…,d0,\ldots,d. This positive form is not available for arbitrary original adjacent rows. Away from g=dg=d, reversal of IgI_g does not in general preserve the Casimir diagonal. There is a further obstruction to replacing all these forms by one positive invariant norm. The full bidiagonal CC is cyclic: all its superdiagonal entries are nonzero. Its minimal polynomial is χN\chi_N, with double roots for N≥3N\ge3. Hence CC is not diagonalizable. It cannot be self-adjoint for a positive definite Hermitian form. This observation concerns the unturned f(C)f(C); it makes no unsupported assertion about diagonalizability of f(C)Jf(C)J. A useful estimate must exploit the actual product and its grade constraints, or another correlated form.

There is a separate applicability condition in passing from this exact bipyramidal source to a volume theorem. The published all-CROSS long-filling theorem is stated for certified marked flat plats. The finite matched-sphere Morse coefficient identity by itself does not establish its nonzero-amplitude and contour hypotheses for the present literal bipyramid. One must either exhibit a qualifying flat-plat presentation with the same marked source and primitive words, or verify a general-Morse extension of those hypotheses for this diagram. This note does not perform that check.

6. Dependencies and present scope

The literal finite inputs are the homogeneous Casimir face source, the matched-sphere Morse coefficient identity, and the frozen cyclic-transfer proof. The only new factorization is (8), with its actual off-grade continuation (10) and marked boundary (12). The finite filling step is the separate uniform-filling-norm theorem. The published certified-flat-plat all-CROSS theorem and the private general-Morse adapters motivate the separate condition above; their applicability to this particular family must not be inferred merely from the finite source identity.

The all-parity two-row telescope in the cyclic-transfer note is a boundary specialization of this signed problem. Its cancellation survives the original spectral normal, but it does not supply the missing general boundary block estimate. The algebraic task isolated here is quantitative control of the correlated signed sum (17), with no additional cost from the finite filling normal. A volume conclusion also requires the separate all-CROSS asymptotic and geometric-applicability verification just stated.
