A simple-sphere family for which every mark fails complete-radius balance

This concerns a sufficient estimate for the original full source. It does not assert failure of the invariant’s volume limit or of a sharper fixed-sector comparison. No terminal tangle is added to the diagrams studied here.

6. Literal knot realization and the scope of the obstruction

The top and bottom spoke matchings above are perfect matchings of the cubic dual. Every matched pair has four distinct white vertices. The actual construction contracts disjoint neighborhoods of the matched dual edges to four-terminal boxes, uses the physical untwisted pairing (0,1),(2,3)(0,1),(2,3), and connects the outside terminals by the embedded nonmatching dual edges. The perpendicular crossing circles lie in disjoint balls. Thus it is a link in S3S^3 with precisely this white sphere and source ordering.

Its incidence graph has the untwisted physical main circles as vertices and the crossing boxes as edges. It is connected. Choosing a spanning tree and making exactly its boxes odd merges the main circles one at a time. This gives a knot parity pattern for each member of the family, with either sign at each odd box. Fixing that parity and assigning independently long powers changes no endpoint pairing. This is a topological qualification of the obstruction family; no new volume theorem for its undeformed full source is inferred.

The result excludes a universal theorem asserting that some permissible mark makes the complete packing satisfy the local balance inequalities. It also excludes an extra continuous Möbius parameter as a remedy after the mark is fixed. The scalar Cauchy radii used in a genuinely shared fixed-sector estimate remain independent comparison variables. The present argument does not constrain the success of those sharper estimates, finite sector covers, or cancellation in the original signed source.

7. Why a single unconditioned envelope cannot be repaired by new weights

Fix one mark of any connected simple white sphere. Put zf=log⁡Lfz_f=\log L_f at its ordinary faces and keep its protected coordinates zero. Let VC(z)=∑eXe(z)−∑fzf,Xe=Q(xe,be,ye)+Q(xe,ce,ye), V_C(z)=\sum_e X_e(z)-\sum_f z_f,\qquad X_e=Q(x_e,b_e,y_e)+Q(x_e,c_e,y_e), VH(z)=∑emax⁡{Xe(z),log⁡be,log⁡ce}−∑fzf,(16) V_H(z)=\sum_e\max\{X_e(z),\log b_e,\log c_e\}-\sum_fz_f, \qquad(16) where protected logarithms are omitted. These are the shared complete cost and the unconditioned local envelope, respectively. The complete packing minimizes VCV_C, uniquely modulo common scaling, with value Φ\Phi.

For clarity this minimum cannot be approached by an escaping sequence after fixing scale. Let αtf>0\alpha_{tf}>0 be the complete supported triangle loads, of row sum 1/21/2 and ordinary column sum one. For a direction zz, the recession function is VC∞(z)=∑t(12maxf∈t,Lf>0zf−∑fαtfzf).(17) V_C^\infty(z) =\sum_t\left(\frac12\max_{f\in t,\,L_f>0}z_f -\sum_f\alpha_{tf}z_f\right). \qquad(17) Every summand is nonnegative. It is zero only when all supported coordinates of that triangle agree. The ordinary adjacency graph is connected after deleting the two protected adjacent vertices; every ordinary adjacency is detected in a supported triangle. Hence (17) vanishes only for a common constant. On the unit sphere of a fixed scale slice it has a positive minimum. The bounded closed triangle entropy then makes VCV_C, and thus VHV_H, coercive on that slice.

It follows that inf⁡VH=Φ⇔max⁡{Xe,log⁡be,log⁡ce}=Xe at every complete quartet.(18) \inf V_H=\Phi \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \max\{X_e,\log b_e,\log c_e\}=X_e \text{ at every complete quartet.} \qquad(18) Indeed VH≥VC≥ΦV_H\ge V_C\ge\Phi; equality forces the unique complete minimizer and equality of every nonnegative local difference. Conversely that minimizer realizes equality if all the local conditions hold. A failed complete condition therefore gives a strictly larger minimum for this single envelope, even after arbitrary shared positive vertex weights. For the family above this happens at every mark.

This last observation is deliberately about the unconditioned envelope (16). A genuine fixed non-CROSS sector fixes at least one local label; its cost is different. A finite collection of conditional shared-radius certificates can succeed when (16) or an anchored envelope fails. No minimax interchange or fixed-sector obstruction is claimed here.
