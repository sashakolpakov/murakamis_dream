Opposite terminal powers do not cancel the long fan as a tangle

The operation in the balanced-terminal construction inserts actual quarter-turned elementary tangles. It does not insert a braid followed by its inverse on one fixed axis. This distinction can be certified by the fundamental group of a twofold branched cover, with all joining strips kept fixed.

1. The literal stack from the attachment table

Let Pv=T−1hvP_v=T^{-1}h_v be the original box exposed in its prescribed product collar. For odd nn, hnh_n has the crossing endpoint pairing (0,2),(1,3)(0,2),(1,3). An even list has the through pairing. Pairing composition proves that no extra closed component is created, but it does not supply a tangle isotopy. The actual quarter-turn is a rotation of a four-ended ball; it is not a homomorphism for the chosen product. In particular one cannot replace T(Rn)T(R−n)T(R^n)T(R^{-n}) by T(RnR−n)T(R^nR^{-n}).

2. The marked branched cover of one factor

The twofold cover of a ball branched over a rational two-string tangle is a solid torus. In this instance no classification theorem is needed: start with the two straight strands, whose cover is the product of the twofold branched cover of a two-point disk with an interval. The two-point disk cover is an annulus, so the product is a solid torus. The braid and quarter-turn are boundary homeomorphisms extending to homeomorphisms of the corresponding branched pairs, and hence preserve this solid-torus description while changing its marked boundary slopes.

On the boundary of the straight-strand cover choose a meridian μ0\mu_0 and the longitude ℓ0\ell_0 given by the annulus core over a joining disk containing the two upper endpoints. They intersect once. A positive half twist of the two branch points lifts to one Dehn twist about that annulus core: cut the two-point disk along the joining branch arc, glue two copies with opposite banks, and the half twist makes the resulting annulus banks wind once before regluing. Consequently, after the braid RnR^n, the solid-torus meridian has the boundary class μ0+nℓ0,(3) \mu_0+n\ell_0, \qquad(3) up to the simultaneous orientation signs fixed by the original disk frame. The quarter-turn exchanges the two separating curves on the four-punctured sphere, so exchanges μ0,ℓ0\mu_0,\ell_0 up to sign on its torus cover.

It follows that, for hn=T(Rn)h_n=T(R^n), the core of the annulus over the actual product-joining disk has image ±n\pm n times a generator of the solid torus’s fundamental group. Equivalently, before the quarter-turn this joining curve is μ0\mu_0; modulo the meridian relation (3), it is −nℓ0-n\ell_0. The signs depend on the chosen orientation of the annulus and do not affect its nonzero multiplicity |n||n|.

This is the precise place where the actual fan differs from ordinary braid stacking. For the unturned braid the joining annulus core is ℓ0\ell_0, of multiplicity one for every nn, and inverse braids do cancel. In the turned factor its multiplicity is |n||n|.

3. The exact obstruction for opposite powers

Stacking two factors identifies their joining disks with the two branch points and their ordered boundary paths. On the branched covers this glues the two solid tori along the corresponding annulus. For nonzero n,mn,m, both annulus inclusions are injective on fundamental groups. Van Kampen therefore gives π1(hnhm̃)=⟨a,b∣an=bηm⟩,η∈{1,−1},(4) \pi_1\bigl(\widetilde{h_nh_m}\bigr) =\langle a,b\mid a^n=b^{\eta m}\rangle, \qquad \eta\in\{1,-1\}, \qquad(4) where η\eta records the fixed gluing orientation. Quotienting by the common element in (4) gives C|n|*C|m|.(5) C_{|n|}*C_{|m|}. \qquad(5) This group is nonabelian when both |n|,|m|≥2|n|,|m|\ge2. Thus the group (4) cannot be cyclic. The cover of the identity two-string tangle is a solid torus with fundamental group ℤ\mathbb Z, so hnhmh_nh_m is not isotopic relative to its four marked endpoints to that identity tangle.

For the potentially misleading inverse-power pair m=−nm=-n, even abelianization already distinguishes it: H1(hnh−ñ;ℤ)≅ℤ⊕ℤ/|n|.(6) H_1\bigl(\widetilde{h_nh_{-n}};\mathbb Z\bigr) \cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/|n|. \qquad(6) The torsion is independent of η\eta. It is nontrivial for |n|≥2|n|\ge2, whereas the identity tangle’s cover has torsion-free first homology. Opposite rational fractions or opposite signed powers therefore do not give cancellation in this product collar.

More generally an ordered stack of k≥2k\ge2 such factors has the iterated annular amalgam. Quotienting by its common annulus class gives C|n1|*⋯*C|nk|C_{|n_1|}*\cdots*C_{|n_k|}. If all |ni|≥2|n_i|\ge2, this excludes identity-tangle cancellation for the whole stack, irrespective of signs or an even number of factors. The fan theorem’s added powers are odd and independently sufficiently long, so they lie in this range. The argument makes no claim for the exceptional unit powers, where (5) no longer supplies an obstruction.

4. What this does and does not establish

The retained even endpoint pairing in the fan theorem is compatible with the nontrivial branched-cover topology above. It is not a claim that paired terminal pieces can be removed while retaining the core knot. The independently signed long-filling quantifier introduces no local inverse-pair cancellation: every factor remains a turned rational tangle whose joining-annulus multiplicity is its power’s absolute value.

This proves the requested local obstruction from the actual attachment maps. It does not claim that every decorated closure is nonisotopic to every possible core closure; special global coincidences would require their own argument. The published construction correctly says that it preserves the endpoint pairing and generally changes the knot. No operation in the present note changes an original knot or supplies an undeformed full-source volume bound.
