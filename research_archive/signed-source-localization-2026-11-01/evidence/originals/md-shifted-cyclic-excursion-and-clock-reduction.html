Shifted clocks and finite excursions in the actual bipyramid source

This note gives an exact reduction of the original full-minus-CROSS numerator on the opposite-offset bipyramid. It retains the protected face, the ordinary coefficient targets, the two middle positions of every coupon, and all source phases. The main new reduction is a nilpotent bad-state transfer and its finite Schur complement. It does not prove the remaining exponential bound on that Schur complement.

Throughout, a high index means a middle monomial exponent greater than ⌊(N−1)/2⌋\lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor, not a physical color. All derivative identities are at the prescribed root. They are not generic-qq identities that can be differentiated in qq.

9. The general middle-incidence obstruction

There is a useful graph formulation that does not depend on this bipyramid. In an original matched quadrilateral source, make a bipartite incidence graph with one left vertex for each coupon and one right vertex for each ordinary middle face. Connect a coupon to its two middle faces, deleting protected faces whose target is zero. A non-CROSS coupon must assign its unique high middle exponent to one of these neighbors. Since two high incidences exceed an ordinary target dd, different such coupons must use different neighbors.

It follows directly, without a matching theorem, that for every set E0E_0 of coupons, #{CROSS coupons in E0}≥|E0|−|𝒩ord(E0)|. \#\{\text{CROSS coupons in }E_0\} \ge |E_0|-|\mathcal N_{\mathrm{ord}}(E_0)|. \tag{40} The right side can be negative, in which case the statement is vacuous. Protected faces, actual middle positions, and ordinary target capacities are part of (40). For the bipyramid north coupons the right side is one. This is a necessary support restriction; it is not a sufficient description of the full source or a statement about every assignment with at least one non-CROSS coupon.

For a matched triangulated sphere with RR coupons, the middle-incidence multigraph has RR edges on R+2R+2 white vertices, including isolated vertices, and two protected vertices. There is an injection of every edge to an ordinary endpoint if and only if each connected component is either a tree containing exactly one protected vertex, or a unicyclic component containing neither protected vertex. To prove necessity, a component with ECE_C edges, VCV_C vertices, and pCp_C protected vertices has EC≤VC−pCE_C\le V_C-p_C. The sums of the two sides are both RR, so equality holds in every component. Connectedness gives EC≥VC−1E_C\ge V_C-1, whence pC≤1p_C\le1 and the stated two possibilities. Conversely, orient each tree away from its protected root, and orient each unique cycle cyclically with its attached trees away from the cycle. Assign an edge to its head. This gives the desired injection, also for multiple edges. This characterizes absence of the all-high incidence obstruction only; the remaining monomial degrees and original targets can impose further restrictions.

If another literal finite transfer realizes the original targets and has a repeated deficient coupon block, the same open-path counting method can be used there. Constructing such a transfer with its actual Morse heads and marked targets is a separate requirement. No general sphere transfer or analytic gap follows from (40) alone.

10. What has and has not been reduced

The full sewn difference is now an exact finite-excursion coefficient (20), with a bad resolvent of length bounded independently of the level. The relative face shifts are the explicit finite seam holonomies (24); actual common face shifts reduce to the correct target scalar (26). The root Ward identities close on those clocks only when their shifted coefficient targets are retained.

The older unshifted Green-current cancellation is false and is not used. Nor is there a claim that every non-CROSS sector requires several spectral perturbations: the exact odd-level one-exception counterexample survives. The remaining analytic problem is to bound the signed, fully coupled terms in (20) at the original geometric rate. Nilpotence supplies a finite list of excursion types; it does not supply their exponential size.

11. Bounded exact reproduction

The standalone standard-library runner md-shifted-cyclic-excursion-audit.py constructs the local numerators by powers of the original matrix (3), checks their full polynomial resolvent equation, and compares direct homogeneous monomial extraction with the transfer and Schur formulas. Its recorded scope is:

The output is md-shifted-cyclic-excursion-results.json. These are finite identity checks, separate from the all-level support proof in §4. They assert neither a uniform spectral estimate nor an asymptotic gap.
