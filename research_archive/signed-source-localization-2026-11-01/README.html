Bounds and finite transfer for the complementary contribution

This archive records the research following public commit d8e0449. It contains two quantitative bounds, an exact transfer reduction, and a check of a possible geometric route. These results reduce parts of the remaining problem. They do not establish the volume conjecture for a new family of knots.

The published manuscript and guide are unchanged by this archive. The guide’s methods chapter explains the existing approaches, and its notation chapter defines their objects. The former Sphinx pages redirect to the guide.

The quantity that still needs to be bounded

Fix one of the marked diagrams to which the exact homogeneous source formula applies. Let rr be its number of crossing regions and let NN be the quantum level. Its spectral variables are 𝝈=(σ1,…,σr)∈[−2,2]r\boldsymbol{\sigma}=(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_r)\in[-2,2]^r. The source polynomial SN(𝝈)S_N(\boldsymbol{\sigma}) is obtained by multiplying the local four-variable polynomials and extracting their prescribed shared face coefficient. A face has one coefficient target, even when it belongs to several local factors.

Set d=N−1d=N-1 and h=⌊d/2⌋h=\lfloor d/2\rfloor. Each local polynomial has two distinguished middle exponents, denoted aa and cc. Retaining only monomials with a,c≤ha,c\le h in every factor gives a second shared coefficient CNC_N. This is the contribution called CROSS in the existing formulas. The letters name a precise projection of the original source; they do not define a second knot invariant. Write

DN=SN−CN. D_N=S_N-C_N.

The published geometric-contribution theorem identifies the exponential rate of the selected contribution after the original filling operations with the volume of the resulting knot complement. It also proves that this contribution is nonzero. To infer the same rate for the whole invariant, the contribution of DND_N must still be controlled. For the broader class of unchanged matched-sphere diagrams, that estimate is open.

One sufficient estimate would be

sup𝝈∈[−2,2]r|DN(𝝈)|≤exp⁡(N(Φ−η+o(1))),Φ=Vol⁡(Y)2π,η>0, \sup_{\boldsymbol{\sigma}\in[-2,2]^r}|D_N(\boldsymbol{\sigma})| \le \exp\!\bigl(N(\Phi-\eta+o(1))\bigr), \qquad \Phi=\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(Y)}{2\pi},\quad \eta>0,
where YY is the augmented complement before the crossing circles are filled. The previously proved filling bound would then transfer this strict gap to independently sufficiently long, fixed signed twists. The strict gap matters: the volume of the filled knot complement is smaller than the limiting augmented volume. An estimate merely at rate Φ\Phi does not suffice.

A fixed interval near the positive spectral endpoint

The first result replaces an estimate at the single endpoint +2+2 by an estimate on an interval whose width is independent of NN. Put

Iδ=[2−δ,2],Aδ=1+δ−1/2,ω(δ)=δ(2+log⁡(1+2/δ)), I_\delta=[2-\delta,2],\qquad A_\delta=1+\delta^{-1/2},\qquad \omega(\delta)=\sqrt{\delta}\bigl(2+\log(1+2/\sqrt{\delta})\bigr),
where 0<δ≤10<\delta\le1. Every coefficient of an original local numerator satisfies
supσ∈Iδ|coefficient|≤AδN3eNω(δ)for N≥π/δ. \sup_{\sigma\in I_\delta}|\text{coefficient}| \le A_\delta N^3 e^{N\omega(\delta)} \qquad\text{for }N\ge\pi/\sqrt{\delta}.
The useful feature is ω(δ)→0\omega(\delta)\to0 as δ→0\delta\to0.

The proof first combines the signed one-step and two-step paths in the local recurrence. An exact Green formula expresses the resulting polynomial in nonnegative perturbation parameters. A weighted norm then bounds its growth. Taking absolute values before combining those paths would lose the small rate. The proof retains the unused spectral factors, so it also applies at coincident roots.

The corresponding part of the filling functional includes both values and derivatives at repeated roots. Markov’s polynomial inequality on IδI_\delta bounds these derivatives with only a polynomial cost in NN. Consequently, the part of the original filling sum with all its spectral coordinates in IδI_\delta is exponentially smaller than the geometric contribution when δ\delta is sufficiently small and the fixed twists are sufficiently long.

The complete interval proof also shows why a polynomial bound on a fixed interval would be false: some individual local coefficients grow exponentially at σ=2−δ\sigma=2-\delta. The theorem proves a small exponential rate, not a polynomial bound.

Mixed spectral regions

The same argument works when only some coordinates are near +2+2. An elementary estimate bounds every local coefficient on the entire interval [−2,2][-2,2] by BN−1B^{N-1}, where

B=4(1+2). B=4(1+\sqrt2).
Combining this bound with the interval estimate gives, for any specified set JJ of kk coordinates,
supσe∈Iδ(e∈J)σe∈[−2,2](e∉J)|DN|≤2AδkN3r+3kexp⁡(N[kω(δ)+(r−k)logB]). \sup_{\substack{\sigma_e\in I_\delta\ (e\in J)\\ \sigma_e\in[-2,2]\ (e\notin J)}}|D_N| \le 2A_\delta^kN^{3r+3k} \exp\!\left(N\bigl[k\omega(\delta)+(r-k)\log B\bigr]\right).

The finite filling sum can be partitioned according to which root coordinates belong to IδI_\delta. The same exponential rate bounds each specified part, including all its mixed derivatives. Thus, if

(r−k)log⁡B<Φ, (r-k)\log B<\Phi,
one can choose δ\delta so that the part with at least kk coordinates near +2+2 is strictly smaller than the geometric contribution after sufficiently long fixed fillings. The mixed-region proof gives the constants and the original filling formula.

This removes a union of regions from the remaining problem. Tuples with fewer than kk coordinates in the interval remain uncontrolled by this argument.

A finite transfer reduction for even bipyramids

The second approach preserves the signs and shared face constraints in a cyclic matrix product. Here the diagram is an opposite-offset bipyramid with an even equator of size m≥6m\ge6. The construction is specific to that class; it has not yet been extended to all matched spheres.

A transfer state records two partial face loads u,vu,v, each between zero and dd, together with the current position around the equator. The matrix LL advances this position by one. Let PP project onto the states that enforce the local conditions defining CNC_N, and put Q=I−PQ=I-P. The new support theorem is

(QLQ)3m=0. (QLQ)^{3m}=0.
The exponent depends on the diagram and is independent of the quantum level and the spectral parameters.

The reason is a capacity constraint. A monomial outside the selected local range has exactly one middle exponent greater than hh. Two such high exponents cannot enter the same ordinary face, whose target is dd. The protected face has target zero and accepts none. A complete run through the north factors therefore contains a factor in the selected range. Repeating the run forces more such factors than a path confined to QQ can accommodate. This proves nilpotence before evaluating any coefficients.

Writing

L=(AB1C1D) L=\begin{pmatrix}A&B_1\\ C_1&D\end{pmatrix}
relative to the PP and QQ spaces, and using a formal variable zz to count transfer steps, the matrix
z2B1(I−zD)−1C1=z2B1(∑j=03m−1zjDj)C1 z^2B_1(I-zD)^{-1}C_1 =z^2B_1\left(\sum_{j=0}^{3m-1}z^jD^j\right)C_1
records a passage from PP through QQ and back. A Schur complement expresses the original DND_N as a coefficient of a finite sum of these passages and the PP-transfer AA. The transfer proof gives the exact formula, phases, and coefficient targets.

The Schur identity itself is general algebra. The additional theorem is the level-independent nilpotence of this particular projected transfer. Neither fact bounds the size of its entries. The next analytic step is to estimate the coupled passages at the geometric exponential rate, retaining their signs.

The same note records a necessary correction to shifted integration-by-parts identities: shifted face targets require the larger range 0≤u≤2d0\le u\le2d, and the common south-face coefficient must still be extracted. The original nilpotence argument cannot be reused after changing a face’s capacity without checking it again.

What representation rigidity does give

For one fixed complete hyperbolic knot complement XX, boundary-parabolic representations have only finitely many signed volumes. The volume bound and its equality case therefore give a strict gap below Vol⁡(X)\operatorname{Vol}(X) away from the geometric representation and its orientation reversal. These geometric facts are established in the literature. The representation note spells out the finite-type and continuity argument, including trivial peripheral images, and records the relevant hypotheses.

The primary coverage is Garoufalidis–D. Thurston–Zickert, §1 and Theorem 1.8, Zickert, Definition 4.3 and Theorem 6.10, and Francaviglia, Theorems 5.1 and 7.2. The note distinguishes the signed volume from the real and imaginary parts of the complex regulator.

The existing full critical-point equations of the original marked source produce representations of the knot group and the correct fundamental-class volume. The gap therefore applies to their nongeometric regular critical points. A maximum obtained by restricting some local exponents need not satisfy those full equations. This is the remaining limitation of using representation rigidity directly on separated parts of the source.

The half-charge calculation makes that limitation concrete. Keeping the exact omitted spectral factors changes a local Fourier charge from an integer ℓ\ell to ℓ±12\ell\pm\tfrac12. It does not change the original prefactor, parity rule, or summation convention. The resulting equation cannot be identified with the original primitive filling relation merely by choosing another logarithm branch. No geometric interpretation or negligibility estimate for these isolated modes is asserted.

Reproduction and preserved evidence

Run the finite checks from this directory with

python3 reproduce.py

The continuant check requires NumPy and SymPy; the other two checks use the standard library. The runner writes to reproduction-output/ and compares the results with the saved records. It checks local matrix identities, direct shared coefficient extraction, transfer and Schur formulas, finite support graphs, shifted targets, and the exact half-charge products. These finite calculations do not replace the all-level proofs or prove an asymptotic gap.

The evidence/originals/ directory preserves the original drafts, result files, and review records. Earlier versions of the representation and half-charge notes are retained there. The dependencies/ directory contains the preceding private algebraic notes, and evidence/public-inputs/ records the exact published dependencies. The readable copies under proofs/ change only local links. The manifest records file hashes.

The obstruction examples remain part of the evidence. In particular, the previously proposed unshifted sewn current does not vanish, and the remainder at the all-−2-2 corner is positive at even levels. The latter has a polynomial lower witness; it does not contradict the desired exponential gap.

The unresolved task is still quantitative: bound the rest of the original complementary contribution, either throughout the spectral cube or directly after the original filling operations. The results above provide exact objects and some controlled regions for that task.
