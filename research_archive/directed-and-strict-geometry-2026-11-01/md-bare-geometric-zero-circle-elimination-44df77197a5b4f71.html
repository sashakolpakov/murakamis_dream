Zero-power circles at bare Conway junctions

This private note separates three statements. A zero-power crossing circle can be inserted at a bare product junction and removed by its meridian filling without changing the ordered tangle. Its angular variable can be eliminated exactly from the classical action. The remaining geometric comparison is a comparison of full face and boundary-group maps at the original complete representation; a stationary value alone does not establish it.

The authoritative inputs are the marked mask construction in `notes/actual_conway_general_mask_marked_filling.md`, the full-circle representation in `notes/actual_all_circle_geometric_characteristic.md`, the complete physical-edge theorem in `notes/actual_main_meridian_component_pressure.md`, the regular-window statement in `notes/actual_main_meridian_general_component_comparison.md`, and the filtered action in `notes/actual_main_meridian_filtered_characteristic.md`. No public source is changed.

1. The literal insertion

Use the reduced tree of original product junctions, with consecutive unturned products already grouped. A bare vertex has full valence at least three. Its incident edges are the original quarter-turns; a binary resolution used to describe its positive kernel does not add physical edges.

At a bare vertex insert the literal unturned coupon R0=IR^0=I, at the distinguished position immediately before the original ordered child product. Encircle its two strands by a new crossing circle. Keep every child in its original order. Put ϵv=pv=nv=0 \epsilon_v=p_v=n_v=0 \tag{1} at this new circle. Filling its original primitive meridian removes the auxiliary circle and restores the literal identity coupon. Thus the filled tangle, its endpoints, its framed main strands and its original peripheral curves are unchanged. No new quarter-turn is inserted. Repeating the operation at all bare vertices produces a literal all-circle tree on the same physical edge set.

This is an ordinary local meridian filling, not the assertion that an exponentiated peripheral equation suffices. For a representation the added meridian must be the identity matrix projectively. The noncentral longitude criterion below verifies this separately.

The original crossing powers, writhe and integer a(D)=−w(D)−∑vnv. a(D)=-w(D)-\sum_v n_v. \tag{2} are unchanged. The added circles have filling parameter zero, so their preferred-longitude surgery correction pvℓv2p_v\ell_v^2 also vanishes.
