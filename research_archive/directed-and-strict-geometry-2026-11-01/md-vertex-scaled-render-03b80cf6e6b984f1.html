A vertex-dependent condition for the knot-volume limit

The local tree proof evaluates each subtree as a polynomial in its parent angle. The condition below uses the same exact polynomial and contours, but retains the degree of the current vertex when comparing the error with its leading term. It also allows any vertex to be evaluated last. That vertex has no parent, so it needs no estimate for an outgoing polynomial.

The comparison separates two requirements: controlling the error in the current trace, and bounding the normalized polynomial that will be passed to the next trace. Keeping their different dependence on dvd_v gives a less restrictive sufficient condition.
