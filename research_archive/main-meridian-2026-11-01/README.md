# Working derivations for the main-meridian estimates

This local archive preserves the proofs, intermediate approaches, and explicit obstructions developed on 1 November 2026. The current public statements are in `notes/actual_main_meridian_source_bounds.md` and `notes/actual_main_meridian_auxiliary_color_gap.md`. Earlier versions here retain their original scope, including candidates later disproved; the cubic tangent candidate is followed by its counterexample. These files are working records, not additional public theorem claims.
