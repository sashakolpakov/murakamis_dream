The exact protected left coefficient, its zero rate, and the sewn difference identity

This note concerns the actual homogeneous Casimir numerator at the protected quartet (x,b,c,y)=(x,0,c,0)(x,b,c,y)=(x,0,c,0). It retains the original one-sided row cutoff, every unused spectral factor, and both value and derivative data at the physical nodes. The sharp scalar spectral rate is zero at every permitted load. Consequently its local weighted estimate cannot improve the shared-radius envelope. The last sections give an exact signed regrouping and explain which inverse norms are polynomial and which are exponentially large.

8. Scope and checks

The exact monomer formula and its limiting zero profile agree with the published protected-outer coefficient theorem. The positive Laurent factorization extends the earlier private marked-row calculation to an explicit finite lower bound and the signed sewn identity. The all-level proofs above do not use finite numerical sampling.
