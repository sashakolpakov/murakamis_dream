Audit of the signed stable cap criterion and its actual source mass

1 November 2026. This is a private mathematical audit of the combined criterion signed-stable-cap-neighborhood-criterion.md. The companion JSON records the exact files read. It separates a proved conditional norm estimate from the remaining construction for the varying original source. No public file is changed.

1. Verdict and precise scope

Equations (15)–(17) of the criterion are correct under their stated hypotheses. The current version explicitly requires a nonzero homogeneous real-stable endpoint polynomial with nonnegative coefficients, degree d=N−1d=N-1, a fixed T≥1T\ge1, and finite real pressures F0,FTF_0,F_T. Those hypotheses remove the two initial exposition ambiguities. No further correction is needed in the finite bound or order of limits.

The result is conditional on one exact decomposition satisfying both the root-count condition and its absolute positive-mass estimate. The actual source already has a useful unsigned Taylor-mass estimate, proved below. This is not automatically the mass of a signed stable decomposition. A positive monomial decomposition attains the unsigned mass but need not have the required root-count control. These are distinct obligations, not an obstruction to every possible construction.

The finite functional estimates hold at both parities. Applying them to the presently proved stable determinant exterior restricts the conclusion to odd levels. This audit makes no even-level, whole-spectral-cube, full-remainder, or volume assertion.

4. Limits and uniformity

Keep δ>0\delta>0, v>0v>0, and T≥1T\ge1 fixed first. Take the upper limit along the specified level set. Then let ε↓0\varepsilon\downarrow0. The high-order rate is F0−δlog⁡(v/ε)+ω(v), F_0-\delta\log(v/\varepsilon)+\omega(v), \tag{A8} which tends to minus infinity. The low-order rates have ω(ε)→0\omega(\varepsilon)\to0. Finally let δ↓0\delta\downarrow0. Since RT≥1/4R_T\ge1/4, this last logarithmic passage is continuous. Neither a uniform-in-vv version of the mass limsup nor an exchange of the NN and ε\varepsilon limits is used.

The resulting upper bound is uniform over every complex deformation vector in the stated polydisk and over the actual cap spectrum σ∈[−2,2]\sigma\in[-2,2]. The level-set restriction is inherited throughout. The notation FTF_T in this criterion is lim⁡N−1log⁡PN(T,1)\lim N^{-1}\log P_N(T,1); it is not the normalized tilted pressure used in some earlier notes. The term −12log⁡T-\tfrac12\log T is applied exactly once.

If the limiting upper bound is strictly below a specified complete action Φ\Phi, the monotonicity of the supremum as the polydisk shrinks gives a fixed positive radius and a strict large-NN margin. This is an upper estimate for the selected source sector; it does not assert a nonzero lower asymptotic or identify a full filled invariant.

6. Why this is not yet the signed stable mass

Suppose a proposed stable expansion has, for each Taylor multiindex, BN,β=∑α:βα=βcN,αQN,α. B_{N,\beta}=\sum_{\alpha:\,\beta_\alpha=\beta}c_{N,\alpha}Q_{N,\alpha}. \tag{A18} Taking the value at (1,1)(1,1) and using the triangle inequality gives EN(v)≤WN(v). E_N(v)\le W_N(v). \tag{A19} This direction cannot turn the upper bound (A17) into an upper bound for WNW_N. If all the coefficients in (A18) are nonnegative, equality holds and (A17) supplies the mass assumption with ω(v)=Lv\omega(v)=L\sqrt v. An exact construction must still supply the root-count/order condition for those same pieces.

There is always a positive stable decomposition of each BN,βB_{N,\beta} into its binary monomials, keeping BN,0=PNB_{N,0}=P_N as one endpoint term. It gives exactly WN=ENW_N=E_N, but its rank cost need not be bounded by the spectral order. This failure can already be seen in the original local source, with no invented Taylor polynomial. Identify its faces by x=y=Ax=y=A, b=c=Cb=c=C. At the endpoint, PC(0;A,C)=N2det⁡(CI+AJm)2,Jm=(H−+H+)/2>0. By (A10), the coefficient of bmb^m in Gb(u)G_b(u) is Am(u)=∏r<m(ar+u)A_m(u)=\prod_{r<m}(a_r+u), and Am(0)=NA_m(0)=N. Hence [u]PC(u;A,C)=2N2(∑r<m1ar)C2m+terms divisible by A. The monomial piece C2mC^{2m} has all d=2md=2m root parameters at zero. Its root-count distance from (A20) is therefore exactly dd, already at spectral order one. This proves that the straightforward positive monomial expansion does not satisfy a fixed JJ. It is a local source test of that construction, not a claim that this monomial survives any specified global face extraction, and not a proof that another stable decomposition is impossible.

Conversely, root-count control alone cannot bound the absolute mass of an unspecified signed decomposition: adding the identically canceling pair ecNuPN−ecNuPNe^{cN}uP_N-e^{cN}uP_N leaves the same original source and has root-count distance zero, but increases WN(v)W_N(v) by 2vecN2ve^{cN}. This elementary observation only clarifies why the mass is a property of a chosen construction, not an invariant of its summed polynomial.

The remaining source problem is consequently precise: construct stable pieces satisfying the root-count/order bound and a mass estimate simultaneously, after the same original shared extraction. The endpoint coefficientwise bounds, the local nonstability result, and the present audit neither supply nor rule out that construction.
