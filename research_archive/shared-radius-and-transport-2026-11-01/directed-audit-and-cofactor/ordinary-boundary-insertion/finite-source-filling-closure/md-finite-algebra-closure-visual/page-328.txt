For completeness, the initial simple-sphere packing theorem does not require the absence
of separating triangles.
Remove one triangular face and pool its three boundary angle
demands. Strict Hall inequalities for every nonempty proper destination subset give a positive
allocation of angles. The strictly concave calibrated triangle entropy has an interior maximizer,
whose multipliers give positive radii. The center triangles have zero interior rotational and
translational holonomy. Their boundary develops once around a convex triangle, so the degree
argument makes the development injective. Vertex-disk sectors stop before opposite center
edges; this proves disjointness, including across separating triangles. The contact circles and
outward halfspaces therefore give the labeled strict support certificate. Sending the marked
contact to infinity gives the two protected parallel lines. No radius-balance inequality enters
this construction.
