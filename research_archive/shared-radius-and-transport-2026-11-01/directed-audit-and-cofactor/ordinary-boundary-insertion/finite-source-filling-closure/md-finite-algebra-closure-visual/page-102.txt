The project pins its Lean version. The runner compiles the declarations and then separately
inspects every theorem’s logical assumptions. The accepted foundational axioms are propo-
sitional extensionality, classical choice and quotient soundness; an admitted proof step is
rejected.
These are formal proofs of the stated finite implications. Their applications still require the
hypotheses proved in the manuscript. For example, the scalar entropy comparison does not
prove the Hessian formula, and the divided-difference identity does not identify the original
knot operator. The monic-remainder calculation does not prove the Chebyshev factorization or
its root multiplicities; these data must still be supplied for the actual spectral polynomial. The
exact diagram adapter, uniform analytic estimates, geometric realization, primitive completion
and Proposition 15.1 remain written proofs. The file repro/MANUSCRIPT_AUDIT.md records
the current proof checks and the boundaries of this initial formalization.
