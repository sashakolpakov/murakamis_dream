The dependency map in repro/manuscript_logic.json records the interfaces used by

and final rate-transfer argument. The general unchanged-sphere route retains its unresolved

The following command checks that the recorded premises have the right roles, retain their
assumptions and level scope, refer to existing statements, and contain no circular dependency:
python3 repro/check_manuscript_logic.py \
--output repro/results/manuscript-logic
It also deliberately introduces nine defects and checks that each is rejected. These include
removing the remainder premise, replacing it by the central contribution, importing an open
estimate, and using an odd-level estimate in an all-level conclusion. The map is supplied by
the author; its consistency is not a proof of the mathematics at its vertices. The recorded
source hashes identify the texts used by the check. Internal analytic and geometric lemmas
still require their written proofs.
For ordinary-boundary insertion, the map additionally records the conditional disk minimum
and the small-boundary certificate hypothesis. The scalar comparison in Appendix R has
a Lean proof. It distinguishes a wholly central inserted block from a block with a central
attaching label but noncentral terms elsewhere. The duplicated target is twice the boundary
logarithm in the doubled Lean normalization; it is not twice the removed triangle potential.
The conditional packing and the existence of the shared witnesses remain separate written

The directory formal/ contains a small formalization using Lean’s standard library. It checks
the exact even- and odd-level degree cutoffs, polynomial divided differences without division
at a spectral root, the double-root Hermite identity after clearing denominators, and the finite
tensor-product subtraction used when node values vanish. The formalization also constructs
synthetic division on coefficient lists and the remainder modulo a split monic polynomial

bound on the remainder, and the formula for its top coefficient as an iterated divided difference.
Repeated nodes are allowed; no inverse of ri −rj is introduced. The constructed remainder
and functional are also proved linear over the coefficient ring. Coefficient-equivalent input lists,
including arbitrary trailing zeros, give the same output. Adding a multiple of the constructed
monic polynomial leaves the remainder and functional unchanged. These statements establish
