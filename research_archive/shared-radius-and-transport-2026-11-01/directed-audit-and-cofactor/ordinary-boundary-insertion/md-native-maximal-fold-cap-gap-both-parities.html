Native maximal fold cap bound

The protected cap bound at the actual maximal folded nodes, at both parities

The odd-level restriction in the existing protected-cap application comes from placing every neighboring central spectrum at the fixed number −2-2. At even levels that number is not a physical spectral node. This note instead keeps each neighbor at its actual maximal folded node. The resulting exterior is stable at both parities, and its common coefficient saddle has the same classical pressure, response and tilted upper bounds. Consequently the existing whole-real-interval cap estimate extends to all sufficiently large levels for this specified source slice.

This does not fill the neighboring regions or vary their spectra. In particular it does not control their first normals, the whole spectral cube, multiple noncentral regions, or the full knot invariant.
