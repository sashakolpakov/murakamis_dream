Source certificates at a thin triangular boundary

This is a private continuation of the protected-sum theorem. It records an argument to be checked independently before any extension of public scope. The published protected operation uses two zero boundary weights. Here the same two boundary vertices may be ordinary in the assembled diagram. No finite polynomial factorization is asserted.

The new ingredients are a uniform boundary extension of a finite unabridged certificate and a local comparison with a favorable boundary correction. A proposed explicit family realizing the required boundary ratios is given in §6. The pending audit obligations are listed in §8.

8. Freeze status and remaining obligations

The exact bridge comparison (14), target identity (15), suppression of newly deprotected labels, and the finite witness selection are the proposed analytic mechanism. No bound for generic stable binary polynomials, no far-node spectral gap, and no exact quantum star-triangle factorization is used.
