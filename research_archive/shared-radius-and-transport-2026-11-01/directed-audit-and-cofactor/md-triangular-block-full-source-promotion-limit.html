What triangular substitution preserves in the full source

This note pressure-tests the triangular-block identity in md-cap-triangular-block-response-invariance.md. That identity is correct at the level of complete triangle entropy and the original coefficient targets. It does not, by itself, promote a full knot-volume theorem from a smaller diagram to a larger one.

Three precise conclusions are available. Arbitrary triangular substitution preserves the complete boundary pressure up to a constant. At a protected triangular attachment there is an exact finite factorization on one odd complete-node spectral slice. On the other hand, arbitrary substitution can destroy weak balance and can import the certified R54 obstruction to the same shared-radius envelopes. An independently variable joining spectrum is a substantive remaining obligation, not a change of notation.

All sources in this note are the literal matched-sphere Morse sources. All diagram constructions are made before the level tends to infinity. Only private statements are recorded here.

1. The classical identity and its precise hypotheses

Let PP be a triangulated disk with boundary abcabc, with distinct boundary vertices, such that capping by abcabc gives a simple triangulated sphere. All kk interior vertices are ordinary, each with target one in the normalized action. No restriction on separating triangles or internal cycles is required. With the original triangle potential QQ, 𝒬P(a,b,c)=infℓi>0{∑t⊂PQt(ℓ)−∑i∈P∘logℓi}=Q(a,b,c)+CP. \mathcal{Q}_P(a,b,c)= \inf_{\ell_i>0} \left\{\sum_{t\subset P}Q_t(\ell)-\sum_{i\in P^\circ}\log\ell_i\right\} =Q(a,b,c)+C_P. \tag{1}

For positive boundary radii, the fixed-boundary packing theorem constructs the unique strict interior solution. Its center triangles develop to the prescribed boundary center triangle with degree one. Thus the aggregate angle at each boundary vertex equals the angle of that triangle. The three derivatives of the difference in (1) vanish: ∂log⁡a𝒬P=Θa2π=1πarctan⁡aab+bc+ca=∂log⁡aQ, \partial_{\log a}\mathcal{Q}_P =\frac{\Theta_a}{2\pi} =\frac1\pi\arctan\frac{a}{\sqrt{ab+bc+ca}} =\partial_{\log a}Q, \tag{2} and similarly at b,cb,c. The positive octant is connected. This fixes the additive constant as 𝒬P(1,1,1)−Q(1,1,1)\mathcal{Q}_P(1,1,1)-Q(1,1,1).

The corresponding compact concave entropy identity is ℰP(αa,αb,αc)=∑i=a,b,cf(αi)+CP,αi≥0,∑iαi=12. \mathcal{E}_P(\alpha_a,\alpha_b,\alpha_c) =\sum_{i=a,b,c}f(\alpha_i)+C_P,\qquad \alpha_i\ge0,\quad\sum_i\alpha_i=\frac12. \tag{3} It follows by closed concave duality from (1). In particular, a protected boundary coordinate has aggregate load zero, hence every corner there has load zero. This retains the original targets exactly. One or two protected boundary vertices are allowed; three protected corners are not. The supported Hall and boundary-exclusion arguments in the earlier note supply finite interior multipliers.

The supporting geometric inputs are the fixed-boundary construction in actual_smoothed_source_capacity_blocks.md, section 4, the simple-sphere packing in actual_balanced_terminal_fan_volume.md, section 2, and the source-normalized entropy in actual_triangle_entropy_algebraic_balance.md. No statement about a noncentral local spectral polynomial is used in (1)–(3).

2. Literal matching and the actual augmented volume

To substitute PP for a triangle tt of a matched sphere, supply a matched simple sphere B=P∪fB=P\cup f, where f=abcf=abc is the removed face. Let gg be the triangle matched to ff. If t′t' is the triangle matched to tt, identify the boundary so that their matched sides agree, delete t,ft,f, and pair t′t' with gg. Keep all remaining matching edges and oriented face orders.

Every interior vertex of PP is new. Thus the result is again a simple oriented triangulated sphere; the two triangles at the new matching edge have four distinct vertices. A protected mark on a boundary side is retained only when that side is nonmatching in both inputs. If the mark lies outside the replaced triangle, it is untouched. These conditions are needed: an abstract triangulation with a newly selected matching is not the same recorded diagram.

The inverse-cut theorem gives the resulting augmented link in S3S^3, with its own primitive disk meridians and longitudes. The connected matching graph admits a knot-producing parity pattern by the original spanning-tree pairing argument. This does not assert that an arbitrary preassigned parity pattern survives the substitution. The substitution can change knot type.

Applying (1) before optimizing the old variables gives Φnew=Φold+CP,Vol⁡(Ynew)=2π(Φold+CP). \Phi_{\mathrm{new}}=\Phi_{\mathrm{old}}+C_P,\qquad \operatorname{Vol}(Y_{\mathrm{new}}) =2\pi(\Phi_{\mathrm{old}}+C_P). \tag{4} The second equality uses the actual complete simple-sphere metric and its original fundamental chain. It is not an identification of the filled complements. Sufficiently long primitive fillings of this fixed new diagram are geometrically realized by the general-Morse completion theorem. Their filled volume is its selected continued action, rather than an asserted sum of independently filled volumes. Without a full-source estimate, this geometric conclusion is not a limit theorem for the full invariant.

3. Arbitrary substitution does not preserve weak balance

Consider a matching edge with nonnegative curvatures (x,b,c,y)(x,b,c,y), A=x+y>0A=x+y>0, and keep the opposite triangle (x,c,y)(x,c,y) fixed. Repeated stellar insertion only in the triangle on the bb side is a special case of triangular substitution. Its actual packing curvatures are bj=b+2jAb+xy+j2A=A[(u+j)2−δ],u=b/A+δ,δ=xy/A2. b_j=b+2j\sqrt{Ab+xy}+j^2A =A\left[(u+j)^2-\delta\right],\quad u=\sqrt{b/A+\delta},\quad\delta=xy/A^2. \tag{5} This is the literal positive Descartes insertion, with the original matching edge retained. The other old curvatures, including cc, do not move.

For fixed x,yx,y with A>0A>0, the triangle entropy gives Q(x,t,y)−12log⁡t→0(t→∞). Q(x,t,y)-\frac12\log t\longrightarrow0 \quad(t\longrightarrow\infty). \tag{6} Indeed its variational formula, after subtracting 12log⁡t\frac12\log t, has nonnegative maximum and penalizes every positive outer load by a logarithm tending to minus infinity. Compactness forces the maximizing loads to the middle simplex vertex, where the entropy is zero. This proof also permits one zero outer curvature.

Consequently the inherited matching edge satisfies Q(x,bj,y)+Q(x,c,y)−log⁡bj=Q(x,c,y)−12log⁡bj+o(1)→−∞. Q(x,b_j,y)+Q(x,c,y)-\log b_j =Q(x,c,y)-\frac12\log b_j+o(1)\longrightarrow-\infty. \tag{7} It eventually violates even nonstrict complete weak balance. Every new matched edge created by this particular fan has its newly inscribed curvature as an outer weight, larger than either middle, so those new matching edges satisfy the elementary strict balance test. The failure is at the inherited interface.

This is a structural obstruction across arbitrary size, not a floating-point example. Starting with any covered weak-balanced core does not prevent it. Even numbers of inserted odd terminal pieces may be used, preserving the original four-endpoint pairing and hence its existing knot test; this still can change knot type. It disproves inheritance of the weak-balance hypothesis under arbitrary triangular substitution, not a volume theorem for the resulting knots.

5. A genuine finite factorization on a protected odd endpoint slice

There is an exact finite statement, narrower than full spectral substitution. Let both closed matched spheres have their mark on uvuv, and glue protected singleton triangles as in section 4. Write eD,eBe_D,e_B for their matching regions and e*e_* for the merged region. For odd N=2m+1N=2m+1, d=2md=2m, use the original homogeneous numerator SNS_N, with all ordinary targets dd.

At the simple complete node, the literal local formula is HVN(−2)(x,b,c,y)=N2DN(x,b,y)DN(x,c,y),DN(0,0,B)=Bm. H_{V_N(-2)}(x,b,c,y) =N^2D_N(x,b,y)D_N(x,c,y),\qquad D_N(0,0,B)=B^m. \tag{11} The endpoint determinant has degree mm. Its normalization follows from the original RmR_m recurrence: DN=Rm/ND_N=R_m/N, and both pure outer and pure middle coefficients equal one by the root sine-product identities. Thus (11) keeps the N2N^2 scalar, not merely its exponential rate.

Specialize the merged spectrum to −2-2; every other original spectrum is arbitrary. The old marked-region factors are N2BmDt′,N2BmDg, N^2B^mD_{t'},\qquad N^2B^mD_g, whereas the merged factor is N2Dt′DgN^2D_{t'}D_g. That partial polynomial is therefore a scalar multiple of BmB^m, regardless of the other spectra or phases. The final BdB^d extraction gives SN,new(𝝈,𝝉;σe*=−2)=N−2SN,D(𝝈;σeD=−2)SN,B(𝝉;σeB=−2). It applies to the original full homogeneous sources on that slice. No independent boundary coefficient target was introduced.

At this node the local full numerator is central. Hence the central parts obey the same factorization. If both input diagrams have full source remainder estimates with gaps γD,γB\gamma_D,\gamma_B and full source upper rates at most their complete actions, then (12) gives an odd-level full remainder gap at least min⁡(γD,γB)\min(\gamma_D,\gamma_B) on the merged slice. This permits every other spectral variable to vary on its full real interval.

It does not permit the merged variable to vary. A filling functional there uses the whole spectral polynomial, including paired-node derivative data; its value at the odd simple node does not determine that functional. Fixing this node is also not the same as inserting the zero crossing power. Thus (12) alone gives no new fully filled knot-volume theorem.

7. The available full-volume consequence and the remaining estimate

For a given substituted diagram, a genuine full volume theorem follows if its new complete packing satisfies the existing weak-balance or quantified-defect hypothesis. Equation (1) makes this a modular geometric check:

  • every matching edge disjoint from the substituted triangle and its matching neighbor has its old four curvatures and old comparison;
  • the new internal matching edges use the fixed-boundary block packing;
  • the single inherited matching edge uses its new block-side middle and the unchanged opposite middle.

If these last two sets of comparisons hold, the existing full-source theorem applies to the actual new source at both parities, the finite Hermite functional restores every independent long filling, and the general-Morse primitive completion identifies the rate with the actual new knot-complement volume. The diagram, parity pattern and powers are fixed before NN grows. This is a useful local way to check the existing theorem, not a new criterion implied by arbitrary block topology.

To transfer a full theorem without those new comparisons, one still needs a bound for the partially extracted, matrix-valued or polynomial-valued block on the original shared boundary variables, uniform in its independent real spectra and with the true Hermite normal data retained. It must control every newly introduced noncentral sector and the inherited interface. The complete pressure identity (1), an odd fixed-node factorization (12), and a cap gap with all other spectra fixed do not supply this estimate. Equations (7), (10), and (15) show three distinct reasons why the most immediate promotions fail.

The most general unconditional geometric statement retained here is (4), together with actual long primitive completion of the new simple-sphere diagram. The most general new finite contraction proved here is (12). No unconditional full invariant-volume equality for arbitrary triangular substitutions has been established.
