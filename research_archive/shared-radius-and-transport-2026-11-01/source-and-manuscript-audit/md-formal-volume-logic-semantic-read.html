Finite Lean semantic audit

Semantic read of the finite Lean kernels

This read covers the working-tree versions of formal/VolumeLogic/FiniteSupport.lean, formal/VolumeLogic/FiniteAlgebra.lean, formal/VolumeLogic/RateComparison.lean, and formal/README.md. The accompanying manifest records their hashes. It includes the two new strict-gap wrapper theorems. I did not rerun Lean or the axiom inspection. No public files were edited.

1. Conclusion

The declarations correctly encode the finite implications they state. I found no wrong parity cutoff, reversed product choice, lost sign in the cleared Hermite formula, or vacuous hypothesis producing a false mathematical conclusion.

The README accurately limits the formal result. It leaves the actual source adapter, Chebyshev root data, analytic estimates, nonzero contribution, primitive geometry and volume identity as written inputs. It also distinguishes theorem parameters from trusted axioms. An axiom scan is therefore not described as proving all the premises.

My initial read identified a description issue: the numerical margins in nonempty_sector_gap and filled_rate_gap were not assumed positive, although the prose discussed strict gaps. Their algebraic conclusions were valid. The author has now added sector_strictly_below and filled_remainder_strictly_below with explicit positive-margin hypotheses, and clarified the README. These additions resolve the issue. The README also now separates the analytic range 0<t<10<t<1 from the weaker t≥0t\ge0 needed by the scalar flattening comparison.

2. FiniteSupport

three_supports uses the genuine middle powers i,ti,t and the total support i+t≤N−1i+t\le N-1. It exhausts the central and two one-sided regions and proves that the two strict one-sided alternatives do not overlap. Its additional validity at small nonphysical natural-number values is harmless.

left_endpoint_cutoff assumes N≥2N\ge2 and j<Nj<N, so natural subtraction N−1−jN-1-j is the actual complementary degree. It correctly identifies the left range j<⌊N/2⌋j<\lfloor N/2\rfloor. central_endpoint_even gives i<m,j≥mi<m,\ j\ge m at N=2mN=2m. central_endpoint_odd gives i≤m,j≥mi\le m,\ j\ge m at N=2m+1N=2m+1. Thus the odd middle row is assigned to the central piece, as required by the original numerator decomposition.

protected_moment_degree retains M=⌊N/2⌋−1M=\lfloor N/2\rfloor-1 and proves i+j≤N−2i+j\le N-2. This is only a finite degree bound. It does not prove moment positivity, an inverse-Gram estimate, or a protected source theorem. The README does not claim those conclusions.

3. FiniteAlgebra

horner uses ascending coefficients. The recurrence for dividedDifference gives, for example, a1+a2(z+w)a_1+a_2(z+w) from a0+a1z+a2z2a_0+a_1z+a_2z^2. The difference identity holds in arbitrary commutative rings, including rings with nilpotents, without dividing by z−wz-w. annihilating_root_identity correctly adds the explicit polynomial-root hypothesis. This is the scalar algebra behind a resolvent numerator; it is not yet a quotient trace theorem or a physical matrix adapter.

double_root_jet_identity substitutes v0=h0q,v1=h0p+h1q v_0=h_0q,\qquad v_1=h_0p+h_1q and proves the denominator-cleared formula for f0p+f1qf_0p+f_1q. It retains the nilpotent term f1qf_1q. The actual Hermite application still needs the Chebyshev values of h0,h1h_0,h_1 and h0≠0h_0\ne0 before division. The README states this separation.

nonemptySecondChoices has exactly the required recursion: select the first entry now and a second one later, or select the second entry now and any suffix. Hence tensor_grid_subtraction removes precisely the all-evaluation term. Repeated equal entries in a list still count as distinct coordinates, as the tensor application requires.

productDifference telescopes as (a−b)∏atail+b(∏atail−∏btail). (a-b)\prod a_{\mathrm{tail}} +b\left(\prod a_{\mathrm{tail}}-\prod b_{\mathrm{tail}}\right). It never divides by a potentially zero factor. The commutative-ring setting is appropriate for scalar source coefficients and makes no claim that neighboring physical crossing matrices commute.

4. RateComparison, including the new wrappers

doubled_flattening_gain reduces to t(h−tenergy)≥0t(h-t\,\mathrm{energy})\ge0. Its hypotheses are exactly sufficient for that implication. It does not establish the preceding entropy or Hessian estimates. strict_weak_balance_gain adds t>0t>0 and h>0h>0; the doubled gain is at least th>0th>0, so this is a genuine strict conclusion.

total_nonnegative and nonempty_sector_gap correctly preserve a supplied numerical margin after adding nonnegative losses. sector_strictly_below now adds 0<gap0<\mathrm{gap} and concludes strictly below ϕ\phi, with the desired nonempty-sector semantics.

filled_rate_gap combines 2actionDrop<sourceGap,ϕ−actionDrop≤filledRate 2\,\mathrm{actionDrop}<\mathrm{sourceGap},\qquad \phi-\mathrm{actionDrop}\le\mathrm{filledRate} to obtain 2(ϕ−sourceGap)<2filledRate−sourceGap. 2(\phi-\mathrm{sourceGap}) <2\,\mathrm{filledRate}-\mathrm{sourceGap}. The new filled_remainder_strictly_below explicitly assumes sourceGap>0\mathrm{sourceGap}>0, and correctly concludes ϕ−sourceGap<filledRate\phi-\mathrm{sourceGap}<\mathrm{filledRate}. No source exponential bound, nonzero leading term, or geometric calibration is hidden as a conclusion of this scalar algebra.

5. Remaining formalization boundary

These kernels guard meaningful finite steps: natural-number cutoffs, normal terms, tensor subtraction and scalar budget arithmetic. Their compilation does not by itself verify the enclosing analytic or topological arguments.

The next connected step identified by the README is appropriate: define the polynomial quotient and top-coefficient trace, prove τ(FV)=F(Ω)\tau(FV)=F(\Omega), derive the local Hermite formula, and connect it to the actual crossing jets. This would link the existing divided difference and cleared jet identities to Appendix N’s finite functional.

The review is semantic and read-only. The author’s reported Lean compilation and axiom inspection were not repeated, and no finite experimental result is used as a proof of an analytic input.
