4. One-sided support and the unsplit tensor cancellation

For actual source use, manuscript lines 233–255 and 277–287 import the one-sided divisibility of the original central-cut decomposition. The relevant cutoff is the input row 0≤j≤⌊N/2⌋−1. 0\le j\le \lfloor N/2\rfloor-1 . \tag{F} The original face operator lowers polynomial degree. 6. Dependency skeleton for formalization

The following modules separate finite algebra, elementary analysis, and the diagram adapter. Each arrow below means a theorem dependency, not a finite numerical test.

  1. Chebyshev polynomial and roots. Define TNT_N, prove its recurrence, factorization of χ\chi, root multiplicities, derivative identities, and odd-angle grid multiplicities.
  2. Finite quotient and coefficient trace. Define ℂ[z]/(χ)\mathbb C[z]/(\chi), the top-coefficient functional τ\tau, Hermite interpolation by the Chinese remainder theorem, and prove (A)–(C). This proves the full resolvent functional without assuming a diagonalizable Casimir.
  3. Actual crossing jets. From the literal signed braid/Casimir matrices prove their value and first-derivative data (D), including the scalar lift and the odd simple value. Match these jets with the displayed Gaussian. This module is independent of global topology.
  4. Midpoint functional. Combine modules 1–3 and the elementary chain rule to obtain uf:eq-12 and uf:eq-15.
  5. Finite trigonometric interpolation. Prove Riesz interpolation on a basis of Fourier monomials, sum its absolute weights by the sine product, and extend to finite-dimensional normed spaces.
  6. One-variable functional bound. Combine modules 4–5 with the two Gaussian modulus estimates; prove (E), the vanishing-value variant, and uf:eq-1/uf:eq-2.
  7. Multivariable polynomial norm. Prove preservation of hyperplane vanishing, mixed derivative bounds, and the nonempty-subset identity (I). This proves uf:eq-3/uf:eq-4 for arbitrary polynomials of the stated multidegree.
  8. Actual source adapter. Define the ordered physical tensors, homogeneous face kernel, mark and target; prove the local Fourier identity, true support, face winding, primitive grid, parity coset and total-degree alias exclusion. This proves uf:eq-6/uf:eq-7 independently of source positivity.
  9. Actual central-cut support. Prove the triangular block restriction (F), factor (G), reflected block, and odd central row assignment. Then local node equality and module 8 prove uf:eq-25.
  10. Conditional rate transfer. For a fixed diagram and fixed signed powers, modules 7–9 turn uf:eq-26 into uf:eq-27. Given the separate nonzero asymptotic uf:eq-28 with its strict real-action inequality, the reverse triangle inequality proves uf:eq-29. Geometric identification is a further theorem, not an output of modules 1–9.
