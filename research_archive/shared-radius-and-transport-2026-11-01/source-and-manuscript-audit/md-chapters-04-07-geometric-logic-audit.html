7. Rate transfer and circularity

I read the new 09-reproduction.tex proposition and its source maps. The recursive source, alias, local packet and full Fourier-charge arguments are assigned to their corresponding chapters and appendices. The geometry in 04/07/D does not depend on their final volume limit. Conversely, the full invariant conclusion requires their nonzero coefficient and entire-remainder estimates, not just the geometry.

The weak-balance dependency paragraph is also consistent with the earlier bounded O/P audit: Appendix O uses Appendix L’s complete packing and exact finite Morse source, not L’s full-invariant volume conclusion.
