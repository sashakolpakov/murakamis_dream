# Shared-radius inequalities and transport estimates

This archive preserves private calculations developed alongside the editorial revision of 1 November 2026. They have not been added to the public manuscript.

The bipyramid files contain rational finite certificates and the analytic shared-radius argument for all even sizes, together with its fixed-constant verifier. The prefix-gauge and marked-row notes retain the full original spectral factors and describe the remaining cost of sewing the estimates. The middle-graph note records the general endpoint extension.

These files retain their own mathematical scope. A local gap or a geometric contribution is not by itself the full Kashaev volume limit. A final written assembly with all source and filling hypotheses is required before a broader theorem is included in the manuscript. The manifest records the exact preserved files.
