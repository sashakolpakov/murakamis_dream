# Finite algebra and support arguments in Lean

This package proves the finite polynomial, support and sector arguments
used in the [reference manuscript](../manuscript/README.md). It uses Lean's
standard library and has no external package dependencies. The companion
[Mathlib package](../formal-real/README.md) treats complex norms, Fourier
coefficients, asymptotic transfer and fixed-boundary optimization.

The two packages contain 28 mathematical modules. Their manuscript
correspondence selects 76 theorem roots: 37 here, 20 in the complex and
asymptotic arguments, and 19 in the variational arguments. These are
selected proof components, not 76 formalized knot-volume theorems.

## What this package proves

| Modules | Mathematical role |
| --- | --- |
| FiniteAlgebra; MonicRemainder, MonicLinearity, MonicUniqueness and MonicProduct | Construct the canonical remainder modulo a monic polynomial; prove its degree, uniqueness, linearity and independence of the representative; reduce products while retaining the derivative data at repeated roots. |
| ResolventFunctional | Construct a polynomial resolvent and recover the top coefficient of the remainder without dividing by differences of roots. |
| FiniteSupport and FaceGridSupport | Prove the central and one-sided support cutoffs at both level parities, and the exponent selection forced by the residue and degree conditions. |
| SourceAssembly | Apply an additive filling functional to the coherent sector sum before taking absolute values, retaining every sector and its multiplicity. |
| RateComparison, ProtectedSum and TriangularBoundary | Combine stated gains and costs, and prove the finite comparisons used in protected gluing and ordinary insertion. |

Polynomials are represented by ascending coefficient lists over a
commutative ring. For a polynomial in one Casimir operator, the coefficient
ring may be the commutative algebra generated by that operator. This
application does not require different crossing operators to commute.
The identification with a particular tangle calculation is a written
application of the algebraic theorem.

## How the statements were compared

The [finite correspondence record](correspondence/finite.json) gives a
symbol dictionary, hypotheses, conclusion and manuscript location for
each of the 37 selected roots. The dictionaries were checked by reading
the mathematical definitions and proofs. They are author-reviewed
interpretations; Lean does not read the TeX or certify that interpretation.

[StatementContracts.lean](StatementContracts.lean) provides a separate
check. Each anonymous example states an expected proposition with its
binders and hypotheses, then proves it using the corresponding library
theorem. Lean checks that application. This tests the mathematical type,
rather than merely confirming that a theorem name exists. The records
distinguish exact correspondence, specialization of a more general
theorem, a component of a longer proof, and a conditional implication
whose application still has written inputs.

The [coverage guide](../repro/PAPER_FORMAL_COVERAGE.md) explains these four
relations and the three compiler controls for essential hypotheses and
normalizations. The [manuscript and logic audit](../repro/MANUSCRIPT_AND_LOGIC_AUDIT_2026-11-25.md)
records the substantive corrections made during the revision.

## Compilation and axiom checks

Run the combined check from the repository root:

~~~sh
python3 repro/check_lean.py --output repro/results/manuscript-and-lean/lean
~~~

It compiles both packages, checks the expected-type contracts and runs
the compiler controls. Separately, it inventories **every public
definition and theorem**, including supporting declarations outside the
76 selected roots, and asks Lean for their axiom dependencies. Only
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` are allowed; admitted proofs
or additional axioms are rejected. Import, manuscript-anchor, dependency
and source-inventory checks guard the scope of this replay. Anchors and
hashes locate and freeze a statement; they do not prove its interpretation.

For this package alone:

~~~sh
cd formal
lake build
lake env lean StatementContracts.lean
lake env lean AxiomAudit.lean
~~~

The [completed replay](../repro/results/manuscript-and-lean/lean/summary.json)
records 28 compiled modules, 534 public declarations audited, 76 checked
statement contracts and all three intended type errors rejected. The
written geometric, contour and knot-application arguments remain outside
these finite Lean proofs.
