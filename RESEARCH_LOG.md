# Research status

Updated 25 November 2026.

The retained volume results and their hypotheses are stated in the
[current papers](papers/README.md) and the
[reference manuscript](manuscript/README.md). The
[manuscript and logic audit](repro/MANUSCRIPT_AND_LOGIC_AUDIT_2026-11-25.md)
records their latest corrections and the precise coverage of Lean.

The broader problem is to extend the sufficient long-filling condition
across an arbitrary fixed short partial filling. For each fixed
admissible construction, the established long-filling condition has
natural density one among integer assignments. It leaves infinitely
many assignments with short coordinates outside its scope.

The [current research note](notes/research_finite_exceptions_progress.md)
collects the general fixed-short-filling problem, the orthogonality and
stationary-elimination reductions, and the remaining noncancellation
step. No finite-exception theorem is claimed.

The former chronological log mixed current results with superseded
attempts. It is preserved with the other pre-cleanup research sources in
[the Git snapshot](https://github.com/sashakolpakov/murakamis_dream/tree/19d5b911bc409486f0e72b51a876a6a2bc70ee02).
The working repository keeps the papers, their proof dependencies,
useful obstructions and the current research directions.
