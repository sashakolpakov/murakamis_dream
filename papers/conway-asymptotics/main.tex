\begin{abstract}
We prove the volume conjecture for knots obtained from a fixed strict
Conway tangle construction by independently sufficiently long signed
twists. The construction and twists are fixed while the quantum level
tends to infinity through all integers. The proof expands each twisting
operator exactly in colored crossing circles, estimates the resulting
finite sum, and identifies its nonzero leading term with the hyperbolic
volume of the knot complement. Two ingredients make the calculation
manageable: linear operations are treated exactly before estimates,
and a single finite-lattice argument
treats both the internal indices and the subsequent Fourier transform.
The filling coefficients are controlled by Fourier orthogonality with
the derivative data at repeated roots included. For each eligible fixed
construction, the covered integer twist assignments have natural density
one.
\end{abstract}

\section{Reproducing the finite algebra}\label{ca:reproduction}
The accompanying program \texttt{check.py} uses Python's standard library.
From the repository root, run
\[
 \texttt{python3 -I papers/conway-asymptotics/check.py}
\]
optionally adding \texttt{--output /path/to/directory}.

For levels $2$ through $12$, the program works over two finite fields
containing the required roots of unity. It constructs the crossing from
its Newton polynomial and forms positive, negative, and zero powers by
polynomial arithmetic. It compares them with Fourier--Hermite
interpolation and the colored-circle expansion, then checks the midpoint
identity, including the zero-deficit multiplicity. Composite levels and
the odd-level simple root are included.

These checks can detect finite-algebra transcription errors. The
all-level identities are proved in Section~\ref{ca:filling}; the
complex estimates and metric completion are written arguments with
the inputs listed in the introduction. In particular the checks do
not establish a contour deformation or a volume asymptotic by sampling.
