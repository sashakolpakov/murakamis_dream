\subsection{The pure source and its physical remainder}
\label{ca:strict-pure-section}

Fix a strict construction from Section~\ref{ca:intro}, with $b\ge2$,
and evaluate every auxiliary circle without a retained half twist.
Section~\ref{ca:filling} will expand each full crossing power in this
pure circle basis. Write $\mathcal A_N(\mathbf h)$ for the resulting
physical source at circle colors $N-h_\ell$.

Put $M_N=\lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor$. For a physical color deficit $h$,
the core coefficient of a leaf is zero when $r<h$ or $r>M_N$.
For $0\le h\le r\le M_N$ it is the exact positive ratio
\begin{equation}\label{ca:pure-leaf}
 A_N(r,h/N)=
 \frac{N^2S_{2r}}{S_r^2S_{r-h}S_{r+h}}.
\end{equation}
This is the pure case of \cite[Proposition 4.1]{Newton}.
In the strict window, continue the color $t=h/N$ and the index
$x=r/N$ by the single function
\begin{equation}\label{ca:pure-index}
 \mathscr L_N(x,t)=
 \frac{N^2G_N(2Nx)}
 {G_N(Nx)^2G_N(N(x-t))G_N(N(x+t))}.
\end{equation}
It agrees exactly with \eqref{ca:pure-leaf} on the physical grid.
Its action is
\[
 g(x,t)=J(2x)-2J(x)-J(x-t)-J(x+t).
\]
The continuation is regular at $x=1/4$ as well.

Let $y_*$ be the unique complete strict maximizer. Choose a fixed
rectangle $\mathcal B$ about $y_*$ in the primitive coordinates
\eqref{ca:offsets}, inside every strict gate domain. Its leaf indices
lie in a compact subset of $(0,1/2)$. Choose a complex color polydisk
small enough that all shifted factorial arguments in
\eqref{ca:pure-index} remain in the strip of Lemma~\ref{ca:factorial}.
Define $C_N(t)$ by summing the product of these leaf functions and
the gate functions \eqref{ca:gate} over $\mathbf k/N\in\mathcal B$,
where every gate factorial $S_j$ is replaced by $G_N(j)$.
The support of this finite sum is independent of $t$.
Thus $C_N$ is holomorphic and separately even. For real small $t$
it is positive for every sufficiently large $N$.

\begin{theorem}[The pure source near its maximum]\label{ca:strict-pure}
For every fixed strict construction with $b\ge2$, there are
holomorphic separately even functions $a_0,\Phi_0$ on a fixed complex
polydisk such that
\[
 C_N(t)=N^{3/2}a_0(t)e^{N\Phi_0(t)}(1+O(N^{-1})).
\]
The expansion is uniform, with every fixed derivative on smaller
polydisks. The amplitude is nonzero and $a_0(0)>0$. If $a_\ell$ are
the complete leaf coordinates, then
\begin{equation}\label{ca:strict-pure-hessian}
 \Phi_0(0)=F_0,\qquad
 \Phi_0''(0)=-2\pi\diag(X_1,\ldots,X_b),\qquad
 X_\ell=\cot(\pi a_\ell)>0.
\end{equation}
There are fixed $\delta,\gamma>0$ such that the entire physical
source satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{ca:source-error}
 |\mathcal A_N(\mathbf h)-C_N(\mathbf h/N)|
       \le e^{N(F_0-\gamma)}
       \qquad(0\le h_\ell\le\delta N)
\end{equation}
for all sufficiently large $N$. If any physical deficit is at least
$\delta N$, the entire source has a strict exponential loss from
$F_0$. Finally, on a smaller complex polydisk,
\begin{equation}\label{ca:color-gaussian}
 |C_N(x+\ii y)|\le
 N^C\exp N\{F_0-c_{\rm col}\|x\|^2+C_{\rm col}\|y\|^2\},
 \qquad c_{\rm col}>0.
\end{equation}
All statements include both level parities.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The phase of the interpolated summand is
\[
 F(y,t)=\sum_{\ell=1}^b g(L_\ell(y),t_\ell)
            +\sum_{\text{gates}}m(L_i,L_j,L_r).
\]
All factorial arguments lie in fixed strict complex neighborhoods.
Lemma~\ref{ca:factorial} gives, with uniform fixed derivatives,
\[
 N^{3-b}b_0(y,t)e^{NF(y,t)}(1+O(N^{-1})).
\]
Each leaf contributes $N^{1/2}$ and each of the $b-2$ gates
contributes $N^{-3/2}$. The unshifted leaf density is
\[
 \frac{\sqrt{\sin(2\pi x)}}
 {2^{3/2}\sin(\pi x)
       \sqrt{\sin(\pi(x-t))\sin(\pi(x+t))}},
\]
and the unshifted gate density is
\[
 b_\mu(x,y,z)=2^{-3/2}
 \sqrt{\frac{\sin\pi(x+y)\sin\pi z}
 {\sin\pi x\sin\pi y\sin\pi(z-x)\sin\pi(z-y)\sin\pi(x+y-z)}}.
\]
Multiply these densities and include each integer offset from
\eqref{ca:offsets}: the leaf offset contributes
$e^{\delta_\ell g_x}$ and a gate contributes
$e^{\delta_i m_x+\delta_jm_y+\delta_rm_z}$.
The derivatives are evaluated at the corresponding changed arguments.
This fixes $b_0$, including its normalization. At real strict
arguments it is positive.

Theorem 6.4 of \cite{Newton} gives the unique maximum $y_*$ and
negative definite Hessian $F_{yy}(y_*,0)$.
The implicit-function theorem gives its continuation $y_*(t)$.
Define
\[
 \Phi_0(t)=F(y_*(t),t),\qquad
 a_0(t)=
 \frac{(2\pi)^{k/2}b_0(y_*(t),t)}
 {\sqrt{\det[-F_{yy}(y_*(t),t)]}}.
\]
The square root is positive at zero and continues holomorphically.
Apply the finite lattice estimate of Section~\ref{ca:lattice-method}
with $\rho=3-b$ and $k=2b-3$.
The strict maximum gives the boundary loss, and the coordinates
have covolume one. This proves the expansion, its derivative bounds
and the prefactor $N^{3-b+k/2}=N^{3/2}$.
Every interpolated factor agrees exactly on the wire grid, so no
index-interpolation error has to be estimated.

Evenness in every color gives $F_{yt}(y,0)=0$, while
$g_{tt}(x,0)=-2\pi\cot(\pi x)$.
To compute the reduced Hessian, differentiate
$F_y(y_*(t),t)=0$ and $\Phi_0(t)=F(y_*(t),t)$. This gives
\begin{equation}\label{ca:stationary-schur}
 Dy_*=-F_{yy}^{-1}F_{yt},\qquad
 (\Phi_0)_{tt}=F_{tt}-F_{ty}F_{yy}^{-1}F_{yt},
\end{equation}
where every block is evaluated on the stationary family. The mixed
block vanishes at zero, proving \eqref{ca:strict-pure-hessian}.
Positivity of the density gives
$a_0(0)>0$; shrinking the disk makes $a_0$ nonzero.
The negative real-color Hessian, evenness and holomorphy give
\eqref{ca:color-gaussian}. The exact finite window and its relative
remainder satisfy this bound, not just its leading approximation.

It remains to compare the window with the physical source.
The uniform endpoint estimate
$\log S_r=NJ(r/N)+O(\log N)$ from \cite[Lemma 6.2]{Newton}
controls the nonnegative ratio \eqref{ca:pure-leaf} on its entire
physical support. Its rate satisfies
\[
 g(x,t)=g(x,0)-\Delta(x,t),\qquad
 \Delta(x,t)=J(x-t)+J(x+t)-2J(x)\ge0
 \quad(0\le t\le x\le1/2).
\]
Indeed, for $0<t<x<1/2$,
\[
 \partial_t\Delta(x,t)=
 \log\frac{\sin\pi(x+t)}{\sin\pi(x-t)}>0,
\]
since the difference of the sines is
$2\cos(\pi x)\sin(\pi t)>0$.
Continuity gives the endpoint cases.
The modulus of continuity $O(\eta|\log\eta|)$ of $J$ shows that
the bounded affine wire offsets cost only $O(\log N)$.

The complete action has its unique maximum inside $\mathcal B$.
Compactness gives a fixed gap on the rest of its state polytope,
and the nonnegative losses $\Delta$ preserve it throughout a small
real color corridor. The polynomial number of states and the
$O(\log N)$ errors preserve a strict exponential loss for the
whole core outside the window.

Finally \cite[Theorem 1.1]{Bounds}, or
\cite[Corollary 9.1]{Bounds} for $b=2$, bounds the entire
full-minus-core difference and supplies the macroscopic-color loss.
Combining these bounds proves \eqref{ca:source-error} and the
remaining physical-source assertions.
\end{proof}
